# Public website versus research: gap audit

## Verdict and scope

**The public website only partially reflects the inspected research.** Its broad separation of mathematical constructions, conditional models, physical hypotheses and exclusions remains appropriate. The principal gaps are stale curation, missing constructive results and counterexamples, incomplete workbench guidance, and navigation/source-publication mismatches—not evidence that the site should make stronger physical claims.

Repository baseline: `7afcae687310b5db324ebfab4fbfe26444c73758`, plus the working-tree research and app documents present during inspection. Pending local material is identified below; its presence is not evidence of publication.

Inspection covered the four canonical synthesis documents, navigation, the construction gallery and viewer guides, all **58 non-ledger public Markdown files by targeted content search**, the **43-entry result inventory**, publication scripts, and selected research packets across Nima, Voevodsky, Strominger, Grothendieck, Aspect, Benincasa, Kitaev and Sontag. Detailed comparisons concentrate on the interval/projective, higher-witness, Ramsey, SU32, isotope-viewer and cellulation developments. This is not an exhaustive scientific review of every file under `research/`.

The initial inspection was a **repository-source audit**, not an inspection of the deployed website. No deployment was fetched or proof-assistant claim independently reverified. Numerical checks and proof statuses in the findings are attributed to their source packets. Subsequent publication builds and regression checks are recorded separately in the closure log.
