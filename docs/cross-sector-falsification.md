# Cross-Sector Falsification

This policy governs promotion of sector-local constructions into Marici core machinery.

## Conjecture

Marici has one resolved scalar stratified carrier with occurrence data, Cutlink structure, flagged normal geometry, duality, and a support-filtered derived coefficient calculus. Scattering theories and cosmological wavefunctions are distinct derived sectors of this carrier.

The conjecture is not that one strict operator algebra acts on amplitudes. The candidate common structure is

```text
self-factorizing stratified carriers
+
derived, support-sensitive dictionaries between their coefficient systems
```

Candidate core mechanisms include

```text
Cut
associated grade
first normal jet
Gysin  Cartier specialization
duality  pairing
flagged excess comparison
```

Cosmological nesting is treated as flag structure in the carrier. Partial-energy data are treated as occurrence-resolved coefficient data.

Physical sectors and realization layers are separate axes.

Prospective sector expansion follows the provisional research-priority vector

```text

< integrability
< flavor
```

Here `<` means smaller expected conditional description length relative to the
current Marici core, and therefore earlier research priority. It is not a claim
of physical containment, importance, or evidential maturity.

`string` is first because the current program already uses moduli-space
compactification, Koba--Nielsen forms, twisted cohomology, intersection
pairings, and Gauss--Manin transport. `gravity` is already represented inside
the scattering family; its position refers to testing whether it closes as an
independently typed sector rather than merely as a double-copy realization.
The remaining entries are prospective sectors only.

This ordering is a search policy, not an ontology. A candidate becomes a
physical sector only after its source objects, coefficient objects, and at
least one hostile cross-sector falsifier have been frozen independently. Until
then it remains a related research program. The vector may be revised only by
new evidence about the additional machinery required to type and close a
sector.


A result may belong to one sector and several layers. A layer is not promoted to a physical sector merely because it has separate mathematics.

## Promotion rule

A structural mechanism proposed in one sector may be promoted to Marici core only if

1. its source object is defined before matching the target physical answer;
2. its normalization is fixed by incidence, orientation, integrality, duality, or another predeclared invariant;
3. no new cells, coefficient summands, support conditions, normalization constants, or fitted corrections are added after the test begins;
4. the nearest structurally independent sector analogue is tested;
5. failure in either sector blocks core promotion.

Sector-local success remains valid in its stated scope when cross-sector promotion fails.

## Hard-to-vary test protocol

For every candidate core mechanism `C`, define at least two independent realizations whenever a structurally comparable sector exists

```text
C_sector_A
C_sector_B
```

Before computation, freeze

- carrier;
- coefficient complexes;
- support filtration;
- normal orientations;
- admissible homotopies;
- normalization;
- expected target class;
- prohibited repairs.

Compute explicit obstruction classes or an equivalent finite falsifier.

Promotion requires both tests to pass without sector-specific repair.

A nonzero obstruction in either sector falsifies the claim that `C` is universal Marici machinery. It does not falsify a sector-local theorem whose assumptions remain satisfied.

## Alternative hypothesis

If cross-sector universality fails, retain the common carrier claim in the weaker form

```text
one scalar carrier organizes factorization strata and local symbols,
but sector-specific global assembly data are required.
```

The discriminating question is therefore whether the global physical dictionaries descend from the common scalar carrier, not whether several sectors share local combinatorics.

## Documentation policy

Program-level conjectures, promotion rules, and falsification protocols belong in `docs`.

Sector-local experiments remain research artifacts until they produce a material result under `docsresearch-lifecycle.md`.

A material cross-sector result gets one ledger entry only when it changes the status or scope of a scientific claim, closes or refines an epistemic-graph edge, introduces a reusable construction or obstruction, or materially changes the next discriminating experiment.

## Research objective

Determine the smallest set of structures on the resolved scalar carrier for which independently tested physical sectors close.

Evidence for the common-core conjecture consists of decreasing independent primitive count together with increasing independently passed cross-sector falsification tests.
