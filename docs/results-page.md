## Mathematical connections: identities versus proposals

- **Boolean carrier:** $\mathrm{Bool}\times\mathrm{Bool}$ has four elements. It is not the free Boolean algebra on two generators, which has 16 elements. A four-label basis can be used for a two-qubit Hilbert space only after linearization and tensor structure are specified; $S_4$ is not the Clifford group.
- **Gram and information models:** a nonzero positive Gram matrix gives a density matrix $G/\operatorname{Tr}G$ after choosing a Hilbert-space interpretation. Kernel, covariance, and entropy constructions are conditional on their declared probes, measures, and state semantics.
- **Finite symmetries and braids:** finite permutation actions and the map from braid groups to permutation groups provide concrete mathematical tools. Neither alone assigns physical interaction amplitudes or derives continuous gauge groups.
- **Broader connections:** modular weights, moduli spaces, stable homotopy, sporadic groups, division algebras, TQFT, and integrable systems remain separate proposed comparisons unless an explicit map and its laws are supplied. Matching dimensions or the identity $12^3=1728=j(i)$ does not establish those identifications; $i$ is not a modular cusp.
