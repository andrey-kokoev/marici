      Diagram grammar

      One EP record owns one red and one blue path. A complete cell owns two EP records. Shared visible edges retain their separate histories.

      One green H or V witness counts as one neutron. A shell boundary counts only when its witness record is present.

      Red and blue directions alternate between shells. A delta is an alternate definition of the complete graph, not a physical isotope-growth claim.

      Wiring accepts absolute graphs or deltas with add, remove, and replace operations. Change the ID and name to create another definition.
