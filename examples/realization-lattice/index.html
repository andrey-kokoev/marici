Navigation & meaning

Drag to rotate freely; scroll or pinch to zoom. Touch targets extend beyond the small visible vertices. For overlapping vertices, zoom or use the vertex list.

Plane opacity is normalized by the number of displayed patches to keep larger windows readable.

With the canvas focused: arrow keys rotate, Q/E roll, +/− zoom, F centres the selection and R resets.

The local directions are geometric—not yet an identification with the tower’s twelve field sectors. Missing neighbours at the window edge are clipping, not a physical boundary.
