Two-attractor momentum field · Three.js

A planar XY field in an orbitable Three.js scene. A (coral) and B (blue) alternately attract one green test particle, retaining its velocity.

Model boundary: prescribed unit-mass toy dynamics with chosen force, softening, damping and simulation time; not a derived gravitational, electromagnetic or nuclear interaction. Numerical trajectories are approximate, not guaranteed periodic or exactly energy-conserving.

F(p) = wₖ(aₖ − p) / (|aₖ − p|² + 0.8²)³ᐟ² for the active pole k. Passing near its center switches attraction to the opposite pole while preserving momentum. Arrows show direction with compressed length 0.44 tanh(3|F|).
