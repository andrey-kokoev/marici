# The Labelled Resonant Source Supplies the Endpoint Nullhomotopy

Let `q_7` denote the retained labelled source with `s_b=1`, `i=7`, and let
`r_7` be its normalized odd exceptional target on `Bl_(u,a^2)`.  Entries
518--519 show

\[
\operatorname{res}_{-}(r_7)=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{res}_{+}(r_7)\ne0.
\]

The positive value is not merely known to vanish in a fitted quotient.  By
construction of the labelled total complex,

\[
\operatorname{res}_{+}(r_7)
=D_{-1}\bigl(\operatorname{res}_{+}(q_7)\bigr)
\]

in the endpoint complex.  Thus the required positive-endpoint
nullhomotopy is the restricted source label itself.  At the negative
endpoint the target restriction is already zero.

For the endpoint restriction map

\[
\rho:C_{E,\mathrm{wt}}
\longrightarrow C_{E,+}\oplus C_{E,-},
\]

the corresponding mapping-fiber representative has the canonical form

\[
\bigl(r_7;(\operatorname{res}_{+}q_7,0)\bigr).
\]

Its cone equation is

\[
\rho(r_7)-D(\operatorname{res}_{+}q_7,0)=0.
\]

No new carrier generator or post hoc homotopy is introduced.  This is
exactly why the source label had to survive Entries 509--515: forgetting it
retains the nonzero endpoint value while deleting its canonical
nullhomotopy.

This establishes existence and typing of the relative lift.  It does not
yet establish that its mapping-fiber class is nonzero or unique.  The next
decisive calculation is the relative boundary matrix: compute whether this
pair survives modulo global boundaries and whether the quadratic absolute
bulk maps injectively to the endpoint complex.
