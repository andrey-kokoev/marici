The Euler determinant chart and the global theta chart evaluate the same
labelled source and agree exactly in \(\Re s>1\).  Any second global analytic
section claiming the same source provenance must agree there and hence agree
everywhere by the identity theorem.

Therefore a hostile divisor-bearing multiplier cannot be a presentation
change of the same completed source: exact Euler normalization forces that
multiplier to equal one on an open domain.

This closes canonical-section rigidity but not RH.  It determines which
section belongs to the source without constraining where that section
vanishes.  The remaining problem is now purely a zero-confinement theorem for
the already rigid theta section.
