# 3235 — Copositivity Does Not Prevent Threshold Cancellation

preimage that cancels a positive portal threshold. At

\[
\lambda_h=\lambda_x=2,
\qquad
\Delta_p=1,
\qquad
p_{\mathrm{UV}}=-1,
\]

three, while (p_{\mathrm{low}}=p_{\mathrm{UV}}+\Delta_p=0).

domain but do not select nonzero portal support or the high-contrast coupling
corridor. A sign restriction on the UV boundary would be a new source
postulate, not a consequence of stability.
