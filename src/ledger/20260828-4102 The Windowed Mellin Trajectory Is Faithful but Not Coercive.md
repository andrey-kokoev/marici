# The Windowed Mellin Trajectory Is Faithful but Not Coercive

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

For an absolutely summable coefficient packet, the complete vertical Mellin
trajectory

\[
F_c(t)=\sum_n c_n n^{it}
\]

packages every Mellin jet. Against any integrable window positive almost
everywhere,

\[
\mathcal E_w(c)=\int w(t)|F_c(t)|^2dt
\]

is strictly positive for every nonzero finite-variation coefficient measure,
by uniqueness of the Fourier transform of measures.

For a Gaussian window its Gram kernel is
\(e^{-a(\log n-\log m)^2}\). Yet no lower \(\ell^2\) frame bound exists:
the unit vectors \((e_{n+1}-e_n)/\sqrt2\) have energy tending to zero.

Hence fixed-state completion faithfulness survives, while uniform coercivity
does not. The next gate is to derive the actual positive window and boundary
terms from the completed theta source rather than selecting a model window.
