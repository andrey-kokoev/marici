# Reciprocal Sewing Is Faithful on Germs but Does Not Confine Zeros

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

The Real reciprocal involution acts continuously and faithfully on the
complete Mellin germ by

\[
m_k(-\overline z)=(-1)^k\overline{m_k(z)}.
\]

A scalar zero imposes only \(m_0(z)=0\), so sewing transports an off-seam
zero and all its higher jets to its reciprocal mate without forcing either
point onto the seam. Fitting minors also show that no omitted Mellin grade is
locally redundant before rank saturation.

Thus completion repairs faithfulness but not confinement. The missing datum
must be a source-derived cross-germ conservation or positive-real transfer
law, not reciprocal symmetry alone.
