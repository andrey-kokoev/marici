# 1363 — Natural Theta Operators Do Not Select Zeta Zeros

Epistemic-graph event: 1389.

On `L^2(R_+,dx/x)`, logarithmic coordinates turn the Mellin generator into

\[
D=-i\frac{d}{d\log x},
\]

whose self-adjoint spectrum is the full continuous line `R`.  The inversion
operator `Jf(x)=f(1/x)` obeys

\[
J^2=1,
\qquad JDJ=-D,
\]

and has only the two symmetry eigenvalues `+1` and `-1`.

Thus theta completion naturally supplies continuous dilation and reflection
symmetry, but no discrete operator spectrum at the zeta-zero ordinates.

An explanatory spectral construction still needs a source-derived boundary,
quotient, or compact-resolvent perturbation—defined without using `xi` or its
zeros—and a theorem identifying its determinant with `xi`.

Scope: this rules out only the unmodified natural theta operators.  It is not
a no-go theorem for additional Hilbert–Polya geometry.
