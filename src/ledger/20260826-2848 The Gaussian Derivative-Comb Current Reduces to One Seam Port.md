# 2848 — The Gaussian Derivative-Comb Current Reduces to One Seam Port

The smallest jet lift that carries the oscillator annihilator yields no new
distributed current. Gaussian parity cancels the odd derivative-comb channel,
while the even number current satisfies

\[
2\pi e^{2u}\sum_{n\ne0}n^2f_u(n)
=
\frac12A(u)-A'(u).
\]

Its half-Mellin transform is

\[
\left(\frac12+iz\right)H(z)+A(0).
\]

Thus the bulk factors through the existing half-line amplitude and the only
new datum is one seam evaluation. The remaining gate is reciprocal-sheet
sewing of that seam port.
