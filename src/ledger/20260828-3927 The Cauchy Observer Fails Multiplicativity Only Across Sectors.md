# 3927 — The Cauchy Observer Fails Multiplicativity Only Across Sectors

For the scale-`1/2` Cauchy character

\[
\phi(u)=e^{-|u|/2},
\]

the multiplicativity defect is

\[
\delta(u,v)
=
e^{-|u+v|/2}-e^{-(|u|+|v|)/2}
\ge0.
\]

It vanishes on either one-sided Mellin cone and is strictly positive for
nonzero opposite-sign frequencies. Thus the observer is an exact character
on each Euler sector separately but not on their reciprocal sewing.

The completion anomaly must therefore live in mixed Fourier--Tate words, not
inside either isolated Euler determinant. The next theorem is to combine this
positive frequency kernel with source-derived mixed-word coefficients and
identify the resulting current with the BSY divisor entropy.

## Scope

positivity of the full matrix residual, identify it with BSY, force it to
vanish, or prove RH.
