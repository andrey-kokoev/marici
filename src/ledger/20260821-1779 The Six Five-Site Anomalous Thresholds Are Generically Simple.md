## Transverse Hessian

\[
n_i+n_j=\lambda n_e.
\]

\[
S=y_i+y_j,
\qquad p=y_i y_j,
\qquad q=y_e\lambda.
\]

On the plane orthogonal to \(n_e\), the Hessian of the Landau combination is
diagonal and has determinant

\[
\boxed{
\left(\frac Sp-\frac q{y_e^2}\right)
\left(\frac{q^2S}{4y_e^2p}-\frac q{y_e^2}\right).
}
\]

The two possible degeneracies become polynomial conditions in the reduced
variables:

\[
S^2y_e^2-pN=0,
\qquad
N-4p=0,
\qquad N=S q.
