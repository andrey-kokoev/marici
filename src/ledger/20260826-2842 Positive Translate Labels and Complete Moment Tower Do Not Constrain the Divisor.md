# 2842 — Positive Translate Labels and Complete Moment Tower Do Not Constrain the Divisor

The positive three-translate packet

\[
e^{-x^2}
+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}e^{-(x-q)^2}
+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}e^{-(x+q)^2}
\]

is uniformly strictly log-concave for (q<1/\sqrt2), and its finite
logarithmic moment tower is faithful by Vandermonde inversion. Nevertheless,
its transform is

\[
\sqrt\pi e^{-z^2/4}[1+\varepsilon\cos(qz)],
\]

with explicit off-axis zeros.

Thus positive labels, augmentation, complete algebraic moment observation,
and divisor-preserving translation transport do not orient the divisor. The
first remaining candidate is a pre-aggregation, infinite-label primal--dual
Poisson boundary current.
