# The Tangential Lower Wall Has Rank Eight, Not Five

Entries 551--554 show that the generic deletion increment does not itself
construct a residue object and that the Kummer resonant normal fiber has two
grades. The missing input is an independent construction of the tangential
coefficient object on \(q_{g1}=0\).

## Frozen tangential critical problem

\[
q_{g1}=c+b+X_1=0
\]

before taking critical equations. Tangent derivations are

\[
\partial_a,
\qquad
\partial_b-\partial_c.
\]

The restricted parallel denominator is constant,

\[
q_{g23}|_{q_{g1}}=X_2+X_3-X_1,
\]

so the frozen tangential master function contains only

\[
K^5q_{g2}^{19}q_{g3}^{23}.
\]

The exact saturated critical ideal is generated by \(q_{g1}\), the two
tangential logarithmic critical numerators, and

\[
zKq_{g2}q_{g3}-1.
\]

The provisional expectation was rank five. Exact execution instead gives

\[

\]

The result replicates over \(\mathbf F_{32003}\) and \(\mathbf F_{65521}\), at
both generic kinematic points and at the soft fiber \(X_1=0\). The reduced
tangential presentation has Gröbner-basis size \(34\) and the same eight
standard monomials in all six full-mark runs.
This rank is obtained directly on the wall and is not the subtraction
\(12-7\).

## Consequence

The Kummer resonant object of Entry 552 is therefore typed as

\[
\mathcal T_{g1}\otimes
[\mathbb Q\xrightarrow0\mathbb Q],
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}\mathcal T_{g1}=8.
\]

Thus its two normal grades are two rank-eight copies, not two rank-five
copies. Entries 553--554 still prohibit selecting one by integer contiguity
or Gysin variance alone.

This falsifies the proposed rank match with Entry 549. The resolved boundary
packet has associated-grade rank five, while the full tangential critical
object has rank eight. Consequently the \(4+1\) packet cannot be the complete
wall coefficient object. Any comparison must locate an independently derived
rank-five subquotient and account for the remaining three tangential classes;
rank matching supplies neither.

The equality of the generic and \(X_1=0\) ranks also shows that the first
physical contact at the soft face produces no tangential rank jump. It changes
the chain incidence, not the coefficient rank.
