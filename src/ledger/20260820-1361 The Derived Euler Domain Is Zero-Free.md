# 1361 — The Derived Euler Domain Is Zero-Free

Epistemic-graph event: 1387.

Evaluating the intrinsic formal Euler product by `[a] -> a^{-s}` is justified
for `Re(s)>1`.  Absolute convergence of

\[
\sum_p p^{-\operatorname{Re}(s)}
\]

then makes the logarithmic Euler product convergent and proves

\[
\boxed{\zeta(s)\ne0\qquad(\operatorname{Re}(s)>1).}
\]

Hence the complete analytic domain currently derived from the Carrier-side
formal product contains no zeta zeros.  Reaching the critical strip requires
a new continuation mechanism, archimedean factor, functional-equation
duality, and global determinant or cohomology.

Importing the classical continuation would answer where the zeros are for the
known zeta function, but would not derive their spectral home from the
Carrier.

Scope: this audits the strength of the conditional Euler realization; it is
not a new theorem about classical zero locations.
