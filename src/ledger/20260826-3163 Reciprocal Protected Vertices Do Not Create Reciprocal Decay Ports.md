# 3163 — Reciprocal Protected Vertices Do Not Create Reciprocal Decay Ports

## Result

The two directional protected-decay margins obey

\[
\Delta_{AB}+\Delta_{BA}=-2m_n<0.
\]

They cannot both be on shell in one positive-mass experiment. The one ordinary
unpolarized width has response rank one and is proportional, in the massless
limit, to \(y^2+z^2\).

Two exact vertex pairs share that width coordinate while having different
loop-erosion coordinates. Thus the ordinary threshold width does not identify
the protected backreaction.

## Consequence

Rank-two identification requires source-derived polarization or angular ports
in the one open decay, with calibrated response and covariance. A formal
opposite directional decay is not a physical second instrument.
