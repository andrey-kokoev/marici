# No Single Weighted Hilbert Space Carries Both Combs and the Gaussian Vacuum

Continuity of the direct logarithmic comb forces

\[
w(q)\gtrsim e^q
\]

at positive infinity. Continuity of the reciprocal comb forces

\[
w(q)\gtrsim e^{-q}
\]

at negative infinity.

But the logarithmic Gaussian vacuum satisfies

\[
|\psi_+(q)|^2\sim e^q
\]

at negative infinity. The forced reciprocal weight makes its norm density
asymptotically constant and therefore nonintegrable. The reflected vacuum
fails symmetrically at the other end.

Thus no single ordinary weighted Hilbert space can contain both source vacua
and support both comb traces. The arithmetic carrier must remain two-sector
and be sewn through a rigged or relative interface.
