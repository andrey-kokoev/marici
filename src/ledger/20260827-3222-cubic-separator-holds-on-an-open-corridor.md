# 3222 — The Cubic Separator Holds on a Continuum Corridor

stable and kinematically open throughout

\[
\frac35<\frac{p}{\lambda}<1.
\]

negative-portal cubic vanishes identically. The lower boundary closes phase
space; the upper boundary removes strict stability of the light radial mode.

This promotes the WP695 witness to a continuum ideal-rate theorem. It remains
an identifier rather than a selector, and its zero must still survive
asymmetric source completion, loops, widths, and detector calibration.
