On the plus Euler chart define

\[
R_+(s)=\Xi(s)\det_3(I-L_s),
\qquad
L_se_p=p^{-s}e_p.
\]

The determinant-three factor is a nowhere-zero unit, so \(R_+\) has exactly
the divisor of \(\Xi\).  In the Euler chamber,

\[
R_+(s)
=B_\infty(s)
\exp\left(
\operatorname{Tr}L_s+\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(L_s^2)
\right).
\]

All cyclic contributions of length at least three cancel identically.  The
normalized logarithmic connection contains only the archimedean, primitive,
and prime-square currents.

This does not authorize separate continuation of the low cumulants.  It
proves instead that the connected Euler tail is divisor-neutral and reduces
the remaining global conservation problem to a three-component typed
boundary packet.
