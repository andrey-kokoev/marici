Scope correction: the divergence of the local valuation volume

\[
V_X=\prod_{p\le X}(1-p^{-1})
\]

belongs to the \(k=2\) prime-square channel, not the \(k=1\) primitive
channel. The primitive amplitude is \(p^{-1/2}\); the determinant area is its
square.

Retaining the square countercurrent at every finite cutoff gives

\[
R_X
=
V_X\exp\left(\sum_{p\le X}p^{-1}\right).
\]

Its logarithm is

\[
-\sum_{p\le X}\sum_{m\ge2}\frac{1}{mp^m},
\]

which converges absolutely. Hence \(R_X\) has a finite strictly positive
limit.

orientation, \(k=2\) renormalizes determinant volume, and the higher even

relative finite-place volume is exactly the scalarizer produced by the full
doubled theta/Tate boundary system.
