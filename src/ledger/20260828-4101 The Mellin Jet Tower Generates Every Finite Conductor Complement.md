# The Mellin Jet Tower Generates Every Finite Conductor Complement

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

For conductor-fiber expectation \(E\), Mellin generator
\(\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\log n)\), and

\[
C_k=(1-E)\Lambda^kE,
\]

the images of the first \(m-1\) currents span the complete zero-mean
complement of every fiber of size \(m\). This follows from the invertibility
of the centered logarithmic Vandermonde system.

The whole tower is source-authorized: it consists of the jets at zero of the
single Mellin transport \(e^{z\Lambda}\). Thus finite Fourier duality
separates the old/new conductor blocks, while Mellin jets reconnect them
faithfully. No finite complementary direction remains invisible.

The unresolved problem has moved to completion and orientation: Vandermonde
conditioning may collapse, fixed-order jets may miss escaping states, and
faithfulness alone does not orient the scalar Wronskian.
