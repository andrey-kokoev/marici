# 3202 — The Portal Threshold Is a Positive Flavor Pairing

## Result

The full flavor factor in the exit-Higgs threshold is

\[
P=\operatorname{Tr}
\left[(Y_q^\dagger Y_q)(Y_X^\dagger Y_X)\right]
=\sum_{i,j}q_j^2|(Y_X)_{ij}|^2.
\]

It has no intergeneration sign cancellations. When every ordinary quark
Yukawa is nonzero, (P=0) exactly when (Y_X=0). For the canonical exit
shape, (P=q_1^2+q_2^2+q_3^2>0).

numerical selector of the total renormalized portal. A massless quark direction
is the exact boundary kernel, and the UV boundary coupling remains free.
