# 2856 — Adjointness of the Boundary Cocycle Characterizes the Critical Seam

The centered interval incidence adds genuine observation rank and is summable
at every prime depth. Reciprocal transport sends (z) to (-z), while Hilbert
adjunction sends (z) to (-\overline z). Equality on the full continuous
boundary cocycle holds exactly when

\[
\operatorname{Im}z=0,
\]

the critical seam in the centered Fourier coordinate.

A scalar zero makes the primitive and square currents summable but does not
force this adjoint relation. The remaining RH-strength gate is an extension
theorem from the discrete zero-induced prime packets to a star-compatible
continuous boundary cocycle.
