# 3904 — The Divisor Supply Is Universally Positive but Need Not Be Atomic

The source-visible divisor-supply measure has the exact decomposition

\[
d\mu_{
\mathrm{supply}}
=
d\nu_{\mathrm{seam}}
+
\sum_{\Re\rho>1/2}
m_\rho
\frac{2a_\rho}{\pi(a_\rho^2+(t-\gamma_\rho)^2)}\,dt.
\]

This measure is unconditionally positive. Its atomic part consists precisely
of critical-line folds; its diffuse part consists precisely of positive
Poisson profiles from right-sector zeros.

One off-seam zero produces a strictly positive diffuse signal on every seam
interval. As it approaches the line, that signal narrows toward an atom while
remaining positive at every finite displacement.

RH is exactly the vanishing of the diffuse component—not positivity of the
full measure, which is already universal.

## Scope

This proves the positive Lebesgue decomposition. It does not prove that the
diffuse component vanishes or prove RH.
