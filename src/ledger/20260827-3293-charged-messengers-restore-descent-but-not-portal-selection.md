# 3293 — Charged Messengers Restore Descent but Not Portal Selection

Heavy charged scalars can couple to neutral real-triplet norms and \(\chi^2\),
so their one-loop determinant descends to the original flavor quotient without
charging or complexifying \(n\) and \(m\). For

\[
m^2=M^2+aR+bX,
\]

the exact scalar mixed curvature is

\[
2ab\log\frac{M^2}{\mu^2}.
\]

This makes portal support unavoidable once the messenger vertices are
admitted. It does not select a physical value: the finite curvature vanishes
at \(\mu=M\), reverses sign across that scale, and an allowed renormalized
counterterm cancels any loop contrast. Representation multiplicity fixes only
an integer factor while the vertex products remain continuous.
