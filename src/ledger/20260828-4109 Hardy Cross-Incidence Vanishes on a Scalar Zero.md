# Hardy Cross-Incidence Vanishes on a Scalar Zero

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

The cross blocks of the positive/negative half-line order kernel are rank one:

\[
S_{+-}=-|1_+\rangle\langle1_-|,
\qquad S_{-+}=|1_-\rangle\langle1_+|.
\]

If \(a\) and \(b\) are the two half-line integrals, their cross current is
\(-2\operatorname{Im}(\overline a b)\). At a scalar zero, \(a+b=0\),
so this current vanishes identically. Hardy incidence is a real boundary
channel but supplies no zero-confinement law by itself.
