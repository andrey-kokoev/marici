# 2803 — Seam Confinement Cannot Be the Kernel of an Additive Quadratic Energy

The seam-confined polynomials

\[
F_+=(z-i)(z-2i),
\qquad
F_-=(z+i)(z+2i)
\]

have sum \(2(z^2-2)\), whose roots \(\pm\sqrt2\) are off the seam. Hence the
full seam-confined class is not linear, while the zero set of every positive
semidefinite quadratic form is a linear subspace. An additive energy can only
measure a linear source defect whose vanishing separately implies confinement,
or act after a source-derived nonlinear lift.
