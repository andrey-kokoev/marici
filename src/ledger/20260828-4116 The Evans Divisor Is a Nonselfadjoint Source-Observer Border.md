Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

The exact theta tail readout is

\[
X(s)=\delta_0A_s^{-1}f,
\]

and its bordered determinant preserves the divisor by the Schur formula.
But this border is not self-adjoint: \(f\) is an interior \(L^2\) source
while \(\delta_0\) is a distributional endpoint observer. Replacing the
observer by \(f^*\) yields a symmetric resolvent form but changes the
divisor. No bounded positive metric can map \(f\) to \(\delta_0\).

The remaining target is therefore a rigged conservative boundary system
that retains the two ports separately and couples them through a
source-derived Green form.
