## Exact antisymmetry

\[

\]

\[
\partial_E^n\eta_{ji}

\[
\boxed{

## Differential localization

Write one ordered residue as

\[
\eta(E)=f(E)S(E)^{-1/2},
\]

where \(S\) is its surface-sheet value. Put

\[
L=\frac{\eta'}{\eta}
=
\frac{f'}f-\frac12\frac{S'}S
\]

and define

\[
R_n=\frac{\partial_E^n\eta}{\eta}.
\]

Then

\[
R_0=1,
\qquad
R_{n+1}=\partial_E R_n+LR_n.
\]

Let \(D\) be the product of the irreducible factors of \(fS\). The
localized ring

\[
\mathbb Q[E,x,y,D^{-1}]
\]

is stable under \(\partial_E\), and \(L\) belongs to it. Induction
therefore gives

\[
R_n\in\mathbb Q[E,x,y,D^{-1}]
\qquad(n\ge0).
\]

No ordinary derivative can create a new irreducible pole divisor.
