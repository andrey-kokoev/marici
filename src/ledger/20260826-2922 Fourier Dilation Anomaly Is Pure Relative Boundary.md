For the centered dilation generator

\[
D=x\partial_x+\frac12,
\]

Fourier sewing satisfies \(\mathcal FD=-D\mathcal F\) exactly on Schwartz
space.  On a finite Mellin window,

\[
M_{a,b}(Df;s)
=
\left[x^sf(x)\right]_a^b
+\left(\frac12-s\right)M_{a,b}(f;s).
\]

Thus the entire generator-reversal anomaly is a two-endpoint current.  It has
no bulk component and vanishes under ordinary Schwartz completion in the
initial Mellin chamber.

This closes archimedean Fourier reversal as an independent source of RH
orientation.  Any surviving curvature must reside in retained arithmetic or
continuation boundary currents.  A nonzero bulk term in the finite-Euler
sewing audit is now a typing or domain error.
