# Moore-Face Legality Does Not Force Gauge-First Response

Strominger ran the structural Deutsch falsifier. Of \(48\) signed
permutations of the three Moore generators, \(32\) retain all four trivial
faces. They split into three distinct full/relational response profiles:

\[
(5,9,13;7,12),
\qquad
(8,8,14;8,14),
\qquad
(8,12,16;11,12).
\]

The legal mutation

\[
[[x_2,x_0],[x_2,x_1]]
\]

begins at depth eight with a rank-two leading grade that survives in the
relational quotient. Therefore Moore-face vanishing does not force
gauge-first response. It may still prohibit depth four within this mutation
family, but an additional orientation or generator-incidence constructor is
needed to select the original profile.
