With \(z=s-1/2\), the reciprocal theta tail is

\[
K(z)
=
4\int_0^\infty
\psi(e^{2u})e^{u/2}\cosh(zu)\,du,
\]

and the completed scalar is

\[
\xi\left(\frac12+z\right)
=
\frac12+
\frac12\left(z^2-\frac14\right)K(z).
\]

Thus scalar completion sees the antisymmetric boundary coordinate only
through reciprocal-even combinations and cannot distinguish \(z\) from
\(-z\).  Its canonical missing orientation port is

\[
K'(z)
=
4\int_0^\infty
u\psi(e^{2u})e^{u/2}\sinh(zu)\,du.
\]

The smallest relational lift is the even--odd pair \((K,K')\).  A scalar zero
constrains only the even channel, so zero confinement requires a source law
coupling the odd channel before scalar compression.
