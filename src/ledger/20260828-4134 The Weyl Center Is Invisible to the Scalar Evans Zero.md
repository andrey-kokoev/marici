# The Weyl Center Is Invisible to the Scalar Evans Zero

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

For the correctly typed Weyl tower

\[
[X,L]=-2P,
\qquad
[X,P]=-I,
\]

evaluation at a zero gives

\[
\varepsilon_{z_0}([X,L]F)=-2F'(z_0),
\qquad
\varepsilon_{z_0}([X,P]F)=-F(z_0)=0.
\]

The odd channel detects simple versus multiple incidence, but the central
channel is invisible to the scalar Evans observer precisely on the zero
locus. Another source commutator will not fix this.

The missing constructor is an independently derived dual observer that pairs
the Weyl center nontrivially. This is the theta analogue of separating a
source orbit from its observer pair.
