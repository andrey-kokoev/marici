Control theory supplies state, observation, intervention, dynamics,
objectives, policies, and feedback. The detector reconstruction indicates
that Marici couples this application template to endogenous authority and
evidence state:

\[
(\text{physical},\text{belief},\text{authority},\text{evidence}).
\]

The predicates feasible, informative, optimal, authorized, executed, and
recorded must remain distinct. In particular, optimality does not grant
authority, authority does not establish capability, and execution does not
establish a durable record.

RBAC is one possible actor--role--permission realization inside the authority
plane, not the whole plane. It does not by itself capture authority basis,
delegation history, conditional obligation, target and locus scope,
embodiment capability, persistent prohibition, or evidence dependencies.

A one-shot grant supplies the smallest endogenous witness: executing an
authorized action consumes the grant, so two histories with identical plant
and policy states have different future action sets. The finite checker passes
12 of 12 tests. Architectural promotion remains blocked until a non-optical
sector supplies an independent history-sensitive authority witness.
