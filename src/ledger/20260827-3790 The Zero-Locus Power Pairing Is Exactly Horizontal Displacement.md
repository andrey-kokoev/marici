# 3790 — The Zero-Locus Power Pairing Is Exactly Horizontal Displacement

On the completed scalar-null locus, the value-current Ward cell gives

\[
C+k(0)=-zG.
\]

Therefore

\[
\operatorname{Re}
\left((C+k(0))\overline G\right)
=-\operatorname{Re}(z)|G|^2.
\]

The theta half-line pair is nonnull, so \(G\ne0\) at a scalar zero. Requiring
the real current-amplitude power to vanish is consequently equivalent to
\(\operatorname{Re}z=0\). It is RH restated, not a conservation law derived
from incidence.

This forces three distinct towers: value, endpoint current, and an independent
sector-control tower. The Ward cell relates the first two but cannot authorize
its own flux cancellation. A valid Fourier–Tate Green law must be constructed
before assuming a zero and must make the total real boundary flux vanish for
admissible scalar-null states.
