# 3393 — The Domain Wall Selects Localization but Leaves the Portal Magnitude Continuous

## Claim

For opposite kink-mass signs on an interval, the normalized zero-mode boundary
densities satisfy

\[
\frac{D_+(x)}{D_-(x)}=e^{2x},
\qquad
D_+(x)-D_-(x)=2x,
\qquad x=ML>0.
\]

The domain-wall Dirac operator therefore detects anomaly-neutral matter
placement and conditionally selects a localization side. Its topological index
is nevertheless constant throughout \(x>0\). The hostile pair \(x=1/2\) and
\(x=3/2\) has the same chirality but contrasts \(1\) and \(3\).

## Classification

The kink is a conditional localization selector and a boundary-presentation
rigidifier, but not a numerical portal selector. Wall orientation and labelled
kink-charge assignments control the sign; the Yukawa coupling, wall scale,
and interval length leave a continuous magnitude fiber. RG transport,
finite-width and Kaluza–Klein thresholds, `physical16` descent, and a calibrated
instrument remain open.
