# 3058 — Reciprocal-Even Dilation Has One Spectral Channel

On the even carrier, the unitary map

\[
(Uf)(q)=\sqrt2\,e^{q/2}f(e^q)
\]

turns dilation into translation:

\[
U R_u U^{-1}h(q)=h(q+u).
\]

The self-adjoint dilation observable is therefore the ordinary momentum on
one logarithmic line and has spectral multiplicity one.

Consequently, on the unitary seam a cross-spectral density is a single product
of scalar fiber amplitudes. Its zero is not destructive interference between
two hidden spectral planes. The two reciprocal grades exist before even
projection; the open half-planes arise from nonunitary Mellin continuation
away from the common seam.

The remaining Hilbert–Pólya bridge is now explicit: prove that the divisor of
the distributional arithmetic matrix coefficient is supported on the unitary
dilation spectrum. Self-adjointness of the carrier alone does not imply this.

## Scope

The logarithmic unitary model and multiplicity-one statement are exact. The
distributional support theorem, zero confinement, and RH remain open.
