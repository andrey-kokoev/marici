# Two-Parameter Total-Energy--Soft Resolution Retains the Legendre Variation

## Question

Entry 368 closed only the ordered specialization (E=0) followed by the
individual soft limit (y=t=0), with (x\ne0).  The hard-to-vary claim is

\[
\boxed{
\text{the resolved }(E,t)\text{ coefficient skeleton is generated by the
frozen conductor, Legendre, Cut, and Rees data.}}
\]

It is falsified if resolution produces a new base-support factor, torsion
prime, or monodromy not generated by those coefficient systems.  Raw
iterated restrictions are not assumed to commute.

## Frozen equations

Put (y=t) and keep (x) invertible.  The two conductor discriminants are

\[
\Delta _1=4x\left[xt^2+2Ext-E^2(x+2t-E)\right],
\]

\[
\Delta _2=4t\left[x^2t+2Ext-E^2(2x+t-E)\right].
\]

The signed-energy elliptic coefficients are

\[
A=(2x-E)(E-2t),\qquad B=E(2x+2t-E),
\]

and (m=A/B).  No normalization is changed before these equations are
pulled back.

## First blowup

Blow up the origin in the ((E,t))-plane.  On the chart

\[
E=u,\qquad t=uv,
\]

one obtains

\[
\Delta _1
=4xu^2\left[x(v^2+2v-1)+u(1-2v)\right],
\]

\[
\Delta _2
=4u^2v\left[x^2v+2xu(v-1)+u^2(1-v)\right].
\]

Both conductor Kummer lines therefore have exceptional multiplicity two.
Their exceptional residue is (-1), so their semisimple monodromy is
trivial.  The strict transform of (Delta _1) meets the exceptional line at

\[
v=-1\pm\sqrt2.
\]

These are directions of the already frozen conductor collision divisor,
not new divisors in the original energy base.

The first blowup is not yet SNC for (Delta _2): its two strict components
and the exceptional divisor meet at (u=v=0).  Put (v=uw).  The new
exceptional multiplicity is four, and the two strict transforms meet it at

\[
w=0,\qquad w=2/x.
\]

Its Kummer residue is (-2), again with trivial semisimple monodromy.  Thus
the conductor resolution introduces no character beyond the existing two
half-Kummer lines and their integral half-sum lattice.

## Elliptic exceptional family

The same first blowup gives

\[
A=u(2x-u)(1-2v),
\qquad
B=u(2x+2uv-u).
\]

After separating the common scale, the exceptional modulus is

\[
\boxed{m|_{u=0}=1-2v.}
\]

Consequently the exceptional divisor does not carry a generically Tate
object.  It carries the original rank-two Legendre variation, with its three
cusps at

\[
v=0\leftrightarrow m=1,
\qquad
v=\tfrac12\leftrightarrow m=0,
\qquad
v=\infty\leftrightarrow m=\infty.
\]

The ordered limits (t=0) then (E=0), and (E=0) then (t=0), select
different cusps.  In a standard (Gamma(2)) homology basis take

\[
T_0=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
T_1=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-2&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
T_\infty=(T_0T_1)^{-1}.
\]

Then

\[
T_1T_\infty\ne T_\infty T_1.
\]

Thus the two selected cusp-monodromy operators do not commute.  This is
exactly the braid of the frozen Legendre coefficient system.  It is not a
computed commutator of the full nearby-cycle functors, an algebraic
exceptional class, or evidence for a new carrier incidence.  The
coordinate-axis iterated Tate limits are boundary fibers of a smooth
elliptic variation over generic exceptional directions.

## Cut and marked cospan

The soft Cut leg retains its frozen Smith type

\[
(1,2,2t).
\]

After pullback, its only nonunit normal is the transform of (t); no mixed
prime beyond (2) and the two blowup coordinates occurs.  The regularized
logarithmic leg of Entry 368 keeps its unit (x^2) minor on the (E=0)
boundary chart, and the common ordinary line remains (langle e_6\rangle).
Entry 352 still gives zero marked principal-part image in the elliptic
quotient.  This records compatibility of the frozen cospan on its audited
boundary charts; it is not a derivation of a new rank-twelve connection over
the whole blown-up plane.

## Verdict

The strong expectation that the joint nearby object is generically an
iterated Tate/Kummer object is falsified.  The smaller surviving statement is

\[
\boxed{
\text{joint nearby}
=\text{Legendre variation on the exceptional direction}
+\text{resolved half-Kummer conductor data}
+\text{the existing Cut/Rees cospan}.}
\]

All exceptional conductor multiplicities are even.  The noncommuting ordered
limits are generated by (T_1,T_\infty) of the existing Legendre system.
No new base-support factor, torsion prime, or carrier datum appears.

## H2 versus H3

This supports the resolved form of H2 at the coefficient-skeleton level.
The two cusp-monodromy operators do not commute, but their mismatch is
explained without a sector-specific operation: resolving the shared base
exposes the already known Legendre variation.  This does not establish
commutation or noncommutation of the full nearby-cycle functors.  It shows
that equality of their raw coordinate presentations is not the appropriate
criterion.

## Scope and next falsifier

The calculation is local at (x\ne0), resolves the conductor support, and
identifies the pure elliptic exceptional family.  It does not compute a full
rank-twelve logarithmic connection on the blowup, nor the simultaneous
double-soft point (x=t=0).

The next finite falsifier is the final triangle soft corner (x=y=0), where
the unit used here disappears.  Resolve ((E,x,y)) with the frozen signed
energy arrangement and test whether the Legendre exceptional map, conductor
half-sums, and Cut/Rees lattice still suffice without a new carrier stratum.
