\[
g_z(u)=i e^{izu}\mathbf1_{u\ge0}
\]

\[
\widehat g_z(\gamma)=\frac1{\gamma-z}.
\]

extended explicit-formula pairing satisfies

\[
\langle g_z,g_w\rangle_W

\frac{M_\Xi(z)-\overline{M_\Xi(w)}}

Mellin half-line test quotient into the Xi Pick boundary preshape. The Cauchy
vectors are source-derived boundary modes, not functions manufactured from a
