# Residual Norm Homology Is the Augmentation Ideal Modulo Its Socle

Epistemic-graph event: 1362.

For a collapsed `k`-bit kernel algebra `A_B` with augmentation ideal `I_B`
and top socle line `F_2 epsilon_B`, the mod-two norm homology is

`(I_B/F_2 epsilon_B) tensor A_(B^c)`.

For `k>=2` its collapsed-kernel Loewy length is `k-1`, with successive layer
ranks

`C(k,1), C(k,2), ..., C(k,k-1)`

times the untouched deck module.  The one-bit module vanishes; the full
five-bit layers have dimensions `5,10,10,5`.

Thus the residual bad-prime object is the proper Boolean augmentation module,
not merely a numerical rank defect.  This remains formal deck algebra and
does not authorize physical branch intersections or relative-chain maps.
