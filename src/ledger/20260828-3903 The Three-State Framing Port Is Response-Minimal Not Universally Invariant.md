presentations has sixteen two-element orbits. The three incidence-and-polarity
classes contain respectively eight, four, and four of those orbits.

source-invariant observations. The repeated-generator sign is an explicit
endpoint-reversal invariant that the port forgets. Nevertheless the computed
reflection response is constant on each of the three fibers and takes three
distinct values, so the port is the minimal sufficient quotient for that
specific response.

This separates source construction from observational selection: incidence
constructs the port, while the response law proves its sufficiency and
minimality.
