# 3714 — No Adams-Natural Gaussian Pivot Pairs the Two Boundary Covectors

Gaussian smoothing in logarithmic label,

\[
K_\tau=\exp(-\tau L^2),
\qquad Le_n=(\log n)e_n,
\]

does provide a continuous map from the arithmetic distributional dual into
the rapid-decay test space for every \(\tau>0\). It therefore repairs the
dual--dual Green contraction at a chosen scale.

It is not natural under the positive-Fock Adams operations. If \(\psi_k\)
sends label \(n\) to \(n^k\), then

\[
K_\tau\psi_k=\psi_kK_{k^2\tau}.
\]

Commutation with any nontrivial \(\psi_k\) forces \(\tau=0\), where the map
ceases to smooth and the variance defect returns.

Thus the heat family organizes possible pivots but selects none. A fixed
positive heat time imports an external clock or regularity scale. The
surviving theta constructor must retain or integrate the whole scale orbit,
provide an independent state-valued control port, or avoid direct
dual--dual contraction.
