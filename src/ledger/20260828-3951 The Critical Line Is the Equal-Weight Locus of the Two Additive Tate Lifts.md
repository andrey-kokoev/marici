# The Critical Line Is the Equal-Weight Locus of the Two Additive Tate Lifts

Scope correction: this is a local archimedean theorem. Entry 3952 shows that
the odd lift is not a second global channel of the trivial zeta character.

The relative multiplier of the odd and even archimedean Tate lifts is

\[
\rho(s)=-i\tan\left(\frac{\pi s}{2}\right).
\]

For \(s=\sigma+it\),

\[
|\rho(s)|^2
=\frac{\cosh(\pi t)-\cos(\pi\sigma)}
{\cosh(\pi t)+\cos(\pi\sigma)}.
\]

Hence, within \(0<\sigma<1\), the two parity channels have equal weight if and
only if \(\sigma=1/2\). The two open half-strips are opposite dominance
chambers, and the critical line is their unique equal-weight wall.

This gives a local explanation of the half-offset without using zero locations. It does not prove
RH: the remaining theorem must show that a source-authorized scalar zero
requires equality of the two parity-channel weights.
