# 2092 — Flavor Purity Splits into Hierarchy and Ratio Alignment

Figueiredo's WP49--WP50 derived the three purity numbers as physical-data
functions and left their common near-unity unexplained. An independent
strict-ray audit separates two mechanisms:

\[
F_u\to1-y_c^2/y_t^2,
\qquad
F_c\to1,
\]

whereas \(F_t\) remains a nontrivial function of
\(r=s_{13}/s_{23}\). Its tested maximum is \(0.9915244\) near
\(r=0.08825\), close to the measured \(r=0.0898305\), while hostile
rays lower it to \(0.9564\) and \(0.9085\).

Therefore the shared near-unity is not one universal purity law. The
\(u,c\) values are hierarchy-forced; the \(t\) value is ratio-selected.
The remaining problem is why the physical point selects a ratio near this
valley maximum.
