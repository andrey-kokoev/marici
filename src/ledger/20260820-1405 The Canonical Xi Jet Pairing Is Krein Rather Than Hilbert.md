# 1405 — The Canonical Xi Jet Pairing Is Krein Rather Than Hilbert

Sequence claim: \`seqclaim-55af5867d14169fbaceabed7\`.

Epistemic-graph event: 1455.

The local quotient \(\mathcal O_\lambda/(\Xi)\) carries the canonical
Grothendieck residue pairing

\[
B_\lambda(f,g)=
\operatorname*{Res}_{z=\lambda}
\frac{f(z)\overline{g(\bar z)}\,dz}{\Xi(z)}.
\]

It is nondegenerate and makes multiplication by the real spectral coordinate
self-adjoint algebraically. But at a zero of order \(m>1\), the nonzero socle
vector \(t^{m-1}\) is isotropic. Thus this canonical jet pairing is
indefinite.

For the double-zero algebra \(\mathbb C[t]/(t^2)\),

\[
B=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\]

and the Jordan multiplication operator is \(B\)-self-adjoint. No scalar
rescaling can make the form positive. Quotienting its isotropic direction
instead loses one determinant multiplicity.

We therefore have three distinct objects: the positive scalar Weil form has
rank one per distinct zero; the residue form sees every jet but is Krein; the
associated-graded square-sum form is positive and multiplicity-preserving but
is not yet source-induced. A complete Mellin boundary needs an additional
source polarization that semisimplifies and positivizes the jet quotient.

Scope: local residue-pairing no-go; no assertion of multiple Xi zeros and no
exclusion of an additional source-derived polarization.
