# 1387 — The Riemann Theta Kernel Is Not Pólya-Frequency

Epistemic-graph event: 1427.

Full total positivity of the theta translation kernel cannot supply the
missing Weyl measure.

Schoenberg's theorem says that a Pólya-frequency function \(f\) of infinite
order has bilateral Laplace transform

\[
\mathcal Lf(s)=\frac1{\Psi(s)}
\]

on a strip, with \(\Psi\) entire Laguerre–Pólya. For the
super-exponentially decreasing Riemann theta kernel,

\[
\mathcal L\Phi(s)=\Xi(is)
\]

is itself entire. If \(\Phi\) were Pólya-frequency, the identity theorem would
give

\[
\Xi(is)\Psi(s)=1
\]

globally, forcing \(\Xi(is)\) to be zero-free. This contradicts the known
nontrivial zeros of completed \(\xi\).

Thus the theta kernel is unconditionally not Pólya-frequency of infinite
order. The required positivity must arise only after the nonlinear operation

\[
R_\Xi(x)=\frac d{dx}\log\Xi(i\sqrt{x}),
\]

not from total positivity of the original convolution kernel.

Scope: full Pólya-frequency total positivity is falsified; weaker finite-order
or derived Pick/Stieltjes positivity remains open.
