# 3667 — Fourier Reciprocity Is an Inner Weyl Reflection and the Mixed Seam Is Four-Dimensional

## Result

Theta Fourier reciprocity acts inside one metaplectic triple:

\[
(E,F,H)\longmapsto(-F,-E,-H).
\]

It is therefore distinct from exchanging two commuting oscillator factors.

If the completed plus and minus sectors supply independent canonical pairs,
their two local `sl2` triples contribute six generators and the four mixed
quadratics complete them to the ten-dimensional algebra \(\mathfrak{sp}_4\).
The exact mixed complement has dimension four.

## Consequence

The missing seam wall has a concrete candidate: the four mixed quadratic
controls coupling the two local sector algebras. A factor swap alone does not
generate them. This also keeps distinct the native six generators and the
six interchange-square tests.

## Scope

The `sp4` closure is algebraic. Its theta interpretation remains conditional
on whether the completed reciprocal sectors are independent canonical pairs
or merely two polarizations of one pair.
