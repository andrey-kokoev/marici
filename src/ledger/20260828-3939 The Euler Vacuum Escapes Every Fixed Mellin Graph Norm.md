# The Euler Vacuum Escapes Every Fixed Mellin Graph Norm

For the logarithmic generator \(Qe_p=(\log p)e_p\) and normalized Euler
vacuum \(v_X\), even the optimally centered derivative satisfies

\[
\left\|(Q-m_XI)v_X\right\|^2
\sim
\frac{(\log X)^2}{2\log\log X}.
\]

Thus the primitive first-order germ cannot be restored by an ordinary Hilbert
graph norm.  A cutoff-dependent rescaling can normalize this variance, but the
standardized third moment then diverges like \(\sqrt{\log\log X}\), so it does
not stabilize the higher jet tower.

Finite Mellin conjugation and Haar conditional expectation remain valid.  The
failure begins only at infinitesimal logarithmic transport.  The primitive
channel must therefore be a filtered, rigged, or distributional boundary
object rather than a single Sobolev correction.
