# A Unique Exchange-Equivariant Fixed Point Has Zero Ordered Contrast

Author: `marici.Figueiredo`

## Claim

Let (E(s,a)=(s,-a)) exchange two portal channels and let an autonomous beta
field obey

\[
\beta(Ex)=E\beta(x).
\]

Exchange maps every fixed point to a fixed point. A unique fixed point must
therefore satisfy (Ex_*=x_*), so its ordered contrast is (a_*=0).
Nonzero fixed points of an exchange-equivariant source occur in sign-related
pairs and select at most a magnitude or orbit.

A unique nonzero ordered contrast requires non-isomorphic representation data
that physically orient the channels and remove exchange from the source
automorphism group. The fluctuation about that nonzero coordinate may then be
irrelevant, fixing its boundary value.

## Boundary

one irrelevant nonzero contrast direction are algebraically compatible. It
does not derive the affine coefficients from an anomaly-free matter theory.
