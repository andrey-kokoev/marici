# 3924 — Euler's Constant Sews the Three Prime-Current Grades

The Mertens normalization splits exactly as

\[
\gamma
=
B_1
+
\frac12\sum_p p^{-2}
+
\sum_p\sum_{k\ge3}\frac1{k p^k}.
\]

These are precisely the primitive `k=1`, prime-square `k=2`, and trace-class
connected `k>=3` channels. Euler's constant is their scalar sewing constant,
not an unrelated counterterm.

Consequently a source-derived third-order relative determinant must reproduce
`B_1` through its first trace, the prime-square constant through its second
trace, and the connected tail through its regular determinant term. Deleting
any grade leaves an explicit normalization deficit.
