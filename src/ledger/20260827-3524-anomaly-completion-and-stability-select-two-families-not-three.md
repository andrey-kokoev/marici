# 3524 — Anomaly Completion and Stability Select Two Families, Not Three

## Claim

In anomaly-free \(E_7\times E_6\times U(1)_R\) gauged supergravity with an
integral monopole \(n\) embedded in
\(E_6\supset SO(10)\times U(1)\), the chiral gaugino index is

\[
N_{\mathrm{fam}}=2|n|.
\]

The complete charged gauge-fluctuation spectrum imposes

\[
|N^I|\leq1.
\]

The stable nonzero one-parameter sectors are therefore \(n=\pm1\), each with
exactly two families. No integral \(n\) yields three; \(n=2\) yields four and
fails stability. The family-producing \(E_6\) monopole is also distinct from
the \(U(1)_R\) BPS embedding that selected orientation in WP791.

## Classification

Anomaly completion plus fluctuation stability is a genuine source selector,
but it selects a stable two-family branch. It refutes this canonical
compactification as the single source of the observed three-family asymmetric
portal. The scale modulus and physical-instrument gate also remain.
