# 2828 — Theta Terminal Band Current Is Globally Negative

Strict log-concavity gives an even negative separation score

\[
\delta_R(y)
=
\frac12
\left[(\log g)'(y+R/2)-(\log g)'(y-R/2)\right]<0.
\]

Reflection pairing under the positive tilt then proves

\[
J_a'(R)<0,
\qquad
J_a'(R)-aR W_a(R)<0
\]

for every \(a,R>0\). Hence every bounded-width consecutive-band block fails
at high frequency. Only a terminal width tending to infinity, or a source
identity before band projection, remains viable.
