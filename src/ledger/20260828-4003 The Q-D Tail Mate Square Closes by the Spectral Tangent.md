# The Q-D Tail Mate Square Closes by the Spectral Tangent

For the source tail resolvent

\[
G_s(q)=e^{-sq}\int_q^\infty f(v)e^{sv}\,dv,
\]

the native phase-space operators satisfy the exact mate laws

\[
R_sD=D_qR_s,
\qquad
R_sQ=QR_s+\partial_sR_s.
\]

Thus the derivative wall commutes strictly, while the position wall closes by
the canonical spectral-tangent homotopy.

At the endpoint, with \(F(s)=G_s(0)\),

\[
E_0R_s(Qf)=F'(s),
\qquad
E_0R_s(Df)=-f(0)-sF(s).
\]

On a scalar zero-state the graph packet is

\[
\bigl(F'(s_0),-f(0)\bigr).
\]

This canonically lifts scalar cancellation into a nonvanishing phase-space
boundary relation and observes transversality. It does not yet confine zeros;
the remaining theorem must pair reciprocal copies of this packet through a
theta-specific conserved form.
