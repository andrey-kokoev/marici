# Entry 1226 — Residual Five-Site Norm Discriminants Are Projection Collisions

## Frozen marked cover

Use the 26 physical marked sections on the rank-32 five-site Kummer cover. Their cut-depth profile is

\[
1+5+20:
\]

one section with no root, five sections with one root, and twenty connected-subgraph sections with two boundary roots.

The deck-orbit norm forgets the labelled sheet while retaining the marked divisor. This operation is not a new carrier construction.

## One-cut orbit

For a one-cut wall $X+2y=0$, with $y^2=R$, the orbit norm is

\[
N_1(X)=X^2-4R.
\]

Its discriminant is

\[
\operatorname{Disc}_X(N_1)=16R.
\]

Thus its only collision is the existing Kummer branch $R=0$.

## Two-cut orbit

For a two-cut wall $X+s_iy_i+s_jy_j=0$, the four-sheet norm is

\[
N_2(X)
=
X^4-2(R_i+R_j)X^2+(R_i-R_j)^2.
\]

Exact multiplication over the four sign choices and an independent root-difference calculation give

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Disc}_X(N_2)
=
4096R_i^2R_j^2(R_i-R_j)^2.
}
\]

On the physical cover

\[
R_i=\frac{F_i}{\det H},
\]

so away from $\det H=0$ the residual collision is

\[
F_i-F_j=0.
\]

The twenty connected-subgraph labels realize all ten unordered edge pairs exactly twice.

## Type of the residual divisor

The loci $F_i=0$ and $F_j=0$ are existing edge-soft/Kummer branches. At

\[
F_i-F_j=0
\]

the Kummer cover is generically smooth. What collides are two labelled signed marked walls after projection to the base. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
F_i-F_j=0
\text{ is marked relative-incidence/coefficient support, not a primitive carrier wall.}
}
\]

Keeping occurrence and sheet labels resolves the collision. Promoting the discriminant of the orbit norm to a new carrier generator would confuse an orbit-forgetting projection with source incidence.
