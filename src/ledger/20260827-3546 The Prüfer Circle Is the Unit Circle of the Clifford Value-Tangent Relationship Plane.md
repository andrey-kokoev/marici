# The Prüfer Circle Is the Unit Circle of the Clifford Value--Tangent Relationship Plane

The relevant geometric-algebra vector is

\[
V=He_1+H_xe_2.
\]

The scalar part of \(V^{-1}dV\) is logarithmic magnitude change, while its
bivector part is the Prüfer connection. Spatial and Newman-heat transport give
exactly the logarithmic-curvature and derivative-Wronskian numerators.

Normalizing \(V\) produces the unit circle of source relationship directions.
An apparent ellipse is this circle under unequal value/tangent channel gains.
A simple Xi zero has \(H=0,H_x\neq0\): only the scalar projection vanishes,
and the tangent direction remains meaningful. A double zero has \(V=0\), so
normalization and angular meaning genuinely fail. The operator's ninety-degree
rotation is literally \(H\mapsto H+iH_x\).
