# 3116 — The Full Moment Boundary Is Still Not Faithful

Choose a nonzero smooth Fourier-side bump supported inside ([1,3]), and let
φ be its inverse Fourier transform. Then φ is a nonzero Schwartz function,
but every derivative of its Fourier transform at zero vanishes. Consequently

\[
\int_{\mathbb R}x^k\phi(x)\,dx=0
\qquad
k\ge0.
\]

Thus even the complete moment tower is unfaithful. It is only the Taylor germ
of the Fourier transform at one point, and smooth functions are not
determined by that germ.

The faithful archimedean observer must retain the full two-sign
function-valued Fourier trace unless the theta constructor module is
independently proved quasianalytic. The corrected observer hierarchy is full
trace, then moment germ, then scalar determinant line. Each compression needs
its own faithfulness theorem.

## Scope

The all-moments-zero hostile is exact on the ambient Schwartz source. A
smaller source-derived quasianalytic module remains possible but unproved.
