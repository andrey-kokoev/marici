charge \(+1\). Its mixed anomaly coefficient is \(3\). The smallest
nontrivial vectorlike representation pair \(6+\overline6\), with charges
\((-3,-3)\), cancels that coefficient.

\[
\sum_i s_i=9,
\qquad
\sum_i s_i^3=297.
\]

Within the declared box \(|s_i|\le12\), there is no solution with fewer than
four singlets; the first solution is

\[
(-7,4,4,8).
\]

\[
81+108+69=258,
\]

\[
\kappa=2+36-258=-220.
\]

linear charge is zero.

## Classification

its orientation or the required tadpole, and it destroys the favorable
spectral basin. The remaining qualitatively different branch is a
