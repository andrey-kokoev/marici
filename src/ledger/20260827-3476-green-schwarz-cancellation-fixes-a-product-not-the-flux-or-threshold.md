# 3476 — Green--Schwarz Cancellation Fixes a Product, Not the Flux or Threshold

## Claim

In the minimal Green--Schwarz effective theory, an anomaly coefficient \(A\),
axion shift coefficient \(k\), and Wess--Zumino coefficient \(c\) obey

\[
A=kc.
\]

The transformation

\[
(k,c)\longmapsto(\rho k,c/\rho)
\]

preserves cancellation. The Stückelberg mass and low-energy exchange instead
transform as

\[
M_F^2\longmapsto\rho^2M_F^2,
\qquad
\frac{g_F^2}{M_F^2}\longmapsto
\rho^{-2}\frac{g_F^2}{M_F^2}.
\]

Thus anomaly cancellation does not fix the physical threshold or portal
magnitude. Charge, axion-shift, and flux reversal is also an equally valid
solution preserving both the mediator mass and chiral index. The integer flux
does not occur in the local cancellation equation.

## Classification

The minimal Green--Schwarz constructor is an anomaly rigidifier, not an
oriented-flux selector or physical-magnitude selector. A genuine source must
be a concrete compactification intersection lattice whose primitive
characteristic vector fixes \(k,c,f_a\), and \(m=3\) simultaneously and whose
massive mediator has calibrated production and decay ports.
