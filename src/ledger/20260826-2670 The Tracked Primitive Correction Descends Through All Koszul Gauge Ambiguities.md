# 2670 — The Tracked Primitive Correction Descends Through All Koszul Gauge Ambiguities

## Frozen ambiguity

Let a tracked reduction primitive be a tuple \(h=(h_1,\ldots,h_4)\) satisfying

\[
f-r=\sum_i h_i g_i.
\]

Two primitives differ by a first syzygy of the four exact generators. Because the generator ideal is a complete intersection, its first syzygy module is generated by the six labelled Koszul relations

\[
g_j e_i-g_i e_j,
\qquad i<j.
\]

The derivative correction in base direction \(\mu\) changes under such a generator by

\[
g_j\,\partial_\mu g_i-g_i\,\partial_\mu g_j.
\]

## Exact audit

All six generator pairs were tested in each of the three labelled momentum-square directions. Across A, B, and HOMA and both replicated primes, all 18 changes reduce to zero in \(R=P/I\).

Thus the tracked primitive correction is independent in the quotient under every first-syzygy ambiguity generated by the regular sequence.
