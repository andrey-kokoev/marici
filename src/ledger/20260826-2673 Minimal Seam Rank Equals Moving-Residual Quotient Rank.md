# 2673 — Minimal Seam Rank Equals Moving-Residual Quotient Rank

For (K=\ker Q), uniform repair first requires
(K\cap\ker H\subseteq\ker R). Hence (R|_K) factors through (H|_K) and
the seam rank is at least (\operatorname{rank}(R|_K)). One scalar row cannot
repair two independent moving residual directions.
