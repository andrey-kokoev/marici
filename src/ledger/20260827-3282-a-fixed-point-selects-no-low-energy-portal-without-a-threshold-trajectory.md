# 3282 — A Fixed Point Selects No Low-Energy Portal without a Threshold Trajectory

For an isolated fixed portal \(p_*\), affine threshold matching gives

\[
p_{\mathrm{low}}=p_*+A\rho.
\]

The contrast covector \(d=(1,-1)\) survives arbitrary relevant threshold
motion exactly when \(dA=0\), unless the source uniquely fixes every visible
component of \(\rho\). Thus an interacting fixed point selects only the
pre-threshold coordinate unless it also fixes its relevant trajectory.

After matching, an unlabelled total detector channel annihilates the contrast
direction. Two independently calibrated representation-labelled channels are
the minimal linear readout that preserves it. The required source explanation
must therefore compose fixed point, relevant deformation, threshold map, and
instrument.
