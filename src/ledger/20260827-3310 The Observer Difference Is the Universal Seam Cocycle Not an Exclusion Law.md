# 3310 — The Observer Difference Is the Universal Seam Cocycle, Not an Exclusion Law

## Result

For seam window `B_L` and character weight `q`, the fixed observer and the
contragrediently transported observer differ by

\[
E_{\mathrm{fix}}-E_{\mathrm{mov}}=-qB_L.
\]

This is exactly the moving endpoint current. At a scalar zero it may remain
nonzero, so it detects source information lost by the Evans readout.

However, it is a universal transport identity. A positive two-atom hostile
with contributions `+1` before the seam and `-1` after it has zero scalar
readout, nonzero seam current, and satisfies the observer-difference identity
exactly. Detection is therefore not exclusion.

The next gate is a global constraint on the composed family of seam cocycles,
not another individual seam formula.

## Scope

This does not prove that no global seam-family law exists and does not prove
RH. It shows that one source-derived moving seam, even though genuinely
information-bearing, is not an admissibility condition.
