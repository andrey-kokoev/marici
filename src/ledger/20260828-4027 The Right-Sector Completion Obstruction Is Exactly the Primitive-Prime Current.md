# The Right-Sector Completion Obstruction Is Exactly the Primitive-Prime Current

For

\[
\log E_X=P_X+H_{2,X},
\qquad
P_X=\sum_{p\le X}p^{-s},
\]

the square-and-higher term \(H_{2,X}\) converges locally uniformly to a
holomorphic unit throughout \(1/2<\Re s<1\). Thus only the primitive current
can obstruct strict completion in the RH right sector.

Möbius inversion gives

\[
P(s)=\sum_{m\ge1}\frac{\mu(m)}m\log\zeta(ms).
\]

All \(m\ge2\) terms have \(\Re(ms)>1\) in the right sector and are
holomorphically harmless. The only possible branch obstruction is
\(\log\zeta(s)\). Therefore a compatible holomorphic primitive-current
branch exists exactly when the right sector is zero-free.

The constructive RH target is now singular: derive that primitive unit from
labelled prime incidence and theta boundary data without choosing a branch
from \(\zeta\). Reciprocal sewing supplies the left sector, and Hurwitz then
confines all zeros to the seam.
