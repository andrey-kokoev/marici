# A Full Theta Collision Forces a Primitive Near-Collision

With

\[
\Phi=\phi_1+\tau,
\qquad
0<\tau<0.006001\,\phi_1,
\]

every full truncated-theta double zero must satisfy

\[
\frac{|C_1(L,x)|}{\int_0^L\phi_1}<0.006001,
\qquad
\frac{|M_1(L,x)|}{\int_0^L u\phi_1}<0.006001.
\]

Thus an infinite labelled collision is possible only inside a simultaneous
\(0.6001\%\) near-collision window of the primitive polynomial--Gaussian
value and tangent transforms.

Both primitive coordinates have an explicit incomplete-gamma form. The
remaining proof divides into compact reserve control and uniform
high-frequency endpoint asymptotics.
