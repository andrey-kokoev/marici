## Frozen oriented chart

Choose base vertices

\[
r_1=(0,0,0),\qquad
r_2=(p,0,0),\qquad
r_3=(u,v,0),
\]

where (p=P_2>0) and (v) is the signed altitude of the external momentum
triangle. Let the loop point be

\[
\ell=(x,y,z).
\]

The three loop distances are

\[
c^2=x^2+y^2+z^2,
\]

\[
a^2=(x-p)^2+y^2+z^2,
\]

\[
b^2=(x-u)^2+(y-v)^2+z^2.
\]

The coarse fixed-base Gram coordinate is

\[
\boxed{
\Lambda_P=-4p^2v^2.
}
\]

Thus (v) is the resolved linear normal and (Lambda_P) is its quadratic
coarse invariant.

## Literal six-term source parity

Under

\[
(v,y)\longmapsto(-v,-y),
\]

all three squared distances (a^2,b^2,c^2) are unchanged. Hence every one
of the ten affine source denominators, the complete six-term cyclic source
weight, and the half-space (z>0) are preserved.

Write the pulled-back period as

\[
I(v)=3pv\,J(v).
\]

The residual integral satisfies

\[
J(-v)=J(v).
\]

At generic positive nonsoft kinematics its literal integrand has one sign,
so (J(0)
eq0) after the same source regularization used away from the
Gram boundary.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
I(-v)=-I(v).
}

\[

\[
\boxed{

\qquad
