# The Canonical Adjoint Weyl Observer Is Logarithmic Curvature

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

The source-authorized observer pair obtained from evaluation and its adjoint
Weyl lift gives

\[
M_F=
\begin{pmatrix}
F&F'\\
F'&F''
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det M_F=FF''-(F')^2.
\]

At a simple zero its determinant is $-(F')^2\ne0$, so the dual observer
repairs central visibility and proves transversality. But its complex phase
is unconstrained off the seam. The construction collapses exactly to the
known logarithmic-curvature obstruction and has no zero-confinement force.

The next observer must be a genuinely global adjoint tail lift with its
kernel quotient and topology retained, not another finite jet.
