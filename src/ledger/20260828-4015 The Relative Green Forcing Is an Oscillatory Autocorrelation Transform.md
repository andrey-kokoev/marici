# The Relative Green Forcing Is an Oscillatory Autocorrelation Transform

For the one-sided positive source, define

\[
A_f(d)=\int_0^\infty e^{-q}f(q)f(q+d)\,dq.
\]

The reciprocal Green forcing mismatch at \(z=x+iy\) is exactly

\[
\mathcal R_f(x,y)
=
4\int_0^\infty
A_f(d)e^{-d/2}\sinh(xd)\cos(yd)\,dd.
\]

At a paired scalar zero, the two Green identities give

\[
E_z+E_{-\overline z}
=
-2\int_0^\infty
A_f(d)e^{-d/2}
\frac{\sinh(xd)}{x}
\cos(yd)\,dd.
\]

The left side is positive for a nonzero state. Therefore nonnegativity of the
source-derived cosine transform on the right would exclude off-seam zeros.

The dynamic top cell is thus not a formal corona boundary. It is an
oscillatory transform of a canonical positive autocorrelation. The remaining
gate is a theta-specific modular orientation theorem; positivity of the
autocorrelation alone is insufficient.
