# 3126 — Prime Shifts Are Unipotent and Reciprocal Windows Escape to the Unit at Completion

Logarithmic translation sends the centered Gaussian tail to itself plus a
Gaussian bulk cocycle. It therefore acts trivially on the five-cell boundary
quotient and unipotently on the full extension.

Reciprocal reflection cancels the quotient class at every finite label and
leaves the even bulk window

\[
W_L(q)=H(q+L)-H(q-L).
\]

For each finite \(L\), this is Gaussian-decaying. But

\[
W_L(q)\longrightarrow-1
\]

locally as \(L\to\infty\). At prime scale \(L=\log p\), the repaired windows
therefore escape every fixed Gaussian step and reconstruct the constant
boundary mode at completion.

## Scope

Finite reciprocal sewing closes exactly. The result shows that this does not
imply restricted-product closure. Primitive and prime-square coefficient
grades must control the widening-window family.
