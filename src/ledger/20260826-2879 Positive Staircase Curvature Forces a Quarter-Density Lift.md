# 2879 — Positive Staircase Curvature Forces a Quarter-Density Lift

The staircase current has amplitude weight \(p^{-k/2}\). A positive
commutator squares transport amplitudes, so native half-density transport gives
\(p^{-k}\) and already misses the \(p^2\) coefficient. Requiring a
multiplicative weighted shift \(p^{-\alpha}T_p\) to reproduce every depth
forces \(\alpha=1/4\). Thus any positive operator realization needs a
source-derived quarter-density or half-form lift. Its existence remains
unproved; inserting it by hand would be a fitted metric.
