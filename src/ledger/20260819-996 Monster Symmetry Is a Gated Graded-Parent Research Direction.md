# Monster Symmetry Is a Gated Graded-Parent Research Direction

## Terminological correction

The Monster is finite.  It therefore has no positive Lie- or
manifold-dimension.  Distinct numerical notions must not be conflated:

- the order of the Monster group;
- its smallest nontrivial irreducible representation, of dimension
  \(196883\);
- the \(196884=1+196883\)-dimensional Griess algebra;
- the infinite-dimensional graded moonshine module \(V^\natural\).

Consequently the statement

\[
\dim(\text{Marici carrier})=\text{dimension of the Monster}
\]

is currently ill-posed.

## Gated future direction

If Monster symmetry occurs in Marici, the architecture developed in Entries
985 and 990 makes its most natural location a graded parent coefficient or
state object, not the finite incidence carrier:

\[
\boxed{
\text{economical carrier calculus}
+
\text{graded parent state object with a large automorphism group}.
}
\]

A possible future pattern would be

\[
\mathscr U
\longrightarrow
V
\longrightarrow
\{(\mathcal K_s,\mathcal O_s,\langle-,-\rangle_s)\}_s,
\]

where \(V\) is a canonically constructed graded completion and the sector
lens--readout packages are realizations, subquotients, or graded traces of
\(V\).  The Monster would then have to emerge as an automorphism group of
\(V\), rather than being imposed from a desired dimension.

## Activation gates

This direction remains dormant unless an independently constructed Marici
completion exhibits at least one unmistakable moonshine structure, followed
by replication.  Admissible signatures include:

1. a canonical graded character with the coefficients
   \(196884,21493760,\ldots\), with grading fixed before comparison;
2. a distinguished modular function related by a derived normalization to
   \(J(\tau)\);
3. a source-derived \(c=24\) chiral or vertex-algebraic structure;
4. McKay--Thompson-type graded traces satisfying the required modular laws;
5. generators and products forcing Monster-compatible representation
   theory.

One numerical coincidence is insufficient.  Several small Marici ranks
embedded into a large representation are also insufficient.

## Prohibitions

Until an activation gate is met, do not:

- interpret ordinary carrier ranks as fragments of \(196883\) or
  \(196884\);
- search large decompositions until a fit is found;
- use Monster terminology to explain an untyped class or missing map;
- promote modular-looking numerical data without a canonical grading and
  transformation law;
- alter H2W, H2S, or H2LR to accommodate a sporadic group.

## Falsifiable research question

The properly typed future question is

\[
\boxed{
\text{Does a naturally constructed graded completion of the Marici parent
develop modular trace functions whose symmetry group is forced to be
sporadic?}
}
\]

A negative answer leaves the established carrier and sector-specific
coefficient program unchanged.  A positive answer would locate Monster
symmetry at the parent-state level while explaining why finite sector
carriers remain small.
