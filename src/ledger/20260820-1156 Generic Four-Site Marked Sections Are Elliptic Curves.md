## Frozen source objects

## Pullback curve

The marked curve on the coefficient boundary is

\[
C_g=\pi^{-1}(L_g).
\]

At generic kinematics, (L_g) meets the quartic branch divisor in four
simple points. Therefore

\[
C_g\longrightarrow L_g\simeq\mathbf P^1
\]

is a double cover branched at four points. Riemann--Hurwitz gives

\[
2g(C_g)-2
=2(2g(\mathbf P^1)-2)+4=0,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{g(C_g)=1.}
\]

Equivalently, (C_g=\pi^*H=-K_{X_4}), and adjunction gives

\[
2g(C_g)-2=C_g(C_g+K_{X_4})=0.
\]

Although the unmarked surface has no transcendental (H^2), deleting a
generic marked component produces a residue quotient containing

\[

\]

Thus the first generic transcendental four-site coefficient data are
elliptic and live in the marked-relative layer. They are generated by the
existing source hyperplanes, not by a K3 carrier or an added incidence
stratum.
