# The Frontier Current Is the Unique Minimal Fourier-Fixed Boundary State

Among states

\[
f(\rho)
=
\left(
a\rho^4+b\rho^2+c
\right)e^{-\pi\rho^2},
\]

Fourier fixedness forces

\[
b=-\frac{3a}{2\pi}.
\]

Boundary nullity \(f(0)=0\) then forces \(c=0\), leaving exactly the line
spanned by

\[
F(\rho)
=
\rho^2(2\pi\rho^2-3)e^{-\pi\rho^2}.
\]

Positive outer orientation fixes its sign. Thus the frontier current is the
unique minimal even Fourier-fixed Gaussian boundary state.

This source-derived minimality rejects higher-Hermite hostile additions, but
does not itself confine zeros of the arithmetic matrix coefficient.
