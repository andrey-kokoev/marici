# 3693 — A Positive Separating Gaussian Seed Can Have Simple Off-Seam Zeros

## Result

The strictly positive even seed

\[
\Phi=\phi_a+c\phi_b,
\qquad a>b>0,
\]

has transform

\[
F(z)=e^{-az^2}+ce^{-bz^2}.
\]

Its zeros obey

\[
z^2=-\frac{\log c+(2k+1)\pi i}{a-b}.
\]

For \(c=1\), all are simple and off the imaginary seam despite positivity,
evenness, heat covariance, spectral multiplicity one, and the seed's
separating property.

## Consequence

Pointed-source rigidity establishes provenance but supplies no zero-location
orientation. The missing RH explanation must use a structural theta property
absent from positive Gaussian mixtures, such as complete labelled Poisson
sewing or arithmetic boundary incidence.
