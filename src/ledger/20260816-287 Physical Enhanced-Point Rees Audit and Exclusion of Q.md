# Physical Enhanced-Point Rees Audit and Exclusion of Q

## Result

Entry 286 localizes every failure of generic semistability at total energy
to four source-generated points. At the physical point

\[
P_{--}=[-y:-x:1],
\]

use the moving marked coordinates

\[
a=E-y+u,
\qquad
b=E-x+v.
\]

The two frozen walls are exactly

\[
u=0,
\qquad
v=0.
\]

Let

\[
R_0
=
xy(x+y)-xa^2-yb^2
\]

be the central-sheet polynomial and define

\[
D(E,u,v)
=
K_E(a,b)-R_0(a,b)^2.
\]

Exact expansion gives

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{gr}^{(1)}_E D
=
-2(x+y)uv(2x-v)(2y-u).
}
\]

Thus the first local smoothing is generated by the existing two-wall
incidence product \(uv\), multiplied by source-fixed kinematic units.

On the double-wall section,

\[
u=v=0,
\]

the complete expression factors as

\[
\boxed{
D(E,0,0)
=
E^3
(E-x-y)
\left[
E^2-3(x+y)E+8xy
\right].
}
\]

Consequently,

\[
\operatorname{gr}^{(1)}_E D|_{u=v=0}=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{gr}^{(2)}_E D|_{u=v=0}=0,
\]

while

\[
\operatorname{gr}^{(3)}_E D|_{u=v=0}
=
-8xy(x+y).
\]

This excludes a proposed home of the algebraic quartic:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal Q
\text{ is not the second-Rees local smoothing discriminant at }P_{--}.
}
\]

Indeed,

\[
\operatorname{gr}^{(2)}_E\mathcal Q=-8xy\neq0
\]

at generic nonsoft kinematics, whereas the physical local surface
smoothing has vanishing second grade.

The negative result is narrow. It does not exclude \(\mathcal Q\) from
the other three enhanced points, their global gluing, the algebraic
\(\mathcal T_7/L_1\) connection, or the extension between the algebraic
kernel and elliptic quotient.

## First Rees grade

Write

\[

=

+

\]

Direct coefficient collection yields

\[

4xy-2xu-2yv+uv

\]

The bracket factors:

\[

(2x-v)(2y-u).
\]

\[
D_1
=
-2(x+y)uv(2x-v)(2y-u).

first smoothing is exactly the product of the two predeclared marked

\[

\[
D(E,0,0)
=

\left(
(E-2x)^2(E-2y)^2
