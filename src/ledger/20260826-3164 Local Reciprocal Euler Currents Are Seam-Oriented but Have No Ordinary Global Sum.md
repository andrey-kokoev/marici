# 3164 — Local Reciprocal Euler Currents Are Seam-Oriented but Have No Ordinary Global Sum

With \(z=s-1/2\) and \(r=p^{-z}\),

\[
C_p(z)=\frac12\coth\!\left(\frac{z\log p}{2}\right).
\]

This current is odd under \(z\mapsto-z\), and all its local zeros and poles
lie on \(\Re z=0\). Its right-chart expansion contains the half-charge
\((\log p)/2\), whose sum over primes diverges.

## Scope

Removing the half-charge restores the Euler chamber current but breaks
manifest reciprocity. Global assembly must therefore be a source-derived
relative current; local seam orientation alone is not RH.
