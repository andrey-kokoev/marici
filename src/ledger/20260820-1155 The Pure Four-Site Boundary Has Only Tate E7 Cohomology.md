## Integral and Hodge census

A smooth degree-two del Pezzo surface is the blowup of (mathbf P^2) at
seven points in general position. Hence

\[
H^2(X_4;\mathbb Z)
=
\mathbb Z\langle H,E_1,\ldots,E_7\rangle
\simeq\mathbb Z^8.
\]

Its Betti numbers are

\[
(b_0,b_1,b_2,b_3,b_4)=(1,0,8,0,1),
\]

and its degree-two Hodge numbers are

\[
\boxed{h^{2,0}=0,qquad h^{1,1}=8,qquad h^{0,2}=0.}
\]

Thus the pure transcendental lattice has rank zero.

## E7 lattice

The canonical class is

\[
K_{X_4}=-3H+E_1+\cdots+E_7,
\qquad K_{X_4}^2=2.
\]

Its orthogonal complement in the Picard lattice has rank seven and
intersection type

\[
E_7(-1).
\]

Families of these surfaces may therefore carry nontrivial Weyl-group
monodromy on an algebraic lattice, but not a K3-like holomorphic two-form
variation.
