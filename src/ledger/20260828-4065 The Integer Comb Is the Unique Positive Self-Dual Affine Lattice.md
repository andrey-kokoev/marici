# The Integer Comb Is the Unique Positive Self-Dual Affine Lattice

For

\[
\Delta_{\alpha,\beta}
=
\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}\delta_{\alpha(n+\beta)},
\]

Fourier transformation produces dual weights

\[
e^{2\pi i k\beta}
\]

on the reciprocal lattice \(\alpha^{-1}\mathbb Z\).

Positivity of every dual weight forces \(\beta\) to be integral, which is only
a reindexing. Equality of primal and dual supports then forces \(\alpha=1\).

Thus the integer comb is the unique positive Fourier-self-dual affine lattice
in one dimension. The frontier-current positivity mechanism is arithmetically
rigid, not generic under lattice deformation.

This still does not prohibit zeros of the resulting Mellin matrix coefficient.
