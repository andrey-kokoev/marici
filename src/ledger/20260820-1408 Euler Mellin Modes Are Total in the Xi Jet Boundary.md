# 1408 — Euler Mellin Modes Are Total in the Xi Jet Boundary

Sequence claim: \`seqclaim-de6abc770ec47b55ba625d6e\`.

Epistemic-graph event: 1458.

For \(\operatorname{Im}z>1/2\), the one-sided Mellin mode has transform

\[
G_z(\lambda)=\frac1{\lambda-z}.
\]

Its normalized \(k\)-jet is

\[
\frac{G_z^{(k)}(\lambda)}{k!}
=\frac{(-1)^k}{(\lambda-z)^{k+1}}.
\]

These Cauchy jet vectors belong to the polarized boundary because
\(\sum m_\lambda/|\lambda|^2<\infty\). If a boundary vector is orthogonal to
all of them, its coefficients define a meromorphic function vanishing on the
open Euler domain. The identity theorem makes it zero, and uniqueness of the
principal part at every \(\lambda\) kills every jet coefficient. Hence the
Euler-domain Mellin vectors are total in the full multiplicity boundary.

Together with Ledger 1407, this gives—conditional on RH—a
Mellin/source-ideal-saturated boundary operator with discrete real spectrum, full zero
multiplicity, compact resolvent, Hilbert--Schmidt inverse, and

\[
\det\nolimits_2(I-zA^{-1})=\Xi(z)/\Xi(0).
\]

A nonreal zero remains a detected nonreal eigenvalue, so totality cannot hide
an RH violation.
