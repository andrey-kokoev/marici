## Conjectural extension

The stronger conjecture is that the Carrier generates the admissible
presentation transformations and their readout algebra together. Current
evidence does not establish that claim. It is weakened if a grounded sector
requires its readout algebra to be inserted externally, or if two sectors
require incompatible coherence calculi.

The still more speculative observer/perception interpretation is not promoted
by this entry.
