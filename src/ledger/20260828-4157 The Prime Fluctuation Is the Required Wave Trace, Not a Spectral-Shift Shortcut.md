# The Prime Fluctuation Is the Required Wave Trace, Not a Spectral-Shift Shortcut

The mean-subtracted logarithmic prime current is

$$
d\nu(q)=\sum_{n\ge2}\Lambda(n)\delta_{\log n}(dq)-e^q\,dq,
$$

with Laplace transform

$$
-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}-\frac1{s-1}.
$$

It cannot simply be declared a spectral-shift function because that exchanges
a Laplace transform for a resolvent transform. Instead, logarithmic
differentiation turns Xi zeros into poles, yielding the precise Hilbert--Pólya
target: construct a fixed self-adjoint evolution whose regularized wave trace
is the completed prime-power fluctuation current.

Prime powers would then be return events, logarithmic prime lengths their
times, and zeros the real spectral poles. The operator and trace formula must
be derived from theta/Tate constructors rather than inverted from Xi.
