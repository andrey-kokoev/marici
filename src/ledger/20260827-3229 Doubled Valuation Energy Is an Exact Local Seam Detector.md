bordered-chain energy factors as

\[

(b=p^{2\operatorname{Re}s-2}). Its sign is exactly the sign of
(1/2-\operatorname{Re}s), so it vanishes only on the critical seam. The
finite Green identity retains both primitive and terminal defects.
