# 3407 — The Chiral Index Has a Vectorlike Portal Kernel

## Claim

For ordered boundary multiplicities, the ordinary domain-wall index

\[
I=n_0-n_\pi
\]

annihilates the diagonal vectorlike direction \((1,1)\). Consequently
\((2,1)\) and \((3,2)\) both have \(I=1\) but predict portal contrasts
\(9/50\) and \(25/338\).

The minimal faithful integral packet is

\[
T=n_0+n_\pi,
\qquad
I=n_0-n_\pi,
\]

which reconstructs the ordered pair by

\[
n_0=\frac{T+I}{2},
\qquad
n_\pi=\frac{T-I}{2}.
\]

## Classification

The ordinary index is an orientation and net-chirality selector, not a total
rank or portal-magnitude selector. A source-derived relative rank-index or
endpoint-resolved K-class is required, together with a rank-sensitive physical
instrument.
