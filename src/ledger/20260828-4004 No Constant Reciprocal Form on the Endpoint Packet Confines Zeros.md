# No Constant Reciprocal Form on the Endpoint Packet Confines Zeros

Reciprocal reflection acts on the lifted zero packet by

\[
S=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1),
\qquad
p(z)=\bigl(F'(z),-f(0)\bigr)^T.
\]

Every invariant constant bilinear or Hermitian form satisfies
\(S^TBS=B\) and is diagonal. It therefore sees only reciprocal-even
quadratic magnitudes and loses half-plane orientation.

Every anti-invariant form satisfies \(S^TBS=-B\) and is off-diagonal. Its
surviving scalar is proportional to \(f(0)F'(z)\), which measures
transversality but contains no horizontal-displacement factor.

Thus no constant symplectic, Clifford, bilinear, or Hermitian pairing of the
endpoint packet can confine zeros. The required factor
\(2\operatorname{Re}z\) must arise dynamically from the spectral generator
and its Green current.
