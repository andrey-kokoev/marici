# 3563 — The Moving-Seam Prefix Current Gives a Canonical Mixed-Prime Difference Cocycle

C_z(a,b)=\mathcal K_b(z)-\mathcal K_a(z).

\[
C_z(a,b)+C_z(b,c)=C_z(a,c),

C_z(b,a)=-C_z(a,b).
