The transition probability \(p\in[0,1]\) is controlled by wave-plate settings in two independent Sagnac interferometers. The experiment performs full bipartite polarization tomography at multiple values of \(p\).

For the initial state

\[
|\Theta\rangle
=
\alpha|HH\rangle+\beta e^{i\theta}|VV\rangle,
\qquad
|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2=1,

\[

\begin{pmatrix}
|\alpha|^2+p^2|\beta|^2&0&0&(1-p)\alpha\beta^*e^{-i\theta}\\

0&0&p(1-p)|\beta|^2&0\\
(1-p)\alpha^*\beta e^{i\theta}&0&0&(1-p)^2|\beta|^2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Thus

\[
b(p)=c(p)=p(1-p)|\beta|^2,
\qquad
|z(p)|=(1-p)|\alpha\beta|.
\]
