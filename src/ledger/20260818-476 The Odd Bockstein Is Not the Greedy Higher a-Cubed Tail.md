# The Odd Bockstein Is Not the Greedy Higher a-Cubed Tail

## Record

Status: falsification of the canonical-module inference suggested by Entry 474.

For the deck-orbit-completed dual-number exact complex, let

\[
\beta_-:
\ker D_{0,-}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{coker}D_{0,-}
\]

be the anti-invariant Bockstein. Membership of a target \(t\) is tested by
the source-defined system

\[
D_{0,-}x=0,
\qquad
D_{1,-}x+D_{0,-}y=t.
\]

At \(D=16,20,24,28\),

\[
\operatorname{rank}\operatorname{im}\beta_-=D-2.
\]

Entry 474's degree-lexicographic missing monomials also span a \(D-2\)
dimensional complement, but direct membership refutes equality: none of the
pure classes

\[
[a^3b^j],
\qquad
1\le j\le D-3,
\]

lies individually in \(\operatorname{im}\beta_-\).

The source deformation factor is nevertheless detected. Every tested class

\[
[a^3b^j(1-b^2)],
\qquad
0\le j\le D-5,
\]

lies in the Bockstein image. All tested \([a^{11}b^j]\) also lie in the
image. However, the natural span

\[
a^3(1-b^2)\mathbb Q[b]_{le D-5}
+
\langle a^{11},a^{11}b\rangle
\]

has rank only

\[
D-3
\]

modulo the frozen exact image. It is one dimension short of the Bockstein
image at every tested stable cutoff.

Therefore the extensive odd obstruction is not canonically the monomial
submodule generated by \([a^3b]\), and it is not exhausted by the obvious
\((1-b^2)\)-tail plus pure resonance endpoints. One additional mixed
direction is forced by the complete exact operators.

This narrows Entry 474: its displayed monomials remain a valid stable greedy
normal form for the failed flat lifts, but not an operator-defined
presentation.
