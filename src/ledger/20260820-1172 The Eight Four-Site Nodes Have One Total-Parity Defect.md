## Frozen node orbit

Entry 1171 gives the eight branch nodes

\[
p_\epsilon=[1:\epsilon_2:\epsilon_3:\epsilon_4],
\qquad \epsilon_i\in\{\pm1\},
\]

as the regular orbit of \(C_2^3\). For a quartic double solid, the standard
defect test evaluates quadrics at its nodes: a double cover of \(\mathbf P^3\)
branched in degree \(2r\) is factorial precisely when its nodes impose
independent conditions in degree \(3r-4\). Here \(r=2\), so the relevant
degree is two.

## Exact evaluation rank

Evaluate the ten quadratic monomials \(y_i y_j\), \(i\le j\), on the eight
sign points. On this orbit:

- all four squares restrict to the trivial character;
- \(y_1y_i\), \(i=2,3,4\), give the three degree-one characters;
- \(y_i y_j\), \(2\le i<j\le4\), give the three degree-two characters.

Thus the evaluation image is

\[
\mathbf 1
\oplus
\chi_2\oplus\chi_3\oplus\chi_4
\oplus
\chi_2\chi_3\oplus\chi_2\chi_4\oplus\chi_3\chi_4,
\]

of rank seven. The unique missing character is

\[
\boxed{\chi_{234}=\epsilon_2\epsilon_3\epsilon_4.}
\]

Equivalently, every quadric \(q\) obeys the exact signed relation

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3,\epsilon_4=\pm1}
\epsilon_2\epsilon_3\epsilon_4\,
q(p_\epsilon)=0.
}
\]

Therefore the nodal defect is

\[
\boxed{\delta=8-7=1.}
\]

smoothing cycles. In character language, the local regular representation

\[

\simeq
