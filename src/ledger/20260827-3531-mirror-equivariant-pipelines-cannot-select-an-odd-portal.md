# 3531 — Mirror-Equivariant Pipelines Cannot Select an Odd Portal

## Claim

On a free two-point mirror orbit, every invariant selector weights both members
equally and therefore cannot have singleton support. This remains true when an
even source potential fixes the nonzero portal magnitude.

Mirror-equivariant RG preserves paired trajectories and basin data.
Sign-even thresholds collapse the pair; signed thresholds and instruments can
distinguish a prepared member but cannot select its source. Adding a reference
orientation produces a relational readout over a stabilizer groupoid. If the
reference is itself dynamical and mirror odd, simultaneous reversal restores
the paired joint states.

## Classification

a non-mirror-completable boundary or irreversible history constructor that
co-generates portal orientation, signed calibration, magnitude, RG initial
condition, and threshold state. This is a necessity theorem, not an existence
claim for such a physical flavor constructor.
