# 1403 — The Scalar Weil Boundary Has Only the Squarefree Xi Determinant

Sequence claim: \`seqclaim-d524480297f27f30b3dd6e54\`.

Epistemic-graph event: 1451.

Under RH, the scalar Weil completion is

\[
L^2\!\left(\sum_\lambda m_\lambda\delta_\lambda\right).
\]

Each distinct atom is one Hilbert dimension; its mass \(m_\lambda\) changes
the boundary-vector norm but not spectral multiplicity. Consequently its
multiplication operator has determinant

\[
D_W(z)=
\prod_{\lambda>0\ {\rm distinct}}
\left(1-\frac{z^2}{\lambda^2}\right),
\]

whereas

\[
\frac{\Xi(z)}{\Xi(0)}=
\prod_{\lambda>0\ {\rm distinct}}
\left(1-\frac{z^2}{\lambda^2}\right)^{m_\lambda}.
\]

The two agree exactly when every Xi zero is simple. A scalar weight
\(m_\lambda\) cannot replace \(m_\lambda\) independent eigendirections.

Therefore a source-derived solution has a sharp fork: prove reality and
simplicity so the scalar Weil/Pick boundary suffices, or derive a
vector-valued/jet Mellin boundary of local rank \(m_\lambda\). Ledger 1402
constructs the latter dimensions algebraically, but not yet from the Weil
pairing.

Scope: determinant-rank theorem; it neither assumes a multiple zero exists nor
rules out a source-derived jet enhancement.
