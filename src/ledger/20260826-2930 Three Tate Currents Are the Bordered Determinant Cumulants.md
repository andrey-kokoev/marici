The source-derived local determinant has logarithm

\[
-\log(1-p^{-s})
=
p^{-s}
+\frac12p^{-2s}
+\sum_{k\ge3}\frac{p^{-ks}}{k}.
\]

After summing over primes, these are exactly the primitive current, the
prime-square current, and the connected trace-class tail.  The third-order
factor

\[
E_p^{[3]}(s)
=
\frac{
\exp\left(-p^{-s}-p^{-2s}/2\right)
}{1-p^{-s}}
\]

has a globally absolutely convergent product for \(\Re s>1/3\).  The first
two currents remain mandatory relative-boundary coordinates because together
with this tail they reconstruct every finite Euler cutoff exactly.

Thus the three-level Tate filtration is forced by the bordered determinant
cumulants, rather than selected as a convenient regularization.
