\[
\boxed{

\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
D(F)=R.

## Why this criterion is typed

On a doubled edge \(e\), the face carries both occurrence vertices.  Its two barycentric factors retain the two signed appearances of the routing coordinate \(y_e\); schematically their product has the form

\[
(u_e-y_e)(u_e+y_e).
\]

Hence the face canonical form can detect motion in that routing direction.  If a free routing edge is absent from \(D(F)\), the corresponding signed pair is absent from the face residue.  In the tested C8 models the companion fold kernel then lies in the logarithmic kernel of the face form, and the odd coefficient vanishes identically modulo the full fold divisor.

This is not a rule fitted to the numerical counts.  Both sides are computed independently:

- \(D(F)\) from source facet--vertex incidence alone;
