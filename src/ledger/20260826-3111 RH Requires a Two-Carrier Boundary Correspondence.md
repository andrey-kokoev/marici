# 3111 — RH Requires a Two-Carrier Boundary Correspondence

The current theta/Tate programme cannot be typed as one carrier with two
coordinate charts. The circle/winding carrier and the logarithmic-tail
carrier have different generators, spectra, domains, and completion laws.
The exact bounded-intertwiner obstruction proves that they cannot be
identified by reversible Hilbert-space transport.

The minimum categorical architecture is therefore a dagger double-category
fragment with four roles:

- a circle carrier `C_circ`, containing winding, circle heat, and the
  discrete spectral grading;
- a logarithmic carrier `C_log`, containing translation, tail/head
  resolvents, and arithmetic scale transport;
- a rigged boundary correspondence `B`, retaining their distinct incidence
  data;
- an observer family `O`, containing the primitive, square, seam,
  connected-tail, and archimedean ports.

The carrier incidences have the form

\[
i_{\mathrm{circ}}:C_{\mathrm{circ}}\rightsquigarrow B,
\qquad
i_{\log}:C_{\log}\rightsquigarrow B.
\]

A source-derived coherence cell, if it exists, must compare transported
boundary data rather than identify the two generators:

\[
\alpha:
i_{\mathrm{circ}}T_{\mathrm{circ}}
\Rightarrow
i_{\log}T_{\log}.
\]

Fourier–Tate and Poisson operations are candidates for generating this cell.
Their scalar functional equation is not sufficient evidence that the
operator-valued cell exists.

## Completion

Fourier saturation on the primitive and square grades is exact, but their
smallest generalized Gram reserve collapses with the prime cutoff. It
preserves the distinction between completion grades rather than merging them.
The connected tail is naturally controlled by absolute-summability
seminorms, while seam and archimedean ports require separately derived graph
seminorms.

Consequently the candidate completion is a Fourier-invariant pro-Gram
topology on the boundary correspondence. It is not one five-channel
Euclidean Gramian.

Completion must preserve the carrier incidences and any coherence cell:

\[
\widehat\alpha:
\widehat i_{\mathrm{circ}}\widehat T_{\mathrm{circ}}
\Rightarrow
\widehat i_{\log}\widehat T_{\log}.
\]

It must also preserve joint observability of the admitted boundary state.
Finite positivity or finite injectivity does not establish that property.

## First categorical calculation

The next finite calculation is the smallest Beck–Chevalley or naturality
square involving:

- circle and logarithmic dynamics;
- both boundary incidences and their adjoints;
- Fourier transport;
- Mellin scaling;
- cutoff bonding.

The first decisive result is one of:

1. a source-derived coherence cell with completion-stable observability;
2. a named residual or anomaly in the first required square;
3. a proof that the construction collapses to the existing scalar
   Weil/Pick kernel and adds no RH-bearing information.

## Falsifiers

The categorical route fails if it:

- identifies the circle Laplacian with logarithmic translation;
- reconstructs the boundary correspondence from the scalar completed zeta
  section;
- merges all five boundary channels into one unauthorized Gramian;
- infers completion stability from finite positivity;
- declares a coherence cell solely from matching scalar readouts;
- forgets which carrier supplied a boundary contribution.

## Scope

This entry records a source-typing programme, not an RH proof. The two-carrier
necessity, the bounded-intertwiner obstruction, and the finite Fourier
saturations are established. The operator-valued coherence cell, full
boundary pro-Gram completion, common-kernel theorem, and zero-confinement
consequence remain open.
