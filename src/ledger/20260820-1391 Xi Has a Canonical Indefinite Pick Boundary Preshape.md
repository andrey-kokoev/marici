# 1391 — Xi Has a Canonical Indefinite Pick Boundary Preshape

Epistemic-graph event: 1432.

From the theta-defined

\[
M(z)=-\frac{\Xi'(z)}{\Xi(z)}
\]

form the Hermitian Pick kernel

\[
K_\Xi(z,w)=
\frac{M(z)-\overline{M(w)}}{z-\overline w}.
\]

The span of formal vectors \(k_w\), with Gram form
\([k_w,k_z]=K_\Xi(w,z)\), has a canonical radical quotient. This gives an
unconditional algebraic indefinite boundary preshape satisfying the exact
Green/Weyl identity

\[
(z-\overline w)K_\Xi(z,w)
=M(z)-\overline{M(w)}.
\]

Its form is positive semidefinite exactly when all Pick matrices are positive,
hence exactly when RH holds. After positivity, Hilbert completion and Weyl
realization yield the compact-resolvent operator and

\[
\det\nolimits_2(I-zA^{-1})=\Xi(z)/\Xi(0).
\]

Before positivity, this is not the requested Hilbert self-adjoint operator.
The remaining provenance gate is a source map from the paired prime–gamma
boundary vectors to this completed Pick preshape.
