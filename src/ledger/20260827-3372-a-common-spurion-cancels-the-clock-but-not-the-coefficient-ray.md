# 3372 — A Common Spurion Cancels the Clock but Not the Coefficient Ray

## Claim

Suppose one positive source deformation generates both masses in the
nondecoupling threshold:

\[
M_V^2=a g_*^2v^2,
\qquad
m_{\mathrm{soft}}^2=b g_*^2v^2,
\qquad
a,b>0.
\]

Then

\[
\epsilon=\frac{b}{a+b},
\qquad
\Delta=\frac{g_*^2b}{2(a+b)}>0.
\]

The common dimensionful clock (v) cancels from the portal magnitude. This
evades the independent-amplitude obstruction of WP748 conditional on the
correlated source relation.

## Exact residual

The relation does not select the coefficient ray. With the same (g_*) and
(v), the packets ((a,b)=(1,1)) and ((a,b)=(1,3)) give portal contrasts
(g_*^2/4) and (3g_*^2/8), respectively. Their exact residual is
(g_*^2/8).

Threshold support also retains the clock through
(E^2<a g_*^2v^2). No calibrated `physical16` instrument follows from the
algebraic cancellation.

## Classification

The common-spurion relation is a conditional magnitude selector and
common-clock canceller. It is not yet a Deutschian source explanation because
the coefficient packet can vary without changing the mechanism. A complete
source must derive the ordered embedding, unique positive ray (a:b), fixed
coupling and basin, threshold spectrum, and calibrated readout from one
anomaly-free construction.
