# 2813 — The Port Ratio Is Gauge-Dependent but the Safety Margin Is Invariant

Under positive port-unit changes

\[
J'=uJ,
\quad K'=vK,
\quad \beta'=\beta/u,
\quad \alpha'=\alpha/v,
\]

the residual is unchanged, while both the raw ratio and its threshold scale by
\(v/u\). Their dimensionless quotient is invariant. The completion theorem
must therefore use a uniform safety margin \(M_X\ge1+\delta\), not a numerical
bound on \(\beta_X/\alpha_X\), unless port units are frozen. The source must
still supply the repair–drift polarization.
