## Test

## Correlated binary coefficient state

Let \(X,Y\in\{\pm1\}\) with

\[
\Pr(X=x,Y=y)=\frac{1+rxy}{4},
\qquad |r|\le1.
\]

Then

\[
\mathbb E[X^2]=\mathbb E[Y^2]=1,
\qquad
\mathbb E[XY]=r,
\qquad
\mathbb E[X^2Y^2]=1.
\]

Gaussian Wick closure predicts

\[
1+2r^2.
\]

The exact connected fourth cumulant is

\[
\boxed{\kappa_{22}=-2r^2,}
\]

so

\[
1+2r^2+\kappa_{22}=1.
\]

## Conditional associativity

Because \(Y^2=1\) pointwise,

\[
\mathbb E\!\left[X^2\mathbb E(Y^2\mid X)\right]=1,
\]

and the opposite conditioning order gives the same result. Thus exact conditional pushforward remains associative.

The checker audits 4,224 rational distributions. Gaussian closure fails for all 4,160 nonzero correlations; the fourth cumulant repairs every case, and exact conditional contraction matches every case.
