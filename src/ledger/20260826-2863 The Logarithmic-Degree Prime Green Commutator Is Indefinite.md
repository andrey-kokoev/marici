# 2863 — The Logarithmic-Degree Prime Green Commutator Is Indefinite

Although prime-power incidence reconstructs the positive operator

\[
L_{\rm inc}=\frac12\log Q,
\]

its linear Green coupling to prime transport is indefinite already on
\(\operatorname{span}\{e_1,e_p\}\). Both self-adjoint transport quadratures
give commutator forms with eigenvalues \(\pm\log p\). Mellin phase rotation
commutes with the degree while rotating those quadratures, so the degree fixes
regularity but not the star frame. The remaining gate is a source-fixed
two-sheet pairing of each prime transport with its adjoint.
