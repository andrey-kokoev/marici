# The Fixed-Prime Homotopy Survives Graph Completion

For the prime-seam differential, fix one prime and use its half-line homotopy
`h_p`. On the algebraic core,

\[
dh_p+h_pd=1-E_p,
\]

where `E_p` is the bounded moving-window projection. This identity gives the
graph estimate

\[
\|dh_px\|
\leq
(1+\|E_p\|)\|x\|+\|h_p\|\|dx\|.
\]

Hence `h_p` extends continuously to the graph closure and the same homotopy
identity survives completion. Infinite primes cannot manufacture cohomology
outside the explicit seam boundary sector.

The divergence of the Euler-product graded trace is therefore not emergent
cohomology of this complex. A nontrivial class would require reciprocal or
archimedean sewing that breaks the homotopy, a different source-derived
domain, noncommutative coefficients, or a relative determinant anomaly.
