# Native Orthogonal Sheet Doubling Cannot Turn the Lorentzian Prüfer Balance Positive

For any constant orthogonal sheet map \(S\), radial and angular Clifford rates
transform as

\[
\kappa(SV)=\kappa(V),
\qquad
\omega(SV)=\det(S)\omega(V).
\]

Their squares are identical on both sheets. Consequently, every linear
combination of the two Lorentzian balances is a scalar multiple of
\(\omega^2-\kappa^2\). Cancelling radial action necessarily cancels angular
action too.

Reflected theta doubling explains mirrored collisions and quartet completion,
but cannot provide positive collision control. The missing capability must be
genuinely independent: a source-derived shear, control channel, symplectic
mate, or enlarged jet carrier.
