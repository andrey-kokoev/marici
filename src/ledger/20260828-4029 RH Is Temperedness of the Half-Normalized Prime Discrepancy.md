# RH Is Temperedness of the Half-Normalized Prime Discrepancy

Set

\[
q(u)=e^{-u/2}\bigl(\psi(e^u)-e^u\bigr).
\]

The prime-density discrepancy obeys the exact Laplace bridge

\[
-\frac{\zeta'(1/2+z)}{\zeta(1/2+z)}
-\frac1{z-1/2}
=
1+(1/2+z)\int_0^\infty q(u)e^{-zu}\,du.
\]

If \(q\) is tempered on the positive logarithmic ray, its Laplace transform
is holomorphic for \(\Re z>0\), excluding every right-sector zero.
Reciprocal symmetry excludes the left sector. Conversely RH gives
\(\psi(x)-x=O(x^{1/2}\log^2x)\), hence \(q(u)=O(u^2)\).

Thus RH is equivalent to temperedness of the prime discrepancy after exactly
one half-unit of exponential normalization. The critical offset is the
regularity threshold: an off-line zero would contribute a forbidden
exponentially growing boundary mode.
