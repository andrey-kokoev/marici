# Entry 2349: Diagnostic, capability, and policy form a cross-sector lifting ladder

A strict four-sector audit required three independently source-typed objects:

1. an operational diagnostic generated by an instrument;
2. an admissible successor-operation fiber;
3. a source-derived policy selecting a section.

The completed-stage census is

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{sector}&\text{completed stages}\\
\hline
\text{QED scattering}&3\\
\text{toric topology}&2\\
\text{cosmology}&1\\
\text{flavor}&0
\end{array}
\]

Scattering realizes the full triple at instrument-density scope: Cut record,
completion fiber, and source-derived elastic section.  Topology has a syndrome
and recovery fiber but no source decoder policy.  Cosmology has a relational
boundary-score diagnostic but no successor instrument.  Flavor has quotient
fibers but no operational diagnostic instrument or proper-image task.

Thus the common result is an ordered lifting skeleton,

\[
\text{diagnostic}\longrightarrow\text{capability fiber}
\longleftarrow\text{source selector},
\]

not a claim that every sector already contains the completed triple.  The
observed `3,2,1,0` staircase is recorded as an anomaly/pattern; it is not used
as evidence beyond the separately typed sector audits.
