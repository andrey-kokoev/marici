# Mellin--Poisson Boundary Curvature Is the Polar Packet

Splitting the theta Mellin integral at the self-dual scale gives

\[
\Lambda(s)
=
\int_1^\infty
\psi(t)\left(t^{s/2}+t^{(1-s)/2}\right)\frac{dt}{t}
+
\frac1{s-1}-\frac1s.
\]

After completion, the two boundary currents are

\[
\frac{s}{2},
\qquad
\frac{1-s}{2}.
\]

Their symmetric coordinate is \(1/2\); their antisymmetric coordinate is
\(s-1/2\). Hence the boundary anomaly following the flat
Gaussian--arithmetic connection is exactly the known polar packet.

At a nontrivial zero the polar packet does not vanish. The completed theta
tail cancels its neutral \(1/2\) channel. Consequently a polar-only Green
current cannot provide the zero-to-flux bridge. The missing state must retain
the tail and neutral carrier as one coupled boundary vector.
