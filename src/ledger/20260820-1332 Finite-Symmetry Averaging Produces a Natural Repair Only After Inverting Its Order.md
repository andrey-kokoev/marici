# Finite-Symmetry Averaging Produces a Natural Repair Only After Inverting Its Order

Epistemic-graph event: 1350.

For a finite symmetry group `Gamma` of order `m` acting on a nonempty radical
repair torsor, invertibility of `m` produces the fixed repair

`C_av=(1/m) sum_gamma gamma C`.

The affine repair equation and pairing invisibility survive averaging, so
the `H^1(Gamma,A)` obstruction vanishes after `m` is inverted.  Natural
repairs are still nonunique unless the invariant translation module
`A^Gamma` vanishes.

Integrally the construction can fail: the `C2` action `x -> 1-x` has formal
fixed point `1/2`, outside `Z`.  Hence a two-group symmetry of a possible
five-site radical repair is automatically resolvable only after inverting
two; integral and mod-two naturality remain separate certificates.
