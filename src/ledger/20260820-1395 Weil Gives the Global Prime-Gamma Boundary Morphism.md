# 1395 — Weil Gives the Global Prime–Gamma Boundary Morphism

Epistemic-graph event: 1439.

For logarithmic test functions, let \(g^*(u)=\overline{g(-u)}\). The Weil
explicit-formula functional has arithmetic side

\[
W(h)=W_\infty(h)+W_{\rm endpoint}(h)
-\sum_{n\ge2}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}
\bigl(h(\log n)+h(-\log n)\bigr)
\]

and spectral side

\[
W(h)=\sum_\rho
\widehat h\!\left(\frac{\rho-1/2}{i}\right).
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{RH}\iff W(g*g^*)\ge0
\text{ for every admissible }g.}
\]

The radical quotient of

\[
\langle g_1,g_2\rangle_W=W(g_1*g_2^*)
\]

is the canonical global, cutoff-independent prime–gamma boundary quotient.
It is source-derived and unconditional, but indefinite until RH positivity is
proved.

Its formal Cauchy-feature Gram kernel is exactly the \(\Xi\) Pick kernel of
Ledger 1391. The remaining analytic gate is extension from compact test
functions to those resolvent vectors, followed by closability, compact
resolvent, and the regularized determinant theorem.
