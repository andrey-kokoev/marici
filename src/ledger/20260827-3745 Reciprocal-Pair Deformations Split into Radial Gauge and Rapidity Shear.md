# 3745 — Reciprocal-Pair Deformations Split into Radial Gauge and Rapidity Shear

For

\[
I_+=Re^\chi,
\qquad
I_-=Re^{-\chi},
\]

reciprocity-compatible deformations have two elementary channels.

An even common multiplier (h) sends (R\mapsto hR) while preserving
\(\chi\). An odd opposite multiplier (e^{\pm g}) sends

\[
\chi\mapsto\chi+g
\]

while preserving (R^2=I_+I_-). These are respectively radial gauge and
rapidity shear.

Both can independently move the scalar intersection

\[
S=\frac12+2PR\cosh\chi=0.
\]

Therefore neither the projective rapidity nor the Lorentz radius can by
itself confine zeros. Positive-source log convexity does imply monotone
rapidity and radius growth on the real boost axis, but this property is
universal and survives hostile positive carriers.

The RH-bearing source rigidity must couple radius and rapidity before scalar
aggregation. A direct falsifier is any nontrivial divisor-changing radial
gauge or rapidity shear generated by the authorized labelled theta
constructors.
