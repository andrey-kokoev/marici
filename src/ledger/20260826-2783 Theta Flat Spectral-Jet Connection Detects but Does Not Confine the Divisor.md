# 2783 — Theta Flat Spectral-Jet Connection Detects but Does Not Confine the Divisor

The causal-prefix pivot

\[
Y(q,z)=e^{-zq}F(z)
\]

has the canonical flat connection

\[
\nabla_q=\partial_q+z,
\qquad
\nabla_z=\partial_z+q,
\qquad
[\nabla_q,\nabla_z]=0.
\]

Its covariant jets are (e^{-zq}F^{(k)}(z)), so the connection detects the
divisor and its multiplicity but does not constrain its location. This
two-parameter flatness is universal for regular source transforms.

A spectral line connection that instead makes (Y) parallel must have

\[
a(q,z)=q-\frac{F'(z)}{F(z)}.
\]

It extends holomorphically across a sector exactly when (F) is zero-free
there. Thus invoking such a connection without a larger independent source
construction would be circular.
