# 3538 — Compact Clockwork Selects Relative Couplings, Not Its Incidence

## Claim

A compact \(U(1)\) clockwork with oriented link charges \((1,-q)\) has the
unique zero-mode profile

\[
v_j\propto q^{-j}.
\]

For a four-site \(q=3\) chain, the nonzero Gram eigenvalues in units of the
link scale are

\[
10-3\sqrt2,\qquad 10,\qquad 10+3\sqrt2.
\]

Thus fixed incidence supplies both an exact relative-coupling selector and a
strict gear gap.

Compactness does not select the integer \(q\), chain length, orientation, or
endpoint species. The \(q=2\) and \(q=3\) chains give endpoint ratios \(1/8\)
and \(1/27\). Chain reversal preserves the full gear spectrum and reverses the
endpoint contrast. Absolute couplings and thresholds retain \(g\) and \(m\).

## Classification

Clockwork replaces the continuous domain-wall overlap fiber by a discrete
incidence fiber plus continuous normalization and threshold scales. It is a
relative selector and basin rigidifier conditional on its source incidence,
not the source principle selecting that incidence or a physical16 instrument.
