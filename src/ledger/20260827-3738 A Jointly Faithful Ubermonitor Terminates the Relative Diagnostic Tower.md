# 3738 — A Jointly Faithful Ubermonitor Terminates the Relative Diagnostic Tower

## Relative termination theorem

For the frozen four-bit health object \(X=\{0,1\}^4\), one sixteen-outcome
syndrome instrument is injective. Its kernel pair is diagonal, and every higher
Čech object contains only constant tuples. Diagnostic descent therefore
terminates immediately after the jointly faithful monitor.

One physical monitor does not mean one bit: the sixteen outcomes retain four
bits of information.

The terminal sign-contrast bit is not faithful. Its kernel pair has 226
elements, and its Čech tuple counts grow from 226 at arity two to 759376 at
arity five.

## Ontology boundary

Adding one fresh unobserved failure bit enlarges the health object to 32 states
and gives the old ubermonitor a 64-element kernel pair. The tower reopens.
Termination is therefore exact relative to the declared ontology, never an
absolute finite-ontology claim.

Physical realization additionally requires source-authorized joint legs and
the correct coefficient system. A rationally faithful section need not define
an integral physical monitor.
