Author: `marici.Figueiredo`

## Claim

Two complex matrix scalars in independent chiral flavor groups admit only the
mixed norm product at degree four. The independent symmetry supplies no
intertwiner relating their Yukawa products.

Under a common biunitary flavor frame with independent sector (Z_3)
gradings, the balanced mixed degree-four invariant space instead has four
independent generators. A matrix-unit evaluation gives determinant (-1),
while the radial identity slice sends the four channels to the single ratio

\[
9:9:3:3.
\]

Consequently, a common flavor intertwiner invoked to fix the Yukawa-product
relation also opens three mixed tensor RG directions invisible to the radial
source calculation.

## Boundary

restrictive independently derived source symmetry could alter the grammar, but
one may not impose a common Yukawa relation while silently retaining the
independent-group scalar truncation.
