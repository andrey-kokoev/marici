# 1914 — The Presentation–Quotient Separation Is the Shared Structural Operation Across Three Sectors (Cross-Sector Synthesis)

## Question

With the flavor admission test closed (Entry WP6 verdict: not admitted;
WP10–WP14b: chart-vs-quotient separation exact on both the \(J\) channel
and the determinant channel), the standing research brief permits the
next step: compare **structural operations only** across the flavor,
string and cosmology lanes — chart atlas, quotient descent, loop or
cocycle holonomy, readout realization, fiber structure.  No isomorphism
claims, no common-parent claims.  This entry is a synthesis of already
published, individually verified records; it contains no new
computation.

It also answers Benincasa's ev-761 request: does the flavor lane
contain a source-defined higher-overlap/readout operation that could
type as an analogue of the intrinsic Čech \(H^1\) versus degree-zero
readout separation (Entry 1078)?

presentation coordinate**:

## Answer to ev-761

No.  The flavor lane contains no source-defined higher-overlap/readout
operation.  The only candidate cohomological object was the
fundamental-cycle holonomy, and WP11 shows it is a sign-twisted
(Möbius) chart cocycle rather than intrinsic \(H^1\) data; the physical
readout is purely degree-zero (weak-basis invariants) and receives loop
data only through the squaring channel.  The highest-information
cross-sector comparison is the descent-obstruction typing above —
specifically, whether the string lane's chamber-phase transport carries
an orientation/monodromy ambiguity analogous to the WP11 Möbius twist.
That is a question back to the string lane, not a claim about it.

## What this entry does not assert

  shared parent (H2LR remains open).
- The \(\pi/8\) clustering plays no role: it is exhausted by CKM-angle
  inheritance at the map level (WP14b) and carries no explanatory
  weight in any comparison.
- Nothing here reopens the WP6 admission verdict; the comparison uses
  the failure, it does not soften it.

## Falsifiers of the synthesis

1. A signed-holonomy-sensitive physical observable in any of the three
   lanes would break the shared pattern.
2. A canonical sign-fixing of the flavor carrier groupoid (a global
   section of the WP11 Möbius bundle) would re-open loop-phase descent
   and move flavor's row of the table to the string/cosmology type.
3. A string-lane computation showing chamber-phase transport is
   monodromy-free would sharpen, not break, the typing difference.
