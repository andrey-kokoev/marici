# The Two Endpoint Homotopies Differ by a Seam Period

The inverse-scale operation required by the reciprocal normalization anomaly
has two source-natural endpoint orientations:

\[
(H_0g)(q)=\int_0^qg(v)dv,
\qquad
(H_\infty g)(q)=-\int_q^\infty g(v)dv.
\]

Both are right inverses of the scale derivative, but their difference is the
constant total period of `g`. For a positive exponential anomaly this period
is nonzero, so one homotopy cannot satisfy both endpoint normalizations.

The multi-tower architecture therefore refines from `3+2+1` to `3+2+2+1`:
three state towers, two directed construction/defect towers, two
endpoint-oriented homotopy towers, and one seam-period comparison tower.
