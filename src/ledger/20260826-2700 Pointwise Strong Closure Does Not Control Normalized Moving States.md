# 2700 — Pointwise Strong Closure Does Not Control Normalized Moving States

every fixed state, and so do their Gram operators, while (\|P_N\|=1) for all
(N). The moving normalized witness (e_N) remains fully visible. RH-strength
uniform exclusion requires operator-norm, tightness, or direct uniform frame
bounds beyond pointwise Gram closure.
