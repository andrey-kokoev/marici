# 3685 — Heat Naturality Rigidifies Scalar Gauges but Not Source Endomorphisms

## Result

Although no nonconstant scalar multiplier commutes with Newman heat, the heat
commutant contains every constant-coefficient differential operator.

For even \(N\),

\[
T=I+c\partial_z^{2N}
\]

commutes with heat, preserves parity and reality, and corresponds to the
positive Fourier-side weight \(1+cu^{2N}\). Yet

\[
T(z^{2N})=z^{2N}+c(2N)!
\]

has only off-seam zeros.

## Consequence

Heat naturality provides scalar-gauge rigidity but not uniqueness of the
initial source. The remaining provenance theorem must retain the exact
labelled theta seed or another content-addressed initial-state invariant.
