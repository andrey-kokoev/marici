# 3145 — The Polarized Heat Boundary Morphism Is Canonical but Not Positive

Nonnegative heat profiles give positive regulated polarized kernels. Their
source-derived Mellin finite part canonically recovers
\(\zeta(s+\bar w)\), so the exterior observer is regulator independent at
the scalar level.

The finite-part map does not preserve positivity. At \(s=w=0\), the
regulated Gaussian theta sum is positive, while Jacobi inversion gives the
canonical finite part

\[
\zeta(0)=-\frac12.
\]

Thus the boundary observer is not another positive Gram vector. Its faithful
carrier is a relative pair consisting of the positive regulated bulk and the
signed heat wall.

## Scope

This closes the positive-extension shortcut for the polarized zeta kernel.
It does not rule out a larger positive completed theta energy with reciprocal
sewing and all boundary currents retained.
