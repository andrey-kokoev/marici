# 3168 — The Tate Ratio and Product Are Birational Shadows of Two Faithful Quadratures

For the local amplitudes \(D_+,D_-\), the ratio and product

\[
\Gamma=\frac{D_+}{D_-},
\qquad
P=D_+D_-
\]

are only birational coordinates. At \(D_+=0\), every pair \((0,c)\) with
\(c\neq0\) maps to \((P,\Gamma)=(0,0)\).

The faithful coordinates are

\[
T=D_++D_-,
\qquad
A=D_+-D_-,
\]

which reconstruct \(D_\pm=(T\pm A)/2\). Reciprocity makes \(T\) even and
\(A\) odd; on the seam they are the two real amplitude quadratures.

## Scope

All determinant, Green, and Clifford comparisons relevant to zero confinement
must be constructed before ratio, product, or scalar compression.
