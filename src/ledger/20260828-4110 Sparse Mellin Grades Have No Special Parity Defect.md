# Sparse Mellin Grades Have No Special Parity Defect

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

For distinct positive scales \(\lambda_i\) and distinct Mellin grades
\(k_j\), the matrix \(V_{ij}=\lambda_i^{k_j}\) is a generalized
Vandermonde matrix with rank \(\min(m,r)\). Each restored grade removes
exactly one blind dimension. Consecutive and parity-decimated grade sets of
the same size have the same rank.

Thus the magnetic collapse under restoration of odd mediators does not
transfer literally. Primitive and square ports remain finite-rank views of
an arbitrarily large valuation fiber. Faithfulness requires a complete tower,
a source-derived recurrence quotient, or a nonlocal all-label operator.
