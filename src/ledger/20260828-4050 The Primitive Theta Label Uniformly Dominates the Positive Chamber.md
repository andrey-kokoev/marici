# The Primitive Theta Label Uniformly Dominates the Positive Chamber

For \(y=e^{2u}\ge1\),

\[
\frac{\phi_n(u)}{\phi_1(u)}
=
n^2
\frac{2\pi n^2y-3}{2\pi y-3}
e^{-\pi(n^2-1)y}.
\]

Every ratio with \(n\ge2\) decreases strictly with \(y\), and

\[
\sum_{n\ge2}\phi_n(u)
<
0.006001\,\phi_1(u)
\qquad
(u\ge0).
\]

The primitive label therefore supplies more than \(99.4\%\) of the completed
positive-chamber source pointwise.

This makes primitive-plus-tail perturbation canonical. It does not itself
exclude transform collisions, because oscillatory integration does not
preserve pointwise dominance. The remaining requirement is a primitive
transversality margin in a norm that also controls the tail.
