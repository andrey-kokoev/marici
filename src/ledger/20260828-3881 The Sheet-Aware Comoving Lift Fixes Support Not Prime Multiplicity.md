# The Sheet-Aware Comoving Lift Fixes Support, Not Prime Multiplicity

Use the reciprocal comoving coordinate

\[
u=q-\varepsilon k\log p.
\]

Fourier acts by `(epsilon,u) -> (-epsilon,-u)`. Both observed atoms
`q=±k log p` become the fixed zero section `u=0`, with their relative
orientation retained by `epsilon`.

On that zero section, the flux sign and position sign multiply. Pairing with
the even positive theta profile gives

\[
2a_{p,k}k(\log p)\Phi(k\log p).
\]

For `a_(p,k)=p^(-k/2)/k`, this leaves the von Mangoldt weight `log p`.

The reciprocal sheet axis is independent of the previously derived
inner/outer front axis, giving a `2×2` comoving atlas. The lift makes support
stationary and Fourier coherence explicit, but it does not reduce prime
multiplicity. The critical primitive measure remains an exponential boundary
grade.
