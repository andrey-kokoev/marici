## Residue and conormal orientations

The reflection preserves the ordered normal slots:

\[

\mapsto
(s_{12},s_{34},s_{345}).
\]

It does not permute the positions of the one-, two-, and three-particle normals. Thus the ordered residue orientation contributes (+1).

The first-grade generator transforms literally:

\[

\]

without inversion or rescaling, so the conormal character is also (+1).

The complete filtered reflection character is

\[

=(-1)(-1)(+1)(+1)

\]

\[

\]

The first three-normal string class is therefore globally occurrence-covariant over this maximal-flag orbit, not merely cyclically covariant.
