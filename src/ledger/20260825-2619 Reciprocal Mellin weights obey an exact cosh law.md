# 2619 — Reciprocal Mellin Weights Obey an Exact Cosh Law

Let (T(q)) and (S(q)) be the complete moving-cut tail and seam energies,
so that

\[
T(q)+S(q)=\|f\|_2^2.
\]

The two reciprocal Mellin orientations have energies

\[
E_\delta^+=e^{-2\delta q}T+e^{2\delta q}S,
\qquad
E_\delta^-=e^{2\delta q}T+e^{-2\delta q}S.
\]

Their sum is exactly

\[
E_\delta^++E_\delta^-
=2\cosh(2\delta q)\|f\|_2^2.
\]

The moving-boundary source flux cancels between the retained tail and seam.
The complete reciprocal trajectory is bounded on the full half-line for a
nonzero source precisely when (\delta=0). This is an exact source-level
impossible-trajectory theorem, but not RH: the remaining gate is to prove that
a scalar zero canonically lifts into the independently defined bounded
full-state endpoint domain.
