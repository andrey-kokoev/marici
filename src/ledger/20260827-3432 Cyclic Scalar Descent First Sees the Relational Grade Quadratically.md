# 3432 — Cyclic Scalar Descent First Sees the Relational Grade Quadratically

## Deutschian prediction

Let the labelled relational sector be the rational (A_2) representation of
the cyclic occurrence group (C_3). If a scalar readout is analytic at the
zero relational class and introduces no asymmetric probe, then its linear
term vanishes. Its first possible nonconstant term is quadratic and is
proportional to the unique cyclic-invariant Cartan, equivalently Clifford,
norm on (A_2).

This is hard to vary: changing the conclusion requires either breaking cyclic
descent, adding an independently typed probe, or changing the representation.

## Finite falsifiers

The prediction fails if any of the following occurs:

1. the invariant dual ((A_2^*)^{C_3}) is nonzero;
2. the invariant symmetric-square space
   ((\operatorname{Sym}^2 A_2^*)^{C_3}) has dimension other than one;
3. the source cyclic observer fails to recover that quadratic norm;
4. an anisotropic quadratic form survives cyclic transport.

## Result and scope

All four falsifiers are rejected. The first cyclic scalar channel available
to the relational occurrence sector is uniquely quadratic.

This does not prove that the physical cosmological observable has a nonzero
coefficient in this channel. It proves the allowed form of its first possible
source-natural scalar response. The next physical falsifier is to derive the
source-normalized second response and determine whether its coefficient is
nonzero; it must not be inferred from this invariant census.

The result also does not identify this norm with the three-site quartic
(\mathcal Q) or with the second total-energy Rees grade. Such an identification
requires a typed source map.
