For

\[
\Xi(z)=\frac12+
\frac12\left(z^2-\frac14\right)K(z),
\]

every reciprocal-even entire factor \(H\) normalized by
\(H(\pm1/2)=1\) has the hostile tail lift

\[
K_H
=
HK+
\frac{H-1}{z^2-1/4}.
\]

The quotient is entire and even, so \((K_H,K_H')\) has the same parity typing
as \((K,K')\) while \(H\) may insert an off-seam zero quartet.  Therefore the
even--odd first jet restores orientation but not source authority.

The surviving hierarchy is scalar, parity pair, positive cosh-measure lift,
then labelled modular lift.  Earlier positive-source hostiles leave only the
last as a plausible divisor-protection mechanism.
