# The Coincidence Obstruction Opens an Infinite Sobolev Jet Tower

The high-frequency obstruction is the first member of a source boundary
tower. For

\[
W_x(d)=A(d)e^{-d/2}\frac{\sinh(xd)}{x},
\]

direct integration by parts gives

\[
W_x'(0)=A(0),
\qquad
W_x''(0)=-f(0)^2,
\]

and

\[
W_x'''(0)
=A(0)\left(x^2+\frac34\right)
-3f(0)f'(0)
-3\int_0^\infty e^{-q}f'(q)^2\,dq.
\]

Repeated Fourier integration by parts reads successive odd endpoint jets as
inverse even powers of frequency. A single coincidence counterterm therefore
does not close the defect: it exposes the next weighted Sobolev channel. The
faithful candidate is the full source jet or graph-norm tower. The next
falsifiable question is whether theta's heat equation closes that tower to a
finite labelled recurrence.
