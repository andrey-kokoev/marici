# The Autocorrelation Orientation Is Forced Negative at High Frequency

For the reciprocal Green reduction, put

\[
W_x(d)=A_f(d)e^{-d/2}\frac{\sinh(xd)}{x},
\qquad
A_f(d)=\int_0^\infty e^{-q}f(q)f(q+d)\,dq.
\]

The coincidence germ obeys (W_x(0)=0) and (W_x'(0)=A_f(0)>0). Two
integrations by parts and the Riemann--Lebesgue lemma give

\[
\int_0^\infty W_x(d)\cos(yd)\,dd
=-\frac{A_f(0)}{y^2}+o(y^{-2}).
\]

Therefore the proposed nonnegative orientation fails for the actual smooth
positive theta forcing at every fixed nonzero (x) and sufficiently large
|(y)|. This does not produce an off-line zero. It closes the scalar
cosine-orientation route and locates the obstruction in the first-order
coincidence germ. A surviving construction must pair that germ in a typed
relative channel before scalar Fourier compression.
