# 1654 — Cumulant Cut Sewing Has a Finite Species Presentation

## Question

Entry 1653 shows that exact conditional Cut sewing survives the first non-Gaussian cumulant grade. Determine whether higher cumulants require independent coherence rules at every arity.

## Partition incidence calculus

For a labelled finite set \(S\),

\[
m_S
=
\sum_{\pi\in\Pi(S)}
\prod_{B\in\pi}\kappa_B,
\]

where \(\Pi(S)\) is the partition lattice and \(\kappa_B\) is the connected cumulant on block \(B\).

Nested Cut/restriction operations are organized by refinement chains

\[
\pi\le\sigma\le\tau.
\]

The two coproduct parenthesizations enumerate the same chains because partition refinement is transitive. Hence the incidence coproduct is coassociative without an arity-specific repair.

The checker verifies Bell counts through arity eight and both parenthesizations across 14,400 refinement chains through arity six.

## Finite rule versus infinite rank

The entire assembly is governed by one species-level rule:

\[
\boxed{
\text{moments}=\operatorname{SET}(\text{connected cumulants}).
}
\]

But an arbitrary state may carry an independent connected cumulant at every arity. Therefore the coefficient object is not finite rank and is not generated by finitely many fixed-arity cumulant coordinates.

## Narrow result

\[
\boxed{
\text{Cumulant Cut sewing is infinitely graded but finitely specified by the partition-incidence coalgebra.}
}
\]

No new coherence generator is required at each grade. The infinite tower is compressed by an arity-uniform labelled rule, exactly as Entry 1649 compresses the infinite evolution hierarchy by a finite differential presentation.

The combined coefficient architecture is therefore

\[
\text{finite differential rule}
+
\text{finite species/coalgebra rule}
\]

acting on an infinite filtered process module.

This is sector-specific coefficient structure over the existing carrier; it is not a new cosmological carrier primitive.
