# Full Labelled Poisson Sewing Rigidly Selects the Unweighted Integer Comb

Grothendieck classified the weighted square-spectrum source cone under the
complete Fourier–Poisson correspondence.

Let

\[
\mu_w=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}w_n\delta_n
\]

be an order-zero tempered measure. If

\[
\mathcal F\mu_w=c\mu_w
\]

for nonzero (c), then all weights are equal.

Indeed, the Fourier transform of an integer-supported distribution is
one-periodic. The sewing identity therefore makes (mu_w) one-periodic.
Comparing its atoms after translation by one gives (w_{n+1}=w_n).

Thus full labelled Poisson sewing rejects every nonconstant weighted
square-spectrum hostile before completion.
