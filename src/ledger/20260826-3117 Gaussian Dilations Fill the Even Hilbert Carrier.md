# 3117 — Gaussian Dilations Fill the Even Hilbert Carrier

In logarithmic coordinates, Gaussian dilations become translations of

\[
w(q)=e^{q/2}e^{-\pi e^{2q}}.
\]

Its Fourier–Mellin multiplier is

\[
\widehat w(\xi)=
\frac12\pi^{-1/4+i\xi/2}
\Gamma\left(\frac14-\frac{i\xi}{2}\right),
\]

which is nonzero for every real ξ. Therefore the Gaussian dilation orbit is
cyclic and its ordinary (L^2) closure is the entire even carrier.

Finite Gaussian packets are analytic, but their Hilbert completion contains
moment-invisible even Schwartz states. Gaussian provenance therefore does not
make moment compression faithful after completion. A quasianalytic rescue
would require a strictly finer source-derived topology with every boundary
constructor proved continuous.

## Scope

constructed finer quasianalytic completion.
