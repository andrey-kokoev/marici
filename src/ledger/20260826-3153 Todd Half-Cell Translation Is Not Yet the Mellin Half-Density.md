# 3153 — Todd Half-Cell Translation Is Not Yet the Mellin Half-Density

The formal Todd half-shift and the spectral Mellin half-shift have different
operator typings. Euler–Maclaurin realizes the Todd variable by \(D_x\), so
\(e^{D_x/2}\) is additive half-cell translation. Mellin shift by \(1/2\) is
multiplication by \(x^{1/2}\), generated by \(\log x\).

Their commutator is

\[
[D_x,\log x]=\frac1x.
\]

Thus their shared coefficient \(1/2\) does not authorize their
identification.
