# Mellin Factorization Separates the Current from the Arithmetic Divisor

The Fourier-fixed frontier current has Mellin transform

\[
2\pi\mathcal M F(s)
=
\frac{s(s-1)}2
\pi^{-s/2}
\Gamma\left(\frac{s}{2}\right).
\]

Integer lattice sampling contributes

\[
\sum_{n\geq1}n^{-s}=\zeta(s).
\]

Their product is exactly

\[
\xi(s)=2\pi\mathcal M F(s)\zeta(s).
\]

The current factor has no zeros in the open critical strip. Thus every
nontrivial divisor event belongs to the arithmetic sampling correspondence;
the current supplies completion, boundary polynomial, and reciprocal
covariance.

RH cannot follow from the local current alone. The remaining theorem must
govern its interaction with the integer sampling comb.
