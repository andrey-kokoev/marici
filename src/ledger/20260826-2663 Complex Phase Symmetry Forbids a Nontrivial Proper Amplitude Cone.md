# 2663 — Complex Phase Symmetry Forbids a Nontrivial Proper Amplitude Cone

Any cone on a complex amplitude space that is invariant under the full phase
action is invariant under (-1). It therefore contains (x) and (-x) and
cannot be pointed unless it is the zero cone. Positivity on amplitudes requires
a source-derived real form or phase frame.

The phase-invariant density lift (q(\psi)=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|) lands in
a proper operator cone but changes the state type. It is nonlinear,
phase-blind, and loses interference unless the full mixed sesquilinear kernel
is retained. Applied downstream to (m(s)\psi), it merely replaces scalar
nonvanishing by positivity of (|m(s)|^2) and is circular.
