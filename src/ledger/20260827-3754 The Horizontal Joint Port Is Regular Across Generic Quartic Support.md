## Exact binary-quartic reduction

In the bivariate chart

\[
y=\frac{u+v}{2}-1,
\qquad
z=\frac{u-v}{2},
\]

\[
F=t^4-(1+y^2-z^2)t^2+y^2.
\]

Direct Griffiths reduction in the basis \((h_1,h_2)\) gives

\[
A_{E,u}=
\begin{pmatrix}
-\dfrac{u-1}{2u(u-2)}&
\dfrac{1}{u(u-2)(u+v-2)}\\[2mm]
-\dfrac{u+v-2}{4u(u-2)}&
\dfrac{u-1}{2u(u-2)}
\end{pmatrix},
\]

\[
A_{E,v}=
\begin{pmatrix}
-\dfrac{v-1}{2v(v-2)}&
\dfrac{1}{v(v-2)(u+v-2)}\\[2mm]
-\dfrac{u+v-2}{4v(v-2)}&
\dfrac{v-1}{2v(v-2)}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

They satisfy the exact flatness identity

\[
\partial_vA_{E,u}-\partial_uA_{E,v}
+[A_{E,u},A_{E,v}]=0.
\]
