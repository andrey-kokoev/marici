# 2854 — A Scalar Zero Is a Completion-Class Jump of the Prime Boundary Current

Every finite two-prime boundary-cocycle square closes exactly. At infinite
prime completion, however, the reciprocal depth-(k) current has leading term

\[
C_{k,p}(z)=p^{-k/2}X(z)+R_{k,p}(z),
\]

where the prime sum of (R_{k,p}) converges absolutely.

Thus the primitive and prime-square currents diverge when (X(z)\ne0), while
both become summable exactly when (X(z)=0). The connected (k\ge3) tail is
always summable.

A scalar zero is therefore an exact completion-class transition of the global
boundary current. The remaining RH question is why this regularity upgrade
could occur only at the unitary Tate seam.
