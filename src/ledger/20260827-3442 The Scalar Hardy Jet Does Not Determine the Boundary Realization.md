but it does not reconstruct the boundary operator producing that scalar.

\[

\begin{pmatrix}

remaining constructor is an operator-valued Schur or Rosenbrock jet in a
declared norm or graph topology, followed by a separate uniform resolvent or
least-singular-value certificate.
