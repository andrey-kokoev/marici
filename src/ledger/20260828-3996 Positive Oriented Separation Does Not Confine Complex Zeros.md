# Positive Absolute Separation Does Not Confine Complex Zeros

Grothendieck reduced the Green residual to the hyperbolic-sine
transform of a positive absolute-separation measure:

\[
K_f(z)=\iint f(q)f(v)\sinh\!\bigl(z|v-q|\bigr)\,dq\,dv.
\]

This explains real-axis positivity but does not orient the complex plane. The
positive three-atom source

\[
f=\delta_0+2\delta_1+\delta_2
\]

gives

\[
K_f(z)=8\sinh z+2\sinh(2z)
=4\sinh z\,(2+\cosh z),
\]

with off-seam zeros

\[
z=\pm\operatorname{arcosh}(2)+(2k+1)\pi i.
\]

Simultaneous reciprocal reflection reverses both source order and signed
separation, so the integrand is invariant. This is an exchange-even
autocorrelation separation port; reciprocal symmetry does not confine its
zeros.

The next admissible theorem must constrain the theta separation measure by a
labelled heat, Poisson, or arithmetic-scale law that rejects this hostile
source before its zeros are examined.
