# 2886 — The Prime Staircase Is a Logarithmic Chart, Not a Zero-State

At finite cutoff, the nonzero Euler tensor product satisfies

\[
\log Z_S=\sum_{p\in S,k\geq1}\frac1k p^{-ks},
\qquad
-\frac{Z_S'}{Z_S}=\sum_{p\in S,k\geq1}(\log p)p^{-ks}.
\]

Thus the additive staircase is the connected logarithmic coordinate of the
multiplicative prime object. At a completed zero this chart is singular, so it
cannot itself define a regular zero-state without importing divisor data. A
boundary-bearing determinant or relative section must precede logarithmic
compression.
