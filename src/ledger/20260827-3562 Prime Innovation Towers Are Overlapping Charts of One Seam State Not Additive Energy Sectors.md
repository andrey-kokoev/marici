# 3562 — Prime Innovation Towers Are Overlapping Charts of One Seam State, Not Additive Energy Sectors

For every prime \(p\), its cumulative innovation transform is already an
isometry of the complete seam state:

\[
\|\mathcal B_pg\|^2=\|g\|^2.
\]

Thus adding the energies of the \(p\)- and \(q\)-charts gives
\(2\|g\|^2\): it duplicates one state rather than assembling independent
sectors.

At finite height, the sorted union of all prime-power boundaries gives a
canonical common refinement. Every prime chart is recovered by isometric
merging of its refinement cells.

Therefore global arithmetic assembly is a gluing problem. The missing
constructor is the mixed-prime overlap incidence that orients moving-seam
window increments on the common refinement. A naive Euler sum over prime
boundary energies is ill-typed.
