Strict theta decrease produces the positive cutoff measure

\[
d\mu_L(t)=-\Phi'(t)\,dt+\Phi(L)\delta_L(dt)
\]

and the exact factorization

\[
izH_L(z)=\int_{(0,L]}(e^{izt}-1)\,d\mu_L(t).
\]

It follows that \(H_L\) is zero-free in the upper half-plane.  On the positive
real axis,

\[
-xS_L(x)=\int_{(0,L]}(\cos(xt)-1)\,d\mu_L(t)<0,
\]

so \(S_L(x)>0\) and the half-transform never passes through the origin.

This repairs the previous overstrong statement: successive simple cosine
zeros alternate crossing direction, so no common sign of the phase velocity
should be demanded.  The exact remaining gate is only

\[
M_L(x)=\int_0^L u\Phi(u)\sin(xu)\,du\ne0
\]

whenever the cosine integral vanishes.
