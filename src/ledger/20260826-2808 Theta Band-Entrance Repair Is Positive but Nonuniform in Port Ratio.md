# 2808 — Theta Band-Entrance Repair Is Positive but Nonuniform in Port Ratio

At the entrance of a canonical band,

\[
R(D)
=
\beta\left[J_a(0)-J_a(L)\right]
-
\alpha bL W_a(L)D
+
O(\beta D+\alpha D^2).
\]

The first coefficient is strictly positive, so it repairs the negative
weighted term for fixed \(\alpha,\beta>0\). The protected interval shrinks
with \(\beta/\alpha\), however, and pointwise positivity fails immediately on
the face \(\beta=0\).

Thus no uniform pointwise conditional-band theorem follows for independent
closed-quadrant coefficients. The minimal survivor is the canonical
block-integrated inequality or a source-derived relation between the ports.
