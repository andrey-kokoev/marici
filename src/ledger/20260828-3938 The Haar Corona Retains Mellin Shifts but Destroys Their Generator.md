# The Haar Corona Retains Mellin Shifts but Destroys Their Generator

In the GNS representation of the prime-harmonic Haar state,

\[
\langle U_t\Omega,U_u\Omega\rangle=\delta_{t,u}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\|U_t\Omega-\Omega\|=\sqrt2
\]

for every nonzero \(t\), however small.  The corona retains each Mellin shift
as a unitary but represents the parameter group as \(\mathbb R_d\).  The
action is not strongly continuous and has no self-adjoint infinitesimal
generator.

This is the first exact boundary-module failure.  Clark differentiation,
gamma currents, and logarithmic label observables cannot descend continuously
to the bare Haar corona.  Prime-harmonic completion preserves transported
states but erases the first-order germ relating nearby spectral heights.

The required additional channel must restore a closable vertical derivation,
likely through the primitive logarithmic current or Mertens finite-part graph
topology, without changing the Haar values of finite shifts.
