# 3648 — The Soft-Signed Endpoint Corners Are A3 Germs with an Unselected Odd Line

## Two reciprocal corners

Use

\[
c=z^2-(x-y)^2.
\]

Near the infinity endpoint, with \(s=1/t\) and
\(\bar W=Ws^2\), the family is

\[
\bar W^2=(x-ys^2)^2+cs^2.
\]

At \(x=c=0\), with generic \(y\ne0\), this becomes

\[
\bar W^2=y^2s^4.
\]

It is an \(A_3\) tacnode. The reciprocal finite-endpoint corner
\(y=c=0\), with generic \(x\ne0\), has the same type:

\[
W^2=x^2t^4.
\]

## Milnor algebra

At the infinity corner the Jacobian ideal is generated by

\[
\bar W,qquad s^3.
\]

The Milnor algebra therefore has basis

\[
1,quad s,quad s^2
\]

and rank three.

## Source-accessible grades

For

\[
H=\bar W^2-(x-ys^2)^2-cs^2,
\]

the first derivatives at the corner are

\[
\left.\partial_xH\right|_0=2ys^2,
\qquad
\left.\partial_cH\right|_0=-s^2.
\]

Thus the soft and signed-energy first-normal directions span the same Milnor
line. Their joint first-order rank is one.

The second soft grade gives

\[
\left.\partial_x^2H\right|_0=-2,
\]

so the source-accessible span through second order is

\[
\langle1,s^2\rangle.
\]

It has rank two. The remaining line \(\langle s\rangle\) is odd under endpoint
reflection \(s\mapsto-s\). The complete frozen family is even in \(s\), so no
source deformation grade selects that line.

## Classification

The deeper corners require no new carrier stratum: they are intersections of
existing soft and signed-energy support. Their coefficient germ is rank-three
\(A_3\), while the frozen source accesses exactly its even rank-two grade.

The odd Milnor line is algebraically present but physically unselected. It may
be activated only by an independently derived orientation-sensitive boundary
or relative-chain operation. It must not be added merely to complete the
Milnor rank.
