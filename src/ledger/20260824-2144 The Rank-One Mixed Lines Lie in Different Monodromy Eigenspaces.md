# 2144 — The Rank-One Mixed Lines Lie in Different Monodromy Eigenspaces

## Hard-to-vary claim

Entry 2143 removes the rank obstruction between the contact-product mixed
variation and the lower square-free line. A monodromy-character obstruction
nevertheless remains.

## Contact-product mixed line

The mixed variation image is generated by

\[
V_iV_j(\tau_i\tau_j)=1.
\]

It is invariant under both labelled loops, so its character is

\[
\chi_{\rm contact}=(+1,+1).
\]

## Lower Kummer mixed line

The lower line is generated by

\[
\sqrt{\nu_i\nu_j}.
\]

It changes sign around either wall:

\[
\chi_{\rm lower}=(-1,-1).
\]

For a scalar horizontal comparison \(h\), equivariance requires

\[
(-1)h=h(+1)
\]

for each labelled loop. Over characteristic zero this forces \(h=0\).
Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Hom}_{\pi_1}
(L_{\rm contact}^{\rm mix},L_{\rm lower})=0.
}
\]

## Minimal missing datum

The unique character correction is

\[
\mathcal K_{(-,-)}
=
\mathcal K_{\nu_i^{1/2}}\otimes
\mathcal K_{\nu_j^{1/2}}.
\]

After this twist the rank-one characters agree. But Entries 2140--2141
already show that character matching does not itself produce a
source-normalized map, and that the dashed integral Kummer factors cannot
supply the half-integral normal twist.

## Narrow conclusion

\[
\boxed{
\text{the source product creates the correct mixed rank but not the lower
Kummer eigencharacter.}
}
\]

The corrected comparison is therefore neither identified nor rank-obstructed:
it is **twist-obstructed**. A Mellin, Leray, orientation, or ramified
specialization functor could bridge the lines only if the frozen source
independently supplies the labelled half-Kummer character.
