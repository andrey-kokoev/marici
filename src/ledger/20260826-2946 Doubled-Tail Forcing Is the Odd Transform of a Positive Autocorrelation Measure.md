The remaining Green forcing has the exact source factorization

\[
\mathcal K(z)
=
\int_0^\infty\rho(d)\sinh(zd)\,dd,
\]

where

\[
\rho(d)
=
2\int_0^\infty f(q)f(q+d)\,dq
\ge0.
\]

For \(z=\sigma+it\), its real part is the oscillatory transform

\[
\Re\mathcal K(z)
=
\int_0^\infty
\rho(d)\sinh(\sigma d)\cos(td)
\,dd.
\]

Every off-seam zero must satisfy
\(\sigma\Re\mathcal K(z)<0\).  Thus the remaining RH-bearing theorem is an
orientation law for this positive theta autocorrelation measure under the
simultaneous theta-zero constraint.  Positivity of the measure alone is
insufficient because of the cosine oscillation.
