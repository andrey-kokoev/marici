linear state coordinate with a scalar metric can carry it, because such a
pullback has rank at most one.

determinant-like relation

\[
\omega_a(x,y)=x^*\Delta_a y.

an evaluated current, a determinant line stores oriented pair data, and an
ordered operator carrier retains the full jet and constructor order.

The primitive anomaly line is therefore admissible as a relation codomain,
not automatically as a replacement state carrier.
