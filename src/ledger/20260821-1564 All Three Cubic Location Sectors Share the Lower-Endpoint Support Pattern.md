# 1564 — All Three Cubic Location Sectors Share the Lower-Endpoint Support Pattern

## Question

Is Entry 1563's split between lower-endpoint and observation-endpoint support
specific to the bulk--bulk sector, or is it common to all three cubic vertex
locations?

## Calculation

The same source-normalized Laurent route algebra was evaluated for:

\[
BB,\qquad BS,\qquad SS.
\]

The bulk--bulk and mixed time integrals use the endpoint-primitive recurrence
verified before Entry 1563. The surface--surface sector has no remaining time
integral and was expanded directly. Every grade-two output reproduces the
previous certified census.

## Common lower-endpoint pattern

For every location sector,

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{normal grade}&\text{lower-endpoint support}\\
\hline
2&\{0,\pm2p\}\\
1&\{\pm2p\}\\
0&\{0,\pm2p\}.
\end{array}}
\]

Thus lowering the normal grade does not add a new lower-endpoint frequency
letter.

## Observation-endpoint column

The sectors containing a bulk vertex also contain coefficient-valued terms
supported at

\[
\omega=\pm(p+q+k)
\]

at grades one and zero:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
BB&\pm(p+q+k)\\
BS&\pm(p+q+k)\\
SS&\varnothing.
\end{array}
\]

Their absence from \(SS\) is forced: two initial-surface vertices leave no
observation-endpoint primitive. Their absence at grade two is likewise forced
by degree.

## Result

The three-sector support is generated by two already declared endpoint
operations:

\[
\boxed{
\text{initial endpoint}:\{0,\pm2p\},
\qquad
\text{observation endpoint}:\{\pm(p+q+k)\}.
}
\]

No additional Carrier wall or incidence generator is required. The new
total-energy letters are coefficient data attached to the observation
endpoint and occur precisely when an unevaluated bulk primitive remains.

This is a support theorem only. The next finite test is whether the labelled
\(BB\) and \(BS\) observation-endpoint primitive classes cancel, combine into
a canonical period, or leave a residual extension after source-normalized
reduction.
