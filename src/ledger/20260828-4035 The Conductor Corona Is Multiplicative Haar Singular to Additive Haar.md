# The Conductor Corona Is Multiplicative Haar Singular to Additive Haar

The mean-one sieve densities

\[
W_y
=
\prod_{p\le y}
\frac{\mathbf 1_{x_p\in\mathbb Z_p^\times}}{1-p^{-1}}
\]

form a positive martingale under additive Haar measure on
\(\widehat{\mathbb Z}\). They converge almost surely to zero while keeping
expectation one, so they are not uniformly integrable.

The measures \(W_y d\mu_+\) nevertheless have a projective limit:
multiplicative Haar measure on
\(\widehat{\mathbb Z}^\times=\prod_p\mathbb Z_p^\times\). This unit
group has additive Haar measure zero. Therefore the limiting multiplicative
measure is singular to the additive carrier.

The conductor corona is exactly this multiplicative idelic boundary. The RH
comparison must be a relative measure-class correspondence coupled to the
archimedean half-density, not a bounded map inside additive \(L^2\).
