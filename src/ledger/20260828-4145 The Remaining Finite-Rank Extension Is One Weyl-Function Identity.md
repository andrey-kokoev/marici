# The Remaining Finite-Rank Extension Is One Weyl-Function Identity

The reciprocal Dirac principal operator with the Evans maximal-isotropic
boundary is self-adjoint. Its smallest symmetric source completion is

$$
\mathcal A=\begin{pmatrix}A_0&V\\V^*&d\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Schur elimination gives

$$
D(\lambda)=d-\lambda-V^*(A_0-\lambda)^{-1}V.
$$

Self-adjointness confines its spectral events to real $\lambda$, hence to the
critical seam. The entire missing bridge is the source-derived identity

$$
\Xi(1/2+i\lambda)=u(\lambda)D(\lambda),
\qquad u(\lambda)\ne0,
$$

with both zero directions, multiplicities, boundary modes, and completion.
Choosing $V$, $d$, or $u$ after inspecting Xi is circular. The primitive,
square, seam, and archimedean constructors must derive them before scalar
compression.
