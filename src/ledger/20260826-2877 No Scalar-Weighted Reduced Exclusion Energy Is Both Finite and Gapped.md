# 2877 — No Scalar-Weighted Reduced Exclusion Energy Is Both Finite and Gapped

After removing the shared vacuum projection, a weighted exclusion energy has
eigenvalues

\[
\lambda_n=\sum_{p\nmid n}w_p.
\]

Nonsummable weights give infinite energy to every nonvacuum basis label.
Summable weights admit primorial labels with \(\lambda_n\to0\). Finite-support
weights have a nontrivial kernel. Hence no scalar weighting is simultaneously
finite, jointly faithful, and gapped modulo the vacuum. The exclusion profile
must remain port-valued or acquire an independently derived coupling to
logarithmic degree.
