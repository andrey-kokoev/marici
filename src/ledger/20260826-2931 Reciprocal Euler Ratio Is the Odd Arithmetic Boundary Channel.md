For \(s=1/2+z\), the direct/dual local determinant ratio is

\[
\gamma_p(z)
=
\frac{1-p^{-1/2}e^{-z\log p}}
{1-p^{-1/2}e^{z\log p}},
\]

with

\[
\log\gamma_p(z)
=
2\sum_{k\ge1}
\frac{p^{-k/2}}{k}
\sinh(kz\log p).
\]

This is the canonical odd arithmetic channel paired with the antisymmetric
archimedean boundary coordinate \(z\).  Its first two cumulants are the
polarized primitive and square currents; its connected \(k\ge3\) product
converges absolutely for \(|\Re z|<1/6\).

However, every reciprocal-even hostile factor cancels from the transition
ratio.  The sewing law explains the sector geometry but cannot protect the
section divisor.  Canonical-section rigidity remains the missing theorem.
