# 2881 — The Canonical Quarter-Density Lift Recovers the Rigging, Not Orientation

The required quarter-density is canonically the square root of the positive
prime-power measure. Its coordinates are

\[
\sqrt{\log p}\,p^{-k/4}
\]

for logarithmic-derivative normalization and \(k^{-1/2}p^{-k/4}\) for
Euler-log normalization. It reproduces the exact positive curvature
coefficients and the primitive/square/tail completion filtration. But any
hostile source with the same positive arithmetic measure inherits it. The
missing datum is a source-specific reciprocal cross-term, not the diagonal
half-form norm.
