# 2746 — Cayley Crossing Form Orients Full Weyl Rank, Not Cross-Transfer Zeros

The finite theta/Tate two-port carrier canonically supplies the Hermitian Weyl
path

\[
W(\lambda)=A_\partial-B^*(A-\lambda I)^{-1}B.
\]

On real resolvent intervals, \(W'\le0\). Its Cayley path therefore has the
positive crossing form

\[
iU^*U'
=
-2(W-iI)^{-1}W'(W+iI)^{-1}
\ge0.
\]

This is a genuine source-derived positivity theorem. But it detects rank loss
of the full Weyl matrix, hence zeros of \(\det W\). The theta observable is the
off-diagonal source-to-endpoint entry \(W_{f0}\), which may vanish while
\(\det W\ne0\).

Therefore primitive, square, archimedean, and pole-current matching for the
Cayley trace density would orient the wrong divisor. A source-derived
lower-minor or Pluecker relation must precede any such promotion.
