# 835 — Full-Radial Tangency Is an Unsplit Cancellation of External and Fiber Normal Defects

## Euler decomposition

Write the full radial Euler field as

\[
R=R_{\rm ext}+R_{\rm fib},
\]

where

\[
R_{\rm ext}=E\partial_E+\sum_iP_i\partial_{P_i},
\qquad
R_{\rm fib}=a\partial_a+b\partial_b.
\]

Every one of the 22 source monomials of \(K_{\rm CM}\) has total degree
six, so

\[
RK_{\rm CM}=6K_{\rm CM}.
\]

Neither summand is separately an Euler field for the polynomial. The exact
termwise identity is

\[
(\deg_{\rm fib}-6)+\deg_{\rm ext}=0.
\]

Therefore, on the branch divisor,

\[
\boxed{
R_{\rm ext}(K_{\rm CM})\big|_{K=0}
=-R_{\rm fib}(K_{\rm CM})\big|_{K=0}.
}
\]

The two nonzero normal defects cancel only in their sum.

The full radial field is tangent to the projective Cayley--Menger branch,
but the external and fiber fields are not generically tangent separately.
Thus there is no canonical factorization

\[
\text{fiber Gysin}
\longrightarrow

their comparison cell. That cell is not an optional homotopy fitted after
restriction: it is forced by the identity

\[

\]
