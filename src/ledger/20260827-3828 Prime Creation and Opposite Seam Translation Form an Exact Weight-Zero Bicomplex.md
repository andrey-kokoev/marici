# Prime Creation and Opposite Seam Translation Form an Exact Weight-Zero Bicomplex

Fermionic prime creation `c_p` raises label energy by `log p`. Backward
translation of the seam coordinate,

\[
(R_pf)(q)=f(q+\log p),
\]

lowers position energy by the same amount. Therefore

\[
d_p=c_p\otimes R_p
\]

commutes with total label-position energy. Since the translations commute and
the creation operators anticommute, the finite sum `d=sum_p d_p` satisfies
`d^2=0`.

On the full line the complex is contractible. On the half-line, the shift is
only a coisometry and the contraction identity becomes

\[
dh_p+h_pd=1-(i_pc_p)\otimes P_{[0,\log p)}.
\]

Thus the moving prime seam is exactly the boundary defect of the weighted
cross-label complex. Prime-label and moving-window structures are two legs of
one Koszul-Toeplitz object.
