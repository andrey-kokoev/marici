# A Positive Seam-Matched Primitive Split

With

\[
q(u)=\frac{1-e^{-2u}}2,
\qquad
\beta=\frac{\phi_1'(0)}{\phi_1(0)},
\]

define

\[
R=\phi_1(1-\beta q),
\qquad
E=\tau+\beta q\phi_1.
\]

Then

\[
\Phi=R+E,
\qquad
R'(0)=E'(0)=0,
\]

and both pieces are positive. Moreover,

\[
0<E(u)<0.0292\,R(u)
\qquad
(u\ge0).
\]

This is the first positive seam-matched primitive decomposition: it retains
pointwise dominance while repairing the leading high-frequency boundary
type. The next audit is the third seam derivative, initiating a possible
odd-jet repair tower.
