# The Primitive and Square Currents Are the det3 Anomaly Coordinates

The common-factor ambiguity is removed by a relative determinant's source
law:

$$
\frac{d}{d\zeta}\log\Delta
=-\operatorname{Tr}\left((A-\zeta)^{-1}-(A_0-\zeta)^{-1}\right).
$$

A common factor preserving this identity has $h'/h=0$ and is constant.

The previously derived arithmetic filtration has an exact determinant
interpretation. For $K\in\mathcal S_3$,

$$
\log\det_3(I+K)
=\sum_{k\ge3}\frac{(-1)^{k+1}}k\operatorname{Tr}(K^k).
$$

The omitted $k=1$ and $k=2$ terms are precisely the primitive and square
boundary currents. The full typed determinant is therefore
$(J_1,J_2,\det_3)$. Retaining the first two currents fixes the regularization
anomaly and the actual section; the regularized determinant fixes the divisor.

The remaining gate is to construct the Schatten-three family $K(\zeta)$ from
theta/Tate incidence and prove that its source-normalized determinant section
is framed Xi.
