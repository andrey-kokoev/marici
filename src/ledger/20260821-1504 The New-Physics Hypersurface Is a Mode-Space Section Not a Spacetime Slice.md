# 1504 — The New-Physics Hypersurface Is a Mode-Space Section, Not a Spacetime Slice

## Status

Primary-source carrier-typing result. This tests the remaining candidate for turning a Planck cutoff into a preferred cosmological time.

## Frozen source

Danielsson imposes initial conditions separately for each Fourier mode when its physical momentum reaches a fixed high-energy scale \(\Lambda\). In de Sitter space,

\[
k=ap=-\frac{p}{\eta H}.
\]

Setting \(p=\Lambda\) gives [hep-th/0203198, Eqs. (30)–(31)]

\[
\boxed{
\eta_0(k)=-\frac{\Lambda}{Hk}.
}
\]

The source explicitly notes that \(\eta_0\) depends on \(k\). Its introductory description is still sharper: fluctuations are continuously created at the same minimum physical scale, with “no unique moment in time.”

## Carrier typing

Define the physical-momentum incidence

\[
\mathcal N_\Lambda
=
\{(k,\eta):k/a(\eta)=\Lambda\}.
\]

For each mode \(k\), \(\mathcal N_\Lambda\) selects one time \(\eta_0(k)\). It is therefore a graph or section over labelled mode space:

\[
k\longmapsto \eta_0(k).
\]

It is not a constant-time spacelike slice

\[
\Sigma_{\eta_*}=\{(x,\eta_*):x\in\mathbb R^3\}.
\]

Calling both objects “hypersurfaces” hides their different base categories:

\[
\boxed{
\text{NPH: incidence in mode--time space}
\neq
\text{BEFT boundary: spacetime initial slice}.
}
\]

The contrast is independently stated in Easther–Kinney–Peiris, arXiv:astro-ph/0505426: NPH normalizes modes when each first crosses a critical physical wavelength, whereas boundary EFT sets all modes on one common time surface.

## Consequence for Planck time

Taking \(\Lambda=M_{\rm Pl}\) fixes a Planck-length crossing condition for each mode, but does not produce a universal Planck time:

\[
\eta_{\rm Pl}(k)=-\frac{M_{\rm Pl}}{Hk}.
\]

Thus Planck **distance/momentum threshold** appears one structural step earlier than Planck **time** in this lane. Time is derived only after combining the threshold with a labelled mode and a background expansion law.

This corrects the provisional expectation that Planck time might manifest first. The primary source reverses that ordering.

## Classification

- Existing carrier data: labelled modes, background evolution, and the incidence \(k/a(\eta)=\Lambda\).
- Sector-specific coefficient data: the vacuum prescription attached at each incidence point.
- Not derived: one preferred spacetime boundary, a universal beginning, or a Big-Bang stratum.

No new cosmological carrier primitive is required. The NPH and common-boundary constructions are two different pullbacks from a larger mode–time incidence carrier; they must not be identified.

## Deutsch–Popperian conjecture

**Conjecture.** Trans-Planckian initial-state prescriptions factor through the incidence carrier

\[
\mathfrak I
=
\{(k,\eta,\Lambda):k=a(\eta)\Lambda\},
\]

with NPH obtained by fixing \(\Lambda\) and common-boundary EFT obtained by fixing \(\eta\). Their differing observables arise from inequivalent pullbacks and coefficient assignments, not from two definitions of the same hypersurface.

**Finite falsifier.** Construct a source-derived natural isomorphism between the fixed-\(\Lambda\) and fixed-\(\eta\) pullbacks that preserves labelled modes, locality, vacuum data, and power-spectrum transport. Failure at any one of these typed conditions preserves the distinction.
