# 3890 — The Minimal Faithful Conductor Map Satisfies the Generating Equivariance Squares

## Prediction

Entry 3888 derives the global transport group

\[
(\mathbb Z_2)^6\rtimes C_3.
\]

If that group acts on the specialization map rather than merely on branch

1. one deck-equivariance square;
2. one cyclic conjugation chain closing at order three.

Use the predeclared domain basis ([1],[u]). For a regular spectator factor

\[

\begin{pmatrix}

## Deck square

Let

\[
P=
\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Exact substitution gives

\[
J(h,-r)=P J(h,r).
\]

For the weighted collision lattice

\[
L(r)=
\begin{pmatrix}
\tfrac12&\tfrac12\\
\tfrac r2&-\tfrac r2
\end{pmatrix},
\]

one also has

\[
L(-r)J(h,-r)=L(r)J(h,r).
\]

Thus the collision matrix descends while retaining its value/derivative
filtration.

## Cyclic chain

Starting from the independently derived (A_0=G_{12}:g_1) spectator factor,
apply the source cyclic action to obtain (A_1) and (A_2). The three exact
squares commute and the third closes identically:

\[
\sigma J_{A_0}=J_{A_1},
\qquad
\sigma J_{A_1}=J_{A_2},
\qquad
\sigma J_{A_2}=J_{A_0}.
\]

No central multiplier is required; the strict (+1) orientation found in
Entry 3862 is preserved.
