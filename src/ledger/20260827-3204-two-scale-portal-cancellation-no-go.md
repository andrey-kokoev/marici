# 3204 — One Boundary Counterterm Cannot Hide Two RG Contexts

## Result

For leading-log transport (lambda_p(L)=a+bL) with source-generated (b>0),
two distinct scale contexts have response determinant (L_2-L_1). Optimizing
over every boundary value gives the exact residual floor

\[
\min_a\max
\left(|\lambda_p(L_1)|,|\lambda_p(L_2)|\right)
=\frac{b|L_2-L_1|}{2}.
\]

A boundary counterterm can hide the portal at one scale, not two.

## Limit

The contexts are source-generated by RG transport, but no two calibrated
same-operator interference measurements at distinct physical scales are
currently admitted. Complete running beyond leading log remains open.
