# 3403 — An Oriented Minimal Tadpole Can Select the Boundary Ratio

## Claim

For positive integer boundary charges with source-fixed total

\[
n_0+n_\pi=T
\]

and labelled orientation \(n_0>n_\pi\), the number of asymmetric ordered
partitions is

\[
N(T)=\left\lfloor\frac{T-1}{2}\right\rfloor.
\]

The fiber is a singleton exactly for \(T=3\) or \(T=4\). These sectors select
ratios \(2\) and \(3\), predicting portal contrasts \(9/50\) and \(8/25\).
At \(T=5\), the pairs \((3,2)\) and \((4,1)\) predict distinct contrasts
\(25/338\) and \(225/578\).

## Classification

Quantized boundary charge plus an independently fixed minimal total and
orientation is the first wall-branch candidate capable of genuine ratio
selection. Quantization alone generally produces only a finite fiber. No
admitted flavor compactification yet derives \(T=3\) or \(T=4\), and matching,
`physical16`, and detector survival remain open.
