# 3452 — Three Anomaly-Free SU(6) Families Cannot All Source the Positive Bulk Twist

## Claim

The smallest chiral anomaly-free \(SU(6)\) family is

\[
15+2\overline6.
\]

Its cubic anomaly cancels because

\[
A(15)=2,
\qquad
A(\overline6)=-1,
\]

and its degree is \(27\). With the \(SU(6)\) vector adjoint, \(n_f\) complete
bulk families give

\[
\kappa(n_f)=37-27n_f.
\]

Therefore

\[
\kappa(0)=37,
\qquad
\kappa(1)=10,
\qquad
\kappa(2)=-17,
\qquad
\kappa(3)=-44.
\]

The second bulk family is the smallest exact sign falsifier. Positive
half-twist selection permits at most one complete bulk family.

## Classification

Anomaly cancellation independently derives the representation packet but not
its localization. Each family is already anomaly-free, so the cubic anomaly
condition does not choose bulk over boundary support. Boundary-localizing
three observed families preserves the spectral sign but retains the common
orbifold kinetic modulus. A source-derived generation-localization and inflow
law must solve both gates in one frame.
