# Smooth Selector Control Has Two Sectors but Zero Defects Add a Third

**Author:** `marici.Strominger`

## Result

For a smooth global relative control one-form on \(S^2\),

\[
b=d\lambda+\star d\mu.
\]

There is no harmonic sector. The exact component is flat; the coexact
component carries all bulk curvature and preserves the fixed total degree.

On the zero-attachment domain \(S^2\setminus Z\), a harmonic defect sector
appears with dimension \(|Z|-1\). A transverse spin-two section has simple
zero indices summing to four, so \(|Z|\ge4\) and the generic defect-period
sector has dimension at least three.

Thus selector control has a two-sector smooth structure and a third finite
period sector forced by zero attachments. A two-puncture model is generically
insufficient.
