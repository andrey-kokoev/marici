# 2811 — The Conditional Residual Is Controlled by a Projective Port Ratio

For

\[
R(x;\alpha,\beta)=\beta J(x)-\alpha xK(x),
\]

the sign depends on \(r=\beta/\alpha\). Pointwise positivity on a block needs

\[
r>\sup xK/J,
\]

while source-weighted integrated positivity needs only

\[
r>\frac{\int wxK}{\int wJ}.
\]

For \(J=K=w=1\) on \([0,L]\), these thresholds are \(L\) and \(L/2\).
Integration can repair pointwise failure but cannot supply the port relation.
Uniform completion requires a source-fixed lower bound on \(\beta/\alpha\).
