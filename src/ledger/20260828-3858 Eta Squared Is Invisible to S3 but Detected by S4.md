# Eta Squared Is Invisible to S3 but Detected by S4

Strominger supplied an independent raw-braid nonidentity certificate for the
explicit representative \(W_{\eta^2}\). The braid acts trivially under the
Hurwitz action on every one of the \(6^4=1296\) tuples in \(S_3^4\), but acts
nontrivially on an explicit tuple in \(S_4^4\). The \(S_4\) witness proves that
the corresponding Artin free-group automorphism, and hence the braid in
\(P_4\), is nonidentity.

This separates three gates: Brunnian deletion, raw-braid nonidentity, and
order-two survival in the filling quotient. It also gives a finite example in
which a complete lower-resolution nonabelian probe is blind while the next
probe detects the obstruction.
