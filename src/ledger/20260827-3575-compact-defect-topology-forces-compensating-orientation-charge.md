# Compact Defect Topology Forces Compensating Orientation Charge

For a single-valued scalar on a compact boundaryless transverse space, the
oriented wall charges telescope:

\[
Q_{\rm tot}
=\sum_i\frac{\phi_{i+1}-\phi_i}{2v}
=0.
\]

Consequently every kink has compensating antikink charge, and anomaly inflow
has zero inclusive current even though its local wall currents are nonzero and
opposite. A single oriented wall on an interval or noncompact line requires
ordered endpoint or asymptotic-sector data. Reversing that order gives the
equal-energy mirror wall.

Fixing one endpoint order therefore restricts the experiment to a stabilizer
groupoid; it does not derive an absolute flavor orientation from the closed
source. A local current instrument additionally needs a localization patch,
signed detector normal, calibration, and a map into `physical16`.
