# The Parallel-Kernel Criterion Is the Exact Active Null-Transport Law

For source transport `v'=Av` and scalar covector `L`, the scalar-null
hyperplane is transport-invariant exactly when

\[
L'+LA=\alpha L
\]

for some scalar `alpha`. Then every parallel scalar readout obeys a scalar
first-order equation, so a zero at one point propagates through its connected
sector. A known nonzero anchor excludes all zeros in that open sector.

The exact action defect is the transverse class

\[
[L'+LA]\in V^*/\langle L\rangle.
\]

At finite cutoff it is falsified when the rows `L` and `L'+LA` have rank
greater than one.

This does not prove RH: the state bundle, connection, covector, and identity
must be constructed from labelled theta/Tate data without dividing by the
completed scalar section. The connection must also be sectorwise. Extending
the same null-preserving law regularly through the seam would propagate the
known seam zeros into the open half-planes.
