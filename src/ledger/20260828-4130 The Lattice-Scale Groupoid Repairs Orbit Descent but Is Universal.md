# The Lattice-Scale Groupoid Repairs Orbit Descent but Is Universal

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

The correct pre-aggregation theta orbit is the scale-indexed lattice family

\[
\Delta_a=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}\delta_{an}.
\]

Poisson summation gives

\[
\mathcal F\Delta_a=a^{-1}\Delta_{a^{-1}},
\]

while dilation moves continuously among the fibers. Thus theta descent is a
groupoid over positive lattice scale, with reciprocal fundamental chamber
$a\ge1$, rather than a finite orbit of integer labels.

This repairs the orbit-descent typing but does not constrain Mellin zeros:
the construction is valid for every Schwartz source and therefore for
hostile Fourier-fixed carriers. The missing discriminator must enrich the
groupoid by the Gaussian vacuum connection and its independently framed
boundary transport.
