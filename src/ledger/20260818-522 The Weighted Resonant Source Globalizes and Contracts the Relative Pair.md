# The Weighted Resonant Source Globalizes and Contracts the Relative Pair

Entry 521 reduces nontriviality to the source-versus-target transition on
`Bl_(u,a^2)`.  The normalized resonant source is obtained from `a^7`:

\[
a^7=a\,u^3t^3
\]

on the `u`-chart, while on the `a^2`-chart it is `a(a^2)^3`.  Removing the
three tautological exceptional powers gives

\[
q_{7,u}=a t^3,
\qquad
q_{7,a^2}=a.
\]

Hence the source transition is `t^3`.

After removing the common Cartier factor, the corresponding target frames
are

\[
r_{7,u}=a t^3(b+1),
\qquad
r_{7,a^2}=a(b+1).
\]

Their transition is also `t^3`.  The factor `b+1` is a global incidence
coefficient and a zero of the target section at `b=-1`; it is not a
source-target Cech transition mismatch on the weighted overlap.

Therefore `q_7` globalizes in the same normalized Rees line as its target,
and the mapping-fiber pair of Entry 520 satisfies

\[
(r_7;\operatorname{res}q_7)=D_{\mathrm{fib}}(q_7,0).
\]

It is contractible.  The divisor `3[1]+4[-1]` remains a correct comparison
divisor for the resonant section, but it does not by itself define a
nonzero relative class.

This falsifies the simplest weighted-exceptional-line realization of the
odd physical resonance.  Any surviving class requires an independently
defined support functor that removes the global source while retaining its
target residue; the ordinary endpoint homotopy fiber does not do so.
