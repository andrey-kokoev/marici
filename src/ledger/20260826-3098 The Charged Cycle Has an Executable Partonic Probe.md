# The Charged Cycle Has an Executable Partonic Probe

After frozen neutral-field insertion, the WP639 contact operator mediates the
massless fixed-helicity, fixed-color process
\(u_L\bar d_R\to u_L\bar d_R\). Its Wilson coefficient is

\[
G={|L_A+L_B|^2h^2s^2x^2\over m_\chi^2},
\]

and, before spin or color averaging,

\[
|\mathcal M|^2=G^2\hat s^2,
\qquad
\hat\sigma={G^2\hat s\over16\pi}.
\]

The unit constructive, destructive, and mass-two contexts give normalized
squared amplitudes sixteen, zero, and one. The process therefore retains both
the destructive-cycle distinction and the charged-mass normalization.

This is an executable source-level partonic probe, not a detector-calibrated
instrument or a `physical16` selector. Its squared-only readout also cannot
identify the sign or complex phase of the total current. PDFs, tagging,
interference, running, cuts, backgrounds, resolution, systematics, and a
calibrated likelihood remain required.
