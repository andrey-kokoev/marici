# 3605 — Reciprocal Reflection Pairs the Relative Residue; It Does Not Annihilate It

The transverse current transforms by the reciprocal sign character:

\[
J(w^{-1})=-J(w).
\]

For the imbalanced two-atom zero `w_+=-a/b` and its reciprocal partner
`w_-=-b/a`, the residues are `b^2-a^2` and `a^2-b^2`. Their scalar orbit sum
vanishes although both fibers are nonzero and off the unit seam.

Thus completed scalar-current cancellation cannot imply RH. It is only the
codiagonal pushforward of an anti-invariant pair. The faithful object is the
sign-local relative fiber, not its scalar totalization.

At the holomorphic reciprocal fixed points `w=±1`, anti-invariance does force
the current to vanish fiberwise. These two points are not the full RH seam.
The seam is fixed by the anti-holomorphic involution `w -> 1/conjugate(w)`,
which constrains the current to a purely imaginary quadrature rather than
forcing it to zero. Ledger 3606 supplies the correction.
