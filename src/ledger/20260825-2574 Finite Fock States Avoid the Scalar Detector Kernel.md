\[
\Omega_{S,s}
=\bigotimes_{p\in S}\sum_{k\ge0}p^{-ks}e_{p,k}.
\]

\[
\mathcal Z_S(s)=\prod_{p\in S}(1-p^{-s})^{-1},

ambient coefficient space violate the Fock constraints and are therefore
tangent or detector-kernel witnesses, not admissible finite source states.
