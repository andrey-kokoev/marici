# 3225 — The Quarter Multiplicities Are Filtered Lifting Obstructions

## Problem

Entry 3217 showed that static relation-space subtraction is not a typed specialization map.  Construct the missing map and determine the provenance of the multiplicities five and seven.

## Construction

At a quarter point \(\gamma=\gamma_0\), write

\[
M(\gamma_0+t)=M_0+tM_1
\]

over the dual numbers.  Reduce the 36 low-readout rows against a dual-number basis of the 720 source rows.  The source quotient is flat to first order: the residual first-normal rank of the source relations is zero at both quarter points.

Let \(R_0+tR_1\) be the resulting low-readout matrix.  A special-fiber relation \(cR_0=0\) lifts to first order precisely when there exists \(c_1\) with

\[
cR_1+c_1R_0=0.
\]

Therefore the canonical first-normal obstruction is

\[
\beta_{\gamma_0}:\ker_L(R_0)\longrightarrow\operatorname{coker}_L(R_0),
\qquad
c\longmapsto[cR_1].
\]

Changing the local row trivialization changes representatives but not this induced map.

## Result

At both primes 32003 and 32009:

\[
\operatorname{rank}\beta_{-5/4}=5,
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}\beta_{-7/4}=7.
\]

Using the total-degree filtration on numerator monomials \(a^ib^j\), the cumulative obstruction ranks through degrees zero to seven are

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\gamma_0&\operatorname{rank}\beta_{\leq d},\quad d=0,\ldots,7\\
\hline
-5/4&(0,0,0,0,0,2,5,5)\\
-7/4&(0,0,0,0,0,2,6,7).
\end{array}
\]

Hence the associated graded multiplicities are

\[
5=2+3
\]

in degrees five and six, while

\[
7=2+4+1
\]

in degrees five, six, and seven.
