# Ordinary Finite Theta Cutoffs Cannot Be Self-Adjoint Characteristic Determinants

The positive reciprocal packet

$$
F_{a,L}(z)=1+2a\cosh(Lz),
\qquad 0<a<\frac12,
$$

has off-seam zeros

$$
z=\pm\frac{\operatorname{arcosh}(1/(2a))}{L}
+\frac{(2k+1)\pi i}{L}.
$$

It therefore cannot be the characteristic determinant of a finite
self-adjoint operator up to a nowhere-zero unit. Exact self-adjoint
determinant matching fails already for the smallest ordinary reciprocal theta
cutoff.

If global Xi is a self-adjoint characteristic section, that meaning must be
created discontinuously by restricted-product completion, a changing domain,
or a relative determinant. The construction must explain where the finite
off-seam divisors go without fitting the completion from Xi.
