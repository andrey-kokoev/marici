# The Mellin--Descent Commutator Is the Canonical Off-Diagonal Current

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

Let \(E\) average over conductor fibers and let
\(\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\log n)\) be the Mellin generator. Then

\[
C=(1-E)\Lambda E
\]

is a source-derived map from the descended block to its complementary
current block. It is the off-diagonal part of the infinitesimal failure of
Mellin transport to commute with descent:

\[
\left.\frac{d}{dz}(e^{-z\Lambda}Ee^{z\Lambda})\right|_{z=0}
=[E,\Lambda].
\]

Moreover \(C^*C=E\Lambda(1-E)\Lambda E\), whose fiberwise coefficient is
the variance of \(\log n\). The current is therefore nonzero precisely when
descent identifies distinct Mellin scales.

This supplies the missing off-diagonal constructor but not yet Wronskian
orientation. The next gate is whether the iterated centered moments
\((1-E)\Lambda^kE\) cyclically generate every complementary fiber direction.
