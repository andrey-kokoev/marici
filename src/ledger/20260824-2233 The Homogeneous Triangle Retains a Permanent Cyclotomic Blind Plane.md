# 2233 — The Homogeneous Triangle Retains a Permanent Cyclotomic Blind Plane

## All-equal specialization

When

\[
y_{12}=y_{23}=y_{31},
\]

the momentum-only Gaussian source assigns the same multiplier to all three
edges. On the contact-kernel basis, every first response is proportional to

\[
(1,1,1).
\]

generators have the same grade profile. Therefore the complete momentum-only
score tower has rank one on the rank-three contact kernel.

Its permanent blind plane is

\[
\boxed{
\{(x_{12},x_{23},x_{31}):x_{12}+x_{23}+x_{31}=0\}
\simeq\mathbb Q(\zeta_3).
}

## Meaning

The cyclic invariant contact response remains observable, but the two
occurrence-relative directions cannot be recovered from any isotropic
momentum-function deformation, regardless of score order.

the physical equal-energy quotient. Finite-to-one or rank-one projection is
not occurrence-level reconstruction.
