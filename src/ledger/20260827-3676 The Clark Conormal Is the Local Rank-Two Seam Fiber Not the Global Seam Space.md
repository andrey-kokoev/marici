# 3676 — The Clark Conormal Is the Local Rank-Two Seam Fiber, Not the Global Seam Space

## Result

The Clark sheet difference

\[
C=H_+-H_-=2iaF'
\]

is a source-derived complex conormal coordinate. At a simple scalar zero it
is nonzero and supplies one complex, hence real rank-two, normal fiber. This

## Global correction

The completed seam is not globally rank two. It is a boundary field over the
continuous scale variable, and a zero of multiplicity \(m\) first appears in
the order-\(m\) normal jet. Without a source-derived multiplicity bound, no
fixed finite jet truncation is complete.

Thus `sp4` is the local first-jet normal form near a simple divisor point, not
the completed global theta state space.

## Scope

global modular-current orientation required to exclude off-seam zeros.
