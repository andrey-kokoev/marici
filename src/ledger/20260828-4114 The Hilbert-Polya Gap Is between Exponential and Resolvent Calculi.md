# The Hilbert--Pólya Gap Is between Exponential and Resolvent Calculi

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

For one self-adjoint generator, the exponential overlap

\[
X(z)=\langle\Omega,e^{zA}\Omega\rangle
\]

may have off-axis zeros even when its spectral measure is positive. The
two-atom example \(X(z)=1+2e^z\) proves this. By contrast, the resolvent
matrix coefficient

\[
M(\zeta)=\langle\Omega,(A-\zeta)^{-1}\Omega\rangle
\]

is Herglotz and has the required spectral orientation.

Hence the Hilbert--Pólya gap is not construction of a self-adjoint carrier.
It is a source-derived divisor-preserving bridge from the exponential
Mellin readout to a Weyl/resolvent boundary event.
