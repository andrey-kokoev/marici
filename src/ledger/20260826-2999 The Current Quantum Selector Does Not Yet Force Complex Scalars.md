\[

Two rebits form a real four-level system with

\[
K_{\mathbb R}(4)=10,
\]

hence nine normalized parameters.  Product measurements constructed from the three-dimensional local effect spaces span at most

\[
K_{\mathbb R}(2)^2=9

unnormalized directions, hence eight normalized directions.  One global coefficient is invisible to all locally generated product probes.

An explicit representative is

\[

\]

Although each (sigma_y) is imaginary and is not a real one-rebit observable, their tensor product is a real symmetric two-rebit observable.  Its expectation is therefore global data with no factorization into admitted local real observables.

For complex quantum theory,

\[

\[

Thus source-derived local tomography would exclude the real theory.  Equal terminal probabilities or correct CHSH values cannot do so.
