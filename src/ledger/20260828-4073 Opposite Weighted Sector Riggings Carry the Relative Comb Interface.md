# Opposite Weighted Sector Riggings Carry the Relative Comb Interface

For every \(\varepsilon>0\), define the opposite Sobolev sectors

\[
\mathcal H_{\pm,\varepsilon}
=
H^1\left(
\mathbb R,
e^{\pm(1+\varepsilon)q}\,dq
\right).
\]

The direct Gaussian vacuum and comb trace belong to the positive sector; their
reciprocal reflections belong to the negative sector. The two spaces have the
continuous relative pairing

\[
\int f(q)\overline{g(q)}\,dq.
\]

The crossed incidences diverge:

\[
B_+\psi_-=B_-\psi_+=\infty.
\]

Comb continuity requires \(\varepsilon>0\); at zero the controlling series is
harmonic. Thus the seam is a critical projective boundary of opposite riggings,
not a shared Hilbert space.
