# 3516 — Curvature--Flux Balance Fixes the Clock and Magnitude but Not the Sign

## Claim

The six-dimensional Einstein-frame potential

\[
V(R)=-\frac{B}{R^4}+\frac{An^2}{R^6}
\]

has the strict minimum

\[
R_*^2=\frac{3An^2}{2B},
\qquad
V''(R_*)=\frac{8B}{R_*^6}>0.
\]

The signed flux threshold becomes

\[
\frac{\sigma n}{R_*}
=\sigma\sqrt{\frac{2B}{3A}}.
\]

Thus the flux integer cancels and curvature--flux balance fixes an absolute
magnitude conditional on the gauge--gravity ratio \(B/A\).

The potential is exactly blind to \(\sigma\). Its unlifted minimum is AdS, and
a stationary Minkowski completion with \(C/R^2\) requires

\[
C=\frac{B^2}{4An^2}.
\]
