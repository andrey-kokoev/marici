# Identical Entrance Doublets Carry an Undeclared Kinetic Gram

Canonical wavefunction normalization is a presentation quotient for isolated
connector fields when every coupling is transformed with the field. The two
entrance doublets are exceptional because they have identical gauge
representations. Gauge symmetry permits a general positive two-by-two
Hermitian kinetic Gram.

The canonical Gram gives orthogonal up/down Yukawa axes. The equally legal
positive Gram `[[2,1],[1,2]]` has determinant three and canonical-axis overlap
squared one quarter. Canonicalizing it while retaining the old axis-aligned
Yukawa tensors silently deletes a source coordinate.

The smallest repair is a source symmetry distinguishing the two doublets, or
an enlarged model containing the full kinetic and Yukawa tensor system. This
is a model-export obstruction, not a flavor selector or detector instrument.
