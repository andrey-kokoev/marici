## Two marked collision occurrences

Set

\[
r^2=5-4\kappa.
\]

At \(\xi=-1\), the Cayley–Menger collision has the labelled germs

\[
a=pr,
\qquad

After taking the \(q_{g1}\) residue, the rational source coefficients are

\[
f_+=\frac{r+1}{2p^3(r-1)^2(r+3)}
\]

and

\[
f_-=\frac{1-r}{2p^3(r+1)^2(3-r)}.
\]

The local \(da/w\) periods at the two germs have opposite orientations. Omitting the common factor \(\pi i\), the complete marked packet is

\[
m_+=
\frac{r+1}{2p^4r(r-1)^2(r+3)},
\]

\[
m_-=
\frac{r-1}{2p^4r(r+1)^2(3-r)}.
\]

## Deck covariance

Under

\[
r\longmapsto-r,
\]

one finds

\[
m_+\longmapsto m_-,
\qquad
m_-\longmapsto m_+.
\]

Thus the marked packet closes on the full two-occurrence permutation module. Both character components

\[
m_++m_-
\]

and

\[
m_+-m_-
\]

are generically nonzero.

## Consequence

The marked residue does not select either the invariant or anti-invariant deck character by itself. Entry 2851's correction is therefore substantive: retaining both occurrences restores a valid local system but leaves physical character selection unresolved.

The next admissible map must come from the physical relative chain. It must specify a covector on the two marked occurrences and a compatible covector on the two unmarked occurrences. Selecting one sheet or one character before deriving that chain would reintroduce the error corrected in Entry 2851.
