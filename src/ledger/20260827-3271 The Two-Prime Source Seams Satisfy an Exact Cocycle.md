# 3271 — The Two-Prime Source Seams Satisfy an Exact Cocycle

Prime Green translations commute, and their moving seams satisfy

\[
B_{\ell+m}(F)
=B_\ell(F)+e^{(z+1/2)\ell}B_m(T_\ell F).
\]

Hence both prime-addition orders give the same total transformed source current
with identity transition. This closes the source/control two-cell.

It does not close the feedback/output two-cell. Order-dependent Schur edge
increments may differ in a coupled joint colligation. The remaining gate is
equality of both sequential eliminations with direct joint elimination under
one determinant normalization, followed by comparison with the independently
derived endpoint Evans section.
