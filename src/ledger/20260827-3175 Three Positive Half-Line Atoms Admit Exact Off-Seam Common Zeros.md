# 3175 — Three Positive Half-Line Atoms Admit Exact Off-Seam Common Zeros

For positive weights (1,3,1) at (0,d,2d), the half-line transform is

\[
H_+(z)=1+3e^{dz}+e^{2dz}.
\]

The polynomial (P(y)=1+3y+y^2) is reciprocal. Its two roots

\[
y_\pm=\frac{-3\pm\sqrt5}{2}
\]

are negative, reciprocal, and off the unit circle. They therefore give exact
common zeros of (H_+(z)) and (H_+(-z)) with nonzero opposite real parts.

## Scope

Positivity, even reflection, and retention of both half-line quadratures do
not exclude off-seam common zeros. A theta-specific modular, heat, or labelled
transport law must reject this hostile packet before its zeros are inspected.
