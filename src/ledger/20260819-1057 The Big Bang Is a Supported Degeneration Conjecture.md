# 1057 — The Big Bang Is a Supported Degeneration Conjecture

## Problem

Marici does not yet have an explanation of the Big Bang. It has a typed
question: can the cosmological initial boundary be generated from the frozen
common carrier and its admissible comparison calculus, or does that boundary
force a genuinely new cosmological carrier stratum?

The distinction is essential:

\[
\text{carrier degeneration}
\neq
\text{coefficient monodromy}
\neq
\text{physical selection}
\neq
\text{a literal first instant}.
\]

## Deutsch--Popperian conjecture

\[
\boxed{
\begin{minipage}{0.86\linewidth}
The cosmological initial singularity is not a new primitive carrier point.
It is a supported, possibly weighted or logarithmic degeneration of the
common Marici carrier. Its observable Big Bang properties arise from
sector-specific nearby-cycle coefficient objects, higher normal/Rees grades,
global time-sheet data, and a source-derived physical specialization.
\end{minipage}
}
\]

This is a hard-to-vary claim because it fixes the division of labor before
calculation:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{carrier} &
\text{energy/Cut incidence, occurrence data, logarithmic resolution}
\\
\text{coefficients} &
\text{nearby cycles, monodromy, Tate/Kummer and elliptic layers}

\\
\text{time} &

\\

\end{array}
\]

No layer may be replaced after seeing the desired cosmological answer.

## Evidence that motivates, but does not prove, the conjecture

The current cosmology partition already exhibits the required ingredients:

- sourced kinematics embeds ordinary scattering as the zero-defect locus;
- the defect direction reconstructs the spatial metric before integration;
- total energy is a resolved linear normal whose coarse modular coordinate is
  quadratic;
- the three-site elliptic variation degenerates at total energy zero to a
  Tate/Kummer nearby-cycle object;
- the algebraic quartic begins at second normal order, so the integrated loop
  problem is not first-jet controlled;
- repeated absolute-quotient candidates for the physical class have failed,
  while supported localization, Gysin, and relative-cycle typing remain the
  legitimate comparison mechanisms.

This evidence supports a degeneration architecture. It does not identify a
physical initial state or prove that every initial-boundary singularity is
resolved by the present carrier.

## Scope

This entry is a conjecture and research contract. It does **not** assert:

- that Marici has explained the Big Bang;
- that the Big Bang is merely a coordinate singularity;
- that all cosmological coefficient systems are shared with scattering;
- that Bunch--Davies data already determine every higher-normal
  specialization;
- that a resolved carrier removes physical curvature or geodesic
  incompleteness;
- that nearby-cycle regularity implies a pre-Big-Bang phase;
- or that the common carrier is complete.

The phrase Big Bang here denotes the observable cosmological
initial-singularity boundary to be typed. Ontological claims about creation,
an absolute beginning, or continuation through that boundary remain outside
the established evidence.
