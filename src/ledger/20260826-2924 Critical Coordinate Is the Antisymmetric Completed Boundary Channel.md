Retaining the two theta continuation currents separately through canonical
completion gives

\[
B(s)=\left(\frac{s}{2},\frac{1-s}{2}\right).
\]

Its symmetric and antisymmetric coordinates are

\[
B_{\mathrm{sym}}=\frac12,
\qquad
B_{\mathrm{asym}}=s-\frac12.
\]

Thus the centered critical coordinate is exactly the boundary direction lost
when the rank-two continuation packet is compressed to the neutral half
carrier.  The critical line is the locus where the two reciprocal boundary
components have equal real share.  The coefficient \(2\Re s-1\) in the
proposed conservation law is twice the real boundary polarization.

This explains the offset and two-sector geometry but does not constrain the
divisor.  A proof still requires a faithful pre-compression coupling to the
antisymmetric channel, stable against both new completion kernels and reduced
minimum-modulus collapse.
