# The Exact-Lift Gauge Module Detects a Relative Fitting Conic

## Hard-to-vary question

Entry 387 established that the projected exact-lift gauge module is a moving
rank-two plane with a regular Pluecker map at the two elliptic base points.
Does its generic Fitting support remain inside the frozen site-energy,
signed-energy, elliptic, and Cayley--Menger arrangement?

No new carrier cell, fitted support summand, or post hoc splitting is allowed.

## Rejected provenance test

Restricting the branch quartic to either marked linear divisor gives an
identically degenerate even-quartic discriminant in this model. That
discriminant vanishes generically and cannot classify the conic. It is
therefore rejected as a provenance test.

The conic is also distinct from
[

]

## Verdict

The stronger claim
[
	ext{all gauge-module Fitting support is exhausted by the previously frozen
energy/elliptic/Cayley--Menger arrangement}
]
is falsified in the tested presentation.

The narrower surviving statement is
[
oxed{	ext{the frozen exact-lift module carries an additional relative
coefficient Fitting conic }X_1X_2-E_T^2=0.}
]

This conic is generated by a rank jump of the source-defined homogeneous
exact-lift module itself. It therefore requires no new incidence cell and is
classified as coefficient support, not carrier structure.

## Consequence for the cosmology architecture

This is a negative update to any hypothesis that the coefficient singular
support is exhausted by the pure elliptic and energy arrangements. It is not a
falsification of shared carrier plus sector-specific coefficients. Instead it
sharpens H2:

[
	ext{shared carrier and calculus}
+
	ext{a relative coefficient object with its own Fitting support}.
]

The result does not locate (mathcal Q). It identifies a different,
source-derived algebraic support component in the exact-lift extension sector.
