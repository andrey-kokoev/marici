# 2492 — Product-Formula Integrality Is a Pure-Spinor Incidence Relation

## Exact finite-place theorem

For a finite place-current space \(V_S\), form the hyperbolic double

\[
 \mathbb H_S=V_S\oplus V_S^*
\]

with evaluation pairing. Let

\[
 \varepsilon_S=\sum_{v\in S}e_v^*.
\]

Then

\[
 L_S=\ker\varepsilon_S\oplus\mathbb C\varepsilon_S
\]

is self-annihilating and therefore maximal isotropic. In the exterior
Clifford representation, the one-form \(\varepsilon_S\) is a pure spinor with

\[
 \operatorname{Ann}(\varepsilon_S)=L_S.
\]

The product formula is thus retained as vector--covector incidence without
using the invalid Riesz identification supplied by the raw Fisher metric.
