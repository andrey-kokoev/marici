# The Newman Equation Fixes the Direction of Every Generic Cubic Collision

At a generic double zero of a Newman family, the heat equation forces the
local normal form

\[
(z-z_0)^2=2(\lambda-\lambda_0).
\]

The source-dependent second derivative cancels from the zero equation.
Therefore increasing Newman time universally absorbs an off-seam conjugate
pair into two seam zeros, while backward heat emits the pair.

Collision orientation is settled; the theta-specific finite-height problem is
reachability of the double-zero locus. At a real candidate this means
simultaneous vanishing of the theta cosine transform and its sine first
moment. Excluding that event for positive Newman time requires a labelled
modular transversality law, not plain source positivity.
