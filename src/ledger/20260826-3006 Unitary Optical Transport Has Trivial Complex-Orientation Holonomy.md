## Unitary transition action

Let \(U\in U(2)\) be a Jones transition. Its adjoint action preserves the real vector space of traceless Hermitian matrices:

\[
U\sigma_iU^\dagger
=
\sum_{j=1}^{3}R(U)_{ji}\sigma_j.
\]

The Pauli trace pairing gives

\[
R(U)^TR(U)=I.
\]

Since the global phase of \(U\) acts trivially, the action factors through

\[
PU(2)\simeq SO(3).
\]

Consequently

\[
\det R(U)=+1.
\]

Every source-derived waveplate transition therefore rotates the complete Pauli frame \((X,Y,Z)\) without reversing its orientation.
