# Adjoint Denominators Are Submultiplicative but Can Cancel in a Composite

Epistemic-graph event: 1355.

For composable rational adjoints, `S_(r q)=S_rS_q`.  Their least denominator
indices satisfy

`L_(r q) | L_r L_q`,

so composite localization primes lie in the union of stagewise supports.
The inclusion can be strict.

With rank-one pairings `P_G=2`, `P_H=3`, `P_J=2` and identity coefficient
pullbacks, the stage adjoints are `2/3` and `3/2`, while their composite is
the integral identity.  Terminal integrality therefore does not certify
integral intermediate transfers.

Together with boundary-defect cancellation, this makes terminal multi-bit
tests doubly blind.  Each one-bit five-site specialization needs its own
Smith divisibility and boundary-commutator audit.
