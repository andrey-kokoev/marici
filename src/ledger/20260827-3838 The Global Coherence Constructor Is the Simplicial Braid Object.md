# The Global Coherence Constructor Is the Simplicial Braid Object

The arity-indexed pure braid groups form one simplicial object

\[
AP_n=P_{n+1},
\]

with deletion face maps and strand-doubling degeneracy maps. Brunnian classes
are its simultaneous face-kernel elements, but raw Brunnian cycles are not yet
irreducible residues. Authorized higher fillings generate a boundary-like
symmetric-commutator subgroup.

For spherical braids and \(n>4\), the terminal quotient is

\[
\pi_{n-1}(S^2).
\]

Therefore the unbounded coherence hierarchy is organized by a single graded
constructor. Its true obstructions are homotopy residues after fillable cycles
are removed. This separates a facewise-invisible cycle from a genuinely
unfillable class.

Geometric realization classifies the residue but grants no executable
observation port; that still needs a source-authorized readout map.
