# The Failure Trichotomy Is a Candidate Pre-Carrier Law

Three recurrent mechanisms admit an invariant preliminary classification:

\[
\boxed{
\text{presentation boundary}
\quad|\quad
\text{supported birth}
\quad|\quad
\text{transport degeneration}.
}
\]

A presentation boundary kills one coordinate while the exterior object
survives in another chart.  A supported birth changes the homology of the
incidence/coherence complex and permits a new port, whose information content
is then decided by the sector lens.  A transport degeneration kills the full
exterior section and canonically births a kernel or residue.

Minimal exact representatives verify that these three tests are respectively
invariant under invertible recharting, label permutation, and invertible
left/right presentation changes.  They type the mechanisms found in
Strominger Entries 2040 and 2052 and Nima Entries 2044–2047.

The Deutsch–Popperian conjecture is that every source-admissible apparent
failure has exactly one such invariant type.  A legal presentation change
that alters the assigned type is a finite falsifier.
