# 2588 — Theta Finite-Part Extraction Is Not Positive

Finite-part extraction is not order preserving. For \(a>0\) and arbitrary
real \(c\),

\[
F_\varepsilon=\varepsilon^{-a}+c
\]

is strictly positive for every sufficiently small \(\varepsilon>0\), while

\[
\operatorname{FP}_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}
\left(F_\varepsilon-\varepsilon^{-a}\right)=c.
\]

Therefore positivity of every regulated theta bulk, even together with
regulator universality, cannot orient or prevent zeros of the relative Euler
finite part. A surviving RH argument must carry a source-derived coupled order
or conservation law on the bulk and its typed boundary current.

## Scope

This closes a proof architecture; it makes no claim about the actual sign or
zeros of \(\zeta\) or \(\Xi\).
