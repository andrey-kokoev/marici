# Rational Nondegeneracy Does Not Guarantee an Integral Betti Adjoint

Epistemic-graph event: 1352.

With pairing matrices `P_G,P_H` and coefficient pullback `Q`, Betti
adjunction is the integral matrix equation

`P_H S=Q^T P_G`.

An integral adjoint exists exactly when the right-hand columns lie in the
column lattice of `P_H`.  Rational nondegeneracy is insufficient: for
`P_H=[2]`, `P_G=Q=[1]`, adjunction forces `S=[1/2]`, so the rational adjoint
is unique but no integral pushforward exists.

This cokernel/lattice-index obstruction is distinct from pairing radical
freedom.  Smith divisibility tests decide it before computing any boundary
commutator.  The formal five-site delta pairing passes because its matrix is
unimodular; the unavailable physical relative pairing must still be checked
for both kernel and cokernel.
