# 2106 — Flavor Textures Rigidify Purity but Do Not Select Its Value

All 1,210 viable texture sheets were compared with 12,100 points drawn
uniformly from the complete OBS17 \(\chi^2\le4\) ball.

The sheet standard deviation of \(F_t\) is \(2.02\times10^{-5}\), only
2.51% of the null standard deviation \(8.03\times10^{-4}\). Thus the
texture atlas strongly rigidifies the readout.

However, the sheet mean \(0.9920323\) remains within one null standard
deviation of the null median \(0.9914444\). The texture constraints do
not significantly select the numerical location; they make a
data-compatible value nearly class-constant.

Hence flavor currently separates:

\[
\text{physical-data localization}
\longrightarrow
\text{texture-induced rigidity},
\]

without a source-derived explanation of the numerical point.
