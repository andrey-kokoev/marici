# The Elliptic Top Residues Satisfy the Cousin Identity

> **Scope correction (Entry 574).**  This entry verifies the two paths through
> the mixed \(q_{\mathcal G_{12}}\)-lower faces.  It does not replace the full
> geometric face vector \((1,-1,1)\) by \((1,-1,0)\); the lower-pair face from
> Entry 341 remains part of the Cousin complex.

## Question

Entry 568 constructed the associated-grade boundary of the unique proper
top-support class.  The next gate was whether its two nonzero mixed-face
components can be represented by actual logarithmic residues whose further
maps to the (q_{\mathcal G_{12}})-closed family cancel canonically.

## Frozen representative

In denominator order

\[
(q_{\mathfrak g_1},q_{\mathfrak g_2},q_{\mathcal G_{12}}),
\]

take the local logarithmic representative

\[
\Omega_{111}
=
K^\gamma
d\log q_{\mathfrak g_1}
\wedge d\log q_{\mathfrak g_2}
\wedge d\log q_{\mathcal G_{12}}.
\]

The clean intersection and invertibility conditions are those proved in
Entry 568.  No master-basis splitting is used.

## First residues

The two mixed (q_{\mathcal G_{12}})-lower faces receive

\[
\operatorname{Res}_{q_{\mathfrak g_1}}\Omega_{111}
=
K^\gamma
d\log q_{\mathfrak g_2}
\wedge d\log q_{\mathcal G_{12}},
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{Res}_{q_{\mathfrak g_2}}\Omega_{111}
=
-K^\gamma
d\log q_{\mathfrak g_1}
\wedge d\log q_{\mathcal G_{12}}.
\]

These are the two mixed-face components of the full geometric boundary
\((1,-1,1)\).

## Second residues

Deleting the remaining lower denominator gives

\[
\operatorname{Res}_{q_{\mathfrak g_2}}
\operatorname{Res}_{q_{\mathfrak g_1}}\Omega_{111}
=
+K^\gamma d\log q_{\mathcal G_{12}},
\]

whereas

\[
\operatorname{Res}_{q_{\mathfrak g_1}}
\operatorname{Res}_{q_{\mathfrak g_2}}\Omega_{111}
=
-K^\gamma d\log q_{\mathcal G_{12}}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Res}_{q_{\mathfrak g_2}}
\operatorname{Res}_{q_{\mathfrak g_1}}
+
\operatorname{Res}_{q_{\mathfrak g_1}}
\operatorname{Res}_{q_{\mathfrak g_2}}
=0.
}
\]

This is the required Cousin identity in the common rank-16
\(q_{\mathcal G_{12}}\)-closed target.  The cancellation is forced by
exterior orientation, not by a chosen relation between master vectors.

## Classification and limit

- top-to-mixed maps: ordinary logarithmic residue/Gysin maps;
- mixed-to-(q\)-closed maps: ordinary logarithmic residues;
- coherence: the standard anticommuting Cartier square;
- carrier input: only the three frozen denominator hyperplanes;
- new carrier datum: none.

Thus the unique top class and its first coherence square are generated by
the shared support calculus.  This is evidence for H2 at representative
level, stronger than rank or associated-grade evidence.

It still does not identify a global horizontal generator in the rank-21
Gauss--Manin module.  The calculation is local in the denominator-support
filtration and proves the residue square, not a direct-sum decomposition.
