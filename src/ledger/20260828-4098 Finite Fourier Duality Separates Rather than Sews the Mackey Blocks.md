# Finite Fourier Duality Separates Rather than Sews the Mackey Blocks

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

For a finite abelian quotient \(q:G\to H\) with kernel \(K\),

\[
\mathbb C[G]=\operatorname{im}q^*\oplus\ker q_*.
\]

Fourier transformation sends the descended block to functions supported on
\(K^\perp\), and the complementary block to functions vanishing on
\(K^\perp\). It therefore separates the two Mackey blocks by disjoint
spectral support; it does not sew them together.

Consequently finite Fourier covariance plus \(q_*q^*=|K|\,\mathrm{id}\)
cannot orient the trivial-character Wronskian. The relative phase of the two
blocks remains free. Any genuine sewing must come from additional source
structure: archimedean theta weighting, primitive/square currents,
multiplicative prime-power induction, or global Poisson coupling.
