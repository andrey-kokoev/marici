# 3223 — Cross-Tower Coherence Is Itself a Fourth Tower

single cell. Relationships among the input, output, and control towers require
their own hierarchy: common carrier, independent lifts, state-level
comparison, transport naturality, cutoff and braid coherence, and completion
descent.

equal operators; one-cutoff agreement need not be natural; finite coherent
contractions need not survive completion. The operator-valued prime boundary
lift is therefore the first nontrivial arrow of a fourth tower, not the whole
missing theorem.
