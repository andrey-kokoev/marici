# 1402 — The Xi Jet Filtration Canonically Restores Spectral Multiplicity

Sequence claim: \`seqclaim-23259dec86e93ac82f1ea20c\`.

Epistemic-graph event: 1450.

The ideal generated by the CCM source range is exactly \((\Xi)\): every source
image is a polynomial multiple of Xi, and Xi itself occurs. The
source-saturated quotient therefore has local algebra at a zero \(\lambda\) of
order \(m_\lambda\)

\[
\mathcal O_\lambda/(\Xi).
\]

Its maximal-ideal filtration has \(m_\lambda\) one-dimensional graded pieces.
Because \(z=\lambda+(z-\lambda)\) and the second term raises filtration,
multiplication by \(z\) induces

\[
\lambda I_{m_\lambda}
\]

on the associated graded. This canonically kills the Jordan nilpotent while
retaining multiplicity as local length; zero simplicity is unnecessary.

The square-sum completion of all graded jet lines carries a diagonal operator
\(A_{\mathrm{Gr}}\). Under RH it is self-adjoint with compact resolvent, and
the Riemann--von Mangoldt bound makes \(A_{\mathrm{Gr}}^{-1}\)
Hilbert--Schmidt. Symmetric regularization then gives

\[
\det\nolimits_2(I-zA_{\mathrm{Gr}}^{-1})
=\Xi(z)/\Xi(0).
\]

This closes the algebraic multiplicity gate without supplying a zero list.
It does not yet close the Mellin-boundary gate: the Weil form supplies one
evaluation channel of weight \(m_\lambda\), not \(m_\lambda\) independent jet
channels. At a double zero its local rank is one while the determinant fiber
has dimension two. A genuine source boundary must derive the missing jet
channels and their positive norm.
