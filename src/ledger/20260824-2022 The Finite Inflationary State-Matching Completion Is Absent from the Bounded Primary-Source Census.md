# 2022 — The Finite Inflationary State-Matching Completion Is Absent from the Bounded Primary-Source Census

## Question

Entry 2019 showed that Collins--Holman--Vardanyan, arXiv:1408.4801, computes only the divergent initial-state tangent and leaves the zeroth- and first-order finite-time matching undone. Was that specific calculation completed in an identifiable primary follow-up?

## Frozen census

The bounded search covered:

1. exact-title, arXiv-identifier, equation-combination, and author searches for a continuation of arXiv:1408.4801;
2. the later Collins--Vardanyan paper *Entangled Scalar and Tensor Fluctuations during Inflation*, arXiv:1601.05415;
3. Collins--Holman--Vardanyan, *The quantum Fokker--Planck equation of stochastic inflation*, arXiv:1706.07805;
4. Garny--Reinosa, *Renormalization out of equilibrium in a superrenormalizable theory*, arXiv:1504.06643;
5. Chaykov--Agarwal--Bahrami--Holman, *Loop corrections in Minkowski spacetime away from equilibrium. Part I*, arXiv:2206.11288;
6. the older Collins--Holman initial-condition renormalization papers and the primary bibliography exposed by the author and citation records.

This is a bounded provenance audit, not a claim of exhaustive bibliographic nonexistence.

## Result

No inspected primary source performs the omitted zeroth- and first-order extraction for the inflationary loop calculation of arXiv:1408.4801 and thereby derives finite renormalized kernels \(A_p,B_p\).

The nearby papers address different objects:

- arXiv:1601.05415 starts from general entangling functions and derives their observational consequences;
- arXiv:1706.07805 derives long-wavelength stochastic evolution;
- arXiv:1504.06643 treats a different nonequilibrium theory and uses non-Gaussian initial correlations;
- arXiv:2206.11288 studies late-time Minkowski correlators and resummation.

None supplies the missing source map

\[
\text{one-loop inflationary bulk dynamics}
\longrightarrow
(\nu,\kappa)
\longrightarrow
(P,S).
\]

## Narrow conclusion

\[
\boxed{
\text{the bounded primary-source census contains no published completion of the omitted finite matching.}
}
\]

Therefore the arXiv:1408.4801 branch is closed under the currently inspected literature for selecting a finite Gaussian section. This does **not** prove that no unpublished derivation or unindexed source exists, and it does not prohibit a new first-principles calculation.

## Programme consequence

The legal positive state space remains

\[
S>-\frac12,
\]

but the frozen source does not select a point in it. A next calculation must begin from a source whose finite state prescription is complete, or explicitly undertake the missing renormalized matching as a new derivation. Positivity, simplicity, or desired late-time behavior may test that derivation but may not choose its finite kernels.
