# 2914 — Bridge Sewing Produces a Pointed Kummer Tensor Product, Not One Affine Torsor

## Type correction

Entry 2906 asked for an affine map from two pointed Kummer torsors into one
composite torsor.  Entry 2913's join factorization corrects that target.
Canonical-form residues multiply coefficient factors.  The bridge face
therefore produces the external tensor product

\[
(1\oplus\mathcal K_L)
\otimes
(1\oplus\mathcal K_R).
\]

Its associated-graded basis is

\[
(1,F_L,F_R,F_LF_R).
\]

## Tensor monodromy

Let \(N_L\) translate \(F_L\) and let \(N_R\) translate \(F_R\).  On the
four-dimensional tensor basis,

\[
N_L^2=N_R^2=0,
\qquad
[N_L,N_R]=0,
\]

but

\[
N_LN_R=N_RN_L\ne0.
\]

The nonzero product has rank one and lands in the mixed grade

\[
\mathcal K_L\otimes\mathcal K_R.
\]

For translation parameters \(a,b\), the exact action is

\[
(F_L,F_R,F_LF_R)
\longmapsto
(F_L+a,F_R+b,(F_L+a)(F_R+b)).
\]

Thus the mixed term \(ab\) is forced by tensor composition.  It is not a
fitted contact correction.

## Pointing

The two source origins

\[
F_L(2)=0,
\qquad
F_R(2)=0
\]

sew to the unique tensor origin

\[
(1,0,0,0).
\]

There is no affine constant mismatch at this associated grade.

## Result

Bridge sewing does not collapse two pointed Kummer systems back to one affine
line.  It generates a canonical rank-four tensor object with a rank-one mixed
logarithmic grade.

The earlier scale ambiguity arose from asking for a mistyped affine target.
Once the source-derived join and tensor variance are retained:

- the unit normalization is fixed;
- the pointed origins sew canonically;
- a mixed coefficient grade appears;
- no new carrier cell is needed.
