# 3174 — The Completed Theta Half-Lines Form the Canonical Pre-Scalar Two-Quadrature Lift

Splitting the fixed real even completed theta source at (u=0) gives

\[
H_+(z),\qquad H_-(z)=H_+(-z).
\]

The channels

\[
T=H_++H_-=X,
\qquad
A=H_+-H_-
\]

recover both half-line amplitudes. Hence a scalar zero (X(z)=0) leaves

\[
A(z)=2H_+(z)
\]

unless both half-line amplitudes vanish. Scalar completion therefore forgets
an antisymmetric relationship channel.

## Scope

The construction does not exclude common off-seam zeros. It reduces the
RH-bearing target to a source-derived common-zero exclusion theorem for
(H_+(z)) and (H_+(-z)).
