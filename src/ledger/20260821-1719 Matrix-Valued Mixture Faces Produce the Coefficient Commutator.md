# 1719 — Matrix-Valued Mixture Faces Produce the Coefficient Commutator

## Scope

This is a hypothetical algebraic falsifier of Entry 1718, not a claim that the
frozen cosmological source contains matrix-valued mixture weights.

Let the two incomparable face transports act on a coefficient fiber by
endomorphisms \(U,V\), and retain a mixed labelled coefficient \(A\).

## Interchange defect

The two blowup orders transport the mixed occurrence to

\[
UVA
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
VUA.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
UVA-VUA=[U,V]A.
}
\]

The defect vanishes exactly on the commuting specialization relevant to
ordinary scalar probability weights.  Generic integral unipotent test
matrices give a nonzero defect.

## Classification

The two labelled faces and their intersection already exist.  A nonzero
commutator therefore calls for a coefficient-level braiding/interchange cell,
not a new carrier incidence.  This is a clean example of sector-specific
coefficient complexity over a fixed carrier.
