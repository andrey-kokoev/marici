# Smith Divisibility Gives the Minimal Localization for a Betti Adjoint

Epistemic-graph event: 1354.

`e_ij=d_i/gcd(d_i,B'_ij)`.

Their least common multiple `L` is the exact denominator index: an integral
adjoint exists iff `L=1`, and localization admits the adjoint precisely after
inverting every prime dividing `L`.
