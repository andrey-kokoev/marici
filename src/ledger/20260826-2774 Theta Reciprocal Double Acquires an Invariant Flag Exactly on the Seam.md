# 2774 — Theta Reciprocal Double Acquires an Invariant Flag Exactly on the Seam

After identifying the shared constant channel, the direct and
reciprocal-conjugate theta tails have generator

\[
K_z(q)=
\begin{pmatrix}
-z&0&-f(q)\\
0&\bar z&-f(q)\\
0&0&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The antisymmetric sheet covector \((1,-1,0)\) cancels the common forcing. It is
an invariant adjoint line exactly when

\[
-z=\bar z,
\]

equivalently \(\operatorname{Re}s=1/2\).

Thus the critical line is the locus where reciprocal spectral weights
coalesce and the relative sheet coordinate gains multiplicative invariant
meaning. This derives the special line independently of zero locations and
gives a precise two-sector fold explanation.

It is not RH: the invariant flag appears on the seam rather than protecting
the open sectors. The remaining theorem is sectorwise open-cell confinement,
with boundary currents absorbing the off-seam mismatch
\(z+\bar z=2\operatorname{Re}z\).
