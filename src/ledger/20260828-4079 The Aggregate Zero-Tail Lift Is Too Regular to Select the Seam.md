# The Aggregate Zero-Tail Lift Is Too Regular to Select the Seam

At a zero of

\[
X(z)=\int_{\mathbb R}\Phi(v)e^{zv}\,dv,
\]

the source tail

\[
G_z(q)
=
e^{-zq}\int_q^\infty\Phi(v)e^{zv}\,dv
\]

decays superexponentially at both endpoints. It therefore lies in every direct
and reciprocal exponential rigging, independently of \(\Re z\).

Thus the aggregate zero-to-tail bridge cannot transmit the primitive
common-domain collapse to the seam. Label summation has erased the arithmetic
divergence.

The required bridge is label-faithful: a zero must lift to a common tail state
compatible with every primitive comb current before aggregation.
