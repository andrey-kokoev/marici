# 3440 — The SU(4) Gauge Link Has a Localization-Normalization Trilemma

## Claim

The parity

\[
P=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1,-1)
\]

splits the \(SU(4)\) adjoint into the seven-generator
\(SU(2)_A\times SU(2)_B\times U(1)\) algebra and an eight-real-dimensional
complex bifundamental (A_5) sector. The link is therefore a gauge component,
not a separate hypermultiplet. The pure vector spectral index is

\[
\kappa=2+15=17>0.
\]

This joint source still has a complete-boundary obstruction. Residual gauge
symmetry permits an exchange-even kinetic coefficient \(\tau\). At fixed bulk
normalization, \(\tau=0\) and \(\tau=1\) change the WP771 portal from \(1/10\)
to \(1/20\).

Promoting the portal operands to bulk hypermultiplets does not remove the
orbifold fixed-point kinetic modulus. It also costs \(32\) degrees and
reverses the spectral index:

\[
\kappa=2+15-32=-15.
\]

Adding the compulsory mediator packet gives \(-63\).

## Classification

Every localization branch of the minimal \(SU(4)\) orbifold parent retains the
boundary normalization modulus, while bulk localization additionally reverses
the half-twist sign. The positive hypermultiplet budget is \(16\), already
smaller than the portal operand cost. A larger source group must derive at
least sixteen additional vector degrees and a principle fixing the common
orbifold kinetic term while preserving flavor embedding, chirality, anomaly
completion, isolated normalization, and physical readout.
