# Norm Splitting and Defect Detection Have Three Arithmetic Regimes

Epistemic-graph event: 1337.

From `S T=d id`, splitting and detection impose different arithmetic
requirements:

- if `d` is a unit, the norm splits and invariant defects are detected;
- if `d` is a nonunit non-zero-divisor, invariant defects are still detected
  although scalar splitting is unavailable;
- with nonzero `d`-torsion, even invariant defects may be annihilated.

The middle regime is essential integrally.  For every five-site branch
degree `2^k`, integral chain modules detect trace-submodule defects despite
the absence of division by `2^k`.  After inverting `2`, splitting also
appears.  In characteristic two, both properties can fail at every one-bit
stage.

This refines the arithmetic audit without constructing the unavailable
physical relative-chain pushforward or trace.
