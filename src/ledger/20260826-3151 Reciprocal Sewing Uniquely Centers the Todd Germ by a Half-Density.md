# 3151 — Reciprocal Sewing Uniquely Centers the Todd Germ by a Half-Density

For \(\tau(t)=t/(e^t-1)\), reciprocal reflection gives

\[
\tau(-t)=e^t\tau(t).
\]

The unique exponential normalization \(e^{at}\tau(t)\) invariant under
reflection has \(a=1/2\). It is

\[
e^{t/2}\tau(t)=\frac{t}{2\sinh(t/2)}.
\]

This centered germ is even. Its logarithmic tangent is \(L(t)+1/2\), so the
\(\zeta(0)=-1/2\) boundary anomaly cancels and the remaining jet tower is
odd.

## Scope

This is a source-local half-density mechanism. The Mellin naturality square
transporting it to the global spectral coordinate \(s-1/2\) remains to be
derived.
