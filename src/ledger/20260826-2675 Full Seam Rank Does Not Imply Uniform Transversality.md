# 2675 — Full Seam Rank Does Not Imply Uniform Transversality

On (K=\ker Q), define

\[
\beta(H,R)=\inf_{Rx\ne0}\frac{\|Hx\|}{\|Rx\|}.
\]

Finite quotient rank gives (\beta_N>0); completion stability requires
(\inf_N\beta_N>0). The exact family with seam rows ((1,0)) and
((1,1/N)) has full rank and the same row space as the residual at every
cutoff, while its domination constant grows at least as (N).
