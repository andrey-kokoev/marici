## Proof as sustained constructive capability

The earlier proposal describes proof as a source-grounded, compositionally reusable realization of a capability. In ordinary terms, it asks how something remains identifiable while participating in change: what supports it, what it enables, how its encounters preserve those capabilities, and how different accounts of those encounters fit together.

Returning to the same configuration therefore includes recovering its ability to participate again, with the evidence required by later use retained. A momentarily matching output and a capability to sustain the defining relation are different claims. The source relation and admitted continuations determine which distinctions matter; complete history need only be retained when those continuations consume it.

For the present proposal, a proof supplies an actual term together with its supporting dependencies, compatible interactions, witnessed recoveries and coherent future use. These data establish the proposed sustained realization of the term. Ordinary type-theoretic inhabitation remains its own precise notion; the stronger sustained-realization claim has the additional interface as part of what is being proved.

### Relation to univalent foundations

Univalent foundations make equivalence of types available as identity, supporting transport of constructions. Paths and higher paths retain the comparisons and coherence relevant to that identity. This supplies a language in which the present certificate can be expressed.

Univalence alone fixes no configuration dimension, dependency-sector inventory or cost convention. Those belong to the proposed theory of the selected configuration. ## The packet and its mark

An n-dimensional configuration packet has one label and n information entries. Here dimension counts independent directions of configuration change, rather than axes of physical space. The case under discussion has n=3, giving the four-slot presentation [l,a,b,c].

The label names a realized configuration and references the retained coherence complex supporting that realization. Its placement at the barycenter expresses that whole-complex role. The other three entries specify realized directions of variation, together with references to the transports and coherence data supporting those directions.

Thus the same mark has two roles: a term-valued point in configuration space and an access point to its supporting construction. The reference is a name for a retained package, not a claim that a bare point reconstructs its history. The proposed typing interpretation must ultimately be implemented through actual carriers and dereference operations.

Here a coherence complex is a complex of configurations, paths and witnesses. A connection to complex-number coordinates would require additional structure. Likewise, one designated configuration can retain several distinguishable transport witnesses; calling the designation a singleton does not erase that information.

## Why three gives twelve: the codimension-one rule

The operator selects the following generalization: in n independent configuration directions, retain the signed coordinate cells of dimension n−1 incident to the common mark. Choose all but one direction, and choose a side along each retained direction.

The general count, for positive integer n, is:

\[
2^{n-1}\binom n{n-1}=n\,2^{n-1}.
\]

Every part has a fixed meaning:

| Factor | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| n | Choose the one omitted configuration direction, equivalently one of the n coordinate hyperplanes through the mark. |
| n−1 in the exponent | The number of directions retained within that hyperplane. |
| Base 2 | Each retained direction has two signed sides, + and −. This binary choice is fixed independently of n. |
| 2^(n−1) | All independent side choices within the selected hyperplane. |

At n=3, the retained cells have dimension n−1=2 and are squares. The general rule specializes to:

\[
2^{3-1}\binom3{3-1}=3\cdot2^2=12.
\]

The side choices are (+,+), (+,−), (−,+) and (−,−). Each of the three coordinate planes therefore contributes four square sectors incident to the center. Twelve counts these sectors, without an additional choice of clockwise versus anticlockwise traversal.

In the drawing, three square sheets intersect at the common mark, each subdivided into four squares. Every small square has a boundary route starting and ending at that mark. Its filling witness compares the two composed routes from the mark to the opposite corner. The displayed complex is two-dimensional; a volume filler is separate data. In general n, the selected coherence cells have dimension n−1; their boundaries and higher witnesses require corresponding source constructions rather than a literal square-path description at every dimension.

This is specifically the codimension-one family. At higher n its cells increase in dimension; the rule does not keep choosing two directions. It explains twelve once three signed configuration directions are supplied. The source construction still has to determine why the selected configuration has three such directions.

## In what sense twelve is minimal

In general n, n times 2^(n−1) is the complete inventory of signed codimension-one sectors. At n=3 this is twelve square sectors. Omitting a sector leaves that omitted-direction and retained-side combination without an explicit certificate in the inventory.

This is coverage minimality. A constructive calculus may derive some square witnesses from others through inversion, composition or symmetry. The minimum number of independent generators is a further question about that calculus.

Calling the sectors dependencies means that the mark references their actual boundary and filling data, and that later constructions can use those data. The diagram specifies the candidate obligations; inhabitants of the witness types establish their fulfillment.

## What it means to sustain the term

A self-map A to A supplies an operation on terms. A sustained configuration requires an actual marked term together with its witnessed return; the identity map alone is also definable when A has no inhabitants.

For a simple typed model, let an input map i go from an external carrier B to the configuration carrier A, and an output map o go from A back to B. Construct the two recovery homotopies:

\[
\alpha:i\circ o\circ i\simeq i,\qquad
\beta:o\circ i\circ o\simeq o.
\]

These express recovery of both input and output capabilities. If a=i(b) for a constructed b, then alpha supplies i(o(a))=a. Recovery holds on the sustained configuration generated through i; these equations alone do not assert that every element of A participates in a global equivalence.

Fourfold composites introduce coherence. The composite i composed with o composed with i composed with o reduces to i composed with o by either alpha applied to the first triple or beta applied to the last triple. A further witness compares the two reductions. The reverse fourfold composite has the corresponding comparison.

Different compatible port pairs also require whatever comparisons are needed to preserve the marked configuration and its admitted future uses. Those comparisons must retain their witnesses. The term, its source construction, ports, recoveries and required higher comparisons form the proposed realization certificate.

## How 1836 enters the question

The earlier counting vocabulary gave twelve complete compositions, three retained markings per composition, and comparisons with three partner classes. Its numerical instance is:

\[
1836=(12\cdot3)\,[3+12+(12\cdot3)]=108+432+1296.
\]

The revised account seeks a constructive reason for such a count: twelve coherence-indexed dependencies, three outgoing configuration directions, their compatible encounters, and the recovery/comparison structure that sustains the mark.

The three summands are a candidate grouping to test against that construction. They are not imposed as the required grouping of recovery and coherence obligations. The next task is to build those obligations, identify reusable or derived witnesses, and determine their actual accounting.

For this revised model, n counts outgoing configuration directions and n times 2^(n−1) counts incoming dependency sectors. Pairing one dependency sector with one outgoing direction gives n squared times 2^(n−1) situated encounters. If the three tagged partner classes remain a direction, a dependency sector and another situated encounter, the candidate count is written directly as three products, with no auxiliary count symbols:

\[
J(n)=(n^2\,2^{n-1})\cdot n
+(n^2\,2^{n-1})\cdot(n\,2^{n-1})
+(n^2\,2^{n-1})\cdot(n^2\,2^{n-1}).
\]

Each summand has two parts:

| First part | Second part | At n=3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A situated dependency-sector/direction encounter | A configuration direction | 36 times 3 = 108 |
| A situated dependency-sector/direction encounter | A dependency sector | 36 times 12 = 432 |
| A situated dependency-sector/direction encounter | Another situated dependency-sector/direction encounter | 36 times 36 = 1296 |

Thus:

\[
J(3)=36\cdot3+36\cdot12+36\cdot36=108+432+1296=1836.
\]

The equivalent three-term expression is:

\[
J(n)=n^3\,2^{n-1}+n^3\,2^{2n-2}+n^4\,2^{2n-2}.
\]

These expressions count the proposed partner vocabulary before composability, recovery and coherence are constructed; they are not yet the proven cost of a realization certificate. The earlier process count n(n−1)^2, with three fixed process markings, is a different general family. The operator selects the codimension-one rule for this revised explanation.
