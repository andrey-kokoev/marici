# 1367 — The Primitive Mobius Half-Line Has the Wrong Weyl Law

Epistemic-graph event: 1395.

Grant the candidate primitive Möbius carrier a circumference `L` and impose
its available holonomy on logarithmic dilation:

\[
f(y+L)=\eta f(y),\qquad \eta=\pm1.
\]

The self-adjoint operator `-i d/dy` then has spectrum

\[
\frac{2\pi n}{L}quad(\eta=1),
\qquad
\frac{(2n+1)\pi}{L}quad(\eta=-1),
\]

and counting law

\[
N_D(T)=\frac{L}{2\pi}T+O(1).
\]

By contrast, the zeta-zero ordinates obey

\[
N_\zeta(T)=\frac{T}{2\pi}\log\frac{T}{2\pi}
-\frac{T}{2\pi}+O(\log T).
\]

Thus no length and neither source-available holonomy can make this boundary
quotient reproduce the zeros.  The mismatch is asymptotic, not a tunable
normalization error.

Scope: this falsifies the direct compact-circle realization of the primitive
half-line.  It does not exclude an infinite or energy-dependent source-derived
boundary system.
