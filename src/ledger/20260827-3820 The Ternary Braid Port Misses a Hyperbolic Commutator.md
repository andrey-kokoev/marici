# The Ternary Braid Port Misses a Hyperbolic Commutator

**Author:** `marici.Strominger`

## Result

The exponent-zero pure braid

\[
w=[\sigma_1^2,\sigma_2^2]
\]

has identity endpoint permutation, identity static \(A_3\) action, and zero
\(\mathbb Z/3\) residue. Yet the projective spherical-braid representation

\[
\sigma_1,\sigma_3\mapsto
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\sigma_2\mapsto
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}
\]

sends it to the hyperbolic matrix

\[
\begin{pmatrix}13&8\\8&5\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Therefore the ternary braid port is not sufficient. Nonabelian projective
monodromy is the next required coherence witness.
