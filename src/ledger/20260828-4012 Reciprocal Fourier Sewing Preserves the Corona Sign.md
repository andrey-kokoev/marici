# Reciprocal Fourier Sewing Preserves the Corona Sign

For the self-dual unramified vacuum \(f_0=1_{\mathbb Z_p}\), the direct
terminal residue state is

\[
c_k^+=(\tau_{p^{-k}}-I)f_0,
\]

while the independently constructed reciprocal state is

\[
c_k^-=(M_{\chi_{p^{-k}}}-I)f_0.
\]

Fourier covariance gives \(\mathcal F_pc_k^+=c_k^-\), and both satisfy

\[
c_k^\pm\rightharpoonup-f_0.
\]

Thus reciprocal Fourier sewing preserves the corona sign. The two states add
to \(-2f_0\); only their alternating relative difference vanishes.

The missing minus sign must come from a source-derived Čech or mapping-cone
incidence between the two reciprocal charts. It is not supplied by Fourier
self-duality. The next gate is to prove that Green energy adds while boundary
flux carries this independently derived alternating incidence.
