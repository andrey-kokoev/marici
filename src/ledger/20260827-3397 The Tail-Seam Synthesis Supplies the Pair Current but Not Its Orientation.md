# The Tail–Seam Synthesis Supplies the Pair Current but Not Its Orientation

For complete labelled tail–seam atoms `u_n`, the synthesis operator `U`
canonically supplies the missing pair current as `K=U*U`, with logarithmic
kernel

\[
K(n,m)=A\!\left(\left|\log(n/m)\right|\right).
\]

This retains the off-diagonal interference needed to reconstruct seam periods.
Positive Fock formation supplies the labels; analytic synthesis and
polarization supply their overlaps.

However, simultaneous prime transport preserves the kernel. It does not yield
the modular factor required to orient the reciprocal sectors. Pairwise
observability is therefore closed, while relative additive-Haar versus
multiplicative-Haar orientation remains open.
