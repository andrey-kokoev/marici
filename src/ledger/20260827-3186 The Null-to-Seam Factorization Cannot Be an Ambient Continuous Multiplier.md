# 3186 — The Null-to-Seam Factorization Cannot Be an Ambient Continuous Multiplier

At a simple seam zero, the quotient (d/X) has incompatible directional
limits: zero tangentially along the seam and a nonzero source vector normally.
Therefore no continuous ambient identity

\[
d(z)=K(z)X(z)
\]

can construct the null-to-seam factorization.

## Consequence

The factorization lives only on the scalar-null pullback. It must be induced
by an incidence-specific Green identity, deficiency-state condition, or exact
boundary complex—not scalar divisibility or a bounded repair multiplier.
