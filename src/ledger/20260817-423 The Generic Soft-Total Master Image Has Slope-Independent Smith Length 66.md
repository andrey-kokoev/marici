# The Generic Soft-Total Master Image Has Slope-Independent Smith Length 66

## Record

Status: truncated-DVR determinantal calculation following Entries 418 and
421.

## Hard-to-vary claim

The pointwise absence of an unclassified first-blow-up direction might hide
a slope-dependent torsion factor. The test must compare the master image
modulo exact columns, not the full exact-lift matrix, because the latter
remains row-full-rank at the center.

## Frozen invariant

On
\[
E=t,\qquad X_2=ct,
\]
write the exact-lift presentation as \([E\ M]\), where \(E\) contains the
exact columns and \(M\) the twelve source-master columns. If the generic
ranks are \(r\) and \(r+q\), compute over
\(\mathbf F[t]/(t^{12})\)
\[
\nu_{\rm master}
=
\delta_{r+q}([E\ M])-\delta_r(E),
\]
where \(\delta_k\) is the valuation of the \(k\)-th determinantal ideal.

Matrix entries were reconstructed to polynomial degree 24 and checked at
two withheld parameter values before Smith reduction.

## Result

For every generic tested slope \(c=2,3,5,7\),
\[
q=10,\qquad \nu_{\rm master}=66.
\]
This is identical at exact-form degrees 8 and 10:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\text{degree}&r&\delta_r(E)&\delta_{r+10}([E\ M])\\
\hline
8&107&85&151\\
10&142&105&171
\end{array}
\]
and both differences equal 66.

The supported slope \(c=1\), for which \(E-X_2=0\) identically, instead has
\[
q=8,\qquad \nu_{\rm master}=48
\]
at both degrees. It is therefore not part of the generic exceptional family.

Hence the tested generic exceptional factor is
\[
\boxed{t^{66}}
\]
with no observed factor depending on the exceptional coordinate \(c\).

## Interpretation

After removing the common exceptional power generated by the joint ideal,
the tested generic master-image determinant is a unit on the first blow-up.
The only distinct behavior occurs on an already frozen signed-energy
divisor. No residual coefficient-support direction was found.

This is stronger than a pointwise rank census: it measures the arcwise
torsion length of the master image modulo exact forms.

## Epistemic boundary

The calculation is over one large finite field and a truncated DVR. The
largest observed invariant valuation is below the truncation order, and the
answer is stable between exact-form degrees 8 and 10. Nevertheless, finitely
many arcs do not constitute a two-variable primary decomposition. Higher
infinitely-near support remains logically possible.

## Classification

- generic tested length: 66;
- supported rank loss at \(c=1\): existing signed-energy support;
- residual sampled exceptional-coordinate factor: none;
- new carrier datum: none.
