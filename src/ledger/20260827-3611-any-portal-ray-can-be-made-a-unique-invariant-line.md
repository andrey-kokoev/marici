# Any Portal Ray Can Be Made a Unique Invariant Line

For every prescribed nonzero coupling ray \(v\), the orthogonal involution

\[
H_v=2\frac{vv^T}{v^Tv}-I
\]

has \(v\) as its unique fixed line. Conversely, its group-average projector is

\[
P_v=\frac{H_v+I}{2}.
\]

Thus a unique invariant tensor does not select a flavor portal unless the
representation is derived independently of that portal. It can encode any
desired ray.

Transitive cyclic permutation symmetry does supply an independently familiar
unique line, but it is \((1,1,1)\) and has zero asymmetric contrast. Partial
marking by one transposition leaves a two-dimensional fixed family.
Normalizing an asymmetric invariant tensor still leaves an arbitrary scalar
coupling \(y\) and its orientation. Equivariant RG leaves a free scalar beta
function, and equivariant threshold matching leaves a free scalar multiplier.
The quadratic residue is blind to \(y\mapsto-y\), while a linear detector
retains a source-gain kernel.
