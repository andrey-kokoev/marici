# Generic Critical Exponents Do Not Define the Physical Top Connection

## Source conventions

For the one-loop three-site family,

\[
n_s=3,
\qquad
L=1.
\]

At the dimension used for the elliptic Picard--Fuchs calculation,
\(d=3\), the Cayley--Menger twist exponent is therefore

\[
\boxed{
\gamma=\frac{d-n_s-L}{2}=-\frac12.
}
\]

The source writes

\[
u=\mu_d=\kappa_0K^\gamma
\]

and places the source denominators in the differential form

\[
\varphi_{\tau_1\tau_2\tau_3}
=
\frac{dc\wedge da\wedge db}
{q_1^{\tau_1}q_2^{\tau_2}q_3^{\tau_3}}.
\]

Thus the literal proper top representative at unit powers is

\[
\boxed{
\Omega_{111}
=
\frac{dc\wedge da\wedge db}
{q_1q_2q_3\sqrt K}.
}
\]

\[

\]

for \((K,q_1,q_2,q_3)\).  These are deliberately generic regulator values
used to count the saturated critical points and hence the generic ranks.

Gauss--Manin connection.

Consequently:

- differentiating that quotient would produce a deformation of the

  mix the rank regulator with the coefficient local system.

\[
D=q_1q_2q_3
\]

and represent a top form as

\[
\omega_P=\frac{P\,dc\wedge da\wedge db}{D\sqrt K}.
\]

For a rational vector field

\[
V=V_c\partial_c+V_a\partial_a+V_b\partial_b,
\]

the exact twisted top form generated by contracting the volume form is
controlled by

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{div}V

-V(\log D).
}
\]

After multiplying by a declared common power of \(KD\), this becomes a
polynomial identity and supplies the three-variable analogue of the exact
Griffiths--Dwork certificates already used for the nine-master residue
surface.

For a kinematic direction \(\partial_{X_i}\), the unreduced derivative of a
literal source representative must include

\[
\partial_{X_i}P
-\frac{P}{2}\partial_{X_i}\log K
-P\partial_{X_i}\log D,
\]

or the corresponding moving-normal expression.  It must then be reduced
modulo the exact forms above, with the deletion filtration retained.
