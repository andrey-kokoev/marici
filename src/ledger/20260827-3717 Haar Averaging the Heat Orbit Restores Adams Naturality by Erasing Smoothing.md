# 3717 — Haar Averaging the Heat Orbit Restores Adams Naturality by Erasing Smoothing

The unique scale-invariant average of the Gaussian pivot family uses
\(d\tau/\tau\). For every integrable wavelet profile \(\varphi\), substitution
of \(u=\tau q^2\) gives

\[
\int_0^\infty
\varphi(\tau q^2)\frac{d\tau}{\tau}
=
\int_0^\infty\varphi(u)\frac{du}{u}.
\]

The resulting operator is a constant multiple of the identity on every
nonzero logarithmic label. It is Adams-natural precisely because it erases
label scale, and therefore it no longer changes distributional variance.

The raw heat average diverges. Reference subtraction yields only a
logarithmic multiplier and imports a subtraction scale. Consequently neither
a fixed heat pivot nor an invariant average of the complete heat orbit can
provide the missing dual--dual Green pairing.

The remaining live mechanisms are a pre-completion state-valued control port,
a strictly test--distribution relative correspondence, or an explicitly
cancelled scale anomaly.
