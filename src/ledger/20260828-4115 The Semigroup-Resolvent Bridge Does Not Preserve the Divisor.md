# The Semigroup--Resolvent Bridge Does Not Preserve the Divisor

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

Sampling the exponential orbit gives the canonical resolvent identity

\[
\sum_{n\ge0}X(z+n\tau)q^n
=\langle\Omega,e^{zA}(I-qe^{\tau A})^{-1}\Omega\rangle.
\]

But \(X(z)=0\) only kills the constant coefficient at \(q=0\); it does
not create a pole, kernel, or eigenvalue. The two-atom hostile example makes
this explicit. Functional calculus therefore supplies a canonical bridge
but not a divisor-preserving one. The physical readout must instead be
derived as an Evans or boundary-condition determinant.
