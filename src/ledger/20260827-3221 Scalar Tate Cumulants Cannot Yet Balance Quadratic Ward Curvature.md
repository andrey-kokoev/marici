# 3221 — Scalar Tate Cumulants Cannot Yet Balance Quadratic Ward Curvature

The prime-exclusion Ward port is a state-dependent quadratic form, whereas
the independently derived primitive, square, and connected Tate currents are
scalar determinant-line cumulants. Their scaling degrees differ, so they
cannot yet inhabit the same defect equalizer.

The missing common refinement is an operator-valued boundary lift

\[
K_{p,s,X}
\]

on the labelled coefficient module. Its determinant readout must reproduce
the Tate cumulants, while its quadratic readout can be compared with the
exclusion projection. The first real finite construction is (K_{2,s}) on
(e_1,e_2,e_3).
