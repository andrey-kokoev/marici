For the fixed-support rescaling

\[
Y_L(w)=\int_0^1\frac{\Phi(Lt)}{\Phi(0)}\cos(wt)\,dt,
\]

smoothness and evenness give

\[
\left\lVert\frac{\Phi(L\cdot)}{\Phi(0)}-1\right\rVert_{C^2[0,1]}
=O(L^2).
\]

Compact Rouché control around the zeros of \(\sin(w)/w\), combined with the
uniform endpoint expansion for remote zeros, proves that every zero of
\(Y_L\) is real and simple for all sufficiently small positive \(L\).

Together with finite-parameter sine-type control and Hurwitz stability, this
removes both spectral-boundary loopholes.  An off-real zero of the completed
transform now requires a finite real double-zero collision satisfying

\[
\int_0^L\Phi(u)\cos(xu)\,du=0,
\qquad
\int_0^L u\Phi(u)\sin(xu)\,du=0.
\]

The remaining RH-bearing theorem is the theta-specific exclusion of a common
zero of these two source integrals.
