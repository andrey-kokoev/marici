# Betti Boundary Defects Obey a Leibniz Law

Epistemic-graph event: 1330.

For composable quotient data `q:G->H` and `r:H->J`, perfect-pairing
uniqueness gives `S_(r q)=S_r S_q`.  Their boundary commutators satisfy

`Omega_(r q)=Omega_r S_q+S_r Omega_q`.

Thus physical chain maps compose, but a passing composite does not certify
its stages: transported defects may cancel.  The smallest hostile example
uses rank-one two-term complexes with `D_G=0`, `D_H=1`, `D_J=0` and identity
maps in both degrees.  Then `Omega_q=1` and `Omega_r=-1`, while the composite
commutator vanishes.

Consequently a five-site collapse of several branch bits must retain and
test every one-bit boundary packet.  Testing only the terminal multi-bit
quotient can hide physical specialization failures.  This composition law
is algebraic; it does not supply the missing source-derived relative-chain
matrices.
