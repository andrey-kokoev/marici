Gluing the two reciprocal \(p\)-valuation cones gives
\(\mathcal H_p=\ell^2(\mathbb Z)\) with unitary shift \(U_p\).  The
operator-valued cumulant

\[
\mathcal J_{p,k}(z)
=
\frac{p^{-k/2}}k
\left(
e^{kz\log p}U_p^k-e^{-kz\log p}U_p^{-k}
\right)
\]

is reciprocal-odd and skew-adjoint on \(z=it\).  Applying the algebraic
augmentation boundary to \(\mathcal J_{p,k}e_0\) recovers the scalar sinh
cumulant.

Thus the primitive and square currents can remain rigged operator forms rather
than circular scalar continuations, while the connected tail converges in
operator norm around the seam.  The bilateral chain supplies a native common
metric and adjoint; nonnormality can arise only from boundary incidence and
completion.
