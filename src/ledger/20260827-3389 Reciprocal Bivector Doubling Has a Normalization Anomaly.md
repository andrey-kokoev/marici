# Reciprocal Bivector Doubling Has a Normalization Anomaly

For a fixed exponentially decaying source, the two reciprocal tail sheets
obey exact augmentation-bivector Green identities. Their raw energies differ.

Rescaling the sheet states into a common frame equalizes their energies and
endpoint norms, but transports their forcing coefficients unequally. On exact
rational data, the forcing mismatch equals and cancels the apparent
critical-line term obtained by subtracting the two Green identities.

Therefore reciprocal doubling and state normalization alone do not confine
scalar-null states. The mate/equalizer coherence tower must transport the
complete Green current packet, rather than only the normalized states.
