# 3172 — No Finite Euler Cutoff Can Null the Faithful Two-Sector Amplitude

For finite cutoff \(X\), \(F_X^+\) has zeros only on \(\Re z=-1/2\), while
\(F_X^-=F_X^+(-z)\) has zeros only on \(\Re z=1/2\). They never vanish
simultaneously.

Hence the faithful quadratures

\[
T_X=F_X^++F_X^-,
\qquad
A_X=F_X^+-F_X^-
\]

have no common zero anywhere. On the seam their squared norm is
\(4|F_X^+|^2>0\).
