# 2800 — Local Odd-Even Dominance Does Not Compose into Inward Divisor Transport

For \(F_t=F_0+tG\), zero velocity depends on both the added block and the
background:

\[
z'(0)=-\frac{G(z_0)}{F_0'(z_0)}.
\]

An exact positive fixture with \(\cosh a=2\), \(z_0=a+i\pi\),

\[
F_0=\cosh z+4\cosh2z+\cosh3z,
\qquad
G=\cosh3z+\cosh4z,
\]

has \(F_0(z_0)=0\), equal odd/even block coefficients, and

\[
z'(0)=\frac{71}{14\sqrt3}>0.
\]

Thus local block dominance does not explain inward transport. A repaired DPC
must derive an invariant admissible background cone and control the relative
phase of \(G(z)\) and \(F'(z)\).
