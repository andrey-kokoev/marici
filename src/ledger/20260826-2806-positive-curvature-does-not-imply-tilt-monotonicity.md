\[
D''(0)=2\|T'_0f\|^2\ge0.
\]

\[
D(b)=2-2\cos b

sign and the defect returns to zero at \(2\pi\). Global tilt monotonicity needs
a source-derived spectral no-wrap or nonunitary-semigroup theorem whose window
survives completion.
