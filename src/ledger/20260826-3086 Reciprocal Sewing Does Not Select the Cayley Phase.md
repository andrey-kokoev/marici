# 3086 — Reciprocal Sewing Does Not Select the Cayley Phase

Fourier–Tate reciprocity narrows the boundary phases but does not select one.
On a two-atom spectral chart, the continuous family

\[
U_a=\operatorname{diag}(a,\bar a),
\qquad |a|=1,
\]

obeys the same reciprocal sewing law, belongs to the same maximal abelian
boundary algebra, and shares the same reciprocal-invariant cyclic vector.
Its self-adjoint Cayley generator still varies with \(a\).

The missing comparison channel is therefore an oriented phase selector: a
source-derived boundary current, infinitesimal generator, or connection.
Reciprocity fixes conjugacy of the two sheets, not their rotor angle.

## Scope

The result closes selection by abstract reciprocal covariance alone. The
complete labelled theta/Tate source may still determine the phase through an
additional current.
