\[
M_X=\sum_{j:\,X\subseteq(w_j=0)}M_j.

\[
\boxed{
[M_i,M_X]=0
\quad
\text{for every }i\text{ with }X\subseteq(w_i=0).
}
\]

Exact integer arithmetic gives

\[
\boxed{

Thus all codimension-two coherence is already generated by the frozen
hyperplane incidence lattice.
