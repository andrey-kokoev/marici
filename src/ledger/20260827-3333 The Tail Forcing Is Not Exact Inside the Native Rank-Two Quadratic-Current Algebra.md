# The Tail Forcing Is Not Exact Inside the Native Rank-Two Quadratic-Current Algebra

For the real native tail system

\[
G'=-sG-cf,
\qquad c'=0,
\]

the most general constant-coefficient quadratic current is

\[
J=AG^2+BGc+Cc^2.
\]

Its derivative is

\[
J'=-2AsG^2-(2Af+Bs)Gc-Bfc^2.
\]

For nonzero `s` and nontrivial `f`, eliminating the unwanted `G^2` and `c^2`
channels forces `A=B=0`, leaving no `Gc` coefficient. Therefore the forcing
channel `2fGc` is not the derivative of a local constant-coefficient quadratic
current built only from the native variables `(G,c)`.

The modular closure must introduce genuine additional structure: an
independent seam/source-primitive coordinate, source-derived variable
coefficients, or a reciprocal-sheet cancellation. A formal nonlocal
antiderivative remains inadmissible as an explanation.

This is the local algebraic counterpart of the earlier completion result that
the seam cannot be reconstructed boundedly from the tail. The next minimal
calculation is the quadratic-current audit after adjoining the actual
source-derived seam flow.
