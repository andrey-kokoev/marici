# 2109 — Selector and Rigidifier Are Independent Operations

Selection reduces an admissible lens fiber; rigidification reduces variation
of a readout across presentations.  A finite four-case census realizes all
four logical combinations, so neither property implies the other.

This corrects the cross-sector architecture to

\[
\text{Carrier}
\longrightarrow
\text{admissible lens family}
\xrightarrow{\text{source selector}}
\text{selected lens}
\xrightarrow{\text{presentation rigidifier}}
\text{stable readout}.
\]

The Gaussian quadratic Hamiltonian is a genuine selector.  The flavor
texture atlas is currently a rigidifier without a numerical selector.
Magnetic stabilization and the RH repair operator require separate typing.
