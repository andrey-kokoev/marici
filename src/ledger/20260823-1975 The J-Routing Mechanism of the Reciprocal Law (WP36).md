# WP36: the J-routing mechanism of the reciprocal law - why th_u(phase).sin(phase) is class-constant

WP35 established the chart law th_u(phi) = 0.0389/sin(phi) - 0.0001 for
the mixed u-singlet branch (equivalently H_12.sin(phi) = 3.621e-2,
cross-class). This package derives the mechanism. Structural trigger:
ALL 303 u-class sheets place the phase edge in the DOWN sector, so Yu
(and Hu) is real and carries no explicit phi - the law cannot be a
per-sheet algebraic identity; it must be a readout constraint.

Method (checker `checkers/wp36_j_routing.py`, results
`results/wp36_j_routing.json`, 303 u-class sheets, 5/5 gates).

## Findings

1. Exact commutator identity (G1, max err 2.1e-34 on all 303 sheets).
   In the canonical frame (singlet s at index 0, block [[a,c],[c,b]]),
   writing u = Hd_01, v = Hd_02, w = Hd_12:

       det[Hu,Hd] = 2i ( c Im(w) (|C1|^2 - |C2|^2)
                         - Im(C1 C12 conj(C2)) ),
       C1 = u(s-a) - c v,  C2 = v(s-b) - c u,
       C12 = w(a-b) + c(Hd_22 - Hd_11).

   The c^0 piece is -Im(u w conj(v)) (s-a)(a-b)(s-b): the DOWN-SECTOR
   LOOP PRODUCT Hd_01 Hd_12 Hd_02* - a second, independent J source
   living entirely in the down chart.

2. J-routing branch theorem (G2, exact). Zeroing c = H_12 kills J on
   every mixed sheet (J -> 3.5e-18 relative) and leaves J exactly
   unchanged on every unmixed sheet (deviation 0.0). So the physical J
   is generated by two DIFFERENT chart structures: on the unmixed
   branch it is the down-loop product; on the mixed branch it is the
   c-linear terms of the identity. The WP35 mixed/unmixed split is the
   discrete choice of J's chart carrier.

3. First-harmonic exactness (G3, max rel err 4.3e-16). Per sheet, every
   Im(Hd_xy) scales exactly as sin(phi) under phi rescaling at fixed
   magnitudes: Im(Hd_xy) = (magnitude product) . sin(phi), no higher
   harmonics (this is the single-phase-edge structure made exact).

4. Magnitude-only factorization (G4, max rel variation 3.6e-11). Per
   mixed sheet, det[Hu,Hd] = -2i . c . sin(phi) . M(mags) with M
   phi-independent at fixed magnitudes: no cos(phi) contamination.
   Combined with the Jarlskog identity det[Hu,Hd] = -2i J Du Dd this is
   the exact per-sheet theorem

       J . Du . Dd = c . sin(phi) . M(mags).

5. Emergent class constancy (G5). Since the fit pins J and the masses,
   the theorem forces q = c.sin(phi_folded) = J.Du.Dd/M. Measured:
   q = 0.0362132 with cross-class relative spread 2.9e-5 - the WP35
   reciprocal law recovered as the emergent, class-level constancy of
   M, not as a per-sheet identity. (M's own raw cross-class spread,
   9.1e-3, is dominated by fit jitter of Du through the tiny difference
   yc^2 - yu^2; within-class spread is fit phi-jitter acting on the
   smooth law.) The chain is complete: J fixed + M class-constant =>
   c.sin(phi) const => th_u = c/Delta_block (1 + O(th^2)) obeys the
   reciprocal law. The b = -1.3e-4 intercept of WP35 is the O(th^2)
   atan2 correction plus subleading identity terms.

## Typing

G1-G4 are exact chart-atlas theorems (machine floor); G5 is an emergent
ensemble law at 2.9e-5. The mechanism does NOT restore physical
invariance of the loop phase (WP11 stands): phi remains chart data, and
the law lives on the fit-solution manifold. What WP36 adds is that the
chart atlas fibers the physical readout through an exact identity with
a discrete J-routing bit - the two branches are two chart presentations
of the same physical J, precisely the chart/quotient distinction the
program tracks elsewhere.
