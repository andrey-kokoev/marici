# The Charged Cycle Backreacts on Neutral Yukawas at One Loop

On a frozen one-generation, diagonal-mass, zero-momentum benchmark, the two
charged cross-edges close through a finite loop and correct both neutral quark
Yukawas. The positive scalar kernel is

\[
K_5={1\over16\pi^2}\int_0^\infty
{t^2\,dt\over\prod_{i=1}^5(t+m_i^2)}.
\]

Exact chiral-projector algebra supplies the momentum numerator. At equal
positive masses,

\[
K_5={1\over192\pi^2M^4}.
\]

The up correction carries
\(Y_H^uC_AY_S^dC_B^*Y_X^u sxM_{A^u}M_{A^d}\); the down correction carries
its sector-conjugate monomial. Both have magnitude \(1/(192\pi^2)\) on the
unit slice and vanish if either cross-edge is removed.

Thus the charged extension's exact product form is only tree-level. The loop
is a genuine neutral-flavor backreaction, but not a selector: its coupling,
vev, and mass coordinates remain free. Generation-tensor lifting, canonical
rediagonalization, and a `physical16` response-rank audit are next.
