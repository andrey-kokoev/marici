# 3304 — Full Mellin Covariance Forbids a Fixed Pointed Label Cone

## Result

On a pure label at nonzero logarithmic coordinate `q`, vertical Mellin
transport acts by

\[
M_t e_q=e^{itq}e_q.
\]

At `t=pi/q`, this sends `e_q` to `-e_q`. Any fixed real convex cone preserved
by the full Mellin group and containing `e_q` therefore contains both rays and
cannot be pointed.

This closes the simplest ordered-module repair of the scalar zero problem.
Any surviving order must vary with spectral height, quotient common phase in
a relational direct--dual object, omit pure labels in favor of distinguished
completed packets, restrict the transport semigroup, or live only on a
nonlinear source orbit.

## Scope

This does not prove RH and does not exclude transported cone fields or
relational orders. It excludes one fixed pointed cone containing pure labels
and invariant under every vertical Mellin translation.
