# 664 — Neither Physical Occurrence Branch Has Quartic Discriminant

## Hard-to-vary claim

The quartic \(\mathcal Q\) is not the branch-collision discriminant of
either individual physical occurrence section on the
post-\(q_{G_{12}}\) Cayley--Menger surface.

## Source-preserving restrictions

Entry 663 rejects intersecting the two occurrence sections. Preserve them
separately instead.

For the \(q_{g_{23}}\) summand set \(b=x\). Since \(K_E\) is even in
\(a\), write

\[
K_E(a,x)=x^2u^2+C_a u+R_a,
\qquad u=a^2,
\]

where

\[
C_a=x^2(x^2-y^2-z^2)+E^2(y^2-x^2-z^2),
\qquad
R_a=y^2x^4+C_bx^2+H.
\]

For the reflected \(q_{g_{31}}\) summand set \(a=y\):

\[
K_E(y,b)=y^2v^2+C_b v+R_b,
\qquad v=b^2,
\]

with

\[
C_b=y^2(y^2-x^2-z^2)+E^2(x^2-y^2-z^2),
\qquad
R_b=x^2y^4+C_ay^2+H,
\]

and

\[
H=z^2E^4+E^2z^2(z^2-x^2-y^2)+z^2x^2y^2.
\]

Their branch discriminants are therefore

\[
\Delta_{23}=C_a^2-4x^2R_a,
\qquad
\Delta_{31}=C_b^2-4y^2R_b.
\]

## Exact divisibility gate

Expand all polynomials in \(\mathbb Z[x,y,z]\), with
\(E=x+y+z\), and divide each degree-eight discriminant by

\[
\mathcal Q
=-16x^2y^2-8xyE^2+8(x+y)E^3-5E^4.
\]

Multivariate division over
\(\mathbb F_{2305843009213693951}\) gives nonzero remainders containing
thirty monomials in both cases:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal Q\nmid\Delta_{23},
\qquad
\mathcal Q\nmid\Delta_{31}.
}
\]

This finite-field result is an exact nondivisibility certificate over
\(\mathbb Q\): an integral polynomial identity
\(\Delta_i=\mathcal Q R\) would remain an identity after reduction
modulo this prime, while \(\mathcal Q\) does not reduce to zero.
