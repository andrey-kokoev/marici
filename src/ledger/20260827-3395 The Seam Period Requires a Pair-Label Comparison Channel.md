# The Seam Period Requires a Pair-Label Comparison Channel

The period comparing the two endpoint homotopies is quadratic in the labelled
source. It contains off-diagonal pair-label interference terms.

The smallest hostile pair has identical individual periods in two cases, but
aggregate periods `2` and `0`, depending only on the relative sign. Thus an
additive collection of one-label primitive or square energies cannot determine
the seam period.

The final comparison tower in the `3+2+2+1` architecture must begin with a
polarized pair-label Gram current. The next gate is whether positive Fock
coproduct and Tate sewing derive its off-diagonal entries.
