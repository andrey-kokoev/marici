# Invariant Positivity Exists Only After Label Completion

For

\[
\Phi=\phi_1+\tau,
\]

the primitive invariant projection \(\mathsf E\phi_1\) is eventually negative.
Because the completed invariant source \(\Phi\) is positive,

\[
\mathsf E\tau=\Phi-\mathsf E\phi_1>\Phi
\]

through that region. The tail exactly overshoots the primitive invariant
deficit.

Projection and label summation commute algebraically, but positivity does not
descend to the projected labels. The source must be completed before its
reflection-invariant readout is treated as positive.

This rules out labelwise positive-transform proofs obtained by symmetrizing
primitive theta terms separately.
