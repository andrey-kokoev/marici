recorded as phenomenology of the research process, not as mathematical

\to

\to
\text{local stabilizer}

A physical frame is not a universally preferred primitive. Generic local
curvature supplies its own principal frame. Where symmetry leaves a
stabilizer, explicitly supplied relations to matter, radiation, detectors, or
boundary conditions may reduce that residual freedom. Perfect symmetry
correctly leaves a physical ambiguity.

This explains both the availability of inertial directions in generic
environments and their non-uniqueness in symmetric ones without violating
local Lorentz invariance.

The research-process lesson is distinct from the physics claim:

\[
\boxed{
\text{intuition selected the pressure direction;}
\qquad
\text{falsification determined the surviving explanation}.
}
\]

Future operators should preserve four separate records:

1. the phenomenology and confidence of an intuition;
2. the conjecture it motivates;
3. the sequence of tests and supersessions;
4. the final explanatory survivor and its remaining falsifiers.

An intuition is valuable even when its first literal interpretation fails.
Its value is measured by whether sustained, disciplined pressure exposes a
hard-to-vary explanatory structure. It must never be promoted directly to
evidence or retroactively rewritten to match the survivor.
