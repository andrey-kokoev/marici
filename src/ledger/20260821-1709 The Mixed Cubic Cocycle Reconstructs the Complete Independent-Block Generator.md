## Independent blocks

For independent centered Gaussian blocks, define

\[
Q=\sum_iw_iQ_i,
\qquad
A=\sum_iw_i^2a_i.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
-t(Q^2-A)
=-t\sum_iw_i^2(Q_i^2-a_i)
-2t\sum_{i<j}w_iw_jQ_iQ_j.
}
\]

The pure terms are exactly the cardinality-rescaled local cubic evolutions.
The mixed term is the labelled binary cocycle derived in Entries 1694--1695.

The merged variables remain Gaussian before the shear, with

\[
A=\sum_iw_i^2a_i,
\qquad
B=\sum_iw_i^2b_i.
\]

Substitution into Entry 1708 therefore gives the complete merged generator

\[
K_{A,B,t}(x,y)
=\frac B2y^2+tAy-\frac12\log(1+2Aty)
+\frac{Ax^2}{2(1+2Aty)}.
\]

Every mixed cumulant is supplied by the multinomial expansion of powers of
`A`; no coefficientwise fitting is used.

## Narrow result

\[
\boxed{
\text{cardinality-weighted local evolution plus the labelled mixed cubic cocycle reconstructs the complete independent-block cumulant generator.}
}
\]

Thus the infinite coefficient object has an exact Cut coproduct generated by
