# 3322 — Simple Zero-State Fibers Do Not Force Real--Reciprocal Isotropy

## Result

One-dimensionality of a zero-state kernel is fiberwise. Reciprocal and Real
symmetries transport it to kernel lines over different spectral parameters;
simplicity supplies no identification between those fibers.

The exact real-even quartic

\[
H_{a,b}(z)
=
\left((z-a)^2+b^2\right)
\left((z+a)^2+b^2\right)
\]

has four simple off-seam roots. The scalar multiplication family `D_z=H(z)`
has a one-dimensional kernel at every root while preserving the complete
Real--reciprocal quartet.

Seam confinement therefore requires a common comparison fiber, equality of
the reciprocal and Real zero-state images in that fiber, and faithfulness in
the spectral parameter.

## Scope

This does not exclude a source-derived comparison fiber or prove RH. It
excludes simple zeros and one-dimensional kernels as sufficient mechanisms.
