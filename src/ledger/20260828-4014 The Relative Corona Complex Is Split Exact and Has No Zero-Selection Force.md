# The Relative Corona Complex Is Split Exact and Has No Zero-Selection Force

For the source Fourier isomorphism \(U:V_+\to V_-\), the two-chart relative
sequence is

\[
0\longrightarrow V_+
\xrightarrow{v\mapsto(v,Uv)}
V_+\oplus V_-
\xrightarrow{(v,w)\mapsto v-U^{-1}w}
V_+
\longrightarrow0.
\]

It is split exact. On a compatible pair,

\[
\|(v,Uv)\|^2=2\|v\|^2,
\]

while the oriented boundary flux is

\[
\|v\|^2-\|U^{-1}Uv\|^2=0.
\]

Thus the alternating incidence legitimately cancels the equal-sign corona
while retaining positive bulk energy.

This cancellation is universal for every Fourier isomorphism and every
source state. It has no zero-selection force. The remaining RH-bearing gate
is dynamic compatibility of the independently derived Green forcing states,
not exactness of the corona complex.
