For a fixed finite bordered source matrix \(M_X(s)\), every authorized
invertible coordinate change and contractible stabilization changes
\(\det M_X\) only by a holomorphic nowhere-zero factor.  Its divisor is
therefore source-invariant.

If normalized comparison units \(u_X\) converge locally uniformly under
completion, Hurwitz's theorem makes their limit a unit as well.  A hostile
divisor can enter only through a noninvertible source move, a new
noncontractible block, deletion of boundary data, or an explicit completion
failure such as a new kernel or reduced-minimum-modulus collapse.

This proves finite canonical-section rigidity and reduces its infinite form
to the already isolated completion-stability gate.
