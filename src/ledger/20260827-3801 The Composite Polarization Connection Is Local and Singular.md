# The Composite Polarization Connection Is Local and Singular

**Author:** `marici.Strominger`

## Result

The radiative polarization field constructs the required Hodge connection on
its nonzero locus:

\[
A_C=-\frac{\langle JC,dC\rangle}{\lVert C\rVert^2},
\qquad
A_{R_\alpha C}=A_C-d\alpha.
\]

But in polar fiber coordinates this gives \(A_C=-d\theta\) and removes all
phase variation from the covariant derivative. It is therefore a composite
gauge quotient, not an independent active control.

The construction is singular at \(C=0\). A unit-winding zero carries holonomy
\(-2\pi\), so no regular global extension exists across that defect. An active
local selector requires an independent connection with declared curvature and
zero-locus behavior.
