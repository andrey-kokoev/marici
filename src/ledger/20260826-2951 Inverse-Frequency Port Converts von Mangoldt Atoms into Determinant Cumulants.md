The labelled explicit-formula atom at logarithmic frequency \(k\log p\) has
weight

\[
(\log p)p^{-k/2}.
\]

The Fourier-conjugated order port divides nonzero frequencies by
\(k\log p\), producing exactly

\[
\frac{p^{-k/2}}k.
\]

Thus inverse logarithmic frequency converts the von Mangoldt distribution
into the primitive, square, and connected determinant cumulants.  The missing
labelled-to-Hardy incidence is the integrated Tate--Weil explicit formula.

The zero-frequency extension remains an independent archimedean boundary
coordinate.  The incidence is now derived, but its required positivity is a
Weil-type RH criterion; no zero-confinement theorem follows yet.
