# 2816 — The Relative-Gain Cocycle Needs a Bounded Normalization Coboundary

Relative gains transform under frame changes by

\[
\gamma_e'=\gamma_e\frac{q_{s(e)}}{q_{t(e)}}.
\]

Every finite open chain is algebraically trivializable. Cycle products are
gauge-invariant and must equal one for a global unit-gain frame. On an infinite
ray, the trivializing frames are topology-preserving exactly when partial gain
products remain uniformly bounded above and below. Constant gain \(1/2\)
gives a degenerate frame \(2^{-N}q_0\), while alternating gains \(2,1/2\)
remain bounded.
