# 2845 — Positive Self-Fourier Poisson Sewing Still Allows Off-Critical Mellin Zeros

Adding a sufficiently small positive multiple of Nima's Fourier-fixed
degree-twelve direction to the Fourier-fixed Gaussian produces a strictly
positive, even, Schwartz, Fourier-fixed carrier. Its normalized Mellin window
is

\[
Q_\delta(y)=1+\delta A(y),
\]

where (A) is a real cubic with positive leading coefficient. Its
discriminant is

\[
-27a^2\delta^2+O(\delta^3)<0
\]

for small positive (delta), so it has nonreal (y)-roots and hence
off-critical Mellin zeros.

Thus positive source carriers and exact primal--dual Poisson sewing still do
not orient the divisor. The next source discriminator must select the Gaussian
vacuum inside the positive Fourier-fixed orbit, then couple that law to the
arithmetic comb.
