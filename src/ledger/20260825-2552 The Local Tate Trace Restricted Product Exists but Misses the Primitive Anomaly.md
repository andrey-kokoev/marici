# 2552 — The Local Tate Trace Restricted Product Exists but Misses the Primitive Anomaly

At finite \(p\), the trace target consists of locally constant functions on
\(\mathbb Q_p^\times\) that vanish at sufficiently negative valuation and
stabilize at sufficiently positive valuation, retaining the value at zero.
These local traces form a Fourier-stable restricted product with the standard
unramified vectors. The primitive cutoff coordinate nevertheless diverges on
the infinite unramified net.

## Scope

The ordinary restricted-product trace exists, but it is not yet the complete
boundary value type and no completed operator closability is claimed.
