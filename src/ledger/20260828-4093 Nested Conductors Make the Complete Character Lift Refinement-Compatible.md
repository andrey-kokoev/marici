# Nested Conductors Make the Complete Character Lift Refinement-Compatible

## Cofinal conductor tower

For the label cutoff

\[
L_N=\{1,2,\ldots,N\},
\]

choose

\[
Q_N=(N+1)!.
\]

Then:

- \(Q_N>N\), so reduction modulo \(Q_N\) is injective on \(L_N\);
- \(Q_N\mid Q_{N+1}\), so the conductors form a nested cofinal tower.

Let

\[
\chi_{N,k}(n)
=
e^{2\pi i kn/Q_N},
\qquad
0\leq k<Q_N.
\]

The complete character analysis at level \(N\) is the discrete Fourier
transform of the coefficient packet extended by zero to
\(\mathbb Z/Q_N\mathbb Z\).

## Exact refinement law

Write

\[
d_N=\frac{Q_{N+1}}{Q_N}.
\]

Every coarse character is exactly a fine character:

\[
\chi_{N,k}(n)
=
\chi_{N+1,d_Nk}(n).
\]

Therefore extension of an old label packet by zero and restriction of the fine
analysis to the embedded coarse frequencies commute exactly.

The diagram joining:

- old coefficient synthesis;
- extension by zero;
- coarse Fourier analysis;
- fine Fourier analysis;
- restriction to coarse frequencies

is strictly commutative.

No fitted normalization is needed when each finite Fourier transform uses the
normalized Haar pairing on its cyclic group.

At every level, the complete character table is injective on the retained
labels. Suppose two proposed finite lifts agree on every character row and are
