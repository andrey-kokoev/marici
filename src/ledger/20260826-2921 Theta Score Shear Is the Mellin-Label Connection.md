For labelled synthesis \(T\), Mellin generator \(Qe_n=\log(n)e_n\), and
primitive seam-matched score synthesis \(S\), the exact operator identity is

\[
S
=
\left(\partial_u+2A_0u\right)T+2A_0TQ.
\]

Thus the logarithmic theta shear is the connection term between
archimedean translation and Mellin label transport.  This explains why a
single Gaussian cannot see the transverse channel.

The connection is flat: \([\partial_u,Q]=0\).  Ordinary finite-label and
exact-tail projections are also diagonal, so they commute with \(Q\).  The
only surviving curvature candidate is therefore the typed generator-reversal
anomaly of finite primal--dual Fourier--Tate sewing,

\[
\mathcal A_N=Q_-W_N+W_NQ_+.
\]

A useful anomaly must be nonzero, source-typed by the declared boundary
currents, and stable under completion.  Exact vanishing or an untyped residual
closes the route.
