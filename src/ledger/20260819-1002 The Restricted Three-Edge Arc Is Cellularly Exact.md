\[

## Result

\[
\boxed{

\quad\text{in}\quad
H^1(C^\bullet_{\rm chamber}(\mathcal K_{\rm KN})).
}
\]

The three-edge arc is a nonzero cochain but a zero cohomology class in the complete twisted cellular complex.  Its transported two-cell closure is therefore strict cellular exactness, not evidence for an additional degree-one coefficient object.
