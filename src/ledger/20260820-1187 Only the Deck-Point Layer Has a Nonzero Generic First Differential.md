## Typed vanishing statements

On the generic normal-crossing locus, Entry 1186's first logarithmic
differential is largely forced.

For every marked surface, the pair curve is anticanonical. Hence every
primitive \(E_7\) or \(A_1^3\) class restricts with degree zero:

\[
E_7/A_1^3\longrightarrow H^2(C_{ij})
\quad\text{vanishes on the primitive kernel}.
\]

\[
H^1(D_i)=0
\]

for degree-two del Pezzo surfaces and their rational \(A_1\) contractions,

\[
H^1(P_{ijk})=0

for triple points. Therefore the elliptic pair systems receive and emit no
generic first differential.

\[
\bigoplus_{i<j}H^0(C_{ij})
\longrightarrow
\bigoplus_{i<j<k}H^0(P_{ijk,+}\sqcup P_{ijk,-}).
\]

## Exact deck-point differential

\[
1\longmapsto(1,1),
\]

with the standard signed triangle boundary. For \(m\) distinct geometric
marks, its rank is

\[
\binom{m-1}{2}.
\]

The deck-anti-invariant line at every triple is untouched. The invariant
cokernel has rank \(\binom{m-1}{3}\). Thus

\[

\mathbf Q_-^{\binom m3}
