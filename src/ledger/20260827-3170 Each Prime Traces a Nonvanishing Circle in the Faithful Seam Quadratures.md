# 3170 — Each Prime Traces a Nonvanishing Circle in the Faithful Seam Quadratures

For \(a=p^{-1/2}\), \(z=it\), and \(B=-iA\), the faithful local
quadratures satisfy

\[
(T-2)^2+B^2=\frac4p.
\]

Each prime therefore traces a circle centered at \((2,0)\) with radius
\(2/\sqrt p<2\). It excludes the origin and has zero local winding.

## Scope

The circle is the local two-sector relationship orbit, not a zero locus. Any
completed scalar zero must arise from cross-prime coupling, completion, or
projection.
