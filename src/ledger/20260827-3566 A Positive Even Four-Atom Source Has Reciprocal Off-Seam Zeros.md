# 3566 — A Positive Even Four-Atom Source Has Reciprocal Off-Seam Zeros

Take positive weights \(1\) at \(q=\pm1\) and \(1/4\) at \(q=\pm2\). The
bilateral transform is even and conjugation-real:

\[
F(z)=e^z+e^{-z}+\frac14(e^{2z}+e^{-2z}).
\]

Writing \(y=e^z+e^{-z}\), its zero equation is

\[
y^2+4y-2=0.
\]

The root \(y=-2-\sqrt6<-2\) yields two distinct negative reciprocal values of
\(e^z\) with unequal moduli, hence reciprocal off-seam zeros.

Therefore positivity, exact reflection symmetry, conjugation symmetry, and
equal weights inside every reflected atom pair still do not imply zero
confinement. The missing theta law must constrain cross-scale interference,
not merely reciprocal pairwise balance.

Checker result: `6/6`.
