# 3582 — Triangle-to-Signed Comparison Units Vanish Only on Soft Endpoints

## Hard-to-vary claim

Along every complete physical triangle--signed intersection branch, the
source-labelled comparison coefficient factors explicitly. It has no nonsoft
physical zero.

Every physical zero is an existing coordinate-soft endpoint.

## Exact branch functions

Using a rational branch parameter (s), the eight physical sheets have
comparison functions of the form

\[
\pm24s(s+1)
\]

or

\[
\pm24s(s-1).
\]

The repeated sheets arise where the fold equation has two nonnegative choices
for the loop edge (c).

For unbounded branches with (s\geq0), the only admitted zero is (s=0);
the second algebraic zero (s=-1) is outside the physical chamber.

For compact branches with (0\leq s\leq1), the zeros are (s=0) and
(s=1).

## Support classification

Substitution into the labelled parameterizations identifies every physical
zero as

\[
a=0
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
b=0.
\]

Thus all failures of the generic odd-line isomorphism lie on existing
coordinate-soft support.

At some (c=0) points the particular signed-normal lift used by the checker
changes chart, but the comparison coefficient remains nonzero. Those are
coordinate failures, not support of the comparison cone.
