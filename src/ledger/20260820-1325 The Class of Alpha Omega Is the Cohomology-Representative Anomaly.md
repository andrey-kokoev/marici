# The Class of Alpha Omega Is the Cohomology-Representative Anomaly

Epistemic-graph event: 1342.

A defective graded map `S` induces `H^n(H)->H^n(G)` exactly when:

- every target `n`-cocycle annihilates `Omega_(n+1)`; and
- every class `tau_n(alpha)=[alpha Omega_n]` vanishes for target
  `(n-1)`-cochains `alpha`.

The identity

`S^*(d_H alpha)=d_G(S^*alpha)+alpha Omega_n`

shows that `tau_n(alpha)` is precisely the obstruction to independence from
the target cocycle representative.  The defect Bianchi identity makes
`alpha Omega_n` a source cocycle once all target cocycles pass the preceding
annihilation condition.

Thus descent of one selected paired observable, cohomological pullback, and
strict chain compatibility are three distinct levels.  Exact-form changes
in the five-site integrand are physically harmless only after the anomaly
classes vanish in source-derived relative complexes.
