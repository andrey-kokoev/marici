\(PSL_2(\mathbb Z)\) map whose kernel is the Klein four group. Its three

\[

\]

Consequently the endpoint-permutation map is injective on the entire
projective kernel. The joint endpoint-projective map is faithful on
\(M(0,4)\).

\[
B_4(S^2)/\langle\Omega\rangle\cong M(0,4),

\Omega=(\sigma_1\sigma_2\sigma_3)^4,
\]

the joint map on the spherical braid group has exactly the binary central
kernel \(\langle\Omega\rangle\). Adding the central lift therefore terminates
the coherence tower for the four-zero model.
