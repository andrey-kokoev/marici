# The Connected Square Current Is the Order Parameter for the Minimal Reciprocal Zero Split

For the positive reciprocal three-cell source

\[
B\delta_{-1}+A\delta_0+B\delta_1,
\qquad c=A/B,
\]

the transform zeros obey

\[
r^2+cr+1=0,
\qquad r=e^z.
\]

Its connected square coefficient is

\[
a_2=1-\frac{c^2}{2},
\]

and the reciprocal-root discriminant is exactly

\[
c^2-4=-2(a_2+1).
\]

Therefore `a_2>-1` keeps the pair on the unit-circle seam, `a_2=-1`
produces the double collision at `r=-1`, and `a_2<-1` splits the pair into
two negative reciprocal radii off the seam. The primitive coefficient
`a_1=c` remains positive throughout and cannot see the transition.

Thus the grade-two connected current is the exact order parameter for the
minimal reciprocal seam bifurcation. Extending this finite identity to the
global theta divisor requires a new comparison from the pro-valued square
current to the completed zero-state boundary condition.
