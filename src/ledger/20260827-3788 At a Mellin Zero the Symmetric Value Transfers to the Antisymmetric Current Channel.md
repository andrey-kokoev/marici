# 3788 — At a Mellin Zero the Symmetric Value Transfers to the Antisymmetric Current Channel

Let \(K(z)\) and \(L(z)\) be the one-sided value and moving-endpoint-current
transforms. The incidence Ward identity is

\[
L(z)=-k(0)-(z+1/2)K(z).
\]

For symmetric and antisymmetric value channels

\[
F=\frac{K(z)+K(-z)}2,
\qquad
G=\frac{K(z)-K(-z)}2,
\]

and completed current channel

\[
C=\frac{L(z)+L(-z)}2,
\]

one obtains

\[
C=-k(0)-\frac12F-zG.
\]

At a scalar zero, therefore,

\[
C+k(0)=-zG.
\]

The full state has not vanished. Meaning lost from the symmetric scalar
projection has transferred to the antisymmetric current channel. This makes
the value-current packet faithful, but does not confine zeros because the law
holds on and off the seam alike.

The remaining RH content is a source-derived sector-control comparison that,
on the antipodal locus alone, forces \(\operatorname{Re}z=0\). This is weaker
and more precise than global de Branges modulus dominance.
