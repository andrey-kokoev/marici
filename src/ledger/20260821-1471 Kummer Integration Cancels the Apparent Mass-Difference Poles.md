# 1471 — Kummer Integration Cancels the Apparent Mass-Difference Poles

## Status

Exact coefficient-side audit of the first integrated perturbative-mass
example in Benincasa, arXiv:1909.02517v1, Eq. (4.7). The apparent poles at
\(y=x_1\) and \(y=x_2\) are removable. Positive-Kummer integration cancels
spurious difference support rather than creating new carrier walls.

## Frozen source coefficient

For one white-site insertion in de Sitter, the source integral is

\[
\boxed{
F(x_1,x_2;y)
=
\frac{N(x_1,x_2;y)}
{(y^2-x_1^2)(y^2-x_2^2)},
}
\]

where

\[
\begin{aligned}
N={}&(x_1+x_2)\log(x_1+x_2)
+2y\log(2y)\\
&-(x_1+y)\log(x_1+y)
-(y+x_2)\log(y+x_2).
\end{aligned}
\]

## Exact cancellation

At \(y=x_1\), the four numerator terms pair as

\[
(x_1+x_2)\log(x_1+x_2)
-(x_1+x_2)\log(x_1+x_2)
\]

and

\[
2x_1\log(2x_1)-2x_1\log(2x_1).
\]

Hence

\[
N|_{y=x_1}=0.
\]

The same labelled pairing with \(x_1\leftrightarrow x_2\) gives

\[
N|_{y=x_2}=0.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Res}_{y=x_1}F
=
\operatorname{Res}_{y=x_2}F
=0.
}
\]

At generic points of these divisors, the finite limits are

\[
\left.F\right|_{y\to x_1}
=
\frac{\log\!\left(2x_1/(x_1+x_2)\right)}
{2x_1(x_1^2-x_2^2)},
\]

and its exchanged counterpart at \(y=x_2\).

## Surviving coefficient alphabet

The integrated expression retains the logarithmic letters

\[
x_1+x_2,
\qquad
2y,
\qquad
x_1+y,
\qquad
y+x_2.
\]

These are the total-energy, internal-energy normalization, and existing
singleton partial-energy letters. The difference factors \(y-x_i\) occur in
an intermediate rational presentation but do not survive as singular support.

two-white-site weight-two coefficient or the all-insertion resummation.
