# 813 — Coordinate-Boundary Sextics Develop an A3 Soft-Signed Excess

## Frozen object

Use Entry 807's source-normalized Cayley--Menger polynomial in

\[
A=a^2,\qquad B=b^2
\]

and retain the ordered residue orientation \(da\wedge db\).  This entry
analyzes the labelled coordinate boundaries; it does not infer multiplicity
from the scalar sextic.

## The single \(a=0\) branch

On \(P_2\ne0\), complete the \(B\)-square and call the translated coordinate
\(y\).  The exact formal expression is

\[
\boxed{
K_{\rm CM}
=P_2^2y^2+
\frac{\Lambda}{4P_2^2}
\left[4E^2P_2^2-\bigl(a^2-(E^2+P_2^2)\bigr)^2\right].
}
\]

Consequently the branch discriminant is

\[
-\frac{(E-P_2)^2(E+P_2)^2\Lambda}{4P_2^2}.
\]

At a generic signed-energy point \(E=\pm P_2\), with \(\Lambda\ne0\),
the completed fiber is an \(A_1\) singularity.  Its Milnor and
anti-invariant Kato ranks are both one.  The generic Entry 804 Kummer line
specializes onto this class, generated by the existing signed-energy nearby
cycle.  The signed normal occurs quadratically, so the coarse monodromy is
the square of the \(A_1\) monodromy; the resolved occurrence remembers the
sheet.

At generic \(\Lambda=0\), the \(a\)-coordinate is free and the singularity
is Morse--Bott with transverse rank one.  Its class is generated by the
existing momentum-triangle Gysin map.

At \(E=\pm P_2\) together with \(\Lambda=0\), the reduced signed and
triangle divisors meet regularly.  Their iterated vanishing rank remains
one, with no reduced excess \(\operatorname{Tor}_1\); the signed direction
retains its length-two Cartier layer.

The locus \(E=0\) is not a singular branch here at generic \(P_2\Lambda\ne0\).
When \(P_2=0\), the affine critical section \(B_{\rm crit}\) has a pole and
exits to the infinity/soft closure.  It must not be counted as another finite
\(a=0\) class.

## The labelled \(b=0\) branch

The source-labelled exchange

\[
(a,P_2)\longleftrightarrow(b,P_1)
\]

gives the second completion.  Its support is

\[
E-P_1=0,\qquad E+P_1=0,\qquad \Lambda=0,
\]

with the same \(A_1\), transverse Morse--Bott, and iterated rank-one
classifications.  The exchange preserves the fixed ambient orientation only
after retaining the corresponding residue-order sign; the two occurrence
objects are not silently identified.

## The double coordinate boundary

At \(a=b=0\),

\[
K_{\rm CM}=P_3^2R_{00},
\]

where

\[
R_{00}=E^4-E^2(P_1^2+P_2^2-P_3^2)+P_1^2P_2^2.
\]

At a generic point of either \(P_3=0\) or \(R_{00}=0\), the two quadratic
normal coefficients do not vanish simultaneously.  The local singularity is
\(A_1\), generated respectively by the existing soft nearby cycle or the
restricted Cayley--Menger Gysin map.  The soft normal enters through
\(P_3^2\), hence carries a length-two Cartier layer.

There is a genuine enhancement at the soft--signed corners

\[
P_3=0,\quad E^2=P_1^2
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
P_3=0,\quad E^2=P_2^2.
\]

After the appropriate labelled exchange and absorption of units, the
completed fiber is

\[
\boxed{w^2+b^2+a^4=0.}
\]

This is \(A_3\), with Jacobian algebra

\[
k[[a]]/(a^3)
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{\mu=3.}
\]

All three vanishing cycles are anti-invariant under the cover involution.
The intrinsic \(A_3\) monodromy eigenvalues are \(i,-1,-i\).  Entry 804's
generic rank-one Kato line supplies only one specialization direction.
Neither the already constructed single-divisor soft map nor the signed map
provides the remaining rank-two part.

Thus the first excess in this audit is

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}phi_{\rm soft,signed}=3
\quad\text{versus generic Kato rank }1.
}
\]

This is coefficient complexity on an existing labelled carrier intersection,
not evidence for a new carrier divisor.

When additionally \(P_1^2=P_2^2\), equivalently on the deeper
soft--triangle corner, the \(A_3\) model degenerates again.  This
codimension-three nonisolated corner is not assigned a finite Milnor rank
here; it requires a separate logarithmic resolution.

## Result for H2

The generic single-coordinate branches pass the H2 test: their classes are
generated by existing signed-energy and momentum-triangle maps.  The generic
double-boundary branches are likewise generated by existing soft and
restricted-Cayley--Menger maps.

The soft--signed corner does not yet pass.  It produces a rank-two
anti-invariant coefficient excess not generated by any already constructed
single-divisor comparison.  H2 survives only if the predeclared
support-sensitive derived calculus constructs this \(A_3\) extension on the
existing intersection without adding a carrier cell.
