# 3105 — Circle Heat Preparation Cannot Collocate the Logarithmic Endpoint Port

The positive theta heat flow and the logarithmic tail live on different
carriers. The circle Laplacian has discrete eigenvectors \(e_n\) with
eigenvalues \(n^2\); logarithmic translation on \(L^2(\mathbb R)\) has no
nonzero eigenvectors.

Therefore every bounded operator intertwining those generators is zero. The
circle seam delta cannot be transported into the logarithmic endpoint or
theta source by a bounded carrier-preserving map.

The missing bridge must retain both carriers as a correspondence, including
its adjoint boundary incidence. Heat-and-trace preparation is not a reversible
port identification.

## Scope

The bounded-intertwiner obstruction is exact. Rigged, unbounded, or
two-carrier correspondences remain possible.
