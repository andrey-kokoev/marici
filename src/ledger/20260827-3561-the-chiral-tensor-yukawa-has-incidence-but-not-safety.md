# The Chiral-Tensor Yukawa Has Incidence but Not Safety

The Higgs-like Yukawa \(T^{ab}\widetilde F_aH_b\) directly contains the
anomaly-essential chiral tensor and therefore repairs WP803's incidence
failure. At finite \(SU(5)\), however, its fixed-flow substitution gives
disjoint perturbative-safety conditions.

The upper branch requires

\[
x>\frac{77}{20}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
x<\frac{1601}{1610},
\]

while the lower branch requires

\[
x>\frac{101}{20}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
x<\frac{5107}{2254}.
\]

Neither branch can generate a physical interacting ultraviolet fixed point at
the admitted order. In the asymptotically free region, the Yukawa can rigidify
a relative fixed-flow ray, but it does not select the ray normalization or
transmutation scale. Its squared coordinate erases sign, and its single
Yukawa hyperedge has no rephasing-invariant phase cycle.
