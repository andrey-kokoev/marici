# The Critical Primitive Current Escapes the Mellin–de Rham Graph Dual

Ledger 3876 constructed the correct finite relative-flux observer, but its
critical primitive completion is not continuous in the polynomial `(Q,D)`
graph topology.

For translated unit-width bumps `chi_R`,

\[
\lVert\chi_R\rVert_{QD}=O(R),
\]

while the prime number theorem gives

\[
\sum_p(\log p)p^{-1/2}\chi_R(\log p)
\asymp e^{R/2}.
\]

The normalized pairing therefore diverges. The same odd-bump witness applies
to the reflected relative current.

This reconstructs the three completion grades: primitive mass grows
exponentially, square mass is asymptotically constant per logarithmic shell,
and depths at least three have finite total variation. The `(Q,D)` observer
handles finite primitive packets and the square/tail completion, but the full
primitive current must remain an independent exponential boundary coordinate
or live in a properly supported comoving correspondence.
