# 3162 — Reciprocal Euler Transport Has a Unit Current Anomaly Uniquely Centered at One Half

For \(E(r)=-\log(1-r)\), reciprocal continuation gives

\[
E(r^{-1})=E(r)+\log r-i\pi.
\]

Its oriented logarithmic current \(J=rD_rE\) satisfies

\[
-J(r^{-1})=J(r)+1.
\]

Consequently, \(C=J+1/2\) is the unique affine normalization satisfying
\(-C(r^{-1})=C(r)\).

## Scope

The unit defect is a primitive boundary current, while \(-i\pi\) is seam
monodromy. Compatibility with the labelled theta and Mellin half-density
constructions remains to be proved.
