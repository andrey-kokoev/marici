high-contrast corridor additionally requires \(\lambda/p<5/3\).

mass-squared spectrum \(\{2,6\}\), but has closed heavy-to-two-light phase
space. The same source grammar also admits \(p=0\) with a positive diagonal
Hessian and no portal contrast.

Thus stability conditionally rigidifies asymmetry support but selects neither
the useful coupling corridor nor a portal branch.
