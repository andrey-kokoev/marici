For the smooth even Gaussian half-source

\[
f_A(u)=e^{-Au^2},
\qquad 0\le u\le1,
\]

integration by parts gives, at every \(y=2\pi k\),

\[
M_A(y)=\frac{y}{2A}C_A(y).
\]

For small positive \(A\), \(C_A(2\pi k)<0\); after Gaussian concentration as
\(A\to\infty\), it is positive.  Thus every \(k\ge1\) has an exact collision
parameter \(A_k\).  The first is approximately

\[
A_1=2.32123509554598.
\]

This source is smooth, even, strictly decreasing on the positive chamber,
strictly log-concave, minimum-phase, and Gaussian.  Therefore the entire
unlabelled archimedean-shape lane is insufficient.  The remaining candidate
force is the polynomial-Gaussian integral winding tower and its modular
cross-label coherence.
