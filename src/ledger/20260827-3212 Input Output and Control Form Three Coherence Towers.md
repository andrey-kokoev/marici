# 3212 — Input, Output, and Control Form Three Coherence Towers

The programme is better typed as three towers over a shared labelled state:
input constructibility, output observability, and control coherence. A state
may be input-detectable, control-preserved, and output-null simultaneously.

The RH-bearing theorem must therefore be a natural cross-tower Ward cell:
output zero-state boundary conditions, transported through a control Green
identity, must annihilate every finite input-curvature packet. Input joint
faithfulness can then exclude the nonzero off-seam state.
