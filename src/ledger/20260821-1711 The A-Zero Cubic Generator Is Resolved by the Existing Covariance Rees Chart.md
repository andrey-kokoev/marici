\[

\]

+\frac{\varepsilon^2x^2+2\varepsilon cxy+c^2y^2}

\[
\boxed{

+\varepsilon^2\left(\frac{x^2}{2}-c^2ty^3\right)

\]

## Narrow result

\[
\boxed{
\text{the vanishing-}A\text{ boundary is resolved by the existing linear covariance normal }c\text{ and its symmetric square }c^2.
}
\]

No new coefficient chart or carrier stratum is required.  The first grade is
linear in the resolved normal; the first nonlinear cubic correction on the
boundary is the deck-even second grade `-c^2 t y^3`.

The boundary is a coefficient compactification of the Gaussian generating
object.  A finite-variance quantum state cannot have `A=0` because of the
uncertainty relation, so this is not asserted to be an interior physical state.
