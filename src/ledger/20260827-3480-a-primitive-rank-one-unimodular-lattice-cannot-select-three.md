# 3480 — A Primitive Rank-One Unimodular Lattice Cannot Select Three

## Claim

A positive rank-one integral Green--Schwarz/string-charge lattice has Gram
form \([N]\). Unimodularity forces \(N=1\), and its only primitive vectors are
\(\pm e\). Their pairings have magnitude one.

The Gram form \([3]\) pairs primitive generators to three but has determinant
three and is not self-dual. The admissible form \([1]\) reaches three only by
using the nonprimitive vector \(3e\). Thus either quantum consistency fails or
the desired integer is written into the source vector.

The automorphism \(e\mapsto-e\) preserves the lattice, and the integral form
does not fix the continuous Hodge/kinetic metric. Orientation, Stückelberg
mass, and portal normalization therefore remain open.

## Classification

The primitive rank-one lattice is neither a flux-three selector nor a
physical-normalization selector. The next finite domain is the smallest
higher-rank unimodular lattice, quotiented by its full automorphism group and
tested for a uniquely distinguished primitive pairing-three orbit.
