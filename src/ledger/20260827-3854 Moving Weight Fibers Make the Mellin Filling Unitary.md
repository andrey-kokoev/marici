# Moving-Weight Fibers Make the Mellin Filling Unitary

Multiplication by `e^(aq)` is unbounded on one fixed unweighted `L^2` space,
but that comparison is mistyped. Define

\[
\mathcal H_a=L^2(\mathbb R,e^{2aq}\,dq)
\]

and

\[
U_{a\to b}f=e^{(a-b)q}f.
\]

Then `U_(a->b)` is unitary from `H_a` to `H_b` and composes exactly. Scale
reflection is unitary from `H_a` to `H_-a`. The two half-plane sectors are
therefore reciprocal weighted fibers, and the seam is where those fibers
coincide.

Consequently the canonical Mellin-orbit filling survives Hilbert completion
when typed in the moving bundle. Fixed-fiber exponential unboundedness is not
an RH obstruction.

The first live analytic gate moves to endpoint evaluation and domain
intersection. Point evaluation is not continuous on bare `L^2`; it requires
a source-derived graph/Sobolev domain. The next theorem must test uniform
endpoint continuity and strictness of the plus/minus endpoint pullback under
Mellin transport and Euler cutoff.
