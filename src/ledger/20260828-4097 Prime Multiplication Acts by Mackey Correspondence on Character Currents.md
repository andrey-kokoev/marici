# Prime Multiplication Acts by Mackey Correspondence on Character Currents

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

For multiplication by a prime on \(G_Q=\mathbb Z/Q\mathbb Z\), let
\(d=(p,Q)\). If \(d=1\), characters are honestly permuted by
\(r\mapsto pr\). If \(d>1\), multiplication factors through \(G_{Q/d}\);
it is a correspondence between conductor objects, not an endomorphism of one
character packet.

The associated transfer and pullback satisfy

\[
q_{p,*}q_p^*=d\,\mathrm{id}.
\]

Thus the kernel-size norm is source-derived and mandatory. The packet splits
into a descended block, a complementary block killed by transfer, and the
retained fiber multiplicity. This gives Entry 4096's Wronskian balance an
authorized arithmetic transport law, but not yet a phase constraint. The
next gate is a sewing law coupling descended and complementary blocks.
