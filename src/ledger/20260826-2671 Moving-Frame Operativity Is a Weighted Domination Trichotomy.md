# 2671 — Moving-Frame Operativity Is a Weighted Domination Trichotomy

For analytic Gram weight (q_N), frame residual (r_N), and authorized seam
response (h_N), the sharp moving-state domination constants are

\[
r_N^2/q_N,
\qquad
h_N^2/q_N.
\]

Their behavior classifies the witness as a harmless quotient, a frame
non-descent obstruction, or a hybrid-port obligation. In the exact fixture
(q_N=N^{-2}, r_N=2, h_N=1), analytic constants diverge, while adjoining the
same-direction seam square uniformly controls both. An orthogonal or
unauthorized seam does not repair the obstruction.
