## Method

Work over (mathbf Z/2^k) without selecting a rational primitive splitting.

At each layer:

1. eliminate every row pivot with odd coefficient;
2. reduce all source rows by those unit pivots;
3. divide the remaining even block by (2);
4. repeat on the divided block.

If the layer ranks sum to the independently certified characteristic-zero rank (2194), the rank at layer (v) counts Smith invariants of exact two-adic valuation (v).

The depth-(12) computations reach cumulative rank (2194) at both fibers.

## Interpretation

Three statements are now separated.

First, the number of positive-valuation directions is stable:

[
45+10+2+2=59.
]

Second, a substantial prefix of the valuation distribution is stable:

[
m_1=45,qquad m_2=10,qquad m_3=2.
]

Third, the two longest bars retain higher-congruence kinematic information:

[
(7,7)longrightarrow(7,11).
]

Therefore there is no single fiber-independent finite abelian torsion group at the present level. The source defines a parameter-dependent arithmetic filtration. Calling the entire rank-(59) layer a normalization artifact or a constant torsion summand is falsified.
