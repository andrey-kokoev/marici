# 2692 — Vanishing Naturality Defects Do Not Imply a Completed Actuator

Uniform boundedness and adjacent defects (\eta_N\to0) do not imply actuator
convergence. Exact rational triangular waves have steps (1/m\to0), remain in
([0,1]), and retain subsequences at both zero and one. Summable transported
defects are a sufficient Cauchy certificate.
