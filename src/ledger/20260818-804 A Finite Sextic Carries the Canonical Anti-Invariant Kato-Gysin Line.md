# 804 — A Finite Sextic Carries the Canonical Anti-Invariant Kato-Gysin Line

## Purpose

Entry 802 derives eight finite projected sextics from intersections of two
source-labelled marked sections with the Cayley--Menger branch surface.  The
first geometric question is whether one such sextic carries a local
vanishing-cycle map generated by the same logarithmic incidence calculus,
rather than by matching expanded sextic polynomials.

Use the ordered representative

\[
(\mathfrak g_1,\mathfrak g_2).
\]

## Frozen local geometry

Take the source loop orientation (da\wedge db) and marked coordinates

\[
x=q_{\mathfrak g_1}=b-(E-X_1),
\qquad
y=q_{\mathfrak g_2}=a-(E-X_2).
\]

Their intersection is

\[
p=(a,b)=(E-X_2,E-X_1).
\]

The marked-coordinate Jacobian is

\[
\det\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(a,b)}=-1.
\]

Let

\[
w^2=K_{\rm CM}(a,b)
\]

be the source-normalized branch cover and define

\[
S=K_{\rm CM}(p).
\]

This (S) is Entry 802's irreducible sextic for the representative pair.

## Invariant normal form

Expanding in the marked coordinates, without expanding (S) in external
kinematics, gives

\[
\boxed{
w^2=S+\alpha x+\beta y+O((x,y)^2),
}
\]

where

\[
\alpha=\left.\partial_xK_{\rm CM}\right|_p,
\qquad
\beta=\left.\partial_yK_{\rm CM}\right|_p.
\]

Exact determinant differentiation shows that both are nonzero polynomials.
Thus on the generic smooth locus where ((\alpha,\beta)\ne(0,0)), the
collision has the standard marked branch-crossing model.  The locus where
both vanish is deliberately excluded and would require a higher
vanishing-cycle calculation.

## Kato kernel

At the marked intersection the cover restricts to

\[
w^2=S.
\]

The involution is

\[
w\longmapsto-w.
\]

Therefore the relevant logarithmic kernel is the anti-invariant trace kernel

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal K_S
=
\ker\left(
\operatorname{Tr}:\pi_*\mathcal O_{w^2=S}	o\mathcal O_S
\right)_{\rm mer},
}
\]

represented meromorphically by (1/w).  Around (S=0) it has Kummer
monodromy (-1).  This is coefficient support on the projected sextic, not a
new carrier component.

## Ordered Gysin map

The logarithmic source generator is

\[
\Omega_{12}
=
\frac{da\wedge db}{q_{\mathfrak g_1}q_{\mathfrak g_2}w}.
\]

Since

\[
da\wedge db=-dx\wedge dy,
\]

the ordered iterated residue is

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Res}_{y=0}\operatorname{Res}_{x=0}\Omega_{12}
=-\frac1w.
}
\]

Hence the local Gysin map is primitive, rank one, anti-invariant under the
deck involution, and fixed by the source orientation.  No normalization is
fitted from the expanded sextic.

## Incidence compatibility

The comparison with the Cayley--Menger carrier is tautologically typed:

\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
V(x,y) & \longrightarrow & V(x,y,w^2-K_{\rm CM})\\
\downarrow && \downarrow\\
\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Q[\text{kinematics}]
\xrightarrow{S=K_{\rm CM}(p)} V(w^2-S).
\end{array}
\]

Thus the Kummer line is obtained by restricting the frozen branch equation
to the frozen marked intersection.  Expanded-polynomial equality or orbit
fitting plays no role.

## Narrow result

\[
\boxed{
\text{generic finite marked sextic}
\longrightarrow
\text{canonical anti-invariant Kato/Gysin line}
}
\]

for the representative pair ((\mathfrak g_1,\mathfrak g_2)), with ordered
orientation sign (-1).

This supports the local geometric half of H2.  Global cyclic naturality is
not yet claimed: it requires Nima's independently derived occurrence action,
transition units, stabilizers, and transported orientations.
