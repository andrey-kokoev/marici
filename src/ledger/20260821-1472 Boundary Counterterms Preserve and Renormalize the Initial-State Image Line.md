## Frozen image line

For (t,t'>t_0), write

\[
G_{k,\mathrm{img}}(t,t')
=c_k b_k(t)b_k(t'),
\qquad
c_k=\frac{i}{2k}e^{\widetilde\alpha_k},
\qquad
b_k(t)=e^{-ik(t-t_0)}.
\]

\[

\qquad
i^*\partial_t b_k=-ik.
\]

\[
\langle i^*G_{\mathrm{img}},
i^*\partial_nG_{\mathrm{img}}\rangle

\mathbb C\langle c_k b_k\rangle_\Sigma.
\]

\[
