# 3178 — RH Is One Projective Antipode Exclusion, Not Full de Branges Separation

The nonnull half-line pair defines

\[
\mathcal R(z)=[H(z):H(-z)]\in\mathbb{CP}^1,
\]

and

\[
X(z)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal R(z)=[1:-1].
\]

RH therefore asks that the inverse image of one projective point lie on the
seam. De Branges magnitude separation excludes the entire unit circle in the
ratio chart and is strictly stronger.

## Scope

The minimal source theorem may permit off-seam unit-circle crossings away
from the antipode. Coercivity, cone, and full magnitude-ordering programmes
must not be treated as necessary formulations of RH.
