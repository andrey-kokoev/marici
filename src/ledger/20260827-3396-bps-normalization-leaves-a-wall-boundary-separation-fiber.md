# 3396 — BPS Normalization Leaves a Wall–Boundary Separation Fiber

## Claim

Even granting a BPS source that fixes the relevant zero-mode profile exponent
to one, the normalized center-to-boundary density contrast is

\[
\Delta(a)=\frac12\tanh^2 a,
\qquad a=kd>0.
\]

The same BPS charge and local profile at

\[
a_1=\operatorname{arctanh}\frac12,
\qquad
a_2=\operatorname{arctanh}\frac34
\]

give contrasts \(1/8\) and \(9/32\). Thus BPS saturation can normalize the
local wall shape without selecting its separation from the physical readout
boundary.

## Classification

BPS structure is a local profile normalizer and conditional localization
rigidifier, not a global portal-magnitude selector. A source-derived
stabilization equation must fix one labelled dimensionless separation \(kd\)
before RG, threshold, `physical16`, and detector gates can be tested.
