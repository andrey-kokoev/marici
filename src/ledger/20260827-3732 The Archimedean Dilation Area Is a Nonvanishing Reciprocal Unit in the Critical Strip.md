# 3732 — The Archimedean Dilation Area Is a Nonvanishing Reciprocal Unit in the Critical Strip

For the Fourier-fixed Gaussian, the infinitesimal dilation two-cycle has area

\[
A_\infty(s)
=
s\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)
=
2\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(1+s/2).
\]

It is holomorphic and nonzero throughout \(0<\operatorname{Re}s<1\). Its
reciprocal mate \(A_\infty(1-s)\) is also nonzero there, and their ratio has
unit modulus on \(\operatorname{Re}s=1/2\).

Together with the exact unit finite-place minors, this proves that every local
boundary determinant is invertible in the critical strip. Local Tate geometry
explains why the critical seam is unitary, but no nontrivial Riemann zero can
be a loss of rank of one local scalarizer.

The RH-bearing object must therefore be the global aggregation map from local
unit lines to the distinguished completed theta section. Cross-place
correlation, not local scalarization, is the remaining source of possible
zeros.
