The source-native Hardy symbol is

\[
\phi_z(\xi)=A(z-i\xi).
\]

Its Hankel kernel is (f(x+y)e^{z(x+y)}).  The imaginary part of (z) is
removed by diagonal unitary conjugation, so every unitary invariant of the
positive Hankel square depends only on \(\Re z\).

The Green forcing instead contains the essential factor

\[
\cos(\Im(z)d).
\]

Therefore it cannot equal the canonical positive Toeplitz/Hankel defect.  The
simple compression-positivity route is closed.  Any surviving operator
construction must retain a reciprocal cross-sheet matrix coefficient and the
independent DC boundary channel.
