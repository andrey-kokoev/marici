# 3907 — Horizontal Zero Displacement Is Poisson Time

The Fourier transform of an off-seam zero's boundary profile is

\[
2e^{-a|x|}e^{-ibx}.
\]

Thus the ordinate `b` is frequency and the horizontal displacement `a` is
exactly Poisson-semigroup time, or exponential resolution decay. A critical-
line zero is the undiffused time-zero atom.

The positive supply measure also defines a canonical self-adjoint
multiplication operator. RH is equivalent not to its self-adjointness—which
is automatic—but to compact resolvent and pure-point spectral type. One
off-line zero creates a positive diffuse density on all of the seam and hence
continuous spectrum.

## Scope

This proves the Poisson-time and compact-resolvent equivalences for the
boundary-derived supply representation. It does not derive that operator
independently from theta dynamics, prove source compactness, or prove RH.
