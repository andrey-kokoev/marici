# The Three-State Port Is One Endpoint Stratum Plus a Two-Sheet Through Cover

The six unsigned legal generator permutations form three orbits under exact
endpoint reversal. Two orbits repeat an endpoint generator; one repeats the
unique through-going generator. The endpoint-versus-through classification is
therefore already a coarse type map, merging the two endpoint orbits.

The relative endpoint-polarity character is defined only on the through-going
stratum and takes both signs. Hence the response port is constructed as one
collapsed endpoint state together with a two-sheet orientation cover over the
through-going state. Its cardinality three is stratified, not a homogeneous
three-fold symmetry.

This explains why polarity affects only the exceptional generator while also
preserving the earlier boundary: the construction is source-lawful, whereas
its minimal sufficiency is certified by the distinct response profiles.
