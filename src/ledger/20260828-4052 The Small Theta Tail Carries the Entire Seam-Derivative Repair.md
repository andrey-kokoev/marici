# The Small Theta Tail Carries the Entire Seam-Derivative Repair

The primitive profile satisfies

\[
\frac{\phi_1'(0)}{\phi_1(0)}
=
\frac52+\frac{4\pi}{2\pi-3}-2\pi
>
0.
\]

But the completed theta source is even:

\[
\Phi'(0)=0.
\]

Hence for \(\tau=\sum_{n\ge2}\phi_n\),

\[
\tau'(0)=-\phi_1'(0).
\]

The tail is below \(0.6001\%\) of the primitive in pointwise value while
carrying the entire seam-derivative repair. It therefore changes the leading
high-frequency boundary asymptotic by order one.

Primitive perturbation remains useful on compact frequency regions, but its
global extension must use a seam-matched reference packet and a norm
controlling boundary jets as well as bulk value.
