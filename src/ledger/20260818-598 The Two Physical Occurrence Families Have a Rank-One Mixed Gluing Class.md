## Scattering degeneration

Put

\[
E=E_T=x+y+z,\qquad s=x+y,\qquad p=xy.
\]

Direct substitution into the frozen Cayley--Menger polynomial gives

\[
\boxed{
K_E(y,x)
=
E^3\left[
2E^3-6sE^2+(5s^2+4p)E-8ps
\right].
}
\]

On the generic scattering boundary \(ps\ne0\), the bracket restricts to
\(-8ps\). Therefore the mixed occurrence intersection collides with the
branch divisor at exactly third ordinary normal order:

\[
\operatorname{ord}_{E=0}K_E(y,x)=3.
\]

This is not the algebraic quartic deformation

\[
\mathcal Q=-16p^2-8pE^2+8sE^3-5E^4,
\]

whose first nonconstant term is second order. The two filtered phenomena
must remain distinct.

## Classification

source-existing denominator sections on the frozen residue surface. It is

\[

## Scope

compute its nearby-cycle monodromy, prove that the physical numerator has a
nonzero component along it, or construct the localization-triangle map.
