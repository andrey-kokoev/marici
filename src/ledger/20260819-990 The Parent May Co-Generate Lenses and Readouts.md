# The Parent May Co-Generate Lenses and Readouts

## Refinement of Entry 985

Entry 985 formulates the strong parent-object hypothesis as canonical
realizations

\[
\mathcal R_s(\mathscr U)\simeq\mathcal K_s
\]

of sector-specific coefficient lenses.  The accumulated Marici evidence
suggests that a coefficient object alone does not define a physical sector.
It must be paired with a correctly typed readout: a relative cycle,
observable functor, boundary condition, or invariant map that can detect it.

This motivates a stronger but still testable refinement.

## H2LR: lens--readout co-generation

The parent object \(\mathscr U\) canonically generates compatible triples

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R_s(\mathscr U)
=
(\mathcal K_s,\mathcal O_s,\langle-,-\rangle_s),
}
\]

where

- \(\mathcal K_s\) is the sector coefficient or transport object;
- \(\mathcal O_s\) is the admissible readout object;
- \(\langle-,-\rangle_s\) is the typed pairing producing sector
  observables.

The physical sector is therefore not the coefficient lens in isolation but
the compatible lens--readout package:

\[
\boxed{
\text{sector}_s
=
(\text{transported distinctions},\text{admissible observations},
\text{their pairing}).
}
\]

## Evidence motivating H2LR

The distinction has repeatedly been operational in the ledger.

1. In cosmology, algebraic and exceptional classes can exist while the
   literal Bunch--Davies/Cayley--Menger chain pairs with them trivially.  A
   nonzero coefficient class is not automatically a physical contribution.
2. In the string sector, Koba--Nielsen loading becomes an amplitude only
   after Pochhammer regularization and pairing with a loaded chamber cycle.
3. In flavor, the sparse Yukawa link graph carries positive edge magnitudes
   and one rephasing-invariant loop phase, while masses and CKM data arise
   only through the flavor-invariant physical readout.

These examples support the typing principle

\[
\text{coefficient existence}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{physical activation}.
\]

They do not yet prove that the parent generates the readout rather than the
readout being supplied as independent boundary data.

## Finite scientific tests

A proposed realization of H2LR must satisfy all of the following.

1. **Readout provenance.**  \(\mathcal O_s\) must be derived from the same
   source structure as \(\mathcal K_s\), not chosen after inspecting the
   desired answer.
2. **Typed pairing.**  Degree, variance, support, deck character, and
   orientation must make \(\langle-,-\rangle_s\) a genuine map.
3. **Naturality.**  The pairing must commute with the sector's localization,
   monodromy, occurrence, and symmetry maps.
4. **Null-readout prediction.**  When the generated readout misses a class,
   the theory must predict zero rather than permit an alternative fitted
   cycle.
5. **Cross-sector reconstruction.**  A common parent candidate must derive
   at least one nontrivial lens--readout pair not used to define it.

H2LR is falsified for a proposed parent if its lens is canonical but its
readout requires an arbitrary regulator hierarchy, splitting, contour, or
projection.  This does not falsify the weaker shared-calculus hypothesis
H2W.

## H2O: observer-selection extension

A further, explicitly philosophical conjecture is that observers are
physical subsystems of \(\mathscr U\) and can access only lens--readout pairs
compatible with their own realization:

\[
\boxed{
\mathscr U
\longmapsto
(\text{world realization},\text{observer realization},
\text{admissible coupling}).
}
\]

On this view, multiplicity of experience can arise from multiple internally
compatible modes of distinguishing and observing the same parent structure.
Human perception may select and compress such a mode, but it need not create
the underlying admissibility constraints.

H2O is not presently a physics result.  It must not be used to explain a
failed physical construction or to replace a missing source-derived
readout.  Causality, gauge invariance, positivity, detector coupling, and
decoherence can constrain observation before any specifically human
representation enters.

## Epistemic posture

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{hypothesis}&\text{current status}\\
\hline
\mathrm{H2W}&\text{evidence-supported shared calculus}\\
\mathrm{H2S}&\text{unproved common-parent conjecture}\\
\mathrm{H2LR}&\text{testable co-generation refinement of H2S}\\
\mathrm{H2O}&\text{philosophical extension, not established physics}
\end{array}
}
\]

This hierarchy preserves the useful insight—that physical manifestation
requires a compatible lens and readout—without turning observer dependence
into an unfalsifiable repair mechanism.
