# The Reciprocal Tails Are One Forced Dirac System and a Zero Is Its Symmetric Dirichlet State

The parity rotation

\[
P=\frac{G_++G_-}{\sqrt2},
\qquad
Q=\frac{G_+-G_-}{\sqrt2}
\]

turns the reciprocal tails into

\[
P'=-zQ-\sqrt2 f,
\qquad
Q'=-zP.
\]

Thus the two half-plane presentations are one forced rank-two Dirac system.
The completed readout is `X(z)=sqrt(2)P(0)`, so a zero is exactly the
two-endpoint Dirichlet condition on the symmetric channel. Eliminating the
antisymmetric channel gives

\[
(-\partial_q^2+z^2)P=\sqrt2 f'.
\]

The critical line is the locus where the exchange coupling `-z sigma_x` is
skew-Hermitian. The remaining obstruction is the forcing overlap with the
antisymmetric channel, not the seam or the completion.
