# 2668 — Strong Real-Structure Transport Does Not Control Moving Tail Frames

On (\ell^2), let (J_N) differ from standard conjugation only by a sign on
the (N)-th coordinate. Then (J_N\to J) strongly because

\[
\|(J_N-J)x\|=2|x_N|\to0
\]

for each fixed state, while (\|J_N-J\|=2) for every cutoff. The fixed real
subspaces likewise converge strongly but retain gap one. The moving witness
(e_N) is invisible to every fixed finite-prefix probe and to the preserved
vacuum (e_1).

Completion therefore requires uniform intertwiner control, gap convergence, a
source topology killing the moving witness, or an authorized observation row
detecting it uniformly. Pointwise finite certification is insufficient.
