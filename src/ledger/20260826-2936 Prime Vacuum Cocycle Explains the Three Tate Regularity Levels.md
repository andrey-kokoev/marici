The bilateral local current has a canonical global probe: its excitation of
the local valuation vacuum.  For

\[
b_{p,k}(z)=\mathcal J_{p,k}(z)e_{p,0},
\]

one has

\[
\lVert b_{p,k}(z)\rVert^2
=
\frac{2p^{-k}}{k^2}
\cosh(2k\Re(z)\log p).
\]

On the critical seam this yields the exact three-level split: the primitive
cocycle is distributional, the square cocycle is Hilbert but has
non-absolutely-summable scalar amplitudes, and the connected tail is
absolutely summable.  The result also corrects an earlier ambiguity: a block
direct sum, an additive restricted-product generator, and a vacuum cocycle
have different norms and cannot be denoted by the same untyped prime sum.

The next construction is a restricted-product rigging retaining the primitive
and square cocycles as explicit boundary coordinates and coupling them to the
archimedean incidence before scalar augmentation.
