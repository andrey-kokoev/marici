## Narrow result

The third-order obstruction is real before derived reduction and exact after it:

\[
\Theta_{jk}\neq0
\quad\text{in the finite presentation,}\qquad
[\Theta_{jk}]=0
\quad\text{modulo the moving-relation image.}
\]

Therefore the source-natural adapter is homotopy-flat, not strictly flat, on the tested finite atlas. No new relation family, carrier stratum, or cosmological coefficient primitive is required at this order.
