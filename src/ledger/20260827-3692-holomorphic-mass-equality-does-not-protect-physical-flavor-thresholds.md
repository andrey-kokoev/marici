needed after WP844. Three vectorlike charge pairs admit the common
gauge-invariant superpotential mass

\[
W=m\sum_i\Phi_i\widetilde\Phi_i.
\]

\[

\[
-\kappa\left(Q^2-\frac{14}{3}I\right),
\]

nonrenormalization transfers the missing source authority to the Kähler
metric. A successful supersymmetric principle needs an all-order physical-
pole or Kähler identity that derives this cancellation while retaining the
asymmetric portal.
