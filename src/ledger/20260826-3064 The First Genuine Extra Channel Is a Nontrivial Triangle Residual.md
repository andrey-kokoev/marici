# 3064 — The First Genuine Extra Channel Is a Nontrivial Triangle Residual

Any labelled operator family of the form

\[
X_a=R_aX_0R_a^{-1}
\]

has canonical comparisons (T_{ab}=R_a^{-1}R_b), and these obey

\[
T_{ab}T_{bc}T_{ca}=I.
\]

Thus every channel factoring through a one-label normalization potential is
flat, even when its matrices look noncommutative in one fixed presentation.

The first invariant extra channel must carry ordered-pair incidence. For
modified comparisons \(\widetilde T_{ab}=T_{ab}A_{ab}\), its first witness is
the triangle residual

\[
\Omega_{abc}
=\widetilde T_{ab}\widetilde T_{bc}\widetilde T_{ca}.
\]

An additional comparison wall matters only if this residual is nonidentity
and source-derived. Adding another endpoint frame merely enlarges the same
flat presentation.

## Scope

This is a criterion, not yet the missing RH mechanism. No source-derived
nonfactorizable pair-incidence law has been constructed. A later argument
must also connect any nontrivial residual to the completed scalar section.
