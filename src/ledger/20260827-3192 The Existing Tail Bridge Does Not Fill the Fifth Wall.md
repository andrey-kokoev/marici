# 3192 — The Existing Tail Bridge Does Not Fill the Fifth Wall

The completed theta zero lifts canonically to an antidiagonal two-tail state,
but that state solves the singular pencil

\[
(D_0+zP)\Psi=0,
\qquad \operatorname{rank}P=1,
\]

not an eigenvalue equation for a fixed skew-adjoint realization. The null
spectral channel is the source-essential constant channel. It cannot be
deleted, and symmetrizing its triangular coupling introduces new reverse
incidence.

Thus the fifth wall requires a genuinely new constructor or a theorem for the
singular relation; it is not already supplied by the tail augmentation.
