# 1394 — Finite Euler Cutoffs Cannot Be Self-Adjoint Xi Determinants

Epistemic-graph event: 1437.

For a nonempty finite prime set \(P\),

\[
D_P(s)=\prod_{p\in P}(1-p^{-s})
\]

has zeros

\[
s=\frac{2\pi ik}{\log p}.
\]

Under \(s=1/2+iz\), these lie on

\[
\operatorname{Im}z=\frac12,
\]

not on the real self-adjoint spectral axis. Thus \(D_P\) cannot be a
self-adjoint characteristic determinant. Its inverse finite Euler product is
meromorphic instead of entire.

Archimedean completion factors do not cancel the nonzero local arithmetic
lattices. Hence no finite prime cutoff is a positive spectral approximation
to the desired \(\Xi\) operator.

Any successful boundary must arise from a global renormalized infinite
completion. Cutoff-dependent counterfactors chosen to retain the target zeros
would be circular.

Scope: every nonempty finite Euler cutoff is ruled out; a source-derived
global infinite-prime completion remains possible.
