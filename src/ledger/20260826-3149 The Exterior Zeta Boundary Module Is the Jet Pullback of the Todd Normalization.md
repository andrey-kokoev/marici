# 3149 — The Exterior Zeta Boundary Module Is the Jet Pullback of the Todd Normalization

Let

\[
\tau(t)=\frac{t}{e^t-1},
\qquad
L(t)=\frac{d}{dt}\log\tau(t).
\]

Then

\[
L(t)=\frac1t-\frac1{1-e^{-t}},
\qquad
L^{(n)}(0)=\zeta(-n).
\]

Hence the exterior Hankel packet \(\zeta(-(i+j))\) is the jet prolongation
of one multiplicative Euler–Maclaurin/Todd normalization germ.
