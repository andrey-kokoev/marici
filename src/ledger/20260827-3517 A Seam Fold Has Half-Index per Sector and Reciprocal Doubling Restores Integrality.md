# A Seam Fold Has Half-Index per Sector and Reciprocal Doubling Restores Integrality

A simple interior zero contributes unit argument index. A simple zero on the
seam is seen through only a semicircular indentation by either open sector and
therefore contributes relative index one half:

\[
\frac12+\frac12=1
\]

after reciprocal sewing.

Thus global integrality decomposes into two relative half-charges when the
half-planes gain their distinction. An off-line simple branch already has full
positive index, and a generic symmetry quartet contributes four; integrality
alone does not forbid it.

The missing theorem is a source-derived relative index budget whose boundary
folds and infinity current exhaust the available charge. Ordinary argument
counting from the scalar section would be circular.
