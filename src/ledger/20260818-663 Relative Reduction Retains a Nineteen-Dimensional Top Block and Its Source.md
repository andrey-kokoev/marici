# 663 — Relative Reduction Retains a Nineteen-Dimensional Top Block and Its Source

## Hard-to-vary claim

Quotienting the literal four-mark complex by proper-face columns before
taking its finite twisted-de-Rham quotient retains a nonzero physical
source class.  This differs sharply from Entry 662's quotient-after-homology
calculation, where proper-face classes span the entire absolute rank-twenty
space.

## Chain-level relative construction

In each occurrence presentation, remove every labelled column having at
least one marked pole level zero.  Project the exact-differential and
localization rows to the remaining columns, retaining pole levels one and
two for all four marked denominators.  Only after this chain-level quotient
is formed are relation pivots computed.

The physical low space then consists of numerator monomials with all four
marked pole levels equal to one.  At cutoff degree five, ambient homotopy
degree ten, and Kummer weight five, both occurrence families give

\[
\boxed{
\dim M_{123,23}^{\rm rel,top}
=
\dim M_{123,31}^{\rm rel,top}
=19.
}
\]

The constant physical source survives in both quotients.  In the normalized
pivot presentation it has support on one free coordinate.  That support
count is basis-dependent; nonvanishing is the invariant statement.

## Replication

The same rank nineteen and nonzero source occur for

- the \(q_{g_{23}}\) and \(q_{g_{31}}\) partners at weight five;
- the \(q_{g_{23}}\) partner at weight seven;
- the \(q_{g_{23}}\) partner after increasing ambient homotopy degree from
  ten to eleven.

All computations use \(\mathbb F_{32003}\) at \((x,y,z)=(2,3,4)\).

## Meaning of the 20 versus 19 comparison

The absolute rank-twenty quotient and the relative rank-nineteen quotient
are not related by naively quotienting the former by the image of its face
classes: that image already has rank twenty.  The discrepancy is the
expected failure of taking homology to commute with this quotient and is
precisely where a connecting/Bockstein class can live.

Thus the calculation provides a concrete finite model of the methodological
rule reached independently in Entries 513 and 662:

\[
\boxed{
\text{retain support and boundary data at chain level before reduction.}
}
\]

It does not yet identify a canonical one-dimensional physical coefficient
line inside the nineteen-dimensional block, construct the unsplit pair of
occurrences, or determine the kinematic connection.

## Updated frontier

nineteen-dimensional quotient and differentiate the full relation matrix
with respect to two independent kinematic variables.  The next invariant is
the dimension of the smallest horizontal subspace generated by the source,
computed before identifying the two reflected occurrence families.
