# 2159 — Component Factorization Canonically Normalizes the Moving Contact Kernel

## Source ratio

Entry 2158 retained the connected spectator periods \(P_{jk}\) and contact
products \(C_jC_k\). The exact two-site component formula, transported
cyclically, fixes their ratio:

\[
\frac{C_jC_k}{P_{jk}}
=
\frac{q_{jk}}{y_{jk}},
\]

where \(q_{jk}\) is the connected component-energy denominator and \(y_{jk}\)
is the retained edge energy. This ratio is an identity between already
constructed scalar summands. It is not promoted to an inter-sector
coefficient morphism.

## Canonical rational kernel

Substitution into Entry 2158's pointwise kernel gives the source-normalized
rational line

\[
\boxed{
v_{\rm rat}
=
\left(
\frac{2q_{23}}{y_{23}},
\frac{2q_{31}}{y_{31}},

}
\]

The exact checker verifies the denominator-cleared kernel identity for all
three cyclic components at three signed generic packets.

Thus Entry 2158's negative result is narrowed precisely:

- there is no kinematics-independent constant circuit;
- there is a canonical source-normalized rational moving circuit.

## Why this is not yet Strominger's exceptional class

On the open locus where all \(q_{jk}y_{jk}\ne0\), the line is generated by
ordinary component-factorization identities. It is therefore generic
presentation redundancy, not yet supported information loss.

The potentially physical test lies where a primary route or its inverse
ceases to exist:

\[
q_{jk}=0
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
y_{jk}=0.
\]

Ordinary substitution into \(v_{\rm rat}\) is forbidden there. The connected
period has a compensating pole at \(q_{jk}=0\), while the rational generator
has a zero; at \(y_{jk}=0\) the generator has a pole. The correct object is
the Rees/nearby-cycle specialization of the complete matrix and kernel line.

\[

This is the direct analogue of simultaneous primary/fallback route failure.
