# Poisson Sewing of Prime Exclusion Generates the Full Ramanujan Packet

Modulo six, the prime-2 exclusion has Fourier support only at \(0,3\), and
the prime-3 exclusion only at \(0,2,4\). Their product is the unit-residue
indicator, whose transform is

\[
(c_6(k))_{k=0}^5=(2,1,-1,-2,-1,1).
\]

The mixed modes \(1,5\) occur in neither individual prime packet. In general
the Fourier transform of the squarefree unit indicator is the full Ramanujan
packet \(c_Q(k)\).

Thus additive Poisson sewing cannot commute primewise with multiplicative
connectedization. A source proof of primitive-current temperedness must retain
the profinite residue/character carrier and its mixed-prime modes before
scalarization. The next exact target is a cutoff-uniform tempered bound for
these completed Ramanujan packets.
