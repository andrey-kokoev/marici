missing magnitude selector. An exact rational audit of all Yukawa nullcline
subsets, with asymptotically free (SU(2)_B) and color independently
interacting or Gaussian, finds

\[
256\text{ solved branches},\qquad0\text{ physical branches}.
\]

\[

\qquad
\alpha_B=-\frac{116978905}{2306226316}.
\]

Thus the source-required mediator completion supplies no positive parent fixed
point, magnitude, or RG basin.
