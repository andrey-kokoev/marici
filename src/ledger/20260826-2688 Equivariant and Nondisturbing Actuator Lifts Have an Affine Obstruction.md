# 2688 — Equivariant and Nondisturbing Actuator Lifts Have an Affine Obstruction

If symmetry-fixed lifts are (B_G+V^G), simultaneous nondisturbance exists
exactly when

\[
-LB_GH\in\operatorname{im}(N\mapsto LNH|_{V^G}).
\]

Compatible lifts form a torsor over the kernel of this map. The affine
reflection (t\mapsto1-t) uniquely fixes (t=1/2), while nondisturbance
uniquely requires (t=0); their intersection is empty.
