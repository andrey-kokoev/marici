## Equivariant lens

The supported corner lens is generated by

\[
A=(y_1+y_2)^2,
\qquad
B=(y_1-y_2)^2,
\]

with edge-sign reversal exchanging (A) and (B). Its scalar infinity jet
depends only on the symmetric combinations

\[
\boxed{
s=A+B=2(y_1^2+y_2^2),
\qquad
p=AB=(y_1^2-y_2^2)^2.
}
\]

Accordingly the scalar readout factors through

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb Q[A,B]
\longrightarrow
\mathbb Q[A,B]^{\mathbb Z_2}
=\mathbb Q[s,p].
}
\]

The even-jet recurrence is precisely

\[
E_m=sE_{m-1}-pE_{m-2}.
\]

## Branch locus

The discriminant of the unordered channel pair is

\[
\boxed{
s^2-4p=(A-B)^2=16y_1^2y_2^2.
}
\]

Thus the quotient is branched where either edge energy vanishes, not on the
generic physical equal-edge locus.

## Physical diagonal

For (y_2=\pm y_1=\pm y), both conjugate signed diagonals have the same
invariant image

\[
\boxed{(s,p)=(4y^2,0).}
\]

Away from (y=0), this point is not on the quotient branch locus:

\[
s^2-4p=16y^4\ne0.
\]

Therefore the scalar readout identifies the two channel selections because it
forgets their ordering, not because the two channels collide.
