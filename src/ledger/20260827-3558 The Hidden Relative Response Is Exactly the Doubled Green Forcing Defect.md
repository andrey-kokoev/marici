# 3558 — The Hidden Relative Response Is Exactly the Doubled Green Forcing Defect

For reciprocal tails

\[
u'=-zu-f,
\qquad
v'=+zv-f,
\]

the doubled Green identity is

\[
2z(u^2+v^2)=-(u^2-v^2)'-2f(u-v).
\]

The two local adjoint responses are \(r_+=-fu\) and \(r_-=-fv\). Hence

\[
r_\Delta=r_+-r_-=-f(u-v),
\]

and the Green forcing defect is exactly \(2r_\Delta\). Meanwhile the common
response \(r_\Sigma=-f(u+v)\) vanishes on the completed scalar-zero domain.

Thus the two-port response split is source-native: the scalar section and the
Green obstruction are its common and relative coordinates. The next residual
is the common-path term left by primitive boundary transfer.
