# 3203 — The One-Way Tail Cannot Be a Positive Skew-Adjoint Quotient

Adding the adjoint mate produces a positive skew-adjoint block but changes the
constant-channel equation. No finite positive skew-adjoint extension can
recover the original nonzero nilpotent tail generator as an operator quotient:
an invariant quotient of a finite-dimensional skew-adjoint operator is again
diagonalizable and skew-adjoint, whereas the triangular tail incidence is
nonzero nilpotent.

The mate cell must therefore live in a relative, unbounded, indefinite, or
derived construction whose recovery notion is not an ordinary operator
quotient.
