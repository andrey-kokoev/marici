# 2581 — No Common Mellin Hilbert Level Exists in the Critical Strip

For the Hilbert scale generated by \(Qe_n=(\log n)e_n\),

\[
\|c\|_m^2=\sum_nn^{2m}|c_n|^2.
\]

The raw Euler detector is continuous exactly when \(m>1/2\). The normalized
Fock state

\[
\Omega_s=\zeta(2\sigma)^{-1/2}\sum_nn^{-s}e_n
\]

belongs exactly when \(m<\sigma-1/2\). A common Hilbert level therefore
exists precisely for \(\sigma>1\), never throughout
\(1/2<\sigma\le1\).

## Scope

Critical-strip Poisson continuation must be a relative boundary-bearing
pairing, not ordinary Riesz duality on one Mellin-weighted Hilbert space. This
does not construct or orient that relative pairing.
