# 3307 — Opposite Mellin Transport Forbids a Fixed Acute Relational Cone

## Result

For a direct--dual amplitude pair, the common-phase-invariant acute comparison
is

\[
R(a,b)=\operatorname{Re}(a\overline b).
\]

Opposite Mellin transport changes it to

\[
R_t(a,b)=\operatorname{Re}(e^{2itq}a\overline b).
\]

At `t=pi/(2q)`, a positive comparison becomes negative. Thus quotienting a
common phase does not yield a fixed acute relational cone compatible with the
full direct--dual spectral action.

The next viable ordered object is a cone field transported by the control
connection. To avoid tautology, its modular closing law must be independently
source-derived and must reject the positive two-cell off-seam hostile.

## Scope

This does not exclude transported cone fields, prove their modular closure, or
prove RH. It excludes a fixed acute cone based on the ordinary direct--dual
real pairing.
