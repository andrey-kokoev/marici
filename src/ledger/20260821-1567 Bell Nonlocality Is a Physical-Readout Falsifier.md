# 1567 — Bell Nonlocality Is a Physical-Readout Falsifier

## Status

Research direction. This entry proposes a bounded falsification program; it
does not claim that the current Carrier derives quantum correlations or the
Tsirelson bound.

## Placement

Bell's inequality belongs first at the physical-readout layer of the current
architecture:

\[
\text{shared Carrier/calculus}
\longrightarrow
\text{sector coefficient complex}
\longrightarrow
\text{relative totalization}
\longrightarrow
\text{physical readout}.
\]

It tests whether a joint record can factor through local pre-existing values,

\[
P(a,b\mid x,y)
\stackrel{?}{=}
\int d\lambda\,\rho(\lambda)
P(a\mid x,\lambda)P(b\mid y,\lambda),
\]

without confusing no-signalling with local-hidden-variable factorization.

## Sharp program

A minimal bipartite packet must establish, in order:

1. source-defined preparation and measurement settings;
2. canonical normalized joint readout;
3. functorial no-signalling marginals;
4. typed local composition;
5. survival of a CHSH value \(|S|>2\) in the complete relative totalization;
6. the stronger quantum constraint \(|S|\leq2\sqrt2\) from declared
   positivity, norm, support, and composition data.

Bell violation alone is weak: an arbitrary contextual probability table can
accommodate it. The discriminating result is the simultaneous derivation of
no-signalling and the Tsirelson bound from one source-defined calculus.

## Falsification outcomes

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{outcome}&\text{interpretation}\\
\hline
|S|\leq2&\text{the chosen readout has collapsed to a classical model}\\
2<|S|\leq2\sqrt2&\text{candidate quantum possibility structure}\\
|S|>2\sqrt2&\text{present rules admit superquantum readouts and are incomplete}
\end{array}
\]

Scattering is the preferred first host because it already has separated
external labels, factorization/locality structure, and physical pairings.
This remains a cross-sector audit rather than a sixth sector until a second
sector independently realizes the same typed packet.
