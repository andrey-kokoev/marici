# 2723 — Fredholm Determinant Detects Kernels, Not Uniform Coercivity or Sheet

A trace-class Fredholm determinant faithfully detects noninvertibility, but
its nonvanishing or magnitude does not control uniform inverse norms across
completion. Identical scalar determinants can also carry kernels in opposite
sheet characters. Determinant authority, scalar identification, kernel
typing, and uniform coercivity are four distinct theorems.
