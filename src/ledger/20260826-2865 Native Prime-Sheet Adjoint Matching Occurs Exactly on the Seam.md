# 2865 — Native Prime-Sheet Adjoint Matching Occurs Exactly on the Seam

In centered coordinate \(z=s-\tfrac12\), the direct and reciprocal prime
terms are

\[
p^{-1/2-z}T_p,
\qquad
p^{-1/2+z}T_p^*.
\]

They form a Hilbert-adjoint pair exactly when \(z=-\overline z\). Away from
the critical seam their modulus mismatch is \(p^{2\operatorname{Re}z}\), so
no unitary label gauge can repair it. Native two-sheet pairing is therefore an
exact seam detector, not an independent zero-confinement theorem.
