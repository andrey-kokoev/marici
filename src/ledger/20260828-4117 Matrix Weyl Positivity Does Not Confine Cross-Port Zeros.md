# Matrix Weyl Positivity Does Not Confine Cross-Port Zeros

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

A rigged conservative realization naturally places the theta readout as an
off-diagonal entry of a matrix Weyl function because its forcing and endpoint
ports are distinct. Strict matrix Herglotz positivity does not constrain such
zeros. The example

\[
M(z)=
\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{pmatrix}
+z\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}
\]

has positive-definite imaginary part in the upper half-plane, while
\(M_{12}(i)=0\). The RH readout must instead be a full boundary determinant,
a source-fixed Schur complement, or belong to a rigid subclass coupling the
two ports.
