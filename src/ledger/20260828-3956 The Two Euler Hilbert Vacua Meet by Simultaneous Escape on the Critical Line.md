# The Two Euler Hilbert Vacua Meet by Simultaneous Escape on the Critical Line

The endpoint-normalized direct and reciprocal Euler vacua have squared norms

\[
\|v_s^+\|^2=\zeta(2\Re s),
\qquad
\|v_s^-\|^2=\zeta(2-2\Re s).
\]

Hence the direct vacuum is an ordinary Hilbert state exactly for
\(\Re s>1/2\), while the reciprocal vacuum is one exactly for
\(\Re s<1/2\). On the critical line both escape through the prime harmonic
divergence.

Thus the critical line is the simultaneous-loss wall of two complementary
Euler sectors. The half-offset comes from the squared half-density reaching
the prime harmonic exponent one. This explains the sewing geometry but does
not yet identify scalar zeros with loss of relative pairing.
