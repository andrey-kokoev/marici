# 2790 — The Hostile Cosh Pair Collapses to the Seam at Square-Root Rate

For \(F_c(z)=\cosh z+c\cosh 2z\), the hostile zero pair has real displacement

\[
\alpha(c)=\operatorname{arcosh}
\frac{1+\sqrt{1+8c^2}}{4c}.
\]

At the sharp threshold \(c=1\),

\[
\alpha(c)^2\sim\frac23(1-c).
\]

Hence \(c_N=1-1/N\) is hostile at every finite stage while its off-seam
separation tends to zero like \(N^{-1/2}\). Limit confinement therefore does
not imply finite-stage confinement, and finite hostility does not survive
completion without a uniform off-seam gap.
