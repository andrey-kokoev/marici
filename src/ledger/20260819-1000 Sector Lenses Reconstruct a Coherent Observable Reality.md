# 1000 — Sector Lenses Reconstruct a Coherent Observable Reality

## Status

Deutsch–Popperian conjecture.  This entry proposes an architecture to be falsified; it does not promote that architecture to the Marici core.

## Problem

The current sector program is often pictured as a universal object followed by specializations:

\[
\mathcal U
\longrightarrow
\mathcal O_s,
\qquad s\in S.
\]

That picture suggests that scattering, cosmology, strings, and other sectors are merely different parameter values or restrictions of one complete observable.  The accumulated comparisons do not support that strong claim.  Across sectors, the same carrier incidence and support calculus recur, while coefficient objects, filtrations, variance, and physical cycles differ.  Direct identifications repeatedly fail even when ranks, divisors, or characters agree.

## Frozen distinction

Keep separate:

- the common resolved carrier \(\mathfrak M\);
- its support-sensitive comparison calculus \(\mathscr C\);
- sector lenses \(L_s\);
- sector outputs \(\mathcal O_s=L_s(\mathfrak M,\mathscr C)\);
- a compatibility or reconstruction object assembled from several outputs;
- any interpretation in terms of conscious observation.

No claim about consciousness may be used to derive the mathematical construction.

## Conjecture M2.25 — Coherent Partial-Lens Reconstruction

There exists a family of partial, information-losing sector lenses

\[
L_s:(\mathfrak M,\mathscr C)
\longrightarrow
\mathcal O_s,
\qquad s\in S,
\]

such that no presently known sector output is the complete observable reality and no direct equality of sector coefficient objects is required.

The jointly accessible observable object is reconstructed from their typed compatibilities:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R_{\rm coh}
\simeq
\operatorname*{holim}_{s\in S}
\mathcal O_s
}
\]

or, where support and variance require it, by the corresponding derived totalization of a sector-comparison diagram.

The expression \(\operatorname{holim}\) is provisional notation for a universal compatibility construction.  It must not be treated as established until the variance of every comparison arrow is fixed.

## Current candidate lenses

The present program suggests, without exhausting the possible family:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\text{sector lens}&\text{information preferentially retained}\\
\hline
\text{scattering}&\text{asymptotic factorization and conserved kinematics}\\
\text{cosmology}&\text{sourced energy defects, time boundaries, and integrated periods}\\
\text{strings}&\text{monodromy, chamber transport, and finite-}\alpha'\text{ completion}\\
\text{support geometry}&\text{incidence, duality, nearby cycles, and Gysin data}\\
\text{physical-chain lens}&\text{which coefficient classes are activated by admissible cycles}.
\end{array}
\]

These are not assumed to be disjoint, jointly exhaustive, or canonically ordered.

## Hard-to-vary content

The conjecture makes three linked claims.

### Partiality

Each lens forgets information that another lens can detect:

\[
\exists s\neq t:
\ker L_s\not\subseteq\ker L_t.
\]

### Coherence

Sector outputs possess source-derived comparison maps on their shared supports.  Compatibility is imposed through those maps, not through fitted identifications of similarly shaped objects.

### Reconstruction gain

For at least one predeclared observable \(P\), the compatible multi-lens diagram determines \(P\), while every participating lens alone leaves a genuine ambiguity:

\[
H(P\mid\mathcal R_{\rm coh})
<
H(P\mid\mathcal O_s)
\qquad
\text{for every participating }s.
\]

Here \(H\) denotes reconstruction ambiguity, not necessarily a probabilistic entropy.

## Relation to the current H1–H3 architecture

This conjecture refines rather than replaces the existing hypotheses.

- It rejects the default expectation behind strong H1 that one universal coefficient object must appear identically in every sector.
- It is naturally compatible with H2:
  \[
  \text{shared carrier and calculus}
  +
  \text{sector-specific coefficient objects}.
  \]
- It strengthens H3 only if typed cross-sector coherence maps actually reconstruct additional observables.  Without those maps, the sectors remain merely parallel descriptions.

## Observer interpretation

If \(\mathcal R_{\rm coh}\) exists and is stable under the admissible physical-chain comparisons, it may model the single observable reality available to an observer: not a view supplied by one sector, but a jointly coherent record assembled from complementary partial lenses.

The additional phrase “as seen by conscious observers” is interpretive and presently unsupported.  A future observer theory would have to specify a further functor

\[
L_{\rm obs}:\mathcal R_{\rm coh}\longrightarrow\mathcal R_{\rm experienced}
\]

and independently derive its admissible records, memory consistency, and coarse-graining.  Consciousness is not inserted as a primitive of the carrier.

## First finite falsifier

Choose two nearby sectors for which the carrier and coefficient objects already exist—initially strings and scattering, or cosmology and scattering—and freeze:

1. one shared resolved support diagram;
2. one object from each sector with incompatible-looking but typed coefficient data;
3. all admissible comparison arrows and their variance;
4. one observable \(P\) not used to fit those arrows.

Then compute the derived compatibility object \(\mathcal R_{s,t}\).

The conjecture fails at this test if any of the following occurs:

- no source-derived comparison diagram can be typed;
- the compatibility object is equivalent to one sector output, so the second lens contributes no information;
- the diagram is inconsistent on a generic existing carrier stratum;
- reconstruction of \(P\) requires adding a fitted support cell, coefficient summand, or comparison map;
- a single sector lens already determines every tested joint observable and functorially factors the other lens.

The conjecture receives support only if

\[
\boxed{
\text{two independently derived partial lenses}
+
\text{typed coherence}
\longrightarrow
\text{strict reconstruction gain}.
}
\]

## Prohibited repairs

Do not preserve the conjecture by:

- defining \(\mathcal R_{\rm coh}\) as an untyped intersection of outputs;
- identifying coefficient objects from matching rank, divisor, character, or period value;
- adding a universal observer that merely stores all sector data without a derived compatibility law;
- declaring every failed comparison to be “complementarity”;
- treating an unavailable physical-chain map as zero;
- using consciousness to select a branch, regulator hierarchy, or normalization absent from the source.

## Narrow implication

The conjecture changes the sector program's primary question from

\[
\text{“Which sector is the universal one?”}
\]

to

\[
\boxed{
\text{“Which typed family of partial lenses has demonstrable reconstruction gain?”}
}
\]

Until the first finite reconstruction succeeds, the surviving claim remains only that shared carriers and comparison patterns make such a multi-lens architecture worth testing.
