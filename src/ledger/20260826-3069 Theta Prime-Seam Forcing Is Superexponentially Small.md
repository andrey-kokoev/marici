# 3069 — Theta Prime-Seam Forcing Is Superexponentially Small

At (L=\log p), the actual moving-seam forcing satisfies

\[
\Phi(L)\bigl(H_+(L,z)-H_-(L,z)\bigr)
\sim8\pi^{5/2}z p^5e^{-2\pi p^2}.
\]

Hence every polynomially weighted prime-seam series converges absolutely and
has a vanishing large-prime tail. Large prime seams faithfully detect the odd
channel, but carry asymptotically negligible magnitude.

The continuous moving-seam current therefore cannot be reconstructed by a
limiting Euler boundary flux with ordinary primitive, square, or valuation
weights. Any surviving identity must obtain the unsampled continuum from an
explicit Poisson or archimedean interpolation operator.

## Scope

The result does not exclude a source-derived interpolation identity. It rules
out reconstruction by polynomially weighted prime-infinity aggregation.
