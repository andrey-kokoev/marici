# 2801 — Inward Divisor Transport Is Not Closed under Positive Superposition

The exact affine pairs

\[
(F_1,G_1)=(z-10,1),
\qquad
(F_2,G_2)=(z+9,-100)
\]

move their respective zeros inward, with products \(-10\) and \(-900\). Their
positive sum has zero \(z=1/2\), velocity \(99/2\), and outward product
\(99/4\). Thus the class of state-current pairs satisfying atomwise inward
transport is not convex, even when both currents are restrictions of one
linear generator on their labelled span.
