# 698 — Generic Lower Algebraic Letters Add Normal Directions, Not Marked Sections

## Correction to the next search

Entry 697 proposes finding the smallest source marked section absent from
the physical wall cone but present in the generic lower algebraic-letter
sector. The comparison of frozen inputs shows that no such missing section
exists.

The generic lower sector uses the same Cayley–Menger carrier and the same
types of marked pole planes. Its additional algebraic behavior comes from
allowing site energies (X_i) and momentum magnitudes (P_i) to vary
independently. The homogeneous physical locus imposes

\[
P_i=X_i.
\]

Thus the missing data are the normal parameters

\[
\nu_i=P_i^2-X_i^2,
\]

not additional wall sections.

## Normal-grade expansion

Every finite two-pole algebraic radicand has the form

\[
\Delta_{ij}
=-4\nu_i\nu_j\Lambda_P F_{ij}.
\]

Hence all radicals vanish on the homogeneous locus, and their first nonzero
normal order is two. Setting (P_i=X_i=(x,y,z)) in the coefficient after
retaining the labelled monomial \(\nu_i\nu_j\) gives four coefficients:

\[
4(x-y-z)^2(x-y+z)^2(x+y-z)(x+y+z),
\]

\[
4(x-y-z)^2(x-y+z)(x+y-z)^2(x+y+z),
\]

\[
-4(x-y-z)(x-y+z)^2(x+y-z)^2(x+y+z),
\]

and

\[
-4(x-y-z)^2(x-y+z)(x+y-z)(x+y+z)^2.
\]

They are products solely of the established signed-energy factors.

## Quartic test

Exact polynomial gcd gives, for every first normal-grade coefficient
\(C_{ij}\),

\[
\boxed{
\gcd(C_{ij},\mathcal Q)=1.
}
\]

Therefore the generic lower algebraic radicals do not specialize to the
homogeneous quartic even in their first nonzero Rees grade:

\[
\boxed{
\text{generic lower letters add transverse coefficient directions, but
their leading normal grade does not produce }\mathcal Q.
}
\]

## Consequence

Searching for an absent marked section inside the homogeneous physical cone
would mix two parameter problems. Restoring generic lower algebraic letters
requires dehomogenizing the external kinematics, and the resulting first
normal coefficients remain supported on known signed-energy walls.

This removes the last explicit marked-section candidate proposed in Entry
697. It does not rule out a higher mixed normal operation coupling these
directions to the top-sector extension, but such an operation would need to
be derived independently.
