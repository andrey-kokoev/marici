# 3363 — Preserving Holonomies Do Not Distinguish the Flavor Carrier

## Claim

For the (SO(4)) vector restricted as (4=3+1) under the full physical
diagonal (SO(3)), the connected centralizer inside (SO(4)) is trivial and
the group centralizer is

\[
\{I_4,-I_4\}.
\]

Both preserving holonomies act identically on triplet and singlet and cannot
generate an ordered contrast. The smallest commuting distinguishing twist is

\[
\operatorname{diag}(-I_3,1),
\]

whose determinant is (-1). It lies outside (SO(4)), changes the groupoid,
and still permits the continuous residual form
(operatorname{diag}(aI_3,b)).

## Classification

Holonomy data obey a dichotomy: preserving holonomies do not distinguish;
distinguishing twists change the experiment and do not normalize (b-a).
Symmetry and holonomy alone therefore cannot select a nonzero CP-even portal
contrast.

## Scope

This covers holonomies acting on the WP743 (SO(4)) vector while preserving
the full declared diagonal (SO(3)). Larger groups and defects with additional
degrees of freedom are not excluded.
