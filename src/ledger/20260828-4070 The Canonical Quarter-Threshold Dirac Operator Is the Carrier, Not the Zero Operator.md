two-sector Dirac operator

\[
\mathcal Q

\begin{pmatrix}

\]

Its square has the common diagonal block

\[
L=-D(D+1)
=
H^2+\frac14

\frac14,
\qquad
H=-i\left(D+\frac12\right).
\]

The theta frontier current is

\[
F=-\frac1{2\pi}Le^{-\pi\rho^2}.
\]

continuous interval \([1/4,\infty)\), not the discrete Riemann-zero measure.

arithmetic boundary condition, extension, or scattering determinant that
selects discrete resonance data.
