# The Renormalized Prime Graph Bound Is RH in Disguise

Raw finite Euler trivializations lose invertibility throughout
$1/2<\operatorname{Re}s<1$ because the primitive prime sum diverges. This is
the deterministic mean-density wall, not a zero event.

After subtracting the pole carrier,

$$
-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}-\frac{s}{s-1}
=s\int_1^\infty(\psi(x)-x)x^{-s-1}\,dx.
$$

A uniform bound extending this connection through every compact subset of
$\operatorname{Re}s>1/2$ is equivalent in strength to the classical RH
estimate for $\psi(x)-x$. Thus graph-norm stability stated analytically merely
renames RH.

The next explanatory target is an independently derived operator conservation
law realizing the mean-subtracted fluctuation as boundary flux.
