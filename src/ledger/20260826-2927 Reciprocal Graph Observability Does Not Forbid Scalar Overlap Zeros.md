For a planar rotation \(T_\theta\) and vacuum \(e_1\),

\[
\langle e_1,T_\theta e_1\rangle=\cos\theta
\]

vanishes at a quarter turn, while

\[
T_\theta^*T_\theta+T_\theta T_\theta^*=2I.
\]

Thus reciprocal graph observability can be uniformly perfect at an exact
scalar overlap zero.  It repairs one-sided state blindness, not scalar
transversality.

The completion gates supplied by Kitaev matter only after a noncircular bridge
identifies \(\Xi(z)=0\) with noninvertibility of a source-derived operator.
The next indispensable theorem is therefore a determinant--section or
cohomology--section bridge derived from the labelled theta graph before any
zero data are used.
