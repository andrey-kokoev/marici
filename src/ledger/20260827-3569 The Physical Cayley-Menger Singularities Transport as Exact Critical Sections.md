# 3569 — The Physical Cayley–Menger Singularities Transport as Exact Critical Sections

## Hard-to-vary claim

The nonnegative singular scheme of the homogeneous Cayley--Menger boundary
contains exactly three Morse points. Under the physical shape deformation,
all three extend as exact critical sections of the full family.

The first local deformation class vanishes. The apparent nonzero second
symbols obtained by freezing the critical points also vanish after critical-
section transport. No residual supported class remains at generic parameter
values.

## Singular scheme at the homogeneous point

Write

\[
A=a^2,\qquad B=b^2,\qquad C=c^2.
\]

Exact active-coordinate elimination of

\[
K_0=K_a=K_b=K_c=0
\]

in the nonnegative chamber gives precisely

\[
(a,b,c)=(1,1,0),\qquad(1,0,1),\qquad(0,1,1).
\]

Each Hessian determinant equals

\[
-288,
\]

so all three germs are Morse.

## Exact critical sections

For

\[
P_1=1+t,\qquad P_2=1-t,\qquad P_3=1,
\]

\[
(1-t,1+t,0),
\qquad
(1,0,1+t),
\qquad
(0,1,1-t).
\]

Substitution into the complete Cayley--Menger family gives, identically in
(t),

\[
K=K_a=K_b=K_c=0
\]

on every section.

Their tangent vectors are

\[
(-1,1,0),
\qquad
(0,0,1),
\qquad
(0,0,-1),
\]

and each solves the exact first transport equation

\[
H_{K_0}v+\nabla K_1=0.
\]

## Second-grade correction

At the frozen homogeneous points,

\[
K_1=0,
\]

while the raw second derivatives are

\[
K_2=24,8,8.
\]

Those values are not invariant deformation classes. After transporting the
critical point, the effective second critical-value derivative is

\[
K_2-
(\nabla K_1)^T
H_{K_0}^{-1}
\nabla K_1.
\]

It vanishes at all three points.

Thus the proposed second-order supported record is refuted. It was created by
holding a moving critical point fixed.
