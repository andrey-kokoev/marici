# 2590 — Theta Boundary-Quotient Positive Cone Is Not Pointed

Let the relative detector be the quotient of regulator germs by divergent
boundary germs. If the boundary space contains a positive divergence
\(b_\varepsilon=\varepsilon^{-a}\), then for every scalar \(c\), both

\[
b_\varepsilon+c>0,
\qquad
b_\varepsilon-c>0
\]

eventually hold. Yet their quotient classes are \([c]\) and \([-c]\).
Consequently the cone induced from eventual bulk positivity contains both
orientations of every scalar finite-part class and is not pointed.

The missing RH orientation must therefore be additional source-derived
structure on the coupled bulk--boundary object, not ordinary positivity
passed through the boundary quotient.
