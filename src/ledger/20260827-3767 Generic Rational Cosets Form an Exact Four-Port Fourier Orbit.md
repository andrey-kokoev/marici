# 3767 — Generic Rational Cosets Form an Exact Four-Port Fourier Orbit

For shifted support and character-weighted combs

\[
P_r=\sum_n\delta_{n+r/q},
\qquad
C_r=\sum_ne^{2\pi i rn/q}\delta_n,
\]

Fourier transport gives

\[
\mathcal FP_r=C_{-r},
\qquad
\mathcal FC_r=P_r.
\]

Hence a generic rational shift follows the exact orbit

\[
P_r\longmapsto C_{-r}\longmapsto P_{-r}
\longmapsto C_r\longmapsto P_r.
\]

The four ports retain support shift, dual character, reflected support, and
conjugate character. The half-shift degenerates to a two-cycle; the unshifted
integer comb collapses all ports to one.

Thus the four-rung tower is forced by Fourier order four, with reflection at
the second step. The integer source only appears intrinsically one-port
because it is the common fixed object of the generic orbit.
