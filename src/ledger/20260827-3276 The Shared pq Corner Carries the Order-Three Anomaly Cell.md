# 3276 — The Shared pq Corner Carries the Order-Three Anomaly Cell

one their combined transfer increment is

\[
c=a+b+ab.
\]

\[
ab(a+b+ab/2).
\]

combined increment produces the negative of this anomaly. The full determinant
section is therefore exactly multiplicative. Deleting the shared \(pq\) corner
removes \(ab\) and breaks even the unregularized transfer identity.
