# 2834 — Quartic Log-Concave Carrier Is a Nonperturbative Hostile Source

The carrier

\[
g_c(x)=e^{-x^4-cx^2}
\]

is positive, even, Schwartz, and uniformly strictly log-concave. Its full
transform is beyond every algebraic high-frequency order, just like the theta
source. Nevertheless, a conjugate quartic saddle pair gives an exponentially
small oscillatory cosine factor, so its Fourier transform has real sign
changes and zeros.

confinement. This packet is not an off-axis hostile witness. Entry 2837 gives
the exact replacement.
