# Physical Effective Charge Does Not Imply a Canonical Flavor Channel

Two observable-defined effective charges of the same physical RG orbit can be

\[
E_A(u)=u,
\qquad
E_B(u)=u+\frac12u^2(1-u).
\]

They share endpoint values, tree normalization, monotonicity, the underlying
orbit, and both endpoint critical exponents. At the same portal event they
read \(1/2\) and \(9/16\).

The curvature extrema of the second record satisfy

\[
60u^4-108u^3+45u^2+4u-2=0.
\]

The old curvature anchors are not roots, and the polynomial has nonzero
resultant 53084160 with its reflection. Thus the effective-charge curvature
events remain process-relative even when both records are operationally
defined and agree at tree level.

## Consequence

The current source-authorized flavor probe family contains multiple labelled
record types, including poles, widths, rates, and interference. Such a family
may be jointly faithful without selecting one canonical scalar record.
Operationality therefore does not imply canonicality or source selection.

The required source principle must derive either a unique conserved-current
experiment, including its threshold completion, or a relation natural across
the complete physical probe family. Selecting an effective-charge channel
after inspecting the desired portal merely relocates the rigidifier.
