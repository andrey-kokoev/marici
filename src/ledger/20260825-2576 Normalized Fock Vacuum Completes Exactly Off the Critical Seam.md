# 2576 — Normalized Fock Vacuum Completes Exactly Off the Critical Seam

The normalized local occupation state is

\[
\omega_{p,s}
=\sqrt{1-p^{-2\sigma}}
\sum_{k\ge0}p^{-ks}e_{p,k},
\qquad \sigma=\Re s.
\]

Its overlap with the empty-occupation reference ray is

\[
\sqrt{1-p^{-2\sigma}}.
\]

The incomplete-infinite-tensor criterion is therefore equivalent to

\[
\sum_pp^{-2\sigma}<\infty.
\]

Hence the direct normalized Fock vacuum exists canonically exactly for
\(\Re s>1/2\), while its reciprocal dual exists exactly for
\(\Re s<1/2\). The critical seam is the representation threshold.
