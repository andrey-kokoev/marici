# Literal Single-Pole Twisted de Rham Closures Reproduce the Deletion Ranks

## Construction

Entry 577 admitted the block-pole twisted-de-Rham reducer after it reproduced
the zero-support rank seven at generic Kummer weight.  The reducer has now
been extended by one literal source denominator \(q\).

Pole presentations are indexed by

\[
(m,n)=(\text{rational }K\text{-pole},\text{literal }q\text{-pole}).
\]

For a polynomial vector field \(V\), the exact differential is represented
by the three adjacent terms

\[
\boxed{
\frac{\operatorname{div}V}{K^m q^n}
+(\gamma-m)\frac{V(K)}{K^{m+1}q^n}
-n\frac{V(q)}{K^m q^{n+1}}.
}
\]

The quotient also retains both localization transitions

\[
\frac{P}{K^m q^n}=\frac{PK}{K^{m+1}q^n},
\qquad
\frac{P}{K^m q^n}=\frac{Pq}{K^m q^{n+1}}.
\]

No generic regulator exponent is assigned to \(q\).  Its pole belongs to the
de Rham form, as required by the source.

## Exact calibration

Over \(\mathbf F_{32003}\) at

\[
(X_1,X_2,X_3)=(2,3,4),
\]

the deletion closure of polynomial forms at \(q\)-levels zero and one has
dimensions

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
q_{\mathfrak g_1}:&\quad 8,\\
q_{\mathfrak g_2}:&\quad 8,\\
q_{\mathcal G_{12}}:&\quad 16.
\end{aligned}
}
\]

These are exactly the independently certified closed ranks from Entry 340:

\[
7+1,qquad 7+1,qquad 7+9.
\]

The results are unchanged for:

- generic weights \(\gamma=5,7,11\);
- Cayley--Menger and denominator pole depths two and three;
- ambient polynomial degrees ten, twelve, and fourteen.

Depth one is insufficient and leaves dimension 28.  This is expected: the
first higher denominator pole is needed to expose cancellations generated by
differentiating a simple pole.  The stable result begins at depth two.

## Significance

This is the first reducer in the current line that simultaneously:

1. uses the physical placement of source denominators in the differential
   form;
2. keeps the Kummer weight generic;
3. retains exact homotopies rather than only a critical ideal;
4. reproduces the zero, lower-single, and \(q_{\mathcal G_{12}}\)-closed
   ranks.

Therefore the generic critical-point census and the literal twisted-de-Rham
presentation are now connected on every one-denominator face.  The former
counts the ranks; the latter supplies the chain-level object needed for
Gauss--Manin transport.

## Remaining gate

The next extension is the two-denominator pole lattice.  It must reproduce

\[
r_{011}=9,
\qquad
r_{101}=r_{110}=18,
\]

denominator has its own pole filtration and localization transition, the
calculation must retain the full product lattice; replacing it by a common
denominator would again lose the source labels and the deletion maps.
