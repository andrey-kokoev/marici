# The Prime-Valuation Tower Is a Geometric Boundary Resolvent

For exact \(p\)-adic valuation \(r\),

\[
B_{p,r}
=
p^{-r/2}B_{p,0}T_{r\log p}.
\]

Hence the full local comb is a geometric resolvent of its primitive
\(p\)-free boundary channel.

In the \(\varepsilon\)-sector rigging, the \(r\)-th contribution is bounded by

\[
p^{-(1+\varepsilon/2)r}.
\]

Every fixed-prime tower converges. Globally, the primitive layer converges for
\(\varepsilon>0\) but becomes \(\sum_p1/p\) at the critical boundary. Depth
two and higher remain prime-summable.

The completion obstruction is therefore the all-prime primitive layer, not
infinite valuation depth at one prime. It must remain as explicit relative
boundary data during reciprocal sewing.
