# Entry 1225 — The Five-Site Coefficient–Betti Pairing Is Deck Invariant

## Typed factors

Entries 1223–1224 produced two rank-32 deck objects:

1. the finite pushforward of the five-site Kummer coefficient system;
2. the regular orbit of the locally selected physical cycle $\Gamma_+$.

Neither the positive coefficient branch nor $\Gamma_+$ is separately invariant. The physical question is whether their pairing is invariant under simultaneous continuation.

## Sheet-basis pairing

Let $e_g$ denote the coefficient sheet basis and $\Gamma_h$ the Betti orbit basis for $g,h\in G=(\mathbb Z_2)^5$. Freeze

\[
\langle e_g,\Gamma_h\rangle=\delta_{g,h}.
\]

Deck transport is regular translation on both factors. Hence

\[
\langle T_ke_g,T_k\Gamma_h\rangle
=
\delta_{g\oplus k,h\oplus k}
=
\delta_{g,h}.
\]

Thus the pairing is strictly deck invariant.

## Character-basis form

Fourier transform by the real character table

\[
H_{S,g}=(-1)^{|S\cap g|}
\]

gives

\[
HH^T=32I.
\]

Both coefficient and Betti factors carry the same order-two character. Their diagonal character pairing is therefore invariant because every character square is one.

The checker verifies:

\[
1024
\]

Hadamard orthogonality identities,

\[
5120+5120
\]

sheet- and character-basis covariance identities, and

\[
1024
\]

joint chamber-evaluation identities. Every check passes.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\langle T c,T\Gamma\rangle
=
\langle c,\Gamma\rangle.
}
\]

A scalar evaluated in the physical chamber is continuation-covariant when its coefficient branch and relative cycle are transported together.

## Classification

The five-site square-root multiplicity is now accounted for by

\[
\boxed{
\text{Kummer coefficient local system}
+
\text{regular Betti orbit}

over the unchanged physical energy/Cut carrier. No carrier enlargement is indicated.

## Scope

This proves representation-theoretic deck descent of the pairing. It does not determine period normalization, endpoint regularization, or monodromy around discriminants not generated by the five Kummer sheet changes.
