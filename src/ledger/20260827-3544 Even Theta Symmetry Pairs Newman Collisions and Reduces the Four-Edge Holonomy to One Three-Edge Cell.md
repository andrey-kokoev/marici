# Even Theta Symmetry Pairs Newman Collisions and Reduces the Four-Edge Holonomy to One Three-Edge Cell

Evenness makes the spatial Prüfer component even and its heat component odd.
Moreover,

\[
H_\lambda(0)=\int_0^\infty e^{\lambda u^2}\Phi(u)\,du>0,
\]

so the central edge is phase-fixed and collision-free.

Every collision at positive height is mirrored at negative height. Their
combined degree is \(-2\), and backward heat emits one full off-seam quartet.
The complete rectangle is therefore two reflected copies of one three-port
half-cell—physical edge, de Bruijn edge, and positive infinity wall—glued
along the inert central source port.

Finite collision freedom reduces to vanishing of the three-edge
half-holonomy. The positive infinity wall remains indispensable.
