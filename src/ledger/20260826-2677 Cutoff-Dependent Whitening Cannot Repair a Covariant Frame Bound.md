# 2677 — Cutoff-Dependent Whitening Cannot Repair a Covariant Frame Bound

The quotient frame bound is exactly invariant under a common invertible source
change:

\[
\beta(HA,RA)=\beta(H,R).
\]

Whitening (H_\varepsilon=\operatorname{diag}(1,\varepsilon)) with
(A_\varepsilon=\operatorname{diag}(1,\varepsilon^{-1})) makes (HA=I), but
covariance also gives (RA=A), leaving the bound equal to (\varepsilon).
Comparing (HA) with untransported (R) is a false repair.
