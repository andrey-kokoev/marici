# Full Marked Total-Energy Nilpotent Has Rank Four

## Result

The separately derived absolute, conductor, and marked-extension blocks
assemble into the complete generic nonsoft total-energy residue of the
rank-twelve marked system. After removing the apparent integral residue and
using the source-forced Rees normalization of the primitive top lift,

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}N_E^{(12)}=4,
\qquad
(N_E^{(12)})^2=0.
}
\]

Its de Rham Jordan type is

\[
\boxed{J_2^{\oplus4}\oplus J_1^{\oplus4}.}
\]

The image decomposes by geometric type as

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{im}N_E^{(12)}
=
\operatorname{im}N_{\rm ell}
\oplus
\left\langle
\Theta_{101}^{\rm fix},
\Theta_{110}^{\rm fix},
\frac{e_6}{8(x+y)}
\right\rangle .
}
\]

The first summand is the nodal elliptic vanishing line. The other three
are algebraic Tate/Kummer marked-extension lines generated by the two
frozen walls and their primitive two-wall occurrence class. No new carrier
stratum is required.

## Nilpotence and rank

Every column of \(C_{\rm alg}\) lands in \(\mathcal T_7\), and Entry 289
gives \(N_{\rm ell}|_{\mathcal T_7}=0\). Since \(N_W=0\),

\[
(N_E^{(12)})^2
=
\begin{pmatrix}
N_{\rm ell}^2&N_{\rm ell}C_{\rm alg}\\
0&0
\end{pmatrix}
=0.
\]

The elliptic image is not contained in \(\mathcal T_7\): its infinity-Gysin
image is the nonzero nodal elliptic vanishing line. Thus it is independent
of the rank-three algebraic image, and

\[
\operatorname{rank}N_E^{(12)}=1+3=4.
\]

## Relation to the four enhanced points

The rank-four answer matches the number of enhanced points

\[

\]

but equality of ranks is not an identification. The missing global datum is
the canonical map from the lattice of four local enhanced-point semistable
classes to \(\operatorname{im}N_E^{(12)}\). It must explain simultaneously:

- the two mixed wall-extension lines;

No character projector on the four points is assumed before this map is
derived from the frozen log pair.

## Scope boundary

The result does not yet prove:

1. an integral normal form of the full rank-twelve lattice;
2. the map from four enhanced-point classes to the four image lines;
3. compatibility with the physical relative integration chain;
4. extension through \(xy(x+y)=0\) or intersecting discriminants;
5. a global decomposition over the entire signed-energy arrangement.
