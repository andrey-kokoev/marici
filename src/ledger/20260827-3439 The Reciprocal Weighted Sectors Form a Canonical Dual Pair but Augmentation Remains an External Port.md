# The Reciprocal Weighted Sectors Form a Canonical Dual Pair but Augmentation Remains an External Port

At off-seam distance `a>0`, the direct and reciprocal weighted prime spaces
`H_a` and `H_-a` are perfect duals under the ordinary coefficient pairing.
Reciprocal reflection exchanges them, Mellin translation preserves the
pairing, and `a=0` is the self-dual locus.

Primitive and prime-square coefficient rows belong to the reciprocal partner
for every positive `a`. The augmentation row belongs only when `a>1/2`.
Hence throughout the critical strip the augmentation/control channel cannot
be absorbed into either reciprocal Hilbert sector. It is the mathematically
forced shared port in the `2(3+2+1)+1` architecture.

The remaining test is whether the completed theta zero-state components land
in these complementary weighted graph domains and whether the full Green
identity uses only the cross-sector pairing plus the explicit augmentation
incidence.
