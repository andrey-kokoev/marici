# 2859 — The Staircase Green Identity Does Not Turn a Zero into an Adjoint State

Stieltjes integration by parts gives the exact staircase identity

\[
izI_W
=
\int_0^\infty WA'e^{izv}\,dv
+\sum_j a_jA(\ell_j)e^{iz\ell_j}.
\]

The scalar zero does not enter this identity. A positive two-shell source can
be chosen to have an off-seam zero while its staircase output remains nonzero
and fails reciprocal adjointness. Therefore zero-induced summability plus the
staircase Green law does not yield star-compatible extension. An additional
theta-specific coupling between heat flow and arithmetic jumps is required.
