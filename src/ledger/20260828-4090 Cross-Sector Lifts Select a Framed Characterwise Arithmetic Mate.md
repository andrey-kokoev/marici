# Cross-Sector Lifts Select a Framed Characterwise Arithmetic Mate

## Catch-up scope

The newest cross-sector results supply five compatible lift principles.

### Route-packet lift

Strominger factors a scalar observation as

\[
\mathcal S
\overset{T}{\longrightarrow}
\mathcal P_L\oplus\mathcal P_R
\overset{\sigma}{\longrightarrow}
\mathcal O,
\qquad
\sigma(A,B)=A+B.
\]

The exact sequence

\[
0
\longrightarrow
\ker T
\longrightarrow
\ker(\sigma T)
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{im}T\cap\ker\sigma
\longrightarrow0
\]

separates route loss from destructive interference. A scalar zero need not be
a vanished transported packet; it may be a nonzero anti-diagonal packet.

For the theta/Tate lane, the direct and reciprocal boundary germs are the two
routes. Their scalar completion is the augmentation.

### Source-frame lift

Strominger's magnetic character is not invariant as an unframed derivative of
a determinant. It becomes invariant after retaining the source framing, or
equivalently the induced determinant-line connection.

The arithmetic analogue must retain:

- the prime-power label basis;
- the reciprocal boundary basis;
- the adelic-height filtration;
- the primitive and square currents;
- the induced connection under every authorized presentation change.

A derivative of the scalar \(\xi\) without this frame is not the desired
object.

### Characterwise lift

Kitaev's mate audit decomposes the boundary packet into Fourier characters and
tests the generalized lower gain block by block. A scalar invariant row sees
only the trivial character and cannot certify an odd repaired block.

The arithmetic mate should therefore be valued in the full Fourier-character
boundary module before scalar compression.

### Cyclic lift

Aspect and Nima show that pairwise metric data can be complete while
orientation remains ambiguous. The first gauge-invariant orientation datum is
a cyclic holonomy, and two independent compilers of the same cycle provide a
strong coherence gate.

Thus a two-route packet and its Gram form are not enough. The determinant-line
connection must be tested around a source-authorized arithmetic cycle.

### Analysis-synthesis lift

Benincasa's barcode theorem retains both source-labelled synthesis rows and
dual analysis rows. Its hostile lift shows that finite-field canonicity alone
does not determine a characteristic-zero operational analyzer.

For the arithmetic packet, construction of boundary states must be accompanied
by independently derived dual functionals. Scalar Mellin evaluation alone is
not a faithful analysis family.

## Selected object

The smallest object incorporating all five lessons is a source-framed,
Fourier-character-valued arithmetic mate

\[
\mathfrak M_X(s):
\mathcal C_X
\longrightarrow
\bigoplus_{\chi\in\{1,-1,i,-i\}}
\left(
\mathcal B_{+ ,X,\chi}
\oplus
\mathcal B_{- ,X,\chi}
\right).
\]

It is constructed at finite adelic-height cutoff \(X\) before augmentation.
It must carry:

- direct and reciprocal synthesis routes;
- dual analysis maps;
- the source determinant-line connection;
- primitive and square boundary rows;
- Fourier-character blocks;
- cutoff-refinement maps.

The scalar completed section is only a final covector applied to
\(\mathfrak M_X(s)\).

## First exact audit

For each reachable character block, compute:

\[
K_{X,\chi}
=
\ker\bigl(
\sigma_\chi\mathfrak M_{X,\chi}
\bigr),
\]

and split it using the route-packet exact sequence into:

- genuine transport loss;
- nonzero interference packets.

Then compute the generalized gain of the full uncompressed block against the
source Gram form.

The first valuable result is not another scalar determinant. It is the
classification of every scalar-null direction by route mechanism and Fourier
character.

## Candidate cycle

The natural three-edge cycle is:

1. prime-power connectedization;
2. Fourier--Tate reciprocal transport;
3. boundary completion back to the framed arithmetic line.

Its holonomy must be defined from source operations before inspecting zeros.
The hostile symmetric multiplier must either fail to lift around this cycle or
change its holonomy.

If the cycle is flat for the actual source and for hostile divisor insertions,
it has no RH force. If the hostile changes the framed holonomy while the actual
source does not, this is the first non-universal orientation discriminator.

## Completion gate

Kitaev's vanishing-tail theorem restricts the possible outcomes. Uniform
completion can be repaired only by:

1. authorized localization;
2. relative renormalization;
3. an independent observation;
4. dynamical exposure.

A small modular tail cannot create uniform faithfulness by itself. The
characterwise generalized gains must be tested under cutoff refinement.

## Falsifiers

The proposed mate fails if:

- its full route transport is already noninjective for a source reason;
- its analysis rows are fitted from the desired scalar section;
- a reachable character block has vanishing generalized gain;
- cutoff refinement changes the framed connection by an uncontrolled term;
- the arithmetic cycle holonomy is unchanged by every hostile divisor;
- the two reciprocal routes cannot be jointly realized under one resource
  law.

## Disposition

The most useful transfer is not an additional positivity inequality. It is a
lift in arity and typing: the scalar zero is the compression of a two-route
character packet, which in turn is the boundary of a source-framed arithmetic
cycle.

The immediate construction target is the finite-cutoff mate
\(\mathfrak M_X(s)\), beginning with the trivial and sign Fourier blocks and
the primitive/square boundary rows.
