# The Prime-Two Additive Incidence Does Not Descend to the Valuation Seam

At a finite place the exact additive derivative analogue is

\[
\Delta_h=\tau_h-I,
\qquad
\mathcal F_p\Delta_h=(M_{\chi_h}-I)\mathcal F_p.
\]

This source-native Fourier square does not descend to the valuation quotient.
At prime \(2\), choose \(h=\tfrac12\). Then

\[
v_2(1/2)=v_2(3/2)=-1,
\]

but

\[
v_2(1/2+h)=v_2(1)=0,
\qquad
v_2(3/2+h)=v_2(2)=1.
\]

Thus no valuation-only \(P_{\mathrm{fin}}\) can represent the additive
incidence. The archimedean Green forcing defect cannot be cancelled directly
by attaching primitive or prime-square Euler currents through such a square.
The surviving object must retain additive residue data and multiplicative
valuation data simultaneously, most naturally as a local recollement or
residue-aware correspondence.
