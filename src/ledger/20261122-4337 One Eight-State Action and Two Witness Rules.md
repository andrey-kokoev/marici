# 4337 — One eight-state action and two witness rules

**Question:** What can we now identify across P/N/E readings and across the P/N/E and Ramsey systems? [Entry 4336](./20261122-4336%20Shared%20Steps%20and%20Shared%20Turns.md) distinguished a shared step from a shared turn on one path. It did not identify occurrences across paths or establish a common computation. The results below are the change since that entry.

**Answer — across paths.** A native N/E transfer can identify the same marked phase occurrence at different word positions: `C1` occurs at positions 2 and 4 in the [two readings](../../research/voevodsky/results/pne_cross_path_face.json). At a separate [route reunion](../../research/voevodsky/results/pne_rejoined_marks.json), three neutron crossings and a stay–cross–stay route reach the same packet. The two transfers of each marked input slot agree at the destination; the intermediate readings do not. Packet identity answers a question about the next reading, not about the path taken.

**Across cells.** The four-sector path gives a turn, a face crossing, and a turn. The crossing compares N with E; it is not the N step along the common edge. The adjacent P cell supplies the missing first P step. On the direct attachment there are [six sectors but five step occurrences](../../research/voevodsky/results/pne_sector_p_extension.json). A sector is not a unit of elapsed time or a particle move.

**Which neutron moves exist?** All 24 seed markings are admitted. Eight agree with the alternating lattice layers. For those seeds the N cell permits both stay and cross; for the other sixteen neither advance becomes available at N. The [local test](../../research/voevodsky/results/pne_all_n_axis_forks.json) is whether the internal diagonal of the split octahedron joins `C1` to `C2`. The P chart can predict this before transfer from the position of its fifth reference mark. This is a geometric test, not a preference for either move. An E packet in the target cell can prohibit a crossing that the geometry permits.

**A contact is not a certificate.** One valid neutron crossing can leave three new faces without a transfer witness. For a single-seed aligned assembly, at most four packet additions on occupied cells witness all three: two boundary transfers to P, at most one stay to select the other P face, and one transfer across the internal exit face. Four are sometimes necessary. In the [20-packet example](../../research/voevodsky/results/pne_three_pending_e_min.json), full exit certification takes **five**, not four. The successful route advances twice within N and takes the exit from the later reading. All earlier packets can then reach E. [All five-addition sets tested](../../research/voevodsky/results/pne_five_step_exit_search.json). Five is an example minimum, not a general exit bound.

**Common computation.** On the aligned N fork retain the site `C1` and the actual cell among the four children of one octahedron. There are eight geometric readings. Write `R` for stay and `X` for cross, in execution order from left to right. The routes `A = R`, `B = RXX`, `C = RX` realize the [Ramsey frame action](../../research/chatgpt/ramsey/ramsey38_frame_interface_freeze.md): each is its own inverse on readings, `AB = BA`, and `CAC = B`. From each reading the eight normal forms reach eight different states. This is the eight-element symmetry group of a square acting on eight states, not just two similar drawings. It also matches directed transitions when the selected move is retained. [Action checker](../../research/voevodsky/checkers/pne_eight_chart_action.mjs) · [transition checks](../../research/voevodsky/results/pne_ramsey_transition_lift.json).

**What does not match.** With `A = R` fixed, `C = XR` gives a second match. The matches agree on the starting square and disagree on the other layer. One crossing acts on the Ramsey wirings as a [four-cycle or its inverse](../../research/voevodsky/results/pne_ramsey_single_cross.json). The native Ramsey rule does not prescribe this as an execution step. In P/N/E the compound routes restore a geometric reading, not a packet's phase origin. The equivalence does not identify individual sites with Ramsey labels, select a preferred crossing direction, or imply a common physical mechanism.

**A decision that transfers.** The Ramsey probes cut one edge of the four-label cycle. Requiring agreement on the P attachment face selects the binding `3:C0, 5:C1, 2:M0, 4:M1`. This matches the *available* face support, not necessarily the selected P step. At address `{3,6}`, the red link selects the shared-step endpoints in four wirings and the two M sites in four. Its value is Ramsey probe bit 4. Given that bit at a reference wiring, the P/N/E reading predicts its change: **stay flips it; cross preserves it**. Neither the `C1` site nor the opposite-cell pair alone suffices. [Face support](../../research/voevodsky/results/ramsey_partner_complement.json) · [probe formula](../../research/voevodsky/results/ramsey_red_reference_probe.json) · [joint readout](../../research/voevodsky/results/ramsey_red_step_joint_test.json).

**Why the Ramsey bit is forced.** Source constraints, not just the eight archived outputs, imply that slots 4 and 74 agree. The triangle ban with size-five and size-six coverage suffices on the full chart; neither coverage size alone with the triangle ban suffices. Without address `{1,6}`, size-seven coverage restores the equality even though the earlier proof through address `14` no longer applies. In the groups needed for that proof, `Q = {2,3,4,5}` has no eligible link. It occupies one place, so two of the other six addresses must have a red link. A mismatch therefore has a checkable witness: six addresses with no internal red link. This explains a constraint of the Ramsey implementation; no corresponding enforcement rule has been shown for P/N/E. [Source-rule ablations](../../research/voevodsky/results/ramsey_probe_equality_constraints.json) · [reduced-chart test](../../research/voevodsky/results/ramsey_without16_incident_link.json).

**Unanswered / restart:** Address incidence reduces 8,008 possible six-address tests to a verified list of 106 for each mismatch; this is not a global minimum. Knowing that one omitted candidate fails does not identify a single replacement. For each of 22 unresolved omission cases, compatible small-rule wirings require different retained witnesses. Can one more observed bit select between two fixed retained tests in each case? Test this before searching for a larger decision tree.
