# 3185 — Identical Beams Do Not Orient the CP Port

## Result

At an unpolarized proton-proton source, the candidate signed CP port

\[
S=D b\sqrt{uv}\sin\phi
\]

is odd under beam exchange (b\leftrightarrow-b). Its untagged expectation
vanishes exactly. Squaring the port descends but erases the phase sign.

## Relational repair

A source-derived beam-odd tag (t) makes (tS) exchange invariant while
retaining its odd phase response. No associated-production constructor or
calibrated tag is currently admitted.
