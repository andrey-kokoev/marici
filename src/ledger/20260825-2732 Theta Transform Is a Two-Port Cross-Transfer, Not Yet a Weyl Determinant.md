# 2732 — Theta Transform Is a Two-Port Cross-Transfer, Not Yet a Weyl Determinant

On the self-adjoint logarithmic transport carrier, use boundary and source
ports

\[
b_0=\delta_0,
\qquad
b_f=f.
\]

The cross resolvent entry

\[
\langle b_f,(A-z)^{-1}b_0\rangle
\]

is the one-sided theta Fourier--Laplace transform up to fixed convention. The
full two-port Weyl determinant additionally contains both diagonal
self-energies and the reciprocal cross entry. No source-derived identity yet
reduces that determinant to the theta scalar up to a nowhere-zero unit.

Theta zeros are therefore presently transmission zeros between noncollocated
ports, not eigenvalues or determinant zeros. A source-derived exteriorization,
Plücker relation, or boundary reduction is the next gate.
