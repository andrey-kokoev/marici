# 2568 — Finite Theta Translate Germs Are Source-Faithful

For every fixed \(d>0\), the explicit completed-theta expansion gives

\[
\frac{\Phi(u+d)}{\Phi(u)}\longrightarrow0
\qquad(u\to+infty)
\]

super-exponentially. Hence, in a finite linear combination of distinct
translates, the least shift asymptotically dominates. Removing it inductively
proves

\[
\sum_jc_j\Phi(t+q_j)\equiv0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
c_j=0\quad\text{for every }j.
\]

The complete seam germ—and equivalently its full Cauchy-jet tower—is therefore
faithful on every finite labelled coefficient packet. Some finite jet
truncation suffices for each fixed packet.

## Scope

The sufficient jet order need not be uniform. The theorem does not provide a
cutoff-independent lower bound or completion-stable germ topology.
