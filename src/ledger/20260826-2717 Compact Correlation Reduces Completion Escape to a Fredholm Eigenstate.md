# 2717 — Compact Correlation Reduces Completion Escape to a Fredholm Eigenstate

If the normalized phase-sensitive correlation is a compact perturbation of a
background with spectral ceiling uniformly below one, diffuse completion
escape is impossible. Coercivity then fails exactly through a finite-
multiplicity eigenstate at eigenvalue one. A rank-one hostile shows that all
finite compressions may remain strictly positive while the completed
eigenstate appears; compactness localizes but does not exclude the obstruction.
