# Character-Orbit Desmoothing Is Exact but Endpoint-Zero Blind

Grothendieck sharpened the Hankel desmoothing obstruction.

For (\operatorname{Re}z<0), the authorized half-line character satisfies

\[
G_z=\mathsf H_fe_z,
\qquad
\mathsf H_f^{-1}G_z=e_z,
\qquad
\|e_z\|_2^2=\frac{-1}{2\operatorname{Re}z}.
\]

Thus desmoothing is already exact and pointwise finite on every character in
the open sector, although its norm diverges toward the seam.

Nevertheless the physical zero condition (G_z(0)=0) is compatible with
nonzero (G_z) and (e_z). Therefore controlling the unbounded inverse on the
character orbit cannot itself imply RH.

The missing theorem must couple the direct and reciprocal desmoothed charts
through a global boundary law and prove that simultaneous two-ended endpoint
nullity is possible only on (\operatorname{Re}z=0).
