# 2884 — The Complete One-Prime Quarter-Density Overlap Is Nonvanishing

For logarithmic-derivative weights, the normalized reciprocal overlap of one
complete prime-power tower is

\[
\rho_p(\theta)
=\frac{(1-p^{-1/2})e^{2i\theta}}
{1-p^{-1/2}e^{2i\theta}},
\]

with modulus at least
\((1-p^{-1/2})/(1+p^{-1/2})\). The Euler-log tower is also nonvanishing.
Therefore cancellation is not local to one coherent prime tower. It enters
through inter-prime aggregation, infinite-product collapse, or the
archimedean interval amplitudes.
