# The Complete Mellin Grade Tower Is Faithful under Source Decay

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

For exponentially decaying coefficients on a prime-power orbit, all Mellin
grades are the derivatives at zero of one analytic generating function

\[
F(z)=\sum_{j\ge0}c_j e^{zj\log p}.
\]

If every grade vanishes, the analytic germ vanishes identically; its power
series coefficients then give \(c_j=0\) for all \(j\). Thus every finite
grade packet is blind, while the complete analytic tower is jointly
faithful. Primitive and square currents are only its first coordinates.
