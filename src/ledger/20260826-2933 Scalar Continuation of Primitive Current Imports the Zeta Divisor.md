Möbius inversion gives

\[
P(s)
=
\sum_{m\ge1}\frac{\mu(m)}m\log\zeta(ms).
\]

Therefore scalar continuation of the primitive current, and hence the square
current \(P(2s)/2\), imports logarithmic singularities and monodromy from the
unknown zeta divisor.  Using such a continuation to construct the global
determinant section is circular.

Only the connected \(k\ge3\) tail may be scalarized near the critical seam.
The first two cumulants must remain labelled boundary operators or
distributions until reciprocal and archimedean sewing have produced the
completed determinant line.
