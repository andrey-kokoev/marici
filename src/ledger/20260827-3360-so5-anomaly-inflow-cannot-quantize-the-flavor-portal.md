# 3360 — SO(5) Anomaly Inflow Cannot Quantize the Flavor Portal

## Claim

The minimal (SO(5)) gauge-connection carrier admits no ordinary local
five-dimensional gauge Chern–Simons selector for the boundary contrast. Every
component of

\[
d_{abc}=\operatorname{tr}\bigl(T_a\{T_b,T_c\}\bigr)
\]

vanishes in both the vector and Clifford-spinor realizations of (SO(5)). The
diagonal (SO(3)) triplet tensor also vanishes. An (SU(3)) comparator is
nonzero, verifying that the checker detects a genuine cubic invariant.

Independently, reducing a five-dimensional Chern–Simons term produces a
parity-odd response of type (A_5F\wedge F), not the required CP-even portal
quadratic in both Higgs and flavor carriers.

## Classification

Ordinary perturbative anomaly inflow is neither an asymmetric portal selector
nor a portal rigidifier. It leaves the continuous residual-symmetry boundary
contrast (b-a) untouched.

## Scope

This covers local perturbative gauge anomalies and Chern–Simons inflow for the
minimal group. Global anomalies, larger groups, discrete torsion, and nonlocal
holonomies remain separate candidates, but must pass the same operator-type
gate.
