# The Newman Threshold Has a Cubic Local Collision Model but May Be Set at Infinity

In a seam coordinate, the universal collision of two critical-line folds is

\[
X_\tau(w)=w^2-\tau,
\qquad
Y_\tau(w)=\frac{w^3}{3}-\tau w.
\]

For positive \(\tau\), the two folds lie on the seam. At zero they form one
cubic critical event. For negative \(\tau\), they leave the seam as a
conjugate pair of interior branch points. This supplies the exact local
geometry behind the operator's “last-moment split” intuition.

The de Bruijn--Newman theorem gives a threshold \(\Lambda\) for the real-zero
phase. RH implies \(\Lambda\leq0\), while Rodgers--Tao proved
\(\Lambda\geq0\). Thus RH would put the physical source exactly at the last
permitted heat time.

The decisive correction is global: the threshold need not be witnessed by a
finite-height cubic collision. A sequence of collisions may escape to
spectral infinity. Local fold confinement therefore cannot prove RH without a
source-derived no-incoming-index-flux theorem at infinity.
