# 3325 — The Complete Interval Observer Is Faithful but Scalar Nullity Fixes Only an Endpoint Projection

## Result

The doubled full interval section

\[
\mathcal O(\zeta)(\ell)
=
\left(B_\ell(\zeta),B_\ell(-\zeta)\right)
\]

is a common comparison fiber for reciprocal and adjoint source paths. It is
faithful in `zeta`: equality of two sections implies equality of their
parameters after differentiation on any source-positive interval.

Scalar nullity fixes only the one-dimensional aggregate of the terminal
doubled state. Reciprocal and adjoint terminal vectors can differ inside that
scalar-kernel hyperplane, and their complete paths can differ as well.

Thus the remaining RH-bearing gate is a source-derived two-stage uniqueness
theorem: lift the zero scalar projection to a terminal doubled state, then
lift that state to the complete interval path.

## Scope

This does not construct the path-lifting law or prove RH. It solves the common
comparison-fiber and parameter-faithfulness gates while isolating projection-
to-state-to-path uniqueness as the unresolved step.
