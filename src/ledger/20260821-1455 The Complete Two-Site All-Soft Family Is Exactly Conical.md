# 1455 — The Complete Two-Site All-Soft Family Is Exactly Conical

## Status

Exact strengthening of Entry 1454. The radial/angular separation holds for
the complete two-site soft family, not only its leading homogeneous grade.

## Joint scaling

The full transformed two-site source form is, up to a source-fixed unit,

\[
\Omega
=
\frac{u^{\alpha_1}v^{\alpha_2}\,du\,dv}
{(u+X_1+y)(v+X_2+y)(u+v+X_1+X_2)}.
\]

Under the simultaneous soft scaling

\[
(u,v,X_1,X_2,y)
\longmapsto
R(u,v,X_1,X_2,y),
\]

the three denominators each have weight one, the measure has weight two, and
the Kummer numerator has weight \(\alpha_1+\alpha_2\). Hence

\[
\boxed{
\Omega
\longmapsto
R^{\alpha_1+\alpha_2-1}\Omega.
}
\]

There are no higher or lower radial terms.

## Strict transform

On the ordinary all-soft blowup, write every variable as \(R\) times its
projective ratio. The strict transform has the form

\[
\boxed{
\Omega
=
\frac{dR}{R}
R^{\alpha_1+\alpha_2-1}
\otimes
\Omega_{\rm proj}.
}
\]

The projective relative form \(\Omega_{\rm proj}\) contains all dependence on
the ratios of

\[
u:v:X_1:X_2:y
\]

and is independent of \(R\). At the central positive chart it reduces to Entry
1454's beta-form interval. Away from the central point it is the corresponding
marked projective hypergeometric coefficient object.

## Consequence

The radial Gauss--Manin connection is rank-one Kummer and cannot mix with the
projective coefficient system through a higher normal differential:

\[
\boxed{
\text{no first or higher nonhomogeneous radial extension exists in the
two-site primitive.}
}
\]

Thus the complete all-soft two-site endpoint is generated by:

- the existing all-soft blowup carrier;
- its inherited projective incidence arrangement;
- a radial Kummer line;
- a sector-specific projective hypergeometric coefficient object.

No new carrier stratum or radial coherence cell is required.

## Scope boundary

are linear and whose Kummer factors are homogeneous. Numerator corrections,
mass scales, or non-power-law backgrounds can break this statement and must be
tested independently.
