# 2584 — Theta Heat Finite Part Constructs the Relative Euler Detector

The theta-native regulator gives

\[
Z_\varepsilon(s)
=\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-s}e^{-\pi\varepsilon n^2}.
\]

\[
Z_\varepsilon(s)
=\frac12\Gamma\left(\frac{1-s}{2}\right)
(\pi\varepsilon)^{(s-1)/2}

\]

therefore yields \(\zeta(s)\). This constructs the critical-strip detector as
a relative boundary value rather than ordinary Hilbert duality.
