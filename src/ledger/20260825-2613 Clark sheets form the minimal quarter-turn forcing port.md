# 2613 — Clark Sheets Form the Minimal Quarter-Turn Forcing Port

\(e=(1,1)\). Its quarter-turn is \(Je=(-1,1)\), which is independent of

\[
\begin{pmatrix}H_a+f\\H_{-a}+f\end{pmatrix}
=(G+f)e-ia\,\partial_zG\,Je.
\]

The two incidence columns are orthogonal. Their norm is

\[
2|G+f|^2+2a^2|\partial_zG|^2,
\]

which is precisely the native positive Clark Gram bulk. Clark doubling is
therefore the minimal source-derived forcing port compatible with the
cofactor quarter-turn.
