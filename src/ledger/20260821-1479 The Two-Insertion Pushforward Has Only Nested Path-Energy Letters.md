# 1479 — The Two-Insertion Pushforward Has Only Nested Path-Energy Letters

## Status

Exact source-derived integrand and exact first pushforward. The complete
twofold integral has not yet been evaluated, so no weight-two closed form is
claimed.

## Source derivation

Expand every conformal bulk-to-bulk edge propagator into its two ordered bulk
terms and its boundary-subtraction term. For each resulting edge-state word,
sum all linear extensions of its time-order poset and integrate the ordered
times by suffix-energy denominators. This derives the path integrand without
fitting a rational ansatz.

As a calibration, the same algorithm applied to one white site, with its de
Sitter weight \(w_1\), reproduces all four logarithmic coefficients of Eq.
(4.7) of Benincasa, arXiv:1909.02517v1 exactly.

## Two-white-site integrand

For the path

\[
x_1\;--\;w_1\;--\;w_2\;--\;x_2
\]

with common edge energy \(y\), the derived rational integrand has the ten
linear denominator factors

\[
\begin{aligned}
&(x_2+y)(w_2+2y)(w_2+x_2+y)(w_1+2y)\\
&\quad\times(w_1+w_2+2y)(w_1+w_2+x_2+y)(x_1+y)\\
&\quad\times(x_1+w_1+y)(x_1+w_1+w_2+y)
(x_1+w_1+w_2+x_2),
\end{aligned}
\]

and the checker derives and retains its exact cubic numerator. The physical
double insertion supplies the measure weight \(w_1w_2\).

## First Kummer pushforward

As a rational function of \(w_2\), the weighted integrand has six simple
poles. Exact residue reconstruction gives

\[
w_1w_2 I_2
=\sum_{j=1}^{6}\frac{A_j(w_1)}{w_2+L_j(w_1)},
\qquad
\sum_jA_j=0,
\]

with

\[
\boxed{
\{L_j\}
=
\{2y,\ x_2+y,\ w_1+2y,\ w_1+x_2+y,
\ x_1+w_1+y,\ x_1+w_1+x_2\}.
}
\]

Therefore the convergent first pushforward is

\[
-\sum_j A_j(w_1)\log L_j(w_1).
\]

Both the vanishing residue sum and the full rational reconstruction are
verified symbolically over \(\mathbb Q(x_1,w_1,w_2,x_2,y)\).

## Interpretation

No new divisor has appeared. Every logarithmic argument is a connected
partial-energy sum inherited from the labelled path, and the four
\(w_1\)-dependent letters are precisely its nested subpath sums. Thus the
second insertion enlarges the coefficient alphabet by iterated path incidence,
not by changing the carrier.

This supports—but does not yet prove—the conjecture that higher mass
insertions are iterated Kummer/divided-difference pushforwards on the existing
path-energy arrangement.

## Next falsifier

Perform the remaining \(w_1\) pushforward and compute its symbol. The
iterated-incidence hypothesis fails if a symbol letter is not generated by
the source partial-energy arrangement, or if a nonremovable mass-difference
pole survives.
