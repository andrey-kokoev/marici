# 3356 — Gauge Invariance Forces the Carrier but Forbids Bulk Contrast

## Claim

For a five-dimensional (SO(5)) gauge connection on (S^1/Z_2) with parity
(P=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,1,-1)), one-form covariance forces exactly four
even (A_5) zero modes. They form the (SO(5)/SO(4)) vector and decompose as
(3+1) under the declared diagonal (SO(3)). There is no independent scalar
intrinsic parity, and one bulk gauge coupling parallelizes the packet.

However,

\[
\operatorname{Sym}(4)^{SO(4)}=\mathbb R I_4.
\]

Every bulk quadratic portal therefore has zero singlet–triplet contrast. At a
boundary preserving only diagonal (SO(3)), the commutant enlarges to

\[
\operatorname{diag}(a,a,a,b),
\]

and the free ordered contrast (b-a) reappears.

## Classification

Higher-dimensional gauge invariance is a source-derived carrier selector and
coupling parallelizer. It is neither an asymmetric portal selector nor a
magnitude selector. The first locus permitting asymmetry also permits its
independent coefficient.

## Scope

The result covers bulk quadratic portal forms and residual-symmetry boundary
forms for this orbifold packet. It does not exclude a quantized asymmetric
holonomy or anomaly-fixed boundary operation derived from additional source
dynamics.
