# 2660 — Positivity Improvement Separates Survival from Faithful Observability

A positivity-improving correspondence guarantees survival of nonzero positive
inputs under positive probes. It need not be injective or informationally
faithful. The exact rank-one replacer sends every normalized positive input to
the same interior ray and kills the signed difference ((1,-1)).

Completion stability separates into at least three additional reserves:
radial gain, anchored distance from the cone boundary, and contextual kernel
separation. Projective cross-ratios alone control none of these: scalar
attenuation leaves them unchanged, and a constant-cross-ratio family can still
approach the boundary in the frozen source frame.

For theta/Tate, positivity is applicable only after deriving the source cone,
proving the relevant state belongs to it, transporting its unit/trace/probes,
and establishing uniform gain and margin. Faithful state observation remains
an independent theorem.
