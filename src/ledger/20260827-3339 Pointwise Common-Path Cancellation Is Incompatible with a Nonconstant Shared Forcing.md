# Pointwise Common-Path Cancellation Is Incompatible with a Nonconstant Shared Forcing

The conditional shortcut `u+v=0` has been tested against the native reciprocal
flows

\[
u'=-zu-cf,
\qquad
v'=+zv-cf.
\]

If `v=-u` throughout an interval, comparison of the two equations forces

\[
zu+cf=0,
\qquad
u'=0.
\]

For nonzero `z` and nonzero amplitude `c`, this makes `f` constant. For `z=0`
it forces `cf=0`. Therefore a nonconstant nontrivial theta forcing admits no
nonzero same-source doubled solution with pointwise common-mode cancellation.

The primitive-lifted Green identity cannot close through a pathwise upgrade of
the terminal Evans condition. The actual Fourier--Tate transport of the dual
forcing is now load-bearing: it must change the forcing incidence, provide an
additional current for the common residual, or close the conservation route.
