# A Simple Line Zero Is a Quadratic Boundary Fold While an Off-Line Zero Would Be an Interior Branch Point

At a simple zero `z_0`, the source potential has local form

\[
Y(z)-Y(z_0)
=\frac14X'(z_0)(z-z_0)^2+O((z-z_0)^3).
\]

For a line zero `z_0=i gamma`, the quadratic coefficient is purely imaginary.
Writing `z-z_0=a+i tau`, the leading real potential is proportional to

\[
a\tau.
\]

Its zero-level branches are therefore the seam and its normal ray, meeting at
ninety degrees. The potential squares the local coordinate and doubles angles.

Thus a simple line zero is a boundary fold of the completed comparison map.
An off-line zero would be an interior quadratic branch point. RH becomes
confinement of the potential's branch divisor to the reciprocal boundary.
