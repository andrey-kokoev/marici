## Epistemic boundary

This result says that the available frozen source does not canonically remove
the tail. It does not prove that a future source-derived sheeted
relative-cycle construction cannot do so. Such additional data would first
have to be acquired and frozen; it cannot be reverse-engineered from the
desired cancellation.

The equality of the eventual symbol image with ((a^4)) remains tested
shellwise through degree 40 over one large finite field, as stated in Entry
441.
