# 3910 — RH Is Almost Periodicity of the Divisor-Resolution Signal

Weight the positive divisor-supply measure by

\[
w_k(t)=(1+t^2)^{-k}
\]

and Fourier transform it. The atomic seam part becomes an absolutely
convergent sum of undamped characters, hence a Bohr almost-periodic function.
The diffuse off-seam part becomes a `C_0` function by the
Riemann–Lebesgue lemma.

Since a nonzero function cannot be both Bohr almost periodic and vanish at
infinity, RH is equivalent to almost periodicity of this regularized
resolution signal.

This creates a source-facing target: prove that theta–Euler completion carries
the discrete Mellin-character grammar to the seam inside the almost-periodic
category. An off-line zero is exactly a forbidden decaying component.

## Scope

This proves the almost-periodicity equivalence for the boundary-derived
signal. It does not derive almost periodicity from theta–Euler constructors or
prove RH.
