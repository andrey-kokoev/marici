# 2729 — Reciprocal-Sheet Winding Cancels Globally, Not Sheetwise

Opposite sheet windings make the combined normalization globally trivial, but
odd winding leaves both constituent sheets without square-root frames. The
pairs \((z,z^{-1})\) and \((z^2,z^{-2})\) have identical combined scalar and
vacuum normalization while differing in sheetwise root existence. Ubersector
closure therefore does not imply constituent-sheet constructor closure.
