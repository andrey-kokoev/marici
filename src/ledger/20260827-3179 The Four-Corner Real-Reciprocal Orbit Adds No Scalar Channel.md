# 3179 — The Four-Corner Real–Reciprocal Orbit Adds No Scalar Channel

For (a=H(z)) and (b=H(-z)), the orbit under reciprocal reflection and
conjugation is only

\[
(a,b,\bar a,\bar b).
\]

Since (T=a+b) and (A=a-b) recover (a,b), conjugation adds no scalar
degree. At a completed zero the four-corner matrix has rank one, but this does
not distinguish seam from off-seam cancellation.

## Consequence

The next nonredundant rung is not another amplitude. It is a source-level
2-cell comparing reciprocal-then-conjugate with conjugate-then-reciprocal
before labelled boundary currents are aggregated.
