# 2202 — The Frozen Deletion Weights Are Not a Positive Instrument

## Positivity test

On the eight Boolean deletion outcomes the frozen coefficients are

\[
(1,-2,-2,-2,4,4,4,-8).
\]

Four outcomes have negative coefficient. Therefore the augmentation cannot
itself be interpreted as an ordinary positive measurement or classical
mixture over deletion histories. In particular, conditioning on a deletion
label is not automatically a physical which-route measurement.

The obstruction is basis-independent at the level of the declared Boolean
outcomes: a positive instrument applied to arbitrary nonnegative sector
readouts cannot reproduce negative coordinate functionals.

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\text{source augmentation is a signed readout, not a deletion probability.}
}

This strengthens Entry 2201: the cancellation is built into a signed
interference functional. A physical preaggregation realization requires a
quasiprobability, an ancilla with a signed observable, or some independently
derived coherent equivalent.
