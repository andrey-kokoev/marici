# The PC-Cech Target Is a Ringed Alexandrov Cellular Space

Entry 421 reduced the remaining geometric problem to a ringed lift. The
first prerequisite from Entry 348—geometrizing the target—is canonical and
can now be completed.

Let \(\mathfrak P_{\rm PC}\) be the finite face poset of the real oriented
boundary blowup of \(K_6\). Its points are the 215 loaded cells
\[
(S,H),\qquad H\subseteq S,
\]
and its Alexandrov topology is generated by open stars. At \((S,H)\), assign
the ring
\[
\mathcal O_{S,H}
=R[X]\bigl[u_a^{-1}:a\in S\setminus H\bigr].
\]

Every covering incidence adds exactly one localization:

- a radial incidence \(S\to S\cup\{a\}\) adds \(u_a^{-1}\), with cellular
  coefficient \(X_a/u_a\);
- a normal incidence \(H\to H\setminus\{h\}\) also adds \(u_h^{-1}\), with
  normalized coefficient \(1\).

Thus every structure map is a flat localization. No occurrence variable or
integer is inverted.

## Exact cellular audit

The independent enumerator reconstructs:
\[
\#\{\text{faces by dissection size}\}=(1,9,21,14),
\]
\[
\#\mathfrak P_{\rm PC}=215,
\qquad
\operatorname{rk}C_\bullet=(14,63,93,45).
\]
It checks every radial, normal, and mixed two-step path. The localization
sets at the two endpoints agree, while the oriented incidence signs cancel.
Consequently the cellular differential
\[
d[S,H]
=\sum_a\epsilon(S,a)\frac{X_a}{u_a}[S+a,H]
+(-1)^{3-|S|}
\sum_{h\in H}(-1)^{\operatorname{pos}(h)}[S,H-h]
\]
satisfies \(d^2=0\) on all 215 cells.

Therefore Entry 143's target is not merely an explicit complex that lacks a
space. It is the cellular-chain realization of a constructible module on
the ringed Alexandrov space
\[
\boxed{(\mathfrak P_{\rm PC},\mathcal O_{\rm PC}).}
\]
The support subspaces \(F_V\subset F_B\subset F_K\) are closed subposets, so
their subquotient filtration is functorial on the ringed finite space.

## Consequence and boundary

This closes d_central_flip_target_geometrization, the first of Entry 348's
three prerequisites. Finite-space restriction, extension by zero, support
sections, and left/right Kan extensions are now typed on the actual target.
Because the stalk maps are flat localizations, the cellular Čech model
computes the corresponding derived restrictions without an additional
resolution.

The next prerequisite is the projection: lift the normalized barycentric
blowdown of Entry 396 to a morphism of ringed finite spaces. Its underlying
poset map already exists and has contractible fibers. What remains is to
verify the stalk-ring maps against the occurrence lcm coefficients and show
that derived left Kan extension has no higher fiber terms. The relative
dualizing trace is a subsequent step.

This construction does not yet identify the finite ringed space with the raw
algebraic normalization-sheet/DNC space.
