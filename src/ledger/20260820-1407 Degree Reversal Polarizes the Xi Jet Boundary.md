# 1407 — Degree Reversal Polarizes the Xi Jet Boundary

Sequence claim: \`seqclaim-17a47868dc921385d10f4852\`.

Epistemic-graph event: 1457.

At a real Xi zero \(\lambda\) of order \(m\), write

\[
\Xi(z)=u_\lambda(z-\lambda)^m+\cdots .
\]

On the maximal-ideal associated graded, the residue form in the canonical jet
basis satisfies

\[
R_\lambda(e_i,e_j)
=u_\lambda^{-1}\delta_{i+j,m-1}.
\]

Define degree reversal by

\[
C_\lambda e_j=u_\lambda e_{m-1-j}.
\]

Then the polarized form

\[
h_\lambda(x,y)=mR_\lambda(x,C_\lambda y)
\]

has Gram matrix \(mI_m\). It is positive, retains every multiplicity
direction, and restricts on the value line to
\(m|G(\lambda)|^2\), exactly the scalar Weil term.

Completing the associated graded of the source-generated ideal quotient
\(\mathcal H_{\leq1}/(\Xi)\) gives a boundary whose diagonal spectral operator
is, conditional on RH, self-adjoint with compact resolvent and

\[
\det\nolimits_2(I-zA^{-1})=\Xi(z)/\Xi(0).
\]

This closes the semisimplification, multiplicity, and local-polarization gates
without a numerical zero list. The remaining analytic gate is exactly RH:
a nonreal Xi zero gives a nonreal eigenvalue and prevents self-adjointness in
every positive metric.
