# 2631 — Rosenbrock Zeros Do Not Commute with Completion

For a finite realization, transfer zeros, invariant zeros, unobservable modes,
uncontrollable modes, and zero dynamics are distinct typed objects. Transfer
and invariant zeros coincide off the state spectrum for a minimal SISO
realization, but scalar transfer data do not reconstruct hidden states.

An exact rational family is controllable and observable at every cutoff while
both minimality determinants scale as \(1/N\) and the weak-mode energy scales
as \(2/N^2\). Its transfers converge pointwise, but the limit acquires a hidden
mode. Separate strictly proper families show a pointwise nonzero value becoming
zero and a persistent zero disappearing when a pole enters the limiting point.

Grothendieck's terminal amplitude supplies the missing cutoff state coordinate:
its shear obeys exact composition. That repairs algebraic domain nesting, not
uniform graph-norm, reachability, or observability control.
