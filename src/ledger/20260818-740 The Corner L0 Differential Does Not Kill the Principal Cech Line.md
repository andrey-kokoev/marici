# 740 — The Corner L0 Differential Does Not Kill the Principal Čech Line

## Question after Entry 739

Entry 739 correctly observes that the packet of Entry 736 did not type the
internal differential adjacent to the principal corner columns.  The first
such differential is nevertheless recoverable from the frozen corner
residues, without assigning a new model.

For a block residue

\[
C=\begin{pmatrix}C_K&0\\ C_E&C_B\end{pmatrix},
\]

the internal extension operator at grade zero is

\[
L_0(C)(X)=XC_K-C_BX.
\]

## Exact corner structure

At all four simple geometric crossings and both weighted endpoints, the
double residue has only its lower-left extension block:

\[
C_K=0,\qquad C_B=0,\qquad C_E\ne0.
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{L_0(C)=0.}
\]

The principal columns exported in Entry 736 are precisely the vectorizations
of the nonzero blocks \(C_E\).  They are therefore internal cycles, and the
zero image of \(L_0(C)\) shows that they are not internal boundaries at this
grade.  This statement is stable under both quadratic Galois involutions and
under the unnormalized \(\mu_2\)-trace.

## Minimal graded consequence

On these three principal lines the horizontal matrix remains

\[
\delta_{\rm pr}=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1&0\\
0&0&1\\
0&-1&1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

It has rank two, and

\[
\lambda=(1,-1,1),\qquad \lambda\delta_{\rm pr}=0.
\]

The grade-zero edge indicial complex is the two-term operator \(L_0(C)\).
Entry 741's relative-cycle criterion therefore specializes to

\[
\frac{E^0}{\delta(\ker\partial_V^0)+\operatorname{im}L_0}.
\]

Here \(\operatorname{im}L_0=0\).  Moreover a representative
\(l=(1,-1,1)\) satisfies

\[
\operatorname{rank}[\delta_{\rm pr}\;l]=3
=\operatorname{rank}\delta_{\rm pr}+1.
\]

Since \(\delta(\ker\partial_V^0)\subseteq\operatorname{im}\delta_{\rm pr}\),
the unknown vertex kernel cannot turn this nonzero horizontal quotient into
a boundary.  Thus the exact grade-zero indicial totalization neither kills
nor hits the horizontal line:

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb Q\langle x_{12}-x_{13}+x_{23}\rangle
\text{ survives the corner }L_0\text{ test.}
}
\]

## Deliberate limit

This proves survival in the exact grade-zero corner indicial totalization,
not in the complete coefficient bicomplex or physical relative-chain object.
Entry 739's demand for the other vertex and edge degrees and all restriction
matrices remains valid.  Mixing between indicial grades, global
Gauss--Manin transport, or the physical integration-chain map could still
remove the class.  The result does not create a physical class.
