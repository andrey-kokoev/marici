# 2878 — Exclusion Plus Valuation Degree Renormalizes to the Square Current

For each prime, primitive exclusion plus valuation number is locally gapped:

\[
P_{p\nmid n}+N_p\geq I.
\]

After subtracting the universal cutoff line, their logarithmically weighted
sum is exactly

\[
\sum_p(\log p)(N_p-P_{p\mid n})
=\sum_{p,k\geq2}(\log p)P_{p^k\mid n}.
\]

Its eigenvalue is \(\log(n/\operatorname{rad}(n))\). Thus the square current
is the typed remainder of primitive-boundary/degree renormalization, not an
independent correction. Its squarefree kernel prevents an RH conclusion.
