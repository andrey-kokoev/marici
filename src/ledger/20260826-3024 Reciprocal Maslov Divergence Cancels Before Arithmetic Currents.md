# 3024 — Reciprocal Maslov Divergence Cancels Before Arithmetic Currents

Reciprocal Fourier–Tate sectors carry signed normalized areas (t) and (-t). Their floor grades satisfy

\[
\lfloor t\rfloor+\lfloor-t\rfloor=-1
\]

away from integer crossings, and sum to zero at an exact crossing where the centered minor vanishes.

The centered convention (\mu(t)=\lfloor t\rfloor+1/2) is reciprocally odd: (\mu(-t)=-\mu(t)). Thus the unbounded parts of the two sector grades cancel automatically. The half-unit is the forced endpoint convention for reciprocal crossing counts.

Primitive and prime-square currents are therefore not needed to renormalize the extensive Maslov grade. Identifying them as Maslov counterterms without a separate source incidence would be a typing error.
