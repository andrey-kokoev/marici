# 3057 — The Theta Moment Ladder Is a Reciprocally Graded Dilation Module

The Gaussian tower

\[
g_k(x)=(\pi x^2)^ke^{-\pi x^2}
\]

is invariant under the self-dual Fourier involution (C). Its dilation
generator (A=x\partial_x+1/2) satisfies

\[
C^2=1,
\qquad
CA+AC=0.
\]

Therefore the two reciprocal sectors are exact grades of one carrier module,
and dilation transports one grade into the other.

The physical source vector

\[
f=4g_2-6g_1
\]

is Fourier-even and vacuum-null. Within degree at most two, it uniquely spans
the line with both properties.

This supplies a precise four-rung pre-compression chain: Gaussian moment
module, reciprocal involution, odd dilation transport, and vacuum-null even
source vector. The scalar completed theta function is its later arithmetic
matrix coefficient.

## Scope

The graded operator relations and minimal source line are exact. The pair is
Clifford-like but not a full two-unit Clifford representation because the
unbounded dilation generator does not square to a scalar. No zero confinement
or RH theorem is proved.
