# 3094 — Reflection-Even Completion Makes the Fourier Graph Lagrangian

Metaplectic phase rigidity alone does not produce a boundary condition. The
full Fourier transform satisfies \(F^2=R\) and is not self-adjoint, so its raw
graph is not isotropic for the standard Green form.

On the reflection-even theta sector,

\[
R=1,
\qquad
F^2=1,
\qquad
F^*=F.
\]

The restricted Fourier graph is therefore maximal isotropic and defines a
Lagrangian boundary relation. The odd sector retains the quarter-turn
obstruction \(F^2=-1\).

This gives even modular completion a new operator meaning: it converts
Fourier transport from a quarter-turn into the self-adjoint involution needed
by the boundary Green form.

## Scope

The finite theorem is exact. The actual adelic doubled Green form, its typed
boundary corrections, closedness, and determinant readout remain to be
established.
