# 2810 — The Remaining Boundary Block Has a Sharp Decay-Elasticity Threshold

For

\[
B_L(D)=D W(D)-(D+L)W(D+L),
\]

positivity is equivalent to

\[
\int_D^{D+L}-\partial_u\log W(u)\,du
>
\log\left(1+\frac LD\right).
\]

Infinitesimally, the sharp threshold is

\[
\varepsilon(D)=-D\partial_D\log W(D)>1.
\]

A uniform margin \(\varepsilon\ge1+\eta\) yields an explicit positive block
modulus and is the completion-ready certificate. Positivity and ordinary decay
of \(W\) alone are insufficient.
