# The Clark Cross-History Port Is an Autocorrelation Derivative

The source-native Clark histories

\[
h_a(q)=(1-aq)f(q),
\qquad
h_{-a}(q)=(1+aq)f(q)
\]

form an authorized exchange pair. Their genuine exchange-odd transform is
nevertheless not a new relational channel:

\[
L_a(z)
=\iint h_a(q)h_{-a}(v)\sinh\!\bigl(z(v-q)\bigr)\,dq\,dv
=aC_f'(z),
\]

where

\[
C_f(z)=\iint f(q)f(v)\cosh\!\bigl(z(v-q)\bigr)\,dq\,dv.
\]

The symmetric product terms vanish against the antisymmetric kernel, leaving
only \(a(v-q)\), exactly the derivative of the even autocorrelation transform.

Thus the Clark involution generates a tangent inside the existing
autocorrelation tower rather than an independent comparison wall. A new port
must arise from two distinct constructor paths, plausibly the primal--dual
pair before scalar modular identification.
