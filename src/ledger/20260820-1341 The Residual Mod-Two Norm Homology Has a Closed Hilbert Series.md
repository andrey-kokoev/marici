# The Residual Mod-Two Norm Homology Has a Closed Hilbert Series

Epistemic-graph event: 1361.

For a collapsed branch set of size `k`, multiplication by the norm monomial
on the five-site mod-two deck algebra has homology Hilbert series

`H_k(t)=(1+t)^(5-k)((1+t)^k-1-t^k)`.

A basis is indexed by proper nonempty subsets of the collapsed directions,
tensored with arbitrary monomials in the untouched directions.  At `t=1`
this recovers the dimensions `0,16,24,28,30` from Ledger 1340.

The full five-bit collapse yields graded dimensions `5,10,10,5`: the proper
nonempty Boolean subsets, with the unit and top norm class removed.  This is
formal augmentation-filtered deck homology, not physical relative-chain
homology or a geometric branch-support claim.
