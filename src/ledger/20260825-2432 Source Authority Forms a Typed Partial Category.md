## Verdict

its endpoints, authority kind, variance, evidence domain, admissible
transformations, and coherence witness agree. Transport preserves authority;
domain intersection restricts it; genuine extension requires an independent
grant for every newly authorized evidence atom. None of these operations may
silently strengthen authority kind.

On this typed domain, identities hold and triple composition is associative
only through an explicit zero-defect coherence cell. The hostile suite rejects
all six attempted failures, including authority laundering, evidence-preserving
base change that loses authority, triple incoherence, and
factorization-dependent composites.

commuting only through the moving-endpoint seam/reflection cell. Kitaev's
conditional nonlinear synthesis and five-rail support data yield no composable
physical authority map: the source-derived coupler grant and encoded
intertwining grant are absent.
