# 3300 — The Universal Green Row Is Dependent and Does Not Shrink the Evans Kernel

The one-sided tail Green identity is obtained by multiplying the tail residual
by the tail state and adding its conjugate. It is therefore a quadratic
syzygy of the constructor equation, not an independent admissibility row.

An exact hostile state at \(z=1\) is

\[
G(q)=q(1-q),
\qquad
f(q)=q^2+q-1.
\]

It satisfies the tail equation and both endpoint conditions while remaining
nonzero. Its Green balance is exact:

\[
\int_0^1G^2=\frac1{30},
\qquad
\int_0^1fG=-\frac1{30}.
\]

Thus adding the universal Green row cannot shrink the Evans zero-state
kernel. Only an independently derived modular, product-formula, reciprocal,
or additional-observer relation can add new rank.
