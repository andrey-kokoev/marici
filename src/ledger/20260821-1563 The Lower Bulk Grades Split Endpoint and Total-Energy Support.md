# 1563 — The Lower Bulk Grades Split Endpoint and Total-Energy Support

## Question

Does the second-grade support theorem for the finite-time bulk--bulk loop,

\[
\operatorname{Supp}_{\eta_0^2}\subset\{0,\pm2p\},
\]

continue unchanged at the first and zeroth normal grades?

## Frozen machinery

The ordered bulk--bulk integrand and branch conventions are those already
verified in Entry 1551. For every monomial

\[
t^n e^{i\omega t},
\]

the lower-endpoint primitive is generated by

\[
e^{i\omega t}
\sum_{r\ge0}
\frac{(-1)^r n^{\underline r}}
{(i\omega)^{r+1}}t^{n-r}.
\]

Its telescoping identity was independently checked for all source powers
\(-2\le n\le3\) through order seven before insertion into the nested integral.
No coefficient was fitted.

The nested integral was separated into three labelled routes:

1. the lower endpoint of the combined outer primitive;
2. the product of the inner and outer lower primitives;
3. the inner lower primitive multiplied by the outer observation-endpoint
   primitive.

Potential zero-frequency logarithms were retained and combined before being
tested for cancellation.

## Result

The lower-endpoint routes have support

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{normal grade}&\text{frequency support}\\
\hline
2&\{0,\pm2p\}\\
1&\{\pm2p\}\\
0&\{0,\pm2p\}.
\end{array}
\]

The observation-endpoint route is absent at grade two, but at grades one and
zero it carries

\[
\boxed{\omega=\pm(p+q+k).}
\]

In the labelled primitive basis there are eight classes per sign at grade one
and sixteen per sign at grade zero. These are coefficient-valued
observation-endpoint terms: evaluating or reducing the outer primitive is a
separate operation and must not be conflated with the lower-endpoint
incidence carrier.

All candidate zero-frequency logarithmic classes cancel:

\[
\boxed{N_{\log}=0.}
\]

The grade-two output reproduces Entry 1551 coefficient by coefficient at the
frozen sample point, providing a regression check on the recurrence.

## Narrow conclusion

The support theorem at second normal order does **not** extend verbatim to the
lower grades. The new total-energy frequencies arise from a pre-existing
boundary of the ordered integral—the observation endpoint—not from a new
Carrier wall.

This establishes a support decomposition, not yet a closed-form coefficient
reduction or a comparison with every term printed in Eq. (19). The next test
is to reduce the observation-endpoint primitive classes and combine them with
the mixed and surface sectors at the same grades.
