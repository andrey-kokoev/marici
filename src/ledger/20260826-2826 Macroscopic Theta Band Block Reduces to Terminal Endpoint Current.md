# 2826 — Macroscopic Theta Band Block Reduces to Terminal Endpoint Current

When the band count grows so that \(R=2mL\) stays fixed, aligned oscillatory
integration gives

\[
I_R(a,L)
=
\frac{2L}{\pi}
\left[J_a'(R)-aR W_a(R)\right]+O_R(L^2).
\]

Thus the growing block is controlled by a terminal endpoint current. For
small positive \(a\), this current remains negative throughout a nonzero
interval of macroscopic widths by the strict Hardy germ. A viable band block
must cross a source coherence length or extend to infinity.
