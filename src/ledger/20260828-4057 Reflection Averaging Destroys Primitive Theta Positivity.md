\[

Indeed, \(\phi_1(-u)\) becomes negative after

\[
u=\frac12\log\frac{2\pi}{3}
\]

superexponentially. The negative reflected term therefore dominates.

Consequently, modular completion is not ordinary symmetrization of the
primitive. It enlarges the labelled source so that reciprocal reflection and
positive-chamber positivity coexist. The next invariant must be the positive
incidence law among labels rather than another finite seam correction.
