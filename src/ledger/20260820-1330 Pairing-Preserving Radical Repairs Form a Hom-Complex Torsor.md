# Pairing-Preserving Radical Repairs Form a Hom-Complex Torsor

Epistemic-graph event: 1348.

When Ledger 1328's radical obstruction vanishes and one correction `C_0`
exists, every other pairing-preserving repair is

`C_0+Z`, with `Z` a radical-valued chain map.

Thus literal repairs form a torsor under
`Z^0(Hom(C(G),R))`; modulo radical chain homotopy, repair classes form a
torsor under `H^0(Hom(C(G),R))`.  Uniqueness up to homotopy holds exactly
when the latter group vanishes.

The one-term control `C(G)=R=Z` has zero differential and zero defect, yet
admits one repair for every integer multiplication.  Hence a degenerate
pairing can leave a genuine canonicity obstruction after existence is
settled.

A physical five-site repair would therefore need both obstruction
vanishing and either radical Hom-rigidity or an independently sourced
normalization selecting one torsor point.
