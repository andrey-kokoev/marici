# The Resonance Line Splits into Three Rees Shifts and One Incidence Shift

For the exact odd resonant coefficient of Entries 517--518, remove the
common Cartier factor `u`.  The remaining weighted monomial is

\[
a^7(b+1).
\]

On the `u`-chart of `Bl_(u,a^2)`, where `a^2=ut`,

\[
a^7(b+1)=a\,u^3t^3(b+1).
\]

After removing the three exceptional powers `u^3`, the normalized generator
is

\[
r_u=a\,t^3(b+1).
\]

On the `a^2`-chart the corresponding normalization uses the odd frame `a`.
On the overlap, the Rees part of the transition is therefore `t^3`.  The
remaining factor `b+1` is not an additional blowup-chart transition: it is
the source incidence line carried by `L_1|_(u=0)`.

Restricting to the Cartier section

\[
t=\frac{b^2-1}{2}
\]

gives the comparison coefficient

\[
t^3(b+1)=\frac18(b-1)^3(b+1)^4,
\]

and hence

\[
\operatorname{div}\bigl(t^3(b+1)\bigr)
=3[b=1]+4[b=-1].
\]

Thus the relative exceptional line is canonically typed as three
tautological weighted-Rees shifts tensor one incidence shift.  The Čech
transition and endpoint divisor are compatible but distinct data: `t^3`
glues the weighted charts, while `b+1` supplies the asymmetric endpoint
order.

This verifies the line-bundle coherence required by Entries 455--456.  It
does not yet construct the boundary differential or its nullhomotopy; that
is the remaining datum needed to place the odd resonance in the relative
homotopy fiber.
