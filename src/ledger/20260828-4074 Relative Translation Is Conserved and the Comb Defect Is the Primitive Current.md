while preserving their relative pairing exactly.

\[

m^{-1/2}B_+T_{\log m}f

\sum_{m\nmid k}
k^{-1/2}f(\log k).
\]

Thus the failure of continuous dilation covariance is precisely the current
carried by labels not divisible by \(m\). At a prime, this is the primitive
\(p\)-free channel.

produces the divisibility defect. This derives the primitive repair current
inside the two-sector rigging.
