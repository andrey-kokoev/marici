# The Boundary Defect Is Invariant Under Chain-Homotopy Changes

Epistemic-graph event: 1344.

For `Omega=D_H S-SD_G`, any ordinary chain-homotopy change

`S'=S+D_H K+K D_G`

leaves `Omega` unchanged.  The square-zero boundary identities cancel every
correction term.  Target cocycle pairings on source cycles are unchanged as
well.

Therefore a nonzero physical boundary defect cannot be repaired by choosing
a different representative in the same chain-homotopy class.  A genuine
repair requires an additional graded correction `C` satisfying
`D_H C-CD_G=-Omega`; under perfect-pairing uniqueness, such a correction
normally changes the forced adjunction and demands new physical data.

Future five-site endpoint or overlap homotopies can compare presentations,
but they cannot erase a nonzero commutator of the forced Betti map.
