Expanding the fixed primitive carrier at a remote moving wall gives exact
rational coefficients for each residual moment ratio.  Under

\[
-h_4+3h_6-3h_8+h_{10},
\]

the first two response layers cancel.  The next combined ratio coefficient
is $32$, and the leading $2c$ scale therefore yields

\[
\Omega'(a)=\frac{64}{c^2}+O(c^{-3}).
\]

Thus the primitive flux is structurally increasing at sufficiently remote
walls.  Its first nonzero response occurs precisely at order four, matching
the mixed-fourth-cumulant reduction and explaining why covariance, hazard,
and three-copy arguments could not determine the sign.
