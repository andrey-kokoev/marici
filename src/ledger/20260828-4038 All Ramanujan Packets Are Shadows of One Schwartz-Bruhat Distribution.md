# All Ramanujan Packets Are Shadows of One Schwartz--Bruhat Distribution

Multiplicative Haar measure on
\(\widehat{\mathbb Z}^\times\) defines a canonical distribution
\(T_\times\in\mathcal S'(\mathbb A_f)\), despite having no additive-Haar
density.

Finite sieve measures converge to it eventually on every Schwartz--Bruhat
test. Its conductor-\(Q\) Fourier coefficient is

\[
\langle T_\times,\chi_{k/Q}\rangle
=
\frac{c_Q(k)}{\varphi(Q)}.
\]

Thus every Ramanujan packet is a finite shadow of one completed distribution.
The angular conductor completion is solved in the rigged category; the weak
escape arose only from demanding an additive-\(L^2\) vector. The radial
valuation/Fock tower remains a separate retained component.

The remaining RH theorem is solely the coupling of this finite-adelic
distributional correspondence to the half-normalized archimedean tempered
boundary.
