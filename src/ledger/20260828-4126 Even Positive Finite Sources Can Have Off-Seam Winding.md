# Even Positive Finite Sources Can Have Off-Seam Winding

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

The positive reciprocal two-mode source

\[
F_a(z)=\cosh z+a\cosh2z,
\qquad 0<a<1,
\]

has exact off-seam zeros because its quadratic in \(w=\cosh z\) has one
root below \(-1\). Hence positivity, reciprocal symmetry, finite
symplectic Evans structure, and positive local intersection index do not
imply zero winding at finite cutoff.

Any open-half-plane zero-winding theorem must be created by the complete
infinite theta source. It cannot be proved cutoff by cutoff under the current
invariants.
