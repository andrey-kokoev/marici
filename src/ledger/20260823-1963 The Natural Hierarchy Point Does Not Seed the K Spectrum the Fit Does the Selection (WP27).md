# 1963 - The Natural Hierarchy Point Does Not Seed the K Spectrum; the Fit Does the Selection

- Date: 2026-08-23
- Author: marici.Figueiredo
- Status: established on all 755 viable textures / 1210 minima
- Supersedes: nothing. Continues 1962 (WP26 level anatomy)

## 1. Question

WP26 reduced the five cluster levels to five realized values of the
phase-free chart combination K = M*W/gap. Since K is phase-free, it
can be evaluated anywhere - in particular at the hierarchy-natural
point nat(i,j) = sqrt(y_i y_j) that seeds the fits. If the natural
point already carried five K peaks, the levels would be an artifact
of the seed parameterization (a chart-of-chart effect). If not, the
fit's convergence to the physical flavor point does the selection.

## 2. Result: no natural seeding

Natural-point ln K over all 755 viable textures: mean -47.2,
std 10.97, range [-74.6, -20.8] - broad and structureless. Grouped
by each texture's dominant fitted cluster, the natural means
(-41.3, -45.2, -50.6, -52.2, -42.9 for clusters 0-4) neither match
the fitted ln K values nor separate: natural spreads (std 4.3-13.3)
are 10^3 times the fitted spreads. The natural parameterization does
not prefigure the spectrum.

## 3. Fifteen textures are singular at the natural point

Exactly 15 textures have K undefined at nat - all of them two-term
transpose-pair W forms (W = -d01^2 + d10^2 and mask images). The
natural point is transpose-symmetric, nat(i,j) = nat(j,i), so a
transpose-pair difference vanishes identically there. For these
textures the fit is FORCED off the symmetric point: their entire
|W| is generated by the fit's transpose-asymmetry breaking. (The
other 116 two-term W forms - non-transpose pairs such as
-d02^2 + d10^2 - are nonzero at nat.)

## 4. The fitted K spectrum is tight everywhere

Per (cluster, decomposed sector), fitted ln K:

| cluster,sector | n   | mean lnK | std     |
|----------------|-----|----------|---------|
| 0,d | 97  | -20.861 | 3.3e-3 |
| 0,u | 113 | -42.050 | 2.9e-2 |
| 1,u | 42  | -42.628 | 1.0e-2 |
| 2,u | 330 | -42.694 | 1.7e-3 |
| 3,d | 83  | -21.692 | 3.5e-3 |
| 3,u | 347 | -42.936 | 4.0e-3 |
| 4,d | 75  | -21.767 | 1.1e-5 |
| 4,u | 123 | -43.008 | 6.0e-4 |

Seven (cluster, sector) values, tied into the five L levels by the
physical ratio Du/Dd: ln K_d - ln K_u = ln(Du/Dd) = 21.24 holds at
0.02-0.05 for clusters 0, 3, 4 (the mixed ones), as it must since
L = K/D_other and Du Dd is physical (1962). The d-decomposed members
occur only in the conspiracy clusters 0, 3, 4 - never in the rigid
clusters 1, 2, consistent with 1959's sector-purity census.

## 5. Consequence

The selection of the five K values is done by the fit, not by the
seed: it is a property of the viable solution set of
(texture + 17 physical observables), not of the hierarchy-natural
starting point. This removes the chart-of-chart artifact reading of
the level spectrum and properly poses the remaining question: what
admits exactly these five K values among all viable (class,
magnitude-assignment) pairs over one physical flavor point. The 15
transpose-pair textures are the extreme witnesses: for them the
selected K has no natural-point precursor whatsoever.
