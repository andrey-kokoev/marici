# The Frontier Current Is Fourier-Fixed and Reciprocally Positive

The moving-front profile

\[
F(\rho)=\rho^2(2\pi\rho^2-3)e^{-\pi\rho^2}
\]

is both an exact derivative and a Fourier fixed point.

Poisson summation therefore converts its signed fine-mesh lattice sum into a
reciprocal sum. On the positive theta chamber, every reciprocal sample lies
beyond the profile's unique positive zero

\[
\sqrt{\frac3{2\pi}}<1,
\]

so every reciprocal term is positive.

One rigid mechanism now explains both completed evenness and positivity:
exact boundary current, Fourier self-duality, lattice discrepancy, reciprocal
transport, and exclusion of the negative lobe.

This closes the source-positivity layer but not RH orientation of its
oscillatory transform.
