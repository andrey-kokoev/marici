# The Reciprocal Tail Domain Is a Trace Pullback but Dual Times Dual Has No Canonical Green Pairing

For fixed spectral parameter, the maximal graph domain of the source tail
differential on each half-line is `H1`. Endpoint evaluation is continuous,
and reciprocal sewing is the pullback of the two endpoint traces. After
orientation reversal this pullback is canonically `H1(R)`.

Thus the archimedean graph-domain problem closes without a fitted boundary
condition. The genuine obstruction appears after arithmetic completion:
completed theta states and primitive boundary currents can both be
dual-valued, but there is no canonical pairing of two elements of the dual.
The divergent formal self-pairing of the continuous constant row is the
smallest hostile witness.

The next source object must be an intermediate reciprocal dual pair carrying
the zero-state and Green form, not a conveniently chosen Hilbert pivot.
