# 2113 — The Correlator Deletion Cube Has No Inherited Coefficient Differential

## Hard-to-vary claim

The wavefunction-to-correlator formula of arXiv:2401.05207 canonically supplies a Boolean family of edge-deleted integrands, but it does not canonically supply morphisms between their coefficient systems.

For the three-edge triangle, the eight vertices are source-derived. The twelve oriented cube edges are only combinatorial incidences unless a separate localization, residue, Gysin, or Gauss--Manin construction types them.

## Source evidence

Equations (2.13)--(2.14) define the correlator as a sum over graphs obtained by erasing subsets of edges. Equations (2.27)--(2.30) define each dashed-edge term by:

\[
e\text{ erased},\qquad
\frac1{y_e}\text{ inserted},\qquad
x_{s(e)},x_{t(e)}\mapsto x_{s(e)}+y_e,x_{t(e)}+y_e.
\]

Equations (4.66)--(4.71) realize the same answer as a weighted-polytope subdivision. Neither construction defines a coefficient morphism between adjacent deletion subsets.
