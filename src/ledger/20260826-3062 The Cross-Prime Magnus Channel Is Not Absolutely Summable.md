# 3062 — The Cross-Prime Magnus Channel Is Not Absolutely Summable

The doubled prime exchanges produce the pair channel

\[
\frac{2i}{\sqrt{pq}}
\sin\left(t\log\frac qp\right)\sigma_3.
\]

For every fixed \(t\ne0\), choose proportional prime intervals where the sine
is bounded away from zero. The prime number theorem supplies order
\(X/\log X\) primes in each interval, while each pair contributes order
\(1/X\). The absolute contribution is therefore bounded below by

\[
C_t\frac{X}{(\log X)^2},
\]

which diverges.

Thus the nonabelian ordered product has a new pair-labelled completion anomaly.
Local primitive and prime-square counterterms are insufficient by themselves.
A surviving construction needs exact cross-prime cancellation, a typed pair
current, or a different coherent global composition.

## Scope

Absolute summability fails. The theorem does not exclude every conditional or
source-renormalized ordering, and it proves no RH statement.
