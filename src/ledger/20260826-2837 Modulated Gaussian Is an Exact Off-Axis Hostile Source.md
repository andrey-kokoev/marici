# 2837 — Modulated Gaussian Is an Exact Off-Axis Hostile Source

For sufficiently small \(\varepsilon k^2\),

\[
g_{\varepsilon,k}(x)=e^{-x^2}[1+\varepsilon\cos(kx)]
\]

is positive, even, Schwartz, and uniformly strictly log-concave. Its transform
is exactly

\[
\sqrt\pi e^{-z^2/4}
\left[1+\varepsilon e^{-k^2/4}\cosh(kz/2)\right],
\]

with explicit zeros having nonzero real parts. It also shares the complete
algebraic boundary cancellation and negative finite terminal currents of the
theta analysis.

Therefore all local and boundaryless properties proved so far are jointly
insufficient for zero confinement. A labelled modular/arithmetic
correspondence must reject the modulation before scalar compression.
