For a finite prime set \(X\), the completed Euler truncation

\[
C_X(s)
=
\frac12s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)
\prod_{p\in X}(1-p^{-s})^{-1}
\]

has the exact reciprocal transition

\[
u_X(z)
=
\frac{C_X(1/2+z)}{C_X(1/2-z)}.
\]

It is holomorphic and nonzero throughout the open critical strip and unitary
on \(z=it\).  Its logarithm is exactly the archimedean anomaly plus the odd
primitive, square, and connected determinant currents.

Thus finite completion and determinant reconstruction agree through an
explicit divisor-preserving unit.  The obstruction occurs only in the global
limit: the cutoff units do not converge as ordinary scalar Euler products in
the critical strip.  Theta modular sewing must lift the complete graded packet
to a canonical transition of the completed determinant line.
