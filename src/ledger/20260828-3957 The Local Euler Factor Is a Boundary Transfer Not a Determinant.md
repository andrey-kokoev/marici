# The Local Euler Factor Is a Boundary Transfer Not a Determinant

This sharpens Entry 3226: its determinant-line statement belongs to the
derived value--flux readout, not to the internal valuation differential.

On the cutoff valuation space,

\[
d_{p,s,N}=I-p^{-s}S_{p,N}
\]

is triangular and has determinant one. Yet its endpoint-to-augmentation
transfer is

\[
\varepsilon_Nd_{p,s,N}^{-1}e_0
=\sum_{k=0}^{N}p^{-ks}.
\]

The infinite limit is the local Euler factor. Thus zeta is source-natively a
boundary transfer function of an exact system, not the determinant of its
valuation differential.

This falsifies the naive zero-to-cohomology bridge at the smallest local
cutoff. A global zero should first be understood as loss of input--output
transmission. Any larger mapping-cone interpretation must derive its boundary
ports independently rather than encoding the scalar readout backward.
