# 2850 — Reciprocal Sewing Makes the Gaussian Seam Port an Observable, Not a Barrier

For symmetric and antisymmetric half-line amplitudes (X=H_++H_-) and
(Y=H_+-H_-), reciprocal sewing of the Gaussian number current gives

\[
N_++N_-=\frac12X+izY+2A(0),
\]

\[
N_+-N_-=\frac12Y+izX.
\]

The seam doubles symmetrically and cancels antisymmetrically. At (X=0), both
number channels remain well-defined and generally nonzero. Thus the seam port
retains information lost by the scalar readout but does not exclude its zero.
The finite additive oscillator-ladder route is closed; any further force must
couple multiplicative arithmetic incidence to the relative route phase.
