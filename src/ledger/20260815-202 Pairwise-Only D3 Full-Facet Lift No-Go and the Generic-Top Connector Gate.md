## Result

class is already supplied, differences of its strict representatives have
unique integral short-facet fillers. The present computation asks the earlier
existence question required by the proposed pair-intersection correspondence:
can the three `P^2` pair-boundaries themselves be lifted equivariantly to the
actual `K_6` carrier?

nine-facet matrix. Exact reconstruction of the labelled `K_6` face poset gives
`rank(d_2^short)=6` and `SNF(d_2^short)=(1,1,1,1,1,1)`.

For each of the three columns of the `P^2` pair incidence matrix `R-I`,
the corresponding complementary-road difference raises the rank of the
augmented short matrix from six to seven. Therefore none of those differences
lies in the short-facet image.

The full matrix has rank eight, a unit maximal minor, and a one-dimensional
kernel generated by the primitive boundary of the associahedral top cell.
Each road difference has an integral full-facet lift. Choose one lift and
rotate it twice. This is the required `C_3`-orbit of lifts, modulo adding the
same integer multiple `n` of the top boundary to all three columns.

For the normalized orbit, the cyclic sum is
`F_14,03 + F_03,25 + F_25,14 = -2 * boundary(K_6^top)`.

Every equivariant change of lift changes the coefficient by `3n`. Closing
the cyclic top equation would therefore require `-2+3n=0`,

which has no integral solution. Equivalently, the obstruction is `1 mod 3`.
The selected orbit matrix has Smith form `(1,1,2)`; throughout the equivariant
family its Smith form is `(1,1,|-2+3n|)`.

It can drop to rank two exactly when the cyclic defect vanishes, which never
occurs for integral `n`.

This does not contradict entry201: entry201 controls the uniqueness of
comparison chains after a compatible relative road class exists. The present
no-go proves that the three pairwise-only classes cannot be assembled into
the required integral `D_3`-equivariant cyclic lift without an additional
