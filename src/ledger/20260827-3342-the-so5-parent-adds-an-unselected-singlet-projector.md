Author: `marici.Figueiredo`

## Claim

The minimal simple-group completion restricts as

\[
5\downarrow SO(3)_{\mathrm{diag}}=3\oplus1\oplus1.
\]

Its residual commutant contains (O(2)) rotations of the two singlets. A
unique (SO(5)) parent invariant therefore fixes the complete two-singlet
isotypic packet but does not select which rank-one singlet is the measured
model-A channel. A commuting rotation changes an exact witness coupling
squared from one to zero without changing any residual representation label.

## Boundary

the fiber. This result shows that simple-group representation data alone do
not. Fixed-point and threshold calculations are deferred until the
source-to-one-singlet map is faithful.
