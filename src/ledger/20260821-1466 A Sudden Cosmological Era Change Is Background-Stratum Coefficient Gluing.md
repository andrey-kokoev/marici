## Frozen transition

The source joins a kinetic-energy-dominated era to inflation at a fixed
transition hypersurface \(\Sigma\). In shifted conformal time the background is
piecewise

\[
a_-(\eta)=\sqrt{1+2H\eta},
\qquad \eta\le0,
\]

\[
a_+(\eta)=\frac{1}{1-H\eta},
\qquad \eta\ge0.
\]

The matching surface is \(\eta=0\). The source retains the gauge-invariant
curvature perturbation \(\mathcal R\), rather than the discontinuous
Mukhanov variable \(v=z\mathcal R\).

The Israel/Deruelle--Mukhanov conditions include

\[
\boxed{[\mathcal R]_{\Sigma}=0}
\]

together with the source's conjugate metric/matter matching condition. These
conditions fix the two post-transition mode coefficients from the incoming
mode.

## Coefficient interpretation

For each spatial momentum \(k\), the quadratic wavefunction is a Gaussian

\[
\Psi_k
=
N_k\exp\!\left[-\frac12
\mathcal R_kF_k\mathcal R_{-k}\right].
\]

Evolution in each era is generated by the quadratic Hamiltonian and its
metaplectic/unitary operator. The transition supplies a canonical linear
relation

\[
\mathsf M_\Sigma(k):
(\mathcal R_-,\Pi_-)
\longmapsto
(\mathcal R_+,\Pi_+),
\]

equivalently the matching of the Gaussian coefficient \(F_k\). It is fixed by
the source junction conditions, not by choosing a basis after the transition.

## Carrier classification

The hypersurface \(\Sigma\) is not a new partial-energy, Cut, soft, or marked
incidence divisor. It belongs to the stratified base of background histories.
Over each open era, the usual energy/Cut carrier is unchanged. Across
\(\Sigma\), the sector coefficient objects are glued by \(\mathsf M_\Sigma\).

The resulting architecture is

\[
\boxed{
(\text{shared occurrence-resolved energy/Cut carrier})
\longrightarrow
(\text{stratified background base}),
}
\]

with sector coefficient descent data on the background strata.

Thus no new energy-carrier generator is established. But a cosmology with
multiple eras requires an additional background-stratification index and a
source-derived gluing morphism. Treating the whole history as one coefficient
object without this descent datum loses physical information.
