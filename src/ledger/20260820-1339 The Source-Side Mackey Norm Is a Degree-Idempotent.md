# The Source-Side Mackey Norm Is a Degree-Idempotent

Epistemic-graph event: 1357.

If `S T=dI`, the source operator `N=TS` satisfies

`N^2=dN`.

Only after inverting `d` does `(1/d)N` become the projector onto the trace
image along the pushforward kernel.  Integrally the image may have finite
index in the trace submodule.

At primes dividing `d`, the norm becomes square-zero.  For the one-bit deck
quotient, `S=[1 1]` and `T=[1;1]`, so modulo two the nonzero matrix
`[[1,1],[1,1]]` squares to zero and has image equal to its invariant kernel
line.

Thus a five-site integral norm identity does not imply a chain splitting;
after mod-two reduction it produces nilpotent rather than projective
structure.  Physical use remains conditional on source-derived relative
maps.
