# 2820 — Multiple Exchanging Loops Need a Common Center

Odd holonomies \(r_i(x)=-x+\ell_i\) individually fix \(\ell_i/2\), but

\[
r_jr_i(x)=x+\ell_j-\ell_i.
\]

Distinct centers therefore generate a nonzero even translation whose iterates
diverge. A bounded affine holonomy orbit exists exactly when every even
translation vanishes and all odd centers agree. Absolute centers depend on
frame origin; their differences are invariant.
