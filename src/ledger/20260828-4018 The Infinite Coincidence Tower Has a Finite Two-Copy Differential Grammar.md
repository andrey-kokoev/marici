# The Infinite Coincidence Tower Has a Finite Two-Copy Differential Grammar

The autocorrelation jets satisfy

\[
A^{(r)}(0)=\langle f,\partial_q^r f\rangle_w.
\]

Weighted integration by parts gives the exact reflected action

\[
\langle g,\partial_qh\rangle_w
=\langle(1-\partial_q)g,h\rangle_w-g(0)h(0).
\]

Hence the infinite coincidence tower is generated by two scale actions, their
affine adjoint reflection, and one endpoint trace. For theta, the existing
scale-derivative theorem adds only one primitive heat generator to the
completed moment tower. The carrier is infinite, but its source grammar is
finite.

This corrects the interpretation of ledger 4017: higher Sobolev coefficients
are not independent added ports. They are matrix coefficients of a finitely
generated two-copy heat module. The remaining problem is orientation of this
module under reciprocal sewing before scalar compression.
