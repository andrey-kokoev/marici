# The Primitive Current Is Not a Fourier-Stable Subchannel

In the exact cyclic model, a primitive-like odd-support distribution maps
under Fourier transform to a complementary even-frequency control
distribution. It is not proportional to its source channel and returns only
after the second Fourier turn.

Thus the analytic regularity filtration should not be assumed Fourier-block
diagonal. The full boundary-bearing packet may carry a unitary or duality
correspondence while primitive, square, control, and tail components mix.

The correct infinite target is the Fourier orbit closure of the complete typed
packet, with explicit cross-level transport and recovery after the reciprocal
cycle. Blockwise equality of the primitive current would falsely reject the
native rotation.
