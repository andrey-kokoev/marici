# Entry 502 — The Even Incidence Boundary Is Cyclic of Cartier Length Two

Entry 501 produces the two invariant incidence principal parts

\[
-{1\over6(b+1)},
\qquad
-{a^2\over6(b+1)}.
\]

Let

\[
t=b+1,
\qquad
A_+=\mathbb Q[a^2]/(a^4)
       \cong\mathbb Q[x]/(x^2),
\quad x=a^2.
\]

In the incidence local-cohomology module

\[
H^1_{(t)}(A_+[t])=A_+[t,t^{-1}]/A_+[t],
\]

write \(\eta=[1/t]\).  Then

\[
[a^2/t]=x\eta,
\qquad
x^2\eta=0.
\]

Consequently the two residues form

\[
\boxed{
A_+\eta\cong A_+,
}
\]

a cyclic module of Cartier length two.  It has two rational coefficient
layers but only one module generator.

## Consequence

The two even conormal generators do not produce two independent incidence
obstructions.  Their boundary is one cyclic object, generated by the unit
layer; the \(a^2\) layer is forced by Cartier multiplication.  This is the
first correctly typed mechanism compatible with Entry 473's cutoff-stable
generator defect one.

However, defect one in Entry 473 is a numerical flatness invariant, not yet
a theorem about minimal module generators.  The cyclic local-cohomology
calculation establishes the candidate coefficient object but does not prove
that the filtered orbit mapping cone contains it with the required
differential.
