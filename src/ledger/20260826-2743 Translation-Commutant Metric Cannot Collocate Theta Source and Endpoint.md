# 2743 — Translation-Commutant Metric Cannot Collocate Theta Source and Endpoint

and collocates the cross-transfer ports only if

\[
GA=AG,
\qquad
G b_0=b_f.
\]

multiplier \(g\). Since the endpoint port has constant Fourier transform,
collocation forces

\[
g=\widehat f.
\]

\(\widehat f(\xi)\to0\). Therefore \(g\) cannot be bounded below, and no
equivalent positive translation-covariant Hilbert metric can identify the
theta source with endpoint evaluation.

information. A viable construction must enlarge or change the operator
system, or prove strict stability in a genuinely rigged relative topology.
Effective resistance remains a scalar-unit certificate downstream of a
source-derived graph energy; it does not repair this Hilbert metric defect.
