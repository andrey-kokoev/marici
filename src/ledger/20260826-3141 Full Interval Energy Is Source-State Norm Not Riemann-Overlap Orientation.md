# 3141 — Full Interval Energy Is Source-State Norm, Not Riemann-Overlap Orientation

For the transported state \(c_s=(n^{-s})\), the derived interval energy is

\[
\mathcal E(c_s)
=2\sum_n(\log n)n^{-2\sigma}
=-\partial_\sigma\zeta(2\sigma).
\]

It is positive but independent of spectral height. Equal-magnitude packets
can have identical energy and observer overlaps zero and nonzero. Therefore
the positivity theorem controls the diagonal source-state norm, not the mixed
Riemann overlap.
