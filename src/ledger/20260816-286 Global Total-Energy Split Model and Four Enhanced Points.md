# Global Total-Energy Split Model and Four Enhanced Points

## Result

At the total-energy boundary

\[
E=x+y+z=0,
\]

the compactified Cayley--Menger surface is globally reducible. Its branch
quartic is the exact square

\[
\overline K_0
=
R_h^2,
\qquad
R_h
=
xy(x+y)s^2-xa^2-yb^2.
\]

Therefore

\[
\overline S_0
=
S_+\cup S_-,
\qquad
S_\pm:\ W=\pm R_h,
\]

and each component is a copy of \(\mathbb P^2\). They meet along the
smooth conic

\[
C:\quad R_h=0.
\]

The infinity boundary consists of one line on each sheet. Those lines meet
at the two points \(C\cap\{s=0\}\), so their dual graph has two
vertices and two edges. Its first homology is rank one, giving the expected
Tate limit of the elliptic boundary.

The first total-energy smoothing coefficient factors canonically:

\[
\boxed{
\left.
\frac{\partial\overline K_E}{\partial E}
\right|_{E=0}
=
-2(x+y)
(a^2-y^2s^2)
(b^2-x^2s^2).
}
\]

Away from its zeros on \(C\), the family has the semistable local form

\[
(W-R_h)(W+R_h)=E\cdot\text{unit}.
\]

The coefficient vanishes on \(C\) at exactly four finite points,

\[
[a:b:s]
=
[\pm y:\pm x:1].
\]

Each is a double zero of the restricted first smoothing coefficient. The
physical marked collision is

\[
[-y:-x:1].
\]

Thus the generic \(E=0\) degeneration is completely Tate/Kummer. All
enhanced total-energy behavior is localized at four points already
generated by the frozen branch conic and occurrence/sign data. No new
carrier stratum has appeared.

## Exact compact central fiber

At \(E=0\), write

\[
z=-x-y.
\]

In the affine chart \(s=1\), entry 282 gives

\[
K_0
=
\left[
xy(x+y)-xa^2-yb^2
\right]^2.
\]

Homogenizing on \([a:b:s]\in\mathbb P^2\) gives

\[
R_h
=
xy(x+y)s^2-xa^2-yb^2.
\]

The weighted-projective double cover

\[
W^2=R_h^2
\]

is the union

\[
W=R_h
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
W=-R_h.
\]

Both sheets project isomorphically to \(\mathbb P^2\). Their double
locus is the conic \(C\). For generic nonsoft \(x,y,x+y\), this conic
is smooth.

## Affine and boundary double loci

The affine surface removes \(s=0\). The conic intersects the infinity
line in the two solutions of

\[
xa^2+yb^2=0.
\]

Hence

\[
C_{\rm aff}
=
C\setminus(C\cap\{s=0\})
\simeq
\mathbb G_m.
\]

This already shows that the central affine gluing data are Tate/Kummer.

On the two sheets, the anticanonical boundary specializes to lines

\[
L_+\subset S_+,
\qquad
L_-\subset S_-.
\]

They meet at the same two points. The normalization-incidence graph is

\[
L_+
\mathrel{\substack{\text{---}\\[-1mm]\text{---}}}
L_-,
\]

with

\[
b_1=2-2+1=1.
\]

This rank-one graph class is the Tate nearby-cycle remnant of the elliptic
boundary. It agrees with the standard nodal Legendre degeneration but is
now visible directly in the compact surface geometry.

## First total-energy smoothing coefficient

Keep

\[
z=E-x-y,
\qquad
c=-E
\]

in the frozen Cayley--Menger polynomial and expand

\[
\overline K_E
=
R_h^2+EJ_h+O(E^2).
\]

Exact collection gives

\[
J_h
=
2(x+y)
\left[
x^2a^2s^2+y^2b^2s^2-a^2b^2-x^2y^2s^4
\right].
\]

It factors as

\[
J_h
=
-2(x+y)
(a^2-y^2s^2)
(b^2-x^2s^2).
\]

This factorization is source-derived. No normalization has been changed to

Set

\[
U=W-R_h,
\qquad
V=W+R_h.
\]

Then

\[
UV
=
EJ_h+O(E^2).
\]

At a point of \(C\) where \(J_h\neq0\), analytic rescaling gives

\[
UV=E.
\]

Therefore the total-energy degeneration is already semistable along the
generic double curve.

## The four enhanced points

Restrict \(J_h\) to \(C\). Its zeros satisfy

\[
a^2=y^2s^2
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
b^2=x^2s^2.
\]

The conic equation forces both conditions. Thus the zero set is

\[
Z
=
\{
[\epsilon y:\delta x:1]
:
\epsilon,\delta\in\{\pm1\}
\}.
\]

Because the restriction of a quartic to a conic has degree eight and
there are four distinct generic points, each zero has multiplicity two.
Equivalently, the two factors in \(J_h\) vanish to first order together
along \(C\).

The source marked-wall intersection at total energy is

\[
a=x+z=-y,
\qquad
b=y+z=-x,
\]

so it selects the occurrence-resolved point

\[
P_{--}=[-y:-x:1].
\]

The other three points are the remaining sign choices of the same frozen
geometry. They are not new cells added after the degeneration was seen.

## Nearby-cycle grade away from the enhanced points

On

\[
C\setminus Z,
\]

the central fiber is a two-component simple normal crossing. Its strata
are:

- two rational surfaces \(S_\pm\);
- the rational conic \(C\);
- after removing infinity, the Tate curve \(\mathbb G_m\);
- the two-point incidence of the boundary lines.

Consequently the generic nearby-cycle weight-graded object is assembled
from Tate/Kummer pieces. No elliptic coefficient survives as a smooth
genus-one object at \(E=0\); its information is carried by the
logarithmic extension and the rank-one dual-graph cycle.

This is the global geometric version of the nearby-cycle statement in
entry 128.

## Relation to the second Rees frontier

The generic semistable locus is controlled at first order by \(J_h\).
At the four points in \(Z\), that first-order coefficient vanishes.
Therefore higher total-energy jets are genuinely required there.

This is exactly the location where the existing warning becomes active:

\[

\not\Rightarrow

\]

The quartic

\[

to the generic double curve. It is to compute the higher local smoothing
at the four forced enhancement points and eliminate the local fiber
