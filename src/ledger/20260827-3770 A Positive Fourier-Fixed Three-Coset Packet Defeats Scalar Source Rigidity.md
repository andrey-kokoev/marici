\[
E=P_++P_-+C_++C_-,
\qquad

\]

\[

unit origin weight while retaining positivity and Fourier self-duality.

distinguished right endpoint per Haar unit cell by extra fractional endpoints
and residue-dependent endpoint weights. Source rigidity must retain this
cell-to-endpoint constructor, not merely aggregate positivity, Fourier
symmetry, and boundary normalization.
