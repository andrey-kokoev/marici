# 3166 — The Seam-Oriented Coth Current Is a Tate Ratio Comparator, Not an Euler Factor

Correction to entry 3164: with \(z=s-1/2\), the local Tate weights are

\[
p^{-1/2}p^{-z},
\qquad
p^{-1/2}p^z.
\]

Thus \(p^{-z}\) is their square-root ratio coordinate, not either Euler
occupation weight. The coth current is a reciprocal comparison-frame
current. Its seam zeros are not local zeta zeros, and its geometric series is
not the ordinary Euler tail.
