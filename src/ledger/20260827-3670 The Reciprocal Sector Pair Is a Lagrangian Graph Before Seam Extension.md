# 3670 — The Reciprocal Sector Pair Is a Lagrangian Graph Before Seam Extension

## Result

Before adding an independent seam state, Fourier-related reciprocal sectors
form the graph of a symplectomorphism in the oppositely signed doubled phase
space. That graph is Lagrangian.

Restricting the ten ambient quadratic `sp4` symbols to the graph has exact
rank three. The second local triple and four mixed quadratics are therefore
not independent bulk controls; they collapse to the original one-mode `sl2`.

## Consequence

The two half-plane sectors are distinct typed presentations of one canonical
state, not two independent oscillator modes. The endpoint `sp4` comparison
can become relevant only after a source-derived seam extension supplies
transverse normal or conormal data.

## Scope

the completed theta seam as a normal bundle or determine its rank.
