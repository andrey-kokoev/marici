# 3273 — The Bordered Prime Transfer Has an Exact Order-Three Determinant Split

For the depth-\(N\) bordered prime transfer \(g_N(q)=\sum_{k=0}^Nq^k\),

\[
g_N(q)^2=e^{2q}e^{q^2}R_N(q),
\qquad
R_N(q)=g_N(q)^2e^{-2q-q^2}.
\]

The first two logarithmic jets of \(R_N\) vanish. The explicit factors are the
independently derived primitive and square currents; the remaining determinant
begins at cubic order. At finite depth it also retains the terminal cutoff
current.

On the infinite local chain,

\[
\log R(q)=\sum_{k\ge3}\frac{2q^k}{k},
\]

which is exactly the order-three regularized determinant filtration.
