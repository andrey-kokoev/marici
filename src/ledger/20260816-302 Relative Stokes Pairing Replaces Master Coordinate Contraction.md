# Relative Stokes Pairing Replaces Master-Coordinate Contraction

## Result

The remaining comparison between the logarithmic marked-extension columns
and the enhanced higher-Rees functionals cannot be obtained by contracting
their equation-(58) master coordinates.

That proposed contraction is not invariant under the exact-lift gauge.
The obstruction is source-visible:

- Entry 291 proves that the one-wall class is defined modulo a
  five-dimensional exact-lift variation whose freely varying coordinates
  include \(e_3,e_5,e_6\);
- Entries 225--226 prove that the exceptional functional is supported
  precisely on \(e_3,e_5,e_6\).

Therefore a representative change that leaves the relative cohomology class
fixed can change the naive coordinate contraction. No matrix obtained by
dotting the displayed source vectors is canonical.

The correct pairing is the mapping-cone/relative Stokes pairing

\[
\boxed{
\left\langle
[(\Omega,\eta)],
[(\Gamma,\gamma)]
\right\rangle_{\rm rel}
=
\int_\Gamma\Omega
-
\int_\gamma\eta,
\qquad
\partial\Gamma=i_*\gamma.
}
\]

The boundary term is not optional. It is exactly the datum carried by the
universal one-wall and two-wall primitives of Entries 296--297.

Thus the missing global comparison has a determined geometric home:

\[
\boxed{
\text{relative de Rham--homology duality on the frozen marked pair},
}
\]

not a new coefficient direction or carrier stratum.

## Frozen relative complex

For the marked pair

\[
i:W_E\hookrightarrow S_E,
\]

use the de Rham mapping cone

\[
\operatorname{Cone}
\left(
\Omega^\bullet(S_E)
\xrightarrow{i^*}
\Omega^\bullet(W_E)
\right)[-1].
\]

A degree-two relative cocycle is represented by

\[
(\Omega,\eta),
\qquad
d\Omega=0,
\qquad
i^*\Omega=d\eta.
\]

A relative cycle is represented by

\[
(\Gamma,\gamma),
\qquad
\partial\Gamma=i_*\gamma,
\qquad
\partial\gamma=0.
\]

No compactification, wall, support summand, or pairing kernel is added.

## Gauge invariance

A relative coboundary change generated by a one-form \(\xi\) has

\[
(\Omega,\eta)
\longmapsto
(\Omega+d\xi,\eta+i^*\xi)
\]

up to the lower boundary gauge on \(W_E\). The pairing changes by

\[
\int_\Gamma d\xi
-
\int_\gamma i^*\xi
=
\int_{\partial\Gamma}\xi
-
\int_{i_*\gamma}\xi
=0.
\]

Hence the relative Stokes expression descends to cohomology and homology.

By contrast, the absolute term alone changes by

\[
\int_\Gamma d\xi
=
\int_\gamma i^*\xi,
\]

which is generically nonzero for the enhanced thimble because it has the
source-selected exceptional boundary.

This is the exact reason that the master-coordinate contraction fails.
