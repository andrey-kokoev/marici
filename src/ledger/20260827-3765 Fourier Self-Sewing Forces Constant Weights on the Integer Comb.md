# 3765 — Fourier Self-Sewing Forces Constant Weights on the Integer Comb

Let

\[
\mu_w=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}w_n\delta_n
\]

be a tempered weighted measure on integer support. Its Fourier transform is
1-periodic. If

\[
\widehat\mu_w=\lambda\mu_w,
\]

then \(\mu_w\) must also be 1-periodic, forcing

\[
w_{n+1}=w_n.
\]

Thus every weight is one constant (c), and the eigenmeasure is
(c\Delta_{\mathbb Z}) with Fourier eigenphase one. The theta multiplicity
and completion boundary fix (c=1).

Nonconstant periodic weights instead produce rational dual cosets and require
an enlarged finite Fourier packet. They are not scalar deformations of the
one-port integer comb.

Together with the affine classification, this uniquely selects the
unit-weight integer comb among same-object one-affine-comb Fourier sources.
Zero confinement remains a property of its canonical completed transform,
not a consequence of source uniqueness alone.
