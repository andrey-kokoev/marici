## Claim

Ordered scalar-wall endpoints trap one four-dimensional chirality, and bulk
Chern--Simons inflow supplies a quantized anomaly response robust under RG and
massive thresholds. For linear wall slope \(\mu\), however, localized modes
have overlap

\[
y_{ij}=\rho e^{-\mu^2(\ell_i-\ell_j)^2/2},
\qquad
\ell_i=-\frac{M_i}{2\mu^2}.
\]

but yield \(\rho e^{-1/2}\) and \(\rho e^{-2}\). Swapping their species
assignment reverses \(g_n-g_m\) without changing the inflow packet.
