# The Charged Messenger Cycle Has a Tree Level Interference Readout

The charged transition between an up first-stage messenger and a down
second-stage messenger has two finite-mass paths. One passes through the up
second stage; the other crosses first and passes through the down first stage.
Their exact amplitude ratio is the unique charged-cycle invariant.

On the equal-magnitude unit slice, constructive relative sign gives coherent
rate four and destructive relative sign gives zero. The distinction survives
common endpoint rephasing and conserves electric charge.

This upgrades the cycle to a conditional source-generated threshold probe.
Both signs remain legal source points, and no calibrated instrument exists
until masses, eigenstate mixing, widths, competing decays, production,
resolution, and a common-frame likelihood are supplied.
