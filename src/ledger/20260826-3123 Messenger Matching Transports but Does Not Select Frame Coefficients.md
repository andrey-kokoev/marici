# 3123 — Messenger Matching Transports but Does Not Select Frame Coefficients

## Result

Six WP435-type one-stage chains match twelve complex vertex couplings to the
six complex frame coefficients by

\[
c_k=-\frac{x_ky_k}{M_k}.
\]

With frozen masses, the exact matching Jacobian has rank six and a
six-complex-dimensional kernel. Reciprocal rescaling of the two vertices leaves
the low-energy coefficient unchanged.

The UV packets ((x,y,M)=(1,1,1)) and ((2,1/2,1)) both give (c=-1), but
their left-vertex strengths are one and four.

## Scope

source identification. Threshold-sensitive calibrated probes remain required.
