# Traceless Symplectic Flow Has No Strict Positive Local Metric

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

For any traceless generator \(H\) and positive metric \(Q\), the Lyapunov
form \(H^*Q+QH\) has zero trace after metric conjugation. If it is
semidefinite, it must vanish identically. Thus a traceless symplectic flow
cannot be strictly contractive or expansive in any fixed positive local
metric.

The half-trace-gauged theta Evans system and its finite reciprocal doubles
are traceless. Confinement must therefore come from a source-derived moving
metric, boundary crossing form, nonlocal Gramian, or completion anomaly—not
bulk positive contraction.
