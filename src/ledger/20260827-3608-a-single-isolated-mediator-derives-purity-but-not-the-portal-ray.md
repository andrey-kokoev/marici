# A Single Isolated Mediator Derives Purity but Not the Portal Ray

One nondegenerate mediator \(X\) coupled to three marked flavor operators with
coupling vector \(g\) has isolated-pole residue and low-energy Wilson matrix

\[
R=gg^T,
\qquad
C=\frac{gg^T}{M^2}.
\]

Rank-one purity is therefore source-derived on this domain. For nonzero
couplings,

\[
R_{12}R_{23}R_{31}=(g_1g_2g_3)^2>0,
\]

so the positive three-operator loop sign is unavoidable.

This does not select the portal ray or scale. Equal-norm coupling vectors can
produce distinct matrices, and \((g,M)\mapsto(\lambda g,\lambda M)\) leaves
the full low-energy matrix unchanged. Multiplicative RG preserves rank one
without selecting a unique projective basin. Additional unresolved mediator
channels can produce a positive residue with negative triangle product.
Physical16 descent and common detector calibration remain absent.
