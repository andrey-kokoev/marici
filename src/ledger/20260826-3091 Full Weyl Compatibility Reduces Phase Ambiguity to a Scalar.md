# 3091 — Full Weyl Compatibility Reduces Phase Ambiguity to a Scalar

The rational stabilizer alone fixes the theta comb but cannot generate the
full boundary module. Adding its noncommuting transverse Weyl polarization
changes the rigidity problem completely.

In an irreducible Weyl representation, any ambiguity compatible with both
polarizations belongs to the joint commutant and is therefore scalar:

\[
V=cI.
\]

Preservation of the normalized theta distribution then forces \(c=1\).

This supplies a viable phase-selection mechanism: not cyclicity for one
maximal abelian algebra, but Schur rigidity of the complete noncommuting Weyl
pair.

## Scope

The finite Weyl theorem is exact. The RH programme must still prove adelic
irreducibility and show that every source-admissible boundary selector
intertwines both polarizations.
