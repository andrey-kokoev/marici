# The Spectral and Theta Approximants Are Two Directed Systems with a Third Comparison Tower

Ordinary theta cutoffs can have off-seam zeros and therefore cannot be finite
self-adjoint characteristic determinants. A viable programme needs two
different directed systems: analytic theta cutoffs converging to Xi, and
source-derived self-adjoint compressions converging to a completed operator.

A third comparison tower must relate their limits while explicitly tracking
finite background divisors and escaping zeros. No termwise divisor-preserving
isomorphism can exist.

Moreover, the two-chart transition does not select the section. Multiplying
both local determinants by the same entire divisor-bearing factor preserves
their ratio and every Čech law. The source must therefore construct a
canonical determinant vector or boundary morphism, not merely the determinant
line and its transition.
