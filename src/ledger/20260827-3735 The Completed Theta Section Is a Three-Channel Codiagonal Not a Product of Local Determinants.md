# 3735 — The Completed Theta Section Is a Three-Channel Codiagonal, Not a Product of Local Determinants

Splitting the theta Mellin integral at the reciprocal boundary gives

\[
\xi(s)=C(s)+U(s)+V(s),
\]

where \(C=1/2\) is the completion carrier and \(U,V\) are the direct and
reciprocal half-Mellin amplitudes.

Thus the global readout is the codiagonal

\[
(c,u,v)\longmapsto c+u+v.
\]

A zero is an intersection of the complete three-channel source curve with
the two-dimensional kernel of this map. It is not loss of rank of any local
Tate determinant.

On the critical line, reciprocal conjugation gives

\[
V(s)=\overline{U(s)},
\]

so the zero equation reduces to one real cancellation. Off the line, the two
Mellin channels remain independent complex directions. RH is therefore a
global intersection-confinement theorem for the particular minimal-theta
curve.

This identifies the literal three towers: direct sector, reciprocal sector,
and completion/control carrier. The scalar zero is a projection event in
their codiagonal, not disappearance of the complete relational state.
