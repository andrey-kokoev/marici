# 1354 — Intrinsic Primes and Norm Support Are Distinct

Epistemic-graph event: 1378.

The conditional pointed `pi_0` construction and regular-fiber norm homology
produce primes in different senses.

On `pi_0`, the free additive Carrier classifies its endomorphisms and becomes
the initial semiring.  Prime elements then arise internally from divisibility
and irreducibility, without an external prime list or degree map.  This is
genuine conditional prime formation.

By contrast,

\[
H_d\cong(\mathbf Z/d)^{d-2}
\]

only detects the prime divisors of an already supplied fiber degree.  If a
source-derived degree functor identifies that correspondence with `dU`, then
for `d>2`

\[
p\mid d \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (H_d)_{(p)}\ne0.
\]

Norm support is therefore a possible downstream realization of intrinsic
divisibility, not its origin.  The exceptional `H_2=0` shows that homology is
not a faithful prime detector by itself.

Scope: the initial semiring remains conditional on the explicit pointed
monoidal relaxation.  No full-Carrier lift, Frobenius, Euler product, or
physical chain realization follows.
