# 3078 — Eisenstein Fourier Modes Repeat Rather Than Constrain the Scattering Divisor

Every nonconstant Fourier coefficient of the modular Eisenstein series shares
the factor

\[
\frac1{\Lambda(2s)}.
\]

Because the (n=1) divisor coefficient equals one, the full Fourier vector
faithfully repeats the scalar scattering-pole divisor. Ratios of modes cancel
that divisor, while the Maass–Selberg Green norm contains only
(-\varphi'/\varphi), the logarithmic derivative of the same scalar
scattering coefficient.

Thus retaining all linear Eisenstein modes enriches the state profile but
does not independently constrain resonance locations. A surviving mechanism
must be nonlinear or relative, involving at least two spectral arguments or
source states.

## Scope

This closes the linear Fourier-mode route, not every Rankin–Selberg or
relative automorphic construction.
