# 3278 — Auxiliary Squares Move the Portal into a Clebsch Gram

The complete real-triplet auxiliary family linear in \(\chi n\) and \(\chi m\)
induces the portal matrix

\[
K=\kappa C^TC.
\]

Independent triplet flips require orthogonal columns of \(C\). Source exchange
then forces equal column norms and zero portal contrast. Unequal fixed
Clebsches can rigidify a ratio, but the rescaling \(C\mapsto sC\) changes the
absolute portal magnitude without changing incidence or orthogonality. The
same portal square also generates no \((n\mathbin\cdot m)^2\) stiffness.

A generic F-term therefore does not explain the portal; it moves the free
data into a positive Gram. Progress requires a representation theorem fixing
unequal Clebsches, a gauge–Yukawa identity fixing their normalization, and a
shared algebra generating the angular constraint.
