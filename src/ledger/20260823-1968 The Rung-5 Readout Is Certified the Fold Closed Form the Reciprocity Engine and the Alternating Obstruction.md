## What was done

The previous arc certified the fold law for all tower orders and left
one labeled gap: the rung-5 readout was a *prediction* under the
declared fold-grade = readout-grade identification, because the
character theorem \(P(E_g)=+u^{g+2}E_g\), \(P(M_g)=-u^{g+3}M_g\) was
certified only for \(g=0..5\) while rung 5 folds at grade 8. This arc
closes the gap — not by pushing the brute-force computation two grades
further, but by collapsing the fold chain into a closed form whose
properties are proved once and for all.

## Results

- **The fold closed form (groups Q, F).** The full grade-\(g\) fold
  chain collapses:
  \(\operatorname{fold}_g(C)=(1+u)^{-2(g+1)}z^{-(g+2)}Q_g(u)\), with
  \(Q_g\) a Laurent polynomial generated by the tridiagonal recursion
  \(Q_1=(\Theta-2)h\), \(h=(1+u)^4(u+u^{-1})\),
  \(Q_{j+1}=T_{-(j+2)}Q_j\),
  \(T_e u^m=(e+m)u^m+2(e+m+1)u^{m+1}+(e+m+2)u^{m+2}\). Certified: base
  against \(D_z(C,2)\), one-step recursion through \(g=8\), direct
  full-chain anchor \(g=1..5\).
- **The reciprocity engine.**
  \(Q_g(u)=(-1)^g u^{2(g+1)}Q_g(1/u)\), certified \(g=1..12\), with
  support law \(\operatorname{supp}Q_g=[-1,2g+3]\), endpoints attained.
  Hand proof by induction: the tridiagonal multipliers at \(e=-(g+2)\)
  are antisymmetric about the pivot, so the reciprocity transports with
  a sign flip at every step. Both readout characters are corollaries of
  this one lemma.
- **Character theorem through grade 8 (group C).**
  \(P(E_g)=+u^{g+2}E_g\), \(P(M_g)=-u^{g+3}M_g\), monomial closure
  \(z^{g+1}A_g=\bar z^{g+1}B_g\), and diagonal weight
  \(P(A_g)=z^{2g+4}\bar z^2 A_g\) — all exact for \(g=0..8\). Witness
  regression ties the engine to the previously certified \(g=4\)
  values.
- **The rung-5 verdict (group R5).** At grade 8: electric
  \(P(E_8)=+u^{10}\), root \(u^5\) \(\sigma\)-invariant — gate PASSES;
  magnetic \(P(M_8)=-u^{11}\), odd exponent — gate FAILS, with
  closed-form obstruction \(P(M_8)-M_8=-(1+u^{11})M_8\) exact and
  unrepairable by rational cocycle dressing (intrinsic-oddness lemma,
  \(\operatorname{val}_u(h\sigma(h))=2\operatorname{val}_u(h)\) always
  even). The square-root obstruction at rung 5 sits on the magnetic
  sector.
- **The alternation theorem (group ALT).** Rung \(n+1\) folds at grade
  \(g_n=n+2\lceil n/2\rceil\) (theorem for all \(n\) via the all-orders
  fold law), so parity\((g_n)\) = parity\((n)\) and exactly one of the
  two character exponents is odd at every rung: exactly one sector is
  square-root obstructed per rung, alternating E, M, E, M along the
  grounded ladder — rung 2 electric (\(+u^5\)), rung 3 magnetic
  (\(-u^7\)), rung 4 electric (\(+u^9\)), rung 5 magnetic (\(-u^{11}\)).

## Method note

The arc began as "extend the certified range from \(g\le5\) to
\(g=8\)" and became structural: the brute-force readouts are rational
functions of growing complexity, but the fold chain has a one-line
Laurent recursion, and the entire character theorem is the reciprocity
of its generator. The checker certifies the engine (symbolic monomial
action, base, recursion step), not just the conclusion — so the
character theorem is now a theorem of the engine, not a finite-range
observation.
