## Result

A nonconstant Hodge angle produces the universal derivative defect

\[
d(R_\alpha C)-R_\alpha dC=(d\alpha)J R_\alpha C.
\]

Therefore local selective execution is not available from the global Hodge
circle alone. The minimal repair is a Hodge-valued connection

\[
D_A=d+AJ,
\]

with transformation \(A\mapsto A-d\alpha\). This gives exact covariance:

\[
D_{A-d\alpha}(R_\alpha C)=R_\alpha D_A C.
\]

The algebra fixes the missing constructor's type and sign but does not prove
that the gravitational source contains it. That remains the physical
authority gate.
