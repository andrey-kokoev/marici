# 1502 — The Planck Scale Defines an Earliest Validity Bound, Not a Canonical Initial Time

## Status

Primary-source typing result for the finite-initial-boundary cosmology lane.

## Frozen source statement

Collins and Holman formulate the initial-state effective theory with a high-energy scale \(M\). For a measured comoving scale \(k_{\rm exp}\), perturbative validity requires [hep-th/0507081, Eq. (2.29)]

\[
\frac{a(\eta_{\rm now})}{a(\eta_0)}\frac{k_{\rm exp}}{M}\ll1.
\]

The earliest admissible initial surface is estimated by saturation [Eq. (2.30)]:

\[
\frac{a(\eta_0^{\rm earliest})}{a(\eta_{\rm now})}
\sim
\frac{k_{\rm exp}}{M}.
\]

The source immediately warns that \(\eta_0\) need not be chosen at this limiting surface or at a time at which the unknown high-energy dynamics occurred. One may choose a later common initial surface once the relevant modes lie within the effective regime.

## Typed consequence

For a finite observational band \(K_{\rm obs}\), a common initial hypersurface is constrained by its largest physical momentum. Schematically,

\[
\frac{a(\eta_0)}{a(\eta_{\rm now})}
\gg
\frac{k_{\max}}{M}.
\]

Saturating the bound separately for every mode would instead define a family

\[
a(\eta_k)/a(\eta_{\rm now})\sim k/M,
\]

not one spacelike initial-boundary carrier. That construction is not the source prescription.

Even if \(M=M_{\rm Pl}\), the equations therefore do not identify a universal “Planck time.” They determine an observational-band- and background-dependent lower bound on admissible initial surfaces. This is not the invariant proper time

\[
t_{\rm Pl}=\sqrt{\hbar G/c^5},
\]

and no equality between the two is derived.

## Classification

\[
\boxed{
M\text{ supplies a coefficient-validity bound on the existing initial-boundary carrier, not a new Planck-time stratum.}
}
\]

The surviving architecture is:

\[
\text{existing initial hypersurface}
+
\text{admissible-domain inequality depending on }(K_{\rm obs},M,a).
\]

No new carrier divisor or preferred cosmic origin is justified.

## Deutsch–Popperian conjecture and falsifier

**Conjecture.** In the finite-initial-state effective theory, the high-energy scale enters only through coefficient data and the domain of validity of a freely chosen common initial hypersurface; it does not canonically select that hypersurface.

**Finite falsifier.** Derive from a frozen primary construction either:

1. a source map selecting the saturated surface dynamically and independently of the observational momentum band; or
2. a background-covariant event whose proper-time location equals \(t_{\rm Pl}\) and through which all relevant modes share one carrier incidence.

Absent either derivation, calling \(\eta_0^{\rm earliest}\) “the Planck time” is a type error.
