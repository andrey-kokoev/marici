# The Scalar Jordan Projection Leaves a Rank-Three Square-Curvature Kernel

The eight-point coherence complex of Entry 19 has four square faces,
canonically indexed by the four octagon diameters
\[
d_i=(i,i+4),\qquad i\in\mathbb Z/4.
\]
Their curvature lattice is therefore the permutation module
\[
C_{\square}\cong\mathbb Z^4.
\]
The central half-turn fixes every unoriented diameter, so the full \(D_8\)
action factors through the order-eight symmetry group of this four-point
module.

## What the scalar endpoint can see

The scalar specialization of the Jordan comparison is necessarily the
invariant augmentation
\[
\epsilon_{\square}=(1,1,1,1):\mathbb Z^4\longrightarrow\mathbb Z.
\]
It is primitive and has rank one. Its kernel is
\[
K_{\square}=
\left\{(c_0,c_1,c_2,c_3):\sum_i c_i=0\right\},
\qquad \operatorname{rank}K_{\square}=3.
\]
Direct enumeration verifies that this kernel is preserved by every
dihedral transport. Over \(\mathbb Q\) it splits as
\[
K_{\square,\mathbb Q}
\cong \mathbb Q_{\rm alt}\oplus V_{\rm std},
\]
where the alternating line is generated by \((1,-1,1,-1)\) and
\(V_{\rm std}\) is the two-dimensional square representation generated by
\((1,0,-1,0)\) and \((0,1,0,-1)\).

Entry 401 proves that the selected endpoint class has zero scalar Jordan
image. This controls only the invariant quotient
\(C_{\square}/K_{\square}\). It cannot, even in principle, decide the three
non-scalar curvature directions.

## Meaning

The endpoint result was not weak; it exhausted exactly the trivial
\(D_8\)-isotypic obstruction. But it also exposes the next irreducible
question. A nonzero curvature vector may still survive in the alternating
line or the standard two-dimensional summand while having zero scalar
augmentation.

No such residual obstruction is asserted here: the actual four curvature
coefficients have not yet been evaluated in the geometric/Jordan target.
The next experiment is now sharply reduced. Evaluate one representative in
the alternating channel and one in the standard channel; \(D_8\) then
determines all four square curvatures. If both vanish, the square-curvature
comparison closes. If either survives, its representation type identifies
the missing coherence datum.
