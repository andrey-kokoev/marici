# The Logarithmic Integer Comb Is Not an Ordinary Hilbert Boundary Trace

After logarithmic unitarization, integer sampling acts as

\[
B\psi
=
\sum_{n\geq1}n^{-1/2}\psi(\log n).
\]

For a compact packet translated to height \(R\), its \(H^1\) norm is constant,
but

\[
B\psi_R
\sim
e^{R/2}
\int e^{t/2}\psi(t)\,dt.
\]

Hence the comb trace is unbounded on the carrier's natural
translation-invariant Sobolev domain.

The canonical quarter-threshold Dirac carrier and the canonical integer comb
cannot be coupled by an ordinary Hilbert boundary triple. Their interaction
requires an exponential rigging, a discrete label port, or equivalent relative
boundary data.
