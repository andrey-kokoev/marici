# A Second Odd Port Creates Relative Sign Not Absolute Sign

The original parity action groupoid on two sign-related entrance vacua has one
isomorphism class. Its descended Boolean probes are constant, and every
absolute sign separator fails descent.

Adding a second odd source object and quotienting by simultaneous sign reversal
creates two relative isomorphism classes. The product of the entrance sign and
reference sign is invariant and jointly faithful on those classes. Forgetting
the reference collapses both classes to the original quotient.

This is a constructive categorical window only in a changed stabilizer
groupoid. It creates relative sign and never recovers absolute sign. The odd
reference, its preparation, discrete-gauge consistency, and its instrument
remain source gates.
