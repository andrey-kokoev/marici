\[
\Lambda(s)
=
I(s)+\frac1{s-1}-\frac1s,
\]

with \(I(s)=I(1-s)\) entire.  Canonical completion maps the two continuation
currents to

\[
\frac12s(s-1)
\left(
\frac1{s-1}-\frac1s
\right)
=
\frac12.
\]

Thus the neutral half carrier is exactly the scalar image of the completed
archimedean boundary anomaly.  The archimedean lane is now classified: flat
bulk, rank-two relative boundary, and rank-one constant compression.  None
orients zeros.  Any remaining force must be a pre-compression coupling to
arithmetic boundary registers.
