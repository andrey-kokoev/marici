# D4 Triality Fixes Orbit Three, Not a Portal Coordinate

WP798 derives the \(G_2\)-type triple incidence from the order-three triality
of affine \(D_4^{(1)}\). The invariant fold is the twisted affine system
\(D_4^{(3)}\), not the untwisted \(G_2^{(1)}\) packet tested in WP797.

The primitive affine \(D_4\) null vector is
\((1,2,1,1,1)^{\mathsf T}\). In the integral invariant basis formed from the
affine node, central node, and sum of the three triality-related outer nodes,
its folded coordinates are \((1,2,1)^{\mathsf T}\). In the orbit-average
basis they are \((1,2,3)^{\mathsf T}\), and after canonical normalization they
are \((1,2,\sqrt3)^{\mathsf T}\). All three coordinate vectors map to exactly
the same \(D_4\) state.

Triality therefore makes orbit cardinality three and the associated
long--short incidence source-invariant. It does not make the displayed
coefficient three a basis-independent flavor coupling. A source action must
still choose the charge lattice, kinetic pairing, electric polarization,
node-to-species map, sign, and overall scale. Matter-complete RG, finite
thresholds, and calibrated physical16 response remain absent.

The smallest exact falsifier is the single source state with the three
coordinate profiles above. Reading the averaged coefficient as a portal value
without naming the action and instrument fails descent. Triality is a stronger
geometric rigidifier than bare \(G_2\), but not the complete source selector.
