# 3671 — The Lagrangian Seam Splitting Has a Three–Four–Three Quadratic Tower

## Result

A two-dimensional Lagrangian reciprocal-sector graph has a two-dimensional
normal seam complement. Its complete quadratic controls decompose as

\[
\operatorname{Sym}^2(L^*)
\oplus(L^*\otimes N^*)
\oplus\operatorname{Sym}^2(N^*)
\]

with dimensions

\[
3+4+3=10.
\]

The four-dimensional layer consists of mixed bulk–seam comparisons. The seam
state itself has rank two and carries three pure quadratic controls.

## Consequence

The smallest completed finite model must retain all three levels: bulk state,
seam state, and their comparison layer. Treating the four comparisons as an
independent state tower, or retaining them without the seam variables they
compare, is a typing error.

## Scope

This is a finite symplectic theorem. The analytic theta seam's actual rank and
boundary topology remain to be derived from the completed source.
