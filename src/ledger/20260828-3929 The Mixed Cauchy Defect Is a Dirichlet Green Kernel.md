# The Mixed Cauchy Defect Is a Dirichlet Green Kernel

The mixed-sector multiplicativity defect of the Cauchy character is the
Dirichlet Green kernel

\[
K(x,y)=e^{-|x-y|/2}-e^{-(x+y)/2}
\]

for \(-d^2/dx^2+1/4\) on the positive half-line.  At prime frequencies,

\[
K(\log p,\log q)=\frac{\min(p,q)-1}{\sqrt{pq}}
=\int_1^\infty
\frac{\mathbf 1_{r\le p}}{\sqrt p}
\frac{\mathbf 1_{r\le q}}{\sqrt q},dr.
\]

Hence every finite mixed-prime matrix is positive definite and its quadratic
form is an exact source-derived Green energy.

This establishes universal coupled positivity, but also closes a tempting
shortcut.  The raw energy cannot equal the BSY entropy: under RH the latter
vanishes, whereas the Green energy can vanish only for the zero mixed packet.
The RH-bearing object must instead be a relative form consisting of this
positive bulk minus a source-derived modular boundary repair.

The next gate is to derive that repair from the primitive, prime-square,
archimedean, and seam currents and test the resulting identity on the smallest
forward--backward prime word.
