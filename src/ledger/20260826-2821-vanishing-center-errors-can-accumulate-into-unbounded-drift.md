# 2821 — Vanishing Center Errors Can Accumulate into Unbounded Drift

If paired exchanging constructors induce translations \(\delta_k\), then

\[
x_N=x_0+\sum_{k=1}^N\delta_k.
\]

Bounded partial sums are necessary and sufficient for a bounded frame;
convergence gives a limiting frame, and absolute summability is the robust
order-independent certificate. The hostile family \(\delta_k=1/k\) tends to
zero locally but accumulates as \(H_N\sim\log N\). An absolute error budget
\(E\) yields multiplicative control \(e^{-E}M_0\le M_N\le e^EM_0\).
