# 3292 — A Positive Moving Graph Cannot Exclude a Scalar Endpoint Kernel

A positive source measure defines a Hankel Gram matrix \(H_N\) and hence a
positive graph \(\Gamma(H_N)\) inside the reciprocal hyperbolic double. Prime
Pascal transport moves it covariantly by

\[
H_N\longmapsto A_{p,N}^{-T}H_NA_{p,N}^{-1},
\]

preserving positivity.

Nevertheless, for moment rank at least two, every scalar linear readout on
the graph has a nontrivial kernel. Thus the graph contains nonzero states with
zero endpoint coordinate and strictly positive relationship energy. Its angle
to the endpoint-kernel is already zero at finite rank, independently of
completion.

Zero exclusion must therefore be proved on a smaller source-generated orbit,
a cone avoiding the kernel, a dynamically unreachable subobject, or a jointly
faithful multi-port readout—not on the entire positive graph.
