# 3146 — The Exterior Zeta Kernel Needs More Than One Negative Boundary Channel

On the exact exterior probe packet \(0,-1,-2\), the continued polarized
kernel \(\zeta(s+\bar w)\) has leading principal minors

\[
-\frac12,
\qquad
-\frac1{144},
\qquad
\frac1{28800}.
\]

Its LDL pivots therefore have signs \((-,+,-)\). The kernel has one positive
and two negative directions on this three-probe packet.

## Scope

A single compensating scalar cannot realize the exterior continuation. The
faithful fifth wall must be a transported boundary module of rank at least
two. This does not yet prove that the full boundary module has infinite
negative index.
