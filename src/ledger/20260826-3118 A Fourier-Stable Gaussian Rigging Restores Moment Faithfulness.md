# 3118 — A Fourier-Stable Gaussian Rigging Restores Moment Faithfulness

Let \(\mathcal G\) be the inductive union of Gaussian test-space steps
\(\mathcal G_{a,b}\), whose members and Fourier transforms decay respectively
like \(e^{-a x^2}\) and \(e^{-b \xi^2}\). Fourier transform swaps \((a,b)\),
while dilation by \(q\) sends it to

\[
(a e^{2q}, b e^{-2q}).
\]

This Roumieu Gaussian Gelfand–Shilov rigging contains the theta Gaussian
orbit, supports the integer-comb pairing, and excludes the flat Fourier-bump
falsifier. Gaussian Fourier decay gives entire continuation, so vanishing of
the complete moment germ forces the state itself to vanish within this class.

Thus moment faithfulness can be restored by a Fourier- and dilation-stable
source topology finer than the ordinary Hilbert completion.

## Scope

This is a surviving archimedean completion candidate, not yet an adelic
completion theorem. Continuity of the primitive, prime-square, connected,
seam, Hankel-trace, cutoff, and Fock constructors remains to be derived.
