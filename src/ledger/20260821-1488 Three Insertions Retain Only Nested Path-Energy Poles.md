# 1488 — Three Insertions Retain Only Nested Path-Energy Poles

## Status

Exact source derivation and exact first-pushforward reconstruction for three
perturbative mass insertions. This tests the extension of Entries 1479 and
1484 one rung beyond the first nontrivial case.

## Derived object

For the labelled path

\[
x_1--w_1--w_2--w_3--x_2,
\]

expand all four bulk propagators into forward-ordered, reverse-ordered, and
boundary-subtraction terms. The checker sums the linear extensions of each of
the \(3^4=81\) resulting time-order posets and integrates each chamber by its
suffix-energy products.

The reduced rational integrand has numerator degree six and denominator degree
fifteen. It obeys the exact reversal identity

\[
I_3(x_1,w_1,w_2,w_3,x_2;y)
=I_3(x_2,w_3,w_2,w_1,x_1;y).
\]

## Terminal pushforward

After applying the de Sitter weight \(w_1w_2w_3\), the integrand has exactly
eight simple poles in \(w_3\). Their positive shifts are

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_3=\{&2y,\ x_2+y,\ w_2+2y,\ w_2+x_2+y,\\
&w_1+w_2+2y,\ w_1+w_2+x_2+y,\\
&x_1+w_1+w_2+y,\ x_1+w_1+w_2+x_2\}.
\end{aligned}
}
\]

The exact residue sum vanishes, and the eight-term partial fraction expansion
reconstructs the weighted rational integrand identically. Hence the first
pushforward is a convergent sum of logarithms of precisely these eight
arguments.

## Incidence pattern

Every shift is the energy of a connected nested subpath ending at, or crossing,
the terminal white site. The list is generated by the two right-end seeds

\[
2y,
\qquad
x_2+y,
\]

and their successive extensions across \(w_2\), \(w_1\), and finally the left
endpoint. No non-path or fitted divisor appears.

Thus the first-pushforward phenomenon of Entry 1479 persists at three
insertions:

\[
\boxed{
\text{terminal Kummer pushforward}
\subset
\text{connected path-energy arrangement}.
}
\]

## Scope and next falsifier

This is not yet an all-chain theorem, nor a statement about the complete
weight-three symbol. The next structural test is to derive the terminal pole
list for a general \(r\)-white path recursively from the ordered propagator
states and prove its nested-subpath form without enumerating \(3^{r+1}\)
states.
