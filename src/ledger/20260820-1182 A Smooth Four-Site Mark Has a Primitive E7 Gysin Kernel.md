# 1182 — A Smooth Four-Site Mark Has a Primitive E7 Gysin Kernel

Sequence claim: `seqclaim-9d00631c7624d52dfc328653`.

## Smooth benchmark

Let \(X\) be a smooth quartic double solid and let \(S\subset X\) be the
preimage of a generic marked hyperplane in \(\mathbf P^3\). Then \(S\) is a
double cover of \(\mathbf P^2\) branched over a smooth quartic: a degree-two
del Pezzo surface.

Use the integral Picard basis

\[
\operatorname{Pic}(S)
=
\mathbf Z\langle H,E_1,\ldots,E_7\rangle,
\]

with intersection form \(\operatorname{diag}(1,-1^7)\). The marked
hyperplane restricts to the anticanonical class

\[
-K_S=3H-\sum_{i=1}^7E_i,
\qquad
(-K_S)^2=2.
\]

## Gysin map

The Gysin map to the rank-one \(H^4(X)\) is intersection with \(-K_S\).
In the chosen basis its primitive row is

\[
\boxed{(3,1,1,1,1,1,1,1).}
\]

It is surjective over \(\mathbf Z\). Its kernel has rank seven and is
generated by

\[
E_1-E_2,\ldots,E_6-E_7,
\qquad
H-E_1-E_2-E_3.
\]

Their intersection matrix is the negative \(E_7\) Cartan lattice and has
discriminant two. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\ker\bigl(H^2(S)(-1)\xrightarrow{\rm Gys}H^4(X)\bigr)
\simeq E_7(-1).
}
\]

The kernel is primitive; no hidden integral cokernel occurs.

## Complement coefficient architecture

For the smooth marked complement \(U=X\setminus S\), localization gives the
rank profile

\[
0\longrightarrow H^3(X)
\longrightarrow H^3(U)
\longrightarrow E_7(-1)
\longrightarrow0,
\]

with

\[
\boxed{20+7=27.}
\]

The rank-twenty threefold middle system is sector-specific non-Tate data;
the marked residue contributes a Tate \(E_7\) extension through the same
