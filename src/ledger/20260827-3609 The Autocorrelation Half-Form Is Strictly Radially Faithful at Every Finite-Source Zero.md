# 3609 — The Autocorrelation Half-Form Is Strictly Radially Faithful at Every Finite-Source Zero

coefficients and define

\[
A(w)=\frac{C_0}{2}+\sum_{k\ge1}C_kw^k.
\]

On the unit circle,

\[
2\operatorname{Re}A=|P|^2.
\]

Hence `Re A` is strictly positive in the open unit disk. At every nonzero root
of `P`, the oriented current satisfies `J=2A(w)` inside and
`J=-2A(1/w)` outside. Therefore

\[
\operatorname{sign}\operatorname{Re}J
=
\operatorname{sign}(1-|w|)
\]

at every off-seam root, while `Re J=0` at unit-circle roots.

for any finite real source packet. Radial faithfulness is universal; the
