# 2608 — Primitive Seam Current Leaves a Mixed Bulk Residual

In the centered doubled-flow frame, the native flux obeys

\[
2\delta N=-J'-2\sqrt2\operatorname{Re}(F\bar d).
\]

Introducing the canonical primitive seam antiderivative \(H'=F\) cancels the
explicit forcing term, but leaves

\[
R_{\mathrm{mix}}
=-2\sqrt2\delta\operatorname{Re}(H\bar m)
+2\sqrt2\operatorname{Re}(i\tau H\bar d).
\]

Thus primitive seam retention alone does not close the conservation law.
The square, Clark, or archimedean channel must differentiate to the negative
of this residual as a typed identity.
