# The Proper Top Line Has a Rank-Two Boundary Extension

## Setup

Entries 581 and 583 establish a coherent deletion cube preserved by the two
kinematic derivatives \(\nabla_x,\nabla_y\).  Entry 582 identifies the proper
top quotient as the line generated by the literal form \(\Omega_{111}\).

Let \(B\subset H_{111}\) be the span of the three codimension-one face
images.  The retained normal-form certificate gives

\[
\dim H_{111}=21,\qquad \dim B=20.
\]

For each direction, reduce the derivative of the literal generator as

\[
\nabla_\lambda\Omega_{111}
=A_\lambda\Omega_{111}+\eta_\lambda,
\qquad \eta_\lambda\in B.
\]

No complement of \(B\) is selected: \(A_\lambda\) is computed in the
one-dimensional quotient, and \(\eta_\lambda\) is then reduced in the full
twisted-de-Rham presentation.

## Result

At both generic points

\[
(x,y,z)=(2,3,4),\qquad(3,5,6),
\]

over \(\mathbf F_{32003}\), both boundary components are nonzero and

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}\langle\eta_x,\eta_y\rangle=2.}
\]

The descended scalar pairs are respectively

\[
(A_x,A_y)=(14226,24891),
\qquad
(30862,14858)
\pmod{32003}.
\]

An independently added signed-quartic comparison in the same checker shows
that neither pair equals \(\tfrac12d\log\mathcal Q\): all four directional
comparisons fail.

## Meaning

The one-parameter insufficiency of Entry 575 is now realized concretely.
The proper physical line exists and its connection descends, but its literal
source generator mixes with two independent boundary directions.  Therefore
the rank-one quotient is not the whole physical packet, and the extension
cannot be reconstructed from a scalar \(\mathcal Q\)-connection.

This also sharpens Entry 526.  The quartic is neither carrier/Fitting support
nor the scalar connection of the proper top quotient in the tested
presentation.  Any remaining role for \(\mathcal Q\) must lie in a larger
relative-chain/Gysin extension, a discriminant comparison, or a different
normalization derived from physical geometry.

The next decisive test is the mixed second jet: compute
\([\nabla_x,\nabla_y]\Omega_{111}\) together with the parameter derivatives
of the reduction coefficients.  Vanishing would produce the first flat
two-direction filtered packet containing the physical line; failure would
locate the missing relative-chain correction.
