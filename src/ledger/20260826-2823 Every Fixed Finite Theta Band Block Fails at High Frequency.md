# 2823 — Every Fixed Finite Theta Band Block Fails at High Frequency

For every fixed number \(m\) of complete phase periods, the physical block has
the asymptotic form

\[
I_m(a,L)
=
\frac{4m}{\pi}
\left[J_a''(0)-aW_a(0)\right]L^2+O_m(L^4).
\]

The strict source Hardy inequality makes the bracket negative for sufficiently
small positive \(a\). Therefore no fixed finite band regrouping repairs the
high-frequency obstruction. Any surviving band mechanism must have a block
count diverging with \(b\), or the proof must act before band projection.
