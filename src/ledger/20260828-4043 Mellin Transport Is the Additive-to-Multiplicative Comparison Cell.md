# Mellin Transport Is the Additive-to-Multiplicative Comparison Cell

For an integer label with valuation packet \((v_p(n))_p\), the theta heat
weight couples all primes through \(n^2\), whereas the Mellin character
factorizes:

\[
e^{-\pi t n^2}
=
\exp\left(
-\pi t\exp\left(2\sum_pv_p(n)\log p\right)
\right),
\]

\[
n^{-s}=\prod_p p^{-s v_p(n)}.
\]

The labelwise identity

\[
\int_0^\infty e^{-\pi n^2t}t^{s/2-1}\,dt
=
\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)n^{-s}
\]

shows that Mellin transport is the exact comparison cell converting additive
heat formation into multiplicative Euler formation. Poisson reflection and
the two polar currents complete this cell beyond its initial convergence
sector.

The cell explains why the two constructions agree but does not orient their
common divisor. Scalar evaluation can still cancel while the entire
comparison square commutes. The missing rung is now a law on the completed
comparison cell itself, not another data port.
