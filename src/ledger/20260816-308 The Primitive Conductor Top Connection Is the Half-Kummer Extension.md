# The Primitive Conductor Top Connection Is the Half-Kummer Extension

## Result

The Gauss--Manin connection on the complete rank-three conductor quotient is
now explicit. Its primitive top column is forced by the integral half-sum
extension of Entry 280. It introduces neither \(\mathcal Q\) nor any other
new divisor.

Set

\[
a_1=-\frac12d\log\Delta_1,
\qquad
a_2=-\frac12d\log\Delta_2,
\]

with the source-normalized discriminants of Entry 307. In the rational
root-character frame

\[
C=(g_{101},g_{110},g_{111}^{\rm inv}),
\qquad
g_{111}^{\rm inv}
=
\widetilde g_{111}-\frac12(g_{101}+g_{110}),
\]

the normalized graph local system is

\[
\boxed{
\nabla C
=
C\,A_C,
\qquad
A_C=\operatorname{diag}(a_1,a_2,0).
}
\]

The top quotient is flat on the labelled generic locus; the two nontrivial
characters are precisely the moving-wall Kummer lines.

## Primitive integral frame

Let

\[
B=(g_{101},g_{110},\widetilde g_{111}).
\]

Then \(B=CQ\), where

\[
Q=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&\tfrac12\\
0&1&\tfrac12\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\nabla B=B\,A_B,
\qquad
A_B=Q^{-1}A_CQ,
\]

and direct multiplication gives

\[
\boxed{

\begin{pmatrix}
a_1&0&\tfrac12a_1\\

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{

}
\]

The quarter-log entries are not new rank-one characters. They are the de
Rham realization of the integral class

\[

\]

## Exact monodromy recovery

In the rational frame, loops around \(\Delta_1=0\) and \(\Delta_2=0\) act
by

\[
D_1=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1),
\qquad
D_2=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,1).
\]

Conjugation by the same fixed half-sum matrix gives

\[
\boxed{
Q^{-1}D_1Q
=
\begin{pmatrix}
-1&0&-1\\
0&1&0\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}
=M_1,
}
\]

\[
\boxed{
Q^{-1}D_2Q
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0\\
0&-1&-1\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}
=M_2.
}
\]

These are exactly the source graph-permutation matrices of Entry 280.
Thus the connection recovers the full integral extension, not only its
rational semisimplification.
