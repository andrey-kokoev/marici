# 3688 — The Positive Theta Seed Separates the Bounded Even Heat Commutant

## Result

On the even theta sector, the heat generator has simple spectral multiplicity
under the cosine transform. Every bounded commuting operator is multiplication
by a spectral function \(m(u)\).

The completed theta seed \(\Phi(u)\) is positive and therefore nonzero almost
everywhere. If the commuting operator fixes it, then

\[
m(u)\Phi(u)=\Phi(u),
\]

which forces \(m=1\) almost everywhere.

## Consequence

Heat dynamics alone admits hostile source endomorphisms. The pointed object
consisting of heat dynamics together with the exact theta seed does not admit
such bounded endomorphisms. Constant-coefficient polynomial endomorphisms are
likewise forced to the identity by Fourier injectivity.

This establishes source-provenance rigidity but does not prove that the actual
theta heat orbit avoids the Newman collision locus.
