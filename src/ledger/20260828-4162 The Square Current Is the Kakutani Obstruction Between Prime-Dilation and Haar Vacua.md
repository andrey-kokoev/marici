# The Square Current Is the Kakutani Obstruction Between Prime-Dilation and Haar Vacua

The local prime-dilation state has Poisson spectral measure with return
amplitude $r_p=p^{-1/2}$. Its Hellinger affinity with Haar measure is

$$
a(r_p)=1-\frac1{4p}+O(p^{-2}).
$$

Since $\sum_p1/p$ diverges, Kakutani's criterion makes the arithmetic and Haar
infinite product states mutually singular. They do not inhabit one ordinary
tensor-product representation as bounded perturbations of each other.

The divergence is exactly the square grade. Thus the primitive current carries
the divisor, the square current records representation inequivalence, and the
$k\ge3$ tail is determinant class.

The global trace formula must therefore use a source-derived correspondence or
relative modular object between the two GNS representations, with the square
current retained as interface data.
