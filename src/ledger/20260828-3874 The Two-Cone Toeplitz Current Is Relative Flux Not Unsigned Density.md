# The Two-Cone Toeplitz Current Is Relative Flux, Not Unsigned Density

For the bilateral valuation basis, positive and negative cones share the
vacuum and are exchanged by reflection. Their depth-`k` unilateral defect
projections are both positive and both have rank `k`.

Three combinations differ:

- direct-sum density has rank `2k`;
- their union identifies the vacuum and has rank `2k-1`;
- oriented relative flux is the anti-diagonal pair `(P_k^+,-P_k^-)` and has
  zero total index without being zero as relative data.

The Euler logarithmic derivative selects the last type because scale reversal
changes the sign of a derivative. Fourier therefore reverses the current’s
orientation, not the positivity of either defect projection.

The common vacuum cancels in the oriented difference. It is seam incidence;
the depth-two and higher cells remain on the two reduced arms. The outstanding
RH gate is the archimedean pairing of this relative flux.
