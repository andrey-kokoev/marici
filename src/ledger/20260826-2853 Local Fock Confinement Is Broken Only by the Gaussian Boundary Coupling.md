# 2853 — Local Fock Confinement Is Broken Only by the Gaussian Boundary Coupling

On a (p)-valuation chain, the occupation shift and quadratic scale satisfy

\[
Q_pS_p=p^2S_pQ_p.
\]

The canonical half-density Fock polynomial is geometric, so all its finite
zeros lie on (operatorname{Re}s=1/2), and its infinite right-sector limit is
zero-free. Gaussian sampling changes the weights to

\[
w_k(t)=e^{-\pi t p^{2k}},
\]

and the first two-level zero moves strictly off the seam. Thus the local Fock
chain and Gaussian vacuum are individually well behaved; finite confinement
is first lost in their rigid mixed coupling. The next gate is the compatibility
of the full commuting prime family with (Q) and reciprocal completion.
