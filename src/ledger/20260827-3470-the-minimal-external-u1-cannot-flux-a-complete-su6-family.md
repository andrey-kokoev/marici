# 3470 — The Minimal External U(1) Cannot Flux a Complete SU(6) Family

## Claim

For \(U(1)_F\) charges \(x,y,z\) on the \(SU(6)\) family
\(15+\overline6+\overline6\), ordinary mixed \(SU(6)^2U(1)_F\) and
gravitational anomaly cancellation gives

\[
4x+y+z=0,
\qquad
15x+6y+6z=0.
\]

Therefore

\[
x=0,
\qquad
z=-y.
\]

The cubic anomaly then cancels identically. The primitive integral charge
lattice is only \((0,1,-1)\) and its reverse.

At flux three, the signed indices are

\[
(0,3,-3).
\]

The \(15\) has no chiral zero modes and the antifundamentals form an
oppositely oriented vectorlike pair. The dimension-weighted linear charge also
vanishes, so the packet cannot supply the nonzero oriented tadpole source
required by WP778.

## Classification

The minimal external \(U(1)_F\) is neither a complete-family selector nor a
tadpole selector. A surviving source must add \(SU(6)\)-charged matter or an
independently derived Green--Schwarz sector, and must prove that the
enlargement forces rather than accommodates both the common-sign family
charge and the oriented tadpole.
