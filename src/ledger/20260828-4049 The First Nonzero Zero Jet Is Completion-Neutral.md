# The First Nonzero Zero Jet Is Completion-Neutral

For \(X=AZ\) with \(A\) holomorphic and nowhere zero,

\[
\mathscr C_X
=
A^2\mathscr C_Z+Z^2\mathscr C_A.
\]

At a zero of \(Z\), the archimedean term vanishes. More generally, if the zero
has multiplicity \(m\),

\[
X^{(m)}(s_0)=A(s_0)Z^{(m)}(s_0),
\]

while all lower derivatives vanish.

Thus the first nonzero local jet at a zero is completion-neutral up to a
nowhere-zero frame factor. Archimedean completion can constrain zero transport
or a global boundary problem, but cannot provide pointwise force at the zero.
