# RH Is Continuity of Connected Realization, Not Existence of the Euler Logarithm

The formal Euler object always has a plethystic logarithm:

\[
H\log Z
=
\sum_{p,k\ge1}(\log p)X_p^k.
\]

Realizing \(X_p^k\) at the logarithmic atom \(k\log p\), subtracting the
continuum current \(e^u du\), and twisting by \(e^{-u/2}\) gives

\[
\tau=e^{-u/2}
\left(
\sum_n\Lambda(n)\delta_{\log n}-e^u du
\right).
\]

For the cumulative discrepancy
\(q(u)=e^{-u/2}(\psi(e^u)-e^u)\),

\[
(\partial_u+1/2)q=\tau
\]

on the positive ray, with an explicit origin delta after extension by zero.
Hence \(q\) is tempered exactly when this connected boundary realization is
tempered.

The missing RH theorem is therefore continuity of formal connectedization
through completed theta realization into the half-normalized tempered
rigging. Formal existence of the Euler logarithm is automatic and has no
zero-selection force.
