# The Two Erased Orientation Bits Live in Different Algebraic Layers

The endpoint orientation bit distinguishing \(A_+\) from \(A_-\) occurs only
in the terminal full Smith factor; their relational Smith packets are
identical. It is a gauge-extension bit.

The ordered-tail bit distinguishing \(C_+\) from \(C_-\) changes both terminal
full and relational Smith factors. It is a relational bit. Their exact
full-to-relational terminal ratios are respectively \(28^2\) and \(36^2\).

Thus 2-adic reduction erases two bits of different algebraic types, not two
homogeneous binary coordinates.
