# 3744 — The Faithful Theta Chart Lies on a Source Lorentz Conic

Let

\[
R^2=I_+I_-,
\qquad
\chi=\frac12\log\frac{I_+}{I_-}.
\]

Then (M=R\cosh\chi), (N=R\sinh\chi), and the faithful completed
coordinates satisfy the exact source conic

\[
\left(S-\frac12\right)^2-A^2=4P^2I_+I_-.
\]

Thus the carrier-centered scalar displacement and the antisymmetric residual
are components of one Lorentz vector. The reciprocal-invariant radius (R)
and reciprocal-odd rapidity \(\chi\) are the two kinds of information merged
by scalar aggregation.

On the critical seam, (A) is imaginary. With (Y=-iA), the conic becomes

\[
\left(S-\frac12\right)^2+Y^2
=4P(i\tau)^2|I_+(i\tau)|^2.
\]

This is the precise circle suggested by the operator: the norm circle of the
completed scalar displacement and residual Fourier quadrature. Its radius
varies with spectral height. It is not a circle traced by zero locations.

At a zero, the source conic meets the affine slice (S=0), giving

\[
\frac14-A^2=4P^2I_+I_-.
\]

The remaining RH theorem must prevent this intersection under nonzero boost.
That requires a joint theta-specific constraint on radial and rapidity
evolution; neither reciprocal symmetry nor the conic invariant alone can do
so.
