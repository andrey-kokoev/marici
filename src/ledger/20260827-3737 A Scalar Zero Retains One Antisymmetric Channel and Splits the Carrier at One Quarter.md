# 3737 — A Scalar Zero Retains One Antisymmetric Channel and Splits the Carrier at One Quarter

Retain the coordinates

\[
S=C+U+V,
\qquad
A=U-V,
\qquad
B=2C-U-V,
\]

with \(C=1/2\). Then \(S+B=3/2\) identically. At every scalar zero,

\[
S=0,
\qquad
B=\frac32,
\qquad
U=-\frac14+\frac A2,
\qquad
V=-\frac14-\frac A2.
\]

Thus the zero fiber is an affine line carrying the antisymmetric residual
channel \(A\); the complete relational state does not vanish.

On the critical line reciprocal conjugation forces \(A\) to be purely
imaginary. The quarter offset is therefore source-derived: the completion
carrier \(1/2\) is split equally between the two reciprocal sectors at
cancellation.

The faithful zero-state chart is \((S,A)\). RH asks that every intersection
with \(S=0\) occur over the reciprocal-conjugation fixed locus. Pure
imaginarity of \(A\) is its observable residual signature, though the converse
requires a separate source-faithfulness theorem.
