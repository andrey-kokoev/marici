# The Lower Cech Lift and Endpoint Cells Are One Beck--Chevalley Datum

## Result

Entry 378 closes the one-road coefficient mismatch, but it cannot promote
the already constructed relative Gysin thimble to the full primal trace by
discarding the lower Cech term.  The exact pre-quotient identity from the
Entry-356 carrier is

\[
d(H_{\rm Morse}p-\widetilde\xi h_3)
=q_Jp-d\widetilde\xi\,h_3.
\]

Therefore any chain realization that sends
\(d\widetilde\xi\,h_3\) to zero also sends \(q_Jp\) to a boundary.  Its
generic \(Q\)-class is then zero.  Multiplying the generic coefficient by
the forced Rees factor \(x_3\) does not repair this: a scalar multiple of a
boundary remains a boundary.

Consequently the following three obligations from Entry 378 are not
sequentially independent:

1. lifting the gallery through the lower Cech terms;
2. constructing the mixed-variance Beck--Chevalley homotopy; and
3. attaching the endpoint connector cells.

They are the three faces of one cell.  A viable realization must carry
\(d\widetilde\xi\,h_3\) to the endpoint/overlap boundary in the literal
Entry-143 target while carrying \(q_Jp\) nontrivially to its generic
\(Q03\) leg.

## Proof

Let \(C=H_{\rm Morse}p-\widetilde\xi h_3\).  The full pulled-back loaded
differential and occurrence-Koszul totalization, independently checked in
`check_d03_pabs_morse_pullback.rs`, give

\[
dC=q_Jp-d\widetilde\xi\,h_3.
\]

For any chain map \(F\),

\[
F(q_Jp)-F(d\widetilde\xi\,h_3)=dF(C).
\]

If \(F(d\widetilde\xi\,h_3)=0\), then \(F(q_Jp)=dF(C)\), so the generic
class of \(F(q_Jp)\) vanishes.  The same holds for the Entry-378 coefficient:

\[
F(x_3q_Jp)=d(x_3F(C))
\]

because \(x_3\) is a closed coefficient.  Thus the Rees bridge fixes the
generic-to-Cartier coefficient comparison but cannot substitute for the
lower Cech/endpoint image.

Conversely, a nonzero generic class requires the second term to survive as
the other boundary face of the same homotopy.  In the actual carrier,
\(d\widetilde\xi\) consists of the two endpoint-supported terms.  Hence the
endpoint connectors are not decorations to attach after a one-road
homotopy is built; they are required for that homotopy to have a nonzero
generic face.

## Consequence

The next construction should not attempt a lower-term-free map or an
endpoint-free one-road trace.  It must define a single morphism on the
small subcomplex generated by

\[
H_{\rm Morse}p-\widetilde\xi h_3,
\qquad q_Jp,
\qquad d\widetilde\xi\,h_3,
\]

into the generic, overlap, and two endpoint rows of the literal Entry-143
BM--Cech object.  The acceptance equation is the image of the displayed
boundary identity.  Only after this three-face cell exists is it meaningful
to ask for the remaining global \(D_3\) and reflection assembly.

## Scope

This proves a dependency/no-go theorem for the existing pre-quotient
carrier.  It does not construct the mixed-variance realization, endpoint
maps, primal trace, or global symmetry square.  It also does not weaken
Entry 378: the coefficient \(x_3\) and its Cartier symbol \(+1\) remain the
forced generic and closed grades.
