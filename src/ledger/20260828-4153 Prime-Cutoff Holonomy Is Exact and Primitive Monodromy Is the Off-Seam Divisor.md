# Prime-Cutoff Holonomy Is Exact and Primitive Monodromy Is the Off-Seam Divisor

The local Euler section has the normalized decomposition

$$
(1-p^{-s})^{-1}
=\exp\left(p^{-s}+\frac12p^{-2s}\right)
\det_3(I-p^{-s})^{-1}.
$$

Distinct prime additions commute exactly and every local component tends to
one at the source basepoint. Hence every two-prime square has identity
holonomy, not merely constant holonomy.

In $\operatorname{Re}s>1/2$, Möbius inversion shows that
$P(s)-\log\zeta(s)$ is locally single-valued and holomorphic. Therefore the
integer monodromy of the primitive current counts the off-seam zeta divisor
exactly, without scaled ghost singularities.

The remaining RH theorem is open-sector triviality of this normalized
primitive-current holonomy after archimedean and reciprocal completion,
derived from the source rather than from $\zeta'/\zeta$.
