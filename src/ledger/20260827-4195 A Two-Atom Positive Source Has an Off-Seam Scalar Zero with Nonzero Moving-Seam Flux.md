# 4195 — A Two-Atom Positive Source Has an Off-Seam Scalar Zero with Nonzero Moving-Seam Flux

Take positive source weights \((1,2)\) at positions \((0,1)\). With \(w=e^z\),

\[
F(z)=1+2w
\]

vanishes at \(w=-1/2\), hence \(z=-\log2+i\pi\), off the centered seam. Its
odd autocorrelation flux is

\[
\mathcal K(z)=4\sinh z=3.
\]

Thus source positivity, two-port retention, the direct ternary moving-seam
cell, and exact chart gluing do not imply that scalar nullity annihilates total
flux.

For general positive two-atom weights \((a,b)\), the zero flux is
\(b^2-a^2\), vanishing exactly when the weights balance and the zero lies on
the unitary seam. The remaining RH theorem must be a theta-specific endpoint
law forbidding unequal reciprocal cancellation.
