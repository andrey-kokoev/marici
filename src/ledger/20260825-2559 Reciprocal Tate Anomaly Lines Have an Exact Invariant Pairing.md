# 2559 — Reciprocal Tate Anomaly Lines Have an Exact Invariant Pairing

The finite-place transition obeys

\[
\gamma_p(1-\bar s)={1\over\overline{\gamma_p(s)}}.
\]

Consequently the pairing

\[
\mathcal P_X(x,y)=x\bar y
\]

between reciprocal cutoff lines is preserved exactly by every pair of bonding
maps. Off-seam expansion in one metric and contraction in the other therefore
do not destroy the relationship: the two lines are dual metric
polarizations.

The pairing is perfect and nowhere zero on transported nonzero vectors. It is
therefore a transition unit, not the distinguished zero-bearing section. The
Riemann divisor must arise from an additional source-derived incidence state.

archimedean incidence, prove its cutoff covariance, or confine zeros.
