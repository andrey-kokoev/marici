# The Prime-Diagonal det3 Is Canonical but the Divisor Lives in the Primitive Current

On $\ell^2(\mathbb P)$ define $K_se_p=p^{-s}e_p$. Then

$$
K_s\in\mathcal S_3
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Re}s>\frac13,
$$

while $K_s$ is not Hilbert--Schmidt on the critical seam. In the Euler chamber,

$$
\zeta(s)
=\exp\left(P(s)+\frac12P(2s)\right)
\det_3(I-K_s)^{-1}.
$$

This realizes the primitive, square, and $k\ge3$ grades exactly. But
$\det_3(I-K_s)$ is zero-free for $\operatorname{Re}s>1/3$. The Riemann divisor
is instead encoded by logarithmic branching of the analytically continued
primitive prime current $P(s)$.

The remaining RH gate is therefore a source-derived theorem converting
primitive-current monodromy into the index of a fixed self-adjoint completed
operator, with the square current and regularized determinant tail fixing the
branch and anomaly data.
