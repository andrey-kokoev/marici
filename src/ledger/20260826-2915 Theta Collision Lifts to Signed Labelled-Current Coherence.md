Before winding-label aggregation, each theta summand carries the signed current

\[
d\mu_{n,L}(t)=-\phi_n'(t)\,dt+phi_n(L)\delta_L(dt)
\]

and

\[
izH_{n,L}(z)=\int(e^{izt}-1)\,d\mu_{n,L}(t).
\]

Only the codiagonal sum is positive:

\[
\sum_n d\mu_{n,L}=-\Phi'(t)\,dt+Phi(L)\delta_L(dt)>0.
\]

Writing

\[
g_{n,L}(x)=\int\sin(xt)\,d\mu_{n,L}(t),
\]

the double-zero collision is exactly

\[
\sum_n g_{n,L}(x)=0,
\qquad
\sum_n g_{n,L}'(x)=0.
\]

Thus scalar minimum phase is created after the same aggregation that erases
the signed label information.  The remaining theorem is a cross-label descent
law transporting integral-square orientation through differentiation,
oscillatory integration, and codiagonalization.
