# 3654 — The Endpoint Dagger Tower Defines a Toeplitz-Cartan Spectral Triple

## Theorem

Let

\[
\mathscr H=\bigoplus_{l\geq0}H_l
\]

and let \(S_a:H_l\to H_{l+1}\) be normalized Cartan multiplication by an axis
vector \(a\in H_1\). Define

\[
D|_{H_l}=\frac{l+1}{2}I_{H_l},
\]

where \(D\) is the reciprocal of the dagger-curvature defect from Entry 3650.
Let \(\mathcal A_C\) be the unital star algebra generated by the bounded shifts
\(S_a\) and their Hilbert adjoints.

Then

\[
(\mathcal A_C,\mathscr H,D)
\]

is an odd spectral triple in the minimal analytic sense.

The shifts are uniformly bounded because Cartan multiplication is a slice of
an orthogonal projection. Their commutators are

\[
[D,S_a]=\frac12S_a,
\qquad
[D,S_a^\dagger]=-rac12S_a^\dagger.
\]

The Leibniz rule gives bounded commutators for every element of
\(\mathcal A_C\). The eigenvalues of \(D\) diverge with finite multiplicities,
so its resolvent is compact.

## Exact spectral zeta function

Since \((l+1)/2\) has multiplicity \(2l+1\),

\[
\zeta_D(s)
=
\operatorname{tr}(D^{-s})
=
2^s\left(2\zeta(s-1)-\zeta(s)\right).
\]

The series converges for \(\operatorname{Re}s>2\). The spectral triple is
finitely summable for every exponent greater than two and has metric dimension
two.

## Explanatory gain

The reciprocal curvature is not merely an externally imposed grade counter.
It is the unbounded operator naturally paired with the Cartan shifts:

- the dagger round trip produces \(D^{-1}\);
- Cartan multiplication produces bounded raising operators;
- linear grade spacing produces bounded commutators;
- harmonic multiplicities produce dimension two.

Thus the algebraic tower, dagger curvature, and spectral dimension assemble
into one analytic object.

## Exact failure boundary

The spectral triple is Toeplitz-like. It is not yet a reconstruction of the
commutative celestial sphere, and it is not yet a geometric Dirac spectral
triple.

No claim is made for a Clifford action, real structure, orienting Hochschild
cycle, opposite-algebra first-order condition, or Poincare duality. Those are
separate source gates.

A nonlinear grade operator would generally make first differences unbounded;
a sublinear one would change the spectral dimension. The linear reciprocal
curvature is the structure that satisfies both gates simultaneously.
