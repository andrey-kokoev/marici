# The Additive and Multiplicative Vacua Are Disjoint Half-Density Sectors

The finite additive-to-multiplicative half-density is

\[
h_Q
=
\sqrt{\frac{Q}{\varphi(Q)}}
\mathbf 1_{(x,Q)=1}.
\]

It has norm one, but its overlap with the additive vacuum is

\[
\langle1,h_Q\rangle
=
\sqrt{\frac{\varphi(Q)}{Q}}
\longrightarrow0.
\]

It also converges weakly to zero against every fixed-conductor cylinder
observable while retaining unit norm. Equivalently, the global product of
local half-density overlaps is zero.

Thus additive Haar and multiplicative idelic Haar define disjoint
infinite-product sectors. Their bridge is a correspondence with a relative
determinant line, not a bounded vector inside additive \(L^2\).
