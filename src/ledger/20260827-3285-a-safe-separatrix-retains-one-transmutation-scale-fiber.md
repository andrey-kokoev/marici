# 3285 — A Safe Separatrix Retains One Transmutation-Scale Fiber

Established gauge–Yukawa theories can possess an interacting ultraviolet
fixed point and a one-dimensional safe critical surface. For the effective
separatrix flow

\[
\frac{d\alpha}{dt}=-B\alpha^2+C\alpha^3,
\]

the fixed point and projective coupling ratios are calculable, but every
solution retains a translation constant \(t_0\), equivalently an RG-invariant
crossover scale. At a fixed experimental energy,

\[
\frac{d}{dt_0}\left[\alpha(C_n-C_m)\right]
=\alpha^2(B-C\alpha)(C_n-C_m),
\]

which is generically nonzero. The safe separatrix therefore selects the
trajectory shape but not the low-energy numerical portal. A detector may
identify the crossover scale; that does not make the scale source-derived.
