\[
r=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\end{pmatrix},

(ker r). Equivalently, every arrow is lower triangular. Every future readout

line and they cannot be jointly faithful.

do not observe its hidden flux. A reciprocal swap or Clark derivative can
observe that flux only by violating zero propagation.

propagation with joint faithfulness on the full linear state space. The likely
replacement is a global conservation law for the nonzero transverse current
created at a scalar zero.
