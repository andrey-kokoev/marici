For the doubled half-tail system

\[
H_+'=-zH_+-f,
\qquad
H_-'=zH_--f,
\]

a completed theta zero imposes

\[
H_-(0,z)=-H_+(0,z),
\]

not separate Dirichlet conditions on the two tails.  The full state may remain
nonzero while its scalar seam aggregation vanishes.

With

\[
N=|H_+|^2+|H_-|^2,
\qquad
J=|H_+|^2-|H_-|^2,
\]

the exact Green identity is

\[
2\Re(z)N
=
-J'-2f\Re(H_+-H_-).
\]

The antidiagonal zero condition and tail decay make the flux vanish at both
endpoints.  The remaining RH-bearing obstruction is therefore the single
integrated forcing term.
