# Entry 1227 — Generic Five-Site Pair Collisions Are Regular Marked Intersections

## Residual locus

Entry 1226 found the residual norm-discriminant loci

\[
F_i-F_j=0
\]

away from $\det H=0$ and the Kummer branches $F_iF_j=0$. Resolve one source-labelled generic point on this locus before forgetting the two sheets.

Choose local roots

\[
y_i^2=R_i,
\qquad
y_j^2=R_j,
\]

with $y_i+y_j$ a unit. Then

\[
\varepsilon
=
y_i-y_j
=
\frac{R_i-R_j}{y_i+y_j}.
\]

Thus $\varepsilon$ is a linear local parameter times $R_i-R_j$; it is not its square root.

## Labelled local model

The two colliding signed walls are

\[
q_+=X+\varepsilon,
\qquad
q_-=X-\varepsilon.
\]

Their Jacobian in $(X,\varepsilon)$ is

\[
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1\\
1&-1
\end{pmatrix}
=-2.
\]

Therefore they form a regular transverse complete intersection. The ordered Koszul complex

\[
0
\longrightarrow R
\xrightarrow{(-q_-,q_+)}
R^2
\xrightarrow{(q_+,q_-)}
R
\longrightarrow0
\]

has $d^2=0$ and no excess homology away from its declared intersection support.

## Inertia and occurrence character

The order-two discriminant of the orbit norm does not produce square-root parameter monodromy: a loop around $R_i-R_j=0$ returns $\varepsilon$ to itself on the chosen Kummer chart.

Exchanging the two occurrence labels acts by the permutation representation in Koszul degree one and by

\[
-1
\]

on their ordered top wedge. Hence the supported coefficient is the ordinary anti-invariant Tate/Koszul intersection line already generated by marked incidence.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\text{generic }F_i=F_j\text{ collision}
=
\text{regular labelled marked intersection with trivial parameter inertia}.
}
\]

There is no excess class and no new carrier datum.
