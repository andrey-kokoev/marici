# 3793 — Spin(5) Anomaly Cancellation Does Not Define One Beta System

The simple-parent portal packet has at least two inequivalent anomaly-free
completions on the half-integer hypercharge lattice. Per family they are

\[
4_{+1/2}\oplus5_{-1}
\]

and

\[
4_{-3/2}\oplus1_0\oplus1_{+1}\oplus1_{+2}.
\]

Both cancel the mixed Spin(5)-hypercharge, gravitational-hypercharge, cubic
hypercharge, and fundamental-spinor parity obstructions of the frozen portal
packet. Their three-family Spin(5) fermion indices differ by three.

Including the spinor Higgs and the two real fundamental breaking fields of
WP877 gives

\[
b_0^A=\frac92,
\qquad
b_0^B=\frac{13}{2}.
\]

Thus the anomaly probe identifies two spectra that the one-loop RG probe
separates by exactly two. Both are asymptotically free at this order, so
anomaly completion supplies neither one authorized beta system nor a nonzero
ultraviolet normalization of the common portal coefficient.

## Scope

This is a nonuniqueness theorem for the admitted local anomalies and
fundamental-spinor parity test. It does not construct full Yukawa grammars,
two-loop fixed points, mass generation for the chiral completion, finite
thresholds, or a calibrated physical instrument. Phenomenological exclusion
of high-charge singlets would be an additional probe, not an anomaly identity.
