# 3917 — The BSY Bound Is an Euler Renormalization Anomaly

For the scale-`1/2` Cauchy variable `T` and finite Euler product `Z_X`, an
exact source calculation gives

\[
\mathbb E\log|Z_X(T)|
=
\sum_{p\le X}-\log(1-p^{-1})
=
\log\log X+\gamma+o(1).
\]

Finite Euler log-energy therefore diverges positively, while analytic
completion produces the finite BSY divisor entropy. The cutoff limit,
logarithm, critical-boundary continuation, Cauchy expectation, and completion
renormalization do not commute. In particular, the desired BSY upper bound
cannot follow by termwise passage from finite Euler products.

The RH-sensitive target is the typed commutator anomaly between
boundary expectation and a source-authorized, boundary-bearing Euler
renormalization. Ordinary Mertens subtraction removes the common divergence
but does not retain the off-line divisor information.

## Scope

obstruction. It does not construct or orient the required anomaly and does
not prove RH.
