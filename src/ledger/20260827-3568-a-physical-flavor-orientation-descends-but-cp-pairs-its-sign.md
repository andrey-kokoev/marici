# A Physical Flavor Orientation Descends but CP Pairs Its Sign

The \(2\times2\) Yukawa plaquette phase is invariant under diagonal field
rephasings but not under the full weak-basis groupoid. An exact unitary left
rotation preserves \(Y^\dagger Y\) while changing its plaquette product from
\(i\) to zero. It is chart data rather than a faithful physical coordinate.

For two noncommuting three-generation Hermitian flavor operators, the
Jarlskog-type invariant

\[
J_{\rm phys}=\operatorname{Im}\det[H_u,H_d]
\]

does descend under simultaneous weak-basis conjugation. The tested packet has
\(J_{\rm phys}=4\sqrt3/3\), while CP conjugation gives
\(-4\sqrt3/3\).

A CP-even source can select its magnitude but not its orientation. The exact
potential

\[
V(\Phi)=-\cos\Phi+\cos2\Phi
\]

has stable degenerate minima at
\(\Phi=\pm\arccos(1/4)\). A CP-odd coefficient lifts the pair but is itself a
new source datum unless derived internally.
