# The Smallest Controlled Local Tate Carrier Has Three States and Is Flat

At one finite place, with the standard self-dual Tate normalization, the
normalized ball states

\[
b_k=p^{k/2}\mathbf 1_{p^k\mathbb Z_p}
\]

satisfy

\[
\mathcal F_p b_k=b_{-k}.
\]

The smallest Fourier-closed carrier containing one reciprocal pair and a
native common-mode control is therefore

\[
V_p^{(3)}=\operatorname{span}\{b_{-1},b_0,b_1\}.
\]

In that basis the source-derived transport reverses the outer pair and fixes
the unramified middle state. It has two even channels, one odd channel, and
squares exactly to the identity.

This supplies the phase-framed `2+1` local architecture without inserting a
convenient discrete Fourier matrix. It also closes the local RH route: the
backtracking holonomy is trivial, so the one-place normalized interface has
no irreducible residue once inverse transport is an authorized filling.

Any RH-bearing class must arise from a nonexact global coupling absent from
the one-place carrier: restricted-product boundary currents, archimedean
coupling, a nonfillable multi-place route, or failure of completed homotopies
to assemble.
