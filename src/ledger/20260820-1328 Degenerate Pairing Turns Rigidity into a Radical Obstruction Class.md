# Degenerate Pairing Turns Rigidity into a Radical Obstruction Class

Epistemic-graph event: 1345.

Let `R` be the right radical of a Stokes-compatible target
coefficient--Betti pairing.  Pairing-preserving changes of a degreewise
adjoint Betti candidate are exactly radical-valued maps `C`.

A chain-map repair exists precisely when the boundary defect first vanishes
in the quotient complex `C(H)/R` and then represents zero in

`H^(-1)(Hom(C(G),R))`.

Equivalently, after the visible defect vanishes, one must solve
`delta C=-Omega` inside the radical.  A nonzero quotient defect or nonzero
radical obstruction class falsifies every pairing-preserving repair.  When
the pairing is perfect, `R=0` and this reduces to the rigid condition
`Omega=0`.

The formal five-site sheet pairing is perfect, but the unconstructed physical
relative pairing need not be presumed so.  Its eventual audit must either
prove perfection or compute this radical obstruction.
