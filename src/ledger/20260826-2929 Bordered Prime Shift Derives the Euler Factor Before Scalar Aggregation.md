On a finite \(p\)-valuation chain, let \(S\) be the forward shift,
\(D_N(q)=I-qS\), \(e_0\) the primitive vacuum, and \(\ell_N(e_k)=1\) the
augmentation boundary.  Then

\[
\mathcal M_N(q)
=
\begin{pmatrix}
D_N(q)&e_0\\
\ell_N&0
\end{pmatrix}
\]

satisfies

\[
\det\mathcal M_N(q)
=
-\ell_ND_N(q)^{-1}e_0
=
-\sum_{k=0}^Nq^k.
\]

For \(q=p^{-s}\), the local Euler factor is therefore derived by eliminating
a source-local prime shift with its boundary ports retained.  No Euler scalar
is inserted as a matrix entry.

This is the first forward determinant bridge.  Its infinite assembly is
necessarily a rigged, boundary-bearing relative determinant because the
augmentation is distributional and the prime product requires primitive and
square-current regularization.
