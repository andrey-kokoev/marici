# 1398 — CCM Semilocal Scaling Quotient Stops Before the Xi Determinant

Sequence claim: `seqclaim-4ba8ad8af7897580aca929e3`.

Epistemic-graph event: 1443.

Connes--Consani--Moscovici provide the closest known realization of our
Weil-radical/scaling architecture.  Their archimedean source map factors under
Mellin transform as

\[
\mathcal F_\mu(E\psi_j^\pm)(s)=R_j^\pm(s)\Xi(s),
\]

and multiplication by (s) on the resulting quotient of the Hadamard
topological ring has spectrum equal to the nontrivial zeta zeros.  This is a
genuine algebraic/topological spectral quotient, not a zero-seeded numerical
fit.

For every finite set (S) of places, their semilocal Hardy--Titchmarsh
transform also gives the exact multiplier

\[
\prod_{p\in S\setminus\{\infty\}}L_p(1/2-is)
\]

and a canonical Hilbert realization of the ambient self-adjoint scaling pair.

These facts do not yet coexist on the operator required here.  Exact zero
recovery is proved on an entire-function quotient; self-adjointness belongs to
the ambient finite-place Hilbert scaling operator; and the prolate comparison
is a limiting infrared/ultraviolet strategy.  No compact-resolvent realization
or determinant identity with completed Xi is proved.

The smallest obstruction is (S=\{\infty,p\}): its Hilbert pair is unchanged
when an omitted prime (q) is varied, while completed Xi changes by the
nonconstant factor (L_q).  A global claim therefore requires an independently
controlled all-places limit preserving domains, self-adjointness, compact
resolvent, and determinants.

What survives is important: the CCM construction validates the analytic
provenance of our Weil radical quotient and supplies exact finite-place
approximants.  It neither proves Weil/Pick positivity nor supplies the separate
physical coefficient--Betti relative-chain pushforward.
