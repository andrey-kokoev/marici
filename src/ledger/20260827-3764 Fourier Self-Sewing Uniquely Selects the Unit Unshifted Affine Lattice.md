# 3764 — Fourier Self-Sewing Uniquely Selects the Unit Unshifted Affine Lattice

right-endpoint Gaussian quantization current. Fourier transport gives

\[
\mathcal F\Delta_{a\mathbb Z+\beta}
=a^{-1}e^{-2\pi i\beta\xi}\Delta_{a^{-1}\mathbb Z}.
\]

translation becomes a dual character, and then forces (a=1), because scale
becomes reciprocal scale.

positive quantization with same-object Fourier sewing. Scale and origin are
separate defect channels coupled by the Fourier control operation.

This excludes translated and rescaled combs but not weighted combs or more
general Fourier pairs. The next hostile family should retain support
