# The Full Weyl Determinant Is Nonzero at a Cross-Port Zero

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

For the canonical two-port Weyl compression

\[
M(z)=
\begin{pmatrix}
u^*R(z)u&u^*R(z)v\\
v^*R(z)u&v^*R(z)v
\end{pmatrix},
\]

the theta readout is the cross entry. At a cross zero, the determinant is the
product of the two diagonal Herglotz responses, and both are nonzero off the
real spectral axis. Therefore the full passive determinant is necessarily
nonzero at an off-axis theta zero.

The physical divisor must be a relative determinant, constrained Schur
complement, or nonstandard self-adjoint Evans boundary condition—not the
ordinary full Weyl determinant.
