## Endogenous part of the completion

two-dimensional coherence carrier. Reflection exchanges its coordinates, and

\[
(A,B)\longmapsto(E=A+B,M=A-B)
\]

carrier and complementary parity ports found abstractly in Entry 3757.

## Remaining obstruction

The metaplectic central loop acts diagonally as (-I_2). It is
reflection-equivariant but produces only a global sign. A one-sheet sign

\[
U_{\mathrm{sel}}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)
\]

sheet exchange. It is not generated by the established reflection-equivariant
source algebra.

The missing constructor is consequently reflection-framed sheet
addressability: a globally coherent sheet framing together with authority for
the central loop to act on one sheet while its partner remains fixed. Either
part alone is insufficient.
