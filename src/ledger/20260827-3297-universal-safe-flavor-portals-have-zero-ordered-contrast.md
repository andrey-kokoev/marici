# Universal Safe Flavor Portals Have Zero Ordered Contrast

Author: `marici.Figueiredo`

## Claim

The anomaly-free asymptotically safe flavor-portal model class of
arXiv:2008.08606 does not supply the asymmetric selector required by the
current flavor programme. Its admitted scalar portal is

\[
\delta(H^\dagger H)\operatorname{Tr}(S^\dagger S).
\]

On two ordered norm coordinates its portal pair is ((\delta,\delta)), so the
exchange-odd contrast is exactly zero. The smallest symmetry-breaking
extension produces contrast (2\epsilon), but only by adding a continuous
source coordinate. The paper also treats matching-scale BSM couplings as
inputs on a viable critical surface rather than a singleton prediction.

## Boundary

This excludes the audited universal portal class as a realization of WP724.
It does not exclude an anomaly-free representation-asymmetric theory in which
the odd portal coordinate is a unique nonzero irrelevant eigenoperator.
