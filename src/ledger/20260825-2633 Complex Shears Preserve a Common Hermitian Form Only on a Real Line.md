# 2633 — Complex Shears Preserve a Common Hermitian Form Only on a Real Line

A coefficient family of nontrivial shears

\[
S(F)=\begin{pmatrix}1&F\\0&1\end{pmatrix}
\]

preserves one fixed nondegenerate Hermitian form exactly when all nonzero
coefficients lie on one real line in \(\mathbf C\). Every such form is
indefinite: it has determinant \(-|b|^2<0\). The two coefficients \(1,i\)
are the smallest hostile family and force every common invariant Hermitian form
to be degenerate.

Every complex shear nevertheless preserves the complex bilinear alternating
form under transpose. Determinant-one complex symplectic preservation is not
Hermitian passivity.

A nonconstant holomorphic coefficient family on a connected open domain cannot
lie on one real line. It therefore cannot preserve one fixed nondegenerate
rank-two Hermitian form. Any theta/Tate conservative structure must be derived
from boundary currents, be parameter-dependent, or enlarge the realization.

## Scope

This is an exact rank-two algebraic theorem. It does not derive a theta metric,
boundary current, larger conservative realization, passivity, KYP positivity,
or RH.
