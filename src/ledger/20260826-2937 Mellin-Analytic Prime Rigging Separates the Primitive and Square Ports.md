The prime-label scale

\[
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{an}}
=
\bigcap_{\delta>0}
\ell^2(\mathbb P,p^{2\delta})
\subset
\ell^2(\mathbb P)
\subset
\bigcup_{\delta>0}
\ell^2(\mathbb P,p^{-2\delta})
\]

is generated by analytic displacement in the Mellin label \(\log p\).  The
primitive cocycle belongs naturally to its distributional dual, the square
cocycle belongs to the Hilbert middle in \(|\Re z|<1/4\), and the connected
tail is absolutely summable in \(|\Re z|<1/6\).

Mellin translation and reciprocal reflection are isometric on every grade.
There is no canonical inclusion of the primitive dual port into the Hilbert
port.  Thus the naive primitive-plus-square operator that produces Nima's
negative prime-five minor is not an operation of the typed source rigging.

The remaining construction is an archimedean, source-derived, closable
incidence relation between the two ports, with its graph domain and boundary
current retained explicitly.
