# The Primal-Dual Cross-History Port Vanishes after Poisson Sewing

For two histories, the exchange-odd comparison is already a determinant of
their complete transform vectors:

\[
L_{f,g}(z)
=\frac12\bigl(
M_f(-z)M_g(z)-M_f(z)M_g(-z)
\bigr).
\]

In the completed theta construction, Fourier--Poisson sewing places the
primal and dual histories in one even self-dual frame. The Gaussian source and
integer comb are Fourier-fixed, so \(g=\mathcal Ff=f\) and

\[
L_{f,\mathcal Ff}(z)=0.
\]

Before completion, the primal--dual mismatch is exactly carried by the two
bulk and two polar-boundary coordinates of the four-channel Poisson packet.
Cross determinants are therefore derived product testers at that level, not
independent joint observables.

The surviving search must move beyond pairs of archimedean histories to the
labelled arithmetic boundary module, where primitive, prime-square, and
connected-tail constructors retain information erased by the completed
transform packet.
