# The Square Observer Lives on the Cyclic Two-Copy Source

The proposed primitive and prime-square observer maps cannot both be linear
components on the one-copy additive theta carrier. At finite cutoff,

\[
\tau_1(A)=\operatorname{Tr}A,
\qquad
\tau_2(A)=\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(A^2),
\]

and

\[
\tau_2(A+B)
=\tau_2(A)+\tau_2(B)+\operatorname{Tr}(AB).
\]

The square current therefore linearizes only after lifting to the cyclic
two-copy source. The correct map is the symmetrized cyclic pairing on
`Sym^2 A`; its diagonal recovers the second cumulant and forces the usual
factor `1/2`.

This retypes the global observer as

\[
A
\longrightarrow
\left(A,\operatorname{Sym}^2A,\operatorname{Cyc}^{\ge3}A\right)
\longrightarrow
\left(\tau_1,\tau_2,\det_3\right)
\longrightarrow
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{rel}}.
\]

Prime dilation of the labelled Gaussian transports the one-copy and tensor
copy carriers exactly. It does not yet construct the global Fourier action on
the Euler cyclic loop. The surviving gate is a source-derived
Fourier--cyclic comparison, with seam and archimedean incidence retained,
before determinant reconstruction or cutoff analysis.
