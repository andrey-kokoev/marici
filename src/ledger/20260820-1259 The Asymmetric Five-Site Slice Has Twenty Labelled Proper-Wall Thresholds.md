## Individual proper-region walls

For a connected proper cyclic region \(A\), the marked wall is

\[
\mathcal E_A=|A|t+y_{\rm left}+y_{\rm right}.
\]

The one-wall stationary endpoint condition gives

\[
\boxed{|A|^2t^2-P_A^2=0},
\qquad
P_A=\sum_{i\in A}P_i.
\]

The exact labelled census is

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
|A|&A&P_A^2\\ \hline
1&1,2,3,4,5&1,1,1,14,29\\
2&12,23,34,45,51&2,2,21,3,26\\
3&123,234,345,451,512&3,26,2,2,21\\
4&1234,2345,3451,4512,5123&29,1,1,1,14
\end{array}
\]

Thus the twenty labelled polynomials are retained separately even when some
happen to coincide numerically. Complementary regions have equal
\(P_A^2\), but their coefficients \(|A|^2\) differ.

The physical-sheet root is

\[
t=-\frac{|P_A|}{|A|}.
\]

## Remaining six walls

The total-energy wall gives \(5t=0\). Each of the five one-cut total walls

\[
5t+2y_e=0
\]

meets its soft endpoint \(y_e=0\) at \(t=0\). Hence all six remaining
individual-wall supports reduce to the existing total-energy/soft locus.

## Physical-domain check

Entry 1257's literal real domain is \(t\ge\sqrt{29}\). Every proper-wall
physical-sheet root is negative, while every positive signed-continuation
root satisfies

\[
\frac{|P_A|}{|A|}\le\sqrt{29}.
\]

Therefore no individual marked wall pinches the literal real physical chamber
in its interior.
