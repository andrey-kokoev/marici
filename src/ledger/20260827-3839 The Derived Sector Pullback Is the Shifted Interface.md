# The Derived Sector Pullback Is the Shifted Interface

For source-derived boundary maps

\[
C_+\xrightarrow{f_+}B\xleftarrow{f_-}C_-,
\]

the homotopy pullback is

\[
P=\operatorname{Cone}(f_+-f_-)[-1].
\]

If both sector complexes are contractible, the defining distinguished
triangle gives

\[
P\simeq B[-1],
\qquad
H^j(P)\cong H^{j-1}(B).
\]

Therefore derived sewing does not create arbitrary bulk cohomology. It lifts
the interface cohomology by one degree. In the RH programme, any class born
from sewing two off-seam-contractible sectors must be a seam/interface class.

The ordinary pullback is unsafe unless a fibrancy or surjectivity condition
makes it a model of the homotopy pullback. The cone construction is the
canonical first object.

The next gate is explicit: construct the interface complex `B_s` from the
primitive, prime-square, archimedean, and mixed-seam currents, prove its
off-seam acyclicity, and establish a noncircular determinant map detecting its
shifted class.
