# 3074 — The Standard Zeta Vacuum Occupies Only the Trivial Norm-One Fiber Mode

Every finite unramified vacuum (mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p}) is invariant under
(mathbb Z_p^\times), and the real completed Gaussian is invariant under
sign. For (mathbb Q), the standard theta/Tate vacuum is consequently
invariant under the full compact norm-one idele-class fiber.

Although radial Mellin compression forgets nontrivial ambient fiber modes,
the Riemann zeta vacuum activates none of them. Restoring the entire fiber
therefore adds capacity but no new physical comparison channel.

The next candidate must retain the moving incidence between the adelic vacuum
and the rational diagonal boundary, rather than decomposing the vacuum alone.

## Scope

This classifies the standard untwisted source. Twisted Hecke or Dirichlet
states can occupy nontrivial fiber characters but are different source
objects.
