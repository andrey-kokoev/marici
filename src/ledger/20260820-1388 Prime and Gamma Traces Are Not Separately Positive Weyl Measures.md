# 1388 — Prime and Gamma Traces Are Not Separately Positive Weyl Measures

Epistemic-graph event: 1428.

For \(y>1/2\),

\[
R_\Xi(y^2)
=\frac1{2y}\frac{\xi'(1/2+y)}{\xi(1/2+y)}
\]

has the exact source decomposition

\[
\frac1{2y}\left[
\frac1{y+1/2}+\frac1{y-1/2}
-\frac12\log\pi
+\frac12\psi\!\left(\frac{y+1/2}{2}\right)
-\sum_{n\ge2}\Lambda(n)n^{-y-1/2}

singularities also cancel only after completion. Therefore the positive
Stieltjes measure required by Ledger 1385 is not a termwise positive
pushforward of primes, gamma modes, or their disjoint union.

positive atomic measure appears only after continuation to the negative
squared axis—precisely the Stieltjes/RH gate.
