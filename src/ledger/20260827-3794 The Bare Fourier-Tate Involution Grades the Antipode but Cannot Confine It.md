# 3794 — The Bare Fourier–Tate Involution Grades the Antipode but Cannot Confine It

On the completed one-sided chart pair

\[
V(z)=\bigl(K(z),K(-z)\bigr),
\]

reciprocal sewing is the constant sheet swap. In the symmetric and
antisymmetric basis it acts by

\[
(F,G)\longmapsto(F,-G).
\]

A scalar zero is therefore an ordinary vector in the negative eigenspace:

\[
V(z)=G(z)(1,-1).
\]

The involution authorizes this state and contains no dependence on
\(\operatorname{Re}z\). Every constant Hermitian metric commuting with the
swap has the same deficiency: it grades the two channels but cannot locate an
antipodal incidence.

The missing control must retain a spectral connection, analytic domain, or
Green boundary form whose transport is sensitive to the half-plane and which
reduces to unitary Fourier–Tate sewing on the seam. Bare Fourier parity cannot
serve as the independent control tower.
