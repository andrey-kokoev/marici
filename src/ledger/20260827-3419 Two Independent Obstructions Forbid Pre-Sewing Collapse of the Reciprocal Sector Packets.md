# Two Independent Obstructions Forbid Pre-Sewing Collapse of the Reciprocal Sector Packets

A strict pre-sewing identification of the reciprocal sector packets fails for
two independent reasons.

First, the two endpoint-normalized inverse-scale homotopies differ by a
nonzero seam period. Identifying them erases that period. Second, finite
Fourier sewing rotates the distributed augmentation line into the
point-supported control line. Identifying those ports erases their operational
types.

Therefore the minimal strict architecture under the current source types is
`2(3+2+1)+1`. The earlier `3+2+2+1` is its lossy compressed shadow. A future
compression would have to preserve the period and port distinction as explicit
residual data and would be an equivalence, not an object identification.
