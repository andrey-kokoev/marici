# A Selected Cocycle Can Descend Without a Betti Homology Map

Epistemic-graph event: 1339.

For a target degree-`n` cocycle `ell` and a graded Betti candidate `S`, the
scalar readout `ell S_n` is well-defined on source homology exactly when

`ell Omega_(n+1)=0`.

This is strictly weaker than either `Omega=0` or the conditions for `S` to
induce a target homology map.  For example, map the generator of a source
`Z` cycle to `(1,1)` in the target complex `Z^2 --[1 0]--> Z`.  Its image is
not a cycle, but the cocycle `[0 1]` still produces the nonzero, well-defined
source-homology readout `1`.

Hence there are three distinct physical claims: strict relative-chain
pushforward, induced homology map, and descent of one selected paired
observable.  A five-site selector contraction might pass the weakest gate
even if the stronger maps fail, but the missing physical boundary matrices
currently prevent that test.
