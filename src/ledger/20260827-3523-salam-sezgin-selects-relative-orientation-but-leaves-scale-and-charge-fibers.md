## Claim

\(\operatorname{Minkowski}_4\times S^2\) unit-monopole background supplies the
first source-derived orientation selector in this flavor branch. Fixed
six-dimensional \(N=(1,0)\) chirality admits one internal BPS projector;
reversing the flux requires the opposite chiral source. The same gauge coupling
parallelizes the flux, curvature, scalar potential, and exact consistent
reduction.

Two exact source fibers survive. The retained scalar is massless and the
physical Kaluza--Klein scale

\[
M_K=g e^{\phi_0/2}
\]

varies along it. For unit flux, the charged Dirac index equals the independently
chosen integer matter charge, so one and three zero modes are both allowed.

## Classification

This is a genuine relative-orientation selector and gauge--gravity
parallelizer. It does not select an absolute scale, charge three, a quantum RG
basin, or a calibrated physical16 flavor readout.
