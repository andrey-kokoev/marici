One exact sector parity is algebraically sufficient to distinguish the two
identically represented entrance doublets. With the up chain even and the down
chain odd, it forbids the off-diagonal entrance kinetic Gram and wrong-sector
Yukawa entrances while preserving all declared messenger vertices, vectorlike
masses, and even cross-invariants supporting the noncollinear vacuum.

The parity equations have a unique solution after the common source
assignments are frozen. This is a declared source-extension rigidifier, not a
derived numerical selector. The nonzero odd down entrance vev spontaneously
breaks the parity, so domain-wall history or an independently typed soft
breaking becomes a new physical gate.
