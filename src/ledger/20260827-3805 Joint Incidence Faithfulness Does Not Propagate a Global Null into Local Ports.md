# 3805 — Joint Incidence Faithfulness Does Not Propagate a Global Null into Local Ports

The interval monitors

\[
M_I(h)=\int_Ih(u)\,du
\]

are jointly faithful when all bounded intervals are supplied. But a zero of
the global integral does not imply that any local monitor is zero. The exact
two-cell witness has response \(+1\) on the first cell and \(-1\) on the
second.

For the theta exchange commutator, a scalar zero kills only the aggregate of

\[
h_{a,t}(u)=k(u)\sinh(au)\sin(tu).
\]

Cell or moment resolution would reveal the nonzero packet off seam, but the
scalar-null event does not activate those additional ports.

Thus three claims are distinct: the monitor family exists, it is jointly
faithful, and scalar nullity lands in its common kernel. Only the third is
RH-strength. It requires a source-derived transport or descent action, not a
larger static monitor census.
