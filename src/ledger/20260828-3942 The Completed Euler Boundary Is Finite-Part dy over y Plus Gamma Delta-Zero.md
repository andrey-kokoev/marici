# The Completed Euler Boundary Is Finite-Part dy/y Plus Gamma Delta-Zero

For every continuously differentiable \(f\) on \([0,1]\),

\[
\lim_{X\to\infty}
\left[
\sum_{p\le X}\frac1p
f\!\left(\frac{\log p}{\log X}\right)
-f(0)\log\log X
\right]
=B_1f(0)
+\int_0^1\frac{f(y)-f(0)}{y}\,dy.
\]

The absolutely convergent square and higher Euler currents collapse to
\(y=0\).  Using \(\gamma=B_1+C_{\ge2}\), the full Euler boundary distribution
is

\[
\operatorname{Fp}\!\left(\frac{dy}{y}\right)
+\gamma\delta_0.
\]

It evaluates to \(\gamma\) on the constant mode and to \(1/r\) on every
positive jet \(y^r\).  Thus the Mertens finite part is exactly the connecting
map between the grade-zero corona and tangent jet tower.

This scalar distribution remains orientation-blind.  Fourier--Tate completion
requires two sheets with generator signs \(\pm y\) and a source-derived
relative endpoint incidence capable of distinguishing Nima's opposite-phase
two-prime witnesses.
