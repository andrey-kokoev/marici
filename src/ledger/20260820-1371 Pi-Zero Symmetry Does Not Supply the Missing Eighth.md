# 1371 — Pi-Zero Symmetry Does Not Supply the Missing Eighth

Epistemic-graph event: 1400.

Stirling expansion of the Riemann–Siegel phase gives

\[
\vartheta(t)=\frac t2\log\frac{t}{2\pi}-\frac t2
-\frac{\pi}{8}+O(t^{-1}).
\]

Thus the difference between the phase-volume constant `1` and the smooth
zero-count constant `7/8` is exactly the archimedean phase

\[
\frac{-\pi/8}{\pi}=-\frac18.
\]

the source `D4` action is trivial on the rank-one component lattice and its
dual.  Its induced symplectic action is identity, whose metaplectic lifts
provide only a central sign—not the required eighth phase.

Therefore the missing constant is not derived from `pi_0` source symmetry.
A successful metaplectic repair needs a richer physical coefficient–Betti
fiber with a canonical nontrivial symplectic rotation.
