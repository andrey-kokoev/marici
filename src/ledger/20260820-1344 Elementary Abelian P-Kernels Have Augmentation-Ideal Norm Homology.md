# Elementary Abelian P-Kernels Have Augmentation-Ideal Norm Homology

Epistemic-graph event: 1364.

For a rank-`k` kernel in `(C_p)^n` over `F_p`, the norm is the top monomial

`product epsilon_i^(p-1)`.

Its square-zero homology is

`(I_B/Soc(A_B)) tensor A_(B^c)`,

with total dimension `p^(n-k)(p^k-2)` and collapsed-kernel Loewy length
`k(p-1)-1`.  Its Hilbert series is obtained from the truncated-polynomial
series by deleting the unit and top socle terms in the collapsed factor.

The only zero case is one `C2` direction in characteristic two.  A one-bit
`C3` kernel already leaves one residual class per fiber.  The five-site
mod-two results are the specialization `p=2,n=5`.

This remains modular deck algebra, not a physical relative-chain theorem.
