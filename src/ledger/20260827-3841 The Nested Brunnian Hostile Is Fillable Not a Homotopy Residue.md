# The Nested Brunnian Hostile Is Fillable, Not a Homotopy Residue

The previously constructed family

\[
\beta_n=[C_{n-2},P^{-1}]
\]

is a nontrivial braid killed by every deletion map, but it is itself an
iterated symmetric commutator of the peripheral deletion-kernel subgroups.
It therefore belongs to the filling subgroup killed by the map

\[
\operatorname{Brun}_n(S^2)\longrightarrow\pi_{n-1}(S^2).
\]

Thus \(\beta_n\) proves failure of literal marginal reconstruction at every
arity, but it does not provide a nonzero irreducible homotopy residue. The
earlier claim is narrowed accordingly.

The next constructor must be an explicit spherical Brunnian representative
whose image in \(\pi_{n-1}(S^2)\) is nonzero. For five points the target is
\(\pi_4(S^2)\cong\mathbb Z/2\), and the existing nested word is not its
generator.
