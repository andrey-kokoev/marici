# 2632 — Completed Theta Shear Is Invertible at Every Scalar Zero

Superexponential theta decay gives normal convergence of the cutoff transfer
coefficients (F_L\to F). Hence the cutoff shears converge with their inverses:

\[
S_L(s)=\begin{pmatrix}1&F_L(s)\\0&1\end{pmatrix}
\longrightarrow
S(s)=\begin{pmatrix}1&F(s)\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The completed transport is entire and invertible everywhere. At a scalar zero,
(S(s)=I): the full relationship remains defined, while its off-diagonal
transmission channel becomes silent.

The transfer completion and the reciprocal energy completion are therefore
distinct. RH requires a source-derived coupling between them; determinant one
or transport invertibility alone cannot constrain scalar zeros.
