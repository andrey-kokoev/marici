# 3914 — The BSY Integral Is Total Diffuse Divisor Entropy

The decay-gap programme collapses to a single classical scalar. For the
endpoint-normalized Hardy carrier, Poisson--Jensen gives

\[
\frac1{2\pi}
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\frac{\log |\zeta(\tfrac12+it)|}{\tfrac14+t^2}\,dt
=
\sum_{\Re\rho>1/2}
m_\rho\log\left|\frac{\rho}{1-\rho}\right|.
\]

This is the classical Balazard--Saias--Yor identity. Its new role here is as
the faithful scalarization of the positive diffuse divisor-supply measure.
For `rho=1/2+a+i gamma`, every summand is strictly positive and begins as

\[
\frac{a}{\gamma^2+\tfrac14}+O(a^3).
\]

Hence one scalar measures the total height-weighted horizontal displacement,
or forbidden Poisson-time entropy. Positivity is unconditional; RH is the
zero-entropy equality case. The remaining source theorem is an independently
derived upper bound on this scalar, not another collection of finite probes.

## Scope

The identity and RH equivalence are classical. This entry records their
divisor-supply interpretation and the compressed source target. It does not
prove the missing upper bound or RH.
