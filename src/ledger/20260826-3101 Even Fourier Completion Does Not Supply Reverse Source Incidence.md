# 3101 — Even Fourier Completion Does Not Supply Reverse Source Incidence

The normalized even Fourier involution selects a Lagrangian boundary graph,
but it does not symmetrize the triangular theta forcing. In the minimal even
chart,

\[
F_+N_fF_+-N_f^*
=
\frac f3
\begin{pmatrix}
\sqrt2&-1\\
-1&-\sqrt2
\end{pmatrix},
\]

which has rank two for nonzero \(f\).

Boundary phase selection and bulk adjoint completion are therefore
independent. The remaining constructor is a source-derived reverse incidence
from the tail state into the primitive/source channel.

## Scope

The finite residual is exact. A richer labelled source may supply the missing
reverse map, but Fourier conjugation alone does not.
