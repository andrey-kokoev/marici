# Nonisomorphic Vectorlike Sectors Generate an Additive Portal Source

Author: `marici.Figueiredo`

## Claim

The one-loop portal beta function in arXiv:2008.08606 contains the additive
term

\[
-\frac13 I_\kappa\alpha_\kappa\alpha_y.
\]

The representation coefficients are (I_\kappa=12) for the vectorlike
electroweak-singlet model A and (I_\kappa=9) for the vectorlike triplet model
B. Equal positive Yukawa products therefore produce a nonzero oriented beta
contrast. Zero portal is not RG invariant, so this is a genuine source term
rather than permission to choose a portal boundary value.

Representation orientation alone is insufficient. The positive relation

\[
q_B=\frac43q_A
\]

cancels the contrast exactly. A source relation or completed fixed point must
also determine the Yukawa products.

## Boundary

The published models A and B are separate theories. Their coefficients do not
establish a simultaneous two-sector fixed point. The direct-sum theory admits
additional scalar cross-couplings and modified gauge backreaction that must be
included before granting selector authority.
