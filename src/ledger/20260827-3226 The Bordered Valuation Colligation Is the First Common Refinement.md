exclusion and the Tate determinant cumulants are two readouts of the same
source-derived bordered valuation colligation ((S,e_0,\ell)).

Its defect projection (I-SS^*) is the valuation-zero projector and becomes
(P_{p\nmid n}) across primitive chains. Its transfer function
(ell(I-qS)^{-1}e_0) is the Euler factor, whose determinant-line logarithm
produces the primitive, square, and connected cumulants.

The first common-refinement rung of the fourth tower is therefore explicit.
The next rung is the doubled colligation's Green comparison, retaining the
finite terminal defect and archimedean incidence.
