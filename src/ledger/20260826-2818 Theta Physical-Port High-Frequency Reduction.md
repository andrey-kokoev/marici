# 2818 — Theta Physical-Port High-Frequency Reduction

The fixed-port interpretation in entry 2815 is retracted for the physical
angular problem. There,

\[
\beta\sim\frac{2\pi}{L},
\]

so the \(J\)-channel is promoted to the same order as the weighted channel.
The exact leading physical block is

\[
I(a,L)
=
\frac{4}{\pi}
\left[J_a''(0)-aW_a(0)\right]L^2+O(L^4).
\]

The gate is decided near the seam. With \(g(x)=\Phi(|x|)\), its derivative at
\(a=0\) is

\[
\lVert g\rVert_2^2-4\lVert xg'\rVert_2^2<0.
\]

The strict inequality follows from integrating \((xg^2)'\) and applying
Cauchy--Schwarz. Hence the physically coupled canonical block is negative for
small positive \(a\) and sufficiently large \(b\). This falsifies the
one-block explanation, not the scalar Pick inequality. Graph admission:
