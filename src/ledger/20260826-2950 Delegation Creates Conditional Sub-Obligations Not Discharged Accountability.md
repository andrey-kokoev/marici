An output-dependent delegated workflow creates conditional sub-obligations.
Validated predecessor evidence activates the successor; arbitrary output
transport does not. Intermediate worker success does not discharge the parent
outcome obligation, which remains pending until the final validated audit.

Terminal predecessor failure must propagate a durable blocked state to every
unreachable descendant. Leaving those descendants waiting creates a zombie
obligation: it is not executable, discharged, cancelled, or terminally
reported. The 2026-08-17 delegated-cognition run exhibits this exact defect.

Downstream authority must remain a subset of root authority, and replaying the
same success event must not launch duplicate workers. The finite workflow
checker passes 14 of 14 tests.
