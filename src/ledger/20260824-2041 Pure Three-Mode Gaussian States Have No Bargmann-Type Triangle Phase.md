# 2041 — Pure Three-Mode Gaussian States Have No Bargmann-Type Triangle Phase

## Question

Nima's rank-three amplitude packet found the gauge-invariant Bargmann product

\[
\gamma_{12}\gamma_{23}\gamma_{31}
\]

as the datum missing from pairwise magnitudes. Does the Gaussian coefficient lens carry an analogous gauge-invariant triangle loop?

## Canonical candidate

Under independent local symplectic gauges

\[
C_{ij}\mapsto S_iC_{ij}S_j^T,
\qquad S_i\in\operatorname{Sp}(2),
\]

the matrices

\[
X_{ij}=JC_{ij}
\]

transform as

\[
X_{ij}\mapsto S_i^{-T}X_{ij}S_j^T.
\]

Therefore the cyclic trace

\[
\boxed{
L_\Omega
=
\operatorname{tr}(JC_{12}JC_{23}JC_{31})
}
\]

is invariant under all three local symplectic gauges. It is the correctly typed Gaussian candidate for a Bargmann-like triangle quantity.

## Purity annihilates the candidate

Serafini--Adesso, arXiv:0705.1136, establish that for pure Gaussian states with at most three modes all direct position--momentum correlations can be removed by local Gaussian unitaries. Thus every pure three-mode covariance has a local symplectic representative in which all three cross blocks \(C_{ij}\) are diagonal.

For arbitrary diagonal \(2\times2\) matrices \(D_1,D_2,D_3\),

\[
\operatorname{tr}(JD_1JD_2JD_3)=0.
\]

Since \(L_\Omega\) is local-symplectic invariant, it follows that

\[
\boxed{
L_\Omega=0
\quad\text{on the complete pure three-mode Gaussian locus}.
}
\]

## Independent source packet

An exact rational pure state generated by two overlapping two-mode squeezers with an intermediate phase rotation satisfies

\[
V\Omega V=\frac14\Omega
\]

and gives \(L_\Omega=0\). Three independent nontrivial local symplectic gauges preserve the same zero. The checker also verifies the diagonal standard-form identity on 125 exact packets.

The Gaussian sector's additional \(\Omega\)-contracted coherence equations do not add a new loop phase; they remove it.
