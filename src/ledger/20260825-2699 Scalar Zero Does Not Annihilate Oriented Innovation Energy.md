# 2699 — Scalar Zero Does Not Annihilate Oriented Innovation Energy

The theta/Mellin readout is a linear functional \(\ell_s\) on the causal
innovation tower. Its reciprocal energy is the quadratic form

\[
\mathcal E_{p,s}(b)
=\sum_{k\ge1}
\left(p^{-2\Re(s)k}-p^{-2(1-\Re(s))k}\right)\|b_k\|^2.
\]

Away from the critical seam, every coefficient has the same strict sign.
Therefore the form is definite, while the scalar functional has a nontrivial
kernel as soon as two independent innovation channels are present. Nonzero
packets can satisfy

\[
\ell_s(b)=0,
\qquad
\mathcal E_{p,s}(b)\ne0.
\]

Thus scalar vanishing alone cannot imply vanishing boundary energy. The causal
tower supplies source-derived observability and orientation, but an independent
Green, Evans, or canonical-system law must make scalar nullity an executable
endpoint condition on the full state.
