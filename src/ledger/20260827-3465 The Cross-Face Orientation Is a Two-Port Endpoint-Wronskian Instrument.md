# The Cross-Face Orientation Is a Two-Port Endpoint–Wronskian Instrument

Integration by parts decomposes the oriented face difference as

\[
\Delta_{\rm face}
=
2\Re(z)|K(0)|^2
+
4\Im(z)J(K),
\qquad
J(K)=\Im\langle DK,K\rangle.
\]

The endpoint amplitude is reciprocal-even. The bulk Wronskian phase current
is reciprocal-odd. They are independent and form the minimal faithful
two-port orientation instrument.

The remaining theta-specific gate is to identify `J(K)` with a declared
primitive, square, odd principal-value, or modular seam current. Endpoint
control alone cannot reconstruct it.
