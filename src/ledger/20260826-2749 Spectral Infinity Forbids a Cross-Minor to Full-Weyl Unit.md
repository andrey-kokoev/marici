# 2749 — Spectral Infinity Forbids a Cross-Minor to Full-Weyl Unit

For the finite pure-resolvent Weyl matrix,

\[
W(z)=-B^*(A-zI)^{-1}B,
\]

independent ports with nonzero source-endpoint incidence give

\[
W_{f0}(z)=O(z^{-1}),
\qquad
\det W(z)=O(z^{-2}),
\]

with nonzero leading coefficients. Their quotient grows linearly at spectral
infinity. Since both are rational, no nowhere-zero rational unit can identify
the theta cross entry with the full Weyl determinant.

Dependent ports do not repair this: they make the full determinant vanish
identically. The minimal faithful determinant promotion is the previously
derived one-coordinate border. It preserves the cross divisor exactly but
remains non-self-adjoint because source and endpoint ports differ.

Thus the positive Cayley orientation of the full Weyl determinant cannot pass
to theta through a normalization unit. A larger source correspondence must act
on the bordered section itself.
