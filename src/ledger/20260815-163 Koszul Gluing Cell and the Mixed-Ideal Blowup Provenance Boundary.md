# Koszul Gluing Cell and the Mixed-Ideal Blowup Provenance Boundary

## Record

Date: 2026-08-15

This entry separates an exact algebraic gluing packet from its absent spatial

## Formal mixed Koszul packet

Let \(A\) be the legal unlocalized coefficient ring and abbreviate

\[
x=x_3,\qquad u=u_3.
\]

Define

\[
\widetilde P=K_A(x,u):
\quad
A\langle\lambda\rangle
\xrightarrow{d_2}
A\langle g,h\rangle
\xrightarrow{d_1}
A\langle p\rangle
\]

by

\[
\boxed{
d\lambda=ug-xh,\qquad dg=xp,\qquad dh=up,\qquad dp=0.
}
\]

Then

\[
d^2\lambda=u\,xp-x\,up=0.
\]

For a regular pair \(x,u\), this is the Koszul resolution of
\(A/(x,u)\). Its homology is

\[
H_0(\widetilde P)=A/(x,u),\qquad
H_1(\widetilde P)=H_2(\widetilde P)=0,
\]

with no integer torsion.

## Strict label-compatible map

The full source is

\[
dH=q-x\xi,\qquad dq=xb,\qquad d\xi=b.
\]

There is a strict chain map

\[
\boxed{
F(H)=\lambda,\qquad
F(q)=ug,\qquad
F(\xi)=h,\qquad
F(b)=up.
}
\]

Indeed,

\[
dF(H)=ug-xh=F(q-x\xi),
\]

\[
dF(q)=uxp=F(xb),\qquad
dF(\xi)=up=F(b),\qquad
dF(b)=0.
\]

More generally, a strict label-compatible map

\[
H\mapsto a\lambda,\quad
q\mapsto Bg,\quad
\xi\mapsto Ch,\quad
b\mapsto Dp
\]

must satisfy

\[
B=au,\qquad C=a,\qquad D=au.
\]

Hence the solution line is uniquely

\[
a(1,u,1,u),
\]

and positive orientation fixes the primitive value \(a=1\).

## Algebraic contraction and syzygy killing

The source remains contractible. With

\[
m=q-x\xi
\]

it splits as

\[
[H\xrightarrow{1}m]\oplus[\xi\xrightarrow{1}b],
\]

with contraction \(s(m)=H\) and \(s(b)=\xi\). The strict map \(F\) is
therefore nullhomotopic.

On the target side, adjoining \(\lambda\) fills the primitive mixed syzygy

\[
ug-xh.
\]

Thus the new generator changes the target: it does not reveal a hidden
cohomology class in the prior packet. This is the exact algebraic meaning of
the gluing completion.

## Conductor and localization audit

At \(x=0\), the packet becomes

\[
\lambda\xrightarrow{u}g,
\qquad
h\xrightarrow{u}p.
\]

It has the expected two grades

\[
\boxed{
H_1\simeq A/(u),\qquad H_0\simeq A/(u),
}
\]

the coefficient shadows of \(\operatorname{Tor}_1\) and
\(\operatorname{Tor}_0\). The image of \(F\) is not saturated in target
degrees one and zero: the cokernels generated by \(g\) and \(p\) are
\(A/(u)\). This is coefficient torsion, not integer torsion and not
permission to invert \(u\) globally.

The factor \(u^{-1}\) is legal only summandwise in the appropriate Cech
chart. After legally localizing this packet at \(u\), both displayed arrows
are units and the packet is contractible. No global localization conclusion
follows.

## Mixed-ideal blowup provenance

The natural geometric candidate is the blowup of the mixed ideal

\[
I=(x,u).
\]

Its Rees algebra has presentation

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Rees}_A(I)
\simeq
A[G,H]/(uG-xH).
}
\]

The two standard charts are

\[
u=tx
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
x=su.
\]

They glue along the overlap without globally inverting \(x\) or \(u\), and
the exceptional divisor is

\[
\mathbb P(I/I^2)\simeq\mathbb P^1
\]

in the regular transverse model. The blowup is not a flat replacement of
the conductor intersection. Its exceptional derived intersection retains
the two Tor grades seen above.

The Rees relation canonically fixes the syzygy \(uG-xH\) and its orientation.
It does not canonically produce a global free generator \(\lambda\).
Geometrically, such a generator belongs either to a chosen Tate/Koszul
resolution or to a tautological line on the blowup. In the latter
description its local representatives have the
\(\mathcal O(1)\)/\(\mathcal O(-1)\) transition dictated by the two charts.
Treating those representatives as one free global scalar cell discards the
transition data and is not a provenance theorem.
