## Additive contact system

For the source-defined additive contact readout, use the local basis

\[
(1,\tau_i,\tau_j),
\qquad
\tau_k=(2\pi i)^{-1}\log\nu_k.
\]

\[
V_i=T_i-I=E_{1,2},
\qquad
V_j=T_j-I=E_{1,3}.
\]

\[
\boxed{V_iV_j=V_jV_i=0.}
\]

Thus the contact readout has no iterated two-normal variation. This is the
monodromy form of Entry 2138's vanishing mixed second normal grade.

\[

\qquad
V_i=V_j=-2.
\]

\[
\boxed{V_iV_j=4,}
\]

with rank one.
