# The Prime-Harmonic Tangent Blowup Restores the Mellin Generator

The rescaled prime-harmonic measures

\[
\nu_X=
\sum_{p\le X}p^{-1}\delta_{\log p/\log X}
\]

converge vaguely on \((0,1]\) to \(dy/y\).  Consequently, every positive jet
grade has the exact limit

\[
\sum_{p\le X}\frac1p
\left(\frac{\log p}{\log X}\right)^r
\longrightarrow\frac1r,
\qquad r\ge1,
\]

while grade zero remains divergent.

Under microscopic shifts \(t=\tau/\log X\), Mellin transport converges to the
strongly continuous action \(f(y)\mapsto e^{-i\tau y}f(y)\) on
\(L^2((0,1],dy/y)\), with generator multiplication by \(y\).

Thus the generator lost in the Haar corona survives on its tangent blowup.
The primitive completion has at least two levels: a grade-zero Haar corona and
a relative tangent chart carrying all positive logarithmic jets.  The next
gate is their Fourier--Tate and archimedean sewing.
