# 3321 — RH Requires Zero-Orbit Isotropy Enhancement, Not Merely Real Equivariance

## Result

Reality and reciprocal symmetry generate a Klein-four action on the centered
divisor. A generic off-seam zero has the four-point orbit

\[
\{z,-z,\overline z,-\overline z\}.
\]

A critical-seam zero instead has enhanced isotropy under
`z -> -conjugate(z)`. RH therefore requires the divisor to land in the fixed
subobject, not merely to be equivariant.

The positive even two-cell source

\[
X_t(z)=2\left(1+\operatorname{sech}(t)\cosh z\right)
\]

has the complete Real-even structure while its zero `t+i*pi` has a free
four-point orbit. Thus the ambient anti-linear wall does not supply the
missing theorem.

## Scope

This does not construct the required zero-domain comparison cell or prove RH.
It excludes reality, evenness, and equivariant divisor closure as sufficient
mechanisms.
