On the primitive prime space, the source loop is

\[
L_s e_p=p^{-s}e_p.
\]

Its prime-power currents satisfy

\[
-\log\det(I-L_s)
=\sum_{k\ge1}\frac1k\operatorname{Tr}(L_s^k).
\]

Thus the primitive, square, and connected-tail levels are cyclic word lengths
of one feedback loop, not independent boundary state spaces.  Adjoining them
as separate Schur ports would double-count prime returns and introduce
unauthorized cross terms.

The additive entire theta Schur section and the multiplicative labelled Euler
determinant are legitimate realizations of different variance.  Their native
comparison belongs at determinant-line level.  Moreover, prime-power length
is not a well-founded state grade: every (L_s^k\) is a closed return to the
same prime mode, so the proposed strictly raising triangularity law is false.

The next legitimate object is a source-derived comparison between the theta
boundary line and the archimedean extension of the graded relative Euler
determinant line, constructed before scalarization and without finite-Euler
Poisson sewing.
