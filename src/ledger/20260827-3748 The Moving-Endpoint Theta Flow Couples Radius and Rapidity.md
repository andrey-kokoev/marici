# 3748 — The Moving-Endpoint Theta Flow Couples Radius and Rapidity

Each theta label is the sample (x=\log n) of reciprocal moving-endpoint
tails (T_\pm(x,z)). Both satisfy an equation with the same positive boundary
forcing (f(x)):

\[
\partial_xT_+=-\left(\frac12+z\right)T_+-f,
\qquad
\partial_xT_-=-\left(\frac12-z\right)T_--f.
\]

For (m=(T_++T_-)/2) and (n=(T_+-T_-)/2),

\[
\partial_xm=-\frac12m-zn-f,
\qquad
\partial_xn=-zm-\frac12n.
\]

Hence the labelled Lorentz radius satisfies

\[
(\partial_x+1)(m^2-n^2)=-2fm.
\]

In radial and rapidity coordinates, the same forcing couples both evolution
equations. Thus the radial gauge and rapidity shear that are independent in
the abstract conic are not independently executable inside the labelled
theta constructor.

This is not yet RH: the typed balance must survive sampling, label summation,
completion-carrier addition, and scalar intersection. The sharp falsifier is
a nontrivial radial/shear deformation preserving the common-forcing system at
every sampled endpoint while changing the completed divisor.
