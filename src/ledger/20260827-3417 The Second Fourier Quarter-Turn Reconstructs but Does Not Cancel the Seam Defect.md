# The Second Fourier Quarter-Turn Reconstructs but Does Not Cancel the Seam Defect

In the exact finite Fourier model, augmentation maps to the zero-frequency
control point, while the control point maps back to augmentation. Two Fourier
turns equal scaled label reflection.

The exterior seam current follows the same route. A nonzero scalar-null
bivector returns as a scaled reflected nonzero bivector. Thus the
input–control–output chain is coherent, but Poisson reconstruction conserves
rather than cancels the boundary defect.

This is the first explicit coherence cell spanning all three state towers. The
remaining RH-bearing boundary law must supply an oppositely oriented typed
arithmetic current; it cannot come from Fourier transport alone.
