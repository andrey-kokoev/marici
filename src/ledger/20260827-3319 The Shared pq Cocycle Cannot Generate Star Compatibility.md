## Result

The shared two-prime seam cocycle is complex-linear and compares two
translation orders. Star compatibility compares reciprocal transport with
Hilbert adjunction and is conjugate-linear in one path. The former cannot
generate the latter without a new Real or adjoint constructor.

The exact positive hostile is `A=delta_0+2 delta_1`. At
`z_0=pi+i log 2`, its scalar readout `1+2 exp(i z_0)` vanishes, all interval
composition identities hold, but for every interval containing both atoms
the reciprocal and adjoint values are `-3` and `0`. The star defect is `-3`.

Prime-ratio density still proves that star equality on all ratio cells would
force continuous star equality and hence the seam. What fails is deriving
those discrete equalities from holomorphic seam composition alone.

## Scope

boundary pairing, adjoint arithmetic incidence, or reflection-positive
localization. It excludes only derivation of star compatibility from scalar
