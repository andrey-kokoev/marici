# A Generic Lower Single Wall Has Rational Nodal Infinity, Not an Elliptic Boundary

## Record

Entry 545 proves that every single denominator in the generic lower family
carries proper support rank five. This entry determines the compactified
boundary geometry for the representative wall \(q_{g_1}=0\).

No divisor, compactification center, or coefficient summand is added after
seeing the result.

## Deutsch--Popperian conjecture tested

The hard-to-vary claim was

\[
\boxed{
\text{the rank-five }q_{g_1}\text{ grade requires an elliptic boundary
component or a new carrier divisor.}
}
\]

The finite falsifier was an exact source compactification whose entire
infinity boundary is rational and whose only degenerations lie on the
already frozen site-face and external Gram supports.

## Frozen wall surface

Use

\[
q_{g_1}=c+b+X_1=0,
\qquad c=-b-X_1.
\]

The residue coefficient surface is the double plane

\[
S_{g_1}:\qquad w^2=K(a,b,-b-X_1),
\]

where \(K\) is the generic Cayley--Menger polynomial of Entry 185.

Compactify with

\[
b=\frac1s,qquad a=\frac ts,qquad w=\frac W{s^2}.
\]

Equivalently, homogenize the wall quartic to

\[
\overline S_{g_1}:\qquad W^2=\overline K_{g_1}(a,b,s).
\]

## Exact infinity divisor

The source polynomial gives

\[
\boxed{
\overline K_{g_1}(a,b,0)
=
P_1^2(a^2-b^2)^2.
}
\]

Hence infinity is

\[
D_\infty=D_+\cup D_-,
\]

\[
D_\pm:\qquad s=0,qquad
W=\pm P_1(a^2-b^2).
\]

Both components are rational. They meet at exactly two points,

\[
p_+=[1:1:0:0],
\qquad
p_-=[-1:1:0:0].
\]

Thus the single-wall boundary is a two-component, two-intersection
normalization--conductor object. It is not the elliptic binary-quartic
boundary of the \(q_{\mathcal G}\)-residue surface.

## Exact local singularity

On the chart \(b=1\), write

\[
a=\varepsilon+t,
\qquad
c=-1-X_1s,
\qquad
\varepsilon=\pm1.
\]

The constant and linear terms of \(\overline K_{g_1}\) vanish at both
points. Its quadratic coefficients satisfy

\[
q_{tt}=4P_1^2,
\]

\[
q_{ts}
=
-4\varepsilon X_1(P_1^2-P_2^2+P_3^2),
\]

\[
q_{ss}=\Lambda_P+4X_1^2P_3^2,
\]

where

\[
\Lambda_P=
(P_1-P_2-P_3)(P_1-P_2+P_3)
(P_1+P_2-P_3)(P_1+P_2+P_3).
\]

The quadratic discriminant is independent of the occurrence:

\[
\boxed{
4q_{tt}q_{ss}-q_{ts}^2
=
16(P_1-X_1)(P_1+X_1)\Lambda_P.
}
\]

Therefore \(p_+\) and \(p_-\) are generically ordinary \(A_1\)
singularities. They become worse only on

\[
P_1^2-X_1^2=0
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
\Lambda_P=0,
\]

which are exactly the predeclared site-face and fixed-base Gram supports.

## Verdict

The conjecture is falsified:

\[
\boxed{
\text{the generic rank-five single-wall grade has rational
normalization--conductor infinity, with no elliptic boundary component.}
}
\]

This excludes one tempting misclassification. The rank-five grade is not a
second Legendre/Gauss--Manin block analogous to the top
\(q_{\mathcal G}\)-sector. Its natural home is the algebraic/relative
cohomology of the resolved double-plane wall together with its conductor
boundary.

This is compatible with the source statement that the generic lower family
is polylogarithmic with algebraic letters.
