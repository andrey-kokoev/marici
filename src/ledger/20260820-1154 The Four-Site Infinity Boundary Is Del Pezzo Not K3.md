## Hard-to-vary claim

The three-site elliptic infinity boundary might begin a dimensional ladder
in which the four-site one-loop polygon has a K3 infinity boundary.

The source-derived falsifier is the degree of the general one-loop
Baikov/Cayley--Menger polynomial before any fitted compactification.

## General one-loop degree

The primary one-loop construction identifies the loop measure with the
Baikov polynomial of a one-loop (n)-point integral after passing to the
squared edge variables (z_i=y_i^2).

Let (G_{m ext}) be the external Gram matrix and let (v) collect the
scalar products of the loop momentum with an external basis. The complete
Gram determinant has the block form

\[
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
\ell^2&v^T\\
v&G_{\rm ext}
\end{pmatrix}
=
\det(G_{\rm ext})
\left(\ell^2-v^TG_{\rm ext}^{-1}v\right).
\]

Here (ell^2=z_1), while every component of (v) is linear in the
(z_i). Therefore the Cayley--Menger/Baikov polynomial is quadratic in
(z_i), hence quartic in the edge variables (y_i), independently of the
number of polygon sites.

## Four-site marked residue

For the four-site polygon, taking one marked cosmological residue eliminates
one edge variable by a linear equation. Three affine edge variables remain.
Linear substitution preserves the generic quartic leading degree. The
compactified infinity branch is therefore

\[
B_4\subset\mathbf P^2,
\qquad \deg B_4=4,
\]

and the coefficient boundary is the double cover

\[
\pi:X_4\longrightarrow\mathbf P^2
\]

branched over (B_4).

## Canonical class

For a double cover of (mathbf P^2) branched over a quartic,

\[
K_{X_4}
=
\pi^*\left(K_{\mathbf P^2}+2H\right)
=-\pi^*H.
\]

Consequently

\[
(-K_{X_4})^2=2.
\]

At a generic smooth fiber,

\[
\boxed{X_4\text{ is a degree-two del Pezzo surface, not a K3 surface}.}
\]

## Meaning for the cosmology program

The elliptic three-site boundary is a low-dimensional coincidence: a double
cover of (mathbf P^1) branched at four points has trivial canonical class.
Increasing the number of sites does not increase the branch degree; the
Baikov polynomial remains quartic. Thus there is no source-forced

\[
\text{elliptic}\to\text{K3}\to\cdots
\]

ladder from the pure infinity boundary.

This falsifies a tempting strong universal-coefficient extrapolation and is
compatible with H2: the same Cayley--Menger carrier construction persists,
while the coefficient geometry changes with dimension.

## Scope

This classifies only the pure Cayley--Menger infinity double cover. It does
not prove that the complete four-site marked-relative period system is
mixed Tate or del Pezzo-only. Marked denominator sections, their relative
cohomology, and singular specializations may generate additional coefficient
complexity. None of that justifies a new carrier stratum without a separate
incidence calculation.
