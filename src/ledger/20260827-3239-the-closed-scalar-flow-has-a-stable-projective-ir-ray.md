(lambda_n=lambda_m=a), (lambda_c=0), and (lambda_x=b>0), the ratio
(r=a/b) obeys

\[
\dot r=4b(2r-1)(2r-3).
\]

approaches the remaining ray (r=3/2), which lies inside the open-decay
corridor.

cone. It is not yet a finite-scale or `physical16` selector: the trajectory
constant remains distinct at every finite projective time, and omitted gauge,
Yukawa, messenger, and threshold terms may move or destroy the ray.
