# 3122 — A Faithful Flavor Frame Does Not Select Its Scalar Couplings

## Result

WP649's ordered frame permits the two-sector family

\[
Y_s=a_sI+b_sJ_n+c_sJ_m.
\]

Its six complex coefficients are (SO(3)) singlets and are not fixed by the
frame action. Their twelve-real-dimensional control space has exact intrinsic
response rank eight and a four-dimensional local kernel at the hostile
witness.

Two equally legal packets, (Y=I) and (Y=I+J_n), are physically distinct:
their exact values of \(\operatorname{Tr}(YY^\dagger)\) are three and five.

## Scope

This result does not exclude a new source action for the scalar coefficients.
It establishes that frame construction and coefficient selection are separate
operations.
