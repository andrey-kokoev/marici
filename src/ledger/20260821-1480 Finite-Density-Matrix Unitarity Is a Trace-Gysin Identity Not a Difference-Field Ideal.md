\[

\[

\[
\boxed{

Geometrically, the two boundary components \(\Sigma_+,\Sigma_-\) are
identified and the Euclidean density-matrix bridge becomes a closed periodic

## Rejected shortcut

The normalization identity is not the pointwise statement

\[
S_\Sigma[\phi,\phi]=0.
\]

A generic density kernel is nontrivial on the diagonal; only its integrated
diagonal is normalized. Consequently it is false that every initial boundary
vertex must contain at least one Schwinger--Keldysh difference-field leg.

Thus the boundary complex cannot be defined as the ideal generated by
\(\phi_+-\phi_-\) without additional source assumptions.
