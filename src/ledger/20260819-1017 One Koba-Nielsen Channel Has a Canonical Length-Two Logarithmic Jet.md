## Source derivative

Entry 929 identifies the missing source operation.  For a channel (c), with
monodromy coordinate (A_c=e^{i\pi s_c}), the Koba–Nielsen factor satisfies

\[
A_c\partial_{A_c}\mathrm{KN}
=
\frac{\alpha'}{i\pi}
\log(f_c)\,\mathrm{KN}.
\]

Therefore the first parameter/Rees jet is canonically generated by

\[
e_0=\mathrm{KN},
\qquad
e_1=\log(f_c)\mathrm{KN}.
\]

After extracting the source coefficient (alpha'/(i\pi)), the associated
first-jet operator is

\[
N=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&0\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\boxed{N^2=0.}
\]

This is a source-normalized length-two unipotent coefficient object.  It is
not an all-orders Gauss–Manin connection: differentiating (e_1) before
first-jet truncation produces ((\log f_c)^2\mathrm{KN}).

## Interaction with dual cellularization

Entry 1016's relative cellular connection is diagonal in chamber degree and
acts identically on the two logarithmic layers.  Hence its residues commute
with (N):

\[
\boxed{[A_{\rm rel},N]=0.}
\]

At the level of the external parameter jet and cellular transport, the
combined object is therefore the tensor product

\[
C^\bullet_{\rm chamber,dual}
\otimes
\bigl(\mathbb Qe_0\oplus\mathbb Qe_1,N\bigr),
\]

with no mixed curvature or new support introduced by the cellular gauge.

## What this does not solve

The logarithmic jet acts inside each occurrence word.  It does not mix the six
word labels and therefore cannot supply the nondiagonal word-level connection
or circuit homotopy left open by Entries 929 and 1016.

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\text{source first logarithmic jet exists}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{finite six-word Gauss--Manin connection}.
}
\]

The static Betti comparison remains closed, while the connection frontier is
now typed as a reduction problem for logarithmic insertions—not a missing
cellular duality map.
