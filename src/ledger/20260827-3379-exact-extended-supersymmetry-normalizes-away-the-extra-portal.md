# 3379 — Exact Extended Supersymmetry Normalizes Away the Extra Portal

## Claim

In the declared canonical \(N=2\) gauge-hypermultiplet normalization,
\(y_{\mathrm{hyper}}^2=2g^2\). The interaction is therefore tied to the gauge
metric rather than assigned an independent Wilson coefficient.

Exact \(N=2\), however, has no soft scalar mass in the gauge-breaking sector:

\[
m_{\mathrm{soft}}^2=0,
\qquad
\epsilon=0.
\]

The additional nondecoupling \(D\)-term portal vanishes.

## Broken-symmetry residual

Breaking to \(N=1\) restores a soft mass \(cx^2\), but also restores the
breaking scale \(x\) and Wilson coefficient \(c\). Even after imposing
\(x^2=g^2v^2\),

\[
\epsilon=\frac{c}{2q^2+c}.
\]

At \(q^2=1\), \(c=1\) and \(c=3\) produce portal contrasts differing by
\(2g^2/15\).

## Classification

Exact extended supersymmetry is an operator normalizer with zero additional
portal. Generic local breaking permits the portal but reintroduces the
coefficient fiber. A viable successor must derive and quantize the breaking
constructor itself.
