# 1848 — The Polar Triple Cusp Resolves to the Legendre Modular Line

## The intersection is a single center

For generic nonzero transverse Hessian weights (h_1,h_2), any two of

\[
\delta_1=0,
\qquad
\delta_2=0,
\qquad
h_2\delta_1-h_1\delta_2=0
\]

force the third.  Hence Entry 1847's three cusp divisors have one common
codimension-two center

\[
Z=\{\delta_1=\delta_2=0\}.
\]

There are no three distinct generic pairwise-cusp germs to analyze.

## Projectivized normal cone

Blow up the two labelled normals and use the affine exceptional coordinate

\[
r=\frac{\delta_1}{\delta_2}.
\]

Entry 1846's modulus restricts to

\[
\boxed{
m=\frac{h_2r}{h_2r-h_1}.
}
\]

This is a Möbius isomorphism of the exceptional projective line.  Its three
distinguished labelled rays are

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{ray}&r&m\\
\hline
\delta_1=0&0&0\\
\delta_2=0&\infty&1\\
h_2\delta_1-h_1\delta_2=0&h_1/h_2&\infty.
\end{array}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mathbf P(N_Z)\setminus\{\text{three labelled rays}\}
\simeq
\mathbf P^1_m\setminus\{0,1,\infty\}.
}
\]

## Interpretation

The all-walls intersection does not collapse the elliptic coefficient to a
single Tate limit.  After the common radial scale is separated, the direction
of approach retains the complete Legendre variation.  The radial scale affects
only the Kummer factor already supplied by the physical current.

This is precisely what the frozen flagged-normal carrier is supposed to retain:
labelled normal directions and their projectivization.  No new carrier stratum
is required.  Forgetting the labels or taking the ordinary pullback directly at
(Z) would erase the surviving modular coordinate.

relative chain over the exceptional line, nor select a physical direction
(r).  A physical specialization depending on an undeclared choice of (r)
would remain noncanonical.
