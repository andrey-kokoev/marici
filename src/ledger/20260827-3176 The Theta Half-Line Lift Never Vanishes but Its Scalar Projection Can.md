# 3176 — The Theta Half-Line Lift Never Vanishes but Its Scalar Projection Can

Strict decrease of the completed theta half-source gives

\[
zH(z)=\int_0^\infty(e^{zt}-1)\,d\mu(t),
\qquad d\mu=-\Phi'(t)\,dt>0.
\]

Therefore (H(z)\ne0) for (Re z<0). Reflection protects (H(-z)) for
(Re z>0), and the seam sine orientation closes the boundary. The faithful
pair ((H(z),H(-z))) never vanishes.

## Scope correction

A completed zero is not a common null. It is the antipodal nonzero incidence

\[
H(-z)=-H(z)\ne0.
\]

The RH-bearing target is to confine this projective incidence to the seam.
This supersedes the common-zero reduction stated in Entries 3174–3175.
