# Anomaly Inflow Quantizes Relative Orientation, Not Absolute Sign

Anomaly inflow supplies a quantized CP-odd bias

\[
E_{\rm odd}=-k\eta J,
\]

where \(k\) is the integer anomaly level, \(\eta\) is the boundary normal, and
\(J\) is a faithful weak-basis flavor orientation. At fixed \(\eta\), this
selects \(J\). Anomaly matching protects \(k\) under symmetry-preserving RG and
gapped vectorlike thresholds.

The unoriented joint source retains two exact minima:

\[
(\eta,J)=(+1,+1),\qquad(-1,-1).
\]

They have the same energy and inflow current but opposite absolute flavor
sign. Fixing \(\eta\) restricts the experiment to a stabilizer groupoid and
creates a relational orientation port. In an anomaly-free channel \(k=0\), so
inflow supplies no bias.
