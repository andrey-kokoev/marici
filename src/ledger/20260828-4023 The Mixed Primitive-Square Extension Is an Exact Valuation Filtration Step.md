\[
E_p-I+N_p=\sum_{k\ge2}T_p^kT_p^{*k}.
\]

identity line, is exactly the square-and-higher current. The candidate
mixed-depth extension is a filtration boundary, not a new coherence class.

\[
\log\left(\frac{n}{\operatorname{rad}(n)}\right),
\]

regularity levels but cannot orient scalar zeros. The next legitimate object
is the nonlinear comparison between the additive theta boundary line and the
