How to read research statuses

Checked: the specific mathematical or implementation result has cited checks, not physical validation. Conditional: a working construction with supplied assumptions. Conjectural: a proposed identification. Illustrative: a display convention. Unconstructed: the specified operation is missing. Refuted: a particular tested proposal fails, not every related mechanism.

Mechanism links indicate relevance or a proposed application, not a completed identification. The elementary rotor and the 144-transfer electron spinor are not yet identified. Source claims are indexed here, not independently rerun by this page.
