# Epistemic graph

A public, read-only projection of admitted claims, problems, tests, sources, and their relations. Proposals, reviews, and admission remain on the private operator surface.

Admission records a research lifecycle decision, not mathematical proof or experimental confirmation. Read each claim with its source, test scope, verification level, and exclusions. Historical or scoped graph records do not override the [current public synthesis](/research/results/).
