# Internal arXiv readiness verdict

## Decision

The package is suitable only as an internal research-program draft. It is not ready for arXiv submission as an RH proof.

This is a mathematical veto, not merely a formatting or reproducibility delay.

## Established core

The current proof ledger records durable local or constructor results for:

- distinct even and odd analytic completion channels;
- the source-weighted derivative bridge and minimal closed derivative square;
- the wall module, Krein Green form, reciprocal sewing, and positive real boundary polarization;
- labelled theta-history incidence and several even/odd Jacobi realization cells;
- the Wronskian relative connecting morphism;
- seam-normal and Wronskian calibration on the completed-theta odd line;
- explicit signed Euler-to-theta sampling;
- the obstruction showing that the theta pullback metric is not uniformly equivalent to the intrinsic Euler topology.

These results justify a technical programme paper if it is presented explicitly as such. They do not yield RH.

## Mathematical release blockers

### G1: completed Adams edge

Open. The mixed-edge domain is not source-authorized. The compact theta incidence is not bounded below on the intrinsic Euler completion, while the theta pullback completion is a different object. Endpoint lifting, complete radical descent, loading, and full prime assembly remain incomplete.

### G2: constructor coherence

Open. A finite monoidal coherence specification exists, but there is no completed source-derived, interface-preserving arithmetic-to-analytic rewrite certificate.

### G3: five global margins

Open. The theta-factored arithmetic lower margin is impossible on the full intrinsic Euler odd sector, but a uniformly positive two-port Euler valuation frame is proved before theta sampling. Its preservation through endpoint lifting and Green reduction is open. The analytic, gluing, diagonal, and mixed margins have not been proved for one completed joint Green system.

### G4: Xi spectral identification

Open. The generalized eigenvalue-one criterion identifies an internal operator collision, but no noncircular bidirectional theorem identifies that collision with completed Xi zeros, including multiplicity and completion spectral exactness. Scalar seam observation loses reciprocal off-seam pairs. The Clark model assumes a Schur condition equivalent to RH.

### G5: independent verification

Open. Internal full audit and both required external expert reviews remain unresolved.

### G6: reproducible package

Open and owned by Aspect. Mechanical packaging cannot override mathematical gates.

### G7: publication authority and metadata

Open. Authorship, license, metadata, and explicit operator authorization are unresolved.

## Permitted publication shape now

A truthful preprint could be framed as:

> A categorical Green-operator programme for RH, with proved local constructor theorems, exact obstruction results, and explicit open completion gates.

It must not be titled, abstracted, announced, or uploaded as a proof of RH. The conditional RH deduction must remain visibly conditional.

## Minimum route to a proof-claim submission

1. Authorize one completed mixed-edge source object.
2. Construct the noncompact arithmetic observer or prove an authorized alternative architecture.
3. Close the full Adams edge and source rewrite certificate.
4. Prove all five uniform margins on the same reduced completed system.
5. Prove bidirectional Xi divisor identification without assuming an RH-equivalent admission condition.
6. Complete independent mathematical review.
7. Pass clean reproducibility audit.
8. Resolve authorship and metadata.
9. Obtain explicit operator publication authority.

Until all nine steps are complete, external proof publication is vetoed by the proof ledger.
