# The radial Real structure closes the internal transpose-adjoint square

## Question

After the canonical fold constructs the half-turn sewing comparison, is the Real comparison between analytic transpose and Hermitian adjoint still mathematically open?

## Claim boundary

\[

## Transpose and adjoint

Let the complex-bilinear pairings be induced from Real-compatible Hermitian metrics:

\[
b_V(v_1,v_2)=\langle J_Vv_1,v_2\rangle_V,
\qquad
b_H(h_1,h_2)=\langle J_Hh_1,h_2\rangle_H.
\]

Define analytic transpose by

\[
b_H(Uv,h)=b_V(v,U^\top h).
\]

Then

\[
U^\top=J_VU^*J_H.
\]

Since \(U\) intertwines the Real structures, its adjoint does also:

\[
J_VU^*=U^*J_H.
\]

Consequently

\[
U^\top=U^*.
\]

This is an exact complex-linear identity on the declared source and target complexifications.
