# Conjecture: tetrahedral circle, interior coherence plane, and layer growth

Status: operator conjecture; recorded, not verified. Construction order matters.

1. **Circle.** Link `l + k` tetrahedra into a circle. The meanings of `l` and `k` are left open; no substitution from the Ramsey parameters is assumed.
2. **Conditional coherencer.** Introduce a coherencer between the linked tetrahedra if their transport needs one. The need may depend on whether `l + k` is odd or even. Neither parity is designated as the one requiring it.
3. **Interior coherence plane.** A coherence plane connects the interior of the circle. This is a separate proposed structure from the conditional coherencer in step 2.
4. **Layer growth.** Coherence layers grow from both sides of that plane. Retain two readings: the layers extend in opposite directions, or both sides extend in the same direction into the next dimension. No equivalence or choice between the readings is asserted.

**Evidence boundary.** The [22-vertex witness](ramsey-37-ordered-star-readout.md) has a cycle of five four-position frames and a reversal that exchanges its poles. This motivates the conjecture but does not establish tetrahedral gluing, a parity rule, an interior plane, or a law of layer growth.

**First discriminating test.** Specify transport around the circle independently of its parity. Compare its return on odd and even circles before adding any coherencer; record what fails to agree and whether the proposed coherencer restores agreement. The incidence of the interior plane and the direction of its layers are separate subsequent tests.
