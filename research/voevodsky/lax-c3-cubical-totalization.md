# Lax C3 cubical totalization after the fiber mismatch

## Question

Can categorical completeness retain the three-coordinate cubical organization after the edge equivalence \(\mathsf{Over}_C\simeq\mathsf{Under}_A\) is falsified?

## Claim boundary

This packet replaces adjacent-fiber equivalences by typed noninvertible residue transports and replaces contractible global coherence by a type of lax fillers. It constructs and checks the local \(C\to A\) model only; the other two edges and global filler remain unrealized.

## Revised conjecture

The RH coherence model is a lax \(C_3\)-indexed cubical diagram. Each vertex has realization, underdetermination, and over-realization directions, but counterclockwise gluing uses a map

\[
T_i:R_i\longrightarrow P_{i+1}
\]

rather than an equivalence of fibers. Each edge carries a possibly noninvertible comparison 2-cell

\[
\alpha_i:G_{i+1}T_iC_i\Longrightarrow\Phi_i.
\]

Categorical completeness requires the boundary maps and lax fillers to exist and compose; it does not require their invertibility or uniqueness.

## Local C-to-A realization

On the signed atomic fixture, take

\[
C_C(\rho_\varepsilon)=\varepsilon,
\qquad
T_C(\varepsilon)=s=\varepsilon,
\qquad
G_A(s)=e^{-(t+s)a^2}\cos(az).
\]

Then

\[
G_AT_CC_C(\rho_\varepsilon)
=e^{-(t+\varepsilon)a^2}\cos(az).
\]

The composite is sensitive to the obstruction direction because its \(\varepsilon\)-derivative is nonzero. No inverse \(P_A\to R_C\) is asserted: analytic parameters may contain coordinates not generated by residues, and the previous tangent-fiber mismatch remains recorded.

## Strongest falsification attempt

A lax transport is vacuous if its composite with the next generator kills the residue tangent. The checker evaluates

\[
\partial_\varepsilon(G_AT_CC_C)|_{z=0}
=-a^2e^{-(t+\varepsilon)a^2}\neq0.
\]

A deliberate constant transport has zero derivative and is rejected. Thus the local lax edge carries residue information without requiring a false fiber equivalence.

## Disposition

The local \(C\to A\) lax edge is realized on the fixture and survives the non-vacuity test. This repairs the first obstruction to cubical organization by weakening the correct structure: equivalence becomes directed transport, and contractible coherence becomes a lax-filler type. It does not realize \(\Phi_C\) in the main model or supply \(\alpha_C\); those remain distinct obligations. Next dependency remains \(G_B\), followed by the other local lax edges and only then the global filler.
