## 1. Work directly with the geometry of arbitrary threefold maps

**Refutation-compatible; best connection to the actual missing theorem, but no known shortcut.**

The source-complete reader already reduces the problem to all smooth projective threefold maps f:Y->X. Repeating its graph, resolution or K-normalization constructions cannot improve the reader. A different attack would have to exploit geometric restrictions on such maps into this particular simple Picard-rank-one abelian variety.

One concrete interface is the Gauss map and holomorphic top forms. On X the two Gaussian eigenspaces in H^(1,0) each have dimension3. Choose their determinant holomorphic three-forms Omega_+ and Omega_-. The native Weil determinant form is a nonzero scalar multiple of Omega_+ wedge conjugate(Omega_-), with the scalar fixed by the marking. On Y the Weil period therefore becomes a cross pairing of the pulled-back canonical forms f*Omega_+ and f*Omega_-.

**First test:** derive that marked formula and its Gauss/Plucker interpretation; compare it with the complete-intersection and known algebraic product controls. Separate the Cauchy-Schwarz bound on the cross pairing from a genuine orthogonality theorem.

**What would count as progress:** a geometric restriction on the Gauss image or on the pulled-back canonical linear systems that forces this pairing to vanish on the fixed X for every allowed source. Merely renaming the old integral or computing another norm does not count. The K-action on X does not automatically lift to Y, so eigenvector orthogonality on Y cannot be assumed.

**Risk:** high. The universal orthogonality statement remains unproved and is equivalent to the old gate once expressed through the reader. The possible new content would have to come from subvariety geometry, not from the reformulation itself.

## 2. Separate a very-general argument from the selected endpoint

**Refutation-compatible, but a two-gate route rather than a genericity slogan.**

The nine-dimensional Weil moduli base admits countably many signed cycle-parameter spaces after finite marking. To show nonalgebraicity at a very general point, one would need to exclude dominance for EVERY component carrying a nonzero Weil class, not just the component carrying a chosen product witness. A second, independent argument would be needed to place the selected explicit point outside the resulting exceptional images.

**First test:** specify an actual family/component and study the differential of its map to full Weil moduli in the five directions transverse to the product-control locus. A proved obstruction for that supplied component is informative; it is not the universal theorem. If one cannot supply actual cycle representatives or an obstruction-compatible deformation model for them, this branch has not reached an executable calculation.

**Failure modes:** confusing first-order obstruction to one witness with obstruction to all witnesses; using independence of analytic period coordinates as algebraic-moduli genericity; using countability without a proper-image or dominance argument.

This route has a clear possible endpoint, but the current material has not removed either gate. It is not currently stronger than route1.

## 3. Screen genuinely new detectors before building more controls

**Refutation-compatible; useful as an early rejection stage.**

A new detector needs an independently defined rational target, descent to the cycle class, an algebraic-specific necessity theorem and nonzero evaluation. The existing Weil projection, its norm, source integral, terminal syzygy and Adams normalization are different presentations of the same readout, not independent detectors.

Two strong screening facts:

* Rational topological K-theory has Chern character isomorphism to even rational cohomology on the compact smooth X. Thus failure of a RATIONAL topological K-lift cannot separate the present class. Integral/torsion obstructions cannot simply be promoted to a rational Hodge counterexample.
* Deligne's theorem that Hodge classes on complex abelian varieties are absolute Hodge blocks a proposed separation by failure of absolute Hodgeness here. This is an imported theorem, not a fresh primary-source audit in this review.

**First test:** require a concrete proposed detector and prove that its universal necessity is not merely chain closure, existing Hodge type, topological realizability or the unknown quotient by algebraic classes. Without that specification this is a research programme, not an executable successor node.

Changing to a nonabelian source may change these restrictions, but needs its own explicit smooth projective source and rational Hodge class. No alternative source with a verified rational separator is supplied here.

## 4. Positive-cycle geometry: an inexpensive screen, not a rescue

On an abelian variety, generic translation makes two complementary-dimensional effective cycles intersect properly. Their intersection degree is nonnegative. Thus for an effective codimension-three class

    z=aE^3+bR+cI,

ampleness gives a>0 and translation gives

    2160a^2-32(b^2+c^2)>=0,
    b^2+c^2 <= (135/2)a^2.

This strengthens the previously recorded positive-degree test, but still does not exclude rational signed Weil classes. The numerical classes E^3+R and E^3-R both have positive square2128 and mutual pairing2192, while their half-difference is R. These are numerical consistency examples, NOT asserted effective cycles on X.

Therefore the displayed positivity constraints alone do not prove the effective cone spans only Q E^3. Proving that much sharper cone statement would again require new geometry. The previously rejected positivity route should not be reopened merely with this stronger inequality.

## Historically available construction routes, not reactivated

### Restricted commutative deformation of the corrected secant source

The old secant audit proves a trace-kernel failure on the FULL Hochschild deformation space and its persistence under the specified transforms. It explicitly does not prove failure on every ordinary complex-structure direction: its witnesses involve bivectors and B-fields.

A genuinely different test would transport the ACTUAL target Weil tangent subspace through the chosen Fourier-Mukai identification, restrict Hochschild evaluation to that subspace, and compute its obstruction image. This requires an actual source/target marking and transform, not simply discarding the bivector coordinates before transport. Even first-order vanishing would still need an all-order transport theorem and target matching.

This is more substantive than repeating the known full-HH failure. However it is a previously dismissed construction direction, and it is not activated here.

### Ramified generalized Prym matching

The retained genus16/8/4 construction is already an actual auxiliary cyclic cover; Lange-Ortega equivariance and Schoen applicability were checked. Repeating the genus calculation or the extra-action test adds nothing. The missing condition is a genuine K-equivariant rational H1/isogeny match to the fixed stabilized target, with the required cycle transfer.

Any reopened effort must first check the later target-specific exclusions and choose a genuinely surviving marking/family; the existence of the old auxiliary family alone is not evidence of target membership. Numerical period resemblance or survival of finite-prime screens is insufficient. This too remains dismissed rather than reopened by this review.
