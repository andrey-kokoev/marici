# Four-prime correlation observer: realization, minimality, and attachment

## Result

The six correlation probes of the four-prime reconstruction theorem admit explicit streaming operator realizations.

- On arbitrary words of directed cube-edge events, the minimal homogeneous linear state dimension is 13: one constant coordinate, six first-event memories, and six accumulated outputs. With the constant implemented as an affine input, there are twelve dynamic scalar coordinates.
- On admissible cube paths with the endpoint retained, the minimal endpoint-conditioned state bundle has total fiber dimension 22 and peak fiber dimension 6. It is realized by small rectangular edge maps.
- Adding its six outputs to the edge packet reconstructs all twenty-four full routes. The combined observation matrix has an exact left inverse.
- The observer attaches to the source route before additive interval/theta aggregation. An explicit equal-response pair proves the necessity of that placement.

These state minima concern linear switched realizations under the specified word/endpoint contracts. They do not bound arbitrary nonlinear encodings or storage in bits.

Executable implementation: `research/voevodsky/four_prime_correlation_observer.py`.

Exact checker: `research/voevodsky/checkers/check_four_prime_correlation_observer.py`.

## 1. Source, probes, and output contract

Cube vertices are four-bit masks. An edge e=(v,w,j) adds the unused prime index j, so w=v OR 2^j. A path starts at any vertex and adds distinct unused indices in order. All 168 paths, including empty paths, are admitted.

Use the six certified ordered-edge probes from the predecessor result:

| i | a_i, first edge | b_i, second edge |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | (1,3,1) | (3,7,2) |
| 1 | (1,5,2) | (5,7,1) |
| 2 | (1,9,3) | (9,11,1) |
| 3 | (2,6,2) | (6,7,0) |
| 4 | (2,10,3) | (10,11,0) |
| 5 | (4,12,3) | (12,13,0) |

For an event word w=e₁…e_k define

\[
y_i(w)=\sum_{r<s}1_{e_r=a_i}1_{e_s=b_i}.
\tag{1}
\]

On a cube path this is 0 or 1. On arbitrary event words it counts every ordered occurrence, including repeated events. A formal source mixture x=Σ_p c_p p has output y(x)=Σ_p c_p y(p).

The source endpoint and the reset location are part of the contract. Resetting at a vertex means no events before that vertex contribute.

## 2. A compositional thirteen-state operator

Let the state be

\[
z=(m,A_0,\ldots,A_5,B_0,\ldots,B_5)^T,
\qquad z_\emptyset=(1,0,\ldots,0)^T.
\]

For an edge event e use the linear update

\[
m'=m,
\quad A_i'=A_i+1_{e=a_i}m,
\quad B_i'=B_i+1_{e=b_i}A_i.
\tag{2}
\]

All right-hand sides refer to the old state. In matrix form,

\[
U_e=I+\sum_i1_{e=a_i}E_{A_i,m}
       +\sum_i1_{e=b_i}E_{B_i,A_i}.
\]

Let C select the B coordinates. Define U_w=U_(e_k)…U_(e_1). Induction on word length gives

\[
A_i(w)=\#\{r:e_r=a_i\},\qquad B_i(w)=y_i(w).
\]

Therefore y(w)=C U_w z_empty. The word composition law is exact:

\[
U_{pq}=U_q U_p.
\tag{3}
\]

The cross term generated by composing two blocks is precisely the second-order signature term:

\[
y_i(pq)=y_i(p)+A_i(p)\,\#\{b_i\text{ in }q\}+y_i(q).
\]

The retained first-event memory implements the cross-interface correlation.

### Weighted source packets

For a linear combination x define U_x=Σ_p c_p U_p. Bilinear concatenation satisfies U_(y∘x)=U_y U_x on a common composition domain. Thus the device reads signed and complex source combinations linearly.

Running it on an actual event stream computes the path observation before summation. `observe_mixture` evaluates the specified route-weighted sum. For factorized edge weights, the states can be propagated through a branching graph by the usual linear dynamic-programming sum. General joint route weights require their conditional routing data to remain available; the first-order edge marginals alone do not determine the result.

## 3. Minimality for unrestricted edge words

For a homogeneous linear realization y(w)=C U_w z with n states, choose prefixes p and suffix/output pairs (q,i). Its Hankel matrix is

\[
H_{(q,i),p}=y_i(pq).
\]

It factors as an observability matrix times a reachability matrix, so rank H≤n. This elementary factorization is the required lower bound.

For our observer, the following thirteen reachable states span the whole state space:

- z_empty;
- U_(a_i)z_empty, i=0,…,5;
- U_(b_i)U_(a_i)z_empty, i=0,…,5.

Subtracting z_empty from the second group gives the six A basis vectors. Subtracting the corresponding second-group vectors from the third gives the six B basis vectors. The construction uses that all selected a_i are distinct, all b_i are distinct, and the two edge families are disjoint.

For observability, use these thirteen rows:

- output i after the empty suffix, reading B_i;
- output i after suffix b_i, reading B_i+A_i;
- output 0 after suffix a₀b₀, reading B₀+A₀+m.

Their span is the full dual state space. Hence the resulting square Hankel submatrix has rank 13. Our realization attains the lower bound, proving homogeneous minimality.

An affine realization with n dynamic coordinates homogenizes in n+1 dimensions. The same lower bound gives n≥12, attained by holding m=1 outside the dynamic state.

This is a free-word theorem: every edge-event sequence is admitted, including repeated and endpoint-incompatible events. Their meaning is the ordered-event statistic (1).

## 4. Endpoint-conditioned minimal state bundle

For cube paths, future admissible events are determined by the current vertex. This allows removal of memories that can no longer affect an output.

Let V_v be the following coordinate subspace of the thirteen-state space:

| v | retained coordinates | dimension |
|---|---|---:|
| 0 | m | 1 |
| 1 | m | 1 |
| 2 | m | 1 |
| 3 | A₀ | 1 |
| 4 | m | 1 |
| 5 | A₁ | 1 |
| 6 | A₃ | 1 |
| 7 | B₀,B₁,B₃ | 3 |
| 8 | none | 0 |
| 9 | A₂ | 1 |
| 10 | A₄ | 1 |
| 11 | B₂,B₄ | 2 |
| 12 | A₅ | 1 |
| 13 | B₅ | 1 |
| 14 | none | 0 |
| 15 | B₀,…,B₅ | 6 |

Write E_v for the coordinate inclusion and P_v=E_v^T for restriction. For e:v→w define

\[
T_e=P_w U_e E_v,
\qquad z_v=P_vz_\emptyset,
\qquad C_v=C E_v.
\tag{4}
\]

These are rectangular maps between the listed state spaces. A path starting at v uses initial state z_v, multiplies the edge maps, and reads C at its endpoint.

### Why the compression is exact

Before the first selected edge, only m can affect future output. At a two-prime vertex, exactly one selected a_i enters that vertex; its A_i is the only memory that can still trigger a selected b_i. At a three-prime vertex, every selected b_i has already occurred or become inaccessible. Only the accumulated B coordinates remain relevant. At the terminal vertex all six outputs are retained.

Discarded coordinates have zero output under every admissible continuation from that vertex. Consequently repeated use of (4) preserves (1) on all source paths. The implementation checks this for every one of the 168 paths and rejects steps that add an already present prime.

### Vertexwise minimality

For each v define a typed Hankel matrix H_v:

- columns: all admissible prefixes ending at v, starting at any vertex;
- rows: a suffix starting at v together with one of the six output coordinates;
- entries: the output of the concatenated path.

Any linear state bundle realizing the same reset and continuation contract gives a factorization

\[
H_v=O_vR_v,
\qquad \operatorname{rank}H_v\le\dim V_v.
\tag{5}
\]

Our retained states meet this bound at every vertex. For the m fibers, a continuation completing a selected pair detects the reachable unit state. For an A_i fiber, a prefix containing a_i reaches its basis vector and suffix b_i detects it. For the B fibers, the corresponding completed two-edge paths reach the independent output basis vectors, visible under the empty suffix. At vertices 8 and 14 every relevant output is zero for every prefix/suffix pair.

Thus rank H_v equals each dimension in the table. The exact checker builds all sixteen typed Hankel matrices and verifies the equality. This proves componentwise minimality, total dimension 22, and peak dimension 6.

The peak refers to the state on one current path, with its vertex retained separately. A simultaneous graph computation has the direct sum of the relevant vertex fibers. The output registers are included in the dimension count. These minima concern the six correlation outputs; existing edge or theta measurement storage is additional.

## 5. Attachment to the analytical source

Let W be the source-to-edge observation on full routes, or any observation that factors through it, such as the additive interval/theta response with linear cycle coordinates. Let B be the six-row correlation readout.

The predecessor packet supplies the difference

\[
h=0123+1032-0132-1023.
\]

Direct calculation gives

\[
Wh=0,\qquad Bh=(1,0,0,0,0,0)^T.
\tag{6}
\]

For linear observations, B factors through W exactly when ker W⊆ker B. Equation (6) violates this condition. More strongly, the positive mixtures 0123+1032 and 0132+1023 have identical W outputs and different B outputs, so any deterministic decoder of W alone has the same obstruction.

The correct attachment is therefore

\[
\text{source route}
\longrightarrow
\begin{cases}
\text{existing interval/theta and cycle channels},\\
\text{streaming correlation observer},
\end{cases}
\longrightarrow
\text{joint output}.
\tag{7}
\]

Equation (7) is an implemented source-level measurement architecture. It specifies the data required at the analytical interface: the event sequence, or an equivalent stateful channel that preserves its joint route statistics.

The existing theta injectivity theorem can recover the finite interval coefficients on its admitted image. It leaves (6) unchanged because those interval coefficients themselves agree. The new branch must be measured before that aggregation.

## 6. Boundedness and full-route reconstruction

Give the finite source path space the orthonormal path-counting metric. Among all 168 paths, each selected pair occurs in exactly four paths: the optional preceding first edge and optional following last edge each have two choices, present or absent. The supports of the six probes are disjoint. Therefore

\[
BB^*=4I_6,\qquad\|B\|=2.
\tag{8}
\]

On the twenty-four full routes, each selected pair identifies exactly one route. Restricting B to that space gives

\[
B_{full}B_{full}^*=I_6,\qquad\|B_{full}\|=1.
\tag{9}
\]

These estimates use the explicitly chosen counting metric. A source-specific weighted inner product requires its corresponding Gram calculation.

Let E be the 32×24 full-route edge matrix. The implemented joint measurement is

\[
O=\begin{pmatrix}E\\B_{full}\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The checker verifies rank O=24 and constructs the exact rational reconstruction

\[
R=(O^TO)^{-1}O^T,\qquad RO=I_{24}.
\tag{10}
\]

Thus the operator realization supplies the six missing dimensions identified in the previous source reconstruction theorem. Under additive source-coordinate noise ε, the standard estimate is ||Rε||≤||R||·||ε||; a source-weighted stability claim requires the chosen input and output metrics.

## 7. Relation to the coherence system

The matrices U_e and the rectangular maps T_e give functors from the free source-path category into finite-dimensional linear spaces. Composition is matrix multiplication along the path. Forest changes affect the existing edge coordinates while the correlation functions remain attached to the same source routes. Therefore joint responses transport by the corresponding forest map on the edge part and identity on the correlation labels.

These observations distinguish different prime orders. They descend to a quotient identifying routes only when their assigned operator maps satisfy those particular relations. The tested contract is the retained free-path source.

The complete joint observation reconstructs the source path algebra, so the earlier exact equivalence of perfect module categories and its finite iterated S-constructions applies. The six-output observer alone is a measurement component; the combined observation supplies full reconstruction.

## 8. Implementation and verification

The reusable implementation has no third-party dependencies:

- `EdgeStreamObserver`: immutable thirteen-coordinate homogeneous realization;
- `EndpointCorrelationObserver`: immutable minimal endpoint-conditioned state;
- `observe_route(word, start)`: one path's six outputs;
