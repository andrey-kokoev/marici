# Packet, link and attested-replay retention are distinct verification tiers

A concrete bounded square-source verifier has three distinct tiers. The mathematical packet tier checks ordered row manifest, multiplier tuple, surplus and target normal/bound. The record-link tier ALSO checks trace-prefix digest and equality of adjacent occurrence commitments. The historical replay tier would additionally need commitment opening and a separately authenticated event/effect issuer attestation. Fresh `check_provenance_retention_tiers.py` removes one field at a time: missing multipliers -> `MATH_PACKET_INCOMPLETE`, missing prefix -> `LINK_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE`, missing opening -> `UNVERIFIABLE_TRACE`, and missing attestation -> `ISSUER_ATTESTATION_MISSING`.

The first two tiers pass in a local synthetic record; the third CANNOT pass here because no authenticated external issuer verifier/evidence is present. The listed fields are necessary for THESE verifier procedures, not a globally minimal sufficient statistic: alternate proof formats could encode the same mathematics differently. A hash's presence also does not independently attest occurrence or publisher authority. In particular neither a synthetic effect declaration nor graph actor admission resolves the Farkas row issuer gap.
