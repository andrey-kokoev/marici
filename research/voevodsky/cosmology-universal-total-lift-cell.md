# Universal cell classifier for the triple-incidence total lift

## Question

What exact typed datum is missing after the relative residue cocycle

\[
\Xi_{\log}+(-\sigma_{123})
\]

has been constructed?

## Claim boundary

This packet classifies the universal one-cell attachment that would kill the minimal relative residue class. It does not claim that this cell is sourced by geometry, resolved/Rees structure, a Cayley--Menger face, a physical contour, or a period.

## Disposition

The residue differential from degree-two generators \((\Xi_{\log},-\sigma_{123})\) to ordered pair-face residues is

\[
D=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&-1\\
-1&1\\
1&-1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its primitive integer kernel is generated by

\[
(1,1),
\]

which is the class \(\Xi_{\log}+(-\sigma_{123})\). The universal minimal attachment is a degree-one cell \(\tau_p\) with

\[
d\tau_p=\Xi_{\log}+(-\sigma_{123}).
\]

Then \(D(d\tau_p)=0\), so \(d^2=0\).

Any one-cell integral attachment with differential vector \((a,b)\) satisfies \(d^2=0\) exactly when \(a=b\). In that case it factors through \(\tau_p\) by multiplication by \(a\). It kills the primitive integral class only when \(a=\pm1\). A nonunit multiple kills only a multiple and cannot be treated as an integral lift of the missing primitive.

Over \(\mathbb F_{101}\) and \(\mathbb F_{103}\), the incoming rank before adjoining \(\tau_p\) is \(0\), after adjoining \(\tau_p\) is \(1\), and the residue differential rank is \(1\).

Thus the missing datum is now precise: find a source-derived generator or chain-map cone cell whose image is \(\tau_p\) with unit coefficient. Acceptable sources are a resolved/Rees exceptional generator, a logarithmic Čech--de Rham chain map, or a Cayley--Menger face cone with compensating residue. Declaring \(\tau_p\) directly, quotienting by the circuit, using a nonunit multiple, or dividing by \(p\) would not supply the source map.
