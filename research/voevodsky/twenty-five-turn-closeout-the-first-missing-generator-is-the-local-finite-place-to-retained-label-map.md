# Twenty-five-turn closeout: the first missing generator is the local finite-place-to-retained-label map

The seven-condition generator audit gives the following ordered result.

1. **Archimedean tail constructor — available on the declared radial core.**
   Full-line translation resolvents, reciprocal Volterra histories, endpoint
   traces, exponential rigging, and all Laplace jets are constructed.

2. **Finite-place source-to-label map — missing at full
   Schwartz--Bruhat strength.**  The retained architecture has explicit prime,
   valuation, shell, grade, and five-cell labels, and it realizes the standard
   theta/unramified orbit.  It does not provide, for an arbitrary local
   Bruhat--Schwartz generator `phi_p`, a canonical map into those retained
   labels that is compatible with local Fourier transform and convolution.

3. **Unramified unit normalization — available on the spherical lane.**  The
   standard local unit and its Euler half-density normalization are fixed, but
   this does not define the ramified generator map in item 2.

4. **Restricted-product continuity — blocked by item 2 and unproved.**

5. **Fourier intertwining — separately constructed on both sides.**  The
   idelic core `J_0` and retained sewing each exist; their cross-interface
   naturality is unformable until item 2 is supplied.

6. **Boundary-coordinate agreement — partial.**  Primitive and square
   value/connection normalizations, ordered Hardy response, endpoint, and
   internal connected-tail coordinates are known.  Their equality with one
   external generator image is not proved.

7. **Extension by continuity — blocked.**

Thus the first missing noncircular constructor is the family

\[
\iota_p:\mathcal S(\mathbb Q_p)
\longrightarrow
D_{{\rm ret},p}^{\rm labelled}
\]

with local Fourier, convolution, valuation-grade, and unramified-unit
compatibility.  Once these maps exist with restricted-product bounds, their
tensor product can define the external source-retaining span and make the
`C34` comparison evaluable.

The twenty-five-turn investigation therefore ends before the RH-strength
identity.  It has reduced the self-dual middle-facet problem from an undefined
higher filler to one explicit local-to-global constructor family, followed by
a known terminal bulk-current equality.
