# Signed selector comparisons telescope but forget the middle

Three finite partial selectors of the same old square source choose A=(0,1,0,1;c1), B=(1,2,0,1;c0) and C=(0,1,1,2;c0) for the same weak-row target. Their signed comparisons satisfy `(B-A)+(C-B)=C-A`, exactly in all four row multipliers AND surplus. Fresh `check_three_selector_comparisons.py` checks the rational equation, and requires the target selector/packet ID of the first edge to match the source selector/packet ID of the next. A same-labelled selector with a different intermediate packet is refused.

The path A->B->C and a path A->C->C have the SAME additive endpoint delta but distinct retained edge lists. Telescoping creates no equality of those proof histories and no automatically admitted higher cell. For historical replay, retain the middle packet ID, selector identities, candidate envelope identifiers and edge order; the endpoint delta alone is insufficient. This is a local mathematical comparison, not an issuer grant or analytic role map.

Without that witness, packet coordinates must not be silently aligned.
