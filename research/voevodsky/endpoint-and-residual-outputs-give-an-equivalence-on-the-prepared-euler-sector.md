# Endpoint and residual outputs give an equivalence on the prepared Euler sector

## Result and boundary

On the prepared exponential spectral sector, the ACTUAL labelled rigged response is a bounded isomorphism onto a closed, complemented output range. Its inverse uses only the endpoint output and a fixed decaying test of the weak window-residual output. Neither source port is retained with its strong input norm.

This establishes an output-only equivalence on that specified sector, including the actual prepared forcing windows and the nonzero first attachment witness. It does NOT establish equivalence on arbitrary Sobolev ports, nor reconstruct forcings from their spectral amplitudes.

An ordinary L2 realization on these same Euler states is unavailable: the already proved endpoint-subtracted zero-pole obstruction still applies.

## 1. Declare the restricted source sector explicitly

Fix the same compact Euler set K, with gamma>1/2, eta<=Im z<=Y, |z|<=Z and delta=eta-gamma>0. Put

    e_z(x)=exp(i z x),  s(z)=1/2-i z.

Let V_exp,K consist of continuous ports

    u(z)=a(z)e_z,   r(z)=d(z)e_z,   a,d in C(K),

with the inherited norm C(K;D_gamma) direct_sum C(K;H_gamma). This is a closed subspace: the scalar profile reconstruction from a bounded endpoint functional is a bounded projection onto each exponential family.

All actual prepared window ports lie here. Their even coefficient is sqrt(2)X_f(z), and their residual coefficient is sqrt(2)[iX_f'(z)-L(s(z))X_f(z)], since the supplied half-line operator acts on e_z by L(s(z)). No nonvanishing assumption on X_f is needed.

This is a restriction on spectral profiles, not on the number of retained source features or seam factors. We make no claim that every vector of the unrestricted Sobolev port belongs to it.

## 2. Use the supplied response, with no added source coordinate

Use precisely Nima's labelled response space, pointwise over K:

    R=H_-^+ direct_sum H_-^- direct_sum C^2
       direct_sum H_-^+ direct_sum H_-^+.

Its norm is the sum of the corresponding C(K) norms; each function coordinate is in the NEGATIVE-weight response space. The response map is

    O(u,r)=(B_rig u,-leak_rig u,J_end beta_end(u),r,u).

The last two entries are weak response copies, not the strong source coordinates from the earlier graph lift. They carry exactly the two prescribed residual cross-term labels. We retain all five supplied output labels.

The preceding theorem gives ||O||<=M=2C_A+C_beta+1. Finite-place maps O_P satisfy the same bound and ||O-O_P||<=3T_N.

## 3. Recover the even state from its endpoint output

Write c=(c_1,c_2)=J_end beta_end(u). For an exponential u=a e_z,

    c_2=ell_+(u)=a/(1/2-i z)=a/s(z).

Thus the output-only recovery is

    u(z)=s(z)c_2(z)e_z.

It divides by no source amplitude. Since

    ||e_z||_Dgamma=sqrt(1+|z|^2)/sqrt(2(Im z-gamma)),

its norm is bounded by

    ||u||_C(Dgamma) <= C_u ||c||_C(C2),
    C_u=(1/2+Z)sqrt(1+Z^2)/sqrt(2delta).

The chosen endpoint was already part of the prescribed arithmetic output; it is not a new coordinate fitted to reconstruct u.

## 4. Recover the residual from its weak output

Fix t>gamma, for example t=gamma+1. The test h_t(x)=exp(-t x) belongs to the positive-weight test space with norm 1/sqrt(2(t-gamma)). Therefore

    lambda_t(w)=integral_0^infinity exp(-t x)w(x)dx

is bounded on H_-^+. Applied to the fourth output w=r=d e_z it gives

    lambda_t(w)=d/(t-i z).

Hence

    r(z)=(t-i z)lambda_t(w(z))e_z,

and

    ||r||_C(Hgamma) <= C_r ||w||_C(H_-^+),
    C_r=(t+Z)/(2sqrt(delta(t-gamma))).

This estimate uses only the weak RESPONSE norm of w. It does not quietly reintroduce the old strong norm of r.

Define J on the entire output carrier by these two recovery formulas, ignoring the other coordinates for purposes of recovery. It maps boundedly into V_exp,K, with

    ||J||<=C_inv=max(C_u,C_r),   J O=identity on V_exp,K.

Its output depends continuously on z even for arbitrary continuous response inputs.

## 5. A genuine closed output range and paired equivalence

The actual prepared output range is

    O_exp,K=O(V_exp,K).

The operator OJ is a bounded idempotent on the ambient output space, since JO is the identity on V_exp,K. Consequently O_exp,K is closed and complemented. On it, J is the bounded inverse of O.

It is essential to keep the range condition. An arbitrary independent five-tuple of response coordinates need not be generated by one prepared source port. The projection OJ reconstructs a prepared port and returns its actual labelled response; it is not claimed to preserve inconsistent arbitrary response data.

Let E(u,r)=(u,0,beta_end(u),u,r) be the independently supplied test map. Define the output pairing using that original test/response evaluation:

    Q_out(o,o')=<E J o,o'>,   o,o' in O_exp,K,

with ordered spectral evaluations when required. This gives exactly

    Q_out(O p,O p')=Q_res(p,p').

The pairing is Hermitian on the range, by the existing response theorem, and bounded by C_E C_inv times the two output norms. It has not been chosen by fitting a scalar Gram: it is the existing evaluation with a PROVED inverse on its actual range.

The positive compression, negative leakage, endpoint and two cross-term labels remain present. In particular, reconstructing the input does not erase the negative response that independent negative tests observe.

## 6. Completed coefficient records and actual source descent

Apply O and J to each retained feature slot, in buffers as well as seams. Ordered projective tensor products give bounds M^m and C_inv^m on m-feature records. Vacua remain identity sectors. Therefore these maps are inverse continuous coefficient-complex maps on the all-feature-radius completion scales, with the explicit radius changes

    b -> max(1,M)b,   b -> max(1,C_inv)b.

The restricted source-generated target is legitimate: every prepared forcing letter lies in V_exp,K, and the coefficient differential, concatenation and normalization only transport such letters. They do not change their spectral profile.

Both maps are linear and commute with the ordered slot operations. Thus the original relation and balancing kernels, the derivative identities D(I^2)=0 and D2(I^3)=0, and the attachment shift are preserved. This is equivalence with the PREPARED-sector coefficient target, not with the whole unrestricted Sobolev target.

At finite P, J O_P is also the identity: the recovery coordinates have zero cutoff error. Their actual output ranges are likewise closed, and O_Q J identifies them consistently. Their forms retain the stated finite arithmetic error with the window residual held fixed.

For arbitrary feature count, tensor telescoping bounds the cutoff error by m M^(m-1)3T_N. For any c>max(1,M),

    m M^(m-1) <= c^m/(c-M).

Thus the same record estimate is uniform after enlarging the input feature radius to c b. This supplies the feature-radius summation on this prepared output sector; it does not confuse two seams with two features.

## 7. The nonzero observer belongs to the recoverable sector

At the witness point z_0=i y, Nima's observer is o_0=(e_z0,-2L_0 e_z0). Replace its constant spectral extension by the continuous prepared extension

    o(z)=(e_z,-2L_0 e_z).

It agrees with o_0 at z_0, where the witness is evaluated, and lies in V_exp,K. The two window normalizations X_1(z_0),X_2(z_0) remain fixed nonzero scalars for this finite witness only.

Transport this observer and the selected source product through O_records. The paired equivalence yields the unchanged value

    w_seam sqrt(2)(mu_2-mu_1)/(2y)>0.

Hence the output-only target retains the actual nonzero completed attachment roof. One can either transport its source-equivariant finite nullhomotopy obstruction or include the restricted target into the old target: a zero restricted roof would give a zero old roof, contradicting the already detected class.

No inverse on the full continuous dual, root-state duplication, or projectivity of completed ideals is used.

## 8. What this establishes instead of an impossible L2 promotion

Established: a bounded, output-only, paired coefficient equivalence ON THE PREPARED EXPONENTIAL SECTOR, using the original endpoint and weak residual outputs; its closed complemented range; and preservation of the nonzero attachment witness.

Not established: a bounded inverse on arbitrary Sobolev ports, a Tate-only inverse after discarding endpoint/residual outputs, recovery of the original forcing from its amplitudes, perfect duality, positivity, or an ordinary L2 response realization.

The latter would contradict the proved Laplace pole obstruction on every nonzero finite Euler packet, even after prescribed endpoint subtraction. The present equivalence succeeds in the weighted-dual topology and does not circumvent that obstruction.
