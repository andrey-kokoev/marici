# Source infinite rank forces enlargement of the radial trace carrier

## Question

Can the coherent 26-coordinate synthetic trace carrier be identified with the source-derived diagonal shell and all-jet response family?

## Claim boundary

No faithful linear identification exists if the source family has the infinite-rank property proved in Nima's diagonal-shell Wronskian packet. This is a rank obstruction, not a failure of the finite synthetic model and not a construction of the required infinite-dimensional carrier.

## Source pullback

The source packet `research/nima/the-diagonal-shell-wronskian-family-is-infinite-rank-and-cannot-factor-through-the-scalar-wall-incidence-plane.md` proves that every finite family at distinct shell locations is linearly independent. The Laplace-jet packet supplies a triangular recurrence for the parameter jets but does not identify them with a finite G4 port.

Choose 27 distinct shell locations. Their source responses span a 27-dimensional subspace. Any linear map from that subspace to the synthetic sampled trace space \(\mathbb R^{26}\) has nonzero kernel by rank-nullity. Therefore the 26-coordinate carrier cannot be jointly faithful on the source family.

This conclusion is stronger than the polynomial sampling example: it uses the currently recorded source-derived infinite-rank family rather than an unrestricted hypothetical polynomial carrier.

## Surviving comparison

The synthetic chain can remain a finite observation functor

\[
\mathcal O_{26}:Y_{\mathrm{source}}\longrightarrow\mathbb R^{26},
\]

but its fibers must remain unresolved. Evans zeros, distances, and RH factors computed after \(\mathcal O_{26}\) are properties of the finite observation, not complete invariants of the source response.

## Required successor

A faithful source comparison requires an infinite-dimensional retained trace carrier, or a source-derived restriction selecting a subspace of dimension at most 26 on which the observation is injective. A growing family of finite stages is admissible only with explicit structural maps and a completion or conservativity theorem; no single finite stage establishes the unbounded claim.

## Disposition

The finite synthetic chain is retained as a tested finite-cutoff model. Its promotion to the full diagonal-shell/all-jet source family is rejected by rank. The next constructive path is an indexed trace tower with declared transition maps, leaving completion and source identification open until separately proved.
