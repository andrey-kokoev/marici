# The two diagonals of the opposite face

Let the installed square have supports `{f,xi,yj}` where X and Y are adjacent pairs. Its covered set U has five sites. The other five sites carry a red pentagon in addition to the required path (all path edges between outside sites belong to the pentagon). The opposite face replaces f with the adjacent outside site g. Assume all eight rows are separated. Keep the prescribed red links to disjoint parent rows.

One opposite port is legal. Its support contains only one outside site and no path edge, so it cannot close a triangle with two sites. Its only red parent link is to the opposite corner, a disjoint support. In a blue set with this port, at most two outside sites are available, since a pentagon has no blue triple. At most three parents are available. With zero, one, two or three parent ports, at most three, two, one or zero covered sites respectively can join them (distinct corners cover at least four sites; any three cover all five). Thus the set has at most six vertices. Two opposite ports share g, so their mutual link must be blue; any new obstruction must use both.

For two corners on an edge write the rows as `{g,x,y0}` and `{g,x,y1}`. The two parents through x, and the unused partner x' of x, are all blue to both ports and to one another. It suffices to find two mutually blue outside sites other than g that are blue to x'. There are four outside choices before this last restriction. Since x' has x as one path neighbor, at most one of those four is adjacent to x'. Cross edges from x' to the outside are only path edges. At least three choices remain, and among them two are blue to each other (the red pentagon has no triangle). These vertices give a blue seven. The same proof works with X and Y exchanged.

For a diagonal the rows use all four movable sites. Of the covered sites only f can join both ports. Exactly two parents meet both rows: the two cross corners. No such parent can join f in blue. Hence the covered sites and parent ports supply at most two vertices in total. The pentagon supplies at most two more. Even including both new ports the total is at most six. Thus both diagonals work, regardless of which legal pentagon is present; no selection of a pentagon or extra repair link is required.
