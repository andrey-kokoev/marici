# Every Suzuki Pontryagin defect is detected at a finite Gaussian rung

## Setting

Let the completed Suzuki/de Branges kernel have a positive-minus-positive realization

\[
Q(f)=\|A_Sf\|^2-\|A_Bf\|^2.
\]

where `A_B` takes values in the Blaschke defect space `K_B`. Let

\[
M_1\subset M_2\subset\cdots,
\qquad
M_\infty=\bigcup_nM_n,
\]

be the forward system generated by Gaussian translates. Assume `M_infinity` is form-dense in the completed observer domain. This is the faithful-completion condition already required by the rung-four programme.

## Finite detection theorem

Suppose `Q` has a negative vector `f` in the completion:

\[
Q(f)<0.
\]

By form density, there exists a sequence `f_n in M_infinity` converging to `f` in a topology in which both feature maps are continuous. Hence

\[
A_Sf_n\to A_Sf,
\qquad
A_Bf_n\to A_Bf,
\]

and therefore

\[
Q(f_n)\to Q(f)<0.
\]

For all sufficiently large `n`, `Q(f_n)<0`. Each `f_n` belongs to some finite stage `M_{N(n)}`. Consequently

\[
\boxed{
Q\text{ indefinite on the completion}
\Longrightarrow
Q|_{M_N}\text{ indefinite for some finite Gaussian rung }N.
}
\]

No zero localization or numerical sampling enters this argument. It is a consequence of form-core density and continuity.

## Generalized contraction constants

At stage `N`, define

\[
\kappa_N^2
=
\sup_{0\ne f\in M_N}
\frac{\|A_Bf\|^2}{\|A_Sf\|^2},
\]

with value `infinity` if `ker A_S` is not contained in `ker A_B`. Since the spaces are nested,

\[
\kappa_1\le\kappa_2\le\cdots.
\]

The restricted form is positive semidefinite exactly when

\[
\kappa_N\le1.
\]

If the completion has a negative direction, finite detection gives

\[
\kappa_N>1
\]

at some finite stage. Conversely, positivity at every finite stage and form-core continuity imply positivity on the completion.

Thus

\[
\boxed{
Q\succeq0
\iff
\sup_N\kappa_N\le1.
}
\]

This is the contraction form of forward rung-four coherence.

## Effect of a finite Blaschke defect

If `deg B=r<infinity`, then `A_B` has rank at most `r`, but this does not imply detection by rung `r`. A vector may need many positive-feature coordinates to cancel its `A_S` component while retaining a defect component. Rank controls the dimension of the hostile quotient, not the first packet stage where the generalized Rayleigh quotient exceeds one.

Nevertheless, once the projected Gaussian features span `K_B`, the finite matrices contain every defect coordinate. What remains is comparison with the positive bulk. This separates two finite questions:

1. **defect observability:** does `A_B(M_N)=K_B`?
2. **defect domination:** is `kappa_N<=1`?

The first is linear and eventually true for a dense source family. The second is the actual positivity gate.

## No invisible off-axis sector under faithful completion

Suppose an off-axis divisor creates a nonzero negative Pontryagin direction but every finite Gaussian rung is positive. The theorem forces that direction to be discontinuous or invisible in the declared completion. Therefore at least one of the required contracts fails:

- the Gaussian union is not a form core;
- `A_B` is not continuous in the completion norm;
- the terminal divisor readout is unbounded;
- or the finite forms are not exact restrictions of the completed Weil form.

Thus a proposed carrier cannot evade finite detection by placing the off-axis sector “only at infinity” while retaining faithful completion.

## Relation to source-derived proof attempts

The monotone sequence `kappa_N` is not itself a proof mechanism: evaluating it is equivalent to finite Gram positivity. Its conceptual value is that it rules out a completion-only repair. Any universal source contraction must provide compatible contractions

\[
C_N:A_S(M_N)\to K_B,
\qquad
A_B=C_NA_S,
\qquad
\|C_N\|\le1,
\]

whose restrictions agree as `N` increases. Their direct-limit contraction then proves global positivity. Independent finite Cholesky or Douglas choices are insufficient because they need not be coherent.

The exact categorical target is therefore a natural transformation of the forward feature system, not unrelated stagewise contractions.

## Disposition

Every genuine Suzuki Pontryagin defect is visible at a finite Gaussian stage:

\[
\boxed{
\text{nonzero negative completed sector}
\Longrightarrow
\text{finite rung cone-admission failure}.
}
\]

Accordingly the only abstract positive construction that could complete the programme is a coherent family of source-defined contractions with a uniform norm bound. Neither an infinite-boundary quotient nor a defect postponed to completion can bypass the finite gates.
