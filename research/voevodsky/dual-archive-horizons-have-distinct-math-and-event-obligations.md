# Dual archive horizons have distinct math and event obligations

Freeze two task deadlines: a fresh mathematical row replay at tick 3 and a fresh event-A archive audit at tick 2. Archive A holds event-A scope; A and B both hold the same mathematical row bytes. Exhaustive `check_dual_archive_horizons.py` checks 25 pairs of inclusive availability lifetimes through tick 4. In this finite menu the necessary-and-sufficient service condition is **A available through tick 2 AND at least one of A or B available through tick 3**. If A expires after tick 1 while B survives through tick 3, math replay succeeds but event audit fails. A prior successful tick-0 event receipt is historical evidence, not a replacement for the required FRESH tick-2 audit.

The condition is a declared service-level retention horizon, not a claim that A's bytes carry special mathematics or that B can inherit A's event authorization. Provider B can substitute for row-byte availability at tick 3, but not for event-A provenance at tick 2. The model has hypothetical process-local provider lifetimes; it does not authenticate real issuers, model concurrent revocation races or calculate complete storage/work cost.

The next discriminating gate is a timed revocation BETWEEN validation and publication: if a provider is revoked after a read but before a promised protected audit result commits, either hold the owner lock across both phases or refuse the commit. Ordinary mathematical replay may retain its earlier checked conclusion; operational publication must not race a stale capability. Analytic S,A,R,C,G remains deferred.
