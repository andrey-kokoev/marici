# Physical coupling census finds no thimble-to-syndrome interaction

## Question

Does an existing physical construction take the unprepared cusp thimble as input and write its two integral parity pairings into measurable syndrome responses?

## Required coupling

A noncircular coupling must have the form

\[
U:
\mathcal T_m\otimes|\mathrm{ready}\rangle
\longrightarrow
\mathcal T'_m\otimes|\mathrm{record}(m)\rangle
\]

with two calibrated responses satisfying

\[
\mathrm{response}_1(m)
=\langle\mathcal T_m,e_6^\vee\rangle\pmod2,
\]

\[
\mathrm{response}_2(m)
=\langle\mathcal T_m,v_{\rm alg}^\vee\rangle\pmod2.
\]

The input must be the source thimble or a proved physical image of it. Preparing a record from chosen bits does not satisfy this condition.

## Candidate census

### Bunch--Davies local germ

It is source-authorized locally, but its punctured family has trivial monodromy and produces no \(\mu_2\)-odd thimble. It cannot write either missing global parity bit.

### Cayley--Menger contour family

It supplies an oriented semialgebraic physical cycle and measure normalization. The target weighted exceptional specialization, coefficient comparison, deck trace, and overlap homotopy are absent. It is not mapped to the cusp thimble or conductor syndrome.

### Published local Leray tube

It fixes the negative-imaginary local tube and normalized residue on generic transverse patches. It does not compute global variation or transport across the remote discriminant, so it cannot choose the global thimble lift.

### Known physical Cut

Its covector is zero on the elliptic coinvariant information. It is a null channel for the target class and cannot supply either response.

### Five- and six-site analogues

They construct nontrivial physical-sheet pairings on different carriers. No specialization or transport theorem maps them to the target three-site cusp. They are patterns, not couplings.

### Optical syndrome encoders

They faithfully encode and decode a supplied conductor class. Their preparation map begins after the missing cusp-to-conductor comparison and is therefore circular as a parity measurement.

## Result

No candidate satisfies all three gates:

1. source-authorized input from the target cusp thimble;
2. nontrivial two-direction response;
3. equality of those responses with the integral \(e_6\) and \(v_{\rm alg}\) pairings.

The Aspect source-authority census reaches the same boundary: local boundary-value authority exists, but transportable global activation authority, deck compatibility, wall compatibility, the odd twisted pairing, and a continued physical-period covector remain open.

## Acceptance test for new candidates

A new coupling is admissible only if it supplies:

- a chain map from the globally marked relative thimble into the physical source space;
- two independently calibrated response covectors;
- a commutative comparison with the two integral Picard duals;
- invariance under allowed path homotopies and declared behavior under site exchange;
- a nonzero hostile test on at least one nontrivial parity class and zero on the trivial class.

## Disposition

The physical coupling search is exhausted within current artifacts. The first missing object is not detector hardware but a target Betti/coefficient comparison that transports the globally marked thimble into a physical interaction. Without it, physical activation and parity readout remain undefined.
