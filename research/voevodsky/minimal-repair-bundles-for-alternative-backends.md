# Minimal repair bundles for alternative coherence backends

## Question

How should blockers be reported when an obligation has alternative evidence backends rather than one conjunctive prerequisite set?

## Claim boundary

This packet gives finite monotone-Boolean repair semantics. It does not choose the scientific validity of any backend or infer dependency edges absent from typed theorems.

## Monotone admission formula

Let each certificate predicate be a Boolean variable. A proof backend with requirement set \(P\) contributes the conjunction

\[
\bigwedge_{p\in P}p,
\]

and an operational backend with requirement set \(O\) contributes another conjunction. Mathematical admission is the monotone formula

\[
\Phi=
\left(\bigwedge_{p\in P}p\right)
\lor
\left(\bigwedge_{o\in O}o\right).
\]

Its realization domain is a union of upward-open sets. It need not be a principal upward set generated by one prerequisite bundle.

## Minimal repair bundles

At certificate state \(b\), a repair bundle is a set of currently false predicates whose addition makes \(\Phi\) true after dependency closure. A bundle is minimal when no proper subset repairs the obligation.

The correct blocker is the antichain

\[
\mathcal R_b(\Phi)
=\{\text{minimal repair bundles at }b\}.
\]

This is an antichain of sets under inclusion, not one antichain of individual predicates. For backends blocked by `{filler}` and `{constructor, faults}`, the repair object is

\[
\big\{\{\texttt{filler}\},
\{\texttt{constructor},\texttt{faults}\}\big\}.
\]

Taking the union of all missing predicates would falsely require both backends.

## Composition algebra

For obligations \(\Phi\) and \(\Psi\):

- alternative admission \(\Phi\lor\Psi\) unions their repair bundles and removes supersets;
- conjunctive composition \(\Phi\land\Psi\) forms all pairwise unions of repair bundles and removes supersets.

This gives an idempotent antichain semiring:

- addition is minimized union;
- multiplication is minimized pairwise set union.

Associativity and distributivity follow from ordinary union followed by canonical minimization.

## Dependency closure

Before minimization, a proposed repair must include or imply every missing predecessor required by the certificate DAG. Closure may make one backend bundle subsume another. Dependency closure and backend disjunction remain distinct operations.

## Pyramid consequence

The singular field `minimal_failed_prerequisites` remains valid for one conjunctive record, but alternative-backend obligations need:

- `admission_formula`;
- `backend_requirement_sets`;
- `minimal_repair_bundles`;
- `dependency_closed_bundles`;
- `bundle_subsumption`;
- `selected_backend`, only after one complete branch is materially realized.

No preferred backend should be selected merely because its bundle has fewer fields; cost, authority, and scientific scope are typed separately.

## Disposition

Alternative evidence changes blocker semantics from a flat prerequisite antichain to an antichain of minimal repair bundles. This is the correct compositional object for the certificate-fibred computad and prevents accidental conjunction of independent proof and operational routes.
