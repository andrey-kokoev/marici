## Foundational interpretation still open

The checks establish a direction-free mathematical account of observational access, compatibility and information loss. They do NOT establish that all conventional execution is identical to positive observer area, that physical time emerges from this structure, or that observers themselves have been internalized as physical subsystems.

Calling supported observation execution is a possible definition. Doing so makes the identification definitional; the explanatory work remains in showing what that identification predicts or faithfully represents. The existing tiny directed machine is a checked separate fragment, NOT yet a theorem equating every machine execution with manifestation in this observation model.

There is also no universal observer that sees all distinctions: coarse observers can conceal real higher coherence obstructions. Neither membership positivity nor an equality of visible outputs licenses fabrication of missing comparisons.
