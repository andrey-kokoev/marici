## 4. Actual balanced reassociation and the pentagon

    alpha: ((A tensor_bal B) tensor_bal C)

The map and its inverse are bounded on the fixed-shape projective/quotient presentations. Canonical tensor reassociation is isometric, and any additional admitted normalization retains its existing bounds. There is no permutation of graded factors. The five parenthesizations are

    (((AB)C)D), ((A(BC))D), A((BC)D), A(B(CD)), (AB)(CD).
