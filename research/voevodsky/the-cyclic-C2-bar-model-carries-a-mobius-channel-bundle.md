# The cyclic C2 bar model carries a Möbius channel bundle

## Cyclic closure

Use the identity-loop component of the cyclic bar construction of \(C_2\). In reduced coordinates its degree-\(n\) simplices are

\[
B_n(C_2)=C_2^n.
\]

Writing the group law additively, define cyclic rotation by

\[
\tau_n(g_1,\ldots,g_n)
=
(g_1+\cdots+g_n,g_1,\ldots,g_{n-1}).
\]

Because every element of \(C_2\) is its own inverse, this is the rotation obtained by restoring the omitted closing edge and moving it to the front. It satisfies

\[
\tau_n^{n+1}=1
\]

and the cyclic face and degeneracy identities. Geometric realization therefore carries the cyclic circle direction needed to close the arity coordinate.

## Twisted channel local system

Let the channel fiber be generated by \(\nu_{\triangleleft}\) and \(\nu_{\triangleright}\). Give one circuit of the cyclic direction the holonomy

\[
J=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-1\\
-1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The associated bundle is the mapping torus

\[
\mathcal V_J=
(\mathbb R\times V)/((t+1,v)\sim(t,Jv)).
\]

The difference vector is fixed by \(J\), while the sum vector changes sign. Hence

\[
\mathcal V_J
\cong
\underline{\mathbb R}\oplus\mathcal M,
\]

where \(\mathcal M\) is the Möbius line bundle. Since the determinant of \(J\) is negative, the rank-two channel bundle is nonorientable.

## Geometric meaning

There are two coherence-plane branches in every local trivialization. Transport around the arity circle exchanges them with the prescribed signs. Consequently there is no global initial plane and no global final plane. Those names exist only after cutting the circle and choosing a local trivialization.

The Möbius gluing therefore removes the suspicious global endpoint labels rather than disguising them.

## Scope

This construction is canonical for the identity-loop component of the cyclic \(C_2\) bar model once the signed channel representation is coupled to its circle action. A bare simplicial 2-Segal object does not automatically possess this cyclic enhancement or local system.

Four-periodic typing remains separate at this stage. Replacing \(J\) by an order-four holonomy would produce a different twisted bundle and requires its own compatibility check.
