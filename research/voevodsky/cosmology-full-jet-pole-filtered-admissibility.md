# Full-jet pole-filtered admissibility

## Result

Among 101,592 positive-jet seed components, 101,580 are certified within their target pole grade. Twelve chosen words violate the filtration by exactly one grade.

All twelve are first-order second-tangent q targets. Their target grades are six, seven, or eight; each three-term primitive reaches the next grade. Every first-jet `nx` and declared tangent word, every second-jet word, and every order-three through order-six word is grade-admissible. Structural zero rows require no primitive.

## Classification

The twelve cases are unresolved observations. A high-grade chosen word proves only that its certificate is not filtered-admissible. It does not prove the target is nonzero in the filtered quotient because another primitive may exist in the full grade-bounded image.

The discriminating test is exact membership of each target in the image generated by source rows of grade at most its target grade.

## Claim boundary

The filtration is algebraic pole order, not a DNC or I-adic filtration. Even a genuine filtered obstruction here would not acquire geometric or exceptional meaning without the missing comparison.
