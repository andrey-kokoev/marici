# Prior research exposes two distinct theta Green branches separated by an explicit wall--tail cross operator

## Question

Does the prior source record already determine the missing theta-history Green polarization, and does Fourier-saturated wall--tail orthogonality settle its target Gram?

## Claim boundary

Prior research determines two different source-authorized quadratic branches, not one identified form. Their difference is the explicit cross operator \(M_\Phi(B+B^*)\). Fourier saturation proves zero cross block only for the saturated observer branch; it does not remove the cross term from the causal history graph. A comparison/congruence theorem is still required.

## History decomposition

Prior work fixes

$$
H_\Phi=M_\Phi I+B.
$$

The genuine input-output history graph energy is

$$
\mathfrak G_{\rm graph}(f,h)
=
\langle f,h\rangle
+
\langle H_\Phi f,H_\Phi h\rangle.
$$

Expanding gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathfrak G_{\rm graph}(f,h)
={}&(1+M_\Phi^2)\langle f,h\rangle
+
\langle Bf,Bh\rangle\\
&+M_\Phi\langle f,Bh\rangle
+M_\Phi\langle Bf,h\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

## Resolved orthogonal branch

A separately selected resolved form is

$$
\mathfrak G_{\rm res}(f,h)
=(1+M_\Phi^2)\langle f,h\rangle
+
\langle Bf,Bh\rangle.
$$

Its complete adjacent-window matrix is already explicit. In particular,

$$
\langle BW_L,BW_{2L}\rangle
=
2\left(
C_{K_+*\rho}(L)-C_{K_+*\rho}(3L)
\right)>0.
$$

Thus ordinary, diagonal-history, and mixed resolved-tail entries are not missing integrations.

## Exact branch difference

The two forms differ by

$$
\boxed{
\mathfrak G_{\rm graph}-\mathfrak G_{\rm res}
=M_\Phi\left(B+B^*\right)
}
$$

in operator notation, or

$$
M_\Phi\left(
\langle f,Bh\rangle+
\langle Bf,h\rangle
\right)
$$

at the polarized level.

This is the wall--tail cross energy of one mass-transport graph, not an arbitrary extra block.

## What Fourier saturation proves

The Fourier-saturated observer decomposes wall and tail into opposite Fourier-square character planes. In that observer metric,

$$
G_{\rm wall,tail}^{\rm sat}=0.
$$

This authorizes \(\mathfrak G_{\rm res}\)-type orthogonality on the saturated observer branch. It does not imply

$$
\operatorname{Re}\langle f,Bf\rangle=0
$$

on the actual Adams incidence range, and therefore does not identify \(\mathfrak G_{\rm graph}\) with \(\mathfrak G_{\rm res}\).

Topology preservation only allows either form to be transported continuously. It cannot make the two forms equal.

## Correction to the recent topology claim

The statement that oscillator/Schwartz transport authorizes the zero wall--tail block remains true only inside the saturated observer metric. It does not close the target theta-history Green polarization if that target is the causal history graph metric.

Therefore the prior conclusion that the target-Gram frontier had reduced solely to fourth-grade pairings was too strong. The fourth-grade scalar response data are explicit, but one must additionally select or compare the two quadratic branches.

## Minimal remaining theorem

There are two legitimate routes:

1. prove on the represented incidence range that
   $$
   \langle f,Bh\rangle+
   \langle Bf,h\rangle=0;
   $$
2. construct an explicit block congruence or Schur transformation carrying the history graph form to the resolved saturated form while preserving labels, reciprocal orientation, and the four matrix units.

Absent one of these, substituting the saturated zero into the history graph erases a source-derived cross energy.

## Evans implication

The augmented history Green identity also retains the forcing pairing

$$
2\operatorname{Re}(c\langle u,\Phi\rangle).
$$

Maximal-isotropic boundary sewing removes endpoint flux but not this bulk term. The arithmetic adjoint residual must supply its exact opposite. This is consistent with, rather than bypassed by, the wall--tail branch distinction.

## Disposition

Prior research does expose the theta Green candidates and the exact unresolved operator between them. The target is not wholly unspecified: the resolved and causal graph forms are explicit, and their discrepancy is \(M_\Phi(B+B^*)\). The open local theorem is now the source-derived branch comparison on the Adams incidence range, followed by the already frozen four matrix-unit test.
