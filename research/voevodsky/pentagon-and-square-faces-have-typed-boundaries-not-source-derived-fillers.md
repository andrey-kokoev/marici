# Pentagon and square faces have typed boundaries, not source-derived fillers

The five-leaf reassociation graph has two different kinds of minimal oriented face boundary: three squares, each with parallel two-step rotation words, and six pentagons, each with parallel two- versus three-step words. Fresh `check_typed_associahedron_faces.py` enumerates all nine short cycles, checks that each has a unique source and target tree with exactly two directed boundary paths, and verifies those route lengths. This refines the earlier cycle count into exact 3-cell BOUNDARIES; a square/interchange generator and a pentagon generator are separate types, not interchangeable witnesses.

All five primitive proof leaves can root the endpoint packets and the rotation edges. They do not supply an independent constructor of a square or pentagon filler. The checker refuses omitted leaf roots, self-asserted fillers, and `equal packet` offered as a filler. Merely adding face names would define a new higher presentation, not prove that existing Farkas records already have those cells. Five-leaf overlap of such added face cells would then ask for 4-cell coherence, still unconstructed.

The defensible current claim is an exact obstruction in the free rotation-path category plus a bounded tagged contract that exposes histories without equating them. A successor should test whether a particular higher construction (e.g. nerve of the poset of bracketings, or contractible associahedral cell complex) can be independently typed as a NEW mathematical enrichment, and explicitly mark where it ceases to be faithful to replayable histories. The analytic S,A,R,C,G map remains deferred.
