# Order commutator gives a cumulative-sum completion

## Question

What completed Hilbert object is actually defined by the finite order--Mellin commutator norm?

## Claim boundary

For an ordered discrete label set, its zero-sum completion is canonically a weighted sequence space on adjacent logarithmic gaps. Equivalently, it is the closed subspace of interval-step functions generated by those gaps. This supplies a source-derived nonlocal topology, but no comparison with the completed Weil form.

## Cumulative-sum transform

Let

\[
\lambda_0<\lambda_1<\cdots
\]

be the arithmetic labels and let \(c\) have finite support with

\[
\sum_i c_i=0.
\]

Define

\[
(Jc)(u)
=
\sqrt2
\sum_{\lambda_i>u}c_i.
\]

Then the order-commutator identity is

\[
\lVert c\rVert_{\rm ord}^2
=
2\int_{\mathbb R}
\left|
\sum_{\lambda_i>u}c_i
\right|^2du
=
\lVert Jc\rVert_{L^2(du)}^2.
\]

Thus \(J\) is an isometry on the finite zero-sum domain.

## Adjacent-difference basis

Set

\[
d_i=e_i-e_{i+1}.
\]

The image \(Jd_i\) is, up to sign, the constant \(\sqrt2\) on

\[
[\lambda_i,\lambda_{i+1})
\]

and zero elsewhere. These intervals are disjoint, so

\[
\langle d_i,d_j\rangle_{\rm ord}
=
2(\lambda_{i+1}-\lambda_i)\delta_{ij}.
\]

Consequently the completion is

\[
\ell^2
\left(
2(\lambda_{i+1}-\lambda_i)
\right).
\]

For \(\lambda_i=\log n_i\), the weights are

\[
2\log\frac{n_{i+1}}{n_i}.
\]

## Important limitation

The completion is not automatically all of

\[
L^2([\lambda_0,\infty),du).
\]

It is the closed subspace of functions constant on each fixed adjacent-label interval. The labels do not refine those intervals merely because the cutoff grows. Claiming the full interval space would silently add intra-gap degrees of freedom.

## Coherencer interpretation

Unlike density multiplication, \(J\) mixes all coefficients above a cut \(u\). It is therefore genuinely nonlocal and evades the multiplication-intertwiner no-go.

It also reveals the primitive arithmetic observer: each adjacent logarithmic gap carries one orthogonal coordinate. The global order norm is obtained by gluing these independent gap observers.

The next comparison problem is now explicit. For a heat-polynomial coefficient packet \(c_{t,h,p}\), determine whether

\[
Jc_{t,h,p}
\]

lies in a domain on which the completed gamma-plus-prime form is closable and bounded below. Any proposed map to the zero-side or Hardy form must be defined on this weighted sequence completion.

## Residual

The unresolved residual is no longer the existence of the order completion. It is the absence of a source-derived form operator \(A_{\Gamma+P}\) on \(\mathcal H_{\rm ord}\) such that

\[
\mathcal Q_{\Gamma+P}(c)
=
\langle Jc,A_{\Gamma+P}Jc\rangle
\]

on a dense core, with \(A_{\Gamma+P}\) closed or form-closable and lower bounded.

## Disposition

A canonical nonlocal arithmetic Hilbert completion has been identified. It does not yet populate `common_closed_form_domain`: Gaussian-jet density, completed-form action, and source--Weil comparison remain unproved.
