# The adelic theta source cannot enter the rapid-label rung, so the common-source span needs an intermediate distribution graph domain

The proposed realization

\[
\iota:\mathcal S(\mathbb A)	o
\mathcal S_{\rm lab}\widehat\otimes_\pi\mathscr G
\]

is too strong even for the canonical unramified theta source.  Its finite part
`1_(Zhat)` produces positive-integer labels with primitive half-density size
approximately `n^(-1/2)`.  A rapid-label seminorm would contain

\[
\sum_{n\ge1}n^{2a}n^{-1},
\]

which diverges for every positive `a`.  Constant occupancy rows fail more
strongly.  Thus neither restricting to the Xi orbit nor to prime labels places
the adelic theta source in the rapid test rung.

The source-retaining interface must instead use a rigging

\[
\mathcal S_{\rm lab}
\subset D_\theta
\subset\mathcal S_{\rm lab}',
\]

and have type

\[
\iota_\theta:\mathcal S(\mathbb A)	o
D_\theta\widehat\otimes\mathfrak B_S.
\]

Equivalently, it may be a continuous source kernel from rapid label tests into
the ordered radial graph.  The existing map `kappa_z^mix` is defined on the
rapid test carrier and cannot simply be evaluated on this distributional
source.

A second obstruction appears for the bulk current: dual membership of both
factors does not define their ordered Green product.  One needs a closed mixed
pairing or an independently specified smoothing of one tensor leg.  The
fixed-forcing Xi pair is promising because `Phi` supplies a rapidly decaying
anchor, but this proves only its particular analytic integral, not a general
adelic distribution pairing.

The corrected common-source target therefore consists of:

1. an intermediate theta distribution graph domain `D_theta`;
2. Fourier and reciprocal invariance of that domain;
3. extension of the labelled tail constructor to the mixed
   test--distribution pair;
4. closedness and cutoff independence of the polarized Green current;
5. continuity of primitive, square, endpoint, and archimedean traces.

The rapid carrier remains the observer test space; it is not the state space
of the completed adelic theta source.
