# What is already inside the 1,836 relationships: return, one-way channels and Hermitian analysis

## Source-only question and answer

No new control is introduced. Reconstruct every typed endpoint and complex
weight of the native L/G/N network from the actual two-triangle source, and
assemble those entries into a single typed transfer operator W.

The relationship structure separates exactly into:

- one collective amplitude, with three distinct stage locations;
- thirteen independent one-way channels, each with a source and a sink;
- a 79-dimensional stage-state sector annihilated on its first application.

The orthogonal dimensions are 3+13+13+79=108. Here 108 is the direct sum of
three 36-coordinate stage spaces, **not** 108 geometric vertices or independent
particle coordinates. The thirteen channels are eleven local-mode disagreement
channels and two frame-aligned but locally rejected channels.

This decomposition explains what the directed relationships do before any
Hamiltonian interpretation: they retain a collective amplitude, transfer some
other information once, and discard the rest. It does not infer rungs from
triangle counts or stage dimensions.

## Preserve the actual stage types

    W = [[0,0,N],[L,0,0],[0,G,0]].

All 1,836 coefficients appear once. W encodes transfers on the direct sum of

## 1. The recurrent collective sector

Let

    P=diag(N,N,N).

Then

    W^3=P,
    WP=PW,
    (WP)^3=P.

On im P, W cyclically moves the same collective amplitude between the three
stage locations. Its three exact eigenvalues are 1, omega, omega^2, where
omega=exp(2 pi i/3), each with multiplicity one. The receipt constructs their
three orthogonal spectral projectors directly from P,W,W^2.

These are phases of the stage-shift representation. They are not a physical
frequency, an additional internal particle triplet or a clock derivation.
Repeated application after collective projection changes the stage location,
not the underlying reconstructed raw input.

The transfer ranks are

    rank W=16, rank W^2=3, rank W^3=3.

Thus even the numerous closed-walk supports do not imply an invertible return
on the full coefficient carrier.

## 2. Thirteen source-sink pairs inside the native map

Subtract the recurrent part:

    T=W-WP.

Define its source and sink projectors

    Ps=diag(L-N,G-N,0),
    Pd=diag(0,L-N,G-N).

Exact products give

    T^2=0,
    T^dagger T=Ps,
    T T^dagger=Pd,
    Ps Pd=0.

Therefore T is an isometry from its thirteen-dimensional source space onto
its thirteen-dimensional sink space. A second native application annihilates
the sink. This is an exact partial-isometry statement, not a claim that
individual arrow weights are all one.

Their origins are distinct:

| Native channel | Independent directions | What passes once |
|---|---:|---|
| raw -> local through L-N | 11 | Locally selected amplitudes with zero collective agreement |
| local -> aligned through G-N | 2 | A common transported spatial vector outside the selected local line |

The second row is accessible as a perturbation of local-stage input in this
stage-carrier analysis; it is absent in a strict raw -> L preparation.

The remaining projector

    Pz=I-P-Ps-Pd

has rank 79 and satisfies WPz=0. On the full staged carrier the transient
Jordan structure is thirteen size-two nilpotent blocks and seventy-nine
size-one zero blocks. On raw inputs alone the earlier 24/11/1 diagnostic
split remains the relevant one; the 79-dimensional count must not replace it.

## 3. What a Hermitian version would actually change

As a derived analysis, form

    K=(W+W^dagger)/2,
    Y=(W-W^dagger)/(2 sqrt(-3)).

Both are Hermitian and together retain the complete directed operator:

    W=K+sqrt(-3)Y.

K alone loses the direction distinction. On each source-sink pair it contains
both the original transfer and its reverse. For the witness above,

    Ky=x/2,

whereas the native map gives Wy=0. The reverse map is the adjoint in the
specified metric; it is **not an extra primitive operation already executed
by the original network**.

The exact spectrum of K is:

| Eigenvalue | Multiplicity | Source of the sector |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 1 | Stage-symmetric collective amplitude |
| 1/2 | 13 | Symmetric combinations of source-sink channels |
| -1/2 | 15 | Thirteen antisymmetric source-sink combinations and two collective stage-phase modes |
| 0 | 79 | Immediately annihilated stage-state sector |

All four orthogonal spectral projectors and their eigen-equations are verified,
not inferred from approximate diagonalization. In particular K is not a
positive rest-energy operator. Assigning it that role or adding an offset
would be an additional law, not a discovery in the list.

This pinpoints the useful distinction for a proposed Hermitian realization:
it reversibly couples directions the native map subsequently rejects. One
must specify how that realization relates to projection and retained records,
rather than assume Hermitian symmetrization is the same processing.

## 4. A response pairing, but not automatically canonical q,p

The quadrature commutator is exactly

    [K,Y]=(Ps-Pd)/(2 sqrt(-3)).

It has rank 26 and trace zero. It vanishes on the recurrent collective and
immediately zero sectors; on the one-way pairs it distinguishes the source
from the sink.

This is a source-resolved relationship between symmetric response, directed
response and source/sink imbalance. It is not a scalar canonical commutator.
A finite-dimensional pair cannot satisfy [q,p]=i hbar I with nonzero hbar,
because every commutator has trace zero whereas the right-hand side does not.
This excludes that literal finite-matrix identification, not all classical
canonical-coordinate constructions or larger-carrier realizations.

A mathematical Hamiltonian can be **assigned** on the complex amplitude
space by choosing its standard symplectic structure and h(z)=z^dagger K z.
That choice gives a norm-preserving unitary flow, which is not the native
nilpotent/projection processing. Neither this assignment nor the two
Hermitian quadratures derives the intended rung4 symplectic readout.
