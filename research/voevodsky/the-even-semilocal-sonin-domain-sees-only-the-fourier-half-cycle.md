# The even semilocal Sonin domain sees only the Fourier half-cycle

## Question

Does the function-valued order-four fourth presentation already apply to the primary-source semilocal Sonin domain?

## Claim boundary

Not as presently sourced. The cited semilocal Sonin amplification starts from the even archimedean subspace. On that subspace reflection is the identity, so Fourier square is the identity and the \(\pm i\) sectors vanish. Full order-four compatibility requires adjoining the odd/sign-character sector or another source-authorized parity-complete domain.

## Even-domain degeneration

The primary semilocal amplification is declared on

$$
\Sigma_S:L^2(\mathbb R)_{\rm ev}
\longrightarrow L^2(X_S)^{K_S}.
$$

For even \(f\),

$$
\mathcal Rf=f.
$$

Since

$$
\mathcal F^2=\mathcal R,
$$

it follows that

$$
\mathcal F^2f=f
$$

on this domain. Therefore the Fourier spectrum is contained in

$$
\{1,-1\},
$$

and the character projectors \(P_i,P_{-i}\) vanish. Generic order-four support/character orbits cannot live entirely in the even Sonin source.

## Response degeneration

For even \(g\),

$$
B_g(-a)=B_g(a),
\qquad
Q_g(-a)=Q_g(a),
$$

while reflected history channels become dependent. The four-function response action remains formally defined, but on the even image its square is already the identity. It realizes the reciprocal half-cycle, not a generic four-port fiber.

## Parity-complete extension

The minimal additive archimedean carrier supporting all Fourier characters is

$$
\mathcal S(\mathbb R)
=
\mathcal S(\mathbb R)_{\rm ev}
\oplus
\mathcal S(\mathbb R)_{\rm odd}.
$$

On the odd sector, reflection acts by \(-I\), so

$$
\mathcal F^2=-I
$$

and Fourier eigenvalues are \(\pm i\). In Tate language this sector corresponds to the real sign character, while the even sector corresponds to the trivial character.

Thus a parity-complete semilocal extension must provide the odd/sign-character analogue of the local Sonin seed and prove that its finite-place tensor factors, topology, and presentation maps obey the same bounds.

## Consequence for current claims

The oriented radial, gamma-matrix, and function-valued response constructions are exact on the full Schwartz test/dual carrier. Their restriction to the currently cited even Sonin source gives only half-turn compatibility. They become a full semilocal order-four interface only after source authorization of the odd/sign-character extension.

## Disposition

The current primary-source semilocal Sonin domain does not support the generic four-port Fourier fiber. The exact missing semilocal interface datum is the odd real-character Sonin chart. Until it is constructed or sourced, full order-four \(D\)-\(V\) compatibility remains an additive-Tate result rather than a theorem on the cited even semilocal Sonin system.
