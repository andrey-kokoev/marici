# An asymptotic threshold theorem reduces to a weighted Hankel discrepancy bound

Let `N=e^{2L}` and let `E_{N,delta}` denote the signed edge measure

\[
dE_{N,\delta}(r)
=
\sum_{n\le N}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}
 \delta_{\log(N/n)/\delta}(dr)
-
\delta\sqrt N e^{-\delta r/2}dr.
\]

After the endpoint main term is cancelled, write `K_{N,delta}` for the Hankel
operator generated by this measure on the rescaled edge packet. Let
`a(delta)>0` be the directed lower bound supplied by the archimedean edge form
minus IMS and lower-order endpoint errors, and let `b(delta)` bound edge--bulk
leakage after regularization.

## Conditional asymptotic threshold theorem

If explicit functions `Phi(N,delta)` and `N0` satisfy

\[
\|K_{N,\delta}\|\le\Phi(N,\delta),
\qquad
\Phi(N,\delta)+b(\delta)^2/\alpha<a(\delta)
\]

for every `N>=N0` and every overlap parameter in the continuation slab, then
all prime-power thresholds above `N0` are positive. Only thresholds below
`N0` remain for computation. This follows directly from the normalized
complement theorem and edge--bulk Schur absorption.

## Why the classical PNT remainder does not supply `Phi`

A zero-free-region estimate of the shape

\[
\psi(x)-x=O\left(xe^{-c\sqrt{\log x}}\right)
\]

becomes, after the `x^{-1/2}` weighting in the edge measure, an absolute error
of scale

\[
\sqrt N e^{-c\sqrt{\log N}}.
\]

This grows faster than every power of `log N`; it cannot be absorbed by the
logarithmic archimedean edge coercivity. Even replacing this by the usual
RH-scale estimate produces polylogarithmic discrepancy that must be compared
carefully with the chosen shrinking edge scale.

Therefore an unconditional asymptotic `N>=N0` theorem does not follow from the
standard PNT plus the analytic estimates already proved. Producing the needed
uniform signed Hankel bound is the central arithmetic problem, not a remaining
compactness or quadrature detail. Since finite-threshold certification plus
such an asymptotic theorem and the form-core passage would imply global Weil
positivity, one should expect this bound to contain RH-equivalent information.
