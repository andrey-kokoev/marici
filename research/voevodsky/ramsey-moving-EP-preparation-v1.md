## 2. A moving preparation passes the lossless comparison tests

    B'_P = G B_E T*,
    B'_N = S_PN B'_P Pi_PN*.

Use the appropriate metric-adjoint readouts R'_P and R'_N. Exact arithmetic verifies

    R'_P B'_P = R'_N B'_N = I5,

The exact checker establishes the source coefficient operators before reindexing:

- L selects slots2,3,4;
- N is the rank-one common-mode projector on slots2,3,4;
- G is that common-mode projector plus identity on slots0,1.

Intertwining N fixes that phase to1, because the other two common-mode components are already fixed. Conversely, any such U commutes with the scalar restrictions of the three target operators on the context plane, with no cross terms to the active subspace. The checker verifies those exact block forms and one reference intertwiner. This gives the stated arbitrary-U result by block algebra; no finite numerical sampling is used as a universal proof. It is a written algebraic proof, not a new formal-kernel theorem.

All choices have the same moving preparation projector. U changes the context readout basis, not the physical prepared subspace. The earlier sign ambiguity is a special case.

## 5. What is and is not now established

No scalar-locality or all-history phase-reader claim follows from the three operator checks.

Those occurrences have different indices: they are not the genuinely retained pair. The compatible family therefore needs an explicit continuation interpretation; it must not be described as keeping all four body values unchanged or identifying their different occurrences.

The E source here remains the retained negative-direction field, not an unannounced substitution of forward native E8. The new target preparation is disclosed throughout.
