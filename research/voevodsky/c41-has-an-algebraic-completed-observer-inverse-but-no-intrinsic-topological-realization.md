# C41 has an algebraic completed-observer inverse but no intrinsic topological realization

## Question

Does the reverse map from the completed trace presentation to the source observer have an analytic realization?

## Claim boundary

The repository constructs an algebraic inverse on each fixed-ratio completed history-plus-cycle image. It does not prove continuity for the independently inherited image topology. A scalar finite-part trace has no inverse.

On a fixed ratio block, the complete observer is

$$
\mathcal O_D=(\widehat B_D,\widehat Z_D):
\mathcal C_{D,\exp}\longrightarrow
\mathcal H_D\times\mathcal Z_D.
$$

Prior kernel theorems give

$$
\ker\widehat B_D\cap\ker\widehat Z_D=0.
$$

Define

$$
V_{4,D}^{\mathrm{obs}}=\operatorname{im}\mathcal O_D.
$$

The algebraic reverse constructor is

$$
C_{41,D}(\mathcal O_Dc)=c.
$$

It satisfies

$$
C_{41,D}\mathcal O_D=\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal C_{D,\exp}},
$$

$$
\mathcal O_D C_{41,D}=\operatorname{id}_{V_{4,D}^{\mathrm{obs}}}.
$$

No source packet supplies a bound of the form

$$
p(c)\leq K\,q(\mathcal O_Dc)
$$

for defining seminorms \(p\) of the projective source and \(q\) of the inherited observer image. Therefore continuity of \(C_{41,D}\) in that topology is not established. Giving the image the transported source topology makes the inverse continuous by definition but does not identify that topology with the pre-existing product-subspace topology.

For the scalar shadow, noninjectivity forbids any reverse constructor:

$$
h\longmapsto W_S(h).
$$

## Fixed-k twistor test

A repository search found no construction naming a common twistor carrier or a quarter-turn operator for these four semilocal presentations. The closest retained four-port result constructs a stratified one-way chain and explicitly states that its completed inverse is not bounded; it also rejects output-metric isometry. Therefore that four-port chain cannot itself be the required invertible quarter-rotation.

The twistor conjecture remains possible only on a larger retained common graph on which all four coordinates are preserved and cyclically permuted. No operator implementing that permutation, no identification of its first three quarter actions with the established presentation maps, and no fourth-power law have been located.

## Disposition

C41 is algebraically realized on the complete fixed-block observer image and satisfies both inverse equations. It is not yet an intrinsic analytic equivalence because continuous reconstruction in the declared observer-image topology lacks an inverse estimate. The proposed fixed-k twistor interpretation is not supported by the existing four-port chain; it requires a new common carrier and quarter-rotation constructor.
