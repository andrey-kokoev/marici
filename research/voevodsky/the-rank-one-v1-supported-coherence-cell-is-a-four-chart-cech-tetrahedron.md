# The rank-one V1-supported coherence cell is a four-chart Cech tetrahedron

## Question

Can the complete rank-one coherence cell touching the source presentation be constructed without first choosing nondegenerate seven-stage factorizations on every edge?

## Claim boundary

Yes in the category of complete Hermitian-form presentations, provided the three source-rooted comparisons are equivalences at their already stated strengths. This constructs the categorical rank-one cell. It does not identify the derived spectral-to-trace edge with an independently constructed unanchored operator intertwiner.

Let the four complete presentations be \(\mathcal Q_1,\ldots,\mathcal Q_4\). Choose source-rooted equivalences

$$
a_1=\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal Q_1},
\qquad
a_i:\mathcal Q_1\xRightarrow{\simeq}\mathcal Q_i
\quad(i=2,3,4).
$$

Here \(a_2\) is the complete source-to-geometric comparison, \(a_3\) is the complete source-to-spectral form equality, and \(a_4\) is the complete source-to-finite-part form equality.

Define all six forward edges by chart change:

$$
e_{ij}=a_ja_i^{-1}
\qquad(1\le i<j\le4).
$$

Thus

$$
e_{12}=a_2,
\quad e_{13}=a_3,
\quad e_{14}=a_4,
$$

$$
e_{23}=a_3a_2^{-1},
\quad e_{24}=a_4a_2^{-1},
\quad e_{34}=a_4a_3^{-1}.
$$

Every reverse edge is

$$
e_{ji}=e_{ij}^{-1}=a_ia_j^{-1}.
$$

For each \(i<j<\ell\), associativity and inverse cancellation give the face path

$$
e_{j\ell}e_{ij}
=
a_\ell a_j^{-1}a_ja_i^{-1}
\simeq
a_\ell a_i^{-1}
=
e_{i\ell}.
$$

These supply the four faces \(H_{123},H_{124},H_{134},H_{234}\). Both reductions of

$$
e_{34}e_{23}e_{12}
$$

to \(e_{14}\) are generated by the same associator and inverse-unit laws. Their equality is the standard coherence theorem for equivalences, which supplies the tetrahedral 3-cell.

## Relation to edge degeneracies

If a source-rooted edge is represented in seven slots by one nonidentity equivalence and six identities, changing its occupied slot changes only inserted unitors. The rank-one Cech tetrahedron identifies route composites independently of those unit insertions. This removes placement dependence categorically; it does not turn the six identity slots into distinct analytic transitions.

## Analytic identification of the derived C34 edge

The local Tate distribution identity already proves, with matching endpoint normalization,

$$
\mathcal Q_3(g_1,g_2)
=
W_S(g_1*g_2^*)
=
\mathcal Q_4(g_1,g_2).
$$

Consequently the derived chart change

$$
e_{34}=a_4a_3^{-1}
$$

is the identity isometry between the completed spectral and cutoff Hermitian forms. This identifies the direct and derived \(C_{34}\) edges at signed/form-valued strength. It does not construct trace-norm convergence of unanchored regulated operators or an ordinary positive Hilbert equivalence. Positive realization remains a relative feature with the previously stated regulator qualification.

## Disposition

The full rank-one coherence cell touching \(V_1\) is constructed as the Cech tetrahedron of the three source-rooted presentation charts. It has six forward edges, six inverse edges, four face homotopies, and one tetrahedral coherence cell. All six edges have signed/form-valued analytic identifications. Degenerate seven-slot placements do not affect this rank-one cell.
