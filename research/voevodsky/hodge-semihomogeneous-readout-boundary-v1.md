# A bounded semihomogeneous-bundle construction class has zero Weil readout

## Fresh task and fixed source

Execute semihomogeneous-bundle-readout-boundary. Keep the original complex abelian sixfold X and its proved certificate NS(X)_Q=Q E. Let e=c1(H) in CH^1(X), with cohomology class E. The target remains the primitive class R; no change of source is made.

For a vector bundle F of positive rank r, semihomogeneous means that for every x in X there is P_x in Pic^0(X) such that t_x^*F is isomorphic to F tensor P_x. Simple means End(F)=C. Neither property is assumed for arbitrary algebraic bundles.

## Imported theorem and exact applicability

Use the characteristic-zero isogeny-splitting theorem for SIMPLE semihomogeneous bundles on an abelian variety: there is an isogeny f:Y->X and a line bundle M on Y such that

    f^*F isomorphic to M^(direct sum r).

This is the classical semihomogeneous-bundle theorem of Mukai, in *Semi-homogeneous vector bundles on an Abelian variety*, J. Math. Kyoto Univ.18(1978),239-272. The theorem is an explicit imported input here; no fresh primary-text retrieval or independent proof verification is claimed. Its hypotheses are exactly the simple semihomogeneous input class, on a complex abelian variety. We do not assert this splitting for an arbitrary nonsimple bundle.

This splits a VECTOR BUNDLE after an isogeny. It does not split X or Y as an abelian product, and so does not conflict with the retained simplicity/isogeny barrier for X.

## Derive the formula using injective pullback

In rational Chow groups, finite flat pullback f^* is injective, since f_* f^*=deg(f) times the identity. On Y,

    f^* ch(F)=r exp(c1(M)),
    f^* c1(F)=r c1(M).

Consequently

    ch(F)=r exp(c1(F)/r) in CH^*(X)_Q.

The exponential truncates after degree6. Similarly

    c(F)=(1+c1(F)/r)^r,
    ch3(F)=c1(F)^3/(6r^2),
    c3(F)=((r-1)(r-2)/(6r^2)) c1(F)^3.

The last formula is zero for r=1 or2, as required. In contrast ch3 need not vanish at those ranks. These formulas follow from the splitting theorem and pullback injectivity; they are not numerical fits.

For COHOMOLOGY, write cl(c1(F))=a E with a rational. The Picard-rank-one certificate then gives

    cl(ch(F))=r exp((a/r)E),
    cl(ch3(F))=(a^3/(6r^2))E^3.

The Picard statement is not a Chow statement: a Pic^0 contribution to c1(F) can be nonzero in CH^1 while vanishing in cohomology. We therefore do not conclude that ch(F) is a polynomial in e in Chow merely from NS(X)_Q=Q E.

## Define the bounded construction class explicitly

Let B be the smallest class of actual vector bundles containing the simple semihomogeneous bundles and closed under these FINITE operations only:

* direct sums;
* extensions with both endpoint bundles already in B;
* tensor products;
* duals.

Line bundles, O_X, their unipotent iterated extensions and the previous L-twisted unipotent control are included. No closure under arbitrary subobjects, quotients or direct summands is asserted. Such closure does not follow just from a polynomial identity for the Chern character of a larger bundle.

For every F in B, cl(ch(F)) belongs to the divisor algebra Q[E]. Proof is inductive: direct sums and extensions add Chern characters, tensor products multiply them, and duals multiply degree-j components by (-1)^j. The same is true for the rational K-subring generated by these classes, including formal differences. This does NOT identify that subring with all of K0(X)_Q.

A generated bundle need not itself satisfy the single-exponential formula. For example L plus L^(-1) has

    ch=exp(l)+exp(-l),

not 2 exp(c1/2)=2. Its degree-two term l^2 is nonzero. We use the additive formula for this direct sum, not the simple-bundle formula.

## Degree-three readout, with correct Chern normalization

Write cl(ch_j(F))=b_j E^j. The universal Newton identity gives

    cl(c3(F))=(b_1^3/6-b_1 b_2+2b_3)E^3.

Thus both degree-three Chern characters and third Chern classes of the explicitly generated objects are multiples of E^3. Any regular rank-three zero locus of a bundle in B has that top-Chern class and has zero Weil-plane projection. This recovers the previous unipotent control without assuming that its bundle is simple or split.

Using the retained cup matrix diag(2160,-32,-32), the projector

    P_W(c)=c-(integral_X c E^3/2160)E^3

kills these classes. This is a cohomological readout boundary for a specified construction class. Except for separately proved examples such as the unipotent flag, no equality to a multiple of e^3 in CH^3 is asserted.

## What has NOT been shown

No theorem here says every algebraic bundle is simple semihomogeneous or belongs to B. No theorem says every codimension-three cycle is a regular zero locus of such a bundle. Vanishing on B is not vanishing on all algebraic cycles, and the imported splitting theorem provides no obstruction to an unknown non-semihomogeneous bundle carrying a Weil component.

In particular this is not a new universal obstruction replacing the original unresolved cycle gate. It is a boundary on a concrete family of constructions. The algebraicity of R remains unresolved.
