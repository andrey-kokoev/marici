# Paired face traces do not generally descend to one scalar edge trace

## Scope and fresh inputs

This turn returns from same-face request admission to a cross-facet observation test. It rereads the12 geometric edge-tangent rows and the actual proton five-channel embedding, checks their live source pins, and composes the matrices exactly.

For each geometric edge, the two rows come from its two different parent faces. The question is whether either scalar face trace determines the other on the declared full five-coefficient preparation. This is distinct from the previous PN/neutron-edge-locality obstruction: it is already a question about the two proton face occurrences before that transport.

## Exact classification

On edges AB, AC, AD, BC and BD, the two prepared rows have rank2. For each direction the receipt stores an explicit coefficient vector k satisfying

    firstTrace(k) = firstTrace(0) = 0,
    secondTrace(k) != secondTrace(0).

Hence no function of the first scalar alone, even a nonlinear one, can recover the second across this preparation. The reverse direction is also checked. A scalar sign or phase change cannot repair these five pairs on the full domain.

CD is different: its rows have rank1 and are exact negatives of one another on the entire five-channel preparation. This exception is preserved rather than hidden in an all-edge no-go claim.

These findings do not refute the retained pair of observations or a context-enlarged edge object. They refute only identifying the full paired data with either one-dimensional scalar occurrence through a fixed all-input recovery map.

## Maximal domain for the explicit opposite-sign candidate

As a separate declared convention, require the two face traces on every edge to sum to zero. This is not silently identified with geometric same-orientation equality or claimed source-selected physics.

The six discrepancy rows vanish identically on coefficients0,1,2 and depend only on coefficients3,4. The AB equation has a nonzero coefficient3 pivot and zero coefficient4 entry; AD has the complementary nonzero coefficient4 pivot. Therefore their common kernel is exactly

    coefficient[3] = coefficient[4] = 0.

Its dimension is3, and substitution into all six equations is checked exactly. Thus opposite-sign scalar matching holds on this proper subdomain, not on the full five-channel preparation. The two additional channels cannot be discarded without changing the admitted field scope.
