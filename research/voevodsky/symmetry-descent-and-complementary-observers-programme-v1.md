# Symmetry descent and complementary observers programme v1

## Question

Can a typed descent calculus explain how a rich presentation symmetry becomes a smaller operational symmetry, while complementary observers jointly retain information that no single descended analytic channel can preserve?

## Claim boundary

This programme studies symmetry descent, complementary observers, constructor-role typing, and Green/Real boundary systems. The current radial/Fourier construction is the first worked example. Number-theoretic confinement and RH are peripheral stress tests, not programme objectives or evidence for the general claims.

## Programme scope

The primary diagram is

\[
X\xrightarrow{C}Y,
\qquad
G\curvearrowright X,
\qquad
H\curvearrowright Y,
\qquad
C\rho_X(g)=\rho_Y(\pi(g))C,
\]

where \(\pi:G\to H\) is an explicitly declared symmetry descent. The programme asks what information is preserved by \(C\), what is retained only by an auxiliary line or observer, and what joint observation theorem replaces impossible faithfulness of one descended channel.

## Hard core

1. A presentation symmetry and its descended operational symmetry are different typed actions even when one is generated by a power of the other.
2. Information discarded by symmetry descent cannot be recovered from the descended carrier without an additional source-derived observer or lift.
3. A compact analytic observer may be exact and coordinatewise nonzero while failing every uniform completed-source lower bound.
4. Constructor roles are part of morphism identity: a forward coefficient, return coefficient, counterterm, and boundary trace are not interchangeable scalars.
5. Green orientation, Real conjugation, reciprocal exchange, and presentation rotation remain separate structures unless comparison cells are proved.

The hard core reopens if an example supplies a faithful descended realization of the full action without auxiliary data, or if constructor-role distinctions fail to predict any substitution error that ordinary typing misses.

## Bold conjecture

A source system with symmetry descent \(\pi:G\to H\) and compact analytic realization \(A:X\to Y\) admits stable reconstruction exactly when a complementary observer \(D:X\to Z\) detects the joint kernel and supplies the missing lower margin on the asymptotically invisible directions of \(A\). The required data are typed by constructor roles and are natural under the descended \(H\)-action.

This is provisional. “Exactly when” requires a theorem with declared norms, domains, and quantifiers; it is not yet established.

## Named rivals

1. **Single-observer sufficiency:** suitable bounded postprocessing of \(A\) restores a uniform margin without an independent observer.
2. **Lift-only repair:** a metaplectic or line-valued lift alone restores all information lost under descent.
3. **Untyped coefficient calculus:** numerical coefficient identities suffice; constructor roles add no predictive power.
4. **Boundary-specific accident:** the observed split is peculiar to the radial Green example and has no reusable categorical content.
5. **Pure direct-sum repair:** injectivity of \((A,D)\) suffices without a quantitative lower bound or closed-range condition.

## Risky consequences

The conjecture predicts:

1. if \(A\) is compact on an infinite-dimensional source, bounded analytic postprocessing cannot make it bounded below;
2. a valid complement \(D\) must remain nonvanishing on every normalized sequence for which \(Ax_n\to0\);
3. joint injectivity without a lower frame bound permits unstable reconstruction and is insufficient;
4. a lift recording \(\ker\pi\) can preserve presentation phase while leaving analytic coercivity unchanged;
5. swapping a determinant counterterm into a forward synthesis slot will violate at least one naturality, domain, or response square;
6. Green/Real sewing will instantiate all four distinctions with explicit operators.

## Principal worked example

Let

\[
F^4=I,
\qquad
R=F^2,
\qquad
W_u^2=I,
\]

and define the canonical fold

\[
(C_uq)(r)=\bigl(q(r),u q(-r)\bigr).
\]

Then

\[
C_uF^2=W_uC_u,
\qquad
W_u=
\begin{pmatrix}0&u^{-1}\\u&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The order-four presentation action descends to reciprocal parity. The quarter-turn residue may be retained by a line-valued metaplectic lift but is not identified with radial sewing.

On the radial double,

\[
\mathbb D=
\begin{pmatrix}\partial_r&0\\0&-\partial_r\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\Lambda_u=\{(c,uc):c\in\mathbb C\},
\]

with Green matrix

\[
J_\partial=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1),
\qquad
W_u^*J_\partial W_u=-J_\partial.
\]

For the Real-compatible synthesis,

\[
J_HU=UJ_V,
\qquad
U^\top=J_VU^*J_H=U^*.
\]

This example separates presentation rotation, reciprocal exchange, coefficientwise conjugation, Green orientation, and analytic transpose.

## Complementary-observer theorem target

Let \(X,Y,Z\) be Hilbert spaces and \(A:X\to Y\), \(D:X\to Z\) bounded. The first exact target is to prove equivalence between stable joint reconstruction and the lower frame inequality

\[
\|Ax\|_Y^2+\|Dx\|_Z^2\ge\delta^2\|x\|_X^2
\]

for some \(\delta>0\), then characterize this condition when \(A\) is compact through the behavior of \(D\) on orthonormal or weakly null sequences invisible to \(A\).

A second target adds equivariance under \(G\to H\) and asks whether \(D\) must detect the kernel of the symmetry descent representation.

## Constructor-role language

Every coefficient-bearing arrow receives a role:

- `forward_synthesis`;
- `return_observer`;
- `determinant_counterterm`;
- `boundary_trace`;
- `comparison_cell`;
- `presentation_lift`.

A substitution is admissible only if source, target, domain, variance, symmetry naturality, and response square agree. Equality of scalar values or asymptotics is insufficient.

## First falsification sequence

1. Prove the elementary joint-lower-bound theorem and its compact-channel corollary.
2. Construct a finite-dimensional counterexample showing joint injectivity without stability.
3. Test whether a line-valued lift can change the lower margin; the predicted answer is no when it is unitary bookkeeping.
4. Encode the radial example in the constructor-role language and deliberately substitute the mixed primitive--square coefficient for the forward loading coefficient.
5. Identify the first failed typed square rather than merely observing a changed scalar response.
6. Compare with one non-arithmetic doubled-boundary example to test whether the pattern is reusable.

## Evidence boundary

Already established:

- the canonical fold and half-turn intertwining;
- the reciprocal Green anti-isometry;
- the Real transpose--adjoint identity on compatible metrics;
- compact, exact Euler-to-radial loading;
- failure of uniform coercivity for the compact analytic channel.

Not established:

- the general complementary-observer characterization beyond the basic lower-bound equivalence;
- necessity of detecting \(\ker\pi\) in general;
- universality of constructor-role typing;
- transfer beyond the principal worked example.

## Disposition

Programme activated. The first executable frontier is the complementary-observer theorem and its hostile finite-dimensional and compact-channel tests. Symmetry descent supplies the conceptual spine; constructor-role typing records why each test succeeds or fails; Green/Real sewing supplies the initial model. RH remains outside the hard core.
