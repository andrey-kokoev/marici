# Falsification of the mixed source-Gram conjecture as stated

## Problem

Test whether the proposed factorization

\[
a_{i+j}(t,h)
=
\langle T_{t,h}^i\Omega_{t,h},T_{t,h}^j\Omega_{t,h}\rangle,
\qquad 0\leq T_{t,h}\leq I,
\]

is a risky source mechanism or merely a reformulation of the desired all-rank cone.

## Bold conjecture tested

The completed gamma--prime remainder admits a genuinely mixed positive-contraction factor compatible with rank truncation, \(t\)-translation, mesh refinement, and endpoint extraction.

## Named rivals

1. Abstract Hausdorff/GNS reconstruction performed only after all-rank positivity is assumed.
2. Block-diagonal gamma and prime Gram factors.
3. One sector serving uniformly as a positive background for the other.
4. A typed mixed source kernel constructed before positivity.

## Strongest falsification attempts

### Rank one

The total sampled zeroth moment remained positive at the tested points, but the gamma zeroth moment crossed sign. This falsifies gamma as a uniform positive background, not the total cone.

### Rank two

Both sector determinants can be negative while the coupled determinant is positive. At a later sample the sector supplying positive centered curvature reverses. This falsifies block-diagonal sector positivity and any fixed-sector compensation narrative.

No certified negative total determinant has been found. The smallest reported positive value is approximately

\[
7.55\times10^{-8},
\]

with uncertified centers.

### Endpoint coherence

Support separation forces an endpoint direct summand, but completed-function decay would extract that summand automatically from remainder Hankel positivity. This does not contradict the mixed factor.

### Logical novelty

This test is decisive against the conjecture as an explanatory statement. Once all Hankel and support localizers are positive, Hausdorff/GNS reconstruction already produces a positive contraction \(T\), a cyclic vector \(\Omega\), and the displayed matrix coefficients.

Therefore the asserted existence of \((T,\Omega)\), without an independently specified source constructor, has no consequence beyond the positivity target itself. “Source-derived” and “genuinely mixed” do not define maps, spaces, or identities that could fail.

## Exact residual

The conjecture was not falsified as an existence statement. It was falsified as a proposed mechanism: its mathematical content is supplied by the cone it was intended to explain.

The residual is the missing typed constructor:

- source space;
- source pairing;
- gamma and prime embeddings;
- coupling map;
- adjoint or involution;
- identity equating its matrix coefficients with the completed remainder;
- compatibility cells for translation and mesh refinement.

Until these are named before positivity is invoked, the conjecture is circular.

## Disposition

Reject the original conjecture as too broad for explanatory use. Retain it only as an equivalent target.

The replacement conjecture must factor the already-declared pre-GNS Weil functional

\[
p\longmapsto
\left\langle
W,
e^{-tu^2}(1-e^{-hu^2})
|p(e^{-hu^2})|^2
\right\rangle
\]

by an exact identity derived from the explicit formula. It must not be constructed from a Cholesky factor, spectral measure, or GNS completion of the target matrix.

The observed gamma--prime cross term arises from taking the determinant of an additive matrix sum. It does not establish, or require, a gamma--prime coupling operator at source level. The risky consequence must instead be a source identity not shared by arbitrary PSD moment families.

## Surviving scope

The numerical evidence does not refute the all-rank remainder cone. It refutes only separable and fixed-background mechanisms. An explicit mixed arithmetic factor remains possible, but none has yet been constructed.
