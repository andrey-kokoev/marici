# Categorical apparatus v2 conformance profiles

## Question

How far does each current candidate realize the layered apparatus without compressing distinct failure modes into one status?

## Claim boundary

Profiles report ten independent dimensions. They are not a total ranking of scientific value.

## Audited profiles

Five candidates were checked:

- Markov analytic realization;
- RH Mellin interface;
- Kitaev associator loop;
- theta–Clark pro-Gram subsystem;
- Benincasa five-residue cover.

Each profile records base incidence, declared cells, completion, relative filler, strict selection, certificate backend, sector vertex, sector edge, horn coverage, and physical backend.

## Results

Markov is the only completed mathematical realization within its declared bounded domain and the only admitted sector vertex. It has no physical backend and no cross-sector edge.

The RH interface realizes a relative filler but lacks modularly invariant strict selection and completion.

Kitaev retains a partial associator representation and split certificate backends, with physical and simplex-coverage gates blocked.

Theta–Clark retains its constructor subset and exact local braid residual, but lacks source incidence into a full sector object.

Benincasa retains five local sectors and cyclic relabelling, but no overlap incidence object.

## Disposition

The conformance matrix prevents `partial` from hiding where a candidate stops. Across five profiles, there is one admitted vertex, no admitted edge, one bounded completed mathematical realization, and no physical backend.
