# C32 is the unitary spectral inverse followed by the integrated representation

## Question

Does the reverse map from the compatible canonical-dual spectral image to observer-generated semilocal geometry have an analytic realization?

## Claim boundary

Yes between the observer-generated essential images. No inverse is claimed on arbitrary spectral pairs or arbitrary semilocal geometric operators.

For a compatible pair \((\xi^+,\xi^-)\), define its common source observer by

$$
g=(\Omega_S^+)^{-1}\xi^+=(\Omega_S^-)^{-1}\xi^-.
$$

The reverse constructor is

$$
C_{32}(\xi^+,\xi^-)=U_S(g).
$$

Equivalently,

$$
C_{32}=C_{12}C_{31}.
$$

The forward constructor on observer-generated geometry is

$$
C_{23}(U_S(g))=(\Omega_S^+g,\Omega_S^-g).
$$

Using faithfulness of \(U_S\) and unitarity of both spectral transforms gives

$$
C_{32}C_{23}(U_S(g))=U_S(g),
$$

and

$$
C_{23}C_{32}(\xi^+,\xi^-)=(\xi^+,\xi^-).
$$

The construction is continuous for the essential-image topologies transported by the faithful integrated representation and the unitary spectral pair.

## Disposition

Both \(C_{23}\) and \(C_{32}\) are analytic inverse constructors between observer-generated semilocal geometry and the compatible canonical-dual spectral image. Their domain restrictions are essential parts of the theorem.
