# Integrated categorical representation of the coherence pyramid

## Question

Do the completed signature, composition, completion, coherence, and countermodel modules constitute the full categorical representation requested for the coherence pyramid?

## Claim boundary

This audit determines representation strength. It does not upgrade a computad presentation into a proved partial double category by terminology.

## Integrated representation

The modules assemble without type-name conflict into one variance-tagged presentation:

- five object sorts: carrier, finite Green, gauge presentation, closed form, and quotient form;
- four arrow variances: under, over, vertical, and comparison;
- three partial reindexing constructors: amalgamation, pullback, and completion transport;
- six cell classes;
- five coherence-law classes;
- ten materialized countermodels mapped to explicit constructor refusals.

The representation preserves the distinctions established by the research programme. Green embeddings are not gauge quotients; sequential composition is not reindexing; finite positivity is not closed-form completion; commuting boundaries are not coherence cells.

## Strength audit

The assembled object is a **typed computad presentation for a candidate partial equipment**. It is not yet a proved partial double category or equipment.

What has been proved mechanically:

- cross-file sort and variance references agree;
- every module is present and its checker result passes;
- declared object, arrow, cell, and law counts match the component files;
- representation-strength flags do not overstate the result;
- selected hostile cases are refused.

What remains unproved:

- closure of analytic certificate bundles under every admitted composition;
- construction of associators and interchange cells for sourced objects rather than symbolic tokens;
- pentagon and pasting laws for those analytic cells;
- invertible Beck–Chevalley cells where claimed;
- pseudofunctoriality of finite-to-closed completion.

## Strongest residual

The first missing theorem is closure of sourced analytic certificates under composition together with the coherence laws. Without it, the presentation specifies what a partial equipment must contain and reject, but does not instantiate one.

This residual is executable rather than an authority-only wait: finite Green, gauge, and bounded closed-form fixtures can be used to construct or falsify analytic associators and interchange cells. A new programme branch is therefore warranted.

## Disposition

The original integration leaf is resolved. The answer to “do we have the full categorical representation?” is now precise:

- full generator-and-obligation representation: yes;
- full categorical realization: no;
- exact next target: analytic realization and closure theorem for the admitted certificate bundles.
