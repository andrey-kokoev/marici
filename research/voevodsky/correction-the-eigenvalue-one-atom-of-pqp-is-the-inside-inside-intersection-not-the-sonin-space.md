# Correction: the eigenvalue-one atom of `PQP` is the inside--inside intersection, not the Sonin space

## Exact eigenspace calculation

Let `P,Q` be orthogonal projections and

\[
B=PQP
\]

acting on `ran P`. For `x in ran P`,

\[
\langle x,Bx\rangle
=
\langle x,Qx\rangle
=
\|Qx\|^2.
\]

If

\[
Bx=x,
\]

then

\[
\|Qx\|=
\|x\|.
\]

Since `Q` is an orthogonal projection, equality holds exactly when `Qx=x`. Thus

\[
\boxed{
\ker(I-B)
=
\operatorname{Ran}P
\cap
\operatorname{Ran}Q.
}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
P_{\{1\}}(B)
=P_{\operatorname{Ran}P\cap\operatorname{Ran}Q}.
}
\]

This is the inside--inside intersection of the two cutoffs.

## Classical Sonin space

For a symmetric physical cutoff `P_Lambda` and its Fourier-conjugate cutoff `Q_Lambda`, the classical Sonin space consists of functions vanishing in the physical central window whose Fourier transforms also vanish in the dual central window:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal S_\Lambda
=
\ker P_\Lambda
\cap
\ker Q_\Lambda
=
\operatorname{Ran}(I-P_\Lambda)
\cap
\operatorname{Ran}(I-Q_\Lambda).
}
\]

It is the outside--outside intersection, not

\[
\operatorname{Ran}P_\Lambda
\cap
\operatorname{Ran}Q_\Lambda.
\]

In the Halmos decomposition of a projection pair, these are two different exact-intersection summands:

\[
H_{11}
=
\operatorname{Ran}P
\cap
\operatorname{Ran}Q,
\]

\[
H_{00}
=
\ker P
\cap
\ker Q.
\]

The contraction `PQP` on `ran P` sees `H_11` as eigenvalue one. It does not see `H_00`, because `H_00` lies outside `ran P`.

## Uncertainty obstruction for the inside--inside atom

In the ordinary real time--band model, a vector in `H_11` is both compactly supported and bandlimited. By the Paley--Wiener/analytic uncertainty theorem, such a vector must vanish.

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Ran}P_\Lambda
\cap
\operatorname{Ran}Q_\Lambda
=
\{0\}
}
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{
P_{\{1\}}(B_\Lambda)=0
}
\]

for every finite ordinary time--band cutoff.

The prolate eigenvalues approach one but never equal one.

## Semilocal caveat

For the semilocal quotient and one-sided module cutoff, the same conclusion requires the corresponding local uncertainty theorem on the exact carrier. It must not be assumed solely from the real model.

Nevertheless, the algebraic identity

\[
P_{\{1\}}(PQP)
=P_{\operatorname{Ran}P\cap\operatorname{Ran}Q}
\]

is unconditional. Calling this atom the Sonin sector is incorrect unless the source defines “Sonin” as the inside--inside intersection, contrary to the standard prolate/Sonin convention used in the cited semilocal literature.

## Where the Sonin sector appears

The Sonin space is the eigenvalue-one atom of the complementary contraction

\[
\boxed{
B^{out}
=(I-P)(I-Q)(I-P)
}
\]

acting on `ran(I-P)`:

\[
\boxed{
\ker(I-B^{out})
=
\ker P
\cap
\ker Q
=
\mathcal S.
}
\]

Thus a complete positive two-polarity/Halmos feature must carry two different contraction towers:

1. the inside tower generated by `PQP`;
2. the outside tower generated by `(I-P)(I-Q)(I-P)`.

Only the second contains the standard Sonin atom at eigenvalue one.

## Correction to the dyadic identity

For the inside contraction,

\[
\sum_{j\ge0}
B^{2^j}(I-B^{2^j})
=
B-P_{\{1\}}(B).
\]

In the ordinary time--band model this reduces to

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{j\ge0}
B^{2^j}(I-B^{2^j})
=B
}
\]

because `P_{\{1\}}(B)=0`.

This exact tower accounts for all inside positive mass, including arbitrarily near-one prolate modes. It leaves no Sonin summand.

For the outside contraction,

\[
\sum_{j\ge0}
(B^{out})^{2^j}
(I-(B^{out})^{2^j})
=
B^{out}-P_\mathcal S.
\]

Here the omitted atom really is Sonin.

## Effect on the balanced-pair proposal

A previous proposal set

\[
Z_\Lambda
=P_{\{1\}}(B_\Lambda)A_g
\]

and called `Z_Lambda` the Sonin feature. This label is withdrawn.

`Z_Lambda` is the inside--inside exact-intersection feature. In the ordinary model it vanishes. A balanced Sonin pair, if required, must instead be built from

\[
\boxed{
P_\mathcal S
=P_{\{1\}}(B_\Lambda^{out}).
}

Whether that outside atom has zero signed readout, couples to an endpoint, or belongs to a separate stable summand remains source-dependent.

## Effect on near-one scaling

The absence of an exact inside atom does not remove the near-one problem. Indeed it sharpens it:

\[
B_\Lambda^m
\to0
\]

strongly at every fixed finite ordinary cutoff, while the largest eigenvalues approach one as the cutoff grows.

Hence

\[
\lim_{\Lambda\to\infty}
\lim_{m\to\infty}
B_\Lambda^m
=0,
\]

whereas a joint limit with

\[
m\varepsilon_\Lambda
\asymp1
\]

can retain nonzero near-one mass. This retained mass is a scaling cloud, not an exact Sonin atom.

## Revised positive boundary inventory

The physical positive filler must distinguish:

- inside near-one prolate cloud from `PQP`;
- outside near-one cloud from `(I-P)(I-Q)(I-P)`;
- exact outside--outside Sonin atom;
- any mixed Halmos generic-angle sectors;
- endpoint/winding channels from Tate scattering.

Collapsing the first and third items loses the exact Halmos typing.

## Acceptance test for the semilocal carrier

For the actual semilocal one-sided cutoff, determine independently:

\[
H_{11,\Lambda}
=
\operatorname{Ran}P_\Lambda
\cap
\operatorname{Ran}Q_\Lambda,
\]

\[
H_{00,\Lambda}
=
\ker P_\Lambda
\cap
\ker Q_\Lambda.
\]

Then verify:

1. whether `H_11,Lambda` vanishes by semilocal uncertainty;
2. whether `H_00,Lambda` is the stable Sonin space of the source theorem;
3. how both spaces behave under cutoff and place-set enlargement;
4. which, if either, contributes to the signed relative trace.

## Disposition

The exact correction is

\[
\boxed{
P_{\{1\}}(PQP)
=
P_{\operatorname{Ran}P\cap\operatorname{Ran}Q}
\ne
P_{\ker P\cap\ker Q}
=P_{\{1\}}((I-P)(I-Q)(I-P)).
}
\]

Therefore the observer-weighted dyadic identity remains valid, but its atom at one must be called the inside--inside intersection, not the Sonin sector. The positive `C_34` construction must add the complementary outside tower before assigning any Sonin boundary leg.
