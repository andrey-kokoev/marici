# One native P frame does not cover all chamber faces

Prospective rejection test on `single-frame-full-face-admission`, a connected rival of `space-admission`. It is not a rejection of every native space interpretation.

## Precise candidate

Map an elementary regular tetrahedral chamber to one native P primitive with its complete C0,M0,C1,M1,C2 reference. Require every geometric facet to be realized as a native PN shared face, in one global C/M assignment. Allow every reframe preserving that assignment, not only the reader steps actually generated by the API.

This is stronger than a point/chart identification: it asserts a full face/attachment interface. It is also stronger than showing separately that each face has some native marking.

## All-frame obstruction

The P body has exactly two C vertices and two M vertices. A facet omits exactly one of its four vertices. Omitting C leaves two M vertices; omitting M leaves only one. Hence exactly two facets can have the two-M signature required by the existing PN interface. The other two geometric facets still exist and have genuine neighboring split primitives, but not that PN signature in this fixed role assignment.

This argument applies to every placement and every complete P marking, not only the canonical example. A role-preserving change of coordinates or reader frame cannot alter these counts. The safe Cubical proof transports the count argument along any labelled vertex realization whose C/M indicator agrees with the frame. The source/API controls bind the formal two-M PN predicate to actual valid transfers.

## Competing presentations and sewing

The runtime trial checks four regular primitives (both orientations and translations), all 24 markings each, reader role preservation, and their actual PN transfers. Group the 24 markings into the six possible two-M vertex sets. The union across **all** markings can cover four faces, while each coherent group covers only two. That union is not one source interface.

The strongest discriminator takes a fixed P frame and an independently source-valid PN target across a previously blocked face. The lower geometric/PN checks pass separately. Joining that target to the fixed original frame fails global C/M role sewing. Both source records and the failed joint description are retained, rather than reducing the comparison to equal face coordinates.

## Falsifiers and boundary

A complete coherent frame whose all four geometric facets pass the declared two-M PN rule would defeat this obstruction. A missing geometric neighboring face or an invalid purported per-face PN witness would defeat the proposed discriminator, not establish the rival theorem.

The result, if verified, excludes only this full-face **single-role-frame particle-port** interpretation. It leaves chart-relative markings with explicit role-transition/coherence witnesses, alternative native cell representations, and higher WG chart inputs available as distinct candidates. The earlier uniform WG task package is not contradicted. No all-path impossibility, all-source admission rejection, physical law, mass/energy conclusion or Ramsey forcing result follows.

Any continuation using different roles on different facets must name and construct the required role-transition/descent cell before reading global incidence or colors. Bare geometry and a union of incompatible frames do not provide it.
