# Fifty-turn closeout: the remaining Xi-sensitive Green defect is either circular or provenance-ineligible

Fresh mutable-state inspection finds `theta-rh-interaction-net-state.v19.json`
as the latest interaction contract.  The realization-provenance gate still
admits only a `source_constructor` with a resolvable evidence locator and
constructor identifier; assumptions, fitted arrows, and unsupported arrows
cannot be promoted.

The strict same-sign passive Green complex is acyclic at every Xi parameter.
Hence a residue-preserving target must add or alter a finite defect module whose
cohomology appears with local length `ord_rho Xi`.

There are only two presently available provenance classes for such a defect:

1. Construct it directly from the Xi section or its Koszul complex. This has
   the right multiplicity but makes the resulting criterion circular: the
   target defect was installed using the divisor it claims to detect.
2. Declare an independent Green defect without a source constructor. The v19
   gate classifies this as unsupported (or at best an explicit assumption) and
   refuses realization promotion.

No third, independently sourced finite-defect constructor is present in the
mutable state. Therefore the current programme has reached a sharp conditional
boundary: it constructs and glues a nontrivial but zero-free passive return
unit around the known scalar determinant line, yet it does not construct an
independent Green cohomology class at Xi zeros.

This closes the investigation, not the RH problem. Any continuation must add
new mathematical input: a provenance-valid, Xi-independent stratified Green
differential with finite defect, together with a chain map proving equality of
local module lengths. Repackaging the existing scalar section, passive unit,
or formal determinant operations cannot supply that input.
