# RH conformance–positivity metaobserver

## Question

How should an infinite observer family check infinitely many identities across the prime, prime-derived, and completed-coupling pyramids?

## Claim boundary

The construction types the metaobserver and separates coherence from positivity. It does not construct the final positive arithmetic transformation.

## Lower-pyramid identity index

Let \(\alpha\) index a pair of source-derived routes

\[
F_\alpha,G_\alpha:X\longrightarrow Y
\]

inside or between:

1. the prime-atomic pyramid;
2. the prime-derived transform pyramid;
3. the completed endpoint–gamma–prime pyramid.

Let \(p\) index a faithful observer: a heat jet \((k,t)\), a character jet, or a finite translate Gram packet. Define the observed conformance residual

\[
C_{p,\alpha}(x)=
O_p(F_\alpha x)-O_p(G_\alpha x).
\]

If the observer family is jointly faithful, vanishing of every residual implies equality of the compared source routes.

## Two independent outputs

The metaobserver must return two coordinates:

- **conformance:** every \(C_{p,\alpha}\) vanishes;
- **positivity:** every observed value belongs to its required positive cone.

Categorically, the target is the intersection of a conformance equalizer with an observer positivity cone. It is not merely the equalizer.

## Exact hostile

Take the even signed distribution

\[
\rho_-=-\frac12(\delta_1+\delta_{-1}).
\]

Its heat observers satisfy

\[
J_{k,t}=-e^{-t},
\qquad
J_{k+1,t}=-\partial_tJ_{k,t},
\]

for every \(k\). Its character kernel

\[
K_\sigma(d)=-e^{-2\sigma}\cos d
\]

has the correct coincident-jet identities with the heat family. Every tested comparison diagram commutes, yet the heat values and Gram diagonals are negative.

Thus perfect transport of infinitely many identities does not imply positivity.

## No observer regress

The metaobserver is a limit specification over the product of the source-identity and probe index categories. It adds no new scientific evidence and is not a fourth lower pyramid. A valid closure is one source-derived natural transformation landing simultaneously in:

\[
\text{conformance equalizer}
\cap
\text{positive observer cone}.
\]

## Disposition

The operator's proposed architecture survives with a necessary correction: observer conformance and positive-cone admission must remain separate outputs. The missing constructor must establish both uniformly. A consistently transported signed source is the exact falsifier to any attempt to infer positivity from coherence alone.
