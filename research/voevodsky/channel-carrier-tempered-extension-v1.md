## Domain and continuity

Fourier, translations and real modulations are continuous automorphisms of Schwartz space and act continuously on its tempered dual. Gaussian multiplication at t>=0 is a continuous Schwartz multiplier: every derivative of the multiplier is polynomial times a Gaussian (constant at t=0), so product seminorms are bounded by finite sums of Schwartz seminorms. Its transpose is therefore defined on every tempered distribution. Finite compositions are licensed without forcing the four-comb span to be invariant.

Negative heat time is NOT licensed: exp(+pi |t| x²) is not a Schwartz multiplier on the whole space. Even positive heat is not asserted to have a tempered inverse. These are analytic arguments from the standard Schwartz framework, not formalized here or proved by the regression tests.

This S'(R) is the additive coordinate before the log-radial/Mellin charts. It does NOT assert the previously rejected ordinary log-tempered extension for raw radial combs. Later analytic chart transport must retain the existing induced test riggings and endpoints.
