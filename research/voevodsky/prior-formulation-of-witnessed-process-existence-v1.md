## Kitaev counting layer supplied by the operator

The operator subsequently supplied the prior Kitaev formulation with three steps and three cuts fixed:

    P(n)=n(n-1)^2,
    C(n)=3P(n),
    J(n)=C(n)[n+P(n)+C(n)]
        =3n^2(n-1)^2[1+4(n-1)^2].

It gives J(1)=0, J(2)=60 and J(3)=1836. These formulas are operator-supplied research pointers; the precise originating Kitaev artifact was not located in the targeted file search. They must not be attributed to an independently replayed source receipt.

The operator then supplied Kitaev's process-level interpretation, sharpening the cardinality reading:

- Elementary acts: n possible moves.
- Composed acts: extend a move twice, excluding the immediately previous move at each extension. The ordered path retains what followed what; hence P(n)=n(n-1)^2. This forbids immediate repetition, not a return to the first move after an intervening different move.
- Situated compositions: mark the path perspective as one move with two remaining, two with one remaining, or the completed path. These three distinct markings give C(n)=3P(n); marking is retained structure, not three unlabelled duplicate copies.
- Relationships: compare each marked path with an elementary move, a complete path, or another marked path. The partner roles remain tagged and distinct, giving J(n)=C(n)[n+P(n)+C(n)].

Thus the vocabulary is already operationally structured: order and marking make possibilities distinguishable. Its relationships are not bare pairs of uninterpreted labels. Nevertheless the count alone does not assert that every comparison is an identity/equivalence, returns to its endpoint, carries a coherence filler, or is physically realized.

This gives a concrete quantitative layer beneath the proposed witnessed-existence criterion:

    declared partner vocabulary -> typed/composable candidates
      -> required route comparisons -> retained coherent certificate.

The arrows are validation/constructor obligations, not an assumed theorem that all candidates survive. The particular endpoint/return filter and coherence conditions must come from the source. In particular, J(3)=1836 cannot be substituted for an endpoint-filtered return count, the support count of another 1836 construction, an execution-cost bound, or a physical mass.

This clarification also prevents a notation collision: P(n) here is a cardinality, whereas P/Pi in the neutral-record control work is an operator/projector. Kitaev's interpretation now supplies a concrete intended process meaning for the earlier P/3P shorthand: complete compositions and their three marked perspectives. A literal typing of the four I/I' routes still requires their actual source maps and comparison witnesses; it should use this recovered meaning rather than invent unrelated primitives.

The metaphysical candidate is therefore process-relational: acts acquire ordered histories; markings retain a situation within a composition; comparisons make those situated compositions relationally distinguishable. The stronger witnessed-existence conjecture asks whether the required compatible comparisons and returns constitute an entity's identity in the common process account. This is a conjecture about that account, not a claim that the counts themselves create or prove physical existence.
