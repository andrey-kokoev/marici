# Consistency complexes retract to corrected observers while discrepancies kill synchronized classes

## Result

For the declared corrected frame tower:

1. The full two-term frame-consistency complex has an explicit, bounded, source-equivariant deformation retraction onto the corrected observer tower. It therefore preserves its depth-extension classes.
2. Synchronized inclusion preserves a nonzero class: it has a compatible retraction, including on the depth-transition kernels.
3. The discrepancy map kills the synchronized class and every genuine synchronized source evaluation.
4. Nevertheless the discrepancy tower's OWN depth transition is the direct sum of F-1 copies of the corrected observer transition. It can be nonsplit even though its source-synchronized readout is zero.

For the concrete cubic-corrected tower, the source argument is rechecked here: the first depth transition remains split, and every later transition, together with its stated full-filtered-source pullback, remains nonsplit. This is not inferred from the frame cocycle.

The distinction is between horizontal consistency and vertical source-filtration depth. No additional Ext degree or new higher operation is created by introducing more frames.

## 1. Freeze the corrected tower and recheck its extensions

Use Nima's corrected tower: the cubic correction is introduced at observation stage three; stages one and two remain unchanged. This is not the alternative convention that adds the cubic discrepancy to the stage-two observer. At later stages retain the original detectors together with the declared corrections.

Write

    F_m=J_1/J_(m+1), O_m=O_m^sharp,
    p_m:O_(m+1)->O_m, K_m=ker(p_m),
    tau_m in Ext1(O_m,K_m).

Each O_m is a finite-dimensional source-bimodule quotient of F_m. Hence O_m I^m=0. The initial two-event source ideal corner c has exactly two dimensions, generated by its forgotten and mixed diamonds. The original O_2 already detects both, and all corrected stages m>=2 contain those original rows. Since source evaluation is onto and vertex-equivariant, no enlargement can make this corner bigger than the source corner. Therefore

    (O_m)_c has dimension two, and (K_m)_c=0, for m>=2.

Let a be the initial mixed diamond and let b=a_2...a_(m+1) be the remaining m relation factors in the supplied (m+1)-depth witness. A section of p_m would have a FORCED value on Obs_m(a), because p_m is an isomorphism at c. But b belongs to I^m, so

    Obs_m(a)b=0,
    Obs_(m+1)(a)b=Obs_(m+1)(v_(m+1)) !=0.

The last inequality follows because the original positive detector is retained by correction. This contradicts equivariance of a section. Thus tau_m is nonzero for every m>=2.

The same proof excludes a source-equivariant lift F_m->O_(m+1) of Obs_m. Consequently Obs_m^* tau_m is nonzero: the full filtered source extension

    0 -> G_(m+1) -> F_(m+1) -> F_m -> 0

still has nonzero pushout under evaluation into K_m. Its restriction to an adjacent layer is a separate question.

The first transition is unchanged by the cubic correction and has the previous explicit vacuum splitting. Hence tau_1=0. No first-stage conclusion is asserted for an arbitrary unrelated enlargement of the first two stages.

This argument gives a useful sufficient condition for other admitted corrections: retaining a fully observed initial corner, the relevant positive witness, and evaluation from F_m preserves this forced-lift obstruction. It uses neither global completed projectivity nor mere nontrivial ideal action somewhere in the target.

## 2. Horizontal maps and their exact splitting identities

Fix F>=2 coherent corrected frames, and align them to frame zero using the supplied source-bimodule transports. Let

    C_m^0=direct_sum_f O_m^f,
    C_m^1=(O_m^0)^(F-1).

Suppress the superscript zero on the reference O_m. The discrepancy map is

    Delta_m(y)_f=T_m^(0<-f)y_f-y_0.

Define

    i_m(z)=(T_m^(f<-0)z)_f,
    r_m(y)=y_0,
    s_m(d)=(0,T_m^(1<-0)d_1,...,T_m^(F-1<-0)d_(F-1)).

Then, literally as bounded source-module maps,

    r_m i_m=id, Delta_m i_m=0,
    Delta_m s_m=id, r_m s_m=0,
    i_m r_m+s_m Delta_m=id_(C_m^0).

All maps commute with restriction in m. These are the owning consistency theorem's actual maps, not chosen nonequivariant linear splittings.

## 3. An explicit homotopy equivalence of consistency complexes

Place

    C_m^bullet=[C_m^0 --Delta_m--> C_m^1]

in degrees zero and one. Regard O_m as a complex in degree zero. The maps i_m and r_m extend to chain maps and r_m i_m=id.

The sole nonzero component of the degree-minus-one homotopy h_m is

    h_m^1=s_m:C_m^1->C_m^0.

The identities in section 2 give

    d h_m+h_m d=id_(C_m^bullet)-i_m r_m.

This is a source-equivariant deformation retraction, natural in m. In fact

    C_m^bullet is isomorphic to
    O_m[0] direct_sum [C_m^1 --identity--> C_m^1].

The second summand is contractible. Thus the full consistency complex carries exactly the corrected observer's derived information, not an additional independent consistency class.

All maps are bounded at each finite stage. Their compatible componentwise maps extend continuously in the declared product/inverse-limit topology. This supplies no uniform norm estimate and does not identify every inverse-limit point with a summable source.

## 4. Vertical depth extensions under this decomposition

Let

    K_m^0=ker(C_(m+1)^0->C_m^0),
    K_m^1=ker(C_(m+1)^1->C_m^1).

After frame alignment these are K_m^F and K_m^(F-1), respectively. Restrict i,r,s,Delta to these kernels, denoting them i_K,r_K,s_K,Delta_K. They obey the same split identities.

The coordinate isomorphism

    C_m^0 -> O_m direct_sum C_m^1,
    y -> (r_m y,Delta_m y)

and its inverse commute with depth transitions. Consequently the depth extension of the state tower C^0 is the direct sum of

    0 -> K_m -> O_(m+1) -> O_m -> 0

and F-1 further copies of that extension. The discrepancy tower C^1 alone has those F-1 copies.

Therefore:
- at a split rung tau_m=0, both state and discrepancy depth extensions split;
- at a nonsplit rung tau_m!=0, both are nonsplit for F>=2;
- for 100 frames the discrepancy depth extension has 99 copies, not 99 new independent source attachment classes.

The short exact sequences of horizontal complexes also retract naturally to the reference depth sequence. Their connecting morphism is identified under the homotopy equivalences with tau_m. This is the precise derived preservation statement for the ENTIRE consistency complex.

## 5. Degree-correct preservation and annihilation of the synchronized class

Let tau_m^0 be the depth extension class of C^0 and tau_m^1 that of C^1. Naturality of the extension diagrams gives

    i_m^* tau_m^0 = (i_K)_* tau_m
        in Ext1(O_m,K_m^0).

The right side is nonzero whenever tau_m is: applying (r_K)_* returns tau_m because r_K i_K=id. This is the precise sense in which synchronized inclusion preserves the class. The same argument preserves its nonzero pullback from F_m established in section 1.

Now push that synchronized class along the discrepancy map on kernels. Since Delta_K i_K=0,

    (Delta_K)_* (i_K)_* tau_m=0
        in Ext1(O_m,K_m^1).

Equivalently, discrepancy naturality gives

    (Delta_K)_* tau_m^0=Delta_m^* tau_m^1,

and pulling back along i_m makes the right side zero because Delta_m i_m=0.

These formulas do NOT say tau_m^1 itself is zero. It lives over the larger discrepancy base C_m^1, whereas the killed class is its pullback along the ZERO map from synchronized O_m. Keeping the base and coefficient modules visible avoids the apparent contradiction.

## 6. Source evaluation and what a consistency test cannot detect

Genuine corrected frame evaluations factor as

    F_m --Obs_m--> O_m --i_m--> C_m^0.

Their composite with Delta_m is exactly zero. Thus discrepancy-only output cannot detect the genuine synchronized source attachment: that source is in its kernel before any scalar pairing or norm estimate.

The discrepancy module still has nontrivial depth extensions describing arbitrary disagreement states. Those are copies of the reference module extension structure; they are not measurements of the synchronized source through the zero discrepancy map.

This is consistent with the shared-bias obstruction. Keeping the full consistency complex retains a synchronized state; discarding that state and retaining only discrepancies loses every common signal, correct or erroneous.

Nothing here provides a new physical scalar observer, removes large frame-conditioning constants, or certifies a source realization from zero discrepancy. Such conclusions would require additional owning maps and analytical budgets.
