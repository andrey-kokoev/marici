# Combined countermodel suite for the coherence pyramid

## Question

Does the candidate computad reject the finite, variance, composition, and unbounded-form counterexamples that motivated its certificate types?

## Claim boundary

This suite verifies rejection coverage against ten materialized countermodels. It does not prove completeness of the refusal system or show that every admitted analytic fixture is sound.

## Matrix

The countermodels cover:

- nonexistence of unrestricted Green-isometry pushouts;
- failure of pairwise amalgamation certificates to compose;
- failure of every fixed finite positivity cutoff;
- negative closed limits;
- dense-core domain escape;
- new completed radicals;
- reduced-minimum-modulus collapse;
- omitted composite kernels;
- Beck–Chevalley inferred from a boundary square;
- completion comparison inferred from core equality.

Each row names a materialized result, an exact witness, and the constructor field that must refuse it.

## Strongest test

The checker loads every cited result rather than reproducing its conclusion in a new fixture. It verifies the exact witness value, confirms that the underlying result passed, and checks that the refusal token occurs in the current signature, completion, composition, or coherence interfaces.

All ten countermodels are mapped to explicit refusals. No row is accepted by weakening its witness.

## Residual

Coverage is not completeness. Three risks remain for integration:

1. an admitted combination of individually valid certificates may violate a higher law not represented by these fixtures;
2. certificate equivalence is currently a typed field, not a constructed analytic equivalence;
3. the interfaces have not been assembled into one machine-readable representation with cross-file referential checks.

## Disposition

The countermodel leaf is resolved with complete coverage of the ten selected hostile classes. The final integration leaf must assemble the files, verify cross-references and nonclaims, and decide whether the result is a full partial equipment or only a presentation for one.
