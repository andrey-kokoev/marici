# Two row permutations may compose to identity without erasing witnesses

Fresh `check_composed_row_permutations.py` transports ordered unit-square row labels and x-upper weights through a nontrivial permutation p=(2,0,3,1), then q=(1,3,0,2). Their composed permutation is identity: the final ordered rows and weights match direct transport by identity. But a synthetic two-edge source->middle->target witness chain has a distinct commitment from a hypothetical one-edge source->target direct witness. Mathematical identity after two transports cannot erase the intermediate row arrangement or turn two alleged events into an observed identity edge.

These witness labels are fictional. No owner-issued source, actual row permutation event or analytic S,A,R,C,G map is inferred.
