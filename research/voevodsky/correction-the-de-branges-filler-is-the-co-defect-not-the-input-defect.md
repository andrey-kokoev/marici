# Correction: the de Branges filler is the co-defect, not the input defect

## Operator orientation audit

For a Schur multiplier \(M_\Theta\) on Hardy space, the de Branges--Rovnyak kernel is generated by

\[
I-M_\Theta M_\Theta^*,
\]

not by

\[
I-M_\Theta^*M_\Theta.
\]

The previous identification used the wrong operator orientation.

## Reproducing-kernel calculation

Let \(k_z\) be the upper-half-plane Hardy reproducing kernel. Then

\[
M_\Theta^*k_z
=
\overline{\Theta(z)}k_z.
\]

Therefore

\[
\left\langle
(I-M_\Theta M_\Theta^*)k_w,
k_z
\right\rangle
=
\frac{
1-
\Theta(z)
\overline{\Theta(w)}
}{
2\pi i(
\overline w-z
)
}.
\]

Multiplication by the outer Clark denominator \(E\) gives

\[
\frac{
E(z)
\overline{E(w)}
-
E^*(z)
\overline{E^*(w)}
}{
2\pi i(
\overline w-z
)
}.
\]

Thus the positive feature is

\[
d_z
=
(I-M_\Theta M_\Theta^*)^{1/2}k_z,
\]

followed by the fixed \(E\)-weighting.

## Inner case

Under the desired Hermite--Biehler property, \(\Theta\) is inner. Then

\[
M_\Theta^*M_\Theta=I,
\]

so the input defect vanishes. This does not make the de Branges space trivial.

Instead,

\[
I-M_\Theta M_\Theta^*
\]

is the orthogonal projection onto

\[
\mathcal K_\Theta
=
H^2\ominus
\Theta H^2.
\]

The missing positive state space is therefore the model-space complement of the outgoing range.

## Correct control geometry

The canonical diagram is

\[
H^2
\xrightarrow{M_\Theta}
H^2,
\]

with co-defect quotient or complement

\[
H^2
\longrightarrow
\mathcal K_\Theta.
\]

For inner \(\Theta\),

\[
H^2
=
\Theta H^2
\oplus
\mathcal K_\Theta.
\]

This is an output-space decomposition, not an input-space Stinespring column.

## Consequence for the tetrahedral comparison

The previously requested factorization

\[
TM_\Theta
=
(I-M_\Theta^*M_\Theta)^{1/2}
\]

is irrelevant to the de Branges kernel and must be discarded.

The meaningful incidence maps are now:

1. inclusion of the outgoing range \(\Theta H^2\) into \(H^2\);
2. quotient or projection from \(H^2\) to \(\mathcal K_\Theta\);
3. the zero composite from \(\Theta H^2\) to the quotient.

These form an exact sequence

\[
0
\longrightarrow
\Theta H^2
\longrightarrow
H^2
\longrightarrow
\mathcal K_\Theta
\longrightarrow
0.
\]

This incidence shape is much closer to a coherence cell: the missing edge is canonically zero because the outgoing range is killed by the co-defect quotient.

## Remaining condition

The sequence becomes a positive orthogonal decomposition only after \(\Theta\) is known to be Schur and inner. Before that, positivity of

\[
I-M_\Theta M_\Theta^*
\]

is exactly the global Pick condition.

## Disposition

The canonical missing filler is the co-defect space

\[
\mathcal K_\Theta
=
\operatorname{ran}
(I-M_\Theta M_\Theta^*)^{1/2}.
\]

This corrects the operator orientation and replaces the failed input-defect tetrahedral analogy by an exact range--ambient--quotient sequence.
