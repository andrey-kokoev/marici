# Site exchange identifies the v_alg line inside the primitive pyramid

## Question

Can the existing site-exchange action identify the second source coordinate inside the primitive \(A_1^3\) closure without a second soft-center calculation?

## Claim boundary

This packet identifies the rational and primitive integral character line. It does not fix the period normalization between that line and the displayed de Rham generator \(v_{\rm alg}\).

## Exchange on the four vertices

Site exchange preserves the endpoint critical values \(0\) and \(2(x+y)\) and swaps the middle values \(2x,2y\). After retaining the corresponding vertex labels, its action is

\[
d_1\mapsto d_1,
\qquad d_2\leftrightarrow d_3,
\qquad d_4\mapsto d_4.
\]

On the primitive edge frame this gives

\[
\alpha_{12}\leftrightarrow\alpha_{13},
\qquad
\alpha_{14}\mapsto\alpha_{14}.
\]

Hence the exchange-odd sublattice is rank one, generated primitively by

\[
\beta_{\rm odd}=\alpha_{12}-\alpha_{13}
=\frac{d_2-d_3}{2}.
\]

Since the \(A_1^3\) Gram matrix is \(-2I_3\),

\[
\beta_{\rm odd}^2=-4.
\]

## Character of v_alg

At \(E=0\),

\[
v_{\rm alg}
=x^2y^2\bigl((x^2-y^2)e_7+2e_8-2e_9\bigr).
\]

Under site exchange

\[
x\leftrightarrow y,
\qquad e_8\leftrightarrow e_9,
\qquad e_7\mapsto e_7,
\]

every term changes sign. Therefore

\[
v_{\rm alg}\mapsto-v_{\rm alg}.
\]

The source \(v_{\rm alg}\) line and the primitive pyramid’s \(\beta_{\rm odd}\) line have the same character. Because the latter is the unique exchange-odd line in \(A_1^3\), any nonzero exchange-equivariant comparison must send

\[
\beta_{\rm odd}\longmapsto c\,v_{\rm alg}
\]

for one scalar \(c\).

## Information flow

The relational information should therefore flow through the two middle vertices as

\[
(d_2,d_3)
\longmapsto
\frac{d_2-d_3}{2}
\longmapsto
v_{\rm alg}.
\]

This differs from the collision-pair half-sum

\[
\frac{d_2+d_3}{2}=-\alpha_{14},
\]

which is exchange-even and belongs to the endpoint/e6 sector. The pyramid needs both operations:

- half-sum of the middle vertices for the invariant endpoint channel;
- half-difference of the middle vertices for the exchange-odd complementary channel.

Both are integral in the primitive closure.

## Disposition

Site exchange already identifies the geometric home of \(v_{\rm alg}\): the unique primitive odd line generated by \((d_2-d_3)/2\). The remaining comparison has been reduced to one nonzero normalization scalar and orientation. It no longer needs to choose among three \(A_1\) axes.
