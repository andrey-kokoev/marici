# The prior Green and curvature packets identify the only admissible kind of construction

## Result of the deeper search

The prior source calculations go beyond suggesting an infinite tail carrier. They rule out several broad meanings of “construction” and isolate the admissible one.

## The faithful phase carrier has dense rotational holonomy

Prime-scale translation acts on the real phase vector

\[
(\cos xS,\sin xS)
\]

by rotation through

\[
x\log p.
\]

For fixed \(x\ne0\), at least one of the rotations generated by \(p=2,3\) is irrational; its powers are dense in \(SO(2)\). Therefore no nontrivial proper convex cone in the phase plane can be preserved by all prime transports.

Consequently a prime-covariant linear cone, sign local system, or finite orientation homotopy cannot prove the required oscillatory sign.

The unique invariant positive quadratic metric is, up to scale,

\[
\cos^2(xS)+\sin^2(xS).
\]

But the logarithmic-curvature readout is the spin-two component

\[
\operatorname{Re}(A+iB)^2=A^2-B^2,
\]

not the invariant trace

\[
|A+iB|^2=A^2+B^2.
\]

Thus ordinary Gram positivity does not by itself orient the target.

## All orienting information is forced to the boundary

The translation-covariant bulk can carry only the invariant positive energy. It cannot select the spin-two direction. Hence all RH-relevant orientation must be supplied where covariance ceases to be two-sided:

1. the primitive lower endpoint;
2. stopped prime-translation currents;
3. the reciprocal modular seam;
4. Clark-differentiated boundary terms.

The “defects” are therefore not errors around an otherwise complete bulk proof. They are the only channels capable of selecting an orientation.

## Exact bilateral decomposition

For a positive half-source transform

\[
F(z)=\int_0^\infty f(u)e^{izu}\,du,
\qquad
X(z)=F(z)+F(-z),
\]

\[
XX''-(X')^2
=
[FF''-(F')^2](z)
+
[FF''-(F')^2](-z)
+
\mathscr S_F(z),
\]

where the seam term is

\[
\mathscr S_F(z)

(u+v)^2f(u)f(v)e^{iz(u-v)}\,du\,dv.

The same-sheet bulk uses squared differences and oscillates in the sum coordinate. The seam uses squared sums and oscillates in the difference coordinate. Modular reflection exchanges the two.

The seam kernel \((u+v)^2\) has algebraic rank at most three. Thus at first curvature order, the infinite bulk is corrected by an explicit finite-rank boundary jet—not an unspecified coherence.

## Why a frame change cannot repair the hierarchy

At generalized Laguerre order \(n\), the source correlation carrier has reversal metric

\[
J_n
\]

with exact signature

\[
(n+1,n).
\]

By Sylvester inertia, no invertible sewing or change of frame can turn this metric positive. Positivity requires a type-changing operation:

1. compression to a source-selected positive image;
2. quotient by a differential-stable negative sector;
3. Hilbert dilation with boundary states; or
4. proof that the physical theta image avoids all negative directions.

This is the precise sense in which “more coherence” is insufficient if coherence means an invertible comparison among the same states.

## Immediate finite computation

At first curvature order, the exact seam rank is three. The next tractable calculation is therefore not an arbitrary infinite Schur complement. It is:

1. form the invariant two-copy difference bulk;
2. adjoin the three moment traces generated by \(1,u,u^2\);

Nevertheless, the rank-three first-order Schur calculation is the smallest exact test of the only surviving architecture.
