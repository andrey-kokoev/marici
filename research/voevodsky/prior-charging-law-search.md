# Prior charging-law search after the common-mass exclusion

## Scope

Searched prior mass, kinetic, inertia, retained-resource and field-action research, then followed the directly relevant source packets and successors. This is a document search; no checker was rerun and no new Hamiltonian was constructed.

The strongest recovered clue is that the assumption excluded by our latest bound—one elementary gap per retained arrow—had already been distinguished from source-generated energy in several independent audits. The new no-go is therefore an exclusion of one explicit occupied-record implementation, not of all source energy readers.

## 1. Implementation count is not an architecture-invariant baseline

`research/nima/twelve-triangle-net-minimality.md` gives the same full 36-port return projector with 1836, 576, 73 and 72 arrows. Geometry and the entire input/output operator are retained. The 72/73 minima have specified network-class proofs; the original graph remains pruning-minimal at fixed weights.

This does not say that physically occupied records of the native implementation may be erased for free. It does show that N=1836 cannot become an invariant rest baseline merely because it counts one implementation. A physical energy interpretation must specify which retained records are real degrees of freedom and why their occupations survive.

`cube-octahedron-architecture-independent-energy-audit.md` already supplies a constructive alternative: a shared-edge connection Laplacian plus local selection penalty. It is invariant under feedback refactoring, and different geometric source adjacencies have different exact excitation gaps. It does not assign energy according to circuit support. Its coherent ground still has zero energy; a positive rest sector is an additional connection.

**Reuse:** apply implementation/refactoring controls to the charging reader before imposing a universal gap per native arrow.

## 2. Source metrics give unequal operation weights already

`proton-electron-shared-state-comparison-hypothesis.md` directly computes raw arrow and state norms in G=11I+ones:

    arrow norm squared = 22,
    state norm squared = 12.

The tensor Gram reader yields 212976/121 rather than 1836. Unit-normalizing both blocks reinstates 1836 by an additional rule; it is not the raw source metric.

`geometry-comparison-energy-bridge.md` makes this distinction executable on the actual geometric carriers. Stored-channel energy gives 324*(Delta_L-Delta_G); coefficient-quadratic energy gives 9*(gamma_G-gamma_L). They are different observables and can have opposite preferences.

`comparison-update-dynamics-and-energy-normalization.md` likewise derives the normalized weak-update spectrum (10,13,65/2 four times), different from the unnormalized storage spectrum (24,30,99 four times). Its exact memory lift retains mismatch budgets but leaves their physical normalization free. Full sweep quantization additionally needs the duration and logarithm branch.

**Reuse:** retain the actual coefficient metric and operation normalization; do not equate an occupied register gap, a constraint eigenvalue and a traversal cost.

## 3. A source-linked kinetic action was already constructed

`research/nima/comparison-kinetic-readout.md` derives a tangent metric from the diagonal Hessian of the same neighboring-probe overlap readout. Independent tensor sewing makes these metrics additive. Its complete scalar action keeps kinetic and potential terms together.

The same interaction has a zero potential quartic in one coordinate chart but a nonzero derivative vertex; the full on-shell vertex agrees across three charts. This is an existing concrete warning against repairing the rest offset using only a potential coefficient while leaving its kinetic normalization unchanged.

The spacetime interpretation and action scales F,U are supplied. The construction supplies a transport method and shape relations, not numerical proton/electron masses.

**Reuse:** source a charging/inertial quadratic form together with its canonical pairing, and transport both under the carrier adapter.

## 4. The closest existing dynamical action is matter/link feedback

`record-gauge-feedback-loop.md` already includes matter kinetic energy, electric field energy, current backreaction and Gauss conservation. Our later `weighted-matter-link-adapter.md` and `open-flux-common-vacuum.md` already adapt that structure to the actual source modes and retain a common empty reference. The older `prior-field-action-search.md` lists this adapter as missing; that statement is superseded by the completed adapter.

This is a genuine alternative energy law to the independent-gap count. Its current completion uses classical internal norm-charge sectors, and exterior attachment changes the ordering. It is not yet a sharp signed electric-charge quantum sector for the elementary spinor.

**Next specific adapter:** identify the actual rank-two elementary spinor's kinetic/current variables and signed charge with that existing action, retaining its source metric and common exterior data. Do not build another generic oscillator or another field-feedback fixture. Compare both absolute and relative energies under the same charging form.

- `mass-primitive-quotient-charge.md` constructs the correct primitive unit charge but leaves the capacitance form independent. Equal inertias are not selected by the quotient.
- `mass-local-sector-protection.md` protects transitions under exact locality/Gauss hypotheses but permits one-site diagonal energy changes. Sector protection is not numerical gap protection.

These are completed tested branches, not untried fixes for the latest exclusion.

## Search conclusion

Prior work contains the charging/kinetic alternatives and their acceptance tests, but the inspected material does not contain a selected physical charging form yielding both mass ratios. The useful recovered direction is an architecture-aware kinetic/current adapter to the existing matter/link action, not another unit-gap record model or a fitted elementary offset.

The latest common-gap no-go should be retained as a control. A new source action must genuinely violate one of its assumptions and justify that difference through retained physical degrees of freedom, rather than change the energy units.
