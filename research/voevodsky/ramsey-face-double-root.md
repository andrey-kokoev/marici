# Four face anchors force a second root and a 21-vertex common core

Fix a legal 8-regular 35-vertex endpoint and the four retained neighborhoods corresponding to one cube face. Let r be the root, P the forced 13-vertex (3,5) graph blue to the four retained newcomers, Q the four erased newcomers in P, and z the core vertex incident to all four retained labels. The other 16 exterior core vertices have nonempty retained masks, so all are blue to z; r is blue to z as well. Thus z's other four red neighbors form a blue four T inside P. This statement does not assign any of the missing label rows. The four retained newcomers are also red neighbors of z. In the new blue core, r takes the all-four incidence formerly held by z, and the nine core vertices from P now exclude T rather than Q; none carry any retained label.

Put R=P minus (Q union T). The common blue neighborhood H of r and z consists of R and the 16 exterior vertices other than z. It is (3,7)-legal: a blue seven would join r,z. Hence n=21+|Q intersect T|<=22. The 16 exterior vertices induce exactly 30 red edges: the 17 exterior vertices had 30 (13 vertices of P have 4 edges leaving P; four of these hit r, leaving 48 to the 17; of the 140 edges, 26 lie inside P, 52 leave it, and 32 are incident to retained newcomers outside P). z is isolated inside those 17. Each vertex of R has exactly four red neighbors outside P, all in the 16. Consequently e(H)=30+4|R|+e(R).

For overlap one, exact enumeration of the blue fours of the unique 13-type gives e(R)<=4, hence e(H)<=58. The 191 stored 22-vertex (3,7) graphs all have at least 60 edges. This rules out overlap one. Therefore r,z share exactly the four retained red neighbors; Q and T are disjoint. Now |R|=5, and the possible induced shapes give e(H)=51..54. The known cube is in the 53-edge layer.

Each marked four independently has to extend H by four mutually blue supports plus a root to a legal 26-vertex (3,8) graph (take the nonneighbors of the opposite root). For p in Q or T its support must include its prescribed red neighbors in R and exactly three of the other 16 vertices. In the 51-edge layer only three catalogue types admit the required five-set with four external neighbors per designated vertex. Testing all 900 labeled embeddings excludes all of them on the first four-support test. This elimination did not assume that the two independently chosen panels glue. In contrast, a largest-clique test ignoring the designated five-set rejected only one of 5747 eligible catalogue types overall. Future tests should keep the designation and require both panels to glue, including the cross-panel red edges and the exterior incidence quotas.
