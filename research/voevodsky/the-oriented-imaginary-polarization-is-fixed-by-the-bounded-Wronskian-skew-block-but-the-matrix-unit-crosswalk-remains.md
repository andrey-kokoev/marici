# The oriented imaginary polarization is fixed by the bounded Wronskian skew block, but the matrix-unit crosswalk remains

The two-endpoint Hermitian cell

\[
G_p=\begin{pmatrix}a&c+ih\\c-ih&b\end{pmatrix}
\]

has one coordinate invisible to endpoint norms and real difference energy:
`h`, the reciprocal odd orientation.  Fresh inspection shows that this
coordinate is not numerically free.  The source-derived ordered Wronskian form
extends continuously to the response orbit and has a unique bounded skew
operator

\[
\omega_{\rm link}(f,g)=\langle J_{\rm link}f,g\rangle,
\qquad J_{\rm link}^*=-J_{\rm link}.
\]

The radial identity fixes its placement and coefficient as

\[
K_{\rm link}=-\frac12J_{\rm link},
\]

not by fitting.  Therefore the imaginary mixed polarization `h` is supplied by
the ordered linking block, while `a,b,c` come from the symmetric endpoint
Green energies.  Together these four real coordinates determine the complete
primewise cell Gram.

Adding the skew block preserves the old symmetric Green concomitant and
maximal-isotropic boundary sewing because

\[
\langle K_{\rm link}f,g\rangle+
\langle f,K_{\rm link}g\rangle=0.
\]

Thus the local obstruction is no longer “find a source value for `h`.”  It is
the representation crosswalk: prove on each of the four endpoint matrix units
that the coefficient form assembled from `(a,b,c,h)` maps under the quadratic
multiplication representation to the analytic Green-plus-skew block on the
common rapid core.

The required identity is

\[
\Gamma_\pi(G_p^{\rm cell})
=G_p^{\rm analytic}\oplus K_{\rm link},
\]

with wall splitting, radical compatibility, and reciprocal orientation.  The
existing radial relation `partial_t rho=e-W/2` verifies the coefficient of the
skew term, but does not by itself establish all four matrix-unit identities or
cutoff-uniform prime assembly.

Accordingly:

- symmetric cell coordinates: source determined;
- imaginary orientation coordinate: source determined;
- bounded skew placement: constructed;
- full matrix-unit quadratic representation: still unproved;
- positivity/sectorial control of the assembled Gram: still unproved.
