# Recovered proof philosophy: constructive capability beyond univalent identification

## Central source

`research/sontag/deutschean-control-explanation-of-marici-object.md` gives the strongest explicit philosophical formulation found:

> A Marici object is a source-relative law of constructive possibility.

Its explanatory core is the minimal context-indexed realization of a source-defined constructor capability, considered up to source-authorized equivalence. A closed packet is a presentation or section of that law, not the invariant object merely because its bytes are closed.

The process law is written as

    gamma_c:X_c -> O_c × product_(u in U_c) X_(c.u).

A state is characterized through its records and its responses to admitted continuations. Histories are identified by their complete task-relevant traces AND successor capabilities across admitted context paths. Resources, retained source distinctions and intervention scope matter when they change those capabilities. Complete genealogy is not automatically essential; present readout is not automatically sufficient.

## Why this goes beyond univalent identification

`research/aspect/univalent-moving-identity-synthesis.md` distinguishes source presentation, localization at source-derived equivalences, univalent completion and downstream readout. Its core question concerns which equivalences constitute identity and how their witnesses are retained.

The constructive-capability proposal asks an additional foundational question: what generates and maintains the operations and comparisons making that identity usable? An equivalence presented in a universe is not by that fact a supplied source operation, directed evaluator or continuation-preserving physical process. The proposed foundation includes an account of these constructors rather than accepting the meaning of their primitives as an unexplained signature.

`research/voevodsky/resolution-net-v1/foundational-alignment.md` makes the distinction explicit. The checked substitution equation

    eval_f(bind_k(d)) = eval_(s -> eval_f(k(s)))(d)

aligns mathematical derivation composition with computation. But it holds for arbitrary supplied operations. Substitution organizes the signature; it does not select or generate its meaningful primitive rules. The stronger target is dependent substitution, with later interfaces and witnesses transformed by earlier results.

`research/voevodsky/resolution-net-v1/dependent-interface-foundation.md` identifies the remaining gap even in a dependent model: it inherits its functions and transport from Cubical Agda, while resource-sensitive execution requires additional structure. `research/nima/dependent-transport-machine.md` then supplies an independently specified fragment of syntax/reduction and proves its interpretation sound; equal endpoints do not determine transport action, and equal output actions do not recover erased route syntax.

`research/nima/generating-grammar/NATIVE-CORE.md` records a concrete grammar with typed premise ports and retained derivations. Rule application retains actual premise derivations; comparison composition retains both pointed comparisons; higher rules retain the actual paths and comparison witnesses. Arbitrary packages are not admitted through a fallback.

`research/nima/retained-tetrahedral-package.md` constructs complete typed boundaries and fillers, with downward recovery of actual types AND their inhabitants. `research/nima/tetrahedral-hierarchy-at-vertex.md` shows why the returned marked value must retain the package if later use needs its edges/faces/filler. `research/voevodsky/closure-four-presentations-hott-proof.md` compares independently constructed operations together with their square witnesses.

These are concrete instances of a proof philosophy in which a result is a reusable constructive capability: it carries the source relation, actual construction, compatible comparisons and the continuations for which it is sufficient.

## Proposed foundational thesis (synthesis of these sources)

A foundation of reusable mathematical construction must account jointly for:

1. how source rules generate typed acts and dependent compositions;
2. how marking makes compositions situated and comparable;
3. how identity/equivalence is witnessed coherently;
4. how those witnesses license substitution and further construction;
5. how resource/context changes preserve or alter that capability.

Proof is not only a certificate that a terminal proposition has an inhabitant. It is a source-grounded, compositionally reusable realization of the capability asserted by the proposition. Existence in this process account is witnessed by a coherent constructor package, not by the bare name or returned endpoint.

Kitaev's operator-supplied progression fits this thesis: acts n, composed paths P(n)=n(n-1)^2, situated compositions C(n)=3P(n), relationships J(n)=C(n)[n+P(n)+C(n)]. It supplies the structured comparison vocabulary; actual compatible comparison witnesses give that vocabulary a realized process law.

The proposed incompleteness claim is about foundation of constructive realization: univalent identification alone does not determine the source-generated contextual constructor law. A proof system that claims realization while supplying only terminal inhabitation or structural equivalence leaves that law unaccounted for. This is an additional obligation, not an assertion that UF cannot express the richer system.
