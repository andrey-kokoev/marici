# Evaluation-path indexing infinity-category

## Question

What indexes all lawful evaluation orders of a typed surface-and-filler diagram without treating coherence degree as transition length or physical order?

## Claim boundary

This packet defines a candidate indexing infinity-category from a finite typed cellular presentation. It does not prove that the current Marici SCC supplies every required generator or relation, that the resulting evaluation space is contractible, or that the existing rungs A0 through A7 are its skeletal stages.

## Disposition

Replace the informal path category by the simplicial localization of a typed contraction category. The ordinary category of schedules loses the higher witnesses that the construction is meant to retain. The first implementation target is the tetrahedral 3-skeleton; the first global theorem is contractibility of each fixed-boundary evaluation fiber.

## 1. Input object

Let X be a finite typed cellular computad with:

- objects labelling interfaces;
- oriented generating 1-cells labelling transports;
- generating 2-cells labelling surfaces between parallel composites;
- generating higher cells labelling fillers;
- source and target maps;
- a declared class of admissible gluings;
- a variance label on every interface;
- declared dual pairs and pairings where mates are used.

Finiteness here concerns the chosen presentation of X. It does not imply that its completion, realization, or family of refinements is finite.

## 2. Dependency order

For elementary contractions c and d in X, write c < d precisely when the output interface created by c is an input interface consumed by d. Take the transitive closure. This is composition dependency, not physical time.

Independent contractions are incomparable in this partial order and have disjoint consumed interiors. Equal dimensions alone do not establish independence.

## 3. Typed contraction category

Define Contr(X).

### Objects

An object is a legal contraction state (Y, q), where Y is a typed subdivision obtainable from X and q records the uncontracted cells, retained boundary, variance, and pairing data.

Initial object: the fully exposed presentation of X.

Terminal objects, when they exist: complete contractions with the declared external boundary retained.

### Generating arrows

1. contraction: evaluate one admissible inner cell;
2. refinement: replace a cell by an authorized finer presentation;
3. associator move: rotate a parenthesized composable triple;
4. interchange move: swap independent contractions;
5. unitor move: insert or remove a declared identity cell;
6. mate move: transport a cell across a declared pairing;
7. filler move: replace an admitted boundary diagram by its filler, or expose that boundary when the inverse move is authorized.

Every generator carries exact source, target, boundary, variance, and provenance. A mate move is undefined without its pairing. A filler move is undefined without its boundary map.

## 4. Why Contr(X) is insufficient

Quotienting paths in Contr(X) by associativity, interchange, and filler equations would erase the witnesses relating evaluation orders. Keeping all paths without higher relations would instead mistake equivalent rewrite histories for distinct structure.

Let W be the class generated by associator, interchange, unitor, mate, and authorized refinement equivalences. Contractions need not belong to W.

The indexing object is

P(X) := L_W Contr(X),

the simplicial localization of the contraction category at W. Equivalently, one may use a Boardman–Vogt resolution of the colored operadic presentation when all compositions are operadic. The simplicial localization is primary because it also permits non-operadic fillers and mixed-variance cells.

P(X) is an infinity-category, not generally an ordinary category or groupoid.

## 5. Generating coherence cells

The localization must retain at least these 2-cells:

- associator pentagon faces;
- unitor triangles;
- interchange squares for independent contractions;
- naturality squares for transport against contraction;
- mate triangles and mate naturality squares;
- filler-boundary comparison cells;
- refinement versus contraction comparison cells.

It must retain higher syzygies among those relations rather than imposing them silently as equality. In particular, the tower–cotower tetrahedron is a 3-simplex whose four faces are constructor naturality, observation naturality, source mate coherence, and realized mate coherence.

With orientation [1234], its boundary is

F1 - F2 + F3 - F4.

A tetrahedral filler witnesses that this boundary is null in the declared mapping object; it is not a shortest path through four stages.

## 6. Evaluation

Let C be the target infinity-category of typed Marici carriers, transports, pairings, and higher cells. An evaluation is a homotopy-coherent functor

Ev_X : P(X) -> C.

For a fixed external boundary b, let P_b(X) be the fiber of complete contractions retaining b.

Path independence at boundary b means that Ev_X restricted to P_b(X) is homotopy constant. A stronger sufficient condition is that P_b(X) is contractible and all elementary coherence maps are sent to equivalences. Contractibility must be proved; it is not inferred from the existence of several comparison cells.

The coherent evaluation object is the homotopy limit

Eval(X,b) := holim_{p in P_b(X)} Ev_X(p).

Its points are compatible families over all admitted evaluation orders, not preferred schedules.

## 7. Skeletal coherence tower

Do not identify this tower with the semantic A0 through A7 tower without a comparison theorem.

Define instead

H_n(X,b) := holim_{p in sk_n P_b(X)} Ev_X(p).

Restriction induces a tower

... -> H_3 -> H_2 -> H_1 -> H_0.

Depending on variance conventions, the displayed arrows may be reversed when stages are presented as extensions rather than restrictions. The convention must be frozen before comparison with the SCC pyramid.

H_0 stores evaluations at schedules. H_1 adds compatible transport along elementary evaluation moves. H_2 adds compatibility across relation surfaces. H_3 adds compatibility across tetrahedral fillers. Higher H_n retain higher syzygies.

## 8. Residual cotower

Let R be a declared response object or coefficient system. The degree-n residual detector is formed from normalized cochains on the nerve of P_b(X) with coefficients in the pulled-back response system:

C^n(P_b(X); R).

The alternating coface differential detects failure of an n-stage assignment to extend to degree n+1. For the tetrahedron this gives the residual

r_3 = F1 - F2 + F3 - F4.

The dual tower therefore records obstruction classes, not merely formal transposes. Its construction requires the coefficient system, topology, and pairing at every stage.

## 9. Relation to existing Marici representations

The typed reconciler pyramid is a coherence-degree projection. It can record which boundary cells a proposed filler requires, but it does not display variance or the full evaluation-order indexing object.

The tower–cotower tetrahedron is one 3-dimensional simplex in N(P(X)), the simplicial nerve of the localization. It is not the entire indexing object.

The semantic tower A0 through A7 may map into the skeletal tower H_n, but its rung index currently records changes of mathematical role. A theorem must identify which semantic promotion realizes which skeleton-extension map. The indices must not be equated by typography.

## 10. First executable presentation

For the current tetrahedral candidate, freeze:

- four typed vertices S, R, S-dual, R-dual;
- forward edge T_real : S -> R;
- response edge T_real-dual : R-dual -> S-dual;
- source and realized pairing edges;
- two mixed mate edges;
- four oriented face witnesses;
- one tetrahedral filler whose boundary is F1 - F2 + F3 - F4.

Then enumerate every complete contraction schedule of this finite presentation and generate elementary arrows by one associator, interchange, mate, or filler move. Compute connected components and low-dimensional homology of its nerve. This is a finite presentation test only.

## 11. Acceptance tests

1. Every schedule retains the same typed external boundary.
2. Every elementary move preserves source, target, variance, and declared pairings.
3. Independent swaps occur only for dependency-incomparable contractions with disjoint consumed interiors.
4. Every mate move cites a pairing.
5. Every filler move cites its complete oriented boundary.
6. The tetrahedral boundary is exactly F1 - F2 + F3 - F4 under the frozen orientation.
7. All complete schedules lie in one connected component, or each extra component is retained as an exact obstruction.
8. Low-dimensional homology is trivial through the tested cutoff if contractibility is claimed through that cutoff.
9. A deliberate missing-face fixture produces the predicted nonzero boundary residual.
10. No finite cutoff is promoted to contractibility of the full indexing infinity-category.

## 12. First missing typed data

The current visualization names the tetrahedral cells but does not yet define:

- the consumed and produced interfaces of each contraction;
- the dependency relation;
- which cross-edge moves are equivalences in W;
- the source pairing needed for mate transport;
- the exact four face maps as composable higher cells.

The first blocker is the typed contraction signature. Enumeration of evaluation orders must stop until that signature is materialized.
