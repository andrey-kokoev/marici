# Free gauge-companion extension of the Markov fragment

## Question

Can the companion/conjoint obstruction be repaired without pretending that a vertical gauge is an existing chain-extension arrow?

## Claim boundary

This packet freely adjoins a new horizontal gauge-correspondence class generated by sourced vertex-sign isometries. It constructs an equipment fragment for the gauge subgroupoid only. It does not prove that these formal correspondences coincide with an independently supplied physical or analytic correspondence theory.

## New horizontal generators

For every vertical sign-gauge isometry

\[
f_\varepsilon:G\to G^\varepsilon,
\qquad
G^\varepsilon=D_\varepsilon GD_\varepsilon,
\]

adjoin two separately typed horizontal generators:

\[
f_{\varepsilon *}:G\rightsquigarrow G^\varepsilon,
\qquad
f_\varepsilon^*:G^\varepsilon\rightsquigarrow G.
\]

They are not chain extensions. Their provenance stores the exact diagonal isometry \(D_\varepsilon\). Since \(D_\varepsilon^{-1}=D_\varepsilon\), companion and conjoint carry opposite variance but the same matrix witness.

## Cells and equations

Adjoin companion unit and counit squares for \(f_{\varepsilon *}\), and conjoint unit and counit squares for \(f_\varepsilon^*\). Quotient the generated cell language by the two companion triangle equations and the two conjoint triangle equations.

For composable gauges \(\varepsilon,\eta\), impose the comparison

\[
(f_\eta\circ f_\varepsilon)_*
\cong
f_{\eta *}\odot f_{\varepsilon *},
\]

with the pointwise-product sign witness. Identity gauge corresponds to the horizontal identity. Because pointwise sign multiplication is associative, these comparison cells satisfy their pentagon strictly.

## Analytic realization

On finite Green matrices, every generator is realized by the orthogonal matrix \(D_\varepsilon\). Composition is matrix multiplication, adjoint is transpose, and

\[
D_\varepsilon^TD_\varepsilon=I.
\]

Thus the formal unit, counit, and triangle equations have an exact finite-dimensional realization. The construction adds a new typed class rather than relabelling an old horizontal extension.

## Interaction boundary

Gauge correspondences commute with Markov concatenation only when seam signs agree, as proved by the restricted interchange certificate. Uniformly contractive completion transports them to diagonal unitaries on \(\ell^2\). No claim is made for gauge quotients, general vertical maps, or other pyramid sectors.

## Disposition

The gauge subgroupoid of the scalar Markov fragment now has companions and conjoints after an explicit free horizontal extension with exact matrix realization. This supplies a restricted equipment fragment, not a full coherence-pyramid equipment.
