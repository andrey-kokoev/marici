# All-rank Weil positivity is universal rung-four Schwarz coherence

## Reframed primitive phase

Let \(L\) be the Hermitian source observer on the unital star-algebra generated by Gaussian translate channels. A primitive at phase \(k\) is allowed to be any finite composite

\[
p=\sum_{j=1}^k c_j u_{a_j}.
\]

Its next observation phase contains \(p^*p\).

The rung-three readings are

\[
L(p),
\qquad
L(p^*p),
\]

and the rung-four incidence object is the Schwarz square

\[
S_L(p)=
\begin{pmatrix}
L(1)&L(p)\\
L(p^*)&L(p^*p)
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

## Universal rung-four theorem

Assume \(L(1)>0\). Then

\[
S_L(p)\succeq0
\]

is equivalent to

\[
L(1)L(p^*p)\geq |L(p)|^2.
\]

If this holds for every admitted composite primitive \(p\), then

\[
L(p^*p)\geq\frac{|L(p)|^2}{L(1)}\geq0.
\]

Thus \(L\) is positive on every finite Gaussian square. Conversely, positivity of \(L\) gives the Schwarz inequality by applying positivity to

\[
(p-\lambda1)^*(p-\lambda1)
\]

and optimizing over \(\lambda\).

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
L\text{ positive on all finite packets}
\iff
S_L(p)\succeq0\text{ for every composite primitive }p.
}
\]

## Rank migration

This does not discard arbitrary-rank information. It moves rank from the matrix into the ancestry of the primitive:

\[
(c_1,\ldots,c_k)
\longmapsto
p=\sum_jc_ju_{a_j}.
\]

A negative rank-\(k\) Gram witness becomes one composite primitive with

\[
L(p^*p)<0,
\]

and therefore immediately violates its two-by-two rung-four Schwarz square.

So rung four is sufficient provided the primitive phase is closed under every finite linear combination.

## RH interpretation

Under the exact endpoint–gamma–prime source identity and Gaussian density, the Weil criterion becomes:

> RH holds if and only if every finite composite Gaussian primitive and its square form a positive rung-four Schwarz incidence square, coherently under enlargement and completion.

Higher coherence rungs no longer demand larger positivity matrices. Their role is to certify:

- closure of the primitive class under finite composition;
- independence of presentation of the same composite primitive;
- compatibility of \(p\mapsto p^*p\) with forward and reverse transport;
- continuity into the completed Schwartz test space.

## Remaining substantive gate

Existence of the readings \(L(p)\) and \(L(p^*p)\) does not force their Schwarz determinant to be nonnegative. The source-level theorem still required is

\[
L(1)L(p^*p)-|L(p)|^2\geq0
\]

for arbitrary source-derived composite \(p\).

The reframing is nevertheless exact: the RH gate can be placed uniformly at rung four rather than represented as an unbounded ladder of unrelated matrix ranks.
