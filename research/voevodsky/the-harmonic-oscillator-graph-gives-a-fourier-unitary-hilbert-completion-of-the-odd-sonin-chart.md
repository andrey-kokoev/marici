# The harmonic-oscillator graph gives a Fourier-unitary Hilbert completion of the odd Sonin chart

## Question

Can the generated odd Sonin test chart be completed Hilbertianly despite unbounded coordinate multiplication and differentiation on ordinary \(L^2\)?

## Claim boundary

Yes with the Fourier-invariant harmonic-oscillator graph norm and the induced quotient norm on the odd image. This constructs a source-generated Hilbert completion; it does not identify it with an independently prescribed odd Sonin \(L^2\) subspace norm.

## Fourier-balanced graph norm

Set

$$
Xf(x)=xf(x),
\qquad
Zf(x)=\frac1{2\pi}f'(x).
$$

For the declared Fourier convention,

$$
\mathcal F X=iZ\mathcal F,
\qquad
\mathcal F Z=iX\mathcal F.
$$

On the even Sonin test core \(E_\lambda\), define

$$
\|f\|_{\rm osc}^2
=
\|f\|_2^2+
\|Xf\|_2^2+
\|Zf\|_2^2.
$$

The exchange formulas and Fourier unitarity imply

$$
\|\mathcal Ff\|_{\rm osc}=\|f\|_{\rm osc}.
$$

Let \(E_\lambda^{\rm osc}\) be the completion. Fourier extends unitarily to it.

## Odd quotient completion

Define

$$
L:E_\lambda^{\rm osc}\oplus E_\lambda^{\rm osc}
\longrightarrow L^2(\mathbb R)_{\rm odd},
\qquad
L(f,g)=Xf+Zg.
$$

This map is bounded in the oscillator graph norm. Give its image the quotient Hilbert norm

$$
\|h\|_{O,\rm osc}
=
\inf_{L(f,g)=h}
\left(
\|f\|_{\rm osc}^2+
\|g\|_{\rm osc}^2
\right)^{1/2},
$$

and complete after quotienting by \(\ker L\). Denote the resulting Hilbert space by \(O_\lambda^{\rm osc}\).

## Fourier action on the quotient

For \(h=L(f,g)\),

$$
\mathcal Fh
=iZFf+iXFg
=L(i\mathcal Fg,i\mathcal Ff).
$$

The map

$$
(f,g)\longmapsto(i\mathcal Fg,i\mathcal Ff)
$$

is unitary on the source direct sum and preserves \(\ker L\). Therefore it descends to a unitary Fourier action on \(O_\lambda^{\rm osc}\).

Since this space is odd,

$$
\mathcal F^2=-I
$$

and its character spectrum is \(\{i,-i\}\).

## Parity-complete Hilbert carrier

The direct sum

$$
\mathcal H_\lambda^{\rm full}
=
E_\lambda^{\rm osc}
\oplus
O_\lambda^{\rm osc}
$$

is Hilbertian and carries a unitary order-four Fourier action with even characters \(\pm1\) and odd characters \(\pm i\). Tensoring with the finite-prime Fourier-invariant Sonin seeds preserves unitarity.

## Norm boundary

The quotient norm is source-generated by the oscillator graph. It may be stronger than, or inequivalent to, the ambient \(L^2\) norm on the odd image. No claim is made that the primary-source even Sonin \(L^2\) isometry extends unchanged without this graph augmentation.

## Disposition

The odd/sign-character Sonin chart has a Fourier-unitary Hilbert completion in the oscillator quotient norm. Together with the even oscillator completion, it gives a parity-complete semilocal Hilbert carrier supporting all four Fourier characters. Comparison with any independently prescribed unweighted Sonin norm remains separate.
