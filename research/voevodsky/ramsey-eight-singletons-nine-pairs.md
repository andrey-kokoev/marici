# Eight singleton cells and at least nine pairs force four-label saturation

At a root of a legal 35-vertex (3,9) coloring, give each of its 26 blue neighbors the mask of its red neighbors among the eight root labels. If all eight singletons occur and at least nine cells have mask size two, some four labels have exactly nine common blue core cells. The four newcomers plus those nine core cells induce the unique 13-vertex (3,5) graph. This is a sufficient condition for a saturated region; it neither forces cube incidence nor asserts that every root has eight singletons.

The >=12, exact-11 and exact-10 cases were settled earlier. For exact nine the pair masks form a simple triangle-free graph: a repeated pair would put four core cells in a two-label region and a triangle puts six in a three-label region, both exceeding predecessor order bounds. The independent enumeration counts 1,163,540 labeled graphs and the symmetry enumeration has 76 types. Nine remaining cells must carry 30 labels. All have sizes 3..5 (at size six the common blue neighborhood of root and cell would exceed the (3,7) maximum). Hence mark six triples; after that, the last three masks have sizes 4,4,4 or 3,4,5. The sorted, symmetry-reduced search need only preserve at least one marked choice from any actual witness.

The last 18 pair graphs had one degree-one label. Six-triple candidates were screened by assuming each candidate cell in turn on top of a common fixed prefix, rebuilding all core edges. New label memberships are variables; future row quotas and the fact that red-adjacent vertices must have disjoint sets of future red neighbors supply additional lower bounds on the internal degrees. A five-triple family must have a sixth triple that passes every previously admitted four-triple test and leaves all eight column sums fillable by three masks. This filter imposes no requirement that the sixth is later than the fifth in a given ordering. At the terminal step, all exact three-mask fillings are enumerated and rejected if even one member is infeasible on its own. For the 874 surviving six-triple assignments, none of 339,846 marked fillings passed even the repeated weaker terminal test (with the future-neighbor cuts disabled). Counts are of marked branches, not distinct 35-vertex graphs.

For eight singletons the only open profiles are now six pairs + twelve triples; seven pairs + ten triples + one quartet; eight pairs + eight triples + two quartets; or eight pairs + nine triples + one quintuple. These follow from 26 cells, 56 total incidences, and the size-five bound. In particular there are at most two nontriple cells left to assign after the singles and pairs, apart from the triple cells themselves. A complementary search can fix the exceptional size-four or size-five masks first and solve an exact three-uniform incidence completion.
