# All-even scalar-probe DPC audit

## Conjecture

There is a source-natural compatible scalar probe that is nonzero on one pure-`x²` grade-eight representative at every even ambient index.

## Attempted constructions

### Fixed quotient coordinate

A fixed column-coordinate functional cannot work. Every pure-`x²` transport increases the representative's `x` exponent by two. For any fixed exponent-labelled column, sufficiently many shifts move the entire transported support beyond that label, so the fixed coordinate pairs to zero. This explicitly falsifies fixed-coordinate persistence; it does not falsify a moving probe.

### Canonical row-echelon pivot

At each tested ambient index a nonzero residual supplies a pivot-coordinate probe. The quotient basis and pivot label depend on the ambient packet. No contravariant compatibility relation between these probes has been constructed, so finite pivot nonvanishing does not establish an all-even probe.

### Global transport injectivity

This route is falsified by the previously constructed parity-pure global transport kernels. Their existence does not kill the distinguished line but prevents global injectivity from certifying it.

## DPC disposition

The all-even scalar-probe conjecture is not established. Two candidate constructions are falsified: fixed coordinates by unbounded exponent shift, and global injectivity by exact kernels. Ambient-specific echelon probes remain finite corroboration only.

The surviving alternative is a moving dual family `ell_A` satisfying

\[
ell_{A+2}\circ x^2=\ell_A
\]

on the distinguished source span, while each `ell_A` annihilates the ambient relation image. This is a typed adjoint recurrence, not a fixed coordinate.

Solve the exact adjoint compatibility equations for A14, A16, A18, and A20. If they have no solution under the source-labelled support restriction, that restriction is falsified. If they do, identify the support-shift rule needed for an induction.

No DNC, geometric specialization, or physical readout is asserted.
