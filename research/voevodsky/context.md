# Voevodsky audit: a site, transfers, and the eight-point excess problem

Date: 2026-08-14

Status: conditional program, not a theorem. The ledger supports a clean three-level
reformulation, but it does not yet support the claim that the scalar master carries a
complete, regular, bounded cd-structure or that the Möbius carrier is a hypercover.

## 1. Three categories that must not be conflated

### Base scalar-strata category

For each arity and marked surface, let \(\mathcal C_{\rm sc}\) be generated by actual scalar
strata:

- closed support strata \(X_{\geq P,D}\) specified by a compatible physical core \(P\), a
  common scalar dissection \(D\), and any mark or polarity datum that is constant on the whole
  stratum;
- the product-associahedral faces \(F(D)\cong\prod_a K_{r_a}\) of entry 31;
- the empty stratum, finite disjoint unions, regional products, and deck/dihedral isomorphisms;
- actual face inclusions, core-specialization inclusions, and their regional product maps.

Use “at least \(P\)” closed support strata when forming pullbacks. Exact-core pieces are locally
closed pieces and belong naturally to a constructible stratification; treating two disjoint
exact-core pieces as though their coefficient overlap were a geometric intersection is exactly
the error ruled out by entries 76 and 83.

The category must be saturated under existing fiber products before a Grothendieck topology is
declared. Occurrence modules, Laurent weights, Gysin maps, Pochhammer factors, and route-to-belt
maps are not morphisms of \(\mathcal C_{\rm sc}\).

### Scalar correspondence category

Let \(\operatorname{Corr}_{\rm sc}\) have the same objects and be generated by:

1. graphs of maps in \(\mathcal C_{\rm sc}\);
2. normally oriented physical Cut/Gysin correspondences, including their ordered normal lines;
3. the weighted occurrence kernels and extension-by-zero maps;
4. derived route-to-belt kernels such as \(q_Q\), and eventually the primitive road counits
   \(\pi_D^{\rm PC}\).

Composition is by derived fiber product/tensor product. Transverse composition must recover the
strict Cut identities of entries 32 and 37. Nontransverse composition retains its Tor/Koszul or
determinant complex; it is not replaced by an invented rank-one excess line. Associativity and
base-change for these derived kernels remain obligations in the definition of
\(\operatorname{Corr}_{\rm sc}\), not consequences of the carrier topology.

### Stable target

For a topology \(\tau\) on \(\mathcal C_{\rm sc}\) and a coefficient ring \(R\), the minimally
typed stable target is

\[
\mathcal D^{\rm tr}_{\rm sc}(R)
=
\left\{
F\in\operatorname{Fun}_{\rm add}
(\operatorname{Corr}_{\rm sc}^{\rm op},\operatorname{Mod}_R)
\;\middle|\;
F|_{\mathcal C_{\rm sc}}\text{ is a }\tau\text{-hypercomplete sheaf}
\right\}.
\]

First take scalar support descent over the polynomial coefficient ring. Then apply the facewise
nonresonant Pochhammer/Cousin realization and its monodromy localization. The scalar
\(X\)-weights and the \((q_D-1)\)-normal factors remain separate. This preserves the established
order

\[
\operatorname{Poch}_{\alpha'}\circ\operatorname{gr}_R
\]

and entry 82's target-first construction. Calling this category a category of “motives” would
require additional interval invariance and localization theorems that are not in the ledger.

## 2. Candidate transverse cd-squares

The following strict squares are candidates for a cd-structure \(P_{\rm tr}\):

| square family | base geometry | coefficient evidence | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| two compatible physical Cuts | intersection of closed core strata, \(X_{\ge P\cup\{e,f\}}=X_{\ge P\cup\{e\}}\times_{X_{\ge P}}X_{\ge P\cup\{f\}}\) | \(G_eG_f=G_fG_e\), entry 32 | candidate |
| refinements in independent associahedral factors | literal product-face pullback in \(\prod_a K_{r_a}\) | cellular product coherence, entry 31 | candidate |
| one scalar refinement and one independent physical Cut | literal mixed product face | strict supported/zero Beck--Chevalley and two-slot lift, entry 37 | strongest candidate |

Entry 48 proves strict Cut monoidality and conservativity on canonical surface functions. That is
strong evidence for transfer functoriality, but it is not by itself a covering or cd-structure
theorem.

No complete/regular/bounded cd-structure is presently proved:

- **Complete:** it is not shown that every covering sieve generated by these squares contains a
  simple cover, or that the selected square family is closed under every base change in
  \(\mathcal C_{\rm sc}\).
- **Regular:** the candidate face squares are pullbacks with monomorphic face legs, but the
  required diagonal/self-intersection condition has not been audited. More basically, a pair of
  boundary faces does not automatically cover its ambient scalar face; the intended excision
  coverage must be stated rather than inferred.
- **Bounded:** at fixed arity, cell dimension/unresolved-block rank supplies a finite candidate
  density structure. One must still prove that every distinguished square is reducing. The
  all-arity union being unbounded in absolute dimension is not fatal if boundedness is proved
  objectwise.

Thus \(P_{\rm tr}\) is a concrete Cartesian/excision skeleton, not yet a basis for the natural
scalar topology. The exact audit below shows that its proper face pairs do not cover ambient
triangulation support; a coverage must either be deliberately non-spatial or include the
interior/contact sector.

## 3. The dependent pentagon/square is not cdh data

At eight points the dependent route pentagon \(P\), companion square \(S\), and fixed-core cube
\(K_Q\) do not form an ordinary scalar pullback diagram:

\[
P\cap K_Q=\{111\},\qquad S\cap K_Q=\{000\},
\]

the route vertex sets are otherwise disjoint, and no associahedral three-face contains both.
Their nonzero overlap is in the coefficient fiber, not in scalar-stratum geometry (entry 76).

Consequently this is not currently an abstract blowup/cdh square. A cdh interpretation would
require an actual combinatorial modification \(\widetilde X\to X\), a closed center, and an
isomorphism away from that center. None is typed. A proposed Rees/core-rank contraction may
become such a modification only after its universal property and complement equivalence are
proved; the Boolean grading that selects one strict pentagon collapse is additional data.

The existing object is instead transfer-level:

\[
q_Q:\mathcal C_Q^{\rm route}\longrightarrow B_Q^{\rm w},
\qquad
\mathsf J_Q^{\rm PC}=\chi_Q^{\rm PC}j_Qq_Q.
\]

If a route-first comparison is desired, its failure of transversality should be encoded by an
exact excess/determinant triangle in \(\mathcal D^{\rm tr}_{\rm sc}\). Such “derived-excess
distinguished squares” are not classical Voevodsky distinguished squares and should not be used
to generate the base topology. Entry 82 shows that the local half-symbol itself needs no
route-first excess square: descend first, then load once on the transverse cube.

## 4. Möbius carrier: only a candidate truncated hypercover

The \((12,24,12)\) Möbius carrier organizes regional cubes, shared rank-one roads, eight
triangles, and four squares. It is presently a two-dimensional correspondence indexing complex.
It is not a Čech nerve of the exact-core cubes: adjacent exact-core cubes have empty geometric
intersection and meet only through noninvertible residue/Gysin cospans (entries 83 and 88).

To call it a 2-truncated hypercover one must first supply:

1. an augmented object \(U_0\to X\) with an effective-cover property;
2. actual matching maps \(U_1\to M_1(U)\) and \(U_2\to M_2(U)\);
3. effective epimorphism/descent conditions for those matching maps;
4. a coskeletal continuation or a proof that higher matching conditions are automatic.

The missing occurrence-resolved primitive road quotient \(\pi_D^{\rm PC}\) is part of this gap.
Until it exists, the three-road channel object cannot be replaced by a line and the Möbius
carrier remains a candidate truncated correspondence hypercover, not a hypercover on
\(\mathcal C_{\rm sc}\).

## 5. Correct primitive-line typing

For

\[
P_D=\mathbb Z\langle q_{D,E_0},q_{D,E_1},q_{D,E_2}\rangle,
\qquad A_2=\ker(\varepsilon_D),
\]

the saturated sequence is

\[
0\to A_2\to P_D\xrightarrow{\varepsilon_D}\mathbf 1\to0.
\]

Hence the stable typing is the exact triangle

\[
\boxed{A_2\longrightarrow P_D\longrightarrow\mathbf1\longrightarrow A_2[1].}
\]

The primitive line is \(\operatorname{cofib}(A_2\to P_D)\simeq\mathbf1\). The two-term complex
\([P_D\to\mathbf1]\) instead models the kernel/fiber with a convention-dependent shift; it is
not the primitive quotient line.

## 7. Exact \(n=8\) base-site audit

`check_n8_scalar_cd_site.rs` implements the closed face category

\[
X_S=\{T:S\subseteq T\}
\]

for every noncrossing octagon dissection \(S\), plus the empty object. It finds 903 nonempty
faces with associahedral face vector

\[
(f_0,f_1,f_2,f_3,f_4,f_5)=(132,330,300,120,20,1).
\]

All 408,156 unordered face intersections agree exactly with union of compatible constraints or
the empty object. The three transverse families contain 2,008 undeformed squares:

\[
324\ \text{physical/physical},\qquad
1012\ \text{independent scalar/scalar},\qquad
672\ \text{independent scalar/physical}.
\]

Saturation along every face inclusion performs 91,488 base-change checks and leaves 17,964
distinct supported square classes. All are pullbacks with monomorphic legs. The well-typed
monic self-intersection identities pass 365,952 checks. Of the base changes, 6,800 remain
nondegenerate, 20,400 become degenerate, and 64,288 acquire an empty leg.

This proves a finite **Cartesian/excision skeleton**, not a cd-topology. The natural cellular
coverage fails sharply: none of the 2,008 proper face pairs covers its ambient triangulation
support. More decisively, the union of all eight physical Cut faces sees only 128 of the 132
octagon triangulations. The four omitted vertices are exactly the zero-core/contact
triangulations, so restriction of the free occurrence module to all Cuts has a rank-four
kernel.

There is a useful distinction. In the abstract face poset the two axes have categorical join
equal to the ambient face, and all 1,813,224 representable square-descent tests pass. Thus one
could *declare* a non-spatial subcanonical cd-topology on the incidence poset. But the physical
occurrence/coefficient object is not separated for that coverage: Cut restriction forgets the
contact kernel. Sheafifying it would erase information that the scalar master retains.

The naive statement

\[
\text{transverse Cut squares alone define the scalar descent site}
\]

is therefore falsified. The smallest corrected alternatives are:

1. enlarge \(\mathcal C_{\rm sc}\) by a locally closed interior/contact object and use a
   recollement/Cousin triangle between it and the Cut boundary; or
2. localize only after supplying ultraviolet/contact boundary data that kills the kernel.

Entry 48's conservativity theorem has exactly this form: Cuts alone are not conservative, while
Cuts plus ultraviolet boundary data are. No simple-cover or boundedness verdict is available
until one of these cover/recollement choices and an explicit density structure \(D_i\) are
declared. The raw codimension profile \((d,d-1,d-1,d-2)\) passes for every undeformed square,
but that profile is not a Voevodsky density structure by itself.

The same checker confirms that the dependent route pentagon and square are disjoint, meet the
fixed-core cube at one opposite vertex each, and remain transfer data rather than base cdh
squares.

## 12. Tate shadow of the augmented triangle

Entry 102 identifies the constant incidence resolution exactly. With
\(C_3=\langle r\rangle\), road basis \((1,r,r^2)\), the shifted tag basis of
entry 94, and the orientation-twisted reflection action, it is

\[
0\to\mathbb Z_{\rm or}\xrightarrow{1+r+r^2}P_{\rm tag}
\xrightarrow{1-r}P_{\rm road}\xrightarrow{\epsilon}\mathbb Z\to0.
\]

It is therefore a finite window of the complete \(C_3\)-Tate resolution and
represents the generator

\[
\beta_\triangle\in
\operatorname{Ext}^2_{\mathbb Z[D_3]}
(\mathbb Z,\mathbb Z_{\rm or})\cong\mathbb Z/3.
\]

The orientation twist is essential: reflection negates both the cyclic
degree-two generator and the coefficient line, so the class survives as a
\(D_3\)-invariant. This explains the Smith index three, the failure of an

Tate theory diagnoses the cyclic nonsplitting; it does not construct the

## 14. Formal peripheral roof and the purity boundary

facewise support--PC object has actually been constructed on the strict
filtration

\[
F_0=PC_{\rm supp}(v_+)
\subset F_1=PC_{\rm supp}(B_{\rm short})

\[
R\xleftarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Cone}(P\to E)
