## What is fixed here, and what is not

This candidate freezes a quantifier schema, admissible source-data distinctions, representation obligations and finite/infinite resource semantics. It does NOT select a foundation, physical primitive account or final mathematical scope. Those are real open selection gates: calling this schema a completed formulation would conceal exactly the choices the parent acceptance asks to freeze.

The bounded mathematical assembly is now constructed at its policy-indexed scope. Its separate Agda and analytic validity policies do not choose the universal parent's foundation, and its six-state Boolean test cannot define the universe of valid mathematics. ## Source rules versus answer insertion

Per-construction syntax, initial data, hypotheses and declared domain laws are admissible mathematical inputs. They must be marked as supplied. The common primitive dictionary cannot quietly acquire a fresh output primitive for each requested answer. Native-generation claims require actual primitive derivations and preservation witnesses.

The finite WG adapter is therefore legitimate as an adapter, not a proof that P generated its supplied category laws. An RH unit seed is a declared input, not a theorem that every source state has normalized xi output. Neither pilot establishes physical realization.

The executable control uses the actual Boolean complement operation twice. It accepts the complete false→true→false and true→false→true traces. It rejects an answer seed without computation, an endpoint-only trace, and the endpoint-correct false→false→false trace. The last rejection matters: correct final values cannot hide a failed intermediate operation square. This is a regression test for the proposed obligation, not a universal detector of concealed answer fitting.

## Identity and resource semantics

Complete source identity, definitional/propositional identity, equivalence and task observation are not synonyms. For a task quotient, equal observations after all licensed continuations AND matching availability guards are required. Preservation/reflection claims must name their identity kind; unrestricted substitution is not inferred from a coarse task quotient.

Each context supplies an ordered resource algebra. Its unit and sequential composition, together with monotone sequencing-compatible cost translation, must be given before claiming execution. Natural-number addition is a bounded test instance, not a universal physical cost model. Two complement steps need budget two in the fixture; a single-step budget is rejected. Two states with the same current observation but budgets one and zero have different continuation availability.

A mathematical source model, positive form or cost estimate is not physical calibration. The six physical-premise slots in the frozen common-witness interface remain independent evidence obligations. No lowest-level source has been selected here.

## Infinite representation is not infinite execution

Distinguish symbolic specification, compatible approximants with an error/topology policy, and semantic completion. Every mode requires its own operation/limit/continuation witnesses. A symbolic name alone does not supply operations on the named object; semantic existence alone supplies no effective approximator.

A family of finite tasks with increasing budgets does not execute infinitely many tasks in finite time. Nor can a finite tested prefix establish all-index or limit behavior: the checker compares a zero sequence with one that changes after the four tested entries. This does not discredit valid completion theorems; it excludes replacing them by finite agreement.

If K includes noncomputable or non-finitely-presented objects, they cannot be removed after a candidate process fails. Whether and how such objects belong to the intended universal scope is an explicit outstanding selection question, not silently answered by available pilots.
