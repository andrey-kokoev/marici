# Normal-form postweakening composes; proof histories do not

For the fixed source `[0,1]` and target rows `x<=T`, T>=1, let P_T be nonnegative Farkas proofs `(a,b,c)` with b-a=1 and b+c=T. The source-rooted normalization `N_T(a,b,c)=(a+c,b+c,0)` is idempotent and has the unique displayed normal form `(T-1,T,0)`. Positive target weakening W_TU adds U-T to the surplus. The restricted normal-form transition is `N_U W_TU`. It composes on normalized proof OBJECTS: `N_V W_UV N_U W_TU = N_V W_TV` for T<=U<=V. Independent exact-rational `verify_farkas_comparison_pasting.py` checks 35 two-step triples, including the concrete staged proof `(1,2,1)` under 3->4->5; changed primitive bound is refused.

This recovers a useful quotient-level structure without retracting the earlier counterexample. Bare local proof selection followed by ordinary W is not functorial; explicitly renormalizing after EACH W gives the same final normal-form proof. The direct and staged derivation strings remain different (and were recorded separately by the verifier). Equality of final certificates is NOT a 3-cell identifying those proof-path histories, nor a new source/operational authority. The original Farkas category has no declared 2- or 3-cell types; installing them would require supported rewrite steps and higher coherence, not a syntax comparison.

The deepest live question is now sharper: should the continuation contract promise only normalized endpoint certificates, or also compare retained proof histories? The former is available on this restricted source-rooted family. The latter still needs a genuine typed higher cell and a source-rooted proof DAG for that cell. Extending normalization across source edits, fine audits, provider revocation or analytic S,A,R,C,G roles is unsupported.
