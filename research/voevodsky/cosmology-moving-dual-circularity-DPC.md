# Moving-dual adjoint compatibility: DPC disposition

## Proposed construction

For the source-labelled transported class `c_A`, seek a dual family satisfying

\[
\ell_A(c_A)=1,
\qquad
\ell_{A+2}\circ x^2=\ell_A
\]

on the distinguished line.

## Exact classification

This construction is equivalent to nonvanishing of the transported line; it cannot prove nonvanishing independently.

- If `c_A` is nonzero for every even `A`, define `ell_A` on the one-dimensional span of `c_A` by `ell_A(c_A)=1`. Since `x²c_A=c_A+2` as source-labelled representatives, compatibility follows. Extension from that line to the full finite quotient dual exists but is nonunique.
- Conversely, any functional with `ell_A(c_A)=1` implies `c_A` is nonzero.

Thus solving unrestricted finite adjoint equations merely restates the desired persistence. The exact A14–A20 solutions are consequences of already verified nonvanishing and add no all-even evidence.

## DPC disposition

The unrestricted moving-dual proposal is rejected as circular evidence, not falsified as an algebraic construction. Its existence is conditional on the claim it was meant to establish.

The surviving noncircular alternative must define `ell_A` before quotient nonvanishing is known—for example by a source formula with:

1. declared support independent of row-echelon pivots;
2. direct annihilation of every relation generator;
3. exact value one on the transported raw target;
4. a proved shift rule in `A`.

A failure of any one identity is an explicit falsifier for that proposed source formula. Absence of such a formula means all-even nonvanishing remains not established.
