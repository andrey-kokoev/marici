In the FREE category on the proposed normalization and redistribution 2-cell generators, `R_(1/2);N_B` and `N_A;R_(3/2)` are distinct parallel paths. Thus the FIRST absent obligation for the mixed square is a typed **3-cell** with exactly this boundary. It is separate from the previously found missing **4-cell** between two paths of discrete trace 3-cells.

Fresh `check_mixed_cell_path_boundary.py` independently replays both intermediates and the common tip. Naming a new `mixed_interchange_3cell` gives only a candidate generator requiring independent source derivation/admission and coherence under repeated redistribution. Source-rooted support for edges is not automatically source-rooted proof of the higher filler.
