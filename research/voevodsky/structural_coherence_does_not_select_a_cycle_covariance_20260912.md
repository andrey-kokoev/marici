# Structural coherence does not select a cycle covariance

## Question

Do positivity, reciprocal invariance, cutoff compatibility, and forest-coordinate congruence uniquely determine a metric on the cycle residue?

## Claim boundary

No. The actual arithmetic interval graph admits many inequivalent positive edge forms satisfying all four structural requirements. Their restrictions to cycle space are positive and coherent but are not proportional. Therefore these requirements cannot promote one of them to physical covariance.

## Two coherent forms

On the universal labelled edge graph, consider

\[
W^{(0)}_e=1
\]

and

\[
W^{(1)}_e=kp_jp_{j+1}=e^{\overline W(e)}.
\]

Both are strictly positive. Both depend only on intrinsic edge labels, so they are unchanged by reciprocal exchange of ratio blocks. Under cutoff inclusion by zero extension, each finite edge form is the restriction of the same global diagonal form.

For a forest presentation \(F_T\) of cycle space, define

\[
Q_T^{(r)}=F_T^*W^{(r)}F_T,
\qquad r=0,1.
\]

Each form is positive definite on cycle coordinates because \(F_T\) is injective and \(W^{(r)}\) is positive on edge chains. Forest changes satisfy

\[
Q_T^{(r)}
=C_{U\leftarrow T}^*Q_U^{(r)}C_{U\leftarrow T}.
\]

Thus both candidates satisfy positivity, cutoff compatibility, reciprocal invariance, and presentation independence.

## Nonuniqueness

The two restrictions are not generally scalar multiples. Cycles supported on edges with different arithmetic grades receive different relative lengths under \(W^{(1)}\), while \(W^{(0)}\) counts unweighted edge coefficients. A finite actual-cutoff census exhibits nonproportional Gram matrices on a cycle space of dimension greater than one.

More generally, every positive reciprocal-invariant function \(f(\overline W(e))\) defines another coherent diagonal edge form, subject to whichever continuity bound is separately imposed. Structural coherence therefore leaves an infinite family.

## Consequence

A physical cycle covariance requires a new source arrow, not another coherence diagram. Acceptable evidence would have to specify a measurement, noise law, action, Green operator, or state-effect pairing whose pullback to the cycle sector produces the form. Positivity and symmetry can reject candidates but cannot select one.

The unweighted form remains available as a mathematical auxiliary metric at finite cutoffs. The arithmetic-weighted form remains available as a topology-compatible auxiliary. Neither is called physical.

## Disposition

The covariance branch reaches an authority blocker: no independently sourced cycle measurement or positive operator has been identified. The exact residual is underdetermination by at least two nonproportional coherent forms. Reopen only when a source-derived readout or covariance is supplied; do not continue selecting weights from symmetry alone.
