# Torsion lift degree correction

## Correction

The two pairs `(1,1)` and `(1,0)` are bookkeeping choices for split tame representatives, not two relative cohomology lifts in one degree. Their difference was identified with `H*(-1)`, but that class lies in `CH^2(P2,1)=H_M^3(P2,Z(2))`, whereas `{u,v}` lies in motivic degree two.

The valid statement is that exact tame localization of `{u,v}` fixes the full unit tuple `(v^-1,u,-v/u)`. Removing its constant sign while leaving the nonconstant units unchanged is impossible without a compensating Gersten class. It is not valid to describe this operation as adding a same-degree closed torsion lift.

## Disposition

The former two-lift claim is withdrawn. Integral provenance is carried by the unsplit K1 unit tuple. The graded-total-complex audit is the next gate.
