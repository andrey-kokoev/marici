# Route-residue metaphysical premise correction

## Defect

The research path treated the proposition that a hidden compositional difference can be distinguished by a compatible faithful probe as though it were itself an open physical question. Once compatibility and faithfulness are assumed, distinguishability is a categorical consequence. The substantive open questions concern existence, source authority, construction, calibration, and reach of the probe.

The inherited word `history` also invited temporal interpretation. In this line it denotes a coarse composition readout. No physical-time map is present.

## Corrected formulation

Let \(C:X\to Y\) be a coarse composition readout and let \(x_1,x_2\in X\) satisfy

\[
C(x_1)=C(x_2).
\]

For a probe context \(P\), distinguish four typed cases:

1. both compositions exist and their records differ;
2. only the composition with \(x_1\) exists;
3. only the composition with \(x_2\) exists;
4. both compositions exist and their records agree.

An empty composition space yields no record through that probe. A physical-experience claim additionally requires a declared positive state--effect pairing and record map.

A `maximal differing stratum` replaces `last rank`. It exists only under a declared grading or partial order with the required maximal element.

## Downstream audit

### Mathematical route-residue results

The kernel, cycle, projective-coordinate, countable generating-family, and finite-rank results remain valid. They concern factorization and separation of typed linear maps. Their proofs do not require physical time or the mistaken metaphysical premise.

### Optical and RF experiment

The experiment cannot test whether compatible faithful probes distinguish; that implication is structural. It can test whether the purchased apparatus realizes the declared modulation and detector maps, whether those maps compose with the encoded inputs, and whether their finite sensitivity exceeds calibrated error.

The apparatus therefore has an engineering and source-identification role. It supplies no metaphysical decision between objects once compatibility and faithfulness are granted.

### Natural arithmetic coupling

The sealed-label benchmark remains relevant after retyping. Its target is source authority for the shell generator and prime-edge incidence. It asks whether arithmetic labels are independently derived or retrospectively fitted. It does not ask whether an already faithful probe distinguishes.

### Cutoff study

The exact ranks and singular margins remain valid in their declared coordinate norm. They quantify reach of a constructed probe. They do not quantify existence, experience, or naturality.

### Covariance recommendation

The requirement to measure modulation, detector normalization, loss, and joint noise remains necessary before calling the response form a physical covariance. Structural distinguishability alone supplies no metric or noise law.

### Procurement

The hardware recommendations remain coherent only under the bounded objective of constructing and calibrating an engineered RF probe and establishing a reusable electronics/RF bench. The purchases were unnecessary for settling the corrected metaphysical implication. Any justification that represented them as deciding that implication is withdrawn.

## Revised research question

The operative question is:

> Which source-derived probe contexts exist, which objects admit composition with them, and which compositional differences survive through a calibrated physical record map?

For the current apparatus, the answer will be finite, engineered, and conditional on the declared RF encoding. A source-natural answer still requires an independently measurable generator \(K\) and prime-edge incidence.

## Disposition

The incorrect metaphysical framing is withdrawn. Mathematical separation results survive. Experimental work is retained as a construction-and-calibration programme with narrower evidential scope. Future descriptions use `coarse composition readout` and reserve temporal language for a separately declared physical-time map.
