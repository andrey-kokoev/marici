# Speculative relative-boundary enlargement for Aspect descent

## Question

Can the rank-two ambient descent obstruction be interpreted and repaired using structures explicitly present in the four newly indexed sources?

## Claim boundary

The matrix repair below is exact. Its interpretation by relative-boundary sectors is a source-motivated conjecture, not an identification of the Marici conductor walls with the surfaces in the cited examples. No physical contour or period is constructed.

## Source mining

### Relative twisted cohomology

`Cosmology meets cohomology`, PDF pages 16, 18, 20, and 21, supplies four relevant facts.

1. The dual complex is relative twisted cohomology of a complement relative to marked surfaces.
2. A relative form is a direct sum of bulk and successively deeper boundary components, encoded by antisymmetric coboundary symbols \(\delta_J\).
3. Its differential contains both the differential intrinsic to a boundary and signed restrictions to deeper boundaries; equations (3.29)–(3.30) are an explicit Stokes totalization.
4. In the worked two-variable example, equations (3.39)–(3.40) force two codimension-two boundary components from restrictions of one boundary integration-by-parts form. The resulting connection is upper triangular, and the full-rank intersection matrix compares dual and canonical-form bases.

This supplies a typed mechanism absent from the two-leg target: independent support components can live in deeper boundary summands instead of being forced into the rank-one bulk residue image.

### Cayley–Menger Gauss–Manin system

`Hypergeometric integrals associated with hypersphere arrangements and Cayley-Menger determinants`, PDF pages 46–49, constructs the Gauss–Manin connection on an admissible twisted-cohomology basis. Theorem 5 states that its matrix entries are rational one-forms in the geometric parameters, with poles on principal Cayley–Menger determinant loci. Equations (72)–(75) give a recursive restriction/contiguity construction.

This is evidence for how a future weighted specialization should be computed: specialize a source-labelled admissible basis and its connection matrix, not only the determinant polynomial.

### Cosmological polytope residues and triangulations

`Cosmological Polytopes and the Wavefunction of the Universe`, PDF pages 43–44, identifies the total-energy residue with the canonical form of a simplex and explains that canonical forms add under triangulation while spurious poles cancel only in the sum.

`Triangulations of cosmological polytopes`, PDF pages 3 and 10, constructs regular unimodular triangulations from squarefree initial ideals and describes their facets by excluded leading monomials. This provides a combinatorial candidate for a source-derived restriction: select a facet/subdivision subcomplex, then prove that omitted incidence directions are precisely internal or spurious boundaries.

Neither source identifies that candidate with the present four-point conductor lattice.

## Exact minimal enlargement

The current target differential has image generated by

\[
r=(1,-1,1)^T.

\Phi=JK=

\[

\[

-1&1&1&-1\\

\[

Then

\[
D_{\rm ext}P=\Phi.

\[

\begin{pmatrix}

Thus the enlargement preserves the original Aspect assignment exactly while absorbing both ambient obstruction directions into two additional support generators.

The lattice generated by \(r,q_1,q_2\) is the equal-parity lattice \(\operatorname{im}J\), of index four in \(\mathbb Z^3\). Therefore the two new generators add exactly the two rational directions detected by the cokernel probe without pretending to remove the inherited \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\) saturation defect.

## Bold conjecture

The two added generators are the algebraic shadows of two codimension-two relative-boundary components in a source-derived Stokes totalization. A weighted Cayley–Menger specialization should produce them as signed restrictions of a one-boundary integration-by-parts form, analogous to equations (3.39)–(3.40), while a compatible unimodular triangulation should identify their ambient incidence columns as internal/spurious boundaries that cancel in the canonical-form sum.

## Named rivals

1. The obstruction sectors are bulk classes rather than boundary-supported classes; then the target requires a larger bulk cohomology, not a relative totalization.
2. A source-derived quotient kills the two directions, so no target enlargement is needed.
3. The equality of ranks is accidental; the physical support arrangement yields a different number or type of boundary components.
4. The Cayley–Menger hypersphere arrangement is not compatible with the cosmological marked complement, so its Gauss–Manin basis cannot be specialized to this problem.

## Risky consequences and falsifiers

The conjecture requires all of the following.

- Two labelled support strata must be derived from the actual marked arrangement.
- Their coboundary signs and restrictions must reproduce \(q_1,q_2\) after the conductor comparison.
- The total differential must square to zero.
- A specialized Cayley–Menger connection must preserve the four-generator enlarged target and be integrable.
- The intersection matrix must be full rank on the intended quotient.
- A polytope subdivision must identify the same two directions as internal/spurious, not merely have the same rank.

Failure of the first item stops the branch at an object-assignment blocker. Equal matrix dimensions or formal factorization do not substitute for these tests.

## Computed result

The extended differential has rank three, the old differential has rank one, and the prior cokernel obstruction has rank two. The displayed \(P\) factors the full ambient incidence comparison exactly and preserves the selected two-leg assignment. The lattice generated by \(r,q_1,q_2\) and the audited conductor image both have index four.

## Disposition

The enlargement is an exact minimal rank-two algebraic completion of the Aspect target. The four sources motivate, but do not establish, its interpretation as two codimension-two relative-boundary sectors. Source support assignment and physical interpretation remain unverified.
