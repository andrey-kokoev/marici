\[
K_R(x,y)
=
\frac{\sin(R(x-y))}{\pi(x-y)}.
\]

Its first \(190\) eigenvectors define the approximate projection \(P\). A second Gauss--Legendre rule in frequency forms the corrected multiplier matrix with

\[
m(u)
=
\frac{
\operatorname{Re}\psi(1/4+iu/2)-\log\pi
}{2}
-
\frac{\log2}{\sqrt2}
\cos(u\log2).
\]

The endpoint term is represented by the symmetric rank-two matrix generated by the weighted vectors \(e^{x/2}\) and \(e^{-x/2}\). The scout then computes

\[
G_{190}
=
PAP
-40\left(PA^2P-(PAP)^2\right).
\]

Two quadrature resolutions were preregistered to expose truncation instability. Even agreement between them would be diagnostic rather than interval certification.
