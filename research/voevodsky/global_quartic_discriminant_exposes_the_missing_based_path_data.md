# The global quartic discriminant exposes the missing based-path data

## Question

Can the explicit infinity quartic determine global vanishing paths that order the paired total-energy thimbles?

## Global discriminant

Write \(u=t^2\). The quartic

\[
F(t)=x^2t^4-igl(x^2+y^2-(E-x-y)^2\bigr)t^2+y^2
\]

becomes a quadratic in \(u\). Its discriminant is

\[
\operatorname{disc}_u F
=-E(2x-E)(2y-E)(2x+2y-E).
\]

The full quartic discriminant is

\[
\operatorname{disc}_t F
=16E^2x^2y^2(2x-E)^2(2y-E)^2(2x+2y-E)^2.
\]

Away from the soft loci \(xy=0\), the four finite critical values in the \(E\)-plane are

\[
0,
\qquad2x,
\qquad2y,
\qquad2(x+y).
\]

Thus the total-energy node pair at \(E=0\) is part of a global four-point discriminant arrangement. A based thimble marking requires paths from a declared regular base value to the critical values, with a homotopy class in the complement of the other three points.

## Chamber dependence

For positive \(x,y\), the order of the middle points depends on whether \(x<y\) or \(y<x\). A physical base value \(E_{\rm phys}=x+y+z\) can lie in different intervals relative to \(2x\), \(2y\), and \(2(x+y)\), depending on \(z\). Therefore neither \((x,y)\) nor the local \(E-i0\) side determines one universal based path system.

Passing below rather than above a remote critical value changes the braid of the four branch points. Such a change may alter the integral Picard marking even though the local nodal germ at \(E=0\) is unchanged.

## Consequence

The explicit quartic does provide the missing global search space: the punctured base is

\[
\mathbb C_E\setminus\{0,2x,2y,2(x+y)\}.
\]

But it does not select a path in that space. To construct ordered ambient thimbles one must add:

1. a regular base value, including the physical value of \(z\);
2. branch-point labels in the fiber over that base;
3. paths to the two colliding roots at \(E=0\);
4. the chosen above/below passage relative to the three remote critical values;
5. transport into the integral degree-two del Pezzo Picard lattice.

## Disposition

The global discriminant is now explicit and reduces the missing marking to a finite braid/path problem. No universal parity can be computed until a source-authorized basepoint and path homotopy are supplied; different kinematic chambers need not share the same Picard marking.
