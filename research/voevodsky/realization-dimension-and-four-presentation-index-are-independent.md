# Realization dimension and four-presentation index are independent

## Correction

The index \(i\in\{1,2,3,4\}\) labels four presentations inside one semilocal tetrahedron. The index \(k\) labels realization dimension. The established internal arrows have type

\[
C_{ij,k}:V_{i,k}\longrightarrow V_{j,k},
\qquad i<j.
\]

None of these arrows changes \(k\). In particular, the terminal internal comparison

\[
C_{14,k}:V_{1,k}\longrightarrow V_{4,k}
\]

does not by itself produce an arrow to \(V_{1,k+1}\).

## Established structure

For each fixed \(k\), retain the tetrahedral presentation diagram and its face and tetrahedral coherences. The collection over \(k\) is presently a discretely indexed family

\[
\{\mathcal T_k\}_{k\ge 0},
\]

not a helix. The observer-valued retyping allows \(V_{1,k}\) and \(V_{4,k}\) to be compared within a common Hermitian-form target. Completed history plus cycle observation may make this within-block presentation algebraically reversible on its image.

## Optional additional datum

A map

\[
V_{4,k}\longrightarrow V_{1,k+1}
\]

would be extra structure. In the twisted-cyclic formalism it plays the role of a separately supplied twisting endofunctor: the 2-Segal theorem establishes coherence after such an endofunctor is given, but does not construct it. No semilocal source formula for this dimension-raising map has been located.

## Consequence

The immediate task is the exact within-dimension comparison \(V_{1,k}\to V_{4,k}\), including its observer-valued type and qualified positive realization. Cross-dimension coherence, successor notation, and helical claims are suspended unless an independent suspension, degree-shift, or arithmetic successor is source-constructed.
