# Compactification invariance of the exceptional triangle obstruction

## Result

The exceptional open surface is `U=P^2` minus three general-position lines, hence `U` is isomorphic to `G_m^2`. Its reference SNC compactification has boundary dual complex a triangle, so integral first homology is `Z`.

In characteristic zero, smooth proper SNC compactifications of the same open variety are connected by weak factorization supported on the boundary. The induced dual-complex moves preserve simple-homotopy type. Therefore every smooth proper SNC compactification of this fixed `U` has boundary dual complex simple-homotopy equivalent to `S^1`.

## Consequence

Changing compactification while retaining the same open source cannot add a genuine face that kills the primitive cycle. The obstruction is not an artifact of choosing `P^2` or of an insufficient sequence of blow-ups.

A filler must instead change the open source object or introduce a relative correspondence not equivalent to another compactification of `U`. Either move requires independently derived source authority and a chain map to the ordered wall carrier.

## Scope

The theorem uses characteristic-zero weak factorization for smooth proper SNC pairs. It does not classify singular, nonproper, or open-changing enlargements.
