# Probe-induced Hankel metric and Clifford algebra

## Question

Can the prime-index geometric algebra be derived from the character probes rather than imposed by choosing an orthonormal shell basis?

## Claim boundary

A finite family of real rational character settings canonically induces a positive-semidefinite Hankel form on the finite shell-direction space. Its radical is exactly the undetected character direction. The associated possibly degenerate Clifford algebra acts on the exterior algebra of shell directions. This is apparatus-conditioned geometry: it does not provide an intrinsic metric on the prime sequence independently of the selected probes.

## Evaluation geometry

Let \(W_n\) have ordered basis \(e_1,\ldots,e_n\), representing adjacent-prime shell directions. For a setting \(t\), define

\[
v(t)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}t^ie_i^*\in W_n^*.
\]

For \(T=\{t_1,\ldots,t_m\}\), the evaluation map is

\[
M_T:W_n\longrightarrow k^m,
\qquad
x\longmapsto\bigl(v(t_r)(x)\bigr)_{r=1}^{m}.
\]

Pull back the standard form on \(k^m\):

\[
Q_T=M_T^*M_T.
\]

In the shell basis its Gram matrix is

\[
(Q_T)_{ij}=\sum_{r=1}^{m}t_r^{i+j}.
\]

Thus \(Q_T\) is a finite Hankel moment matrix. Over the rationals embedded in the reals,

\[
Q_T(x,x)=\sum_{r=1}^{m}v(t_r)(x)^2,
\]

and consequently

\[
\operatorname{rad}Q_T=\ker M_T.
\]

The blind direction is therefore the null direction of the metric actually induced by the apparatus.

## Rank-one metric extension

Adding one setting \(s\) gives

\[
Q_{T\cup\{s\}}=Q_T+v(s)^{\mathsf T}v(s).
\]

This is a rank-one update. When \(|T|=n\), the evaluation matrix is square and

\[
\det Q_T=(\det M_T)^2.
\]

The Vandermonde determinant is therefore the squared volume density of the probe-induced shell geometry.

For the five-cube with four total evaluations, \(Q_T\) has rank four and a one-dimensional radical generated by the coefficient vector of

\[
q(z)=z(z-1)\left(z-\frac56\right)
\left(z-\frac34\right)\left(z-\frac7{10}\right).
\]

Adding \(s=2/3\) makes the form nondegenerate because \(q(2/3)\neq0\).

## Clifford action

Let \(\varepsilon_i\) denote exterior multiplication by \(e_i\) on \(\Lambda^\bullet W_n\), and let \(\iota_j\) denote contraction by \(e_j^*\). Define

\[
c_i=\varepsilon_i+
\sum_{j=1}^{n}(Q_T)_{ij}\iota_j.
\]

The exterior anticommutation identities imply

\[
c_ic_j+c_jc_i=2(Q_T)_{ij}.
\]

Hence \(\Lambda^\bullet W_n\), already identified with the Boolean subset/Walsh space, is a module for

\[
\operatorname{Cl}(W_n,Q_T).
\]

When \(a\in\operatorname{rad}Q_T\), its Clifford action has no contraction component:

\[
c(a)=\varepsilon(a),
\qquad
c(a)^2=0.
\]

The undetected prime-index direction is thus a nilpotent exterior generator inside the degenerate Clifford algebra. The next independent setting removes it from the radical and gives it nonzero metric pairing.

## Cubical interpretation

Completion of an \(n\)-cube realizes a new full-support exterior mode in \(\Lambda^nW_n\). With only \(n-1\) total evaluations, the apparatus-induced form on \(W_n\) has a one-dimensional radical. The cube lifts that null index direction into an actual route class. The \(n\)-th evaluation supplies a rank-one metric extension that detects it.

This separates three structures:

- exterior degree records how many independent shell directions participate;
- the Hankel form records which combinations the chosen character probes distinguish;
- prime arithmetic determines the cutoff at which a full-support cubical route becomes available.

## Strongest falsification attempt

For the five-shell settings, compute \(M_T\) and \(Q_T\) exactly over the rationals. Verify the radical equality, the rank-one update, the determinant square, and the radical generator \(q\). Construct creation and contraction operators on all 32 exterior basis states and test every Clifford anticommutator. Require the radical Clifford generator to square to zero and the fifth setting to make the Gram form full rank.

## Disposition

The geometric-algebra interpretation is source-derived once the apparatus settings are included: the probes induce the metric they use. An intrinsic prime metric remains unestablished and should not be inferred from this apparatus-conditioned Clifford algebra.
