# A second local readout yields a three-state compositional net summary

Extend the accumulator agent to `(parity,has-one)` with a fixed principal-port active-pair rule `ACC(p,h)--BIT(b) -> ACC(p xor b,h or b)`. The closed output admits parity and whether any bit is one. Fresh `check_two_observation_net_summary.py` verifies 20,481 words/splits up to length 10: summaries compose as `(p1 xor p2,h1 or h2)` and empty word is `(0,0)`. Exactly three states are reachable over arbitrary finite words: `(0,0)` (all zero), `(1,1)` (odd), `(0,1)` (positive even). Their immediate two-bit readouts differ, so no two can be merged under this observation policy. The local rule and state set stay fixed as n increases.

This is a controlled success for a deliberately limited interface. It does not model indexed reads or Nima E/E_B overlap/multiplicity operations. To stress the archetype, next allow a bounded threshold readout 'at least k ones' with k supplied externally: for fixed k summary saturates at k, but if k ranges through all 0..n then the required count states grow with n. Specify which thresholds are admissible and distinguish fixed versus variable observation vocabularies.
