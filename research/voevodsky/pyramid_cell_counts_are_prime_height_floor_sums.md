# Pyramid cell counts are prime-height floor sums

## Question

Can surface and filler dimensions be computed directly from prime data without enumerating every subset vertex of every admitted cube?

## Claim boundary

The formula counts effective-divisor cubes generated by distinct adjacent-prime shell directions under the established maximum-edge grade. It covers positive-dimensional cells in the current pyramid census. Repeated same-shell divided-power cells are outside this count.

## Cell parameterization

Fix a finite shell set

\[
I=\{i_1<\cdots<i_n\}.
\]

Its minimal base divisor has one copy of each source prime \(p_i\), and its minimal completion grade is

\[
L(I)=p_{i_n}\prod_{i\in I}p_{i+1}.
\]

Every cell with this direction set has a unique base multiplier \(k\geq1\). All its vertices and edges are obtained by multiplying the minimal cell by \(k\), so its completion grade is

\[
g(k,I)=kL(I).
\]

Therefore cells with direction set \(I\) admitted at cutoff \(L\) are indexed by

\[
1\leq k\leq
\left\lfloor\frac{L}{L(I)}\right\rfloor.
\]

## Exact filler-count formula

The number of admitted \(n\)-cells is

\[
c_n(L)=
\sum_{\substack{I\subset\mathbb N\\|I|=n}}
\left\lfloor\frac{L}{L(I)}\right\rfloor.
\]

Only finitely many terms are nonzero. For edges this becomes

\[
c_1(L)=
\sum_i
\left\lfloor\frac{L}{p_ip_{i+1}}\right\rfloor.
\]

The total number of maximal directed paths contributed by positive-dimensional cells is

\[
R(L)=
\sum_{n\geq1}n!c_n(L).
\]

This counts a path separately for every ordering of a cell's directions.

## Attachment multiplicities

The number of \(n\)-cells entering exactly at grade \(L\) is

\[
\Delta c_n(L)=c_n(L)-c_n(L-1)
=
\#\{(k,I):|I|=n,\ kL(I)=L\}.
\]

Thus a complete attachment package can be predicted by divisibility of one grade, without constructing the entire lower-cutoff complex.

The observed packages are

\[
(\Delta c_0,\Delta c_1,\Delta c_2,\Delta c_3)
=(1,2,2,1)
\]

at 525 and

\[
(\Delta c_0,\Delta c_1,\Delta c_2,\Delta c_3,\Delta c_4)
=(1,3,4,3,1)
\]

at 8085. The positive-degree formula should reproduce these and decide the predicted five-cube package at 165165.

## Dirichlet series

Weight each \(n\)-cell by its completion grade. Then

\[
\mathcal Z_n(s)
=
\sum_{k,I}(kL(I))^{-s}
=
\zeta(s)
\sum_{|I|=n}L(I)^{-s}.
\]

Grouping by \(m=\max I\) gives

\[
\frac{\mathcal Z_n(s)}{\zeta(s)}
=
\sum_{m\geq n}
(p_mp_{m+1})^{-s}
 e_{n-1}(p_2^{-s},\ldots,p_m^{-s}),
\]

where \(e_{n-1}\) is the elementary symmetric polynomial. This series packages filler density by cubical degree.

## Strongest falsification attempt

Compute the floor sums independently at cutoffs 522, 525, 8084, and 8085 and compare every positive-dimensional cell count and factorially weighted path count with the direct cubical censuses. Verify exact attachment multiplicities at 525 and 8085. Then evaluate the divisibility formula at 165165 to obtain a pre-enumeration five-cube attachment prediction.

## Computed comparison

The floor sums exactly reproduce every positive-dimensional cell count and factorially weighted path count in the direct censuses at 522, 525, 8084, and 8085. They also reproduce the attachment packages at 525 and 8085.

At the five-cube grade 165165, the formula predicts the full attachment vector

\[
(\Delta c_0,\Delta c_1,\Delta c_2,
\Delta c_3,\Delta c_4,\Delta c_5)
=(1,4,7,7,4,1).
\]

Its positive-dimensional cells contribute

\[
4+7\cdot2+7\cdot6+4\cdot24+1\cdot120=276
\]

new typed maximal paths. The independently generated graph census supplies the vertex increment \(\Delta c_0=1\).

## Disposition

The prime-height floor sum is an exact positive-cell and path-count generator for the tested pyramid cutoffs. It confirms the five-cube attachment prediction without enumerating the roughly fifty-thousand-edge cubical complex.
