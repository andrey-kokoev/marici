# Subdivision preservation is not construction

## Question

Can localization at subdivisions turn an unsourced cone into a geometric horn?

## Claim boundary

No. A sourced object is the structured tuple consisting of a geometric witness, its realized derived pair, and its regulator comparison. Localization inverts only morphisms induced by witnessed toroidal subdivisions. Formal inverse arrows therefore connect objects that already carry witnesses.

An isomorphism after forgetting geometry does not automatically lift to this category. A different chain presentation is admissible only when supplied with an explicit geometric witness and comparison isomorphism; the isomorphism alone carries no provenance.

This also preserves the rank-26 obstruction. Any comparison-respecting equivalence from the absorbed relation pair to the geometric horn would preserve the `Xi` projection, contradicting coefficients zero and one. No sequence of subdivisions changes that invariant.

Subdivision can transport, refine, or contract an existing sourced horn. It cannot supply the first geometric witness or primitive relative class.

## Disposition

Birational localization is valid only in the witnessed category, not in a replete closure of its algebraic projection. The next leaf states the rank-26 obstruction directly in the relative-pair category.
