# RH modular-filling constructor support

## Question

What executable support can be supplied before the arithmetic/theta owner materializes the missing completed chain data?

## Claim boundary

This packet defines acceptance tests and finite hostile fixtures. It does not manufacture cutoff maps, graph topology, or a modular action absent from the source.

## Constructor contract

A strict filler requires a source-derived section \(s\) of the boundary map and naturality cells under every admitted modular or basis action. A completed filler additionally requires cutoff compatibility, uniform reciprocal-orbit control, boundary-null preservation, endpoint continuity, and retention of all declared arithmetic and archimedean channels.

Six source objects are required:

- relative chain objects \(C_2,C_1,C_0\);
- boundary maps with square zero;
- modular and basis actions on each chain object;
- cutoff transition maps;
- completed graph topology;
- endpoint evaluation.

## Executed hostile fixtures

The representative-dependence fixture was reproduced exactly.

A second fixture tests reciprocal naturality. Direct-edge selection is equivariant under the finite label reflection \(w\leftrightarrow-w\). A detour selection through a hidden fixed anchor \(a\) is not: reflecting the selected chain passes through \(-a\), while selecting the reflected boundary still passes through \(a\). The routes have equal boundary but are distinct chains.

Therefore an unspecified intermediate choice cannot be hidden inside `canonical`. It needs either strict invariance or an explicit representative-change 2-cell. Those cells then require composition and tetrahedral coherence.

## Remaining nonexecutable test

The cutoff nonclosability fixture cannot run until cutoff maps, completed chain action, and graph topology are supplied. Its acceptance condition is fixed: every boundary-null family must map uniformly to zero along the reciprocal orbit. One nonzero limiting family rejects completed descent.

## Disposition

The strict and completed constructor claims now have a bounded acceptance contract and two executed finite falsifiers. The first source request is the declared chain-level modular action and natural contraction on the completed cutoff system.
