# Relative-boundary labels narrow but do not identify Aspect support

## Question

Does the worked relative twisted-cohomology example identify the two support generators in the exact Aspect target enlargement?

## Claim boundary

The source fixes a two-branch relative-boundary pattern in its own hyperplane arrangement. It does not provide a map from those surfaces to the enhanced conductor occurrence labels.

## Source-derived boundary pattern

In `Cosmology meets cohomology`, PDF pages 15 and 18–21, the two-site/four-point arrangement has twisted surfaces \(T_1,T_2\) and relative surfaces \(S_1,S_2,S_3\). Its dual cohomology is assembled from boundary strata using antisymmetric symbols \(\delta_J\).

Only \(S_3\) has nontrivial one-boundary twisted cohomology. The codimension-two strata \(S_{12},S_{23},S_{13}\) each support a constant class. For the integration-by-parts form on \(S_3\), equations (3.39)–(3.40) give

\[
A_{12}=0,
\qquad
A_{13}=-\xi|_2,
\qquad
A_{14}=-\xi|_1.
\]

Thus one candidate boundary channel vanishes and exactly two deeper restrictions survive. Their source labels are the intersections reached from \(S_3\): \(S_{23}\) and \(S_{13}\). The third point stratum \(S_{12}\), which does not contain \(S_3\), is not generated by this boundary Stokes step.

## Comparison with the exact enlargement

The exact ambient completion adds two vectors

\[
q_1=\Phi_{++}=(1,1,1)^T,
\qquad
q_2=\Phi_{-+}=(1,-1,-1)^T.
\]

The count now matches a source-derived relative pattern: one active one-boundary class can restrict to two nonzero codimension-two classes. This makes the tentative typing

\[
\{q_1,q_2\}
\dashrightarrow
\{\delta_{23}(1),\delta_{13}(1)\}
\]

more specific than the earlier statement that two unspecified support sectors are needed.

No arrow in the sources determines which occurrence vector maps to which relative stratum. Before orientations are fixed, there are two bijections and independent sign choices. The hyperplane variables \((S_1,S_2,S_3,T_1,T_2)\) are not identified with the conductor point order \((++,+-,-+,--)\), and equal cardinality does not construct that identification.

## Explicit relative points and the factor-two oddball

The source gives

\[
S_1=x_1+X_1+Y,
\qquad
S_2=x_2+X_2+Y,
\qquad
S_3=x_1+x_2+X_1+X_2,
\]

with twisted coordinates \(T_1=x_1\), \(T_2=x_2\). Solving the pairwise intersections gives

\[
p_{23}=(Y-X_1,-X_2-Y),
\qquad
p_{13}=(-X_1-Y,Y-X_2),
\]

and

\[
p_{12}=(-X_1-Y,-X_2-Y).
\]

The omitted surface evaluates as

\[
S_1(p_{23})=2Y,
\qquad
S_2(p_{13})=2Y,
\qquad
S_3(p_{12})=-2Y.
\]

Hence generic \(Y\ne0\) separates the three point strata, while \(Y=0\) is a triple-intersection degeneration. The factor two is structurally visible in the source arrangement. The ambient obstruction matrix also has only entries \(0,\pm2\). This numerical coincidence motivates a sharper conjecture: the obstruction may measure failure to retain the \(2Y\)-separated relative point strata when passing to the two-leg target. It is not evidence for an identification because no parameter map sends \(Y\) to a conductor coordinate and the obstruction is parameter-independent.

## Strongest falsification attempt

The tempting assignment fails at the interface rather than at rank:

1. the relative source yields the labels \(S_{23},S_{13}\);
2. the conductor source yields the occurrence labels \(++,-+\) for \(q_1,q_2\);
3. neither source supplies a common geometric support map;
4. the relative example's third point stratum is present in cohomology even though its coefficient vanishes for the particular IBP step.

Consequently the two-vector enlargement cannot yet be called the relative Stokes completion of the conductor system.

## Acceptance test

A valid identification must provide equations for the actual marked walls, a map sending the two selected conductor occurrences to \(S_3\cap S_2\) and \(S_3\cap S_1\), induced orientation signs reproducing the columns \(q_1,q_2\), and a proof that the analogue of the \(S_{12}\) component vanishes. It must then verify the total differential squares to zero.

## Disposition

The source search narrows the speculative support type to the two restrictions from one active one-boundary class. The first missing typed object is the common support map from conductor occurrence strata to relative hyperplane strata. Until it is derived, all eight signed bijections are formal candidates and none has physical authority.
