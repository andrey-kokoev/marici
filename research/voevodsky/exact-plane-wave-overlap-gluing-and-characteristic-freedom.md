# Exact plane-wave overlap gluing and retained characteristic freedom

## Result

Two local exact vacuum plane-wave solutions in rotated charts can be glued
with their central observer frames. Their actual overlap maps satisfy the
metric/tidal compatibility and triple cocycle equations. This is an explicit,
bounded Einstein gluing example inside the Brinkmann ansatz.

The same left patch admits distinct vacuum right extensions with different
future observer tidal invariants. Thus gluing of a GIVEN compatible pair is
not the same as unique reconstruction of the other patch from overlap data.
The difference is physical profile information, not merely frame-sign choice.

This is neither general nonlinear Einstein constraint gluing nor a claim of
nonuniqueness from complete spacelike Cauchy data.

## Local packets and an actual overlap

Choose

    A0=diag(1,-1), B=[[0,1],[1,0]],
    f(u)=0 for u<=1, f(u)=(u-1)^3 for u>1,
    A_b(u)=A0+b f(u) B.

This is a C2 profile for each real parameter b, with a possibly discontinuous
third derivative at u=1. Define open patches

    U_L={u<0}, U_R={u>-1}.

They cover the spacetime coordinate domain, and overlap on -1<u<0. The left
packet uses g_A0 in coordinates x. The right packet uses coordinates y with
x=Q y, where Q is a constant transverse rotation, and profile

    A_R,b=Q^T A_b Q.

On the overlap f=0. Therefore

    dy^T dy=dx^T dx,
    y^T A_R,b y=x^T A0 x.

The transition is an actual metric isometry on the overlap, not a guessed
spectral identification. It also carries the observer rest frames and tidal
forms correctly. In the checker Q has cosine 3/5 and sine 4/5, so raw matrix
components differ even though the tensors agree under the supplied transition.
Requiring literal equality of those components would reject a valid gluing.

For three constant chart frames Q_i, set g_ij=Q_i^T Q_j (j-local -> i-local).
Then g_ij g_jk=g_ik. The checker tests this with two nontrivial rational rotations.
Given these compatible local fields and transitions, they define the global
g_A_b. Uniqueness here is the ordinary uniqueness of the glued tensor for
specified patch data, not selection of b.

## Regularity and the seam at u=1

The right patch itself contains the future profile seam. The value and first
two derivatives of (u-1)^3 vanish at u=1, matching the zero branch. Thus A_b,
the metric and its inverse are C2, the Levi-Civita connection is C1 and the
curvature is continuous. There is no distributional shell generated by this
seam. Each side is Ricci-flat, and the continuous Ricci tensor vanishes at
the seam as well. This is a classical C2 solution; it is not advertised as
C-infinity or analytic.

There is also a smooth variant:

    f_infinity(u)=0 for u<=1,
    f_infinity(u)=exp[-1/(u-1)^2] for u>1.

Every right derivative tends to zero at 1 because an exponential dominates
every inverse power. Hence f_infinity is smooth with all seam jets zero. The
same trace-free profile argument gives smooth vacuum extensions with identical
infinite-order data on u=1 and different fields beyond it. This universal
smooth argument is written mathematics; the checker uses the C2 polynomial
variant and does not approximate the exponential numerically.

## Distinct extensions, not merely distinct frames

Compare b=1 and b=2 with the same initial observer frame and proper-time
reference. Both solutions agree on U_L, on the entire overlap, and for all
u<=1. At u=2 their transverse profiles are

    A_1=[[1,1],[1,-1]], A_2=[[1,2],[2,-1]].

The mixed observer tidal components are -1/2 and -1. More importantly,

    tr(E_1^2)=1, tr(E_2^2)=5/2.

This is a rotation-invariant distinction for the retained unit observer at the
corresponding proper-time event. It cannot be erased by an eigenframe sign
change or spatial reorientation. This is an observer-dependent tidal invariant,
not a claim about a nonzero scalar polynomial spacetime invariant of plane waves.
The observer, its normalization and event comparison are retained inputs.

At every u, the tidal eigenvalues are

    (-sqrt(1+b^2 f(u)^2)/2, 0, +sqrt(1+b^2 f(u)^2)/2).

The spectrum is simple throughout. Thus even generic local frame selection
does not determine the unobserved future wave profile. The present example
needs no degeneracy to exhibit extension freedom.

## Why this does not violate causal uniqueness

The surfaces u=constant are null. The region u<=1 is a range of retarded
coordinate, NOT all data on a complete spacelike Cauchy surface. Agreement
there need not determine incoming characteristic gravitational degrees of
freedom at larger u. A complete appropriate Cauchy problem includes additional
data that distinguish these extensions.

The smooth infinite-jet example is a characteristic unique-continuation
counterexample in this ansatz, not a counterexample to Einstein Cauchy
uniqueness modulo diffeomorphism. Neither solution claims asymptotic flatness,
finite total wave energy, or prescribed global incoming radiation conditions.

## Fibration/descent interpretation

For specified charts and transitions, the restriction map

    {right-patch vacuum profiles and observer records}
       -> {overlap metric and observer records}

has a fiber containing the whole b-family (and, more generally, arbitrary
admissible trace-free future profiles supported beyond the overlap).
The left packet fixes which overlap record is required; it does not choose a
point of that fiber.

Compatible pairs form the ordinary pullback of left and right solution records
over the overlap record in this fixed-coordinate ansatz. Their tensor fields
glue. If one passes to solutions modulo diffeomorphism, transition isomorphisms
and their coherence must be retained; no general homotopy-stack theorem is
proved by this coordinate-level example.

There are TWO freedoms that must not be conflated:

1. frame stabilizers: multiple descriptions of the same tidal tensor;
2. extension fibers: distinct gravitational states with different later tides.

Adding a coherent frame cocycle addresses the first and checks descent. It
does not eliminate the second. An extra radiative/initial condition would
select a physical extension; fibration alone merely records the alternatives.
