# The middle facet cannot live entirely in a Hilbert cone category because the characteristic edge is line-valued and multigraded

Fresh inspection of the multigrade Euler completion invalidates global
promotion of the doubled Clark--Grushin candidate on one Hilbert/rigged
carrier.  At finite cutoff the arithmetic column splits as

\[
u_X=u_X^{(1)}+u_X^{(2)}+u_X^{(\ge3)},
\]

but its completed components have different types:

- primitive: distributional current/anomaly line;
- square: Hilbert boundary/anomaly coordinate;
- connected: Schatten-three determinant carrier.

There is no declared completed Banach or Hilbert space in which these three
columns add and on which the aggregate border is a bounded automorphism.
Therefore the proposed global pencil `P_loc` with a complete graded arithmetic
diagonal is not merely unfinished; in the monolithic Hilbert-cone category it
is ill-typed.

The characteristic interval `X_SC` belongs instead to the Picard/determinant
layer

\[
\mathcal L_{\rm ar}
=\mathcal L_{\rm prim}\otimes
 \mathcal L_{\rm sq}\otimes
 \mathcal L_{\det_3}\otimes
 \mathcal L_{\rm seam}\otimes
 \mathcal L_\infty,
\]

while the Green interval is a bounded rigged/Hilbert complex.  Thus the
middle facet cannot be a tetrahedron internal to `C_cl` alone.  Its proper
ambient object must be a stratified total category fibred over at least:

1. a Picard groupoid of anomaly/determinant lines;
2. a stable category of rigged Green complexes;
3. a category of source-derived comparison correspondences between them.

The missing `CG` edge is exactly such a cross-fibre correspondence.  Its
required natural transformation starts at the line-valued arithmetic anomaly
complex and lands in the determinant line/kernel complex of the Green pencil;
its first variation must realize the rigged transpose `B^times`, and its
Hilbert shadow must realize `B^dagger`.

This reclassifies the root obstruction.  A full two-term Green differential on
the same domain and codomain as a monolithic Grushin pencil cannot be demanded,
because that monolithic completed pencil does not exist.  The valid target is
a stratified mate preserving determinant lines and finite defect modules
without coercing primitive, square, and connected strata into one vector.

Consequently the Waldhausen four-face formulas remain valid only in the total
stratified category.  Applying them inside the Hilbert fibre alone necessarily
loses the divisor-bearing anomaly strata; applying them inside the Picard
fibre alone loses the Green state complex.
