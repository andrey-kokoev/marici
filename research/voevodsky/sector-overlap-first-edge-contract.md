# Sector-overlap first-edge contract

## Question

What exact data are sufficient to admit the first nondegenerate edge in the coherence-pyramid sector nerve?

## Claim boundary

The contract governs mathematical overlap admission. Physical claims require an additional operational extension and are not prerequisites for a purely mathematical edge.

## Required edge packet

An edge must provide:

1. two named sectors;
2. one common object in a declared computad sort;
3. a source-derived incidence map from each sector;
4. domain, codomain, variance, and naturality domain for each map;
5. a comparison cell with parallel boundaries, direction, and invertibility status;
6. a proof or exact residual for that cell;
7. a certificate dependency DAG and minimal failure bundle;
8. an explicit scope.

The common sort must be one of `carrier`, `gauge_presentation`, `finite_green`, `quotient_form`, or `closed_form`.

## Nonconstructors

None of the following creates an edge:

- a shared scalar;
- equal dimensions;
- cyclic relabelling alone;
- evidence dependency alone;
- semantic analogy alone;
- constructors merely occurring in one module.

## Current audit

Five candidate edges were tested: Markov–Kitaev associator, Markov–theta–Clark, Markov–`R_zeta`, a Benincasa local-sector pair, and Sontag–Flavor calibration. Every rejection now names its first missing typed data. No candidate satisfies the contract.

## Physical extension

A physical edge additionally requires a source-admitted effect, positive state–effect pairing, physical record map, and operational backend. This extension does not retroactively block a mathematical overlap whose scope excludes physical interpretation.

## Disposition

The first-edge problem is now constructive rather than terminological. A candidate can enter the nerve exactly when its ten-field packet passes; until then, its local calculations or evidence dependencies retain their own types without edge promotion.
