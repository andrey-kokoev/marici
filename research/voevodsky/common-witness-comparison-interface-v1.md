## What a comparison must bind

A source binding includes source ID AND domain, constructor digest, supplied parameters and retained archive. Both routes must bind that exact record. Same dimension, current total energy or a shared name is insufficient; a different seed, clock, metric, normalization or source rule is a different binding.

Each route declares its target type, actual map, source operations and their target implementations, common domains, context, resources, retained records and evidence. Required diagrams are:

    phi_i A_source = A_i phi_i
    C phi_math = phi_process

The first is checked on the stated domain for every licensed operation. The second requires an independently specified typed cross-route map C; two separately successful representations do not generate it automatically. Domain/availability guards and resource policy participate in these equations. Composition additionally requires matching intermediate types and domains. Observation equality includes declared units/normalization; it is not a substitute for those diagrams.

### Identity and licensed continuations

Complete source identity compares values, provenance and archive. For a fixed task/context/resource policy, define x~y by equality of operation-availability profiles and task observations along every licensed **finite** continuation word. With partial operations, unequal guards are distinguishable: merely restricting to the intersection of available words can yield a nontransitive relation and is not an admissible identity. A predictive quotient must be a congruence for the admitted operations and availability guards.

This quantifies over the declared mathematical protocol, not arbitrary physically available operations or infinite physical execution. Adding archival restoration, phase/matrix readout, interactions, independent actuators or a different resource policy requires another equivalence review. Hidden records remain retained even if a forward task reaches a zero readout.
