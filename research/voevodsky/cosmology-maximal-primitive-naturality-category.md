# Maximal primitive naturality category

## Question

Which fan-compatible morphisms preserve the primitive integral horn?

## Claim boundary

The exceptional triangle has rays

`(1,0)`, `(0,1)`, `(-1,-1)`.

Its integral fan-automorphism group is `S3`. Determinant records the orientation sign of the induced permutation. If all three labels and their order are fixed, the character-lattice map must be the identity. Rees-compatible strict transverse base changes and multiplication of wall equations by pulled-back base units remain allowed because their lattice part is identity and their actual wall ratios pull back functorially.

Without labels, all six permutations act using the sign local system; the three even permutations preserve orientation.

A determinant-one shear preserves the primitive torus orientation but generally does not preserve this three-ray fan. Unimodularity is therefore necessary but not sufficient.

## Disposition

Among maps preserving the exact labeled triangle without extra strata, the strict category is maximal. Broader unimodular maps require fan subdivisions and a separate comparison. The next leaf tests whether the derived geometric pair is invariant under those subdivisions.
