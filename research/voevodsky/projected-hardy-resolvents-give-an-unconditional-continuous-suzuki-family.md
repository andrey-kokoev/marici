# Projected Hardy resolvents give an unconditional continuous Suzuki family

## Goal

Suzuki's Proposition 5.8 requires a sufficiently rich family in

\[
V=H_+\cap KH_+,
\]

but constructing vectors by selecting individual zeros is spectrally circular. There is nevertheless a canonical continuous family obtained solely from the boundary involution and Hardy geometry.

## Hardy resolvents

In the upper-half-plane Hardy space, let `k_w` be the reproducing vector at `w in C_+`, normalized so that

\[
F(w)=\langle F,k_w\rangle_{H^2}

=
c\int_\mathbb R
F(x)\frac{dx}{x-\bar w}.
\]

Equivalently on the time side, `k_w` is represented by the positive-half-line exponential

\[
t\longmapsto c_w e^{-i\bar w t}1_{t>0}.
\]

Let

\[
P_V=1_{\{1\}}(PQP)
=s\!\!\lim_{n\to\infty}(PQP)^n.
\]

Define the projected resolvent family

\[
\boxed{
r_w=P_Vk_w,
\qquad w\in\mathbb C_+.
}
\]

This family is unconditional whenever the boundary involution `K` is defined. It uses no zero list and every operation is contractive or isometric.

## Reproducing property

For `F in V`, `P_VF=F`, so

\[
\langle F,r_w\rangle
=
\langle P_VF,k_w\rangle
=
\langle F,k_w\rangle
=F(w).
\]

Thus `r_w` is the reproducing vector of the closed subspace `V`. Its kernel is

\[
\boxed{
K_V(z,w)
=
\langle r_w,r_z\rangle
=
\langle P_Vk_w,k_z\rangle.
}
\]

It is positive semidefinite by construction.

## Completeness is automatic inside the intersection

If `F in V` is orthogonal to every `r_w`, then

\[
F(w)=0
\qquad(w\in\mathbb C_+).
\]

Analytic uniqueness gives `F=0`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\overline{\operatorname{span}}\{r_w:w\in\mathbb C_+\}=V.
}
\]

This solves the internal completeness problem for `V`. It does not prove that `V` is nonzero or sufficiently rich for Weil interpolation.

## Explicit alternating-projection approximation

The family admits source-defined approximants

\[
r_w^{(n)}=(PQP)^nk_w.
\]

For every fixed `w`,

\[
r_w^{(n)}\to r_w
\]

strongly. Their Gram kernels

\[
K_V^{(n)}(z,w)
=
\langle(PQP)^nk_w,(PQP)^nk_z\rangle
\]

are positive at every finite stage. Unlike arbitrary finite Cholesky factors, these kernels are coherent because all are generated by one positive contraction.

However, their limit may be identically zero if `V={0}`. Positivity survives collapse.

## Source-side formula for the geometric kernel

Since

\[
Q=KPK,
\]

each finite approximant is an alternating word in

\[
P,
\quad K=\mathcal F^{-1}M_\Theta J\mathcal F.
\]

Therefore `K_V^(n)` is computable from the completed analytic boundary symbol `Theta` without zeros. Formally,

\[
K_V^{(n)}(z,w)
=
\langle (PKPKP)^nk_w,(PKPKP)^nk_z\rangle.
\]

This is the canonical continuous counterpart of the finite forward-rung constructor.

## The arithmetic comparison kernel

The missing Green identity can now be stated without a zero-indexed basis. On a common domain where the Weil pairing is defined, set

\[
\mathcal D_V(z,w)
=
W(r_w*r_z^*)-2K_V(z,w).
\]

Suzuki's Proposition 5.8(1) asks for vanishing of the polarized form on all of `V`. Since the projected resolvents are total, continuity would reduce that condition to

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal D_V(z,w)=0
\qquad(z,w\in\mathbb C_+).
}
\]

Thus an infinite family of test-function equalities is compressed into one two-variable kernel identity.

A source proof must justify three analytic points:

1. `r_w` lies in the domain or closure domain of the Weil form;
2. `W(r_w*r_z^*)` is jointly continuous in the `V` norm;
3. endpoint--gamma--prime evaluation equals `2K_V(z,w)`.

None follows merely from the boundedness of `P_V`.

## Interpolation gate in continuous form

For any candidate spectral coordinate `gamma`, evaluation is represented by `r_gamma` whenever `gamma in C_+` and the vector is nonzero. The normalized kernel vector

\[
e_\gamma=
\frac{r_\gamma}{\sqrt{K_V(\gamma,\gamma)}}
\]

satisfies

\[
e_\gamma(\rho)
=
\frac{K_V(\rho,\gamma)}{\sqrt{K_V(\gamma,\gamma)}}.
\]

Hence Suzuki's zero-separating interpolation requirement becomes a decay/invertibility statement for the sampled kernel matrix

\[
(K_V(\rho,\gamma))_{\rho,\gamma}.
\]

This cleanly separates existence of the continuous family from the much stronger discrete interpolation theorem.

## Exact collapse indicator

The diagonal

\[
K_V(w,w)=\|P_Vk_w\|^2
\]

is positive at one point if and only if `V` contains a function nonzero there. If it vanishes on an open set, analytic uniqueness forces `V=0`. Thus nontriviality is equivalent to

\[
K_V(w,w)>0
\]

for at least one `w in C_+`.

But finite approximants only give decreasing or convergent positive values whose limit may be zero. A lower bound uniform in projection depth is required to certify survival.

## Disposition

The requested continuous source family exists canonically:

\[
\boxed{
r_w=P_Vk_w.
}
\]

It is total in Suzuki's intersection and has a source-defined positive kernel. The remaining two gates are now exact kernel statements:

\[
W(r_w*r_z^*)=2K_V(z,w)
\]

and discrete interpolation/invertibility of sampled values of `K_V`. This advances the architecture without assuming RH, but does not establish either arithmetic identity or faithfulness.
