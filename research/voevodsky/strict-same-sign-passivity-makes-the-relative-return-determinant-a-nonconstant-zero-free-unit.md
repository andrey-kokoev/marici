# Strict same-sign passivity makes the relative return determinant a nonconstant zero-free unit

On the causal chart, the history boundary form has

\[
\operatorname{Im}R_+(z)\ge0,
\]

while the selected strict prime-delay arithmetic complement has

\[
\operatorname{Im}D_U(z)\ge\delta I
\]

with `delta>0`.  The paired arithmetic characteristic is

\[
Q_U(z)=D_U(z)+B^\dagger R_+(z)B.
\]

If `Q_U(z)x=0`, taking the imaginary part gives

\[
\langle x,\operatorname{Im}D_U(z)x\rangle
+\|\operatorname{Im}R_+(z)^{1/2}Bx\|^2=0.
\]

Strictness forces `x=0`. Thus the arithmetic paired block is injective; the
existing coercive Schur bound gives surjectivity and a bounded inverse. Under
the relative factorization this is exactly invertibility of `I-K(z)`.

Consequently

\[
D_{\rm ret}(z)=\det_F(I-K(z))
\]

is nowhere zero on the causal passive chart and on its seam boundary. The
reflected chart has the conjugate statement. Combined with the large-parameter
asymptotic

\[
D_{\rm ret}(s)=1-\|B\|_2^2/s+o(s^{-1}),
\]

`D_ret` is a nonconstant holomorphic unit on each strict passive chart.

This means multiplication by `D_ret` does not change the divisor there, even
though cancelling it algebraically would erase the coupled kernel model. The
unit theorem derives from passivity rather than from fitting Xi.

The scope is essential: the theorem applies to the strict same-sign Cayley
complement. It does not turn that complement into the complete graded
arithmetic diagonal, nor prove continuation of the unit through a different
indefinite or degenerate arithmetic law. It also shows that this passive return
cannot generate Xi zeros; all divisor information remains in the separately
constructed bare determinant line.
