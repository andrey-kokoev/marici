# Conditional selection of cube incidence at order 35

Assume a legal 35-vertex (3,9) coloring and fix a root. By the predecessor edge-floor argument it is red 8-regular. Label its eight red neighbors. The other 26 vertices carry masks: a label is present when the corresponding newcomer is a red neighbor. **Extra hypothesis:** the automorphism group of the multiset of masks acts transitively on the eight labels. Neither the root stabilizer of the completed coloring nor the red core is required to act transitively.

Every label appears seven times (56 incidences). Mask sizes are 1–5: the residual (3,8) core degrees are at most 7 and at least 3, the latter because at most 22 nonneighbors of a core vertex form a (3,7). In an 8-regular endpoint a mask has size eight minus core degree. A size-k mask can occur at most k times: the copies are blue to each other and to the 8-k unused newcomers. More generally, any blue core clique must have at least as many labels in its union as vertices, and size at most seven.

## Why the finite group search covers the hypothesis

For a transitive label group H on eight points, choose a Sylow 2-subgroup of the point stabilizer and extend it to a Sylow 2-subgroup P of H. Orbit-stabilizer gives a P-orbit of size eight. Up to conjugating labels, P lies in a fixed Sylow 2-subgroup of S8 (order 128). Enumerate transitive subgroups down from this group by its index-two subgroups. Every maximal subgroup of a finite 2-group has index two, and a supergroup of a transitive group is transitive. Enumerate index-two kernels as hyperplanes in the elementary abelian quotient by the subgroup generated by squares. The resulting 85 transitive subgroups have 35 inclusion-minimal members, 32 regular of order 8 and three of order 32. A regular degree-eight action need only be tested once per group isomorphism type.

For the five regular group types and the three nonregular minimal actions, enumerate mask orbits of sizes 1–5 (mask cardinality, not orbit length), with constant orbit multiplicity bounded by the cardinality. Retain exactly 26 cells and 56 incidences. Deduplicate by colored bipartite incidence-graph isomorphism. The union is 234 incidence types. Search for completions with **no symmetry constraints on core edges**. Allowed edges require disjoint masks, exact degrees are 8 minus mask cardinality, and red triangles are forbidden. For each label subset T at the tested scope impose a clause for every |T|+1 vertices whose masks lie inside T: at least one available red edge must join two of these vertices. Increment the scope from three to five.

| Required label demands | Types admitting completion of these partial constraints |
|---|---:|
| Through three | 65 |
| Through four | 5 |
| Through five | 1 |

The survivor is isomorphic to the eight singleton vertices, twelve two-element edges and six four-element faces of the ordinary cube (as subsets of its eight corners). It admits a verified legal full completion. Thus, **up to relabeling, cube incidence is necessary and sufficient among label-transitive patterns**. This does not prove every legal endpoint has transitive incidence, nor explain a priori why transitivity should be imposed. No cyclic distances or stored endpoint enter candidate generation. The question and the symmetry test were motivated by earlier discovery of the cube.
