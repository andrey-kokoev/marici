# Marked-cut relations preserve reciprocal history queries

## Construction

Use the independently verified behavioral runtime packet. For each initial local-view fiber and each pair of label/admission strata, form the exact source-witness path triples (x0,x1,x2). Restrict these triples by the observed middle and final local views.

Retain their adjacent relations R01 and R12. Both share the same behavioral witness at the middle cut. This is source-joined compatibility before existential projection, not a join on equal compressed local-view tuples.

## Exact result

The checker exhausts 20,406 nonempty conditioned three-cut diagrams, including 2,274 retaining two possible witness paths. It verifies:

    triples = R01 join R12,
    R02 = R12 composed with R01,
    R02^op = R01^op composed with R12^op.

Across all orientations and all subsets at the queried cut, 408,240 conditioning checks agree with direct projection of the full witness triples. Both orientations therefore answer questions relative to the same history constraints.

The earlier audit/delivery and delivery/delivery histories remain distinguishable at their marked intermediate cut despite having identical endpoint relations. The incompatible accepted origin-audit sequence retains an empty join.

## Structural meaning

The retained object is a marked development: a chain of source-compatible witness relations. A history or possibility query chooses a cut and direction within it. Composition removes an interior boundary when only endpoint information is wanted; keeping that boundary retains the correlations needed to ask about it later.

Multiple witnesses are allowed. The two-witness examples preserve their correlations across cuts without selecting an actual origin. Reversal transposes the relations and reverses their order; it leaves the underlying conditioned development available for reciprocal queries.

## Essential boundary

Lossless reconstruction from adjacent relations uses the frozen source kernel's local path factorization and per-cut observations. Pairwise information does not determine an arbitrary multi-cut relation. An exact negative control uses even-parity binary triples: four admitted triples have full adjacent projections, whose join fabricates eight triples.

Thus nonlocal source constraints require retention as higher-arity factors or sufficient additional state. The three-cut result does not discard such constraints or prove they are absent in arbitrary systems.

For longer histories generated by the same local kernel, witness-path joins compose by associativity. The finite run exhausts two-step strata; the extension to arbitrary finite chains follows from that local factorization theorem, with cut constraints retained.
