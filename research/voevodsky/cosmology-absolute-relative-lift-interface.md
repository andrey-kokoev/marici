# Absolute-relative split-residue correction

## Correction

The parity graph `n -> (n,n mod 2)` correctly describes constant-sign bookkeeping for monomial tame-symbol representatives. It is not the image of an absolute localization map into a cohomology group `Z plus Z/2`.

The alleged cokernel generator `H*(-1)` lies one motivic degree above `{u,v}`. It measures the obstruction to realizing an isolated line sign as the sole Gersten residue, not a missing coset of same-degree relative lifts.

## Disposition

The parity formula remains valid at chain level. The exact-sequence and index-two cohomological interpretation are withdrawn. Subsequent work must use the unsplit tame units in the correctly graded localization complex.
