# Ordinary exceptional H-to-Xi comparison is zero

## Calculation

On the exceptional divisor `E=P^2`, the strict transforms of the ordered walls cut the three lines

- `L1: U=0`,
- `L2: V=0`,
- `L3: U+V+P=0`.

Their coefficient determinant is one, so they form a projective triangle. The common complement is isomorphic to `G_m^2`; its degree-two logarithmic cohomology has rank one, generated by the logarithmic circuit represented by `Xi_log`.

The exceptional Gysin class is the hyperplane class `H=c1(O_E(1))`. On the common complement, any `Li` is nowhere zero and trivializes `O(1)`. Therefore ordinary restriction sends `H` to zero. It cannot send `H` to the nonzero `Xi_log` generator.

## Disposition

The `H -> Xi_log` comparison by ordinary restriction is falsified. This preserves the source-side primitive Gysin class but blocks its direct use as the missing horn leg.

The next admissible leaf is a relative localization/connecting morphism for the pair consisting of `E` and its ordered three-line boundary. Such a map must retain the divisor complex that ordinary restriction discards. Matching residues, dimensions, or primitive coefficients cannot substitute for it.
