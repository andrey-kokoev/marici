# The hyperbolic Clifford double closes positive pole--residue coherence but carries no Xi-divisor energy

constructed.  Let `H_res` be the completed shell-labelled residue carrier and

\[
P_{\rm res}=H_{\rm res}\oplus H_{\rm res}

\[
S_{\rm res}=\begin{pmatrix}0&I\\I&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
J_{\rm res}=\begin{pmatrix}I&0\\0&-I\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
F_{\rm res}=J_{\rm res}S_{\rm res}.
\]

\[
\Lambda_{\rm res}=\{(p,p):p\in H_{\rm res}\}.

\[

\quad
F_{\rm res}^2=-I.
\]

\[
