# DPC: external deformation interface

## Problem

Does current prior art define a sourced deformation parameter outside the declared `(x,y,z)` first jet?

## Bold conjecture

The `c` pivot, gamma-normal mechanism, total-energy Rees class, or `tau_p` classifier supplies such an interface.

## Named rivals

The `c` and total-energy functions may remain inside the verified parameter space; gamma may provide only a nontransferable algorithm; `tau_p` may lack source data; or the relative base-fiber parameter may be absent.

## Risky consequences

A viable candidate must supply a parameter object, source action or family, derivative rule, target module, quotient or admissibility rule, comparison authority, and provenance.

## Strongest falsification

Only provenance is populated. The `c` and total-energy functions are linear in `(x,y,z)`, while the `c`-kernel comparison is absent. Reuse of the gamma-normal vector is explicitly prohibited. The abstract `tau_p` classifier has no source unit map. The required relative base-fiber comparison and residue-exact section do not exist.

## Disposition

The conjecture is rejected. Current artifacts contain no sourced external first-jet interface. The first missing object is a deformation family or altered admissibility quotient that does not factor through the verified `(x,y,z)` presentation. This audit does not prove that no such deformation exists.

The next independent executable rival is the second jet of the existing source family; first-jet exactness alone does not determine second derivatives.
