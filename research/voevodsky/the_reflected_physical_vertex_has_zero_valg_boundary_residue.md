# The reflected physical vertex has zero v_alg boundary residue

## Coordinate dictionary

The missing dictionary is contained in the source wall geometry:

\[
E=x+y+z,
\qquad
W_1:\ b=y+z.
\]

On the central fiber \(E=0\),

\[
z=-x-y,
\qquad
y+z=-x.
\]

Thus the physical anchored split fiber is

\[
d_1:\ b=-x,
\]

which is exactly the wall \(l_1=b+x=0\) used by the central-sheet extension packet. The global reflection \(r_b:b\mapsto-b\) sends it to

\[
d_2:\ b=x.
\]

## Literal v_alg form

On the central sheet,

\[
R=xa^2+yb^2-xy(x+y)
\]

and

\[
\varphi_{v_{\rm alg}}
=
\frac{x^2y^2(x^2-y^2+2a^2-2b^2)}{R}\,da\wedge db.
\]

Its polar divisor is \(R=0\). Neither \(b+x\) nor \(b-x\) divides \(R\). Explicitly,

\[
R|_{b=\pm x}=x(a^2-y^2).
\]

Therefore the logarithmic boundary residues on both reflected wall divisors vanish:

\[
\operatorname{Res}_{b=-x}\varphi_{v_{\rm alg}}=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{Res}_{b=x}\varphi_{v_{\rm alg}}=0.
\]

The isolated intersections \(a=\pm y\) with \(R=0\) do not create a \(d\log(b\mp x)\) coefficient: taking the boundary residue first still gives zero. This is exactly the order prescribed by equation (A.1) of `Cosmology meets cohomology`.

## Decision

The reflected physical vertex has zero \(v_{\rm alg}\) coordinate under this boundary-residue readout. Hence the response plane is not generated by the \(r_b\)-orbit of the \(e_6\) anchor. The second response must come from the transverse odd route direction

\[
\beta_\perp=\alpha_{13}-\alpha_{14},
\]

rather than the orbit-odd direction

\[
\beta_{\rm orbit}=\alpha_{13}+\alpha_{14}.
\]

Equivalently, the reflection-orbit plane

\[
\mathbb Z\alpha_{12}\oplus\mathbb Z(\alpha_{13}+\alpha_{14})
\]

is invisible to the \(v_{\rm alg}\) boundary covector after the anchored \(e_6\) coordinate is removed.

## Claim boundary

This determines the zero/nonzero route choice. It does not normalize the nonzero transverse period to \(\pm1\); that still requires one residue computation on a wall representing \(\beta_\perp\).
