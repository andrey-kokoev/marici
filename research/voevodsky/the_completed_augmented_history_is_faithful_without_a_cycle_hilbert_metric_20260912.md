# The completed augmented history is faithful without a cycle Hilbert metric

## Question

Can the all-ratio completed carrier retain the cycle residue faithfully before a physical covariance or Hilbert norm on that residue is known?

## Claim boundary

Yes as a locally convex source observer. Use the symmetric all-ratio grade

\[
W_{\mathrm{all}}(a,b;j,k)=\log(abkp_jp_{j+1})
\]

and one cutoff-natural greedy forest in the universal labelled interval graph. The chord selector is continuous for every weighted \(\ell^1\) seminorm and extends to the completion. Appending it to common history is faithful. This constructs no cycle-sector Hilbert metric and no physical probability rule.

## Completed chord target

Let \(T\) be the greedy global forest and \(K=E_{\mathrm{all}}\setminus T\) its chord set. Define

\[
\mathcal Z_{\mathrm{all},\exp}
=
\bigcap_{\delta>0}
\ell^1(K,e^{\delta W_{\mathrm{all}}}).
\]

The chord selector

\[
Z(c)=(c_e)_{e\in K}
\]

obeys

\[
q_\delta^K(Zc)
=
\sum_{e\in K}|c_e|e^{\delta W_{\mathrm{all}}(e)}
\leq q_\delta^E(c).
\]

Thus it extends continuously and with seminorm operator bound one.

## Faithfulness after completion

Suppose \(\widehat B_{\mathrm{all}}c=0\). The completed interval-coverage derivative identifies this kernel with the completed cycle subspace, provided the boundary and interval-synthesis maps extend with the declared topology. On that subspace, fundamental-cycle reconstruction expresses every tree coefficient from the chord coefficients. Hence \(Zc=0\) implies \(c=0\).

Equivalently, finite-support faithfulness is exact, and completed faithfulness follows if the cycle-kernel identity is closed under projective completion. This closure is an explicit premise here; continuity of history alone does not prove it.

Under that premise, the augmented map

\[
(\widehat B_{\mathrm{all}},\widehat Z):
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{all},\exp}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{all}}\oplus
\mathcal Z_{\mathrm{all},\exp}
\]

is continuous and injective.

## Presentation independence

A different cutoff-natural forest gives another chord target. The forest-presentation groupoid supplies integral coordinate changes on finite cycle packets. If these changes are uniformly continuous for the projective grade, they extend and identify the completed chord targets. Without that uniform estimate, finite groupoid equivalence does not establish completed presentation independence.

## Metaphysical consequence

The faithful completed carrier need not force the invisible residue into the same Hilbert geometry as analytic history. Its two components have different established structures:

- common history has an \(L^2\) norm;
- route residue has a projective locally convex topology and exact integral coordinate changes.

Treating both as one Hilbert state would add a covariance not derived here.

## Disposition

A continuous completed cycle port exists immediately and yields conditional faithfulness. Two gates remain: prove the completed cycle-kernel identity and uniform continuity of forest-change matrices. Physical cycle covariance remains a separate calibration problem.
