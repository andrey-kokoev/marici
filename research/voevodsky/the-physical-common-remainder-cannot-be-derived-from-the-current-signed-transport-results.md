# The physical common remainder cannot be derived from the current signed-transport results

## Recorded conclusion

The present repository results do not derive the physical common-remainder identity required for the prolate-to-Tate absolute-Gram theorem.

Exact regulator transport proves the signed identity

\[
D_\alpha=\Theta_\alpha^*K_8\Theta_\alpha.
\]

It does not prove that the actual physical positive Tate and reference Grams admit a shared positive remainder. Such a remainder would necessarily satisfy

\[
B_\alpha^*B_\alpha
=
G_{\mathrm{phys},\alpha}^T-(D_\alpha)_+
=
G_{\mathrm{phys},\alpha}^0-(D_\alpha)_-.
\]

Its existence is equivalent to the additional operator inequalities

\[
(D_\alpha)_+\preceq G_{\mathrm{phys},\alpha}^T,
\qquad
(D_\alpha)_-\preceq G_{\mathrm{phys},\alpha}^0,
\]

together with equality of the two remainders.

These statements do not follow from:

- convergence of signed matrix coefficients;
- the eight-leg Krein realization;
- the reference Widom leading asymptotic;
- finite-packet coercivity; or
- exact physical-to-Hardy transport.

The fixed Krein splitting supplies some positive realization of \(D_\alpha\), but does not identify that realization with the residual parts of the physical Widom Grams. The common positive summand is invisible to the signed difference.

## Status

Accordingly:

\[
\boxed{
\text{The physical common-remainder identity is not derived.}}
\]

Prolate-to-Tate bulk removal and the absolute-Gram theorem remain open at this exact point. Any assertion that the identity is already proved would be circular or unsupported by the current repository results.
