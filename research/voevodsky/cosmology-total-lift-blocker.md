# Total lift blocker for the relative residue cocycle

## Question

Does the minimal material constructed so far contain an incoming total differential whose image is the relative residue cocycle

\[
\Xi_{\log}+(-\sigma_{123})?
\]

## Claim boundary

This packet tests only the minimal total complex generated by the already constructed logarithmic denominator carrier and ordered three-wall Čech residue cone. It does not exclude future progress after adding a sourced resolved/Rees exceptional generator, a full logarithmic Čech--de Rham chain map, or a Cayley--Menger face cone.

## Disposition

The target relative cocycle is closed at residue level. To make it a chain-level lift or boundary in the minimal total complex, an incoming generator must map to the degree-two pair

\[
(\Xi_{\log},-
\sigma_{123}).
\]

No such incoming generator is available in the current minimal material:

- degree-one logarithmic denominator generators are closed and do not hit \(\Xi_{\log}\);
- the ordered three-wall Čech nerve has no 3-simplex whose boundary can hit \(\sigma_{123}\);
- no resolved/Rees exceptional generator or Cayley--Menger face generator has been sourced.

The checker represents the incoming matrix to rows \((\Xi_{\log},-\sigma_{123})\) as the two-row zero-column matrix. Over \(\mathbb F_{101}\) and \(\mathbb F_{103}\), the coefficient rank is \(0\), while augmenting by the target vector \((1,1)\) gives rank \(1\). Therefore the total lift equation is inconsistent in the minimal complex.

This closes the local denominator investigation at the current source boundary. The sequence of tests has now covered the pure logarithmic complex, bounded rational pole-depth search, local double-residue obstruction, ordered pair-face residue vector, ordered Čech residue cancellation, and minimal total-lift equation. Further progress requires new source data rather than another enlargement of the same minimal carrier.
