# Discrete proof-trace completion terminates; higher cells still need authority

Restrict surplus consumption on the fixed primitive source interval to steps of 1/2 or 1, with a frozen nonnegative budget c. A proof trace is a word in these two positive step labels. Candidate path-level rewrites merge adjacent `(1/2,1/2)->(1)` and sort `(1/2,1)->(1,1/2)`. Both preserve total consumed surplus and the final Farkas certificate. The lexicographic measure (word length, number of half-before-one inversions) strictly decreases; an irreducible word has all units before at most one half, so its total consumption determines its unique normal TRACE. Fresh `check_discrete_rewrite_completion.py` checks all 127 words of length at most six, 258 individual reductions and 84 branching critical sources, finding unique normal traces. A 3/2 budget admits `(1,1/2)` but rejects `(1,1)`.

This supplies a TERMINATING finite-grid **candidate** for comparing rewrite traces and fixes the arbitrary-rational-halving nontermination of the previous unrestricted grammar. It does not retroactively make `(1/2,1/2)` and `(1)` identical historical executions. Merge and swap must be declared as higher comparison cells, typed by the primitive source rows, and admitted through source-rooted support; endpoint equality alone cannot grant authority. Even a unique normal trace does not validate an analytic completion or arbitrary fine continuation.

The next exact gate is therefore not more exhaustive enumeration alone. Freeze the proposed 3-cell boundaries and support DAG, test at least one real branching diamond for path-cell compatibility (not just common endpoint), and refuse a self-asserted merge cell or missing primitive bound. If only the normalized endpoint contract is needed, the current result is sufficient on the discrete fixed-source stratum; the proof-history contract requires this stronger admission.
