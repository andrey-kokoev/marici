# Rational-function graphs and an explicit multiplicativity filler

## Question and conventions

Execute the higher-cycle boundary task from the freshly read Hodge workbench. Use the cubical model square=P1 minus {1}, with faces y=0,infinity, and

    partial = sum_i (-1)^(i-1) (partial_i^0 - partial_i^infinity).

The group z^p(X,n) is generated by codimension-p algebraic cycles on X times square^n meeting every face properly, modulo cubical degeneracies. Standard higher-Chow definitions and proper pushforward of cycles are imported, not derived from finite matrices. SCC obligations are face admissibility, boundary compatibility and witness-composition coherence.

## Actual degree-one graph

Let W be an integral codimension-(p-1) subvariety of smooth X and f a nonzero rational function on W. Take the closure of its graph in W times P1, then restrict to X times square. Equivalently one may use the normalization/graph construction and proper pushforward. If f=1 the graph lies entirely in the deleted face and its restricted cycle is zero.

For f not identically 0 or infinity, the graph is not contained in either face. Its intersections have the required codimension; face multiplicities pushed to X are the usual zero and pole valuations of f. Exceptional components mapping into smaller-dimensional subsets do not contribute to the pushed codimension-p cycle. Therefore

    partial Gamma_f = div_W(f) pushed into X.

This formula is the standard graph/valuation calculation. It supplies an actual admissible witness for the elementary rational equivalence, not an alpha lift. Degree-one admissibility involves only the two faces; higher-degree proper intersection is an additional condition.

For X=W=P1, p=1 and f=t^m with nonzero integer m, this is completely explicit. The graph of the morphism P1 -> P1 is closed; delete the points where t^m=1. If m>0, the zero and pole fibers have multiplicity m at 0 and infinity. For m<0 their roles exchange. Thus

    partial Gamma_(t^m) = m[0] - m[infinity].

For f=1 the chain is empty and boundary zero. For constant f=2 the graph P1 times {2} is nonempty and also has boundary zero. It is not a cubical degeneracy: degree-one degeneracies here are pullbacks of codimension-one cycles of P1, supported on points times square. Boundary zero does not distinguish absence from a present closed witness. Neither case is a nonalgebraic object.

## Important failure of a naive chain map

The rational-function witness group uses multiplication as addition: [fg]=[f]+[g]. Graph cycles do NOT obey Gamma_(fg)=Gamma_f+Gamma_g as degree-one cycles. Their boundaries agree, but their difference is a closed chain. Thus the graph recipe on functions cannot simply be declared a homomorphism from R^p to z^p(-,1). Relations need actual higher chains and, globally, face/moving control.

This is a precise composition gate, rather than an unspecified higher-coherence demand.

## An explicit degree-two filler over the field

Take a,b in C* with a,b,ab different from 1. In square^2, take the closure/restriction of the parametrized curve

    y=t,   z=(t-ab)/(t-a).

This is an integral codimension-one cycle T_(a,b) for the nondegenerate constants tested below. Its four face intersections are:

    y=0:        z=b,
    y=infinity: z=1, excluded from square,
    z=0:        y=ab,
    z=infinity: y=a.

All retained face points avoid 0,1,infinity. No corner survives, so all face intersections are proper. Each has multiplicity one when a,b are nonzero and b differs from 1. With the frozen sign convention,

    partial T_(a,b) = [b] - [ab] + [a].

For a=2,b=3 the boundary is [3]-[6]+[2]. It is nonzero as a free degree-one cycle, yet is explicitly a boundary. All three points have zero degree-zero boundary, so partial squared is zero.

This construction is on Spec(C), p=1. Exterior product with an actual codimension-(p-1) support W produces the corresponding constant-function relation over W. It does not prove that arbitrary nonconstant f,g on W admit the same global formula without extra terms. On an open subset where f,g,fg are invertible and avoid 1, the formula describes the generic candidate; closures across removed divisors must be checked separately. No correction is silently discarded.

## Consequence for the Hodge attack

There is now a source-derived degree-one realization, and a genuine degree-two cell enforcing one multiplication relation. The sequence is

    rational functions -> graph witnesses -> higher relation chains,

not a single equivalence between a class and an object. The chain-level multiplicativity failure is real, but in this controlled sector it is repaired by an algebraic filler. Calling the uncorrected difference an obstruction would wrongly reject these algebraic witnesses.

Likewise the nonempty boundary-zero constant graph gives a concrete instance of readout blindness in the algebraic source itself. It supports the distinction sought by the domain/darkness conjectures, but does not separate alpha from algebraic classes. A useful defect must survive the actual allowed relation chains, not merely be nonzero before quotienting.
