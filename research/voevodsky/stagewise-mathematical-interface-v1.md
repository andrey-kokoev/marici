## Validity and universes

Formal evidence is a named universe-indexed judgement in the safe Cubical Agda source and its admitted dependencies. Reification of complete packages can raise a universe; a record must state its level rather than assuming all packages belong to one unqualified Type.

Analytic evidence is an owner-qualified mathematical argument with explicit standard premises, domains, topology and normalization. It is not upgraded to a formal proof by finite testing. Conversely, a finite source-bound formal theorem does not establish an analytic completion or physical premise. The common interface retains both policies; no classical/Cubical interpretation equivalence is supplied merely by giving them common field names.

A stage records its actual type/universe, source, guards, operations, phase/index, task/resources, retained state, proof policy and completion. A map records its actual formula and admitted domain, operation squares, recovery or task quotient, dependent payload action and evidence. An expected output inserted without the source operation and intermediate witnesses fails the realization obligation.
