## Exact source expansion

Since C is orthogonal and C^4=I,

  D^T D=2I-C^2-C^(-2)=2(I-C^2).

The all-ones matrix kills D, so GD=10D. Define the orthogonal projector
P_odd=(I-C^2)/2. Then

  H(x)=5 x^T(I-C^2)x=10||P_odd x||^2.

Both odd and even source spaces have dimension six. The source energy is
exactly the norm of the part changed by the half-turn. Its expansion contains
an identity term and a half-turn correlation, not logarithmic prime-power
translations or von Mangoldt weights. Negative cross terms from the half-turn
are not, by themselves, the arithmetic prime terms.

This explanation is stronger than the previous information-loss objection:
the repaired six-coordinate reader now recovers the complete energy, and that
energy still has this parity-only form.

## Direct Fourier realization

For the self-dual additive Fourier operator F, F^2 is reflection R. Thus
D_F=F-F^(-1) satisfies

  D_F^* D_F=2(I-R)=4 P_odd.

The direct all-test extension with the source coefficient scale is

  H_F(f)=(10/4)||D_F f||_2^2=10||f_odd||_2^2.

In particular it vanishes for every even Schwartz test. No multiplicative
normalization changes that zero. Even if a different positive output metric
were chosen for D_F f, its norm would still vanish whenever D_F f=0.
The source-to-Fourier representation identifies the skew generator, not a
nondegenerate positive energy on all even functions.

## What the test decides

The current rotor energy does not become the prime-zero quadratic form under
the direct skew-Fourier extension. The available arithmetic packet supplies
specific negative prime-power correlations that are absent from its source
expansion. The six-coordinate repair fixed loss of energy information, not
this arithmetic mismatch.

This does not exclude a different source-derived operator or a different
specified test-space map. Such a proposal must account for the even test
sector and produce the logarithmic translations and their weights from its
source. Adding those terms from the known explicit formula would define the
target form rather than derive it.
