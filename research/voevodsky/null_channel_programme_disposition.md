# Null-channel programme disposition

## Question

Did interpreting the physical channel as a link to a null sector determine the two-bit ambient cusp extension?

## Claim boundary

This disposition separates the completed algebraic and detector constructions from the unresolved source comparison. It makes no physical or geometric promotion.

## Surviving construction

The Cut-null interpretation produced a coherent conditional architecture:

1. the relevant finite sector is a two-bit quotient;
2. two conductor wall characters form a minimal jointly faithful complementary probe;
3. pairwise photon parities realize those characters as a number-resolving POVM coarse-graining;
4. the declared finite loss and dark-count response is exactly invertible;
5. single-rail and number-conserving dual-rail encoders realize the four conductor classes;
6. direct fixed-codeword likelihood gives a 35-trial sufficient classification bound under the exact model;
7. conclusive two-count events identify arbitrary classical codeword mixtures;
8. dual-rail complementary settings expose coherent leakage.

Each statement is conditional on the conductor class already entering the encoder.

## Failed governing conjecture

The conjecture that this channel by itself resolves the cusp parity fails. The cusp parity space

\[
K_{\rm cusp}
\]

and conductor quotient

\[
Q_{\rm cond}
\]

remain distinct typed objects. The missing arrow is

\[
\phi:K_{\rm cusp}\longrightarrow Q_{\rm cond}.
\]

Equal cardinality, equal abstract group structure, exchange symmetry, and successful optical decoding do not construct \(\phi\). The known physical Cut map is zero and cannot do so.

## Exact stopping condition

This branch reopens only if one of the following is materialized:

- a globally ordered integral Picard basis embedding the paired cusp thimbles;
- integral pairings of those thimbles with duals of \(e_6\) and \(v_{\rm alg}\);
- a source-derived physical interaction whose two calibrated responses are proved equal to those pairings;
- an explicit comparison from the physical parameter/path system to the globally marked quartic family.

Raw optical data without such a coupling test the decoder but not the cusp parity.

## Disposition

The null-channel conjecture is retained as a complete conditional detector architecture and rejected as an independent derivation of the ambient parity class. No nonredundant source-internal continuation remains: further encoder or estimator refinements cannot supply the missing geometric comparison.
