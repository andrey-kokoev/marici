# Width 8→9: what is transported?

**Table fact.** The greedy upper-table calculation in `checkers/ramsey_table_cube_differences.py` forms each placement as the product of ascending pairs `(a_i,a_i+1)`. It subtracts earlier *literal rows* before recording new pieces. At width nine these have sizes 8,4,4,4; eleven upper rows lie outside them. It does not assign graph vertex IDs to corners of different placements.

**Proposed bridge axiom (role transport).** Treat a corner first as an occurrence `(a,r)`, not as the row `r` alone. If placement `a` is translated to adjacent legal placement `a+d`, transport `(a,r)` to `(a+d,r+d)`. Identify these occurrences for the purpose of assigning the newcomer label. The three bits `r-a` are invariant. Root this coordinate convention at the width-eight cube. This is the coordinatewise order-preserving bijection of the directed pairs; it is NOT an identity on their overlap. Use the three width-nine fresh squares and their reflected copies as face charts. Taking intersections gives the twelve edges and eight corners of the abstract cube. Read the resulting 26 cells by inclusion of corner labels. Neither selecting these patches nor using their inclusion as red incidence is specified by the display count alone.

**Checks.** `ramsey_excel_successor_chart_audit.py` compares all 56 incidences with the independent 35-vertex constructor. `ramsey_excel_chart_axioms.py` shows that named reversible swaps plus the fixed reverse-complement mirror alone allow eight different chart choices. Only one preserves all three adjacent-placement translations.

**Where the lift stops.** The half-turn acts on the three bits by reverse-complement and on cells by the same map, leaving wildcards as wildcards. It preserves incidence by construction. `ramsey_excel_mirror_core.py` applies it to all eight cube-symmetry translates of the verified 76-edge internal coloring (the eight labeled completions classified in ledger 4348). It fixes none. It pairs valid colorings rather than acting as a color-preserving symmetry of any one of them. The unsupplied internal coloring cannot be selected by demanding this fixed symmetry. This is not an obstruction to an equivariant *family*, nor to another lift with changed marks.
