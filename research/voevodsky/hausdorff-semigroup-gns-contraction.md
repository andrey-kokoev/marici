# Hausdorff semigroup and the GNS contraction

## Question

Can the three Hausdorff cones and finite-difference hierarchy be generated by one positive contraction with coherent heat-scale composition?

## Claim boundary

The positive-contraction realization and its finite atomic semigroup fixture are constructed. Positivity of the explicit arithmetic matrices and descent of separate step-size GNS spaces to one semigroup remain open.

## Undifferentiated moment chart

For positive \(t,h\), define

\[
b_n(t,h)=H(t+nh).
\]

Under a positive Laplace representation, set

\[
y=e^{-h\lambda}\in[0,1].
\]

Then

\[
b_n(t,h)=
\int_0^1 y^n\,d\mu_{t,h}(y).
\]

This chart uses only undifferentiated values of \(H\).

## Two cone families

Define

\[
M_y=(b_{i+j})
\]

and

\[
M_{1-y}=(b_{i+j}-b_{i+j+1}).
\]

Their quadratic forms test

\[
\int|p(y)|^2d\mu
\]

and

\[
\int(1-y)|p(y)|^2d\mu.
\]

The shifted Hankel cone for multiplication by \(y\) is the first cone evaluated at base point \(t+h\). Thus positivity over every positive base point supplies both

\[
0\leq Y_h\leq1.
\]

## GNS constructor

Use \(M_y\) to define a positive functional on polynomials. Quotient its null space and complete. Multiplication by \(y\) descends to a positive contraction \(Y_h\), and

\[
b_n(t,h)=
\langle v_t,Y_h^n v_t\rangle.
\]

The localizing cone for \(1-y\) gives the upper contraction bound.

This single operator generates the moment and finite-difference charts. Setting

\[
X_h=1-Y_h,
\]

we obtain

\[
D_{k,h}H(t)
=
\langle v_t,X_h^k v_t\rangle.
\]

Hence the earlier three cones for \(1,x,1-x\) are coordinate shadows of one positive contraction.

## Cross-step coherence

A separate GNS construction for every \(h\) is insufficient. The contractions must inhabit one Hilbert space and satisfy

\[
Y_0=1,
\]

\[
Y_{h_1+h_2}=Y_{h_1}Y_{h_2},
\]

with strong continuity in \(h\). Only then does semigroup theory produce a positive self-adjoint generator \(L\) with

\[
Y_h=e^{-hL}.
\]

The representing measure is the spectral measure of \(L\) at the cyclic vector. Thus the positive measure is reconstructed from coherent contractions rather than assumed.

## Arithmetic advantage

Every matrix entry is an undifferentiated value

\[
H(t+nh).
\]

Prime completion therefore uses ordinary Gaussian tail bounds rather than bounds multiplied by derivative powers of \(\log n\). The finite-difference factors appear only after the GNS contraction is constructed.

## Exact fixture

For two rational contraction eigenvalues and positive rational weights, the checker verifies:

- the Hankel Gram factor;
- the \(1-y\) localizing Gram factor;
- the common contraction representation of finite differences;
- five semigroup powers;
- detection of a signed atomic weight by a negative Hankel determinant.

## Categorical interpretation

The objects \(Y_h\) should form a functor from the additive monoid of nonnegative heat scales to positive contractions:

\[
(\mathbb R_{\geq0},+)
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Contr}_+(\mathcal H).
\]

The moment matrices are representable observers of this functor. Cross-step coherence is therefore the missing descent condition joining the independently constructed Hausdorff fibers.

This is a sharper identity-generator statement: the generator \(L\) produces every scale contraction, every moment sequence, every finite difference, and—through Bernstein and the entire-kernel nerve—every Gram packet.

## Disposition

The most compressed positive constructor is no longer an arbitrary J-fraction. It is one strongly continuous positive-contraction semigroup, with the J-fraction appearing as the tridiagonal representation of its generator or contraction in a cyclic polynomial basis. The unresolved arithmetic task is to prove the two matrix cones from the explicit completed \(H\) and then prove cross-step coherence on one common GNS space.
