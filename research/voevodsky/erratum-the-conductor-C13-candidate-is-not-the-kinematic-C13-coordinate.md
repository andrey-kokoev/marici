# Erratum: the conductor C13 candidate is not the kinematic C13 coordinate

## Collision

Two unrelated uses of the string `C13` were conflated.

### Conductor programme

In `C13_moves_from_the_conductor_to_its_elliptic_square_root_cover.md`, `C13` is the identifier of a rejected conjectural claim:

> the rational function \(g(m)\) directly determines a theta characteristic whose quadratic refinement equals cusp parity.

Its surviving elliptic cover

\[
\Sigma_g:\quad \xi^2=g(m)
\]

belongs to the cosmology conductor, Bunch--Davies orientation, and Picard-parity programme.

### Kinematic positive geometry

In the scattering-amplitudes transcript, \(C_{13}\) is a positive source/support coordinate in

\[
X_{13}+X_{24}=C_{13}.
\]

It belongs to four-point kinematic space and the associahedral interval.

There is no recorded source map identifying these objects.

## Retraction

The proposed comparison

\[
\Sigma_g\longrightarrow\operatorname{SpecCover}(S_L(p))
\]

is unsupported and is withdrawn. Likewise, no identity relating \(g(m)\) to the Schwarz discriminant is licensed by prior research.

The abstract universal Schwarz spectral cover remains algebraically valid, but it has no established relation to the conductor cover.

## Search result

Repository provenance distinguishes the two meanings clearly:

- `research/voevodsky/results/C13_theta_characteristic_axiom_test.json` defines `C13` as a sentence-valued conjecture identifier;
- `research/sources/nima/talks/scattering-amplitudes-and-dualities-at-infinity/transcript.md` uses \(C_{13}\) as a positive kinematic constant.

`pnpm pdf:search` found no indexed PDF containing `C13`, `spectral cover`, or `eigenline`. Broader matches supplied only generic positive-geometry background.

## Correct frontier

The potentially useful prior datum is the kinematic relation itself:

\[
X_{13}+X_{24}=C_{13},
\qquad
X_{13},X_{24},C_{13}>0.
\]

To connect it with rung-four Schwarz positivity, one still needs a source-derived map from the RH primitive/prime-prime observation square to these kinematic coordinates. No elliptic conductor cover currently supplies that map.
