# Prior work narrows G4 sewing to the reciprocal radial swap with a Real comparison gate

## Question

Does prior radial work determine the candidate G4 sewing operator strongly enough to reduce the missing comparison arrow?

## Claim boundary

It determines the conservative radial carrier, opposite differential orientations, maximal-isotropic wall relation, and the unique phase-decorated reciprocal swap compatible with that relation. The remaining source gate is a Real fiber comparison identifying this swap with the retained Fourier--Poisson sewing and equating analytic transpose with Hermitian adjoint. This is a narrowed candidate, not an owner-authorized G4 declaration.

## Problem

The retained response graph has a unitary Fourier--Poisson sewing \(W_{\rm FP}\), but the current G4 contract exposes no operator \(W_{G4}\). Prior radial packets nevertheless constrain any conservative candidate.

## Bold conjecture

Any unitary sewing of two positive half-line histories can serve as the conservative G4 reciprocal operator.

## Named rivals

1. The Green boundary form forces opposite derivative orientations and a phase-decorated channel swap.
2. Two copies with the same derivative orientation can be repaired by changing only the boundary metric.
3. Equality of boundary dimensions suffices to identify radial and Fourier--Poisson sewing.

## Conservative radial double

The minimal carrier is

\[
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{rad}}^+\oplus
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{rad}}^-.
\]

Its differential is

\[
\mathbb D
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\partial_t&0\\
0&-\partial_t
\end{pmatrix},
\]

and its boundary Green matrix is

\[
J_\partial
=
\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

For a unit phase \(u\), the maximal-isotropic wall relation is

\[
\Lambda_u=\{(c,uc):c\in\mathbb C\}.
\]

## Candidate reciprocal sewing

The phase-compatible channel swap is

\[
W_u
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&u^{-1}\\
u&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

It satisfies

\[
W_u^2=I,
\qquad
W_u^*W_u=I,
\qquad
W_u\Lambda_u=\Lambda_u.
\]

It exchanges the two differential orientations and reverses the boundary Green form:

\[
W_u^*J_\partial W_u=-J_\partial.
\]

The minus sign is required by reciprocal orientation reversal; treating \(W_u\) as an ordinary symplectic isometry would erase the oriented Green sign.

This rejects the bold conjecture. A general unitary does not preserve \(\Lambda_u\), exchange the two oriented equations, or implement the required anti-isometry.

## Real comparison gate

On the doubled Hilbert carrier, the metric return is a Hermitian adjoint. On the projective test/dual rigging, the path return is an analytic transpose. Prior radial work states that they agree only after a Real comparison exchanges the reciprocal channels and has trivial mixed square.

Thus \(C_{\rm FP}\) must include an antilinear or Real structure \(\mathfrak r\) satisfying the declared source conventions and an exact return square of the form

\[
C_{\rm FP}\,A^{\rm tr}
=
A^{\dagger}C_{\rm FP}
\]

on the common graph domain. Equality after scalar readout is insufficient.

## Strongest falsification attempt

Rival 2 fails because equal differential orientations produce a boundary form with one sign and no nontrivial maximal-isotropic wall graph. Rival 3 fails because many abstract unitaries have the same dimension and spectrum while acting differently on labels, traces, and the Green form.

The surviving candidate is unique only after fixing:

- ordered positive and reciprocal channels;
- phase \(u\);
- opposite derivative orientations;
- wall relation \(\Lambda_u\);
- boundary orientation convention.

## Disposition

Prior work narrows the missing G4 sewing operator to the reciprocal radial swap \(W_u\). The external comparison problem is no longer an unconstrained search for \(W_{G4}\); it is the construction of one source-derived Real fiber map \(C_{\rm FP}\) intertwining \(W_{\rm FP}\) with \(W_u\) and analytic transpose with Hermitian adjoint. G4 owner authority is still required to confirm that this radial double is its intended conservative carrier.
