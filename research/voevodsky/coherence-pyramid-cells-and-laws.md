# Coherence cells and laws for the partial equipment

## Question

Which higher cells and equations are required before the typed partial composition interface can be called a partial double category or equipment?

## Claim boundary

This packet specifies and tests coherence obligations on admitted symbolic fixtures. It does not prove that every analytic Green, gauge, or closed-form fixture supplies the required certificate equivalences.

## Cells

The representation requires six cell classes:

- associators compare admitted parenthesizations with equivalent certificate bundles;
- left and right unitors normalize typed identities;
- interchange compares horizontal-then-vertical with vertical-then-horizontal composition;
- Beck–Chevalley cells compare pullback and amalgamation routes;
- completion-comparison cells compare finite composition with closed-form completion.

A commuting boundary square does not construct any of these cells by itself.

## Laws

- The pentagon requires equality of the two certificate-transport routes across four composable arrows.
- The triangle requires agreement between the associator and both unitors.
- The interchange hexagon requires both orders of a typed two-by-two grid to have the same outer certificate.
- Beck–Chevalley pasting requires component cells to reproduce the composite cell.
- Completion pasting additionally requires a common closed domain, stable radical, and positive composite coercivity.

## Invertibility gates

Beck–Chevalley is invertible only when the comparison map is an isomorphism and its inverse preserves the transported certificates. Completion comparison is invertible only with closed-domain equivalence and positive reduced minimum modulus in both directions.

These conditions prevent a formally invertible boundary map from erasing Green positivity, gauge kernels, radicals, or coercivity.

## Executable falsification

The checker verifies symbolic pentagon, triangle, and interchange normal forms on admitted certificate bundles. It admits a fully certified Beck–Chevalley fixture and refuses five hostile cells:

1. associator from boundary equality alone;
2. interchange from commuting maps alone;
3. Beck–Chevalley from an uncertified square;
4. assumed Beck–Chevalley invertibility;
5. completion comparison inferred from core equality.

## Disposition

The coherence leaf is resolved as a bounded cell-and-law presentation. The result is still a candidate partial equipment: the tests establish the signature and rejection semantics, not closure for every sourced analytic object. The countermodel leaf must now run the combined representation against known finite and unbounded hostile examples before integration.
