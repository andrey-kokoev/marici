# Geometric chart cocycles do not supply total PN-domain transport

This tests the connected `fixed-geometry-total-PN-domain-transport` rival under `face-chart-transition-descent`. The broader transition/descent conjecture remains distinct.

## Source/target and lower gates

Use the canonical regular P chamber. Keep C0 and M1 fixed and interchange the geometric vertices named M0 and C1. Both full P charts and their PN transfers must validate independently through the existing source API. Their selected PN face is the same geometric triangle, their C0 point is identical and their complete five-reference **vertex sets**, including C2, agree. Their named C/M assignments do not agree.

For two vertex frames f and g, the geometry-only label transition is inverse(g) composed with f. Its target evaluation fixes each world vertex. For three frames the intermediate g cancels, giving the cocycle. The formal proof establishes these cells for arbitrary vertex isomorphisms and, when the outside C2 is fixed, for the full five-reference extension. Runtime controls check all 24 body markings, all pair cells, all body triple cells and the applicable same-C0 full-reference triples.

These are deliberately successful lower gates. They do not say the geometric permutation is a source-admitted role transition.

## The first failed typed cell

The source role assignment has C vertices C0,C1 and M vertices M0,M1. The target has C at the original C0,M0 positions and M at the original C1,M1 positions.

The facet opposite original C0 is PN-eligible in both charts: its source and target M count is two. This is the shared actual PN interface. But the facet opposite original C1 has source M count two and target M count one. It is a valid source PN port (possibly after a role-preserving reader reframe), not a target PN port.

Let the source and target domain types be pairs (geometric face, proof of the respective two-M PN predicate). A total transition over geometric face identity must send this lost source face to itself and produce its target predicate. That predicate reduces to 1=2, contradiction. The safe Cubical no-map theorem proves this directly. It excludes any native implementation satisfying these constraints because even the underlying typed function does not exist.

This is not only a failure to find a path or a depth bound. It is an explicit law-domain obstruction after the geometric cocycle gate succeeds.

## What survives

The common-domain span retains both source and target PN certificates and fixes the face. A partial identity on that intersection exists mathematically; it is not refuted. Nor is this a proof that the full role-changing source transition or partial span is source-admitted. That admission and its proper overlap composition remain on the parent conjecture.

The result does not refute all chart interpretations, higher WG comparison objects that retain different law domains, context-indexed or partial adapters, the uniform WG chart task package, or Ramsey forcing. It forbids declaring a changed role assignment to be a **total standard-PN gauge equivalence over fixed geometry** merely because reference sets and cocycles agree.

## Evidence discipline

Fresh source controls retain both full reference packets, PN and reader witnesses, the explicit vertex permutation, the lost geometric face and the shared interface. The kernel proves the geometry cocycle and no-total-domain-map cells, but does not interpret the sector JavaScript. Source bindings remain runtime checked, not inferred from the abstract finite theorem.
