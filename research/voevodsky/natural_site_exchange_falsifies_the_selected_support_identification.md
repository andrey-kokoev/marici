# Natural site exchange falsifies the selected support identification

## Question

Is the conditional identification of conductor epsilon flip with relative site exchange compatible with the audited meanings of those actions?

## Claim boundary

This is a symmetry audit of the proposed support assignment. It does not exclude nonsymmetric restrictions justified by additional source structure.

## Correction of the conditional hypothesis

The enhanced conductor audit states

\[
D_\epsilon J=J(-M_1),
\]

where \(M_1\) is the first conductor root swap. Thus epsilon flip is tied to root swap 1, up to the recorded orientation twist. It is not mechanically identified with exchange of the two wall labels.

The prior conditional count of four assignments remains a correct calculation under its stated hypothesis, but that hypothesis conflicts with the natural source semantics unless a new map proves that relative site exchange is transported to first-wall root swap.

## Natural conductor site exchange

Exchanging the two conductor walls swaps the first two enhanced characters and fixes their product. In the enhanced coordinate basis this is

\[
S=\begin{pmatrix}
0&1&0\\
1&0&0\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

On the four occurrence columns,

\[
S\Phi_{++}=\Phi_{++},
\qquad
S\Phi_{+-}=\Phi_{-+},
\qquad
S\Phi_{-+}=\Phi_{+-},
\qquad
S\Phi_{--}=\Phi_{--}.
\]

Therefore the selected support pair

\[
\{q_1,q_2\}=\{\Phi_{++},\Phi_{-+}\}
\]

is not invariant under natural site exchange: it maps to

\[
\{\Phi_{++},\Phi_{+-}\}.
\]

## Relative site exchange

The relative site exchange fixes \(S_3\) and swaps the two active point strata

\[
\{p_{23},p_{13}\}
\longrightarrow
\{p_{23},p_{13}\}.
\]

Hence its active support pair is invariant as a set, unlike the selected conductor pair.

## Falsification result

No bijection from the selected conductor pair to the active relative pair can intertwine the natural site exchanges because the domain subset is not invariant. Signs cannot repair failure of setwise invariance.

There are three surviving revisions:

1. enlarge the conductor support selection to a site-exchange-invariant subset;
2. derive a nonsymmetric source restriction that selects one wall/root direction and explicitly relinquishes site-exchange covariance;
3. construct a nontrivial transport proving that relative site exchange corresponds to conductor root swap 1 rather than conductor wall exchange.

The third revision requires new geometric evidence and cannot be inferred from the matching two-element orbits.

## Disposition

The natural equivariant identification is rejected. The four-candidate conditional fiber is retained only as a formal result under an unsupported transport hypothesis. The current two-support completion is exact algebraically but has no site-exchange-covariant relative-support interpretation.
