# No uniform bounded proof grid survives all positive rational row scales

Take the square's one-row proof of `x<=1`, with x-upper multiplier 1. For ANY fixed upper magnitude cap M, rescale that SOURCE row normal and bound by 1/p for integer p>M; exact transport requires multiplier p, beyond M. For ANY fixed reduced denominator cap D, rescale by prime p>D; exact transport requires multiplier 1/p with denominator p. Both new packets certify exactly x<=1 and keep the product of multiplier and bound unchanged. Fresh `check_uniform_grid_scale_obstruction.py` checks caps 2,3,5,10,20 and records the general algebraic argument.

Therefore a single finite raw coordinate envelope cannot be covariant under ALL positive rational row isomorphisms. This does not prevent finite witness-relative envelopes: take each old coordinate grid, divide by the row witness scale, permute according to its retained root bijection, and leave the surplus grid fixed. That construction is bounded PER WITNESS but has no uniform global size/denominator caps. The mathematical row manifest must be included in the coverage claim; a source event grant is not carried by it.
