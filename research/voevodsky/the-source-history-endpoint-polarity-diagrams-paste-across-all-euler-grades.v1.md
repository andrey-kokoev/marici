# The source, history, endpoint, and polarity diagrams paste across all Euler grades

## Question

Do the primitive, square, and connected observer tetrahedra share identical typed faces and assemble into one completed higher-stratum observer diagram?

## Claim boundary

For every prime \(p\) and grade \(k\ge1\), let \(U_{p,k}^{\mathrm{rec}}\) be the doubled source fiber, let \(H_{p,k}^{\mathrm{rec}}\) be the doubled translated half-density history fiber, let \(E_{p,k}=\mathbb C^2\) be the endpoint fiber, and let \(P_{p,k}=H_{p,k}^{\mathrm{rec}}\otimes\mathbb C_{\mathrm{pol}}^2\) carry the Clifford polarity action.

The source-to-history edge is \(\mathcal I_{p,k}(c_+,c_-)=k^{-1}p^{-k/2}(c_+\tau_{k\log p}\Phi,c_-\tau_{-k\log p}\Phi)\). The source-to-endpoint edge is \(V_{p,k}=\frac1{2k}\begin{pmatrix}1&p^{-k}\\p^{-k}&1\end{pmatrix}\). The history-to-endpoint edge is the pair of half-density traces \((M_-,M_+)\).

The first shared face commutes because \(\operatorname{Tr}_{\partial}\mathcal I_{p,k}=V_{p,k}\). The second shared face commutes because reflection exchanges \(\tau_{k\log p}\Phi\) with \(\tau_{-k\log p}\Phi\). The third shared face commutes because \(V_{p,k}\) is a linear combination of the identity and reciprocal slot exchange, so \(V_{p,k}R_U=R_UV_{p,k}\).

The physical polarity lift applies the same Clifford matrix to every \((p,k)\) fiber. Hence endpoint trace intertwines reciprocal history exchange with polarity exchange in each grade, completing the fourth face.

The relative Adams coefficients satisfy \(\rho_{rs}(p,k)=\rho_s(p,rk)\rho_r(p,k)\), so iterated grade transport gives identical coefficients along every factorization path. Fourier presentation transport is unitary inside each analytic fiber and leaves \((p,k)\) and the source coefficient fixed. Thus Adams and Fourier transports preserve the pasted faces.

The primitive and square incidences are Hilbert–Schmidt in their declared weighted source norms, while the connected tail is nuclear. Unitary reciprocal and Fourier transport preserve these ideals. Finite prime-grade pastings therefore extend through the declared projective and ideal completions.

The boundary-jet extension attaches along the common endpoint edge because its zeroth jet is the same endpoint evaluation and its reciprocal parity is even. The Green–Stokes–adjoint diagram attaches along the common history-to-endpoint edge because its boundary form uses the same complete trace, while the bounded linking operator occupies a separate skew response block.

## Disposition

Global analytic observer pasting is constructed as a bicategorical pasting complex. APCT, EPCT, HSPF, the central analytic interface, the reciprocal augmented extension, and the augmented GSAC diagram share identical typed faces on the source-generated response carrier. Strict simplex recognition is unnecessary because attenuation and projection edges are noninvertible. The remaining questions are theorem-strength comparisons connecting this analytic observer complex to an independent Xi divisor state, complement theorem, and positive confinement.
