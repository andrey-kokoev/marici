## 1. What each actual triangle computes today

The retained source cycles are

    left:  AB,BC,CA,
    right: BA,AD,DB.

Each primitive cyclic operator acts on its ordered triple by

    C(a,b,c)=(b,c,a), C³=I.

Neither evaluates primes, digamma integrals or zeros. Those arithmetic
operations are not produced simply by labeling the three entries differently.

For oriented cochain x=(AB,BC,CA,AD,DB), the restrictions are

    R_left x=(AB,BC,CA),
    R_right x=(-AB,AD,DB).

The source convention identifies BA=-AB while retaining the two directed
occurrences. This is an edge-orientation identification, not an explicit
formula identity.

Independent updates already fail to glue on x=(0,1,0,0,0): the left cycle
proposes AB=1 and the right proposes AB=0. The synchronized restriction

    x=(a,b,c,-b,-c)

makes the two local triples opposites and keeps their cycles compatible.
This provides an exact geometric/coefficient seam but no zeta arithmetic.
These cyclic cochain operators and the native L/G/N networks are different
retained protocols; the audit does not silently identify them.

## 2. What the RH triangles must compute instead

The required roles are independently sourced comparison routes:

**Arithmetic route:** on a shared test h, evaluate

    Q_arith(h)=endpoint(h)+gamma(h)+prime(h).

For the existing packet,

    g_i(u)=exp[-t(u-a_i)²/2], t=1/10, a_i=0,14,21,
    h_ij(u)=g_i(u)g_j(u).

Polarization gives

    h_ij=exp[-t(a_i-a_j)²/4] exp[-t(u-(a_i+a_j)/2)²].

Each source matrix entry is the corresponding scalar multiple of the live
endpoint/gamma/prime explicit formula. The certified arithmetic packet retains
all three term labels and encloses gamma and infinite prime tails. Its
three-test positivity is certified in that source convention.

**Spectral route:** evaluate the full zero divisor on the same test,

    Q_divisor(h)=(1/2) sum_rho m_rho h((rho-1/2)/i),

with common normalization, admitted test class and convergence convention.
For complex coefficients c, the packet test is

    h_c(u)=sum_ij conjugate(c_i)c_j g_i(u)g_j(u).

This is the analytic continuation of the polarized test. Only when the
spectral parameter u is real does it become |sum_i c_i g_i(u)|².
Using a sum of positive real-ordinate squares as the definition of the full
spectral route would remove exactly the off-critical issue we intend to test.

The formula source itself still carries its stated external normalization
verification boundary. Calling the displayed targets 'triangle roles' does
not discharge that boundary or construct native face operations.

## 4. Prevent an RH assumption in the zero route

A hypothetical off-critical quartet has parameters

    u=+/-20pi +/- i/4.

For h(u)=exp(-u²/10), its half-divisor evaluation is

    -2 exp[-((20pi)²-1/16)/10] <0,

because the cosine factor is cos(pi)=-1. Replacing those complex parameters
with their real ordinates instead gives

    +2 exp[-(20pi)²/10] >0.

The sign loss is exact. This is a hostile hypothetical divisor, **not a claim
that zeta has those zeros**. It establishes why a critical-line-only scout
cannot stand in for the full spectral triangle.

## 5. Same finite form in the two actual native structures

The proton uses Euclidean coordinates. The neutron store gives coefficients

    numerator_ij/sqrt(d_i d_j)

in physical orthonormal coordinates. In its stored numerator coordinates u,
the operator is numerator_ij/d_j and the metric is M=diag(1/d_i).
Replacing M by I would change the form and is not allowed.

For each carrier reconstruct

    W=[[0,0,N],[L,0,0],[0,G,0]],
    M_stage=diag(M,M,M),
    F=(M_stage W+W^dagger M_stage)/2.

F is the ordinary Hermitian matrix of the weighted signed form. Let
Ps=diag(L-N,G-N,0), choose three independent source columns X, and set
Y=(W-W diag(N,N,N))X. The checker establishes

    D=X^dagger M_stage X=Y^dagger M_stage Y,
    X^dagger F X=Y^dagger F Y=0,
    X^dagger F Y=D/2.

Therefore for any supplied Hermitian three-by-three G,

    B_G=X+Y D^-1 G,
    B_G^dagger F B_G=G.

The rounded arithmetic packet, rounded zero scout and hostile are transported
by this exact formula in both structures. Arithmetic and scout matrices happen
to coincide after30-decimal rounding; that is not an analytic full-divisor
identity. For the hostile, both structures preserve the negative observation
-3/5 on coefficient vector (1,1,1).

## 6. What survives, and what does not

The packet decoder

    A_p=D_p^-1 X_p^dagger M_p

satisfies A_p B_p=I. Consequently J=B_n A_p obeys

    J B_p=B_n

and preserves the signed comparison on that packet. This is genuine finite
form transport, with explicit retained matrices in the receipt.

But W_p B_p=Y_p, A_p Y_p=0, while W_n B_n=Y_n!=0. Thus

    J W_p B_p=0 != W_n J B_p.

This particular final-packet adapter discards native source/sink processing.
Its failure does not exclude every possible enlarged adapter.

There is also a global typed-stage obstruction:

    rank(L_p,G_p,N_p)=(12,3,1),
    rank(L_n,G_n,N_n)=(3,12,1).

An invertible stage-preserving intertwiner would require equal local ranks.
It cannot exist for these unchanged carriers. A mixed-stage identification,
restricted subspace or retained record-bank extension is a different proposal
and must be declared and tested, not inferred from equal arrow totals.

**Answer:** the same finite signed form survives in both. The same operation-
level RH comparison has not yet been realized in either actual pair of faces,
and cannot be transferred by the tested packet-only decoder.
