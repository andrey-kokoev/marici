## Viable triangle

For the six-short-normal product Delta, define

\[
A_{min}=[Rk\xrightarrow{\Delta}Rb]
\]

and

\[
(E_{min})_3=Ra\oplus Rk,\qquad(E_{min})_2=Rb,
\quad d(a)=b,\quad d(k)=\Delta b.
\]

The quotient E_min/A_min is the generic line generated by the class of a. The generic generator is deliberately not closed in E_min. Its normalized multiple has the closed lift

\[
\Delta a-k,\qquad d(\Delta a-k)=0.
\]

This resolves the iteration-2 contradiction without turning the nonzero source normalization into an exact class: the normalized generic datum survives in the quotient, while its nonzero connecting boundary b is retained in support.

A map into the actual target is controlled by three images

\[
a\mapsto\widetilde\vartheta,\qquad b\mapsto\beta,
\qquad k\mapsto W
\]

and the two equations

\[
d\widetilde\vartheta=\beta,
\qquad dW=\Delta\beta.
\]

These imply the complete chain-map equation. A morphism of support triangles therefore asks the normalization source's connecting class to map to beta; it does not ask beta to vanish.
