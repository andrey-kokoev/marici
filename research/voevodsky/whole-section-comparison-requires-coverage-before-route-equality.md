# Whole-section comparison requires coverage before route equality

The existing full-segment gate `verify_full_segment` verifies *each* candidate mesh against the owner-admitted migration: common `migration_binding`, checked source lifts at every vertex, ordered knots along the full public segment, and exact Farkas implications showing the current domain lies in that segment. Thus its source-lift interpolation is a full-domain section on the rank-one forced-zero case, not a collection of coincident sampled points. The four full-domain mesh presentations in `check_live_resource_cone.py` have different certificate digests and the identical affine formula `(u,u)->(u,0,0)` on the WHOLE declared base.

A comparison certificate between verified meshes must carry the exact owning root, both checked attachment packets and live generation handles, source formula and interval scope. Equality of source formula is well-defined only after both coverage and source-admission checks. Pairwise comparison on the common scope by restriction gives direct and staged routes the same extensional section mapping. This does not equate checkpoint identity, attachment digests, independent authority to advance, or cost histories. Public-domain restriction remains one-way; a restricted provider cannot widen to full-domain lifting.

Existing controls already check 56 parenthesizations and reject a stale provider in the nine-live-provider cone; the full-segment control separately rejects missing/negative coverage weights, wrong lift, unordered knots, foreign migration, off-line points and stale handles. These are not automatically a *single* checker of direct versus staged whole-section comparison under negative attachments. Next implement a bounded owner-local checker that recomputes each mesh's attachment gate, checks the full common-interval formula and direct/staged comparison, and refuses malformed coverage and mismatched owner root without publishing a head. An owner's verifier and live sessions should be called, not replaced by hashes. SCC classification: static coverage/face incidence then route/coherencer compatibility; point equality alone is insufficient.
