# The translated renormalized theta jets supply the canonical prime-power forward columns

For a prime power `(p,k)`, let

\[
L_{p,k}=k\log p,\qquad a_{p,k}=k^{-1}p^{-k/2}.
\]

Let `Phi^bulk(t)` be the Poisson-renormalized completed-theta jet and `E(t)`
the retained modular endpoint coefficient.  If `C` denotes the already
constructed translation-covariant Volterra/cut lift, define the stratified
column

\[
b_{p,k}(t)
=a_{p,k}\,\mathcal C\tau_{L_{p,k}}
\bigl(\Phi^{\rm bulk}(t),E(t)\bigr).
\]

This formula is source-derived: it uses the primitive valuation length, Euler
half-density, completed theta jet, modular endpoint split, translation, and the
existing cut lift.  No jet profile is fitted.

At the physical base point, `E(0)=0` and `Phi^bulk(0)=Phi`, so

\[
b_{p,k}(0)=a_{p,k}\mathcal C\tau_{L_{p,k}}\Phi,
\]

which is exactly the previously declared prime-power incidence column.  The
higher `t` derivatives obey the augmented `H--Phi` connection, and the
boundary pieces retain the coefficientwise modular cancellation before
history projection.

On every strict jet disc, translation is isometric in the object-indexed
transported topology and the Volterra lift has the established uniform local
bound. Since

\[
|a_{p,k}|\le2^{-1/2},
\]

the local column norms are cutoff-uniform.  If the valuation/Fock idempotents
are orthogonal and the cut lift intertwines them, the direct-sum forward
incidence is bounded with norm controlled by the base jet-lift norm.

The final clause is still conditional: existing analytic estimates do not by
themselves prove exact prime-label preservation.  Thus the individual
source-derived columns and physical-base recovery are constructed, while the
all-label diagonal operator remains conditional on the idempotent-intertwining
theorem.
