# Retaining the labelled source coordinate makes the global analytic--arithmetic joint graph complemented and closed

## Question

Does global assembly of the resolved response cell with labelled arithmetic, theta, connected-grade, and endpoint coordinates still face a closed-range obstruction when the common source coordinate is retained?

## Claim boundary

No for the retained joint-graph architecture. Any continuous family of labelled source maps has a complemented closed joint graph because projection to the retained source is a continuous inverse. This does not prove closed range of an output-only codiagonal pushout or bounded recovery after forgetting the source coordinate.

## Global source and coordinates

Let \(E\) be the declared labelled projective source core/completion. Collect all already continuous source maps into a product target:

$$
T=(T_\alpha)_\alpha:
E\longrightarrow
Y=\prod_\alpha Y_\alpha.
$$

The labels \(\alpha\) may include:

- prime and grade;
- theta label;
- shell and ordered pair;
- resolved radial/history response;
- endpoint and wall traces;
- primitive and square dual-rung coordinates;
- connected trace-class grades;
- archimedean Mellin response.

Use the product/projective topology declared for these rungs. Existing cutoff, Euler-majorant, nuclearity, and response estimates establish continuity of each coordinate and hence of \(T\) into the product target.

## Retained joint graph

Define

$$
\mathcal G_T
=
\{(x,Tx):x\in E\}
\subset E\oplus Y,
$$

with graph embedding

$$
J_Tx=(x,Tx).
$$

Projection to the first coordinate satisfies

$$
\pi_EJ_T=I_E.
$$

Consequently

$$
P_T=J_T\pi_E
$$

is a continuous idempotent on \(E\oplus Y\), with

$$
\operatorname{ran}P_T=\mathcal G_T.
$$

Therefore \(\mathcal G_T\) is complemented and closed.

No compact-output lower bound and no inversion of any individual coordinate map is required.

## Label and cutoff naturality

Because every coordinate retains its labels before codiagonalization, finite cutoff maps act diagonally:

$$
P_FJ_T=J_{T_F}P_F^E.
$$

Thus the finite joint graphs form a compatible projective system. If Adams or Fourier transports satisfy

$$
T' A=A_YT,
$$

the induced graph action is simply

$$
(x,Tx)\longmapsto(Ax,A_YTx),
$$

and remains inside the corresponding retained graph.

## Radical compatibility

Let the global positive graph form contain the faithful source term:

$$
\|(x,Tx)\|_{\rm graph}^2
=
\|x\|_E^2+
\sum_\alpha \|T_\alpha x\|_{Y_\alpha}^2,
$$

with the sum interpreted by the declared projective/weighted construction. If a graph vector is in the positive radical, then \(x=0\), hence every coordinate vanishes. Therefore the retained graph has zero radical and all graph transports descend automatically.

The same conclusion holds for a multi-rung positive family of seminorms as long as the source family separates points.

## Distinction from an output-only pushout

The output map

$$
T:E\to Y
$$

may be compact, have nonclosed range, or fail to be bounded below. The codiagonal quotient may also erase theta-label or prime-label directions. None of these defects affects closedness of \(J_T(E)\), but they prevent the inference

$$
\text{closed retained graph}
\Longrightarrow
\text{closed output-only range}.
$$

Thus any G4 constructor requiring source-forgetting Hilbert synthesis still has a separate closed-range gate. The faithful retained architecture does not.

## Consequence for the current frontier

The newly closed local response retract, labelled Adams identities, connected-grade nuclearity, and cutoff estimates can be assembled globally without a new closed-range proof, provided the G4 object is the already declared faithful retained joint graph.

The unresolved prime-cell work is therefore quadratic representation equality and Evans membership, not existence or closedness of the global retained graph.

## Disposition

The global labelled analytic--arithmetic joint graph is a complemented closed subspace with zero radical because it retains the source coordinate. Full source recovery is first-coordinate projection. Output-only codiagonalization remains potentially nonclosed and must not be substituted for this theorem.
