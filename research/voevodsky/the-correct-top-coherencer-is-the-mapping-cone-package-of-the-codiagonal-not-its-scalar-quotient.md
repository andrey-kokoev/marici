# The correct top coherencer is the mapping-cone package of the codiagonal, not its scalar quotient

## Failure of scalar descent

Let

\[
D:\mathcal G\to Y
\]

be the physical codiagonal from the faithful joint graph to the scalar or finite-port output. Since \(D\) is not injective, quotienting directly to \(Y\) forgets positive source directions.

The appropriate relative object is therefore not \(Y\) alone.

## Mapping-cone carrier

Retain the two-term complex

\[
\operatorname{Cone}(D)
=
\left[
\mathcal G
\xrightarrow{D}
Y
\right].
\]

Its cohomological data are

\[
H^{-1}
\operatorname{Cone}(D)
=
\ker D,
\]

\[
H^0
\operatorname{Cone}(D)
=
\operatorname{coker}D.
\]

Thus every source direction lost by scalarization remains present as relative kernel data.

## Positive structure

The faithful joint-graph energy remains on the degree-minus-one term. A Hodge or graph totalization may use

\[
Q_D
=
D+D^*,
\]

with Laplacian

\[
Q_D^2
=
\begin{pmatrix}
D^*D&0\\
0&DD^*
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

This is the correct place for Pythagorean orthogonality. It does not follow from the simplex boundary alone; it uses the adjoint supplied by the Green/Weyl mate.

The kernel directions are retained as harmonic or relative states rather than incorrectly assigned zero energy in the scalar output.

## Determinant line

The determinant functor sends the cone to

\[
\operatorname{Det}
\operatorname{Cone}(D)
=
\operatorname{Det}(Y)
\otimes
\operatorname{Det}(
\mathcal G
)^{-1},
\]

with the corresponding kernel/cokernel correction at noninvertible points.

A scalar Evans or Xi section is a coordinate of this oriented determinant line. It is not the whole cone.

At a simple crossing, the cone retains:

- the right-kernel state;
- the left-kernel covector;
- their determinant orientation;
- the Green pairing between them.

These are exactly the data lost by scalar determinant evaluation.

## Incidence chamber

A distinguished source-to-observer minor selects a line inside the determinant package. Its nonvanishing condition says that this line remains in an open Schubert chart.

It does not require the complete cone to be acyclic. It requires the chosen oriented incidence coordinate not to vanish off the seam.

Thus:

\[
\text{mapping cone}
=
\text{faithful relative carrier},
\]

\[
\text{determinant line}
=
\text{oriented scalarization},
\]

\[
\text{chamber condition}
=
\text{nonvanishing of the selected minor}.
\]

## 4-simplex role

The five tetrahedral facets should be functors into the same category of cone packages. Their shared triangles compare:

1. source incidence complexes;
2. reciprocal cone complexes;
3. Clark/polarity totalizations;
4. Green adjoint complexes;
5. determinant-line images.

The top 4-cell then states that forming cones, applying reciprocal mates, taking Green adjoints, and passing to determinant lines commute coherently.

This is stronger than equality of scalar outputs and weaker than identifying all internal states. It is the correct relative notion of coherence.

## Control interpretation

The cone is the system together with its unobservable and unreachable residual modes. A scalar transfer suppresses those modes; the cone retains them as explicit state defects.

The Hodge totalization supplies storage on the retained defects. The determinant reports transmission singularity. The chamber law prevents the selected transmission from rotating into a complementary channel.

## Concrete constructor contract

A genuine top coherencer must provide:

1. a closed codiagonal \(D\) on the faithful joint graph;
2. a closed adjoint \(D^*\) from the independently constructed Green mate;
3. the cone graph norm;
4. reciprocal equivalences of cone packages;
5. determinant-functor comparison maps;
6. compatibility of these maps on all ten shared triangles;
7. the 4-simplex pasting law;
8. a separate proof that the distinguished determinant minor stays in its open chamber.

## Disposition

The top coherencer can be represented simplicially only after replacing scalar Xi vertices by mapping-cone packages. The 4-simplex filler is then a coherence among relative cone constructions, while Xi is the oriented determinant shadow of that filler.
