# The Bunch--Davies oriented Cech chain pairs opposite conductor marks

Order the conductor marks cyclically as

\[
p_{++},p_{-+},p_{--},p_{+-}.
\]

The Bunch--Davies factor \(\sqrt{-EU}\) has one common phase \(\pi/4\). Multiplying that common orientation by the real signs of the global square-root section \(g\) gives the oriented edge coefficients

\[
(-1,+1,-1,+1)
\]

on the four cyclic intervals.

The integral boundary of this checkerboard chain is

\[
(2,-2,2,-2).
\]

It is divisible by two, and its primitive half-boundary is

\[
(1,-1,1,-1).
\]

Thus its positive and negative parts are

\[
\{p_{++},p_{--}\},
\qquad
\{p_{-+},p_{+-}\}.
\]

The induced matching is therefore

\[
(p_{++},p_{--})\mid(p_{-+},p_{+-}),
\]

the opposite-point or diagonal matching.

A global swap of the two normalization sheets multiplies the entire chain and half-boundary by \(-1\). It exchanges positive and negative parts but leaves the unoriented matching—and hence its mod-two class—unchanged. The sheet-label ambiguity therefore disappears at this stage.

This supplies the global based-path/Hurwitz output on the conductor itself. It derives the diagonal geometric class from the actual Bunch--Davies phase and the global smoothing section, rather than from symmetry fitting.

It still must not be identified numerically with the source pair \((a,b)\) until the integral specialization map into \(\langle e_6,v_{\rm alg}\rangle/2\) is constructed.
