# Duplicate-row redistribution is a typed compositional candidate cell

For separately rooted primitive rows `-x<=0`, upper-A `x<=L`, and upper-B `x<=L`, a certificate `(a,u,v;c)` of x<=T obeys `u+v-a=1` and `L(u+v)+c=T`. The signed zero-bound kernel gives a bounded redistribution **candidate proof 2-cell** `R_theta:(a,u,v;c)->(a,u-theta,v+theta;c)` for `-v<=theta<=u`. It preserves target normal, bound and surplus, while retaining both distinct primitive row identities. Vertical composition satisfies `R_phi R_theta=R_(theta+phi)` when typed; inverse is `R_(-theta)` on the target proof. Positive target scaling sends theta to k theta, while refining both identical upper bounds from L to K commutes with redistribution strictly.

Fresh `check_duplicate_row_redistribution.py` passes 51 exact rational composition/scaling/refinement controls. The initial scaling check erroneously kept the target normal at 1 after multiplying it by k; corrected to carry alpha=k before the passing run. Overdraw, missing upper-B root, and unequal upper bounds are refused. An untyped claim that *any* equal row normal can be redistributed would be false: bounds must match, or surplus must be explicitly transported under a different type.

This is a genuine mathematical construction of a bounded labelled-arrow operation between proof records, not a cell declared by the original Farkas 1-category or an equality of historical executions.
