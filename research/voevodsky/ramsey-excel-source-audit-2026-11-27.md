### Where graph assumptions enter

At width nine the literal row `(2,5,8)` has local roles `111,110,100,000`. A fold that must recover the row preserves its literal entries. Transport of left/right **choices** instead holds the role fixed while moving the literal positions. Treating that role as a newcomer label is an extra identification; reading cell membership as a red link is an extra color rule. Once those are supplied, eight corners each lie in one corner cell, three edges and three faces: 56 incidences. That verifies an incidence layer, not 56 independent color decisions. Likewise the oriented graph square starts with a colored core, and the retention count compares orders, not the genealogy of the core.
