# The five-role 4-simplex has a reflection pairing four facets and fixes the middle facet

Use the already proposed ordered vertices

\[
[S,A,R,C,G],
\]

where `S` is the source role, `A` the analytic/reciprocal role, `R` the residue
role, `C` the characteristic/determinant role, and `G` the Green-defect role.
The reciprocal constructors suggest the order-reversing involution

\[
\iota:[S,A,R,C,G]\longmapsto[G,C,R,A,S].
\]

Thus

\[
S\leftrightarrow G,\qquad A\leftrightarrow C,\qquad R\leftrightarrow R.
\]

This induces the facet symmetry

\[
d_0\leftrightarrow d_4,\qquad d_1\leftrightarrow d_3,
\qquad d_2\leftrightarrow d_2.
\]

So four tetrahedral facets occur in reciprocal pairs, while the middle facet

\[
d_2\Delta^4=[S,A,C,G]
\]

is self-dual.  Its two routes compare the analytic reciprocal transport with
the characteristic/Green transport without using the residue vertex as an
intermediate definition.

This locates the independence gate.  The paired facets can be manufactured by
upper/lower chart reflection, determinant conjugation, and source/observer
transpose.  The fixed facet cannot: reflection maps it to itself.  Its closure
requires an independently constructed mate identifying determinant cofactor
orientation with Green transpose orientation.  Defining either side from the
other gives a formally symmetric boundary but no independent filler.

The recent passive-return result sharpens this.  On the strict same-sign chart,
the `C-G` return is a zero-free unit.  Hence the self-dual facet commutes only
at the determinant-line level; it has no Green cohomology at the residue
divisor.  The symmetry is therefore present on the boundary, but its fixed
locus is acyclic on one side and divisor-bearing on the other.

The missing constructor should consequently be sought as a self-dual finite
mate cell on `[S,A,C,G]`, not as an arbitrary sixth object or another reflected
chart.  It must satisfy all three conditions:

1. invariance under the reciprocal involution;
2. finite local cohomology with the determinant multiplicity;
3. source provenance independent of the scalar Xi section.

This is the geometric reason chart gluing closed four reciprocal directions
without closing the Xi-to-Green realization: paired faces follow from
reflection, whereas the fixed face requires genuinely new data.
