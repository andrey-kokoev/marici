# Constraint energy, reversible records and representation-independent accounting

## Result in the synthesis

We have constructed and tested a common positive constraint Hamiltonian on the actual proton and intrinsic-belt coefficient carriers:

    H = a(I-L) + b(I-G),    a,b > 0.

It selects the existing collective return N as its unique lowest subspace, distinguishes the two excitation structures, and transports exactly through the belt's 36-to-54 coordinate embedding. We also supplied reversible two-bit constraint records and identified the preparation boundary.

The important separation is now explicit:

- **Constraint energy** measures departure from the selected persistent state.
- **Preparation work** changes the system and reservoirs to obtain that state.
- **Record energy** belongs to physically maintained registers and their implementation.
- **Rest energy** compares a particle sector with a common physical vacuum.

The tested constraint law fixes the first of these. It does not by itself fix the other three. In particular, the previous stage-count expression 324(Delta_L-Delta_G) has not been derived from this Hamiltonian.

## 1. Use the actual retained operators

For both the proton and the intrinsic three-sector belt, the source coefficients satisfy

    L*=L, G*=G, L^2=L, G^2=G,
    LG=GL=N, N^2=N, rank(N)=1.

The checker replays the proton Merkle coefficients. For the belt, it uses the actual stored numerators and denominator factors, not a replacement uniform vector.

The belt uses numerator coordinates y_i=sqrt(d_i)x_i. Its inner product is y*D^(-1)y, where x are the physical orthonormal coordinates. Thus a stored matrix numerator A_ij acts as A_ij/d_j in the y coordinates. Self-adjointness is checked in that metric. No square-root denominator is silently dropped.

## 2. A common energy law selects the return

The four simultaneous orthogonal sectors are

    P_both = N,
    P_selection = L-N,
    P_alignment = G-N,
    P_neither = I-L-G+N.

They sum to I. Positivity of H follows from

    H = b P_selection + a P_alignment + (a+b) P_neither.

Here 'selection only' means the local-selection constraint is satisfied but alignment fails. It therefore costs b, not a.

| Sector | Constraint energy | Proton multiplicity | Belt multiplicity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Both satisfied | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| Selection only | b | 11 | 2 |
| Alignment only | a | 2 | 11 |
| Neither | a+b | 22 | 22 |

Consequences:

- The lowest subspace is exactly im(N), and the gap is min(a,b).
- Under unitary evolution generated by H, the selected subspace is stationary, but excited states do not automatically relax.
- Mathematically exp(-tH) tends to N. Turning this imaginary-time filter into deterministic preparation requires a specified environment, measurement/feedback, or another physical dynamics; it is not ordinary unitary time evolution.
- When a=b the two Hamiltonians have the same spectrum. When a and b differ, the two intermediate multiplicities are exchanged.

This connects the return projector to an explicit candidate energy landscape without assigning a separate arbitrary projector as its ground state.

## 3. Excitation spectra are not ground rest-energy offsets

Both constraint-ground energies are zero under this common definition. This does not predict zero particle masses or equal physical masses: a common vacuum and particle-number sectors have not been constructed on this 36-coordinate carrier.

For the declared H,

    tr(H_proton) = 24a + 33b,
    tr(H_belt)   = 33a + 24b,
    difference  = 9(a-b).

These traces sum the excitation energies. They are not the energy of the selected state. Promoting the trace, a zero-point sum, or another spectral functional to rest energy would be another operation to construct.

Likewise, a sector offset mu_s I leaves its internal projectors and gaps unchanged. Internal constraint dynamics cannot select that offset relative to a different particle sector. Once sectors interact, their relative offsets become meaningful and need a common Hamiltonian/vacuum law.

The previous occupied-channel result

    E_belt-E_proton = 324(Delta_L-Delta_G)

belongs to a different energy observable: stored energy in fully occupied retained channel registers. It is not contradicted by the zero constraint-ground splitting; the variables and physical accounting differ. The next bridge must relate those record occupations and their gaps to this carrier and its preparation/maintenance dynamics.

## 4. Reversible constraint-outcome records

Associate two syndrome bits with failure of L and G. For the joint projectors P_ij, define the unitary

    U = sum_(i,j) P_ij tensor X_1^i tensor X_2^j.

Here i,j are the two failure indicators. Orthogonality of the four P_ij gives U*=U and U^2=I. Acting on ready bits gives

    psi tensor |00> -> sum_(i,j) P_ij psi tensor |ij>.

The original state is recoverable by applying U again. No measurement outcome or old input component has been erased.

For degenerate syndrome-register energy, U commutes with H tensor I. Ideal outcome recording therefore preserves the system's mean constraint energy and can preserve total energy in this explicitly declared register model. Register readiness, the control mechanism, timing, and eventual erasure are not assigned zero physical cost by that statement.

Selecting outcome 00 has effect N and success probability ||N psi||^2. Recording without selection does not force the state into N. Discarding the outcome record generally produces dephasing between syndrome sectors rather than preparation of the desired sector.

Two-bit syndrome recording also does not claim to record every individual coefficient-arrow history. Previously retained history registers can be included as spectators, U tensor I_history, without erasure. Their physical Hamiltonian and new traversal costs remain additional data. This is an explicit constraint implementation, not a declaration that the old 1836-arrow protocol can be replaced cost-free by two bits.

This supplies a concrete accounting table:

| Operation | What is retained | System energy statement | Additional resource needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Record L/G outcomes | Input superposition and two syndrome bits | Mean H unchanged in the degenerate-record model | Ready register and implementation/control |
| Read and select outcome 00 | Outcome and accepted state | Accepted state has zero constraint energy | Success conditioning and accounting for other outcomes |
| Feed back to prepare the selected state | Old state/records or reservoir outputs | Can move energy into a reservoir | Explicit feedback and conservation law |
| Reset records | Old information must go elsewhere if reversible | Not fixed by H alone | Reservoir/erasure accounting |

The previously constructed history-retaining charge-locking controller remains the concrete example of feedback with charge and work reservoirs. Its externally supplied charging law is not derived by this new syndrome construction.

## 5. Exact intrinsic-to-ambient energy transport

Let W be the stored isometric embedding of 36 intrinsic belt coordinates into 54 ambient coordinates, and let D be its metric-adjoint decoder in the implemented numerator-coordinate representation. The exact tests give

    DW=I_36,
    Q=WD,
    rank(Q)=36.

Transport the Hamiltonian as

    H_image = W H_intrinsic D.

Then

    D H_image W = H_intrinsic,
    H_image W = W H_intrinsic.

Thus every intrinsic state has the same energy and time evolution under the two coordinate descriptions. This is an equality of operators, not a check on one selected vector.

The ambient complement has dimension eighteen. For any positive kappa, set

    H_ambient = H_image + kappa(I-Q).

Its compression back to the intrinsic carrier is unchanged. Exact controls use kappa=1 and kappa=7. Their differing extra-mode energies are not a mass change of an intrinsic state; they concern added ambient degrees of freedom outside the embedded physical subspace.

No scalar energy cost is added merely because W and D each have 102 nonzero entries. A physical implementation that stores extra records or maintains a converter needs its own Hamiltonian; a coordinate description does not supply that cost.

## 6. The old ambient belt protocol is not just this coordinate change

The same check distinguishes two transformations that had to be kept separate:

- Transporting the intrinsic sector-local projector gives W L_intrinsic D of rank three.
- The earlier ambient protocol's triangle-local projector has rank eighteen.

They are not equal. Their collective returns do agree exactly:

    W N_intrinsic D = N_old_ambient.

So equality of the final return is weaker than equality of the intermediate operations or their dynamics. The change from eighteen triangle selections to three sector-wide selections is a genuine implementation change, not representation overhead.

This means the 1836-versus-5346 protocol comparison cannot be classified as a mere coordinate change. The 36-to-54 embedding of a fixed intrinsic protocol can; replacing its local rule cannot.

## 7. A bookkeeping-refinement control

Insert a reversible coordinate permutation and its decoder into the proton description. Each has 36 nonzero transfers, adding 72 implementation arrows to a naive stage-support accounting. Conjugating the Hamiltonian during the encoding and decoding it afterward returns exactly the original H.

Therefore reversible re-description can increase support count without changing intrinsic state energy. This does not declare physical traversal free. It separates a coordinate choice from an actual device executing those traversals. If those 72 records represent maintained physical apparatus, include that apparatus and its energy explicitly rather than charging the represented state by definition.

## 8. Where this drives the next construction

We have now imposed three simultaneous constraints on a common model:

1. **State selection:** its lowest subspace must be the checked N.
2. **Representation independence:** the actual W/D interface must preserve the same state's energy.
3. **Retained accounting:** measurement outcomes and history cannot disappear when enforcement is interpreted as preparation.

The current constraint Hamiltonian passes all three in its declared scope. It does not generate a nonzero maintenance cost for a stationary selected state. To obtain one, identify an actual persistent coupling, occupation, circulation, or reservoir operation rather than inferring work from a repeated algebraic projection.

The next high-value object is a joint carrier-plus-record Hamiltonian: place the existing dressing registers on the constructed arrow-record map, couple them to the constraint sectors, and test whether the same laws select their occupied state, protect the particle sector, and determine the stage gaps relative to the elementary reference. That would connect persistent structure to stored rest energy rather than counting preparation attempts as mass.
