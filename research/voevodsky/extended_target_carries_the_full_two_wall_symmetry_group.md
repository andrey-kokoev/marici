# The extended target carries the full two-wall symmetry group

## Question

Does the exact four-generator target completion carry both conductor root swaps together with wall exchange, rather than only the site-exchange involution?

## Claim boundary

This constructs an integral representation of the audited occurrence symmetries on the algebraic target completion. It does not give the generators a physical implementation or establish contour authority.

## Occurrence actions

In point order \((++,+-,-+,--)\), let

\[
T_\epsilon:
++\leftrightarrow-+,
\quad
+-\leftrightarrow--,
\]

and

\[
T_\delta:
++\leftrightarrow+-,
\quad
-+\leftrightarrow--.
\]

These are commuting involutions. Wall exchange \(T_s\) swaps the two signs and conjugates them:

\[
T_sT_\epsilon T_s=T_\delta.
\]

## Transport to the completed target

Because the ambient comparison \(P\) is unimodular, define

\[
A_\epsilon=PT_\epsilon P^{-1},
\qquad
A_\delta=PT_\delta P^{-1}.
\]

Explicitly,

\[
A_\epsilon=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1&0&0\\
1&0&0&0\\
1&-1&0&1\\
1&-1&1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\]

\[
A_\delta=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0&1\\
1&0&1&0\\
-1&1&0&-1\\
0&0&0&-1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

They satisfy

\[
A_\epsilon^2=A_\delta^2=I,
\qquad
A_\epsilon A_\delta=A_\delta A_\epsilon.
\]

Together with transported wall exchange \(A_s\),

\[
A_sA_\epsilon A_s=A_\delta.
\]

Thus the completion carries the semidirect product generated by two root swaps and their exchange.

## Differential compatibility

For enhanced residue actions

\[
D_\epsilon=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1),
\qquad
D_\delta=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,-1),
\]

the extended differential obeys

\[
D_{\rm ext}A_\epsilon=D_\epsilon D_{\rm ext},
\qquad
D_{\rm ext}A_\delta=D_\delta D_{\rm ext}.
\]

The ambient comparison simultaneously satisfies

\[
A_\epsilon P=PT_\epsilon,
\qquad
A_\delta P=PT_\delta.
\]

## Invariant closed line

Both root swaps and wall exchange fix the primitive closed vector

\[
z=f_{\log}+f_{\rm face}.
\]

The individual route and support generators are mixed, but their closed line is a trivial subrepresentation of the full symmetry group.

## Disposition

The rank-two completion is closed under all audited two-wall occurrence symmetries and retains the original closed Aspect class invariantly. The remaining defect is not algebraic symmetry closure; it is source typing of the support generators and construction of the contour/readout map.
