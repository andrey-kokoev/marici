## Strongest successor: coherent optical two-mode record, not dead-time memory

`research/nima/exchange-oscillator-preparation-and-measurement.md` provides an explicit passive difference-mode Hamiltonian, including diagonal terms, phase convention, pulse area and oscillator/readout assumptions. `research/sontag/conditional-statistical-optical-binding-v1.md` supplies another lossless two-mode mixing law and explicitly separates coupling profiles, physical time, and external apparatus resources. These are concrete conditional implementation clues, not just matching two-dimensional state counts.

For a DECLARED single-excitation two-mode sector, identify neutral |0> with |10> and |1> with |01>. A standard number-conserving optical candidate

    H_record/(hbar Omega) = b†b - (a†b+b†a)/2

restricts to [[0,-1/2],[-1/2,1]], exactly the prepared scalar-sector neutral Hamiltonian. Omega is an independently supplied time/energy scale, not derived from model-time. This combines detuning/diagonal and exchange terms; a mixing term alone would give the wrong generator. It is a new candidate synthesis guided by prior machinery, not a previously admitted packet.

Crucial unmatched cell: the whole reaggregation model uses a controlled vertex -g P tensor X with P=Pi or c-Pi on the 100-state carrier. A passive two-mode optical record alone realizes the active record block, NOT that matter-controlled operator or both rules on all inputs. Need an explicit physical encoding of the matter sectors and a conditional coupling/control implementation. Preparing the known scalar state hides that missing control instead of proving it.

## Reset and history are already treated honestly

`research/nima/exchange-preparation-reset-resource-ledger.md` distinguishes reverse coherent execution from swapping records with fresh vacuum environments. Record-only reset fails its common preparation; resetting all modes transfers data/energy to ancillas and does not erase the enlarged history. Reverse recovery is checked before destructive detection. Reuse that discipline for repeated neutral-record trials; do not insert an unaccounted reset into the failed detector-binding comparison.
