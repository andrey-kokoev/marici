# Split of the non-K exactness problem

## Result

The degree-14 non-K module contains 480 IBP derivative rows and 25,200 marked-q multiplication rows. Their arbitrary-degree problems are structurally different.

Marked-q relations commute with monomial multiplication. Their descriptors reduce to 960 A12 parity seeds: five marked-q choices, three K poles, 16 compatible level patterns, and four exponent parities.

IBP relations do not commute literally with square multiplication. Along the differentiated axis,

`I(e+2)-x^2 I(e)`

contains the Leibniz term `2*x^(e+1)`. Therefore solving the 16 formal pole/axis/parity seeds would not establish unbounded IBP absorption without a sourced correction for that extra column.

## Disposition

The non-K leaf splits into two branches. First exact-solve the 960 q seeds using established Q naturality. Separately derive the corrected IBP square-shift identity through admitted K, q, or IBP relations and verify its arbitrary-degree closure.

No full rank-26 characteristic-zero theorem is asserted yet.
