# Translated Gaussian observers detect every finite Blaschke defect direction

## Observer family

Fix \(t>0\). Consider translated Gaussian observers whose Mellin/entire amplitudes have the form

\[
m_a(z)
=e^{-tz^2}e^{iaz},
\qquad a\in\mathbb R,
\]

up to the fixed Fourier normalization.

The Gaussian factor never vanishes. Detection of finite complex evaluation data is therefore controlled by the exponential family

\[
a\longmapsto e^{iaz}.
\]

## Finite evaluation map

Let

\[
Z=(z_1,\ldots,z_N)
\]

be pairwise distinct complex points. Define

\[
V_Z:
\operatorname{span}
\{m_a:a\in\mathbb R\}
\longrightarrow
\mathbb C^N
\]

by

\[
V_Zm
=
\begin{pmatrix}
m(z_1)\\
\vdots\\
m(z_N)
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then \(V_Z\) is surjective.

## Proof by exponential independence

Suppose a vector \(c=(c_1,\ldots,c_N)\) annihilates every translated Gaussian evaluation vector. Then

\[
\sum_{j=1}^N
c_j
e^{-tz_j^2}e^{iaz_j}
=0
\]

for every real \(a\).

The left side is an entire exponential polynomial in \(a\). Since it vanishes on the real line, it vanishes identically.

Distinct exponentials are linearly independent. Therefore

\[
c_j
e^{-tz_j^2}=0
\]

for every \(j\). The Gaussian factor is nonzero, so

\[
c_j=0.
\]

Thus the annihilator of the evaluation range is zero. Since the target is finite dimensional, the range is all of \(\mathbb C^N\).

## Finite interpolation packet

Surjectivity implies that there exist real translations

\[
a_1,\ldots,a_N
\]

such that the evaluation matrix

\[
\left(
m_{a_k}(z_j)
\right)_{j,k=1}^N
\]

is invertible.

Hence every prescribed finite value vector on \(Z\) is realized by a finite linear combination of translated Gaussian observers.

## Blaschke model space

Let \(B\) be a finite Blaschke product with distinct zeros

\[
z_1,\ldots,z_N
\]

counted with multiplicity. The model space

\[
K_B=H^2\ominus BH^2
\]

is generated by reproducing kernels and, for repeated zeros, their derivative kernels at these points.

For simple zeros, the preceding evaluation theorem shows that the translated Gaussian source detects every coordinate of \(K_B\).

For a zero of multiplicity \(m\), differentiate the amplitudes:

\[
\partial_z^\ell m_a(z)
=
P_{\ell,t}(z,a)
m_a(z),
\qquad
0\le\ell<m.
\]

As functions of \(a\), these are exponential-polynomial jets. Standard confluent exponential independence gives surjectivity onto the full finite jet space.

Therefore translated Gaussian observers detect every finite-dimensional Blaschke model-space direction.

## Convolution degree

The translated Gaussian family already lies in degree one. Consequently the graded union

\[
E_{fin}
=
\bigcup_{r\ge1}E_r
\]

is also evaluation faithful on every finite divisor packet.

Convolution degree is not needed to create faithfulness, but its natural transformations preserve it. Products multiply the nonzero Gaussian factors and exponential characters.

## Consequence for endpoint-only absorption

Suppose the source-compressed negative feature is claimed to be only the odd endpoint line. If the Krein--Langer factor has any additional finite Blaschke direction, translated Gaussian observers detect it.

Therefore the projection

\[
P_{K_B}\mathcal O(E_{fin})
\]

cannot collapse to the endpoint line when additional finite divisor directions are present.

The odd-parity contraction can absorb the complete source-observed defect only in the endpoint-index-one case.

## Hostile-case use

This result does not make absence of additional divisor directions an objective. It supplies a hostile audit.

Given a fixture with an off-axis finite Blaschke factor, the source observation must expose its additional defect dimension. Any proposed positive construction that reports only the odd endpoint line on that fixture is nonfaithful or has silently quotiented the defect.

## Infinite divisor caution

The argument proves finite-packet faithfulness. For an infinite Blaschke sequence, one additionally needs convergence and sampling estimates in the relevant analytic graph topology.

However, every individual finite subset is detected. Thus no finite collection of off-axis defect directions can remain invisible to all translated Gaussian source packets.

## Disposition

Translated Gaussian observers are surjective on every finite complex evaluation and jet packet. They detect every finite Krein--Langer model-space direction.

Hence the generic convolution-degree source does not hide finite Blaschke defects. Endpoint odd parity is a complete defect carrier only when the source-compressed negative index is genuinely one.
