# Free trace paths obstruct unlicensed interchange

The first branching word `(1/2,1/2,1)` has parallel paths of proposed trace 3-cells: `merge@0` versus `swap@1; swap@0; merge@1`. Both reach `(1,1)`, so the arithmetic certificate image agrees. But in the FREE category on the named rewrite generators the paths are distinct words, of lengths one and three. The permitted fine continuation `replay the cell history` distinguishes them, even as the coarse public-endpoint continuation cannot. Fresh `check_free_trace_interchange_obstruction.py` independently replays both paths, checks their common target and different word/step counts, and refuses endpoint equality as a source for the missing 4-cell.

This is a typed obstruction **relative to the declared free grammar**, not an impossibility theorem for all enriched categories. To give an invertible higher comparison one must adjoin a 4-cell whose boundary is exactly these two paths and support it by an independently typed source constructor; ordinary row arithmetic proves equal images but not equality of paths. Alternatively declare a path-forgetting quotient and explicitly stop promising replay fidelity. The prior source-rooted proof DAG can validate provenance of an existing constructor, but a DAG whose only root is `the paths have equal endpoints` would silently replace a proof-history control by a public-answer control.

For the bounded research interface, the defensible choice is currently the normalized ENDPOINT contract on the fixed primitive interval (including its lax postweakening law). The stronger proof-history coherencer is blocked at a missing 4-cell under the free presentation; analytic S,A,R,C,G roles remain deferred.
