## Exact native-operation obstruction

The unchanged real four-comb coefficient span is already not closed under T: its character coefficients acquire nonreal phases. Complexifying is a possible declared extension, not an automatic operation on the original real source.

Even after complexification, any operator in span(I,C12,C12²,C12³) commutes with C12. If it lifted T through the surjective K, the induced T would commute with F. It does not. The same fact is checked by the exact linear system K sum_j c_j C12^j=T K: its coefficient rank is four and augmented rank five. Thus there is no such lift in that native clock algebra, over complex coefficients as well as real ones. This includes the algebra generated by the existing skew generator, but not every conceivable carrier operation.

There IS an explicitly added operator lift after complexification: with section S=E J^-1 and N=I-S K, set L=S T K+N. Then K L=T K and N L=N. The checker verifies both. L does not commute with C12, so it is not a hidden member of the old clock algebra. This demonstrates the precise repair obligation rather than a universal no-go: adding L changes the source operation dictionary and needs its own provenance/admission review.

## Heat is a separate domain obstruction

On the P_a support, every vector of the original four-comb span has a constant coefficient on the coset Z+a. Positive heat gives different weights at a and a+1 because (a+1)²-a²=5/3>0. The disjoint shifted supports and independent character ports prevent other basis terms from cancelling that variation. Consequently positive heat takes P_a outside this four-dimensional prepared span.

An infinite packet/test-space enlargement can retain the operation, but cannot be claimed to be this unchanged finite image. The pointed-axis task permits heat on its declared larger source; dropping it just to match the finite carrier would restrict the task and requires explicit acceptance review.
