# The Schwartz oscillator scale is preserved by Fourier, Adams translations, and cutoff bonding

## Question

Can the source-generated Fourier-saturated boundary Gram be used legitimately through completed Adams words and finite cutoff bonding, rather than only on an isolated local core?

## Claim boundary

Yes on the harmonic-oscillator Schwartz scale. Fourier is isometric on every oscillator rung, translations act continuously with explicit polynomial loss, and label cutoffs commute exactly. Under the already declared Euler majorants this loss is summable at every fixed jet order. This closes saturated-topology transport, but not the independently missing fourth-grade Green pairings.

## Oscillator scale

Let

$$
\mathsf H=-\partial_x^2+4\pi^2x^2
$$

and define Hilbert seminorms

$$
q_N(f)=\|(1+\mathsf H)^{N/2}f\|_2,
\qquad N\ge0.
$$

The projective intersection

$$
\mathscr S_{\rm osc}
=\bigcap_{N\ge0}\mathcal D((1+\mathsf H)^{N/2})
$$

is the Schwartz space with its standard equivalent nuclear Fréchet topology.

## Fourier saturation

Because

$$
\mathcal F\mathsf H=\mathsf H\mathcal F,
$$

one has for every \(N\)

$$
q_N(\mathcal Ff)=q_N(f).
$$

Thus all four Fourier orbit components live in the same projective scale, and Fourier saturation changes neither domain nor seminorm class.

## Translation transport

Let

$$
(U_af)(x)=f(x-a).
$$

The basic conjugation laws are

$$
U_a^{-1}XU_a=X+a,
\qquad
U_a^{-1}\partial_xU_a=\partial_x.
$$

Every polynomial in \(X\) and \(\partial_x\) of total order at most \(N\) therefore conjugates to a polynomial of the same order whose coefficients are polynomial in \(a\). Equivalence of oscillator and Schwartz seminorms gives constants \(C_N,d_N\) such that

$$
q_N(U_af)
\le C_N(1+|a|)^{d_N}q_N'(f),
$$

where \(q_N'\) is an equivalent finite sum of oscillator seminorms of order at most \(N\). Hence every finite Adams translation word acts continuously on \(\mathscr S_{\rm osc}\).

For prime translations \(a=k\log p\), the loss is bounded by a fixed polynomial in \(k\log p\) at each rung.

## Modulation and Fourier-conjugate words

For

$$
(M_bf)(x)=e^{2\pi ibx}f(x),
$$

one has

$$
M_b^{-1}\partial_xM_b
=\partial_x+2\pi ib,
\qquad
M_b^{-1}XM_b=X.
$$

Thus the same polynomial estimates hold for modulations. Since Fourier exchanges translation and modulation, arbitrary words generated by the Fourier quarter turn and Adams translations preserve the oscillator scale continuously.

## Label cutoffs

On a labelled direct sum or projective product, let \(P_E\) retain a finite set of prime, grade, shell, and theta labels. Fourier and the local Adams operators act labelwise before codiagonalization. Therefore

$$
P_E\mathcal F=\mathcal FP_E,
$$

and, whenever the Adams word does not relabel outside its declared grade map,

$$
P_{A(E)}\mathsf A=\mathsf AP_E.
$$

These are exact bonding identities, not asymptotic cutoff statements. In particular, the saturated four-orbit packet survives every finite cutoff.

## Completion estimate

The declared completed comparisons use prime majorants of the form

$$
\sum_p\frac{(\log p)^m}{p^{3/2+\sigma}}<\infty
$$

for fixed \(m\) and \(\sigma\ge0\). The polynomial translation loss at any fixed oscillator rung and finite jet order is absorbed by such a majorant after increasing \(m\). Hence labelwise Fourier-saturated Adams words extend through the declared projective completion.

## Consequence for the target Gram

The source-derived vanishing

$$
G_{\rm wall,tail}^{\rm sat}=0
$$

may now be transported through Adams translations and cutoff bonding on this saturated oscillator/Schwartz completion. It is no longer blocked by topology transport.

This does not compute

$$
\langle f_3,f_j\rangle_{\mathfrak G_p^\theta},
\qquad 0\le j\le3,
$$

in the independently declared completed theta-history Green form. Oscillator Hermite orthogonality cannot be substituted for that target metric without a comparison theorem.

## Disposition

Fourier-saturated topology is preserved by the complete translation/modulation Adams semigroup and exact cutoff bonding on the oscillator Schwartz scale, with completion controlled by the existing Euler majorants. The first-Adams target Gram frontier is reduced to the translated fourth-Gaussian-grade pairings and their labelled Green-metric comparison.
