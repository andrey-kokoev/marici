# The 180-byte sensitivity wall separates receipt choice from total cost

For the frozen three-provider menu, both admissible two-provider bundles retain the SAME restricted archive A. The receipt-only alternatives are A+C=3,658 and A+B=3,838 bytes, where C is a full-domain archive-free lift and B a full-domain archived lift. Let u_C and u_B be ADDITIONAL nonnegative provider-specific costs expressed in the SAME scalar units as the receipt-byte objective, and assume common A costs cancel. Then the cheaper receipt bundle wins precisely if `u_C-u_B<180`; at 180 they tie, and above 180 the archived-lift bundle wins. If no upper bound on the omitted differential is available, nonnegativity alone cannot establish a total-cost winner. If a verified upper bound is strictly below 180, the receipt preference survives that particular scalar accounting model.

Fresh `python research/voevodsky/checkers/check_bundle_cost_sensitivity.py` passed rational boundary and reversal controls: 179.5 remains cheap, 180 ties, 181 reverses when u_B=0, and a two-unit additional charge on B restores the cheap preference at (u_C,u_B)=(181,2). A REAL stale handle makes C ineligible for full-domain lifting regardless of its symbolic price; cost adjustment cannot repair missing service authority.
