# A supplied four-periodic seam would upgrade the 2-Segal baseline to a twisted successor system

## Question

Would an independently supplied seam

\[
V_{4,k}\simeq V_{1,k+1}
\]

make the existing endpoint-marked 2-Segal construction cyclic or paracyclic?

## Flattened four-phase system

Write

\[
W_{4k+i-1}=V_{i,k},\qquad i=1,2,3,4.
\]

Let the within-stage comparison be

\[
C_{14,k}:V_{1,k}\longrightarrow V_{4,k}
\]

and conditionally supply a seam equivalence

\[
R_k:V_{4,k}\xrightarrow{\simeq}V_{1,k+1}.
\]

Then the stage successor on the first presentation is

\[
S_k=R_k\circ C_{14,k}:V_{1,k}\longrightarrow V_{1,k+1}.
\]

This is a four-phase homotopy-coherent successor system. Each tetrahedron is a
fundamental domain, and the seam identifies its terminal phase with the first
phase of the next domain.

## Literature comparison

The local decomposition law is closest to Dyckerhoff--Kapranov 2-Segal
coherence: polygonal decompositions and their different triangulations recover
equivalent global data. Their twisted cyclic nerve is especially relevant. For
an endofunctor \(F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal C\), its simplices are chains whose
closing arrow lands in \(F(x_0)\), and the resulting simplicial set is 2-Segal
(`1212.3563v1.pdf`, Section 3.2, especially page 42).

The seam plays the role of this twist: after traversing one four-presentation
fundamental domain, the closing object lies at the successor stage.

The decomposition-space formulation is also relevant: a unital 2-Segal space
retains compatible decompositions instead of replacing them by one strict
factorization (`1512.07573v4.pdf`, Sections 2--3).

## Classification

The presently justified name is

> **four-periodic twisted-successor 2-Segal system**.

Equivalently, after choosing the stage successor as an endofunctor, it is a
candidate for an **\(S\)-twisted cyclic 2-Segal object**.

The seam alone does not prove an ordinary cyclic object. Ordinary cyclicity
requires degreewise cyclic operators \(\tau_n\), all cyclic face and degeneracy
identities, and

\[
\tau_n^{n+1}=\mathrm{id}.
\]

Nor does the seam alone prove a paracyclic object. A paracyclic structure still
requires degreewise rotation operators and the paracyclic face/degeneracy
identities; it merely allows the full rotation to remain a nontrivial central
stage translation rather than imposing finite-order closure.

Consequently the natural expected upgrade is paracyclic/twisted cyclic only if
one proves that full rotation equals successor transport:

\[
\tau_n^{n+1}\simeq S_n.
\]

If the successor is trivialized, this descends to ordinary cyclicity. If the
successor remains nontrivial, the structure is genuinely twisted or
paracyclic.

## Mixed coherence theorem still required

For arbitrary paths across tetrahedra to be canonically homotopic, it is enough
to construct and verify:

1. the four face 2-cells and tetrahedral 3-cell at every stage;
2. seam naturality for every generating edge;
3. seam compatibility with every generating face homotopy;
4. compatibility of the tetrahedral 3-cell under successor transport;
5. associativity coherence for iterated seams.

These conditions define a homotopy-coherent functor from the periodic
presentation indexing category. They avoid a separate proof for every long
path: local 2-Segal reconstruction and the mixed seam cells generate global
parallel-path coherence.

## Unbounded indexing theorem

Let \(\mathcal I\) be generated by objects \((k,i)\), with
\(k\in\mathbb N\) and \(1\leq i\leq4\), internal arrows

\[
c^k_{ij}:(k,i)\to(k,j)\qquad(i<j),
\]

and seams

\[
r_k:(k,4)\to(k+1,1).
\]

Impose the tetrahedral triangle relations

\[
c^k_{j\ell}c^k_{ij}=c^k_{i\ell}
\qquad(i<j<\ell).
\]

**Theorem.** For every two objects \((k,i)\) and \((m,j)\), the hom-set
\(\mathcal I((k,i),(m,j))\) is empty when the flattened order forbids a
forward path and is a singleton otherwise. Hence every pair of legal parallel
paths across arbitrarily many presentation tetrahedra agrees.

**Proof.** A path cannot leave a stage except through its unique seam and cannot
enter the next stage except at vertex one. Consequently every path from stage
\(k\) to a later stage \(m\) factors uniquely by stages:

\[
(k,i)\rightsquigarrow(k,4)
\xrightarrow{r_k}(k+1,1)
\rightsquigarrow(k+1,4)
\xrightarrow{r_{k+1}}\cdots
\xrightarrow{r_{m-1}}(m,1)
\rightsquigarrow(m,j).
\]

Inside each stage, repeatedly replace
\(c^k_{j\ell}c^k_{ij}\) by \(c^k_{i\ell}\). Each replacement strictly lowers
path length, so reduction terminates. Its result is the unique direct internal
arrow between that stage's entry and exit (or an identity when they coincide).
Stage boundaries cannot overlap an internal triangle reduction, so reductions
in distinct stages commute. Every legal path therefore has the same normal
form

\[
c^m_{1j}\,r_{m-1}c^{m-1}_{14}\cdots r_{k+1}c^{k+1}_{14}
\,r_kc^k_{i4},
\]

with identity factors omitted. This proves uniqueness for arbitrary
\(m-k\). \(\square\)

Equivalently, flattening \((k,i)\mapsto4k+i-1\) identifies \(\mathcal I\)
with the thin category associated to the ordinary order on \(\mathbb N\).
This is the symbolic theorem; no finite-stage cutoff is involved.

### Weak/homotopy-coherent version

If triangle relations are 2-cells rather than strict equalities, the same
normal-form proof identifies the required generating 2-cells, but uniqueness
of 1-morphisms no longer by itself proves contractibility of the space of
reductions. One must additionally supply the tetrahedral 3-cell in every stage.
Because reductions in disjoint stages commute, these local 3-cells together
with interchange generate coherence across any finite number of seams. For an
analytic realization, the functor must carry these generators to the actual
\(C_{ij,k}\), \(R_k\), face homotopies, and tetrahedral fillers.

## Finite path-coherence check

`check_four_periodic_twisted_successor_path_coherence.py` constructs stages zero
through six of the strict presentation indexing category. Each stage has the
six directed tetrahedral edges and each terminal vertex has the seam
\(V_{4,k}\to V_{1,k+1}\). Triangle contractions are oriented as rewriting
rules.

The checker enumerates 116,457 paths in 406 parallel endpoint families. Every
family has one normal form; the largest family contains 16,384 paths. A hostile
variant withholding the `1 -> 4` contraction at stage zero leaves three normal
forms for paths from \(V_{1,0}\) to \(V_{1,1}\), so the check detects a missing
generating face rather than passing from endpoint counting alone.

This verifies the finite strict combinatorics behind the proposed global
coherence argument. It does not instantiate any analytic comparison map.

## Boundary

The repository contains genuine mathematical 2-Segal and cyclic models and
finite executable checks, while its own `Fact_n` closeout records that the
generic presentation/dissection object has not been internalized as a checked
Rzk Segal/Rezk type. The classification above is therefore a theorem-level
architectural synthesis, not a new claim that the full analytic presentation
pyramid has already been formalized in Rzk.

## Verdict

Without an independently constructed map \(V_{4,k}\to V_{1,k+1}\), the established structure is a family of within-dimension 2-Segal presentation systems; no helical successor is implied by the four presentations. If \(V_{4,k}\simeq V_{1,k+1}\) is separately supplied, the construction is beyond a bare
2-Segal object. It is a four-periodic twisted-successor 2-Segal system. The
precise next gate is not another polygon decomposition theorem; it is the mixed
seam coherence theorem, followed by construction of degreewise rotations if a
paracyclic or twisted-cyclic classification is desired.
