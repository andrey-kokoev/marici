# The physical basepoint chamber is equivalent to triangle inequalities

## Question

Can positivity of the physical energy variables place the regular base value \(E_{\rm phys}=x+y+z\) in a definite chamber of the four-point quartic discriminant?

## Exact chamber differences

Relative to the three nonzero critical values,

\[
E_{\rm phys}-2x=y+z-x,
\]

\[
E_{\rm phys}-2y=x+z-y,
\]

\[
E_{\rm phys}-2(x+y)=z-x-y.
\]

Therefore

\[
\max(2x,2y)<E_{\rm phys}<2(x+y)
\]

holds exactly when

\[
x<y+z,
\qquad
y<x+z,
\qquad
z<x+y.
\]

These are the strict triangle inequalities for \((x,y,z)\).

## Chamber classification

For positive \(x,y,z\), at most one triangle inequality can fail.

- If all three hold, the basepoint lies between \(\max(2x,2y)\) and \(2(x+y)\).
- If \(x>y+z\), then \(E_{\rm phys}<2x\).
- If \(y>x+z\), then \(E_{\rm phys}<2y\).
- If \(z>x+y\), then \(E_{\rm phys}>2(x+y)\).

Equalities place the basepoint on a discriminant value and are not regular basepoints.

Thus the previously continuous-looking basepoint ambiguity reduces to four open kinematic chambers and their singular walls.

## Source-authority gate

The Cayley–Menger contour source declares external parameters \((P_1,P_2,P_3)\) and positivity of specified minors. Existing artifacts do not identify those parameters with the quartic variables \((x,y,z)\), nor prove that the required minor inequalities imply the three strict triangle inequalities above.

Accordingly, triangle inequalities would select a canonical interval for the basepoint, but they cannot be imported from Cayley–Menger positivity without the missing parameter map.

Even within the triangle chamber, an interval does not choose a based path homotopy around the remote critical values. It only fixes the starting component on the real axis.

## Disposition

The physical-basepoint problem is reduced to a finite chamber test. The next source check is an explicit map

\[
(P_1,P_2,P_3)\longrightarrow(x,y,z)
\]

and verification that the source semialgebraic contour enforces one of the four chambers. Global branch-cut and Picard marking data remain separate.
