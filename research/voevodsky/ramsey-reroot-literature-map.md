# Literature map: local verdicts, sufficient indexes, and change of root

The six customer/product roles are a modeling proposal. The testable mathematical objects underneath them are local extension predicates, Boolean contrasts, view determinacy, and separator messages.

1. **Neighborhood extension.** Given an admissible old red graph G, choose the new root's red link set S and its complementary blue link set. Red passes exactly when G[S] avoids a red clique of order l−1; blue passes exactly when the complement on V−S avoids a blue clique of order k−1. This follows by deleting the new root from a forbidden clique. Compare the neighborhood gluing method in [Goedgebeur and Radziszowski, §3](https://doi.org/10.37236/2824). Our [reciprocal verdict experiment](results/ramsey_reciprocal_verdicts.json) checks interchange of these predicates after graph complementation, neighborhood complementation, and parameter exchange.
2. **Fourier analysis on the Boolean cube.** The four functions 1,p,c,pc form the Walsh character basis on two signs (see [O'Donnell, Analysis of Boolean Functions, ch. 1](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~odonnell/analysis-of-boolean-functions/)). Dropping the constant gives tetrahedral coordinates. This explains three contrasts and four joint readings once the signs are given. It confers neither statistical independence nor a canonical Ramsey tetrahedron. A quantum CSS claim additionally requires specified operators and commuting checks.
3. **View determinacy.** [Nash, Segoufin and Vianu, Views and Queries](https://doi.org/10.1145/1806907.1806913) distinguish determining an answer from rewriting the query to compute it. Relativize their determinacy condition to admissible graphs: equality of retained views must imply equality of the requested queries. Record 36 is a finite instance; the forced-color chain is an actual rewriting on that instance. A one-root continuation view and the family of all rerooted continuations are different workloads.
4. **Reversible direction of inference.** [Kschischang, Frey and Loeliger, Factor Graphs and the Sum-Product Algorithm](https://doi.org/10.1109/18.910572) give exact message passing on cycle-free factor graphs. For our purpose, use OR for sum and AND for product. Organize constraints in a tree of bags with the running-intersection property. Each side of a tree edge sends a relation on the shared variables: the boundary assignments its side can extend. With messages in both directions, either bag can serve as root; existentially joining at the new root gives the same feasibility answer. Retaining witness assignments needs backpointers. Dense Ramsey constraints can require large bags. Rerooting this computation is distinct from changing the distinguished graph vertex; the latter changes the query and requires access to the new vertex's attachment variables.

**Proposed control.** Build the messages for the known [four-role path/cycle counterexample](../chatgpt/ramsey/ramsey_reroot_port_square.py). Ask for the same new attachment after exchanging the distinguished role. First retain the separating link or an equivalent boundary statistic; then omit it. Check the answer at both roots against direct extension. Minimize the retained separator over this query family. This tests sufficiency for change of root rather than merely counting sign states. No new proof outcome claimed.
