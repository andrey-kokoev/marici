## Source normalization binding: an easily missed export condition

Let c be the coefficient of the unshifted comb, c=sum of cyclic coefficients. On the carrier route it is r12 x; on the history route it is ell O_n x. The selected source coefficient e_AD has c=1 in the existing source order, so it supplies a unit coefficient seed under the explicitly chosen a=0 analytic specialization.

Ordinary Lambda and xi use c=1 AND the already declared unit self-dual lattice, Gaussian and measure. A general source state instead yields c Lambda(s) and c xi(s), with the whole pole packet and endpoint constant scaled consistently:

    endpoint constant = c/2.

This follows from linear distribution pairing, theta/Mellin construction and endpoint cancellation. The package does not silently divide every result by c. At c=0 that division is unavailable; hidden source states may still have nonzero norm or energy. After native history extension, c need not stay fixed, so resetting it to one would change the source operation and erase the established continuation hostile.

The ordinary zeta seed is therefore a declared mathematical input, not a law forcing every source vector to realize the same normalized xi. The checker retains scalar examples 0,1,2,-1,2/7 and their endpoint constants. These are normalization regression controls, not new zeta or positivity proofs.
