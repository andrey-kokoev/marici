# Audit of the univalent coherence handoff

## Question

For one bounded Marici bridge, what are the source localization, univalent completion, and observational quotient, and which comparisons are actually defined?

## Claim boundary

This packet audits the flag-to-logarithmic comparison bridge only. It does not construct the global Marici presentation category, a displayed attachment category, a physical readout, or a general moving operator. It treats graph communication as provenance, not evidence.

## Bounded bridge

Let \(A_{\log}\) be the weight-four total-degree-two logarithmic residue complex for the three-line pair and let \(B_{\mathrm{flag}}\) be the shifted flag complex of the oriented boundary triangle. The checked ordered-residue map is

\[
\operatorname{Res}:A_{\log}\longrightarrow B_{\mathrm{flag}},
\qquad
\operatorname{Res}([\Xi_{\log}])=[\sigma_{123}].
\]

Its minimal top-weight model is the identity \(\mathbb Z[-2]\to\mathbb Z[-2]\). The comparison is therefore a source-authorized equivalence for this bounded bridge. The associated cone cell is target-side comparison data; no tested source supplies an element mapping to it.

## Source localization

Let \(\mathcal P\) be the small structured category generated by the two oriented complexes, their structure-preserving maps, and the checked map \(\operatorname{Res}\). Let \(W\) be the subcategory generated by identities and \(\operatorname{Res}\), restricted to maps certified as quasi-isomorphisms with the fixed orientation.

The localization

\[
L:\mathcal P\longrightarrow \mathcal P[W^{-1}]
\]

formally inverts \(\operatorname{Res}\). Its universal property concerns functors that send \(W\) to equivalences. It does not identify maps merely because a probe gives equal values, and it does not supply a source-chain primitive.

## Univalent completion

Choose a small relative-category or Segal-space presentation of \((\mathcal P,W)\). Its Rezk completion

\[
j:\mathcal P[W^{-1}]\longrightarrow\widehat{\mathcal P}
\]

is a further operation: it presents the localized homotopy theory univalently, so equivalences correspond to identity paths in its object type. This changes the identity presentation, not the class of source-authorized weak equivalences. It retains whatever automorphism paths are admitted by the structured bridge.

Thus localization and univalent completion are not interchangeable:

- localization supplies the universal inversion of \(W\);
- completion makes the resulting equivalence structure univalent;
- neither is an observational quotient.

## Observational quotient

No physical or stable-record readout functor is materialized for this bridge. A candidate would require a typed functor or transformation

\[
Q:\widehat{\mathcal P}\longrightarrow\operatorname{Rec}
\]

with a declared record object, source-derived action on morphisms, and descent along \(jL(\operatorname{Res})\). Equality of the single cohomology generator can be used as a mathematical check of the comparison, but it is not a physical record map and cannot define upstream identity.

Consequently the proposed three-way comparison stops after two arrows:

\[
\mathcal P\xrightarrow{L}\mathcal P[W^{-1}]
\xrightarrow{j}\widehat{\mathcal P}.
\]

There is no admitted third arrow to an observational quotient.

## Strongest falsification attempt

The strongest available replacement for readout is the degree-two cohomology functor. It sends both minimal complexes to \(\mathbb Z\) and sends \(\operatorname{Res}\) to the identity. This verifies that the bounded equivalence is visible to that invariant. It fails as an observational quotient because no record ontology, detector map, physical interface, or descent authority is attached to it.

The residual is therefore typed: the first missing object is the readout functor \(Q\), not an additional localization or a different completion.

## Disposition

The Aspect architecture survives this bounded audit with one correction of scope. Source localization and univalent completion are distinct and explicitly realizable for the checked flag/log bridge. The observational quotient is undefined, so no claim about readout descent, physical nullity, or reconstruction from records is admitted.

The next acceptance gate is a displayed attachment over this two-object completed bridge: specify one attachment fiber, its pullback along \(\operatorname{Res}\), and the first displayed-univalence obstruction without importing a readout quotient.
