# Twelve-turn closeout: the self-dual middle facet is a shifted-self-dual span in a stratified perfect-complex category

The corrected ambient target is not a single Hilbert cone category.  Let
`Perf_ar` contain the source-derived arithmetic/Koszul perfect complexes, let
`Perf_Gr` contain admitted rigged Green Fredholm complexes, and let

\[
\operatorname{Det}:\operatorname{Perf}\to\operatorname{Pic}
\]

be the determinant functor.  The middle-facet `CG` datum must be represented by
a span in the total stratified category,

\[
K_\tau\xleftarrow{\simeq}M_{CG}\xrightarrow{\simeq}C_{\rm Gr},
\]

or an equivalent derived correspondence, together with the determinant-line
identification

\[
\operatorname{Det}(K_\tau)
\simeq\mathcal L_{\rm ar}
\simeq\operatorname{Det}(C_{\rm Gr}).
\]

The span is stronger than the line identification: it transports local defect
modules and their multiplicities.  It is also the correct place to demand that
first variation agrees with the independently rigged transpose `B^times`.

Reciprocal reversal fixes the middle facet with opposite orientation.  Hence
the correspondence must carry shifted self-duality

\[
M_{CG}\simeq\mathbb D(M_{CG})[1].
\]

This forces the Green target to be Fredholm of index zero and pairs its kernel
and cokernel defect data.  Locally, an order-`m` Xi zero must produce a
finite-length module of length `m` and its shifted dual, not an
infinite-dimensional kernel and not an acyclic passive block.

The scalar Koszul complex already has the required shifted-dual determinant
orientation.  The strict same-sign passive Green complex cannot be the target,
because it is acyclic at every Xi point.  A monolithic bordered Hilbert pencil
cannot be the target either, because the primitive, square, and connected
arithmetic strata do not form one completed vector.

Therefore the exact missing constructor contract is:

1. a stratified Green perfect/Fredholm complex `C_Gr`, independently sourced;
2. finite defect modules supported on the Xi divisor;
3. shifted reciprocal self-duality and index zero;
4. a derived span from `K_tau` preserving local length;
5. determinant compatibility with the primitive/square/det3/seam/infinity
   tensor line;
6. first-variation compatibility with `B^times` and Hilbert shadow `B^dagger`;
7. compatibility with the Clark codiagonal and its incidence chamber.

No object in the freshly inspected state satisfies this contract.  The
anti-simplicial symmetry determines the type, parity, index, and multiplicity
of the missing mate, but does not construct it.  This is the final boundary of
the twelve-turn facet audit.
