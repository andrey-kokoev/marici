# Filler-fiber and selection layer

## Question

What is the minimum categorical extension needed to distinguish filler existence, relative class, selected representative, and completed descent?

## Claim boundary

This is a conservative layer over the existing coherence-pyramid computad. It does not replace the five object sorts or assert an unrestricted higher category.

## Added objects

The layer adds four relative sorts:

- boundary datum;
- filler;
- relative class;
- completed filler.

They are organized by a chain interface

\[
C_2\xrightarrow{d_2}C_1\xrightarrow{d_1}C_0,
\qquad d_1d_2=0.
\]

For \(c\in C_0\), the fiber of fillers is

\[
\operatorname{Fill}(c)=\{M\in C_1:d_1M=c\}.
\]

Class projection quotients representative changes generated by \(d_2\). Selection is a separate partial constructor requiring source authority.

## Added cells

The extension records:

- representative-change 2-cells;
- selection-naturality 2-cells;
- completion-comparison 2-cells;
- selection-coherence 3-cells;
- completion-pasting 3-cells.

Thus a selected filler can be tested under gauge, sewing, and refinement rather than treated as canonical because its class is canonical.

## Completion gate

Completion remains partial. It requires graph-topology continuity, preservation of boundary-null families, uniform orbit control, and cutoff coherence. Physical selection is a further backend with an admitted effect, positive state–effect pairing, record map, and operational certificate.

## Verified instances

- Markov ordered amalgamation has a singleton filler fiber in the faithful ordered-list coordinate and an authorized strict identity selection.
- The RH Mellin interface has a nonempty filler fiber and endpoint-determined relative class, but no invariant strict modular selection or completed selection.

The finite labelled triangle verifies that equal boundary does not imply equal filler while the two fillers become equal after quotienting by the admitted 2-boundary.

## Disposition

The apparatus is strengthened by one conservative relative layer. The certificate DAG separates relative class, strict selection, completed selection, and physical selection, preventing promotion between them.
