# Sufficient descent-vanishing criteria

## Question

Which hypotheses force all local-total-lift descent obstructions to vanish?

## Claim boundary

Any one of the following is sufficient:

1. a global multiplicative Rees splitting, global ordered wall lifts, and compatible regulator data;
2. a contractible homotopy fiber of total-to-special mapping spaces over `tau0`;
3. an affine center `C` with local lifts, a connective abelian lift-fiber sheaf or spectrum, quasicoherent homotopy sheaves, and a convergent Postnikov tower.

In the third case, positive quasicoherent cohomology vanishes, so every obstruction in `H^(q+1)(C,pi_q L)` is zero.

Common-line conormals, normal-bundle orientation, local polynomial splittings, or rank-26 square transport alone do not imply any of these certificates.

These conditions prove existence. Only the contractible-fiber condition gives uniqueness up to contractible choice; geometric witnesses may otherwise remain nonunique.

The local `A3` model satisfies a local split certificate. No global artifact establishes any certificate for the intended carrier.
