## Extension argument

Every elementary memory-slot normalization N_i has norm at most one. Its source-constructed collision form T_i has bilinear norm at most one. Hence each composite normalization is bounded, and each transported form is continuous.

For each of the five schedules s, write its finite total as

    N_s=N_3 N_2 N_1,
    T_s=T_1+N_1^* T_2+(N_2 N_1)^* T_3.

The finite theorem establishes N_s=N and T_s=T_N on the common dense algebraic domain. Uniqueness of continuous extensions gives these equalities on the cut-l1 completion. The direct new-match estimate supplies ||T_N||<=1, not merely the bound three obtained by the triangle inequality on the displayed decomposition.

Thus

    q_external + T_N = N^* q_normal

holds on the completion, independently of schedule. All original finite weights, forms, root factors, and cut labels are unchanged. The completion norm, not the finite Green pairing, differs from the raw orthogonal-cut Hilbert completion.

The associators of already balanced normal forms are ordered tensor reassociations and induce isometries of these shape norms. They introduce no collision currents. The nontrivial relative current remains attached to normalization of external records.

## Boundary that still matters

The finite pentagon is a chain-map theorem. Extending its degreewise pairing and normalization identities is not by itself proof that the full differential is closed or that its homology commutes with completion. No such implication is used here.

Likewise, finite faithful associated-layer maps need not remain injective after taking completed source quotients. Separation of that completed quotient, control of differential domains, and exclusion of inverse-limit phantom families remain distinct tasks. The result here removes coherence of the bounded relative normalization packets from that list: it follows by density once the stated topology is fixed.
