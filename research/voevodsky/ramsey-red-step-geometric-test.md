Keep address {3,6} fixed across the eight named wirings (do not co-transport the probe). Use only the two bits already used for the neutron square: is C1 at its starting site, and is the reader in the starting pair of opposite cells? Test each bit separately; count exact partition matches up to interchange of 0 and 1. Test all reference wirings and both rooted correspondences, with no best-root selection. A 4/4 split is insufficient; membership must agree on every state. Do not search other Boolean functions for this test.
