## Decision

Select `completion-stable-observability:v2`. Its target is preservation of the independently declared readout, not reconstruction of every source coordinate:

    readout O = r F on the admitted source,
    r extends through the actual declared completion.

In a source admitting normed-linear modeling, seek ||O(v)||<=C||F(v)|| with the required uniformity; in a supported/derived source retain its own filtration, exactness and action data. Do not impose a Gaussian or Banach topology on a sector merely to use that estimate.

The immediate executable task is to recover a source-authorized completion/readout tuple for an existing realization and audit its actual missing extension or noncollapse witness. This is not permission to create a new arbitrary common physical observable.

## Existing evidence changes what is missing

- The spectral source supplies a direct-score recovery mechanism and an explicit compatible-output complex. Do not ask again whether any realization exists. Its physical operator/contact extraction and completion topology are scoped gates.
- The integral Tate bridge is already supplied as an odd coefficient comparison. Do not re-prove rank agreement or invert3 to force a simpler presentation.
- The later fs/Kato source already realizes the even primitive physical line and its compatible diagonal packet. Do not reopen closed normalization-sheet/purity work or demand arbitrary ambient triples unless the independently intended observation needs them.
- The even-to-odd untwisted bridge is forbidden under the declared actions, but the project has not required that identification. It is a typed boundary of the common calculus, not a mandatory repair task.
- The restored period has a same-density pole/log comparison. That does not identify a physical finite part, contour or full graph sum, and further auxiliary asymptotics are not the present priority.

## Acceptance criteria for the selected successor

A subsequent completion result must name the source, V, F, O, reading type, compatible image, physical selection and topology. It must state whether the proposed extension reaches closure(im F) or a larger target, and supply the relevant density/realization witness. Source actions, orientation and boundary labels remain attached.

Success is an explicit observation-preserving extension with its scoped noncollapse control. A valid negative result is a source-admissible loss witness for the specified observation and topology. Neither may be promoted to a statement about all sectors or all observables.

Owner-input branches remain asynchronous and nonterminal while local source evidence can be examined. Existing requests are retained rather than duplicated while Benincasa is offline. No owner artifact or public source-objective page is changed by this reprioritization.
