# Source-indexed readout comparisons: consolidated structural result

## What the contract now contributes

The common contribution is an obligation for preserving an independently intended observation, not an identification of every sector's coefficients. Its four parts are:

1. **Compatible outputs:** supply their equations/witnesses, prove compatibility, and distinguish this from realizing every compatible output.
2. **Retained data:** the intended readout must factor through the supplied ports. Keep the necessary coefficients, labels, sheets, actions and orientation lines.
3. **Completion:** establish continuity of that readout in the actual declared topology. Full source inversion is sufficient in some examples but not necessary.
4. **Source authority:** the source supplies the objects, maps, intended reading and admissible changes. Matching ranks, moments or scalar signatures is not source identification.

A module-level summary is ker(F) subset ker(O), together with the required equivariance and topology. A normed-linear completion certificate is ||O(v)||<=C||F(v)||, uniformly on the declared domain, with a complete target. Neither formula is imposed on a sector without the corresponding structure.

No shared physical reading requiring a direct spectral-to-fs/Kato comparison has been specified in this audit. That comparison is **not posed**, rather than a theorem that we should manufacture by finding convenient normalizations or twists.

## Three separations that survived both source tests

- **Compatibility is not realization:** HF=0 is weaker than im(F)=ker(H); the full ambient target can contain unrealized data.
- **Recovery is readout-relative:** one readout can remain bounded when full inversion fails; another can be lost by the same comparison.
- **Agreement is type-relative:** integral torsion, support, orientation and source normalization cannot be discarded merely because reduced coefficients agree.

These are reusable diagnostics with source instances. They are not a proof of a universal carrier category, a universal physical observable, or a common completion topology.

## Claim registry and validation

`readout-comparison-claims.json` records the distinct source models, exact claim scopes, evidence paths and statuses: proved_scoped, source_recorded, refuted, source_conditional or not_posed. A refuted claim is not a failed checker: several tests succeed precisely by rejecting it.

`check_readout_synthesis_closure.py` reruns the weighted-road certificate and five comparison checkers, then cross-checks the registry's central distinctions against their results. It does not assign scientific truth merely from a status string. In particular the weighted-star result remains inconclusive, and the later fs/Kato geometric realization remains prior source evidence rather than a newly re-proved theorem.
