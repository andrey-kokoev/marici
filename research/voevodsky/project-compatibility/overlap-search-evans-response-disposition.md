# Overlap search: existing sewing, rejected unchanged-state gluing

## Purpose and result

Search prior overlap/descent research before proposing a new common-calculus gluing theorem. The search recovered a concrete, already-required comparison: placement of independently constructed Evans history data into the existing source-generated positive response graph.

The principal result is recovery of a prior source-supported obstruction, not a new theorem: completed response sewing exists, but the unchanged Evans state fails the tested necessary Hilbert membership condition at the first certified zero. Another general overlap contract would duplicate existing work.

## Structural consequence

The obstruction is to a required unchanged-state placement, not to defining the sewing relation or writing another coherence diagram. Any renewed attempt must supply independently sourced corrected history data and prove their response membership, or independently construct the other physical comparison map. It may not repair the failed overlap by renaming an existing map, imposing membership, or changing the observed state without declaring that change.

No new overlap framework, numerical benchmark, graph reprioritization or owner modification is warranted by this search. The useful result is to retain this explicit rejected gluing attempt alongside the already constructed local calculus. The broader common-calculus conjecture remains delimited rather than refuted.
