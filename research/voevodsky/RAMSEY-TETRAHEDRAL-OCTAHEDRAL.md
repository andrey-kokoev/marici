## First precise target: arbitrary finite vertex colorings

For every integer $r\ge1$ and finite nonempty labelled pattern $S\subset L$, construct a finite bound $B(r,S)$ such that every coloring

$$
c:L_{B(r,S)}\longrightarrow\{0,\ldots,r-1\}
$$

admits $a\in L$ and a positive integer $m$ with

$$
a+mS\subseteq L_{B(r,S)},\qquad c(a+ms)=q\quad\text{for every }s\in S
$$

for some color $q$.

The certificate retains $a,m,q$ and every labelled image point. A native certificate must also retain the construction history. Start with T and O as fixtures, but do not replace the arbitrary-pattern quantifier by those two examples.

The distinction between **any positive scale** and **unit scale** is essential. A counterexample to an elementary-cell version is not automatically a counterexample to this dilation-allowing statement. Likewise, an avoiding coloring at one finite N refutes only a claimed bound, not the existence of every larger forcing bound.
