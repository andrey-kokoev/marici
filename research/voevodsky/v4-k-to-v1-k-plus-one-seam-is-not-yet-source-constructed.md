# The cross-dimension V4-to-V1 seam is not yet source-constructed

## Question

Does prior research construct a source map from the observer-valued trace presentation at realization dimension \(k\) to the source presentation at realization dimension \(k+1\)?

## Claim boundary

No such formula was located. Two nearby maps are constructed, but neither has this type. This conclusion concerns the searched repository packets and does not prove that no unindexed source contains the map.

## Required type

A cross-dimension seam would require a source transport

\[
a_k:\mathsf{Obs}_k\longrightarrow\mathsf{Obs}_{k+1}
\]

and a natural comparison

\[
\mathcal Q_{4,k}\Longrightarrow a_k^*\mathcal Q_{1,k+1}.
\]

Its component equation would be

\[
\mathcal Q_{4,k}(g,h)
=
\mathcal Q_{1,k+1}(a_kg,a_kh).
\]

## Nearby constructed maps

First, completed common history plus the projective cycle observer gives an injective map

\[
\mathcal O_D:\mathcal C_{D,\exp}\longrightarrow\mathcal H_D\times\mathcal Z_D.
\]

After defining the codomain as its image, it has the algebraic inverse

\[
D_{41,D}^{\mathrm{obs}}(\mathcal O_Dc)=c.
\]

This returns from the completed observer image to the source at the same ratio block. Continuity of the inverse and cross-block assembly remain open. The packet then assumes, rather than constructs, a further seam to the next block.

Second, the retained cutoff presentation

\[
\mathcal P_L(h)=(\mathcal V_L(h),\mathcal B_L(h))
\]

has an exact affine volume successor and an asymptotically natural boundary successor as \(L\) changes. This is transport within the \(V_4\) cutoff presentation, not a map to \(V_1\) at realization dimension \(k+1\).

## Disposition

The cross-dimension comparison remains a missing source-typed arrow. The available algebraic inverse at fixed block and the cutoff successor inside \(V_4\) cannot be substituted for it. Acceptance requires explicit \(a_k\), the displayed natural-form identity, preservation of the completed observer image, and continuity in the declared source topology.
