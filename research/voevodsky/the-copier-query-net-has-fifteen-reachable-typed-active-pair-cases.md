It observes all 15 declared typed principal-pair cases: COPY with B0/B1/N; Q_B with K/N; Q_S and Q_R with B0/B1/N; ERASE with B0/B1/K/N. Across 25,419 rewrites it detects no unlisted pair. This is an empirical RULE-TABLE audit, not an inductive theorem about ALL reachable graphs. Strict lexicographic rank from the prior checker supplies termination for this restricted initial shape.
