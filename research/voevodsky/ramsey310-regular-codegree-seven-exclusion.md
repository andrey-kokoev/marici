# Conditional computational exclusion at order 40: 9-regular, codegree seven

Assume triangle-free, no independent ten-set, 9-regular, 40 vertices. A nonadjacent pair of codegree c leaves 20+c common nonneighbors with independence number at most seven, so c<=7 by R(3,8)=28. At c=7 the two roots have two exclusive neighbors apiece. The 27 common nonneighbors H have 81+t edges by the deleted-degree sum, where t<=4 is the edge count between the exclusive pairs. Using the complete (3,8;27) catalogue, e(H)>=85 and precisely four records attain 85. Thus t=4. The census and exclusion below depend on that catalogue's completeness, not just the validity of its graphs.

On each side the exclusive vertices use two independent six-sets in H; their union hits every independent seven-set (otherwise add both exclusives and the opposite root). Opposite sides' supports cannot intersect because of the four cross edges. The Python packer lists all unordered pairs of admissible opposite sides, retaining all vertex labels. Each common neighbor uses an independent seven-set in H. Distinct common supports intersect in at most five vertices because their owners already share both roots. On fixing one side, at vertices it touches the seven supports must meet the exact remaining degree; elsewhere at most two incidences can come from the unknown side. Two chosen supports cannot both miss an independent T that extends to size eight with that side. No three chosen supports can miss the same independent seven-set. All filters are necessary, not assumed sufficient.

The native checker branches on seven mutually compatible supports, pruning via the remaining per-vertex bounds and the triple test. When a positive lower bound remains, branch on the first chosen support hitting that vertex, retaining untried non-hitters. Otherwise branch over the available supports in order. An impossible side excludes every incident four-support choice. The checker skips a side only when all its choices have already been eliminated (run without the optional test limit). All four full runs ended with zero unresolved choices.

Reproduction: compile `checkers/ramsey310_bulk_side.rs` with `rustc --edition=2021 -O`. For each record n in 49241,49289,49915,49939 run `python checkers/ramsey310_pack_frontier.py n tmp.txt` (replace n by the integer), then the compiled binary with `tmp.txt` as its sole argument. Outputs: `results/ramsey310_bulk_<n>.txt`. The four input edge counts match the independent totals in `results/ramsey310_exclusive_census.jsonl`. These are executable searches, not proof traces checked by a separate verifier. No result is claimed for regular pairs of smaller codegree or for nonregular graphs.
