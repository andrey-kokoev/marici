# The Clark ports force a rank-two skew cross-supply in the doubled theta KYP equation

## Moment observations

On the theta-tail space define

\[
\ell_0(f)
=
\int_0^\infty
f(u)\Phi(u)\,du,
\qquad
\ell_1(f)
=
\int_0^\infty
u f(u)\Phi(u)\,du.
\]

For the reciprocal spectral pair

\[
f_+(u)=e^{-su},
\qquad
f_-(u)=e^{su},
\]

we have

\[
\ell_0(f_+)=F(s),
\qquad
\ell_1(f_+)=-F'(s),
\]

\[
\ell_0(f_-)=F(-s),
\qquad
\ell_1(f_-)=-F'(-s).
\]

Define doubled observations

\[
a(f_+,f_-)
=
\ell_0(f_+)+
\ell_0(f_-),
\]

\[
d(f_+,f_-)
=
\ell_1(f_+)-
\ell_1(f_-).
\]

Then the Clark ports are exactly

\[
E=
\frac12(a-d),
\qquad
E^*=
\frac12(a+d).
\]

## Supply form

Their incoming-minus-outgoing energy is

\[
|E|^2-|E^*|^2
=
-
\operatorname{Re}
\left(a\overline d\right).
\]

The associated Hermitian supply operator is

\[
Q_{\rm Clark}
=
-
\frac12
\left(a^*d+d^*a\right).
\]

Writing the operator in the two-sheet decomposition, its diagonal blocks are

\[
Q_{++}
=
-
\frac12
\left(
\ell_0^*\ell_1+
\ell_1^*\ell_0
\right),
\]

\[
Q_{--}
=
+
\frac12
\left(
\ell_0^*\ell_1+
\ell_1^*\ell_0
\right),
\]

and its cross block is

\[
Q_{-+}
=
\frac12
\left(
\ell_1^*\ell_0-
\ell_0^*\ell_1
\right).
\]

Thus the missing cross supply is source-defined, rank at most two, and skew-adjoint between the two reciprocal sheets.

Under the Riesz identification in \(L^2(\Phi du)\), the vectors representing \(\ell_0\) and \(\ell_1\) are \(1\) and \(u\). Hence

\[
Q_{-+}
=
\frac12
\left(
|u\rangle\langle1|
-
|1\rangle\langle u|
\right).
\]

No zero data enter this formula.

## Forced Sylvester equation

For doubled storage

\[
P_K
=
\begin{pmatrix}
I&K^*\\
K&I
\end{pmatrix},
\]

the cross KYP block is forced to satisfy

\[
A_-^*K
+
KA_+
=
Q_{-+}.
\]

Testing against reciprocal exponential sections gives the formal matrix-element formula

\[
\langle e_t^-,Ke_s^+\rangle
=
\frac{
\langle e_t^-,Q_{-+}e_s^+\rangle
}{
\overline t+s
},
\]

away from the Sylvester characteristic set \(\overline t+s=0\).

The numerator is explicit:

\[
\langle e_t^-,Q_{-+}e_s^+\rangle
=
\frac12
\left[
\ell_1(e_t^-)
\overline{\ell_0(e_s^+)}
-
\ell_0(e_t^-)
\overline{\ell_1(e_s^+)}
\right],
\]

up to the fixed inner-product convention.

## New obstruction

The usual stable Sylvester formula

\[
K
=
\int_0^\infty
 e^{rA_-^*}
Q_{-+}
 e^{rA_+}
\,dr
\]

is not automatically available: the reflected generator \(A_-\) has the opposite transport orientation. The pair is not jointly exponentially stable.

The denominator \(\overline t+s\) displays the same issue as a resonance along the reciprocal characteristic set.

Therefore the remaining construction is not the identification of the forcing term; that term is now explicit. The remaining task is to select a source-defined boundary condition or principal-value inverse for this Sylvester operator and prove that the resulting \(K\) is bounded and contractive.

## Control interpretation

The zeroth moment is the common-mode output. The first moment difference is the differential-mode output. The Clark supply is their rank-two gyrator coupling.

The missing storage is the bounded energy operator generated by this gyrator inside a stable reciprocal feedback realization.

## Disposition

The cross forcing in the doubled KYP equation has been derived entirely from the theta moments. It is the rank-two operator

\[
\frac12
\left(
|u\rangle\langle1|
-
|1\rangle\langle u|
\right).
\]

The unresolved analytic gate is inversion of the oppositely oriented Sylvester operator with a source-derived boundary prescription and contraction bound.
