# Prior-research search for the G4 source-division theorem

Date: 2026-09-08

## Search target

The target was a parameterwise source identity or chain homotopy proving

\[
r_U=\tau h_U
\]

for the complete arithmetic Evans residual, before restriction to the Xi
divisor and with multiplicity jets.

## Strongest positive result found

Grothendieck's Mellin source saturation proves that the multiplier-invariant
source ideal is exactly

\[
I_E=(\Xi).
\]

The quotient `B/(Xi)` canonically recovers the zero divisor and local lengths.
Its associated-graded jet boundary carries a closed normal compact-resolvent
operator with

\[
\Xi(z)=\Xi(0)\det_2(I-zA_J^{-1}).
\]

This is a genuine source-derived divisor and multiplicity theorem.  It does
not construct the physical coefficient/Green chain pushforward, identify the
Evans residual with the Mellin source ideal, or prove self-adjointness.  It
therefore closes the triangular divisor compiler, not conservative promotion.

## Strongest exact negative result found

For reciprocal tails, the combined reverse-arrow current is

\[
\int_0^\infty f(G_++G_-)\,dq=G_+(0)G_-(0).
\]

At a scalar zero, `G_-(0)=-G_+(0)=-b`, so the residual is

\[
-b^2,
\]

not zero in general.  Reciprocal sewing preserves the interference channel
rather than supplying the required adjoint cancellation.  Requiring both
routes to vanish would replace scalar cancellation by route collapse and
change the divisor.

The minimal even Fourier chart independently gives a full-rank reverse-
incidence defect.  Fourier involution fixes the maximal-isotropic boundary but
does not generate bulk adjoint backreaction.

## Rejected apparent matches

- The Mellin jet operator has the correct determinant but is self-adjoint iff
  RH; it is not the missing physical Green operator.
- Reciprocal-tail adjointization yields the interference square, not zero.
- Even Fourier completion leaves a rank-two reverse-incidence residual in the
  minimal chart.
- Low-order source moment constraints leave nearly the full tested character
  range and cannot force cancellation.
- Defining `h_U=r_U/tau` merely restates divisibility unless removability is
  independently proved.

## Search disposition

No prior packet supplies `r_U=tau h_U` or the complete residual-jet vanishing.
The closest positive theorem constructs the Xi divisor intrinsically in a
Mellin source quotient; the closest physical calculations are exact no-go
results showing why reciprocal/Fourier closure does not produce the adjoint
map.  New work must construct an explicit comparison from the Mellin source
ideal/jet boundary to the complete five-port Evans residual, or an independent
parameterwise cancellation identity.  Without that comparison, importing the
Mellin determinant into the Green pencil is not authorized.
