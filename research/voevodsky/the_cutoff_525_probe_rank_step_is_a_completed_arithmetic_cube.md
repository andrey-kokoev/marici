# The cutoff-525 probe-rank step is a completed arithmetic cube

## Question

Does the repeated factor \(5^2\) at grade 525 complete categorical squares, and does their coherence explain the new cycle direction invisible to one shell probe?

## Claim boundary

Yes in the finite 1-skeletal cubical model. At cutoff 525 the first three consecutive-prime shell actions complete an exact Boolean 3-cube from 30 to 105. Its six square faces have one boundary relation. The unique one-setting blind cycle is a four-face linear combination inside this cube. This establishes a finite arithmetic cubical mechanism; it supplies no physical realization or higher-categorical filler beyond the explicitly constructed commuting-action cube.

## Shell actions

The first three shell actions multiply a divisible source by

\[
\frac32,\qquad\frac53,\qquad\frac75.
\]

Starting at 30, their Boolean subsets give the eight vertices

\[
30;
\quad45,50,42;
\quad75,63,70;
\quad105.
\]

Each path applies a subset of the three commuting ratios. The total composite is

\[
30\cdot\frac32\cdot\frac53\cdot\frac75=105.
\]

The resulting diagram is a functor from the Boolean cube \(\{0<1\}^3\) into the finite route category.

## Grade-525 completion

Immediately below the transition, at cutoff 522, the graph has 148 edges, cycle dimension 21, and 21 arithmetic commuting squares. Their boundary vectors are independent and span the cycle space.

At cutoff 525 two edges enter:

The graph then has 150 edges, cycle dimension 22, and 23 square boundaries of rank 22. Hence the square-boundary module acquires exactly one relation.

## Cube boundary relation

Orient each square by the ordered shell pair. The unique square relation uses the six faces:

\[

-S_{30,63}^{13}
+S_{30,70}^{23}

## Blind cycle

For the first setting \(t=5/6\), the joint kernel of ordinary and modulated incidence is one-dimensional. A generator decomposes into four square faces:

\[

\frac56S_{30,75}^{12}
-S_{30,70}^{23}

Thus the 12-edge countercycle is square-generated. Its invisibility is organized by cube coherence rather than by an isolated long polygon.

At cutoff 522 this joint kernel is zero. At 525 the source cycle dimension rises from 21 to 22 while the one-setting response rank remains 21. A second modulation setting separates the new direction.

## Interpretation

The arithmetic square \(5^2\) is the grade marker because

\[
525=3\cdot5^2\cdot7.
\]

The shared prime 5 mediates the three adjacent shell actions. Its repeated occurrence admits the final edge \(75\to105\), completing the cube face. The geometrical rank step is the simultaneous appearance of one new square-generated cycle and one relation among square boundaries.
