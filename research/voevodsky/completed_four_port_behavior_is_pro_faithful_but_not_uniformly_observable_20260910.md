# Completed four-port behavior is pro-faithful but not uniformly observable

## Question

Does finite character-by-arithmetic separation extend to the unbounded prime-and-grade carrier, and in what sense?

## Claim boundary

Prior results imply faithfulness into a pro- or full-orbit record object when the already retained discrete prime/grade source is used. They do not provide one fixed finite observer packet or a cutoff-independent lower frame bound in every analytic topology.

## Prime multiplicity

For a finite prime packet \(c=(c_p)\), the complete Mellin orbit is

\[
(\mathcal Mc)(t)=\sum_pc_pe^{it\log p}.
\]

With the Fourier--Bohr invariant-mean norm,

\[
\|\mathcal Mc\|_{B^2}^2=\sum_p|c_p|^2.
\]

Thus the full orbit is isometric to the discrete prime register. A fixed finite observation window does not have this property because logarithmic prime frequencies crowd, but the complete invariant orbit separates them exactly.

In the retained architecture, the discrete prime/grade carrier is already a source object. Therefore the observer need not derive discrete labels from norm-continuous Mellin transport. Fourier--Bohr synthesis is an equivalent presentation of the source-labelled prime register.

## Grade multiplicity

Let

\[
J_N:X\to Y_N
\]

record the first \(N\) source-authorized grade or Mellin-jet coordinates. Prior analytic uniqueness gives

\[
\bigcap_N\ker J_N=\{0\}.
\]

Hence

\[
J_\infty:X\to\varprojlim_NY_N
\]

is monic. No fixed \(J_N\) is uniformly injective when the number of independent grades is unbounded.

This agrees with the shift-defect behavior theorem: finite cutoff \(n\) is separated by native words of length at most \(2n-1\), but no fixed word depth covers all cutoffs.

## Four-character factor

The universal boundary character packet \(W\) has a fixed faithful four-row Fourier observer \(A_F\). Tensoring a monomorphism with this finite-dimensional faithful factor preserves separation on the declared algebraic/pro system. Therefore

\[
A_F\otimes J_\infty
:
W\otimes X
\longrightarrow
\varprojlim_N(W_{\rm rec}\otimes Y_N)
\]

is monic.

Equivalently, if every finite character-by-grade record of a state vanishes, then the state vanishes in the retained source quotient.

The four-port observer and the arithmetic tower perform different tasks:

- four Fourier characters separate boundary type;
- the prime/grade behavior family separates multiplicity within each type.

## No uniform promotion

Three properties remain distinct:

1. **pro-faithfulness**
   \[
   \bigcap_N\ker(A_F\otimes J_N)=0;
   \]
2. **finite observability:** one fixed \(N\) is injective;
3. **uniform observability:** one completed record norm has a cutoff-independent lower bound.

The first is supported. The second fails over unbounded grade multiplicity. The third is not implied by analytic uniqueness and remains topology-dependent.

A sequence may have unit source norm while escaping every fixed finite probe packet. Such a sequence does not contradict pro-faithfulness; each nonzero state is detected somewhere in the tower, but the detecting stage and conditioning need not be uniform.

## Coalgebra formulation

For the bounded constructor category \(C\), define

\[
\beta(x)=\bigl(O(T_wx)\bigr)_w.
\]

The character-by-prime/grade records form a source-derived subfamily of these behavior coordinates. Their pro-faithfulness implies that the behavioral radical is zero on the retained algebraic source and its admitted analytic-uniqueness class:

\[
\operatorname{Rad}_{\rm beh}
=
\bigcap_w\ker(O\circ T_w)
=0,
\]

provided the word family includes the full prime/grade probes represented by \(J_\infty\).

This equality is a monicity statement. It is not a coercivity estimate.

## Disposition

The completed four-port observer is best typed as a pro-faithful behavior coalgebra:

\[
\text{four boundary characters}
\otimes
\text{complete prime/grade probe tower}.
\]

It separates the retained source without requiring a fixed finite observer packet. A uniform completed lower bound remains unproved and should not be inferred from pro-faithfulness.
