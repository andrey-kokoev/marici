    q' = q + r(z-a),    z' = a.

It is an involution. At z=0 it realizes the projection on the carrier, while

    ||q'||^2 + (r dot r)*(z')^2 = ||q||^2 + (r dot r)*z^2.

not discarded as an endpoint-only description. Whole-composite comparisons and

1. Specify which existing witness is being transported: reversible amplitude

2. Construct its map to the retained native phase/seam records and test the

   accounting. The new geometric phase bridge already recovers the native seam
   controls for the occupation branch.

   actual seam projectors, including record reuse/back-action. Do not claim
   autonomous stable storage merely from one projection endpoint.
4. Retain the earlier mass-comparison failures and the independent electron

A uniform witness satisfying the declared construction contract is legitimate
progress even if alternative witnesses exist. Claims that occupation is forced,
that one gap is unique, or that measured masses follow are separate stronger
claims and require separate tests.
