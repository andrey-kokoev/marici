# The Bunch–Davies boundary germ does not order the paired thimbles

## Question

Can the source-authorized negative-imaginary boundary prescription provide the missing global ordering of the two total-energy thimbles and thereby recover the second mod-two direction?

## Claim boundary

This tests the local boundary germ and the paired-node equation. It does not classify contours that leave the local disk and encounter remote singularities.

## Even paired-node geometry

The total-energy boundary quartic is

\[
F(t)|_{E=0}=(xt^2+y)^2.
\]

Its two nodes satisfy

\[
t_\pm=\pm\sqrt{-y/x}
\]

and are exchanged by \(t\mapsto-t\). The first normal derivative at either node is identical:

\[
\left.\partial_EF\right|_{E=0,t=t_+}
=
\left.\partial_EF\right|_{E=0,t=t_-}
=
\frac{2y(x+y)}x.
\]

Thus a prescription acting only on the common total-energy normal coordinate approaches both nodes from the same local side. It does not distinguish \(t_+\) from \(t_-\).

## Boundary-value prescription

Prior source work gives a negative-imaginary germ

\[
p\mapsto p-i(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+3\epsilon_3),
\qquad \epsilon_i>0.
\]

Scaled-monodromy analysis proves that its punctured family is trivial, does not permute the walls, and generates no \(\mu_2\)-odd thimble. Therefore this germ cannot turn the unordered pair \(\{\Theta_+,\Theta_-\}\) into two globally marked integral classes.

## Mod-two consequence

Since the prescription preserves the exchange symmetry, it retains only

\[
\Theta_++\Theta_-
\mod2.
\]

It supplies no independent class separating the two nodes. Hence it cannot determine both coordinates of

\[
(a,b)\in(\mathbb Z/2)^2.
\]

## Remaining route

A genuine ordering must come from global information absent from the local boundary germ, such as:

- based vanishing paths from a fixed regular fiber to each critical value;
- a global branch-cut system on the quartic cover;
- an integral Picard marking of the resulting thimbles;
- a contour coupling to remote singularities that is independently source-authorized.

The local Bunch–Davies side is boundary-value authority, not global thimble-marking authority.

## Disposition

The source-authorized local boundary prescription cannot provide the missing second parity direction. The global ordered Picard marking remains the first absent typed object.
