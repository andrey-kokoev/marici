## Disposition

The consumer-side v3 interface shape is complete. All internal observer and analytic response cells have declared types and witnesses. Two external cells remain open: canonical arithmetic loading and equality of G4 sewing with retained sewing. Both require authority or declarations from the G4 source owner; further abstraction cannot close them. The determinant-line compiler remains a separate project.
