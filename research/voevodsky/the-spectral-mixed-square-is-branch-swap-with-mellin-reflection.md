# The spectral mixed square is branch swap with Mellin reflection

## Question

What action on the canonical-dual spectral pair corresponds to reciprocal reflection of the source observer?

## Claim boundary

On the compatible paired spectral image, reciprocal reflection is analytically realized by spectral-variable reflection together with exchange of the canonical and dual branches, under the recorded paired local-factor normalization. This is the half-turn induced by Fourier square, not yet a quarter-turn action on the multiplicative observer presentation.

Let

$$
(\iota g)(a)=g(a^{-1}).
$$

Multiplicative Mellin transform gives

$$
m_{\iota g}(s)=m_g(-s).
$$

The opposite canonical and dual local factors satisfy the reflected normalization that exchanges them under \(s\mapsto-s\). Hence define

$$
\rho_3(\xi^+,\xi^-)(s)
=
(\xi^-(-s),\xi^+(-s)).
$$

For the paired source-to-spectral transform,

$$
C_{13}(g)=(\Omega_S^+g,\Omega_S^-g),
$$

one obtains

$$
\rho_3C_{13}(g)=C_{13}(\iota g).
$$

Thus

$$
\rho_3C_{13}=C_{13}\iota.
$$

Applying the swap-reflection twice gives

$$
\rho_3^2=\operatorname{id}.
$$

The scattering ratio transforms by

$$
J_{\mathrm{loc},S}(s)\longmapsto J_{\mathrm{loc},S}(-s)=J_{\mathrm{loc},S}(s)^{-1},
$$

and its logarithmic connection obeys

$$
V_{\mathrm{loc},S}(s)\longmapsto -V_{\mathrm{loc},S}(-s).
$$

## Quarter-turn distinction

The previously constructed source action \(\iota\) is the reflection associated with additive Fourier square. Therefore the mixed squares currently proved compare the \(D\)-fiber half-turn with presentation-dependent reciprocal actions. A genuine quarter-turn square requires an additive Fourier action on a larger source carrier before passage to the multiplicative observer algebra.

## Disposition

The source-to-spectral mixed square is analytically constructed for the reciprocal half-turn. Together with the source-to-geometric square, this gives two half-turn-equivariant presentation maps. It does not yet construct the quarter-turn equivariance needed for the full four-port twistor fiber.
