# A second-position-graph dual functional realizes the primitive current

## Question

After the fixed Hilbert Green transpose range fails, is there an explicit source-derived distributional functional whose pullback is the primitive row \((\log p)p^{-1/2}\)?

## Claim boundary

Yes for translated cut atoms with nonzero finite mass and finite first moment. A centered first-moment functional is continuous on the second-position graph and evaluates a translate at its displacement. This realizes the primitive row through Euler-weighted synthesis. It does not provide a Riesz representative in the original Green Hilbert space.

## Problem

For

\[
Ke_p=p^{-1/2}c_{\log p},
\]

primitive provenance requires a functional \(L\) satisfying

\[
L(c_a)=a.
\]

No bounded functional on the fixed Green Hilbert space can do this when \(\|c_a\|\) is independent of \(a\).

## Bold conjecture

Adding only the first position graph norm \(\|f\|_2+\|Qf\|_2\) makes the moment functional \(f\mapsto\int qf(q)\,dq\) continuous.

## Named rivals

1. The second position graph \(\operatorname{Dom}Q^2\) is sufficient.
2. A fitted coordinate evaluation can reproduce \(a\) but lacks translation covariance.
3. The cut atom has zero mass, so no affine moment can recover displacement.

## Position graph

Let \(Q\) be multiplication by \(q\) on \(L^2(\mathbb R)\), and define

\[
\mathcal G_{Q^2}=\operatorname{Dom}(1+Q^2)
\]

with norm

\[
\|f\|_{Q^2}=\|(1+q^2)f\|_2.
\]

The linear-moment functional

\[
M_1(f)=\int_{\mathbb R}qf(q)\,dq
\]

is continuous because

\[
|M_1(f)|
\le
\left\|\frac{q}{1+q^2}\right\|_2
\|(1+q^2)f\|_2.
\]

Similarly, the mass functional

\[
M_0(f)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(q)\,dq
\]

is continuous on the same graph.

The first-position graph alone is insufficient for this proof: \(q/(1+q^2)^{1/2}\) is not square integrable. Thus the bold conjecture is rejected at exactly one missing position order.

## Translation coordinate

Let

\[
c_a(q)=\Phi(q-a),
\]

and set

\[
m_0=\int\Phi(q)\,dq,
\qquad
m_1=\int q\Phi(q)\,dq.
\]

Assume \(m_0\ne0\), as required independently for a nontrivial mass/seam channel. Translation gives

\[
M_0(c_a)=m_0,
\qquad
M_1(c_a)=am_0+m_1.
\]

Define the centered functional

\[
L_{\rm prim}
=
\frac1{m_0}M_1-rac{m_1}{m_0^2}M_0.
\]

Then

\[
L_{\rm prim}(c_a)=a.
\]

For an even centered Gaussian packet, \(m_1=0\), so this reduces to \(M_1/m_0\).

## Primitive transpose identity

Applying this functional to the Euler-weighted columns yields

\[
(K'L_{\rm prim})_p
=
L_{\rm prim}(Ke_p)
=
p^{-1/2}L_{\rm prim}(c_{\log p})
=
(\log p)p^{-1/2}.
\]

Therefore the primitive arithmetic row belongs to the transpose range

\[
a_{\rm prim}\in K'(\mathcal G_{Q^2}').
\]

It does not belong to \(K'(\mathcal G)\) when \(\mathcal G\) is the fixed Hilbert Green space with uniformly normed translated columns.

## Translation covariance

For translation \((\tau_bf)(q)=f(q-b)\),

\[
M_0(\tau_bf)=M_0(f),
\qquad
M_1(\tau_bf)=M_1(f)+bM_0(f).
\]

Hence

\[
L_{\rm prim}(\tau_bc_a)=L_{\rm prim}(c_a)+b=a+b.
\]

The logarithmic coordinate is therefore generated by the source translation action rather than fitted independently at each prime.

## Dagger and seam compatibility

The functional lies on the distributional side of the rigging

\[
\mathcal G_{Q^2}
\subset L^2(\mathbb R)
\subset\mathcal G_{Q^2}'.
\]

Its dagger action is transpose transport on this rigging. Since seam translation acts affinely on \((M_0,M_1)\), the pair forms a two-dimensional translation module; centering selects the displacement coordinate while retaining the mass coordinate required to state the affine law.

No Hilbert adjoint vector should be inferred from this dual functional.

## Strongest residual

This construction assumes the analytic local column is the declared translated packet \(c_a=\tau_a\Phi\) in a common position coordinate and that \(m_0\ne0\). If wall extension changes the column by a nontranslated correction, that correction's first two moments must be included explicitly. The acceptance test is still exact:

\[
L_{\rm prim}(c_{\log p})=\log p
\]

for every source label.

## Disposition

Primitive-current transpose provenance is constructed on the second-position graph dual. The fixed Hilbert obstruction remains valid and identifies why this stronger rung is necessary. The next separate range tests are the square, connected, and archimedean rows; none may inherit primitive provenance merely from stronger Euler decay.
