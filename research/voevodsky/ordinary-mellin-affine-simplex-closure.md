# Ordinary Mellin affine-simplex closure

## Question

Does the ordinary-domain Mellin chain object admit every affine simplex supported on the four fixture vertices?

## Claim boundary

The declaration applies only where the spectral domain contains the relevant convex hull and Mellin evaluation, differentiation, and compact simplex integration are valid. It does not establish modular naturality or completed cutoff descent.

## Chain object

The ordinary spectral parameter has declared affine structure. On its admissible convex domain, take the affine singular chain complex generated by

\[
[x,y](t)=(1-t)x+ty
\]

and

\[
[x,y,z](r,s)=(1-r-s)x+ry+sz.
\]

Its triangle boundary is

\[
d_2[x,y,z]=[y,z]-[x,z]+[x,y].
\]

This extends the previously declared straight Mellin orbit by admitting its affine homotopies; it does not choose an antiderivative or use zero data.

## Exact fixture result

For \(w,-w,a,-a\), all 12 oriented triangles supported by the ten retained edges are nondegenerate affine simplices and are closed under reciprocal reflection. Their boundary span has rank 7, equal to the full cycle-space dimension. Hence

\[
H_1=0.
\]

The former odd generator is filled exactly by

\[
-[w,-w,-a]+[-w,w,a].
\]

## Revised obstruction

The one-dimensional odd class was an artifact of restricting the chain object to four selected triangles. It is absent on the ordinary affine-simplex domain.

The remaining gates are:

- naturality of affine fillers under the actual modular and basis actions;
- preservation under Euler-cutoff transitions;
- uniform graph-topology descent and boundary-null preservation.

## Disposition

Ordinary-domain algebraic exactness is now closed through the affine 2-simplex layer. The RH-bearing obstruction, if any, lies in modular naturality or completion, not in the finite ordinary-domain \(H_1\) fixture.
