# Suzuki's intersection kernel is the Hardy kernel minus the leakage short

## Leakage operator

Work in the Hardy realization and define

\[
L=P_-K|_{H_+}:H_+\longrightarrow H_-.
\]

Then

\[
V=H_+\cap KH_+=\ker L.
\]

Because `K` is isometric and `P_-` is an orthogonal projection, `L` is a contraction.

The projected resolvent family from the previous note is

\[
r_w=P_Vk_w,
\qquad
P_V=P_{\ker L}.
\]

## Closed-range formula

Assume first that `ran L` is closed. Then `LL^*` is invertible on `ran L`, and the orthogonal projection onto the closure of `ran L^*` is

\[
P_{\overline{\operatorname{ran}L^*}}
=
L^*(LL^*)^{-1}_{\operatorname{ran}L}L.
\]

Since

\[
H_+=\ker L\oplus\overline{\operatorname{ran}L^*},
\]

we obtain

\[
\boxed{
P_V
=I-L^*(LL^*)^{-1}_{\operatorname{ran}L}L.
}
\]

Using the Moore--Penrose inverse, this is

\[
P_V=I-L^*(LL^*)^\dagger L.
\]

## Exact kernel subtraction

Let

\[
k_0(z,w)=\langle k_w,k_z\rangle
\]

be the free Hardy kernel. Substituting the projection formula gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
K_V(z,w)
&=\langle P_Vk_w,k_z\rangle\\
&=k_0(z,w)
-
\left\langle
(LL^*)^{-1/2}Lk_w,
(LL^*)^{-1/2}Lk_z
\right\rangle.
\end{aligned}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
K_V=k_0-K_{leak},
}
\]

where

\[
K_{leak}(z,w)
=
\left\langle d_w,d_z\right\rangle,
\qquad
d_w=(LL^*)^{-1/2}Lk_w.
\]

Both `k_0` and `K_leak` are positive kernels, and their difference is positive because it is an orthogonal short of `k_0`, not because of a separate arithmetic inequality.

This is an exact source-derived positive-minus-positive identity using only `K` and Hardy projections.

## Relation to operator shorting

`P_V` is the shorted identity operator to the closed subspace `ker L`. The leakage term is the least positive correction that removes every component detected by `L`. In variational form,

\[
\|P_Vf\|^2
=
\inf_{h\in\overline{\operatorname{ran}L^*}}
\|f-h\|^2.
\]

Thus no smaller orthogonal correction can force exact Hardy compatibility.

## Non-closed range

Without closed range, `(LL^*)^dagger` is unbounded. The formal vector

\[
(LL^*)^{-1/2}Lk_w
\]

may fail to belong to `H_-`, even though `P_Vk_w` remains well-defined. A bounded regularization is

\[
P_{V,\epsilon}
=I-L^*(LL^*+\epsilon I)^{-1}L.
\]

Spectral calculus gives

\[
P_{V,\epsilon}
\xrightarrow[s]{\epsilon\downarrow0}
P_V.
\]

The corresponding kernels are

\[
K_{V,\epsilon}(z,w)
=k_0(z,w)-
\left\langle
(LL^*+\epsilon I)^{-1/2}Lk_w,
(LL^*+\epsilon I)^{-1/2}Lk_z
\right\rangle.
\]

For each `epsilon>0`, the subtracted term is positive. However, `P_{V,epsilon}` is a positive contraction but not a projection, and its limit can still be zero.

## Coercivity is exactly bounded inverse leakage

Closed range of `L` is equivalent to the existence of `c>0` such that

\[
\|Lf\|\ge c\|f\|
\qquad
(f\perp\ker L).
\]

Equivalently, the nonzero spectrum of `L^*L` is bounded away from zero. This is the same Friedrichs-angle gap identified in the alternating-projection analysis.

Therefore a bounded formula for the leakage correction requires precisely the missing coercivity theorem. The Moore--Penrose expression does not evade it; without closed range it hides the difficulty in an unbounded inverse.

## Candidate arithmetic Green identity in normal form

The desired equality

\[
W(r_w*r_z^*)=2K_V(z,w)
\]

would become

\[
\boxed{
W(r_w*r_z^*)
=2k_0(z,w)-2\langle d_w,d_z\rangle.
}
\]

This is now a rigid endpoint--gamma--prime target: the completed arithmetic correction to the free Hardy kernel must equal the normalized leakage Gram generated by

\[
L=P_-\mathcal F^{-1}M_\Theta J\mathcal F|_{H_+}.
\]

A proof cannot obtain the right side merely from positivity; it must compute the Green boundary term produced by the completed symbol.

## Source status

The leakage operator is source-defined through the completed analytic function `E` and its boundary ratio `Theta`. But it is not prime-local: `Theta` already contains the globally completed zeta function. To satisfy the stronger endpoint--gamma--prime provenance requirement, one must derive the same `L^*(LL^*)^dagger L` correction from the explicit formula before invoking the analytic quotient `E#/E`.

That comparison is precisely where the prime sign and endpoint cancellation must be retained.

## Disposition

The continuous Suzuki kernel has the exact operator normal form

\[
\boxed{
K_V=k_0-
\left\langle
(LL^*)^{-1/2}Lk_\bullet,
(LL^*)^{-1/2}Lk_\bullet
\right\rangle.
}
\]

This constructs the correction abstractly and identifies two remaining gates:

1. closed-range coercivity for a bounded leakage inverse;
2. a source explicit-formula identity equating the arithmetic correction with this leakage short.
