## Relative cells

For a nonempty shell set \(I\), let

\[
B_I=\prod_{i\in I}p_i,
\qquad
L(I)=p_{\max I}\prod_{i\in I}p_{i+1}.
\]

A cell with direction set \(I\) and scale \(k\) is born at grade

\[
g(k,I)=kL(I).
\]

Therefore cells born at the target grade \(L_6\) are exactly pairs satisfying

\[

\]

Their base natural is \(kB_I\). Degree-zero generators are vertices incident to a newly born edge whose least incident grade is \(L_6\).

\[
Y=X_{\leq L_6},
\qquad
A=X_{<L_6}.
\]

The relative chain group \(C_r(Y,A)\) is generated by cells born exactly at \(L_6\). For a new cell, compute its ordinary cubical boundary and discard every face already in \(A\). The remaining faces define

\[
\bar d_r:C_r(Y,A)\longrightarrow C_{r-1}(Y,A).

\[
