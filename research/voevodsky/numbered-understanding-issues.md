## Important corrections after searching

- Do not say photon states are wholly unconstructed: conditional physical-field
  models exist. U8 is the missing native propagation/energy/realization bridge.
- Do not say equal-and-opposite charge has no construction: the primitive quotient
  supplies one. U4 is its external-action selection and physical identification.
- Do not say coupling or energy accounting must be invented from scratch: signed
  record gates, common-reference dressing, reversible memory and occupation laws
  exist. U6/U7/U9 require their correct transport, admission and discriminating
  physical consequences.
- Do not say every 3D binding model has failed: the large-stiffness gapless-field
  witness exists. U10 retains its exact parameter and infrared boundaries.

## Work deliberately excluded from this list

The remaining operational whole-return source/schedule binding and certificate
assembly are mainly concrete proof work. Tighter adaptive real-axis integration,
energy sweeps and propagation through known optical identities are mainly
calculation work. A digital circuit for the invariant sixteen-state quantum model
is a known engineering route once a platform/design contract is chosen.

Such work is unfinished, but not automatically evidence of missing conceptual
understanding.

## Suggested order

U8 deserves a direct native spatial-carrier/continuation comparison because prior
research already isolates the wrong-reader and bounded-displacement failures.
U4/U7 deserve a source contract test distinguishing phase registration, neutral
pair operation, number-preserving dressing and actual conversion. U6 should use
held-out energy comparisons with the law frozen, not fitted masses. These tests
can discriminate mechanisms rather than merely refine an arbitrary fixture.
