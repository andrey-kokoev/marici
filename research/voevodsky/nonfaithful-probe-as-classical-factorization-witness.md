# A nonfaithful probe as a classical-factorization witness

## Question

What can Kitaev's negative Bargmann loop establish even though the scalar probe is not faithful enough to reconstruct the full associator configuration?

## Claim boundary

This packet uses the exact negative ordered product to exclude positive multiplicative factorization through a commutative event algebra. It does not exclude signed or complex quasiprobability simulations, establish computational advantage, or reconstruct the underlying projectors.

## Two distinct uses of a probe

Probe faithfulness and rival exclusion are different tasks.

- **Reconstruction:** infer the underlying coherence composite from all probe values. This requires a monic or jointly faithful coordinate family.
- **Separation:** show that the observed value lies outside the image of a named rival model class. This requires only a witness functional separating that image from the observed point.

The Bargmann trace fails the first task globally but succeeds at the second for positive commutative factorization.

## Commutative positivity cone

Let \(E_1,\ldots,E_4\) be commuting positive operators in a finite-dimensional commutative event algebra and let \(\varphi\) be a positive state. Simultaneous diagonalization gives nonnegative scalar functions \(e_i\), so

\[
\varphi(E_1E_2E_3E_4)\ge0.
\]

Therefore any negative ordered loop value lies outside this positive commutative image.

Kitaev's frozen electric witness is

\[
\Omega=\operatorname{Tr}(P_AQ_AP_BQ_C)=-\frac18.
\]

Every factor is a positive rank-one projector, but they do not commute. The negative sign is generated by ordered noncommutative composition.

## Pyramid overlay

This changes the role of the loop record. It should carry two independent strengths:

- `coordinate_strength`: how much of the underlying holonomy it reconstructs;
- `rival_exclusion_strength`: which factorization classes its value rules out.

For the current witness:

- coordinate strength is one nonfaithful scalar invariant;
- rival exclusion strength rules out positive multiplicative commutative-record factorization;
- physical constructor status remains proposed;
- shared-control and route-dephasing faults remain separate.

Thus nonfaithfulness does not make the probe uninformative. It prevents reconstruction claims while preserving an exact no-go theorem.

## Record-boundary localization

The negative witness also constrains where classical record formation may occur in any physical realization. A commuting pointer may record the final scalar only after the noncommutative cycle has been compositionally closed by an admitted physical interface.

This statement concerns composition stage and declared physical readout, not an implicit temporal interpretation of the Carrier.

## Exact fixture

Four real qubit rays at angles \(0,\pi/3,2\pi/3,\pi\) yield positive rank-one projectors with ordered trace \(-1/8\). Their adjacent commutators are nonzero. Commuting positive diagonal fixtures always give nonnegative ordered trace.

The negative associator loop is not a faithful coordinate, but it is a sharp separating witness against positive commutative record factorization. The pyramid registry should preserve both modalities instead of collapsing every probe assessment into `verified` or `nonfaithful`.
