# RH and the shared-edge seed: signed packet expressibility, not an arithmetic realization

## Result

The actual native relationship space can faithfully express a supplied finite
Hermitian comparison packet, including its negative directions. It does not
force that packet to be positive.

Using thirteen source-sink channels already extracted from the 1,836 transfers,
we construct an injective linear attachment B_G satisfying

    B_G^dagger K B_G=G

for **every supplied Hermitian packet G of dimension at most thirteen**.
Here K is the analytically derived Hermitian part of the native transfer
operator, not a newly selected physical Hamiltonian.

A source-rooted pair of commuting comparison triangles can carry this form.
An exact indefinite example still gives a negative value -3/5 while both
triangles commute. Thus geometric/shared-edge coherence alone does not prove
the positivity needed for RH.

**Not completed:** an incidence-respecting realization of the actual prime,
gamma, endpoint and zero operations on the two geometric seed triangles.
The tested triangles are explicit changes of a supplied packet's chart;
their commutation is not an independently derived explicit formula for zeta.

## Governing question and source boundary

The task was to test whether the seed's existing relationships can carry the
RH comparison without replacing it with an automatically positive Gram form.
The contract froze four rivals: automatic positivity from positive local
projectors, automatic faithful observation from an injective attachment,
arithmetic identity from source-rooted coherence, and automatic completion
from finite embeddings.

The source object is the unchanged typed coefficient network generated by
AB,BC,CA,AD,DB,BA and its supplied geometry. Its exact operator W and
K=(W+W^dagger)/2 are reused from
`proton-native-relationship-structure-v1.md`. All bound source hashes are
verified. No probe control or Cayley synthesis is used here.

The supplied packet G is an input, **not something derived from the bare seed**.
This distinction prevents the construction from claiming arithmetic content
merely because its matrix can be embedded.

## Exact signed attachment

Let T be the thirteen-channel transient map, with T^2=0. Choose thirteen
independent columns X of its source projector. Define

    Y=TX,
    D=X^dagger X.

Then D is positive definite, X and Y are orthogonal and

    X^dagger K X=0,
    Y^dagger K Y=0,
    X^dagger K Y=D/2.

For a supplied 13-by-13 Hermitian matrix G, put

    B_G=X+Y D^-1 G.

Direct multiplication gives

    B_G^dagger K B_G=(G+G^dagger)/2=G.

Projection to the source sector gives Ps B_G=X, so B_G is injective regardless
of the sign or degeneracy of G. For n<13 use the first n independent source
columns, their sink images and their n-by-n Gram. The same proof holds over C;
the executable hostile packets use exact Q(sqrt(-3)) arithmetic.

This is a finite signed-form representation theorem. It is **not** an isometry
for the positive native coefficient norm: the represented form is the signed
K pairing. It preserves the negativity question instead of suppressing it.

## Two comparison triangles sharing the attachment

For an invertible packet chart change R define

    G_R=R^-dagger G R^-1,
    B_R=B_G R^-1.

The two source-rooted triangle equations are

    B_R R=B_G,
    R^dagger G_R R=G.

They share the packet-to-native signed-form attachment. Both routes produce
the same comparison. The checker materializes R, its inverse, both form
charts and both coefficient maps.

These are algebraic comparison triangles, not yet the actual geometric cycles
ABC and ADB realizing independently sourced arithmetic and spectral operations.
B_G is generally supported across native stage channels; no local face-support
or seed-generator equivariance for these chart maps is claimed. Assigning
those interpretations afterward would add exactly the missing attachment.

## Three hostiles that the construction must preserve

### 1. Commuting triangles with a negative packet

Use

    G=[[1,-3/5,-3/5],[-3/5,1,-3/5],[-3/5,-3/5,1]].

Each two-coordinate principal restriction is positive definite: diagonal
entries are one and determinant is 16/25. Yet

    (1,1,1)^dagger G (1,1,1)=-3/5.

The native attachment preserves this exact value. Both comparison triangles
still commute. Therefore neither local two-coordinate positivity nor signed
route coherence guarantees complete packet positivity. This hostile is not
asserted to be an actual Weil packet or evidence against RH.

### 2. Injective attachment with an invisible observation

For G=diag(1,0,0), B_G remains injective. The nonzero embedded second-coordinate
vector has positive native norm but zero G quadratic observation.

Consequently an injective carrier map does not establish faithful observation
or eliminate a completed observer kernel. The two properties must be tested
separately on the intended source domain.

### 3. Coherent forms with incompatible chosen vector lifts

The principal restriction of the three-coordinate hostile to two coordinates
is exactly its smaller signed form. But the constructed B_3, restricted to
those two input columns, is not B_2. New cross terms change the sink component.

Thus the finite signed observations cohere while this particular choice of
native vector lifts does not give a strict forward inclusion-compatible tower.
This rejects automatic promotion of the recipe. It does not rule out a
transported comparison, a different recipe or an enlarged completed carrier.
Those would require actual bonding maps and their compatibility proof.

## Fixed-space capacity cannot stand in for the full RH quantifier

The native K has fourteen positive and fifteen negative eigen-directions.
Therefore the positive index of any pullback B^dagger K B is at most fourteen,
and its rank is at most twenty-nine. A positive definite 15-by-15 packet cannot
be faithfully represented by this fixed K pairing.

The thirteen-channel theorem deliberately stays within its guaranteed capacity.
It supplies no representation of all arbitrarily large Weil packets in the
same fixed carrier. Repeating charts or pointing to the number 1,836 does not
remove the inertia bound. A genuine all-packet realization needs a declared
enlargement/completion and source-compatible bonding maps.

`compact-weil-source-identity-instance.json`. It specifies:

- compact logarithmic support and Fourier normalization;
- the gamma/digamma multiplier;

- the mixed rank-two polar endpoint term;
- zero extension and a packet basis/domain convention.

Its status is **internally derived, external source verification pending**.
This turn reads that record; it does not run its older numerical pipelines,
upgrade its status, compute a certified zeta packet or reconstruct zeros.

The first unconstructed connection here is a pair of **independently
source-derived packet realizations**—arithmetic and zero-side—in one verified
normalization, with their shared-edge identity and an incidence-respecting map
to the seed relationships. The universal formula B_G only consumes their
form once supplied; it cannot create those operations or their identity.

The acceptance test is concrete: preserve every labelled arithmetic/spectral
term, certify equality including the omitted tail, verify the intended
seed-face/generator attachment and restriction maps, and leave positivity as
an independently checked condition. Defining both routes from the same chosen
G makes commutation tautological and does not meet that test.

Without new source-realization evidence, this branch stops at the finite
expressibility theorem and its explicit failed promotions. It does not create
a waiting research leaf or claim an RH proof. The remaining all-packet
positivity question is the substantive RH-bearing obligation, not another
finite diagram identity.
