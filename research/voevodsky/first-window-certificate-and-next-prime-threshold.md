# The fixed-window impossibility programme is closed only through the first prime threshold

The combined-symbol route survives the failure of uniform prime cancellation
by retaining the exact multiplier

\[
a_L(u)=
\frac{\operatorname{Re}\psi(1/4+iu/2)-\log\pi}{4\pi}
-
\sum_{\log n\le2L}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\cos(u\log n).
\]

For `L=7/20`, only the first prime channel is active. Prior directed Arb
calculations enclose the bad-frequency set, its concentration operator,
endpoint terms, projected tails, and every pivot of the resulting finite
Schur form. After correction of the endpoint factor `1/2`, the minimum pivot
lower bound remains

\[
0.0017645830061413439.
\]

Thus no violating compactly supported Weil test exists on that specific
window, subject to the separately recorded normalization audit.

## Why this continuation is honest

Compact-torus recurrence prevents replacing the exact symbol by a uniformly
smaller prime norm. The concentration construction instead pays only for the
actual sublevel set

\[
\Omega_{L,\delta}=\{u:a_L(u)<\delta\}.
\]

A successful second-window certificate therefore requires directed bounds
for:

1. all components of `Omega_(L,delta)`;
2. its total measure and negative depth;
3. the concentration trace residual for a declared finite space;
4. endpoint and projected multiplier tails;
5. every interval `LDL*` pivot of the final Schur matrix.

Positivity cannot be propagated across `L_2` merely by continuity: the new
translation channel changes the source operator. The first-prime certificate
supplies the template, not the second-prime result.
