## Additional failure discovered: the coefficient convention

The previous reversal test carried the old five-slot reference basis. In the forward grammar PN swaps slots0/3 and NE swaps slots1/4. Under reader reversal the old inverse NE is supposed to become the new PN, and old inverse PN the new NE.

For all8 full-chart-valid cases, both proposed transfers fail the actual full validator with `Lost a full coefficient coordinate`. Their inverse old coefficient maps are not the required new PN/NE maps. Local phase compatibility had not tested this condition.

If the reversed proton root is explicitly host-configured and ordinary outgoing PN/NE are generated from it, the8 chains pass the native transfer guards and reproduce the intended reversed chart geometry. But their coefficient maps follow the new PN/NE convention, not the previous carried-basis reversal. This is a conditional construction from an additional root, not the missing transition.

## Why this still is not source admission

Every original electron packet is terminal in the forward generator: its outgoing list is empty. For each valid reversed chart family, a direct `reader-reversal` bridge is rejected as an unknown native transfer. Chart/packet constructors can configure the target, but do not emit that bridge.

This does not prove that every alternative path from every original root is impossible. Nor does it show that a newly specified reversed-boundary theory would be inconsistent. It locates the absent operation in the currently declared source interface.

## Rebasings: possible algebra, absent source justification

Exactly4 permutations of the five coefficient slots conjugate the two inverse old operations into the new PN/NE pair. The checker exhausts all120 slot permutations to obtain this fact. These are candidate algebraic adapters, not already admitted basis changes.

No permutation of the original five **geometrically named** references realizes the six-reference reversal. The new five-slot chart includes old M2 and excludes old C0. All six references are retained in this audit, but M2 was geometric context rather than an independently supplied sixth native mode in the source model.

Thus a bare coefficient conjugacy is not a reference-induced relabeling. Any acceptable adapter must explain how the full reference and coefficient provenance is transported, rather than inventing a sixth amplitude or dropping the old outside reference.
