# A finite square proof envelope makes selector coverage explicit

Fix ordered four old square row roots, optionally adjoin weak row `x+y<=3`, target normal (1,1), exact bound 3, and grid {0,1/2,1,3/2,2} for EACH nonnegative multiplier and surplus. Exhaustively enumerate all grid tuples, retaining precisely those whose row-linear normal and row-bound PLUS surplus equal the target. Fresh `check_finite_square_proof_envelope.py` counts 6 old packets and 10 new packets; every old packet embeds with zero new multiplier. Both the c=1 lift of the old upper-row proof and a c=0 old x-cycle proof occur, alongside the new-row one-step proof. The finite selector minimizing new-row mass therefore has an old candidate to choose here.

Completeness is ONLY relative to the explicit grid, target, row order and manifest. A valid old witness (2,3,0,1;c1) for the DIFFERENT target normal (1,1), bound 5 exceeds the multiplier cap and must be reported `OUT_OF_ENVELOPE`, never `no old proof`. These details prevent a finite generator's omission from being promoted to mathematical impossibility. Enumeration still says nothing about which packet was actually used in a historical proof and has no owner attestation.
