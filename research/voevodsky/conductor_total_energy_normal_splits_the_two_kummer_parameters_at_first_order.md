# The conductor total-energy normal splits the two Kummer parameters at first order

## Question

Can the conductor total-energy coordinate itself authorize the normal deformation while retaining one common relative Kummer parameter for both walls?

## Claim boundary

This computes the first normal jet of the audited conductor discriminants. It does not construct the two required relative nearby-cycle objects.

## Discriminant splitting

The audited discriminants obey

\[
\Delta_1-
\Delta_2
=
4E(x-y)\bigl(E^2-E(x+y)+2xy\bigr).
\]

Along the total-energy hypersurface \(E=0\), they agree:

\[
\Delta_1=\Delta_2=4x^2y^2.
\]

However their first derivatives in the conductor normal coordinate \(E\) differ by

\[
\left.\partial_E(\Delta_1-\Delta_2)\right|_{E=0}
=8xy(x-y).
\]

For the orientation branch

\[
Y_1|_{E=0}=Y_2|_{E=0}=Y_0=2xy,
\qquad Y_i^2=\Delta_i,
\]

the square-root derivatives satisfy

\[
\left.\partial_E(Y_1-Y_2)\right|_{E=0}
=
\frac{8xy(x-y)}{2Y_0}
=2(x-y).
\]

Thus for generic \(x\ne y\), the two conductor Kummer parameters separate at first order away from total energy.

## Obstruction to one common deformation

A single relative deformation with one parameter \(Y(q)\) cannot simultaneously satisfy

\[
Y(q)^2=\Delta_1(E=q),
\qquad
Y(q)^2=\Delta_2(E=q)
\]

to first order unless \(x=y\). Holding \(Y=Y_0\) in the symmetric relative normal deformation therefore constructs a valid relative approach, but not the generic normal jet of both conductor walls.

The equalizer component \(x=y\) removes this first-order obstruction, but it is a different specialization and cannot be silently added to the total-energy condition.

## Two-copy consequence

The conductor normal coordinate \(E\) is available, but its lift requires two wall-labelled Kummer parameters

\[
Y_1(E)=\sqrt{\Delta_1(E)},
\qquad
Y_2(E)=\sqrt{\Delta_2(E)}.
\]

They coincide at \(E=0\) and split with relative derivative \(2(x-y)\). Therefore a generic normal comparison must use two site-exchanged relative models or a larger relative arrangement carrying both square roots. One common relative copy cannot encode the first normal jet.

## Disposition

Conductor-side normal authority is supplied by the typed total-energy coordinate \(E\), but it falsifies the one-common-Kummer deformation generically. The next constructor must be wall-labelled: two relative nearby-cycle maps with separate \(Y_i(E)\), glued only on their common special fiber.
