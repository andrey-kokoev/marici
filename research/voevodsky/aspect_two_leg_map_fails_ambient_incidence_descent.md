# The Aspect two-leg map fails ambient incidence descent

## Question

Does the chain map on the realized minimal two-leg subcomplex extend to the full audited enhanced incidence complex?

## Claim boundary

This is an exact integral extension test for the existing incidence and residue matrices. Failure blocks promotion of the selected two-leg map to an ambient source-derived pairing. It is not a theorem excluding a different support-sensitive relative complex or a future physical contour map.

## Complexes

The enhanced incidence differential and conductor comparison are

\[
K:\mathbb Z^4\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^3,
\qquad
J:\mathbb Z^3\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^3,
\]

with

\[
\Phi_{\rm exc}=JK.
\]

The Aspect target differential is

\[
D=\begin{pmatrix}
1&-1\\
-1&1\\
1&-1
\end{pmatrix}:
\mathbb Z^2\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^3.
\]

An ambient degree-one comparison \(P:\mathbb Z^4\to\mathbb Z^2\) compatible with \(J\) would have to satisfy

\[
DP=JK=\Phi_{\rm exc}.
\]

## Cokernel obstruction

The image of \(D\) is generated by \((1,-1,1)^T\). Two integral covectors annihilating it are the rows of

\[
L=\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&0\\
1&0&-1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Therefore existence of \(P\) requires \(L\Phi_{\rm exc}=0\). Direct computation gives

\[
L\Phi_{\rm exc}=
\begin{pmatrix}
2&0&0&-2\\
0&0&2&-2
\end{pmatrix},
\]

which is nonzero and has rank two. Thus no rational or integral ambient chain map \(P\) exists with degree-zero component \(J\).

## Restriction to the realized two-leg subcomplex

For

\[
e_{\log}=(1,0,1,1)^T,
\qquad
e_{\rm face}=(0,1,0,0)^T,
\]

write \(E=(e_{\log}\ e_{\rm face})\). Then

\[
L\Phi_{\rm exc}E=0
\]

and

\[
\Phi_{\rm exc}E
=D
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Hence the desired map exists exactly on the selected minimal subcomplex, but its extension to the full enhanced source is obstructed by two independent cokernel directions.

## Interpretation

The algebraic selection of two nonnegative chains does not by itself define a support-sensitive relative Stokes pairing. Ambient incidence directions carry boundary data outside \(\operatorname{im}D\). A physical comparison must therefore either construct a source-derived restriction/quotient whose kernel contains the obstruction or enlarge the target complex with the missing support sectors. Merely assigning the two selected chains to the two Aspect legs does not descend from the ambient source.

## Computed result

Every matrix identity above verifies integrally. The obstruction has exact rank two. It vanishes after restriction to the selected two-leg subcomplex, where the identity assignment to the two Aspect legs gives a commuting chain square.

## Disposition

The selected two-leg assignment is an exact chain map but not an ambiently descended pairing: no map \(P:\mathbb Z^4\to\mathbb Z^2\) can satisfy \(DP=JK\) for the fixed audited conductor comparison \(J\). A source-derived restriction or quotient must kill the rank-two obstruction, or the target must be enlarged by its missing support sectors. Physical-chain compatibility remains unverified.
