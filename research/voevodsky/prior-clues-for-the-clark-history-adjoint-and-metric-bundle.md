# Prior clues for the Clark-history adjoint and metric bundle

## Search finding

Prior research provides a more source-specific next target than declaring tensor creation adjoint to its own inverse. It separates source star, contragredient transpose, inverse transport, and Hilbert adjoint. Several of these maps are already constructed on different, explicitly typed carriers.

The full-formal positivity obstruction remains valid under its hypotheses. The source documents do not require every proposed history comparison to act unitarily on a single terminal-indexed positive space.

## 1. The existing full system already uses a dagger relation

Source: `the-full-analytical-system-is-the-joint-graph-realization-of-the-regular-and-endpoint-channels.md`, section “Source star and opposite-polarity dagger”.

The source star is antilinear, involutive, and reverses convolution. The completed comparison is the closed relation

    closure{(Psi_+ g, Psi_- g*) : g in the admitted source}.

It retains the source and is involutive after exchanging polarity labels. Its endpoint projection is the reciprocal conjugate swap.

Promotion to an ambient antiunitary requires an independently constructed map I satisfying both

    I G_+ = G_- I,
    I Z_+ g = Z_- g*.

The note explicitly declines to transfer a dagger from an earlier feature realization to a new functional-calculus feature without these equations.

Application: our new tensor-history Clark receiver should first supply the source-star comparison relation between its two declared polarities. Its existence as a bounded algebra representation does not give an ambient dagger or identify inverse comparison with Hilbert adjoint.

## 2. Contragredient uniqueness fixes the return once the forward map is fixed

Source: `contragredient-uniqueness-reduces-the-final-g4-return-gate-to-one-forward-map-identification.md`.

For a forward map T:P->B on declared nondegenerate dual pairings, the return T^top:B^vee->P^vee is uniquely specified by

    <T^top beta,x>_P = <beta,T x>_B.

This is a dual-space statement. Identification with a Hilbert adjoint needs the relevant real/metric comparisons. Identification with T^(-1) additionally needs an isometry theorem.

Application: specify the history receiver's primal and reciprocal source pairings and compare its actual forward Clark map first. A second arbitrary choice of reverse coefficients is unnecessary once those data are fixed. The earlier note concerns a specific pair-to-bordered interface; it does not itself supply our new history source map.

## 3. Source-derived metric-bundle transport already exists

Sources:

- `research/nima/moving-seam-transport-and-complete-endpoint-pushforward-form-an-exact-metric-natural-transformation.md`;
- `globally-completed-endpoint-channels-form-a-source-reached-metric-natural-bundle.md`.

Unitary reassembly of cut fibers gives

    T_(b<-a)=R_b^(-1) R_a.

The complete endpoint trace intertwines this transport through its Green-trace mate. In the endpoint frame,

    D_(L->M)* G_M D_(L->M)=G_L.

The metrics vary with the source object and need not be uniformly equivalent to a single Euclidean metric. This is an established source construction, rather than a freely fitted family on the new tensor history.

Application: compare our history-indexed Clark forms with these source cut/endpoint fibers. The unresolved part is the full bulk coupling and the map from ordered history into this bundle. The endpoint family itself need not be rebuilt.

## 4. A completed invariant indefinite pairing is also available

Source: `research/nima/linking-metric-is-a-hopf-module-pairing.md`.

The half-density translation frame preserves its Wronskian form. The completed translation orbit satisfies

    rho(Q_L)* J_link rho(Q_L)=J_link,

with adjoints in the transported Hilbert fibers. The doubled linking form carries the separate orientation law

    S* G_link S = -G_link.

Application: this supplies a candidate independently prescribed pairing against which a history receiver can be tested. The typed translation action is already known. Identifying it with our ordered tensor creation/comparison operators remains a source intertwining obligation; matching signatures alone is insufficient.

The companion note `research/nima/reciprocal-doubling-makes-the-spectral-generator-hamiltonian-but-still-indefinite.md` confirms that reciprocal doubling supplies a legitimate signed Hamiltonian structure. It also records the signs that prevent treating that doubling as a positive-metric construction.

## 5. Finite synthesis has a canonical adjoint, but normalization has a cost

Source: `research/nima/finite-synthesis-and-its-adjoint-form-the-reciprocal-linking-pair-on-the-source-generated-range.md`.

For an injective finite synthesis U, the pair is U and U*. The polar map

    W=U(U*U)^(-1/2)

is unitary only onto the generated range. Completion needs a lower Gram bound and reciprocal phase compatibility. Polar normalization moves the Gram factors; it does not erase them.

Application: the finite chamber map into our Clark features has a well-defined adjoint and generated range. This is a legitimate local comparison to compute. It does not turn its tensor creation operators into inverse history updates, or improve the old raw theta decoder bound.

## 6. Typed histories and coefficient features should precede representation

`research/nima/the-eight-axes-act-by-partial-homomorphisms-on-a-typed-history-fock-carrier.md` retains separate history-labelled summands and represents directed/unbounded operations by graph correspondences. Its completion is pro-locally-convex, rather than one bounded Hilbert representation of all formal series.

Its hostile shows that correct endpoint vectors together with an arbitrarily chosen orthogonal feature metric can erase the mixed Green block.

Application: both precedents support retaining the algebraic history and its actual form data separately. They give no license to choose a tensor Green form solely because it makes the algebra convenient.

## Most productive next comparison

The next source test should be a square of forward maps and their source-induced mates:

1. take the actual shell Clark map on the weighted source domain;
2. construct its opposite-polarity source-star relation, retaining word and shell labels;
3. compare that relation with the existing Wronskian/endpoint pairing and its moving-seam transport;
4. derive the contragredient return from the identified pairing;
5. only then ask whether the relation promotes to an antiunitary or whether a history-indexed metric family is the correct strength.

This avoids imposing the stronger equation “creation adjoint equals comparison inverse” before the source has supplied it. An abstract dual doubling could manufacture an invariant indefinite pairing for any invertible comparison, but its equality to the Clark/Wronskian source form would still need proof.
