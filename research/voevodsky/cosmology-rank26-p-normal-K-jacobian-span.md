# K-directional polynomial Jacobian-span test

## Question

Does the uniform polynomial \(D_vK\) factor through the ideal generated by \(K\) and its two integration-variable derivatives, using polynomial multipliers of degree at most 6?

## Result

No. Over \(\mathbb F_{32003}\), none of

\[
D_{n_x}K,\qquad D_{n_y}K,\qquad D_{n_x-n_y}K
\]

belongs to the tested bounded span of monomial multiples of \(K,\partial_uK,\partial_vK\).

An initial implementation reused a pivot basis after adjoining the failed `nx` target, contaminating subsequent membership tests. This produced contradictory positive results for `ny` and the tangent direction. The defect was repaired by rebuilding the generator-only basis independently for every target. The corrected run rejects all three targets.

## Disposition

The Jacobian-only factorization leaf is falsified at the tested bound. The exact rowwise absorption therefore requires marked-wall relations or another part of the full source complex; it is not explained by the bare \(K\)-Jacobian ideal.

This does not prove nonmembership in an unbounded ideal, although degree growth beyond the tested range cannot directly explain the degree-4 targets without cancellations. The next active leaf is the full source-complex \(K\) homotopy, beginning with augmentation by marked-wall generators.
