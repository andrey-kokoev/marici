Constructor audit of the selected source-contract leaf. This is not a new execution of the owner's verifier.

It fixes 638 reachable states. `source.record(0,w,m)` from `research/voevodsky/certificates/verify_filtered_obstruction.py` checks typed non-repeating source words and computes the noncommutative potential record. The verifier distinguishes `current=(start,end,record,source-observer)` from `enhanced=current+(producer,received,issued)`; the latter's classes have checked transition patterns in the declared finite graph.

The relational contract realizes here with X equal to reachable *coupled* states, one separately labelled R_a per source/acquire/deliver/issue event, and a partial transition represented by an empty successor fiber (rejection). `acquire` is offered only before producer evidence exists at the prescribed end; `deliver` only when producer evidence exists; `issue` requires the issued endpoint and a received value, with the label matching that value. Missing edges are rejections, not silent successful self-loops. The actual transition is deterministic for each state/label in this construction, though the general contract permits relations. No present source-only readout can replace the evidence fields: the verifier explicitly checks a source-relative current-class collision with opposite issuance labels. Conversely its enhanced-class transition-pattern test gives the finite quotient its exact labelled one-step successor behavior; induction gives finite-word endpoint behavior, conditional on the recorded graph and checked owner replay. This does not authenticate physical acquisition or external messages.

The `record` function is a readout of a valid typed word: it is NOT an independent mutation `record -> record'` realizing acquisition, and the linear ideal action on source columns does not supply an event transition on coupled states. Attempting to promote algebraic source-action closure to protocol-congruence would cross the missing product-state port. For the source-action lane alone, a typed linear action requires the original start/end corners and admissible word support before any quotient claim; this packet does not establish that a bare potential record determines those corners.
