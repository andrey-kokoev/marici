# Local window ceiling, without assumed shift symmetry

Hypothesis: vertices have a cyclic ordering; every 13 consecutive vertices induce a 4-regular plus graph. Assume the full graph avoids a plus triangle and a minus nine-clique. Claim: the maximum order is 35. Only orders n>=25 matter for this ceiling.

1. A vertex sees four plus neighbors in the next twelve positions and four in the previous twelve. These ranges are disjoint. Since its plus neighborhood is minus-complete, eight exhausts its degree budget. All other edges incident with it are minus.
2. Slide a window by one. Each of the twelve retained vertices loses one neighbor candidate and gains one. Its degree remains four, so its sign to the outgoing vertex equals its sign to the incoming vertex. Writing e(i,d) for the sign between positions i and i+d, this says e(i,d)=e(i+d,13-d) for 1<=d<=12. Apply it again: e(i,d)=e(i+13,d). All indices here are modulo n.
3. If 13 does not divide n, translation by 13 generates the whole cyclic group. Short signs depend only on d, with d and 13-d equal. Exactly two of the six pairs are plus, hence 15 possible templates. The standard-library checker enumerates all 15. For each it finds a minus nine-set on the integer line of span L. This same set works for every n>L+max(plus distances), since no pair then wraps to a plus distance. It checks the remaining finite coprime orders by direct triangle and independent-set search. At 35 only distances 4,6,7,9 in the supplied order survive; none survive above 35. The checker does not read a 35-vertex graph or the value of R(3,9).
4. If 13 divides n and n>=39, short edges project to the same edge signs on the 13 residues: the slide identity identifies the two ways around one 13-window, and translation by 13 preserves each short edge. Long edges are minus by (1). Pick three plus neighbors of one vertex inside the first window. They are pairwise minus, by triangle avoidance. Take three copies of each of these three residues, spaced 13 apart. Within a residue all links are long; across the three residues links are either long or have the minus sign of the projected edge. Hence a minus nine-clique. This excludes the noncoprime tail without assuming circulancy.

The cyclic ordering and window-regularity condition remain assumptions. No general Ramsey upper bound follows. The threshold below 25 need not be classified to prove the maximum.
