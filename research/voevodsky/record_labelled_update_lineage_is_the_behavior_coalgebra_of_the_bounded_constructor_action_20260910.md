# Record-labelled update lineage is the behavior coalgebra of the bounded constructor action

## Question

Once the four-port retained graph carries a bounded constructor action, what additional object serializes update lineage without introducing physical time or an external observer pyramid?

## Claim boundary

This packet constructs canonical lineage and behavioral equivalence from the already declared constructor action and four-port observation map. It does not prove behavioral faithfulness, finite representability, or physical execution of every constructor.

## Constructor category

Let \(C\) be the bounded category generated by the admitted operations on the retained four-port graph \(X=\mathcal B_4\):

- Adams grade maps;
- seam transports;
- endpoint attachments;
- reciprocal/Fourier sewing;
- cutoff maps;
- archimedean character transport;
- causal odd compression.

For every admissible word \(w\) in these generators, functoriality supplies a map

\[
T_w:X_{s(w)}\to X_{t(w)}.
\]

Composition order is constructor order only. It has no physical-time meaning.

Let

\[
O_X:X\to R_X
\]

be the complete four-port record map before terminal scalar projection.

## Lineage object

For a state \(x\in X\), define its record-labelled lineage on an admissible word \(w\) by

\[
\operatorname{Lin}_x(w)
=
O_{t(w)}(T_wx).
\]

The complete behavior map is

\[
\beta_X:X
\longrightarrow
\prod_{w\in\operatorname{Path}_C(X,-)}R_{t(w)},
\qquad
\beta_X(x)=(\operatorname{Lin}_x(w))_w.
\]

This product is an indexing object for constructor paths, not an assertion of a finite global product or an independently existing multiplicity of pyramids. On a bounded finite word set, it is an ordinary finite packet. On the full generated category, it is a pro- or function-valued record object whose topology must be declared separately.

## Coalgebra update

For a generator \(a:X\to Y\), define the derivative of a behavior by prefixing the constructor word:

\[
(\partial_a\beta_X(x))(w)
=
\beta_X(x)(w\circ a)
=
O(T_wT_ax).
\]

Functoriality gives

\[
T_wT_a=T_{w\circ a},
\]

so

\[
\partial_a\beta_X(x)=\beta_Y(T_ax).
\]

Hence the behavior object carries the canonical coalgebra update law. No independently fitted continuation state is needed: the updated lineage is the shifted constructor-path function.

The immediate record is the identity-word coordinate:

\[
O_X(x)=\beta_X(x)(1_X).
\]

Thus observation and continuation are two projections of one behavior object.

## Behavioral equivalence

Define

\[
x\sim_Cy
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
O(T_wx)=O(T_wy)
\text{ for every admissible word }w.
\]

This is the largest equivalence relation invisible to every declared continuation record. It is stable under every constructor:

\[
x\sim_Cy
\Longrightarrow
T_ax\sim_CT_ay,
\]

because every continuation after \(a\) is another admissible composite word.

The quotient

\[
X/\!\sim_C
\]

is therefore the minimal behavioral observer state relative to \(C\), when that quotient exists in the declared carrier category. The current record map alone is faithful precisely when its kernel relation already equals \(\sim_C\).

## Relation to the residue classifier

For the even visible readout, the instantaneous invisible packet is

\[
\ker\rho_4
=
\operatorname{im}h_4
=
\{(r,-r,0,j)\}.
\]

A residue state belongs to the behavioral radical only when every admitted constructor word keeps it invisible:

\[
h_4(r,j)\in\operatorname{Rad}_{\rm beh}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
O(T_wh_4(r,j))=0
\quad\text{for all }w.
\]

Thus the residue classifier identifies what the current even readout forgets, while the behavior coalgebra determines whether later constructor compositions expose that residue. These are different claims.

The four-port record itself sees both odd coordinates and therefore removes this particular instantaneous kernel. A further behavioral kernel can still exist if all four ports jointly miss a source direction preserved by the declared action.

## Prefix and cutoff compatibility

For a cutoff inclusion \(i_N:X_N\to X_{N+1}\), generator naturality gives

\[
T_w^{N+1}i_N=i_NT_w^N
\]

for every word formed from natural generators. Record naturality then yields

\[
\operatorname{Lin}_{i_Nx}^{N+1}(w)
=
i_N^R\operatorname{Lin}_x^N(w).
\]

The previously constructed observation/coherence interval boundary records the new extension coordinate without rewriting old word coordinates.

## Serialization schema

A durable lineage record needs only:

1. source state or source-state reference;
2. canonical constructor word with typed intermediate objects;
3. resulting complete four-port record;
4. residue-classifier coordinate when a projected readout is also stored;
5. source and target cutoff/grade labels;
6. comparison-cell identifier when two words have the same endpoints;
7. exact naturality or coherence residual.

Equivalent generator words should be normalized only after their comparison cell is proved. Scalar equality does not authorize path erasure.

## Falsifiers

1. Two states have equal immediate four-port records but some admitted word separates them. Then immediate records are not a coalgebra quotient.
2. Two declared-equal words produce different records. Then a claimed coherence cell or generator naturality law fails.
3. A cutoff inclusion changes an existing lineage coordinate. Then completion stability fails.
4. A finite word packet is called complete while a longer admitted word distinguishes its kernel.
5. Physical time is inferred from word prefixing without an independently supplied time map.

## Disposition

Record-labelled update lineage requires no new primitive observer port. It is canonically the behavior map of the bounded constructor action:

\[
x\longmapsto\bigl(O(T_wx)\bigr)_w.
\]

This closes the serialization form of the coalgebra. What remains empirical or source-specific is behavioral faithfulness: whether the complete four-port records along all admitted constructor words separate the retained source quotient intended by the theory.
