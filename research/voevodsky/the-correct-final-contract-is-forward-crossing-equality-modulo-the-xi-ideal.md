# The correct final contract is forward-crossing equality modulo the Xi ideal

## Question

Is the authoritative G4 forward map required to equal the constructed pair-to-bordered crossing literally, or only modulo a divisor-preserving chain homotopy invisible to all Evans multiplicity jets?

## Claim boundary

Only equality modulo the Xi ideal is necessary for unchanged-Evans chain promotion. The annihilator of all divisor jets in the holomorphic response module is precisely the principal ideal generated by the completed Xi section \(\tau\). Thus the natural final contract is divisibility of the crossing defect, not strict equality. A growth-restricted global completion may impose an additional condition on the quotient.

## Holomorphic defect

Once the authoritative forward map is exposed, define the holomorphic operator-valued defect

$$
\Delta(z)
=U_{\rm G4}(z)-T_{\rm PB}(z).
$$

After taking the declared contragredient and evaluating on the unchanged Evans section, obtain

$$
r_\Delta(z)=\Delta(z)^\top e(z)
\in U_{\rm ar}.
$$

The radial Stokes calculation shows that this is exactly the residual left after subtracting the constructed bordered return.

## Divisor-jet ideal

Let \(z_0\) be a zero of \(\tau\) of multiplicity \(m\). The Banach-valued local division theorem gives equivalence between:

$$
\partial_z^jr_\Delta(z_0)=0,
\qquad0\le j<m,
$$

and

$$
r_\Delta(z)=\tau(z)h(z)
$$

for a local holomorphic \(U_{\rm ar}\)-valued section \(h\).

If the condition holds at every divisor point, the local quotients patch uniquely, yielding

$$
\boxed{
r_\Delta\in\tau\,\mathcal O(U_{\rm ar}).}
$$

Therefore the annihilator of the complete Xi divisor-jet family is the Xi ideal, not necessarily zero.

## Correct equivalence relation

Define two forward crossings to be Evans-equivalent when

$$
U_1\sim_{\rm Ev}U_2
$$

if their transpose difference on the Evans section is Xi-divisible:

$$
(U_1-U_2)^\top e
\in\tau\mathcal O(U_{\rm ar}).
$$

Then the exact promotion requirement is

$$
\boxed{
U_{\rm G4}
\sim_{\rm Ev}
T_{\rm PB}.
}
$$

Strict equality is one representative choice, not the invariant chain-level statement.

## Chain-homotopy interpretation

If

$$
r_\Delta=\tau h,
$$

then \(h\) is precisely the holomorphic homotopy coefficient absorbing the defect through the Evans wall differential \(\tau\). The two crossings induce the same map on the Xi-divisor module, including multiplicities.

Thus an operator difference proportional to \(\tau\) is not a physical residual; it is a null-homotopic change of chain representative.

## Non-totality consequence

In the unrestricted holomorphic response module, sections \(\tau h\) are nonzero yet vanish with the required multiplicity at every Xi zero. Hence divisor-jet evaluation is not total there. One cannot infer

$$
\Delta=0
$$

from Evans residual cancellation without an additional growth or minimal-type theorem excluding nonzero Xi multiples.

## Revised owner acceptance test

The minimal authoritative test is:

1. expose \(U_{\rm G4}\), its pairings, and its holomorphic response domain;
2. form
   $$
   r_\Delta=(U_{\rm G4}-T_{\rm PB})^\top e;
   $$
3. prove a source-derived factorization
   $$
   r_\Delta=\tau h;
   $$
4. verify that \(h\) belongs to the required completed growth/rigging class.

Proving \(U_{\rm G4}=T_{\rm PB}\) sets \(h=0\) and is sufficient but unnecessarily strong.

## RH boundary

Existence of the factorization is exactly the full residual-jet theorem and therefore remains RH-bearing. This reformulation does not prove it. It prevents the external contract from demanding an unjustified global operator equality when chain-level Xi-divisibility is the actual invariant.

## Disposition

The final G4/Evans interface should be typed in the quotient of holomorphic returns by the Xi ideal. The required statement is forward-crossing agreement modulo \(\tau\), equivalently a divisor-preserving chain homotopy. Strict forward equality is an optional normalization that cannot be inferred from Evans jets alone.
