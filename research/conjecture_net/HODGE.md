## The mathematical target

For smooth projective X over C, let

\[
\operatorname{Hdg}^p(X,\mathbb Q)
=H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X).
\]

The classical rational Hodge conjecture says

\[
\operatorname{cl}:\operatorname{CH}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}
\longrightarrow\operatorname{Hdg}^p(X,\mathbb Q)
\quad\text{is surjective.}
\]

This allows rational formal combinations of arbitrary codimension-p algebraic cycles, including negative coefficients. It does not require effective representatives, uniqueness, a constructive algorithm, or generation by divisors.

Our search hypothesis is that some higher coherence obstruction can distinguish a rational Hodge class from every algebraic cycle class. The general conjecture and this proposed attack are separate records, both unresolved. Initial higher-codimension attention was d>=4 and 2<=p<=d-2, with a fourfold window considered first. Iteration 4 now binds a specific nonsplit abelian sixfold in codimension three, defined by exact exponential-prime periods, and a nonzero integral Weil class. Its algebraicity is not assumed either way.

Official starting source: [Clay Mathematics Institute, Hodge Conjecture](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/hodge-conjecture/), including the official problem description by Pierre Deligne. Further standard sources and the exact trust boundary are recorded in `contracts/hodge-control-fixtures.v1.json`. The historical Hodge/Lefschetz anecdote is motivation, not mathematical evidence.

## The connection to our main net—and its limit

The witnessed-identity programme asks for typed operations, independent comparisons, retained kernels, recoveries and coherent future use. That is useful methodology for asking what a cohomological description forgets.

**But the cycle-class map is not being asserted to be a reversible identification.** Homologically trivial cycles can lie in its kernel. Hodge asks whether every target class has some algebraic preimage, not whether every source cycle and its history can be recovered from its cohomology class.

Consequently:

- A failure to reconstruct a chosen cycle witness is not a Hodge counterexample.
- A stronger packet-coherence condition cannot be imposed on all algebraic cycles without a necessity theorem.
- Packet arity, real configuration direction, cell dimension and complex algebraic codimension are different notions until a typed comparison proves otherwise.
- Failure of one source operation library or one algorithm is not failure of all algebraic realizations.

A cycle-supplied fundamental-chain dictionary and a class-level candidate Weil projector are constructed. Its all-algebraic-cycle vanishing theorem remains **open**; an ordinary algebraic correspondence is not automatically a validated coherence obstruction.

## What would count as an obstruction route

For a fixed, independently specified X,p, construct

\[
O:\operatorname{Hdg}^p(X,\mathbb Q)\longrightarrow V_{\mathbb Q}
\]

with an independently defined rational target and establish:

1. O is Q-linear and depends only on the cohomology class, not charts, cocycles, covers or selected witness histories.
2. O(cl(Z))=0 for **every** codimension-p algebraic cycle Z on this X, hence for all rational cycle combinations.
3. An explicit rational class alpha has Hodge type (p,p).
4. O(alpha) is genuinely nonzero after rationalization.
5. All witnesses use the same X,p,alpha,O and coefficient conventions, and an independent review checks the assembly.

Then alpha cannot be algebraic. Merely naming the unknown quotient Hdg/image(cl) is not a computed invariant or new progress.

The Q-linear detector is the selected attack format, not a claim that every possible disproof must have an algorithmically available detector of this form.

### A non-divisor algebraic control in precisely the first search dimension

Take the smooth quadric Q4=Gr(2,4) in P5, with Plucker equation

    p12*p34 - p13*p24 + p14*p23 = 0.

Two explicit disjoint algebraic planes have free coordinates (p12,p13,p14) and (p23,p24,p34), respectively. The checker verifies their containment, disjointness and the nondegenerate gradient matrix. The standard imported Schubert-cell description gives middle rank two. With the two plane classes A,B,

\[
A^2=B^2=1,\qquad AB=0,\qquad h^2=A+B.
\]

The self-intersection computation uses c2(O(1)^3/O(2))=1. Thus the divisor-product span has rank one but the algebraic middle-class span has rank two.

The primitive class A-B is still algebraic as a rational formal cycle. The naive detector O(aA+bB)=a-b kills h² yet is nonzero on actual algebraic planes. It is therefore rejected as a Hodge obstruction.

This prevents the attractive but invalid inference “not built from codimension-one cycles, therefore not algebraic.” The finite checker audits the stated equations and arithmetic; it is not a newly formalized proof of the imported Schubert geometry.

This is an algebraic diagnostic, not a claimed variety or a new Hodge counterexample. Integral nonalgebraicity—including that of a nontorsion integral class—does not by itself establish rational nonalgebraicity. The integral conjecture's known failures cannot be imported as rational failures.
