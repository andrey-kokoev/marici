# Weighted Cayley–Menger calculations do not define the coefficient map

The weighted contour pullback and resolved measure current establish:

- exact exceptional initial forms;
- cancellation of the scalar normal pole by the collision-normal Jacobian;
- order-zero strict-transformed current;
- no leading dependence on the exceptional ratio;
- a computed first correction.

A separate four-dimensional Gysin weighted-blowup algorithm also exists. No
typed map connects these two constructions. In particular, there is no chain
map from the semialgebraic Cayley–Menger current to the Gysin coefficient block,
no identification with \(\Xi_{\log}\), and no comparison with the ordered
blow-up face.

Therefore the available calculations establish regularity and nonselection at
leading order, not the source-to-coefficient specialization required for the
primitive \((1,1)\) column.
