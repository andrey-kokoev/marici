# Source Laurent audit at the (P_6)-wall intersections

The source reduction is restricted to transverse arcs through split points of
the two resultant branches

\[
\operatorname{Res}_v(P_6,D)
=u^4(u-1)^2(1-8u+12u^2-4u^3),
\]

\[
\operatorname{Res}_v(P_6,H)
=u^3(-2+2u+2u^2-u^3).
\]

For (D), use the labelled wall-1 source column; for (H), wall 2.  In each
case project the fixed final four-vector to the relative-sign line
(mathcal L_{P_6^{-1/2}}), pull back the connection to

\[
u=u_0+s,\qquad v=v_0+2s,
\]

and reconstruct the resulting rational function over the finite field.

At two independent split primes, all four reconstructions pass 24 held-out
samples.  Their valuations at (s=0) are all zero:

\[
\operatorname{ord}_s B_{D,\mathrm{wall1}\to-}=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{ord}_s B_{H,\mathrm{wall2}\to-}=0.
\]

Hence no logarithmic exceptional residue appears in the tested source
branches.  This is multiprime source evidence, not a characteristic-zero
resultant-module certificate.
