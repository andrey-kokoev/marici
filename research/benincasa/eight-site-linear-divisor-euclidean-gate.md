# Eight-site linear-divisor Euclidean gate

## Frozen object

The labelled routing vectors have symmetric circulant Gram matrix with first
row

\[
(2,3/2,1/2,k,l,k,1/2,3/2).
\]

The real Euclidean Cayley--Menger routing chain requires this Gram matrix to
be positive semidefinite. Its Fourier eigenvalues include

\[
\lambda_3=2+\frac{2k-3}{\sqrt2}-l,
\qquad
\lambda_4=l-2k.
\]

The previously frozen four-cumulative-route principal Gram determinant is

\[
-\frac14k(6+7k).
\]

No source wall, coefficient factor, or continuation path is changed.

## The \(2k-3\) family

On \(2k-3=0\),

\[
\lambda_3=2-l,
\qquad
\lambda_4=l-3,
\qquad
\lambda_3+\lambda_4=-1.
\]

Thus at least one eigenvalue is negative for every real \(l\). None of the 21
occurrence orbits in this family meets the real Euclidean routing cone.

## The \(7+6k-8l\) family

Set

\[
l=\frac{7+6k}{8}.
\]

Nonnegativity of the cumulative principal determinant requires

\[
-\frac67\le k\le0.
\]

On this interval,

\[
8\sqrt2\lambda_3
=(9-6k)\sqrt2+8(2k-3).
\]

Its slope \(16-6\sqrt2\) is positive, while its value at the upper endpoint
is \(9\sqrt2-24<0\). The exact square comparisons are

\[
16^2-2\cdot6^2=184>0,
\qquad
24^2-2\cdot9^2=414>0.
\]

Hence \(\lambda_3<0\) throughout the necessary interval. None of the 15
occurrence orbits in this family meets the real Euclidean routing cone.

## Classification and remaining gate

The two universal linear factors are genuine coefficient divisors but cannot
be pinches of a Leray continuation that retains the source real Euclidean
Cayley--Menger routing chain. This is independent of the positive-site-energy
obstruction and requires no new Carrier structure.

This does not decide complex-routing continuations or any irreducible
component of the 36 companion discriminants. Those companions are now the
remaining C8 activation frontier.
