# Finite differential presentation of the infinite moment hierarchy

The observable algebra is

\[
\mathcal A=\mathbb Q[q,p]
\]

with derivation fixed on generators by

\[
Dq=p,
\qquad
Dp=-q^2.
\]

Leibniz then gives every hierarchy relation:

\[
D(q^ap^b)
=a q^{a-1}p^{b+1}-bq^{a+2}p^{b-1}.
\]

Thus Entry 1648's infinite observable space is a finitely generated differential algebra.  No new generator is needed at each degree; degree growth is produced by repeated use of the same two derivation rules.

The dual moment functional remains infinite rank: specifying a general state requires values on all monomials.  The finite presentation compresses the dynamics, not the state.  Hence the appropriate coefficient object is an infinite filtered module over a finitely presented differential algebra, rather than a non-Noetherian list of unrelated moments.
