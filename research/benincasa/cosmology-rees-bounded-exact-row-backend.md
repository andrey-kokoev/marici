# Bounded exact rational Rees row

Problem: construct one source-faithful rational Rees row and compare its modular
projections with `raw_relations`.

Bold conjecture: the first twisted-derivative row has one rational half-twist
normalization whose coefficients reproduce the existing modular row at three
primes.

Rivals: prime-specific rows have no common lift; a coefficientwise rational lift
exists; or equal dimensions hide coefficient disagreement.

Risky consequence: every structural column coefficient must agree after
projection at primes 101, 103, and 107, with mismatches recorded sparsely.

Strongest falsification attempt: the row labelled by the twisted-derivative
family, pole grade zero, all five levels one, first fiber axis, and exponent
`(0,0)` was rebuilt from the exact integer fiber polynomial and half twist
`-1/2`. At ambient degree two, all three projected rows equal the existing
modular rows coefficientwise; every residual is empty.

Disposition: retained for this one actual row. The test supplies structural
column labels, a deterministic row label, and one exact rational normalization.
It constructs no full backend, generator, boundary, or connecting image. The
next leaf tests representatives of the remaining raw-relation families and
checks whether interpolation introduces prime-dependent data.
