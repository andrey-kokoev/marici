# Gaussian–cubic parity filtration of the covariance jet

A centered Gaussian state is even under number parity.  A single cubic
insertion is odd.  The centered two-point operators

\[
a^\dagger a,
\qquad aa,
\qquad a^\dagger a^\dagger
\]

are all parity-even.  Therefore their matrix elements between the Gaussian
sector and the one-cubic sector vanish:

\[
\langle\Psi_{\rm G}|O_2|\Psi_{\rm cubic}\rangle=0.
\]

This remains true for a nonzero squeezed Gaussian first jet; squeezing changes
the coefficients within the even sector but not its parity.

Hence no covariance term linear in the cubic production amplitude exists.
The first statistical cubic contribution is quadratic and is the labelled Cut
norm derived in Entries 1617--1618 and 1623.

The checker verifies the support rule for number states through occupation 13
and audits positivity of complex quadratic Cut norms.  The argument assumes a
centered state; a nonzero one-point function must first be removed by the
source tadpole condition before applying it.
