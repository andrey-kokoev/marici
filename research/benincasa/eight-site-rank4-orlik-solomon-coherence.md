# Orlik–Solomon coherence for the private C8 circuit supports

## Distinction under test

A circuit support can be private in the global occurrence-attachment graph while its exterior boundary relation is generated by other circuits inside the same wall matroid. Entry 1922 established the first statement but did not test the second.

## Degreewise audit

For a circuit (C=(c_0,\ldots,c_k)), use

\[
\partial e_C
=
\sum_i(-1)^i e_{C\setminus c_i}.
\]

The checker generates the complete exterior ideal by wedging every circuit boundary with every admissible exterior monomial. It compares:

\[
I_{\rm shared}
\subseteq
I_{\rm full}
\subseteq
\bigwedge^\bullet\mathbb Q^7.
\]

Across all 36 orbit representatives the possible Orlik–Solomon dimension vectors are

\[
\begin{aligned}
&(1,4,6,4,1),\\
&(1,5,10,9,3),\\
&(1,6,15,17,7),\\
&(1,6,15,18,8),\\
&(1,7,21,29,14),
\end{aligned}
\]

with zero in degrees above four, as required for rank-four arrangements.

The four representatives carrying a private circuit all satisfy

\[
I_{\rm shared}=I_{\rm full}
\]

degreewise. Exact particular syzygies expressing each private degree-four boundary in shared generators are serialized in the result packet. The particular witnesses are not canonical; ideal membership is.
