# Two primes do not reconstruct all canonical seed coefficients

The 18 canonical interior-seed coefficients were paired by typed source
descriptor, combined by CRT, and subjected to standard unique-height rational
reconstruction with preregistered bound

`floor(sqrt((32003*32009)/2)) = 22631`

for both absolute numerator and positive denominator.

Twelve coefficients admit unique candidates within this bound and replay both
prime residues. Six do not: one of seven pole-0 coefficients and five of eleven
pole-1 coefficients remain unreconstructed. No characteristic-zero relation
was replayed.

Thus two-prime finite coherence does not yet promote to a rational seed. A
third prime or an independently sourced coefficient-height bound is required;
the successful twelve candidates are not promoted independently of the full
relation.
