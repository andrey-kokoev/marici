# The unsplit source reaches the principal face with coefficient two

The physical five-pole numerator decomposes algebraically as

\[
\frac{q_{g_{23}}+q_{g_{31}}}
 {q_{g_1}q_{g_2}q_{g_3}q_{g_{23}}q_{g_{31}}}
=
\frac1{q_{g_1}q_{g_2}q_{g_3}q_{g_{31}}}
+
\frac1{q_{g_1}q_{g_2}q_{g_3}q_{g_{23}}}.
\]

These are the two oriented four-wall cells `(g1,g2,g3,g31)` and
`(g1,g2,g3,g23)`. Their simplicial boundaries each contain the same oriented
principal face `(g1,g2,g3)` with coefficient `-1`. The source sum therefore
has principal coefficient `-2`, together with six distinct mixed exceptional
faces of unit coefficient. Its full boundary squares to zero.

This derives the previous factor-two obstruction from the literal unsplit
source rather than from a hypothetical correction. Projecting away the six
mixed faces is inadmissible, and selecting either four-wall summand separately
has no source authority: the rank-26 cyclic source is the numerator sum and
individual exceptional periods remain regulator-hierarchy dependent.

Thus the existing source gives a nonunit principal comparison, not the unit
map required by the universal tau classifier. No physical period is obtained.
