# Legendre real structure

The Legendre equation

\[
m(1-m)y''+(1-2m)y'-\frac14y=0
\]

has a real rational first-order connection in the fixed basis \((y,y')\).
Thus plain complex conjugation \(K\) is horizontal.

The selective involution \(ZK\), \(Z=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)\), would
require

\[
ZAZ=A.
\]

This fails generically because both off-diagonal entries of the Legendre
connection are nonzero. It also fails on the source-normalized cusp
monodromy:

\[
Z\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}Z
=\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Therefore the pure elliptic block carries \(K\), not the specific \(ZK\)
needed to realize Entry 1760's selective deletion in this labelled basis.
