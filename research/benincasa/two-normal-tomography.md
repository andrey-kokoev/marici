# Two-normal tomographic degeneration

Use the rank-one effect generated by

\[
|v_{\varepsilon,\eta}\rangle
=|0\rangle+(\varepsilon+i\eta)|1\rangle.
\]

Its scalar readout is

\[
q_{\varepsilon,\eta}
=h_{00}
+\varepsilon(h_{01}+h_{10})
+i\eta(h_{01}-h_{10})
+(\varepsilon^2+\eta^2)h_{11}.
\]

At the origin the ordinary packet retains only the diagonal measurements.
The two labelled first grades recover the two missing off-diagonal
directions. There is no \(\varepsilon\eta\) coefficient, so the two iterated
specializations commute exactly and create no excess mixed class.
