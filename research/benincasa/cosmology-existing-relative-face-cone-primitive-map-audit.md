# Existing relative-face cones stop below the primitive chain map

The ordered three-wall Čech cone is source-derived. Its relative residue class

\[
\Xi_{\log}-\sigma_{123}
\]

is closed, satisfies \(d^2=0\), and is nonboundary in the minimal residue
complex. If an exceptional face leg with unit coefficient were sourced, the
chain-map equations would uniquely force the primitive integral column
\((1,1)\).

That conditional uniqueness is not a construction. The audited total complex
has no incoming generator, no comparison morphism from the blow-up or
relative-face source, no logarithmic primitive, and no total chain-level lift.

Thus existing cones close the residue obstruction and determine the only
admissible coefficient conditionally, but they do not supply the source arrow
required by the objective. Progress requires a resolved/Rees exceptional
generator, a logarithmic Čech–de Rham chain map, or a Cayley–Menger/relative-face
cone generator with a typed comparison.
