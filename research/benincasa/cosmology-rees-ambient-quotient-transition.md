# Exact ambient quotient transition

Problem: construct the ambient-four to ambient-five map and prove descent to the
assembled relation quotients.

Bold conjecture: source-label inclusion sends every ambient-four relation to the
identical ambient-five relation and sends tau-p to tau-p.

Rivals: cutoff relations fail preservation; target representatives disagree; or
a quotient transition exists without global injectivity.

Risky consequence: all 7,572 ambient-four assembled rows and the target must be
preserved exactly.

Strongest falsification attempt: all 7,572 relation keys occur among the 11,628
ambient-five relations. No row is missing or mismatched. The target remains
coefficient 3 at the same grade-one structural label. Exact normal forms prove
that this target is nonzero in both quotients.

Disposition: retained. Source-label inclusion descends to a quotient map and
carries the nonzero ambient-four tau-p class to its nonzero ambient-five class.
Global injectivity is not proved. This establishes one canonical transition,
not ambient-six coherence, a colimit, or physical promotion. The next test must
construct the next transition and verify composition on shared relations and
tau-p.
