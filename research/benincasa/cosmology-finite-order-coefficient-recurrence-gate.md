# Finite-order coefficient fitting is not a source recurrence

Three rational values of any normalized descriptor coefficient determine a
quadratic interpolant. They are equally compatible with that interpolant plus
an arbitrary rational multiple of

\[
(A-12)(A-14)(A-16).
\]

The alternatives agree on every observed adjacent-edge degree and differ at
the next degree. Consequently fitting cutoff-dependent correction or syzygy
coefficients cannot establish a recurrence unless the recurrence order and the
descriptor-support law are independently derived from the source presentation.

Promotion requires a source-derived order, an ambient-independent support
rule, enough base cases for that order, and exact verification of the recurrence.
The finite exact and coherence results supply none of the first two data.
