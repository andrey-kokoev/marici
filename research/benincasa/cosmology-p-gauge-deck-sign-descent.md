# The gauge descends only with a sign twist

For `y^2=D(u)`, let `g=u(u-1)(u-2)/y`. The deck involution fixes `u` and sends
`y` to `-y`, hence sends `g` to `-g` while fixing `dlog(g)`. Ordinary descent is
therefore falsified.

Twisted descent succeeds formally with the integral character `chi=-1`.
Its square is one; the four branch monodromies are all `-1` and their product is
one. Thus the horizontal connection correction descends as a rank-one integral
sign local system.

The resulting comparison is meromorphic on the complement of `u=0,1,2` and
`D=0`, not a global regular isomorphism. No source sign local system with this
branch support is declared. The obstruction is therefore a missing integral
sign local system, not a failure of the correction after that twist.
