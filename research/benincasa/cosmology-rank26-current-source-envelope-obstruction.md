# Rank-26 promotion obstruction in the current source envelope

Exact physical half-twist contractions and coherent axis descent are established
through ambient degree 18. They do not promote to an unbounded theorem from the
available source data.

The independent gates are:

- a finite exact prefix does not determine its unbounded continuation;
- the IBP family is not a commutative polynomial submodule because coordinate
  multiplication produces a nonzero Leibniz residual;
- forcing coordinate-stable closure makes every bare master a relation and
  trivializes the target quotient;
- contractions form a nontrivial torsor under exact source syzygies;
- natural constructor transport, exact overlap cells, and strict axis squares
  give coherent descent but no canonical torsor section;
- no audited pairing or normalization is both typed over and nontrivial on the
  contraction kernel.

Therefore the present rank-26 route has no source-natural promotion from its
finite contractions to an unbounded theorem. This closes that route inside the
audited source envelope. It does not exclude a future source enlargement that
supplies a kernel-sensitive pairing, symmetry-fixed normalization, or separately
proved generation theorem, and it does not construct the primitive
\(\tau_p\) lift.
