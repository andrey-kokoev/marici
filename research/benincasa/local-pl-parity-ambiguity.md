# Local Picard--Lefschetz parity ambiguity

The nodal anticanonical germ determines the vanishing cycle and the
width-two relation. It does not mark a global lift of the thimble in
\(H^2(S\setminus D_\infty;\mathbb Z)\).

With algebraic plane
\(\mathcal A_{--}=\mathbb Z\langle e_6,v_{\rm alg}\rangle\), every global
completion has a presentation

\[
2m=a e_6+bv_{\rm alg},
\qquad (a,b)\in(\mathbb Z/2)^2.
\]

Changing a lift by \(m\mapsto m+k_1e_6+k_2v_{\rm alg}\) changes the displayed
integer coefficients by \((2k_1,2k_2)\), preserving their parity. Conversely,
the same local nodal germ is compatible with all four parity vectors.

Thus a completed local normal form cannot decide the extension. One needs a
globally marked integral thimble and its comparison with the primitive
infinity-Gysin lattice.
