# Relative transition kernel

Problem: separate kernel and cokernel dimensions of the first ambient quotient
map.

Bold conjecture: the A4-to-A5 transition is monic.

Risky consequence: its image rank must equal the full A4 quotient dimension
8,814.

Strongest falsification attempt: sparse relative reduction modulo 101 gives A5
relation rank 5,888 and image rank 7,072. The modular kernel dimension is 1,742
and the cokernel dimension is 2,880; their difference satisfies the required
Euler identity. Tau-p remains nonzero at both cutoffs and is therefore outside
this kernel.

Disposition: falsified modulo 101. This does not yet prove a 1,742-dimensional
rational kernel: modular rank is bounded above by characteristic-zero rank, so
the modular kernel only bounds the rational kernel from above. Global monicity
is not established. The next test must lift modular-kernel candidates and reduce
them exactly against A5 relations. Two broader attempts exceeded the 120-second
execution bound; the retained test isolates A4-to-A5 at one prime.
