# All-order covariance-Rees completion of the cubic generator

In the weighted chart `A=epsilon^2`, `C=epsilon c`, the only denominator is

\[
D=1+2\varepsilon^2ty.
\]

Since `D=1` on the exceptional divisor, both `D^{-1}` and `log D` have regular
formal power series in `epsilon^2`.  The rational numerator is

\[
c^2y^2+2\varepsilon cxy+\varepsilon^2x^2.
\]

Under the physical deck action

\[
(\varepsilon,c)\longmapsto(-\varepsilon,-c),
\]

the denominator and numerator are invariant.  Hence the entire completed
generator descends, not only its first two grades.  There is no logarithmic
monodromy at `epsilon=0` because the logarithm's argument is a unit there.
