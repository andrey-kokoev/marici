# Noncommuting calibration curvature

Let two infinitesimal frame calibrations act through
\(\operatorname{ad}_A\) and \(\operatorname{ad}_B\). The two mixed
specialization orders differ by

\[
\operatorname{ad}_A\operatorname{ad}_B(H)
-\operatorname{ad}_B\operatorname{ad}_A(H)
=\operatorname{ad}_{[A,B]}(H).
\]

Thus the mixed \((1,1)\) grade is the curvature of the coefficient-frame
calibration. It vanishes for commuting calibrations and can be nonzero for
noncommuting ones.

The four scalar effects of Entry 1753 reconstruct this entire defect matrix,
so coefficient curvature is observable through a labelled scalar packet.
