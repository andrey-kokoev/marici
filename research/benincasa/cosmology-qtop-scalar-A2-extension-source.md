Two occurrence-labelled `q_top` copies are not algebraically required. After
coefficient extension, `alpha` acts canonically as `alpha I_A2`; this action is
cyclic and commutes with the normalized identity shear.

The corpus contains one `q_top` basis line and one corresponding final-block
source column. It declares neither a tensor-product connection between that
line and the constant A2 system nor a map from A2 to either rank-twelve
extension grade. Thus the formal scalar action does not become the marked `q0`
action. It is also occurrence-insensitive and cannot encode the distinguished
