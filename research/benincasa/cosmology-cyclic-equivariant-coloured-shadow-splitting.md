# Cyclic equivariance reduces four lifts to two

Every integral \(C_3\)-equivariant splitting of the colour-forgetful map has
form \((A,I-A)\), where \(A\) is an integral circulant matrix. These splittings
form an affine \(\mathbb Z^3\)-family.

The minimum column \(\ell^1\)-norm is one. Exactly two splittings attain it:
all occurrence coordinates use singleton-complement colour, or all use
same-pair colour. The two mixed minimal lifts of the individual shadow do not
extend to a minimum-norm equivariant splitting of the full cyclic family.

Cyclic symmetry therefore reduces the ambiguity from four local lifts to a
binary choice, but does not select between the two colours. The literal joint
generator's denominator provenance is the next possible discriminator.
