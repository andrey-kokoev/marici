# Fubini–Study normalization of a measurement tangent

At the limiting measurement line \([e_0]\), a transverse tangent is represented
by \(v=ae_1+be_2\).  The Fubini–Study norm is proportional to
\(a^2+b^2\).  For \(|\psi\rangle=e_1\), unit-speed normalization turns Entry
1735's second coefficient into

\[
c_{\rm FS}([v])
=\frac{|\langle v|\psi\rangle|^2}{\langle v|v\rangle}
=\frac{a^2}{a^2+b^2}.
\]

This is invariant under nonzero rescaling and central phase, but varies with
the projective tangent direction.  The metric removes speed ambiguity; it does
not derive the direction from the endpoint.
