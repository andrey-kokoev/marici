# Cardinality-weighted merge preserves cumulant species

For independent blocks of sizes (m,n), normalized collective variables merge
with weights

\[
\alpha=\sqrt{\frac m{m+n}},
\qquad
\beta=\sqrt{\frac n{m+n}}.
\]

Cumulants transform homogeneously:

\[
\kappa_r(\alpha Q_m+\beta Q_n)
=\alpha^r\kappa_r(Q_m)+\beta^r\kappa_r(Q_n).
\]

Along every merger tree, each leaf coefficient is (N^{-r/2}). Hence no
cumulant order mixes with another, although each species carries its own
cardinality character.

The exact checker certifies 990,000 ordered binary bracketings for cumulant
orders and occurrence counts through twelve.
