# Action-level renormalization source bridge

## Compatible parent action

The direct action parent of the cosmological-polytope construction is
equation (1) of Arkani-Hamed--Benincasa--Postnikov, arXiv:1709.02813:

\[
S=\int d^d x\,d\eta
\left[
\frac12(\partial\phi)^2
-\sum_{k\ge3}\frac{\lambda_k(\eta)}{k!}\phi^k
\right].
\]

The one-loop three-site graph is the sector with one cubic vertex at each
labelled site.  This source is compatible with the denominator geometry, but
does not itself define a renormalization prescription.

## One-loop inventory

For cubic theory in four spacetime dimensions,

\[
\omega=4L-2I=4-E-V.
\]

At one loop, cubic incidence gives (V=I=E), hence

\[
\omega=4-2E.
\]

Therefore:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
E&\omega&\text{possible local datum}\\
\hline
1&2&\text{background/tadpole}\\
2&0&\text{mass or conformal-weight term}\\
3&-2&\text{none; triangle finite}.
\end{array}
\]

The two-point divergence has momentum degree zero, so it does not generate a
one-loop field-strength counterterm.  The triangle generates no vertex
counterterm.

The full action-level extension of Entry 2470 has consequently reduced to two
lower-point normalization maps:

1. the condition selecting the zero one-point background;
2. the condition preserving the declared massless/conformally coupled free
   state.

Their counterterm insertions into the labelled three-point wavefunction must
be derived before claiming that the complete action-level finite map acts
invertibly on the rank-seven quotient.

## Typed reduction

The mass insertion does not inhabit a kinematic normal direction.  The
variables

\[
\nu_i=P_i^2-X_i^2
\]

measure off-diagonal kinematics, whereas a mass or conformal-weight
counterterm changes the external mode-function coefficient object.  Treating
the latter as a normal shift would mix carrier coordinates with coefficients.

Before normalization, the correctly typed source is therefore

\[
R_7\oplus B_1\oplus M_3,
\]

where (B_1) is the background/tadpole line and (M_3) contains the three
labelled mass-mode directions.  The zero-one-point and conformal-mass
conditions set the renormalized (B_1) and (M_3) coordinates to zero.  The
induced counterterm reduction is

\[
\boxed{
[I_7\mid0\mid0]:R_7\oplus B_1\oplus M_3\longrightarrow R_7.
}
\]

It has rank seven and is cyclically equivariant.  Finite nonlocal self-energy
dressing is not part of this matrix: it is lower-point dynamics, not scheme
freedom.  A full action-level three-point observable would need that dressing
as an additional source-derived coefficient map.

External-leg sewing nevertheless cannot erase the existing rank-seven data
generically.  If its analytic dressing factor is (F(\nu)), multiplication on
the finite jet tower is filtration triangular with diagonal (F(0)).
Projection onto the original eleven CM-generated labels has determinants

\[
F(0)^{11}
\quad\text{and}\quad
F(0)^{10}
\]

on the full and response-only towers.  Perturbatively (F(0)=1+O(\lambda^2)),
so both are invertible.  A generic dressing can create additional pure cubic
or higher coefficient jets not generated by the CM polynomial.  Those are a
coefficient/readout enlargement, not new Carrier incidences; the invertible
projection already recovers the original (R_7).

## Rejected direct import

Farren--McCulloch--Pajer--Tong, arXiv:2603.08794, supplies a powerful general
renormalization architecture: local EFT counterterms have the same operator
form as the action, real coefficients follow from unitarity, and leading
logarithms are scheme invariant.  Its concrete starting theory is instead a
derivatively coupled massless, IR-safe de Sitter EFT.  It is therefore a
methodological comparison source, not direct authority for the
non-derivative conformally coupled cubic model without an explicit
specialization proof.
