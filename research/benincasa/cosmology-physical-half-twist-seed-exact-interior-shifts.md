# Exact interior shifts stop one degree below the proposed range

The physical half-twist source words were shifted descriptorwise and replayed
over the rationals at ambient degrees 12, 14, and 16.

Every descriptor-admissible shift reconstructs both complete targets:

| ambient degree | shifts per pole | maximum verified degree |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 12 | 15 | 4 |
| 14 | 28 | 6 |
| 16 | 45 | 8 |

These maxima are `A-8`, not the proposed `A-7`. At degree `A-7`, every shift
requires tangent source row 6811, and for pole 1 also row 8491, with a q-family
exponent of total degree `A`. The finite presentation generates those tangent
q rows only through degree `A-1`, so the descriptors do not exist.

Thus the exact canonical word generates the complete strict interior through
`A-8`. The `A-7` shell is not a failed rational identity: it is outside the
word's descriptor domain and requires a separately typed boundary correction
or a one-degree enlargement. Treating it as an ordinary interior shift is
falsified.
