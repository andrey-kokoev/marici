# Axis descent is coherent but does not select a contraction

The two squared-axis transports give local contractions whose overlap
differences are exact source syzygies:

- A12 to A14: 36 overlaps;
- A14 to A16: 48 overlaps;
- A16 to A18: 60 overlaps.

The descriptor multiplication squares commute strictly on 48 A12-to-A16 and
60 A14-to-A18 coordinates. These facts define coherent descent data for the
contraction torsor: local contractions, kernel-valued transition cells, and a
cocycle condition.

They do not define a canonical section. A kernel shear preserves evaluation,
overlap equations, and square coherence while moving every chosen contraction.
Using pivot-selected contractions to express the cocycle as a coboundary proves
finite existence only; it does not source that trivialization.

Thus axis descent removes a consistency obstruction but leaves the
canonical-section obstruction unchanged.
