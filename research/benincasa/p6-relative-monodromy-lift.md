# The (P_6) relative monodromy does not lift diagonally

Entry 867 splits the algebraic kernel as

\[
\mathcal L_{P_6^{-1/2}}\oplus\mathcal L_{D_1}.
\]

At a generic point of (P_6=0), away from (D_1=0), the residues are

\[
\left(-\frac12,0\right),
\]

and the local monodromy on the algebraic submodule is therefore

\[
Z_{P_6}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,+1).
\]

This is exactly the relative sign sought by the companion-deletion model,
but only on the algebraic submodule.  Entry 1764 proves that the generic
marked extension has source support graph (K_{3,2}).  A diagonal lift would
have to assign each marked generator a sign equal to both (-1) and (+1),
which is impossible.

Therefore (Z_{P_6}) is an intrinsic boundary monodromy that does not extend
as a diagonal symmetry of the marked rank-twelve object.  The full local
monodromy may still be triangular and nonsplit; this calculation does not
claim its absence.
