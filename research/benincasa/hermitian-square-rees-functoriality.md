# Functoriality of the Hermitian-square Rees packet

For a labelled amplitude map \(f\), the density packet transforms by

\[
\rho\longmapsto f\rho f^\dagger.
\]

In particular,

\[
H(fu,fv)=fH(u,v)f^\dagger,
\qquad
H(u,v)=|u\rangle\langle v|+|v\rangle\langle u|.
\]

The identity holds separately on the diagonal and mixed Rees bidegrees and is
strict under composition.  Thus relative phase transport is intrinsic to the
Hermitian-square functor, not a basis-dependent coherence choice.
