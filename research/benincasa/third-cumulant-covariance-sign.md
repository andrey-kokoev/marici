# Third cumulants versus covariance sign costalks

The source third cumulant is odd under reversal of the cubic orbit parameter:

\[
\kappa_3(-t)=-\kappa_3(t).
\]

The covariance Rees deck acts independently by `u -> -u`.  Holding the source
cubic parameter fixed, it leaves `kappa_3` unchanged.  Thus the two characters
are

\[
(\chi_t,\chi_u)=(-1,+1).
\]

Labelled support restriction of the third-cumulant tensor is ordinary
restriction of all three occurrence slots.  It does not use the covariance
component-sign costalk.  A nontrivial coupling would require the mixed normal
comparison already found absent in Entries 1675 and 1699.
