# Every even-cutoff interior target has an exact contraction

At ambient degree \(A\), interior exponents have total degree from zero through
\(A-7\). Four low exponents,

\[
(0,0),\ (1,0),\ (0,1),\ (1,1),
\]

are preserved by unchanged-descriptor ambient inclusion from the exact A12
base. Every other nonnegative exponent has at least one coordinate at least two.
Subtracting two from that coordinate produces an interior exponent at cutoff
\(A-2\).

The squared-axis constructor identities therefore transport an exact
contraction inductively. The number of interior targets per pole is

\[
\frac{(A-6)(A-5)}{2}.
\]

The A12 base contains 21 exact interior targets per pole. Together with the
four stable low contractions and arbitrary-exponent constructor naturality,
this proves exact rational contraction existence for every interior target at
every even \(A\ge12\).

Combined with the separately proved boundary cover, this yields all-degree
rank-26 absorption for the tested marked stratum and physical half twist. It
does not construct the primitive integral \(\tau_p\) column.
