\[
q_{g_3}-q_{g_{23}}-q_{g_{31}}=E,
\qquad E=x+y+z.
\]

\[
p=x+y+3z.
\]

are distinct. Importing the identity as a p-normal comparison would repeat the
forbidden total-energy/source-type substitution. The checker retains this

period is constructed.
