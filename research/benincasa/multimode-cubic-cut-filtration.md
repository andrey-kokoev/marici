# Multi-mode cubic Cut filtration before and after internal pushforward

Before tracing the two internal occurrences, the source production jump is

\[
L_{p;qk}
=C_{p;qk}
a_p^\dagger a_q^\dagger a_k^\dagger.
\]

On the three-mode number-moment algebra, its trace-balanced adjoint generator
acts as

\[
\mathcal L_{p;qk}^\dagger f
=(N_p+1)(N_q+1)(N_k+1)
\left[
f(N_p+1,N_q+1,N_k+1)-f(N_p,N_q,N_k)
\right].
\]

For a number polynomial of total degree \(D\), the finite difference has
degree at most \(D-1\), while the prefactor has degree three.  Thus the output
has number degree \(D+2\), corresponding to physical Weyl degree shift

\[
\boxed{+4}.
\]

The complete occurrence-resolved three-mode channel is therefore not a
same-level endomorphism of the global moment tower.

After the relative-state pushforward placing the internal modes in the vacuum
sector,

\[
(N_q+1)(N_k+1)\mapsto1.
\]

For observed moments \(f(N_p)\), the generator reduces to

\[
(N_p+1)[f(N_p+1)-f(N_p)],
\]

which preserves observed degree, recovering Entries 1634--1635.

The formulas are strictly invariant under the labelled occurrence exchange
\((q,k)\leftrightarrow(k,q)\).  Therefore the degree collapse is caused by the
physical internal pushforward, not by forgetting which occurrence was which.
