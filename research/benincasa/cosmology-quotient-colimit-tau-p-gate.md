# Finite quotient compatibility does not produce the integral tau lift

At every verified target, quotienting a nonempty contraction fiber by exact
source syzygies leaves one class. Constructor transport and exact overlap cells
make these classes compatible through ambient degrees 12, 14, 16, and 18.

This is conditional uniqueness after existence. A class at a later cutoff is
defined only after its target is proved to lie in the evaluated source image.
No all-degree contraction or generation theorem exists, so the finite diagram
does not define an unbounded colimit class.

The quotient is rational and evaluation-level. No map from it to the required
integral \((1,1)\) column has been derived, and it does not preserve an integral
source lattice. The known principal coefficient lattice remains \(2\mathbb Z\),
so quotienting by rational source syzygies cannot manufacture a primitive
integral unit.

Thus finite quotient compatibility proves neither unbounded existence nor the
source-authorized primitive \(\tau_p\) lift.
