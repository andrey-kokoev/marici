# Cofinal diagonal death requires all three special-relation families

For the map `(ambient,K_DEPTH)=(10,3)->(12,4)`, every one of the nine source
image generators is exact modulo the complete target-special relation module.
Over both primes, deleting any one family destroys exactness for all nine:

- `IBP + K`: zero exact generators;
- `IBP + marked-q`: zero exact generators;
- `K + marked-q`: zero exact generators;
- `IBP + K + marked-q`: nine exact generators.

The diagonal vanishing is therefore not attributable to K-depth truncation,
IBP alone, or marked-wall level lowering alone. It is a genuinely joint
three-family reduction in this finite presentation. This family-necessity
census does not yet provide sparse coefficients or a uniform homotopy in
ambient degree.
