# Physical syzygy images form a nonmonotone finite persistence diagram

For physical half-twist image spaces at ambient degrees 8, 10, 12, and 14,

`dim(V8,V10,V12,V14) = (8,7,11,14)`.

Adjacent transition ranks are `3,5,9`. Composite ranks are
`rank(V8->V12)=rank(V8->V14)=3` and `rank(V10->V14)=5` over both primes.
The resulting finite barcode is:

- three degree-eight directions persist through degree fourteen; five die at degree ten;
- two directions born at degree ten persist through degree fourteen; two die at degree twelve;
- four directions born at degree twelve persist through degree fourteen; two die at degree fourteen;
- five degree-fourteen directions have unresolved lifetime.

This separates persistent directions from replacements and explains why raw
image ranks are not stable-class counts. Every space and transition remains
inside the certified p-tangent image. Four envelopes do not define an
unbounded or colimit theorem; further cutoff extension is lower-value than a
source-level persistence rule.
