# Degenerate tomography Rees packet

Let the fourth effect be generated by

\[
|v_\varepsilon\rangle=|0\rangle+(1+i\varepsilon)|1\rangle.
\]

At \(\varepsilon=0\) it coincides with the real-superposition effect of
Entry 1753, so the ordinary measurement frame has rank three. Its readout is

\[
q_\varepsilon
=q_x+i\varepsilon(h_{01}-h_{10})+\varepsilon^2h_{11}.
\]

Hence the first Rees grade is exactly the missing tomographic direction,

\[
\operatorname{gr}^1_\varepsilon(q_\varepsilon-q_x)
=i(h_{01}-h_{10}),
\]

while the second grade is the already known diagonal entry \(h_{11}\).
Together with \(h_{01}+h_{10}\) from \(q_x\), the first grade reconstructs
both off-diagonal entries.

The reconstruction is canonical when the source parameter \(\varepsilon\)
and its orientation are retained. An unparametrized tangent retains only a
projective direction and does not fix the scalar normalization.
