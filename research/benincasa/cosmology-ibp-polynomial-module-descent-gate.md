# The IBP relation family is not a commutative polynomial submodule

For one coordinate axis, let the exponent-labelled IBP constructor be
\(I_e=\partial(x^eF)\). The source constructor obeys

\[
xI_e=I_{e+1}-x^eF.
\]

The last term is the Leibniz residual. Its master coefficient is `-1`, so
multiplying an IBP relation by a coordinate is not another IBP relation unless
the bare master is independently zero. The audited source constructor contains
no such relation.

The K- and q-denominator identities shift monomial exponents algebraically,
but that does not remove the IBP residual. Therefore the combined relation rows
do not define the proposed commutative graded polynomial submodule merely from
their indexed formulas.

A source-level induction would instead need a differential-operator or Weyl
module presentation that includes both multiplication and derivation, plus a
finite-generation certificate in that structure. Accidental membership in a
finite truncated row span does not establish this descent.
