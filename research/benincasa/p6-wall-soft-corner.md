# The repeated `u=0` resultant factor is the total-energy/site-soft corner

In the homogeneous chart

\[
X_1=1,\qquad u=E_T,\qquad
X_2=\frac{u+v}{2}-1,\qquad X_3=\frac{u-v}{2},
\]

the common center \((u,v)=(0,2)\) is exactly

\[
E_T=0,\qquad X_2=0.
\]

Writing \(z=v-2\), exact translation gives

\[
4P_6=z^2+2u(1+2u-2u^2)z+u^2(1-2u)^2,
\]

\[
D=-z^2+(-6u+4u^2)z-u^2,
\]

\[
H=(1+2u-u^2)z+u(1-u)^2.
\]

Their initial forms are

\[
(z+u)^2,\qquad -(z^2+6uz+u^2),\qquad z+u.
\]

Thus \(P_6\) and \(H\) share a tangent, with the \(P_6\) branch doubled,
while \(D\) has two distinct tangent directions. This explains the factors
\(u^3\) and \(u^4\) in the two resultants. The factor is not a new carrier
divisor: it is a nontransverse coefficient collision over the already frozen
total-energy/site-soft incidence.
