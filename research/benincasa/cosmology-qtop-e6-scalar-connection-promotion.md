# The qtop/E6 equality is a specialized line comparison

The E6 operand is one diagonal coefficient of a 9-by-9 connection, specialized
to `u=0`. The quotient operand is `alpha`, the `q_top` diagonal in a 3-by-3
marked-wall connection. Their equality to `-1/(v-2)` proves zero connection
difference on those two lines.

It does not identify `q_top` with the marked `q0` extension grade, construct a
two-dimensional A2 action, identify occurrences across the two bases, extend
away from `u=0`, or prove connector naturality. Hence it cannot promote to the
missing common marked action.

Formally, `alpha I_A2` would commute with the normalized identity shear. The
remaining question is whether any source supplies two occurrence-labelled
`q_top` copies that authorize this scalar extension.
