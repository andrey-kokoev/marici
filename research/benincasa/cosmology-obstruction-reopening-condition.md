# Reopening the parity gate is not sufficient for a lift

Conjecture: an odd `Xi_log` coefficient, the ordered Cech correction, explicit
`g23/g31` routing, and an integral chain map form a complete reopening package.

These data are necessary to evade the even-lattice and total-complex gates, but
they do not construct a source-labelled generator, base map, shared relative
pair, homology–de Rham pairing, weighted specialization, target projection, or
integral physical readout. The conjecture is therefore falsified as a
sufficiency claim.

An odd sourced coefficient weakly reopens only the parity gate. A sufficient
reopening must construct and verify the full typed chain from source geometry
and support through integral coefficients, transport, canonical comparison,
and physical readout, carrying a primitive generator to `[1,1]`.

The next leaf fixes the final strength boundary and its precise falsifier. The
current result remains active-envelope nonexistence, not global impossibility.
