# The interaction co-Leibniz defect is already a coherent cocycle

For

\[
\Theta_D
=
\Delta D-(D\otimes1+1\otimes D)\Delta,
\]

coassociativity of \(\Delta\) implies

\[
\boxed{
(\Delta\otimes1)\Theta_D
+(\Theta_D\otimes1)\Delta
=
(1\otimes\Delta)\Theta_D
+(1\otimes\Theta_D)\Delta.
}
\]

For the nonlinear force \(Dp=-q^n\), \(\Theta_D(p)\) is the mixed binomial/reduced coproduct of \(-q^n\). The checker expands both triple-tensor routes exactly for degrees two through twenty and finds coefficientwise equality.

Therefore the binary co-Leibniz defect is a co-Hochschild cocycle fixed by the interaction polynomial. No independent ternary coherence generator is required at this level. Higher Cut coherence is inherited from the coassociativity of the existing partition/cumulant coproduct.
