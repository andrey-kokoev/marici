# Matrix-valued mixture interchange

Let two incomparable simplex-face transports act on a coefficient fiber by
endomorphisms \(U\) and \(V\).  On a mixed labelled coefficient \(A\), the two
orders give

\[
UVA,
\qquad
VUA.
\]

Their exact difference is

\[
\boxed{[U,V]A}.
\]

Thus Entry 1718 extends precisely to commuting coefficient transports.  For
noncommuting transports, the missing datum is a coefficient-level interchange
or braiding cell.  The underlying pair of labelled simplex faces is unchanged,
so this calculation does not demand a new Cut carrier stratum.

This is a hypothetical algebraic boundary test.  No frozen cosmological source
currently supplies matrix-valued mixture weights.
