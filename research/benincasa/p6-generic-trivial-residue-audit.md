# Generic `P6` source audit on the trivial algebraic line

The rank-twelve source reducer was projected to the split trivial algebraic
line and pulled back along arcs transverse to \(P_6=0\). The audit retains all
three labelled quotient generators:

\[
q_{\rm top},\qquad q_{\rm wall1},\qquad q_{\rm wall2}.
\]

It was run at three generic \(P_6\)-points over each of two independent
61-bit primes. Every one of the eighteen rational reconstructions used 48
discovery samples and 24 held-out samples.

All eighteen have Laurent valuation

\[
0,
\]

not \(-1\). Thus the indicially admissible trivial-line slot at generic
\(P_6\) is unoccupied in every tested source direction and specialization.

This is multiprime source evidence, not yet an exact characteristic-zero
divisibility theorem on the full \(P_6\) function field.
