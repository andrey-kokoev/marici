# Diagonal parabolic-measurement Rees packet

Write the regularized curvature as

\[
xK_x=R+xS+x^2T.
\]

For the degenerating fourth measurement, diagonal pullback
\(\delta=x\) gives first coefficient

\[
q_x(S)+i(R_{01}-R_{10}).
\]

The companion, nondegenerating real-superposition measurement has first
coefficient \(q_x(S)\). Their source-labelled difference therefore recovers
the residue jet canonically.

An isolated diagonal scalar would be ambiguous, but the complete labelled
measurement packet is not: it contains the required finite-part subtraction.
