# Component-energy/soft Koszul fault packet

The source-normalized moving relation for one complementary component is

\[
v_{\rm rat}=(2q/y,1).
\]

Clearing its denominator while retaining both routes gives the canonical
Koszul complex

\[
0\to R
\xrightarrow{(2q,y)^T}
R^2
\xrightarrow{(y,-2q)}
R\to0.
\]

The composition vanishes identically. At a generic point, on \(q=0\) with
\(y\ne0\), and on \(y=0\) with \(q\ne0\), both differentials have rank one
and the fiber complex is acyclic. At \(q=y=0\), both maps vanish and the
fiber homology dimensions are

\[
(1,2,1).
\]

Thus neither support alone creates a new class. Their intersection carries a
canonical derived Tor packet. This is source-derived from the two existing
component-factorization routes and requires no new divisor.
