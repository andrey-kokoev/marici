# K-pole depth classifies every tested physical image transition

Across both primes and all six tested maps among ambient degrees 8, 10, 12,
and 14, a labelled source generator survives target specialization exactly
when its `K_pole` is 2. Every `K_pole=1` generator becomes special-exact after
envelope enlargement.

This single rule accounts for the finite barcode:

- the three degree-eight survivors are precisely its three `K_pole=2` generators;
- the two deaths at each later adjacent transition are the two `K_pole=1` generators;
- all previously present `K_pole=2` directions persist through degree fourteen.

At degree fourteen the unresolved five births split into twelve total
`K_pole=2` persistent candidates, including three new ones, and two new
`K_pole=1` candidates expected by the finite pattern to die at degree sixteen.
That expectation is not yet a theorem.

The rule initially coincided with the hard boundary `K_DEPTH=2`, where level
2 has no outgoing K-multiplication row. The isolated `K_DEPTH=3` test falsifies
that boundary as the immediate survival mechanism: all eight old directions,
including all three former terminal-level directions, embed injectively, and
the deeper presentation adds five new directions. This remains finite
compatibility evidence, not geometric or unbounded persistence. All directions
remain p-tangent.
