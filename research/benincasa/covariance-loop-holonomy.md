# Loop holonomy of covariance Rees edge products

For a connected graph, arbitrary signs on edges modulo vertex sign changes are
classified by

\[
H^1(G,\mathbb Z_2).
\]

The source-derived covariance packet is more restrictive:

\[
\Xi_{ij}=u_i u_j.
\]

Its sign around every cycle is `+1`, so it is a coboundary and has trivial
holonomy.  Conversely, a nontrivial cycle product obstructs the existence of a
global resolved vector `u`.

Thus `H^1(G,Z2)` classifies nonliftable edge packets; it is not an additional
generic coefficient of the source-derived covariance Rees object.
