# Scalar-cubic evolution is not autonomous on a finite Hankel cone

Consider two positive states supported on (p=q):

- the symmetric distribution on (q=-2,0,2) with weights (1/4,1/2,1/4);
- the asymmetric distribution on (q=-1,2) with weights (2/3,1/3).

They have identical moments through degree two:

\[
\langle q\rangle=\langle p\rangle=0,
\qquad
\langle q^2\rangle=\langle qp\rangle=\langle p^2\rangle=2.
\]

Their third moments differ:

\[
\langle q^3\rangle=0
\quad\text{and}\quad
2.
\]

For (Dq=p, Dp=-q^2),

\[
D(p^2)=-2pq^2.
\]

Since (p=q) on both supports, the two states induce derivatives (0) and
(-4) on the same retained quadratic moment. Thus scalar-cubic evolution does
not descend to an autonomous vector field on the quadratic Hankel cone.
