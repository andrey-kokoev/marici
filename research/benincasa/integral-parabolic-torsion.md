# Integral parabolic torsion

For opposite relative character, the integral lift-change operator is
(\chi-1=-2). Hence

\[
H^1(S^1,\mathbb Z_-)=\mathbb Z/2.
\]

The parity of the unipotent shear is invariant under every integral change of
lift. Tensoring with \(\mathbb Q\) or \(\mathbb C\) makes two invertible and
kills the class.

Any additive scalar readout into a torsion-free group also annihilates this
class. Its detection therefore requires integral-lattice, mod-two, or another
discrete coefficient-sensitive observable; an ordinary complex Born scalar
cannot detect it as an additive extension class.
