# Rank-one covariance has an intrinsic pseudoinverse but a nontrivial nearby normal

In a basis adapted to a rank-one exceptional covariance block, write

\[
K_0=\begin{pmatrix}k&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Positivity of

\[
\begin{pmatrix}a&x&y\\x&k&0\\y&0&0\end{pmatrix}
\]

forces (y=0). Hence the cross vector lies in the image of (K_0), and the
Moore--Penrose Schur correction (x^2/k) is intrinsic on the exact stratum.

For nearby families

\[
K_t=\begin{pmatrix}k&0\\0&t^2\beta\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
v_t=(x,t\gamma),
\]

the finite correction is

\[
\frac{x^2}{k}+\frac{\gamma^2}{\beta}.
\]

Thus the exact rank-one restriction is canonical, while nearby specialization
retains a second flagged normal coordinate. The checker verifies 174,376
positive nearby families and finds multiple limits at 2,652 common rank-one
boundary points.
