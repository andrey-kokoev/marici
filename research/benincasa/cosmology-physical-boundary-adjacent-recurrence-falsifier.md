# Adjacent exact correction words are not stationary under vertical transport

For every A12 boundary coordinate, the exact A12-to-A14 correction word was
transported into the A16 descriptor presentation and compared with the exact
A14-to-A16 correction at the vertically matched coordinate.

- coordinates tested: 48;
- coefficient-identical stationary matches: 0.

Thus the strict direct/composite coherence theorem does not arise from one
adjacent correction word repeated at every cutoff. Coherence is a compositional
property of cutoff-dependent cells.

This falsifies the simplest descriptor-local recurrence candidate. It does not
exclude a nonstationary recurrence carrying explicit ambient-degree dependence,
a recurrence defined only modulo source syzygies, or a finite-generation
theorem for the correction module.
