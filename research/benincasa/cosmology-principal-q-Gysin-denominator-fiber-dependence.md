# The q-based Gysin denominator retains fiber degree five

Conjecture: the principal relation `q_g3=q_g1+q_g2` makes the q-based Gysin
denominator independent of fiber coordinates.

Exact substitution gives
`Lambda_P=-4*q_g1*q_g2` and denominator
`-4*q_g1^2*q_g2^2*(q_g1+q_g2)`. Its two surviving monomials have total fiber
degree five. The conjecture is falsified: the principal relation sharpens the
fiber factorization rather than producing a divisor on the `u` base.

The next test classifies rational diagonal rescalings
`(a*q_g1,b*q_g2,c*q_g3)`. It asks whether any nonzero coefficients can cancel
the fiber dependence; such a formula would still require separate source
authority.
