# Orbit-summed root pairings occupy an index-three shear lattice

A single tensor of the divisor root with a covector is rank one and cannot be a
nonzero cyclic endomorphism. Summing its three cyclic transports repairs
equivariance. For a covector with coordinates `(a,b)`, the resulting shear has
centralizer coordinates
\[
x=-a-2b,\qquad y=2a+b.
\]
The parameter matrix has determinant three, so the image is exactly
\(x\equiv y\pmod 3\), an index-three sublattice of all cyclic shears.

The invariant detector restricts to zero on A2, and no sourced
occurrence-sensitive covector is present. Thus current data select no shear.
Even if an integral covector is added, its orbit sum cannot leave the
index-three sublattice. The canonical A2 Cartan covector is the next bounded
candidate.
