# (P_6) Hom-indicial gate

At generic (P_6=0), the exact wall quotient connection of Entry 855 is
regular, hence

\[
R_{W_3}=0_3.
\]

Entry 867 gives the split algebraic residue

\[
R_{\mathcal A}=\operatorname{diag}\left(-\frac12,0\right).
\]

The degree-zero Hom indicial operator on a map
(h:W_3\to\mathcal A) is

\[
L_0(h)=R_{\mathcal A}h-hR_{W_3}=R_{\mathcal A}h.
\]

Therefore its ranks and kernels by target line are

\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
&\mathcal L_{P_6^{-1/2}}&\mathcal L_{D_1}\\
\hline
\operatorname{rank}L_0&3&0\\
\dim\ker L_0&0&3.
\end{array}
\]

Consequently every logarithmic extension residue landing in the relative
(-1)-monodromy line is removable by a regular triangular gauge.  Only the
trivial-monodromy algebraic line has a resonant rank-three extension slot.
