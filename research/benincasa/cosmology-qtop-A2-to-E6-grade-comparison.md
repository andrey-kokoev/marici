# A primitive cyclic grade map exists, but horizontality is open

The matrix
\[
B=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-1&1\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}
\]
identifies A2 with the occurrence-difference submodule of the three E6 chart
lines. Its maximal minors are `1,-1,1`, so the image is primitive. It also
satisfies `P B = B Q` for the source and target cyclic actions, and both sides
have homogeneity `-2`.

This constructs the integral associated-grade comparison. It does not prove a
horizontal comparison from the `L_top` tensor connection: only one E6 diagonal
coefficient is known to equal `alpha` at `u=0`. The E6 connection on all three
occurrence lines, horizontality of cycle scales `2,3,1/6`, and the connection
intertwiner residual are not serialized.

The next gate tests whether those constant cycle scales are horizontal gauges.
