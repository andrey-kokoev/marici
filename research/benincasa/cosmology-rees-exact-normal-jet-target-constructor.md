# Exact normal-jet target constructor

Problem: construct the exact width-three normal-jet presentation and a typed
principal tau-p coordinate vector.

Bold conjecture: seven exact offset fibers and rational coefficient weights
define the jet relations, while `p=x+y+3z` maps to coefficient 3 in normal
grade one at the constant five-denominator column.

Rivals: tau-p remains untyped; modular interpolation has no exact lift; or the p
cell selects one labelled jet coordinate.

Risky consequence: every `r0`, `r1`, and `r2` component must project to the
modular construction at primes 101, 103, and 107, and the target must name its
grade, raw label, and coefficient.

Strongest falsification attempt: seven exact fibers generated 2,524 raw rows
each and 7,572 assembled relations. Every jet component projects without
mismatch at all three primes. Since `p(3,6,-3)=0` and
`p(z+offset)=3 offset`, tau-p is the vector with coefficient 3 at normal grade
one and raw label `(0,1,1,1,1,1,(0,0))`.

Disposition: retained for the ambient-four width-three presentation. Membership
is now decidable but has not been computed. No physical period is asserted. The
next test performs exact assembled elimination and retains any nonzero target
residual.
