# Increasing K depth preserves the complete old image and adds five directions

At ambient degree eight, the physical half-twist resolved image has rank 8 for
`K_DEPTH=2` and rank 13 for `K_DEPTH=3`. Over both primes, the labelled map
from the shallow presentation is injective: all eight old directions survive,
including all three former terminal `K_pole=2` generators. Five new directions
complement the embedded image.

Thus the absence of an outgoing K-multiplication row at depth two was a real
interface confound but is not the immediate cause of survival. Adding that row
does not kill the old terminal block. The result still has finite-presentation
strength and does not establish geometric or unbounded persistence.

The next discriminating object is a two-parameter persistence square varying
ambient degree and K depth independently.
