# Shared-kernel chains and valuation doubling

For a finite chain

\[
f_i=\operatorname{diag}(1,\lambda_i),
\]

the composite kernel amplitude is multiplied by \(L=\prod_i\lambda_i\).
The Hermitian packet is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}a^2&Lab\\Lab&L^2b^2\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Hence the kernel amplitude and image–kernel cross term have multivaluation
\((1,\ldots,1)\), while the pure kernel density has
\((2,\ldots,2)\).  On the common diagonal \(\lambda_i=t\), their orders are
\(n\) and \(2n\).  This follows for every finite length by multiplicativity.
