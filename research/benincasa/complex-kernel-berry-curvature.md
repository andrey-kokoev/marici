# Complex kernel lines and Berry curvature

On the affine chart of \(\mathbb{CP}^1\), take

\[
|\psi(z)\rangle=\frac{(1,z)^T}{\sqrt{1+|z|^2}},
\qquad z=x+iy,
\]

and \(P=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|\).  A local phase changes the Berry
connection by an exact one-form but leaves \(P\) invariant.  Nevertheless,
the curvature is recovered from the projector:

\[
F=-i\operatorname{Tr}(P,dP\wedge dP)
=\frac{2,dx\wedge dy}{(1+x^2+y^2)^2}
\]

up to the stated orientation convention.  Thus density forgets the gauge
phase but not the curvature of the tautological amplitude line.  An additional
flat central local-system twist remains invisible to the projector.
