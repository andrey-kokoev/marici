Conjecture: an audited route still gives a source-authorized primitive integral
`tau_p` lift.

Every route fails a distinct gate. The existing principal coefficient lattice
is `2Z`. The formal gauge requires an unauthorized sign system; equivariant
descent to the untwisted lattice is zero, while norm descent doubles the class.
No chain map identifies that norm image with the principal lattice. The weighted
Gysin route lacks a p-locus base map. The naive `X` assignment has incompatible
support; the principal `q` functions remain fiber-valued; and no nondegenerate
linear or affine assignment converts their Gysin denominator into a base
function.

Thus no source-authorized primitive integral route exists in the audited source
envelope. This is not a global impossibility theorem. Rational absorption, the
closed relative cocycle, and a conditional primitive column survive but do not
supply the missing specialization, projection, integral readout, or source
generator.
