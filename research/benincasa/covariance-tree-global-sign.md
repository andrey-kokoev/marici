# Global sign propagation for covariance Rees normals

Suppose the nonzero resolved normals `u_i` are attached to vertices of a
connected labelled tree and the even exceptional packet records the edge
products `Xi_ij=u_i u_j`.

Choosing the sign of one root determines every other sign recursively along
the unique paths.  The only alternative is the simultaneous flip

\[
u_i\longmapsto-u_i
\]

for every vertex.  Thus the generic deck group is one global `Z2`.

If a soft zero removes sign transport through a vertex or edge, the remaining
connected components acquire independent signs.  This is a support effect.
