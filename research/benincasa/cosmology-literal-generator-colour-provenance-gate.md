# Literal provenance exposes a missing third colour

The literal identity deletes `q_g3,q_g23,q_g31`. Under the faithful refinement,
`q_g3` has singleton-complement colour. The lowercase marked-pair denominators
`q_g23,q_g31` are distinct from both singleton axes and the uppercase
`q_Gij` same-pair axes.

Thus the literal colour pattern is `S,L,L`, where `L` denotes the lowercase
marked exceptional-pair orbit. It selects neither minimum equivariant
splitting `all-S` nor `all-P`. The binary ambiguity is therefore not resolved.

This reveals that the two-colour target omitted the orbit carrying the actual
boundary terms. A cyclic refinement needs a third colour `L` and must adjoin
`q_g12` to close the orbit containing `q_g23,q_g31`.
