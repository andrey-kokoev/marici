# Exceptional Bunch--Davies moment covector

Let \(\Gamma_{\rm exc}\) be the projectivized specialization of the complete
Bunch--Davies relative chain, and let

\[
\omega_{\rm exc}
=
\frac{d_{\rm rel}\widehat a\wedge d_{\rm rel}\widehat b}{W}
\]

include the source orientation, Kummer branch, and marked denominator
factors. Pairing the exceptional parity-check row

\[
(\widehat y,-2\widehat q)
\]

with this current gives

\[
\Lambda_{\rm BD}
=
\left(I_y,-2I_q\right),
\qquad
I_y=\int_{\Gamma_{\rm exc}}\widehat y\,\omega_{\rm exc},
\qquad
I_q=\int_{\Gamma_{\rm exc}}\widehat q\,\omega_{\rm exc}.
\]

If this pair is finite, nonzero, and independent of admissible weighted
lifts, its kernel is the source-normalized line

\[
\mathbb Q\langle(2I_q,I_y)\rangle.
\]

This construction does not predict that \(I_y/I_q\) is constant. In
general it is a projective period coordinate on the all-soft exceptional
coefficient family.
