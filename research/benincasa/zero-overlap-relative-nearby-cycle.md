# Zero interference overlap and nearby cycles

Let the interference section vanish as

\[
c(s)=s^m v,
\]

where \(v\) belongs to the relative comparison line
\(L\otimes R^*\).  The Rees grade retains \(v\), but the integral vanishing
factor \(s^m\) has trivial monodromy.  Therefore nearby monodromy remains the
relative flat holonomy of the comparison line.

For a nontrivial one-dimensional twist, the invariant and coinvariant spaces
vanish over the generic field because \(T-1\) is invertible.  The supported
costalk exists, but it does not produce a global scalar without a matching
source cycle or compensating reference twist.
