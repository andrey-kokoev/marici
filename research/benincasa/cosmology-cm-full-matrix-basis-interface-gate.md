# The CM quotient lacks a source-labelled three-dimensional complement

The generic Cayley–Menger quotient has rank seven. The ten labelled normal
coefficients span rank four, with basis `nu1`, `nu2`, `nu3`, and `nu1^2` in
every tested presentation. Hence three quotient directions remain outside the
labelled normal tower.

Row reduction can choose three complementary pivots, but the receipts neither
identify them as source classes nor match them across points and primes. The
current derivative result stays inside the labelled rank-four image.
Consequently a source-labelled seven-element basis and the three
\(7\times7\) connection matrices are not constructed.

The missing interface must serialize seven standard quotient monomials, embed
the rank-four normal subspace, source-label the three-dimensional complement,
differentiate every basis element in each direction, and prove basis transport
across points and primes. Equal rank alone does not authorize the complement.
