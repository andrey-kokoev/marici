# Tau-p residue stabilization

Problem: test persistence and quotient typing under ambient enlargement from
four to five.

Bold conjecture: the exact tau-p class remains nonzero at ambient five and is
identified canonically with the ambient-four class.

Rivals: the 69-coordinate residue is a truncation artifact; nonvanishing
persists; or equal modular signatures lack an exact comparison map.

Risky consequence: ambient-five exact reduction must retain a nonzero residue
and replay at primes 101, 103, and 107.

Strongest falsification attempt: ambient-five assembly has 11,628 rows, rank
5,888, and 5,740 dependencies. Tau-p reduction uses 74 pivots and leaves 219
nonzero coordinates. The residue replays exactly at all three primes. Its digest
is `d14fc526bca4d96110be1a09e3f88ea4d1535c0eb4131fbd307c387786411165`.

Disposition: revised. Nonvanishing persists from ambient four to five, so the
ambient-four residue is not eliminated by this enlargement. The normal forms
have different dimensions; no proved quotient transition identifies them
canonically. This remains two finite-cutoff results, not a colimit or physical
class. The next test must construct the exact quotient transition and test the
target image.
