# Saturation of the disjoint-cut five-site eliminants

After removing \(x=t^2\), the remaining forbidden reductions are

\[
p=ab=0
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
q=c\lambda=0.
\]

Since \(c=-5t/2\), the second condition is precisely \(\lambda=0\) away
from total energy. The first cubic equation forces either condition onto the
ordinary region threshold

\[
m^2x=d_{ij}^2.
\]

For every one of the six labelled orbit representatives, substituting that
threshold into the second cubic gives two exact obstruction values: one at
\(p=0\), and one at the unique \(p\) forced by \(q=0\). All twelve values are
nonzero in \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\).

Therefore none of the six degree-six eliminants is generated by a zero root
distance or a vanishing Landau multiplier. They survive the first saturation
gate. The remaining gate is Hessian/nondegeneracy certification at a generic
root of each eliminant.
