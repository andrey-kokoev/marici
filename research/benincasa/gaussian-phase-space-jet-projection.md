# Gaussian phase-space jet projection

At one marked time, the value of the statistical propagator supplies only one
real coordinate and cannot recover a general Gaussian tangent.  The first
phase-space jet supplies the symmetric covariance matrix

\[
\delta\Gamma=
\begin{pmatrix}
\delta\langle qq\rangle &
\delta\langle\{q,p\}/2\rangle\\
\delta\langle\{q,p\}/2\rangle &
\delta\langle pp\rangle
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Writing \(\beta=x+iy\), statistical coordinate \(n\), and
\(c+is=e^{2i\omega\eta_0}\), the rescaled projection matrix is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
c&-s&1\\
-c&s&1\\
s&c&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det=-2(c^2+s^2)=-2.
\]

Thus the rank-three Gaussian coefficient object is canonically recoverable
from the first phase-space jet on the source-marked initial hypersurface.  No
choice of endpoint primitive is needed.  Without the marked hypersurface, the
coordinates evolve by symplectic transport and are not absolute functions of
an unmarked propagator value.
