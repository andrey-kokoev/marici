The physical half-twist image was computed at the four corners

`(A,K_DEPTH)=(8,2),(10,2),(8,3),(10,3)`

over both primes. Their dimensions are `8,7,13,9`.

Inside the target `(10,3)` quotient:

- the direct image of `(8,2)` has rank zero;
- the image of `(10,2)` has rank four;
- the image of `(8,3)` has rank three;
- the two edge images intersect trivially and have union rank seven;
- the target has a further rank-two complement born only at the combined corner.

The labelled square commutes: both paths from `(8,2)` have zero composite.
Thus persistence along either coordinate separately does not survive the first
cofinal diagonal enlargement. The former K-pole persistence barcode was a
one-parameter projection of a genuinely two-parameter replacement system.

This is finite bifiltration evidence, not an unbounded theorem. Every target
image remains p-tangent, and no tau map or physical period is constructed.
