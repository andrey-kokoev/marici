# The first central Weyl grade is absorbed by the binary Cut defect

In the homogenized two-occurrence CCR algebra, use

\[
\Delta q=q_L+q_R,
\qquad
\Delta p=p_L+p_R,
\qquad
\Delta\hbar=\hbar_L+\hbar_R.
\]

Entry 1658 gives

\[
D(p^3)=-3q^2p^2+6i\hbar qp+2\hbar^2.
\]

Define

\[
\Theta_D(p^3)
=
\Delta D(p^3)
-(D_L+D_R)\Delta(p^3).
\]

Exact normal ordering in both tensor factors yields eight nonzero terms. The only term containing no \(q\) or \(p\) is

\[
\boxed{4\hbar_L\hbar_R.}
\]

There is no scalar residual independent of the occurrence-labelled central generators.

Thus the \(2\hbar^2\) single-occurrence correction does not create a new support or standalone anomaly after sewing. Primitive central coproduct turns it into the cross term in

\[
2(\hbar_L+\hbar_R)^2
-2\hbar_L^2-2\hbar_R^2
=4\hbar_L\hbar_R,
\]

which belongs to the existing binary co-Leibniz defect.
