# A single primitive correction enters the partial-fraction cone with coefficient two

In ordered pair coordinates `(12,13,23)`, the p partial fraction has vector
`(1,-1,-1)`. Its Čech boundary is

`(0,2,-2) = 2*(0,1,-1)`.

Assume a minimal augmentation supplies one correction generator whose boundary
is the primitive vertex direction `(0,1,-1)`. For a combination with p-cell
coefficient `a`, arbitrary Čech-face coefficient `b`, and correction coefficient
`k`, closure requires

`2*a+k=0`.

Hence a unit p coefficient forces `k=±2`; no attachment with both coefficients
units exists. Under this one-generator hypothesis, the partial fraction cannot
map to the universal integral tau cell with unit coefficient.

This is conditional: two independently sourced exceptional corrections could
split the factor two into unit contributions. The next test is therefore to
compute the separate ordered boundaries contributed by `g23` and `g31`, not to
divide the obstruction by two.
