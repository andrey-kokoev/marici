# Principal Rees target membership gate

Problem: test whether the principal three-wall tau-p target belongs to the exact
rank-1,382 raw-row space.

Bold conjecture: tau-p is already a labelled vector in the single-fiber column
packet and reduces to zero against that basis.

Rivals: raw-fiber membership is defined; tau-p exists only in an assembled
normal-jet space; or the affine p identity alone selects its coordinates.

Risky consequence: the target must have explicit coordinates in the same width
as the basis, including its normal-jet grade and assembly map at the wall.

Strongest falsification attempt: the source identity
`p=-q_g1-q_g2+q_g3` is established, but its receipt explicitly records that the
tau-p column was not computed. At `(3,6,-3)`, `p=0`; tau-p is therefore a
normal-jet object assembled from interpolation grades. The exact rank-1,382
basis contains only single-fiber raw columns, and prior receipts serialize no
labelled target transformation.

Disposition: rejected as ill-typed for the current basis. No membership test or
zero residual is claimed. This does not prove nonmembership of a future
assembled target. The required successor constructs exact rows at the seven
sampling offsets, the width-three jet assembly, and an explicit target map from
the p coefficient cell.
