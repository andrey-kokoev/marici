# The current occurrence projector cannot define the Čech connector

The deletion source is an eight-object, twelve-arrow Boolean cube with axes
`q_g1`, `q_g2`, and `q_G12`; every arrow changes the denominator-module mask.
The occurrence target has axes `G12:e6`, `G23:e6`, and `G31:e6`, and its
candidate projector is an endomorphism on one chart module.

The axis labels are disjoint, the objects are not identified, and no component
maps or naturality squares are provided. The zero connector exists abstractly
but cannot send the antisymmetric word to its nonzero occurrence shadow.
Therefore the current projector does not define a nonzero source-derived
connector.

A viable enlargement requires an object map for all eight deletion masks, an
orientation-preserving axis map, component maps, and commutativity on all twelve
edges. The only recorded candidate source is the incomplete A2 `e6` occurrence
morphism; it would need complete rank-twelve triangular transport.
