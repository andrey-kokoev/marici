# Interchange of incomparable simplex faces

The smallest transverse corner is the labelled table

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
\delta\theta a&\delta b\\
\theta c&d
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The two simplex-face normals are independent.  Resolving the \(\delta\)-face
and then the \(\theta\)-face sends the mixed occurrence to
\(\delta(\theta a)\); reversing the order sends it to
\(\theta(\delta a)\).  These agree strictly.  The side occurrences \(b,c\)
remain separately labelled, so the comparison does not collapse the complete
joint table to its marginals.

The transverse blowup square therefore has no interchange class in this
finite model.  Its closure depends on retaining the complete labelled joint
coefficient, not on adding a carrier incidence.
