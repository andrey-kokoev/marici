# Degenerating measurement projectors

Let \(|\psi\rangle=e_1\) and

\[
|m_\varepsilon\rangle
=\frac{e_0+\varepsilon(ae_1+be_2)}
{\sqrt{1+\varepsilon^2(a^2+b^2)}}.
\]

The limiting measurement is normalized and orthogonal to the state, but

\[
p_\varepsilon
=\frac{\varepsilon^2a^2}{1+\varepsilon^2(a^2+b^2)}.
\]

Thus the first probability grade vanishes and the second is
\(|\langle v|\psi\rangle|^2=a^2\).  Different projective tangents \([a:b]\)
and different parameter speeds share the same normalized endpoint but give
different supported coefficients.  Born normalization of the endpoint does
not select the missing measurement jet.
