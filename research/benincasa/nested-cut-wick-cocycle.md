# Independent Gaussian Cut coherences satisfy the nested cocycle

Take two internal occurrences \(3,4\), interaction

\[
H_{\rm int}=q_1q_3q_4,
\]

and observable

\[
f=p_1q_3q_4.
\]

The derivation gives

\[
Df=-q_3^2q_4^2+p_1p_3q_4+p_1q_3p_4.
\]

For independent centered Gaussian pushforwards with variances \(\nu_3,\nu_4\),

\[
C_3C_4(Df)=-\nu_3\nu_4,
\qquad
D_{\rm obs}(C_3C_4f)=0.
\]

Define the first coherence at each stage by

\[
H_i=C_iD-D_{\widehat i}C_i.
\]

Then the composite commutator obeys the telescoping identity

\[
\boxed{
H_{34}=C_3H_4+H_3C_4.
}
\]

For the test observable, \(H_4(f)=-\nu_4q_3^2\), so \(C_3H_4=-\nu_3\nu_4\), while \(H_3C_4(f)=0\). Reversing the Cut order gives the same answer.

Thus the first Wick coherence satisfies the nested-Cut cocycle and order independence on independent Gaussian coefficient states. No higher obstruction appears in this finite test. Correlated internal states remain a separate case because their joint pushforward need not factor into commuting one-occurrence expectations.
