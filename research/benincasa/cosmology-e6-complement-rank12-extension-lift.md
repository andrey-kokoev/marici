# Cyclic symmetry leaves a rank-two shear lattice

The primitive complement is fixed on the A2 associated grade. For the cyclic
A2 matrix
\[
Q=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},
\]
the integral endomorphisms commuting with `Q` are exactly
\[
M(x,y)=\begin{pmatrix}x&y\\-y&x+y\end{pmatrix},\qquad x,y\in\mathbb Z.
\]
Thus cyclic extension shears form a rank-two lattice.

The two previously exhibited triangular lifts differ by a nonzero shear that
is invisible on the associated grade. Cyclic equivariance alone therefore does
not select a rank-twelve lift. Selection requires the marked `q0` action,
explicit `C2 dlog(X3/X2)` shear coefficients, the full connection, and
connector naturality components.
