# Generic finite labelled-Cut realization of the Gaussian second-Rees cone

Let the observed mode be accompanied by orthogonal environment labels \(r\).
At first order, take the normalized perturbative state

\[
|\Psi\rangle
=|0;0_E\rangle
+\epsilon\left(
a|2;0_E\rangle
+\sum_r b_r|1;r\rangle
\right)+O(\epsilon^2).
\]

The anomalous first jet is carried only by the coherent two-particle
amplitude:

\[
|\beta_1|^2=2|a|^2.
\]

The occupation second grade is

\[
n_2=2|a|^2+\sum_r|b_r|^2.
\]

Therefore

\[
n_2-|\beta_1|^2
=\sum_r|b_r|^2\geq0.
\]

Equality holds exactly when every environment-crossing labelled Cut amplitude
vanishes.  The checker verifies the identity over a three-label finite census;
the algebra is independent of the number of orthogonal labels.

The statement is a tangent-cone/purification result.  It does not identify the
continuum label measure or prove that a particular interacting kernel spans
every abstract Gaussian mixed direction.
