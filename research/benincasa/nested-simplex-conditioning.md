# Nested zero-marginal conditioning

Let two nested mixture faces be approached through

\[
\pi_{0k}=\varepsilon\delta s_k,
\qquad
\pi_{1k}=\varepsilon r_k,
\]

and write \(S=\sum_k s_k\), \(R=\sum_k r_k\).  Conditioning first on
the outer row and then inside row zero gives

\[
\frac{\delta S}{R+\delta S}\frac{s_k}{S}
=\frac{\delta s_k}{R+\delta S},
\]

which is exactly the result of direct conditioning after cancelling the common
scale \(\varepsilon\).

The first exceptional coordinate records the relative row scale \(\delta\).
Over its zero section, the second exceptional coordinate records the labelled
projective direction \([s_k]\).  Their transition is the displayed product;
there is no residual cocycle or extension datum on the overlap.

Thus nested zero-marginal conditioning is represented by the ordinary iterated
blowup of labelled simplex faces.  The coefficient object acquires an
exceptional flag, while the Cut carrier acquires no new incidence stratum.
