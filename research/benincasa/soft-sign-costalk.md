# Soft component-sign costalk

For the nonzero support graph `H`, the incidence boundary over `Z2` has

\[
\operatorname{rank}\partial_1=|V(H)|-c(H).
\]

Therefore

\[
H_0(H,\mathbb Z_2)\cong\mathbb Z_2^{c(H)}.
\]

The generic connected global sign specializes to the diagonal copy of
`Z2`.  The supported quotient is

\[
\widetilde H_0(H,\mathbb Z_2)
\cong\mathbb Z_2^{c(H)-1}.
\]

This types the additional soft component signs as a canonical incidence
costalk rather than an independent carrier generator.
