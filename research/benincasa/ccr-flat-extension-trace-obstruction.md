# Exact CCR forbids finite-rank flat moment extension

Suppose a truncated quantum moment matrix admitted a rank-preserving flat
extension with rank \(r>0\).  Its GNS quotient would be an \(r\)-dimensional
space, and left multiplication by \(Q\) and \(P\) would define finite matrices
obeying the retained canonical relation

\[
[Q,P]=2iI_r.
\]

But every finite pair of matrices satisfies

\[
\operatorname{tr}[Q,P]=0,
\]

whereas the exact CCR requires

\[
\operatorname{tr}(2iI_r)=2ir\neq0.
\]

This contradiction is independent of the fourth-cumulant coordinate.  Hence
no exact-CCR packet can close by a finite-rank flat extension.  Any physical
completion must be infinite-dimensional, non-flat at every finite truncation,
or replace the CCR by an explicitly different finite approximation.

The momentum EFT cutoff does not evade the obstruction: it truncates admitted
momentum channels, not oscillator occupation number in each admitted mode.
