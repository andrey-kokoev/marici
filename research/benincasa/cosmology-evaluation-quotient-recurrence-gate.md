# The full evaluation-kernel quotient gives only tautological recurrence

Let the descriptor-labelled rational source-word module at cutoff A map to the
sparse row module by exact evaluation. Quotienting by the complete evaluation
kernel is canonical, and evaluation is a faithful coordinate on that quotient:
the quotient is its image.

In this quotient the finite boundary correction classes compose. Their q-word
boundaries telescope under vertical transport, so the direct class equals the
sum of adjacent classes.

This does not promote the finite theorem. The quotient removes every
source-syzygy and correction-capability distinction, and membership in its
image already presupposes that the boundary has a source lift. It cannot prove
that a new A18 target lies in the image. Therefore its recurrence restates the
finite evaluated boundary identity rather than generating later exactness.

A useful quotient route would require a smaller source-natural equivalence that
preserves enough correction capability to constrain new cutoffs, together with
a coordinate proved faithful on that quotient.
