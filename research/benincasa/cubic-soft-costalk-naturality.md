# Cubic coefficient naturality on soft covariance costalks

Let `C_ij` be the labelled cubic Cut pair coefficients and `Xi_ij=u_i u_j` the
exceptional covariance tensor.  On a nonzero support graph `H`, the exceptional
coefficient is the componentwise contraction

\[
F_C(H)=\sum_{(i,j)\in E(H)}C_{ij}u_iu_j.
\]

Restriction to a deeper support deletes precisely the labelled rows and
columns outside that support.  Summing the resulting component contractions is
identical to direct restriction of `C:Xi`.  Multiplication by the conditional
fiber factor `(s^2-1)` commutes with both operations.

Thus disappearing components contribute zero by extension-by-zero; no hidden
coefficient extension remains on the relative-H0 costalk.
