# Frozen-source audit of the proposed Big-Bang boundary

## Question

Does the primary cosmological-integral source already define the physical
initial-boundary object required by Entry 1057?

## Frozen source

Paolo Benincasa and Francisco Vazão, *The Asymptotic Structure of
Cosmological Integrals*, arXiv:2402.06558v3.

The source constructs perturbative wavefunction observables with conformal
time integrated over

\[
\eta\in(-\infty,0),
\]

with Bunch--Davies data at past conformal infinity and a wavefunction readout
at the late spacelike boundary. Its Cayley--Menger contour and sector
decompositions type loop-momentum geometry and infrared/ultraviolet
asymptotics of those observables.

## Endpoint classification

The source fixes

\[
a(\eta)=\left(\frac{-\ell_\gamma}{\eta}\right)^\gamma,
\qquad \eta\in(-\infty,0).
\]

Writing \(r=-\eta\), proper time obeys

\[
dt=a\,d\eta=-\ell_\gamma^\gamma r^{-\gamma}dr.
\]

Therefore:

- \(\gamma>1\): \(a\to0\) at \(\eta=-\infty\), at finite proper-time
  distance. This is the source's candidate Big-Bang endpoint, and it already
  carries the Bunch--Davies prescription.
- \(0<\gamma\le1\): \(a\to0\) at \(\eta=-\infty\), but at infinite
  proper-time distance; vanishing scale factor alone does not establish a
  finite initial singularity.
- \(\gamma<0\): \(a\to0\) at \(\eta=0\), a finite proper-time future
  endpoint in the source orientation.

Thus the source does contain a background-dependent initial-boundary
candidate and a state prescription. What it does not construct is a
support-sensitive nearby-cycle comparison that treats this endpoint as a
degeneration of the energy/Cut carrier.

Consequently none of the following may be renamed the Big-Bang carrier
stratum *without the background map proving that identification*:

- the total-energy divisor;
- the all-soft locus;
- an infrared or ultraviolet graph-weight boundary;
- either conformal-time endpoint independently of \(\gamma\).

Entry 1057 therefore has a source-defined first background to test:
\(\gamma>1\), with normal coordinate \(\rho=(-\eta)^{-1}\) at the
Bunch--Davies endpoint.

## Exact missing datum

A source-grounded test requires, for a specified cosmological background:

1. the normal coordinate \(\rho=(-\eta)^{-1}\) at \(\eta=-\infty\);
2. the \(\gamma>1\) scale-factor/proper-time classification above;
3. the Bunch--Davies relative chain and energy-space \(i\epsilon\)
   prescription approaching \(\rho=0\);
4. orientation, sheet, regulator, and monodromy conventions;
5. a specialization or nearby-cycle map into the frozen energy/Cut carrier.

Only after these are supplied can one test whether the initial boundary is a
supported degeneration of the existing carrier or a genuinely new carrier
stratum.

## Narrow conclusion

The current cosmology program already contains the candidate boundary and its
state prescription for \(\gamma>1\). The remaining object is the typed
comparison from this background endpoint into the carrier's supported
nearby-cycle calculus. This is absence of a comparison map, not evidence for a
new primitive.
