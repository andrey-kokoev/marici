# Diagonal witnesses split into six structural classes

Exact witness DAG profiles were extracted for all nine source generators of
`(10,3)->(12,4)` over both primes. Every profile, including its complete
unnormalized skeleton digest, is prime-stable.

The witnesses do not share one common skeleton. Their closure sizes are
`557,571,819,835,1135,1151`, producing six classes. K-pole level and position
relative to the monomial cutoff both affect the witness.

Candidates 5--8 form the only repeated closure-size class: each has 1135
nodes and family counts `IBP/K/q = 287/165/683`. Their relation-support
multisets remain distinct even after subtracting the candidate pivot monomial
from every relation-row exponent. The four normalized support hashes are
prime-stable and pairwise different.

This falsifies both a single candidate-independent skeleton and the proposed
pivot-DAG monomial-shift template for the interior terminal-K stratum. A
source-natural stratified homotopy not derived from greedy pivot witnesses
remains open.
