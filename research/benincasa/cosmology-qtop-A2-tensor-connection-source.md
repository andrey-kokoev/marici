# The tensor connection exists only on the top quotient grade

Under the source-column convention, the two wall vectors span an invariant
submodule. The `q_top` span is not invariant: at `(u,v)=(1,3)`, its coupling to
`q_wall1` is `beta1=-1/2`. Therefore the canonical object is the quotient line
\[
L_{\rm top}=Q/\langle q_{\rm wall1},q_{\rm wall2}\rangle,
\]
not a selected subline.

Its flat scalar connection extends canonically to
\(L_{\rm top}\otimes A_2^{\rm const}\) as `d + alpha I_A2`. This construction
needs no splitting, but it supplies only an associated-quotient connection. It
does not split the rank-twelve extension, encode the logarithmic root, or
provide an E6 grade comparison map.
