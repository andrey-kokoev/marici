# Cumulant positivity is a filtered Hankel condition

Moments are coupled polynomials in cumulants:

\[
m_n=\sum_{k=1}^n\binom{n-1}{k-1}\kappa_km_{n-k}.
\]

Consequently cumulant species do not possess independent positivity. At unit
variance and zero odd cumulants, positivity through order four requires

\[
\kappa_4\ge-2.
\]

When (kappa_4=0), positivity through order six requires

\[
\kappa_6\ge-6.
\]

For example, (kappa_6=-7) preserves the positive order-four packet but makes
the order-six Hankel matrix indefinite.

Thus finite grades have finite positivity tests, while a global positive state
is a compatible inverse system of all such Hankel cones.
