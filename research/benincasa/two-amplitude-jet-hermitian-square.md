# Two amplitude jets and the Hermitian-square Rees packet

Let the selected zero-probability outcome carry two independent amplitude
normals,

\[
|\chi(\varepsilon,\eta)\rangle
=\varepsilon|u\rangle+\eta|v\rangle.
\]

Its unnormalized density is

\[
\varepsilon^2|u\rangle\langle u|
+\varepsilon\eta
\bigl(|u\rangle\langle v|+|v\rangle\langle u|\bigr)
+\eta^2|v\rangle\langle v|.
\]

The relative phase already appears in the mixed bidegree \((1,1)\): replacing
\(|v\rangle\) by \(-|v\rangle\) preserves both diagonal PSD rays and reverses
the mixed term.  Thus the second total normal grade retains the complete
Hermitian square of the labelled amplitude jets.  No higher flag is required.
