# Isolated-contact null-syndrome audit

At the three labelled normal lines \(\nu_i=0\), the relevant grade-two and
grade-three routes have different spectator coefficient objects. In columns
\((S_1,S_2,S_3,S_{123})\), the source-weighted residue matrix is

\[
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
4P_{23}&0&0&-8C_2C_3\\
0&4P_{31}&0&-8C_1C_3\\
0&0&4P_{12}&-8C_1C_2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Here \(C_i\) is an isolated-contact coefficient and \(P_{jk}\) is a
connected two-site spectator period. At a fixed generic point the kernel is
generated projectively by

\[
\left(
\frac{2C_2C_3}{P_{23}},
\frac{2C_1C_3}{P_{31}},
\frac{2C_1C_2}{P_{12}},
1
\right).
\]

This line varies with the spectator periods. Forgetting them manufactures
the constant vector \((2,2,2,1)\). Two independent exact packets have
nonproportional kernel lines, and that constant vector lies in neither.
