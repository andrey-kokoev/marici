# Eight-site ordinary supported-activation conventions

## Hard-to-vary claim

Let \(S_O\) be one of the 36 source-admitted rank-four wall strata and let
\(D_O\) be one of its simple horizontal coefficient divisors.  The minimal
ordinary physical comparison is the support-local map

\[
\Phi_O^{\rm ord}:\mathcal C_{\Gamma_{\rm BD}}
\longrightarrow
R\Gamma_{S_O\cap D_O}(\operatorname{gr}_{D_O}\mathcal V_O),
\]

where \(\Gamma_{\rm BD}\) is the source Bunch--Davies chain.  No analytically
continued chain, new support summand, or carrier facet is admitted after
seeing \(D_O\).

The falsifier is finite: a single occurrence for which the source equations
allow \(X_i>0\), or a source-derived operation that creates support after the
empty pullback, defeats physical dormancy.

## Frozen variance and support

The target is supported on \(S_O\cap D_O\), not merely on the ambient routing
divisor \(D_O\).  The admitted operations here are ordinary restriction,
ordinary boundary Gysin/residue, and ordinary localization/Cut support
pullback.  Each is support-local: after pullback to an empty support, its
physical-chain source is zero.

For every rank-four orbit, the exact Gordan certificate produces

\[
g_O\in\operatorname{rowspan}(R_{X,O})\cap\mathbb Q_{\ge0}^8,
\qquad g_O\ne0.
\]

If \(R_{X,O}X=0\) and every \(X_i>0\), then

\[
0=g_O\cdot X>0,
\]

a contradiction.  Hence

\[
\Gamma_{\rm BD}\cap S_O=\varnothing
\]

before imposing either coefficient divisor.

## Coefficient grade versus physical image

The two linear families are

\[
2k-3=0 \quad (21\text{ orbits}),
\qquad
7+6k-8l=0 \quad (15\text{ orbits}).
\]

Each occurs to first order in the source-normalized square Jacobian
determinant.  Over the generic point of that factor, the determinant has
valuation one, so the coefficient associated grade is a length-one,
rank-one object.  The adjugate identity

\[
\lambda J=\det(J)e_1
\]

fixes its labelled conormal line.  This is coefficient geometry, regardless
of whether a physical chain sees it.

Each orbit also carries a nonconstant companion coefficient discriminant.
No irreducibility or total nearby-cycle rank is inferred from its displayed
factorization.  Every irreducible component nevertheless lies over the same
source wall \(S_O\), so its pullback to the physical chain is empty by the
same certificate.

By contrast, the supported physical map is the unique zero map because its
source support is already empty.  The eight occurrence orientations are
retained; changing a wedge-sorting sign multiplies zero by \(\pm1\).  The
ordinary physical image is therefore zero, and the vanishing is
lift-independent before any ordinary normal or weighted lift is chosen.

The comparison cone retains the coefficient nearby-cycle grade but contains
no physical class.  It is classified as dormant coefficient support over the
unchanged Carrier.
