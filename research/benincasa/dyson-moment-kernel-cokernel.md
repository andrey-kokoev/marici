# Finite Dyson-to-moment kernels are exactly the conserved Hamiltonian powers

For

\[
F_{D,n}:P_{\le D}\to P_{\le D+n},
\qquad f\mapsto D^nf,
\]

the tested kernel dimension is

\[
\left\lfloor\frac D3\right\rfloor+1
\]

for (0\le D\le18) and (1\le n\le6). It is spanned by the filtered powers
of the conserved Hamiltonian

\[
\widetilde H=3p^2+2q^3.
\]

These explicit characteristic-zero kernel vectors give a rank upper bound;
matching ranks over two large primes attain that bound and certify equality.

The cokernel grows with the padded target (P_{\le D+n}), so this census does
not identify a stable relative coefficient class.
