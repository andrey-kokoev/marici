# Physical half-twist seed words transport exactly across three cutoffs

The complete exact degree-14 source words were mapped by typed descriptors into
ambient degrees 12 and 16. At each cutoff, source and target rows were rebuilt
with physical gamma `-1/2` over four primes and rationally reconstructed before
comparison.

Both pole identities reconstruct their complete sparse targets:

| ambient degree | pole-0 rows | pole-1 rows | exact reconstruction |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 12 | 27 | 45 | yes |
| 14 | 27 | 45 | yes |
| 16 | 27 | 45 | yes |

Thus the same descriptor-labelled rational source words are compatible across
these three ambient presentations. The result remains a finite-cutoff theorem
for the canonical exponent `(0,0)`. It does not supply a recurrence for all
ambient degrees or for monomial-shifted targets.
