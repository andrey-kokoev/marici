# Wilson character of the two-loop holonomy

For the holonomy representation generated by Entry 1741's matrices, the
frame-free packet is

\[
W(w)=\operatorname{Tr}\rho(w)
\]

for loop words \(w\).  It is invariant under simultaneous conjugation and
cyclic rotation of a word.  The commutator has

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(ABA^{-1}B^{-1})=-2,
\]

so Wilson data detects noncommutativity.  But
\(\operatorname{Tr}(AB)=\operatorname{Tr}(BA)\), so it cannot recover a based
matrix ordering or fiber frame.
