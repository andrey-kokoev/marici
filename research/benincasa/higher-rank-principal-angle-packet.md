# Higher-rank projectors and principal angles

For state and measurement subspaces with projectors \(P,Q\), the nonzero
eigenvalues of \(PQP\) are \(\cos^2\theta_i\), the squared principal-angle
cosines.  The total overlap \(\operatorname{Tr}(PQ)\) records only their sum.

Already in rank two, the spectra

\[
\{0,1\},
\qquad
\left\{\frac9{25},\frac{16}{25}\right\}
\]

have the same trace but different determinant.  Thus the complete static
readout packet is the characteristic polynomial of \(PQP\), equivalently all
principal angles, not one total Born scalar.
