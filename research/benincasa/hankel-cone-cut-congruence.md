# Cardinality-weighted Cut merge preserves finite Hankel cones

Let (L_X,L_Y) be positive truncated moment functionals through degree (2d),
and set

\[
Z=\alpha X+\beta Y.
\]

Substitution of (z^k) into bivariate monomials defines a matrix (T). The
output Hankel matrix satisfies

\[
H_Z=T^T(H_X\otimes H_Y)T.
\]

Therefore positivity of the product functional implies positivity after
cardinality-weighted merge. This works independently at every finite grade and
is compatible with the inverse system required globally.

The exact checker verifies 810 congruence entries and 810 entries of explicit
positive Gram decompositions across sixty rational cardinality-weighted merge
cases.
