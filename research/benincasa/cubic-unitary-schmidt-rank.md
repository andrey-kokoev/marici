# The cubic unitary generates unbounded Schmidt rank

Across the bipartition (q_1mid(q_2,q_3)), the cubic interaction unitary is

\[
e^{-igtq_1q_2q_3}
=
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}
\frac{(-igt)^n}{n!}
q_1^n\otimes(q_2q_3)^n.
\]

For generic product wavefunctions, the two monomial families are linearly
independent. The order-(K) Dyson truncation therefore has Schmidt rank

\[
K+1.
\]

At generic nonzero time the complete series has infinite Schmidt rank. The
exact checker verifies this diagonal rank pattern through order 256.
