# Legendre integral cusp

The source-defined elliptic block is the level-two Legendre variation with
modulus \(m=A/B\). At \(E_T=0\), \(B=\ell_3E_T\), so the inverse modulus is a
linear local coordinate.

At this infinity cusp the Legendre factor has, up to integral symplectic
conjugacy and orientation,

\[
T_{\rm Leg}=\begin{pmatrix}-1&-2\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The Kummer factor contributes the second sign. In the source-normalized
tensor product the signs cancel:

\[
T_{\rm src}=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad N=T_{\rm src}-I,
\qquad \operatorname{rank}N=1,
\quad N^2=0.
\]

The integer two is the cusp width, not a fitted normalization. This agrees
with Entry 289 and rechecks the input of Entries 1147 and 1152. It does not
resolve Entry 1152's integral extension class in \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\).
