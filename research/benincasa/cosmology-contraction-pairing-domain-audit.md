# Existing pairings do not section the rank-26 contraction torsor

A functional that descends through source evaluation annihilates its kernel.
It is therefore constant on each contraction torsor and cannot select one
source representative. A selecting functional must instead be typed directly
on the kernel and nonzero there.

The available candidates fail that gate:

- the integral-reference audit derives neither coefficient normalization nor a
  map to its required reference flag;
- the infinity Leray covector is defined on the separate `e6`–`e9` quotient,
  explicitly annihilates its algebraic kernel, and has no map to the rank-26
  contraction kernel;
- the relative Stokes audit finds that the naive contraction does not descend,
  while its residue-compatible Gauss–Manin pairing remains uncomputed;
- the renormalization audit lacks a source-derived physical normalization
  observable.

No audited pairing supplies a functional that is both typed over and
nontrivial on the relevant kernel. This is a current source-data obstruction,
not a theorem excluding a future pairing.
