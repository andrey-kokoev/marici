# The moving connection generates the exceptional difference but not an integral unit selector

At the physical half twist, the finite connection orbit of the sourced sum of
the two four-wall cells has rank 12. Over both `F_32003` and `F_32009`, it
contains their antisymmetric difference.

This does not remove the integral obstruction. The source sum has principal
face coefficient `-2`; the difference has principal coefficient zero. Their
integral span therefore has principal coefficient lattice `2 Z`. Recovering an
individual four-wall cell from sum and difference requires division by two,
which is permitted in the tested odd finite fields but forbidden as an
integral tau construction.

Thus moving transport opens an antisymmetric finite-field direction without
constructing a unit principal-face map. The next gate is provenance for the
orbit membership and a search for an independently integral odd-principal
cell outside the sum/difference lattice.
