must introduce an odd principal coefficient and the unique ordered correction

\(d^2=0\), and Rees-shear invariance.

principal lattice is \(2\mathbb Z\); its source census has no asymmetric
incidence object; the relative pairing is undefined; and the Rees extraction
fails its production contract with seven candidates for eight factors and no
labelled coordinates.

Therefore no source-authorized primitive \((1,1)\) lift exists in the audited
serialized source envelope. This is not a global impossibility theorem. The
minimal escape is a source-labelled integral generator with odd principal
coefficient and boundary \((0,-2,2)\), followed by a proved comparison map,
exact replay, and semantic two-prime coherence.
