# Cut descent of coherent component kernels

For independent coherent inputs with component density kernels \(\rho^X\) and
\(\rho^Y\), the labelled joint kernel is

\[
\rho^{XY}_{(j,k),(j',k')}
=\rho^X_{jj'}\rho^Y_{kk'}.
\]

Partial trace gives

\[
\operatorname{Tr}_Y(\rho^X\otimes\rho^Y)
=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho^Y)\rho^X,
\]

and transition-kernel expectations factor.  Hence normalized inputs recover
each factor exactly.  Coincident packet displacements do not identify their
occurrence labels.

The independent coherent sector therefore closes under ordinary labelled
tensor product.  Its interchange is the canonical bosonic tensor symmetry,
not the unconstrained matrix commutator of Entry 1719.
