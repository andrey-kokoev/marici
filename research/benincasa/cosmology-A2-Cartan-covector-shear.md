# The Cartan covector yields identity, not the root-sensitive shear

For the divisor root `(0,-1)`, the A2 Cartan form gives covector `(1,-2)`.
Their single tensor remembers the root but fails cyclic equivariance. Summing
the three cyclic transports gives `3I`; exact coefficientwise division by three
produces the primitive identity shear with centralizer coordinates `(1,0)`.

This exposes a dichotomy. The single tensor is occurrence-sensitive but breaks
`C3`; the normalized orbit sum is canonical and equivariant but forgets which
root was `dlog(X3/X2)`. It therefore does not select the required root-sensitive
`C2 dlog(X3/X2)` rank-twelve shear.

Whether the normalized identity satisfies the missing `q0` and connector
naturality equations remains undefined because those operators are not
serialized.
