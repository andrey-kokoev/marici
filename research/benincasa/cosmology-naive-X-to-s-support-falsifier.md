# The naive X-to-s identification fails its support test

Conjecture: under the deliberate assignment `(s1,s2,s3)=(X1,X2,X3)` with
`X1=1`, the Gysin and norm singular supports agree on the p locus.

On `v=2u`, the assignment is `(1,3u/2-1,-u/2)`. Exact substitution gives
`Lambda_P=2*(2u^2-3u+2)` and pulled denominator
`-u*(3u-2)*(2u^2-3u+2)/2`. Its greatest common support factor with
`u*(u-1)*(u-2)*D` is only `u`. The candidate has two unsupported components,
while `u-1`, `u-2`, and `D` occur only in the norm residual.

The conjecture is falsified. The assignment was not source-authorized, and its
factor mismatch also rules it out as a formula capable of identifying the two
parity obstructions.
