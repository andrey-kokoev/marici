# Unipotent parabolic extension

Let χ=σ₁σ₂ be the relative character on
\(\operatorname{Hom}(L_2,L_1)\). A unipotent shear around the loop is a
one-cocycle \(a\), and changing the local quotient lift by \(b\) acts as

\[
a\longmapsto a+(\chi-1)b.
\]

Therefore

\[
H^1(S^1,\mathbb Q_\chi)=\operatorname{coker}(\chi-1).
\]

For \(\chi=1\), the shear is unchanged by every regular change of lift and
defines a rank-one extension class. For \(\chi=-1\), multiplication by
\(-2\) is invertible over \(\mathbb Q\), so the shear is removed by
\(b=a/2\).

The extension is thus controlled by coefficient monodromy, not by a new
carrier incidence.
