# The corner is the unique horizontal coordinate fiber

Conjecture: the E6-minus-`q_top` diagonal mismatch vanishes on a
source-relevant coordinate fiber larger than `u=0`.

The `dv` residual numerator factors as
`u*((-2*u^2+2*u+1)*v+4*u^3-3*u-2)`. On a fiber `u=c`, only the `dv` component
pulls back. Requiring it to vanish identically forces `c=0`: for nonzero `c`,
the coefficient of `v` and the constant term would imply both
`-2c^2+2c+1=0` and `3c=0`.

No constant-`v` fiber works. The `du` numerator's leading coefficient forces
`v=5`, while its cubic coefficient remains `-3`. In particular, `v=2` is not a
horizontal comparison locus.

The conjecture is falsified for coordinate fibers. The exact survivor is the
pullback to `u=0`; the full bivariate residual does not vanish there. The next
test asks whether the principal three-wall target factors through this corner.
