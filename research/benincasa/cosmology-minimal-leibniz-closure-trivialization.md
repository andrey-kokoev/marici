# Coordinate-stable IBP closure trivializes the master quotient

Suppose the relation space contains every exponent-labelled IBP row \(I_e\)
and is closed under multiplication by a fiber coordinate. The Leibniz identity
gives

\[
M_e=I_{e+1}-xI_e.
\]

Hence every bare master \(M_e\) lies in the closed relation space. The physical
boundary targets are bare master-coordinate derivative targets, so all their
classes then vanish by definition rather than by a derived source identity.

The minimal coordinate-stable enlargement is therefore noninformative: it
trivializes the quotient whose target exactness it was intended to prove. This
is the standard typing failure behind trying to make fiber de Rham cohomology a
module over fiber-coordinate functions; exact forms are not stable under that
multiplication.

A nontrivial recurrence would need a separately sourced parameter-space
Gauss–Manin action. The ambient monomial cutoff and its fiber-coordinate shifts
do not provide that action.
