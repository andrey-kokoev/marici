# Only a predeclared time-reversal character removes the residual derivative counterterm

Compare

\[
H_W=qpq
\]

with the residual Hermitian counterterm

\[
\lambda\hbar q.
\]

Under phase-space inversion \(q,p\mapsto-q,-p\), both are odd. Under canonical scaling \(q\mapsto s q,\ p\mapsto s^{-1}p\), both have weight \(+1\). Neither symmetry distinguishes the terms.

Under antiunitary time reversal,

\[
q\mapsto q,
\qquad
p\mapsto-p,
\]

one has

\[
qpq\mapsto-qpq,
\qquad
\hbar q\mapsto+\hbar q.
\]

Therefore, if the source derivative interaction is independently required to be purely time-reversal odd, then

\[
\boxed{\lambda=0.}
\]

This is conditional. In a cosmological sector with a chosen time orientation, boundary condition, or regulator that does not preserve this time-reversal character, the argument is unavailable. The shared Cut calculus cannot promote an absent symmetry into source authority.
