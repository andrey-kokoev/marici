# The gauge elliptic curve has no sourced match

Conjecture: the gauge curve is already identified with an elliptic cover in the
source envelope.

Its binary-quartic invariants are `I=241`, `J=-2450`, and its j-invariant is
`13997521/7232`. The sole structured elliptic candidate is the unspecialized
five-site family
`w^2=x(1-x)(x*h1+(1-x)*h2)(x*delta1+(1-x)*delta2)`.
It supplies neither a parameter specialization from the `p` locus, a typed base
map, nor a fixed invariant. Fitting its free parameters to the gauge curve
would manufacture the missing map.

The conjecture is falsified as a source identification. The next test asks
whether the algebraic gauge nevertheless descends as a sign-twisted comparison:
the deck involution changes `sqrt(D)` by a sign while leaving its logarithmic
derivative invariant.
