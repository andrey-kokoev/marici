# The source Kummer character does not activate the `A2` Milnor plane

The link of

\[
Z^2-U^3=0
\]

is the trefoil. In the source orientation, use the Seifert matrix

\[
V=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its Alexander presentation is \(tV-V^T\), with determinant

\[
\Delta(t)=t^2-t+1.
\]

The algebraic coefficient line is \(P_6^{-1/2}\), so its meridian character
is \(t=-1\). At that character,

\[
tV-V^T=
\begin{pmatrix}-2&1\\1&-2\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det=3.
\]

Over characteristic zero this presentation is invertible. Since the character
is nontrivial, degree-zero invariants also vanish; the local twisted link
cohomology is therefore zero in every degree.

Thus the rank-two ordinary Milnor object of the cusp is not selected by the
source Kummer line. This is a coefficient-character cancellation, not a claim
that the underlying cusp or its untwisted Milnor cycles are absent.
