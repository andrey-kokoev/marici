# No audited artifact supplies the rank-twelve shear

Four candidate certificates were checked. The exceptional `e6` bridge covers
only the invariant line and marked top-column bridge. Cyclic Leray naturality
covers six local germs but no overlap transition or equality of global
connection matrices. The double-soft cospan explicitly leaves the logarithmic
extension open. The triple-soft exceptional census explicitly excludes a full
nearby connection.

Thus the audited artifacts contain zero source-derived \(q_0\)-to-`e6` shears
and zero cyclically coherent full rank-twelve transports. Local cyclic
equivariance, bridge persistence, and absence of a new exceptional carrier do
not promote to extension data.

The remaining concrete candidate is a derivation of the Cayley–Menger cyclic
transverse connection in a source-normalized triangular frame, followed by an
explicit test of its \(q_0\)-to-`e6` block.
