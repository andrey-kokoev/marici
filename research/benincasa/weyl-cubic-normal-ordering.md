# Cubic Weyl evolution produces a central quantum moment grade

Work in the homogenized CCR algebra

\[
[q,p]=i\hbar
\]

before fixed-\(\hbar\) specialization. For

\[
H_{\rm int}=\frac{q^3}{3},
\]

the primitive evolution rules remain exact:

\[
Dq=p,
\qquad
Dp=-q^2.
\]

There is no quantum correction to the primitive nonlinear force or to Entry 1655's binary defect on \(p\).

Higher observables do acquire ordering terms. Using

\[
[p^m,q^3]
=
\sum_{k=1}^{\min(m,3)}
(-i\hbar)^k k!
\binom mk\binom3k
q^{3-k}p^{m-k},
\]

one obtains

\[
D(p^m)
=
-m q^2p^{m-1}
+2i\hbar\binom m2 qp^{m-2}
+2\hbar^2\binom m3p^{m-3}.
\]

The first central correction occurs at \(m=3\):

\[
\boxed{
D(p^3)
=
-3q^2p^2+6i\hbar qp+2\hbar^2.
}
\]

Thus quantum ordering enriches the filtered moment coefficients while leaving the primitive interaction presentation intact. The \(\hbar^2\) term is genuine coefficient data, not a new carrier incidence.
