# Maximal A12 shell closures replay exactly over the rationals

The physical q-only correction at ambient degree 12 was tested on shift `(5,0)`,
a maximal dependency-closure representative for each pole. Four-prime rational
row reconstruction and exact elimination give:

- pole 0: 45 independent base rows, 45 pivot columns, full corrected-target
  equality, maximum denominator 1134;
- pole 1: 94 independent base rows, 94 pivot columns, full corrected-target
  equality, maximum denominator 1111065984.

The exact q template is held fixed; only the tangent and K base coefficients are
freshly solved. This proves two maximal A12 shell representatives over the
rationals. It does not yet prove every shift, the A14/A16 families, or an
ambient recurrence from closure profiles alone.
