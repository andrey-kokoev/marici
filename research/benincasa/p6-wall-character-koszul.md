# The two-loop character kills the exceptional resonance

Near a transverse (P_6)-(D) crossing, the relative-sign algebraic target
has monodromy

\[
(-1,+1),
\]

around the ordered loops ((P_6,D)).  The labelled wall-1 source line has

\[
(+1,-1).
\]

Their Hom character is therefore

\[
\chi=(-1,-1).
\]

The same statement holds at (P_6)-(H), with wall 2 replacing wall 1.
The local group-cohomology complex is

\[
\mathbb C
\xrightarrow{(-2,-2)^T}
\mathbb C^2
\xrightarrow{(-2,2)}
\mathbb C.
\]

It is exact.  Although the exceptional loop is the product of the two
original loops and has character (+1), that product resonance is not in the
image of a local extension class on the punctured bidisk.
