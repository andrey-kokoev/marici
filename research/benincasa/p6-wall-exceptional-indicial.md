# Exceptional indicial gate at (P_6\cap(D\cup H))

At a transverse (P_6)-(D) or (P_6)-(H) crossing, ordinary blowup adds
the two incident logarithmic residues on the exceptional divisor.

In doubled units, the split algebraic target residues are

\[
(-1,0),
\]

while Entries 864 and 874 give the marked quotient residues

\[
(0,0,-1).
\]

The zero-exponent Hom pairs are exactly

\[
(\mathcal L_{-},w_{-}),\qquad
(\mathcal L_{+},w_{0,1}),\qquad
(\mathcal L_{+},w_{0,2}).
\]

Thus the total zero-exponent multiplicity remains three, but one slot has
moved onto the relative-sign algebraic line.  It is the unique labelled wall
direction: wall 1 at (D), wall 2 at (H).

This is only an admissible slot.  The actual exceptional residue of the
source extension must still be derived and projected to it.
