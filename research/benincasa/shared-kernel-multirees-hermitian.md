# Shared-kernel singular maps

For two singular maps with the same kernel,

\[
f_\varepsilon=\operatorname{diag}(1,\varepsilon),
\qquad
g_\eta=\operatorname{diag}(1,\eta),
\]

the composite suppresses the kernel amplitude by \(\varepsilon\eta\).  The
Hermitian packet is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
a^2&\varepsilon\eta ab\\
\varepsilon\eta ab&\varepsilon^2\eta^2b^2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Thus the shared-kernel amplitude has multidegree \((1,1)\), and its pure
density costalk has multidegree \((2,2)\).  On the diagonal
\(\varepsilon=\eta=t\), these become ordinary orders two and four.  The
iterated and direct packets agree when the multigrading is retained.
