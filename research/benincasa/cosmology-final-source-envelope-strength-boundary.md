# Final strength boundary and falsifier

The strongest established claim is: no source-authorized primitive `tau_p` lift
exists in the audited source envelope.

This is stronger than failure to find a gauge: parity, equivariance, source
authority, comparison, and base-map routes were separately obstructed. It is
weaker than global impossibility across future source enlargements.

An internal falsifier would be an already-admitted but omitted typed chain from
source support through integral coefficients, transport, canonical comparison,
and physical readout, carrying a primitive generator to `[1,1]` in the
untwisted integral target. A newly authorized chain instead enlarges the source
envelope and supersedes the fixed-envelope result; it does not retroactively
falsify the audit.

Conditional rational absorption, the relative cocycle, and the conditional
primitive column are retained at their proved strengths and are not promoted to
a lift.
