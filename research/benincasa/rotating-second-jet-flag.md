# Rotating second-jet flag

For a rank-one leading reference frame, labelled occurrence subtraction
canonically produces a filtration (L_1\subset V) with graded lines
(L_1) and (L_2=V/L_1). Its transition functions have parabolic form

\[
P=\begin{pmatrix}\sigma_1&a\\0&\sigma_2\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The shear (a) records a local lift of the quotient line. It disappears on
the associated grade, whose transition is
(\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_1,\sigma_2)). The two diagonal endomorphism
grades have trivial character; both off-diagonal grades carry the relative
character (\sigma_1\sigma_2).

Thus the source jet defines a canonical parabolic local system without a
chosen Smith basis. A globally fixed scalar matrix is not canonical unless
the filtered frame is separately trivialized.
