# Rotating kernel and real Berry holonomy

A loop of real kernel lines can be represented by

\[
k(\phi)=
\begin{pmatrix}
\cos(\phi/2)\\ \sin(\phi/2)
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad 0\le\phi\le2\pi.
\]

Then \(k(2\pi)=-k(0)\), while

\[
k(2\pi)k(2\pi)^T=k(0)k(0)^T.
\]

Thus the amplitude kernel line has gauge-invariant \(\mathbb Z_2\) holonomy,
but its Hermitian-square density projector has trivial holonomy.  The missing
sign is coefficient-local-system data erased by quadratic readout, not a new
carrier incidence.
