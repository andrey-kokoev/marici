# Affine shifts cannot cancel the rank-two leading term

Conjecture: base-dependent constants in `s_i=L_i(A,B)+c_i(u)` remove the fiber
dependence of a rank-two assignment.

The Gysin denominator polynomial is homogeneous of degree five in the `s_i`.
Consequently the fiber-degree-five part after an affine substitution is exactly
`F(L_1,L_2,L_3)`; constants affect only lower degrees. For a rank-two linear
part with nonzero rows, the preceding gate proves this leading polynomial is
nonzero. No lower-degree term can cancel it.

The conjecture is falsified for this full class. A bounded census of 12,960
affine assignments verified the leading-term identity without failure. Cases
with rank at most one or a zero leading row remain outside this result.

The next test classifies rank-one affine lines on which the Gysin polynomial
could be constant.
