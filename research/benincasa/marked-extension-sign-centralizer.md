# Marked-extension sign centralizer

Entry 868's exact flat candidate maps each of three marked generators
nontrivially into both split algebraic lines. Its extension-incidence graph
is therefore \(K_{3,2}\), hence connected.

A diagonal sign real structure preserving a nonzero extension edge must give
the same sign to its source and target. Connectivity forces all five signs
to agree. The only possibilities are global \(+1\) and global \(-1\).

Thus the candidate permits only a global sign times plain conjugation \(K\);
it does not permit a relative \(ZK\) between the two algebraic lines. This is
a theorem about the exact flat candidate, not yet the uncertified source
rank-twelve block.
