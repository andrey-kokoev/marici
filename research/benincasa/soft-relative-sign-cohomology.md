# Relative sign cohomology on soft covariance support

Let `H` be the induced subgraph on vertices where the resolved covariance
normal is nonzero.  If `H` has `c(H)` connected components, source-derived edge
products determine vertex signs up to

\[
\mathbb Z_2^{c(H)}.
\]

Within every component, source-derived cycle holonomy is trivial.  Arbitrary
edge packets still have obstruction group `H^1(H,Z2)`, but nontrivial classes
do not lift to a resolved vector.

On the generic localized locus every normal is nonzero and the original graph
is connected, so `c(H)=1`.  All additional sign freedom is soft-supported.
