# Cut descent of finite Gaussian-mixture generators

Let independent blocks have labelled mixture sets `J_X,J_Y`.  Their product
state has labelled components

\[
J_{XY}=J_X\times J_Y,
\]

with weights and collective displacements

\[
p_{jk}=p_jq_k,
\qquad
\mu_{jk}=w_X\mu_j+w_Y\nu_k.
\]

The complete cubic generator is obtained by inserting these product data into
the finite log-sum-exp factor of Entry 1713.  Coincident numerical
displacements are not identified: their occurrence labels remain distinct.

For three blocks, nested products only reassociate the same labelled triples,
and cardinality weights compose to the same final displacement.  Component
growth is multiplicative coefficient growth, not a new carrier operation.
