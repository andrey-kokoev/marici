# The algebraic Kummer divisor has an `A2` cusp at the soft scattering corner

At \((u,v)=(0,2)\), write \(z=v-2\) and

\[
Z=z+u(1+2u-2u^2).
\]

Exact completion of the square gives

\[
4P_6=Z^2-4u^3(2-u)(1-u^2).
\]

The final factor is a unit with value \(8\) at the center. Over the completed
characteristic-zero local ring it can be absorbed by a formal coordinate
change, so the divisor germ is

\[
Z^2-U^3=0.
\]

This is an \(A_2\) cusp. Its Jacobian algebra is

\[
\mathbb Q[[U,Z]]/(Z,U^2)
=\mathbb Q\langle1,U\rangle,
\]

and has Milnor number two. This rank belongs to the singular coefficient
divisor over the already existing \(E_T=X_2=0\) carrier corner. It is not by
itself a physical nearby-cycle rank or evidence for another carrier cell.
