# The primitive observable coproduct requires an occurrence-additive quantum parameter

In the fixed-parameter Weyl algebra,

\[
[q,p]=i\hbar\,1.
\]

Attempt the primitive coproduct

\[
\Delta q=q\otimes1+1\otimes q,
\qquad
\Delta p=p\otimes1+1\otimes p.
\]

Then

\[
[\Delta q,\Delta p]
=
[q,p]\otimes1+1\otimes[q,p]
=
2i\hbar\,1\otimes1.
\]

But a scalar/unital image of the right-hand side is only

\[
\Delta(i\hbar\,1)=i\hbar\,1\otimes1.
\]

Hence the defining ideal is not preserved. The primitive \(q,p\) coproduct does not descend to the Weyl algebra after fixing one global nonzero value of \(\hbar\).

The relation is restored in the homogenized central extension where \(\hbar\) is itself primitive:

\[
\Delta\hbar=\hbar\otimes1+1\otimes\hbar.
\]

Equivalently, each labelled occurrence carries its own central contribution and sewing adds them before any physical diagonal specialization.

Thus quantum Cut/cumulant coalgebra must be built before fixing the coarse scalar \(\hbar\), or in an equivalent braided/relative formulation. This is a type correction, not an interaction anomaly.
