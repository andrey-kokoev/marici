# Quotient-to-reachable-image comparison is tautological

For every finite filtered transform

\[
F_{D,n}:P_{\le D}\to P_{\le D+n},
\]

the induced map

\[
P_{\le D}/\ker F_{D,n}\to\operatorname{im}F_{D,n}
\]

is canonically an isomorphism by the first isomorphism theorem.

Consequently defining the target as the reachable image cannot test whether a
relative coefficient class survives. A nontrivial comparison requires a target
filtration declared independently from (F_{D,n}), together with its own
normalization and support.
