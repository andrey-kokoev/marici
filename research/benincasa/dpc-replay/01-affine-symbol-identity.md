# Replay 01 — affine versus symbol identity

## Problem
The full affine operator was confused with its leading homogeneous symbol.
## Bold conjecture
The radial symbol solution lifts to the affine equation.
## Named rivals
Exact affine lift; lower-order affine obstruction.
## Risky consequence
Full affine substitution has zero residual.
## Strongest falsification
Exact substitution leaves 29 nonzero terms; the affine cokernel pairs nontrivially with the target.
## Disposition
Reject the affine lift. Retain the radial identity only in the associated-graded system.
