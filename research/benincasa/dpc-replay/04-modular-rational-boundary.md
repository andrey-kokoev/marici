# Replay 04 — modular versus rational consequence

## Problem
A modular augmented-rank gap was promoted without rank preservation.
## Bold conjecture
The modular gap lifts to a rational cokernel certificate.
## Named rivals
Good-prime lift; bad-prime rank drop; exact certificate failure.
## Risky consequence
A reconstructed rational functional annihilates every column and detects the target.
## Strongest falsification
Modular ranks alone provide no such functional.
## Disposition
Treat modular gaps only as preflight. Rational claims require exact reconstruction and verification.
