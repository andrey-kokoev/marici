# Replay 02 — affine grade separation

## Problem
Homogeneous grade separation was applied to an inhomogeneous operator.
## Bold conjecture
Affine image components remain separated by total degree.
## Named rivals
Grade-separated affine image; lower-order degree coupling.
## Risky consequence
Every operator column is homogeneous in the asserted grade.
## Strongest falsification
The affine coefficients of H and Q generate lower-degree terms and couple grades.
## Disposition
Reject grading as an affine obstruction proof. Use the exact finite-support cokernel instead.
