# Transverse Hessian certificate for the five-site anomalous thresholds

At a disjoint-cut critical point choose the common gradient direction as
\(n_e\), with

\[
n_i+n_j=\lambda n_e.
\]

On the transverse two-plane, the Hessian of the Landau combination is
diagonal. Writing

\[
S=y_i+y_j,\qquad p=y_iy_j,\qquad q=y_e\lambda,
\]

its determinant is

\[
\left(\frac Sp-\frac q{y_e^2}\right)
\left(\frac{q^2S}{4y_e^2p}-\frac q{y_e^2}\right).
\]

The two possible degeneracies are therefore polynomial:

\[
S^2y_e^2-pN=0,
\qquad
N-4p=0,
\]

where \(N=S q\) in the reduced equations. Exact elimination over
\(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\) shows that neither degeneracy ideal contains an entire
degree-six Landau component for any source-labelled orbit.

The first elimination initially leaves a degree-one factor in (x=t^2). To require the
same critical value of (p), reduce both Landau cubics modulo the quadratic
Hessian equation. If their linear remainders are

\[
r_f=a_fp+b_f,\qquad r_g=a_gp+b_g,
\]

then compatibility requires (a_fb_g-a_gb_f=0). For every orbit the gcd of
this determinant with the pairwise resultant factor is exactly

\[
x=t^2.
\]

Thus the only compatible deeper locus is the already frozen total-energy
point (t=0); there is no additional nonzero Hessian-degeneracy locus.

Thus the generic critical point on every degree-six eliminant has nonzero
transverse Hessian. Each eliminant defines a genuine simple anomalous-threshold
divisor of the source period on the frozen cyclic five-site slice.
