# Integral orientation-cover operations do not create the odd leg

Let `s` be the deck involution. The integral operations available from a
sourced double cover are the difference `1-s` and transfer `1+s`. For a
principal coefficient with trivial deck action, difference gives zero and
transfer doubles. For sign action, difference doubles and transfer gives zero.
Thus both operations have principal coefficient zero or even.

A sheet difference can formally produce the required boundary `(0,-2,2)`, but
it cannot simultaneously produce an odd principal leg from the existing even
source. The projector `(1-s)/2` would remove the parity obstruction only after
inverting two or proving integral divisibility; neither is source-authorized.

Therefore a double cover generated from the current source cannot realize the
formal generator `u`. This does not exclude a new independently sourced
primitive anti-invariant class.
