# The exact coherence packet is a rank-four conormal module

## Typing correction

Let

\[
P=\mathbb F_p[a,b,c,z]
\]

and let (I) be generated by the three logarithmic gradient relations and
the localization relation (zK-1). The tested quotient

\[
R=P/I
\]

has dimension seven over (mathbb F_p).

Because (P) has dimension four, finite quotient length gives

\[
\operatorname{ht}(I)=4.
\]

The ideal has four generators. Hence it is a complete intersection, and

\[
I/I^2\simeq R^4.
\]

Its underlying finite-field dimension is therefore (28), not three.

## Consequence

The three base directions define three labelled sections of (I/I^2). They
are not three scalar coefficient classes. Scalar reduction forgets which of
the four exact generators produced each component and therefore destroys the
Koszul differential before transport is formed.

After base change to (R), the exterior grades of the complete-intersection
Koszul packet have dimensions

\[
(7,28,42,28,7).
\]

This explains why Entries 2648 and 2651 could not produce cross-grade maps:
they scalarized the conormal packet before totalization.
