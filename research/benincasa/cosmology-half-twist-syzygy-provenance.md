# The physical half twist has eight replayable degree-eight candidates

The degree-eight resolved-syzygy extraction was repeated at the physical
coefficient `gamma=-1/2` over `F_32003` and `F_32009`. It produces eight,
rather than seven, source-replayable candidates. Labelled pivots, source support
counts, and relation-family support agree across primes.

The additional physical candidate has pivot label
`(k=1,q-levels=(1,1,1,1,1),monomial=(0,8))`, uses 48 source rows, and draws
from `IBP`, `K`, `g2`, `g3`, and `g31`. Other physical candidates range from
IBP/K-only support to all five marked families.

The complete eight-dimensional image was independently proved equal to the
p-tangent image. This packet therefore corrects the generic-twist provenance
interface without constructing an intrinsic normal class. The extra candidate
disappears under enlargement to degree ten; an explicit cutoff relation for
that disappearance remains to be extracted.
