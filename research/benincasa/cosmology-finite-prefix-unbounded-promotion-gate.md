# Exact finite coherence does not determine an unbounded theorem

The complete tested prefix consists of ambient degrees 12, 14, and 16, with
every marked-stratum K target exact and the boundary correction cells strictly
coherent over the rationals.

Two continuations agree on that entire prefix:

1. every later target remains exact;
2. a new source-labelled obstruction class first appears at degree 18.

The finite receipts contain no arrow that excludes the second continuation.
Consequently they cannot be promoted to unbounded exactness without at least
one additional source-derived datum: a recurrence, a finite generation bound,
a no-new-class theorem, or a cofinal comparison map covering every later
cutoff.

This is an underdetermination obstruction to promotion, not evidence that a
recurrence is impossible. It neither withdraws the finite theorem nor proves an
unbounded nonvanishing class.
