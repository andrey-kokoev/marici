# Cubic evolution has a canonical co-Leibniz defect

Give the canonical observables the primitive coproduct

\[
\Delta q=q\otimes1+1\otimes q,
\qquad
\Delta p=p\otimes1+1\otimes p.
\]

For free evolution, \(Dq=p\) is compatible with this coproduct.

For the cubic potential, \(Dp=-q^2\). The two coderivation routes are

\[
\Delta(Dp)
=
-q^2\otimes1-2q\otimes q-1\otimes q^2,
\]

and

\[
(D\otimes1+1\otimes D)\Delta p
=
-q^2\otimes1-1\otimes q^2.
\]

Their difference is

\[
\boxed{
\Theta_D(p)=-2q\otimes q.
}
\]

This is the connected split term of the nonlinear force. More generally, \(Dp=-q^n\) produces all mixed binomial terms

\[
-\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\binom nk q^k\otimes q^{n-k}.
\]

Thus the evolution derivation is a strict coderivation only for linear dynamics. Interacting evolution forms a lax/curved differential coalgebra whose co-Leibniz defect is canonically generated by the interaction polynomial. The defect requires no fitted grade-specific generator.
