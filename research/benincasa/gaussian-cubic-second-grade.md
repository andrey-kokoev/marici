# Second cubic normal grade of the Gaussian density-state orbit

Let `Pi = P - t(Q^2-a)` for an initially diagonal centered Gaussian with
`<Q^2>=a` and `<P^2>=b`.  Weyl moments may be evaluated by ordinary Gaussian
Wick contraction because the initial Wigner function is Gaussian and the
cubic unitary acts by the exact shear.

The required moments are

\[
\langle \Pi^2\rangle=b+2t^2a^2,
\]

\[
\langle Q^2\Pi^2\rangle=ab+10t^2a^3.
\]

Therefore

\[
\kappa_{QQ\Pi\Pi}=8t^2a^3.
\]

This is the first connected fourth-cumulant grade.  The pure momentum fourth
cumulant begins later:

\[
\kappa_{\Pi\Pi\Pi\Pi}=48t^4a^4.
\]

All sixth and higher moments are fixed by the same exact density operator;
there is no independent flat-extension choice on this orbit.
