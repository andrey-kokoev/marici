# The A2 e6 morphism does not determine rank-twelve transport

The Clifford-to-`e6` map is source-derived and cyclically equivariant on the
\(A_2\) occurrence-difference grade, with common weight \(-2\). It does not
include the marked quotient \(q_0\), the off-diagonal shear
\(C_2d\log(X_3/X_2)\), or the complete rank-twelve connection.

This omission is structural: triangular lifts with shear zero and shear one
have the same associated-graded map but are distinct extension morphisms.
Hence the relational-grade map cannot determine the full transport. The
invariant detector \((1,1,1)\) annihilates \(A_2\) and cannot recover the
missing shear.

Promotion is therefore refused. The next bounded test is a census of existing
rank-twelve connection artifacts for a source-derived \(q_0\)-to-`e6`
off-diagonal shear with cyclic coherence.
