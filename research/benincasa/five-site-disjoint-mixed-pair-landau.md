\[
q_e=5t+2y_e,
\qquad

\qquad e\notin\{i,j\},
\]

\(p=ab\). Stationarity with both multipliers nonzero implies

\[
n_i+n_j=\lambda n_e.
\]

cubics in \(p\). Their Sylvester resultant was computed over
\(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)[x]\) for the six source-admitted disjoint-cut orbits.

Every raw resultant contains the universal total-energy factor \(x\). After
removing it, every orbit retains a degree-six polynomial. Exact Symbolica
factorization does not reduce any of the six polynomials into the previously
frozen one-wall factors.

These are source-derived candidate anomalous-threshold divisors. Before they
are promoted to singular support, the remaining gate is saturation by both
Landau multipliers and verification of a nondegenerate critical point on each
irreducible factor. Their appearance is coefficient/period support over the
existing marked-wall carrier, not evidence for a new carrier incidence.
