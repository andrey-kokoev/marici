# Gaussian Dyson–Cut factorization

Let \(C\) be the occurrence- and endpoint-location-resolved production map,
and let \(R\) be the retarded response from a source vertex to the marked
phase-space jet.  The Keldysh noise kernel is the Cut Gram object

\[
N=C C^\dagger.
\]

Then the statistical Dyson response factors identically:

\[
RNR^\dagger
=RCC^\dagger R^\dagger
=(RC)(RC)^\dagger.
\]

The labelled Wick census gives the same connected fish multiplicity in the
\(BB,BS,SS\) sectors.  The ordered internal occurrences \((q,k)\) and
\((k,q)\) are exchanged by the source involution.  Their factor two cancels
the identical-pair \(1/2!\), leaving one physical unordered Cut norm.

This proves the algebraic integrand-level factorization once \(C\) is the
source production map.  It does not independently certify the continuum
measure, regulator, or positivity of a non-Hermitian convention.
