# Canonical interior marked-q seeds survive two primes and three cutoffs

The canonical seed extraction was replayed over prime 32009 at ambient degrees
12, 14, and 16 and compared with the existing prime-32003 packets.

At each prime the pole-0 and pole-1 terms are exactly constant under all three
ambient inclusions. Their row counts remain 7 and 11. The complete typed
support descriptors agree across primes; only coefficient residues differ.

This closes the finite two-prime compatibility prerequisite for the interior
seeds. It does not reconstruct rational coefficients, establish uniqueness of
the deterministic representative, define the top-three-degree boundary
operator, or construct a tau map.
