# Composition of singular Hermitian costalks

For

\[
f_\varepsilon=\operatorname{diag}(1,\varepsilon),
\qquad
g_\eta=\operatorname{diag}(\eta,1),
\]

the composite is \(g_\eta f_\varepsilon=\operatorname{diag}(\eta,\varepsilon)\).
Applied to \((a,b)^T\), its density packet is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
\eta^2a^2&\eta\varepsilon ab\\
\eta\varepsilon ab&\varepsilon^2b^2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The iterated kernel filtration gives exactly the same three labelled
bidegrees.  The mixed occurrence is essential, but no additional Tor or
extension class remains.
