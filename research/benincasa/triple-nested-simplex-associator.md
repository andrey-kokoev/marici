# Triple nested simplex conditioning

For three labelled rows, freeze

\[
\pi_{0k}=\varepsilon\delta\theta t_k,
\qquad
\pi_{1k}=\varepsilon\delta s_k,
\qquad
\pi_{2k}=\varepsilon r_k.
\]

Writing \(T=\sum t_k\), \(S=\sum s_k\), and \(R=\sum r_k\), the
three-stage conditional weight is

\[
\frac{\delta(S+\theta T)}{R+\delta(S+\theta T)}
\frac{\theta T}{S+\theta T}
\frac{t_k}{T}
=
\frac{\delta\theta t_k}{R+\delta(S+\theta T)}.
\]

This equals direct normalization.  The two parenthesizations agree because the
labelled normal transition is the ordinary product \(\delta\theta\).  The
deepest face retains the flag \((\delta,\theta,[t_k])\); no associator class is
left on chart overlaps.
