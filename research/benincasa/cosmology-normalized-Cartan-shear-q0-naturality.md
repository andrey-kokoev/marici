# Identity shear reduces naturality to equality of actions

For the normalized Cartan shear `M=I`, the marked action equation
`Q_top M = M Q_bottom` reduces to `Q_top=Q_bottom`. Connector naturality
similarly requires the two transport representations to agree under all eight
components and twelve edge squares.

No top or bottom marked `q0` action is serialized, and none of the required
connector components or naturality squares exists. A deliberate pair of unequal
`q0` matrices gives residual `diag(1,-1)`, so identity does not make the test
automatic.

The candidate is therefore compatible only under an unproved equality of
extension actions. Even if that equality held, identity contains no information
about the distinguished `dlog(X3/X2)` root. The next test is a source-artifact
census for a common marked `q0` action.
