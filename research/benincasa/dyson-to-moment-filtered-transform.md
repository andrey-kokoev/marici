# The adjoint Dyson transform maps bond order to moment degree

For the free-plus-cubic Hamiltonian derivation

\[
D=p\partial_q-q^2\partial_p,
\]

the Heisenberg/Dyson coefficients of an observable are

\[
O_n=\frac1{n!}D^nO.
\]

They obey

\[
(n+1)O_{n+1}=DO_n.
\]

Since (D) raises total polynomial degree by at most one, an initial degree
(D_0) observable has

\[
\deg O_n\le D_0+n.
\]

This is the source-derived filtered map from Dyson bond order to moment degree.
It does not by itself identify the two completed coefficient modules.
