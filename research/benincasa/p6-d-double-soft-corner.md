# The repeated `(u-1)^2` resultant is the double-site-soft corner

In the homogeneous chart

\[
X_1=1,\qquad X_2=\frac{u+v}{2}-1,\qquad X_3=\frac{u-v}{2},
\]

the repeated rational intersection \((u,v)=(1,1)\) is precisely

\[
X_2=X_3=0.
\]

With \(x=u-1\) and \(z=v-1\), exact translation gives

\[
4P_6=z^2+8x-2xz+21x^2-8x^2z+16x^3-4x^3z+4x^4,
\]

\[
D=-z^2-4x+2xz-5x^2+4x^2z.
\]

Both branches are smooth and have the same tangent \(x=0\), since their
initial forms are \(8x\) and \(-4x\). Their tangency explains the repeated
\((u-1)^2\) resultant factor. It is supported on the existing labelled
double-site-soft incidence and does not define another carrier divisor.
