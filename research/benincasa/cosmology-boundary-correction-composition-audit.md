# Direct and composite lower-edge boundary corrections agree exactly

The direct degree-12-to-16 correction was independently compared in the
degree-16 source-row basis with the composite of the translated degree-12-to-14
correction and the degree-14-to-16 correction.

For K pole 0, both coefficient dictionaries contain the same 40 source rows.
For K pole 1, both contain the same 66 rows. In both cases their coefficient
difference is identically zero, stronger than merely evaluating to a source
syzygy.

This verifies strict compositional coherence for the two lower-edge
representatives at prime 32003 and finite degrees 12, 14, and 16. It does not
cover the other 46 degree-12 boundary coordinates, a second prime, or an
unbounded source-natural operator.
