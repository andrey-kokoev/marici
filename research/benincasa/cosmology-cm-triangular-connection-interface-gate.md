# The reconstructed CM scalar is not a triangular matrix connection

The Cayley–Menger calculation reconstructs three rational scalar coefficient
vectors of length fifteen with denominator
\(s_1s_2s_3\Lambda_P\), and all validation residuals vanish. It also proves
horizontality of the cyclic transverse port only in the quadratic quotient.

The artifact does not serialize a labelled seven-element cohomology basis,
three \(7\times7\) connection matrices, a marked \(q_0\) vector, an embedding
of the `e6` subspace, or a source-normalized triangular gauge. Its curvature
receipt further states that the pointwise closure scalar is not a flat quotient
connection.

Consequently no \(q_0\)-to-`e6` shear can be extracted from the existing
\(3\times15\) scalar data. The required constructor begins by serializing a
source-labelled seven-element basis and reconstructing all three matrix
connections.
