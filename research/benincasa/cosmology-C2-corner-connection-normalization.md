# C2 and the corner coefficient do not fix the bivariate connection

Conjecture: `C2=-1/8` and equality of the E6 and `q_top` `dv` coefficients at
`u=0` remove the torsor's common one-form freedom.

The risky consequence is equality of the full bivariate connection forms. At
the regular point `(u,v)=(1,3)`, the E6 coefficients are `(-1/2,-1/2)` while
the `q_top` coefficients are `(0,-1)`. Their `(du,dv)` residual is
`(-1/2,1/2)`, falsifying the conjecture.

`C2` still normalizes the transition-difference class, and the corner equality
still fixes one tangential pullback. Neither controls the transverse
connection. Independently, adding the closed form `du` preserves every torsor
transition difference and pulls back to zero on `u=0`.

The next test factors the full diagonal residual and classifies pullback loci on
which it vanishes.
