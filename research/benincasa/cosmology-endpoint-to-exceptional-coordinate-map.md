# The three-point coordinate map is unique but unsourced

Let \(f(\xi)=(a\xi+b)/(c\xi+d)\) preserve the marked points
\(-1,0,1\). The conditions give \(b=0\), \(a=c+d\), and \(a=d-c\), hence
\(c=0\) and \(a=d\). Thus the unique projective candidate is

\[
r=\xi.
\]

This uniqueness does not source the map. The parameter \(\xi\) belongs to the
soft endpoint chain, with local endpoint coordinate \(t=\xi+1\); \(r\) belongs
to the weighted exceptional wall-intersection construction. Matching three
numerical marks supplies a presentation identification, not a morphism between
those source objects.

Neither compatibility with the four wall labels nor transport of the continued
cycle is proved. Therefore the normalized cospan cannot be promoted through
this coordinate coincidence.
