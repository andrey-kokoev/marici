# Cartan and Cosmological Endpoint Type Separation

## Question

Does the frozen source define an action of Strominger's Cartan endpoint algebra
on the cosmological endpoint two-torsion of Entry 3627?

## Source objects

The Cartan construction has objects

\[
H_l\cong\operatorname{Sym}^{2l}\mathbb C^2
\]

and labelled grade-changing maps

\[
J_{\hat n}:H_l\longrightarrow H_{l+1}.
\]

Here an endpoint is an extremal weight of a celestial harmonic
representation. The label \(\hat n\) is an axis vector.

The cosmological construction has the elliptic curve

\[
E_X:\quad
W^2=x^2t^4-(x^2+y^2-z^2)t^2+y^2
\]

and the marked divisor class

\[
\tau=
(p_\infty^+-p_0^+)-(p_\infty^--p_0^-)
\in\operatorname{Pic}^0(E_X)[2].
\]

Here an endpoint is one of the marked fiber points over \(t=0\) or
\(t=\infty\). Its labels are sheet and marked-fiber occurrence.

## Required map

A Cartan-module claim would require, at minimum, source-defined objects
\(T_l\) containing transported copies of \(\tau\) and maps

\[
H_1\otimes T_l\longrightarrow T_{l+1}
\]

compatible with the quadratic trace relation. No such \(T_l\), grade map, or
comparison functor appears in the frozen cosmological source.

The shared word “endpoint” does not construct this map. Neither does the
reuse of three coordinate symbols: the celestial coordinates are generators
of \(H_1\), while the cosmological \(x,y,z\) are energy parameters of the
elliptic family.

## Disposition

The characteristic-two module found in Entry 3630 is an admissible algebraic
possibility, not a source-derived cosmological structure. The proposed
cross-sector attachment is currently untyped.

This does not weaken either established result:

- the complex Cartan quadric still organizes Strominger's rational endpoint
  tower;
- the cosmological marked divisor still has order dividing two.

It only prohibits composing them without an independently derived comparison
map.

## Next admissible test

Do not search the Cartan algebra for an action on \(\tau\). Instead test the
operations native to the elliptic family—deck transport, marked-section
permutation, specialization, and physical relative pairing—on the integral
two-torsion class. A cross-sector Cartan action may be reconsidered only if one
of those source operations independently produces an \(H_1\)-labelled grade
tower.
