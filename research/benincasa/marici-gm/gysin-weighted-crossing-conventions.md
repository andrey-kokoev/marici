- center: `u=0`, `y=(u+v)/2-1=0`
- strict divisors: `D2=y-u^2`, `D3=y+u^2`
- Newton weights: `wt(u)=1`, `wt(y)=2`
- `u` chart: `u=e`, `y=e^2*t`, `v=2-e+2e^2*t`
- stack chart: `u=e*s`, `y=e^2`, `v=2-es+2e^2`, with `mu_2` action
- overlap: `t=s^-2`; sheared-frame transition `diag(1,1,s^-4,s^-2)`
- raw exceptional valuations:
  `[-1,inf,inf,inf; 3,-1,inf,inf; -1,-5,-1,-2; 1,-3,2,-1]`
- minimal normalized integral shear: `(0,0,4,2)`
- exceptional packet in both charts: `rank=4 kernel=0 L_m_kernel=0` for `1<=m<=10`
- in the `u` chart, strict transforms occur at `t=+1,-1`; each remains
  logarithmic and has first indicial kernel dimension two.
