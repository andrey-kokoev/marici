# Anomalous middle-phase hard suppression

## Source Wightman variation

For the Bunch--Davies mode

\[
h_q(\eta)\propto q^{-3/2}(1+iq\eta)e^{-iq\eta},
\]

a first Bogoliubov variation of the greater Wightman function contains

\[
\delta_\beta G_q^>(\eta_1,\eta_2)
\propto
\beta_q h_q^*(\eta_1)h_q^*(\eta_2).
\]

Multiplying by the unvaried internal line

\[
G_k^>(\eta_1,\eta_2)
=h_k(\eta_1)h_k^*(\eta_2)
\]

gives the phase

\[
\exp\left(
i(q-k)\eta_1+i(q+k)\eta_2
\right).
\]

The conjugate Bogoliubov character gives the conjugate phase and the same
hard-frequency grading.

## Hard-region frequency split

Let (q=Q\to\infty) with fixed external momentum (p) and angle
(\cos\theta).  Then

\[
k=|p-q|=Q-p\cos\theta+O(Q^{-1}),
\]

so

\[
q+k=2Q-p\cos\theta+O(Q^{-1})
\]

is hard, while

\[
q-k=p\cos\theta+O(Q^{-1})
\]

remains external-scale.

Thus only one of the two time variables carries a guaranteed hard
oscillation.  Two independent integrations by parts in (Q) are not
available generically.
