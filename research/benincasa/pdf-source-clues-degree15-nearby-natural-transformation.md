# PDF source clues for the degree-15 to nearby-cycle comparison

## Search result

The indexed PDFs do not contain a direct construction for the present degree-15 presentation. They do contain a coherent template for what the missing comparison map must be.

## Transfer candidate

Do not seek an arbitrary homomorphism from 6,040 presentation columns to the three nearby generators. Construct the comparison as the composite

1. specialization to the normal cone/boundary degeneration;
2. canonical identification on the shared cut/horocycle incidence object;
3. pushforward (constant term/Radon-type integration) to the nearby occurrence basis.

The candidate must satisfy four independently testable equations:

1. **grading:** degree-15 and nearby grading shifts agree;
2. **base change:** restriction to each of the three marked cuts commutes with specialization;
3. **transitivity:** direct triple-cut degeneration equals either iterated pair-cut degeneration;
4. **projection formula/orientation:** integral augmentation is preserved with the source Leray orientation.

These equations could determine the map on the 984 supported columns even if they do not determine a map on the entire 6,040-column complement.

## Claim boundary

The PDFs provide structural precedents, not the required map for this cosmology source. In particular, the spherical-variety asymptotics results do not prove that the present signed-minor family satisfies their hypotheses. A source-specific normal-cone correspondence and its action on the degree-15 column dictionary remain to be materialized.
