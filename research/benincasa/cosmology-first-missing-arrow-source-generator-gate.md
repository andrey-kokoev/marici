# First compositional reopening target

Problem: choose the first missing arrow for reopening the primitive-lift chain.

Bold conjecture: every surviving primitive route requires a source-labelled
generator with verified boundary and connecting image.

Rivals: develop downstream pairing first; treat the conditional relative
cocycle as a source; or infer generators from invariant-factor multiplicity.

Risky consequence: any existing composable bypass into the coefficient complex
refutes generator-first ordering.

Strongest falsification attempt: the Rees census, relative face cones, and
Cayley-Menger/blowup/wall routes were audited. The census discarded labelled
transformation vectors; the cones have a conditional unit column and closed
cocycle but no incoming total-chain generator; the remaining candidates supply
only a face leg, lack specialization, or lack a selected absolute lift.

Disposition: provisionally retained in the audited envelope. The generator is
the first dependency, not the only useful parallel construction and not proof
that Rees is globally unique. The next test asks whether the existing Rees
engine can serialize the transformations needed to construct it.
