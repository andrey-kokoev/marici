# The weighted relative pairing is not yet defined

The correct home is logarithmic residue/Leray-tube duality on the marked
complement. It pairs an oriented Leray tube with an orientation-twisted
logarithmic class through the normalized-wall residue.

A source semialgebraic contour family, weighted polynomial initial forms, and
three central-fiber complement primitives are available. The naive contraction
fails to descend, however, and no common relative pair identifies the contour
current with the logarithmic coefficient system. The weighted initial form
supplies neither a continued tube cycle nor a coefficient comparison map.

Therefore the pairing has no defined value. The next constructor is the
residue-compatible Gauss–Manin connection in the orientation-twisted conductor
frame. It must retain the distinction between central-wall intersections at
\(r=0\) and moving-wall intersections at \(r=1,-1\).
