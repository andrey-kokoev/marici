# Tomographic holonomy readout

For a complex rank-two holonomy \(H=(h_{ij})\), use four labelled rank-one
effects generated by

\[
|0\rangle,\quad |1\rangle,\quad |0\rangle+|1\rangle,\quad
|0\rangle+i|1\rangle.
\]

Their scalar evaluations are

\[
q_0=h_{00},\qquad q_1=h_{11},
\]

\[
q_x=h_{00}+h_{11}+h_{01}+h_{10},
\]

\[
q_y=h_{00}+h_{11}+i(h_{01}-h_{10}).
\]

Writing \(s=q_x-q_0-q_1\) and \(t=q_y-q_0-q_1\),

\[
2h_{01}=s-it,\qquad 2h_{10}=s+it.
\]

Thus four labelled scalar readouts reconstruct the complete based matrix.
The denominator two belongs to inversion of the measurement frame, not to a
new carrier incidence.
