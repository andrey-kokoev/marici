# Q-only shifts repair the first excluded shell modulo two primes

At ambient degree 12, the complete-word shift fails on the degree-5 shell
because transported tangent descriptors exceed the presentation. Keeping only
the exact rational q coefficients, shifting those q descriptors, and reducing
each resulting residual against a freshly generated physical `T+S_K` base
succeeds for all shell targets:

- six monomials for pole 0;
- six monomials for pole 1;
- primes 32003 and 32009;
- zero residuals in all 24 reductions.

This confirms that the descriptor-domain failure is repaired at finite-field
level by fresh base corrections rather than by enlarging the transported word.
It is not yet an exact rational shell theorem because the fresh tangent and K
coefficients were not reconstructed over the rationals.
