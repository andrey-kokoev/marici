# Second-Rees positivity as a cut completion

Consider the normalized perturbative state, through the required orders,

\[
|\Psi\rangle
=|0,0\rangle
+\varepsilon a|2,0\rangle
+\varepsilon b|1,1\rangle
+O(\varepsilon^2).
\]

For the first, observed mode,

\[
|\beta_1|^2=2|a|^2,
\qquad
n_2=2|a|^2+|b|^2.
\]

Therefore the second uncertainty/Rees grade is

\[
n_2-|\beta_1|^2=|b|^2\ge0.
\]

The same-mode pair amplitude supplies the pure Bogoliubov completion.  The
amplitude crossing from the observed mode into the second mode supplies the
statistical excess after that mode is forgotten.  It is literally a squared
cut channel in this finite model.

This types a sharper cosmological conjecture: the second-Rees positivity
completion of the one-loop Gaussian coefficient object should be generated by
the physical Cut pairing of first-order production amplitudes.  The finite
model establishes the mechanism, not the full cosmological identity.
