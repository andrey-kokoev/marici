# Source cubic momentum channel and the second-Rees covariance map

For a fixed observed momentum occurrence \(p\), the source-normalized cubic
production component has the form

\[
|\Psi_p\rangle
=|0\rangle
-i\int_{\mathcal D_\Lambda}d\Pi_{qk}\,
\bigl(H_{p;qk}-S_{p;qk}\bigr)
|1_p,1_q,1_k\rangle+O(g^2),
\]

with the momentum-conservation delta function included in \(d\Pi_{qk}\).

Tracing the two labelled internal occurrences gives

\[
x_1(p)=y_1(p)=0,
\]

and

\[
n_2(p)
=\int_{\mathcal D_\Lambda}d\Pi_{qk}\,
|H_{p;qk}-S_{p;qk}|^2.
\]

The anomalous first jet vanishes because \(a_pa_{-p}\) changes observed
particle number by two, whereas the vacuum and cubic production component
differ by three total quanta.  Distinct labelled \((q,k)\) channels are
orthogonal, so there is no cross-channel interference.  Bulk/surface endpoint
interference is retained within each channel through \(|H-S|^2\).

The ordered \((q,k)\), \((k,q)\) occurrence trace cancels the identical-pair
factor \(1/2!\) channelwise.  On the positive symmetric finite-EFT measure,

\[
n_2(p)-|\beta_1(p)|^2=n_2(p)\geq0.
\]

Saturation requires \(H_{p;qk}=S_{p;qk}\) on every admitted channel, modulo
measure-zero coincident-label strata handled by occurrence resolution.

The finite checker audits endpoint complex amplitudes and the occurrence
normalization.  It does not numerically integrate a model-specific continuum
kernel.
