# Matched holonomy and normalization

When signal and reference twists match, the comparison line has trivial
monodromy.  This supplies a rank-one space of flat sections, not a preferred
nonzero section: its generators form a \(\mathbb C^\times\)-torsor.

Under \(v\mapsto qv\), a fixed-coordinate functional changes the scalar
pairing.  A genuine dual cycle transforms as \(\gamma\mapsto q^{-1}\gamma\),
and evaluation is invariant.  Therefore matched holonomy becomes a canonical
number only when the source provides a normalized dual cycle, metric/basepoint
phase, or integral-polarized lattice.
