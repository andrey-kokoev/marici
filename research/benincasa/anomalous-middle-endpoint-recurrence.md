# Anomalous middle-placement endpoint recurrence

## Frozen object

This audit uses the source bulk--bulk ordered nested commutator already checked
against the four Schwinger--Keldysh branches.  Its vertex measure is
`dt1 dt2/(t1^2 t2^2)`.  Exactly one labelled internal occurrence is varied:

\[
\delta_{\beta_q}G_q^>(t_1,t_2)
=\delta_{\beta_q}G_q^<(t_1,t_2)
\propto
\beta_q(1-iqt_1)(1-iqt_2)e^{iq(t_1+t_2)}.
\]

The other internal occurrence remains the Bunch--Davies \(k\)-propagator.
No equal-frequency, equal-time, or equal-regulator specialization is made.

## Exact finite result

The product contains the two labelled frequency letters

\[
\omega_-=q-k,
\qquad
\omega_+=q+k.
\]

Applying the source endpoint-primitive recurrence through order ten and then
summing the complete nested-commutator sectors leaves lower-endpoint grades

\[
\eta_0^0,\quad \eta_0^1,\quad \eta_0^2
\]

and observation-endpoint grades \(0,1\).  Thus the sector sum does **not**
cancel all positive endpoint powers.  It does cancel every zero-frequency
logarithmic route in the generic noncollinear test.

On the hard ray

\[

\]

the coefficients of every surviving lower-endpoint grade scale as \(Q^1\)
before the radial measure and \(\beta_Q\) are included.  The surviving
observation-endpoint coefficients scale as \(Q^2\).

## Narrow interpretation

The earlier statement that the anomalous phase supplies one guaranteed hard
time primitive remains valid, but it cannot be strengthened to cancellation
of positive endpoint grades inside the bulk--bulk sector alone.  With the
radial \(Q^2\) measure and only the Hadamard condition
\(\beta_Q=o(Q^{-2})\), the lower endpoint remains superficially
\(o(Q^1)\).  Absolute convergence is not established.

The next admissible comparison is against the source boundary sectors and the
already frozen local counterterm response space.  A divergence claim before
that comparison would be premature.
