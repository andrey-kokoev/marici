# Exact transport syzygies are not stationary across adjacent cutoffs

For each A12 boundary coordinate, the A12-to-A14 complete-word transport
syzygy was moved into the A16 descriptor presentation and compared with the
matched A14-to-A16 syzygy.

- coordinates tested: 48;
- coefficient-identical stationary matches: 0.

Thus passing from literal correction words to literal source-syzygy words does
not produce a stationary adjacent recurrence. The direct syzygy still equals
the compositional sum of adjacent syzygies, but this is cocycle coherence, not
repetition of one cutoff-independent representative.

This falsifies representative-level stationarity in the evaluation kernel. A
recurrence modulo syzygies still requires a specified quotient and a faithful
coordinate on that quotient; evaluation to zero alone collapses every syzygy
and therefore cannot distinguish or generate later correction data.
