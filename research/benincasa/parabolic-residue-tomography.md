# Parabolic-residue tomography

Let \(x\) be the singular calibration normal and \(\delta\) the independent
measurement-degeneration normal. Entry 1757 gives the supported curvature
residue

\[
R_{\rm par}=\left.xK_x\right|_{x=0}.
\]

The ordinary degenerate measurement packet records both diagonal entries and
\(R_{01}+R_{10}\). Its first \(\delta\)-grade records

\[
i(R_{01}-R_{10}).
\]

These four labelled scalars reconstruct \(R_{\rm par}\) completely. No
higher measurement grade is needed.

The two normals must remain distinct through this calculation: \(x\) tracks
support/parabolic order, while \(\delta\) tracks readout rank.
