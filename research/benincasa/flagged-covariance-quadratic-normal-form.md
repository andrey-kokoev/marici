# Flagged covariance degeneration has a uniform quadratic normal form

Let a vanishing covariance block and its cross vector factor as

\[
K(t)=W(t)BW(t),
\qquad
v(t)=W(t)\gamma,
\]

where (W(t)) is diagonal with arbitrary positive integral flag weights and
(B) is an invertible correlated normal-block covariance. Then

\[
K(t)^{-1}=W(t)^{-1}B^{-1}W(t)^{-1}
\]

and the finite Schur correction is

\[
v(t)^TK(t)^{-1}v(t)=\gamma^TB^{-1}\gamma.
\]

Every weight cancels termwise. Thus a graded null block contributes one
quadratic form, not one independently meaningful scalar ratio per coordinate.
The latter description is valid only after diagonalizing (B).

The exact checker verifies 60 positive-definite correlated blocks through rank
six, 910 inverse identities, and 3,640 arbitrary flag-weight cancellations.
