# Physical one-mode Gaussian packets extend to density operators

For \([Q,P]=2i\), a centered one-mode Gaussian covariance is physical when

\[
V>0,\qquad \det V\ge1.
\]

Williamson form writes

\[
V=\nu SS^T,\qquad S\in Sp(2,\mathbb R),\qquad \nu=\sqrt{\det V}\ge1.
\]

The covariance \(\nu I\) is realized by a thermal density operator with
geometric ratio

\[
r=\frac{\nu-1}{\nu+1},
\]

and the metaplectic lift of \(S\) realizes \(V\). Thus the source Gaussian
subcone has a complete density-operator extension. Cardinality-weighted
covariance merge preserves the uncertainty cone by convexity. Cubic evolution
does not preserve Gaussianity.
