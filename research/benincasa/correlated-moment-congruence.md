# Correlated Cut positivity is a joint-moment congruence

Let (M_{XY}) be the multivariate truncated moment matrix of a correlated pair
and set (Z=\alpha X+\beta Y). The substitution matrix from powers of (z) to
bivariate monomials gives

\[
H_Z=T^TM_{XY}T.
\]

Thus positivity survives correlated linear pushforward whenever the complete
joint positive functional is retained.

Marginals do not suffice. Independent, perfectly correlated, and perfectly
anticorrelated Rademacher pairs have identical marginal moments at every order,
but their normalized sums have three different variances.

The exact checker verifies 486 congruence entries and 486 positive Gram entries
across 36 correlated finite-grade cases.
