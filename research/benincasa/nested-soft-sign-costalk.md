# Nested specialization of soft sign costalks

For nested nonzero support graphs `H2 subset H1`, every component of `H2` maps
to its unique containing component of `H1`.  The induced map on `H0` has:

- kernel: new component signs created by splitting inside a retained parent;
- cokernel: parent components removed entirely by the deeper softening.

For `H3 subset H2 subset H1`, the component maps compose strictly because
containment of connected components is transitive.  These are exactly the
maps in the standard relative homology sequence; no extra simultaneous-soft
coherence class occurs.
