# Every even-cutoff boundary target is covered by squared-axis transport

At ambient degree \(A\), the boundary exponents have total degrees

\[
A-6,\quad A-5,\quad A-4.
\]

For \((i,j)\) in this set, at least one of \(i\ge2\) or \(j\ge2\) holds for
every integer \(A\ge9\), hence every even \(A\ge10\). The bound is sharp: at \(A=8\), the exponent \((1,1)\) is uncovered. Subtracting two from such a coordinate produces an
exponent in the boundary set at ambient degree \(A-2\). Thus multiplication by
\(x^2\) and \(y^2\) covers every boundary target.

Per pole, the target and overlap counts are

\[
3A-12\quad\text{and}\quad 3A-24.
\]

The A12 base contains exact contractions for all 48 two-pole targets.
Arbitrary-exponent constructor naturality therefore transports at least one
exact contraction to every boundary target at each later even cutoff. This
proves boundary exactness for every even \(A\ge12\) by induction.

The result concerns the top-three-degree boundary family. It does not by itself
prove the corresponding arbitrary-degree theorem for interior targets or
construct an integral primitive \(\tau_p\) lift.
