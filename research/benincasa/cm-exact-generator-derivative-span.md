# Exact-generator derivatives supply the missing three fiber directions

## Question

The physical first-normal packet and cyclic quadratic class span rank four,
whereas the complete localized Cayley--Menger quotient has rank seven. Entry
2625 made the four source exact generators available with common primitive
provenance. Do their base derivatives account for the three missing fiber
directions?

## Frozen packet

The associated-grade packet is

\[
(\nu_1,\nu_2,\nu_3,c_{\rm cyc}).
\]

The exact ideal is generated by the three logarithmic gradient relations and
the localization relation

\[
(5K_a,5K_b,5K_c,zK-1).
\]

For each labelled base direction, the test differentiates these four source
generators before reduction. The twelve derivative classes are admitted in
the fixed order recorded by the result packet. No class is selected by its
effect on rank.

## Result

For every tested point and prime, the incremental rank sequence is

\[
4,5,6,7,7,7,7,7,7,7,7,7,7.
\]

Thus the derivatives of the first base-direction gradient triple supply
exactly three independent fiber directions. The remaining gradient and
localization derivatives add no further fiber rank. The result replicates at
A, B, and HOMA and at two primes.

## Interpretation

The rank-four packet is an associated-grade object, not the whole filtered
coefficient system. Motion of the exact ideal contributes a rank-three
coherence packet, and together they span the complete rank-seven fiber.

This is not yet a connection theorem. The derivative classes must be combined
with the primitive coefficients from Entry 2625 to construct common lifted
base derivatives. Mixed flatness remains the acceptance gate.
