# Graded transition growth

Problem: separate new-degree presentation growth from actual quotient-transition
cokernel dimensions.

Bold conjecture: labelled degree strata determine cokernel growth and a stable
renormalized tau-p coordinate.

Risky consequence: the data must determine transition kernels and cokernels
separately, not only their dimension difference.

Strongest falsification attempt: raw relation counts are 2,524, 3,876, and
5,536. Exact assembled dimensions are 12,960, 15,840, and 19,008; relation
ranks are 4,146, 5,888, and 7,924. Quotient dimensions are therefore 8,814,
9,952, and 11,084, with increments 1,138 and 1,132. Labelled family formulas
explain raw-row increments 1,352 and 1,660 exactly.

Disposition: revised. The quotient-dimension increments equal cokernel dimension
minus kernel dimension. They are not cokernel dimensions because global
monicity was never proved. Tau-p supports 69, 219, and 315 also do not define a
stable coordinate across changing complements. The next test must compute
relative transition kernels by reducing source quotient-complement vectors
against target relations.
