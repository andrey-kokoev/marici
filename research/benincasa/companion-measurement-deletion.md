# Companion-measurement deletion

At diagonal first order, let

\[
s=q_x(S),\qquad d=i(R_{01}-R_{10}).
\]

The complete packet observes

\[
(s,s+d),
\]

whose matrix is unimodular. Deleting the companion observation leaves only
\(s+d\). Its kernel is

\[
\mathbb Q\langle(1,-1)\rangle.
\]

Therefore the finite calibration contribution and supported residue jet form
a rank-one filtered extension after deletion. This class is intrinsic to the
declared deletion map, but no frozen cosmological source has yet been shown
to impose that deletion.
