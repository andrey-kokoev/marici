# The double-soft `P6`–`D` tacnode has no relative-sign class

At \((u,v)=(1,1)\), the \(P_6\) and \(D\) branches are both smooth and
have the same tangent. The exact resultant is

\[
\operatorname{Res}_v(4P_6,D)
=16u^4(u-1)^2(1-8u+12u^2-4u^3).
\]

The final factor equals one at \(u=1\), so the local intersection
multiplicity is exactly two. Two smooth plane branches with contact order two
form an \(A_3\) tacnode:

\[
Y^2-X^4=0.
\]

Its total-link Alexander polynomial is

\[
\Delta_{A_3}(t)=(t-1)(t^2+1).
\]

The relative Hom line has character \((-1,-1)\) on the ordered
\((P_6,D)\) meridians, hence is the pullback of the total character \(t=-1\).
But

\[
\Delta_{A_3}(-1)=-4\neq0.
\]

Therefore the characteristic-zero twisted local link cohomology vanishes.
The tacnode has an ordinary Milnor space of rank three, but the source
relative-sign character selects none of it.
