# No source-derived p-locus map to the Gysin variables

Conjecture: active artifacts assign all three Gysin variables as rational
functions of the p-locus coordinate.

The sourced radial chart gives
`X2/X1=(u+v)/2-1`, `X3/X1=(u-v)/2`, and the principal divisor `v=2u`.
Thus its specialized ratios are `3u/2-1` and `-u/2`. A bounded census found no
artifact containing both this p-base data and assignments for `s1,s2,s3`.
The coordinate-origin inventory also has no route passing its typing gates.

The conjecture is falsified under the active source envelope. The map
`Q[s1,s2,s3] -> Q(u)` and hence the Gysin-denominator pullback remain undefined.

The next test deliberately applies the tempting but unauthorized identification
`(s1,s2,s3)=(X1,X2,X3)` with `X1=1` and compares its support to the norm
residual. A mismatch rules out even this candidate formula.
