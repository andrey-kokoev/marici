# The cubic Dyson-Schmidt tower has a finite shift presentation

Writing

\[
U(z)=e^{zA},
\qquad A=q_1q_2q_3,
\]

the coefficients (c_n=z^n/n!) obey

\[
(n+1)c_{n+1}=zc_n.
\]

This one shift relation presents the entire infinite Dyson/Schmidt bond index.
Composition is binomial convolution, and threefold composition satisfies the
multinomial identity independently of parenthesization.

Each finite truncation (U_{le K}) defines the completely positive map

\[
\rho\mapsto U_{\le K}\rho U_{\le K}^{\dagger}.
\]

It is generally not trace-preserving. Partial trace preserves its positivity.
