# Antiunitary companion deletion

Let

\[
\Theta=ZK,
\]

where \(K\) is complex conjugation and \(Z=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)\).
On Hermitian Pauli directions,

\[
\Theta:(I,X,Y,Z)\longmapsto(I,-X,Y,Z).
\]

Thus the invariant sector is \(\langle I,Y,Z\rangle\). It retains diagonal
measurements and the imaginary-superposition direction, while the
real-superposition companion loses its \(X\) component:

\[
(I+X)+\Theta(I+X)=2I.
\]

This realizes exactly the labelled deletion used in Entry 1760. Whether the
frozen cosmological coefficient system carries this antiunitary real
structure remains open.
