# The p-locus residual requires a quadratic gauge

Conjecture: the residual on `p=0` is the logarithmic derivative of a rational
gauge over `Q(u)`.

Let `D=-4u^4+12u^3+u^2-12u+4`. Exact polynomial arithmetic gives
`residual=dlog(u(u-1)(u-2))-(1/2)dlog(D)`. The quartic `D` has no rational root
or rational quadratic factor, hence is irreducible over `Q`. A rational gauge
would require valuation `-1/2` along `D=0`, contradicting integrality of
rational-function valuations.

The conjecture is falsified. The residual is removed only after adjoining
`sqrt(D)`, by the algebraic gauge `u(u-1)(u-2)/sqrt(D)`. No source authority for
that quadratic extension is established.

The next test compares `D` with every declared source two-cover radicand, up to
rational squares and typed base maps.
