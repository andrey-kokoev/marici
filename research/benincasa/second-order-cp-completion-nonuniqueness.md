# Second-order Cut data does not select a global CP completion

For (H^2=I), the truncated Dyson amplitude is

\[
A_2(g)=\left(1-\frac{g^2}{2}\right)I-igH.
\]

Two exact unitary completions are:

\[
U_{\exp}(g)=e^{-igH},
\]

and the polar completion

\[
U_{\rm pol}(g)
=
\frac{A_2(g)}{\sqrt{1+g^4/4}},
\]

because

\[
A_2^\dagger A_2=\left(1+\frac{g^4}{4}\right)I.
\]

Both are exactly unitary and induce CPTP channels.  Both agree with the real-plus-virtual Dyson/Cut packet through order (g^2).  They differ at order (g^3): the exponential has the (iH g^3/6) term, while the polar completion has no cubic term.

Therefore a second-order filtered packet does not determine its global CP completion.  Exponential evolution becomes canonical only after adjoining the stronger source datum that (g) parametrizes a one-parameter group with fixed generator (H).  That datum is not contained in the second-order Cut jet alone.
