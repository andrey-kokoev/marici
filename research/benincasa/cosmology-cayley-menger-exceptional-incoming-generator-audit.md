# Cayley–Menger and exceptional cells do not supply the incoming generator

The blow-up exceptional simplex is sourced but has column \((0,1)\): it
provides only the ordered Čech face leg and lacks the \(\Xi_{\log}\) unit leg.

The Cayley–Menger construction supplies an oriented fiber-cycle family with
identity local monodromy at the tested punctures. It does not yet supply its
weighted-Rees specialization, exceptional coefficient map, or comparison with
the logarithmic class.

The physical-wall mapping cone is closed with zero pairwise residue
differential, but no absolute lift is selected.

None of these objects is therefore the missing incoming total-complex
generator. The remaining candidate construction is a weighted exceptional
specialization of the sourced Cayley–Menger incidence family, followed by a
typed comparison to both \(\Xi_{\log}\) and the blow-up face.
