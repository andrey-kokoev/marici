# Weighted strict transform at vanishing Q variance

Positive semidefiniteness near `A=0` forces `C=O(sqrt(A))`.  Set

\[
A=\varepsilon^2,
\qquad
C=\varepsilon c.
\]

Entry 1710's generator becomes

\[
K=t\varepsilon^2y-\frac12\log(1+2\varepsilon^2ty)
+\frac12(B-c^2)y^2
+\frac{\varepsilon^2x^2+2\varepsilon cxy+c^2y^2}
       {2(1+2\varepsilon^2ty)}.
\]

Its first grades are

\[
K=\frac B2y^2+\varepsilon cxy
+\varepsilon^2\left(\frac{x^2}{2}-c^2ty^3\right)
+O(\varepsilon^3).
\]

The apparent `C/A` pole has disappeared.  The first exceptional coefficient is
linear in the resolved normal `c`; the second uses only its symmetric square.
