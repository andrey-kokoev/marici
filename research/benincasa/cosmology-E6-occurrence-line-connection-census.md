# The e6/e7/e8 occurrence conjecture is falsified

Conjecture: basis entries `e6,e7,e8` in the 9-by-9 bivariate connection are the
three cyclic E6 chart occurrences.

Falsifier: the source must declare an occurrence-to-basis map and compatible
chart-coordinate pullbacks. The artifact has one literal `e6` entry and no
chart or occurrence mapping fields. The cyclic target instead says “three e6
chart lines.” Relabelling `e7,e8` as further e6 occurrences is therefore
unauthorized.

Only `connection_v[5][5]` at `u=0` supplies one restricted coefficient; three
typed restrictions are required. Horizontality is not testable.

Next conjecture: separate chart-specific artifacts contain the three e6 line
connections. It is falsified unless a source-envelope search finds three
explicit chart identifiers, e6 basis lines, and pullbacks to one common base.
