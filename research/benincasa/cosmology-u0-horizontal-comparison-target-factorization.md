# The principal p target does not factor through u=0

Conjecture: the principal three-wall target factors through the unique
horizontal pullback `u=0`.

Since `u=E/X1`, this is the total-energy divisor `E=0`. The target divisor is
`p=x+y+3z=0`. At `(x,y,z)=(2,3,-5)`, `E=0` but `p=-10`, so the divisors are
distinct. The existing five-mark audit likewise types its `E=0` partial
fraction as a total-energy bridge and records no `p`-normal coefficient map,
twisted de Rham chain map, or `tau_p` column.

The conjecture is falsified. The `u=0` result remains a tangential E6/`q_top`
connection comparison; transporting it into the `p`-normal target would violate
source typing.

and pulls back the exact connection residual there.
