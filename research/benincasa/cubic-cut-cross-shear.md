# Cubic evolution versus cardinality-weighted Cut merge

For two equal blocks define the collective canonical variables

\[
Q=\frac{Q_1+Q_2}{\sqrt2},
\qquad
P=\frac{P_1+P_2}{\sqrt2}.
\]

Collective cubic evolution sends

\[
P\mapsto P-tQ^2.
\]

To match its pure-square coefficients, evolve each block for
`tau=t/sqrt(2)` and then merge.  The projected local route contributes

\[
-\frac t2(Q_1^2+Q_2^2),
\]

whereas the collective route contributes

\[
-\frac t2(Q_1^2+2Q_1Q_2+Q_2^2).
\]

Their exact defect is

\[
\Theta_P=-tQ_1Q_2.
\]

For independent centered Gaussian blocks its mean vanishes and

\[
\langle\Theta_P^2\rangle=t^2a_1a_2.
\]

The defect is therefore a second-order mixed joint moment on the existing two
labelled occurrences.  It needs the mixed coefficient sector of Entry 1677,
but no new carrier incidence.
