# Normalized boundary correction words do not transport literally

The retained lower-edge `T/S_K` correction words were independently decoded
at the degree-12-to-14 and degree-14-to-16 inclusions.

For K pole 0, each word has 30 terms, but only 20 normalized descriptors are
shared. Ten occur only in each inclusion, and all 20 common coefficients
differ. For K pole 1, each word has 50 terms, with 34 common descriptors,
16 unique to each inclusion, and zero matching common coefficients.

Thus invariant row counts and provenance sizes do not define a literal
source-descriptor correction operator. This falsifies coefficient-identical
word transport. It does not yet test whether the translated word difference
is a source syzygy; such a higher coherence cell is the remaining route.
