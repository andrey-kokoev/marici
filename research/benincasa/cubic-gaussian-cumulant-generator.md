# Complete cumulant generator of the cubic Gaussian orbit

For an initially diagonal centered Gaussian with variances `a,b`, the exact
cubic shear gives

\[
\Pi=P-t(Q^2-a).
\]

The Weyl-ordered moment-generating germ is

\[
M(x,y)
=\left\langle e^{xQ+y\Pi}\right\rangle_{m Weyl}
=e^{by^2/2+tay}
(1+2aty)^{-1/2}
\exp\!\left(\frac{ax^2}{2(1+2aty)}\right).
\]

Therefore the cumulant generator is

\[
K(x,y)=\frac b2y^2+tay-\frac12\log(1+2aty)
+\frac{ax^2}{2(1+2aty)}.
\]

Its nonzero connected coefficients include

\[
\kappa_{Q Q\Pi^r}=a(-2at)^r r!,
\]

and, for `r>=3`,

\[
\kappa_{\Pi^r}
=(-1)^r2^{r-1}(r-1)!a^rt^r.
\]

No connected cumulant contains more than two `Q` slots.  The apparent divisor
`1+2aty=0` lies in the auxiliary moment-generating variable, not in the
physical kinematic carrier.
