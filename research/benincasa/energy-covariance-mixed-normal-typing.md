# The mixed energy-covariance normal comparison is not yet typed

The total-energy/Rees normal (E_T) belongs to the integrated cosmological
period family. The covariance normal forms of Entries 1669--1673 belong to the
Gaussian process coefficient model with canonical variables and internal Cut
pushforward.

A source audit found no frozen family, differential, Gysin map, localization
map, or relative chain whose domain contains both (E_T) and the process
covariance matrix (Sigma). Apparent text matches were unrelated uses:

- Gaussian elimination in a metric Schur-complement calculation;
- cyclic or master covariance meaning equivariance, not a stochastic
  covariance matrix.

Therefore neither a strict external tensor rule nor a mixed
energy--covariance normal term is presently defined. Constructing one by
forming a formal product would transport analogy as authority.

The two flag calculi remain compatible candidates, but their comparison is an
open typing problem.
