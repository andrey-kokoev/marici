# Singular reference frames and parabolic matrix readout

For

\[
F_\varepsilon=\operatorname{diag}(1,\varepsilon),
\qquad H=(h_{ij}),
\]

the framed readout is

\[
F_\varepsilon^{-1}HF_\varepsilon
=\begin{pmatrix}
h_{11}&\varepsilon h_{12}\\
\varepsilon^{-1}h_{21}&h_{22}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The source valuations \((0,1)\) force Hom weights \((0,1,-1,0)\).  After
retaining these parabolic grades, all four entries of \(H\) are recovered;
ordinary specialization alone loses or diverges on the off-diagonal entries.
