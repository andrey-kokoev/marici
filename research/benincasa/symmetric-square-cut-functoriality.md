# Cut functoriality of the symmetric-square Rees grade

Let `u` be the resolved covariance-normal vector and `Xi=u u^T`.  A labelled
linear Cut map `T` transports the normal vector to `Tu`.  Therefore

\[
\operatorname{Sym}^2(Tu)=T\Xi T^T.
\]

For nested Cut maps `T` and `S`,

\[
S(T\Xi T^T)S^T=(ST)\Xi(ST)^T.
\]

Rank one, positive semidefiniteness, and deck evenness are all preserved.  This
is an internal covariance-sector comparison and does not identify the
conditioning cover with the cosmological energy-root cover.
