# Born normalization and interferometric calibration

Take

\[
|\psi_\theta\rangle
=\frac{|0\rangle+e^{i\theta}|1\rangle}{\sqrt2},
\qquad
|m_\phi\rangle
=\frac{|0\rangle+e^{i\phi}|1\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
\]

The source-specified rank-one measurement gives

\[
|\langle m_\phi|\psi_\theta\rangle|^2
=\frac{1+\cos(\theta-\phi)}2.
\]

Normalization removes positive rescaling, and independent global phases of
the state and measurement cancel.  The surviving datum is the calibrated
relative phase \(\theta-\phi\).  Thus Born normalization removes the radial
part of the generator torsor; the measurement operator supplies the remaining
relational phase convention.
