# Unequal-block cubic Cut pair coherence

For occurrence blocks of sizes `m_i`, let

\[
M=\sum_i m_i,
\qquad
w_i=\sqrt{m_i/M},
\qquad
Q=\sum_iw_iQ_i.
\]

Match the pure-square terms by evolving block `i` for time `t w_i`.  The
collective-minus-local momentum shear is then

\[
\Theta_P=-2t\sum_{i<j}w_iw_jQ_iQ_j.
\]

For three blocks, either binary merge tree produces exactly the same three
labelled pair coefficients.  For independent centered Gaussian blocks,

\[
\operatorname{Var}(\Theta_P)
=4t^2\sum_{i<j}\frac{m_im_j}{M^2}a_ia_j.
\]

Thus the operator cocycle and its first physical moment are both assembled by
the existing labelled pair species.
