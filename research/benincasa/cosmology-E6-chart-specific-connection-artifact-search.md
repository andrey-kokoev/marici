# The three-restriction conjecture is falsified; a torsor survives

Conjecture: the source envelope contains three chart-specific E6 line
connection restrictions with pullbacks to one base.

The strongest candidate is `cyclic_leray_transition_torsor.json`. It supplies
three occurrence frames `G12,G23,G31`, three transition functions in common
projective coordinates, a closed cocycle, and one explicit homogeneous
logarithmic edge. It supplies no individual E6 connection restriction for any
of the three occurrences. Two radial charts and one exceptional-line chart do
not complete that data.

Thus the full conjecture is falsified. The revised survivor is a three-frame
occurrence torsor with one explicit logarithmic edge. This may type occurrence
descent, but it does not yet prove horizontal A2-to-E6 comparison or selection
of the logarithmic class as the rank-twelve extension shear.
