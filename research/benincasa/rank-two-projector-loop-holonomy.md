# Rank-two projector-loop holonomy

Let \(P_n\) project onto the plane orthogonal to \(n\in S^2\).  Transport the
plane around the geodesic octant triangle

\[
e_1\to e_2\to e_3\to e_1
\]

using the three minimal quarter-turn rotations.  Their ordered product fixes
\(e_1\) and acts on the endpoint plane \(\langle e_2,e_3\rangle\) by

\[
\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The endpoint projector and its principal angles return identically, but the
rank-two fiber has nontrivial holonomy.  The ordered projector path, not its
endpoint invariant packet, is required to reconstruct transport.
