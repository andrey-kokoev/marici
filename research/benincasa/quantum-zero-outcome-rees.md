# Zero-probability quantum conditioning

Consider the unnormalized pure state

\[
|\Psi_\varepsilon\rangle
=|00\rangle
+\varepsilon\bigl(u|01\rangle+v|11\rangle\bigr).
\]

Conditioning on the second system's outcome \(|1\rangle\) produces the
unnormalized first-system block

\[
\varepsilon^2
\begin{pmatrix}
u^2&uv\\uv&v^2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The outcome probability is \(O(\varepsilon^2)\), while the resolved amplitude
is \(O(\varepsilon)\).  Ordinary specialization loses the conditional state;
the second density-matrix normal grade retains

\[
|\chi\rangle\langle\chi|,
\qquad |\chi\rangle=(u,v)^T.
\]

Thus the exceptional datum is the projective amplitude \([u:v]\), equivalently
a rank-one positive-semidefinite ray.  This is a coefficient Rees direction
over the existing measurement/outcome incidence, not a new Cut stratum.
