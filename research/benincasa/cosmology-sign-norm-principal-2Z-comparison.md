the principal `2Z` obstruction.

punctured `p` divisor. Its evenness comes from norming a sign-twisted line. The
principal object is the integral `Xi_log` coefficient lattice in the `tau_p`

the weighted Gysin block and then to `(Xi_log,-sigma123)`. The weighted
specialization, projection, and integral readout are all absent. Equal parity
therefore does not identify the objects, falsifying the conjecture.
