# The fiber congruence erases deletion provenance

The OR quotient sends 64 six-axis masks to eight occurrence masks. It collapses
56 excess objects and identifies 468 unordered pairs of distinct labelled
masks. Seven of its eight fibers have nonconstant source labels.

In each axis fiber, singleton-only, pair-only, and both-deleted states have the
same occurrence image. For example, deleting `q_g3`, deleting `q_G12`, and
deleting both all map to the `G12:e6`-active state. Their immutable source masks
remain distinct.

Therefore the labelled-mask functor does not factor through the quotient. An
unlabelled absorption descent exists, but a provenance-preserving descent does
not. The connector cannot be authorized by this quotient. A faithful repair
must retain two colours—singleton complement and same pair—before any
forgetful projection to occurrence axes.
