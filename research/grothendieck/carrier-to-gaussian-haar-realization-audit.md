# Carrier-to-Gaussian/Haar realization audit

## Question

Does the coefficient-neutral Carrier select the analytic module on which intrinsic classes become integer labels, Gaussian heat eigenvalues, positive-real dilations, and additive/multiplicative Haar sectors?

## Claim boundary

The conditional initial-semiring theorem supplies a canonical abstract class for each finite component word and multiplication transport by every class. It does not supply an inner product, ordered completion, continuous scale group, measure, or functional calculus.

The approved Gaussian/Haar packets begin after adding all of the following:

- the basis `e_m` indexed by positive-integer labels;
- a positive operator `Q e_m=m^2 e_m`;
- Gaussian atoms `exp(-pi m^2 exp(2v))`;
- the positive-real dilation group;
- additive Haar measure `dx` and multiplicative Haar measure `dx/x`.

Within that sector, prime-power sampling, logarithmic degree, relative Haar half-density, and the critical seam are exact. No inspected packet constructs these analytic objects functorially from occurrence-resolved Carrier geometry.

## Exact nonuniqueness witness

For every positive parameter `a`, the same intrinsic label module admits

`Q_a e_m = m^(2a) e_m`.

Multiplication transport satisfies

`Q_a T_p = p^(2a) T_p Q_a`,

and functional calculus gives degree `(1/2) log Q_a = a log m`. Each choice preserves the initial semiring action, positivity, diagonalizability, and multiplicative transport, while changing all prime grades by the factor `a`. Rescaling the continuous coordinate compensates inside the Gaussian orbit, so the abstract Carrier action cannot detect `a`.

Likewise, the relative-Haar argument forces a half-density only after the positive-real dilation object and its two Haar sectors are fixed. It does not construct that object from the Carrier.

## Source-identity consequence

The map from abstract component classes to the analytic basis is not enough. A valid realization must preserve the source generator, multiplication transports, topology, Gaussian scale, both measures, and their normalization. Equal label sets or spectra do not construct this comparison.

Cross-sector readout packets reinforce the obstruction: formal repetition is natural on quotient labels but fails physical-selection compatibility in an admitted cosmology fibre. Therefore no common analytic realization may be inferred from the shared diagram shape.

## Disposition

No coefficient-neutral Carrier-to-Gaussian/Haar realization functor is constructed or unique. The first unavailable datum is a source-derived analytic realization with a normalization eliminating the `Q_a` family. Without it, the completed-zeta spline remains an exact theorem inside a chosen arithmetic analytic lens, not arithmetic forced by the shared Carrier.
