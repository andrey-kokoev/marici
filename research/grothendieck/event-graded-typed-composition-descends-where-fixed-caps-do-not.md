# Event-graded typed composition descends where fixed caps do not

## Result

The admitted polarity supplies the left creation needed for balancing. With fixed segment capacities, however, the prescribed rejoining fails the balancing equation because of overflow truncation. This is an exact obstruction, already at capacities (1,1).

Retaining the source's existing event-length grading repairs the type of the comparison. The resulting capacity-raising actions define a typed two-term bar complex for the free marked source. Rejoining annihilates its boundary and therefore gives a genuine map from the derived balanced tensor product. Its signed mate lands in the annihilator of the boundary relations.

The derived composition map need not be an equivalence. The finite fixture has nonzero negative homology and a large degree-zero kernel. No information-preserving descent or nondegenerate quotient metric is inferred.

## 1. The left action is admitted, not fitted

The tail-polarity comparison takes right creation on one orientation to left creation on the reflected orientation, with the conjugated source label and reversed order. Choose a common spectral orientation: its ordinary right creation is already available, and its left creation is transported from the opposite orientation by that comparison.

After correctly transporting scalar conjugation, these are the right and left multiplication actions of the same feature letter. No contraction or inverse-event action is substituted. The two sides of a typed boundary reverse the boundary endpoint in precisely the way required of a right source module and a left source module.

The discussion uses the fixed prepared source lines. Their reassembly frames remove only the common unitary source transport from the coefficient formulas; they do not turn a forgotten arithmetic arrow into an identity in the original source category.

## 2. Fixed-capacity balancing fails

Let mu_(a,b):F_a tensor F_b->F_(a+b) concatenate records. If the right and left actions are truncated to remain within F_a and F_b, take a=b=1, u=g and v=vacuum, for nonzero g.

The right action on u is zero by capacity truncation, whereas the left action on v is g. Consequently

mu_(1,1)(R_g u tensor v)=0,

mu_(1,1)(u tensor L_g v)=g tensor g != 0.

The latter tensor is nonzero over the scalar field. Thus this prescribed map cannot descend through the balanced tensor product of those fixed-capacity modules. A derived replacement does not remove failure of the underlying degree-zero balancing relation.

## 3. Keep event length as a grade

The source already distinguishes event length from the number of retained letters. Put

Rcal_n=F_n=direct_sum_(r=0)^n W^(tensor r),

Rcal=direct_sum_(n>=0) Rcal_n.

The same record word in different Rcal_n components is distinguished by its event length. Multiplication is the existing untruncated concatenation F_a tensor F_b->F_(a+b).

Equivalently, Rcal is the Rees algebra of tensor-word length. It has a central bookkeeping generator t of event degree one, representing a forgotten event, and feature generators x(g) of event degree one. Its homogeneous basis is t^(n-r) times a word of r feature generators. The parameter t is not physical time and is not set to one inside the graded construction.

The source actions become

forgotten event: u -> u t, and v -> t v;

retained event: u -> u x(g), and v -> x(g) v.

Each goes from event grade a to a+1 instead of overflowing a fixed cap. Associativity now gives

mu_(a+1,b)(u x(g) tensor v)
 =mu_(a,b+1)(u tensor x(g) v),

and the same equation for t. Both sides lie in F_(a+b+1).

This uses no new fitted feature. It restores the event-count metadata already present in a complete marked source path. A fixed chart is one capacity allocation inside this graded object, not a module closed under all capacity-raising actions.

## 4. Typed derived composition

Let S be the free marked quiver algebra, graded by source event length, and B its vertex-idempotent algebra. Use one copy of Rcal at each boundary vertex as the right module M and left module N, with actions above and the actual typed endpoint transitions. If the opposite carrier is written in conjugate coordinates, perform the previously specified polarity identification before writing these common-orientation module equations.

For a free quiver algebra, the standard length-one bimodule resolution gives the following two-term complex computing M tensor_S^L N:

C^(-1)=direct_sum_(e:x->y) M_x tensor N_y,

C^0=direct_sum_x M_x tensor N_x,

partial_e(u tensor v)=(u e) tensor v-u tensor(e v).

Each edge term includes the internal degree one of e. Thus the total event-degree n component of C^(-1) has inputs of grades a,b with a+b=n-1, while C^0 uses a+b=n. Every fixed n component is finite-dimensional at a finite feature and source cutoff.

This resolution applies to the free marked source algebra. It is not automatically a resolution for a quotient with extra route relations, nor for the Rees algebra with its centrality relations. Those require their own resolutions.

Define mu:C^0->Rcal by multiplication of record coefficients, summing over boundary-vertex tags. The admitted actions give

mu partial=0.

Therefore (0,mu) is a chain map

M tensor_S^L N -> Rcal

in the derived category, and degreewise it is an actual finite-complex map. This is the typed composition-correspondence descent. It holds on arbitrary homogeneous memory inputs in this graded carrier, not just on prepared-vacuum columns.

A marked source path cut at adjacent positions represents two degree-zero tensors whose difference is a boundary associated with the crossed actual event. Hence all rejoinings of that path have the same derived degree-zero composition class. The intermediate labels are present in C^0 before descent; they are not declared equal in the source.

## 5. Signed mate and its correct target

At each fixed event grade n, use the prescribed feature-record degree weights and the nondegenerate sheet-reduced signature on W. The bookkeeping t does not contribute an extra feature-degree weight. Give C^0 the direct sum of the product pairings on its vertex and capacity allocations.

The unique mate mu^sharp sends an output word to its sum of permitted splittings over all capacity allocations and admitted vertex labels. Its coefficients are the same split-and-copy coefficients as before, now assembled over the entire event-graded correspondence.

Taking the conjugate dual of mu partial=0 shows

partial^vee beta_(C^0) mu^sharp=0.

Thus the mate lands in the annihilator of the boundary relations. This is the correct dual descended observation. It does not assert that the signed form on C^0 descends as a nondegenerate form on its quotient, or that mu^sharp is an inverse.

In particular no metric on C^(-1) has to be invented to make this statement. Its canonical conjugate dual suffices. The dual complex has the previously established cochain sign on its differential.

## 6. Regular coefficient comparison and its limitation

If both modules are the full regular Rees algebra and the interface algebra is that same Rees algebra, multiplication gives

Rcal tensor_Rcal^L Rcal equivalent to Rcal.

This follows from freeness of the regular modules. It explains the simple coefficient-level composition model but must not be substituted for the actual typed source tensor above.

In the Clark envelope, admitted arithmetic features span W0, whereas W=W0+J W0 may include additional dual-observation vectors. Moving every such extra vector across the interface would require a larger interface action than that generated by the arithmetic events. Likewise the source algebra keeps vertices and has its own representation kernel. The regular coefficient equivalence therefore does not establish equivalence of the typed correspondence.

## 7. Exact finite result

The checker uses the four diamond vertices, their eight marked arrows, two independent feature coordinates, and event grade two. The typed bar complex has dimensions

48 in degree -1, 92 in degree 0, and target dimension 7.

The exact boundary rank is 45, giving

H^(-1) dimension 3,

H^0 dimension 47.

Rejoining is surjective onto the seven-dimensional coefficient target, but is not a quasi-isomorphism. This is consistent with deliberate information loss at composition. The signed mate annihilates all bar boundaries exactly.

The separate regular-Rees calculation verifies the ordinary balanced quotient in degree two. Its generator-relations matrix is used only to compute H^0; it is not falsely declared a two-term derived resolution of the Rees algebra.

The follow-up `prior-path-kernel-results-identify-the-three-and-forty-seven.md` identifies these dimensions more precisely. The ambient-memory dimensions above remain correct for their declared fixture, but are not intrinsic arithmetic diamond invariants.
