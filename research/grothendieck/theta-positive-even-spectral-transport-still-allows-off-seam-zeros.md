# Theta positive even spectral transport still allows off-seam zeros

## Bounded question

Does moving before scalar compression to a positive labelled source module,
with a self-adjoint spectral generator and reciprocal symmetry, supply the
missing zero confinement?

## Source-level spectral dynamics

Let (Q) be multiplication by the source coordinate on a positive source
space, and let (Omega) be its cyclic source vector. The bilateral Laplace
readout has the matrix-coefficient form

\[
F(z)=\langle\Omega,e^{zQ}\Omega\rangle.
\]

Here (Q) is self-adjoint. If the source measure is even, reflection (R)
satisfies

\[
RQR=-Q,
\qquad
R\Omega=\Omega,
\]

and therefore

\[
F(-z)=F(z).
\]

This is a genuine spectral transport law defined before scalar compression.

## Smallest hostile positive source

Take the even four-atom probability geometry supported at

\[
\{-2,-1,1,2\}
\]

with positive relative weight (c) on the outer pair. Ignoring an irrelevant
positive normalization, its readout is

\[
F_c(z)=\cosh z+c\cosh 2z,
\qquad
c>0.
\]

Set (x=\cosh z). Then

\[
F_c(z)=2cx^2+x-c.
\]

The two roots in the quotient coordinate are

\[
x_\pm=\frac{-1\pm\sqrt{1+8c^2}}{4c}.
\]

For (c=1/10),

\[
x_-=-\frac{5+3\sqrt3}{2}<-1.
\]

Hence

\[
z=\operatorname{arcosh}(-x_-)+i\pi
\]

is a zero with nonzero real part. Reflection and conjugation generate the
expected off-seam packet.

## What survives the hostile test

This source has all of the proposed precompression features:

- a positive labelled measure;
- a finite-dimensional source Hilbert space;
- a self-adjoint multiplication generator;
- an even cyclic source vector;
- an exact reciprocal involution;
- an entire matrix-coefficient readout.

Nevertheless its scalar readout has off-seam zeros. The cancellation is not a
failure of source positivity or spectral unitarity on the imaginary axis. It
is ordinary destructive interference between distinct positive source shells
after complex transport.

## Result

Passing to the labelled source module is necessary for provenance but is not
yet sufficient for RH. Positivity, self-adjoint spectral generation, and
reciprocal evenness do not confine zeros to the seam.

The missing law must constrain the relative weights and spacings of source
shells under transport. In theta language, it must use more than the abstract
pair ((Q,\Omega)): prime-scale recursion, Gaussian heat structure, Poisson
sewing, or an equivalent source-specific coherence must exclude the hostile
two-shell geometry.

## Sharp falsifier

Any proposed theorem using only positivity of the source, self-adjointness of
(Q), cyclicity of (Omega), and the involution (Q\mapsto-Q) accepts
(F_{1/10}). It therefore cannot imply critical-line confinement.
