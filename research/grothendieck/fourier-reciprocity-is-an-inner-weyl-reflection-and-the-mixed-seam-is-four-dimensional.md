# Fourier Reciprocity Is an Inner Weyl Reflection and the Mixed Seam Is Four-Dimensional

## Fourier does not exchange commuting factors

For one theta oscillator, Fourier conjugation acts on the Weyl generators by

\[
Q\longmapsto P,
\qquad
P\longmapsto-Q.
\]

Consequently its action on the metaplectic triple is

\[
E\longmapsto-F,
\qquad
F\longmapsto-E,
\qquad
H\longmapsto-H.
\]

This is the Weyl reflection inside one `sl2`. It is not the exchange of two
commuting `sl2` factors found on the two-spinor endpoint.

Therefore two operations must remain distinct:

- Fourier reciprocity reverses raising and lowering inside a factor;
- sector or spinor exchange swaps two factors.

Identifying them erases one coherence level.

## Doubled quadratic closure

Suppose the two theta sectors admit independent canonical pairs

\[
(Q_+,P_+),\qquad(Q_-,P_-).
\]

The two local metaplectic triples span

\[
\mathfrak{sl}_2^{(+)}\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_2^{(-)}
\]

of dimension six. The full quadratic Weyl algebra also contains four mixed
generators, represented by

\[
Q_+Q_-,\qquad P_+P_-,\qquad Q_+P_-,\qquad Q_-P_+.
\]

Together the ten quadratic generators close as \(\mathfrak{sp}_4\). Hence

\[
\dim\mathfrak{sp}_4-
\dim(\mathfrak{sl}_2\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_2)=4.
\]

The four mixed controls are not another arbitrary tower. They are the exact
complement required to let the two local oscillator systems interact.

## Interpretation of the fourth wall

The earlier `3+3` system described the two local control algebras. A seam
that only swaps them gives a semidirect symmetry but no mixed dynamics. The
four-dimensional complement records comparisons that use one coordinate or
momentum from each sector.

This supplies a precise candidate for the missing wall:

```text
three plus controls
+ three minus controls
+ four mixed seam controls
= the ten-dimensional sp4 quadratic closure.
```

It also explains why a single scalar seam port repeatedly proved
insufficient: scalar or rank-one boundary data cannot represent the complete
mixed quadratic module.

## Source-authority gate

The algebraic closure is unconditional once two independent canonical pairs
exist. The theta conclusion is conditional because the reciprocal sectors may
be two polarizations of one phase space rather than two independent phase
spaces.

The next source test must decide whether completed theta sewing supplies:

1. two independent canonical pairs;
2. only one pair with two polarizations; or
3. a relative correspondence whose mixed operators exist only as boundary
   distributions.

Only the first or third outcome can support the four mixed seam controls.

## Falsifier

The proposed theta `sp4` bridge fails if the plus and minus Weyl algebras are
identified before completion, if the mixed products are not defined on the
completed domain, or if their commutators leave the declared boundary space.
The endpoint `sp4` theorem alone does not authorize any of these theta maps.
