# Stopped Dirichlet ingress carries the logarithmic Dirac flux cocycle

## 1. Reciprocal label spinor

For an integral label \(N\ge1\), put

\[
 \chi_N(z)=N^{-1/2-iz}
\]

and retain both reciprocal sheets:

\[
 a_N(z)=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 \chi_N(z)\\
 \chi_N(-z)
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]

This is the rank-one jump added to the stopped Dirichlet packet when the
moving boundary crosses \(v=\log N\).

## 2. Exact polarized flux

Use the Dirac boundary metric

\[
 \sigma_3=
 \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The polarized ingress flux is

\[
\begin{aligned}
 J_N(z,w)
 &=a_N(z)^*\big|_{\text{polarized}}\sigma_3a_N(w)\\
 &=\chi_N(z)\overline{\chi_N(w)}
   -\chi_N(-z)\overline{\chi_N(-w)}\\
 &=\frac1N\left(
 e^{i(\bar w-z)\log N}
 -e^{-i(\bar w-z)\log N}\right).
\end{aligned}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
 J_N(z,w)
 =\frac{2i}{N}
 \sin\!\big((\bar w-z)\log N\big).}
\]

Every label is isotropic on the ordinary real diagonal:

\[
 J_N(x,x)=0.
\]

More strongly, the flux is analytically divisible by the de Branges
denominator:

\[
\boxed{
 \frac{J_N(z,w)}{\bar w-z}
 =\frac{2i\log N}{N}
 \operatorname{sinc}\!\big((\bar w-z)\log N\big).}
\]

Up to the fixed Green-form factor \(i\), its diagonal limit is the positive
logarithmic weight \(\log N/N\).

Thus the logarithmic repair current previously derived from

\[
 [1-au,S_N]=a\log N\,S_N
\]

is the infinitesimal reciprocal-sheet boundary flux of the same scale
character.

## 3. Multiplicative cocycle law

Characters multiply:

\[
 \chi_{MN}(z)=\chi_M(z)\chi_N(z).
\]

Put

\[
 A_N(z,w)=\chi_N(z)\overline{\chi_N(w)},
 \qquad
 B_N(z,w)=\chi_N(-z)\overline{\chi_N(-w)}.
\]

Then \(J_N=A_N-B_N\), and

\[
\begin{aligned}
 J_{MN}
 &=A_MA_N-B_MB_N\\
 &=J_MA_N+B_MJ_N\\
 &=A_MJ_N+J_MB_N.
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
J_{MN}=J_MA_N+B_MJ_N,}
\]

with the equivalent oppositely ordered expression above. This is a twisted
multiplicative one-cocycle, not an additive collection of unrelated label
defects.

Taking the diagonal divided-difference limit converts this twisted product
law into

\[
 \frac{\log(MN)}{MN}
 =\frac{\log M}{M}\frac1N
  +\frac1M\frac{\log N}{N}.
\]

The arithmetic logarithm is therefore forced by flux composition.

## 4. Connected versus aggregate labels

The stopped Dirichlet packet contains all integers. The Weil boundary
contains prime powers with von Mangoldt weights. These are not the same
label space.

The passage

\[
 \sum_{N\ge1}N^{-s}
 \longmapsto
 -\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}
 =\sum_{N\ge2}\frac{\Lambda(N)}{N^s}
\]

is the connected or determinant-line logarithm of the aggregate arithmetic
amplitude. It removes multiplicative composites as independent channels and
retains the primitive prime-power current.

However, this operation cannot be applied after taking the raw spinor Gram
flux. The raw flux has coefficient \(1/N\), while the Weil prime-power
boundary has coefficient \(\Lambda(N)/\sqrt N\). Their ratio
\(\Lambda(N)\sqrt N\) is nonconstant and unbounded. Thus the complete boundary
chain must be ordered as

\[
\boxed{
\text{aggregate integral amplitude}
\to\text{determinant-line logarithm}
\to\text{connected von Mangoldt current}
\to\text{GNS/Dirac polarization}.}
\]

This explains why the logarithmic derivative and prime-power support appear
together in the Weil Green form. The raw \(1/N\) flux remains an exact
cocycle identity, but it is not yet the faithfully normalized Weil boundary.

## 5. Infinite synthesis is not automatically bounded

At real spectral parameter,

\[
 \|a_N(x)\|_{\mathbb C^2}^2=\frac2N.
\]

Therefore

\[
 \sum_{N\ge1}\|a_N(x)\|^2
\]

diverges logarithmically. The raw synthesis of all integral ingress vectors
is not Hilbert--Schmidt, and no unweighted finite-energy boundary vector is
obtained by simply summing labels.

The stopped packet at finite \(v\) is exact and finite. Passing
\(v\to\infty\) requires the same global completion/renormalization that turns
the Dirichlet series into the completed zeta function. This divergence is not
a numerical inconvenience; it is the operator-domain location of analytic
continuation.

Consequently, flux covariance is proved at every finite stopping boundary,
but closability of the global synthesis map remains open.

## 6. Implication for self-adjoint sewing

Each real-axis ingress line is individually Dirac-isotropic. The finite
stopped boundary relation generated by them is therefore a candidate
isotropic relation. Self-adjointness requires more:

1. convergence to a closed global relation;
2. maximality, not merely isotropy;
3. replacement of aggregate integer channels by the connected prime-power
   boundary without losing the flux law; and
4. inclusion of the archimedean and endpoint currents required by completion.

The source has supplied a local aggregate conservation law. The first global
gate is whether the nonlinear connected transform has a source-derived
polarization with the exact Weil weights. Only then can maximal
self-adjointness be asked.

## 7. Sharp falsifiers

The proposed boundary mechanism fails if:

1. determinant-line logarithm does not admit a compatible polarization with
   the exact von Mangoldt weights;
2. renormalized finite stopped relations do not converge in graph topology;
3. the limit is isotropic but not maximal;
4. completion leaves an uncontrolled archimedean flux; or
5. the construction requires zero locations to choose its domain.
