# Reciprocal symmetry neutralizes total divisor charge but preserves relative charge

## Two sectorwise phase records

Use the centered coordinate

\[
z=s-\frac12
\]

and the reciprocal Real involution

\[
Rz=-\overline z.
\]

The imaginary axis is its fixed seam. Give the right and left half-planes
their boundary orientations induced from their respective interiors. These
orientations are opposite on the common seam.

For a right-half-plane zero `w`, its Blaschke factor is

\[
b_w(z)=\frac{z-w}{z+\overline w}.
\]

On the seam it contributes one integral phase-delay unit:

\[
\frac{1}{2\pi}
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\frac{d}{dt}\arg b_w(it)\,dt=-1
\]

in the right-sector convention. Reciprocal reflection produces a partner in
the left sector. Because the induced seam orientation reverses, its local
charge has the opposite sign when both are written in one common seam frame.

Hence one reciprocal pair contributes

\[
(q_+,q_-)=(m,-m)
\]

for its multiplicity `m`.

## Exact decomposition

The codiagonal and anti-diagonal projections are

\[
q_{\mathrm{tot}}=q_++q_-,
\qquad
q_{\mathrm{rel}}=q_+-q_-.
\]

For every reciprocal off-seam divisor packet,

\[
q_{\mathrm{tot}}=0,
\qquad
q_{\mathrm{rel}}=2m.
\]

Thus reciprocal symmetry enforces neutrality of the total oriented divisor
charge. It does not annihilate the divisor. The complete information resides
in the relative phase channel.

Complex conjugation may double the packet again, but does not change this
conclusion: the total signed charge cancels between sectors while the
sector-resolved multiplicity remains nonzero.

## Relation to the anomaly line

The primitive and square cutoff anomaly connections are exact logarithmic
derivatives of nowhere-zero exponentials. Their loop residues vanish. They
can change the continuous phase frame, but cannot alter the integral relative
divisor charge.

The canonical value type for the missing phase record is therefore not an
ordinary scalar current. It is the quotient of the two-sector phase-current
pair by exact anomaly-frame connections. The residue map on this quotient
retains `q_rel`.

This is the divisor analogue of the earlier two-cone Toeplitz result:
unsigned density and total oriented flux forget the relative boundary class;
the anti-diagonal channel retains it.

## Consequence for the RH programme

The scalar functional equation sees the reciprocal codiagonal and is
compatible with arbitrary neutral off-seam divisor packets. Consequently it
cannot, by itself, distinguish RH from a hostile symmetric multiplier.

The next source theorem must construct the anti-diagonal phase port before
scalar Poisson aggregation and prove that its class vanishes for the theta
source in each open sector. Equivalently, it must prove absence of the
sectorwise Hardy inner record. Global phase neutrality is insufficient.

This gives a precise comparison-channel requirement:

1. two sector-local phase towers;
2. one common seam frame;
3. codiagonal reciprocity coherence;
4. an anti-diagonal divisor-charge observer modulo exact anomaly transport.

## Hostile test

Insert a reciprocal/conjugate Blaschke packet. It preserves boundary modulus,
the scalar functional equation, and total oriented charge. It changes the
relative charge by a nonzero even integer. Any proposed observer that reports
no change lacks the RH-bearing comparison channel.

## Scope

This theorem identifies the invariant channel carrying an off-seam divisor.
It does not construct the theta source's anti-diagonal phase port, prove that
its relative class vanishes, or prove RH.
