# Constructor-generated pro-Gram topology for theta labels

## Problem left by the fixed-prime obstruction

The analytic Gram seminorm does not make the authorized valuation projectors
continuous.  Adding an arbitrary orthogonal copy of every integer label would
repair this, but would not explain which discrete information the source
requires.

There is a smaller canonical construction.  Let `C_fin` be the finite-support
prime-power-labelled source module, let `U` be the full tail--seam synthesis,
and let `M_src` be the monoid generated by the authorized finite source
operations:

\[
 P_{p,r}e_n=\mathbf 1_{v_p(n)=r}e_n,
 \qquad
 M_te_n=n^{it}e_n,
\]

together with the declared finite Fock and boundary maps whenever their
codomains have source norms.

## Operational topology

For every `C` in `M_src`, define

\[
 \boxed{q_C(c)=\lVert U(Cc)\rVert.}
\]

Equip `C_fin` with the locally convex topology generated by all these
seminorms.  This is the weakest topology that retains the analytic readout and
makes every authorized constructor visible after synthesis.

It is not fitted to a desired lower bound.  Its generating family is fixed by
the independently declared constructor repertoire.

## Constructor continuity

For `A,C` in `M_src`,

\[
 q_C(Ac)=q_{CA}(c).
\]

Hence every authorized constructor acts continuously: it merely reindexes the
defining seminorm family.  Mellin transport need not be uniformly bounded in
one analytic Gram norm; it is an exact continuous action on the full
operational topology because all `M_t` are retained as constructors.

## Finite-packet faithfulness

Assume the occupation projectors `P_(p,r)` belong to `M_src`.  Let

\[
 c=\sum_{n\in F}c_ne_n\ne0
\]

have finite support, and choose `n_0` with `c_(n_0) != 0`.  For every other
`m` in `F`, unique factorization supplies a prime `p` for which
`v_p(m) != v_p(n_0)`.  A finite product `C_(n_0,F)` of the corresponding
occupation projectors therefore satisfies

\[
 C_{n_0,F}c=c_{n_0}e_{n_0}.
\]

Since the full analytic atom `Ue_(n_0)` is nonzero,

\[
 q_{C_{n_0,F}}(c)>0.
\]

Thus the operational seminorm family separates every nonzero finite source
packet.  The discrete port forced by divisibility can be the valuation
constructor algebra itself; an independently postulated orthogonal integer
basis is unnecessary at this stage.

## What this does not prove

The completion may be a non-Hilbert pro-Gram space.  Faithfulness on finite
packets does not prove that its completion has no invisible nonzero elements,
that endpoint currents extend, or that the doubled Green operator has closed
range.  Those are now explicit completion questions rather than ambiguities
about which coefficient norm to guess.

## Acceptance gates

1. Derive every generator of `M_src` from theta/Tate/Fock constructors.
2. Prove the completed space is nontrivial and Hausdorff.
3. Extend the primitive, square, seam, and archimedean currents continuously.
4. Extend the doubled differential and Green identity.
5. Test whether a Cauchy net can vanish in every `q_C` while retaining a
   nonzero source boundary state.

The last item is the new completion-at-infinity falsifier.  It is stronger
than testing the untransported Gram norm alone.

## Honest frontier

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{source constructors}
 \longrightarrow
 \text{pro-Gram topology}
 \longrightarrow
 \text{completion-stable Green identity?}}
\]

The first arrow is now canonical and separates finite packets.  The second
arrow is the remaining analytic obstruction.
