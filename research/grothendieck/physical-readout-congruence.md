## Typed input

Let a source symmetry group `G` act on a coefficient lens `L`. A physical
sector may also supply a selection/cycle object `B` and a pairing

\[
p:L\otimes B\longrightarrow A.
\]

Both variances must be transported. The relevant datum is not merely one
scalar value but the resulting `G`-representation `rho_p` on the complete
physical-observable object `O_p`. This requirement makes `ker(rho_p)` a
normal subgroup. If only a single non-stable value is given, take its whole
`G`-orbit before forming a kernel.

For a nonempty family `P` of admitted, typed physical readouts define

\[
K_{\rm obs}=\bigcap_{p\in P}\ker(\rho_p),
\qquad
K_{\rm phys}=[G,G]\cap K_{\rm obs}.
\]

Both subgroups are normal. The **physical-readout congruence** is the group
congruence with kernel `K_phys`, and the universal physical shadow is

\[
\boxed{G_{\rm phys}=G/K_{\rm phys}}.
\]

For a source groupoid, perform the construction on every isotropy group and
take the wide normal subgroupoid generated by the invisible isotropy
commutators. Its quotient is the corresponding groupoid coequalizer.

## Universal property

The quotient map `q:G -> G_phys` kills every commutator invisible to every
admitted physical readout. If `f:G -> Q` is any other quotient with those
commutators in its kernel, then `K_phys` is contained in `ker(f)`, so there is
a unique map

\[
\bar f:G_{\rm phys}\longrightarrow Q,
\qquad f=\bar f\circ q.
\]

This is the smallest congruence forced by the admitted readouts. It is an
abelianization exactly when

\[
[G,G]\subseteq K_{\rm obs}.
\]

If a physical observable detects one commutator, that commutator survives in
`G_phys`; automatic abelianization is then false. If no global physical
readout exists, `rho_p`, its kernel, and the quotient are untyped—not
negative or positive evidence.

## The invariant quotient is a different universal object

The preceding construction is representation-kernel linear: it answers
which source transformations remain observable in the complete covariant
physical representation. It must not be used as the universal object for a
nonlinear invariant readout.

For a `G`-representation `V` over a coefficient ring `A`, invariant scalar
records instead define the affine quotient

\[
V/\!/G:=\operatorname{Spec}A[V]^G.
\]

Its map `pi:V -> V//G` is universal among `G`-invariant affine maps out of
`V`: every such map factors uniquely through `pi`. This quotient classifies
closed orbits in the usual affine invariant-theory sense; it does not replace
`G` by `G_ab`, and it need not forget the action of `[G,G]` on `V`.

Thus the two constructions answer disjoint questions:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
G/K_{\rm phys}&\text{which transformations remain visible?}\\
\operatorname{Spec}A[V]^G&\text{which invariant scalar records distinguish orbits?}
\end{array}
\]

Neither construction is derivable from the other without additional typed
hypotheses. Arithmetic may organize the commutative invariant-record algebra
while the transformation group and covariant multiplet remain nonabelian.

## Descent of the conditional `pi_0` semiring

There are two cases.

First, quotienting only source automorphisms changes isotropy but not objects
or connected-component classes. Then

\[
\pi_0(\operatorname{Surf}^{\sqcup}_U)
\]

and its conditional endomorphism multiplication are unchanged.

Second, a physical readout may identify object classes. Let `R` be the
induced equivalence on the free commutative monoid `M` generated by `U`. The
necessary condition is that `R` be an additive monoid congruence. It is also
sufficient for the conditional multiplication

\[
a\cdot b=f_a(b).
\]

Indeed, multiplication by a fixed class is repeated addition. Therefore
`a R a'` implies `na R na'` for every finite component word `n`; applying
this in both variables proves

\[
a R a',\ b R b'
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
ab\ R\ a'b'.
\]

Thus every additive congruence on the rank-one free additive Carrier is
automatically a congruence for the derived initial-semiring multiplication.
No surface-level tensor is assumed.
