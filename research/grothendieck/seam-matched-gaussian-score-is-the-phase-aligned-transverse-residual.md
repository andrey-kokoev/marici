## Canonical seam Gaussian

The completed theta source is smooth, even, and strictly log-concave at the
seam.  Define its matched Gaussian curvature by

\[
A_0=-\frac{\Phi''(0)}{2\Phi(0)}>0.
\]

The Gaussian with the same value and second logarithmic jet is

\[
G_0(u)=\Phi(0)e^{-A_0u^2}.
\]

Its score equation is

\[
G_0'(u)+2A_0uG_0(u)=0.
\]

This selects the theta score residual

\[
r(u)=\Phi'(u)+2A_0u\Phi(u).
\]

Evenness and the definition of \(A_0\) give

\[
r(0)=0,
\qquad
r'(0)=0.
\]

Thus \(r\) begins beyond the Gaussian seam jet.  It is not a fitted collision
coefficient; it is fixed before \(L\), \(x\), or any zero is chosen.

## Exact phase-aligned identity

Define

\[
C_L(x)=\int_0^L\Phi(u)\cos(xu)\,du,
\]

\[
M_L(x)=\int_0^L u\Phi(u)\sin(xu)\,du,
\]

and

\[
R_L(x)=\int_0^L r(u)\sin(xu)\,du.
\]

Integration by parts gives

\[
R_L(x)
=
\Phi(L)\sin(xL)-xC_L(x)+2A_0M_L(x).
\]

At every phase-aligned endpoint

\[
xL=2\pi k,
\qquad
k\ge1,
\]

this reduces to

\[
R_L(x)=-xC_L(x)+2A_0M_L(x).
\]

Consequently, on the cosine-zero locus,

\[
M_L(x)=\frac{R_L(x)}{2A_0}.
\]

The phase-aligned double-zero collision is therefore equivalent to

\[
C_L(x)=0,
\qquad
R_L(x)=0.
\]

\[

## Label lift

\[

\]

\[

\[
\sum_nr_n(0)=0,

The exact translate law gives

\[

\left[
\phi_1'(u+\log n)+2A_0u\phi_1(u+\log n)

Writing \(r_1(v)=\phi_1'(v)+2A_0v\phi_1(v)\) makes the shear explicit:

\[
r_n(u)
=n^{-1/2}
\left[
r_1(u+\log n)-2A_0\log(n)\phi_1(u+\log n)
\right].
\]

Thus the residual has two independently typed parts: translated primitive
score and logarithmic label current.  After taking the sine readout,

\[

=
\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-1/2}
\int_0^L r_1(u+\log n)\sin(xu)\,du

The second sum is a von-Mangoldt-like logarithmic shear before any Euler
decomposition: its weight is generated by translating the seam-matched score,
