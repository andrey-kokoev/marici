# Universal zero propagation and joint faithfulness are incompatible

Author: `marici.Grothendieck`

Date: 2026-08-26

Status: exact linear no-go theorem

## The two proposed gates

Let the value--flux state space be two-dimensional and let the distinguished
scalar readout be

\[
r=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The proposed zero-propagation law requires every authorized arrow (A) to
preserve the scalar zero sieve:

\[
r(x)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r(Ax)=0.
\]

Joint faithfulness requires the future rows (rA_w) generated by all words to
have trivial common kernel.

These two requirements are incompatible.

## Exact obstruction

Write

\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The scalar kernel is spanned by the flux vector (e_2). Therefore universal
zero propagation is equivalent to

\[
rAe_2=b=0.
\]

Every authorized arrow must be lower triangular. Products of such arrows are
again lower triangular, and hence every future readout has the form

\[
rA_w=\lambda_w r.
\]

The common future kernel still contains (e_2). The behavioral map has rank at
most one and is not monic on a genuine value--flux space.

Thus universal zero propagation protects the scalar kernel so completely that
no future probe can observe what lies inside it.

## Audit of the named generators

For a multiplicative source block,

\[
S_g=\begin{pmatrix}g&0\\g'&g\end{pmatrix}.
\]

One Euler jet, the archimedean jet, and every cutoff product of such jets all
preserve the zero sieve. They also leave the flux line behaviorally invisible.

Reciprocal sewing has two possible typings:

1. As a comparison between distinct sector readouts, it may preserve zeros
   covariantly, but it is not an intra-sector future observer and cannot repair
   joint faithfulness there.
2. As an internal coordinate exchange, it sends flux into value, observes the
   hidden line, and immediately violates zero propagation.

The Clark differentiated channel exhibits the same tradeoff constructively:
at a simple scalar zero it reads (F'\ne0). This restores observability by
falsifying propagation.

## Correction to the categorical DPC

The implication

\[
r(x)=0\Longrightarrow r(A_wx)=0
\]

cannot be required for every state in a nontrivial linear boundary object and
then combined with joint faithfulness. A viable theorem must weaken or retype
one side. Possibilities are:

1. propagation only for the distinguished nonlinear source subvariety;
2. propagation of a higher-grade ideal rather than the scalar kernel;
3. a conservation relation among future outputs rather than their individual
   vanishing;
4. a contradiction obtained from the nonzero transverse flux of a zero-state.

The fourth option matches the existing Clark result most naturally: a scalar
zero does not propagate as silence; it produces an observable transverse
current whose global boundary balance may be impossible off seam.

## Falsifier and scope

The theorem is elementary and exact. It would fail only if an authorized word
both preserved the scalar kernel universally and mapped some vector in that
kernel to a nonzero scalar output, which is a contradiction in terms.

This closes the universal zero-sieve route. It does not rule out a restricted
zero-propagation theorem on the distinguished theta orbit or a global law that
uses the transverse output rather than requiring it to vanish.
