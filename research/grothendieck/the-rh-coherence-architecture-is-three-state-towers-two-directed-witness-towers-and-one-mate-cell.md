## Two directed witness towers

The previous fourth tower was overloaded. It implicitly attempted to witness
two opposite implications.

```text

```

The global Tate construction largely supplies it already.


This implication is not the inverse of the forward construction. Scalar

## Why `3+2+1` is minimal

The previous no-go results separate the six roles.

- Three primary towers are necessary because constructibility,
  observability, and legal transport are mutually nonimplicative.
- One forward witness is necessary to prevent manufactured determinant
  sections.
- One backward witness is necessary because output nullity does not imply
  input Ward nullity or balance.
- One mate cell is necessary because independently valid forward and backward
  constructions can refer to different incidence, completion, or factor
  presentations.

The recent connected-grade results sharpen the last point. Primitive and
square currents type a single reciprocal split; the `k>=3` connection is
needed for multifactor identity. Therefore the mate cell must compare the
whole boundary-bearing pro-object, not only its first two grades.

## Deutsch--Popper conjecture

For every finite cutoff `X`, the source-derived forward Tate witness and the
source-derived backward Green witness admit a unique typed mate on the
dynamical scalar-null pullback. These mates are natural under cutoff
restriction and survive restricted-product completion. Their balanced defect
identity has a faithful positive relationship-energy coefficient off the
unitary seam.

The conjecture is falsified by any cutoff where:

- the backward boundary map cannot be constructed independently;
- forward and backward incidence disagree;
- a primitive, square, archimedean, seam, or connected-tail residual remains;
- the mate exists only after scalar summation;
- cutoff restriction breaks the cell;
- completion creates an escaping null state.

If the conjecture holds, the completed zero-state conservation identity forces
the centered real part to vanish. If it fails, `3+2+1` still localizes the
failure to one named witness or coherence layer.
