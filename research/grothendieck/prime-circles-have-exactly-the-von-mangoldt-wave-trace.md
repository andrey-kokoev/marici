# Prime Circles Have Exactly the von Mangoldt Wave Trace

## Source-derived arithmetic evolution

For every prime $p$, let

$$
L_p=\log p
$$

and form the circle

$$
C_p=\mathbb R/L_p\mathbb Z.
$$

On $L^2(C_p)$, the periodic translation generator

$$
A_p=-i\frac{d}{dx}
$$

is self-adjoint with spectrum

$$
\frac{2\pi m}{L_p},\qquad m\in\mathbb Z.
$$

Its unitary evolution translates around the prime circle. Distributional
Poisson summation gives

$$
\operatorname{Tr}e^{-itA_p}
=\sum_{m\in\mathbb Z}e^{-2\pi imt/L_p}
=L_p\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}\delta(t-kL_p).
$$

Restricting to positive return times,

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_+e^{-itA_p}
=(\log p)\sum_{k\ge1}\delta(t-k\log p).
$$

Summing over primes yields

$$
\sum_p\operatorname{Tr}_+e^{-itA_p}
=\sum_{p,k\ge1}(\log p)\delta(t-\log p^k)
=\sum_{n\ge2}\Lambda(n)\delta(t-\log n).
$$

This is exactly the arithmetic part of the required wave trace.

## Explanation of the prime-power grammar

The geometry explains the connected coefficients without Euler algebra:

- every prime is one primitive circle;
- traversing it $k$ times produces the prime power $p^k$;
- every repetition retains the primitive length $\log p$;
- a composite $pq$ is not a primitive return because there is no circle of
  length $\log p+\log q$;
- the direct sum over prime circles adds independent primitive sectors.

Thus

$$
\Lambda(p^k)=\log p,
\qquad
\Lambda(n)=0
$$

for non-prime-power $n$ is a periodic-orbit theorem for the prime-circle
carrier. This is the operator form of the positive Fock constructibility law.

## Dynamical determinant

The primitive return on $C_p$ has length $L_p$. Its dynamical zeta factor is

$$
\exp\left(\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{e^{-skL_p}}k\right)
=(1-e^{-sL_p})^{-1}
=(1-p^{-s})^{-1}.
$$

Therefore the direct sum of prime circles simultaneously produces:

- the local Euler factors as dynamical determinants;
- the prime-power logarithmic current as the wave trace;
- the primitive, square, and $k\ge3$ regularization grades by repetition
  depth;
- the prime-diagonal Schatten-three operator after compressing to primitive
  return amplitudes.

These constructions are now one object rather than parallel analogies.

## Why this is not yet the RH operator

The spectrum of the uncoupled arithmetic generator

$$
A_{\mathbb P}=\bigoplus_p A_p
$$

is the explicit union

$$
\left\{\frac{2\pi m}{\log p}:p\text{ prime},\ m\in\mathbb Z\right\}.
$$

It is not the set of Riemann-zero ordinates. Moreover, the full direct-sum
trace diverges and contains infinitely many zero modes. The arithmetic carrier
supplies the periodic-orbit side of a trace formula, not its desired spectral
side.

The missing operation must couple or relatively compare this prime-circle
carrier with the continuum and archimedean sectors. A successful completed
operator $A_{\mathrm{comp}}$ would need a relative trace identity of the form

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathrm{rel}}
\left(e^{-itA_{\mathrm{comp}}}-e^{-itA_{\mathrm{ref}}}\right)
=\sum_{n\ge2}\Lambda(n)\delta(t-\log n)
-e^t
+J_\infty(t),
$$

with reciprocal orientation and the primitive/square boundary channels
retained.

## Sharp frontier

The arithmetic wave evolution has now been constructed without zero data.
The remaining theorem is global scattering or sewing:

1. derive the reference continuum and archimedean operator;
2. couple it to the prime circles through the adelic product formula;
3. prove that the relative evolution is generated by one self-adjoint
   operator;
4. show that its relative trace is the completed explicit-formula current;
5. prove that the resulting spectral determinant is framed Xi;
6. explain how the obvious local circle spectra cancel from the relative
   spectral side, leaving only the completed global spectrum.

The final item is the immediate hostile gate. If local modes survive as
uncancelled poles, the prime-circle construction is only an Euler-factor
realization and not a Hilbert--Pólya mechanism.
