# Theta half-plane zero-freeness is causal cyclicity, not zero leakage

## Bounded question

Which Hardy-space operator detects the inner zeros lost by the
retarded--advanced product?

## Inner--outer factorization

Let the retarded theta factor belong to the upper-half-plane Hardy space and
write its canonical factorization as

\[
F_+=UO,
\]

where \(U\) is inner and \(O\) is outer. Source provenance fixes \(F_+\), but it
does not by itself imply that \(U\) is constant. The authorized source can
possess a native inner factor.

Under the standard entire-continuation and exact-support hypotheses, singular
boundary factors and pure delay factors can be audited separately. The
remaining Blaschke part records the half-plane zero divisor.

## Hankel leakage is blind

For an inner analytic symbol \(U\), define

\[
H_U=P_-M_U\big|_{H^2_+}.
\]

Since multiplication by \(U\) preserves \(H^2_+\),

\[
H_U=0.
\]

Thus causal leakage vanishes for every inner factor, including one containing
arbitrarily many half-plane zeros. Causal unitarity, positive delay support,
and zero Hankel leakage do not imply zero-freeness.

## The model-space defect

The divisor-sensitive object is

\[
K_U=H^2_+\ominus UH^2_+.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
K_U=\ker T_{\bar U}.
\]

For a finite Blaschke product of degree \(m\),

\[
\dim K_U=m.
\]

A one-zero hostile factor therefore has zero causal leakage but a
one-dimensional Toeplitz defect. The defect, not the Hankel block, records the
missing causal states.

## Causal cyclicity

The closed shift-invariant subspace generated by \(F_+\) is

\[
\overline{H^\infty F_+}=UH^2_+,
\]

because \(O\) is outer. Therefore the following conditions are equivalent:

1. \(F_+\) is outer;
2. \(U\) is constant;
3. \(K_U=0\);
4. \(F_+\) is cyclic for the causal shift semigroup;
5. the retarded factor has no Hardy inner zero divisor, after the separately
   audited singular and delay factors are removed.

In the time domain, cyclicity means that the closed span generated from the
theta tail by authorized causal translations fills the declared causal state
space.

This is an operational form of half-plane zero-freeness. An off-seam zero is a
missing direction in the shift orbit, not causal leakage into the wrong
half-plane.

## Source typing of the ninth rotation

The actual theta integral defines \(F_+\) directly. Its inner symbol \(U\) is
obtained by canonical Hardy factorization, not by a currently known finite
prime constructor. Therefore primitive, prime-square, archimedean, and residue
states cannot yet be assigned to \(K_U\) without an additional source
correspondence.

At a finite rational approximation, the Toeplitz defect dimension counts the
retained Blaschke zeros. Under completion it may:

- stabilize;
- grow without bound;
- acquire a singular-inner component;
- lose continuity under the chosen cutoff maps.

Finite zero leakage remains identically zero in every case.

## Relation to the Hermite programme

For finite bordered numerators, negative directions of the Hermite form count
nonreal conjugate structure. For Hardy factors, the model space counts
half-plane inner zeros. These are two coordinate systems on divisor failure:

- Hermite signature is algebraic and coefficient-based;
- model-space defect is causal and invariant-subspace-based.

A source-derived map between them would convert Hermite positivity into causal
cyclicity. Agreement after computing zeros would not provide such a map.

## Result

The ninth rotation closes the leakage route and identifies the correct
operator invariant. Half-plane zero-freeness is equivalent to causal
cyclicity, or vanishing of the Toeplitz model-space defect, under the declared
Hardy factorization hypotheses.

The live source theorem is:

> Prove that labelled theta/Tate causal translations generate the complete
> retarded state space, with primitive, square, archimedean, and seam channels
> retained.

This is equivalent in force to the desired half-plane zero-free statement, but
it is operationally typed and admits source-level constructor attacks.

## Sharp falsifier

For any proposed causal-completeness theorem, insert a one-zero Blaschke
factor. It has

\[
H_U=0,
\qquad
\dim K_U=1.
\]

Any test using only leakage, boundary modulus, or causal support accepts this
hostile. A valid source theorem must detect the nonzero model-space vector or
exclude the inner factor before scalar aggregation.
