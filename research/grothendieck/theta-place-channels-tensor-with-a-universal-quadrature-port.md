# Theta place channels tensor with a universal quadrature port

## Two different channel notions

The proposed flavor analogy contains a valid factorization only after two
types of channels are kept distinct.

At a place (v), the Tate transition coefficient is a complex scalar

\[
 \gamma_v(s)=a_v(s)+ib_v(s).
\]

On the underlying real amplitude plane, multiplication by this scalar is

\[
 R_v(s)=a_v(s)I+b_v(s)J,
 \qquad
 J=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The place label (v) and the quadrature basis (e,Je) therefore describe
different factors. Places distinguish arithmetic channels. The pair (e,Je)
is the universal two-dimensional real representation needed to carry one
complex channel without erasing its quarter-turn.

## Exact commutation

Because (R_v(s)) is a polynomial in (J),

\[
 R_v(s)J=JR_v(s).
\]

Consequently the source-native local module has the form

\[
 \mathcal V_v=\mathcal L_v\otimes\mathbb R^2.
\]

where Tate transport acts on the local place data and by (R_v(s)) on the
quadrature factor, while the Clark sheet involution acts by (J) on the
quadrature factor. Their commutation is exact; no Maslov phase is required.

This verifies that the Clark forcing pair is the minimal representation of
one complex local channel. It does not identify the two quadratures with two
arithmetic flavors.

## Prime powers are modes, not new places

At a finite prime (p), the local Euler expansion

\[
 (1-p^{-s})^{-1}=\sum_{k\ge0}p^{-ks}
\]

places all prime powers in the orbit tower of the single (p)-channel. Thus
(p^k) is a repeated local mode indexed by (k), not an additional place.
The archimedean factor supplies a distinct place channel rather than another
prime-power mode.

The typed comparison has three factors: place (v), orbit mode (k), and
quadrature port \(\mathbb R^2\).

## Global recombination obstruction

The local tensor factorization does not by itself form an ordinary global
Hilbert tensor product. On the critical seam, the local transitions are
unitary, but the raw implementability defect obeys

\[
 \sum_p|1-\gamma_p(\tfrac12+it)|^2=\infty
\]

for fixed nonzero (t). The primitive and prime-square currents therefore
cannot be erased when the place channels are recombined. The restricted
product must retain

\[
 k=1,
 \qquad
 k=2,
 \qquad
 k\ge3,
 \qquad
 v=\infty
\]

as separately typed boundary and tail levels.

Accordingly, the exact local statement is stronger than an analogy, while
the naive global flavor decomposition is false. A lawful global object must
be a boundary-bearing restricted product, not the tensor product of the
scalar local transition coefficients alone.

## Explanatory consequence

The closest defensible flavor reading is relational:

- a place identifies which local comparison channel is active;
- its orbit modes record repeated transport within that channel;
- the universal quadrature port preserves the complex relationship carried
  through the channel; and
- the completed scalar is a compression after coherent recombination.

The arithmetic places are not a finite particle-flavor multiplet. What is
shared with flavor is the architecture of typed channels, transport, and a
late scalar matrix coefficient.

## Falsifier

The local factorization fails if a source-derived transition has a real
quadrature action outside the algebra generated by (I,J), or if Clark
transport changes the place label. The global construction fails if the
primitive, square, and archimedean boundary data do not admit a coherent
restricted-product recombination with the connected tail.

## Scope

This proves the local commutation and types prime powers as orbit modes. It
also rejects an ordinary global tensor-product interpretation. It does not
construct the boundary-bearing adelic restricted product or derive RH.
