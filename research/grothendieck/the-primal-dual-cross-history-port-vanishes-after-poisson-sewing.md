# The primal-dual cross-history port vanishes after Poisson sewing

## Quotient levels

## General cross-history factorization

For two histories \(f,g\), define their bilateral transforms

\[
M_f(z)=\int f(q)e^{zq}\,dq,
\qquad
M_g(z)=\int g(q)e^{zq}\,dq.
\]

Their exchange-odd comparison factors as

\[
L_{f,g}(z)
=\frac12\bigl(
M_f(-z)M_g(z)-M_f(z)M_g(-z)
\bigr).
\]

Thus, whenever the two full transform vectors share an authorized common
frame, \(L_{f,g}\) is already a product tester. It is not independent joint
information.

\[

\[

\]

The primal--dual pair is genuinely typed before sewing, but it does not remain

## Before completion

Any cross determinant formed from these components is derived from the
