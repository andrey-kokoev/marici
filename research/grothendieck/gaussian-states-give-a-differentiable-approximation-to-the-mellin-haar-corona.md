# Gaussian states give a differentiable approximation to the Mellin Haar corona

## Question

Can one construct the missing differentiable corona channel without choosing zero-derived nodes?

## Construction

On the source Mellin group algebra generated by symbols `U_t`, define, for `sigma>0`,

\[
\omega_\sigma(U_t)=e^{-\sigma^2t^2/2}.
\]

This is positive definite because it is the characteristic function of the centered Gaussian probability measure

\[
d\gamma_\sigma(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma}
e^{-x^2/(2\sigma^2)}dx.
\]

Hence it defines a state and a GNS representation. An explicit realization is

\[
H_\sigma=L^2(\mathbb R,\gamma_\sigma),
\qquad
(\pi_\sigma(U_t)f)(x)=e^{-itx}f(x),
\]

with cyclic vector `1`.

## Differentiable vertical action

For every `f` in `H_sigma`, dominated convergence gives strong continuity of `t -> pi_sigma(U_t)f`. Its self-adjoint generator is multiplication by `x`,

\[
(Q_\sigma f)(x)=x f(x),
\]

on the dense domain where `x f` is square-integrable. Thus this channel retains the first-order Mellin germ absent from the Haar-corona GNS representation.

The cyclic vector lies in every polynomial domain and

\[
\|Q_\sigma^k1\|^2
=\int x^{2k}d\gamma_\sigma(x)
=(2k-1)!!\,\sigma^{2k}.
\]

## Corona limit

For every fixed nonzero `t`,

\[
\omega_\sigma(U_t)\longrightarrow0
\]

as `sigma -> infinity`, while `omega_sigma(I)=1`. Therefore the states converge pointwise on the Mellin algebra to the Haar-corona state.

The differentiable structure is not uniform in this limit:

\[
\|Q_\sigma1\|=\sigma\longrightarrow\infty.
\]

This exactly explains how the limiting corona retains each fixed shift while losing its generator.

## Relation to the order completion

The cumulative-sum completion of finite zero-sum packets is source-derived and nonlocal, with adjacent-gap norm

\[
2\sum_i(\lambda_{i+1}-\lambda_i)|a_i|^2.
\]

The Gaussian GNS channel supplies the independently missing differentiable vertical representation. These two objects are not yet connected by a proved map. In particular, no theorem identifies the adjacent-gap generator with `Q_sigma` or represents the completed gamma–prime form on their pullback.

## Strongest falsification attempt

The construction is a family of regular states, not a single regular representation of the Haar-corona state. Its generator moments diverge in the corona limit. Consequently it cannot by itself provide a cutoff-uniform cross operator or Schur bound. Choosing `sigma` as a function of the heat parameter would require deriving that scale from the completed transform rather than fitting it afterward.

## Disposition

A differentiable approximation exists and is source-defined from Mellin transport plus Gaussian smoothing. The next admissible test is whether the exact heat kernel fixes `sigma(t)` and yields a bounded map between the adjacent-gap completion and `H_sigma(t)`. Without that map, this is a regularization mechanism rather than the missing completed-form factorization.
