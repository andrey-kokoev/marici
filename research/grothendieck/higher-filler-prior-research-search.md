## Claim boundary

It reports two successive degrees of filling, sphere and support obstructions, and a surviving higher obstruction. ## Disposition

First reuse the actual-source complete-comparison contract and its faithful admission envelope, rather than postulating equality between AB and AD;DB or building a generic new horn constructor. The next discriminating question is whether the intended pair admits source-derived maps into those complete comparison packages while retaining its observations. No such adapter was constructed in this search. The alternative chain-level route requires its own endpoint/support-preserving boundary adapter. Neither framework supplies a canonical incremental segment-selection rule automatically.
