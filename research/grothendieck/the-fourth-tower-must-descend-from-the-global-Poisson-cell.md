## Why the finite valuation route cannot be lifted upward

Finite Euler cutoff is not preserved by global Fourier--Poisson transport. If
(P_X) is a finite source projection, the Beck--Chevalley leakage is

\[
\mathfrak A_X=P_X\mathcal F(I-P_X),
\]

trigonometric polynomial, not another finite comb.

There is a second obstruction. Prime-power seam sampling (S) is not
faithful on the continuous source. There are nonzero variations (b) between

\[

\]

but nonzero Poisson-sensitive response. Hence Poisson sewing cannot factor as

\[

\]

Therefore the local bordered valuation colligations and their seam-oriented
energies cannot be assembled upward into the global reciprocal cell.
