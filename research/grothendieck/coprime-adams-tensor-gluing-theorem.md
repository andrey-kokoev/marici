# Exact coprime Adams multiplicativity positively glues prime lattices

Let finitely many primes carry normalized edge correlations `r_p`, with
`|r_p|<=1`. On exponent vectors define

```
K(alpha,beta)=product_p r_p^|alpha_p-beta_p|.          (1)
```

Every rectangular finite Gram matrix of `K` is a Kronecker product of the
one-prime geometric Toeplitz matrices and is therefore positive.

For the first mixed rectangle `0,log p,log q,log(pq)`, the matrix is
`G_p tensor G_q`. Its eigenvalues are
`(1 +/- |r_p|)(1 +/- |r_q|)` and its determinant is

```
(1-|r_p|^2)^2(1-|r_q|^2)^2.                          (2)
```

If one-prime Adams towers compose exactly and coprime Mackey squares satisfy
base-change interchange, their positive dilations therefore tensor together.

This remains a target, not a property of the completed Weil form. The Euler
logarithm is additive over primes, but normalized Weil correlations need not
factor as (1); gamma and endpoint terms couple prime directions globally.

The first mixed-prime falsifier must compare the direct `pq` edge with the
two routes `p` then `q` and `q` then `p`. A source theorem must give exact
interchange or a contractive operator-valued completion defect. Uncontrolled
route holonomy prevents positive one-prime towers from gluing globally.

This identifies the desired global explanation: Adams composition along
prime powers plus Mackey interchange across coprime primes. Proving it for
actual completed Weil blocks remains RH-strength work.
