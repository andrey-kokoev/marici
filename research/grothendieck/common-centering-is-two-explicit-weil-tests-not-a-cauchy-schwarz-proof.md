# Common centering is two explicit Weil tests, not a Cauchy--Schwarz proof

## Question

What does the stable common-center rank-two identity mean inside the explicit Weil source pairing?

## Two amplitudes

Fix `t,h` and define

\[
G_0(u)=e^{-tu^2/2}q_h(u),
\qquad
G_r(u)=G_0(u)(e^{-hu^2}-r),
\]

where

\[
|q_h(u)|^2=1-e^{-hu^2}.
\]

Let `g_0,g_r` be their inverse Fourier transforms. Under the completed heat--Weil normalization,

\[
A_0=W(g_0*g_0^*),
\]

\[
B_r=W(g_0*g_r^*)=A_1-rA_0,
\]

and

\[
C_r=W(g_r*g_r^*)=A_2-2rA_1+r^2A_0.
\]

Therefore

\[
D_2=A_0C_r-|B_r|^2
\]

is the determinant of the Weil form restricted to the explicit two-dimensional source span generated by `g_0` and `g_r`.

## What common centering achieves

Choosing rational `r` near `A_1/A_0` makes `B_r` small. It selects an approximately Weil-orthogonal pair without dividing intervals. This improves numerical conditioning and gives a direct finite falsifier.

For the larger-heat sample, `r=0.989` produced a centered linear residual near `3.83e-7`, while the positive centered quadratic remained near `2.93e-5`.

## What it does not achieve

The inequality

\[
|B_r|^2\le A_0C_r
\]

looks like Cauchy--Schwarz, but invoking Cauchy--Schwarz would assume that the restricted Weil form is already positive. The identity is a coordinate transformation of the rank-two target, not its proof.

Likewise, the negative Dirac prime atoms in the full Weil distribution prohibit a pointwise positive-density factor. Positivity can only hold on this restricted nonlocal test family through the completed transform.

## Concrete proof target

A noncircular rank-two source proof may use the explicit tests `g_0,g_r`, but must establish all three inequalities directly from the completed explicit formula:

\[
A_0>0,
\qquad
C_r>0,
\qquad
A_0C_r-|B_r|^2>0.
\]

The outward-rounded four-sample checker is exactly such a proof for isolated parameter points. Extending it uniformly requires parameter-box enclosures or a new analytic inequality for these two Weil tests.

## Mesh refinement

The two-channel identity for `q_h` transports both amplitudes under mesh composition. Hence rank-two certificates at refined meshes must be compatible with one declared dilation rather than independent numerical coordinates.

## Boundary

The exact Fourier constants and sesquilinear convention for `W(g_0*g_r^*)` must be checked against the repository explicit formula. No positivity is inferred from the notation.

## Disposition

Use common centering as a numerically stable restriction of the Weil form to two explicit Schwartz tests. Reject any argument that labels its determinant a Gram determinant before proving the three source inequalities.
