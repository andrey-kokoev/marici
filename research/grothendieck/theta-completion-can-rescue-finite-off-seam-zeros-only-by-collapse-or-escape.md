# Theta completion can rescue finite off-seam zeros only by collapse or escape

## Bounded question

If finite positive shell approximants naturally possess off-seam zeros, in
what precise sense can the completed theta source still confine its zeros to
the seam?

## Local-uniform completion gate

Let (F_N) be source-authorized entire approximants converging locally
uniformly to a nonzero entire function (F). Suppose (z_N) is a zero of
(F_N).

If a subsequence of (z_N) remains in a compact set, then it has a further
subsequence converging to some (z_*). Local uniform convergence gives

\[
F(z_*)
=
\lim_N F_N(z_N)
=0.
\]

Therefore a bounded family of approximant zeros cannot disappear in the
completion. Its accumulation points are zeros of the completed transform.

## Compact zero-free form

Let (K) be a compact set on which (F) is nonzero. Then

\[
m_K=\min_{z\in K}|F(z)|>0.
\]

Local uniform convergence implies that, for all sufficiently large (N),

\[
\sup_{z\in K}|F_N(z)-F(z)|<m_K.
\]

Hence (F_N) is also nonzero on (K). Equivalently, completion cannot repair
a persistent off-seam zero contained in a fixed compact zero-free region.

## The only two rescue mechanisms

If the completed transform has zeros only on the seam, every sequence of
off-seam zeros of finite approximants must do one of two things:

1. escape every compact set;
2. approach the seam and converge to a completed seam zero.

There is no third option in locally uniform entire completion. In particular,
an off-seam zero cannot be annihilated invisibly in the interior of an open
half-sector.

## Rouché form

For a bounded domain (D) whose boundary contains no zero of (F), local
uniform convergence eventually gives

\[
|F_N-F|<|F|
\]

on the boundary. Rouché's theorem then fixes the number of zeros in (D),
counted with multiplicity. Thus any change of finite zero count must be
carried through the boundary before stabilization.

## Meaning for the theta programme

The claim that all-shell completion creates confinement now has an exact
kinematic contract. It must produce compact-wise horizontal collapse toward
the seam or transport every hostile zero to spectral infinity.

This does not prove RH. It identifies what an infinite-shell explanation must
actually explain. A source theorem should control zero transport without
learning zero locations from the scalar readout. Candidate forms include:

- a source-derived horizontal velocity law pointing toward the seam;
- a monotone quotient coordinate whose finite divisors can cross only toward
  the seam;
- a compact-wise lower bound obtained directly from Poisson or arithmetic
  sewing;
- a conservation law showing that any off-seam divisor charge is expelled to
  infinity under completion.

## Result

Completion is not a black-box repair operation. Under local uniform
convergence, finite off-seam zeros can be reconciled with completed seam
confinement only by seam collapse or escape to infinity. Stable bounded
off-seam zeros survive.

The next hard-to-vary target is therefore a source-derived divisor-transport
law for the completed theta approximation, not another positivity statement
for each finite truncation.

## Sharp falsifier

Find a source-authorized approximant sequence with off-seam zeros (z_N) that
remain a fixed positive distance from the seam and inside one compact set. Any
locally uniform limit then has an off-seam zero.
