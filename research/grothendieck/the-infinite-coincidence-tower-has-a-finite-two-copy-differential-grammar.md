# The infinite coincidence tower has a finite two-copy differential grammar

## Autocorrelation jets as matrix coefficients

Use the weighted pairing

\[
\langle g,h\rangle_w
=\int_0^\infty e^{-q}g(q)h(q)\,dq
\]

and let (L=\partial_q). For

\[
A(d)=\int_0^\infty e^{-q}f(q)f(q+d)\,dq,
\]

every coincidence derivative is exactly

\[
A^{(r)}(0)=\langle f,L^r f\rangle_w.
\]

Thus the infinite jet is not an unstructured list of new scalar ports. It is
the sequence of matrix coefficients generated by one operator acting on the
second source copy.

## Reflected generator and boundary control

Weighted integration by parts gives

\[
\langle g,Lh\rangle_w
=\langle(1-L)g,h\rangle_w-g(0)h(0).
\]

Therefore moving the derivative between the two copies requires exactly:

1. the forward generator (1\otimes L);
2. the reflected generator ((1-L)\otimes1); and
3. the endpoint trace \(\mathrm{ev}_0\otimes\mathrm{ev}_0\).

This is the finite grammar behind the infinite asymptotic tower. The endpoint
term is not an optional counterterm; it is the defect of adjoint transport.

## Theta closure

The completed theta source already has the exact linear scale-derivative
closure

\[
D\,\operatorname{span}\{H_1,M_0,\ldots,M_k\}
\subseteq
\operatorname{span}\{H_1,M_0,\ldots,M_{k+1}\},
\]

where (D=t\partial_t). Only one primitive heat line (H_1) lies outside the
oriented completed moment tower. Equivalently, the linear source is a cyclic
heat module augmented by one primitive generator.

The autocorrelation tower is consequently its two-copy matrix-coefficient
module. It remains infinite-dimensional, but its constructors are finite:
the two commuting scale actions, their affine adjoint reflection, the
primitive heat generator on either copy, and the endpoint trace.

## Correction to the previous frontier

The preceding result correctly found an unbounded Sobolev carrier, but the
phrase “another Sobolev channel at every order” must not be read as infinitely
many independent constructors. The source theorem compresses their provenance
to a finite differential grammar. What remains unproved is not closure of the
jet data. It is orientation of the resulting two-copy module after reciprocal
sewing.

This also identifies the faithful replacement for a scalar coincidence
counterterm: retain the two-copy heat module and its boundary evaluation map,
then perform Fourier--Tate sewing before taking matrix coefficients. Scalar
subtraction after the cosine transform loses the adjoint relation that
generated the endpoint defect.

## New falsifier

The next candidate conservation law must be equivariant for both scale
generators and must reproduce

\[
L_w^*=1-L
\]

with the exact endpoint trace. It fails if it:

- treats the two derivative actions as one before sewing;
- deletes the primitive heat line;
- represents the endpoint trace by a fitted scalar counterterm; or
- obtains positivity only after scalar aggregation.

The RH-bearing question is now narrower: does reciprocal theta sewing orient
this finitely generated two-copy differential module, including its primitive
and endpoint generators?
