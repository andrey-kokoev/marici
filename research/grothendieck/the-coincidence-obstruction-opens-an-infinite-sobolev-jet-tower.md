# The coincidence obstruction opens an infinite Sobolev jet tower

Let

\[
A(d)=\int_0^\infty e^{-q}f(q)f(q+d)\,dq,
\qquad
W_x(d)=A(d)e^{-d/2}\frac{\sinh(xd)}{x}.
\]

The negative high-frequency tail of the reciprocal Green forcing is governed
by the odd endpoint derivatives of (W_x). Those derivatives are not merely
scalar artifacts: they successively expose source boundary data and weighted
Sobolev energies.

## First three endpoint coefficients

Write

\[
A_0=A(0)=\int_0^\infty e^{-q}f(q)^2\,dq,
\qquad
D_1=\int_0^\infty e^{-q}f'(q)^2\,dq.
\]

Integration by parts gives

\[
A'(0)=\frac{A_0-f(0)^2}{2},
\]

and

\[
A''(0)=-f(0)f'(0)+A'(0)-D_1.
\]

Combining these with the half-density and hyperbolic factors yields

\[
W_x'(0)=A_0,
\qquad
W_x''(0)=-f(0)^2,
\]

and

\[
W_x'''(0)
=A_0\left(x^2+\frac34\right)-3f(0)f'(0)-3D_1.
\]

Thus the first spectral obstruction is the bulk norm (A_0); the next
endpoint coefficient is the seam-value square; and the following odd
coefficient already contains the first Sobolev energy (D_1).

## Full asymptotic tower

Repeated integration by parts gives, whenever the required derivatives are
integrable,

\[
\int_0^\infty W_x(d)\cos(yd)\,dd
\sim
\sum_{j\ge0}\frac{(-1)^{j+1}W_x^{(2j+1)}(0)}{y^{2j+2}}.
\]

Successive odd derivatives of the autocorrelation introduce higher weighted
derivative energies of (f), together with endpoint jets. The scalar defect
therefore presents an entire source Sobolev tower at infinity.

## Consequence

Adding one scalar coincidence counterterm can remove the leading
(-A_0/y^2) term, but it only exposes the next odd endpoint coefficient. A
finite boundary repair cannot be claimed complete without a source theorem
closing the remaining tower. The natural faithful object is the full jet or
graph-norm packet generated by (f,f',f'',\ldots), with its endpoint traces;
the scalar cosine transform is its decategorified asymptotic readout.

This explains why the earlier finite-port architectures repeatedly left one
new residual. The residual was not a single missing wall. It was the first
visible face of an unbounded regularity tower.

The next sharp question is whether the theta heat equation closes this tower
to a finite labelled recurrence. If it does, the repair is source-derived. If
it does not, the correct carrier is genuinely infinite and any finite-rung
zero-confinement proof is structurally incomplete.
