# Theta score shear is the Mellin-label connection

## Question

The seam-matched score residual contains a forced \(\log n\) term after
theta labels are translated to one primitive profile.  Is that term merely a
suggestive arithmetic weight, or is it the action of a source-authorized
operator?

## Label synthesis and Mellin generator

Let

\[
(Tc)(u)
=
\sum_{n\ge1}c_n n^{-1/2}\phi_1(u+\log n)
\]

on finite labelled packets.  Mellin translation acts on the coefficient
module by

\[
(U_tc)_n=n^{it}c_n.
\]

Its infinitesimal generator is the diagonal label operator

\[
(Qc)_n=\log(n)c_n.
\]

This operator is fixed by the existing vertical spectral action; it is not
introduced to repair a collision.

## Exact connection identity

Fix the theta seam curvature

\[
A_0=-\frac{\Phi''(0)}{2\Phi(0)}
\]

and define the primitive score residual

\[
r_1(v)=\phi_1'(v)+2A_0v\phi_1(v).
\]

Let

\[
(Sc)(u)
=
\sum_{n\ge1}c_n n^{-1/2}r_1(u+\log n).
\]

Direct substitution gives the exact intertwining law

\[
Sc
=
\left(\partial_u+2A_0u\right)Tc
+2A_0TQc.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
S
=
\left(\partial_u+2A_0u\right)T+2A_0TQ.
\]

For the physical all-label packet \(c=\mathbf1\), the common-frame residual
is therefore

\[
r
=
S\mathbf1-2A_0TQ\mathbf1.
\]

The logarithmic shear is exactly the connection term required when the
label-centred coordinate \(u+\log n\) is compared with the common seam
coordinate \(u\).

## Geometric meaning

The source naturally carries two commuting directions:

- translation in the archimedean coordinate \(u\);
- Mellin modulation generated by \(Q\) on the arithmetic labels.

The primitive Gaussian frame differentiates along their diagonal
combination.  The physical seam frame differentiates only along \(u\), so
the difference is the label connection \(2A_0Q\).

This realizes the previously suspected quarter-turn in viewpoint: theta is
not a scalar sum with decorative labels.  It is a matrix coefficient of a
two-direction transport system, and scalar aggregation hides the connection
term that distinguishes the two directions.

## Real obstacle: the connection is flat

On finite packets,

\[
[\partial_u,Q]=0.
\]

Therefore this bare two-direction connection has zero curvature.  The exact
identity explains the origin and typing of the logarithmic shear, but it does
not orient its oscillatory sine transform.  Every translated profile family
admits the same flat identity.

Consequently the next RH-bearing datum cannot be the Mellin generator alone.
It must involve another source operation.  The available candidates are:

- Fourier--Tate reflection, which reverses the valuation orientation;
- the modular tail projection, whose boundary changes under label transport;
- primitive and prime-square current extraction, which does not commute with
  unrestricted label aggregation.

The useful curvature must therefore be a commutator or braid residual between
the Mellin connection and one of those completion-sensitive operations.

## The ordinary tail split also remains flat

Let \(P_N\) retain labels \(n\le N\), and let \(P_N^\perp\) retain the exact
omitted label coordinate.  Because all three operators are diagonal in the
same label basis,

\[
[P_N,Q]=0,
\qquad
[P_N^\perp,Q]=0.
\]

Thus adjoining the tail as an independent port is necessary for faithful
completion, but the split itself creates no curvature.  This rules out a
second tempting shortcut: the logarithmic shear does not become oriented
merely because the omitted labels have been retained exactly.

The nontrivial operation must mix the primal and dual presentations.  If
\(W\) is Fourier--Tate sewing between their correctly oriented label spaces,
its interior covariance should have the typed form

\[
Q_-W+WQ_+=0.
\]

This is generator reversal, not a nonzero bulk commutator.  Hence any useful
residual must be supported where the sewing domains, cutoffs, or boundary
currents fail to match.  The programme is forced back to a boundary cocycle,
now with an explicit generator insertion.

## Next theorem and falsifier

Let \(W_N\) denote finite-cutoff primal--dual sewing with every primitive,
prime-square, and archimedean boundary coordinate retained.  The next source
calculation is the typed anomaly

\[
\mathcal A_N=Q_-W_N+W_NQ_+.
\]

The desired result would show that \(\mathcal A_N\) is entirely a named
boundary current and express the phase-aligned score readout \(R_L\) as one
of its matrix coefficients.

Two outcomes close the proposed route.  The first is exact covariance with no
boundary residual:

\[
\mathcal A_N=0.
\]

The second is a nonzero residual that cannot be expressed through the
source-declared boundary channels.  Only a nonzero, source-typed, and
completion-stable anomaly is a genuine curvature candidate.

## Result

The logarithmic theta shear is canonically the Mellin-label connection term.
This explains why scalar Gaussian models cannot see it.  The calculation also
finds the next honest obstacle: the unsewn connection is flat, so only its
boundary anomaly under primal--dual modular sewing can carry new orientation.
