# Local strict seam comparisons survive exactly when logarithmic defect is summable

## Operator stimulus

## Multiplicative reserve theorem

Suppose comparison stage `j` retains the fraction

\[
a_j=1-\rho_j,
\qquad 0<a_j\le1.
\]

After `N` stages the surviving reserve is

\[
R_N=\prod_{j=1}^N a_j.
\]

Taking logarithms gives the exact additive defect

\[
D_N=-\log R_N
=
\sum_{j=1}^N-\log(1-\rho_j).
\]

Therefore

\[
\inf_N R_N>0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\sum_{j=1}^\infty-\log(1-\rho_j)<\infty.
\]

For `rho_j` tending to zero, this is equivalent to summability of the local
losses `rho_j`.

## Exact harmonic hostile

Take

\[
\rho_j=\frac1{j+1}.
\]

Every stage is strictly contractive, since `rho_j<1`, but

\[
R_N
=
\prod_{j=1}^N\frac{j}{j+1}
=
\frac1{N+1}
\longrightarrow0.
\]

The accumulated logarithmic defect is `log(N+1)` and diverges. Finite strict
gaps therefore coexist with complete loss of the global reserve.
