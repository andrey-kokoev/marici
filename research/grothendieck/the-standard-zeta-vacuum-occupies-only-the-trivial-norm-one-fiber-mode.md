# The standard zeta vacuum occupies only the trivial norm-one fiber mode

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-26

Status: exact candidate-channel collapse

## Local unit invariance

At a finite prime, the standard unramified vacuum is

\[
\phi_p=\mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p}.
\]

For every local unit (u\in\mathbb Z_p^\times), multiplication preserves
(mathbb Z_p), so

\[
\phi_p(ux)=\phi_p(x).
\]

At the real place, the completed Gaussian descendant is even and therefore
invariant under the sign subgroup.

## Global norm-one fiber

For (mathbb Q), class number one identifies the compact norm-one
idele-class fiber with the finite unit ideles modulo the diagonal sign. The
standard factorizable theta/Tate state

\[
\Phi_{\mathbb A}
=f_\infty\otimes\bigotimes_p'\mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p}
\]

is invariant under this entire compact fiber.

Equivalently, its decomposition under characters of
(C_{\mathbb Q}^{1}) has only the trivial component. Nontrivial components
would lead toward twisted Hecke or Dirichlet sectors, not the standard
Riemann zeta vacuum.

## Consequence

Ledger 3073 correctly identifies a large kernel erased by radial Mellin
compression on the ambient adelic source space. But the distinguished zeta
state itself lies entirely in the invariant line of that fiber. Restoring all
norm-one modes therefore adds ambient capacity without adding an activated
comparison channel for the Riemann section.

The proposed fifth wall collapses on the physical source:

- the ambient fiber is real;
- radial compression is nonfaithful on general packets;
- the standard vacuum has no nontrivial angular component to lose.

Thus the missing RH force cannot come merely from decomposing the zeta vacuum
into norm-one idele characters.

## What remains before Mellin compression

The rational diagonal boundary is not just a vector in the vacuum's compact
fiber representation. It is a distributional incidence relation between the
additive adelic source and its quotient. That relation can remain nontrivial
even when the vacuum is fiber-invariant.

The next source-level Green candidate must therefore involve the pair

\[
(\Phi_{\mathbb A},\Delta_{\mathbb Q})
\]

and the way dilation moves their incidence, rather than the representation
content of (Phi_{\mathbb A}) alone.

This returns us to the relational principle in its sharpest form: neither the
state nor the boundary separately supplies the missing channel. Only their
moving comparison can.

## Falsifier and scope

The collapse would fail if the standard local vacuum transformed under a
nontrivial unit character, or if the real completion were sign-odd. Neither
is true.

The result does not show that every source-authorized perturbation is
fiber-trivial. It classifies the standard untwisted Riemann source.

## Verification

The checker verifies local-unit preservation on finite prime-power models,
real sign invariance, and the disappearance of all nontrivial characters of
a constant finite fiber vector.
