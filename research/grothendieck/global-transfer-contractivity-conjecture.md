# Global transfer contractivity is the sharpened RH-strength conjecture

Suppose a determinant-class holomorphic family `K(s)` and a nonvanishing
completed local factor `E(s)` satisfy

```
xi(s)=E(s) det[I-K(s)],                                (1)
K(1-s) is reflection-equivalent to K(s),               (2)
K(1/2+iT)=C(T)*C(T).                                   (3)
```

The critical-line restriction is then Hermitian and nonnegative at the
operator level. Zeros on the line occur when a singular value of `C(T)`
reaches one.

The decisive off-line condition is

```
spectral_radius K(s)<1       when Re(s)!=1/2.          (4)
```

Under (1)--(4), `I-K(s)` is invertible off the critical line, so every
nontrivial zero of `xi` lies on that line. This is a conditional RH theorem.

## Why (4) is explanatory rather than cosmetic

Condition (4) says the prime--archimedean feedback loop is strictly lossy
away from reflection balance and can reach unit gain only on the fixed line.
It translates RH into a stability statement for one global correspondence:

```
off line: strict contraction;
on line: Hermitian threshold resonance.                (5)
```

This is more mechanistic than asserting that an already-zero-derived
operator is self-adjoint. But constructing `K` and proving (4) remain
RH-strength tasks.

## Hostile symmetry test

Reflection symmetry and critical-line adjoint pairing do not imply (4).
Write `w=s-1/2` and take the scalar real-coefficient family

```
c(w)=w^2+sqrt(2)w+2,
F(w)=1-c(w)c(-w)
    =-(w^4+2w^2+3).                                   (6)
```

On the critical line `w=iT`, `c(-w)=conj(c(w))`, so

```
F(iT)=1-|c(iT)|^2.                                    (7)
```

Nevertheless its zeros satisfy

```
w^2=-1+-i sqrt(2),                                    (8)
```

giving a genuine off-line quartet. Thus the exact symmetry/Hermitian
architecture still needs an independent contractivity theorem.

## Best current long-horizon conjecture

There exists a canonical prime--oscillator transfer correspondence `K(s)`,
built from the relative divisor/difference system and the even harmonic
oscillator, such that:

1. its regularized determinant gives completed `xi`;
2. reflection becomes the adjoint operation on the fixed line;
3. it is strictly contractive off the line; and
4. unit singular-value crossings reproduce Xi zeros and multiplicities.

Each clause has a direct falsifier. This is now the most concentrated form
of the Deutsch--Popperian conjecture in this research lane.
