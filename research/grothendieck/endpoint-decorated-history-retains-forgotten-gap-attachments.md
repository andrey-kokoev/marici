# Endpoint-decorated history retains forgotten-gap attachments

## Result

Constructed a faithful marked-history extension of the retained arithmetic path source. Each forgotten event remains an actual typed arithmetic arrow. It is suppressed only by the later coefficient projection.

The marked lift preserves composition and the full source path-cut coproduct exactly. It has an explicit linear left inverse, and every marked history has a unique gap decomposition retaining all intermediate endpoints. Its coefficient projection recovers the existing tensor signature on all 168 admissible source paths.

This separates two claims that previously risked being conflated:

- the decorated history preserves the source path and its cuts;
- the coefficient-only record requires forgotten-gap localization to recover its own deconcatenation.

## 1. The typed source

Use the free retained prime-path category P of the four-prime cube. Objects are the sixteen source masks, with integer labels n=2 times the product of their primes. An elementary arrow e:n->np adds one unused prime.

Parallel orders remain different paths even when their final integer agrees. We do not impose the commutative-terminal quotient. This is the source convention used by the existing arithmetic history adapter.

Form a marked category D by replacing each elementary arrow with two parallel generators

e^0:n->np, e^1:n->np.

The superscript means forgotten or retained in the coefficient record. Both arrows have the same actual arithmetic source and target, and neither is an identity. D is the free path category on this marked graph.

A decorated history is a route together with one Boolean mark per event. Thus intermediate source labels are computed from the arithmetic transitions, not reconstructed from a terminal label.

## 2. Universal typed receiver

Given any category B, an assignment of its objects to source vertices and two composable receiver arrows b_e^0,b_e^1 for every source event extends uniquely to a functor D->B. The extension is ordered composition along the marked word. Uniqueness follows because every marked path is generated by its elementary arrows.

After rational linearization, the same statement holds for a Q-linear category B with linearly extended Hom maps. This is the relevant free universal property for the decorated carrier. It prescribes neither a metric nor a quotient equating forgotten arithmetic transitions with identities.

It is a universal construction on the declared retained source graph. It does not claim that the polymorphic stable closure operator, without this source realization, selects that graph or the two-mark observation convention.

## 3. Unique forgotten-gap normal form

Every marked word has a unique decomposition

g_0 e_1^1 g_1 ... e_k^1 g_k,

where each g_r is its complete, possibly empty, consecutive path of forgotten arrows. Each g_r retains the ordered primes, all intermediate integer labels, and its two endpoints. The e_r^1 are the retained events.

Rejoining these components at their matching endpoints recovers the original marked word exactly. The construction and inverse are implemented and tested.

On composing two normal forms, only the last forgotten gap of the first and the first forgotten gap of the second need to be joined. Their common arithmetic endpoint is retained. Associativity follows from typed path composition. Empty gaps are identity paths at specified vertices, not a single untyped scalar unit.

A gap is not replaced by the single integer quotient of its endpoints: doing that would again lose the order of its forgotten primes.

## 4. Additive marked lift of the original source

On the Q-linear path categories define the identity-on-objects functor L by

L(e)=e^0+e^1.

For a path w=e_1...e_n, bilinearity of composition gives

L(w)=sum_(M subset {1,...,n}) (w,M).

Thus the optional-event expansion is generated locally and multiplicatively. It is not assembled by a final-route lookup.

Define a second linear functor A by

A(e^0)=0, A(e^1)=e,

and by the identity on vertex identity arrows. Then A L=id. On a marked word, A keeps the all-retained term and kills every term containing a forgotten arrow.

This is a genuine split faithful lift on every linearized Hom space. It does not merely store a copy of the terminal integer. Notice that A(e^0)=0 is different from the later coefficient projection, which maps a forgotten event to coefficient 1.

Simply dropping the marks from every summand of L(w) would yield 2^n copies of w, not a left inverse. The explicit all-retained map avoids that multiplicity error.

## 5. Exact preservation of source cuts

For a typed path w, define its path coproduct by

Delta_P(w)=sum_(j=0)^n prefix_j tensor suffix_j,

with the common cut vertex retained in each summand. On decorated paths use the identical construction, restricting the mark sequence to each side. The counit is one on length-zero paths and zero on every nonempty path, including wholly forgotten paths.

Then

Delta_D L=(L tensor L) Delta_P.

Proof: both sides are indexed by a cut position j together with a mask on all n events. Restriction of the mask is a bijection to an independently chosen prefix mask and suffix mask. All endpoint labels agree. This argument applies to any number of cuts.

Coassociativity follows by indexing both iterated coproducts by pairs 0<=i<=j<=n. Repeated cuts give typed identity paths and are retained. The map A also respects cuts: a marked path with a forgotten event has that event in at least one factor of every cut, so every projected tensor vanishes; the all-retained case is the original path coproduct.

Consequently the source sits as a split subcoalgebra of the decorated path coalgebra, as well as having a split faithful linear compositional realization.

These are path-decomposition statements. They are not an assertion that arbitrary cofiber constructions commute with sums of morphisms.

## 6. Coefficient projection and the established tensor signature

For an elementary arithmetic event e:n->np, let v_e be its independently calculated chamber incidence vector. Define the coefficient evaluation on D by

P(e^0)=1, P(e^1)=v_e,

and extend by ordered tensor multiplication. A decorated word therefore evaluates to the tensor of its retained chamber vectors. Its gaps contribute no letters, but still exist upstairs.

This is a functor to the one-object coefficient category. It forgets the arithmetic object labels; it is not an algebra map sending every orthogonal vertex idempotent of a global path algebra to the same scalar identity. Alternatively, endpoint labels can be carried in separate matrix blocks, with their own composition rule.

The composite is

P L(w)=product_j(1+v_(e_j))=S_w.

It recovers exactly the established coefficient signature, while L itself preserves the full source cut structure.

The coefficient map P is not a path-coalgebra map. A forgotten one-event path has two source cuts and is sent to 1, which has one coefficient cut. The gap-localization theorem in the preceding packet supplies the corrected cut comparison only after passing to the marked coefficient realization.

This yields a precise factorization:

source path --split faithful L--> decorated history --P--> coefficient history.

The information loss occurs at P, not at L.

## 7. Concrete endpoint witness

From source label 2, mark both events forgotten in the routes (2,3) and (3,2). The decorated gaps retain respectively

2 -> 4 -> 12,

2 -> 6 -> 12.

Their source and terminal labels agree, but their gap histories are distinct. Both coefficient projections equal 1. No identification of their intermediate arithmetic objects has been introduced upstairs.

Thus even a wholly unobserved route retains its actual attachment data in the decorated carrier. This is precisely what the coefficient-only history could not supply.

## 8. Relation to full history and closure

The construction is defined on arbitrary finite words in any admitted source graph, not only length four. Every degree of the coefficient projection lands in the full tensor history constructed earlier; compatible finite projections follow from that coefficient target.

No inverses of forgotten arithmetic arrows have been added to D. The coefficient receiver may admit inverse comparison units without implying that the original directed prime arrows are invertible. An analytic or groupoidal realization requiring such inverses must supply its own admissibility theorem.

Within the retained free-path model, cutting and rejoining are now faithful before projection. The remaining main-operator comparison is to realize this decorated diagram in the selected stable closure category with its prescribed attachments. The present path-level result does not automatically establish that additional cofiber-level realization.

A productive next formal step is to verify L, A, their split identity, and the cut-intertwining bijection. This is a finite, explicit theorem independent of the unfinished analytic metric comparison.
