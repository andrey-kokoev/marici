# Scalar nullity does not annihilate prime-exclusion curvature

## Source-specific chamber candidate

Integer-label multiplication and partial division supply the exact positive
curvature

\[
[T_p^*,T_p]=P_{p\nmid n}.
\]

The family of all prime-exclusion projections is jointly faithful. This is a
genuine arithmetic invariant and rejects generic unlabelled rotations.

Could a scalar theta zero force the corresponding labelled state into the
common curvature kernel?

## Smallest exact obstruction

Use the two-label module with basis (e_1,e_2), scalar aggregation

\[
L(c_1,c_2)=c_1+c_2,
\]

and the prime-two exclusion projection

\[
P_{2\nmid n}=\operatorname{diag}(1,0).
\]

For

\[
c=(1,-1),
\]

we have

\[
Lc=0,
\qquad
\lVert P_{2\nmid n}c\rVert^2=1.
\]

Including prime three only strengthens the separation, since neither label is
divisible by three. Thus scalar cancellation does not imply annihilation of
even one exclusion-curvature channel, much less all of them.

## Consequence for the coherence tower

Arithmetic curvature and scalar incidence occupy different branches of the
tower:

- the scalar zero says the labelled packet lies in one aggregation
  hyperplane;
- exclusion curvature measures whether it lies in the intersection of
  infinitely many divisibility ranges.

There is no factorization from the scalar-null pullback into the common
curvature kernel using the existing aggregation map.

The required next rung is a source-derived Ward or Green coherence law whose
zero-state boundary condition forces

\[
P_{p\nmid n}c_z=0
\]

for every prime (p).

Joint faithfulness would then give (c_z=0), contradicting a nonzero
off-seam state. This would be a true arithmetic chamber theorem.

## Why this is not another positivity problem

The exclusion forms are already positive and jointly faithful. Nothing is
gained by proving stronger positivity of them. The missing information is the
coherence arrow from scalar boundary nullity to curvature-nullity.

It must arise before label aggregation and must retain primitive, square,
seam, and archimedean terms. A scalar identity assembled after summing labels
cannot recover the separate projections.

## Candidate identity

The useful theorem would have the schematic form

\[
\mathcal B_z(\Psi)
=
\sum_p w_p\lVert P_{p\nmid n}c_z\rVert^2
+\mathcal E_{\mathrm{other}}(\Psi),
\]

where every (w_p>0), the other term is nonnegative, and the completed
zero-state boundary conditions force (mathcal B_z=0) off the seam.

The right-hand side must be derived as a typed source identity. Defining it
from the desired projections would be circular.

## Falsifier

The two-label vector ((1,-1)) is the first rejection witness for any claim
that scalar cancellation alone controls exclusion curvature. For a proposed
completed Ward identity, the first nonzero labelled residual at any prime is
the decisive falsifier.

## Result

Prime-exclusion curvature is the strongest source-specific chamber invariant
currently available, but it is not yet connected to the theta divisor. The
next coherence rung is exactly that connection.
