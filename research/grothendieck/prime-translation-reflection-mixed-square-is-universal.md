# The prime-translation/reflection mixed square is universal

## Bounded question

Does the mixed coherence square between prime addition and Fourier--Tate
reflection produce a theta-specific residual capable of carrying the missing
conserved charge?

## Full-line square

On the logarithmic scale line define translation and reflection by

\[
(\tau_af)(q)=f(q+a),
\qquad
(Rf)(q)=f(-q).
\]

A direct calculation gives

\[
R\tau_aR=\tau_{-a},
\]

or equivalently

\[
R\tau_a=\tau_{-a}R.
\]

For prime transport, \(a=\log p\). Hence the mixed square between a prime
scale step and reciprocal reflection commutes exactly on the full logarithmic
line.

This is the dihedral relation for translations and reflection. It holds for
every function or distribution on which the operations are defined; no theta
source enters.

## Half-line compression

Let \(P_+\) denote restriction to the positive logarithmic chart. Translation
does not commute with this projection. For \(a>0\),

\[
([P_+,\tau_a]f)(q)
=-\mathbf1_{[-a,0)}(q)f(q+a).
\]

Thus compressing the full-line square produces a residual supported exactly on
the interval crossed by the moving seam. After reflection back to the positive
chart, this is the familiar finite endpoint interval \([0,a]\).

For \(a=\log p\), prime typing selects the endpoint length but does not alter
the residual formula.

## Universality of the seam cocycle

The compressed residual depends on the source values on the crossed interval,
but its existence and operator form depend only on:

- translation on the logarithmic line;
- reflection;
- half-line projection.

Any decaying hostile source has the same mixed-square residual. A self-Fourier
hostile source also satisfies the full-line reflection coherence. Therefore
neither flatness of the full square nor retention of the half-line seam term
distinguishes the theta source.

## Result

The first mixed coherence candidate is completely classified:

- before polarization, its residual is zero universally;
- after polarization, its residual is the universal crossed-interval seam
  cocycle.

It cannot supply the missing RH charge. This closes the proposal that prime
addition versus bare Fourier--Tate reflection alone contains a nontrivial
theta-specific curvature.

## Remaining source-specific possibility

A nontrivial mixed cell must include an operation beyond translation,
reflection, and chart restriction. The smallest remaining candidate is the
Gaussian heat generator together with discrete prime-power incidence. Its
square must compare

\[
g_n(k\log p)=g_{np^k}(0)
\]

against reciprocal sewing while retaining the label \(n\). Any residual that
survives after forgetting \(n\) is already covered by the universal seam
cocycle and has no new force.
