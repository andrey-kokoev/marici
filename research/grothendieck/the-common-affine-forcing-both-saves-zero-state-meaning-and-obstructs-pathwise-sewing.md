# The Common Affine Forcing Both Saves Zero-State Meaning and Obstructs Pathwise Sewing

## Two consequences of one source law

Fourier--Tate reflection gives the centered reciprocal flows in a common theta
frame:

\[
u'=-zu-f,
\qquad
v'=+zv-f,
\]

in the real specialization. The same source forcing enters both sectors with
the same forward incidence.

Let the completed scalar endpoint readout be

\[
X=u(0)+v(0).
\]

At a scalar zero, \(v(0)=-u(0)\). The affine endpoint energy is then

\[
|u(0)+f(0)|^2+|v(0)+f(0)|^2
=2|u(0)|^2+2|f(0)|^2>0.
\]

Thus the common forcing is exactly what prevents loss of the full reciprocal
state when its scalar augmentation vanishes.

## The same term blocks pathwise cancellation

Define common and relative modes

\[
P=u+v,
\qquad
Q=u-v.
\]

The source equations give

\[
P'=-zQ-2f,
\qquad
Q'=-zP.
\]

Scalar nullity supplies only \(P(0)=0\). It does not imply \(P(q)=0\) away
from the endpoint. Indeed, preserving \(P=0\) would require the additional
pointwise constraint

\[
zQ+2f=0.
\]

The affine forcing therefore has a precise dual role:

1. it prevents the lifted zero-state from disappearing;
2. it prevents the reciprocal endpoint cancellation from extending as a
   pathwise anti-diagonal state.

These are not competing interpretations. They are the same incidence viewed
at the boundary and in the bulk.

## Constructor obstruction

Fourier--Tate reflection reverses the spectral drift but transports the source
arrow forward in both sectors. It does not provide the contravariant mate

\[
B^*: \mathcal H\longrightarrow\mathcal U
\]

to the forcing arrow \(B:\mathcal U\to\mathcal H\). Consequently the native
reflected double has a faithful affine state but not a conservative adjoint
completion.

This locates the missing capability more sharply than a positivity request:
the programme needs a source-authorized variance reversal or lower incidence.
An endpoint condition cannot manufacture it, and another forward reflected
copy repeats the same obstruction.

## Deutschian explanation

Why does the scalar zero fail to mean destruction of the underlying object?
Because the source injects the same nonzero affine witness into both
perspectives. Why does that fact not already prove RH? Because a shared
forward witness is not an adjoint comparison: it preserves information but
does not conserve reciprocal flux.

The missing theorem is therefore operational:

> Construct the lower, variance-reversed incidence from labelled theta/Tate
> operations, or prove that no such authorized constructor exists.

At finite cutoff its falsifier remains the typed residual

\[
R_X^{\mathrm{adj}}=B_{-,X}-B_{+,X}^*.
\]

The zero-confinement lane cannot close while this residual is nonzero or
undefined.
