# Theta two-shell seam confinement requires nondecreasing outer weight

## Bounded question

What exact weight relation separates seam-confined from off-seam zeros in the
smallest positive reciprocal spectral model?

## Complete two-shell classification

Consider

\[
F_c(z)=\cosh z+c\cosh 2z,
\qquad
c>0.
\]

With (x=\cosh z), its zero equation is

\[
2cx^2+x-c=0,
\]

with roots

\[
x_\pm=\frac{-1\pm\sqrt{1+8c^2}}{4c}.
\]

The positive root always satisfies

\[
0<x_+<1.
\]

For the negative root,

\[
x_-\ge-1
\]

is equivalent to

\[
1+\sqrt{1+8c^2}\le4c.
\]

The right side first permits squaring at (c\ge1/4), and the squared
inequality reduces to

\[
c(c-1)\ge0.
\]

Since (c>0), the exact threshold is

\[
c\ge1.
\]

Therefore every zero of (F_c) lies on the imaginary seam exactly when
(c\ge1). At (c=1), the negative quotient root is the endpoint (x=-1).
For (0<c<1), one has (x_-<-1), producing the off-seam pair

\[
z=\pm\operatorname{arcosh}(-x_-)+(2k+1)i\pi.
\]

## Meaning of the threshold

In this smallest model, seam confinement requires the outer shell to carry at
least as much weight as the inner shell. Ordinary positive decay has the
opposite ordering. Thus positivity plus decreasing shell weights does not
merely fail to prove confinement; it points into the off-seam regime in the
first nontrivial reciprocal truncation.

The theta source can therefore succeed only through structure absent from an
isolated finite shell pair. Candidate mechanisms include:

- coherent contributions from the entire infinite shell tower;
- Gaussian or heat-flow relations coupling all shell derivatives;
- Poisson duality linking the tail to an independently retained boundary
  sector;
- arithmetic recursions whose repair currents cannot be represented by a
  finite positive truncation.

## Result

The two-shell model has an exact phase boundary at (c=1). Finite positive
shell dominance is not the theta explanation: for the natural decaying order
(c<1), off-seam zeros are forced.

Any successful RH mechanism must therefore be irreducibly global or must use
a source relation stronger than independent positive shell weighting. This
also warns against proving finite truncation positivity and extrapolating it
to the completed theta source; completion may create the confining coherence
rather than merely preserve a finite property.

## Sharp falsifier

For any claimed theorem based on positivity, reciprocal pairing, and
monotone-decaying shell weights, choose (0<c<1). The exact root (x_-<-1)
produces an off-seam zero.
