# The BSY integral is total diffuse divisor entropy

## Identification with a classical criterion

Let

\[
E_+(s)=\frac{s-1}{s}\zeta(s).
\]

This is the endpoint-reduced Hardy carrier in the right half-plane. It has
the source-fixed normalization

\[
E_+(1)=1,
\]

and on the critical seam its rational prefactor has modulus one. Therefore

\[
\log |E_+(\tfrac12+it)|
=
\log |\zeta(\tfrac12+it)|.
\]

Poisson--Jensen factorization in the half-plane gives the Balazard--Saias--Yor
identity

\[
\frac1{2\pi}
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\frac{\log |\zeta(\tfrac12+it)|}{\tfrac14+t^2}\,dt
=
\sum_{\Re\rho>1/2}
m_\rho\log\left|\frac{\rho}{1-\rho}\right|.
\]

The formula and the resulting equivalence with RH are classical. The present
packet contributes only their interpretation inside the divisor-supply and
decay-threshold programme.

## One positive scalar sees the whole forbidden cone

Write a right-sector zero as

\[
\rho=\frac12+a+i\gamma,
\qquad 0<a<\frac12.
\]

Its contribution is

\[
d(a,\gamma)
=
\frac12\log
\frac{(\tfrac12+a)^2+\gamma^2}
     {(\tfrac12-a)^2+\gamma^2}
>0.
\]

Consequently the BSY functional is faithful on the positive cone of diffuse
divisor supply: it vanishes exactly when that cone is empty. No cancellation
between distinct off-line zeros is possible.

For small horizontal displacement,

\[
d(a,\gamma)
=
\frac{a}{\gamma^2+\tfrac14}+O(a^3).
\]

Thus the criterion measures a height-weighted first horizontal moment of the
off-seam divisor. In the Fourier-resolution picture, `a` is Poisson time and
also the sub-threshold decay rate. The BSY quantity is therefore the total
positive entropy of forbidden Poisson evolution, as seen from the canonical
endpoint anchor.

## Why the infinite localizer tower collapses

The preceding decay-gap packet appeared to invite an infinite family of
localizers separating rates in `(0,1/2)` from declared rates beginning at
`1/2`. That is unnecessary at the divisor-measure level. Every forbidden
atom already has the same strict sign under `d(a,gamma)`, so one positive
functional separates the zero measure from every nonzero positive measure.

This collapse depends on retaining the positive divisor type. After an
untyped scalar Fourier aggregation, signed cancellations can obscure the
same fact.

## The exact remaining theorem

Poisson--Jensen supplies the unconditional inequality

\[
\mathcal D_{\mathrm{BSY}}\ge0.
\]

RH is the equality case. Therefore a source-side proof must derive the
opposite inequality

\[
\mathcal D_{\mathrm{BSY}}\le0
\]

from completed theta--Euler constructors without importing zero locations or
an equivalent positivity theorem. Together the two inequalities would force
vanishing.

This is a sharper Deutsch--Popperian target than another finite zero census:
identify a source law that forbids positive diffuse entropy. A hostile
symmetric multiplier inserting any off-line quartet must increase
`D_BSY` by an explicit positive amount and must fail that source law before
its zeros are inspected.

## Scope

The BSY identity and its equivalence with RH are known. This packet neither
proves the missing upper bound nor proves RH. It identifies the classical
criterion as the faithful scalarization of our positive diffuse divisor
measure and states the remaining source theorem without an infinite tower of
redundant tests.
