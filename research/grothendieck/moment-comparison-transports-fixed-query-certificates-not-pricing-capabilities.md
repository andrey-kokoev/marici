# Moment comparison transports fixed-query certificates, not pricing capabilities

An exact comparison between two orderings of the same append-only fine rows
transports a verified moment checkpoint without a new master solve. The source
chart, objective, frames, block decomposition and common base must be identical.
The predecessor checkpoint must already have been fully verified; matching its
serialized digest alone does not establish that premise.

The comparison replays both operation lists from the independently supplied
base, compares normalized rational row *multisets*, and checks the head against
the claimed source path. It reindexes each block's complete dual flow by actual
normalized graph-edge identity, including duplicate edges, then checks both
old and destination node balances and bound values. It rechecks all retained
source columns and the mixed optimal allocation. Exact equality of complete
row systems transfers the predecessor's global optimality (or infeasibility)
without rerunning the master. It does not equate path proof tips or authorize a
history from semantic equality.

The owning workload starts from the active U+V moment-frame fine-rebase control,
adds a second row in the other block and compares the two operation orders.
The second update is verified by the original rebase checker. Flow reindexing
and a round trip pass, and a fresh cold destination solve agrees on the optimum.
Missing or changed operations fail. A process-local service additionally requires
an opaque external owner grant bound to the event, base, source path and
destination path. Foreign grants, stale handles, revoked grants and comparisons
after backend detachment fail without changing the head. Saved-head replay
continues after detachment.

This is a **fixed-query** transport rule. It does not provide fresh-objective
pricing or archive re-exposure. Detachment makes that operational difference
explicit. The test owner models authorization rather than authenticating an
external archive; a caller-supplied serialized head is not an import protocol.
The comparison still performs graph reconstruction, row matching, flow checking
and column admission: zero new master solves does not mean zero new work.

The result is an exact edge of the proposed enriched diagram, not its complete
resource-decorated cone. In particular, certifying replacement pricing providers,
general polyhedral equivalence, and coherent continuation for new objectives
remain open.
