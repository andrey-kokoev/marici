# Dyadic theta refinement is contractive exactly in the unresolved half-strip

Status: exact modular reduction; positivity consequence not yet proved

For `u>=0`, write the completed theta kernel as its positive labelled sum

\[
 \Phi(u)=\sum_{n\ge1}T_n(u),                          \tag{1}
\]

where

\[
 T_n(u)=
 \left(4\pi^2n^4e^{9u/2}-6\pi n^2e^{5u/2}\right)
 e^{-\pi n^2e^{2u}}.                                 \tag{2}
\]

Every term is positive on the half-line because
`pi n^2 exp(2u)>3/2`.

## Exact even--odd refinement

Put `ell=log 2`.  Direct substitution in (2) gives

\[
 \boxed{T_{2m}(u)=2^{-1/2}T_m(u+\ell).}               \tag{3}
\]

Consequently, if

\[
 \Phi_{\rm odd}(u)=\sum_{n\ge1,\ n\text{ odd}}T_n(u)>0,
\]

then

\[
 \boxed{
 \Phi(u)=\Phi_{\rm odd}(u)+2^{-1/2}\Phi(u+\ell),
 \qquad u\ge0.
 }                                                     \tag{4}
\]

Iteration, with the superexponential remainder tending to zero, yields

\[
 \Phi(u)=\sum_{k\ge0}2^{-k/2}
 \Phi_{\rm odd}(u+k\ell).                            \tag{5}
\]

This is the labelled decomposition by the `2`-adic valuation of the theta
index.  Unlike positivity, evenness, or one-fold geometry, it is absent from
a generic positive source.

## The refinement multiplier knows the critical-strip width

For a half-line Fourier--Laplace phase `e^{izu}`, shifting by `k ell` produces
the transfer factor

\[
 q(z)^k,
 \qquad
 q(z)=2^{-1/2}e^{-iz\ell}.                            \tag{6}
\]

For `z=x+iy`,

\[
 \boxed{|q(z)|=2^{y-1/2}.}                            \tag{7}
\]

Therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 |q(z)|<1
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad y<1/2.
 }                                                     \tag{8}
\]

The unresolved RH domain is `0<y<1/2`; outside it, positivity of the normal
modulus current already follows from the classical critical-strip theorem.
Thus the source's dyadic scale transport is strictly contractive on exactly
the remaining domain and becomes unit-modulus at its outer boundary.

The constants are forced together: `2^{-1/2}` is the amplitude covariance of
the even theta labels, while `ell=log 2` is their logarithmic displacement.
Their product gives the boundary exponent `1/2` without fitting zero data.

## Stopped geometric transport formula

Let

\[
 F_+(z)=\int_0^\infty\Phi(u)e^{izu}\,du.
\]

Using (5), shifting `v=u+k ell`, and exchanging the absolutely convergent
sum and integral gives

\[
 \boxed{
 F_+(z)=\int_0^\infty
 \Phi_{\rm odd}(v)e^{izv}
 \sum_{0\le k\le v/\ell}q(z)^k\,dv.
 }                                                     \tag{9}
\]

The inner factor is the stopped geometric packet

\[
 G_{N(v)}(q)=\frac{1-q^{N(v)+1}}{1-q},
 \qquad N(v)=\lfloor v/\ell\rfloor.                  \tag{10}
\]

For the unresolved strip it is generated by a strict contraction.  This is a
source-derived discrete evolution in scale, not a postulated operator.

## What remains

Contractivity of `q` alone does not imply the Schur inequality for

\[
 \Theta(z)=\frac{X(z)-iX'(z)}{X(z)+iX'(z)}.
\]

The stopped packets have phases, and the terminal index `N(v)` is correlated
with the positive odd-label innovation.  The required theorem must show that
this causal scale recursion makes the full one-fold transform
`E=X+iX'` outer/stable in the upper half-strip, or directly factor
`1-|Theta|^2` as accumulated scale loss.

The ideal identity would have the system-theoretic form

\[
 |E(z)|^2-|E^*(z)|^2
 =(1-|q(z)|^2)\,\mathcal E(z),
 \qquad \mathcal E(z)>0,                             \tag{11}
\]

possibly plus a source-fixed terminal boundary term.  Since

\[
 1-|q(z)|^2=1-2^{2y-1}>0                              \tag{12}
\]

on the exact target strip, such a factorization would prove Clark admission
and RH.

The sharp falsifier is algebraic: after substituting (9) into the
denominator-free current, if the residual cross-scale form has an
uncontrolled negative direction not absorbed by the loss factor (12), the
dyadic-passivity mechanism fails.  Finite scale checks cannot settle that
question; the next move is an exact telescoping/Stein identity for the
stopped geometric packets.

## Faithful two-channel transfer

For each theta label retain its cosine and sine transforms together:

\[
 V_n(z)=
 \binom{\int_0^\infty T_n(u)\cos(zu)du}
 {\int_0^\infty T_n(u)\sin(zu)du},
\quad
 B_n(z)=
 \binom{\int_0^\ell T_n(u)\cos(zu)du}
 {\int_0^\ell T_n(u)\sin(zu)du}.                     \tag{13}
\]

Shifting (3) gives the exact recursion

\[
 \boxed{V_{2n}(z)=\mathsf T(z)[V_n(z)-B_n(z)]},       \tag{14}
\]

where

\[
 \mathsf T(z)=2^{-1/2}
 \begin{pmatrix}
 \cos(z\ell)&\sin(z\ell)\\
 -\sin(z\ell)&\cos(z\ell)
 \end{pmatrix}.                                      \tag{15}
\]

The fixed exponential-channel basis diagonalizes this matrix.  At
`z=x+iy`, its singular values are

\[
 \boxed{
 \sigma_+(\mathsf T)=2^{y-1/2},
 \qquad \sigma_-(\mathsf T)=2^{-y-1/2}.
 }                                                     \tag{16}
\]

Hence

\[
 \boxed{\|\mathsf T(z)\|<1\iff |y|<1/2.}             \tag{17}
\]

Both reciprocal exponential channels contract precisely in the full
classical critical strip.  At either boundary one becomes lossless while the
other remains strictly contractive.

The compact vector `B_n` is the source-fixed innovation injected whenever a
dyadic descendant is shifted through the half-line boundary.  It cannot be
dropped.  The revised target is a Stein balance

\[
 \|V_n\|_P^2-\|V_{2n}\|_P^2
 =Q_n(z)+\text{telescoping boundary polarization},    \tag{18}
\]

with `P>0` and `Q_n>=0`, whose sum over odd roots and all dyadic descendants
is exactly `|E|^2-|E^*|^2`.  The strict contraction supplies the bulk loss;
the remaining sign is concentrated in the exact innovation polarization.
