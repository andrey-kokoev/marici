# The Third Seam Jet Reopens After First-Jet Repair

## Repaired reference

Recall

\[
R(u)=\phi_1(u)\left(1-\beta q(u)\right),
\qquad
q(u)=\frac{1-e^{-2u}}2,
\]

where

\[
\beta=\frac{\phi_1'(0)}{\phi_1(0)}.
\]

The construction gives \(R'(0)=0\).

## Exact third derivative

Write

\[
g(u)=\log\phi_1(u).
\]

At the seam,

\[
g'(0)=\beta,
\]

\[
g'''(0)
=
\frac{48\pi(2\pi+3)}{(2\pi-3)^3}
-
8\pi.
\]

Since

\[
q'(0)=1,
\qquad
q''(0)=-2,
\qquad
q'''(0)=4,
\]

the product rule initially gives

\[
R'''(0)
=
\phi_1'''(0)
-
\beta
\left(
3\phi_1''(0)-6\phi_1'(0)+4\phi_1(0)
\right).
\]

Substituting the logarithmic derivatives cancels every \(g''(0)\) term:

\[
\frac{R'''(0)}{\phi_1(0)}
=
g'''(0)
-
2\beta^3
+
6\beta^2
-
4\beta.
\]

The right side is strictly positive. In particular,

\[
R'''(0)\ne0.
\]

## Repair escalation

Let \(E=\Phi-R\). Completed evenness gives

\[
\Phi'''(0)=0.
\]

Therefore

\[
E'''(0)=-R'''(0).
\]

After first-jet repair, the value-small remainder again carries the entire
opposite boundary correction at the next odd derivative.

Repeated integration by parts exposes this channel at order \(x^{-4}\).
Thus first-jet matching repairs the leading seam asymptotic but does not
produce a globally stable high-frequency perturbation.

## Structural conclusion

Boundary type is an odd-jet tower:

\[
f'(0),\quad f'''(0),\quad f^{(5)}(0),\ldots
\]

The completed source annihilates every rung because it is even. A positive
primitive reference does not inherit that property after finitely many
repairs unless an additional theorem closes the entire germ.

This gives the precise form of the next wall:

- bulk pointwise dominance is one channel;
- completed seam-germ parity is another;
- oscillatory high-frequency transport reads both.

A finite jet packet may certify a bounded frequency range, but an all-frequency
argument needs an even-germ reference or a remainder norm that controls the
whole odd boundary tower.

## Candidate exact completion

The formal evenization

\[
\frac{\phi_1(u)+\phi_1(-u)}2
\]

kills every odd seam derivative at once. But the reflected primitive profile
is not positive on the entire positive chamber, so this operation does not
preserve the positive perturbative architecture. The modularly completed
all-label source achieves both evenness and positivity; that coexistence is
the nontrivial source property the finite repair tower was approximating.

## Falsifier

The third-jet result is falsified if the displayed exact expression vanishes.
A proposed finite seam repair fails globally if its remainder cancels the
first unmatched odd derivative while that derivative is omitted from the
controlling norm.
