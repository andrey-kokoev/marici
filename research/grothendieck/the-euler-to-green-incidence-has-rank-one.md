# The Euler-to-Green incidence has rank one

Scope correction: this rank-one theorem applies after passage through the
even scalar source evaluation. The full source-authorized boundary module has
an additional odd Mellin-position response, derived in the successor packet.

## Typed source path

The primitive, prime-square, and connected-tail Euler currents inhabit a
labelled arithmetic boundary module. They do not inhabit the archimedean Green
state space. Their authorized interaction with that space is the composite

\[
E_\theta'
\xrightarrow{B_f^\times}
\mathbb C
\xrightarrow{B_f}
E_\theta
\xrightarrow{R_s}
H^1(\mathbb R_+),
\]

where

\[
B_f^\times(\nu)=\nu(f),
\qquad
B_f(c)=cf,
\qquad
R_s(f)=G_s.
\]

Therefore the complete Euler-to-Green map is

\[
T_s(\nu)=\nu(f)G_s.
\]

Its image is contained in the single line spanned by \(G_s\).

## Filtered current packet

Write the current packet as

\[
\boldsymbol\nu=(\nu_1,\nu_2,\nu_{\geq3},\nu_\infty).
\]

Even if the four evaluations are retained before aggregation, propagation by
the same source incidence gives

\[
T_s(\boldsymbol\nu)
=
\left(
\nu_1(f)+\nu_2(f)+\nu_{\geq3}(f)+\nu_\infty(f)
\right)G_s.
\]

The map has rank at most one. Its kernel contains every nonzero labelled
packet whose evaluated coefficients sum to zero.

## Meaning of the kernel

These kernel packets are not nonexistent. They retain arithmetic provenance,
regularity type, and Fourier-orbit information. But the declared analytic
system cannot distinguish them: they produce the same Green state as the zero
packet.

This is marginal incompleteness of the scalar control port. It is not failure
of local tomography, because no complete family of independent
source-authorized product testers coupling the arithmetic labels to distinct
analytic responses has yet been constructed.

## Consequence for zero confinement

Retaining primitive and square currents prevents an ill-typed deletion at
completion. It does not by itself add a new analytic comparison coordinate.
Under the present incidence, every arithmetic refinement changes only the
scalar coefficient of the same tail state.

Thus the labelled boundary module supplies provenance but no additional RH
force until the source construction produces a higher-rank response map

\[
\nu\longmapsto
\bigl(\nu(f_0),\nu(f_1),\ldots\bigr)
\]

with independently derived test histories \(f_j\), or an operator-valued
incidence that does not factor through one scalar control line.

## Minimal new gate

Within the scalar quotient, the smallest possible advance is a second
source-derived response \(f_1\)
such that the two evaluation rows

\[
\nu\longmapsto \nu(f),
\qquad
\nu\longmapsto \nu(f_1)
\]

are not proportional on the primitive/square/tail packet, and such that both
responses enter the same completed Green identity. A derivative or Clark
tangent already determined by the autocorrelation tower does not qualify.

## Falsifier

For any proposed arithmetic repair, form its incidence matrix from typed
Euler currents to analytic response states. If all columns are multiples of
one Green history, the repair has rank one and cannot recover information in
the evaluation kernel. A claimed new conservation law must exhibit the first
nonzero minor of this matrix before scalar aggregation.
