# The one-way tail cannot be a positive skew-adjoint quotient

## Why the mate cell does not yet close the construction

Given positive pairings, the adjoint mate of

\[
p:F\longrightarrow Y
\]

is (p^*:Y\to F), and the block

\[
K=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&p\\
-p^*&0
\end{pmatrix}
\]

is skew-adjoint. But this adds backreaction to the forcing coordinate. The
original homogeneous tail augmentation has a constant forcing channel and a
one-way nilpotent incidence

\[
N=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&p\\
0&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad N^2=0,quad N\ne0.
\]

Can an enlarged positive skew-adjoint system recover (N) by an exact
operator quotient?

## Quotient no-go theorem

Let (K) be a finite-dimensional skew-adjoint operator on a positive Hilbert
space, and let

\[
\pi K=N\pi
\]

for a surjective linear map (pi). Then (ker\pi) is (K)-invariant.
Because (K) is normal, every invariant subspace is reducing. Hence the
induced quotient operator (N) is itself skew-adjoint in the quotient metric.

A skew-adjoint finite-dimensional operator is diagonalizable with imaginary
spectrum. A nonzero nilpotent operator is not diagonalizable. Therefore the
one-way tail incidence (N) cannot be such a quotient.

## Meaning for the coherence tower

The adjoint-mate 2-cell supplies positive conservative dynamics only by
changing the source equation. No finite bounded Hilbert extension can both:

1. be skew-adjoint and positive;
2. retain the reverse mate dynamically;
3. project exactly onto the original nonzero triangular tail generator.

Thus “add the mate and quotient away its backreaction” is not a legitimate
repair.

## Falsifier

Any proposed finite positive dilation must exhibit its surjective recovery
map (pi). If it satisfies (pi K=Npi), the theorem forces (N) to be
skew-adjoint and therefore contradicts the nonzero nilpotent tail incidence.
If it recovers only scalar transfer data, it must be typed as a colligation,
not claimed as an operator quotient.
