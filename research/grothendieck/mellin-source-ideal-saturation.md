## Why the closure quotient is insufficient

CCM Proposition 3.6 states that multiplication by the spectral coordinate on
a quotient of the Hadamard topological ring has spectrum equal to the zeta
zero set. It does not state the local algebraic multiplicity of that quotient.
The earlier Connes--Marcolli discussion explicitly presents the polynomial
ideal picture as motivation and notes that function-space realizations depend
on topology.

Therefore the spectral-set theorem alone cannot authorize the local
\`O_lambda/(Xi)\` jet algebras used in Ledgers 1402--1408.

## Canonical operator-invariant saturation

Let \`B=H_<=1\` be the Hadamard ring of entire functions of order at most one,
and let \`R_E\` be the linear span of the multiplicative Fourier images of the
explicit CCM source family.

Proposition 3.6 proves two facts:

1. every element of \`R_E\` is \`P Xi\` for a polynomial \`P\`;
2. every polynomial multiple of Xi occurs, including \`Xi\` itself.

A quotient carrying multiplication by arbitrary spectral functions must kill
the multiplier-invariant ideal generated by the source range:

\`I_E=B R_E\`.

The two facts immediately give both inclusions:

- \`R_E subset (Xi)\`, hence \`I_E subset (Xi)\`;
- \`Xi in R_E\`, hence \`(Xi) subset I_E\`.

Thus

\`I_E=(Xi)\`.

This equality is algebraic and independent of the closure topology.
Multiplier saturation is not a fitted determinant condition: it is the
smallest ideal containing the source range, hence the smallest quotient on
which the full spectral multiplier algebra acts.

## Exact local divisor

Define the source-saturated quotient

\`Q_E=B/I_E=B/(Xi)\`.

At every zero \`lambda\`, localization gives the length-\`m_lambda\` algebra

\`(Q_E)_lambda=O_lambda/(Xi)\`,

where \`m_lambda=ord_lambda(Xi)\`. The maximal-ideal filtration, residue
pairing, associated-graded semisimplification, degree-reversal polarization,
and Cauchy-jet totality of Ledgers 1402--1408 therefore descend from this
source quotient with their exact multiplicities.

No zero coordinates or multiplicities are inputs. The maximal ideals and
their lengths are recovered functorially from \`Q_E\).

## Noncircularity audit

The chain of construction is

\`Mellin dilation + source sum E\`
\` -> Fourier source range R_E\`
\` -> smallest multiplier ideal I_E\`
\` -> quotient Q_E\`
\` -> intrinsic local-length associated graded\`
\` -> polarized jet boundary\`.

The identity \`I_E=(Xi)\` is a derived factorization theorem from the theta and
source map, not a supplied numerical zero list. The final determinant is still
divisor-theoretic: once the quotient ideal is identified, its canonical
product necessarily reproduces Xi. The construction explains the boundary
and multiplicity mechanism but does not prove the spectrum real.

## Falsifier

If the source range failed to contain Xi itself, its generated ideal could be
a proper subideal of \`(Xi)\). If any source image were not divisible by Xi,
the generated ideal could be larger and erase part of the zero divisor.
Either event would falsify the equality. Proposition 3.6 supplies both exact
controls.
