# The Bordered Evans Wall Records but Does Not Remove Scalar Cancellation

## Source orientation

In the reciprocal Clark pair, sheet reversal changes the signed component and
fixes the common component. The source-authorized carrier orientation is
therefore the sheet sum, not an arbitrarily chosen difference.

On a positive moving graph \(y=Hx\), the sum carrier is

\[
u=x+y=(I+H)x.
\]

For positive \(H\), \(-1\) is outside its spectrum, so

\[
u=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=0.
\]

Thus the source sum channel cannot undergo full antipodal cancellation.

## Bordered endpoint wall

Let \(\ell\) be the scalar Evans endpoint observer. The canonical bordered
map is

\[
D_{H,x,\ell}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
I&u\\
\ell&0
\end{pmatrix}
\]

on \(V\oplus\mathbb C\). Its determinant is

\[
\det D_{H,x,\ell}=-\ell(u).
\]

If the scalar endpoint vanishes, then

\[
D_{H,x,\ell}
\binom{-u}{1}=0.
\]

Conversely, every nonzero kernel vector is a scalar multiple of this vector
when the identity bulk block is retained.

This is an exact zero-to-state lift. But because positive sum orientation
gives \(u\ne0\) for every nonzero source state, the lifted kernel has a
nonzero carrier component. The border does not promote scalar cancellation to
full carrier cancellation.

## Smallest hostile witness

Take

\[
H=I_2,
\qquad
x=(1,-1)^T,
\qquad
\ell=(1,1).
\]

Then

\[
u=(I+H)x=(2,-2)^T\ne0,
\qquad
\ell(u)=0.
\]

The bordered matrix has a kernel generated by

\[
(-2,2,1)^T.
\]

The state has positive graph energy, nonzero sum carrier, and zero scalar
endpoint. It does not land in the difference channel, a seam loss, or a
completion defect. It is ordinary endpoint interference inside the admitted
sum carrier.

## Exact capability boundary

The bordered Evans wall provides:

- a division-free determinant section;
- an exact equivalence between scalar zero and bordered rank loss;
- a canonical kernel line at a simple zero;
- provenance for the evaluation operation.

It does not provide:

- carrier cancellation;
- a contracting homotopy;
- a forbidden defect classification;
- endpoint nonvanishing;
- an open-half-plane conservation law.

Calling the scalar wall a defect channel does not solve the problem. Its wall
coordinate is nonzero for every canonical zero-state by construction. To rule
that state out, another source law must make the bordered kernel inadmissible
or contractible.

## Categorical consequence

The positive graph and bordered wall are complementary but insufficient:

\[
\text{positive graph}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{nonzero full sum carrier},
\]

while

\[
\text{bordered wall}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{scalar zero becomes a kernel state}.
\]

Together they show exactly what an off-seam zero would mean: not disappearance
of the relationship, but a nonzero source sum lying in the endpoint observer's
kernel and acquiring one evaluation-wall coordinate.

The next RH-bearing constructor must act on this kernel line. Plausible forms
are:

- a source-derived contraction of the bordered complex in each open sector;
- a second endpoint port jointly faithful on the source orbit;
- a conserved current whose boundary law rejects the kernel vector;
- a nonlinear cone or orbit condition excluding \(\ker\ell\).

## Falsifier

For any claimed bordered zero-exclusion theorem, exhibit its action on the
canonical vector \((-u,1)\). If the theorem merely reconstructs this vector,
assigns it to the generic evaluation wall, or invokes graph positivity while
\(u\ne0\), it supplies no exclusion force.

## Scope

This proves the exact capability boundary of the first positive-graph plus
Evans-wall lift. It does not construct the missing contraction, second port,
conservation law, zero confinement, or RH.
