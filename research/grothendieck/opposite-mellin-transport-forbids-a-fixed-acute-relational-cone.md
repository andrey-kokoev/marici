# Opposite Mellin transport forbids a fixed acute relational cone

## Candidate relational order

After the fixed label-cone no-go, the next natural proposal is to order only
the relationship between direct and dual amplitudes. For a pair `(a,b)`, the
basic common-phase-invariant comparison is

\[
R(a,b)=\operatorname{Re}(a\overline b).
\]

The acute relational cone would require `R(a,b)>0`.

## Exact reversal

At logarithmic label `q`, direct and dual Mellin transport act with opposite
characters:

\[
(a,b)\longmapsto
(e^{itq}a,e^{-itq}b).
\]

Therefore

\[
R_t(a,b)
=
\operatorname{Re}(e^{2itq}a\overline b).
\]

For a pair with positive real comparison, choose

\[
t=\frac{\pi}{2q}.
\]

Then the relative phase is `-1` and

\[
R_t(a,b)=-R(a,b)<0.
\]

Thus quotienting the common phase does not produce a fixed acute cone
preserved by the full direct--dual Mellin action. The relative phase is itself
an active spectral coordinate.

## Meaning for the three towers

Input and output do not merely acquire a common gauge phase. They counter-rotate,
and the control tower must remember that relative transport. A fixed comparison
cone discards the control coordinate and consequently mistakes a transported
positive relation for a negative one.

The surviving object is a cone bundle with connection:

\[
C_t=M_t C_0,
\]

or an equivalent covariantly transported order. Its meaningful question is
not whether one state remains in one fixed cone, but whether the source state
remains in the transported cone selected by its control path.

## Remaining danger

A cone field can always be defined by transporting a chosen initial cone. That
alone is tautological. To carry RH force it must also satisfy an independently
derived closing condition under Fourier--Tate reflection and modular sewing.
The holonomy around the direct--dual--control coherence cell must constrain the
distinguished scalar section rather than merely restate the transport law.

## Disposition

Both fixed-order branches are closed:

- a fixed cone on labels fails under a Mellin half-turn;
- a fixed common-phase-quotiented relational cone fails under the doubled
  relative phase.

The next live order-theoretic object is a source-derived transported cone field
with a nontrivial modular closing law. Its hostile test is whether an
off-seam-null two-cell source can be equipped with the same connection and
closing coherence.
