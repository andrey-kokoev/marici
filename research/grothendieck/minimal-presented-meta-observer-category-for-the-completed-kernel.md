# Minimal presented meta-observer category for the completed kernel

## Question

Which generators and cells are already typed without importing unproved Mellin or theta comparisons?

## Objects

Use the following source objects at fixed normalization:

- `P_N`: prime-power distribution with arithmetic cutoff `N`;
- `P`: completed labelled prime distribution obtained from the log-Gaussian limit;
- `E` and `Gamma`: endpoint and archimedean distributions;
- `W=P+E+Gamma`: completed Weil distribution;
- `K_(s,z)`: heat--character matrix coefficient `W(e^{-su^2}e^{izu})`;
- `Theta_(t,xi)`: shifted-Gaussian source kernel `W(e^{-t(u-xi)^2})`;
- `J_(k,s)`: heat jet of order `k`; 
- `G_(I,t)`: Gram matrix for a finite rational translate packet `I`.

## Generating arrows

1. `c_N:P_N->P_(N+1)`, prime-cutoff inclusion.
2. `c_inf:(P_N)_N->P`, labelled source completion with a tail certificate.
3. `q:P->W`, affine completion with residual `E+Gamma`.
4. `S_s:W->K_(s,0)`, Gaussian heat smoothing.
5. `T_z:K_(s,0)->K_(s,z)`, character evaluation.
6. `d_k:K_(s,0)->J_(k,s)`, the operator `(-partial_s)^k`.
7. `shift_(t,xi):W->Theta_(t,xi)`, shifted-Gaussian evaluation.
8. The holomorphic imaginary-character pullback `Theta(t,xi)=e^(-t xi^2)K(t,-2it xi)`, admitted with its gauge and continuation domain.
9. `g_I:K_(s,-)->G_(I,s)`, difference-kernel matrix assembly.
10. Packet maps acting by pullback on Gram matrices.

These arrows use only the explicit formula, Gaussian smoothing, translation, differentiation, and finite matrix assembly.

## Generating cells and residuals

### Cutoff composition

\[
c_{N+1}c_N=c_{N,N+2}.
\]

Its finite computational defect is zero algebraically; only the omitted tail to `P` remains.

### Completion naturality

Applying smoothing or translation before versus after affine completion has the prescribed residual

\[
S_tT_z(E+\Gamma).
\]

The curvature is zero when both routes transport this same term.

### Heat--character and shifted-Gaussian cells

\[
\partial_sK(s,z)=\partial_z^2K(s,z).
\]

For the distinct shifted-Gaussian kernel,

\[
4t^2\partial_t\Theta(t,\xi)
+\partial_\xi^2\Theta(t,\xi)
+2t\Theta(t,\xi)=0.
\]

These cells must not be conflated. Their finite-cutoff curvatures require the correspondingly differentiated tails.

### Jet recursion

\[
J_{k+1}(t)=-\partial_tJ_k(t).
\]

Domain and differentiated-tail bounds must be carried with the arrow.

### Gram restriction

For a packet map represented by `r`,

\[
G_I=r^*G_Jr.
\]

The positive residual section obeys the same pullback law.

### Heat--Gram comparison

Both `J_(k,t)` and `G_(I,t)` are restrictions of the same `K_(t,z)`. Their common-source conformance is generated by the heat equation and evaluation at `z=0`; no direct identity between an arbitrary jet and arbitrary Gram matrix is asserted.

## Excluded arrows

Mellin and theta arrows are omitted until their domains, normalizations, and endpoint boundary terms are materialized. Adding their names without those maps would enlarge the category without defining its residual transport.

## Disposition

This minimal category already links prime refinement, affine completion, heat jets, and translate-Gram observers. Its local curvature tests are explicit. New transforms should enter only by a conservative extension proving their generator maps and all new mixed cells.
