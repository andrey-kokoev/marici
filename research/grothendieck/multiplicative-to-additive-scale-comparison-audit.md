# Multiplicative-to-additive scale comparison audit

## Question

Does an approved source law normalize the map from intrinsic multiplication to continuous additive scale without importing Euler jump locations?

## Formal classification

The nonzero multiplicative monoid of the conditional initial semiring is freely generated by its intrinsic primes. Therefore every additive scale character

`ell:M_nonzero -> (R,+)`

is determined by freely chosen values `ell(p)=c_p` and satisfies

`ell(product p^m)=sum m c_p`.

Neither unique factorization nor the intrinsic formal Euler product restricts the `c_p`. The formal determinant identity exists coefficientwise before any analytic character. Its evaluation by `a -> a^(-s)` is explicitly an added analytic readout.

## Candidate normalizers

Adelic height satisfies `H(n)=n` on positive integers and synchronizes direct and reciprocal cutoff clocks. Taking `log H` gives the desired scale. But its definition uses rational labels, all places, normalized absolute values, and the product formula. Those are arithmetic/adelic inputs, not outputs of the coefficient-neutral Carrier.

The Gaussian heat identity also yields `ell(p)=log p`, but only after choosing `Qe_m=m^2e_m`. Fourier self-duality fixes the continuous Gaussian once its additive character and Haar measure are declared; it does not fix the label-to-coordinate exponent.

Formal Adams/repetition transport supplies composition `T_m T_n=T_(mn)` but not a real translation length. Sectorwise repetition is not universally compatible with physical selection, so naturality cannot manufacture the missing character.

## Hostile discriminator

For any positive family `lambda_p`, the analytic character

`chi_s(product p^m)=exp(-s sum m lambda_p)`

is multiplicative and evaluates the intrinsic formal Euler product to

`product_p (1-exp(-s lambda_p))^(-1)`

in its convergence region. All formal factorization and determinant laws survive. The zeta evaluation occurs only for `lambda_p=log p`. Thus formal Euler compatibility cannot select the completed-zeta scale.

## Disposition

No coefficient-neutral normalized multiplicative-to-additive comparison is constructed. Adelic height closes the clock only inside an already arithmetic adelic lens. The first missing datum is a Carrier-derived norm/height functor into a positive ordered multiplicative object, followed by an authorized logarithm. Without it, the intrinsic Euler product is genuine but its zeta analytic evaluation remains lens-relative.
