# Intrinsic-prime to spline analytic-realization audit

## Question

Does the conditional initial Carrier semiring canonically send its intrinsic prime powers to the completed-zeta spline grades and weights?

## Claim boundary

The conditional `pi_0` construction determines addition, multiplication, component length, intrinsic primes, and unique factorization. It does not determine a real-valued logarithmic grade.

For any assignment of real constants `c_p` to intrinsic primes, unique factorization defines an additive valuation on nonzero classes by

`ell(product p^m) = sum m c_p`.

Hence the multiplicative-to-additive law alone leaves one free parameter per intrinsic prime. The completed-zeta choice `c_p=log p` is one member of this family, selected only after identifying the initial semiring with nonnegative integers inside an ordered analytic coefficient field and using that field's logarithm.

The spline weight

`log(p)/sqrt(p^m)`

adds further structure: it combines the chosen grade with the exponent `1/2`. That exponent comes from the critical normalization of the assumed completed-zeta functional equation, not from initiality, factorization, or Carrier component length.

## Naturality obstruction

Formal repetition maps satisfy multiplicative composition at the quotient layer, but admitted sector readouts do not support one common selection-compatible arithmetic action. In the five-site cosmology fixture, every even repetition collapses the deck-difference group and changes 992 of 1024 selection values. Thus algebraic repetition cannot be transported to an analytic physical readout without an independently derived sectorwise operation and selection-coherence cell.

A real/complex coefficient lens may provide logarithm and square root, but then the realization is lens-relative. Existing shared diagram shapes do not create a cross-sector algebra map.

## Hostile family

Choose two prime assignments `c_p` and `c'_p` differing at one intrinsic prime. Both extend uniquely to additive grades compatible with multiplication and powers. Defining

`w_c(p,m)=c_p exp(-m c_p/2)`

produces two weighted prime-power alphabets obeying the same formal grade-additivity and critical-weight shape, but with different finite spline functionals. No law in the initial semiring distinguishes them.

This is stronger than arbitrary relabelling: the hostile alternatives preserve intrinsic multiplication, factorization, powers, and the declared functional form of the weight.

## Disposition

The analytic realization is not Carrier-derived. Its first missing datum is a source-authorized ordered analytic coefficient lens together with a normalized multiplicative-to-additive character that forces `c_p=log p`; the critical half-density then requires a separately derived reciprocal or functional-equation normalization. Importing the Euler logarithmic derivative supplies both outputs but does not prove their emergence.
