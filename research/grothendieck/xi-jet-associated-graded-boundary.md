# The jet associated graded canonically semisimplifies the Xi quotient

Sequence claim: \`seqclaim-23259dec86e93ac82f1ea20c\` (1402).

Epistemic-graph event: 1450.

## Source-saturated quotient

Let \`E\` be the CCM Mellin-dilation source map

\`E(f)(u)=u^(1/2) sum_(n>=1) f(nu)\`.

On the explicit Hermite source family, Proposition 3.6 of CCM proves that the
multiplicative Fourier images are precisely polynomial multiples of the
theta-derived completed function \`Xi\`. Let \`I_E\` be the ideal generated by
that range in the Hadamard ring. Since the range lies in \`(Xi)\` and contains
\`Xi\` itself, \`I_E=(Xi)\`. We use the source-saturated algebraic quotient
\`H_<=1/I_E\`, not the paper's weaker closure quotient, which certifies only
the spectral set. Thus local multiplicities are retained without supplying a
numerical zero list.

Write its local model at a spectral point \`lambda\` as

\`A_lambda=O_lambda/(Xi)\`.

If Xi has vanishing order \`m_lambda\`, then \`A_lambda\` has length
\`m_lambda\` and maximal-ideal filtration

\`A_lambda superset m_lambda superset ... superset m_lambda^(m_lambda)=0\`.

The repeated use of \`m_lambda\` for the ideal and integer is avoided below by
writing the maximal ideal as \`q_lambda\`.

## Canonical associated-graded boundary

Define

\`Gr_lambda = direct_sum_(k=0)^(m_lambda-1)
              q_lambda^k/q_lambda^(k+1)\`.

Every summand is one-dimensional. Multiplication by the spectral coordinate
\`z\` preserves the filtration. Since

\`z=lambda+(z-lambda)\`

and the second term raises the filtration, the induced degree-preserving
operator on every graded quotient is exactly multiplication by \`lambda\`.
Therefore

\`Gr(T_z)|Gr_lambda = lambda I_(m_lambda)\`.

This is the desired semisimplification with multiplicity. It does not choose
or enumerate \`m_lambda\` copies: their number is the intrinsic length of the
local source quotient. Equivalently, it is the associated graded of the
canonical maximal-ideal filtration. The double-zero Jordan block of Ledger
1401 degenerates canonically to \`lambda I_2\`.

The spectral coordinate fixes a canonical local parameter
\`t_lambda=z-lambda\`; the classes of
\`1,t_lambda,...,t_lambda^(m_lambda-1)\` identify the graded lines. Completing
the algebraic direct sum in the standard square-sum norm gives

\`H_Gr=ell^2{(lambda,k):0<=k<m_lambda}\`.

Finite-support jet data come from entire interpolation, so the algebraic jet
image is dense in this completion.

## Conditional operator theorem

Let \`A_Gr\` act by \`lambda\` on the fiber indexed by \`(lambda,k)\`.

- \`A_Gr\` is self-adjoint exactly when every Xi zero is real, hence exactly
  under RH.
- Its spectrum is discrete with algebraic and Hilbert multiplicity
  \`m_lambda\`.
- Its resolvent is compact because the Xi zeros form a discrete divisor
  escaping to infinity with finite local multiplicity.
- \`A_Gr^(-1)\` is Hilbert--Schmidt because the Riemann--von Mangoldt bound
  \`N(T)=O(T log T)\` implies \`sum m_lambda/|lambda|^2<infinity\`.
- Pairing the symmetric zeros cancels the exponential terms in the modified
  determinant and gives

  \`det_2(I-z A_Gr^(-1))=Xi(z)/Xi(0)\`.

Thus the associated-graded operation closes the multiplicity defect without
assuming simplicity.

## What this does and does not derive

This construction is noncircular with respect to a *zero list*: the divisor,
local lengths, and fibers are recovered from the source range of \`E\` and the
theta Mellin transform. It is nevertheless determinant-tautological in a
stronger explanatory sense: the source quotient has already been identified
as the Xi ideal, and the determinant follows by reading its divisor.

Moreover, the square-sum jet norm is a canonical analytic Hilbertization of
the spectral-coordinate graded lines, but it has not been derived from the
Weil pairing on Mellin test functions. The Weil form sees

\`sum_lambda m_lambda |G(lambda)|^2\`,

which is one evaluation channel of weight \`m_lambda\`, whereas the graded
boundary has \`m_lambda\` separate jet lines. No source isometry from those
jet lines to independent Mellin boundary modes has yet been produced.

Hence this is a source-derived **algebraic boundary and conditional Hilbert
operator**, not yet the requested positive Mellin-dilation boundary. The last
noncircular gate is a source pairing that orthogonalizes the jet filtration
and agrees with the Weil form on its evaluation channel. Its positivity would
still imply RH.

## Sharp falsifier

At a hypothetical double zero, the Weil evaluation form has rank one:

\`2|G(lambda)|^2\`.

The graded fiber has dimension two. Therefore no isometry from the full graded
fiber to the scalar Weil evaluation channel exists. A proposed scalar
Mellin-boundary construction either loses one determinant multiplicity or
contains a null direction. To retain the squared determinant, it must exhibit
a second source-derived boundary channel (for example a typed jet/derivative
channel), not merely reinterpret the weight two.

## Scope

Unconditional algebraic semisimplification and a conditional self-adjoint
determinant theorem. RH/positivity is not proved, and the jet Hilbert norm is
not yet induced by the Mellin/Weil source pairing.
