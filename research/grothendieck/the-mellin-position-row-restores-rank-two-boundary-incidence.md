# The Mellin position row restores rank-two boundary incidence

## Scope of the rank-one bottleneck

The scalar theta probe \(f\) is even. Consequently it annihilates every odd
relative boundary current. The rank-one Euler-to-Green factorization is exact
after this even scalar quotient, but it is not the complete source-authorized
boundary incidence.

The logarithmic position operator

\[
(Qf)(q)=qf(q)
\]

is already source-derived as the generator conjugate to Mellin-character
transport. It supplies an odd response without introducing a fitted history.

## Exact two-by-two witness

For \(L>0\), define the symmetric and antisymmetric boundary atoms

\[
s_L=\delta_L+\delta_{-L},
\qquad
j_L=\delta_L-\delta_{-L}.
\]

If \(f\) is even, then \(Qf\) is odd, and the response matrix is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
\langle s_L,f\rangle & \langle j_L,f\rangle\\
\langle s_L,Qf\rangle & \langle j_L,Qf\rangle
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
2f(L)&0\\
0&2Lf(L)
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its determinant is

\[
4L f(L)^2.
\]

For the positive theta profile and \(L>0\), this is strictly positive. Thus
the complete finite boundary-to-source incidence has rank two on the
symmetric/antisymmetric packet.

## Fourier completion

The row \(Q\) alone is not Fourier-stable. Fourier conjugation exchanges
position and derivative, up to the fixed normalization and phase. Therefore
the completed observer is the phase-space graph pair

\[
(Q,D),
\qquad D=\partial_q.
\]

The second wall was already present in the source grammar. It disappeared
only because the previous Green reduction passed through the even scalar
evaluation \(B_f^\times\) before retaining the relative boundary current.

## What is now proved

- The rank-one neck is a property of the scalar even quotient, not the full
  boundary module.
- The source supplies a canonical second response row.
- Symmetric and antisymmetric atomic currents are separated by a strictly
  nonzero minor before aggregation.
- Fourier closure requires the derivative mate rather than deletion of the
  odd row.

## Remaining obstruction

The rank-two observer has not yet been coupled to the scalar zero-state
condition. Existing results prove that \(Qf\) detects relative prime flux, but
not that a completed Mellin zero supplies a boundary state whose full
\((Q,D)\) response enters the doubled Green conservation identity.

The live gate is now a source-derived commutative square. One route sends the
Euler relative current into the \((Q,D)\) graph response. The other sends the
completed scalar zero-state into that same graph boundary domain.

Without that common domain, rank-two observability and zero confinement remain
separate facts.

## Falsifier

At finite prime-power cutoff, derive both routes into the \((Q,D)\) graph
domain. A nonzero mate-square residual, loss of the odd current, or a graph
norm depending on the chosen cutoff closes the proposed coupling. Scalar
endpoint agreement is insufficient.
