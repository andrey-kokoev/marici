# Arithmetic theta attachment cocone and two-prime polarized gluing

## Constructed data

This packet constructs a local arithmetic attachment from the source translation law: its piece maps, boundary inclusion, tail quotient, chain contraction, complete polarized Gram entries, and two-prime common-refinement comparison. The construction applies to any admitted square-integrable forcing Phi. The recorded first theta atom additionally gives explicit incomplete-gamma formulas for every matrix entry.

The resulting object is the source-derived theta/Stein attachment. Identification with the independent relative-Haar Green object remains a specific further construction. This packet records the exact equation reached by the new attachment on a putative spectral state.

## 1. Source generators and arithmetic action

Let V be the complex vector space of finite labelled packets on positive integers. Use the admitted source columns

j_n(u)=n^(-1/2) Phi(u+log n), u>=0,

and Jc=sum_n c_n j_n in H=L2(0,infinity). Restrict to any admitted prime-generated submonoid as needed. Finite packets avoid completion assumptions.

For p prime, set L_p=log p, S_p e_n=e_(pn), A_p=sqrt(p) S_p. Define C_L h(u)=h(u+L). Direct substitution gives

J A_p=C_(L_p) J,

A_p A_q=A_q A_p=A_(pq).

These equations hold on generators and hence on finite packets. They specify the attachment's arithmetic action before spectral specialization.

## 2. Actual pieces, attachments, and quotient

For L>0, take W_L=L2(0,L). Let E_L:W_L->H extend by zero, R_L:H->W_L restrict, and T_L:H->H be the right shift with zero filling:

(T_L h)(u)=0 for u<L, and h(u-L) for u>=L.

Then

R_L E_L=I, C_L T_L=I, R_L T_L=0, C_L E_L=0,

E_L R_L+T_L C_L=I_H.

Thus the two piece maps E_L,T_L construct the unitary gluing

W_L direct-sum H -> H, (w,t) -> E_L w+T_L t,

with inverse h -> (R_L h,C_L h). The window and tail are orthogonal by their disjoint supports.

The exact attachment sequence is

0 -> W_L --E_L--> H --C_L--> H -> 0.

On the arithmetic source its boundary map is B_p=R_(L_p) J, and its tail map is J A_p. Hence the complete source cocone is

Jc=E_(L_p) B_p c+T_(L_p) J A_p c.

Every map is specified by restriction, translation, or zero extension. This derives the comparison frame from the same cut operation.

## 3. Explicit cofiber realization

Use cochain complexes and form Cone(E_L), with W_L in degree -1, H in degree 0, differential E_L. Its projection pi to H concentrated in degree zero is C_L on degree zero. Its section i is T_L.

The degree-minus-one homotopy h is R_L:H->W_L. The displayed identities give

pi i=I, d h+h d=I-i pi.

Therefore Cone(E_L) deformation-retracts onto the tail H. This constructs the interval cone package and its comparison, with bounded maps. The source-generated range J(V) maps into this ambient sequence; no assertion is made that every window vector lies in J(V), or that its source-labelled norm completion is already identified with H.

## 4. Complete polarized attachment

Use the convention <x,y>=integral conjugate(x)y. Define

G(c,d)=<Jc,Jd>, Q_p(c,d)=<B_pc,B_pd>.

The support decomposition gives the full sesquilinear identity

G(c,d)=Q_p(c,d)+G(A_pc,A_pd).

All four piece-pair blocks are explicit:

<E_L w,E_L v>=<w,v>, <T_L h,T_L k>=<h,k>,

<E_L w,T_L h>=0, <T_L h,E_L w>=0.

This supplies the polarized attachment equality, including cross blocks, for this window/tail realization. The Green boundary form of an independently prescribed differential operator would require its own identification with these entries.

## 5. Two-prime gluing on the common source

Let p<q. The total window [0,L_p+L_q) has the common refinement

I_0=[0,L_p), I_1=[L_p,L_q), I_2=[L_q,L_p+L_q).

The middle interval has length log(q/p). For I subset [0,infinity), write Q_I(c,d)=integral_I conjugate(Jc)Jd.

Order (p,q) gives

Q_p(c,d)+Q_q(A_pc,A_pd)=Q_[0,L_p+L_q)(c,d).

Order (q,p) gives

Q_q(c,d)+Q_p(A_qc,A_qd)=Q_[0,L_p+L_q)(c,d).

Thus their polarized forms agree on all finite packets, and both equal

Q_I0+Q_I1+Q_I2.

The comparison map restricts to the three pieces, then regroups their zero extensions into the chosen pair of windows. It is an isometry of the full function-valued window packets. Translation of the second window returns its coordinate to a half-line origin. The path of regroupings is generated by direct-sum association and those actual restriction maps.

The earlier two-scalar-port obstruction concerns summing interval coefficients and discarding the function-valued subwindows. The present comparison retains all three support pieces. Its middle energy is part of the attachment.

I_r(c;a,b)=c^(-r)[Gamma(r,c exp(2a))-Gamma(r,c exp(2b))],

The general forcing construction in sections 1–5 uses the admitted Phi directly. The closed gamma expression in this section specializes to Phi_1; a completed sum of theta atoms requires its own sum/interchange estimates.

## 7. What the attachment gives on a spectral vector

Suppose the source equation J A_p c=lambda Jc is meaningful with finite energy. The constructed identity implies

Q_p(c,c)=(1-|lambda|^2)G(c,c).

At lambda=p^(-z), this is

Q_p(c,c)=(1-p^(-2 Re z))G(c,c).

The residual has become the explicit seam-window energy. For a nonzero analytic Jc, its vanishing on an open window forces Jc identically zero whenever analytic continuation applies. Thus the unilateral theta realization retains that energy as an attachment component. Deleting it would contradict its own gluing identity.

This equation identifies the next exact construction required by the confinement programme: the reciprocal/Xi state must enter a larger source-generated attachment in which this seam contribution is accounted for, with the independent Green form and its polarized cross terms explicitly derived. A formal unitarity argument on generalized translation eigenvectors would additionally need a valid norm domain; generalized eigenvectors do not automatically have finite Hilbert energy.

No Xi-specific input was used in constructing the present attachment. Consequently no Xi-specific cancellation has been inferred.
