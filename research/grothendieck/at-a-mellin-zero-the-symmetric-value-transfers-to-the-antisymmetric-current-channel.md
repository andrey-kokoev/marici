# At a Mellin zero the symmetric value transfers to the antisymmetric current channel

## Two value charts and two current charts

Let

\[
K(z)=\int_0^\infty k(u)e^{zu}\,du
\]

be the one-sided value chart, and let

\[
L(z)=\int_0^\infty B(u)e^{zu}\,du
\]

be the transformed moving-endpoint current. The incidence Ward identity gives

\[
L(z)=-k(0)-(z+1/2)K(z).
\]

Resolve the reciprocal pair into symmetric and antisymmetric value channels:

\[
F(z)=\frac{K(z)+K(-z)}2,
\qquad
G(z)=\frac{K(z)-K(-z)}2.
\]

The completed current codiagonal is

\[
C(z)=\frac{L(z)+L(-z)}2.
\]

Direct substitution gives the exact channel law

\[
C(z)=-k(0)-\frac12F(z)-zG(z).
\]

No zero information or division by the completed function enters this
identity.

## What happens at a scalar zero

If the completed scalar channel vanishes, then

\[
F(z)=0
\]

and hence

\[
C(z)+k(0)=-zG(z).
\]

Thus the full value-current packet does not vanish. The symmetric value has
been lost under codiagonal projection, while its surviving antisymmetric
amplitude is carried by the endpoint-current channel.

For nonzero \(z\), the current reconstructs that amplitude:

\[
G(z)=-\frac{C(z)+k(0)}z.
\]

This gives a precise mathematical meaning to the proposed loss-of-meaning
picture: a zero is not annihilation of the two-sector state. It is transfer
of observable content from one grade to its complementary current grade.

## Why this still does not prove confinement

The transfer law is valid at seam and hypothetical off-seam zeros alike. It
therefore restores faithfulness but supplies no location theorem. The
identity

\[
C+k(0)=-zG
\]

is a kinematic compatibility relation, not an orientation law.

This rules out another shortcut. Adding the scalar endpoint-current transform
as a second monitor prevents complete information loss, but it cannot make
the antipode unreachable. A third comparison must determine whether the
current transfer is compatible with the sector type.

The minimal RH-bearing statement can now be localized to the zero locus:
whenever \(F(z)=0\), a source-derived pairing of \(G(z)\) with
\(C(z)+k(0)\) must force \(\operatorname{Re}z=0\). Such a theorem would be
strictly weaker than global de Branges modulus dominance because it constrains
only the antipodal incidence locus.

## Multi-tower interpretation

The value tower and current tower are complementary. Their Ward cell explains
how information transfers when the value projection vanishes. The missing
next rung is a control cell that types this transfer by reciprocal sector.

So the present structure is not one two-channel tower. It is a coupled packet:

- symmetric and antisymmetric value channels;
- symmetric and antisymmetric current channels;
- a Ward comparison between them;
- a still-missing sector-control comparison on the zero locus.

## Operator stimulus

The operator's repeated claim that a zero marks where something loses meaning,
rather than where the full object disappears, is realized exactly here. The
calculation also explains why another comparison channel was repeatedly felt
to be necessary: the current restores the hidden amplitude but does not yet
say where that transfer is authorized.
