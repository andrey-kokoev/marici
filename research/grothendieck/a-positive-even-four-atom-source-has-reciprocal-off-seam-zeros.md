# A Positive Even Four-Atom Source Has Reciprocal Off-Seam Zeros

## Testing the obvious repair

The two-atom hostile suggests that off-seam cancellation comes from unequal
reciprocal weights. Does global reflection symmetry of the positive source
force the required balance?

No.

Take equal positive atoms at \(q=\pm1\) with weight \(1\), and at \(q=\pm2\)
with weight \(1/4\). Its bilateral transform is

\[
F(z)=e^z+e^{-z}+\frac14\left(e^{2z}+e^{-2z}\right).
\]

It is real under conjugation and exactly even:

\[
F(-z)=F(z).
\]

## Exact off-seam roots

Put

\[
x=e^z,
\qquad
y=x+x^{-1}.
\]

Then

\[
F(z)=y+\frac14(y^2-2).
\]

The scalar zero equation is

\[
y^2+4y-2=0,
\]

with roots

\[
y=-2\pm\sqrt6.
\]

For

\[
y_-=-2-\sqrt6<-2,
\]

the equation \(x+x^{-1}=y_-\) has two distinct negative real reciprocal
solutions. Their moduli are unequal and neither is one. Therefore

\[
z=\log|x|+i\pi
\]

gives reciprocal off-seam zeros with nonzero real parts of opposite sign.

## Consequence

The following package is still insufficient for zero confinement:

1. a positive source;
2. exact reflection symmetry;
3. conjugation symmetry;
4. reciprocal zero pairing;
5. equal weights inside every reflected atom pair.

These properties explain the quartet geometry but not confinement to the
unitary seam.

## Correction to reciprocal balance

The required balance cannot mean only that the source is invariant under
reflection. Several reflected scale pairs can interfere so that the effective
cancellation channels at a zero have unequal magnitudes.

The missing theta law must constrain the interaction among different scales.
Candidates remain:

- the exact prime-scale recursion with its signed seam window;
- a variation-diminishing law for the complete separation measure;
- a conserved total boundary flux that the four-atom source fails;
- a source-derived operator ordering stronger than evenness.

Any proposed explanation based only on functional-equation symmetry is now
falsified by this four-atom source.
