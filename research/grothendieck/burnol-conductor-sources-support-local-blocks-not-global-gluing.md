# Burnol conductor sources support local blocks, not global gluing

## Question

Does external literature on conductor operators, Sonine spaces, or support-restricted Weil positivity supply the missing global form closure?

## Search authority

The operator previously authorized shell-based external search. The arXiv API was queried for Burnol, Weil positivity, conductor operators, and Sonine spaces.

## Located sources

Jean-François Burnol, “The Explicit Formula in simple terms,” arXiv `math/9810169`, describes Weil's RH criterion, local contributions, Haran's additive-convolution formulation, and a conductor operator whose spectral analysis is equivalent to the explicit formula. Its abstract states that finite-place conductor operators have positive cuspidal spectrum.

Jean-François Burnol, “Sur les Formules Explicites I: analyse invariante,” arXiv `math/0101068`, explains local explicit-formula terms through the conductor operator and states that Weil positivity is checked under a support condition.

Jean-François Burnol, “Sur certains espaces de Hilbert de fonctions entières, liés à la transformation de Fourier et aux fonctions L de Dirichlet et de Riemann,” arXiv `math/0105120`, constructs a zeta-related subspace of a Sonine space and identifies quotient vectors attached to zeros and multiplicities.

Jean-François Burnol, “Two complete and minimal systems associated with the zeros of the Riemann zeta function,” arXiv `math/0203120`, connects zeta-zero Hilbert systems, co-Poisson formulae, and de Branges Sonine spaces.

## Comparison with existing Marici results

The first two sources support the already recorded fixed-support architecture:

- positive local Weil blocks under a support restriction;
- archimedean conductor control;
- finite-place structure organized by prime translations.

They do not, from the inspected abstracts, state a theorem gluing all translated local blocks into a globally positive form. Existing Marici analysis identifies the missing cross-block condition: individual edge contractions do not prevent negative cycle modes in the infinite block graph.

The Sonine-space sources attach Hilbert-space vectors to zeros and multiplicities, but their abstracts do not provide an arithmetic positive factor whose positivity is independent of the zero locations. Importing zero-indexed vectors as a positive spectral realization would therefore not discharge the source-side noncircularity gate without theorem-level inspection.

## Exact residual

A usable literature theorem would need one of:

1. a positive dilation of the complete prime-translation block graph;
2. a chordal or Markov factorization eliminating cycle defects;
3. a source-defined closed semibounded conductor form whose lower bound is proved without assuming Weil positivity or zero reality.

Support-restricted positivity alone supplies none of these arrows.

## Disposition

Burnol's conductor and Sonine programmes remain relevant source frameworks, but the external search found no abstract-level claim closing global block gluing. Do not present local positivity or zero-indexed Hilbert completeness as the missing RH proof.
