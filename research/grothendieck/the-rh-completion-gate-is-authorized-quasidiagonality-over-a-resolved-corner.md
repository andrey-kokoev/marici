# The RH completion gate is authorized quasidiagonality over a resolved corner

## Transfer from the newest team results

The local theta/Tate layers now have natural exact typings: Fourier transport,
trace sewing, Mellin flow, weighted graph completion, and scalar endpoint
pullback are flat or strict. This does not imply that the authorized Euler
cutoff filtration is compatible with the completed global operator.

Nima's leakage theorem supplies the exact missing operator condition. Aspect's
directional-specialization falsifier supplies the correct geometry of the

## Authorized quasidiagonality

Let `F` be the completed sewing or Green-response operator on a common
completed carrier, and let `P_X` be the finite-rank projections belonging to
the actual source-authorized Euler cutoff filtration. Relative to

\[
H=P_XH\oplus(I-P_X)H,
\]

the two leakage blocks are

\[
L_X^{\mathrm{in}}=P_XF(I-P_X),
\qquad
L_X^{\mathrm{out}}=(I-P_X)FP_X.
\]

Uniform strict descent through the cutoff family is equivalent to

\[
\|[P_X,F]\|
=
\max\bigl(\|L_X^{\mathrm{in}}\|,\|L_X^{\mathrm{out}}\|\bigr)
\longrightarrow0.
\]

The projections cannot be replaced by a convenient unrelated filtration.
The statement required by the RH programme is authorized quasidiagonality.

If forward and reverse sewing live in different weighted fibers, the
commutator notation is mistyped. One must retain the two directed naturality
residuals between the corresponding source and target cutoff projections.

## Why fixed observers are insufficient

For every fixed finite diagnostic `P_Y`, boundedness of `F` gives

\[
\|P_YF(I-P_X)\|\longrightarrow0
\]

as `X` grows, because `P_YF` has finite rank. This does not imply the moving
boundary estimate

\[
\|P_XF(I-P_X)\|\longrightarrow0.
\]

A shift operator has one unit-strength crossing edge at every moving cutoff
despite perfect eventual reconciliation of each fixed observer. Thus no
amount of fixed finite scouting establishes the required uniform theorem.

## The shell cocycle

For nested cutoffs `P_X <= P_Y`, incoming leakage decomposes as

\[
P_XF(I-P_X)
=
P_XF(P_Y-P_X)+P_XF(I-P_Y).
\]

The finite defects therefore form a coherent additive shell cocycle. If their
norms do not decay, the correct completed object may retain the limiting
boundary operator as a typed port rather than declare strict descent.

This is the first concrete candidate for the primitive and prime-square
currents: they may be the leading nonvanishing shell-leakage channels.

## Resolved joint limit

There are at least two boundary parameters:

- spectral distance `a=Re(s)-1/2` from the reciprocal seam;
- inverse cutoff scale `epsilon_X`, tending to zero as the Euler cutoff grows.

The corner

\[
(a,\epsilon_X)=(0,0)
\]

cannot be treated as one undirected limit without proof. Aspect's
direction-dependent specialization result warns that different ratios

\[
a/\epsilon_X
\]

may carry different leading leakage ranks or boundary filtrations. The
correct completion audit should therefore resolve this corner into approach
directions and test the cutoff commutator on every source-authorized chart.

This does not assert that direction dependence occurs for theta. It prevents
a theorem proved on one path, such as fixed `a` followed by `X -> infinity`,
from being silently promoted to a uniform seam statement.

## Sewing before subtraction

Benincasa's active-wall result proves that two individually divergent wall
objects can have cancelling residues at their shared endpoint, while their
interior conductor Laurent jets remain distinct. The transferable ordering
law is:

1. retain all source-incidence-related boundary channels;
2. sew their common endpoint algebraically;
3. only then subtract or renormalize independent interior singularities.

Applied here, primitive and prime-square currents must first be assembled with
their reciprocal and archimedean mates. Separate finite-part subtraction may
destroy the common endpoint cancellation and manufacture a completion class.

\[

\qquad

\qquad

\]

or their correctly typed two-carrier versions. Then require one preregistered

Failure of all three closes the current completion route. Fixed-observer

## Cross-transfer from Strominger

Strominger's eta-squared witness is invisible to every `S_3` probe but visible
to an `S_4` probe. The lesson here is not a shared rank. It is that a complete
low-capability probe family can be blind to the first irreducible residue.
Therefore the primitive/square boundary test must establish faithfulness of
its port family; passing all smaller scalar diagnostics is not enough.

## Scope
