# Audit of a refined theta Green object beyond four-channel reciprocity

## Decision

No approved source-specific refinement currently supplies both an independent
flux row and a positive relative Green form. Existing refinements discriminate
some hostile sources, but none couples all data required for zero orientation.

## Discrimination hierarchy

1. The prime-square connected coefficient rejects the positive convolution
   hostile at grade two: its coefficient is `-17/8`, violating positive
   Euler/Fock grammar. This proves why the square port must be retained, but it
   does not reject a hostile that leaves every finite place unchanged.
2. The degree-twelve adelic hostile keeps the complete standard finite Fock
   source and changes only the archimedean Fourier-fixed profile. It therefore
   preserves primitive, square, connected, transition, and finite Tate data
   while introducing certified off-seam zeros.
3. The Gaussian oscillator equation `Dg=(partial_x+2 pi x)g=0` rejects that
   hostile before zero inspection. However its separate Mellin image is only
   the gamma recurrence; tensored with unchanged finite data, it imposes no
   orientation on the Euler divisor.
4. Full labelled Poisson sewing rigidly selects the unweighted integer comb,
   but the selected comb is precisely the arithmetic source whose completed
   Mellin readout retains the unresolved zeros. Source rigidity alone is not a
   Green law.

## Missing nonseparable constructor

The standard Tate constructor closure is too broad: all its coherence cells
remain valid after the archimedean hostile substitution. Conversely,
archimedean vacuum minimality alone is too narrow. No current construction
simultaneously uses

- Gaussian annihilation at infinity,
- diagonal integer/prime-power labels, and
- a boundary comparison current surviving Mellin compression.

Therefore a refined theta state with a source-derived second observation row
has not been constructed. The earliest plausible object is a nonseparable
commutator or curvature between the archimedean annihilator and diagonal
integer formation before Poisson summation. It must be label-sensitive, reject
the degree-twelve excitation, and yield an exact or signed boundary current
for the Gaussian vacuum—not merely another scalar gamma recurrence.

## Confinement boundary

Prime-square discrimination and Gaussian vacuum discrimination are necessary
provenance tests, not sufficient confinement laws. Until their coupling is
derived on the complete Green carrier, the three-dimensional four-channel
readout kernel remains admissible and scalar-null confinement is not proved.
