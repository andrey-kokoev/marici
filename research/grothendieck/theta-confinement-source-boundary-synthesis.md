## Independent missing gates

- a completed source-selected response state and parameter map;

- a positive nonzero bulk and residual absorption bound;

No proved item implies another by notation, positivity, reciprocity, or scalar
aggregation.

## Strongest authorized theorem

reciprocity theorems with the existing conditional statement:

> If the full constraint promotion, Green identity, endpoint-current closure,
> positive bulk, and residual absorption interfaces are supplied on one

The new audits do not strengthen this implication. They identify its first
missing constructive object as either a complete Green-current observer lift
