# Scalar theta zeros versus total endpoint flux

## Decision

Scalar theta nullity factors the affine two-endpoint mismatch, but does not
factor the total oriented Green/work flux. These are distinct boundary
functionals.

## What does factor

For the source-selected common-forcing envelopes,

`E_aff(z)=b(+infinity)-b(-infinity)=2 z xi(1/2+z)`.

After removing the central factor `z`, noncentral scalar zeros are exactly the
parameters for which the unique finite-bulk affine response closes both
endpoint values. Equivalently, the common mode `w=u+v` vanishes at both ends.
Thus this linear endpoint compatibility residual belongs to the principal
ideal generated by the scalar theta section (up to the known central factor).

## What does not follow

Endpoint value closure does not imply interior common-mode closure or work
closure. On the same theta-compatible response,

`R_theta(z)=-Re(z) B_theta(z)`, with `B_theta(z)>0` for a nonzero response.

Consequently a hypothetical off-seam scalar zero is fully compatible with the
closed affine endpoints and nonzero work. Vanishing of this flux at all scalar
zeros would itself force `Re(z)=0`; it is the RH-bearing law, not a consequence
of the already proved affine factorization.

The source also supplies no holomorphic current section whose radical
membership can be tested in place of this Hermitian work. `R_theta` depends on
`Re(z)` and the response norm, so ordinary principal-ideal divisibility by
`xi` is not even the correct analytic type without a separately constructed
complex current cell.

## Exact hostile boundary

`a-two-atom-positive-source-has-an-off-seam-scalar-zero-with-nonzero-moving-seam-flux.md`
gives the smallest universal falsifier. For `F(z)=1+2e^z`, the zero
`e^z=-1/2` has odd flux `4 sinh(z)=3`. Positivity, a full two-port zero-state,
direct moving-seam incidence, and cocycle gluing therefore do not establish a
zero-to-flux implication. The missing source-specific input is reciprocal
weight balance.

The augmentation-bivector Green identity supplies a second exact warning: on
the scalar-null subspace its faithful endpoint norm is positive whenever the
cancelled state is nontrivial, and is balanced by forcing overlap rather than
vanishing.

## Boundary theorem

The approved theta source presently proves

`xi=0 => affine endpoint mismatch=0`,

but not

`xi=0 => total oriented endpoint/work flux=0`.

The latter implication, combined with the exact positive bulk identity, is
already equivalent in force to seam confinement. It cannot be inserted as a
routine endpoint lemma. A valid advance must construct an independently
source-derived reciprocal-sheet current whose complete boundary sum cancels
the forcing/work term, and prove that identity before restricting to scalar
zeros.
