# No Finite Binary Resolution Can Replace the Native Ternary Seam Cell Off the Origin

## Combining the arity and raising-wall results

The primitive-transfer residual is the native ternary germ

\[
R(z,h,P)=-2zhP.
\]

Aspect's arity gate permits two possible treatments:

1. a direct ternary mate;
2. a binary factorization retaining the full three-instance carrier and its
   associator.

The second possibility can be tested using the already derived source
antiderivative recursion.

## Finite binary resolution attempt

Let \(H_1'=f\) and \(H_{j+1}'=H_j\). A binary current that cancels the level
\(j\) residual must contain a pairing of \(H_{j+1}\) with the reciprocal state
\(x\). Differentiation creates the next residual row

\[
r_{j+1}=r_jA_z,
\]

where the homogeneous reciprocal flow has

\[
A_z=
\begin{pmatrix}
-i\tau&-\delta\\
-\delta&-i\tau
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det A_z=-|z|^2.
\]

For \(z\ne0\), the matrix is invertible. Therefore

\[
r_nA_z=0
\]

forces \(r_n=0\), and backward induction forces the initial nonzero residual
row to vanish. No finite antiderivative/bilinear tower can terminate.

## Sharpened disposition

The arity theorem and the invertible raising wall together eliminate every
finite binary resolution in this source-primitive class. The surviving
choices are:

1. a direct source-derived ternary incidence whose boundary is \(-2zhP\);
2. the complete infinite shift-space carrier with a closed associator/current;
3. a distinct finite source equation not generated by the antiderivative
   recursion.

The third choice is unavailable for the exact theta translate family under a
constant-coefficient finite-state model. Thus the practical frontier is a
direct ternary cell versus an infinite completed shift-space cell.

## Meaning for the tower count

Adding one binary comparison rung at a time will never finish off the seam.
The failure is not that the tower is one rung too short. Its proposed binary
shape is wrong for the target relation.

This is the categorical counterpart of the analytic raising wall:

```text
native ternary relation
    -> forced binary factorization
    -> new associator residual
    -> another binary factorization
    -> nonterminating tower
```

A direct ternary constructor compresses the whole sequence only if it is
derived from the theta/Tate source before inspecting the residual. Otherwise
it is merely a name for the desired cancellation.
