# Scale and valuation commute up to a canonical bulk two-cell

## The one-prime square

Fix a prime \(p\) and write

\[
L=\log p.
\]

There are two ways to combine the compactified scale pullback with valuation
depth \(k\).

The primitive-first path pulls back the reciprocal window at scale \(L\) and
then transports its coefficient to valuation depth \(k\). Its archimedean
window remains

\[
W_L.
\]

The valuation-first path recognizes the actual prime-power transport
\(p^k\), whose logarithmic scale is \(kL\). Its window is

\[
W_{kL}.
\]

Therefore the square is not strictly commutative. Its exact residual is

\[
\beta_{p,k}
=
W_{kL}-W_L.
\]

## The residual is bulk, not boundary

Each reciprocal window has the same scale-boundary specialization \(-1\).
Hence

\[
[W_{kL}]=[W_L]
\]

in the fifth-wall quotient. Their difference is an ordinary even Gaussian
bulk vector:

\[
\beta_{p,k}(q)
=
-\int_{q-kL}^{q-L}e^{-\pi v^2}\,dv
-\int_{q+L}^{q+kL}e^{-\pi v^2}\,dv.
\]

For \(k=2\), this is the pair of outer bands added when the primitive window
is widened to the square window.

Its Fourier transform is

\[
\widehat\beta_{p,k}(\xi)
=-
\frac{
\sin(2\pi kL\xi)-\sin(2\pi L\xi)
}{\pi\xi}
e^{-\pi\xi^2},
\]

which is smooth at \(\xi=0\) and Gaussian-decaying.

## Boundary behavior

For each fixed compact set of \(q\)-values,

\[
\beta_{p,k}(q)\longrightarrow0
\qquad(p\to\infty).
\]

Thus the Beck–Chevalley defect creates no new scale-boundary fiber. It is a
finite-label bulk comparison that disappears at the compactified endpoint.

The earlier widening-window escape arose because each \(W_L\) tends to the
unit. The difference of two valuation-compatible windows cancels that common
unit before completion.

## Coherence in valuation depth

The residuals telescope:

\[
\beta_{p,k+\ell}
=
\beta_{p,k}
+
\left(W_{(k+\ell)L}-W_{kL}\right).
\]

Equivalently, adjacent depth cells

\[
\alpha_{p,j}=W_{(j+1)L}-W_{jL}
\]

satisfy

\[
\beta_{p,k}=\sum_{j=1}^{k-1}\alpha_{p,j}.
\]

This is exactly the valuation filtration: the square and higher currents are
assembled from consecutive scale-widening bands rather than appended as
independent counterterms.

## Beck–Chevalley interpretation

The scale/valuation square is pseudocommutative:

- its two paths agree on the boundary quotient;
- the comparison 2-cell \(\beta_{p,k}\) is source-derived;
- the 2-cell lies in the Gaussian bulk;
- adjacent cells compose by telescoping;
- the comparison becomes identity at the large-prime boundary.

Strict commutation would incorrectly identify \(p\) and \(p^k\) as having
the same transport scale. Declaring failure would discard the canonical bulk
comparison. The correct structure is a coherent lax square.

## Consequence for primitive-to-square sewing

The local identity relating primitive projection, valuation degree, and the
square-and-higher remainder now has an archimedean companion. Moving from
primitive depth to square depth widens the reciprocal window and contributes
\(\alpha_{p,1}=W_{2L}-W_L\).

Therefore the candidate finite-part lift is not a scalar cancellation between
primitive and square coefficients. It is a coupled pair:

1. the arithmetic valuation remainder;
2. the archimedean bulk two-cell generated by scale widening.

Both must be retained before the heat/Fock pushforward.

## Remaining gate

The next calculation is the weighted sum of the adjacent cells

\[
\sum_p p^{-s}\alpha_{p,1}
\]

together with the square coefficient dictated by valuation degree. Because
\(\alpha_{p,1}\to0\) locally faster than any power of \(p^{-1}\) on compact
\(q\)-sets, the bulk comparison is much better behaved than either raw
boundary current. The unresolved issue is continuity in the full Gaussian
step topology, whose constants move with \(L\).

## Result

Compactified scale pullback and valuation/Fock transport do not commute
strictly. They commute up to a canonical, coherent Gaussian bulk 2-cell
\(W_{k\log p}-W_{\log p}\). The common fifth-wall boundary cancels inside
this comparison, so no new wall is required. Primitive-to-square sewing must
include this scale-widening cell before scalar finite-part extraction.
