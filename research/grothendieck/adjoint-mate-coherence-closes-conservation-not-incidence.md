# Adjoint-mate coherence closes conservation, not incidence

## What the relative colligation repairs

The adjoint-mate cell can place forward actuation and reverse observation in a
single conservative colligation without requiring the open one-way tail to be
an invariant operator quotient. This is the correct response to the nilpotent
quotient obstruction.

It does not, however, constrain zeros of a selected transmission coefficient.

## Minimal conservative hostile

Let

\[
T(\theta)=
\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\theta&-\sin\theta\\
\sin\theta&\cos\theta
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
v=e_1,
\qquad
\ell=e_1^*.
\]

The full transfer is unitary and orientation-preserving for every real
(	heta). Its source and observer ports are related by the standard positive
pairing, so all adjoint-mate coherence is exact. Nevertheless,

\[
\ell(T(\pi/2)v)=0.
\]

The state has rotated into the complementary channel. Nothing failed in the
colligation.

Therefore positive conservation, complete observability, and exact mate
coherence do not exclude scalar transmission zeros.

## The next rung

The coherence tower now separates into four tasks:

1. retain the source and observer as distinct typed ports;
2. derive actuation and its adjoint mate;
3. assemble their conservative relative colligation;
4. prove that the source-generated transfer path stays inside the open
   incidence cell

   \[
   \Omega_{\ell,v}
   =\{T:\ell(Tv)\ne0\}
   \]

   throughout each open reciprocal sector.

The fourth task is not a consequence of the third. It is a chamber-coherence
law for a generalized minor.

## Which side owns this rung?

This rung lies on the source--observer relationship side. The source must
provide an orientation or flag that makes (ell(Tv)) a distinguished minor,
and transport must preserve its chamber. Neither a new internal state nor a
new scalar readout suffices.

Candidate forms include:

- a source-derived Gauss factorization with nonvanishing pivots;
- total positivity or sign regularity of the labelled transfer matrix;
- an oriented-matroid chamber preserved by every authorized constructor;
- a monotone positive semigroup containing the completed transfer path.

The earlier local acute-cone attempt failed, so any viable chamber theorem
must be global and boundary-bearing.

## Falsifier

Every proposed higher coherence law must reject the rotation above, or its
direct-sum embedding, through an invariant already present in theta/Tate
source data. If it accepts that rotation, it may explain conservation but not
zero confinement.

## Result

Relative colligation is still the correct next construction for dynamics, but
it is only the penultimate rung. RH-bearing force would reside in an additional
source-derived incidence-chamber coherence theorem.
