# The External Five-Cell Controller Closes Both Resolvent-Face Squares

## Graded controller

The archimedean Fourier cell splits canonically as

\[
\mathcal A
=
\langle\phi\rangle
\oplus
\langle1,\delta_0,K,V\rangle.
\]

The first summand is the Fourier-fixed theta source line.  The second is the
boundary representation consisting of the constant–delta exchange plane and
the odd tail–principal-value quarter-turn plane.

The previous obstruction arose from trying to send all of `A` into an
ordinary bulk state domain.  The correct targets are graded packets

\[
P_i=E_i\oplus B,
\qquad
B=\langle1,\delta_0,K,V\rangle,
\qquad
i\in\{+,0,-\},
\]

where `E+`, `E0`, and `E-` are respectively the direct tail, homotopy, and
reciprocal tail graph domains.  The boundary representation is retained as a
port, not reinterpreted as an `L2` state.

## Three incidence maps

Define

\[
i_+(a\phi+b)=(ax_+,b),
\]

\[
i_0(a\phi+b)=(aK_z,b),
\]

and

\[
i_-(a\phi+b)=(ax_-,b).
\]

These maps preserve all five controller capabilities.  Only the theta-fixed
line enters the bulk.  The other four coordinates remain explicitly typed as
boundary data.

## Two face maps

Extend the resolvent faces to the graded packets by

\[
d_+(u,b)=((D+z)u,b),
\qquad
d_-(u,b)=((D-z)u,b).
\]

Then the two controller squares commute exactly:

\[
d_+i_0=i_+,
\qquad
d_-i_0=i_-.
\]

On the bulk line these are the two resolvent identities.  On the boundary
representation they are identity transport.  No constant or delta
distribution is inserted into a bulk graph domain.

Fourier transport is also typed.  It fixes `phi`, exchanges `1` with
`delta_0`, and quarter-turns `K` with `V`.  Because all three packets retain
the same external boundary representation, Fourier acts before or after
incidence without changing its capability type.

## Meaning of the architecture

This realizes the source-faithful reading of `3(3+2+1)+2+1`:

- three bulk realizations, each accompanied by the same typed boundary
  representation;
- two differential faces out of the relational bulk;
- one external controller whose three incidence maps populate the bulk source
  line and retain the four boundary coordinates.

The copies are equal as graded packets but not as Hilbert state spaces.  Their
shared boundary summand is a port representation, not three duplicated
physical distributions.

## What this does and does not prove

The domain and incidence architecture now closes.  The construction is still
universal for a sufficiently regular source and therefore does not orient the
RH zero set.

The remaining source-specific theorem is a Green-form statement on the
three-sector graded packet.  It must show that:

1. the two face Green forms and the homotopy Green form combine with the
   external boundary representation;
2. the anti-diagonal completed zero trace annihilates the total external
   flux;
3. the surviving bulk relationship energy has a definite coefficient equal
   to the centered real part;
4. hostile even sources fail a theta-specific boundary coherence before
   their zero locations are examined.

## Result

The external five-cell controller has three canonical graded incidences, and
both resolvent-face squares commute.  The earlier constant–delta obstruction
was not a failure of the architecture; it was a demand that boundary ports be
bulk states.  With that typing corrected, the full three-sector incidence
diagram closes.  The next obstruction can only occur in the combined Green
form or in theta-specific boundary coherence.
