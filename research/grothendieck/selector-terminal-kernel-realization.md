# Every normal kernel is realized by a quotient selector

## Realization theorem

Let `G` be finite. Every selector has a terminal kernel

`K_c=Core_G(Stab_R(c))`,

which is normal in `G`. Conversely, for every normal subgroup `K`, the
canonical quotient-label selector

`c_K:G->G/K`, `c_K(g)=gK`,

has right stabilizer exactly `K`. Hence its terminal kernel is exactly `K`.

Therefore the terminal kernels realizable by coefficient selectors are
precisely the normal subgroups of `G`. The assignment

`K |-> c_K |-> K_(c_K)`

is the identity on the normal-subgroup lattice.

## Arithmetic corollary

Every finite-surjection resonance spectrum

`U(K)={n:gcd(n,rad(exp(K)exp(A_K)))=1}`

is realized by a coefficient selector, namely `c_K`. This is an existence
statement in the algebraic coefficient category, not a physical admission.

## Exact nonabelian control

For `S3`, exact enumeration finds six subgroups and three normal subgroups,
of orders `1,3,6`. Their canonical quotient selectors have stabilizers—and
therefore terminal kernels—of exactly `1,3,6`.

## Scope

The quotient-label selector may not be the frozen observable supplied by a
sector. Realizability after changing the selector does not create a physical
readout, relative-chain pushforward, or pairing.
