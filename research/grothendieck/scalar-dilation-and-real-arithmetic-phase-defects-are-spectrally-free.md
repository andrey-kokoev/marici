## Bare dilation

Ledger 3058 identifies the reciprocal-even dilation observable with

\[
P=-i\partial_q
\]

on \(L^2(\mathbb R,dq)\). Fourier transformation sends \(P\) to multiplication
by the real variable. Hence its spectrum is purely absolutely continuous and
equal to \(\mathbb R\). It has no \(L^2\) eigenvectors.

Therefore the bare dilation carrier cannot have the Riemann ordinates as
point spectrum. Zeros of its generalized matrix coefficient are not
eigenvalues of \(P\).

## Scalar arithmetic potentials are gauge

Let \(V(q)\) be real and locally integrable, and choose a real primitive

\[
\chi(q)=\int_{q_0}^qV(r)\,dr.
\]

Multiplication by

\[
(U_\chi h)(q)=e^{-i\chi(q)}h(q)
\]

is unitary. Direct differentiation gives

\[
U_\chi P U_\chi^{-1}=P+V.
\]

Thus every scalar real potential on the logarithmic line is unitarily
equivalent to free dilation. It cannot create a discrete spectrum or select
the Riemann ordinates.

The same conclusion holds for real point measures when the operator is typed
through phase-jump boundary conditions. A delta contribution at \(q_*\)
changes the wavefunction by a unit-modulus phase across \(q_*\). A collection
of prime-logarithm phase jumps remains a multiplication gauge.

## Consequence for arithmetic constructors

Placing scalar weights or phases at

\[
q=\log p^k
\]

on the already projected one-channel dilation line cannot produce a
Hilbert–Pólya operator. Such data may alter a chosen matrix coefficient or its
presentation, but not the self-adjoint spectral type.

The arithmetic interaction must therefore do at least one of the following:

1. mix two or more reciprocal components through a matrix-valued potential;
2. change the global boundary topology or compactness;
3. introduce a nonlocal operator not reducible to scalar multiplication; or
4. act on the full labelled valuation module before the multiplicity-one
   quotient.

The first option is the minimal source-compatible continuation of the current
programme. Reintroduce the \(C=+1\) and \(C=-1\) grades as a two-component
Dirac-type state. Arithmetic sewing must occupy an off-diagonal channel. A
diagonal real channel is again removable phase.

## Geometric interpretation

The final scalar seam is too small to carry nontrivial holonomy on the line:
its connection is abelian and exact. Nontrivial spectral geometry requires a
comparison whose transports do not all commute. This is the precise sense in
which another comparison channel is necessary.

## Scope

The free spectral type and scalar gauge equivalence are exact for the stated
one-dimensional self-adjoint operators, including correctly typed phase-jump
extensions. The theorem does not construct the required doubled arithmetic
operator, prove that its determinant is \(\xi\), or prove RH.
