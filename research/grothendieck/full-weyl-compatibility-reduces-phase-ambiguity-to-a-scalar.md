# Full Weyl compatibility reduces phase ambiguity to a scalar

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-26

Status: exact finite rigidity theorem with an open adelic bridge

## Correction and replacement

The adelic theta comb is fixed by the rational Weyl stabilizer and therefore
cannot be cyclic for that same maximal abelian algebra. Phase rigidity must
instead use both complementary, noncommuting Weyl polarizations.

Let \(W\) be an irreducible Weyl representation generated by a clock action
and a transverse shift action. If two boundary selectors are equally
compatible with the complete Weyl transport, their relative ambiguity \(V\)
must commute with both actions. Irreducibility then gives

\[
V=cI.
\]

If both selectors preserve the normalized theta distribution, then \(c=1\).
The selector is unique.

## Finite exact model

For the order-three finite Weyl pair, let

\[
Z=\operatorname{diag}(1,\omega,\omega^2)
\]

and let \(X\) be the cyclic shift. They obey

\[
ZX=\omega XZ.
\]

Solving the simultaneous commutant equations

\[
VZ=ZV,
\qquad
VX=XV
\]

for a general matrix produces only scalar matrices. Fixing one nonzero
normalized vacuum vector forces the scalar to equal one.

## Explanatory content

The fifth relation is not another wall added beside the others. It is the
noncommutative compatibility between the two complementary walls. Either
polarization alone leaves a large phase algebra. Their Weyl commutator makes
the joint representation irreducible and collapses the ambiguity.

This matches the operator's intuition that the RH object may secretly be two
sectors pretending to be one. The scalar readout forgets the noncommuting
relation that makes their common normalization rigid.

## Remaining source theorem

The finite Schur argument becomes relevant to RH only if the adelic source
establishes all of the following before determinant compression:

1. the rational stabilizer and its transverse Fourier–Tate polarization act
   as a complete Weyl pair;
2. their completed representation is irreducible in the required rigged
   category;
3. any admissible boundary selector intertwines both actions; and
4. theta normalization fixes the remaining scalar unit.

The third item is the sharpest gate. A boundary selector might be
source-compatible without commuting with the entire Weyl representation.
That implication must be derived, not inserted as the definition of
admissibility.

## Hostile falsifier

Produce two distinct completed boundary selectors that preserve every named
theta/Tate constructor and normalization but whose ratio does not intertwine
the full Weyl pair. Such a pair would show that the declared constructor list
is incomplete or that full-Weyl compatibility is too strong to be authorized.
