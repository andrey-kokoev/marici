# Component-count augmentation descent audit

## Question

Which Carrier constructors descend through the quotient identifying every connected source type while retaining component multiplicity?

## Universal quotient

Let `A` be the free commutative monoid on connected Carrier isomorphism classes, and let

`epsilon:A -> N_init`

send every connected generator to the additive unit. This is a canonical additive monoid morphism and is universal among additive readouts assigning equal unit weight to all connected types. It supplies legitimate source provenance for a scalar component-count arithmetic lens; no preferred connected generator is needed.

## Exact descent criterion

For an additive constructor `F:A->A`, descent through `epsilon` means that there is an additive map `bar F:N_init->N_init` with

`epsilon F = bar F epsilon`.

Since additive endomorphisms of the initial monoid are multiplication by one class, this holds exactly when every connected generator `C` has the same output component count

`epsilon(F(C))=d`

for one fixed `d`. Then `bar F(n)=dn`. If two connected types produce different counts, the constructor distinguishes classes identified by the quotient and cannot descend.

Disjoint union and the identity descend. The derived rank-one arithmetic multiplication is constructed after quotienting and therefore descends internally. Generic restriction, residue, boundary, nearby-cycle, and supported-transform operations do not descend merely from their names or coherence laws; their component counts must satisfy the constant-degree test.

## Hostile witness

Take connected types `C` and `D` with a constructor yielding one connected output on `C` and two on `D`. The augmentation identifies `C` and `D`, but their images have counts one and two. No `bar F` exists. Equal matrix dimensions after a later realization cannot repair this failed quotient square.

## Authority boundary

The augmentation is independently characterized by a universal property, so it is authorized as a mathematical scalar readout of connected multiplicity. It is not authorized as a physical quotient or a faithful cross-sector carrier. The theta, Euler, and Archimedean operations constructed internally after reaching the initial arithmetic lens are valid there; they do not imply descent of unrelated pre-quotient Carrier constructors.

## Disposition

A source-derived arithmetic lens exists through canonical component-count augmentation, but its common-Carrier content is limited to equal-weight multiplicity. Pre-quotient constructor descent is governed by the constant connected-degree criterion and is not established for the operations needed to identify the radial G4 interface. The first return-to-objective gap is therefore still the owner-derived comparison from the labelled completed-zeta source to the G4 carrier, not arithmetic existence itself.
