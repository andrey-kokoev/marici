# The Reciprocal Normalization Anomaly Is Not a Finite-Jet Boundary Current

## The remaining repair proposal

The reciprocal augmentation-bivector audit leaves a forcing anomaly. One can
always call its antiderivative a boundary current, but that construction is
post hoc. The relevant question is whether a finite-order source-local
differential constructor produces the current uniformly over source modes.

For an exponential source mode

\[
h_\lambda(q)=e^{-\lambda q}h_0,
\qquad \lambda>0,
\]

the normalized reciprocal-sheet forcing difference is proportional to

\[
(1-2s)\lVert h_\lambda(q)\rVert^2
=(1-2s)e^{-2\lambda q}\lVert h_0\rVert^2.
\]

The irrelevant common factor may be removed. A source-local finite-jet repair
would require a polynomial `P` such that

\[
\frac{d}{dq}P(\partial_q)e^{-2\lambda q}=e^{-2\lambda q}
\]

for every positive `lambda`. Equivalently,

\[
(-2\lambda)P(-2\lambda)=1
\]

on an infinite set. No polynomial satisfies this identity: its required value
is the reciprocal multiplier `P(x)=1/x`.

## Consequence

The anomaly is not the derivative of any finite-jet current built uniformly
from the local source density. Its canonical primitive is

\[
J(q)=-\int_q^\infty\lVert h(v)\rVert^2dv,
\]

or, spectrally, an inverse-generator operation. That is nonlocal in the scale
coordinate. Naming it after observing the anomaly is the forbidden fake-current
construction.

Therefore primitive, square, seam, or archimedean data can repair the anomaly
only if their independently derived constructors already realize this inverse
scale operation. Merely matching finitely many local jets cannot do so.

## Multi-tower interpretation

The mate/equalizer tower needs another distinction:

- local coherence compares finite jets and boundary incidences;
- global coherence supplies an authorized inverse or homotopy along scale.

This is not necessarily another state tower. It is a higher coherence cell
certifying that the global contracting homotopy is source-derived and compatible
with both reciprocal sheets. Without that cell, converting the bulk anomaly to
a boundary term is tautological.

The next attack is now narrow: inspect the independently known primitive and
prime-square recursions for an inverse-scale homotopy. If they supply only
finite shifts or finite jets, they cannot close this gate.
