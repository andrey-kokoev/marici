# Forest--cycle Schur factorization conjecture for unbounded Weil positivity

## Problem

Prime translations compose exactly between local fibers, but exact path coherence does not control the Weil cross form. The first genuinely global information lives on mixed-prime cycles and on the survival of those cycles under completion.

## Conjecture

For every finite prime-power cutoff and logarithmic support cover, form the translated-interval graph `G_X`. Choose any spanning forest `T_X` and decompose its edge space into forest and fundamental-cycle coordinates.

The complete finite Weil form admits a source-derived block factorization

`Q_X = L_X* diag(P_X, C_X) L_X`,

where:

1. `P_X` is the forest transport form assembled from the complete one-prime support operators, including the proved prime-two gamma-tower block;
2. `C_X` is the mixed-cycle form obtained from residue-aware prime-rectangle 2-cells, not from a fitted Schur complement;
3. `P_X>=0` and `C_X>=0`;
4. changing the spanning forest acts by an invertible congruence on cycle coordinates and leaves `Q_X` unchanged;
5. cutoff inclusions intertwine `L_X`, forest transport, and cycle forms;
6. the projective limit has no additional radical beyond the compatible finite radicals.

The conjecture requires explicit source formulas for `P_X`, `C_X`, and `L_X`. Defining `C_X` as whatever Schur complement makes the identity true is circular.

## Why this addresses the remaining obstruction

- One-prime composition is carried by the forest paths, where no cycle coherence is needed.
- Mixed-prime discrepancies are localized in fundamental-cycle ports; the first is the `2 x 3` rectangle.
- Positive forest and cycle pivots imply every finite Weil Gram form is positive.
- Cutoff-compatible factorization and radical exactness imply unbounded positivity of the limit.

This gives a nontrivial mechanism: global positivity is assembled from already positive one-prime transport plus positive energies assigned to the exact cycle classes that common radial history forgets.

## Existing support

At finite cutoff, common radial history has kernel exactly the graph cycle space. A spanning-forest chord observer is dimension-minimal and restores faithfulness. Prime-two complete gamma-tower positivity supplies the first forest pivot. These results provide the correct dimensions and first positive edge but do not construct the cycle energy.

## Strongest falsifier

On the smallest graph containing the primes two and three, compute the complete four-cell Weil Gram on

`{1,2,3,6}`.

Eliminate a three-edge spanning tree using the independently constructed forest pivots. If the remaining one-dimensional rectangle Schur pivot is negative, the conjecture fails. If it vanishes while the rectangle is nonzero in the algebraic cycle quotient, completion faithfulness already fails. If its value changes incompatibly under another spanning tree after congruence, the proposed source factorization is defective.

## First unavailable object

The finite chord observer exists, but no source-derived positive form on its cycle coordinates is currently supplied. The valuation-only finite-place port cannot provide it because additive translation does not descend through valuation; residue data must be retained. The first construction is therefore a residue--valuation rectangle 2-cell whose norm is the `2 x 3` cycle pivot.

## Disposition

This is the next nonredundant RH-strength conjecture. Unlike positive Kolmogorov completion, it predicts a specific finite elimination structure and can fail first on one mixed-prime rectangle before any unbounded limit.
