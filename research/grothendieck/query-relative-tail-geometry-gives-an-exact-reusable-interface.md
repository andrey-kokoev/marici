## Three distinct failure controls

### Independent ranges lose the joint image

The point S=0, F=the negative minimum of the original block functional lies
inside both separate scalar ranges. It does not belong to their joint image:
nonnegative source masses with S=0 must all vanish, forcing F=0.
The compiler therefore retains a relation, not a rectangle of summaries.

### Reopening the fiber fabricates a coherent history

The frames S<=B_3/4 and S>=B_3/2 are separately feasible. Their persistent
conjunction is empty. The image-update constructor and direct full-source
refinement agree on that emptiness. Reapplying each frame to the original
polygon would instead fabricate two independently admitted steps.

### An undeclared language extension needs a lift

The map L has a nonzero kernel direction

    (c_2-c_3, c_3-c_1, c_1-c_2).

Two small, explicitly admitted perturbations of (1000,1000,1000) along that
direction have identical S and F but opposite answers to x_1<=1000. That new
bin-level query is not a function of the declared observables. It cannot be
added to the interface by assuming that one stored lift was the actual one.
A richer retained carrier or a newly justified lift is required.
