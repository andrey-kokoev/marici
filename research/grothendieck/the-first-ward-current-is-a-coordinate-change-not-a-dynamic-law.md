# The first Ward current is a coordinate change, not a dynamic law

## Value and current coordinates

Let the reciprocal one-sided value charts be (K(z)) and (K(-z)), and set

\[
F(z)=\frac{K(z)+K(-z)}2,
\qquad
G(z)=\frac{K(z)-K(-z)}2.
\]

The source-derived moving-incidence Ward identity gives the symmetric current

\[
J(z)+k(0)=-\frac12F(z)-zG(z).
\]

Here (F) is the scalar codiagonal and (G) is the antisymmetric dynamic
cokernel coordinate, up to the fixed completion factor used in the
three-channel packet.

## Invertibility away from the seam center

For (z\neq0), the map

\[
(F,G)\longmapsto(F,J)
\]

is invertible:

\[
G=-\frac{J+k(0)+F/2}{z}.
\]

Thus the current port retains the hidden antisymmetric value, but it adds no
new constraint. It is a source-derived change of coordinates on the same
two-dimensional dynamic packet.

On the scalar zero fiber,

\[
F(z)=0,
\]

the identity reduces to

\[
J(z)+k(0)=-zG(z).
\]

The current is therefore exactly the surviving cokernel coordinate multiplied
by the spectral displacement.

## Differentiated tower

Repeated differentiation yields

\[
J^{(m)}
=-\frac12F^{(m)}-zG^{(m)}-mG^{(m-1)}
\]

for (m\geq1), with the last term omitted at (m=0). Every finite Ward-current
jet is consequently an invertible triangular repackaging of the value jets
away from (z=0). No derivative order creates an independent equation.

This is a structural no-go, not a failed estimate. The Ward tower is faithful,
but faithfulness is not orientation.

## Hostile freedom

Choose arbitrary analytic germs (F) and (G) with (F(z_0)=0) at any
nonzero off-seam point (z_0). Defining (J) by the Ward identity satisfies
the complete differentiated Ward tower automatically. Hence the tower alone
cannot distinguish an allowed seam zero from a hypothetical off-seam zero.

Source positivity or continuation conditions may restrict which germs lift to
theta data, but that restriction is additional structure and must be stated
independently.

## Next DPC

The theta source supplies a second operation on (G), independent of the Ward
coordinate change, whose compatibility with the Ward transport forces any
zero-state to have (\operatorname{Re}z=0).

Candidates must be genuinely independent:

- reciprocal history propagation;
- a nonlocal heat-scale evolution;
- a Green boundary pairing involving an independent adjoint state;
- a nonlinear conservation law on the full labelled packet.

The falsifier is immediate: if the proposed law can be generated by
differentiating or algebraically composing the Ward identity, it adds no RH
force.
