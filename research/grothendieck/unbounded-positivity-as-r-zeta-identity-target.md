# Unbounded positivity as the radial comparison identity

## Operator stimulus

The operator proposed that unbounded positivity is likely required and may be the relevant notion of identity.

## Weakest precise conjecture

Let `D_zeta` be a source-defined dense test domain carrying the completed-zeta explicit-formula quadratic form `Q_zeta`. Let `U_G4` carry a closed polarized Green form `G_G4` with declared radical and quotient. The comparison should not be identified by a finite basis match alone. Its defining identity should be

`Q_zeta(f,g) = G_G4(R_zeta f, R_zeta g)`

for every `f,g` in `D_zeta`, together with label and reciprocal covariance on a common invariant core.

If `G_G4` is positive on the quotient and `R_zeta` has dense quotient range, this identity promotes source positivity exactly when the closed forms and domains agree. It makes `R_zeta` an isometry of form domains modulo radicals, not merely a matrix between finite fixtures.

## Why finite positivity is insufficient

The current positive certificate concerns one compact spline. A single positive value neither determines a quadratic form nor excludes a negative direction on another test. Finite cutoff positivity also does not imply positivity of the closed limit unless the forms converge in a topology controlling domains and reduced minimum modulus.

The required unbounded statement is positivity of `Q_zeta` on the full admissible Weil test domain. Under the standard explicit-formula criterion, that is RH-bearing; it cannot be assumed as an input to define `R_zeta`.

## Identity versus circularity

The form identity can characterize `R_zeta` only if the G4 form, source form, domains, radicals, and range condition are independently constructed. Defining `G_G4` by pulling back `Q_zeta`, or choosing `R_zeta` after inspecting zeros, is circular.

A viable direction is to derive both forms from one source incidence operator `B`:

`Q_zeta = B* J B`,

`G_G4 = C* J C`,

and construct an intertwiner `R_zeta` satisfying `C R_zeta = B` on a common core. Then equality of forms is a consequence of source factorization rather than a fitted equality.

## Strongest falsifier

Construct an exhaustion `D_N` of the proposed core. Require:

1. exact finite pullback identities on every `D_N`;
2. compatibility of inclusions and labels;
3. Mosco or strong-resolvent convergence of the closed forms;
4. no new completed radical;
5. a uniform quotient coercivity bound where coercivity is claimed.

A sequence with positive finite restrictions but a negative limiting direction, domain escape, new radical, or reduced-minimum-modulus collapse falsifies the identity claim.

## Disposition

The operator's proposal sharpens the target: the missing comparison is an unbounded form-domain identity modulo radicals. It does not remove the need for an owner-derived G4 form and intertwiner, but it explains why the finite fixture cannot be the identity object and specifies the completion theorem required for an RH-bearing result.
