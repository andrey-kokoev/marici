# Theta principal angle is perfect before aggregation and tautological after

## Bounded question

Can the labelled principal-angle reserve from packet 215 provide an
independent off-seam zero-confinement law?

## Common source trace

For every positive scale label \(q\), the translated tail and reflected seam
atoms satisfy

\[
g_q(0)=h_q(0)=\Phi(q).
\]

For a finite labelled packet \(c\), both endpoint maps are therefore the same
source row

\[
\tau_X(c)=\sum_{q\in Q_X}c_q\Phi(q).
\]

Before arithmetic aggregation, the direct and reciprocal incidence rows are
not merely transverse. They coincide in the common source frame.

## Upstream principal-angle theorem

Let \(P_X=\tau_X\), and let the transported dual row be the same common trace,
as forced by continuity of the undecomposed source across the cut. On the
orthogonal complement of \(\ker\tau_X\), the normalized principal-angle
reserve is

\[
\tau_X^{\rm angle}=1.
\]

Thus the rank-two quarter-turn hostile from packet 215 is rejected by the
actual pre-aggregation theta trace: an arbitrary unitary rotation of the
forcing fiber is not source-authorized sewing.

This is a genuine coherence theorem, but it is not seam-selective. The same
identity holds for every spectral parameter for which the finite packet is
defined. It protects the continuity of the source trace, not the nonvanishing
of the completed scalar section.

## Arithmetic aggregation

The common trace must still pass through a source-derived arithmetic map

\[
\lambda_{X,s}:\operatorname{Ran}\tau_X\longrightarrow L_{X,s},
\]

and then enter the four-channel additive Poisson incidence. The completed
scalar readout is a compression of that boundary-bearing object.

A nonzero labelled state may lie in the kernel of the completed aggregation
even though its two pre-aggregation traces coincide perfectly. Therefore
perfect upstream angle does not imply a nonzero scalar readout.

Moreover, \(\operatorname{Ran}\tau_X\) is one-dimensional whenever the trace
row is nonzero. Hence the restricted arithmetic map has the form

\[
\lambda_{X,s}|_{\operatorname{Ran}\tau_X}
=m_X(s)\,\iota_X
\]

for a scalar multiplier \(m_X(s)\) and a fixed nonzero target inclusion after
choosing a source frame. It follows that

\[
\ker\lambda_{X,s}\cap\operatorname{Ran}\tau_X
=0
\]

when \(m_X(s)\ne0\), while the full common trace line is killed when
\(m_X(s)=0\).

There is therefore no intermediate principal-angle geometry on the common
trace line. The unresolved divisor is exactly the zero set of the arithmetic
multiplier \(m_X\). Any proof that merely renames \(m_X\) as an angle, mass,
or incidence coefficient is circular.

If one instead defines the angle after scalar aggregation, its reserve
vanishes exactly when the completed scalar pairing vanishes. Such an angle is
a geometrical restatement of the zero condition, not an independent law
excluding it.

## Exact fork

The principal-angle programme has only two placements:

1. Before arithmetic aggregation, its reserve is source-derived and maximal,
   but insensitive to the critical seam and to zeros.
2. After arithmetic aggregation, it sees zeros, but proving its off-seam
   positivity is the original zero-confinement problem.

There is no intermediate scalar angle with both source authority and new RH
force unless the arithmetic map \(\lambda_{X,s}\) carries an independently
derived conservation, sign, or exactness law.

## Revised live object

The missing structure is therefore not transversality of the two raw source
traces. It is a law governing the kernel of the arithmetic Poisson aggregation
on their common image:

\[
\ker\lambda_{X,s}\cap\operatorname{Ran}\tau_X.
\]

At finite cutoff, the sharp hostile is a nonzero source packet \(c\) with

\[
\tau_X(c)\ne0,
\qquad
\lambda_{X,s}\tau_X(c)=0.
\]

Any proposed conservation law must reject this packet using primitive,
prime-square, connected-tail, and archimedean boundary data before scalar
aggregation.

## Explanation

The two sectors really do normalize into one common source boundary value.
That explains their coherence. But a shared meaning can still be annihilated
by a later readout. Agreement between perspectives is not the same as
faithfulness of the observation applied to their agreement.

This locates the RH burden more precisely: not in sewing the two sectors, but
in proving that the authorized arithmetic observation cannot erase their
common nonzero source state away from the seam.

## Scope

This packet proves perfect pre-aggregation trace alignment and shows why its
principal angle has no independent RH force. It does not construct
\(\lambda_{X,s}\), control its kernel, derive the required boundary-current
law, or prove RH.
