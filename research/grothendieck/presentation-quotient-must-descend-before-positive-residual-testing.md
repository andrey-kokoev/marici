# Presentation quotient must descend before positive-residual testing

## Question

What exact conditions allow presentation relations, observers, and the substantive residual form to live on one quotient?

## Presentation sequence

Let `V_I` be the coefficient space of a finite observer packet and let

\[
P_I\hookrightarrow V_I
\]

be the subspace generated by declared presentation relations, such as duplicate-label differences. Let

\[
q_I:V_I\longrightarrow Q_I=V_I/P_I.
\]

The presentation quotient is prior to observer faithfulness and positivity.

## Observer descent

Let

\[
O_I:V_I\longrightarrow M_I
\]

be the product of the selected observer coordinates. It factors through `Q_I` exactly when

\[
P_I\subseteq\ker O_I.
\]

The induced observer on `Q_I` is faithful exactly when

\[
\ker O_I=P_I.
\]

Thus the completeness residual is not `ker O_I` itself but the quotient

\[
\ker O_I/P_I.
\]

Declaring additional presentation relations after seeing this kernel would manufacture faithfulness and is prohibited.

## Form descent

Let `R_I` be the Hermitian substantive residual form. It descends to `Q_I` exactly when

\[
P_I\subseteq\operatorname{rad}(R_I),
\]

where

\[
\operatorname{rad}(R_I)
=
\{p\in V_I:R_I(p,v)=0\text{ for every }v\in V_I\}.
\]

The weaker condition `R_I(p,p)=0` is insufficient before positivity is known: an indefinite Hermitian form can have isotropic vectors that are not radical. Hence checking only zero diagonal value for a presentation relation is circular or incomplete.

For a Gram factorization `R_I=D_I^*D_I`, radical descent becomes

\[
P_I\subseteq\ker D_I.
\]

## Exact meta-observer contract

The quotient lane must verify, in order:

1. `P_I` is generated independently of observer outputs;
2. every observer annihilates `P_I`;
3. the substantive form annihilates `P_I` against all vectors;
4. observers are jointly faithful on `Q_I`;
5. the descended form on `Q_I` is positive.

Under a packet map, the presentation subspaces and quotients must form a commuting square. Otherwise a relation can disappear or be invented under refinement.

## Duplicate-label fixture

For two identical labels, `p=(1,-1)` is annihilated by every kernel column, so it belongs to the radical of the Gram form, not merely its isotropic cone. The quotient identifies the duplicate coordinates and leaves the one-point positive form. Near-duplicates do not belong to `P_I` and must retain their small positive eigenvalue.

## Disposition

Refine the five-residual calculus by an exact descent sequence. Presentation quotienting is admissible only through observer-kernel and form-radical checks. Joint faithfulness means exactness `ker O_I=P_I`, while positivity belongs to the descended substantive residual on `Q_I`.
