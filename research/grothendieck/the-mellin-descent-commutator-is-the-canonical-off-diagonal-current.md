# The Mellin--descent commutator is the canonical off-diagonal current

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

## Construction

Let a finite labelled packet be partitioned into fibers of a conductor
descent map. Let \(E\) be orthogonal conditional expectation onto functions
constant on each fiber, and let

\[
\Lambda e_n=(\log n)e_n
\]

be the source-derived Mellin scale generator. The old and new conductor
blocks are

\[
V_{\mathrm{old}}=\operatorname{im}E,\qquad
V_{\mathrm{new}}=\ker E.
\]

Define

\[
C=(1-E)\Lambda E:V_{\mathrm{old}}\longrightarrow V_{\mathrm{new}}.
\]

This is an off-diagonal source current. It is not chosen after inspecting a
Wronskian: both \(E\) and \(\Lambda\) precede scalar aggregation.

## Infinitesimal transport meaning

Conjugate descent by Mellin transport:

\[
E_z=e^{-z\Lambda}Ee^{z\Lambda}.
\]

Then

\[
\left.\frac{dE_z}{dz}\right|_{z=0}=[E,\Lambda].
\]

The old-to-new block of this commutator is \(-(1-E)\Lambda E=-C\).
Therefore \(C\) measures exactly the failure of conductor descent to commute
with vertical Mellin evolution.

## Variance identity

For \(f\in V_{\mathrm{old}}\), write \(f_y\) for its constant value on a
fiber \(F_y\), and let

\[
\overline{\lambda}_y=\frac1{|F_y|}\sum_{n\in F_y}\log n.
\]

Then

\[
(Cf)_n=(\log n-\overline{\lambda}_y)f_y,\qquad n\in F_y.
\]

Hence

\[
\|Cf\|^2
=
\frac1{|H|}\sum_y |f_y|^2
\frac1{|F_y|}\sum_{n\in F_y}
(\log n-\overline{\lambda}_y)^2.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
C^*C=E\Lambda(1-E)\Lambda E,
\]

and its fiberwise coefficient is the logarithmic variance. The current is
nonzero exactly where coarse descent identifies labels with distinct Mellin
scales.

## Cokernel interpretation

Entry 4098 showed that Fourier and ordinary refinement preserve the direct
sum and cannot reconstruct the complementary block. The commutator supplies
a canonical pairing with that block:

\[
\langle g,Cf\rangle,\qquad
g\in V_{\mathrm{new}},\ f\in V_{\mathrm{old}}.
\]

It detects the part of the complementary sector generated by scale
inhomogeneity inside a conductor fiber. This is the arithmetic analogue of
Strominger's universal current outside the ordinary transport image.

## What is and is not closed

The missing off-diagonal constructor now exists at every finite labelled
cutoff. Its positive norm is automatic and its coefficient is a source
variance. But it need not cover all of \(V_{\mathrm{new}}\); one scale
generator supplies at most one centered direction per fiber. Nor does its
existence alone orient the complex Wronskian.

The next gate is a cyclicity question: do the iterated centered Mellin
moments

\[
(1-E)\Lambda^kE,\qquad k\ge1,
\]

span the entire complementary fiber space? If so, the full new-conductor
block is generated by one source observable and its moment tower. If not,
the smallest orthogonal survivor identifies the next missing port.

## Finite falsifier

For each occupied fiber \(F\), form the centered Vandermonde matrix with
columns \(1,\log n,\ldots,(\log n)^{|F|-1}\). Distinct labels imply distinct
logarithms, so the full matrix is invertible. The substantive remaining
audit is whether the source authorizes the entire moment tower and whether
its conductor-refinement maps preserve this cyclic generation.
