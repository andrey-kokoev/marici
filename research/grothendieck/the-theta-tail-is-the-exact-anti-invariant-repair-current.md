# The Theta Tail Is the Exact Anti-Invariant Repair Current

## Source split

Write the completed positive source as

\[
\Phi=\phi_1+\tau,
\qquad
\tau=\sum_{n\geq2}\phi_n.
\]

Let the reflection involution act by

\[
(\mathsf Rf)(u)=f(-u),
\]

and define the anti-invariant projector

\[
\mathsf A=\frac{1-\mathsf R}{2}.
\]

## Exact incidence equation

Completed modular symmetry gives

\[
\mathsf A\Phi=0.
\]

By linearity,

\[
\mathsf A\tau=-\mathsf A\phi_1.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\tau(u)-\tau(-u)
=
\phi_1(-u)-\phi_1(u).
\]

Thus the aggregate higher-label tail carries exactly the opposite of the
primitive label's complete reflection defect. This holds as an analytic
identity, not merely derivative by derivative at the seam.

## Relation to the odd-jet tower

Differentiating at zero recovers every repair rung simultaneously:

\[
\tau^{(2j+1)}(0)
=
-\phi_1^{(2j+1)}(0)
\qquad
(j\geq0).

The first- and third-derivative repairs previously computed are shadows of this

## Why value-smallness was misleading

On the positive chamber, \(\tau\) is pointwise tiny relative to \(\phi_1\).
But reflection compares both chambers. The identity couples \(\tau(u)\) to
\(\tau(-u)\), so a one-sided relative-value norm forgets exactly the coordinate
in which the repair is large.

The correct object is therefore not a perturbation of values on one chamber.
It is a two-chamber correspondence:

\[
(\phi_1,\tau)
\longmapsto
\mathsf A\phi_1+\mathsf A\tau=0.
\]

This is the source incidence missing from ordinary Reynolds averaging.
Reflection averaging changes the primitive itself and loses positivity.
Theta completion adds a positive labelled tail whose anti-invariant current
cancels the primitive defect.

## Scope boundary

The identity explains reciprocal coherence and the whole seam-germ repair. It
does not orient the invariant component

\[
\mathsf E\Phi=\Phi.
\]

RH-relevant oscillatory positivity must act on that surviving invariant
component.

\[
\mathsf A\tau+\mathsf A\phi_1\ne0
\]

as an analytic identity, even when finitely many seam jets happen to cancel.
