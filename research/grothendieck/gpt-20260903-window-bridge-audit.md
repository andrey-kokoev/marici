# Audit of the 2026-09-03 GPT compact-window bridge packet

## Question

Does `references/20260903-gpt/` supply a noncircular global replacement for finite double-contact exclusion?

## Claim boundary

The packet supplies a coherent **compact-rectangle reduction**, conditional on replay of its localized Weil-form margin and generation of a full-source truncation-error certificate. It does not supply the missing unbounded-parameter promotion to RH.

## Findings

### Exact useful interface

With the declared Suzuki additive convention, the proposed identity

\[
Q_W(v_{t,\xi})=2\Theta(t,\xi)
\]

maps shifted Gaussians into the complete signed Weil form rather than a positive zero-side surrogate. If the convention factors are independently checked, localized positivity for `P_L v` plus

\[
|Q_W(v)-Q_W(P_Lv)|
<\lambda_L\|P_Lv\|_2^2
\]

proves positivity on the declared compact parameter rectangle.

The norm formula in the template is consistent with

\[
\|P_Lv_{t,\xi}\|_2^2
=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{\pi t}}\operatorname{erf}\!\left(\frac{L}{\sqrt t}\right).
\]

The supplied `verify_window_bridge.py --self-test` passes. The verifier checks schema and the final decimal inequality; it does not certify any analytic error component.

### Missing evidence

The stated `L=0.8` margin `8.9e-18` is not admitted evidence because the supplementary certificate was not retrieved or replayed. The packet itself records this limitation. Its metadata discrepancy and retracted earlier support claim increase the need for independent replay but do not by themselves refute the theorem.

The bridge generator is absent. Endpoint, gamma, finite prime overlap, infinite prime tail, quadrature, and rounding entries in the JSON remain placeholders. Consequently no window is presently certified.

### Global obstruction remains

A bridge over one compact `t` interval is strictly weaker than RH. A finite collection of such bridges becomes an RH reduction only after a source-derived theorem gives either:

1. one bounded `t` range containing every possible first contact; or
2. uniform positivity outside that range.

Neither theorem appears in the packet. Its own margin-versus-tail diagnostic suggests the `L=0.8` margin may cover only approximately `t<0.0174`, or `t<0.00845` under square-root tail scaling. Increasing `L` does not automatically solve this because the localized margin may decay faster than the Gaussian truncation error.

Thus the compact-window bridge can replace the positive-tail cone on bounded small-`t` rectangles, but it does not repair the unproved global cutoff identified in `exact-prefix-positive-tail-contact-obstruction.md`.

The correct global question is not whether each finite `L` has a positive margin. It is whether there exists a declared function `L(t)` such that

\[
E_W(t,L(t))<\lambda_{L(t)}\|P_{L(t)}v_t\|_2^2
\]

for every unbounded `t`. Gaussian truncation requires `L(t)/sqrt(t)` not to collapse, hence `L(t)` must grow at least on the scale `sqrt(t)` for a fixed mass loss. The cited localized margins deteriorate from `8.9e-18` at `L=0.8` to an upper scale near `3.2e-283` at `L=2`. Without an asymptotic lower bound on `lambda_L` that beats the complete Weil-form truncation error along some `L(t)`, the bridge is not globally composable. The packet supplies no such comparison theorem.

### Exact-cardinal obstruction

The Paley--Wiener/Jensen count correctly blocks a nonzero finite-exponential-type function from vanishing at all but finitely many zeta zeros: its zero count is `O(R)`, whereas the zeta multiset has order `R log R`. This eliminates exact compact-support cardinal isolation, not uniform nonzero suppression.

## Disposition

Retain the packet as a conditional local architecture and verifier schema. Do not promote its Zhu margin, any populated bridge certificate, or a global RH consequence until:

- the external theorem and numerical certificate are retrieved and replayed;
- every source-error component is generated by audited interval code;
- the convention identity is independently derived against the established Marici normalization;
- an unbounded-parameter promotion theorem is supplied.
