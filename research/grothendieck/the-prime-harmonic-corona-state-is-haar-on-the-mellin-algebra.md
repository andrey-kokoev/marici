# The Prime-Harmonic Corona State Is Haar on the Mellin Algebra

## The authorized subalgebra

Let \(\mathcal H=\ell^2(\mathbb P)\).  Vertical Mellin transport acts by the
diagonal unitaries

\[
U_t e_p=p^{-it}e_p,
\qquad t\in\mathbb R.
\]

Let \(\mathcal A_M\) be the unital C*-algebra generated by the \(U_t\).  Its
diagonal symbols are the uniform closure of finite linear combinations of

\[
p\longmapsto p^{-it}.
\]

Equivalently, they are restrictions to the prime logarithms of almost
periodic functions on \(\mathbb R\).  Unlike the full space of bounded
sequences possessing a prime-harmonic mean, \(\mathcal A_M\) is closed under
multiplication for a source-derived reason:

\[
U_tU_u=U_{t+u}.
\]

This is the smallest natural comparison algebra on which the corona-state
uniqueness question can be asked without inventing a boundary product.

## Prime-harmonic cutoff states

With

\[
H_X=\sum_{p\le X}\frac1p,
\qquad
v_X=H_X^{-1/2}
\sum_{p\le X}p^{-1/2}e_p,
\]

define

\[
\omega_X(A)=\langle v_X,Av_X\rangle.
\]

On a Mellin generator,

\[
\omega_X(U_t)
=\frac1{H_X}\sum_{p\le X}p^{-1-it}.
\]

For \(t=0\), this equals \(1\).  For every fixed \(t\ne0\), the prime number
theorem and partial summation give

\[
\sum_{p\le X}p^{-1-it}=O_t(1),
\]

whereas

\[
H_X=\log\log X+O(1).
\]

Therefore

\[
\lim_{X\to\infty}\omega_X(U_t)
=
\begin{cases}
1,&t=0,\\
0,&t\ne0.
\end{cases}
\]

## Haar selection theorem

The characters \(U_t\) span a dense self-adjoint subalgebra of
\(\mathcal A_M\).  Since the \(\omega_X\) are states, convergence on this
dense span extends to every element of \(\mathcal A_M\).  The limit state
\(\omega_M\) is uniquely determined by

\[
\omega_M(U_t)=0
\quad(t\ne0),
\qquad
\omega_M(I)=1.
\]

Under the identification of the almost-periodic algebra with continuous
functions on the Bohr compactification, \(\omega_M\) is Haar integration.

Thus the nonuniqueness of arbitrary Calkin cluster states disappears after
restriction to the source-authorized Mellin algebra.  Every subnet cluster

## Scope and next gate

The Mellin algebra contains vertical transport but not yet the non-diagonal
seam, prime-creation, Toeplitz, or theta--Tate completion operators.  Haar
uniqueness on \(\mathcal A_M\) therefore does not determine the full RH
corona state.

\[
