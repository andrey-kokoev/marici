# The seven-shift cross packet retains the double pole annihilator

## Potential defect

The generalized checker omits the completed-zeta pole evaluations. This is valid for the baseline spline only because its Laurent shift multiplier has double zeros at the two pole characters. A translated cross packet must retain those zeros or the negative Gram certificate would be evaluating the wrong form.

## Exact factorization

Let the baseline Laurent multiplier be

`P(z)=z^2-5z+33/4-5z^(-1)+z^(-2)`

`=(z+z^(-1)-5/2)^2`.

The symmetric translation by one shift unit multiplies it by

`(z+z^(-1))/2`.

Expanding gives the seven-shift coefficients

`(1/2,-5/2,37/8,-5,37/8,-5/2,1/2)`.

Therefore the cross multiplier is

`P_cross(z)=((z+z^(-1))/2)P(z)`.

The double zeros of `P` at `z=2` and `z=1/2` remain double zeros of `P_cross`. Hence the two completed-zeta pole evaluations still vanish for the cross packet.

The coherent and disagreement autocorrelations have multipliers

`2P(z)(1 plus or minus (z+z^(-1))/2)`

up to the declared translation orientation, and also retain the same double pole-annihilator.

## Disposition

Pole omission is not the source of the negative two-translate determinant. The corrected `N=3` disagreement energy is

`[-0.008709098829,-0.008708642965]`

in the declared executable form. External source authority and independent evaluator reproduction remain the only criterion-level gates.
