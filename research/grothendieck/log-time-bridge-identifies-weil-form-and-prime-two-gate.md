# The completed log-time bridge is the Weil form, leaving prime-two gluing

After reflection and endpoint completion, the signed current suggested by the
log-time Fourier bridge is exactly the centered Weil explicit-formula
distribution

```
W(h)=W_infinity(h)+W_endpoint(h)
 -sum_(n>=2)Lambda(n)n^(-1/2)
  [h(log n)+h(-log n)].                               (1)
```

For convolution squares `h=g*g*`, its spectral side is the regularized sum
of `|G((rho-1/2)/i)|^2`. Therefore

```
RH  iff  W(g*g*)>=0 for every admissible g.           (2)
```

The new shell, Adams, and mapping-cone language does not evade this
equivalence. A global positive factorization of the completed current would
prove Weil positivity and hence RH; it cannot be assumed merely because the
prime and gamma measures now share a Fourier variable.

## What the new work contributes

The current synthesis still sharpens the construction problem:

- quarter shift and gamma phase derive the smooth reference and `7/8`;
- cumulative ranks derive an integer Weyl allocation;
- shell no-go theorems prohibit radial scalarization and hard/soft physical
  truncation;
- an acyclic coefficient--Betti tail can regularize without changing net
  Weyl multiplicity;
- Adams doubling forces the first two determinant coefficients;
- the log-time transform gives the correct measure-level comparison.

All of these must now be tested against the already canonical form `W`, not
used to define a replacement functional.

## First genuinely open gluing gate

Short-support Weil positivity supplies unconditional positive local blocks.
Choose logarithmic cells narrower than `log 2`. The first arithmetic coupling
between two cells appears at separation `log 2`, with weight
`(log 2)/sqrt(2)`. If `A_0` is the local positive Weil form and `B_2` the
combined archimedean-plus-prime-two cross form, two-cell positivity is exactly

```
||A_0^(-1/2) B_2 A_0^(-1/2)|| <= 1.                 (3)
```

This is the next noncircular attack. It uses no zero data and can be falsified
by one pair of short-support test functions whose normalized cross pairing
exceeds one.

Passing (3) would not prove RH: cycles and later prime-power edges may still
destroy global positivity. But failing it would kill the current local-gluing
explanation immediately. The mapping-cone proposal earns relevance only if
it derives this contraction or a dilation implying it.

## Avoided circularity

No new ledger claim should say that the completed current has been made
positive. We have identified its source decomposition and narrowed a local
operator inequality. The global positivity statement remains precisely RH.
