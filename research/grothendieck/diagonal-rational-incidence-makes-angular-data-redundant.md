# Diagonal Rational Incidence Makes Angular Data Redundant

## Question

Local unramified Fourier--Tate transport stays spherical. Could the diagonal
embedding of rational points into the adeles nevertheless create an
independent angular--radial channel before scalar compression?

For the ordinary Riemann source, the answer is no. Diagonal incidence restores
global compatibility, but its finite angular coordinates are determined by
the complete radial valuation packet.

## Complete valuation faithfulness

For a positive rational number \(r\), write

\[
r=\prod_p p^{v_p(r)},
\]

where only finitely many integers \(v_p(r)\) are nonzero. Unique factorization
gives an injective map

\[
\mathbb Q_{>0}^{\times}
\longrightarrow
\bigoplus_p\mathbb Z,
\qquad
r\longmapsto (v_p(r))_p.
\]

At a fixed place \(p\), write

\[
r=p^{v_p(r)}u_p(r),
\qquad
u_p(r)\in\mathbb Z_p^\times.
\]

The unit coordinate is

\[
u_p(r)=\prod_{\ell\ne p}\ell^{v_\ell(r)}
\]

as an element of \(\mathbb Z_p^\times\). Hence \(u_p(r)\), and therefore every
finite-conductor residue of \(u_p(r)\), is a function of the valuation
coordinates at the other primes.

The sign is the only additional coordinate on
\(\mathbb Q^\times\). For the positive winding labels used by the Riemann
theta source, even that coordinate has already been fixed.

## Meaning of the Ramanujan carrier

Ramanujan packets remain valuable because they exhibit the finite-conductor
Fourier shadows of diagonal rational incidence. They test whether local
residue presentations are compatible with one global rational label.

But on the authorized diagonal source they do not supply a new independent
state variable. They are coherence witnesses for the radial valuation packet.
Once all valuations are retained, adjoining every angular residue duplicates
information already present.

This explains why two apparently different completions coexist:

- the radial prime-power/Fock tower records the rational label directly;
- the angular Schwartz--Bruhat carrier records its compatible residue shadows.

Their comparison tests provenance. It does not enlarge the scalar Riemann
section by an independent angular degree of freedom.

## Consequence for the RH programme

The hoped-for finite angular rescue is now closed twice:

1. every local unramified Fourier--Tate operation preserves the spherical
   subspace;
2. the global diagonal embedding makes the surviving angular residues
   functions of the complete valuation vector.

Therefore an RH-bearing cross-place law cannot merely compare radial and
angular descriptions of the same rational label. It must act on something
not reconstructible from either presentation separately.

The smallest remaining candidates are:

- the relative completion current at the archimedean boundary;
- a determinant-line comparison between additive theta summation and
  multiplicative Euler formation;
- or a higher coherence witnessing that these two constructions of the same
  rational packet commute through completion.

This retypes the missing object. It is not another data port. It is a
comparison-of-constructions port.

## Falsifier

This theorem would fail for a source whose local angular coordinates can vary
while every valuation remains fixed. Such a source is necessarily larger than
the diagonal positive-rational orbit, for example a general finite adele or a
ramified character packet.

Any proposed use of that extra freedom for the Riemann section must therefore
derive why the enlarged source is authorized and why its compression still
has the Riemann divisor. Otherwise it changes the problem rather than
explaining it.
