# The derivative companion is the oriented velocity of the unitary defect

## 1. Canonical first-order companion

Let

\[
  A(z)=\widehat X(z)=\frac{X(z)}{X(0)}
  =
  \langle\Omega,e^{izQ}\Omega\rangle.
\]

Character differentiation gives

\[
  A'(z)
  =
  i\langle\Omega,Qe^{izQ}\Omega\rangle.
\]

Thus \(A'\) is not an arbitrary scalar companion. It is the source-derived
current obtained by inserting the logarithmic position generator into the
vacuum crossing.

At a simple real zero \(x_0\),

\[
  A(x_0)=0,
  \qquad
  A'(x_0)\ne0.
\]

The unitary defect determinant \(|A(x)|^2\) loses orientation because it
vanishes quadratically. The derivative records the signed first-order
crossing velocity.

## 2. Oriented localization

Choose the orientation

\[
  E(z)=A(z)+iA'(z).
\]

Because \(A\) is real entire,

\[
  E^\#(z)=A(z)-iA'(z),
\]

and

\[
  A(z)=\frac{E(z)+E^\#(z)}2.
\]

At a simple real zero,

\[
  E(x_0)=iA'(x_0),
\]

so the oriented localization remains nonzero while its symmetric shadow
vanishes.

## 3. Exact vertical-growth identity

For \(z=x+iy\),

\[
  \partial_yA(z)=iA'(z).
\]

Consequently

\[
  |E(z)|^2-|E^\#(z)|^2
  =
  2\,\partial_y|A(z)|^2.
\]

Therefore the Hermite--Biehler condition for this derivative companion is
exactly outward vertical monotonicity:

\[
  |E(z)|>|E^\#(z)|
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \partial_y|A(z)|^2>0
  \qquad(y>0).
\]

This reproduces the de Branges diagonal reduction from the unitary-defect
geometry.

## 4. Meaning

The two oriented sectors are:

\[
  E=A+iA',
  \qquad
  E^\#=A-iA'.
\]

Their symmetric projection forgets crossing direction and returns \(A\).
The half-plane Hermite--Biehler inequality says that the derivative current
orients every projective chart crossing consistently.

\[
\boxed{
\text{RH orientation}
=
\text{coherent sign of the defect velocity after complex rotation}.}
\]

This gives the derivative companion a hard-to-vary meaning: it is the
Noether/current insertion generated by character transport.

## 5. No-shortcut audit

Every sufficiently regular positive source has the same derivative
construction. Hostile Fourier-stable sources also produce

\[
  E=A+iA'.
\]

They fail the Hermite--Biehler inequality precisely when their overlap has
off-real zeros. Hence the construction of \(E\) is explanatory typing, while
positivity of its de Branges kernel remains the RH-strength statement.

The missing arithmetic theorem is not “find a companion.” It is:

> Derive the sign of the character-current flux from labelled modular sewing
> before aggregating to \(A+iA'\).

## 7. Scope

The generator insertion, oriented companion, and vertical-growth identity
are exact. They explain the meaning of \(A'\) but do not prove its required
orientation. No positive labelled current identity or RH theorem is
established.
