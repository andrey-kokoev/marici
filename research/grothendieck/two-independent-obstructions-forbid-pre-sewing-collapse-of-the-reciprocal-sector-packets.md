# Two Independent Obstructions Forbid Pre-Sewing Collapse of the Reciprocal Sector Packets

## Candidate compression

The compressed architecture `3+2+2+1` would identify corresponding objects of
the two reciprocal sector packets before the final seam comparison. A strict
identification must preserve both endpoint normalization and the typed
augmentation/control ports.

Two exact witnesses forbid that identification.

## Endpoint obstruction

The two source-natural inverse-scale homotopies are

\[
(H_0g)(q)=\int_0^qg(v)dv,
\qquad
(H_\infty g)(q)=-\int_q^\infty g(v)dv.
\]

Their difference is the seam period

\[
H_0g-H_\infty g=\int_0^\infty g(v)dv.
\]

For a positive exponential mode this is nonzero. Therefore identifying the
two homotopies before retaining their comparison forces a nonzero period to
zero. The identification is lossy.

## Port obstruction

In the exact finite Fourier model,

\[
F_N\Omega=Ne_0,
\qquad
F_Ne_0=\Omega.
\]

For `N>1`, the augmentation line and control line are distinct. Identifying
them before Fourier sewing erases the distinction between a distributed
common mode and a point-supported zero-frequency port.

One can manufacture a Fourier eigenline by mixing the two, but that line is
not either source port. Such a diagonalization is a presentation change after
forgetting the operational types, not a source-authorized identification.

## Minimal strict architecture

The two obstructions are independent:

- the first concerns endpoint normalization and a nonzero period;
- the second concerns Fourier support and port type.

Hence each reciprocal sector must retain its own internally coherent packet
until the final mate is formed. Under the current constructor inventory, the
minimal strict architecture is

\[
2(3+2+1)+1.
\]

Each `3+2+1` contains three typed state towers, two directed witness towers,
and their internal coherence tower. The outer `2` retains reciprocal sector
identity. The final `1` carries the seam comparison, including the period and
Fourier port rotation.

## Scope

This is a minimality result relative to the declared types and constructors.
It does not assert that the notation is a universal categorical invariant.
A future source-derived equivalence could compress the diagram only if it
preserves both the nonzero period and the augmentation/control distinction as
explicit residual data. Such a compression would be an equivalence of the
doubled packet, not an identification of its objects.
