# Boundary-wave-packet audit of the interval logarithmic remainder

## Question

Is

`K_L=Gamma_L-log(1+sqrt(-Delta_D))`

bounded on `L^2((-L,L))`, or does boundary concentration leave an unbounded logarithmic remainder?

## Interior principal symbol

Both operators have principal growth `log|u|`. Their interior difference is order zero. The only possible unbounded residual is therefore a boundary singular Green/Hankel term; the explicit formula alone does not remove this boundary-calculus obligation.

## Boundary dilation test

Near one endpoint, use the normalized packet

`f_delta(x)=delta^(-1/2) phi((L-x)/delta)`,

with `phi` supported in a fixed compact subset of the positive half-line. Zero-extension Fourier scaling gives

`Gamma_L(f_delta,f_delta)=log(delta^(-1))||phi||_2^2+C_res(phi)+o(1)`.

The half-line Dirichlet spectral model uses the sine transform. Its logarithmic form has the same scaling:

`<log(1+sqrt(-Delta_D))f_delta,f_delta>`

`=log(delta^(-1))||phi||_2^2+C_spec(phi)+o(1)`.

Hence the leading boundary logarithm cancels in `K_L`. A single concentrating packet cannot produce the hostile residual `-log(delta^(-1))`.

This is evidence for order-zero remainder, not a proof: boundedness requires a uniform estimate of `C_res(phi)-C_spec(phi)` over all normalized packet profiles and superpositions.

## Exact half-line reduction

On the half-line, write `C phi` and `S phi` for cosine and sine transforms. The zero-extension Fourier energy contains both transform channels, while the spectral Dirichlet logarithm uses the sine channel. After matching Plancherel normalizations, the remainder is a logarithmic cosine--sine imbalance plus lower bounded terms.

The required theorem is a uniform weighted transform estimate of the form

`| integral log(1+xi)(|C phi|^2-|S phi|^2) dxi |`

`<= C ||phi||_2^2`.

Equivalently, this is boundedness of the boundary Hankel/commutator operator generated by the logarithmic symbol. Standard interior pseudodifferential estimates do not prove it; one needs the half-line boundary symbol.

## Cheapest falsifier

Choose an orthonormal or weakly null sequence of half-line profiles `phi_n` and compute the cosine--sine imbalance. If it diverges, `K_L` is not order zero. Simple dilations are not enough because their leading logarithms cancel. Candidate hostile families are profiles with increasing oscillation combined with endpoint concentration, or Mellin-localized packets probing the boundary Hankel symbol.

## Consequence for the natural log-form construction

Even if `K_L` is unbounded on `L^2`, the natural zero-extension logarithmic form remains closed and semibounded after bounded prime and endpoint perturbations. What fails would be only comparison with the spectral Dirichlet logarithm and its explicit sine-mode threshold. Compact resolvent and finite local negative index survive.

## Disposition

The order-zero comparison is plausible but unproved. The elementary boundary concentration attack does not falsify it because both logarithmic operators carry the same `log(delta^(-1))` scaling. The next exact object is the half-line logarithmic cosine--sine imbalance operator.
