\[

controlled infinite packet when its weighted total variation is uniformly
bounded after the lowest exponential is factored out.

\[

\qquad h_K(q)=q^{2K}e^{-q^2}.
\]

\[

\[

finite integer heat packet or a controlled infinite packet of the stated
class. The global source constructor rejects the hostile before scalar
