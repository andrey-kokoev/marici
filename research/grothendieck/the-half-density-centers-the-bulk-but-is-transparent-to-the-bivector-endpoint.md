# The Half-Density Centers the Bulk but Is Transparent to the Bivector Endpoint

## Endpoint audit

The canonical half-density is

\[
W(q)=e^{q/2}.
\]

It centers the scale differential, but at the finite seam

\[
W(0)=1.
\]

Therefore it leaves every endpoint state at `q=0` unchanged. In particular,
for the augmentation bivector

\[
\widetilde W_s(q)=e^{q/2}\Omega\wedge G_s(q),
\]

one has

\[
\widetilde W_s(0)=\Omega\wedge G_s(0).
\]

Scalar nullity kills only the augmentation projection of `G_s(0)`. It does
not kill this bivector endpoint.

## Exact lifted hostile state

Take

\[
\Omega=(1,1),\qquad v=(1,-1),\qquad
f(q)=e^{-2q}v,\qquad s=\frac13.
\]

The canonical tail is

\[
G_s(q)=\frac35e^{-2q}v.
\]

Its scalar augmentation vanishes identically. After half-density lift,

\[
\widetilde G_s(q)=\frac35e^{-3q/2}v,
\qquad
\widetilde f(q)=e^{-3q/2}v,
\qquad
z=s-\frac12=-\frac16.
\]

Writing `D` for the squared norm of `Omega wedge v`, the centered bivector
Green packet is

\[
\widetilde E=\frac{3D}{25},
\qquad
\widetilde B=\frac{9D}{25},
\qquad
\widetilde F=\frac D5.
\]

It obeys

\[
2\operatorname{Re}(z)\widetilde E
=\widetilde B-2\widetilde F
=-\frac D{25}.
\]

Thus the centered identity admits an exact off-seam scalar-null state with a
nonzero relationship endpoint.

## Consequence

The half-density mate explains the spectral center and transforms the bulk
metric correctly. It does not close the boundary packet. A further
source-derived incidence must map the pair-label bivector endpoint into the
primitive, square, seam, or archimedean currents.

The required channel is now tightly typed: it must be quadratic in labels,
live at the finite seam, and preserve the nonzero relationship state while
making the total completed flux vanish. A scalar boundary correction cannot
do this faithfully.
