# The interface complex is normalized source boundary data

## Transfer audit

Three independent team results determine how the interface complex `B_s`
must be built.

Strominger's simplicial-braid result distinguishes a cycle from a genuine
residue. Vanishing under every face map produces a Brunnian-type cycle, but
authorized higher fillings may still make that cycle a boundary. The
irreducible datum is the quotient of simultaneous face kernels by declared
fillings.

Aspect's adaptive-cell falsifier supplies the authority rule. One may not add
a new higher cell whenever a residual appears. The generators, face maps,
degeneracies, filling operations, and stabilization depth must be frozen from
the source before testing the residual.

Nima's associator interferometer supplies the readout rule. A higher class is
not identified by its separate endpoint magnitudes. It is identified by a
coherent comparison of two constructor routes, with a source-derived
common-mode control that removes apparatus phase.

Benincasa's conductor calculation adds a boundary warning. Two individually
source-prescribed wall logarithms do not cancel wall to wall. The full
relative object must retain bulk current, endpoint faces, conductor terms,
and their chain homotopy. Hence `B_s` cannot be the direct sum of selected
wall residues.

## Candidate construction

Let `B_n(s)` be the source-labelled interface packets at constructor depth
`n`. Restriction to a proper boundary component gives face maps

\[
d_i:B_n(s)\longrightarrow B_{n-1}(s),
\]

and source-authorized insertion or filling gives degeneracy and filling maps.
The frozen operations should form a simplicial, semi-simplicial, or Čech-type
object before scalarization.

Its normalized chain complex is

\[
N_n(B_s)=\bigcap_{i<n}\ker d_i,
\qquad
\partial=(-1)^n d_n
\]

under the usual normalization convention. Equivalent normalized models are
acceptable when derived from the same source maps.

Write `Z_n` for the simultaneous face cycles and `F_n` for the subgroup of
source-authorized fillings. The genuine interface residue is then

\[
H_n(N(B_s))
=
\frac{Z_n}{F_n}.
\]

This gives concrete content to the previous theorem

\[
C_+\times_B^h C_-\simeq B[-1].
\]

The `B` on the right should be the normalized full boundary-current complex,
not an untyped boundary value space.

## Three-tower interpretation

The transfer also clarifies the multi-tower language.

1. The plus tower records one valuation-sector resolution.
2. The minus tower records its Fourier–Tate dual resolution.
3. The interface tower records all common boundary faces and authorized
   fillings.

The comparison between the first two towers is itself computed through the
third. The global class is the degree shift of the interface residue. No
further tower should be added merely because the residue is nonzero; a new
cell is allowed only if it belongs to the preregistered source signature.

## Observable bridge

The scalar determinant or Evans readout must be formed after coherent route
comparison. Schematically, for target and control constructor routes,

\[
z_t=\kappa\,\omega_t,
\qquad
z_c=\kappa,
\qquad
z_t/z_c=\omega_t.
\]

Here the shared factor `kappa` must be proved common by theta/Tate transport.
Without that proof, a scalar phase delay cannot be attributed to an interface
class. This is the precise readout gate missing from the bare derived
pullback theorem.

## Frozen finite falsifier

Before examining zeros, freeze a finite interface signature containing:

- primitive and prime-square boundary currents;
- connected tail current;
- archimedean endpoint current;
- mixed reciprocal-seam current;
- restriction face maps;
- source-authorized filling maps;
- one common-mode comparison map to the scalar readout.

Then test:

1. the simplicial or Čech identities among face and filling maps;
2. whether the typed defect is a normalized cycle;
3. whether it is a boundary of an authorized filling;
4. whether the target/control comparison detects its quotient class;
5. whether a hostile symmetric multiplier changes the readout without
   producing a class in the frozen interface complex.

The route fails if it needs a packet-specific cell, if wall-only scalar
cancellation substitutes for the full relative current, or if the supposed
class disappears under an already authorized filling.

## Scope

This packet transfers proved structural mechanisms from the braid,
contextual-control, associator, and relative-boundary programmes. It proposes
the correct type of `B_s`; it does not yet derive the theta/Tate face maps,
compute its homology, prove the detector bridge, or prove RH.
