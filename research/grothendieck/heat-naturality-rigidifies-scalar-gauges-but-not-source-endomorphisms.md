# Heat Naturality Rigidifies Scalar Gauges but Not Source Endomorphisms

## The larger heat commutant

The scalar-multiplier theorem remains exact: multiplication by \(H(z)\)
commutes with \(\partial_z^2\) only when \(H\) is constant. But scalar
multipliers are not the full endomorphism algebra of the heat-bearing source.

Every constant-coefficient differential operator

\[
T=P(\partial_z)
\]

commutes with the Newman generator:

\[
[\partial_z^2,T]=0.
\]

Even operators such as

\[
T_{N,c}=I+c\partial_z^{2N}
\]

also preserve reciprocal parity and conjugation reality.

## Exact divisor-changing witness

Apply \(T_{N,c}\) to the even polynomial \(F(z)=z^{2N}\). One obtains

\[
T_{N,c}F=z^{2N}+c(2N)!.
\]

For positive \(c\) and even \(N\), its roots form reciprocal-conjugate orbits
and none lies on the imaginary seam. Thus a heat-natural linear endomorphism
can change the divisor and create off-seam zeros.

On the Fourier side, \(\partial_z^{2N}\) acts by \((-1)^Nu^{2N}\). For even
\(N\), the multiplier

\[
1+cu^{2N}
\]

is positive on the real source coordinate. Heat covariance, parity, reality,
and positive spectral reweighting therefore still do not select the theta
initial state.

## Correct scope of canonical-section rigidity

Newman heat naturality proves:

```text
no divisor-bearing scalar gauge is a presentation change.
```

It does not prove:

```text
the heat equation uniquely determines the source section.
```

The heat equation is dynamics. Its commutant contains transformations that
change the initial condition while respecting the dynamics.

## Missing source invariant

To reject \(T_{N,c}\), the construction must retain data not fixed by the heat
generator alone. Candidate invariants include:

- the exact labelled theta seed;
- its lattice weights and primitive/square filtration;
- the boundary value at one heat time as a content-addressed object;
- or a constructor law that excludes polynomial spectral reweighting.

This is the precise form of the remaining provenance gate: dynamics plus an
authorized initial object, not dynamics alone.

## Scope

The polynomial witness is not claimed to be the completed theta section. It
falsifies the inference from heat naturality to full source uniqueness. The
scalar-gauge theorem remains valid and useful.
