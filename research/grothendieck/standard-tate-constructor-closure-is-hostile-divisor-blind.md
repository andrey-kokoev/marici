# Standard Tate Constructor Closure Is Hostile-Divisor Blind

## Constructor class

Consider the source operations already established in the programme:

1. restricted tensor formation from one archimedean profile and the standard
   unramified finite vectors;
2. placewise Fourier transform;
3. diagonal rational sampling;
4. additive Poisson summation;
5. Mellin transport;
6. reciprocal functional-equation sewing;
7. retention of the two polar boundary currents.

Each operation is source-derived and together they construct the completed
Tate comparison cell.

## Hostile substitution

Replace only the archimedean Gaussian profile by a Schwartz profile \(h\)
having the same Fourier eigenvalue and the required reality and parity. Keep
every finite unramified vector unchanged.

Then:

- the restricted tensor product still exists;
- placewise Fourier covariance still holds;
- diagonal sampling and Poisson summation still hold;
- labelwise Mellin transport still compares additive and multiplicative
  presentations;
- reciprocal sewing still gives the scalar functional equation;
- the finite prime-power and Fock provenance is unchanged.

Nima's degree-12 hostile supplies an explicit member of this class whose
Mellin window has off-critical zeros while its nonzero integer samples remain
positive. Therefore every invariant functorially generated by the operations
above is compatible with an off-seam divisor.

## No-go theorem

The standard Tate constructor closure cannot by itself imply RH.

This is not because one of its squares fails to commute. They commute for too
large a source class. The hostile substitution changes the scalar divisor
without violating their declared coherences.

The Gaussian annihilation equation rejects the hostile profile, but its
Mellin image is only the archimedean gamma recurrence. When tensored with the
unchanged finite source it does not constrain zeros of the Euler factor.

Hence the missing operation must simultaneously use:

- the Gaussian vacuum law at infinity;
- diagonal arithmetic labels;
- and a boundary or comparison current surviving Mellin compression.

No currently derived constructor has all three inputs.

## Real obstacle

The programme has reached an honest structural boundary. A new theorem cannot
be obtained by adding another coherence cell built functorially from standard
Tate operations. It requires a nonseparable Gaussian--arithmetic constructor.

The smallest plausible target is a commutator or curvature comparing the
archimedean annihilator with diagonal integer formation before summation.
Its output must be label-sensitive, must vanish or become exact for the
standard Gaussian source, and must fail for the degree-12 hostile before
zero locations are inspected.

## Falsifier

Any proposed new constructor is nonexplanatory if it:

- acts only on the archimedean profile;
- acts only on the finite Euler packet;
- is natural for every Fourier-self-dual Schwartz profile;
- or reduces after Mellin transport to a scalar recurrence multiplied by the
  unchanged zeta readout.
