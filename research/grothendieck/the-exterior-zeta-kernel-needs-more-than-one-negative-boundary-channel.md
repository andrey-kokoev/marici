# The exterior zeta kernel needs more than one negative boundary channel

## Exact three-probe witness

Continue the polarized kernel

\[
K(s,w)=\zeta(s+\bar w)
\]

to the exterior real probes

\[
s_0=0,
\qquad
s_1=-1,
\qquad
s_2=-2.
\]

Using

\[
\zeta(0)=-\frac12,
\quad
\zeta(-1)=-\frac1{12},
\quad
\zeta(-2)=\zeta(-4)=0,
\quad
\zeta(-3)=\frac1{120},
\]

the continued Gram matrix is

\[
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
-\frac12&-\frac1{12}&0\\
-\frac1{12}&0&\frac1{120}\\
0&\frac1{120}&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its leading principal minors are

\[
\Delta_1=-\frac12,
\qquad
\Delta_2=-\frac1{144},
\qquad
\Delta_3=\frac1{28800}.
\]

All are nonzero, so the signs of the exact LDL pivots are

\[
\operatorname{sgn}
\left(
\Delta_1,
\frac{\Delta_2}{\Delta_1},
\frac{\Delta_3}{\Delta_2}
\right)
=(-,+,-).
\]

Therefore \(M\) has exactly two negative directions and one positive
direction.

## Consequence

The loss of positivity at the heat boundary is not accounted for by one
negative scalar coordinate. Already three canonical exterior evaluations
require a negative boundary sector of dimension at least two.

This corrects the simplest five-wall picture. The constant–delta incidence
may be one generator, but the completed exterior observer is not a
one-dimensional correction to the interior Hilbert space. Its translated or
jet descendants carry independent signed directions.

Categorically, a pullback through a single scalar boundary object cannot
recover the continued kernel. The boundary-bearing target must retain a
larger module, or a pro-object generated by the observer under source
transport.

## Scope

This is an exact finite obstruction, not a proof that the full exterior
kernel has infinite negative index. It proves the minimum rank-two claim and
supplies a falsifier for any proposed scalar-wall realization.

The completed theta construction may still pair these directions with
reciprocal channels to obtain a positive total Green energy. If so, that
pairing must occur before scalar finite part and must act on the whole
boundary module.

## Result

The continued polarized zeta kernel on probes \(0,-1,-2\) has inertia
\((1,2,0)\): one positive, two negative, and no null directions. Hence the
exterior heat wall is already at least rank two. The next construction must
transport the full boundary module, not attach one compensating scalar.
