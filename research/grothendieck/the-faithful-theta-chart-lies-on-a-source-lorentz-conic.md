# The faithful theta chart lies on a source Lorentz conic

## Radial and rapidity coordinates

For the reciprocal half-Mellin amplitudes, choose locally

\[
R(z)^2=I_+(z)I_-(z),
\qquad
\chi(z)=\frac12\log\frac{I_+(z)}{I_-(z)}.
\]

Then

\[
I_+=Re^\chi,
\qquad
I_-=Re^{-\chi},
\]

and the symmetric and antisymmetric moments are

\[
M=R\cosh\chi,
\qquad
N=R\sinh\chi.
\]

The sign choices for (R) and the logarithm do not affect the quadratic
identities below.

## The exact conic equation

The faithful completed coordinates satisfy

\[
S-\frac12=2PM,
\qquad
A=2PN,
\qquad
P(z)=\frac{z^2-1/4}{2}.
\]

Consequently,

\[
\left(S-\frac12\right)^2-A^2
=4P^2\left(M^2-N^2\right)
=4P^2I_+I_-.
\]

This is the exact geometric relation missing from the earlier scalar chart.
The carrier-centered readout (S-1/2) and the residual (A) are not two
independent observables. They are the two components of one Lorentz vector,
whose source-derived squared radius is (4P^2I_+I_-).

The decomposition also identifies two kinds of information that scalar
aggregation had merged:

- (R) is reciprocal-invariant radial information;
- \(\chi\) is reciprocal-odd relational or rapidity information.

The scalar section sees only (R\cosh\chi). The faithful residual retains
(R\sinh\chi).

## The seam circle

On the critical seam (z=i\tau), one has

\[
I_-(i\tau)=\overline{I_+(i\tau)},
\qquad
R^2=|I_+|^2,
\]

while (S-1/2) is real and (A) is purely imaginary. Writing

\[
Y=-iA,
\]

the Lorentz conic Wick-rotates into

\[
\left(S-\frac12\right)^2+Y^2
=4P(i\tau)^2|I_+(i\tau)|^2.
\]

This answers the operator's circle question exactly. The circle is the
Euclidean norm circle of the completed scalar displacement and the surviving
antisymmetric quadrature. It is not a circle traced by the Riemann zeros, and
its radius varies with spectral height through (P) and the half-Mellin
amplitude.

At a scalar zero, (S=0), so the conic gives

\[
\frac14-A^2=4P^2I_+I_-.
\]

On the seam this becomes

\[
\frac14+Y^2=4P(i\tau)^2|I_+(i\tau)|^2.
\]

Thus a zero is an intersection of the source conic with the vertical affine
slice (S=0). The zero does not annihilate the Lorentz vector; it fixes its
carrier-centered component at (-1/2) and leaves the orthogonal quadrature.

## The remaining dynamical question

The conic identity is kinematic and universal for reciprocal pairs. It does
not confine intersections to the seam. An off-seam zero would be a point of
the complexified Lorentz conic whose scalar component reaches (-1/2) while
the rapidity has nonzero real part.

The RH-bearing theorem must therefore constrain the joint radial and
rapidity evolution of the minimal theta source. In its sharpest present
form, it must exclude

\[
2P(z)R(z)\cosh\chi(z)=-\frac12
\]

when \(\Re z\neq0\). A theorem controlling only (R), only \(\chi\), or only
the quadratic invariant cannot suffice: destructive interference is a joint
phase-and-magnitude condition in their product.

The natural hostile test is to preserve reciprocal symmetry and the conic
identity while independently perturbing radial evolution or rapidity
evolution. Whichever perturbation first creates an off-seam intersection
identifies the theta-specific conservation law still missing.
