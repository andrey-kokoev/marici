# Reciprocal sewing makes the Gaussian seam port an observable, not a barrier

## Bounded question

Does reciprocal-sheet sewing turn the single Gaussian derivative-comb seam
value into a transverse constraint that excludes a scalar zero off the seam?

## Two half-line routes

Let

\[
H_+(z)=\int_0^\infty A(u)e^{izu}\,du,
\qquad
H_-(z)=\int_0^\infty A(u)e^{-izu}\,du.
\]

Their symmetric and antisymmetric combinations are

\[
X(z)=H_+(z)+H_-(z),
\qquad
Y(z)=H_+(z)-H_-(z).
\]

The scalar completed readout is the symmetric channel (X). The
antisymmetric channel (Y) retains which half-line route contributed.

For the even number current from the Gaussian jet adapter, the two half-line
transforms are

\[
N_+(z)
=
\left(\frac12+iz\right)H_+(z)+A(0),
\]

and

\[
N_-(z)
=
\left(\frac12-iz\right)H_-(z)+A(0).
\]

## Exact sewing

Adding and subtracting gives

\[
N_+(z)+N_-(z)
=
\frac12X(z)+izY(z)+2A(0),
\]

and

\[
N_+(z)-N_-(z)
=
\frac12Y(z)+izX(z).
\]

Thus the seam value doubles in the symmetric number channel and cancels in the
antisymmetric number channel. No untyped residual remains.

## Behavior at a scalar zero

If

\[
X(z)=0,
\]

then

\[
H_-(z)=-H_+(z),
\qquad
Y(z)=2H_+(z).
\]

The number channels become

\[
N_++N_-=izY+2A(0),
\qquad
N_+-N_-=\frac12Y.
\]

They remain well-defined and need not vanish. A zero of the distinguished
symmetric readout therefore does not annihilate the derivative-comb state or
its seam output. It merely transfers the surviving information into the
antisymmetric route channel and the symmetric seam offset.

## Result

The reciprocal seam calculation closes the finite oscillator-ladder route:

1. full-source Fourier covariance is exact;
2. value compression loses the derivative port on ambient Schwartz space;
3. the Gaussian shift module has a canonical jet adapter;
4. its odd current cancels;
5. its even bulk factors through the original half-line amplitudes;
6. reciprocal sewing leaves a doubled seam value but no vanishing condition;
7. a scalar zero remains compatible with nonzero transverse route data.

Hence (A(0)) is a faithful additional observable, not a barrier separating
the admitted orbit from the scalar kernel.

## Explanatory consequence

This realizes the operator's earlier intuition precisely: at a zero, the full
relationship need not disappear. Meaning moves from the symmetric scalar
projection into an antisymmetric route component. But that fact explains what
a zero forgets; it does not explain why forgetting can occur only on the
critical seam.

Any surviving RH law must constrain the relative phase of (X) and (Y), or
provide an independently conserved current that a scalar zero forces to
vanish. The oscillator ladder supplies neither. The next candidate must be
multiplicative arithmetic incidence, not another additive Gaussian jet.
