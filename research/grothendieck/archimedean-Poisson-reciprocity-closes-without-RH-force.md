## Why this does not prove RH

This coherence is universal for the Tate--Poisson construction. It says the
two presentations are related correctly; it does not prevent their invariant
sum from vanishing.

In particular, pairwise swapping preserves the scalar cancellation locus. It
does not supply:

- a positive energy attached to a zero-state;
- an acute comparison cone;
- transversality of the four-channel incidence;
- equality between completed zero-state energy and the oriented valuation
  diagnostic.

Thus no RH-bearing residual is hiding in reciprocity itself.
