# Square, Clark, and archimedean audit for the mixed residual

## Decision

The proposed coefficient cancellation cannot currently be formed as a typed
sum. The available square, Clark, and archimedean results inhabit different
objects and none supplies a current derivative in the centered `(m,d,H)`
carrier matching either monomial of

`R_mix=-2 sqrt(2) delta Re(H conjugate(m))`
`      +2 sqrt(2) Re(i tau H conjugate(d))`.

## Channel inventory

### Prime-square channel

The canonical square countercurrent renormalizes the scalar relative overlap
between additive and multiplicative vacua. It is line-valued in the relative
determinant correspondence; it does not repair or converge as a Hilbert state
vector. Consequently it has no declared action on `m`, `d`, or `H`, and cannot
be differentiated as a Green current on this carrier.

### Clark shear

The two Clark-sheet bulks satisfy the exact parallelogram identity

`|X+iY|^2+|X-iY|^2=2|G+f|^2+2a^2|partial_z G|^2`.

This proves equality, spin-two cancellation, and faithfulness of the positive
Gram bulk. It explicitly leaves all primitive, square, archimedean, and mixed
reflection-coboundary defects outside the theorem. It supplies no boundary
current derivative with coefficients opposite to `R_mix`.

### Archimedean channel

A fixed gamma/archimedean current cannot absorb even the primitive finite-cutoff
port: its latter odd readout has an oscillatory envelope of order
`sqrt(X)/log(X)`. Any valid operation must instead be a joint cutoff-covariant
primitive--archimedean constructor or an inseparable global Poisson pairing.
No such completed constructor is supplied, and hence there is no archimedean
row in the centered mixed-residual frame.

## Consequence

No legal coefficient table exists to compare with `R_mix`. Assigning formal
coefficients to these three channel names would conflate a determinant-line
renormalization, a positive state Gram form, and an endpoint current. The first
missing incidence is a joint source-derived map into one complete Green
current carrier, including cutoff transitions and regularity grades.

The Clark result secures positive bulk but does not help flux closure. The
square result secures a nonzero relative determinant overlap but not a response
vector. The fixed archimedean route is explicitly divergent. Therefore the
mixed residual remains uncancelled and the complete-current conservation route
is not constructed.
