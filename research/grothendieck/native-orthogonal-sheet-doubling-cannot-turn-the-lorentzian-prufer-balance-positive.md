# Native Orthogonal Sheet Doubling Cannot Turn the Lorentzian Prüfer Balance Positive

## Proposed repair under audit

The Lorentzian identity suggested pairing the two reciprocal theta sheets so
that radial actions cancel while angular actions add. Native reflection does
not have that effect.

Let a constant orthogonal sheet map \(S\in O(2)\) act on the Clifford
relationship vector:

\[
V_-=SV_+.
\]

For the radial and angular rates,

\[
\kappa(V)=\frac{V\mathbin{\cdot}V_x}{|V|^2},
\qquad
\omega(V)=\frac{V\mathbin{\wedge}V_x}{|V|^2},
\]

orthogonality gives

\[
\kappa(V_-)=\kappa(V_+),
\qquad
\omega(V_-)=\det(S)\omega(V_+).
\]

Reflection reverses angular orientation, while preserving radial compression.
After squaring,

\[
\kappa_-^2=\kappa_+^2,
\qquad
\omega_-^2=\omega_+^2.
\]

## Linear-combination no-go

Each native sheet carries the same Lorentzian density

\[
Q_\pm=2(\omega^2-\kappa^2).
\]

For any coefficients \(a,b\),

\[
aQ_++bQ_-
=2(a+b)(\omega^2-\kappa^2).
\]

Choosing \(a+b=0\) cancels radial action, but it cancels angular action as
well. Choosing \(a+b\neq0\) retains the original split signature.

Therefore no constant orthogonal doubling—reflection, conjugation, rotation,
or sheet swap—can convert the balance into a positive angular square.

## Meaning for reciprocal theta sectors

The native even reflection acts by

\[
(H,H_x)\mapsto(H,-H_x).
\]

It is precisely an orthogonal reflection. It explains collision pairing and
quartet completion, but supplies no positive completion of the radial--angular
energy.

Thus the two reflected half-cells solve the integrality and support-orbit
problem, not the collision-exclusion problem.

## What kind of extra structure could work

A surviving repair must be nonorthogonal or higher-dimensional. Possibilities
are:

1. the source-derived Clark shear, whose parameter dependence introduces
   cross terms rather than merely reversing orientation;
2. an independent amplitude/control channel giving a second quadratic law;
3. a symplectic mate that pairs radial motion from one sheet with angular
   motion from the other;
4. a higher jet carrier such as \((H,H_x,H_{xx})\) with a source-fixed positive
   form.

Every option has a hostile gate. The added map must be derived before testing
positivity, remain regular at the collision locus, and preserve the complete
infinity germ. A fitted nonorthogonal change of frame can always manufacture a
positive form and carries no explanatory force.

## Multi-tower correction

Two copies of the same relationship tower do not create a new comparison
capability merely because one is reflected. Their squared invariants occupy
the same fiber.

The missing channel must be genuinely independent—another constructor, not
another presentation of \(V\). This is Aspect's selection rule in quadratic
form: a reflected carrier descends to the same squared quotient and cannot
select a positive lift.

## Scope

The no-go theorem covers constant orthogonal sheet actions. It does not rule
out source-dependent shears, enlarged jet modules, or nonlocal modular mates.
