# Source audit of the relative determinant identification

## Exact determinant bridges that exist

### Source-bordered endpoint family

The split completed Mellin source gives an exact `3 x 3` analytic family

`M_s=[[s,0,1],[0,s-1,1],[1,-1,H(s)]]`.

Its Schur complement is the completed Mellin section and

`det M_s=2 xi(s)`.

The symmetric outgoing endpoint line, antisymmetric return line, and unit
normalization are source-derived; reciprocal covariance is a left--right
comparison, and the remaining port rescaling is a presentation gauge. This is
a genuine noncircular zero-to-kernel bridge and a canonical bordered analytic
determinant.

### Universal rank-one Fredholm encoding

A rank-one family can also be arranged with determinant `2X(z)`. It is exact
but universal: every holomorphic scalar target, including hostile quartet
modifications, admits the same construction. Its kernel equation merely
rewrites the scalar value-one equation and has no independent Green or
transport law.

## What is not constructed

The bordered matrix is parameter-dependent, non-self-adjoint, and not the
relative determinant of a fixed logarithmic Dirac extension selected by an
adelic Lagrangian boundary relation. No source metric makes its open-half-plane
invertibility a consequence of definiteness or conservation.

Moreover, determinant nullity does not imply work orthogonality. The latter is
an adjoint range condition requiring a typed forcing vector, identification of
the adjoint nullspace, and a range theorem. An exact diagonal countermodel
shows the same determinant zero supports either zero or nonzero forcing
obstruction.

Finite bordered presentations do have canonical divisors under independently
authorized invertible coordinate changes and contractible stabilization, and
normalized locally uniform unit convergence would preserve that divisor at
completion. But the full finite prime--archimedean operator packet, stable
comparison units, reduced-minimum-modulus bound, and completed relative
operator determinant are not supplied.

## Final determination

The determinant-identification gate is partially passed only in the category
of source-bordered analytic matrix families. It is not passed for the
RH-bearing category of closed maximal self-adjoint boundary operators.

Accordingly, no approved operator pair has both:

1. a source-derived relative determinant equal to `xi` up to a nowhere-zero
   unit; and
2. an independent positive Green, self-adjointness, or range law excluding
   off-seam kernel.

This closes the audit: scalar-null confinement is not derived. The strongest
authorized theorem remains conditional on construction of the missing
boundary operator/current interface.
