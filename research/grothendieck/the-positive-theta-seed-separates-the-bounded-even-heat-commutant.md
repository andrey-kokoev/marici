# The Positive Theta Seed Separates the Bounded Even Heat Commutant

## Spectral typing of the heat generator

The completed theta source is even. Under the cosine transform, its heat
generator is multiplication by \(u^2\) on the half-line source space. This
spectral representation has multiplicity one.

Consequently the bounded commutant of the even heat generator is the maximal
abelian multiplication algebra:

\[
T\longleftrightarrow M_m
\]

for an essentially bounded spectral multiplier \(m(u)\).

The earlier differential witnesses \(I+c\partial_z^{2N}\) correspond to the
unbounded or polynomial multipliers \(1+c(-1)^Nu^{2N}\). They change the
initial spectral vector.

## The seed is separating

Let \(\Phi(u)\) be the completed theta seed on the positive source half-line.
It is strictly positive, hence nonzero almost everywhere. If a bounded
heat-commuting operator fixes the seed, then

\[
m(u)\Phi(u)=\Phi(u)
\]

almost everywhere. Therefore

\[
m(u)=1
\]

almost everywhere, and \(T=I\).

Thus the pair

```text
even heat dynamics + exact positive theta seed
```

has no nontrivial bounded spectral endomorphism fixing the source object.

## Polynomial version

If \(T=P(\partial_z)\) is a constant-coefficient differential operator and it
fixes the theta transform, Fourier injectivity gives

\[
(P(iu)-1)\Phi(u)=0.
\]

Because \(\Phi\) is nonzero on an interval, the polynomial identity theorem
forces \(P=1\). This rejects the explicit hostile operators from the preceding
packet once the initial seed is frozen.

## Canonical source object

The correct rigid object is not the heat equation alone and not one scalar
section alone. It is the pointed dynamical object

\[
(L,\Phi),
\]

where \(L\) is the even heat generator and \(\Phi\) is the labelled positive
initial vector. An admissible presentation automorphism must preserve both.

This supplies the Deutschian missing ingredient: laws determine possible
motion, while the content-addressed initial substrate selects the actual
orbit.

## Scope

The theorem covers bounded operators in the even spectral commutant and
constant-coefficient polynomial operators on their natural core. General
unbounded relations require domain and closability control.

This is source-provenance rigidity, not RH. It proves that hostile divisors
cannot be inserted without changing the pointed theta source. It does not
prove that the actual orbit avoids the Newman collision locus.
