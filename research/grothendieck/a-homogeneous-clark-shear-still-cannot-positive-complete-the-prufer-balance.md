# A Homogeneous Clark Shear Still Cannot Positive-Complete the Prüfer Balance

## Question

Orthogonal reciprocal doubling cannot turn the Lorentzian Prüfer density into
a positive energy. The smallest genuinely different candidate is a pair of
opposite shears

\[
S_\pm=\begin{pmatrix}1&\pm a\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Does positive aggregation of the two sheared sheets repair the signature?

## Exact hostile fiber

It is enough to inspect the source-independent fiber

\[
V=(u,0),\qquad V_x=(p,q),\qquad u\ne0.
\]

After the two shears,

\[
\kappa_\pm=\frac{p\pm aq}{u},
\qquad
\omega_\pm=\frac{q}{u}.
\]

Thus their Lorentzian densities are

\[
Q_\pm
=
\frac{2}{u^2}\left(q^2-(p\pm aq)^2\right).
\]

For nonnegative sheet weights \(c_+,c_-\), the coefficient of \(p^2\) in
\(c_+Q_++c_-Q_-\) is

\[
-\frac{2(c_++c_-)}{u^2}.
\]

It is strictly negative whenever either sheet is retained with positive
weight. Therefore no positive aggregation of the homogeneous opposite Clark
shears is positive semidefinite on the complete value--tangent fiber.

For equal weights the formula becomes

\[
Q_++Q_-
=
\frac{4}{u^2}\left((1-a^2)q^2-p^2\right).
\]

The original radial negative direction survives for every \(a\).

## What the obstruction selects

The Clark mechanism can still work, but not as a homogeneous change of frame.
Its essential new information must be the affine source forcing, the endpoint
current, or an enlarged jet component. Removing those data and retaining only
the shear collapses the proposed repair back to an indefinite two-dimensional
quadratic form.

This separates two meanings of independence:

1. a nonorthogonal presentation is algebraically different;
2. a new source channel contributes an additional observable or boundary
   current.

Only the second can repair the missing radial direction without assigning a
negative weight to one of the physical sheets.

## Next gate

Retain the affine Clark equation before homogenization and derive its completed
quadratic form on \((H,H_x,f)\), or on the next source jet. The required test is
whether the forcing component supplies a positive square whose restriction to
the hostile fiber controls \(p\). A shear-only derivation is now closed.
