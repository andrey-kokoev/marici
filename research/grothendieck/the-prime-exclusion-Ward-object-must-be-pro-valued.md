# The prime-exclusion Ward object must be pro-valued

## Why one scalar energy is the wrong next rung

For a labelled coefficient packet (c), the source-specific curvature ports
are

\[
P_p^{\mathrm{excl}}c.
\]

A tempting Ward energy sums their squared norms with weights (p^{-k/2}).
The values (k=1) and (k=2) are precisely the primitive and prime-square
grades.

## Exact divergence theorem

Let (c) be a nonzero finite-support integer-label packet. Every sufficiently
large prime divides none of its supported labels. Hence its exclusion
projection fixes (c) for every sufficiently large prime. The weighted energy
is therefore bounded below by

\[
\lVert c\rVert^2\sum_{p>P}p^{-k/2}.
\]

For (k=2), the prime harmonic series diverges. For (k=1), divergence
follows because (p^{-1/2}\ge p^{-1}). Thus the primitive and square
exclusion energies are infinite for every nonzero finite packet.

For (k\ge3), the scalar majorant converges, but that connected tail omits
exactly the primitive and square boundary currents that completion must retain.

## The next coherence rung

The all-prime curvature cannot be compressed into one ordinary positive norm.
It must remain the compatible finite-cutoff family

\[
\mathcal W_X(c)
=
\bigl(P_p^{\mathrm{excl}}c\bigr)_{p\le X}.
\]

For (X\subset Y), restriction of (mathcal W_Y) to ports indexed by (X)
must equal (mathcal W_X). The completed Ward object is therefore a
pro-object or product-valued boundary register, not a Hilbert direct sum with
the primitive and square weights.

The RH-bearing implication must be formulated cutoffwise: every completed
zero-state must have (mathcal W_X(c_z)=0) for every finite prime cutoff,
compatibly under inclusion. Joint faithfulness then forces (c_z=0) without
ever summing the divergent curvature energy.

## Why this is a higher coherence level

No single finite port is faithful, and no natural scalar sum is finite.
Faithfulness belongs to the coherent family of all restrictions. The relevant
statement is therefore not objectwise positivity but naturality of the Ward
cells across the cutoff category.

The tower now contains local curvature objects, finite-product Ward cells,
restriction 2-cells between cutoffs, and an inverse-limit compatibility
theorem carrying the zero-state condition.

## Falsifiers

The pro-Ward route fails if a finite cutoff leaves a nonzero typed residual,
restriction changes an earlier port, zero-state conditions hold only after
summing ports, coefficient packets vary incompatibly with cutoff, or
completion deletes primitive or square coordinates.

## Result

The next rung is a pro-coherence theorem. Scalarizing the all-prime curvature
before completion either diverges or discards the load-bearing boundary
grades.
