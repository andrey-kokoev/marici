# Oriented two-front pushforward has positive Gaussian density

## The two limiting front profiles

The comoving inner and outer charts of one adjacent valuation cell carry

\[
I(u)=H(u)-1
\]

and

\[
O(u)=-H(u).
\]

They admit two canonical combinations, fixed before any prime-scale
integration.

## Symmetric overlap channel

Their sum is

\[
I(u)+O(u)=-1.
\]

This is exactly the constant fifth-wall overlap. It contains no bulk
variation and Fourier-transforms to the negative delta boundary.

## Oriented relative channel

The boundary orientation of an interval takes outer face minus inner face:

\[
C(u)=O(u)-I(u)=1-2H(u).
\]

For the Gaussian tail,

\[
C(u)=\operatorname{erf}(\sqrt\pi u)
\]

and

\[
C'(u)=2e^{-\pi u^2}>0.
\]

Thus the two complementary localizations generate a strictly monotone
relative current whose density is the positive source Gaussian. Positivity
is not imposed on either chart; it appears only after taking their oriented
incidence.

## Geometric-algebra reading

The chart pair decomposes into two orthogonal representation types:

- a symmetric scalar overlap, carried by the constant–delta plane;
- an antisymmetric oriented comparison, whose derivative is positive bulk.

The raw traveling window mixed these channels. The two-chart blowup followed
by oriented incidence diagonalizes them. This is the same structural move as
separating scalar and bivector grades before projecting a comparison.

The relative current runs from

\[
C(-\infty)=-1
\]

to

\[
C(+\infty)=1.
\]

Its total positive flux is

\[
\int_{\mathbb R}C'(u)\,du=2.
\]

The value two is the incidence number of the two oriented faces.

## Consequence for prime-scale pushforward

Pairing the front charts before integrating over prime scale separates the
problem into:

1. a constant overlap coefficient, where primitive and square finite-part
   divergences live;
2. a positive Gaussian density, multiplied by the arithmetic scale measure.

This is much sharper than summing traveling windows in \(q\). The geometric
part of the relative pushforward is now universally positive and independent
of \(p\) and valuation depth. All remaining sign and convergence questions
belong to the one-dimensional scale coefficient.

## Relation to the desired Green identity

The sought global conservation law had the form of a positive bulk plus
typed boundary currents. The two-front calculation supplies exactly that
local architecture:

\[
\text{oriented chart incidence}
\longrightarrow
2\phi(u)\,du
\quad+quad
\text{constant overlap boundary}.
\]

It does not yet prove that the arithmetic coefficient of the positive density
has the required sign. It proves that no further archimedean oscillatory
orientation is missing at this stage.

## Remaining gate

Apply the primitive-projection-plus-valuation-degree identity to the scale
coefficient before integration. The decisive question is whether it turns
the primitive coefficient of \(C'(u)\) into the nonnegative square-and-higher
coefficient while routing the universal subtraction entirely into the
constant overlap channel.

The smallest falsifier is a negative coefficient multiplying \(2\phi(u)\) at
one prime and valuation depth after this incidence is compiled.

## Result

The relative pushforward of the two comoving front charts splits exactly into
the fifth-wall constant and an oriented current with strictly positive
Gaussian density. The archimedean geometry is now solved locally; the next
gate is the arithmetic sign and completion of its scale coefficient.
