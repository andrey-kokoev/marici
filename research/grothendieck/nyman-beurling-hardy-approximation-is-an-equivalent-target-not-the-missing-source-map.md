# Nyman–Beurling Hardy approximation is an equivalent target, not the missing source map

## Question

Can the Nyman–Beurling or Baez–Duarte Hilbert-space constructions supply the missing arithmetic map into Hardy space?

## External source check

The operator-authorized arXiv search inspected these abstracts:

- Jean-François Burnol, “A note on Nyman's equivalent formulation of the Riemann Hypothesis,” arXiv `math/9910055`;
- Jean-François Burnol, “On an analytic estimate in the theory of the Riemann Zeta function and a Theorem of Baez-Duarte,” arXiv `math/0202166`;
- Jean-François Burnol, “A lower bound in an approximation problem involving the zeros of the Riemann zeta function,” arXiv `math/0103058`.

The first states that membership of the constant function in the Nyman subspace is equivalent to RH and identifies its projection norm with a product over zeros having real part greater than `1/2`. The second gives an RH-conditional critical-line estimate and a variant proof of a strengthened Nyman–Beurling criterion. The third supplies lower bounds and Hilbert-space vectors for that approximation problem.

## Comparison with the current gate

These constructions provide arithmetic Hilbert subspaces, but the decisive density or approximation statement is already equivalent to RH. Burnol's projection formula makes the obstruction explicit: the norm defect records off-critical zeros. Therefore importing the Nyman closure statement as continuity or cyclicity of a prime–theta map would move the unresolved zero information into the range-density hypothesis.

A usable map for the present programme must have continuity and intertwining proved unconditionally, before any dense-range, cyclicity, or projection-zero assertion. None of the inspected abstracts claims such a map from the leading Bernstein moment GNS space to the zero-side Hardy form with the required lower bound.

## Disposition

Do not use Nyman–Beurling density as the missing source arrow. It is a comparison target and possible diagnostic for the defect norm. Reopen only if a theorem supplies an unconditional bounded or closable intertwiner whose lower-bound implication is weaker than the Nyman density statement.
