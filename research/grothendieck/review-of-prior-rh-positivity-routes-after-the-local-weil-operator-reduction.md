# Review of prior RH positivity routes after the local Weil-operator reduction

## Question

Which prior Marici constructions still offer a noncircular route beyond fixed-support semiboundedness?

## 1. Strict-peak interpolation is the sharpest logical reduction

Nima's reciprocal-pair audit identifies the exact hostile block for a hypothetical off-seam zero pair:

`[[0,1],[1,0]]`,

with negative disagreement vector `(1,-1)`. Compactly supported smooth Mellin packets exactly realize this vector on every finite divisor packet. Convolution powers then preserve the target value `-2` while suppressing every other divisor contribution, provided one source transform satisfies the global strict-peak condition

`Phi(rho)=1`, `Phi(rho^vee)=-1`, and `|Phi(z)|<1` at every other zero.

This route does not require uniform positivity constants in `L`. It reduces RH to one completion-stable interpolation theorem for each hypothetical off-seam pair. Its defect is precise: finite interpolation plus rapid vertical decay does not automatically close the moving cutoff loop.

Priority test: prove or falsify strict-peak interpolation in the compactly supported Mellin transform class. A reproducing-kernel or Beurling-density obstruction would be as decisive as a construction.

## 2. The weighted compact Weil operator is the strongest computational route

The local operator reduction and the unshifted factorization

`W_L=A^(1/2)(I+K_L)A^(1/2)`

turn fixed-support positivity into the spectrum of a compact self-adjoint `K_L`. This retains signed cancellation and avoids an artificial exponential starting mode.

The route is executable only after two missing objects are supplied:

1. one source-normalized explicit-formula identity fixing every sign and prefactor;
2. componentwise weighted tail bounds for prime, endpoint, Carleman, archimedean, and localization cells.

The repaired preconditioned-spline checker supplies a concrete candidate convention tuple:

`(log p^m, log(p)/sqrt(p^m), -2, kernel shift 1/4, -(gamma+log pi)f(0))`.

It is not source-authorized, but it is the best existing fixture for the required Gaussian normalization test. Its deliberate prime-sign failure infrastructure should be reused.

## 3. Canonical-system and scattering routes remain constructor-blocked

A positive source-derived canonical system would imply the required Wronskian sign through a Lagrange energy identity. Prior audits also prove a no-go theorem: the uncoupled reciprocal spectral pair `{-1,+1}` cannot remain a nonradical reducing block of a positive canonical generator. Any successful lift must either couple that pair to auxiliary source channels or remove it by an authorized constraint.

No such source intertwiner or positive Hamiltonian has been materialized. Building an inner function directly from `xi` is circular because upper-half-plane contractivity already encodes zero location. This route should remain frozen until a finite source cell exhibits off-diagonal spectral coupling and a positive Green identity independently of `xi`.

## 4. Li conditional negative definiteness is a useful acceptance test, not yet a constructor

RH is equivalent to conditional negative definiteness of the even Li sequence, or positivity of its anchored Gram kernels. This packages all Li inequalities coherently and supplies finite matrix falsifiers.

The positive cocycle representation currently comes from the zero-side unit-circle formula and therefore cannot be used upstream of RH. The missing theorem is an arithmetic construction of the anchored kernel directly from primes and completion data. Until then this is a diagnostic projection of the same positivity problem.

## 5. Pairwise logarithmic-form bounds and scalar resonance are exhausted

Unrestricted scalar resonance has its exact maximum at zero frequency. Tail-restricted scalar symbols do not control the finite interval because translations mix Dirichlet modes. Independent pairwise Baker bounds yield only logarithmic frequency cost in phase accuracy, far below the exponential prime-mass scale. These branches should not be reopened without a genuinely simultaneous theorem or an operator-valued interval estimate.

## Ranked executable frontier

1. Source the standard explicit formula matching the existing spline convention tuple and build the independent Gaussian equality/sign-failure fixture.
2. Audit strict-peak interpolation using Paley--Wiener/reproducing-kernel density criteria; this may bypass uniform-in-`L` operator estimates entirely.
3. Once normalization is fixed, assemble the full signed weighted matrix and derive componentwise tails.
4. Use Li Gram matrices only as cross-checks of any proposed arithmetic positive kernel.
5. Keep canonical-system work frozen until source spectral coupling removes the real reciprocal pair.

## Disposition

No prior route proves RH. Two routes remain live and nonredundant: strict-peak completion, which attacks the hypothetical off-seam negative vector directly, and the normalized compact weighted operator, which attacks positivity support-window by support-window. The first missing object for the computational route is source normalization; the first missing theorem for the conceptual route is global strict peaking in the authorized Mellin class.
