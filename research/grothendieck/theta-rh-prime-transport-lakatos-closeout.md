## 3. Disposition of the phase-space result

The adelic phase lattice

\[
 \Lambda_{\mathbb Q}
 =\mathbb Q^2\subset\mathbb A_{\mathbb Q}^2
\]

is exactly self-annihilating for the symplectic Heisenberg bicharacter. This
is a genuine new group-level theorem and a plausible source of integral
boundary maximality.

It does **not** presently reopen the RH block. Missing are:

1. a boundary trace from the maximal reciprocal Dirac domain;
2. a proof that group-level self-annihilation linearizes to a closed
   self-adjoint relation;
3. a determinant comparison with \(X\); and
4. an independent inequality or monotone form law for \(C_Y\).

Until one of these produces orientation before scalar projection, the
phase-lattice formulation remains a separate conjectural bridge rather than
an RH advance.
