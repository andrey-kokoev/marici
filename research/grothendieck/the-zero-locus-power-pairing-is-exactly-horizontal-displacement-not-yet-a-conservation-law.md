# The zero-locus power pairing is exactly horizontal displacement, not yet a conservation law

## Exact power identity

The value-current Ward cell is

\[
C(z)+k(0)=-\frac12F(z)-zG(z).
\]

On the scalar-null locus \(F(z)=0\), this reduces to

\[
C(z)+k(0)=-zG(z).
\]

Pairing the current with the surviving antisymmetric amplitude gives

\[
\bigl(C(z)+k(0)\bigr)\overline{G(z)}
=-z|G(z)|^2.
\]

Hence

\[
\operatorname{Re}
\left(
\bigl(C(z)+k(0)\bigr)\overline{G(z)}
\right)
=-\operatorname{Re}(z)|G(z)|^2,
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{Im}
\left(
\bigl(C(z)+k(0)\bigr)\overline{G(z)}
\right)
=-\operatorname{Im}(z)|G(z)|^2.
\]

Thus the current-amplitude power records the spectral displacement itself.

## Sharp logical boundary

If the half-line pair is nonnull at a completed zero, then \(G(z)\ne0\).
Under that already established theta condition,

\[
\operatorname{Re}
\left(
\bigl(C+k(0)\bigr)\overline G
\right)=0
\]

on every scalar zero is equivalent to

\[
\operatorname{Re}z=0.
\]

Therefore declaring the real power to vanish is not yet an explanation or a
derived conservation theorem. It is RH written in the value-current frame.

The Ward identity alone supplies no independent reason for this pairing to be
purely imaginary. It transports information between grades but does not
orient the transport by sector.

## Forced three-tower architecture

This separates three roles that cannot be collapsed:

1. The value tower contains symmetric and antisymmetric Mellin amplitudes.
2. The current tower contains the moving endpoint response.
3. The control tower must supply an independently derived adjointness,
   conservation, or flux law.

The Ward cell relates the first two towers. It cannot serve simultaneously as
the third tower because its power identity already contains the unconstrained
factor \(z\).

The missing theorem must therefore construct a control pairing before a zero
is assumed and prove that admissible zero-boundary states have vanishing real
flux. Fourier–Tate sewing is the natural candidate constructor, but its
unitarity on the seam cannot be extrapolated into the desired off-seam result
without a separate Green identity.

## Minimal acceptance gate

A valid sector-control construction must provide a form \(J\), derived before
scalar compression, such that:

- its Green defect is the current-amplitude power above;
- source boundary conditions make the total real flux vanish for a scalar-null
  state;
- the form retains the primitive, square, seam, and archimedean channels;
- the proof does not substitute \(F=0\) until after the control law is derived;
- a hostile multiplier fails to lift to the control object without reference
  to its zeros.

Without such an independent control tower, the higher-rank lift restores
meaning but supplies no zero confinement.
