# Reciprocal sewing is faithful on germs but does not confine zeros

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

## Analytic pro-boundary germ

Center the complete Mellin germ at a spectral point \(z_0\):

\[
F_{z_0}(w)=F(z_0+w)
=\sum_{k\ge0}\frac{m_k(z_0)}{k!}w^k.
\]

Use the usual Fréchet topology of uniform convergence on compact subdisks.
The reciprocal Real involution is

\[
(\mathcal CF)_{-\overline {z_0}}(w)
=\overline{F_{z_0}(-\overline w)}.
\]

On jets it acts diagonally:

\[
m_k(-\overline {z_0})
=(-1)^k\overline{m_k(z_0)}.
\]

It is anti-linear, continuous, involutive, and faithful on the complete germ
tower.

## Zero transport

A scalar zero is only the condition

\[
m_0(z_0)=0.
\]

Reciprocal sewing gives

\[
m_0(-\overline {z_0})=0
\]

and transports every higher derivative faithfully with alternating parity.
It imposes no further condition on \(m_1,m_2,\ldots\). Thus an off-seam
zero germ is carried to a second off-seam zero germ. On the fixed seam the
same relation becomes a Real parity structure, but the involution alone does
not force a germ to be fixed.

Completing the boundary tower repairs information loss, yet still produces
symmetry of the divisor rather than confinement of the divisor.

## Local Fitting-minor audit

Let \(M_K\) be the moment packet for retained grades \(K\), and add a
distinct grade \(\ell\). The nearest-neighbor sewing criterion is

\[
\operatorname{rank}[M_K\mid M_\ell]
=\operatorname{rank}M_K.
\]

Before saturation this equality is false. Every relevant augmented maximal
minor is a generalized Vandermonde minor on distinct positive scales and is
nonzero. Hence no source relation makes a missing Mellin grade locally
redundant. The magnetic Fitting-minor collapse depends on path-column
relations absent from the Mellin monomial carrier.

## Consequence

We now have both required facts:

1. finite grade packets are intrinsically blind;
2. the complete germ is faithful and reciprocal-covariant.

Neither fact supplies RH orientation. The missing operation must relate the
two reciprocal germs by more than the involution itself. It must be a
source-derived cross-germ conservation law or positive-real transfer law
whose boundary term uses the full analytic germ and whose equality case
forces the base point onto the fixed seam.

Any construction depending only on

\[
m_k\longmapsto(-1)^k\overline{m_k}
\]

will accept hostile symmetric off-seam zero pairs and is therefore
explanation-inert.
