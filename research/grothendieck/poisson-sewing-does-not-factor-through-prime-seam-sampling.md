## Nonfactorization theorem

Let (S) denote prime-power seam sampling and (P) the full source-level
Poisson correspondence or any continuous readout sensitive to unsampled seam
variation. Ledger 3066 supplies a nonzero smooth variation (b), supported
between (log2) and (log3), such that

\[
Sb=0,
\qquad
Pb\ne0.
\]

Therefore

\[
\ker S\not\subseteq\ker P.
\]

No operator (I) can satisfy

\[
P=I\circ S
\]

on a source class containing that variation. Poisson sewing does not factor
through the prime-seam projection.
