# Weakest joint source-compatible theorem boundary

## Unconditional analytic theorem

For the approved dominant theta kernel,

`D_theta(q,y)>32`

on `q in [3/10,1/2]`, `y in [1/1000,1/28]`. This is established by the exact
Chart-1 and Chart-2 derivative certificates and the exact lower-corner
certificate.

## Conditional confinement theorem

For a source-selected solution of the approved base flow, scalar-null
confinement follows if the source additionally supplies all of:

1. the full path constraint and compatibility prolongations, implying the
   required sheet relation along the path;
2. the source-derived Green identity on the same completed state;
3. vanishing endpoint flux and closure of the declared current cell;
4. nonzero positive sheet bulk; and
5. either exact residual cancellation or the coercive response-form bound
   `|R|<B`, uniformly through completion.

Under those hypotheses the closed Green identity reduces to a nonzero positive
bulk multiplied by the scalar parameter, forcing that parameter to vanish.

## Logical relation

The analytic theorem and conditional confinement theorem are presently
independent. No approved map identifies `D_theta` with `B`, `B-R`, the
response matrices `A_X,Q_X`, or the derivative of the closed current. Hence
`D_theta>32` discharges none of conditions 1--5 and does not strengthen the
conditional confinement implication.

Even a hypothetical coercivity bridge

`B-|R| >= alpha*D_theta`, `alpha>0`,

would discharge only condition 5. Endpoint closure, constraint promotion, and
the typed Green identity would remain necessary. This is sharp: the approved
extension gives explicit positive-energy off-null flows that fail endpoint or
constraint closure.

## First missing bridge object

The cheapest nonredundant new object is a source-authorized, completion-stable
typed comparison from the dominant theta response to the Green response forms,
for example a map proving a uniform inequality between `D_theta` and
`B-|R|`. It must specify the selected state, parameter map, grades, topology,
and cutoff-uniform bound. A numerical similarity or reuse of the symbol `D`
is insufficient.

Therefore scalar-null confinement is not derived by the current approved
source. The strongest joint result is the conjunction of an unconditional
analytic dominant-kernel theorem and the pre-existing conditional Green
confinement theorem.
