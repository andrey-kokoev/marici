# Antiunitary reciprocal sewing preserves the order current

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

## The sign question

Entry 4107 left one possible confinement mechanism: perhaps reciprocal
theta/Tate sewing identifies two universally upward order-current crossings
with opposite signs away from the critical seam.

The native Real reciprocal transformation does not do this.

## Operators

Let \(R\) reverse the ordered scale coordinate. Then

\[
R\Lambda R=-\Lambda,
\qquad
RSR=-S.
\]

Let \(K\) denote complex conjugation and define the antiunitary Real
reciprocal map

\[
C=RK.
\]

The real order current is

\[
J(v)=-i\langle v,Sv\rangle.
\]

## Current transformation

Because \(S\) is real and skew-adjoint,
\(\langle v,Sv\rangle\) is purely imaginary. Direct calculation gives

\[
\langle Cv,S(Cv)\rangle=\langle v,Sv\rangle.
\]

Equivalently, scale reversal contributes one minus sign through
\(RSR=-S\), while complex conjugation contributes the second minus sign by
reversing the imaginary scalar. Therefore

\[
J(Cv)=J(v).
\]

The physical antiunitary sewing preserves the order current. Using \(R\)
alone would reverse it, but that would discard the Real structure and use
the wrong typed comparison.

## Positive slope transformation

Let

\[
P=[\Lambda,S].
\]

The two operator reversals cancel:

\[
RPR=P.
\]

Since \(P\) is real,

\[
\langle Cv,P(Cv)\rangle=\langle v,Pv\rangle.
\]

Thus the strict upward slope at a scalar zero is also preserved under Real
reciprocal sewing.

## Consequence

The hoped-for bulk sign contradiction is absent. Reciprocal symmetry carries
an upward crossing to another upward crossing. This is exactly what a
symmetry of the zero divisor should do; it does not select the fixed seam.

Together with the earlier flat Fourier--dilation calculation, this confines
any possible RH force to the relative boundary:

- primitive-prime current;
- prime-square current;
- half-line Hardy incidence;
- archimedean continuation jets;
- restricted-product completion anomaly.

No interior combination of \(R\), \(K\), \(S\), and \(\Lambda\) can supply
the missing sign.

Write the completed sewing as

\[
C_{\mathrm{full}}=C_{\mathrm{bulk}}+B_{\mathrm{rel}},
\]

where the notation means a boundary-bearing correspondence rather than a
literal sum if their domains differ. Compute the anomaly

\[
\Delta J(v)=J(C_{\mathrm{full}}v)-J(v)
\]

at every finite Euler cutoff, typed by primitive, square, Hardy, and
archimedean channels.

The route remains live only if this boundary anomaly is source-specific,
survives completion, and vanishes exactly on the critical seam. If it
vanishes identically or is universal for hostile carriers, reciprocal
