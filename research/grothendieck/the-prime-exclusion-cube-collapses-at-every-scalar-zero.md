# The prime-exclusion cube collapses at every scalar zero

## Exact source port

Write the completed theta source as a sum over positive integer labels. For
each prime `p`, its label scaling gives

\[
\Phi(u)=\Phi_{p\nmid}(u)+p^{-1/2}\Phi(u+\log p).
\]

Hence the primitive-remainder port found by Aspect's port-creation gate is
canonical:

\[
\Phi_{p\nmid}
=
E_p\Phi,
\qquad
E_p=I-p^{-1/2}T_{\log p}.
\]

For distinct primes, the exclusion operators commute. For a finite prime set
`S`, inclusion-exclusion gives

\[
\Phi_{(S)}=
\prod_{p\in S}E_p\Phi.
\]

This is the complete Boolean cube of divisibility exclusions.

## Transform collapse

Let

\[
X(z)=\int_{\mathbb R}\Phi(u)e^{zu}\,du.
\]

Translation acts by

\[
T_{\log p}\longmapsto p^{-z}.
\]

Therefore every exclusion vertex has transform

\[
X_S(z)=
\prod_{p\in S}\left(1-p^{-1/2-z}\right)X(z).
\]

At every scalar zero `z_0` of `X`, all finite vertices vanish:

\[
X(z_0)=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
X_S(z_0)=0
\quad\text{for every finite }S.
\]

The mixed-prime square is exact,

\[
X_{\{p,q\}}
=X-p^{-1/2-z}X-q^{-1/2-z}X+(pq)^{-1/2-z}X,
\]

but its commuting coherence contains no transverse value at a scalar zero.

## Scope correction

The primitive-remainder port is genuinely source-derived and lossless as a
function-level decomposition. It exposes which source labels remain after an
exclusion. But its value at a fixed spectral point is not a faithful
zero-discriminator: every finite port shares the common scalar factor `X`.

Thus Aspect's admitted port creation is real, while the stronger phrase
“exposes the hostile before scalar cancellation” needs qualification. It does
so at source level, not in the value-only spectral fiber.

## Why the first jet does not finish the job

Writing

\[
m_S(z)=\prod_{p\in S}(1-p^{-1/2-z}),
\qquad X_S=m_SX,
\]

gives at a zero

\[
X_S'(z_0)=m_S(z_0)X'(z_0).
\]

The jet prevents total collapse at a simple zero, but after known multiplier
normalization it recovers only the common derivative `X'(z_0)`. It detects
multiplicity and transversality, not whether the zero is on the seam. A hostile
symmetric source can possess the same jet architecture.

## Structural verdict

All finite exclusion ports and all their commutative comparison cells form a
principal module generated by `X`. Adding more primes enlarges the Boolean
presentation but not its rank over the multiplier algebra.

Therefore the missing constructor cannot be another finite divisibility
exclusion, another commuting square, or another value-only mixed-prime
incidence. It must introduce information not divisible by `X` in the spectral
fiber—such as a source boundary current, a nontrivial extension class, or a
relative comparison whose value is not obtained by multiplying `X`.

This is the first exact falsifier for the newly admitted primitive-remainder
port as an RH-bearing observation channel.
