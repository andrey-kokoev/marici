# Reciprocal completion moves the problem from factor selection to quotient interval stability

## Why a phase port is not enough

For a degree-`n` packet, the complete reversal-odd phase port is

\[
D(w)=P(w)-w^nP(w^{-1}).
\]

It distinguishes a generic factor from its reciprocal reverse. But `D=0`
exactly when the packet is palindromic, and palindromicity does not confine
zeros to the unit circle.

The smallest existing hostile already proves this. Its cleared polynomial is

\[
P(w)=w^4+4w^3+4w+1.
\]

Its coefficient packet `(1,4,0,4,1)` is positive and palindromic, so every
reversal-odd phase statistic vanishes. Nevertheless, it has a reciprocal
off-unit zero pair.

Thus the phase-selection target in ledger 3615 applies before reciprocal
completion. Once the completed object is already self-reciprocal, RH asks a
different question.

## The reciprocal quotient

Put

\[
y=w+w^{-1}.
\]

Every even-degree reciprocal polynomial has a unique quotient presentation

\[
P(w)=w^mQ(y).
\]

The unit circle maps to the real interval

\[
y=2\cos\theta\in[-2,2].
\]

Conversely, for every real `y` in that interval, both roots of

\[
w^2-yw+1=0
\]

lie on the unit circle. Therefore

\[
\text{all nonzero roots of }P\text{ lie on }|w|=1
\]

is equivalent to

\[
\text{all roots of }Q\text{ are real and lie in }[-2,2].
\]

## Hostile in quotient coordinates

For the positive palindromic hostile,

\[
P(w)=w^2Q(w+w^{-1}),
\qquad
Q(y)=y^2+4y-2.
\]

Since

\[
Q(-5)=3,
\qquad
Q(-2)=-6,
\]

`Q` has a root strictly below `-2`. This is exactly the reciprocal off-unit
orbit seen without choosing either sheet.

## Corrected target

The completed theta problem should be attacked on the quotient object, not by
choosing between two reciprocal factors that completion has already
identified.

The source-derived theorem must force the quotient readout to be hyperbolic
with support in the physical interval. In finite form:

> The theta-authorized reciprocal coefficient packet produces a quotient
> polynomial whose roots all lie in `[-2,2]`.

In the continuous completed setting, `y` is replaced by the appropriate
two-sheet quotient coordinate, such as the centered square coordinate. The
same distinction remains:

- reciprocal symmetry guarantees descent to the quotient;
- RH requires real-support localization inside the physical quotient ray or
  interval.

This resembles a Lee–Yang or stability theorem more than spectral-factor
recovery. The next useful attack is to identify which theta label interaction
could enforce quotient hyperbolicity and to test it against `Q=y^2+4y-2`.
