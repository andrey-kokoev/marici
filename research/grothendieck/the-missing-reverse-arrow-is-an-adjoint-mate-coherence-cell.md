# The missing reverse arrow is an adjoint-mate coherence cell

## Reinterpretation of the capability gap

At the level of executable arrows, the theta tail compiler has

\[
p:F_+\longrightarrow Y_+
\]

but no authorized reverse map (Y_+\to F_+). The dual sector contains a
formally reversed arrow, yet comparison transport alone does not authorize it
as feedback in the direct sector.

The missing datum may therefore be one categorical rung higher than a new
primitive arrow.

## Required mate cell

Suppose the completed source supplies nondegenerate pairings on the forcing
and tail objects. Then the reverse incidence (q:Y_+\to F_+) is determined as
the adjoint mate of (p) by the coherence law

\[
\langle pf,y\rangle_Y
=
\langle f,qy\rangle_F.
\]

This equality is a 2-cell relating actuation, observation, and sewing. It does
three things that a bare transported arrow does not:

1. derives (q) uniquely from (p) and the source pairings;
2. fixes the phase and sign of the reverse incidence;
3. licenses the reverse map as an operation internal to the completed direct
   carrier.

With (q=p^*), the bordered incidence

\[
K=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&p\\
-p^*&0
\end{pmatrix}
\]

is skew-adjoint in the positive direct-sum metric. Unlike the separated
direct--dual double, this block need not be nilpotent. The mate cell therefore
breaks the Jordan-chain obstruction without fitting an arbitrary correction.

## Which side needs the next rung?

The coherence cell lives on the sewing/comparison side, because it compares
the direct arrow with its dual mate. Its pairings and boundary deltas must,
however, be generated on the source side. Thus neither side alone suffices:

- source data provide the forcing and tail pairings;
- sewing provides the mate identification;
- their 2-cell authorizes the executable reverse incidence.

Adding another scalar observer on the readout side would remain one rung too
low.

## Candidate source of the pairings

The already derived positive Clark-sheet identity supplies a natural tail
Gram form. The forcing coordinate is not in the ordinary relative-Haar domain,
so its pairing must include the constant--delta boundary plane of the
five-component Fourier cell and the primitive/square relative currents.

The exact next square is therefore

\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
F_+&\xrightarrow{p}&Y_+\\
\downarrow r_F&&\downarrow r_Y\\
F_-^*&\xleftarrow{q_-^*}&Y_-^*,
\end{array}
\]

together with a source-derived 2-cell filling it. Here (r_F,r_Y) are not
arbitrary Riesz maps; they must retain the completed boundary currents.

## Falsifiers

The proposal fails if:

- either pairing is degenerate on an admissible zero-state;
- the square commutes only after scalar compression;
- the primitive or square boundary delta remains unmatched;
- the mate has the wrong Green-form sign;
- cutoff pairings are incompatible or their graph norms diverge;
- a hostile source admits the same mate cell without the theta constructors.

## Result

The missing fifth-wall operation can be reformulated more economically: seek
one source-derived adjoint-mate coherence cell, not an independently chosen
reverse arrow. This is the next rung of the coherence tower. It is sufficient
to convert directional duality into positive skew-adjoint dynamics, but it has
not yet been constructed for the completed theta/Tate boundary object.
