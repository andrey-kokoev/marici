# The completion differential selects a positive circle operator

## Bounded question

Does endpoint completion canonically select a readout from the arithmetic
circle, or must a matrix minor be chosen after seeing `Xi`?

## Circle Laplacian and seam jet

On the mean-zero winding sector of `L2(R/Z)`, let

\[
 Ae_n=n^2e_n,
 \qquad n\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}.
\]

For `t>0`, the smoothed seam ket is

\[
 v_t=e^{-\pi tA/2}\delta_0.
\]

Its first two energy norms are

\[
 \|A^{1/2}v_t\|^2
 =\sum_{n\ne0}n^2e^{-\pi tn^2},
\]

\[
 \|Av_t\|^2
 =\sum_{n\ne0}n^4e^{-\pi tn^2}.
\]

Thus the endpoint differential acts on a finite jet of the same canonical
seam state; it introduces no fitted probe.

## Exact completed kernel

Set `t=e^(2u)`.  The standard Riemann Fourier kernel on the completed chart
`u>=0` is

\[
 \Phi(u)
 =\sum_{n\ge1}
 \left(
 4\pi^2n^4t^{9/4}-6\pi n^2t^{5/4}
 \right)e^{-\pi tn^2}.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
 \Phi(u)=\operatorname{Tr}B_t.
\]

where

\[
 B_t
 =\pi t^{5/4}
 A^{1/2}(2\pi tA-3I)A^{1/2}e^{-\pi tA}
\]

on the full mean-zero winding sector.  The two signs of each nonzero winding
produce exactly the displayed positive-`n` coefficients.  The polynomial
`2 pi t A-3I` is the operator form of the classical `n^4`--`n^2` completion
coefficients.

## Positivity from integrality and the completed chart

On the mean-zero integral sector,

\[
 A\ge I.
\]

On the completed chart, `t>=1`.  Therefore

\[
 2\pi tA-3I
 \ge(2\pi-3)I>0.
\]

Every other factor in `B_t` is positive and commutes with `A`, so

\[
 B_t>0\qquad(t\ge1).
\]

and consequently `Phi(u)>0` for `u>=0`.  The coercivity reserve of the
spectral polynomial is bounded below by the explicit source-independent
constant `2 pi-3` before the heat and derivative weights are applied.

This positivity depends on three already derived structures:

1. finite-place completion restricts winding to integers;
2. removal of the constant endpoint yields the gap `A>=1`;
3. modular sewing permits restriction to the chart `t>=1`.

Without any one of them, the simple inequality need not hold.

## Authorized compression

The scalar kernel is not an arbitrary diagonal chosen to avoid the
Gaussian-integer enlargement of packet 106.  It is the trace of the operator
obtained by applying the completion differential to the circle heat family.
The source-derived order is: adelic vacuum, integral circle Laplacian,
endpoint differential, \(B_t\), and finally
\(\operatorname{Tr}B_t=\Phi(u)\).

The Epstein coherence matrix remains a valid uncompressed seam object, but
the completed Riemann readout is the trace of this particular differential
jet, not unrestricted all-ones aggregation.

## RH boundary

The completed function has the cosine-transform form, up to the standard
normalization convention,

\[
 \Xi(x)=\int_0^\infty\Phi(u)\cos(xu)\,du.
\]

Positivity of `B_t` and `Phi` does not imply that this cosine transform has
only real zeros; hostile positive kernels already forbid that shortcut.  The
remaining theorem must constrain how the positive operators `B_t` vary with
`u`, not merely their pointwise sign.

The sharp next candidates are operator-valued total positivity, a canonical
system generated by the monotone spectral family `B_t`, or a variation-
diminishing theorem derived from the integral spectral gap.  The falsifier is
a positive gapped diagonal family with the same completion polynomial whose
cosine transform has nonreal zeros.
