# The bare Fourier–real 2-cell collapses before character transport

## Native operator relation

On the Schwartz rigging, let (mathcal F) use the kernel
(e^{-2\pi i x\xi}), and let (C) be complex conjugation. Directly from the
kernel,

\[
C\mathcal F C=\mathcal F^{-1}.
\]

Thus the paths (mathcal F C) and (C\mathcal F) are generally different.
Their discrepancy is controlled by

\[
\mathcal F^2=P,
\]

where (P) is parity.

## Collapse on the completed vacuum

The Gaussian source and arithmetic comb are real, even, and Fourier-fixed in
the self-dual normalization. On this prepared vacuum,

\[
C\Omega=\Omega,
\qquad
P\Omega=\Omega,
\qquad
\mathcal F\Omega=\Omega.
\]

Consequently both constructor paths return the same state. The bare 2-cell is
trivial after restriction to the completed vacuum and carries no RH force.

## Transport makes the comparison nontrivial

Character transport (M_z) does not generally preserve the real-even fixed
subspace. Therefore the relevant square is built from the transported state

\[
M_z\Omega,
\]

not from (Omega) alone. One must compare the two paths only after this
transport and retain the resulting labelled boundary discrepancy before
taking the vacuum scalar readout.

The admissible construction order is:

1. prepare the completed labelled source;
2. apply the Mellin or character transport;
3. apply Fourier–Tate and real comparison paths;
4. retain their 2-cell, including seam and endpoint currents;
5. scalarize last.

## Falsifier

Any proposed comparison computed on the untransported real-even vacuum must
be trivial. Any nontrivial phase obtained there has been imported through a
choice of frame or half-form rather than derived from this source action.

Conversely, a transported 2-cell that reduces solely to the final scalar
functional equation is still too late and adds no provenance.

## Result

The missing comparison channel is not merely higher categorical; it is
order-sensitive. Completion alone kills it. Character transport must break
the fixed-vacuum degeneracy before the two constructor paths are compared.
