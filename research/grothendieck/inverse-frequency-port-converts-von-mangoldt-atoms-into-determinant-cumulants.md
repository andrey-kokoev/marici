# Inverse-frequency port converts von Mangoldt atoms into determinant cumulants

## Question

The Fourier-conjugated order port is universal and contains no prime labels.
What source operation joins it to the labelled arithmetic currents without
fitting their coefficients?

## Labelled prime-power distribution

On the logarithmic multiplicative line, the arithmetic side of the Tate--Weil
explicit formula carries the symmetric prime-power distribution

\[
\mathcal V
=
\sum_p\sum_{k\ge1}
(\log p)p^{-k/2}
\left(
\delta_{k\log p}
+
\delta_{-k\log p}
\right).
\]

The coefficient is source-derived:

\[
\Lambda(p^k)=\log p.
\]

This distribution retains the prime and valuation labels before scalar Euler
aggregation.

## Inverse-frequency incidence

Let (Q) be multiplication by the logarithmic frequency:

\[
(Qh)(x)=xh(x).
\]

The Fourier-conjugated order operator has principal-value multiplier
proportional to (Q^{-1}).  On every nonzero prime-power atom,

\[
Q^{-1}
\left(
(\log p)p^{-k/2}
\delta_{k\log p}
\right)
=
\frac{p^{-k/2}}k
\delta_{k\log p}.
\]

The cancellation

\[
\frac{\log p}{k\log p}=\frac1k
\]

is exact.  Therefore the order port converts the von Mangoldt source weights
into the logarithmic determinant cumulants

\[
\frac{p^{-k/2}}k.
\]

This is the missing labelled-to-Hardy incidence at every nonzero arithmetic
frequency.

## The three regularity levels reappear

Splitting the resulting distribution by valuation depth gives:

- (k=1), the primitive determinant current;
- (k=2), the prime-square current;
- (k\ge3), the connected determinant tail.

Thus the regularity filtration is simultaneously:

- the cyclic-cumulant filtration of one valuation transition;
- the summability filtration of its vacuum cocycle;
- and the inverse-frequency image of the von Mangoldt distribution.

These are three constructions of the same source coefficients.

## Zero-frequency boundary

The operator (Q^{-1}) is undefined at (x=0).  Principal value fixes the odd
nonzero-frequency action but does not supply a DC state.  The missing
zero-frequency coordinate must remain explicit.

On the completed Tate side it is carried by the archimedean and neutral
boundary packet.  It cannot be removed by selecting a convenient pulse,
subtracting a constant, or declaring the inverse derivative to vanish on the
zero mode.

Aspect's gated-Fourier analogy gives the exact operational warning: finite-band
principal-value data can be reproduced while the DC endpoint extension is
chosen by hand.  Arithmetic reconstruction is faithful only when that endpoint
channel is separately retained.

## Relation to the explicit formula

The Tate--Weil explicit formula is the source correspondence joining:

- the prime-power distribution;
- the archimedean gamma distribution;
- the Fourier-transformed test function;
- and the spectral divisor distribution.

Applying the inverse-frequency port to its arithmetic side explains the
determinant cumulants.  It does not prove their desired orientation.  On the
spectral side, positivity of the resulting quadratic form on the full accepted
test class is a Weil-type RH criterion.

Thus the missing incidence was not absent.  It is the integrated explicit
formula.  What remains absent is the sign theorem.

## Noncircular scope

The distributional incidence and the coefficient identity above use no zero
locations and no RH assumption.  However, replacing the spectral divisor by a
positive measure on the critical line would assume the desired conclusion.

The permitted next question is narrower: does the special theta zero-state
subclass force the required sign of the integrated explicit-formula pairing
without invoking positivity for every Weil test function?

## Falsifiers

The proposed join fails if:

- a labelled two-shell or three-shell packet does not acquire coefficient
  (p^{-k/2}/k) after inverse-frequency transport;
- the constant-mode prescription discards the archimedean endpoint;
- prime labels are aggregated before (Q^{-1}) acts;
- or positivity is obtained only by placing the divisor on the critical line
  in advance.

## Result

The universal Hardy/order boundary and the arithmetic determinant packet are
joined by the labelled Tate--Weil explicit formula.  Inverse logarithmic
frequency converts von Mangoldt atoms exactly into determinant cumulants.  The
DC boundary remains archimedean, and the surviving hard problem is the
orientation of this already-derived correspondence.
