# The null-to-seam factorization lives in a Real, not holomorphic, category

## The seam involution

In the centered parameter plane, reciprocal reflection is (z\mapsto-z) and
the real structure is (z\mapsto\bar z). Their composite is the
antiholomorphic involution

\[
R(z)=-\bar z.
\]

Its fixed locus is

\[
\operatorname{Fix}(R)
=\{z:z=-\bar z\}
=\{z:\Re z=0\}.
\]

Thus the comparison equalizer is a Real fixed locus, not a complex-analytic
subspace of the parameter plane.

## Category mismatch

The scalar section (X(z)) is holomorphic, so its null pullback is a complex
divisor. The seam detector

\[
d(z)=(P-C)M_z\Omega
\]

depends on both (z) and (ar z). It is Real-analytic and equivariant, but
not holomorphic.

Consequently the desired factorization from the null divisor to the seam
cannot be a factorization of complex-analytic subspaces in an ambient
holomorphic neighborhood. The seam has real codimension one and is not the
zero locus of a nonzero holomorphic function on any open set.

This explains the directional obstruction to (d=KX): that proposal tried
to extend a Real fixed-locus statement as a holomorphic divisibility law.

## Correct categorical home

The parameter object should be a complex space equipped with the
antiholomorphic involution (R). Its morphisms may include equivariant
semilinear maps, and its distinguished boundary is the Real fixed object.

In that category:

1. the scalar divisor (Z_0) is an (R)-invariant discrete object;
2. the critical seam is (operatorname{Fix}(R));
3. RH asks for support of (Z_0) on the fixed object;
4. the proof witness may be a Real Green form, an equivariant boundary
   relation, or a semilinear exactness statement;
5. no holomorphic retraction of a neighborhood onto the seam is expected.

## Ordered conjugate paths

Scalar aggregation can average a conjugate pair to a real value while erasing
which ordered route carried which phase. Therefore the pre-scalar observer
must retain the ordered reciprocal and conjugate paths as a Real pair. Their
comparison effect is canonical without authorizing amplitude transport
between them.

This matches the source typing:

- (P) is linear reciprocal transport;
- (C) is antilinear real comparison;
- (R=PC) is an antiholomorphic parameter involution;
- scalar completion is a downstream Real-valued compression on the fixed
  locus.

## Result

The next wall is a change of enrichment, not another pullback. The two
pullbacks have already been found; their factorization must be proved in a
Real/equivariant boundary category. Holomorphic operator repair is the wrong
type.
