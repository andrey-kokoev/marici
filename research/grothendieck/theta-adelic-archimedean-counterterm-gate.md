## 1. The adelic hostile lift

Keep every finite-place datum fixed:

\[
 \Phi_f=\bigotimes_p'\mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p},
\]

the diagonal rational boundary, the standard global additive character, and
self-dual Haar measures. At the real place replace the minimal state

\[
 f_2=D(D+1)e^{-\pi x^2}
\]

by Nima's degree-twelve Fourier-fixed, vacuum-null Gaussian-polynomial state
(f_{12}). The global Tate integral still factors in its Euler chamber:

\[
 Z_{\mathbb A}(f_{12}\otimes\Phi_f,s)
 =\zeta(s)Z_\infty(f_{12},s).
\]

Its real Mellin factor is the minimal completed real factor times a
nonconstant reciprocal polynomial (H). Up to a nonzero normalization,

\[
 Z_{\mathbb A}(f_{12}\otimes\Phi_f,s)
 =2\xi(s)H((s/2-1/4)^2).
\]

Fourier self-duality still gives reciprocal sewing, and every nonzero
integral sample remains positive. Nevertheless, (H) has off-critical
zeros. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\text{local Heisenberg rigidity}
+\text{global rational sewing}
+\text{Fourier self-duality}
+\text{positive integral samples}
\not\Rightarrow\mathrm{RH}.}
\]

Tensoring the hostile real carrier with every standard finite vacuum thus
promotes it to a full adelic hostile readout. But its new off-critical zeros
form a finite archimedean divisor contributed by (H); they are not displaced
Riemann zeros. This distinction is essential.

## 2. What distinguishes the actual theta state

The finite places and rational boundary are identical in the actual and
hostile models. The distinction is the real differential provenance.
Fourier conjugation sends

\[
 D\longmapsto-D-1.
\]

Hence the invariant polynomial algebra is generated by (D(D+1)). Among
nonconstant invariant differential operators annihilating the Gaussian
vacuum value, the actual theta operator

\[
 D(D+1)
\]

is the unique normalized operator of least differential order. The
degree-twelve carrier uses a higher polynomial in this invariant. It passes
the broad adelic coherence laws but violates minimality.

Minimality therefore supplies a genuine source selector. It excludes the
hostile deformation without examining its zeros and prevents higher-order
real differential operators from injecting parasitic completed divisors.

It does **not** prove RH. Selection of one completed readout and orientation
of that readout's remaining divisor are different theorems.

## 3. Divisor hygiene under the logarithmic quarter-turn

The adelic product is multiplicative. Its logarithmic derivative rotates it
to the additive explicit formula:

\[
 \text{Euler product over places}
 \longmapsto
 \text{prime-power distribution plus archimedean boundary}.
\]

All finite-place contributions form the rigid von Mangoldt boundary. The
choice of real state changes only the archimedean distribution and finite
completion polynomial. For the hostile carrier,

\[
 \frac{d}{ds}\log Z_{12}(s)
 =\frac{d}{ds}\log(2\xi(s))
  +\frac{d}{ds}\log H((s/2-1/4)^2).
\]

The second summand is a finite rational spectral packet. The logarithmic
quarter-turn separates the divisor rather than mixing it:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Riemann divisor}
\sqcup\text{finite archimedean parasitic divisor}.}
\]

Minimal differential order deletes the second component. It explains why
the selected completed readout contains no avoidable higher-order real-place
zeros. It supplies no orientation of the remaining Riemann divisor.

## 4. Canonical archimedean normalization theorem

Put (A=D(D+1)) and (g=e^{-\pi x^2}). Every Fourier-invariant polynomial
differential carrier in the Gaussian module has the form

\[
 f_Q=Q(A)g
\]

for a polynomial (Q). Mellin transport intertwines the invariant generator
with its reciprocal spectral polynomial:

\[
 \mathcal M[Af](s)=s(s-1)\mathcal M[f](s).
\]

Consequently,

\[
 \mathcal M[f_Q](s)
 =Q(s(s-1))\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2).
\]

If vacuum nullity forces (Q(0)=0), write (Q(T)=T\widetilde Q(T)). The
adelic readout then factors canonically as

\[
 Z_Q(s)=2\xi(s)\widetilde Q(s(s-1)).
\]

Thus the extra divisor is labelled by the source operator
(widetilde Q(A)) before Mellin transformation. Removing it is not divisor
fitting; it is functorial cancellation of a known differential presentation
factor. The minimal carrier has (widetilde Q=1), so it is already primitive.

This proves a useful separation:

\[
\boxed{
\text{archimedean differential divisor}
\quad\text{versus}\quad
\text{primitive arithmetic divisor}.}
\]

Nima's degree-twelve packet falsifies unnormalized orientation claims, but it
cannot falsify an orientation claim stated on the primitive quotient.

## 5. Deutsch--Popperian conjecture

**Minimal archimedean energy conjecture.** Let (W_2) be the centered Weil
form obtained from the standard finite vacua, rational diagonal, and the
minimal real state (D(D+1)e^{-\pi x^2}). There is a source-derived positive
energy space and boundary map (J), constructed before the zero divisor,
such that

\[
 W_2(g*g^*)=\|Jg\|^2
\]

for every admissible logarithmic test function (g).

The construction must first quotient all real-place differential
presentation factors. Minimality makes that quotient trivial for (f_2).
What remains must factor through the arithmetic rational boundary, not
through an inserted divisor.

This conjecture is hard to vary:

- the finite boundary is fixed by the unramified local vacua;
- the real counterterm is fixed by minimal invariant differential order;
- the rational diagonal fixes the sewing; and
- the norm factorization must be derived without zero data.

If true, Weil positivity and hence RH follow. Conversely, merely declaring
(W_2) positive is Weil's criterion and supplies no explanation. The
degree-twelve model tests the divisor-hygiene stage; it is not a hostile model
for the already normalized arithmetic positivity stage.

## 6. Smallest falsifiers

The conjecture fails if any one of the following occurs:

1. an admissible test (g) has (W_2(g*g^*)<0);
2. the proposed (J) uses the zero divisor, \(\xi'/\xi\), or an RH-dependent
   completion;
3. the construction treats the degree-twelve finite divisor as arithmetic
   instead of identifying it as an archimedean presentation factor;
4. the alleged positive bulk is universal integration by parts and the
   arithmetic boundary is added only after the sign is known; or
5. minimality enters only as a label and performs no step in the energy
   identity.

The degree-twelve model is therefore the mandatory control experiment for
the normalization stage. After canonical removal of (H), it reduces to the
same arithmetic RH problem and ceases to be an independent falsifier of that
problem.
