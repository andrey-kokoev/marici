## Normalized prime axes

On the unitary seam \(s=1/2+it\), normalize the prime exchange by

\[
X_p(t)=\sqrt p\,E_p(1/2+it).

\[

\begin{pmatrix}

e^{i\theta_p}&0

=\cos\theta_p\,\sigma_1+\sin\theta_p\,\sigma_2.

\[
X_p^*=X_p,
\qquad

\]

\(\sigma_1,\sigma_2\) plane.

## Two-prime closure

For distinct primes \(p,q\), put

\[
\Delta_{pq}=t\log(q/p).
\]

Then

\[
X_pX_q
=\cos\Delta_{pq}\,I+i\sin\Delta_{pq}\,\sigma_3,
\]

\[
[X_p,X_q]=2i\sin\Delta_{pq}\,\sigma_3.
\]

If

\[
\sin\Delta_{pq}\ne0,
\]

the generated algebra contains \(\sigma_3\). Multiplying \(\sigma_3\) by
\(X_p\) supplies the direction in the \(\sigma_1,\sigma_2\) plane orthogonal
to \(X_p\). Therefore

\[
\operatorname{Alg}(I,X_p,X_q)=M_2(\mathbb C).
\]

comparison.

\[

algebra is the commutative span of \(I\) and \(X_p\).
