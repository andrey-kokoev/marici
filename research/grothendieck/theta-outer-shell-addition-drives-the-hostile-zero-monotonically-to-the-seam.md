# Theta outer-shell addition drives the hostile zero monotonically to the seam

## Bounded question

Does the smallest hostile positive-shell model contain a constructive
prototype of the divisor transport required from theta completion?

## Off-seam branch

For

\[
F_c(z)=\cosh z+c\cosh 2z,
\qquad
0<c<1,
\]

the hostile quotient root is

\[
x_-(c)
=
-\frac{1+\sqrt{1+8c^2}}{4c}
<-1.
\]

Write the corresponding right-half-plane zero as

\[
z_-(c)=a(c)+i\pi,
\qquad
a(c)=\operatorname{arcosh}(-x_-(c))>0.
\]

## Exact inward velocity

Set

\[
s(c)=\sqrt{1+8c^2}.
\]

Direct differentiation gives

\[
x_-'(c)
=
\frac{1+s(c)-8c^2/s(c)}{4c^2}
=
\frac{1+1/s(c)}{4c^2}
>0.
\]

Therefore

\[
a'(c)
=
-\frac{x_-'(c)}{\sqrt{x_-(c)^2-1}}
<0.
\]

As the positive outer-shell weight increases, the horizontal displacement of
the hostile zero decreases strictly. Its endpoints are

\[
\lim_{c\downarrow0}a(c)=\infty,
\qquad
\lim_{c\uparrow1}a(c)=0.
\]

Thus the zero enters from spectral infinity and collapses monotonically onto
the seam at the exact threshold (c=1).

## General divisor velocity

For a source deformation

\[
F_\tau(z)=F_0(z)+\tau H(z),
\]

a simple zero (z(\tau)) obeys

\[
z'(\tau)
=
-\frac{H(z(\tau))}{F_\tau'(z(\tau))}.
\]

Positivity of the added source (H) does not orient this complex ratio in
general. The two-shell calculation is special because the reciprocal quotient
reduces the motion to one ordered real root.

## Constructive conjecture

The model suggests a sharper Deutsch--Popperian target for an authorized theta
completion (F_\tau): every simple off-seam zero should satisfy

\[
\operatorname{Re}z(\tau)
\operatorname{Re}z'(\tau)
\le0,
\]

until it reaches the seam, while new divisor branches may enter only from
spectral infinity.

This is not asserted for theta. It is a finitely falsifiable transport law. A
single source-authorized completion step with outward horizontal velocity
rejects it.

## Result

The smallest positive reciprocal model exhibits exactly the completion rescue
allowed by the preceding Rouché gate: an off-seam zero arrives from infinity
and moves monotonically onto the seam as the missing outer-shell weight is
restored.

This is the first constructive prototype of a global theta divisor-flow
explanation. The next task is to define an authorized continuous completion
parameter for the fully sewn theta source and derive its zero velocity from
labelled source variation before inspecting the zeros.

## Sharp falsifier

For the chosen theta completion flow, find one simple zero with

\[
\operatorname{Re}z
\operatorname{Re}z'>0.
\]

That zero moves away from the seam and disproves monotone inward divisor
transport.
