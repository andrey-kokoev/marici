# Valuation excess times oriented front density is universally positive

## Coupling the two exact identities

The arithmetic filtration supplies, for each prime \(p\),

\[
Q_p=E_p-I+N_p.
\]

On a label with valuation \(r=v_p(n)\),

\[
Q_p(r)=
\begin{cases}
0,&r=0,\\
r-1,&r\ge1.
\end{cases}
\]

The two-front archimedean incidence supplies the positive density

\[
C'(u)=2e^{-\pi u^2}.
\]

Their source-derived product is

\[
\mathcal E_{p,r}(u)
=
2(\log p)Q_p(r)e^{-\pi u^2}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\mathcal E_{p,r}(u)\ge0
\]

for every prime, valuation, and real \(u\).

## Exact support

The density is strictly positive precisely when

\[
r\ge2.
\]

Thus it detects square and higher prime-power occupation and vanishes on:

- the prime vacuum \(r=0\);
- primitive single occupation \(r=1\).

Summed over primes, its kernel is exactly the squarefree label sector.

The result is the archimedean realization of

\[
\sum_p(\log p)(E_p-I+N_p)
=
\log\frac{n}{\operatorname{rad}(n)}.
\]

The arithmetic coefficient and Gaussian orientation were derived
independently from source constructors and meet without a fitted sign.

## Boundary routing

The universal subtraction \(-I\) does not enter the positive bulk alone. In
the two-chart decomposition it belongs to the symmetric constant overlap
channel. Combining it with primitive projection and valuation degree leaves
the nonnegative excess \(r-1\) in the oriented channel.

This is why operation order matters:

1. retain the two front charts;
2. split symmetric overlap from oriented incidence;
3. apply primitive projection and valuation degree;
4. route the universal line through the overlap;
5. push the remaining excess into the positive Gaussian density.

Scalar aggregation before this split would mix the negative unit overlap with
the positive oriented bulk.

## What this explains

The prime-square current is not an arbitrary correction to a divergent
primitive current. It is the coefficient of the first positive oriented
archimedean cell after primitive occupancy and the universal boundary line
are removed.

This gives a hard-to-vary explanation for the square grade:

- two complementary scale localizations create orientation;
- orientation differentiates to the positive Gaussian source;
- valuation excess counts repeated occupation;
- their product is the square-and-higher positive bulk.

## Remaining kernel

The theorem is not faithful. Every squarefree label has zero valuation excess
at every prime. Therefore the coupled positive bulk cannot by itself exclude
a zero-state supported in the squarefree sector.

This is the next real obstacle. A faithful global theorem must either:

1. show that admissible theta/Tate zero-states cannot lie wholly in the
   squarefree sector;
2. derive a complementary primitive-oriented density on that kernel; or
3. prove that the constant–delta overlap and archimedean boundary pair
   squarefree states nondegenerately.

Any additional channel must be source-derived and must not destroy the exact
positivity already obtained on valuation excess.

## Result

Valuation excess multiplied by the oriented two-front Gaussian density gives
a universal nonnegative arithmetic–archimedean bulk. It is strict on repeated
prime occupation and has exact squarefree kernel. The positivity problem has
therefore reduced to a complementary source law on squarefree states.
