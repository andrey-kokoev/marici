# Prior path-kernel results identify the three and forty-seven

## Finding

The previous dimensions 3 and 47 are properties of a particular two-feature ambient-memory fixture, not intrinsic dimensions of the actual Clark diamond.

The prior marked-diamond kernel supplies the decisive clue. Exact calculation now identifies the entire negative bar homology with the kernel of that fixture's terminal marked-path map. Its three classes consist of the two familiar diamond collisions plus one extra collision introduced by representing three chamber features in only two coordinates.

The 47 is the ordinary balanced quotient of the deliberately enlarged ambient-memory modules. Restricting to the actual source-generated prefix and suffix modules changes it to 5 in the compressed fixture, or 6 in the faithful three-chamber model. The corresponding negative homology remains 3 or 2, respectively.

## 1. Prior research used

### Voevodsky: the two-prime marked Clark diamond

`research/voevodsky/the-two-prime-marked-clark-diamond-is-faithful-before-rejoining-its-cut.md` computes the eight marked terminal columns. With three independent chamber letters a,b,c, their rank is six. The two kernel directions are:

- the difference of the two wholly forgotten routes;
- the difference between their sums of single-retained-event records.

The later sheet-reduction note proves that the actual spectral family preserves the finite chamber source. It does not compress three independent chambers into two numerical coordinates at one spectral point.

This immediately distinguishes our two-coordinate bar fixture from the actual finite source.

### Nima: free paths, cycle observers, and quotients

`research/nima/prime-cube-faithful-observers-paths-relations-and-nested-reconstruction.md`, section 6, distinguishes nonzero free route differences from the quotient imposing endpoint commutativity. It also records that the free acyclic path algebra is hereditary. These are precisely the appropriate source and resolution conventions for the current two-term bar calculation.

`research/nima/a-spanning-forest-cycle-port-is-the-minimal-finite-observer-completing-common-radial-history.md` identifies interval-additivity kernels with graph cycles. This motivates the boundary-cycle search, but its interval graph is not automatically the present coefficient-decorated bar complex.

`research/nima/common-history-forces-the-interval-cycle-quotient-but-does-not-make-it-a-green-radical.md` explicitly warns that an observation kernel and a Green radical require separate comparison hypotheses. We retain that distinction here.

### Nima: mapping coefficients rather than cofiber objects

`research/nima/joint-records-type-as-attachment-parameters-not-cofiber-objects.md` identifies the joint record as a projective mapping coefficient and constructs the cofiber attachment from that coefficient. It also shows that an all-input trace overcounts prefixes relative to typed-unit readout.

This reinforces the need to distinguish source-generated coefficients from all ambient memory states. Its factor-four trace calculation is not a formula for our 47; the models and maps are different. Nor should our bar cokernel be called the original joint-record cofiber merely because it is computed by a two-term complex.

## 2. The extra third class

The original bar fixture uses

a=(1,0), b=(0,1), c=(1,i),

so c=a+i b. This is an extra chamber relation. The actual independent three-chamber source does not satisfy it.

Order the marked paths by the route through 4, then the route through 6, each with markings 00,01,10,11. Besides the two familiar kernel vectors, the compressed fixture has the terminal relation

(0,-i,-1,0, 0,1+i,0,0).

Its value is -i(b+c)-a+(1+i)c=c-a-i b=0.

The terminal marked-path map therefore has rank five, not six. Its kernel has dimension three. With a,b,c replaced by independent coordinate vectors, rank six and the two familiar kernel directions return.

## 3. The path-to-bar cycle map

For a two-event route ef, with emitted homogeneous degree-one records sigma_e and sigma_f, define its bar derivative by the two edge terms

D(ef)=(e;1,sigma_f)+(f;sigma_e,1).

The two middle-cut contributions cancel under the bar boundary, leaving

partial D(ef)=(terminal;sigma_e sigma_f,1)
              -(initial;1,sigma_e sigma_f).

Thus D sends every terminal path relation to a negative bar cycle. It retains actual source edge labels; this is not an untyped cycle guess.

The new checker verifies that D is injective on the eight marked paths and that the images of the terminal kernel span all of H^(-1) in both tested ambient fixtures. Consequently the identification of the 3 is exact, not merely a match of dimensions.

The retained-letter grading makes its structure visible:

| Retained degree | H^(-1), two-feature fixture | H^0, two-feature fixture |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 1 | 5 |
| 1 | 2 | 10 |
| 2 | 0 | 32 |
| Total | 3 | 47 |

One negative class is the forgotten-route cycle; the other two are single-record relations, one of which comes from the extra feature compression. These are not the three ordinary graph cycles of the four-vertex diamond: that unmarked graph has only one independent cycle.

## 4. Why 47 is not intrinsic

The ambient construction puts a whole F_a on the left and F_(2-a) on the right at every vertex, for every allocation a=0,1,2. It includes memories unrelated to prefixes and suffixes of the admitted two-event packet, as well as features not produced by those paths.

For feature dimension d,

dim C^0=4[2 dim F_2+(dim F_1)^2]=12+16d+12d^2,

dim C^(-1)=16(1+d).

Their difference is 12d^2-4. Hence

dim H^0=12d^2-4+dim H^(-1).

At d=2 this is 44+3=47. At d=3, with faithful chamber features, the same ambient construction gives 104+2=106. This dependence on ambient feature dimension rules out reading 47 as an invariant of the arithmetic diamond.

In the original fixture the ambient composition map is onto F_2 of dimension seven, so its induced degree-zero kernel has dimension forty. That statement concerns the large ambient-memory quotient, not forty independent emitted arithmetic route classes.

## 5. Source-generated prefix and suffix modules

Replace the arbitrary memories at vertex x by their actual spans:

M_x=span of records of admitted paths from the fixed initial vertex to x,

N_x=span of records of admitted paths from x to the fixed terminal vertex.

They are closed under the typed right and left source actions. Their event grades are the actual prefix and suffix lengths. This is a change of modules, not an assertion that the previous ambient complex was computed incorrectly.

The dimensions become:

| Model | Ambient H^(-1), H^0 | Source-generated H^(-1), H^0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compressed two-feature fixture | 3, 47 | 3, 5 |
| Independent three-chamber model | 2, 106 | 2, 6 |

The source-generated degree-zero composition is an isomorphism onto the terminal record image. Its negative homology retains precisely the terminal relations.

For this diamond there is also a short proof. Write s and t for the initial and terminal vertices, and K for the terminal marked-path kernel. The intermediate local marked records are independent, so the right prefix module has the projective resolution

0 -> K tensor e_t S -> e_s S -> M -> 0.

The relation kernel is supported at the sink t. Tensor with the left suffix-record module N. Since N_t is the one-dimensional vacuum and N_s is the terminal record image, the resulting complex is

K -> N_s.

Its differential is zero: exactly those terminal relations have already vanished in N_s. Therefore

Tor_1^S(M,N)=K,

M tensor_S N=N_s.

This identifies the negative classes as relation data and the degree-zero classes as the observed terminal records, without dimension fitting. The faithful three-chamber case has dim K=2 and dim N_s=6, agreeing with the prior marked-diamond result.

## 6. Consequence for the lane

The useful target is no longer to find a new meaning for the integers 3 and 47. Their origin is now located:

- 3 includes a nonphysical feature-compression relation in the chosen fixture;
- 47 counts the enlarged ambient-memory balanced quotient;
- the source-generated faithful diamond instead gives the known pair (2,6).

The subsequent structural question should distinguish source-generated relation modules from dual-observation or arbitrary-memory enlargements. Nima's attachment construction supplies a complementary realization of the source coefficients; it does not identify the larger ambient module with that source.

No claim is made that these relation modules are Green radicals, new cofiber objects of the original source, or higher homotopy levels.
