# The mixed primitive-square extension is an exact valuation filtration step

## Candidate extension

After separate reciprocal adjointness proved global, the next candidate was a
mixed-depth class coupling the primitive and prime-square completion currents.
The source valuation module already decides whether such a class is new.

For a prime (p), let (T_p) multiply integer labels by (p), let

\[
E_p=I-T_pT_p^*
\]

be the primitive boundary projection, and let

\[
N_p e_n=v_p(n)e_n
\]

be valuation degree. The incidence resolution is

\[
N_p=\sum_{k\ge1}T_p^kT_p^{*k}.
\]

Therefore

\[
E_p-I+N_p
=\sum_{k\ge2}T_p^kT_p^{*k}.
\]

The proposed primitive-square extension is thus an exact filtration boundary:
primitive exclusion plus valuation degree, after removal of the universal
identity line, equals the square-and-higher current.

ports nor a nontrivial extension between two ports. They are consecutive
