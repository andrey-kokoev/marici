# The Three-Sector Face Pair Is a Tight Frame and Erases the Orientation Defect

## Combined bulk energy

The two faces of the homotopy sector are

\[
x_+=(D+z)K,
\qquad
x_-=(D-z)K.
\]

Their squared norms satisfy the pointwise polarization identity

\[
|x_+|^2+|x_-|^2
=
2|DK|^2+2|z|^2|K|^2.
\]

After integration,

\[
\|x_+\|^2+\|x_-\|^2
=
2\left(\|DK\|^2+|z|^2\|K\|^2\right).
\]

Thus the natural homotopy energy is exactly half the sum of the two face
energies.  The three-sector energy defect

\[
\Delta_3
=
\|x_+\|^2+\|x_-\|^2
-2\|DK\|^2-2|z|^2\|K\|^2
\]

vanishes identically for every source and every admissible `K`.

## The missing information

The sum retains no orientation.  The difference is

\[
\|x_+\|^2-\|x_-\|^2
=
4\operatorname{Re}\langle DK,zK\rangle.
\]

This cross-face interference is precisely the information discarded by the
tight-frame sum.  It is neither positive nor determined by the three
individual norms.  On a differential domain, integration by parts moves part
of it to the endpoint, but a bulk or phase contribution remains unless an
additional source law controls it.

The anti-diagonal completed zero trace cancels the oriented endpoint norm
difference.  It does not determine the interior cross-face pairing.

## Consequence for the tower architecture

The `3(3+2+1)+2+1` incidence architecture is coherent, but coherence alone is
orientation-neutral.  Its natural positive energy closes tautologically.
Therefore it cannot by itself force the centered real part to vanish.

The next required object is not a fourth copy of the same state packet.  It is
an interference instrument retaining

\[
\langle DK,zK\rangle
\]

before real projection.  In geometric algebra this is the oriented bivector
between the two face components `DK` and `zK`; in categorical language it is a
comparison of the two face maps, not another face target.

The instrument must be source-derived.  Declaring the cross term to vanish or
choosing a phase that makes it positive would simply restate the missing RH
orientation.

## Hostile universality

The tight-frame identity holds for arbitrary vectors `u` and `v`:

\[
\|u+v\|^2+\|u-v\|^2
=
2\|u\|^2+2\|v\|^2.
\]

Hence successful checks of the summed three-sector energy provide no evidence
specific to theta.  The only potentially explanatory observable is the
antisymmetric difference or its full complex polarization.

## Result

The three-sector face system is a perfect tight frame.  This explains why its
incidence and positive-energy checks close so cleanly, but it also proves that
their sum cannot carry RH force.  The missing rung is a source-derived
cross-face interference instrument.  The programme has reached orientation,
not positivity or completion, as its singular obstruction.
