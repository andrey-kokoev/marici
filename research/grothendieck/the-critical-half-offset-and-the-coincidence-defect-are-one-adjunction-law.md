# The critical half-offset and the coincidence defect are one adjunction law

On the weighted half-line use

\[
\langle g,h\rangle_w=\int_0^\infty e^{-q}g(q)h(q)\,dq.
\]

For (L=\partial_q), integration by parts gives

\[
\langle g,Lh\rangle_w
=\langle(1-L)g,h\rangle_w-g(0)h(0).
\]

Center the generator at the fixed point of the affine reflection:

\[
K=L-\frac12.
\]

Then the same identity becomes

\[
\langle g,Kh\rangle_w
=-\langle Kg,h\rangle_w-g(0)h(0).
\]

Thus (K) is skew-adjoint modulo one source boundary trace. The half-unit
center and the coincidence anomaly are inseparable parts of the same Green
adjunction.

## Relation to Fourier--Tate reflection

The source Euler operator obeys the Fourier reflection

\[
D\longmapsto-D-1.
\]

After centering (D+1/2), Fourier acts by sign reversal. The weighted
half-line generator has the identical algebraic form: after centering
\(L-1/2\), adjunction acts by sign reversal, with the endpoint trace recording
the failure of the open half-line to be boundaryless.

Therefore two effects previously treated separately have one origin:

- the distinguished spectral seam at real part (1/2);
- the coincidence germ producing the negative high-frequency tail.

The seam is the fixed locus of the centered reflection. The scalar defect is
the boundary anomaly of implementing that reflection on one half-line.

## What this buys

Reciprocal doubling can cancel the oriented endpoint traces when both copies
are retained with opposite boundary orientation. It cannot, from this
universal adjunction alone, orient the remaining matrix coefficient. The law
holds for every sufficiently regular forcing and therefore has no
theta-specific zero-selection force.

The theta-specific content is now isolated in the primitive heat line and its
interaction with the two-copy centered generator. A successful theorem must
show that Poisson sewing identifies the two primitive heat representatives in
a way that produces a sign or exactness law not implied by the universal
skew-adjoint relation.

## Falsifier

Any proposed confinement proof based only on the centered adjunction,
reciprocal sign reversal, and cancellation of the endpoint trace is universal
in (f). It must therefore accept hostile positive forcings and cannot prove
RH. The first admissible discriminator must explicitly use the labelled theta
heat generator before aggregation.
