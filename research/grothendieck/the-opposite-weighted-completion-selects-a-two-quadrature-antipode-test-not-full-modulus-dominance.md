# The Opposite-Weighted Completion Selects a Two-Quadrature Antipode Test, Not Full Modulus Dominance

## Scope correction

The opposite exponential sheet weights canonically produce the two endpoint
amplitudes

\[
F_a(t)=\int_0^\infty f(q)e^{aq}e^{itq}\,dq,
\qquad
\overline{F_{-a}(t)}.
\]

Strict modulus dominance between them is a source-authorized sufficient route
to RH. It is not equivalent to RH. Equal moduli can occur without the two
amplitudes being antipodal.

The scalar zero condition is only

\[
F_a(t)+\overline{F_{-a}(t)}=0.
\]

Thus the forbidden object is one projective direction, not the entire
equal-modulus circle.

## Native two-quadrature form

For real `f`, separate the completed readout into

\[
X(a+it)=2C_a(t)+2iS_a(t),
\]

where

\[
C_a(t)=\int_0^\infty
f(q)\cosh(aq)\cos(tq)\,dq,
\]

and

\[
S_a(t)=\int_0^\infty
f(q)\sinh(aq)\sin(tq)\,dq.
\]

Therefore the exact minimal condition is

\[
a\ne0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\left(C_a(t),S_a(t)\right)\ne(0,0).
\]

On the seam, `S_0` vanishes identically. Only the symmetric quadrature remains,
and its ordinary zeros are allowed. Off the seam, the antisymmetric quadrature
acquires meaning. RH says the two active quadratures never lose meaning at the
same point.

This matches the two-sector interpretation more closely than global modulus
dominance: the seam is where one comparison channel degenerates by symmetry,
not necessarily the boundary of a globally timelike cone.

## Projective formulation

When `F_a(t)` is nonzero, define

\[
\rho_a(t)=\frac{\overline{F_{-a}(t)}}{F_a(t)}.
\]

RH requires only

\[
\rho_a(t)\ne-1
\]

for `a` nonzero. Full de Branges separation demands `|rho_a(t)|` differ from
one and therefore forbids every point of the unit circle. That stronger theorem
may fail even if the antipode remains protected.

## Better attack target

The minimal source theorem can take either of two forms:

1. a common-zero exclusion theorem for `C_a` and `S_a`;
2. a projective transport law preventing `rho_a` from reaching `-1` off the
   seam.

The second may use winding, oriented incidence, or labelled prime-scale
transport without imposing a global norm order. It is therefore a better
Deutsch-Popperian conjecture: it forbids exactly the impossible construction
and no continuum of unrelated configurations.

The smallest hostile falsifier is a source-admissible labelled packet for
which both quadratures vanish at one off-seam parameter. Mere equality of
their parent amplitudes' norms is not a falsifier.

## Relation to the completed boundary system

The weighted completion remains essential. It removes homogeneous forgers and
makes both endpoint amplitudes source-canonical. The correction concerns only
the final comparison:

- completion selects the faithful projective pair;
- scalar aggregation selects the antipode;
- RH protects that antipode off the seam;
- modulus dominance is one possible but overstrong protection mechanism.

## Result

The source-faithful completion reduces RH to simultaneous nonvanishing of two
real quadratures, or equivalently exclusion of one projective antipode. This
is strictly weaker and more precise than full vertical-modulus dominance.
