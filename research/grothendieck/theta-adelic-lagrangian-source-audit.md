# Source audit of the adelic Lagrangian confinement route

## Exact algebraic/group-level results

The rational phase lattice

`Lambda_Q=Q+Q subset A+A`

is self-annihilating for the adelic Heisenberg symplectic bicharacter. Likewise,
`Z_p+Z_p` is self-annihilating locally. Hence these are exact maximal
commuting Heisenberg subgroups in the LCA/Pontryagin sense.

At each finite place set, the product-formula charge covector defines a pure
spinor with maximal-isotropic annihilator

`L_S=ker(epsilon_S)+C epsilon_S`.

An algebraic all-place version also exists in the hyperbolic double of
finite-support currents and the full algebraic dual.

These results establish source incidence and group-level maximality without
using zeros.

## Failure of ordinary linearization

The diagonal `Q` is discrete in the adeles, so

`T_0 Lambda_Q=0`.

Ordinary tangent/Lie differentiation therefore sends the group-level maximal
isotropic lattice to the trivial, nonmaximal linear subspace. Pontryagin
self-duality does not produce a Hilbert Lagrangian boundary condition by
differentiation.

The all-place pure-spinor route has a parallel completion obstruction: the
charge covector exists algebraically, but its Fourier-rotated all-ones current
is not in the finite-support current space or the raw Fisher completion.

## Four confinement gates

1. **Construction:** only group/pure-spinor incidence is constructed. No
   source-derived boundary trace `Gamma:Dom(S*)->B+B`, regular Weyl
   representation, or Cayley graph relation for the logarithmic Dirac carrier
   is supplied.
2. **Isotropy:** exact for the exponentiated Heisenberg bicharacter, but no
   theorem identifies it with the operator Green form on a closed boundary
   domain.
3. **Maximality/closure:** exact as an LCA annihilator and finite algebraic pure
   spinor, but not as a maximal self-adjoint linear relation after
   restricted-product completion.
4. **Determinant:** no relative determinant of such an extension is identified
   with `xi(1/2+iz)` up to a nowhere-zero source unit.

## First missing gate

The earliest missing object is representation-theoretic, before analytic
maximality or determinant comparison: a source-selected regular representation
of the adelic Weyl relations in which the rational lattice generates the
correct maximal abelian boundary algebra, Fourier exchanges the polarization
algebras, and the completed theta state is cyclic. Only then can a closed
Cayley/graph relation and Dirac extension be asked for.

## Decision

The abstract forbidden-nonreal-incidence theorem is exact once a maximal
self-adjoint Lagrangian boundary relation and determinant comparison exist.
The approved source constructs neither. Self-dual lattice terminology cannot
be promoted to scalar-null confinement; ordinary linearization is explicitly
falsified.
