# Archimedean completion and logarithm audit

## Question

Does the conditional initial ordered ring canonically determine the analytic operations needed by the completed-zeta spline?

## Algebraic completion chain

From the conditional initial semiring, additive group completion gives the initial ordered ring. Localizing its nonzero positive classes gives its ordered fraction field. Abstractly, the Dedekind or Cauchy completion of this ordered field is the complete Archimedean ordered field, unique up to the unique order-preserving field isomorphism fixing the dense fraction field.

Consequently, if these completion operations are admitted functorially, the real target is not one arbitrary coefficient field among many. Positive square roots are then uniquely selected by order, so the half-power `n^(-1/2)` is canonical in the real closure/completion.

## Logarithmic normalization residual

The ordered field structure and topology do not by themselves normalize an additive logarithmic coordinate. Every continuous multiplicative-to-additive character on positive reals has the form

`ell_c(x)=c log(x)`

for a real constant `c`. Order chooses the sign when monotonicity is required, but not the positive scale `c`. Equivalently, continuous exponential isomorphisms form the reciprocal one-parameter family `exp_c(t)=exp(t/c)` until a tangent, a unit of additive scale, or one value is declared.

The Gaussian orbit fixes this constant only after its scale parameter and generator are normalized. The completed-zeta gamma factor also fixes it, but then the desired analytic source has already been supplied.

## Authority boundary

No approved Carrier packet constructs the ordered fraction-field completion as a source functor or proves compatibility with occurrence-resolved Carrier transports and physical readouts. The construction is mathematically canonical from the conditional `pi_0` ring, but its admission into the shared Carrier calculus remains a missing promotion arrow.

Even granting that arrow, the logarithm retains one global scale constant rather than one parameter per prime. This sharply reduces the prior ambiguity.

## Hostile test

Keep the same completed ordered field, positive square roots, intrinsic height, and multiplication. Replace `ell` by `c ell` for `c>0`, while inversely rescaling the Mellin spectral parameter. ## Disposition

The Archimedean target and half-power are canonically recoverable in principle from the conditional initial ordered ring, subject to an unconstructed Carrier-compatible completion functor. The surviving normalization defect is one global logarithmic scale. The next gate is a source-derived tangent or scale generator comparing Carrier multiplication with Gaussian/Mellin translation.
