# Even Theta Symmetry Pairs Newman Collisions and Reduces the Four-Edge Holonomy to One Three-Edge Cell

## Connection parity

For the even Newman family on the real spectral axis,

\[
H(\lambda,-x)=H(\lambda,x).
\]

Hence \(H_x,H_{xxx}\) are odd and \(H_{xx}\) is even. The Prüfer connection
from the preceding packet satisfies

\[
\omega_x(\lambda,-x)=\omega_x(\lambda,x),
\qquad
\omega_\lambda(\lambda,-x)=-\omega_\lambda(\lambda,x).
\]

The complete rectangular holonomy is therefore twice the holonomy of the
positive-height half-rectangle.

## No central collision

At the symmetry center,

\[

=\int_0^\infty e^{\lambda u^2}\Phi(u)\,du>0.
\]

Also \(H_x(\lambda,0)=0\) by evenness. Thus the collision section
\(G=H+iH_x\) never vanishes on the central edge \(x=0\), and its phase there
is canonically zero.

Every collision at \(+x_0\) is accompanied by a collision at \(-x_0\). Since
each has degree \(-1\), a symmetry-linked collision event has total degree
\(-2\).

Under backward heat, the positive collision emits two conjugate off-seam
zeros near \(+x_0\); its negative partner emits their reciprocal images near
\(-x_0\). The physical output is one full four-zero orbit. The quartet is
therefore created by two mirrored cubic collisions, not one isolated fold.

## Three active boundary ports

On the half-rectangle

\[
0\leq\lambda\leq\lambda_+,
\qquad
0\leq x\leq T,
\]

the central edge is inert. Three active ports remain:

1. the physical heat edge \(\lambda=0\);
2. the de Bruijn real-phase edge \(\lambda=\lambda_+\);
3. the positive spectral-height wall \(x=T\).

If their oriented phase integrals are denoted by \(-q\), \(p\), and \(-r\),

\[
\operatorname{Hol}_{\mathrm{full}}

\]

The half-cell degree \((p-q-r)/(2\pi)\) counts positive-height collision

## Multi-tower interpretation

The full four-wall rectangle is not four independent towers. Reciprocal
symmetry glues two copies of one three-port cell along a central port whose
source value is fixed and nonzero.

The minimal architecture is therefore:

\[
2\times
(\text{physical edge}+\text{de Bruijn edge}+\text{infinity wall})

\]

This is a mathematically derived instance of the operator's recursive tower

half-holonomy. The negative spectral wall carries no independent information;

because it carries the infinity incidence.

1. the de Bruijn-edge Prüfer phase;
