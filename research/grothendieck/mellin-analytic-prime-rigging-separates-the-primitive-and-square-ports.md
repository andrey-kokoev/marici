# Mellin-analytic prime rigging separates the primitive and square ports

## Question

Nima's prime-five minor proves that adding the primitive and square unilateral
currents on one carrier destroys hyponormality near the seam.  Is their
separation merely a declaration, or does the source supply different
completion grades in which naive addition is not an admitted operation?

## Prime-label Hilbert scale

Let

\[
\mathcal E_0=\ell^2(\mathbb P)
\]

on the prime labels.  For every \(\delta>0\), define

\[
\mathcal E_\delta
=
\left\{
c:
\sum_p p^{2\delta}|c_p|^2<\infty
\right\}.
\]

The projective-limit test space and its strong dual are

\[
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{an}}
=
\bigcap_{\delta>0}\mathcal E_\delta,
\qquad
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{an}}'
=
\bigcup_{\delta>0}\mathcal E_{-\delta}.
\]

The weights are generated by the Mellin label \(\log p\), since

\[
p^\delta=e^{\delta\log p}.
\]

Thus \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{an}}\) is the space of coefficient packets analytic
under real Mellin displacement, rather than an externally fitted sequence
space.

## Placement of the three currents

For \(\sigma=\Re z\), the primitive vacuum coefficient has worst magnitude

\[
p^{-1/2+|\sigma|}.
\]

For every fixed \(\sigma\), choose \(\delta>|\sigma|\).  Then

\[
\sum_p
p^{-1+2|\sigma|-2\delta}
<\infty.
\]

Hence the primitive cocycle defines an element of
\(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{an}}'\) throughout every finite vertical strip, although
it does not belong to \(\mathcal E_0\).

The square cocycle belongs to \(\mathcal E_0\) for

\[
|\sigma|<\frac14,
\]

and the connected coefficient tail is absolutely summable for

\[
|\sigma|<\frac16.
\]

The native graded carrier is therefore

\[
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{an}}'
\oplus
\mathcal E_0
\oplus
\ell^1(\mathbb P),
\]

with the primitive, square, and connected channels occupying distinct ports.

## Source symmetries are continuous

Mellin translation acts diagonally by

\[
(M_tc)_p=p^{it}c_p.
\]

It is isometric on every \(\mathcal E_\delta\), hence continuous on the test
space and its dual.  Reciprocal reflection acts on the local valuation factor
by

\[
R_pe_{p,j}=e_{p,-j}.
\]

It leaves the prime weights unchanged and is isometric on every grade.
Therefore both vertical spectral transport and direct--dual reflection extend
without mixing the three regularity levels.

## Why naive grade addition is forbidden

There is no canonical continuous inclusion

\[
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{an}}'
\longrightarrow
\mathcal E_0.
\]

In particular, the primitive source coefficient is not an \(\ell^2\) vector.
Consequently the operation that places the primitive and square currents on
one Hilbert carrier and forms their sum is not defined by this source rigging.
Nima's negative mixed minor is the precise result of forcing that undeclared
identification.

This does not manufacture positivity.  It says only that the failed mixed
self-commutator belongs to a lossy quotient presentation, not to the typed
source object.

## Determinant assembly, not grade addition

The source does not yet authorize a linear incidence from the primitive port
to the square port.  The two currents are cyclic cumulants of one bordered
transition, so their native assembly is determinant-line reconstruction, not
operator addition.  Any archimedean constructor that compares the ports must
therefore act on the full graded determinant packet rather than begin with a
map

\[
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{an}}'
\longrightarrow
\mathcal E_0.
\]

The correct next target is the archimedean action on the relative determinant
packet consisting of the first cumulant, second cumulant, and third-order
determinant tail.  No mixed primitive--square operator term is required unless
that action derives one.

## Falsifier

The construction fails if either:

- reciprocal reflection or Mellin translation leaves the declared rigging;
- the archimedean determinant incidence needs a topology depending on the
  Euler cutoff;
- its domain excludes the actual primitive cumulant;
- or its boundary form agrees only after scalar cancellation of separately
  typed channels.

## Result

The primitive and square currents are separated by a source-derived
Mellin-analytic Hilbert scale.  Their naive addition is not an authorized
operation.  The remaining RH-bearing question is the archimedean action on
their complete relative determinant packet.
