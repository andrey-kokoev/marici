# Compact-Open Coherence Cannot See a Symmetric Divisor Escaping Through Infinity

## Exact hostile family

For \(R>0\), define

\[
Q_R(w)=\frac{((w-R)^2+1)((w+R)^2+1)}{R^4}.
\]

It is real and even, so it preserves conjugation and reciprocal reflection.
Its zeros are

\[
R\pm i,
\qquad
-R\pm i.
\]

Thus every member contains an off-seam symmetry quartet.

Expanding gives

\[
Q_R(w)
=1+\frac{2(1-w^2)}{R^2}
+\frac{(w^2+1)^2}{R^4}.
\]

Consequently \(Q_R\to1\) uniformly on every compact set. More explicitly,
for \(|w|\leq M\),

\[
|Q_R(w)-1|
\leq
\frac{2(1+M^2)}{R^2}
+\frac{(M^2+1)^2}{R^4}.
\]

Multiplying any completed readout \(X(w)\) by \(Q_R(w)\) therefore inserts
an off-seam quartet while converging locally uniformly back to \(X\).

The in, out, and control towers describe local states and their comparisons.
The forward/backward coherence towers establish compositionality. None of

Properness is an additional outer control channel. It observes escape of
support rather than disagreement of finite comparisons. In the programme's

\[
\text{local coherence}
\longrightarrow
