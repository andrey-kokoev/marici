# RH requires zero-orbit isotropy enhancement, not merely Real equivariance

## Ambient Real structure

In the centered coordinate, the completed theta transform carries two
involutions:

\[
F:z\longmapsto-z,
\qquad
C:z\longmapsto\overline z.
\]

Their product is

\[
R=FC:z\longmapsto-\overline z.
\]

The fixed locus of `R` is the centered critical seam.

For a real even completed source, the scalar divisor is invariant under the
Klein-four group generated by `F` and `C`. This is a genuine source-derived
anti-linear structure.

## Equivariance versus isotropy

At a generic off-seam zero `z`, the symmetry orbit is

\[
\{z,-z,\overline z,-\overline z\}.
\]

The divisor is equivariant, but the point has trivial stabilizer. A seam zero
satisfies

\[
z=-\overline z
\]

and therefore has nontrivial isotropy under `R`.

Thus RH is not the existence of the Real action. It is the assertion that
every nontrivial zero orbit has isotropy enhancement:

\[
Rz=z.
\]

Categorically, the divisor must land in the `R`-fixed subobject, not merely in
the category of `R`-equivariant objects.

## Exact positive-source hostile

For `t>0`, take the positive even two-cell transform

\[
X_t(z)=2\left(1+\operatorname{sech}(t)\cosh z\right).
\]

It is real and even, so it carries the full `F`, `C`, and `R` action. Yet

\[
z_t=t+i\pi
\]

is a zero, and its symmetry orbit has four distinct points. In particular,

\[
Rz_t=-t+i\pi\ne z_t.
\]

The ambient anti-linear wall therefore accepts the hostile rather than
rejecting it.

## Consequence

The missing constructor cannot be merely:

- complex conjugation of source coefficients;
- reality of the completed transform;
- reciprocal evenness;
- an equivariant determinant line;
- closure of the divisor under the Klein-four action.

All are present in the hostile model.

The required law must force stabilizer enhancement specifically on the
zero-state subobject. Equivalent forms include:

- a canonical comparison cell identifying the reciprocal and Real images of
  each zero-state;
- a Hermitian boundary condition making those two images the same admissible
  state;
- a localization theorem placing zero-state support in the `R`-fixed sector;
- a conserved current whose vanishing forces `R`-isotropy.

## Deutsch--Popper form

The impossible task is now precise: construct a source-authorized zero-state
with a free four-point symmetry orbit. The positive two-cell source performs
that task under the generic Real-even constructor repertoire. Theta arithmetic
must supply an additional constructor invariant that makes the same task
impossible for the completed theta source.

## Disposition

The ambient Real-structure branch is closed. The next attack must act on the
zero-state domain or boundary condition and prove isotropy enhancement there;
otherwise it only reproduces quartet symmetry.
