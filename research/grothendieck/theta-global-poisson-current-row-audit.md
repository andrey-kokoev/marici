# Audit of a global Poisson construction for the complete Green-current row

## Decision

Global Tate--Poisson completion is legally and noncircularly constructed at the
scalar-section level, and global Fourier--Poisson reciprocity exists at the
undecomposed state level. No approved packet lifts either construction to the
complete tail--seam Green-current row. The proposed bypass therefore stops at
an explicit operator-valued observer-lift gate.

## What exists

`theta-global-tate-integral-is-the-legal-joint-completion-operation.md`
constructs the adelic matrix coefficient `Z(phi,s)` directly from the complete
Schwartz--Bruhat source. Reciprocal norm splitting and Poisson summation give
continuation without separately continuing primitive, square, and connected
log currents. This is the correct joint operation and avoids the fixed
archimedean-current no-go.

The undecomposed additive theta carrier also has a state-valued reciprocal
cell: Fourier maps `g_q` to `g_-q` and fixes the integer comb. Thus source-level
reciprocity itself is not missing.

## Missing lift

The Tate packet explicitly leaves absent an operator-valued map from the global
scalar matrix coefficient to seam incidence/return maps. The fourth-tower
packet formulates the required observer

`J : global Poisson state carrier -> full multiplicative boundary vessel`

with naturality `J F = F_Tate J`, retaining primitive, square, connected,
seam, mixed, and archimedean coordinates before Mellin scalarization. No such
`J` or naturality proof is supplied.

This absence is structural. Prime-power seam sampling is not faithful on the
continuous source: a nonzero variation supported between sample locations has
zero samples but nonzero Poisson response. Hence global sewing cannot be
reconstructed bottom-up from Euler diagnostics, and finite prime cutoffs are
not Fourier-stable source subobjects.

## Relation to the forcing residual

The full-source Green forcing is independently identified as the oscillatory
autocorrelation transform

`R_f(x,y)=4 integral A_f(d)e^(-d/2)sinh(xd)cos(yd) dd`.

Global reciprocity gives the correct relative sign but does not turn this bulk
transform into a boundary derivative. Positivity of `A_f` is insufficient
because of the cosine factor. Without `J`, there is no typed current row whose
derivative can be compared with this residual or with `R_mix`.

## First missing object

Construct, before scalar Mellin evaluation, a source-level doubled
differential/correspondence carrying both Poisson sewing and the Green boundary
form, together with the full observer lift `J`. Its naturality residual must
vanish in every retained boundary grade. Reconstructing it backward from
`Z(phi,s)` or from Euler samples would be tautological or impossible.
