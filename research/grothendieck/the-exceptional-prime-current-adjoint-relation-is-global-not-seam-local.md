# The exceptional prime-current adjoint relation is global, not seam-local

## Completion-class candidate

For a real completed half-line source (A), define

\[
B_L(z)=\int_0^L A(v)e^{izv}\,dv
\]

and the reciprocal prime-power boundary current

\[
C_{k,p}(z)
=p^{-k/2}\bigl(B_{k\log p}(z)+B_{k\log p}(-z)\bigr).
\]

At a scalar zero, the sums over primes in the exceptional depths (k=1,2)
upgrade from divergent to convergent. The proposed confinement mechanism was
that their completed reciprocal limits might become adjoints only on the
critical seam.

## Exact real-structure identity

Reality of (A) gives

\[
\overline{B_L(z)}=B_L(-\overline z).
\]

Therefore, without assuming a zero or restricting (z),

\[
C_{k,p}(-\overline z)=\overline{C_{k,p}(z)}.
\]

Whenever a prime sum converges, the same identity passes to its limit:

\[
C_k(-\overline z)=\overline{C_k(z)}.
\]

This adjoint relation is global. It is the ordinary real structure of the
source, not a seam-local consequence of unitary Tate sewing.

## Consequence

The completion-class jump remains meaningful: a zero removes the leading
divergent coefficient of both exceptional currents. But the reciprocal
adjointness of their upgraded limits cannot confine that jump to real part
one half, because the relation holds identically wherever the limits exist.

Thus the exceptional completion levels provide a faithful interpretation of
what changes at a zero, but no law governing where the change may occur. The
distinction mirrors the programme's recurring boundary:

- regularity type detects the scalar zero;
- real structure pairs its reciprocal location;
- neither supplies orientation toward the fixed seam.

## Falsifier and revised target

Any proof claiming confinement from

\[
C_k(-\overline z)=C_k(z)^*
\]

alone is invalid, since that identity is source-real and global. A surviving
completion theorem must use an additional relation sensitive to the
horizontal displacement, such as a nontrivial comparison between the
primitive and square limits that is not inherited termwise from reality.

The next candidate must therefore be a mixed-depth extension class, not two
separate adjoint current limits.
