# Todd half-cell translation is not yet the Mellin half-density

## The tempting identification

Reciprocal sewing uniquely centers the Todd germ by

\[
e^{t/2}\frac{t}{e^t-1}.
\]

The completed zeta functional equation is centered at \(s=1/2\). It is
tempting to identify these two half-shifts. That identification is not yet
typed.

## Two different generators

In ordinary Euler–Maclaurin calculus, the Todd variable is realized by an
additive differential generator

\[
t\rightsquigarrow D_x=\frac{d}{dx}.
\]

Then

\[
e^{D_x/2}f(x)=f\!\left(x+\frac12\right),
\]

so the Todd centering is a half-cell translation.

For the Mellin transform

\[
\mathcal M f(s)=\int_0^\infty f(x)x^{s-1}\,dx,
\]

the spectral half-shift is instead generated by multiplication by \(\log x\):

\[
\mathcal M(x^{1/2}f)(s)=\mathcal M f\!\left(s+\frac12\right).
\]

These source operators are distinct. Their commutator is

\[
[D_x,\log x]=\frac1x.
\]

Thus no naturality square may identify them without an additional
source-derived comparison.

## Mellin-side witness

Integration by parts gives

\[
\mathcal M(D_xf)(s)=-(s-1)\mathcal M f(s-1),
\]

under the appropriate endpoint conditions. Additive differentiation induces
a spectral shift together with a coefficient. It is not the same operation
as the pure half-density shift induced by \(x^{1/2}\).

For the explicit source \(f(x)=e^{-x}\), half translation gives

\[
\mathcal M(f(x+1/2))(s)=e^{-1/2}\Gamma(s),
\]

whereas half-density multiplication gives

\[
\mathcal M(x^{1/2}f(x))(s)=\Gamma(s+1/2).
\]

The ratio \(\Gamma(s+1/2)/\Gamma(s)\) depends on \(s\), so no constant unit
identifies these two operations.

## The missing level

The formal germ by itself does not specify what its variable acts on. The
next pullback must therefore retain an operator realization:

- the carrier space;
- the infinitesimal generator assigned to \(t\);
- the Mellin transform functor;
- the comparison between additive and logarithmic transport;
- the boundary term created by their noncommutation.

This is a sixth datum beyond the multiplicative germ. It determines whether
the local reciprocal half-density has any legitimate image in the global
spectral coordinate.

## Geometric meaning

The two half-shifts live along different axes. Todd centering moves the
origin of an additive lattice cell. Mellin centering changes weight along a
multiplicative scale orbit. Passing between them requires the exponential
map \(x=e^q\), including its Jacobian and endpoint incidence.

That exponential comparison is the next source square. Its anomaly is not
optional: the commutator above is the infinitesimal witness that additive
and multiplicative transport do not commute.

## Scope

This result does not disprove a connection between the two half-shifts. It
disproves their identification from the formal coefficient \(1/2\) alone.
A connection can become valid only after deriving the exponential-coordinate
pullback and all its boundary currents.

## Result

The Todd half-density is canonically forced inside the formal reciprocal
boundary germ, but it is initially a half-cell translation, not the Mellin
spectral half-density. The next level is the operator-valued realization of
the germ and the exponential-coordinate comparison between additive and
multiplicative transport.
