# Theta zeros reduce to unit eigenvalues of the seam return map

## Two invertible sectors and one interface

Let the completed doubled source operator have block form

\[
 \mathcal D_s=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 A_{+,s}&B_s\\
 C_s&A_{-,s}
 \end{pmatrix},
\]

where `A_+` and `A_-` are the two oriented first-order tail operators and
`B,C` are the source-derived seam incidence maps.  Packet 165 proves the
one-sided operators are boundedly invertible on their decaying graph domains

## Exact Schur reduction

A kernel state `(x,y)` obeys

\[
 A_+x+B y=0,
 \qquad
 Cx+A_-y=0.
\]

Eliminating `x` gives

\[
 \left(A_--CA_+^{-1}B\right)y=0.
\]

Equivalently, define the seam round trip

\[
 \boxed{
 R_s=A_-^{-1}CA_+^{-1}B.}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{
 \ker\mathcal D_s\ne0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 1\in\operatorname{Spec}_p(R_s),}
\]

subject to the typed domains of the four maps.  The corresponding state is

\[
 x=-A_+^{-1}By,
 \qquad R_sy=y.
\]

## Meaning of a zero

After one-sided invertibility, a completed scalar zero can no longer mean a
puncture created inside either half-sector.  It means that a nonzero interface
state survives one complete route

\[
 \text{minus seam}
 \xrightarrow{B}
 \text{plus bulk}
 \xrightarrow{A_+^{-1}}
 \text{plus response}
 \xrightarrow{C}
 \text{minus bulk}
 \xrightarrow{A_-^{-1}}
 \text{minus seam}
\]

with total multiplier exactly one.

Thus the two half-planes are shadows of two independently solvable sectors;
the zero belongs to their relationship, not to either sector separately.

## RH-bearing target

The sharp source theorem is now

\[
 \boxed{
 \Re(s)\ne\tfrac12
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 1\notin\operatorname{Spec}_p(R_s).}
\]

A sufficient but not logically necessary statement is strict contractivity

\[
 \lVert R_s\rVert<1
\]

in a source-derived interface metric.  On the seam, reciprocal Fourier--Tate
transport may become unitary, so unit eigenvalues are no longer excluded.

This is not the previously falsified primewise acute-cone claim.  `R_s` is the
complete global return map containing all primes, primitive and square

## Relation to the Green identity

The missing doubled conservation law should be interpreted as the energy
balance of this return map.  If it proves that every off-seam circuit loses a
strictly positive amount of the completed relationship energy, then `R_sy=y`
is impossible.  At the seam the horizontal loss coefficient vanishes.

The typed residual is precisely the part of the proposed energy balance not
accounted for by the four source maps defining `R_s`.

## Hostile multiplier test

An arbitrary symmetric divisor factor can create a scalar zero only by
adjoining an additional interface loop with unit return.  It is rejected if
that loop cannot be generated by the authorized seam incidence maps `B,C`.
This makes canonical-section rigidity operational rather than declarative.
