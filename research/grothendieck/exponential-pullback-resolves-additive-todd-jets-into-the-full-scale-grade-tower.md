# Exponential pullback resolves additive Todd jets into the full scale-grade tower

## Scope correction

The operator powers below construct the full ambient scale-grade carrier,
but the Todd coefficients do not activate every grade. The uncentered Todd
series has a linear term and even higher powers; every odd coefficient above
degree one vanishes. The centered Todd series has only even powers. The full
Euler prime-power current therefore requires the separate Euler logarithm.
The earlier wording that Todd functional calculus itself generated every
connected grade was too strong.

## Exact power law

Under the unitary exponential pullback, the additive generator becomes

\[
A=UD_xU^{-1}=e^{-q}\left(D_q-\frac12\right).
\]

The multiplier and derivative do not commute. Their commutation relation
nevertheless gives the closed formula

\[
A^k
=
e^{-kq}
\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}
\left(D_q-\frac12-j\right).
\]

Moving \(D_q-1/2\) through \(e^{-kq}\) lowers it by \(k\), supplying the
next falling-factorial term and proving the formula inductively.

## Arithmetic specialization

At a prime scale \(q=\log p\),

\[
e^{-kq}=p^{-k}.
\]

Thus powers of one source-derived generator supply carrier slots for the
primitive grade \(k=1\), square grade \(k=2\), and connected grades
\(k\geq3\). Their spectral action is the centered falling factorial

\[
\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}
\left(D_q-\frac12-j\right),
\]

which records the ordering anomaly exactly.

## Pullback of the Todd germ

Applying a functional calculus to \(D_x\) and conjugating by \(U\) sends its
\(k\)-th coefficient into precisely this scale-grade packet. For the Todd
series, the active packets are \(k=1\) and the even grades. For the centered
Todd series, only even grades remain. The full prime-power current must be
supplied by the Euler logarithm, whose coefficients are nonzero at every
positive grade.

This still explains why the primitive and square packets occupy canonical
carrier slots. It does not derive all of their arithmetic coefficients from
Todd normalization alone.

## Scope

The formula derives the algebraic grade and scale decay. It does not by
itself prove Hilbert or trace-class thresholds, which also depend on measure,
tensor product, and local normalization. Those analytic classifications must
now be recomputed from this common source operator.

whether reciprocal sewing pairs the two sectoral series term by term or only

\(k=2\) with no named boundary incidence.

The stronger next gate is a typed comparison between the Todd boundary
cocycle and the Euler-logarithm current. Closure only after scalar summation

## Result

The exponential pullback converts the \(k\)-th additive operator jet into a
scale weight \(e^{-kq}\) times a centered falling-factorial spectral
operator. At prime scales this is the \(p^{-k}\) carrier filtration. Todd
normalization occupies only its linear and even slots; the Euler logarithm
is required for the full arithmetic tower.
