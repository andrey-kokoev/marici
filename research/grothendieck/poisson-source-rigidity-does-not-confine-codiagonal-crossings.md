# Poisson source rigidity does not confine codiagonal crossings

## Consequence of source rigidity

Full Poisson sewing selects the standard integer comb uniquely in the declared
order-zero tempered class. The completed Mellin packet nevertheless retains at
least two typed components:

\[
v(s)=(e,b(s)),
\]

where (e\neq0) is the fixed endpoint channel and (b(s)) is the completed
bulk channel. The scalar readout is an incidence functional

\[
\xi(s)=\ell_s(v(s)).
\]

Because the endpoint coordinate is fixed and nonzero,

\[
v(s)\neq0
\]

for every (s). Thus every nontrivial scalar zero is necessarily a
codiagonal event

\[
v(s)\in\ker\ell_s,
\]

not annihilation of the full labelled source packet.

## Smallest hostile geometry

Put (z=s-1/2). Consider the reciprocal analytic packet

\[
v_a(z)=(1,z^2-a^2)
\]

with the fixed projection

\[
\ell(x,y)=y.
\]

It has all of the following properties:

- the packet is nowhere zero;
- the endpoint coordinate is fixed;
- reciprocal reflection (z\mapsto-z) preserves the packet;
- the readout is even;
- for real nonzero (a), the scalar readout vanishes at (z=\pm a), away
  from the imaginary seam.

Therefore uniqueness of the source, reciprocal symmetry, a fixed endpoint
channel, and a fixed scalar projection do not confine codiagonal crossings.

## What is now closed

The following proposed explanations are insufficient:

1. off-seam zeros require a hostile reweighting of the integer comb;
2. full labelled Poisson provenance alone protects the scalar divisor;
3. a nowhere-zero completed packet makes its scalar readout zero-free;
4. reciprocal symmetry confines crossings to its fixed seam.

All four fail before analytic estimates enter.

## Singular remaining theorem

The actual theta Mellin orbit must obey a source-derived incidence orientation
that the hostile packet lacks. In projective language, its curve must remain
inside one open cell of the hyperplane arrangement on each open half-plane.
In geometric algebra, the endpoint–bulk blade must retain a fixed signed
scalar component off the seam.

This condition cannot be inferred from source uniqueness because it concerns
the relative motion of the source packet and the readout hyperplane after
completion.

The next attack is therefore to derive the projective velocity of the actual
endpoint–bulk packet from the heat generator and ask whether it admits a
closed cone, monotone angle, or variation-diminishing law. The earlier
Laguerre and Evans residuals are different coordinate expressions of this same
orientation gate.
