# Theta nontrivial shear has no fixed positive passivity metric

## Positive contraction no-go

Let

\[
 S(F)=\begin{pmatrix}1&F\\0&1\end{pmatrix}
\]

with (F\ne0). Suppose a positive-definite Hermitian matrix (P) satisfied

\[
 S(F)^*PS(F)\le P.
\]

Conjugating by (P^{1/2}) gives

\[
 A=P^{1/2}S(F)P^{-1/2}
\]

with

\[
 A^*A\le I.
\]

Both singular values of (A) are at most one. But

\[
 |\det A|=|\det S(F)|=1,
\]

so their product is one and both singular values equal one. Hence (A) is
unitary.

This is impossible: (S(F)) is a nontrivial unipotent Jordan block, whereas
a matrix similar to a unitary matrix is diagonalizable. Therefore no such
positive (P) exists.

Applying the same argument to (S(F)^{-1}) rules out

\[
 S(F)^*PS(F)\ge P.
\]

Thus a nontrivial shear is neither contractive nor expansive in any fixed
positive-definite metric.

## Doubled version

The conjugate-doubled shear from packet 208 also has determinant of modulus
one and retains nontrivial Jordan blocks. The same singular-value argument
rules out a fixed positive-definite metric making the doubled transport
contractive or expansive.

This does not conflict with its fixed indefinite Hermitian form. Indefinite
conservation and positive passivity are categorically different capabilities.

## Consequence for the RH programme

A static bounded-real or KYP inequality for the shear cannot be the missing
orientation theorem. Any successful positive law must contain an explicit
source supply or boundary-current term. Schematically, it must compare a
change of stored positive energy with work entering through the primitive,
square, seam, and archimedean ports.

The required identity is therefore dynamic rather than a fixed-metric
contraction. Its spectral real part must multiply a faithful positive internal
energy, while scalar silence must force the completed boundary supply to
vanish. This is the Green-current architecture already isolated before the
Rosenbrock construction, now with an exact reason it cannot be replaced by
generic passivity.

## Time-dependent metrics

One can manufacture a transported metric by pulling a positive form backward
through (S(F)). Such a metric is automatically conserved, but it is
tautological unless independently derived from the theta/Tate boundary
currents. Likewise, choosing a metric after seeing the zero locus has no
source authority.

The seam-selective reciprocal cosh norm is source-derived, but packet 204
shows that the scalar zero-state does not automatically belong to its bounded
domain. The missing boundary-supply identity must provide precisely that
coupling.

## Falsifier

Any fixed positive matrix claimed to make a nonidentity shear contractive or
expansive is falsified by determinant one and the Jordan form. A dynamic
metric claim fails if its derivative includes an undeclared bulk term, if its
boundary supply omits a typed current, or if the metric is defined by
transporting the desired answer backward.

## Scope

This closes fixed-positive-metric passivity for the single and conjugate-
doubled shear realizations. It does not rule out a source-derived dynamic
storage-and-supply identity, construct that identity, or prove RH.
