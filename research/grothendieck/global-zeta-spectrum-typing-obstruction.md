# The present global zeta-spectrum question is ill-typed

Epistemic-graph event: 1377.

## Local-to-global audit

Ledger 1352 proves that every canonical determinant available from the norm
homology is the constant `1`.  Therefore any Euler product assembled only
from these admitted local determinants is

`product_p 1=1`.

It has no nontrivial zeros or poles and cannot reproduce `zeta(s)`, its
completion, or any nontrivial `L`-function.

One could insert weights `p^(-s)`, choose invertible operators, or impose a
regularized product.  But those operations require precisely the prime
indexing, spectral parameter, Frobenius eigenvalues, convergence prescription,
and archimedean factor that the emergence program is meant to derive.  Their
manual insertion would not test the conjecture.

## Missing typed structure

A meaningful global spectral claim minimally requires:

1. canonical local objects indexed by intrinsic closed-point loci;
2. source-derived Frobenius operators with nonzero eigenvalues;
3. cohomological grading and signs for determinant formation;
4. a global restricted product or trace-class operator;
5. an archimedean contribution and functional-equation duality; and
6. a theorem relating its determinant or spectrum to a completed `L`-function.

None of these follows from the regular-fiber torsion module alone.  In
particular, a set of torsion-support primes is not a space of closed points,
and nilpotent scalar multiplication is not geometric Frobenius.

## Verdict and falsifier

The current construction cannot host Riemann's nontrivial zeros.  The
question becomes well-typed only after an independently derived global
operator meets the six conditions above.

This verdict is falsified by producing, from the admitted Carrier and
correspondence data without inserted prime labels, a canonical global
operator whose local determinant factors are nontrivial and whose completed
determinant is proved equal to `xi(s)` (or another explicitly named completed
`L`-function).

## Scope

This is a typing obstruction for the present construction, not a theorem
that no future geometric enlargement can realize a zeta spectrum.
