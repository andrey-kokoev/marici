# No Finite Central-Jet Tower Can Orient the Modular Conormal

## Hostile multiplier family

Fix any finite central-jet depth \(M\). Choose an even integer \(N\) with
\(2N>M\), and define

\[
H_N(z)=1+\left(\frac zR\right)^{2N},
\qquad R>0.
\]

This multiplier is even and real-entire:

\[
H_N(-z)=H_N(z),
\qquad
H_N(\bar z)=\overline{H_N(z)}.
\]

Moreover,

\[
H_N(z)=1+O(z^{2N}),
\]

so its derivatives through order \(M\) agree with those of the unit at the
center.

Its zeros are

\[
z_k=R\exp\left(\frac{(2k+1)\pi i}{2N}\right),
\qquad k=0,\ldots,2N-1.
\]

Because \(N\) is even, none lies on the imaginary axis. The multiplier
therefore inserts reciprocal-conjugate off-seam zero orbits while remaining
invisible to the prescribed finite central-jet tower.

## Consequence for a completed section

Let \(F\) satisfy the completed parity and reality laws and put

\[
F_N=H_NF.
\]

Then \(F_N\) has the same symmetry. Since \(H_N-1\) vanishes to order \(2N\),
the central jets of \(F_N\) and \(F\) agree through order \(M\). Nevertheless,
\(F_N\) contains the off-seam divisor of \(H_N\).

Thus no condition depending only on:

- reciprocal evenness;
- conjugation reality;
- and finitely many central value or conormal jets

can exclude off-seam zeros.

## Meaning for the local sp4 model

The Clark conormal and the `3+4+3` quadratic normal form remain correct local
geometry. They cannot supply global orientation at any fixed finite jet depth.
Increasing the finite-dimensional normal form merely postpones the hostile
multiplier to a higher order.

The missing RH law must therefore use information that \(H_N\) cannot
preserve, such as the complete labelled theta source, all-scale boundary
transport, or an infinite source-generated current identity.

## Deutschian conclusion

The hard-to-vary explanation cannot be “enough derivatives at the modular
center determine the divisor.” They never do at finite order. The explanation
must say why the hostile multiplier is not constructible from the authorized
theta operations, and it must detect that failure before inspecting its
zeros.

## Scope

This is a finite-jet no-go theorem. It does not rule out a complete infinite
jet, a quasianalytic source class with independently proved growth bounds, or
a nonlocal modular-current theorem.
