# Fourier self-duality does not remove the Gaussian label-exponent ambiguity

## Question

Can the approved Fourier-fixed Gaussian vacuum eliminate the family `Q_a e_m=m^(2a)e_m` and thereby select the completed-zeta logarithmic calibration from the Carrier?

## Claim boundary

Self-dual Haar normalization canonically fixes the continuous Gaussian

`phi(x)=exp(-pi x^2)`

inside a declared additive locally compact group and Fourier character. It fixes the coefficient `pi` and the reciprocal scale convention in the continuous variable. It does not identify an abstract Carrier component class `m` with the real argument `m` of that Gaussian.

For every `a>0`, define the labelled orbit

`g_m^(a)(v)=exp(-pi m^(2a) exp(2v))`.

Every member uses the same Fourier-normalized Gaussian. Multiplication by an intrinsic prime obeys

`g_(pm)^(a)(v)=g_m^(a)(v+a log p)`.

Thus the family preserves the Gaussian source, positivity, multiplicative label transport, and translation covariance. It changes only the comparison between intrinsic multiplication and continuous scale. Choosing the shift to be exactly `log p` is equivalent to choosing `a=1`; it is not a consequence of Fourier fixedness.

## Cheapest discriminator

A source law must compare two independently defined objects:

1. multiplication transport `T_p e_m=e_(pm)` from the initial semiring; and
2. a continuous scale translation of fixed length derived without evaluating `p` in the real coefficient field.

The current Gaussian packet defines their equality at the already labelled jump `v=k log p`. It checks coherence after the comparison has been supplied. It does not construct the comparison from coefficient-neutral Carrier data.

Fourier quarter-turn covariance cannot distinguish the alternatives because it acts on the continuous Gaussian coordinate, while `a` lies in the label-to-coordinate realization map.

## Disposition

Fourier self-duality fixes the analytic Gaussian normalization but leaves the Carrier-label exponent `a` free. The missing arrow remains a normalized comparison from intrinsic multiplicative transport to continuous additive scale. A valid acceptance test must derive one nonzero scale unit independently and prove `T_p` translates by `log p` relative to it; reusing prime jump locations is circular.
