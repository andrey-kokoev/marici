# Theta/Fourier--Poisson constructor descent audit

## Question

Must the theta/Poisson operation already exist on the occurrence-resolved Carrier, or is it canonically generated after the conditional `pi_0` arithmetic realization?

## Canonical target construction

Group completion of the initial component semiring gives the initial ring as a discrete additive lattice. Its canonical embedding into the complete Archimedean ordered field gives a self-dual lattice pair after the standard additive character and self-dual Haar normalization are fixed by the tangent-normalized real field structure.

On the resulting Schwartz space, Fourier transform is canonical. The Fourier-fixed Gaussian and Poisson summation for the embedded lattice then produce the Jacobi theta relation. Mellin transform of its vacuum-subtracted positive ray gives the archimedean gamma factor and reciprocal functional equation.

Therefore theta/Fourier--Poisson need not be the image of an operation on the full `D4`-resolved Carrier groupoid. It is a canonical constructor internal to the Archimedean realization of the group-completed `pi_0` lattice. Requiring pre-quotient Fourier structure was too strong.

## Compatibility with formal Euler arithmetic

Unique factorization in the initial semiring gives the intrinsic formal Euler product. Evaluation in the same Archimedean/complex Mellin character yields the Dirichlet series and Euler product in the absolute-convergence half-plane. The theta/Mellin route supplies completion and reciprocal continuation. Both routes begin from the same conditional initial arithmetic object, so their scalar agreement is the standard integer-lattice identity rather than a comparison inferred from dimensions.

## Remaining source issue

This chain is conditional on a pointed rank-one Carrier subcategory freely generated under disjoint union by one connected object `U`. The full shared Carrier contains multiple source objects and nontrivial automorphisms. No approved theorem selects `U` canonically across sectors or proves that restricting to its disjoint-union subgroupoid is a source-invariant arithmetic quotient rather than a chosen presentation.

Different connected generators each produce an abstract initial semiring and an isomorphic theta/zeta lens, but no source map identifies which generator carries the declared physical or cross-sector provenance. Abstract uniqueness of the initial semiring does not identify the embeddings of those copies into the full Carrier.

## Disposition

Theta/Fourier--Poisson descent passes conditionally at the `pi_0` lattice level; pre-quotient Fourier structure is unnecessary. The first remaining emergence gate is pointed-generator authority: derive or uniquely characterize the connected Carrier unit `U` and the rank-one disjoint-union subcategory functorially inside the shared occurrence-resolved Carrier.
