# The unilateral seam cocycle is filled by the Mellin orbit before completion

## Target

The first nonexact-looking post-trace residual is

\[
C_f(z)
=A(-\overline z)-\overline{A(z)},
\qquad
A(w)=\int_0^\infty f(q)e^{wq}\,dq,
\]

for real `f`. Is this a genuine interface residue, or is it the boundary of
an already authorized source path?

## Canonical spectral-flow filling

Put `w=conjugate(z)`. Since `f` is real,

\[
C_f(z)=A(-w)-A(w).
\]

The multiplicative character transport `e^(wq)` is a one-parameter source
action generated by multiplication by `q`. Its straight reciprocal orbit is

\[
t\longmapsto A(tw),
\qquad
-1\le t\le1.
\]

The fundamental theorem of calculus gives

\[
A(-w)-A(w)
=-w\int_{-1}^{1}A'(tw)\,dt.
\]

Since

\[
A'(tw)=\int_0^\infty qf(q)e^{twq}\,dq,
\]

the filling evaluates to

\[
-w\int_{-1}^{1}A'(tw)\,dt
=-2\int_0^\infty f(q)\sinh(wq)\,dq
=C_f(z).
\]

Thus `C_f` is exactly the boundary of the source-derived Mellin orbit from
`w` to `-w`. No fitted antiderivative and no zero location is used.

## Disposition

On the ordinary test-function domain where differentiation under the integral
and orbit integration are valid, the seam cocycle is fillable. It is not a
nontrivial normalized interface class.

This is directly analogous to Strominger's nested Brunnian hostile: every
proper face can exhibit a nonzero residual, yet the full residual lies in the
subgroup generated by an authorized higher filling.

## Where a genuine class can remain

The filling uses the generator

\[
Qf(q)=qf(q)
\]

and the entire orbit `e^(twQ)f`. It survives completion only if:

1. `Q` is defined on the completed boundary domain;
2. the orbit remains in that domain for every `t` between `-1` and `1`;
3. endpoint evaluation is continuous along the orbit;
4. primitive, square, seam, and archimedean currents retain the resulting
   boundary terms;
5. the family of fillings is compatible and equicontinuous under Euler
   cutoff refinement.

Failure of any condition can turn a fillable algebraic cycle into a genuine
completion class. The RH-bearing obstruction is therefore not `C_f` itself.
It is possible non-descent of its canonical Mellin-orbit filling to the
completed boundary category.

## Exact completion residual

Let `M_X(w)` denote the finite-cutoff orbit filling and let `partial` be the
typed boundary map. Define

\[
R_X(w)=C_{f,X}(w)-\partial M_X(w).
\]

Algebraically, `R_X=0` whenever the full orbit and all endpoint channels are
retained. The completed test is stronger. For every boundary-null sequence
`x_X`, one must have

\[
M_X(w)x_X\longrightarrow0
\]

uniformly along the reciprocal orbit in the declared graph topology.

A sequence for which the source packet becomes null while its orbit filling
has a nonzero boundary limit is the smallest genuine witness. It is the
homotopy analogue of a nonclosable operator graph.

## Consequence for the critical seam

The factor `w` in the filling does not make the cocycle vanish at nonzero seam
height. Instead, it shows that the residual is generated by continuous
spectral flow between the reciprocal endpoints. The special role of the
critical seam must arise from the completed domain or flux law, not from
algebraic non-fillability of the unilateral cocycle.

## Next attack

Determine the constructor-generated boundary seminorms for which the
dilation generator `Q`, its reciprocal orbit, and endpoint evaluation are
jointly continuous. Test the primitive and square channels first because
they are precisely the levels discarded by trace-class-only completion.

## Scope

This proves algebraic fillability of the unilateral seam cocycle by a
source-authorized Mellin orbit. It does not prove that the filling survives
restricted-product completion, identify a nonzero completion class, connect
one to the Evans divisor, or prove RH.
