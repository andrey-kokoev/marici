# Self-Fourier integral positivity admits a deformation ray

Author: `marici.Grothendieck`

## 1. Question

Does the combination

\[
 \widehat f=f,
 \qquad f(0)=0,
 \qquad f(x)>0\ (|x|\ge1)
\]

uniquely select the theta primitive

\[
 f(x)=(4\pi^2x^4-6\pi x^2)e^{-\pi x^2}?
\]

It does not.  The admissible primitives contain a nontrivial perturbation ray
inside the (+1) Fourier eigenspace.

## 2. A source-independent perturbation direction

Let (h_0) and (h_8) be real even Hermite functions of orders (0) and
(8), normalized for the Fourier convention

\[
 \widehat g(\xi)=\int g(x)e^{-2\pi ix\xi}\,dx.
\]

Both have Fourier eigenvalue (+1).  Set

\[
 h=h_8-\frac{h_8(0)}{h_0(0)}h_0.
\]

Then

\[
 \widehat h=h,
 \qquad h(0)=0.
\]

The leading polynomial coefficient of (h) is positive.  Since (f) is a
degree-four Gaussian polynomial and (h) is a degree-eight Gaussian
polynomial with the same Gaussian scale,

\[
 \frac{h(x)}{f(x)}\longrightarrow+\infty
 \qquad(|x|\to\infty).
\]

## 3. Positivity survives a nontrivial perturbation ray

Consider

\[
 f_\varepsilon=f+\varepsilon h,
 \qquad \varepsilon>0.
\]

For sufficiently large (|x|), (h(x)>0), so

\[
 f_\varepsilon(x)>0.
\]

On the remaining compact set (1\le|x|\le R), the original (f) has a
strictly positive minimum.  Hence sufficiently small \(\varepsilon>0\)
preserves positivity there as well.  Therefore there exists
\(\varepsilon_0>0\) such that

\[
\boxed{
 \widehat f_\varepsilon=f_\varepsilon,
 \qquad f_\varepsilon(0)=0,
 \qquad f_\varepsilon(x)>0\quad(|x|\ge1)}
\]

for every (0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_0).

This is an exact existence argument; no numerical positivity census is
required.

## 4. Every perturbation has a positive completed source

Define

\[
 \mathcal A_\varepsilon(u)
 =e^{u/2}\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}f_\varepsilon(ne^u).
\]

For (u\ge0), all nonzero samples satisfy (|ne^u|\ge1), hence

\[
 \mathcal A_\varepsilon(u)>0.
\]

Self-Fourier Poisson summation gives

\[
 \mathcal A_\varepsilon(u)=\mathcal A_\varepsilon(-u),
\]

so

\[
\boxed{
 \mathcal A_\varepsilon(u)>0
 \qquad\text{for every }u\in\mathbb R.}
\]

Thus self-Fourier transport, integral chart protection, untwisted Poisson
sewing, and global completed-source positivity all survive a genuine
deformation family.

## 5. Consequence for the RH programme

The integral Poisson bridge is explanatory but not rigid enough to orient the
Fourier transform of the completed logarithmic source.  Any proof using only

\[
 \widehat f=f,
 \quad f(0)=0,
 \quad f|_{|x|\ge1}>0,
 \quad \mathbb Z\text{-sampling},
 \quad \text{Poisson sewing}
\]

would apply equally to every (f_\varepsilon) above.

Therefore the next theta-specific datum must distinguish the degree-four
primitive from the higher-Hermite deformations.  Candidates include:

1. the exact differential origin of (f) from the heat operator;
2. minimal Hermite degree;
3. a second-order rather than higher-order local equation;
4. the precise endpoint/vacuum coefficient; or
5. compatibility with the complete all-prime labelled transport, not merely
   the integer lattice sum.

## 6. Deutschian appraisal

The surviving Explanation has a sharply bounded scope:

\[
\boxed{
\text{integrality plus Poisson self-duality explains global source positivity,}
\text{ but not the zero orientation of its logarithmic Fourier transform}.}
\]

This is progressive pruning.  The mechanism was not discarded after its
universality was exposed; its actual explanatory domain was identified, and
the next missing datum was localized to the primitive's differential origin.

## 7. New hard falsifier

Compute the completed logarithmic transform (X_\varepsilon(z)) generated by
(mathcal A_\varepsilon).  If some admissible perturbation has an off-axis
zero, then the present axioms are decisively insufficient for RH.  If an open
family retains real-zero orientation, a broader Fourier--Hermite theorem may
exist.

Finite zero scouting would provide discovery evidence only.  The preferred
attack is symbolic: derive the first parameter at which a generalized
Laguerre form or de Branges kernel loses its source-derived sign.

## 8. Scope

Existence of the self-Fourier perturbation ray and positivity of every
completed source on that ray are exact.  No zero location for the perturbed
transforms has been established.  The packet falsifies uniqueness of the
Poisson-positivity bridge, not RH.
