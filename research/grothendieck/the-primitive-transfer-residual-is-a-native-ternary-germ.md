# The Primitive-Transfer Residual Is a Native Ternary Germ

## Residual after the relative-response transfer

The source primitive \(h'=f\) transfers the relative Green response into a
boundary current but leaves the common-path residual

\[
R(z,h,P)=-2zhP,
\qquad
P=u+v,
\]

after fixing the authorized nonzero source amplitude. Its three typed inputs
are:

1. centered spectral control \(z\);
2. moving-seam primitive \(h\);
3. common reciprocal path \(P\).

## Aspect native-arity test

At the simultaneous basepoint \((z,h,P)=(0,0,0)\), the residual vanishes on
every coordinate axis and every coordinate two-plane:

\[
R(z,0,0)=R(0,h,0)=R(0,0,P)=0,
\]

and

\[
R(z,h,0)=R(z,0,P)=R(0,h,P)=0.
\]

Equivalently, its value, every first derivative, and every derivative involving
at most two of the three typed directions vanish at the origin. But

\[
\partial_z\partial_h\partial_P R=-2.
\]

The first nonzero local witness is therefore ternary.

The exact finite-difference cube says the same thing. For increments \(a,b,c\),

\[
\Delta_z^a\Delta_h^b\Delta_P^cR(0,0,0)=-2abc,
\]

while every lower-dimensional face difference is zero.

## Consequence for the tower

No collection of separately totalized unary or binary germs can detect this
residual. There are only two legitimate constructions:

1. retain a direct ternary mate joining control, seam primitive, and common
   reciprocal path;
2. factor through binary mates while retaining the complete three-instance
   carrier and a proved associator until the final comparison.

This is precisely Aspect's arity gate. A scalar antiderivative or a sequence of
pairwise endpoint equalities would erase the only nonzero germ.

## Relation to the hidden two-port result

The response pair solved one earlier quotient error:

\[
(r_+,r_-)\longmapsto(r_\Sigma,r_\Delta).
\]

The primitive current then transports \(r_\Delta\) but produces a residual in
the complementary common mode \(P\). The next coherence cell must therefore
see both:

- the relative response being transferred;
- the common path created by that transfer;
- the spectral control that weights their mismatch.

The defect has moved from a hidden binary split to a native ternary incidence.

## Scope of the source amplitude

Before fixing the common nonzero amplitude \(c\), the raw term is

\[
-2zhcP.
\]

If amplitude variation is part of the declared target, this is a fourth typed
input and must be tested at arity four, unless the source supplies an
independently authorized multiplication \((h,c)\mapsto hc\). The ternary
theorem is exact on the normalized source fiber; it is not an absolute
ontology-closure claim.
