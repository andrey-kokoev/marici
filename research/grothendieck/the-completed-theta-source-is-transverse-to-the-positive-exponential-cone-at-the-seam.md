# The Completed Theta Source Is Transverse to the Positive Exponential Cone at the Seam

## Membership question

The positive-mode orientation theorem applies to functions of the form

\[
f(u)=\int_{[0,\infty)}e^{-\alpha u}\,d\mu(\alpha),
\qquad
\mu\geq0.
\]

Finite positive exponential packets are the atomic case. Their natural
locally smooth closure is the cone of completely monotone functions.

The completed theta forcing on the positive chamber is

\[
\Phi(u)=4M_2(u)-6M_1(u),
\]

where

\[
M_k(u)=e^{u/2}\sum_{n\geq1}x_n(u)^k e^{-x_n(u)},
\qquad
x_n(u)=\pi n^2e^{2u}.
\]

## First-jet obstruction

Modular completion makes `Phi` smooth and even. Hence

\[
\Phi'(0)=0.
\]

For a positive exponential mixture with a finite first jet,

\[
f'(0)=-\int\alpha\,d\mu(\alpha).
\]

Consequently `f'(0)=0` forces the representing measure to be supported at
`alpha=0`, making `f` constant. Since `Phi` is nonconstant, it is not in the
positive exponential cone or its smooth completely-monotone closure.

This already decides the transfer gate.

## Curvature obstruction

The same failure appears in the invariant that generated the recent
orientation reduction. Every positive exponential mixture satisfies

\[
f f''-(f')^2
=
\frac12\iint
(\alpha-\beta)^2e^{-(\alpha+\beta)u}
\,d\mu(\alpha)d\mu(\beta)
\geq0.
\]

Thus positive exponential mixtures are log-convex. At the theta seam,
evenness and strict seam log-concavity give

\[
\Phi(0)\Phi''(0)-\Phi'(0)^2<0.
\]

Using the exact raising law

\[
M_k'=\left(2k+\frac12\right)M_k-2M_{k+1},
\]

the seam jets are

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Phi(0)&=4M_2-6M_1,\\
\Phi'(0)&=30M_2-15M_1-8M_3,\\
\Phi''(0)&=165M_2-112M_3+16M_4-\frac{75}{2}M_1.
\end{aligned}
\]

Direct evaluation has a very large sign margin:

\[
\Phi(0)\approx0.89339,
\qquad
\Phi''(0)\approx-16.7305,
\qquad
\Phi\Phi''-(\Phi')^2\approx-14.9469.
\]

## Meaning of the obstruction

The positive-mode theorem is valid but cannot be transferred to theta by a
cone-membership argument. The failure is not hidden at high mode count. It is
created at the modular seam itself: reciprocal sewing changes the source from
a one-sided completely-monotone decay cone into an even, locally
log-concave object.

The signed coefficient in

\[
\Phi=4M_2-6M_1
\]

is therefore essential source structure, not an inconvenient presentation.
Any successful extension must retain at least two moment grades and their
signed comparison. Replacing them by a single positive scalar mode erases the
operation that creates modular completion.

## Next attack

Lift the rank-two endpoint Gram identity from scalar exponential weights to
the two-grade moment module generated by `M_1` and `M_2`. The target is not
positivity of each scalar coefficient. It is a signed or Krein Gram identity
whose negative grade is paired by the raising incidence

\[
M_k'\longrightarrow M_{k+1}.
\]

The smallest falsifier is the `M_1,M_2,M_3` truncation: if its source-derived
raising boundary leaves an unpaired negative square in the coupled face
current, the positive-mode mechanism does not extend to theta.

## Result

The completed theta source is excluded from the positive exponential cone by
its first seam jet and from its log-convex closure by its second seam jet. The
next viable object is the signed, graded theta moment module, not a larger
positive exponential census.
