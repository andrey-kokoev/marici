# Outer source mates preserve the polynomial-moment observer complex

## Result and relation to the supplied module theorem

Outer coefficient actions and their prescribed Green mates preserve Voevodsky's endpoint/depth POLYNOMIAL-moment observer space. The mate actions commute with its observer differential and give the continuous conjugate-opposite representation. No endpoint-exponential strengthening is needed for these outer actions alone.

Nima's newly supplied `the-endpoint-depth-observer-scale-is-a-continuous-contragredient-module.md` proves this structure on the stronger endpoint-radius space and also controls normalization mates there. This note independently verifies the outer-action gate on the larger moment-only space. It does NOT transfer normalization-mate bounds to that larger space.

The target is the declared analytical coefficient seam carrier with its J-invariant ambient feature envelope. No Green inverse on the forcing space and no action on independently prepared root states is introduced.

## 1. Counts, source control, and a single word

Keep the observer seminorms

O_(s,b,p,q)(y)=sum_shapes (1+n)^p (1+r)^q b_s(k)b^m ||y_shape||,

where n is endpoint event length, r total seam factor count, k active edge count, and m retained feature count. The intersection over the declared integer parameters is O_mom.

For a composable source word w, write l for its event length and d for its number of retained events. Its terminal history is an ordered tensor of actual analytical letters g_e. Put

M(w)=product_(retained e) tau ||g_e||.

Forgotten letters contribute one. Left multiplication concatenates this history into the first coefficient buffer; right multiplication uses the last one. It changes (n,m) to (n+l,m+d), preserving r,k. Incompatible types give zero.

Thus, for either outer action,

O_(s,b,p,q)(L_w y)
 <= (1+l)^p b^d M(w) O_(s,b,p,q)(y).

The endpoint factor follows from 1+n+l<=(1+n)(1+l). There is no new seam separator or root-state copy.

## 2. The mate contracts a specified prefix or suffix

On the admitted positive memory carrier, one-letter creation has norm tau||g||. Its prescribed Green mate is tau^2 times contraction against g through the fixed signature. Because that signature has norm one, the contraction has the SAME positive operator norm, tau||g||, between the corresponding homogeneous weighted carriers.

For a word, contract precisely its d outer feature slots and remove its specified endpoint transport. A short buffer or incompatible type gives zero. The result has endpoint length n-l and feature count m-d; r,k are unchanged.

The control-weight ratios are

(1+n-l)^p/(1+n)^p <= 1,

b^(m-d)/b^m <= 1.

Hence

O_(s,b,p,q)(L_w^sharp y) <= M(w) O_(s,b,p,q)(y).

There is no sum over possible incident edges or unrestricted cuts. The word fixes the transport, side, and number of contracted slots. This explains why outer mates do not require the incidence factors needed by differential mates.

The graph and moment multipliers are not inserted into the Green form when computing this mate. They enter only the subsequent norm comparison.

## 3. Completed source actions

Choose A>=1 bounding tau||g_e|| and define the common exponential source seminorms

a_C(x)=sum_w (C A)^(length(w)) |x_w|.

Since M(w)<=A^l, and (1+l)^p<=2^(p l), summing the word estimates gives

O_(s,b,p,q)(L_x y) <= a_(2^p b)(x) O_(s,b,p,q)(y),

O_(s,b,p,q)(L_x^sharp y) <= a_1(x) O_(s,b,p,q)(y).

These are absolutely convergent operator sums and jointly continuous actions of the all-radius exponential source on O_mom. Factorial control is unnecessary for the outer actions themselves. No infinite sum of vertex identities is adjoined.

There is also the actual-letter source version. Put gamma_e=sqrt(w_seam)||f_e||_beta, Gamma(w)=product gamma_e over retained letters, and let C_feature bound forcing-to-analytical features. With

kappa=max(1,lambda C_feature),

one has M(w)<=kappa^l Gamma(w). The already declared actual-letter factorial seminorms therefore give

O_(s,b,p,q)(L_x y) <= Q_(ceil(2^p b kappa))(x) O_(s,b,p,q)(y),

O_(s,b,p,q)(L_x^sharp y) <= Q_(ceil(kappa))(x) O_(s,b,p,q)(y).

Their factorial factor only strengthens these inequalities. No Gamma-weighted factorization lift is used.

## 4. Contragredient variance and differential

For finite source words the prescribed Green mates satisfy

L_(xy)^sharp=L_y^sharp L_x^sharp,

L_(alpha x)^sharp=conjugate(alpha)L_x^sharp.

They are therefore the conjugate-opposite actions, not ordinary left multiplication and not opposite-history creation. Left and right outer actions supply the corresponding commuting mate actions with their proper variance.

The primal differential is source-linear: d L_x=L_x d. Taking its signed Green transpose gives

delta_O L_x^sharp=L_x^sharp delta_O.

All finite identities extend by density. Indeed the moment differential estimate is

O_(s,b,p,q)(delta_O y)
 <= lambda^2 s O_(s,b,p+1,q+2)(y),

and the mate-action bound works in BOTH of these seminorms with the same source control. Thus every side of the asserted identity is continuous on the declared domain.

Ordinary outer creation also preserves O_mom as a space, but is NOT generally a chain action for delta_O. The checker exhibits this failure in actual vacuum two-seam complexes. The observer chain representation is the mate representation.

The source record ideal acts trivially on the outer terminal-record target and hence on its mates; continuity extends this to its closure in the specified acting source algebra. This does not split the source extension carried by its separate attachment roof.

## 5. Graph-domain and paired compatibility

The estimates without endpoint/depth moments also bound each fixed outer mate on the receiver scale itself. Thus its action preserves the maximal representable-mate graph domain:

(y,z) -> (L_x^sharp y,L_x^sharp z).

To see this, use the defining graph equation beta(z)=d^h beta(y), together with

beta(L_x^sharp y)=L_x^vee beta(y)

and commutation of the primal action with d. No assertion that every receiver vector has a Green mate is needed.

The inclusion y->(y,delta_O y) from O_mom is equivariant for these actions. Restricting the existing transposed attachment square consequently gives a source-contragredient square on O_mom.

For the unweighted factorial source attachment use its compatible acting source subalgebra, in particular S_fact. For the inherited actual-letter attachment use S_Gamma. The target estimates do not authorize an arbitrary S_Gamma vector to act on an unweighted factorial SOURCE complex whose multiplication domain is different.

For each fixed acting source element, transpose is continuous on the strong scalar dual because it maps bounded sets to bounded sets. Joint continuity on the represented observer side follows from the displayed estimates. No unrestricted joint-action or perfect-duality theorem for the whole strong dual is inferred.

## 6. Boundaries

Closed here: polynomial-moment observer stability under outer coefficient actions, continuous conjugate-opposite chain action by their mates, and equivariance of the graph inclusion and the paired attachment square.

Still distinct:

- normalization mates, for which Nima supplies endpoint-radius/feature-radius bounds on a stronger observer space;
- equality with the maximal representable-mate graph domain;
- surjectivity onto the source continuous dual, already obstructed even with actual-letter normalization;
- actions on other prepared root modules without their own norm checks;
- strict strong-dual exactness and completed perfect-module tensor-Hom equivalence.

No signed pairing, physical memory weight, or source connecting map is changed.
