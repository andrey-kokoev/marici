# The Remaining Finite-Rank Extension Is One Weyl-Function Identity

Freeze the reciprocal-tail principal operator

$$
A_0=-iJ\frac{d}{dq},\qquad J=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1),
$$

on the maximal-isotropic Evans boundary line. This makes $A_0$
self-adjoint. Let the completed theta source determine
$V:\mathbb C\to\mathcal H$ and $d\in\mathbb R$. The smallest symmetric
homogenization is

$$
\mathcal A=\begin{pmatrix}A_0&V\\V^*&d\end{pmatrix}.
$$

For $\lambda$ outside the spectrum of $A_0$, its eigenvalue equations give

$$
\Psi=-(A_0-\lambda)^{-1}Vc
$$

and the scalar Schur condition

$$
D(\lambda)c=0,\qquad
D(\lambda)=d-\lambda-V^*(A_0-\lambda)^{-1}V.
$$

The finite-rank symmetric-completion problem therefore reduces to one
Weyl-function identity.

If $V$ is bounded, or relatively bounded in the required graph topology,
$\mathcal A$ is self-adjoint. Its nonzero eigenstates then have real
$\lambda$. Under $z=-i\lambda$ and $s=1/2+z$, this is the critical seam.

This yields RH only if the source independently proves

$$
\Xi(1/2+i\lambda)=u(\lambda)D(\lambda),
\qquad u(\lambda)\ne0,
$$

including both directions of zero-to-eigenstate transport, multiplicities,
boundary modes, and completion. Choosing $V$, $d$, or $u$ after inspecting
Xi would merely manufacture a Hilbert--Pólya operator.

The source must independently derive the principal domain, the coupling
column $V$, its reverse incidence $V^*$, the boundary coefficient $d$ or its
dynamic replacement, and the determinant normalization. Primitive, square,
seam, and archimedean channels may force a larger boundary system; the block
above is the minimal exact falsifier.

Reciprocal transport over unbounded scale has two-sided norm ratio
$\exp(\pm2\operatorname{Re}(z)q)$. Uniform two-sided boundedness holds only
on $\operatorname{Re}z=0$. This matches Strominger's unit-circle failure
boundary, but supplies only a completion gate, not the Weyl identity.

The route fails if the reverse incidence is not the adjoint on the common
domain, if hidden block modes add zeros, if either implication between Xi
zeros and block eigenstates fails, or if the finite identities do not survive
restricted-product completion.

The next calculation is therefore not another positivity search. It is to
derive the completed source coupling and compute whether its reduced
determinant is the framed Xi section up to a source-fixed nowhere-zero unit.
