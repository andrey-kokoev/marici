# RH is already a jointly faithful infinite source-observer problem

## Operator stimulus

The operator proposed that the last RH problem may be solved by infinitely many observers and infinitely many source-derived identities.

## Two infinite observer families

The current construction realizes that proposal in two equivalent forms.

### Heat-jet observers

For every `t>0` and integer `k>=0`, define

\[
\mathcal O_{k,t}(\rho)
=
\left\langle
\rho,u^{2k}e^{-tu^2}
\right\rangle
=
(-1)^k\partial_t^k\Theta(t,0).
\]

Each observer has an explicit source identity obtained by differentiating the completed endpoint--gamma--prime formula. The prime sector is governed by the Laguerre polynomial `L_k^(-1/2)`.

The family is jointly faithful on even tempered spectral distributions: positivity of every observer for all `k,t` gives a positive Bernstein measure for the squared pushforward, and Laplace uniqueness plus evenness recovers positivity of `rho`.

### Translate-Gram observers

Fix one `sigma>0`. For every finite tuple of translates and coefficients, define

\[
\mathcal G_{\sigma,a,c}(\rho)
=
\sum_{i,j}c_i\overline{c_j}
K_\sigma(a_i-a_j).
\]

The verified imaginary-character explicit formula makes every such value source-derived. Positivity of all observers gives one-width positive definiteness; Bochner--Schwartz and division by the Gaussian recover positivity of `rho`.

## Evidence that infinity is essential

The hostile fixtures separate every bounded substitute:

1. all derivative orders at one heat parameter can miss a signed measure;
2. every finite derivative cutoff can pass for all heat parameters while the next order fails;
3. all separated rank-two inequalities can pass while rank four fails;
4. finite prime cutoffs cannot control arbitrarily large translate separation.

Thus the infinite indexing is not rhetorical. Each axis detects defects invisible to every bounded truncation.

## What has and has not been tried

Yes: the repository has constructed both infinite observer families, proved their joint faithfulness, derived their source identities, and falsified finite replacements.

No: it has not proved simultaneous positivity of the full family. Checking observers individually cannot finish an infinite theorem. The missing object is a uniform source mechanism that maps the completed arithmetic input into the positive cone before evaluation by every observer. Equivalent forms are a positive spectral measure, an all-order sign-preserving recurrence, or a Gram factorization valid at every finite rank.

## Bold conjecture

The appropriate final theorem may be a jointly faithful descent statement: one source-derived transformation intertwines the endpoint--gamma--prime calculus with the positive cone, and the infinitely many observers merely detect that single positive object.

The conjecture is falsified if the identities require observer-dependent regularizations, incompatible cutoffs, or positivity repairs that do not arise from one common source object.

## Disposition

Adopt the operator's infinite-observer framing as the correct architecture, not as evidence of RH. Stop adding isolated observers. Search for the common positivity-preserving source transformation whose evaluations reproduce both established infinite families with one normalization and one cutoff law.
