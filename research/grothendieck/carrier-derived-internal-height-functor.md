# Carrier-derived internal height functor

## Question

Does the conditional pointed Carrier supply any canonical positive multiplicative height before an adelic or real coefficient lens is chosen?

## Exact construction

Let `M` be the free commutative component monoid on the connected generator `U`, equipped with the derived endomorphism multiplication. Connected-component length

`nu(a) = number of U-components in a`

is source-defined before identifying `M` with a familiar numeral set. It obeys

`nu(a+b)=nu(a)+nu(b)`,

`nu(ab)=nu(a)nu(b)`,

`nu(0)=0`, and `nu(U)=1`.

Equivalently, initiality makes `M` its own canonical initial positive semiring, and `nu` is its unique unit-preserving semiring coordinate. The positive-cone order

`a <= b iff there exists c with a+c=b`

is also intrinsic. Therefore a positive ordered multiplicative height exists internally at `pi_0`. The previous audit was too strong in saying that no Carrier-derived norm/height is available.

## What this repairs

Intrinsic prime powers now carry a canonical internal height class: the class `p^k` itself, or its component length `nu(p^k)`. This eliminates arbitrary independent prime assignments at the purely initial-semiring level. A semiring realization into any chosen characteristic-zero ordered coefficient object must send these classes to its canonical numerals.

## Remaining analytic gate

The spline needs not merely internal height but

`log(nu(p^k))`

and the half-density `nu(p^k)^(-1/2)`. The ordered discrete semiring has no subtraction-complete Archimedean field, topology, exponential, logarithm, square root, Haar measure, or Mellin character. Initiality gives a unique semiring map into each chosen target, but it does not choose the target or prove that one analytic completion is forced by Carrier geometry.

A hostile comparison can keep the same internal height while realizing it in distinct ordered topological semirings or with inequivalent norms. The internal arithmetic identities remain fixed, while analytic convergence, Gaussian scale, and Mellin weights differ.

## Disposition

The Carrier conditionally derives an internal positive multiplicative height on `pi_0`; arbitrary prime-by-prime scales are disallowed once realization must preserve that semiring coordinate. The first missing arrow is narrower: a canonical Archimedean analytic completion of the initial ordered ring, with source-authorized logarithm and half-power. This does not yet produce adelic places, Frobenius, or the completed zeta function.
