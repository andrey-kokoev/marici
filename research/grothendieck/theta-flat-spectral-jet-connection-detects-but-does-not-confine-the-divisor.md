# Theta flat spectral-jet connection detects but does not confine the divisor

## Bounded question

Does the causal-prefix pivot acquire RH force when its scale transport is
extended by the canonical spectral derivative?

## Two-parameter connection

The preceding packet constructed

\[
Y(q,z)=e^{-zq}F(z)
\]

and proved

\[
(\partial_q+z)Y=0.
\]

The source-independent spectral covariant derivative compatible with this
gauge is

\[
\nabla_z=\partial_z+q.
\]

Together with

\[
\nabla_q=\partial_q+z,
\]

it is flat:

\[
[\nabla_q,\nabla_z]
=
\partial_q q-\partial_z z
=0.
\]

Moreover,

\[
\nabla_z^kY(q,z)=e^{-zq}F^{(k)}(z).
\]

Thus the coupled scale--spectral geometry is exact and needs no division by
the transform.

## Source jet realization

Let

\[
G_k=\partial_z^kG.
\]

Differentiating the source-tail equation gives the triangular jet ladder

\[
\partial_qG_0=-zG_0-fc,
\]

and, for every (k\ge1),

\[
\partial_qG_k=-zG_k-kG_{k-1}.
\]

This is a source-derived prolongation of the bordered tail system. The same
construction applies to the causal prefix, and their sum yields the covariant
jets above.

## What the flat connection knows

At a zero (z_0) of multiplicity (m),

\[
\nabla_z^kY(q,z_0)=0
\]

for (k<m), while

\[
\nabla_z^mY(q,z_0)=e^{-z_0q}F^{(m)}(z_0)\ne0.
\]

The flat jet connection therefore detects the divisor and its multiplicity.
It does not constrain the horizontal location of that divisor. The same
ladder exists for every sufficiently regular source transform, including a
hostile source with off-seam zeros.

## The parallelization trap

One could instead demand a rank-one spectral connection

\[
D_z=\partial_z+a(q,z)
\]

for which

\[
D_zY=0.
\]

Where (F\ne0), this forces

\[
a(q,z)=q-\frac{F'(z)}{F(z)}.
\]

If (F) has a zero of multiplicity (m) at (z_0), then (F'/F) has
residue (m) there. Hence (a) cannot extend holomorphically across that
zero.

Consequently, existence of a holomorphic parallelizing line connection on an
open spectral sector is equivalent to zero-freeness of (F) there. Producing
that connection from (F'/F) would merely rename the desired theorem.

## Result

The theta tail admits a canonical flat two-parameter connection and a fully
source-derived spectral jet ladder. This geometry measures divisor
multiplicity but supplies no zero confinement. A holomorphic rank-one
connection that makes the pivot spectrally parallel exists exactly when the
sector is already zero-free.

The next admissible object must therefore be richer than the scalar pivot
line. It must derive a spectral conservation law or comparison on a larger
labelled source module whose regularity is established before compression to
(F).

## Sharp falsifier

Choose any regular source transform with an off-seam zero. Its pivot still
satisfies

\[
[\nabla_q,\nabla_z]=0
\]

and possesses the complete triangular jet ladder. Therefore flatness and jet
compatibility alone have no RH-selective content.
