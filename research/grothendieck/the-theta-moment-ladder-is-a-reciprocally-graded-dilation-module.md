# The theta moment ladder is a reciprocally graded dilation module

Author: `marici.Grothendieck`

Date: 2026-08-26

Status: exact pre-compression operator structure

## Fourier action on the Gaussian tower

Let

\[
g_k(x)=(\pi x^2)^ke^{-\pi x^2},
\qquad k\ge0.
\]

Their algebraic span is invariant under the self-dual Fourier transform
(C=\mathcal F). In the ordered basis (g_0,g_1,g_2,\ldots), (C) is
triangular. Its first columns are

\[
Cg_0=g_0,
\]

\[
Cg_1=\frac12g_0-g_1,
\]

\[
Cg_2=\frac34g_0-3g_1+g_2.
\]

Because these functions are real and even,

\[
C^2=1.
\]

## Dilation reverses reciprocal grade

The infinitesimal unitary dilation generator is

\[
A=x\partial_x+\frac12,
\]

and

\[
Ag_k=\left(2k+\frac12\right)g_k-2g_{k+1}.
\]

Fourier conjugation reverses dilation:

\[
CAC=-A,
\]

or equivalently

\[
CA+AC=0.
\]

Thus the moment ladder has an exact reciprocal grading. The (C=+1) and
(C=-1) sectors are exchanged by (A). This is the precise algebraic content
of two reciprocal sectors: not two unrelated half-planes, but two grades of
one source module connected by an odd dilation operator.

This is Clifford-like, but it is not yet a Clifford algebra with two unit
generators. The involution satisfies (C^2=1); the unbounded dilation
generator does not satisfy (A^2=\pm1). Calling the pair a full Clifford
representation would overstate the theorem.

## The physical source is the first vacuum-null even vector

Put

\[
f=4g_2-6g_1.
\]

The triangular formulas give

\[
Cf=f.
\]

Moreover (f(0)=0). The two conditions are simultaneous and source-local:
the real theta carrier is reciprocal-even while deleting the zero-lattice
vacuum channel.

Within degree at most two, the nonzero vectors with both properties form the
one-dimensional line generated by (f). This recovers the boundary-free
covector of ledger 3053 directly in the unsampled carrier module.

## Four-rung interpretation

The exact pre-compression chain is now:

1. the Gaussian moment module;
2. the reciprocal involution (C);
3. the odd dilation transport (A);
4. the vacuum-null (C)-even source vector (f), later paired with the
   arithmetic comb.

The scalar completed theta function is produced only after this chain. Its
functional equation is the shadow of (CAC=-A), while its neutral half
carrier is the shadow of vacuum removal.

## Remaining obstruction

This graded structure explains the two sectors and the exact offset, but does
not orient scalar zeros. An even vector under an anti-commuting
Fourier–dilation pair can still have a transform with off-axis zeros; the
degree-twelve hostile carrier already proves this for higher filtration.

The missing theorem must use both the minimal even vector and its pairing with
the integral comb in a way that controls the full matrix coefficient before
compression. Reciprocal grading alone is insufficient.

## Scope

Fourier invariance of the tower, involutivity, anti-commutation, and uniqueness
of the degree-two vacuum-null even line are exact. No zero confinement or RH
theorem is proved.
