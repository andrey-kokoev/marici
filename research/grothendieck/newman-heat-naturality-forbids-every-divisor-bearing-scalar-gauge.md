# Newman Heat Naturality Forbids Every Divisor-Bearing Scalar Gauge

## Source-natural presentation changes

Let

\[
L=\partial_z^2
\]

be the constant-coefficient generator of the Newman heat transport, up to the
source convention for its overall sign. Suppose multiplication by an entire
function \(H\) is claimed to be a presentation change of the complete source
family rather than a new source.

Such a change must be natural with respect to heat evolution. Infinitesimally,
it must satisfy

\[
[L,M_H]=0
\]

on the admitted test-function core.

The product rule gives the exact commutator

\[
[\partial_z^2,M_H]psi
=
H''\psi+2H'\psi'.
\]

For this operator to vanish on the source test class, both coefficients must
vanish. Hence

\[
H'=0,
\]

so \(H\) is constant.

After fixing one source normalization, the constant is one. Therefore the
heat-natural scalar gauge group contains no divisor-bearing multiplier.

## Minimal two-probe proof

The operator conclusion already follows on a local polynomial core. Applying
the commutator to \(1\) gives \(H''=0\). Applying it to \(z\) then gives
\(2H'=0\). No zero data or positivity statement enters.

The argument is equally valid for the opposite heat-sign convention because
the commutator changes only by an overall sign.

## Hostile multiplier rejection

For

\[
H_N(z)=1+(z/R)^{2N},
\]

one has \(H_N'\ne0\). Therefore multiplication by \(H_N\) does not commute
with the Newman generator. The multiplier may preserve reciprocal symmetry,
reality, finite central jets, order, and much of the scalar asymptotic data,
but it is not a natural automorphism of the heat-bearing theta source.

This rejects the hostile divisor before inspecting the location of its zeros.

## Canonical-section rigidity obtained

The completed source should be typed as a heat-evolving object, not as one
isolated entire function. Within that category, the distinguished scalar
section is rigid up to a constant unit under scalar multiplication.

This closes one necessary gate of the puncture programme:

```text
source-equivalent scalar presentation changes cannot insert a divisor.
```

It also explains why finite-jet and static functional-equation tests were too
weak: they forgot the source's time-direction constructor.

## Scope

This does not prove RH. It prevents manufacturing off-seam zeros by a
heat-natural scalar gauge. The actual theta section could still possess
off-seam zeros produced by its own source evolution.

Naturality on the whole admitted source test class is essential. If one asks
only that \(HF_t\) accidentally satisfy the heat equation along one
distinguished orbit, additional solutions may exist and must be audited
separately.

The next RH-bearing gate remains a conservation or incidence theorem for the
actual source conormal under heat transport.
