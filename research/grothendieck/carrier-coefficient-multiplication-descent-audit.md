# Carrier coefficient multiplication and spline-label descent audit

## Question

Does the pre-arithmetic Carrier already construct enough multiplication and irreducibility to generate the completed-zeta spline labels without importing primes?

## Corrected claim boundary

Under the explicit relaxation that disjoint union is the symmetric-monoidal addition on the subgroupoid freely generated by one connected source object `U`, the component monoid

`M = pi_0(Surf_U^disjoint-union)`

is free commutative on `U`. Evaluation at `U` identifies additive endomorphisms of `M` with `M`; composition therefore defines a canonical commutative multiplication. This proves that `M` is the initial commutative semiring and that its additive group completion is the initial commutative ring. Intrinsic divisibility, irreducibles, primality, and unique factorization then follow from component length and Euclidean division.

Thus the preceding audit's statement that no Carrier-derived multiplication exists was too strong. A conditional decategorified construction does exist, and its universal properties precede identification with familiar natural numbers and integers.

## Exact remaining failures

The multiplication does not lift to the full Carrier groupoid: the source generator has a noncommuting `D4` automorphism subgroup, whereas a second monoidal product with `U` as unit would force unit endomorphisms to commute. Power, Burnside, and Witt operations therefore do not follow from the `pi_0` semiring.

Initiality also forces every unital semiring endomorphism of `M` to be the identity. The additive maps used to define multiplication are not nontrivial Frobenius endomorphisms. Hence the same identity occurs at every intrinsic prime and carries no place-dependent spectral information.

Most importantly for the spline, no source-derived comparison maps an intrinsic prime class and exponent to

`(log(p^m), log(p)/sqrt(p^m))`.

The completed-zeta convention packet obtains these values from the already assumed Euler logarithmic derivative and an analytic real/complex coefficient lens. The canonical initial-ring identification supplies arithmetic classes, but it does not supply logarithm, the critical normalization, prime-indexed local cohomology, or a Frobenius spectrum.

## Hostile test

Retain the initial semiring and its intrinsic prime classes, but choose two distinct positive analytic embeddings or grade assignments on those classes. Multiplication and unique factorization remain unchanged while the spline grades and weights change. Therefore semiring emergence alone cannot select the completed-zeta weighted functional.

## Disposition

Carrier-derived multiplication and intrinsic primes survive conditionally at `pi_0`; the full-Carrier tensor and Frobenius claims fail. The first spline-specific missing arrow is now retyped as an analytic realization

`T_an : (intrinsic prime, exponent) -> (grade, weight)`

whose required output is `(log(p^m), log(p)/sqrt(p^m))`. Acceptance requires deriving this realization and its normalization from admitted Carrier/coefficient transport rather than the displayed Euler factor.
