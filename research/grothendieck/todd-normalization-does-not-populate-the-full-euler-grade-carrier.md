# Todd normalization does not populate the full Euler grade carrier

## Correction to the power-law interpretation

The conjugated additive generator has a slot at every grade:

\[
A^k
=
e^{-kq}
\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}
\left(D_q-\frac12-j\right).
\]

That is an ambient carrier theorem. It does not say that a chosen functional
calculus has a nonzero coefficient in every slot.

For the Todd germ

\[
\tau(t)=\frac{t}{e^t-1},
\]

the coefficient support is:

- degree one;
- every positive even degree;
- no odd degree above one.

After half-density centering, the linear term also vanishes and only even
degrees remain.

By contrast, the Euler logarithm

\[
-\log(1-r)=\sum_{k\geq1}\frac{r^k}{k}
\]

has a nonzero coefficient at every positive grade. It is the arithmetic
source of the full prime-power tower.

## Reciprocal reflection

Reflection \(R f(q)=f(-q)\) preserves the grade number:

\[
RA^kR
=
(-1)^k e^{kq}
\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}
\left(D_q+\frac12+j\right).
\]

Thus reciprocal sewing does not mix a missing odd Todd grade out of lower
ones. If a coefficient vanishes before reflection, it remains absent from
the Todd sector. Scalar completion cannot repair this loss of provenance.

## The next pullback level

The source object must retain two distinct multiplicative functions on the
same grade carrier:

- the Todd normalization, controlling lattice-to-continuum boundary
  comparison;
- the Euler logarithm, controlling connected prime-power occupation.

They meet through the exponential-coordinate operator cell, but neither is
the other. The next theorem must construct a typed comparison or distributive
law between them. It must explain how the Todd boundary current acts on every
Euler grade while preserving the distinction between primitive, square, and
connected contributions.

## Falsifier

A proposed comparison fails if it:

- derives odd prime-power coefficients from the centered Todd series;
- cancels missing grades only after scalar summation;
- forgets the falling-factorial spectral operator attached to each grade;
- identifies the Euler logarithm with the logarithm of the Todd germ.

The last functions have different coefficient support and encode different
source operations.

## Result

The exponential pullback supplies a common grade carrier, not a common
coefficient law. Todd normalization occupies the linear and even slots;
centered Todd occupies only even slots; the Euler logarithm occupies every
positive slot. The next categorical level is their typed comparison on the
shared two-axis operator carrier.
