# Reflection Averaging Destroys Primitive Theta Positivity

## Canonical group average

For an analytic function \(f\), the Reynolds projection onto the reflection
invariants is

\[
\mathsf E f(u)=\frac{f(u)+f(-u)}2.
\]

It closes the entire odd seam-jet tower in one operation. Apply it to

\[
\phi_1(u)
=
2\pi e^{5u/2}
\left(2\pi e^{2u}-3\right)
e^{-\pi e^{2u}}.
\]

## Reflected sign transition

For \(u\geq0\), the direct primitive is positive. Its reflected profile has
the factor

\[
2\pi e^{-2u}-3.
\]

It changes sign at

\[
u_*=\frac12\log\frac{2\pi}{3}.
\]

Thus \(\phi_1(-u)<0\) for \(u>u_*\).

More strongly, as \(u\) tends to infinity,

\[
\phi_1(-u)
\sim
-6\pi e^{-5u/2},
\]

whereas

\[
\phi_1(u)
=
O\!\left(
e^{9u/2-\pi e^{2u}}
\right).
\]

The direct term is superexponentially smaller than the negative reflected
term. Therefore

\[
\mathsf E\phi_1(u)<0
\]

for every sufficiently large \(u\).

## Structural consequence

The two desired properties do not coexist inside the primitive orbit under
ordinary reflection averaging:

- Reynolds projection supplies exact reciprocal symmetry;
- it destroys positive-chamber positivity.

Hence the completed positive even theta source is not the symmetrization of
one primitive label. It requires new labelled source contributions whose
collective transport repairs the negative reflected chamber.

This distinguishes two operations that had looked interchangeable:
reflection projection is not modular completion.

The former erases the anti-invariant germ by averaging. The latter enlarges the
source packet and obtains reflection coherence while retaining positivity.
