# Riemann zeros cannot arise from absolutely convergent nonzero local determinants

For every prime and every `s` with `Re(s)>0`,

```
|p^(-s)|<1,
1-p^(-s) != 0.                                        (1)
```

The gamma function also has no zeros. Hence every finite product of Euler
and archimedean local determinant factors is nonzero in the critical strip.

More generally, if holomorphic local factors `f_p(s)` are nonzero on a
domain and

```
sum_p |f_p(s)-1|                                      (2)
```

converges locally uniformly, then the infinite product `product_p f_p(s)`
is holomorphic and nonzero there. Therefore a locally absolutely convergent
Euler determinant architecture cannot create Riemann zeros in
`0<Re(s)<1`.

## Source of the zeros

The zeros must enter through the failure of local absolute convergence and
the global operation used to repair it:

1. analytic continuation;
2. a relative/regularized determinant anomaly;
3. a global boundary condition coupling infinitely many primes; or
4. a nonlocal source--archimedean correspondence.

This explains why the third regularized determinant is nonvanishing: it
restores sufficient local summability by removing exactly the channels that
carry the continuation anomaly.

## Consequence for the explanation program

Euler-factorized Hilbert metrics remain valuable for domains, norms, and
locality. But the spectral equation itself must be a global gluing condition.
No theorem that proves positivity independently prime by prime can also
explain nonzero Xi zeros unless it includes a nonlocal compatibility or
boundary term.

This is the emerging division of labor:

```
local Euler geometry       -> control, adjoints, coefficient norms;
global regularization      -> zero-producing interference;
archimedean correspondence -> confinement and boundary phase.           (3)
```

## Falsifier

A proposed determinant model fails if it claims an absolutely convergent
product of nonvanishing local prime determinants equals `xi(s)` throughout
the critical strip. Such a product cannot have the required zeros.
