# Mixed-prime rectangle diagonals are correlations, not route holonomy

## Correction

For vertices indexed by `1,p,q,pq`, a real stationary normalized correlation `k` gives

`r=k(log p)`,

`s=k(log q)`,

`c=k(log(pq))`,

`d=k(log(q/p))`.

The entry `c` pairs the opposite vertices `1` and `pq`; the entry `d` pairs the opposite vertices `p` and `q`. They are not two transport maps from `1` to `pq`. Therefore

`h=(c-d)/2`

is a diagonal parity contrast, not route holonomy. Likewise,

`delta=(c+d)/2-rs`

is an average correlation defect, not failure of vector-level prime composition.

The earlier terminology conflated exact composition of translation maps with multiplicativity of matrix coefficients. That conflation is withdrawn.

## Exact rectangle theorem retained

The Gram matrix remains

`G=[[1,r,s,c],[r,1,d,s],[s,d,1,r],[c,s,r,1]]`.

Parity decomposition is exact:

`G_+=[[1+c,r+s],[r+s,1+d]]`,

`G_-=[[1-c,r-s],[r-s,1-d]]`.

Hence positivity is equivalent to positivity of both parity blocks. In terms of `delta` and `h`, the determinant formulas remain

`D_+=(1-r^2)(1-s^2)+2delta(1+rs)+delta^2-h^2`,

`D_-=(1-r^2)(1-s^2)-2delta(1-rs)+delta^2-h^2`.

Only the interpretation changes.

## Consequence for the forest--cycle conjecture

A graph cycle coordinate may organize the parity contrast, but no source transport law forces `c=d` or `(c+d)/2=rs`. Any forest--cycle factorization must reproduce both independent diagonal correlations from the complete Weil kernel. It cannot derive them from edge composition alone.

The smallest complete test is therefore a four-point positive-definite-function test on the multiplicative subgroup generated by two and three:

`k(log2)`, `k(log3)`, `k(log6)`, `k(log(3/2))`.

This test is intrinsic and does not require naming a route holonomy or a G4 cycle energy.

## New source target

A non-tautological explanation would derive the restriction of the completed Weil distribution to the two-generator multiplicative subgroup as a positive-definite function by an independently positive representation or measure. Bochner/Kolmogorov representation cannot be assumed: its existence is exactly the finite positivity being tested.

The cheapest numerical scout is to evaluate these four normalized correlations for an authorized one-parameter Gaussian or compact bump family and search both parity determinants. A negative determinant would be an explicit negative Weil square; a pass is only finite evidence.

## Disposition

The algebraic parity theorem survives. The route-holonomy interpretation is rejected. The research frontier is now correctly stated as positivity of the stationary Weil correlation on the `2,3` multiplicative rectangle.
