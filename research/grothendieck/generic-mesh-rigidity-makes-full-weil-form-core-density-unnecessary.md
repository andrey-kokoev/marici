# Generic-mesh rigidity makes full Weil form-core density unnecessary

## Question

Must the sampled Gaussian-square cone be promoted to the full even-and-odd Weil test domain before it can imply RH?

## Two available arrows

Small-mesh scaling proves that the sampled amplitudes are dense in the odd real-line Schwartz sector. Extending positivity through that closure would require continuity of the completed Weil form in an analytic graph topology.

Independently, generic-mesh pole rigidity acts directly on the sampled moment sequences. It uses:

1. all-rank remainder Hankel positivity;
2. endpoint asymptotics and compact moment determinacy;
3. continuity from rational to real meshes;
4. a generic mesh separating the zero-side bases;
5. comparison of the positive-measure transform with the general-zero meromorphic expansion.

That chain forces every sampled base `e^(-h lambda_rho)` to be real and hence every zero onto the critical line.

## Consequence

A full Weil form-core theorem is not needed for the implication

\[
\text{sampled remainder cones}\Longrightarrow\mathrm{RH}.
\]

The sampled family detects off-line zeros by its mesh-dependent pole structure, not by density in the entire classical test space. Oddness of the small-mesh amplitude limit is therefore not an unresolved parity loophole for this implication.

## What density still supplies

Odd-Hermite density remains useful for classifying candidate mechanisms:

- the source image is infinite-dimensional;
- no fixed finite recurrence or finite transfer operator can represent it;
- pointwise positive-density factors are incompatible with the signed prime atoms;
- any closure-based realization needs an analytic topology stronger than real Schwartz topology.

These statements constrain representations but do not advance the positivity proof itself.

## Remaining analytic gate

The direct RH implication still depends on a rigorous common continuation domain for the compact positive-measure transform and the locally uniform grouped zero expansion. This is narrower than proving graph-norm density in the full Weil domain.

## Disposition

Stop extending Gaussian observer density as a route to positivity. Use generic-mesh pole rigidity for logical sufficiency, and concentrate proof effort on the endpoint--gamma--prime remainder cone and its exact zero-side continuation.
