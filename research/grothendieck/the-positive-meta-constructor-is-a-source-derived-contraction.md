# The positive meta-constructor is a source-derived contraction

## Question

Can the order-enriched meta-observer architecture be reduced to one operator whose conformance and norm are checked by the infinite lower identities?

## Signed source factorization

On the fixed-width Gaussian probe span, split the completed source quadratic form into positive and negative feature channels:

\[
Q(f)=\|Af\|_{\mathcal H_+}^2-\|Bf\|_{\mathcal H_-}^2.
\]

Here `A` and `B` must be built from one common endpoint--gamma--prime explicit formula, after a declared Jordan decomposition of its source measures or finite endpoint channels. This is a Krein-space presentation, not a positivity claim.

Then

\[
Q(f)\ge0\quad\text{for every }f
\]

is equivalent to

\[
B^*B\le A^*A.
\]

## Conformance identities define the candidate map

A map initially defined by

\[
C(Af)=Bf
\]

is well-defined exactly when

\[
Af=0\Longrightarrow Bf=0.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\ker A\subseteq\ker B.
\]

This is where infinitely many conformance identities belong: every relation among positive-channel source features must be observed as the same relation among negative-channel features. They test that `C` is one map rather than an observer-dependent repair.

## Order enrichment is the contraction bound

Once `C` is well-defined on `ran A`, positivity is equivalent to

\[
\|C(Af)\|=\|Bf\|\le\|Af\|.
\]

Thus `C` extends to a contraction

\[
C:\overline{\operatorname{ran}A}\longrightarrow H_-.
\]

Conversely, any such contraction gives

\[
Q(f)=\|Af\|^2-\|CAf\|^2
=\left\|(1-C^*C)^{1/2}Af\right\|^2.
\]

This is the desired source Gram factorization. It is the Douglas factorization principle applied to the completed source channels.

## Meta-observer interpretation

The two tasks are now cleanly separated:

1. **Conformance observers** verify `ker A subset ker B` over the infinite identity family.
2. **Order observers** verify the single uniform bound `||Bf||<=||Af||` over the jointly faithful probe family.

Finite Gram certificates are restrictions of the same defect operator

\[
D=(1-C^*C)^{1/2}A.
\]

Their comparison isometries are automatic because `D` is global. This removes the arbitrary-unitary defect of independent Cholesky factors.

## Source-specific obligation

The construction is noncircular only if `A`, `B`, and `C` are derived before assuming positivity. Choosing the positive and negative spectral zero atoms would assume RH geometry. The admissible input is the completed endpoint--gamma--prime formula with common cutoffs.

The lower pyramids should therefore seek identities defining `C` on their generating features. A proposed formula is rejected if it changes with heat order, translate packet, cutoff, or decomposition chamber.

## Strongest falsification attempt

Linear conformance alone proves only well-definedness of `C`; it does not prove contraction. The signed coherent fixtures have `ker A subset ker B` but operator norm greater than one. Conversely, packetwise norm bounds without kernel compatibility define inconsistent local contractions and do not assemble.

Both clauses are necessary.

## Disposition

The final positive meta-constructor can be sought as one source-derived contraction from the positive completed channel to the negative channel. Infinite identities establish its well-definedness; infinite positivity observers establish its norm at most one. Producing an explicit contraction formula from the prime/completion calculus would simultaneously generate every coherent Gram certificate.
