# The Full Theta Trajectory Is Faithful While One Mellin Port Can Be Dark

## Finite labelled packet

Let (1\le n_1<\cdots<n_N\) be distinct integer labels and let

\[
F_c(x)=\sum_{j=1}^{N}c_j e^{-\pi n_j^2x},
\qquad
x>0.
\]

Suppose (F_c(x)=0\) for every (x>0\). Multiplying by
\(e^{\pi n_1^2x}\) and sending (x\to\infty\) gives

\[
c_1=0.
\]

Repeating after removing the first term gives

\[
c_1=\cdots=c_N=0.
\]

Therefore the complete Gaussian observation map

\[
c\longmapsto F_c|_{(0,\infty)}
\]

is injective on every finite labelled integer packet.

## Stronger kernel statement

The kernel

\[
K(x,\lambda)=e^{-x\lambda}
\]

is strictly sign-regular when both variables use the same order, and becomes
strictly totally positive after reversing the order of one variable.
Consequently suitable ordered samples (x_1<\cdots<x_N\) already give an
invertible observation matrix

\[
\left(e^{-\pi n_j^2x_i}\right)_{i,j=1}^{N}.
\]

The continuum trajectory is more than sufficient; finite source-authorized
ports can be jointly faithful at each finite label cutoff.

## Infinite extension

For coefficient classes defining a finite signed measure

\[
\mu=\sum_nc_n\delta_{\pi n^2},
\]

the trajectory is its Laplace transform. Standard uniqueness of Laplace
transforms makes the full observation faithful there as well. Larger
distributional completions require their own topology and cannot inherit this
claim without proof.

## Correction to the integrality-loss picture

Theta's dependence on the collective coordinate (Q=\log n\) does not erase
integer labels when the whole (x\)-family is retained. Distinct values of
(Q\) generate distinct exponential trajectories. The continuous null
directions of a single rank-one differential do not become null directions of
the nonlinear trajectory.

Thus a scalar completed zero should not be described as loss of the entire
source's meaning. The source remains visible through complementary (x\)-ports.

## Mellin selected output

The completed zeta section is obtained by applying a Mellin-type functional to
the theta trajectory, with reciprocal sewing and boundary subtraction. A zero
means that this distinguished selected output is dark:

\[
\mathcal M_s(F_c)=0.
\]

It does not imply

\[
F_c(x)=0
\]

for every (x\). This is exactly the transmission-zero type isolated by the
Rosenbrock and Aspect classifiers: selected port dark, complementary packet
bright.

## Revised RH question

The complete source is already observable. The missing theorem is narrower:

Why can the source-derived Mellin selected port become dark only when the
direct and reciprocal Hilbert realizations simultaneously fail?

Any explanation based solely on source reconstruction, integrality, or joint
faithfulness cannot answer that question, because those properties survive at
the zero.
