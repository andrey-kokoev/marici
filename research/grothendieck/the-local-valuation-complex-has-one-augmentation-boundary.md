# The Local Valuation Complex Has One Augmentation Boundary

## Untwisted discrete differential

Let

\[
V_p=c_{00}(\mathbb N_0)
\]

with valuation basis (e_k\), and let (S_pe_k=e_{k+1}\). Define

\[
\Delta_p=I-S_p.
\]

The augmentation

\[
\varepsilon\left(\sum_kc_ke_k\right)=\sum_kc_k
\]

satisfies \(\varepsilon\Delta_p=0\). Conversely, if (b\in V_p\) has zero
augmentation, its cumulative sum

\[
a_k=\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_j
\]

has finite support and obeys \(\Delta_pa=b\). Also \(\Delta_p\) is injective
on finite-support sequences. Hence

\[
0\longrightarrow V_p
\xrightarrow{\Delta_p}V_p
\xrightarrow{\varepsilon}\mathbb C
\longrightarrow0
\]

is exact.

The finite-place analogue of the orientation-bearing derivative therefore has
one canonical boundary coordinate: augmentation.

## Character-twisted contraction

Put (a=p^{-s}\) and define

\[
d_{p,s}=I-aS_p.
\]

The local geometric vacuum

\[
v_{p,s}=\sum_{k\ge0}a^ke_k
\]

satisfies the exact endpoint equation

\[
d_{p,s}v_{p,s}=e_0.
\]

Whenever \(|a|<1\), the contraction is the convergent shift resolvent

\[
d_{p,s}^{-1}=\sum_{k\ge0}a^kS_p^k.
\]

For the direct local sector, \(|p^{-s}|<1\) when \(\Re s>0\). For the
reciprocal sector with parameter (p^{s-1}\), the same statement holds when
\(\Re s<1\). Thus both local contractions exist throughout the open critical
strip.

## Consequences

No individual finite prime can create a zero-state inside the critical strip.
Each local state is paired with its endpoint through an explicit bounded
resolvent. The first nontrivial object is the infinite restricted product of
these contractions.

The primitive boundary current now has a concrete algebraic origin: it is the
augmentation coordinate of the untwisted valuation complex. It is not a term
that may be deleted after regularizing the connected tail.

Differentiation in (s\) inserts the valuation label:

\[
\partial_s d_{p,s}
=(\log p)p^{-s}S_p.
\]

This is the local first-jet source of the primitive logarithmic current. Higher
expansion orders produce the square and connected prime-power grades already
seen in the Euler filtration.

## Global frontier

The archimedean even--odd complex is strictly exact, and every character-
twisted local valuation complex is contractible in the critical strip. The RH
problem has therefore moved entirely to whether the infinite tensor or
restricted-product contraction survives with its augmentation coordinates,
prime-square currents, and archimedean endpoint retained.

This still does not provide the zero-to-cohomology bridge. It does show that
any such cohomology would be irreducibly global: it cannot be supported at one
place.
