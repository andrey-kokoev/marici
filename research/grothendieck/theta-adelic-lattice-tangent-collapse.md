# The adelic lattice has zero tangent: ordinary linearization erases arithmetic

## 1. The tempting bridge

The rational phase lattice

\[
  \Lambda_{\mathbb Q}=\mathbb Q^2\subset\mathbb A^2
\]

is self-annihilating for the adelic Heisenberg bicharacter. It is tempting to
differentiate this maximal isotropic subgroup and declare its tangent to be a
maximal Lagrangian boundary subspace for the reciprocal Dirac operator.

That bridge fails.

## 2. Exact obstruction

The diagonal copy of \(\mathbb Q\) is discrete in \(\mathbb A\). For example,
the adelic neighbourhood

\[
  (-\tfrac12,\tfrac12)\times\prod_p\mathbb Z_p
\]

meets \(\mathbb Q\) only in \(0\): membership in every \(\mathbb Z_p\) forces
a rational number to be an integer, and the real interval then forces that
integer to vanish. Consequently \(\mathbb Q^2\) is discrete in
\(\mathbb A^2\).

Every ordinary tangent cone of \(\Lambda_{\mathbb Q}\) at the identity is
therefore zero:

\[
  T_0\Lambda_{\mathbb Q}=0.
\]

But the tangent of the ambient real direction is nonzero. Hence
differentiation sends a group-level maximal isotropic object to a
non-maximal, indeed trivial, linear subspace.

\[
\boxed{
\text{Pontryagin maximal isotropy}
\not\Longrightarrow
\text{Hilbert Lagrangian maximality by differentiation}.}
\]

This is not a technical incompleteness. It is a categorical mismatch:
finite-place information is locally totally disconnected and is destroyed
by an ordinary real tangent functor.

## 3. Consequence for the RH programme

The proposed chain

\[
  \Lambda_{\mathbb Q}
  \longrightarrow
  T_0\Lambda_{\mathbb Q}
  \longrightarrow
  L_{\mathbb Q}
\]

cannot construct the RH-bearing boundary relation. In particular, the
phase-lattice linearization conjecture is false if “linearization” means
ordinary tangent or Lie-algebra differentiation.

The correct bridge must keep the exponentiated commutation law:

\[
  W(v)W(w)
  =\omega_{\mathbb A}(v,w)\,W(w)W(v).
\]

The rational lattice is then visible as a maximal commuting family of Weyl
operators, even though it has zero tangent. One must first represent this
family on a Hilbert or rigged Hilbert space and only then extract an operator
boundary object from its joint invariant, fixed, or spectral data.

## 4. Revised architecture

The surviving order is

\[
\boxed{
\text{adelic phase lattice}
\to
\text{Weyl representation}
\to
\text{maximal commuting unitary boundary algebra}
\to
\text{Cayley/graph relation}
\to
\text{Dirac extension}.}
\]

This replaces infinitesimal linearization by representation-theoretic
linearization. It also explains why the two half-planes can be shadows of two
sectors: the primary distinction is between complementary Weyl
polarizations; deficiency half-planes arise only after a generator and a
Cayley chart have been chosen.

## 5. New hard gate

Construct, from the completed theta source rather than from \(\xi\), a
regular representation of the adelic Weyl relations in which:

1. the rational lattice generates a maximal abelian unitary algebra;
2. reciprocal Fourier rotation exchanges the two polarization algebras;
3. the completed theta state supplies a cyclic distributional vector;
4. the corresponding Cayley boundary relation is closed and maximal
   self-adjoint; and
5. its relative determinant is \(X(z)\) up to a source unit.

The first three items are representation-theoretic. The fourth is the
off-seam exclusion mechanism. The fifth is the non-tautological readout gate.

## 6. Smallest falsifier

The immediate falsifier is nonuniqueness: two inequivalent regular Weyl
representations or two inequivalent Cayley boundary relations may carry the
same rational-lattice character and reproduce the same scalar Poisson
pairing. If the completed theta state does not canonically select between
them, group-level integrality still lacks operator authority.

## 7. Scope

Discreteness of \(\mathbb Q\subset\mathbb A\) and tangent collapse are exact.
The need to retain exponentiated Weyl data follows. No canonical boundary
representation, self-adjoint extension, determinant identity, or RH theorem
is established here.
