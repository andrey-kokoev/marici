# Theta history correlation has essential spectrum touching one

## Bounded question

Does the full seam-history correlation reduce completion escape to a finite
Fredholm eigenstate through a compact-remainder decomposition?

## Correlation symbol

For the bilateral history operator from packet 235,

\[
\mathcal F H_+\mathcal F^{-1}=M_{m_+},
\]

so

\[
\mathcal F H_+^*H_+\mathcal F^{-1}=M_{|m_+|^2}.
\]

Assume \(\Phi\ge0\) and normalize by

\[
\widetilde H=\frac{H_+}{\|\Phi\|_1}.
\]

Then

\[
|\widetilde m(\xi)|\le1,
\qquad
|\widetilde m(0)|=1.
\]

For a nondegenerate source, strict inequality holds away from zero frequency.

## Essential-spectrum obstruction

The spectrum of the multiplication operator

\[
\widetilde H^*\widetilde H
\]

is the essential range of \(|\widetilde m|^2\). Continuity at zero places (1)
in the essential spectrum. If equality occurs only at \(\xi=0\), there is no
nonzero (L^2) eigenfunction with eigenvalue (1), yet

\[
I-\widetilde H^*\widetilde H
\]

has no positive lower bound.

Low-frequency packets concentrated near \(\xi=0\) form an exact approximate
kernel sequence. In label coordinates these are the long coherent Følner
packets found earlier.

## Consequence for the compactness fork

There is no decomposition

\[
\widetilde H^*\widetilde H=D+K,
\qquad
0\le D\le dI,
\qquad
d<1,
\]

with (K) compact, because compact perturbations do not remove the essential
spectral point (1).

Thus completion escape is not reduced to a finite-multiplicity eigen-equation
for the raw history correlation. It is an essential-spectrum edge mode.

## What can still change the verdict

A source-derived enlarged port may alter the essential symbol rather than add a
compact correction. The cumulative innovation tower does exactly this by
retaining interval-resolved boundary history. Any successful Schur--Evans
colligation must preserve that noncompact history information.

A finite-rank seam repair or compact phase-sensitive correction cannot open the
missing uniform gap.

## Scope

This packet computes the essential spectrum of the normalized bilateral
history correlation and identifies its low-frequency completion escape. It
does not analyze an enlarged innovation colligation or prove RH.
