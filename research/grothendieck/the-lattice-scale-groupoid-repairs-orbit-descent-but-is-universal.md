## Repair of the finite-label obstruction

For $a>0$, let

\[
\Delta_a=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}\delta_{an}.
\]

With the self-dual Fourier convention, Poisson summation is the
distributional identity

\[
\mathcal F\Delta_a=a^{-1}\Delta_{a^{-1}}.
\]

Dilation transports lattice fibers:

\[
R_u\Delta_a=c(u)\Delta_{e^u a},
\]

where $c(u)$ is the normalization fixed by the chosen representation.
Therefore the family

\[
\{\Delta_a:a>0\}
\]

is closed under both continuous scale transport and the Fourier
quarter-turn. Fourier acts on its base by

\[
a\longmapsto a^{-1},
\]

and logarithmic scale $q=\log a$ turns this into $q\mapsto-q$.

The correct theta orbit is thus not a finite orbit of integer labels. It is a
groupoid over the positive scale line whose arrows are dilation and
primal--dual Fourier transport.

## Fundamental chamber

The quotient of the base by $a\leftrightarrow a^{-1}$ has the canonical
chamber

\[
a\ge1
\]

with seam $a=1$. This is the exact theta counterpart of a source orbit
fundamental interval. It exists before Mellin transformation and before the
Evans endpoint determinant is evaluated.

For a Schwartz source $f$, define

\[
\Theta_f(a)=\langle\Delta_a,f\rangle .
\]

Then

\[
\Theta_f(a)=a^{-1}\Theta_{\widehat f}(a^{-1}).
\]

For a Fourier-fixed source, this is reciprocal sewing on the lattice bundle.

## Why the repair does not yet constrain zeros

The entire construction above is universal. It holds for every Schwartz
source, with $f$ merely replaced by $\widehat f$ under the reciprocal
arrow. If $f$ is Fourier-fixed, it gives the same scalar functional
symmetry used by the hostile degree-twelve carrier.

Hence the lattice-scale groupoid explains:

- where the reciprocal seam comes from;
- why the two scale chambers are one completed object;
- how the infinite comb closes before scalar aggregation;
- why finite label truncations were the wrong orbit object.

It does not explain why the determinant-framed Mellin readout cannot wind
off the seam. The groupoid transports the framed section but supplies no
source-specific law orienting its divisor.

The actual Gaussian is not selected merely by Fourier fixedness. It carries
the first-order vacuum equation

\[
(\partial_x+2\pi x)f=0
\]

after normalization. The lattice-scale groupoid must therefore be enriched
by an independently sourced connection that remembers this annihilation law
and transports the Evans boundary frame.

The candidate architecture is:

1. the lattice-scale groupoid supplies reciprocal orbit descent;

The first three pieces are independently defined before the scalar $X$.

## Result and falsifier

The infinite lattice-scale groupoid repairs the absence of a finite theta
orbit, but it is universal coordinate machinery and has no RH force by
itself.

A proposed next theorem must fail on at least one Fourier-fixed hostile source
before its Mellin zeros are inspected. If it uses only Poisson summation,
reciprocal scale, and determinant framing, it cannot do so. The discriminator
must use the source-specific vacuum connection or an equally strong
constructor.
