# The first genuine extra channel is a nontrivial triangle residual

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-26

Status: exact structural obstruction

## Endpoint-potential no-go

Let source labels be (a,b,c), let (R_a) be an authorized normalization at
each label, and suppose a family of local operators has the form

\[
X_a=R_aX_0R_a^{-1}.
\]

The canonical comparison maps are

\[
T_{ab}=R_a^{-1}R_b.
\]

They satisfy

\[
T_{ab}T_{bc}=T_{ac},
\qquad
T_{ab}T_{bc}T_{ca}=I.
\]

Therefore any channel whose complete label dependence factors through the
one-label potential (R_a) is flat. Its matrices may fail to commute in a
single untransported presentation, but that failure is not invariant under
the authorized source normalization.

The prime reciprocal family is the instance

\[
R_p=\operatorname{diag}
\left(e^{-it\log(p)/2},e^{it\log(p)/2}\right).
\]

## The first invariant obstruction

Suppose boundary incidence modifies the comparison to

\[
\widetilde T_{ab}=T_{ab}A_{ab},
\]

where (A_{ab}) is derived from data belonging to the ordered pair rather
than fitted after scalar compression. The first gauge-covariant obstruction
is the triangle residual

\[
\Omega_{abc}
=\widetilde T_{ab}\widetilde T_{bc}\widetilde T_{ca}.
\]

If (Omega_{abc}=I) for every authorized triangle and the label complex has
no further topology, the added channel remains a coboundary presentation. A
nonidentity residual is the first evidence of genuine relational curvature.

This answers the earlier wall-count question precisely. Another comparison
channel is useful only if it introduces pair incidence that cannot be reduced
to endpoint normalization. Merely adding another one-label frame creates a
larger presentation of the same flat object.

## Source candidates and falsifier

The primitive, prime-square, mixed-seam, and archimedean currents are viable
only to the extent that their ordered-pair incidence survives common-frame
transport. For each candidate, compute its smallest two-prime comparison and
then the three-label product (Omega_{pqr}).

The route is falsified at this level if every candidate factorizes as

\[
A_{pq}=B_p^{-1}B_q.
\]

Then all triangle residuals telescope, and the enlarged construction still
has no intrinsic curvature capable of protecting a divisor.

## Current obstacle

No source-derived nonfactorizable (A_{pq}) has yet been constructed. The
known prime phase, reciprocal exchange, and their normalized Pauli algebra
all factor through one-label frames. The missing object is therefore not an
additional matrix dimension by itself. It is an authorized pair-incidence
law with nontrivial triangle residual, followed by a bridge from that
residual to the scalar determinant section.
