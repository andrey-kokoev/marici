# The full theta tail--seam Gram form is faithful but not closed-range on unrestricted packets

## Direct-sum architecture

Retain the seam as an independent state component and define the full source
synthesis

\[
 Uc=(Gc,Hc)
 \in\mathcal H_{\rm tail}\oplus\mathcal H_{\rm seam}.
\]

The exact cut decomposition gives

\[
 \lVert Uc\rVert^2
 =\lVert Gc\rVert^2+\lVert Hc\rVert^2
 =\iint A(|p-r|)c(p)\overline{c(r)}\,dp\,dr.
\]

This full energy retains the norm lost by tail projection. In particular,
for each translated source atom,

\[
 \lVert g_p\rVert^2+\lVert h_p\rVert^2
 =\lVert\Phi\rVert_2^2.
\]

Translation escape is exactly repaired.

## Fourier multiplier form

The kernel `A` is the autocorrelation of the bilateral source. On an
appropriate full-line packet realization, Plancherel gives

\[
 \lVert Uc\rVert^2
 =C\int_{\mathbb R}|\widehat c(\xi)|^2
 |\widehat\Phi(\xi)|^2\,d\xi,
\]

with a convention-dependent positive constant `C`.

For the nonzero analytic theta source, `widehat(Phi)` is not identically zero
and its real zero set has measure zero under the usual analyticity
hypotheses. Hence

\[
 \lVert Uc\rVert=0\implies c=0
\]

for ordinary `L2` packets: the full Gram form is faithful.

This injectivity statement must retain its analytic and domain hypotheses; it
is not asserted for arbitrary distributions.

## High-frequency no-closed-range obstruction

The theta source is integrable, so the Riemann--Lebesgue lemma gives

\[
 |\widehat\Phi(\xi)|\longrightarrow0
 \qquad(|\xi|\to\infty).
\]

Choose a nonzero compactly supported packet `c_0` and modulate it:

\[
 c_N(p)=e^{iNp}c_0(p).
\]

Then

\[
 \lVert c_N\rVert_2=\lVert c_0\rVert_2,
 \qquad
 \widehat c_N(\xi)=\widehat c_0(\xi-N).
\]

Dominated convergence in the multiplier formula yields

\[
 \lVert Uc_N\rVert\longrightarrow0
 \qquad
 \lVert c_N\rVert_2=\lVert c_0\rVert_2>0.
\]

Therefore `U` is not bounded below on the unrestricted packet Hilbert space.
If it is injective, its range is not closed.

## Meaning

Adding the seam eliminates loss under large translation, but not loss under
rapid oscillation of packet coefficients. The full source state remains
faithful pointwise in packet space, yet no uniform completed lower bound
exists relative to the unweighted `L2` coefficient norm.

This is not a zero of the source state. It is a mismatch between the chosen
coefficient completion and the source-induced relationship norm.

## Consequence for the RH architecture

The requirement

\[
 \Psi\ne0\implies E_{\rm full}(\Psi)>0
\]

can hold without any uniform bound against the ambient packet norm. But a
closed-range or completion-stability proof cannot use unrestricted `L2(dp)`
as its reference coefficient space.

There are three admissible responses:

1. complete packets in the source Gram norm itself;
2. prove that physical arithmetic packets form a smaller source-authorized
   class excluding arbitrary high-frequency modulation;
3. add an independently derived label-regularity energy that controls the
   missing frequencies.

Choosing a stronger Sobolev weight merely to restore a lower bound is
inadmissible unless it comes from the labelled theta/Tate constructors.

## Relevance to zero-states

The modulated packets are a fatal completion witness only if they belong to
the completed domain used for zero-states. The arithmetic readout may select
a much smaller coefficient family than arbitrary complex `L2` packets.
Therefore this result does not falsify the direct-sum RH route. It fixes its
next source-authority gate:

The remaining question is which coefficient completion is generated by
physical prime and scale constructors.

Without that answer, “closed range of the full energy” is ill-posed.
