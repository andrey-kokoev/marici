# The Logarithmic Hermite Phase Is Not the Tate Sheet Phase

Entry 3944 correctly established that (h_1(q)=q e^{-\pi q^2}\) separates the
Mellin endpoint from every nonzero translate. It incorrectly promoted the
ordinary logarithmic Fourier eigenphase \(\mathcal F_qh_1=-ih_1\) to a
Fourier--Tate sheet phase.

The transforms have different coordinate types. Ordinary Fourier transform
in (q=\log|x|\) is Mellin spectral duality. Tate sewing uses additive Fourier
transform in (x\). After logarithmic pullback, the latter becomes the nonlocal
operator

\[
K_{\mathrm{Tate}}=U\mathcal F_xU^{-1},
\]

not ordinary Fourier transform in (q\). Reciprocal oddness (q\mapsto-q\)
also differs from additive oddness (x\mapsto-x\).

Endpoint pointing therefore survives, but Tate sheet orientation remains
unproved. The next exact calculation is (K_{\mathrm{Tate}}h_1\), with the
source normalization and endpoint terms retained. If it is not a scalar
multiple of (h_1\), that is the smallest direct falsifier of the prior phase
claim.
