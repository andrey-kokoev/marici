# Central-extension power--Mackey spectrum

## Theorem

Let `q:G->H` be any finite group surjection with central kernel `K`.  The
`n`-th power correspondence commutes with every coefficient fiber sum and
basis-level fiber lift if and only if

`gcd(n,exp(K))=1`.

For a fiber representative `g` and `k in K`, centrality gives

`(gk)^n=g^n k^n`.

Thus the restricted power map on every fiber is a translate of the power map
`k->k^n` on `K`.  It is bijective exactly when `n` is coprime to the kernel
exponent.  The quotient group and the extension cocycle contribute no extra
resonance primes.

## Nonsplit controls

The checker exhausts indices `1..18` for:

- `C4 -> C2` with central kernel `C2`;
- `Q8 -> V4` with central kernel `{+1,-1}`;
- the order-27 Heisenberg group over `F3` mapping to `C3^2`, with central
  kernel `C3`.

These include nontrivial central extension classes and nonabelian total
groups.  Every fiber agrees with the kernel-exponent criterion.

## Scope and falsifier

This removes the split-extension hypothesis only for central kernels.  A
noncentral nonsplit extension requires an outer-action/cocycle analysis.
For nonabelian total groups the power correspondence is basis-linear, not
generally a ring Adams operation.  No physical chain transfer is supplied.
