# Fixed transfer protocol: projected blade supports
Use the nine-site support rows of v1; sweep all 1024 port colorings, excluding both original parent colorings. Keep legal parents only. At every gap compare all 32 marked port sets (including empty) to direct child legality. Before any child check, compute the parent red triangle gate, a single compatible blue five if present at an interior seam, and blue sixes avoiding the flanks. Project each such six onto its port labels. Keep one original blade per distinct inclusion-minimal projection; ties do not change predictions. If a five vetoes the entire gap, no sixes are needed. A move passes iff it passes the triangle gate, the five veto, and hits every retained port set. Record raw gap-eligible and retained six-blade counts separately from decisions. This is compression at a fixed gap, not a shared dictionary across gaps. Stop on the first mismatch. No fitting or runtime advantage claimed.
