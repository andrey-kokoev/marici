## Source-native transport

Let a source-derived arithmetic block multiply a scalar section (F(s)) by a
nonzero factor (g(s)). Retain the value--flux jet

\[
j(F)=\binom{F}{\partial_sF}.
\]

The product rule gives the transport before any scalar compression:

\[
j(gF)=S_gj(F),\qquad
S_g=\begin{pmatrix}g&0\\g'&g\end{pmatrix}.
\]

This matrix is forced by multiplication and differentiation; it is not fitted
to the desired boundary form. Its determinant is

\[
\det S_g=g^2.
\]

For the alternating value--flux form with matrix

\[
J=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix},
\]

one has exactly

\[
S_g^TJS_g=g^2J.
\]

Thus adjoining one block does not strictly preserve boundary area. It acts by
a determinant-line multiplier (g^2). After separating that line, the
normalized shear

\[
g^{-1}S_g=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\g'/g&1\end{pmatrix}
\]

has determinant one. The fifth datum suggested by the four-observer audit is
therefore an arrow-level coherencer, not another observer of the state.

## One labelled prime block

The bordered valuation-chain construction derives

\[
g_{p,N}(s)=\sum_{k=0}^{N}p^{-ks}
\]

at finite depth. In its convergent infinite-chain limit,

\[
g_p(s)=\frac{1}{1-p^{-s}}.
\]

Writing (q=p^{-s}), the determinant-line logarithm is

\[
\log\det S_{g_p}
=-2\log(1-q)
=2q+q^2+\sum_{k\ge3}\frac{2}{k}q^k.
\]

The decomposition is typed rather than cosmetic:

- (2q) is the doubled primitive current;
- (q^2) is the doubled prime-square current;
- the terms with (k\ge3) are the connected determinant-three carrier;
- the completed archimedean multiplier contributes its own squared
  determinant-line factor by the same jet law.

Consequently the previously identified primitive, square, connected-tail, and
archimedean data account for the entire multiplicative anomaly. No additional
state channel is generated by this transport.

## Exact finite falsifier

For every finite derived factor (g_{p,N}), compute (S_{g_{p,N}}) directly.
The proposal fails if any of the following occurs:

1. the jet transport has an entry not supplied by the product rule;
2. its boundary-form multiplier differs from (g_{p,N}^2);
3. composition does not multiply the determinant-line factors;
4. the Euler limit contains a logarithmic coefficient other than (2/k);
5. an archimedean presentation changes the finite-prime factor.

The symbolic checker verifies the matrix identity and the first six Euler
coefficients exactly.

## Scope

This proves transport coherence for multiplicative arithmetic and
archimedean blocks. It does not prove that the completed Clark connection
preserves a positive sheet, that endpoint flux vanishes, or that the infinite
determinant line is nonvanishing after continuation. It identifies the exact
place where those questions live and prevents the determinant anomaly from
being mistaken for a missing observer.
