# Unitary Sewing Transports the Entire Finite Green Packet, Not Three Separate Invariants

## One source statement

Let `U` be the normalized Fourier sewing map between two finite reciprocal
boundary packets. Suppose it transports both the relationship state and its
forcing state:

\[
W_-=UW_+,
\qquad
h_-=Uh_+.
\]

Because `U` is unitary, it follows pointwise that

\[
\lVert W_-\rVert^2=\lVert W_+\rVert^2,
\qquad
\langle h_-,W_-\rangle=\langle h_+,W_+\rangle.
\]

After integration, the Green energy and forcing terms agree:

\[
E_-=E_+,
\qquad
F_-=F_+.
\]

If the endpoint anchor is transported as part of the same packet, exterior
functoriality gives

\[
B_-=B_+.
\]

Thus the three equalities required by the relative mapping-cone Green lemma
are not independent miracles. They are shadows of one unitary packet mate.
