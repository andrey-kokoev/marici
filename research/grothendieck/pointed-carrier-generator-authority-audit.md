# Pointed Carrier generator authority audit

## Question

Does the conditional arithmetic construction depend essentially on choosing one connected Carrier object `U`?

## Abstract independence

For every connected choice `U`, the free disjoint-union subgroupoid generated by `U` has the initial commutative semiring as its component arithmetic. Any two outputs are uniquely isomorphic as unital semirings, because both are initial. Therefore the abstract arithmetic object, its intrinsic primes, formal Euler product, ordered completion, and theta lattice do not depend on a coordinate identification between the two generators.

This removes pointing ambiguity at the level of the abstract arithmetic output.

## Missing Carrier comparison

The embeddings of the two rank-one subgroupoids into the full Carrier are not identified by initiality. To obtain one functor from all finite Carrier objects, let `C_conn` be the set of connected isomorphism classes. Disjoint-union components form the free commutative monoid on `C_conn`. There is a canonical component-count augmentation

`epsilon: free_commutative_monoid(C_conn) -> initial_additive_monoid`

sending every connected class to the unit. This is universal among additive readouts that assign unit weight to every connected component.

The augmentation supplies a point-free route to the same initial arithmetic, but it deliberately collapses all connected source types. Carrier packets contain occurrence and automorphism data that some physical readouts detect, so this collapse is not a faithful or universal physical quotient.

## Authority test

The arithmetic lens is source-compatible only if its declared readout is allowed to forget connected type while retaining component multiplicity. The acceptance condition is not a canonical choice of `U`; it is an independently authorized augmentation policy and proof that every constructor used downstream respects its kernel congruence.

A hostile pair of connected source objects with distinct typed boundary incidence has equal augmentation. Any downstream operation distinguishing their incidence fails to descend, even though their arithmetic counts agree.

## Disposition

Pointed-generator nonuniqueness does not obstruct the abstract initial arithmetic. It retypes the first missing arrow as authorization and constructor descent for the component-count augmentation from the full connected-type monoid. This quotient can support a scalar arithmetic lens, but it cannot be promoted to the shared physical Carrier without checking each retained constructor.
