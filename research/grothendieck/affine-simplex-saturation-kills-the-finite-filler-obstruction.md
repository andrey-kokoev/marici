# Affine-simplex saturation kills the finite filler obstruction

## Source readback

Voevodsky's invariance audit states that the ordinary Mellin parameter has declared affine structure and therefore supplies the canonical straight orbit from `w` to `-w`. It separately warns that endpoint incidence alone does not select a strict representative and phrases each detour homotopy conditionally: its triangle boundary exists when that simplex is admitted.

## Exact saturation result

On the reflection-closed four-vertex fixture, the ten declared edges support twelve oriented affine triangles. Their boundary matrix has rank seven, equal to the full dimension of `ker d1`. Therefore admitting every affine composition triangle gives

\[
H_1=0.
\]

In particular both reciprocal character sectors vanish. The previously observed odd class exists only in the sparse subcomplex containing four selected detour triangles. Its explicit fill is

\[
-[w,-w,-a]+[-w,w,a].
\]

## Meaning

If the ordinary-domain chain object is the affine singular or simplicial chain complex generated by its declared parameter-space affine structure, the finite representative obstruction closes. No theta arithmetic is used: affine contractibility already kills it.

This does not construct the completed modular filler. The unresolved operations are precisely those that need not preserve the affine contraction:

1. modular sewing on chain representatives;
2. basis mutation;
3. Euler-cutoff transition;
4. graph-topology completion and boundary-null descent;
5. retention of labelled arithmetic and archimedean channels.

## Falsifier

The finite closure claim fails if the source chain object excludes either composition triangle, types its direct edge outside the same affine chain complex, or uses a boundary map incompatible with the affine simplex boundary. The completed claim fails if affine contractions have unbounded norm or fail to commute with cutoff maps.

## Disposition

Do not treat the finite odd class as theta evidence. Read back whether the ordinary source admits all affine simplices. If yes, close the finite homology branch and move the entire obstruction to naturality and completion. The first quantitative residual is then the cutoff commutator of the affine straight-orbit contraction, not existence of a finite filler.
