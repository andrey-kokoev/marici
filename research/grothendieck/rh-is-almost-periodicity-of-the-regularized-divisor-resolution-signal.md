# RH is almost periodicity of the regularized divisor-resolution signal

## Finite supply normalization

Let `mu_supply` be the positive divisor-supply measure. Choose

\[
w_k(t)=(1+t^2)^{-k}
\]

with an integer `k` large enough that

\[
\nu_k=w_k\mu_{\mathrm{supply}}
\]

is finite. The standard zero-counting growth and the half-plane Blaschke
condition make `k=1` sufficient; retaining general `k>=1` makes the
regularization class explicit.

Write the Lebesgue decomposition

\[
\nu_k=\nu_k^{\mathrm{atom}}+\nu_k^{\mathrm{diff}}.
\]

Its Fourier-resolution signal is

\[
R_k(x)=\widehat\nu_k(x).
\]

## Atomic channel is almost periodic

The atomic part is

\[
\nu_k^{\mathrm{atom}}
=
\sum_\gamma
m_\gamma w_k(\gamma)\delta_\gamma.
\]

Its coefficients are absolutely summable. Therefore

\[
R_k^{\mathrm{atom}}(x)
=
\sum_\gamma
m_\gamma w_k(\gamma)e^{-i\gamma x}
\]

converges uniformly and is a Bohr almost-periodic function.

## Diffuse channel vanishes at infinity

The weighted diffuse part has an `L^1` density. By the Riemann--Lebesgue
lemma,

\[
R_k^{\mathrm{diff}}(x)
\longrightarrow0
\qquad (|x|\longrightarrow\infty).
\]

It is nonzero exactly when an open-sector zero exists, because every such
zero contributes a strictly positive Poisson density and the weight is
strictly positive.

## Exact almost-periodicity criterion

A Bohr almost-periodic function that tends to zero at both infinities is
identically zero. Moreover the decomposition of a bounded uniformly
continuous function into an almost-periodic component and a `C_0` component
is unique when it exists.

Hence `R_k` is Bohr almost periodic if and only if

\[
R_k^{\mathrm{diff}}=0.
\]

By positivity and uniqueness of Fourier transform for finite measures, this
is equivalent to `nu_k^diff=0`, hence to `mu_supply^diff=0`. Therefore RH
holds if and only if `R_k` is Bohr almost periodic.

The statement holds for one admissible `k`, and then for every admissible
`k`.

## Why this may transfer to the arithmetic source

The zero-free Euler chamber is generated by discrete Mellin characters

\[
e^{-it\log n}.
\]

Discrete character grammars naturally produce almost-periodic signals. The
new proof target is therefore:

> Show that labelled theta--Euler completion transports the regularized
> divisor-resolution signal from the Euler chamber to the seam inside the
> Bohr almost-periodic category.

This is stronger than pointwise convergence and weaker than constructing
individual zeros. It forbids the `C_0` decay channel in which off-seam Poisson
profiles live.

The transport must be source-authorized and completion-stable. Declaring
almost periodicity after expanding in the unknown zero ordinates would be
circular.

## Hostile falsifier

One off-seam zero adds a nonzero term in `C_0` after regularization. No
nonzero `C_0` function is Bohr almost periodic. Thus a hostile reciprocal
quartet fails the target category even though it preserves positivity,
functional-equation symmetry, and the total divisor budget.

## Scope

This packet proves the almost-periodicity equivalence for the
boundary-derived supply signal. It does not derive almost periodicity from
the labelled arithmetic source and does not prove RH.
