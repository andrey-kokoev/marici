## Explicit conditional hypothesis

The surface typing audit found that geometric disjoint union is a
multiplicity-sensitive symmetric monoidal operation, but not an admitted
categorical coproduct.  This packet asks how far Phase I would advance under
the explicit relaxation

\[
\text{finite categorical coproduct}
\quad\rightsquigarrow\quad
\text{symmetric-monoidal geometric disjoint union}.
\]

The relaxation is analyzed, not adopted.  It changes Nima's finalized plan
and therefore cannot be installed silently.

## The unit-generated surface groupoid

Fix a source-derived connected marked surface `U`.  Let
`Surf_U^sqcup` be the symmetric monoidal subgroupoid whose objects are built
from `U` and the empty surface by finite geometric disjoint union and whose
morphisms are mapping classes.

Every object has a connected-component decomposition

\[
U\sqcup\cdots\sqcup U.
\]

Mapping classes preserve connected components and can only permute
isomorphic copies.  Consequently two such objects are isomorphic precisely
when their finite `U`-words have the same number of factors.  This is a
derived normal form, not a cardinality label attached to an input object.

## Free additive monoid theorem

Let `M` be a commutative monoid and choose `m in M`.  There is one and only
one monoid map

\[
\Phi_m:\pi_0(\operatorname{Surf}^{\sqcup}_U)\longrightarrow M
\]

which sends `[U]` to `m`: send a disjoint-union word to the repeated sum of
one `m` for each connected component.  Existence follows from disjoint-union
normal form; uniqueness follows because every object is generated by `U`.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\pi_0(\operatorname{Surf}^{\sqcup}_U)
\text{ is the free commutative monoid on one generator.}
}
\]

Only after proving this universal property may one identify that monoid with
`N`.  The natural numbers are the output of connected decomposition, not an
index set supplied to the Carrier.

## Group completion theorem

Construct the group completion intrinsically from pairs of `U`-words:

\[
(P,Q)\sim(P\sqcup C,Q\sqcup C).
\]

Cancel common connected components.  Every class has a unique normal form
consisting either of a surplus positive `U`-word, a surplus negative
`U`-word, or the empty pair.  The classes of `(U,empty)` and `(empty,U)` are
mutual inverses.

For any abelian group `G` and chosen `g in G`, the same normal form gives a
unique homomorphism sending `(U,empty)` to `g`.  Hence the completion is the
free abelian group on one generator:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Gr}(\pi_0\operatorname{Surf}^{\sqcup}_U)
\cong\mathbb Z.
}
\]

Again, `Z` is identified by a universal property after the categorical
construction; it is not posited as a coefficient ring.

## Why this is not yet the initial semiring

An additive monoid and its group completion do not provide multiplication.
Suppose independently that the Carrier had a second symmetric monoidal
operation `tensor` such that

1. `U` were its unit; and
2. `tensor` distributed over geometric disjoint union.

Then its law on the generated objects would be forced:

\[
\left(\bigsqcup_{i\in I}U\right)
\otimes
\left(\bigsqcup_{j\in J}U\right)
\cong
\bigsqcup_{(i,j)\in I\times J}(U\otimes U)
\cong
\bigsqcup_{(i,j)\in I\times J}U.
\]

Thus arithmetic multiplication would emerge from distributivity and the
unit law rather than being separately inserted.  The problem is existence,
not the formal consequence.

The exact quadrilateral test in the unmarked-assembly packet already blocks
the smallest connected sewing candidate: its sixteen interface choices form
one source-symmetry orbit and have no fixed point.  The additive completion
does not choose one.  Summing the orbit would use the additive structure to
manufacture a different operation and would still need a proof that it is the
Carrier tensor with unit `U`.

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\text{conditional additive }\mathbb N\text{ and }\mathbb Z
\quad\not\Rightarrow\quad
\text{initial semiring or intrinsic primes}.}
\]

intrinsic derivation:

\[
\pi_0\cong\mathbb N,
\qquad
\operatorname{Gr}(\pi_0)\cong\mathbb Z.

so the initial semiring, intrinsic multiplication, irreducibility, primes,
Burnside--Witt structure, and Euler products remain unavailable.
