# Gaussian dilations fill the even Hilbert carrier

## Question

The complete moment tower is unfaithful on the ambient Schwartz source.  Can
the theta programme avoid that hostile by declaring that only the
Gaussian-generated source module is admissible and quasianalytic?

## Logarithmic form of the dilation orbit

On the even real carrier, restrict to (x>0) and use the unitary logarithmic
coordinate

\[
(Wf)(q)=e^{q/2}f(e^q).
\]

For the Gaussian

\[
g(x)=e^{-\pi x^2},
\]

its log profile is

\[
w(q)=e^{q/2}e^{-\pi e^{2q}}.
\]

Unitary dilation of (g) becomes ordinary translation of (w).  Therefore
the closed dilation orbit of the Gaussian is determined by the translation
cyclicity of (w) in (L^2(\mathbb R,dq)).

## The exact Mellin multiplier

The Fourier transform of (w) in the (q)-coordinate is

\[
\widehat w(\xi)
=
\frac12
\pi^{-1/4+i\xi/2}
\Gamma\left(\frac14-\frac{i\xi}{2}\right).
\]

The Gamma function has no zeros, and its argument has real part (1/4) for
real ξ.  Hence

\[
\widehat w(\xi)\ne0
\qquad
\xi\in\mathbb R.
\]

By the translation cyclicity theorem, the closed span of all translates of
(w) is the whole logarithmic (L^2) carrier.  Transporting back through
(W), the closed span of all positive dilations of (g) is the entire even
subspace

\[
L^2_{\mathrm{even}}(\mathbb R).
\]

## Completion destroys the proposed escape

Every finite linear combination of Gaussian dilations is analytic.  But its
ordinary Hilbert closure is not a quasianalytic Gaussian class; it is the
whole even Hilbert carrier.  That closure contains nonzero even Schwartz
states whose Fourier transforms vanish near zero and hence whose complete
moment towers vanish.

Thus the source declaration

"generated by Gaussian dilations and then Hilbert-completed"

does not exclude the moment-invisible hostile.  It admits it by density.

This is the same completion phenomenon encountered in the Euler and seam
lanes: a property true on every algebraic finite packet can disappear when
the contracting or reconstruction constants are not uniform.

## What remains possible

A stricter Gaussian-generated topology may preserve analyticity or
quasianalyticity, but it must be derived independently and must pass all of
the following gates:

- dilation and Fourier transport are continuous;
- the integer-comb pairing is continuous;
- primitive, square, connected, and seam currents extend;
- cutoff reconstruction converges;
- the topology does not depend on zero locations;
- its inclusion into the full trace carrier is faithful and completion
  stable.

The source-Gram topology alone is not enough if it completes the cyclic orbit
to the same even Hilbert space.

## Consequence for the observer programme

The full function-valued trace remains the honest completion-level observer
under the ordinary Hilbert realization.  Moment or determinant compression
cannot be made faithful merely by citing the Gaussian origin of the dense
algebraic orbit.

The next constructive option is a nuclear analytic-vector topology for the
dilation generator.  Its seminorms must come from the theta constructors and
must retain the arithmetic boundary currents.  If that topology completes to
the full even Schwartz space, the same hostile returns.

## Scope

Gaussian dilation cyclicity in the even Hilbert carrier is exact.  The result
does not rule out a stricter source-derived quasianalytic completion.  It
shows that ordinary (L^2) completion cannot supply one.

## Result

The Gaussian source orbit is already maximally large after Hilbert
completion: it fills the entire even carrier.  Gaussian provenance therefore
does not rescue moment faithfulness.  Either retain the full function-valued
trace, or construct and justify a strictly finer quasianalytic source
topology.
