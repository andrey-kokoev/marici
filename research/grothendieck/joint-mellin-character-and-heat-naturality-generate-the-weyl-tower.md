\[

\[

\[

$z\partial_z$.

## Joint-commutant theorem

Let $T$ be an endomorphism of the polynomial core $\mathbb C[z]$ that
commutes with both $X$ and $L$. Then $T$ is scalar.

Because $1$ is cyclic for $X$, write

\[
h=T1.
\]

Commutation with $X$ gives

\[
Tp=ph
\]

for every polynomial $p$. Commutation with $L$, first on $p=1$, gives

\[
h''=0.
\]

Applying it to $p=z$ gives

\[
2h'+zh''=0,
\]

and hence $h'=0$. Therefore $h$ is constant and

\[
T=hI.
\]

The result extends to an admitted analytic completion when the polynomial
core is dense and the operators are continuous on the declared domains.

\[

\[
