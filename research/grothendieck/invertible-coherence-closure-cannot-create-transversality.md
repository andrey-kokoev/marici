# Invertible coherence closure cannot create transversality

## The algebraic no-go

Let `V` be a constructor-state space, let

\[
\ell:V\longrightarrow \mathbb C
\]

be its scalar readout, and let `T` be an invertible source coherence. Suppose
the readout is equivariant:

\[
\ell T=\chi\ell,
\qquad \chi\ne0.
\]

Then

\[
T(\ker\ell)=\ker\ell.
\]

Consequently, adjoining every transported copy of a state and every equation
that says those copies are related by `T` does not shrink the scalar zero
fiber. It only equips that fiber with coherent transport.

This applies directly to the completed Tate--Poisson channel swap. Reciprocal
sewing is an involutive permutation of the bulk and polar channels, and the
scalar completed readout is invariant under it. Therefore reciprocity carries
every scalar cancellation to another scalar cancellation. It cannot exclude
one.

## Smallest hostile witness

Take

\[
V=\mathbb R^2,
\qquad
\ell(x,y)=x+y,
\qquad
T(x,y)=(y,x).
\]

The nonzero state `(1,-1)` lies in `ker ell`. It is perfectly coherent:

\[
T(1,-1)=(-1,1),
\qquad
\ell T=\ell.
\]

Adding the transported state and the exact comparison equation changes
neither the existence nor the dimension of the zero fiber.

## Relation to the Green-row result

Two superficially different extra rows have now been classified.

1. The universal Green row is generated by the constructor differential
   equation, so it is a syzygy on the constructor graph.
2. The modular reciprocity row is an invertible automorphism of the completed
   channel object, so it preserves the scalar zero fiber.

Neither is the missing fifth wall. The first repeats an existing equation;
the second transports its solutions.

## What kind of relation could add rank

The missing relation must couple the distinguished scalar section to data not
preserved automatically by constructor closure. Algebraically, at least one
of the following must occur:

- a second observer whose kernel is transverse to `ker ell`;
- a source-derived quadratic form that is faithful on `ker ell`;
- a boundary incidence whose admissible domain excludes the hostile null
  orbit;
- a conservation law equating scalar nullity to a nonzero oriented current;
- a non-invertible localization whose kernel is independently controlled.

Merely adding further invertible coherences, pullbacks, or orbit copies cannot
do this. A tower of equivalences can become arbitrarily high while leaving
the cancellation fiber unchanged.

## Multi-tower interpretation

The input, output, and control towers may each be fully coherent, and the
comparison cells among them may themselves form a coherent tower. If every
new cell is invertible, however, the whole construction is still a groupoid.
Groupoid completion preserves isotropy and transports zero fibers; it does not
create a transverse measurement.

Thus the next rung is not simply one more coherence cell. It must change
logical variance: from reversible comparison to a source-authorized
incidence, observer, or energy that can distinguish a null scalar shadow from
a null full relationship.

## Falsifier for future proposals

For any proposed additional wall `R`, first restrict it to the completed
constructor graph and ask whether it factors through existing equations and
invertible coherences. If it does, test the hostile null orbit generated by a
nonzero `v` in `ker ell`.

If every transported copy of `v` still satisfies `R`, then `R` adds no RH
force. The proposal becomes relevant only when it rejects that orbit for a
source-derived reason visible before inspecting zero locations.
