# Positive staircase curvature forces a quarter-density lift

## Bounded question

Does the positive multiplication/division curvature reproduce the actual
prime-power coefficients of the staircase Green current, beginning at
\(p^2\)?

## Amplitude-linear staircase weights

At the critical half-density, the logarithmic-derivative staircase uses

\[
w_{p,k}^{\rm LD}
=(\log p)p^{-k/2}.
\]

The Euler-log staircase instead uses

\[
w_{p,k}^{\log}
=\frac1k p^{-k/2}.
\]

These are coefficients of a linear boundary amplitude.

## Native half-density transport squares to the wrong coefficient

If one places the half-density directly on prime multiplication,

\[
S_p=p^{-1/2}T_p,
\]

then the positive depth-\(k\) commutator is

\[
[S_p^{*k},S_p^k]
=p^{-k}P_{v_p(n)<k}.
\]

At \(k=2\), this gives \(p^{-2}\), whereas the staircase amplitude has
\(p^{-1}\). The direct positive-curvature identification therefore fails by
one factor of \(p^{-1}\) at the first square channel.

## Unique multiplicative square root

Let

\[
U_p=p^{-\alpha}T_p.
\]

Its depth-\(k\) positive curvature carries modulus coefficient

\[
p^{-2\alpha k}.
\]

Matching \(p^{-k/2}\) for every \(k\) forces

\[
2\alpha=\frac12,
\qquad
\alpha=\frac14.
\]

Thus the unique multiplicative positive lift is

\[
U_p=p^{-1/4}T_p.
\]

It satisfies

\[
[U_p^{*k},U_p^k]
=p^{-k/2}P_{v_p(n)<k}.
\]

Multiplication by \(\log p\) then matches the logarithmic-derivative weight at
every depth. Multiplication by \(1/k\) matches the Euler-log weight, with the
connected-depth factor retained separately.

## Meaning of the quarter density

The quarter-density is not present in the scalar staircase amplitude. It
appears because a positive quadratic form squares its transport amplitude.
Therefore any positive operator realization of the linear half-density current
must contain a canonical square-root or half-form construction.

This is not permission to insert \(p^{-1/4}\) by hand. The lift must arise from
an independently derived determinant, Pfaffian, Fock, or correspondence metric
that maps amplitude data to quadratic energy while preserving finite Euler
cutoffs.

## Result

The \(p^2\) coefficient audit neither validates the naive half-density
operator nor destroys the positive-curvature route. It uniquely identifies the
missing type: linear half-density amplitude must pass through quarter-density
transport to become positive half-density curvature. Hilbertization therefore
requires a source-authorized square root of the arithmetic amplitude.

## Sharp falsifier

Construct the proposed determinant or Fock lift at one prime. If its native
transport coefficient is not \(p^{-1/4}\), or if the depth-two curvature is not
exactly \(p^{-1}P_{v_p(n)<2}\), then it cannot realize the staircase Green
current as this positive exclusion energy.
