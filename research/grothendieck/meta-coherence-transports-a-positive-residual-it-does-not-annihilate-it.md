# Meta-coherence transports a positive residual; it does not annihilate it

## Correction

Yes: forcing every meta-level residual to vanish would erase the positivity content or wrongly identify a prime-only route with the completed route.

Three residual types must be separated.

## Commutator residual

For two routes that are declared to represent the same operation, the defect

\[
\mathfrak C_{p,\alpha}
=
O_p(F_\alpha x)-O_p(G_\alpha x)
\]

should vanish in the completed limit. At finite arithmetic cutoff it is generally nonzero and must be bounded by the source tail certificate.

## Completion residual

The prime-only transform does not equal the completed transform. Its canonical residual is

\[
\Omega_{\rm comp}
=
\mathcal K_{\rm completed}-\mathcal K_{\rm prime}
=
\mathcal K_{\rm endpoint}+\mathcal K_\Gamma
\]

in the selected normalization. Meta-coherence requires every observer to reproduce this same residual naturally. It must not set it to zero.

## Positive defect residual

For source feature rows `A` and `B`, define

\[
\mathfrak R=A^*A-B^*B.
\]

If `B=CA` with a contraction `C`, then

\[
\mathfrak R
=
A^*(1-C^*C)A
=
D^*D.
\]

This is the surviving positive residual. Setting it to zero would require `C` to be isometric on the source range and would assert that the Weil form vanishes identically.

For each finite observer packet `I`, the residual is restricted by

\[
\mathfrak R_I=\iota_I^*\mathfrak R\iota_I.
\]

Meta-coherence requires these residuals to agree under packet restriction and heat--translation comparison. Positivity requires `R_I` to be PSD. Neither requirement annihilates `R_I`.

## Correct categorical object

The apex is therefore not the equalizer of all observer values. It is an equalizer of route defects together with a natural section of positive residual cones:

\[
(\mathfrak C=0)
\quad\text{and}\quad
(\mathfrak R_I\ge0,
\;r^*\mathfrak R_Jr=\mathfrak R_I).
\]

Completion residuals appear as prescribed nonzero cocycle data; cutoff residuals converge to zero; positivity residuals survive as coherent positive forms.

## Disposition

Retain residuals as typed objects. Vanish only genuine commutator defects. Match completion anomalies to endpoint--gamma input, bound cutoff errors, and transport the nonzero positive defect naturally across the entire observer family.
