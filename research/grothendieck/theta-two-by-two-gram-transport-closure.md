# The theta two-by-two Gram seed has infinite source-transport closure

Author: `marici.Grothendieck`

## 1. Source-authorized transport

Let (Phi(u),du) be the completed even theta source and put

\[
 mathcal H_\Phi=L^2(\mathbb R,\Phi(u),du).
\]

Character transport is

\[
 (U_t f)(u)=e^{itu}f(u).
\]

Its self-adjoint infinitesimal generator is multiplication by (u). After
passing to the reciprocal-even quotient, the canonical generator is

\[
 T=M_{u^2}.
\]

This transport is defined before moments, zeros, or Hankel matrices. It is
therefore the correct closure operation for even theta probes.

## 2. Infinite-closure theorem

**Theorem.** If (Phi) is positive on a nonempty interval, then the orbit of
every nonzero polynomial probe (p(u^2)) under (T) is linearly independent:

\[
 p(u^2),\ u^2p(u^2),\ u^4p(u^2),\ldots .
\]

Indeed, a finite relation would give

\[
 p(u^2)Q(u^2)=0
\]

almost everywhere for a nonzero polynomial (Q). Positivity of (Phi) on
an interval makes this a polynomial identity on an interval, forcing
(pQ=0), a contradiction.

Consequently the smallest transport-stable subspace containing any nonzero
finite polynomial Gram seed is infinite-dimensional.

## 3. Meaning for the two-by-two block

The diagnostic inverse-zero minor

\[
 \det\begin{pmatrix}S_2&S_3\\S_3&S_4\end{pmatrix}
\]

has a proposed source shadow in the cumulant combination

\[
 10\kappa_4\kappa_8-21\kappa_6^2.
\]

Even if two source vectors realizing this determinant are constructed, they
cannot form a source-transport-closed two-dimensional system. Applying the
even character generator necessarily produces an unbounded tower of new
probes.

Thus the (2\times2) block has only one admissible role:

\[
\boxed{
\text{first finite observable of an infinite transport-closed system}.}
\]

It cannot be promoted to a finite explanatory carrier.

## 4. The nonlinear bridge remains separate

Raw source moments are Gram entries of the polynomial orbit in
(mathcal H_\Phi). Cumulants are nonlinear combinations of those moments.
Inverse-zero moments (S_r) are logarithmic-divisor data. Therefore the
three towers

\[
 \text{raw source moments},\qquad
 \text{source cumulants},\qquad
 \text{inverse-zero moments}
\]

must not be identified merely because their first determinants have matching
coefficients under the desired product representation.

The remaining bridge theorem must construct a transport-compatible map from
the completed source tower to the primitive Weil or inverse-zero tower. Its
positivity cannot be imported from the raw moment Gram matrix.

## 5. Possible finite objects

The infinite-closure theorem leaves only two ways a finite matrix can acquire
intrinsic meaning:

1. a source-derived quotient on which a polynomial relation for (T) is
   proved; or
2. a finite observation fiber that is explicitly a truncation of the full
   tower and carries no closure claim.

For the theta density, ordinary polynomial probes admit no nontrivial finite
transport quotient by support alone. Any finite quotient must therefore come
from additional adelic boundary relations, a radical of the Weil form, or a
cohomological construction.

## 7. Falsifier and next target

The infinite-closure theorem is falsified by a nonzero polynomial probe whose
(M_{u^2})-orbit satisfies a finite linear relation in
(L^2(\Phi,du)). Positivity of the theta source on an interval excludes this.

The next target is not a larger finite Hankel census. It is the construction
of the source-derived map

\[
 \overline{\mathbb C[u^2]}^{L^2(\Phi du)}
 \longrightarrow
 \mathcal H_{\mathrm{Weil}}^{\mathrm{rel}}
\]

or a proof that no positive transport-intertwining version of this map can
exist. Only its radical or supported quotient could turn the infinite source
tower into an RH-bearing spectral object.
