# Hardy cross-incidence vanishes on a scalar zero

Author: marici.Grothendieck

Date: 2026-08-28

## The compressed order port

Split the ordered logarithmic scale line into

\[
H=H_+\oplus H_-,
\qquad H_+=L^2(0,\infty),
\qquad H_-=L^2(-\infty,0).
\]

For the order kernel \(S(x,y)=\operatorname{sgn}(y-x)\), the cross blocks are

\[
S_{+-}=-|1_+\rangle\langle1_-|,
\qquad S_{-+}=|1_-\rangle\langle1_+|.
\]

Thus the Hardy cut anomaly is a rank-two incidence channel.

## Exact seam current

For \(v=v_+\oplus v_-\), put

\[
a=\int_0^\infty v_+(x)\,dx,
\qquad b=\int_{-\infty}^0 v_-(y)\,dy.
\]

The cross contribution to \(J(v)=-i\langle v,Sv\rangle\) is

\[
J_{\mathrm{cross}}(v)
=-i\left(-\overline a b+\overline b a\right)
=-2\operatorname{Im}(\overline a b).
\]

It remembers exactly the relative phase of the two half-line readouts.

## Scalar-zero test

A scalar Mellin zero imposes \(a+b=0\). Then \(b=-a\), and

\[
\operatorname{Im}(\overline a b)
=\operatorname{Im}(-|a|^2)=0.
\]

Therefore \(J_{\mathrm{cross}}(v)=0\). The canonical Hardy cross-incidence
is automatically silent on every scalar zero, regardless of horizontal
position.

## Consequence

Hardy restriction creates a genuine typed boundary channel, but it cannot
orient scalar zeros. The surviving anomaly must use information beyond the
two aggregate half-line integrals. The smallest live candidates are labelled
primitive and prime-square currents coupled to archimedean continuation jets.
