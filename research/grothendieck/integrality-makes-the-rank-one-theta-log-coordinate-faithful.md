## One collective coordinate

The theta observation depends on the valuation label only through

\[
Q(k)=\sum_pk_p\log p.
\]

Its differential and Hessian in label directions therefore have ranks at most
one. In a continuous label space, every perturbation (v\) satisfying

\[
\sum_pv_p\log p=0
\]

is invisible to the theta observation to all orders, because it leaves (Q\)
unchanged exactly.

## Faithfulness on the arithmetic lattice

Let (k\) and \(\ell\) be finite-support vectors in \(\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^{(P)}\).
If

\[
Q(k)=Q(\ell),
\]

then exponentiation gives

\[
\prod_pp^{k_p}=\prod_pp^{\ell_p}.
\]

Unique factorization forces

\[
k_p=\ell_p
\]

for every prime (p\). Thus the rank-one collective coordinate is injective on
the labelled arithmetic source even though it is highly noninjective on its
continuous linear span.

## Where completion can lose meaning

The same formula therefore has two different information types:

- on integral finite-support valuation packets, (Q\) is a faithful source

- after continuous linearization, (Q\) is a quotient with a large invisible
  kernel.

This is an exact mechanism by which completion can manufacture internal dark
directions without changing the scalar observation formula. The loss is not
caused by theta aggregation alone. It occurs when the completion admits label
directions no longer constrained by arithmetic integrality.

not literally a real vector-space relaxation of its labels. One must derive
the actual completed coefficient module and its topology. The continuous
kernel above is therefore a precise hostile model, not yet a theorem that
