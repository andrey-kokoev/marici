# The completed theta half-lines form the canonical pre-scalar two-quadrature lift

## Source construction

Let \(\Phi(u)\) be the fixed real, even completed theta source, and write

\[
X(z)=\int_{\mathbb R}\Phi(u)e^{zu}\,du.
\]

Split the source contour before applying its even scalar readout:

\[
H_+(z)=\int_0^\infty\Phi(u)e^{zu}\,du,
\qquad
H_-(z)=\int_{-\infty}^0\Phi(u)e^{zu}\,du.
\]

Evenness of \(\Phi\) gives

\[
H_-(z)=H_+(-z).
\]

This split uses the existing reciprocal source sectors and does not construct
an operator backward from \(X\).

## Faithful quadratures

Define

\[
T(z)=H_+(z)+H_-(z),
\qquad
A(z)=H_+(z)-H_-(z).
\]

Then

\[
T(z)=X(z),
\]

while \(A\) is the odd comparison channel. The half-line amplitudes are
recovered linearly:

\[
H_+=\frac{T+A}{2},
\qquad
H_-=\frac{T-A}{2}.
\]

Thus \((T,A)\) is a faithful pre-scalar lift of the completed section.

## Seam interpretation

For \(z=it\), reality of \(\Phi\) gives

\[
H_-(it)=\overline{H_+(it)}.
\]

Consequently,

\[
T(it)=2\Re H_+(it),
\qquad
A(it)=2i\Im H_+(it),
\]

and

\[
|T(it)|^2+|A(it)|^2=4|H_+(it)|^2.
\]

The two source sectors become the two real quadratures of one half-line
amplitude on the seam.

## Meaning of a scalar zero

If

\[
X(z)=T(z)=0,
\]

then \(H_-(z)=-H_+(z)\), and therefore

\[
A(z)=2H_+(z).
\]

A scalar zero is generically an antisymmetric relationship, not a null full
state. The full two-channel object vanishes only if

\[
H_+(z)=H_+(-z)=0.
\]

This is the precise transversality condition hidden by scalar completion.

## Hostile multiplier discrimination

An even hostile multiplier can alter \(X\) while preserving scalar symmetry.
It does not automatically supply a new positive even source \(\Phi_H\) whose
two half-line transforms realize both \(H X\) and a compatible odd channel.

The lift therefore creates a source-provenance test unavailable to scalar
functional-equation data: derive the modified half-line amplitudes from an
authorized source operation before accepting the new divisor.

## What remains: corrected

Strict decrease of the actual theta half-source already proves that the full
pair has no common zero anywhere. This does not exclude scalar zeros: they are
antipodal incidences between two nonzero amplitudes.

The sharp theorem is to prove that the projective route
\([H_+(z):H_+(-z)]\) reaches \([1:-1]\) only on the reciprocal seam.

## Result

The fixed completed theta contour supplies the required pre-scalar
Fourier–Tate lift canonically. Its even quadrature is the completed scalar
section, and its odd quadrature survives every scalar zero. RH-relevant
transversality is the location of one projective antipodal incidence for this
source-derived pair.
