# The doubled-tail zero domain is universal and admits off-seam hostiles

## Bounded question

Does the actual doubled theta-tail domain supply an endpoint condition that
forces a zero-induced summability upgrade to become a star-compatible boundary
state?

## Universal doubled tails

For any rapidly decaying positive half-line source (A), define

\[
G_+(q,z)=\int_q^\infty A(v)e^{izv}\,dv,
\]

and

\[
G_-(q,z)=\int_q^\infty A(v)e^{-izv}\,dv.
\]

They satisfy the source-derived first-order equations

\[
\partial_qG_+(q,z)=-A(q)e^{izq},
\]

\[
\partial_qG_-(q,z)=-A(q)e^{-izq},
\]

with

\[
G_+(\infty,z)=G_-(\infty,z)=0.
\]

The seam values are the two half-line transforms:

\[
G_+(0,z)=H_+(z),
\qquad
G_-(0,z)=H_+(-z).
\]

Hence the completed symmetric scalar readout is

\[
X(z)=G_+(0,z)+G_-(0,z).
\]

## Exact zero-state domain

A scalar zero is therefore equivalent to the single seam-incidence condition

\[
G_+(0,z)+G_-(0,z)=0,
\]

together with the two automatic tail conditions at infinity.

This is a genuine zero-to-state bridge, but it is universal: no theta label,
prime current, Gaussian annihilator, or arithmetic constructor entered its
derivation. Homogenizing the two inhomogeneous equations with constant
channels does not change that fact.

Requiring instead

\[
G_+(0,z)=G_-(0,z)=0
\]

would be a stronger domain not equivalent to a zero of (X). It cannot be
introduced as a hidden endpoint condition.

## Hostile state lies in the domain

Use the positive two-shell source

\[
A=a\delta_1+b\delta_2,
\qquad
b=a\frac{\cosh y}{\cosh 2y},
\]

at

\[
z=\pi+iy,
\qquad
y\ne0.
\]

Its symmetric readout vanishes. Its two tails solve the displayed first-order
system, vanish at infinity, and satisfy the exact seam-incidence condition.
Nevertheless reciprocal transport is not Hilbert adjunction because (z) is
off the real seam.

Positive smooth narrow bumps give the same conclusion after a small parameter
adjustment. Thus the failure is not caused by distributional atoms.

## Staircase augmentation does not repair the domain

Multiplying the tail equations by the arithmetic staircase and integrating by
parts adds the independent prime-power jump current, but the hostile source
still satisfies the same identity. The scalar zero fixes one seam sum; it does
not impose the star relation on the two tails or on their staircase images.

## Result

The doubled-tail/Fredholm realization supplies no extra zero-state boundary
condition beyond the scalar equation itself. It cannot derive adjoint
compatibility.

This closes the proposed tail-domain shortcut. The statement that every
zero-state in this domain is star-compatible is equivalent to adding the
missing confinement law, not deriving it.

## Remaining source discriminator

The next admissible operation must fail for the hostile source before its zero
is inspected. The first such candidate still standing is the exact positive
prime-exclusion recursion

\[
A(u)=A_{p\nmid}(u)+p^{-1/2}A(u+\log p)
\]

for every prime (p), with all primitive remainders jointly compatible. Any
future zero-state theorem must use this labelled recursion or another equally
specific theta constructor, rather than the universal tail domain.
