# A positive Fourier-fixed three-coset packet defeats scalar source rigidity

## The smallest nontrivial rational orbit

Take denominator three and define

\[
P_+=\Delta_{\mathbb Z+1/3},
\qquad
P_-=\Delta_{\mathbb Z-1/3},
\]

\[
C_+=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}e^{2\pi i n/3}\delta_n,
\qquad
C_-=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}e^{-2\pi i n/3}\delta_n.
\]

The complete four-port orbit sum is

\[
E=P_++P_-+C_++C_-.
\]

Fourier transport permutes its four summands, so

\[
\mathcal FE=E.
\]

The character pair is real on integer support:

\[
C_++C_-
=2\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}\cos(2\pi n/3)\delta_n.
\]

## A positive hostile source

For (0<\varepsilon<1), set

\[
\mu_\varepsilon
=\Delta_{\mathbb Z}+\varepsilon E.
\]

At an integer divisible by three, its weight is

\[
1+2\varepsilon.
\]

At either other integer residue class, its weight is

\[
1-\varepsilon.
\]

At every point of the shifted cosets \(\mathbb Z\pm1/3\), its weight is
\(\varepsilon\). Therefore \(\mu_\varepsilon\) is a positive measure, and

\[
\mathcal F\mu_\varepsilon=\mu_\varepsilon.
\]

This is an explicit nontrivial positive Fourier-fixed rational-coset source.

## Normalization does not remove it

The mean mass per unit interval of the weighted integer component is one,
because the character pair has zero mean over the three residue classes. The
two shifted support combs contribute total mean mass \(2\varepsilon\). Hence
the total mean density is

\[
1+2\varepsilon.
\]

The atom at the origin also has weight \(1+2\varepsilon\). Consequently the
renormalized measure

\[
\nu_\varepsilon
=\frac1{1+2\varepsilon}\mu_\varepsilon
\]

is still positive and Fourier-fixed, while having both unit mean density and
unit origin weight.

Thus these three properties do not characterize the theta comb:

1. positivity;
2. Fourier self-duality;
3. matching continuum density and origin normalization.

This is a direct falsifier of scalar source rigidity based only on those
attributes.

## The property that fails

The hostile packet does not preserve the original one-sided quantization
incidence. The integer source assigns exactly one distinguished right endpoint
to each unit Haar cell. The three-coset source has additional endpoints at
fractional positions and residue-dependent weights on the original endpoints.

Therefore it cannot be generated by the same local constructor

\[
[n-1,n]\longrightarrow n
\]

with unit multiplicity. Its positivity is measure positivity after orbit
assembly, not positivity of the original one-endpoint-per-cell transport.

The missing source invariant is consequently more specific than positive
self-duality. It is the incidence-preserving quantization rule relating Haar
cells, fractional coordinates, and distinguished endpoints.

## Why this matters for RH

The completed scalar transform of \(\nu_\varepsilon\) will inherit a Fourier
functional equation and the usual leading normalization, yet it belongs to a
larger three-coset source type. Its zero geometry need not match the Riemann
case.

This supplies the strongest hostile programme so far:

1. derive its completed Mellin section with every coset port retained;
2. determine whether its scalar compression has off-seam zeros;
3. identify the first zero-sensitive statement that fails at the local
   incidence port rather than at positivity or self-duality;

If off-seam zeros occur, the packet will explain exactly why Fourier symmetry
and positive source measures are insufficient. If they do not, the rational
coset category may contain a larger real-zero class worth classifying.

## Multi-tower interpretation

The hostile source passes the aggregate source, Fourier-control, and boundary
normalization checks. It fails only when the input incidence tower is retained
at full resolution.

This is the requested higher-wall phenomenon: a coherence-complete four-port
object can still be illegitimate relative to the original constructor because
its cell-to-endpoint comparison differs. Coherence of transport does not imply
identity of source incidence.
