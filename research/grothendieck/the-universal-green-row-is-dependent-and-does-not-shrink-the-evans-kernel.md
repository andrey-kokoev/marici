# The Universal Green Row Is Dependent and Does Not Shrink the Evans Kernel

## Candidate independent relation

The common constructor graph needs an additional source relation before its
scalar Evans kernel can descend to full carrier cancellation. The first
candidate is the tail Green identity.

For the finite terminal-value system

\[
G'(q)+zG(q)+f(q)=0,
\qquad
G(L)=0,
\]

the elementary energy identity is

\[
\frac{d}{dq}|G|^2
=-2\operatorname{Re}(z)|G|^2
-2\operatorname{Re}(f\overline G).
\]

This identity is obtained by multiplying the tail equation by
\(\overline G\) and adding its conjugate. It therefore belongs to the
differential ideal generated by the constructor residual. It is not an
independent row.

## Zero-state specialization

At an Evans zero, the terminal solution also satisfies \(G(0)=0\). Integrating
the Green identity gives

\[
\operatorname{Re}(z)\int_0^L|G|^2\,dq
=-\operatorname{Re}\int_0^Lf\overline G\,dq.
\]

The right-hand side is an indefinite forcing pairing. Nothing makes it vanish
for a generic source. Hence this relation balances an off-seam zero-state; it
does not contradict one.

## Exact hostile polynomial

Take \(L=1\), \(z=1\), and

\[
G(q)=q(1-q).
\]

Define the source from the forward tail constructor:

\[
f(q)=-G'(q)-G(q)=q^2+q-1.
\]

Then

\[
G'+G+f=0,
\qquad
G(0)=G(1)=0.
\]

Thus the finite transform has a genuine off-seam endpoint zero while the tail
state is nonzero. Exact integration gives

\[
\int_0^1G(q)^2\,dq=\frac1{30},
\qquad
\int_0^1f(q)G(q)\,dq=-\frac1{30}.
\]

The Green identity holds perfectly:

\[
\int_0^1G^2+\int_0^1fG=0.
\]

Adding this identity to the constructor graph leaves the hostile zero-state
untouched.

## Categorical consequence

A relation derived solely by pairing the tail residual with the tail state is
a coherence witness for the existing constructor, not a new admissibility
condition. In row-module language it is a quadratic syzygy of the tail row.
It cannot increase the source rank.

The doubled and Clark-differentiated Green identities inherit the same
limitation until modular sewing supplies an additional boundary law not
generated by the one-sided tail equation.

The needed relation must therefore contain genuinely new source information,
for example:

- a modular boundary current whose derivative equals the forcing pairing;
- a product-formula charge constraint coupling distinct places;
- a reciprocal matching condition between independently constructed sectors;
- a second source observer not generated by the first tail equation.

Merely rewriting the forcing term as a named residual does not create such a
relation.

## Independence test

For any proposed coherence row \(K_X\), perform two checks:

1. reduce \(K_X\) modulo the differential or algebraic ideal generated by the
   constructor graph rows;
2. evaluate the remainder on an exact hostile endpoint-zero solution.

If the remainder is zero identically, the row is dependent and cannot shrink
the kernel. If it is nonzero on the hostile solution and is independently
source-derived, it is a genuine new constraint.

## Scope

This closes the universal one-sided Green identity as the missing independent
row. It does not close a modularly completed Green law, construct the
arithmetic boundary current, establish kernel descent, confine zeros, or prove
RH.
