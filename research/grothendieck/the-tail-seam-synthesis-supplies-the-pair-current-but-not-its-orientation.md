# The Tail–Seam Synthesis Supplies the Pair Current but Not Its Orientation

## The missing pair current is already present

Let `u_n` be the complete tail–seam atom at logarithmic label `log n`, and let

\[
Uc=\sum_n c_nu_n.
\]

The source-derived pair current is the Gram operator

\[
K=U^*U,
\qquad
K(n,m)=\langle u_n,u_m\rangle.
\]

Translation covariance of the source gives

\[
K(n,m)=A\!\left(\left|\log\frac nm\right|\right).
\]

Consequently the seam period of a finite labelled packet is

\[
\mathcal P(c)=\langle c,Kc\rangle
=\sum_{n,m}\overline{c_n}c_mK(n,m).
\]

This contains exactly the off-diagonal interference absent from a list of
one-label energies.

## Division of explanatory labor

Positive Fock formation determines which arithmetic labels and occupations
exist. It does not by itself determine their analytic overlap. The tail–seam
synthesis `U` supplies that overlap, and polarization produces the pair
current.

Thus the relevant constructor is not

`Fock coproduct alone -> pair current`,

but

`labelled Fock packet -> tail–seam synthesis -> Gram polarization`.

This is source-derived and does not require fitting the desired scalar zero.

## What remains absent

Prime multiplication preserves logarithmic differences:

\[
K(pn,pm)=K(n,m).
\]

Therefore the unweighted Gram current is invariant under simultaneous prime
transport. It does not supply the modular factor `p` needed to orient the two
reciprocal sectors around the half-density seam.

The previously proposed weighted kernel

\[
K_{1/2}(n,m)=\sqrt{nm}\,K(n,m)
\]

has the desired covariance, but the half-density gauge audit showed that this
weight is not yet authorized as an energy of the original source. A consistent
basis change can transport it contragrediently.

## Result

The pair-label rung of the final comparison tower is available and faithful:
it is `U*U`. The unresolved rung is its mate with the endpoint homotopies and
the additive-to-multiplicative Haar comparison. In particular:

- pairwise observability is no longer missing;
- modular orientation is still missing;
- those are distinct claims and must not be conflated.

The next theorem must derive a relative pairing between the additive-Haar and
multiplicative-Haar sectors in which the half-density factor is invariant
content rather than removable gauge.
