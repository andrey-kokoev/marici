# The autocorrelation orientation is forced negative at high frequency

Let the positive completed theta forcing be (f), and define

\[
A_f(d)=\int_0^\infty e^{-q}f(q)f(q+d)\,dq,
\qquad
W_x(d)=A_f(d)e^{-d/2}\frac{\sinh(xd)}{x}.
\]

The proposed zero-confinement shortcut was nonnegativity of

\[
I_x(y)=\int_0^\infty W_x(d)\cos(yd)\,dd
\]

for every real (y). The completed theta source is smooth and
superexponentially decreasing, so the required derivatives are integrable on
every fixed admitted (x)-strip.

## Endpoint theorem

The hyperbolic factor vanishes simply at coincidence. Hence

\[
W_x(0)=0,
\qquad
W_x'(0)=A_f(0)=\int_0^\infty e^{-q}f(q)^2\,dq>0.
\]

Twice integrating by parts gives

\[
I_x(y)=-\frac{W_x'(0)}{y^2}
-\frac1{y^2}\int_0^\infty W_x''(d)\cos(yd)\,dd.
\]

The Riemann--Lebesgue lemma therefore yields

\[
I_x(y)=-\frac{A_f(0)}{y^2}+o(y^{-2}).
\]

Consequently (I_x(y)<0) for all sufficiently large |(y)|. This applies
to the actual completed theta source, not merely to a generic two-packet
hostile.

## Consequence

At a paired scalar zero, the reciprocal Green identity reads

\[
E_z+E_{-\overline z}=-2I_x(y).
\]

The negative high-frequency sign is compatible with the positive left side.
Thus direct nonnegativity of the scalar autocorrelation transform cannot
exclude high-frequency off-seam zero-states.

This does not produce an off-line zero and does not disprove RH. It closes one
proof architecture. Any surviving conservation argument must change the
object before scalar compression: it must retain a typed channel that pairs
the universal coincidence germ, or use a zero-state relation unavailable to
the unrestricted transform.

The explanatory mechanism is local. Reciprocal subtraction inserts a simple
separation factor, and Fourier transformation turns its positive endpoint
slope into a negative spectral tail. Arithmetic correlations at nonzero
separation cannot change the leading coefficient.

> A positive first-order coincidence germ necessarily produces a negative
> high-frequency cosine tail.
