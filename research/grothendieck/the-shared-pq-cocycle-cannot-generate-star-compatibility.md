# The shared pq cocycle cannot generate star compatibility

## The two paths have different variance

For a real half-line source, the interval port is

\[
B_\ell(z)=\int_0^\ell A(v)e^{izv}\,dv.
\]

Prime translation and shared-corner composition are complex-linear. They
produce identities among `B` evaluated at `z` and among shifted source
windows. Reciprocal transport is also holomorphic in the parameter:

\[
B_\ell(z)\longmapsto B_\ell(-z).
\]

Hilbert adjunction has different variance:

\[
\overline{B_\ell(z)}=B_\ell(-\overline z).
\]

The star defect

\[
\mathfrak S_\ell(z)
=
B_\ell(-z)-B_\ell(-\overline z)
\]

therefore compares a complex-linear path with a conjugate-linear path. The
shared `pq` seam cocycle contains no conjugate-linear constructor. It can prove
commutation of two translation orders, but it cannot generate this equality.

## Exact positive hostile

Take the positive source

\[
A=\delta_0+2\delta_1
\]

and its Fourier--Laplace readout

\[
F(z)=1+2e^{iz}.
\]

At

\[
z_0=\pi+i\log2,
\]

one has `e^(i z_0)=-1/2`, so `F(z_0)=0`. This point is off the centered real
seam.

For every interval length greater than one, the interval port contains both
atoms. At the reciprocal and adjoint parameters,

\[
B_\ell(-z_0)=1+2e^{-iz_0}=-3,
\]

while

\[
B_\ell(-\overline{z_0})
=1+2e^{-i\overline{z_0}}
=0.
\]

Hence

\[
\mathfrak S_\ell(z_0)=-3.
\]

The same source satisfies every one-window and shared-corner interval
composition identity because those identities hold for every measure. Scalar
nullity plus two-prime seam composition therefore does not force star
compatibility.

## Consequence for the density bridge

Prime-ratio density remains correct and useful: star equality on all
prime-ratio cells would extend to the full interval family and force the seam.
But the existing shared `pq` cocycle does not supply those equalities. The
conditional bridge cannot be closed using complex-linear translation
coherence alone.

## Missing constructor

The next wall must carry Real or adjoint authority before scalarization. It
must be one of:

- a source-derived anti-linear involution on the completed zero-state domain;
- a Hermitian boundary pairing whose two arguments arise from independent
  source paths;
- an adjoint incidence for the primitive and square current maps;
- a reflection-positive localization that rejects the two-atom hostile before
  evaluation.

Merely adjoining more prime translations, shared windows, or holomorphic
jets cannot change variance and therefore cannot create the required star
cell.

## Disposition

The two-prime-composition route to star compatibility is closed. The density
theorem relocates rather than solves the problem: one source-authorized
conjugate-linear comparison at the arithmetic ratio cells would suffice, but
that comparison is a genuinely new constructor.
