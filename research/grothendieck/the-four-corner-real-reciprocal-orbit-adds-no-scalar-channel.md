# The four-corner real–reciprocal orbit adds no scalar channel

## The orbit square

Let (F:z\mapsto-z) be reciprocal reflection and (C:z\mapsto\bar z) be
the real involution. For the real half-line source transform (H), put

\[
a=H(z),
\qquad
b=H(-z).
\]

The four orbit values are

\[
H(z)=a,
\quad
H(-z)=b,
\quad
H(\bar z)=\bar a,
\quad
H(-\bar z)=\bar b.
\]

Thus the (F)-(C) square contains only the two complex amplitudes already
present in the half-line lift.

## No new evaluation port

The even and odd channels

\[
T=a+b,
\qquad
A=a-b
\]

recover (a=(T+A)/2) and (b=(T-A)/2). Their conjugates recover the other
two corners. Consequently any scalar statistic formed only from the four
evaluations factors through ((T,A)).

Adding conjugate evaluations is a realification, not a new faithful port.

## What a scalar zero does to the square

At a completed zero, (b=-a\ne0). The orbit square becomes

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
a&-a\\
\bar a&-\bar a
\end{pmatrix},
\]

which has rank one. This records antipodal scalar cancellation, but it does
not distinguish a seam zero from a hostile off-seam zero.

The determinant

\[
a\bar b-b\bar a
=2i\Im(a\bar b)
\]

vanishes whenever (a/b) is real, a much larger locus than the antipode.
It therefore supplies no zero-confinement theorem by itself.

## The genuinely missing cell

At the scalar-function level, (F) and (C) commute automatically:

\[
FC=CF.
\]

The missing information cannot be another corner value. It must be a
source-level comparison between the two constructor paths

The two paths are (F\circ C) and (C\circ F).

before aggregation. In categorical language, the candidate new datum is a
coherent 2-cell, or a typed anomaly when the square fails to commute strictly

This makes the next test precise:

1. lift (F) and (C) to the labelled theta/Tate source packet;
2. compute both composites with primitive, square, seam, and archimedean

3. identify their comparison 2-cell;
4. determine whether its value distinguishes the antipode on and off the
   seam;
5. reject the construction if the 2-cell becomes identity before scalar

## Result

The fourth wall is not a fourth scalar amplitude. The four-corner orbit is
already the realification of the two-channel lift. The next nonredundant rung
is coherence between constructor paths.
