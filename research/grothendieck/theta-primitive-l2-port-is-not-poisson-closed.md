# Theta primitive L2 port is not Poisson closed

## The tempting next map

Packet 196 isolates a canonical primitive vector

\[
 P(s)=(p^{-s})_{p\in\mathbb P}
 \in\ell^2(\mathbb P)
\]

for \(\operatorname{Re}s>1/2\). It is tempting to seek Fourier--Tate sewing
directly on this prime-labelled Hilbert port.

That operation is not source-authorized.

## Support obstruction

Poisson sewing acts on the complete additive lattice comb. The integer comb
is Fourier-stable because \(\mathbb Z\) is a lattice and its annihilator is
again a lattice. The primes are neither an additive subgroup nor a lattice.

Consequently, the Fourier transform of a prime-supported distribution such
as

\[
 \mu_{\mathbb P}=\sum_p c_p\delta_p
\]

is not another prime-supported distribution. In the ordinary real Fourier
coordinate it is

\[
 \widehat\mu_{\mathbb P}(\xi)=\sum_p c_p e^{-2\pi i p\xi},
\]

which is a global oscillatory distribution rather than an element of the
same prime-labelled sequence space.

There is therefore no source-derived endomorphism

\[
 \mathcal F_{\mathbb P}:\ell^2(\mathbb P)\longrightarrow\ell^2(\mathbb P)
\]

obtained by restricting the additive Fourier transform.

## Forced operation order

The authorized chain is:

1. form the aggregate integer or adelic source;
2. perform additive Poisson/Fourier--Tate completion with all endpoint data;
3. form the completed determinant or scalar section;
4. apply the determinant-line logarithm;
5. read the connected prime-power current.

Reversing steps 2 and 4 discards the composite integer channels needed for
Fourier closure. It leaves a prime port with no native reciprocal sewing.

Thus packet 196 identifies the location of the scalar discontinuity, but its
primitive \(\ell^2\) port is a downstream diagnostic of the completed
determinant line. It is not the pre-completion full block.

## Consequence for the RH construction

The smallest admissible full block must retain the aggregate theta lattice,
its seam and archimedean boundary data, and the two reciprocal sectors before
the connected transform. The primitive, square, and higher currents may then
appear as graded outputs of the determinant logarithm.

A direct prime-space parametrix would be fitted unless an independent
multiplicative Fourier transform and its comparison with additive Poisson
sewing are derived from the source.

## Falsifier

This obstruction is overturned by an explicit source-derived transform on
the prime-labelled port that:

1. closes on \(\ell^2(\mathbb P)\);
2. is induced from the actual additive Fourier--Tate operation;
3. reconstructs the finite aggregate Poisson identities; and
4. retains composite and endpoint information without adding it by hand.

Merely defining an abstract unitary matrix on prime labels does not meet the
test.

## Scope

This proves an operation-ordering obstruction. It does not deny the value of
the primitive Hilbert port after the connected transform, nor does it
construct the required aggregate full block.
