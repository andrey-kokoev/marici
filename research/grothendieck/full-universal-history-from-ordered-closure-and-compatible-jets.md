# Full universal history from ordered closure and compatible jets

## Construction

Fix the admitted chamber object V over Q, or over C after scalar extension. Construct

T(V)=direct_sum_(r>=0) V^(tensor r),

with V^(tensor 0)=the scalar unit and multiplication by chronological tensor concatenation. Complete in the positive-degree filtration:

H(V)=product_(r>=0) V^(tensor r).

This is the full universal filtered history. Finite records are its quotients H(V)/F^(N+1), rather than a separately chosen record law at each depth. The topology here is the formal degree topology; no analytic Hilbert norm or positivity is implied.

## 1. Ordered closure supplies the multiplication

For h=(h_0,h_1,...) and k=(k_0,k_1,...), define

(h k)_n=sum_(a+b=n) h_a tensor k_b.

Every coefficient is a finite sum. Associativity follows by identifying both triple products in degree n with the sum over a+b+c=n. The unit is (1,0,...).

Let F^r H consist of histories with degrees below r zero. Then F^a H F^b H is contained in F^(a+b) H. This is the algebraic expression of adjoining recorded events in order.

The arithmetic endpoint is retained separately. A route from n to m evaluates into the Hom block labelled (n,m); the tensor coefficient alone does not replace those source/target labels.

## 2. Necessity theorem: the universal property

A receiver is a unital augmented algebra A with a complete separated decreasing filtration, F^a A F^b A contained in F^(a+b) A, and a linear chamber map ell:V->F^1 A. Morphisms are continuous filtered unital algebra maps preserving the declared chamber maps.

For every such receiver there is exactly one morphism

Eval_ell:H(V)->A.

On a finite tensor,

Eval_ell(v_1 tensor ... tensor v_r)=ell(v_1)...ell(v_r).

For a full history its value is the limit of the partial degree sums. Degree-r terms land in F^r A, so these partial sums are Cauchy; completeness gives existence. Multiplication commutes with the limit because it respects the filtration. Uniqueness holds on finite tensor polynomials because they are generated by V, then on the completion because polynomials are dense and the receiver is separated.

Thus H(V) is initial in this category of filtered record realizations. This is a proved uniqueness theorem relative to the stated record category. The underlying polymorphic cofiber operator still needs that category of realizations specified; it does not select a scalar field or a filtered associative receiver without a realization definition.

The uncompleted T(V) has the corresponding universal property for all unital associative algebras without the convergence requirement. H(V) is its canonical degree completion.

## 3. The compatible jet tower

Let H_N=H/F^(N+1)=direct_sum_(r=0)^N V^(tensor r), with multiplication truncated after degree N. Projection pi_N:H_(N+1)->H_N drops only the last component.

The map H->inverse_limit_N H_N sends a full sequence to its prefixes. Conversely a compatible tower of prefixes determines each coefficient by choosing any prefix long enough to contain it. These constructions are inverse and multiplicative. Therefore

H(V) is isomorphic to inverse_limit_N H_N.

Degree four is now a canonical quotient of the one full object. It is adequate for the forward four-event packet because the constructed event product is polynomial of degree at most four. Comparison inverses are generally infinite and are represented by compatible inverse jets.

## 4. Atomic event and its inverse

The natural linear record argument from the preceding packet gives the single-event record

U(v)=1+v.

For a source word w with chamber increments v_1,...,v_k,

S_w=(1+v_1)...(1+v_k).

Expansion gives the event-segmented signature in every degree. The degree-r coefficient is zero for r>k, so the finite forward histories embed into the full object without losing any coefficient.

Every augmentation-one history 1+a, a in F^1 H, has the convergent formal inverse

(1+a)^-1=sum_(r>=0)(-a)^r.

The remainder after degree-order N lies in F^(N+1). This proves both inverse identities. In particular event inverses retain arbitrarily high powers in the full history. At each quotient they reduce to the finite geometric inverse already used in the arithmetic adapter.

The same formula applies to S_w and gives its unique continuous group of comparison units.

## 5. Generated comparison and its receivers

For route presentations w,w', define C_(w,w') by right multiplication with S_w^-1 S_w'. Then

C_(w,w')(S_w)=S_w',

C_(w',w'') composed C_(w,w')=C_(w,w''),

C_(w,w)=identity.

These identities hold degree by degree and hence in the completion. Every Eval_ell transports them to the receiver, since filtered algebra maps preserve augmentation-one inverses. Each pi_N transports them to the finite jet chart.

Thus the arithmetic history, its comparison maps, its finite records, and its receiver realizations are generated by one universal object. No new comparison coefficients are selected at successive jet orders.

## 6. Cutting the recorded words

There is also a canonical continuous deconcatenation map. On a tensor word,

Delta(v_1...v_n)=sum_(j=0)^n (v_1...v_j) tensor (v_(j+1)...v_n).

The codomain is the degree-completed double tensor space. Each bidegree receives a finite expression, so the map is well-defined. Splitting a word at two positions proves coassociativity. Projection to degree zero is the counit.

Cutting and joining satisfy

Delta(a b)=(a tensor 1) Delta(b)+Delta(a)(1 tensor b)-a tensor b.

Indeed a cut in the concatenated word belongs either to a or to b; the common join is counted twice and is subtracted once. The identity extends linearly and continuously. This is an explicit cut/rejoin compatibility law on the record realization.

Delta is not asserted to be an algebra homomorphism: a naive bialgebra identity adds extra crossed cuts. Also, cuts of the recorded letters differ from all physical positions in a source word containing forgotten events. Multiple source cuts can lie in the same forgotten gap. A full comparison with the original decomposition-space comultiplication must retain or account for that cut multiplicity. The present statement defines the full record and its own exact cut law; it does not silently identify these two coalgebras.

The follow-up `forgotten-gap-localization-reconciles-source-and-record-cuts.md` constructs this comparison for marked route ordinals: forgotten-gap cut fibers contract after localization, compatibly with multiple cuts and further forgetting. It also proves that raw source cut counts are not preserved and that retaining arithmetic endpoints requires additional attachment data or an admitted equivalence target.

## 7. An intrinsic characterization of a one-event increment

For this record cut map, primitive elements satisfy Delta(a)=1 tensor a+a tensor 1. Its primitive subspace is exactly V: for every degree n>=2, the (1,n-1) component of deconcatenation identifies that entire degree component, so primitiveness forces it to vanish. The scalar component vanishes as well.

Hence an atomic event required to have no internal recorded cut has primitive increment U-1. Fixing its first-degree chamber incidence then forces U=1+v. This gives a second necessity argument, phrased in cut data rather than scalar dilation.

It requires that an atomic arithmetic event be represented as an indivisible recorded event. Its numerical window may contain several chamber intervals; they are linear coordinates of one event, not automatically several successive prime events. The local atomic-cut specification makes that distinction explicit.

## 8. Arithmetic realization

Insert the source map (n,p)->v(n,p) from the independently defined prime-window restriction. The degree-four quotient agrees with the established 168-path implementation and its selected reconstruction matrix.

Higher source packets use the same construction with their chamber objects and source maps. A linear refinement V->V' induces degreewise tensor maps H(V)->H(V'), commuting with all finite projections, event recording, and comparison inverses. A change of source cutoff requires its actual chamber refinement map; the universal construction then supplies its lift.

A commutative receiver can forget order even though the universal source retains it. Faithfulness of a chosen analytical realization remains a property to prove, rather than a consequence of its existence by universality.
