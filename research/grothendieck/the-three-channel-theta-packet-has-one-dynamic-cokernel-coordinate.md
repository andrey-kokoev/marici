# The three-channel theta packet has one dynamic cokernel coordinate

## Exact coordinate reduction

The completed theta packet has three typed channels:

\[
(C,U,V),
\qquad C=\frac12,
\qquad X=C+U+V.
\]

Define the reciprocal-antisymmetric coordinate

\[
A=U-V.
\]

Because (C) is fixed, the pair ((X,A)) recovers the full packet:

\[
U=\frac{X-C+A}{2},
\qquad
V=\frac{X-C-A}{2}.
\]

Thus the apparently three-dimensional packet has exactly two dynamic
coordinates, one observed and one transverse.

## Zero-fiber theorem

On the scalar zero fiber,

\[
X=0,
\]

the entire dynamic packet is determined by the single surviving coordinate
(A):

\[
U=\frac{-C+A}{2},
\qquad
V=\frac{-C-A}{2}.
\]

This is the precise cokernel content of a theta zero. The scalar channel
vanishes, the control channel remains fixed, and one antisymmetric sector
comparison remains free.

## Static-port saturation

Let (L(C,U,V)) be any further static linear port. After the coordinate
change it has the form

\[
L=\alpha X+\beta A+\gamma C.
\]

Restricting to (X=0) gives

\[
L=\beta A+\gamma C.
\]

Therefore no family of additional static linear ports can reveal another
independent dynamic coordinate on the zero fiber. They only remeasure (A)
and the already fixed control value.

This is stronger than saying that one proposed monitor failed. The
three-channel decomposition is statically saturated.

## Consequence for the coherence tower

The missing next rung cannot be another value wall or another static
comparison channel. It must act on the surviving coordinate. Viable types
are:

- a transport or history law for (A);
- a derivative or Green current coupling (A) to scale evolution;
- a nonlinear invariant whose zero set restricts (A);
- a higher coherence comparing the direct and reciprocal evolutions of (A).

The already derived Ward current is correctly typed as the first dynamic
candidate. Its identity remains kinematic at both seam and off-seam zeros, so
one more static port cannot repair it. A genuine control law must constrain
the evolution of (A) on the source-admissible zero-state locus.

## Minimal hostile

Any proposed next-rung theorem that depends only on instantaneous linear
values of (C,U,V) is falsified by two packets having the same (C), the same
(X=0), and arbitrary distinct values of (A). Distinguishing them requires
transport, derivatives, history, or a nonlinear constructor.

This gives a hard stop to static tower enlargement.
