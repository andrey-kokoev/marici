# Krein transport is conserved parity-weight transfer

## Bounded question

What dynamical quantity is conserved as the transported theta jet approaches
or crosses its reflection-Krein light cone?

## Constant Hilbert norm

For

\[
 F_{n,x}=\Delta^ne^{ix\Sigma/2}\Omega^{\otimes2},
\]

the operators `Delta` and `Sigma` commute. Therefore

\[
 F_{n,x}=e^{ix\Sigma/2}F_{n,0}
\]

and unitarity gives

\[
 \boxed{
 \|F_{n,x}\|^2=\|F_{n,0}\|^2=:N_n.}
\]

Nothing is created or destroyed by spectral transport. It only redistributes
the fixed norm between reflection parities.

## Literal parity probabilities

Using the cosine--sine decomposition from packet 123, define

\[
 p^+_n(x)
 =\frac{\|\Delta^n\cos(x\Sigma/2)\Omega^{\otimes2}\|^2}{N_n},
\]

\[
 p^-_n(x)
 =\frac{\|\Delta^n\sin(x\Sigma/2)\Omega^{\otimes2}\|^2}{N_n}.
\]

They satisfy

\[
 p^+_n(x)+p^-_n(x)=1,
\]

so these are genuine Hilbert-space probabilities, not an analogy.  The Krein
reserve is

\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{(2n)!\mathcal L_n[X](x)}{N_n}
 =p^+_n(x)-p^-_n(x)
 =1-2p^-_n(x).}
\]

Thus the light cone is exactly the balanced point `p_minus=1/2`; negativity is
a majority inversion into the wrong parity channel.

## Spectral-measure form

Let `mu_n` be the normalized spectral measure of `Sigma` in the state
`F_(n,0)`.  Reflection symmetry makes it even, and

\[
 \frac{(2n)!\mathcal L_n[X](x)}{N_n}
 =\int_{\mathbb R}\cos(x\sigma)\,d\mu_n(\sigma).
\]

Consequently

\[
 p^-_n(x)
 =\int_{\mathbb R}\sin^2(x\sigma/2)\,d\mu_n(\sigma).
\]

The RH-relevant hierarchy is now a family of exact majority bounds:

\[
 \boxed{
 \int\sin^2(x\sigma/2)\,d\mu_n(\sigma)\le\frac12
 \quad\text{for every }n,x.}
\]

## Explanation and boundary

This makes the earlier operational intuition precise: finite observations may
retain strictly positive reserve arbitrarily near a cone boundary, while the
limiting support geometry selects the exact seam. But positivity is not
automatic for a probability distribution; a characteristic cosine transform
can become negative.

The missing arithmetic theorem must prohibit parity-majority inversion for
the special measures `mu_n` generated by the integral theta source. The
all-order winding sign-regularity theorem constrains their labelled formation,
but its implication for the majority bound remains unproved.

The smallest falsifier is a pair `(n,x)` for which the exact sine-sector
probability exceeds one half.  This is equivalent to a negative generalized
Laguerre form, but it supplies a conserved dynamical interpretation rather
than merely restating a determinant sign.
