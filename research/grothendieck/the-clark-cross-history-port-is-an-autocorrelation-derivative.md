# The Clark cross-history port is an autocorrelation derivative

## Authorized pair

The theta construction already supplies a canonical pair of differently typed
Clark source histories:

\[
h_a(q)=(1-aq)f(q),
\qquad
h_{-a}(q)=(1+aq)f(q).
\]

They are exchanged by the native sheet involution \(a\mapsto-a\). No second
source has been introduced to manufacture antisymmetry.

Define the genuine exchange-odd cross-history transform

\[
L_{a}(z)
=
\iint h_a(q)h_{-a}(v)
\sinh\!\bigl(z(v-q)\bigr)\,dq\,dv.
\]

## Exact collapse

The product of the two histories is

\[
h_a(q)h_{-a}(v)
=f(q)f(v)\bigl(1+a(v-q)-a^2qv\bigr).
\]

The terms \(1\) and \(qv\) are symmetric under \(q\leftrightarrow v\), while
the hyperbolic sine is antisymmetric. Their double integrals vanish. Therefore

\[
L_a(z)
=a\iint (v-q)f(q)f(v)
\sinh\!\bigl(z(v-q)\bigr)\,dq\,dv.
\]

Let the even autocorrelation transform be

\[
C_f(z)
=\iint f(q)f(v)
\cosh\!\bigl(z(v-q)\bigr)\,dq\,dv.
\]

Differentiation gives the exact identity

\[
L_a(z)=aC_f'(z).
\]

## Interpretation

The Clark pair passes the source-authority and exchange-typing gates. It is
nonzero whenever the source has nontrivial separation. But its cross-history
antisymmetry is not an independent relational port: it is a derivative of the
ordinary autocorrelation channel.

The sheet involution creates a tangent direction inside the same
autocorrelation object. It does not create an additional comparison wall.

## Consequence

Neither of the two currently visible candidates supplies new RH force:

- inserting \(\operatorname{sgn}(v-q)\) makes the kernel exchange-even and
  recovers the absolute-separation transform;
- using the authorized Clark pair makes the kernel exchange-odd but recovers
  \(aC_f'\).

A genuinely new port must involve two histories whose relative deformation is
not generated by multiplication by an affine function of the same scale
coordinate. Equivalently, their cross-correlation must not be determined by
the self-correlation tower of \(f\).

The next admissible place to look is the primal--dual or direct--reciprocal
pair before they are identified by scalar modular sewing. That pair is typed
by distinct constructor paths rather than by opposite tangents of one source.

## Falsifier

For any proposed cross-history port, compute whether it lies in the
differential algebra generated by \(C_f\). If it does, it is another coordinate
on the autocorrelation tower and cannot be counted as an independent
comparison channel.
