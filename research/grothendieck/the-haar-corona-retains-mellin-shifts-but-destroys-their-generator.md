# The Haar Corona Retains Mellin Shifts but Destroys Their Generator

## GNS representation of the corona state

Let \(\omega_M\) be the Haar state from Entry 3937 on the Mellin algebra
generated by

\[
U_te_p=p^{-it}e_p.
\]

Its defining coefficients are

\[
\omega_M(U_t)=
\begin{cases}
1,&t=0,\\
0,&t\ne0.
\end{cases}
\]

Let \((\pi_M,\mathcal H_M,\Omega)\) be the GNS representation.  Then

\[
\langle\pi_M(U_t)\Omega,
\pi_M(U_u)\Omega\rangle
=\omega_M(U_{u-t})
=\delta_{t,u}.
\]

Thus the orbit of the Euler corona vacuum is an orthonormal family indexed by
\(\mathbb R\) with the discrete topology.  The GNS representation is the
regular representation of the discrete group \(\mathbb R_d\), not a
continuous representation of the ordinary real line.

## Failure of strong continuity

For every \(t\ne0\),

\[
\left\|
\pi_M(U_t)\Omega-\Omega
\right\|^2
=2.
\]

Hence

\[
\pi_M(U_t)\Omega
\not\longrightarrow\Omega
\qquad(t\to0).
\]

The Mellin shifts survive individually and remain unitary, but their
one-parameter family is not strongly continuous in the corona GNS topology.
Stone's theorem therefore supplies no self-adjoint infinitesimal generator.

This is the first exact module-closure failure requested after Entry 3937.
Finite vertical transport descends; vertical differentiation does not.

## Consequence for source currents

At finite cutoff the infinitesimal Mellin generator is the logarithmic label
operator

\[
Qe_p=(\log p)e_p.
\]

Clark differentiation, gamma-current comparison, and the earlier position-row
observables all require some form of \(Q\) or of differentiation with respect
to the vertical spectral parameter.  They cannot be continuous modules over
the bare Haar corona representation because the required generator is absent.

This does not mean those currents are illegitimate.  It means the Haar corona
forgets precisely the first-order germ of vertical transport.  A completed
source object that retains both the Euler vacuum and its analytic variation
must contain an additional differentiable boundary channel.

The distinction is exact:

- \(U_t\) is authorized for every fixed \(t\);
- \(U_tU_u=U_{t+u}\) remains exact;
- \(t\mapsto U_t\) becomes discontinuous in the corona state;
- the derivative at \(t=0\) is not represented.

## Interpretation

Prime-harmonic normalization turns finite vertical displacements into mutually
orthogonal sectors.  The corona retains neutral relationship energy,

\[
\omega_M(U_t^*U_t)=1,
\]

but erases every infinitesimal comparison between nearby heights.  This is a
precise loss of meaning: the completed state knows that each transported copy
exists and has unit norm, yet no longer knows that copies at nearby \(t\) were
nearby before completion.

The topology has changed from \(\mathbb R\) to \(\mathbb R_d\).

## Required additional channel

The Mertens finite part and primitive logarithmic current are now naturally
typed as candidates for the missing first-order germ.  They should not alter
the Haar state on finite shifts.  They should extend it to a graph or Sobolev
module carrying a closable derivation

\[
\nabla U_t=-iQU_t.
\]

The next theorem must construct this derivative channel from source cutoff
data and test whether its graph completion restores strong continuity without
reintroducing the divergent Euler norm.

The smallest falsifier is universal.  Any proposed corona module claiming a
strongly continuous Mellin action while retaining the Haar coefficients above
must explain how it avoids

\[
\|U_t\Omega-\Omega\|=\sqrt2
\]

for every nonzero \(t\).  Without an enlarged state space or a different
topology, the claim is impossible.
