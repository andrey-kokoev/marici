# Theta characteristic-model orbit is universal, not RH-selective

## Bounded question

Does closing the Hardy factor through its model contraction, defect ports,
Julia flow, and characteristic transfer impose a theta-specific restriction
on half-plane zeros?

## Canonical contraction after factorization

Given a scalar inner factor \(U\), form the model space

\[
K_U=H^2_+\ominus UH^2_+
\]

and the compressed shift

\[
S_U=P_{K_U}M_z\big|_{K_U},
\]

using the disk model after a fixed Cayley coordinate if desired.

\(S_U\) is a completely nonunitary contraction. Its Sz.-Nagy--Foias
characteristic function is \(U\), up to constant unitary gauges on the incoming
and outgoing defect ports.

Write \(D_U\) for the defect-port packet and \(J_U\) for the Julia
colligation. Thus the orbit

\[
U
\longmapsto
K_U
\longmapsto
S_U
\longmapsto
(D_U,J_U)
\longmapsto
U
\]

closes exactly.

## Why closure is not selective

The construction works for every scalar inner function. In particular, every
finite Blaschke product, including a one-zero hostile factor, produces a
perfectly valid contraction, Julia unitary, and characteristic transfer.

Consequently, commutation of the Julia-flow and characteristic-transfer square
is a functorial coherence theorem of contraction model theory. It does not
distinguish the theta inner factor from a hostile one.

## Defect-port dimension

For a nonconstant scalar inner function, the model contraction has scalar
defect indices

\[
\dim\mathcal D_{S_U}
=
\dim\mathcal D_{S_U^*}
=1,
\]

under the standard pure scalar model hypotheses.

This is independent of

\[
\dim K_U,
\]

which equals the Blaschke degree in the finite case and may be infinite.

Therefore primitive, prime-square, seam, archimedean, and residue currents
cannot be five independent canonical defect ports of the scalar model
contraction. If they enter, they must:

1. map into the same one-dimensional incoming or outgoing defect channel;
2. live as internal labelled states in \(K_U\);
3. define a larger matrix-valued characteristic function;
4. carry boundary data external to the universal contraction model.

Those identifications require source-derived comparison maps.

## Source-authority obstruction

The theta integral canonically gives \(F_+\). The inner symbol \(U\) is obtained
by factoring \(F_+=UO\). Constructing \(S_U\) afterward is analytically
canonical but does not derive \(U\)'s triviality from theta constructors. It
packages the existing inner divisor into a contraction.

A finite theta/Tate contraction would add RH force only if it were built
before scalar Hardy factorization from labelled primitive, square, seam,
archimedean, and residue operations, and its characteristic function were then
proved to equal the theta \(U\).

## Exact finite audit

For a finite Blaschke factor of degree \(m\):

- the model-state dimension is \(m\);
- the two scalar defect spaces each have dimension one;
- the characteristic transfer reproduces the degree-\(m\) factor;
- the Julia colligation is unitary;
- causal leakage remains zero.

Hence all universal gates pass for arbitrary \(m\). The only theta-selective
data could lie in a labelled decomposition of the \(m\)-dimensional internal
state or in restrictions on the allowed characteristic function.

## Relation to causal cyclicity

Causal cyclicity asks whether \(K_U=0\). The universal model orbit begins after
\(K_U\) is admitted and describes it faithfully. It cannot prove that the
space vanishes.

This is the categorical distinction:

- model theory reconstructs the defect;
- RH requires a source law forbidding the defect.

## Result

The turn-10 through counter-11 orbit closes, but its closure is universal for
scalar inner functions and has no RH selectivity. The standard contraction has
only one incoming and one outgoing defect port, irrespective of zero count.
The five theta current classes cannot be declared to span those ports without
source-derived typing maps.

The next legitimate theorem must construct a pre-factorization labelled
contraction or matrix characteristic function directly from theta/Tate
currents and show which inner functions its source grammar permits.

## Sharp falsifier

Apply the proposed construction to a one-zero Blaschke factor. If the
contraction, Julia unitary, defect-port span, and characteristic square all
close, the construction is universal and supplies no zero exclusion. A
theta-selective theory must reject that factor through a labelled source
condition absent from ordinary model theory.
