# The exterior zeta boundary module is the jet pullback of the Todd normalization

## The next pullback

The continued boundary kernel on nonpositive integral probes is not an
unstructured indefinite matrix. Define the multiplicative germ

\[
\tau(t)=\frac{t}{e^t-1}.
\]

Its logarithmic tangent is

\[
L(t)
=
\frac{d}{dt}\log\tau(t)
=
\frac1t-\frac1{1-e^{-t}}.
\]

The Taylor expansion begins

\[
L(t)
=
-\frac12
-\frac1{12}t
+\frac1{720}t^3
-\frac1{30240}t^5
+\cdots.
\]

The exact jet identity is

\[
L^{(n)}(0)=\zeta(-n).
\]

Consequently, for probes \(s_i=-i\), the exterior polarized kernel is

\[
K(s_i,s_j)
=
\zeta(-(i+j))
=
L^{(i+j)}(0).
\]

The entire exterior Hankel packet is therefore the jet prolongation of one
source-derived multiplicative normalization germ.

## Why the previous rank obstruction was informative

The three-probe packet has two negative directions, so one additive scalar
boundary coordinate cannot realize it. The jet identity explains what that
obstruction was asking for. It was not asking for two unrelated corrective
scalars. It was asking us to retain the germ together with its translation
jets.

Thus two statements coexist:

- a single scalar boundary value is insufficient;
- a single multiplicative germ can generate the full higher-rank boundary
  module under jet prolongation.

The distinction is categorical. The boundary object is not a point in a
line; it is a formal neighbourhood carrying translation action.

## Euler–Maclaurin meaning

The germ \(\tau\) is the Todd normalization appearing in the local
Euler–Maclaurin comparison between a lattice sum and its continuum
integral. Heat finite part differentiates its logarithm and thereby passes
from a multiplicative normalization unit to an additive signed boundary
current.

This explains why positivity is lost. Logarithmic differentiation preserves
composition laws, not Gram order. The negative exterior directions are
tangent directions of the normalization cocycle, not negative-norm source
states in the regulated bulk.

## Revised pullback tower

The emerging sequence is:

1. the positive heat-regulated lattice Gram object;
2. its bulk–boundary relative pair;
3. the signed finite-part Hankel kernel;
4. the jet module generating that kernel;
5. the multiplicative Todd normalization germ before logarithmic tangent.

The fifth level is more primitive than the signed kernel. Reciprocal theta
sewing should act on the multiplicative germ or its determinant line before
the logarithmic tangent and scalar evaluation are taken.

## Sharp next question

Does Fourier–Tate reciprocity identify the two sectoral Todd germs by a
multiplicative cocycle whose completed product is a unit off the critical
seam? If so, the additive Green current should be derived by differentiating
that cocycle, rather than guessed from the indefinite finite-part kernel.

The hostile test is equally sharp: multiply the final scalar section by a
symmetric divisor-bearing factor and ask for a lift to an invertible
transformation of the Todd jet module. Failure to lift would detect the
hostile factor before inspecting its zeros.

## Scope

The jet identity is exact. It does not yet supply the reciprocal
multiplicative cocycle, prove its off-seam invertibility, or establish RH.
It identifies the next source level on which those questions should be
asked.

## Result

The exterior zeta boundary module is the logarithmic jet shadow of the Todd
normalization \(t/(e^t-1)\). The required additional pullback is therefore
from the signed additive boundary kernel to its multiplicative formal germ.
The several negative directions are descendants of one transported germ,
not independently introduced walls.
