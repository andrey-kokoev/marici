# The Logarithmic Curvature Is the Prüfer Connection Whose Holonomy Counts Newman Collisions

## Native complex collision section

Define on the real heat--spectral plane

\[
G(\lambda,x)=H_\lambda(x)+i\partial_xH_\lambda(x).
\]

The zeros of \(G\) are exactly the real multiple zeros of the Newman family.
Away from those points, its phase defines the connection one-form

\[
\omega=d\arg G.
\]

This is source-derived before any collision is located.

## Spatial component is logarithmic curvature

Direct calculation gives

\[
\partial_x\arg G
=
\frac{H H_{xx}-H_x^2}{H^2+H_x^2}.
\]

The numerator is exactly the order-two logarithmic-curvature object that had
appeared independently in the Clark, Green, Stieltjes, and Laguerre lanes.
The denominator is the native faithful Gram trace.

Thus logarithmic curvature is not merely another positivity candidate. It is
the spatial density of the Prüfer phase that measures collision degree.

At a simple zero of \(H\),

\[
\partial_x\arg G=-1.
\]

Every ordinary zero therefore crosses the phase chart with the same local
orientation.

## Heat component

Using the Newman equation gives

\[
\partial_\lambda G=-H_{xx}-iH_{xxx},
\]

and hence

\[
\partial_\lambda\arg G
=
\frac{H_xH_{xx}-HH_{xxx}}{H^2+H_x^2}.
\]

The heat component is the next Wronskian in the same derivative tower. No new
scalar object has been introduced.

## Flatness and quantized singular curvature

Away from collisions, mixed derivatives agree:

\[
\partial_\lambda\omega_x
=
\partial_x\omega_\lambda.
\]

The checker verifies this identity exactly for arbitrary five-jets satisfying
the heat derivative relations. Thus \(d\omega=0\) on the collision-free
region.

At a collision, \(G=0\) and the phase connection is singular. The preceding
degree theorem gives the distributional curvature

\[
d\omega
=-2\pi\sum_c m_c\,\delta_c
\]

for generic collisions of multiplicity \(m_c=1\), with the sign fixed by
\(\det D(H,H_x)<0\).

Stokes' theorem therefore yields

\[
\frac1{2\pi}\int_{\partial\Omega}\omega
=-#\{\text{generic Newman collisions in }\Omega\}.
\]

## Explanatory compression

Three formerly separate structures are now one object:

1. logarithmic curvature is the spatial connection component;
2. the derivative Wronskian is its heat component;
3. collision number is its quantized holonomy defect.

Agreement among those lanes is no longer independent evidence. It is one
Prüfer connection viewed in different coordinates.

This also clarifies why pointwise curvature positivity was too strong. RH does
not require \(\omega_x\) to have one sign everywhere. It requires the complete
four-edge holonomy to contain no negative collision charge on the relevant
heat strip.
