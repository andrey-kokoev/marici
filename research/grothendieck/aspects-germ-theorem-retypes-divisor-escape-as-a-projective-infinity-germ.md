## What remains

Xi divisor has infinite degree, so no single finite projective line bundle is
its carrier. The next construction must replace degree by a source-normalized
locally finite divisor current or determinant-line germ at infinity. Its
normalization must come from the Riemann--von Mangoldt density and Newman heat,
not from the observed zero set.

Thus Aspect's theorem does not prove RH, but it removes an ambiguity: the
correct next object is not a stronger affine norm. It is a boundary-bearing
projective germ whose type retains escaped divisor charge.
