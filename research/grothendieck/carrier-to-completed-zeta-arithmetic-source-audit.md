## Question

Do the frozen spline data derive `Spec(Z)`, prime-power generators, Frobenius/Euler factors, and the completed zeta L-function from the shared Carrier calculus?

## Claim boundary

The frozen finite source is internally coherent once the completed-zeta factorization is assumed. The convention map derives the executable tuple

`(log(p^m), log(p)/sqrt(p^m), -2, n+1/4)`

from the logarithmic derivative of that factorization and the declared transform normalization. These are finite source-compatibility results, not an arithmetic emergence theorem.

## Typed derivation audit

The required chain is

`G_carrier -> C_arith -> T_Euler -> Q_compare -> R_readout`.

No packet constructs the first arrow. The data already name rational primes, powers, logarithms, the Euler expansion of `zeta'/zeta`, and the completed-zeta gamma factor. Consequently:

- the radial synthetic target tests finite linear laws but cannot reconstruct `Spec(Z)` or authorize the absent `R_zeta` comparison.

The first missing typed arrow is therefore

`G_carrier -> C_arith`,

prior to the already identified cross-sector map `R_zeta:S_zeta^labels -> U_G4`.

## Hostile relabelling test

Replace the prime-power index set by 198 abstract symbols carrying the same ordered grades and weights. Every downstream finite operation that consumes only basis size, grades, weights, support, reciprocal labels, and matrices remains definable and can reproduce the same scalar and synthetic interface laws. The replacement has no multiplication, divisibility, irreducibility, residue fields, or Frobenius.

Thus matching Euler-shaped coefficients, dimensions, or finite interface equations cannot distinguish arithmetic derivation from a fitted weighted alphabet. A successful emergence claim must first construct multiplication on a Carrier-derived coefficient object, prove descent and factorization, and then show that its irreducibles generate the labels and Euler factors used by the spline.

## Disposition

The completed-zeta-to-spline map is exact conditional arithmetic transport. It does not derive arithmetic from the Carrier. The branch is blocked at `G_carrier -> C_arith`; acceptance requires a source-defined coefficient object with descended multiplication and a proved identification of its irreducibles and local factors, not another synthetic radial fixture.
