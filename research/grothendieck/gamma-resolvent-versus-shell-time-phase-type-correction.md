# Gamma spectral parameter is not shell-center time evolution

The even oscillator `A e_k=(k+1/4)e_k` represents the gamma factor through a
spectral determinant/resolvent at `1/4+iT/2`. Its logarithmic derivative uses
regularized terms `(k+1/4+iT/2)^(-1)`, not unitary phases
`exp[-iT(k+1/4)]`.

Prime height, by contrast, genuinely acts as
`exp[-iT log p]=p^(-iT)`. Replacing `log p` by a shell center and comparing
the resulting unitary phases with oscillator phases is a time-evolution
surrogate, not the completed gamma determinant.

The sinc formulas remain correct measurements of prime information lost by
radial shell compression. Their periodic resonance lattices are aliasing in
the shell-center surrogate; they are not singularities of gamma or Xi. The
Adams identity `C_2(s)=(1/2)C_1(2s)` and its alignment of the two prime-shell
phase defects also remain exact, but do not complete prime--gamma
continuation.

A valid comparison must join different functional calculi through a
non-diagonal kernel:

```
prime Fourier/Mellin phase   exp(-iT L_prime),
gamma spectral resolvent     (A+iT/2)^(-1).
```

The next finite block must therefore retain exact prime phases and gamma
resolvent matrix elements. Any periodic shell resonance seen before that
comparison is an aliasing diagnostic, not a physical spectral singularity.
