# Gaussian-translate presentation exactness is source-level

## Question

Can duplicate-label quotienting and form descent be proved before any RH positivity claim?

## Synthesis map

For a finite translate packet `I=(a_1,...,a_r)`, define

\[
\Phi_I:\mathbb C^r\longrightarrow\mathcal S,
\qquad
\Phi_I(c)=\sum_{j=1}^rc_j\tau_{a_j}g_\sigma,
\]

where `g_sigma` is the fixed Gaussian source test function.

After Fourier transform,

\[
\widehat{\Phi_I(c)}(u)
=
\widehat g_\sigma(u)
\sum_{j=1}^rc_je^{-ia_ju}.
\]

The Gaussian Fourier factor never vanishes. For pairwise distinct `a_j`, the finite exponentials are linearly independent, so `Phi_I` is injective.

For repeated labels, its kernel is exactly generated by coefficient differences within equal-label blocks. Hence

\[
\ker\Phi_I=P_I
\]

without using zeros, Gram positivity, or observer values.

## Form descent by construction

Let the completed source Hermitian form be `W(-,-)` on the Gaussian translate span. Define the packet residual form by pullback:

\[
R_I(c,d)=W(\Phi_I(c),\Phi_I(d)).
\]

Then every presentation vector lies in the radical automatically:

\[
p\in P_I
\Longrightarrow
\Phi_I(p)=0
\Longrightarrow
R_I(p,d)=0
\quad\text{for every }d.
\]

Thus the form descends to `Q_I=C^r/P_I` before positivity is considered. The quotient identifies canonically with the actual finite-dimensional source-test span.

## Packet naturality

For every inclusion, permutation, or repetition map represented by `r`,

\[
\Phi_I=\Phi_Jr.
\]

Consequently

\[
R_I=r^*R_Jr
\]

is a source synthesis identity. Presentation descent and Gram naturality are therefore generated by the same map `Phi`, rather than checked independently after matrix construction.

## Observer completeness clarified

The coefficient presentation is already faithful after quotient because `Q_I` embeds into the source test space through `Phi_I`. A further observer family is needed to distinguish completed distributions or forms on that source space, not to distinguish coefficients inside one packet.

This prevents conflating:

1. injectivity of packet synthesis modulo repetitions;
2. joint faithfulness of heat/Gram observers on the completed source object;
3. positivity of the pulled-back Weil form.

## Disposition

Construct every finite Gram observer as a pullback along the source synthesis map. This proves the presentation kernel and radical descent unconditionally. The RH-strength gate remains positivity of `W` on the union of these source spans, not packet-coordinate faithfulness.
