# The completed bridge identifies the remainder cone as the RH content

## Question

After closing the paired-Hadamard and endpoint–gamma–prime bridge, is the remaining all-rank remainder Hankel inequality an auxiliary estimate, or does it contain the unresolved RH-strength implication itself?

## Claim boundary

It contains the unresolved implication itself. The bridge is now a proved reduction, not a proof of the reduced positivity statement.

## Reduction

The source-normalized identity is

\[
H(t)=K_E(t)+K_\Gamma(t)+K_P(t),
\]

with one heat atom per functional-equation zero orbit and original multiplicity. Under RH,

\[
H(t)=\sum_{\gamma>0}m_\gamma e^{-\gamma^2t},
\]

so every sampled Hankel quadratic form generated by heat polynomials is nonnegative.

Conversely, the established sampled-localizer argument converts positivity of the gamma-plus-prime remainder Hankel family into positivity of the completed Weil convolution-square form on

\[
G_{t,h,p}(u)=e^{-tu^2/2}q_h(u)p(e^{-hu^2}),
\qquad |q_h(u)|^2=1-e^{-hu^2}.
\]

Generic-mesh pole rigidity then forces every paired spectral parameter `lambda_a=-a^2` to be real and nonnegative, hence every nontrivial zero to lie on the critical line.

Therefore an unconditional proof of the all-rank remainder cone would prove RH. Transform equality, pointwise positivity, finite-rank scans, or asymptotic dominance cannot substitute for it.

## Strongest falsification attempt already available

Prior searches rejected sectorwise Gram decompositions, PNT-main/fluctuation blocks, positive densities for the signed Weil distribution, fixed recurrences, rational transforms, finite transfer operators, terminating continued fractions, stabilized finite Jacobi matrices, Gaussian first-contact arguments, and determinant condensation without independently positive pivots.

Finite computation found positive samples only through rank four on bounded boxes. Such samples do not control arbitrary polynomials, ranks, meshes, or continuous parameter ranges. The endpoint asymptotic is conditional on RH and cannot be used to establish the unconditional cone.

## Exact residual

No independently positive source factor, proper compression, or nonterminating spectral realization is known whose image is exactly the heat-polynomial family. Polarization of a kernel defined from the target Weil form merely restates the desired positivity and is circular.

## Disposition

Stop this branch at the RH-equivalent cone. The source-normalized bridge is complete; the remaining statement must be reported as the unresolved RH content. Reopen only if new source-derived structure supplies an independently positive factorization or a discriminating theorem not equivalent by definition to the target cone.
