# Sampled heat Hankel cones should construct the positive semigroup

## Question

Can finite differences and Hausdorff moments construct one positive operator semigroup directly from source values, avoiding differentiated prime tails?

## Sampled moments

Fix rational `t>0` and `h>0`, and define

\[
b_n(t,h)=H_{\rm heat}(t+nh).
\]

Under a positive heat measure,

\[
b_n(t,h)
=
\int_0^\infty e^{-t\lambda}
(e^{-h\lambda})^n\,d\rho(\lambda).
\]

With `y=e^(-h lambda)`, this is a Hausdorff moment sequence on `[0,1]`.

## Two source Hankel cones

The exact compact-support criterion is positivity of

\[
M_0(t,h)=
(b_{i+j}(t,h))_{i,j\ge0}
\]

and the upper-support localizer

\[
M_{1-y}(t,h)=
(b_{i+j}(t,h)-b_{i+j+1}(t,h))_{i,j\ge0}.
\]

The second entries are first finite differences in heat scale. Higher cubical differences are generated by repeated application of the same positive contraction defect.

Equivalently, using `x=1-y`, the moments

\[
a_k(t,h)=D_{k,h}H_{\rm heat}(t)
\]

have the three standard Hausdorff Gram/localizing families. The `y` presentation is smaller because its support localizer is already the source time-shift difference.

## GNS constructor

If both infinite matrices are PSD, complete polynomials under

\[
\langle y^i,y^j\rangle=b_{i+j}(t,h).
\]

Multiplication by `y` defines a positive contraction

\[
0\le Y_{t,h}\le1.
\]

The cyclic vector `Omega` satisfies

\[
b_n(t,h)=
\langle\Omega,Y_{t,h}^n\Omega\rangle.
\]

This is a source-moment operator, not an operator guessed from zero locations.

## Meta-coherence across steps

A family at separate `h` values is not yet one heat generator. Meta-observer coherence must construct intertwiners enforcing

\[
Y_{h_1+h_2}=Y_{h_1}Y_{h_2},
\qquad
Y_{mh}=Y_h^m,
\]

with common cyclic moments and strong continuity as `h->0`. Then the positive-contraction semigroup theorem gives

\[
Y_h=e^{-hL},
\qquad L\ge0.
\]

Its spectral measure is exactly the Bernstein heat measure, and the entire kernel construction supplies all Gram observers.

## Arithmetic advantage

Every matrix entry is an undifferentiated source value `H_heat(t+nh)` or a finite difference of such values. Prime truncation errors are controlled by sums of the ordinary log-Gaussian tails at the sampled widths; no powers of `log n` from derivatives occur.

## Remaining sign gate

The two Hankel cones are not automatically positive. They are a structured replacement for all scalar inequalities, not a proof. Their determinant/J-fraction recurrences are the natural place to reuse prior Dodgson, Hurwitz, and Jacobi machinery, now in the direct heat coordinate with exact semigroup relations.

## Disposition

Use sampled heat Hankel cones as the primary candidate unlock. They turn source values into positive contractions, while meta-observer coherence assembles those contractions into one nonnegative generator. This directly implements the operator's observer-of-observers proposal and avoids derivative-tail amplification.
