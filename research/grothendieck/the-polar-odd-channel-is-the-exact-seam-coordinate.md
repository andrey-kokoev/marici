# The polar odd channel is the exact seam coordinate

## Self-dual source reduction

For the standard self-dual Tate source,

\[
f=\widehat f,
\qquad
f(0)=\widehat f(0)=c,
\qquad
c\ne0.
\]

The two polar channels in the four-channel incidence are

\[
C(s)=\frac{c}{s-1},
\qquad
D(s)=-\frac cs.
\]

Their reciprocal-odd readout is

\[
R_3(s)=C(s)-D(s).
\]

## Exact factorization

Direct calculation gives

\[
R_3(s)
=
c\left(\frac1{s-1}+\frac1s\right)
=
c\frac{2s-1}{s(s-1)}.
\]

Writing (z=s-1/2),

\[
R_3(z)
=
\frac{2cz}{z^2-1/4}.
\]

Away from the polar endpoints (s=0,1), the denominator is nonzero.
Therefore

\[
R_3(s)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
s=\frac12.
\]

as a complex equation in the full (s)-plane only at (s=1/2). More
relevantly for reciprocal geometry, (R_3) is exactly the complex seam
coordinate (2s-1) multiplied by a nowhere-zero meromorphic unit away from
the endpoints.

Under reciprocal reflection,

\[
R_3(1-s)=-R_3(s).
\]

## Correct interpretation

The critical line is the fixed locus of the Real reciprocal involution
(s\mapsto1-\overline s), whereas the holomorphic coordinate (2s-1)
vanishes only at the central point. Thus (R_3) records the full complex
displacement from the reciprocal center, not merely horizontal distance.

Its Hermitian horizontal component is

\[
(s-\tfrac12)+(\overline s-\tfrac12)
=
2\operatorname{Re}s-1.
\]

Any Green identity intended to confine zeros must extract this Real component
from the polar odd channel rather than demand the entire holomorphic channel
vanish along the whole critical line.

## Zero-state consequence

At a nontrivial scalar zero, the four-channel state lies in the
three-dimensional hidden fiber. If the zero is off the critical line, then
its polar odd channel has nonzero horizontal component:

\[
2\operatorname{Re}s-1\ne0.
\]

Hence an off-line zero can now be typed as:

> a scalar-null completed incidence carrying nonzero Real polar-odd charge.

This is sharper than saying only that direct and reciprocal sectors have
unequal parameters.

## What theorem would suffice

A source-derived conservation law of the form

\[
(2\operatorname{Re}s-1)N_s
=
\mathcal J_B
-
\mathcal J_P,
\qquad
N_s>0,
\]

would confine zeros if the completed zero-state boundary conditions make the
right-hand side vanish.

The polar contribution is now explicit and cannot be fitted. The missing
calculation is the bulk-odd current and its attachment to the primitive,
square, seam, and archimedean relative observer.

## Scope correction

It would be wrong to demand (R_3(s)=0) for every critical-line zero, because
(R_3(1/2+it)) is generally nonzero when (t\ne0). The RH-bearing quantity is
the Real horizontal charge (2\operatorname{Re}s-1), not the full complex
polar-odd coordinate.

This distinction prevents a holomorphic-center statement from being confused
with critical-line confinement.
