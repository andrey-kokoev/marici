# Poisson reflection selects the first boundary-free moment covector

Author: `marici.Grothendieck`

Date: 2026-08-26

Status: exact reciprocal-sewing theorem

## Fourier action on the first moments

Let

\[
g_k(x)=(\pi x^2)^ke^{-\pi x^2}.
\]

For the self-dual Fourier convention,

\[
\widehat g_1=(1/2-\pi\xi^2)e^{-\pi\xi^2},
\]

\[
\widehat g_2=((\pi\xi^2)^2-3\pi\xi^2+3/4)e^{-\pi\xi^2}.
\]

Apply Poisson summation to the scaled lattice and use the positive moments
from ledger 3052. For (u\ge0), reflection gives

\[
M_1(u)=\frac14e^{-u/2}+\frac12M_0(-u)-M_1(-u),
\]

\[
M_2(u)=\frac38e^{-u/2}+\frac34M_0(-u)-3M_1(-u)+M_2(-u).
\]

The explicit exponential terms are the zero-lattice vacuum boundary. The
(M_0) terms are the lower moment channel generated by Fourier transport.

## Exact cancellation

For the physical readout

\[
\Phi=4M_2-6M_1,
\]

the vacuum coefficient is

\[
4\left(\frac38\right)-6\left(\frac14\right)=0,
\]

and the (M_0) coefficient is

\[
4\left(\frac34\right)-6\left(\frac12\right)=0.
\]

The remaining terms reproduce the same covector:

\[
\Phi(u)=4M_2(-u)-6M_1(-u)=\Phi(-u).
\]

Thus reciprocal evenness is not imposed on the scalar after aggregation. It
is the statement that ((-6,4)) is the first nonzero moment covector whose
Fourier–Poisson transport has no vacuum or lower-grade leakage.

## Minimality becomes structural

A general order-two readout (aM_1+bM_2) is boundary-free only if

\[
\frac a4+\frac{3b}8=0,
\qquad
\frac a2+\frac{3b}4=0.
\]

These equations coincide and give (2a+3b=0). Hence the boundary-free line
is one-dimensional and generated by

\[
(a,b)=(-6,4).
\]

This derives the theta completion coefficients from reciprocal transport and
boundary cancellation. Higher Hermite deformations correspond to higher
boundary-free covectors, but the physical theta source is the first nontrivial
one in the moment filtration.

## Remaining obstruction

The reciprocal action on the state space is now explicit. What remains is not
to discover the second sheet, but to derive a Green or Clifford pairing on the
doubled infinite ladder whose boundary-free readout is faithful enough to
exclude an off-seam null state. Scalar evenness alone still does not do this.

## Scope

The Fourier formulas, Poisson moment transport, cancellation, and uniqueness
of the order-two boundary-free line are exact. No positive conserved form,
completion theorem, zero confinement, or RH statement is proved.
