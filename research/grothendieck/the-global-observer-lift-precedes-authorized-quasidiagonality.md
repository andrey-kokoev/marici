# The global observer lift precedes authorized quasidiagonality

## Scope correction

Authorized quasidiagonality is an exact completion gate only after the sewing
operator and cutoff projections have been typed on a common completed
carrier. The current RH programme does not yet have that object for the full
arithmetic boundary.

Two existing calculations demonstrate why substitutes cannot be conflated.

## Continuous logarithmic carrier

For interval projection `P_R` on `L^2(R)` and ordinary Fourier transform `F`,

\[
\|P_R\mathcal F(I-P_R)\|=1
\]

for every positive `R`. This filtration is maximally nonquasidiagonal. It is a
valid hostile for any construction factoring through the continuous
log-window norm.

But `P_R` is not the discrete Euler cutoff projection on the arithmetic label
rigging. Its failure cannot be transferred by name alone.

## Finite arithmetic pro-Gram carrier

On the doubled finite prime-label module, the source-derived reciprocal sheet
transport

\[
J_X=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-I\\
I&0
\end{pmatrix}
\]

preserves each Fourier-saturated primitive and square seminorm and preserves
the prime cutoff exactly. Thus its cutoff commutator vanishes.

But `J_X` is the reciprocal sheet action on already-labelled boundary
coordinates. It is not the global additive Fourier–Poisson operator on the
theta source. Its success also cannot be promoted by name alone.

## Missing first arrow

Let `A` be the global additive theta/adelic carrier and let `B` be the full
boundary pro-object retaining primitive, square, connected-tail, seam, and
archimedean grades. The first missing construction is an observer lift

\[
\mathcal J:A\longrightarrow B
\]

together with a boundary sewing operator `F_B` satisfying the two-carrier
naturality identity

\[
\mathcal J\mathcal F_A
=
\mathcal F_B\mathcal J.
\]

This identity must hold before scalar Mellin evaluation and before finite
Euler projection. It is the distributive correspondence between additive
Poisson sewing and multiplicative boundary construction.

Only after `J` and `F_B` exist may one define source-authorized boundary
diagnostics `Q_X` and ask whether

\[
Q_X\mathcal F_B(I-Q_X)
\]

decays, is summable, or converges to a typed boundary port.

## Correct two-stage gate

### Stage one: global naturality

Construct `J` and compute

\[
R(\phi)
=
\mathcal J(\mathcal F_A\phi)
-
\mathcal F_B(\mathcal J\phi)
\]

in every boundary grade. Any nonzero typed residual falsifies the proposed
observer lift or identifies an additional preregistered coherence cell.

### Stage two: diagnostic descent

After stage one passes, compute the two cutoff leakage directions for the
actual `Q_X` on `B`. Authorized quasidiagonality is then meaningful. Fixed
observer convergence remains insufficient.

## Where the primitive and square currents enter

The primitive and square currents are not yet entries of one finite operator
matrix. They occupy inequivalent regularity grades in a pro-Gram boundary
topology. The observer lift must therefore land grade-wise:

\[
\mathcal J
=
(\mathcal J_1,\mathcal J_2,\mathcal J_{\ge3},
  \mathcal J_{\mathrm{seam}},\mathcal J_\infty).
\]

Naturality is tested componentwise before any cross-grade scalar
cancellation. A single Hilbert Gram matrix would erase the distinction the
construction is supposed to preserve.

## Revised immediate target

Do not search yet for a numerical `F_X`. Derive the first two components

\[
\mathcal J_1,
\qquad
\mathcal J_2
\]

directly from the additive theta source into the primitive and square dual
grades. Then compute their Fourier–Poisson naturality residuals. The existing
finite label incidence and local sheet swap provide target coordinates, not
the global lift itself.

## Falsifiers

The construction fails if:

1. `J_1` or `J_2` is defined only through scalar `log zeta` continuation;
2. source variations invisible to prime sampling have nonzero Poisson
   response but vanish under `J`;
3. naturality holds only after summing boundary grades;
4. the boundary sewing is chosen to make `R` vanish rather than derived from
   source Fourier transport;
5. a continuous log-window cutoff or finite sheet swap is substituted for the
   actual two-carrier correspondence.

## Scope

This corrects the ordering of the completion programme. It does not construct
the global observer lift, its boundary sewing operator, its first two grade
maps, or RH.
