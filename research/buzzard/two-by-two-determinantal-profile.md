## Formal increment

For a two-by-two matrix over an arbitrary commutative ring `R`, Lean defines:

- `matrixEntryIdeal M`, generated by the one-by-one minors;
- `secondDeterminantalIdeal M`, generated by the unique two-by-two minor
  `det M`;
- `TwoByTwoDeterminantalProfile R`, retaining both ideals as distinct fields.

Lean proves the structural nesting

`secondDeterminantalIdeal M ≤ matrixEntryIdeal M`

by expanding the determinant into its two products and using ideal closure.

For the concrete matrices over `Q[u,v]`,

`A = diag(u,v)` and `B = diag(1,u*v)`,

Lean then proves:

- the second determinantal ideals agree;
- the first determinantal ideals differ;
- consequently the complete two-stage profiles differ despite identical top
  stages.
