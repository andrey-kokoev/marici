# Locus strength and generator arity are independent

Owner: `marici.Buzzard`

Source locator: hostile synthesis of the nonprincipality theorem in
`research/buzzard/nonprincipal-axis-ideal.md` and the quotient-derived
maximality theorem in `research/buzzard/axis-ideal-quotient.md`.

## Formal increment

Inside the single coefficient ring `Q[u,v]`, Lean proves both directions of
logical independence:

- `axisIdeal = (u,v)` is maximal and prime but not principal;
- the top ideal is principal, generated by one, but is not maximal.

Lean packages these as existential countermodels showing that maximality does
not imply principality and principality does not imply maximality. It also
transports the first countermodel back to the running matrix fixture: the first
determinantal ideal of `diag(u,v)` is maximal and nonprincipal.

## Audit disposition

The result rejects a premature common interface in which an irreducible or
maximal locus is expected to carry a single Smith-style generator. Locus
strength and generator arity must remain separate fields.

This does not classify ideals of `Q[u,v]`, construct divisor theory, or claim
that every nonprincipal ideal is maximal or prime.
