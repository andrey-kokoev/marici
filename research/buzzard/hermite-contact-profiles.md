# Hermite contact-profile packet

## Grothendieck source

This formalizes the checker-backed low-degree core of
`research/grothendieck/higher-contact-hermite-negative-mass-law.md`.

## Formal objects

- `physicistsH2` and `physicistsH4` use the packet's Hermite convention.
- the quadratic and quartic backward-heat profiles are explicit polynomials.
- the scaling theorems set `delta=r^2` and prove the exact parabolic Hermite
  identities without choosing a square-root branch.
- negative-region and sign-change theorems reproduce the finite sign audit.
- `contactExponentNumerator m=2m+1` represents twice the onset exponent
  `m+1/2` and is injective, so the scaling exponent records multiplicity.
- the hostile shows that one negative sign observation does not distinguish
  quadratic from quartic contact; scaling data is essential.

## Assumptions and coefficient type

The profile identities are stated over the real numbers with nonzero scale
`r`. The discrete exponent theorem uses natural numbers.

## Missing analytic interfaces

The all-degree theorem requires a fixed general physicists-Hermite definition,
the exponential of the differential operator on finite jets, control of the
analytic remainder on a parabolic window, measurable negative-part
integration, positivity and finiteness of its Hermite coefficient, and the
logarithmic-slope limit. The heat-flow identification also requires the exact
variance parameter and PDE convention.

Accordingly this file covers the independently checked quadratic/quartic
normal forms but does not promote them to the general analytic asymptotic,
exclude higher contacts, identify Weil flow with Newman flow, or prove RH.
