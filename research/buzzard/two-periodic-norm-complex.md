# Integral two-periodic norm complex: Lean packet

## Source boundary

This increment formalizes the abstract algebraic constructor in
Grothendieck's `integral-two-periodic-norm-complex.md`. It does not silently
replace the required group ring, augmentation, augmentation ideal, or
physical chain maps by an arbitrary module.

## Formal objects and coefficient types

The coefficient type is an arbitrary commutative ring `R`; `M` is an
arbitrary `R`-module whose additive structure is a commutative group. A linear
endomorphism `T` and scalar `d` satisfy the explicit relation `T ∘ T = d T`.
The complementary differential is `d id - T`. Neither invertibility nor
vanishing of `d` is assumed.

## Theorems and hostile

- `normMap_comp_complement_eq_zero` proves `T(d-T)=0`.
- `complement_comp_normMap_eq_zero` proves `(d-T)T=0`.
- `normRelation_defines_twoPeriodicComplex` packages both adjacent
  zero-composition laws.
- `missing_normRelation_hostile` takes `T=id` and `d=2` on `ℤ`; the norm
  relation fails and both adjacent composites take `1` to `1`.

## Missing interfaces

The group-ring specialization needs a finite group, the regular group ring,
the norm element, and a proof that multiplication by it satisfies the
quadratic relation. The homology formula needs augmentation, its kernel,
injectivity of the coefficient line generated by the norm element, quotient
typing, and the image calculation. Characteristic specializations need fixed
base-change maps. A physical interpretation additionally requires sourced
chain maps commuting with the physical boundary.
