## Three mechanisms

- Access denial changes only the admitted probe indices. The witness
  `access_denial_leaves_class_nonzero` retains a nonzero vector.
- Readout loss changes the morphism applied to an unchanged nontrivial
  capability object. `readout_loss_leaves_object_nontrivial` supplies an
  explicit noninjective coordinate projection.
- Constitutive collapse changes the equivalence relation itself.
  `collapse_can_kill_nonzero_class` exhibits a class nonzero modulo bottom
  and zero after quotienting by its span.
- `removing_probe_does_not_kill_capability` contrasts access denial directly
  with constitutive collapse: the removed coordinate becomes inaccessible,
  while the vector remains nonzero.

These countermodels prove non-interchangeability, not realization in a frozen
physical sector.

## Accessible operators

`accessibleImage C` is the submonoid generated by declared endomorphisms.
`accessibleImage_mono` proves monotonicity. The outside criterion is the exact
negation of membership, not a claim about laboratory availability.
`OperatorSpanning C` separately states that the generated image is top.

The coordinate projectors commute and jointly separate vectors, but every
composition they generate preserves the first coordinate axis. The explicit
off-diagonal `coordinateSwap` violates that invariant and is therefore not in
their generated image. Thus commuting coordinate access does not encode all
operator coherences.

## Physical/source blockers

1. The frozen sources must provide actual modules and typed repair/residue
   maps, including the proof `residue.comp repair = 0`.
2. A physical probe must be supplied as a linear map and proved to vanish on
   the repair image before quotient descent is available.
3. Enlargement `B ≤ B'` must come from a new legal/source-derived relation;
   the formal theorem does not authorize enlarging repairs to kill a class.
4. Constructor membership does not imply laboratory constructibility. A
   source operation monoid, its representation, and a physical realization
   map remain required.
5. Coordinate separation reconstructs vectors only in the declared carrier;
   it does not reconstruct arbitrary operations, coherences, or records.
