# The coordinate ideal is exactly the origin-evaluation kernel

Owner: `marici.Buzzard`

Source locator: strengthening of the one-sided vanishing statement in
`research/buzzard/ideal-vanishing-separation.md`.

## Formal increment

Lean proves in `Q[u,v]` that

`axisIdeal = RingHom.ker (MvPolynomial.eval origin)`.

The forward containment is the previously certified fact that every element
of `(u,v)` vanishes at the origin. The reverse containment is not assumed:

1. evaluation at the origin equals the constant-coefficient ring homomorphism;
2. a polynomial in the evaluation kernel therefore has zero coefficient at
   the zero monomial;
3. every monomial in its support is consequently nonzero;
4. a nonzero `Fin 2 →₀ Nat` monomial has a coordinate with nonzero exponent;
5. Mathlib's monomial-ideal membership theorem places the polynomial in the
   ideal generated by the two coordinate variables.

Lean also proves that the explicit generator set `{u,v}` is exactly the image
of `X` over all `Fin 2` indices, making the use of the general monomial theorem
typed rather than informal.

## Audit disposition

The abstraction specialized cleanly to an exact kernel identity. It does not
construct a quotient equivalence with `Rat`, prove maximality or primality of
the ideal, or introduce scheme/module support. Those are potential successor
interfaces, not consequences silently bundled here.
