# Local synthesis: status of an authoritative `U_G4`

## Conclusion

The repository contains a nearly complete **source-side construction and acceptance framework**, but no independently exposed authoritative `U_G4` carrier/map/pairing packet.

The local evidence is stronger than “no map exists”: `T_pair_to_border` is a fully typed analytic crossing to a canonical bordered radial surrogate, and the exact weakest comparison relation is known. What is absent is the owner-side G4 realization against which that crossing can be compared.

## Layered inventory

### 1. Arithmetic labels and source coefficients

Constructed conditionally on imported completed-zeta arithmetic:

- prime, power, logarithmic grade, shell, ordered-pair, theta, and reciprocal-pole labels;
- exact finite coefficient/sign conventions;
- semilocal Sonin orientation `K_p=-p^-s`;
- reflected determinant-pair finite-stage compiler.

Not constructed from bare Carrier operations:

- `G_carrier -> C_arith`;
- multiplication, irreducibles, residue fields, or Frobenius generating the imported prime labels.

This blocks an arithmetic-*emergence* claim, but not conditional arithmetic transport from the admitted completed-zeta source.

### 2. Source-side analytic crossing

Constructed:

- faithful source-retaining multi-rung joint graph;
- first-Adams graph over `E_p,12` with window/resolved and cut/Wronskian legs;
- source-derived order-four Fourier sewing and cutoff naturality;
- logarithmic connection covariance;
- maximal-isotropic response graph;
- oriented half-density radialization;
- `T_pair_to_border` from the projective ordered-pair carrier to the bordered radial response;

Authoritative record: `research/voevodsky/r-zeta-u-g4-transfer-record-v3.json` calls this a constructed analytic surrogate and explicitly forbids renaming it `U_G4`.

### 3. G4-side target realization

Absent:

- owner-authorized G4 forward carrier on the same source;
- exact codomain and coordinate/basis order;
- nondegenerate source and target pairings;
- arithmetic/radial forward incidence formula or matrix `U_G4`;
- frozen proof that the Evans lower row uses its contragredient;
- an independent derivation of every map entry.

The conformance manifest consistently marks the G4 comparison rows blocked because canonical G4 carrier, differential/domain, traces, incidence, synthesis, and return arrows are not exposed.

### 4. Final comparison theorem

The local records identify a hierarchy of sufficient conditions:

1. strongest: `U_G4 = T_pair_to_border` on the common source;
2. weaker: `(U_G4-T_pair_to_border)^top` annihilates the Evans cyclic subspace;
3. invariant chain-level form: the Evans residual lies in the Xi ideal, i.e.
   `(U_G4-T_pair_to_border)^top e in tau O(U_ar)`.

The third is the correct unchanged-divisor-jet equivalence. Strict equality is sufficient but need not be necessary. All three remain untestable until `U_G4` and its pairings are exposed.

## Synthesized verdict

No hidden complete authoritative packet was found. The local repository already supplies most of the packet's **comparison half**, including the source map, conventions, contragredient rule, and exact acceptance relation. The missing object is narrowly owner-side: an independently derived G4 carrier, pairings, and forward incidence.

The cheapest next operation is not further source-side reconstruction. It is a repository-wide owner audit for any file that defines the canonical G4 carrier under a different name, followed by a mechanical field-by-field conformance check against the required target rows. If none exists, the G4 owner must construct that target packet.
