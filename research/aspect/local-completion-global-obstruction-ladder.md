# Local-completion/global-obstruction ladder

## Deutschean question

How can two records be complete under every admitted local constructor while their joint record has no local factorization?

## Conjecture

For an EPR-Compton acquisition, completing a genuinely local route can change local closure residuals and uncertainty, but cannot by itself remove a Bell obstruction while preserving the same no-click-inclusive joint distribution. If the obstruction disappears at a completion rung, that rung has exposed either a previously omitted physical route, a selection rule, setting dependence, memory, or a frame defect. It is then explanatory rather than a nuisance correction.

The test therefore has two axes which must never be collapsed:

1. constructor completion: progressively add setting health, photon spectroscopy, recoil-electron momentum, target excitation, no-click accounting, analyzer-frame transport, and reset/memory records;
2. factorization obstruction: at every rung compute the same preregistered CHSH witness and local marginals on the inclusive acquisition packet.

The predicted signature is a vertical column: local closure improves with each rung, while the conservative CHSH lower bound remains above 2. The conjecture is falsified by the first rung that is locally admissible yet makes the inclusive obstruction vanish. That rung names the missing explanation.

## Why this is more than another Bell test

A conventional Bell test rejects a class of local hidden-variable models. This ladder also asks whether the rejection was stable under explicit completion of the local physical record. It converts the vague reply “perhaps an unmeasured local mechanism did it” into a finite sequence of named, instrumented hostile routes.

The surviving explanation is structural: locally closed components need not form a globally separable record. The obstruction belongs to compatibility of the whole record, not to transport between already completed parts.

## Claim boundary

The current execution is an exact mathematical fixture. It does not certify a bound entangled source, spacelike setting choices, detector efficiency, acquired counts, or a physical Bell violation. Those require the acquisition packet specified by the EPR-Compton experiment.
