# Cross-sector transfer audit for the affine theta optics apparatus

## Frontier synthesis

The current Aspect chain ends at a precise obstruction.  Prime-local affine
Green cells compose, their co-moving Schur first jets are exact, and their
odd/odd evenizations are locally seam-confined.  Scalar two-prime addition
nevertheless creates an off-seam transmission zero while the faithful dagger
packet remains bright.

The other sectors now provide five direct transfers and two prohibited ones.

## Transfer 1: Grothendieck supplies the missing source discriminator

The positive Fourier-fixed three-coset hostile passes positivity, Fourier
self-duality, mean density, and origin normalization.  It fails the canonical
one-endpoint-per-Haar-cell incidence.

This is exactly the kind of invariant missing from independent prime
codiagonalization.  The next theta optics packet should retain, for every
source contribution:

- its Haar cell;
- its distinguished endpoint;
- its integer, fractional-coset, or character port;
- the incidence map sent into the scalar sewing port.

The decisive comparison is no longer canonical theta versus an arbitrary
positive kernel.  It is canonical unit-endpoint incidence versus the
denominator-three Fourier orbit, with identical aggregate normalization.

Optical transfer: encode endpoint class in spatial mode or time bin, implement
the four-port Fourier orbit coherently, and measure both the scalar sum and the
incidence-resolved packet.  A scalar match with a packet mismatch certifies
source-type erasure before any zero scan.

## Transfer 2: Buzzard identifies the correct formal zero type

The Rosenbrock compiler proves that a selected output can have a transmission
zero while a complementary output remains bright, even in a passive,
reachable, visible realization.

Aspect's two-prime off-seam null is the same typed object:

`state/source packet -> selected codiagonal output = 0`,

while the local and dagger outputs are nonzero.  It should therefore be
compiled as a Rosenbrock transmission-zero witness, not described as loss of
observability or as a zero of the complete packet.

Optical transfer: expose the affine Green state, common forcing input,
codiagonal output, and complementary local outputs as one state-space record.
The resulting kernel witness can be checked by Buzzard's formal interface.

## Transfer 3: Sontag gives the calibration protocol

Two physical output records do not prove two independent response directions.
Baseline plus one common excitation aliases rank one and rank two.  Baseline
plus two independent excitations reconstructs the response columns.

Optical transfer: before interpreting a bright dagger monitor as faithful,
inject:

1. an equal common displacement into both prime channels;
2. a differential displacement into only one prime channel.

Recover the two-column response matrix and certify rank two.  Also record its
diagonal common-mode direction.  Passive observation of two detector ports is
insufficient.

## Transfer 4: Kitaev supplies the lossless renormalization port

Flattening every scalar central jet forces the normalized transition to one
and erases the arithmetic cocycle.  The minimal lossless extension retains
the logarithmic transition history as an additive reflection-odd port.

This is the same role played by Aspect's Duhamel forcing reservoir.  A
completed apparatus must retain both:

- the flattened scalar line;
- the graded logarithmic or affine history required to reconstruct prime and
  prime-square currents.

Optical transfer: record the applied counterphase and gain history, labelwise,
alongside the dark-port output.  A perfectly flattened scalar trace without
that control history is evidence of erasure, not coherence.

## Transfer 5: Strominger supplies a path-component falsifier

The selective projective gate is a valid reflection-fixed endpoint but lies
in a different connected component from the identity inside the fixed locus.
Any continuous implementation must break reflection symmetry en route,
change carrier, or use a primitive discontinuous constructor.

Optical transfer: implement the sheet exchange as a mode swap and the
selective gate as a relative phase flip.  Tomographically sample the whole
control path.  An endpoint-only symmetry check misses the obstruction; the
intermediate projective character must change component.

This is directly analogous to the co-moving return row: endpoint covariance
does not establish horizontal constructibility.

## Conditional transfer: Flavor's primitive dual pairing

A primitive self-dual product excludes the zero cusp and fixes a positive
self-dual magnitude.  If an independently derived dual optical coupling and
primitive unit pairing existed, it could fix Nima's free current-to-line gain.

The transfer is conditional.  Complementary readout channels are not dual
source couplings, and an integral pairing does not fix kinetic magnitude.
Optics can test a supplied duality-intertwining threshold law; it cannot
manufacture the missing source authority by calibration.

## Prohibited transfer: endpoint word matching

Benincasa's Cartan/cosmological audit shows that shared names and coordinate
shapes do not construct a cross-sector map.  Aspect should likewise not
identify optical endpoints, Haar-cell endpoints, Green boundaries, and
elliptic marked points without an explicit comparison functor.

## Priority experiment stack

The highest-information next apparatus is one acquisition with four layers:

1. canonical integer incidence and denominator-three coset incidence as
   separately labelled source packets;
2. common and differential excitations identifying the full response rank;
3. simultaneous scalar codiagonal, local-channel, and dagger-power outputs;
4. retained counterphase and affine forcing histories.

Run the Rosenbrock zero compiler on every scalar dark event.  Classify it as:

- packet zero: every resolved channel vanishes;
- transmission zero: selected scalar vanishes with a bright complement;
- normalization erasure: scalar is flat while history is nontrivial;
- incidence alias: aggregate metadata match while endpoint-labelled packets
  differ.

This stack directly attacks the live theorem boundary: whether the canonical
theta source curve avoids the scalar sewing kernel off the reciprocal fixed
locus because of its unit-endpoint incidence, rather than because of generic
positivity, Fourier symmetry, or detector faithfulness.
