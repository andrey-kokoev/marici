# A physical auxiliary generator requires a source-native zero-trace tail constructor

## Question

Can the current source formulas provide vertical Green auxiliary generators on the forcing skew product and prove that recovery annihilates them?

## Canonical conditional construction

Let

\[
T:H_s\to E
\]

be endpoint recovery and let

\[
P_{\min}:E\to H_s,
\qquad
TP_{\min}=I_E
\]

be the source harmonic lift. For any raw tail/PV feature \(J_{\rm raw}\), define

\[
J_{\rm reg}=(I-P_{\min}T)J_{\rm raw}.
\]

Then

\[
TJ_{\rm reg}
=TJ_{\rm raw}-TP_{\min}TJ_{\rm raw}=0.
\]

Thus every regularized generator lies in the maximal recovery-dark vertical bundle \(\ker T\). The operator \(I-P_{\min}T\) is idempotent and fixes every zero-trace history, so it is the canonical projection onto \(\ker T\) associated with the declared harmonic lift.

## Source audit

This algebra does not prove that the physical tail/PV constructor is \(J_{\rm reg}\). Current source readback blocks the prior identification of the odd tail operator with a theta Fourier quarter-turn:

- the normalized completed Gaussian source and arithmetic comb are Fourier-fixed;
- the full history adjoint is generated by causal reflection, equivalently a Fourier half-turn;
- no source arrow identifies a Fourier quarter-turn with the tail/PV skew form.

The available source-native candidate is instead the skew history difference

\[
T_{\rm hist}=\frac{H_+-H_+^*}{2},
\]

but its common closed domain, even-block compatibility, label preservation, radical descent, and Euler compression remain unproved. Therefore there is no admitted raw physical generator on which to verify pre-representation subtraction.

## Why post hoc subtraction is invalid

Replacing an already formed Schur auxiliary by its zero-trace projection changes the eliminated subspace and can change the effective determinant. The regularization must occur in the source constructor before representation. Hence one cannot define the desired inclusion by projecting current outputs after inspecting their traces.

## Exact acceptance test

A physical auxiliary constructor must supply a source map \(J_{\rm phys}\) and prove either

\[
TJ_{\rm phys}=0
\]

directly, or a factorization

\[
J_{\rm phys}=(I-P_{\min}T)J_{\rm raw}
\]

before Green representation. It must additionally act on the same forcing skew-product carrier and preserve prime labels and reciprocal parity.

## Disposition

The conditional zero-trace generator and its recovery-kernel inclusion are exact. The physical generator is not currently source-derived because the tail/PV skew constructor itself is missing. Defer physical Schur elimination and request the closed forward/reflected-history constructor with its source-native endpoint subtraction.
