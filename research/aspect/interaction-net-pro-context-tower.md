# Pro-context tower for the interaction-net pilot

No fixed bounded context basis separates the unbounded source category. The
smallest nonadaptive repair is to preregister the entire tower of finite
apparatus contexts.

At level R, use chart and occurrence ports together with sorters capped at R
for k and u and at 2R for v. The map from level R+1 back to level R is ordinary
coordinatewise saturation. These maps form a fixed inverse system; they are
not chosen after a target is observed.

Every level fails globally. Each level is faithful on the matched source
resource truncation. The tower is jointly object-faithful: for distinct finite
states x and y, every preregistered level at least as large as both resource
grades records their exact coordinates and separates them.

This is not a uniformly bounded instrument. Spatial, spectral, detector-range,
calibration, and uncertainty resources grow with R. Physical explanatory
authority therefore requires source-compatible calibration maps between
levels and one uniform noise/degradation law. Neither has been executed.

The result is an algebraically faithful pro-context candidate. It does not
certify apparatus existence or morphism-level faithfulness of the enriched
nerve, and it does not permit target-adaptive full-Yoneda probes.
