# Recovery on the forcing skew product retains the source connection

## Question

Should the theta forcing-jet directions be annihilated as Schur auxiliary histories or retained by the augmented source recovery?

## Bundle lift

Let \(S\) be the nonlinear theta source manifold with coordinates \((y,f)\), and let \(E\) be the retained endpoint source. Over each \(s\in S\), suppose

\[
I_s:E\to H_s,
\qquad
R_s:H_s\to E,
\qquad
R_sI_s=I_E.
\]

The source-preserving skew-product lift is

\[
\widetilde I(e,s)=(I_s e,s),
\qquad
\widetilde R(h,s)=(R_s h,s).
\]

Then

\[
\widetilde R\widetilde I=I_{E\times S}.
\]

At the tangent level, the derivative of \(\widetilde R\) recovers both the endpoint tangent and the source-connection tangent.

## No-annihilation theorem

Let \(J\ne0\) be a tangent vector in the retained source manifold, including the theta forcing direction generated by

\[
y'=2y,
\qquad
f'=-2\pi yf.
\]

If \(D\widetilde I(J)\) belonged to an auxiliary subspace \(N\) annihilated by \(D\widetilde R\), then

\[
J=D\widetilde R\,D\widetilde I(J)=0,
\]

a contradiction. Therefore no nonzero retained forcing-connection tangent can be placed in a recovery-annihilated auxiliary subspace.

## Dichotomy

There are only two typed choices:

1. retain \((y,f)\) as source coordinates, in which case their forcing jets survive recovery and cannot be Schur-eliminated as dark auxiliary histories;
2. use a recovery that forgets \((y,f)\), in which case it is not a left inverse for the augmented source and cannot certify source-faithful quotient descent.

The forcing-jet output may couple to auxiliary route histories, but the source tangent itself is retained. The physical auxiliary subspace must therefore be vertical over \(S\) and disjoint from the image of the retained source tangent.

## Claim boundary

This determines the placement of forcing-source directions relative to recovery. It does not construct the Green metric on the skew product or prove that all vertical route auxiliaries lie in the augmented recovery kernel.

## Disposition

The forcing connection belongs to the retained source base, not to the eliminated auxiliary fiber. The next gate is to define the vertical physical auxiliary bundle \(N_s\subset H_s\) and prove \(R_s(N_s)=0\) uniformly over the theta source manifold.
