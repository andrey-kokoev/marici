# Barcode basis export and optical compiler boundary

The rank barcode fixes channel counts but not channel weights. The missing
source packet must export an adapted quotient basis with four labelled blocks:
first deaths, second deaths, through-depth-six survivors, and depth-four
emergence.

Rows use sparse finite-field encoding together with exact source labels. The
consumer checks nested kernels, complements, dual pairings, and chart
transport. A positional basis without labels is insufficient because it cannot
bind a coefficient to a physical source mode.

Finite-field coefficients are not optical amplitudes. After algebraic
validation, a separate declared lift chooses integer or complex
representatives, normalizes the analysis rows, and embeds them in a unitary
mode-transform network. Signed or complex rows require coherent homodyne
readout. Ordinary intensity readout is valid only for nonnegative rows.

The checker currently reports awaiting source export. It includes a synthetic
barcode fixture that catches wrong death-block assignments, so the ingestion
path is executable before the large packet arrives.
