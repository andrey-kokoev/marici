# Cosmology relative p-normal Bockstein audit

Owner: `marici.Aspect`

## Proposal

Replace the failed local-monodromy activation route with a relative normal construction for

\[
p=x+y+3z,
\qquad
\omega_{23}-\omega_{13}+\omega_{12}=p\eta.
\]

Use the principal ideal \(I=(p)\), a normal Koszul factor

\[
K_p=[R\langle h_p\rangle\xrightarrow{p}R\langle e_p\rangle],
\]

and apply \(I^\vee=\operatorname{Hom}_R(I,R)\) only to an explicitly proved \(I\)-supported class.

## Assessment

This is the strongest current candidate because it does not require nontrivial punctured-base monodromy. It is also compatible with the established facts that the logarithmic circuit has a normalized transverse class and that local \(p\)-monodromy is the identity.

It is **not yet a constructed Bockstein**. The displayed divisibility identity is an equality of logarithmic forms, not by itself a connecting morphism. A Bockstein requires a typed complex and a lift \(H\) satisfying

\[
dH=p\Xi
\]

in a declared relative quotient, with \([\Xi]\) proved nonzero there. Setting the circuit relation equal to zero by definition would make survival tautological and is not admissible.

## Exact transfer from the Voevodsky pattern

`research/voevodsky/check_d03_pabs_morse_pullback.rs` authorizes only the following pattern:

1. construct an ambient chain complex and its differential;
2. identify an endpoint/boundary subcomplex \(E\);
3. quotient by \(E\), then by a separately proved subcomplex generated by the generic relation;
4. prove the chosen lift has differential divisible by the principal normal;
5. define the connecting class in \(H_*(I B)\) for the resulting relative carrier \(B\);
6. prove the normalized target survives by an independent specialization/rank test;
7. apply the ideal dual only on the \(I\)-supported submodule;
8. retain orientation dependence explicitly;
9. forbid promotion to an ambient or global-period map.

The Voevodsky checker explicitly shows that ideal-dual evaluation is invalid on terms not lying in the principal ideal and that the relative Bockstein does not create a nonzero ambient class.

## Required cosmology carrier

Before writing \(\delta_p\), construct:

\[
B_{\rm cos}=(C_{\rm log/face}/E_{\rm CM})/J_{\rm circuit},
\]

where:

- \(C_{\rm log/face}\) is a source-derived Čech, logarithmic, or bulk–face total complex;
- \(E_{\rm CM}\) is the explicitly listed Cayley–Menger endpoint/face subcomplex;
- \(J_{\rm circuit}\) is a proved differential-stable subcomplex, not merely the imposed equation \(p\eta=0\);
- multiplication by \(p\) is typed on every retained coefficient object.

A valid candidate must supply \(H_p\) and \(\Xi_p\) with

\[
dH_p=p\Xi_p\quad\text{in }B_{\rm cos},
\]

and then prove:

\[
0\ne[\Xi_p]\in H_*(I B_{\rm cos}).
\]

Only after this may \(I^\vee\) evaluate \([p\Xi_p]\) to \([\Xi_p]\).

## Hostile fixtures

A checker should reject:

1. dividing by \(p\) in the ambient ring;
2. applying \(I^\vee\) to a term not proved in \(I B_{\rm cos}\);
3. defining \(J_{\rm circuit}\) so that nontriviality is tautological;
4. omitting the Cayley–Menger face subcomplex;
5. using the all-soft \(\mathbb Z/3\) class as the coefficient object;
6. inferring the class from identity monodromy;
7. omitting the orientation sign of the normal and relative chain;
8. promoting the relative class to a global contour or physical period;
9. claiming survival without a rank/specialization witness;
10. forgetting to prove \(d^2=0\) after Koszul totalization.

## Minimal next computation

The smallest decisive task is to locate or construct a degree-one relative cochain \(H_p\) in the resolved principal Čech or bulk–face complex whose boundary is the circuit combination modulo a separately justified generic relation. Then:

- verify differential stability of \(E_{\rm CM}\) and \(J_{\rm circuit}\);
- totalize with \(K_p\) and check \(d^2=0\);
- specialize \(p\mapsto0\) over at least two finite fields and compare boundary rank before and after adjoining \(\eta\);
- report either a surviving one-dimensional class or a no-go.

## Claim boundary

The proposal is admitted as a source-derived **candidate coefficient construction**, not as a global contour, Betti activation, physical-period covector, or cosmological observable. The existing identity supplies divisibility; it does not yet supply the relative complex, connecting morphism, or survival theorem.
