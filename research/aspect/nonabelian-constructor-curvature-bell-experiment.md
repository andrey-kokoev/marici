# Non-Abelian constructor-curvature Bell experiment

## Question

Can two locally invisible constructor words have the same coarse Bell action yet fail to compose interchangeably, leaving a measurable commutator fringe?

## Construction

Each Bell wing receives two registered local ancilla compilers, `U` and `V`. A path-control interferometer compares the composite words `UV` and `VU`. The ordinary polarization result and every no-click are retained. Forgetting the path control and local ancilla must return the same inclusive Bell packet for both words.

The complex order-comparison fringe is the overlap between the two word outputs. Equivalently, it reads a matrix element of the group commutator `U† V† U V`. A nonunit value or nonzero phase is constructor curvature: the local completion square does not flatten even though both routes have the same coarse action.

This is not a claim that time or causal order is indefinite. `UV` and `VU` are two typed composites in the instrument category. The experiment asks whether their comparison loop is flat.

## Controls

- commuting-axis hostile: replace `V` with a compiler on the same axis as `U`; the fringe must return to one;
- orientation reversal: exchange the two arms; the fringe must complex-conjugate;
- dephased control: destroy path coherence; the fringe must vanish;
- Bell pushforward: inclusive CHSH and local marginals must remain invariant;
- compiler/frame/reset binding: any change invalidates the loop receipt.

## Explanation

A single constructor-loop phase detects Abelian holonomy. The word-commutator detects curvature: failure of local comparison maps to compose interchangeably. Coarse channel equivalence therefore identifies objects while discarding their higher composition law. The curvature fringe recovers part of that discarded law.

The closest literature includes quantum switches, coherent control of channels, and photonic non-Abelian holonomy. The proposed combination with a spatial Bell obstruction and exact same-ledger pushforward is a candidate frontier, not yet a priority claim.

The current execution is an exact two-dimensional ancilla fixture. Physical compilers and detector packets remain unrun.
