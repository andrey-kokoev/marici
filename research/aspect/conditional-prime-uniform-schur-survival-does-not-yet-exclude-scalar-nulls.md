# Conditional prime-uniform Schur survival does not yet exclude scalar nulls

## Question

What follows from the exact prime-uniform inequality

\[
\lVert d_p\rVert_\nu^2<8(1-M_\Phi)^2a_p
\]

when the shifted-history and source-incidence constructors are retained as explicit premises?

## Conditional Schur statement

Assume, for every prime-labelled block:

1. the shifted-history factors are the source operators \(I\pm iH\);
2. \(M_\Phi<1/2\), hence their Gram blocks obey
   \[
   D_{p,\pm}\ge \frac{(1-M_\Phi)^2}{2}I;
   \]
3. the source odd incidence is the co-moving endpoint/Wronskian current \(d_p\);
4. radical compatibility permits the Schur return
   \[
   R_p=C_pD_p^\dagger C_p^*;
   \]
5. the endpoint coordinate has raw loading \(a_p\).

The inverse bound for \(D_{p,\pm}\), together with the certified inequality, gives a strict residual in the theta-mass endpoint coordinate. The exact rational margins are greater than \(1.106754\) at \(p=2\) and greater than \(0.784\) for \(p\ge3\). Thus this one observed coordinate survives auxiliary Schur elimination uniformly in the prime label.

## Why scalar-null exclusion does not follow

A scalar null belongs to an Evans or scalar readout. The mass inequality controls one diagonal Schur residual. It does not construct any of the following arrows:

- the independent source equality \(J_{\rm src}=J_{\rm top}\) in a common Green quotient;
- a second diagonal residual or Green-orthogonal vanishing needed for the full Pauli-observed frame;
- the Schur--Evans comparison \(S_X(z)=u_X(z)F_X(z)\) with source-derived, nowhere-zero \(u_X\);
- a no-dark-source incidence on the completed retained carrier;
- the completed Green identity, index-zero theorem, and norm-stable cutoff passage.

The first item is already deferred at the missing labelled arithmetic mate square. The published no-dark-source packet is explicitly withdrawn on the completed carrier because the unweighted diagonal forcing is not an object of the retained labelled history space.

## Hostile finite block

Let

\[
A=1,\qquad D=1,\qquad C=0.
\]

Then the controlled Schur residual is positive. Independently choose the scalar Evans readout \(F(z)=z\). It has a scalar null at \(z=0\). Without a sourced comparison cell between the positive Schur block and \(F\), Schur survival and scalar nullity coexist. This falsifies the implication from the mass margin alone.

## Claim boundary

The demonstrated result is a prime-uniform, one-coordinate Schur-survival theorem conditional on typed shifted-history and incidence premises. It is not scalar-null confinement and does not establish any missing source comparison.

## Disposition

Reject the proposed direct implication. The next executable mathematical gate is to prove the full two-diagonal Pauli residual theorem or exhibit genuine Green orthogonality. Scalar-null exclusion remains conditional additionally on a source-derived Schur--Evans comparison and completed no-dark-source/Fredholm premises.
