# Aspect full optical wishlist

This is one staged apparatus. Each rung removes a blindness and retains every earlier hostile.

1. Relational metric frame: diagonal purity and all pairwise HOM fidelities.
2. Oriented frame: ordered triple Bargmann X/Y quadratures, with mirror and reversal controls.
3. Compiler redundancy: sequential projector loop and three-copy cyclic shift agree as complex numbers.
4. Bell cell: randomized CHSH settings, local bound 2, quantum fixture 2 sqrt(2).
5. Loophole ledger: efficiency, setting predictability, coincidence window, memory, drift, and no-click outcomes are first-class data.
6. Device-independent readout: an admitted CHSH lower confidence bound yields a randomness lower bound; a target value never does.
7. Network cell: entanglement swapping is checked against bilocal-source and ordinary Bell-local models.
8. Context cell: a five-cycle KCBS witness separates compatibility assumptions from spacelike Bell assumptions.
9. Composition cell: independent sources, phase references, resets, and repeated-use covariance require explicit certificates.
10. Source-lift cell: finite-field amplitudes remain quarantined until a source-authorized characteristic-zero lift, normalization, and analyzer compilation exist.

Frozen fixtures are: mirror contrast 2/(3 sqrt(3)); CHSH target 2 sqrt(2); symmetric efficiency threshold 2/(1+sqrt(2)); KCBS target sqrt(5); and the standard CHSH guessing-probability bound.

Promotion requires a raw record, calibration authority, uncertainty rule, hostile outcomes, and claim boundary. Pairwise agreement cannot promote orientation; a Bell target cannot promote randomness; a finite-field row cannot promote an optical analyzer.

The next build uses one source and detector ledger for the mirror-cocycle and CHSH cells, retains all no-click outcomes, records setting-source health and timing, and measures the cyclic observable with both compilers.
