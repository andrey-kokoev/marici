# Coefficient-residual obstruction classifier DPC

## Question

How should a coefficient discrepancy surviving an attempted comparison be classified without fitting it away?

## Claim boundary

This packet gives a typed decision procedure and exact finite witnesses. It is irredundant for the declared input fields, not a theorem that every future obstruction belongs to these classes.

## Required comparison datum

A comparison request must declare coefficients \(x\in X\), \(y\in Y\), a common codomain \(Z\), and canonical maps

\[
\alpha:X\to Z,
\qquad
\beta:Y\to Z.
\]

Only then is

\[
\Delta=\alpha(x)-\beta(y)
\]

defined. The classifier uses the earliest applicable gate:

1. **type obstruction:** \(X\) or \(Y\) is undeclared;
2. **comparison obstruction:** no authorized common codomain or canonical map exists;
3. **presentation behavior:** an admitted gauge/frame map makes \(\Delta=0\);
4. **mate obstruction:** the unit–counit mate equation has nonzero residual;
5. **coherencer obstruction:** the two composition routes have nonzero residual;
6. **pairing obstruction:** transported state–effect values disagree;
7. **record obstruction:** pairings agree but the physical-record square fails;
8. **nonfaithful quotient ambiguity:** images agree in \(Z\), but the comparison map is not monic;
9. **source-law obstruction:** all declared structural maps apply and a source equation still fails.

The final class is a defect if the source law is proved and implementation violates it; otherwise it is an unresolved observation or an independently sourced parameter.

## DPC cycle

### Governing conjecture

Every surviving coefficient discrepancy can be called a coherence defect.

### Rivals

A discrepancy may instead be undefined, presentation-dependent, hidden by a nonfaithful quotient, caused by record descent, or left independent by missing source authority.

### Risky consequences

The classifier must distinguish fixtures having equal raw numbers but different types, unequal raw numbers reconciled by gauge, equal quotient images with unequal sources, preserved probabilities with failed records, and genuine nonzero mate or compositor residuals.

### Falsification attempt

The checker constructs one exact witness for every class. It also injects a tempting but invalid classifier that compares raw scalars before checking types; the equal-number/different-type fixture defeats it.

### Residual

The classes depend on declared structure. A new canonical operation can refine a comparison obstruction into a later class. Classification is therefore versioned by the available maps, not inferred from scalar appearance.

### Disposition

The governing conjecture is rejected. “Coefficient mismatch” is not one obstruction type. The typed decision procedure is provisionally retained.

## Explanation

The residual acquires meaning only at the first square that is both defined and noncommuting. Earlier failures mean the comparison itself was unauthorized; later failures must not be blamed on an earlier layer.

## Disposition

Report the first failed typed gate, its exact residual if defined, and whether the governing source law is proved. Never repair a later residual by silently changing an earlier coefficient.
