# Isotropy-preservation gate for probe matching

## Question

When does a `Set`-valued matching object or its orbit set erase automorphism information needed to classify a probe rewrite?

## Claim boundary

This packet compares finite sets, fixed points, orbit sets, and action groupoids. It does not construct a groupoid-valued matching limit, homotopy limit, stack, gauge quotient, or physical symmetry.

## Swap action fixture

Let \(S_2=\{1,s\}\) act on

\[
M=\{0,1\}^2
\]

by swapping coordinates. The ordinary fixed-point set is

\[
M^{S_2}=\{(0,0),(1,1)\}.
\]

The coarse orbit set has three elements: two diagonal singleton orbits and one mixed orbit containing \((0,1)\) and \((1,0)\).

The action groupoid \(M\mathbin{//}S_2\) retains all four objects and the morphisms generated by the action. Stabilizer orders are

\[
|\operatorname{Stab}(0,0)|=2,
\quad
|\operatorname{Stab}(1,1)|=2,
\quad
|\operatorname{Stab}(0,1)|=|\operatorname{Stab}(1,0)|=1.
\]

Passing to connected components recovers the three-element orbit set and discards these automorphism groups.

## Same orbit set, different isotropy

Compare with the trivial \(S_2\)-action on a three-element set \(N\). Its action groupoid also has three connected components, so the two coarse orbit sets have equal cardinality. Every object of \(N\mathbin{//}S_2\), however, has stabilizer order two.

Using one representative per component, the stabilizer-order profiles are

\[
[2,2,1]
\quad\text{and}\quad
[2,2,2].
\]

An equivalence of groupoids preserves automorphism groups up to isomorphism. The action groupoids are therefore not equivalent despite indistinguishable orbit-set cardinality.

## Relation to ordinary limits

For a one-object index groupoid \(BS_2\) acting on a set, the ordinary `Set`-valued limit is the fixed-point set. It answers which sections satisfy every internal action equation. It does not retain the stabilizer profile of the full action presentation or the nonfixed orbit.

The action groupoid is used here only as an explicit symmetry-retaining object. It is not automatically the matching object and is not asserted to be a homotopy limit.

## Escalation gate

Remain in `Set` when the claim concerns only:

- existence of compatible sections;
- equality or cardinality of ordinary matching fibers;
- a separately recorded external action whose stabilizers are irrelevant to rewrite classification.

Require symmetry-retaining data—at minimum an action groupoid or equivalent stabilizer certificate—when the claim depends on:

- automorphism groups of matching data;
- free versus fixed occurrence action;
- equivariant rewrite equivalence rather than coarse orbit equality;
- preservation of isotropy under transport.

A full groupoid-valued or homotopical semantics requires a separate constructor and theorem. The gate does not supply it.

## Disposition

Coarse orbit sets and ordinary fixed-point sets erase different parts of an action presentation. Equal orbit counts do not preserve isotropy. SCC may remain `Set`-valued for current local fiber claims, but an isotropy-sensitive rewrite must carry explicit stabilizer/action-groupoid evidence and cannot be certified from orbit records alone.
