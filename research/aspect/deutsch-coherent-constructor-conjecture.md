# Deutsch coherent-constructor conjecture

## Conjecture

For every finite family of jointly realizable physical constructors, if the source theory independently constructs typed associators between bracketings claimed to realize the same composite, then those associators obey every associahedron coherence identity after full transport in every admitted realization.

Equivalently, physical constructor networks form at least a bicategorical composition system. Curvature and nontrivial associators are possible; incoherent associators are not.

This is a Deutsch-style conjecture because it states a counterfactual constraint on possible transformations, not merely a regularity of observed outcomes. It says that source-authorized implementations of one composite cannot obtain contradictory full outputs solely by choosing different rebracketing paths.

Joint realizability and a common coarse pushforward are gates, not constructors of the associators. SCC must reject any test packet that does not carry source-derived associator maps, their types, and their authority.

## Why strict associativity is not conjectured

Different bracketings may be implemented in different frames, environments, or compiler layouts. Requiring literal matrix equality would mistake presentation for physics. The invariant requirement is the pentagon: all legitimate rebracketing paths give the same transported result.

## Decisive test

Use four constructors and the five bracketings of their composite. Derive every pentagon edge from one integral or polynomial source packet, specialize the same data at bad primes and a good control prime, and compare the two paths as exact typed matrix identities before scalarization. A defect that vanishes generically but survives in one exceptional fiber immediately falsifies the proposed global coherence.

## Falsification

The conjecture is falsified by a nonzero full-process pentagon residual that survives:

- exact common coarse pushforward;
- informationally complete edge tomography;
- compiler, frame, environment, and reset binding;
- drift and injected-phase controls;
- independent construction of the two pentagon paths;
- repetition at exceptional calibration loci.

Scalar fringe disagreement is not a falsifier because projection does not preserve composition.

The floating fixture attacks the conjecture with frame gauges, scalar projection, injected edge defects, and an exceptional-context defect. Its small residual is a numerical witness, not proof of exact closure. Promotion now requires an exact source packet and exact specialization checker. It does not physically test the conjecture.
