# Morphism faithfulness of the pro-context tower

Object separation does not generally imply morphism separation.

In the free path presentation, the words O1 O2 and O2 O1 have the same source
and target and therefore the same endpoint pro-record. They remain distinct
morphisms until the independent-generator square is admitted. Endpoint
instrumentation is consequently not faithful on the free path category.

After quotienting the bounded cubical pilot by all independent-generator
swaps, every path has one normal form determined by its occurrence support and
fiber depth. The resulting reachability category is thin. On this restricted
quotient, object faithfulness does imply full faithfulness because every hom
set has at most one element.

This does not settle Entry 4013. Its exact relation module remains absent.
Without it, we cannot prove that all executable contexts descend, nor that no
additional parallel operations survive. More detector range cannot repair this
gap.

A synthetic silent-loop hostile makes the boundary explicit. A nonidentity
endomorphism acting trivially on every endpoint record is invisible to the
current tower. We do not claim such a loop exists in the source. We require the
source presentation to exclude it or a source-authorized operation-sensitive
readout to detect it.
