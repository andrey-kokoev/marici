# The seven-axis compiler separates coefficient character from action

## Live defect caught by transfer

The six-axis compiler separated scalar, packet, incidence, history, path, and
coefficient predicates.  Benincasa's cosmology repair shows that this is still
not enough.

A co-moving detector may carry the correct deck-odd coefficient character,
and its coefficient-cycle pairing may descend, while no operational selector
action exists.  Coefficient compatibility does not construct an action.

## Seventh axis

Add an independent action axis:

- `authorized`: a source-derived operation acts on the declared carrier;
- `missing`: the required action or conditionalization map is absent;
- `unknown`: the acquisition did not test operational authority.

The coefficient axis continues to record:

- `native`;
- `requires_extension`;
- `unknown`.

The path axis continues to record:

- `admissible`;
- `disconnected`;
- `unknown`.

No implication is admitted among these three axes.

## Distinguishing fixtures

### Cosmology coefficient without selector

`coefficient=native`, `path=unknown`, `action=missing`.

The source character and descended pairing exist.  The operational
conditionalization map does not.

### Selective projective gate

`coefficient=requires_extension`, `path=disconnected`, `action=missing`.

The desired endpoint is algebraically defined, the standard continuous path
leaves the reflection-fixed locus, the mixed generator uses complex
coefficients, and the physical controlled implementation remains absent.

### Optical transmission zero

`coefficient=unknown`, `path=unknown`, `action=unknown`.

The state/output witness alone makes no source-authority claim.

## Consequence for theta optics

The co-moving odd Schur return row has an exact coefficient formula and an
exact first jet.  That does not yet prove that the theta/Tate source contains
an authorized operation implementing the return incidence.  The local optical
emulator demonstrates mathematical and physical executability on its own
carrier; it does not establish arithmetic source authority.

The higher coherencer audit must therefore ask separately:

1. does the coefficient transform correctly?
2. is there a path or horizontal transport realizing it?
3. is the required action source-derived on the declared object?
