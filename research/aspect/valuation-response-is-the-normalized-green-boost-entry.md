# Valuation response is the normalized Green boost entry

## Moving-tail propagator

The homogeneous part of the symmetric/antisymmetric moving-tail system is

`d_x [m,n]^T = -L_z [m,n]^T`,

with

`L_z=[[1/2,z],[z,1/2]]`.

Across a valuation interval of length `ell`, its propagator is

`K_ell(z)=exp(-ell L_z)`

`=exp(-ell/2)[[cosh(ell z),-sinh(ell z)],`

`              [-sinh(ell z),cosh(ell z)]].`

The off-diagonal entry is the reciprocal-sector mixing generated by the
Green transport.

## Exact adelic-to-Green comparison

Normalize that mixing by the vacuum interval loss `1-exp(-ell)`.  Then

`2[K_ell(z)]_12/[1-exp(-ell)]`

`=-sinh(ell z)/sinh(ell/2)`

`=rho_ell(z)`.

This is exactly the normalized valuation response family.  For a finite
prime, `ell=log p`; its differential limit as `ell` tends to zero is the
archimedean response `-2z`.

Therefore the odd adelic boundary column is not merely compatible with the
moving-endpoint Green system.  It is a normalized matrix coefficient of its
homogeneous propagator.

## Coherence properties

- Reciprocal exchange sends `z` to `-z` and reverses the off-diagonal entry.
- Propagators compose by interval addition:
  `K_(ell+eta)=K_ell K_eta`.
- The valuation normalization uses the same vacuum attenuation appearing in
  the diagonal factor.
- The archimedean column is the infinitesimal boost generator of the same
  propagator.

These properties construct the local adelic-to-Green comparison cell that
was missing from the typed braid cube.

## Common forcing

The full moving-tail flow is affine, with forcing vector `(-f,0)`.  The
comparison above identifies the homogeneous response block.  The source
forcing must be carried separately through the Duhamel term

`-integral_0^ell K_(ell-u)(z)[f(u),0]^T du`.

Omitting this term preserves the odd response symbol but loses the
common-endpoint current and therefore does not close the global braid cube.
The local comparison cell is complete only as the pair `(K_ell,Duhamel_f)`.

## Optical implementation

Implement `L_z` as a two-mode coupled drift generator.  Measure the complete
transfer matrix over interval `ell`, not only its odd output.  The compiler
then checks:

- the normalized off-diagonal equals `rho_ell(z)`;
- interval concatenation matches matrix multiplication;
- reciprocal reversal flips only the odd mixing sign;
- the measured affine displacement equals the Duhamel response to the shared
  endpoint injection.

This supplies a direct finite-cutoff construction for the previously open
adelic--Green face.  Completion continuity and the higher braid residual
remain separate tests.
