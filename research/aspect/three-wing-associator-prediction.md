# Three-wing associator prediction

## Question

Can the fiberwise, native-arity calculus identify a new optical observable
whose value is not already contained in the paired apparatus?

## Source and lower-order null

Prepare a three-wing path-marker state with coherence `gamma` between the
carrier branches `000` and `111`. Scan the summed analyzer phase

\[
\Phi=\phi_A+\phi_B+\phi_C.
\]

Tracing out any wing deletes the `000`--`111` coherence. Therefore every
phase-sensitive one-wing and two-wing marginal is exactly flat. No collection
of pairwise fringe measurements reconstructs the target phase.

## Direct ternary prediction

A coherent three-wing parity mate measures

\[
C_{ABC}(\Phi)=\gamma\cos\Phi.
\]

At `gamma=3/5` and four quarter-turn phases, the exact curve is

```text
3/5, 0, -3/5, 0.
```

This is a native ternary relational signal with zero phase visibility on every
proper subsystem.

## Associator experiment

Implement the same ternary analyzer by two coherent sewing orders:

```text
left:   (A sew B) sew C
right:  A sew (B sew C)
```

Neither intermediate sewing may measure, discard, or marginalize its open
carrier port. Match the two circuits' full transfer matrices, vacuum ports,
loss, delay, and phase conventions.

Ordinary quantum composition predicts

\[
C_L(\Phi)=C_R(\Phi)=\gamma\cos\Phi.
\]

Hence the associator residual

\[
\mathcal A(\Phi)=C_L(\Phi)-C_R(\Phi)
\]

must vanish at every phase.

## New discovery channel

The calculus predicts where a previously unmeasured higher relational datum
could live: not in any local or pairwise marginal, and not in the direct
ternary visibility, but in a reproducible bracketing residual.

A simple hostile higher phase `omega=pi/2` would shift the right curve to

```text
0, -3/5, 0, 3/5
```

and produce residual

```text
3/5, 3/5, -3/5, -3/5.
```

The calculus does not derive that nonzero phase. It derives the channel, the
standard null, and the fact that every lower-order audit is blind to it.

## Falsification protocol

Randomize direct, left-bracketed, right-bracketed, and deliberately destructive
pairwise routes within one epoch. Retain every herald, single, multiple, and
no-click result. Interleave coherent calibration pulses through every vacuum
port and use the ABBA word on ancillary phase nulls.

The current architecture fails if the direct ternary fringe is absent despite
verified source coherence, or if a proper marginal carries the declared
ternary phase. Ordinary associative quantum composition fails if the matched
left-right residual remains nonzero under circuit exchange, phase reversal,
source reversal, and detector reassignment.

## Deutschean explanation

The experiment asks whether two construction paths that preserve the same open
carrier distinctions implement the same possible transformation. The null is
not merely equality of observed final probabilities; it is coherence of the
composition law across counterfactual bracketings.
