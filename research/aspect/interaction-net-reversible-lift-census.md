# Reversible-lift census for balanced interaction-net optics

The balanced-word theorem is exact, but the current pilot does not instantiate
its physical hypotheses.

Totalized occurrence addition sends both absent and present states to present,
so it is not injective. Restricted to the absent sector it is an isometry
between proper subspaces, not a unitary on the declared state space. A
reversible lift must retain the old bit in an environment or supply a
source-authorized coherent deletion inverse.

The K operation is a unilateral successor on nonnegative depth. It is
injective but not surjective. A unitary implementation needs a backward
successor or an explicit boundary/history port that returns catalytically.

Duplication and deletion are also not reversible on unknown inputs. Their
environment records cannot be ignored in an interference claim because they
can reveal which word was applied.

The smallest balanced occurrence pair does not help. O1 O2 and O2 O1 agree on
every Boolean state and are identified by the admitted cubical square. A
balanced interferometer would therefore predict no ordered-residue signal.

The next valid target must be a source-authorized reversible coherent pair that
survives the exact relation quotient. It also needs common branch control,
which-word erasure, and word-independent apparatus reset.
