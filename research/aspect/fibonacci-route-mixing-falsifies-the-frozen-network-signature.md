# Fibonacci route mixing falsifies the frozen network signature

## Frozen theory

The preceding vacuity audit proposed the smallest cross-sector signature already used in the session. It has now been frozen mechanically with cell creation disabled.

Its admitted cells are:

- Gram or pairing cells;
- scalar braided-exchange cells;
- scalar relative-lift cells;
- augmentation cells.

It admits restriction, extension, and contextual-exposure arrows at maximum declared cell depth two. It explicitly forbids retyping a Gram matrix as a composition associator.

## Hostile packet

For Fibonacci fusion,

\[
\tau\otimes\tau=1\oplus\tau.
\]

The two routes by which three \(\tau\) objects fuse to \(\tau\) are related by

\[
F=
\begin{pmatrix}
\varphi^{-1}&\varphi^{-1/2}\\
\varphi^{-1/2}&-\varphi^{-1}
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\varphi=\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}.
\]

Exactly,

\[
F^*F=I,
\qquad
F^2=I.
\]

But every entry is nonzero. The associator mixes both factorization routes into both outputs. It is not a scalar phase, a scalar lift, a permutation, or a scalar times a permutation.

## Decision

The frozen v1 signature rejects the packet.

This is the first genuine falsification of the preregistered network theory. The hostile residual has a forbidden type: non-scalar mixing between distinct composition routes. Cell creation is disabled, so the theory cannot rename the residual as a new associator and pass.

A Gram cell cannot rescue it. The same matrix can numerically define a pairing, but its typed role here is comparison between two composition brackets. Reusing it would violate the frozen typing law and restore the vacuity defect.

## Meaning

The rejection shows that the v1 grammar is too small for non-Abelian fusion. It does not refute bivariant network mathematics. It refutes the claim that the smallest signature extracted from theta optics, scalar braid lifts, and augmentation already transfers universally.

The disciplined disposition is:

1. record v1 as falsified;
2. do not mutate it;
3. propose a separate v2 containing a matrix-valued route-associator type;
4. preregister pentagon, dagger, fusion multiplicity, and contextual-exposure laws for that type;
5. test v2 on packets not used to design it.

## Transfer back to tower syntax

The hostile distinguishes additive rank from recursive route structure. Two fusion routes do not form \(2+1\) merely because the fusion space has dimension two. Their non-diagonal associator is an irreducible cell relating two bracketings. Tower syntax must retain that cell; channel arithmetic alone loses it.
