# First raw-acquisition portfolio

The first six-rung portfolio is now specified by raw records rather than
prototype costs.

Environment-port tomography uses forty cells: the full four-input by
three-system-basis by three-environment-basis science grid plus four marker
controls. At one million attempted trials per cell its load is forty million.
It retains every no-click event, binds one coherent environment-port manifest
and phase reference, rotates all cells over every block position, and requires
an environment control that separates states while the system-population
control remains blind.

Sealed controller replay uses 64 replay cells crossing bound predecessor,
actual predecessor, and target, plus 16 live-control cells with bound equal to
actual predecessor. Its load is 80 million attempts. The removed 48 live
counterfactual-bound cells identify nothing because the live controller has no
sealed transcript to bind counterfactually. The
sealed transcript, digest, and correction command depend only on the bound
predecessor, target, and calibration epoch. The actual predecessor is excluded
from the controller input manifest. Live invariance cannot substitute for
replay invariance.

The comb-referenced flavor comparison uses eight cells and eight million
attempts. It identifies the exact difference between the `a=1` and `a=2`
comparison responses while retaining the horizon, flavor, detector, and comb
components of the quaternary record. It measures the realized comparison; it
does not derive the source selector.

The arithmetic member is the common-prefix mixed-prime cell, with 400 exact
verification cases reported by its owner. Arithmetic cases and optical trials
remain separate resource axes.

The resulting campaign envelope is 128 million attempted optical trials, 128
optical setting cells, and 400 arithmetic verification cases. These numbers
are derived from the acquisition contracts. They become an executable calendar
only after the operator binds measured shot rates, setup durations, and actual
environment-port availability.

The required binding fields are frozen in
`contracts/apparatus-capacity-binding.v1.json`. The repository currently
contains none of those measurements. The acquisition schedule is complete;
the calendar schedule remains deliberately unbound.

Available optical seconds is itself a required independent measurement. The
calendar compiler exhausts all optical subsets under that bound, maximizes
independent quotient directions and sector coverage, then minimizes occupied
seconds. It does not assume the apparatus can execute the entire acquisition
portfolio.

Finite exclusion ports remain divisible by the common scalar `X`; their first
jets diagnose multiplicity but add no transverse value-level confinement rank.
The newly admitted replacement is the sign-local relative residue modulo
`(X)`, governed on the seam by the anti-holomorphic law
`J(1/conj(w))=-conj(J(w))`. The seam current is purely imaginary, not zero:
at `w=(3+4i)/5`, `w-w^-1=8i/5`. Scalar-current-zero and holomorphic
fixed-point substitutions are therefore prohibited. It remains unscheduled
until the source derives polarization as absence of normal current, proves
that normal current faithfully detects radial displacement, and supplies an
acquisition contract.
