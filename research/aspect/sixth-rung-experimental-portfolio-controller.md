# Sixth rung: control the experiment portfolio

## Question

Once ontology extensions have been admitted or deferred, which experiments
receive finite apparatus capacity, and how do we prevent aliases, cheap loops,
closed branches, or authority from capturing the schedule?

## Controller

Rung six performs exhaustive subset search under a frozen budget. Its objective
is lexicographic: maximize independent quotient directions separated, maximize
independent sectors represented, minimize cost, and use stable keys only for a
final deterministic tie break. Proposal count is never an information metric.

Candidates must be admitted, faithful to the claimed capability, open, and
dependency-ready. Gain coordinates are evaluated after common-source
factorization. This prevents several isometric charts of one state from
counterfeiting several independent discoveries.

## Prototype portfolio

Under budget ten, the first portfolio is environment tomography, controller
transcript replay, and mixed-prime incidence. It costs nine and separates four
independent quotient directions across optics, control, and arithmetic. After
those directions retire, the second portfolio schedules the flux dual-gain and
three-back Hankel probes. Every live faithful candidate is scheduled within two
cycles.

Those dimensionless costs are now superseded as scheduling authority. They
remain only as a regression fixture for the controller logic.

## Acquisition-derived portfolio

The authoritative acquisition loads are read from four contracts. Environment
tomography requires forty million attempted trials over forty cells. Sealed
controller replay requires 80 million trials over 80 cells. The
comb-referenced flavor comparison requires eight million trials over eight
cells. Mixed-prime incidence requires 400 exact arithmetic verification cases,
kept on a distinct resource axis.

The resulting frozen campaign contains all four admitted members and separates
five quotient directions. Its optical load is 128 million attempts over 128
setting cells. This is acquisition-authoritative, not yet calendar-authoritative:
measured shot rates, reconfiguration durations, and coherent environment-port
availability are not present in the repository and therefore are not invented.

The theta transition is explicit. Finite binary repair is retired because the
direct moving-seam ternary constructor exists. Prime-local chart probes are
suppressed as unfaithful duplicates of one seam state. The surviving capability
is mixed-prime incidence through the common prefix current.

## Hostile schedulers

The checker rejects rejected/deferred work, budget violation, dependency
inversion, closed-branch persistence, duplicate gain inflation, cheap alias
substitution, starvation by cheap loops, speaker/name capture, desired-verdict
optimization, and failure to rescore after results.

## Boundary

Optimality is exact only for the frozen candidates, costs, quotient gains,
dependencies, budget, and objective order. Rung six schedules experiments; it
does not make those inputs infallible.
