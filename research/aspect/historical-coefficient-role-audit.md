# Historical coefficient-role audit

## Question

Do documented Marici coefficient multiplicities arise from the newly isolated equipment and record-module roles?

## Claim boundary

This audit classifies three representative prior cases by their declared source types. It does not infer a universal explanation from repeated vocabulary, equal dimensions, or numerical resemblance.

## Governing conjecture

Historical coefficient proliferation is largely the coordinate shadow of one construction viewed through different canonical operations: gauge, mate, composition, quotient lens, or readout action.

## Rivals

1. The differences are arbitrary convention choices.
2. Every displayed coefficient should be one literal scalar.
3. Each coefficient is an independent physical parameter.
4. Several mathematically distinct mechanisms were conflated under the word coefficient.

## Case A: reflection-component path tomography

Source: `research/aspect/reflection-component-path-tomography-and-coefficient-authority.md`, especially lines 39, 51, and 66–82.

The source changes from a sheet presentation to an electric/magnetic Hadamard frame. A generator acting on a rational basis can acquire an imaginary coordinate, so admissibility requires preservation of the coefficient ring or an authorized scalar extension.

Classification: **explained presentation behavior plus scalar-extension gate**. This is not a forward/backward mate discrepancy and not a new physical coefficient. A frame map relates the presentations, but a map over the original rational coefficient object need not exist. Equipment language helps by keeping the frame transport typed; the essential obstruction is coefficient-ring authority.

## Case B: coefficient lenses on constructor words

Source: `research/kitaev/coefficient-lenses-are-quotients-of-constructor-history.md`, especially lines 18–22, 38–102, 156–210, and 218–246.

Additive, determinant, and ordered-holonomy lenses send one typed constructor word to different codomains and forget different relations. The source explicitly states that a hierarchy exists only through declared forgetful maps.

Classification: **explained readout-module behavior**. These are not competing coordinates of one scalar. They are distinct readout functors or module projections from richer transport data. Equality is meaningful only after a declared forgetful map sends both values into a common quotient. This is the strongest historical support for the equipment-plus-module explanation.

## Case C: arithmetic codiagonal loading

Source: `research/nima/arithmetic-loading-commutes-with-the-oriented-radial-codiagonal-under-one-explicit-prime-majorant.md`, especially lines 50–64 and 98–103.

Two coefficient systems satisfy the same sufficient prime-majorant and define two admissible analytic assemblies. The source explicitly refuses to identify the coefficients or authorize either as the canonical G4 loading.

Classification: **unresolved source-selection ambiguity**. Shared convergence and commutation properties do not construct a gauge, mate, quotient, or module comparison. Equipment-plus-module typing prevents the false identification, but does not explain the difference. A source-derived selection or comparison arrow remains missing.

## Risky consequences and falsification attempt

The conjecture predicts that canonical maps should classify every audited difference as one typed transformation. The three cases refute that universal form:

- Case A is a frame/scalar-extension issue;
- Case B is a family of quotient readouts from one richer transport object;
- Case C has no admitted comparison map and remains unresolved.

The companion checker verifies the exact source statements and file digests, and deliberately tests that convergence of two assemblies does not supply coefficient equality.

## Residual

No audited case yet instantiates the exact forward-map versus backward-mate numerical relation from the rational fixture. The new structure explains Case B, organizes Case A, and blocks an illicit conclusion in Case C. It does not collapse all coefficient discrepancies into one mechanism.

## Disposition

The governing conjecture is **revised**: coefficient proliferation is evidence of suppressed typing, but the suppressed type may be a frame, coefficient object, quotient lens, mate, compositor, module action, or unresolved source choice. The strongest common explanation is not that all coefficients are images of one cell. It is that scalar comparison was attempted before constructing a common typed codomain.

The decisive rule is:

> No coefficient comparison without a declared common codomain and canonical maps into it.
