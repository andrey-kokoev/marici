# Univalent reconstruction of the Marici coherence pyramid

## Question

What is the Marici coherence pyramid when identity is typed through source-authorized equivalence, univalent completion, displayed structure, and readout descent?

## Claim boundary

This packet proposes the categorical architecture and transfers its ownership to `marici.Voevodsky` for audit and development. It does not construct the required localization, Rezk completion, displayed univalent categories, moving operation, motivic comparison, or physical readout.

## Presentation category

Let \(\mathcal C_{\rm pres}\) be the category of fully structured source presentations. An object includes the dependent fields already required by Marici: carrier and support; coefficients; constructor grammar; transport; quotient; completion; probes; readout interface; and authority. Morphisms preserve the declared structure. Coordinate equality and probe indistinguishability are not primitive identity.

## Source-authorized localization

Let \(W\) contain only proved source-authorized equivalences: structured isomorphisms, chain-homotopy equivalences, descent equivalences, lattice-preserving unitaries, moving-lemma quasi-isomorphisms, and other typed comparison equivalences. Form

\[
\mathcal C_{\rm loc}=\mathcal C_{\rm pres}[W^{-1}].
\]

The class \(W\) is generated by source structure, not by the kernel of a selected probe family.

## Univalent completion

Apply the appropriate Rezk or univalent completion

\[
\mathcal C_{\rm loc}\longrightarrow\widehat{\mathcal C}.
\]

The target must satisfy

\[
(X=Y)\simeq\operatorname{Equiv}_{\rm str}(X,Y).
\]

Automorphisms remain loops in the object identity type. A set quotient that erases these paths is not a substitute.

## Attachment fibration

Attachments form a displayed univalent category

\[
\operatorname{Att}:\widehat{\mathcal C}^{op}
\longrightarrow\mathbf{Cat}_{\rm univ}.
\]

The fiber \(\operatorname{Att}(X)\) contains the Green extensions, boundary attachments, theta realizations, auxiliary sectors, and other constructions admitted over \(X\). A morphism \(f:X\to Y\) induces pullback

\[
f^*: \operatorname{Att}(Y)\to\operatorname{Att}(X).
\]

The body formerly called the coherence pyramid is the Grothendieck total category

\[
\int_{\widehat{\mathcal C}}\operatorname{Att}.
\]

## Higher coherence

Functoriality is carried by specified paths or equivalences

\[
\mathrm{id}_X^*\simeq\mathrm{Id},
\qquad
(g\circ f)^*\simeq f^*g^*,
\]

together with naturality, unit, associativity, and higher coherence cells. The old “faces” are identity terms between composite routes, not geometric faces forced by counting.

## Readout fibration and fourth face

Stable records form another indexed category

\[
\operatorname{Rec}:\widehat{\mathcal C}^{op}
\longrightarrow\mathbf{Cat}_{\rm univ}.
\]

Readout is a transformation

\[
Q:\operatorname{Att}\Longrightarrow\operatorname{Rec}.
\]

For \(f:X\to Y\), the independent fourth obligation is readout descent:

\[
Q_X(f^*A)
\simeq
f_{\rm rec}^*Q_Y(A).
\]

Attachment coherence does not supply this comparison.

## Moving representatives

A moving lemma supplies a restricted presentation category

\[
\mathcal C_{\rm good}\hookrightarrow\mathcal C_{\rm pres}
\]

whose inclusion becomes an equivalence after the authorized localization or homology. It justifies better-positioned representatives inside an existing identity class. It is not observational forgetting.

## Theta incidence

Theta incidence is a realization morphism

\[
S_\theta:X_{\rm Euler}\to X_\theta.
\]

On the full Euler Hilbert completion it is compact and not bounded below, so it is not an equivalence there. It may instead be a noninvertible realization, an equivalence onto a differently completed pullback object, or one leg of an arithmetic–analytic graph object. The chosen typing must be source-derived.

## Readout truncation

A physical readout may truncate higher identity information:

\[
\widehat{\mathcal C}
\longrightarrow
\|\widehat{\mathcal C}\|_0
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Rec}_{\rm phys}.
\]

This downstream truncation cannot define upstream identity retroactively. A zero matching residual may certify presentation equivalence; physical nullity requires a separately descended physical readout.

## Four-obligation translation

1. Forward realization is a morphism in \(\widehat{\mathcal C}\).
2. Attachment transport is the displayed functor \(\operatorname{Att}\).
3. Route coherence consists of identity types between composite pullbacks and their higher paths.
4. Readout descent is naturality of \(Q\).

## Governing conjecture

> The Marici coherence pyramid is the Grothendieck total category of a displayed univalent attachment category over a univalent completion of source-structured presentations, equipped with a separately coherent readout transformation. Source-authorized localization constructs equivalence; moving lemmas improve representatives within it; readouts supply downstream truncations.
