# A standalone reset need not be a common terminal operation

Reset qualification in one optical context does not establish transport across the apparatus. The hostile passes standalone qualification and has zero insertion residual in fifteen contexts. It leaves residual `1/5` only for the right bracketing at coherence `3/5` under the second detector assignment.

The full transport family contains sixteen contexts: direct, left, right, and destructive routes; coherence `3/5` and zero; and both detector assignments. One immutable reset identity must have null insertion residual in every context. Failure of one cell means there is no common terminal `+1`; there are context-indexed operations instead.

This gate is stronger than repeating the same calibration label. It compares the reset's realized action after transport through each physical route and detector assignment.

Executable witness: `checkers/check_cross_context_reset_transport.py`.
