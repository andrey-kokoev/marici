# SCC window-localization explanation programme

## Question

Which explanatory constructions become executable once localization windows carry typed laws, derivative budgets, cover geometry, and surviving margins?

## Programme

1. Certified-budget algebra distinguishes exact quadratic-surd values from directed dyadic upper enclosures. Composition preserves direction and provenance.
2. Localization law is causal data: linear partitions support commutator localization; square partitions support quadratic IMS localization.
3. Resolution versus leakage is quantified by inverse-square overlap-width scaling.
4. Global cover composition aggregates local budgets by a declared sum or maximum and tests the surviving physical or analytic margin.
5. Profile comparison ranks admitted windows by leakage without promoting the ranking to source authority.
6. The executable failure taxonomy separates sharp-cut regularity failure, finite-mode support impossibility, partition-law mismatch, malformed budget provenance, and exhausted margin.
7. Auxiliary-choice stability records bounded changes under admissible cover or profile replacement.
8. Cross-sector transport is admitted only for the shared typed interface: chart, support, partition law, width, budget, aggregation, and margin. It does not transport sector interpretations.
9. Certified numerics uses outward-rounded hexadecimal dyadic endpoints with direction, cell count, interval backend, checker/result locators, and upper-Darboux aggregation.

## Claim boundary

These compilers explain why a localization certificate passes or fails and how its budget composes. They do not source a physical cover, prove arithmetic relevance, optimize over undeclared profiles, or convert an upper enclosure into an equality.
