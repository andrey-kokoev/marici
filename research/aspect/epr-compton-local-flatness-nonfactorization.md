# EPR-Compton local-flatness/nonfactorization experiment

## Question

Can each separated wing possess a complete locally flat measurement atlas while the joint record forbids every globally factorizable assignment of pre-existing local values?

## Preparation and wings

An entangled photon pair feeds two independently controlled Compton analyzers. Each wing chooses one of two polarization settings and records the scattered photon, recoil electron, target excitation channel, no-click outcome, analyzer frame, setting-source health, coincidence-class label, and reset identity.

The coincidence class is an instrumental equivalence relation, not an ontological assertion about a fundamental event or time.

## Frozen tests

- Local spectral, angular, recoil, and calorimetric routes close on each wing.
- Local marginals are independent of the remote setting.
- Every no-click outcome remains in the trial ledger.
- The complete deterministic local assignment polytope obeys CHSH at most 2.
- The entangled polarization fixture predicts CHSH equal to 2 sqrt(2).
- Frame reflection, postselection, leakage, predictable settings, and memory are explicit hostiles.

The conjecture survives this fixture when local route holonomy is identity and the global correlations are nonfactorizable. Physical spacelike separation and detector acquisition remain unperformed.
