# Interaction-net filtration homology

The primitive presentation is frozen before introducing macros. Its generators
are the five occurrence moves and the fiber successor. The resource grade is

[
N=k+|S|,
]

where (k) is source fiber depth and (S) is occupied occurrence support.
Constructor word length is not used: replacing a primitive word by one macro
does not change (N).

At grade (N), the local coherence complex contains vertices ((S,k)) with
(k+|S|leq N). A cell is a set of pairwise-independent primitive directions,
and it enters when its upper vertex enters. The checker constructs every
boundary matrix over (mathbf F_2) through grade six.

The source cubical pilot is connected and has no positive-degree homology at
any checked grade. The root component is born at grade zero and survives under
every inclusion. There are therefore no candidate nonlocal source classes to
promote from this bounded presentation.

This extends to every grade. The admitted cells form a coordinatewise
downward-closed cubical set with one root. Contracting the five Boolean
coordinates and the fiber coordinate toward that root never leaves the
resource sublevel. Hence every grade is contractible, the induced map on
(H_0) preserves the root class, and all positive-degree persistence modules
are zero.

The death machinery is tested separately on a hostile fixture. Withhold the
mixed occurrence/fiber square at grade two: its four-edge boundary is a
candidate cycle. Admit the named square at grade three: that square is the
explicit local filler whose boundary kills the earlier class. This fixture is
not reported as a source result.

An algebraic separating cocycle may be constructed after a target is known and
serves only as a nonmembership certificate. The preregistered source example
assigns one to the O1 edges and zero to all other primitive edges. It is
target-independent and closes on every admitted square, but it is
cohomologically trivial here. Explanatory authority still requires a fixed
source-generated, physically executable context basis whose restricted
enriched nerve is faithful.
