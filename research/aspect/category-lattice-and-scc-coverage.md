# The categorical lattice now watched by SCC

The base category has typed assembly contexts as objects and source-authorized
context maps as arrows. It carries no temporal interpretation. Assembly,
restriction, specialization, observation, and completion are map types.

Above each context sits a four-coordinate gate object:

carrier × action × observation × estimate.

Each coordinate has three ordered values. Across the five assembly scales this
gives 405 finite schema cells. The order and meet are coordinatewise. A join is
defined only when the proposed witnesses belong to one source-authorized
context; combining unrelated certificates does not manufacture a stronger
cell.

The observer is not a scalar attached to each cutoff. It is an affine natural
transformation with a two-coordinate increment: primitive fluctuation and
square compensation. Nested increments must obey the cocycle triangle after
reference transport. Faithfulness is zero joint kernel on the reachable
increment module. Completion additionally requires a preregistered positive
lower singular bound.

SCC already had the ingredients: typed packet and stratum gates, port and
observer quotients, static/dynamic separation, authority, kernel and rank
witnesses, restriction maps, cross-level calibration, first-jet transport,
pro-context towers, completion margins, pentagon checks, hostiles, freezes,
impact propagation, and automatic rechecks.

The new model makes their product explicit. It adds the four-axis partial
lattice, forbids joins across unrelated authority contexts, installs the affine
observer triangle and mixed action-observation square as named cells, and
propagates typed obstructions through all five scales.

This is a schema theorem, not the theta result. In the active theta row, the
product system currently supplies a correspondence carrier only. Action
splitting is incomplete until a source-derived noncentral off-diagonal linking
generator is constructed and shown to have the required action rank. The
affine-observer hostile is downstream of that gate and is not live yet.

After action splitting passes, the row still needs exact observer increments,
reference transport, the reachable increment module, and a preregistered
completion margin.

## Realization-fiber rung

Kitaev's eighth rung is now a separate layer above the classifier. The 405
cells form the SCC Instrument Profile Lattice: possible instrument-capability
and certification-strength profiles, not source types or instrument identities.
The eight-rung contiguous-interval
tower instead has 36 interval cells, 56 left/right face maps, and 36
theta/Tate overlays. These counts are not interchangeable.

The fiber over one SCC instrument profile contains its concrete realizations. A verified
fiber morphism must compare named constructor actions, observers, viewing
forms, authority, completion topology, and instrument backaction. Only an
invertible comparison satisfying every slot enters the realization-intertwiner
groupoid. Equal SCC instrument profiles alone never establish source-type
identity, instrument identity, or equivalence.

The exact hostile uses two real two-dimensional realizations with identical
observer and Gramian. Their named actions are minus the identity and the
diagonal matrix with entries 1 and -1. Their characters differ, and the
intertwiner equations force the first row of every candidate comparison to
zero, so its rank is at most one. Thus the shared strongest SCC instrument profile does
not collapse the realization fiber.

SCC now checks this obstruction and preregisters two first-failure probes:
deleting the named-action slot fails comparison-slot completeness first;
mutating equal-profile non-equivalence into equivalence fails classifier
separation first. Higher coherence degrees two through five remain explicit
formal slots marked unsupported.
