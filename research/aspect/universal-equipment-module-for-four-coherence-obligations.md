# Universal equipment-module for four coherence obligations

## Question

Can one universal categorical structure generate the four proposed coherence obligations without inserting them independently?

## Claim boundary

This packet identifies a minimal candidate structure and a finite relational model. It does not prove universality among all possible higher-categorical presentations.

## Candidate structure

The minimal candidate is not a bare category and not a bare tetrahedron. It is:

1. an **equipment** of realization contexts and attachment correspondences;
2. a **module** from that equipment to record objects.

For each realization context \(R\), let \(\operatorname{Att}(R)\) be its attachment category. A realization arrow \(f:R\to S\) is assigned a profunctor

\[
P_f:\operatorname{Att}(R)^{op}\times\operatorname{Att}(S)\to\mathbf{Set}.
\]

Profunctor composition is the coend

\[
P_g\odot P_f.
\]

Unit profunctors and associators supply the internal composition coherencers. Representable profunctors recover contravariant pullback functors; nonrepresentable profunctors retain deletion or branching.

Readout is a right module \(Q\) over this equipment. For each \(R\), it assigns a record-valued profunctor or functor \(Q_R\). Its action comparison has the form

\[
Q_R\odot P_f\Longrightarrow Q_S
\]

or the conventionally reversed orientation. Module unit and associativity laws state that readout agrees with identity and composite attachment transport.

## Derivation of the four obligations

This structure generates:

1. **forward realization coherence:** composition and identities of realization arrows;
2. **backward attachment coherence:** units and associators of the profunctors \(P_f\), with functorial pullback on the representable locus;
3. **transport–coherencer compatibility:** naturality of squares and compatibility of profunctor compositors;
4. **readout descent:** module action, unit, and associativity for \(Q\).

The fourth obligation is not generated by the equipment alone. It is generated by equipping the equipment with a record module.

## DPC cycle

### Governing conjecture

An equipment of attachment correspondences equipped with a record module is the minimal unified explanation of the four obligations. The mechanism is hard to vary: equipment composition explains forward/backward transport and coherencers, while the module is exactly the additional structure required to transport readout.

### Rivals

1. A single ordinary category generates all four obligations.
2. A bare equipment generates readout descent without extra data.
3. A literal tetrahedron generates the obligations by its geometry.
4. The equipment-plus-module structure generates the obligations from standard unit, associativity, square, and module laws.

### Risky consequences

The governing conjecture predicts a finite model in which relational transport satisfies identity and associativity while readout descent remains freely variable. Adding a lawful module action must repair the readout equation without altering transport. It also predicts that deterministic relations recover ordinary pullback while branching relations remain lawful nonrepresentable transport.

### Falsification attempt

The checker uses finite sets and relations. It exhaustively verifies relational identity and associativity, deterministic and branching transport, and two readout predicates over identical transport. One predicate is closed under relational transport and defines a module action; the other is not. Thus the equipment laws hold in both models while only one admits the declared readout module.

### Residual

The finite model uses Boolean-valued relations rather than general Set-valued profunctors, enriched effects, or probabilistic records. Minimality is established only relative to the tested rivals and the requirement that readout remain independent of internal transport.

### Disposition

Rivals 1, 2, and 3 are rejected. Rival 4 is provisionally retained. The explanation requires two layers—equipment and module—not one unadorned categorical object.

## Explanation

The four obligations divide into internal and external coherence. The equipment governs lawful construction and transport inside the modeled system. The module governs the interface from that system to records. Their coupling produces the four-part picture without treating readout as implicit or deriving it from geometric face counting.

## Disposition

Proceed to the second research direction: determine whether representable backward transport is related to forward realization by an adjunction or mate construction inside the equipment.
