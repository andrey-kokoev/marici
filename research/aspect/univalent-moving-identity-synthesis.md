# Univalent and moving identity: source synthesis

## Question

How should the Marici coherence network type identity after comparing Univalent Foundations with the moving lemma for higher Chow groups?

## Claim boundary

This packet gives a structural conjecture and its acceptance tests. It does not construct a Marici univalent category, a Rezk completion, a moving operator on Marici carriers, motivic cohomology, prime emergence, or physical nullity.

## Univalent correction

Univalence identifies the identity type of structured objects with their appropriate equivalence type. For a univalent category, the canonical map

\[
(a=b)\longrightarrow(a\cong b)
\]

is an equivalence. Identity is therefore internal to a declared universe of structured objects. It is not created by whichever probe family currently distinguishes the most coordinates.

A kernel quotient

\[
V/\bigcap_i\ker F_i
\]

records indistinguishability relative to selected linear maps. It is not automatically a univalent identity object: it may erase equivalence witnesses, automorphisms, lattices, supports, grading, and marked primitive classes. Rezk completion is the closer categorical model: it saturates object identity by isomorphism through a universal construction while preserving mapping behavior.

## Moving correction

For Bloch's higher Chow complex \(z^p(X,\bullet)\), moving lemmas replace arbitrary admissible cycles by representatives satisfying additional proper-intersection conditions. Under the theorem's hypotheses, inclusion of the restricted cycle subcomplex is a quasi-isomorphism. A cycle and its moved representative need not have equal support or occurrence coordinates; they determine the same higher Chow class because their difference is a boundary.

Moving is not observational forgetting. It requires a source-authorized chain relation and preserves the homology theory. The result says that a geometrically better representative subcomplex presents the same derived object.

## Combined architecture

The coherence network should separate four operations:

1. **Presentation:** source occurrences, supports, coordinates, and coefficient data.
2. **Localization:** invert source-authorized equivalences such as isomorphisms, chain homotopies, descent equivalences, or structured unitaries.
3. **Univalent completion:** internalize the admitted equivalences as identity paths while retaining automorphism and higher-coherence data.
4. **Readout truncation:** apply an observational or physical quotient only after identity has been constructed.

Schematically,

\[
\mathcal C_{\rm pres}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal C_{\rm pres}[W^{-1}]
\longrightarrow
\widehat{\mathcal C}_{\rm univ}
\longrightarrow
I_{\rm readout}.
\]

The class \(W\) must be source-derived. Readouts may test or truncate identity but may not define \(W\) merely by current indistinguishability.

## Consequences for the arithmetic–theta graph

The retained arithmetic coordinate is potentially structural rather than evidential: it keeps the complete arithmetic object available while a theta presentation is attached. The residual \(Uv-h\) can witness one component of presentation agreement, but it cannot establish equality of full structured objects unless support, lattice, grading, transport, and primitive data also cross.

A residual zero may therefore mean successful equivalence rather than physical nullity. Physical nullity requires a separate readout map descending from the univalent identity object.

Prime identity, if derived, must be an equivalence-invariant irreducibility property of a source-generated multiplicative object. Neither occurrence enumeration nor probe indistinguishability constructs it.

## Falsifiers and acceptance tests

The conjecture fails or remains undefined if:

- no source-derived equivalence class \(W\) is specified;
- localization changes a declared readout without a typed descent residual;
- a proposed moving operation lacks a chain homotopy or quasi-isomorphism certificate;
- a kernel quotient erases automorphisms or marked integral data later used by the theory;
- graph matching is treated as equality of full objects while only analytic coordinates agree;
- physical nullity is inferred before a physical readout descends;
- or prime assignments change under admitted structured equivalences.

The first executable Marici test is to choose one bounded bridge diagram, enumerate its structured objects and source-authorized equivalences, and compare its localization/univalent completion with the previously computed observational quotient. Equality of dimensions is not a comparison map.

## Disposition

Replace the “finest jointly detected quotient defines identity” conjecture. The surviving conjecture is:

> Marici identity is generated by localizing full source structures at source-authorized equivalences and, where needed, completing univalently. Moving lemmas justify better representatives inside those classes. Probe and physical quotients are downstream truncations, not primitive identity constructors.
