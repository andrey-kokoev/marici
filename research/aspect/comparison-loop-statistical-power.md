# Comparison-loop statistical power

There are four primary bounded estimands: X and Y for each of the two optimal probes. With familywise error at most 0.01 and quadrature tolerance 0.04, the union Hoeffding bound requires 8356 effective trials per estimand.

The primary acquisition therefore needs 33424 trials, not 400000. Hardware angle, phase, and loss calibration remains an independent bright-reference packet; it is not estimated from these single-event trials.

The systematic allowance is 0.016 per fringe and the statistical allowance is 0.04. The two-sided uncertainty on the fringe difference is 0.112. Against the predicted optimal spread 0.227538, the remaining decision margin is approximately 0.115538, above the frozen 0.10 minimum.

Same-route A/A and B/B controls must return unit X fringe. The phase fixture must return its declared X/Y orientation. These controls are calibration records and do not enter the primary route-difference estimate.
