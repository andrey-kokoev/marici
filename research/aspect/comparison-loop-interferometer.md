# Comparison-loop interferometer

## Purpose

The instrument tests whether an alleged observable is independent of its route of realization.

Two source-authorized routes begin at the same labelled source class and end at the same reconstructed class:

    source
    -> characteristic-zero lift
    -> primitive normalization
    -> optical compiler
    -> calibrated measurement
    -> source reconstruction

The routes must expose every edge map. Agreement of their endpoint scalars is insufficient. The comparison loop is the relative transformation obtained by traversing one route forward and the other backward.

## Holonomy classes

- Identity: the realization descends coherently.
- Scalar phase: the ray descends, but the determinant or phase-reference line retains holonomy.
- Conjugation: metric data descend while orientation reverses.
- Nonunitary leakage: the route loses norm into an environment or uses incompatible normalization.
- Source-dependent residual: no single route-independent observable has been constructed.

The classification uses a probe basis plus a non-coplanar oriented triple. A basis alone cannot separate a common antiunitary conjugation from an admissible orthogonal relabelling.

## Acceptance

Every route edge needs source authority, domain and codomain, normalization convention, uncertainty rule, and reset/environment record. The loop must close on a spanning probe set. A promoted complex observable requires identity holonomy. A projective observable may admit a source-independent scalar phase only when the declared target explicitly quotients that phase. Conjugation is rejected for oriented targets. Leakage and source dependence always reject promotion.

## Physical compilation

Interfere the two route compilers under a common control qubit or path. X and Y control readout estimate the complex route overlap. Repeat on four tetrahedral probes and their ordered triple cocycle. Independently record bypass transmission, environment ports, phase fixture, compiler identity, and reset identity.

This artifact freezes the mathematical instrument and acquisition boundary. No physical comparison loop has been executed.
