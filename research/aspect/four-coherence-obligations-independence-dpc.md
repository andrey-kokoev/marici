# Four coherence obligations: independence and relative minimality

## Question

Are forward realization coherence, backward attachment coherence, transport–coherencer compatibility, and readout descent mutually independent?

## Claim boundary

The result proves irredundancy for the declared finite scalar presentation: each obligation fails in a model satisfying the other three. It does not prove that every equivalent axiomatization needs four primitive axioms, or that no stronger universal principle derives them jointly.

## Obligations

For composable generators \(f,g\), write forward coefficients \(F_f,F_g,F_{gf}\), backward coefficients \(B_f,B_g,B_{gf}\), transport comparison routes \(K_L,K_R\), and readout routes \(Q_L,Q_R\). The four equations are

\[
F_{gf}=F_gF_f,
\qquad
B_{gf}=B_fB_g,
\qquad
K_L=K_R,
\qquad
Q_L=Q_R.
\]

The scalar equations are coordinates of four separately typed squares; equality of their numerical fixtures does not identify the squares.

## DPC cycle

### Governing conjecture

One of the four obligations follows from the other three, so the presentation is redundant.

### Rivals

1. Forward and backward coherence force each other.
2. Internal coherence forces readout descent.
3. Transport compatibility follows from independent forward and backward composition.
4. All four obligations are independent in the declared model class.

### Risky consequences

Rival 4 requires four single-failure models. Each model must violate its named equation with a nonzero residual and satisfy the remaining equations exactly. Any unavoidable second failure refutes independence for that pair.

### Falsification attempt

The checker constructs four rational fixtures. The forward model replaces the forced composite \(6\) by \(5\); the backward model does the analogous replacement only in the backward square; the compatibility model gives its two comparison routes values \(1\) and \(2\); the readout model gives its two record routes values \(0\) and \(1\). Every unmodified square uses exact coherent values.

### Residual

The four models are deliberately decoupled finite witnesses. They prove logical nonimplication among these four predicates only in a model class admitting independent realization, attachment, comparison-cell, and record data. Additional sector laws can correlate them.

### Disposition

The governing conjecture and Rivals 1–3 are rejected for the declared presentation. Rival 4 is retained. The presentation is irredundant, but only relatively minimal.

## Explanation

The four obligations govern different interfaces: forward construction, backward attachment, agreement of internal transport routes, and descent to records. Shared scalar notation had obscured this separation. Single-failure models show that repairing one interface supplies no theorem about the others.

## Disposition

Use all four gates unless a separately proved sector theorem derives one from stronger premises. Call the result irredundancy or relative minimality, not absolute minimality.
