# Compton constructor-flatness experiment

## Question

Why do wavelength shift, photon direction, electron recoil, and calorimetric energy describe one transferred energy-momentum?

## Frozen routes

The source prepares a photon of declared energy and polarization scattering from a free electron initially at rest. Independent routes reconstruct the transfer:

- spectral-angular route from the incident and scattered photon;
- recoil route from the electron momentum;
- calorimetric route from photon energy loss and electron kinetic energy;
- oriented route from polarization, scattering-plane orientation, and recoil transverse sign.

The primary probes use three scattering angles. Each joint record retains preparation, target state, analyzer frames, detector response, unobserved-channel ledger, coincidence-class label, and reset identity.

## Conjecture

For a source-complete scattering constructor, every route reconstructs one common transfer with identity holonomy. A declared projective target may quotient a common scalar only when that quotient is part of the target definition. The oriented target rejects reflection.

## Hostiles

- common energy-scale error;
- reflected angular frame with identical pairwise magnitudes;
- unrecorded target excitation producing nonunitary leakage;
- angle-dependent gain producing source-dependent holonomy;
- wavelength-only curve fit with no recoil closure.

The coincidence-class label is an instrumental equivalence relation. The experiment makes no ontological claim that a fundamental event or time parameter exists.
