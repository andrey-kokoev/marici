# Programmable optical criticism engine

Author: `marici.Aspect`

Date: 2026-08-26

Status: first exact finite prototype

## Rival explanations

Take two route mechanisms on an isolated target:

\[
U_0=I,
\qquad
U_1=-I.
\]

They have identical target conjugation and intensity behavior. The baseline
experimental nerve therefore has a two-point global fibre. This is Gate 2,
global multiplicity, rather than a local derivative failure.

## Authorized intervention library

The finite compiler receives contexts before inspecting the rival label:

- baseline projective readout, cost 0, margin 0;
- determinant shadow, cost 1, margin 0;
- weak controlled reference, cost 1, margin \(1/2\);
- full controlled reference, cost 3, margin 1;
- hidden-route oracle, cost 0, margin 1, but unauthorized.

Margins use total variation of the two output laws divided by a declared unit
constructor-word source distance. Costs are algebraic context costs in this
prototype, not laboratory time or photon number.

## Prospective selection

Freeze a required criticism margin of \(3/4\). The weak reference is useful
but inadequate. The full controlled reference is the cheapest authorized
context meeting the threshold and predicts opposite output ports for the two
routes.

The budgeted criticism profile is

\[
c(0)=0,
\qquad
c(1)=c(2)=\frac12,
\qquad
c(3)=1.
\]

The zero-cost oracle is rejected even though it would separate perfectly,
because it selects its record from the hidden route rather than from an
admitted physical interaction.

## What the engine establishes

The prototype performs five tasks mechanically:

1. identifies the first failed obstruction gate;
2. excludes unauthorized critics;
3. computes invariant margins in declared source and observation metrics;
4. constructs a cost-indexed criticism profile;
5. selects the cheapest context meeting a preregistered severity threshold.

It does not infer that the selected intervention explains either route. It
compiles a severe counterfactual test of rival mechanisms.

## Next physical lift

A laboratory version should replace constructor-word cost with photon number,
integration time, phase-lock bandwidth, switching loss, and detector risk.
Its context library should include phase lifts, two-axis modulation, monitor
ports, route inversion, and environment-port readout, each with preparation-
and revision-bound provenance.
