# Integral covector to optical bridge

The bridge compiles every quotient covector derived by the integral-complex constructor, in declared order. It does not choose a favorable row.

For each covector it verifies primitive integral normalization and annihilation of the relation matrices, applies positive real L2 normalization without changing the upstream integral sign, and emits the minimal adjacent-rotation analyzer mesh. Good-prime reductions remain attached as provenance.

The bridge also computes the complete pairwise analyzer overlap and projector-distance atlas. This makes inequivalent source covectors visible before hardware programming.

Until Benincasa's source packet exists, the bridge consumes the synthetic constructor fixture and marks every optical program synthetic_not_promotable.
