# Branch A: full D03 packet under the fixed-beta normal-graph/Gysin comparison

Date: 2026-09-07.

## Result

The source-prescribed fixed-nonzero-beta Koba–Nielsen graph makes the previously constructed primary cycle `Omega03` exact. This is proved on the complete corrected 430-state complex, not by substituting into its homology module.

The two retained annihilating primitives become divisible polynomial chains. Their exact principal-ideal evaluations give two primitives of the same specialized cycle. Their difference `Z03` is a nonzero degree-three cycle with two nonzero endpoint components. It is explicitly the image of the all-marked top cycle under the chain map replacing the native 35-circle by the **distinct** occurrence-35 partner.

The ordinary coefficient complex contains a strict chain summand with differential `-X35` in degrees four to three, whose lower generator maps to `Z03`. In particular, the cyclic homology module generated by `Z03` has exact annihilator `(X35)`. This is not the old `(u03,mu)` primary module, and it is not a scalar physical Q-unit.

All normal-line units and the full fourteen-state Q quotient are retained. The calculation does not construct or identify the independently required physical conductor–Morse class.

\quad S_+=(13,15,35),\quad S_-=(02,04,24),
\quad L=(03,14,25).
\]

The starting coefficient ring is

\[
B=\mathbb Z[X_d,t_s,u_l]/(X_eX_o:e\in S_-,\ o\in S_+).
\]

There is one occurrence variable for each of the nine diagonals, one Rees parameter for each short diagonal, and an independent normal parameter for each long diagonal. The short normal is `u_s=t_s X_s`.

The corrected finite coefficient complex has all states

\[
[F,H,\epsilon],\qquad H\subset F,\quad\epsilon\in\{0,1\},
\qquad |[F,H,\epsilon]|=3-|F|+|H|+\epsilon.
\]

The face `F` is an actual noncrossing dissection. The last bit records the separate occurrence Koszul factor with differential `X35`; it is not an identification with the native 35-circle. There are 430 states. The complete endpoint and short-boundary supports have 32 and 416 states; the quotient has fourteen.

The native circle differential has coefficient `t_s X_s` for a short diagonal and `u_l` for a long diagonal. The radial coefficient is `X_a`, with the incidence sign determined by the ordered face. The occurrence partner has the tensor differential sign. These are the source rules from `check_absolute_unlocalized_support_pc.rs` and `check_d03_pabs_morse_pullback.rs`, at the pinned repository commit listed below.

## 2. The source-prescribed specialization

The source works at fixed nonzero beta in characteristic zero, after the scalar extraction. It supplies

\[
q_d=\exp(\beta X_d),\qquad
u_d=q_d-1=g_d(X_d)X_d,
\]
\[
g_d(X_d)=\beta\sum_{n\ge0}\frac{(\beta X_d)^n}{(n+1)!},
\qquad g_d(0)=\beta\ne0.
\]

Here `nu_d` denotes the specialized native normal, not a new occurrence variable. Each `g_d` is a unit in the formal coefficient ring. This is a unit statement at fixed beta, not an inversion of `X_d`.

Thus the normal-graph map is

\[
\phi(X_d)=X_d,\qquad
\phi(t_s)=g_s,\qquad
\phi(u_l)=g_lX_l.
\]

To avoid relying on truncated power series, the checker first works over the universal unit ring

\[
R_{\mathrm{univ}}=
\mathbb Z[X_d,g_d^{\pm1}]/(X_eX_o:e\in S_-,o\in S_+).
\]

Every displayed identity holds there. Substituting the actual formal units then proves it for the physical coefficient graph. Exact power-series coefficients are additionally checked through order twelve as an implementation control; the all-order assertion uses the nonzero constant term, not that finite test.

The complete starting complex is bounded and free over `B`. Its termwise base change therefore computes the derived base change. No flatness of the graph map, and no preservation of the old homology group, is assumed.

The source's warning about the order of operations remains in force. This is the normal-graph specialization of the already extracted coefficient complex. It does not exponentiate an unextracted scalar family of the form `X+sigma/t`.

## 3. Normal-line comparison and the supported Gysin frame

The graph-pulled complex has native normal coefficients `g_d X_d`. Define

\[
\Phi[F,H,\epsilon]
=\left(\prod_{h\in H}g_h\right)[F,H,\epsilon].
\]

The receiving complex `C_X` has all native normal coefficients equal to `X_d`, while the occurrence differential remains `X35`. Direct columnwise calculation gives

\[
d_X\Phi=\Phi d_{\mathrm{graph}}.
\]

This is a diagonal chain isomorphism. It preserves every face label, both endpoint packets, and the entire short-boundary/Q diagram. The inverse only uses the already existing units `g_d`. The native and occurrence 35-factors remain separate states.

For the ordered pair of annihilating parameters, put

\[
b=g_{03},\qquad c=g_{15}g_{24}g_{14}g_{25}.
\]

Their pullbacks are

\[
(u_{03},\mu)\longmapsto
(bX_{03},cX_{14}X_{25}).
\]

The ordered Koszul comparison to the pair `(X03,X14 X25)` has matrices

\[
1,\qquad
\begin{pmatrix}b&0\\0&c\end{pmatrix},\qquad bc
\]

in exterior degrees zero, one, and two. The determinant dual transforms by `(bc)^(-1)`. The checker verifies these maps and their differentials, rather than replacing their frames by scalar signatures.

Both pairs generate the same ideal after the graph map. The pair `(X03,X14 X25)` is regular because these are independent long-occurrence coordinates over the two-sheet short-coordinate algebra. Its codimension-two supported dual therefore retains

\[
\det(I/I^2)^\vee[-2].
\]

This is the supported Gysin **normal-change comparison**. It does not identify a chosen homotopy with an Ext generator or supply an additional cross-support physical kernel.

Along the single physical Cartier divisor `X03=0`, the conormal identity is

\[
[u_{03}]=b|_{X_{03}=0}[X_{03}]=\beta[X_{03}].
\]

The dual factor is the inverse of this unit. It is retained whenever the `u03` and physical channel normal frames are compared.

## 4. Exact factorization of the entire packet

Write

\[
a=g_{02}g_{04}g_{13}.
\]

There are explicit integral polynomial chains `O03,H03,Hmu,Z03` in the unit-linearized model satisfying

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Phi\phi(\Omega)&=aO_{03},\\
\Phi\phi(W_u)&=abX_{03}H_{03},\\
\Phi\phi(W_\mu)&=acX_{14}X_{25}H_\mu,\\
\Phi\phi(\Theta)&=abcX_{03}X_{14}X_{25}Z_{03}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Every factor is displayed. These equations are checked termwise, including the endpoint terms of `Wmu` and `Theta`.

The two nonunit divisions used to expose `H03` and `Hmu` are exact factorizations of chains already in the principal ideals `(X03)` and `(X14 X25)`. Every resulting occurrence exponent is nonnegative. Equivalently, one evaluates a framed principal-ideal dual only on its own principal-ideal submodule. This is not a scalar localization or a global division operation on arbitrary chains.

The complete differential gives

\[
dH_{03}=O_{03},\qquad dH_\mu=O_{03},\qquad
Z_{03}=H_\mu-H_{03},\qquad dZ_{03}=0.
\]

Thus `Omega` becomes exact under the graph specialization. Its primitive in the physical, unlinearized normal basis is `Phi^(-1)(a H03)`.

The first primitive has fifteen terms and zero endpoint components. The second has thirty terms and two endpoint components. No lower-face term is omitted.

The specialized `Wu` itself is divisible by `X03`, so it becomes the zero chain on the Cartier fibre `X03=0`. The formerly nonzero independent-normal Bockstein cannot be transported by treating the old homology generator as unchanged.

## 5. Full Q and endpoint components

Use the full seven-state Q packet before its independent occurrence tensor. Write `T` for its degree-three top, `h_l` for its degree-three native long-normal states, and `p_l` for its degree-two unmarked long facets. In the linearized graph model,

\[
dT=X_{03}p_{03}+X_{14}p_{14}+X_{25}p_{25},
\qquad dh_l=X_lp_l.
\]

The four computed Q components are

\[
\begin{aligned}
\pi_QO_{03}&=X_{03}p_{03},\\
\pi_QH_{03}&=h_{03},\\
\pi_QH_\mu&=T-h_{14}-h_{25},\\
\pi_QZ_{03}&=T-h_{03}-h_{14}-h_{25}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The two primitives fill the same Q-boundary in different ways. Their difference reaches the complete top/three-normal Q-cycle. It is not the false degree-one three-edge quotient considered earlier.

Let

\[
E_-=[\{02,04,24\},\{02,04,24\},0],
\]
\[
E_{+,\mathrm{occ}}=[\{13,15,35\},\{13,15\},1],
\qquad
E_{+,\mathrm{nat}}=[\{13,15,35\},\{13,15,35\},0].
\]

Then

\[
\operatorname{pr}_VH_{03}=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{pr}_VH_\mu=
\operatorname{pr}_VZ_{03}=E_-+E_{+,\mathrm{occ}}.
\]

The positive endpoint uses the distinct occurrence partner, not the missing native 35-mark. These are two different retained rows of the endpoint packet.

The entries `1` in these formulas are in the declared unit-linearized frames. For example, the two endpoint coefficients of `Phi^(-1)(a Z03)` in the actual graph-pulled frames are

\[
\frac{g_{13}}{g_{24}},\qquad
\frac{g_{02}g_{04}}{g_{15}}.
\]

They are units, not discarded normal factors. Their constant terms at the full short-occurrence conductor are respectively `1` and `beta`; the occurrence partner has not been rescaled into a native monodromy circle.

## 6. Structural description and nonvanishing of the difference

Define the all-marked top cycle

\[
\Psi=
\sum_{F}(-1)^{|F|(|F|+1)/2}[F,F,0].
\]

The sum runs over all 45 noncrossing faces, including the empty face. The normal and radial terms cancel pairwise, so `dPsi=0` over the complete coefficient ring.

There is an explicit chain endomorphism `P35` of `C_X`:

\[
P_{35}[F,H,\epsilon]=
\begin{cases}
[F,H,\epsilon],&35\notin H,\\
[F,H\setminus\{35\},1],&35\in H,\ \epsilon=0,\\
0,&35\in H,\ \epsilon=1.
\end{cases}
\]

The diagonal `35` is last in the specified normal order, so this formula has no additional reordering sign. It is a chain map precisely because the two displayed normal differentials are both `X35` after the source-prescribed unit conjugacy. It is idempotent and preserves every face label. It is a map between retained factors, not an assumption that their physical normal lines are identical.

The exact computed identity is

\[
Z_{03}=P_{35}\Psi.
\]

This explains the 45-term secondary difference and its two endpoint entries independently of its original matrix table.

### Complete homogeneous verification

In fine occurrence degree zero, the linearized full complex has chain ranks

\[
(8,59,108,56)
\]

in degrees zero through three. Every eligible monomial is uniquely determined by the state's degree; this is the whole homogeneous component, not a degree cutoff. Integral cancellation gives

\[
H_2(C_X)_{(0)}\cong\mathbb Z^3,
\qquad H_3(C_X)_{(0)}\cong\mathbb Z^2,
\]

and no other homology in that component. Its degree-three cycles have the integral basis `(Z03,Psi)`. There are no degree-four states in this weight.

Their coefficient restriction to `(E_-,E_{+,occ},E_{+,nat})` is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1\\
1&0\\
0&1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

A two-by-two minor is one. Consequently no nonzero degree-three cycle in this homogeneous component has all endpoint coefficients zero. The endpoint-zero primitive `H03` and the primitive `Hmu` with the displayed endpoint column are each fixed uniquely in this component by their complete boundary and pointwise endpoint values. They are not two primitives within the same endpoint frame.

## 7. A strict ordinary coefficient summand, with both occurrence grades retained

Let `S35` be exterior multiplication by the separate occurrence partner, including its tensor sign:

\[
S_{35}[F,H,0]=(-1)^{3-|F|+|H|}[F,H,1],
\qquad S_{35}[F,H,1]=0.
\]

The full matrix verifies

\[
dS_{35}+S_{35}d=X_{35}\,1.
\]

Let `Qtop` be the two-term quotient retaining the actual top state and its occurrence partner:

\[
Q_{\mathrm{top}}=[R\xrightarrow{-X_{35}}R]
\]

in homological degrees four and three. Projection onto these two source rows is a chain map: no differential increases support back into the empty face, and the only degree-four term entering the top has coefficient `-X35`.

There is an explicit section

\[
\sigma(q_3)=Z_{03},\qquad
\sigma(q_4)=-S_{35}Z_{03}.
\]

It satisfies

\[
d\sigma(q_4)=-X_{35}\sigma(q_3),\qquad
\pi_{\mathrm{top}}\sigma=1.
\]

Thus this is a strict ordinary chain summand of the complete coefficient model. Its section includes the endpoint and short-boundary corrections. It is **not** an endpoint-local or support-diagonal splitting.

The projection and section give the exact statement

\[
R[Z_{03}]\cong R/(X_{35})
\]

inside degree-three homology. The top coefficient detects every nonzero multiple modulo `(X35)`, and `S35` explicitly annihilates every `X35` multiple. Hence the exact annihilator is `(X35)`, with no additional annihilator involving the long channels.

Since the coefficient ring is singular, `X35` is a zero divisor. The retained summand is not a free resolution of `R/(X35)`:

\[
H_3(Q_{\mathrm{top}})=R/(X_{35}),
\qquad
H_4(Q_{\mathrm{top}})=\operatorname{Ann}_R(X_{35})
=(X_{02},X_{04},X_{24}).
\]

Dropping its upper grade would again lose part of the source.

The strict splitting persists under the actual formal unit substitution. In particular, nonvanishing of the secondary difference is not inferred from a potentially nonflat base change of homology.

On the open set where `X35` is invertible this ordinary summand contracts, with the displayed occurrence homotopy. Such localization removes the negative normalization sheet and is not claimed admissible for the full two-endpoint frame. No such localization was used above.

## 8. Which secondary data are fixed

After specialization the new polynomial primitive `H03` permits the coherent choices

\[
W_u^{\mathrm{new}}=X_{03}H_{03},\qquad
W_\mu^{\mathrm{new}}=X_{14}X_{25}H_{03}.
\]

Their compatibility is zero. These are the degree-one components of an explicit nullhomotopic map from the ordered two-parameter Koszul complex, shifted so that its bottom maps to `O03`.

The originally retained second primitive instead equals `X14 X25 Hmu` in the normalized units. The required change is

\[
X_{14}X_{25}H_\mu-X_{14}X_{25}H_{03}
=X_{14}X_{25}Z_{03}.
\]

It is a nontrivial closed change, and it alters exactly the previously displayed endpoint and Q components. Thus:

- the primary specialized class is zero;
- the specified pair of primitives has a nonzero difference;
- the unframed two-parameter obstruction with arbitrary choices of primitives contains zero;
- replacing the specified second primitive by the coherent one changes the retained frame data.

These conclusions must not be merged into either a claim of automatic physical vanishing or a claim that the physical `Delta_J` has been obtained.

## 9. The older Rees-supported pair is a different operation

The supported complex for the older pair `(t04,t35)` pulls back, at this same fixed nonzero beta, to the Koszul complex on `(g04,g35)`. It is contractible: exterior multiplication by the first generator divided by the already existing unit `g04` gives a homotopy of its identity.

This is the derived pullback of an object supported at `t04=t35=0`; that support has empty preimage under this fixed-beta graph. It is not a claim that a previously evaluated residue on another base is numerically zero. A push–pull or scalar-grade extraction that changes the base or order of operations would require its own comparison square.

Likewise the beta-zero family is not covered. At beta zero the normal factors are not units, so the present unit conjugacy is unavailable. No conclusion about that degeneration is extrapolated from this test.
