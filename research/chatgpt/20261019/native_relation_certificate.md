# Native comparison relations, rung transport, and the Hopf benchmark

## Result

For the actual finite `BoundaryGeneratedQuestions.fourQ` carrier, the pointed
comparison semantics is the six-element group S3. Its independent factor lifts
have a complete uniform presentation giving S3^m (or C2^m for the chosen swap
alone). The independent-composition square is already filled in the source;
the same-carrier comparison sector nevertheless has nonidentity holonomy.
These are different typed constructions.

A degree-respecting comparison with the preceding free-simplicial-circle
benchmark cannot send its primitive Hopf cycle to either of these native
permutation commutators. The classifying groupoids of this finite comparison
sector are 1-types. This result does not classify the native pushout/cell
attachment calculus, all source types, or the full retained history semantics.

## 2. The actual same-carrier relations

Let r=(123), a permitted pointed equivalence, and set b=r a r^-1=(23).
The comparisons generated by a and b have the presentation

    < a,b | a^2=1, b^2=1, aba=bab > = S3.

Equivalently the third relation is (ab)^3=1. Every word reduces to

    1, a, b, ab, ba, aba.

After removing equal adjacent letters, words alternate. The braid relation
reduces alternating words of length at least four; it identifies the two
length-three forms. The six displayed maps are distinct permutations, so
there are no additional semantic identifications. This is an all-word
normal-form proof, not extrapolation from bounded word enumeration.

The commutator [a,b]=aba^-1b^-1 is a nonidentity 3-cycle. It is an ordinary
automorphism in this sector, not a Hopf element of pi3 of a spatial sphere.
Its scalar determinant is +1, illustrating the loss under the sign observer.

The classifying groupoid of this fixed semantic comparison group has type
B(S3): pi1=S3 and pi_k=0 for k>=2. The zero higher groups follow from the
nerve/classifying-space theorem for ordinary discrete groups, not a finite
homology computation. The full universe of native types and their records
is not asserted to have this truncation level.

## 3. Independent-slot lifts and a uniform recurrence

`ObserverCoherenceCube.Geometry` defines actual source operations on products:

    expandLeft(u,v)  = (expand(u),v)
    expandRight(u,v) = (u,expand(v)).

For expand=a, write A=a x id and B=id x a on Q x Q. Their composites agree
on values and the source proves

    route-homotopy : run leftFirst = run rightFirst
    route-homotopy = refl.

Thus A^2=B^2=1 and [A,B]=1. Raw `leftFirst` and `rightFirst` schedules remain
provably distinct and are retained in `ComparedRoutes` and `RealizedSquare`.
`nextQ` retains that whole structure; it does not attach an independent
higher cell of a different topology.

For m factors, native pointwise product congruence permits factorwise
pointed comparisons g=(g_1,...,g_m), acting by

    (x_1,...,x_m) -> (g_1 x_1,...,g_m x_m).

Operations on separate slots commute. Each slot has the preceding S3
relations. Sorting all slot indices and reducing each slot gives 6^m
normal forms. They are distinct because varying one coordinate detects
its factor permutation. Therefore the generated factorwise subgroup is
exactly S3^m. Using only the exhibited swap in each slot gives C2^m.

This is a uniform all-m classification of this source-backed factorwise
sector. It is NOT the full group of pointed bijections of Q^m: those include
maps that mix the factors, and they have not been identified with this
independent-expansion rule.

The canonical classifying groupoid of the factorwise sector is B(S3^m),
a 1-type. Increasing m changes the product carrier and its ordinary
symmetries, not the homotopy height of this particular groupoid. Recording
more schedules also does not change this conclusion about that semantic
image. It does not eliminate distinctions in the retained syntax.

## 4. A native closed route survives ordinary rung re-fibration

Keep fourQ's distinguished point 0. Let U={1,2,3} be the other values.
An actual pointed comparison g in H can also be observed at i in U.
This produces the table

    Rows = {(i,j,g) : i in U, g in H, j=g(i)}.

There are 18 rows. Identity, reversal and composition use the source
permutations themselves. The row label retains g; an endpoint-only label
would lose which of the two maps from i to j was supplied.

For distinct i,j,k, apply the existing swap orbit along

    i -> j -> k -> i.

The composite fixes 0 and i, and swaps the other two unmarked values. It is
nonidentity of order two. This is already recognized in the prior
`source-swap-transport-scope` note; the new checker rechecks it and transports

The action groupoid U//H is transitive, with C2 stabilizer, hence has type
B(C2). It is not the same object as the one-object groupoid B(H); it retains

    Sigma(k:Keys). Sigma(r:Rows). p(r)=k equiv Rows.

It preserves every original row and membership witness. Transport identity
and composition through this equivalence: the resulting presented action

## 5. Why the previous Hopf commutator cannot simply be renamed

The benchmark G=F[S1] is a reduced free simplicial group with G0=1 and a
degree-one generator t satisfying d0(t)=d1(t)=1. Its elements s1(t) and s0(t)
in degree two are degeneracies, not operations on separate product factors.
The previously calculated [s1(t),s0(t)] is a primitive pi2(G), hence pi3(S2),
class.

The present native finite group H, modeled as a constant simplicial group,
has every face and degeneracy equal to the identity map of H. A simplicial
homomorphism Phi:F[S1] -> constant(H) must satisfy

    Phi1(t) = d0(Phi1(t)) = Phi0(d0(t)) = 1.

Every higher generator is a degeneracy of t, so the entire homomorphism is
trivial. In particular a,b or A,B cannot be assigned to the two benchmark
degeneracies while preserving the actual face structure.

At the classifying-space level, any based map S2 -> B(H) is nullhomotopic
because B(H) is a 1-type. This rules out a comparison faithfully carrying the
Hopf class into this native permutation sector. It does not rule out a
source-defined topology-changing attachment in the separate closure branch.

The correct finding is not 'native WG kills the Hopf class.' No compatible
nontrivial identification with that benchmark has been made. The finding is
that the native comparison sector and the benchmark have different typed
incidence and relation data, so the asserted transfer would be invalid.

## 6. The original A4 question: an explicit decoration square

The native pointing explains why H=S3, not A4: a fixed marked point excludes
all nonidentity V4 double translations. A4 acts transitively on four points;
S3 is their point stabilizer in S4.

For comparison, let Or(Q) be the two parity classes of orders of the four
values. This is a derived orientation decoration, not claimed selected by
the current native comparison rule. S4 acts on Q and on Or(Q), the latter
through sign. This gives the four structured-set moduli types:

    unmarked                 B(S4)
    marked                   Q//S4                  ~ B(S3)
    oriented                 Or(Q)//S4              ~ B(A4)
    marked and oriented      (Q x Or(Q))//S4         ~ B(C3).

The forgetful square is homotopy Cartesian:

    B(C3)  ---->  B(A4)
      |             |
      v             v
    B(S3)  ---->  B(S4).

Indeed both covering families arise from the same S4 action; taking their
fiber product over B(S4) retains their product decoration. The S4 action on
Q x Or(Q) is transitive with stabilizer the even point-fixing permutations,
C3. This is a proof using the actual actions, not only matching group orders.
The horizontal covers have four-element fibers; the vertical ones have
two-element fibers.

The three double transpositions and identity form V4, normal in A4; the
even point stabilizer C3 intersects V4 trivially and its product with V4
is A4. Thus A4=V4 semidirect C3 is recovered in this decoration comparison.

This ties the original tetrahedral groups to the native pointed carrier
without identifying distinct symmetry constraints. It neither identifies a
prior independently derived BC3 transport groupoid with this one nor proves
that native WG selects orientation preservation. Those require actual maps.
