## 1. Research objective and present position

The long-range conjecture is that one explicitly specified source, closed under a finite family of Witness Generator operations, can generate the symmetry-bearing structures needed by physics and the relationships between their descriptions.

The immediate question is narrower and more discriminating:

**Which symmetry types, actions, and higher relations are consequences of the source, and which enter through choices of inputs, constructor parameters, realization, or observation?**

A common source may determine a family of alternatives rather than one unique space. In that case, its alternatives, transformations, and invariant properties are the result to derive. Selecting one member and finding familiar physics in it does not establish that the source selects that member.

The current evidence now includes a concrete native six-arrow source whose incidence determines an outgoing-witness family under an explicit exactness contract; retained outputs feed subsequent actual native applications; and a nonidentity source comparison can be internalized, followed by further attachment. A derived interchange compares the two orders of construction while retaining both histories. These are source-specific implementations, not only generic gluing possibilities. Their declared task policy, source modes, and continuation convention remain inputs. Physical symmetry coverage and a global higher-coherence theorem are not established. [N2–N8]

The change from revision 2 is substantive: several of its proposed local integration tasks have implementations in the newly committed research. The next work should start from those implementations, not repeat the circle or generic feedback constructions. The organizing source-determination question is unchanged.

Tetrahedral geometry and familiar gauge groups are comparison targets and candidate consequences. Their role in the initial source must be declared rather than alternately treated as an assumption and an emergence result. Accepted physical models supply experimentally tested constraints; their microscopic ontology is not imposed on this investigation.

## 2. Four claims that must not be conflated

**Expressibility:** given a structure and its defining data, the language can encode it.

**Certified generation:** a construction derives the structure from declared primitives and inputs, with the origin of every decisive parameter accounted for.

**Source determination:** the fixed source constrains the result independently of presentation choices, or determines a classified family of alternatives. This is where explanatory content beyond a general-purpose language must appear.

**Physical adequacy:** the generated structure acts on an identified physical model in the required way and matches observations in a stated regime.

A universal compiler could establish expressibility and certified compilation of supplied presentations without establishing source determination. Conversely, a source permitting alternatives need not fail physical adequacy. The question is whether its alternatives and selection conditions have been derived.

“All physical symmetries” is therefore a research objective, not yet a quantified theorem. The first theorem must name a model class and a notion of symmetry. This brief initially concerns invertible transformations with their parameter dependence and higher comparisons. Claims about noninvertible symmetry operations require a separately specified composition structure.

## 3. Trajectory: why the investigation reached this point

The starting question concerned tetrahedral symmetry, including

$$

in a space-filling and recursively coarse-grained geometry. Could a grain become an effective point and retain the organizing structure of the ground space?

The supplied FCC Gaussian calculation showed that exact elimination leaves the nearest-neighbor coupling family. Exact composition remained possible when the full effective kernel and integration normalization were retained. This was evidence against a particular small parameter closure, not against recursive geometry. It redirected attention from cell shape to the data an effective object must retain. This historical calculation was not independently rerun for this brief. [H]

Selected blocking descriptions gave an action-groupoid model equivalent to $BA_4$. WG supplies a successor together with a witness. Repeating a completed request can be stationary; a path-coherence filler can demonstrate nullhomotopy in a fixed target. Neither fact excludes an operation that adjoins a new cell and changes the target.

The archive distinguishes these operations. A filler already present in $X$, a newly attached filler in a larger space, and a subsequent comparison between the two are different data. This resolved the ambiguity in the proposed “fifth invocation” mechanism: the boundary map and the operation on the target matter, not the invocation number. [S1, B3]

## 5. What a source specification must include

At its generic interface, WG is a term

$$
W:\prod_{s:S}\sum_{t:S}R(s,t).
$$

This supplies a witnessed transition; it does not choose the relation $R$ or certify the origin of its parameters.

A seed alone is not the entire source. Freeze the ambient foundation, initial data, constructor syntax, allowed parameter programs, generated comparison laws, and realization convention. Make an admitted extension distinguishable from the original system.

The ambient foundation currently includes Cubical type theory, univalence, and homotopy-pushout machinery. Their expressive power is available to the constructions; it is not derived from the four-point carrier. Universe levels, truncations, and completions must remain explicit.

The source-certification problem is demonstrated by existing code. Given an arbitrary supplied function $f:A\to B$ and a unit seed, the original dependent-sum/product rules can encode

$$
F_f(a)=E(B,\lambda b.U,f(a))
$$

and recover $f(a)$ from the resulting product package. The function was already in the index argument. Closure membership therefore does not prove generation from the unit. `ResolutionMapIntroductionGap.agda` also proves a narrower syntax-level restriction on bare map packages; that restriction must not be misread as semantic inability to represent functions. [S6]

The existing certified program fragment generates swap and currying without accepting arbitrary functions as primitive programs. It is a useful starting point, but embedding the old unrestricted rules preserves their parameter-injection possibility. Adding a safe fragment does not certify every old derivation. [S6]

The new results reduce, but do not erase, this source-certification problem. Out(v) is derived from the six-arrow incidence relation once the task “all outgoing attachments” is fixed. ExactSourceAdmission proves decoder uniqueness under the exactness requirement. It does not prove that source retention alone selects that requirement, the initial four endpoint seeds, or the task over every alternative. The generic E rule still accepts unrestricted indices. The new adapters establish disciplined sublanguages of its applications. [N2, N3, N7]

External parameters remain legitimate when declared. A continuous symmetry can be generated as one parameterized family; no enumeration of every real parameter value is required. The certificate must distinguish the family’s derived action law from data supplied to select a member. Finite syntax, finite input information, finite homotopy height, and finite execution are separate requirements.

## 7. What this proof would—and would not—decide

The local source family now supplies a coherent-reuse problem over an actual native implementation, not merely a proposed generative interface. Completing its higher compatibility would establish reusable composition within the declared contract. The source-selection task still asks what distinguishes that contract from alternatives, and the coverage task asks which further structures it can reach. Neither can be replaced by a larger count of retained histories. [N1, N7, N8]

Two inequivalent source-authorized outcomes would refute uniqueness under those assumptions, not expressive coverage. A derived family containing both could still be the intended common object. A successful selection theorem must identify which source condition excludes an alternative. In particular, the conditions responsible for orientation preservation, a marked point, a product relation, or abelianization must remain visible.

This classification should operate on full structured objects. A fixed-target coherence resolution, a cell attachment, a coordinate change, and an observation quotient have different effects. A map into a retained graph can be reversible merely because its input was stored; that does not make passage to its output reversible.

The joint-cut calculation illustrates the latter problem: compatible marginals left 51 independent linear reconstruction directions. Those affine fibers are contractible over continuous coefficients; the separately constructed incidence graph has nontrivial cycles. Neither object is automatically the native homotopy space. Keep this as a loss-detection regression, not the next organizing theory. [B5]

Similarly, the benchmark $S^3\to S^2\to BU(1)$ is a comparison of a selected suspension with its abelian description. It is not proof that a native product commutator is a Hopf class. A native $BS_3$ comparison groupoid has a different truncation level and face structure. [B2, B4]

## 8. From mathematical generation to physical symmetry

The physical comparison must concern actions, not group names alone. For a declared structured model $M$ with symmetry action $\alpha:H\times M\to M$, seek generated objects and an action whose realization admits structured equivalences $e_H,e_M$ satisfying

$$
e_M\circ R(\widehat\alpha)
\simeq
\alpha\circ(e_H\times e_M),
$$

with the relevant parameter dependence, composition, and higher comparisons retained. Here $R$ must preserve the product structure used by the action, or provide the specified comparison that replaces strict preservation. Coverage must include the claimed class of actions, not only one chosen subgroup of automorphisms.

A stronger sufficient route is object coverage plus full faithfulness of a realization on the relevant structured mapping objects. It implies equivalence of the corresponding automorphism objects because transformations, inverses, and their witnesses all lift. This implication is a standard reduction of the objective, not evidence that the hard premises hold. Do not make equivalence with an undefined category of “all physics” the next task. [T1]

The target must also retain more than bare homotopy when the physical claim requires it. The additive group $\mathbb R$ is contractible as a space but has a nontrivial translation action. Smooth parameterization and action data can disappear under passage to homotopy type. A $BU(1)$ construction therefore does not, by itself, derive electromagnetism, its dynamics, or charge normalization. Cohesive approaches explicitly distinguish topological structure from its homotopy shape. [T2]

Gauge redundancy, global transformations, and emergent effective symmetries must be distinguished in the target. A symmetry of an effective model need not lift to an exact microscopic automorphism; the required relation may instead pass through a specified coarse-graining or observational equivalence. Returning to the original grain question will require that relation and the retained effective data—not an assumption of exact microscopic scale symmetry.

The next physical domain should be narrow enough to specify its model, observables, admissible transformations, and tested regime independently of WG. Its adequacy remains an empirical claim. A mathematical completeness theorem is relative to that declared domain.
