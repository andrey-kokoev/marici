# Branch A — conductor-ideal source relations and derived marked-chain comparisons

Date: 2026-09-07.

## Result

The source-side test now uses the actual conductor ideal as a module, not six independent formal generators.

At occurrence degree zero and regulator-normal degree three, with the conductor ideal placed in homological degree three, the specified endpoint/Q-framed target admits:

- a rank-16 integral lattice of strict module chain maps;
- a rank-40 integral lattice of maps from a free resolution of the conductor ideal;
- a rank-34 lattice of values on its six generators, with a rank-six kernel consisting of nonzero maps on source relations;
- rank-five strict and rank-thirteen resolved families equivariant under the published dihedral transport.

Among the thirteen equivariant resolved directions, eleven are visible on the source's alternating scalar conductor element, while two are higher relation maps invisible on that element. Every admissible invariant generator assignment has an integral invariant resolved lift; the corresponding image lattice is saturated.

All these statements have a fixed type: maps from the conductor ideal placed in degree three into the conductor-valued, complete endpoint/Q-framed subcomplex. The placement matches the deformation degree being tested. It is not a newly derived geometric Gysin shift, and none of the maps is identified with the physical conductor–Morse comparison.

## 2. The actual source relations

Let e_a denote the generator mapping to X_a. A presentation of I begins

\[
R^{24}\xrightarrow{d_1}R^6\longrightarrow I\longrightarrow0.
\]

Six relations are the same-sheet Koszul relations

\[
X_a e_b-X_b e_a\qquad(a<b\text{ on the same sheet}).
\]

Eighteen are the ordered mixed-sheet relations

\[
X_a e_b\qquad(a,b\text{ on opposite sheets}).
\]

The relations are complete. Restricting an arbitrary polynomial relation to a sheet leaves the ordinary first syzygies of its three polynomial variables. The remaining opposite-sheet coefficients lie in the mixed annihilator module. Both pieces are generated by the displayed relations, over the full polynomial coefficient base.

For a strict chain map f from I in degree three, put

\[
Y_a=f(X_a)\in N_3.
\]

The conditions are

\[
dY_a=0,
\]

\[
X_aY_b-X_bY_a=0\quad\text{on one sheet},
\qquad X_aY_b=0\quad\text{on opposite sheets}.
\]

The sixty independently closed candidate images do not automatically satisfy these equations. The complete matrix has rank forty-four and a saturated rank-sixteen kernel. Its pivots are integral units. Both the matrix and its kernel basis are serialized.

For example, assigning the earlier two-term gallery cycle only to X_02 and zero to X_04 fails the relation X_04 e_02-X_02 e_04. Source linearity requires correlated assignments to the other negative-sheet generators.

## 3. An integral resolution retaining all source coherences

A strict test alone could reject a valid derived map. The calculation therefore resolves I.

For each sheet, take nonempty exterior blocks of its three ordered variables. Form words of blocks whose sheet labels alternate. Their word degree is the total number of variables in all blocks. Let D_n be the free R-module on words of degree n, including the empty word in degree zero.

For a word with first block A=(a_1<...<a_k) and remaining word w, set

\[
\partial(A\mid w)=\sum_{j=1}^k(-1)^{j-1}X_{a_j}
(A\setminus\{a_j\}\mid w),
\]

where an empty first block is removed. This is the alternating-word resolution for a fibre product of polynomial algebras. The first ranks are

\[
1,6,24,92,354.
\]

The augmented D resolves C=R/I. Removing D_0 gives a resolution of I; place D_1,D_2,D_3 in homological degrees three, four, five. Its remaining terms continue in higher degrees. The displayed differential is the chosen signed representative of that shifted resolution.

### Exactness over the integer coefficient base

The resolution is not justified merely by dimensions. There is an A-linear contraction on coefficient monomials and alternating words.

A surviving nonconstant short-variable monomial belongs to exactly one sheet. If its sheet differs from that of the first block, take its least variable, remove one copy from the coefficient, and prepend the corresponding singleton block. Its boundary recovers the original term because mixed products vanish.

If its sheet agrees with the first block, use the ordinary ordered polynomial Koszul contraction on that block: choose the least variable in the union of coefficient support and first-block support. If it occurs in the block, the contraction is zero. Otherwise remove one coefficient copy and insert the variable into the first block with the exterior sign. Keep the remaining word unchanged.

For a constant coefficient, apply the same rule to the first block. For the empty word and constant coefficient, use zero.

The Koszul identity on a first block, and the mixed-product identity when a singleton is removed, give

\[
\partial h+h\partial=1-\iota\epsilon
\]

on every monomial and every word. Thus the construction is exact integrally, not only over a field. The contraction is A-linear, not R-linear and not claimed equivariant. Exactness requires neither of those stronger properties.

The checker tests the formula on words through degree three and representative pure-sheet monomials, and verifies square-zero through word degree four. The argument above proves the all-degree, arbitrary-exponent statement. Moore's alternating-word fibre-product resolution is a primary literature reference for the same construction.

## 4. Complete derived-map equations

A degree-zero map from the resolved source to N has only two possible sets of nonzero components:

\[
Y_a\in N_3,\qquad A_r\in N_4,
\]

where a labels six generators and r labels twenty-four relations. All source terms of degree at least five have zero image because the full target has no such degree.

The chain equations are

\[
dY_a=0,
\qquad dA_r=f(d_1r),
\qquad f(d_2s)=0
\]

for all ninety-two next relations s. The last equation reads the actual coefficients of d_2 on the chosen A_r.

The sixty-dimensional Y space is already the full solution of the first equations. The degree-four possibilities for A_r give 288 additional unknowns, occurring on eight relation generators involving 35. The complete remaining system has 348 unknowns, 1,101 coefficient equations, and rank 308. Its kernel is therefore rank forty.

Unit-pivot integer elimination constructs and verifies every solution. There is no coefficient-degree truncation. At the prescribed occurrence and regulator grades, the coefficient monomial of every allowed source-to-target entry is forced. Its remaining parameter is an integer.

Higher source generators add no omitted map equations: their boundaries land in source terms whose images are already zero. The ninety-two equations are sufficient because the alternating-word complex is a full resolution.

### No homotopy quotient has been omitted

A chain homotopy would map D_1, placed in degree three, into N_4 at one of the six single occurrence weights. There are no conductor-valued N_4 states at those weights. D_2 would map into N_5=0, and all later terms also have zero target.

Thus the degree-minus-one Hom group, and every lower Hom group, vanish in this grading. The forty maps are distinct derived classes, with no positive mapping-space homotopy groups in the specified component. This conclusion concerns maps into N, not maps obtained by forgetting the conductor ideal or the endpoint/Q frame.

### Values and hidden source-relation data

The rank-sixteen strict map lattice embeds as a saturated sublattice in the rank-forty resolved lattice. Restricting to strict module maps would discard twenty-four legitimate resolved directions.

## 5. Explicit nonzero maps

Write

\[
U=[\{03,13,35\},\{03,13,35\},0]
-\beta[\{13,35\},\{13,35\},0].
\]

Although dU is not zero, all its coefficients are supported on the positive sheet. Therefore multiplication by I_- kills dU.

The strict module map

\[
g_-(f_-+f_+)=f_-U,
\qquad f_\pm\in I_\pm,

is a chain map from I in degree three into N. It sends each negative generator X_a to X_aU and every positive generator to zero. Its six nonzero coefficient terms on source generators satisfy all twenty-four module relations and all ninety-two resolved equations. It is nonzero in the computed map space.

This is an actual map on the whole conductor ideal. It is not asserted to be the physical two-sheet comparison; in particular its positive-sheet part is zero.

\[

\[

and zero on the other relation generators and all higher source terms.

The three nonzero images are closed. The ninety-two source compatibility equations hold: the negative-sheet relations cancel by commutativity and the positive-sheet products vanish. It preserves the complete endpoint and Q values, but records information on source relations that no scalar conormal readout sees. The occurrence partner in its formula is essential.

## 6. Actual symmetry

Use the published action

\[
r(v)=v+2\pmod6,\qquad s(v)=2-v\pmod6.
\]

The target carries its cellular top signs and ordered normal-mark signs. The source resolution carries the natural permutation of conductor variables and the exterior signs within each source block. No extra character is inserted.

Reflection stabilizes the occurrence correction 35. Rotation takes it through 35,15,13. The checker reconstructs all three target complexes and transports every equivariant resolved map on the complete source resolution. It checks all source chain equations, r cubed, and the reflection relation on the transported family.

The fixed ranks are

\[
\operatorname{rank}L_{\mathrm{strict}}^{\langle s\rangle}=5,
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}L_{\mathrm{resolved}}^{\langle s\rangle}=13.
\]

Two of the thirteen invariant directions vanish on the six source generators. The invariant generator-image lattice has rank eleven, and

\[
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^2\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^{13}
\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^{11}\longrightarrow0

is exact and saturated. The eleven nonzero factors in its integer diagonalization are all one. Thus an invariant admissible generator assignment has an integral invariant resolved lift, with two parameters left on source relations.

This is a different sequence from the previous index-two obstruction for preimages of nonzero quadratic target defects. That result is not transferred to the present source-resolution problem. Here the specified invariant generator-lifting map has no finite-index defect.

For example, g_- plus its reflected map is an integral invariant strict map. Its rotation orbit defines a full three-label dihedral family. This is a sum, not division by the group order.

The source's additional one-step sheet-exchange polarity and any geometric normal-line transport outside the stated subgroup remain separate input. No unsupported full twelve-element action inside a single corrected target is assumed.

## 8. Physical scope and next missing datum

The construction has replaced independent conormal image choices by actual maps from a source module supplied by the normalization square, with its full source relations resolved. It yields explicit strict and derived candidates, and a finite complete classification in the declared degree and frame.

It does not identify the physical conductor–Morse homotopy. The homological placement of I is the tested deformation placement; a geometric specialization must derive its own shifts, reciprocal normal lines, and comparison cells. The regulator family is algebraic at beta zero. The existing fixed-nonzero-beta purity theorem is not silently extended there.

The remaining physical comparison must select one of the thirteen equivariant resolved maps in this specified candidate category. Its alternating conormal value leaves two parameters undetermined. Those parameters can only be fixed by data acting on the source relations or their comparison homotopies, not by a scalar unit residue.
