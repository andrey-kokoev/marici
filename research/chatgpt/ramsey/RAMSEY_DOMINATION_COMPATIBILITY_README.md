# Ramsey(3,7): domination compatibility and minimal incidence obstructions

All vertices are labelled from 0. The red graph `G` is triangle-free and has independence number 6; the blue simplices are red independent sets. The catalogue consists of the 191 critical Ramsey(3,7;22) graph6 graphs from Brendan McKay: https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~bdm/data/ramsey.html .

## General exact criterion

For a vertex subset `U`, let `W=V\U`. The following are equivalent:

1. `U` intersects every maximal red-independent set of `G`.
2. There is no red-independent `S⊆W` that red-dominates `U`, i.e. with `N_G(u)∩S != ∅` for all `u∈U`.
3. The independence complex `K=Ind(G)` equals the union of the simplicial stars `st_K(u)`, `u∈U`.

Proof: maximal red-independent sets are precisely independent dominating sets. A maximal independent set missing `U` dominates `U`; conversely, any independent `S⊆W` dominating `U` extends to a maximal independent set entirely inside `W`. The star condition follows from `st_K(u)=K[V\N_G(u)]` and maximal independent extension.

If every vertex in `U` has red degree 6, then each `N_G(u)` is a maximum blue 5-simplex; the three equivalent conditions establish a 7-facet obstruction to adding one vertex.

## The domination compatibility complex

Fix `U` and `W=V\U`. Define

    D_U = {Q ⊆ U: exists independent S ⊆ W with S ∩ N_G(q) != ∅ for all q in Q}.

`D_U` is downward closed. A minimal nonface `Q` is an inclusion-minimal jointly infeasible set of red-domination requirements. On its own vertices, `D_U[Q]=∂Δ^(|Q|-1)` and has nonzero reduced homology in dimension `|Q|-2`. This need not survive in the full `D_U`. A cone vertex of `D_U` is precisely a vertex appearing in none of its minimal nonfaces.

The Stanley–Reisner ideal is generated by squarefree monomials `∏_{q∈Q} y_q` for these minimal nonfaces. Both the ideal and the minimum nonface arity depend on the choice of `U`.

### Graph 57: a four-demand contradiction

`U={8,9,11,12,13,15,20}`. Minimal nonfaces:

    {8,11,12,13}, {8,11,13,15}.

Outside `U`, the neighbor-choice domains are:

    N(8)∩W={2,10,19}
    N(11)∩W={7,14,17}
    N(12)∩W={1,17,21}
    N(13)∩W={0,14,16}

Any red-independent set simultaneously dominating 8 and 13 contains at least one of the only three compatible pairs `{0,2}`, `{0,10}`, `{2,16}`. The first and third exclude all red neighbors of 11. The second permits only 7 for 11 and only 1 for 12, but 7--1 is a red edge. Therefore all four demands are incompatible. Every triple is compatible, witnessed respectively by `{2,17}`, `{0,7,10}`, `{0,1,2}`, `{1,14}` for the triples (8,11,12), (8,11,13), (8,12,13), (11,12,13).

The Stanley–Reisner ideal of this chosen `D_U` is

    I=(y8*y11*y12*y13, y8*y11*y13*y15).

### Graph 124: a five-demand contradiction

`U={7,8,9,11,14,18,21}`. Its unique minimal nonface is `{7,8,9,11,21}`.

Outside `U`, the domains are

    N(7)∩W={3}
    N(8)∩W={4,13,17}
    N(9)∩W={2,12,16,20}
    N(11)∩W={5,6,10,16}
    N(21)∩W={0,1,2,6}.

Dominating 7 forces 3. Then dominating 8 forces 4. With 3 and 4 selected, dominating 9 requires 12 or 16; dominating 11 requires 10 or 16. Since 12 and 10 are red-adjacent, both requirements force 16. All remaining choices for dominating 21 are red-adjacent to at least one of 3,4,16. No independent dominating choice exists. Every four-demand subfamily is feasible, with witnesses in `ramsey_domination_compatibility_results.json`.

The Stanley–Reisner ideal here is `(y7*y8*y9*y11*y21)`.

## Cover dependence: exact enumeration of minimum covers

Each of graphs 57 and 124 has minimum saturated-vertex transversal size 7. All such seven-element transversals have been enumerated:

* Graph 57 has **4** minimum saturated covers. Their minimum incompatible-demand arities are 3,3,4,4.
* Graph 124 has **26** minimum saturated covers. Their minimum incompatible-demand arities occur with multiplicities: 2 (5 covers), 3 (5), 4 (4), 5 (8), 6 (4).

Thus minimal-incompatibility arity **is not an invariant of the underlying Ramsey graph**. A four-demand infeasibility for fixed `W=V\U` is **not** automatically a four-facet cover, because shrinking `U` enlarges the available independent-set universe `W`.

## Two-direction minimality theorem

Suppose `U` is an inclusion-minimal transversal of maximal independent sets, and `Q` is a minimal nonface of `D_U`. Let `F=U\Q`. Then the feasibility condition "an independent set outside Q∪F red-dominates every q∈Q" is false. It becomes true upon dropping any one domination demand q from Q while keeping the forbidden set U fixed, by minimality of Q. It also becomes true upon allowing any one f∈F while keeping all demands Q: inclusion-minimality of U supplies a maximal independent set M with M∩U={f}; since M is maximal, it red-dominates U\{f}, including Q.

Graph 57 realizes a 4-demand/3-additional-exclusion presentation; graph 124 realizes a 5-demand/2-additional-exclusion presentation. These are valid conditional descriptions of the same seven-facet obstruction and **not** general conservation laws about Ramsey numbers.
