# Cyclic phase locking survives and yields a sharper discriminator

ramsey_repair_cyclic_phase.py recovers root-fixing graph automorphisms independently of repair data: permute the six neighbors of Hall center0 and force the fifteen duad images. Both source190 and source191 have exactly five root-fixing automorphisms, forming C5. All 80 triangle-free individual switches split into sixteen orbits of five.

For source191, the ten switches involved in mutual repair form exactly TWO complete C5 orbits. One orbit's members each create one independent seven; the other's each create six. The five successful pairs form a unique equivariant one-to-one correspondence between these orbits, generated by simultaneous rotation of one representative pair. No switch participates in multiple successful pairs. Source190 has no mutually repairing pairs.

For a fixed A in the one-defect orbit, rotate B through the six-defect orbit. With phase0 chosen to be its successful partner, and generator as saved in JSON:
phase | vertex-disjoint | A repairs all B | B repairs all A | A repairs B's root-containing seven
0 | yes | yes | yes | yes
1 | yes | no  | yes | no
2 | yes | no  | no  | no
3 | no  | no  | yes | yes
4 | yes | no  | no  | no
Each B has exactly ONE independent seven containing root0. Thus among the DISJOINT candidates, repairing that distinguished root-containing obstruction ALONE locks the correct phase. Once aligned, the other five B obstructions and the one A obstruction are also repaired; independently enumerating joint sevens confirms none remain. Phase3 also repairs the distinguished obstruction but overlaps A's support, so cannot be silently counted as a disjoint cooperative pair.

Explanatory gain: five successes are one relative-phase configuration, not unrelated coincidences. Root symmetry is recovered before computing repairs, so the grouping is not a fitted permutation. The external independent-six obstruction supplies a concrete relative-phase discriminator even though pure separation of constraint channels was false.

Limit: this is a finite symmetry reduction and exact certificate. It does not by itself prove why the other fourteen switch orbits cannot participate or why all joint-only defects are absent in the aligned configuration without a representative check. The broader mutual-repair implication was already exhaustively tested; a short structural proof remains open. Do not confuse root-graph C5 phase locking here with literal BRNN row swapping: no row comparison was imposed in this experiment.
