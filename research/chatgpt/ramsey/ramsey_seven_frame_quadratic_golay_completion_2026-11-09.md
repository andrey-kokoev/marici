# Seven-rotation quotient and quadratic Golay completion

9 November 2026

## Scope

This note derives one exact, conditional route from the binary red-support encoding of `RGRGR00` to a binary [23,12,7] perfect code. No 23-position Golay generator or parity-check matrix is used as input. The route depends on the *proposed* extra constructor: represent the intrinsic six-dimensional quadratic comparison space as the quadratic-pair space of four binary phases, and attach one incidence column for **every** four-phase state.

The derived mathematical objects are not yet a red/blue edge coloring of a 22-vertex graph, nor a proof of R(3,7)=23. A Golay length matching a Ramsey threshold is not a Ramsey theorem.

## 1. Derive the six-dimensional root from the row

Encode the red positions of `RGRGR00` as `x=1010100` (indices 0,2,4 marked), in F₂^7. Let T be the cyclic position shift. Its seven translates `x,T x,...,T⁶ x` are linearly independent, as verified by binary elimination. Their XOR is the all-ones vector `q=1111111`.

Quotient by this source-generated invariant direction:

`V = F₂^7 / <q>`, with `dim(V)=6`.

Let `h_j=[T^j x]`. The seven quotient generators span V and satisfy **exactly one** linear dependence: `h_0+...+h_6=0`. Thus the map assigning syndrome `s(A)=sum_{j in A} h_j` is injective on subsets `A` of size at most three. There are `1+7+21+35=64=|V|`, so it is bijective. This root is the perfect three-error packing of the binary [7,1,7] repetition code, represented here by the rotated primitive rather than an inserted parity-check matrix.

The quotient direction is uniquely forced *among nonzero binary linear quotient kernels retaining three-mark injectivity*: measured in the **basis of seven rotation occurrences**, any kernel combination involving 1..6 generators is the XOR of two configurations with at most three marks and would cause a collision. The unique eligible one-dimensional kernel is generated by the sum of all seven occurrences, which evaluates to the all-ones position vector.

### A useful failed promotion

Two independently supplied copies of the six-dimensional quotient have 12 dimensions. Quotienting by one vector produces 11 dimensions, numerically `2^11=2048`. However, for the 14 combined positions, the 470 configurations of at most three marks have 470 distinct syndromes before quotienting, and their pairwise differences exhaust *all* 4,096 vectors of F₂^12. Therefore every one of the 4,095 nontrivial one-dimensional seam quotients creates a collision. Independent doubling plus a one-bit identification is not Golay's completion.

## 2. The canonical full-exchange quadratic structure

Any permutation of the seven generators `h_j` preserves their unique linear relation and so induces a linear automorphism of V. Hence the completed root has an emergent S₇ symmetry, even though the original ordered colored row had only a smaller distinguished rotation/reflection action.

An S₇-invariant alternating bilinear form B on V takes one common value on each pair of *different* generators. The zero choice is degenerate; the unique nondegenerate choice is

`B(h_i,h_j)=1` for `i != j`, and `B(h_i,h_i)=0`.

Its 7×7 Gram matrix has all off-diagonal entries 1; its kernel is the single all-ones relation, so it descends to a nondegenerate form on six dimensions.

The S₇-invariant quadratic refinement Q with this polar form must assign the same value `c` to each `h_i`. Since `sum_i h_i=0`,

`0=Q(0)=7c+binom(7,2)=c+1` in F₂.

Thus `Q(h_i)=1` is forced. For a subset A with t elements,

`Q(sum_{i in A} h_i)=t+binom(t,2) mod 2`.

In particular Q has 36 zero vectors and 28 vectors of value 1. It is a plus-type six-dimensional quadratic form.

## 3. A four-dimensional quadratic-phase realization

A six-dimensional plus-type quadratic space admits an isometry with the coordinate space Λ²F₂⁴, whose six basis positions are the unordered pairs of four phase coordinates. The associated Pfaffian quadratic form is

`Q0(w)=w_01 w_23 + w_02 w_13 + w_03 w_12`.

This identification is a **chosen realization**, not a unique phase frame supplied by the source row. The four-dimensional phase size is the minimal positive solution of `binom(d,2)=6` with d≥2.

Within this coordinate space, one can find a seven-element *symplectic heptad* `(h_0,...,h_6)` satisfying `Q0(h_i)=1`, `B0(h_i,h_j)=1` for i≠j and `sum h_i=0`. The checker searches the 28 nonsingular vectors and finds the first heptad lexicographically, without using a Golay matrix. One such heptad in 6-bit coefficient encoding is

`(12,22,27,39,41,53,58)`.

For every `z=(z_0,z_1,z_2,z_3)` in F₂⁴ define the *squarefree quadratic phase map*

`nu2(z)=(z0 z1, z0 z2, z1 z2, z0 z3, z1 z3, z2 z3) in F₂^6`.

It is not the wedge `z∧z` (which is zero). Introduce the 16 new syndrome columns

`g_z=(nu2(z), 1, z) in F₂^6 ⊕ F₂ ⊕ F₂⁴`.

Together with the seven old columns `(h_i,0,0)` they provide

`7+2^4=23` position columns in `6+1+4=11` syndrome dimensions.

Rank 11 is exact: the seven old columns span the first six dimensions; `g_0` supplies the parity direction, and `g_{e_j}+g_0` supplies each of the four phase directions.

## 4. A proof of minimum distance seven

Suppose at most six distinct columns have XOR zero. Let r be the number of columns chosen from the new 16-point family.

* If r is odd, their common parity coordinate sums to 1, impossible.
* If r=0, the old seven form a single circuit of length seven, so no subset of at most six sums to zero.
* If r=2, the two distinct new phase labels have nonzero XOR in the last four coordinates, impossible.
* If r=4, the four distinct labels must sum to zero in F₂⁴. They are an affine parallelogram `z,z+a,z+b,z+a+b`, with linearly independent a,b. Their quadratic contributions XOR to the nonzero **decomposable** bivector `a∧b`, which satisfies `Q0(a∧b)=0`. It cannot be zero or equal the sum of one or two old heptad vectors, because such nonempty sums have Q0-value 1. (At most two old columns can accompany r=4.)
* If r=6, there are no old columns. Suppose six distinct phase points have zero sums of the constant, all four linear coordinates, and all six quadratic coordinates. Form the 5×6 matrix whose columns are `(1,z)`. Then `AA^T=0` over F₂. Its row space is self-orthogonal, so `rank(A)≤3`. All six distinct points must therefore lie in an affine subspace of dimension at most two, which contains at most four points: contradiction.

There is therefore no nonzero dependency of weight at most six. The seven old columns sum to zero, so the minimum dependency length is exactly seven.

The 23-column check matrix has rank 11. Its kernel is a linear `[23,12,7]` code, and the 2048 vectors of weight at most three have distinct syndromes. Because their number equals `2^11`, the code is perfect. By the classical classification, this is a binary Golay code up to equivalence.

## 5. Finite verification and Witt completion

The companion executable uses only exact F₂ arithmetic and checks:

- seven rotated source vectors, rank seven and the quotient with one seven-term relation;
- 64 distinct three-mark syndromes for the root;
- the complete 4,095-seam no-go for two independent copies;
- automatic search for a seven-vector symplectic heptad;
- all 16 columns generated from the degree-two map;
- rank 11, all 2048 distinct three-mark syndromes;
- all 4096 binary codewords, with weights `{0:1,7:253,8:506,11:1288,12:1288,15:506,16:253,23:1}`;
- each of the 8855 four-subsets of 23 positions in exactly one of the 253 weight-seven supports, giving `S(4,7,23)`;
- each of the 42504 five-subsets of 24 positions in exactly one of the 759 parity-extended octads, giving `S(5,8,24)`.

Command: `python ramsey_heptad_quadratic_golay_constructor.py`.

## 6. What is established, and what remains conjectural

**Established:** Conditional on the symplectic exchange structure and the *quadratic phase-completion rule*, the original seven-position root extends to the 23-position perfect Golay code. No 23-position codeword table or Golay parity-check matrix was used to construct it.

**Not established:** That the actual R/G/0 rotation/reflection histories require the full S₇ exchange symmetry, or that they require the specific Λ²F₂⁴ realization and addition of all 16 phase points. These are mathematically motivated constructor choices, not consequences of the original row alone. The code's 23 coordinate positions have no proved identification with vertices of a triangle-free graph of independence number six.

**Discriminating next test:** Attempt to derive the six-dimensional alternating form, its quadratic refinement, and the 16-point exterior-square phase completion *as operations on the retained native comparison records* (not just on their binary quotient). A successful proof would remove the current main selection assumptions. Separately, construct a Ramsey incidence readout to connect 23 Golay coordinate positions to the obstruction `R(3,7)=23`; a match of numerical lengths does not supply that readout.
