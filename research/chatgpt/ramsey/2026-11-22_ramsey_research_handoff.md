## Construction and obstruction

- **Finishing a port is a transversal problem.** Fixing other ports leaves a family of old rows that the last support must hit while remaining independent. In the [last-port kernel experiment](ramsey_all_last_port_kernels.json), 3,548 of 3,721 triples had a unique minimal support, but others had several. A frequently reused answer is not a mandatory answer. [Blind selection control](ramsey_blind_port_cover.json).
- **A local repair is not a completion.** Degree capacity, small blocking rows and fractional prices screen choices; none should be substituted for integral joint compatibility without a sufficiency proof. The [frozen blocker countersearch](ramsey_blocker_transfer_countersearch.py) saved a false acceptance after earlier positive tests. [Counterexample with legal maximal repairs](ramsey_blocker_transfer_countersearch.json). For the distinct question of bounding partner supply see [rational dual pricing](ramsey_partner_threshold_pricing.py); for candidate-dependent obligations see the [attachment basis experiment](ramsey_intrinsic_attachment_basis.json).
- **A genuinely forward four-port rule also transferred.** Start at two induced matching edges and copy endpoint neighbourhoods into four supports in one of two orientations. The [unchanged square rule](ramsey_square_level_transfer.py) produced witnesses on 1/3, 0/1, 1/7 and 69/191 of the catalogued cores of orders 8, 13, 17 and 22, and 22/64 sampled order-27 cores. These are cores with a witness, not percentages of all legal orientations. It is a different construction and sample from the three-port template transfer. [Results](ramsey_square_level_transfer.json).
- **Removing an edge between ports can consume scarce old degree.** In the catalogued five-port `K₂,₃` minus one edge motif, its two endpoints share an old degree-four vertex in all 82 recorded decompositions. The mutually nonadjacent right ports also need an overlap: no three disjoint supports satisfy every pair's five-row coverage on any of the seven 17-cores. The first shared vertex cannot pay for both overlaps. This explains the shortage on the archived core; the first overlap is a census fact, not a general theorem. [Decompositions](ramsey_missing_cross_edge_bridges.json) · [forward test](ramsey_exceptional_bridge_projection.py). The productive cores for this motif are types 1 and 2; that statement is not about arbitrary five-port attachments.
- **The source of a contradiction changes with level.** Almost-neighbourhood rows alone refute disjoint right supports on all seven 17-cores; they do not do so on most 22-cores. On order 22, 28 cores pass all maximum rows, but only 17 pass the next-size rows too. The [front door](README.md) gives the general criterion and the subsequent positive block construction. [Cross-level test](ramsey_near_row_level_transfer.json) · [local versus global agreement](ramsey_patch_glue.tsv).

## Address, transport and execution experiments (different claims)

- **An address chart is a prior constraint, not a consequence of a Ramsey number.** On the seven-label synthesis the unrestricted wiring produced a verified 27-vertex graph where prescribed symmetry/row rules failed. In the fixed nine-chart problem the [native synthesis](ramsey_nine_chart_native_sat.py) stored eight wirings, with 1,242 learned clauses in 18 rounds. Distinguish this from the [embedding exhaustion](verify_ramsey_nine_chart_exhaustion.py), which takes uniqueness of the order-35 witness as an external premise. In the [equivariant cube-rule problem](ramsey_cube_rule_necessity.json) there are two solutions; reducing the equivariant problem to 35 representatives is not uniqueness of the unconstrained graph. [Seven-label comparison](ramsey_seven_transport_synthesis.json).
- **The observed trace need not determine its source binding.** The [basepoint control](ramsey_basepoint_identifiability.json) admits both a stationary and a recut-following binding history for the recorded observations. A replay that reproduces a word is weaker than an online reader that can select it from presently available events. Compare [selector read barriers](ramsey_selector_read_barrier.json) with [exit payload ablations](ramsey_exit_payload_ablation.json): in the saved edge-once versus arrows control, enabling the first crossing does not suffice for reversal and operand reuse.
- **What identifies a continuation depends on what is retained.** The [triad join](ramsey_triad_join_decoder.json) records cases where the same flags allow two next exits until an additional root/parity restriction is supplied. The [unused-flag decomposition](ramsey_unused_flag_threads.json) has five paths and a residual ten-cycle; the [complement matching](ramsey_intrinsic_complement_matching.json) separates forced edges from an alternating choice. The [source-apex holdout](ramsey_source_apex_holdout.json) exhibits closing and nonclosing outcomes at the same fork and exit. [Crossing-run counts](ramsey_crossing_even_runs.py) recover matching multiplicities including zero. These are tests of specified readers, flags and wirings, not physical identifications or general Ramsey bounds.

## What remains open

The square and block methods both construct witnesses without starting from a completed target, but neither is necessary. Separate restrictions on the number and sizes of ports, the leftover matching, and the allowed blocks before explaining a failed transfer. For chart and execution results, retain the failing controls alongside the successful decoder: a conclusion that needs a binding, parity or source choice does not explain how that choice was made.
