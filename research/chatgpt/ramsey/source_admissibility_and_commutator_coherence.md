## Question

Can local compositions determine whether a green comparison must fill, may carry a nontrivial defect, or must be rejected — without independently attaching a 2-cell at every incompatibility?

Previous stages establish two boundaries. The actual six-arrow Marici source is freely generated by `AB,BC,CA,AD,DB,BA`, with two triangles as directed edge cycles, not supplied faces. The free realization has no equality between `AB` and `AD·DB`, and no two-step commuting diamond. Even conditionally adding the R/B faces leaves `pi_1 = Z`, generated by `z=AB·BA`. ## Conditional effect model

Let `H` be a group and `theta` an involutive *automorphism* of `H`. For a forward operation `a`, define its green return obstruction `N_theta(a)=a theta(a)`. Set

`S_theta = {a in H | N_theta(a)=1}`.

These are the nonabelian `C2` 1-cocycles for this action. Their definition does **not** assert that the source determines `H`, `theta`, or a native green 2-cell.

### Theorem 1 — Closure of green-admissible operations

For any `a,b in S_theta`,

`N_theta(ab) = ab a^-1 b^-1 = [a,b]`.

Therefore `ab in S_theta` **if and only if** `[a,b]=1`. Also `a^-1 in S_theta`. Consequently:

> `S_theta` is a subgroup of `H` if and only if every pair of elements of `S_theta` commutes; when this holds it is an abelian subgroup.

Proof: the displayed identity follows from the automorphism law `theta(ab)=theta(a)theta(b)` and `theta(a)=a^-1`, `theta(b)=b^-1`. Inverses preserve the norm-one condition since `theta(a^-1)=a`. Closure under multiplication is exactly pairwise commutation. The statement is elementary group theory; the contribution here is its application as the exact compositional obstruction in the proposed green model.

### Theorem 2 — Continuation-invariant discrepancy

Compare the two local routes `f=ab` and `g=ba`. Define their oriented discrepancy

`d = g f^-1 = [b,a]`.

With any prefix `p` and suffix `q`, the discrepancy between `p g q` and `p f q` is

`d_(p,q) = (p g q)(p f q)^-1 = p d p^-1`.

Thus **d has a context-independent value for all H-valued prefixes if and only if d is central**. If only a subgroup `P` consists of admitted prefixes, the exact condition is `d in C_H(P)`, the centralizer of P. Suffixes impose no extra condition under this orientation of the difference.

This provides a three-way operational classification:

1. `d=1`: strict comparison (no defect).
2. `1 != d in Z(H)`: a nontrivial, continuation-invariant central comparison label.
3. `d notin Z(H)`: context-dependent discrepancy; a fixed scalar green label is impossible, although conjugation-transported comparison data may be possible.

The centrality requirement is **derived from the stated context-invariance criterion**; context invariance itself is not yet derived from the native Marici source.

### Theorem 3 — Canonical weak comparisons in class-two groups

If every commutator of `H` belongs to `Z(H)`, then `H/Z(H)` is abelian. For every adjacent swap `ab -> ba`, the central difference is exactly `[b,a]`, computed from the operands.

For a word `w=a_1 ... a_n` and a permutation `sigma`,

`a_(sigma(1)) ... a_(sigma(n)) = c_sigma(w) (a_1 ... a_n)`,

where

`c_sigma(w) = product over inversions i<j with sigma^-1(i)>sigma^-1(j) of [a_i,a_j]^-1`.

All factors are central, so this is independent of the sequence of swaps used to realize `sigma`. In particular, the two three-branch swap routes around the permutohedral hexagon agree; no independent higher *scalar* choice is needed for this finite permutation calculus. Distinct original words and their full construction histories remain retained.

This yields a mathematically explicit **candidate green comparison generator** in a specified class-two group-effect carrier. It does not create a 2-cell in the original unfilled six-arrow geometric realization. Treating central differences as 2-morphisms is an additional semantic choice (for example, via the central extension `Z(H) -> H -> H/Z(H)`).

### Covariance matters

The preceding obstruction assumes `theta` is a covariant group automorphism. Inversion `a |-> a^-1` is always a *contravariant* anti-automorphism: `(ab)^-1=b^-1 a^-1`. Therefore a dagger/reversal operation automatically preserves inverse closure, whereas a covariant reflection can generate commutators. Equating these two structures would silently impose an extra rule on the Marici seed, where `AB` and `BA` are distinct directed occurrences.

## A Ramsey non-prediction theorem

**Warning:** A commutation graph by itself cannot explain a Ramsey threshold if the effect group is fitted to each graph.

For every finite simple graph `Gamma`, the right-angled Artin group

`A_Gamma = <v in V(Gamma) | [v,w]=1 for {v,w} in E(Gamma)>`

has exactly `Gamma` as the commutation graph of its designated generators. Nonadjacent generators are noncommuting, as seen by the retraction onto the free group on those two generators. Thus **every graph, including every Ramsey-extremal graph, can be encoded by commutator vanishings**. This does not predict anything. A predictive Ramsey mechanism must fix its source, effect carrier, or generating rule independently of the target coloring, and recover forbidden patterns from that fixed rule.

Background: right-angled Artin groups and their graph presentations (https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/node/32014); nonabelian 1-cocycles (https://math.jhu.edu/webarchive/grad/EKLee_thesis.pdf); Newman/confluence as a potential *separate* continuation theorem (https://math.univ-lyon1.fr/~malbos/Ens/lar.pdf).

## Research disposition

**Established in this model:** strict green closure is equivalent to pairwise commutation; context-invariant nontrivial defects are precisely central elements (or centralizers relative to the admitted context subgroup); class-two effects yield explicitly calculable, swap-order-independent central comparisons.

**Not established:** that the original simplex primitive selects a group effect semantics, a covariant `theta`, a class-two quotient, or any 2-cell making a comparison physically/geometrically valid. These cannot be imported from the existence of the algebraic formulas.

**Strongest next discriminator:** identify from the native source which *continuations* are admitted and what must remain invariant under them. Compute the associated centralizer of those continuations. If all source-admitted returns force a nontrivial central discrepancy, a canonical candidate G may be constructible. If no invariant nontrivial discrepancy survives, prove the no-go rather than imposing an algebraic phase. Then test the same fixed semantics on branching objects without refitting.
