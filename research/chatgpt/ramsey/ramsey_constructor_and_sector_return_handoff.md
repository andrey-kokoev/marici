# From Ramsey borders to twelve-sector return: research handoff

**Later BRNN/common-frame investigation:** see [the dedicated handoff](ramsey_brnn_common_frame_handoff.md) for the conditional direct constructor, proofs, duality distinctions, and remaining seed-necessity question. This older note remains background for the sector branch.

**Question.** Can a relabeling-invariant operation generate addresses and their link signs, rather than fit a known matrix? **Status:** we have exact border and encoding tests, and a geometric tour. We do not have a native sector-return law that generates a Ramsey matrix. A graph vertex, a column label, a sector/move, an FCC site, and a retained mark are not interchangeable addresses.

## Ramsey branch: what was constructed vs represented

- **Borders.** For (3,k), the new red neighborhood must be red-independent and hit every old blue (k−1)-clique. This generates all legal next borders, not a preferred successor. A terminal witness is not an upper bound. See the [extension theorem](ramsey_disjointness_saturation_and_cyclic_duality_2026-11-08.md), [target-free (3,8) run](ramsey_38_blind_frontier_run.txt), and [joint-pin audit for all 191 extremal (3,7) records](ramsey_37_joint_pin_audit.py). The pairwise refill result there concerns individually flippable links; [arbitrary partial matrices differ](ramsey_37_arbitrary_partial.py).
- **Constructions.** [Cyclic (3,9)](ramsey_39_cyclic_constructor_run.txt) reached 35; missing a cyclic order at 34 did not preclude 35. [Two-vertex](ramsey_310_rebuild_three_run.txt) and [three-vertex](ramsey_310_rebuild_four_run.txt) replacements in Exoo's (3,10) graph failed exhaustively in those families. [One-qubit coordinated edits](ramsey_310_pauli_xyz_lift.py) were ruled out together with the earlier two-suffix and core tests; the [unrestricted two-qubit trial](ramsey_310_two_qubit_coordinates_run.txt) **timed out**, not an impossibility result. [(3,11) trial](ramsey_311_two_qubit_trial_run.txt).
- **Arithmetic.** For (4,5), maximizing cyclic order first and then minimizing the odd prime for the tau nonzero-square rule gives [24 and 991](ramsey_45_target_free_prime_run.txt). The cyclic/tau rule was a modeling choice. The [same test](ramsey_3k_target_free_prime.py) failed at maximal cyclic order for (3,7) through (3,10).
- **Term/type and Fourier readings.** [Type realization](ramsey_38_term_type_realization.py) leaves mutual signs undetermined. [Walsh border moments](ramsey_37_walsh_multiple_borders.py) distinguish forced, optional, and correlated links; [larger cases](ramsey_38_39_walsh_borders.py) and [Exoo's graph](ramsey_310_walsh_borders.py). Reading an existing border in Fourier coordinates is not an independent constructor.
- **Cell layouts.** The [3×7×22 example](ramsey_37_incidence_core.json) uses the 21 cells as addresses and an additional two-block search. The [six-bit 3×8 layout at 27](ramsey_38_cell_6bit_27_from_catalogue.json) represents catalogue record 1499, not a catalogue-free discovery. Five bits work at 26; [sampled records](ramsey_38_sample_flat_records_run.txt) do not settle five-bit feasibility at 27. The [budgeted separator](ramsey_budgeted_separator.py) is exact for a fixed layout. [Pair lookahead](ramsey_38_pair_lookahead_run.txt) moved checks earlier but did not reduce constraint updates. These are encoding results, not a reason the Ramsey parameters should be matrix dimensions.

## Sector branch: geometry, return, and what a reader sees

Keep the [twelve FCC root translations](../../strominger/fcc-first-sector-transport-v1.md) distinct from the [twelve native quaternion comparison sectors](../../strominger/native-path-sector-calculus-v1.md). No physical identification of the two was assumed.

|---|---|
| [One full pass](ramsey_twelve_sector_return_run.txt) and [contra pass](ramsey_contra_tour_run.txt) | Using every directed FCC move once gives zero displacement when admitted. Quaternion sector products can be +I or −I; repeating the same full product restores the signed state. An exchanged neighboring pair distinguishes same from different axes. |
| [Relative link census](ramsey_native_relative_readings_run.txt) | On the twelve projective dictionary states, no nonempty undirected relative-operation red-link rule invariant under native axis cycling is triangle-free. Tetrahedral frames preserving each opposite pair give the even flips; adjoining local complement gives all three independent flips. The opposite depends on the cell, not solely the site. |
| [Twelve-step equator tour](ramsey_fcc_twelve_sector_equators.py) | The directed tour `13 14 24 23 13 12 14 34 23 12 24 34 13` exists in all 56 octahedra. Reflection upon each equatorial closure complements a vertex-star. **Reflection on closure is an added update**, not measured transport. |

| [Native anchor probe](ramsey_fcc_native_anchor_probe_run.txt) | In the recorded PN→NE replay, slot 2 (`C1`) alone is fixed by both permutations; its embedded site is (2,2,1,2). Selecting coordinate 3 by that site gives opposite feedback readings. Distance to the very same anchor ties in 12 cells. Fixedness along PN→NE does **not** establish fixedness or a coupling along square edges. |

## Prior checks and next discriminator

The [filled-boundary transport result](../../nima/tetrahedral-return-transport.md) separates returning from actively flipping a reference. [Retained record feedback](../../nima/tetrahedral-record-return.md) and its [frame control](../../nima/tetrahedral-hidden-readout-frames.md) show why equal endpoints may nevertheless give different next readings. The [native FCC replay](../../strominger/native-fcc-end-to-end-binding.md) identifies actual packet marks and interfaces, not a square-edge reader.

**Direct binding test:** the [native packet-path checker](ramsey_fcc_packet_edge_binding.mjs) tested the current C1 site's trajectory under two validated operations from each of 24 marked seed P packets and their PN successors. Of 224 two-step paths, 24 gave two root moves, none on the two disjoint coordinate pairs required by the square. This excludes the direct C1-site interpretation on those starts, not an independently bound position observable with a separate stationary reference. [Run log](ramsey_fcc_packet_edge_binding_run.txt).

**Open gate:** bind an FCC edge on each of the two routes to an independently specified native operation on the *same* marked packet. Verify slot identity and coefficient transport edge by edge, then predict the two outputs before selecting a new probe. If no such binding is available, retain the result as conditional; do not promote the equator reflection to native dynamics.
