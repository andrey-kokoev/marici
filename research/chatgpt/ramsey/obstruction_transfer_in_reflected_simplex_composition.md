# Obstruction transfer in reflected simplex composition

**Research note — 9 November 2026**  
**Scope:** Marici six-occurrence source, with two *conditionally* attached R/B triangular faces. Exact cellular computations and standard van Kampen theorems. No new native source constructor and no new Agda compilation.

## Research question

When does a local composition force a closed path to be the boundary of an admitted higher cell, and when should its homotopy class remain as a global obstruction?

This replaces the overstrong proposed principle that every compositional cycle should fill. A closed manifold itself has nontrivial top-dimensional homology; indiscriminate filling destroys desired topology.

## The normal-closure criterion (existing theorem, applied here)

Let `Q` be a connected graph and let `L` be an admitted family of loops. Attach 2-disks along `L`, forming `K=Q ∪_L D²`. For any loop `γ` in `Q`,

    γ admits a nullhomotopy (disk filling) in K
    iff [γ] lies in the normal closure of [L] in π₁(Q).

This follows from the van Kampen presentation of π₁(K); the criterion is *not* an assertion that any matching-endpoint paths in a free path category are equal. Equivalently, a van Kampen diagram made from supplied faces and their context/conjugation operations exists exactly for such a `γ`.

For nonabelian π₁, checking whether the abelianized 1-cycle vanishes is only necessary, not sufficient. Higher homotopy data is still required to compare different disk-fillings, even when a disk exists.

## Exact six-occurrence calculation

Use the actual Marici vertices `A,B,C,D` and directed occurrences

    AB:A→B, BC:B→C, CA:C→A,
    AD:A→D, DB:D→B, BA:B→A.

`AB` and `BA` are distinct occurrences. Reversing a geometric path is not identification with the opposite supplied arrow.

Adjoin the red and blue triangular face boundaries

    R = AB + BC + CA,
    B = AD + DB + BA.

These 2-cells are **not** native source facts; their adjunction is a conditional experiment. The two proposed green face boundaries are

    G1 = AB - AD - DB,
    G2 = BA - BC - CA.

Signs refer to orientations of 1-chains; in π₁ use inverse paths, not additive commutativity.

Choose the spanning tree `(AB, AD, CA)`. Independent based loops may be taken as

    x = AB · BC · CA,
    y = AD · DB · AB⁻¹,
    z = AB · BA.

The red and blue face relators become `x=1` and `y z=1`. Consequently

    π₁(K_RB) = ⟨x,y,z | x, yz⟩ ≅ ⟨z⟩ ≅ ℤ.

The first green loop is `y⁻¹`, whose class is `z`; the second, transported to base A through AB, is `z x⁻¹`, again `z`. Therefore **neither G1 nor G2 is generated by R/B relations**, including arbitrary conjugation and continuation. At least one new 2-dimensional relation (or a changed source/interpretation) is required.

### A reflection-respecting separating interpretation

The actual directed-graph involution exchanges `A↔B, C↔D` and the arrows

    AB↔BA, BC↔AD, CA↔DB.

Define an integer-valued effect on the six edges:

    w(AB)=w(BA)=1,
    w(BC)=w(AD)=-1,
    w(CA)=w(DB)=0.

It respects reflection (with identity action on ℤ), composition and inverse-path evaluation. Both existing face boundaries have effect zero:

    w(R)=w(B)=0.

But

    w(G1)=w(G2)=2,   w(AB·BA)=2.

This is a countermodel to any universal claim that reflection, reversible composition, and R/B face admission *alone* force the green equality. Modulo 2, these green values vanish: a mod-2 balancing test would miss this particular obstruction.

## Obstruction transfer when a genuinely new G relation is adjoined

The exact cellular chain complex uses four vertices, six oriented edges and the selected face columns. Its relevant invariants are:

| Admitted faces | χ | H₁(−;ℤ) | H₂(−;ℤ) | Topological status |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| R,B | 0 | ℤ | 0 | Homotopy equivalent to S¹; not a closed surface |
| R,B,G1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | Δ-complex disk (3 boundary vertices) |
| R,B,G1,G2 | 2 | 0 | ℤ | Δ-complex sphere S² |

The first additional green 2-cell kills the generator `z`, leaving a simply connected disk. The reflected second face caps its boundary, resulting in a sphere. Both vertex links and integral Smith normal forms were independently checked.

A chain identity certifies the resulting 2-cycle:

    R - B - G1 + G2 = 0.

It is nonzero as a 2-chain, and there are no 3-cells, so it generates `H₂ ≅ ℤ`. By Hurewicz, it also represents a nontrivial π₂ class. Therefore **cancelling a π₁ obstruction can create a π₂ class**, rather than yielding a featureless closed object.

This is an explicit case of a general obstruction-transfer phenomenon, not a newly discovered theorem of homotopy theory.

## The Poincaré sphere warning

For general complexes, `H₁=0` does **not** establish that every loop is fillable. The Poincaré homology sphere has the perfect binary icosahedral fundamental group `I*` (order 120), despite having vanishing first integral homology. Hence additive/cellular balancing can report zero while genuine nonabelian holonomy remains.

The six-arrow example has a simpler obstruction, fully detected by ℤ. In larger constructions we must test normal-closure membership or fundamental-group information before relying on homology.

## What would actually generate higher coherence?

There are two distinct mathematical tasks.

1. **First new face (G1).** Its boundary is not a consequence of the existing source relations. An additional *admission principle* is logically unavoidable: a source-derived comparison operation, a prescribed class of normal-form equalities, a geometric incidence requirement, or a richer semantics containing an explicit 2-witness. Calling the face a forced filler without such data is incorrect.
2. **Coherence among admitted faces.** Once a rewriting presentation supplies actual 2-dimensional relations, critical-branching analysis can organize higher comparisons. For convergent higher-dimensional rewriting presentations, Squier-style completion adds confluence cells at critical branchings and obtains a coherent presentation. These 3-cells do **not** retrospectively prove that an initially absent 2-cell was source-admissible.

For a general filtered cell construction `K¹⊂K²⊂K³...`, the connecting map in the relative homotopy exact sequence records which n-dimensional classes are killed by attaching (n+1)-cells, while kernels and relations among attachments record the next coherence questions. This gives a rigorous formulation of the progression from a defect to a higher class.

## Revised mathematical target

**Obstruction-selective completion conjecture (conditional research target):** Find a source-defined, continuation-stable admission predicate `Admit(γ)` for based or typed parallel-history defects such that:

- `Admit(γ)` supplies a witnessed 2-cell only when the source's actual relations or a new certified primitive authorize it;
- the predicate is natural under admissible whiskering and reflection;
- applying it to all selected defects produces controlled higher homotopy classes, not universal contractibility;
- critical overlaps admit specified higher coherence witnesses without erasing retained histories.

The decisive problem is how to **derive the predicate**, not how to implement a disk attachment after it is supplied. There is currently no proof that the native Marici source defines such a predicate or that the resulting topology has a Ramsey translation.
