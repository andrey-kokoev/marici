# Sector-intersection-readout: direction-to-color falsifier

Domain: twelve centroid-to-corner sides in the (3,9) chart, one for each cube edge address. At a side the preceding triangle ends its outer arrow at the corner; the succeeding triangle starts there. Thus the oriented within-face turn across a side gives a canonical entrance and exit. Between two side crossings allow forward within-face turns and reciprocal crossing of the outer seam AB to BA. Measure shortest number of intermediate moves from exit to entrance in EACH direction. Keep the unordered distance pair to enforce symmetric color. Count best possible truth-table error on the stored cube rules independently for eight states; this optimistically fits colors rather than predicting held-out data, so any collision rules out a perfect predictor. Stronger control: exact proper directed-sector rotation orbits, with and without a point mark (corresponding state corner), regardless of choice of traversal algorithm. Report exact failures on disjoint pairs separately to exclude trivial forced-blue shared-address cases. No new source for the mark assumed.
