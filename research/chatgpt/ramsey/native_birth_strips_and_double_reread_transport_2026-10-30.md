# Native Birth Strips and Double-Reread Transport

Date: 2026-10-30. Source: `andrey-kokoev/marici`, commit `cb0c25477b0b710fc1feb9dd5cc1e82e8ecdef6c`. Executed on an isolated copy with Node.js v22.16.0 and Python/SymPy 1.14.0. No upstream source was edited. All ten imported Git blob hashes matched before and after execution.

## Question and result boundary

Continue the previous birth-boundary test without identifying different occurrences or resetting absolute origins. Test whether the native strips compose, and whether native routes supply a common complete boundary for the proposed comparison.

Two results hold. The occurrence-labelled contact surfaces compose as triangulated strips, with an explicit chain homotopy between their distinct end edges. Separately, P-side and N-side *double* rereading yield two different native paths to exactly the same complete reader. Single rereading does not. The second result gives a source-derived same-boundary comparison request; it does not prove equality of its retained path histories.

Active obligations are forward realization of the occurrence strip and route/coherencer compatibility of the two native reader paths. The complexes used for those obligations are different and are not silently identified.

## 1. A native quadrilateral in each N reading

At origin i, the N window/context is

    [M_i, C_(i+1), M_(i+1) | C_(i+2)].

The selected PN and NE transfers expose the following two contact triangles, oriented here to agree along the strip:

    [C_(i+1), M_i, M_(i+1)]
    [C_(i+1), M_(i+1), C_(i+2)].

Write their sum as Q_i. Its four outside edges are the old cross-channel edge, the M continuation, the renewed cross-channel edge, and the reversed C continuation. The common diagonal is [C_(i+1),M_(i+1)]. These are actual native contact faces, not newly attached faces chosen to force a square.

An occurrence vertex retains (role, absolute index, geometric coordinate). Source reader IDs, complete aliases, five-slot permutations, outside reference marks, and actual operation records remain separately present in the accompanying data. The incidence calculation is a faithful occurrence-label test; it is not asserted to reconstruct those richer records by itself.

Consecutive N readers share the complete occurrences M_(i+1) and C_(i+2). Consequently Q_i and Q_(i+1) glue along precisely the same cross-channel edge. No equal-coordinate substitution is needed.

## 2. The relative birth-strip theorem

For k consecutive N readings, let

    B_(o,k) = Q_o + ... + Q_(o+k-1).
    b_i = [C_(i+1), M_i].
    mu_(o,k) = sum [M_i, M_(i+1)].
    gamma_(o,k) = sum [C_(i+1), C_(i+2)].

The sums range over i=o,...,o+k-1. Exact oriented chains satisfy

    boundary B_(o,k) = b_o + mu_(o,k) - b_(o+k) - gamma_(o,k).

All internal cross-channel edges cancel. The outside boundary is retained; it is not declared zero. This is the staircase triangulation of an interval times a k-edge path. An explicit combinatorial isomorphism sends C_(o+i+1) to (0,i) and M_(o+i) to (1,i).

Its cell counts are

    vertices = 2k+2,
    edges = 4k+1,
    triangular faces = 2k,
    outside boundary edges = 2k+2.

An induction that attaches each new quadrilateral along the current end edge proves it remains a disk for every k. In particular, absolute H_2 is zero. Relative to its *entire* outside boundary, H_2 is Z, generated by the strip. This relative class is not a new independent class for each subdivision and is not a count of occupied N bodies.

Define H on the source interval by H(C)=gamma, H(M)=mu, and H([C,M])=-B. Then

    boundary H + H boundary = inclusion_final - inclusion_initial.

Thus the two distinct end edges are joined by a chain homotopy with the C/M continuation edges retained. This is an incidence-chain theorem, not a native identity-type 3-cell. The usual simplicial cylinder and its chain-homotopy interpretation supply the external terminology; the displayed formula is checked directly from the actual native faces.

## 3. Composition, without folding origins

Contiguous strips compose by gluing their identical end edge:

    B_(o,k) + B_(o+k,l) = B_(o,k+l).

The two copies of the common end edge have opposite boundary signs. Three-piece composition is associative as an equality of oriented chains and of the retained union of face records. Every occurrence index remains absolute.

These equations do not assert that different native construction schedules become the same history. They assert that partitioning a fixed already-labelled strip into contiguous subpieces does not change the resulting chain. Overlapping four-face strips cannot be added as if they were disjoint; the elementary Q_i units avoid double counting.

For the admissible same-body N reread, these strips remain on a fixed occupied patch. One N reading gives 1P/1N/1E. Two or more readings, with the inverse-PN and NE contacts selected at each reading, occupy exactly 2P/1N/2E. Further steps add readers and occurrence-labelled comparisons, not additional N bodies. k readings use k-1 same-body N advances.

## 4. A new common-boundary native comparison

Let R_P and R_N denote source-native same-body reader advances. R_N is used only where it is admitted. Relative chart actions and coefficient permutations are involutive, but both advance absolute origin by one. Therefore

    R_P^2(chi,o,pi) = (chi,o+2,pi),
    R_N^2(chi,o,pi) = (chi,o+2,pi).

For P, the four body aliases are swapped in pairs; doing this twice restores them, and the unique completeP reference reconstructs the original fifth mark. The source coefficient permutation is [2,3,0,1,4], whose square is identity. For N, the body relabelling is also a double swap, completeN reconstructs the fifth reference, and [0,3,4,1,2] also squares to identity. This is a rule-level explanation, not extrapolation from a finite period census.

PN is independent of the absolute origin and preserves it. It follows that the following two actual native routes have the same complete start and end records and the same full five-coordinate action:

    P_o --R_P--> P_(o+1) --R_P--> P_(o+2) --PN--> N_(o+2)
    P_o --PN--> N_o --R_N--> N_(o+1) --R_N--> N_(o+2).

Equivalently, at the reader-transport realization,

    PN_(o+2) composed R_P^2 = R_N^2 composed PN_o.

The intermediate P_(o+1) and N_(o+1) here are the actual reread charts. The endpoints on each vertical side have the same relative chart but different absolute occurrences. No origin is equated with its old value.

This is a square of compound operations and a six-edge boundary of primitive operations. It is not a two-operation diamond. A direct census of non-backtracking paths from all 24 root markings finds no convergences at primitive lengths one or two. At length three it finds one convergent endpoint for each of the eight admitted markings, represented by precisely the two routes above. Immediate PN/inverse-PN cancellations were excluded from that census.

All even blocks generalize:

    PN_(o+2r) composed R_P^(2r) = R_N^(2r) composed PN_o.

All tested odd blocks disagree, including in their full coefficient action. This parity result does not identify opposite absolute origins.

The union of the two paths, followed by one native NE exit from their common endpoint, is a compatible rooted exit-certified 1P/1N/1E selection. Both histories can therefore be retained together; their compatibility is not merely an equality of independently computed endpoint coordinates.

## 5. A +2 continuation exists even without a direct N reread

Every tested initial N reader admits the actual detour

    inverse-PN; R_P; R_P; PN.

It returns to the same N chart and five-slot permutation at origin o+2. This is distinct from claiming a direct same-body N advance exists: 16 of the 24 root markings lack that direct operation. The detour, capped by NE, passes root, contact, and exit checks in all 192 tested initial cases. It is not a native E advance; E remains terminal.

## 6. What the common boundary does not prove

The source-native route histories are different. In the free path realization of their six chosen primitive edges, the difference of those paths is a nonzero 1-cycle. Its incidence matrix has rank five, so the six-edge circuit has H_1=Z. Its freely reduced loop word has length six.

A discriminator already available in the retained histories is the number of P-side rereads. One route contains two; the other contains zero. This is not a proposed physical observable. It demonstrates that complete endpoint and coefficient equality do not recover, or identify, the route record.

Accordingly, the result supplies all boundary data for a comparison between two source-native routes. It does not supply a filler in the free path graph, nor prove that the repository's higher comparison calculus fills this specific boundary. A category of concrete full-record transport maps has an equality here; lifting that equality to the proof-relevant native histories is the remaining comparison/reflection obligation. Adding a cell for that equality is an explicit extension, not something the raw generator has already performed.

The occurrence contact strip of Sections 1-3 and the reader-route circuit of Section 4 have different vertices and arrows. Their homology results cannot be substituted for one another. In particular, the existence of the contact disk does not by itself fill the reader-route circuit.

No fresh Agda compilation is claimed. No native operation or higher-filler constructor was added to obtain these results.

## 8. Relation to the shell question

This supplies a native linear strip and a two-reread same-boundary transport comparison. Its triangular face count is 2k, not 2k^2; a second independently generated extension direction has not been supplied. It does not yet give the note's state spaces W_k or degree-lowering operator Q_k: W_k -> W_(k-2). A forward origin increase by two is not that contraction.
