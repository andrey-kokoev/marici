# sector-intersection-readout: eliminate Cartesian hand calculation

Exploratory translation of the signed bridge to the stored oriented triangles. For a skew pair use its unique cross connector. Its ends are necessarily one face centroid F and an original vertex b. Let a be the other original vertex on the input side incident to F. Read only the stored arrow on face F between b and a. Test whether following b→a selects the near bridge and the reverse selects its central opposite. Do not tune per pair, row or mark; check orientation reversal and restoration by global antipodal mark transport. This tests sufficiency of a named ordered crossing once the geometric mark is given, not derivation of the marked state, nor that Ramsey admissibility alone privileges forward over reverse.
