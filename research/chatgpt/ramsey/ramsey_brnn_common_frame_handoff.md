## Three-layer geometry

All 72 ten-cycles have layers of sizes 10,10,10. Write C_i,M_i,D_i modulo 10:
C edges i±1; spokes C_i-M_i-D_i; middle edges M_i-M_(i+5); outer edges D_i-D_(i±3).
Outer-cycle replacement pairs the 72 cycles into 36 pairs. Choosing +3 at both transfers returns original cycle with reversed orientation (3²=-1 mod10); four transfers restore orientation. Sign choice is a convention unless supplied separately.

Every ten-cycle with a type-preserving reflection has three r-invariant residual fillings. Exactly one uses all ten cross-layer edges M_i-D_i, and exactly that filling gives M,rM a common C5 frame.
