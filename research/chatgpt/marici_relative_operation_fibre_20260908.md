# The relative mixed-operation algebra and its nonlinear coefficient realization

Date: 2026-09-08. Lane: Branch B. Repository baseline: `andrey-kokoev/marici`, commit `d1947b67a60d3e88ba77f4ca60ea02c2a306ee61`.

## Result and scope

The previous comparison loses the two-sided ideal generated by nine mixed quadratic operations. This note constructs the canonical **relative Hopf subalgebra**, rather than declaring that ideal zero or trying to invert the endpoint arrow. The subalgebra is free associative on 49 explicit primitive nested commutators, in degrees 2 through 6. It has an exact nonlinear realization as the loop homology of the moment-angle homotopy fibre determined by the same two-sheet combinatorics.

The subalgebra, its 49 generators, its full labelled dihedral action, and its action on the existing conductor endpoint modules are computed. One reflection adds a nonzero commutator of relative generators to the signed image of another generator. Thus signed permutations on generator ranks alone do not implement source transport.

The associated nonlinear space contains 36 independent infinite-order Whitehead classes in degree five that are invisible in its ordinary cellular homology. This is a calculation in a newly specified, canonical **coefficient-combinatorial realization**, not an assertion that the previous additive conductor marking space acquires these classes.

No physical target cell, permitted coefficient ring, occurrence/Rees factor, normalization attachment, or generic Q boundary is altered. No native physical endpoint connector is constructed by this coefficient realization. The earlier no-go for uniform tensoring/duality of the fixed endpoint arrow remains valid.

## 1. The fixed coefficient map

Keep the ordered occurrence triples

\[
(x_1,x_2,x_3)=(X_{13},X_{15},X_{35}),\qquad
(y_1,y_2,y_3)=(X_{02},X_{04},X_{24}).
\]

In words: positive and negative occurrence labels remain distinct. The spectator ring C retains exactly the previously admitted Rees and independent long-normal coefficients.

The coefficient rings and map are unchanged:

\[
S=C[x_1,x_2,x_3,y_1,y_2,y_3],\quad
U=S[a,b]/(ab),\quad
B=S/(x_i y_j),\quad
\psi:U\longrightarrow B.
\]

In words: psi sends a and b to zero and fixes all six occurrences before imposing the native mixed relations. The source alternating algebra is the face ring of the simplicial complex K consisting of two disjoint filled triangles, one on each occurrence triple.

The previously constructed native Yoneda algebra and occurrence quotient are

\[
\mathcal E_B=\Lambda_C(\xi_1,\xi_2,\xi_3)*_C
\Lambda_C(\eta_1,\eta_2,\eta_3),\qquad
\rho:\mathcal E_B\longrightarrow H:=\Lambda_C(C^6).
\]

In words: retain the two exterior algebras as a free product, then impose cross-sheet anticommutation in H. All six generators have operation degree one. The restriction to the auxiliary Yoneda algebra factors through rho followed by inclusion of this exterior occurrence sector. The auxiliary periodic operators remain a separate part of that algebra; they are not included in H.

The ordinary additive kernel of rho is the two-sided ideal generated by

\[
r_{ij}=\xi_i\eta_j+\eta_j\xi_i\qquad(1\le i,j\le3).
\]

In words: these are the nine lost quadratic operations established in the previous calculation.

## 2. Construct the relative algebra without killing its operations

Give every degree-one occurrence operator its primitive graded coproduct. Define

\[
\mathcal R=\{z\in\mathcal E_B:
(1\otimes\rho)\Delta(z)=z\otimes1\}.
\]

In words: this is the right Hopf kernel, or coinvariant subalgebra, of rho. It is a unital algebra and is **not** the ordinary additive kernel above. The latter is obtained by extending the positive-degree part of this subalgebra to a two-sided ideal in the whole algebra.

There is one generator for each nonempty subset P of the positive triple and nonempty subset Q of the negative triple. Put J=P union Q, choose i=min P in the fixed global occurrence order, and list J without i as j1<...<jk. Define

\[
g_{P,Q}=[u_{j_1},[u_{j_2},\ldots,[u_{j_k},u_i]\ldots]],
\qquad [v,w]=vw-(-1)^{|v||w|}wv.
\]

In words: take the displayed nested graded commutator of the existing six operators. No new independent ambient operator is introduced. Since a commutator of primitive elements is primitive and its exterior image is zero, every g belongs to the relative subalgebra.

The generator counts are

| Relative operation degree | Generator count |
|---:|---:|
| 2 | 9 |
| 3 | 18 |
| 4 | 15 |
| 5 | 6 |
| 6 | 1 |

They sum to 49. Each generator retains its full six-coordinate multidegree.

### All-degree freeness

Use Theorem 1.1 of Vylegzhanin [M1], specialized to K. Every full subcomplex K_J is either empty, one simplex, or the disjoint union of two simplices. A mixed J has reduced zeroth homology C and no first homology. The theorem therefore gives exactly one nested-commutator generator for every mixed J and **no defining relations**. Its hypotheses hold: K is a flag complex, and there are no ghost vertices. The theorem is stated for arbitrary commutative coefficient rings; no characteristic-zero extension is assumed.

Consequently

\[
\mathcal R\cong T_C(V),\qquad
V=\bigoplus_{\varnothing\ne P\subseteq S_+,\ \varnothing\ne Q\subseteq S_-}
C g_{P,Q},\qquad |g_{P,Q}|=|P|+|Q|.
\]

In words: the relative algebra is the free associative algebra on these 49 specified primitive generators. Its Hopf structure is determined by their primitive coproducts.

The corresponding extension of Hopf algebras has a module factorization

\[
\mathcal R\otimes_C\Lambda_C(C^6)\xrightarrow{\cong}\mathcal E_B,
\qquad r\otimes u_{i_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge u_{i_k}
\longmapsto r u_{i_1}\cdots u_{i_k},\quad i_1<\cdots<i_k.
\]

In words: every native operator has a unique expression in the ordered exterior basis with relative-operation coefficients on the left. This is an isomorphism of graded modules and right exterior comodules, not an algebra tensor-product decomposition. Proposition 3.7 of [M1] supplies the exact factorization theorem. The checker independently verifies the integral inverse by unit-pivot elimination in every occurrence multidegree through total degree six, including all 5,240 native degree-six words.

Its rank series is

\[
P_{\mathcal R}(z)=
\frac1{1-9z^2-18z^3-15z^4-6z^5-z^6},\qquad
P_{\mathcal E_B}(z)=(1+z)^6P_{\mathcal R}(z).
\]

In words: the infinite relative algebra is generated by finitely many labelled types, and the native rank series agrees with the earlier word resolution in every degree. The rational-function identity follows by expanding

\[
((1+z)^3-1)^2=9z^2+18z^3+15z^4+6z^5+z^6.
\]

The nine quadratic elements generate the ordinary **two-sided ideal**, because ambient degree-one occurrences can multiply them. They do not generate the unital relative algebra by multiplication alone. For example, its eighteen degree-three generators cannot be products of degree-two relative elements. All forty remaining indecomposable generators have the same interpretation at their specified degrees.

### Relation to the coefficient syzygies

The same enumeration labels the mixed-ideal resolution of B over S. The product of the two disjoint variable ideals has the tensor of their truncated Koszul resolutions. Resolving its quotient gives S-module ranks

\[
(1,9,18,15,6,1).
\]

In words: the relative generator types correspond, with the appropriate degree shift and dual/orientation convention, to the actual mixed-relation syzygies. This is consistent with the explicit product-of-transverse-ideals constructions in [M2]. It is not a replacement of the full B-free conductor resolution by this finite S-free resolution.

## 3. A nonlinear realization of the same coefficient comparison

Let T denote the topological circle group in this section; this notation is unrelated to the previously used product of six Rees parameters. Write BT for its classifying space. Set

\[
\mathcal D_K=(BT)^3\vee(BT)^3,\qquad
q:\mathcal D_K\longrightarrow(BT)^6.
\]

In words: q includes each three-coordinate classifying space into its own coordinate block. The cohomology ring of the wedge is B when occurrence cohomology is placed in degree two, whereas the product has the polynomial ring S. Thus q realizes the quotient S to B, not an unrelated six-label space.

The moment-angle fibre is the explicit finite polyhedral product

\[
\mathcal Z_K=((D^2)^3\times(S^1)^3)
\cup_{(S^1)^6}((S^1)^3\times(D^2)^3).
\]

In words: one sheet supplies three disks and the other three circles, and the roles reverse on the other sheet. Their intersection is the six-torus. There is a canonical homotopy-fibre sequence

\[
\mathcal Z_K\longrightarrow\mathcal D_K\xrightarrow{q}(BT)^6.
\]

This is the standard moment-angle/Davis-Januszkiewicz construction [M1, M3]. It is functorial in the labelled simplicial complex. The coefficient ring has cohomological generator degree two here; total loop-homology degree one then agrees with the earlier Yoneda operation degree.

The identification in [M1, Proposition 3.3 and Theorem 3.2] gives

\[
H_*(\Omega\mathcal D_K;C)\cong\mathcal E_B,
\qquad H_*(\Omega\mathcal Z_K;C)\cong\mathcal R.
\]

In words: the constructed relative algebra is the loop homology of this actual homotopy fibre. Its inclusion is the computed Hopf-kernel inclusion. This identifies graded Hopf algebras; it does not identify the complete global physical source diagram with singular cochains on these spaces.

The auxiliary coordinates also have a topological realization. Put

\[
\mathcal D_U=(BT)^6\times(BT\vee BT),\qquad
\widetilde q=(q,*):\mathcal D_K\longrightarrow\mathcal D_U.
\]

In words: the two auxiliary directions map to the base point. The cohomology pullback is the fixed map U to B. Its homotopy fibre is

\[
\operatorname{hofib}(\widetilde q)\simeq
\mathcal Z_K\times\Omega(BT\vee BT).
\]

In words: the native mixed-operation fibre and the two discarded auxiliary directions are separate factors. Neither this fibre nor Z_K is the earlier Dold-Kan cone of the map on conductor Tor modules. In particular, their homotopy-group degrees and ranks must not be interchanged.

### Compute the homotopy type of Z_K

The two disk products are contractible. The defining union is a homotopy pushout, so replacing its legs by their torus projections identifies it with the join of two three-tori:

\[
\mathcal Z_K\simeq (S^1)^3*(S^1)^3
\simeq\Sigma\big((S^1)^3\wedge(S^1)^3\big).
\]

In words: retain the two torus factors and suspend their smash product. For connected well-pointed spaces, the join-to-reduced-suspension comparison is a homotopy equivalence.

The suspension splitting of a product gives

\[
\Sigma(S^1)^3\simeq
\bigvee_{p=1}^{3}\bigvee^{\binom3p}S^{p+1}.
\]

Smashing this splitting with the other torus and applying its suspension splitting yields

\[
\mathcal Z_K\simeq
\bigvee^9S^3\ \vee\ \bigvee^{18}S^4\ \vee\
\bigvee^{15}S^5\ \vee\ \bigvee^6S^6\ \vee\ S^7.
\]

In words: 49 spheres, with dimensions determined by the same mixed subsets. This is a nonequivariant homotopy decomposition. The space and source relabellings are canonical; a pure signed permutation action on this displayed wedge decomposition is not asserted. General wedge-of-spheres results for these chordal flag complexes are also available in [M4].

### Explicit integral cellular verification

Use the product CW structure with one base-point cell, one circle cell, and one disk cell in each coordinate. A cell is `(J,D)`, where J is the set of non-basepoint coordinates and D is its subset of disk coordinates. D must lie wholly in one sheet. Its dimension is |J|+|D|. There are 368 cells in this coefficient realization; none are attached to the physical 215-state target.

The boundary removes one disk coordinate to a circle coordinate, with sign

\[
\partial[J,D]=\sum_{i\in D}(-1)^{\operatorname{pos}_J(i)-\operatorname{pos}_D(i)}
[J,D\setminus\{i\}].
\]

In words: apply the actual tensor-product cellular boundary. Positions are numbered from zero.

Reorient by alpha(J,D)=(-1) to the sum of the positions in J of the members of D. The fixed-J block becomes the augmented simplex-chain complex of K_J, shifted by |J|+1. On each simplex component, coning to its least vertex contracts all higher chains. Cone the shared augmentation to the least negative vertex when present, and otherwise to the least positive vertex. In mixed support this leaves exactly the difference of the two component roots. This constructs an integral contraction in every block.

The checker verifies the full contraction identity on all 368 cells and all six dihedral chain actions. A mixed support J has one primitive cycle in degree |J|+1; a pure support has none. These chain calculations prove the homology enumeration and orientation formulas. The homotopy-pushout and suspension-splitting argument, not the chain contraction alone, proves the wedge decomposition.

## 4. Source symmetry has decomposable corrections

The physical label rotation and reflection are the already specified vertex maps v to v+2 and v to 1-v on the hexagon. The latter sends

\[
\xi_1\leftrightarrow\eta_2,\qquad
\xi_2\leftrightarrow\eta_1,\qquad
\xi_3\leftrightarrow\eta_3.
\]

In words: use the actual short-diagonal labels, not an independently fitted sign character.

Take the relative generator

\[
g=[\xi_2,[\eta_1,[\eta_2,\xi_1]]].
\]

Then its exact reflection image is

\[
s(g)=-g+[r_{21},r_{12}]
=-g+r_{21}r_{12}-r_{12}r_{21}.
\]

In words: reflection changes the degree-four generator by a nonzero product commutator of degree-two relative generators, in addition to reversing its indecomposable sign. The two product words are distinct free-algebra basis elements.

The checker computes every source-dihedral image of all 49 generators, decomposes each through the exact integral factorization, and records every term. All exterior tails vanish, as required for the relative subalgebra. On indecomposables the resulting sign is the occurrence-permutation sign multiplied by minus one for a sheet exchange; it agrees with the explicit cellular orientation calculation. On complete operators, additional product terms are necessary. The exact group-composition equations hold without averaging or dividing by two or three.

These corrections are fixed formulas inside the original six-operator algebra. They do not supply physical endpoint 2-cells by themselves.

## 5. The lost operations act on both endpoint modules

Retain the earlier central-chart module identification. One occurrence-ideal channel has

\[
\operatorname{Ext}_B^n(I_\sigma,C)
\cong(\mathcal E_B\mathcal E_\sigma^{>0})^{n+1}.
\]

In words: its coefficient operations are the native words ending on the specified sheet, with degree lowered by one. Seven copies occur on each side of the complete source, including the endpoint copy on that side. This is the previously checked connecting-map identification, not a new source assumption.

For every i,j,

\[
r_{ij}\xi_i=\xi_i\eta_j\xi_i\ne0,
\qquad
r_{ij}\eta_j=\eta_j\xi_i\eta_j\ne0.
\]

In words: the same relative quadratic operation acts nontrivially on both endpoint-channel types. These are degree-two operations on the degree-zero channel generators. The words are nonzero in the native module and are lost in the exterior quotient.

Therefore neither endpoint channel, with its existing operation action retained, can be obtained merely by restricting a module over H along rho. Such a restricted module would be annihilated by every r_ij. This is a statement about the operation-preserving coefficient-module comparison. It does not exclude a different supported-dual source or an operation that changes the comparison category.

The generic coefficient summand by itself has the augmentation action and does not detect these operations. Matching its scalar unit consequently supplies no substitute for this endpoint-module test.

## 6. A nonlinear infinity-groupoid and an explicit linearization loss

Define

\[
\mathfrak Z=\Pi_\infty(\mathcal Z_K).
\]

In words: take the fundamental infinity-groupoid of the constructed coefficient homotopy fibre, retaining its genuine nonlinear homotopies. It is 2-connected and has nine independent third-homotopy generators, coming from its bottom three-spheres.

Let a and b be distinct such generators, with loop Hurewicz images r_a and r_b. Their Whitehead bracket is detected by the Samelson/Hurewicz identity

\[
\operatorname{hur}(\operatorname{ad}[a,b])
=r_ar_b-r_br_a\ne0
\quad\text{in }H_4(\Omega\mathcal Z_K;\mathbb Z).
\]

In words: the bracket becomes the difference of two distinct relative word products. It has infinite order. For the 36 unordered pairs of bottom generators, these differences have independent free word coordinates. Thus they span a free rank-36 subgroup of pi-five of Z_K. No computation of the remaining pi-five summands is claimed.

Every bracket factors through the wedge of bottom three-spheres and has zero ordinary Hurewicz image in H-five of Z_K. Consequently the ordinary integral chain linearization loses this entire subgroup. The relationship between Samelson, Whitehead, and loop-product calculations is also used in [M5]. The displayed nonvanishing here is checked directly in the free algebra.

This does not contradict the earlier statement that the additive Dold-Kan conductor marking space has no such nonlinear operations. Z_K is a different, explicitly constructed space realizing the operation algebra. An equivalence between these two spaces is neither asserted nor implied by matching graded coefficient ranks. No new arithmetic-prime torsion is asserted by this construction.

## 7. Remaining physical comparison

This construction solves a coefficient-level realization problem: it retains all the mixed operators lost by the occurrence exterior quotient, gives their all-degree product algebra, realizes it in a canonical homotopy fibre, and specifies their transport and endpoint-channel action.

It does not erase the eighteen-term target connecting class, change the non-split endpoint attachment, or realize a normalization-provenanced geometric correspondence to the seven-state Q object. The source relation X times an excess class is not replaced by one. The branch and normal-support distinctions used by the parallel calculations remain unchanged.

The new test for a native comparison is concrete: it must account for the nontrivial r_ij action on both endpoint modules and for the decomposable terms in their transported higher operators. A comparison factoring through the occurrence exterior quotient cannot do so while keeping those module structures fixed. A comparison whose variance or target operations differ requires a separately typed action map.
