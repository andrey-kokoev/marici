What was selected, and what was derived
The new routing convention places a port at each seam midpoint and chooses the fewest-seams route for each specified history pair. Every one of the 32 requests has a unique shortest route. This policy was not present in the earlier construction.
The seam reflections are calculated from the existing geometry. They obey S(j←i) = Tⱼ Tᵢ⁻¹ on every complete labelled 4-simplex. Thus every allowed route between the same endpoint cells gives the same transport, even when the route histories differ. Distinct construction histories and route records are retained.
32 counts endpoint requests, not independent physical wire channels or sheets of a bundle. Routes share seams. A separate, explicitly defined cover of the five vertex-label states has five sheets and trivial return. No native Su-32 port admission is claimed.
