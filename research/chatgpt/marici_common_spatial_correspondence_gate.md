## 1. Outcome and scope

The requested nonzero supported-Verdier realization is **not established by this calculation**. A specific, natural common finite spatial correspondence is constructed and tested completely. It uses the actual marked log subdivision, both labelled endpoint collars, the existing seven Morse triangles, and the previously constructed supported Rees residue. It requires no new physical filling cell.

This candidate preserves the normal Gysin morphism and the independent source excess. It also supplies both endpoint comparison homotopies, in the same cone map rather than as unrelated scalar checks. Nevertheless its generic morphism is explicitly nullhomotopic. It therefore cannot realize the previously computed primitive reverse generic pairing by any support-compatible comparison that factors through this generic morphism.

The failure is not a claim that all logarithmic or supported-Verdier correspondences are impossible. In particular it does not rule out a nonfactorizing, complementary-support dual-cell/cap construction that uses the whole filtered source and its higher comparison data. Applying a duality functor to the particular zero generic morphism below, however, does not produce such a construction.

The calculation exposes an additional source-audit issue: the subposet consisting of the chamber and three long facets is not the relative chain quotient by the short-boundary subcomplex. A projection that simply discards mixed flags is not a chain map. Counting one generic flag with coefficient one cannot replace the needed comparison to the actual generic object.

## 3. One literal carrier correspondence with both endpoint collars

Use the source's local exceptional fibre

\[
F=\{h\subset b_+,\ h\subset b_-\},\qquad A_F=\{b_+,b_-\},
\]

where

\[
h=\{E,x_3\},\qquad
b_+=\{E,x_1,x_3\},\qquad
b_-=\{E,D03,x_3\}.
\]

In words: the two oriented edges form the existing exceptional V-shaped tree; they are not an extra formal suspension.

Let `Z` be the simplicial subcomplex of the actual subdivided flag space generated by the seven source Morse triangles together with the two endpoint collars and the exceptional interval. The collar path visits, in order,

\[
v_+,\ \{x_1,x_3\},\ b_+,\ h,\ b_-,\ \{D03,x_3\},\
 c,\ \{D03,x_0\},\ d,\ \{x_0,x_4\},\ v_-,
\]

where `c={D03,x0,x3}` and `d={D03,x0,x4}`. In words: every edge is an existing comparable pair of subdivided faces, traversed with its appropriate sign.

There is an explicit simplicial map `r:Z->F`. It collapses the positive collar to `b_+`, the negative collar to `b_-`, and is the identity on the exceptional fibre. On the additional Morse vertices it sends `o` to `h` and `D03` to `b_-`. Every triangle maps to a simplex of `F`, possibly degenerate. Both endpoints map to their prescribed rays.

The right map `p:Z->P` is the actual normalized blowdown. Thus the finite pair correspondence is

\[
(F,A_F)\xleftarrow{\ r\ }(Z,\{v_+,v_-\})
\xrightarrow{\ p\ }(P,\{v_+,v_-\}).
\]

In words: the same middle space carries both endpoint collars and all seven Morse triangles. It has 13 vertices, 19 edges, and 7 triangles. Eighteen integral signed-unit cancellations reduce its relative chain complex to one degree-one generator; `r` sends that generator primitively to the exceptional interval class.

For occurrence coefficients, this equivalence is made in the actual principal-line subobject whose flag module is generated by `m_{S_0}[S_0,...,S_n]`. In that framed line the differential is ordinary incidence. Division by `m_{S_0}` is only the inverse isomorphism from the principal ideal to its named free rank-one frame; it is not inversion in `A`. The inclusion of that line subobject into the ambient occurrence complex remains a separate map. No equivalence of the entire ambient complex with the normalized line subobject is claimed.

This is a finite labelled carrier correspondence with a normal-support coefficient construction. It is not, by these definitions alone, a raw ringed normalization-sheet-to-PC correspondence.

## 6. The actual generic quotient makes this map nullhomotopic

The generic spatial quotient is

\[
Q_{\mathrm{flag}}=C_*(NP)/C_*(NP_B).
\]

In words: only flags lying entirely in the short-boundary subposet are killed. Mixed flags beginning in the chamber or a long facet and continuing to a short-boundary face remain present.

Because the original endpoint map factors through the short boundary,

\[
\pi_Q\Psi=0,
\qquad

=d(\pi_QL)+(\pi_QL)d.
\]

In words: the entire generic morphism, not just one scalar readout, has the displayed nullhomotopy.

In particular,

\[
\pi_Qq_{\partial}=d(\pi_QH).
\]

In words: the coefficient-one generic flag occurs in an actual boundary. Its occurrence does not establish a nonzero derived generic class.

## 8. Comparison with the previously established reverse pairing

The preceding calculation established, in its stated finite homogeneous mapping complexes,

\[
\partial^\#[\Theta_B]=[\Theta_Q],\qquad
\langle\Theta_B,\beta_+\rangle
=\langle\Theta_Q,G_+\rangle=[\varepsilon_x]\ne0.
\]

In words: the reverse pairing has a primitive Gysin-valued generic evaluation. That result remains in force within its stated domain. It was not a claim that the full ringed dual was already constructed.

The new candidate cannot provide the missing identification of that nonzero generic morphism: its generic composite is nullhomotopic. Any chain or derived comparison respecting the actual support quotient sends that null morphism to zero. Derived tensoring, supported base change, or taking the derived dual of that particular morphism also preserves zero. In a chain model, evaluating a closed functional on the displayed boundary produces a coboundary, not the class `epsilon_x`.

This is a factorization obstruction. It does not identify `q_partial`, a spatial degree-one roof, with `G_+`, a differently framed derived normal-source class. Rather, it proves that a proposed identification through the factorized carrier route cannot be the required one. A shift alone does not change this conclusion: shifting a null morphism leaves it null.

The filtered object containing `H`, its lower path, and the endpoint data is not declared trivial. A secondary or complementary-support operation may depend on those retained data before passage to the underlying generic derived morphism. Such an operation is exactly what the tested factorization does not construct.

## 9. Research consequence

The common factorized carrier/normal candidate is now explicit, including its two endpoint connector cells and every mixed tensor term. Its failure is explained by five existing Morse triangles, not by an uncomputed rank or an assumed splitting.

A successful physical comparison must be genuinely nonfactorizing at this step: it must derive the complementary-support dual-cell/cap or equivalent logarithmic operation on the whole filtered diagram, including its endpoint collars. It cannot be obtained by first taking this ordinary generic carrier morphism and then attaching a normal residue or transposing that morphism.

The requested full spatial supported-Verdier comparison, with a nonzero primitive reverse generic pairing and both physical endpoint connectors, remains unconstructed. This note narrows the admissible construction rather than presenting the failed candidate as that realization.
