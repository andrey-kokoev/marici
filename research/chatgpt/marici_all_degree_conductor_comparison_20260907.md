# All-degree conductor comparison and its operation kernel

Date label: 2026-09-07. Lane: Branch B. Repository baseline: `andrey-kokoev/marici`, commit `d1947b67a60d3e88ba77f4ca60ea02c2a306ee61`.

## Results and scope

The occurrence-preserving algebra map constructed earlier induces a conductor self-intersection comparison in every degree. Its image is exactly the exterior coalgebra on the six occurrence classes. The map is zero in every homological degree above six, although both the auxiliary and native conductor resolutions are unbounded.

On Yoneda operations, restriction is a map from the native algebra to the auxiliary algebra. Its kernel is the two-sided ideal generated by the nine mixed anticommutators. All higher operation failures are generated by these nine degree-two elements. They are not nine newly independent obstruction classes at every degree.

These statements concern the fixed augmentation-based map of coefficient algebras. They do not compare the full physical source with its target, construct either endpoint connector, or identify an occurrence puncture with a generic deformation. The previous equivalence of *total conductor kernels after derived base change* remains valid; it is a different map.

A second consequence concerns the previously computed relative endpoint comparison. Its fibre contains a free coefficient summand. Uniform derived tensoring or coefficient duality with a nonzero coefficient object cannot make that fixed comparison an equivalence. This does not obstruct changing the diagram, its variance, or its source-admitted grading operation.

## 1. Keep the source rings and the map fixed

Let C be the retained commutative spectator ring. Occurrence labels are ordered as

\[
(x_1,x_2,x_3)=(X_{13},X_{15},X_{35}),\qquad
(y_1,y_2,y_3)=(X_{02},X_{04},X_{24}).
\]

In words: the two occurrence triples are separately retained. Rees and long-normal parameters remain in C; the calculation does not adjoin any additional inverse.

Set

\[
S=C[x_1,x_2,x_3,y_1,y_2,y_3],\qquad
U=S[a,b]/(ab),\qquad B=S/(x_i y_j:1\le i,j\le3).
\]

In words: U adds the auxiliary two-branch node; B is the native alternating occurrence algebra supplied by the normalization-conductor construction [S1]. The auxiliary branch coordinates a and b are not occurrence coordinates. The source's universal stalk construction keeps occurrences in the base [S2].

The chosen comparison factors as

\[
U\xrightarrow{a,b\mapsto0}S\longrightarrow B.
\]

In words: remove only the auxiliary coordinates, then impose the native mixed occurrence relations. Every ring is augmented to the same spectator ring C, by setting all its occurrence and auxiliary coordinates to zero.

The induced comparison of derived self-intersections is

\[
f:C\otimes_U^L C\longrightarrow C\otimes_B^L C.
\]

In words: compare the conductor with itself using the auxiliary algebra and the native algebra, respectively. A normalized relative bar construction makes this map canonical for the fixed algebra map. It is not the earlier map from a base-changed normalization kernel to a native normalization kernel. Derived tensor products must be computed with the relevant resolutions [M1].

All degrees below are homological unless explicitly called Yoneda or Ext degrees. The internal occurrence/auxiliary polynomial grading is retained separately.

## 2. Explicit free resolutions and a comparison in every degree

### The native resolution

A native basis word consists of nonempty exterior blocks, alternating between the two sheets. Labels inside a block are increasing; labels may recur in later blocks after an opposite-sheet block. The empty word has degree zero. The homological degree of a word is its total number of labels.

Write a word as \([A_1|\cdots|A_k]\). The differential is

\[
d[A_1|\cdots|A_k]
=\sum_{i\in A_1}(-1)^{\operatorname{pos}_{A_1}(i)}
v_i[A_1\setminus\{i\}|A_2|\cdots|A_k].
\]

In words: apply the exterior differential to the first block; omit an empty block. Products of opposite-sheet occurrences vanish, which makes the differential square zero when the first block disappears.

An integral exactness proof is available directly on monomials. Every coefficient of B is a unique C-linear combination of constants, pure positive monomials, and pure negative monomials. For a nonconstant monomial m, use its sheet. Take A to be the first word block if it has that sheet, and otherwise take A empty. Select the least label in the union of the support of m and A. If it already lies in A, the contraction is zero. Otherwise remove its known factor from m and insert it in A with the exterior insertion sign. For a constant coefficient the contraction is zero.

Denote this C-linear operation by h and the augmentation by epsilon. It satisfies

\[
dh+hd=1-\iota\epsilon.
\]

In words: the free word complex resolves C over B in every degree, with no division by an integer or polynomial. On matching sheets this is the monomial Koszul contraction. On different sheets h prepends a singleton and its boundary returns the original term. For constant coefficients only the least-label term returns the word. These cases prove the identity at arbitrary polynomial degree.

This is the explicit fibre-product resolution underlying the known Ext free-product theorem [M2]. The monomial contraction proves the spectator-ring version used here without extending a field-only statement by assumption.

### The auxiliary resolution

For the auxiliary node C[a,b]/(ab), use alternating singleton words in a and b. Its differential removes the first singleton and multiplies by its label. There is one empty generator and two generators in every positive degree. This is the same first-block construction with one variable per sheet.

Tensor it with the Koszul resolution on the six occurrence variables. An auxiliary generator is \((e_J,w)\), where J is an occurrence subset and w is an alternating auxiliary word. Its differential is

\[
d(e_J\otimes w)=d_K(e_J)\otimes w
+(-1)^{|J|}e_J\otimes d_{ab}(w).
\]

In words: retain both the six-coordinate Koszul differential and the auxiliary periodic differential, with the tensor sign. The separated variable sets and their explicit contractions prove that this is a free U-resolution of C [M3].

### The comparison formula

For an increasing occurrence subset J, let \(\mathcal W(J)\) contain *all* native block words using every member of J exactly once. There is no requirement that occurrences in separated blocks retain their relative order. If fl(w) is the flattened word, let its sign be the permutation sign relative to the increasing order on J. Define

\[
\Phi(e_J\otimes1)
=\sum_{w\in\mathcal W(J)}
\operatorname{sgn}(\operatorname{fl}(w))\,w,
\qquad
\Phi(e_J\otimes w_{ab})=0\quad(|w_{ab}|>0).
\]

In words: sum every permitted alternating block ordering with its exterior sign. Kill positive auxiliary-word degrees, as forced by the factorization through S. Coefficients are mapped through U to B.

For example,

\[
\Phi(e_{x_i}\wedge e_{y_j})=[x_i|y_j]-[y_j|x_i].
\]

In words: the auxiliary mixed exterior class maps to the difference of the two native ordered mixed classes, not to either ordered class separately.

The first three-letter example is important:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Phi(e_{x_1}\wedge e_{x_2}\wedge e_{y_1})={}&
[x_1,x_2|y_1]-[x_1|y_1|x_2]\\
&+[x_2|y_1|x_1]+[y_1|x_1,x_2].
\end{aligned}
\]

In words: all four terms are necessary. A three-term shuffle that preserves the total order of separated positive labels would fail the chain equation.

To prove the chain equation in every degree, fix a removed label i and a remaining native word. There is exactly one word that produces this term: insert i in its first block if that block has the same sheet, and otherwise prepend i as a singleton. The product of its permutation sign and removal sign is the Koszul sign for deleting i from J. Thus

\[
d\Phi=\Phi d.
\]

In words: Phi is an actual semilinear map of free resolutions lifting the conductor identity. The comparison theorem identifies its conductor reduction with the canonical bar map. No arbitrary homology-level map has been substituted.

## 3. Compute the entire Tor image, kernel, and cokernel

Both conductor reductions have zero differential. The image on the six-coordinate Koszul sector is split injective degree by degree: for every J, the word with all its positive labels in one block followed by all its negative labels in one block has coefficient +1. Images for different J have disjoint fine degrees. These give explicit unit pivots.

Consequently

\[
\operatorname{im}H_n(f)\cong\Lambda_C^n(C^6),
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}_C\operatorname{im}H_n(f)=\binom6n.
\]

In words: the entire image is the six-occurrence exterior sector. The binomial coefficient is zero above degree six. Every auxiliary-periodic component maps to zero.

This is a statement about the comparison, not about the vanishing of either conductor resolution. Their rank series are

\[
P_U(z)=\frac{(1+z)^7}{1-z},\qquad
P_B(z)=\frac{(1+z)^3}{1-3z-3z^2-z^3},\qquad
P_{\mathrm{im}}(z)=(1+z)^6.
\]

In words: the auxiliary ranks eventually stay at 128, the native ranks grow indefinitely, and the image is finite. Here z is only a rank-series variable, not an auxiliary coordinate or Rees parameter.

| Degree | Auxiliary Tor rank | Native Tor rank | Image rank | Cokernel rank |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| 1 | 8 | 6 | 6 | 0 |
| 2 | 29 | 24 | 15 | 9 |
| 3 | 64 | 92 | 20 | 72 |
| 4 | 99 | 354 | 15 | 339 |

| 7 | 128 | 20,160 | 0 | 20,160 |

All kernels and cokernels are free over C. The unit-pivot proof holds integrally, including in characteristic two. No integer-prime torsion is inferred from these ranks.

The exterior-to-word map also respects the degreewise coalgebras dual to the Yoneda products. The coproduct of a block word includes cuts between blocks and all nonempty ordered bipartitions of a block, with their exterior signs. The checker verifies the comparison for every exterior subset. This does not assert an additional geometric E-infinity diagonal or identify these operations with Whitehead products.

## 4. Nine mixed operations generate the complete Yoneda kernel

The native Yoneda algebra is

\[
\mathcal E_B=
\Lambda_C(\xi_1,\xi_2,\xi_3)
*_C
\Lambda_C(\eta_1,\eta_2,\eta_3).
\]

In words: within each sheet the three degree-one operators obey exterior relations; across sheets the product is freely noncommutative. The asterisk is the associative free product. This is the fibre-product cohomology structure [M2].

It has explicit endomorphism representatives. For label i in the last block, remove i with sign equal to the degree of the preceding blocks plus its position in the last block. These operators satisfy the chain cocycle identity, the square-zero relation, and all within-sheet anticommutators. Products evaluate on the word basis with the common degree sign \((-1)^{n(n-1)/2}\). This identifies every Ext degree and its products, rather than only its rank.

The auxiliary Yoneda algebra is the graded tensor product

\[
\mathcal E_U=
\Lambda_C(\xi_1,\xi_2,\xi_3,\eta_1,\eta_2,\eta_3)
\widehat\otimes_C
C\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle/(\alpha^2,\beta^2).
\]

In words: the six occurrence operators form one exterior algebra; the two auxiliary operators form their own alternating-word algebra. The hat here denotes the graded tensor-product sign, not completion.

Restriction of Yoneda operations goes in the reverse direction to the Tor map:

\[
\psi^*:\mathcal E_B\longrightarrow\mathcal E_U.
\]

In words: restrict a native extension along the algebra map U to B. It fixes all six degree-one occurrence operators, and its image is the occurrence exterior subalgebra. The explicit resolution comparison proves this identification.

Its kernel is

\[
\ker\psi^*
=
\bigl(\xi_i\eta_j+\eta_j\xi_i:1\le i,j\le3\bigr).
\]

In words: exactly nine mixed anticommutators generate the two-sided ideal of lost operations.

For proof, impose those nine relations on the native free product. Together with the existing within-sheet exterior relations, they give the exterior algebra on all six generators. An integral normal-ordering algorithm provides an explicit witness: whenever adjacent opposite-sheet labels are out of order, replace their product by minus the swapped product plus the mixed anticommutator. Same-sheet swaps use the existing exterior relation; adjacent equal labels vanish. The output is the exterior normal form plus a sum of prefixes and suffixes multiplying the nine generators. No integer is divided.

The defects are not nilpotent. For one mixed pair,

\[
(\xi_i\eta_j+\eta_j\xi_i)^m
=(\xi_i\eta_j)^m+(\eta_j\xi_i)^m\ne0
\qquad(m\ge1).
\]

In words: only the two alternating products remain, and they are distinct native word-basis elements. The entire family maps to zero in the auxiliary operation algebra.

The nine-dimensional quadratic cokernel found earlier is therefore the first degree of this precise all-degree operation failure. The higher cokernel ranks do not require a new freely chosen operation at each degree.

## 5. A comparison infinity-groupoid with computed higher groups

Form the cofiber as a C-linear complex and its simplicial abelian-group model:

\[
\mathcal O=\operatorname{Cone}(f),\qquad
\mathscr O=\operatorname{DK}(\mathcal O).
\]

In words: retain the failure of the auxiliary conductor self-intersection to reproduce the native one. Dold-Kan applies because the cone is nonnegative [M4]. This is a derived coefficient comparison space, not the physical state moduli space.

Write a_n, b_n, and e_n for the coefficients of the three rank series above, and put a_{-1}=e_{-1}=0. Its homology ranks are

\[
\operatorname{rank}_C H_n(\mathcal O)
=b_n-e_n+a_{n-1}-e_{n-1}.
\]

In words: the cone retains the missing native directions and the preceding-degree auxiliary directions that were killed. Unit pivots give a direct C-module decomposition with these two summands; the internal degrees distinguish them.

The resulting space is connected and simply connected, with

\[
\pi_2(\mathscr O)=C^{11},\qquad
\pi_3(\mathscr O)=C^{86},\qquad
\pi_4(\mathscr O)=C^{383}.
\]

In words: the first eleven directions consist of nine missing quadratic native classes and two killed linear auxiliary classes, shifted into cone degree two. Nonzero higher groups continue indefinitely. Eleven is a rank, not a claimed new arithmetic prime or torsion order.

Equivalently, the homotopy fibre of the original Dold-Kan comparison has these groups one degree lower. The cofiber construction itself is not being confused with a quotient of physical states.

## 6. A separate, exact test for proposed endpoint repairs

The preceding derived normalization note constructs the fibre D of the fixed quotient-and-road endpoint comparison. Its zero-differential coefficient model gives

\[
D\simeq I_{\mathrm{or}}[1]\oplus
\bigoplus_{n\ge2}B^{\oplus2}[n].
\]

In words: the occurrence ideal remains in degree one, and there are two free coefficient copies in each higher degree. This decomposition concerns that endpoint comparison, not the Tor-map cone of Section 5.

Choose either free coordinate in degree two. Inclusion and projection give

\[
B[2]\xrightarrow{j}D\xrightarrow{p}B[2],
\qquad pj=1.
\]

In words: the endpoint defect contains an actual free coefficient retract, not just a free-looking homology class.

For every nonzero object K in the ungraded derived category of B-modules,

\[
K\otimes_B^L D\ne0,
\qquad
R\operatorname{Hom}_B(D,K)\ne0.
\]

In words: uniform derived tensoring and uniform coefficient duality cannot remove the defect while retaining a nonzero coefficient object. The first object has the retract K[2]; the second has the retract K[-2]. Exact functors preserve a retract identity. Applying the same operation to the two sides of the fixed endpoint arrow therefore cannot turn it into an equivalence [M1, M5].

More generally this holds for an exact functor on the ungraded coefficient category which does not annihilate B. In a category with internal grading, the required hypothesis is nonvanishing on at least one of the displayed internally shifted free summands. An operation selecting or regrading internal components is not silently covered by an ungraded argument.

This has a constructive consequence for the remaining search: an external orientation line, a nonzero perfect supported tensor factor, or the same nonzero derived coefficient dual on both sides is insufficient for this particular arrow. A different row-dependent correspondence, source diagram, or source-admitted graded operation must be specified and tested. This is not a no-go theorem for those different operations or for the constructions in the parallel branches.

## 7. Why the total-kernel equivalence is unaffected

The preceding result was

\[
B\otimes_U^L\mathcal K_U\simeq\mathcal K_B\simeq B.
\]

In words: the normalization and conductor rows cancel their matched defects after totalization. Applying that total kernel does not replace the algebra B by U in a subsequent conductor self-intersection.

The present map is instead \(C\otimes_U^L C\to C\otimes_B^L C\). Its comparison factors through the smooth six-occurrence algebra S. Its finite exterior image is therefore compatible with, and not contradicted by, the total-kernel equivalence.

All formulas are natural under spectator-ring changes and the label permutations preserving or exchanging the two triples. The checker verifies the six source dihedral permutations with the exterior signs. This does not set an unassigned physical reflection parity.

No new target chain map or endpoint 2-cell is claimed. The previously computed genuine generic Q-cycle and its eighteen connecting terms are not modified here. The whole normalization and punctured-overlap diagram remains necessary to identify the native physical operation.
