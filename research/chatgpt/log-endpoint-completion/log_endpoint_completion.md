## Outcome

The occurrence-weighted **carrier endpoint calculation is completed**. Both endpoint comparison homotopies are explicitly constructed, their relative mapping-cone map is checked, and the exceptional relative generator has a nonzero image. There is an exact restriction on the coefficient of the collapsed fibre: it must lie in the principal occurrence ideal generated by `X_D03 X_1 X_3`. This is an exhaustive statement for the specified collapse-and-endpoint problem, not only a failed trial of one homotopy.

This is **not a completion of the full physical comparison**. The new endpoint map lands in the short-boundary carrier, so its literal generic-Q projection is zero. Separately, normalized log blowdown carries the source's Morse identity and a nonzero generic *flag coefficient*, but that does not identify a map into the fixed supported-dual PC target. A local dual Koszul comparison is also constructed and preserves the central Tor class. Its assembly with the carrier map and the actual reciprocal/PC endpoint-Q object is not established.

These qualifications distinguish three explicit morphisms that cannot be interchanged: ordinary normalized-chain blowdown, a homotopy-commutative endpoint square, and an extraordinary/dual normal comparison. Neither the old coefficient-level order-six obstruction nor the two-element detector torsor is reassigned a physical meaning here.

## 1. Actual labelled complexes

Let the short hexagon diagonals be

\[
x_i=(i,i+2),\qquad i\in\mathbb Z/6,
\]

with vertex labels reduced modulo six, and let the long diagonals be `D03`, `D14`, and `D25`. In words: these are the nine diagonals of the labelled hexagon, with the three physical roads distinguished.

Let P be the poset of noncrossing sets of these diagonals, including the empty set, ordered by inclusion. It has 45 elements. Its normalized order complex has 45 vertices, 170 edges, 210 triangles, and 84 tetrahedra.

The local source log subdivision is the stellar subdivision at the two-ray face `{D03,x1}`. A new ray E replaces that face and its cofaces by the usual stellar faces. This is a combinatorial extension of the local source formula to the full labelled noncrossing complex, not an assertion about the complete ringed algebraic log scheme. It has 51 faces and normalized-chain counts 51, 194, 240, and 96.

For a subdivided face S, define

\[
p(S)=\begin{cases}(S\setminus\{E\})\cup\{D03,x_1\},&E\in S,\\S,&E\notin S.\end{cases}
\]

In words: replace the exceptional ray by its original centre. This is the source's normalized blowdown rule [S1]. It is order-preserving and takes every subdivided face to a legal hexagon face.

Work over the independent occurrence ring

\[
R=\mathbb Z[X_0,\ldots,X_5,X_{D03},X_{D14},X_{D25}].
\]

In words: no occurrence coordinate is invertible. Polynomial spectators may be adjoined. The following carrier calculation does not silently replace this ring by the full reciprocal/PC coefficient system with its stalk-specific normal inverses.

Set

\[
m_S=\prod_{a\in p(S)}X_a.
\]

In words: a source face has the squarefree occurrence label of its blowdown image. A target face uses its own label. In particular an exceptional ray has label `X_D03 X_1`, not an independent new occurrence variable.

For a strict flag of length n+1, with n at least one, the weighted boundary is

\[
\begin{aligned}
\partial[S_0,\ldots,S_n]
={}&\frac{m_{S_1}}{m_{S_0}}[S_1,\ldots,S_n]\\
&+\sum_{j=1}^{n}(-1)^j[S_0,\ldots,\widehat S_j,\ldots,S_n].
\end{aligned}
\]

In words: the first face removal uses the occurrence transition and the remaining removals use the simplicial signs. The displayed ratio is a polynomial monomial because the labels divide along each flag. Vertices have boundary zero: this is the unaugmented complex, not an unjustified augmentation sending all differently weighted vertices to one.

The normalized pushforward applies p to every face of a flag and sends a flag to zero if its image is degenerate. The checker verifies on **every flag**

\[
\partial^2=0,\qquad p_\#\partial=\partial p_\#.
\]

In words: both full normalized complexes and the entire occurrence-weighted blowdown map satisfy the chain identities. This strengthens the source's checks on the three distinguished chains. It does not promote a flag to a generator of a differently graded target complex.

## 2. The complete local Morse identity

Use the following labels:

\[
\begin{gathered}
o=\varnothing,\quad D=\{D03\},\quad a=\{x_1,x_3,x_5\},\quad c=\{D03,x_0,x_3\},\\
e_c=\{x_1,x_3\},\quad e_r=\{D03,x_3\},\quad h=\{E,x_3\},\\
b_+=\{E,x_1,x_3\},\qquad b_-=\{E,D03,x_3\}.
\end{gathered}
\]

In words: a is the positive physical endpoint, h is the central exceptional fibre point, the b terms are its two endpoint rays, and c is a road vertex. The symbols are precisely the marked local geometry used in the source counit.

The source's seven-term chain is

\[
\begin{aligned}
H={}&[o,e_c,a]-[o,e_c,b_+]+[o,h,b_+]-[o,h,b_-]\\
&+X_{D03}[D,e_r,b_-]-X_{D03}[D,e_r,c]+[o,D,b_-].
\end{aligned}
\]

In words: this is the explicit two-chain; no extra filling generator is adjoined.

The path and corrected roof are

\[
\begin{aligned}
\xi={}&X_1([e_c,b_+]-[e_c,a])\\
&+X_{D03}X_1([h,b_-]-[h,b_+])\\
&+X_{D03}([e_r,c]-[e_r,b_-]),\\
q_J={}&-[o,a]+[o,D]+X_{D03}[D,c].
\end{aligned}
\]

In words: the path has six terms and the roof includes the positive generic flag with coefficient one and its endpoint correction.

Direct calculation gives

\[
\partial H=q_J-X_3\xi,
\qquad
\partial\xi=X_{D03}X_0[c]-X_1X_5[a].
\]

In words: the generic roof and the occurrence-weighted lower path are compared by the actual source two-chain, and both endpoint weights are retained. The same equations hold after blowdown. Seven upstairs triangles become five nondegenerate downstairs triangles; six path terms become four. All six relabelled charts satisfy the corresponding identities exactly [S1]. This is verification of a family of local carrier identities, not a substitute for global coefficient-Cech descent.

## 3. Why the exceptional interval needs endpoint comparison cells

The nontrivial fibre over

\[
m=\{D03,x_1,x_3\}
\]

has vertices h, b-plus, and b-minus and two edges from h to those vertices. In words: this is a V-shaped tree. Its relative class is

\[
\beta=[h,b_-]-[h,b_+],
\qquad
\partial\beta=[b_-]-[b_+].
\]

In words: the difference of its two edges is a cycle only after its two endpoint rays are made relative.

Ordinary normalized blowdown has

\[
p_\#\beta=0,\qquad p(b_+)=p(b_-)=m.
\]

In words: both edges collapse and both endpoints become the same labelled road vertex. Moreover m is neither physical endpoint. Thus ordinary blowdown alone is not a map from this interval pair to the physical pair with endpoints `v+` and `v-`.

This does **not** prove that every endpoint-corrected or extraordinary comparison vanishes. It identifies exactly why a homotopy-commutative endpoint square is required. A pair map through the collapsed pair `(point,point)` is zero, but endpoint comparison homotopies in the larger target can contribute a nonzero relative chain. We construct those next.

## 4. Construct both weighted endpoint homotopies

Put

\[
v_+=\{x_1,x_3,x_5\},\qquad v_-=\{x_0,x_2,x_4\},\qquad d=\{D03,x_0,x_4\}.
\]

In words: the two endpoints are the source's odd and even triangulations. The vertex d and the earlier c lie on the labelled D03 corridor [S2].

For adjacent triangulations s and t with their common two-diagonal face e, define

\[
\ell(s,t)=m_e([e,t]-[e,s]).
\]

In words: take the oriented barycentric edge path with its forced occurrence multiplier. Its boundary is

\[
\partial\ell(s,t)=m_t[t]-m_s[s].
\]

In words: the intermediate edge vertex cancels, leaving the correct weighted endpoint values.

The two comparison homotopies are

\[
\begin{aligned}
h_+&=\ell(v_+,m),\\
h_-&=\ell(v_-,d)+\ell(d,c)+\ell(c,m).
\end{aligned}
\]

In words: use the source's two labelled half-corridors, now split at the actual blowdown point m. The negative path is the source negative half-corridor followed by the reverse of the final segment of the positive half-corridor. No edge outside the short-boundary carrier is added.

Define

\[
\mu=m_m=X_{D03}X_1X_3,\qquad
w_+=X_1X_3X_5,\qquad w_-=X_0X_2X_4.
\]

In words: these are the midpoint and two endpoint occurrence labels. Then

\[
\partial h_+=\mu[m]-w_+[v_+],\qquad
\partial h_-=\mu[m]-w_-[v_-].
\]

In words: both endpoint comparison equations close over the unlocalized occurrence ring.

### The actual relative mapping-cone map

Let A be the two endpoint rays of the exceptional fibre F. Let V be the two physical endpoints in K. Let i and j denote their inclusions. Define g on the fibre chains by sending all three vertices to `mu[m]` and both edges to zero. Define f on A by the weighted endpoint values `w+[v+]` and `w-[v-]`. The two h terms give

\[
\partial h+h\partial=gi-jf.
\]

In words: the endpoint square commutes by specified chain homotopies, rather than by deleting its discrepancy.

With the homological cone differential

\[
d(z,a)=(dz+ia,-da),
\]

define

\[
\Psi:\operatorname{Cone}(i)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Cone}(j),
\qquad \Psi(z,a)=(gz+ha,fa).
\]

In words: the comparison homotopies enter the off-diagonal component of the cone map. The checker verifies its chain equation on all seven source cone generators.

The relative generator is represented by `(beta,b+ - b-)`. Its image has chain component

\[
\Gamma=h_+-h_-,\qquad
\partial\Gamma=w_-[v_-]-w_+[v_+].
\]

In words: the collapsed interval acquires the actual weighted road corridor through its two endpoint comparisons. Its endpoint boundary is nonzero. Therefore its relative homology class is nonzero; indeed the resulting map from the source free relative line is injective over R, since its nonzero endpoint vector cannot be annihilated in this domain.

This constructs the **carrier-level Tor-class attachment and both carrier endpoint homotopies**. It does not identify that relative line with every algebraic excess Tor module or identify these homotopies with the reciprocal/PC endpoint connector cells. Those are comparisons of different coefficient and variance data.

## 5. Classify all permissible midpoint coefficients

The factor mu is forced for the collapse specified above. It is not inserted merely to make the displayed paths work.

Suppose the same collapsed fibre map sends each vertex to `k[m]`, and a polynomial endpoint map has values `a+[v+]` and `a-[v-]`. Any endpoint homotopies in the complete occurrence-weighted target must satisfy

\[
\partial h_\pm=k[m]-a_\pm[v_\pm].
\]

In words: this is the endpoint condition for an arbitrary replacement, not only one supported on our chosen paths.

There is an R-linear map from degree-zero chains to the fraction field of R,

\[
\varepsilon([S])=m_S^{-1},
\]

which annihilates every weighted boundary. In words: reciprocal weights are used only as a mathematical detector of an obstruction. They are not admitted as source coefficients or used to construct a map.

Applying it forces

\[
a_+=k\frac{w_+}{\mu},\qquad
 a_-=k\frac{w_-}{\mu}.
\]

In words: both endpoint coefficients are uniquely determined by k. The monomials mu and w-minus are relatively prime. Consequently polynomial endpoint coefficients exist only if mu divides k. Conversely, if `k=mu b`, multiply f, g, and h in Section 4 by b. This constructs an admissible square. Let L denote the set of k for which these two polynomial endpoint homotopies exist. The two implications give

\[
L=(X_{D03}X_1X_3).
\]

In words: the unit-coefficient collapsed-fibre square has no polynomial lift. The obstruction already occurs in the occurrence lattice, separately from the normal principal-part obstruction in the earlier 215-generator target calculation.

The all-polynomial necessity follows coefficientwise or from divisibility in the polynomial unique-factorization domain. It cannot be evaded by choosing more elaborate carrier homotopies. We do **not** conclude that the full physical comparison is impossible: it uses normal/conormal lines and mixed variance, whereas this test fixes the ordinary collapsed-fibre coefficient map.

## 6. What the generic-Q test actually says

All terms in Gamma lie in the actual short-boundary carrier. Let pi-B denote projection to its ordinary relative bar-chain quotient. Then

\[
\pi_B(\Gamma)=0.
\]

In words: the endpoint repair alone does not supply the nonzero generic leg. The short-boundary support statement is literal, not an assertion after forgetting coefficients.

The separate corrected roof q-J has a nonzero generic flag coefficient. Yet in the **ordinary relative bar complex** its class satisfies

\[
q_J=\partial H\pmod{C_*(NB_{\rm short};R)}.
\]

In words: xi is short-boundary supported, so the Morse identity makes q-J exact in that particular quotient. A nonzero coefficient on a flag is not the same as a nonzero homology class.

Neither statement is a calculation of the source's physical based-Q restriction in its mixed-variance Hom complex. The actual seven-generator Q object, its grading, and its supported dual are not identified with this relative bar complex by the formulas above. A source-defined map between them must be supplied before transferring either a vanishing claim or a unit claim.

This is why the successful carrier endpoint construction does not, by itself, finish the requested physical kernel.

## 7. The relative interval trace exists, but is not ordinary blowdown

On the exceptional fibre the relative interval complex is

\[
J=[R^2\xrightarrow{(-1,-1)}R],
\]

in homological degrees one and zero. Its positive relative generator is `(-1,1)`. Define

\[
\tau:J\longrightarrow R[1],\qquad \tau_1=(0,1),\quad\tau_0=0.
\]

In words: evaluate the oriented relative interval class, not the ordinary image of its collapsed edges. This sends the relative generator to one.

An explicit section sends one to `(-1,1)` and a contraction sends the degree-zero centre to `(-1,0)`. They give

\[
dH+Hd=1-i\tau,\qquad\tau i=1.
\]

In words: the trace is an integral homotopy equivalence in the relative interval model. No averaging or inversion is needed.

Every normalized trace has row `(a,a+1)`; any two differ by a chain homotopy through the degree-zero centre. Reflection, after the single orientation compensation, acts by minus the endpoint swap in degree one and minus one in degree zero. The trace's reflection discrepancy equals the interval boundary row and therefore has an explicit comparison homotopy. This supplies local coherent orientation, not a value of the physical endpoint parity. The orientation used here is that of the displayed path from the positive physical endpoint toward the negative endpoint. A comparison with any independently ordered ray/Thom suspension frame must retain the resulting determinant sign; it is not inferred merely from a trace coefficient of one.

The source log-Tor checker [S3] assigns an ordinary exceptional-edge image of zero and separately assigns a descended relative value of one. The relative trace above explains how one can obtain a genuine primitive value in a different, correctly shifted operation. Those two scalar assignments alone do not construct its comparison with the whole target. The source's generic log-trace calculation [S4] fixes a relative logarithmic orientation; it does not supply the missing spatial attachment.

## 8. The exact local ordinary/dual normal comparison

A useful second local component is completely explicit. On a chart with

\[
u=\lambda t,\qquad B=R[\lambda,t],
\]

let K(s) be the homological complex `[B --s--> B]` in degrees one and zero. In words: this is the usual two-term resolution for a framed normal equation.

The ordinary comparison and its oriented dual have components, written in degrees zero and one,

\[
f:K(\lambda t)\longrightarrow K(t),\qquad(f_0,f_1)=(1,\lambda),
\]

\[
g:K(t)\longrightarrow K(\lambda t),\qquad(g_0,g_1)=(\lambda,1).
\]

In words: reversing variance moves the factor from the normal class to the ordinary coefficient. The second map is obtained by dualizing the first and using the framed Koszul duality identification; it is not a modification of the first map in the same direction. The matrices themselves verify the claim directly.

They obey

\[
fg=\lambda\operatorname{id},\qquad gf=\lambda\operatorname{id}.
\]

In words: over the generic locus where lambda is invertible, they differ from inverse equivalences by this specified factor.

After derived specialization at the closed locus `lambda=t=0`, the free complexes have zero differentials, and

\[
f_1=0,\qquad g_1=1.
\]

In words: the ordinary comparison kills the central degree-one class; the dual comparison preserves it. The latter has a first-order generic coefficient and an oriented closed normal coefficient of one. This is a proved local matrix calculation over the entire polynomial ring.

If `f0=1` is fixed, its chain equation forces `f1=lambda`; no alternative polynomial chain map with the same coefficient normalization repairs that central effect. Likewise, fixing `g0=lambda` forces `g1=1`. These are uniqueness statements for the specified two-term complexes because t and lambda-t are non-zero-divisors.

The source supplies a generic normal relation of the form `u=Xt` [S4]. This section does **not** identify lambda with all of the independent occurrence and Rees coordinates in the physical diagram, nor identify ordinary Koszul Tor with the full excess Tor packet without its source base-change square. The result supplies an explicit local dual component; it does not finish the global mixed-variance comparison.
