# Branch A: relative interval trace and the nonsplit occurrence-35 extension

## Result and scope

The closed reciprocal-basepoint evaluation from the preceding calculation can be composed with the actual quotient by both endpoint packets of the opposite edge. The resulting relative target is the complete two-term occurrence complex, not a scalar line.

On the two established excess classes the relative projection is

\[
q_*=-\beta I_2.
\]

In words: it preserves two independent classes at nonzero regulator. The factor of beta remains part of the homogeneous comparison.

Projection from the resulting occurrence complex to its degree-three homology has matrix

\[
(1\quad 1).
\]

In words: that projection identifies the two excess classes and kills their primitive relation-only difference.

The occurrence complex itself has a nonzero, primitive two-extension connecting its two homology modules. This is proved over the full polynomial normalization ring. It cannot be replaced in the derived category by the direct sum of those modules.

A separate source-typing test shows that directly applying the Laurent coefficient localization of ledger Entry 97 to this normalization ring gives the zero ring. This does not invalidate Entry 97 over its independent coefficient ring. It excludes that particular base-change interpretation of the proposed physical comparison.

The constructed map is a relative coefficient projection. It has not been identified with the physical tangential Pochhammer--Cousin trace, the full spatial Gysin correspondence, or the physical conductor--Morse Delta_J.

## 5. The occurrence complex is not a split sum of its homology

This statement holds over the full polynomial ring R, not just one graded slice.

The two homology modules of O, with the common orientation factor understood, are

\[
H_3(\mathcal O)=R/(z),\qquad
H_4(\mathcal O)=I_-\otimes\mathcal L_z.
\]

The annihilator identity Ann_R(z)=I_- follows from the two-sheet normal form. Multiplication by z is injective on the positive polynomial sheet; its kernel consists exactly of the negative-sheet ideal.

The truncation comparison is represented by the exact two-extension

\[
0\longrightarrow I_-\otimes\mathcal L_z
\longrightarrow R\otimes\mathcal L_z
\xrightarrow{-z}R
\longrightarrow R/(z)
\longrightarrow0.
\]

In words: the remaining occurrence factor connects the scalar quotient and its upper annihilator module. The occurrence line makes the differential homogeneous.

### Exact computation of its extension class

Use the displayed two projective middle modules for dimension shifting:

\[
\operatorname{Ext}_R^2(R/(z),I_-\otimes\mathcal L_z)
\cong
\operatorname{End}_R(I_-)
\big/
\operatorname{im}\operatorname{Hom}_R(R,I_-).
\]

The internal occurrence line is retained on both copies in this calculation.

Every R-linear endomorphism of I_- is linear over the negative sheet

\[
R_-=A[X_{02},X_{04},X_{24}].
\]

Let f_i be the image of X_i for a negative-sheet generator. The relations X_j f_i=X_i f_j and coprimality of distinct polynomial variables imply f_i=X_i a for a common a in R_-. Conversely every multiplication by a is an endomorphism. Therefore

\[
\operatorname{End}_R(I_-)=R_-,\qquad
\operatorname{im}\operatorname{Hom}_R(R,I_-)=I_-.
\]

It follows that

\[
\operatorname{Ext}_R^2(R/(z),I_-\otimes\mathcal L_z)
\cong A,\qquad [\mathcal E_z]=1.
\]

In words: the actual occurrence two-extension is the primitive nonzero class, in its retained occurrence frame.

A concrete free resolution of R/(z) begins with ranks 1,1,3,12. The maps are multiplication by -z, then the three negative variables, followed by their three same-sheet Koszul and nine mixed annihilator relations. The extension cocycle sends each of the three degree-two generators to the corresponding X_i e1. The checker constructs that resolution segment and verifies all twelve cocycle equations.

Nonvanishing is also immediate from a hypothetical boundary: it would require b in I_- with X_i b=X_i for all three negative variables. Restriction to the negative polynomial sheet gives b=1, contradicting b in I_-. This proof covers arbitrary polynomial coefficients; it is not a finite-degree search.

The nonzero truncation class excludes a derived splitting

\[
\mathcal O\simeq(R/(z))[3]\oplus(I_-\otimes\mathcal L_z)[4].
\]

In words: retaining both homology modules as an unrelated direct sum still discards an actual two-extension. This conclusion uses the canonical truncation triangle, not an identification with the physical Delta_J.

Ledger Entry 97 uses the independent ring in which x0,x1,x3,x4 and both native Pochhammer coefficients are inverted. Its labels include X02 and X35. In the present normalization ring,

\[

\[

\]

In words: simultaneously inverting those mixed-sheet factors makes one equal zero. The same conclusion holds after inserting the graph's formal unit factors. This is the universal property of ring localization; the multiplicative set contains zero.

A spatial or supported correspondence must specify the different source/target coefficient actions; a formal cancellation of mixed-sheet denominators is not such a map.
