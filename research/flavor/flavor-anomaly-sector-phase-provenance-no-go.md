# Anomaly-sector phase-provenance no-go: WP1125

## Question

Can anomaly-sector coefficients provide \(H_6\) phase-observable provenance?

## DPC resolution

- **Conjecture:** the seven anomaly-sector coefficients determine a sourced
  phase character that selects an \(H_6\) representative and six event
  channels.
- **Rivals:** raw anomaly coefficients as phases; normalized anomaly
  character; boundary symmetry observable; no anomaly phase provenance.
- **Risky consequences:** produce unit-modulus phases, a
  selected-packet-preserving map onto six event channels, and a sourced
  \(H_6\) representative.
- **Falsification attempt:** the source has seven integer inflow coefficients
  \((-7,-2,-1,-1,1,2,3)\) with sum \(-5\), only three real unit magnitudes,
  no normalization map, no phase character, no six-channel bijection, and no
  \(H_6\) representative.
- **Residual:** a future boundary character could convert anomaly sectors into
  phases and event channels.
- **Disposition:** reject anomaly-sector phase provenance for the current
  source.

## Boundary

Anomaly integers are inflow charges. They may be exponentiated, normalized, or
permuted only after the source supplies a character map. Without that map,
seven channels do not become six physical16 event indices and 36 Hadamard
entries remain absent.
