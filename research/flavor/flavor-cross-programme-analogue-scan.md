## 7. Non-transferable patterns

The scan found repeated hostile patterns that must not be imported into
Flavor:

- equal matrices or support classes treated as common-object identity;
- coherent phase bookkeeping treated as zero confinement or unitarity;
- finite cutoff invertibility treated as completed bounded invertibility;
- source-Gram injectivity treated as target surjectivity;
- formal instrument completions treated as physical state-update rules;
- pairwise calibration connectors treated as one flat connection;
- bare support rank treated as coefficient-bearing transport rank;
- perfect combinatorial matching treated as typed closure without coefficient
  and holonomy checks;
- primitive projection or gcd data treated as a complete integral boundary
  lift.
