Work package: WP933

## Tested structure

WP932 is tested against Aspect's seven-axis compiler, its namespaced
capability registry, and Strominger's added domain axis.  The construction is
split into distinct events rather than assigned one ambiguous status.

The formal Boolean transform has a bright packet, matched incidence, native
integer coefficients, and an authorized mathematical action.  It is faithful
on the labelled route quotient.

The proposed Spin7 multiplet deletion is a different event.  Its action is
missing, its path is unknown, and preservation of the admitted gauge and
boundary domain is unknown.  The corresponding calibrated threshold
experiment is also missing.  Descent from labelled routes to `physical16` is
unproved.

Finally, the ordering functional is independent of both reconstruction and
deletion.  It is missing.  Two counterfactuals enforce this separation:

- authorizing the deletion action does not create an ordering functional;
- postulating an ordering formula does not authorize the deletion action.

## Verdict

Aspect's tester classifies WP932 as deferred, not rejected.  The formal
reconstruction theorem survives.  The physical promotion fails independently
at source action, domain preservation, experiment, quotient descent, and
ordering authority.

This also refines the ledger comparison with WP753.  A source effective
potential would address the ordering axis only.  It would not by itself prove
that all deletion interventions preserve the source domain or define a
physical threshold instrument.
