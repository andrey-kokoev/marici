# Fixed-point and relevant-deformation separation

## Question

Could completing WP465's coupled gauge-Yukawa beta functions finish the stated
selection objective by fixing `g_F f/v`?

This packet grants the strongest favorable premise: suppose the complete
dimensionless gauge, messenger-Yukawa, and scalar-quartic system has a unique,
perturbatively controlled fixed point. It asks what that premise can actually
select.

## Dimensionless fixed point versus physical clock

WP447 fixes the normalized irreducible vacuum shape and

\[
f^2=6\mu^2.
\]

A dimensionless fixed point may fix `g_F` and dimensionless mass ratios such as
`M_U/mu` and `M_D/mu`. It does not fix the relevant deformation `mu` relative
to the observed electroweak clock `v`.

Let all dimensionless couplings and the two messenger ratios be held fixed.
For any positive dilation `s`, take

\[
\mu\longmapsto s\mu,\qquad
M_U\longmapsto sM_U,\qquad
M_D\longmapsto sM_D.
\]

The fixed-point coordinates and all flavor-sector threshold ratios remain
unchanged. Yet

\[
{g_Ff\over v}=\sqrt6,g_F{\mu\over v}
\longmapsto s\sqrt6,g_F{\mu\over v}.
\]

The exact hostile pair `s=1,2` therefore has the same complete dimensionless
fixed-point record and different physical clock ratios.

## Consequences

This kernel occurs before detector response. A full gauge-Yukawa fixed-point
calculation remains valuable because it may select `g_F`, but it cannot select
`g_F f/v` unless it also supplies a source-authorized relevant trajectory or a
portal that locks the flavor and electroweak scales.

The distinction also prevents a false composition with WP448–WP449. Their
poles and widths scale with `mu`; fixed dimensionless pole ratios and width
fractions do not determine the masses in detector units.

## Classification

- Quotient: the dilation pair consists of physically inequivalent points, not
  weak-basis presentations of one point.
- Fixed-point operation: potentially selects dimensionless couplings.
- Target operation: does not select `g_F f/v`.
- Rigidification: none.
- Smallest exact falsifier: `s=1` and `s=2` preserve every dimensionless fixed
  point and threshold ratio but double `g_F f/v`.
- Remaining constructor: a source-derived common-clock interface, for example
  a portal whose coefficient and vacuum branch are fixed independently and
  whose coupled electroweak–flavor vacuum predicts `mu/v`.
