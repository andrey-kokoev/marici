\[

## Polarized identity

For arbitrary traceless \(3\times3\) matrices, polarized Cayley–Hamilton gives

\[
4\operatorname{Tr}(A^2B^2)+2\operatorname{Tr}(ABAB)
=\operatorname{Tr}(A^2)\operatorname{Tr}(B^2)
+2\operatorname{Tr}(AB)^2.
\]

After summing port labels,

\[
4C+2D=I_{\rm rad}+2I_{\rm gram},
\qquad
C-D=I_{\rm comm}.
\]

Therefore

\[
C={I_{\rm rad}+2I_{\rm gram}+2I_{\rm comm}\over6},
\qquad
D={I_{\rm rad}+2I_{\rm gram}-4I_{\rm comm}\over6}.
\]

sector once WP495's Gram-square coupling is included.
