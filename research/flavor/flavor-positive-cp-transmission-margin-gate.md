observed small scale?

## DPC resolution

  coefficients, and a source-dynamical positive \(T\) margin before fitting.
- **Bold conjecture:** scale, norm, cyclic incidence, maximal CP, or
  minimal Landau structure fixes a positive margin.
- **Named rivals:** scale-free portal ray; unit-orthogonal portal vectors;
  fully cyclic fixed-norm incidence; maximal-CP invariant; minimal

  misses cyclic support, permits exact cancellation, selects a false orbit,
  or imports a small fitted coefficient.
- **Exact residual:** independently derived small dimensionless source
  ratio, oriented-volume law or margin, radiative closure, and calibrated
  readout/preparation instrument.
- **Disposition:** reject all tested margin constructors; select the
  independent small-source-ratio rival.
