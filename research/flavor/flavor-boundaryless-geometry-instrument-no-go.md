# Boundaryless geometry removes the instrument with the modulus: WP941

## Question

Can a smooth circle or freely acting compactification eliminate WP940's
boundary coefficient while preserving the same flavor selector and calibrated
two-endpoint experiment?

## Two source geometries

Let `I` be the orbifold interval.  It has two fixed boundary components,
supports boundary gauge-kinetic operators `(O_0,O_L)`, and admits the
boundary-to-boundary response used by WP769 and WP770.  Endpoint exchange
reduces the coefficient space to the common line generated by `(1,1)`.

Let `S1` be a smooth boundaryless circle.  It has no codimension-one fixed
locus, hence no endpoint-supported gauge-kinetic operator of the interval
type.  A Wilson loop can provide a relational holonomy observable, but there
are no labelled endpoint current ports and no opposite-boundary transfer
function.

Thus the relevant object counts are

\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
&\text{exchange-even boundary coefficients}&\text{endpoint ports}\\
I&1&2\\
S^1&0&0.
\end{array}
\]

The boundaryless geometry kills the `tau` modulus and the WP770 instrument
together.  It does not select `tau = 0` inside the interval theory; it changes
the admitted state domain and physical groupoid.

## No experiment-preserving comparison

An experiment-preserving comparison would have to map both labelled interval
ports into source-defined circle ports while retaining their independent
current injections and opposite-port response.  No such ports exist in the
boundaryless source object.  Choosing two marked points on the circle repairs
the count only by adding a new relational reference structure.  Those marks
permit localized operators again unless an additional source law forbids
them.

This is a comparison of source geometries and observation functors.  It does
not treat categorical composition as temporal or causal evolution.

## Holonomy branch

A Wilson-line potential can conditionally select a dimensionless angle, as
WP789 establishes.  That is a different selector over a different lens
family.  It retains a radius/clock fiber and does not reconstruct the
interval's boundary normalization or two-port response.  WP745 further shows
that holonomies preserving the declared physical group cannot uniquely fix
the required nonzero contrast.

## Classification

Boundaryless compactification is a source-domain replacement, not a selector
on the original admissible interval family.  It removes both the nuisance
coefficient and its physical instrument.  Adding marked reference ports
defines a new relational experiment and must state its stabilizer groupoid.

The smallest exact falsifier of experiment preservation is the port-rank
drop `2 -> 0`.  The remaining progressive route is a boundaryful UV
constructor that fixes the even coefficient, or a fully rederived
boundaryless flavor model with its own `physical16` descent and calibrated
holonomy instrument.  Results from the two experiments cannot be combined by
coordinate resemblance alone.
