# Minimal source-packet authority bundle: WP1106

## Question

What minimal authority bundle must a successful single source packet supply?

## Frozen six-output interface

WP1090--WP1105 close seventeen partial shortcuts. The remaining source packet
must supply all six outputs below, with explicit provenance and no target
fitting:

1. **Absolute UV boundary lift.** The seven-channel Chern–Simons lift,
   counterterm convention, endpoint orientation, and WP1070 coset
   realization.
2. **Integer clock lift.** Integer \(n\), sign \(\sigma\), unit orbit
   \(B/A=6n^2\), and source normalization.
3. **Oriented second-stage frame.** Oriented adjoint ray, ordered \(B\)
   lines, cyclic seed, and history dilation without destroying the localized
   quartet.
4. **Independent \(\rho\).** A weight-\((-3)\) section with line bundle,
   descent law, temporal scope, and comparison node.
5. **Production and gain.** A six-row branch-to-physical16 coupling matrix,
   event reweighting, channel selection, and gain \(3/2\).
6. **Physical16 descent.** Source-authorized quotient descent, comparison
   node, normalization, and frozen readout provenance.

No admitted packet currently supplies any of these six outputs. The closed
shortcuts include reciprocal determinant, source-natural scalars, Wilson
phase/lattice/denominator/integer traces, internal epsilon or bifundamental
pairings, history isometry, finite-scheme ports, and contact counterterms.

## Classification

Conditional gate. This freezes the interface for a positive source packet; it
is not itself a construction. The remaining gate is to obtain one packet
supplying all six outputs.
