# Declared Spin(5) Scalars Connect Completion B but Do Not Prove Full Mass Rank

## Recovered scalar typing

hypercharge. The shared portal Yukawa uses

\[
4_{-1/2}\otimes5_{+1}\otimes\Phi_4.
\]

Since (4\otimes5\otimes4) contains a Spin(5) singlet, hypercharge neutrality
fixes

\[
\Phi_4=4_{-1/2},
\qquad \Phi_4^*=4_{+1/2}.
\]

The two ordered breaking scalars are (U_5,V_5\sim5_0).

Every Completion-B multiplet is incident to at least one admitted Yukawa

## Why the graph is not a mass-rank theorem

The incidence graph forgets:

- Clebsch--Gordan tensor symmetry for identical Weyl fields;
- the component direction of the spinor-Higgs vacuum;
- correlations among matrix entries generated by one invariant;
- family indices and Yukawa textures;
- gauge Goldstone and unbroken-subgroup decompositions.

A graph matching or complete vertex coverage is therefore only a necessary
typing test. Algebraic span is not executable mass control. The actual
component mass matrix must be constructed from explicit Spin(5) gamma
intertwiners and evaluated on the declared vacuum before generic rank can be
claimed.
