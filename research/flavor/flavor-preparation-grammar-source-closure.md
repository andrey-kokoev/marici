# WP970 — Flavor preparation-grammar source closure

Owner: `marici.Figueiredo`.

## Question

Does any currently declared flavor source authorize the repeated slot/reset
grammar required to transport the WP968 feature-covariance certificate?

## Claim boundary

A one-use stochastic kernel is not a repeated-use constructor. To authorize the
moment instrument, a source package must jointly provide:

1. a physical preparation substrate;
2. a one-use kernel on the admitted flavor domain;
3. a composition or labelled-slot operation;
4. an apparatus/reset state and its return law;
5. a source-derived joint law for repeated uses;
6. descent to the faithful flavor quotient;
7. calibration of the full feature packet ((X,X^2));
8. a domain-stable covariance or mixed-coherence certificate.

## Source audit

- FDM-1 explicitly leaves the empirical flavon bath open.
- FDM-2 explicitly leaves the physical unbiased orientation source open and
  supplies only a conservative per-use drift total.
- The OU packet explicitly classifies its microscopic bath and
  fluctuation-dissipation scale as unvalidated.
- WP273 requires the reset/degradation budget from one source rather than
  supplying it.
- WP76 is a typing theorem: absent reset data are untyped, not zero.
- Standard Model RG supplies quotient-descending transport but neither route
  creation nor apparatus reset.

No currently admitted physical source passes the full gate.

## Domain, quotient, instrument, and falsifiers

- State domain: labelled repeated preparations of the FDM-2 route attribute, or
  any successor physical flavor-domain preparation.
- Faithful quotient: `physical16`; the sign route is the CP-conjugate
  relational coordinate already typed there.
- Probe family: the full feature packet required by WP968, not sign marginals
  alone.
- Contextual partition: repeated constructors are equivalent only when their
  joint feature laws agree for every admitted finite labelled packet.
- Classification: negative source-authority census; neither selector nor
  rigidifier.
- Smallest falsifier: fresh versus shared two-use FDM-2 compilations.
- Remaining instrument gate: an empirically admitted flavor apparatus whose
  reset mechanism derives the labelled joint law and its stable feature
  covariance.

## Disposition

Cross-context covariance transport closes negative for the currently declared
flavor source class. WP968 remains a pointwise conditional readout theorem.
The branch reopens only with a new physical preparation/reset package that
defines repeated-use composition and predicts its joint feature law before
using the moment data.
