# Portal-to-sector dilation gate: WP1178

## Question

Can a portal-to-sector dilation produce the 23-dimensional boundary ensemble?

## DPC resolution

- **Conjecture:** a portal-to-sector dilation can produce the dimension-trace
  state from the dark-state attractor.
- **Rivals:** input-erasing CPTP replacement channel; input-dependent portal
  dynamics; sourced threshold intertwiner; future UV state.
- **Risky consequences:** the replacement channel has 69 Kraus operators, is
  CPTP, and maps the dark projector to the required state, while erasing all
  portal information.
- **Falsification attempt:** the channel exists mathematically, but it is
  independent of the portal state and has no source-derived dynamics.
- **Residual:** an input-dependent portal-to-sector channel or independent UV
  boundary state remains open.
- **Disposition:** accept conditional replacement-channel existence; reject
  sourced portal dilation.
