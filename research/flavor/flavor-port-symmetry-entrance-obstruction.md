# Port-symmetry versus messenger-entrance obstruction

## Minimal executable chain

WP482 tests whether a source symmetry can enforce WP479's equal orthogonal
messenger Gram in the one-stage WP435 chain

\[
\bar QH U_R,
\qquad \bar U_LX_i u_R,
\qquad \bar U_LMU_R.
\]

The Standard Model quarks and Higgs are singlets under the new adjoint-label
symmetry. The `X_i` form the irreducible oriented real triplet used by WP447.

## Irreducible triplet result

The exact vector representation has no invariant vector:

\[
\operatorname{Hom}_{SO(3)}(1,3)=0.
\]

Its symmetric matrix commutant is one-dimensional, so an invariant positive
pairing is necessarily

\[
W=\kappa I_3.
\]

This is exactly the desired Gram law, but it forbids the entrance
`Qbar H U_R` when `U_R` is a triplet. If `U_R` instead contains a singlet so
that the entrance exists, the flavor exit requires a triplet messenger
component. A symmetry-preserving mass cannot mix the singlet and triplet
irreducible components. Thus no one-stage chain connects both vertices.

## Permutation alternative

The three-dimensional permutation representation decomposes as `1+2`. It has
the invariant entrance vector

\[
(1,1,1)^T.
\]

However, its symmetric commutant is two-dimensional:

\[
W=aI_3+b\mathbf1\mathbf1^T.
\]

The symmetry therefore does not enforce isotropy. Moreover, the invariant
entrance alone has Gram `11^T`, which is rank one and sees only the singlet
port direction. Setting `b=0` would be another unsupported coefficient choice.

## Relational repair boundary

The exact irreducible symmetry enforces the desired Gram but forbids executable
entrance. The reducible symmetry permits entrance but leaves a nonisotropic
Gram modulus. This is a source-interface obstruction, not a detector failure.

A repair needs a new dynamical connector or prepared port vector. Such a field
creates a relational reference experiment and changes the stabilizer groupoid;
it does not reveal an absolute orientation of the original triplet. Its action,
vacuum, fluctuations, and coefficients must be included before deriving the
portal, fixed ray, residues, or widths.

WP482 is neither selector nor rigidifier. Its smallest exact falsifier is the
joint fact that the irreducible triplet has zero invariant-vector dimension and
one-dimensional symmetric commutant, whereas the permutation triplet has
dimensions one and two respectively.
