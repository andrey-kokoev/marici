# Radial-quartic RG closure

## Closure test

WP489's four canonical radial variables are the physical flavor norm \(F\),
the electroweak radius \(H\), the connector norm \(R\), and \(\sigma\). Its
positive squares contain the mixed quartics

\[
F^2\sigma^2,
\qquad H^2\sigma^2,
\qquad R^2\sigma^2,
\]

but no direct \(F^2H^2\), \(F^2R^2\), or \(H^2R^2\) coupling.

## Exact one-loop support obstruction

A one-loop bubble using two distinct leaf–\(\sigma\) vertices and two internal
\(\sigma\) propagators produces the corresponding leaf–leaf quartic
counterterm. The relevant products of WP489 coefficients are

\[
12\rho\eta a y^2,
\qquad24\rho\lambda_S b y^2,
\qquad2\eta\lambda_Sab.
\]

They are nonzero everywhere on the admitted positive coefficient domain.
Therefore all three missing portals are generated. This claim concerns exact
counterterm support; it does not assert scheme-dependent overall loop factors.

## Minimal radial completion

The minimally support-closed radial basis contains four self quartics and all
six pairwise portals, ten invariants in total. Their coefficients must run
independently. The sum-of-squares relations may still be imposed as boundary
data, but cannot be treated as an RG-invariant surface without a separate
proof.

This is a source-truncation obstruction, not a numerical fixed-point result.
After adding the three radial portals, closure must still be checked for the
nonradial matrix/tensor invariants before a complete beta vector field exists.
