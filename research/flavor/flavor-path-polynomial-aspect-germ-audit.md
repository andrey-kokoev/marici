# Path-Polynomial Audit under Aspect's Germ Tester

## Question

Does the source-closed completion

\[
Y(a)=aI+F+F^2,
\qquad F^3=0,
\]

turn the Kirchhoff-selected chiral word into an authorized selector on the
faithful flavor quotient?

## Claim boundary

The declared carrier is the marked length-three path algebra generated by the
oriented partial isometry (F).  Its native arity is three: the identity,
one-step, and two-step path occurrences must remain labelled until their sum
is formed.  Under a weak-basis transformation (F\mapsto U_LFU_R^\dagger)
and (I\mapsto U_LIU_R^\dagger), the whole matrix transforms covariantly, so
the singular spectrum and the two-sector weak-basis invariants descend.

For (a=x+iy), the elementary symmetric polynomials of (YY^\dagger) are

\[
e_1=3(x^2+y^2)+\frac54,
\]

\[
e_2=3(x^2+y^2)^2+\frac54(x^2+y^2)-\frac{x}{2}+\frac14,
\qquad
e_3=(x^2+y^2)^3.
\]

These are full-orbit invariants, not presentation coordinates.  Thus the
proposal passes the covariance and quotient-fiber parts of the tester
conditional on the marked path constructor.

It fails the completion-authority gate.  The same source algebra admits

\[
Y_c(a)=aI+F+cF^2.
\]

Neither nilpotence, path grading, orientation, nor the Kirchhoff normalization
of the one-step word fixes (c=1).  At (a=1), varying real (c) through one
changes both (e_1) and (e_2) at first order.  Hence this is not a
weak-basis motion or a harmless chart change: it moves the physical orbit.
Calling (I+F+F^2) the complete geometric path sum therefore inserts the
desired coefficient unless a separate source operation derives that sum.

The physical-instrument gate also remains open.  WP859 supplies a lossless
boundary difference port for the selected two-component dark ray, but no
calibrated flavor experiment measures the three path occurrences coherently
in one common frame or reconstructs the resulting `physical16` point.

## Smallest exact falsifier

The one-parameter completion (F^2\mapsto cF^2) is the smallest hostile.  It
preserves the marked path, nilpotence, orientation, and all source data used to
derive (F), while changing the faithful quotient invariants.  Therefore the
unit two-step coefficient is a free source modulus in the currently admitted
grammar.

## Disposition

Negative but useful.  The polynomial has the correct marked-germ shape and
descends covariantly to the physical quotient, but it is not yet a
source-generated selector.  It is a candidate presentation rigidifier with an
unfixed completion modulus.  Ensemble fitting is not authorized until a
source theorem fixes (c), and even such a theorem would still require a
calibrated common-frame instrument.
