# Boundary-density five-cycle gate: WP1263

## Question

Do five conjecture-falsification-residual cycles produce the boundary-density
instrument from existing source data?

## DPC resolution

- **Problem:** execute five independent attempts to obtain the selected
  boundary-density instrument without importing new source authority.
- **Bold conjecture:** five independent existing-source routes can jointly
  construct the instrument or eliminate the need for it.
- **Named rivals:** direct attractor density; dimension-trace preparation;
  common-twist radius clock; rows/support/anomaly kernel and channel;
  calibrated Nima candidate composition.
- **Risky consequences:** WP1177 has zero dilations and zero boundary density
  matrices; WP1254 constructs the dimension-trace state but no physical
  preparation; WP1255 has the radius law but no absolute clock; WP1256 through
  WP1258 have rows, map classifications, and anomaly invariance but no selected
  channel, matching, or kernel; WP1246 has calibrated acquisition but no common
  UV packet.
- **Strongest falsification attempt:** replay WP1177, WP1246, and WP1254
  through WP1258, then test five independent conjectures against their exact
  outputs.
- **Exact residual:** all five cycles are falsified; the remaining object is a
  source-derived boundary-density instrument packet or an owner-side
  microscopic grammar/no-go.
- **Disposition:** five-cycle gate resolved conditionally; boundary-density
  instrument remains selected.

## Five cycles

1. **Conjecture:** the sourced dark-state attractor directly supplies the
   23-dimensional UV boundary density matrix. **Falsification:** source and
   target disagree in dimension, rank, sector support, and spectrum; zero
   portal-to-sector dilations and zero boundary density matrices exist.
   **Residual:** derive a portal-to-sector dilation or independent UV boundary
   state.
2. **Conjecture:** the exact dimension-trace state and sector weights already
   constitute physical preparation. **Falsification:** the state is exact, but
   no normalized sourced boundary state, preparation operator, source measure,
   or physical preparation exists. **Residual:** obtain the UV preparation
   packet.
3. **Conjecture:** common-twist equality plus the radius law selects the
   absolute mass clock. **Falsification:** the radius law is exact, but flux
   sector, gauge-gravity ratio, localized descent, momentum frame, and absolute
   clock are absent. **Residual:** materialize the compactification clock
   packet.
4. **Conjecture:** exact rows, support/rank/orbit algebra, and anomaly
   invariance select the physical16 channel and kernel. **Falsification:** no
   physical16 channel, gain cascade, selected matching class, or selected
   kernel exists. **Residual:** materialize the channel, gain-cascade, and
   production-matching packets.
5. **Conjecture:** calibrated acquisition and existing Nima candidate packets
   construct the common UV boundary-action instrument. **Falsification:**
   calibration is valid, but the candidate packets fill none of the required
   source-dynamics slots and no common UV packet exists. **Residual:** obtain
   an owner-side microscopic source grammar or Nima construction/no-go.

The one-boundary-instrument conjecture **survived** these five cycles. It was
not proven, and no boundary packet was constructed.
