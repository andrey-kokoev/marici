## Authority boundary

This is a genuine common-source constructor, not a collection of separately
frozen masses. It conditionally selects the physical clock ratio, thresholds,
and total tree-level widths once its dimensionless coefficients are fixed.

The numerical witness is not predicted. The same symmetries admit continuous
positive changes of \(a,b,y,z_A,z_B,g_F,g_P\). A complete coupled RG trajectory
or other independent coefficient dynamics must select them before the witness,
residues, or widths gain numerical prediction authority. The WP486 physical
instrument also remains unrealized.
