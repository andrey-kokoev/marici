# Zero-drift bracket closure: WP998

## Question

Can ordered composition of the single formal actuator evade WP997's rank-one
obstruction when the branch is evaluated at zero observed drift?

## Domain and quotient

Work directly on the two-dimensional quotient-level control plane with
coordinates ((Q,R)).  This is a local accessibility calculation, not an
assertion of physical time evolution.  An admitted operation is a composable
local transformation generated by a vector field.

The single-port actuator distribution is

\[
\mathcal D=\operatorname{span}\{\partial_Q\}.
\]

Perfect feedback may change its scalar gain using the full observed state, so
the largest family generated by that port is

\[
X_f=f(Q,R)\partial_Q.
\]

## Exact closure theorem

For arbitrary smooth gains (f) and (g),

\[
[X_f,X_g]=(f\partial_Qg-g\partial_Qf)\partial_Q.
\]

Hence (mathcal D) is involutive. Every finite iterated bracket and every
finite ordered word generated by the admitted one-port family remains on a
leaf (R=\text{constant}). The contextual accessibility classes are exactly
the connected components of those leaves. Distinct points ((Q_0,R_0)) and
((Q_1,R_1)) with (R_0\ne R_1) remain inequivalent under the complete
one-port operation family.

This conclusion uses zero drift. A separately source-derived drift (F) could
enlarge the distribution only if (F) or some bracket ([F,X_f]) has a
nonzero transverse component. The declared branch supplies no such drift
constructor or calibrated cost.

## Hostile and repair witnesses

Take (X=(1,0)). Its Jacobian vanishes, so ([X,X]=0), and the point
((0,1)) cannot reach any point with (R=0). Adding the independent field
(Y=(0,1)) produces rank two immediately, but this is the same unauthorized
second actuator already isolated in WP277 and WP997.

## Classification

The complete zero-drift, one-port, feedback-closed family separates neither
the (R)-leaves nor selects among them. Within a leaf it can alter a formal
presentation coordinate, so it is at most a leafwise control rigidifier. It is
not a physical selector and has no declared instrument.

## Smallest exact falsifier

Two points with the same (Q) and different (R), for example ((0,0)) and
((0,1)), are the smallest exact hostile pair. Every admitted generator and
every iterated bracket has zero (R)-component at both points.

## Claim boundary

WP998 proves bracket closure only for zero drift and for the complete family of
smooth scalar modulations of one fixed actuator line. It does not exclude a
source-derived transverse drift, a genuinely second actuator, a discontinuous
operation, or an enlarged substrate. It assigns no laboratory cost to formal
words.

## Disposition

Close the ordered-bracket escape for the current zero-drift one-port branch.
Reopen only with a named source operation whose transverse component or drift
bracket descends to the quotient, plus a finite executable cost and instrument.
