# Spectral-component `P_det` audit

Owner: `marici.Figueiredo`.

## Frozen source domain

The source grammar is fixed independently of the held-out data:

1. a physical Z-pole dimuon source with PDG natural width, mapped through the
   WP235 calibrated Gaussian detector resolution;
2. a smooth continuum dimuon source with the exponential slope calibrated on
   the WP235 calibration fold.

The detector record is the 40-bin invariant-mass spectrum from 70 to 110 GeV.
The source coefficients are nonnegative yields `(N_Z, N_continuum)`.

## Physical map

\[
P_{\mathrm{det}}(N_Z,N_C)
=N_Z V_Z(m;M_Z,\Gamma_Z,\sigma_{\mathrm{det}})
+N_C C(m;\lambda),
\]

where `V_Z` is the finite-width Voigt response and `C` is the positive
continuum response. Both are evaluated in CMS invariant-mass units and use the
calibration fold only. Odd event identifiers provide the held-out record.

The response Jacobian is the 40-by-2 matrix whose columns are these two
normalized detector templates. With the calibration-fold inverse-Poisson
metric, its Gram matrix is positive definite. Therefore `P_det` is injective
on the declared coefficient cone: every contextual equivalence class is a
singleton coefficient pair.

Calibration uncertainty is propagated by varying detector log-resolution and
continuum slope over their two-standard-error corner box. Admission additionally
requires the Gram determinant to remain strictly positive at every corner.

## Empirical identification

The held-out nonnegative fit identifies both spectral components with positive
yields. This is not yet microscopic source identification: the continuum
column is a calibration-fold exponential nuisance law, not a response derived
from a named source action. The map is a genuine component-identifying physical
readout, but source authority remains open.

## Hostile falsifier

If the continuum response is replaced by a second copy of the pole response,
the Jacobian rank becomes one, the Gram determinant vanishes, and `(1,-1)` is
a kernel generator. Thus source identification follows from the independently
typed spectral difference, not from naming two coefficients.

## Descent and scope

Invariant mass and source yields descend under the full flavor weak-basis
groupoid. No nine-link texture chart or measured-ten uniqueness inference is
used.

This construction identifies pole-shaped versus continuum-shaped record
weights. It does not yet identify the WP192 five-GeV mediator source: the current mediator
grammar fixes a threshold location but no nonzero source-normalized residue.
A decoupled mediator is therefore still observationally equivalent to a local
source. Transferring `P_det` requires either a derived minimum residue or an
executable epsilon control.
