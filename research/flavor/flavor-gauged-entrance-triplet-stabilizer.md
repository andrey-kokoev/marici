# Gauged entrance-triplet stabilizer

## Renormalizable entrance carrier

Replace the fixed equal-entrance vector by electroweak-doublet row triplets
(H^u_\alpha) and (H^d_\alpha). Gauge the restored row action as
(SO(3)_E). The enlarged gauge group is

\[
SU(3)_F\mathbin\times SO(3)_P\mathbin\times SO(3)_E.
\]

The entrance vertices are ordinary dimension-four contractions,

\[
y_{Hu}\bar Q \widetilde H^u_\alpha A^u_{R\alpha},
\qquad
y_{Hd}\bar Q H^d_\alpha A^d_{R\alpha}.
\]

The connector (S_{\alpha i}) is a bifundamental of the two orthogonal gauge
factors. The (A_L,A_R) messengers are vectorlike row vectors. The new scalar
fields generate no fermion anomaly, while the row-charged fermions occur in a
real integer-spin representation and vectorlike pairs.

## Residual diagonal stabilizer

At (X_i=J_i) and (S=I_3), the enlarged gauge algebra has fourteen
generators. The exact tangent matrix has rank eleven. A three-dimensional
diagonal subgroup survives: a row rotation can be compensated by the same port
rotation and the corresponding spin-one flavor conjugation.

Thus simply gauging the row symmetry does not produce a fully massive gauge
spectrum. It restores a continuous stabilizer that WP484 avoided by keeping
the row frame external to the gauge group.

## Entrance-vev completion

One nonzero row-triplet electroweak vacuum vector reduces the diagonal
stabilizer from dimension three to its axial dimension one. The orbit rank is
thirteen, so one gauge boson remains massless.

Two noncollinear row-triplet vacuum vectors have no common continuous rotation.
The exact fourteen-generator orbit then has rank fourteen and positive Gram
determinant. Hence two noncollinear entrance vevs are necessary and sufficient
to remove the continuous diagonal stabilizer in this declared vacuum.

## Changed groupoid and remaining gates

The entrance directions are now dynamical relational vevs in the enlarged
gauge quotient. They are not absolute channel axes. This construction supplies
a renormalizable three-component carrier, but it does not yet prove that three
components survive as independently preparable detector channels.

The complete two-triplet electroweak potential, a stable noncollinear vacuum,
all gauge and scalar masses, finite-width mixing, and detector calibration are
still absent. No numerical selector or width authority follows from the orbit
rank alone.
