# Gauge-beta gate for the triplicated messenger repair

## Strong favorable premise

WP480 grants the missing WP479 constructor at the algebraic level: three equal
orthogonal messenger ports are present in each quark charge sector. Up- and
down-type messengers cannot be shared because they carry different Standard
Model quantum numbers.

With three colors, the active messenger multiplicity is therefore

\[
3\ {m ports}\mathbin\times2\ {m sectors}\mathbin\times3\ {m colors}
=18
\]

Dirac `SU(3)_F` fundamentals. Including the six ordinary colored quark
fundamentals gives `n_f=24`, together with the three real adjoint flavons.

## Exact gauge-only coefficients

Using the same two-loop convention as WP464-WP465, the repaired active spectrum
has

\[
b_0=-{13\over2},
\qquad b_1=-265.
\]

The formal nonzero solution of the truncated gauge beta function is

\[
g_F^2=-{104\pi^2\over265},
\qquad \alpha_F=-{26\pi\over265}.
\]

It is outside the positive coupling domain. The triplicated active theory has
lost asymptotic freedom, and the gauge-only subsystem supplies no physical
nonzero fixed point.

## Threshold trilemma

The three relevant regimes are distinct:

- With all triplicated messengers active, the isotropic port Gram is available,
  but the gauge-only fixed-point candidate disappears.
- After messenger decoupling, the coefficients return to `b0=11/2` and
  `b1=-37`; the finite portal threshold may remain, but `g_F` depends on the
  trajectory and threshold ratios.
- If messengers are open or threshold-adjacent at the flavor-vector poles,
  their decay channels invalidate WP449 and WP476's quark-only widths.

No branch simultaneously supplies the isotropic portal, a gauge-only selected
coupling, and the independently frozen closed-channel widths.

## Disposition

The triplication repairs the interaction Gram conditionally and is therefore a
rigidifier. It does not select the numerical ratio. No reference port or
physical instrument is introduced.

The smallest exact falsifier of the gauge-only selector route is the negative
root above. A progressive successor must compute a complete gauge–Yukawa
system and find a controlled positive fixed point whose Yukawa relations also
enforce the equal-port Gram, survive finite thresholds, and retain the declared
width support. Merely restoring a formal algebraic root is insufficient.
