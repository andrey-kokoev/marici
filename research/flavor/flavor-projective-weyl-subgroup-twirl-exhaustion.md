# Every projective Weyl subgroup twirl is nonselective or CP-blind: WP946

## Question

WP945 tested the clock subgroup and the full Weyl symmetry. Can another subgroup of the exact two-holonomy source define a canonical conditional expectation onto a proper noncommuting flavor module?

## Exhaustive subgroup lattice

Modulo central phases, the three-family Weyl words are labelled by the additive plane

\[
V=\mathbb F_3^2,
\qquad
(a,b)\longmapsto D^aS^b.
\]

Its linear subgroup lattice has exactly six members: the zero subgroup, four one-dimensional lines, and the full plane. The four lines are generated by

\[
(1,0),\quad(0,1),\quad(1,1),\quad(1,2).
\]

For a subgroup `K`, define the conjugation average

\[
E_K(X)=\frac1{|K|}\sum_{(a,b)\in K}D^aS^bX(D^aS^b)^\dagger.
\]

The image is the commutant of the represented subgroup.

## Exact classification

- For the zero subgroup, `E_K` is the identity. Its image has dimension nine and is noncommutative, so it selects nothing.
- Every line is generated by a unitary with three distinct eigenvalues. Its commutant has dimension three and is a commutative maximal abelian algebra. If both Yukawa sectors lie in that image, their Grams commute and all commutator CP invariants vanish.
- The full plane contains the noncommuting clock and shift. Its representation is irreducible, so its commutant is the scalar algebra of dimension one.

Thus no projective Weyl subgroup average has a proper noncommutative image. The result includes every canonical unweighted subgroup twirl of the declared pair, not merely the two examples in WP945.

## Quotient and claim boundary

Simultaneous weak-basis conjugation carries each fixed algebra to a conjugate fixed algebra without changing its dimension, commutativity, or Gram commutator invariants. The classification therefore descends under the full weak-basis groupoid. The subgroup lattice is algebraic; no ordering in it is assigned physical time or causal meaning.

The exhaustion does not cover weighted channels, non-group word supports, defects, distinct left and right actions, or representations enlarged by additional source objects. Each such repair adds source data. Its weights or marked supports must be derived independently of measured flavor coordinates and accompanied by an executable instrument.

## Disposition

Canonical symmetry projection is closed negative for the projective Weyl carrier:

\[
\text{no averaging}
\longmapsto M_3(\mathbb C),
\qquad
\text{line averaging}
\longmapsto \text{commutative dimension }3,
\qquad
\text{full averaging}
\longmapsto \mathbb CI.
\]

The smallest remaining possibility is not another subgroup. It is a source-derived asymmetric channel whose image is proper and noncommutative, with declared Kraus or module data, completion stability, `physical16` descent, and a calibrated physical instrument.
