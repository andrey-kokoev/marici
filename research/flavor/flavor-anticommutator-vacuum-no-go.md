# Anticommutator vacuum no-go: WP442

## Question

Does the WP440 charged instability continue to a stable global vacuum that fully Higgses (SU(3)_F)?

## Global theorem

For traceless Hermitian three-by-three matrices,

\[
\operatorname{Tr}A^4=\frac12(\operatorname{Tr}A^2)^2.
\]

Hilbert-Schmidt Cauchy-Schwarz then gives

\[
\lVert[A,D]\rVert_F^2+\lVert\{A,D\}\rVert_F^2
=4\operatorname{Tr}(A^2D^2)
\leq \frac12R^2.
\]

When \(\eta>\lambda\),

\[
\lambda\lVert[A,D]\rVert_F^2+\eta\lVert\{A,D\}\rVert_F^2
\leq \frac{\eta}{2}R^2.
\]

Equality forces the commutator norm to vanish. Hence every global minimizer on this coefficient side is commuting and leaves at least a Cartan stabilizer. Its (SU(3)_F) gauge-mass rank is at most six.

Because WP441's instability requires \(\eta>3\lambda\), its entire unstable region lies inside this commuting-global regime. The proposed completion cannot pass WP440's rank-eight gate.

## Exact hostile point

At \((m^2,\lambda,\eta,\rho)=(1,1,4,5)\), the eigenvector continuation has

\[
A=a\lambda_3+x\lambda_6,\qquad
D=a\lambda_1+x\lambda_4,\qquad x^2=2a^2.
\]

It is stationary but commuting, has energy \(-3/208\), gauge rank six, and a nongauge Hessian eigenvalue \(-16/13\). A commuting proportional configuration reaches the global radial bound \(-1/48\), has gauge rank six, and no negative Hessian direction. It also has one nongauge flat direction, so it is not an isolated numerical selector.

## Disposition

The squared-anticommutator channel genuinely destabilizes the WP438 presentation but drives the theory toward more commuting structure. It is neither a full-breaking selector nor a route to `physical16`. WP441's earlier phrase “lower-symmetry branch” is superseded: local charged instability alone did not determine the stabilizer of the endpoint.

A successor must change the independently motivated invariant grammar or field representation. It may not tune a term after inspecting the desired flavor vacuum. The absolute ratio (g_Ff/v), flavor-current constraints, widths, residues, and physical instrumentation remain open.
