# Flavor DPC after hostile reduction: bounded source-relative audit (WP118)

Owner: `marici.Figueiredo`.

## Retraction

There is no defensible universal Deutsch–Popperian law saying that every
physical flavor explanation must have a unique microscopic source, a
nonconstant map on source support, an invariant probability measure on the
presentation space, closed Markov dynamics on `physical16`, exact encoding
intertwining, or an unconditioned detector distribution.

Those requirements reject valid point predictions, quotient measures,
universality classes, effective dynamics with memory, approximate matching,
and ordinary calibrated selection effects. The existential claim “some UV
theory exists” is conversely too broad to falsify.

## Surviving flavor DPC

Fix a bounded candidate/resource class `C`, an admitted physical domain, a
protected readout packet, and finite accuracy, scale, time, and resource
budgets `(epsilon, Lambda_UV, Lambda_IR, T, B)`. Then:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{minipage}{0.88\linewidth}
A flavor ensemble has a source-relative explanation when an independently
validated physical model in `C` produces a quotient-level ensemble and
nontrivial counterfactual limits, while a separate verification model
certifies the recovered `physical16` ensemble and its observations within the
declared budgets.
\end{minipage}}
\]

This is a bounded audit framework, not a universal metaphysical conjecture.

The source produces the ensemble or channel. The verifier produces the
certificate. Neither is allowed to borrow selector authority from RG,
canonicalization, thermal branching, or detector inversion.

## Audit contract

For each candidate `c in C`, record:

1. **Bounded class:** field/resource class, coefficient domain, quotient,
   scales, scheme, time horizon, and error/resource budgets.
2. **Independent validation:** evidence constraining the physical model that
   is not merely inversion of the protected flavor target. Chronology alone is
   neither necessary nor sufficient.
3. **Quotient measure:** a normalized measure directly on the completed UV
   quotient, or a quasi-invariant presentation measure with its cocycle and
   gauge-fixing factors.
4. **Recovered ensemble:** an explicit comparison satisfying, for a declared
   probability metric,
   \[
   d(\mathcal R_*\nu_{\rm implemented},\nu_{16}^{\rm claim})\le\epsilon.
   \]
   Exact presentation-level intertwining is not required.
5. **Closure or augmentation:** prove RG/matching lumpability on the claimed
   state, or enlarge the state by the minimal memory/environment record.
6. **Observation factorization:** represent detector acceptance by an
   independently calibrated kernel. Conditioning may change observed weights
   but cannot redefine the upstream ensemble.
7. **Predictive contrast:** predeclare a finite statistic or probability
   metric against a baseline. Delta laws and full-support laws are admissible.
8. **Counterfactual restriction:** remove a resource class and all its derived
   descendants; demonstrate loss of the bounded capability or a monotone
   obstruction. Necessity of one named microscopic implementation is not
   required.
9. **Separate verification:** replayable evidence certifies behavior but is
   not represented as a causal output of the UV dynamics.

## Exact hostile countermodels

- **Point source:** `mu=delta_u` pushes to `delta_q`; constancy on support is a
  valid maximally sharp prediction.
- **Universality fiber:** distinct microscopic measures inside a physical
  fiber have the same quotient pushforward; microscopic uniqueness is not
  required.
- **Non-lumpable marginal:** hidden states with the same current physical label
  have different next-label laws; adding the hidden record restores Markov
  closure.
- **Detector conditioning:** calibrated unequal acceptance changes the observed
  weights while leaving the source ensemble fixed.
- **Full-support contrast:** two strictly positive distributions can have
  nonzero total-variation distance without excluding an open set.
- **Approximate recovery:** an implemented ensemble outside the target chart
  can recover within epsilon without exact presentation intertwining.
- **Descendant leakage:** deleting a source label while retaining its derived
  fitted coefficients does not enact the counterfactual restriction.
