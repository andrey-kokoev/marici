# Source Gramian pairing

Work package: WP556  
Owner: marici.Figueiredo

## Question

Does the frozen source resolvent itself provide the positive pairing required
by WP555, and if so does that pairing select a proper source subspace?

## Minimal source realization

Write the exact WP520 scalar kernel as

\[
F(z)=c(zI-A)^{-1}B.
\]

The degree-six companion realization is controllable and observable. Because
all six pole squares are positive, \(-A\) generates a stable auxiliary
semigroup. Define the exact Gramians by

\[
AW_c+W_cA^T=BB^T,
\qquad
A^TW_o+W_oA=c^Tc.
\]

Equivalently, these are the positive integrals generated by \(e^{-At}\). The
checker solves both rational Lyapunov systems exactly. Every leading principal
minor of both matrices is positive, so each is positive definite.

## Quotient descent

Under a realization similarity \(T\),

\[
W_c'=TW_cT^T,
\qquad
W_o'=T^{-T}W_oT^{-1}.
\]

Hence \(W_c^{-1}\) is a covariant source-coordinate metric of precisely the
kind WP555 requires, and the eigenvalues of \(W_cW_o\) are similarity
invariants. The checker verifies the transformation laws and metric Gram
descent exactly for a nontrivial six-state similarity.

## Selector test

Both Gramians have rank six. The controllable and observable subspaces are the
entire minimal realization. Consequently this pairing distinguishes weighted
directions but selects neither a proper subspace nor a point. It is a source-
generated geometry and rigidifier, not a source selector.

## Physical-instrument gate

The auxiliary parameter \(t\) has inverse-mass-squared units and the Gramian
definition assumes an impulse input through \(B\), continuous access to the
full semigroup, and output through \(c\). The flavor action has not supplied a
physical preparation and detector implementing that experiment. Algebraic
source generation therefore does not yet imply executable control.

The smallest exact selector falsifier is full rank: a positive-definite
Gramian has no nonzero nullspace and does not reduce the admissible six-state
family. The smallest instrument falsifier is the absence of a declared
operation realizing the auxiliary impulse-time integral.

## Status

WP556 supplies an invariant source pairing on the frozen WP520 realization.
It repairs the missing mathematical metric in WP555 conditionally, while
remaining an uninstrumented rigidifier and not a selector of \(g_Ff/v\).
