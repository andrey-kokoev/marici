# Minimal complex entrance flat-direction obstruction

## Minimal Hermitian lift

Promote the two real row vectors of WP503 to complex neutral entrance vectors
(u,d\in\mathbb C^3). The direct Hermitian lift is

\[
V=\lambda_u(u^\dagger u-a^2)^2
+\lambda_d(d^\dagger d-b^2)^2
+\kappa|u^\dagger d|^2.
\]

It remains positive and has the real orthogonal representative

\[
u=a e_3,
\qquad d=b e_1
\]

as a global zero.

## Exact neutral Hessian

The two complex row vectors have twelve real coordinates. At the orthogonal
vacuum, the Hessian has rank four and nullity eight. Four null directions are
gauge:

- three common row rotations;
- one common hypercharge phase.

The other four are physical neutral flat directions. Exact witnesses are a
relative entrance phase, two imaginary fluctuations along the row axis
transverse to both vevs, and one imaginary cross fluctuation invisible to the
Hermitian inner-product response.

The four positive masses squared are

\[
8\lambda_u a^2,
\qquad
8\lambda_d b^2,
\qquad
2\kappa(a^2+b^2),
\qquad
2\kappa(a^2+b^2).
\]

## Consequence

WP503 cannot be transported directly into the complex electroweak entrance
theory. Its three real physical masses become only four positive modes in a
twelve-real-dimensional neutral space, while four additional physical zeros
remain after gauge quotient.

This is a defect of the minimal Hermitian lift, not a universal no-go for every
multi-doublet source. The next packet must enumerate the complete
renormalizable invariant basis and identify independently justified
phase-sensitive terms. A physical spectrum requires the full charged,
CP-even, and CP-odd Hessian kernel to equal the complete gauge orbit.
