# Spin(5) matter-completion selector gate: WP1213

## Question

Do anomaly, threshold, and massability probes select Spin(5) completion A
or B?

## DPC resolution

- **Problem:** select anomaly-equivalent Spin(5) completion A versus B from
  parent representation, zero-mode index, or locality before solving fixed
  points.
- **Bold conjecture:** anomaly cancellation, finite thresholds, or
  massability selects one completion.
- **Named rivals:** completion A bare masses; completion B generic Yukawas;
  finite matching constant; threshold mass; anomaly/global parity.
- **Risky consequences:** A and B cancel identical anomaly and parity
  classes; their one-loop beta coefficients differ by two; finite matching
  leaves a two-dimensional fiber; A has two charged bare pairs; B has no
  charged bare pair but generic full-rank Yukawa masses.
- **Strongest falsification attempt:** the same completion and mass admit
  \(k=0\) versus \(k=1\); A's bare scales vary; B's zero-Yukawa hostile has
  rank zero; anomaly and parity classify both together.
- **Exact residual:** derive an independent parent, zero-mode, locality, or
  vacuum principle selecting completion and Yukawa matrices before RG,
  widths, and pole calibration.
- **Disposition:** reject probe-based completion selection; require an
  independent completion principle.
