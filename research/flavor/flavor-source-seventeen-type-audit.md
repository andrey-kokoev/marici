# Existing source seventeens do not realize the integer mass norm: WP1029

## Question

Does an independently derived occurrence of seventeen in the admitted flavor
programme provide the source authority missing from WP1028?

## Bounded inventory

Six exact occurrences survive inspection:

- the rank-17 complete gauge-tangent Gram of WP507;
- the rank-17 scalar Hessian at the frozen WP468 benchmark;
- seventeen field labels in the WP635 messenger incidence;
- the positive SU(4) spectral index of WP772 and WP773;
- the negative spectral index for two bulk SU(6) families in WP775.

These are respectively ranks, a presentation census, and signed spectral
indices. None is a declared count of equal orthogonal contributions to the
WP90 messenger mass.

## Closest-candidate hostile test

WP507 is the closest candidate because it is already a positive mass Gram with
rank seventeen. Rank, however, counts nonzero support; WP1028 requires equal
norm. The exact electroweak subblock alone has positive eigenvalue classes

\[
\frac{g_2^2v^2}{4},
\qquad
\frac{(g_2^2+g_Y^2)v^2}{4}.
\]

Their splitting is

\[
\frac{g_Y^2v^2}{4}>0.
\]

Thus the rank-17 Gram is not a rank-17 equal-norm projector even before its
fourteen-dimensional flavor-port block is diagonalized. Replacing its rank by
the trace coefficient in \(M^2=17f^2\) is invalid.

## Contextual partition and instrument

The inventory contains six source objects carrying the integer seventeen and
zero typed interfaces into the WP90 messenger mass. WP507 itself classifies
the gauge rank as a rigidifier, leaves all scales and couplings free, and has
no pole instrument. The spectral indices control vacuum signs rather than
messenger mass multiplicity.

## Smallest exact falsifier

The charged-neutral electroweak splitting
\(g_Y^2v^2/4\) is already nonzero. This single subblock falsifies the claim
that WP507 supplies seventeen equal contributions.

## Claim boundary

This closes the six existing exact seventeens as lifts of WP1028. It does not
exclude a new source representation whose canonical mass Gram is literally a
rank-17 projector and which couples to the WP90 messenger.

## Disposition

Negative. WP1028 remains a sharply data-compatible normal form, not a
source-derived theorem. The next constructive target is a representation-level
tight frame or projector with rank seventeen and a named messenger coupling.
