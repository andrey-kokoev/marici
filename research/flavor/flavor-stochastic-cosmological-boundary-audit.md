## Frozen cosmological source

Add a real weak-basis-singlet spectator `delta` during quasi-de Sitter
expansion with Hubble parameter `H` and

\[
V(\delta)=\frac12m^2\delta^2.
\]

The leading stochastic-inflation equilibrium law is

\[
P_{\rm eq}(\delta)\propto
\exp\!\left[-\frac{8\pi^2}{3H^4}V(\delta)\right]
=\exp\!\left[-\frac{4\pi^2m^2}{3H^4}\delta^2\right],
\]

with

\[
\langle\delta\rangle=0,
\qquad
\sigma^2=\frac{3H^4}{8\pi^2m^2}.
\]

The law is frozen before flavor or detector targets. Because `delta` is a
weak-basis singlet, preparation descends under the flavor groupoid. Mapping it
to WP135's relevant eigenoperator still requires a declared reheating arrow.

## Pushforward to crossover scales

For critical exponent `theta=2`, `Lambda` is proportional to
`|delta|^(1/2)`. Normalize `z=delta/sigma`; then

\[
\langle z\rangle=0,\qquad \langle z^2\rangle=1,
\qquad\langle z^4\rangle=3.
\]

Both `z=1` and `z=4` have nonzero density. Their log-density ratio is
`-15/2`, while their crossover scales differ by exactly two. A calibrated
detector reach between those scales makes the first draw accessible and the
second inaccessible. The operation therefore supplies a probability measure
over accessibility classes, not a unique class.

The scale pushforward also identifies `z` and `-z`. Unless the sign flip is a
source-authorized gauge symmetry, threshold masses have a sign kernel and
sign-sensitive multi-point records are required.

## Classification and authority

This is the first independently motivated operation after WP135 that supplies
a normalized relevant-amplitude law. It replaces an arbitrary unweighted
fiber by a source-generated probability measure. But it is an **ensemble
producer**, not a distinguished-trajectory selector: its variance is nonzero,
so finite fiber is not singleton fiber.

The numerical ensemble depends on `H`, `m/H`, inflationary duration, the
initial state away from equilibrium, and the reheating map from `delta` to the
flavor eigenoperator. The Hubble scale is a relational cosmological clock and
changes the source groupoid; it is not an absolute scale of the original
flavor experiment.

Pushing each draw through WP128 produces distributions of masses, widths, and
multi-point amplitudes. WP133's rank must therefore become a reliability
statement conditioned or averaged over this distribution. No
cosmological-to-collider instrument is established.

The smallest exact falsifier of point selection is `z=1,4`: both are
source-admissible and their thresholds differ by two. The physical-instrument
gate is a source-derived path from inflationary patch records through
reheating to the local flavor vacuum and executable threshold probes.

## Disposition

WP136 finds a genuine source-generated boundary **ensemble**, but not the
boundary **point** sought by the current objective. It leaves nonzero
accessibility uncertainty and a possible sign kernel. Source identification
therefore remains conditional and probabilistic.

unique trajectory or executable instrument is added. These reports are
