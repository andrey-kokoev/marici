# Weyl executable closure and canonical expectations do not select viable flavor: WP945

## Question

Can WP944's missing proper word module be obtained without adding a new source object, either by closing the two admitted holonomies under executable algebra operations or by averaging over their intrinsic finite symmetry?

## Closure theorem

Retain the exact clock-shift pair

\[
D=\operatorname{diag}(1,\omega,\omega^2),
\qquad
DS=\omega SD,
\qquad
\omega^3=1,
\quad \omega\ne1.
\]

Any unital complex algebra containing both `D` and `S` contains every word `D^a S^b`. These nine words are a basis of `M3(C)`. Consequently the smallest unital adjoint-closed executable algebra containing both source operations is already

\[
\operatorname{Alg}^*(D,S)=M_3(\mathbb C).
\]

Requiring multiplication, linear combination, identity, and adjoint therefore cannot produce a proper selector. It supplies universal algebraic reachability only; it does not fix which element is realized.

## Canonical conditional expectations

The pair does define exact finite twirls. The clock twirl is

\[
E_D(X)=\frac13\sum_{a=0}^{2}D^aXD^{-a}.
\]

Its fixed algebra is the three-dimensional diagonal algebra. It annihilates every Weyl word with nonzero shift grade. If both Yukawa sectors are restricted to this image, their Grams commute and the WP943 CP obstruction returns.

The full Weyl twirl is

\[
E_{D,S}(X)=\frac19\sum_{a,b=0}^{2}D^aS^bX(D^aS^b)^\dagger.
\]

Its image is the one-dimensional scalar algebra:

\[
E_{D,S}(X)=\frac{\operatorname{Tr}X}{3}I.
\]

It erases masses and mixing more strongly. Thus the two canonical expectations supplied by the declared Weyl symmetry are proper source-derived projections, but neither selects a phenomenologically viable noncommuting `physical16` family.

## Claim boundary

This is not an exhaustion of every source action that might relate two holonomies. It exhausts the ordinary unital adjoint-closed algebra generated by the pair and the canonical subgroup twirls requiring no extra weights, orientation, marked word, or fitted coefficient. A noncanonical weighted expectation can preserve a noncommuting module, but its weights and support are additional source data and must not be inferred from measured flavor coordinates.

The statements are invariant under simultaneous weak-basis conjugation. Composition is algebraic composition; no physical time or causal order is assigned.

## Disposition

The Weyl pair alone gives a forced dichotomy:

- executable algebra closure yields the full matrix algebra and selects nothing;
- canonical symmetry averaging yields diagonal or scalar images and destroys viable mixing.

The next missing arrow is a source-derived asymmetric module selector: a declared action, defect, representation, or positive pairing that distinguishes a proper noncommuting word sector. It must descend to `physical16`, survive allowed completions, and possess a calibrated physical instrument. A marked word or reference port changes the relational experiment and its stabilizer groupoid.
