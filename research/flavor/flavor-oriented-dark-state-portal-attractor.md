# Oriented Dark-State Portal Attractor

## Question

Can one source semigroup repair WP856's classical-coherence gap and make the
antisymmetric portal state a unique global attractor?

## Minimal three-state source

Use the orthonormal basis

\[
|v\rangle,
\qquad
|+\rangle=\frac{|A\rangle+|B\rangle}{\sqrt2},
\qquad
|-\rangle=\frac{|A\rangle-|B\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
\]

Here 
(|v\rangle) represents the absent portal and 
(|\pm\rangle) are the two coherent path-parity sectors. Define the source
jumps

\[
L_v=\sqrt\kappa|-\rangle\langle v|,
\qquad
L_+=\sqrt\kappa|-\rangle\langle+|,
\qquad \kappa>0.
\]

Their Lindblad generator is

\[
\mathcal L(\rho)=\sum_{a\in\{v,+\}}
\left(L_a\rho L_a^*-rac12\{L_a^*L_a,\rho\}\right).
\]

## Exact global basin

The nine-dimensional Liouville matrix has spectrum

\[
0,
\quad -\kappa\text{ with multiplicity }4,
\quad -\kappa/2\text{ with multiplicity }4.
\]

Its kernel is one-dimensional and generated by

\[
\rho_*=|-\rangle\langle-|.
\]

Every density matrix converges to this state. In particular, the absent source
obeys

\[
|v\rangle\langle v|
\longmapsto
e^{-\kappa t}|v\rangle\langle v|
+(1-e^{-\kappa t})|-\rangle\langle-|.
\]

Thus the portal is generated from absence, and the spectral gap

\[
\kappa/2
\]

gives a global convergence basin. The selected port amplitudes have fixed
magnitude (1/\sqrt2) and antisymmetric relative phase.

## What sign is fixed

The density matrix fixes the odd ray, not its global phase:

\[
|-\rangle\langle-|=(-|-\rangle)(-\langle-|).
\]

Therefore this source fixes relative path parity but cannot by itself fix the
sign seen against WP855's external coherent reference. That sign becomes a
relative observable only after the reference is included in the experiment.

## Source-authority hostile

Complete positivity, trace preservation, equal rates, exchange covariance,
and a unique pure fixed point do not select the odd ray. The rival jumps

\[
L'_v=\sqrt\kappa|+\rangle\langle v|,
\qquad
L'_-=\sqrt\kappa|+\rangle\langle-|,
\]

have the same Liouvillian spectrum and global basin structure but select the
symmetric ray. Hence writing the odd jump operators without deriving their
relative port phase would encode the desired answer in the reservoir.

The required source principle must derive the odd coupling from the oriented
boundary termination of WP854 or another independently specified microscopic
interaction. Exchange covariance alone is insufficient because the density
projectors onto both parity rays are exchange invariant.

## RG and threshold typing

This is a genuine physical-time open-system attractor. It becomes an RG basin
only if a source theorem identifies the same completely positive semigroup
with flavor coarse graining. Calling physical relaxation "RG" does not create
that interface.

A threshold map preserves the prediction only if it intertwines the dark
projector and the referenced difference effect. Preserving populations or the
Liouvillian gap alone is insufficient.

## Classification

Conditional coherent portal selector with a complete global basin. It fixes
antisymmetric parity and magnitude (1/\sqrt2), generates the portal from
absence, and supplies an exact convergence gap. It does not yet explain why
the source reservoir selects odd rather than even parity, does not fix an
absolute reference sign, and is not yet an RG, threshold, or detector theorem.
