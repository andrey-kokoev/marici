# Mixed connector-gauge poles and residue authority

## Common source domain

WP485 uses the WP484 gauge theory with canonical kinetic terms and vacuum

\[
X_i=\mu J_i,
\qquad S=sI_3.
\]

Here \(\mu\) is the adjoint amplitude. The established physical flavor norm
is \(f_{\rm phys}^2=\sum_i\operatorname{Tr}X_i^2=6\mu^2\).

Write the two gauge couplings as \(g_F\) and \(g_P\). Quarks carry only the
flavor current; the port gauge field reaches them only through vacuum-induced
mixing. This is the first pole calculation in the changed WP484 groupoid.

## Exact mass decomposition

The five principal-spin-two flavor vectors do not mix. Their mass squared is

\[
m_5^2=3g_F^2\mu^2.
\]

For each of the three principal-spin-one labels, the flavor and port vectors
have the common mass-squared block

\[
M_1^2=
\begin{pmatrix}
g_F^2\mu^2 & 2g_Fg_P\mu^2\\
2g_Fg_P\mu^2 & g_P^2(4\mu^2+2s^2)
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its determinant is \(2g_F^2g_P^2\mu^2s^2\), so the connector scale is exactly
what removes the diagonal gauge zero. The full vector spectrum has
multiplicities five, three, and three.

## Current residues

Let \(m_-^2<m_+^2\) be the eigenvalues of the displayed block. Since the
quark current couples only to its flavor entry, the two triplet pole residues
are \(g_F^2w_-\) and \(g_F^2w_+\), with

\[
w_-+w_+=1.
\]

The individual weights depend on both \(g_P/g_F\) and \(s/\mu\). The exact
zero-momentum triplet current coefficient is nevertheless coupling independent:

\[
g_F^2(M_1^{-2})_{FF}={2\mu^2+s^2\over \mu^2s^2}.
\]

Thus the enlarged action freezes the multiplet grammar, a residue sum rule,
and the contact kernel, but it does not independently freeze the individual
mixed-pole residues.

## Hostile pair and boundary

At \(g_F=\mu=s=1\), changing only \(g_P\) from one to two changes the lower-pole
quark-current weight. Both points obey the same representation assignments and
vacuum equations. This is the smallest exact falsifier of residue uniqueness.

The old WP448 triplet pole cannot be transported into WP484: it splits into
two mixed poles. Numerical residues require a source selector or independent
calibration of \(g_P/g_F\) and \(s/\mu\). Widths additionally require the full
connector and messenger thresholds and every open decay channel. No detector
instrument is claimed.
