# Dynamical coefficient-representation census: WP452

## Question

What is the smallest (SO(3))-covariant dynamical coefficient content capable of using WP447's irreducible vacuum algebra without reverting to WP450's arbitrary lookup coefficients?

## Exact decomposition

For the spin-one generation space,

\[
\operatorname{End}(\mathbb C^3)
=\mathbf1\oplus\mathbf3\oplus\mathbf5.
\]

An exact matrix basis is supplied by:

- the singlet (I);
- the vector (J_i);
- five symmetric-traceless quadrupoles
  \[
  Q_{ij}=\frac12\{J_i,J_j\}-\frac23\delta_{ij}I.
  \]

The three blocks have ranks one, three, and five, are pairwise Hilbert-Schmidt orthogonal, and together have rank nine.

## Why one vector is insufficient

A triplet-only order parameter produces

\[
Y-y_0I=n_iJ_i,
\]

whose exact characteristic polynomial is

\[
x\left(x^2-|n|^2\right).
\]

Its three eigenvalues are equally spaced. For two real triplet order parameters, an (SO(3)) rotation places both vectors in one plane; the corresponding spin-one matrices and their relative eigenframe can be chosen real, so no CKM-type CP invariant is generated.

## Minimal source-field typing

A generic complex Yukawa therefore requires, per quark sector, a complex singlet, a complex triplet, and a complex symmetric-traceless quintet—or a separately justified source law selecting a smaller predictive subfamily.

This is a representation census, not permission to assign 18 arbitrary real components. The coefficient fields need a positive source action whose allowed invariants, vacuum orbit, relative up/down orientation, and complex phase are frozen before flavor comparison.

## Selector and instrument boundary

WP452 selects no coefficient value and no `physical16` point. It merely identifies the smallest covariant carrier capable of general flavor. No current constraint or detector instrument descends until the coefficient-field vacuum and messenger map are defined.

The smallest exact falsifier is failure of the displayed (1+3+5) basis to span all nine matrix directions, or a generic triplet-only matrix with non-equally-spaced eigenvalues.
