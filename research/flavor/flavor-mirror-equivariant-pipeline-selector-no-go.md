# Mirror-Equivariant Pipeline Selector No-Go

## Question

Can an equilibrium, mirror-complete flavor source become an asymmetric portal
selector after RG flow, thresholds, or a discriminating instrument are added?

## Claim boundary

Let the source domain contain a free mirror orbit \(\{x_+,x_-\}\), with
\(R x_+=x_-\). Let the desired portal coordinate be nonzero and odd:

\[
a(Rx)=-a(x).
\]

Assume source admissibility and source cost are invariant under \(R\), RG and
threshold transport are equivariant, and detector channels are either
orientation even or explicitly signed. The theorem still applies when an even
potential uniquely fixes \(|a|\).

## Selector theorem

A selector on the orbit is a weight vector \(s=(s_+,s_-)\). Descent under the
source groupoid requires \(Rs=s\). For the swap representation this gives
\(s_+=s_-\). No invariant selector therefore has singleton support on a free
orbit. If an invariant energy has a minimum at \(x_+\), it has an equal minimum
at \(x_-\).

Write \(X_{\mathrm{src}}\) for the mirror-complete source and
\(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{pair}}\) for its paired portal orbit. The first
nonfaithful arrow is

\[
X_{\mathrm{src}}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{\mathrm{pair}}.
\]

No downstream operation can retroactively delete one source constructor.

## RG and threshold descent

For mirror-equivariant transport \(T_t\),

\[
T_tR=RT_t.
\]

Paired initial points have paired trajectories, fixed points, critical
exponents, and basin boundaries. A symmetric communicating kernel has the
symmetric stationary state; it cannot make one orientation the unique
attractor without an orientation-odd source term.

Inclusive thresholds depend on even combinations such as \(a^2\) and identify
the pair. A signed threshold can retain the sign, but retention is
discrimination rather than selection.

## Instrument and reference theorem

A signed calibrated instrument can separate \(x_+\) and \(x_-\). It does not
explain why the source prepared one. With a reference orientation \(r\), the
physical readout is relational, for example \(ar\). Under simultaneous mirror,

\[
(a,r)\longmapsto(-a,-r),
\qquad
ar\longmapsto ar.
\]

Fixing \(r\) chooses a stabilizer groupoid and defines a new experiment. If
\(r\) is dynamical under the symmetric source, the paired joint states return.

The same applies to a bias \(\mu a\). Fixed pseudoscalar \(\mu\) selects only
because a source boundary already supplied it. If \(\mu\) is dynamical and
mirror odd, simultaneous mirroring preserves \(\mu a\) and restores paired
vacua.

## Classification

- Mirror-invariant equilibrium gates are rigidifiers, not singleton selectors.
- Mirror-equivariant RG preserves paired basins.
- Inclusive thresholds erase sign; signed thresholds retain but do not select.
- A signed instrument distinguishes a prepared state without preparation
  authority.
- A reference port changes the experiment to a relational stabilizer groupoid.

## Necessary source principle

The missing principle must be a non-mirror-completable boundary or irreversible
history constructor. It must co-generate:

1. portal orientation;
2. the reference calibrating its signed readout;
3. the magnitude-setting scale;
4. the initial condition inside a unique RG basin;
5. threshold states and their finite-width response.

Calling an inserted coefficient, axion expectation value, flux sign, or
detector convention that constructor merely moves the unexplained choice. The
constructor must arise from a declared physical source operation and lack an
admitted mirror history in the same source domain.

This is a necessary type, not an existence proof. No admitted flavor object
currently realizes all five outputs.

## Smallest exact falsifier

Take

\[
R=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\]

the invariant gate \((1,1)\), odd portal \((1,-1)\), and any symmetric
communicating transport kernel. Both source states pass, RG treats them
equally, and inclusive widths coincide. A signed detector distinguishes them
but cannot change source support.

## Disposition

Close the search among mirror-complete equilibrium potentials, equivariant RG
improvements, and readout-only repairs. Search for a physical flavor
boundary/history operation whose non-equivariance is derived from its source
construction and whose calibration port is generated by the same history.
