# Dark-Corner Conditional Expectation Audit

## Question

Does a source-defined positive conditional expectation remove the channel
freedom left by WP865 and thereby derive the portal basin?

## Claim boundary

Fix a normalized dark ray (d) supplied by the reciprocal Kirchhoff pairing.
The Heisenberg operation

\[
\mathcal E^*(X)=\langle d|X|d\rangle I
\]

is unital, completely positive, and idempotent.  Its Schrödinger dual is

\[
\mathcal E(\rho)=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho)P_d.
\]

It is the unique trace-preserving operation whose range is the one-dimensional
state corner generated by (P_d).  Thus every normalized input reaches the
selected state in one application.

Inside the WP865 lossless channel family, write (s=\sqrt q).  Requiring
idempotence gives (s\in\{0,1}).  The (s=1) branch has an additional fixed
bright state, so idempotence together with a unique stationary ray forces
(s=0), equivalently (q=0).  The free basin parameter is therefore removed
by a genuine operation law rather than fitted.

The associated normalized continuous semigroup is

\[
T_\tau=\mathcal E+e^{-\tau}(I-\mathcal E),
\]

with one stationary direction and eight modes of exponent (-1) on the
three-state matrix space.  This supplies a global dimensionless basin.  It is
not yet a physical flavor RG theorem: a source-derived map from (	au) to a
physically normalized RG coordinate is still required, and an arbitrary rate
(kappa) reappears under (	au=\kappa t).

## What the expectation does not select

For every reciprocal phase (z), the normalized ray

\[
d_z=\frac{(-z,1)}{\sqrt2}
\]

defines an equally valid conditional expectation.  Positivity and
idempotence therefore fix the basin after (d_z) is supplied; they do not fix
the relative phase.  WP859's positive Kirchhoff row fixes equal magnitudes and
its kernel, while a retained oriented reference is still needed to type the
relative sign.  The map from the normalized ray to the numerical portal
coupling remains a separate physical normalization.

Threshold survival separates into two levels:

1. Selector survival requires only that the dark line have no heavy leakage.
2. Readout survival requires the complete bright/dark two-port projector to be
   reducing, because the complementary port must also transport isometrically.

A unitary threshold can preserve (d) while mixing (b) with a heavy state.
The conditional expectation then survives, but the complementary readout is
attenuated.  Hence naturality of the selector does not imply naturality of its
instrument.

## Smallest exact falsifiers

- (z=1) and (z=i) give different idempotent expectations with identical
  rank and basin spectrum.
- Rescaling the continuous generator by (kappa>0) preserves its basin and
  conditional-expectation endpoint while changing the physical rate.
- A unitary rotation of the bright port into one heavy state preserves the
  dark selector but destroys full two-port readout survival.

## Disposition

Progressive but incomplete.  A source-defined conditional expectation is the
first operation in this chain that makes the basin channel unique once the
dark corner is fixed.  It does not derive that corner's phase, its portal-unit
normalization, a physical RG clock, full two-port threshold reduction, or a
calibrated `physical16` detector.  The sought end-to-end principle must derive
the conditional expectation and its bright/dark instrument as one natural
operation of an exact microscopic flavor boundary algebra.
