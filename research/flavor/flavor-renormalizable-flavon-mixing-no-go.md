# Renormalizable invariant flavon mixing no-go (WP124)

Owner: `marici.Figueiredo`.

## Bounded question

Can the complete renormalizable polynomial potential for two complex
bifundamental flavons, invariant under the full weak-basis group

\[
U(3)_Q\times U(3)_u\times U(3)_d,
\]

select a generic nonzero CKM mixing point on `physical16`?

These ratings are non-evidential.

## Exact source class and quotient

Let `Phi_u` transform as `(3, anti-3, 1)` and `Phi_d` as
`(3, 1, anti-3)`, and put

\[
H_u=\Phi_u\Phi_u^\dagger,\qquad H_d=\Phi_d\Phi_d^\dagger.
\]

Through field degree four, the invariant potential is generated by

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\operatorname{Tr}H_u,\quad \operatorname{Tr}H_d,\\
&(\operatorname{Tr}H_u)^2,\quad \operatorname{Tr}(H_u^2),
\quad(\operatorname{Tr}H_d)^2,\quad \operatorname{Tr}(H_d^2),\\
&\operatorname{Tr}H_u\operatorname{Tr}H_d,
\quad \operatorname{Tr}(H_uH_d).
\end{aligned}
\]

All descend under the full weak-basis groupoid. Determinants are excluded:
they carry the independent right-handed `U(1)` charge and become admissible
only after reducing the group or adding a charged spurion. Likewise a direct
`Phi_u Phi_d^dagger` contraction is forbidden because the two right indices
belong to independent groups. Adding either object changes this theorem's
source class and must be typed as such.

At fixed spectra, every displayed invariant except
`C=Tr(H_u H_d)` is orientation-independent. Thus the entire orientation
problem is one-dimensional in invariant-function space.

## No-go theorem

Write the orientation term as `c_ud C`. The rearrangement theorem bounds `C`
between oppositely and equally ordered eigenvalue pairings. When both spectra
are nondegenerate, its extrema occur only at permutation-aligned eigenbases.
These configurations satisfy `[H_u,H_d]=0` and give a permutation CKM matrix,
not generic mixing.

There are only two branches:

- If `c_ud` is nonzero, a global orientation minimum is aligned or
  anti-aligned according to its sign. Generic noncommuting mixing is not a
  minimum.
- If `c_ud=0`, the entire relative orientation is flat, so mixing is not
  selected at all.

Degenerate spectra add stabilizer-flat rotations; they do not uniquely select
a generic CKM point. Therefore this complete renormalizable full-group source
class may select norms and spectra conditionally, but it cannot select generic
CKM mixing.

## Smallest exact hostile point

Take

\[
H_u=\operatorname{diag}(3,2,1),\qquad
H_d=\operatorname{diag}(6,4,1)
\]

and rotate `H_d` in the first two coordinates by the rational orthogonal
matrix with cosine `3/5` and sine `4/5`. Then

\[
C_{\rm aligned}=27,\qquad C_{\rm reverse}=17,\qquad
C_{\rm rotated}=\frac{643}{25}.
\]

The rotated point is physical and noncommuting:

\[
\lVert[H_u,H_d^{\rm rot}]\rVert_F^2=\frac{1152}{625}.
\]

Its exact orientation derivative is `-48/25`. Hence it is not stationary
when `c_ud` is nonzero; when `c_ud` vanishes the same direction is flat. This
single pair falsifies a claimed generic-mixing minimum in both branches.
