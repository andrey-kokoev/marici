# Charged-Spurion Lift Makes the Incidence Equivariant but Reopens the Ray

## Question

Can WP820 be lifted to a genuine (U(1))-equivariant complex whose source
data also supplies WP846's representation-valued threshold memory?

## Minimal equivariant lift

Give the three domain lines weights (1,2,3) and take two neutral target
lines. Constant entries cannot intertwine these weights because each row of
(B) connects distinct domain weights to the same target line. Promote the
entries to charged spurions (s_j) of weights (-j):

\[
\widetilde B(s)=
\begin{pmatrix}
2s_1&-s_2&0\\
3s_1&0&-s_3
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Every nonzero term now has total weight zero, so the pre-vacuum polynomial
map is equivariant.

## Kernel and vacuum fiber

For nonzero spurions, the kernel is generated by

\[
k(s)=
\begin{pmatrix}
s_2s_3\\
2s_1s_3\\
3s_1s_2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

At (s_1=s_2=s_3=1), this becomes ((1,2,3)^T). But the kernel ray depends
on two independent projective VEV ratios. For example,
((s_1,s_2,s_3)=(1,2,1)) gives the ray ((1,1,3)^T), not the WP820 ray.
Thus the equivariant lift does not select the desired vacuum alignment.

## Index and groupoid

Before symmetry breaking, the virtual equivariant index of the displayed
two-term complex is

\[
\chi_{\rm virt}(z)=z+z^2+z^3-2,
\]

because the codomain contains two neutral lines. It is not WP846's positive
character (z+z^2+z^3).

After all three charged spurions acquire nonzero VEVs, the common stabilizer
inside (U(1)) is trivial because

\[
\gcd(1,2,3)=1.
\]

Hence the vacuum that recovers the numerical incidence breaks the continuous
reference group used by the holonomy character. A holonomy-resolved readout
would then be a relational experiment involving the pre-vacuum background or
additional defect data, not an observable of the broken vacuum alone.

## Source consequence

The lift is mathematically useful: it names the missing fields and makes the
equivariance interface explicit. It does not close the selector because a
source potential must still:

1. select the VEV ray (s_1:s_2:s_3=1:1:1);
2. protect it under RG and thresholds;
3. explain how the pre-vacuum equivariant character survives symmetry
   breaking as physical threshold memory;
4. realize the corresponding reference ports.

Choosing equal VEVs merely to recover (q=(1,2,3)) is the original target
fitting in the spurion sector.

## Disposition

Progressive typing repair but negative selector result. Charged spurions can
make the incidence equivariant, yet they introduce a two-dimensional vacuum-
alignment fiber, change the virtual index, and break the holonomy group to the
trivial stabilizer. A source-derived spurion potential and defect/index
transport theorem are required before WP846 becomes physical.
