# CMS `P_det` response calibration

Owner: `marici.Figueiredo`.

## Empirical record

WP235 imports the full 100,000-event CMS Run2010B derived dimuon record from
CERN Open Data record 700. The local Adler-32 checksum `19050705` matches the
portal metadata. The record carries DOI
`10.7483/OPENDATA.CMS.CB8H.MFFA`, run and event identifiers, GeV units, the
CMS instrument identity, parent dataset, license, and the portal's limitations.

The external instrument certificate is CMS-MUO-10-004. It reports a momentum
scale precision of 0.2 percent from Z decays, barrel relative transverse-
momentum resolution of 1.3--2.0 percent, and endcap resolution better than 6
percent for the stated 2010 collision sample.

## Frozen calibration

Events in `70 <= M <= 110 GeV` are divided before fitting:

- even event identifier: calibration fold;
- odd event identifier: held-out evaluation fold.

The 573 supported events split into 312 calibration and 261 evaluation events.
The response model is:

- PDG natural Z width `2.4955 GeV`, frozen rather than detector-fitted;
- Voigt line shape: Breit-Wigner finite width convolved with Gaussian detector
  resolution;
- positive exponential background;
- two-state complex propagator with off-diagonal mass-squared mixing;
- explicit zero-mixing and heavy-mediator decoupling limits.

The calibration fold gives detector Gaussian sigma `1.261610466 GeV` and an
observed-likelihood Hessian on `(log sigma, log background yield)`:

\[
W=\begin{pmatrix}
17.0095 & 6.23825\\
6.23825 & 129.2875
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its eigenvalues are positive (`16.664`, `129.633`) and determinant is
`2160.20`. The held-out Pearson statistic is `41.81` for 40 bins. These numbers
calibrate a detector nuisance metric independently of the held-out evaluation
fold.

## Model completion tests

The executable checker verifies:

- data checksum and run/event support;
- disjoint calibration and evaluation folds;
- positive finite-width and background models;
- normalized resolution convolution;
- exact numerical zero-mixing recovery of the Z propagator;
- monotone heavy-mediator decoupling at masses 500, 1000, and 5000 GeV;
- positive-definite independently calibrated detector metric.

All eleven checks pass.

## Authority boundary

This closes the missing run-data, instrument-certificate, detector-metric, and
width/mixing/decoupling/resolution-model fields. It does **not** close the
source-intervention gate. CMS Run2010B observes collisions generated by one
physical source; it does not independently excite the counterfactual mediator
directions used in WP234. Therefore the result is an empirically calibrated
detector response model, not a source-identifying `P_det` and not a flavor
selector.

The CERN portal also states that this derived educational selection may differ
from CMS physics selections and is unsuitable for a full physics analysis.
That limitation is part of the admitted support.
