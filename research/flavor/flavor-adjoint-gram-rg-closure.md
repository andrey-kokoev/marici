# Adjoint port-Gram RG closure

## Invariant basis under test

For the adjoint triplet define

\[
(G_X)_{ij}=\operatorname{Tr}(X_iX_j).
\]

WP447 contains the radial quartic \((\operatorname{Tr}G_X)^2\) and the
commutator square. After gauging the port group, the exact vector-generator
contraction has support on

\[
(\operatorname{Tr}G_X)^2-\operatorname{Tr}(G_X^2).
\]

In canonical Gell-Mann normalization the component-vector expression is four
times this matrix-normalized combination. The overall factor is irrelevant to
the invariant-support question.

## Exact independence witness

Compare two commuting configurations:

\[
(X_1,X_2,X_3)=(T_3,T_3,0)
\]

and

\[
(X_1,X_2,X_3)=(T_3,T_8,0).
\]

Both have radial invariant one and commutator invariant zero. Their Gram-square
invariants are respectively one and one-half. Therefore
\(\operatorname{Tr}(G_X^2)\) is not a function of the two WP447 quartics.

At the spin-one vacuum, \(G_X=2\mu^2I_3\). The radial and Gram-square values
are \(36\mu^4\) and \(12\mu^4\), leaving a nonzero gauge-supported combination
\(24\mu^4\).

The Gram-square coefficient must therefore run independently. This proves a
further closure requirement, not a full adjoint invariant census. Independent
single-trace adjoint quartics and cyclic-row connector invariants remain to be
classified before recomputing the vacuum and thresholds.
