## Frozen rival family

2. the WP127 single auxiliary-adjoint constructor, supplemented by the same

this hostile audit they are deliberately matched to the identical packet

\[

The exact response matrix has

\[

\qquad

\[

This is the Deutschian failure. WP128 can be varied into inequivalent source
stories without changing any currently admitted flavor record. Its success is
architectural, not yet explanatory identification.

## Complementary threshold family

The union source grammar suggests a formal spectral family: resolve a
two-adjoint pole sector, a single auxiliary pole sector, and a contact/no-pole
sector. Their incidence columns are independent, giving

\[
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}R_{\rm threshold}=3,
\qquad \ker R_{\rm threshold}=0.
\]

This is algebraic separation only. The ports require energies, couplings, and
detectors capable of resolving the mediator spectrum. They are not operations
of the original low-energy flavor experiment. Adding them creates a new
relational threshold experiment over the stabilizer groupoid of its calibrated
source and detector. It does not reveal hidden absolute information in the
original flavor readout.

Moreover, choosing one bespoke port per known rival risks target-fitted
discrimination. To carry identification authority, threshold spectroscopy
must be independently derived as a complete probe family from a frozen source
grammar before rival responses are inspected.
