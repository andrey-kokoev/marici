# Positive noncollinear entrance vacuum

## Scoped source

Work first on the neutral CP-even row-order-parameter subspace of the two
entrance triplets. Let (u,d\in\mathbb R^3), and declare

\[
V=\lambda_u(u\mathbin\cdot u-a^2)^2
+\lambda_d(d\mathbin\cdot d-b^2)^2
+\kappa(u\mathbin\cdot d)^2,
\]

with (a,b,\lambda_u,\lambda_d,\kappa>0). This is a positive renormalizable
scalar potential on the declared subspace.

## Global zero and gauge orbit

Every configuration satisfying

\[
u\mathbin\cdot u=a^2,
\qquad d\mathbin\cdot d=b^2,
\qquad u\mathbin\cdot d=0
\]

is a global zero. Choose the representative

\[
u=(0,0,a)^T,
\qquad d=(b,0,0)^T.
\]

The vectors are nonzero and noncollinear, so they meet WP502's exact condition
for removing the continuous diagonal gauge stabilizer.

The six-dimensional Hessian has rank three. Its three zero modes are exactly
the simultaneous row-rotation tangents and are removed by the gauged row
action. No additional flat direction remains on this scoped field space.

## Physical row-sector masses

The three modes transverse to the gauge orbit are two radial modes and one
relative-angle mode. Their exact masses squared are

\[
m_u^2=8\lambda_u a^2,
\qquad
m_d^2=8\lambda_d b^2,
\qquad
m_\theta^2=2\kappa(a^2+b^2).
\]

They are strictly positive throughout the declared coefficient domain.

## Authority boundary

The source rigidifies the relative angle to orthogonality. It does not select
the two norms, their ratio, or a flavor clock: (a) and (b) are independent
source inputs.

This is not yet a complete electroweak vacuum theorem. The full complex
doublets contain charged and CP-odd directions, and all symmetry-allowed
invariants, gauge mixing, and Yukawa corrections must be included before the
three masses above can be transported into physical pole residues or widths.
