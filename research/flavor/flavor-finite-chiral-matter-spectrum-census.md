# Finite Chiral Matter-Spectrum Census

## Question

Does the admitted generalized Georgi–Glashow integer matter grammar contain a
unique controlled fixed-point packet that can be sourced by WP820's oriented
charge incidence?

## Frozen census domain

The source grammar and truncation are inherited unchanged from WP803–WP805:
the generalized Georgi–Glashow action with both scalar sectors and the three
Yukawa interactions \((a_H,a_M,a_1)\), evaluated at two-loop gauge and
one-loop Yukawa order.

The first finite census is frozen to

\[
5\leq N\leq12,
\qquad
1\leq p\leq8N,
\]

with integer \(N,p\). This contains 544 packets. The bound is computational
scope, not a physical theorem, and no conclusion is extrapolated outside it.
Every packet cancels the Georgi–Glashow gauge anomaly exactly.

## Exact fixed-point census

For each nonsingular integer packet, the three nonzero Yukawa fixed-flow
equations were solved exactly and substituted into the gauge beta function.
The census finds:

- 544 nonsingular algebraic packets;
- 4 packets with all four fixed coordinates positive;
- 2 packets with all coordinates below one;
- 0 packets with all coordinates below \(0.1\).

The four positive packets are

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
N&p&\max(a_g,a_H,a_M,a_1)\\
\hline
5&26&34182/5585\\
6&30&32438/214155\\
8&39&55836/231379\\
11&52&223443/139165
\end{array}
\]

and the sub-unit pair is \((6,30)\) and \((8,39)\). WP805's
\((5,26)\) fixed point is reproduced exactly and remains strongly coupled.

The threshold “below one” is only a permissive algebraic screen. The absence
of any packet below \(0.1\) prevents a claim of uniformly weak perturbative
control.

## Stability

Exact characteristic polynomials were constructed for both sub-unit
survivors. Sturm root counting gives one negative and three positive real
stability exponents for each. With \(t=\log\mu\), both have a
three-dimensional infrared-stable tangent and one infrared-repulsive
direction. Neither is a full-dimensional infrared attractor.

Thus the frozen grammar fails uniqueness twice:

1. two sub-unit positive packets survive;
2. neither supplies the global attractive basin demanded by the portal
   objective.

## Missing WP820 interface

More fundamentally, WP820 and this census do not yet inhabit one source
grammar. WP820 specifies the integer complex

\[
Bq=0,\qquad
q=(1,2,3)^T,\qquad
\mathcal A(q)=36,
\]

while the generalized Georgi–Glashow action is indexed by gauge rank \(N\),
vectorlike multiplicity \(p\), representation content, and scalar sectors.
No admitted constructor maps \((B,q,k)\) to \((N,p)\) and those
representations.

Consequently none of the 544 packets has authority to count as a WP820
completion. Numerical resemblance or anomaly cancellation cannot replace the
missing interface.

## Threshold and readout

Changing active matter content changes the beta coefficients and can move or
remove the fixed point, as WP821 showed explicitly. This census contains no
decoupling completion proving that either sub-unit packet survives to the
physical scale. It also supplies no physical16 descent or calibrated detector.

## Aspect germ audit

The spectrum census is a finite source search only inside the generalized
Georgi–Glashow grammar. Its candidate list cannot be composed with the WP820
charge-selector germ because the connecting constructor is undefined.
Therefore candidate counting grants neither source authority nor acquisition
authority. Global basin, threshold completion, quotient descent, and detector
calibration remain open even within the census grammar.

## Classification and falsifiers

The bounded census is negative for a unique unavoidable portal:

- the permissive sub-unit screen has two survivors;
- the strict \(0.1\) screen has none;
- both survivors are saddles rather than full-dimensional infrared
  attractors;
- zero candidates possess a typed WP820 source interface.

The smallest exact falsifiers are the surviving pair \((6,30)\), \((8,39)\)
for uniqueness and the one-negative-exponent count for basin completeness.
The undefined \((B,q,k)\mapsto(N,p,\text{representations})\) constructor is
the authority falsifier.

## Deutschian appraisal

The census changes the explanatory problem. It is not enough that a finite
integer spectrum lands near a weak fixed point. A hard-to-vary source must
derive the spectrum from the same oriented incidence, make the survivor
unique, and explain the one repulsive direction or eliminate it.

The next viable constructor must be a literal chain complex or quiver whose
nodes and arrows simultaneously determine:

- the WP820 primitive charge kernel and inflow;
- gauge and scalar representations;
- multiplicities and Yukawa hyperedges;
- beta-function coefficients;
- the relevant deformation that places the physical trajectory on the fixed
  point's critical surface.

Without that single combinatorial object, spectrum enumeration remains a
search over tunable ultraviolet stories.

## Disposition

Negative bounded census. The admitted integer chiral grammar has two
permissive sub-unit saddles, no uniformly weak packet, no unique survivor, and
no source-authorized interface to WP820.
