1. a bounded field/resource and coefficient class;
2. an independently validated physical implementation model;
3. a physical source producing an ensemble or channel;
4. a normalized quotient measure on the completed UV domain;
5. a covariant recovered-`physical16` certificate;
6. accuracy, UV/IR scale, scheme, time, and resource budgets;
7. a descendant-closed resource-class counterfactual;
8. a genuinely protected prediction not used in construction.

Missing data remain `undefined`; they are never encoded as zero.
