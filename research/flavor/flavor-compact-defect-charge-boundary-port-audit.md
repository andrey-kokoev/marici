# Compact Defect Charge and Boundary-Port Audit

## Question

Can the anomaly-inflow source of WP807 generate its own unique oriented defect,
so that the relative sign becomes an absolute flavor selector without an added
reference port?

## Admitted source domain and quotient

The admitted source is a local mirror-symmetric scalar-wall theory on either a
compact boundaryless one-dimensional transverse space or an interval. Its wall
charge is

\[
Q_i=\frac{\phi_{i+1}-\phi_i}{2v}.
\]

The flavor orientation is the weak-basis invariant

\[
J=\operatorname{Im}\det[H_u,H_d],
\]

not a texture-loop phase. WP807's inflow coupling is proportional to
\(kQ_iJ\), with integer anomaly level \(k\). Literal wall profiles, local
coordinate charts, the full mirror groupoid, its endpoint-order stabilizer,
and equality of inclusive detector records remain distinct equivalence
notions.

## Exact compact obstruction

For a periodic chain, \(\phi_N=\phi_0\), and hence

\[
Q_{\rm tot}
=\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}\frac{\phi_{i+1}-\phi_i}{2v}
=0.
\]

This is independent of the wall potential and of every fitted flavor
coordinate. A kink of charge \(+1\) therefore requires compensating total
antikink charge \(-1\). The corresponding inclusive inflow also cancels:

\[
j_{\rm incl}=k\sum_i Q_i=0.
\]

A local probe can return \(kQ_i\), but choosing the localization patch and its
normal supplies precisely the relational orientation data that the source was
supposed to derive.

## Boundary and noncompact escape hatches

On an interval, ordered endpoint values give

\[
Q=\frac{\phi_R-\phi_L}{2v}.
\]

Thus \((\phi_L,\phi_R)=(-v,+v)\) selects \(Q=+1\), whereas the reversed order
selects \(Q=-1\). For an even scalar potential, the kink and antikink have
identical energy density. The source action therefore does not choose between
the two endpoint orders. Fixing one order restricts the full mirror groupoid
to its stabilizer and defines a new relational experiment.

The noncompact line does not repair this. Values at the two asymptotic ends
are superselection or preparation data. Calling them boundary conditions at
infinity does not make their order source-derived.

The possible escape routes are consequently explicit:

1. add ordered physical boundaries;
2. prepare a nonzero asymptotic topological sector;
3. introduce a genuinely mirror-odd source term;
4. use a global topology whose admitted field bundle has a nonzero index
   without a mirror-completable partner.

Routes 1 and 2 are reference or preparation ports. Route 3 merely relocates
the question to the origin and normalization of its odd coefficient. Only
route 4 could still furnish an intrinsic source principle, and WP793 showed
that ordinary vertical flux retains the mirror pair.

## Selector and instrument classification

The compact source is neither an absolute selector nor a calibrated flavor
instrument. It rigidifies the relative local relation among wall normal,
anomaly level, and \(J\). Ordered endpoints select within the stabilizer
experiment but do not descend to an absolute sign under the full mirror
groupoid. Inclusive readout annihilates the kink--antikink pair. A local
readout additionally requires wall localization, signed detector orientation,
gain calibration, and a map into the faithful `physical16` quotient.

Anomaly matching continues to protect \(k\) under symmetry-preserving RG and
gapped vectorlike thresholds, but it protects a zero inclusive index on the
compact packet. Threshold survival does not create the missing source charge.

## Smallest exact falsifier

The two-link compact packet

\[
(Q_1,Q_2)=(+1,-1)
\]

is the smallest falsifier of the claim that one compact kink supplies an
absolute selector. It has a locally nonzero inflow on either wall but zero
global charge and zero inclusive current. Selecting only the first entry
requires a localization and orientation port.

## Deutschian appraisal

The telescoping theorem is a hard-to-vary explanation of why a closed compact
mirror-symmetric source cannot produce the desired isolated orientation. It is
not yet the positive Deutschian explanation of observed flavor. Such an
explanation must derive a non-mirror-completable global index and must
co-generate its magnitude, RG basin, threshold state, and calibrated
`physical16` readout. Merely stipulating ordered endpoints fails that test.

## Claim boundary and falsifiers

The theorem assumes a single-valued scalar on a compact boundaryless
transverse space and ordinary periodic gluing. It is falsified by an admitted
source bundle with a provably nonzero net index whose mirror is absent from the
same source theory. A local signed current alone is insufficient: the theory
must also derive the localization patch, detector normal, calibration, and
weak-basis-invariant flavor coupling.

Primary physical precedent for defect-localized chirality and anomaly inflow:
Callan and Harvey, [Anomalies and Fermion Zero Modes on Strings and Domain
Walls](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(85)90489-4).

## Disposition

Negative but progressive. Compact defect topology closes the attempted
internalization of WP807's orientation port. The next candidate must carry an
intrinsic non-mirror-completable index rather than a prepared wall order.
