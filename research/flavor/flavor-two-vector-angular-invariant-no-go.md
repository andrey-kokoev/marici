# Two-vector angular invariant no-go: WP707

## Complete renormalizable grammar

For two real triplets under the admitted diagonal (SO(3)), the polynomial
invariants are generated by the Gram entries

\[
a=|n|^2,
\qquad
b=|m|^2,
\qquad
c=n\mathbin\cdot m.
\]

At field degree at most four, independent flips of (n) and (m) remove every
odd power of (c). The complete potential is therefore

\[
V=gamma+mu_n a+mu_m b+lambda_n a^2+lambda_m b^2
+lambda_xab+lambda_cc^2.
\]

This is the same four-quartic support completed in WP661 and evolved in WP662.
There is no omitted renormalizable flip-even angular invariant.

## Angular stiffness theorem

Hold both norms at one and rotate through the orthogonal configuration:

\[
n=e_1,
\qquad
m(\theta)=\cos\theta\,e_2+\sin\theta\,e_1.
\]

Then (a=b=1) and (c=\sin\theta). Every norm-only term is constant, while

\[
\left.\frac{d^2V}{d\theta^2}\right|_{\theta=0}=2\lambda_c.
\]

Consequently (lambda_c=0) implies an exactly flat physical relative angle.
Any renormalizable flip-even source that rigidifies an orthogonal two-triplet
frame must contain the same correlation operator whose transverse RG mode
made the WP704 ray an infrared saddle.

## Reopening classes

The no-go is bounded to two real triplets, diagonal (SO(3)), independent
flips, polynomial locality, and field degree at most four. It can be escaped
only by changing at least one declared ingredient:

- add a new field or relational port;
- relax a flip and admit odd-(c) operators, changing the source groupoid;
- use higher-dimensional angular invariants with an independently derived
  suppression scale;
- use nonlocal or threshold dynamics and retain its generated (c^2) matching;
- find a full-domain RG ray with nonzero (lambda_c).

Adding a port does not recover the old absolute orientation. It defines a new
relational experiment over the stabilizer of that port.

## Disposition

Within the current renormalizable two-triplet grammar, slice protection and
angular rigidification cannot coexist. This upgrades WP706 from an audit of
the obvious product symmetry to a complete invariant-theory obstruction on
the admitted field content.

The smallest exact falsifier is the zero angular second derivative at
(lambda_c=0). The next progressive branch must enlarge the source grammar or
solve for a stable attractive ray with nonzero (lambda_c); another symmetry
label on the same two-vector quartics cannot suffice.
