# Irreducible-vacuum messenger word grammar: WP450

## Question

Does the minimal finite messenger grammar over the WP447 irreducible vacuum select a restricted flavor family, or merely parameterize every Yukawa matrix?

## Frozen grammar

For each quark sector, admit the identity, one-flavon words, and two-flavon words generated by the three vacuum matrices (J_i). A one-flavon effective operator arises from the WP435 one-stage vectorlike messenger chain; a two-flavon word requires a declared two-stage vectorlike chain. No word of degree above two is admitted.

\[
I,\ J_1,\ J_2,\ J_3,\ J_1^2,\ J_1J_2,\ J_1J_3,\ J_2^2,\ J_2J_3.
\]

Each sector has one complex coefficient per basis word.

## Exact result

The identity-plus-linear grammar has complex matrix rank four. Adding degree-two words raises the rank to nine, and the displayed nine-word coefficient map is invertible. It therefore spans all of (M_3(\mathbb C)).

As a hostile witness, the matrix unit (E_{13}), forbidden to the linear span, has the exact degree-two reconstruction

\[
E_{13}=\frac12J_3+\frac12J_1^2+iJ_1J_2-\frac12J_2^2.
\]

Thus WP447 repairs WP446's support obstruction with finite messenger depth two.

## Selector disposition

The result is capacity, not selection. Nine arbitrary complex coefficients per Yukawa give a bijective coordinate system on literal complex matrices. They can reproduce any desired fitted answer, but the source action supplies no law choosing those coefficients or their relation between up and down sectors.

Consequently the grammar is a universal carrier and presentation rigidifier. It does not select a proper `physical16` family, a CKM value, (g_Ff/v), or a mass-basis flavor-current orientation. Attaching experimental current bounds after fitting these coefficients would constrain a chosen model point, not validate a source prediction.

## Instrument and falsifier

No instrument is attached. The smallest exact falsifier is one complex three-by-three matrix outside the degree-two word span or a nonzero null vector of the frozen coefficient map.

A progressive successor needs an independently motivated symmetry or dynamics that reduces and selects the messenger coefficient family before comparison with flavor data.
