# Threshold-intertwiner no-go: WP1182

## Question

Can threshold transport identify the sector basis and production
normalization?

## DPC resolution

- **Conjecture:** the conditional transient interface determines a threshold
  intertwiner.
- **Rivals:** sector-basis selection; unitary orbit of \(A\); epsilon
  amplitude interval; sourced threshold transport.
- **Risky consequences:** \(\rho_{\rm dim}=I_{23}/23\) is \(U(23)\)
  invariant; \(A=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0,\ldots,0)\) has an
  86-dimensional conjugation orbit; epsilon has one amplitude dimension.
- **Falsification attempt:** every conjugated \(UAU^*\) and every epsilon
  below \(1/23\) gives a valid conditional interface, while no sourced
  intertwiner exists.
- **Residual:** a physical threshold packet must supply sector basis and
  amplitude scale.
- **Disposition:** reject threshold-intertwiner identifiability.
