# Aspect Retest After the Co-moving and Mass-Constructor Repairs

## Question

How much of WP880's six-dimensional diagnostic kernel has actually been
removed by WP881--WP890?

## Exact status change

subsequent packets change only one direction from unresolved to separated:

- WP882 supplies the source-derived co-moving connection on smooth ordered
  projector histories, detecting the formerly frozen-frame hostile.

The source observation rank therefore increases from six to seven, and the
declared kernel dimension decreases from six to five.

The other advances are constructors and classifications, not selector rows:

  could detect common gain, but no such acquisition cell exists.

  retains mass and finite-term fibers.

  not select their scales or Yukawas.
- WP888--WP890 distinguish packet-rank zeros and their topology, but do not
  supply the complementary detector output needed to distinguish packet zeros
  from transmission zeros in an experiment.

## Remaining five-dimensional kernel

On the WP880 mutation carrier, the unresolved directions are now:

1. common portal gain;
2. numerical mass spectrum and finite threshold jump;
3. calibrated detector-rank collapse;
4. selected-port transmission zero versus source-packet zero;
5. end-to-end source/RG/threshold/readout coherence.

The smooth moving-frame direction is no longer in this list. At threshold
discontinuities, matching remains conditional, but that ambiguity is included
in the numerical threshold direction rather than counted twice.

## Selector verdict

row. It still has no genuine source-generated numerical selector on
`physical16`. The texture/Hodge sector rigidifies presentations and transport;
the completion sector makes thresholds calculable; neither selects the
observed flavor point.

The smallest surviving hostile remains the common-gain pair (g=1,2) for
normalized observations. The smallest source-side mass hostile is the pair of
full-rank WP888 witnesses with different scale-free spectra.

## Next physical gate

capable of advancing the tester must combine:
