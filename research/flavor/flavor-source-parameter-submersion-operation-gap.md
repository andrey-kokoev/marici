# Source-parameter submersion and operation gap: WP1000

## Question

Does the WP977 mediator source already contain two independent directions that
map onto the effective energy coefficients ((q,k)), and if so does that solve
the actuator problem?

## Source map

Write (a=m_A^2>0), (c=m_s^2>0), and freeze the nonzero vertices
(mu,gamma). Leading elimination gives

\[
q=\frac{\mu^2}{2a},
\qquad
k=\frac{\gamma^2\mu^6}{2ca^6}.
\]

In logarithmic source coordinates ((\alpha,\chi)=(\log a,\log c)), the
logarithmic response matrix is constant:

\[
J_{\log}=
\frac{\partial(\log q,\log k)}{\partial(\alpha,\chi)}
=\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\-6&-1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \det J_{\log}=1.
\]

Thus the WP977 parameter-to-coefficient map is a local submersion everywhere
on its positive leading-elimination domain. Algebraic lack of a second source
direction is not the obstruction.

## Exact global inversion

For fixed positive (mu,gamma), every positive target ((q,k)) has the
unique positive preimage

\[
a=\frac{\mu^2}{2q},
\qquad
c=\frac{32\gamma^2 q^6}{\mu^6 k}.
\]

This recovers WP980's full positive coefficient ray and strengthens it to the
two-dimensional positive quadrant when both mediator masses vary.

## Operation gap

The submersion compares distinct source Lagrangians. A tangent in theory-
parameter space is not an executable transformation of a fixed physical
flavor substrate. Promoting ((\alpha,\chi)) to controls requires new dynamical
mass-setting fields or laboratory knobs, a common preparation/reset protocol,
and calibrated work or resource costs. None is declared by WP977-WP999.

Consequently the formal control pair ((u,v)) can be pulled back
algebraically to mediator-mass choices, but the pullback has no physical
actuator authority. This is the precise first nonfaithful arrow:

source-parameter choice to repeatable within-experiment intervention.

## Descent and instrument

The coefficients (q,k) multiply weak-basis invariants, so their source map is
presentation independent at the effective-action level. That descent does not
turn parameter replacement into an instrument. The leading matching also
requires a controlled threshold domain; finite-mass corrections and
uncertainties have not been propagated into an actuator Jacobian.

## Classification

The mediator family is an algebraically complete coefficient constructor. It
is neither a selector nor a rigidifier because its positive image is the full
admissible quadrant. It is not an executable control instrument.

## Smallest exact falsifier

The benchmark (mu=gamma=a=c=1) has response determinant one in logarithmic
coordinates, while no declared operation changes either mass inside a fixed
experiment. It simultaneously falsifies “the source map has rank one” and
“full source-map rank implies physical controllability.”

## Claim boundary

WP1000 concerns the leading-elimination coefficient map. It does not establish
global coupled-vacuum control, finite-threshold accuracy, dynamical mass knobs,
or detector calibration. It does not treat variation between Lagrangians as
physical time evolution.

## Disposition

Retain the full-rank source map as a capacity theorem. Keep actuator authority
closed until the mediator masses arise from named dynamical substrates with
repeatable, calibrated interventions and a source-derived cost.
