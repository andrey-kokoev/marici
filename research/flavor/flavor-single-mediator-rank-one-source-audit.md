# Single-Mediator Rank-One Source Audit

## Question

Can a unique isolated mediator supply the source authority missing from WP817,
and does it also fix the asymmetric portal's sign, magnitude, RG basin,
threshold survival, and physical readout?

## Source action and exact residue

Let one real mediator \(X\) of mass \(M\) couple linearly to three independently
marked real flavor operators:

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{int}}=X\sum_{i=1}^3 g_i O_i.
\]

At its isolated one-particle pole, the residue matrix and tree-level Wilson
matrix are

\[
R_{ij}=g_i g_j,
\qquad
C_{ij}=\frac{g_i g_j}{M^2}.
\]

This is not an assumed purity condition. A single nondegenerate intermediate
state derives \(R=gg^T\) and hence rank one. If all three couplings are nonzero,

\[
R_{12}R_{23}R_{31}=(g_1g_2g_3)^2>0.
\]

The isolated-mediator source therefore makes WP817's positive loop character
unavoidable and fixes every squared normalized cross residue to one.

## What the source does not select

Single-mediator factorization does not select the coupling ray. The vectors

\[
g=(1,2,3)^T,
\qquad
h=(1,3,2)^T
\]

have the same norm and positive loop character but generate different Wilson
matrices. Nor does the source fix an absolute scale. At fixed \(M\), replacing
\(g\) by \(\lambda g\) scales \(C\) by \(\lambda^2\). Simultaneously replacing
\((g,M)\) by \((\lambda g,\lambda M)\) leaves the complete low-energy Wilson
matrix unchanged, giving an exact source-identification kernel.

This constructor derives purity and loop sign but still permits tuning the
portal direction and magnitude. It does not yet explain a particular value of
\(g_n-g_m\).

## RG basin

For diagonal multiplicative running,

\[
g_i(t)=e^{\gamma_i t}g_i(0),
\]

the residue remains an outer product and its nonzero triangle product stays
positive. However,

\[
\frac{g_1(t)}{g_2(t)}
=e^{(\gamma_1-\gamma_2)t}\frac{g_1(0)}{g_2(0)}.
\]

The rank-one manifold is RG invariant, but it is not a unique basin. Equal
anomalous dimensions preserve every initial ray; unequal dimensions retain
continuous dependence on initial data at finite scale. A genuine basin
selector requires a source-derived nonlinear flow with a unique attractive
projective fixed point and a frozen physical normalization.

## Threshold survival

Positive diagonal matching preserves factorization and the positive loop.
More general real congruence also preserves rank-one positivity while the
isolated state remains the only channel. The decisive condition is spectral
exclusivity. Once additional unresolved mediators or contact terms contribute,
the effective matrix becomes a sum of outer products. The positive-definite
hostile matrix

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1/4&-1/4\\
1/4&1&1/4\\
-1/4&1/4&1
\end{pmatrix}
\]

has negative triangle product. Threshold survival therefore requires a
source-protected one-channel spectral sector or a theorem excluding competing
residues, not merely positivity of the completed effective action.

## Physical readout

Three independently calibrated cross channels would preserve the loop sign.
Without their gains, the detector map has rank three on six
source-plus-calibration variables and hence a three-dimensional kernel. No
current constructor maps the mediator couplings to a weak-basis-invariant
physical16 coordinate or supplies those calibrated channels. Formal Wilson
coefficients are not themselves an admitted instrument.

## Aspect germ audit

The minimal source object is quaternary. It contains the mediator \(X\), the
three operators \(O_1,O_2,O_3\), the primitive attachments \(g_1,g_2,g_3\),
isolated-pole provenance, coupling-ray authority, absolute normalization,
projective RG basin, threshold exclusivity, physical16 descent, and detector
calibration.

The mediator and three operator germs must coexist before contraction; the
three couplings cannot be recovered from endpoint labels. Native arity,
primitive attachments, and purity authority pass because the isolated pole
derives the outer product. Coupling-ray authority, absolute scale, RG basin,
threshold exclusivity, quotient descent, and physical instrument remain open.
Aspect's acquisition deferral therefore remains correct.

## Classification and smallest falsifiers

The single isolated mediator is a genuine source-derived sign and normalized-
magnitude selector on its marked residue domain. It is neither a numerical
portal selector nor a complete physical selector.

The smallest falsifiers are:

1. the equal-norm coupling rays \((1,2,3)\) and \((1,3,2)\), which produce
   distinct portal matrices;
2. the exact rescaling \((g,M)\mapsto(\lambda g,\lambda M)\), which leaves the
   low-energy record unchanged;
3. the positive-definite three-channel matrix above, which shows that
   unresolved additional mediators can reverse the loop character;
4. two initial coupling rays under multiplicative RG, which never become a
   source-selected finite-scale ray;
5. the uncalibrated source-gain detector kernel.

## Deutschian appraisal

The explanation has improved: positive loop sign is no longer a postulated
Gram property. It follows from a single intermediate physical state coupling
to all three operators. But “one mediator” explains only why the portal is
rank one and orientation-consistent. It does not explain why that mediator has
the observed coupling vector, why its scale is fixed, why other channels are
absent, or why the detector realizes the same quotient coordinate.

The successor must make the mediator coupling vector itself the unique
normalized invariant tensor of a source representation, and must derive a
projective RG attractor, a spectral exclusivity rule, and the calibrated
physical16 attachment from that same representation rather than adding them
as independent conditions.

## Disposition

Progressive source derivation, incomplete objective. The single isolated
mediator closes WP817's purity-authority gate but leaves six full-objective
gates open.
