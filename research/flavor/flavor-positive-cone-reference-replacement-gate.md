# Positive-cone reference replacement gate: WP1004

## Question

What exact conditions must a replacement for WP991's unreachable commuting
reference satisfy inside the positive mediator cone?

## Two independent gates

Let three source configurations have invariant score points
(z_i=(K_i,P_i)), with energy (E_i=c-QK_i-RP_i). Relative-energy records
reconstruct ((Q,R)) exactly iff

\[
\det(z_1-z_0,z_2-z_0)\ne0.
\]

This affine-independence test is not enough for preparation. Configuration
(i) is a unique minimum for some positive source coefficients iff (z_i) is
a strictly exposed point of the finite score set for a normal ((Q,R)) in the
positive quadrant. Both gates must pass.

## Existing endpoints and a capacity witness

Retain the certified WP978 endpoints

\[
A=(2,0),\qquad B=(2/9,2/27783).
\]

The synthetic rational point

\[
C=(1,1/20000)
\]

is not asserted to arise from flavor matrices. It is a capacity witness for
the acceptance test. The three points are affinely independent. As the
positive ratio (r=R/Q) increases, (A) is uniquely exposed at (r=1),
(C) at (r=25000), and (B) at (r=100000). Thus a positive-cone
three-preparation instrument is geometrically possible.

By contrast, any third point on the segment joining (A) and (B) fails the
response determinant and is never a strict new exposed vertex.

## Physical gate

The missing object is now sharply localized: construct an actual
weak-basis-invariant flavor configuration whose ((K,P)) score lies above the
chord (AB), prove it is a stable unique minimum for an open positive
coefficient interval, and supply preparation/reset and relative-energy
calibration. A fitted or synthetic score pair has no source authority.

## Classification

WP1004 is an exact instrument-design criterion and a formal capacity theorem.
It is neither a selector nor a rigidifier and provides no physical instrument.

## Smallest exact falsifier

The midpoint ((A+B)/2) is the smallest rational hostile construction: it is
distinct from both endpoints but collinear, so the relative-response
determinant is zero and no positive normal exposes it uniquely.

## Claim boundary

The packet treats a finite three-point score set. It does not prove global
minimality against every flavor configuration, nor that the synthetic point is
realizable by Hermitian coefficient fields.

## Disposition

Keep the signed-(Q) extension unnecessary in principle but not yet avoided in
the actual flavor source. The next search is for a source-realizable third
score point passing both the affine and positive-exposure gates.
