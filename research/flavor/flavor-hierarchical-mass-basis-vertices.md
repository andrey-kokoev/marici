## Corrected authority

two-vector threshold with a nonzero mass-basis gauge vertex. Its witness uses

\[
g_F=\sqrt{2},\quad g_P=\frac{1}{10},\quad
g_E=\frac{1}{50},\quad \mu=1\ {\rm GeV},\quad s=16\ {\rm GeV},
\]

\[
\frac{a}{b}=400,\qquad 2(a^2+b^2)=(246\ {\rm GeV})^2.

licensed as \(B_s\)-compatible. It remains a valid source-model existence

\[

A channel is retained only if its threshold margin is positive and its
transported coupling exceeds \(10^{-7}\).
