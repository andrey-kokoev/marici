# Transient sector-interface gate: WP1181

## Question

Can a finite-time portal transient define a sector interface?

## DPC resolution

- **Conjecture:** the finite-time dark-state transient can drive a
  sector-space interface.
- **Rivals:** stationary replacement map; conditional CPTP channel
  composition; time-dependent sector polarization; source threshold
  intertwiner.
- **Risky consequences:** the vacuum transient contributes
  \(e^{-\kappa t}\), producing the conditional sector output
  \(\rho_{\rm dim}+\epsilon e^{-\kappa t}A\) with Choi margin \(77/2300\).
- **Falsification attempt:** the composition is CPTP and time dependent, but
  no source-derived threshold intertwiner selects the channel or sector
  basis.
- **Residual:** threshold transport must identify the sector basis, epsilon,
  and production normalization.
- **Disposition:** construct the conditional transient interface; reject
  sourced threshold realization.
