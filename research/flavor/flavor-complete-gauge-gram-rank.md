\[

## Exact support and kernel

The complete tangent Gram has rank seventeen and nullity one. Its
flavor–port–row block has rank fourteen. The electroweak block has rank three
and equals

\[
{a^2+b^2\over4}
\begin{pmatrix}
g_2^2&0&0&0\\
0&g_2^2&0&0\\
0&0&g_2^2&-g_2g_Y\\
0&0&-g_2g_Y&g_Y^2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

At the real neutral vacuum, the flavor–port–row and electroweak cross block
vanishes exactly. The unique null generator is

\[
{T_3\over g_2}+{Y\over g_Y},
\]

which is the unbroken electromagnetic direction. Thus seventeen gauge
directions are supported by the declared vacuum and the photon is the only
massless gauge field.
