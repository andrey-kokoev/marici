# Global selector source-ratio no-go: WP1010

## Question

Does the strongest currently admitted determinant-mediator source grammar
derive the exact coefficient ratio that makes WP1009 a global selector?

## Matching map

The WP977 renormalizable mediator chain produces

\[
Q=\frac{\mu^2}{2a},
\qquad
R=\frac{\gamma^2\mu^6}{2ca^6},
\]

where a and c are the positive adjoint and pseudoscalar squared masses.
Consequently

\[
\rho=\frac RQ
=\frac{\gamma^2\mu^4}{ca^5}.
\]

The WP1009 orbit requires

\[
\rho_\star=\frac{44376}{275}.
\]

All displayed quantities are source parameters or invariant coefficient
ratios. No physical time is introduced.

## Exact surviving source orbit

For every positive h, the transformation

\[
c\longmapsto hc
\]

preserves:

- the mediator field content and weak-basis representation;
- power-counting renormalizability;
- positivity of both auxiliary masses;
- the strict quartic coercivity condition, which does not involve c;
- the existence of the same controlled low-field elimination.

It acts on the score ratio as

\[
\rho\longmapsto\frac{\rho}{h}.
\]

This action is simply transitive on the complete positive ratio ray. Choosing
c so that rho equals rho-star is therefore a parameter fit, not a consequence
of the admitted source grammar.

## Selector consequence

WP1009 proves that a frozen rho-star globally selects one normalized Hermitian
coefficient orbit. WP1010 proves that the present source family does not
select rho-star: it admits every positive ratio while preserving all declared
source constraints.

The two claims are compatible:

\[
\text{conditional global selector}
\ne
\text{source-generated numerical selector}.
\]

At h=2, the source remains admitted but rho becomes rho-star/2. The WP1009
witness then has a nonzero score gradient and is no longer selected.

## Classification

The current determinant-mediator architecture is a source-capable actuator
and conditional global selector family. It is not a numerical source selector
and not a presentation rigidifier. It descends under the full weak-basis
group, but descent alone does not remove its positive coefficient fiber.

## Smallest exact falsifier

A source-derived relation involving c that is not invariant under the
positive rescaling, fixes rho independently of the desired orbit, and survives
the coupled-vacuum and threshold gates would falsify this no-go.

Deleting the c-rescaling freedom only by calibrating against the selected
flavor answer does not count as a falsifier.

## Claim boundary

This no-go applies to the currently declared WP977/WP1001 source grammar and
its stated positivity/coercivity constraints. It does not exclude a larger
theory with a symmetry, anomaly condition, quantized topological coefficient,
or independently solved vacuum relation that fixes the ratio.

## Disposition

Close the present source-ratio branch negatively. The next admissible
constructor must break the rescaling orbit upstream of flavor fitting and
derive the resulting dimensionless ratio before applying the WP1009 readout.
