## Frozen closure probe

A single rational \(U(1)\) holonomy \(1/n\in\mathbb R/\mathbb Z\) generates a
cyclic subgroup of order \(n\), exactly like a primitive \(\mathbb Z_n\)
defect. Adding only its rational powers changes nothing:

\[
\langle 1/n,2/n\rangle\cong\mathbb Z_n.
\]

If the continuous source independently supplies a complementary irrational
holonomy \(\alpha\), then

\[
\langle 1/n,\alpha\rangle
\]

is infinite, whereas every subgroup of \(\mathbb Z_n\) remains finite. Define
the topological closure capability

\[
C=
\begin{cases}
0,&\text{finite discrete closure},\\
1,&\text{source-authorized infinite continuous closure}.
\end{cases}
\]

Unlike WP164's susceptibility, this ideal capability does not decay with a
mediator mass.

## Exact response ranks

The return tower alone has rank four on WP161's seven sources. Adding \(C\)
separates every discrete/continuous point pair but raises mixture rank only to
five. As in WP162, append the order-conditioned closure records

\[
nC,
\qquad n^2C.
\]

The ranks become

\[
4\to5\to6\to7.
\]

Thus the complete closure tower is jointly faithful on the frozen seven-source
coefficient packet and, at the algebraic topological level, evades WP165's
decoupling theorem.

## Authority gate

The complement exists only relative to the admitted source-generated port
family. If a continuous source supplies only the rational holonomy \(1/n\) and
operations within its cyclic subgroup, its closure is order \(n\) and remains
exactly equivalent to \(\mathbb Z_n\). Importing an arbitrary irrational
holonomy because it exists in the ambient mathematical group would confuse
algebraic span with executable control.

The reference port therefore changes the physical groupoid. It defines a new
relational experiment over the stabilizer of the original rational holonomy;
it does not reveal an absolute property through the original one-port setup.

## Smallest exact falsifier

Restrict the \(U(1)\) source to \(\{k/n:k\in\mathbb Z\}\). Then its one- and
two-port rational closures both have order \(n\), exactly matching
\(\mathbb Z_n\). Without the independently supplied irrational port, the
claimed complement does not exist physically.

## Remaining instrument gate

many group elements. The operational stopping rule, error rate, and resource
bound must be stated. A formal declaration that the ambient group is
continuous is not a measurement.
