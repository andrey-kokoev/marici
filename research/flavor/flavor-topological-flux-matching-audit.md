# Topological flux-matching audit (WP144)

Owner: `marici.Figueiredo`.

## Bounded question

Can a topological matching law derive WP143's coefficient ratio before flavor
and detector readout, rather than inserting the selected flux integer by hand?

Pre-objective process report: excitement `9/10`, confidence `9/10` that fixed
topology derives the ratio but relocates selection to the geometry class,
expected information gain `9/10`. Confounds are the toy `chi/24` contract and
absence of a microscopic compactification. These reports are non-evidential.

Freeze the source contract

\[
N=\frac{\chi(X)}{24}\in\mathbb Z,
\qquad h=2\alpha N,
\]

where `chi(X)` is a presentation-invariant topological characteristic of the
compact source geometry. This is a declared bounded matching model, not a
universal compactification theorem.

Substitution into WP143 gives

\[
E_X(n)=\alpha n^2-2\alpha Nn
=\alpha(n-N)^2-\alpha N^2.
\]

For every fixed geometry the unique integer ground sector is therefore

\[
n=N=\frac{\chi(X)}{24}.
\]

At `alpha=1,chi=96`, the source derives `N=4` and `h=8`; no detector datum is
used in that algebra. The selected sector has WP143's accessible threshold
`1/2`. The coefficient relation descends under changes of triangulation or
coordinate presentation because `chi(X)` is topological.

## Hostile topology pair

The equally admissible source geometry `chi=24` derives `N=1,h=2`, selecting
the inaccessible sector `n=1`. The two geometries are not presentation charts
of one physical source; they are distinct topology classes.

Thus WP144 genuinely derives the coefficient ratio **within a fixed
geometry**, improving WP143. It does not explain why the `chi=96` geometry is
realized. The selector label has moved from a free coupling to a discrete
topological datum.

Classification: **topology-conditional numerical sector selector; no topology
selector**. This is more rigid than coefficient fitting because continuous
variation of `h/alpha` is removed inside the geometry, but the topology fiber
remains non-singleton.

The smallest authority falsifier is the pair `chi=96,24`, producing accessible
`n=4` and inaccessible `n=1`. The remaining gate is an independently
normalized topology-preparation law or a source theorem admitting one
geometry, plus equivariant flavor matching and an executable multi-point
instrument. Choosing the topology after inspecting detector reach is still
target fitting.
