# WP522 H0 instrument census

## Bounded question

Does an existing primary-source neutral-B instrument instantiate the direct
source-shaped functional required by WP522,

\[
H_0[\mu]=\int \frac{d\mu(q^2)}{D(q^2)}?
\]

This packet freezes a bounded primary-source census dated 2026-08-26. It is
not a global literature-absence theorem.

## Neutral-B instruments found

The neutral-B lattice sources in the census report matrix elements or bag
parameters for a finite basis of local mixing operators. This is explicit in
[Dowdall et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.01025),
[Bazavov et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.03560),
[Chang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.6820), and
[Boyle et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.11287).

Those are valid instruments for the local WET packet used in WP511. They do
not by themselves evaluate the finite-propagator convolution in WP520 or its
compressed WP522 coordinate \(H_0\). Replacing the propagator by a local
coefficient would erase the momentum dependence that WP519 required us to
retain.

## Bilocal precedent found

The required measurement architecture is not alien to lattice field theory.
[Christ](https://arxiv.org/abs/1012.6034) constructs space-time integrated
products of two weak operators with subtraction and finite-volume control,
and [Christ et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.2065) develop the corresponding
long-distance kaon-mixing calculation.

This establishes a technical precedent in the kaon sector. It is not
calibrated \(B_s\) data and does not authorize importing a kaon matrix element
into the WP520 likelihood.

## Exact interface still required

An executable WP522 instrument must provide one common-frame calculation with:

- a Euclidean integrated \(B_s\) correlator weighted by the exact frozen
  \(D^{-1}\) kernel;
- nonperturbative renormalization and coincident-point subtraction;
- finite-volume and continuum limits;
- controlled heavy-quark systematics;
- matching across the light-mediator thresholds rather than a local WET
  substitution;
- the normalization and covariance needed by the measured
  \(\Delta M_s\) likelihood.

Until that interface exists, the source model supplies the kernel and the
local lattice programme supplies a different calibrated readout, but there is
no admitted parallelization between them.

## Disposition

- Admitted domain: the frozen WP520 aligned finite-propagator \(B_s\) kernel.
- Faithful coordinate: the WP522 direct resolvent functional \(H_0\), not a
  finite ordinary-moment projection.
- Source-authorized probe: the single frozen \(D^{-1}\)-weighted functional.
- Contextual classification: neither selector nor rigidifier; it is an exact
  source-shaped readout target without a realized physical instrument.
- Smallest falsifier: one primary neutral-B calculation in the frozen census
  explicitly reports \(H_0\) with normalization, renormalization, continuum
  control, and covariance.
- Remaining gate: adapt the established bilocal integrated-correlator method
  to \(B_s\) with the exact WP522 kernel and threshold matching.

The generated result records every censused source and checks this bounded
classification mechanically. A future source meeting the falsifier defeats
the census conclusion without revising WP522's algebra.
