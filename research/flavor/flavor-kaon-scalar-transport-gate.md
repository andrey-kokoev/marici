# Kaon-to-scalar transport gate (WP411)

## Hostile typing question

Does the successful neutral-kaon material experiment physically instantiate
WP400's common shift of scalar curvature and tadpole?

No. The two operations have different types.

WP400 has scalar potential derivative

\[
\frac{dV}{dA}=HA-N.
\]

For $N\ne0$, the origin is not stationary. By contrast, neutral-kaon matter
transport has homogeneous evolution

\[
i\frac{d\psi}{dt}=H_K\psi.
\]

Its zero state is fixed, including after every invertible linear change of
basis. This fixed-origin property is invariant under linear conjugacy. Therefore
no identification of the two real components
$(\operatorname{Re}\Delta f,\operatorname{Im}\Delta f)$ with $(H,N)$ can carry
physical authority from the kaon experiment to the scalar-tadpole operation.

An affine scalar flow can be written homogeneously only by augmenting the state
with a constant coordinate,

\[
\begin{pmatrix}H&-N\\0&0\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}A\\1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

That constant coordinate is a reference/source port. It changes the admitted
state domain and physical groupoid; it cannot be smuggled in as a coordinate
renaming.

## Disposition

WP406--WP410 remain valid results about a real, executable, independently
predicted flavor intervention. WP410 supplies a genuine zero-refit withheld
complex displacement. But this branch does not complete the original scalar
mediator objective: it replaces curvature/tadpole shifts by
dispersive/absorptive propagation.

The missing constructor remains a real scalar flavor mediator with a physical
operation that changes both its curvature and a genuine affine source term, or
an explicitly admitted reference port that realizes the affine augmentation.
Such a port defines a new relational experiment over its stabilizer groupoid.
