# Source-modulus selection gate: WP1210

## Question

Can the \(q,z\) source moduli be selected from one current flavor source
packet?

## DPC resolution

- **Problem:** derive the \(q,z\) source moduli rather than retaining them
  as colligation parameters.
- **Bold conjecture:** a primitive nondegenerate self-dual boundary pairing
  \(\Delta_Q g g_D=1\) selects the common source modulus.
- **Named rivals:** pairing level \(n=2\); pairing-preserving but
  duality-breaking threshold; removed dual port; joint observer; monodromic
  \(G_2\) pair; rank-one unimodular lattice.
- **Risky consequences:** zero coupling is excluded; the unique positive
  self-dual solution is \(g=g_D=1/\sqrt2\) for \(\Delta_Q=2\); the entire
  positive pairing locus flows to it; protected thresholds must preserve
  pairing and electric-dual exchange.
- **Strongest falsification attempt:** level two moves the selected value,
  \(\lambda=2\) breaks self-duality, removing the dual port restores the
  zero cusp, and all three audited flavor candidates fail.
- **Exact residual:** construct a microscopic chiral flavor dual pair
  satisfying pairing, threshold, RG, and calibrated physical16 gates.
- **Disposition:** refute current-corpus modulus selection; require flavor
  dual-pair existence.
