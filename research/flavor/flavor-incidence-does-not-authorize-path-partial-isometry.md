# Incidence Does Not Authorize the Path Partial Isometry

## Question

Does the already admitted WP820 incidence and oriented inflow derive WP852's
finite-path partial-isometry constructor, or does WP852 add new source data?

## Forward construction

The path number operator has positive-grade spectrum

\[
q=(1,2,3)^T.
\]

Its primitive integer relations may be presented by

\[
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
2&-1&0\\
3&0&-1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad Bq=0.
\]

Thus a marked finite path with its vacuum, number operator, and normalized
creation operator can generate the WP820 charge ray and incidence relations.
The oriented cubic record is likewise (1^3+2^3+3^3=36).

## Reverse-interface hostile

Fix the same four-level number operator and consider the positive weighted
degree-one family

\[
T(a,b,c)=ae_1e_0^*+be_2e_1^*+ce_3e_2^*,
\qquad a,b,c>0.
\]

Every member obeys ([N,T]=T), has the same cyclic support, induces the same
positive-grade charge vector, the same incidence, and the same cubic inflow.
The WP820 probe family is therefore constant on the entire
((\mathbb R_{>0})^3) weight fiber.

Number-preserving unitaries cannot remove these magnitudes. The spectrum of

\[
T^*T=\operatorname{diag}(a^2,b^2,c^2,0)
\]

is invariant. In particular, (T(1,1,1)) and (T(1,2,1)) have identical
WP820 records but are not unitarily equivalent under the admitted path
groupoid. Only the first satisfies

\[
T^*T=I-P_3.
\]

## First nonfaithful arrow

The relevant factorization is

\[
(N,T,e_0)
\longrightarrow
(q,B,k=36)
\longrightarrow
\text{portal charge contrast}.
\]

The first arrow forgets the three path singular values. WP820's incidence and
inflow can therefore be the image of WP852, but they cannot authorize its
partial-isometry normalization. Polar decomposition does not repair this:
extracting the partial isometry from (T) deliberately discards the positive
singular-value operator that contains the physical magnitude data.

## Selector classification

WP852 remains a genuine selector on the stronger path-source domain, because
the partial-isometry relation reduces the weight fiber to one unitary orbit.
Relative to the currently admitted WP820 source, however, that relation is an
additional axiom. It is not reconstructed by the charge lattice, oriented
anomaly, or incidence kernel.

The smallest exact falsifier is the pair

\[
T(1,1,1),\qquad T(1,2,1).
\]

They are contextually equivalent under all current topological flavor probes
and inequivalent in the faithful path coordinate.

## Consequence for the portal objective

The path candidate cannot yet explain the portal magnitude. Its normalization
is precisely information erased by the known incidence readout. A valid
source principle must derive the partial-isometry relation before incidence
projection, or supply an independent physical probe of (T^*T). Such a probe
must be generated by the same microscopic flavor source and calibrated in the
frame used by `physical16`; a formal singular-value readout is insufficient.
