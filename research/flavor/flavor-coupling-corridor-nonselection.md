# Coupling-corridor nonselection: WP701

## Stability does not select the instrument corridor

On the symmetric radial family, strict stability requires

\[
\lambda>p.
\]

The heavy-to-two-light decay is open only when

\[
5p>3\lambda.
\]

Therefore the WP700 corridor

\[
1<\frac{\lambda}{p}<\frac53
\]

is a proper subset of the stable source domain, not a consequence of
stability alone.

The exact inside witness \(\lambda/p=3/2\) is stable and has open phase space.
The hostile witness \(\lambda/p=2\) is also strictly stable, with mass-squared
spectrum \(\{2,6\}\), but its heavy-to-two-light phase space is closed.

Moreover, the declared grammar still admits \(p=0\) with a positive diagonal
radial Hessian and no portal-branch contrast.

## Classification

Stability supplies compact asymmetry support after a coupling corridor is
declared. It neither selects that corridor nor excludes the zero-portal
stratum. WP700 is therefore a conditional identifier/instrument margin, not a
source selector.

The remaining selector gate is an independent source operation that restricts
\(\lambda/p\) to a proper predictive domain and excludes \(p=0\) before the
cubic contrast can carry unconditional flavor authority.
