`physical16` subfamily with a typed physical realization. The negative result

1. descend under the full weak-basis groupoid;
2. push forward to a positive-codimension `physical16` family;
3. retain its numerical prediction on the complete declared ensemble;
4. place the associated thresholds within a predeclared detector domain;
