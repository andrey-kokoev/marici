# An S3-doublet cubic selects the missing transposition flag up to permutation: WP954

## Question

Can WP953's missing `S3`-to-`Z2` flag be derived from a source action without fitting the observed flavor geometry?

## Standard-doublet source

Represent the real standard doublet as

\[
V=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3:x+y+z=0\},
\]

with `S3` permuting coordinates. Freeze a positive radial normalization

\[
x^2+y^2+z^2=6
\]

and use the lowest odd-degree symmetric invariant

\[
C(x,y,z)=xyz.
\]

The monic cubic with roots `(x,y,z)` is

\[
t^3-3t-xyz.
\]

Its discriminant is

\[
\Delta=108-27(xyz)^2
=
(x-y)^2(y-z)^2(z-x)^2\geq0.
\]

Therefore

\[
-2\leq xyz\leq2.
\]

The minimum orbit is the three permutations of `(1,1,-2)`; the maximum orbit is their negatives.

## Derived flag and coefficient ray

Choose the minimum orbit through a positive coefficient multiplying `C`. At the representative

\[
n=(1,1,-2),
\]

the stabilizer is exactly the `Z2` generated by interchange of the first two projector labels. Inside the standard plane, the line orthogonal to `n` is

\[
n^\perp\cap V
=
\operatorname{span}(1,-1,0).
\]

Thus one source order parameter derives both the marked transposition and WP350's down-sector projective coefficient direction. The democratic up ray remains the full-`S3` invariant line `(1,1,1)`.

The other two minima give the other two transpositions. They form one `S3` orbit, so the action selects a relational flag type rather than an absolute projector label. If simultaneous projector permutations are admitted chart permutations, these vacua are presentation-equivalent; no absolute flavor label is inferred.

## Authority boundary

This is a genuine conditional source selector of the missing flag, not yet a declared flavor source. The construction assumes:

- a real standard-doublet field attached to the projector channels;
- a positive radial normalization;
- a nonzero cubic invariant with independently fixed sign;
- a coupling rule sending the down sector to the orthogonal line while keeping the up sector on the trivial line.

None follows from Standard Model gauge charges alone. The radial coupling magnitudes, the complex third projector ray, completion stability, and the calibrated Gram/CP instrument remain open. Reversing the cubic coefficient selects the opposite vector orbit. Whether that sign survives in the projective flag output is tested separately in WP955; it must not be counted as physical authority merely from the vector presentation.

## Disposition

WP953's group-theoretic gap has a minimal constructive repair. An `S3`-standard order parameter with cubic anisotropy selects a three-element orbit of `Z2` flags and derives the WP350 down ray projectively. It is a source-selector template, not yet a full `physical16` selector.

No deformation coordinate is assigned physical time. The smallest falsifier is absence of a nonzero source cubic or a completion that changes the minimum away from the transposition-axis orbit.
