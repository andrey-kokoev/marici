# Optimization polarity repairs the affine interior obstruction: WP964

## Question

Classify the CP-even invariant algebra on the normalized `3+3+1` Gram
carrier, and determine the least functional class capable of a strict
full-rank oriented extremum without inserting a target value.

## Invariant algebra

For rank-one projectors `P_i`, define

\[
x_{ij}=\operatorname{tr}(P_iP_j),\qquad
\mathcal B=\operatorname{tr}(P_1P_2P_3)=r+iq.
\]

The triangle algebra is generated by the three pairwise overlaps and the
Bargmann word, with \(|\mathcal B|^2=x_{12}x_{23}x_{31}\). Its CP-even part
uses `x_12,x_23,x_31,r,q^2`, where

\[
D=1-(x_{12}+x_{23}+x_{31})+2r,
\qquad q^2=x_{12}x_{23}x_{31}-r^2.
\]

Both `D` and `q^2` descend under full weak-basis equivalence. Conjugation flips
`q`, so a CP-even extremum remains a handedness pair.

## Polarity audit

WP963 established that maximizing the co-positive reward

\[
F_\lambda=q^2+\lambda D
\]

selects an endpoint for every `lambda>0`. It did not establish that every
affine use of the invariants is endpoint-selecting. On the exact envelope,

\[
F_\lambda(u)=u^3(1-u)+\lambda(1-u)(1-2u),
\qquad
F_\lambda'(u)=(4u-3)(\lambda-u^2).
\]

When the same positive functional is minimized as a source action, the
stationary point `u=sqrt(lambda)` is a strict interior minimum for
`0<lambda<1/4`. At the coefficient-free hostile value `lambda=1/16`,

\[
u=\frac14,\qquad D=\frac38,\qquad q^2=\frac{3}{256},
\qquad F=\frac{9}{256},
\]

while both endpoints have value `1/16`. Thus unrestricted optimization
polarity makes invariant-generator degree one algebraically sufficient.

The quadratic product `Dq^2` is also sufficient under maximization and peaks
at `u=1/3`, but it is not the least algebraic class. Its role is only a
polarity-independent boundary-zero witness.

## Authority disposition

- that `F_lambda` occurs as an action to be minimized rather than a reward;

The smallest exact falsifier of the prior broad no-go is `lambda=1/16` under
minimization. No time or causal order is assigned to these arrows.
