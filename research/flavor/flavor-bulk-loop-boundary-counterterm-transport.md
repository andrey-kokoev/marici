# Bulk-loop boundary counterterms transport the bare coefficient: WP939

## Question

Can the generic boundary renormalization generated by bulk loops supply the
completion-stable selector missing after WP938, even before the complete
`SU(4)` coefficient is known?

## Imported structural result

Georgi, Grant, and Hailu compute loop corrections on a five-dimensional
`S1/Z2` orbifold and show that bulk interactions generate divergent
contributions requiring renormalization by four-dimensional couplings on the
fixed planes.  Thus bulk couplings generate RG running of brane couplings.

Primary source:
[Georgi, Grant, and Hailu, hep-ph/0012379](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0012379).

Muck, Nilse, Pilaftsis, and Ruckl independently treat brane kinetic terms as
natural tree- and quantum-level members of the five-dimensional orbifold
action and derive the corresponding gauge identities.

Primary source:
[Muck et al., hep-ph/0411258](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0411258).

These sources authorize the counterterm architecture.  They do not provide
the complete coefficient for the present `SU(4)` flavor realization.

## Exact affine structure

Let `tau_B` be the bare exchange-even boundary inverse-kinetic coefficient and
let `L(mu, Lambda; S)` be the loop correction determined by regulator,
renormalization prescription, and complete source spectrum `S`.  The
renormalized coefficient has the affine form

\[
\tau_R(\mu)=\tau_B+L(\mu,\Lambda;S).
\]

For two legal bare packets with the same source completion,

\[
\tau_B'=\tau_B+\delta,
\]

one obtains

\[
\tau_R'(\mu)-\tau_R(\mu)=\delta.
\]

The loop map therefore preserves the complete one-dimensional bare fiber.
Knowing `L` exactly would determine transport but would not determine
`tau_B`.

The RG scale is a scale coordinate.  The displayed affine relation contains
no physical-time or causal claim.

## Hostile pair

Take `tau_B = 0, 1` and the same exact loop correction `L = 3/2`.  Their
renormalized values are `3/2` and `5/2`.  The difference remains one.  This is
the smallest exact falsifier of loop-induced selection.

WP938 supplies a special kernel inside this architecture: the one-loop bulk
hypermultiplet contribution to `L` vanishes on five-dimensional `S1/Z2`.
Other source sectors may change `L`, but no additive correction removes the
bare fiber.

## Classification

The largest currently source-supported boundary-RG family is affine
transport on the exchange-even coefficient.  Its contextual equivalence
classes remain singleton-labelled by the bare difference, so it separates
bare packets if the correction is known but selects none.  It does not act on
texture-chart data and does not define a proper subspace of `physical16`.

A genuine selector requires an additional noninvertible condition: for
example, a UV regularity boundary, an independently derived fixed point with
nonzero linear response, or a UV completion that computes the finite bare
coefficient.  Such a law must be stable under every admitted matter
completion.  Choosing a subtraction convention or setting the bare term to
zero is not that law.

The remaining instrument gate is WP770's realization in calibrated
production and decay channels.  A readout of `tau_R` cannot reconstruct the
bare/source split without an independently authorized matching law.
