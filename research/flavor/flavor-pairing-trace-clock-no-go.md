# Pairing-trace clock no-go: WP1104

## Question

Can the bifundamental pairing's trace or rank select the mass-clock
coefficient or orbit?

## Exact comparison

The aligned pairing \(I_3\) has

\[
\operatorname{rank}(I_3)=3,
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}(I_3)=3.
\]

For \(n=-2,-1,0,1,2\),

\[
6n^2=24,6,0,6,24.
\]

Neither rank \(3\) nor trace \(3\) is a clock value. The identity
\(2\operatorname{tr}(I_3)=6\) would require an additional source-authorized
factor \(2\), which is absent. Even after such a factor, \(n=\pm1\) remains
unresolved and \(\sigma\) is absent.

## Classification

Negative gate. Pairing rank, trace, or unstated normalization cannot select
\(n\), \(\sigma\), or \(B/A=6n^2\).
