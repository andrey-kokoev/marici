# Third-configuration global dominance falsifier: WP1006

## Question

Does WP1005's third Hermitian configuration remain exposed when tested against
another admitted point from the same coefficient-field family?

## Exact hostile point

Keep

\[
X=\operatorname{diag}(-1,0,1)
\]

and replace WP1005's closing-edge weight (1/22) by one:

\[
Y_D=\begin{pmatrix}
0&1&-i\\
1&0&1\\
i&1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

This is the same Hermitian source-domain family. Its exact normalized scores
are

\[
K_D=1,
\qquad
P_D=\frac1{108}.
\]

For the WP1005 candidate (C),

\[
K_C=\frac{162}{323}<1,
\qquad
P_C=\frac{58564}{969^3}<\frac1{108}.
\]

Therefore for every (Q,R>0),

\[
QK_D+RP_D>QK_C+RP_C,
\]

and hence (E_D<E_C). The WP1005 orbit is never a global minimum on the
admitted Hermitian domain.

## Consequence

WP1005 remains a correct finite-set capacity witness, but it does not repair
the physical preparation instrument. Positive-cone exposure must be checked
against the complete source domain; pairwise fitting of a new score point
between two old endpoints is insufficient.

The hostile point also dominates the old full-rank endpoint (B) in both
scores. Within the now-tested four-point set, only the rank-two endpoint (A)
and (D) can be exposed by positive normals. Thus the context again has at
most two preparable labels.

## Classification

The candidate remains a mathematical discriminator point but is neither a
selector, rigidifier, nor preparable reference. No instrument authority is
gained.

## Smallest exact falsifier

The single replacement (1/22\mapsto1) is the smallest same-family hostile.
It increases both invariant scores strictly, so no optimization or numerical
scan is required.

## Claim boundary

WP1006 falsifies WP1005's global exposure; it does not classify the complete
upper convex hull of all Hermitian score points. A third globally exposed
orbit could still exist elsewhere on that hull.

## Disposition

Reject the WP1005 orbit as a preparation reference. Continue only through a
global score-hull theorem or an independently restricted source domain that
excludes the dominating orbit before coefficient outcomes are inspected.
