# Dimensionful threshold-anchor no-go: WP1184

## Question

Can a dimensionful threshold anchor be derived for the conditional sector
interface?

## DPC resolution

- **Conjecture:** dimensional transmutation supplies the missing dimensionful
  threshold anchor.
- **Rivals:** RG-invariant conditional scale; boundary-selected trajectory;
  sector threshold anchor; physical threshold packet.
- **Risky consequences:** two presentations of one RG trajectory have
  \(\log\Lambda=-4\), while a distinct boundary changes it to \(-1\), an exact
  residual of \(3\).
- **Falsification attempt:** the conditional scale is presentation invariant
  but not boundary invariant, and it supplies no sector-basis packet or
  threshold transport.
- **Residual:** an authority-bearing threshold boundary packet remains absent.
- **Disposition:** accept the conditional RG scale; reject a derived physical
  threshold anchor.
