# Gauge–Yukawa tensor-completeness gate

## Missing source object

WP489 replaces explicit messenger masses by singlet Yukawa masses and fixes all
relational vacuum scales. That does not yet define the coupled RG vector field.
The two-stage messenger action also contains the interaction tensors
\(Y_H,Y_S,Y_X\), separately in the up and down sectors. Their normalizations
and contractions are absent from the WP489 coefficient packet.

The isotropic connector Gram does not repair this omission. It constrains the
route geometry, not every quadratic and quartic Yukawa contraction.

## Exact hostile pair

On the 18-dimensional active messenger space, compare the block-diagonal maps

\[
Y_X^{(A)}=I_{18},
\qquad
Y_X^{(B)}=2I_{18}.
\]

Each identity block stays inside one Standard Model charge sector; no up/down
mixing is introduced.

Both choices preserve the same representations, oriented-port symmetry,
radial vacuum, singlet-generated messenger masses, and WP487 threshold order.
Nevertheless,

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(Y_XY_X^\dagger)
\]

changes by a factor four, while

\[
\operatorname{Tr}\bigl((Y_XY_X^\dagger)^2\bigr)
\]

changes by a factor sixteen. Hence the gauge–Yukawa and quartic beta functions
are not determined by the existing source packet.

## Minimal completion

A calculable successor must freeze, independently of the desired fixed point,
the ten maps

\[
Y_H^{u,d},\quad Y_S^{u,d},\quad Y_X^{u,d},
\quad Z_A^{u,d},\quad Z_B^{u,d},
\]

together with their index contractions, all retained scalar quartics, Standard
Model couplings in the truncation, scheme and loop order, and finite matching
maps at both messenger thresholds.

Until that packet exists, WP490's call for a complete gauge–Yukawa fixed point
is not merely computationally unfinished: the vector field itself is
underdetermined. No fixed-point or numerical-selector authority follows.
