# Phase-gauge production law: WP1173

## Question

Can a phase-gauge production law be derived from the certified modulus?

## DPC resolution

- **Problem:** turn the WP1171 modulus into a phase-gauge law.
- **Conjecture:** a sourced phase-gauge production law follows directly.
- **Rivals:** diagonal phase quotient; `physical16` channel map; common
  coupling scale; production authority.
- **Risky consequences:** the phase fiber has dimension \(11\); the canonical
  invariant is \(P_{ij}=|S_{ij}|^2\); a general stochastic \(16\times6\)
  channel has \(90\) parameters.
- **Falsification attempt:** the quotient law is constructed, but it collapses
  to the modulus and leaves the \(16\times6\) channel unconstrained.
- **Residual:** a source-derived channel map and common coupling scale remain
  open.
- **Disposition:** accept the gauge quotient, but reject it as sourced
  physical production.
