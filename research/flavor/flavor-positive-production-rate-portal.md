# Positive production-rate portal: WP462

## Question

Can an on-shell production rate replace the detector-erased intrinsic width as WP457's second parameter-identification coordinate?

## Signed mixing residues versus positive production weights

For the WP453 hostile orientation, WP456 found the same-transition Delta-F equals two bilinears

\[
v^TP_1v=-\frac14,
\qquad
v^TP_3v=\frac14.
\]

Collider production instead sums absolute amplitudes and therefore uses the sesquilinear weights

\[
v^\dagger P_1v=\frac14,
\qquad
v^\dagger P_3v=\frac14.
\]

Both pole multiplets have equal positive entrance strength for the mass-basis (1,2) down-quark channel. The low-energy cancellation is absent on shell. This is a complementary probe generated by the same source projectors, not an added reference port.

## Inclusive entrance width

For one unordered pair of distinct down flavors, summing the charge-conjugate final states and all degenerate vectors in a multiplet gives

\[
\Gamma_{r,d\bar s+s\bar d}^{\rm entrance}
=\frac{N_cg_F^2m_r}{6\pi}v^\dagger P_rv
=\frac{g_F^2m_r}{8\pi}.
\]

This is an inclusive multiplet entrance weight. It does not assign basis-dependent partial widths to individual members of a degenerate multiplet. For an inclusive dijet final state on WP449's quark-only support, the decay branching fraction is one.

## Formal rate Jacobian

Let (kappa_1) be the complete positive experimental factor containing the narrow-width flux normalization, the (d anti-s plus s anti-d) parton luminosity, integrated luminosity, selection acceptance, and efficiency. The expected triplet signal yield has the form

\[
N_1=\kappa_1g_F^2v^\dagger P_1v
=\frac{\kappa_1g_F^2}{4}.
\]

Together with (m_1=g_F mu),

\[
\frac{\partial(m_1,N_1)}{\partial(g_F,\mu)}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\mu&g_F\\
\kappa_1g_F/2&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det=-\frac{\kappa_1g_F^2}{2}.

Thus a physically calibrated positive rate factor restores rank two even when detector resolution deletes the width coordinate. The inverse readout is

\[
g_F=2\sqrt{N_1/\kappa_1},
\qquad
\mu=\frac{m_1}{2}\sqrt{\kappa_1/N_1}.
\]

## Instrument boundary

WP462 proves source support and formal identifiability, not experimental reach. The ATLAS generic Gaussian limit does not by itself supply (kappa_1) for this flavor-changing source. A legal interface must freeze:

- a named PDF ensemble and its high-x strange/down covariance;
- the exact narrow-width normalization and perturbative order;
- the (d anti-s plus s anti-d) initial-state support;
- showering, jet response, acceptance, and efficiency for the source angular distribution;
- luminosity and background likelihood;
- treatment of the triplet degeneracy and the separate quintet pole.

The source residue may not be converted into an event count by fitting (kappa_1) to the desired exclusion. Until these objects are supplied, the detector response rank is conditional rather than admitted.

## Classification and falsifiers

The operation is a source-derived positive production probe and a formal parameter identifier. It is neither selector nor rigidifier. A measured rate would infer the realized (g_F); it would not explain its value.

Smallest exact falsifiers:

- either positive pole weight differs from (1/4);
- the inclusive entrance width is nonpositive;
- the rate Jacobian vanishes for positive (g_F,kappa_1);
- a purported experimental factor lacks the source-specific parton and acceptance map.
