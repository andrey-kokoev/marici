# Spin(5) cross-sector independence transfer (WP909)

## Surveyed results

The newest relevant packets transfer into WP908 as hostile typing gates, not
as flavor theorems.

### Sontag: marginal calibration does not type a joint failure carrier

`broadcastable-records-need-a-joint-failure-carrier.md` constructs independent,
common-mode, and exclusive three-copy errors with identical one-copy error
probability but different majority-failure probabilities. Agreement cannot
detect a common-mode flip. `physical-diversity-needs-disturbance-excitation.md`
adds that separate registers or rank-two response do not prove stochastic
independence; active probes only identify declared response directions.

Flavor transfer: marginal validation is necessary but cannot authorize the
binomial count law. Seed-family and run-boundary interventions are falsifiers
of common modes, not proofs of iid pairs.

### Strominger: proper marginals do not compose upward

`four-point-faithfulness-does-not-compose-to-five-points.md` and
`brunnian-coherence-obstructions-exist-at-every-higher-arity.md` exhibit
nontrivial global classes invisible on every deletion marginal.

Flavor transfer: no fixed tower of lower-order autocorrelations or subset
tests proves a global product law. The checker gives the finite probability
analogue: the uniform even-parity law on five bits has every four-bit marginal
equal to the iid fair law, but its full five-bit law is not iid.

### Nima: composed sources carry a mixed corner

`two-source-additions-force-a-shared-moving-seam-corner.md` shows that two
individually typed source additions require a source-derived mixed state;
adding their first-order records is incomplete.

Flavor transfer: event keys, luminosity-block conditions, pile-up overlays,
and run calibration cannot be declared independent components merely because
each has a valid marginal generator. Their shared run/event corner must be
retained or removed by an admitted randomized design.

### Aspect: aggregate return can hide channel transport

`an-exceptional-point-monodromy-falsifies-frozen-v9.md` shows a single-valued
total resolvent with exchanged eigenchannel sheets.

Flavor transfer: an aggregate output law may replay while event-key or latent
channel labels undergo hidden permutation. This matters for semantic lineage
and coupling efficiency, although WP905 shows it does not alone invalidate a
coupling with correct marginals.

### Benincasa: formal source structure does not instantiate calibration

`finite-time-source-normalization-closure.md` finds a valid finite-time source
relation but no rank-three physical normalization map.

Flavor transfer: naming an entropy source, cryptographic primitive, or health
test does not instantiate joint-law calibration. The acquisition contract must
expose independently calibrated stochastic units and the raw-entropy-to-event-
key map.

## Exact transferred hostile

Let \(X=(X_1,\ldots,X_5)\) be uniform on the sixteen even-parity five-bit
strings. Every deletion of one coordinate is uniform on all sixteen four-bit
strings. Thus every proper four-coordinate observation agrees exactly with
five iid fair bits. Globally,

\[
\Pr(X_1+\cdots+X_5=0\pmod 2)=1
\]

instead of \(1/2\). The full support has size sixteen rather than thirty-two.

Together with Sontag's three-copy fixtures, this proves two failures:

- one-event marginals do not determine decoder or count risk;
- even all maximal proper marginals do not determine the global product law.

## Flavor disposition

The largest currently source-authorized probe family consists of replay,
one-arm marginal validation, registered finite-order dependence diagnostics,
seed-family perturbations, and run-boundary stratification. It can falsify
specific dependence models but does not separate iid event laws from all
higher-order hostiles.

The missing object is a full joint failure carrier plus an acquisition theorem
or randomized experimental design that factors it into independently sampled
event-key units. A finite diagnostic tower cannot certify its own closure.

This transfer creates no selector. It sharpens the remaining physical
instrument gate for the detector-response experiment.
