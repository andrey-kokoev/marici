# Shared-entrance commuting selector no-go: WP1015

## Question

What proper coefficient family does the largest currently executable flavor
source grammar select inside the WP1014 universal compiler, and does that
family survive the fitted ensemble?

## Source-authorized coefficient module

WP500 derives the coherent tree operation from the WP492 normalized equal
entrance, componentwise connector incidence, identity messenger masses, and
one shared connector frame. Its exact two-sector port matrix has rank one:
the up/down linear-word coefficient vectors are collinear.

Let \(L\) be the resulting shared Hermitian word. For sector \(q\), a linear
Yukawa map has the form

\[
Y_q=a_qI+b_qL.
\]

Its Gram matrix is

\[
H_q=Y_qY_q^\dagger
=|a_q|^2I+
(a_q\overline{b_q}+\overline{a_q}b_q)L+
|b_q|^2L^2.
\]

Thus both \(H_u\) and \(H_d\) are real quadratic polynomials in the same
Hermitian matrix \(L\). Positivity restricts their polynomial coefficients but
does not alter the common spectral projectors.

## Exact selected image

The shared functional calculus gives

\[
[H_u,H_d]=0,
\qquad
\det[H_u,H_d]=0,
\qquad
J=0
\]

whenever both spectra are nondegenerate. The construction descends under
simultaneous weak-basis conjugation. It therefore selects the proper commuting
subspace of the physical quotient and rigidifies both sectors to one shared
eigenframe.

All 1,210 stored viable sheets have nonzero \(J\). The source-selected image is
therefore disjoint from the complete fitted ensemble.

## Classification

Within the exact WP500 domain, the operation is both a source-derived selector
and a shared-frame rigidifier. It is not a viable physical selector because
its numerical prediction \(J=0\) is falsified by every fitted sheet. The
already typed CKM/Jarlskog readout supplies the falsifying instrument; the
formal six-coordinate connector-loop probe remains a separate, uninstrumented
operation.

This does not contradict WP500's warning that loop rank cannot be transported
to tree matching. The conclusion uses only the executable rank-one tree port.

## Smallest exact falsifier

Add a second independently labelled Hermitian entrance. The witness
\(L_u=X,\ L_d=Y\) gives

\[
\det[X,Y]=\frac{24i}{5}\neq0.
\]

Hence entrance independence is exactly the resource that can leave the
commuting locus. Merely changing two scalar normalizations cannot do so.

## Claim boundary

The theorem is conditional on the WP500 shared-frame, shared-entrance,
identity-mass grammar and a Hermitian linear word. It does not cover
non-Hermitian word packets, independently labelled entrances, higher messenger
routes, or coefficients derived by a new source action. Graph admission does
not certify the source model as realized in nature.

## Disposition

Retain the shared-entrance family as the first genuine source-derived proper
selector found in this branch, but close it experimentally: its complete
fitted fiber is empty. Any viable successor needs at least two noncollinear
source-labelled entrance directions plus an upstream law relating their
coefficients before comparison with physical16.
