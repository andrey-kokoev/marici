# Positive mediator reachability cone: WP1003

## Question

Does WP1001-WP1002's locally rank-two actuator realize all three WP992
preparation labels once the source positivity domain is enforced globally?

## Exact image

For positive mediator masses and nonzero real vertices, leading WP977 matching
gives

\[
Q=\frac{\mu^2}{2m_A^2}>0,
\qquad
R=\frac{\gamma^2\mu^6}{2m_s^2(m_A^2)^6}>0.
\]

WP1000 proved that varying the two positive masses covers the full positive
quadrant and nothing outside it.

Intersect this image with WP992's three unique-minimum regions:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l|l}
\text{label}&\text{WP992 condition}&\text{positive-source intersection}\\
0&Q<0,\ R<-3087Q&\varnothing\\
2&Q>0,\ R<24696Q&0<R<24696Q\\
3&R>-3087Q,\ R>24696Q&R>24696Q.
\end{array}
\]

Thus the source actuator reaches the rank-two and full-rank labels but never
the commuting reference. Local rank two did not imply global surjectivity onto
the formal additive-control plane.

## Exact witnesses

The positive points ((Q,R)=(1,1)) and ((1,24697)) lie strictly in the
rank-two and full-rank regions. No positive point lies in the commuting region
because its first inequality requires (Q<0).

At the boundary (R=24696Q), states 2 and 3 are degenerate rather than
uniquely prepared, so it supplies neither missing label.

## Consequence for the WP991 instrument

WP991's rank-two relational energy reconstruction requires all three
configurations, including the internal commuting baseline. WP1001-WP1002
therefore do not yet implement that instrument. They form a genuine two-port
actuator on the positive mediator image but only a two-label preparation
family for the WP992 task.

This also corrects the scope of “algebraically complete” in WP1000: it means
complete on the positive source quotient, not on the signed additive-control
plane used by WP992-WP996.

## Classification

The positive mediator operation is neither selector nor rigidifier. It can
prepare two competing energy minima but cannot close the relational readout
context. Its contextual partition has one inaccessible label, independent of
local response rank or source metric.

## Smallest exact falsifier

The inequality (Q>0) throughout the admitted source domain is the smallest
falsifier of three-label reachability. A single source-authorized operation
producing (Q<0) while retaining stability would falsify this no-go.

## Claim boundary

WP1003 applies to the leading WP977 mediator matching with positive quadratic
masses and real squared vertices. It does not exclude an independently stable
negative effective commutator-square coefficient generated by another source
channel, an interference operation, or a different internal reference
configuration.

## Disposition

Retain WP1001-WP1002 as a source-priced two-port local actuator, but reject it
as a complete WP991 preparation instrument. Reopen with either a stable
source-derived signed (Q) channel or a replacement internal reference whose
response matrix remains rank two inside the positive source cone.
