## Question

bypassing a sourced boundary character?

## DPC resolution

  classifies H6/F3 tensor F2 but no sourced generator, phase provenance, or
  channels; WP1253 finds zero current-source passes and zero admissible
  corpus packets.
- **Strongest falsification attempt:** replay WP1249 through WP1253 and
  search the current source and corpus for an oriented ray that bypasses the
  sourced boundary character.
- **Exact residual:** no current or corpus route supplies the oriented
  adjoint ray; the sourced-boundary-character necessity conjecture survives.
  The residual is an external or newly derived UV event-production packet
  admitted by the WP1128 contract.
- **Disposition:** sourced-boundary-character necessity survives attempted
  falsification; external/new UV event-production packet selected.

attempted falsification. It remains unproven, and the oriented adjoint ray is
still not source-derived.
