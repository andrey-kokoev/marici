# Curvature-jet control-rank gate

Work package: WP575  
Owner: marici.Figueiredo

## Question

WP573 recovers two linearly independent local measure derivatives from one
source parameter. May those two derivative vectors be used as the two source
directions required by the rank-two detector theorems WP569--WP574?

## Frozen source curve

Use WP573's exact completed measure

\[
\mu(\theta)=((1+\theta)^2,1,\theta^2).
\]

At the central source point,

\[
j_1=\dot\mu(0)=(2,0,0)^T,
\qquad
j_2=\ddot\mu(0)=(2,0,2)^T.
\]

The formal jet matrix (J=(j_1\ j_2)) has rank two. This is a statement
about the order-two Taylor packet, not about executable source control.

The exact displacement is

\[
\mu(\theta)-\mu(0)=a j_1+b j_2,
\qquad
a=\theta,
\qquad
b={\theta^2\over2}.
\]

Therefore the admissible jet coefficients obey the source constraint

\[
2b=a^2.
\]

Its tangent space at the central point is (b=0), hence one-dimensional.
The executable first-order source-control rank is one even though the stored
order-two jet matrix has rank two.

## Smallest exact falsifier

The formal coefficient vector ((a,b)=(0,1)) requests the pure curvature
displacement (j_2). No real source setting realizes it: (a=0) forces
(\theta=0), and then (b=0). Symmetric settings can estimate (j_2) by a
second difference, but they do not provide an operation that independently
turns on (j_2) while holding (j_1) fixed.

Thus an identity detector can give the formal Gram

\[
J^T J=
\begin{pmatrix}
4&4\\
4&8
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det(J^TJ)=16,
\]

while the physically executable differential still has rank one. Positive
formal Gram determinant is insufficient unless its columns are generated by
independently admitted source interventions.

## Consequence for the flavor-detector join

WP573 supplies a source-authorized one-parameter measure curve and its
support-safe order-two jet. WP574 supplies a theorem for a genuinely
two-dimensional source tangent. They cannot be composed by identifying
WP573's derivative order with WP574's source-direction label.

A valid rank-two entrance must add a second independently variable flavor
source coordinate (\eta), with a declared operation preparing
(\mu(\theta,\eta)), and must prove

\[
\operatorname{rank}
\left.
{\partial\mu\over\partial(\theta,\eta)}
\right|_{(0,0)}=2.
\]

That source extension must be fixed before detector fitting and must descend
under the full weak-basis groupoid. A second derivative, additional reweight
point, null record, or exposure reference does not by itself supply the
missing source control.

## Classification

The curvature jet is a richer readout of one source operation. It can reveal
support birth and test nonlinear dynamics, but it is neither a second source
selector nor a second executable control. The exact contextual partition is:

- one-parameter source curve: control rank one;
- order-two predictive jet: two independent coefficient vectors;
- fictitious freely controlled jet plane: rank two but unauthorized;
- admitted two-parameter source surface: not currently present.

No reference port repairs source-control rank. A reference port can preserve
a rate direction after it has been generated; it cannot manufacture an
independent source perturbation.
