# Irreducible-triplet width packet: WP449

## Question

Can the WP448 pole residues be completed with widths derived from a particle spectrum fixed independently of a detector fit?

## Frozen support domain

WP449 declares the following conditional spectrum before calculating any width:

- both flavor-gauge multiplets lie above the top-pair threshold;
- every vectorlike messenger-pair threshold lies above the quintet pole;
- every physical flavon-containing decay threshold lies above the quintet pole;
- no additional light (SU(3)_F)-charged state exists;
- (g_F^2/(4\pi)) is perturbative.

The quintet-to-two-triplet gauge cascade is kinematically closed because (\sqrt3<2). These assumptions are part of the claim, not conveniences to be silently changed after comparison with data.

## Inclusive leading widths

For one vectorlike Dirac quark type and a canonically normalized flavor generator,

\[
\Gamma_q=\frac{N_cg_F^2M}{12\pi}\operatorname{Tr}(T^2),
\qquad \operatorname{Tr}(T^2)=\frac12.
\]

Summing the up- and down-type Dirac multiplets with (N_c=3) gives

\[
\frac{\Gamma}{M}=\frac{g_F^2}{4\pi}.
\]

Using WP448's two masses,

\[
\Gamma_1=\frac{g_F^3\mu}{4\pi},\qquad
\Gamma_3=\frac{\sqrt3g_F^3\mu}{4\pi}.
\]

The width ratio equals the pole-mass ratio. The result is invariant under quark mass-basis rotations because it is an inclusive trace over all three generations.

## Residue and instrument boundary

The current-space residues remain the independently frozen (g_F^2P_1) and (g_F^2P_3) of WP448. WP449 supplies a conditional leading fixed-width model, not the exact complex pole after loop resummation.

No detector resolution, acceptance, background model, or flavor tag is attached. Once a threshold opens, the massless inclusive expression must be replaced by threshold-resolved partial widths using the physical messenger and quark rotations. A detector fit may test those widths but may not choose them.

## Smallest falsifier

One open messenger or flavon channel in the declared pole range, a triplet pole below (2m_t), or loss of perturbativity falsifies the width packet's domain.
