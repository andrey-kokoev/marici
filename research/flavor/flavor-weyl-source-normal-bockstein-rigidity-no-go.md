# The exact Weyl source has no physical normal Bockstein direction: WP947

## Question

Can Benincasa's source-normal Bockstein mechanism transfer directly to the exact two-holonomy Weyl source of WP944–WP946?

## Frozen source relations

Let the source pair obey

\[
D^3=I,
\qquad
S^3=I,
\qquad
DS=\omega SD,
\qquad
\omega^2+\omega+1=0.
\]

Write a dual-number deformation

\[
D\mapsto D+\epsilon A,
\qquad
S\mapsto S+\epsilon B,
\qquad
\epsilon^2=0.
\]

Linearizing the three matrix relations gives

\[
\sum_{k=0}^{2}D^kAD^{2-k}=0,
\qquad
\sum_{k=0}^{2}S^kBS^{2-k}=0,
\]

and

\[
AS+DB-\omega(BD+SA)=0.
\]

The exact coefficient matrix has rank ten on the eighteen complex entries of `(A,B)`. Its tangent kernel therefore has dimension eight.

## Weak-basis orbit exhaustion

An infinitesimal simultaneous change of family basis generated by `X` produces

\[
A=[X,D],
\qquad
B=[X,S].
\]

The common commutant of the Weyl pair is scalar, so this orbit tangent has dimension `9-1=8`. Exact comparison shows that adjoining its columns to a basis of the relation tangent does not increase rank. Hence

\[
T_{(D,S)}\mathcal R
=
T_{(D,S)}\bigl(GL_3\cdot(D,S)\bigr).
\]

Every first-order deformation preserving the frozen Weyl relations is simultaneous conjugation. After quotienting by the full weak-basis groupoid, the tangent space is zero.

## The phase is discrete, not a normal coordinate

Allowing `omega` to vary does not create the missing direction. Linearizing

\[
\omega^3=1
\]

at a primitive root gives

\[
3\omega^2\,\delta\omega=0,
\]

and therefore `delta omega = 0` over characteristic zero. Treating the commutation phase as a continuous source-normal coordinate would leave the declared finite Weyl source domain.

## Classification and disposition

The exact Weyl representation is infinitesimally rigid modulo weak basis. A derivative along its legal relation family is quotient-trivial, so it cannot induce a nonzero flavor Bockstein on `physical16`. This differs from Benincasa's gamma construction, where gamma is an independently existing source parameter with a nonzero normal direction.

The smallest exact falsifier is the equality of the eight-dimensional relation tangent and the eight-dimensional conjugation tangent. This is an algebraic deformation statement; no parameter is assigned physical time or causal meaning.

The Bockstein search must move one source layer outward. A viable normal coordinate must belong to an independently declared action, boundary condition, representation-completion module, threshold matching relation, or defect coupling. Its induced map must have a proper noncommuting image after quotienting, not merely before quotienting, and its instrument remains separately required.
