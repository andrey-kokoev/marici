# Ward-spectral completion gate: WP1195

## Question

Can a scale-free Ward-spectral functional derive the completion?

## DPC resolution

- **Conjecture:** a positive scale-free functional selects the full Ward
  spectral completion.
- **Rivals:** minimal Ward index; dimensionful spectral penalty; scale-free
  Ward-spectral shape; source-derived completion action.
- **Risky consequences:** the base score is \(14+3\lambda\), every nonempty
  completion has positive gap, and the ordering is robust for every
  \(\lambda>0\), while scale and mixing fibers remain.
- **Falsification attempt:** \(\Phi\) uniquely selects \(k=\ell=0\) and equal
  singular values inside the declared finite grammar, but \(D\mapsto mD\) and
  distinct irreducible Householder mixings retain the same score.
- **Residual:** the source must derive \(\Phi\), select scale and mixing, and
  realize a calibrated Ward instrument.
- **Disposition:** construct the finite-completion selector; reject full
  source authority.
