# Pole-plus-current inverse instrument

## Probe family

WP486 stays on the positive WP485 source domain. Here \(\mu\) is the adjoint
amplitude and the physical flavor norm is \(f_{\rm phys}^2=6\mu^2\). It uses
four readouts:

- \(Q\), the unmixed quintet mass squared;
- \(T\), the sum of the two mixed-triplet mass squares;
- \(D\), their product;
- \(C\), the zero-momentum triplet quark-current coefficient.

These readouts are source derived. Their joint use is only physically admitted
if all three poles and the low-energy current response are calibrated in the
same source domain.

## Exact inverse theorem

Put \(a=Q/3\), \(r=3D/(2Q)\), and

\[
k={T-a-2r\over4r}.
\]

Then the unique positive source point is reconstructed by

\[
s^2={k^{-1}+2\over C},
\qquad
\mu^2={1+2k\over C},
\qquad
g_F^2={a\over \mu^2},
\qquad
g_P^2={r\over s^2}.
\]

The exact substitution round trip is the identity. The positive image is
characterized by \(Q,D,C>0\) and

\[
T-{Q\over3}-{3D\over Q}>0.
\]

Its pullback is \(4g_P^2\mu^2>0\). Hence this probe family is jointly faithful
on the declared positive source domain.

The physical clock ratio is therefore read out without ambiguity as

\[
{g_Ff_{\rm phys}\over v}={\sqrt{2Q}\over v}.
\]

## Residues and partial widths

Writing the two mixed masses as \(m_-^2<m_+^2\), their quark-current weights
are determined directly from pole positions:

\[
w_-={m_+^2-Q/3\over m_+^2-m_-^2},
\qquad
w_+={Q/3-m_-^2\over m_+^2-m_-^2}.
\]

The inverse map also recovers \(g_F^2\), so the absolute current residues are
calibrated. Under the independently derived six-massless-quark approximation,
the corresponding partial fractional widths are \(g_F^2w_\pm/(4\pi)\). They
obey an exact sum rule with the unmixed quintet fractional width.

These are partial widths, not total widths. Connector scalars, port vectors,
and the enlarged messenger sector may open additional channels.

## Authority boundary

The operation identifies a source point; it does not select one dynamically.
The smallest failure is removal of \(C\): all pole masses then retain a common
rescaling of \(\mu^2,s^2\) against the two gauge couplings. Current evidence
supplies neither three populated resolved-pole
records nor a calibrated low-energy current likelihood in the same domain.
The instrument is therefore exactly specified but not physically realized.
