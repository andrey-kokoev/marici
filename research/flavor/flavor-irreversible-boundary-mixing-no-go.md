# Irreversible boundary-mixing no-go: WP1121

## Question

Can a UV boundary source derive irreversible six-state mixing as physical16
event production?

## DPC resolution

- **Conjecture:** the UV boundary source derives irreversible six-state mixing
  rates and a finite event-time map producing \(J_6/6\).
- **Rivals:** anomaly inflow supplies mixing; localized-brane Green response
  supplies mixing; no irreversible boundary dynamics exists.
- **Risky consequences:** a nonzero \(6\times6\) Lindblad/Markov generator
  \(L\) with \(L(1/6)^6=0\), six nonnegative mixing rates, and a
  source-derived map \(\tau:C\to T_{\rm phys}\).
- **Falsification attempt:** the admitted boundary data contain seven anomaly
  channels, parent coefficient \(-3\), and a rank-one Green response, but zero
  dissipative rates and zero event-time maps.
- **Residual:** a future UV defect could supply a Lindblad/Markov generator
  and a physical-time interface.
- **Disposition:** reject the current-source irreversible-mixing conjecture.

## Boundary

The zero generator is stationary but does not mix. Conservative anomaly,
Green–Schwarz, endpoint-localization, and Green-response data cannot be used
to infer dissipation. No variable named \(t\) acquires physical-time meaning
without the source-derived readout map.
