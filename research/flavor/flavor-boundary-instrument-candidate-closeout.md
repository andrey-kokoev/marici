# Boundary-to-instrument candidate-class closeout (WP139)

Owner: `marici.Figueiredo`.

## Objective and scope

Determine whether the currently frozen flavor boundary candidates supply an
independently generated RG trajectory or distribution, a physical scale
relative to a clock, accessible WP128 thresholds, and an executable WP133
probe that is faithful on an enlarged UV rival class.

This is a bounded four-candidate audit, not a universal no-go over future
gravitational, cosmological, or flavor sources.

Pre-objective process report: excitement `8/10`, confidence `10/10` that no
current candidate closes the chain, expected information gain `8/10`.
Confounds are the bounded candidate census and conditional detector models.
These reports are non-evidential.

Frozen optionality snapshot: four candidates; seven gates copied from the
operator objective; five precursor result packets; one exact admission matrix;
twelve checks. No candidate or gate is changed after replaying results.

## Frozen gates

1. independently motivated boundary source;
2. derived scale or normalized coefficient distribution;
3. RG, weak-basis, and presentation descent;
4. pushforward through reheating and mediator matching;
5. uniform accessibility over the admitted source domain;
6. detector-smeared faithfulness on an enlarged non-gauge rival class;
7. executable physical instrument.

## Exact dispositions

### Dimensional transmutation

WP134 constructs an RG-invariant scale map and passes descent. It fails at the
first authority arrow: the RG trajectory and dimensionful boundary are inputs.
It cannot determine accessibility or instrument rank before that boundary is
supplied.

### UV fixed point with a physical clock

WP135 shows that adding a fixed point and clock still leaves every relevant
amplitude as a critical-surface coordinate. Two trajectories approaching the
same fixed point generate different crossover scales. The fixed point
rigidifies critical data but does not select the relevant trajectory.

### Gaussian cosmological preparation

WP136 supplies the first independent normalized relevant-amplitude measure,
and WP137 transports it faithfully through reheating. It passes the first four
gates. Its unbounded support gives a nonzero inaccessible class for every
finite detector domain; uniform faithfulness and an executable multi-point
instrument fail.

### Compact cosmological preparation

WP138 shows that compact support can be wholly accessible in principle. But
the support scale relative to detector reach is not generated by compactness:
two admissible packets give accessibility `1` and `9/16`. The route therefore
fails the derived-scale gate before it can claim uniform physical authority.

## Objective-wide conclusion

No candidate passes all seven gates. The current flavor programme contains:

- genuine quotient selectors and renormalizable mediator architectures;
- RG-invariant conditional scale maps;
- normalized cosmological boundary ensembles;
- conditional finite-reach and multi-point separation theorems.

It does **not** contain an independently generated complete arrow

\[
\text{boundary source}\to\text{trajectory/scale}\to
\text{accessible mediators}\to\text{executable faithful instrument}.
\]

The result is negative but progressive. The missing object is no longer an
unspecified numerical flavor law. It is a precisely typed combination of
source-selected trajectory/support scale and physical multi-point instrument.

The reopening condition is fixed in advance: admit a new source whose action
selects its relevant amplitude or full compact support relative to a physical
clock, push it equivariantly to WP128, and demonstrate an executable detector
whose convolution has no non-gauge kernel on a predeclared enlarged rival
class. Agreement with CKM values or detector reach cannot define that source.

accessibility, detector contextual classes, hostile rival, and physical-
instrument authority have each received an explicit disposition in
WP134--WP139. The exact closeout checker rereads every precursor JSON and
requires all of them to pass before evaluating the admission matrix.
