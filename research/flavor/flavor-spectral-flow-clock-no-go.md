# Spectral-flow clock no-go: WP1190

## Question

Can spectral flow plus dimensional transmutation derive a clock law?

## DPC resolution

- **Conjecture:** a quantized spectral-flow index and RG-invariant scale
  jointly select the threshold clock.
- **Rivals:** orientation-only spectral flow; crossing-location law;
  dimensional transmutation; common spectral/RG clock.
- **Risky consequences:** crossings \(1/4\) and \(3/4\) share index one,
  every transmutation scale has a boundary coupling, and
  \(m=\rho\Lambda\) retains two continuous dimensions.
- **Falsification attempt:** the index is stable under crossing displacement,
  while transmutation trades scale for boundary coupling; their product fixes
  neither factor.
- **Residual:** the spectral path must itself be an RG trajectory with a
  dynamically selected zero and calibrated physical clock.
- **Disposition:** accept orientation selection; reject clock-law selection.
