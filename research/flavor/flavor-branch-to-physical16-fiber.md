# Branch-to-physical16 fiber (WP294)

## Return to the faithful quotient

The domain programme through WP293 can prepare or certify a binary branch. To
connect it to flavor, grant the strongest favorable interpretation: the branch
maps to the sign of the signed Jarlskog invariant already carried by
`physical16`.

On the nondegenerate $J\neq0$ domain, this readout descends under the full
weak-basis groupoid. It partitions the quotient into positive- and negative-
$J$ components. Descent does not imply point selection.

## Exact hostile pair

The checker constructs two exact standard-parameterization CKM matrices with
the same six ordered masses and positive $J$. Both matrices are exactly
unitary. Their CKM moduli and Jarlskog magnitudes differ, so their `physical16`
coordinates are physically inequivalent, while the binary branch readout is
identical.

The first nonfaithful arrow is therefore `physical16 -> sign(J)`.

Its fibers are therefore nonsingleton. Reliable domain coarsening
cannot remove this kernel because it improves preparation of the branch, not
resolution within the branch.

## Classification

If a flavor source derives the branch-to-$\operatorname{sign}(J)$ map, the
operation is a conditional orientation selector. It is neither a point
selector nor a texture rigidifier. Without that source map it remains only a
domain-model selector with no admitted flavor image.

A stronger `physical16` claim requires independently source-derived probes or
dynamics for CP-even coordinates and the magnitude of $J$. The binary branch
cannot acquire that authority from reliable preparation alone.
