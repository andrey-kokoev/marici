# Two-entrance CP-even selector no-go: WP1016

## Question

Does the existing source-derived two-entrance vacuum escape the WP1015
commuting selector while retaining a viable CP-violating physical image?

## Admitted source domain

WP503 supplies two real entrance vectors with a positive renormalizable
potential whose global minima obey

\[
u\cdot u=a^2,\qquad
d\cdot d=b^2,\qquad
u\cdot d=0.
\]

The relative angle is selected to orthogonality, while \(a,b\) remain
independent source inputs. WP505 closes the neutral complex Hessian kernel with
additional positive invariants but retains a real orthogonal vacuum. Neither
packet derives a CP-odd phase.

The argument below uses only the real CP-even Gram domain. It assigns no
physical-time meaning to minimization or parameter variation.

## Exact quotient obstruction

Let \(H_u,H_d\) be arbitrary real symmetric \(3\times3\) Gram matrices produced
on this domain. Their commutator satisfies

\[
C=[H_u,H_d],\qquad C^T=-C.
\]

Every odd-dimensional antisymmetric matrix has zero determinant, so

\[
\det[H_u,H_d]=0,\qquad J=0.
\]

This does not force the matrices to commute. The exact positive-spectrum
witness

\[
H_u=\operatorname{diag}(1,2,4),\qquad
H_d=
\begin{pmatrix}
3&1&0\\
1&5&1\\
0&1&7
\end{pmatrix}
\]

has a rank-two nonzero commutator. Two real entrances therefore repair mixing
capacity but not CP violation.

The statement \(J=0\) is a full weak-basis invariant even though the convenient
real presentation is tied to the CP-even source domain.

## Ensemble and instrument

All 1,210 stored viable sheets have nonzero \(J\). The complete source-selected
CP-even image is disjoint from the fitted ensemble. The existing signed
Jarlskog/CKM readout is the physical instrument for this falsifier; no
connector-loop instrument is inferred.

## Smallest exact falsifier

Leaving the real CP-even domain is load-bearing. The complex Hermitian witness
\(X,Y\) from WP1009 satisfies

\[
\det[X,Y]=\frac{24i}{5}\neq0.
\]

Thus the next required resource is not merely a second entrance. It is a
source-derived complex CP-odd invariant whose vacuum fixes a nontrivial
relative phase. Adding a reference phase would instead define a new relational
experiment and would not derive an absolute phase.

## Claim boundary

The result applies to the WP503 real neutral entrance domain and the real
vacuum retained by WP505. It does not complete charged electroweak directions,
connector mixing, pole residues, or detector response. It does not exclude a
complex source potential with an independently derived CP-violating minimum.

## Disposition

Retain two-entrance noncollinearity as a source-derived mixing rigidifier and
proper CP-even selector, but close it phenomenologically. Reopen only with a
declared CP-odd source invariant, a stable complex minimum, a descending
physical16 map, and the existing signed-CP instrument applied without fitting
the phase.
