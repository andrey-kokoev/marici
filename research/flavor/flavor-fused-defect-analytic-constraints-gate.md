# Fused-defect analytic-constraints gate: WP1251

## Question

Can analytic constraints select the fused-defect field values?

## DPC resolution

- **Problem:** derive anomaly and analytic constraints on the 23
  fused-defect fields, then construct source-authorized values for the
  boundary lift, clock orientation, adjoint frame, \(\rho\), kernel, gain,
  and Physical16 descent.
- **Bold conjecture:** anomaly and analyticity constraints may narrow the
  fused defect enough for endpoint, Wilson, extended-line, or descent routes
  to select the fields.
- **Named rivals:** exact anomaly/analytic constraint fiber; local
  Green-Schwarz split fiber; endpoint exchange; orientation-odd Wilson/flux
  datum; extended determinant/Wilson line; quotient descent.
- **Risky consequences:** the defect must satisfy the seven-channel lift,
  coset, parent coefficient \(-3\), clock law, \(\rho\) weight \(-3\),
  \(6\times6\) kernel, gain \(3/2\), and reweighting;
  \(g_0=-3/2+t\) and \(g_\pi=-3/2-t\) realize the split with exact target
  \(t=0\) and coset domain \(\mathbb Z\); endpoint reflection maps the
  quartet to a different coset; Wilson orientation does not couple to the
  split and \(6n^2\) erases clock sign; extended lines reduce to \(c/D\) or
  lack global sections; quotient descent is permutation/projection with
  gain 1.
- **Strongest falsification attempt:** no route selects an orientation-odd
  boundary datum, split, independent \(\rho\), source interaction kernel,
  or Physical16 descent.
- **Exact residual:** construct an independent orientation-odd UV boundary
  datum selecting the split and clock orientation, then derive \(\rho\),
  kernel, gain, and descent.
- **Disposition:** accept the constraint/split fibers conditionally; close
  the attempted selector routes.
