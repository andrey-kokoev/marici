# Source-to-six-port system pencil

## Question

What is the rank of the complete source-to-pole-to-instrument chain before and
after scalar \(\Delta M_s\) compression, and where does information first
become nonfaithful?

## Exact pencil

WP520 supplies an exact rational current kernel

\[
K(z)={N\over D(z)}
\]

with constant nonzero \(N\) and a squarefree degree-six denominator. Normalize
\(D\) to be monic and use its controllable companion realization

\[
P(z)=zI_6-A,\qquad
x=P(z)^{-1}Bu.
\]

Choose \(B=e_6\) and \(C=(N/d_6,0,\ldots,0)\), where \(d_6\) is the leading
coefficient of the original denominator. The checker verifies exactly that

\[
C(zI_6-A)^{-1}B=K(z).
\]

The denominator discriminant is nonzero and the constant numerator is
coprime to it. The realization is therefore minimal. Direct computation gives

\[
\operatorname{rank}[B,AB,\ldots,A^5B]=6
\]

and the dual observability matrix also has rank six.

## Three ranks that must not be conflated

One instantaneous scalar source command enters through \(B\), so its
pointwise image has rank one. The six algebraic contexts generated by repeated
action of \(A\) span the full state space. An identity readout of those six
state coordinates therefore has rank six.

The scalar current output \(C\), and hence a single \(\Delta M_s\)-type
compression, again has rank one with a five-dimensional kernel. The resulting
factorization runs from one source command, through the six-context closure
and six pole ports, to one scalar output. Its successive ranks are \(1\),
\(6\), \(6\), and \(1\). The first rank-one arrow is not a defect: contextual
closure can make a minimal SISO system jointly reachable. The final rank-one
arrow is a distinct readout kernel.

## Source fiber

Along WP537's exact fixed-electroweak family,

\[
a=t^2,\qquad w={1\over t},\qquad v^2=2,
\qquad {g_Ff_{\mathrm{phys}}\over v}={\sqrt3\over t},
\]

the normalized denominator becomes

\[
D_t(z)=t^{-12}D(t^2z).
\]

Its six coefficient derivatives form a nonzero rank-one tangent at \(t=1\),
while the target-ratio derivative is \(-\sqrt3\). The source therefore moves
the pole pencil and the target ratio without changing the six-state instrument
class. This is the system-level version of WP537's hostile pair.

Uniform pole scaling gives the formal scalar current response

\[
K_t(z)=t^2K(t^2z).
\]

Consequently,

\[
{\partial K_t(0)\over\partial t}\bigg|_{t=1}=2K(0)\ne0.
\]

The \(t\)-direction is therefore not in the formal zero-momentum scalar-current
kernel. Whether it survives the physical \(\Delta M_s\) compression is still
unproved because the required calibrated bilocal hadronic functional has not
been supplied.

## Authority boundary

The rank-six Krylov closure is an exact algebraic property of the admitted
source pencil. It is not yet executable physical control. Authority requires
six independent source-derived lattice preparations or equivalent resolvent
contexts, all renormalized in one scheme and returned with WP535's 48-real
covariance.

Nor does contextual faithfulness select \(t\). A separate source equation
must fix \(t\) before any clock-ratio or pole readout is used. The selected
vacuum, complex poles, residues, widths, and likelihood must then be
recomputed at that point.
