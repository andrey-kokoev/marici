# WP153 — trivial-representation amplitude obstruction

## Bounded question

Can a simultaneous symmetry among WP152's three central fixed sectors force
the total correction \(F\) to vanish modulo 96?

## Frozen three-sector coherence

Measure fixed-set Euler values in units of 24:

\[
f=(f_{\rm CP},f_{-1},f_{-\rm CP})\in\mathbb Z^3,
\qquad F=24(f_1+f_2+f_3).
\]

The strongest relation generated solely by permutation coherence of the three
sectors is equality:

\[
f_1-f_2=0,
\qquad f_2-f_3=0.
\]

These two independent relations remove the two-dimensional difference
representation and leave the diagonal kernel

\[
f=a(1,1,1).
\]

## Exact obstruction

On the symmetric kernel,

\[
F=72a.
\]

The modulus-four selector requires \(F=0\pmod{96}\), equivalently
\(3a=0\pmod4\), hence \(a=0\pmod4\). Sector symmetry does not impose this
amplitude condition.

The smallest hostile packet is

\[
f=(1,1,1).
\]

It satisfies every equality relation but gives \(F=72\). At
\(\chi(X/G)=1\),

\[
k=4-\frac{72}{24}=1,
\]

which is the inaccessible sector.

Equivalently, cyclic averaging is a projector onto the diagonal. It removes
differences while preserving the total sum. For example,

\[
(1,2,3)\longmapsto(2,2,2),
\]

and both vectors have total six.

## Selector versus rigidifier

The three-sector symmetry is a rigidifier: it aligns the fixed-set values. It
does not select their invariant amplitude. Adding the equation

\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=0
\]

raises the relation matrix from rank two to rank three and kills the amplitude,
but that equation is exactly the missing fixed-point vanishing law. It cannot
be credited to permutation symmetry merely because it is symmetric.

The weaker congruence \(f_1+f_2+f_3=0\pmod4\) would suffice physically, but it
is likewise an additional discrete source constraint.

## Smallest exact falsifier

\[
f=(1,1,1)
\]

obeys the complete symmetry-generated equality system but yields \(F=72\)
and inaccessible \(k=1\).

## Reopening condition

Derive an amplitude-sensitive equation from a declared anomaly polynomial,
equivariant index, or source action. Its coefficient and modulus must be fixed
before evaluating the desired sector, and it must imply
\(f_1+f_2+f_3=0\pmod4\) on the complete admitted family. Symmetric notation is
not sufficient evidence that the equation is symmetry-generated.
