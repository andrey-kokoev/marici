# Flavon scale-orbit obstruction: WP444

## Question

Does the completed WP443 source action select the absolute dimensionless ratio (g_Ff/v), rather than merely a fully broken normalized vacuum shape?

## Exact dilation

Hold all dimensionless quartic and gauge couplings fixed and apply

\[
(A,D,\phi)\longmapsto s(A,D,\phi),
\qquad
(m^2,\mu^2)\longmapsto s^2(m^2,\mu^2),
\qquad s>0.
\]

Every quadratic term and every quartic term then scales by (s^4). Stationary vacua map to stationary vacua, the normalized adjoint pair and fundamental ray are unchanged, and the gauge-mass rank remains eight. The vacuum energy scales by (s^4).

At the unit-normalized WP443 representative,

\[
f^2=\operatorname{Tr}(A^2+D^2)+\phi^\dagger\phi=1.
\]

Along the dilation orbit (f\mapsto sf), so with fixed electroweak reference (v),

\[
\frac{g_Ff}{v}\longmapsto s\frac{g_Ff}{v}.
\]

The exact hostile pair (s=1) and (s=2) has identical normalized vacuum shape and full gauge rank but differs by a factor of two in the clock ratio and in every flavor-gauge pole mass.

## Disposition

WP443 selects neither an absolute flavon scale nor (g_Ff/v). The full-breaking vacuum is a dynamical rigidifier of the gauge presentation and a source-free vacuum-shape construction, but the admitted action retains an independent relevant mass scale.

Flavor-current data can place conditional lower bounds on (f) or on mediator masses. Such bounds are physical readouts, not source-generated numerical selectors. A genuine selection claim requires a new source-derived relation tying the flavon relevant deformation to the electroweak clock and surviving the complete allowed coefficient ensemble.

## Smallest exact falsifier
