# Common-order residue and width composition

## Purpose and corrected authority

WP518 matches WP509 quark-current residues and WP517 vector partial widths in
one fourteen-pole ordering at the WP516 source witness. WP519 retracts the
\(B_s\)-compatibility label: the ratio passes a formal contact inequality, but
the propagating pole packet lacks a threshold-matching descent to WP511's WET
domain. The residue and width algebra remains valid on the source-model domain.

## Residue reconstruction

For each cubic sector, at simple root \(\rho\),

\[
{\rm tr}\,R_{r,\rho}
=\frac{\operatorname{tr}N_r(\rho)}
{(\rho-Q)C_r'(\rho)},\qquad Q=3g_F^2\mu^2.
\]

All nine roots match uniquely to nonquintet entries with squared-mass error
below \(10^{-10}\ {\rm GeV}^2\). The other five entries are the exact quintet
pole \(Q=6\ {\rm GeV}^2\), each with residue trace \(g_F^2=2\).
Every cubic residue is positive, each cubic sector obeys

\[
\sum_{C_r(\rho)=0}{\rm tr}\,R_{r,\rho}=g_F^2,
\]

and the full packet obeys

\[
\sum_i{\rm tr}\,R_i=8g_F^2=16.
\]

## Width composition

For six effectively massless quarks,

\[
\frac{\Gamma_{q,i}}{M_i}=\frac{{\rm tr}\,R_i}{4\pi}.
\]

Resolved vector widths add only to states 4, 5, 12, and 13:

| State | Mass (GeV) | Quark fraction | Vector width (GeV) | Lower fraction |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 | 2.26520696 | 0.001284565 | 0.004931517 | 0.003461636 |
| 5 | 2.26520705 | 0.001284565 | 0.004931519 | 0.003461636 |
| 12 | 4.94624847 | \(7.08053\mathbin{\cdot}10^{-8}\) | 0.0000186566 | \(3.84268\mathbin{\cdot}10^{-6}\) |
| 13 | 4.94626372 | \(7.08032\mathbin{\cdot}10^{-8}\) | 0.0000186559 | \(3.84251\mathbin{\cdot}10^{-6}\) |

The five quintet states each have quark fractional width \(1/(2\pi)\).

- Classification: common-order independently normalized partial-width lower
  bounds; neither selector nor total-width packet.
- Instrument: no admitted source-to-WET map or pole-resolved detector
  likelihood.
- Smallest exact falsifier: a root fails unique matching, a residue is
  nonpositive, or a sector violates completeness.

Scalar, off-shell, and sub-resolution channels remain outside the sums.
