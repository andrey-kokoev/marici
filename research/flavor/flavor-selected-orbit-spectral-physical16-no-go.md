# Selected-orbit spectral physical16 no-go: WP1011

## Question

Can the unique WP1009 coefficient orbit descend to the fitted physical16
domain through the smallest weak-basis-covariant spectral maps?

## Admitted map class

Take the selected representative

\[
X=\operatorname{diag}(-1,0,1),\qquad
Y=\begin{pmatrix}
0&1&-6i/5\\
1&0&1\\
6i/5&1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The tested map class is

\[
H_u=f(X),\qquad H_d=g(Y),
\]

where f and g are real spectral functions that keep each spectrum
nondegenerate and positive. Such maps descend under simultaneous weak-basis
conjugation, but they preserve the spectral projectors of X and Y. Therefore
they preserve the CKM matrix, up to row/column phases and simultaneous
permutations.

This is a map-class test. No propagation time or causal ordering is assigned.

The characteristic polynomial and discriminant of Y are

\[
\chi_Y(\lambda)=\frac{\lambda(25\lambda^2-86)}{25},
\qquad
\Delta_Y^2=\frac{2544224}{15625}.
\]

For X, the Vandermonde factor is 2, and

\[
\det[X,Y]=\frac{24i}{5}.
\]

Using the WP109 identity

\[
\det[H_u,H_d]=2iJ\Delta_u\Delta_d,
\]

the selected orbit has

\[
J^2=\frac{5625}{636056},
\qquad |J|\simeq0.0940402.
\]

This value is invariant under every nondegenerate map in the admitted spectral
class.

## Complete fitted-ensemble test

All 1,210 stored viable physical16 sheets have

3.1413\times10^{-5}

The direct identity map also fails positivity because X has a negative

preserves the excluded CKM projectors and merely fits three spectral

## Classification

The WP1009 operation is a global selector on an auxiliary coefficient
quotient. Under every separately spectral, weak-basis-covariant map to Yukawa
Gram matrices, its physical16 image is empty on the fitted ensemble. It is
neither a viable physical selector nor a texture rigidifier.

A source-derived mixed covariant map

\[
(X,Y)\longmapsto(H_u,H_d)
\]

that changes the relative spectral projectors, remains nondegenerate and
positive, and lands in the fitted physical16 domain would falsify this
map-class no-go. A polynomial fitted directly to the measured CKM matrix is
not source-derived.

## Claim boundary

The result excludes separate spectral functional calculus maps, not all
possible mixed mediator/Yukawa portals. The stored ensemble is an external
viability test, not source authority. The coefficient score still lacks a
calibrated instrument.

## Disposition

next admissible arrow must be a named mixed source portal that changes
