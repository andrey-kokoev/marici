# Boundary-instrument falsification gate: WP1262

## Question

Can the strongest algebraic survivors falsify the one-boundary-instrument
conjecture without new boundary source data?

## DPC resolution

- **Problem:** test whether a matrix-first route can refute the conjecture
  that the five Flavor openings are projections of one missing source-derived
  boundary instrument.
- **Bold conjecture:** a support-four or exact support-five algebraic
  survivor can supply phase and physical production independently of the
  missing boundary instrument.
- **Named rivals:** support-four interior phase lift; regular support-four
  carrier class; boundary or irregular candidate; exact support-five
  unistochastic survivor; modulus-to-production route; phase-gauge quotient;
  fitted physical16 channel; minimum-rank kernel; UV ensemble matching.
- **Risky consequences:** WP1160 gives unequal two-overlap products
  \(1/1764\) and \(83/5292\); WP1161 imposes at least 18 two-overlap
  constraints on every support-four carrier; WP1162, WP1163, WP1165, and
  WP1166 leave no regular support-four interior class; WP1167 rejects all six
  known boundary or irregular candidates; WP1171 certifies an exact real
  orthogonal support-five fixed-\(q\) solution; WP1172 leaves 11 unobserved
  phase parameters and no production components; WP1174 leaves a
  69-dimensional generic channel fiber; WP1175 and WP1176 supply no source
  certificate or boundary state.
- **Strongest falsification attempt:** replay WP1160--WP1176 and force the
  strongest exact support-five survivor through production, phase-gauge,
  channel, kernel, and UV-state gates without adding boundary source data.
- **Exact residual:** the conjecture survives this attempt; a boundary
  density/instrument packet or an independent lawful composition remains
  required.
- **Disposition:** attempted refutation fails conditionally;
  boundary-density instrument rival selected.

## Result

The exact support-five unistochastic certificate is the strongest algebraic
survivor, but it supplies no sourced production map, selected physical16
channel, source-certified kernel, boundary density matrix, or microstate
uniformity law. The one-boundary-instrument conjecture is therefore **not
refuted** by the current matrix-first corpus. Survival is evidence for the
conjecture, not proof of it.
