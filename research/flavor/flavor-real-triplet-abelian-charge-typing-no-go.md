# Real-triplet Abelian-charge typing no-go: WP725

## Question

Can the WP715–WP716 Abelian moment-map mechanism act directly on the admitted
real irreducible \(SO(3)\) flavor triplets?

## Exact commutant theorem

Let \(X\) generate a continuous internal transformation on one real triplet.
To coexist with diagonal flavor \(SO(3)\), it must commute with all three
triplet generators:

\[
[X,L_a]=0.
\]

The exact commutant is

\[
X=cI_3.
\]

Preserving the real kinetic metric also requires

\[
X^T=-X.
\]

These conditions imply \(c=0\), hence \(X=0\). A connected \(U(1)\) has no
nontrivial orthogonal action on one real irreducible triplet. Therefore

\[
q_n=q_m=0
\]

on the original carrier, and a direct Abelian moment-map portal contrast
vanishes.

## Minimal domain enlargement

A nonzero commuting Abelian action requires a complex structure. On two real
triplets it is generated by

\[
J=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-I_3\\
I_3&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
J^2=-I_6.
\]

This generator is nonzero, skew, and commutes with block-diagonal \(SO(3)\).
But it doubles each triplet's real dimension. It introduces phase directions
and a larger quotient; it is a new relational experiment rather than an
operation descending to the existing `physical16` flavor carrier.

## Correction to WP715 and WP716

The charge-algebra identities in WP715 and WP716 remain correct on a
complexified charged-triplet domain. They do not define direct operations on
the admitted real-triplet domain. Their earlier wording is corrected
accordingly.

Consequently the clock-locked architecture of WP724 cannot be realized by
assigning commuting Abelian charges directly to \(n\) and \(m\). A viable
matter model must place the representation asymmetry in charged messengers or
auxiliary fields and prove that eliminating them induces a weak-basis-invariant
operation on the real-triplet quotient.

## Claim boundary and disposition

WP725 is a typing no-go, not a no-go against every ultraviolet portal. It rules
out one direct carrier assignment. The progressive branch is mediated descent:
derive unequal representation factors in an anomaly-free charged messenger
sector, integrate it out, and test whether the resulting real-triplet portal
retains sign, normalization, angular stiffness, RG basin, threshold support,
and labelled readout.
