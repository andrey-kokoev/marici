# Charged flavon bifurcation preregistration: WP440

## Purpose

WP439 proves that an invariant gradient cannot linearly source a direction charged under the WP438 residual (U(1)). WP440 freezes a curvature-driven successor before solving its vacuum or inspecting measured flavor.

This is a preregistration, not a vacuum result.

## Source authorization

The field content and symmetries are unchanged: two traceless Hermitian adjoints of gauged diagonal (SU(3)_F), simultaneous conjugation, exchange symmetry, independent sign flips, and power-counting renormalizability. No Yukawa, CKM, texture, `physical16`, threshold, or detector datum is admitted.

WP437 included the squared commutator channel but omitted its equally local complementary product channel, the squared anticommutator. Completing this product grammar gives

\[
V_{440}=-\frac{m^2}{2}R+\rho R^2
-\lambda\lVert[A,D]\rVert_F^2
-\eta\lVert\{A,D\}\rVert_F^2,
\qquad
R=\operatorname{Tr}A^2+\operatorname{Tr}D^2.
\]

The coefficient domain is frozen as

\[
m^2>0,\qquad \lambda>0,\qquad \eta>0,
\qquad \rho>\frac{\lambda}{2}+\eta.
\]

The final inequality is a conservative, source-independent coercivity margin obtained from the Frobenius bounds on the two product channels. It is not optimized after solving the vacuum.

## Why this can reach the missing directions

At the WP438 embedded-Pauli representative the anticommutator vanishes, so the new invariant has zero gradient and does not evade WP439. Its Hessian, however, has negative eigenvalues in the eight-component residual-charged fluctuation block. It therefore supplies a genuine bifurcation channel rather than an imported orientation.

The successor calculation must determine whether the total charged curvature crosses zero inside the frozen coercive domain and whether the emerging branch is globally stable and fully Higgses (SU(3)_F).

2. Prove the complete Hessian is nonnegative and its kernel is exactly the gauge orbit.
3. Prove boundedness and global, not merely local, preference.

## Frozen falsifiers

- every stable global minimum retains a continuous stabilizer;
- full breaking exists only at an isolated or target-fitted coefficient point;
- a nongauge Hessian zero or negative mode remains;
