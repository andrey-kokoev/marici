# Flavor-clock normalization correction

## Defect

WP485–WP487 originally used \(f\) for the coefficient in \(X_i=fJ_i\). Earlier
programme packets reserve the physical flavor clock for the invariant norm.
Those quantities differ and must not share a label.

## Canonical normalization

Write

\[
X_i=\mu J_i,
\qquad
\sum_i\operatorname{Tr}J_i^2=6.
\]

Then

\[
f_{\rm phys}^2=\sum_i\operatorname{Tr}X_i^2=6\mu^2.
\]

WP485's quintet pole coordinate is

\[
Q=3g_F^2\mu^2.
\]

Consequently the physical clock readout is

\[
{g_Ff_{\rm phys}\over v}={\sqrt{2Q}\over v}.
\]

## Scope of correction

The mass Gram, five–three–three multiplicities, residue weights, WP486 inverse,
rank-four response, and WP487 closure inequalities are unchanged under this
coordinate repair. The corrected packets now use \(\mu\) for the adjoint
amplitude and reserve \(f_{\rm phys}\) for the invariant norm.

Any interpretation that identifies \(\mu=f_{\rm phys}\) misses the requested
clock ratio by the exact factor \(\sqrt6\). No numerical selector is created by
this correction.
