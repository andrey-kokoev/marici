# Higgs rate and leading-width gate

## Frozen response domain

WP472 tests WP471's withheld Higgs residue. The leading domain assumes:

- no non-Standard-Model decay channel is open below `m_H/2`;
- vectorlike colored messengers are decoupled from Higgs production;
- no new loop amplitude competes with the Standard Model production loops;
- all Standard Model Higgs vertices are rescaled only by the Higgs-radial
  component of the calibrated pole;
- the narrow-width factorization used by the experimental signal-strength
  combination applies.

On this domain every Standard Model partial width and production cross section
is multiplied by the WP471 residue `Z_H`, while branching fractions remain
unchanged. Therefore

\[
\mu_{\rm pred}=Z_H
={45822595065274178\over68734007773010799}
\simeq0.66666555.
\]

## Calibrated rate instrument

The 2025 Particle Data Group summary table reports the combined-final-state
signal strength

\[
\mu_{\rm obs}=1.03\mathbin{\pm}0.04.
\]

The prediction lies below the frozen 95-percent working lower edge
`1.03-1.96(0.04)=0.9516`. Its Gaussian standardized residual is about `9.08`.
Thus the mass-calibrated unit-geometry portal is rejected by the withheld rate
instrument.

Opening exotic decays cannot repair the mixing-only rate deficit: it further
reduces Standard Model branching fractions. A repair needs an independently
derived new production amplitude or a different source geometry with larger
Higgs residue; either changes the admitted response model and must be tested
anew.

## Independently frozen leading width

The same PDG review quotes the Standard Model prediction
`Gamma_H^SM=4.10 plus or minus 0.06 MeV`. With no new open channel, WP471 gives
the independently predicted central width

\[
\Gamma_H^{\rm pred}=Z_H\Gamma_H^{\rm SM}
\simeq2.73333\ {\rm MeV}.
\]

The PDG direct/off-shell summary `3.7` MeV with asymmetric uncertainty is too
coarse to reject this width. Width compatibility does not override the much
sharper rate failure; the two instruments test different response coordinates.

## Classification

- Selector: unchanged conditional common-clock selector.
- Residue: independently frozen by WP471.
- Width: independently derived at leading order on the declared closed-new-
  channel domain.
- Instrument: PDG combined signal strength and width summary.
- Outcome: rate-rejected; width-compatible but nonrescuing.
- Smallest exact falsifier: `Z_H < 0.9516`.
- Reopening gate: preregister a source-derived production enhancement or a new
  portal geometry, recompute mass, residues, and all widths, and retain the
  combined signal strength as a withheld test.
