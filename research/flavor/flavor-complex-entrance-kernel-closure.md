# Positive complex neutral entrance-kernel closure

## Additional invariant resources

The real (SO(3)_E) representation admits holomorphic quadratic contractions
in addition to Hermitian ones. Add four positive quartic invariants to WP504:

\[
\alpha_u((u^\dagger u)^2-|u^Tu|^2),
\qquad
\alpha_d((d^\dagger d)^2-|d^Td|^2),
\]

\[
\rho|u^Td|^2,
\qquad
\eta\left|u^Tu-{a^2\over b^2}d^Td\right|^2,
\]

with positive coefficients. The first two are nonnegative by the triangle
inequality and also admit explicit sums of real squares. Every term is
renormalizable and invariant under the real row action and common hypercharge.

## Exact kernel closure

The real orthogonal vacuum

\[
u=a e_3,
\qquad d=b e_1
\]

remains a global zero. On the twelve-real-dimensional neutral space, the
completed Hessian has rank eight and nullity four. Its kernel is exactly the
three row-gauge tangents plus the common hypercharge phase. All four physical
flat witnesses from WP504 acquire nonzero Hessian response.

Thus the complex neutral kernel is closed without adding a new reference port.
The physical groupoid is still the enlarged row-plus-electroweak gauge quotient.

## Authority cost

The phase-locking square contains the dimensionless input (a^2/b^2). It
ensures that the declared vacuum is compatible with phase locking, but it does
not derive that ratio. The operation is a spectrum rigidifier, not a numerical
selector.

This result is still restricted to neutral row order parameters. Charged
doublet components, connector mixing, gauge mixing, pole residues, and decay
channels remain outside the packet. Their Hessian and instrument must be
derived before any width is frozen.
