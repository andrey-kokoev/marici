## Authority boundary

The matrices are valid members of the Hermitian coefficient-field domain and
all scores descend under simultaneous weak-basis conjugation. But they were
found by an analyst-side witness search. No source action presently prepares
this exact orbit, fixes the (1/22) entry ratio, proves it is a global coupled
minimum against the full configuration domain, or resets it reproducibly.

Therefore this is a source-domain capacity theorem and formal instrument
architecture, not a source-selected state or physical instrument.

## Smallest exact falsifier

Replacing (1/22) by zero kills the determinant score and collapses the
candidate onto the rank-deficient boundary. More decisively, any admitted
configuration with lower energy than (C) throughout its proposed positive
exposure interval falsifies the global preparation claim.

## Claim boundary

WP1005 proves strict exposure only within the exact three-configuration set
({A,C,B}). It does not prove that (C) is exposed in the full Hermitian
configuration space. It does not infer source authority from realizability.
