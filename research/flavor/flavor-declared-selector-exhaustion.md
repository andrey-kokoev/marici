# Declared flavor-selector exhaustion

Work package: WP931

## Scope

This packet asks whether any constructor already declared through WP930 passes
all five gates required of a physical flavor selector:

1. independent source authority;
2. descent to `physical16`;
3. a proper reduction of the admissible family;
4. isolation of a distinguished point rather than a residual fiber;
5. a typed physical instrument.

The claim is relative to the current Spin5 and conditional Spin7 grammar.  It
does not quantify over unknown ultraviolet theories.

## Exhaustion

The normalized CP portal and the double-commutator gradient descend and would
reduce `physical16`, but neither is generated by the declared source.  The
completion rank-index record is source-authorized and distinguishing, but it
acts on upstream completion labels and does not prepare either label or act on
`physical16`.

Orbifold parity and exchange-reflection rigidify field content or conjugate
channel presentation only after extra boundary choices.  Even on the exchange
fixed locus, an arbitrary complex family tensor remains.  The exact diagonal
hostiles

\[
Y_A=\operatorname{diag}(1,2,3),\qquad
Y_B=\operatorname{diag}(1,2,4)
\]

obey the same exchange relation but have normalized Gram discriminants
\(40/243\) and \(135/1024\).

The complete cubic one- and two-tensor normal forms have no isolated
nondegenerate hierarchical CP fixed point.  The first commutator covariant is
Lax transport, and the double-commutator contraction has no source-authorized
Yukawa lift.  No row reaches the instrument gate.

## Verdict

Within the declared grammar, flavor currently contains completion
identification, conditional field-content and interaction rigidification, and
transport.  It contains no source-authorized operation that selects a proper
subspace or distinguished point of the admissible `physical16` family.

The negative result is constructive: each rejected branch has a named first
nonfaithful or unauthorized arrow.  A successor must begin with a genuinely
new independently declared three-family source action or geometry.  Extending
the fitted-scalar, conditional-boundary, or closed Spin5 double-commutator
branches would not answer the selector question.
