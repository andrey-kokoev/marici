# Residual-stabilizer gradient obstruction: WP439

## Bounded question

Can a newly added, independently justified renormalizable invariant directly push the WP438 embedded-Pauli vacuum into directions that break its residual (U(1))?

## Admitted domain and quotient

The state domain is a pair of traceless Hermitian adjoints (A,D\in\mathfrak{su}(3)). The physical equivalence used here is simultaneous conjugation by the newly gauged (SU(3)_F). At the WP438 representative,

\[
A=a\lambda_1,\qquad D=a\lambda_2,
\]

embedded in the upper two-dimensional block. Its stabilizer is generated by

\[
H=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-2).
\]

This is a vacuum-geometry statement in the enlarged flavor theory. It is not a claim about weak-basis gauge fixing and supplies no detector instrument.

## Exact result

For any differentiable simultaneous-conjugation invariant (V(A,D)), equivariance of its gradient implies that the gradient at a point fixed by (H) is itself fixed by (H). Consequently its component in every nontrivial (U(1)) weight space vanishes.

The checker exhausts all 28 binary trace words in (A,D) of degrees two through four. For perturbations of either adjoint along each of the four Hermitian generators connecting the upper block to the third flavor direction, every first directional derivative is exactly zero.

Thus neither the natural cubic Casimirs nor any other renormalizable trace-word invariant can linearly tilt the WP438 vacuum into a residual-charged direction. This corrects the tempting but invalid move of merely adding an allowed cubic and expecting full Higgsing.

## Hostile control

An explicitly oriented linear source has a nonzero derivative along a charged direction. It evades the obstruction precisely because it imports a flavor orientation and is not simultaneous-conjugation invariant. It therefore cannot count as source-free selection.

## Consequence

Full breaking can occur only through a different mechanism:

1. an independently frozen invariant changes a charged Hessian eigenvalue through zero and a lower-stabilizer branch is followed;
2. a disconnected generic vacuum is globally preferred; or
3. an additional dynamical representation changes the source grammar.

The next admissible test is therefore a preregistered charged-mode bifurcation calculation for the general source-authorized renormalizable potential, not a coefficient search against measured flavor.

## Smallest exact falsifier

One simultaneous-conjugation-invariant trace polynomial of degree at most four with a nonzero first derivative at the WP438 representative along any of the four residual-(U(1))-charged Hermitian directions falsifies the computed claim.

## Claim boundary

WP439 proves a first-order obstruction only. It does not prove that all two-adjoint potentials retain (U(1)), exclude charged Hessian instabilities, classify disconnected minima, select (g_F f/v), map a vacuum to `physical16`, or supply a physical instrument.
