# Threshold source-candidate gate: WP1187

## Question

Can the finite chiral fixed-point census construct a threshold source
candidate?

## DPC resolution

- **Conjecture:** a sub-unit fixed-point packet supplies the threshold
  boundary source.
- **Rivals:** the \((6,30)\) candidate; the \((8,39)\) candidate; WP805's
  strongly coupled packet; a WP820 incidence-derived candidate.
- **Risky consequences:** exactly two sub-unit candidates survive, each has
  one infrared-repulsive direction, and no WP820-to-GG constructor exists.
- **Falsification attempt:** both exact candidates were tested against the
  five-field threshold packet. Each supplies only a conditional RG object and
  lacks boundary authority, sector basis, transport, and production
  normalization.
- **Residual:** an oriented-incidence-to-spectrum constructor and threshold
  transport remain missing.
- **Disposition:** construct the candidate pair; reject threshold source
  authority.
