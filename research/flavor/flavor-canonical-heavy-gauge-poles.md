# Canonical heavy gauge poles and current resolvents

## Kinetic normalization

Use (SU(3)_F) generators (\lambda_A/2). The canonical field-space metric
has weight one on real connector coordinates and weight two on both adjoint
coordinates and the real–imaginary coordinates of complex doublets. This
recovers

\[
f_{\rm phys}^2=6\mu^2,
\qquad
Q=3g_F^2\mu^2,
\qquad
v_{\rm phys}^2=2(a^2+b^2).
\]

## Exact fourteen-pole factorization

The heavy flavor–port–row mass matrix has dimension fourteen. In the
Gell-Mann coordinate basis, its exact sparsity components are two singleton
blocks and three four-generator blocks.

Each singleton is a quintet pole at (Q). Each four-generator characteristic
polynomial factors into the same quintet factor (z-Q) and one cubic mixed
sector. Hence the complete spectrum contains five quintet factors and three
cubic flavor–port–row sectors.

The three cubic polynomials share the form

\[
\begin{aligned}
C_r(z)={}&z^3-
\left[g_F^2\mu^2+4g_P^2\mu^2+2g_P^2s^2
+2g_E^2(s^2+r)\right]z^2\\
&+\left[2g_F^2g_P^2\mu^2s^2
+2g_E^2g_F^2\mu^2s^2
+8g_E^2g_P^2\mu^2s^2\right.\\
&\left.\qquad
+r(2g_E^2g_F^2\mu^2+8g_E^2g_P^2\mu^2
+4g_E^2g_P^2s^2)\right]z\\
&-4r g_E^2g_F^2g_P^2\mu^2s^2,
\end{aligned}
\]

for

\[
r\in\{a^2,a^2+b^2,b^2\}.
\]

The entrance vacuum therefore splits the three formerly identical WP485
mixed sectors.

## Matrix current readout

Each four-generator block contains two Gell-Mann flavor coordinates, one port
coordinate, and one entrance-row coordinate. One flavor combination belongs
to the quintet and the other to the embedded triplet. The exact source-normalized
(2\times2) flavor-current resolvent is

\[
g_F^2[(zI-M_r^2)^{-1}]_{FF}.
\]

The generated result records its exact adjugate numerator and factored
denominator for all three sectors. This freezes the rational current-response
grammar before choosing cubic roots. Individual pole locations and residues
remain functions of (g_F,g_P,g_E,\mu,s,a,b).

## Disposition

WP508 supplies canonical pole polynomials and exact current resolvents, not
numerical poles or widths. A physical instrument must still couple calibrated
currents to these matrix responses, and every open decay channel must be
included before total widths are frozen.
