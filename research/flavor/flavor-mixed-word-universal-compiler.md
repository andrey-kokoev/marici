# Mixed-word universal compiler: WP1014

## Question

After the minimal linear portal fails, can additional weak-basis-covariant
mixed words supply a physical16 image without merely fitting the answer?

## Exact word algebra

For the WP1009 representative \(X,Y\), consider the nine Hermitian words

\[
I,\ X,\ Y,\ X^2,\ Y^2,\ \{X,Y\},\
i[X,Y],\ XYX,\ YXY.
\]

Represent a Hermitian \(3\times3\) matrix by its three real diagonal entries
and the real and imaginary parts of its three upper-triangular entries. The
resulting \(9\times9\) coefficient matrix has

\[
\det B=-\frac{27936}{625}\neq0.
\]

These words therefore form a real basis of the complete Hermitian matrix
space. Their common Hermitian commutant is the scalar line, consistent with
irreducibility of the generated matrix algebra.

Each word transforms by simultaneous conjugation when \(X,Y\) do. Hence any
real linear combination descends under the full weak-basis groupoid.

## Quotient capacity

Let a target positive nondegenerate Gram pair be given. Use one simultaneous
weak-basis transformation to identify the target \(H_d\) eigenbasis with the
\(Y\) eigenbasis. Since \(Y\) has three distinct eigenvalues, a quadratic
spectral polynomial

\[
H_d=d_0I+d_1Y+d_2Y^2
\]

interpolates its three target eigenvalues uniquely. In that basis, the nine
mixed words reconstruct the arbitrary target \(H_u\) uniquely.

Thus unrestricted real coefficients in this finite word family reach every
positive nondegenerate Gram pair modulo simultaneous weak-basis conjugation.
In particular, they can reach the complete fitted physical16 domain.

## Why this is not selection

The reconstruction coefficients are obtained by inverting \(B\) on the target
matrix. They therefore encode the desired physical point. With coefficients
unrestricted, the portal image is the full admissible quotient; it selects no
proper subspace and no distinguished point.

This is algebraic span, not executable control. No admitted flavor source
action independently prepares the nine word coefficients, the three spectral
coefficients, or their normalization. The construction introduces no new
physical instrument.

## Smallest exact falsifier

Removing the word \(i[X,Y]\) reduces the span to rank eight. Its unique left
kernel pairs nontrivially with the omitted direction, giving an explicit
nonzero obstruction. Any claimed smaller compiler must kill that obstruction
or provide a different exact rank-nine basis.

A zero determinant for the displayed basis, a degenerate \(Y\) spectrum, or a
target Hermitian matrix outside the reconstructed span would falsify the
universal-capacity theorem.

## Claim boundary

The result proves finite algebraic reachability for the selected pair. It does
not establish source authorization, a physical preparation operation,
repeatable control, detector calibration, or a numerical prediction.
Target-dependent coefficients cannot be read backward as source-derived
couplings. Universal capacity is not evidence that the WP1009 orbit selects
flavor.

## Disposition

Stop enlarging the portal by unconstrained covariant words: the finite word
algebra is already universal. The remaining research question is whether an
independent source principle restricts or fixes its coefficients before
physical16 data are consulted.
