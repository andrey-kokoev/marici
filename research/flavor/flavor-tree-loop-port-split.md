# Coherent-tree and traced-loop connector split

## Two source operations

The canonical tensor grammar has a normalized equal entrance, componentwise
connector incidence, and identity messenger masses. These data generate two
different contractions.

Tree-level elimination preserves amplitude coherence across the entrance
routes. Its adjoint-port vector is

\[
p=S^Th.
\]

A closed threshold loop instead traces the propagating row label. With the
normalized equal-route shape, its pairing is

\[
W_{\rm loop}=S^TDS,
\qquad D={1\over3}I_3.
\]

The tree outer product (pp^T) and the traced loop Gram (W_{\rm loop}) are
different source operations. They cannot be identified merely because both
contain the same connector field.

## Exact vacuum ranks

Use the adapted basis in which (h=(1,0,0)^T), and set (S=sI_3). Then

\[
p=(s,0,0)^T,
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}(pp^T)=1,
\]

whereas

\[
W_{\rm loop}={s^2\over3}I_3,
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}W_{\rm loop}=3,
\qquad
\det W_{\rm loop}={s^6\over27}.
\]

The factor (1/3) follows from the WP492 normalized entrance shape. It may be
absorbed into a separately declared scalar coupling, but it cannot silently be
dropped when absolute residues or widths are claimed.

## Contextual partition of connector modes

On the six physical connector modes of WP499, the tree-port Jacobian has rank
three. It sees the longitudinal singlet and the entrance-vector doublet, while
the transverse-trace singlet and traceless-tensor doublet form an exact
three-dimensional kernel.

The formal loop-Gram Jacobian has rank six. Thus the traced loop is algebraically
faithful on the full physical connector tangent space even though the coherent
tree readout is not. No detector instrument currently realizes all six loop
coordinates, so formal faithfulness is not yet experimental faithfulness.

## Up/down hostile consequence

With one shared connector frame and the same frozen entrance shape, the up and
down linear-word tree ports are scalar multiples of the same vector. Their
combined adjoint-label port matrix has rank one. Independent scalar couplings
change normalization but do not create a second flavor direction.

Therefore the loop-level isotropic portal repair does not by itself provide a
generic tree-level map to `physical16`. A generic Yukawa pair requires
independently labelled external entrance channels, additional messenger words,
or another declared rank-three tree constructor.

## Disposition

WP500 preserves the loop rigidifier but rejects transport of its rank to the
tree-level flavor map. Connector residues and widths must be tagged by which
operation produces them. The smallest exact falsifier is the rank pair one
versus three at the same frame vacuum.
