## 4. Differences that must stay separate

### Positron: inverse seed versus conjugate coefficient data

The atlas describes an illustrative viewer using conjugate phases and retains an unconstructed charge-conjugation/annihilation gate. The newer arrow entry reverses the electron affine seed generator, retaining metric and geometry. Its `arrow_reversal_of` field identifies electron; its physical-identification flag remains false.

These statements concern different operations. No conflict is inferred, but no full-native-protocol intertwiner or physical charge-conjugation operator is imported either.

### Photon: retained stage versus completed geometry

The collection records a two-face stage with f-vector (4,5,2), while unchanged full completion still has four supporting facets. This distinction is explicit in both the live entry and photon note. A separately retained continuation plane does not derive why that stage is emitted, a two-output factorization or a physical photon channel.

### Electron: geometric orbit versus coefficient carrier

The atlas separately checks pentachoron geometry and tetrahedral spinorial constructions, while keeping compatible five-cell spinor/energy transport unconstructed. The arrow record supplies geometric seed action, not that missing transport. No shared particle name can identify their state spaces or operations.

Proton and neutron geometry links likewise do not import energy, memory, coupling or transfer-cost protocols from catalogue labels.
