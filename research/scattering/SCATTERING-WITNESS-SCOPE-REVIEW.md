## Criterion

A claim of equivalence, faithful reduction, or reusable realization must say which source constructions, retained records and admitted continuations it preserves, and provide the corresponding maps/witnesses. A scalar or terminal readout theorem may be perfectly valid without doing this: label it as such rather than silently promoting it to construction equivalence.

This does **not** require measuring every coherent path or retaining infinite classical history. Recording a traversal can change interference. Preserve the source's actual coherent state and its declared records; identify only distinctions irrelevant to the specified continuation family, with a justification. Reuse ledger 4296 (WG), 4310 (witnessed assembly), 4312–4313 (typed identities/gluing/fillers/transport), 2065 (probe faithfulness), and 4224 (joint refutation). See `research/conjecture_net/REFERENCED-PACKET-COHERENCE-HANDOFF.md` for links and the operator's formulation.

Review categories: **keep** = sharp within its scope; **scope repair** = precise algebra but a stronger interpretation is unearned; **sharpen** = missing hypotheses, quantifiers or failure criterion; **split** = bundles logically different statements or remains a work area.

### Decisive source fact for the new philosophy

`high-energy/pentachoron_open_product_flux_note.md`, section 'Coherent order dependence', says:

- the three face projectors do not commute;
- six ordered shortest products are added coherently, with a specific nontrivial operator identity;
- traversal histories are **not** separately measured or permanently copied to history registers.

Therefore 'both constructions must agree' must not be imposed on every switch order. Distinct histories may be physically different contributions. The right comparison is between independently derived **representations of the same full coherent process**, preserving the order dependence. A witness is not permission to identify noncommuting processes.

`strain_diagnostics_note.md` likewise retains intra-degeneracy coherence and distinguishes energy-resolved histograms from distance-dependent coherent observations. The source already guards this distinction better than the top-level net statements. `response128_packet_note.md` explicitly restricts four prepared columns and fixed-grid/fixed-quadrature comparisons; its 14.244725% quadrature change defeats interpreting the small response-prefix difference as a total error bound.

## Proposed repair pattern — reuse rather than replace

For each claim of faithful transport, specify source preparations P, licensed operations U, retained state/record spaces, comparisons E and target operations U'. The required commuting comparison is, in its appropriate exact or approximate sense,

    E(U(x)) ~ U'(E(x)),

with domain, source assumptions and retained complements explicit. If the maps are composable through two independent presentations, compare the resulting witnesses as in `closure-four-presentations-hott-proof.md`, rather than only checking scalar readouts.

For approximate numerical work, choose a declared family of continuations/readouts and bound their errors from a full-state norm estimate. Do not demand equality under arbitrary unbounded observations, infer a positive construction from no contradiction, or require a mathematically irrelevant tetrahedral package for every scalar estimate.

Suggested order of repairs:
1. Clarify C10/C8 as scoped carrier/readout diagnostics; do not replace the operator's proof proposal with them.
2. Split high-energy local switch correctness from coherent order composition; reuse actual source Q_f and J_f.
3. Put numerical norms, input classes and thresholds into the quadrature/tail/continuum propositions before asking the planner to treat them as decisive tests.
4. Form one candidate operation-preserving comparison using existing retained-source maps. Carry its witness and continuation test; do not create a new universal comparison framework.
