## 4. Retained unitarity deficit

A finite positive W=W2+W3 is not automatically an exact probability. Keeping the incoming state unchanged and simply adding the orthogonal photon Born output gives squared norm 1+W. The adjoint process and the elastic/forward correction cannot be deleted. For a self-adjoint off-diagonal first-order block K, (I+i*K)^dagger*(I+i*K)=I+K^2. At second order an elastic amplitude D must satisfy 2*Re(<in|D|in>)=-W. Its phase and other dynamics are not determined by the positive strengths alone.

Indeed choose the reference scalar coefficient g2*m^3=1 and a pure singlet. Using pi<22/7, pi^2<10 and the lower bound on I6 gives W2>1. This is a deliberately nonperturbative reference value, not a physically assigned coupling or a valid exact probability. Even at arbitrarily weak nonzero coefficients, the missing elastic correction is required at the corresponding perturbative order. Renormalizing only the photon output would condition on conversion and erase survival rather than establish unitarity.
