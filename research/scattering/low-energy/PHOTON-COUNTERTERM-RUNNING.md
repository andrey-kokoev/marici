## 1. Precisely which basis is constructed

The [crossed IR-log audit](CROSSED-PHOTON-IR-LOGS.md) gives B_log=-(r^2/pi)*sum_a H_a*log(-S_a/nu), with q=2*k=4,6,8 in its three leading-IR monomial regimes. T=(b/2)*A+b^2*B+..., b=epsilon2^2. Here construct a **minimal local on-shell basis in the parity-even, log-supported all-equal-duality and two-plus/two-minus sectors** at each homogeneous momentum degree 2*q. It suffices to cancel the logarithmic reference-scale dependence.

This is NOT the complete off-shell EFT operator basis or full EFT renormalization. Three-plus/one-minus finite contact terms, parity-odd sectors, other orders, off-shell/EOM/evanescent representatives and higher-point counterterms are not classified or excluded. An on-shell polynomial representative is not a proof of an interacting local QFT. The leading r is held fixed at this order; the new coefficients below are not the earlier c0,c1 and are not a derived running of the dimensionful source couplings g2,g3.

## 2. Polynomial classification and local representatives

Use all-outgoing massless momenta. For any pairing let S,T,U be the three invariants with S+T+U=0. Put e2=ST+TU+US, e3=STU. The gauge-invariant chiral pair vertices V_ij^h are those of the parent audit. Write W_S=V_ij^h*V_kl^h in the all-equal-duality sector and W_S=V_ij^h*V_kl^(-h) in the two-plus/two-minus sector. The covariant extension sums the appropriate projected field-strength products; a pure-duality external configuration selects the indicated terms.

### All equal dualities

Four-point pure-helicity identities give the common phase P=-4*W_S/S^2=-4*W_T/T^2=-4*W_U/U^2 where these ratios are defined. They are only a classification device. A Bose-symmetric amplitude in this sector is P times a symmetric homogeneous polynomial of degree q. The symmetric ring modulo S+T+U=0 is generated by e2,e3. The allowed monomials e2^a*e3^b satisfy 2*a+3*b=q:

| q | all-equal basis, with common P stripped |
|---:|---|
| 4 | e2^2 |
| 6 | e2^3, e3^2 |
| 8 | e2^4, e2*e3^2 |

These have manifestly local tensor representatives. Define

    A_n=-4*sum_pairings W_S*S^(n-2),
    E=-(4/3)*sum_pairings W_S*(T*U)^2.

Then A_n=P*(S^n+T^n+U^n), E=P*e3^2. The representatives in the table's order are

    q=4: A_4/2;
    q=6: (3*E-A_6)/2, E;
    q=8: A_8/2+4*e2*E, e2*E.

The identities follow from p4=2*e2^2, p6=-2*e2^3+3*e3^2 and p8=2*e2^4-8*e2*e3^2. All expressions used for evaluation are polynomial, including at vanishing S,T or U. No division by a Mandelstam invariant is implemented.

### Two plus and two minus dualities

The unique same-duality pairing selects S. The stripped amplitude must contain S^2 and be symmetric under T<->U. Since T+U=-S, its basis is

    S^(q-2*j)*(T*U)^j,    j=0,...,(q-2)/2.

Restoring the phase 4*W_S/S^2 gives local tensor representatives

    O_mixed,j=4*W_S*S^(q-2-2*j)*(T*U)^j.

Sum over pairings and the two dualities to extend to arbitrary external fields. The exponents are nonnegative. Each term contains all four field strengths, so it obeys the Ward identities; the pairing sums obey Bose symmetry/crossing. Proper Lorentz covariance follows from field-strength contractions and invariant polynomials. The checker also verifies these properties on exact witnesses, including forward kinematics.

There are respectively 3,5,6 independent coefficients in the **two stated sectors** at q=4,6,8. Independence is transparent after setting S=1,T=x,U=-1-x: within either sector the listed basis polynomials have distinct highest powers of x. Sector separation follows by evaluation on pure dualities. Completeness within the specified polynomial classes follows from the two invariant-ring arguments, not from a finite kinematic rank test. It is not asserted for omitted helicity sectors or off-shell operators.

The corresponding contact operator dimensions are 4+2*q=12,16,20: four field strengths supply four momenta and dimension eight; the remaining 2*q-4 derivatives supply the rest. These are on-shell contact representatives, not an enumerated off-shell Wick-operator basis.

## 3. Exact decomposition of the scale polynomial

Let H=sum_a H_a be the sum of logarithmic coefficients, before the common factor -r^2/pi. In the physical equal-helicity block its off-diagonal and diagonal sections have the respective phase-stripped polynomials

    H_same=O_k*(S^q+T^q+U^q),
    H_mixed=S^q*D_k((T-U)/S)
            +T^q*M_k(-S/T)+U^q*M_k(-S/U).

They are the parent audit's cut coefficients, not an independent optical normalization. The apparent divisions expand into polynomials because D is even of degree <=k and M starts at degree two. Decompose H=sum_i h_i*O_i in the basis above:

| q | all-equal h, table order | mixed h, increasing j |
|---:|---|---|

The checker decomposes both sections as exact rational polynomials on S=1. Homogeneity extends their identities to general nonzero S and polynomial continuity to S=0. It independently checks the tensor identity H=sum_i h_i*O_i in all physical helicity configurations, the physical basis normalization, and all-outgoing permutations. This does not construct the subleading or mixed-monomial cut spectrum.

## 4. Running and finite scale changes

Define the new local coefficients by

    B_local(nu)=(r^2/pi)*sum_i a_i(nu)*O_i.

Factoring r^2/pi is a normalization convention within each declared nonzero-r regime, not a relation fixing finite quartic couplings to the lower-order subtraction coefficients. The unfactored finite coefficients remain arbitrary.

Here nu>0 is the **squared-mass reference** of the parent's logarithms, not its IR-window endpoint or a selected physical source scale. At this perturbative order r is held fixed. Since

    d B_log/d log(nu)=+(r^2/pi)*H,

reference-scale independence requires

    beta_i = d a_i/d log(nu) = -h_i.

Thus the normalized beta vectors are the negatives of the preceding table. For coefficients defined without factoring r^2/pi, the beta vectors are -(r^2/pi)*h_i. Restore r=c0,c1,1/(6*pi) for k=2,3,4 respectively, and the full T carries b^2. Using a mass scale mu with nu=mu^2 instead gives d a_i/d log(mu)=-2*h_i; dropping this factor of two is a normalization error.

For any real L and nu'=exp(L)*nu,

    B_log(nu')-B_log(nu)=(r^2/pi)*L*H,
    a_i(nu')=a_i(nu)-L*h_i,

so the changes cancel in every external field-strength configuration. This is an exact reference-scale cancellation in the declared leading-IR kernel, not an all-orders RG improvement. The checker tests finite symbolic-log shifts with several rational L values; no numerical branch evaluation or scale choice is needed. Polynomial real counterterms introduce no absorptive cut.

For the scalar parity projection, the mixed-basis moments are integral_0^1 alpha^j*(1-alpha)^j d alpha=(j!)^2/(2*j+1)!. Integrating the all-equal polynomials with e2=-1+alpha-alpha^2 and e3=alpha*(1-alpha) gives the off-diagonal moments. The mixed minus/plus all-equal combinations reproduce exactly the parent's six Gamma values. Consequently the projected normalized local coefficient runs as -Gamma, cancelling the arbitrary reference in the real scalar log without removing its physical momentum dependence. Right-cut absorption remains unchanged.

## 5. What running does not determine

The solution a_i(nu)=a_i(nu0)-h_i*log(nu/nu0) contains an independent finite integration constant for every basis element. Ward identities, crossing and this scale equation do not select those constants, nu0, the source preparation or the charged-sector matching. Different real finite constants yield different analytic scattering amplitudes with identical logarithmic scale cancellation. Nonzero tensor witnesses ensure this is actual finite-amplitude freedom, not a zero-operator artifact. Setting constants to zero is a scheme/matching choice, not a prediction.

The full nonanalytic infrared terms survive; running only removes the arbitrary reference. The parent finite angular log constants are not complete matching data. No physical rate, branching ratio, coupling identification, complete renormalized EFT or local UV completion follows. Native-source licensing, C1-C12 dispositions, exhausted C12 effort, the high-energy pause and historical owner/hash warnings are unchanged.
