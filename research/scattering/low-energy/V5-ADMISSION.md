The model, source binding, metric, preparation space, operator-norm quantifiers and falsifier are fixed. Exact ground-sector work has established the automatic two-dimensional dark kernel and free dark transmission. It has **not** decided the remaining bright limit. Waiting for that proof before declaring a conjecture would conceal executable research from the planner again.

```

||R(k)+(I_G-Q_D)|| <= C k,
```

uniformly for sufficiently small positive k. The amplitude claim implies its stated packet bound, not conversely. It is not all-ground vanishing transmission: pure dark packets transmit with probability one.

There is no C1/C4 premise edge and no positive premise borrowed from a refuted carrier. The exact ground certificate is an explicit supporting artifact. Its prerequisite is checked freshly by the v5 validator, alongside the frozen v4 net. C12 remains open after those supporting checks pass.
