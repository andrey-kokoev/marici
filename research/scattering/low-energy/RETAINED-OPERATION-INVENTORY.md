## Exact discriminator already present in the source

Freshly inspected sources:

Under the **additional** proposal that an arrow a→b acts as F(a,b) in S4, with F(a,b)(a)=b and full relabelling equivariance, exactly two policies exist. Of the six permutations sending A to B, only (AB) and (AB)(CD) commute with the ordered edge's stabilizer {identity,(CD)}. Each extends uniquely by conjugation to all twelve ordered edges.

Both invert under edge reversal. On B→C→A→B:

- Endpoint swaps give (AB)(CA)(BC)=(AC).
- Endpoint-and-spectator swaps give identity.

Both fix the endpoint B, but their full witness actions differ. Their edge generators generate S4 and the Klein four group respectively. Requiring generation of S4 would select one policy, but that is an **extra** source requirement, not a consequence of relabelling covariance.

The new small checker independently exhausts the finite permutation calculation, checks all relabellings and reverse edges, and rejects an edgewise fitted policy and the inference “anchor return implies full return.” This is a scoped replay of an existing source distinction, not a new universal obstruction. It does not rerun the source's geometric placements, Agda compilation, field dynamics or complete scientific suites.

## Sharpened admission gate, not an invented proposition

Before proposing a successor **using this spatial adapter**, identify:

1. The actual supplied equivalence for each primitive continuation, including spectator action and retained parent information; alternatively identify an existing reader that uses additional history.
2. Its actual typed interface with phase evolution: spaces, maps and compatibility identities. Neither a selected permutation policy nor a phase-return time is sufficient.
3. For a claimed two-output construction, independently generated output factors and the licensed composition/separation and independent factor operations, with residual information retained.

Only then freeze an existence/compatibility statement, quantifiers and falsifier. Do not choose a policy because it gives the desired loop, insert a new metric/clock unnoticed, identify a body exchange with translational propagation, or reuse C10's raw-amplitude obstruction as a joint-witness no-go.

The source-operation preflight is now concrete: **supplied witness selection and joint transport are missing inputs in this adapter**, not an invitation to try another arbitrary carrier. The separate branch-evidence audit remains executable and should proceed without waiting for physical binding.
