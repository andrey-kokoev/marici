# Marici-native re-audit of the control programme

Owner: `marici.Sontag`

## Correction being made

The first four milestones correctly established finite control-theoretic
gates and hostiles, but their language sometimes made a typed state-space
diagram sound like a demonstrated factorization through Marici.  That was too
strong.  Marici's candidate common structure is not an arbitrary category of
typed maps.  It is a resolved carrier from scalar master geometry together
with a predeclared algebra of incidence, restriction, residue, gluing,
specialization, transport, and coherence.  Sector coefficient/response
objects, authorized physical selection, and readout are additional layers.

This audit preserves every verified finite control statement and retracts only
the unsupported carrier-level promotion.

## Audit gate

A control construction counts as a demonstrated Marici factorization only if
all of the following are supplied:

1. an established scalar-carrier source object or stratum;
2. an established carrier-algebra operation with declared source and target;
3. sector-specific coefficient and response objects with variance typed;
4. a source-derived intervention or selection authority;
5. a physical effect/readout and record map;
6. an occurrence/provenance preservation or explicit quotient proof;
7. the required strict or higher coherence tests;
8. an analytic-type declaration distinguishing local impedance, scattering
   readout, and entire completed section.

Dimension agreement, a commuting state-space diagram, or a passing rank test
does not fill any missing item.

## The impedance/scattering correction

Ports related by a formula remain different typed response/readout objects.
For \(a>0\),

\[
Z_a(s)=\frac{s}{a},\qquad
h_a(s)=\frac{Z_a(s)-1}{Z_a(s)+1}=\frac{s-a}{s+a}.
\]

The impedance is strictly positive-real in the right half-plane, but the
reflection readout satisfies \(h_a(a)=0\).  Thus strict positivity attached to
the impedance response does not cross the Cayley adapter into zero-freeness of
the scattering record.  The adapter must be source-authorized, and the
zero-exclusion law must apply to the actual distinguished scalar port.

This is a concrete Marici lesson: transporting a property between two legal
objects is a new typed arrow with its own coherence requirement.  Algebraic
convertibility is capability, not authority.

An analytic-type gate precedes even this port conversion.  A sector-local
impedance or Weyl function may be positive-real; its Cayley scattering channel
is a different readout and may vanish; an even nonconstant entire completed
section cannot itself be positive-real on either half-plane.  A proposed
realization must record its domain, poles or boundary data, sewing parity, and
completion map before transporting positivity.

## Revised Sontag constructor contract

Future packets must begin with one of two declarations:

- **carrier-bound:** cite the established carrier stratum and operation, then
  type coefficients, response, authority, readout, and coherence; or
- **control gate on a proposed realization:** state explicitly that the
  carrier arrow is absent and test only the consequences if a sector supplies
  the proposed realization.

The phrase "factorization through Marici" is reserved for the first branch.
The second branch remains valuable because it supplies finite falsifiers and
prevents invalid promotions, but it is not evidence that the carrier generated
the system.
