# A Deutschean control-theoretic explanation of the Marici object

## Question

What is a Marici object, and why must it have the structure it has?

The question is not asking how to recognize an already specified packet. It is
asking what kind of thing can remain a reusable constructive object across
change, and why the recurring source, continuation, authority, resource,
boundary, and readout structures appear.

## Short answer

A Marici object is a source-relative law of constructive possibility.

It consists invariantly of two mutually determining sides:

1. a source-defined problem relation saying what construction or preservation
   is at issue, retained only to the resolution consumed by authorized future
   contexts; and
2. the minimal contextual process law saying how the capability to satisfy
   that relation behaves under every admitted control, disturbance, resource
   change, authority change, and continuation.

A closed packet is a finite transportable presentation or section of that law.
It is not the invariant object simply because its bytes are closed.

## Why control theory sees this

Control theory distinguishes a state value from a maintained capability. A
plant can momentarily have the desired value through accident, reset, or an
unrepeatable intervention. It has a reusable capability only when an admissible
policy can keep or restore the defining relation over the declared disturbance
family and resource bounds.

The same distinction applies to Marici. Present agreement is not object
identity. The operative identity is what the packet can lawfully construct,
repair, transport, and expose next.

For a context `c`, let `X_c` be the minimal state space, `O_c` the typed record
space, and `U_c` the admitted commands. The dynamic core has the actual
mathematical form

\[
\gamma_c:X_c\longrightarrow O_c\times\prod_{u\in U_c}X_{c\cdot u}.
\]

The target context may differ because a command consumes resources, changes
authority, advances protocol epoch, or reduces continuation depth. This is why
a single stationary state space is not generally primary.

Histories are identified only when they have the same complete task-relevant
traces and successor capabilities under all admitted future context paths.
That future-capability quotient is the minimal causal memory of the object.

## Why the structure is necessary

### The source-defined problem relation

Raw dynamics does not determine what counts as success. The same transition
system can support incompatible tasks with different unique repair policies.
Moving the task into an effect, base category, boundary condition, or parent
constructor transports this distinction but does not eliminate it.

Therefore Marici objecthood is relational to a source problem. At a deeper
explanatory rung the problem may itself be generated. At any declared rung,
source distinctions remain operative only when a jointly conservative family
of authorized future contexts consumes them; distinctions invisible to every
such context are redundant presentation.

### Context-indexed successor capability

Terminal values forget what can happen next. Reset and genuine repair may end
at the same value while differing in source replay and future response.
Instruments with the same one-use output law may have different successor
states. A stationary closure also cannot represent an alternating process.

Therefore the object requires a process or coalgebraic law of continuation,
not merely a value, fixed point, or terminal experiment.

### Controls and disturbances

Success on the realized history does not establish a constructor. Lookup
tables and accidental states can fit that history. Controls state what can be
done; disturbances state the counterfactual range over which the ability is
claimed. Together they define the criticism domain that makes the claim hard
to vary.

### Authority and resources

A mathematically definable control is not automatically operative. An actor
may lack the actuator, permission, energy, readiness, time, or continuation
budget required to execute it.

Authority and resource need not be universal stored fields. They are genuine
axes exactly when lawful context changes address them independently and change
future capability. Otherwise behavioral minimization removes their separate
presentation.

### Persistence or repair witness

A quiet state is not evidence of preserved construction. Reset can erase the
reference against which failure was defined. A valid repair must retain or
transport the source relation and demonstrate that the post-state satisfies
the inherited task.

In control terms this is the source-relative controlled-invariant or viability
witness. In other sectors its realization may be a refinement map, commuting
square, conserved pairing, decoder certificate, or supported transport.

### Readout and record

Evidence must be exposed, but a readout can be many-to-one. Treating it as the
object collapses hidden memory, route, phase, source epoch, or successor
capability. The record map is therefore a projection of the object, not its
identity, unless proved sufficient for all admitted continuations.

### Minimization and authorized gauge

Complete genealogy retains too much. Present readout retains too little. The
correct state lies between them: histories are quotiented by equality of all
admitted future task capabilities.

Presentation changes derived from the source and preserving every typed
composition are gauge. Rival mechanisms outside that equivalence must remain
distinct. Explanatory uniqueness means one surviving rival class after the
source-authorized gauge quotient, measured using coordinates faithful on that
quotient.

### Boundary and provenance

Boundary and provenance are conditional consequences, not universal essence.
Retain them when a future constructor consumes them, when they transport the
source relation, or when they determine the meaning or authority of a
transition. Delete them when every admitted future behavior is unchanged.

This prevents both amnesia and history maximalism.

## The Deutschean explanation

The object has this structure because reusable construction is a
counterfactual property. It is not exhausted by what is currently present; it
is the organized ability to continue satisfying a source relation as the
context changes.

Two independent variations can destroy that identity:

1. change the source problem while keeping all raw dynamics fixed, and a
   different policy becomes repair;
2. change the contextual capability law while keeping the current value fixed,
   and different continuations become possible.

These are not arbitrary schema variations. They change what problem is being
solved or whether it remains constructively solvable. Conversely, a change
that affects neither the source problem nor any future capability is removable
presentation.

This yields a hard-to-vary explanatory core:

> A Marici object is the minimal context-indexed realization of a
> source-defined constructor capability, with source distinctions and future
> contexts minimized jointly, considered up to source-authorized equivalence.

## Why the packet vocabulary kept appearing

The recurring packet components are coordinate expressions of the invariant
core:

| Packet term | Invariant causal job |
| --- | --- |
| source or provenance | identifies or transports the problem relation |
| carrier state | minimal causal memory for future capability |
| constructor or control | realizes an admitted transformation |
| closure or stabilization | one possible persistence mechanism |
| boundary | interface required by a later constructor |
| authority | selects controls available to this actor and context |
| resource | bounds feasible transformations |
| coherence witness | proves that different compositions preserve one task |
| readout or record | exposes typed evidence |
| gauge relation | removes presentation without erasing capability |

No row is automatically a stored field. A representation may reconstruct a
row from the contextual process law. The row is necessary only as a causal
job that some part of the representation must perform.

## Rival explanations already rejected

The research chain rejects the following candidates in exact finite models:

- present value as object identity;
- complete history as object identity;
- universal idempotent closure;
- one-use output experiment as process identity;
- reset as repair;
- lookup fit as explanation;
- pointwise replay as compositional certificate;
- arbitrary invariant predicates;
- naturality under an insufficient criticism family;
- transitive criticism as sufficient for rigidity;
- packet fields as intrinsically primitive metadata;
- raw capability dynamics as a source of its own task.

## What this does and does not explain

It explains why operative Marici objects repeatedly require source typing,
future behavior, intervention scope, resource and authority sensitivity,
persistence witnesses, and quotient discipline. These are consequences of
being reusable constructive capabilities under context change.

It does not explain why the universe supplies the ultimate source generators,
why a particular physical task is fundamental, or whether all sectors admit
one common mathematical category. The explanation is explicitly relative to a
declared constructive rung.

It also does not prove that every Marici object is a deterministic finite
control system. Probabilistic, quantum, geometric, and higher-categorical
sectors require their own process functors and coherence laws. The common claim
is structural: task relation plus contextual capability law plus minimal
source-authorized realization.
