# A control-theoretic explanation of the Marici object

## The explanandum

What is a Marici object, and why must it have the structure it has?

The target is not a recognition checklist. It is an explanation of why one
closed packet remains capable of lawful, source-faithful construction while a
packet with the same present readout may not.

## Proposed explanatory principle

A Marici object is the minimal causal memory of a source-defined task whose
constructive availability is maintained under admitted future interaction.

In control language, its center is a source-relative robust controlled
invariant together with the continuation dynamics needed to use it again. Its
operative state is minimized by equality of complete future capabilities, not
by equality of current values or complete histories.

The object exists operatively when there is an admitted policy that, within
declared resources, keeps or restores the source relation against the declared
disturbance family and leaves a typed successor on which further constructors
can act.

## Why the familiar structure follows

This principle predicts the parts rather than merely collecting them.

The source presentation states what must be constructed or preserved. Without
it, reset and lookup can impersonate repair.

The transition and continuation coalgebra state what the packet can do next.
Without them, terminally equal packets with different successor capabilities
collapse.

The intervention and disturbance families state the counterfactual domain of
the claim. Without them, accidental success on one history impersonates a
reusable constructor.

Authority and resources restrict which mathematically definable controls are
operative. Without them, an oracle or unavailable actuator can be smuggled
into the policy.

The controlled-invariant or replay witness states persistence of the same
source task through intervention. Without it, quiet reset is confused with
repair.

Readouts and records expose evidence without becoming object identity. Without
this separation, compressed observation is mistaken for faithful state.

Behavioral minimization and authorized gauge remove history and presentation
that make no difference to future construction. Without them, the object
becomes an ever-growing genealogy rather than reusable causal memory.

Residual boundary and provenance are not universal extra fields. They survive
only when consumed by a future constructor, needed to interpret or authorize a
transition, or required to transport the source relation.

## Finite hostile model

The source bit is the required plant bit. A disturbance may flip the plant.
The available controls are no-op, set-zero, and set-one. Repair has unit cost.

Exact enumeration shows:

- a robust constructor policy exists;
- no source-blind policy repairs both source tasks;
- constant-one lookup and reset-to-zero each fail one source;
- an observer without set actions has no robust policy;
- zero repair budget destroys robustness;
- a constant terminal readout hides failed source relation;
- source-relative replay exposes that hidden failure;
- resource-sensitive minimization uniquely selects the relational policy that
  does nothing when correct and sets the plant to the source when damaged.

Thus each retained component answers a different counterfactual. The parts are
coupled because they jointly define constructive availability, not because a
schema designer chose to bundle them.

## Deutschean content

The explanation is hard to vary in the following bounded sense. Change the
source relation, admitted disturbances, controls, authority, resources, or
continuation semantics and the set of constructible tasks changes. Remove any
one without reconstructing it from the remaining structure and a named rival
architecture passes the present readout while failing a declared future test.

This is stronger than predictive minimality. A minimal automaton says which
histories have the same future behavior. The Marici explanation also says why
that future behavior counts as preservation of this task, which interventions
are legitimate criticisms, and which resources make the transformation
possible.

## Current object candidate

The Marici object is therefore not best understood as data plus auth metadata,
nor as a closure fixed point, nor as complete provenance. It is an authorized,
resource-bounded realization of a source-defined constructor capability,
represented by its minimal future-capability state and equipped with the
source transport needed to preserve its task identity under composition.

At finite continuation depth this realization is graded by residual experiment
context. A stationary coalgebra appears only after stabilization or an
independently justified unbounded completion.

## Claim boundary and next attacks

The checker proves only a finite deterministic instance. It does not prove
that every physical or mathematical Marici sector admits a controlled-invariant
presentation. It also leaves open whether source authority and task relation
are independent primitives or can be reconstructed from a deeper constructor
calculus.

The next serious rivals are:

1. a pure constructor-theoretic task algebra with no state ontology;
2. a sheaf or process object whose local composition reconstructs authority and
   source transport;
3. a coalgebra with typed inputs where source and authority are encoded in the
   input category rather than stored in each object.

If one of these reconstructs every claimed component canonically, the packet
form is presentation rather than the Marici object itself.
