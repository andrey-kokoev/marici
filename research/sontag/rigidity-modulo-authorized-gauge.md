# Explanatory rigidity modulo authorized gauge

## Bounded question

Does transitive counterfactual reach force a hard-to-vary mechanism? If rival
natural mechanisms remain, when are they gauge presentations and when are they
genuine explanatory alternatives?

## Transitive hostile model

The source is the four-element cyclic set. Its admitted counterfactual action
is the cyclic shift, which is transitive. Exhaustive enumeration finds four
mechanisms commuting with that action: the four translations.

The actual readout initially reports only parity. At source zero, both identity
and shift-by-two have the correct even readout. Transitive criticism has not
made the mechanism unique.

Now the classification bifurcates according to source authority.

If the source is authorized only modulo two, identity and shift-by-two induce
the same map on the two source classes. They are not two explanations at that
typed level; their distinction has been quotiented away.

If the source construction retains all four labels, the two mechanisms remain
distinct. A second source-derived bit, paired with parity, is faithful on the
four labels and separates them. Until that probe is admitted, they are an
unresolved two-element family, not one explanation.

## Deutsch-style correction

“No surviving variation” must never mean no variation in a chosen
presentation. It must mean no variation outside authorized gauge that survives
the independently admitted criticism interface.

The finite criterion is therefore:

```text
1. freeze the source-derived equivalence relation;
2. quotient presentation changes authorized as gauge;
3. use coordinates proved faithful on that quotient;
4. compute the surviving natural rival classes;
5. call the explanation rigid only when exactly one class remains.
```

This directly refutes the tempting extrapolation that connected or transitive
counterfactual reach is sufficient. A transitive action can have a nontrivial
centralizer, and a compressed readout can fail to distinguish its elements.

## Claim boundary

The mod-2 quotient is analyzed conditionally. The checker does not establish
that a particular physical Marici source is mod two rather than mod four. That
choice must come from its source construction and admitted interventions, not
from the desire to remove a rival.
