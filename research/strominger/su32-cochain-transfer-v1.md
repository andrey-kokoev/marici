# Su-32: support cochains and a first higher comparison

## Question and claim boundary

Following the operator's request to pursue Pavel Mnëv's simplicial Chern–Simons construction, test whether the actual layer-4 operands admit a differential, products and contraction data, and whether the two reconciliation routes agree as operations.

SCC obligation: **route/coherencer compatibility**. The constructed carrier is the abstract simplicial complex of native **reference supports**, not a triangulation of spatial cells. Rational cochains, the product convention and a coarse filled-square target are declared mathematical choices. They are not assigned to the unspecified native payloads as physical amplitudes.

Conjecture tested: the two native support routes admit an explicit comparison at cochain-operation level, beyond equality of their final record union. Rivals: incidence selects a unique product/contraction; equal boundary data force strict equality of the transferred products; local comparisons exhaust global cycle data.

## The actual two routes are two diagonal triangulations

Order the four labels cyclically as

```
0=P+, 1=E+, 2=P-, 3=E-.
```

The four incoming channels are edges `01,12,23,30`. The E-reference route has triangles `012,023`, joined along `02`; the P-reference route has triangles `013,123`, joined along `13`. This identification is checked against the actual input channels and comparison supports at every occurrence.

Both disks have the same oriented boundary. Their difference is

```
z = [012] + [023] - [013] - [123],    boundary(z)=0.
```

Keeping both disks gives the four-face record sphere, rather than one disk. The 36 distinct local cycles are independent in the global support complex. They span 36 of its 43 second-homology dimensions, leaving **seven global classes outside their span**. This is additional cycle data, not proof of a consistency failure. Even hypothetically attaching one 3-cell along each local sphere would leave those seven classes. No such native fillers are attached here.

## Differential and two products

Take rational simplicial cochains, with the signed incidence coboundary. The global differential squares to zero and commutes with restriction to every comparison sphere.

Two explicit products were tested:

- Alexander–Whitney cup product, using one declared global vertex order: associative and compatible with the differential.
- Integrated Whitney product: graded-commutative and compatible with the differential, but not strictly associative.

For the vertex-0 cochain `v` and oriented edge cochain `e` on any edge:

```
v cup_AW e = e,       e cup_AW v = 0,
v cup_W e = e/2,      e cup_W v = e/2,
(v cup_W v) cup_W e - v cup_W (v cup_W e) = e/4.
```

Thus incidence plus rational linearization does not select the product. The graded AW commutator supplies a candidate differential graded Lie bracket; the scalar Whitney graded commutator is zero. Pavel Mnëv uses matrix-valued forms, so our scalar Whitney associator is not automatically a nontrivial transferred Lie interaction. Higher associativity operations and internal coefficient algebra remain distinct questions.

Exact checks: 189,728 basis Leibniz identities, 49,392 local AW associativity identities covering all potentially nonzero global coefficients, and 56,448 product restriction checks. A corrupted incidence sign gives a nonzero `d²` residual of `-2`.

## Explicit contractions, with their free parameter retained

Let the coarse filled square have cochain dimensions `(4,4,1)` and oriented boundary edge values `w0,w1,w2,w3`. Its differential is

```
(df)_j = f_(j+1) - f_j,    d(w) = w0+w1+w2+w3.
```

For the E triangulation, dimensions `(4,5,2)`, define the projection I by retaining vertices and boundary edges and summing the two face values. For any rational lambda, the inclusion i retains the same vertices/boundary edges and assigns

```
w02 = (1-lambda)(w0+w1) - lambda(w2+w3),
(Omega012, Omega023) = (lambda Omega, (1-lambda) Omega).
```

The degree-minus-one homotopy K has only one nonzero component:

```
(K Omega)_02 = lambda Omega023 - (1-lambda) Omega012.
```

Then `Ii=id`, `d_fine i=i d_coarse`, `d_coarse I=I d_fine`, and

```
dK+Kd = id-iI,    K²=0,    IK=0,    Ki=0.
```

The P triangulation uses the cyclically shifted formula, with diagonal `13`. The checker verifies the chain-map equations explicitly, avoiding the compressed notation above. It tests five rational parameters for each route and all 25 cross-route parameter pairs. Maps `i_P I_E` and `i_E I_P` compose to the respective projections `iI`, with K witnessing their difference from identity.

The parameter is not fixed by incidence. These are cellular contraction packages, not a numerical evaluation of the continuous Dupont operator. A contraction discards an acyclic mode; it is not strict recovery of arbitrary fine cochain values. Original records and route certificates remain retained separately.

## A constructed higher comparison of the binary operations

At the declared symmetric choice `lambda=1/2`, transfer the Whitney product through each contraction:

```
m_E(a,b) = I_E(i_E(a) cup_W i_E(b)),
m_P(a,b) = I_P(i_P(a) cup_W i_P(b)).
```

Strict equality fails already on vertex 0 and the square's face cochain:

```
m_E(v0,Omega) = Omega/3,
m_P(v0,Omega) = Omega/6.
```

Nevertheless an explicit bilinear homotopy compares them. Define

```
L(w) = -(w01+w23)/6,
H(w,Omega) = L(w) Omega,
H(Omega,w) = Omega L(w),
```

and set H to zero on all other degree pairs. H has degree -1 and satisfies

```
m_E(a,b)-m_P(a,b)
  = d H(a,b) + H(da,b) + (-1)^degree(a) H(a,db).
```

All 81 ordered pairs of coarse basis elements pass exactly; bilinearity extends this to arbitrary rational cochains. H has only face-valued outputs, so its restriction to every boundary edge is zero. The two routes therefore agree **up to this boundary-relative binary-operation homotopy**, not as identical operations. Removing H leaves the explicit `Omega/6` residual.

This is the first constructed higher comparison in this candidate. It is not a full A-infinity/L-infinity morphism, a BV effective action, or a spatial 3-cell filling the record sphere. Those are different types of witness.

## Disposition

Constructed: a single global support complex and differential, two restriction-compatible products, parameterized local chain contractions, and an exact boundary-relative homotopy between the two transferred Whitney binary products at the symmetric choice.

The strict-equality and uniqueness rivals fail. Local cycle tests do not exhaust the global support complex: seven second-homology classes remain outside the span of local route spheres. The local contraction and H packages have not been sewn into one cross-cone higher comparison. No native payload product, phase connection or Hopf characteristic class has been selected.

The concrete next mathematical obligation is cross-cone compatibility of these higher operations on the already constructed global complex, retaining its additional cycle classes rather than testing each cone in isolation.
