# The source-derived puncture constructor is a coherent spin-two kernel

## Result

Let

\[
 S(z;\xi)=\frac{(z-\xi)(\bar z-\bar\xi)}{(1+z\bar z)(1+\xi\bar\xi)},
 \qquad G(z;\xi)=S(z;\xi)\log S(z;\xi).
\]

With `Gamma^z_zz=-2 bar(z)/(1+z bar(z))`, direct differentiation gives

\[
 \boxed{\widehat K^+_\xi=D_z^2(S\log S)
 =\frac{(\bar z-\bar\xi)(1+\bar z\xi)^2}
 {(z-\xi)(1+z\bar z)^3(1+\xi\bar\xi)}.}
\]

The squared and cubed factors are the terms that make this a global spin-two
section. The earlier expression obtained from `S log|z-xi|^2` was coordinate
dependent and is superseded.

## 1. Centered puncture and locked completion

At `xi=0`, with `u=z bar(z)`,

\[
 \widehat K^+_0=\frac{\bar z}{z(1+u)^3}.
\]

Its north-chart and exterior expansions are

\[
 \widehat K^+_0=\sum_{n\geq0}(-1)^n\binom{n+2}{2}
 z^{n-1}\bar z^{n+1},
\]

\[
 \widehat K^+_0=\sum_{n\geq0}(-1)^n\binom{n+2}{2}
 z^{-n-4}\bar z^{-n-2}.
\]

The exterior support obeys `a=n+4`, `m=2-a`, starts at pole depth four,
and contains both parities. Its coefficients are locked by

\[
 (n+1)c_{n+1}=-(n+3)c_n,\qquad c_0=1.
\]

The physical object is this coherent rational section, not a coefficientwise
product completion. Changing one coefficient violates the recurrence and
leaves the source image.

## 2. Finite labelled source packets

For punctures `P={xi_i}` and finite jet bound `J`, the authorized packet is
generated by

\[
 \partial_{\xi_i}^{r}\partial_{\bar\xi_i}^{s}
 \widehat K^+_{\xi_i},\qquad r+s\leq J,
\]

and the conjugate-helicity generators. Its completion is the strict LF union
over finite `J`, not an unrestricted product of Laurent coordinates. Each
source derivative raises local principal-part order in a controlled way while
preserving the coherent global tail.

For several hard punctures,

\[
 C_{zz}=\sum_i e_i\widehat K^+_{\xi_i}
\]

before separately imposed conservation and scalar-zero-mode relations. The
independent records are labelled source coefficients and jets, not annular
Laurent coefficients.

## 3. Towers and named exceptions

A finite Laurent vector cannot equal `Khat_0`: multiplication by `(1+u)^3`
leaves a nonzero terminal boundary coefficient. Generic punctures also retain
the divisor `z=xi`. Consequently:

- no individual finite magnetic tower vector is a point-source generator;
- `E_1` and `E_2` are not in this leading point-source image;
- no finite Laurent cutoff is an exact source restriction;
- a truncated calculation is physical only when its coherent remainder port
  is retained.

This does not yet exclude distributional or quotient analogues after the
grade-three readout. That is decided at the first nonfaithful arrow.
