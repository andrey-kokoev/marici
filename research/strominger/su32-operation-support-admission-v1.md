## Relaxed diagnostic: exhaustive label support

To expose what a mere pair-name match can and cannot establish, enumerate all **120 injections of four record labels into five coefficient slots**. These are explicitly not admitted encodings. Each injection is fixed across all sixteen complete candidates in its trial; nothing is fitted per candidate.

- **72** injections have an image preserved by the native permutation and contain its moved pair.
- **48** omit a moved endpoint, so that four-slot restriction is not closed.
- Each of the six possible record pairs is the moved pair for exactly **12** injections.

For every support-closed injection, every candidate contains its matched pair exactly once: all sixteen placements already contain all six pair identities. Therefore pair membership alone cannot select a placement.

## Actual outgoing-face role screen

For the matched selected pair, inspect its actual middle N location and the outgoing faces already selected in that candidate's complete plan. The relaxed NE screen requires an outgoing electron destination. No rerouting or face is added.

This necessary role/incidence condition removes some label matches: a split-tip occurrence can have only a P destination in the retained plan. It still does not supply the marked primitive transfer.

For every single trial injection, the surviving candidate counts in the two distance families are equal:

```
(0,0), (7,7), or (8,8).
```

The zero case is the unclosed four-slot restriction. Cross-role matched pairs leave seven candidates in each family; same-role matched pairs leave eight in each. These are diagnostic support labels, not identifications of P/E signs with native C/M marks.

Across all trials, each family has 576 pair-support matches and 528 face-role matches. These counts are not probabilities, weights or a physical ensemble measure. Their equality is not a symmetry admission for the full retained histories.

## What the test establishes

- A source operation can genuinely depend on active pair support in the prior model.
- A pair-label match alone is too weak to discriminate these complete Su-32 placements.
- Adding the actual outgoing E-face availability condition still leaves both families under every fixed trial identification.
- Native admission remains **not formulated**, because the required marked reference/primitive/face binding is absent.

No physical impossibility is inferred. The 120 injections are one bounded label-only diagnostic class, not all possible state encoders or contextual adapters. A real occurrence-specific binding could impose additional constraints; it cannot be replaced by choosing whichever of these injections gives a desired pair.
