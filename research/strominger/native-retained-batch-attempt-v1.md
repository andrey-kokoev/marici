# First native retained construction attempt: a real shared-continuation conflict

## What was attempted

Instead of modifying the viewer, this attempt uses Nima's existing
`GenerativeAssemblyRules` unchanged. It begins with the admitted origin P seed,
its ordered C0/M0/C1/M1 marks, absolute origin and all five coefficient slots.
There is no Pb layout, isotope quota, finite envelope or species recoloring input.

The proposed collective update was: obtain continuations from all packets in one
retained source snapshot, validate their native witnesses, then attach compatible
results simultaneously. This directly tests the missing joint-operation step;
it is not an untyped geometric frontier.

## Positive native construction

Actual PN gives a1P/1N assembly with its shared face witnessed. From that same
snapshot both of these separate continuations pass the native and global checks:

- NE produces1P/1N/1E with a certified exit under the source's graph definition.
- NN-phase-advance produces1P/2N and retains its origin increment and full
  coefficient permutation.

The root PN and root reader-phase-advance are also jointly admissible. The result
therefore is not a blanket rejection of parallel retained operations.

## Concrete failed attachment

The NE and NN-phase-advance alternatives from the same N packet cross the SAME
face into the SAME primitive. One assigns it electron; the other assigns it
neutron. Keeping both violates the actual occupation constraint:

    species(cell) = electron AND species(cell) = neutron.

The source validator returns `occupied-cell-species-conflict`. Each singleton
batch is valid; their pair is invalid. Thus this is an inclusion-minimal conflict
of operations on the fixed admitted PN source, not a claimed global impossibility.

The generated artifact preserves the complete source packet set, both operations,
shared-face coordinates, target vertices, absolute origins, five-slot maps and
reference charts. It does not replace the conflict with a cartoon or a count test.

This rejects the proposed "attach every available native continuation" update.
The two alternatives may remain separate retained possibilities; treating both as
simultaneously occupied particles would require a new, explicitly justified law.
This failure is specific to the primitive occupied-species interpretation. It
does not reject a whole-assembly presentation in which E/N labels have a separately
bound reader meaning rather than being simultaneous particle assignments.

## Constructive continuation after the failure

The implementation enumerates EVERY nonempty subset of the available target
packets at each tested snapshot, validates the whole union, and retains all
operation witnesses when several operations lead to the same target packet.
Repeated operations that add no packet remain in the native grammar but are
excluded from this progress-batch enumeration.

It then explores three rounds of maximal compatible batches, keeping ALL maximal
alternatives rather than choosing one by address order or a desired isotope count.
The maximality policy is an explicit exploratory scheduling hypothesis, not a
source-derived physical preference. Valid nonmaximal branches are counted and
remain legal; in particular the terminating PNE alternative is not refuted by
this schedule. The candidate bound is14; exceeding it returns an explicit
incomplete result, not a negative construction verdict.

Operations in a batch must all read the prior snapshot. A freshly produced N
cannot supply an NE operation in that same batch. Within an admitted batch,
reversing its operation enumeration preserves the resulting packet set, while
operation records remain retained. This is a set-level retained-construction
check, not a proof of analytical higher coherence or physical state cloning.

## What remains hard

We now have a concrete reason why geometric filling cannot stand in for the Pb
construction: available native continuations can demand incompatible species on
one shared carrier. A rule relating or selecting these alternatives must preserve
their actual marks, origins and coefficients. It cannot be obtained merely from
esd7 incidence or from the desired82/126 output.

Next confront this competing-continuation problem in the retained whole-assembly
interpretation, and test whether the source supplies a comparison/selection rule.
Also retain the separate whole-octahedron/composite-body binding problem: this
native grammar counts primitive carriers, whereas the legacy Pb representation
counts whole octahedral/composite bodies. This attempt does not identify them.

No Pb-208 construction, physical formation, stability, full4x28 transport or
isotope selection is established. The current viewer is not promoted by this
result. This is an actual typed construction attempt and a reproducible failed
joint attachment, as required by the corrected conjecture-net frontier.
