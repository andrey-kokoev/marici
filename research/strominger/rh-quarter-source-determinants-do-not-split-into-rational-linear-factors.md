# Quarter source determinants do not split into rational linear factors

## Question

Can exact division be controlled by multiplicities of positive rational linear factors?

## Claim boundary

The order-two obstruction rules out complete rational linear splitting. The bounded census checks only eighth-lattice roots through order eight; it is not a complete factorization of higher orders.

## Disposition

The first nontrivial source polynomial factors as

\[
D_2(a)=(a+15/8)Q_2(a),
\]

where the quadratic factor has discriminant \(-11\). Hence \(D_2\) already has a nonreal conjugate root pair and cannot split into positive rational linear factors. At orders three through eight, the eighth-lattice census finds no such roots. Factor-multiplicity induction in the proposed linear basis is therefore excluded. The next leaf is `quarter-source-determinant-hurwitz-stability`, testing the broader invariant that all source-polynomial roots lie in the open left half-plane, which is preserved under exact factor division.
