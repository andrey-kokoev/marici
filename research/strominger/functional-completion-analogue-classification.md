# The smooth low kernel is a new spectral stratum, not a tower or E-class

## 1. Stable law

For every Sobolev level and for the full distribution carrier, the paired
grade-three kernel is the same finite smooth block. On the real magnetic
sector,

\[
 \boxed{\dim\ker\mathcal A_3^M=21.}
\]

It is independent of Laurent cutoff, puncture number, pole depth, and Sobolev
index. It appears exactly when the source category contains smooth
`l=2,3,4` flux modes.

The closure-sensitive law is

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{source category}&\dim\ker\mathcal A_3^M\\
\hline
\text{finite labelled point packets}&0\\
\text{bounded-TV atomic closure}&0\\
\text{weak-* Radon/smooth flux completion}&21\\
H^s\text{ or }\mathcal D'\text{ containing smooth modes}&21.
\end{array}
\]

## 2. Magnetic towers

Tower classes are formal Laurent zero columns tied to exponent reflection at
each admitted pole depth. Their number grows with the chosen pole-depth set.

The functional low kernel instead consists of globally smooth spin harmonics,
is fixed at `l=2,3,4`, and is forced by zeros of one spectral multiplier.
In a stereographic chart its components are coherent rational sections with
sphere denominators and exact two-chart transitions. They are not independent
Laurent monomials and do not define one tower per pole depth.

The only common feature is that both are symmetry-compatible kernel families.
Their constructors, support, and stability laws differ.

## 3. E1

`E1=1-bar(z)^(-2)` was an initialization/boundary degeneration of the formal
grade-two engine. The smooth low block is also an initialization defect in the
sense that the first nonzero spectral multiplier occurs only at `l=5`.

This is the closest structural analogue, but not an identification:

- `E1` is one rational-exact Laurent line;
- the physical defect is 21 real magnetic harmonic modes;
- `E1` has no source preimage;
- the low modes are generated by smooth hard flux.

Thus the functional completion contains an `E1`-type *role*—a finite initial
block missed before stable transport—but not the `E1` vector.

## 4. E2 and `(1,-3,2)`

`E2` was a three-column internal circuit. No such relation occurs among
`l>=5` harmonic columns, whose multiplier is diagonal and nonzero.

The primitive vector `(1,-3,2)` survives only in the collision blow-up

\[
 \delta_0-3\delta_\epsilon+2\delta_{3\epsilon/2},
\]

where it cancels value and first moment. After `epsilon^-2` rescaling it tends
to a detected second delta derivative, not to a smooth low harmonic kernel.
Without rescaling it tends to zero. It is therefore a collision-coordinate
circuit, disjoint from the spectral low stratum.

## 5. Characteristic ridge waves

The ridge families of `p^4-q^4` are presentation-level characteristic
solutions. They are extended or distributional, fail localization/L2 tests,
and do not satisfy the paired global bundle equation except through its smooth
low modes. They provide no tower or E2 analogue in the authorized completion.
