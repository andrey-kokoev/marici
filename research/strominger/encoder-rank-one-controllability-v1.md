## Rank-one phase controllability lemma

Work in the inherited positive Hermitian metric (whitening is an isometry, not
a change of physical metric). Suppose rank-one orthogonal phase projectors have
independent directions v_1,...,v_m, ordered so each v_k for k>1 has a nonzero
projection into V_(k-1)=span(v_1,...,v_(k-1)). Then their skew-Hermitian generators
iP_k generate u(V_m), acting as zero on its orthogonal complement.

Base: iP_1 generates u(1).

Induction: assume u(V) is already in the Lie algebra. For the next vector write
v=a+b, with nonzero a in V and nonzero b orthogonal to V. Let H be the orthogonal
projector onto V; iH belongs to u(V). Write the normalized projector P_v as the
sum P_diag+P_cross of its two diagonal blocks and two off-diagonal blocks relative
to V plus span(b). Direct block multiplication gives

    [iH,[iH,iP_v]] = -i P_cross.

Hence iP_cross and iP_diag belong to the generated Lie algebra. The aa* block
of iP_diag already lies in u(V); subtracting it leaves a nonzero multiple of
ibb*. Thus the new one-dimensional diagonal generator is present. Commutators
with u(V) and with iH generate both skew-Hermitian cross-block types between b
and every vector in V: u(V) moves the nonzero a through its full complex span,
and the second commutator supplies the other cross quadrature. Together with
u(V) and u(span(b)), these are all of u(V plus span(b)).

This proves the induction for every finite m, not just this numerical dimension.
The exact certificate verifies every nondegeneracy hypothesis for m=39. The
resulting Lie algebra has real dimension 39^2=1521. The checker verifies the
hypotheses; the general Lie argument is the written proof above, not an Agda
certificate or a direct 1521-matrix closure computation.

## Consequence for the encoder

Each direction is V_j v_0 for an explicitly retained word V_j of existing
discrete operations. Its phase family is implemented by conjugation:

    exp(-i theta P_j)=V_j exp(-i theta P_0) V_j^-1.

The conjugating words are at most six discrete operations long in the stored
basis certificate. This is not a bound on the full encoder's implementation.

The closure of the group generated by these phase families is U(39) on the
active space and identity on the complement: its Lie algebra contains u(39),
and U(39) is connected. The polar encoder is metric-unitary and fixes the
complement pointwise, so it belongs to that closure. Consequently every positive
operator-norm tolerance admits a finite implementing word in this declared
continuous software model. This conclusion covers the whole 45-dimensional
encoder, hence also the five-dimensional bright input task.

It does not prove that a specified search algorithm terminates, that any finite
floating approximation has a rigorous error certificate, or that the controller
conserves the storage Hamiltonian during its pulses. The storage Hamiltonian and
contact contract were not changed. Endpoint task compatibility is distinct from
an energy-conserving implementation with apparatus included.
