# The Two-Extremal Law Is the Hilbert Function of the Cartan Quadric

## The Cartan algebra is not free

The endpoint grade tower is coherently generated by axis vectors, but it is not the free symmetric algebra on three generators. Harmonic projection kills the invariant quadratic trace

\[
q=x^2+y^2+z^2.
\]

The complete Cartan algebra is

\[
\mathcal C
=\bigoplus_{l\geq0}H_l
\cong
\operatorname{Sym}(H_1)/(q).
\]

Thus the coherence theorem has one genuine relation: the quadratic trace vanishes.

## One-step resolution

Let (S=\operatorname{Sym}(H_1)\cong\mathbb C[x,y,z]). Since (q) is a non-zero-divisor, the Cartan algebra has the minimal graded resolution

\[
0\longrightarrow S(-2)
\xrightarrow{\;q\;}
S
\longrightarrow\mathcal C
\longrightarrow0.
\]

There is one quadratic relation generator and no higher syzygy module. The higher coherence did not disappear by accident; it terminates because the defining ideal is principal and regular.

## Hilbert law

The Hilbert series is

\[
H_{\mathcal C}(t)
=\frac{1-t^2}{(1-t)^3}
=\frac{1+t}{(1-t)^2}.
\]

Its degree-(l) coefficient is

\[
\dim H_l
=\binom{l+2}{2}-\binom{l}{2}
=2l+1.
\]

Therefore

\[
\dim H_{l+1}-\dim H_l=2.
\]

The two-extremal-per-grade law is exactly the Hilbert growth of a quadric quotient.

For the spin-two chain,

\[
5,7,9
\]

are the degree-two, degree-three, and degree-four Hilbert values. The filtered expression

\[
5+2+2=9
\]

is the cumulative Hilbert growth from degree two to degree four.

## Geometric meaning

The affine cone defined by (q=0) is the complex null quadric. Harmonic highest-weight multiplication is ordinary polynomial multiplication restricted to this cone. Trace terms vanish because they are multiples of (q).

This explains simultaneously:

- why projected multiplication is associative;
- why axis order does not matter;
- why each new grade contributes two coordinates;
- why there is exactly one primitive relation;
- why no unbounded hierarchy of coherence repairs appears in this subsystem.

## A dimension-specific prediction

The constant increment two is special to harmonics in three ambient dimensions. In ambient dimension (d),

\[
\dim H_l(\mathbb R^d)
=\binom{l+d-1}{d-1}
-\binom{l+d-3}{d-1}.
\]

Only (d=3) gives constant increment two across grades. Changing the celestial cross-section or ambient representation dimension changes the port-growth law. This is a direct falsifier of any claim that two-new-directions is purely formal or dimension-independent.

## Relation to higher towers

The endpoint tower does contain a relation level above its generators, but that level is exhausted by (q). In tower language:

```text
degree-one axis generators
  -> symmetric composites
  -> one quadratic trace relation
  -> no further syzygy generators
```

Systems defined by several relations or by a nonregular ideal would require additional relation and syzygy towers. This subsystem does not.
