# The Sheet Pair Supplies the Coherence Carrier but Not Selective Loop Authority

## Endogenous carrier

Before electric or magnetic parity projection, the magnetic construction
already carries the two-sheet packet ((A,B)). On every reflected orbit the
reflection involution exchanges the two coordinates, and the joint readout

\[
(A,B)\longmapsto(E=A+B,M=A-B)
\]

is the exact Hadamard change of frame. Thus the source already supplies an
orbitwise two-dimensional coherence carrier and its complementary parity
ports.

Globally this carrier should be typed as a reflection local system unless a
source framing chooses the two sheets everywhere. The parity atlas is
canonical even when an ordered sheet basis is not.

## Selective-action obstruction

The metaplectic central loop acts identically on both sheet components:

\[
U_{\mathrm{diag}}=-I_2.
\]

It commutes with reflection and produces only a global sign. Consequently it
does not interchange the electric and magnetic parity channels and cannot be
detected by their relative interference.

The desired operation is a one-sheet sign such as

\[
U_{\mathrm{sel}}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1).
\]

This converts an even sheet vector into an odd one, but it does not commute
with sheet exchange. It therefore cannot be generated by the established
reflection-equivariant source algebra.

## Refined frontier

The missing (C^2) is not wholly external: its carrier and Hadamard ports are
already present in the completed sheet packet. What remains external is the
authority to address one sheet while holding its partner fixed.

The minimal missing constructor is therefore:

```text
reflection-framed sheet addressability
  input: the unordered or locally ordered sheet pair
  output: a selective central-loop action on one sheet
  coherence: compatibility across changes of local sheet frame
  failure boundary: nontrivial sheet monodromy or restored reflection symmetry
```

A framing that merely labels the sheets is insufficient unless the selective
loop is also source-derived. Conversely, an abstract selective matrix without
the framing is ill-typed globally.
