# The quarter four-staircase cross limit points to thirteen sixtieths

## Question

What coefficient \(\theta_2\) is supported by

\[
\Theta_n\sim\frac{\theta_2}{n^2}?
\]

Exact rational staircase determinants extend the cross-ratio grid through degree eighteen. The scaled values decrease from

\[
14^2\Theta_{14}=0.23655
\]

to

\[
18^2\Theta_{18}=0.23194.
\]

A quadratic fit in \(1/n\) over degrees fourteen through eighteen gives

\[
\theta_2=0.21674294.
\]

Among the tested small rational candidates, the nearest is

\[
\theta_2=rac{13}{60}=0.216666\ldots,
\]

with discrepancy \(7.63\times10^{-5}\).

## Disposition

Select \(13/60\) as the finite cross-limit candidate. The next leaf is `quarter-cross-thirteen-sixtieths-proof`: derive this coefficient from the vector-shifted staircase asymptotic. The subsequent full balance must explain how \(13/60\) contributes to the required \(13/48\) free-energy logarithm.

## Claim boundary

Rational recognition from five exact finite values is not a limit proof. The fitted constant is sensitive to the assumed inverse-degree remainder class.
