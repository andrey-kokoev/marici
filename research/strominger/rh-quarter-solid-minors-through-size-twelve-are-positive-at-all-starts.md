# Quarter solid minors through size twelve are positive at all starts

## Question

Does strict Newton positivity persist at sizes nine through twelve when the exact sharp degree replaces the earlier loose degree bound?

## Claim boundary

The result proves positivity for contiguous minors through size twelve at arbitrary nonnegative integer starts and shifts. It is not an all-size coefficient theorem and does not cover noncontiguous minors.

## Disposition

At sizes nine, ten, eleven, and twelve, every Newton coefficient through degrees 108, 135, 165, and 198 is strictly positive, respectively; the next forward difference vanishes exactly. Combined with the positive-weight start reduction, every solid minor through size twelve is strictly positive for arbitrary nonnegative integer row start, column start, and shift. Further size extension is stopped absent a named finite rival.
