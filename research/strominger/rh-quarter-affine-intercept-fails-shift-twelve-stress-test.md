## Bold conjecture

The same absolute affine formula survives through shift twelve under the unchanged \(0.005\) gate.

## Named rivals

The rivals are an incorrect intercept, a parity mode invisible to the two-step balance, and high-shift contamination in the cubic degree expansion.

## Risky consequences

The latest maximum residual across shifts zero through twelve must stay below \(0.005\), improve from the early window, and retain the predicted signs.

## Strongest falsification attempt

The maximum residual improves from \(0.03948\) to \(0.02273\), and all signs remain correct. However, the latest residual is more than four times the gate; at shift twelve the observed normalized correction is \(-8.31440\), versus the predicted \(-8.29167\). Five of six diagnostic conditions survive, but the decisive residual condition fails.

## Disposition

Withdraw finite recognition of the absolute intercept \(29/24\) on the enlarged shift range. Retain the sign profile, amplitude curvature, exact Y-system, and its conditional two-step slope relation. The next leaf is `quarter-two-step-slope-shift-twelve-test`, testing only the recurrence-forced difference rather than the unsupported intercept.

## Claim boundary

The finite failure does not prove that the affine formula is asymptotically false because the residual improves with cutoff. It does show that the current degree-forty-eight evidence cannot support its full shift-uniform form.
