# Quarter exterior dominance is not full Monge positivity

## Question

Is exterior-pivot dominance part of strict TP2 for entrywise-absolute principal Schur complements?

## Claim boundary

The exhaustive classification covers all 19,264 absolute \(2\times2\) minors and 5,376 pivot triples of the order-eight transfer Schur family. It does not exclude a narrower structured cone tailored to Hurwitz matrices.

## Disposition

Strict TP2 fails: the first negative absolute minor uses rows \((0,1)\) and columns \((1,4)\). Full multiplicative triple dominance also fails in 191 interior-pivot cases, beginning with \((i,p,j)=(0,1,2)\). All 3,584 exterior-pivot inequalities remain strict, with no failure. Therefore exterior dominance is an anchored subsystem, not a consequence of a global absolute Monge cone. This attempted closure is stopped. The next leaf is `quarter-hurwitz-terminal-minor-source-formula`, seeking a direct determinant formula for terminal bordered minors from the two endpoint Hurwitz coefficient arrays, bypassing circular Schur-sign propagation.
