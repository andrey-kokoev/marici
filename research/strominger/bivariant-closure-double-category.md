# Bivariant closure is a typed double category

The \(3+2+1\) architecture is naturally a partial double category.

- Objects are typed source, representation, and instrument states.
- Vertical arrows are contravariant-invariance maps: atlas changes, descent maps,
  and normalization factorizations.
- Horizontal arrows are covariant constructors: extension, composition, and
  execution.
- Squares compare the two orders in which a vertical and horizontal arrow can
  be applied.

For a square

\[
\begin{matrix}
A'&\xrightarrow{h'}&B'\\
\downarrow v_A&&\downarrow v_B\\
A&\xrightarrow{h}&B,
\end{matrix}
\]

the mixed tower asks for a source-derived comparison between \(hv_A\) and
\(v_Bh'\). Equality gives a strict square. A declared residual gives a typed
cell. If either boundary arrow lacks authority, no square exists.

The magnetic fixtures separate every case:

- The preferred-to-alternate atlas repair is strict at the invariant-object
  level.
- Factorial normalization followed by grade continuation has a rational
  curvature cell \(2(g+2)/(g+1)I\).
- The same cell is not typed over the ambient integral lattice.
- A divided-power instrument is a missing horizontal arrow, not a failed
  square.
- Independent ray saturation is an unauthorized vertical arrow, so it cannot
  participate in composition at all.

This framework predicts the next obstruction. Pairwise comparison squares may
all exist while two different pastings around a cube disagree. A double
category assumes interchange; the source-derived system must prove it.
Failure produces a modification or 3-cell residual. Thus the next tower is
not another readout: it is coherence among mixed comparison squares.

For central square residuals, the first cube test is the oriented boundary of
the six face cells. Vanishing gives coherent pasting. A nonzero value requires
a source-derived central 3-cell; fitting one from the desired endpoint is
forbidden. In the current magnetic packet the cube is not yet constructible:
the divided-power execution arrow is absent. Hence magnetic cube coherence is
undefined rather than silently satisfied.
