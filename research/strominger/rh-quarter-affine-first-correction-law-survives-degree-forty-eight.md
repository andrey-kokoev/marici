# Quarter affine first-correction law survives degree forty-eight

## Problem

At degree thirty-two, the candidate

\[

failed the fixed \(0.005\) residual gate.

## Bold conjecture

The failure was higher-order finite-window contamination, so the unchanged gate should be crossed on a degree-forty-eight recurrence grid.

## Named rivals

The rivals are a genuinely nonaffine profile, residual saturation above \(0.005\), and cutoff improvement confined to low shifts.

## Risky consequences

The maximum residual over shifts zero through six must fall below \(0.005\) on the latest nested window, improve relative to the early window, and retain every predicted sign.

## Strongest falsification attempt

Exact recurrence fits were extended through degree forty-eight with lower cutoffs ten, eighteen, twenty-six, and thirty-four. The maximum residual falls from \(0.02576\) to \(0.00215\), crossing the unchanged gate. Every sign remains correct. All six gates passed.

## Disposition

Promote the affine formula to finite recognition across shifts zero through six, not to an asymptotic theorem. The next leaf is `quarter-affine-law-recurrence-coefficient-balance`, substituting a uniform shifted expansion into the exact recurrence to determine whether the affine slope and intercept are forced or remain independent data.

## Claim boundary

Regression survival does not prove the expansion, uniform remainder, or exact coefficients. The shift-zero cross-limit remains non-exact.
