# Fourth energy search: avoid repeating the vacuum/contact loop

## Most important finding

The negative-belt mechanism from our latest common contact test was already anticipated in `research/voevodsky/belt-coherent-port-and-vacuum-stability.md`. That note gives an explicit negative Rayleigh trial whenever a zero-energy coherent mode couples to a positive auxiliary with nonzero overlap. Our endpoint/gauge-compatible realization adds a new adapter and exact block values, but not a new general explanation of the instability.

A positive bare offset would stabilize the old exchange model, but its threshold is not a positive mass prediction: the threshold state has zero energy. This should be a preflight check, not another repeated parameter experiment.

## 1. Complete generators matter, but do not automatically solve the hierarchy

`research/voevodsky/elementary-gap-contact-bridge.md` already constructs a positive common-clock exchange from a supplied swap:

    P_exchange=(a-w)(a-w)* / 2.

It includes a contact diagonal term as well as exchange. With native K, a matched zero vector remains and proton/belt splitting is zero. Adding the same auxiliary occupation operation makes both shifts positive, but gives the reverse ordering (approximately 0.0438854 proton versus 0.0243144 belt).

Thus simply completing our off-diagonal coupling to a positive exchange projector is an already tested rival, not an untried cure.

Do not confuse that projector with the signed-record construction in `finite-record-mediator-constructor.md`:

    Q_plus+Q_minus=I-P tensor X.

The latter has a scalar diagonal, which is present in all occupied sectors AND the empty pool. Common subtraction legitimately gives -P tensor X. Our latest coupling was not missing a secretly mandatory P diagonal from that different construction.

## 2. Existing source modes cannot manufacture a new rest offset by elimination

`research/voevodsky/source-channel-closure-and-all-face-dressing.md` proves the source-contact Schur relation

    Sigma(-t)=h00+t-1/R(t).

Since the actual contact overlaps a coherent zero mode, Sigma(0)=h00 and the effective source ground stays zero. Relabelling retained modes as an auxiliary cannot turn a constraint Hamiltonian into the independently postulated interaction.

The same work audits contact selection: 78 of 126 compared pairs have the proposed response ordering, 40 have counterexamples and 8 remain uncertified. The all-cross-face rule succeeds structurally because it is a specific retained profile, not because every geometrical contact must have the same ordering.

## 3. A valid heavier-belt mechanism already exists; its baseline remains an input

`research/voevodsky/charge-compatible-internal-contact.md` puts each auxiliary in the SAME charge/baryon sector as its carrier. With shared gap and coupling,

    H_s=mu I + [[Delta,g<w_s|],[g|w_s>,K_s]],
    E_s=mu-x_s.

The source response gives x_proton>x_belt, so the belt is heavier independently of the common mu. This repairs charge compatibility and makes the relative difference meaningful. It does not select mu, Delta or g. Reusing equal unit-gap baselines plus the already tested normalized mediator leads back to the prior common-charging exclusion.

## 4. Nima's protected-sector clue is narrower than an energy law

`research/nima/mass-local-sector-protection.md` shows that exact Gauss locking and restricted tensor support can forbid collective/elementary sector conversion. However, a one-site diagonal term changes their relative energy without violating those same assumptions. Transition protection is not protection of the gap.

A symmetric microscopic parent with a dynamically selected constraint phase is named as a possible new construction. It is not supplied by the tested rotor model. This is a genuinely upstream direction: source the constraint and charging metric jointly instead of appending another register to an already chosen zero-ground carrier.

## 5. Source emission already separates energy supply from a coupling matrix

`research/nima/photon-source-emission.md` retains source gap, broadband field Hamiltonian and drive/work account. Even tuning the source gap to the field's mean frequency leaves nonzero work variance; a mode-matched two-state exchange is not automatically an autonomous interaction.

Likewise, `four-packet-record-feedback.md` gives exact conservative recurrence with retained records, but its Hamiltonian/readout must not be promoted to a distinguished bound-particle energy by naming the fixed state a particle.

## Revised next-work gate

Before another three-particle energy experiment, require a candidate input package to specify:

1. The degrees of freedom carrying occupied-sector energy relative to a common empty state.
2. The complete action or generator, including diagonals, charging metric and vacuum/reference terms.
3. The mechanism selecting the conserved collective sector and its charge, with admissible interactions.
4. Whether the source actually supplies that package or it is a declared competing model.

Then run three immediate controls: zero-mode Schur cancellation, negative-contact variational trial, and common-gap mass bound. Only candidates surviving those controls warrant another coupled spectrum computation.

The searched literature supplies the components and these exclusions, but not a completed source-selected charging action fixing the proton/electron hierarchy. That missing input cannot be filled by another wrapper, re-diagonalization or tuned offset.
