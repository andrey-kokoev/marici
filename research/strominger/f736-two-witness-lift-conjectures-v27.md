## Conjecture A: canonical polarized-kernel reconstruction

Node: `f736-polarized-kernel-witness-lift-conjecture`.

### Proposed construction

For each actual cutoff Lambda, use the independent scalar remainder functional E_Lambda on D and polarize it by the source convolution:

    b_Lambda(g,k)=E_Lambda(g*k_star).

The continuous sesquilinear form determines a distribution kernel K_Lambda on the source pair, with the conjugation convention retained. Formally, if E_Lambda is represented by a distribution e_Lambda in the difference variable, then

    K_Lambda(u,v)=e_Lambda(u-v),
    b_Lambda(g,k)=<K_Lambda,g(u) conjugate(k(v))>.

The general definition is the distribution-kernel representation of b, not a demand that e or K be ordinary functions. The reverse ordered family determines its own kernel. This source-kernel construction and its scalar recovery are not the conjectural leap; the hard assertion is its interpretation as an ACTUAL analytical boundary witness.

**Conjecture:** there is a continuous source-linked kernel realization L into the already specified representative-bearing boundary/modification target such that

    partial_native L(K_Lambda)
      = B_cut,Lambda - B_spec,

where B_cut and B_spec are the independently constructed parent boundary transformations, interpreted in that target. The displayed subtraction is required only in a declared additive realization of their boundary complex; otherwise use its explicitly typed comparison equation instead of pretending arbitrary natural transformations can be subtracted.

L must preserve actual source whiskering, both ordered placements, endpoint/counterterm conventions and finite representatives; its observation must recover b_Lambda. These properties must be proved, not obtained by defining partial_native to be the desired difference. The native/word comparison then transports L(K_Lambda) while retaining the full boundary package.

### Why this is a specific mechanism

It fixes the candidate from the full POLARIZED source kernel, not an arbitrary operator with the right trace, a fitted five-by-five matrix or a chosen higher filler. It proposes a local reconstruction in the source variables. No integration along the cutoff parameter is required.

Kernel faithfulness on source forms is not faithfulness of the scalar trace on physical operators. The claim is about a source-authorized realization of that kernel in the intended boundary target, not an unsupported identification of unlike Hilbert operators.

### First hard test

Choose one non-diagonal compact source pair and write the independently supplied boundary operators/transformations BEFORE scalar evaluation. Construct L(K_Lambda) by an explicit kernel/graph formula and compute its boundary. Check the same formula on an admitted source whisker and on the reverse placement. A fixed-packet matrix equality alone is insufficient.

### Falsifiers

Reject this mechanism if the proposed L does not land in the declared domain, if its actual boundary contains an unaccounted term, or if it fails a required source-whiskering/endpoint/placement equation. A stronger rejection would be a concrete obstruction to any continuous kernel realization satisfying these independently fixed conditions; failure of one formula is not such a universal no-go.

The mechanism cannot recover additional independently chosen feature-frame data merely because its signed kernel is fixed. If those data are necessary and not determined by this construction, the conjecture fails at that claimed strength or needs an explicitly weaker scope.

## Conjecture B: geometric cutoff-current transgression

Node: `f736-cutoff-current-transgression-witness-conjecture`.

### Proposed construction

Use logarithmic cutoff L=log Lambda and the ACTUAL physical projections/regulators, not a surrogate window. Write the centered boundary family

    T_L(h)=tau_exp(L)(h)-2L h(0).

Differentiate the physical cutoff construction on the admitted source core. The proposed current has scalar observation

    J_L(h)=d/dL tau_exp(L)(h)-2h(0).

The derivative must be obtained from the actual cutoff boundary/kernel calculus. For sharp projections it may initially be distributional. Derivatives of the Fourier cutoff, actual outer regulator where varying, endpoint and placement terms must all be retained. It is not enough to DEFINE J as the derivative of a previously known scalar error and stop there.

**Conjecture:** this geometric current has a lift j_L in the independently specified analytical boundary complex, with sufficient source-local integrability that

    H_L = - integral_(r=L)^infinity j_r dr

exists in the declared witness topology and satisfies the intended cutoff-versus-spectral boundary equation. Its scalar observation is

    obs(H_L)=T_L-W=E_exp(L).

The sign follows from integration toward the source-matched limiting spectral boundary. The sign/orientation on both ordered placements must be checked in the actual target.

The construction must commute with the admitted source/native pullbacks and pasting operations. Any comparison to another cutoff schedule must come from the actual two-parameter boundary/Stokes calculation, not from generic contractibility.

### Why this is a different mechanism

It builds a primitive from the physical regulator deformation and its boundary current. It is nonlocal in cutoff and may require additional boundary coordinates or domain control absent from the kernel route. It does not require a right inverse from arbitrary source kernels to native modifications.

Conversely, a polarized remainder kernel could exist with no absolutely integrable witness-valued cutoff derivative. Rapid decay of the scalar E alone does NOT imply corresponding derivative estimates or integrability in an operator/witness topology. Therefore A and B are not interchangeable.

### First hard test

At finite regulator and for one actual polarized source pair, differentiate the two ordered physical kernels, including the volume subtraction. Identify every boundary term, then estimate the tail of the lifted current in the declared graph/trace/distribution topology. Check that its integrated boundary agrees with the independently constructed spectral route, including endpoint terms.

This is a concrete differentiation-and-domain problem, not another search for a categorical wrapper.

### Falsifiers

Reject the proposed transgression if its actual current is not admissible or integrable on the required source, if integration leaves a nonzero endpoint/placement term, or if it depends on an inadmissible regulator replacement. Failure of absolute integrability rejects the stated integral mechanism, not all possible renormalized or distributional alternatives; such an alternative would be a new explicit conjecture.

In particular, a scalar fundamental-theorem-of-calculus identity without a witness-valued primitive does not satisfy this conjecture.

## Comparison and work order

| | A: kernel reconstruction | B: cutoff transgression |
|---|---|---|
| Candidate fixed by | Full polarized source remainder kernel | Actual differentiated geometric cutoff |
| New construction needed | Continuous kernel-to-witness realization | Admissible integrable witness-valued current |
| First computation | Boundary of one explicit kernel lift | Physical boundary derivative and tail estimate |
| Characteristic failure | Kernel realization misses native boundary/frame data | Current has nonintegrable or unaccounted boundary terms |

Neither conjecture implies the other on current evidence. They may both succeed, both fail, or require a restricted target. They are mechanisms for the existing open witness-record task, not formal prerequisites whose failure disproves the broader F736 problem.

Start with B's finite-regulator derivative calculation as the more direct physical attempt; keep A as the independent reconstruction route. This recommendation is a proposed experiment, not a mechanically established ranking or operator-confirmed proof route.
