# Forty-nine raw ports quotient to seven contextual types

## The operator's proposed division

The suggestion that one factor of seven should be divided out of forty-nine
is exactly correct for the contextual port family.

Raw scalar interfaces on the two-route record space are labelled by

\[
(u,v)\in\mathbb F_7^2.
\]

There are forty-nine such coordinate ports:

\[
g_{u,v}(a,b)=ua+vb.
\]

The reachable route image is the anti-diagonal

\[
A=\{(x,-x):x\in\mathbb F_7\}.
\]

On this image,

\[
g_{u,v}(x,-x)=(u-v)x.
\]

Therefore adding the same \(t\) to both coefficients changes no reachable
record:

\[
(u,v)\sim(u+t,v+t).
\]

The seven-element diagonal subgroup

\[
\Delta=\{(t,t):t\in\mathbb F_7\}
\]

is exactly the annihilator of the reachable anti-diagonal.

## Typed quotient

The behavioral quotient is

\[
\mathbb F_7^2/\Delta\cong\mathbb F_7,
\]

with faithful coordinate

\[
[(u,v)]\longmapsto u-v.
\]

Hence

\[
49/7=7
\]

is not merely cardinal arithmetic here.  It is the orbit count of a canonical
contextual-equivalence action.

The seven quotient types are:

- one zero-contrast class, which is blind on the route image;
- six nonzero-contrast classes, which are faithful.

Every class contains exactly seven raw coordinate presentations.

## Relation to the seven Boolean obligations

The contextual quotient produces a seven-element coefficient object.  The
three-axis Boolean packet also has seven slots.  No canonical map between them
has yet been constructed.  One is indexed by contrast residues; the other is
indexed by nonempty subsets of axes.  Equal cardinality does not identify
their elements.

A source-derived comparison would need to assign each nonempty axis subset a
specific contrast class and prove compatibility with inclusion and
interchange.  Until then, the equality of cardinalities remains a typed
coincidence.

## Relation to the nonsplit lift

There are also two different groups of order forty-nine:

\[
\mathbb F_7^2
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\mathbb Z/49.
\]

The raw port space is the split group \(\mathbb F_7^2\).  The magnetic lift
fiber belongs to the nonsplit cyclic group \(\mathbb Z/49\).  Both admit a
quotient by a seven-element subgroup, but their exponents differ:

\[
\exp(\mathbb F_7^2)=7,
\qquad
\exp(\mathbb Z/49)=49.
\]

Thus the operator's division is exact in both cases, while the middle objects
remain differently typed.

## Deutschian interpretation

The explanatory quotient is not “divide because the numbers fit.”  It is:

> Restrict ports to the source-reachable image, quotient by its annihilator,
> and use the induced contrast coordinate, proved faithful on the quotient.

This is precisely the faithful-quotient-coordinate rule required by the
repository policy.
