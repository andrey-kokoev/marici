# Prior-research audit: do not turn internal Hopf winding into spatial charge by default

## Question and correction

The global Hopf trial produced 44 units of auxiliary boundary flux. This followed from an explicit added hypothesis: one equal-sign spatial monopole at every interior vertex. Neither that hypothesis nor a zero-flux native boundary law has been established. Thus there is not yet a source-defined contradiction requiring a compensating sink.

A bounded repository search found a better-supported direction than tuning those 44 signs. This audit reads prior notes; it does not freshly replay their reported exact checkers.

## Most relevant prior results

`research/voevodsky/spinor-potential-link-bridge-v1.md` constructs a retained electron spinor/potential/link bridge. Its state space is `T x (C^2 minus {0})`; at fixed norm the phase bundle is Hopf over the potential boundary sphere. It explicitly says that FCC representation coordinates are **not** the CP1 base coordinates. Projector/curvature data also do not replace the retained connection and loop data.

These are direct warnings against our trial's unbound identification of internal direction with `(cellCentre-vertex)/distance`, followed by interpreting every vertex-link sphere as a spatial monopole.

### Opposite phase weights have a conditional source candidate

`research/voevodsky/electron-pairing-coupling.md` distinguishes a frozen complex-linear attachment from the state-dependent phase registration `D(p)=p/conjugate(p)`. The latter can relate opposite variable phase weights, but the retained arrow equations do not select it over the frozen-frame rival.

Its neutral spinor bilinear `B(p,e)=p^T epsilon e` requires opposite weights if neutral output is separately supplied. This is a two-input operation, not deterministic conjugation of one unknown spinor. The note explicitly leaves the proton spinorial input and native coupling selection open.

`research/strominger/baryon-doublet-extension-v1.md` confirms that adding a baryon doublet is a declared rank-changing enlargement, not a consequence of the original rank-one return. Its spin/coupling gate remains open.

### Other flux results cannot be substituted

`research/voevodsky/smaller-negative-flux-carrier.md` retains a source decomposition `+4/3 - 1/3 = +1`. Its inherited negative component cannot be reset to unit negative flux without changing amplitude. This is not an authorized set of 44 compensating Chern charges.

`research/voevodsky/gauss-derived-spatial-binding.md` explicitly supplies opposite species charges and spatial translations as additional inputs. It does not derive them from internal face polarization. It therefore cannot supply the missing native charge map here.

## Best current conjecture

Keep the Hopf construction as an **internal retained phase/spinor bundle**, and derive the native chart comparisons before attaching any spatial monopole reading. Investigate whether the operator's paired records retain complementary/dual phase lines rather than only one chosen polarized line.

The mathematical model for the latter possibility is `E = L direct-sum L*`. It retains both channels and has `c1(E)=c1(L)+c1(L*)=0`, with determinant-one transitions `diag(g,g^-1)`. Individual line winding remains nonzero; vanishing determinant flux does not mean flat transport or erase histories. This is different from collapsing to the scalar tensor contraction `L tensor L*`, which would not retain an independent two-component record.

The cited work motivates this conjecture but does not establish that native P/E records are these two lines. Nor does it authorize introducing a new independent doublet where the source has only a line. The four direction pairings cannot be identified with spinor components merely by matching counts.
