# Rung symmetry classification by retained structure

## What is classified

Machine-readable per-rung classifications: `rung-symmetry-atlas-v3.json`, extending the v2 object/map atlas.

This replaces a list of known generators with **full automorphism groups in explicitly stated categories**. The categories are complex-linear, unitary for the supplied positive field metric, with specified endpoint/fiber/projector/action data retained. They are not interchangeable.

The result concerns the current P field and its lossless candidate field-slot tower. It does not identify that candidate with the numerical/DG tower or a physical gauge system. Antiunitary transformations, projective quotients, arbitrary nonlinear automorphisms and unspecified scalar-observer stabilizers are outside this classification.

## Exact tensor form in the existing coframes

The independently constructed realization matrix B changes from ordered corner coefficients to native spatial vectors. In those corner coordinates, exact matrix tests give

```
H = C^12 tensor C^3
K = I12 tensor K3
L = I12 tensor P
G = (J12 / 12) tensor I3
```

Here P is a rank-one K3-orthogonal projector. Proper tetrahedral symmetries permute the twelve directed-arrow fibers regularly and act identically on the corner component index. Their realization in spatial coordinates rotates the vectors as already checked by B.

This is more informative than the bare equation 36=12×3. It explains the projector-sector dimensions:

| Sector | Arrow part | Component part | Dimension |
|---|---|---|---:|
| LG | Constant arrow line | P line | 1 |
| L−LG | Arrow contrasts | P line | 11 |
| G−LG | Constant arrow line | P complement | 2 |
| I−L−G+LG | Arrow contrasts | P complement | 22 |

These are not four equal channels and are not numerical rung assignments.

## Full field groups, with the category specified

### Metric only

All complex-linear metric isometries form **U(36)**. This deliberately forgets projectors, endpoint fibers, named operations and observers.

### Metric and the two named projectors

Requiring both L and G to be preserved fixes their four joint sectors. The complete unitary group is

```
U(1) × U(11) × U(2) × U(22).
```

No sector exchange is allowed: the projectors are named. This group includes many transformations mixing different arrow fibers. Its real Lie dimension is 610.

### Also commute with every named proper tetrahedral action

This is a **pointwise commutant**, not the group allowed to relabel the geometric operations among themselves.

The exact A4 character decomposition of the joint sectors is:

| Sector | Multiplicities of (1, 1′, 1″, 3) |
|---|---|
| LG | (1,0,0,0) |
| L−LG | (0,1,1,3) |
| G−LG | (2,0,0,0) |
| I−L−G+LG | (0,2,2,6) |

By the complex unitary commutant theorem, the full group is

```
U(1)^3 × U(2)^3 × U(3) × U(6).
```

Its real Lie dimension—and the complex dimension of the corresponding linear commutant—is **60**. The associative algebra generated by the declared projectors and geometric actions has complex dimension **24**, refining the earlier upper bound of 48.

The nonabelian A4 rotations themselves do not generally commute with every named A4 operator. Thus the previously generated 48-element group must not be described as a subgroup of this pointwise commutant. It belongs to a different action-preservation category.

## Retain the actual labelled arrow fibers

For a field automorphism fixing each arrow fiber, write it in corner coordinates as diag(T_e). Commutation with G gives T_e=T_f for every pair of arrows, because every off-diagonal G block is I3/12. Therefore there is one shared 3×3 component operation T.

Commutation with L requires TP=PT. Metric preservation then gives the **complete** fiber-fixing group

```
U(1) × U(2).
```

This is a completeness argument, not just a search for some matrices. An independent exact commutator calculation finds dimension 5 for the linear algebra End(C) ⊕ End(C²), as expected. Exact K-orthogonal Householder reflections in the two-dimensional complement give noncommuting examples. A phase 3/5 + 4i/5 on the P line gives a checked local unitary outside the old 48-element group.

If the actual named corner three-cycle is also retained pointwise, the result is smaller still:

```
U(1).
```

The cycle has order three, but it is **not K3-unitary** and does not commute with P. It must not be replaced by three orthogonal phase channels. Exact simultaneous commutator equations for T with P and this cycle have rank eight in nine unknown entries: only scalar T survive. Their metric-unitary subgroup is the common phase U(1).

These factors describe allowed component-basis changes under the stated constraints. They are not newly derived physical gauge fields.

### Permit endpoint relabelling

The four endpoints belong to one shared vertex carrier. Each directed arrow is determined by its ordered endpoints; each comparison slot is determined by its branch and ordered parents. Hence an incidence-preserving combinatorial automorphism has no independent freedom to permute the 12 arrows or 137 slots once its vertex permutation is fixed.

All 24 vertex permutations are checked. In the corner presentation they preserve K,L,G, giving

```
S4 × (U(1) × U(2)).
```

These coframed relabellings are a mathematical extension. Odd permutations are **not** thereby certified as native spatial mirror operations. Restricting to the independently sourced proper geometric subgroup gives A4 × (U(1) × U(2)).

For the actual candidate with a **fixed directed reference arrow**, the endpoint group becomes S2 (the spectator swap). Within proper A4, the ordered-edge stabilizer is trivial. Thus:

- Fixed reference, coframed endpoint-incidence category: **S2 × U(1) × U(2)**.
- Fixed reference, proper-geometric subfamily: **U(1) × U(2)**.
- Additionally fixing the named corner cycle replaces U(1) × U(2) by **U(1)**.

Across the family of all transported reference choices, the larger S4 or A4 action remains covariant. A fixed-reference object and the family of reference-marked objects must not be conflated.

## Why the earlier two binary switches are not two local edge switches

The local reflection 2L−I is fiberwise. The alignment reflection 2G−I has nonzero blocks between different arrow fibers. It is a **global mixing operation** in this decomposition, not an independent switch on each transport edge.

Consequently the generated A4 × V4 group is a valid finite source-field action, but not the full endpoint-fiber-preserving automorphism group—and its V4 is not wholly contained in the local U(1) × U(2) fiber action.

For the endpoint-incidence-preserving, metric/L/G-preserving category with fixed reference, the candidate tower has:

For the outputs, this assumes the full typed parent/endpoint structure and repeated-occurrence consistency are retained. With those conditions, every output automorphism determines a unique source field automorphism and vice versa. A horizontal correspondence has symmetry pairs (a,b) satisfying bE=Ea; since E is an isomorphism onto its constrained image, b is forced by a. It does not have two independent input/output group factors.

This is not merely transporting a known 48-element subgroup: the source group has been classified completely in this category, and reconstruction plus parent incidence rules out extra output automorphisms in the same category. Forgetting parent maps, allowing arbitrary nonlinear maps, or varying inconsistent slot values would be a different problem.

### Fields and observers

The metric on the consistent slot image is the **reconstructed field metric**, not an assumed unweighted sum of repeated slot payload norms. A different slot-weight metric would require a new invariance check.

A fixed field value or observation can reduce these groups further. For the fiber-fixing U(1) × U(2) action, fixing a specified list of vectors leaves

```
U(1-r1) × U(2-r2),
```

where r1 and r2 are the span ranks of all specified vectors projected into the P line and its two-dimensional complement. This follows from pointwise vector fixing; it is not the stabilizer formula for every nonlinear scalar observable. A generic enough field has r1=1,r2=2 and no continuous fiber-fixing stabilizer. Endpoint symmetries must then also be tested against the actual values.

No specific physical rung4 observer was supplied, so its stabilizer is not assigned a fabricated numerical group.
