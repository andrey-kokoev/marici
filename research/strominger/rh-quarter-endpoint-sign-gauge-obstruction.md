# Quarter endpoint sign-gauge obstruction

## Question

Can source-index checkerboard signs and row/column permutations repair the direct endpoint coupling?

## Claim boundary

For the exact bounded coupling `T=A^{-1}B`, the bipartite sign graph is inconsistent with every factorization `sign(T_ij)=r_i c_j`. The first detected inconsistency occurs at entry `(1,1)`. Therefore no independent diagonal row and column sign gauge makes `T` entrywise nonnegative. Row and column permutations only relabel this graph, so they cannot remove the obstruction. This does not test non-diagonal similarities, stabilization, rectangular lifts, or a common network formed before endpoint specialization.

## Disposition

Reject the diagonal-sign and permutation repair of the direct coupling. The remaining highest-value rival is a source-derived common network or rectangular lift constructed before specialization.
