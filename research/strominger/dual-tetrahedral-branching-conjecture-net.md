## Distinguish the parameters before comparing numbers

- d: main configuration/space dimension.
- b: binary branching depth.
- n: principal electron-shell number.
- r: representation label, with independent index dimension dim(V_r).
- sigma: separately declared spin label, not an antipodal vertex sign.

these roles and an independently sourced grading map are fixed. Space dimension

## A literal geometric source for the proposed expression

A d-cross-polytope has vertices +/-e_i. Its facets are labelled by sign vectors
s in{+1,-1}^d and contain the d vertices s_i e_i. Each of the2d vertices belongs
to2^(d-1) facets, so its vertex-facet FLAGS number

    F(d)=2d*2^(d-1)=d*2^d.

At d=4 the facets are tetrahedra: the16-cell has8 vertices,16 tetrahedra and64
vertex-tetrahedron incidences. The actual16-cell geometry is already present in
the source-pinned capacity experiment. This count also equals directed edges of
the dual-coordinate d-hypercube, but counting agreement is not a particle map.

Here the leading2 counts opposite vertices, NOT spin. In the alternative raw
history interpretation the factors would instead be d choices, d-1 binary
branch decisions, and a separate spin doublet. These are different constructions
even though they have the same integer cardinality.

The same16-cell's vertex operator has only8 scalar modes, or16 under our supplied
spin factor. Its band capacities are2,8,6, not64. Thus an actual shared source
already distinguishes geometric incidence count from retained channel rank.
Choosing functions on64 flags is a new carrier/task, not a reindexing of that
8-vertex operator or a derived physical electron source.
