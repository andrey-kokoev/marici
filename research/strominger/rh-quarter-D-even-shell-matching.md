# Even reflected-convolution shell matching

## Question

Can negative-parity terms be injected into positive-parity terms by pairing adjacent symmetric shells around the center of each even convolution?

## Claim boundary

Writing the coefficient at degree `2k` as an alternating sum of symmetric shell weights is exact. If those shell weights decreased away from the center, adjacent pairing would prove positivity. The condition fails at `(n,s,k)=(3,0,2)` and in 1,026 of 1,092 exact profiles, although every final alternating sum is positive. This rejects centered adjacent-shell matching, not weighted dominance. The governing DPC case remains `rh-quarter-D-imaginary-axis-dominance.md`.

## Disposition

Local shell monotonicity is not the mechanism. The next admissible weighted matching must use cumulative capacity across multiple shells, with exact prefix or suffix majorization inequalities rather than a nearest-shell injection.
