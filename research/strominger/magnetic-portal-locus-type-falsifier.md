# The residual zero is not the magnetic portal locus

The natural first guess is that the continued odd-core equation

\[
P(g,d)=0
\]

defines the support of a magnetic defect portal. It does not.

At grade six,

\[
P(6,d)=4(3d-17)(3d-37),
\]

so the reduced residual chart vanishes at \(d=17/3\) and \(d=37/3\).
But this chart is obtained only after an integer-tail elimination. Its three
source labels vary with \(d\):

\[
(0,d-1,d+1).
\]

At \(d=17/3\), these occupy depth cosets

\[
\left(0,\frac23,\frac23\right)\pmod 2,
\]

rather than the single even-depth coset of the classified source grammar.
Thus \(d\) is not a scalar parameter in one fixed source module here. Varying
\(d\) changes the module's grading support.

There is a stronger rank falsifier. Direct construction of the three full path
columns at \((g,d)=(6,17/3)\) has the nonzero maximal minor

\[
\Delta=-\frac{48775302133841920000}{729}.
\]

Consequently the maximal-minor Fitting ideal of the full three-column map is a
unit in the local ring at this point. The full map has no rank defect there.
The equation \(P=0\) therefore describes a zero of a reduced coordinate after
an unavailable elimination, not the degeneracy locus of the full transport
object.

The same typing obstruction appears on the cubic cover. The three candidates
have cover pole depths and barred exponents

\[
A=(-4,10,16),\qquad r=(-50,6,0).
\]

The essential pullback image of the original grammar requires

\[
A=2\pmod 6,\qquad r=0\pmod 3.
\]

None of the three candidates satisfies both conditions.

Hence the current evidence determines what the portal locus is not:

- it is not the scalar zero scheme of \(P(g,d)\);
- it is not generated by clearing the denominator of \(d\);
- it is not a Fitting stratum of the existing full path matrix.

A genuine portal must first construct a fixed enlarged source family carrying
the missing congruence classes, charge adapters, and fractional tail. Only then
does its full transport map possess a legitimate Fitting ideal whose non-flat
support can be tested.

The next constructive target is therefore not another root calculation. It is
a grade-changing correspondence

\[
\mathcal G_{\mathrm{even}}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal G_{\mathrm{extended}}
\]

with explicit tail and deck-charge data. Without that constructor, the
rational zeros remain local analytic shadows rather than portal-supported
states.
