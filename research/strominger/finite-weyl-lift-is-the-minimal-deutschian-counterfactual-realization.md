charge coordinates. Supertranslations act by real translations, and the soft
charge supplies a classical Hamiltonian functional. These data do not include:

- an integral period lattice in the relevant two-dimensional plane;
- a prequantum line bundle;

Therefore the existence of a classical symplectic or Green boundary form does
not authorize a seventh-root phase. Restricting a continuous coordinate to

## Minimal finite Weyl packet

Let

\[
D=\mathbb Z/7,
\qquad
\zeta=\exp(2\pi i/7),
\]

and let \(\mathcal K_D\) have orthonormal basis \(\{|t\rangle:t\in D\}\).
Define the shift and clock operators

\[
U|t\rangle=|t+1\rangle,
\qquad
V|t\rangle=\zeta^{2t}|t\rangle.
\]

They satisfy

\[
UV=\zeta^{-2}VU,
\]

equivalently \(VU=\zeta^2UV\). The central phase is exactly the restricted
affine-discriminant linking coefficient.

\[

Prepare a coherent reference state

\[

\[

This is the requested counterfactual: the outcome changes with the

## Minimality

A diagonal character operator alone is insufficient operationally if no
coherent comparison basis is available. The shift \(U\) supplies the
noncommuting conjugate polarization. Together \(U\) and \(V\) generate the
full matrix algebra on \(\mathcal K_D\); consequently the finite character is
observable and every pair of finite states is distinguishable.

This is strictly stronger than adjoining a passive seven-valued label. The
commutator records the binary primal--dual linking law and provides the
counterfactual operation required by Deutsch's criterion.
