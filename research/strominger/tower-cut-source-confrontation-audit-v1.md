## 1. The central distinction was already explicit

[`../nima/three-witness-retained-cone.md`](../nima/three-witness-retained-cone.md) distinguishes TWO triangles:

1. the original comparison of three physical descriptions, including its supplied compatibility witness;

More decisively, the prior checker already contains a false-original-comparison control: its lossless presentation transports still commute, while the original comparison validator rejects the packet. The typed specialization requires the original face proof as an input.

**Consequence:** our proposed next test, asking whether generic presentation compatibility rejects a false physical law, largely repeats an already answered negative. It is not the missing discovery. Our oscillator left inverse is useful source reconstruction, but does not identify the original physical face with the raised presentation face.

## 2. There is a substantive candidate meaning of a formed domain triangle

[`../nima/charge-mass-recoil-triangle.md`](../nima/charge-mass-recoil-triangle.md) supplies an example with named, independently meaningful maps:

- coupling to paired momentum increments: J=q1*q2*G;
- momentum increments to velocity increments: divide by the respective masses;
- direct coupling to velocity increments;
- a face comparing those two routes.

Omitting recoil, replacing signed coupling by intensity, or using the wrong mass leaves a typed physical residual. This is not merely three copies of a packet. But its interaction and inertia laws are supplied, and face closure does not select charge/mass normalization.

[`../nima/three-observers-generate-a-transport-triangle.md`](../nima/three-observers-generate-a-transport-triangle.md) gives a second precise role: three common-source frames yield direct and composed relative transports, with full group-valued holonomy testing coherence. A scalar character can hide a real defect. This is a structural reason for a triangle, not a proof that every physical domain has this form.

**Consequence:** we need not demand that a triangle derive a law from nothing. We do need to identify its actual vertices, operative maps and comparison witness in the domain, rather than substituting the automatic new presentation triangle for them.

## Confrontation and executable next direction

The supported criticism is precise: **our current physical examples bind physical packets to tower presentations, but they have not identified the original physical comparison triangle with the triangle claimed to be exposed by the source construction.** Some related source-action bindings do exist, so claiming that nothing beyond arbitrary packaging has ever been done would also be false.

For a chosen physical domain, the meaningful comparison is between independently specified source operations and physical operations, with admitted parameters explicit. It must commute on actual source-generated states, preserve the required observations and relations, and identify the original face witnesses rather than only their stored encodings. Defining the physical-side operation afterward by decode/apply/encode establishes presentation covariance, not this independent comparison.

The next productive work is to follow the existing seed→exchange→mechanical-readout chain and inspect the face/action identification on its common ports. The known elastic success and signed-charge failure are starting evidence, not tasks to rediscover. If using the original charge–mass–recoil domain, the charged impulse cannot be identified with that exchange without a separately admitted charge-dependent source operation; do not hide the missing operation in a fitted kernel. If using the elastic domain instead, declare that different target openly and test the multiport/global-body binding, which the prior single-port result expressly leaves open.

This is a concrete source-operation problem, not a request for another oscillator coefficient test, another wrapper equivalence theorem, or an axiom that triangle compatibility alone derives physics. No parent conjecture is closed or refuted by this audit, and no new conjecture is silently substituted for the existing one.
