# Relational DPC v2

A Distinction-Preserving Configuration is indexed by a coefficient domain
\(K\) and an authority root. It consists of:

- typed base-axis objects;
- contravariant invariance arrows;
- covariant composability arrows;
- a source-derived horn classifier \(\mathcal R_K(h)\);
- partial pasting correspondences between compatible classifier values;
- typed higher-boundary residues;
- instrument incidences
  \(\rho_I\subset R_D\times P_I\times O_I\).

Admissibility is static. It means that all claimed cells belong to the
declared classifier objects, all claimed joint cells lie in their
compatibility fiber products, and all higher boundaries have source-derived
dispositions.

## Hostile results

Local inhabitation is insufficient. Two residue objects

\[
R_1=\{0\},\qquad R_2=\{1\}
\]

are individually nonempty, while their equality fiber product is empty.

Presentation equivalence is insufficient. A bijection exchanging \(a\) and
\(b\) carries the authorized subset \(\{a\}\) to \(\{b\}\), but does not
authorize \(b\) unless an authority comparison accompanies the bijection.

Port existence is insufficient. Nonempty residue, preparation, and detector
sets yield no readout when the instrument incidence relation is empty.

Ordinary pullbacks are insufficient in a derived evidence domain. For the
span

\[
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z/2\longleftarrow0,
\]

the ordinary pullback is zero, while the homotopy pullback contains the
shifted class \(\mathbb Z/2[-1]\). An undeclared ordinary pullback would erase
the compatibility residue. DPC must therefore type pullback semantics as
ordinary, homotopy, or derived according to the evidence domain.

Coefficient extension is insufficient. The magnetic curvature filler exists
over \(\mathbb Q\) and not over \(\mathbb Z\); evidence survives extension,
but authority and integrality do not descend.

Pairwise cells are insufficient because the cube boundary may be nonzero.
An unpointed line is also insufficient to select a scalar.

## Surviving formulation

A configuration is DPC-admissible only relative to its declared coefficient
domain and authority root. Local classifiers are combined by the declared
ordinary, homotopy, or derived pullback; variance changes require authorized
classifier comparisons; pasting is partial and residue-valued; and physical
values require explicit instrument incidence.

This is a relational horn theory, not a temporal repair process and not a
total category of automatically composable arrows.
