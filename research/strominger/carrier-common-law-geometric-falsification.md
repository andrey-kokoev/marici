## Predeclared alternative and nonpromotion

A general affine point map B(x)=Kx+b can satisfy the two B observations only if
K(M1-M0)=0. Thus any such solution collapses the geometric realization-axis
direction. Retain that predicted kernel rather than presenting the fitted map as
an invertible transport. The explicit coordinate retraction B(q,h,r)=(0,h,0)
is a consistency witness, not a derived full-state operating law or a repair of
the rejected translation class.

The source common junction C1 could supply a base for a different law. That is a
new, explicitly based candidate, not evidence that the unbased translation
survived. No based family, extra response channel or per-particle map is silently
inserted by this experiment.

Most importantly, collapse of geometric positions does not establish erasure of
M0/M1 information in C1's COMPLETE carrier-state response. That state operator
and its source-authorized context maps remain the first missing typed objects.
No additional abstract acceptance ladder or waiting-only successor is created.

## Issue-boundary DPC

1. **Problem:** the general common-law claim had no instantiated source operator;
   arbitrary matrix countermodels alone could not falsify its source realization.
2. **Bold conjecture under test:** the source's geometric arrows are generated
   by one uniform translation per primitive type, trained once and reused.
3. **Named rivals:** independent arrow translations; an injective common point
   map; a common affine collapse; a full-state law not represented by positions.
4. **Risky consequences:** held-out primitive endpoints must agree exactly;
   residuals must transport under a common invertible affine coordinate change;
   no coordinate origin or hexagonal presentation change may remove the mismatch.
5. **Strongest test:** hold out electron M1->C1, compare with proton-trained B,
   retain the complete nonzero vector residual and metric defect. Test the
   minimal two-arrow witness, an affine collapse control, and honest chart
   transport. A translation-model pass in spite of inconsistent B displacements
   would falsify the checker.
6. **Disposition criterion:** a successful falsification harness must label the
   translation and injective-point candidates REJECTED, not report them as having
   survived because the checker exits zero. The unrestricted full-state conjecture
