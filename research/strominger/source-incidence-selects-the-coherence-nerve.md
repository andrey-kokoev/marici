# Source incidence selects the coherence nerve

## Replacement for a universal tower arithmetic

The next coherence boundary is not determined by the number `3+2+1`.
It is generated by the incidence grammar of the source constructors.

Three primitive incidence signatures suffice for the current packet.

| Constructor signature | Generated nerve | Next boundary |
|---|---|---|
| one source and one target comparison | globular | two parallel faces |
| an ordered composable chain with omissions | simplicial | one face per omission |
| independent binary axes with interchange | cubical | two faces per axis |

The obstruction problem begins only after this nerve has been selected.

## Current sector classification

### Magnetic chart comparison

The preferred and alternate minors are two presentations of one invariant
subspace.  A comparison between the two complete presentations is globular:
two endpoints and one chart-transition witness.  The cocircuit itself is a
presentation defect, not a higher horn.

### Magnetic reflection coherence

Repeated composition of the reflection action is computed by the group bar
construction.  Its natural nerve is simplicial.  The classes
\(H^n(C_2,A)\) obstruct fillers of simplicial cocycles, not globular duplicate
packets.

### RH source/composition/reciprocity packet

Source realization, forward composition, and reciprocal transport are three
independent binary directions.  Their pairwise interchanges are square faces.
The next obligation is a cube with six square faces.  This agrees with the
existing source--composition--reciprocity interchange calculation.

### Cutoff and completion descent

Chains of cutoff inclusions and their omission maps form a simplicial descent
nerve.  A completion anomaly is therefore not automatically the same kind of
cell as the RH interchange cube, even when both first appear at degree three.

## Mixed packet

The full research object is consequently neither one globular category, one
simplicial set, nor one cubical set.  It is a typed computad or pasting scheme
whose generating cells carry their own incidence shapes.  Cross-shape
comparisons require declared functors between nerves; no authority follows
from the fact that their dimensions match.

This explains why several earlier numerical tower formulas felt predictive
but failed under composition: they counted roles while forgetting face
incidence.

## Constructive compiler rule

For every constructor, record:

```text
constructor_id
arity
variance
ordered_inputs
independent_axes
face_maps
degeneracies
source_authority
coefficient_system
```

Then apply:

1. derive the nerve shape from the declared incidence;
2. enumerate its complete boundary faces;
3. verify that every face is source-authorized and well typed;
4. compute the boundary residual;
5. request a filler only when that residual is a cocycle;
6. classify inequivalent fillers by the appropriate obstruction group.

The decisive rejection is `nerve_shape_inferred_from_tower_count`: two source
packets may have the same `3+2+1` inventory and different next horns.

## New prediction

For the present RH three-direction packet, a two-boundary globular test is
incomplete.  It can compare the two total routes but cannot localize which of
the six square faces carries the anomaly.  The minimal diagnostic packet must
retain all six face residuals plus the cube residual, with Koszul parity typed
before declaring a central sign anomalous.
