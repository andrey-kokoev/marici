# The Involutive Tail Lattice Removes the Gram Form but Needs a Dual Grade

The candidate root-norm explanation used an undeclared coordinate Gram form.
That metric is unnecessary.

Let the endpoint reversal exchange the two tail ports.  Its primitive integral
eigenlattices are

\[
L^+=\mathbb Z(1,1),
\qquad
L^-=\mathbb Z(1,-1).
\]

Their sum has index two in the labelled tail lattice:

\[
[\mathbb Z^2:L^+\oplus L^-]=2.
\]

This index is intrinsic to the integral involution.  It disappears after
adjoining \(1/2\), exactly as an integral parity defect should.

For every C presentation, division by the uniform matrix content \(768\) gives

\[
\frac{|\partial_C|}{768}=2\kappa_C^2.
\]

Hence the numerical structure is exactly

\[
\text{eigenlattice splitting defect}
\times
\text{degree on }L^-
\times
\text{degree on }(L^-)^*.
\]

This explains the square without two independent physical channels and without
a metric: one conductor acts covariantly and contravariantly.

The remaining authority gap is categorical.  The current packet identifies the
anti-invariant tail line, but it does not identify the snake target with its
integral dual or prove that the conductor acts with the same degree on both
variances.  The smallest missing constructor is therefore a source-derived
duality cell

\[
K_{\mathrm{snake}}^{\mathrm{target}}\simeq(L^-)^*
\]

compatible with the full-to-relational boundary map.

Confirmation would turn the affine conductor square into an involutive-lattice
discriminant theorem.  Refutation would show that the matching square is only a
numerical coincidence between unrelated covariant and contravariant grades.
