# Truncated exterior mass retains n-minus-two scaling through degree fourteen

## Question

Does direct evaluation of the variational upper bound falsify \(O(n^{-2})\) exterior mass?

Using 420-digit incomplete-gamma moments, the degree-\(n\) monic minimizer on \([X,\infty)\) was normalized and continued over \([0,X]\), with \(X=\log12\). For \(n=8,\ldots,14\),

\[
n^2\int_0^X|Q_n^{\geq X}(x)|^2d\nu(x)
\]

increases slowly from \(0.11184\) to \(0.11854\), while the unscaled exterior mass decreases monotonically.

## Disposition

Complete the direct finite exterior-mass diagnostic. It supports the \(n^{-2}\) variational upper bound and is consistent with leverage being strictly smaller than that bound.

The next leaf is `truncated-exterior-recurrence-bound`: derive compact continuation estimates from the truncated Jacobi recurrence rather than high-precision Gram--Schmidt.

## Claim boundary

The finite grid supplies no uniform degree bound or limiting coefficient.
