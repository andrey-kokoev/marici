# Quarter cross limit excludes rationals below denominator 7914

## Problem

After rejecting \(96/443\), the mature extrapolation interval required an unbiased rational census.

## Bold conjecture

No reduced rational with denominator below several thousand lies in the mature degree-forty-eight interval.

## Named rivals

The rivals are a lower-denominator rational missed by heuristic approximation, a high-denominator compatible rational, and a nonrational limit.

## Risky consequences

Exhaustive enumeration must identify the first compatible reduced fraction and verify that its denominator exceeds 443.

## Strongest falsification attempt

Every reduced rational with denominator below 7914 is excluded from

\[
[0.216704520832,0.216704588098].
\]

The first compatible fraction is

\[
\frac{1715}{7914}=0.216704574172\ldots.
\]

All six gates passed.

## Disposition

Exclude rational candidates with denominator below 7914, conditional on the mature inverse-power interval. Do not promote \(1715/7914\): it is the first enumerated interval hit and has no source derivation. The next leaf is `quarter-cross-limit-degree-sixty-stress-test`, which can move or narrow the interval independently of rational selection.

## Claim boundary

The denominator bound is conditional on a model-derived interval, not a proof of irrationality. It says nothing about rationals outside that interval if the asymptotic model changes.
