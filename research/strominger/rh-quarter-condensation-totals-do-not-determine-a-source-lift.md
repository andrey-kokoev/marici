# Quarter condensation totals do not determine a source lift

## Question

Do adjacent-degree condensation marginals canonically determine their configuration-level coupling?

## Claim boundary

The result proves nonidentifiability from binary partition totals. It does not show that no source-derived deletion or interlacing map exists once configurations are supplied.

## Disposition

For every 234 tested adjacent-degree pairs, the nested and independent couplings are both nonnegative exact joint distributions with the same marginals \(\Theta_{n,a}\) and \(\Theta_{n+1,a}\), but they are distinct. Hence partition totals do not select a canonical coupling. A source lift requires additional configuration objects and a deletion/interlacing relation; neither is encoded by the condensation recurrence. This branch stops at that missing constructor. The next independent leaf is `quarter-cross-ratio-hankel-condensation-recurrence`, seeking an all-order sign induction directly among Hankel minors rather than through an underdetermined source coupling.
