# Quarter cross ratios admit positive finite Jacobi recurrences

## Question

Can the positive finite moment data be organized into exact orthogonal-polynomial recurrences?

## Claim boundary

The construction covers Jacobi orders zero through four and shifts zero through four. It does not prove an infinite representing measure or locate the support of a limiting measure.

## Disposition

Exact Gram–Schmidt gives positive polynomial norms, recurrence coefficients \(\beta_j>0\), diagonal coefficients \(0<\alpha_j<1\), exact orthogonality, and exact three-term recurrences in all 25 tested cases. Thus the finite moment data have a positive Jacobi/Stieltjes representation compatible with support in \([0,1]\). The next leaf is `quarter-cross-ratio-jacobi-tail-universality`, extending the exact recurrence order and testing whether \(\alpha_j\) and \(\beta_j\) approach the regular \([0,1]\) limits \(1/2\) and \(1/16\) uniformly across shifts.
