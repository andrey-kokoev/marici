# Endpoint Gram data do not identify Hall coupling

## Question

Can separate positive endpoint Gram certificates supply the missing source-derived interlacing Hall coupling?

## Claim boundary

No. Equal positive endpoint weights `(1,1)` have the same diagonal Gram data and margins for both the identity and swap couplings. On the two-edge interlacing graph containing only the diagonal edges, the identity coupling is supported while the swap coupling is not. Endpoint positivity therefore cannot identify joint edge capacities or certify interlacing support. This finite countermodel disposes the universal inference from separate endpoint Gram certificates to Hall coupling; it does not refute a Gram construction equipped with an additional source-derived joint map.

## Disposition

Reject endpoint Gram positivity as a standalone all-order Hall mechanism. The exact residual is the previously identified typed object: a source-derived joint map from signed source terms to Gram coordinates and interlacing-edge capacities. The positive-real/Gram rival is blocked at that interface, not rescued by further endpoint matrix censuses.
