# Translation/combination coherence and retained coefficient frames

## Commuting-square test

Enumerated native retained-map configurations through three additions for all
24 seed markings. For each of the 12 elementary lattice translations, compared:

```
combine windows -> translate the whole decorated collection
translate windows -> combine the translated collection.
```

The comparison includes actual contact admission, C/M roles, origin and aliases,
all five symbolic root-coefficient maps, contact witnesses, and exit predicates.

- 5,568 retained configuration squares pass.
- 44,736 candidate extension squares pass.
- 94,560 symbolic coefficient entries agree.
- 768 compared configurations have certified native exits.
- 1,632 candidate extensions are rejected on both sides for occupied-cell
  species conflicts. Translation does not make incompatible combinations valid.

Collections here are the native retained construction maps. They are not
simultaneously cloned physical states or independent copies of a quantum field.

## Native path coherence is not endpoint independence

A separate depth-eight path search found 576 marked-chart/absolute-origin
endpoints with multiple coefficient permutations, summed across the 24 seeds.
They are distinct full packets even though their geometry, marking and origin
agree. This is why the coefficient frame must remain part of the state.

A concrete root-marking example reaches the same P chart at origin 3 by:

```
reader, reader, reader

PN, N-reader, inverse-PN, P-reader, PN, N-reader, inverse-PN.
```

The resulting coefficient permutations are respectively

```
[2,3,0,1,4]
[3,4,2,0,1].
```

Their relative map is `[1,4,0,2,3]`, an order-five permutation. Both retained
path collections pass geometric/role admission and have all incidental contacts
witnessed. They are open paths without an electron exit, not certified completed
assemblies. The receipt also retains other examples, some with pending contacts;
those are not presented as fully contact-witnessed combinations.

The relative map is an algebraic comparison of two forward paths, not an
admitted reverse-time physical loop. Its order five is a coefficient-frame
property, not evidence for a fivefold spatial vortex.

## Consequence

Translation and admissible combination commute in this bounded native test.
But geometry plus realization origin alone does not determine the transported
coefficients. Coherence must mean consistent composition **with the coefficient
frame/history retained**, not equality of all path results.

The phase-dependent five-to-field binding alone therefore cannot be assumed
to settle full path sewing. A field connection must reproduce these relative
coefficient actions (or supply a justified interpretation of their frame changes).
The 36-amplitude PN/NE field maps are not yet derived, so this test does not
certify a globally sewn PNE field or arbitrary window combinations.
