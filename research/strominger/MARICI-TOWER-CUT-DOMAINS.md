# Marici tower-cut domains

## Question and governing conjecture

> Any known physical law's domain is represented by a cut in an admissible representation of the Marici tower.

**Operator clarification (revision 6): cuts may be taken in any representation of the same tower.** No formed triangle, FCC cell, tetrahedron or particular record format is required. Those constructions remain useful scoped examples and optional stronger claims, not universal prerequisites.

The current quantifier is: for each intended law-domain, some admissible representation and cut realize it, preserving its required structure. “Any representation” removes the privileged presentation family; it does not assert that every representation must contain every domain or that every cut is physical.

Admissibility is **not yet frozen**: specify what tower structure a representation preserves, which cut operations are allowed, and which domain comparisons preserve the needed operations, constraints and observations. An arbitrary encoding does not establish that it is a tower representation. Where a representation equivalence preserves the chosen cut operation and required structure, corresponding cuts should represent equivalent domains; no unconditional transport theorem is claimed.

This is the operator's proposed organizing principle. The tower is sequential serialization/deserialization of a WG-generated system. QM can be present throughout; a law is not assigned an exclusive rung. The question is the domain exposed by the cut, not initially relations between different cuts or selection of a unique physical universe.

Executable net: [marici-tower-cut-domain-net.v1.json](contracts/marici-tower-cut-domain-net.v1.json).

## Four proof-attempt ports (introduced in revision 2)

The net now uses the shared [`proof-attempt-outcomes-v1`](../conjecture_net/outcome_reference.py) semantics, documented in [the conjecture-net README](../conjecture_net/README.md#cross-net-proof-outcome-references):

| Port | Attempt | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `++` | Prove | Succeeded |
| `+-` | Prove | Failed |
| `-+` | Disprove (`reject` in the shared API) | Succeeded |
| `--` | Disprove | Failed |

These are not composed signed arrows, tetrahedral vertices or confidence scores. Each node has an exact `scope_id`, an `outcome` and an `attempts` history with intent, scope, evidence receipt and verification level. Only `++` and `-+` settle that proposition. `+-` and `--` leave truth unresolved. `null` means no concluded local attempt; a partial result or an unexecuted test is not silently classified as failure.

Current examples:

- Native-feedback elastic **observer/action binding: `++`**, conditional on the declared calibration and masses, not a whole-domain theorem.
- Equality of AB and AD;DB full mechanical effects: **`-+`**. The legacy node ID names the obstruction, but its tested proposition now explicitly states the equality being refuted.
- Old four-coefficient cut's prepared-noise completeness: **`-+`**.
- Distinct v2 preparation-bearing source recovery: **`++`**. This does not overwrite the old failed candidate.
- Governing all-domain and full physical-face claims: **`null`, still open**. Component outcomes do not propagate to them.

Previously inspected results keep `recorded` workflow status with null local outcomes. They remain useful prior evidence, not silently accepted cross-net proofs. A proof import requires the shared pinned outcome-reference protocol. Shared `evidence_group` values prevent treating the interface and oscillator applications of the same receipt as independent evidence. No failed scientific attempts were fabricated from runtime errors, unattempted goals or out-of-scope probes.

The validator imports the shared `PORTS`/`SEMANTICS`, checks receipt/source integrity, rejects scope and intent mismatches and keeps proof outcomes separate from research-order links. The validation result exposes each claim's outcome and truth disposition; it is not itself a mathematical proof. New unit tests cover all four ports and retention of failed-attempt history.

Do not invent three vertices by naming three ingredients. The binding must specify actual source operations, incidences, retained data and reconstruction/representation maps.

Their tests and failure conditions are recorded once in the contract.
