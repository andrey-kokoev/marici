# BMS returning-pulse endpoint kernel

## Question

Do finite news energy and a no-magnetic condition imposed only at the two endpoints remove the low magnetic BMS kernel?

## Claim boundary

Take `f(u)=u(1-u)` on `[0,1]` and zero outside. Its news is `N(u)=1-2u` on the interior, with exact normalized energy `1/3`; both endpoint shears and endpoint memory vanish, while the midpoint shear factor is `1/4`. Tensoring this fixed profile with normalized magnetic spin-two harmonics for `l=2,3,4` gives dimensions `5+7+9=21`. Hence endpoint memory and endpoint no-magnetic conditions have a 21-dimensional finite-energy kernel for this single profile, while time-resolved shear or news-energy readout detects it. The full endpoint kernel is larger. This does not establish a source-derived constructor restriction.

## Disposition

Reject finite energy plus endpoint-only magnetic conditions as a no-go mechanism. A valid exclusion must impose the magnetic restriction throughout the radiative history or specify a time-resolved readout that detects returning pulses, together with a source-authorized constructor restriction. Next test: distinguish all-u magnetic exclusion from endpoint readout by an explicit readout map and state its kernel on the finite-energy radiative space.
