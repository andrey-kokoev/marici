# Quarter cross ratios have an exact finite Motzkin representation

## Question

Can the verified finite Jacobi data reconstruct the cross-ratio moments as positive path sums?

## Claim boundary

The representation reconstructs moments zero through ten for shifts zero through four. It does not prove positivity of Jacobi coefficients or moment representation at all orders.

## Disposition

For each tested shift, the exact Jacobi matrix satisfies

\[
m_k=m_0(J^k)_{0,0},
\qquad 0\leq k\leq10.
\]

Every Motzkin level weight \(\alpha_j\) and down weight \(\beta_j\) is positive, every level weight is below one, and all moments reconstruct exactly. Thus the finite moment evidence has an exact positive path representation rather than only determinant-sign tests. The next leaf is `quarter-cross-ratio-hankel-planar-network`, seeking an all-order network for the Hankel matrices that would prove Jacobi positivity without extending expensive exact Gram–Schmidt calculations.
