# A complete sector reader atlas, its duality, and the missing inter-plane command

## Standing construction directive

The operator subsequently directed that **local reader rules are verification constraints only, not a construction method**. See [the standing assumption](global-coherence-standing-assumption.md). Construct from global coherence conditions; use the results below to check restrictions of that construction. The earlier proposal to construct by adding an inter-plane native command is superseded; the finite results remain unchanged.

## Result

The local phase pilot now extends to a **complete atlas of native reference packets over the edge-7 symmetric sector**. It covers all **679 whole geometric cells and all 1,372 native primitive tetrahedra**.

The geometric involution also has a checked lift to this atlas's **five-slot phase-reference states and commands**. It preserves physical species, coefficient-slot order, absolute origin and transported histories while exchanging geometric P/E orientation classes. This closes a reference-level duality gate, not physical charge conjugation.

However, the atlas is **not one simultaneously occupied assembly**, nor one component reachable from the canonical root by the current native commands. That root reaches only **176 whole cells / 284 primitive supports** in the frozen finite graph. Every compatible native command preserves a spatial M-reference plane. The geometric dual exchanges the root's plane with a neighboring plane but is not itself a native advance command.

**35 finite checks and two aggregate SCC route cells pass.** No viewer, physical loadout or stability claim is changed.

## 2. Why this atlas is not a particle inventory

Across the independent atlas:

- 448 regular primitive supports occur as either P or E in different packets;
- 896 primitive supports occur as either N or E;
- 28 occur only as E under this bounded constructor/domain.

These are alternative readings, not simultaneous occupants. Summing occurrences, counting one species per host, or merging identical coordinates would change the object.

A separate occupancy and reference-selection constructor must decide which packets coexist and supply every actual contact witness. The enumeration has not made that choice.

## 3. A spatial C/M family field is constructible—but is not an absolute phase field

The canonical root markings extend to the field with family C exactly when

\[
q_0+q_2\equiv1\pmod 2,
\]

with M otherwise. The parity is normalized also for negative coordinates.

Every one of the 1,372 native primitives has exactly two C vertices and two M vertices under this field. Independently, the checker starts with only the canonical root's four family labels and propagates the MMCC constraints. Each inference retains its primitive and previously known antecedents.

All **344 vertex families are forced**, and they agree with the parity formula. This is a finite uniqueness result under the stronger requirement that **every native primitive**, including reference-only geometry, be MMCC. It does not assign absolute occurrence indices, identify histories, or prove that every primitive is physically occupied.

Of the 9,408 internal packets, **1,600** are compatible with this single family field. Their atlas covers **560 whole cells / 896 primitive supports**, leaving **119 octahedra** unrepresented. Those 119 hosts have no boundary triangle with the two M vertices required for a PN entry under this field.

The compatible family is also checked to be invariant under every admitted outgoing command in the full finite reader graph. Thus the canonical-root reachability result below does not depend on silently importing incompatible packet families along a path.

## 5. The M-reference-plane invariant

For each family-compatible packet, all three M references satisfy

\[
h=q_0+q_2.
\]

All three have the same value of \(h\). Every admitted native operation edge preserves that value. The canonical root has \(h=0\).

The compatible independent atlas has these spatial sectors:

The root reaches 176 of the 180 available hosts in its own plane. Four additional same-plane hosts are not reachable in this finite internal-packet graph; the plane invariant alone does not explain those four omissions.

The full coverage gap therefore separates into:

- 119 hosts outside the root-compatible family atlas;
- 380 compatible hosts in other M-reference planes;
- four further same-plane hosts outside the root's reachable component.

The plane label is a spatial property of this fixed subdivision and marking. It is **not** identified with a realization-cycle index or a proof of the proposed two-cycle coherence horizon.

## 6. A checked reference-level lift of geometric duality

Use the existing involution

\[
D(q)=2c-q,\qquad c=(1/2,1/2,1/2,-1/2).
\]

For each of the 9,408 internal packets, the checker finds the actual packet produced by the native constructor with all four seed bindings transformed by \(D\). It then checks:

- all six resulting geometric bindings;
- all three primitive carrier images;
- physical species unchanged;
- source PN/NE faces and new contexts;
- ordered five-slot permutations and source mode records;
- retained packet histories;
- wrapped images under \(FDF^{-1}\).

Every one of the **42,016 native command edges** maps to an actual edge of the same kind, origin increment and coefficient permutation. The map is involutive on packets and command identities, hence on complete command words without history erasure. SCC verifies the evaluated command/duality comparison and the command-word involution.

This is a lift at the **five-slot reference-protocol level**. It is not a claimed action on every 36-component physical-field model or a derived charge-conjugation law.

Crucially, physical species are **not exchanged**. A proton reader maps to another proton reader in geometrically inverted support, not to a terminal electron reader. This is why the earlier warning about a species-exchanging reader duality does not block the present, differently typed construction.

On the M-reference plane,

\[
h\longmapsto2-h.
\]

The canonical root's \(h=0\) component is compared with its \(h=2\) dual. But its dual root is **not reachable by the original native command graph**. An explicit reference symmetry is not automatically an additional evolution or assembly command.

Nor is the dual copy an independently rooted physical witness: it is constructed from the same source data. This calculation does not establish a no-self-witnessing horizon.

## 7. Dense occupancy fails even before the plane question

For a marked MMCC regular tetrahedron, exactly two of its four faces have two M vertices. The existing direct P-contact grammar requires PN across such a face; P-P and P-E do not repair the other two contacts.

The checker enumerates all 24 canonical-root markings. The root has four actual neighboring primitive supports in the sector, but **every marking leaves two fully occupied faces unwitnessable** under the current grammar.

Thus “occupy every primitive and then attach phase labels” is rejected by a local contact obstruction. This does not reject configurations with unoccupied/reference-only neighbors, nor new source-authorized contact rules. It explains why atlas coverage cannot replace occupancy selection.

## 8. What remains—and what is not a repair

We now have complete local reference coverage and an explicit dual comparison. The missing scientific constructor is a compatible **relative attachment/occupation rule**, including any intended active inter-plane handoff and every occupied contact witness.

It cannot be replaced by:

- selecting whichever independent packet has a desired label;
- interpreting the 176 reachable hosts as ESD7's 175 cells plus one special record;
- calling a coordinate symmetry a native phase advance;
- assigning a phase index from spatial height;
- choosing a wrap or removing records to obtain 82/126/82.

The prior [rigid-field covariance result](arbitrary-rigid-field-transport-v1.md) explicitly translates the same object **and its chart**. It does not move one reader through fixed occupancy or supply attachment to a fixed exterior. Passive translation is therefore not a demonstrated repair of the missing relative handoff.

Changing octahedral diagonals would also change the source primitive supports. Existing [fixed-root atlas research](octahedral-reader-atlas-result-v1.md) records both subdivision-sensitive obstructions and prior mixed-subdivision attempts. Such a change needs an explicit transported comparison; it is not an untried free adjustment to force the counts.

No stable Pb-208 loadout follows from this result. The current source grammar exposes a precise missing operation rather than a numerical shortfall to patch.
