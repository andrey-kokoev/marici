# The magnetic seven is lattice content, not reflection coherence

## Exact origin

On the preferred even magnetic locus, the two dependent observations satisfy

\[
(2g+7)R_1=(3g+7)R_0.
\]

The unsaturated relation vector is therefore

\[
v_g=(2g+7,-(3g+7)).
\]

Its integral content is

\[
\operatorname{cont}(v_g)
=\gcd(2g+7,3g+7)
=\gcd(g,7).
\]

The equality follows from the unimodular combinations

\[
(3g+7)-(2g+7)=g,
\]

and

\[
3(2g+7)-2(3g+7)=7.
\]

Thus the prime seven is the determinant of the affine coefficient frame

\[
\det\begin{pmatrix}2&7\\3&7\end{pmatrix}=-7,
\]

up to sign.  It measures when the preferred cocircuit ray is nonprimitive in
the integral observation lattice.

## Why this is not another coherence rung

Bare reflection is a \(C_2\) action.  Its positive-degree integral group
cohomology contains only two-primary torsion.  Therefore the seven-primary
content above cannot be a reflection associator or a higher reflection nerve
class.

The two mechanisms have different types:

- reflection coherence: an obstruction to filling compatible compositions,
  classified by a cohomology class;
- cocircuit content: failure of a presentation vector to be primitive,
  classified by Smith saturation in a fixed lattice.

Both can disappear after rationalization, but that common behavior does not
identify them.

## Consequences

1. The occurrence of \(7\mid g\) predicts a nonprimitive preferred
   observation relation, not a new kernel or loss of rank over \(\mathbb Q\).
2. The alternate chart can remain injective while the preferred chart carries
   seven-primary lattice content.
3. Independently saturating the ray removes this content only by changing the
   integral observation lattice; it is not automatically source-authorized.
4. Any categorical compiler must type at least two defect coordinates:
   coherence obstruction and lattice saturation defect.

This supplies a hostile test for the tower language: a framework that reports
the factor seven as a higher coherence cell has conflated presentation
arithmetic with compositional coherence.
