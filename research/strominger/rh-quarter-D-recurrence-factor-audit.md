# D-recurrence factor audit

## Question

Do the tested D-polynomials split into simple quarter-lattice linear factors?

## Claim boundary

Exact rational root extraction across 15 tested polynomials finds a first unfactored residual, rejecting complete quarter-lattice linear splitting. It does not classify the remaining factors.

## Disposition

Reject the simple-factor rival and move to the distinct Routh/interlacing backend under the single governing programme.
