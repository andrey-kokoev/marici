# Quarter order-two pencil is Hurwitz stable for all shifts

## Question

Can the continuous order-two certificate be made uniform in the source shift?

## Claim boundary

The theorem covers the order-two condensation pencil only. It does not establish the corresponding proper-position relation at higher source order.

## Disposition

Keeping the shift \(s\) symbolic, every Routh first-column numerator and denominator for

\[
q(a+s+1)-tq(a+s)
\]

has nonnegative Bernstein coefficients in \(t\in[0,1)\), and every such coefficient is a polynomial in \(s\) with nonnegative monomial coefficients. The degree-dropped \(t=1\) cubic has the same all-shift certificate. Therefore the pencil is Hurwitz stable for every real \(s\geq0\) and every \(t\in[0,1]\). This is an exact two-parameter theorem. The next leaf is `quarter-order-three-hurwitz-bernstein-certificate`, testing whether symbolic Routh–Bernstein positivity survives the first genuinely determinant-coupled condensation pencil.
