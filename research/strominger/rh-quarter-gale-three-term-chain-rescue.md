# Three-term Gale chains do not universally repair adjacent deficits

## Question

Does every nonzero two-cover chain have nonnegative terminal-oriented alternating mass?

## Claim boundary

No. The first case \((i,j)=(0,1)\) has a positive \(+,-,+\) triple, but the next terminal case \((i,j)=(0,2)\) gives the same label chain

\[
\{0\}<\{1\}<\{2\}
\]

with oriented pattern \(-,+,-\) and exact negative three-term residual

Therefore neither edges nor unrestricted three-term chains provide a universal local decomposition. This is compatible with the successful nonlocal Hall flow.

## Disposition

Retain checkerboard parity and zero-extended log-supermodularity as exact finite structure, but reject fixed-radius chain repair. The next bounded locality test should respect the case-positive parity: audit Gale intervals whose minimal and maximal corners both have positive oriented sign. Positive-corner interval sums could provide variable-size blocks; failure would isolate branching Hall neighborhoods as essential.
