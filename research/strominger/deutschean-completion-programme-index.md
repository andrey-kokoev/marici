# Deutschean completion research programme

Programme owner: `marici.Strominger`

Status: active, conjectural at the all-orders nonlinear completion layer.

## Scope

Explain whether the oriented positive cone generated by normal jets at the
memoryless divisor is preserved contractively under Gevrey/Borel--Laplace
completion, with the finite-jet reconstruction defect remaining inside the
available positivity reserve for \(4\leq q\leq10\) and \(0<t\leq1/60\).

The programme does not assert the Riemann hypothesis, a scalar Stieltjes
representation, global Hankel total positivity, or an untyped analytic Taylor
completion.

## Hard core

- The source carrier and its Gamma/Mellin shape transport are fixed before
  projection.
- The memoryless divisor carries the fixed relative-response section
  \(r=1\).
- The margin is the relative-curvature readout of that section.
- Completion is Gevrey-1 and must use an admitted Borel--Laplace direction.
- Coefficient-cone preservation and continuous nonlinear completion control
  are distinct gates.
- Coordinate reversion does not create source authority.

The hard core reopens if the source carrier is corrected, the curvature
identity fails, or the physical completion direction is shown to differ from
the oriented negative Borel ray.
