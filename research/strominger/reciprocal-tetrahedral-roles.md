# Reciprocal coordination tetrahedra: structural marking experiment

## Scope and provenance

Operator requests a structural/control-theoretic construction, with no added
forces. The geometry is the diamond network: two FCC sublattices, each site the
barycenter of four opposite-family nearest neighbors. These are COORDINATION
tetrahedra, not cells of a tetrahedral space-filling tiling. P/N remain proposed
whole-tetrahedron roles, not derived particle identities.

## 1. Geometry alone does not supply two intrinsic species

The involution J(x)=(1,1,1)-x exchanges families. More strongly, the PROPER motion

    g(x)=R_z(90 degrees)x+(1,1,1)

also exchanges them and preserves every adjacency. Thus simply calling the two
regular tetrahedra oppositely oriented does not establish intrinsic chirality,
electric charge or particle identity. Zincblende supplies unlike constituents;
our experiment must not smuggle in that distinction by naming the sublattices.

## 2. A single oriented reference can distinguish reciprocal roles

Choose the common stacking normal n=(1,1,1). For the four outgoing neighbor
vectors d, zeroth moment is4, first moment0, second moment tensor4I for BOTH
families. The third directional moment is

    sum_d (n dot d)^3 = +24 on A, -24 on B.

More generally it is +/-24 n_x n_y n_z. The full third tensor is +/-4 when all
three indices differ and zero otherwise. This is a common, source-defined
readout, not a different fitted measurement for each family. First/second moments
cannot distinguish the stars; the third can relative to an oriented frame.

Reversing n exchanges the score, and some directions give zero. Thus the mark
provides interface polarity, not intrinsic electric charge. No preferred
physical axis or origin has been explained. Under simultaneous transformation
of source and reader this is ordinary covariant geometry.

## 3. The naturally paired spaces are center states and shared-interface states

Let V be diamond centers and E its bonds. Coordination tetrahedron corners lie
on opposite centers. Separately, taking the bond midpoints makes corner-sharing
tetrahedra centered on V: the PYROCHLORE construction. These midpoint tetrahedra
have half the original radius, one quarter the squared edge length. Each
midpoint belongs to two tetrahedra; its line-graph coordination number is6.
Do not identify the original coordination corners with these new shared corners.

For unsigned endpoint incidence C (128 center rows,256 bond columns), verify

    C C^T = 4I+A_diamond,
    C^T C = 2I+A_pyrochlore.

Unit-weight combinatorial Laplacians consequently satisfy

    L_pyrochlore C^T = C^T L_diamond.

The code checks this equality on EVERY original center basis vector. These are
incidence continuation maps, not asserted physical time dynamics or forces.

The symmetric shared-interface reader is R=C^T/2. Crucially,

    R tau=0,  tau=(+1 on A,-1 on B).

Connectedness and x_i+x_j=0 on each bond prove ker(R)=span(tau), so rank(R)=127.
The proposed0/1 role scores q=(1-tau)/2 and1-q BOTH read as1/2 on every bond.
A complete collection of symmetric shared corners still loses precisely the
mode distinguishing the roles. Equal geometry and commuting operations do not
make this a faithful interface for a role-sensitive task. In contrast to the
prior flag-to-vertex compression, this erased mode remains invisible under every
continuation through R, because this map DOES intertwine the operators.

The midpoint space also has256-127=129 independent modes in ker(C), the cycle
space of this connected bipartite periodic graph. They cannot be generated by
lifting only center states. A genuine six-loop with alternating coefficients is
exported and checked: C cycle=0 and L_pyrochlore cycle=8 cycle. This is independent
collective interface information, NOT an identified electron. Counts refer to
this finite quotient, not a universal particle capacity.

## 4. A grading is useful, but conservation is not automatic

The center state space is H_A directsum H_B. The SAME barycentric averaging map
T=A_diamond/4 acts on both roles. Its grading Sigma anticommutes with T:

    T1=1, T tau=-tau, T^2 tau=tau.

One-step continuation flips the role score; two steps preserve it. Declaring only
even steps physical would be an additional rule, not a result about nuclear
processes. Nor does a two-component geometric grading derive SU(2) isospin,
spin1/2, mass splitting or particle charge.

An added boundary counter z_next=z+q(v)-q(u) conserves q+z on each edge, but
this transfer telescopes on EVERY closed path. It is an exact-gradient ledger,
not an independent emitted electron or nontrivial holonomy. A six-loop regression
explicitly checks zero net counter transfer. Real cycle modes above are a
different, independently reconstructible kind of interface state.

## DPC, disposition and nonredundant continuation

Problem: make A/B physically meaningful without importing forces or named
species. Candidate: intrinsic opposite tetrahedral orientation suffices. Rival:
only a marked common interface distinguishes roles. Falsifier: an unmarked
proper motion exchanges the complete source. Outcome: intrinsic-orientation
candidate rejected; a boundary-relative cubic readout is explicitly constructed.
Symmetric center-to-shared-corner encoding is operator-compatible but loses the
very role mode, while independent midpoint cycle modes also exist.

Next source-enabled test: construct a LOSSLESS typed center/shared-interface
packet retaining the staggered role mode and explicitly resolved cycle modes;
compare reconstruction and composition with the lossy scalar midpoint average.
Do not merely append a charge counter and rename it an electron. Only after
identifying a structural invariant and allowed state operations can it be tested
against proton/neutron/electron properties. No existing flag-family frontier or
iteration completion is changed by this separate exploratory branch.
