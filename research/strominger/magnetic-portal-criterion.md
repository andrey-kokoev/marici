# Magnetic portal criterion

A portal is not an extension of coefficients. It is a typed correspondence that
can carry charge between the magnetic source packet and a readout packet.

Let \(R\) be the source ring and let

\[
F:X\longrightarrow Y
\]

be the full magnetic transport map. There are three distinct constructions.

## Transparent lift

For a faithfully flat \(R\)-algebra \(S\), the lifted map is
\(F_S=F\otimes_R S\). Flatness gives

\[
\ker F_S\cong (\ker F)\otimes_R S.
\]

Hence an injective \(F\) remains injective. Covers, invertible scalar spurions,
and presentation changes of this kind cannot activate the rational magnetic
interference fiber.

## Defect portal

A defect portal is restriction through a non-flat \(R\)-module \(P\). Tensoring
the exact sequence

\[
0\longrightarrow X\xrightarrow{F}Y\longrightarrow Q\longrightarrow0
\]

gives the boundary term

\[
\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(P,Q)\longrightarrow P\otimes_R X
\xrightarrow{P\otimes F}P\otimes_RY.
\]

Therefore

\[
\ker(P\otimes F)=
\operatorname{im}\!\left(\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(P,Q)\right).
\]

This is the precise sense in which a portal can create a state that did not
exist in the original kernel.

The minimal exact model takes \(R=\mathbb Z[t]\), \(F\) to be multiplication
by \(t\), and \(P=R/(t)\). The original map is injective, but \(P\otimes F\)
is the zero map. Its new kernel is \(P\), generated by the Tor boundary and
supported exactly on \(t=0\).

## Operator-changing portal

A correspondence may instead change the transport map, add a charged target
quotient, or supply the missing fractional-tail map. Such a constructor is not
base change and is not covered by the flatness obstruction. It must declare
its source authority, charge, support, variance, and readout map independently.

## Magnetic consequence

At \((g,d)=(6,17/3)\), the full three-column magnetic path matrix has the
nonzero maximal minor

\[
\Delta=-\frac{48775302133841920000}{729}.
\]

Diagonal charged spurions replace it by
\(\Delta s_0s_1s_2\). Thus every invertible spurion fiber remains injective.
A zero spurion deletes a route rather than producing interference.

Consequently a magnetic portal capable of activating the missing rational
fiber must be one of the following:

1. a non-flat charged defect portal, whose new class is supported on its defect
   locus and is detected by Tor;
2. an operator-changing charged correspondence with a separately authorized
   target quotient or tail map.

The falsifier is a source-authorized faithfully flat lift of the unchanged
operator that creates a kernel. A second falsifier is a defect portal whose
new kernel survives away from its declared non-flat support.
