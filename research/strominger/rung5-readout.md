# The rung-5 readout, certified: the fold closed form, the character theorem through grade 8, and the alternation of the obstructed sector

Companion to `checkers/rung5_readout_checks.py` (groups Q, F, C, R5, ALT,
19/19, exit 0; results in `results/rung5_readout.json`). This packet
upgrades the rung-5 readout from **prediction** to **certified readout**,
closing the clause left open in `rung4-foldrule.md` §5: rung 5 (tower
order 4) folds at grade 8 (checker R2: every order-4 channel closes at
\((8,-2)\)), but the character theorem of `readout-parity-mechanism.md`
was certified only for \(g=0,\dots,5\). The gap is closed by a closed
form for the grade-\(g\) fold whose engine is a single reciprocity
identity — and the same engine proves the character theorem is not a
finite-range accident but a structural consequence of one lemma.

## 1. The fold closed form (F)

Same conventions as `readout-parity-mechanism.md` §1: anchor datum
\(C=(u+u^{-1})/z^{2}\), \(u=z\bar z\), fold chain
\(D_z(f,s)=\partial_z f-s\,\Gamma f\), \(\Gamma=-2\bar z/(1+u)\), weights
\(s=2,3,\dots,g+1\). The full chain collapses:

\[
\boxed{\;\operatorname{fold}_g(C)
=(1+u)^{-2(g+1)}\,z^{-(g+2)}\,Q_g(u)\;}
\]

where \(Q_g\) is a Laurent polynomial generated by the tridiagonal
recursion

\[
Q_1=(\Theta-2)\,h,\qquad h=(1+u)^{4}\left(u+u^{-1}\right),\qquad
Q_{j+1}=T_{-(j+2)}\,Q_j,
\]
\[
T_e\,\varphi=(1+u)\big[(e+\Theta)+u\,(e+\Theta+2)\big]\varphi,
\qquad \Theta=u\,\partial_u,
\]

acting on monomials as (Q1)

\[
T_e\,u^{m}=(e+m)\,u^{m}+2(e+m+1)\,u^{m+1}+(e+m+2)\,u^{m+2}.
\]

Certification: the base \(g=1\) against \(D_z(C,2)\) (F1), the one-step
recursion \(D_z(\operatorname{cf}_g,\,2+g)=\operatorname{cf}_{g+1}\) for
\(g=1..7\) (F2) — together certifying \(\operatorname{cf}_g=
\operatorname{fold}_g\) for every \(g=1..8\) — and the direct full-chain
anchor \(g=1..5\) against the definition used in the previously certified
checkers (F3).

## 2. The reciprocity lemma — the engine of both characters (Q)

\[
\boxed{\;Q_g(u)=(-1)^{g}\,u^{2(g+1)}\,Q_g(1/u)\;}
\]

certified exactly for \(g=1..12\) (Q3) — well beyond the rung-5 grade 8.

*Proof (hand).* Base \(g=1\): \(h(1/u)=u^{-4}h(u)\) (Q2), and
\((\Theta-2)\) preserves the reciprocity class with pivot \(2\), so
\(Q_1(1/u)=-u^{-4}Q_1(u)\). Induction: suppose
\(Q(1/u)=\rho\,u^{-2(g+1)}Q(u)\), \(\rho=(-1)^{g}\). For
\(\varphi=u^{m}\), reciprocity about the pivot \(g+1\) means the
coefficient at exponent \(m\) equals \(\rho\) times that at
\(2(g+1)-m\). The tridiagonal action of \(T_{-(g+2)}\) maps the triple
\((m-2,m-1,m)\) around exponent \(m\) to coefficients around \(m+1\);
substituting the inductive symmetry and using that the three multipliers
\((e+m),(e+m+1),(e+m+2)\) at \(e=-(g+2)\) are antisymmetric about
\(m=-(g+1)\), the image satisfies reciprocity about the pivot \(g+2\)
with sign \(-\rho=(-1)^{g+1}\). The algebra closes exactly. \(\square\)

The support law (Q4): \(\operatorname{supp}Q_g=[-1,\,2g+3]\) with both
endpoints attained — the Laurent width \(2g+5\) grows linearly while the
reciprocity pivot sits at exponent \(g+1\). (Another instance of the
support-law theme of `rung4-foldrule.md` §S.)

*Corollaries.* With
\(W_g=(1+u)\Theta Q_g-2(g+1)u\,Q_g\) and
\(\widetilde R_g=u^{-1}(1+u)^{-2g-3}W_g\):

\[
A_g=\partial_{\bar z}\operatorname{fold}_g
=z^{-(g+1)}\,\widetilde R_g(u),\qquad
W_g(1/u)=(-1)^{g+1}u^{-(2g+3)}W_g,
\]
\[
\widetilde R_g(1/u)=(-1)^{g+1}u^{2}\,\widetilde R_g(u).
\]

The key identity is
\((1+u)\big(2(g+1)Q_g-\Theta Q_g\big)-2(g+1)Q_g=-W_g\), which holds for
both parities and transports the \(Q_g\)-reciprocity to
\(\widetilde R_g\) (certified within C; \(A_g\) closed form verified
against direct differentiation).

## 3. The character theorem through grade 8 (C)

From the corollaries both characters follow by pure algebra:

\[
P(\operatorname{fold}_g)=u^{g+2}\,\sigma(\operatorname{fold}_g)
\;\Longrightarrow\;
P(E_g)=+u^{g+2}E_g,
\]
\[
P(M_g)=u^{2}\left(z^{g+1}-\bar z^{g+1}\right)\widetilde R_g
=-u^{g+3}M_g .
\]

Certified exactly for \(g=0..8\) (C1, C2; \(g=0\) direct, \(g\ge1\) via
the closed form) — extending the range \(g=0..5\) of
`readout-parity-mechanism.md` §3 to the rung-5 grade. The monomial
closure \(z^{g+1}A_g=\bar z^{g+1}B_g\) (C3) is now *automatic*:
\(z^{g+1}A_g=\widetilde R_g(u)\) is \(\sigma\)-invariant. The diagonal
weight \(P(A_g)=z^{2g+4}\bar z^{2}A_g\) holds through \(g=8\) (C4). A
witness regression ties the new engine to the old computation where both
apply: the closed-form readouts at \(g=4\) reproduce the certified
values \(M_4|_{W2}=38822265502889/1443587184\),
\(E_4|_{W2}=1606143346495/28789488\) (C5).

## 4. The rung-5 verdict (R5)

Rung 5 = tower order 4 folds at grade \(g=8\) (`rung4-foldrule.md` R2,
certified). Under the arc's declared identification fold grade = readout
grade (same status as rungs 3 and 4):

- **Electric sector PASSES the square-root gate** (R5.1):
  \(P(E_8)=+u^{10}E_8\), even exponent, root \(u^{5}\)
  \(\sigma\)-invariant.
- **Magnetic sector FAILS the square-root gate** (R5.2):
  \(P(M_8)=-u^{11}M_8\), odd exponent — no square root of \(-u^{11}\)
  exists in \(\mathbb{Q}(u)\).
- Closed-form obstruction (R5.3):
  \[
  P(M_8)-M_8=-(1+u^{11})\,M_8
  \]
  exactly; the factor \(1+u^{11}\) is a nonzero rational function, so
  the readout can never be \(P\)-invariant.
- Unrepairable by dressing (R5.4): a cocycle root \(F\) would need
  \(F\,\sigma(F)=-u^{11}\) of odd diagonal valuation 11, but the
  intrinsic-oddness lemma
  \(\operatorname{val}_u(h\,\sigma(h))=2\operatorname{val}_u(h)\)
  (`rung3-cocycle.md` K4) forces every such product to even valuation.

The square-root obstruction at rung 5 sits on the **magnetic** sector.

## 5. The alternation theorem (ALT)

Rung \(n+1\) (tower order \(n\)) folds at grade

\[
g_n=n+2\left\lceil n/2\right\rceil ,
\]

the \(p=n\) instance of the closure law (`rung4-foldrule.md` §5), a
theorem for all \(n\) via the universal channel closed form
(`channel-closedform.md`). Since \(2\lceil n/2\rceil\) is even,

\[
\operatorname{parity}(g_n)=\operatorname{parity}(n),
\]

so at every rung **exactly one** of the exponents \(g_n+2\) (electric),
\(g_n+3\) (magnetic) is odd: exactly one sector is square-root
obstructed at every rung, electric iff \(n\) is odd. The grounded ladder
instantiates the alternation (ALT3):

| rung | order \(n\) | grade \(g_n\) | obstructed sector | character |
|------|-----|-----|------|------|
| 2 | 1 | 3 | electric | \(P(E_3)=+u^{5}\) |
| 3 | 2 | 4 | magnetic | \(P(M_4)=-u^{7}\) |
| 4 | 3 | 7 | electric | \(P(E_7)=+u^{9}\) |
| 5 | 4 | 8 | magnetic | \(P(M_8)=-u^{11}\) |

The obstruction alternates E, M, E, M along the ladder — a cross-sector
selection rule forced by the fold grade, not by any convention.

## 6. What is proved, and what is not

**Proved:** the fold closed form and its reciprocity engine (Q, F); the
character theorem, the monomial closure, and the diagonal weight through
grade 8 (C); the rung-5 gate verdict with closed-form obstruction and
unrepairability (R5); the alternation of the obstructed sector as a
theorem of the grade law (ALT).

**Not proved (labeled):** the identification fold grade = readout grade
remains the arc's declared identification (same status as rungs 3 and 4
— the per-rung prescription fixed by closure, not an independent
grounding). The rung-5 *operator* itself is not yet grounded; this
packet certifies the readout at the derived grade.
