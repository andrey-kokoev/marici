# Reflection coherence depth is coefficient-sensitive

## Return from the universal hostile family

The unbounded \(\mathbb F_2\) example proves that no source-independent finite
coherence cap exists.  It does not prove that the magnetic reflection source
has a nonzero obstruction in every degree.

For reflection, the relevant symmetry is

\[
C_2=\langle s\mid s^2=1\rangle.
\]

Its periodic resolution alternates the two source operators

\[
T=s-1,\qquad N=1+s.
\]

Thus the obstruction tower is already controlled by the same invariant and
anti-invariant splitting that appears in the magnetic readout.

## Integral coefficients

For the trivial integral module \(\mathbb Z_+\), one has \(T=0\) and \(N=2\).
Consequently

\[
H^{2j}(C_2,\mathbb Z_+)\cong\mathbb Z/2\quad(j\geq1),
\qquad
H^{2j+1}(C_2,\mathbb Z_+)=0.
\]

For the sign module \(\mathbb Z_-\), one has \(T=-2\) and \(N=0\), giving

\[
H^{2j}(C_2,\mathbb Z_-)=0\quad(j\geq1),
\qquad
H^{2j+1}(C_2,\mathbb Z_-)\cong\mathbb Z/2.
\]

The possible higher obstruction therefore alternates with reflection parity.
It is not predicted by the number of state towers; it is predicted by the
coefficient module and the two maps \(T,N\).

## After inverting two

Over any coefficient ring in which \(2\) is invertible, averaging supplies a
contracting operator.  Both parity modules have

\[
H^n(C_2,A)=0\qquad(n>0).
\]

Hence the reflection-generated coherence tower terminates after rational or
complex localization.  Any surviving higher obstruction there must come from
additional source structure: domains, support, completion, incidence, or a
different symmetry action.  It cannot be attributed to reflection alone.

## Magnetic prediction

The magnetic source now yields a concrete separation.

1. Over \(\mathbb Q\) or \(\mathbb C\), reflection coherence has no higher
   group-cohomological obstruction.
2. Over the unsaturated Laurent lattice, even- and odd-parity packets may carry
   alternating \(2\)-torsion obstruction classes.
3. A rationally coherent comparison that fails integrally should first be
   tested against the relevant \(T/N\) quotient.
4. Odd-prime torsion, factorial index, or the projective diagnostic \(7\)
   cannot be explained by bare \(C_2\) coherence and therefore identifies
   extra arithmetic incidence rather than another reflection rung.

This is falsifiable.  A bare reflection module over a ring with \(2^{-1}\)
carrying a nonzero higher group-cohomology class would refute the calculation.
Conversely, an integral reflection anomaly of odd-primary order cannot be
assigned to this tower without additional source action.

## Shift in perspective

The coherence depth is not a geometric dimension secretly encoded by `3+1`.
It is the homological dimension seen by a particular source action on a
particular coefficient system.  Changing coefficients can collapse an
apparently infinite tower without changing the three lower state axes.
