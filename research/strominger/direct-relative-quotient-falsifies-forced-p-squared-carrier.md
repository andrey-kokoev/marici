# A direct relative quotient falsifies the forced p-squared carrier

## Prediction under attack

The strong prediction was:

> If reflection moves an index-\(p\) chart to a transverse chart, it forces a
> \(p^2\)-class stable packet before a \(p\)-class relative sector can be
> formed.

The pullback--pushout theorem canonically constructs the \(p^2\)-class joint
packet. The claim that every adequate relative carrier must pass through it is
false.

## Direct hostile construction

Let

\[
L=\ker\ell\subset\Lambda=\mathbb Z^2,
\qquad
\ell:\Lambda\longrightarrow\mathbb F_p,
\]

and let \(X\) be an involution. The reflected chart is detected by

\[
\ell_X=\ell\circ X.
\]

Define the direct odd and even functionals

\[
q_- = \ell-\ell_X,
\qquad
q_+ = \ell+\ell_X.
\]

They obey

\[
q_-(Xv)=-q_-(v),
\qquad
q_+(Xv)=q_+(v).
\]

If \(p\) is odd and the chart lines are transverse, \(q_-\) is nonzero and
hence surjective. Its kernel is an \(X\)-stable index-\(p\) lattice. Therefore

\[
\Lambda/\ker q_-\cong\mathbb Z/p
\]

is already a complete reflection-odd carrier for the selected relative
observable. It has \(p\), not \(p^2\), classes.

## Magnetic instance

The one-chart discriminant functional is

\[
\ell(x,y)=3x-2y\pmod7.
\]

After exchange,

\[
\ell_X(x,y)=3y-2x.
\]

Thus

\[
q_-(x,y)=5(x-y),
\qquad
q_+(x,y)=x+y
\pmod7.
\]

Since five is invertible modulo seven, the odd quotient is exactly the
previous contextual coordinate \(x-y\). It is obtained directly from the
source chart and its reflected mate without first materializing 49 classes.

## What remains true

For odd \(p\), the pair \((q_+,q_-)\) reconstructs both chart observations:

\[
\ell=\frac{q_++q_-}{2},
\qquad
\ell_X=\frac{q_+-q_-}{2}.
\]

Therefore the joint map

\[
(q_+,q_-):\Lambda\longrightarrow\mathbb F_p^2
\]

has \(p^2\) values in the transverse case. The 49-class packet is precisely
the jointly faithful even-plus-odd refinement. Either selected parity port has
only seven values and forgets its complement.

The corrected theorem is:

> Transverse reflected charts force a \(p^2\)-class carrier only when the
> contract requires joint reconstruction of both chart observations, or
> equivalently both parity ports. A contract requiring only one relative
> parity sector has a direct \(p\)-class quotient.

## Aspect's theorem selects the smaller object

Aspect's theorem is target-sensitive. For the target relation \(q_-\), the
legal quotient is exactly

\[
\Lambda\longrightarrow\Lambda/\ker q_-.
\]

All directions in its kernel are invisible to the target by construction.
Retaining the full intersection packet would preserve additional distinctions
not required by that target.

If the target instead consists of both \(\ell\) and \(\ell_X\), then its kernel
is

\[
\ker\ell\cap\ker\ell_X=L\cap XL,
\]

and the \(p^2\)-class packet is minimal. Thus the same source supports two
different minimal carriers because the observation contracts differ.

## Meta-level correction

There is no target-independent minimal completion. The lattice intersection
is canonical as the jointly faithful refinement, not universally mandatory.
The required coherence depth depends on which lower-level distinctions the
declared higher relation must retain.

This also matches the completed magnetic parity theorem: either electric or
magnetic port is individually nonfaithful, while their joint port reconstructs
the transported packet.

## Boundary cases

- At \(p=2\), division by two fails and even/odd reconstruction collapses.
- If the chart line is reflection-invariant, one of \(q_+\) or \(q_-\) may
  vanish; transversality is what makes the joint parity coordinates faithful.
- The direct \(p\)-class quotient is a mathematical coefficient carrier, not
  yet an executable characteristic-zero phase observation.
