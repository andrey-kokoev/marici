# D-polynomial Routh ladder

## Question

Are the tested D-polynomials Hurwitz stable by exact Routh certificates?

## Claim boundary

The regenerated checker certifies 21 polynomials for sizes 2 through 8 and three shifts, with a deliberate unstable control. This is a finite-cutoff result.

## Disposition

Retain as bounded evidence for an analytic endpoint backend; arbitrary-size stability remains unproved.
