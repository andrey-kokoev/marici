# Oriented source signs are not monotone in Gale order at size eight

## Question

Do oriented terminal source signs form an upset or downset in componentwise Gale order?

## Claim boundary

No. Across 3,584 exact terminal cases, positivity fails to be a Gale upset in 3,553 cases and fails to be a Gale downset in 3,558. Both first fail for empty base and endpoints `(0,1)`: labels `{0}<{1}<{2}` already exhibit positive-negative-positive orientation with exact nonzero values. Thus no threshold or monotone-sign argument on Gale order can certify the bounded terminal sums. The long census completed and materialized its result despite the synchronous carrier call timing out.

## Disposition

Reject Gale sign monotonicity as the source certificate. Nonmonotone signs do not yet refute a weighted Gale transport, because positive mass on several comparable labels could dominate negative mass without forming an upset. Next solve the exact bipartite transportation problem in both Gale directions for every terminal case and record the first unmatched negative mass.
