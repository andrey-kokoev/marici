# The Factor Two Is a Parity Extension, Not a Bockstein Line

The tail reversal eigensublattices fit into the exact sequence

\[
0\longrightarrow L^+\oplus L^-
\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^2
\longrightarrow\mathbb Z/2
\longrightarrow0.
\]

Indeed, the eigenbasis matrix

\[
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}
\]

has Smith packet \((1,2)\).  Modulo two, its two columns coincide and its rank
drops from two to one.  At every odd tested prime its rank remains two.

This promotes one part of the C factorization to an integral theorem:

\[
|\partial_C|/768
=
\underbrace{2}_{\text{parity extension}}
\kappa_C^2.
\]

The quotient is finite torsion, not a rank-one Bockstein subbundle.  Calling it
a line would obscure the rank change that creates it.  Aspect's horizontality
hostile is therefore directly relevant: a torsion specialization defect cannot
be treated as a constant-rank transported line.

This theorem does not explain \(\kappa_C^2\).  It supplies only the factor two.
The equal covariant and contravariant conductor grades still require a separate
source-derived correspondence.
