# Quarter log-supermodularity has opposite almost-principal pairs

## Question

Do paired almost-principal minors locally generate the reverse Hadamard–Fischer inequalities with a fixed sign orientation?

## Claim boundary

The result covers all 1,792 local identities of the order-eight transfer. It does not prove the paired-product sign at arbitrary Hurwitz size.

## Disposition

Every Desnanot–Jacobi identity is exact, and every paired product is strictly negative:

\[
p(S\cup i)p(S\cup j)-p(S)p(S\cup\{i,j\})
=u_{S;i,j}v_{S;i,j}<0.
\]

Thus strict log-supermodularity has a local, cancellation-free generator. The stronger fixed-orientation gate fails: for nonadjacent index pairs, the sign of \(u_{S;i,j}\) changes with \(S\), while \(v_{S;i,j}\) always changes oppositely. No single pairwise sign assignment explains the products. The next leaf is `quarter-transfer-principal-product-lower-bound`, testing the consequence that log-supermodularity bounds every principal minor below by the product of its diagonal minors, which would isolate paired-product negativity as a sufficient P-matrix mechanism.
