native carrier and fixed metric. This is different from accessibility by the
forward spatial-reader grammar studied in `four-progression-directions-v1`.

Classify nested notions of preserved structure rather than treating every
unitary basis change as a spatial direction:

1. Arbitrary metric-unitary commutants of L and G, using their exact joint
   sector dimensions.
2. Patch-preserving internal transformations, retaining the native geometric
   frame transports and patch support blocks.
3. Ambient isometries of the actual P tetrahedron or N belt, including their
   induced patch permutations, sector grouping and cyclic character.
4. Add the actual ordered local cyclic operator, distinguishing forward
   transport from inverse-cycle/orientation-reversing duality.

Accept geometric candidates only if all native squared distances and carrier
faces are preserved. Test their induced full field maps against native metrics,
L/G and the ordered cycle. Improper transformations must explicitly distinguish
complex-linear and antiunitary transport; do not erase conjugation.

For continuous patch-local residual freedom, derive constraints from G's
transport orbits and L's full-support selected lines. Test whether nonorthogonal
cyclic eigenvectors force the remaining unitary phases to agree.

Counts of Lie-group parameters are not configuration-space dimensions. Finite
geometric symmetry does not imply a realizable native reader operation. Source
markings, C/M roles and allowed histories remain further restrictions.
