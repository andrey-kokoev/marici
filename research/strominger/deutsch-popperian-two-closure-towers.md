# Deutsch–Popperian conjecture: two second-level closure towers

## Conjecture

Every universal second-level closure obligation in a source-authorized
multi-axis system is generated by two independent requirements:

\[
I=\text{contravariant invariance},\qquad
C=\text{covariant composability}.
\]

When both are present, their compatibility is additional mixed mate data
\(M\). A single undifferentiated closure predicate cannot recover
\((I,C,M)\).

This is a static variance claim, not a temporal sequence.

## Predictions

The conjecture predicts realizable off-diagonal configurations:

\[
(I,C)=(1,0),\qquad(I,C)=(0,1),
\]

and predicts that

\[
I=C=1
\]

does not force \(M=1\).

All three occur in the current evidence.

- Magnetic preferred chart: the readout is defined, but aliases one
  distinction, giving \((I,C)=(0,1)\).
- Magnetic factorial normalization: the kernel is invariant, but no
  divided-power execution port is authorized, giving \((1,0)\).
- Magnetic integral curvature square: both boundary structures exist, while
  the mate is rational-only, giving \((1,1,0)\) over \(\mathbb Z\).
- Grothendieck prime-scale recursion: the backward equalizer is exact through
  square grade, while the sesquilinear or exterior-square operator lift is
  open, giving \((1,0)\).
- Kitaev five-rail code: the code and recovery structure are exact, while
  railwise multiplication and Fourier fail to preserve the code, again
  giving \((1,0)\).

These cases falsify the one-tower hypothesis.

## Attempted falsification

The independence claim would fail if source laws forced \(I\) and \(C\) to
coincide. The realized off-diagonal cases refute that collapse across three
independent sectors.

The stronger exhaustiveness claim is now falsified.  Its original decisive
falsifier was stated as:

> Exhibit a source-authorized universal second-level failure for which
> \(I=1\), \(C=1\), and the mixed mate \(M\) is authorized, but which is
> neither an instrument-incidence issue nor a higher-nerve coherence issue.

Such a case exists as a finite source-authorized categorical model.  For
\(G=\mathbb Z/2\), all binary invariance, composition, and mate gates can pass
while triple pasting carries the nontrivial normalized cocycle
\(\omega(a,b,c)=(-1)^{abc}\).  The class cannot be removed by a binary
rephasing.  See `deutsch-popperian-two-tower-falsifier.md`.

The qualification “neither ... nor a higher-nerve coherence issue” was not a
legitimate protection of the conjecture: a nontrivial higher-nerve class is
exactly evidence for a missing constructor.

Conversely, the next sharp tests are:

- lift Grothendieck’s prime equalizer to the declared operator level and
  classify any residual as \(C\) or \(M\);
- supply Kitaev with one explicit nontransversal fault-tolerant gadget and
  verify that it repairs \(C\) without changing the invariant code object;
- continue searching all sectors for an \(I=C=M=1\) failure occurring before
  instrument incidence or higher pasting.

Current verdict: one second-level tower is falsified; the independence of
invariance and composability is supported; their exhaustion by one binary mate
is falsified.  At least one additional associator datum is required in the
general source-authorized setting.
