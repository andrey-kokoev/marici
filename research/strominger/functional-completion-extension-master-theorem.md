# Functional-completion extension master theorem

## The theorem

Let the global grade-three spin transport on the sphere be

\[
\mathcal A_3(C_+,C_-)
=(\bar\eth\eth^3 C_+,\eth\bar\eth^3 C_-).
\]

The following twelve statements hold.

1. Finite point jets form a strict LF source space. Smooth sections form a
   nuclear Frechet-Schwartz space and distributions its strong nuclear DFS
   dual. Moving between these spaces is a constructor change, not scalar
   extension.
2. On spin harmonics the multiplier is
   \[
   \lambda_l=-(l-4)(l+5)
   \sqrt{(l-2)(l+3)(l-3)(l+4)}.
   \]
3. Helicity and real electric/magnetic parity give compatible global
   decompositions; antipodal parity does not turn the paired bundle map into
   one scalar operator.
4. The only spectral zeros are `l=2,3,4`. Their multiplicity is
   `5+7+9=21` per helicity, hence 21 in each real parity sector.
5. For every Sobolev index `s`,
   \[
   \mathcal A_3:H^s(L^{+2}\oplus L^{-2})
   \longrightarrow H^{s-4}(L^{+4}\oplus L^{-4})
   \]
   is Fredholm. Per helicity its kernel has dimension 21, cokernel dimension
   9, and index 12. Every distributional homogeneous solution is smooth.
6. The finite news energy condition admits both low-mode transitions and
   returning pulses.
   A no-magnetic endpoint condition is additional boundary authority and does
   not exclude a returning history.
7. Low electric modes are generated by the displacement source channel and
   low magnetic modes by a coexact angular-flux channel. Their source
   multipliers are nonzero for `l=2,3,4`; stronger nonlinear matter
   realizability is not inferred.
8. Total-variation closure of finite atomic measures is countably atomic and
   contains no pure smooth low harmonic. Weak-* Radon closure does contain
   smooth flux densities. Colliding atoms can converge distributionally to
   jets only with diverging variation norm.
9. The grade-three magnetic readout loses exactly 21 real coefficients. The
   joint map `(A_3,P_low)` is faithful, and rank-nullity makes 21 independent
   scalar ports minimal.
10. The smooth low block is a fixed spectral kernel, not a pole-depth tower.
    The `(1,-3,2)` class remains a finite collision/second-jet stencil, not a
    new smooth harmonic family.
11. The local projected symbol `p^4-q^4` has four characteristic ridge
    families, but none supplies a nonzero compactly supported homogeneous
    distribution or a planar `L2` state. Cutoff creates a boundary source.
12. Therefore the characteristic locus supports no genuine localized
    finite-energy global kernel class. The source-authorized weak-* smooth
    completion nevertheless supports a genuine, hard-flux-constructible
    21-dimensional magnetic kernel, caused by the separate global spectral
    zeros `l=2,3,4`.

## Exact scope

This theorem refines the finite-point classification without reversing it.
No nonzero point-supported distribution is a finite smooth harmonic sum. The
new classes become available only after a topology-bearing source completion.
The scalar equation `p^4-q^4=0` remains useful as a local chart diagnostic but
does not possess authority to define the global physical kernel.

Numerical spectral evaluation through `l=10000` finds no late zero and confirms
the elliptic and pseudoinverse bounds. Exact symbolic checks, rather than the
cutoff, establish the unbounded statement.
