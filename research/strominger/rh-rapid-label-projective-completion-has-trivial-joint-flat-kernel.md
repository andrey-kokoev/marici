# Rapid-label projective completion has trivial joint-flat kernel

## Question

Can the ordered-pair arithmetic carrier be completed so that every product-ratio moment and the polarized theta synthesis are continuous while retaining a nonzero state invisible to every joint moment?

## Candidate completion

On finite ordered-pair packets

\[
c=\sum_{n,m}c_{nm}e_n\otimes\overline{e_m},
\]

define, for every integer \(r\geq0\),

\[
p_r(c)^2=\sum_{n,m\geq1}|c_{nm}|^2(nm)^{2r}.
\]

These are the graph seminorms of the joint functional calculus generated by \(e^L e_n=ne_n\). Their projective limit is the rapid-label Fréchet space

\[
\mathscr S_{\rm lab}^{(2)}=\bigcap_{r\geq0}\ell^2((nm)^{2r}).
\]

Every mixed logarithmic moment is continuous there. Indeed, for fixed \(j,k\), Cauchy--Schwarz bounds

\[
\sum_{n,m}|c_{nm}|(\log(nm))^j|\log(n/m)|^k
\]

by \(p_r(c)\) times a convergent reciprocal polynomial-label sum when \(r>1/2\), after increasing \(r\) to absorb the logarithms. Fixed prime multiplication and partial division are continuous, although their bounds depend on the prime; no uniform inverse estimate is asserted.

## Joint transform

Define the two-variable Dirichlet transform

\[
F_c(z,w)=\sum_{n,m\geq1}c_{nm}n^z m^w.
\]

Rapid label decay makes this series normally convergent on every compact subset of \(\mathbb C^2\), so \(F_c\) is entire. Since

\[
L_\Sigma=L\otimes I+I\otimes\overline L,
\qquad
L_\Delta=L\otimes I-I\otimes\overline L,
\]

the joint product-ratio moments are the derivatives at the origin along the invertible coordinates

\[
\partial_\Sigma=\partial_z+\partial_w,
\qquad
\partial_\Delta=\partial_z-\partial_w.
\]

If every joint moment vanishes, every mixed \(z,w\) derivative of \(F_c\) at the origin vanishes. Entire analyticity then gives \(F_c=0\).

## Coefficient uniqueness

Set \(z=w=-t\) only after fixing one variable separately; a diagonal restriction alone would erase ordered-pair multiplicity. For fixed real \(w\), uniqueness of the one-variable Dirichlet series in \(z\) gives

\[
\sum_m c_{nm}m^w=0
\]

for every \(n\). Uniqueness again in \(w\) gives \(c_{nm}=0\) for every ordered pair. Hence

\[
\bigcap_{j,k\geq0}
\ker\!\left(\varepsilon^{(2)}L_\Sigma^jL_\Delta^k\right)
\cap\mathscr S_{\rm lab}^{(2)}
=\{0\}.
\]

## Disposition

The rapid-label projective completion is a valid common domain for every joint moment and fixed-prime transport, but it cannot contain the requested nonzero joint-flat witness. This is a quasi-analytic obstruction, not a finite-rank artifact.

A weaker topology might admit moment-flat distributions, but then continuity of the complete moment family and of the boundary current requires a different source-derived dual pairing. The band current cannot be added as a defining seminorm merely to force continuity: that would assume the comparison being constructed.

## Claim boundary

This packet does not prove continuity of the polarized theta synthesis or boundary current on \(\mathscr S_{\rm lab}^{(2)}\); those estimates remain independently required. It proves that even if those maps extend, this natural rapid-label completion has no nonzero joint-flat state for them to detect.
