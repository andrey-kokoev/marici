# Even-shell cumulative majorization

## Question

Can negative shells be matched cumulatively from either the center outward or the boundary inward?

## Claim boundary

Among 1,092 exact profiles, 45 satisfy prefix majorization only, 21 satisfy both prefix and suffix majorization, none satisfy suffix only, and 1,026 satisfy neither. The first bidirectional failure is `(n,s,k)=(3,0,2)`, where both the prefix and suffix sum first fail at shell index 1 although the complete alternating sum is positive. This rejects one-dimensional directional matching, not total dominance. The governing DPC case remains `rh-quarter-D-imaginary-axis-dominance.md`.

## Disposition

The local and cumulative shell-matching backends are exhausted by the same exact obstruction. Do not generate another shell-order variant. Reallocate to the distinct positive-real backend: certify the rational function `R/P` as strictly bounded on the right half-plane using a source-derived realization or Pick matrix.
