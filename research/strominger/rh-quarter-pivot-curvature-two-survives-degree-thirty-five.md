# Quarter pivot curvature two survives degree thirty-five

## Problem

The local expansion

\[
c_m=1+\frac2m+O(m^{-2})
\]

had only a degree-seventeen finite diagnostic.

## Bold conjecture

The exact pivot curvature satisfies

\[
\lim_{m\to\infty}m(c_m-1)=2.
\]

## Named rivals

The rivals are a nearby noninteger limit, slow inverse-degree drift, and a correction model whose truncation creates the value two.

## Risky consequences

An enlarged exact grid and nested tail fits must retain the same limit under changes of the lower cutoff.

## Strongest falsification attempt

Exact determinants were extended through degree thirty-six, giving pivot curvatures through \(m=35\). Four nested fits used corrections through \(m^{-3}\). Their limits are \(2.00001169,2.00000492,2.00000265,2.00000165\): all within \(1.17\times10^{-5}\) of two, with nested spread below \(5\times10^{-5}\). A unit limit fails deliberately. All five gates passed.

## Disposition

The coefficient two survives the enlarged finite falsification. Do not promote it to a theorem: the first missing object is a fixed-shift pivot asymptotic with a controlled \(O(m^{-2})\) remainder. Stop this proof branch at that blocker and reallocate to `quarter-global-shift-first-correction-proof`.

## Claim boundary

Nested extrapolation is not convergence proof. The correction basis was selected in advance from the observed inverse-degree structure but remains an assumed remainder model.
