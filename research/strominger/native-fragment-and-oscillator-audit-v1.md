# First two specification gates: frozen fragment and bounded oscillator audit

## 1. Frozen source language

`contracts/native-query-source-fragment.v1.json` fixes a one-object FREE WORD language acting on X=A4 x Bool. A4 is generated by the actual abc and adb tables extracted from the existing native Agda module.

There are eight generators: two left translations and six controlled Boolean transition queries. Every finite list is allowed. Evaluation is chronological; concatenation means execute the left word first. The empty word is identity. No reset or measurement operation is silently inserted.

This is not the old endpoint-typed six-edge walk language. For example Q_AB;Q_CA is a valid query word regardless of seed-edge composability. The state variable is a supplied transport g, not a particle being traversed along AB.

Correctness is specified on all 24 states, with initialization optionally selecting (g,false). Observe the Boolean at declared boundaries and retain the complete successor for every future continuation. Mathematical reading does not imply physically nondestructive observation.

Literal words remain distinct even when their state permutations agree. Thus Q_AB;Q_AB and the empty word have equal execution but different retained histories. Semantic involutions and cube identities do not quotient the grammar.

The generator interpretation into native data is specified: embed g as a Vertex equivalence, use the proved native recorder for Q_e, and postcompose that equivalence with Query.ABC or Query.ADB for left translations. Word interpretation is chronological composition with retained intermediate packages. The corresponding finite-group embedding and all-word native comparison have NOT been newly Agda-proved; those are explicit proof obligations, not hidden new inputs to the syntax specification.

The independent checker verifies the 24-state permutations, all generator inverse periods, 585 words through length three with every split, and negative syntax examples. The general evaluation composition law follows structurally by induction on lists; the bounded regression is not represented as an exhaustive all-word proof.

## 3. Stronger result: a passive-pulse rank obstruction

The proposed six-port oscillator pulses have the form

    S_e(theta)=I+(exp(-i theta)-1) P_e,

where each P_e is the rank-one projector onto the corresponding difference mode. All six difference directions lie in a common six-dimensional space D. They are independent because their record coordinates are distinct. Every pulse therefore fixes D-perp, a 24-dimensional subspace of the 30-mode amplitude carrier, pointwise. Any finite ordered pulse product U has

    rank(U-I) <= 6.

This is an all-word invariant, not a failed search over short pulse schedules. It holds even granting arbitrary pulse areas.

The target native triangle L_ABC on A4 x Bool has eight disjoint three-cycles. Its permutation representation L therefore satisfies

    rank(L-I)=16.

Suppose an injective linear encoding J of its 24-dimensional amplitude space into the 30-mode oscillator space intertwines a pulse word:

    U J=J L.

Then

    16 = rank(J(L-I)) = rank((U-I)J) <= 6,

a contradiction. Thus this entire direct faithful linear amplitude/single-excitation implementation class is excluded BEFORE trying to compile the recorders.

Allowing a common global phase does not help. Each cube root of unity is an eigenvalue of L with multiplicity eight, so rank(L-zI) is at least sixteen for every scalar z over C. The pulse word is still scalar on its common fixed subspace after a global phase.

The real raw-coordinate checker avoids square roots: use metric diag(1/6 I24,I6) and difference vectors (f_e,-record_e). Each vector has squared norm two and their span has rank six. This is the same positive-metric geometry as the normalized oscillator amplitudes.

## Scope of rejection

This excludes an injective linear intertwiner into the documented passive 30-mode amplitude space, equivalently its one-excitation representation. It does NOT exclude arbitrary multiparticle Fock encodings, nonlinear encodings, additional physical interactions, measurement-assisted protocols, or other physical models. None of those alternatives has been supplied or admitted here.

In particular, the absence of a faithful linear realization does not refute the mathematical conditional pairing theorem or native Boolean recorder. The target and available physical action have different invariant structure despite some matching feature overlaps.
