# Five-side oct: principled review of the geometry, Hopf and packet branches

**Disposition: retain the computations; do not promote their models to the wiring specification.** No construction gate is closed. No implementation, route, renderer or historical receipt is changed by this review.

## 1. Findings

1. The strongest established material is native incidence, the specified entry-comparison sites, and the integer vertex-link sphere-sweep witnesses.
2. The first missing map is from the operator's retained signed-pair object to the operands and comparison operations used by a model. It precedes the Hopf, amplitude and five-slot choices.
3. The 44 spatial monopoles, four independent complex values and fifth-coefficient problem were introduced by our experiments. None is a source requirement.
4. The standing global-first directive still governs. The recent native path executor is a diagnostic, not a construction method for the whole wiring. Our sequence of next-step proposals drifted back toward local-first construction despite leaving admission flags false.
5. The negative results reject specified extra assumptions, not the intended wiring. In particular, the fixed-codec theorem does not refute a history-dependent, globally defined retained object.
6. The next useful action is to specify one shared-reference comparison's record semantics. No further coefficient or topology modification is justified before that.

### An ambiguity already present in the obligations contract

The contract says splitting partitions owned records disjointly. The four cross-pairings contain repeated signed roles. These can be compatible if pairings are views referring to shared records, or if a specified subrecord partition is involved. Treating the four pairs as eight independently owned payloads is not justified. The ownership/reference distinction must be made explicit rather than enforcing one interpretation silently.

“No content transformation” does **not** by itself mean that every coefficient chart change must be a permutation. A reversible change of representation can mix coordinates while preserving contents. Conversely, an invertible matrix does not by itself preserve source occurrences or physical inventory.

Retaining history does not mean that no two histories can ever be related. Such a relation must retain the histories and carry an authorized comparison witness; deleting one is different from comparing them.

## 5. Corrected claim ledger

### Keep as geometric evidence

- The known entry comparisons require adjacent N-face operands. The old opposite-face passage grammar cannot by itself express those comparison presentations. This does not license arbitrary bent wire paths or identify comparison paths with wire trajectories.
- Residual diagrams are full interior vertex stars with `4 P + 6 N + 4 E` cells. Their link is S², and their nonzero integer sweep witnesses represent its fundamental class.
- “Whole-depth” in the comparison note means all carrier layers were covered for paths of at most four interfaces. It is not complete higher-coherence enumeration.

### Keep as conditional mathematical/source tests

- Standard Hopf and ordinary-frame references have different transition degrees. Neither was selected by the native signed-pair source.
- The source electron spinor retains an internal phase bundle and dark-phase data independently of host position. This is relevant prior research, not a spatial monopole law.
- Complementary lines cancel determinant winding while retaining nonidentity paired holonomy. This does not identify the P/E pair or prove global coherence.
- The two-contrast/three-context decomposition is invertible. The native NN reader mixes those parts; a spinor-only fixed adapter loses information.
- The executable packet preserves the supplied five-slot data and actual path history within its older grammar. It is a verification instrument under the global-first policy.

### Keep the counterexamples with their hypotheses attached

- Local shared-ring mismatch rejects the selected connection-preserving same-cell gluing. It does not reject all Hopf lifts or more general base comparisons.
- Pairwise conjugation fails the stated triple chart cocycle. It is not a theorem prohibiting every retained comparison system with additional witnesses.
- The five-cycle theorem rejects fixed endpoint-only linear encodings of four independently permuted complex records. Those content and operation assumptions were not specified by the operator. The two tested paths need not be equivalent whole assemblies or both belong to the intended global object.
- The four-mode projector codec is not faithful on the unrestricted five-slot source: it drops a real return mode. It nevertheless passes on its declared transported sector. Thus it neither falsifies nor validates the broad signed-wire conjecture.

### Remove from the construction's critical path

- **“We must cancel 44 units of boundary charge.”** That is a consequence of the equal-sign spatial reference trial, not a native obligation. Its dual boundary also differs from the physical octahedral boundary.
- **“We must determine the fifth coefficient.”** This follows only after selecting the five-slot representation and a proposed four-parameter wire model.
- **“More local path depth will settle global wiring.”** This contradicts the standing construction policy and does not address the missing semantic map.

These corrections do not invalidate the scoped calculations. They invalidate promoting them beyond their stated premises.

## 6. Tests that pass by construction, and information stored outside the visible channel

Several checks remain useful implementation tests but cannot select the intended semantics:

- `E_h=Pi_h E0` transports an encoding with any retained coefficient permutation. Its transport identity is built into the definition.
- Scalar comparison phases defined as differences of path phases telescope on closed presentation cycles. This is not an independent native filling witness.
- Gauge changes `g_b-g_a` obey triple cancellation by construction. The important unproved part is identifying these gauges and fibres with source records.
- The retained packet contains the anchored original five-vector and history as well as derived coordinates. That is legitimate provenance retention, but a receiver's access to those records must be specified. Recovery using an archive cannot establish that a smaller interface alone transmits the contents.
- Branch enumeration explores alternative histories; it does not perform a simultaneous physical split. It has not established disjoint ownership or no-copying for a global branching wire.

The independent `joinCoordinates` check does establish reconstruction from all five contrast/context coordinates, rather than only from the root archive. It does not establish recovery from the two contrast coordinates alone.

## 7. Next discriminating fixture: one shared-reference comparison

Use the already identified five-edge site from the incidence note:

```
common P reference: proton:-1,-1,2,2
N presentations: neutron:-1,0,2,2 and neutron:0,-1,2,2
E comparison site: electron:-1,-1,1,1
```

At the semantic level this is the comparison of two E directions at a shared P reference. Do not bind those directions to axes, numerical signs, spinor columns or five-slot indices yet.

The first fixture should carry opaque role-labelled occurrences and separately declared underlying-record identities. It must be able to express both shared-reference views and distinct occurrences without confusing equal values with equal records. This is a proposed test format, not a decision that the four roles have independent payloads.

### Required source decisions before executing an operator

1. In the two pair presentations sharing P, is their P reference the same retained record, two views of it, or distinct subrecords with a declared common origin?
2. Does the comparison produce a certificate relating unchanged operands, a reversible regrouping of them, or a new derived content? What is its output type?
3. Which records and histories must its inverse recover, and which may the receiving site actually read?

### Freeze these hostile cases

- Two different record IDs with equal values must not merge unless an authorized equality says so.
- Two views of one record must not become two independently owned payloads.
- Recombination and reversal must recover all declared occurrences and histories.
- A changed reference occurrence or erased context must be rejected.
- Two histories with the same endpoints must not be silently collapsed; any comparison must retain its explicit witness.

This fixture tests a semantic restriction that any proposed global solution must satisfy. It is **not** a local rule for constructing the whole assembly. Extend to global overlap tests only after its operands and comparison law are supplied.
