# Graded parent and free transport: first bounded gate

## Prior research and operator correction

Ledger985 separates shared carrier operations (H2W) from a canonical parent
object (H2S). Ledger990 requires compatible coefficient/readout pairings
(H2LR). Ledger996 locates possible Monster symmetry in a graded parent, not
in a finite spatial carrier; it requires independently derived moonshine
structure before activating that research direction. Ledger2002 still records
no common parent construction. These are historical scope statements, not

The operator has made whole-cell assembly optional and proposed transport in

## Frozen first execution

1. Construct the conditional free-transport comparison on Euclidean R3 with
   a trivial vector bundle R3 x V: T_v(p,z)=(p+v,z). Prove the additive action,
   inverse, pairwise-distance preservation and fiber-data preservation by
   exact affine coefficient identities. An arbitrary decorated shape can be
   translated this way. This introduces an ambient model/trivialization; it
   does NOT derive a native field connection, physical space or sewing law.
2. Retain the BCC center lattice from the geometric screen. Exhibit a rational
   translation taking its origin outside the lattice. Thus free transport of
   objects is not a symmetry of every fixed background decoration.
3. Use the existing rank-three alignment space and36-dimensional baryon field
   only as two declared diagnostic inputs. Test the unmodified ordinary
   symmetric algebras Sym(C^d), with generators in degree1 and grading frozen
   before comparison. Compute degrees0..3 independently by a Hilbert-series
   recurrence and the binomial formula. Compare with dimensions1,0,196884,
   21493760 of the usual moonshine grading (equivalently its q^-1-shifted
   character). No shifts, added generators, fitted quotients or dimension search.
   This tests a simple candidate, NOT a source-derived common parent. A failure
   rejects only that candidate as a grading-preserving moonshine realization.
4. List the missing actual inputs: canonical graded completion, multiplication
   and preserved operations, sector realization/readout maps, modular law or
   other ledger996 activation witness and independent replication. No central
   charge is inferred from a finite vector-space dimension. No Baby Monster
   action or2A involution is presumed.

## Decision boundary

Passing the conditional affine transport identities is not Monster evidence.
The ordinary symmetric algebra also admits a nontrivial continuous scalar
circle action preserving its induced Hermitian metrics; its grading and metric
alone do not select a finite sporadic automorphism group. This is a structural
observation about this candidate, not a no-go for enriched completions.

The research node remains open after this first screen. Continue by defining
source-supported transition/connection maps and graded product/realization
structure, or report that they are absent. Do not modify native geometry,
field data or historical ledger entries to force a moonshine signature.
