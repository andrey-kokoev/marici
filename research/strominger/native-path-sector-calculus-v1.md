## Strongest falsification attempts and residuals

1. **Replace the comparison by a flat square.** Actual $ab-ba=2c\ne0$. The flat model fails on the original source, not on a fitted extension.
2. **Erase the central sign through density.** The two route spinors have equal density but pairing $-1$ against the same retained route reference. Density is a weaker reader, not a faithful replacement for this full-amplitude task.
3. **Forge a phase or boundary in a derivation.** Certificate validation rejects both an altered exchange phase and a wrong final word, even when other source data remain valid.
4. **Use the exchange rule outside its domain.** Same-axis exchanges, invalid positions and zero signs are rejected.
5. **Identify an endpoint normal form with execution history.** An operation followed by its inverse restores the spinor but changes the retained command archive from length zero to two. Endpoint reduction does not license that identification.

These discriminate the preregistered rivals: independent sector fixtures and a nonnative cyclic constructor are unnecessary for this scope; flatness and endpoint-faithful history erasure fail.
