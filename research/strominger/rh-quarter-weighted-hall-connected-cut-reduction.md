# Weighted Hall inequalities reduce to connected demand subsets

## Question

Must an all-order Gale transport proof test every subset of negative labels independently?

## Claim boundary

No. Join two negative labels when they share a comparable positive neighbor. For any demand subset, distinct connected components in this overlap graph have disjoint positive neighborhoods. Neighborhood supply, demand, and Hall slack are therefore additive across components. Any deficient subset contains a deficient connected component, so weighted Hall inequalities need only be proved for connected negative subsets. An exact finite witness verifies the reduction and a deliberate connected deficit. This theorem reduces the test family but does not prove any quarter-source inequality.

## Disposition

Sharpen the all-order request: derive nonnegative Hall slack for every connected negative-label subset, not every subset. Continue locally by computing the fixed-eight connected-cut census and minimum exact connected slack; this identifies the extremal shapes an eventual source proof must control while the parameterized constructor remains with Nima.
