# D negative-cross capacity profile

## Question

Does coefficientwise dominance require transport between powers of `omega^2`, or can every negative cross coefficient be paid by square capacity at the same index?

## Claim boundary

Across the 21 exact condensations with `2<=n<=8` and `0<=s<=2`, every negative coefficient of `2 Re(H D_n conjugate(R))` has nonzero, strictly larger coefficient in `|H D_n|^2` at the identical index. This is bounded classification, not a uniform inequality. The governing DPC case remains `rh-quarter-D-imaginary-axis-dominance.md`; no new explanatory DPC case is introduced here.

## Disposition

No cross-index Hall matching is needed in the bounded family. The all-order obligation reduces to the diagonal inequalities

\[
[\omega^{2k}]|H D_n|^2\geq-[\omega^{2k}]2\operatorname{Re}(H D_n\overline R)
\]

for indices where the cross coefficient is negative. The next executable test is whether coefficient log-concavity or a convolution Cauchy inequality proves these diagonal bounds uniformly.
