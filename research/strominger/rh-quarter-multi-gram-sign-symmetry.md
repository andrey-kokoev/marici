# Multi-Gram positivity preserves independent sector sign ambiguity

## Question

Can a minimal multi-rectangle Gram decomposition force nonnegative Hall capacities?

## Claim boundary

Not when capacities depend linearly on cross-block entries. Each block Gram sector is invariant under `X_r -> -X_r` by congruence with `diag(I,-I)`. Independent sector flips therefore preserve every positive-semidefinite certificate and support rectangle while changing assembled capacity signs. An exact three-sector row-star witness has squared cross norms `3/16,1/8,1/16`; flipping the middle sector preserves all Gram tests but makes two capacities negative. This does not rule out a quadratic extraction such as entrywise squares.

## Disposition

Reject linear multi-Gram cross blocks as a nonnegativity mechanism. Quadratic extraction is the sole remaining Gram route to automatic positive weights, but it identifies opposite signs and must separately prove source reconstruction and Hall margins. Next test whether squaring can reconstruct signed source terms or whether its sign quotient destroys the information required by the source-derived coupling.
