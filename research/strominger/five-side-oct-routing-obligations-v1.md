**Status: first specification deliverable, not a wiring construction.** No global coherence or native realization is admitted. No new routes or rendering are produced by this work.

## 1. Source hierarchy

The machine-readable [contract](contracts/five-side-oct-routing-obligations-v1.json) records source identities, scope, requirements, open bindings, unvalidated conventions and completion gates.

### Governing requirements

1. The operator's current-task correction: “it is full fibration, so naturally they divide”; the incoming bundle scales with the five-edge carrier, not Sulfur's sixteen. The accepted carrier-dependent target is **80 fibres**, not a universal sixteen-ID alphabet.
2. The operator requires dense triangle wiring and coherence groups realized inside the filler, not edge coverage or a decorative embedding. Neither native edges nor required filler interiors may disappear from the problem merely by changing the representation.
3. [Global construction, local verification](global-coherence-standing-assumption.md): define the complete global object, compatibility, closure and retained histories before choosing local readings. Local checks cannot manufacture missing global attachment operations.
4. [Reversible channel specification](sulfur-32-reversible-channel-specification.md): retain identities, occurrences, paths, grouping and histories; reverse the presentation without inventing a content transformation. The P/E-pair and N-tube descriptions must retain their correspondence. “Double-twist” does not authorize an invented rotation or phase operator.
5. The same specification provides signed-entry ordering and the shared-reference layer-4 comparisons. It explicitly does **not** identify the signed pairings with the coordinate-axis 2-by-2 array, choose a scattering matrix, or uniquely select the middle regrouping.

### Conditional evidence, not governing laws

- [Sulfur periodic wiring](sulfur-32-global-wiring-v1.md) declares a complete **edge-three** translation quotient. Its closure and coordinate-axis indexing are hypotheses/conventions. Reproducing it is a scoped regression; it does not authorize a period-four closure for edge five.
- The [middle-layer checker](checkers/check_sulfur32_middle_layer.mjs) finds four incidence assignments per reading at edge three. It supplies neither uniqueness nor a binding to signed pairings, nor an edge-five construction.
- The [sixteen surface paths](sulfur-32-sixteen-strand-surface-candidate.md) are a different candidate. Their sorted binary choices and middle matching are not an iterative full-fibration rule.
- The simplicial-braid discussion in `the-global-coherence-constructor-is-the-simplicial-braid-object.md` is not bound to these cell ports or retained wire records. Its title or mathematical analogy cannot supply the missing binding.

These distinctions matter: the previous work tested one conditional candidate against another and described the agreement as if it validated the operator's construction target.

## 4. Required global object and witnesses

Before solving for paths, the construction must supply:

1. **Retained state and port binding.** A source-defined representation of fibre contents, occurrence identities, histories and endpoint presentations. Explicitly bind the signed P/E pairings and incoming N attachment to native sites. Do not replace the shared incoming attachment by a distributed endpoint display without proving the correspondence.
2. **Comparison object.** For every required comparison, retain its operands, common reference, context, admissible comparison map and incident boundary. Include the relations among alternative readings and overlapping comparisons. Groups must be justified by these obligations, not extracted from guessed routes.
3. **Composition/filling requirements.** Specify which route composites must agree and what witness or filling is required when they differ. Do not assume every closed composition is the identity, invent a phase action, or use connectedness as a substitute. Completeness of this obligation list is itself a gate.
4. **Simultaneous assignment.** Assign admissible passages, partitions, regroupings and comparisons together. Each split partitions retained records disjointly; each regrouping has a checked inverse. Local native restrictions verify the global assignment; they do not generate it by greedy extension.
5. **Native realization.** Every required connection has a sequence of permitted carrier incidences and internal passages with compatible endpoints and junctions. A seam label, line segment or period-class equality is not that witness. The edge/face/interior binding must account for the operator's triangle-edge requirement as well as filler depth.
6. **Boundary closure.** Derive or explicitly admit the edge-five boundary law and prove its realization. Enumerate all remaining choices. Neither nearest-slot selection nor lexical ID order may stand in for a missing equation.

If the constraints admit multiple solutions, retain the alternatives or prove the claimed equivalence. If they are inconsistent, return the conflicting obligations and their provenance. If the comparison algebra is not yet bound, report **not formulated**, not “no solution” and not “coherent.”

## 5. Open bindings and gates

The contract leaves these bindings explicitly open:

- retained fibre contents and occurrence representation;
- signed-pair to geometric-port identification;
- the admitted comparison/composition/filling algebra for this carrier;
- the complete whole-depth obligation complex;
- edge-five boundary closure;
- permitted geometric routing support and incoming attachment.

These are **missing bindings in the reviewed construction**, not a claim that no relevant theory exists elsewhere in the repository. A source must be identified and its applicability checked before an algorithm is selected.

The completion gates are: semantic binding → complete global obligations → global solution → native realization → independent acceptance. None is closed by this deliverable. Geometry and entry-incidence tests do not promote any gate.

## 6. Reproducible rejection evidence for the old candidate

`checkers/audit_five_side_oct_construction.mjs` records the following probes without mutating the candidate:

- merge all its alleged groups into one group;
- relabel every seam as a native interface;
- move an internal connection outside its owner while preserving coordinate sum.

The current candidate validator accepts all three. Thus it does not even enforce all of the candidate's own stated geometric/group definitions, let alone the independently required coherence.

An alternative boundary ordering preserves all native matches, the declared period condition and every 80-target-per-axis bijection, but changes **64 endpoint matches** and the component count from **62 to 68**. The audit recomputes transports and checks those preserved conditions. This is an ambiguity witness within the candidate's conditions, **not another coherent solution**.

Of 320 candidate slot wires, **100 depend on assumed seams** and **19 use nearest-lift admissions**. The maximum recorded N-cell chain has length two. That does not prove a longer chain is required; it shows that the code has not supplied a whole-depth composition derivation.
