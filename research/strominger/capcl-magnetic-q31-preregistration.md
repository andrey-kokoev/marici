# CapCl prediction 002: magnetic q=31 exact rank

The magnetic component constructor and Hall-support problem are already
defined at reflection distance \(q=31\). Complete Hall matchings at this
distance lie inside the frozen support census through \(q=60\), while the
frozen exact-rank census ends at \(q=30\). Thus \(q=31\) is the first eligible
out-of-range exact-rank target.

Before performing that rank calculation, prediction 002 states that every
full reflected component matrix at \(q=31\), \(k=10\), and
\(2\leq g\leq20\) has column rank 22. In closure-capability coordinates this
predicts no new observation obstruction and no internal cancellation behind
the complete Hall matching. A zero of one preferred maximal minor is only a
chart event and does not falsify the prediction. A rank defect of the full
matrix does.

This is a bounded prediction only. It makes no unbounded claim in \(q\),
\(g\), or pole cutoff.
