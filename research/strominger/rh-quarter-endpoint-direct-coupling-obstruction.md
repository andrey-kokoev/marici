# Quarter endpoint direct-coupling obstruction

## Question

Do the independently positive endpoint Hurwitz matrices couple through a totally nonnegative fixed-order matrix?

## Claim boundary

For the exact bounded matrices `A` and `B`, the unique direct coupling `T=A^{-1}B` satisfies `AT=B` but is not entrywise nonnegative: its `(0,2)` entry is negative. Hence it is not totally nonnegative, and the two independent Neville factorizations do not compose through this direct fixed-order coupling. This does not exclude a source-level common network, checkerboard conjugation, permutation, stabilization, or a non-square coupling.

## Disposition

Reject the direct fixed-order coupling backend. Continue with a distinct source-level common-network construction or a preregistered gauge/permutation class whose admissibility is derived from the source indexing rather than fitted after the obstruction.
