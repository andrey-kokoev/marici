# Reflected-convolution swap involution

## Question

Does the canonical index swap provide both odd cancellation and positive even residues in the symmetric reflected-product expansion?

## Claim boundary

On formal terms, exchange `AR(i,j)` with `RA(j,i)` and `AA(i,j)` with `AA(j,i)`. The map is involutive. For odd total degree, it is fixed-point-free and reverses `(-1)^j`, proving exact odd cancellation independently of coefficient values. For even total degree it preserves the sign and has diagonal fixed points, so it cannot prove positivity. This is an algebraic parity statement, not a uniform weighted inequality. The governing DPC case remains `rh-quarter-D-imaginary-axis-dominance.md`.

## Disposition

The involution completely explains odd cancellation and sharply fails on even residues. The remaining proof must be weight-sensitive: establish dominance of positive-parity coefficient pairs over negative-parity pairs in each even reflected convolution.
