# The Authorized Dagger Turns Grade Normalization into Round-Trip Curvature

## Why the scalar gauge theorem is not the end

On the forward endpoint grade chain, every nonzero scalar edge coefficient is
removable by independent rescaling of the graded spaces. A Hermitian structure
changes the category of admissible presentation changes: vertex maps must now
be unitary, and each forward constructor acquires an adjoint reverse arrow.

The forward chain

```text
H_l -> H_(l+1)
```

therefore becomes a dagger pair

```text
H_l <-> H_(l+1).
```

The resulting two-step round trip carries invariant spectral data.

## Normalized Cartan projection

Let

\[
C_l:H_1\otimes H_l\longrightarrow H_{l+1}
\]

be the normalized orthogonal Cartan projection, so that

\[
C_lC_l^\dagger=I_{H_{l+1}}.
\]

For an orthonormal basis \((e_a)\) of \(H_1\), define

\[
C_{l,a}(v)=C_l(e_a\otimes v).
\]

The axis-summed round trip

\[
K_l=\sum_a C_{l,a}^\dagger C_{l,a}
\]

is \(SO(3)\)-equivariant. Since \(H_l\) is irreducible, Schur's lemma gives

\[
K_l=\alpha_lI_{H_l}.
\]

Taking traces yields

\[
(2l+1)\alpha_l
=
\operatorname{tr}(C_l^\dagger C_l)
=
\operatorname{tr}(C_lC_l^\dagger)
=
2l+3.
\]

Therefore

\[
\sum_a C_{l,a}^\dagger C_{l,a}
=
\frac{2l+3}{2l+1}I_{H_l}.
\]

## Spin-weighted endpoint curvature

The endpoint constructor is the normalized transfer of Cartan multiplication:

\[
J_l=c_lR_{l+1}C_lR_l^{-1},
\qquad
c_l^2=\frac{2(2l+1)}{l+1},
\]

where the maps \(R_l\) are unitary under the authorized endpoint norms. Hence

\[
\sum_a J_{l,a}^\dagger J_{l,a}
=
\kappa_l I_{H_l},
\]

with

\[
\kappa_l
=
c_l^2\frac{2l+3}{2l+1}
=
\frac{2(2l+3)}{l+1}.
\]

The sequence decreases from \(6\) toward \(4\):

\[
\kappa_l=4+\frac{2}{l+1}.
\]

## Theorem-level distinction

The bare graded module and the dagger module answer different questions.

- In the bare module, \(c_l\) is presentation gauge.
- In the dagger module, \(\kappa_l\) is a unitary invariant measured by a
  canonical round trip.
- Supplying an arbitrary norm after seeing the desired coefficients would be
  authority laundering. The norm must come from the spin-weighted source
  construction.

Thus the deeper object required for numerical cross-grade comparison is not a
new carrier. It is a dagger enhancement of the Cartan module.

## Relation to higher coherence

The first nontrivial loop in the endpoint system is generated by adjunction,
not by the forward grade graph. This explains how an acyclic constructor tower
can acquire invariant curvature without contradicting scalar triviality.

The round-trip scalar is central because the axis sum restores full rotational
equivariance. A single fixed-axis round trip need not be scalar and should not
be substituted for the invariant sum.

## Scope

This theorem uses four explicit hypotheses:

1. the standard Hermitian structures on the irreducible harmonic spaces;
2. the orthogonal normalization \(C_lC_l^\dagger=I\);
3. an orthonormal complete axis basis in \(H_1\);
4. unitary spin-weight transfer \(R_l\).

Dropping any of these changes the numerical representative. The bare Cartan
algebra alone does not determine \(\kappa_l\).
