# Quarter-source interlacing Hall programme

## Question

Can all weighted Hall inequalities for interlacing Hurwitz source terms be proved at arbitrary source size?

## Problem

Finite max-flow feasibility does not identify an all-order transport mechanism. The proof must connect source-minor weights, interlacing neighborhoods, and a positive network or equivalent analytic certificate without promoting finite censuses to a uniform theorem.

## Bold conjecture

**Endpoint Hurwitz matrices admit source-derived positive network factorizations whose path composition induces a feasible interlacing transport for every source size.**

## Named rivals

1. A preregistered canonical source order—lexicographic, colexicographic, or sum-lex—reduces Hall transport either to interval cuts or to a monotone northwest-corner coupling.
2. Hall cuts reduce to ideals or filters in componentwise order.
3. A uniform local branching kernel allocates each negative source mass independently across its interlacing neighborhood; the capacity-proportional kernel is the first exact representative.
4. Local normalization fails, but a globally coordinated branching network exists and is governed by a source-derived recurrence; canonical augmenting paths are a bounded diagnostic of coordination, not the recurrence itself.
5. No combinatorial transport proof exists, but a positive-real or Gram certificate proves the necessary endpoint positivity.
6. Finite feasibility is accidental and fails at a larger source size.

## Risky consequences

The linear-order and poset reductions must survive every bounded graph if they are universal. A network backend must produce exact nonnegative factors, identify the source-derived coupling between endpoints, and prove rather than sample the all-order sign law. Any first counterexample disposes only the tested rival.

## Strongest falsification attempt and exact residual

The work is being regenerated from source under this single governing DPC case. Evidence backends are executable checkers with three-field packets; they do not instantiate new DPC cases. Deleted nonconformant artifacts and superseded events are excluded. The current residual remains the absent all-order coupling theorem.

## Disposition

Execute the named rivals as bounded evidence backends. Preserve exact counterexamples, stop a backend at its first stable residual, and move only to a distinct rival. The programme survives only at the strength independently re-established by regenerated checkers.

## Claim boundary

This packet defines the explanatory issue boundary and process discipline. It asserts no Hall theorem, network factorization, or endpoint stability by itself.
