# Quarter source determinants are finitely Hurwitz stable

## Question

Does a stability invariant survive the nonreal roots that obstruct rational linear splitting?

## Claim boundary

The exact Routh test covers source orders two through seven. It does not prove stability at arbitrary order or imply Jacobi positivity without a closure theorem for the condensation subtraction.

## Disposition

Every first-column entry of every exact Routh array is strictly positive. Hence all roots of each tested \(D_n(a)\), through degree 63, lie in the open left half-plane. Translation then gives the same bounded statement for nonnegative shift offsets. Hurwitz stability is preserved by factor division and explains why nonreal conjugate factors need not obstruct positivity on \(a\geq0\). The unresolved operation is subtraction in the condensation numerator. The next leaf is `quarter-condensation-hurwitz-pencil`, testing whether the two product terms remain in proper position along the exact difference pencil needed for stability closure.
