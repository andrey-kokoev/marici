# Task-first symmetry conjectures

These six statements were registered as open conjectures, not questions or established physical claims. The first now has a scoped finite-task witness; the four-discrete-value extraction is rejected for that task's exact readout, and the other four original conjectures remain open. A continuously decorated carrier is an explicit new successor.
The framing is inspired by explanatory and counterfactual criteria discussed
in this session; it is not attributed to David Deutsch as his statement or
endorsement. Five mathematical candidates depend on a task-first prerequisite.

## T — Independently specified explanatory task

A source-supported transport task fixes its input/output distinctions, retained
surroundings, admissible implementations and observational success criteria
independently of a desired symmetry group, and yields a discriminating
possibility/impossibility constraint that a preregistered rival fails.

Test: freeze those data, implementation/repeatability requirements and at least
one rival before computing a group. Derive a witness or obstruction, rather
than renaming a group already supplied by the chosen generators. Reject the
candidate if success needs retrospectively altered generators, readouts or
retained data. No particular physical task is asserted to have been selected.

## C — Task-relevant four-state carrier

The independently specified task admits a source-defined four-state extraction
whose states retain exactly the distinctions required at that comparison level.

Test: construct the extraction/readout, characterize its fibers and check its
operation compatibility and task sufficiency. Four joint sectors, four group
elements and four state values remain different types. Four is a falsifiable
candidate cardinality, not a mandated output; an extra necessary distinction
or merged task-distinct inputs refutes this candidate.

## L — Exact classification of lifted relabellings

The task-preserving invertible field intertwiners project onto a well-defined
subgroup of the extracted carrier's S4, with explicit witnesses for every
admitted permutation and obstructions for every excluded permutation.

Test all24 permutations under frozen preservation requirements. The subgroup
may be trivial; neither a nontrivial lift nor full S4 is assumed. Unequal whole
sector dimensions1,11,2,22 already prohibit their arbitrary permutation by
invertible linear sector maps. Nonuniqueness within a lift fiber must be retained.

## P — Relabelling equivalence of presentations

All relabellings of the extracted carrier extend to reversible comparisons
between correspondingly transported presentations of the same task, preserving
its success criterion and retained surroundings.

Test identity, inverse, composition and readout equivariance on actual decorated
presentations. This is stronger than renaming symbols and different from an
automorphism of a fixed decorated object. A fixed exterior or reference that
cannot be transported as required can falsify this candidate. Success does
not establish a new physical operation.

## I — Full-field interface coherence

Source-defined interfaces between the admitted presentations compose with
specified route comparisons that preserve the task's full retained information,
and those comparisons satisfy tetrahedral compatibility on shared boundaries.

Test an independently constructed triangular route and then a tetrahedral
boundary. Strict equality and allowed residual/holonomy are distinct contracts;
choose before testing. The known full-field PN geometric obstruction remains a
negative control. A higher comparison cannot erase an unaccounted residual.

## R — Task-complete readout family

The frozen, source-supported readout family separates every pair of admitted
transformations that differ in a task-relevant retained distinction, across
all admissible inputs and contexts of the declared domain.

Test separation or construct an indistinguishable task-distinct pair. Full
state recovery is not required when the task deliberately identifies states,
but that equivalence must be fixed first. Return-only readout already misses
the known PN relative-sector obstruction. No retrospective detector additions
are allowed to rescue the frozen candidate; a revised family is a new test.
