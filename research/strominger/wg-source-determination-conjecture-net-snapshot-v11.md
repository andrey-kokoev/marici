## Governing conjecture

**A declared source and certified parameter discipline determine a classified family of retained operations whose internal comparisons and higher witnesses compose coherently.**

The result may be a family of alternatives, not a uniquely selected policy or physical space. Four different obligations remain visible:

1. **Native admission:** derive a package with its actual native premises.
2. **Certified generation/source determination:** account for decisive parameter programs and derive the permitted alternatives/selection conditions.
3. **Coherent composition:** compare full operations and witnesses, preserving histories and the continuation interface.
4. **Realization and physical adequacy:** supply a structured action/model interface and independent tests. These are separate branches, not consequences of the first three.

Recursion/composition here is structural, not necessarily temporal. Counts of histories, parameters, vertices or invocations are not physical states, dimensions or rungs.
