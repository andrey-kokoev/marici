# Physical parity is exact two-chart transport of the puncture kernel

## Correction and result

For the invariant Green representative,

\[
 \widehat K^+_\xi
 =\frac{(\bar z-\bar\xi)(1+\bar z\xi)^2}
 {(z-\xi)(1+z\bar z)^3(1+\xi\bar\xi)}.
\]

Let `p(z)=-1/z` and move the source label simultaneously. The pullback of a
covariant `zz` component is

\[
 (\mathsf P_+K)(z;\xi)=z^{-4}K(p(z);p(\xi)).
\]

Exact simplification gives

\[
 \boxed{\mathsf P_+\widehat K^+_\xi=\widehat K^+_\xi.}
\]

The conjugate component obeys the corresponding identity with
`bar(z)^(-4)`. Thus the bi-kernel has ordinary spin-two transition functions
and no additional parity cocycle. The previously reported `xi^2/z^2` factor
came from the coordinate-dependent representative `S log|z-xi|^2` and is
superseded.

## 1. Module-level transport

For finite puncture set `P`, parity maps

\[
 \mathsf P:\mathcal H^{J,+}_{P}\longrightarrow
 \mathcal H^{J,+}_{p(P)}.
\]

Observation chart, source label, and source jets move together. Differentiated
generators transform by the chain rule; their triangular coordinate matrices
are the ordinary jet functor of one global section, not cocycle data. The
incidence divisor is preserved, and parity is involutive because `p^2=id` and
the spin-two Jacobians multiply to one.

## 2. Helicity is separate

Complex conjugation exchanges

\[
 \widehat K^+_\xi\longleftrightarrow\widehat K^-_\xi
 =\frac{(z-\xi)(1+z\bar\xi)^2}
 {(\bar z-\bar\xi)(1+z\bar z)^3(1+\xi\bar\xi)}.
\]

The completed packet therefore carries commuting operations: antipodal
chart/source transport preserving helicity, and helicity conjugation
exchanging the two spin components. Electric/magnetic parity readouts may
combine these ports later, but that projection is not chart gluing.

## 3. Centered chart

The exterior exponents of `Khat_0` are `(-n-4,-n-2)`. Spin-two inversion
maps `(p,q)` to `(-p-4,-q)`, hence to `(n,n+2)`, exactly the north-chart
expansion of the same global kernel. Neither one-sided Laurent lane is parity
closed; the two-chart section is. This is a chart boundary, not a kernel.

## 4. Engine consequences

- Magnetic tower zero columns are not generated by a physical parity cocycle.
- `E_1` and `E_2` are not failures of physical chart transport.
- A physical exceptional class must arise later: at readout projection,
  conservation quotient, collision specialization, contour pairing, or
  grade-three rank loss.
- The first-nonfaithful-arrow diagnostic begins after the completed two-chart
  module, whose parity transport is faithful.
