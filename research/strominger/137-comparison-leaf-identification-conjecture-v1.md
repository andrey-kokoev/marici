electron describing where the proton can be “next,” are the 137 structured
comparison leaves of the candidate field-slot Marici tower.

than another retained interface, represents the proposed available connections.

## Relation to the operator's 144 minus 7 proposal

The preceding proposal was: symmetric proton/electron reachability gives twelve
possibilities on each side, hence 144 connections, with seven required to keep
fibration reversible and 137 available connections remaining.

The atlas instead supplies the typed accounting

\[
144-23+16=137.
\]

Its 23 excluded arrow pairs touch a marked reference; its 16 added entries are
state-fiber pairs. The net difference is seven, but this arithmetic alone does
not identify seven particular connections or prove a seven-connection minimum
for reversibility. The 144-minus-7 mechanism remains an additional open claim,
not an established explanation of the atlas construction. Neither formulation
is silently substituted for the other.

## Acceptance and falsification

Acceptance requires a specified connection model and an explicit correspondence
with the atlas leaves preserving its incidence, reference roles and reversible
recovery. If maximality is claimed, show both that 137 is attained and that a
larger admissible connection domain is excluded under the same source rules.

A decisive rejection of a fixed proposed correspondence would be a required
connection absent from the leaves, a leaf with no admissible connection role,
or identification of two connections that the proposed domain must distinguish.
A changed count under the same independently admitted rules would reject the
claimed capacity. Failure to find a correspondence alone is not a refutation.

No exact connection-to-leaf map or executable attempt is frozen here. The
conjecture is recorded for formulation, not presented as a ready proof task.
