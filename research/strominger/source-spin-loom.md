## Correctly typed comparisons

U_ij maps the two-component fitting fiber i to fiber j, with U_ji=U_ij*.
The loop comparison

    K_ijk = U_ik^-1 U_jk U_ij

is an endomorphism of fiber i. K_jkl instead lives at j. Equal matrix sizes DO
NOT permit multiplying those maps as if their base were the same.

For four distinct fittings i,j,k,l, expand U_kl U_jk U_ij in its two groupings:

    U_il K_ikl K_ijk
      = U_il K_ijl (U_ij^-1 K_jkl U_ij).

Thus the required covariant tetrahedral comparison is

    K_ikl K_ijk = K_ijl U_ij^-1 K_jkl U_ij.

This is an exact identity for any invertible edge maps with the K defined above.
Nontrivial loop curvature is permitted. Matrix associativity and the derived
comparison data fill this cell; it is not independently tunable as in the toy.
The finite calculation checks the actual geometric maps and their types.
