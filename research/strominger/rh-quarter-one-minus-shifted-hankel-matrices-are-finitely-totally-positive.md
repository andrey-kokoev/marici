# One-minus-shifted quarter Hankel matrices are finitely totally positive

## Question

Does the second Hankel family required by a measure supported on \([0,1]\) have the full bounded minor sign pattern?

## Claim boundary

The result covers matrix size seven and shifts zero through four. It neither constructs a representing measure nor proves all-order total positivity.

## Disposition

All 17,155 exact minors of

\[
\bigl(m_{i+j}-m_{i+j+1}\bigr)_{0\leq i,j<7}
\]

are strictly positive; a row-reversal control is negative. Together with the ordinary Hankel result, both bounded matrix families have strict total positivity. Further cutoff enlargement would test the same finite factor and is stopped. The next leaf is `quarter-cross-ratio-source-network-linearization`: either construct positive source-level path weights whose Lindström minors yield both families, or identify the first algebraic obstruction caused by the determinant-ratio normalization.
