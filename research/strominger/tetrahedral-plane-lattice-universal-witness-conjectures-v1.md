## Conjecture 1 — space from the tetrahedral plane lattice

The intended space is constructed as in the working example: four families of
parallel planes with tetrahedrally related normals, equal repeat spacing, and
their intersection structure. The proposed lattice has equal elementary edge
lengths and equal angles of corresponding types. The finite disks, clipping,
camera, and shaded tetrahedron are display choices, not the space's definition.

For the example's nondegenerate setting, write d > 0 and

    n0 = ( 1, 1, 1)/sqrt(3)
    n1 = (-1,-1, 1)/sqrt(3)
    n2 = (-1, 1,-1)/sqrt(3)
    n3 = ( 1,-1,-1)/sqrt(3).

The displayed families sample the planes

    n_i . x = d + 4 d k,     k in Z, i = 0,1,2,3.

The unbounded lattice idealization extends these families beyond the finite
repeat count and disk radius of the viewer. At d = 0 the example degenerates;
that display setting does not define the nondegenerate lattice.

These equations specify the reference geometry, not a proof of its proposed
role. Four families must not silently be replaced by a filled 4-simplex or by
four independent Euclidean coordinates. Conversely, no interpretation of the
operator's proposed higher-dimensional construction/measurement structure is
settled merely by the viewer's three-dimensional coordinates.

## Conjecture 2 — universal construction and measurement by witnessing

Operator's proposed procedure, preserving its order and self-reference:

1. **Pick any place in the lattice; keep it steady.**
2. **Witness itself, using the same procedure as step 4. That is the proton.**
3. **Pick any other place.**
4. **In codimension one, check its 12 reachable endpoints against the 12
   endpoints of the proton in the codomain.**

Conjecture: this is a universal construction and measurement procedure on the
proposed lattice. The proton is the self-witnessed fixed reference produced by
the same comparison procedure, not a separate preassigned solid or an external
reference object inserted by a different construction.

“Proton” here records the proposed identification. It is not a conclusion that
physical proton properties have been derived.

### Structure to retain when formalizing

- The choice of reference place is arbitrary; it is then held steady.
- Self-witnessing uses step 4, rather than an unrelated initialization rule.
- Comparison with another place is made in codimension one.
- The reference's 12 endpoints are in the codomain of that comparison.
- Both twelve-endpoint structures must be retained; replacing them with a
  scalar, a mean, or an unlabelled shape is not the stated procedure.

### Unspecified details — do not silently supply them

The following are to be fixed by the later test, not resolved in this record:

- What counts as a “place”: vertex, cell, point, or another marked local object.
- The reachability rule and why it supplies exactly 12 endpoints.
- The ambient object relative to which the comparison has codimension one.
- The domain/codomain map, endpoint labels, and meaning of “check against”.
  In particular, this does not yet select either 12 matched comparisons or
  all 144 ordered endpoint pairs.
- How self-witnessing is defined without circular initialization, and precisely
  what is held steady during it.
- Which outputs make the procedure a measurement, and the scope meant by
  “universal”.

The earlier cone/link (more generally join/face-link) proposal is a candidate
mechanism to test against this procedure. It is not assumed to identify the
codimension-one operation already, nor does it replace the operator's four
steps.

## Conjecture 3 — domain–codomain–witness and lower-rung dynamics

Operator's further proposal: the trio **domain–codomain–witness** is the
underlying structure and dynamics of many lower-rung projections.

Start with an arrow carrying **a point and a direction**. These two roles are
themselves dual. Under the proposed Hamiltonian projection:

| Source role | Hamiltonian manifestation |
|---|---|
| Point | Position |
| Direction | Momentum |
| Realization | Time |

Thus position, momentum and time are proposed manifestations of the relational
construction, rather than independently chosen primitives supplied before it.
The realization direction is not privileged in advance: in the current lattice
proposal, the selected cell relation determines the local witnessing interface.

The point/direction duality and the domain–codomain–witness trio are retained as
the operator's claims. This record does not silently equate direction alone
with a fully normalized momentum, choose a symplectic form or Hamiltonian, or
identify a geometric plane coordinate with a calibrated clock. Those are parts
of the proposed projection to specify and test, not extra assumptions to insert
into its statement.

## Conjecture 4 — general relativity as lattice signal propagation

Operator proposal: **general relativity concerns signal propagation in this
lattice**.

Together with Conjecture 3, the proposed division is:

- **Hamiltonian mechanics:** evolution of a local point–direction state through
  realization.
- **General relativity:** signal propagation between such local structures,
  defining causal order, elapsed time and effective spacetime geometry.

Signal propagation and clock comparisons determine the effective spacetime
metric. The gravitational dynamics are proposed as a feedback relation:
**matter/energy changes the propagation geometry, and that geometry governs
subsequent propagation**.

The propagation, clock-comparison and feedback laws are to be constructed from
the lattice procedure. No particular metric, continuum limit or Einstein field
equation is asserted to have been derived by recording this conjecture.
