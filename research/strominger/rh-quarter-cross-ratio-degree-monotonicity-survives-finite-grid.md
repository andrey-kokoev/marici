# Quarter cross-ratio degree monotonicity survives the finite grid

## Question

Do the shifted cross ratios decrease strictly with determinant degree?

## Claim boundary

The exact recurrence test covers degrees two through forty and shifts zero through twelve. It does not prove monotonicity at all degrees; strict total positivity alone does not immediately compare different minor sizes.

## Disposition

Every one of the thirteen exact profiles satisfies

\[
\Theta_{n+1,a}<\Theta_{n,a}
\]

at every tested degree, and every successive ratio is below one. All five preregistered gates pass. The result is finite evidence for degree monotonicity, not a theorem. The next leaf is `quarter-cross-ratio-neville-product`, rewriting the determinant curvature as products or ratios of positive Neville data to isolate the all-degree comparison inequality.
