# Completed physical source category for punctured radiative data

## 1. Objects

Let `P=(xi_1,...,xi_n)` be an ordered configuration of distinct points of
`S^2`.  A label is retained even when two points later approach a collision.
For an integer jet bound `J>=0`, define the free real source packet

\[
 \mathsf H_P^J
 =\bigoplus_{i=1}^n\bigoplus_{r+s\le J}
 \left(
 \mathbb R e^{\rm flux}_{i,r,s}
 \oplus\mathbb R e^{\rm spin}_{i,r,s}
 \right),
\]

with the conjugate orbital components carried as a real two-component pair.
The generator `e_(i,r,s)` means a source-derived derivative of order `(r,s)`
at the named puncture; it does not mean an independently selected Laurent
coefficient.

The source realization is generated by

\[
 \widehat K^+_\xi(z,\bar z)
 =\frac{(\bar z-\bar\xi)(1+\bar z\xi)^2}
 {(z-\xi)(1+z\bar z)^3(1+\xi\bar\xi)},
 \qquad
 K^-_\xi=\sigma K^+_\xi,
\]

their source derivatives

\[
 \widehat K^{\pm}_{\xi;r,s}
 =\partial_\xi^r\partial_{\bar\xi}^s\widehat K^\pm_\xi,
\]

and the PSZ distributional generators

\[
 \partial_\xi^r\partial_{\bar\xi}^s\delta^2(z-\xi),
 \qquad
 \partial_\xi^r\partial_{\bar\xi}^s
 \partial_{\bar\xi}G(\xi;z).
\]

The physical realization map is kept distinct from the free labelled packet:

\[
 \mathsf S_P^J:\mathsf H_P^J
 \longrightarrow
 \mathcal D'(S^2;\operatorname{Sym}^2T^*S^2)
 \oplus\mathcal D'(S^2).
\]

This prevents a coincident-support relation in the target from silently
erasing source labels.

## 2. Completion and topology

The completed source is the strict LF union

\[
 \boxed{\mathsf H_P^{\rm fin}=\varinjlim_J\mathsf H_P^J.}
\]

Every element has finite puncture support and finite jet order.  A set is
bounded only if it lies in one finite-dimensional stage and is bounded there.
This is the smallest completion containing every finite collision jet while
excluding arbitrary infinite jets and arbitrary Laurent coefficient strings.

The realization uses the strong distribution topology on bounded jet-order
subspaces.  Within a fixed collision-free stratum, source positions and
coefficients vary smoothly.  A sequence whose jet order tends to infinity is
not convergent merely because each finite Laurent coefficient converges.

There are therefore three different notions which must not be conflated:

1. rational equality of global puncture kernels;
2. convergence in the distribution topology at fixed bounded order;
3. coefficientwise convergence of one Laurent chart.

Only the first two are physical source notions.

## 3. Allowed distributions

The category admits:

- smooth Bondi shear/news satisfying the frozen boundary conditions;
- finite sums of the source-derived puncture kernels;
- finite derivatives of their pole and delta distributions;
- finite collision jets obtained as distributional limits of labelled
  punctures;
- the fixed sphere zero-mode subtraction accompanying the Green kernel.

It excludes unless a new source law is supplied:

- freely prescribed principal parts;
- infinite-order delta jets;
- independent Laurent monomials or arbitrary Laurent sequences;
- fractional local orders and branched-cover deck sectors;
- punctures without labelled flux/angular-momentum records;
- removal of the Green zero-mode background.

## 4. Local support filtration

At a fixed puncture, `partial_xi^r Khat_xi^+` has holomorphic pole order
`r+1`, with leading principal coefficient

\[
 r!\frac{\bar z-\bar\xi}
 {(1+z\bar z)(1+\xi\bar\xi)}\bigg|_{z=\xi}.
\]

Thus increasing jet order enlarges a support filtration by principal-part
order.  For distinct punctures the germs are support-separated.  The
filtration, not a Laurent depth cutoff, is the physical finite-stage
coordinate.

The labelled packet can still have a realization kernel on a collision
stratum if insufficient jet ports are retained.  Such a kernel belongs to
the realization/readout map; it is not quotiented at the definition of the
source.

## 5. Collision completion

For a test function `phi`, a displaced delta has the Taylor expansion

\[
 \langle\delta_{\xi+\epsilon},\phi\rangle
 =\sum_{r+s\le J}
 \frac{\epsilon^r\bar\epsilon^s}{r!s!}
 (\partial^r\bar\partial^s\phi)(\xi)
 +\operatorname{Rem}_{J+1}.
\]

Consequently a family of labelled punctures whose lower moments cancel can
converge, after the corresponding rescaling, to a derivative-of-delta jet.
The moment vector is the collision boundary coordinate.  Naively setting all
positions equal before taking moments destroys this information.

## 6. Morphisms

Admitted morphisms are generated by:

- permutation of labels together with their full packets;
- inclusion `H_P^J -> H_P^(J+1)`;
- smooth motion inside a collision-free configuration stratum;
- source-derived parity/helicity transport;
- collision specialization into the jet-completed boundary;
- the physical realization, constraint, Green, and readout maps derived in
  subsequent packets.

Deletion of a label, projection away from a jet, or truncation of a Laurent
tail is not an inclusion morphism.  Each is an explicit quotient/effect
selection and may create a kernel.

## 7. Falsifier

The definition fails if a source-derived history requires an infinite-order
jet as one state, or if the physical parity/constraint operator sends a
finite-order generator to genuinely infinite order.  The next two moves test
exactly those closure conditions.
