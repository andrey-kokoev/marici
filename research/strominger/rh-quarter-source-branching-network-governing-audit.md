# Quarter-source branching-network governing audit

## Question

Which preregistered source-network rivals survive one integrated falsification boundary: canonical monotone transport, independent local branching, or globally coordinated branching?

## Claim boundary

Across the same 769 exact bounded Hall cases, lexicographic, colexicographic, and sum-lex monotone couplings succeed in 217, 295, and 217 cases; the capacity-proportional local kernel succeeds in 86; canonical lexicographic Edmonds–Karp flow succeeds in all 769 and uses reverse residual edges in 108. Reverse-edge use describes that canonical algorithm only and does not prove every forward-only allocation impossible. These finite results do not construct an all-order network or source recurrence.

The direct-coupling, sign-gauge, rectangular-lift, and lex-only packets are excluded from the governing evidence chain. The canonical-order, local-kernel, and augmenting-path computations are evidence backends under this packet, not separate DPC cases.

## Disposition

Reject the three canonical monotone couplings and the capacity-proportional local kernel. Retain canonical globally coordinated max-flow as bounded constructive evidence. The sole surviving research residual is a source-derived all-order recurrence for branching weights or augmenting choices and termination.
