# Quarter upper interval-avoiding source-mass dominance

## Question

What exact cancellation inequality remains for upper terminal minors whose base avoids the open terminal interval?

## Claim boundary

The census covers all 769 upper-oriented interval-avoiding cases of the order-eight transfer. It does not provide an all-order injection or uniform quantitative margin.

## Disposition

Orient every nonzero Jacobi--Cauchy--Binet source summand by the required terminal sign and write its positive and negative masses as \(P_+\) and \(P_-\). Every case is genuinely mixed-sign, but exact arithmetic gives

\[
P_+>P_-
\]

in all 769 cases. The worst bounded ratio is attained at base \(\{5,7\}\), terminals \((0,4)\), and is extremely close to one. Thus a coarse uniform margin is not indicated; the all-order theorem should construct a weight-preserving injection or telescoping pairing from negative source terms into positive ones. The next leaf is `quarter-source-negative-positive-mass-injection`.
