# Barnes multiplicities are not identifiable from the amplitude

## Question

Can the Barnes arguments and signed multiplicities be recovered from

\[
\prod_r a_r^{\varepsilon_r}=rac{1575}{104}?
\]

A prime-supported witness is

\[
(3,+1),(3,+1),(5,+1),(5,+1),(7,+1),
(2,-1),(2,-1),(2,-1),(13,-1).
\]

Its signed product is

\[
\frac{3^2 5^2 7}{2^3 13}=rac{1575}{104}.
\]

This witness is not identifiable from the scalar product. For every positive rational \(r\), adjoining

\[
(r,+1),(r,-1)
\]

leaves the product unchanged. Factor redistributions such as replacing one argument by several arguments with the same product generate further inequivalent lists. Therefore the amplitude supplies one multiplicative constraint, not a Barnes decomposition.

The required source datum is the full parameter-dependent constant \(K(t)\), modulo affine functions and Barnes identities. Its divisor or shift equation must be derived from the determinant before multiplicities can be assigned.

## Disposition

Resolve multiplicity extraction as underdetermined from amplitude alone. The next leaf is `quarter-barnes-constant-shift-equation`: derive a functional equation for \(K(t+1)-K(t)\) directly from the common-shift determinant quotient.

## Claim boundary

The prime-supported list is a witness to the scalar factorization, not a claimed determinant asymptotic. No listed Barnes factor has source authority yet.
