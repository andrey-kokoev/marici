## Why this matters to the programme

The first informative coverage object is not a list of group names. It is a diagram of source contracts and their action representations, recording which extra marking, relation, parameter programme or realization policy changes the represented sector.

There is already a concrete example of this principle: the S4/point/orientation decoration square explains the relation between S3, A4 and C3 without claiming that pointing selects orientation. Likewise the native pointed finite sector is a 1-type and cannot faithfully receive the Hopf class of the distinct free-simplicial benchmark under the proposed degree-preserving comparison. That is a scoped comparison obstruction, not a global limit on WG.

The recovered Layer-3 action theorem also corrects the impression that only a separate retained-comparison record supplies an action notion. WG already has witnessed-edge payload movement and history execution. What is not automatic is generation of that payload movement or its laws from the fixed source rather than supplied interpretation data.
