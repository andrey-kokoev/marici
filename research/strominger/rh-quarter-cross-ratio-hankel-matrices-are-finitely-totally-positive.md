# Quarter cross-ratio Hankel matrices are finitely totally positive

## Question

Does the positive moment sign pattern extend from principal Hankel determinants to every bounded nonprincipal minor?

## Claim boundary

The result covers matrix size seven and shifts zero through four. It does not construct an all-order path network or prove infinite Hankel total positivity.

## Disposition

All 17,155 exact minors are strictly positive, across every minor size one through seven; a row reversal is negative. Thus the finite Hankel matrices are strictly totally positive, strengthening the principal-determinant and Motzkin evidence. The original determinant-ratio formula for \(\Theta_{n,a}\) still lacks a positive linearization into source-network path weights, so no all-order network is promoted. The next executable leaf is `quarter-one-minus-shifted-hankel-total-positivity`, testing the second matrix family required for support in \([0,1]\).
