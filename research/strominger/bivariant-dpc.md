# Bivariant distinction-preserving configuration

The \(3+2+1\) double-category architecture becomes a DPC defect signature

\[
(I,C,M,H).
\]

These coordinates are typed witnesses, not Boolean flags. \(B\) carries the
lost distinction, \(F\) the unavailable residual capability, \(M\) the
commutator or comparison residual together with its coefficient domain, and
\(H\) the oriented pasting cocycle. A coordinate is discharged when a
source-authorized repair or higher cell disposes of its witness; the residual
need not be algebraically zero.

Here \(I\) is contravariant invariance loss, \(C\) is covariant composability loss,
\(M\) is a missing or anomalous mixed comparison square, and \(H\) is failure
of higher pasting coherence.

The fillers are typed and non-substitutable. Their prerequisites are logical
boundary dependencies, not temporal precedence:

- \(R_I\) witnesses contravariant invariance;
- \(R_C\) witnesses covariant composability;
- \(C_2\) supplies a source-derived comparison between the two orders;
- \(C_3\), and higher cells when required, make comparison cells cohere on
  larger nerves.

The first tempting theorem is false. Let

\[
P=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
Q=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Both are unimodular, hence each individually preserves every integral
distinction. Nevertheless

\[
PQ-QP=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}\ne0.
\]

Therefore \(I=C=0\) does not imply \(M=0\). A mixed cell is independent data
and requires independent source authority.

The second tempting theorem is also false: six individually typed face cells
need not have zero oriented sum around a cube. Their residual is an \(H\)
defect requiring a 3-cell.

The magnetic system supplies the third falsifier. Its curvature comparison
\(2(g+2)/(g+1)I\) exists over \(\mathbb Q\) but not over the ambient integral
lattice for \(g\ge2\). Rational mixed closure does not descend automatically.

The corrected DPC theorem is therefore partial:

> A configuration is distinction-preserving exactly when contravariant
> invariance and covariant composability have their own typed witnesses,
> every mixed boundary has a source-derived comparison cell, and every
> admitted pasting nerve has source-derived higher coherence.

Endpoint equality, objectwise rank, or pairwise cells cannot substitute for
these gates.
