# Factorial target factorization

The raw integral readout is source-defined: after clearing the Green
denominator it maps into the free coefficient lattice \(Y_g\). No
divided-power port normalization is part of that definition.

Nevertheless every matrix entry is divisible by \(g!\), so there is a unique
integral factorization

\[
S_g\xrightarrow{N_g}Y_g\xrightarrow{\,g!\,}Y_g,
\qquad M_g=g!N_g.
\]

Equivalently, \(M_g\) factors through the sublattice \(g!Y_g\). This
factorization preserves the kernel exactly. For the alternate chart of order
\(n=g+10\), its image index decomposes as

\[
[Y_g:M_g(S_g)]
=(g!)^{g+10}[Y_g:N_g(S_g)].
\]

This separates universal derivative multiplicity from the residual arithmetic
index. The factorization is mathematically canonical once \(M_g\) is given,
but it does not authorize an instrument to report \(N_g\)-coordinates.
Executable divided-power ports would require a calibration constructor.
Thus this is invariant closure without executable closure.
