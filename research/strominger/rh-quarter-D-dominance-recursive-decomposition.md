# D-dominance recursive decomposition

## Question

Does the two-summand recurrence reduce dominance to separately nonnegative square and cross terms?

## Claim boundary

The regenerated checker verifies the exact decomposition in 21 cases and verifies square-term nonnegativity, while exhibiting negative coefficients in the separate cross term. The naive termwise proof is therefore invalid.

## Disposition

Reject separate cross-term positivity. Any surviving recursive proof must preserve cancellation or use a different positive certificate.
