# Quarter source matrices are strictly totally positive

## Question

Does all-size positivity of contiguous minors promote to strict total positivity of every finite source matrix?

## Claim boundary

The theorem concerns the quarter determinant kernel at nonnegative integer shifts. It does not establish asymptotic limits or total positivity for unrelated parameter sets.

## Disposition

The planar-network argument proves every solid minor positive at arbitrary size and start. In particular, it proves both initial-minor families required by complete Neville elimination: consecutive rows with the first columns, and the first rows with consecutive columns. The initial-minor criterion therefore makes every finite quarter source matrix strictly totally positive. Exact size-eight checks verify 288 initial minors and 35,536 bounded arbitrary minors; a row reversal is negative. The next leaf is `quarter-cross-ratio-global-unit-interval-proof`, using strict total positivity and condensation to prove \(0<\Theta_{n,a}<1\) and recurrence division validity for all degrees and shifts.
