# Quarter cross ratios pass finite Hankel moment tests

## Question

Do the shifted cross-ratio sequences satisfy positive-moment conditions stronger than signed finite differences?

## Claim boundary

The test covers Hankel sizes one through five, starting degrees two through eight, and shifts zero through twelve. It neither proves an all-order Hausdorff representation nor constructs a measure.

## Disposition

All 455 exact Hankel determinants and all 455 corresponding one-minus-shifted Hankel determinants are strictly positive. A row-reversal control is negative. Together with finite complete monotonicity, this supports a positive Hausdorff-moment model for the degree sequence. The next leaf is `quarter-cross-ratio-bernstein-measure-reconstruction`, using exact signed differences as finite Bernstein weights to test whether the putative measure has the endpoint profile required for inverse-square decay.
