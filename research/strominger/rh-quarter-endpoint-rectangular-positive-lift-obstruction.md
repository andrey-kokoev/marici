# Quarter endpoint rectangular positive-lift obstruction

## Question

Can a rectangular nonnegative lift repair the direct endpoint coupling?

## Claim boundary

The exact direct coupling `T=A^{-1}B` has a negative entry. For every finite inner dimension, a product `UV` of entrywise nonnegative rectangular matrices is entrywise nonnegative. Therefore `T=UV` is impossible for this entire factorization class. This does not exclude a common source network whose endpoint maps are separately positive, because recovering `T` may then require an inverse with signs.

## Disposition

Reject every direct rectangular nonnegative factorization of `T`. Retain only the structurally distinct common-network construction formed before endpoint specialization.
