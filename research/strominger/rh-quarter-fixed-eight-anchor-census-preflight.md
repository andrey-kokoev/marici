# Fixed-eight Gram anchor census stops at an absent product relation

## Question

Can the retained `k=8` source backend supply a quadratic-product connectivity graph and orientation anchors?

## Claim boundary

No such graph is present. The retained evidence contains 12,869 exact transfer identities, 3,584 oriented terminal cases, and explicit mixed-sign summands; the first has two positive and one negative nonzero term. It records neither source-declared quadratic-product edges between labelled terms nor nonvanishing orientation probes. Co-occurrence in a signed sum is not a quadratic product and cannot be promoted to a Gram edge. Hence even the bounded anchor-component census is undefined from current source authority. This does not refute a separately derived quadratic relation.

## Disposition

Block the bounded Gram-anchor branch at its first missing typed object: a source-derived quadratic-product relation plus one nonvanishing orientation probe per connected component. Do not fabricate that relation from term co-occurrence. Reallocate to the executable signed-source alternative: test whether mixed-sign terminal summands admit a source-labelled cancellation pairing that proves the oriented sum without quotienting signs.
