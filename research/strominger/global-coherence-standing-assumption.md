# Standing assumption: global construction, local verification

Status: operator-directed research assumption, not a proved impossibility theorem. The operator's clarification governs: local reader rules may serve as verification constraints only, not as a construction method.

## Governing rule

Construct the whole object from global coherence conditions. Do not construct it by extending locally admissible reader steps and postponing global closure until the end.

Local reader rules may reject a proposed global object or verify its restrictions. They do not generate the object, establish its existence, or supply missing global attachment operations. Passing all local checks is necessary where those checks apply, but is not sufficient evidence of global coherence.

## Consequences for the current sector work

- Retain existing local constructors, atlas enumerations and native-operation graphs as diagnostic and verification instruments, not as the authoritative construction grammar.
- The 679-cell atlas establishes local coverage, not global existence. The 176-cell root component diagnoses the tested local grammar; its reachability is not a necessary construction criterion for a globally defined object.
- Do not resume the previous plan of adding a local inter-plane reader command as the route to constructing the whole assembly. An inter-plane relation must instead be supplied or derived within a globally coherent construction and then checked locally.
- Specify the global object, simultaneous compatibility conditions, closure requirements and retained state/history data before selecting local readings. Existence of a solution to those global conditions remains an obligation, not an assumption supplied by this policy.
- Derive local readings as restrictions of the global construction and apply the existing native checks to them. Any mismatch remains an explicit unresolved constraint; do not erase it or weaken a rule merely to obtain closure.

This directive supersedes the construction strategy in `symmetric-sector-phase-atlas-v1.md` and subsequent chat proposals. It does not invalidate the recorded finite computations or assert that local reader rules mathematically do not exist.
