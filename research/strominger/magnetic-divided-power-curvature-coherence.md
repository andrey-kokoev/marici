# Divided-power transport requires a curvature cell

The fold recursion supplies successive raising maps, but the magnetic readout
places a lowering operation after the chain. Their source-derived comparison
law is

\[
B_{t+1}E_t-E_{t-1}B_t=2tI.
\]

For the normalized step

\[
\delta_g=\frac{1}{g+1}E_{g+2},
\]

the corresponding comparison residual is

\[
\frac{2(g+2)}{g+1}I.
\]

It is nonintegral for every \(g\ge2\). Therefore:

- over \(\mathbb Q\), divided-power transport and magnetic readout are related
  by a typed central curvature 2-cell;
- over the ambient integral harmonic lattice, that comparison cell does not
  exist;
- an integral version could exist only on a source-derived reachable
  submodule carrying the required divisibility, and its stability under both
  raising and lowering remains to be proved.

Thus grade-wise integrality of \(N_g=M_g/g!\) does not automatically assemble
into an integral coherent family. The next tower is genuinely needed: it must
carry the curvature comparison between normalized construction and readout.
