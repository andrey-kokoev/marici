# Quarter terminal Dodgson-condensation source induction

## Question

Does Desnanot--Jacobi condensation reduce terminal signs noncircularly to smaller bases and principal positivity?

## Claim boundary

The identity is general. The exact census covers all 3,584 oriented order-eight terminal cases. It does not prove the interval-avoiding cases or the lower-oriented magnitude inequality.

## Disposition

For \(q\in S\), \(T=S\setminus\{q\}\),

\[
D(S;i,j)P(T)=P(S)D(T;i,j)-D(T;q,j)D(T;i,q).
\]

There are 2,046 cases with \(q\) strictly between the terminals. Their 1,023 upper-oriented cross products oppose the target sign, so subtraction reinforces it and gives a noncircular induction from smaller-base terminal signs and principal positivity. The 1,023 paired lower-oriented cross products have the target sign and require a magnitude inequality. Another 1,538 cases have interval-avoiding bases and admit no internal pivot. The next branches isolate the upper interval-avoiding source theorem and the lower-versus-upper magnitude comparison.
