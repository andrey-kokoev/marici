# The Conductor Square Is a Primitive-Tail Phenomenon

The hypothesis that \(\kappa_C^2\) is a universal second-order intersection
multiplicity is false.

Replace the two tail letters in the Moore word by nonzero integral powers.  The
sixteen combinations with powers in \(\{-2,-1,1,2\}\) all continue to satisfy
the Moore deletion condition.  They therefore provide legal counterfactual
cycles inside the same free-group constructor grammar.

The primitive opposite-polarity cases retain

\[
1568=2\cdot28^2,
\qquad
2592=2\cdot36^2.
\]

But the opposite-polarity power-two cases produce cyclic target indices

\[
363,
\qquad
15987,
\]

neither of which is twice a square.  Mixed tail powers supply further failures,
and the uniform full-matrix content \(768\) also ceases to be stable.

Thus the square is not forced by Moore deletion geometry alone.  It belongs to
the primitive unit-tail stratum.  Any valid explanation must use the atomic
primitivity of both tail constructors; an argument expressed only through the
completed Moore cycle or its target intersection has forgotten essential source
data.
