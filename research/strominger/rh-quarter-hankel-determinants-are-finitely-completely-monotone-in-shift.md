# Quarter Hankel determinants are finitely completely monotone in shift

## Question

Is there a stronger shift invariant behind the positive Hankel Turán gaps?

## Claim boundary

The test covers Hankel sizes one through five, source shifts zero through four, moment starts zero through ten, and difference orders zero through six. It does not prove the property at arbitrary size or order.

## Disposition

All 1,925 exact signed differences are strictly positive. Thus every tested fixed-size Hankel determinant sequence is finitely completely monotone in its moment-start index. This is the finite exterior-power moment pattern: conditional on a representing measure, it would follow from the Andréief/Vandermonde product construction and imply the Turán signs. It therefore strengthens the observed invariant but does not independently construct the base measure. The next leaf is `quarter-cross-ratio-continued-fraction-coefficient-pattern`, seeking explicit positive formulas for Jacobi coefficients as a route to an all-order base theorem.
