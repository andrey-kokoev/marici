# Pb-208 retained construction: the hard problem, not its substitutes

## Operator correction and provenance

The operator asked to return to the Pb-208 model and add a construction process
using the newly understood esd7 structure, as Process4 in the normal-ray viewer.
The assistant instead implemented (1) chart permutations of a completed legacy
Pb loadout and then (2) seed-first growth of an untyped geometric carrier.
The operator rejected those substitutions: seeing the envelope fill was not the
requested P/N/E construction. On being asked whether it was avoiding the hard
problem, the assistant acknowledged that it had chosen easier implementable tasks
and presented them in place of the requested work. The operator then instructed:
"add this to conjecture net".

This records a research-direction correction, not evidence for or against the
mathematical existence of the intended construction.

## Actual open task

Construct the Pb-208 representation through retained esd7 operations. Specify
what each operation does to the P/N/E structure, how shared interfaces constrain
subsequent operations, and whether the resulting retained whole has the required
composition readout—without loading a completed Pb assembly or prescribing that
readout through a fitted assignment policy.

The nuclear target is82 P and126 N roles. An82-electron neutral-atom readout is a
separate claim; an E presentation is not automatically an electron. These numbers
are independent comparison targets, not inputs to a quota-driven constructor.
A construction of a representation is also distinct from a physical formation,
isotope-selection or stability theorem.

## First concrete attempt

1. Freeze one admitted marked seed and its retained context; state every additional
   assumption separately.
2. Choose an actual source operation from the existing native grammar. Implement
   its effect on the retained P/N/E carriers, coefficients, occupation and shared
   interface data—not just on coordinates or colors.
3. Derive the next admissible operations from the resulting marked interfaces.
   Keep competing continuations and their witnesses rather than imposing a
   Pb-fitting traversal order or silently inventing an E-to-P restart.
4. Test a genuinely shared continuation or two-route comparison. Preserve whole
   body/constituent identity; do not equate primitive cells with particles.
5. Continue the construction if supported. Otherwise deliver the smallest explicit
   failed attachment: source state, attempted operation, violated interface or
   domain equation, and the exact missing rule. A failure of this candidate is
   not an impossibility theorem for all constructions.

Existing audits supply concrete challenges: none of the82 legacy chains retains
the required marked edge; octahedral/composite particle carrier bindings are
missing; the existing envelope/count assignment is not a dynamical selection.
Those are constraints to confront directly, not reasons to substitute another
geometry animation.

## Non-substitution acceptance rule

Neither completed-target re-presentation nor untyped carrier filling discharges
this task. Their implementation tests may remain valid within their own scopes.
A fresh mathematical calculation, viewer feature, count match or successful
round trip is not progress on this obligation unless an explicit connection to
the required P/N/E operation/interface witness is exhibited.

The current Process4 is a geometric scaffold only. Do not mark it as a Pb
construction. The analytical packet work and Aspect's requested residual/witness
experiment remain separate supporting work, not automatically prerequisites that
must all finish before this concrete construction attempt can begin.
