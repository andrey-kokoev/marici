# No source-authorized charge lattice realizes the cross-grade affine seven

## Completion of the candidate audit

Two routes remained after separating the cross-grade affine discriminant from
the fixed-grade physical operator:

1. interpret the symbolic grade ladder as a physical charge-changing system;
2. quantize the 21 low harmonic modes and compare their occupation lattice
   with the contour observation frame.

Both fail source-authority tests.

## No physical cross-grade constructor

The Bondi \(G_{uz}\) constraint and its imaginary curl derive exactly the
grade-three word

\[
\mathcal A_3=\bar\eth\eth^3.
\]

The general family \(\mathcal A_{s,r}=\bar\eth\eth^r\) is a valid spectral
classification family.  It does not supply physical arrows

\[
Q_r\longrightarrow Q_{r+1}
\]

between charge sectors.  Changing the operator order or grade changes the
observable and introduces different curvature terms.  Therefore the grade
coordinate in

\[
v_g=F(g,1)^T
\]

is not an additive physical charge generator.

This rejects the cross-grade route unless a new source equation derives a
grade-changing operation and proves compatibility with the magnetic charge.

## Why the 21 harmonic modes do not supply the required lattice

Quantizing 21 bosonic modes gives occupation labels, but this does not produce
the required integral lattice of memory amplitudes.

- Occupation labels form \(\mathbb N^{21}\), a monoid rather than a charge
  group closed under subtraction.
- The linear shear and memory observables are quadratures built from creation
  and annihilation operators; they are not diagonal functions of occupation
  number.
- An \(m\)-resolved occupation basis depends on an axis choice.  The source
  canonically supplies the \(SO(3)\) representation blocks, not 21 independent
  scalar charge generators.
- The physical contour matrix has algebraic entries depending on contour
  geometry and normalization.  It is a faithful frame over characteristic
  zero, not an integral charge pairing.

Clearing denominators in one chosen contour presentation can create a Smith
form, but rescaling a single observation port changes that Smith form while
leaving the underlying real observation capability equivalent.  An integral
Smith index is therefore not physical until the source fixes primitive units
for both states and ports.

## No-go statement in the current source category

The current source category contains an independently quantized charge lattice
that couples to spin memory—particle helicity—but contains no
source-authorized span placing that lattice and the affine observation frame
inside one primitive integral carrier.  Moreover, the determinant-seven frame
is cross-grade, whereas the physical magnetic charge equation is fixed at
grade three and has primitive preferred observation vector \((13,16)\).

Consequently no presently authorized comparison has physical Smith index
seven.

This does not prove that every extension fails.  It identifies the minimal
new evidence that would reopen the result:

1. a source-derived grade-changing magnetic charge constructor whose integral
   matrix is \(F\); or
2. a fixed-grade integral state/port normalization yielding a new physical
   pairing matrix with Smith factor seven.

Until one is supplied, the affine seven is an arithmetic invariant of the
operator family, not a quantized physical charge residue.
