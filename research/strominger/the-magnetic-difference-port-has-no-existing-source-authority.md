# The magnetic difference port has no existing source authority

## Verdict

The algebraic comparison

\[
\omega(a,b)=R(b)-R(a)
\]

is not an operative magnetic port in the established constructor grammar.

The source constructs every individual grade, its integral response, and its unary Smith packet. It does not construct the typed composite

\[
(a,b)\longmapsto R(b)-R(a)
\]

because four additional capabilities would be required:

1. preparation of an ordered pair of independently labelled grades;
2. two Smith readouts with their grade provenance retained;
3. subtraction authorized in the valuation codomain;
4. transport of that comparison under constructor substitution.

## Why the existing joint observers do not suffice

The magnetic system has several paired objects, but each uses a different axis.

| constructor | paired axis | reason it does not supply \(\omega\) |
|---|---|---|
| \((C^n,C^{-n})\) | inverse responses at one grade | not two grades |
| route packet \((A,B)\) | reflected sheets | pre-Smith and same source |
| joint \((E,M)\) observer | reflection parity | pre-Smith and same source |
| puncture ports | support labels | different target type |
| electric–magnetic bridge | cross-parity control | absent, and still the wrong axis |

The distinction is structural. Smith valuation is nonlinear. A difference of two valuation packets is not the Smith readout of a source difference, and the source grammar does not even contain subtraction of free-group states as a constructor.

## Exact hostile

The grades (0) and (1) have the same packet:

\[
R(0)=R(1)=(1,2,3).
\]

After the positive generator acts, their comparison requires

\[
R(2)-R(1)=(1,3,4).
\]

No existing source or instrument port returns this ordered cross-grade residual.

## Consequence

The global coboundary is a coherent mathematical explanation of the mixed defect, but not an authorized lax repair. The present magnetic system therefore has:

- internally valid source construction;
- internally valid unary valuation observation;
- failed strict mixed reconciliation;
- no operative lax comparison cell.

The smallest missing constructor is a provenance-preserving binary grade-comparison port. Introducing it would extend the instrument grammar; it cannot be inferred from the existence of individual grade readouts.
