# The Conductor Square Is One Cyclic Target Defect

The hypothesis that \(\kappa_C^2\) splits into equal domain and target
saturation indices is false.

Let the full diagonal kernel and the lifted relational kernel generate a
rank-two plane in the full domain lattice.  The gcd of its primitive Plucker
coordinates is one in every signed C presentation.  Hence this plane is already
saturated: the domain contributes no conductor-sized index.

After dividing the full response by its uniform content \(768\), its
intersection with the primitive diagonal target has index

\[
2\kappa_C^2.
\]

Therefore the entire reduced modulus is carried by one cyclic quotient

\[
\mathbb Z/(2\kappa_C^2)\mathbb Z.
\]

This differs from two conductor channels even when their orders agree.  At a
prime dividing \(\kappa_C\), the cyclic quotient has one-dimensional mod-prime
generator space; a product of two conductor-bearing cyclic groups has dimension
two.

Thus the current lattice diagram contains neither two independent snake
channels nor two equal saturation defects.  The square is arithmetically
irreducible at this presentation level.  Any factorization of \(\kappa_C^2\)
requires a genuinely finer constructor, not a reinterpretation of the existing
kernel and target lattices.
