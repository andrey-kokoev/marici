# Puncture cycles supply the integral axis but gravitational periods are not compact

The completed physical engine already contains a source-derived integral

\[
H_1(S^2-P,\mathbb Z).
\]

positively oriented loop around the first puncture. The complete contour

missing finite Weyl theorem is not starting from no integral structure.

## The actual coefficient target

The reconstructed gravitational one-form has real periods. For two punctures,

\[
H^1_{\mathrm{dR}}(S^2-P)\cong\mathbb R.
\]

Let \(\eta=d\theta/(2\pi)\) on \(\mathbb C^*\), normalized by

\[
\oint_C\eta=1.
\]

The family

\[
A_t=\frac{2t}{7}\eta,
\qquad t=0,\ldots,6,
\]

has zero local curvature on the punctured domain and periods \(2t/7\). Hence
all contractible local curvature probes give the same result, while the
authorized contour period distinguishes the states.

Exponentiating the period gives

\[
\exp\left(2\pi i\oint_C A_t\right)
=\exp(2\pi i\,2t/7),
\]

which is exactly the affine-discriminant character.

## Where the construction fails to be a finite source sector

The map

\[
t\longmapsto \frac{2t}{7}
\]

is not a homomorphism from \(\mathbb Z/7\) to \(\mathbb R\): adding seven to
\(t\) changes the real period by two rather than returning to the same state.
This is another instance of the torsion-free no-go.

It becomes a well-defined injective homomorphism only after compactification:

\[
\mathbb Z/7
\longrightarrow
\mathbb R/\mathbb Z,
\qquad
t\longmapsto\frac{2t}{7}\pmod{\mathbb Z}.
\]

The present magnetic gauge quotient removes exact real one-forms. It does not
identify periods differing by integral values. The spin-memory contour is a
real time-delay observable, not a declared compact Wilson phase.

Therefore exponentiating the period is mathematically available but not yet a
source-authorized replacement for the real instrument.

## Symmetry audit

This construction does not require a rotation-invariant lattice inside every
nontrivial harmonic representation. The integral lattice belongs to puncture
homology and is transported with the ordered puncture configuration. The
source labels and sphere orientation select its generator.

What cannot be obtained from topology alone is compactness of the coefficient
period. Integral cycles do not imply integral gauge shifts.
