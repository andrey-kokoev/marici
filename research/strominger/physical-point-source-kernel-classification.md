# Corrected point-source kernel classification

For distinct punctures, complete local residue, principal-part, and delta-jet
ports are injective on the finite labelled magnetic source packet. The
arbitrary-jet theorem is proved in
`completed-distinct-puncture-kernel-classification.md`.

Ordinary contour periods are not equivalent to those complete local ports.
They retain only simple-pole residue cohomology, subject to the global sum
relation, and kill exact higher poles and delta derivatives. This quotient is
classified in `completed-local-to-contour-transform.md`.

At collisions, a label-blind field sees the sum of coincident packets. The
kernel and its restoration by tangent moments are classified in
`completed-collision-strata-classification.md`.

Thus:

- distinct complete local magnetic target: kernel zero;
- full source before magnetic projection: electric eigenspace kernel;
- ordinary periods: exact/higher-jet quotient kernel;
- selected contours: additional instrument kernel;
- collision specialization: moment-map kernel.

The old statement that all ordinary contours reconstruct every local jet is
withdrawn.
