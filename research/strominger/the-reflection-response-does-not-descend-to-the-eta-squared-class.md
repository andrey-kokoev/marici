# The Reflection Response Does Not Descend to the Eta-Squared Class

## Fixed-explanandum classification

The signed Moore-mutation census left open whether its \(32\) legal cycles
represented one homotopy class. Mikhailov's degree-four lower-central
calculation closes that gate.

Modulo Moore boundaries and Lie terms of weight at least five, the three
repeated-generator brackets are represented by Hall elements \(e_7,e_8,e_9\).
The source calculation proves

\[
e_7\equiv e_8\equiv e_9,
\qquad
2e_7=0,
\qquad
e_7\ne0.
\]

Consequently the quotient is \(\mathbb Z/2\), generated by any one of these
three brackets.

Every legal signed mutation in the census has a weight-four leading Lie term
equal to \(e_7\), \(e_8\), or \(e_9\), up to sign. Generator inversion changes
only that sign. It is invisible in the order-two quotient. Higher group-
commutator corrections have weight at least five and vanish in the quotient
used by the source theorem.

Therefore all \(32\) legal mutations represent the same nonzero eta-squared
class.

Primary authority: Roman Mikhailov, *Simplicial Constructions Associated with
S2*, Theorem 3 and its Hall-basis calculation of
\(Z_3/(B_3+L_3^5)\).

## Failure of descent

The same eta-squared class has at least three reflection-response profiles:

| Full depths | Relational depths | Multiplicity |
|---|---|---:|
| \((5,9,13)\) | \((7,12)\) | 16 |
| \((8,8,14)\) | \((8,14)\) | 8 |
| \((8,12,16)\) | \((11,12)\) | 8 |

Hence there is no well-defined map

\[
\pi_4(S^2)\longrightarrow
\{\text{reflection response matrices}\}
\]

assigning the computed matrix to the eta-squared residue alone.

The response is defined on a finer object:

\[
(\text{eta-squared class},\text{Milnor/Artin lift framing})
\longmapsto R.
\]

The repeated-generator incidence is part of that framing. Forgetting it keeps
the homotopy residue but loses the response type.

## Correction of language

The previously computed matrix, snake boundary, and two-primary barcode are
properties of the selected lift

\[
[[c_{31},c_{13}],[c_{31},c_{22}]],
\]

not invariants of eta-squared without qualification. The homotopy class is
presentation-invariant; the reflection response is lift-sensitive.

This does not invalidate the response theorem. It identifies its correct
domain and prevents authority from being transported through the filling
