Can the proposed

## 1. A genuine source distinction, not an imposed bit

Every actual pair decomposes into four joint sectors:

    both:           Q11=N
    local-only:     Q10=L-N
    alignment-only: Q01=G-N
    neither:        Q00=I-L-G+N.

Fresh exact source relations and joint-sector witnesses give:

| Candidate source | Both | L-only | G-only | Neither | Alignment alone gives return? |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Proton | 1 | 11 | 2 | 22 | no |
| Neutron | 1 | 2 | 11 | 22 | no |
| Electron | 2 | 2 | **0** | 4 | **yes: G=N** |

Thus proton/neutron are dual **incomparable** ordered pairs at this level. Electron is genuinely **nested**: `Image(G) subset Image(L)`, and the alignment image already is its return doublet. These facts survive coefficient-frame changes with transported metrics.

For the **full-raw endpoint task**, electron can omit L because `GL=G`; neither baryon can omit either stage. Actual nonzero L-only/G-only witnesses certify those failures. This gives a natural **absorbing-alignment preparation property**, rather than merely observing that the electron has rank2.

It does **not** yet derive a sector handoff or electron two-cycle. Applying the same return again remains idempotent.

## 2. Dual pair structure is not full proton/neutron identity

Swapping L and G exchanges the two baryon joint signatures. But fresh orbit/action reconstruction from their actual geometric sources gives generated groups of sizes **12** and **3**. Equal exchanged pair invariants do not identify the complete source objects, their geometry or enabled words. This iteration does not claim a native coordinate/operation isomorphism from the signature alone.

The general commuting-pair axioms also admit equality, either nesting orientation and incomparability with nonzero return. They do not select exactly these three physical species without additional source laws.

## 3. Pair data cannot intrinsically split the electron payload into M/C

On `Image(N)`, every element of the algebra generated only by `I,L,G` is scalar: all three generators restrict to identity.

More strongly, two actual noncommuting native electron spin operations, with their proper face permutations, preserve the source pair. Their logical commutant is one-dimensional: scalar identity. A natural labelled rank-one/rank-one sector partition of the active doublet cannot be recovered from those data.

Nor does allowing the native group to permute the two labels rescue this interpretation: the actual group is generated by its order-three elements, each of which must act trivially on a two-element set. Its two-label action is therefore trivial.

**Scope:** this excludes an intrinsic two-way *payload partition* equivariant under the retained electron symmetries. It does not exclude geometric ports, an additional packet context, a chosen mark or a richer source interface. We do not identify M/C with doublet coordinates by assumption.

## 4. Naturality alone does not uniquely select the prior handoff bit

Two frame-natural predicates fit the three fixture modes0/0/1:

    b_nest = [G=N]
    b_mult = [rank(N)>1].

They are not the same law. Construct a nonzero input v from the actual electron neither-sector. Add its normalized metric rank-one projection to G. The changed pair still has exactly the same N, active metric and restricted stage actions, but its joint dimensions become `(2,2,1,3)`. Now `b_nest=0` while `b_mult=1`.

A stronger control adds the **entire** neither-sector to G. Its joint dimensions become `(2,2,4,0)`, and all **24 original native spin/face actions remain symmetries**. N is still unchanged and the predicates still disagree. Native symmetry plus active return data therefore do not select between these proposed laws.

However, neither is silently the same full preparation source:

    original: Gv=0
    changed:  G'v=v.

The countermodels are active-return equivalent but **not full-reader-task equivalent**. They show why the generic packet's retained source/preparation interface matters. They do not make the original native G arbitrary, license replacing it, or refute a handoff rule derived from the complete unchanged assembly constructor.
