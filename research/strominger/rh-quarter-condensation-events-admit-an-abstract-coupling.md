# Quarter condensation events admit an abstract coupling

## Question

Can the separately normalized condensation events be placed on one probability space across degree?

## Claim boundary

The construction couples the binary event labels only. It does not lift transitions to determinant configurations, construct a Hausdorff representing measure, or supply physical selection.

## Disposition

For each shift, define an absorbing survival chain with transition

\[
r_{n,a}=\frac{\Theta_{n+1,a}}{\Theta_{n,a}}.
\]

Every tested transition for degrees two through twenty and shifts zero through twelve lies strictly between zero and one. Telescoping reconstructs every marginal exactly, while the transition ratios are nonconstant, excluding a homogeneous one-parameter kernel. Thus a common abstract coupling exists despite nonseparable entry normalization. The next leaf is `quarter-cross-ratio-condensation-coupling-source-lift`: construct a deletion/interlacing map between adjacent determinant ensembles whose event-label marginal is this kernel, or record the first configuration-level obstruction.
