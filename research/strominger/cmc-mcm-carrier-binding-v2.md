# Corrected proposed carrier binding: CMC[M] and MCM[C]

Operator clarification supersedes the earlier common-carrier fourth-vertex
assignment used by the marked-tetrahedron trial:

    proton = (C0,M0,C1)[M1],
    neutron = (M0,C1,M1)[C2].

The active triple and continuation vertex together define each four-vertex
carrier. The proton's final three vertices are the neutron's initial three:
M0,C1,M1 is their WHOLE shared face. Neutron's fourth is C2, not C0. C0 is
outside the neutron carrier. C2 remains the same retained fifth pentachoron
source identity; its unfolded display coordinate is not a new source vertex.

The existing two selected tetrahedral facets therefore receive the operator's
explicit proposed proton/neutron binding. The earlier UI labels 'P/N carrier'
and 'N/E continuation' are removed. Selecting P or N highlights its actual body,
and the details show the full bracketed quadruple. The default diagram contains
ONLY P and N. No electron MCC face, reverse arrow, identity-anchor leg, selection
button or old three-window trace is included in that view. C2 is N's continuation
vertex, not an electron assignment. The old electron record/trace is retained
only in the explicitly separate legacy chart views.

This corrects an assignment, not a proof of native equivalence. The earlier
native CMC/MCM stage/readout audit remains binding. Geometric placement and
identity retention do not by themselves derive particle identity, physical C/M
meaning or native operation admissibility. Historical trials/receipts are not
rewritten to claim they already used the corrected assignment.
