# Natural source-labelled cancellation groupings fail at fixed size eight

## Question

Do fixed `k=8` mixed-sign terminal summands admit an exact sign-coherent grouping by natural source-index statistics?

## Claim boundary

No tested one-statistic partition does. A fresh exact census covers 3,584 terminal cases and tests grouping by `sum(K)`, `min(K)`, `max(K)`, intersection with the base, symmetric-difference size from rows, and counts inside, below, or above the terminal interval. No grouping is successful. Bad oriented-group counts range from 2,727 to 18,310; the first obstruction already occurs for empty base and endpoints `(0,1)`. This refutes these eight grouping rules, not every possible source-labelled cancellation mechanism.

## Disposition

Reject natural one-statistic cancellation grouping as the fixed-eight sign certificate. The exact residual is widespread negative oriented groups, not a checker defect. A distinct executable rival remains: replace exact grouping by a positive-to-negative mass transport certificate ordered by source labels, and test whether cumulative dominance holds in every terminal case.
