# Distinction-preserving completion theorem

Let (Q:V\to W) be a quotient or presentation map and let (R:V\to D)
be the complete packet of distinctions required by a later repair. There is a
well-defined induced repair observation \(\bar R:W\to D\) with
\(R=\bar RQ\) exactly when

\[
\boxed{\ker Q\subseteq\ker R.}
\]

This is the precise algebraic form of the repair-precedence law. The tempting
expression “\(\ker Q\cap\mathrm{Req}(R)=0\)” is ill-typed unless the required
distinctions have first been represented as a subspace of states. The kernel
inclusion works directly and supplies a counterexample vector when descent
fails.

For completion, kernel inclusion is only the finite algebraic gate. Continuous
descent additionally requires

\[
Qv_n\to0\Longrightarrow Rv_n\to0.
\]

Across cutoffs, the operational certificate is one fixed source-authorized
constructor family and one uniform constant satisfying

\[
R_N^*R_N\le C^2Q_N.
\]

Full rank at every cutoff is not enough.

## Theta consequence

The arithmetic incidence previously missing from the inventory now exists at
finite cutoff:

\[
I_k(p,k)=\frac1k p^{-k/2}\delta_{k\log p},\qquad P=I_1,\quad Q=I_2.
\]

But this discovery splits the proposed operation `L` into three stages:

1. finite labelled incidence;
2. completion in the current's correct test-space topology;
3. scalar spectral readout.

Only the first is presently unconditional. The primitive current requires an
exponential/Laplace rigging, whereas the square current is tempered but neither
finite nor trace class. Neither completed current admits the undamped sine
readout by naive absolute pairing. Therefore finite incidence does not close
the original `S,C,L` triangle.

Two independent hostile sequences enforce the same theorem:

- tail states can vanish analytically while the seam norm remains nonzero;
- adjacent arithmetic labels can merge in the analytic Gram topology while
  an admitted valuation constructor retains a persistent jump.

The global scalar completion is now source-derived: the adelic Tate integral
jointly reassembles (P,Q,T_{\ge3}) with the archimedean source and continues
the resulting global section by Poisson sewing. It does not separately
continue the three log-current grades.

The smallest missing constructor has therefore moved one level downstream.
It is a functorial **operator-valued lift** from the global Tate--Poisson
section to the retained tail--seam incidence maps \((B_s,C_s)\). Recovering
those maps backward from the completed scalar function would violate the
distinction-preservation theorem: the many-to-one scalar readout has already
forgotten the route-level boundary data.

This also explains why a fixed archimedean current cannot repair the primitive
port: the finite primitive readout grows along subsequences like
\(\sqrt X/\log X\). A successful operation must be cutoff-covariant or form an
inseparable global relative pairing.
