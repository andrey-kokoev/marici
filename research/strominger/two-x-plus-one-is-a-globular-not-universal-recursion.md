# `2X+1` is a globular, not universal, recursion

## The hidden hypothesis

The proposed next packet

\[
2(3+2+1)+1
\]

assumes that a higher cell has exactly two parallel lower boundaries: a source
packet, a target packet, and one comparison.  That is the boundary shape of a
globular higher category.

It is a legitimate model, but the current source architecture does not force
all coherence to be globular.

## Three inequivalent locking modes

For an \(n\)-dimensional cell, the number of codimension-one faces is:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{locking mode}&\text{number of faces}\\
\hline
\text{globular}&2\\
\text{simplicial}&n+1\\
\text{cubical}&2n
\end{array}
\]

Thus at the first genuinely higher comparison:

- a globular three-cell compares two parallel two-cells;
- a simplicial three-cell fills four compatible triangular faces;
- a cubical three-cell fills six compatible square faces.

These are not different presentations of the same boundary packet.  They ask
for different source data.

## Falsification

The arithmetic recursion \(X_{n+1}=2X_n+1\) is therefore false as a universal
law.  It applies only after the source declares globular variance.

The same binary inventory \((I,C,M)\) can be embedded into:

- a globular comparison theory;
- a simplicial descent nerve;
- a cubical interchange theory.

Those embeddings agree on the named low-level gates but demand respectively
two, four, or six faces at degree three.  Hence the visible `3+2+1` inventory
does not determine its own next boundary shape.

## Sector diagnosis

- Reflection and group-cohomology calculations naturally use the simplicial
  bar nerve.
- Independent forward/backward operations with interchange naturally produce
  squares and cubes.
- A comparison between two already assembled complete implementations is
  naturally globular.

Our research packet contains all three situations.  Therefore no single flat
numeric recursion should be imposed across them.

## Correct prediction rule

The source must declare an incidence or axes-locking functor before the next
coherence obligation can be enumerated:

\[
\text{lower cells}+\text{incidence shape}
\longmapsto
\text{next horn boundary}.
\]

Only then can one ask whether a filler exists.  The obstruction class answers
existence; it does not determine the incidence shape.

Thus `2(3+2+1)+1` remains the correct free presentation for the globular case,
but it is falsified as the universal continuation of `3+2+1`.
