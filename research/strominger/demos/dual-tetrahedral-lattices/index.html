Declared geometric construction · not nuclear packing

Two mutually centered tetrahedral lattices

Every A site is the barycenter of four B neighbors; every B site is the barycenter of four A neighbors.
P = tetrahedron with A vertices, centered at B. N = tetrahedron with B vertices, centered at A.

What the construction forces

4 reciprocal neighbors per center.
6 links in the shortest closed center loop.
Same-family selected tetrahedra meet at most at a vertex.

A fully saturated closed finite region has equal P and N counts. Unequal isotope counts require boundary/occupation data.

What is not claimed

This is reciprocal barycentric tetrahedral structure, not a conventional face-to-face cell-dual tiling. The P/N naming is the proposed assignment, not a derived charge or native particle law.

No nuclear forces, stability, fiber transport or molecular capacity is calculated.

Fourfold adjacency is structural here, not an experimentally derived nuclear coordination limit. A drawing cutoff is not a maximum coherent size. Periodic checks retain translation lifts rather than averaging wrapped positions.
