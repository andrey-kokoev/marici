About this geometry

The proton and neutron are unfolded pentachoron facets. The electron adds a tetrahedron across the MCC face. This is a geometric carrier model, not a specification of particle preparation or dynamics, nor a space-filling lattice.

The shared edges

Proton and neutron share M₀ → C₁. Neutron and electron traverse C₁ ⇄ M₁ in opposite directions.
