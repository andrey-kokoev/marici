# The Square Law Is a Joint Atomicity Stratum

The word and response atomicity conditions were varied simultaneously over the
64 cases

[
a,p,qin{-2,-1,1,2},
]

where (a) is the elementary transvection parameter and (p,q) are the two
tail powers.

The cyclic target index is twice a square exactly when all three conditions
hold:

1. (|a|=1), so the elementary response is a primitive transvection;
2. (|p|=|q|=1), so both tail Fox derivatives are group-ring units;
3. (pq<0), so the tail pair is anti-invariant.

There are no false positives and no false negatives in the complete joint
family. In particular, combining nonprimitive word data with a nonprimitive
response never accidentally restores the square.

Thus the bounded constructor law is the intersection

[
	ext{primitive response}
cap
	ext{Fox-unit tails}
cap
	ext{anti-invariant polarity}.
]

This is stronger than the two separate hostiles because it excludes compensating
interactions between their defects. The remaining unbounded theorem must derive
the same conjunction symbolically for arbitrary nonzero word powers and
transvection content, rather than extend the finite census.
