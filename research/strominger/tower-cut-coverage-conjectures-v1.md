# Refining the remaining tower-cut coverage claim

The governing conjecture is unchanged: every known physical law's domain is exposed by a Marici tower cut as the formed triangle in a decorated FCC/equivalent tetrahedral representation. The generic representation theorem and the three scoped examples are completed inputs, not new conjectures.

## C1 — Native compilation of retained incoming-family promotion

**Claim.** For every finite, uniquely labelled input row family and either of the existing inherited-key/common-target policies, the actual `incoming_promotion` operation has a compositional implementation in the existing native constructor/application framework. It retains all member identities, creates the declared fresh labels/endpoints, computes the declared aggregate, and recovers every input row. Its compiled second application consumes the actual compiled first result.

The target is the implementation in `research/nima/checkers/check_rung_transport_diagram.py`, not an unspecified promotion operation. Coefficient algebra and endpoint policy are declared inputs. The candidate source basis is the existing original/native constructors plus the already declared native-application extension; any enlargement must be recorded as a changed conjecture rather than hidden as a successful proof.

**Rival.** The present bridge retains only an opaque precomputed promotion output, or implements grouping but not its full next-level fields and membership.

**First test.** Inspect the existing native application and grouping bridges, then compile this exact operation with parameter/seed origins. Distinguish repeated references from conflicting row identities and preserve the nested bath corruption control. No final promotion result may be admitted as an answer seed.

**Success.** A theorem for this row class and both policies, with constructor/output/recovery equations and actual two-step composition. The Python and formal boundary must be explicit; matching readouts alone is insufficient.

**Failure.** A legal input cannot be compiled with the declared basis, a parameter lacks a permitted producer, a member is lost, or second-stage execution substitutes a fresh target instead of consuming the first output.

This is an integration conjecture, not a demand that every possible WG extension reduce to twelve old rules.

## C2 — Continuous rank-one Berry domains from refining cuts

**Claim.** For a C2 loop of rank-one orthogonal projectors in C^n on a compact parameter interval, with equal endpoint projectors, the normalized cyclic overlap product on partitions converges as mesh tends to zero to its canonical Berry holonomy. A retained tower presentation of the partition data preserves that product at every stage and its limit. No uniform partition bound over all loops is claimed.

The source is an independently specified smooth projector loop and its ordered sample records, not the desired phase. For sufficiently fine partitions adjacent overlaps are nonzero. Choose local normalized frames only to compute the product; vertex rephasings cancel. Use the existing convention A=-i u†du and phase exp(i integral A), with endpoint transition included when the frame is not periodic.

**Rival.** Exact finite recovery is insufficient because the chosen approximation/readout lacks a controlled limit, or an endpoint/chart transition disappears during refinement.

**First test.** Prove convergence with a bound controlled by the loop's regularity on the compact interval; test a latitude loop and a chart-crossing loop. Keep the annular flat-twist counterexample: identical projectors do not determine an independently supplied noncanonical connection. This claim is only about the canonical projector-induced connection.

**Success.** An all-refinement argument in the stated class, including ordering, nonzero-overlap and chart-transition conditions, plus preservation by the already checked retained representation.

**Failure.** A loop satisfying the stated hypotheses has a different limit, or the proposed cut comparison destroys required ordering/transition information. A degenerate/discontinuous input outside the class is not a refutation.

This is one substantive continuum-coverage route. It is not made a prerequisite for domains already represented by complete analytic function packets.

## C3 — Physical-domain source coverage

**Claim.** Every independently specified known physical-law domain admits a WG source presentation whose retained state, admissible operations, constraints and observations reproduce the structure needed to state and apply that law. Universe level, analytic completion and source policy are part of the witness where required. Combined with the established representation theorem, this gives the governing tower-cut claim.

The target is the physical domain, not just a computed scalar or familiar symmetry group. The source witness must expose its declared primitive inputs and rules; calling the complete target an atom proves only supplied-object encoding, not the asserted source-generation comparison. Supplied physical parameters and mathematical operations remain permitted when explicitly accounted for; this claim does not require deriving today's laws from no inputs or selecting a unique universe.

**Rivals.** Some known domain needs an operation/structure outside the proposed WG source coverage; or apparent coverage is merely opaque target import with no operation-preserving source comparison.

**Risky consequence.** The same declared source interface must retain distinctions the law can detect even when an observer cannot: bath noise, reference orientation, connection/loop data and any further domain-specific witnesses. It cannot preserve only terminal values while changing intermediate allowed operations.

**Success.** A structural coverage/classification argument with exact source and target scope, plus physical-domain bindings; examples or a completed registry alone do not establish universality.

**Failure.** An independently admitted physical domain has no representation under the claimed source contract preserving the required operations/observations. Failure of one optional compiler route does not establish this stronger obstruction.

## Dependency and scheduling discipline

C1 and C2 are bounded probes and possible components of C3, not logical equivalents of C3 and not mandatory descriptions of all WG systems. The governing node depends on C3 plus the already completed representation results. C1 and C2 remain separately executable research branches; completing them must not automatically resolve C3.

The initial C1-first scheduling choice is superseded by the [intrinsic cut-determination test](intrinsic-cut-determination-conjecture-v1.md); C1 remains open and is not refuted. When executing C1, search existing implementations first. If the source basis lacks a necessary operation, report that precise obstruction once; do not create a chain of increasingly abstract specification gates.
