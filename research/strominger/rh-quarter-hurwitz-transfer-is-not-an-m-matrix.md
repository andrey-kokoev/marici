# Quarter Hurwitz transfer is not an M-matrix

## Question

Does a nonsingular M-matrix structure explain the positive principal minors of the endpoint transfer?

## Claim boundary

The classification covers the order-eight transfer matrix and its checkerboard signature conjugate. It does not exclude other diagonal signatures or other P-matrix subclasses.

## Disposition

The plain transfer matrix has a positive off-diagonal entry and a negative inverse entry. Its checkerboard conjugate also has a positive off-diagonal entry and a negative inverse entry. Exact multiplication verifies the computed inverse. Therefore neither matrix is a nonsingular M-matrix, while all principal minors remain positive. M-matrix theory cannot supply the missing all-order mechanism. The next leaf is `quarter-hurwitz-transfer-gkk-inequalities`, exhaustively testing the generalized Hadamard–Fischer inequalities among principal minors; passing would place the transfer in the GKK P-matrix class and provide a multiplicative principal-minor invariant.
