## Construction

1. Retain the actual two face-glued esd7 subdivisions and their five specified corner roles.
2. Give every primitive a whole-cell comparison role: the two orientations of regular tetrahedra are P/E; octahedral subdivision wedges are N. Apply the corner boundary conditions explicitly.
3. Inspect all shared triangular faces. At the inter-copy seam, matching charged halves become one two-component comparison carrier, after verifying equal roles and opposite-sided geometry. This is a declared gluing rule, not a native-particle identity.
4. Start **only at −P**, adding the next face-adjacent frontier. +N, E, −N and +P remain boundary constraints, never independent sources. No desired words, target body count or fixed round cutoff selects a frontier. Verify that +P is the unique final outward body.
5. Start the return **only at +P**, traversing the retained face network back toward −P. Every return attachment has its own actual shared-face witness and an earlier return parent. Keep every outward body and its admission history. Verify that −P is the unique final return body.

The return is not obtained by simply reversing outward BFS layers: side branches need actual return connectivity. In this fixture only 134 of 660 bodies lie on a shortest outward entry/exit DAG; a separately checked return traversal covers all 660.

## Measured result

- **686 primitive tetrahedra**, each retained exactly once with unchanged volume.
- **660 comparison bodies**, including **26** two-component charged seam carriers.
- **49** paired seam triangles; **294** triangles on the closed outer surface.
- **37 outward rounds**, emerging from face-graph distance to the single −P seed, ending at +P.
- **37 return rounds**, beginning at +P and ending at −P. Together: 74 transitions plus the initial state. This replaces the previous, incorrect multi-seed 14+14 traversal.
- No nonmanifold triangular faces, unreachable bodies, mixed whole-body roles, or direct charged/charged face contacts between distinct bodies.
- The outer triangulation has Euler characteristic two. Shared faces have opposite-sided occupants. Total retained volume equals the two original subdivisions.

The visual checkerboard is generated by tetrahedral orientation and neutral wedges; it is not the earlier word-painted pattern. All 16 specified non-null surface words were compared afterwards: **none match**. This is an alternative coherent tiling under the user's permission to choose a more natural process, not a claimed derivation of those words.

## Interpretation boundaries

The geometric tiling is certified, but a full native P/N/E particle realization is **not**. Six corner primitives override the geometric role rule. The 26 seam composites are not primitive one-cell particles. Complete five-coefficient native packets and their analytic witnesses have not been sewn across the entire tiling.

The return checks certify a reverse-directed traversal of the retained face network, not dual coefficient dynamics or an Artin braid. The 37+37 face-graph traversals have not been derived from the 28×2×2 comparison bank. The original-sheet geometric P/E assignment still places most E readings internally. The subsequent [paired-polarity extension](paired-polarity-v1.md) retains a second P↔E sheet on the same geometry, exposing opposite-sheet E readings without cloning cells. This is an explicit candidate role/field comparison, not a derived surface-electron law.

These distinctions are displayed in the viewer's construction panel.
