# qRB microstep 30: cofinal diagonal construction

Prior research supplies the missing convergence pattern on a countable observer core. For each finite stage `k`, choose increasing conductor/depth/cutoff/regulator indices so the first `k` observer pairs have error below `2^{-k}`. The resulting coordinatewise increasing sequence is cofinal and converges pointwise on every core pair to the Weil readout.

Thus the infinite cube can be shown analytically on `E_count` by:

1. exact finite cube commutativity;
2. iterated relative trace convergence;
3. finite-stage diagonal selection;
4. cofinality of the selected regulator path.

This proves a cofinal diagonal spectral readout only under the stated iterated-limit hypotheses. It does not prove unrestricted net convergence or ordinary uniform Hilbert completion.
