## Quantitative versus algebraic separation

The argument proves algebraic injectivity of the combined source incidence.
It does not by itself give a uniform lower bound

\[
\|C(c,x)\|
\ge m_C\|(c,x)\|.
\]

Because \(B\) is compact/Hilbert--Schmidt, approximate dark sequences may
still exist unless the theta direction is separated from the closure of
\(\operatorname{ran}B\).

The stronger condition is

\[
\operatorname{dist}
\bigl(
\Phi_{\rm diag},
\overline{\operatorname{ran}B}
\bigr)>0.
\]

That angle is not proved by failure of exact range membership.
