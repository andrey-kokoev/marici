Key results:
- Theta arithmetic constructors form a lax monoidal action on the Green carrier, not merely a pointwise operation.
- Three distinct thresholds for mixed Green completion: bounded, Hilbert-Schmidt, and trace-class.
- Three-lens typing: scalar, determinant, and ordered lenses each require their own typed Green completion.
