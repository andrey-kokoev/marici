# The full relative completion trace has rank two, while the frozen theta source occupies one ray

## Increment map on the quotient

Let

\[
\mathcal C=\partial_u^2-\frac14
\]

on a precursor test domain over \([0,\infty)\). For \(g=\mathcal Cf\), the Wronskian increment identities are

\[
\Delta\mathcal B_-(f)
=
\int_0^\infty e^{-u/2}g(u)\,du,
\]

\[
\Delta\mathcal B_+(f)
=
\int_0^\infty e^{u/2}g(u)\,du,
\]

whenever both integrals and endpoint traces are defined.

Therefore the increment map descends through the completion kernel:

\[
\mathcal T_{\mathrm{inc}}:
[f]\in\operatorname{Dom}\mathcal C/\ker\mathcal C
\longmapsto
\begin{pmatrix}
\langle e^{-u/2},\mathcal Cf\rangle\\
\langle e^{u/2},\mathcal Cf\rangle
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Adding either homogeneous kernel mode changes absolute traces but not their increments.

## Rank-two theorem on a genuine test domain

On any source test space containing two compactly supported completed forcings, the functionals

\[
g\longmapsto\int e^{-u/2}g(u)\,du,
\qquad
g\longmapsto\int e^{u/2}g(u)\,du
\]

are linearly independent.

Indeed, choose nonzero nonnegative bumps \(g_1,g_2\) concentrated near distinct points \(u_1\ne u_2\). Their moment matrix approaches

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
e^{-u_1/2}&e^{-u_2/2}\\
e^{u_1/2}&e^{u_2/2}
\end{pmatrix},
\]

whose determinant is

\[
2\sinh\left(\frac{u_2-u_1}{2}\right)\ne0.
\]

Hence

\[
\operatorname{rank}\mathcal T_{\mathrm{inc}}=2
\]

on the full relative test domain.

## Resolution of the rank-one observation

The physical theta forcing \(\Phi\) supplies only one vector

\[
\mathcal T_{\mathrm{inc}}[h]
=
\begin{pmatrix}
a\\
b
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its one-vector Gram is necessarily rank one. This does not imply that the relative trace functor itself has rank one.

The distinction is:

- functor rank: two on a sufficiently rich precursor domain;
- frozen-source orbit rank: one until a second authorized source variation is supplied.

The auxiliary Adams constructor needs the second notion, not merely universal test-space rank. Arbitrary bump forcings prove analytic capacity but not arithmetic source authority.

## Source-authorized rank test

Let \(\mathcal O_{\theta}\) be the precursor orbit generated by admitted operations such as:

- prime shifts;
- valuation grades;
- seam displacement;
- completion-compatible derivatives;
- reciprocal reflection.

The decisive finite question is

\[
\operatorname{rank}
\left(
\mathcal T_{\mathrm{inc}}
\big|_{\mathcal O_{\theta}}
\right).
\]

Rank two requires two authorized precursors \(f_1,f_2\) with

\[
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
\int e^{-u/2}\mathcal Cf_1&
\int e^{-u/2}\mathcal Cf_2\\
\int e^{u/2}\mathcal Cf_1&
\int e^{u/2}\mathcal Cf_2
\end{pmatrix}
\ne0.
\]

## Candidate smallest pair

The first source-native candidate pair is:

\[
h,
\qquad
\partial_L h_L\big|_{L=0},
\]

where \(h_L\) is the admitted scale-shifted precursor. Equivalently, use two distinct prime-scale translates if the scale derivative is not an admitted constructor.

Translation changes the two weighted moments oppositely. For an ideal translate \(g_L(u)=g(u+L)\) without boundary truncation,

\[
M_-(g_L)=e^{L/2}M_-(g),
\qquad
M_+(g_L)=e^{-L/2}M_+(g).
\]

Thus \(g\) and a nonzero translate have non-collinear trace vectors whenever both moments are nonzero.

Half-line truncation adds boundary terms and must be retained in the actual source calculation.

## Completion margin

Finite rank two is not enough. The assembled two-column observer requires a lower singular-value bound after source normalization. Prime translates can become nearly collinear if their scale separation tends to zero or if one weighted moment vanishes.

The relevant local margin is

\[
\delta_{\mathrm{trace}}
=
\sigma_{\min}
\begin{pmatrix}
M_-(g_1)&M_-(g_2)\\
M_+(g_1)&M_+(g_2)
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

It is a constructor-rank margin upstream of Schur positivity.
