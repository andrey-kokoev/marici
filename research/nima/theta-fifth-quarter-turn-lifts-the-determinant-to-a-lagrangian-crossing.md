## Symplectic port space

Let \(\mathcal U\) be the finite port space and equip

\[
\mathcal U\oplus\mathcal U
\]

with the Green symplectic form

\[
\Omega
\left(
(x,y),(x',y')
\right)
=
\langle y,x'\rangle
-
\langle x,y'\rangle.
\]

For a Hermitian operator \(W(\lambda)\), its graph

\[
\Gamma(W(\lambda))
=
\left\{
(v,W(\lambda)v):v\in\mathcal U
\right\}
\]

is Lagrangian:

\[
\Omega|_{\Gamma(W(\lambda))}=0,
\qquad
\dim\Gamma(W(\lambda))=\dim\mathcal U.
\]

Thus the real spectral family is a path in the Lagrangian Grassmannian.

## Determinant zero as plane intersection

Fix the horizontal reference plane

\[
\mathcal L_0=\mathcal U\oplus\{0\}.
\]

Then

\[
\Gamma(W(\lambda))\cap\mathcal L_0
=
\left\{
(v,0):v\in\ker W(\lambda)
\right\}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\det W(\lambda)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Gamma(W(\lambda))\cap\mathcal L_0\ne\{0\}.
\]

The intersection dimension is

\[
\dim\ker W(\lambda),
\]

regularity.

## Crossing form

At a real characteristic value \(\lambda_0\), the tangent orientation of the
intersection is the quadratic form

\[
Q_{\lambda_0}(v)
=
\langle v,W'(\lambda_0)v\rangle,
\qquad
v\in\ker W(\lambda_0).
\]

For the fixed selfadjoint feedback Weyl function

\[
W(\lambda)
=
A-\lambda I
-
B^*(D_H-\lambda)^{-1}B,
\]

differentiation gives

\[
W'(\lambda)
=
-I
-
B^*(D_H-\lambda)^{-2}B
\]

for real \(\lambda\) outside \(\operatorname{spec}D_H\).

Hence

\[
Q_{\lambda_0}(v)
=
-\|v\|^2
-
\|(D_H-\lambda_0)^{-1}Bv\|^2.
\]

Every nonzero crossing vector has strictly negative crossing form. The
Lagrangian path crosses the reference cycle in one source-fixed direction.

At an isolated real crossing:

1. there are no tangential characteristic branches;

4. the local Maslov contribution has fixed negative sign;
5. multiplicity is geometric rather than a fitted scalar order.

This is stronger information than real-axis confinement of \(\det W\).

corank, or orientation.

## Finite falsifiers

The Grassmannian route fails if:

1. two source-admissible reference planes yield different divisors;
2. the proposed graph is not isotropic;
3. a determinant match has the wrong corank;
4. the crossing form is degenerate or has the wrong sign;
5. lower Plücker coordinates disagree;
6. the reference plane depends on \(\lambda\) without source authority;
7. finite crossings converge only to essential-spectrum saturation;
8. cutoff transition maps fail to preserve the Schubert incidence.

## Verdict

The fifth quarter-turn converts an invariant scalar zero into an oriented
intersection of source-derived Lagrangian planes. For a true selfadjoint
feedback Weyl family, the crossing orientation is strictly fixed. But the
rotation raises the source-authority bar: one must derive the complete graph,
reference plane, and Plücker transition system. Without that data, replacing
the theta scalar by a determinant or Maslov crossing is another repackaging,
not an explanation.
