# qRB microstep 14: refinement admission boundary

The current qRB construction admits refinements in three typed classes:

1. **place/transport refinements**: strict when the regulator is transported with the source;
2. **affine chart refinements**: Gaussian observers remain in the varying-width family;
3. **independent sharp-window refinements**: lax only, with no quotient-descent claim until a separate comparison is supplied.

Accordingly, the qRB quotient functor is currently defined only for refinements whose observer pullback preserves the declared Schwartz/projective observer family.

A sharp window cannot be silently promoted to a strict refinement merely because its finite carrier is positive.

Status: admission boundary recorded. The next useful step is one explicit source refinement from the place/transport class, followed through the observer pullback and radical descent tests.
