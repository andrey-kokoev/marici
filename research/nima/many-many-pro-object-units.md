# Many-many pro-object units

$$
R_{\alpha\alpha}=I_{\mathcal E_\alpha}.
$$

$$
R_{\beta\alpha}R_{\alpha\alpha}=R_{\beta\alpha},

R_{\alpha\alpha}R_{\alpha\beta}=R_{\alpha\beta}.
$$
