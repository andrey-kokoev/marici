# Finite trace rank plus compact parameter continuity gives the required metric comparison

## Kitaev's authority square

Let \(V_{p,L}\) be the two-dimensional source incidence plane generated by the even completed theta forcing and its reciprocal-odd derivative. Let

\[
G_L
\]

be the source Mellin half-density metric, and let

\[
P_{p,L}(\sigma)
=
\mathcal I_{p,L}^{*}
E_{\sigma,L}
\mathcal I_{p,L}
\]

be the pullback of the Laplace-weighted jump-energy form.

The required authority statement is

\[
c_-G_L
\le
P_{p,L}(\sigma)
\le
c_+G_L
\]

with constants uniform over labels, cutoffs, and compact off-seam parameter regions.

## Reduction to the seam fiber

Mellin translation is isometric between the object-indexed source and history fibers. The Euler coefficient multiplies both sides quadratically. Therefore, after dividing by the source coefficient, the generalized eigenvalues of

\[
(P_{p,L}(\sigma),G_L)
\]

are the same as those of the base pair

\[
(P_0(\sigma),G_0).
\]

Prime and grade disappear from the comparison. This uses the valuation-labelled fiber theorem and does not rely on scalar prime orthogonality.

## Positivity on the two-dimensional source plane

The twisted endpoint trace matrix on the base theta pair is

\[
F_0=
\begin{pmatrix}
\frac12&\frac14\\
\frac12&-\frac14
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det F_0=-\frac14.
\]

Because \(E_{\sigma,0}\) contains a positive endpoint term, any vector in the kernel of \(P_0(\sigma)\) has zero endpoint traces. Since \(F_0\) is invertible, that vector is zero. Hence

\[
P_0(\sigma)>0
\]

on the source incidence plane for every \(\sigma>0\).

## Uniform compact comparison

Assume the twisted incidence map depends continuously on \(\sigma\) in the graph topology. Then the two generalized eigenvalues of

\[
G_0^{-1/2}P_0(\sigma)G_0^{-1/2}
\]

are continuous and positive.

On a compact open-sector interval

\[
\sigma\in[\sigma_0,\sigma_1],
\qquad
0<\sigma_0<\sigma_1<\infty,
\]

their minimum and maximum satisfy

\[
0<c_-\le c_+<\infty.
\]

Transport then gives Kitaev's comparison uniformly for every \((p,k,L)\) and cutoff.

Thus exact equality of Green metrics is unnecessary. Finite trace rank, graph continuity, and transported label covariance yield uniform equivalence on every compact off-seam region.

## Two remaining qualifications

First, this comparison authorizes the jump energy only on the two-dimensional theta incidence range. It does not declare the same metric on arbitrary auxiliary bulk directions.

Second, factorization of the actual odd operator through the endpoint trace remains a separate Green-identity theorem. The formal first-order identity supplies the candidate factorization, but closure must preserve it on the twisted reduced domains.

## Completion boundary

If one takes the whole projective family \(\sigma>0\) with no lower bound, then

\[
c_-\to0
\]

may occur and the auxiliary margin collapses. The theorem is intentionally compact-off-seam, matching the scope of all previous coercivity claims.
