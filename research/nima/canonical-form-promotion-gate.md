# Conditional canonical-form promotion gate

## Question

Which parts of the polygon residue architecture are derived, and which source inputs are still required before identifying the weighted sum with a positive-geometry canonical form?

## Claim boundary

This packet gives a conditional theorem. It does not assert that a source-selected associahedral embedding, physical normalization, or generic Jacobian identity has been constructed.

## Derived arrows

For every finite polygon dissection `D`, the face-product bijection proves Laurent coefficient factorization into independent regional weighted sums. Tensoring with

\[
\operatorname{or}(D)=\det(\mathbb Z^D)
\]

makes iterated residues independent of cut ordering and gives associative wedge transport for disjoint regional cuts.

For any fixed reduced affine hyperplane arrangement of positive dimension, two rational top forms with at most simple facet poles, equal normal-first residues, and no pole on the projective infinity divisor coincide. The proof is the degree gap between infinity regularity and divisibility by the complete facet divisor. The zero-dimensional form is not fixed by residue recursion.

At five points, every bounded simple common-Jacobian pentagon has interval boundary residues and no infinity-divisor pole. Exact non-reference positive rivals share both properties. Therefore these recursion and regularity conditions determine the form on a fixed embedding but do not select the embedding.

## Required source inputs

Canonical-form identification additionally requires:

1. a source-derived dimension-`n-3` affine or projective embedding whose facets are the polygon channels;
2. a positive region with face incidence identified with the polygon dissection poset;
3. a Jacobian or volume identity converting the scalar triangulation sum into the embedded top form;
4. a zero-dimensional base normalization with compatible orientation;
5. verification that the candidate has only the admitted simple poles and is regular on the infinity divisor.

Under these inputs, coefficient factorization supplies the recursive residues and fixed-arrangement uniqueness identifies the candidate with the canonical form. This implication is one-way: uniqueness supplies no reverse arrow deriving the embedding or its supports.

A physical amplitude or wavefunction interpretation further requires a source map from sector kinematics to the arrangement plus its normalization and any analytic boundary-value or contour prescription. Combinatorial residue algebra does not provide those maps.

## Disposition

The conditional promotion theorem is established. Unqualified canonical-form and physical identifications are withheld because the generic embedding, support constants, Jacobian identity, base normalization, and sector readout remain unsupplied. The five-point rival family proves that recursion and infinity regularity cannot replace those inputs.
