# The ratio-Stokes cocycle vanishes on the dominant diagonal pair, so the reciprocal port must carry the late-shell bulk correction

## Question

Can the newly isolated ratio-Stokes cocycle supply the leading late-shell
compensation required by candidate one?

## Claim boundary

Not on its own. The ratio cocycle vanishes identically when the two theta labels
coincide, while late prime shells are superexponentially dominated by the
\((1,1)\) pair. Therefore this cocycle can organize off-diagonal linking but
cannot supply the dominant diagonal correction. The reciprocal response, or an
additional diagonal linking component with separate source authority, must do
so.

## Diagonal vanishing

For an ordered pair,

\[
 d_{nm}=\log\frac mn.
\]

The ratio-Stokes section is

\[
 C_{nm}(z)
 =\int_0^{d_{nm}}
 e^{z(d_{nm}-w)}K_{[A_n,B_n]}(w)\,dw.
\]

If \(n=m\), then \(d_{nn}=0\), so

\[
 C_{nn}(z)=0
\]

identically, including every parameter derivative.

## Dominant late-shell pair

For a late shell with lower endpoint \(a\),

\[
 \rho_{a,b}(t)
 =d_1^2\rho_{11}^{[a,b]}(t)
 +O\!\left(P(e^a)e^{-3\pi e^{2a}}\right)
\]

on compact separation ranges, with a double-exponentially small large-range
tail. Thus the ordinary Evans shell and its fixed parameter jets are dominated
by the diagonal first-label pair.

The ratio cocycle contributes zero on that pair. Every nonzero ratio-cocycle
term comes from a superexponentially smaller off-diagonal label pair.

## Required diagonal response

Under the frozen unit port coefficients, the dominant reciprocal-plus-linking
response must satisfy

\[
 R_j^{\rm diag}
 =-\left(1-\frac1{2\Lambda(a)}+O(\Lambda(a)^{-2})\right)
 E_j^{\rm diag}
\]

at first relative order, where \(E_j^{\rm diag}\) is the full curvature-aware
endpoint jet of the \((1,1)\) pair.

Since the ratio-Stokes component is zero there, this obligation lands in:

1. the diagonal reciprocal response; or
2. a distinct diagonal linking section not generated by ratio translation.

The second option requires separate source construction and cannot inherit the
ratio-cocycle authority.

## Port separation

The candidate-one response should therefore be decomposed before theta-label
summation into:

\[
 R^{\rm recip}_{\rm diag}
 \oplus
 R^{\rm recip}_{\rm off}
 \oplus
 C^{\rm ratio}_{\rm off}
 \oplus
 R^{\rm link,extra}_{\rm diag},
\]

with absent summands explicitly set to zero. Codiagonal cancellation is tested
only after each summand is source-normalized.

## Hostile

A proposal that attributes the full late-shell correction to the oriented ratio
cocycle fails on \((n,m)=(1,1)\) before any asymptotic coefficient is evaluated.
A proposal that silently extends the cocycle to a nonzero diagonal value
violates its zero-initial condition.

## Multiplicity

Because \(C_{nn}(z)\) is identically zero, all its Evans parameter jets vanish.
The diagonal reciprocal law must therefore carry the full multiplicity
hierarchy, not merely the zeroth shell value.

## Disposition

The ratio-Stokes construction is confined to off-diagonal linking. Candidate
one's principal late-shell burden is now assigned to the source-normalized
diagonal reciprocal response, unless an independently derived diagonal linking
port exists. No such formula appears in the current G4 declaration. No RH
conclusion is authorized.
