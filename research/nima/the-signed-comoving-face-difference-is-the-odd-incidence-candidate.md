\[

## Why \(C\) alone is insufficient

The contrast \(C\) is odd and has the correct derivative, but a Wronskian is a
quadratic or polarized readout. A single odd vector does not determine its
normalization against the tail--PV plane. The source still must prove:

1. the map from \(C\) into \((K,V)\);
2. the polarized Wronskian pairing on that image;
3. continuity on the relative Green domain;
4. equality with the zero-section position current;
5. cutoff-uniform lower bounds.

Thus the incidence leg is a source-derived candidate, while the analytic
linking identity remains open.
