# Many-many Xi characteristic-state metric

For

$$
 u(s,c)=c\begin{pmatrix}-1/s\\-1/(s-1)\\1\end{pmatrix}
$$

and `K=diag(1,-1,1)`, the endpoint–radial Pontryagin norm is

$$
\langle Ku,u\rangle
=|c|^2\left(
1+\frac1{|s|^2}-\frac1{|s-1|^2}
\right).
$$

This quantity is generally indefinite and does not by itself force
`Re(s)=1/2`. On the critical line, the two rational endpoint contributions have equal modulus and cancel, leaving

$$
\langle Ku,u\rangle=|c|^2.
$$

Therefore the critical-line condition must arise from the radial Green flux or a maximal-neutrality condition, rather than from the pointwise Pontryagin norm alone.
