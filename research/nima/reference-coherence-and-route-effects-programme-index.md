## Programme identity

  bracketing, calibration, and route forgetting
- Evidence boundary: exact finite algebra, topology, representation theory,

- Excluded claims: no physical implementation is inferred; no theta/Tate,

## Question

What is the minimum source-derived structure required to recover a distinction
that disappears under scalar projection or gauge quotient, and when does the
remaining coherence residue become lawful higher structure rather than a
defect?

## Hard core

The programme retains five constraints:

1. downstream processing cannot recover information already erased by a
   quotient;
2. a repair must be nontrivial on the unresolved fiber and source-authorized;
3. transport, exchange modality, pointing, path order, and projection order
   are independent types;
4. higher residuals must pass their coherence laws and admissible
   trivialization tests before promotion;
5. source-derived curvature or anomaly may be structure, but retrospective
   route or cell insertion is a fitted escape.

The hard core reopens if a source-valid downstream-only repair becomes jointly
faithful on a quotient kernel, or if a promoted higher residual fails its
declared coherence law.

## Superseded statements

The following stronger formulations are withdrawn:

- “Exchange leaves only a sign” is restricted to complex-linear exchange.
  Dagger exchange alone leaves \(U(1)\).
- “One-dimensional kernel needs one scalar reference” is restricted to linear
  local carriers. A phase circle needs two real coordinates globally.
- “Two quadratures solve phase” is incomplete. Their representation weight
  must be primitive and their frame must be pointed or relational.
- “Pentagon success makes the system strict” is false on a fixed source
  carrier. A nontrivial third cohomology class may remain.
- “Nonzero calibration holonomy is always a defect” is false. It is a defect
  only for endpoint descent; a route-labelled source connection may carry
  lawful curvature.

## Exact reusable gates

For a proposed reference or coherencer, run the gates in order:

1. quotient kernel: what distinction is erased?
2. incidence: is the new port nontrivial on that fiber?
3. constructibility: can the source prepare and interrogate it?
4. representation: does its transport carry a faithful weight?
5. pointing: is the result absolute or only relational?
6. triangle: do pairwise transports share one edge system?
7. tetrahedron: do face defects satisfy transported compatibility?
8. strictness: are associators equalities or cells?
9. pentagon: does weak composition cohere?
10. cohomology: can the associator be removed by admissible rephasing?
11. observability: does a calibrated instrument detect the surviving class?
12. route descent: is flatness required, or are route effects retained?

Failure at one gate blocks every downstream interpretation.
