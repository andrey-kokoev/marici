# Uniform coordinate truncations are the completion certificate for prime labels

## The exact functional-analytic gate

Let (E_{mathrm{fin}}=igoplus_{p}^{mathrm{alg}}E_p) be the finite
valuation/Fock source, with coordinate idempotents (P_p). Let (E) be its
declared rigged or Banach completion. For a finite prime set (F), write

[
P_F=sum_{pin F}P_p.
]

The finite labels survive completion as an unconditional coordinate
decomposition if:

[
sup_F|P_F|_{E	o E}<infty
]

and, along cutoff sets (F_X={ple X}),

[
P_{F_X}xlongrightarrow x
qquad(xin E).
]

Under these two conditions every (P_p) extends continuously, the extensions
remain pairwise orthogonal idempotents, and no completion vector acquires an
ambiguous prime decomposition.

The same criterion must hold separately for the primitive exponential rigging
and square tempered rigging.

Thus the completion theorem factorizes into:

1. unconditional coordinate survival;
2. domain invariance;
3. projected convergence of the mixed form.

## Why individual projection bounds are insufficient

It is not enough that each (P_p) extend boundedly. The norms of finite sums
(P_F) can diverge, so the coordinate system can be conditional. Then
reordering, sewing, or cutoff completion can change the represented vector even
though every individual label remains readable.

The hostile is a conditional Schauder decomposition with

[
|P_p|le C
]

for every (p), but

[
sup_F|P_F|=infty.
]

Finite prime packets are perfectly typed; completion transport amplifies
selected collections and loses a uniform label frame.

## Solid sequence-space route

[

[

]

Weighted (ell^r) and many Köthe sequence riggings have this property. The
primitive and square weights may differ; what matters is that each grade
retains a solid prime coordinate norm before Green sewing.

## Sewing audit

Even with unconditional coordinates, a sewing map (S) can mix labels. The
required law is

[
SP_F=P_FS
]

for every finite (F), equivalently (SP_p=P_pS) for each prime together with
uniform coordinate control. Wall subtraction must satisfy the same law.

The finite audit should therefore output:

[
sup_F|P_F|,qquad
sup_F|Q_F|,qquad
|[S,P_F]|,qquad
|[S,Q_F]|.
]

For graph or form maps, commutators are tested on the invariant core and after
closure.

## Frontier

The next source calculation is no longer a two-index kernel estimate. It is to
identify the actual prime-coordinate norms of the exponential and tempered
riggings and decide whether their cutoff projections are uniformly bounded,
ideally contractive. Then audit comoving Green transport, wall subtraction, and
sewing for coordinate commutation.

This is the quantitative completion certificate that turns finite source
labels into exact completed prime diagonality.
