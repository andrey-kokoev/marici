# The distinguished zeta vacuum is finitely spherical, so it cannot source a primewise odd Weyl port

## Distinguished adelic source

The completed zeta section uses the factorizable Schwartz–Bruhat vector

\[
\phi_0(x)
=
e^{-\pi x_\infty^2}
\prod_p\mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p}(x_p).
\]

At every finite prime, the local factor

\[
\phi_{0,p}=\mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p}
\]

is spherical and fixed by the maximal compact unit action. Its local Tate integral gives the unramified Euler factor.

## Finite Fourier stability

With self-dual normalization,

\[
\mathcal F_p\mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p}
=
\mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p}.
\]

Therefore the global Fourier/Poisson operation preserves finite sphericality of the distinguished source. It exchanges the global Tate charts but does not generate a finite nonspherical Weyl direction.

The resulting finite-place information remains in the radial valuation carrier.

## Archimedean odd direction

At the real place, additive differentiation produces the source-derived odd vector

\[
\partial_x e^{-\pi x^2}
=
-2\pi x e^{-\pi x^2}.
\]

Tensoring it with the unchanged finite vacuum gives

\[
\phi_{\mathrm{odd},\infty}
=
\left(-2\pi x_\infty e^{-\pi x_\infty^2}\right)
\prod_p\mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p}(x_p).
\]

This is a genuine global odd Tate vector, but its oddness is purely archimedean. Every finite component remains spherical.

Hence it supplies an archimedean conjugate port, not a primewise finite Weyl port.

## Primewise odd obstruction

A finite odd Weyl direction requires a nonspherical factor such as

\[
\psi_p(\eta x)\mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p}(x)
\]

or a translated compact-open set. Such a vector is not generated from \(\mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p}\) by the spherical Fourier operation alone.

Introducing it changes the local source type. Its Tate integral need not equal the unramified factor

\[
(1-p^{-s})^{-1}.
\]

Therefore a nonspherical finite port cannot be attached to the distinguished zeta source without an additional comparison theorem proving exactly how its altered local factor is canceled or routed.

## Rank consequence

The standard global Tate operation constructs the distinguished completed section from one factorizable spherical source direction. Fourier mixing and boundary basis changes cannot increase the finite local source-incidence rank.

The archimedean derivative adds an independent real-place direction, but it does not create independent primitive, square, seam, endpoint, and finite odd columns at each prime.

This agrees with the finite comparison audit: displayed ports may outnumber source-generated directions.

## Two lawful programmes

### Spherical zeta programme

Retain the standard finite vacuum. Then:

- the Euler factors remain canonical;
- radial Fourier and valuation incidence are authorized;
- no primewise odd Weyl port exists;
- the boundary law must derive orientation without finite nonspherical phase.

### Ramified enlargement programme

Admit nonspherical local Schwartz–Bruhat vectors. Then:

- finite Weyl covariance becomes available;
- conductor-dependent state spaces appear;
- local zeta factors change;
- the source must construct a relative determinant comparison back to the distinguished unramified section.

The second programme is not automatically an extension of the first.

## Failure of the proposed four-port shortcut

The wall–jump pair plus the archimedean even–odd pair is a valid global boundary packet. But calling its odd coordinate prime-labelled would overstate its source support.

A nonzero mixed block between prime valuation atoms and the archimedean odd port may arise through a global operation, but it must be computed from the full adelic tensor product. It cannot be inferred from local finite Weyl phase.

If the global operation factorizes placewise, the finite and archimedean factors remain separable, and no new finite odd direction appears.

## Verdict

The distinguished zeta vacuum is exactly the wrong source for a finite odd Weyl port: its finite factors are spherical and Fourier-fixed.

The programme must now choose. Either derive RH orientation inside the spherical valuation–theta system, or enlarge to ramified additive Tate carriers and prove a noncircular relative comparison back to the distinguished unramified zeta section.
