# Many-many directed positive-edge system

For finite indices `(L,F,N)` satisfying

$$
L\ge\delta_{L,F,N}+F+C_S+K_{F,N},
$$

let `E_(L,F,N)` denote the aligned positive common edge. The admissible indices form a directed set: two indices have a common successor obtained by taking the larger conductor and observer cutoffs and then increasing `L` above the finite threshold.

The source-labelled refinement maps satisfy the relative Gram transport law on this directed system. The finite many-many triangle therefore assembles into a directed pro-object of positive relative carriers.

A cofinal sequence gives a countable presentation of this pro-object. A global physical limit additionally requires packet-independent residual and tail estimates.
