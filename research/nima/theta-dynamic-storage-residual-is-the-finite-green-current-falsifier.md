## Input

For an interval \([a,b]\), let (S_{ab}) be the source-derived transfer and
let (W_{ab}) be its accumulated relationship energy. Their concatenation
laws are

\[
S_{ac}=S_{bc}S_{ab},
\qquad
W_{ac}=W_{ab}+S_{ab}^{*}W_{bc}S_{ab}.
\]

one fixed positive metric. Consequently, positivity cannot come from a static
KYP certificate.

## Dynamic replacement

Let (P_a) and (P_b) be endpoint storage forms, and let (B_{ab}) be the
typed boundary supply containing the primitive, prime-square, seam, and
archimedean currents. Define the finite-interval residual

\[
\Delta_{ab}
=
W_{ab}+S_{ab}^{*}P_bS_{ab}-P_a-B_{ab}.
\]

The proposed Green-current theorem is exactly

\[
\Delta_{ab}=0
\]

for every authorized finite interval and cutoff. This formulation does not
ask the shear itself to be passive. Energy may be stored, transported, and
exchanged with the typed boundary channels.

## Cocycle theorem

Assume the boundary supply composes by

\[
B_{ac}=B_{ab}+S_{ab}^{*}B_{bc}S_{ab}.
\]

Then direct substitution gives

\[
\Delta_{ac}
=
\Delta_{ab}+S_{ab}^{*}\Delta_{bc}S_{ab}.
\]

Thus exactness is local-to-global. A vanishing residual on a generating
interval decomposition forces vanishing on every concatenated interval.
Conversely, one nonzero residual on one finite source-labelled interval is a
complete falsifier of the proposed storage law.

## Why the typing matters

The equation is not valid after replacing (B_{ab}) by an untyped scalar
remainder. Each current must have a declared incidence into the same state
carrier on which (S_{ab}), (W_{ab}), and the endpoint forms act. Otherwise
the displayed subtraction is not defined.

The primitive and prime-square currents cannot be discarded as divergences:
they may be precisely the supply terms required for the cocycle identity to
survive restricted-product completion.

\[
