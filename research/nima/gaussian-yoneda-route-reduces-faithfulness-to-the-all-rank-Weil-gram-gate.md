# Gaussian Yoneda route reaches the all-rank Weil Gram gate

One fixed-width Gaussian and all its translates form a jointly faithful observer family. Therefore a continuous completed coherencer is uniquely determined by this observation profile.

For the source-derived Weil form `Q`, define

$$
K_\sigma(a,b)=Q(T_ag_\sigma,T_bg_\sigma).
$$

The remaining positivity condition is

$$
[K_\sigma(a_i,a_j)]_{i,j=1}^n\succeq0
$$

for every finite set of translation parameters and every rank `n`.

Fresh scouts at width `0.005` and spacing `0.25` give positive rank-three and rank-four packets. Their smallest reported margins are

$$
\lambda_{\min}^{(3)}\approx0.0238115
$$

and

$$
p_{\min}^{(4)}\approx0.0561568.
$$

These are numerical scouts, not directed source certificates.

Because Gaussian translates are Schwartz-dense, all-rank positivity of these source-derived Gram matrices is equivalent to positivity of the continuous Weil form on its Schwartz core, once the external source normalization is fixed. This is the RH-bearing gate, not an auxiliary observer-faithfulness lemma.

Thus the higher-coherence truncation has split cleanly:

1. observer separation: proved by Gaussian translate faithfulness;
2. existence of a positive observed filler: equivalent to all-rank source Weil Gram positivity;
3. uniqueness after existence: follows from joint faithfulness.

Additional isolated finite-rank scouts are tertiary and do not resolve the all-rank gate.
