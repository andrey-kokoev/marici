# Combining two compatible domain presentations

`agda/ComposeDomainWitness.agda` defines a first **composition operation on domain presentations relative to one WG**. Inputs are a certified first pair `(D₁,G)` and a second domain `D₂` on its generated admitted-state space, together with agreement and admission-closure proofs relative to the actual first generator. The combined domain over the original state space has dependent admission `Allowed₁(s) × Allowed₂(s,ok₁)`, the original computation, and a tagged sum of both law indices. First-domain laws are kept verbatim. Second-domain laws are transported to an equation on the original state coordinate using first-stage compatibility, second-stage compatibility, and projection of the second law. Neither law is inferred merely from matching carrier shapes.

`combined-input` retains the original base WG and supplies the combined admission closure. The directly compiled WG and the nested two-stage WG have definitionally equal flattened output states; their generated original `R`-witnesses agree. A certified-selection fixture checks an instance of that comparison. The combined relation is **not** identified with the full nested relation: the latter contains intermediate admission/comparison evidence and the full second law in its original dependent state type, whereas the combined law transports its projection to `S`. Thus this operation does not yet establish full witness-faithful equivalence of domain specifications or associative composition for three domains. The next gate is a composition object that retains the intermediate second law and witnesses (perhaps as a dependent provenance field), with an equivalence of full step packages and a coherent three-domain associator.
