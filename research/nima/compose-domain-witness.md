# Combining two compatible domain presentations

`agda/ComposeDomainWitness.agda` defines a first **composition operation on domain presentations relative to one WG**. Inputs are a certified first pair `(D₁,G)` and a second domain `D₂` on its generated admitted-state space, together with agreement and admission-closure proofs relative to the actual first generator. The combined domain over the original state space has dependent admission `Allowed₁(s) × Allowed₂(s,ok₁)`, the original computation, and a tagged sum of both law indices. First-domain laws are kept verbatim. Second-domain laws are transported to an equation on the original state coordinate using first-stage compatibility, second-stage compatibility, and projection of the second law. Neither law is inferred merely from matching carrier shapes.

`combined-input` retains the original base WG and supplies the combined admission closure. The directly compiled WG and the nested two-stage WG have definitionally equal flattened output states; their generated original `R`-witnesses agree. A certified-selection fixture checks an instance of that comparison. The **compressed** combined relation is still not identified with the full nested relation: it replaces the second dependent law by its projection to `S` and omits intermediate comparison evidence.

The subsequent proof-relevant construction `FullComposition` therefore retains the first source pair, second domain, second agreement and closure, and a generated `full-WG` with a correctness witness. Its relation is the *entire* nested relation, including intermediate law evidence. `full-step-iso` gives a two-sided isomorphism of full endpoint-and-witness packages between nested and directly flattened presentations; `full-second-law` recovers the original unprojected second law from an actual generated step. Both source records are recovered by reflexivity. This repairs the specific witness-loss of the compressed readout **for this relative two-stage composition**, but is not a general associativity law for composing `FullComposition` objects. The next gate is a typed three-domain composition operation with an associator, not merely a rebracketing of already nested records.
