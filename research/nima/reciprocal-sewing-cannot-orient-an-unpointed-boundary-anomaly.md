# Reciprocal sewing cannot orient an unpointed boundary anomaly

## Question

Can reciprocal reflection alone determine which charge survives in the
escaping-counterboundary model of the completed BSY anomaly?

## Finite reflection theorem

On the \(N\)-dimensional shift basis, let \(J_N\) reverse the order:

\[
J_Ne_j=e_{N-1-j}.
\]

For the boundary commutator

\[
C_N=[S_N^*,S_N]
=|e_0\rangle\langle e_0|-|e_{N-1}\rangle\langle e_{N-1}|,
\]

reflection exchanges its endpoint charges and therefore

\[
J_NC_NJ_N=-C_N.
\]

Let \(A_N\) be a linear scalar anomaly readout. If it is unpointed and
reflection invariant, then

\[
A_N(J_NC_NJ_N)=A_N(C_N).
\]

Linearity and the sign reversal give

\[
A_N(C_N)=-A_N(C_N),
\]

so \(A_N(C_N)=0\).

## Meaning

Reciprocal sewing supplies an exchange law between the two boundary ports. It
does not supply an origin on their two-point torsor. Any nonzero oriented
anomaly needs additional pointing data that transforms with the odd character
under reflection.

The minimum pointed readout is the difference of endpoint evaluations:

\[
A_N^{\mathrm{odd}}(C)=
\langle e_0,Ce_0\rangle-
\langle e_{N-1},Ce_{N-1}\rangle.
\]

It satisfies

\[
A_N^{\mathrm{odd}}(J_NCJ_N)=-A_N^{\mathrm{odd}}(C)
\]

and evaluates to two on \(C_N\). The symmetric endpoint sum is reflection
invariant and evaluates to zero.

## Theta--Tate consequence

The functional equation and reciprocal dagger can certify that the two
counterboundaries are paired correctly. They cannot select the sign of the
relative boundary anomaly while remaining unpointed. The missing source law
must provide an odd endpoint character or an equivalent oriented incidence.

Candidate source data must be audited in this order:

1. identify the two endpoint carriers before scalar completion;
2. derive reciprocal exchange between them;
3. derive a source endpoint pointing or odd character;
4. prove that the relative trace respects that character under refinement;
5. only then evaluate the completed anomaly.

The endpoint and gamma channels therefore cannot be treated as counterterms
that disappear after regularization. They are the only presently visible
source candidates capable of pointing the reciprocal boundary torsor.

## Hostile

Replace the endpoint difference by the endpoint sum. It preserves reciprocal
reflection exactly but annihilates every antisymmetric boundary commutator.
Thus symmetry, conservation, and correct exchange can all hold while the
orientation information is erased.

## Verdict

Reciprocal sewing is necessary coherence but cannot orient an unpointed BSY
boundary anomaly. Any nonzero source-derived sign must factor through a
separately authorized odd endpoint character. The next calculation is whether
the coupled endpoint--gamma incidence supplies that character before scalar
completion.
