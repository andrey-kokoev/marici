This generic test alone would be poorly discriminating, so the checker also constructs one interior positive source matrix in EACH of the six candidate cells. This rules out an immediate overlap or omission at those nine rational targets. It is finite evidence, NOT global coverage or global uniqueness. Open neighborhoods of individual cells follow from their local image-rank gates, but finite points cannot cover the full amplituhedron.

Every positive membership decision checks ALL 21 ordered minors and the exact zero pattern, not just the named two cyclic minors.

These do not settle untested walls or prove equality to physical generalized-R history forms.
