# The naive branch-to-union maps fail relation descent

The formula

\[
\frac{f}{q_{g1}q_{g2}q_{g3}q_{g23}}
\longmapsto
\frac{q_{g31}f}{q_{g1}q_{g2}q_{g3}q_{g23}q_{g31}}
\]

is an identity of rational forms, but the finite product-pole quotient packets
used in the rank census do not automatically provide a functorial map.  The
complete cutoff-seven audit maps every pivot relation of the four-mark domain
through this formula and reduces it against the five-mark target relations.

Of 5,780 fully represented domain relations,

\[
\boxed{852\text{ have nonzero target remainder}.}
\]

No target term was missing.  Thus this is not a boundary-degree omission in
the mapping code: the two independently truncated quotient presentations do
not retain a chain map between them.

Consequently the previously proposed branch images, their rank-15
intersection, rank-25 sum, transverse quotient line, quartic support gate,
Kato identification, and (15+5+5+1) filtration are withdrawn.  They were
statistics of chosen quotient representatives rather than invariants of a
typed morphism.

The following results survive:

1. the intrinsic five-mark quotient stabilizes at rank 26 and matches the
   exact Euler characteristic;
2. its literal physical numerator horizontally saturates that rank-26 packet;
3. labelled cyclic chart transport is natural in characteristic zero;
4. the exact marked-line crossing factorization is valid as geometry, without
   the withdrawn cohomological identification.

The correct next construction must derive both branch and union packets inside
one common localization/deletion complex before taking quotient or cutoff.
