# Opposite-null screen orientation

Fix spacetime and time orientation.  For the future null directions along
\(\pm z\), orient each screen so that

\[
(t,\widehat k,e_1,e_2)
\]

has the spacetime orientation.  In one ambient transverse frame, reversing
\(\widehat k\) therefore reverses the screen orientation:

\[
J_{-k}=-J_k,
\qquad
P_+(-k)=P_-(k).
\]

This is the geometrically forced helicity swap anticipated after Entry 1575.
It is not an ambiguity in the physical Cut: the correctly typed Cut is the
coevaluation in \(V_k\otimes V_{-k}^*\), invariant under the paired screen
transport \(S\otimes S^{-T}\).

Thus spacetime orientation, time orientation, Cut energy sign, and the in/out
choice determine the local helicity transport.  No fitted swap convention is
needed.
