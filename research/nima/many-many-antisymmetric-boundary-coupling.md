# Many-many antisymmetric boundary coupling

The antisymmetric reciprocal sector supplies the oriented boundary coordinate. Introduce a coupling

$$
L_-:\mathcal W^-_{\rm pair}\to\mathcal W_{\rm end},
$$

and place it in the global relative block

$$
\mathfrak Q_{\rm pair/end}
=\begin{pmatrix}
Q^-_{\rm pair}&L_-^*\\
L_-&Q_{\rm end}
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The source coefficient difference

$$
\alpha_{nm}-\alpha_{mn}
=\frac{m^2-n^2}{n^2+m^2}
$$

is retained in `L_-`. Reciprocal exchange reverses its sign, matching the orientation reversal of the linking form.

The symmetric sector may proceed to scalar codiagonalization. The antisymmetric sector remains boundary-relative until its coupling and radical are explicitly controlled.
