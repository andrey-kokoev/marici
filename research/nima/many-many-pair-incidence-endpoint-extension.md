# Many-many pair-incidence endpoint extension

Let `G_pair,int` be the interior pair-response graph and let `J_pair,end` retain

$$
\rho_{nm}(0),
\partial_zR_{nm},
\text{and endpoint Wronskian data}.
$$

The endpoint extension is the joint graph closure of

$$
\rho_{nm}\longmapsto
\bigl(\rho_{nm},\rho_{nm}(0),\partial_zR_{nm}\bigr).
$$

For finite pair packets, the first-order equation

$$
\rho'_{nm}+2\pi\kappa_{nm}t\rho_{nm}=\alpha_{nm}f_{nm}
$$

controls the derivative coordinate, while the endpoint graph retains the independent value coordinate. The direct-sum endpoint sector remains projective as the shell lower endpoint tends to zero.

The pair-to-wall map extends continuously when its endpoint rows are bounded in this joint graph norm and agrees with the finite-shell Laplace identity.
