frozen Wronskian readout gives

\[
J_{\mathrm{Wr}}(\kappa_p\Phi')=-\frac12\kappa_p>0.
\]

Thus the arithmetic side already has a normalized positive oriented output.
The boundary side has the positive output \(s_p\).  A typed comparison map
\(T_p\) between the retained boundary odd line and the completed-theta odd
line must therefore satisfy, on the distinguished generators,

\[
J_{\mathrm{Wr}}\,T_p(j_p^{\mathrm{St}})
=-\frac12\kappa_p,
\qquad
J_{\mathrm{St}}(j_p^{\mathrm{St}})=s_p.

\[

for \(T_p\), because the required half-density spectral-location comparison is
itself missing.
Defining \(T_p\) by this ratio alone would be circular unless the source
constructor independently supplies that map and proves it commutes with the
relevant incidence, completion, and Adams operations.
