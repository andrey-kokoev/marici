# Correction: the mixed Schur covariance is unique only within the equal-diagonal baseline ansatz

## Scope of the previous reduction

The formula

\[
R_{\rm mix}
=
\frac{\operatorname{Re}\alpha}{4}\sigma_z
+
\frac{\operatorname{Im}\alpha}{4}\sigma_y
\]

is exact for an incidence block

\[
C=wd^*+jc^*.
\]

It is the unique source of `sigma_y` and `sigma_z` only under the additional
ansatz that:

1. the unreduced target baseline has equal diagonals and real off-diagonal;
2. all missing polarization is generated by Schur elimination;
3. no direct relative-boundary term already carries `sigma_z` or `sigma_y`.

Those assumptions are not established for the complete Green target.

## Saturated parity constraint

On the Fourier-saturated boundary topology, the wall and tail character planes
have opposite Fourier-square characters. Subject to preservation of that
topology by the completed constructors, their cross Green block vanishes:

\[
G_{\rm wall,tail}^{\rm sat}=0.
\]

If the complete auxiliary operator and its generalized inverse preserve this
character decomposition, then

\[
\alpha_p
=\langle d_p,D_p^\dagger c_p\rangle
=0.
\]

This is incompatible with using the mixed Schur covariance to generate the
required nonzero `4u sigma_z` term.

## Correct fork

A successful target must therefore realize one of two source-derived cases.

### A. Saturated parity is preserved

Then `alpha_p=0`, and the required diagonal polarization

\[
4u\sigma_z
\]

must already be present in the direct relative endpoint/wall form before
Schur elimination. The ordered Stokes/Wronskian orientation remains a
separately typed linking block.

### B. The complete relative extension mixes the character sectors

Then `alpha_p` may be nonzero, but the construction must explicitly show why
the saturated wall--tail orthogonality theorem does not apply after domain
extension, radical quotient, or boundary coupling. The exact mixed target is
then conditional on the baseline contribution.

## Revised finite ledger

Write the complete target as

\[
G_\theta^{\rm eff}
=G_{\rm direct}-R_{\rm Schur}.
\]

The only unconditional requirement is the total Pauli table

\[
\operatorname{Pauli}(G_\theta^{\rm eff})
=(a+4b,\ a-4b,\ 4v,\ 4u).
\]

Neither `G_direct` nor `R_Schur` may be assigned the entire `sigma_z` entry
without a source decomposition theorem.

## Disposition

The proposed single-scalar equality for `alpha_p` is a useful test of the
mixed-Schur model, not yet the universal first-Adams gate. The immediate audit
is to decide whether completed relative Green transport preserves the
Fourier-saturated character splitting. That decision determines where the
missing `sigma_z` channel is allowed to live.
