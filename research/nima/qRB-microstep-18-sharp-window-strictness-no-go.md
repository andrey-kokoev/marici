# qRB microstep 18: sharp-window strictness no-go

The regulator classification already gives the decisive test:

$$
\widetilde Q_XC=CP_X
$$

is required for strict q-C-R compatibility. For an independently imposed sharp window this equality is not source-forced; its failure is precisely `E_X`.

Therefore no strictness claim may be made for the unlabeled regulator coordinate. The correct object is the stratified coordinate

$$
R=(R_{\rm place},R_{\rm transported},R_{\rm window}),
$$

with strict laws on the first two strata and a lax law on the third.

This closes the logical classification. It does not estimate the size of `E_X`; that quantitative estimate is a separate analytic problem.
