# Theta decay gives graph continuity, and continuous traces preserve the Green factorization under closure

## Parameter continuity on the theta incidence plane

Let \(v_0=\Phi\) and \(v_1=\Phi'\). Their completed theta decay is faster than every exponential:

\[
|\partial_u^j v_r(u)|
\le
C_{j,r,N}e^{-N|u|}
\]

for every fixed \(N>0\), after the wall modes have been removed.

For the Laplace graph form

\[
E_\sigma(f,g)
=
\int e^{2\sigma|u|}
f'(u)\overline{g'(u)}\,du
+
\langle\operatorname{Tr}f,
\operatorname{Tr}g\rangle,
\]

each matrix entry

\[
E_\sigma(\mathcal I v_r,\mathcal I v_s)
\]

is continuous, indeed real-analytic, in \(\sigma\) on every bounded positive interval. Differentiation under the integral is justified by domination with a stronger exponential weight.

Therefore the pullback Gram

\[
P_0(\sigma)
=
\mathcal I^{*}E_\sigma\mathcal I
\]

is norm-continuous on the two-dimensional source plane.

## A common core

Take the twisted history core consisting of smooth functions whose conjugated derivatives decay faster than every exponential and whose renormalized endpoint traces exist. The theta histories and their derivative companion belong to this core.

On the core, the first-order Green identity is exact:

\[
\langle A_+f,g\rangle
+
\langle f,A_-g\rangle
=
\langle\operatorname{Tr}f,
\Omega\operatorname{Tr}g\rangle.
\]

## Passage to closure

Let \(f_n\to f\) and \(g_n\to g\) in the corresponding twisted graph norms. Then:

- \(A_+f_n\to A_+f\) in the bulk Hilbert rung;
- \(A_-g_n\to A_-g\);
- endpoint traces converge because the trace maps are continuous;
- the two bulk pairings converge by Cauchy--Schwarz.

Taking limits in the core identity gives

\[
\langle A_+f,g\rangle
+
\langle f,A_-g\rangle
=
\langle\operatorname{Tr}f,
\Omega\operatorname{Tr}g\rangle
\]

on the closed reduced domains.

Hence the closed odd operator still factors through the endpoint trace:

\[
J_{\mathrm{cl}}
=
\operatorname{Tr}_-^{*}
\Omega
\operatorname{Tr}_+.
\]

No interior remainder is generated by closure.

## Uniform labelled transport

Mellin translation and valuation labels carry the common core isometrically to every \((p,k,L)\) fiber. Euler coefficients multiply both sides of the Green identity by the same scalar factors. Therefore graph continuity and closed boundary factorization persist uniformly under finite cutoffs and labelled direct sums.

The two remaining hypotheses from the metric-comparison theorem are now verified on the theta-generated incidence system:

1. \(P_0(\sigma)\) is continuous on compact off-seam intervals;
2. the actual closed odd operator factors through the authorized boundary traces.

Combined with the invertible exact trace matrix, this yields the uniform comparison

\[
c_-G_L
\le
\mathcal I_{p,L}^{*}E_{\sigma,L}\mathcal I_{p,L}
\le
c_+G_L
\]

and removes the dark-interior qualification on this source incidence range.
