# The prime-shell adjoint residual is an unchanged-Evans obligation, not the corrected-pair frontier

## Question

Should the SCC v2 missing constructor “complete prime-shell adjoint residual
family” be pursued on the current corrected pair lane?

## Claim boundary

No. That family tests promotion of the unchanged Evans state through

\[
B_\Sigma^\dagger u_z=0.
\]

Its shell residuals

\[
\mathcal S_{p,q}(z)
=\langle q^{1/2}b_q-p^{1/2}b_p,u_z\rangle_G
\]

have a strictly signed ordinary tail component and no known shellwise
compensator. This correctly refutes unchanged promotion.

The corrected pair lane uses a different source object. It already has the
linear chain

\[
P_{\rm pair}\longrightarrow B_{\rm jet}
\longrightarrow E_{\rm aug,p},
\]

retaining every shell label, endpoint jet, cutoff map, and orientation. Its
nonrapid diagonal coefficient is handled by the two-variable residue

\[
\operatorname{Res}_{\zeta=z}
\frac{\tau(z)A_{[a,b]}(z)}{\zeta-z}
=\tau(z)A_{[a,b]}(z),
\]

which is Xi-divisible and cutoff-natural. Therefore prime-shell trace
membership is constructed linearly and at diagonal-residue level for the
corrected source.

## SCC consequence

The registered `theta-rh-corrected-g4-v2` model still lists the prime-shell
adjoint family because its contract tracks the earlier unchanged-Evans
promotion. Applying that hostile to the corrected pair would compare different
source types.

For the corrected lane, the surviving obligation is metric:

- realize the pole--residue extension in a positive conservative Green carrier;
- keep its exact residue null in the derived quotient;
- compare the nonzero retained pair-state form with the independent arithmetic
  Green form.

## Disposition

Keep the countable shell residual family as counterevidence against unchanged
Evans promotion. Do not place it on the executable frontier of the corrected
pair lane. The current SCC classification needs a source-type split before its
missing-constructor list can govern the v19 architecture.
