# The Wronskian and seam-normal ports are exactly calibrated on the completed-theta odd line

## Result

On the one-dimensional source line generated by the completed-theta odd current \(\Phi'\), the Wronskian boundary port and the reciprocal seam-normal port are nonzero and exactly proportional.

This proves joint calibration on that line. It does not prove that the full source-authorized odd sector is one-dimensional.

## The source odd line

Let

\[
\mathcal O_\Phi=\mathbb C\Phi'.
\]

The reciprocal involution acts by

\[
R\Phi'=-\Phi',
\]

so \(\mathcal O_\Phi\) is a one-dimensional odd representation.

Two independently constructed observers act on this line:

1. the asymptotic Wronskian boundary observer;
2. the local reciprocal seam-normal observer.

## Wronskian coordinate

For the completed source, the previously established boundary moments are

\[
M_-(\Phi')=\frac14,
\qquad
M_+(\Phi')=-\frac14.
\]

Choose the oriented Wronskian odd coordinate

\[
J_{\mathrm{Wr}}(f)
=
M_+(f)-M_-(f).
\]

Then

\[
J_{\mathrm{Wr}}(\Phi')
=
-\frac12.
\]

The sign is fixed by ordering the positive boundary after the negative boundary. Reversing that boundary orientation reverses the coordinate.

## Seam-normal coordinate

Define

\[
J_{\mathrm{N}}(f)=f'(0)
\]

on reciprocal-odd scale currents. For \(f=\Phi'\),

\[
J_{\mathrm{N}}(\Phi')
=
\Phi''(0).
\]

The dominant-label theorem gives

\[
\Phi''(0)<0.
\]

Hence both observers are nonzero on the generator of \(\mathcal O_\Phi\).

## Exact linking coefficient

Since \(\mathcal O_\Phi\) is one-dimensional, the two observers are proportional there. Their source-derived coefficient is

\[
c_{\mathrm{N/Wr}}
=
\frac{J_{\mathrm{N}}(\Phi')}
{J_{\mathrm{Wr}}(\Phi')}
=
-2\Phi''(0)>0.
\]

Therefore

\[
J_{\mathrm{N}}
=
c_{\mathrm{N/Wr}}J_{\mathrm{Wr}}
\qquad
\text{on }\mathcal O_\Phi.
\]

Using the exact theta-label series,

\[
c_{\mathrm{N/Wr}}
=
-2
\sum_{n\ge1}
\lambda_n
P(\lambda_n)e^{-\lambda_n},
\]

where

\[
\lambda_n=\pi n^2,
\qquad
P(x)=32x^3-224x^2+330x-75.
\]

The coefficient is not fitted. It is fixed by the completed theta source and the two independently frozen orientation conventions.

## Orientation consistency

Under reciprocal reversal:

- the source generator \(\Phi'\) changes sign;
- the ordered Wronskian coordinate changes sign through boundary exchange;
- the seam-normal coordinate changes sign when the oriented normal line is reversed.

The ratio \(c_{\mathrm{N/Wr}}\) is positive and orientation-independent when both ports are reversed together.

If only one sign frame is reversed, the coefficient becomes negative. That mismatch is a typing defect, not a harmless normalization.

## Frame statement on the source line

For

\[
f=a\Phi',
\]

we have

\[
|J_{\mathrm{Wr}}(f)|
=
\frac{|a|}{2}
\]

and

\[
|J_{\mathrm{N}}(f)|
=
|a|\,|\Phi''(0)|.
\]

Thus either observer is individually faithful on \(\mathcal O_\Phi\), and the joint observer

\[
J_{\mathrm{joint}}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
J_{\mathrm{Wr}}\\
J_{\mathrm{N}}
\end{pmatrix}
\]

has squared output

\[
\|J_{\mathrm{joint}}(a\Phi')\|^2
=
|a|^2
\left(
\frac14+|\Phi''(0)|^2
\right).
\]

After choosing the source norm \(\|a\Phi'\|_{\mathrm{source}}=|a|\|\Phi'\|_{\mathrm{source}}\), this gives a fixed positive local frame constant.

## Rank qualification

The theorem is scoped to \(\mathcal O_\Phi\). It does not establish

\[
\dim\mathcal V_{\mathrm{odd}}=1
\]

for the complete arithmetic–analytic constructor.

A second odd direction could be invisible to both ports. Therefore the global theorem still requires either:

- a source selection law proving every admitted odd incidence factors through \(\mathcal O_\Phi\); or
- a joint rank audit on the larger odd sector, including the reciprocal Euler port.

This distinction prevents one-vector calibration from being promoted into full odd-sector faithfulness.
