# Source derivation of the realization choices

## 1. Cell designation

The maximal geometric coverage is source-supported. The existing census and completion results explicitly attach analytical forms to all 560 edges, 784 triangles, and 343 tetrahedra of `esd_7(Delta^3)`. The all-cell profile therefore follows from the repository's complete-coverage theorem when the realization target is the signed/relative category used there.

The universal-category note uses the word “designated” without defining a subset. The all-cell set is the unique profile matching the later complete-coverage artifacts. A smaller profile would require an additional source declaration.

**Disposition:** derived from the complete analytical census, relative to its signed/relative target.

## 2. Flag order

The ordering comes from the Freudenthal construction. Every maximal tetrahedron is generated by a base lattice point and an ordered sequence of three coordinate increments. Its vertices form the monotone chain

\[
z^{(0)}<z^{(1)}<z^{(2)}<z^{(3)}.
\]

The existing census serializes this as `increment_order`. Lexicographic cumulative-coordinate order recovers the same vertex chain because every increment is positive in one coordinate. Triangle orders are inherited by restriction.

The cone and octahedral flags should therefore be called **Freudenthal monotone flags**, rather than lexicographic choices.

**Disposition:** source-derived; the earlier terminology obscured its provenance.

## 3. Rank-four channel embedding

The map based on the selected polygon channel `(0,2)` is not source-derived. No source identifies the four tetrahedral coordinates with that polygon-channel orbit.

It is also unnecessary. The full realization uses

\[
N_*\mathbb C[\sigma]\widehat\otimes V
\]

and lets `rho` act directly on the labelled simplex-chain factor. The channel factor carries its own canonical polygon-coordinate action. Their tensor product realizes the semidirect relation without embedding tetrahedral coordinates into four selected channels.

**Disposition:** delete from the final construction; retain only as an example of a generator-level embedding.

## 4. Quadratic heat operator

The historical source already contains a trace-class weight

\[
w_s(t)=(1+t^2)^{-s}
\]

on its labelled rotor carrier. That operator belongs to the moving-divisor completion and is not the same as the channel-lattice heat operator.

On the universal channel lattice, the quadratic operator is characterized up to scale by three structural requirements:

1. channel locality/additivity:
   \[
   Q(m)=\sum_d Q_d(m_d);
   \]
2. reciprocal symmetry: `Q_d(k)=Q_d(-k)`;
3. invariance under channel relabelling.

For a quadratic regulator these force

\[
Q(m)=c\sum_d m_d^2.
\]

The constant `c>0` is absorbed into heat time. Hence

\[
N(m)=\lVert m\rVert^2
\]

is canonical for the channel-lattice model once locality, reciprocity, and relabelling invariance are imposed. It is structurally derived from the new universal carrier, while the older polynomial rotor weight remains the source-derived regulator for the prior carrier.

**Disposition:** canonical in the channel-lattice realization; comparison with the historical rotor regulator remains a regulator-independence question.
