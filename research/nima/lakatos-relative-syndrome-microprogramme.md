- owner: `marici.Nima`, coordinated with `marici.Benincasa`;

## Hard core

\[
\boxed{
\text{Stable decoder homology has physical meaning only through a
source-derived natural comparison with a predeclared relative object.}
}
\]

The established machinery statement is narrower:

\[
H_n^{(4)}\cong
\ker(\epsilon:\mathbb Q^4\to\mathbb Q)
\otimes(\mathbb Q^4)^{\otimes(n-1)}
\]

as a vector-space model for the four-branch decoder. The equality of its
dimension with any sector object provides no physical evidence.

## Protective belt

1. A source sector may derive \(k\ne4\) legitimate channels.
2. Its folding map need not be the ordinary all-ones augmentation.
3. The target may be a relative-support complex, extension object,
   obstruction space, or higher-coherence object.
4. Physical readout may select a subquotient of the stable syndrome.
5. A source comparison may exist only after derived localization or a
   character decomposition.

## Positive heuristic

\[
\text{derive channel object }A
\to
\text{derive folding }\epsilon
\to
\text{compute }\ker\epsilon
\to
\text{derive }\tau:H^{\rm decoder}\to\mathcal R_{\rm source}

Required comparisons include dimension, grading, character, support,
transition law, and compatibility with the source differential.

## Negative heuristic

Do not:

- infer meaning from \(3\cdot4^{n-1}\);
- identify equal-dimensional spaces;
- retrofit four physical channels to `model, record, obstruction, coherence`;
- fit a basis or projector for \(\tau\);
- alter the folding map after inspecting a target;
- call persistent homology an observable without physical readout.

## First falsifier: constructor-refinement dependence

For a freely selected branching number \(k\), the same decoder calculation
gives

\[
\dim H_n^{(k)}=(k-1)k^{n-1}.
\]

The companion checker verifies this for \(1\le k\le7\). Splitting or merging
one bookkeeping constructor changes the syndrome. Therefore the current
three-plane is not presentation-invariant under arbitrary changes of the
self-model vocabulary.

This immediately blocks its physical interpretation. It does not falsify the
RS-1 hard core; it activates it: a legitimate channel object and folding map
must be derived independently before any comparison is allowed.

## Progressive and degenerating shifts

A **progressive** result derives \((A,\epsilon,\tau)\) from source geometry and
predicts a new character, boundary, or readout behavior before calculation.

A **degenerating** move changes the constructor alphabet, folding, basis, or
target quotient to recover an observed rank.

## Crisis condition

RS-1 enters crisis if two source-equivalent presentations induce inequivalent
reduced channel objects, or if every proposed comparison requires fitted
choices. A source sector with no compatible relative object is a negative
result for that sector, not permission to weaken the hard core.
