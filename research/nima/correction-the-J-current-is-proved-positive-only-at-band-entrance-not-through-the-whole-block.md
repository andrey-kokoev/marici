# Correction: the J-current is oriented only at the band entrance

The theta log-concavity theorem proves

$$
J_a(0)-J_a(L)>0.
$$

It does not prove

$$
J_a(D)-J_a(D+L)\geq0
$$

for every `D` in `(0,L)`.

Therefore the full canonical block

$$
\int_0^L
\left\{
\beta\cos(bD)[J_a(D)-J_a(D+L)]
+
\alpha\sin(bD)[D W_a(D)-(D+L)W_a(D+L)]
\right\}dD
$$

cannot yet be described as a hostile weighted lobe plus an already-positive J-current. Only its entrance value has that orientation.

The weighted term has a single-crossing law after conditional integration, but that fact alone does not determine its integral. The J term also requires a full-band order theorem or must remain coupled to the weighted term.

The valid next target is positivity of the complete canonical block integral. Splitting it into independently signed J and W pieces is not currently justified.
