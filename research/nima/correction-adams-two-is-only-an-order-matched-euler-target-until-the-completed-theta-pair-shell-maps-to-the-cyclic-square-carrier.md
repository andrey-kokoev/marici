# Correction: Adams two is only an order-matched Euler target until the completed-theta pair shell maps to the cyclic square carrier

## Question

Does the exact agreement between the Adams-two pair weight and the required
\(p^{-2}\) shell order establish the missing diagonal reciprocal response
carrier?

## Claim boundary

No. Adams doubling is source-authorized on the Euler cyclic-grade carrier, but
the dominant completed-theta shell term lives on a different polarized pair
carrier. Equal prime order does not construct the comparison map. The Adams-two
range is a sharply typed target candidate, not yet the carrier of the physical
diagonal response.

## Two distinct diagonal objects

The completed-theta autocorrelation uses ordered theta-label pairs

\[
 (n,m)\in\mathbb N^2
\]

and its dominant late-shell term is the \((1,1)\) pair localized to the
arithmetic interval \([\log p,\log p^+]\).

The Adams-two object instead belongs to the Euler prime-power cyclic tower:

\[
 e_{p,k}\longmapsto e_{p,2k}.
\]

Its first reciprocal-balanced coefficient is

\[
 w_{p,2}^2=\frac14p^{-2}.
\]

The common prime symbol records the shell and Euler prime, but does not identify
theta pair label \((1,1)\) with Euler grade \(k=2\).

## Cyclic-square typing

The Euler square current is the second cyclic grade

\[
 \mathfrak c_2(L)=\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(L^2).
\]

It is polynomial in the Euler loop. It is not a second linear row of the
one-particle tail--seam state. After polarization it may be represented on a
pair carrier, but that representation requires the cyclic multiplication map.

Therefore a linear endpoint transpose cannot be declared to land in the square
current merely because both outputs scale as \(p^{-2}\).

## Required comparison chain

The missing construction must contain distinct arrows

\[
 \mathcal C_\theta\otimes\overline{\mathcal C_\theta}
 \xrightarrow{\;\operatorname{Loc}_p\;}
 \mathcal G_{\theta,\operatorname{pair}}^{[p,p^+]}
 \xrightarrow{\;T_{\theta\to\operatorname{cyc}2,p}\;}
 \operatorname{ran}(S_2\otimes S_2)
 \xrightarrow{\;T_{\operatorname{end}}\;}
 \mathcal G_{\operatorname{recip/link},p}.
\]

Here:

- \(\operatorname{Loc}_p\) retains the shell and theta ordered-pair labels;
- \(T_{\theta\to\operatorname{cyc}2,p}\) is the unconstructed additive-to-multiplicative comparison;
- \(T_{\operatorname{end}}\) supplies the Green endpoint normalization and analytic-transpose sign.

The first map is known from the explicit completed-theta pair density. The
second and third are not constructed.

## Mellin conditional expectation does not close the gap

On an Euler seam carrier \(bb^*\), Mellin averaging gives the prime-square
diagonal:

\[
 \mathbb E_{\rm M}(bb^*)
 =\operatorname{diag}(|c_p|^2/p).
\]

This is a lawful route from an Euler pair carrier to its invariant square
current. It does not map the completed-theta \((1,1)\) shell density into that
Euler pair carrier. Using it requires a prior source incidence.

## What the order match proves

The identity

\[
 w_{p,2}^2=\frac14p^{-2}
\]

proves only:

1. the Euler cyclic tower contains an authorized even-grade target with the
   correct first prime order;
2. its endpoint comparison would need coefficient \(-1/\pi\) under the current
   normalization;
3. odd Euler grades must be absent if this route realizes the full asymptotic
   selection rule.

It proves none of those absences or comparison arrows.

## Hostile

Choose two completed-theta pair densities with the same leading \(p^{-2}\)
order but different finite-shell kernels. Any map defined only by matching the
leading coefficient sends them to the same cyclic-square coordinate and loses
the density distinction. Such a map cannot establish exact shell cancellation
or jet compatibility.

## Disposition

Adams two remains the best currently typed Euler-side target for the diagonal
response, but it does not replace the missing additive-to-multiplicative
comparison. Candidate one now requires the explicit
\(T_{\theta\to\operatorname{cyc}2,p}\) and endpoint map, not another asymptotic
identification. No RH conclusion is authorized.
