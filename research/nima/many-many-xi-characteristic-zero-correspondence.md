# Many-many Xi characteristic-zero correspondence

With the forced transfer

$$
H(s)=\frac{2\xi(s)-1}{s(s-1)},
$$

the characteristic determinant becomes

$$
\det\mathsf M(s)=2\xi(s).
$$

Therefore the coupled endpoint–radial system has a nontrivial characteristic state exactly at zeros of `xi(s)`:

$$
\mathsf M(s)u=0,
\quad u\ne0
\Longleftrightarrow
\xi(s)=0.
$$

The source symmetry gives spectral reflection `s -> 1-s` and conjugation symmetry. The location of characteristic states is consequently encoded by the completed Xi divisor inside the relative interconnection.

A metric or dissipativity theorem for the Pontryagin coupling could constrain these characteristic states. Such a theorem would be an additional analytic consequence of the interconnection, with RH emerging as a spectral-location property.

Status: characteristic-zero correspondence established; spectral-location constraint remains open.
