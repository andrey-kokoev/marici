# The theta-connection joint graph is closed and label-uniform

## Operators

On \(L^2(\mathbb R,du)\), let

\[
S=-\partial_u^2+\frac14
\]

with its standard positive self-adjoint realization, and let

\[
T=e^{-u}\left(\partial_u-\frac12\right)
\]

be the maximal distributional first-order operator. The label-(n)
transported ray derivative is

\[
T_n=n^{-1}T.
\]

## Closedness of the transported derivative

Suppose

\[
f_k\to f,
\qquad
Tf_k\to g
\]

in \(L^2\). On every compact interval, multiplication by \(e^u\) is
bounded. Hence, distributionally on compact sets,

\[
\left(\partial_u-\frac12\right)f_k
=
e^uTf_k
\longrightarrow
e^ug.
\]

Passing to the distributional limit gives

\[
\left(\partial_u-\frac12\right)f=e^ug.
\]

Therefore \(f\in\operatorname{dom}T\) and \(Tf=g\). Thus the maximal
operator \(T\) is closed.

## Closed joint graph

Define

\[
\mathcal D_{S,T}
=
\operatorname{dom}S^{1/2}
\cap
\operatorname{dom}T
\]

with norm

\[
\|f\|_{S,T}^2
=
\|f\|^2
+
\|S^{1/2}f\|^2
+
\|Tf\|^2.
\]

If \(f_k\) is Cauchy in this norm, then its three graph components converge.
Closedness of \(S^{1/2}\) and \(T\) puts the common limit back in
\(\mathcal D_{S,T}\). Consequently \(\mathcal D_{S,T}\) is a Hilbert
space.

This supplies the previously missing closable history-connection carrier.

## Source cyclic carrier

Let \(\mathcal G_{\mathrm{odd}}\) be the algebraic odd
Gaussian-polynomial cyclic core generated by the curvature seed. Define the
source carrier by

\[
\mathcal K_{\mathrm{odd}}^{\mathrm{joint}}
=
\overline{\mathcal G_{\mathrm{odd}}}^{\|\cdot\|_{S,T}}.
\]

No claim is needed that the Gaussian core is a core for the full maximal
intersection domain. The source object is its own graph closure. This avoids
admitting left-translated Sobolev packets not generated by the constructor.

## Uniform label control

For every integer \(n\ge1\),

\[
\|T_nf\|
=
n^{-1}\|Tf\|
\le
n^{-1}\|f\|_{S,T}.
\]

Thus

\[
\|T_n\|_{
\mathcal K_{\mathrm{odd}}^{\mathrm{joint}}
\to L^2
}
\le
n^{-1}.
\]

The family is uniformly bounded, and higher theta labels are strictly
suppressed. The common topology must be defined using the unscaled operator
\(T\); defining a separate norm with \(T_n\) at each label would weaken
the left-end control as \(n\to\infty\).

## Reflection pair

For the two moving rays, use the common carrier twice and attach the opposite
connection signs:

\[
\mathbb T_n
=
\begin{pmatrix}
T_n&0\\
0&-T_n
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The ray-exchange reflection \(R\) then satisfies

\[
R\mathbb T_nR=-\mathbb T_n.
\]

This gives the reciprocal-odd typing on a closed graph domain without adding
a phase convention.

## Remaining distinction

Boundedness of

\[
T_n:
\mathcal K_{\mathrm{odd}}^{\mathrm{joint}}
\to L^2
\]

does not imply that \(iT_n\) is a bounded form relative to the weaker
energy \(S\). The joint graph norm has deliberately added control.

For the positive auxiliary block, one still needs either:

- an even energy containing the \(T^*T\) graph term; or
- a source estimate controlling the causal-minus-anticausal form by the
  declared even energy.

Using the joint norm as an energy without source authority would merely hide
the contraction problem.
