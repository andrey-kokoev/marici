# First attack on the Marici Interaction-Surface Conjecture

## Conjecture under attack

A stable public physical record should arise from a complete instrument whose
outcome and state-update maps descend and compose together.

## Attack

The newest cross-sector checker attached Lüders updates

\[
\mathcal I_i(\rho)=E_i\rho E_i
\]

to the established scattering and flavor effects. The algebra is exact, but
availability of this completion does not establish physical provenance.

The source audit finds:

1. Entry 1576 explicitly leaves detector effects, phase-space support, and
   accepted-event conditionalization outside the amplitude trace.
2. Entry 1586 derives fixed-kinematics Bell probabilities but leaves the
   angular-bin/accepted-event pushforward open.
3. The flavor source records weak-basis invariants, masses, and CKM data; it
   does not specify a repeatable projector measurement or its post-interaction
   state.

## Verdict

\[
\boxed{
\text{formal instrument completion exists}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{physical source selects it}.}
\]

The conjecture survives, but our claimed positive evidence for its
cross-sector realization does not. What remains established is the
state--effect probability interface. The state-update half is the new
provenance gate.

This is information-bearing: effects with identical probabilities can have
different backaction, so no amount of probability-table agreement can recover
the missing update map.

## Next severe test

Use one sector with explicit detector or transition dynamics. Construct its
completely positive outcome maps from the source interaction Hamiltonian,
relative-chain operation, or detector channel. Then test:

- outcome probabilities reproduce the existing readout;
- the nonselective update is trace preserving;
- the full instrument descends through internal equivalences;
- sequential composition is source coherent;
- public coarse-graining agrees at both record and update levels.

Until such a packet exists, “physical UI” remains an architectural
conjecture—not an established Carrier output.
