# Local Tate Oddness Is Not Reciprocal-Orbit Oddness

## Two involutions

The current programme contains two different \(C_2\)-actions.

The local Tate parity acts on the additive source variable:

\[
P:f(x)\longmapsto f(-x).
\]

The reciprocal zero action acts on the Mellin parameter:

\[
J:s\longmapsto1-s.
\]

The strictly exact local complex

\[
d:\mathcal S_{\rm ev}(\mathbb R)
\longrightarrow
\mathcal S_{\rm odd}(\mathbb R)\,dx
\]

is graded by \(P\). The Burnside deficit of an off-seam zero packet is the
sign component for \(J\). These gradings are not interchangeable.

## Product symmetry

Before an intertwiner is proved, the correct symmetry type is

\[
C_2^{P}\times C_2^{J}.
\]

A state has two independent characters. Removing the \(P\)-odd channel does
not imply removal of the \(J\)-odd channel. Likewise, exactness of the local
derivative complex does not compute the fixed-point mark of the reciprocal
zero packet.

This repeats the distinction already found in the topological sector between
a sheet involution and geometric ribbon reversal: commuting involutions can
look similar after scalar projection while carrying different authority.

## Required source bridge

To transfer the local Tate contraction to the RH obstruction, one must
construct one of the following:

1. an intertwiner identifying the relevant \(J\)-odd zero complex with a
   completed \(P\)-odd source subcomplex;
2. a bigraded complex with a differential whose contraction is proved on
   every component carrying the \(J\)-sign character;
3. a spectral sequence from local parity incidence to reciprocal-orbit
   cohomology, with convergence and no completion leakage.

The scalar Mellin transform does not supply this bridge because it compresses
the source variable before the reciprocal orbit type is read.

## Finite falsifier

Take a four-dimensional carrier indexed by independent parity and reciprocal
signs. Let \(P\) flip the parity coordinate and \(J\) flip the reciprocal
coordinate. Their odd projectors have equal rank but different images.

A vector can be \(P\)-even and \(J\)-odd. Any claimed implication from local
parity exactness to reciprocal fixed-point confinement fails on that vector.

## Revised frontier

The local Tate differential remains valuable: it proves that the
archimedean additive parity channel is source-paired before completion. It
does not yet contract the Burnside odd zero sector.

The next finite calculation is the joint \((P,J)\)-character table of the
doubled tail, seam, endpoint, primitive, and square-current ports, followed by
the actual differential matrix between those typed components.
