# Constructor coherence and Xi spectral identification: current gate status

## Verdict

G1.4, G2, and G4 remain open. Existing results identify the exact operator geometry and several necessary convergence cells, but they do not supply the source-authorized bidirectional theorem required for an RH deduction.

## G1.4: prime assembly

The completed-theta odd Jacobi contraction is prime diagonal and cutoff natural in its source half-density metric. This does not prove prime diagonality of the complete closed mixed Green/Stokes form, because endpoint lifts, wall routing, radical descent, and later sewing can introduce cross-prime components.

The required theorem is still

\[
Q_q B P_p=0
\qquad (p\ne q)
\]

for the fully assembled and closed mixed edge, or a completed Schur/Hilbert--Schmidt estimate for the off-diagonal kernel. Consequently G1.4 is partially realized but not closed.

## G2: constructor coherence

Abstract finite coherence has been reduced to a finite basis:

- binary assembly;
- associator and unitors;
- triangle and pentagon;
- reciprocal/dagger compatibility;
- completion-stable transport and Adams-ray end control.

This is a specification, not a source certificate. The repository does not yet contain one convergent, interface-preserving rewrite connecting the arithmetic and analytic constructors while preserving the complete Green block, wall data, prime labels, cutoffs, and reciprocal orientation. In particular, scalar Euler/Mellin agreement is insufficient.

G2 is therefore open at both the source-rewrite and completed-transport levels.

## G4: spectral identification

### Correct finite operator statement

For the complete Green form \\(B_X(s)\\) and arithmetic frame \\(F_X(s)\\), residual failure is the generalized eigenvalue equation

\[
F_X(s)h=B_X(s)h.
\]

When form domination and radical reduction authorize normalization, this becomes

\[
1\in\sigma\!\left(
B_X(s)^{\dagger/2}F_X(s)B_X(s)^{\dagger/2}
\right).
\]

This identifies the internal operator collision. It does not yet identify that collision with a completed Xi zero.

### Completion cell

Finite exclusion does not survive completion automatically. A fixed reduced carrier plus collective compactness and parameter-uniform strong convergence, or norm convergence, or norm-resolvent convergence of the generalized pencil is required to prevent spectral pollution at one. None has been proved for the fully assembled RH pencil.

### Divisor-faithfulness cell

A scalar seam boundary current cannot detect a reciprocal off-seam zero pair: reciprocal contributions cancel on the seam. Therefore bidirectional Xi identification must retain the normal parameter before restriction, through a full polarized logarithmic-derivative family, a faithful normal jet, or an equivalent two-sector observer.

### Clark-model circularity

The theta-derived Clark transfer gives an exact zero-support model only after assuming the Schur/Hermite--Biehler admission condition. Within the completed Xi class that contractivity condition is equivalent to RH. Hence this construction is a conditional spectral representation, not a noncircular proof of G4.

## Missing bidirectional theorem

To close G4, one theorem must prove, without assuming Xi nonvanishing or an equivalent positivity condition, that for every off-seam parameter \\(s\\),

\[
\Xi(s)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
1\in\sigma_{\mathrm{gen}}(F(s),B(s)),
\]

with:

1. source-authorized completed carriers and radicals;
2. reciprocal and archimedean completion;
3. both directions of implication;
4. equality of algebraic multiplicities;
5. parameter-holomorphic or meromorphic control;
6. completion spectral exactness at one;
7. no scalar seam projection before divisor identification.

No existing artifact supplies all seven items.

## Publication consequence

The manuscript may state:

- the semidefinite Green-pencil criterion;
- the spectral-exactness requirements;
- the conditional Clark representation;
- and the scalar-seam no-go.

It may not state that the completed Green collision has been identified bidirectionally with Xi zeros. The conditional RH deduction must remain conditional.
