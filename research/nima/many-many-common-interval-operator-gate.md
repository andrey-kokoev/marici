# Many-many common interval-operator gate

$$
\operatorname{Tr}K_a=J_a^{(1)},
$$

$$
\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(K_a^2)=J_a^{(2)},
$$

$$
\det_3(I-K_a)^{-1}.
$$

$$
\det(I-K_a)^{-1}
=e^{J_a^{(1)}+J_a^{(2)}}
\det_3(I-K_a)^{-1}.
$$
