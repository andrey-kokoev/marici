# The two closed squares still lack the middle natural transformation

## Two independent closed results

The repository now contains two exact labelwise constructions.

### Analytic first-Adams square

The base-plus-curvature source satisfies

\[
s_p=Pb_p,
\qquad
\mathcal C\mathcal M_n=\mathcal M_nP,
\]

and therefore

\[
\mathcal M_n^{-1}\mathcal C^{-1}\mathcal M_ns_p=b_p
\]

on every retained theta label.  Exponential conjugation then sends the
primitive and square reciprocal pairs into their closed half-density history
trace lines.

### Arithmetic sampling square

The Euler prime-power current has the explicit theta incidence

\[
\nu_{p,k}^{\mathrm{Euler}}
\longmapsto
\kappa_p^{(k)}\Phi',
\]

and the Wronskian readout is

\[
J_{\mathrm{Wr}}(\kappa_p^{(k)}\Phi')
=-\frac12\kappa_p^{(k)}.
\]

This square is reciprocal-equivariant, labelwise faithful, and cutoff-natural.

## Why the two squares do not yet compose

The analytic square starts from the Gaussian front line generated by
\(q_{kL}=U_{-kL}f_0-U_{kL}f_0\).  The arithmetic square starts from the Euler
current line generated by \(\nu_{p,k}^{\mathrm{Euler}}\).  These are different
source objects.

To compose the squares one needs a middle natural transformation

\[
\Theta_{p,k}:
\operatorname{span}\{q_{kL}\}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{span}\{\nu_{p,k}^{\mathrm{Euler}}\}

after the declared orientation twist, or the corresponding arrow between
their completed source modules.

Neither closed square supplies \(\Theta_{p,k}\).  Sharing the labels \((p,k)\),
reciprocal parity, and a one-dimensional output does not identify the source
lines.

## The ratio map is unique but not constructed

On one-dimensional fibers, commutativity forces the scalar of any such map.
For \(k=1,2\), after the source sign

\[
\sigma_1=-1,
\qquad
\sigma_2=+1,
\]

the half-density front trace is

\[
\sigma_kt_{p,k},
\qquad
t_{p,k}=2\sqrt2e^{1/(16\pi)}\sinh\frac{kL}{2}>0.
\]

Hence the only possible scalar is

\[
\theta_{p,k}
=\frac{-\kappa_p^{(k)}/2}{\sigma_kt_{p,k}}.
\]

This reproduces the diagonal matrix \(S_{12}D_{p,12}\).  But defining
\(\Theta_{p,k}\) by this quotient merely forces the desired commuting square;
it does not derive the arrow from the constructor.  It is a uniqueness result,
not an existence result.

## Required source evidence

A noncircular construction of \(\Theta_{p,k}\) must arise from an already
source-declared operation, for example:

1. a valuation-to-front boundary map whose transpose is the Euler sampling
   current;
2. a Green adjunction identifying the front generator with the Euler current
   in a common dual pair;
3. a universal property of the arithmetic--analytic pushout that produces the
   arrow before evaluating either trace;
4. an explicit kernel intertwining prime-power evaluation with Gaussian
   translation and commuting with \(P\), \(\mathcal C\), and reciprocal
   reflection.

The proof must recover \(\theta_{p,k}\) after applying traces, rather than use
that scalar to define the map.

## Earliest unresolved implication

The local frontier is now exactly:

> Construct the middle natural transformation \(\Theta_{p,k}\) on grades
> \(k=1,2\), before trace scalarization, and prove that its trace is the forced
> diagonal coefficient \(S_{12}D_{p,12}\).

The connected tail requires its own extension of this transformation.  Until
such an arrow exists, the resolved positive Gram and arithmetic linking block
remain parallel source constructions rather than one assembled quadratic
form.

Conditional scalar positivity is closed, but common-carrier existence,
radical descent, and global closed range remain open.  No RH conclusion is
authorized.
