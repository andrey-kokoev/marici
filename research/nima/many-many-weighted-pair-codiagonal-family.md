# Many-many weighted pair codiagonal family

For a finite labelled family `F` and source weights `omega_lambda,mu`, define

$$
\mathcal C_{\mathcal F}(x)
=\sum_{(\lambda,\mu)\in\mathcal F}
\omega_{\lambda\mu}
C_{\rm wall}B_{\rm rad}(x_{\lambda\mu}).
$$

Reciprocal symmetry requires

$$
\omega_{\lambda\mu}=\overline{\omega_{\mu\lambda}}.
$$

The weighted family is continuous whenever

$$
\sum_{\lambda,\mu}
|\omega_{\lambda\mu}|
\|C_{\rm wall}B_{\rm rad}(e_\lambda\otimes\overline{e_\mu})\|<\infty.
$$

On interior shells this follows from the Gaussian pair bound. The endpoint sector uses the heat/projective topology. Finite-family restrictions commute with the weighted codiagonal when weights restrict consistently.
