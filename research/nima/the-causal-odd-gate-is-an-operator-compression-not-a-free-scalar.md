# The causal odd gate is an operator compression, not a free scalar

## Correction of the scalar target

The positive wall polarization fixes the even boundary metric as

\[
S_{\partial}=I.
\]

It does not reduce the causal history to one scalar. On the completed history
carrier the source-native odd part is

\[
T_{\mathrm{hist}}=\frac{H-H^{*}}{2},
\qquad
iT_{\mathrm{hist}}=\frac{i}{2}(H-H^{*}),
\]

where \(H\) is the causal history and reciprocal reflection gives its
anti-causal adjoint. The finite coefficient \(\tau\) appears only after a
declared two-channel incidence compression.

Let \(V:\mathbb C^2\to\mathcal H_{\mathrm{hist}}\) be the source incidence
from the normalized reciprocal wall module. If the compressed odd form has
the standard orientation, then

\[
V^{*}iT_{\mathrm{hist}}V
=
i\tau
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-1\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Thus \(\tau\) is determined by \(H\) and \(V\); it is not an independent
normalization parameter.

## Relation to the scalar inequality

If \(V\) is isometric for the source wall metric and the compressed even
block is exactly \(I\), positivity of both compressed reciprocal blocks
implies

\[
|\tau|<1.
\]

But the converse controls only the selected two-dimensional compression. It
does not establish positivity of the full history block unless the source
also proves that the incidence range is reducing and exhaustive for the
auxiliary return, or separately controls the orthogonal complement.

Therefore the former publication gate “derive \(\tau\) and prove
\(|\tau|<1\)” was too weak as a completed theorem. The authoritative order
is:

1. construct \(H\), \(H^{*}\), and their common closed form domain;
2. prove reflected-adjoint covariance;
3. prove the graph-energy identity from the source wall and history;
4. prove uniform lower bounds for \(I\pm iH\);
5. construct the two-channel incidence \(V\);
6. compress to obtain the source coefficient \(\tau\);
7. identify its sign and magnitude with the Euler or Wronskian odd port.
