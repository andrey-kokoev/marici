# The RH interface is multiplicative Fock versus additive Poisson

## Status

Research packet. It identifies the algebraic type of the missing pre-trace
lift and supplies a coherence-square falsifier. It does not construct the
required distributive correspondence or prove RH.

## Two different compositions

The source has two genuinely different organizing laws.

The Euler/Fock side is multiplicative. Prime constructors generate typed
prime-power states, unique factorization controls their composition, and the
Euler product is nonzero in its convergent chamber because it is assembled
from nonzero local factors.

The continuation side is additive. Fourier transform and Poisson summation
split the completed Tate section into two reciprocal bulk terms and two polar
boundary currents. Their final readout is a sum, so nonzero typed channels
can cancel.

Thus analytic continuation is not simply extension of one scalar formula. It
crosses from multiplicative factorization to additive sewing.

## Where zeros enter

Local multiplicative Tate transitions are invertible and cannot create a
divisor. The zero-bearing operation is the additive Poisson aggregation

\[
\Sigma=(1,1,1,1)
\]

on the four continuation channels. Its kernel is three-dimensional, and an
exact Fourier-fixed hostile source occupies that kernel at off-seam points.

The full seam germ and Mellin orbit remain faithful to finite labelled states,
but evaluation at one spectral parameter is lossy. Enlarging the detector
does not by itself constrain the distinguished scalar matrix coefficient.

## The missing object is a distributive correspondence

A nontrivial pre-trace lift must retain both structures and specify how they
interact:

\[
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{mult}}
\overset{\mathfrak D}{\longleftrightarrow}
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{add}}.
\]

Here:

- \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{mult}}\) is the prime-valuation/Fock constructor
  system;
- \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{add}}\) is the Schwartz–Poisson boundary system;
- \(\mathfrak D\) is a source-derived distributive or bimodule
  correspondence carrying prime constructors into typed Poisson boundary
  incidences.

The Tate integral already signals this architecture: it relates additive
adeles to multiplicative ideles on a rigged test-function space. The ideles
are additive-Haar null, so the correspondence cannot be reduced to a bounded
restriction between the completed \(L^2\) spaces.

## Coherence square

For every admitted prime or prime-power constructor \(P\), the source must
derive an additive boundary action \(\widetilde P\) and a comparison cell

\[
\mathfrak D\circ P
\Longrightarrow
\widetilde P\circ\mathfrak D.
\]

At a finite cutoff, the residual

\[
\mathfrak R_{P,X}
=
\mathfrak D_XP_X-\widetilde P_X\mathfrak D_X
\]

is the smallest distinction-preserving falsifier. It must be computed before
the four channels are summed by \(\Sigma\).

The primitive-prime, prime-square, seam, connected-tail, and archimedean
coordinates are not optional corrections. They are the typed components
required for this square even to be defined.

## Why a larger Hilbert space is insufficient

An operator dilation with a reducing scalar trace line merely embeds the
divisor. A faithful seam germ or Mellin orbit merely remembers the state away
from the chosen point. A positive two-variable kernel merely supplies a
Gramian. None tells multiplication how to pass through additive continuation.

The distributive correspondence adds new information only if:

1. prime constructors act nontrivially on the additive boundary carrier;
2. both incidence directions are source-derived;
3. the trace line is not a reducing subrepresentation;
4. the coherence residuals vanish before scalar readout;
5. these properties survive the constructor-generated completion;
6. hostile Fourier-fixed sources fail because they lack a required
   multiplicative constructor or violate one named square.

## Relation to analytic continuation

This reframes the historical intuition that analytic continuation created a
second sector. The added sector is not only another region of the complex
plane. It introduces a different composition law:

- Euler factorization organizes arithmetic multiplicatively;
- Poisson continuation organizes boundary data additively;
- the completed section is produced at their interface.

The critical seam is the unitary locus of reciprocal additive sewing. RH
would require an additional theorem that the actual multiplicative source,
transported through the distributive correspondence, cannot land in the
additive aggregation kernel away from that seam.

## Hostile-source gate

The degree-twelve Fourier-fixed hostile already proves that additive
Fourier–Poisson coherence plus positive integer samples is insufficient. A
successful distributive law must reject it for a precise source-local reason.

Possible legitimate failures include:

- absence of the prime-power constructor grammar;
- failure of the primitive-current square;
- failure of the prime-square boundary incidence;
- incompatibility with the archimedean constructor;
- or loss of continuity in the restricted-product completion.

If the hostile satisfies every proposed square, the correspondence has no RH
force.

## Decisive outcomes

1. A source-derived distributive correspondence exists, has nonreducing
   arithmetic incidence, and rejects the hostile. This supplies genuinely new
   structure before scalar projection.
2. The correspondence exists but every prime action becomes reducing or dark
   after Poisson transport. The higher lift adds no information.
3. No typed action \(\widetilde P\) or comparison cell can be constructed.
   Then multiplicative arithmetic and additive continuation meet only at the
   scalar Tate functional in the present architecture.

## Conclusion

The live RH problem is no longer just a two-sector spectral problem. It is a
compatibility problem between multiplicative arithmetic construction and
additive analytic continuation. The missing operator lift is the
source-derived distributive correspondence between them. Its first finite
coherence residual is the honest next falsifier.
