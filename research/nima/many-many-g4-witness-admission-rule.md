# Many-many G4 witness admission rule

A generator witness is admissible only when the G4 evaluation is supplied independently of the analytic pair-to-border construction.

The witness compares

$$
U_{G4}(f\otimes\overline g)
\quad\text{against}\quad
\mathcal T_{\rm pair\to border}(f\otimes\overline g).
$$

Reusing the right-hand side as the G4 evaluation produces a self-consistency fixture. It verifies the analytic map internally and carries no identification authority.

An admitted witness must include provenance for the G4 evaluation, the finite source labels, both variance lanes, and the residual packet. Zero residual on a generating family then extends by continuity.

Status: witness admission rule fixed; independent G4 evaluation absent.
