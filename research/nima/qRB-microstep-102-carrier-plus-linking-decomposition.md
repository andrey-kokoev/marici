# qRB microstep 102: carrier plus linking decomposition

On the two-output carrier `Y_link=H_orb direct_sum H_orb`, use the ordinary positive norm

$$
\|(f_+,f_-)\|_{\rm car}^2
=\|f_+\|^2+\|f_-\|^2.
$$

The linking operator `G_link` is an additional bounded Hermitian polarization acting on this carrier. It is not the carrier metric and need not be positive.

The relative qRB datum is therefore

$$
(\,Y_{\rm link},\ \|\cdot\|_{\rm car}^2,\ G_{\rm link},\ J_2\,).
$$

A positive stabilized form may be introduced only after an explicit norm bound for `G_link`; such stabilization changes the carrier presentation, not the signed linking readout.

Status: carrier/polarization separation fixed; no positivity claim is made for the linking operator.
