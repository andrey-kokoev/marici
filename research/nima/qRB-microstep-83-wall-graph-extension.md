# qRB microstep 83: wall-graph extension

The algebraic spectral observer extends continuously to the wall graph domain if the source readout satisfies

$$
|W_S(u*v^*)|
\le C\|u\|_{\rm wall}\|v\|_{\rm wall}
$$

on the countable core.

Prior research supplies this type of estimate for the regular-plus-wall linking form using the prime half-density and jump bounds. If the algebraic spectral readout is identified with that continuous linking form, it extends uniquely to the wall completion by density.

The identification is an additional source-normalization step; continuity of the linking form alone does not prove equality with the Weil readout.
