# Many-many trace-class response continuity

Assume the bilinear bordered response satisfies

$$
\|\mathcal B_{\rm rad}(f,g)\|_{\rm graph}
\le
M\|f\|_{H_{\rm ar}}\|g\|_{H_{\rm ar}}
$$

on the admitted Hilbert source core. The projective tensor universal property then gives a unique bounded linear response

$$
\mathscr B_{\rm rad}:\mathfrak S_1(H_{\rm ar})
\to
\mathcal G_{\rm pair,rad},
$$

with

$$
\mathscr B_{\rm rad}(|f\rangle\langle g|)
=\mathcal B_{\rm rad}(f,g).
$$

Reciprocal exchange is adjoint on the trace-class source, and the response map is equivariant when the original bilinear form is reciprocal.

Interior Gaussian bounds supply `M` on finite shells. Endpoint/projective bounds and global Hilbert completion remain separate source gates.

Status: trace-class response continuity criterion fixed; Hilbert-source boundedness remains conditional.
