# The required complex lines are Xi-zero-free but raw supremum deconvolution is exponentially unbounded

## Question

Can the two-line projective recovery observer be transported from the deconvolved response to the raw completed-theta history by division along those lines?

## Claim boundary

The required lines lie outside the critical strip, so their completed-theta factor is zero-free for source-classical reasons and does not require RH. However, the factor decays exponentially along each horizontal line. Its reciprocal is therefore unbounded in the raw supremum norm. Pointwise division is algebraically legitimate but not a bounded raw-history observer.
