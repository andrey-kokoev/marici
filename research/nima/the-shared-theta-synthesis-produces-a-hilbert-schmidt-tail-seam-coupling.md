\[
(Gc)(t)=\int_0^\infty\Phi(t+p)c(p)\,dp

\[
(Hc)(t)=\int_t^\infty\Phi(p-t)c(p)\,dp.

\[

\[

## Operator classes

The Hankel kernel of \(G\) is \(\Phi(t+p)\). Its Hilbert–Schmidt norm is

\[
\lVert G\rVert_2^2
=
\int_0^\infty\int_0^\infty
|\Phi(t+p)|^2\,dt\,dp
=
\int_0^\infty u|\Phi(u)|^2\,du.
\]

For the theta source this is finite.

The seam operator \(H\) is a one-sided convolution/correlation with \(\Phi\). If \(\Phi\in L^1(0,\infty)\), Young’s inequality gives

\[
\lVert H\rVert\le\lVert\Phi\rVert_1.
\]

It is generally not Hilbert–Schmidt on the unrestricted half-line because its kernel has infinite translation multiplicity.

The ideal property now yields

\[

\]

\[
\lVert GH^*\rVert_2
\le
\lVert G\rVert_2\lVert H\rVert,
\qquad
\lVert HG^*\rVert_2
\le
\lVert H\rVert\lVert G\rVert_2.
\]

Thus the first nonzero analytic off-diagonal block exists and is Hilbert–Schmidt.
