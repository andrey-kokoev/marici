# qRB microstep 97: conditional noncompact closure

Prior research proves that the regular-plus-wall linking form is continuous on the wall graph carrier:

$$
|\omega_{\rm link}(f,g)|
\le C\|f\|_{\rm wall}\|g\|_{\rm wall}.
$$

Therefore, if the separately defined source readout and `omega_link` are both placed on the same graph carrier, and cutoff approximants converge in the wall graph norm, then

$$
Q_{\rm proj}(f_L,g_L)
\longrightarrow
\omega_{\rm link}(f,g).
$$

This gives a cutoff-independent noncompact extension conditionally on the readout identity.

The remaining gap is compatible block placement and owner adoption of the bounded skew linking operator; no equality between the source form and linking form is required.
