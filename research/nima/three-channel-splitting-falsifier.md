# Three-Channel Splitting Falsifier

## Target

The repaired Deferred-Coherence Conjecture distinguished boundary/value data,
coherence-defect data, and an operational-record interface. Its strongest
remaining reading said that the first two channels are transported separately.

## Minimal exact countermodel

Let (E) have basis ((v,c)), where (v) represents value data and (c)
coherence-defect data. Let one transport step act by

\[
T=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The defect line (C=\langle c\rangle) is invariant, and the quotient
(E/C\simeq V) transports trivially. Thus both typed grades exist canonically:

\[
0\longrightarrow C\longrightarrow E\longrightarrow V\longrightarrow0.
\]

But no transport-invariant complement exists. Any complement is generated by
(v+a c), while

\[
T(v+a c)=v+(a+1)c.
\]

Invariance would require (a+1=a). Moreover,

\[
T^n=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\n&1\end{pmatrix},
\]

so the mixing accumulates under composition rather than disappearing.

## Falsification

\[
\boxed{\text{typed distinction}\not\Rightarrow\text{separate transport}.}
\]

The three-channel direct-sum picture is too rigid. The surviving structure is
a filtered extension: value and defect are canonical associated grades, while
their coupling is extension data. An operational record is then a typed
morphism out of the total extension object, not generally a third parallel
wire.

## Surviving Deutsch--Popperian conjecture

An interaction carries a filtered information object

\[
0\to C_{\rm coh}\to E_{\rm int}\to V_{\rm bdry}\to0,
\]

possibly with higher grades. Witnesses determine whether its extension class
is filled, localized, retained, or mapped into an operational record. The
extension must be derived from source transport; a chosen splitting has no
physical authority.

This predicts precisely the recurring Marici failure mode: associated grades
can be correct while a proposed absolute decomposition or quotient is not.
