# A witness-retaining representation of the correlated two-electron trial

## Question

Represent the existing helium trial in WG's typed state/witness interface rather than further optimizing conventional helium physics. Test whether the representation retains its source and pair relation and reproduces the existing readout. Active obligation: forward realization and readout preservation for a supplied kernel, not physical selection of that kernel.

## Claim boundary

`agda/CorrelatedPairWitness.agda` constructs a `TypedGeneratorLayers.Layer1` adapter parameterized by types of parameters, configurations and scalars, a supplied independent orbital, a supplied correlation factor and scalar multiplication. States are independent or correlated with the same parameters. The generating step attaches the factor and returns a typed witness linking the exact source and target. Reapplying the generator to a correlated state retains it rather than multiplying the factor twice.

The module proves:

- generated evaluation is exactly the supplied orbital times supplied factor;
- generation preserves the parameter record;
- the initial construction has an explicitly retained one-step history;
- spatial electron-exchange symmetry is inherited from supplied symmetry proofs of the orbital and factor.

The spin-singlet interpretation is external to this scalar theorem. The generic module also instantiates the existing `MetaWitnessNativeBridge` into the separately application-extended runtime. Executing there remains conditional on derivations of the function and argument inputs. This is not a derivation from the frozen twelve-rule signature or a proof that WG selects an electronic Hamiltonian.

\]
