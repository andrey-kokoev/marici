# Coefficient-domain obstruction to channel recovery

## Question

Do faithful complex quotient characters detect actual rational channel-image cosets?

## Claim boundary

Distinguish the rational points C(Q) of the channel-image algebraic torus from the image of rational channel parameters. These need not coincide. Previous faithful-coordinate claims explicitly used complex coefficients.

At six points assign every triangulation weight the rational value two. This satisfies each local cross-product identity. It also comes from rational triangle factors: put two on triangles incident to a fixed boundary edge and one elsewhere. Both saturated quotient characters evaluate to one.

If rational channel scales represented these weights, every flip would equate its two exchanged scales. The connected crossing graph forces a common rational z, and each triangulation then requires z^3=2. Taking the 2-adic valuation gives 3 v_2(z)=1, impossible for rational z. Thus this point and the unit point have identical quotient coordinates but distinct cosets modulo the image of rational channel parameters.

More generally, the constant-weight family a has a channel solution over a field k precisely when a belongs to (k*)^(n-3), by the same flip argument. Its obstruction is the power class of a, not an additional complex torus character. Over the reals the constant-family obstruction disappears for odd n-3, but negative a fails for even n-3. No classification of nonconstant weights over arbitrary fields is inferred here.

## Disposition

The proposed extension of complex coset detection to rational parameter-image cosets is refuted. The checker verifies six-point crossing connectivity, trivial quotient coordinates, the valuation obstruction, and the positive control a=8 with all channel scales two. General recovery needs a coefficient-field power-class test in addition to geometric image membership.
