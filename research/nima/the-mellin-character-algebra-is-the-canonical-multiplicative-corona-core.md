# The Mellin Character Algebra Is the Canonical Multiplicative Corona Core

## The source supplies its own product

For each real frequency \(t\), define the prime-label Mellin character

\[
\chi_t(p)=p^{-it}.
\]

These characters are closed under the operations required of a commutative
involutive algebra:

\[
\chi_t\chi_u=\chi_{t+u},
\qquad
\chi_t^*=\chi_{-t},
\qquad
\chi_0=1.
\]

Let \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm M}\) be the uniform closure of their finite linear
span. Unlike the full prime-harmonic mean domain, this source-native subspace
is a commutative unital C-star algebra. Its multiplication is not fitted at
the corona; it is inherited from composition of Mellin transports.

## Prime-harmonic mean becomes Haar selection

The prime number theorem and partial summation give, for fixed \(t\ne0\),

\[
\sum_{p\le X}\frac{p^{-it}}p=O_t(1).
\]

Since \(H_X\) diverges,

\[
\lim_X\omega_X(\chi_t)=0
\qquad
(t\ne0),
\]

while \(\omega_X(\chi_0)=1\). Therefore the corona state restricts to

\[
\omega_\infty(\chi_t)=
\begin{cases}
1,&t=0,\\
0,&t\ne0.
\end{cases}
\]

This is the Haar coefficient rule on the Bohr compactification of the Mellin
flow. The corona does not choose a phase frame. It retains exactly the
frequency-neutral part of a relationship.

## Relationship energy survives when individual phases vanish

For \(t\ne0\),

\[
\omega_\infty(\chi_t)=
\omega_\infty(\chi_{-t})=0,
\]

but

\[
\omega_\infty(\chi_t\chi_{-t})
=
\omega_\infty(1)
=1.
\]

Thus the corona state is positive but not multiplicative. Two individually
invisible phase channels can have a fully visible correlation. This is not a
defect: it is the precise relationship energy retained by the coherencer.

The construction also explains why arbitrary multiplication failed on the
larger mean domain. Multiplication is authorized only where the source
transports close under composition. The Mellin character algebra has that
closure; an arbitrary bounded prime observable does not.

## Typed extension by finite-label data

Finite-label observables form the ideal \(c_0(\mathbb P)\), which the corona
state annihilates. The C-star algebra generated by
\(\mathcal A_{\mathrm M}\) and \(c_0(\mathbb P)\) therefore carries both:

1. local labelled probes, observed before passage to the corona;
2. global Mellin relationships, observed by zero-frequency selection at the
   corona.

The two kinds remain typed. Killing the local ideal does not reconstruct its
labels, and Haar selection does not become a character.

## Finite falsifier

Any proposed corona multiplication that returns

\[
\omega_\infty(\chi_t\chi_{-t})=0
\]

has multiplied after scalar observation and erased the relationship. Any
proposal returning a nonzero value for an isolated nonzero character has
inserted a phase frame not selected by prime-harmonic averaging.

The exact finite model is a cyclic character group. Uniform averaging kills
every nontrivial character, preserves the identity, and retains every
conjugate-pair product.

## Frontier

The multiplicative corona core is now source-derived. What remains is to
identify whether the endpoint, gamma, primitive, and square currents define
elements or continuous modules over this algebra. Failure of module closure
would locate the residual channel more sharply than another scalar
