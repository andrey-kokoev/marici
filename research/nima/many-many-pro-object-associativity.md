# Many-many pro-object associativity

For indices `alpha <= beta <= gamma`, source-labelled refinement maps satisfy

$$
R_{\gamma\alpha}=R_{\gamma\beta}R_{\beta\alpha}
$$

on the common source core. Therefore every triple composite has one canonical source-labelled map, and the associativity square commutes strictly.

The same law propagates to finite-family graph maps and their projective restrictions. The higher associator is consequently the identity on source-labelled coordinates.

Endpoint and relative boundary terms retain their typed projective maps throughout the composition.

Status: higher associativity is closed for the source-labelled directed pro-system; higher positivity remains a coordinatewise carrier property.
