# qRB microstep 74: balanced stabilization lemma

Suppose the forced common remainder satisfies

$$
C_\alpha\succeq-\varepsilon_\alpha B_\alpha,
\qquad
\varepsilon_\alpha\to0,
$$

for positive graph-control forms `B_alpha`. Add the same feature

$$
\sqrt{\varepsilon_\alpha}\,B_\alpha^{1/2}
$$

to both polarities. Then

$$
\widetilde G_\alpha^T=G_\alpha^T+\varepsilon_\alpha B_\alpha,
\qquad
\widetilde G_\alpha^0=G_\alpha^0+\varepsilon_\alpha B_\alpha,
$$

preserve the signed difference while making the common remainder positive.

Thus exact Douglas domination is not necessary at each stage. It is enough to prove asymptotic lower domination and uniform graph control of `B_alpha`.

Status: stabilization lemma available; the source-derived lower bound remains the first open estimate.
