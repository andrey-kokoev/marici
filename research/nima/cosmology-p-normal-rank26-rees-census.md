At `(x,y,z)=(3,6,-3)`, `p=x+y+3z=0` while total energy is `6`. A finite-cutoff
Rees census of the complete labelled rank-26 Laurent relation module was run
along both integral unit normals `nx=(1,0,0)` and `ny=(0,1,0)`, over
`F_101` and `F_103`.

All four runs agree:

- generic cokernel dimension: `4447`;
- special cokernel dimension: `4460`;
- support excess: `13`;
- elementary lengths: seven length-one and six length-two summands;
- no summand of length at least three at ambient relation degree eight.

Thus the zero result in the minimal wall Čech carrier does not imply vanishing
in the full source presentation. The source module contains genuine
`p`-supported candidates, and the normal-independent census survives two
primes.

length-one generators, compute their normal derivative images modulo the

ordered `(Xi_log,-sigma123)` total complex. No tau lift or period is claimed.
