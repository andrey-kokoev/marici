# Fresh independent source-current audit confirms the final bulk witness is not derivable from scalar nullity

## Question

Does the Grothendieck source-current construction supply new independent
evidence for the missing Hermitian bulk balance?

## Comparison

The independently derived two-sheet current gives

\[
D_{\rm bw}
=J_0(q_1)-J_0(q_0)+2R
=-2\operatorname{Re}(z)B,
\qquad B>0.
\]

This is the same typed residual as the final placewise comparison

\[
\mathcal R_p(z)
=(1-p^{-2\operatorname{Re}z})E_p(\Delta_\Xi(z)).
\]

Both identities turn vanishing of an independently sewn boundary/work defect
into critical-line confinement. Neither proves that scalar Xi nullity makes
that defect vanish.

## Independent falsification

The source-current programme supplies an exact hostile. Homogeneous two-sheet
responses can have scalar terminal zeros off the target line while retaining
positive bulk and nonzero `D_bw`. Reflection only supplies the paired response
and no second constraint.

More generally, if a scalar terminal probe `ell` is claimed to force a
Hermitian quadratic defect `H` to vanish, then

\[
\ker\ell\subseteq\{y:\langle y,Hy\rangle=0\}
\]

forces `H` to factor through `ell`. A semidefinite such form has rank at most
one. The positive bulk form has higher rank on the admitted response family,
so scalar terminal nullity cannot supply the missing implication without a
source-proved rank-one restriction, jointly faithful extra probes, or an
independent boundary relation.

The corrected pair selects a distinguished response, but no theorem proves
that the admissible global response family collapses to the required rank-one
source subspace.

## Disposition

The fresh independent packet does not reopen the branch. It strengthens the
stopping result: the sole SCC witness is absent, and approved source
constructors admit explicit counterexamples to deriving it from scalar
nullity. Reopening requires new evidence for one of three typed objects:

1. a source-proved rank-one admissible response restriction;
2. jointly faithful terminal probes through which the bulk form descends;
3. an independent boundary/work relation vanishing on Xi zeros.
