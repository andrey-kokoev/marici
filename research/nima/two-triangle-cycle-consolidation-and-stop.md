## Useful structural results, with their limits

1. The joint simultaneous model splits into four compatible coordinates and two retained defects. The defect is an existing triangle contrast representation, not a new coupling.
2. Both retained rung transport candidates reconstruct their input. Their canonical images are reversibly comparable; differing outer family counts do not by themselves imply physical information loss.
3. Under the full-edge-once objective there are two cyclic tours, exchanged by the seed automorphism A<->B,C<->D. An unmarked equivariant deterministic single-tour choice is impossible. Marking a sewing endpoint suffices for a conditional selector, not a physical choice.
4. The existing ordered vertex-action response changes by conjugation under its valid triangle-block cuts. Transported observers agree. It cannot be extended as the tested pointed seed-edge holonomy because g and h move the base mark.
5. For the declared disjoint union of two cyclic successors, observation refinement is 4->6->10->12. Four vertex readings identify tour and slot. This predictive-memory result does not select the schedule or identify a physical state dimension.
6. The monitor retains failures and explicit reset receipts through both transports. Those receipts document caller decisions, not native authority.

These results delimit model choices. They do not supply masses, proper time, an energy readout, a proton-electron identification or pair-level pruning-minimal C(n,3).
