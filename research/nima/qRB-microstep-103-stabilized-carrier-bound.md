# qRB microstep 103: stabilized carrier bound

If `G_link` is bounded self-adjoint on the positive carrier, then

$$
I+\lambda G_{\rm link}\succeq0
$$

whenever

$$
|\lambda|\,\|G_{\rm link}\|\le1.
$$

This produces an ordinary positive stabilized carrier form while retaining `G_link` as the signed polarization coefficient. It is a presentation device, not a proof that the original linking form is positive.

The bound is exact at the operator-norm level, but its useful source normalization and compatibility with the qRB refinements must still be specified.

Status: finite/bounded stabilization rule recorded.
