# Many-many incidence landing gate

Let `U_F` be the arithmetic pair-family synthesis into the finite-family source space, and let `D_F` be the joint graph map. The analytic realization requires

$$
D_FU_F(E_{\rm core})\subseteq\mathcal G_F
$$

continuously in the finite-family graph norm.

For `F` contained in `G`, compatibility requires

$$
\pi_{G,F}D_GU_G=D_FU_F,
$$

where `pi` is projective coordinate restriction.

A sufficient estimate is

$$
\|D_FU_Fu\|_{\mathcal G_F}
\le C_Fp(u),
$$

for each finite family, together with compatibility of the constants along the directed system when a uniform realization is desired.

This gate connects arithmetic source incidence to the closed many-many graph. It preserves the relative target and leaves scalar spectral positivity as a later property.
