# The prime-dependent bulk incidence is the Duhamel curvature transport

## Transport setup

Let \(U(t,s)\) be the comoving transport on one ray, generated by \(G\):

\[
\partial_tU(t,s)=GU(t,s),
\qquad
U(s,s)=I.
\]

For the two window fronts, \(G\) is the appropriately oriented copy of
\(\partial_x\). Let

\[
P=A(A+1),
\qquad
A=x\partial_x,
\]

be the ray form of theta completion.

The fixed Wronskian columns do not carry prime dependence. The interval

\[
[L,2L],
\qquad
L=\log p,
\]

does. The exact dependence is the failure of \(P\) to commute with
\(U(2L,L)\).

## Duhamel identity

For fixed \(s<t\), define

\[
F(r)=U(t,r)PU(r,s).
\]

Differentiating,

\[
F'(r)
=
U(t,r)[P,G]U(r,s).
\]

Integrating from \(s\) to \(t\) gives

\[
PU(t,s)-U(t,s)P
=
\int_s^t
U(t,r)[P,G]U(r,s)\,dr.
\]

For \(G=D=\partial_x\),

\[
[P,D]
=
-2(A+1)D
=
\Omega.
\]

Therefore

\[
PU(2L,L)-U(2L,L)P
=
\int_L^{2L}
U(2L,r)\Omega U(r,L)\,dr.
\]

This is the exact source-derived prime-dependent comparison term.

## Reciprocal pair

The opposite ray has generator \(-D\), so its curvature is \(-\Omega\).
The two-ray defect is consequently reciprocal-odd:

\[
\mathcal R_p
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\displaystyle
\int_L^{2L}U_+(2L,r)\Omega U_+(r,L)\,dr&0\\[12pt]
0&
\displaystyle
-\int_L^{2L}U_-(2L,r)\Omega U_-(r,L)\,dr
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Reflection exchanges the two diagonal entries and reverses the sign.

## Candidate bulk incidence

The prime-dependent incidence is not an arbitrary congruence matrix. Before
theta label summation, its one-label core is the transported curvature
integral

\[
C_{p,n}^{\mathrm{bulk}}
=
\mathcal M_n
\mathcal R_p
\]

together with the endpoint wall and odd ports. The half-density map
\(\mathcal M_n\) is fiberwise isometric.

After label synthesis and theta-tail propagation, the candidate becomes

\[
C_p
=
\sum_{n\ge1}
\mathsf W_n
\mathsf H_{K,n}
\mathcal M_n
\mathcal R_p,
\]

where \(\mathsf W_n\) denotes the frozen theta label weight and
\(\mathsf H_{K,n}\) the bounded tail-history component. This formula is
schematic only in the assembly notation; its noncommuting local core
\(\mathcal R_p\) is exact.

## Why this carries the Stieltjes scale

The integral length is \(L=\log p\), and the propagators retain the full
moving-center history. Thus \(C_p\) can vary with the strictly varying
Stieltjes endpoint Gram while its terminal Wronskian ports remain universal.

Projecting to the Wronskian columns before forming \(\mathcal R_p\) deletes
this dependence and recreates the constant-incidence no-go.

## Ordered resolution

Since

\[
j_\theta\Omega=A,
\]

the odd Wronskian port resolves the local curvature inside the integral to the
dilation generator. Consequently,

\[
j_\theta\mathcal R_p
=
\int_L^{2L}
U(2L,r)A U(r,L)\,dr
\]

with the corresponding orientation convention, provided the port
intertwines the propagators on the common core.

This gives a concrete first expression for the odd bulk column.

## Analytic gates

The Duhamel formula is algebraic on the rapid core. Quadratic Green
functoriality still requires:

1. strong integrability of the propagated curvature;
2. cancellation or typing of the moving-cut trace at \(x=0\);
3. continuity into the joint theta-connection graph;
4. label-sum convergence;
5. radical annihilation;
6. uniform prime bounds after Euler weights.

## Hostile

Replace \(\mathcal R_p\) by \(L\Omega\), as if curvature commuted with
transport. This retains the correct interval length and first derivative at
\(L=0\), but loses higher transport terms and can give the wrong Green
polarization at every actual prime.

## Frontier

The source-derived prime-dependent bulk incidence is now identified at the
operator level:

\[
\mathcal R_p
=
\int_L^{2L}
U(2L,r)\Omega U(r,L)\,dr.
\]

The next theorem is its bounded quadratic action between the Stieltjes window
history and the completed theta graph, not discovery of another incidence
ansatz.
