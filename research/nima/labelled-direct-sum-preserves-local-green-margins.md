# Labelled direct sum preserves local Green margins

Let the source-labelled loading decompose orthogonally as

$$
U_{\rm lab}=\bigoplus_{p,k}U_{p,k},
$$

and let the corresponding positive Green denominator be

$$
B_{\rm lab}=\bigoplus_{p,k}B_{p,k}.
$$

On reduced supports define

$$
K_{p,k}=B_{p,k}^{\dagger/2}U_{p,k}U_{p,k}^*B_{p,k}^{\dagger/2}.
$$

If

$$
\sup_{p,k}\|K_{p,k}\|\le1-\delta
$$

for one `delta>0`, then

$$
K_{\rm lab}=\bigoplus_{p,k}K_{p,k}
$$

satisfies

$$
\|K_{\rm lab}\|=\sup_{p,k}\|K_{p,k}\|
\le1-\delta.
$$

Hence orthogonal prime/grade completion does not consume a uniform local margin.

The wall-history even margin and completion-graph odd margin are independent of the prime label before arithmetic codiagonalization. Source half-density coefficients are contractions in the transported labelled fibers. Therefore the labelled local packet retains a strict diagonal margin.

Possible loss occurs only after maps that mix labels or parity sectors:

1. arithmetic codiagonalization across primes/grades;
2. reciprocal/linking off-diagonal blocks;
3. connected compact/nuclear perturbations;
4. completion if the local margin is not uniform in grade.

A compact or nuclear perturbation preserves essential separation from one but may create finitely many eigenvalue-one crossings. Those crossings require a finite-dimensional source test; compactness alone does not exclude them.

Status: strict local margins lift to the orthogonal labelled completion; the remaining contraction gate is confined to codiagonal mixing, linking, and finitely many compact exceptional modes.
