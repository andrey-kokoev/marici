# Twisted exchange allows a fatal mixed quartic

## Correction

The quarter-twisted exchange forces opposite coefficients for the two individual D4 anisotropies and removes generalized-CP stabilizers from the resulting axis–diagonal vacua. That calculation used an incomplete invariant potential.

The complete renormalizable angular audit contains the mixed monomial

\[
z_1^2z_2^2.
\]

Its real part is invariant under every declared source operation:

- a common quarter-turn multiplies the monomial by one;
- CP maps it to its complex conjugate;
- the twisted exchange swaps the two squared factors with cancelling phases.

Therefore the CP-even quartic

\[
\mu\operatorname{Re}(z_1^2z_2^2)
\]

is allowed by common D4, bare CP, and the twisted exchange.

## Finite falsifier

At fixed radii, the complete angular potential includes

\[
V(\theta_1,\theta_2)
=
\lambda\bigl(\cos4\theta_1-\cos4\theta_2\bigr)
+\mu\cos2(\theta_1+\theta_2).
\]

The proposed vacua place one angle on an axis and the other on a diagonal. The two individual-anisotropy derivatives vanish there. But the mixed term contributes the same nonzero derivative to both angles:

\[
\partial_{\theta_1}V
=
\partial_{\theta_2}V
=
\pm2\mu.
\]

The checker enumerates all 16 axis–diagonal pairs. None is stationary for nonzero \(\mu\).

Thus the previous 32-element stabilizer census remains correct for the truncated potential but does not describe the vacua of the complete invariant ring.

## Consequence

Setting \(\mu=0\) is not protected by the declared symmetry. Radiative completion will generally regenerate every allowed quartic unless an additional source law forbids it. The twisted-exchange architecture therefore does not yet explain the CP-breaking vacuum.

This is a stronger and more useful failure than the earlier free-sign problem. The missing datum is no longer a coefficient value; it is a symmetry or representation law that simultaneously:

1. preserves the opposite-sign anisotropy relation;
2. forbids \(\operatorname{Re}(z_1^2z_2^2)\);
3. does not enlarge the group enough to restore a generalized CP stabilizer;
4. survives renormalization in the complete Flavor field content.

Any proposed repair should first enumerate its complete degree-four invariant ring. If the mixed quartic remains, one nonzero coefficient is the finite falsifier.
