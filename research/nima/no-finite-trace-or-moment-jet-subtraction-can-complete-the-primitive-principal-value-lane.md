# No finite trace or moment-jet subtraction can complete the primitive principal-value lane

## Result

A finite-dimensional correction of the principal-value tail cannot produce the exponential decay required by the primitive arithmetic grade.

Let \\(\varphi\\) be a localized test with moments

\[
m_j(\varphi)=\int x^j\varphi(x)\,dx.
\]

For large translation \\(L\\), the Hilbert-transform response has the asymptotic expansion

\[
H\varphi(L)
=
\frac1\pi\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}\frac{m_j(\varphi)}{L^{j+1}}
+
O(L^{-N-1}).
\]

Suppose a source correction cancels exactly the first \\(N\\) moments. Unless the remainder vanishes nonperturbatively, the corrected response is still only

\[
O(L^{-N-1}).
\]

On prime-power scale \\(L=k\log p\\), the primitive pushforward carries exponential scale growth. In the dyadic model \\(L=n\log2\\), multiplication by \\(2^n/n\\) gives

\[
\frac{2^n}{n}O(n^{-N-1}),
\]

which fails the term test for every fixed \\(N\\).

Therefore no fixed finite moment jet, and in particular no two-endpoint affine trace subtraction, can by itself complete the primitive principal-value channel.

## Consequence for the zero-trace projector

The projector

\[
\Pi_{0,L}=I-R_L\Gamma_L
\]

removes two endpoint coordinates. It does not imply cancellation of the infinite algebraic Hilbert tail. Even if an authorized comparison identifies those endpoint coordinates with the first two moments, the residual generally remains algebraic.

Thus the implication

\[
\Gamma_Lh=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{primitive PV summability}
\]

is false. Zero trace protects endpoint typing; it is not the analytic completion mechanism.

## Exact alternatives

The source-relative lift must supply at least one of:

1. **All-moment cancellation on an authorized analytic class.** Every algebraic coefficient vanishes and a source theorem gives exponential remainder control.
2. **An exact nonperturbative identity.** Reciprocal or archimedean sewing rewrites the full principal-value tail as an exponentially decaying difference before arithmetic aggregation.
3. **A different completed target topology.** The primitive current acts continuously on a declared analytic-function dual without termwise scalar aggregation, and the eventual readout is proved independently.

A cutoff-dependent number of cancelled moments is insufficient unless the resulting maps converge in one fixed source topology with uniform bounds.

## Strong hostile

For every fixed \\(N\\), choose a compactly supported smooth test satisfying

\[
m_0=\cdots=m_{N-1}=0,
\qquad
m_N\ne0.
\]

Its corrected Hilbert tail begins at \\(L^{-N-1}\\), yet primitive dyadic weighting still grows exponentially. Every finite moment checker passes up to its declared order while completion fails.

## Revised frontier

The odd moment-jet coherencer is necessary for coefficient matching and orientation preservation, but a finite jet is not sufficient for completion. The missing theorem must couple that finite source coefficient to an exact analytic cancellation mechanism:

\[
\text{two-chart odd incidence}
\longrightarrow
\text{all-order or nonperturbative PV cancellation}
\longrightarrow
\text{relative zero-trace history}.
\]

Constructing a finite first-cumulant incidence closes only the local typing gate, not the global primitive principal-value convergence gate.
