# Many-many labelled mode-to-wall detector

Let `h_jk,nm` be the source-derived wall response vector for shell `j`, grade `k`, and ordered pair `(n,m)`. Define the detector coupling on finite packets by

$$
C(f_{j,k,n,m})
=\sum_{j,k,n,m}
\langle f_{j,k,n,m},h_{j,k,n,m}\rangle\,w_{j,k,n,m}.
$$

A sufficient boundedness condition is the square-summable response bound

$$
\sum_{j,k,n,m}
\|h_{j,k,n,m}\|^2
\|w_{j,k,n,m}\|^2<\infty.
$$

The response vectors retain ordered-pair orientation. The reciprocal involution satisfies

$$
\sigma h_{j,k,n,m}=h_{j,k,m,n},
$$

and the Hadamard wall/jump map is applied on each reciprocal pair fiber.

The physical scalar detector is a later codiagonal of this labelled wall output. The labelled coupling preserves collision-cycle data and supports the countable attenuation family.
