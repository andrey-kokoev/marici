# qRB microstep 89: compact-support prime tail closure

If `f` and `g` are supported in `[-L_0,L_0]`, then

$$
\langle f,T_a g\rangle=0
\qquad\text{for }a>2L_0.
$$

Since `a=log n`, every prime-power term with `log n>2L_0` vanishes exactly. Therefore the full prime channel on compact-support tests is already a finite sum:

$$
P_\infty(f,g)=P_{L_0}(f,g).
$$

This closes the prime-tail convergence problem on the compact smooth core without any coefficient summability estimate. The infinite-tail issue reappears only after passing to noncompact graph completions or endpoint limits.
