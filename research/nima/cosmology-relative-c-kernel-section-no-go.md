# The relative c-kernel does not canonically section the p-normal line

The relative three-chart route restores the pivot `c`, with frozen graph
relation `c=-E` and `E=x+y+z`. A tangent lift of a base vector `n` therefore
has `dc=-dE(n)`.

This coefficient does not descend to the normal quotient defined by
`p=x+y+3z`. Integral vectors with `dp=1` give different values:

- `(1,0,0)` gives `dc=-1`;
- `(-2,0,1)` gives `dc=+1`;
- `(4,0,-1)` gives `dc=-3`.

The first two differ by the p-tangent vector `(-3,0,1)`, on which `dE=-2`.
Equivalently, `dE` does not vanish on `ker(dp)` and does not factor through
`dp`. The rank of `(dp,dE)` is two over both `F_101` and `F_103`.

Hence `c=-E` supplies relative tangent lifts but no canonical kernel section
with a fixed unit Xi coefficient. Choosing one splitting would reproduce the
forbidden cover/Rees choice. No tau lift or period is constructed.
