# qRB microstep 95: projective relative observer

Support-rung compatibility allows the family `Q_L` to define one projective relative observer on the compactly supported logarithmic test space:

$$
Q_{\rm proj}(f,g)=Q_L(f,g)
$$

for any rung containing the supports of `f` and `g`.

The restriction result makes this definition independent of the chosen rung. Each finite rung retains its positive relative carrier realization, while the projective limit retains the signed source readout.

This is the strongest unconditional globalization currently justified from the recovered identity. It is stronger than one isolated finite packet but weaker than a uniform Hilbert completion or a noncompact wall completion.
