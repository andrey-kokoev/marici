# The polarized port comparison is an internal prerequisite of the prime-shell candidate, not a third RH candidate

## Question

Does the open polarized Stokes--Wronskian normalization add a third RH frontier
candidate beyond the two emitted by SCC v2?

## Claim boundary

No. It is an internal source-definition prerequisite of the five-port adjoint
whose shell and multiplicity jets form the first SCC candidate. The second SCC
candidate remains the independent global Fourier--Poisson response
intertwining. Generic squares or connecting maps do not add terminal cut sets.

## Two terminal cut sets

The current corrected SCC model has exactly two minimal blocking sets:

1. `prime_shell_adjoint_residual_family`;
2. `global_fourier_poisson_response_intertwining`.

Either one alone blocks the Evans-to-conservative chain map. Both must be
constructed before the RH-bearing promotion exists.

## Internal structure of candidate one

The first candidate is the assertion

\[
 B_\Sigma^\dagger\partial_z^ju(\cdot;z_0)=0,
 \qquad 0\le j<m(z_0),
\]

for every Xi zero and every prime shell, with the limiting common mode retained.

Its evaluation has an internal order:

1. define the ordinary, derivative, wall, reciprocal, and linking port maps;
2. freeze their Green metrics and reciprocal signs;
3. form the unique joint-column adjoint;
4. compute every consecutive-prime shell pairing;
5. differentiate through the full local Xi multiplicity;
6. prove all resulting vectors vanish, equivalently prove divisibility by
   \(\tau\).

The polarized Stokes--Wronskian comparison belongs to steps 1 and 2. It does
not define a separate route to the terminal.

## Internal structure of candidate two

The second candidate is the response square

\[
 \mathcal O_{\partial,+}\operatorname{Tr}_+
 =W_{\rm FP}\mathcal O_{\partial,-}\operatorname{Tr}_-
\]

with response dual transported contragrediently and with primitive, square,
connected, wall, reciprocal, and archimedean strata retained. Scalar functional
equation agreement tests only one matrix coefficient and cannot discharge this
candidate.

## Why the candidates are independent

Prime-shell cancellation is an arithmetic lower-equation statement in
\(U_{\rm ar}\). Response intertwining places the complete boundary trace in a
global maximal-isotropic sewing relation. A vector may satisfy one without the
other:

- an arithmetic residual can vanish while a response stratum has the wrong
  reciprocal transport;
- a boundary trace can lie in the sewing graph while an arithmetic shell
  residual remains nonzero.

Therefore neither candidate should be made a dependency of the other. They
meet only at the Evans-to-conservative chain map.

## Suppressed generic suggestions

A differentiated square is relevant only when it computes the actual residual
jets in candidate one. A connecting map is relevant only when it is the
source-derived chain map assembled after both candidates. Without those exact
signatures, either phrase names a generic method rather than an RH obligation.

## Immediate work target

The current bounded target is candidate one. The next admissible artifact is a
source-locator table for the five fixed shell terms, followed by the first
complete consecutive-prime shell evaluation. If a port coefficient lacks a
source locator, that is a typed missing input inside candidate one; it is not a
new terminal candidate.

## Disposition

SCC v2's two-candidate frontier is correct. The recent normalization and rigging
packets refine the first candidate's prerequisites and do not enlarge the
terminal frontier. No RH conclusion is authorized.
