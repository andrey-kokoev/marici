# qRB microstep 96: noncompact cutoff extension

For a noncompact Schwartz pair `f,g`, choose cutoffs `chi_L` equal to one on `[-L,L]` and define

$$
 f_L=\chi_Lf,
 \qquad
 g_L=\chi_Lg.
$$

The projective observer extends if `Q_proj(f_L,g_L)` is Cauchy and independent of the cutoff family. A sufficient condition is a common wall-graph bound

$$
|Q_proj(f_L-f_M,g_N)|
\le C\|f_L-f_M\|_{wall}\|g_N\|_{wall},
$$

with analogous control in the second variable.

This isolates the final noncompact step: compact-rung compatibility is already closed; only cutoff Cauchy control in the wall topology is needed.
