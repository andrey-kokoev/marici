# Transfer audit from prior pair-current, hyperbolic-centering, and Poisson-sewing research to the diagonal shell blocker

## Question

Which earlier constructions transfer to candidate one's missing
source-normalized diagonal reciprocal/linking density, and which similarities do
not define comparison maps?

## Claim boundary

Three prior constructions transfer at the level of carrier architecture and
boundary decomposition:

1. tail--seam synthesis supplies a faithful polarized pair current;
2. completed theta label pairs admit an exact hyperbolic ratio center;
3. Poisson sewing retains two endpoint ports rather than cancelling boundary
   data.

Together they narrow the missing response to an oriented, shell-localized map
from a faithful pair current through a centered bulk plus moving-endpoint
boundary packet. They do not supply that map's analytic-transpose orientation
or its equality with the shell autocorrelation density.

## Transfer 1: pair polarization

Prior tail--seam research constructs

\[
 Uc=\sum_n c_nu_n,
 \qquad
 K=U^*U,
\]

with pair entries

\[
 K(n,m)=\langle u_n,u_m\rangle.
\]

This transfers directly as the reason candidate one must use the ordered-pair
module

\[
 \mathcal C_{\rm ar}\otimes\overline{\mathcal C_{\rm ar}}
\]

rather than a one-copy scalar incidence. It also supplies a reusable hostile:
one-label energies cannot reconstruct off-diagonal interference.

What does not transfer is the kernel identity. The global Gram kernel depends
on logarithmic label ratio, while the localized completed-theta density

\[
 \rho_{nm}^{[a,b]}(t)
 =\int_a^b\Phi_n(u)\Phi_m(u+t)\,du
\]

also retains shell endpoints and the separation coordinate. Equality requires
a localization theorem for \(U^*U\), not only translation covariance.

## Transfer 2: hyperbolic centering

The earlier two-copy calculation uses the same completed atom, since

\[
 \Phi_n(u)
 =\pi n^2e^{5u/2}
 \left(2\pi n^2e^{2u}-3\right)
 e^{-\pi n^2e^{2u}}.
\]

For two source coordinates \((u,v)\), define

\[
 S=u+v,
 \qquad
 D=u-v,
\]

and center the ratio coordinate at

\[
 c_{nm}=\log(m/n).
\]

Writing \(D=c_{nm}+\delta\) makes the exponential pair energy depend on

\[
 2nm\,e^S\cosh\delta.
\]

Thus the unrestricted bulk is even in the centered ratio variable. The
localized chart is not centered because its interval endpoints move with the
shell. This transfers an exact decomposition:

- centered hyperbolic bulk;
- moving-endpoint defect.

For candidate one, the ratio-Stokes cocycle may organize the off-diagonal
centered transport, but diagonal \(n=m\) has zero ratio displacement. The
moving-endpoint and nonlocal bulk data therefore remain in the diagonal
reciprocal/linking obligation.

## Transfer 3: Poisson boundary rank

The completed Mellin split shows that reciprocal Poisson sewing maps one
continuum boundary mode to a two-endpoint packet. It does not erase the
boundary term. The two ports are exchanged by reciprocal reflection and are
removed only after applying the completion polynomial.

This transfers as a prohibition: diagonal reciprocal sewing must not be
modeled as cancellation of the two shell endpoints. The response carrier must
retain both endpoint coordinates until the boundary complex or annihilator is
applied.

It does not transfer the Mellin scalar annihilator into the shell response.
The Mellin endpoint pair and the finite-shell autocorrelation density are
different typed objects.

## Combined transferable diagram

The reusable architecture is

\[
 \mathcal C_{\rm ar}^{(2)}
 \xrightarrow{\widetilde B}
 \mathcal G_{\rm pair}^{[a,b]}
 \xrightarrow{\rm center}
 \mathcal G_{\rm bulk,even}
 \oplus
 \mathcal B_{\rm moving\ endpoint}
 \xrightarrow{\mathcal T_{\rm orient}}
 \mathcal G_{\rm recip/link}^{[a,b]}.
\]

The first two arrows are available at the algebraic and density levels. The
last arrow remains undefined.

## Nontransferable candidates

The review rejects the following transfers:

- global pair-Gram positivity as analytic-transpose orientation;
- equality of logarithmic ratio dependence with finite-shell localization;
- Poisson endpoint closure as cancellation of shell autocorrelation;
- the additive-Gaussian separation resolvent, which is coordinate-mistyped for
  the completed logarithmic Evans forcing;
- Adams-two coefficient matching as reconstruction of the full separation
  density;
- a scalar half-density gauge as the missing oriented pair response.

## Exact remaining transfer theorem

A successful transfer must construct

\[
 \mathcal T_{\rm orient}:
 \mathcal G_{\rm bulk,even}
 \oplus
 \mathcal B_{\rm moving\ endpoint}
 \longrightarrow
 \mathcal G_{\rm wall,inc}
\]

and prove, on every ordered pair and shell,

\[
 \mathcal L_z\bigl(\mathcal T_{\rm orient}ho_{nm}^{[a,b]}\bigr)
 =-\mathcal L_z\bigl(\rho_{nm}^{[a,b]}\bigr)
\]

with the separately frozen endpoint-flux convention, reciprocal reversal,
shell concatenation, and all parameter jets.

The map must be defined before inspecting Xi zeros.

## Disposition

Prior research transfers a faithful pair carrier, an exact centered-bulk versus
moving-endpoint decomposition, and a two-port reciprocal boundary rule. It does
not transfer the missing oriented density comparison. Candidate one remains
blocked at \(\mathcal T_{\rm orient}\), but the target domain is now narrower
than an unspecified reciprocal response. No RH conclusion is authorized.
