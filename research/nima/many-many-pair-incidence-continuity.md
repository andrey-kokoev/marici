# Many-many pair-incidence continuity

On a compact shell `[a,b]` with `a>0`, define

$$
B_{\rm rad}(f,g)=\rho_{f,g}^{[a,b]}.
$$

Gaussian label bounds give

$$
|\rho_{nm}^{[a,b]}(t)|
\le
(b-a)e^{-\pi(n^2+m^2)a^2/2}
$$

uniformly on compact separation sets, up to the fixed normalization of the completed atoms.

Hence the matrix-unit assignment

$$
 e_n\otimes\overline{e_m}
\mapsto
\rho_{nm}^{[a,b]}
$$

extends continuously for any pair-source topology whose weights dominate the displayed Gaussian factor. Endpoint and derivative coordinates obey the corresponding graph estimates from the first-order pair equation.

This closes continuity on the interior finite-shell pair carrier. The endpoint limit and filtered global extension require the endpoint graph topology and a source-supported tail majorant.
