# The universal uncoupled colligation exists; the missing datum is off-diagonal coupling

## Correction to the common-block obstruction

The current theta packets do not construct one mixed Green operator. That does not mean the typed channels lack a common categorical carrier.

Each derived channel already has a source object, target object, and incidence map on its declared regularity rung. In an additive rigged category their biproduct is canonical. What is missing is the coupling that turns this uncoupled biproduct into one interacting colligation.

## Channel biproduct

Let the source objects be

\[
A_1,\ A_2,\ A_{\ge3},\ A_{\mathrm{seam}},\ A_\infty
\]

and the corresponding boundary objects be

\[
B_1,\ B_2,\ B_{\ge3},\ B_{\mathrm{seam}},\ B_\infty.
\]

The archimedean source need not equal the arithmetic source. Form the biproducts

\[
A_0=\bigoplus_i A_i,
\qquad
B_0=\bigoplus_i B_i.
\]

The existing typed incidence maps \(J_i:A_i\to B_i\) induce the canonical block-diagonal observer

\[
J_0=\bigoplus_i J_i:A_0\to B_0.
\]

This construction uses only the universal property of the biproduct. It preserves every channel type and regularity index.

## What the biproduct proves

The uncoupled colligation supplies:

- one carrier containing all declared ports;
- canonical injections and projections;
- a block-diagonal observer;
- a place to state Fourier–Poisson naturality channel by channel;
- a common codomain for typed transition packets.

It does not supply:

- primitive–square interaction;
- arithmetic–seam return;
- endpoint–archimedean cancellation;
- Schur return blocks;
- an ordered global sewing operator;
- a determinant comparison beyond the already typed diagonal blocks.

## The missing constructor

A mixed Green colligation has the form

\[
J=J_0+C,
\]

or, at the operator level,

\[
G=G_0+C,
\]

where \(C\) has off-diagonal components

\[
C_{ij}:A_j\longrightarrow B_i,
\qquad i\ne j.
\]

Every \(C_{ij}\) must declare:

- source and target channel types;
- regularity-index motion;
- graph domain;
- Fourier–Poisson naturality;
- reciprocal-sheet variance;
- operator class;
- boundary contribution.

The source-derived object still absent is this off-diagonal coupling matrix.

## Why scalar completion cannot recover it

Let \(\ell:B_0\to\mathbb C\) be the completed scalar readout. Distinct couplings \(C\) and \(C'\) can satisfy

\[
\ell C=\ell C'
\]

while acting differently on \(\ker\ell\). Hence equality of completed scalar sections determines at most the quotient of the coupling through \(\ell\), not the coupling itself.

Likewise, equal determinant-line transitions do not determine Schur return blocks. Determinant and scalar lenses forget off-diagonal operator information.

## Minimal finite falsifier

Take two one-dimensional channels with scalar readout

\[
\ell(x,y)=x+y.
\]

The couplings

\[
C=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1\\
0&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
C'=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&0\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix}
\]

are distinct. On suitable source vectors their difference lands in the scalar-null direction \((1,-1)\), so a scalar projection can identify their outputs even though their return paths and ordered compositions differ.

Any compiler reconstructing \(C\) from \(\ell C\) fails this two-channel witness.

## Revised frontier

The global observer problem divides into two claims:

1. Common typed carrier and diagonal observer: canonically available as a biproduct of the constructed ports.
2. Interacting Green colligation: absent until the off-diagonal source couplings are derived.

The next source calculation should not search for another ambient space. It should derive one nonzero cross-channel incidence, preferably the endpoint–seam or primitive–archimedean block, and verify its regularity motion and Fourier–Poisson naturality.

## Verdict

The programme has a canonical universal uncoupled colligation. The unresolved RH-bearing datum is the source-derived off-diagonal coupling and its coherence, not the mere coexistence of the six channels.
