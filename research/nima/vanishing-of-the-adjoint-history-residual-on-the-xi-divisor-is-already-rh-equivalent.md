# Vanishing of the adjoint-history residual on the Xi divisor is already RH-equivalent

## Residual

For the natural nonzero theta history \(u_s\), define

\[
\mathcal R_{\rm adj}(s)
=
V^*u_s
=
\langle\Phi,u_s\rangle.
\]

At a zero of the Mellin endpoint section, the boundary flux vanishes and the
exact history identity gives

\[
2a(s)\|u_s\|^2
=
-2\operatorname{Re}
\bigl(c_s\mathcal R_{\rm adj}(s)^*\bigr),
\]

with

\[
a(s)=\operatorname{Re}s-\frac12.
\]

Assume the source coordinate \(c_s\ne0\) and the forced history \(u_s\ne0\).

## Residual vanishing implies RH confinement

If every nontrivial Xi zero satisfies

\[
\mathcal R_{\rm adj}(s)=0,
\]

then the Green identity gives

\[
a(s)\|u_s\|^2=0.
\]

Since \(u_s\ne0\),

\[
\operatorname{Re}s=\frac12.
\]

Thus residual vanishing on the Xi divisor implies RH for the nontrivial zeros
represented by this history family.

## RH confinement gives only the real residual condition

Conversely, if a represented zero lies on the seam, then the Green identity
gives

\[
\operatorname{Re}
\bigl(c_s\mathcal R_{\rm adj}(s)^*\bigr)=0.
\]

It does not force the full complex residual to vanish.  Therefore
\(\mathcal R_{\rm adj}=0\) is strictly stronger than seam confinement unless
an independent reciprocal or phase law removes the imaginary component.

The exact RH-equivalent condition is the weaker real pairing identity

\[
\operatorname{Re}
\bigl(c_s\mathcal R_{\rm adj}(s)^*\bigr)=0
\]

at every represented Xi zero.

## Circularity test

Any proposed proof of the divisor-to-state square that establishes the
adjoint equation by substituting \(\tau_s=0\) must exhibit a source identity
independent of zero location.  If its only argument is that the resulting
Green form is positive off the seam, then it has assumed the desired
confinement in operator language.

Likewise, defining a correction term

\[
q_s=-\mathcal R_{\rm adj}(s)
\]

from the evaluated history fits the missing source equation after scalar
projection.  It is not a source-derived arithmetic reservoir.

## Acceptable stronger input

A noncircular cancellation would be an operator identity valid for every
parameter and every source state:

\[
\mathcal R_{\rm arithmetic}(x,s)
+
2\operatorname{Re}
\bigl(c\langle u,
\Phi\rangle\bigr)=0,
\]

where \(x\), \(u\), and \(c\) are related by the declared source differential.
Such an identity can be checked before imposing \(\tau_s=0\).  No current
packet supplies it.

## G4 stopping condition

The adjoint residual is now a sharp stopping boundary.  Proving its required
real vanishing only on Xi zeros is already the RH-bearing theorem.  Further
algebraic reformulation of that condition does not advance G4 unless it
produces a parameterwise source cancellation identity or a completed
chain-level factorization independent of the Xi divisor.

## Disposition

The natural history, maximal-isotropic boundary relation, and Green identity
reduce G4 to one explicit forcing-pair residual.  Its needed vanishing is not a
remaining normalization detail; it contains the unresolved RH content.  No RH
conclusion is authorized.
