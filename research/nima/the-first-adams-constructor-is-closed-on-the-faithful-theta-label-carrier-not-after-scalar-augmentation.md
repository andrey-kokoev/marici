\[

To close the label-valued edge at completion, prove:

1. \(\mathbf M\) is closed on the rapid wall-reduced source core;
2. the Euler--Maclaurin wall subtraction makes the labelled orbit convergent;
3. \(\mathbf C^{-1}\) is uniformly bounded by the quarter-gap;

already derived loading margin.

## Verdict

The source has already supplied the exact first Adams constructor on the
faithful theta-label carrier. The unresolved issue is not the four-front
coefficient identity; it is whether the completed scalar theta observer has
an authorized counit and, independently, how the labelled Green metric
compares with the Stieltjes disagreement metric.

The safest architecture keeps the Adams edge label-valued and postpones scalar
theta synthesis to the observer layer.
