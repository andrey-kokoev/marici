# The free doubled radial resolvent has one oriented rank-one cross-channel per parameter half-plane

## Question

What boundary response does the zero-free reciprocal radial double contribute before arithmetic loading?

## Claim boundary

Off the continuous-spectrum seam, its resolvent is explicit. In each parameter half-plane it contains one Volterra diagonal and one sewing-induced rank-one cross-channel, with the cross-channel orientation reversed between half-planes. This fixes the free response that any G4 realization must retain. It does not determine the arithmetic synthesis or continue the Schur section through the seam.

## Operator and sewing

Let

\[
\mathbb D_u=\partial_t\oplus(-\partial_t),
\qquad
f_-(0)=u f_+(0),
\qquad |u|=1.
\]

Solve

\[
(\mathbb D_u-z)F=G
\]

for \(G=(g_+,g_-)\).

## Right parameter half-plane

Assume \(\operatorname{Re}z>0\). The \(+\) channel is fixed by its terminal \(L^2\) condition:

\[
f_+(t)
=-\int_t^\infty e^{-z(s-t)}g_+(s)\,ds.
\]

Its boundary value is

\[
f_+(0)=-\int_0^\infty e^{-zs}g_+(s)\,ds.
\]

The \(-\) channel is

\[
f_-(t)
=e^{-zt}u f_+(0)
-\int_0^t e^{-z(t-s)}g_-(s)\,ds.
\]

Thus the only free cross-channel term is

\[
g_+\longmapsto
-u e^{-zt}\int_0^\infty e^{-zs}g_+(s)\,ds.
\]

with the sign fixed by the terminal solution. It is rank one and factors through the wall trace.

## Left parameter half-plane

Assume \(\operatorname{Re}z<0\). The \(-\) channel is fixed by its terminal \(L^2\) condition:

\[
f_-(t)
=\int_t^\infty e^{z(s-t)}g_-(s)\,ds.
\]

The sewing condition gives

\[
f_+(0)=u^{-1}f_-(0)
=u^{-1}\int_0^\infty e^{zs}g_-(s)\,ds.
\]

Then

\[
f_+(t)
=e^{zt}u^{-1}f_-(0)
+\int_0^t e^{z(t-s)}g_+(s)\,ds.
\]

The cross-channel now runs from \(-\) to \(+\):

\[
g_-\longmapsto
u^{-1}e^{zt}
\int_0^\infty e^{zs}g_-(s)\,ds.
\]

Hence the two half-plane resolvents are related by reciprocal exchange, not by using one fixed causal kernel on both sides.

## Rank-one wall factorization

For \(\operatorname{Re}z>0\), define

\[
\ell_z^+(g)=\int_0^\infty e^{-zs}g(s)\,ds,
\qquad
k_z^-(t)=e^{-zt}.
\]

The cross response is

\[
-u\,k_z^-\otimes\ell_z^+.
\]

For \(\operatorname{Re}z<0\), define

\[
\ell_z^-(g)=\int_0^\infty e^{zs}g(s)\,ds,
\qquad
k_z^+(t)=e^{zt},
\]

and the cross response is

\[
u^{-1}k_z^+\otimes\ell_z^-.
\]

The response is therefore a source-fixed wall channel, while the diagonal pieces are Volterra propagation. This is the minimal free boundary-response decomposition.

## Adjoint and reciprocal checks

Within a fixed Hilbert half-plane, the rank-one adjoint exchanges its kernel vector and Laplace functional with complex conjugation. Reciprocal transport additionally exchanges parameter half-planes and replaces \(u\) by \(u^{-1}\). These are different operations. Their equality requires the Real coherencer already isolated in the transfer audit.

The analytic transpose omits Hilbert conjugation and therefore follows the bilinear Laplace functional. It cannot be substituted for the adjoint without the explicit real-structure comparison.

## Schur consequence

For a source column \(U_X^{\rm rad}\), the free return

\[
N_X^{\rm rad}(\mathbb D_u-z)^{-1}U_X^{\rm rad}
\]

splits into:

1. same-channel Volterra propagation;
2. one wall-mediated rank-one reciprocal crossing.

The function-valued Wronskian incidence must enter through \(U_X^{\rm rad}\), \(N_X^{\rm rad}\), or the border. It is not generated by the free rank-one wall crossing, whose range is one-dimensional at fixed \(z\).

This recovers the earlier infinite-rank no-go from the explicit resolvent: a fixed scalar wall channel cannot replace the radial Wronskian family.

## Seam limitation

The exponential vectors cease to lie in \(L^2\) when \(\operatorname{Re}z=0\). The two formulas therefore do not define a bounded Hilbert resolvent on the seam. Any seam continuation must occur in the declared rapid/Laplace rigging and preserve the two oriented boundary values. Identifying them by scalar analytic continuation would discard the incoming/outgoing distinction.

## Hostiles

A checker must reject:

1. the right-half-plane cross-channel oriented from \(-\) to \(+\);
2. the left-half-plane cross-channel oriented from \(+\) to \(-\);
3. omission of the sewing phase or replacement of \(u^{-1}\) by an unrelated phase;
4. treatment of the wall rank-one response as the full Wronskian incidence;
5. use of the bounded Hilbert resolvent formula on the seam;
6. identification of reciprocal exchange, Hermitian adjoint, and analytic transpose.

## Disposition

The free radial response is now explicit off the seam: Volterra propagation plus one oriented rank-one wall crossing, reversed between parameter half-planes. G4 conformance can test this kernel directly. The remaining nonfree content is the source-derived infinite-dimensional radial synthesis, its typed return, arithmetic loading, and rigged seam continuation. No RH conclusion is authorized.
