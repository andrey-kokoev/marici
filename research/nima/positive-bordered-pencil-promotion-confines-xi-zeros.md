# Positive bordered-pencil promotion confines Xi zeros

Let

$$
L(z)=L_0-zP
$$

be a holomorphic bordered pencil on a finite or Fredholm carrier. Assume there is a bounded strictly positive invertible metric `J` such that

$$
L_0^*J=JL_0,
\qquad
P^*J=JP,
$$

with `P` positive on the spectral carrier after quotienting any fixed reference kernel.

Conjugating by `J^(1/2)` gives

$$
\widehat L(z)
=J^{1/2}L(z)J^{-1/2}
=\widehat L_0-z\widehat P,
$$

where `Lhat_0` and `Phat` are self-adjoint and `Phat` is positive on the active carrier. If

$$
\widehat L(z)v=0,
$$

then pairing with `v` gives

$$
\langle\widehat L_0v,v\rangle
-z\langle\widehat Pv,v\rangle=0.
$$

The two quadratic quantities are real and the denominator is positive, hence

$$
z\in\mathbb R.
$$

Thus every active characteristic zero of the positive promoted pencil lies on the spectral seam.

If its relative Fredholm determinant satisfies

$$
\det_{\rm rel}L(z)=u(z)\tau(z),
$$

with `u` holomorphic and nowhere zero on the declared domain, then the Xi divisor, including multiplicities, equals the active characteristic divisor and is confined to the seam.

This avoids the impossible claim that Xi itself is a positive scalar resolvent. Xi remains a bordered/relative determinant section.

For the theta bordered pencil, coefficient comparison reduces existence of `J` to a positive carrier metric `G` satisfying

$$
GA=AG,
\qquad
Gb_0=j b_f,
\qquad j>0.
$$

Consequently the remaining RH-bearing constructor is exactly a source-derived positive port-collocation metric plus a completion-stable relative determinant unit.

Status: positive bordered-promotion confinement theorem proved; source construction of `G` and authorization of the relative unit remain open.
