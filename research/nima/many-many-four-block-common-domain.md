# Many-many four-block common domain

Let `C_S` be the finite-character Mellin–Schwartz core and let `C_E` be the compact physical analytic core with rank-two endpoint correction. Use

$$
\mathcal C_{\rm common}=\mathcal C_S\cap\mathcal C_E
$$

as the initial domain for all four theta-history blocks and four wall/linking blocks.

For a finite family, define the joint graph embedding

$$
J_{\mathcal F}u
=\bigl(u,
P_\mathcal Fu,Q_\mathcal Fu,R_\mathcal Fu,N_\mathcal Fu,
P^{\rm wall}_\mathcal Fu,Q^{\rm wall}_\mathcal Fu,
R^{\rm wall}_\mathcal Fu,N^{\rm wall}_\mathcal Fu\bigr).
$$

The common completed domain is the closure of `J_F(C_common)` in the finite product graph norm. The theta and wall block maps extend together whenever each coordinate is closable on this core.

The intertwiner identity is first checked on `C_common`, then transported to the joint closure. Refinement compatibility passes to the projective limit coordinatewise.
