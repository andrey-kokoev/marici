# Future probes and finite information speed

## Operator stimulus

The Operator reported sensing that the speed limit for information propagation
may result from the need for future probing. This is recorded as a sensed,
non-evidential research direction.

## Necessary correction

Future probing alone does not imply a finite speed. A category may contain a
morphism from one location to every other location in a single step. The
weakest defensible conjecture therefore requires both:

1. physical identity or influence is exposed through future probes;
2. admissible interactions compose locally.

Then a causal cone is defined operationally:

[
J^+(e)=
{	ext{future probes whose outcome law can distinguish whether }e
	ext{ occurred}}.
]

A propagation-speed bound says that this distinguishability support cannot
expand faster than some rate (v):

[
operatorname{dist}(e,P)>vDelta t
quadLongrightarrowquad
P	ext{ cannot yet distinguish }e.
]

This reframes a speed limit as a limit on the creation of operationally
available distinctions.

## Future-probe causal-speed conjecture

For a Marici sector with local composition, the support of every
source-admissible interaction expands under sequential composition inside a
sector-derived causal cone. The maximal asymptotic expansion rate of that
support is the sector's information-propagation speed.

In schematic form,

[
oxed{
	ext{local constructor grammar}
+	ext{future-probe separation}
Longrightarrow
	ext{bounded growth of distinguishability}.
}
]

The conjecture does not yet identify this bound with (c). Recovering the
relativistic light cone additionally requires Lorentzian symmetry or an
equivalent invariant causal structure.

## Exact bounded pilot

On a finite chain, one nearest-neighbor composition layer enlarges the support
of a local perturbation by at most one edge. After depth (d), only probes
within graph distance (d) can distinguish it. The checker verifies the
exact cone through five layers.

The hostile control admits one nonlocal edge. A distant probe then
distinguishes the source after one layer, violating the unit-speed bound.
Therefore:

[
oxed{
	ext{future probing defines propagation, but locality bounds its speed}.
}
]

## Physical falsifiers

1. Source-admissible local operations produce distinguishability outside the
   proposed cone.
2. The bound depends on gauge, chart, or presentation.
3. Composition creates superluminal support even though individual
   generators are local.
4. The record functor signals outside the state-update causal support.
5. A finite bound exists only after imposing a background metric not derived
   or independently admitted by the sector.
