# Real reflection extends through pro-Gram completion; Fourier–Poisson sewing does not descend cutoffwise

## Pro-Gram extension theorem

Let $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{fin}}$ be the finite labelled coefficient module,
$\mathcal M_{\mathrm{src}}$ its authorized constructor monoid, and

$$
q_C(c)=\lVert U(Cc)\rVert,
\qquad C\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{src}}.
$$

Let $R$ be conjugate reciprocal transport on coefficients and $J$ the
antiunitary Real reflection on the synthesized tail-seam carrier. Assume:

1. $J U=U R$ on finite packets;
2. $R^2=1$;
3. $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{src}}$ is closed under
   $\Theta(C)=RCR$;
4. $J$ preserves the carrier norm.

Then

$$
q_C(Rc)=q_{\Theta(C)}(c).
$$

Hence $R$ is a homeomorphism of the pro-Gram coefficient topology and extends
uniquely to its Hausdorff completion. The completed synthesis square remains
natural wherever $U$ extends.

For valuation projectors, $\Theta$ fixes the constructor. For Mellin
characters, $\Theta(M_t)=M_{-t}$. Both signs must therefore belong to the
authorized monoid.

## What this closes

Conjugate Real reflection is not obstructed by adjacent-label collapse or the
absence of a diagonal Riesz norm. It only permutes the full defining seminorm
family. This closes the reciprocal Real action on the constructor-generated
pro-Gram completion, conditional on the already declared monoid and Hausdorff
completion.

No uniform lower frame bound follows. The topology may remain non-Hilbert and
the completed synthesis may have nonclosed range in another ambient norm.

## The completion-level distinction

Full Fourier–Poisson sewing is a different constructor. It already exists on the global additive theta rigging. A finite Euler cutoff
is not preserved by the global Fourier operation. If $P_X$ is cutoff
projection and $F$ is global sewing, the leakage residual is

$$
\mathfrak A_X=P_XF(1-P_X).
$$

This residual is generally nonzero. Therefore no family of finite-cutoff
Fourier endomorphisms can be inferred from the exact Real-reflection square.

The missing boundary-lift order is:

1. retain the existing global additive Fourier–Poisson action;
2. construct the observer lift into the multiplicative boundary pro-object;
3. derive the boundary sewing action and prove the mixed naturality square;
4. use finite Euler projections only as diagnostics afterward.

## Falsifiers

The Real extension fails if $\Theta(C)$ leaves the authorized monoid for any
generator. The global sewing proposal fails if it treats
$\mathfrak A_X\ne0$ as numerical error or silently projects it away. A claimed
completion cell fails if its finite diagnostics do not arise by projection of
one global source action.
