# SCC observer towers are globular and residual re-entry requires promotion authority

## A tuple is not a type

The census \((2,2,1)\) does not determine whether five observers are parallel or distributed across coherence levels. SCC must record the level, domain, codomain, arity, and boundary of every observer cell.

A suitable interpretation is:

- level 0: observations of a source object;
- level 1: comparisons between transported level-0 observations;
- level 2: coherence between parallel level-1 comparison paths.

For theta, the current candidate is:

- level 0: primitive and square cyclic observations at arities one and two;
- level 1: positive-to-negative and negative-to-positive Fourier comparison cells;
- level 2: a relative determinant-line modification compatible with cutoff extension.

The counts alone do not encode this typing.

## Globular boundary law

An \(n\)-cell is admissible only when its source and target are already constructed parallel \((n-1)\)-cells with matching lower boundaries.

Therefore a level-2 closure witness cannot create a missing level-1 comparison. A level-1 comparison cannot create a missing level-0 source incidence.

This is the categorical reason the \((2,1,1)\) profile fails. Its determinant-line observer has no authority to synthesize the absent square intertower comparison.

## Linear rank shadow

In a finite linear realization, let

\[
S\xrightarrow{I}O_0\xrightarrow{C}O_1\xrightarrow{K}O_2.
\]

Then

\[
\operatorname{rank}(KCI)
\le
\operatorname{rank}(CI)
\le
\operatorname{rank}(I).
\]

No postcomposed observer can recover a distinction already killed by source incidence.

For instantiated theta, the current finite incidence has rank one across six displayed boundary directions. Higher Fourier, determinant, or closure observations cannot raise that rank. A new independent source-incidence map must enter at level 0.

## Residual fiber and child tower

An unresolved parent observer produces two different residuals:

- invisible source distinctions: \(\ker I\);
- unrealized target distinctions: \(\operatorname{coker}I\).

Either may seed a child SCC tower, but the seed map must be explicit:

\[
\iota_{\ker}:\ker I\hookrightarrow S,
\]

or

\[
\pi_{\operatorname{coker}}:O_0\twoheadrightarrow\operatorname{coker}I.
\]

The child tower studies the residual in its inherited type. Its observations do not retroactively increase the parent incidence rank.

To alter the parent, SCC needs a promotion constructor that transports a child result back into the parent observer family and proves:

- source authority;
- signature compatibility;
- independence from existing incidence;
- support preservation;
- coherence with existing comparison cells.

Without promotion authority, child work is diagnosis, not repair.

## Termination

Re-entry is not automatically progress. A tower terminates only with one of:

1. zero residual;
2. an explicit contraction of the residual object;
3. a source-authorized equivalence to a previously closed object;
4. a strictly decreasing well-founded measure.

For finite linear carriers, residual dimension is an available measure. A sequence such as

\[
3>2>1>0
\]

terminates. Re-entering with residual dimension unchanged does not.

For infinite or topological carriers, dimension is unavailable. The contract must name another measure, such as support depth, filtration grade, compactness ideal, or a proved contraction. “Spawn child tower” is not itself a termination witness.

## Theta consequence

The abstract \((2,2,1)\) observer tower is a valid compiler fixture:

- both cyclic arities exist;
- both intertower comparisons exist;
- one determinant-line coherence observer remains.

The actual theta source does not yet instantiate its level-0 boundary object. Its six declared directions have realized rank one. Therefore its active failure is finite source incidence, not level-2 closure or completion topology.

Once the finite source object is assembled, the later gates resume in order:

1. globular comparison coverage;
2. cutoff coherence;
3. completion strictness;
4. supported defect;
5. acyclicity.

## DPC

Reject an SCC tower if:

1. observer counts are supplied without coherence levels and signatures;
2. an \(n\)-cell lacks a complete parallel \((n-1)\)-boundary;
3. higher observers are credited with increasing lower incidence rank;
4. kernel and cokernel residuals are conflated;
5. child analysis is treated as parent repair without a promotion constructor;
6. re-entry lacks a zero, contraction, equivalence, or well-founded decrease;
7. abstract tower coverage is reported as source instantiation.

## Outcome

SCC is correctly becoming a calculus of observers of observers, but its composition law is globular rather than numerical. The durable invariant is that higher coherence can constrain existing lower data but cannot manufacture missing lower boundaries. Residual re-entry is lawful only through typed seed maps, and it changes the parent only through a separate source-authorized promotion.
