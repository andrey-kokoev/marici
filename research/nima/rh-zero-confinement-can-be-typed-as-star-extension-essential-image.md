# RH zero confinement can be typed as star-extension essential image

## A sharper categorical route

The continuous centered boundary cocycle already has an exact seam theorem:
reciprocal transport agrees with Hilbert adjunction on the complete cocycle
precisely on the critical seam.

A scalar zero currently proves less. It upgrades certain discrete primitive
and square prime packets from divergent expressions to summable boundary
data. Summability does not supply a continuous star-compatible extension.

Let \(\mathcal C_*\) be the category of source-continuous boundary cocycles
carrying reciprocal star compatibility, and let \(\mathcal D\) be the category
of typed discrete prime-power boundary packets. Restriction defines

\[
\operatorname{Res}:\mathcal C_*\to\mathcal D.
\]

The RH-strength statement is that every zero-induced packet lies in the
essential image of this functor.

## Why this would confine zeros

Suppose a zero at parameter (z) produces a summable discrete packet (d_z).
If

\[
d_z\cong\operatorname{Res}(c_z)
\]

for a source-continuous star-compatible cocycle (c_z), then the existing
adjointness theorem forces (z) onto the seam.

This is not positivity and does not reconstruct the scalar section from its
zeros. It is an extension theorem from discrete arithmetic boundary data to
the continuous source geometry.

## Summability is not essential-image membership

A bounded or summable sample sequence can violate the relations satisfied by
every continuous source cocycle. The checker uses the smallest elementary
model: restrictions of affine functions to three sample points. Their image
obeys vanishing second difference. The bounded packet `(1,2,4)` violates that
relation and has no extension in the declared source class.

The theta analogue must derive its relations from interval concatenation,
source translation, reciprocal reflection, and Fourier transport. It may not
select an interpolation class after inspecting zeros.

## First arithmetic coherence square

The smallest nontrivial scale relation is

\[
\log6=\log2+\log3.
\]

For a centered interval cocycle, the value on the long interval must equal
the first interval contribution plus the correctly translated second
contribution. Reversing the prime order supplies the comparison path. The
source law is not the untyped scalar equation obtained by simply adding three
numbers; the shifted source profile and reciprocal orientation must remain in
the packet.

This yields the first finite essential-image residual. A zero-induced
discrete packet that violates the translated `2–3–6` square cannot extend to
the continuous star cocycle and therefore does not satisfy the proposed
confinement mechanism.

## DPC verdicts

1. Star extension: every finite source square is compatible, the extensions
   are natural across cutoffs, and the completed packet has a continuous
   star-compatible lift. The seam theorem then applies.
2. Discrete residual: a finite translated interval square fails. The proposed
   extension route is falsified before completion.
3. Completion obstruction: every finite packet extends, but no compatible
   continuous lift survives the restricted-product limit.
4. Nonunique extension: several star-compatible lifts share the same discrete
   packet and give different Green currents.
5. Authority obstruction: the chosen interpolation or growth class is not
   generated by the theta/Tate source.

## Relation to the mixed Green route

The two routes meet but should not be conflated. The mixed Green programme
asks for a positive oriented conservation law on the coherent Mellin line.
The star-extension programme asks whether zero-induced discrete boundary data
belong to the continuous adjoint source geometry. Either could confine zeros
if completed independently. Neither follows from flat finite prime transport
or scalar analytic continuation.

The extension route may be the more categorical one because its decisive
object is a restriction functor and its essential image. Its immediate test
is finite source coherence, not an inequality.
