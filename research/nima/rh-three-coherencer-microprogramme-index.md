# RH Three-Coherencer Microprogramme

## Scope

This microprogramme studies the comparison among reciprocal transport,
finite-to-archimedean valuation transport, and moving-endpoint Green boundary
transport before scalar projection. It does not assert RH or identify a
constructed determinant line with the completed xi section.

## Hard core

- Prime and prime-power labels remain typed in the source category.
- Endpoint forcing is transported with its exact theta connection.
- Reciprocal, adelic, Green, dagger, and determinant operations are distinct
  typed constructors.
- A scalar determinant cannot reconstruct a forcing or boundary port erased
  before its formation.
- Zero confinement requires a source-derived characteristic section and may
  not be inferred from symmetry or positivity alone.

The hard core reopens if an exact source construction proves that one of these
distinctions is redundant while preserving every declared hostile test.
