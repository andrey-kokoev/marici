# qRB character target: first translation derivative

Differentiate the complete target with respect to the translation parameter `d` at `d=0`.

The endpoint and gamma channels are even in `d`, so

$$
\partial_dK_{\rm endpoint}(t,0)=0,
\qquad
\partial_dK_{\rm gamma}(t,0)=0.
$$

The prime channel gives

$$
\partial_dK_{\rm prime}(t,0)
=-\frac1{4t\sqrt{\pi t}}
\sum_{n\ge2}\frac{\Lambda(n)\log n}{\sqrt n}
 e^{-(\log n)^2/(4t)}.
$$

Thus the first translation derivative isolates a weighted prime moment. It is a concrete diagnostic for the oriented translate-Gram interface and must be matched by the corresponding derivative of the relative linking block.
