# Many-many projective pair-response topology

Let `p` range over source seminorms and `q_K` over compact-local graph seminorms on the bordered response. The pair response is continuous when

$$
q_K(\mathcal B_{\rm rad}(f,g))
\le
M_{K,p}\,p(f)p(g)
$$

for every compact parameter set `K` and source seminorm `p`.

The projective tensor universal property then gives

$$
\mathcal B_{\rm rad}:
\mathscr T_\theta\widehat\otimes_\pi\overline{\mathscr T_\theta}
\to
\mathcal G_{\rm pair,rad}
$$

continuously. Endpoint rows use the separate projective endpoint seminorms, and reciprocal exchange acts continuously on both factors.

This topology is the global many-many replacement for an unavailable uniform bare-Hilbert endpoint norm.
