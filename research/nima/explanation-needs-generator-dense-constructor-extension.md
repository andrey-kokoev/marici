# Explanation needs generator-dense constructor extension

## Attack on bare naturality

A natural constructor functor can still be a lookup table indexed by an entire
source category. Store one realization for every source object and one
commuting map for every source morphism. Naturality then holds, but no global
behaviour is generated from a smaller law.

The missing condition is reconstructibility from a proper source-authorized
generator packet.

## Generator packet

Let

\[
i:\mathcal D\hookrightarrow\mathcal C_{\mathrm{src}}
\]

be a predeclared subcategory containing the local source generators, their
primitive transformations, and their defining relations. Let

\[
F:\mathcal C_{\mathrm{src}}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{real}}
\]

be the proposed realization functor.

The restriction

\[
i^*F:\mathcal D\longrightarrow\mathcal C_{\mathrm{real}}
\]

is the finite or otherwise bounded constructor seed.

An explanatory extension requires a canonical comparison such as

\[
\operatorname{Lan}_i(i^*F)\longrightarrow F
\]

or

\[
F\longrightarrow\operatorname{Ran}_i(i^*F)
\]

to be an isomorphism on the declared source family. The choice of left or
right extension is dictated by the source operations; it is not a cosmetic
duality.

This says that the global realization is forced by the generator data and its
composition laws.

## Finite-presentation form

When the source operation category is presented by generators

\[
g_1,\ldots,g_m
\]

and relations

\[
r_1,\ldots,r_k,
\]

the realization is specified by the endomorphisms

\[
F(g_1),\ldots,F(g_m)
\]

provided every relation is satisfied:

\[
F(r_j)=I.
\]

Every composite intervention is then predicted by multiplication of the
generator images. A separate fitted matrix for each word is prohibited.

The explanatory content is the descent from the free word category through
the independently frozen relations.

## Relation to the three lenses

The Endo seed assigns ordered transformations to source generators. Product
and Sum readouts descend from this assignment:

\[
F(g_j)\longmapsto\Delta(g_j)\longmapsto j(g_j).
\]

Their values on composites must be derived using their own coefficient laws.
If a lower lens requires a correction not generated by the Endo seed or an
already declared anomaly cocycle, the claimed explanation is incomplete.

Conversely, lower-lens agreement on all generators need not determine the
Endo relations. Commutator and coherence probes remain necessary.

## Toric-code instance

The toric-code family is generated locally by:

- vertices, oriented edges, and faces;
- edge-to-endpoint incidence;
- face-to-boundary-edge incidence;
- primal and dual Pauli labels;
- periodic gluing relations.

The global chain complex is assembled from these generators. The identity

\[
\partial_1\partial_2=0
\]

is forced locally by paired cancellation of face-boundary endpoints and then
extends to every chain.

The Pauli lift assigns \(X\)- and \(Z\)-actions to primal and dual edge
generators. Their local intersection cocycle generates global loop
anticommutation. No separate rule is fitted for each noncontractible loop.

Global homology still depends on the periodic gluing data. The local cell
packet without gluing does not determine the torus logical sector. This
prevents “local generators” from silently erasing the Carrier topology.

## Residue of failed extension

If the Kan comparison fails, the discrepancy

\[
\operatorname{Lan}_i(i^*F)\longrightarrow F
\]

locates behaviour not generated by the declared seed. That discrepancy may
be:

- an omitted global gluing datum;
- an anomaly or cocycle;
- a boundary or completion state;
- a genuinely new source constructor;
- or a fitted correction.

It counts as explanatory progress only when its target type and incidence are
derived independently. Merely adjoining the discrepancy after observing it
would make generator extension unfalsifiable.

## Completion gate

Finite-stage generator density need not survive completion. Let \(F_N\) be
generated from one fixed seed family at every cutoff. The completed
constructor is explained by the same seed only if the comparison maps extend
continuously and remain jointly faithful.

A hostile family has exact finite reconstruction while the norm of the
extension or inverse comparison diverges. Then every finite object is
generator-controlled but the completed object is not.

## Falsifiers

- Choose \(\mathcal D=\mathcal C_{\mathrm{src}}\); reconstruction becomes
  tautological.
- Add a new generator after each failed prediction.
- Fit independent images for composite words.
- Enforce source relations only after scalar projection.
- Ignore a nontrivial anomaly cocycle in the ordered lift.
- Reconstruct local incidence while omitting global gluing data.
- Use a cutoff-dependent generator family.
- Obtain finite Kan comparisons whose continuity constants diverge.
- Call a failed comparison a residue before its target object is sourced.

## Revised conjecture

An explanation is a proof-carrying constructor functor generated from a
proper, predeclared source packet by a source-typed universal extension. Its
authorized moduli embed stably into family-level intervention behaviour, and
all lower-lens shadows are derived from the same generator action.

This replaces vague explanatory compression with an exact question:

> Which smallest independently authorized generator packet determines the
> complete intervention constructor, including its gluing, anomaly, and
> completion data?
