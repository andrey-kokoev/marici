# Interface coherence closes as a source-rooted proof DAG

## Result

An interface coherence cell is not exempt from the compiler. It is itself an
authority-bearing constructor and must carry semantic, operational, and source
support appropriate to its claim.

This does not force an infinite tower. The regress closes when the operative
certificate is a finite well-founded dependency graph whose leaves are
primitive source-authorized constructors.

The graph is generally a DAG rather than a tree because several higher cells
may share the same source witness. Admission requires:

- every nonprimitive node names its complete dependencies;
- every dependency has the required type;
- every path terminates at a primitive authority root;
- the dependency graph is acyclic;
- no node acquires authority solely from another node that depends on it.

## Admitted pattern

Primitive semantic and operational witnesses support their respective
profiles. Those profiles support the cross-axis comparison. The operative
constructor then depends on both profiles and the comparison cell. Shared
support is explicit and finite.

## Cyclic hostile

Consider two comparison cells \(\omega_1\) and \(\omega_2\). Let \(\omega_1\)
cite \(\omega_2\) as its sole authority support, while \(\omega_2\) cites
\(\omega_1\). Their signatures may match and their extensional equations may
even hold, but neither path reaches a primitive source root.

This is authority laundering by a support cycle. Declaring either cell valid
because the other is valid does not close the regress.

## Canonical coherence

When comparison cells are generated by normalization to a source-addressed
normal form, the normalization derivations are the support DAG. Triangle or
higher coherence may then telescope by cancellation rather than requiring
freely asserted higher certificates. The normal form, rewrite relations, and
termination proof still require source authority.

## Falsifier

The checker admits a finite shared-support DAG and rejects both a two-node cycle
and an unsupported nonprimitive leaf. The typed residuals are
`cyclic_authority_support` and `missing_primitive_authority_root`.
