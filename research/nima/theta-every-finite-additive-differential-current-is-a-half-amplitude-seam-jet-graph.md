# Every finite additive theta differential current is a half-amplitude–seam-jet graph

## Status

Exact all-orders finite closure theorem. The first Gaussian number current was
not an isolated failure. Every current obtained by applying a finite-order
constant-coefficient differential operator in the additive scale coordinate to
the half-source has a Mellin transform determined by the old half-amplitude and
finitely many seam jets.

Therefore no finite additive oscillator or derivative ladder supplies an
independent distributed orientation port. New information must enter through
an infinite-order or nonlocal operation, a multiplicative arithmetic incidence,
or a completion-at-infinity boundary functional.

## Half-amplitude

Let (A(u)\) be a smooth rapidly decaying half-source and define

\[
H(z)=\int_0^\infty A(u)e^{izu}\,du.
\]

For each integer (m\geq0\), let

\[
I_m(z)=\int_0^\infty A^{(m)}(u)e^{izu}\,du.
\]

Rapid decay removes the boundary at infinity.

## Repeated integration-by-parts formula

One integration by parts gives

\[
I_m(z)
=-A^{(m-1)}(0)-izI_{m-1}(z).
\]

Iterating yields

\[
I_m(z)
=(-iz)^mH(z)
-\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}
(-iz)^{m-1-j}A^{(j)}(0).
\]

Thus the transform of the (m)-th additive derivative lies in the span of

\[
H(z),A(0),A'(0),\ldots,A^{(m-1)}(0)
\]

with explicit polynomial coefficients in (z\).

## Finite differential operator

Let

\[
P(D)=\sum_{m=0}^Mp_mD^m,
\qquad
D=\partial_u.
\]

Then

\[
\int_0^\infty P(D)A(u)e^{izu}\,du
=P(-iz)H(z)
+\sum_{j=0}^{M-1}q_j(z)A^{(j)}(0),
\]

where each (q_j\) is an explicitly determined polynomial.

The transformed current is an affine linear graph coordinate over the old
half-amplitude and a finite seam-jet packet.

## Observation-rank consequence

At fixed (z\), adjoining any finite family of such differential currents does
not increase observation rank beyond the span of

\[
H(z),A(0),A'(0),\ldots,A^{(M-1)}(0).
\]

If those seam jets are already retained, the rank increment is zero. If they
are not retained, the current only reveals which boundary jets were previously
discarded; it does not create a new bulk observable.

## Reciprocal double

For the two half-amplitudes

\[
H_+(z)=H(z),
\qquad
H_-(z)=H(-z),
\]

every finite additive current on either sheet lies in the span of

\[
H_+(z),H_-(z)
\]

and the two oriented seam-jet packets. Reciprocal sewing permutes these graph
coordinates and applies the appropriate jet parity. It does not impose a new
relation between (H_+\) and (H_-\).

The scalar channel

\[
X=H_++H_-
\]

may therefore vanish while the transverse channel

\[
Y=H_+-H_-
\]

and every derived finite current remain well-defined.

Grothendieck's sewn number-current identities are the first-order instance of
this general theorem.

## Finite oscillator ladders

Creation, annihilation, Euler, and oscillator-number expressions built from a
finite polynomial in (u\) and \(\partial_u\) require a slight enlargement:
multiplication by (u\) transforms into spectral differentiation of (H\).
Consequently any finite Weyl-algebra current transforms into the finite jet
span generated by

\[
\partial_z^kH(z)
\]

and finitely many seam derivatives.

This is still a graph extension of the finite spectral-jet state. It can improve
observability but cannot orient the distinguished scalar projection without an
independent relation among those jets.

## What escapes the theorem

The closure does not cover:

- infinite-order differential operators whose series introduces a new domain
  or flat remainder;
- nonlocal convolution, translation, or integral operators in (u\);
- multiplicative prime and prime-power incidence at logarithmic scales;
- distributional primitive boundary currents;
- boundary functionals created only at restricted-product completion;
- marked ports with independent monodromy or grading.

These are precisely the remaining locations where a new port can live.

## Finite falsifier

For a proposed finite additive current, compute its transformed row and reduce
it against the matrix of half-amplitude and seam-jet rows. A zero rank increment
proves that the current is derived.

Any claim of an independent bulk coordinate is falsified by the repeated
integration-by-parts identity above.

## Consequence for the RH lane

The finite additive oscillator-ladder programme is closed in full generality.
It selects and observes the Gaussian source but cannot constrain the relative
half-amplitude (X/Y\) phase beyond existing spectral and seam jets.

The earliest still-unclosed crossing is the source map from multiplicative
valuation/Fock incidence into the additive boundary object. That map is
nonlocal in the additive scale coordinate and was already identified as
missing. The present theorem shows why it cannot be replaced by any finite
local differential surrogate.
