# Common factorization carrier: DPC falsification

## Problem

Test the conjecture that one source-derived factorization carrier explains scalar, NLSM, Yang–Mills, gravity, CHY localization, soft limits, and loop cuts by changing coefficient systems.

## Bold conjecture tested

There is one carrier generated by decorated factorization strata and incidence relations. Theory-specific amplitude structures are boundary-preserving coefficient functors from that carrier.

## Named rivals

1. A block-diagonal aggregate of theory-specific complexes can mimic a common carrier without explaining their agreement.
2. Locality and unitarity impose common boundary equations, while color, helicity, and worldsheet exactness require independent mechanisms.
3. Several carriers are related only on restricted quotients; no faithful common source exists.

## Strongest falsification attempt

### Test 1: nontriviality of the existential claim

For arbitrary theory complexes `C_i`, form

\[
F_{\mathrm{triv}}=\bigoplus_i C_i
\]

with block-diagonal boundary and projection coefficient functors. This construction automatically satisfies existence of one carrier, boundary preservation, and recovery of every theory. It works even when the theories have no shared explanation.

Therefore the conjecture as stated does not distinguish a common mechanism from a disjoint archive. “One carrier” has no risky content unless indecomposability, generation, minimality, or a universal property excludes this direct-sum construction.

### Test 2: minimal factorization-strata carrier

Impose the strongest natural repair: the carrier is generated only by common labelled factorization cells and their incidence boundaries, with no target-specific generators. At six points the minimal common complex is the labelled five-simplex complex. All 24 fundamental BCJ-weighted NMHV chains have nonzero boundary residues:

\[
dT(b)\ne0.
\]

Hence they are not cycles and cannot encode BCJ descent in this source. Repair requires four new degree-four defect lifts and four degree-five null-homotopies. Those generators are introduced specifically to cancel the Yang–Mills/BCJ defect and have no derivation from the common factorization strata.

## Exact residual

The conjecture faces a disjunction:

- permit arbitrary coefficient-specific summands, in which case it is satisfied trivially and explains nothing;
- forbid them and use only shared factorization incidence, in which case the six-point BCJ square already fails.

No currently defined intermediate axiom selects the eight-generator repair from common source geometry.

## Disposition

The conjecture is rejected in its stated form. It is either non-falsifiable because direct sums always realize it, or false for the minimal common incidence carrier tested at six points.

The surviving explanatory statement is weaker: physical factorization supplies a shared interface, while different theories may require additional, independently typed structures. A new unification conjecture is admissible only after specifying a nontrivial universal property that excludes direct sums and independently derives the BCJ repair generators.
