# Six-port coherence does not imply the RH energy-cycle law

Let

$$
H=\mathbb C[q,q^{-1}]
$$

with

$$
\Delta(q)=q\otimes q,
\qquad
\varepsilon(q)=1,
\qquad
\mathsf S(q)=q^{-1}.
$$

This is the group Hopf algebra of `Z`. Let the source module be one dimensional and let

$$
\rho_z(q)=e^{-2z}.
$$

Its contragredient is

$$
\rho_z^\vee(q)=e^{2z},
$$

so the evaluation pairing is invariant. Unit, multiplication, comultiplication, antipode, realization, and dual realization satisfy every six-port identity.

Give the source the positive energy

$$
\mathcal E(v)=|v|^2.
$$

Then the Hermitian modular covariance is

$$
\mathcal E(\rho_z(q)v)
=e^{-4\operatorname{Re}z}\mathcal E(v).
$$

Choose any off-seam point `z_0` with `Re z_0 != 0` and define an independent holomorphic transverse section

$$
\tau(z)=z-z_0.
$$

Its kernel state is the nonzero vector `v=1`. All Hopf and paired-module coherence remains valid at `z_0`, but the energy defect is

$$
\delta(z_0)
=(1-e^{-4\operatorname{Re}z_0})\mathcal E(1)
e0.
$$

Thus six-port bialgebraic coherence, duality, positivity, and a holomorphic transverse divisor do not imply that divisor zeros close the modular energy cycle.

The missing input must specifically couple the transverse section to the conservative energy routes. Any proof of that coupling in the theta system carries genuine arithmetic content and cannot be replaced by abstract coherence axioms.

Status: logical independence established by a finite countermodel; formal six-port completion alone cannot prove the RH-equivalent cycle law.
