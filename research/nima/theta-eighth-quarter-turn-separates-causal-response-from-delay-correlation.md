\[

Therefore a causal unitary response can have a positive delay density whose
time-domain transform is not causal.

## Why the support doubles

\[

\]

time-reversed adjoint response. Correlation records relative delay but erases
the arrow distinguishing retarded from advanced propagation.

This is the same structural loss seen elsewhere in the programme:

- a Gram form preserves magnitude while forgetting a frame;
- a determinant preserves total vanishing while forgetting the kernel plane;
- a delay density preserves oriented phase speed while forgetting the causal

The one-pole example already falsifies the simpler claim that positivity of
