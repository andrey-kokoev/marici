## Labelled vacuum module

For each positive integer (n), let

\[
\phi_n(x)=e^{-\pi n^2x^2},
\qquad
a_n=\partial_x+2\pi n^2x.
\]

Then

\[
a_n\phi_n=0.
\]

Embed a finite arithmetic coefficient packet (c=(c_n)) into the labelled
archimedean carrier by

\[
\mathcal Uc=(c_n\phi_n)_n.
\]

Define the direct-sum annihilation operator

\[
\mathcal A=\bigoplus_n a_n.
\]

Linearity gives

\[
\mathcal A\mathcal Uc=0
\]

for every coefficient packet (c), not only for the distinguished positive
Fock vacuum.

## Energy no-go theorem

The positive oscillator form is

\[
\mathcal H=\mathcal A^{*}\mathcal A.
\]

On the complete labelled Gaussian vacuum module,

\[
\langle\mathcal Uc,\mathcal H\mathcal Uc\rangle=0
\]

for every (c). Therefore this form has no lower bound on arithmetic
coefficient directions and cannot distinguish:

- the actual prime/Fock packet;
- a signed coefficient perturbation;

The kernel contains the entire synthesized arithmetic module.
