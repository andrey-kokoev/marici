Joint Green-form domination is the exact certificate for Euler-weight compactness -- the two conditions are equivalent, establishing the precise operator-theoretic hypothesis for the spectral analysis of the weighted Euler operator.

Key results:
- Euler-weight compactness reduces to bounded Green-form domination, not an independent assumption.
- Joint Green-form domination is the exact certificate: the Euler-weighted operator is compact if and only if the joint Green form dominates the relevant norms.
