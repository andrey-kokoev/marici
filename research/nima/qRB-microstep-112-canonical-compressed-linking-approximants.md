# qRB microstep 112: canonical compressed linking approximants

Once the bounded limit operator `J_link` is fixed on `H_orb`, choose finite-rank projections `P_n` from the source-adapted filtration and define

$$
J_n=P_nJ_{\rm link}P_n.
$$

Then automatically

$$
\|J_n\|\le\|J_{\rm link}\|,
$$

and, if `P_n` tends strongly to the identity,

$$
\langle J_nu,v\rangle\to\langle J_{\rm link}u,v\rangle
$$

for all `u,v` in the orbit.

This supplies uniformly bounded canonical approximants. The remaining issue is whether these compressed blocks coincide with the original regulator-defined finite readouts; that equality is not automatic and must be tested as a separate source-compatibility condition.

Status: canonical bounded approximation available at operator level; identification with physical finite regulators remains open.
