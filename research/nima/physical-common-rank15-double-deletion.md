# The rank-fifteen common sector is the intrinsic double-deletion face

The repaired five-mark localization presentation contains both physical
deletion faces before quotient.  Inside that one presentation, impose both
label conditions

\[
\ell_{g_{23}}=\ell_{g_{31}}=0.
\]

The resulting double-deletion face has rank 15.  It lies in each rank-20
physical face, and its rank equals the independently computed intersection
rank.  Therefore

\[
\boxed{F_{23}\cap F_{31}=F_{\varnothing\{23,31\}}}
\]

at the tested stable cutoff.  This identifies the common sector intrinsically;
it is not an intersection obtained by mapping between independently truncated
quotients.  Both computed Gauss--Manin directions preserve it exactly.

At the generic test twist \(\gamma=5\), the three remaining marked
denominators give a further source-labelled filtration.  Retaining zero, one,
two, or three of those marks gives ranks

\[
9,\qquad 10,\qquad 12,\qquad 15,
\]

respectively.  Equivalently, the successive rank increments are

\[
1,\qquad2,\qquad3.
\]

For each choice, the one-mark face has rank 10 and the two-mark face has rank
12.  Pairwise sums of the rank-12 faces have rank 14, their pairwise
intersections have rank 10, and all three rank-12 faces together span the
rank-15 common sector.  Every labelled face tested here is preserved by both
connection directions.

At generic \(\gamma\), the rank-nine no-mark core is therefore a canonical
horizontal subobject of the rank-fifteen common sector, with a six-dimensional
marked-localization quotient carrying a source-derived filtration of
associated ranks

\[
1+2+3=6.
\]

The canonical localization map from the generic-twist absolute no-mark presentation into
the common presentation is obtained by inserting five zero pole levels.  Its
typing was audited on every retained absolute relation.  All relation images
vanish, its image has rank 9 and equals the intrinsic no-mark core, and it
intertwines both connection directions exactly.  Hence, at the tested cutoff,

\[
\boxed{H_{9}^{\rm abs}\;\xrightarrow{\sim}\;H_{9}^{\rm core}
       \subset H_{15}^{\rm common}.}
\]

This is a genuine finite-cutoff isomorphism of the computed generic-twist
connection objects, not a dimension coincidence or an independently chosen
quotient map.

## The physical half-twist changes the filtration

For the double-cover coefficient \(K^{-1/2}\), set

\[

\qquad

\]

absolute presentation still has rank 9, but its natural zero-level map into
the common marked quotient has rank 6.  Every retained absolute relation maps

kinematic direction.  Thus

\[
\boxed{H^{\rm abs}_{9}\not\hookrightarrow H^{\rm common}_{15}}
\]

as the naively localized physical connection object.  The six-dimensional
image is generated by the six simple \(K^{-1/2}\) forms in the equation-(58)
basis.

interpretation.  The three literal normal double-pole classes
