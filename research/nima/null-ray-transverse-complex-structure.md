# Null-ray transverse complex structure

For a future null ray \([k]\) in an oriented, time-oriented four-dimensional
Lorentz space, the physical Ward quotient

\[
Q_k=k^\perp/\langle k\rangle
\]

is a canonically oriented Euclidean two-plane.  Its metric and orientation
determine the quarter-turn \(J\), hence the helicity projectors

\[
P_\pm=\frac{1\mp iJ}{2}.
\]

The exact frame audit shows that every orientation-preserving transverse frame
commutes with \(J\), while an orientation reversal sends \(J\mapsto-J\) and
swaps \(P_+\leftrightarrow P_-\).

Thus Entry 1574's local Bell lens does not require a freely chosen complex
structure once spacetime orientation, time orientation, and the future null
ray are part of the source.  Entry 126's global time-root equalizer is not,
without a comparison map, the missing spacetime-orientation datum.
