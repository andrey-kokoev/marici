# Relative-Haar energy-cycle law is equivalent to Xi seam confinement

Fix one prime `p>1`. For every Xi zero, the stable histories glue:

$$
u_+(z)=u_-(z)=u_z.
$$

Assume the fixed-forcing graph state is nonzero and belongs to the relative-Haar form domain, so

$$
\mathcal E_z(J_\Phi u_z)>0.
$$

The route-energy defect is then

$$
\delta_p(z)
=(1-p^{-2\operatorname{Re}z})
\mathcal E_z(J_\Phi u_z).
$$

Therefore

$$
\delta_p(z)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
p^{-2\operatorname{Re}z}=1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Re}z=0.
$$

Consequently, on the Xi divisor, the statement

$$
\delta_p(z)=0
$$

for one fixed prime is equivalent to critical-seam confinement. Requiring it for every prime adds redundancy, not a weaker preliminary coherence condition.

Thus the energy-cycle identity cannot be established solely from generic Hopf, Fourier, Stokes, or determinant coherence unless those structures contain the missing arithmetic confinement theorem. All formal and analytic setup has reduced the problem to an exact equivalent form; it has not proved the equivalent statement.
