# Xi-torsion lift iteration 1: two-line deconvolved observation proves a strict horizontal codiagonal

## Fresh correction to the topology ledger

The earlier topology summary left post-codiagonal projective recovery open. A
subsequent source result already proves it after retaining the deconvolved
entire response on two complex horizontal lines. This closes objective 1 in a
precise deconvolved graph category, though not yet in the raw-history topology.

## Source and observer spaces

Let `K` be the projective Köthe space with seminorms

\[
q_\delta(c)=\sum_{p,k}
(|c_{p,k}^+|+|c_{p,k}^-|)e^{\delta L_{p,k}},
\qquad L_{p,k}=k\log p.
\]

Define the deconvolved codiagonal

\[
Jc(z)=F_c(z)
=
\sum_{p,k}\frac{e^{-L_{p,k}/2}}k
\left(c_{p,k}^+e^{izL_{p,k}}
+c_{p,k}^-e^{-izL_{p,k}}\right).
\]

Give its range the analytic graph topology generated by

\[
S_R(F)=\max\left\{
\sup_x|F(x+iR)|,
\sup_x|F(x-iR)|
\right\},
\qquad R>0.
\]

## Tame two-line inverse

Bohr extraction on the two lines gives, for every `R>delta+3/2`,

\[
q_\delta(c)
\le
K_{R-\delta-1/2}S_R(Jc),
\]

where

\[
K_\varepsilon
=2\sum_p\frac{p^{-\varepsilon}}
{(1-p^{-\varepsilon})^2}<\infty
\quad(\varepsilon>1).
\]

Conversely, each `S_R(Jc)` is bounded by a sufficiently stronger `q_delta`.
Hence `J` is a topological isomorphism from `K` onto its range, with finite
seminorm shifts. Since `K` is complete, the range is complete and therefore
closed in the Hausdorff observer graph space. Thus `J` is strict.

No frequency-separation or Hilbert-frame estimate is needed.

## Horizontality

Differentiation acts coefficientwise by

\[
\partial_zJc
=J(Dc),
\qquad
(Dc)_{p,k}^+=iL_{p,k}c_{p,k}^+,
\qquad
(Dc)_{p,k}^-=-iL_{p,k}c_{p,k}^-.
\]

For every `eta>0`,

\[
L e^{\delta L}\le C_\eta e^{(\delta+\eta)L},
\]

so

\[
q_\delta(Dc)\le C_\eta q_{\delta+\eta}(c).
\]

Therefore `D` is continuous on the projective Köthe source and `J` intertwines
it with the holomorphic connection `partial_z`. The deconvolved codiagonal is
both strict and horizontal.

## Raw-history qualification

The raw common history satisfies

\[
\widehat h_c(z)=\widehat\Phi(z)Jc(z).
\]

The proof above uses `Jc` as retained observer data. It does not prove that the
two-line seminorms of `Jc` are continuously recoverable from ordinary raw-
history seminorms, because division by `widehat Phi` along complete horizontal
lines may cross zeros or have unbounded reciprocal growth.

Accordingly, objective 1 is proved in the **deconvolved analytic graph
category**. Extending it to a topology that is authoritative for the raw
bordered Green target still requires a source-derived theta parametrix or a
proof that the bordered construction already outputs the deconvolved channel.

## Consequence for the remaining objectives

In this category the codiagonal cokernel is Hausdorff, inherits the spectral
connection, and has no torsion arising from nonclosed range. The remaining
question is now purely a membership/type question:

\[
H_{\rm border}\stackrel{?}{\in}\operatorname{im}J.
\]

The next turn should audit the actual construction of `H_border` and determine
whether its Fourier transform factors as `widehat Phi` times a two-line
controlled exponential sum, rather than inferring this from
`Delta_border=tau H_border`.
