## The coupling was already explicit

Local updates do not generally agree on the shared edge. For x=(0,1,0,0,0), the full-cycle proposals for AB are 1 and 0. The synchronized conditions remove this conflict without averaging:

    x_BC+x_AD=0,
    x_CA+x_DB=0.

They give R1 K=I and R2 K=-I. The two local triples therefore evolve compatibly under the same C or S. This is a genuine shared-seam compatibility calculation, not just a retained comparison of arbitrary sections.

The alternative serial extensions are also explicit: full steps return in five iterations; short half-steps have infinite order by an exact nonintegral-trace obstruction. Consequently the shared seed and norm preservation alone do not choose the intended process.
