# A primitive left cokernel detects new incidence but not its orientation

## Cross-sector lift

Strominger's bounded sewing audit finds a signed inter-component defect outside
the ordinary target-component image. The natural exact certificate is an
integral left-cokernel functional.

Let

\[
A:\mathbb Z^m\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^n
\]

be the ordinary incidence matrix and let \(K\in\mathbb Z^n\) be the proposed
new current. A primitive row \(\ell\) satisfying

\[
\ell A=0,
\qquad
\ell K\ne0
\]

proves that \(K\) is not in the integral image of \(A\). The same equation
also excludes membership over \(\mathbb Q\).

## What this proves

The nonzero value detects a new quotient direction. This is stronger than
failure at a list of finite cutoffs because one symbolic functional can
annihilate every ordinary column in the declared family.

The invariant obstruction is sign-free:

\[
|\ell K|>0.
\]

## What this does not prove

The primitive generator of a rank-one left cokernel is unique only up to sign:

\[
\ell\longleftrightarrow-\ell.
\]

Consequently the signed value \(\ell K\) is not source-oriented until a
determinant-functor comparison fixes the generator across presentations and
cutoffs.

This cleanly separates two claims:

1. Cokernel novelty: the defect is a genuine additional incidence direction.
2. Oriented current: the source canonically decides which sign of that
   direction is positive.

The first can be established by Smith or Hermite data. The second requires the
typed determinant functor.

## Minimal model

Take the ordinary column

\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
K=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then \(\ell=(1,-1)\) annihilates \(A\) and gives \(\ell K=2\). The
generator \(-\ell\) gives \(-2\) with identical image and quotient data.

## Theta/Tate consequence

The same calculation should be performed on the finite theta/Tate incidence
matrices:

- derive a primitive integral left-cokernel row;
- prove it annihilates the entire ordinary constructor family symbolically;
- test it on the primitive, square, seam, and archimedean defect packet;
- retain the absolute pairing as the presentation-independent novelty test;
- orient its sign only through the source determinant functor.

This avoids conflating a new arithmetic port with a positive arithmetic port.
