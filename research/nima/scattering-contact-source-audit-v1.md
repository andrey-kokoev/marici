## Coordinate gauge versus a genuinely different model

Put y=x/ell and phi(y)=sqrt(ell) psi(ell y). This is unitary on the moving field and gives

    integral ||psi(x)||^2 dx = integral ||phi(y)||^2 dy,
    kappa integral ||psi'(x)||^2 dx
      =(kappa/ell^2) integral ||phi'(y)||^2 dy,
    sqrt(ell) psi(0)=phi(0).

Thus the kinetic parameter relative to the fixed native energy convention is

    beta=kappa/ell^2.

Changing (kappa,ell) from (1,1) to (4,2) without changing beta, eta or thresholds is unitarily equivalent under that dilation, with the coordinate reader transported too. This equivalence concerns the unanchored encounter-coordinate model and its channel fluxes. If an independent physical/geometric position reader is held fixed rather than transported, the models need not be observationally equivalent for that expanded task. By contrast, varying beta with the core, ports, threshold and eta fixed changes dimensionless predictions. An overall energy-unit change must scale the native core too; it is not the rival tested here.

## Exact source-preserving scattering rivals

For equal thresholds theta and E>theta, diagonalize the actual rim exchange into even/odd ports. Write

    v_s(E)=eta/(1-eta r0_s(E)),
    z=2 sqrt(beta(E-theta)),
    t_s=iz/(iz-v_s),       r_s=t_s-1.

In each parity channel,

    T_s=z^2/(z^2+v_s^2),       R_s=v_s^2/(z^2+v_s^2),
    T_s+R_s=1.

For incidence in one original rim port, the total flux into the other rim port is

    P_other = z^2 (v_even-v_odd)^2
              /[2(z^2+v_even^2)(z^2+v_odd^2)].

This is a subdivision of reflection/transmission, not an extra probability to add to them. At rational E=113/100 and theta=1/2 these probabilities are exact rational numbers even though the intermediate momentum is a square root.

## Positivity alone does not choose a diagonal completion

Even if a signed off-diagonal strength g is fixed, the matrices

    C_s=[[g s,g],[g,g/s]],       g>0, s>0,

are different positive semidefinite rank-one completions with the SAME off-diagonal g. They have determinant zero and positive trace. A negative off-diagonal convention has the same family after one port phase reversal. In the finite source, auxiliary metric 3I and W^T M W=3I cancel in the whitening of the scalar g; the checker verifies that normalization rather than silently copying g across unmatched norms.

The equal mismatch prescription corresponds to a particular relative weighting. Exchange symmetry or a stipulated minimal-trace rule can select the balanced member inside this family, but those are additional assumptions. Positivity and conservation alone do not select it. This finite contact-form control does not assert that all members have already been admitted as native continuum operations.
