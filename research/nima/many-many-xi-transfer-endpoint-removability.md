# Many-many Xi transfer endpoint removability

The forced transfer is

$$
H(s)=\frac{2\xi(s)-1}{s(s-1)}.
$$

For the standard completed Xi normalization,

$$
\xi(0)=\xi(1)=\frac12,
$$

so the numerator vanishes at both endpoint roots of the denominator. Hence `H` has removable singularities at `s=0` and `s=1`, with values determined by the corresponding derivatives:

$$
H(0)=-2\xi'(0),
\qquad
H(1)=2\xi'(1).
$$

The functional equation `xi(s)=xi(1-s)` gives

$$
\xi'(1)=-\xi'(0),
$$

and therefore

$$
H(0)=H(1).
$$

Status: endpoint removability and reciprocal endpoint matching established under the standard Xi normalization.
