# The nonspherical interior hostile does not block the distinguished zeta lane because that source is finitely spherical

## Question

Does failure of endpoint-kernel invariance under finite translations obstruct
the source actually used by the completed zeta section?

## Claim boundary

No. The conductor-three hostile is valid for a full Weyl realization, but the
distinguished source does not generate that realization. Its finite component
at every prime is

\[
\phi_{0,p}=\mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p},
\]

which is spherical and Fourier fixed. Valuation shifts and radial Fourier
transport remain in the radial carrier. They do not produce translated or
character-twisted shells.

The source-derived finite propagation currently admitted is reflection
equivariant. It preserves even and odd sectors exactly. Consequently a finite
odd port is dark to the spherical even observer, and a parity-changing mixed
return cannot be inferred from the existing source.

## Programme separation

The finite translation hostile applies only to a ramified enlargement containing
the full additive Weyl orbit. Such an enlargement changes the local Tate
section and would require a relative determinant comparison back to the
unramified Euler factor. No such comparison is constructed.

For the distinguished zeta lane, the admissible architecture is instead:

- finite radial valuation and Euler channels;
- the Fourier-fixed unramified vacuum;
- the source-derived archimedean odd direction;
- endpoint wall/jump transport through the weighted Hadamard map.

A primewise finite odd Weyl port is neither present nor required by this source.

## Correct residual

The unresolved mixed cell is global rather than a local nonspherical
compression. One must determine whether the full adelic operation produces a
nonzero coupling between finite radial arithmetic loading and the
archimedean reciprocal-odd port. If the operation factorizes placewise, those
components remain separable and the mixed block vanishes. A nonzero block must
come from an independently constructed global connection, determinant
variation, or Poisson boundary term.

## Disposition

Freeze the ramified Weyl enlargement as an optional programme, not a blocker
for the distinguished zeta lane. The next executable audit is the provenance
of the existing global finite-radial/archimedean-odd cross term: source-derived
global operation, endpoint coboundary, or observer-fitted scalar.
