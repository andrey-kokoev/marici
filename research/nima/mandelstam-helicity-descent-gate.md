# Mandelstam–Helicity Descent Gate

## Result

The product character found from the two independent binary normalizations is
geometrically canonical, but the existing alternating-fusion conductor section
does **not** occupy that mixed character line.

The scaffold paper supplies the source-level dictionary

\[
q_i=p_{2i-1}+p_{2i},\qquad
\epsilon_i\propto p_{2i}-p_{2i-1}.
\]

On the fusion residue this gives null momentum and a null transverse
(``circular'') polarization.  In four dimensions the factorization

\[
(p_{2i-1}+p_{2i})^2
=\langle 2i-1,2i\rangle[2i-1,2i]=0
\]

has two spinor branches exchanged by parity.  However, Entries 50 and 66 have
already pushed the three-gluon section and its conductor symbol down to the
Mandelstam invariant ring generated by the \(X_{ij}\).  Parity fixes this ring.
Therefore

\[
\Pi_{\rm spin}^{-}A_3^+=
\Pi_{\rm spin}^{-}A_3^-=0,
\qquad
\Pi_{\rm spin}^{-}\sigma_{\rm alt}=0.
\]

The exact checker verifies this directly for

\[
A_3^+=y_0x_5+y_2x_1+y_1x_3-
(y_0y_1+y_0y_2+y_1y_2),
\]

\[
A_3^-=y_1x_0+y_0x_2+y_2x_4-
(y_0y_1+y_0y_2+y_1y_2)
\]

and for all six coefficients of \(\sigma_{\rm alt}\).

## Meaning

This is not a failure of the mixed character.  It is a descent obstruction.
The \(X\)-space packet is a polarization-covariant amplitude, whereas helicity
is a coefficient/readout choice on the spinor normalization.  Forgetting that
coefficient data before taking the conductor destroys the spin-odd grade.

The next typed object is consequently a commuting (or derived-commuting)
square

\[
\begin{CD}
\text{scalar momentum/spinor normalization}
@>{\text{helicity evaluation}}> 
\text{helicity coefficient line}\\
@V{\text{alternating fusion conductor}}VV
@VV{\text{relative normal / soft residue}}V\\
\text{scaffold conductor line}
@>>{\text{mixed boundary trace}}>
\text{gravitational soft coefficient line}.
\end{CD}
\]

Its mixed-curvature defect is the first legitimate obstruction to the desired
Carrier-to-BMS orientation bridge.  Applying a spin-odd projector directly to
the existing Mandelstam polynomial is forbidden: it is identically zero and
would confuse a descended invariant section with its helicity-valued lift.
