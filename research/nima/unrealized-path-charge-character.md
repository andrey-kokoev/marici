# Sharp test: does an unrealized alternative acquire opposite charge character?

## Hypothesis and source boundary

The operator proposes that electron negative charge may involve an added unrealized future-path potential. Test the precise status-only version: retaining a forward alternative without executing it forces an opposite phase response.

Fresh sources inspected:

- the actual six primitive records in `check_natural_tower_return.py`;
- the existing finite execution adapter, which validates requested words and emits fresh occurrence IDs;
- native inverse/composition definitions in `NativeTableRules.agda`;
- the previously declared Hom-like endpoint phase adapter in `137-local-endpoint-phase-adapter.md`;
- the source audit distinguishing native derivations from execution events.

Neither the execution adapter nor the inspected native definitions contains an electromagnetic phase law or a constructor mapping unrealized alternatives into dual representations. Thus a phase probe is an explicit conditional representation, not already a derived WG electromagnetic field.

## Minimal same-direction pair

Use the supplied paths

    p=AB,              A->B,
    r=AD followed DB,  A->B.

Tag p as executed and r as available. This models the proposed distinction for the test; the checker does not claim to execute a native future-path constructor.

Assign formal unit-phase links U_e and endpoint changes g_A,...,g_D, with

    U_(a->b) -> g_b U_(a->b) g_a^-1.

Then

    U_p -> g_B U_p g_A^-1,
    U_r -> g_B U_r g_A^-1.

The D endpoint cancels in the second expression. Both have the same integer endpoint-character vector (-1,+1,0,0). This is a universal monomial identity, not an inference from finitely many angles.

Changing execution status or duplicating the description leaves the endpoints, direction and character unchanged in this adapter. A linear superposition of the two paths still has the same character; two descriptions or two alternative amplitudes do not constitute twice the physical charge.

This supplies a countermodel to the claim that retention/composition alone forces opposite character. It does not prohibit adding a different, explicitly justified interpretation of unrealized potential.

## What survives: a neutral relative phase

The ratio U_r/U_p has zero endpoint character but need not equal one. Its edge exponents are

    -AB + AD + DB.

A control assigns exp(2*pi*i/3) to AD and identity to the other links. The ratio remains nontrivial under all 81 assignments of cubic-root endpoint phases. These chosen link values are a hostile probe, not curvature generated by the source. In a separately imposed flat connection the ratio could instead be one.

Thus an unrealized alternative can carry relative holonomy/interference information in a phase realization without becoming oppositely charged. Endpoint neutrality of this comparison is not absence of a physical effect if an appropriate physical coupling is later supplied.

## Reversal and duality controls

Formal inversion of AB reverses its character. The separately supplied BA arrow has the same reversed endpoint character but is a different primitive: the source does not identify its transport with U_AB^-1.

An actual dual representation also has inverse character. But dualization is additional structure, not a consequence of an unexecuted tag. Evaluation of a vector against its dual is neutral. Interpreting the dual as a prediction/effect/continuation does not by itself establish an oppositely charged matter particle.

The phase exp(2*pi*i/3) distinguishes z from z^-1 exactly; testing only z=-1 would conceal the distinction.

## Realization and conservation test

Suppose, additionally, that an unrealized alternative is assigned weight -q while its realized state has weight +q. A nonzero complex-linear intertwiner t would have to satisfy

    t z^-1 = z t

for every z in U(1), which is impossible for these unequal one-dimensional characters. A complex-antilinear conjugation does intertwine them, but is an extra operation, not the source execution-status change.

If those weights are identified with physical charges and realization changes -q into +q, an accompanying system must change by -2q to conserve total charge. The inspected construction supplies no such reservoir or transition law. Alternatively, a dual effect need not become a matter state at all; then a physical charge-conversion claim was never established.

## Outcome

The status-only hypothesis does not yield negative charge in the existing conditional path-phase realization. The available and executed routes transform alike; their relative comparison is neutral. This is a bounded result, not a refutation of every interpretation of future potential.

For the intuition to become a stronger candidate, the source must distinguish an available *parallel path* from a *dual continuation/effect*, derive the latter's transformation and give an explicit realization/evaluation operation. Calling that structure an electron would still require a matter coupling and charge-conservation account.
