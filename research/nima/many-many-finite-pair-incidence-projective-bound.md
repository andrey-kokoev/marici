# Many-many finite pair-incidence projective bound

For a compact shell `[a,b]` with `a>0`, the Gaussian pair response satisfies a finite bound

$$
\|\mathcal B_{\lambda\mu}^{[a,b]}\|_{\rm graph}
\le
C_{a,b}\,q_\lambda q_\mu,
$$

where `q_lambda` is a rapidly decreasing source seminorm. Hence the bilinear response extends to a continuous linear map on the completed projective tensor product:

$$
B_{\rm rad}^{[a,b]}:
\mathscr T_\theta\widehat\otimes_\pi
\mathscr T_\theta
\to
\mathcal G_{\rm pair,rad}^{[a,b]}.
$$

The same estimate applies to endpoint, Wronskian, and Laplace-jet coordinates. Finite cutoff restrictions commute with the map, producing a directed projective family.
