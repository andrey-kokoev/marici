# Correction: NNMHV coherence histories and RH theta histories are distinct source types

## Type collision

Two independently constructed objects have been called histories:

1. NNMHV coherence histories indexing positive cells whose pushforwards
   triangulate `A_(n,2,4)`;
2. RH translated-theta histories generated by prime-labelled scale translations
   and the theta semigroup functional.

The shared word and the shared numerical `2 by 4` signature do not define a map
between them.

On the NNMHV side there is a source map

\[
G_+(2,n)\xrightarrow{\ C\mapsto CZ\ }\mathcal A_{n,2,4}
\]

and a verified history-to-positive-cell correspondence.

On the RH side there is an exact chain

\[
\text{valuation shells}
\longrightarrow
\text{prime-mesh steps}
\longrightarrow
\text{translated-theta histories},
\]

preserving the local Tate resolvent and

\[
\kappa_p=p^{-z}+2+p^z.
\]

No source-authorized arrow currently connects the translated-theta carrier to
`G_+(2,n)`, its positive cells, or the amplituhedron canonical form.

## Consequence

Reality of NNMHV projective matrices cannot be transferred to the RH
trace-square invariant. Doing so would silently identify two carriers whose
labels, boundary maps, metrics, and source equations differ.

Therefore the implication

\[
\text{amplituhedron realness}
\Longrightarrow
\kappa_2,\kappa_3\in\mathbb R
\]

is unavailable. The two-prime criterion remains a valid seam detector, but
positive geometry does not presently supply its hypothesis.

## Required bridge

A legitimate bridge must construct a map

\[
\Psi:
\mathcal H_{\theta}^{\rm labelled}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal C_{n,2,4}^{\rm positive}
\]

that preserves at least:

- the two reciprocal orientations;

- projective trace-square;

This is substantially stronger than matching dimensions or matrix shapes.

source-derived positive-geometric filler for the RH rung-four horn. Until
