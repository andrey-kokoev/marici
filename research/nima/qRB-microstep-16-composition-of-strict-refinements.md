# qRB microstep 16: composition of strict refinements

If two regulator transports are defined by

$$
Q_X=C P_X C^{-1},
\qquad
Q_Y=C P_Y C^{-1},
$$

and the source refinements satisfy `P_X=P_Y P_X` on the retained rung, then

$$
Q_YQ_XC
=Q_YCP_X
=CP_YP_X
=CP_X
=Q_XC.
$$

Therefore strict transported refinements compose strictly. The same composition descends through the observer quotient whenever each factor preserves the radical.

This is only a categorical composition fact for typed transported regulators. It does not upgrade a lax independent-window comparison into a strict one and does not provide simultaneous regulator convergence.
