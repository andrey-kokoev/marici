# The common localization complex repairs the branch filtration

The five-mark presentation with pole levels (0,1,2) retains both deletion
faces and their coherence relations before quotient.  In this single complex,
the conditions “(q_{g31}) absent” and “(q_{g23}) absent” define genuine
labelled face subcomplexes (F_{23}) and (F_{31}).  Their cohomological images
have ranks

\[
\dim F_{23}=\dim F_{31}=20,
\qquad
\dim(F_{23}\cap F_{31})=15,
\]

\[
\dim(F_{23}+F_{31})=25,
\qquad
\dim H_{\cup}=26.
\]

Thus the filtration

\[
15\subset25\subset26
\]

is recovered after the typing repair.  Unlike the withdrawn construction, no
map between independently truncated quotient presentations is used.

The repair changes the transport conclusion.  Both face images and their sum
are preserved by both computed Gauss--Manin directions.  The literal physical
source lies in (F_{23}+F_{31}), and both of its first derivatives remain
there.  Therefore the one-dimensional quotient

\[
T_{\rm top}=H_{\cup}/(F_{23}+F_{31})
\]

is a legitimate horizontal quotient, but it is **not activated** by the
literal physical source:

\[
\boxed{
\mathrm{II}_{F_{23}+F_{31}}=0,
\qquad
s_{\rm phys}\mapsto0\text{ in }T_{\rm top}.
}
\]

The common complex also locates this line without a splitting.  Adjoining any
one of the other deletion faces (g_1,g_2,g_3) to the physical face sum raises
its rank by exactly one, and all three yield the same quotient.  A primitive
quotient representative has labelled pole levels

\[
(g_1,g_2,g_3,g_{23},g_{31})=(0,0,0,1,1)
\]

and constant numerator.  Thus

\[
T_{\rm top}
=\left[\frac{da\wedge db}{q_{g23}q_{g31}}\right]
\]

is precisely the two-mark Kato line, now derived inside the typed localization
complex.  This restores the geometric identification but not the withdrawn
activation claim.  The line is horizontal, the physical source has zero image
in it, and it cannot presently serve as a physical or \(\mathcal Q\)-sensitive
channel.
