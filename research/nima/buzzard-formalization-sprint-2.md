# Buzzard formalization sprint 2: disappearance, homology, and accessible ports

Owner: `marici.Buzzard`

## Objective

Formalize the distinction among:

1. access denial: a class exists but is absent from the admitted probe family;
2. readout loss: a class exists but a quotient/projection kills it;
3. constitutive collapse: an enlarged repair relation makes the class itself
   zero in a successor quotient.

Use the project and conventions established in sprint 1. Do not create another
Lean project root.

## Milestone A: two-step complexes and capability quotients

For modules over explicit ring assumptions, formalize

\[
R\xrightarrow r H\xrightarrow s S,
\qquad sr=0,
\]

and the capability quotient \(\ker s/\operatorname{range}r\). Prefer
Mathlib's canonical submodule/quotient APIs; do not choose complements.

Prove that a probe \(\ell:H\to P\) descends to the capability quotient iff it
vanishes on the repair image, and that the combined residue/probe family is
jointly faithful modulo repairs iff

\[
\ker s\cap\ker\ell=\operatorname{range}r.
\]

## Milestone B: three disappearance mechanisms

Define three separate structures and prove they are not interchangeable:

- **Access denial:** replace a jointly faithful probe family by a subfamily,
  leaving the capability object and original maps unchanged.
- **Readout loss:** apply a noninjective readout map to the capability object,
  leaving that object nonzero.
- **Constitutive collapse:** enlarge the repair submodule
  \(B\subseteq B'\subseteq\ker s\), inducing a canonical quotient map
  \(\ker s/B\to\ker s/B'\) that may kill a formerly nonzero class.

Give minimal finite countermodels showing every pair of mechanisms differs.
Prove the exact criterion for a class to die under the successor quotient:
its representative lies in \(B'\).

## Milestone C: functorial invalidation

Formalize the induced map between capability quotients under an enlargement
of legal relations. Prove composition for \(B\subseteq B'\subseteq B''\) and
identify its kernel. This is the mathematical core of dependency-aware
invalidation: successor relations recompute the derived quotient rather than
merely hiding a value.

## Milestone D: accessible operator image

For a representation of a source operation algebra or monoid on a protected
module, define the accessible endomorphism image. Establish only the generic
facts supported by available APIs:

- enlarging the constructor family enlarges the generated image;
- a proposed port outside that image is not generated by the declared
  constructors;
- commuting generators need not generate all endomorphisms;
- full reconstruction requires a separately stated faithfulness/spanning
  condition.

Do not assert laboratory constructibility from algebraic membership.

## Milestone E: finite canonical models

Formalize small exact examples reflecting the toric census:

- a two-dimensional capability space;
- one probe detects but does not reconstruct;
- two commuting coordinates separate a declared basis but do not encode
  arbitrary operator coherences;
- an enlarged repair subspace kills one formerly nonzero capability;
- removing a probe does not kill that capability.
