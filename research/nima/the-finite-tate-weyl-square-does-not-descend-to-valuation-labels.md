# The Finite Tate Weyl Square Does Not Descend to Valuation Labels

## The correct finite-place analogue

At the real place, additive differentiation and coordinate multiplication obey
the Fourier coherence square

\[
\mathcal F_\infty\partial_x
=
2\pi i\,M_x\mathcal F_\infty.
\]

At a finite place there is no ordinary additive derivative with this role.
The source-native analogue is the local Weyl pair. For \(h,\eta\in\mathbb Q_p\),
let

\[
(T_hf)(x)=f(x+h)
\]

and

\[
(M_\eta f)(x)=\psi_p(\eta x)f(x),
\]

where \(\psi_p\) is the chosen additive character. They satisfy

\[
T_hM_\eta
=
\psi_p(\eta h)M_\eta T_h.
\]

Local additive Fourier transform exchanges these translation and character
ports. This is the finite Tate derivative--coordinate square.

## Valuation projection erases the square

The current arithmetic carrier records valuation or prime-power labels.
Additive translation does not descend to that quotient.

At \(p=3\), the numbers \(1\) and \(2\) occupy the same valuation shell:

\[
v_3(1)=v_3(2)=0.
\]

Translate both by \(h=2\):

\[
v_3(1+2)=v_3(3)=1,
\qquad
v_3(2+2)=v_3(4)=0.
\]

Thus one valuation class has two different translated outputs. No map on
valuation labels can represent \(T_2\).

The character multiplier is also additive-coordinate sensitive. Radializing
or projecting to valuation before applying the Weyl pair destroys the phase
\(\psi_p(\eta h)\).

## Exact typing consequence

The missing finite-prime incidence cannot be constructed solely inside the
valuation/Fock module. It must first live on an additive local Tate carrier,
such as a Schwartz--Bruhat space with its translation and character actions.
Only after the Weyl coherence square is formed may one project to valuation
grades.

The required order is:

1. additive local source;
2. translation--character Weyl pair;
3. local Fourier transport;
4. radial or valuation projection;
5. primitive and square current readout.

Reversing steps two and four is undefined, not merely lossy.

## Relation to the missing incidence maps

The earlier valuation/Fock programme correctly constructed which prime-power
labels exist and how their weights behave. It could not construct the full
boundary incidence because the additive-character port had already been
forgotten.

This explains why a valuation-only candidate for the Schur maps \(B_s,C_s\)
keeps collapsing to scalar or rank-one endpoint data. The non-tautological
finite seam response belongs to the additive Tate lift before valuation
projection.

## Hostile gate

Any proposed finite-place analogue of the archimedean incidence must pass two
tests:

1. reproduce the Weyl phase \(\psi_p(\eta h)\) before projection;
2. exhibit an authorized comparison showing what survives under valuation
   readout.

The pair \(1,2\in\mathbb Q_3^\times\) translated by \(2\) is the minimum
non-descent falsifier. If a purported valuation-shell translation assigns one
output to their common input shell, it disagrees with at least one source
element.
