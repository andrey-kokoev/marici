# Pointed theta one-leg bridge is bounded below on Hilbert completion

Let `H_Ev` be the Evans history Hilbert carrier and let `H_theta` contain the fixed nonzero forcing vector `Phi`. On the Hilbert tensor product define

$$
\Delta_\Phi:H_{\rm Ev}\to H_{\rm Ev}\widehat\otimes H_\theta,
\qquad
\Delta_\Phi(v)=v\otimes\Phi.
$$

The Hilbert tensor norm gives the exact identity

$$
\|\Delta_\Phi(v)\|
=\|v\|\,\|\Phi\|.
$$

Therefore `Delta_Phi` is bounded with norm `||Phi||`, bounded below by the same positive constant, injective, and has closed range. Its inverse on its range has norm `||Phi||^-1`.

If `T_PB` is the constructed unitary pair-to-bordered crossing, then

$$
\|T_{\rm PB}\Delta_\Phi(v)\|
=\|v\|\,\|\Phi\|.
$$

Hence the completed bridge does not collapse Xi kernel states or parameter jets. Since the bridge is independent of the spectral parameter,

$$
\partial_z^j\Delta_\Phi(v(z))
=\Delta_\Phi(\partial_z^jv(z)),
$$

so root-chain multiplicities are preserved after Hilbert completion.

The induced arithmetic synthesis form

$$
f(v,w)
=\langle T_{\rm PB}\Delta_\Phi(v),
T_{\rm PB}\Delta_\Phi(w)\rangle
$$

is exactly

$$
f(v,w)=\|\Phi\|^2\langle v,w\rangle.
$$

Thus it is closed and positive on the Evans Hilbert carrier. Its domination by the complete Green form `b_s` is equivalent to an observability lower bound

$$
\|v\|^2\le C_s b_s[v,v]
$$

on the Green support. This lower bound, not bridge completion, is the remaining analytic gate.

Status: one-leg bridge completion, injectivity, closed range, and Xi multiplicity preservation closed; normalized endpoint-image comparison and Green observability remain open.
