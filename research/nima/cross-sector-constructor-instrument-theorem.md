# Cross-sector constructor--instrument theorem

Owner: `marici.Nima`

## 1. Typed vocabulary

Work in a symmetric monoidal category `C` with unit `I`, declared discard
maps, and a class of classical record objects.  A source category `S` and a
strong monoidal realization functor

\[
A:S\longrightarrow C
\]

define **source authority**: admissible maps are generated by the image of
`A`, the declared structural maps, and explicitly admitted limits or
completions.  Existence of a morphism in `C` is not source authority for it.

- A **substrate** is an object `X` whose future state can be changed.
- An **operation** is an authority-admissible morphism `f:X->Y`.
- A **readout port** is an admissible morphism `r:X->R` to a record object.
- An **instrument** is an admissible morphism
  `J:X->R tensor X'` whose record marginal is `r` and whose substrate
  marginal supplies the conditional successor.  In probabilistic models its
  branches are the usual subnormalized operations.
- A **task** is a declared idempotent or split-idempotent target `p:X->X`.
- A **constructor** is a reusable source resource `c:I->C` and an admissible
  realization `Gamma:C tensor X->C tensor R tensor X` that implements the
  task, returns `C` (exactly, catalytically, or with a declared arbitrarily
  improvable degradation bound), and remains valid under repetition.

These definitions use no dimensions, matrices, or Hilbert-space structure.

## 2. The authority-lifting diagram

Every sector is tested against the same sequence of increasingly strong
claims:

\[
S\xrightarrow{A}C_{\rm adm}
\longrightarrow \operatorname{Op}(X)
\longrightarrow \operatorname{Read}(X,R)
\longrightarrow \operatorname{Instr}(X;R,X')
\longrightarrow \operatorname{Constr}(p).
\]

The arrows mean, respectively: realize the source; obtain an available
operation; expose an admissible record; lift that record to a state-updating
instrument; and supply a reusable implementation of a declared task.  A
failure at one arrow cannot be repaired by proving a later object exists in
the ambient category.

## 3. Joint faithfulness

A family of ports `(r_i:X->R_i)` is **jointly faithful** when it is jointly
monic:

\[
(\forall i,\ r_i f=r_i g)\Longrightarrow f=g
\]

for every pair `f,g:Z->X`.  With a product, this is equivalent to the assembled
map `r:X->product_i R_i` being monic.  On a quotient or groupoid, the same
definition is applied after descent; equality means equality in the declared
physical quotient, not equality of presentations.

Joint faithfulness is a reconstruction theorem.  It says nothing about the
existence of a morphism preparing, selecting, or stabilizing the reconstructed
state.

## 4. Repeatability and stabilization

For a sharp record value `a`, let `p_a:X->X` denote the induced task when it
exists.  Exact repeatability requires

\[
p_a p_a=p_a,
\]

and record stability requires `r_a p_a=r_a` on its accepted subobject.  A
stabilized task is preferably typed as a split idempotent

\[
X\xrightarrow{q_a}X_a\xrightarrow{i_a}X,
\qquad q_ai_a=1,\quad p_a=i_aq_a.
\]

A physical constructor additionally requires source realization, resource
return/reset, sequential composability, and a declared error or degradation
law.  Idempotence of an abstract endomorphism alone is not constructibility.

## 5. Two separation theorems

### Theorem A: faithful readout does not imply constructibility

Take any nontrivial object `X` in a source theory whose admitted operations
form only a groupoid.  The identity port `id_X:X->X` is faithful.  No admitted
operation has a proper image, so no proper split-idempotent task can be
prepared or stabilized.  Therefore faithful readout does not imply a
constructor.  Flavor realizes the same obstruction nontrivially: `physical16`
probes are generically faithful while the declared RG operation is reversible
transport and the threshold map supplies no independent selector.

### Theorem B: algebraic controllability does not imply an instrument

An instrument requires a record object, a record-producing dilation, and a
successor map.  Closure of source operations under composition may generate
all desired substrate automorphisms while providing no controlled versions,
pointer preparation, measurement, or reset.  Hence algebraic controllability
does not imply an executable instrument.  Finite `D(S3)` realizes this
obstruction: its compiled Lie algebra has dimension 34 and is channel
sufficient, while controlled central powers and the record apparatus remain
outside the frozen source.

## 7. Hostile tests

1. **Fitted selector.** A projector chosen because its fixed locus matches
   data is an ambient idempotent, not a constructor, unless it lies in the
   source-generated operation closure.  Flavor fails this test.
2. **Undeclared reference.** Enlarging `X` to `X tensor Ref` may restore
   faithfulness, but changes the theory unless the reference preparation,
   stabilizer groupoid, interaction, and degradation are source-derived.
3. **Arbitrary dilation.** Stinespring/Lueders existence proves a formal
   instrument completion, not that the source selects its apparatus state,
   coupling, pointer, or reset.  `D(S3)` fails precisely here.
4. **Type-erased projection.** Cancellation or faithfulness after forgetting
   deck, support, occurrence, tail, or orientation grades is not physical.
   The smooth-Bondi/Laurent comparison fails before such a projection is
   legal; QED succeeds only after retaining the oriented Cut.

Each hostile test changes the verdict when its forbidden shortcut is
allowed, so the tests are discriminating rather than decorative.

## 8. Common higher structure and its limit

The four sectors do share one higher operation: **authority-indexed
realization followed by a lifting problem**.  Formally, each sector supplies
a realization functor `A_s:S_s->C_s`, and the question is whether a readout
port lifts through the forgetful maps

\[
\operatorname{Constr}_s\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Instr}_s\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Read}_s.
\]

This common structure explains the repeated failure pattern.  The stronger
claim that all sector constraints and readouts are projections of one common
physical morphism is not established: their coefficient objects, discard
laws, apparatus resources, and support conditions differ.  The theorem is a
common calculus of typed lifts, not universal coefficients or a universal
instrument.

## Conclusion

The slogan `Carrier + lens + readout` is too coarse.  The composable
architecture is

\[
\text{source authority}
\to\text{admissible operation}
\to\text{readout port}
\to\text{state-updating instrument}
\to\text{repeatable constructor}.
\]

Scattering reaches the instrument-density arrow; flavor stops between
faithful record and task; `D(S3)` stops between controllability and executable
record production; and the smooth Bondi comparison stops before the formal
Laurent substrate is source-admitted.  These are distinct, testable failures
of one typed lifting architecture.
