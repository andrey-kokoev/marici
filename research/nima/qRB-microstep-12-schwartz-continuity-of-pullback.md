# qRB microstep 12: Schwartz continuity of observer pullback

The correct analytic requirement for the enlarged Gaussian family is continuity on the test core, not a uniform Hilbert norm bound.

For an affine pullback

$$
(T_{a,b}f)(x)=f(ax+b),
\qquad a\ne0,
$$

`T_{a,b}` maps `Schwartz(R)` continuously to itself. Hence Gaussian observers and all finite derivative jets remain admissible after affine refinement.

The induced observer pullback is therefore continuous in the Schwartz topology, while its spectral-carrier operator norm may still be unbounded at endpoints.

This separates two requirements that were previously conflated:

$$
\text{test-core continuity}
\neq
\text{uniform Hilbert boundedness}.
$$

Status: affine test-core continuity closed. Non-affine source refinement and simultaneous regulator convergence remain open.
