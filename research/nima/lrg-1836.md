# LRG test of the 1836 construction

## Question

Can the LRG procedure of Villegas et al., *Laplacian renormalization group for heterogeneous networks*, derive the existing 1836 construction from the seed packets rather than receiving its projectors as inputs?

## Claim boundary

The source is the existing regular tetrahedral realization, the two cycles ABC and ADB, their twelve oriented triangle records, and the eighteen shared-edge wires. Local frames are computed from that source. The source geometry and its construction cost are retained as explicit inputs.

The first obligation is forward realization: incidence and cycle constraints must produce positive operators before any comparison with the desired projectors. Subsequent obligations are transport compatibility and preservation of the collective return. This is a 36-coordinate test; there is no physical-time or mass identification.

The paper's ordinary scalar graph Laplacian is tested separately from the complex, phase-conditioned constraint extension needed for the existing cyclic mode. Numerical specific-heat scans are scouting; the operator comparisons and support counts use exact arithmetic.

## Construction

1. Generate the twelve proper rotations from the two source cycles and form their centroid-edge triangles. Their boundary closes and their positive cone volumes sum to 8/3.
2. Construct the ordinary twelve-agent Laplacian from the eighteen shared edges. Its characteristic polynomial gives eigenvalues 0, 1, 3, 4, 5 with multiplicities 1, 3, 2, 3, 3.
3. Obtain its zero-mode projector by multiplying the exact factors `I - L/lambda` for the nonzero eigenvalues. The result is the uniform averaging operator; it was not supplied as an input. Transport it through the source frames to obtain the 36-coordinate alignment projector G.
4. For each local triangle frame X and cyclic coefficient shift C, construct the residual `D = X C inverse(X) - sigma I` and positive operator `D†D`. Here sigma is a declared cyclic sector. Extract its kernel projector P using its characteristic polynomial.
5. Verify that the local and alignment operators commute. Their common kernel has dimension one. The joint ground projector is N = GP = PG. Only then compare N with the previous assembled-mode projector.

For the oriented sector, the two nonzero local eigenvalues are the roots of:

```text
lambda² - (58/3) lambda + 45 = 0
```

The normalized LRG density limits are P/12, G/3, and N. Kernel-rank normalization recovers the amplitude-preserving maps P and G. The coarse Laplacian on the single retained zero mode is zero; N is the lifted coarse identity.

## Tested dispositions

### The peak-selected run passes through a tetrahedral four-record operator

For the oriented, unit-strength constraint model, iterating the paper's spectral cutoff and rescaling at the first detected specific-heat peak gives:

```text
36 coordinates -> 4 retained modes -> 1 retained mode
```

The first peak is approximately 0.945499, giving cutoff 1.057643. The four retained modes have unscaled eigenvalues 0, 1, 1, 1. Recomputing the heat peak after reduction and rescaling gives the final one-mode reduction.

The tetrahedral identification is exact and separately checked. For triangle `(F_abc, a, b)`, let c complete its face and d be the opposite source vertex. The embedding is:

```text
fine_ab = (3/4) q_d + (1/4) q_c
```

Applying the incidence Laplacian to the two labelled coordinate maps forces the coefficient ratio 3:1; constant-preserving normalization fixes the displayed coefficients. The induced operator on the four labelled records is:

```text
[ 3/4  -1/4  -1/4  -1/4 ]
[-1/4   3/4  -1/4  -1/4 ]
[-1/4  -1/4   3/4  -1/4 ]
[-1/4  -1/4  -1/4   3/4 ]
```

This is one quarter of the unit-edge tetrahedral K4 Laplacian, before the LRG resolution rescaling. Exact embedding, left-inverse, and spectral-projector comparisons establish the identification; no spatial body is inferred merely from rank four.

### The existing operators are recovered

Their nonzero-entry counts in the inherited fine-coordinate basis are:

```text
P: 108
G: 432
N: 1296
```

Implementing P, then G, then a separate dense N gives 1836 coefficient arrows.

### Ordinary LRG does not select the chiral mode

The ordinary triangle Laplacian has a constant zero mode and two chiral eigenmodes of eigenvalue 3. Selecting one chiral sector requires additional source specification. The unit sector and both conjugate sectors all give the same three support counts, so 1836 does not determine the sector.

### The proposed local-first hierarchy is not present at unit strength

The smaller oriented local excitation is about 2.706, while the alignment band extends to 5. Consequently the twelve local ground modes do not form an isolated lowest-energy band of the summed unit-strength operator.

Doubling the local constraint strength opens that separation; the exact cutoff 21/4 lies in the gap. This is a tested change of the model's relative strengths, not a scale ratio selected by the original packets. The report separately records approximate specific-heat peaks and cutoff ranks for the scalar graph and for the phase-conditioned operator at strengths one and two.

### The arrow count is not fixed by the collective return

Two explicit realizations preserve the same exact return N:

| Feedback realization | Arrow counts | Total |
|---|---|---:|
| Dense N | 108 + 432 + 1296 | 1836 |
| Identity feedback | 108 + 432 + 36 | 576 |
| Contraction to one coarse scalar, followed by its lift | 108 + 432 + 36 + 36 | 612 |

For the scalar factorization, the lift is obtained directly from a nonzero column of the spectrally derived N. No previous collective eigenvector is supplied to construct it.

All three directed networks are strongly connected. Their edge coefficients become positive under the common-mode phase gauge, so every arrow belongs to a positive closed walk. The identity-feedback alternative does not put every arrow on a length-three cycle; the factorized alternative introduces a fourth interface. Those architectural differences are recorded rather than identified away.

These are compiled coefficient-arrow counts. They do not include the arithmetic cost of constructing geometry, frames, or spectral coefficients, and they do not establish a minimum under a separately specified primitive-agent language.

## Disposition

The phase-conditioned LRG run recovers a four-record tetrahedral operator and then the collective identity. Independent source-constraint spectral projections also recover the existing projectors and therefore reproduce the 1836 expanded realization. These constraint projections are not the successive cutoffs of the peak-selected 36-to-4-to-1 run, which does not independently require that realization. A unique 1836 derivation still needs source rules selecting the cyclic sector, the scale hierarchy, and the separate dense-feedback architecture.
