## Fixed Hardy splitting

Retain the boundary decomposition

\[
L^2(\mathbb R)
=
\mathcal H_+\oplus\mathcal H_-
\]

with orthogonal projections \(P_+\) and \(P_-\). Let \(M_U\) denote
multiplication by a bounded unitary boundary symbol \(U\).

Relative to this splitting,

\[
M_U
=
\begin{pmatrix}
P_+M_UP_+ & P_+M_UP_-\\
P_-M_UP_+ & P_-M_UP_-
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Define the retarded Toeplitz block and its wrong-sector leakage block by

\[
T_U=P_+M_U\vert_{\mathcal H_+},
\qquad
H_U=P_-M_U\vert_{\mathcal H_+}.
\]

## The ninth quarter-turn

The analytic-factor question becomes an invariant-subspace question:

\[
H_U=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
M_U\mathcal H_+\subseteq\mathcal H_+
\]

for bounded boundary multipliers. Under the Hardy identification, this is the
condition that \(U\) be the boundary value of a bounded analytic multiplier in
the selected half-plane.

Thus the arrow of influence is encoded as absence of a cross-sector Hankel
block. This is a sharper statement than two-sided correlation positivity.

## Exact one-pole hostile

Return to

\[
S_a(z)
=
\frac{z-ia}{z+ia},
\qquad
a>0.
\]

It is analytic and inner in the upper half-plane. Therefore

\[
H_{S_a}=0.
\]

Nevertheless \(S_a\) has a zero at \(z=ia\). Multiplication by \(S_a\) is an
isometry of \(\mathcal H_+\), but it is not onto. Its defect space is

\[
\mathcal K_{S_a}
=
\mathcal H_+\ominus S_a\mathcal H_+,
\]

and

\[
\dim\mathcal K_{S_a}=1.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\ker T_{S_a}=0,
\qquad
\dim\operatorname{coker}T_{S_a}=1,
\qquad
\operatorname{ind}T_{S_a}=-1.
\]

The zero has not violated causality. It has become one stored defect mode.

For a finite Blaschke product \(B\) of degree \(d\),

\[
H_B=0,
\qquad
\dim\mathcal K_B=d,
\qquad
\operatorname{ind}T_B=-d.
\]

Hence causal analyticity, boundary unitarity, and positive delay can coexist
with arbitrarily many half-plane zeros.

## Physical interpretation

The Hardy factor supplies an arrow of influence because its impulse response
is one-sided. The Toeplitz defect records internal memory that the output
subspace does not immediately reproduce. A zero of the inner factor is a
memory mode, not an acausal puncture.

This gives a precise correction to the emerging time conjecture:

- time ordering supplies a direction for processes;
- causality forbids wrong-direction leakage;
- memory measures the failure of causal transport to be onto;
- zeros may inhabit that memory without violating either time ordering or
  causality.

Therefore causality alone cannot explain RH.

## What stronger law is missing

The zero-excluding property is minimum phase or an appropriate outer/cyclic
condition, not causality itself. In Hardy language, the source must control the
defect space rather than only the leakage block.

The required source question is:

> Which labelled theta/Tate operation forces the relevant causal factor to
> have no unauthorized inner defect while preserving its required seam phase
> and boundary currents?

This law cannot be inferred from the scalar functional equation, delay
positivity, or Hardy invariance. Hostile Blaschke multiplication preserves all
three while adding defect modes.
