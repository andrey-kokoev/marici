# Marici through a constructor-principle lens

## Status

Cross-sector research hypothesis and organizational interpretation.  This is
not an established consequence of constructor theory, nor a proof that the
Marici Carrier is a constructor-theoretic substrate.

## Hypothesis

The shared Marici core may be better typed as a calculus of possible and
impossible **supported transformations** than as a universal coefficient
object:

\[
\boxed{
\text{shared substrate/task grammar}
+
\text{resource-sensitive composition and obstruction calculus}.
}
\]

Physical sectors then realize different constructors, coefficient media, and
readouts over that common task grammar.

## Paired-readout refinement

Three independently sourced sectors now sharpen “coefficient medium plus
readout” to a mixed-variance pairing:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal C_S\otimes\mathcal B_S
\longrightarrow
\mathcal R_S,
\qquad
\langle T_{mathcal C}c,T_{mathcal B}\gamma\rangle
=
\langle c,\gamma\rangle.
}
\]

Here \(S\) is the sector, \(\mathcal C_S\) is a coefficient, detector, or
de Rham object, \(\mathcal B_S\) is a Betti, cycle, memory, or response
object, and \(\mathcal R_S\) is the physical record.  The factors are
sector-specific; the shared candidate is their variance-sensitive
composition law.

The exact positive controls are:

1. **Cosmology:** the rank-32 Kummer coefficient local system paired with the
   regular Betti chamber orbit.
2. **Five-point strings:** the reflected Parke--Taylor class and oriented
   twisted chamber each acquire sign \(-1\), so their transported period
   pairing is invariant.
3. **Radiative gravity:** the supertranslation detector \(f\) pairs with
   the memory field through \(\int f\mathcal O N\); simultaneous transport
   is invariant, while holding the detector fixed fails.

This refinement is stronger than a sequential “lens then readout” metaphor.
The physical scalar generally belongs to neither factor separately.  It is
created by their sourced pairing.

For a noninvertible correspondence \(q:G\to H\), the factors have opposite
variance:

\[
q^*:\mathcal C_H\to\mathcal C_G,
\qquad
q_*:\mathcal B_G\to\mathcal B_H,
\]

with

\[
\langle q^*c,\gamma\rangle_G
=
\langle c,q_*\gamma\rangle_H.
\]

Finite fiber-sum transfer and Beck--Chevalley supply an algebraic
correspondence calculus, but physical \(q_*\) still requires a
source-derived supported chain/Gysin map.  Automorphism covariance therefore
does not authorize specialization or quotient pushforward.

## Existing evidence reinterpreted

Several recurring failures become resource-relative impossibility statements:

1. Generic global data does not produce supported Cartier/Gysin data without
   the missing Beck--Chevalley coherence.
2. A nonzero associated grade does not produce a physical class without a
   source-derived relative-support map.
3. A sparse flavor-chart invariant does not produce a weak-basis invariant;
   the physical quotient forgets more than the chart groupoid.
4. A coefficient class does not produce a nonzero period without a physical
   chain that meets its support.
5. Additive Carrier structure does not produce a full equivariant unital
   multiplication while retaining the admitted automorphisms.

These are not absolute impossibility theorems.  Each is conditional on the
declared substrates, task type, and retained resources.

## Arithmetic readout refinement

The radiative-memory hostile test rules out a universal abelianization of
physical readouts.  A covariant physical multiplet may retain a nonabelian
commutator.  Even after invariant scalarization, the correct construction is
generally an orbit quotient, not the abelianization of the acting group:

\[
\text{nonabelian Carrier process}
\longrightarrow
\text{covariant sector readout }V
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Spec}A[V]^G
\longrightarrow
\text{commutative invariant-record algebra}.
\]

For the standard \(D_3\) radiative-memory plane,

\[
\mathbb Q[a,b]^{D_3}
=
\mathbb Q[a^2-ab+b^2,ab(a-b)].
\]

This commutative algebra retains nonabelian orbit information.  It is not
obtained by replacing \(D_3\) with \(D_3^{\rm ab}\).

Scattering, cosmology, five-point string periods, and radiative gravity
provide positive invariant-pairing examples at their established scopes.
The previously displayed all-arity string character belongs to a
Parke--Taylor/fixed-trivialization comparison; it must not be called the
fully transported physical-period character without separately transporting
the twisted cycle.  Flavor shows why no physical quotient can be moved
backward to every coefficient lens.

The refined conjectural lesson is that arithmetic may organize stable
composable invariant records, while the transformation groupoid and its
covariant physical multiplets remain nonabelian.  Grothendieck's kernel
quotient \(G/K_{\rm phys}\) and the invariant quotient
\(\operatorname{Spec}A[V]^G\) answer different questions and must both be
retained.

## Research protocol

For every proposed cross-sector operation, declare:

1. the substrate and source-labelled task;
2. both paired objects and their input/output variances;
3. the pairing, constructor, or comparison map;
4. the support, framing, chain, coherence, and monodromy resources;
5. repeatability and composition laws;
6. the forgetful functor under which the result becomes impossible or
   contractible.

## Sharp falsifiers

The hypothesis fails or requires revision if any of the following occurs:

1. A physical sector canonically realizes a transformation that the frozen
   resource calculus declares impossible, without supplying the missing
   support, chain, framing, or coherence datum.
2. Two sectors require incompatible composition laws on the same typed
   Carrier task—not merely different coefficient objects.
3. A proposed physical readout cannot be represented as a typed supported
   pairing/transformation even after all source-declared resources are
   retained.
4. The arithmetic readout shadow detects a commutator that every admitted
   physical selection map was claimed to kill.

Conversely, the hypothesis receives a strong update when an obstruction is
removed by adding exactly the independently derived resource it predicted,
with no fitted carrier enlargement.

## Research direction: Bell and Tsirelson as readout audits

Bell nonlocality should first be treated as a cross-sector audit of the
physical readout, not as evidence for a new Carrier divisor or a new sector.
For a bipartite source packet, ask whether the final record factors through a
local hidden variable:

\[
P(a,b\mid x,y)
\stackrel{?}{=}
\int d\lambda\,\rho(\lambda)
P(a\mid x,\lambda)P(b\mid y,\lambda).
\]

The constructor-principle hypothesis predicts that a physical quantum record
need not factor this way: measurement context and the joint supported pairing
may remain present through relative totalization.  It must nevertheless obey
the sector's declared marginalization and no-signalling maps.

The first bounded packet should test, in order:

1. a source-defined bipartite preparation and two settings per wing;
2. a normalized joint readout and functorial marginals;
3. commuting or otherwise explicitly typed spacelike-local operations;
4. CHSH violation, \(|S|>2\), after complete relative totalization;
5. derivation of the quantum bound, \(|S|\leq2\sqrt2\), from the admitted
   positivity, norm, composition, and support structure.

The evidential hierarchy is strict.  Reproducing a Bell violation by fitting
an arbitrary contextual probability table is weak accommodation.  Deriving
no-signalling and the Tsirelson bound from the same source-defined calculus is
the discriminating result.  A superquantum value \(|S|>2\sqrt2\) with all
declared Marici rules satisfied would falsify the claim that the present
rules characterize quantum possibility structure; inability to exceed two
would show that the chosen packet has collapsed to a classical readout.

Scattering is the natural first host because it already supplies separated
external labels, factorization/locality structure, and physical pairing
maps.  The audit remains cross-sector until another sector independently
realizes the same typed Bell packet.

## Scope boundary

This interpretation does not identify human perception with physical
selection, assert that all laws are constructor-theoretic, or derive a
universal constructor.  The evidence now supports a common calculus in which
sector-specific lens/readout factors are admitted together as a paired
object.  The stronger claim that one parent object *generates* those paired
factors remains a separate parent-object conjecture.

Evidence base: the cross-sector synthesis of Ledger Entry 1213 and the
subsequent scattering, flavor, cosmology, and string physical-readout audits.
