# Minimality under composition: the essential-image gate

## Decisive result

For finite-dimensional Gram constructors, honest serial and tensor composition
preserve minimality. Apparent failures come from declaring an ambient codomain
larger than the essential image of a lens.

Let \(J:V\to H\) be minimal, so

\[
\operatorname{span}J(V)=H.
\]

For a lens \(L:H\to K\), the composite \(LJ\) is minimal in the declared
carrier \(K\) exactly when

\[
\operatorname{rank}(LJ)=\dim K.
\]

Because \(J\) is onto its generated carrier, this is equivalent to \(L\)
being onto \(K\). Thus:

\[
\boxed{\text{minimality + faithful lens does not imply minimality}}
\]

but

\[
\boxed{\text{minimality + essentially surjective lens does.}}
\]

The intrinsic completed carrier is therefore not the ambient \(K\), but the
essential image

\[
K_{\rm ess}=\operatorname{span}(LJ(V))=\operatorname{im}L.
\]

## Preservation laws

If \(J_1:V_1\to H_1\) and \(J_2:V_2\to H_2\) are minimal, then
\(J_1\otimes J_2\) is minimal in \(H_1\otimes H_2\). If additionally
\(L:H_1\otimes H_2\to K\) is onto, then
\(L(J_1\otimes J_2)\) is minimal in \(K\).

Likewise, a serial chain of minimal constructors and onto lenses remains
minimal. Consequently, a dark direction appearing after composition
identifies one of three concrete defects:

1. an ambient codomain was substituted for the essential image;
2. a lens claimed as completion is not onto the declared carrier;
3. transport leaves the essential image and therefore lacks closure.

## Smallest counterexample to faithfulness

Take

\[
J=I_2:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2,\qquad
L=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}:
\mathbb R^2\hookrightarrow\mathbb R^3.
\]

Both \(J\) and \(L\) are injective, and \(L\) is perfectly faithful. Yet
\(LJ\) has rank two in a declared three-dimensional carrier. The projector
\(Z=|e_3\rangle\langle e_3|\) is dark:

\[
(LJ)^*Z(LJ)=0.
\]

A rotation mixing \(e_1\) and \(e_3\) exposes it. Hence ambient transport is
not closed on \(K_{\rm ess}\). The correct repair is either to type the
carrier as \(\operatorname{im}L\), or to supply source authority for the
extra direction and its coupling.

## Finite falsifier

Given a proposed composition \(V\xrightarrow{J}H\xrightarrow{L}K\):

1. compute \(r=\operatorname{rank}(LJ)\);
2. compare \(r\) with the declared \(\dim K\);
3. if \(r<\dim K\), construct a nonzero projector \(Z\) onto
   \(\operatorname{im}(LJ)^\perp\);
4. test whether every admitted transport preserves
   \(\operatorname{im}(LJ)\).

The rank deficit disproves minimality. A nonzero off-diagonal block
\((I-P)UP\), where \(P\) projects onto the essential image, disproves
transport closure.

## Boundary

This theorem does not manufacture source authority or positivity. It does
remove a common false move:

> Information-preserving embeddings may be faithful while still creating
> unauthorized ambient constructor directions.

The durable compositional requirement is a source-derived minimal
constructor, an essentially surjective completed lens, and transport closed
on the resulting essential image.
