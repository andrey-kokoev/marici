## Status

Speculative future question.  This is not evidence for Everettian quantum
mechanics and is not a claim that Marici has derived quantum measurement.

## Sharp question

Can two mutually incompatible source-selected readouts be constructed as
simultaneous, internally coherent restrictions of one parent Marici object,
with neither readout operation deleting or irreversibly modifying the parent?

## Discriminating outcomes

1. **Non-destructive coexistence.** Both readouts exist simultaneously as
   compatible restrictions of one parent object.  This supports an
   Everett-compatible architecture.
2. **Irreversible selection.** Applying one readout changes the parent so
   that the alternative is no longer available.  This is structurally closer
   to collapse.
3. **Presentation multiplicity only.** The two readouts are gauge-equivalent
   descriptions of one physical record.  Neither Everett nor collapse is
   implicated.
4. **Missing physical typing.** The parent restrictions exist algebraically
   but no source-derived observer, record algebra, Born weighting, or
   decoherence map selects them.  The measurement interpretation remains
   unavailable.

- a derivation of weights rather than fitted probabilities;
