# Dagger covariance closes the DqR leakage cube

Let the chart leakage for a chart arrow be

$$
A_X^q=P_Xq(I-P_X).
$$

Assume the centered cutoff is dagger invariant:

$$
DP_XD^{-1}=P_X.
$$

Dagger reverses the chart arrow:

$$
DqD^{-1}=q^\dagger.
$$

Conjugating the leakage gives

$$
DA_X^qD^{-1}
=
P_Xq^\dagger(I-P_X)
=
A_X^{q^\dagger}.
$$

Thus dagger sends forward chart leakage to the leakage cell on the reversed chart edge. This is the required modification on the 3-face

$$
DqR.
$$

For nested centered cutoffs, dagger also preserves the shell decomposition:

$$
DA_X^qD^{-1}
=
P_Xq^\dagger(P_Y-P_X)
+
P_Xq^\dagger(I-P_Y).
$$

The source reflection preserves centered apertures, so the cutoff-invariance premise holds on the declared projective graph completion. The chart-dagger relation supplies the second premise. The cube therefore closes by the dagger mate of the original leakage modification.
