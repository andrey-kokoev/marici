# Many-many two-output linking metric status

Let `J_link` be the bounded skew operator representing the ordered linking form and set

$$
K_{\rm link}=-\frac12J_{\rm link}.
$$

On the forward/reciprocal output carrier

$$
Y_{\rm link}=H_{\rm orb}^{\rm fwd}\oplus H_{\rm orb}^{\rm recip},
$$

$$
G_{\rm link}
=\begin{pmatrix}0&K_{\rm link}\\-K_{\rm link}&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

$$
S^*G_{\rm link}S=-G_{\rm link}.
$$

Under v17 faithful-joint-graph semantics, no comparison with an old conservative linking metric is required. The two source-derived metric observations are retained together.
