A new packet must state what remains after reusing the strongest applicable predecessor. A repository-wide absence claim is not licensed by a local failed adapter.

**Control:** Report a closed claim, its fixed assumptions, the alternatives eliminated, and the precise independent residual. ## Corrective priority for driving closure

1. **Restore trustworthy evidence first.** Repair or quarantine the two defective checker paths and put superseding notices at stale claim entry points. Do not infer that the later formal classification or shared-recoil result is invalid merely because an earlier checker is defective.
2. **Resume from actual source operations, not a newly simplified substitute.** Reuse the existing native history exchange, triangle successor, and six-port pulse realization at their distinct types. Require an explicit comparison when moving between them.
3. **Freeze the explanatory target before extending the model.** Write what result would settle it positively, negatively or by underdetermination, and which supplied assumptions are accepted for that task. A later physical-selection question must not retroactively invalidate a completed conditional theorem.
4. **Require a decision-changing consequence from the next task.** It should distinguish live interpretations of the same admitted source, construct their missing comparison, or establish a genuine obstruction. If it only re-expresses a supplied law and does not discharge an identified obligation, it is not on the closure path.
5. **Use the existing stopping rules.** An absent physical bridge is a typed blocked branch after relevant prior interfaces have been tested. New continuation requires new source data, an explicitly proposed law, or a different discriminating consequence—not another generic covariance or normalization calculation.

These changes use existing research protocols. They do not require another compiler, new governance framework, or further infrastructure before addressing the science. The primary failure is not an insufficient number of calculations. It is unreliable preservation of the source and the settled explanatory state across calculations, compounded by localized checker defects and weak consolidation.
