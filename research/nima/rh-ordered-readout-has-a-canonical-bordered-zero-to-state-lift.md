## From coefficient to bordered operator

Let (V) have dimension three. Suppose the source construction provides:

- a relative transporter (M:V\to V);
- a source state (x\in V);
- an endpoint observer (y\in V^*).

The ordered readout is

\[
f=y(Mx).
\]

Put (u=Mx), and adjoin one scalar evaluation wall. Define

\[
D_{M,x,y}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
I_V&u\\
y&0
\end{pmatrix}
:
V\oplus\mathbf 1
\longrightarrow
V\oplus\mathbf 1.
\]

The Schur-complement identity gives

\[
\det D_{M,x,y}=-y(u)=-f.
\]

Thus the ordered coefficient becomes a maximal exterior coordinate only
after the evaluation wall is retained. This is the precise rank-three plus
one construction suggested by the earlier missing-observer intuition.

## Exact zero-to-state bridge

If (f=0), then

\[
D_{M,x,y}
\binom{-u}{1}=0.
\]

Conversely, if (D_{M,x,y}) has a nonzero kernel, its first block equation
forces (v=-ut). The second equation then gives

\[
-t,f=0.
\]

When (u\ne0), a nonzero kernel therefore exists exactly when (f=0). At a
simple rank drop, the canonical kernel line is generated by

\[
(-Mx,1).
\]

This turns a scalar cancellation into an unpaired state of a source-forward
bordered system. It does not infer a puncture from bundle topology and does
not divide by the scalar section.

## Categorical genesis

The construction uses only canonical categorical operations:

1. apply the transporter to the source state;
2. form a biproduct with the scalar object;
3. insert the transported state as a coevaluation column;
4. insert the observer as an evaluation row;
5. take the top exterior power only after the complete bordered map exists.

The additional scalar wall is not another observer equivalent to (x) or
(y). It is the result type of their evaluation pairing. Its role is to keep
the relationship as an operative port rather than compressing it immediately
to a number.

In a typed partial category, the constructor has signature

```text
bordered_evaluation:
  (transporter, source_state, endpoint_observer, evaluation_wall)
  -> bordered_operator
```

The determinant readout is downstream of this constructor.

## Relation to the A2 minor algebra

For the relative transporter (M(a,b,c)), the vector (u=Mx) is assembled
from the generalized-minor coordinates and the source port. The bordered
determinant therefore evaluates the source-selected linear functional on the
(A_2) minor algebra.

The (A_2) exchange law guarantees presentation coherence of (u). The
bordered lift remembers which observer evaluates it. Neither property forces
the resulting determinant to be nonzero.

## Canonical acyclicity target

The bordered map defines a two-term complex

\[
0
\longrightarrow
V\oplus\mathbf 1
\xrightarrow{D_{M,x,y}}
V\oplus\mathbf 1
\longrightarrow
0.
\]

Its cohomology vanishes exactly when (f\ne0). An RH proof in this language
would construct the theta/Tate version of this complex and a canonical
contracting homotopy on each open half-plane.

This recovers the puncture-as-cohomology programme with the previously
missing bridge supplied: the complex is derived forward from the transporter
and its ordered ports, not backward from the zero set.

## What this does not prove

The finite construction supplies equivalence between scalar zero and bordered
cohomology. It supplies no contraction. Hostile coherent transporters can
still make (y(Mx)=0).

The remaining source gates are:

1. construct (M(s)), (x(s)), and (y(s)) from labelled theta/Tate data;
2. prove their bordered operator is defined on one common rigged domain;
3. identify its determinant with the completed theta section up to a
   nowhere-vanishing authorized factor;
4. derive an open-sector contraction that survives restricted-product
   completion.

The first three establish the correct zero-to-state bridge. The fourth is the
RH-bearing theorem.

## Finite falsifier

For any proposed contraction, choose a coherent transporter and ordered ports
with (y(Mx)=0). The explicit vector ((-Mx,1)) must either be removed by a
source-authorized boundary condition or remain as nonzero cohomology. A
claimed homotopy that maps it to zero without typing that boundary action is
invalid.
