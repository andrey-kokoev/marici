# The unequal-energy path has unit soft-normal compatibility

Date: 2026-08-23

The source-normalized path

\[
(X_1,X_2,X_3)=(1,\eta,1)
\]

joins the equal-energy cyclic point to the second soft conductor:

\[
(u,v)=(2+\eta,\eta),
\qquad
\eta=1\mapsto(3,1),
\qquad
\eta=0\mapsto(2,0).
\]

Along this path the unequal-energy cotangent is exactly the soft-normal
coordinate:

\[
d(X_2-X_3)=d\eta.
\]

The resolved node equation at the soft center, up to its frozen nonzero source
unit, is

\[
t=\eta(B-1).
\]

Hence on the generic conductor locus \(B\ne1\),

\[
\frac{\partial_\eta t}{B-1}=1.
\]

Thus the source path reaches the soft center in exactly the normal direction
whose Gysin multiplicity is one.  This establishes the geometric compatibility
required for a possible composition

\[
\boxed{\text{candidate:}\quad
d(X_2-X_3)
\longmapsto
[g-g^{-1}]
\xrightarrow{\operatorname{Gys}_{X_2=0}}
\tau_{\rm soft}
}
\]

but it does not yet establish the middle class's Gauss--Manin transport from
\(\eta=1\) to \(\eta=0\).

This is not a generic physical activation.  Away from the conductor, ordinary
source sewing still factors through augmentation and annihilates the Tate
line.  The nonzero map appears only after applying the independently declared
supported functor at \(X_2=0\).

A tangential-path negative control keeps \(X_2=0\) while varying \(X_1\).
Its soft-normal derivative is zero, so it does not activate the class.  The
positive result therefore comes from the source normal direction, not merely
from arriving at the same endpoint.

## Remaining gate

RS-3 now has the two endpoints and their common source normal coordinate:

\[
\text{unequal-energy cotangent at }\eta=1
\dashrightarrow
\text{Tate coefficient transport along }\eta
\dashrightarrow
\text{soft Gysin line at }\eta=0.
\]

The dashed arrows cannot be filled by ordinary one-parameter transport:
away from \(\eta=1\), one arm breaks the cyclic isotropy that defines the
Tate quotient.  The typed replacement is the cyclic saturation of all three
softening arms.  Its arm module remains the regular \(C_3\)-module, and its
three endpoint Gysin maps form an equivariant supported readout.  The generic
scalar channel remains zero, and no new carrier divisor is introduced.
