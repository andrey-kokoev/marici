## Conceptual steps: Newton → QM → Ours

| Newtonian physics | Quantum mechanics | Our model |
|---|---|---|
| **Time** is absolute, uniform parameter | **Time** remains external parameter; not an operator (Pauli theorem) | **θ** is development parameter; **T = −ψ** is affine clock reading at unit rate |
| **Trajectory** q(t), p(t) is deterministic | **Amplitude** exp(iS/ℏ); superposition of paths | **Retained history** of filler compositions; paths distinguished by their typed record |
| **Action** S = ∫(p dq − H dt) generates canonical flow | **Action/phase link:** exp(iS/ℏ) is fundamental; S quantized: ∮p dq = nℏ | **Connection integral** ∫α = Δφ − ΔF/κ is amplitude exponent; 4πκ is action quantum from spinor period |
| **Phase space** (q,p) is continuous manifold | **Hilbert space** of states; observables as operators | **Packages** with pointed fillers; pullback of coordinate projections |
| **Poisson bracket** { , } gives dynamics | **Commutator** [ , ]/iℏ; Heisenberg equation | **Cocycle/Jacobi identity** governs phase lift associativity |
| **Observable** f(q,p) is a function | **Operator** acting on Hilbert space; Born rule | **Pullback** of fst or snd along a filler; no Born rule yet |
| **∮p dq** is continuous (any area) | **∮p dq = nℏ** (Bohr−Sommerfeld); discrete action | **∮α = −area/κ**; 4π period forces action quantization |
| **No spin**; rotation period 2π | **Spinor** 4π period; −1 under 2π | **4π minimal return**; twist² = id; (LR)² gives −1, (LR)⁴ gives +1 |
| **Classical probability** = ignorance | **Born rule** | **Not yet present**; retained history is algebraic, not probabilistic |

The third column **includes** the second (κ → ℏ, 4π → spinor, connection → action one-form) and adds the discrete finite-carrier skeleton that the continuous model cannot provide alone. The missing piece—Born rule, measurement, probability—is where the model is silent, just as QM was before Born's 1926 paper.
