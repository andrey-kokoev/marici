# The Tate modulus cannot supply the reciprocal odd phase

Suppose the source sewing arrow has polar decomposition

\[
S_a=U_a|S_a|.
\]

Event 10274 identifies the canonical candidate

\[
|S_a|=s_\infty(a)
=
\pi^{a-\frac12}
\frac{\Gamma((1-a)/2)}{\Gamma(a/2)}.
\]

This is a positive real modulus. It controls endpoint scale and the relative
metric connection, but it cannot determine the ordered reciprocal phase
\(U_a\).

The distinction is exact:

\[
\log S_a
=
\log |S_a|+\log U_a
\]

only in a compatible scalar line frame. The first term is real Hermitian
modular transport; the second is skew/unitary transport. Additive energies
and determinant magnitudes see the former. The odd seam character requires
the latter.

## Central gamma slope

The modular connection is nontrivial even at the central seam. Since

\[
\frac{d}{da}\log s_\infty(a)
=
\log\pi
-\frac12\psi((1-a)/2)
-\frac12\psi(a/2),
\]

one obtains

\[
\left.
\frac{d}{da}\log s_\infty(a)
\right|_{a=1/2}
=
\log\pi-\psi(1/4).
\]

Using

\[
\psi(1/4)
=
-\gamma-\frac{\pi}{2}-3\log2,
\]

the exact slope is

\[
\log\pi+\gamma+\frac{\pi}{2}+3\log2.
\]

This real slope splits the primitive and square metric connections by equal
and opposite halves. It is not an imaginary orientation coordinate.

## Reciprocal laws

Sewing involution requires

\[
S_{1-a}S_a=I.
\]

At the polar level this supplies two separate conditions:

\[
s_\infty(1-a)s_\infty(a)=1,
\]

and, after the required transport/conjugation convention,

\[
U_{1-a}U_a=I.
\]

The first is already satisfied by the Tate ratio. It gives no proof of the
second and no sign choice for the odd port.

## Constructor consequence

The archimedean sewing audit must retain two outputs:

1. the positive modular line \(s_\infty\), controlling amplitude and endpoint
   topology;
2. the unitary ordered line \(U_a\), controlling reciprocal orientation.

A scalar gamma-factor magnitude can contribute to the real finite defect
\(\Delta(a)\), but it cannot close the causal-history/Wronskian odd
identification. Conversely, a unitary phase can orient the Adams edge while
leaving the amplitude blow-up unresolved.

The smallest hostile has the correct \(s_\infty\) and replaces \(U_a\) by
\(U_a^{-1}\). Every pole coefficient, metric modulus, determinant magnitude,
and real finite correction is unchanged, while reciprocal orientation
reverses.

Thus the next source theorem must extract the full Tate local coefficient,
not merely its absolute gamma ratio, and prove that its phase agrees with the
causal-history odd port.
