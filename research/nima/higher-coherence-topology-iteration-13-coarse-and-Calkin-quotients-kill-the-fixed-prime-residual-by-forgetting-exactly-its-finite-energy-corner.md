# Higher-coherence topology iteration 13: coarse and Calkin quotients kill the fixed-prime residual by forgetting exactly its finite-energy corner

## Candidate topology

Represent the completed system on a graph Hilbert carrier and pass from bounded
operators to a coarse or essential quotient, schematically

\[
\mathcal Q(H)=B(H)/\mathcal K(H),
\]

or to a Roe/localization algebra modulo locally compact operators. Residuals
supported in bounded regions or finite-dimensional boundary corners then become
zero in the quotient.

This topology is designed precisely to ignore compact local defects while
retaining propagation and essential behavior at infinity.

## Fixed-prime Haar corner

At one prime, the terminal residual is represented by the rank-one operator

\[
R_p(z)
=
\bigl(1-p^{-2\operatorname{Re}z}\bigr)
|b_z\rangle\langle b_z|.
\]

It is compact. Therefore

\[
[R_p(z)]_{\mathcal Q(H)}=0
\]

for every `z`, independently of the critical line.

Thus coarse/Calkin topology does absorb the residual—but for the wrong reason.
It discards the entire finite-energy prime corner, including

\[
|b_z\rangle\langle b_z|.
\]

The quotient can no longer use `E_p(b_z)>0` to infer confinement.

## Infinite prime diagonal

One might retain all primes simultaneously. A diagonal family of prime blocks
has a nonzero essential class only if its block norms fail to tend to zero. The
existing Euler/half-density loading is designed to be summable or compact in
several completed directions. In those directions the entire prime residual
family remains compact and vanishes in the quotient.

If the weights are changed so the block norms stay bounded below, the family
may survive essentially, but prime summability and the declared arithmetic
completion are lost. This is not the same system.

## Localization-algebra refinement

A localization algebra can retain a path whose propagation tends to zero. Its
evaluation-at-zero map records the original local operator, while the kernel
records the higher homotopy. Sending the residual into that kernel does not
make its evaluation vanish; it packages the finite defect as a boundary or
index class.

To recover confinement one would need injectivity of the relevant evaluation
or assembly map on the positive prime corner. But the fixed rank-one operator
is already zero in coarse `K`-theory unless additional labelled coefficient
data is retained.

## Boundary typing warning

The Clark/Haar rows are initially rigged-dual distributions. They acquire
Calkin classes only after representation as bounded endomorphisms on a declared
graph Hilbert rung. A finite-dimensional graph trace always has zero Calkin
class and cannot provide an essential observation margin.

## Verdict for topology 13

Coarse, Roe, localization-algebra, and Calkin topologies can erase the
fixed-prime residual. They simultaneously erase the finite positive state
energy that makes the residual RH-bearing. They are useful for essential bulk
stability, not placewise confinement.

The next nonredundant topology to test is a noncommutative `L^p` or semifinite
von Neumann topology, where finite prime corners retain a faithful trace while
infinite-volume bulk can still be quotiented or renormalized.
