# The prime-shell residual is the localized form of the known odd autocorrelation obstruction

## Question

Is the shell autocorrelation density a new obstruction, or the localized form
of an existing RH-bearing forcing object?

## Claim boundary

It is the localized form of the previously derived odd source-autocorrelation
obstruction. This identifies the correct nonlocal source carrier for candidate
one: the ordered-pair arithmetic module and its polarized correlation
synthesis. It does not construct the theta-specific current that cancels the
obstruction.

## Global forcing object

For a one-sided source \(f\), define

\[
 K_f(t)=\int_0^\infty f(x)f(x+t)\,dx.
\]

The exact doubled forcing obstruction is

\[
 C_f(z)=2\int_0^\infty K_f(t)\sinh(zt)\,dt.
\]

The scalar Xi section constrains the even transform of \(K_f\), while the
Green forcing uses this odd ordered transform. One does not determine the
other.

## Shell localization

For a prime shell \([a,b]\), define

\[
 \rho_{a,b}(t)=\int_a^b\Phi(x)\Phi(x+t)\,dx.
\]

Then

\[
 K_\Phi(t)
 =\rho_{0,a}(t)+\rho_{a,b}(t)+\rho_{b,\infty}(t)
\]

for any partition of the positive half-line. The ordinary shell residual is

\[
 I_{a,b}^{(0)}(z)
 =-\int_0^\infty e^{-zt}\rho_{a,b}(t)\,dt.
\]

Thus consecutive-prime shell tests resolve the global autocorrelation
obstruction into cutoff-natural localized densities.

\[

## Existing hostile

A positive two-shell source can have a zero of its even scalar section while
its odd autocorrelation transform remains nonzero. Therefore any proposed law
based only on positivity, reciprocal symmetry, rapid decay, or endpoint flux is
universal enough to admit a known counterexample.

A valid cancellation law must use labelled theta structure and fail on this
two-shell hostile before examining its zero.
