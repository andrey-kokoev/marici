## Exact inputs needed before a closed-domain computation

1. A source preparation map or relation from retained geometric histories to
   the logical pair state, including proton spin extension and electron lift.
2. A relative-position/contact record; it cannot be inferred from (A,E) or
   equated with arbitrary matching endpoint letters.
3. The action of every registered request on these fields and the mediator,
   with the appropriate retained metric and coefficient embedding.
4. The actual restriction: preservation of a subspace, a fixed expectation,
   an operator, or a transported reference. A projector in a Hamiltonian is
   not by itself a Boolean rule rejecting every vector outside its image.

With those supplied, the existing quotient descent test can determine whether
60 states suffice or need enrichment. Only then can one compute the allowed
transitions, their closed domain and the corresponding rung squares.
