# The Corrected Zero Packet Has a Klein-Four Mark Table

## Symmetry group

Let \(G\) be generated by holomorphic reciprocity \(J\) and conjugation
\(K\). Their product is reciprocal conjugation \(I=JK\). Thus

\[
G\cong C_2\times C_2.
\]

Finite completed zero packets are finite \(G\)-sets. The full Burnside ring
has one transitive generator for each subgroup of \(G\). Because \(G\) is
abelian, there are five:

\[
\{e\},
\quad
\langle J\rangle,
\quad
\langle K\rangle,
\quad
\langle I\rangle,
\quad
G.
\]

## Mark table

For a subgroup \(L\), the mark \(m_L(X)) counts points fixed by every element
of \(L\). On a transitive orbit \(G/H\), the mark is \(|G/H|\) when
\(L\subseteq H\), and zero otherwise.

| Orbit type | Total | (J)-fixed | (K)-fixed | (I)-fixed | (G)-fixed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| (G/\{e\}) | 4 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| (G/\langle J\rangle) | 2 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| (G/\langle K\rangle) | 2 | 0 | 2 | 0 | 0 |
| (G/\langle I\rangle) | 2 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 0 |
| (G/G) | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

The mark matrix is nonsingular. The five marks reconstruct an arbitrary
finite \(G\)-packet.

## Geometric orbit types

The action on the complex parameter plane realizes a restricted subset of
the abstract table:

- a generic nonreal off-seam point has trivial stabilizer and a four-point
  orbit;
- a real off-seam point has stabilizer \(\langle K\rangle\) and a two-point
  orbit;
- a nonreal seam point has stabilizer \(\langle I\rangle\) and a two-point
  orbit;
- the central point \(s=1/2\) has stabilizer \(G\).

The abstract \(G/\langle J\rangle\) orbit is not realized by this parameter
action: a point fixed by \(J\) is \(s=1/2\), which is also fixed by \(K\).
Thus the five-channel Burnside algebra should not be mistaken for five
geometrically populated zero sectors.

## Exact RH mark

RH states that every nontrivial zero is fixed by \(I\). At every stable finite
truncation this is equivalent to

\[
m_{\{e\}}=m_{\langle I\rangle}.
\]

This single equality excludes both generic quartets and real off-seam pairs.
It allows nonreal seam pairs and a possible central fixed point.

## Real representation form

On the real permutation module of the packet, \(I\) is a real-linear
involution. Therefore

\[
m_{\{e\}}-m_{\langle I\rangle}
=
2\dim V_I^-.
\]

This repairs the previous complex-linear typing. The relevant odd component
is the anti-fixed real subspace for reciprocal conjugation.

## Source objective

Construct a Real-equivariant source complex whose zero cohomology carries the
full \(G\)-action. Then derive the \(I\)-fixed mark, or contract the real
anti-fixed component, without reading the divisor. The other marks become
hostile controls that detect accidental conflation of holomorphic reciprocity,
reality, and seam reflection.
