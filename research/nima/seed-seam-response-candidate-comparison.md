Keep the actual retained paths

    x0=AB, x1=AD DB : A->B,

    C_ij=y_j after x_i : A->A.

Their formal mixed boundary Delta=C11-C10-C01+C00 is nonzero in the free span of words and is annihilated by all additive primitive-edge costs. No equality of direct and indirect paths is required to evaluate a function of these records.

This note compares existing laws; it introduces no numerical matrices or new checker. Evidence is the inspected prior constructions and the already checked seed rectangle, not fresh verification of every referenced branch.

## 1. Shared-leg law: best type match, not yet a physical choice

The established matrix construction composes leg responses in their actual order. Its rectangular factorization needs neither a fitted mixed coefficient nor an assertion that X0=X1 or Y0=Y1. A nonzero rectangle would NOT prove noncommutativity by itself. Ordered multiplication and joint dependence suffice; it can also vanish despite nonzero leg differences.

The DG variant adds leg-comparison witnesses and higher fillers to organize comparisons of these effects. Those fillers are required if that higher-coherence interpretation is asserted, but they are NOT logically required merely to evaluate the four composites and retain their difference. Seeking native equality witnesses before specifying a response was therefore stronger than the response question itself requires.

A tuple of response maps alone need not reconstruct the paths. Keep the original word/occurrence records alongside the maps, as the existing response fixture does. Faithful provenance and a potentially nonfaithful numerical reader are separate channels.

Source: `typed-assembly-boundary-and-mixed-defect.md`, `check_shared_leg_dg_realization.py`, and `seed-seam-mixed-comparison-boundary.md`.

## 2. Covariance: a downstream consequence, not an alternative interaction law

For a declared reference-return composition, full mean/fluctuation coordinates preserve the original associative response operation and derive its correction. This can be reused once the seam response and admissible incidence are supplied.

The four path corners do NOT themselves supply probability weights or show that the two leg choices are statistically independent. Nor are the two forward alternatives automatically repetitions of one same-slot array. Direct import of the 137-slot measure would change the source problem.

Source: `mean-fluctuation-transport-and-context-policy.md`, `promoted-context-incidence-and-generator-separation.md`, `endpoint-composition-and-reference-return-lift.md`.

## 3. Tensor overlap: keep on a separate branch

The prior mixed residual uses A=P tensor 1 and B=1 tensor P on independent source factors. Its positive mixed action and quartic restriction follow from a declared counting pairing and multiplicative probe. Linear copying instead gives a quadratic response; the source distinguishes these constructions explicitly.

Tensor sewing then conditionally selects a logarithmic scalar action from additivity. It does not identify the forward and return legs of a shared path with independent tensor factors. Applying that formula here without an independence/preparation adapter would reproduce the earlier type mismatch in another form.

Source: `comparison-mixed-residual-quartic.md`, `comparison-tensor-sewing-logarithm.md`, `comparison-kinetic-readout.md`.

## Smallest defensible candidate hypothesis

If continuing with a response hypothesis, first state ONLY this:

> The actual seed paths have a compositional response interpretation; a retained path's response is composed from its primitive responses in traversal order, while its occurrence history remains retained separately.

This would make the shared-leg algebra applicable. It does not require primitive responses to be invertible, BA to invert AB, equality of parallel effects, DG leg fillers, probability weights, global flatness or a specific energy formula.

It is still an extra hypothesis. Its content must come from an actual operation on an identified target, not just arbitrary matrices attached to labels. A nonzero physical mixed signal additionally needs a prepared/interrogated reader that does not annihilate (Y1-Y0)(X1-X0).

## Decision before further construction

The candidates are not three interchangeable ways to normalize one thing. Sequential response is a possible interpretation; covariance is a derived coarse-observation correction; tensor overlap requires a different preparation operation. The first is the closest structural fit to this seam, but the repository search has not selected its primitive physical responses.

Do not build another equality contract merely to get started, and do not choose nonzero matrices to force interaction. The next evidence should specify what a primitive seed traversal DOES to the responding object, or explicitly nominate a physical task as a hypothesis to test. Until then the honest result is this ranked comparison, not a derived interaction.
