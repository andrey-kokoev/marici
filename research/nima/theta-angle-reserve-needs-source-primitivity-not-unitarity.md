# Angle reserve needs source primitivity, not unitarity

## Status

Research packet. It classifies which structures cannot force sewn
transversality and identifies a stronger noncircular candidate. It does not
establish that the theta/Tate source has that property.

## Finite obstruction

Let

\[
p:F\longrightarrow Y,
\qquad
q:Y\longrightarrow F
\]

be nonzero direct and reciprocal incidence arrows, and let \(V\) be unitary
sewing on \(F\). The closed scalar channel contains

\[
\kappa=pV^{-1}q.
\]

Nonzero arrows and unitary sewing do not imply \(\kappa\ne0\). In rank
two, choose \(p=(1,0)\), \(q=(1,0)^T\), and let \(V\) be a quarter-turn. Then
\(V^{-1}q\) lies in \(\ker p\) and \(\kappa=0\).

Therefore none of the following explains nonvanishing:

- invertibility of sewing;
- boundedness of sewing and its inverse;
- unitarity on the critical seam;
- nonzero forward and reverse incidence;
- positivity of the diagonal Gramian;
- complete positivity of a kernel factorization;
- equality of source and target dimensions.

Each permits a sewn vector to rotate into the readout kernel.

## Why an angle bound can be circular

For distinguished one-dimensional ports, requiring a positive angle between
the sewn input line and the readout line is exactly requiring their scalar
pairing to be nonzero. If that pairing is the completed zeta readout, the
angle statement is merely RH nonvanishing in geometric language.

In a higher-rank carrier, a uniform principal-angle bound is stronger, but it
still has no explanatory force if it is fitted after examining the scalar
section. It must follow from an independently defined source operation.

## Minimal stronger mechanism

The standard structural route from nonnegative dynamics to strict overlap is
not positivity alone but positivity improvement or primitivity.

Seek a source-derived proper cone \(\mathcal C_X\) in the complete labelled
boundary carrier such that:

1. the sewn transfer maps every nonzero element of \(\mathcal C_X\) into its
   interior;
2. the distinguished source vector lies in \(\mathcal C_X\);
3. the distinguished readout is strictly positive on the interior of
   \(\mathcal C_X\);
4. the cone, interior, and strictness constants survive cutoff bonding and
   restricted-product completion.

These conditions imply a nonzero distinguished pairing without assuming that
pairing in advance. They amount to a Perron–Frobenius-type source law on the
sewn correspondence.

## Immediate qualification

The local Tate comparator does not preserve a naive acute cone: the prime-two
hostile already falsifies that hypothesis. Hence any viable cone cannot be a
separate positive cone at each prime or a product of independently positive
ports.

If a primitive cone exists, it must be global and boundary-bearing. Its
definition must couple:

- primitive-prime current;
- prime-square current;
- connected tail;
- seam state;
- archimedean completion;
- reciprocal sewing.

This is consistent with the earlier result that none of those channels is an
independently positive port.

## Completion obstruction

Even finite positivity improvement is insufficient. A family may have strict
overlap at every cutoff while its lower angle bound tends to zero. The exact
sewing hostile with \(\kappa_N\to0\) while \(V_N\) and \(V_N^{-1}\) remain
bounded realizes this failure.

The completed theorem therefore needs one source-derived uniform reserve,
expressed in the constructor topology rather than unrestricted coefficient
\(L^2\).

## Hostile arithmetic gate

A proposed cone or primitive channel must reject signed prime perturbations
that preserve archimedean symmetry and the scalar functional equation. If the
same positivity-improving law survives those perturbations, it cannot explain
the arithmetic orientation.

The rejection must occur before scalar readout, in a finite source-local
witness such as:

- one cone-preservation failure;
- one noninterior image;
- one vanishing strictness minor;
- one boundary-current incidence with the wrong sign;
- or one cutoff at which the uniform reserve decreases without source
  control.

## Decision tree

There are now three possibilities:

1. Theta/Tate sewing generates a global primitive cone or equivalent strict
   source order. Then transversality follows from an independent mechanism.
2. It generates only positivity, unitarity, or boundedness. Then zeros remain
   allowed and the mechanism is insufficient.
3. The only available strict-angle statement is the distinguished scalar
   nonvanishing itself. Then the correspondence programme has returned to an
   RH-equivalent reformulation and should be closed.

## Conclusion

Sewing transversality is the correct location of the unresolved issue, but it
is not yet an explanation. The next admissible mechanism is a source-derived
global positivity-improving law, or an equivalent reduction of the sewing
structure group that forbids rotation into the readout kernel. Anything less
permits the exact quarter-turn falsifier; anything defined from the scalar
overlap is circular.
