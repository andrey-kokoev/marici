# Finite boundary rank is compatible with infinite prime memory only through defect-space minimality

## Apparent conflict

Two source conclusions must be reconciled:

- a finite boundary pencil can carry the zero divisor through its Weyl return;
- a fixed finite collection of scalar traces cannot be faithful on a growing prime-labelled reservoir.

Both are correct because they concern different domains.

A boundary triple does not require its trace map to be injective on the whole bulk space. It requires the Poisson lift to identify the boundary carrier with the spectral defect space.

## Defect-space criterion

Let \(A_{\min}\subset A_{\max}\) be the closed history pair and let

\[
N_s=\ker(A_{\max}-s).
\]

For a boundary carrier \(\mathcal B\), the decisive condition is

\[
\Gamma_0|_{N_s}:N_s\longrightarrow\mathcal B
\]

being an isomorphism, or the corresponding closed relation in the quasi-boundary case. Equivalently, the Poisson operator

\[
\gamma(s)=\bigl(\Gamma_0|_{N_s}\bigr)^{-1}
\]

must exist on the declared boundary support.

The trace map may have a large kernel on \(\operatorname{dom}A_{\max}\). That kernel defines the reference extension and is not automatically an invisible spectral state.

Thus fixed finite boundary rank is legitimate exactly when the deficiency dimension is fixed:

\[
\dim N_s=\dim\mathcal B<\infty.
\]

The infinite prime memory then enters the finite Weyl function

\[
M(s)=\Gamma_1\gamma(s)
\]

through bulk propagation, not through independent boundary coordinates.

## When the growing-jet obstruction applies

Suppose instead that the reciprocal comparison leaves a common reservoir subspace

\[
E_X\subset N_s
\]

with \(\dim E_X=N_X\to\infty\). Any fixed-rank boundary trace then satisfies

\[
\ker\Gamma_0|_{E_X}\ne0
\]

for sufficiently large \(X\). In that case no fixed finite boundary triple can represent the completed defect space faithfully. One needs a growing boundary jet, a label-resolved boundary object, or a proof that the apparent reservoir directions lie in the reference-extension domain rather than in \(N_s\).

This is the exact fork:

1. fixed deficiency rank: finite boundary Weyl pencil is valid;
2. growing deficiency rank: finite boundary pencil is an undersized compression;
3. unresolved placement of the reservoir: spectral identification remains undefined.

## Why a finite determinant can still have infinitely many zeros

Finite matrix size does not imply finitely many spectral zeros. The entries of \(M(s)\) are analytic return functions containing the full prime-power resolvent. Hence

\[
\det_{\mathcal B}(\Theta(s)-M(s))
\]

may have infinitely many zeros even when \(\dim\mathcal B\) is fixed.

What finite boundary rank limits is the geometric multiplicity of a defect at a fixed parameter, not the total number of parameter values where defects occur.

Therefore the infinite zero set is not evidence for growing deficiency indices.

## Minimality replaces global boundary coercivity

The correct faithful condition for the boundary realization is not injectivity of a scalar or finite trace on the entire prime carrier. It is system minimality:

- controllability: the closed span generated by the resolvent orbit of \(U\mathcal B\) reaches the declared bulk support;
- observability: no nonzero state in that support is annihilated by every returned observation \(V^*S^k\);
- boundary defect faithfulness: \(\Gamma_0|_{N_s}\) is invertible.

At finite cutoff, one may write the cyclic spaces

\[
\mathcal C_X
=
\operatorname{span}\{S_X^kU_Xb:k\ge0,\ b\in\mathcal B\},
\]

and

\[
\mathcal O_X^\perp
=
\bigcap_{k\ge0}\ker(V_X^*S_X^k).
\]

Minimality requires

\[
\mathcal C_X=H_X,
\qquad
\mathcal O_X^\perp=\{0\},
\]

on the source-authorized reduced realization. Completion additionally requires a topology in which these cyclic statements do not lose closed range.

This is how a low-rank boundary channel may control and observe a high-dimensional bulk without being an instantaneous injective map.

## Placement of the fifth margin

The fifth mixed-output margin applies after restriction to the boundary defect packet or to the minimal representation-valued realization. It must not be interpreted as a lower bound for

\[
\Gamma_0:\operatorname{dom}A_{\max}\to\mathcal B
\]

on the whole bulk domain.

If coherent and disagreement defect coordinates retain an orthogonal typed direct sum, their mixed margin is one. If they merge in \(\mathcal B\), the normalized Schur cross block must be strictly contractive there.

Bulk dark states are excluded separately by minimality, not by the boundary output angle.

## Source test now required

For the proposed four-character or five-wall boundary carrier, determine at finite cutoff:

1. the exact defect space \(N_{s,X}\);
2. the rank of \(\Gamma_{0,X}|_{N_{s,X}}\);
3. whether the common reservoir kernel lies in \(N_{s,X}\) or in the reference-extension domain;
4. the controllable span of \((S_X,U_X)\);
5. the unobservable subspace of \((V_X^*,S_X)\);
6. stabilization of these ranks and subspaces as \(X\to\infty\).

Only if

\[
\dim N_{s,X}=\dim\mathcal B
\]

stabilizes and the restricted trace remains uniformly invertible is the finite boundary determinant a faithful completed spectral pencil.

## Minimal hostile

Let \(S_X\) be diagonal on \(\mathbb C^X\), and let \(U_X,V_X\) touch only the first coordinate. The scalar return

\[
V_X^*(I-S_X)^{-1}U_X
\]

is analytic and may be fitted to a desired determinant, but \(X-1\) bulk coordinates are neither controllable nor observable. The boundary determinant is then spectrally correct only for the compressed subsystem, not for the declared completed carrier.

Conversely, a cyclic vector may generate all \(X\) diagonal coordinates through its resolvent orbit. Rank-one boundary data can then define a minimal realization even though no single scalar observation is injective on \(\mathbb C^X\).
