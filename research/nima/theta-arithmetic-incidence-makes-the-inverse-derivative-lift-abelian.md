# Arithmetic incidence makes the inverse-derivative lift abelian

## Exact incidence

The integrated Tate–Weil explicit formula supplies the labelled arithmetic spectral distribution. At the nonzero frequency

\[
\xi_{p,k}=k\log p,
\]

the von Mangoldt atom has weight

\[
w_{p,k}
=
(\log p)p^{-k/2}.
\]

The ordered port has multiplier proportional to \(1/\xi\). Ignoring the already fixed global convention factor, its action on the atom is

\[
\frac{w_{p,k}}{\xi_{p,k}}
=
\frac{(\log p)p^{-k/2}}{k\log p}
=
\frac{p^{-k/2}}{k}.
\]

These are exactly the Euler logarithm coefficients. The three regularity levels are therefore the inverse-frequency image of one labelled von Mangoldt distribution:

- \(k=1\): primitive current;
- \(k=2\): square current;
- \(k\ge3\): trace-class connected tail.

This derives the arithmetic incidence into the order port. It does not derive positivity.

## Zero-frequency distinction

The frequency \(\xi=0\) is absent from the prime atoms. Its value belongs to the neutral or archimedean boundary incidence.

This does not contradict uniqueness of the full order operator \(S\). The two statements concern different objects:

- \(S\) is fixed on its declared source core by the two-sided inverse, reciprocal, and dilation signatures;
- the arithmetic spectral distribution has no prime-labelled atom at \(\xi=0\), so its completed incidence needs separate archimedean data there.

The missing datum is in the source input to \(S\), not a free modification of \(S\).

## Abelian collapse

In Fourier coordinates,

\[
S
=
M_{-2/(i\xi)},
\]

where \(M_m\) denotes multiplication by \(m\). The labelled arithmetic incidence is also diagonal in \(\xi\). Hence both belong to the same commutative multiplier algebra.

For any two admissible multipliers \(m\) and \(n\),

\[
[M_m,M_n]=0.
\]

Therefore the nonzero-frequency arithmetic lift does not exploit the noncommutative information of the full ordered-transformation lens. On this source image, inverse differentiation is simply scalar division by frequency.

Integrating the logarithmic current, obtaining the Euler logarithm, and exponentiating it recovers the scalar Euler product. No additional orientation law appears.

## Three-lens consequence

The ordered lens is richer than the scalar lenses only when the represented source operations generate a genuinely noncommutative state algebra.

The current chain—from the von Mangoldt multiplier, through the inverse-frequency multiplier, to the Euler logarithm—remains abelian throughout. Its scalar determinant and additive current lose no commutator information because none was generated.

Thus the inverse-derivative lift explains the coefficient \(1/k\) and the three Tate regularity levels, but it does not explain RH.

## Only surviving source of noncommutativity

Let \(P_+\) denote half-line or Hardy projection and let \(M_\phi\) be a Fourier multiplier. In general,

\[
[P_+,M_\phi]\ne0.
\]

Compression produces the Toeplitz operator

\[
T_\phi=P_+M_\phi P_+,
\]

and the complementary block is a Hankel operator. Products of compressed multipliers acquire a defect term rather than remaining purely multiplicative.

For suitable conventions, the difference

\[
T_\phi T_\psi-T_{\phi\psi}
\]

is the Hankel defect.

When the two symbols are adjoint-related, this defect becomes a signed square of a Hankel operator. Its semidefiniteness is an operator-algebra theorem, not an assumption about zeros.

This is the first remaining place where the ordered lens may contain an independently oriented law absent from the scalar Euler product.

## Candidate RH gate

A viable construction must derive a theta/Tate symbol \(\phi_z\) before inspecting the divisor and prove that the doubled Green forcing or completed boundary correction is exactly the appropriate Toeplitz multiplicativity defect.

The required comparison has three parts:

1. derive \(P_+\), \(M_{\phi_z}\), and their domains from the half-line theta source;
2. compute the complete block product, retaining both Toeplitz and Hankel pieces;
3. compare the signed Hankel square with the primitive, square, seam, and archimedean boundary packet.

If the forcing equals the source-fixed signed Hankel square plus declared endpoint terms, orientation would come from compression geometry. If any typed residual remains, the route closes.

## Immediate falsifiers

A proposed compressed-symbol law fails if:

- \([P_+,M_{\phi_z}]=0\), because the construction then remains abelian and adds no information;
- the proposed symbol is fitted from \(\Xi\) or its zeros;
- the scalar Toeplitz determinant matches while the Hankel block does not;
- the sign of the defect changes under a source-authorized presentation;
- primitive or square currents are inserted as untyped scalar corrections;
- the residual is nonzero on a two-shell, three-shell, or finite labelled theta cutoff.

## Disposition

The arithmetic incidence problem for nonzero frequencies is solved:

\[
(\log p)p^{-k/2}
\longmapsto
\frac{p^{-k/2}}{k}.
\]

That gain is exact arithmetic provenance. It also proves that the bare inverse-derivative lift is abelian and cannot supply RH orientation. The next and only live ordered-lens mechanism is the Toeplitz/Hankel defect created by half-line projection before scalarization.
