# The window-to-seam comparison must pass through theta completion

## Kernel mismatch

The local adjacent-window history is built from the Gaussian tail

\[
W_t(q)=H(q+t)-H(q-t),
\qquad
H'(x)=-e^{-\pi x^2}.
\]

Its path derivative is the two-front Gaussian forcing

\[
\partial_tW_t(q)
=
-e^{-\pi(q+t)^2}
-e^{-\pi(q-t)^2}.
\]

The causal seam history is built from the completed theta kernel

\[
\Phi(u)
=
e^{u/2}
\sum_{n\ge1}
\left(
4x_n(u)^2-6x_n(u)
\right)e^{-x_n(u)},
\qquad
x_n(u)=\pi n^2e^{2u}.
\]

Therefore there is no direct identity between one local window history and the completed causal history. Any comparison must include arithmetic label synthesis, scale transport, and the completion differential.

## Wall transport is not automatic

The constant overlap wall of the two-front window atlas is a Čech transition coordinate. The completed theta kernel is a differentiated and label-summed source current. A completion differential need not preserve the constant wall with coefficient one.

Thus the required wall statement is not merely

\[
e_{\mathrm{wall}}\mapsto I.
\]

It is the full calculation of the image of the wall through label synthesis and completion:

\[
e_{\mathrm{wall}}
\longmapsto
\lambda_p e_{\mathrm{wall}}^{\mathrm{seam}}
+
\text{Jordan or endpoint components}.
\]

If extra components appear, the auxiliary identity term is not a pure wall scalar and the two-term shifted-history square must be enlarged.

\[

The output must be decomposed into the \(j=0,1,2\) Jordan grades and endpoint jets.

## Minimal hostiles

1. The wall maps partly into a nilpotent Jordan grade, so no scalar \(\lambda_p\) captures it.
2. The two fronts map to different theta grades; their scalar sum has the right \(\Phi\) but the odd incidence is not one \(H-H^{*}\).
3. Label summation creates cancellations that erase the wall before causal history is formed.
4. The one-label comparison is bounded, but its constants grow with \(n\), preventing theta completion.
