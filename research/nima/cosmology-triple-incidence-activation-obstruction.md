# Triple-incidence nearby line is not yet a literal physical cosmology readout

The source wall arrangement supplies a genuine universal logarithmic nearby
line at

\[
p=x+y+3z=0.
\]

However, the activation gates remain separate from the algebraic construction.
The current packet gives two obstructions to reading this line as a literal
generic positive-energy cosmological period.

1. **Support.** In the physical chamber
   \(x=X_1>0, y=X_2>0, z=X_3>0\), the incidence parameter is
   \(p=X_1+X_2+3X_3>0\).  Thus the divisor has no strict-positive support and
   meets the closed nonnegative chamber only at the all-soft origin.
2. **Deck descent.** The restricted coefficient is algebraically nonzero and
   its norm is a rational square, but the loaded line is sheet-odd.  The split
   function-field coefficient does not by itself construct an invariant
   \(\mu_2\)-trace or a physical-chain pairing.

Therefore the nearby line is real as logarithmic arrangement geometry, but it
is not currently activated as a literal generic physical readout.

## Current decision

\[
\text{universal nearby line constructed}
\quad\not\Rightarrow\quad
\text{literal positive-chain period}.
\]

A nonzero physical use of this line now requires either:

- a source-authorized analytic-continuation contour with support on the
  incidence divisor; or
- an all-soft specialization theory that is explicitly separated from the
  generic positive chamber; and in either case
- a twisted coefficient pairing that handles the odd deck character.
