# Flavor Alternating Carrier Needs Three Typed Ports

## Result

The alternating cubic carrier required by the Flavor stabilizer theorem cannot
be built from three copies of one commuting scalar triplet. It vanishes
identically.

The first nonzero realization requires three independently typed vector ports,
or three source-distinct operations whose outputs cannot be identified before
the alternating contraction.

## Alternating source port

For three triplet-valued ports \(u,v,w\), define

\[
\Omega(u,v,w)
=
\sum_{i,j,k=1}^{3}
\varepsilon_{ijk}u_i v_j w_k.
\]

This is the determinant of the three port vectors.

Because the coefficient is alternating while commuting multiplication is
symmetric,

\[
\Omega(x,x,x)=0
\]

for every commuting triplet \(x\). More strongly,

\[
\Omega(x,y,y)=0,
\qquad
\Omega(x,x,y)=0.
\]

Thus neither one triplet nor two triplets with one repeated input can supply
the required orientation carrier.

## First nonzero realization

For three independently typed basis ports,

\[
\Omega(e_1,e_2,e_3)=1.
\]

Exchanging two ports reverses the sign:

\[
\Omega(e_2,e_1,e_3)=-1.
\]

A simultaneous cyclic transformation of the three-dimensional family space
preserves \(\Omega\), while a reflection reverses it. Its stabilizer is exactly
the oriented subgroup required by the preceding census.

## Orientation selection on the frame locus

Suppose the other source relations constrain \((u,v,w)\) to an aligned
orthonormal frame. Then

\[
\Omega(u,v,w)\in\{-1,1\}.
\]

A real interaction

\[
V_{\mathrm{orient}}=-\epsilon\Omega(u,v,w),
\qquad
\epsilon>0,
\]

selects the positive orientation. On this constrained locus it splits the two
reflection-related frames without changing their individual norms.

This is only a conditional source mechanism. A complete model must derive the
three ports, their alignment constraints, and the coefficient \(\epsilon\).

## Typing consequence

The three slots of \(\Omega\) are not three appearances of one datum. They must
remain independently typed until contraction. Viable realizations include:

- three inequivalent triplet fields;
- one triplet observed through three source-distinct derivative or momentum
  ports;
- three ordered boundary or mediator incidences carrying a common alignment.

These alternatives are not interchangeable. Each requires its own constructor
and continuation laws.

## Finite falsifiers

A proposed cubic Flavor orientation source fails immediately if:

1. two of its three input ports are the same commuting field without a
   distinguishing operation;
2. its contraction is unchanged under a reflection;
3. its three port frames are independently relabelable;
4. its coefficient or frame constraint is fitted from the desired vacuum.

The exact checker verifies the first two algebraically and evaluates the two
orientation energies on an aligned basis frame.

## Cross-sector lesson

This realizes the earlier warning that one visible element may be the shadow of
several source elements. The cubic readout can look like one scalar only after
three typed relations have been joined. Reconstructing those relations from
the final scalar is impossible.

## Status

The minimal algebraic carrier is now explicit, but no existing Flavor source
has yet been shown to generate its three typed ports and alignment. The next
search should examine candidate mediator or derivative grammars for these
three independently authorized incidences.
