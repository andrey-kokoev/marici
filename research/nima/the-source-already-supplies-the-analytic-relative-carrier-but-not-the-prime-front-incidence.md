# The source already supplies the analytic relative carrier, but not the prime-front incidence

## Contraction of the topology-changing route

The topology-changing target need not be invented. The theta completion
operator
\[
\mathcal C=\partial_u^2-\frac14
\]
already supplies a source-derived relative carrier
\[
\operatorname{Cone}(\mathcal C)
=
\ker\mathcal C\oplus\operatorname{Dom}\mathcal C\oplus\operatorname{Ran}\mathcal C
\]
together with the Lagrange connecting morphism
\[
\partial_{\mathcal C}:
\operatorname{Ran}\mathcal C\longrightarrow(\ker\mathcal C)^*.
\]

In the half-density wall basis, the theta source gives
\[
\partial_{\mathcal C}(\Phi)
=
\begin{pmatrix}\frac12\\[2pt]\frac12\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\partial_{\mathcal C}(\Phi')
=
\begin{pmatrix}\frac14\\[2pt]-\frac14\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Hence this carrier retains both the even wall and the odd reciprocal jump after
completion kills the wall in the ordinary image. This is exactly the kind of
cohomological residual that the compact-front moment no-go permits.

## What is and is not closed

Closed on the analytic side:

- the topology change is source-derived rather than fitted;
- the residual is a boundary class, not a compact scalar front;
- its even and odd coordinates have exact normalization;
- reflection has the correct parity;
- the boundary map is the Green/Lagrange concomitant.

Still missing:

- a source-derived map from the primitive Gaussian adjacent-window packet into
  \(\operatorname{Dom}\mathcal C\);
- proof that its compact-front PV asymptotic is represented by the same
  Lagrange boundary relation;
- compatibility with prime labels and Adams grade doubling;
- continuity in the global Laplace-rigged prime completion.

Thus the topology-changing carrier exists, but the arithmetic incidence into it
does not.

## Why the logarithmic-derivative route is circular here

The distributional boundary value of
\[
-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}
\]
does provide a noncompact analytic dual containing prime coefficients, zero
jumps, the pole, and branch terms. But identifying its critical boundary with
the complete zero divisor is already the spectral-identification theorem. It
cannot serve as the noncircular constructor of the first Adams edge.

The Lagrange cone is therefore the preferred target: it is defined from theta
completion without importing the zeta divisor.

For every finite cutoff and compact off-seam region:

1. \(J_{\mathrm{front}}\) lands in the relative Green domain;
2. the image is independent of chart representative modulo the recorded wall
   coordinate;

## Revised blocker

The problem is no longer absence of a topology-changing target. The exact
missing constructor is the prime-labelled adjacent-window incidence into the
already available theta Lagrange cone.
