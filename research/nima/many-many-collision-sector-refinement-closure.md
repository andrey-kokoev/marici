# Many-many collision-sector refinement closure

A multiplicative refinement by `lambda>0` sends rectangle vertices

$$
(pr,qr,ps,qs)
\mapsto
(\lambda pr,\lambda qr,\lambda ps,\lambda qs).
$$

The image remains a multiplicative rectangle, so its four-term interval cycle belongs to the same generated sector:

$$
R_\lambda(\mathcal Z_{\rm coll})\subseteq\mathcal Z_{\rm coll}.
$$

Shell concatenation preserves interval additivity and maps cycle generators to sums of cycle generators. Reciprocal exchange preserves the span and reverses orientation on reversal.

Thus the collision sector is closed under the multiplicative refinement operations used by the source. General nonmultiplicative refinements require an explicit vertex-action comparison.
