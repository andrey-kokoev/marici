# RH det3 anomaly is an exact two-cocycle

## Result

The mixed anomaly of the order-three regularized determinant does not require an independent next-rung coherencer. It is the coboundary of the removed low-order counterterm.

For commuting scalar increments, define

\[
a\star b=a+b+ab
\]

so that (1+a\star b=(1+a)(1+b)). Let

\[
r(x)=-x+\frac{x^2}{2}.
\]

The logarithmic bulk anomaly is

\[
\alpha(a,b)=r(a\star b)-r(a)-r(b).
\]

Associativity of (star) forces

\[
\alpha(a,b)+\alpha(a\star b,d)
=
\alpha(b,d)+\alpha(a,b\star d).
\]

The exact rational checker verifies this on a nontrivial three-increment fixture. The residual is zero.

## Meaning

The boundary coherencer is not an arbitrary correction attached to each pair of primes. Once the low-order counterterm object is source-derived, all pairwise anomaly cells and their triangle coherence are generated by normalization through that common object.

This has the same categorical shape as path normalization elsewhere in Marici:

- each composed path maps to one complete normal form;
- pairwise cells are comparisons through that normal form;
- triangle coherence telescopes because the middle comparison cancels.

Here the normal form is the full unregularized finite determinant, and the normalization consists of the primitive and square counterterms plus the order-three bulk determinant.

## What remains nonformal

The cocycle theorem does not establish that theta/Tate prime additions realize this algebra. Three source questions remain:

1. Are prime additions represented by direct sums, successive endomorphisms, or correspondences with partial composition?
2. Do the actual primitive and square currents equal the low-order counterterm (r) in the same normalization?
3. Does the common finite normal form coincide with the independently derived endpoint Evans section?

Only the first two make the anomaly cell source-authorized. The third is still the zero-to-dagger promotion gate.

## Finite falsifier

For three labelled additions, calculate the actual pairwise boundary anomalies in both parenthesizations. Any nonzero cocycle residual proves that the proposed low-order boundary packet is incomplete or that the additions do not inhabit the claimed composition algebra.
