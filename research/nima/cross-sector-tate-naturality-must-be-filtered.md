cross-sector transformation

\[
\boxed{

\mathcal C_{m soft\ star}^{F}
\Longrightarrow

1. its coefficient coinvariant is the canonical integral bridge

3. its string associated grade is the exact weighted three-road star;
4. its three string road restrictions are the proved local Cousin/purity
   maps;

   annihilating its associated supported record; and
6. no normal, Rees parameter, or integer three is inverted.

The sector asymmetry is therefore allowed and predicted: the same filtered
calculus can realize an honest boundary map in one sector and a secondary
extension class in another.

## Falsifiers

The conjecture fails if any of the following occurs:

- no filtered comparison \(\alpha_+\) has the weighted star as associated
  grade and the three established local traces as restrictions;
- the resulting Rees object requires inversion of its filtration parameter or
  any support normal;
- the cosmological unit Gysin star cannot be equipped with a compatible
  filtration whose Tate quotient is the previously proved integral class;
- the canonical coefficient bridge fails to intertwine the induced filtered
  boundary morphisms; or
- a purported construction retains the class only after choosing a road,
  endpoint, splitting, or projector.
