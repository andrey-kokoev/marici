# Holomorphic endpoint output recovers actual residual ports, but not arbitrary graph inputs

## Result and distinction

The concurrent `../voevodsky/endpoint-and-residual-outputs-give-an-equivalence-on-the-prepared-euler-sector.md` proves a bounded inverse on the prepared exponential sector using ALL five prescribed output labels, including the weak residual response. That theorem needs no spectral derivative or compact-margin loss. It already supplies the output-only paired equivalence and nonzero attachment transfer on that sector.

The question here is strictly narrower: discard the weak residual and copied even-response coordinates as well, retaining only Tate responses and endpoint data. The following holomorphic recovery is a supplement, not an obstruction to Voevodsky's five-label theorem.

There are two different reduced-output questions.

1. On the arbitrary graph port (u,v) in D_gamma direct-sum H_gamma, retaining only Tate responses and endpoint data loses the independent v coordinate. There is no inverse there.
2. On the compatible holomorphic spectral letters generated by actual forcing, the first endpoint output determines both U and V. Recovery is continuous with a compact-spectral margin loss. It uses endpoint data and spectral differentiation, not inversion of the Tate operator.

This gives an output-only realization of the compatible primal letter/record image, including its residual-labelled attachment. It does NOT recover the original forcing norm, identify the whole ambient observer carrier, or provide recovery from a finite spectral packet alone.

Input: `../voevodsky/the-rigged-tate-response-lifts-the-residual-labelled-nonzero-attachment.md`.

## 1. Precisely which output is retained

For a port (u,v), forget both retained input coordinates and keep

`O(u,v)=(t_+(u),t_-(u),b_+(u),b_-(u))`,

where t_+,t_- are the actual weighted-dual Tate responses and b_+,b_- the prescribed endpoint integrals. Do not count the copied u or v cross-coordinates of a test/response construction as independent arithmetic recovery.

This map does not depend on v. In particular every (0,v) lies in its kernel. Whether the remaining response map on arbitrary u is injective is not decided by this observation and is not claimed below.

## 2. Actual spectral letters obey an additional relation

Choose an open Euler spectral region Omega with gamma<Im z<beta, gamma>1/2. Write

`s(z)=1/2-i z`, `e_z(t)=exp(i z t)`.

An actual forcing letter has a(z)=sqrt(2)X_f(z) and

`U(z)=a(z)e_z`,

`V(z)=[i a'(z)-L(s(z))a(z)]e_z`.

The amplitude is holomorphic. These are not arbitrary independent port coordinates.

For clarity define H_port to be the larger compatible holomorphic graph consisting of these pairs for arbitrary holomorphic a on Omega, with the compact-open D_gamma/H_gamma port seminorms. Actual forcing-generated letters lie in it. No assertion that every such a comes from H_beta forcing is made.

This is a closed complete subspace of the compact-open port carrier: convergence of U determines locally uniform convergence of a via the endpoint functional, holomorphic convergence controls a' on smaller compacta, and the defining equation for V passes to the limit.

## 3. Recovery from the first endpoint family

The prescribed endpoint integral gives

`h(z)=b_+(U(z))=a(z)/s(z)`.

Since s(z) is nonzero on the Euler region,

`a=s h`,

`i a'=h+i s h'`.

Hence the reconstruction is explicit:

`U(z)=s(z)h(z)e_z`,

`V(z)=[h(z)+i s(z)h'(z)-L(s(z))s(z)h(z)]e_z`.

The other endpoint and both Tate responses remain retained labels, but are not needed to reconstruct this compatible input. They must satisfy the corresponding response graph equations on the output image. The formula is not an inverse Tate transform.

At finite prime cutoffs the endpoint family h is unchanged, so the same reconstruction works on each compatible finite-cutoff output image. The residual continues to use the fixed all-prime half-line L; no cutoff-dependent correction is introduced.

## 4. Explicit compact-margin estimate

Let K be compact in Omega and choose K' compact in Omega containing every closed disk of radius delta about points of K. Put

`S_K=sup_K |s(z)|`, `L_K=sup_K |L(s(z))|`,

`eta=min_K Im z`, `Z=max_K |z|`,

`E_H=1/sqrt(2(eta-gamma))`, `E_D=sqrt(1+Z^2) E_H`.

Cauchy's estimate gives

`sup_K |h'| <= delta^(-1) sup_(K') |h|`.

The reconstruction therefore satisfies

`||(U,V)||_(P_K) <= C_(K,K') ||h||_(C(K'))`,

`C_(K,K')=S_K E_D+[1+S_K/delta+L_K S_K]E_H`.

Projection onto h is bounded by the output norm, so this is a continuous inverse on the compatible output image. The forward response map has the previously proved compact-spectral graph bounds. Thus H_port and its output image are topologically isomorphic in their compact-open topologies.

The compatible output image is closed in the corresponding compact-open holomorphic response space. If output families converge, their endpoint components give locally uniform convergence of a=s h. Their U and V then converge by the preceding estimates, and boundedness of the rigged response gives the required limiting response coordinates.

This is an image-topology assertion about analytical ports, not a lower bound for unweighted source coefficients or forcing norms.

## 5. A finite-point hostile using actual windows

At z_0=i y, take the two successive positive window forcings f_1,f_2 used in the diamond witness. Put X_i=X_(f_i)(i y)>0 and mu_i=iX_(f_i)'(i y)/X_i, with mu_2>mu_1.

The admitted signed forcing combination

`f=f_2/X_2-f_1/X_1`

has

`X_f(i y)=0`, `i X_f'(i y)=mu_2-mu_1>0`.

At this point U_f is zero. Both arithmetic responses and both endpoint values are therefore zero, while

`V_f(i y)=sqrt(2)(mu_2-mu_1)e_(i y)`

is nonzero. Even on actual forcing-generated letters, pointwise output data alone cannot recover the residual.

The holomorphic endpoint family of this forcing is not identically zero. Its derivative at z_0 contains exactly the missing information. This explains why the compact-margin step in section 4 is substantive. No claim is made that finitely many output samples determine arbitrary forcing derivatives.

## 6. Ordered records and the attachment

Apply output projection and reconstruction to each compatible holomorphic letter, retaining its independent spectral argument. Both operations are linear. For m ordered slots, their projective tensor powers are inverse on the corresponding completed compatible letter spaces. The reconstruction bound costs C_(K,K')^m, absorbed by enlarging the input feature radius by max(1,C_(K,K')).

One can therefore use compatible compact-open, all-feature-radius record spaces. Vacua, typed endpoints, edge labels and physical multipliers remain unchanged. Letterwise reconstruction commutes with ordered concatenation, boundary insertion and admitted artificial-cut normalization. No tensor-injectivity claim is being inferred from mere injectivity: there is an actual continuous inverse with the stated margin loss.

Consequently the residual-port attachment factors through this compatible arithmetic-output record image and reconstructs back to itself. Its source relation and balancing kernels are the same. The previously nonzero attachment roof remains nonzero after this factorization, because reconstruction would send a zero output roof to a zero residual-port roof.

The explicit residual-sensitive functional can likewise be pulled back through reconstruction. It becomes a continuous functional involving endpoint evaluation and its spectral derivative on a larger compact set. This does NOT say its ambient observer vector itself belongs to H_port: the full observer envelope contains independent moment/signature directions outside this compatible primal subspace.

These constructions are for the compatible primal image. They do not supply an output-only inverse on the entire graph carrier, the full observer space, or every independently specified response completion.

## 7. Pairings, forcing instability and Tate leakage

On the output image reconstruct U and V and use the previously prescribed residual-port pairing. Equivalently, with a=s h and m=h+i s h', the one-letter Clark kernel is

`[conjugate(a(w))m(z)+conjugate(m(w))a(z)]/[-i(z-conjugate(w))]`.

This is the existing form in endpoint-derivative coordinates, not a new fitted pairing. The rigged response and endpoint arithmetic terms remain available with their original labels.

Recovering an analytical port from its holomorphic endpoint family does not recover its H_beta forcing norm. The translated fixed-length approximate-null sequences still have small analytical ports and endpoint outputs while their prescribed source norms stay bounded away from zero. Thus the source-dual nonsurjectivity obstruction remains intact.

Negative-side Tate leakage remains a weighted-dual response. Nothing here gives it an ordinary L2 limit, removes its endpoint-subtracted zero poles, or establishes a full semilocal operator equivalence.
