## Why they do not compose

The theta-history carrier is generated by

\[
\Phi(u)
=
e^{u/2}\sum_{n\ge1}
(4x_n(u)^2-6x_n(u))e^{-x_n(u)},
\qquad
x_n(u)=\pi n^2e^{2u}.
\]

The adjacent-window carrier is generated by

\[
W_t(q)=H(q+t)-H(q-t),
\qquad
H'(q)=-e^{-\pi q^2}.
\]

There is no identity map between these carriers. In particular,

\[
\mathcal I_{\theta,p}
\not\Rightarrow
J_{P,p},J_{Q,p}
\]

for the comparison maps required in

\[
b_p(x,y)
=
\langle J_{Q,p}y,D_pJ_{P,p}x\rangle_{\mathrm{rel}}.
\]

Using \(\mathcal I_{\theta,p}\) directly as either comparison map is ill-typed.
