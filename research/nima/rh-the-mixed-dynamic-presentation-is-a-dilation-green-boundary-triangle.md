\[

\]

\[
Ag_s=\left(\frac12-s\right)g_s.
\]

\[
\left(1-2\operatorname{Re}(s)\right)
\int_\ell^u x^{-2\operatorname{Re}(s)}\,dx

u^{1-2\operatorname{Re}(s)}-

as oriented normal flux while the tangential Mellin state remains meaningful.
