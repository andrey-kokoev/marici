# Closure of the theta line makes the arithmetic adjoint residual dominate the theta residual

## Hilbert adjoint identity

For the centered incidence

\[
B_\Sigma:U_{\rm ar}\to H,
\]

one has the general relation

\[
\ker B_\Sigma^\dagger
=
\bigl(
\overline{\operatorname{ran}B_\Sigma}
\bigr)^\perp.
\]

The exact one-prime approximants prove

\[
V1=
\Phi
\in
\overline{\operatorname{ran}B_\Sigma}.
\]

Therefore every \(u\in\operatorname{Dom}B_\Sigma^\dagger\) satisfying

\[
B_\Sigma^\dagger u=0
\]

also satisfies

\[
\langle\Phi,u\rangle_H=0,
\]

which is precisely

\[
V^\dagger u=0.
\]

Hence

\[
\ker B_\Sigma^\dagger
\subseteq
\ker V^\dagger.
\]

## Direct approximation proof

Let

\[
x^{(p)}=p^{1/2}e_p.
\]

Then

\[
B_\Sigma x^{(p)}\to\Phi.
\]

If \(B_\Sigma^\dagger u=0\), then for every prime

\[
0
=
\langle x^{(p)},B_\Sigma^\dagger u\rangle_U
=
\langle B_\Sigma x^{(p)},u\rangle_H.
\]

Passing to the history-space limit gives

\[
\langle\Phi,u\rangle_H=0.
\]

The diverging norms of \(x^{(p)}\) do not obstruct this argument because each
pairing is exactly zero before the limit.

## Evans promotion residual

For the unchanged Evans history, injectivity of \(B_\Sigma\) forces the
arithmetic source coordinate to be zero. The apparent lower residual pair

\[
V^\dagger u_z=0,
\qquad
B_\Sigma^\dagger u_z=0
\]

therefore reduces to the single vector condition

\[
B_\Sigma^\dagger u_z=0.
\]

Once this arithmetic adjoint residual vanishes, the theta forcing pairing
vanishes automatically.

## Green consequence

At an Evans zero, endpoint flux is zero. If

\[
B_\Sigma^\dagger u_z=0,
\]

then \(V^\dagger u_z=0\), so the forced Green identity yields

\[
\operatorname{Re}z\,\|u_z\|^2=0.
\]

For the nonzero Evans state,

\[
\operatorname{Re}z=0.
\]

Thus vanishing of the arithmetic adjoint residual on every Xi zero is already
a sufficient RH confinement theorem.

## Scope qualification

The implication uses the same completed history pairing for:

- closure of \(B_\Sigma x^{(p)}\to\Phi\);
- definition of \(B_\Sigma^\dagger\);
- definition of \(V^\dagger\).

It must be rechecked if the Green pencil strengthens the history norm or adds
wall coordinates not present in the closure theorem. On the declared centered
wall-extended graph carrier, the approximants and adjoints must descend
through the same completion.

## Disposition

The theta adjoint equation is not an independent promotion gate once the
arithmetic adjoint residual vanishes. The entire unchanged-Evans promotion
reduces to

\[
B_\Sigma^\dagger u_z=0.
\]

This is a sharper but still RH-bearing vector interpolation theorem. It is not
proved by the scalar Evans mismatch. No RH conclusion is authorized.
