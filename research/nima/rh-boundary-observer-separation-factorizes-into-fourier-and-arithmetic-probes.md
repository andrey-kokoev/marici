# RH boundary-observer separation factorizes into Fourier and arithmetic probes

## Completion multiplicity

The universal five-cell boundary quotient (W) has four Fourier-character
lines. After adjoining a finite labelled arithmetic module (C_X), the
combined boundary carrier is

\[
W\otimes C_X.
\]

If (C_X) has dimension (N), every Fourier character now occurs with
multiplicity (N). The character projectors remain canonical, but each has
rank (N). Fourier transport alone cannot select a frame inside those
multiplicity spaces.

This is the precise place where the finite universal closure and arithmetic
completion differ.

## Rank factorization

Let (A_F) be the four-row Fourier-character observer and let (A_X) be a
source-authorized arithmetic probe family. The combined observer is

\[
A_F\otimes A_X.
\]

Its rank factorizes:

\[
\operatorname{rank}(A_F\otimes A_X)
=
\operatorname{rank}(A_F)\operatorname{rank}(A_X).
\]

Therefore the combined boundary is separated exactly when both factors are
separated. Fourier supplies the fixed rank four. The remaining burden is
entirely the arithmetic probe rank inside each character sector.

In the three-label checker, the full character-by-cylinder family has rank
twelve on a twelve-dimensional boundary carrier. Four character rows paired
with only one arithmetic scalar have rank four and leave an
eight-dimensional torsor. One total scalar has rank one.

## Source-derived arithmetic factor

The authorized valuation/Fock constructor algebra already supplies finite
valuation cylinders that separate every finite labelled packet by unique
factorization. These probes are therefore the correct algebraic partner of
the Fourier character projectors. Arbitrary coordinate evaluations or an
imposed Euclidean label basis are unnecessary.

The canonical finite observer packet has the form

\[
\{P_\lambda\otimes q_C:
\lambda\in\{1,-1,i,-i\},
C\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{src},X}\}.
\]

The family may be redundant. Separation, source authority, and transport
naturality matter; minimal row count does not.

## Completion DPC

Finite separation is not completion separation. The exact alternatives are:

1. Stable tensor descent: the arithmetic probe family is natural across
   cutoffs, continuous in the authorized topology, and jointly separates the
   completed multiplicity spaces.
2. Arithmetic frame torsor: every cutoff is separated but normalized states
   escape all fixed finite probe families at completion.
3. Transport defect: cutoff bonding mixes arithmetic labels incompatibly with
   one or more Fourier-character sectors.
4. Unauthorized repair: separation is obtained only by adding a label norm or
   probe not generated by the source constructor monoid.

The hostile sequence for the second verdict has unit state norm while every
fixed finite character-by-constructor probe tends to zero. The hostile for
the third is a nonzero commutator between cutoff bonding and
(P_\lambda\otimes q_C).

## Consequence for the scalar Riemann section

The completed scalar section is a severe quotient of this tensor observer
family. Even retaining all four Fourier characters while aggregating the
arithmetic factor to one Mellin row leaves a large finite kernel. Therefore
the scalar section cannot construct the boundary observer packet backward.

The RH-bearing cross-tower theorem must start from the tensor packet, derive
its mixed Green transport, and only then prove that scalar output nullity
forces vanishing of the jointly faithful arithmetic Ward family.
