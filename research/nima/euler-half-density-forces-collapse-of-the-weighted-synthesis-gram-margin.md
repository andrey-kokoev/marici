# Euler half-density forces collapse of the weighted synthesis Gram margin

## Weighted primitive columns

Let \(e_p\) be the unit vector of the unweighted primitive arithmetic
coefficient space. The weighted analytic synthesis sends

\[
e_p
\longmapsto
Ue_p
=
p^{-1/2}u_{p,1}.
\]

Every unweighted cut atom has the same source norm:

\[
\lVert u_{p,1}\rVert
=
\lVert\Phi\rVert_2.
\]

Therefore

\[
\lVert Ue_p\rVert^2
=
p^{-1}\lVert\Phi\rVert_2^2.
\]

This already decides the uniform Gram question.

## Gram-margin no-go

Let \(U_X\) be synthesis restricted to primes \(p\le X\), and let

\[
G_X=U_X^*U_X.
\]

For every included prime \(p\),

\[
\lambda_{\min}(G_X)
\le
\langle e_p,G_Xe_p\rangle
=
p^{-1}\lVert\Phi\rVert_2^2.
\]

Choose the largest prime \(p\le X\). As the cutoff grows,

\[
p^{-1}\lVert\Phi\rVert_2^2\longrightarrow0.
\]

Hence

\[
\inf_X\lambda_{\min}(G_X)=0.
\]

No off-diagonal Gram estimate can repair this upper bound: one normalized
basis vector already has arbitrarily small analytic image.

Thus weighted synthesis from the unweighted arithmetic Hilbert carrier is not
bounded below and cannot yield completion-stable Morita equivalence.

## Polar-link consequence

At each finite cutoff, polar normalization gives

\[
W_X=U_XG_X^{-1/2}.
\]

But

\[
\lVert G_X^{-1/2}\rVert
=
\lambda_{\min}(G_X)^{-1/2}
\longrightarrow\infty.
\]

Therefore the finite reciprocal links \(W_X,W_X^*\) do not descend uniformly
to completion in the unweighted source topology.

This is not a technical gap awaiting a sharper estimate. The Euler
half-density itself forces the collapse.

## Square and connected grades

The same obstruction appears more strongly at higher grades. For a unit
prime-power basis vector \(e_{p,k}\),

\[
\lVert Ue_{p,k}\rVert^2
=
\frac1{k^2}p^{-k}\lVert\Phi\rVert_2^2.
\]

For every fixed \(k\),

\[
\lVert Ue_{p,k}\rVert\longrightarrow0
\]

as \(p\to\infty\). For fixed \(p\), it also tends to zero as \(k\to\infty\).

The Hilbert--Schmidt and nuclear gains of the square and connected grades are
therefore the opposite of a lower frame bound. Greater compactness improves
summability while making invertible synthesis less plausible.

## Adjacent-column obstruction

Even if one renormalized away the Euler weights, smooth dependence of the cut
atom on the scale creates a second obstruction. For two normalized columns
\(v_a,v_b\), the Rayleigh vector \((1,-1)/\sqrt2\) gives

\[
\lambda_{\min}
\begin{pmatrix}
\langle v_a,v_a\rangle&\langle v_a,v_b\rangle\\
\langle v_b,v_a\rangle&\langle v_b,v_b\rangle
\end{pmatrix}
\le
\frac12\lVert v_a-v_b\rVert^2.
\]

When source scales approach each other and the cut section is continuous, the
right side tends to zero. Thus weight renormalization alone would not supply
a robust uniform frame on a scalar analytic projection.

The weight obstruction is unconditional and already sufficient; the adjacent
column estimate explains the remaining smooth-Gram collapse.

## What topology would hide the failure

Define a weighted arithmetic norm by

\[
\lVert e_{p,k}\rVert_{\mathrm{weighted}}
=
\frac1k p^{-k/2}.
\]

In that norm, the column norms become constant. Equivalently, one may pull the
analytic norm back along \(U\).

Either choice builds the Euler decay into the source metric. It does not prove
that the original valuation/Fock carrier acts faithfully. It changes the
source topology so that small analytic images are declared small arithmetic
states.

Such a renorming is admissible only if independently generated by the source
constructors and shown compatible with multiplication, Adams maps, reciprocal
transport, and anomaly traces. It cannot be introduced solely to make the
Gram bound true.

## Minimal repair

The source label must remain visible in the target. Define an augmented graph
synthesis

\[
\widehat U_Xv
=
(v,U_Xv)
\]

into

\[
V_X\oplus H_X.
\]

With the graph norm,

\[
\lVert\widehat U_Xv\rVert^2
=
\lVert v\rVert_V^2+\lVert U_Xv\rVert_H^2
\ge
\lVert v\rVert_V^2.
\]

Hence

\[
\widehat U_X^*\widehat U_X
=
I+U_X^*U_X
\ge I.
\]

The augmented synthesis has a uniform Gram margin of at least one.

This is the smallest honest completion-stable linking carrier. It preserves
the arithmetic state rather than demanding its reconstruction from a
vanishing analytic image.

## Why the repair is not yet RH

The identity arithmetic channel makes the augmented link faithful, but it can
also trivialize observability if treated as a direct readout. The completed
determinant must distinguish:

- the arithmetic identity channel, which preserves provenance;
- the analytic theta/Tate channel, which carries the candidate \(\Xi\)
  section;
- the residual \(Uv-h\), which enforces matching.

A determinant formed from the identity channel alone is a nowhere-zero unit
and says nothing about \(\Xi\). The RH-bearing Schur complement must still
couple the retained arithmetic state to the analytic, seam, endpoint, and
archimedean blocks.

Thus graph augmentation repairs Morita fullness but does not prove
zero-confinement.

## Revised linking block

The completion-stable source link should use

\[
\widehat M_X=\widehat U_X,
\qquad
\widehat N_X=\widehat U_X^*,
\]

on the graph carrier. Its Gram operator satisfies

\[
\widehat N_X\widehat M_X=I+G_X.
\]

The complete determinant must then quotient or Schur-eliminate only through
source-authorized matching maps. Eliminating the arithmetic identity block
without retaining the synthesis residual would return to the collapsing
analytic projection.

## Hostile tests

1. Any claimed positive uniform lower bound for weighted \(U_X\) contradicts
   the single-column estimate.
2. Polar normalization of \(U_X\) has unbounded inverse Gram factor.
3. Greater Schatten regularity does not imply a lower frame bound.
4. Pulling back the analytic norm silently changes source authority.
5. Graph augmentation followed by deletion of the residual loses the repair.
6. Reading the arithmetic identity channel as the RH determinant produces a
   vacuous nowhere-zero factor.

## Consequence for categorical RH

The direct weighted-synthesis Morita route is closed:

\[
\inf_X\lambda_{\min}(U_X^*U_X)=0.
\]

The viable categorical carrier is the arithmetic--analytic graph, whose
augmented synthesis has a uniform lower bound by construction while retaining
the operative matching residual.

The next exact task is to build the two-sided linking determinant on this graph
carrier and prove that its nontrivial Schur complement, not its identity
arithmetic factor, is the completed theta/\(\Xi\) section.

## Verdict

Euler half-density makes weighted analytic synthesis compact in precisely the
way that destroys completion Morita fullness. The obstruction is exact and
cannot be repaired by estimation. A completion-stable categorical RH operator
must retain the arithmetic label through graph augmentation and place the
theta determinant in the residual Schur complement.
