# qRB microstep 80: algebraic diagonal transport

For each finite core packet `E_k`, choose the eventual Douglas transport supplied by the normalized Gram bounds. If the transports agree on overlaps, they define an algebraic map

$$
\mathcal D_{\rm alg}:E_{\rm count}\to\varinjlim\mathcal K_\Lambda.
$$

The overlap condition is

$$
\mathcal D_{k+1}|_{E_k}=\mathcal D_k.
$$

Then every finite linear combination has a well-defined image, and the diagonal spectral observer is defined algebraically on the countable core.

This is weaker than bounded extension: packetwise Douglas constants do not control the norm of `D_alg` on the completed domain. A common graph estimate or a uniform bound is still required.
