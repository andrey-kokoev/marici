# q-C-R depends on the regulator type

The historical q-C map is the logarithmic Mellin intertwiner

$$
C=\mathcal M_{\log}.
$$

For a radial cutoff projection

$$
P_X,
$$

define the transported spectral regulator

$$
Q_X=CP_XC^{-1}.
$$

Then

$$
Q_XC=CP_X
$$

and the q-C-R cube commutes strictly. The same conclusion holds for the semilocal place cutoff in the source-derived sewing ledger: the complete characterwise Fourier operator is cutoff-natural.

An independently chosen sharp spectral window

$$
\widetilde Q_X
$$

need not equal

$$
CP_XC^{-1}.
$$

Its comparison defect is

$$
E_X
=
\widetilde Q_XC-CP_X.
$$

The corresponding q-C-R cube is lax, with coefficient

$$
E_X.
$$

Thus q-C-R has a complete classification:

| Regulator | q-C-R status |
|---|---|
| transported spectral regulator | strict |
| semilocal place cutoff | strict |
| independently imposed sharp spectral window | lax |

The logarithmic-window leakage

$$
A_X=P_X\mathcal F(I-P_X)
$$

belongs to the third line. It is not the semilocal place-cutoff comparison.

This separates two regulator notions that had been conflated in the eight-axis discussion:

$$
R_{\rm place}
$$

and

$$
R_{\rm window}.
$$

They may be packaged into one stratified regulator coordinate when the regulator type is retained as a label. Treating them as one unlabeled arrow produces contradictory strict and lax classifications.
