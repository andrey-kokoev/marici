## The jet-number generator

Introduce the spectral-displacement generating function

\[
\Psi_z(\tau)=G(z+\tau)
=\sum_{m\geq0}\frac{\partial_z^mG(z)}{m!}\tau^m.
\]

The Euler derivation in the displacement variable is

\[
N_\tau=\tau\partial_\tau.
\]

It acts diagonally on the jet grading:

\[
N_\tau\tau^m=m\tau^m.
\]

After factorial normalization this is exactly the Fock number operator

\[
Ne_m=me_m,
\qquad
N=a^*a.
\]

Thus the missing positive diagonal in the oscillator completion has a canonical
algebraic type: it is spectral-displacement dilation.

This does not yet prove that it is present in the theta source energy. The
generating function must belong to the domain of the closed dilation operator,
and the Green identity must produce its graph energy with the required
coefficient.

## Source-side realization

For a Mellin-Laplace representation of the form

\[
G(z+\tau)=\int_0^\infty F(q)e^{zq}e^{\tau q}\,dq,
\]

the coefficient of \(\tau^m\) is the source moment with probe \(q^m\). On the
probe algebra the Euler operator

\[
E_q=q\partial_q
\]

\[
E_q(q^m)=mq^m.
\]

Therefore \(N_\tau\) is dual to scale-probe dilation. Moving this operator onto
the source requires integration by parts and retains its endpoint term. The
probe Euler operator, its source adjoint, and the resulting boundary current
must remain separately typed.

## Arithmetic transport does not commute with dilation

Let arithmetic translation act by

\[
(S_nf)(q)=n^{-1/2}f(q+\log n).
\]

For \(D=q\partial_q\), direct calculation gives

\[
[D,S_n]=-(\log n)S_n\partial_q.
\]

The failure of commutation is not an error term that may be erased. It is an
oriented logarithmic derivative current generated by prime transport itself.
Any source-derived coupling between arithmetic incidence and jet number must
reproduce this commutator and its boundary contribution.

## Why the prime-square current is not the number operator

The prime-square incidence produces moment weights

\[
(2\log p)^m.
\]

The jet-number operator multiplies jet level \(m\) by \(m\). These are
different gradings:

- prime-power depth records arithmetic constructor history;
- jet degree records differentiation of the spectral probe.

Already on two nonzero jet levels, no scalar identifies them. If

\[
v_m=x^m/\sqrt{m!},
\]

then

\[
(Nv)_1=v_1,
\qquad
(Nv)_2=2v_2.
\]

A fixed incidence multiplier would have to equal both \(1\) and \(2\). This
two-level comparison is a finite typing falsifier for any direct identification
of bare prime-square incidence with \(N\).
