# Correction: global reciprocal-jet neutrality does not localize primewise without an adelic factorization theorem

## Overclaim

The preceding packet placed the global reciprocal-jet line separately inside
every local hyperbolic carrier `K_p`. The functional equation does not authorize
that localization.

The relation

\[
\tau_m(-\bar z)=(-1)^m\overline{\tau_m(z)}
\]

is global. It identifies two completed adelic jet amplitudes after all finite
and archimedean factors have been assembled. It does not provide a canonical
equal-norm pair in each prime fiber.

## Correct carrier

The reciprocal jet line belongs first to a global restricted-product or
determinant-line carrier

\[
L_{\rm jet}^{\mathbb A}
\subset\mathcal K_{\mathbb A}.
\]

The local valuation lines

\[
L_{p,z}
=
\operatorname{span}\{(b_{p,z},p^{-z}b_{p,z})\}
\]

belong to separate local carriers. Comparing
`L_jet^A` with every `L_p,z` requires a factorization map preserving:

- the retained global base;
- local prime labels;
- the archimedean factor;
- determinant orientation;
- the global reciprocal metric.

Global equality permits local scaling factors whose product is neutral. It
therefore does not imply that each local modulus equals one.

## Revised rung-four candidate

The useful construction from the preceding packet survives only locally:

\[
[L_{p,z}]_{J_p}
=
(1-p^{-2\operatorname{Re}z})E_p(b_{p,z}).
\]

The missing comparison is an adelic localization theorem

\[
L_{\rm jet}^{\mathbb A}
\longrightarrow
\bigotimes_p' L_{p,z}\otimes L_{\infty,z}
\]

whose metric compatibility is strong enough to separate local factors rather
than merely their product.

## Disposition

The global jet and local valuation coordinates do not yet form two
presentations in one local carrier. The rung-four filler cannot be obtained by
primewise Krein comparison until adelic factorization with jointly faithful
local probes is constructed.
