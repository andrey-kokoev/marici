# RH evaluation descent must hold over the regular source ring

## Result

Pointwise factorization of the carrier port through the bordered equations is insufficient. The factorization must hold over the ring of source-authorized regular spectral coefficients.

The hostile is one-dimensional:

\[
M(z)=z,
\qquad
R(z)=1.
\]

At every sampled point \(z\ne0\),

\[
R(z)=\frac1z M(z).
\]

So numerical row-space tests away from zero pass. But the factor \(1/z\) is singular, and at \(z=0\),

\[
M(0)=0,
\qquad
R(0)=1.
\]

The unexplained carrier residual is exactly the pole hidden inside the pointwise certificate.

## Correct algebraic gate

Let \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{src}}(U)\) be the declared ring of regular coefficients on an open spectral domain \(U\). The evaluation wall requires

\[
R_X\in
\operatorname{Row}_{\mathcal O_{\mathrm{src}}(U)}(M_X).
\]

Equivalently, there must be a matrix \(L_X\) with entries in the same regular source ring such that

\[
R_X=L_XM_X
\]

throughout \(U\).

This condition detects poles without locating zeros of the endpoint section. Polynomial finite cutoffs can use exact module or ideal membership. Holomorphic and rigged completions need their corresponding sheaf-level factorization.

## Two circular hostiles

### Meromorphic division

Constructing \(L_X\) using the inverse of the scalar endpoint row hides the desired zero exclusion in a pole. This is the operator form of dividing by the completed section.

### Carrier-row injection

Appending \(R_X\) itself to \(M_X\) makes descent tautological. Every row admitted to the bordered operator must have independent source authority. A transported or desired conclusion cannot authorize its own premise.

## Successful fixture

For

\[
M(z)=z,
\qquad
R(z)=z^2,
\]

the factorization

\[
R(z)=zM(z)
\]

is regular. This is genuine module descent.

## Completion strengthening

Regularity is still not enough at infinite cutoff. The factors must be uniformly controlled in the constructor topology, and their limit must remain regular. Thus the complete compiler has three nested gates:

1. source-authorized row provenance;
2. regular module membership at every cutoff;
3. uniform continuous descent through completion.
