# Many-many loading-branch comparison

Two source-admissible prime loadings are currently available:

$$
\omega_p^{\rm mix}(\sigma)=\frac12p^{-3/2-\sigma},
$$

and

$$
\omega_p^{\theta}
=2(\log p)\sum_{k\ge1}p^{-k/2}\Phi'(k\log p).
$$

Each produces a convergent prime-loaded relative pro-limit and commutes with the oriented codiagonal. Their equality requires a source identity after applying the corresponding feature maps:

$$
\sum_p\omega_p^{\rm mix}X_p^{\rm mix}
=\sum_p\omega_p^{\theta}X_p^{\theta}.
$$

The analytic majorant proves existence of both branches. It does not identify them. The many-many architecture can therefore carry a loading-labelled family until the canonical G4 comparison cell is supplied.
