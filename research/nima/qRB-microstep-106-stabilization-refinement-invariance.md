# qRB microstep 106: stabilization refinement invariance

The stabilized carrier is refinement-compatible if the linking operator is transported covariantly:

$$
R^*G_{\rm link}^{(Y)}R=G_{\rm link}^{(X)}.
$$

For a common scalar `lambda`, this implies

$$
R^*M_{\rm car}^{(Y)}R=M_{\rm car}^{(X)},
\qquad
M_{\rm car}=I+\lambda G_{\rm link}.
$$

Thus positivity stabilization does not introduce a new refinement defect when the source linking block itself is qRB-covariant.

If `lambda` depends on the regulator, the scalar family must additionally satisfy compatibility under the refinement maps. No arbitrary regulator-dependent retuning is permitted.
