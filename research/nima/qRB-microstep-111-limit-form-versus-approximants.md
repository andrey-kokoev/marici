# qRB microstep 111: limit form versus finite approximants

The prime half-density result already gives a bounded limiting linking form:

$$
|\omega_{\rm link}(u,v)|
\le C\|u\|_{\rm orb}\|v\|_{\rm orb}.
$$

This closes the Riesz representation of the limit form and yields the fixed bounded operator `J_link`.

It does not automatically give

$$
\sup_\alpha\|J_\alpha\|<\infty
$$

for arbitrary finite presentations. The finite-to-limit passage still needs either uniform form bounds or a source-labelled convergence theorem.

Thus the limiting relative observer is constructed, while uniform control of its chosen approximating presentations remains a distinct regulator gate.
