# Many-many refinement-admission functor

Admit a source refinement `phi` into the many-many relative functor precisely when its pullback `R_phi` satisfies

$$
\omega_{\sigma,t}\circ R_\varphi
\in
\overline{\operatorname{span}}
\{\omega_{\sigma',t'},\text{ endpoint jets}\},
$$

and:

$$
R_\varphi(\mathcal C_S)\subseteq\mathcal C_S,
\qquad
R_\varphi(\mathcal N_{\rm obs})\subseteq\mathcal N_{\rm obs},
$$

with projective endpoint continuity.

For admitted refinements, the induced maps preserve the retained labelled graph, collision sector, and labelwise quotient. Strict transported regulators compose strictly. Refinements failing the pullback gate remain external/lax comparison cells.
