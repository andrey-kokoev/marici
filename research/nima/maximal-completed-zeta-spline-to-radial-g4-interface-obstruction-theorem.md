# Maximal completed-zeta spline to radial–G4 interface obstruction theorem

## Question

What is the strongest source-to-interface theorem supported after the analytic, label, weighted-functional, interface-shape, and synthetic exact-law audits?

## Claim boundary

Let `S_zeta` denote the frozen finite completed-zeta spline datum consisting of:

- the even compact log-coordinate test `f` and transform `h`;
- the 198 labelled prime powers `(p,m)` with `p^m≤1024`, grade `log(p^m)`, and weight `log(p)/sqrt(p^m)`;
- arithmetic coefficient `-2`;
- reciprocal pole generators `2` and `1/2`, annihilated by `P(z)=(z+z^-1-5/2)^2`;
- the completed-zeta archimedean distribution with kernel shift `n+1/4`;
- the certified positive explicit-formula interval.

The following source-side arrows are constructed and checked:

1. prime-power enumeration to faithful labels `(prime, power, shell, grade, ordered_pair, theta_label)`;
2. those labels to exact arithmetic weights and shifted-spline evaluations;
3. weighted codiagonalization to the frozen prime interval with coefficient `-2`;
4. reciprocal pole labels to exact zero under `P`;
5. analytic compact support to cutoff stabilization beyond `q=1024`;
6. the prime, archimedean, and pole components to the frozen finite completed-zeta explicit-formula scalar.

The weighted assembly exactly reproduces the frozen prime interval. Reversing the arithmetic sign or permuting weights between labels changes that interval. Raw truncated-power interval evaluation outside support may fail to simplify cancellation to exact zero; the analytic support inequality, rather than that dependency-overapproximated expression, proves omitted terms vanish.

## Conditional interface theorem

There exists a finite synthetic target `X_syn` with explicit ordered bases and exact matrices for source synthesis, reciprocal transport, codiagonalization, cycle quotient, recovery, return, and a Green form. On this fixture, checkers prove:

- reciprocal covariance;
- exactness of the declared kernel sequence;
- recovery identity on the source range;
- descent of retained projections through the quotient;
- equality of the declared cycle line with the Green radical;
- nondegeneracy on the quotient range;
- continuity on the finite projective diagram.

Targeted mutations break each law. The full labelled spline datum also composes with this fixture while preserving distinct prime grades, reciprocal poles, coefficient `-2`, pole annihilation, recovery, and radical equality.

This establishes only the implication:

> Given an owner-authorized G4 carrier and a source-derived identification with the checked finite witness data, the listed finite interface laws are executable and the current hostile tests distinguish the audited failure modes.

It does not establish the antecedent.

## First absent arrow

The first unavailable comparison remains

\[
R_{\zeta}:S_{\zeta}^{\mathrm{labels}}\longrightarrow U_{G4},
\]

where `U_G4` must be an owner-authorized G4 projective oriented-edge source. A valid `R_zeta` must derive the target basis from G4, preserve prime, grade, shell, ordered-pair, theta, and reciprocal-pole structure, and commute with the declared radial feature map. Calling a synthetic target basis `G4` does not construct this arrow.

Conditional on `R_zeta`, the next absent data are the exact full polarized Green matrix, its rank witness, the quotient map, and the proof that its radical equals the declared cycle space and is nondegenerate on the quotient range.

## Nonimplications

The checked results do not imply:

- that the candidate contract is an authoritative G4 declaration;
- that completed-zeta labels are geometric G4 labels;
- that the positive source scalar is a full Green metric;
- that interval cycles equal a G4 Green radical;
- that finite positivity extends to all admissible tests;
- a canonical radial–G4 identification;
- RH, a physical readout theorem, or an unbounded result.

Equal dimensions, matching signs, faithful synthetic coordinates, and passing hostile fixtures cannot replace `R_zeta` or the external Green witnesses.
