# The primitive PV completion has a three-way nonperturbative frontier

## Status

This packet sharpens the first unresolved Adams-edge arrow. It does not close it.

## Closed negative routes

For a nonzero compactly supported smooth front:

1. cancelling any fixed finite moment jet leaves an algebraic principal-value
   tail, which is incompatible with primitive exponential aggregation;
2. cancelling every moment forces the front itself to vanish;
3. matching every moment of two compact fronts forces the two fronts to be
   equal, so their anti-diagonal orientation class vanishes;
4. scalar cancellation of the two universal chart PV coefficients also selects
   the diagonal, orientation-free chart direction.

Thus neither one compact front nor a pair of compact scalar fronts can retain a
nonzero reciprocal-odd residue while cancelling the full algebraic PV tail.

## Surviving source architectures

A valid constructor must use at least one of these mechanisms.

1. **Topology-changing relative cancellation.** Compact chart data maps into a
   declared noncompact, hyperfunctional, Hardy, or cohomological carrier before
   cancellation. The surviving odd class is a jump or mapping-cone coordinate,
   not a compact scalar difference.
2. **A larger analytic boundary carrier.** Exponential Fourier control is
   supplied directly by source boundary-value geometry.
3. **Exact nonperturbative source recursion.** A modular or scale-refinement
   identity reorganizes the complete kernel before PV completion.

Changing carrier merely to evade the compact-front theorem is not authority;
the comparison map must be source-derived.

## Audit of dyadic theta refinement

The exact identity
\[
T_{2m}(u)=2^{-1/2}T_m(u+\log 2)
\]
produces
\[
q(z)=2^{-1/2}e^{-iz\log 2},
\qquad
|q(x+iy)|=2^{y-1/2}.
\]
The scalar transfer is strictly contractive for \(y<1/2\), and the faithful
two-channel transfer is contractive for \(|y|<1/2\).

This is genuinely nonperturbative. It does not yet construct the missing
primitive PV sewing because:

- it acts on completed theta labels, not yet on the typed primitive prime-front
  carrier;
- the exact sign of the retained boundary innovation in the Green current is
  unresolved;
- bulk contractivity does not imply the required Schur or Stein positivity;
- no source comparison identifies the innovation class with the reciprocal-odd
  Euler residue.

Dyadic refinement is therefore a viable alternative architecture, not a
completed bridge for the current Adams edge.

## Earliest executable theorem

The next theorem must prove one of two exact bridges.

### Topology-changing relative route

Construct a source-derived map
\[
S_{\mathrm{rel}}:
C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)\oplus C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)
\longrightarrow \mathcal R
\]
into a declared noncompact or cohomological carrier \(\mathcal R\). The
represented tails must cancel to exponential order while a separately typed
jump, boundary, or mapping-cone class remains nonzero. The residual cannot be a
compact scalar difference because the full compact-front moment map is
injective.

### Dyadic route

Construct an intertwiner from the typed primitive incidence packet to the
dyadic innovation packet and prove an exact Stein balance
\[
\|V_n\|_P^2-\|V_{2n}\|_P^2
=
Q_n+\text{boundary polarization},
\qquad Q_n\ge0,
\]
whose sum over dyadic descendants has boundary polarization exactly equal to
the scoped Euler/Wronskian odd current.

Either theorem must also prove radical descent, reciprocal orientation, cutoff
naturality, and a completion-uniform bound.

## Consequence

The blocker is no longer “find a better finite PV subtraction” or “subtract a
second compact chart.” Those search classes are exhausted. The live question
is whether the source supplies a topology-changing relative comparison or an
exact scale-recursive replacement with a proved odd-current intertwiner.
