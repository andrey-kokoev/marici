# The natural one-channel radial forcing does not cancel the free gluing tail

## Question

Does the completed-theta radial Stokes source automatically annihilate the explicit free gluing functional?

## Claim boundary

No for the only presently materialized half-line source lift. Placing the radial forcing in its native derivative channel and leaving the reciprocal channel empty produces a gluing coefficient equal to the nonzero endpoint-minus-Wronskian Laplace section. Exact cancellation would require a new reciprocal source lift satisfying a bilateral Laplace identity. No such lift is currently source-derived.

## Native radial source

For one retained theta label and shell, the radial graph gives

\[
D_t\rho=f,
\qquad
f=e-\frac12w,
\]

where \(e\) is the endpoint density and \(w\) is the ordered Wronskian current. The natural incidence into

\[
\mathbb D_u=\partial_t\oplus(-\partial_t)
\]

is therefore

\[
U_{\rm nat}c=\binom{fc}{0}.
\]

This is the only channel assignment forced by the existing first-order radial equation. Reciprocal doubling of the carrier does not by itself supply a second forward source.

## Evaluation of the gluing functional

The explicit free gluing coordinate is

\[
A_z(g_+,g_-)=\ell_z(g_+)+u^{-1}\ell_{-z}(g_-).
\]

Hence

\[
A_z(U_{\rm nat}c)
=\left(E(z)-\frac12W(z)\right)c.
\]

The radial Stokes identity gives the equivalent form

\[
A_z(U_{\rm nat}c)
=\left(zR(z)-\rho(0)\right)c.
\]

This entire section is not identically zero. At large positive real \(z\), its first coefficient is controlled by

\[
f(0)=e(0)=\frac12\left[\Phi(b)^2-\Phi(a)^2\right],
\]

which is nonzero for a nondegenerate shell of the strictly varying completed-theta atom. Therefore the native source does not cancel the homogeneous tail.

## Exact missing reciprocal lift

A two-channel source \(U^{\rm rad}=(U_+,U_-)^\top\) cancels the free gluing cell exactly only if

\[
\ell_{-z}U_-
=-u\,\ell_zU_+
\]

as an entire operator-valued identity, with every theta label, shell, ordered pair, and parameter jet retained. This condition is stronger than wall sewing and cannot be obtained by assigning a scalar phase to the same half-line density: generally

\[
\ell_{-z}f\ne\ell_z f.
\]

A valid construction must either derive a reciprocal source with this transformed Laplace response or map the nonzero defect into a declared G4 boundary feature.

## Boundary-feature interpretation

For the native source, the defect coordinate already has the exact typed decomposition

\[
zR(z)-\rho(0)=E(z)-\frac12W(z).
\]

Thus, if G4 does not cancel it, its boundary carrier must expose the initial wall value, endpoint response, and function-valued Wronskian response with the coefficient \(-1/2\). A scalar wall phase or rank-one free crossing cannot represent the whole Wronskian family.

## Hostiles

A proposed reciprocal lift fails if it:

1. copies \(f\) into the second channel with a constant phase without proving the bilateral Laplace identity;
2. cancels only the zeroth parameter value rather than the entire jet family;
3. sums theta labels or ordered pairs before verifying the labelled identity;
4. removes the nonzero defect without exposing a G4 boundary feature;
5. treats reciprocal-forward incidence as the metric adjoint return.

## Disposition

The free gluing calculation now separates two possibilities. The existing native radial source yields a nonzero, explicitly typed boundary defect. Cancellation requires a source-derived reciprocal incidence not present in the current radial or SCC interfaces. Candidate one is therefore blocked at that reciprocal source constructor or, alternatively, at identification of the defect with G4's complete arithmetic boundary feature. No RH conclusion is authorized.
