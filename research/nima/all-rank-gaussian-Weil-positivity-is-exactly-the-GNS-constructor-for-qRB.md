# All-rank Gaussian Weil positivity is exactly the GNS constructor for qRB

Fix a Gaussian `g` and define the source-derived translate kernel

$$
K(a,b)=Q(T_ag,T_bg).
$$

The all-rank condition is

$$
[K(a_i,a_j)]_{i,j=1}^n\succeq0
$$

for every finite tuple. This condition is neither extra certification nor a consequence of symmetry. It is exactly the hypothesis needed for the Moore--Aronszajn/GNS construction.

Form the vector space generated by symbols `k_a`, quotient by the radical, and complete with

$$
\langle k_a,k_b\rangle=K(a,b).
$$

Then the reduced `qRB` structure becomes concrete:

- `R` sends a finite observer packet to the span of its kernel vectors;
- `B` observes by inner product with kernel vectors;
- `q` acts by the source symmetry on labels and extends isometrically when the kernel is invariant.

Joint faithfulness follows because the completed GNS space is generated by the `k_a`. A vector orthogonal to every `k_a` is zero.

Higher coherencer uniqueness is then ordinary Hilbert-space uniqueness: an isometry agreeing on every generating kernel vector agrees everywhere. Thus the higher identity spaces become proposition-valued, and existence makes the admissible realization contractible up to unique unitary equivalence.

This identifies the simple endpoint of the coherence tower:

$$
\text{positive-definite source kernel}
\longrightarrow
\text{canonical generated Hilbert realization}.
$$

The construction does not prove that the arithmetic kernel is positive definite. That all-rank assertion is the Weil/RH-bearing premise. It does show that no additional independent higher-coherence theorem is needed after all-rank positivity, source normalization, and symmetry invariance are established.

The explicit-formula distribution is not a positive local measure because of its negative prime atoms. Therefore the GNS feature cannot be a termwise local positive density in the explicit-formula coordinate. It must be nonlocal or live on the restricted translate algebra.
