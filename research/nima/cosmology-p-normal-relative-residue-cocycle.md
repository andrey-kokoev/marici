# The ordered Čech face produces a relative residue cocycle

The logarithmic target has ordered pair-face residue vector

\[
r=(1,-1,1).
\]

The oppositely oriented ordered Čech face \(-\sigma_{123}\) has boundary
\(-r=(-1,1,-1)\). Therefore, in generators
\((\Xi_{\log},-\sigma_{123})\), the residue differential is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1&-1\\
-1&1\\
1&-1
\end{pmatrix},
\]

and the vector \((1,1)\) is a cocycle. Its residue is zero, and no incoming
boundary exists in the minimal residue complex: the ordinary logarithmic
primitive is obstructed and a three-wall nerve has no Čech 3-simplex.

Over both \(\mathbf F_{101}\) and \(\mathbf F_{103}\), the differential has
rank one, incoming-boundary rank zero, and the relative cocycle is nonzero.
This is not a circuit quotient and does not divide by \(p\).

The result is confined to the residue complex. It does not yet provide a chain
map into the full logarithmic Čech–de Rham totalization, a resolved/Rees lift,
a Bockstein, ideal-dual evaluation, or a physical period.

The next gate is the source-derived total chain-level lift of
\((\Xi_{\log},-\sigma_{123})\).
