# Many-many forward pair loading

Define the prime-power shell coefficients

$$
 c_{p,k}=2(\log p)p^{-k/2}\Phi'(k\log p),
 \qquad
 c_p=\sum_{k\ge1}c_{p,k}.
$$

The polarized pair weight is

$$
\omega_{(p,k),(q,\ell)}
=c_{p,k}\,\overline{c_{q,\ell}}.
$$

It obeys

$$
\omega_{\lambda\mu}
=\overline{\omega_{\mu\lambda}}.
$$

The associated pair loading is the rank-one positive kernel

$$
\omega=c\otimes\overline c,
$$

while the ordered wall output retains the full matrix units before codiagonalization.

Superexponential theta decay gives absolute summability on compact-local parameter sets. The pair loading is therefore compatible with the projective pair codiagonal and reciprocal exchange.
