# Experienced worlds as typed local minima of incoherence

Status: Deutsch--Popperian conjecture and hostile test protocol. No universal
incoherence scalar or observer-selection theorem is currently established.

## Operator stimulus

On 2026-08-22 the Operator proposed:

> experienced worlds are local minima of the incoherence functional.

This is recorded as a research stimulus, not as evidence. Prospectively,
marici.Nima reports excitement 8/10, confidence 3/10, and expected
information gain 8/10. The main confound is current conceptual priming toward
coherence, obstruction, and readout language.

## Scalar version

The literal conjecture asks for a gauge-invariant functional

\[
I:\operatorname{Obj}(\mathcal C)\longrightarrow\mathbb R_{\geq0}
\]

such that physically experienced worlds are local minima under every
source-admissible elementary transformation. This is the strongest and most
fragile version. Nothing currently shows that heterogeneous obstruction
classes admit a canonical scalarization.

## Typed-preorder version

For a source packet \(X\), retain the obstruction record

\[
\mathfrak I(X)=
(\operatorname{ob}_{\rm type},\operatorname{ob}_{\rm descent},
\Theta_{\rm curvature},\operatorname{ob}_{\rm support},
\operatorname{ob}_{\rm readout})
\]

in its native graded categories. Let \(\preceq\) be a source-derived preorder
generated by legal repairs, factorizations, nullhomotopies, and support
removals. A readout-bearing state \(X\) is a typed local minimum when

\[
\mathfrak I(X)\preceq\mathfrak I(TX)
\]

for every admitted elementary variation \(T:X\to TX\) in a declared
neighborhood.

The readout-bearing condition is essential. A coherent algebraic stratum not
met by the physical chain is not an experienced world in this conjecture.

## Current hostile control

The source-derived cosmological sequence

\[
0\to F_{25}\to H_{26}\to T_{\rm top}\to0
\]

has no simultaneous constant invariant lift at two independent primes. This
provides a non-removable residue under one restricted class of variations.
It does **not** instantiate the conjecture:

1. parameter-dependent rational gauges remain untested;
2. the literal physical source and its first derivatives map to zero in
   \(T_{\rm top}\);
3. no source-derived scalar or preorder compares this extension residue with
   the other obstruction grades.

Thus the first candidate is rejected at the readout gate before any claim of
experienced local minimality.

## Reversible-transport no-go

There is a general obstruction to using all admissible transformations as
the neighborhood relation. Suppose \(T:X\to Y\) is invertible and a proposed
incoherence order obeys

\[
\mathfrak I(X)\preceq\mathfrak I(Y)
\]

for every admitted arrow. Applying the same rule to \(T^{-1}\) gives the
reverse inequality. Hence

\[
\boxed{\mathfrak I(X)\simeq\mathfrak I(Y)}
\]

on every connected transport orbit. For a scalar functional, \(I(X)=I(Y)\).
Consequently every orbit point is simultaneously a local minimum and maximum.
Reversible Carrier transport cannot select experienced worlds by local
minimality.

The conjecture has nontrivial content only after declaring a **directed**
class of arrows not closed under inverse: repair, specialization,
coarse-graining, forgetting, resource consumption, or supported physical
activation. That direction must be source-derived. Orienting arrows after
seeing which way lowers incoherence would make the conjecture circular.

## Minimal-sufficient-readout refinement

The physical readout descent criterion supplies a canonical directed arrow.
For a source-declared surjection \(q:V\twoheadrightarrow W\) and readout
\(\ell\in V^*\),

\[
\ell=\bar\ell\circ q
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\ell(\ker q)=0.
\]

When this holds, \(q\) removes distinctions that cannot affect the physical
record. If raw dimension or presentation redundancy is an incoherence grade,
then \(V\) is not minimal: it admits a strict readout-preserving reduction to
\(W\). This suggests the harder-to-vary survivor

\[
\boxed{
\text{experienced state}
=
\text{minimal sufficient object for the source-derived physical readout}
}
\]

within a declared class of source quotients.

Radiative gravity is a positive control: the charge operator annihilates the
discarded \(l=0,1\) modes, so its readout descends. The five-site
cosmological Kummer quotient is a negative control: the chamber functional
distinguishes sheets in the collapsed kernel, so the readout does not descend.
The latter quotient cannot be called a coherence-improving arrow merely
because it lowers dimension.

This refinement still does not identify conscious experience with a
mathematical quotient. It defines only the smallest physical record-bearing
object visible to the frozen readout family. Jointly conservative families,
not a single scalar detector, are required when several physical questions
must remain answerable.

In the linear case the refinement has a universal, non-variational form. The
object \(V/\ker R\) is terminal among all quotients through which the complete
readout family factors. Thus “minimal” need not mean a minimum of anything:

\[
\boxed{
\text{minimal sufficient readout object}
=
\text{terminal sufficient quotient}.
}
\]

This is currently the hardest-to-vary survivor of the original local-minimum
intuition.

## Spectral-sequence proposal: useful but insufficient

A source-derived filtered complex could organize sequential obstruction gates,
and filtration-preserving legal maps would induce maps of its spectral
sequence. This is a valuable testable structure. It does not make the
obstruction preorder automatic:

- the obstruction types may not be pages of one filtration;
- legal retyping may change rather than preserve the filtration;
- earlier versus later nonvanishing pages have no intrinsic interpretation as
  less versus more incoherent;
- spectral sequences do not generally retain extension data.

The last point is decisive here. The filtered groups

\[
0<2\mathbb Z/4<\mathbb Z/4
\]

and

\[
0<\mathbb Z/2<(\mathbb Z/2)^2
\]

have identical associated gradeds \((\mathbb Z/2,\mathbb Z/2)\) and identical
zero-differential spectral sequences, but the first extension is nonsplit and
the second is split. The candidate Marici residue often lives precisely in
such extension data. Therefore the minimum viable organizer is a filtered
derived object together with its extension/coherence structure—not its page
number alone.

## Torsion correction

Rationalization kills torsion by sending it to zero:

\[
(\mathbb Z/n)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q=0.
\]

It does not protect a torsion extension from rational splitting. The string
road/contact class in \(H^1(C_3,A_2)\cong\mathbb Z/3\) is an integral
sector-specific example and disappears after rational semisimplification.
Our cosmological \(25\to26\to1\) obstruction is presently defined only at
finite-field fibers of a differential module; no integral class has been
constructed, so asking whether it is torsion is currently mistyped.

## Bounded falsification program

1. Select a source packet with a nonzero, already typed physical readout.
2. Separate reversible transport arrows from source-directed repair,
   specialization, forgetting, or activation arrows. Declare the latter
   before computing outcomes.
3. Export the full typed obstruction record before and after each variation.
4. Test whether legal repairs induce a well-defined preorder independent of
   presentation and gauge.
5. Search for a readout-preserving variation that strictly lowers the record.
   One such variation falsifies local minimality for that world.
6. Test scalarization separately. Two obstruction records that are forced to
   reverse order under different legal compositions falsify any universal
   real-valued incoherence functional compatible with those compositions.
7. For quotient arrows, compute the intersection of the kernels of the full
   frozen readout family. Minimal sufficiency requires that no further
   source-derived quotient lies inside this invisible subspace.

## Stop conditions

- If “admissible variation” is defined by preserving the proposed minimum,
  the conjecture is circular and must be retired.
- If all admitted variations are invertible, local minimality is constant on
  transport orbits and cannot select experienced worlds.
- If readout is added after selecting the minimum, the observer claim is
  untyped.
- If incomparable obstruction classes are assigned fitted numerical weights,
  retain only the typed-preorder version.
- If every state is a local minimum because the neighborhood has no arrows,
  the construction has no explanatory content.

## Relation to the constructor principle

This conjecture refines the possibility structure rather than replacing it.
The constructor calculus supplies legal variations and missing-resource
certificates. The new claim is that persistent readout-bearing worlds occupy
locally irreducible regions of that typed possibility structure. Whether this
is true remains entirely open.
