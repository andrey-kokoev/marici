## Typed meaning

Each conjecture has four possible ports for the result of one attempted test:

| Port | Attempt | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `++` | prove | proved |
| `+-` | prove | failed to prove |
| `-+` | reject | succeeded in rejecting |
| `--` | reject | failed at rejecting |

A failed proof is not a proof of the negation; failure to reject is not proof of the claim. A not-yet-attempted conjecture has no assigned outcome. This is the user's four-port convention, not the older `E_ab` interpretation where the same strings denote source/target polarity arrows.
