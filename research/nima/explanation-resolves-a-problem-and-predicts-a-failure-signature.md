# Explanation resolves a problem and predicts a failure signature

## Correction to the audit

The nine-gate audit evaluates realizations, provenance, and stability. It does
not yet distinguish explanatory character from truth or evidential support.

A false conjecture can be explanatory and then refuted. A true lookup table
can remain nonexplanatory. These axes must be separated.

## Four independent axes

Every candidate should receive four distinct assessments.

1. **Explanatory content:** does it propose a specific, hard-to-vary resolution
   of a stated problem?
2. **Empirical status:** which tests has it passed or failed?
3. **Realization adequacy:** does its intervention model reproduce and stably
   identify the behaviour?
4. **Source status:** are its terms typed, derived, conjectural, or physically
   implemented?

No axis substitutes for another.

In particular, source typing means that a conjecture is meaningful relative
to the source. It does not mean that the source certifies the conjecture as
true.

## Problem packet

An explanation begins with a problem situation

\[
\mathfrak P=(B,O,\rho),
\]

where:

- \(B\) is prior background expectation;
- \(O\) is the recalcitrant observation, theorem, or constraint;
- \(\rho\) is the typed incompatibility or missing-constructor residue between
  them.

A candidate explanation \(E\) must show how its constructor resolves
\(\rho\), revises the domain of \(B\), or predicts why \(O\) should occur.

Without a stated \(\rho\), the candidate may be an accurate representation but
there is no identified explanatory problem.

## Conjectural constructors are permitted

The constructor of \(E\) need not be derivable from accepted source theory.
Bold explanations commonly introduce a new law.

It must instead be:

- source-typed;
- compositionally defined;
- explicit about which prior rule it replaces or extends;
- independently criticizable;
- committed to prospective consequences.

The epistemic record should classify such a constructor as conjectural rather
than laundering it into a derived claim.

## Failure signature

Let \(\mathcal V_E\) be a predeclared family of admissible variations of the
candidate constructor. An explanation should predict a failure signature

\[
\mathfrak F_E:
\mathcal V_E
\longrightarrow
\mathcal D,
\]

where \(\mathcal D\) records which conclusions, interventions, or coherence
laws fail under each variation.

A component is load-bearing when varying it changes a specific derived
consequence. A freely variable component with no effect belongs to gauge,
redundancy, or unused decoration for the declared problem.

The strongest test is prospective: freeze a variation \(v\) and its predicted
failure \(\mathfrak F_E(v)\) before evaluating the varied system.

## Toric-code ablation signature

The toric-code explanation supplies clean predicted failures.

### Remove periodic gluing

The local boundary identity remains:

\[
\partial_1\partial_2=0.
\]

Local syndrome and face-repair typing survive, but the two torus homology bits
need not:

\[
H_1\not\simeq\mathbf F_2^2
\]

for a contractible planar patch.

Thus periodic attachment is load-bearing for topological capability, not for
local syndrome.

### Remove the dual coefficient channel

Primal chain homology survives, but primal--dual intersection and the
anticommutation witness are unavailable. The missing conclusion is quantum
logical-operator algebra, not cellular topology.

### Abelianize ordered Pauli multiplication

Additive error labels and syndrome remain, but

\[
XZ=-ZX
\]

is erased. This isolates the Product cocycle and Endo realization as
load-bearing for order-sensitive quantum behaviour.

### Change the noise model

Syndrome, repairs, homology, and loop algebra remain. The preferred decoder
may change. This confirms that decoder selection is not load-bearing for the
topological explanation and requires a separate dynamics packet.

The ablation pattern is more explanatory than the unvaried census because it
locates which constructor controls which conclusion.

## Control interpretation

For a state-space explanation, vary:

- state coupling;
- actuator incidence;
- sensor incidence;
- feedback law;
- disturbance model;
- sampling or completion regime.

The explanation should predict which poles, zeros, observable subspaces,
closed-loop responses, or stability properties change.

A fitted transfer curve without such a dependency map confirms behaviour. A
constructor with a correct prospective ablation signature explains why the
behaviour depends on particular internal laws.

## Framework falsifiers

The programme fails as a theory of explanatory character if:

- a widely accepted good explanation cannot be expressed as a
  problem-resolving constructor with criticizable variation;
- a retrospective table passes every explanatory-content test without
  independently frozen variations;
- explanatory status is withdrawn merely because a conjecture is later
  refuted;
- source derivability is required for creative theoretical proposals;
- every failed variation is reclassified as gauge after inspection;
- the predicted failure signature adds no information beyond rerunning the
  full fitted table.

## Revised Deutsch--Popperian conjecture

A candidate has explanatory content when it proposes a source-typed
constructor that resolves an explicit problem and predicts a nontrivial,
prospectively testable failure signature under admissible variations of its
load-bearing structure.

Truth, evidential support, realization identifiability, physical
implementation, and explanatory content remain distinct assessments.
