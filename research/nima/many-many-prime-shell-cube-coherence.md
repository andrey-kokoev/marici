# Many-many prime-shell cube coherence

$$
B_I=\prod_{i\in I}p_i,
\qquad
v_S=B_I\prod_{j\in S}\frac{p_{j+1}}{p_j}
\quad(S\subseteq I).
$$

The action `p_i -> p_{i+1}` generates commuting cube directions. The vertex map is injective by prime-exponent recovery, so source labels remain faithful through every cube face.

A cube face corresponds to applying two shell actions in either order. Multiplicative commutation gives strict square coherence. Higher cube faces inherit strict coherence from the commuting direction operators.
