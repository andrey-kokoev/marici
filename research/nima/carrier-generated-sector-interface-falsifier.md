# Carrier-generated sector-interface falsifier

## Conjecture under attack

The strong Carrier-generated sector-interface conjecture asserted that every
physically realizable sector packet

\[
(\mathcal L,\mathcal C,\mathcal R)
\]

is generated, up to typed equivalence, by the bare common Carrier.  Here
\(\mathcal L\) is the coefficient lens, \(\mathcal C\) its admissibility and
coherence data, and \(\mathcal R\) its physical readout.

## Structural falsifier

The conjecture is false before any sector-specific calculation.  A nonzero
proper subspace cannot be assigned naturally to every bare vector space and
every linear map: full naturality forces the assignment to be either zero or
the whole space.  The exact \(\mathbf F_5^2\) census in
`bare-carrier-naturality-no-go.md` gives the bounded control: none of the six
lines is invariant under all 480 automorphisms.

Therefore the bare Carrier cannot select a nontrivial coefficient ray,
filtration, polarization, boundary orientation, or readout.

## Surviving Deutsch--Popperian conjecture

### Source-indexed interface generation

For a common Carrier \(\mathcal C\) and an admitted source record

\[
\sigma=(\mathcal K,\mathcal S,\mathcal B,\mathcal P),
\]

consisting of coefficient, support/marking, boundary/orientation, and pairing
data, the shared indexed calculus canonically generates the complete sector
interface:

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak W(\mathcal C;\sigma)
=
(\mathcal L_\sigma,\mathcal C_\sigma,\mathcal R_\sigma)
}
\]

up to typed equivalence.  The Carrier fixes the grammar and the admitted
source record selects a realization.  No fitted projector, normalization,
homotopy, boundary current, or readout may be added after inspecting the
desired result.

This is stronger than mere compatibility: every interface component must be
obtained by the shared operations (restriction, localization, transport,
duality, nearby cycles, Gysin maps, or supported pairings), and every change
of source presentation must induce a naturality square for the readout.

## Finite falsifiers

Freeze \((\mathcal C,\sigma)\) before calculation.  The surviving conjecture
fails if any one of the following occurs:

1. two inequivalent interface packets satisfy all source-derived conditions;
2. a required map needs a fitted coefficient or undeclared projector;
3. a physical readout requires boundary information absent from \(\sigma\);
4. source-equivalent presentations fail readout descent,
   \(\mathcal R_{\sigma'}T_{\sigma\sigma'}\ne\mathcal R_\sigma\);
5. a generated packet contradicts an invariant physical record;
6. the operation inventory differs between sectors rather than only its typed
   inputs and coefficients.
