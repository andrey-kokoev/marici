# The physical triangle integrand does not contain an isolated T7 term

The printed source-normalized triangle integrand contains six simplex terms.
Every term shares

\[
q_{\mathcal G}^{-1}
q_{\mathfrak g_1}^{-1}
q_{\mathfrak g_2}^{-1}
q_{\mathfrak g_3}^{-1}.
\]

The two terms containing \(q_{\mathcal G_{12}}^{-1}\) have complete denominator
supports

\[
\{q_{\mathcal G},q_{\mathfrak g_1},q_{\mathfrak g_2},q_{\mathfrak g_3},
q_{\mathcal G_{12}},q_{\mathfrak g_{23}}\}
\]

and

\[
\{q_{\mathcal G},q_{\mathfrak g_1},q_{\mathfrak g_2},q_{\mathfrak g_3},
q_{\mathcal G_{12}},q_{\mathfrak g_{31}}\}.
\]

There is no literal source summand with denominator support
\(\{q_{\mathcal G_{12}}\}\).  The nine-master family containing only that
denominator is a lower sector of the reduction system, not the physical graph
by itself.

Consequently, neither the physical Bunch--Davies covector nor its counterterm
image is typed on isolated \(T_7\).  They must be derived by reducing the full
source integrand while retaining the maps among denominator sectors.  UV
directions that look independent inside \(T_7\) may cancel against, or be tied
to, higher-sector contributions.

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\text{physical triangle readout}
\notin T_7^\vee\ \text{primitively};
\quad
\text{it belongs to the full sector complex.}
}
\]

This closes the T7-only counterterm-admissibility route.  The next admissible
object is the source-normalized full-sector IBP/residue chain map, with UV
subtraction performed before projecting to the lower T7 sector.
