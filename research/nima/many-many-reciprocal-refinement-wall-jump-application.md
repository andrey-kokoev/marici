# Many-many reciprocal refinement and wall/jump sectors

Let the reciprocal involution be

$$
B(n,m)=(m,n).
$$

Any refinement generated by reciprocal-symmetric pair data commutes with `B`. On the two-coordinate endpoint fiber this gives

$$
RB=BR,
$$

so `R=aI+bB` and the exact wall/jump criterion applies.

The transported refinement has eigenvalues

$$
R_{\rm wall}=a+b,
\qquad
R_{\rm jump}=a-b.
$$

The corrected even normalization multiplies the wall coordinate by `-2`; it leaves the sector eigenvalues and reciprocal exchange law unchanged.
