# Many-many detected/loss effect faithfulness

$$
E_{\rm det}(t)=V_t^*C^*CV_t,
$$

one has

$$
\langle x,E_{\rm det}(t)x\rangle=\|CV_tx\|^2.
$$

Thus vanishing detected effect response implies `CV_t x=0`. Applying the countable shell-generating family and the full Fourier injectivity theorem gives

$$
\bigcap_n\ker(CV_{t_n})\cap\mathcal Z_{\rm coll}=\{0\}.
$$

The detected effects jointly separate the collision sector. The loss effects

$$
E_{\rm loss}(t)=I-V_t^*V_t
$$

retain the complementary attenuation record. Together they form a faithful countable observation family on the completed labelled source, subject to the bounded-coupler hypothesis.
