# The coherent constant wall is exactly the repairing tail direction

## Parity identification

Return to the reciprocal sheet basis, where

\[
H_{\mathrm{tail}}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-1\\
-1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its normalized eigenvectors are

\[
v_{\mathrm{coh}}
=
\frac1{\sqrt2}
\binom{1}{1},
\qquad
H_{\mathrm{tail}}v_{\mathrm{coh}}=-v_{\mathrm{coh}},
\]

and

\[
v_{\mathrm{dis}}
=
\frac1{\sqrt2}
\binom{1}{-1},
\qquad
H_{\mathrm{tail}}v_{\mathrm{dis}}=v_{\mathrm{dis}}.
\]

Thus the negative tail mode is the coherent common-sheet direction.

If the source constant wall is sewn with the same sign into the two reciprocal histories, its incidence vector is exactly \(v_{\mathrm{coh}}\). A direct positive wall energy of strength \(\alpha\) then gives

\[
H_{\mathrm{tail}}
+
\alpha
v_{\mathrm{coh}}v_{\mathrm{coh}}^{*},
\]

with eigenvalues

\[
1,
\qquad
\alpha-1.
\]

Therefore the constant wall is not merely a plausible repair channel. Its coherent reciprocal character selects the unique optimal rank-one direction.

## Source gate

The conclusion is conditional on the sign of reciprocal sewing. The source must prove that the constant carrier maps as

\[
1_{\mathrm{wall}}
\longmapsto

\]

If it instead maps antisymmetrically,

\[

A scalar wall norm cannot distinguish these cases. The reciprocal incidence map must.

## Exact remaining scalar

Once coherent incidence is established, the angular problem disappears. The local positivity gate reduces to one source-normalized strength:

\[
\alpha>1.
\]

At \(\alpha=1\), the coherent direction remains a radical. For completion, one needs

\[
\inf(\alpha-1)>0
\]

over cutoffs and compact off-seam parameter sets.

The number \(1\) here is measured in the normalized tail Hamiltonian frame. It has no invariant meaning until the source Green identity fixes the relative scale between reciprocal tail flux and constant-wall energy.
