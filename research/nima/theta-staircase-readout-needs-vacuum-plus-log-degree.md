# Theta staircase readout needs vacuum plus logarithmic degree

## Status

Exact continuity theorem and finite obstruction. The centered arithmetic
staircase readout is continuous in a source-derived graph norm, but not in the
pure logarithmic-degree seminorm. Its unique missing direction is the Gaussian
vacuum label.

This supplies completion control for the new arithmetic readout. It does not
supply adjointness, star compatibility, or zero orientation.

## Source synthesis

Let the Gaussian scale synthesis be

\[
(Uc)(v)=\sum_{m\geq1}c_m e^{-\pi m^2e^{2v}},
\qquad v\geq0.
\]

For a locally finite prime-power staircase \(W\), define the centered row

\[
R_zc=-\int_0^\infty W(v)(Uc)(v)e^{izv}\,dv.
\]

On finitely supported packets this has coefficient representation

\[
R_zc=\sum_{m\geq1}r_m(z)c_m,
\]

where

\[
r_m(z)=-\int_0^\infty
W(v)e^{-\pi m^2e^{2v}}e^{izv}\,dv.
\]

Because the first prime-power jump is at \(\log 2\), the integral begins
effectively at that scale.

## Super-exponential coefficient decay

Assume the staircase has at most exponential growth,

\[
|W(v)|\leq Ce^{av}.
\]

For \(z=x+iy\), substitution \(u=me^v\) gives

\[
|r_m(z)|
\leq C m^{y-a}
\int_{2m}^\infty u^{a-y-1}e^{-\pi u^2}\,du.
\]

Hence \(r_m(z)\) decays faster than every inverse power of \(m\), locally
uniformly for \(z\) in a fixed horizontal strip. In particular,

\[
\sum_{m\geq2}\frac{|r_m(z)|^2}{\log m}<\infty.
\]

## Pure logarithmic degree fails

The source-derived degree operator satisfies

\[
L_{\rm inc}e_m=(\log m)e_m,
\qquad
\ker L_{\rm inc}=\operatorname{span}\{e_1\}.
\]

For a nonzero nonnegative staircase, the vacuum coefficient is nonzero on the
real seam:

\[
r_1(0)=-\int_{\log2}^\infty
W(v)e^{-\pi e^{2v}}\,dv<0.
\]

Therefore

\[
\langle e_1,L_{\rm inc}e_1\rangle=0,
\qquad
R_0e_1=r_1(0)\neq0.
\]

No constant \(C\) can satisfy

\[
|R_0c|^2\leq C\langle c,L_{\rm inc}c\rangle
\]

on the source module. This one-label vacuum packet is the smallest exact
falsifier.

## Minimal repaired graph energy

Let

\[
P_0=|e_1\rangle\langle e_1|,
\qquad
Q_0=P_0+L_{\rm inc}.
\]

Then Cauchy--Schwarz in the diagonal graph norm gives

\[
|R_zc|^2
\leq M_z^2\langle c,Q_0c\rangle,
\]

with the sharp dual norm

\[
M_z^2
=|r_1(z)|^2
+\sum_{m\geq2}\frac{|r_m(z)|^2}{\log m}.
\]

Thus the centered staircase row extends continuously through the completion
generated by vacuum anchoring plus logarithmic arithmetic degree.

## Interpretation

The source has selected the same two-part architecture encountered in the
oscillator graph norm: a ground-state anchor plus a positive excitation
degree.

Here the excitation degree is not fitted. It is the complete prime-power
incidence operator

\[
L_{\rm inc}=\frac12\log Q.
\]

The vacuum port is also not optional: it is forced by the exact kernel of that
degree and detected by the staircase row itself.

## What this does not prove

The theorem establishes continuity and rules out completion escape for this
scalar row on the repaired graph domain. It does not imply that reciprocal
transport is the adjoint of forward transport. It therefore does not evade
the two-shell hostile source satisfying the same tail equations and Green
identity.

The distinction is exact: vacuum-plus-degree control implies bounded
observation, but bounded observation alone does not imply star-compatible
observation.
