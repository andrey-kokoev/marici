The first inventory audit was **out of date**: later files propose codimension-two rank tables for all six matched histories. But a new exact fibre test shows THREE of those tables collapse to at most seven-dimensional images under `CZ`; see `seven-point-positroid-compiler-has-three-collapsed-image-cells.md`. The proposals are not a certified six-cell positive-geometry triangulation. Neither the older `requires_on_shell_graph` status nor the later rank-table success flag alone settles image/form admission.

An explicit chart has now been constructed for history zero, whose matched parity simplex is `[1,3,4,6,7]` and whose omitted labels are 2 and 5. Its source columns are `C_i=w_i(1,t_i)` with classes `t=(0,1,1,2,u,u,v)`, `w1=1`, other weights positive and `2<u<v`. This is an eight-parameter positive chart: exactly minors (2,3) and (5,6) vanish, and every other ordered source minor is positive. For positive moment-curve external data `Z_j=(1,j,j²,j³,j⁴,j⁵)` the exact determinant of the target Grassmannian affine-coordinate Jacobian at weights one, `u=3,v=4` is `102400/194481`, nonzero. Thus the chart maps locally to a full-dimensional region of `G(2,6)`.

This **does not** prove the chart canonical form equals the matched generalized-R history, nor establish target overlap or coverage. An eight-dimensional image alone does not select the correct form. The next discriminating test is to compare the chart's pushed-forward canonical form and residues against history zero for the SAME positive Z. A rational inverse has since been obtained, and a subsequent symbolic top-cell double-residue computation corrected the naive local form by a nonconstant factor `2/v` (`seven-point-chart-boundary-residue-corrects-the-naive-form.md`). Equality with the history is not yet established. Avoid equating a rational-kinematics parity-form match with an all-positive-Z geometric equality.

A later exact eight-point construction shows the key geometric limitation is stronger than missing types: even over a strictly positive target, a fixed `CZ` source fibre has a curved boundary (`the-eight-point-positive-fibre-is-curved-so-the-seven-point-polygon-method-does-not-scale.md`). The seven-point polyhedral Farkas method cannot simply scale with `n`.

The analytic `n^-2` law remains fitted selected-component evidence, not a norm-uniform canonical-form bound. A fixed pole-avoiding domain, normalization and uniform-in-n shell estimate are still required for completion transfer.
