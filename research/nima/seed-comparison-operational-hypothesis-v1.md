# Operational hypothesis v1: reversible exchange with a relationship record

## Status and decision

This is a NEW candidate model for assessment, not a recovered source law or a physical conclusion. It makes the missing operation explicit using an existing event law and existing probe construction. No new checker, fitted parameter or measured constant is introduced.

Choose the relationship-preservation channel for this candidate: use the existing endpoint-fixing probes. Keep directed-transition probes as a rival hypothesis, not as another normalization or a channel silently mixed into this model.

The proposed primitive task is NOT 'send the carrier point A to B'. It is 'exchange the currently prepared relationship-probe value with the retained record labelled AB'. The seed endpoints type the allowed sequence and identify the probe; they are not the carrier's evolving position.

## What is prepared

Retain the actual six seed occurrences, their endpoints and execution words. Introduce a real carrier probe field q on the 24 permutations of A,B,C,D and one real active record w_e for each primitive role e.

For e=(s,t), use the pre-existing indicator

    u_e(g)=1[g(s)=s and g(t)=t].

Use the normalized counting pairing

    <q,z> = (1/2) sum_g q(g) z(g),

so each u_e has unit norm. Prepare q=0, w_AB=1 and the other five active records zero for the discriminator experiment. This is a named, symmetry-breaking preparation; labels and preparation are transported together under relabelling.

NEW ASSUMPTIONS: this probe field is the responding carrier; this counting pairing controls the interaction; record amplitudes are meaningful jointly in the stated units; this preparation is available. The seed alone supplies none of these physical identifications. The reference relationship remains the retained pair {AB,BA}; exciting AB does not redefine the source as intrinsically oriented.

## What one comparison changes

For an explicitly requested, endpoint-compatible event labelled e, compute

    delta_e = w_e - <u_e,q>,
    q' = q + u_e delta_e,
    w'_e = w_e - delta_e,
    w'_f = w_f for f != e.

Thus <u_e,q'>=w_e and w'_e=<u_e,q>: the operation exchanges the old probe amplitude and old record value. It does not copy a state, project irreversibly, enforce equality or relax the mismatch to zero. Its own mismatch changes sign; other mismatches change through the already computed feature overlaps.

This is the old conservative exchange law, now EXPLICITLY PROPOSED for this task. It preserves the model budget <q,q>+sum_e w_e^2 and is its own inverse. That budget is not yet physical energy.

Retain a fresh execution receipt containing the primitive label and its place in the requested word. Reversing a known event word recovers the numerical state; a return word does not erase its history. Global event-generation/admission, timing and reset authority are not derived by this proposal.

## What is read

Read named record coordinates directly after the requested word. For example, prepare as above, execute AB then BA, and read w_BA. This is a declared ideal readout, not a derived measurement apparatus, probability or charge.

The operation is a relationship-record exchange, while the indicator used to couple it is a relationship-preservation query. The bridge between those two meanings is precisely the new physical hypothesis. It must be justified by a source construction or an operational realization; the shared vocabulary 'comparison' is not evidence for it.

## Rejection criteria and missing realization

Reject or revise this candidate if:

- the source-defined task is transition of a marked value rather than exchange with a relationship record;
- no admitted operation realizes the proposed field/record exchange;
- the operational pairing or preparation differs from the declared one;
- a calibrated implementation of the stated experiment disagrees with the predicted named-record transfer;
- a supposedly faithful model identifies the distinct execution/record histories merely because their carrier probes coincide.

No result here establishes physical time, energy units, particle identity, a mass ratio, a calibrated rung4 reader or the exchange law's inevitability. The next substantial evidence would be a source-derived or operationally realizable implementation of this one primitive exchange task. Until then, this is an explicit candidate to compare with the rival—not another step in a claimed derivation.
