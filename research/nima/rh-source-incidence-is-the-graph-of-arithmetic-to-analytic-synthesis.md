# Source incidence is the graph of arithmetic-to-analytic synthesis

## The incidence map is not a raw trace

The valuation/Fock carrier first produces labelled atomic data. The theta
source then sends each arithmetic label to its full tail–seam atom:

\[
Ue_n=u_n.
\]

Thus the source-derived comparison between arithmetic and analytic carriers
is the synthesis operator

\[
U:V\longrightarrow H,
\]

not an equality between atomic distributions and smooth boundary functions.
The abstract incidence cone specializes to

\[
V\oplus H
\xrightarrow{D_U}
H,
\qquad
D_U(v,h)=Uv-h.
\]

Its matched states form the graph of \(U\).

## Why the graph must retain both components

The analytic synthesis is faithful on finite packets but is not bounded below
in the unrestricted completion. Arithmetic directions can therefore have
vanishing analytic norm while retaining nonzero valuation data and anomaly
trace.

The graph carrier keeps the source packet and its analytic realization
together. Its natural norm has the form

\[
\lVert(v,Uv)\rVert_{\Gamma(U)}^2
=
\lVert v\rVert_V^2+
\lVert Uv\rVert_H^2.
\]

This does not impose an arbitrary arithmetic norm. The topology on \(V\) must
still be generated by the authorized valuation/Fock constructors. Once that
topology is supplied, the graph construction prevents analytic completion
from erasing the source label.

## The operative residual

The extra port is now concrete:

\[
r=Uv-h.
\]

It measures failure of an analytic state to be the realization of its claimed
arithmetic source packet. An operative coherencer must act on this residual and
remain continuous under cutoff completion. Merely projecting to \(h\) sets no
law on \(r\) and loses the information needed to distinguish genuine source
states from analytic lookalikes.

## Anomaly traces

Let \(\ell_1\) and \(\ell_2\) be the primitive and square traces on \(V\).
They define characters on the graph by reading its arithmetic component. They
descend to the analytic component alone only if

\[
\ker U\subseteq\ker\ell_r
\]

and the induced functionals are continuous in the analytic topology. The
smooth-Gram adjacent-label witness defeats the continuity condition even when
finite synthesis is injective.

Thus graph formation is a safe repair: it preserves exact analytic synthesis
without claiming a bounded analytic inverse. It is minimal only after its
common full target fibers have been quotiented.

## Completion gate

The completed graph exists when \(U\) is closed from the source-authorized
arithmetic topology into the analytic boundary topology. This is weaker than
requiring a bounded inverse and stronger than finite-cutoff faithfulness. The
remaining source tasks are:

1. define the arithmetic completion generated by valuation/Fock constructors;
2. prove that tail–seam synthesis is closed on that completion;
3. prove continuity of the two anomaly traces on its domain;
4. extend reciprocal dagger sewing to the graph;
5. derive a contraction or conservation law for the synthesis residual.

## Finite falsifier

A family of injective synthesis maps can have smallest singular value tending
to zero. A normalized arithmetic state then has vanishing analytic image while
its anomaly trace stays nonzero. This rejects analytic quotienting but remains
perfectly controlled in the graph norm.

## DPC verdict

The graph of arithmetic-to-analytic synthesis is a faithful prequotient
presentation. The minimal source state is its quotient by directions invisible
to the complete declared target family. The analytic tail–seam packet remains
a realization of the resulting germ, not a replacement for it. The operative
coherencer acts on the retained synthesis and sewing defects.
