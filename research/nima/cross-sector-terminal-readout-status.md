# Terminal sufficient readouts are a theorem schema with one full sector lift

For a declared linear readout family \(R:V\to W\), the quotient

\[
V_R=V/\ker R
\]

is terminal among sufficient quotients.  This universal property is exact
mathematics.  Its cross-sector physical realization requires more: the
readout family must be source-complete for the stated protocol, its kernel
must be stable under the sector transport and support calculus, and the
quotient must retain the declared composition laws.

The current typed census is:

| Sector | Strongest established status |
|---|---|
| Radiative gravity | Full source-supported sector lift: \(C^\infty(S^2)/(H_0\oplus H_1)\) |
| QED \(D_{12}\) | Exact bounded coefficient-plane control; transport-stable sector theorem not claimed |
| Cosmology | Blocked on the literal physical chain covector on the stabilized rank-26 packet |
| Flavor | Audited observable subalgebra; complete weak-basis readout family absent |
| Strings | Character invariant algebra; complete physical protocol absent |

Therefore the present cross-sector conclusion is

\[
\boxed{
\text{universal terminal-quotient schema}
+\text{ one full physical sector realization}
+\text{ one bounded algebraic control}.
}
\]

It is premature to claim a multi-sector physical law.  The sharp replication
target is cosmology because its carrier and connection packet are already
large enough; only the oppositely variant physical chain map remains missing.
Flavor and strings require protocol completion before their quotients can be
compared without artificially shrinking them.
