# qRB microstep 33: closability test

To extend the finite-span relative observer beyond `E_count`, it is enough to prove a graph estimate

$$
|W_S(u*v^*)|
\le C\,\|u\|_{\mathcal D}\,\|v\|_{\mathcal D}
$$

on a dense graph domain `D`, together with the same estimate for the regulated forms along the chosen diagonal path.

A weaker route is closability: if

$$
 u_k\to0,\qquad v_k\to v
$$

in the graph topology and `T_infty(u_k,v_k)` converges, its limit must be zero; similarly in the second variable.

The finite-span algebraic result supplies no such estimate. It is therefore incorrect to promote the diagonal observer automatically to a completed Hilbert or graph-space observer.

Status: exact analytic criterion isolated; no common bound or closability proof is currently claimed.
