# The primitive candidate is a sewn generating correspondence

## Status

Research packet. It identifies a candidate object from which the present
transport, jet, observability, and storage structures could be derived. It
does not construct the completed theta/Tate object and does not prove RH.

## Why the current finite system is not primitive

The source-native incidence audit separates the arrows:

- the direct tail generator carries forcing into the endpoint;
- Clark differentiation is an endpoint observation, not reverse dynamics;
- the adjoint Fourier–Tate sector carries the opposite endpoint-to-forcing
  arrow.

Therefore a bidirectional conservative system does not live inside either
sector. It can exist only after an explicit sewing correspondence joins the
direct and adjoint sectors. Assuming the two arrows are adjoints before that
sewing would insert the desired conservation law by hand.

The analytic-germ audit gives a parallel lesson. Arbitrary finite jet towers
are not primitive. One analytic germ port generates all finite coefficient
readouts before a zero or its multiplicity is inspected.

## Candidate object

For each finite source interval or arithmetic cutoff (X), seek a typed
two-variable generating correspondence

\[
\mathbb K_X(w,\overline v)
:
\mathcal V_X^{-}\longrightarrow\mathcal V_X^{+}
\]

together with:

1. a direct tail realization in \(\mathcal V_X^{+}\);
2. an adjoint/Fourier–Tate realization in \(\mathcal V_X^{-}\);
3. a source-derived sewing map between their boundary carriers;
4. the primitive, square, seam, and archimedean boundary incidences;
5. a common rigged topology through which all four structures extend.

The notation is deliberately operator-valued. A scalar kernel reconstructed
from the completed zeta function would fail the source-authority test.

## Its shadows

If this object exists, the structures already found should be recoverable as
different operations on the same correspondence.

### Scalar transfer

Evaluation at the distinguished boundary vectors gives the scalar transform:

\[
F_X(s)=\langle\Omega_X^{+},
\mathbb K_X(0,0)\Omega_X^{-}\rangle.
\]

This makes a zero a silent matrix coefficient, not disappearance of the
correspondence.

### Complete jet port

Coefficient extraction gives every finite jet:

\[
\partial_w^m\mathbb K_X(0,0).
\]

Multiplicity is then order of tangency of one scalar boundary matrix
coefficient. It does not require adding a new state constructor after the
zero is seen.

### State realization and directional incidence

A source-derived factorization of \(\mathbb K_X\) should produce the direct and
adjoint state carriers. The two off-diagonal incidence maps remain distinct
until the sewing map relates them. Their equality or adjointness is a theorem
of the correspondence, not a typing convention.

### Relationship energy

The diagonal polarized part should yield the observability Gramian, while
interval concatenation should yield

\[
W_{ac}=W_{ab}+S_{ab}^{*}W_{bc}S_{ab}.
\]

The boundary incidences similarly generate (B). The difference (W-B)
is the storage cocycle whose exactness is the Green-current problem.

### Reciprocal sewing

Fourier–Tate reflection should exchange the two variables and the two sector
carriers. The critical seam is the locus where this sewing is unitary. Away
from it, the direct and reciprocal realizations form a hyperbolic pair rather
than one conservative system.

## Feedback interpretation

The completed object is therefore closer to a feedback closure than to a
single Hamiltonian:

\[
\text{direct causal tail}
\longrightarrow
\text{boundary}
\longrightarrow
\text{reciprocal adjoint tail}
\longrightarrow
\text{sewn return}.
\]

The scalar zeta transform is one closed-loop transmission coefficient. A zero
is a closed-loop cancellation. RH would require a source law showing that an
admissible cancellation can occur only on the unitary sewing locus.

## Why this is deeper than the control realization

The control system is a realization of the correspondence after choosing
state coordinates. Different realizations can have the same scalar transfer.
The generating correspondence retains:

- both directional sectors;
- the whole analytic germ;
- boundary incidence typing;
- path energy before scalar projection;
- and the topology required for completion.

It is therefore a candidate invariant object beneath the realization, not
another coordinate model around the scalar function.

## Source-authority gates

The candidate is useful only if all of the following are derived forward from
labelled theta/Tate data:

1. the two-variable kernel or correspondence;
2. its direct and reciprocal factorizations;
3. the sewing map;
4. all boundary-current incidences;
5. the common radius and coefficient topology;
6. the cutoff bonding maps;
7. the dynamic storage identity or its finite obstruction.

Dividing by the completed zeta function, inserting its zeros, or polarizing a
known RH-equivalent scalar kernel would make the construction circular.

## Finite falsifiers

The programme fails at the first cutoff where any of these occurs:

- the direct and reciprocal factorizations do not share the declared boundary
  carrier;
- sewing does not intertwine the two source realizations;
- a current incidence cannot act on the correspondence domain;
- coefficient extraction lacks one cutoff-independent radius/topology;
- the Gramian and boundary supply fail their transported cocycle laws;
- a closed identity-transport loop has nonzero (W-B);
- hostile signed prime data preserve the proposed correspondence law.

## Deepest current statement

The present evidence points below both the scalar zeta function and the
finite control realization. The primitive candidate is a boundary-bearing,
two-sector analytic correspondence. Its scalar transfer, jet hierarchy,
state realization, and relationship energy are shadows. The missing RH
content is whether source-derived sewing makes its storage cocycle exact only
on the unitary seam.
