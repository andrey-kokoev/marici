# Relationship promotion produces growing incidence graphs

## Rule and input

Start with four addressed records and the twelve directed packets of the
previous tetrahedral example. Pair reciprocal packets into six undirected edges.
Each promoted edge-record retains both directed packets and both endpoint IDs.
Two promoted records become adjacent precisely when their previous edges share
an endpoint; retain that endpoint as the new adjacency's witness.

Repeat on the new graph. This is the line-graph operation with retained ancestry.
It is an explicitly supplied generative rule, separate from the existing
normalization/grouping compiler. The executable checker implements it directly.

## Exact finite execution

| Promotions | Current record-vertices | Undirected relationships | Directed packet slots | Degree |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 4 | 6 | 12 | 3 |
| 1 | 6 | 12 | 24 | 4 |
| 2 | 12 | 36 | 72 | 6 |
| 3 | 36 | 180 | 360 | 10 |
| 4 | 180 | 1620 | 3240 | 18 |

The first promoted graph is octahedral: six vertices whose three missing pairs
are disjoint opposite-vertex pairs. This is checked from the generated incidence
rather than assumed from the edge count.

For this regular seed, one promotion satisfies V'=E, d'=2(d-1), E'=V'd'/2.
Hence d at promotion k is 2+2^k. Degree increases strictly, so the graphs cannot
repeat up to graph isomorphism. The general incidence count implemented in the
checker is E'=sum_v choose(degree(v),2).

Each stage's records point into a persistent ancestry archive. Decoding their
endpoint fields reconstructs the whole preceding edge set exactly. The first
promotion also reconstructs all twelve original directed packets. New adjacency
witnesses are checked against intersections of those retained endpoint sets.

## Controls and interpretation

A triangle seed keeps three vertices and three edges through four promotions.
Two disjoint edges produce two records with no new link. These controls show
that growth depends on incidence and the shared-endpoint rule, not merely on
recording another layer of history.

The observed growth is in explicitly constructed graph incidence. Since the
construction is deterministic from the seed and rule, it does not demonstrate
an increase in independent information or establish physical particle dynamics.
No minimality claim among all possible growing seeds is made. Simplicial face
fillers and geometric distances are not generated by this graph checker.
