# Generic rank-six fibre degree and chart covariance of the sixfold contour

The local two-sheet fibre contour is **not confined to the specially framed `Y₀` target**. For positive rank-six eight-point moment-curve external data (equivalently the nine-point data with physical twistor 3 inserted), take a generic non-`Y₀` target `Y=CZ` from the sourced positive four-pair cell. Every other source matrix representing the same target plane can be row-gauge-normalized to `C+TK`, where `K` is a `2×8` basis of the left kernel of `Z` and `T` is `2×2`. Put `q=det(T)`.

The four defining parallel-pair minors `(1,2),(3,4),(5,6),(7,8)` of `C+TK` are **linear in the four entries of `T` and in `q`**, because their only quadratic contribution is `det(K_{ij})q`. If the `4×4` coefficient matrix is invertible, solve `T=T₀+qT₁`; the consistency equation `q=det(T₀+qT₁)` is **quadratic**. At two independent exact generic non-`Y₀` target witnesses its leading coefficient and discriminant are nonzero, with two distinct regular rational inverse sheets. Therefore the source map is generically degree two on the corresponding open chart; extra unseen source sheets cannot be postulated there. This is a local algebraic-degree statement, not global image coverage.

Both oriented local complex fibre residues `source_density/J₈` were recomputed directly in a **non-`Y₀` `Gr(2,6)` chart**. Their sum is nonzero and differs from the positive-source-sheet residue alone. Under the explicit determinant-one six-dimensional change of external frame taking the target to `Y₀`, the `Gr(2,6)` eight-coordinate Jacobian changes by the **inverse sixth power of the chart-frame determinant**. The checker verifies this weight-six rule separately on both source sheets and verifies that the complete two-sheet trace transforms accordingly, exactly reproducing the independently computed `Y₀` coefficient. This rules out interpreting the two-sheet trace as an artifact of that special frame.

The same zero-column embedding `D₉Z₉=D₈Z₈` and `D₉χ₉=D₈χ₈` carries the full fermionic numerator into nine-point variables. Neither the algebraic degree nor chart covariance chooses the positive canonical six-dimensional integration cycle of the full image.
