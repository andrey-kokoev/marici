# The generic p-normal corner has no Cayley–Menger endpoint faces

At the marked-wall collision \(p=x+y+3z=0\), the restricted Cayley–Menger
coefficient is generically nonzero:

\[
K_{\rm CM}|_{p=0}\propto
64(x-2y)^2(x+y)^2(2x-y)^2.
\]

On the open stratum where this expression is inverted, the source contour
boundary \(K_{\rm CM}=0\) is absent. Consequently the endpoint/face
subcomplex required by the relative \(p\)-normal audit is not an unknown
nonzero complex:

\[
E_{\rm CM}=0.
\]

This zero complex is canonical and differential-stable. The Cayley–Menger
current remains an interior coefficient/current on this stratum.

The existing weighted chart `y=u^2*t` resolves the different degeneration
where \(A=B\) at its chart variable \(u=0\). No source packet identifies that
\(u\) with the incidence normal \(p\). Importing its exceptional faces would
therefore conflate the all-soft Rees stratum with the generic marked-wall
corner.

This removes the Cayley–Menger endpoint gate but does not construct the
marked-wall Čech/bulk–face differential, the generic circuit subcomplex, a
lift \(dH_p=p\Xi_p\), or a Bockstein.
