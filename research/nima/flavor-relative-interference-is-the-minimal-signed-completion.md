# Flavor relative interference is the minimal signed completion

## Exact quotient problem

Let two source couplings be nonzero real numbers \(g_\phi\) and \(g_\psi\). Simultaneous sign reversal is treated as the admitted global convention:

\[
(g_\phi,g_\psi)
\sim
(-g_\phi,-g_\psi).
\]

Separate partial widths retain only

\[
\Gamma_\phi=g_\phi^2,
\qquad
\Gamma_\psi=g_\psi^2.
\]

Every fixed rate pair contains two inequivalent classes modulo the global sign: equal relative sign and opposite relative sign. No function of the separate rates can distinguish them.

The signed cross-sector channel is

\[
I=g_\phi g_\psi.
\]

It is invariant under simultaneous global sign and changes under either individual sign reversal. It satisfies

\[
I^2=\Gamma_\phi\Gamma_\psi.
\]

The square relation proves compatibility with the sector magnitudes but does not make the sign reconstructible from them.

## Boundary-reference realization

\[

\qquad
S_-=B-2\nu I,
\]

\[
I=\frac{S_+-S_-}{4\nu}.
\]

Their sum is \(2B\) and contains no interference. Summing the ports before retaining their labels therefore destroys the relationship channel. Zero visibility does the same.
