# Many-many order-exchange face curvature

For restriction directions `i,j`, define the face-curvature defect

$$
\Omega_{ij}
=
 r_i(\beta_j)\beta_i
-
 r_j(\beta_i)\beta_j.
$$

Strict coherence is the vanishing condition

$$
\Omega_{ij}=0.
$$

When the defect is nonzero, retain `Omega_ij` as a typed relative 2-cell in the collision-aware carrier. Its boundary is the difference between the two order-exchange routes, so it measures failure of strict interchange while preserving both constructions.

For three directions, the defects must satisfy the Bianchi relation

$$
D_k\Omega_{ij}-D_j\Omega_{ik}+D_i\Omega_{jk}=0,
$$

with the appropriate transported differentials. This is the first higher filler condition beyond square coherence.
