# Many-many canonical trace-class pair lift

After choosing a Hilbert completion `H_ar` of the arithmetic source, use the projective pair carrier as a trace-class source:

$$
\mathfrak S_1(H_{\rm ar})
\cong
H_{\rm ar}\widehat\otimes_\pi\overline{H_{\rm ar}}.
$$

For the original locally convex source, retain the projective tensor carrier until a Hilbert completion has been source-authorized.

The canonical lift is the rank-one operator map

$$
\Lambda(f\otimes\overline g)=|f\rangle\langle g|.
$$

This is basis-independent. Diagonal source states become positive rank-one densities

$$
\Lambda(f\otimes\overline f)=|f\rangle\langle f|,
$$

while polarized matrix units retain ordered cross-pair data.

A pair response is a continuous linear functional or operator-valued map on `S_1(U_ar)`:

$$
\mathscr B_{\rm rad}(|f\rangle\langle g|)=\rho_{f,g}^{\rm graph}.
$$

The trace-class projective norm supplies the canonical continuity topology. Reciprocal exchange is adjoint: `A -> A^*`.
