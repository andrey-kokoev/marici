# Do the existing witnesses decide reference flatness?

## Verdict

**Not on the carrier endpoint graph.** The audited witnesses prove fixed-domain
anchor coherence and supply higher comparison fillers. They do not supply the
source edge transports needed to decide carrier reference holonomy. This is a
type/adapter gap, not evidence that a physical connection is flat or curved.

The active obligation is source typing before attachment transport or readout
descent. The audit preserves the declared objects and degrees of the existing
shared-leg and reanchoring constructions.

## What the witnesses actually establish

There are171 anchors: the direct reference,137 slot comparisons,32 retained
family anchors and the total mean. Each anchor C_i is a degree-zero map A->B.
Its comparison witness h_i has degree one and satisfies

    boundary(h_i)=C_i-d.

The canonical change from anchor i to anchor j uses h_j-h_i. These differences
telescope around every anchor-label loop. The checker tests all171 triangles
through the reference and total anchor. Reanchoring and returning also restores
the current reference/unit/triangle payload exactly, while keeping parent
history. This is exact coherence of the declared reference-coordinate operation.

The two shared-leg routes to an individual comparison have a different status.
They agree strictly for28 slots. For the other109, their difference is nonzero
but equals the boundary of the existing degree-two rectangle filler. Thus those
routes agree through retained higher data, not as identical witness expressions.
Neither distinction is erased by calling all of them simply 'flat'.

## Why this does not calculate carrier holonomy

The witness construction has four object types A,U,V,B and one degree-zero
return B->A. The carrier fixture has32 endpoint labels. Its reference connection
would require a supplied transport for ordered carrier edges/routes, with
specified endpoint spaces and composition.

The comparison h_i:A->B is a degree-one witness between maps. It is not itself
a degree-zero invertible transport between carrier vertices. Likewise, a
rectangle filler is not automatically the holonomy of a carrier loop. A
noninjective or higher adapter could relate these constructions, but its object,
degree, route and observation assignments must be given and checked.

The conditional global-frame construction supplies one possible attachment and
is flat by construction. Its invertible twist supplies a nonflat control. The
existing fixed-domain witnesses do not select either attachment. Their exact
anchor-loop telescoping cannot be transferred to carrier loops without the
missing adapter.

## Existing programme result, not a new flatness principle

The earlier [route-calibration result](nonflat-calibration-is-route-structure-not-endpoint-data.md)
already separates endpoint descent from retained-route transport. Its finite
phase model admits both flat and curved connections, but does not instantiate
the present carrier generator. The [programme index](reference-coherence-and-route-effects-programme-index.md)
explicitly requires source-defined incidence, ordered routes and a calibrated
observation before attributing route effects to physics.

This audit therefore returns to that source-instantiation gate rather than
adding another abstract connection example.

## Required adapter contract

To decide the question, supply:

1. the source operation associated with each elementary carrier edge;
2. its port spaces, reference attachment and transport degree;
3. the ordered-composition rule, including any existing higher cells;
4. the map from those routes into the fixed-domain witness construction;
5. the rung4 observation, retaining route identity until its admissible descent
   has been established.

Only then evaluate generating loops. Strict identity supports endpoint descent;
a supplied filler supports the corresponding weak comparison; a nontrivial
source-derived loop response must remain route data. A zero mean alone cannot
settle which case holds.

The source operation is still unspecified in the audited carrier generator.
Flatness should not be chosen as a default physical conclusion, and curvature
should not be introduced retrospectively to explain a discrepancy.
