# The twelfth theta quarter-turn rotates port gauge into arithmetic curvature

## Ordered-port correction

Every fiber of the arithmetic connection must now be a framed colligation
\((\mathfrak C_X,\mathfrak f_X)\), where \(\mathfrak f_X=(b_{0,X},b_{f,X})\).
State transport without ordered-flag transport is not an admissible theta
connection. All curvature and homotopy cells must intertwine the bordered
transmission pencils, not merely the unframed full Weyl systems.

Source-facing categorical DPC. The local operator orbit closed at the eleventh
turn, but only up to unitary gauges on its incoming and outgoing defect ports.
The twelfth turn varies the complete local colligation over the arithmetic
constructor category. Port gauges become transport data; incompatibility of
prime-addition paths becomes curvature.

Strict flatness is not required. The correct target is source-authorized
homotopy flatness, with higher coherence on cubes of constructor paths.

## Arithmetic base

Let \(\mathcal P\) be the typed category whose objects are finite authorized
prime or valuation cutoffs and whose generating arrows adjoin one admitted
arithmetic constructor. Write

\[
X\xrightarrow{p}Xp
\]

for adjoining the constructor labelled by \(p\).

At each object \(X\), retain the complete local colligation fiber

\[
\mathfrak C_X
=
\left(
\mathfrak f_X,
\mathcal K_X,
A_X,
\mathcal D_X^{\rm in},
\mathcal D_X^{\rm out},
\mathcal J_X,
\Theta_X,
\mathcal R_X
\right).
\]

Here \(\mathcal R_X\) is the typed residue and boundary filtration.

## The twelfth quarter-turn

For every admitted arrow \(X\to Y\), require bonding maps

\[
V_{X,Y}^{\mathcal K}:
\mathcal K_X\longrightarrow\mathcal K_Y,

\[

\mathcal D_X^{\rm in}\longrightarrow\mathcal D_Y^{\rm in},

They must intertwine the internal dynamics, defect incidence, boundary

The turn is therefore

\[
\left(

## Prime transport square

For two independently admissible constructors \(p\) and \(q\), compare

\[
X\xrightarrow{p}Xp\xrightarrow{q}Xpq
\]

and

\[
X\xrightarrow{q}Xq\xrightarrow{p}Xpq.
\]

On any one typed component, the strict curvature residual is

\[
\Omega_{p,q;X}
=
V_{Xp,Xpq}^{q}V_{X,Xp}^{p}
-
V_{Xq,Xpq}^{p}V_{X,Xq}^{q}.
\]

Strict flatness would require

\[
\Omega_{p,q;X}=0.
\]

That is stronger than the current cross-sector evidence warrants.

## Homotopy-flat correction

Suppose the fiber carries a source-derived complex with differential \(d_X\)
and an already authorized labelled relation map. Homotopy flatness requires a
source-labelled degree-minus-one cell \(h_{p,q;X}\) satisfying

\[
\Omega_{p,q;X}
=
d_{Xpq}h_{p,q;X}
+
h_{p,q;X}d_X.
\]

More generally, the residual may factor through the derivative image of the
authorized relation object. What matters is that the factorization itself is
source-derived and natural. A fitted row-space section is not a coherence
cell.

This typing matches the emerging amplitude-side result: mixed Euler
commutators can be nonzero as raw maps while vanishing modulo the derivative
image of the complete labelled relation map.

## Higher coherence

For three constructors \(p,q,r\), the six boundary squares of the transport
cube carry homotopies. Their alternating composite must itself vanish or be
filled by an authorized higher cell.

Schematically, the next obstruction is

\[
\delta h_{p,q,r;X}.
\]

If this class is nonzero in the quotient by already authorized relations, the
connection has genuine arithmetic curvature. If it is exact, the primitive
must again be source-labelled rather than selected from a noncanonical
complement.

Thus the completion is naturally a pseudofunctor or higher representation of
the constructor category, not necessarily a strict functor.

## Why this may distinguish hostile factors

A hostile Blaschke factor can imitate every local analytic property:

- causal Hardy support;
- boundary unitarity;
- positive delay;
- finite model-space positivity;
- minimal conservative realization;
- and local characteristic-function closure.

But a separately inserted factor must also transport through every prime and
prime-power constructor with the correct port incidence and higher coherence.
Pointwise admissibility does not provide those cells.

The possible RH-selective law is therefore no longer local positivity. It is
global arithmetic naturality of the complete colligation bundle.

## Exact source obligations

At each finite cutoff, the theta/Tate construction must supply:

1. the local colligation fiber \(\mathfrak C_X\);
2. the state and port bonding maps for every admitted constructor;
3. intertwining with \(A_X\), \(\mathcal J_X\), and \(\Theta_X\);
4. typed changes in primitive, square, seam, archimedean, and residue channels;
5. a named homotopy for every non-strict transport square;
6. coherent fillers for the alternating boundaries of those homotopies;
7. continuity of the entire pseudofunctor under restricted-product
   completion.

No scalar functional equation can substitute for these maps.

## Finite falsifiers

The first decisive falsifier needs only two constructors and one typed vector:

\[
v\in\mathfrak C_X,
\qquad
\Omega_{p,q;X}v
\notin
\operatorname{Im}d_{Xpq}
+
\operatorname{Im}R_{Xpq}.
\]

The programme also fails at finite level if:

- the residual is exact only through a noncanonical row-space section;
- state transport commutes while one defect-port transport does not;
- the characteristic functions agree only after determinant projection;
- a square has a homotopy but the first transport cube has a nonfillable
  alternating boundary;
- or the required homotopy norms diverge under completion.

## Relation to rotation closure

The local orbit closed because all forgotten local fibers were restored. The
twelfth turn asks whether these closed fibers assemble over arithmetic source
variation. New objects appear again because global coherence is additional
data not contained in any one fiber.

This does not contradict local periodicity. It changes dimension:

\[
\mathfrak C_{\rm loc}
\longrightarrow
\nabla_{\mathcal P}.
\]

In mathematical symbols,

\[
\mathfrak C
\longrightarrow
\left(\mathfrak C_X,V_{X,Y},h_{p,q;X},\ldots\right).
\]

## Decisive conclusion

Further rotation is worthwhile only at this higher level. The local passive
system is complete and nonselective. Rotating its boundary gauge across the
prime-constructor base yields a source-testable higher connection. RH-bearing
information could reside in the existence and completion stability of its
arithmetic coherence cells. A single nonexact prime transport square or
nonfillable cube closes that possibility.
