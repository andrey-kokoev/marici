# qRB microstep 37: uniformity input gap

The scalar gate

$$
\sup_P\Lambda_P<\infty
$$

cannot presently be evaluated because the research record does not specify, in one common notation, the completed comparison norm `\|\cdot\|_*` and the full finite-packet matrices for the relative readout.

Finite fixtures use different coordinates and are not evidence for a regulator-uniform bound.

The next real calculation therefore requires a normalized packet schema containing:

1. one common basis;
2. the source and observer Gram matrices;
3. the signed cross-readout matrix;
4. regulator inclusion maps;
5. the exact comparison norm.

Until that schema is assembled, claiming a numerical or analytic bound on `\Lambda_P` would be unjustified.
