# The wave-trace frontier is a sector-completeness lattice

## Correction of research motion

The recent wave-trace work has been moving through the same lattice already isolated by Kitaev:

1. carrier splitting;
2. action or controllability splitting;
3. observation or joint-faithfulness splitting;
4. estimate or completion splitting.

The apparent sequence of new RH obstructions is largely a traversal of these four gates at increasing assembly scales. The right move is to expose the product lattice and walk its first failed cell, not continue generating scalar reformulations.

## Assembly-scale axis

The second axis has four relevant levels:

1. one prime;
2. a finite labelled prime cutoff;
3. the infinite arithmetic restricted product;
4. reciprocal and archimedean reconciliation.

The research state is therefore a grid, not a single chain.

## Why the next cell is action, not observation

Kakutani singularity proves that the arithmetic Poisson product state and Haar product state define disjoint global representations. The carrier split is therefore established.

The raw tensor product of local prime dilations fails implementability. Consequently there is no authorized global action on one vacuum tensor product to which a global observer could yet be applied.

Constructing a scalar adelic functional before constructing the cross-sector action would skip a cell. It could agree with the Euler scalar while erasing the representation-change anomaly.

## The required noncentral correspondence

Let \(\mathcal A\) be the source algebra, with disjoint GNS representations

\[
(\pi_{\mathrm{arith}},\mathcal H_{\mathrm{arith}})
\qquad
(\pi_{0},\mathcal H_{0}).
\]

The next constructor must be a source-derived correspondence or closed relation \(\mathcal E\) joining these sectors. It must not identify them. Its finite restrictions must recover the local Poisson/Haar comparison and its square-grade anomaly.

The minimum contract is:

1. left and right source actions on \(\mathcal E\);
2. finite-prime restriction maps;
3. compatibility with local Sz.-Nagy dilations;
4. a typed square-current cocycle measuring representation change;
5. reciprocal transport to the opposite valuation cone;
6. a declared domain for the later trace functional;
7. a finite falsifier for any claimed bounded intertwiner.

Because the GNS sectors are disjoint, an ordinary nonzero bounded intertwiner is already excluded. The constructor must genuinely be a correspondence, unbounded relation, or relative modular object.

## Reconciliation-pyramid reading

At each assembly scale, the pyramid has:

- two sector objects;
- one comparison object joining them;
- an observer of the comparison;
- a completion law preserving that observation.

Moving upward in assembly scale without moving laterally through comparison, observation, and estimate creates the repeated pattern we have been encountering. Conversely, attempting the global observer before the global comparison smuggles scalar completion across a missing action cell.

## Ordered traversal

The next research order is fixed:

1. construct or falsify the global cross-GNS correspondence;
2. construct the fixed adelic functional on that correspondence;
3. verify adjoint doubling and the extended archimedean kernel;
4. prove closability and weighted scale-energy estimates;
5. only then compare the resulting real-support wave trace with the completed determinant section.

The first decisive outcome should be one of:

- a source-derived correspondence with a typed square anomaly;
- proof that only an unbounded or distributional relation can exist;
- proof that the local dilation family has no coherent global correspondence of the required type.

## Immediate falsifier

Any proposal that places all local dilations in one ordinary vacuum tensor product is rejected by the divergent vacuum-displacement sum and by Kakutani singularity. Any proposal that jumps directly to a scalar adelic trace is rejected by the missing action cell and by Kitaev's scalar-equality seam-erasure hostile.
