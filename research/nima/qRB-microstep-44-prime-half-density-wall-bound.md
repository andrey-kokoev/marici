# qRB microstep 44: prime half-density wall bound

Prior research supplies a noncircular analytic bound on the broken-H1 wall carrier:

$$
\sum_p p^{-1}|f(\log p)_{\rm avg}|^2
\le C\|f\|_{\rm wall}^2.
$$

Together with the jump estimate

$$
\|([f]_{\log p})_p\|_{\ell^2}\le\|f\|_{\rm wall},
$$

Cauchy–Schwarz gives continuity of the wall linking form

$$
|\omega_{\rm wall}(f,g)|
\le C\|f\|_{\rm wall}\|g\|_{\rm wall}.
$$

This is precisely the missing type of estimate for the relative boundary interface: the divergent common prime mode is not made Hilbert-bounded, but the half-density-weighted wall pairing is continuous on the correct graph carrier.
