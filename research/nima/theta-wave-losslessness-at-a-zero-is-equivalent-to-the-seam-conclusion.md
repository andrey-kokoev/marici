# Theta wave losslessness at a zero is equivalent to the seam conclusion

## Status

Exact no-go theorem for the local scattering reformulation. The impedance and
wave-port identities remain useful architecture, but imposing equal incoming
and outgoing flux on a scalar zero-state merely restates the desired critical
line conclusion. The noncircular target is a source-derived,
spectral-parameter-independent maximal Lagrangian boundary domain.

## Universal wave identity

For every forced tail equation

\[
G'=-zG-fc,
\]

the impedance variables

\[
u=c,
\qquad
y=-\overline fG
\]

and Cayley waves

\[
a_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{u+y}{\sqrt2},
\qquad
a_{\mathrm{out}}=\frac{u-y}{\sqrt2}
\]

satisfy

\[
\partial_q|G|^2
+
2\operatorname{Re}z\,|G|^2
=
|a_{\mathrm{in}}|^2-|a_{\mathrm{out}}|^2.
\]

This construction uses no arithmetic property of \(f\). Every hostile forcing
has the same local port identity.

## Integrated equivalence

For a two-ended state satisfying

\[
G(0)=G(\infty)=0,
\]

integration gives

\[
\Phi_{\mathrm{in}}-\Phi_{\mathrm{out}}
=
2\operatorname{Re}z\,\|G\|_{L^2}^2,
\]

where

\[
\Phi_{\mathrm{in}}
=
\int_0^\infty|a_{\mathrm{in}}|^2dq
\]

and

\[
\Phi_{\mathrm{out}}
=
\int_0^\infty|a_{\mathrm{out}}|^2dq.
\]

For a nonzero tail state,

\[
\Phi_{\mathrm{in}}=\Phi_{\mathrm{out}}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Re}z=0.
\]

Therefore a theorem asserting losslessness only on scalar zero-states is
logically equivalent to the desired seam confinement. It cannot serve as an
independent explanation.

\[

Let \(\mathcal K_X\) be the finite source state space with Green boundary form
\(\Omega_X\). A boundary domain \(\mathcal L_X\) must be constructed from the
source before the spectral parameter or scalar zeros are inspected.

The required conditions are

\[
\Omega_X|_{\mathcal L_X}=0
\]

and maximality:

\[
\dim\mathcal L_X
=
\frac12\dim\mathcal K_X

These conditions make \(\mathcal L_X\) a Lagrangian boundary relation. With
the corresponding adjointness and completion theorems, the source generator

## Verdict

The wave-port reformulation identifies the correct boundary variables but
does not prove RH. Equal wave flux at a zero is exactly the seam conclusion in
different coordinates. The next legitimate route is stronger and more
geometric: derive a fixed maximal Lagrangian boundary relation from the
completed theta/Tate source, prove its operator selfadjoint, and only then
identify its characteristic determinant with the completed scalar section.
