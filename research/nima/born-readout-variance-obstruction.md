# Born readout requires conjugate doubling

## Minimal obstruction

Let \(A\) be an amplitude. A Born weight satisfies

\[
q(zA)=|z|^2q(A),
\qquad
q(iA)=q(A).
\]

A complex-linear map \(L\) would instead satisfy \(L(iA)=iL(A)\). Therefore
phase invariance and complex linearity force

\[
(i-1)L(A)=0,
\]

so a nonzero Born readout cannot be another complex-linear amplitude counit.

The minimal typed domain is

\[
\mathcal A\otimes\overline{\mathcal A},
\]

followed by positive evaluation against detector effects and normalization by
the total accepted rate. The first map \(A\mapsto A\otimes\bar A\) is
quadratic on amplitude vectors; normalization is projective/rational.

## Cut compatibility

If a physical amplitude Cut is a strict map \(C\), its density-level lift is

\[
C\otimes\bar C.
\]

Functorial composition is automatic:

\[
(D\otimes\bar D)(C\otimes\bar C)
=
(DC)\otimes\overline{DC}.
\]

Thus Entry 45's strict Cut result removes one potential coherence obstruction,
but it does not supply the conjugate object, positive cone, analyzer effects,
accepted phase-space support, or nonzero trace denominator.
