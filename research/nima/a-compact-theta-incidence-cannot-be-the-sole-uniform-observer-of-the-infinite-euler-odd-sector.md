# A compact theta incidence cannot be the sole uniform observer of the infinite Euler odd sector

## Result

If the Euler-to-theta incidence is retained as the source-supported compact forward correspondence, then no bounded analytic observer downstream of it can be uniformly coercive on the full infinite Euler odd source.

Therefore the theta/Wronskian/seam-normal route may identify every finite prime coordinate, but it cannot supply the global odd lower margin by itself.

A complementary arithmetic observer, a restricted source, or a change to the theta-control object is mathematically necessary.

## Compact factorization

Let

\[
H_E
\]

be the infinite Euler odd source and

\[
S_\theta:H_E\to H_\theta
\]

the theta-sampling incidence. In normalized prime coordinates,

\[
S_\theta e_p=\eta_pe_p,
\qquad
\eta_p\to0.
\]

Hence \(S_\theta\) is compact.

Let

\[
A:H_\theta\to Y
\]

be any bounded downstream constructor, such as:

- Jacobi heat propagation;
- derivative-comb contraction;
- Wronskian readout;
- seam-normal readout;
- a finite direct sum of these bounded ports.

The completed observer is

\[
J=AS_\theta.
\]

Since bounded maps preserve compactness under composition,

\[
J:H_E\to Y
\]

is compact.

## Coercivity no-go

Suppose there were a uniform lower bound

\[
\|Jx\|
\ge
\delta\|x\|_E
\qquad
(x\in H_E)
\]

with \(\delta>0\).

Then \(J\) would be bounded below. Its range would be closed and \(J^{-1}\) would be bounded on that range.

But a compact operator bounded below on an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space would map the unit ball to a relatively compact set containing a uniformly separated image of an infinite orthonormal sequence. This is impossible.

Equivalently, for the normalized prime vectors,

\[
\|Je_p\|
\le
\|A\||\eta_p|
\longrightarrow0.
\]

Therefore

\[
\inf_{\|x\|_E=1}\|Jx\|=0.
\]

No choice of bounded analytic postprocessing can repair this.

## Finite cutoffs

At every finite cutoff \(X\), the restricted observer

\[
J_X
\]

may be injective and have a positive smallest singular value. However,

\[
\sigma_{\min}(J_X)
\le
\|A\|
\min_{p\le X}|\eta_p|.
\]

As the cutoff grows through new primes,

\[
\sigma_{\min}(J_X)\to0.
\]

Thus finite faithfulness and finite positivity coexist with mandatory completion collapse.

This is exactly the hostile pattern that the earlier margin taxonomy was designed to expose.

## What the theta route still proves

Compactness does not make the route useless. It proves:

- exact primewise incidence;
- orientation preservation;
- strict finite faithfulness;
- Schatten or determinant-class regularity;
- a canonical analytic realization of each arithmetic odd coordinate;
- compatibility of Wronskian and seam-normal sign frames.

What it cannot prove is stable inverse observability of the entire Euler source.

## Complementary arithmetic port

Let

\[
J_E:H_E\to Y_E
\]

be an upstream arithmetic observer that does not factor through \(S_\theta\), for example the retained reciprocal Euler or bilateral valuation port.

The joint observer is

\[
J_{\mathrm{joint}}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
J_E\\
AS_\theta
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

A positive lower bound is possible only if

\[
J_E^*J_E
+
S_\theta^*A^*AS_\theta
\ge
\delta^2I.
\]

Since the second term is compact, the essential lower bound must come from \(J_E^*J_E\). The theta term may repair finitely many defects or supply orientation calibration, but it cannot lift the essential spectrum away from zero.

This is the exact operator form of the categorical distinction:

\[
\text{arithmetic observability}
\quad\text{versus}\quad
\text{analytic source realization}.
\]

## Relation to global margins

The odd theta incidence should not be folded into a global coercivity margin as though it observed the full Euler source.

Instead:

- the arithmetic plane margin must control the infinite prime-labelled odd sector;
- the theta incidence certifies constructor coherence and analytic realization;
- the seam-normal/Wronskian ports certify oriented compatibility on the image;
- mixed cancellation must be tested only after these roles are separated.

Otherwise one asks a compact source-realization arrow to perform an impossible essential-coercivity task.

## Three legitimate resolutions

### Upstream arithmetic coercivity

Retain a noncompact, source-metric-faithful Euler observer and use theta sampling only as a compact comparison certificate.

### Theta-control domain

Declare the theta pullback completion to be the actual domain of the downstream Adams edge. Then the incidence is isometric, but this edge no longer has the full Euler completion as its source.

### Restricted Euler source

Admit only an inverse-weighted subspace on which \(S_\theta\) is bounded below. This requires new source authority and excludes general Euler source vectors.

No fourth resolution can be obtained by bounded analytic postprocessing.
