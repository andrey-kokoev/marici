# Eight-level simplex / many-many dictionary

The many-many resolution admits the following provisional simplex filtration:

| Level | Simplex role | Analytic content |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | source vertices | prime, grade, shell, pair labels |
| 1 | source edges | Gaussian pair responses and affine/transport refinements |
| 2 | response faces | Laplace recurrence and endpoint/Wronskian maps |
| 3 | coherence squares | Hadamard four-block reconstruction and reciprocal exchange |
| 4 | collision faces | multiplicative rectangle cycles and label-retained defects |
| 5 | quotient faces | labelwise radical descent and physical codiagonal |
| 6 | completion faces | projective graph closure, loading, and tail control |
| 7 | terminal filler | theta–Green mapping cone, Xi characteristic, and spectral constraint |

The face maps are refinement, reciprocal exchange, shell concatenation,
Hadamard transport, codiagonal projection, and projective restriction.

The radial flux identity occupies the transition between Levels 2 and 3: it
converts the pair-response face into the ordered wall/jump polarization. The
mapping-cone contraction occupies the terminal filler level.

This is a structural dictionary. A formal simplex identification requires explicit objects, face maps, degeneracies, and filler equations for every level.
