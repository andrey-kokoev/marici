# qRB microstep 110: uniform form-bound route

A common operator bound for finite `J_alpha` is stronger than necessary. It is enough to prove a uniform sesquilinear estimate on the dense core:

$$
|\langle J_\alpha u,v\rangle|
\le C\|u\|_{\rm orb}\|v\|_{\rm orb}
$$

with one `C` independent of `alpha`.

The bounded-form theorem then produces a unique bounded limit form, and core convergence identifies its Riesz operator with `J_link`.

This is the natural target for the relative construction because the finite stages are defined by trace-class cross-readouts, not necessarily by uniformly bounded operators on the raw carrier.
