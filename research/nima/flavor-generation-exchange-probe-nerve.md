# Flavor generation-exchange probe nerve

## Reconstruction gate

Ledger 2015 asks whether the complete authorized probe family distinguishes
objects that terminal physical readouts intentionally identify.  The smallest
certified flavor fiber is a generation-exchange doublet.

Figueiredo's WP24c establishes that 72 of the 73 diagonal nine-link textures
carry such a partner over the same physical16 point.  The remaining texture is
the declared six-edge oddball on which the involution is undefined.  Of the 72
partners, 35 were stored directly and 37 were independently recovered.

## Two probes

On a doublet \(\{s,Ts\}\), every physical quotient readout is deck-even.  Its
minimal matrix is

\[
R_{\rm phys}=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \operatorname{rank}R_{\rm phys}=1.
\]

The source-defined generation-exchange involution supplies the complementary
anti-invariant probe \(1-T\), defined up to an irrelevant overall sign.  The
combined probe nerve is

\[
R_{\rm nerve}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1\\
1&-1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det R_{\rm nerve}=-2,
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}R_{\rm nerve}=2.
\]

No desired separator was fitted.  The odd probe is generated by the deck
involution that WP24c derived independently from generation exchange.

## Interpretation

The result separates two meanings of faithfulness:

- physical readout correctly identifies both sheets as one quotient point;
- the internal probe nerve retains enough information to distinguish and
  transport their chart presentations.

Thus readout nonfaithfulness is intentional, while the source-indexed
interface remains jointly faithful on this finite fiber.

This is the smallest positive test of categorical self-realization:

\[
\boxed{
\text{even physical probe} + \text{source-derived odd deck probe}
\;\text{separates the lens fiber}.
}
\]

It does not prove global faithfulness of the flavor groupoid.  Larger fibers,
non-involutive transitions, and morphism separation remain open.
