# qRB microstep 94: support-rung restriction compatibility

Let `L_1 <= L_2`, and let `f,g` be supported in `[-L_1,L_1]`. Every prime-power translation with

$$
\log n>2L_1
$$

has zero correlation with `f,g`. Therefore the larger-rung prime sum contributes no additional term:

$$
Q_{L_2}(f,g)=Q_{L_1}(f,g).
$$

The endpoint and gamma terms are also intrinsic to the same zero-extended functions, so they agree under the support-rung inclusion.

Thus the explicit source identity defines a compatible projective family on finite support rungs.

Status: support restriction compatibility closed on the compact core; projective completion beyond compact support remains separate.
