# Many-many Xi transfer symmetry

The forced transfer

$$
H(s)=\frac{2\xi(s)-1}{s(s-1)}
$$

inherits the completed Xi functional equation. Since

$$
\xi(1-s)=\xi(s),
\qquad
(1-s)(-s)=s(s-1),
$$

one has

$$
H(1-s)=H(s).
$$

For real `s` away from the removable endpoint points, `H(s)` is real. On the critical line `s=1/2+it`, the transfer is even in `t` under conjugation:

$$
H\left(\frac12-it\right)=\overline{H\left(\frac12+it\right)}.
$$

Thus the radial characteristic channel has the required reciprocal and Hermitian boundary symmetry.

Status: transfer symmetry established from Xi symmetry.
