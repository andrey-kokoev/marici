# Theta jet-number energy is a Bargmann Gram, not a local dilation form

## Status

Exact correction to the Euler-dilation proposal. Euler dilation identifies the
jet grading, but the positive Fock number form is not the quadratic form of
ordinary dilation on the source (L^2(dq)) space. Pulling it back through the

This distinction blocks a false positivity proof and gives a finite-cutoff

## Bargmann realization of the jet tower

Let

\[
g_m(z)=\partial_z^mG(z),
\qquad
h_m(z)=\frac{g_m(z)}{\sqrt{m!}}.
\]

The associated vector-valued Bargmann function is

\[
\Psi_z(\tau)
=\sum_{m\geq0}h_m(z)\frac{\tau^m}{\sqrt{m!}}
=\sum_{m\geq0}g_m(z)\frac{\tau^m}{m!}
=G(z+\tau).
\]

On this Bargmann module,

\[
N=\tau\partial_\tau
\]

and its positive quadratic form is

\[
\langle\Psi_z,N\Psi_z\rangle_{\mathcal F}
=\sum_{m\geq0}\frac{m}{m!}\lVert g_m(z)\rVert^2.
\]

The Euler operator therefore identifies the degree, while the Bargmann inner
product supplies positivity. These are two pieces of structure, not one.

## Exact pullback to the source

Suppose the tail transform has the schematic source form

\[
G(z)=\int_0^\infty F(q)e^{zq}\,dq.
\]

Then

\[
g_m(z)=\int_0^\infty q^mF(q)e^{zq}\,dq.
\]

For a scalar source, expanding the number form and summing the exponential
series gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal E_N(z)
&=\sum_{m\geq0}\frac{m}{m!}|g_m(z)|^2\\
&=\int_0^\infty\!\int_0^\infty
F(q)\overline{F(r)}e^{zq+\bar z r}
qr e^{qr}\,dq\,dr.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus the pulled-back number kernel is

\[
K_N(q,r)=qr e^{qr}.
\]

For Hilbert-valued source amplitudes, the scalar product of the two amplitudes
occupies the same position in the double integral.

Positivity is immediate from the jet expansion whenever interchange of sum and
integral is justified. It is not immediate from the pointwise sign of the
kernel for complex or vector-valued sources, and convergence is a genuine
domain condition.

## Why ordinary source dilation is insufficient

On polynomial probes,

\[
E_q=q\partial_q,
\qquad
E_q(q^m)=mq^m.
\]

This explains the grading. But on ordinary (L^2(dq)), integration by parts
gives the formal adjoint

\[
E_q^*=-q\partial_q-I,
\]

subject to the boundary pairing

\[
[qf(q)\overline{g(q)}]_0^\infty.
\]

Consequently (E_q) is not a positive source operator, and its local graph
norm is not automatically the pullback of the Bargmann number form. A proof
that merely inserts (q\partial_q) into the existing (q)-flow energy does not
construct (N).

The required constructor is instead the transform-level correspondence

\[
F
\longmapsto
\Psi_z
\longmapsto
N^{1/2}\Psi_z,
\]

together with its domain, boundary current, and reciprocal-sector action.

## Arithmetic transport defect

\[

\]

\[
[E_q,S_n]=-(\log n)S_n\partial_q.
\]

But this commutator records only how the grading changes under translation. It
does not prove that (S_n) acts boundedly on the Bargmann number domain. That
requires domination of the translated two-source kernel, uniformly over the
authorized arithmetic constructor family.

This separates two gates:

1. the algebraic commutator gate for the jet grading;
