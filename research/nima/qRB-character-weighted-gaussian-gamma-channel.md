# qRB character-weighted Gaussian: gamma channel

For the Hermitian real part of the centered character probe,

$$
\operatorname{Re}h_{t,d}(u)=e^{-tu^2}\cos(du).
$$

The archimedean contribution is therefore

$$
K_{\rm gamma}(t,d)
=-\frac{\log\pi}{4\sqrt{\pi t}}e^{-d^2/(4t)}
+\frac1{4\pi}\int_{\mathbb R}
 e^{-tu^2}\cos(du)\operatorname{Re}\psi(1/4+iu/2)\,du.
$$

Together with

$$
K_{\rm endpoint}(t,d)=e^{t/4}\cosh(d/2)
$$

and the shifted logarithmic-prime Gaussian from the preceding microstep, this gives the complete real character-weighted Gaussian target.

The formula keeps the same Fourier normalization as the compact source identity. It is a target formula, not yet an equality with the relative linking operator.
