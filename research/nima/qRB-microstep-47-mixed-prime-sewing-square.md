# qRB microstep 47: mixed prime-sewing square

For each prime, reciprocal transport is not an identity arrow. With

$$
E_p^+(s)=(1-p^{-s})^{-1},
\qquad
E_p^-(s)=(1-p^{s-1})^{-1},
$$

its comparison cell is

$$
\gamma_p(s)=\frac{1-p^{-s}}{1-p^{s-1}},
$$

and exact naturality is

$$
d\log E_p^- -d\log E_p^+=d\log\gamma_p.
$$

This is the analytic prime/prime-prime sewing square. The two families can be assembled coherently only after inserting `gamma_p`; identifying them by the identity loses the polarity comparison.

The infinite family additionally requires local uniform convergence of the normalized comparison cells and compatibility with archimedean completion.
