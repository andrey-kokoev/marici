# The finite Euler second moment is a global max-kernel, not an Euler product

The normalized finite Euler exponential has an exact quadratic form under the
Cauchy observer.

Let \(\mathcal N_X\) be the multiplicative semigroup generated by a finite
prime set \(X\), and write

\[
Y_X(T)
=
e^{L_X(T)}
=
\sum_{n\in\mathcal N_X} n^{-1/2}X_{\log n}(T).
\]

Using

\[
\mathbb E\!\left[
X_{\log n}\overline{X_{\log m}}
\right]
=
e^{-\frac12|\log(n/m)|},
\]

one obtains

\[
\mathbb E|Y_X|^2
=
\sum_{n,m\in\mathcal N_X}
\frac{1}{\sqrt{nm}}
e^{-\frac12|\log(n/m)|}
=
\sum_{n,m\in\mathcal N_X}\frac1{\max(n,m)}.
\]

If

\[
A_X=\mathbb E Y_X
=\sum_{n\in\mathcal N_X}\frac1n
=\prod_{p\in X}(1-p^{-1})^{-1},
\]

then the mean-one normalization \(Z_X=Y_X/A_X\) satisfies

\[
\mathbb E|Z_X|^2
=
\frac{1}{A_X^2}
\sum_{n,m\in\mathcal N_X}\frac1{\max(n,m)}.
\]

Equivalently, with the smooth-number counting function

\[
\Psi_X(u)=\#\{m\in\mathcal N_X:m\le u\},
\]

the numerator is

\[
\sum_{n\in\mathcal N_X}\frac{2\Psi_X(n)-1}{n}.
\]

This is the first exact nonlinear Green quantity. Unlike the first moment, it
does not factor primewise. The kernel \(1/\max(n,m)\) compares the total
ordered magnitudes of two composite labels, coupling all primes through the
Archimedean order.

For one prime \(X=\{p\}\), putting \(r=p^{-1}\) gives

\[
\sum_{a,b\ge0}r^{\max(a,b)}
=
\frac{1+r}{(1-r)^2},
\]

and hence

\[
\mathbb E|Z_{\{p\}}|^2=1+p^{-1}.
\]

For two or more primes, coordinatewise valuation maxima do not determine
\(\max(n,m)\); cross-prime order is genuine. Thus the quadratic completion
cannot be assembled as an orthogonal product of local prime Grams.

The completion question is now an arithmetic counting theorem:

\[
\sup_X
\frac{
\sum_{n,m\in\mathcal N_X}1/\max(n,m)
}{
\left(\sum_{n\in\mathcal N_X}1/n\right)^2
}
<\infty?
\]

A uniform bound would give \(L^2\) control of the normalized nonlinear Euler
states. Divergence would prove that the Cauchy Hilbert carrier cannot survive
Euler completion without the theta/archimedean Schur return.

This ratio is not yet the Xi Loewner kernel, but it is the exact finite
quadratic interface that any source-derived endpoint transport must preserve.
It also supplies the first nontrivial cross-prime term demanded by constructor
coherence.

The sharp hostile is a model replacing \(1/\max(n,m)\) by the coordinatewise
Euler product kernel

\[
\prod_{p\in X}p^{-\max(v_p(n),v_p(m))}.
\]

That kernel factors beautifully but is not the Cauchy covariance unless the
labels lie in a single prime ray. It silently deletes the Archimedean ordering
interaction.
