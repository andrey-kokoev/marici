## Positive clue without overclaim

The end-to-end trace map IS a source-dependent preparation in the supplied shared-leg fixture. Changing its source matrices changes the instrument preparation and predicted readout. It demonstrates how a fully specified preparation adapter can be tested, and its provenance/gain controls can be reused.

However, treating an edge deletion as a particular change to those matrices would be a NEW law. The 137-slot fixture and six-occurrence seed have different typed domains. Its success is not an implementation of co-constructed seam preparation.

The trace adapter also has a known closure limit: a selective exchange can raise the comparison block rank outside the image of the original 2x2 shared-leg products. The retained correction records solve storage, not source selection of independently writable memory. This prevents circularly declaring the exchange output to be the next native matrix preparation without checking its image.

## Why running the exchange longer does not solve preparation

Every event is homogeneous and reversible; zero input remains zero. Its conserved anchor sector prevents unique convergence of arbitrary states to one complete prepared state. A specified random schedule can contract the mean while moving budget into covariance, but this is not autonomous selection of the input distribution or physical scale.

These are existing model constraints, not a theorem forbidding preparation by some other admitted open/control operation. Such an operation is precisely the missing input.

## Disposition

No inspected prior rule supplies the requested whole-six-arrow preparation and its edge-deletion behavior. Operational hypothesis v1 remains a CONDITIONAL LOCAL INSTRUMENT model. It should no longer be described as a seed-derived explanation of the two-triangle response merely because its pulse words use the seed labels.

To reopen that explanatory claim, propose or locate a preparation mechanism with:

1. the complete retained seed relationship as its input;
2. a defined state output, including scale/covariance or an explicitly specified preparation family;
3. a source-defined response to deletion or change of an indirect relation;
4. the SAME independently fixed surviving interaction/readout for comparison.

Such a mechanism must be stated as a new hypothesis if not sourced. No parameter, matrix assignment, environment-dependent metric or ensemble was chosen during this search.
