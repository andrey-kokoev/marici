# Many-many reciprocal sector decomposition

For each unordered pair `{n,m}`, decompose the ordered coordinates into

$$
Y_{nm}^{+}=\frac12(Y_{nm}+Y_{mn}),
\qquad
Y_{nm}^{-}=\frac12(Y_{nm}-Y_{mn}).
$$

The symmetric codiagonal sees the `+` sector. The reciprocal orientation and forcing asymmetry are carried by the `-` sector together with the coefficient difference

$$
\alpha_{nm}-\alpha_{mn}
=\frac{m^2-n^2}{n^2+m^2}.
$$

Thus the scalar output should be assembled from the symmetric sector while the antisymmetric sector remains as a typed relative boundary coordinate.

Discarding the `-` sector before the boundary pairing erases orientation data. Quotienting it is valid only after proving that it lies in the readout radical.
