# Many-many projective intertwiner compatibility

For finite families `F ⊂ G`, let `r^theta_G,F` and `r^wall_G,F` be the source-labelled restriction maps. The theta-to-wall intertwiners form a projective natural transformation when

$$
 r^{\rm wall}_{\mathcal G,\mathcal F}I_{\mathcal G}
=I_{\mathcal F}r^{\theta}_{\mathcal G,\mathcal F}.
$$

Together with

$$
I_{\mathcal F}C^\theta_{\mathcal F}
=C^{\rm wall}_{\mathcal F}I_{\mathcal F},
$$

this yields a well-defined map on compatible projective families:

$$
I_\infty:\mathcal E^\theta_\infty\to\mathcal E^{\rm wall}_\infty.
$$

The finite maps use the same Hadamard frame, odd compression, and source factor `-2` on every labelled coordinate. Their compatibility is therefore reduced to compatibility of the source-labelled restrictions.
