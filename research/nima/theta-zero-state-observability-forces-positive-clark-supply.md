## Abstract theorem

Let \(C\) be the scalar endpoint readout and let \(J\) be the retained Clark
observation map. Define the scalar zero-state class

\[
\mathcal Z=\{x:Cx=0\}.
\]

Suppose \(J\) is faithful on that class:

\[
\ker J\cap\mathcal Z=\{0\}.
\]

The positive observation supply is

\[
\mathcal Q_J(x)=\lVert Jx\rVert^2.
\]

For every nonzero \(x\in\mathcal Z\), faithfulness gives

\[
\mathcal Q_J(x)>0.
\]

Consequently the two requirements

\[
Cx=0
\]

and

\[
\mathcal Q_J(x)=0
\]

are incompatible for a nonzero zero-state whenever \(J\) is faithful.
