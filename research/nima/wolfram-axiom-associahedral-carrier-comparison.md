# Wolfram’s axiom and the associahedral Marici carrier

## Status and provenance

This note records the operator-supplied structural proposal: the recognizable comparison is with an associahedral/Catalan coherence complex above the reduced eight-point carrier, rather than directly with \(L(M_8)\).

The Catalan counts and ordered leaf patterns below are direct combinatorial observations. The Marici counts and classifications are reported from the supplied research references; they have not been independently rerun for this note. The displayed maps are proposed comparisons, not yet constructed chain maps, functors, or equivalences. The external references are supplied sources, not independently verified citations in this documentation update.

## 1. The seven-leaf Catalan sector

Wolfram’s axiom is

\[
((p\mid q)\mid r)\mid\bigl(p\mid((p\mid r)\mid p)\bigr)=r.
\]

Its left-hand side has six binary NAND operations and seven leaf occurrences. Forgetting the variable labels, the space of all planar full binary tree shapes on seven ordered leaves has size

\[
C_6=132.
\]

These shapes correspond to the 132 triangulations of an octagon. The axiom itself specifies one such shape, not all 132 shapes or proof states.

The supplied source describes the proof operations as tree rewrites generated by substitution and bisubstitution:

- [MathWorld: Wolfram Axiom](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/WolframAxiom.html).

The reported eight-point Marici carrier independently has

\[
132=96+32+4
\]

scalar triangulations projecting to 12 quadrangulations.

Thus the relevant candidate comparison is with the full Catalan sector above the reduced carrier. Equality of the counts alone does not identify rewrite edges, cell boundaries, or quotient fibers.

For the usual associahedral rotation/flip complex, the two-dimensional faces have exactly two forms:

\[
\text{independent rotations}\longrightarrow\square,
\]

\[
\text{adjacent interacting rotations}\longrightarrow\text{pentagon}.
\]

This matches the reported Marici rank-two carrier classification.

The match concerns associahedral rotations. It does not by itself establish that all critical overlaps of Wolfram substitution or bisubstitution have only these forms. NAND is not associative, so an arbitrary tree rotation is not automatically a valid Boolean rewrite. ## 3. Proposed factorization through tree shapes

The natural candidate is

\[
\text{Wolfram binary rewrite state}
\longrightarrow
\text{binary-tree shape}
\longrightarrow
\text{octagon triangulation}
\longrightarrow
\text{Marici core quotient}
\longrightarrow
L(M_8).
\]

The first two arrows describe a combinatorial assignment for individual seven-leaf expressions once the planar leaf order is fixed. Extending the assignment to rewrites and higher cells, and constructing the Marici quotient arrows, are separate obligations.

This refines the earlier hypothesis: the recognizable candidate structure is the **associahedron above the reduced carrier**, not primarily \(L(M_8)\). The reported reduced eight-point carrier forgets much of the 132-state tree information.

Here \(M_8\) and \(L(M_8)\) retain the supplied carrier notation. No algebraic quotient denoted \(M_8/L(M_8)\) is defined in this note; the intended claim is a reduction from the associahedral sector to the reported Marici carriers.

## 5. Variable arity and the larger rewrite system

Substitution can change expression size. The complete automated proof therefore cannot be confined to a single 132-vertex associahedron. The proposed picture uses associahedral sectors of different arities together with transitions induced by actual substitutions.

Supplied external reference:

- [Stephen Wolfram, “Who Can Understand the Proof? A Window on Formalized Mathematics”](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2025/01/who-can-understand-the-proof-a-window-on-formalized-mathematics/).

The proposed structural comparison is schematically

\[
C_{\mathrm{Wolfram}}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname*{colim}_{n}K_n
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{Marici}_{\mathrm{Bool}},
\]

where \(K_n\) denotes the associahedral complex indexed here by the number of leaves. The colimit notation is provisional: the arity-changing maps and indexing diagram must be supplied. A family of associahedra alone does not define that colimit, and substitution transitions need not form a simple sequential diagram.

With this leaf-count convention, the seven-leaf/eight-polygon-vertex slice is

\[
K_7,\qquad \#\operatorname{Vert}(K_7)=C_6=132.
\]

Its proposed Marici reduction contains the reported carrier

\[
L(M_8):\qquad12\text{ states},\quad24\text{ flips}.
\]

Other conventions index associahedra by dimension or polygon size; the convention here is explicitly by leaf count.

## 6. Disposition and next checks

The proposal is tighter than a direct conjecture \(C_{\mathrm{Wolfram}}\to L(M_8)\): it separates the full tree-shape sector, ordered Boolean labels, actual proof moves, and the reduced carrier.

To promote the proposal to a structural result:

1. Resolve the three internal references and reproduce the 132-to-12 projection and the 24 reported flips.
2. Specify the image of each actual rewrite step; test whether it gives an edge, a path, or an arity-changing transition.
3. Verify square and pentagon boundaries under that assignment, without assuming all Boolean rewrite overlaps are associahedral.
4. Define the carrier quotient and test compatibility with its flips and higher cells.
5. Supply the variable-arity diagram and the labelled specialization comparison.

No executable audit, labelled Boolean equivalence, or functor on the full proof system is claimed by this note.
