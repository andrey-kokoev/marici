# The independent Xi-divisible bordered defect does not imply the relative-Haar energy cycle

## Newly closed statement

The independent two-history crossing gives

\[
\Delta_{\rm border}(z)=\tau(z)H_{\rm border}(z),
\qquad
H_{\rm border}\not\equiv0.
\]

Therefore every Xi zero, with multiplicity, annihilates the transverse analytic
bordered defect. This establishes that the Evans criterion is independent of
the universally vanishing canonical Stokes defect.

## Distinct Hermitian statement

Critical-line confinement requires the local relative-Haar identity

\[
\delta_p(z)
=
\bigl(1-p^{-2\operatorname{Re}z}\bigr)
E_p(b_z)=0
\]

on the noncollapsed Xi state, with

\[
E_p(b_z)>0.
\]

The bordered identity is complex-linear and holomorphic in `z`. The energy
identity is Hermitian, depends on both `z` and `bar z`, and compares a global
reciprocal state with a local valuation dilation. No linear functional of

\[
\Delta_{\rm border}(z)=0
\]

can produce the positive factor `1-p^(-2 Re z)` without an additional
metric-compatible localization theorem.

## Exact missing implication

One would need an independently proved map

\[
\mathfrak L_p:
\text{global Evans bordered carrier}
\longrightarrow
\text{local relative-Haar carrier}
\]

such that, on the Xi state,

\[
\|\mathfrak L_p^-b_z\|^2
-
\|\mathfrak L_p^+b_z\|^2
=
\bigl(1-p^{-2\operatorname{Re}z}\bigr)E_p(b_z),
\]

and such that vanishing of the independent bordered defect forces equality of
these two local norms. Global reciprocal neutrality alone does not provide
this primewise factorization.

## Equivalence boundary

For every fixed prime `p>1`, positivity gives

\[
\delta_p(z)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Re}z=0.
\]

Thus proving the missing implication at even one prime for every Xi zero is
already equivalent to critical-line confinement. It is not a remaining
continuity, rigging, normalization, G4-authority, or coherence lemma.

## Disposition

The analytic independent-Evans programme now reaches its terminal boundary:
Xi divisibility and nonidentity are proved, while promotion to the positive
relative-Haar cycle is RH-strength. Proceeding requires new arithmetic input
establishing primewise metric localization; it cannot be obtained by further
formal manipulation of `T_PB`, `K_1`, or canonical Stokes cancellation.
