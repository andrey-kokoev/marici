# qRB character target: second translation derivative

At `d=0`, the second derivative separates the three channels:

$$
\partial_d^2K_{\rm endpoint}(t,0)=\frac14e^{t/4}.
$$

For the gamma channel,

$$
\partial_d^2K_{\rm gamma}(t,0)
=-\frac1{4\pi}\int_{\mathbb R}u^2e^{-tu^2}\operatorname{Re}\psi(1/4+iu/2)\,du
+\frac{\log\pi}{8t\sqrt{\pi t}}.
$$

For the prime channel,

$$
\partial_d^2K_{\rm prime}(t,0)
=-\frac1{2\sqrt{\pi t}}
\sum_{n\ge2}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}
 e^{-(\log n)^2/(4t)}
\left(\frac{(\log n)^2}{4t^2}-\frac1{2t}\right).
$$

This is the first nontrivial even curvature test of the translate-Gram target. It must be compared with the second derivative of the separately placed relative linking block.
