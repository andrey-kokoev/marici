# Collective triplet has two CP-conjugate phase orbits

## Why this branch

The two-doublet D4 programme failed its DPC. The symmetry that forbids the fatal mixed quartic also restores generalized CP. The first surviving architecture must use collective rather than pairwise phase information.

A known source of calculable collective phases is a complex triplet transforming under \(\Delta(27)\) or \(\Delta(54)\). Primary work derives geometrical CP phases in renormalizable three-field potentials, while a separate classification shows that generalized CP for \(\Delta(27)\) is a finite, nontrivial consistency problem rather than bare conjugation alone: [Geometrical CP Violation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.5477), [Generalized CP symmetries in Delta(27) flavor models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.0877).

## Exact phase-lattice gate

Restrict equal-magnitude triplet components to cubic-root phases

\[
v_i=\omega^{q_i},
\qquad
q_i\in\mathbb Z_3.
\]

The collective renormalizable phase term has angular part

\[
E(q)=
\sum_{i=1}^{3}
\cos\left(2\theta_i-\theta_j-\theta_k\right).
\]

The checker evaluates all 27 labelled phase assignments exactly, using twice the cosine so every value is integral.

The minimum contains 18 assignments. Under the triplet action generated by cyclic permutation, the diagonal \(\mathbb Z_3\) phase, and the center, these minima split into two disjoint orbits of size nine.

Bare CP negates every phase exponent and exchanges the two orbits. In particular,

\[
(1,0,0)
\longmapsto
(2,0,0),
\]

and the two representatives are not related by an internal \(\Delta(27)\) transformation.

## What this establishes

The collective phase term has three properties absent from the failed D4 pair:

- the phase is discrete and coefficient-independent within the selected branch;
- CP conjugation exchanges whole vacuum orbits rather than moving within one orbit;
- the phase relation is carried collectively by three components.

This passes the internal flavor-orbit gate.

## What remains unproved

Internal orbit separation is not yet spontaneous physical CP violation. The cited generalized-CP classification finds a finite family of consistent antiunitary extensions of \(\Delta(27)\). The next checker must instantiate every generalized CP admitted by the complete scalar source and test whether any maps a chosen minimum back to its own flavor orbit.

The existing Marici Flavor source also does not contain this complex triplet. Its triplicated messenger sector is multiplicity of messenger fields, not authority for a \(\Delta(27)\) triplet order parameter. Importing this construction would require new field, representation, potential, portal, anomaly, and completion packets.

## Revised DPC

A collective Flavor construction passes only if:

1. its complete degree-four invariant ring has the two-orbit minimum structure;
2. every source-compatible generalized CP is broken by the selected orbit;
3. the portal maps the orbit distinction to a nonzero physical CP invariant;
4. the complex triplet and its symmetry arise from independently admitted field content;
5. radiative and threshold completion preserve the orbit separation.

The present packet passes only the exact phase-orbit subtest.
