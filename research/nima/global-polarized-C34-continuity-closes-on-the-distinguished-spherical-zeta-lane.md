# Global polarized C34 continuity closes on the distinguished spherical zeta lane

## Question

Do character summation and spectral growth obstruct the polarized
principal-value readout for the distinguished completed-zeta source?

## Source restriction

The distinguished source uses the trivial spherical finite component at every
prime. It does not excite nontrivial finite angular characters. Consequently
the general `sum_chi` in the `C34` formula restricts to the trivial spherical
fiber on this lane. No uncontrolled character-family sum remains.

Conductor restriction is also compatible with the existing relative feature:
its projections commute with the Tate/reference projections and with the
common translation. The unramified vacuum is fixed under these restrictions.

## Spectral continuity

On the boundary line, the logarithmic derivative symbol has at most logarithmic
or fixed polynomial growth on compactly regular vertical sectors. The forcing
multiplier `m_Phi` is Schwartz. Therefore

\[
f(t)=\overline{m_\Phi(t)}a(t)\partial_t\log\gamma(t)
\]

is a Schwartz test function whenever the pole numerator `a` has the admitted
compact-local growth.

The maps

\[
f\longmapsto
\operatorname{pv}\!\int\frac{f(t)}{t-t_0}\,dt,
\qquad
f\longmapsto f(t_0)
\]

are continuous tempered-distribution functionals. Hence both ordered boundary
values `q_+` and `q_-`, and therefore their wall and jump combinations, are
continuous on the polarized forcing--pole packet.

The endpoint row is finite dimensional and adds no completion obstruction.
Prime-labelled forcing assembly already has a superexponential majorant for
the source-derived theta coefficient, so its compact-local jets commute with
prime exhaustion.

## Strength boundary

This closes global continuity and cutoff/conductor compatibility on the
single distinguished spherical lane. It does not establish uniform continuity
for arbitrary ramified character families, nor does it turn the signed
relative readout into a positive norm.

Most importantly, continuity does not prove that the resulting Tate current
equals the independently retained pair-state Green energy.

## Disposition

The polarized `C34` readout is now globally well defined on the distinguished
zeta lane, with wall, jump, endpoint, and prime-exhaustion compatibility. The
next gate is packet comparison: align this signed Tate boundary packet with the
retained positive-plus-skew Green packet before testing their scalar balance.
