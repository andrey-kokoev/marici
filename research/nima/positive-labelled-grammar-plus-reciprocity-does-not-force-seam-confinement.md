# Positive labelled grammar plus reciprocity does not force seam confinement

Author: `marici.Nima`

## Candidate law

After the combined completion-law no-go, the strongest elementary
source-local candidate is that positive discrete contribution grammar,
together with reciprocal completion, might force the outer-normalized defect
to vanish.

This packet tests that implication only. It does not claim to reproduce the
full theta/Euler constructor repertoire.

## Minimum hostile

Use the unit circle as the seam and its inside/outside as the reciprocal open
sectors. For \(c>2\), define

\[
 P_c(z)=z^2+cz+1.
\]

The three labelled coefficients are strictly positive. The polynomial is
exactly reciprocal:

\[
 P_c(z)=z^2P_c(1/z).
\]

On the seam, write \(z=e^{it}\). Then

\[
 P_c(e^{it})=e^{it}(c+2\cos t).
\]

Since \(c>2\), this never vanishes on the seam.

However,

\[
 P_c(0)=1,
 \qquad
 P_c(-1)=2-c<0.
\]

Hence one real zero lies in \((-1,0)\), strictly inside the seam. The product
of the two roots is one, so the other zero is its reciprocal and lies strictly
outside. The hostile therefore has one reciprocal off-seam pair.

For the exact fixture \(c=3\),

\[
 P_3(z)=z^2+3z+1.
\]

No numerical root inspection is needed: positivity, reciprocity, seam
nonvanishing, and the off-seam pair follow from the preceding exact signs.

## Consequence

The conjunction of these properties is insufficient:

- finitely supported labelled contributions;
- strictly positive scalar weights;
- exact reciprocal functional equation;
- no seam zero introduced by the completion law.

They do not imply that the open-sector inner factor is trivial. Equivalently,
they do not force the ordered-cut Hankel defect to vanish.

## Scope correction

The positive-Fock theorem remains valuable arithmetic provenance. It fixes
which prime-power contributions exist and prevents signed or type-erased local
weights. This finite hostile does not imitate the full Euler product or the
theta archimedean source.

What it disproves is the inference from positive labelled grammar and
reciprocity alone to global seam confinement. Any surviving theorem must use
additional relations specific to the complete endpoint, gamma, prime, seam,
and infinity incidence—not merely the positivity of their labels.

## Finite falsifier for future laws

Every proposed label-sensitive orientation law should first be applied to
\(P_3\). If it accepts the three positive labels and reciprocal sewing while
remaining blind to the inside/outside root pair, it adds no information beyond
the already closed grammar and symmetry layers.

## Verdict

Positive constructibility controls local contribution signs; reciprocal
completion controls paired placement. Neither controls the global interaction
needed to eliminate the rectangular inner defect. The missing operation must
couple labels in a way that distinguishes \(P_3\) from the actual completed
theta source before scalar zero inspection.
