# Many-many interior–endpoint coupling

With the endpoint sector retained independently, the global relative form has block structure

$$
\mathfrak Q
=\begin{pmatrix}
Q_{\rm int}&L^*\\
L&Q_{\rm end}
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Here `Q_int` is the interior many-many relative form, `Q_end` is the endpoint graph form, and `L` is the boundary coupling map. The coupling is the correct location for endpoint-flux and Wronskian data.

The minimal analytic requirements are:

1. `L` is continuous from the interior graph to the endpoint sector;
2. `L` respects prime and shell refinement;
3. the finite regulated block is well-defined;
4. the relative cross-readout agrees with the source identity;
5. endpoint projective limits are preserved.

This block form permits positive carrier norms with signed off-diagonal observations. A positive Schur complement is an optional stronger property.
