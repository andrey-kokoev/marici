# Extension-admission census

## Question

The corrected Deutsch--Popperian conjecture distinguishes two legitimate
ways to enlarge a calculus:

\[
U=\text{conservative/universal extension},
\qquad
P=\text{independently source-provenanced content}.
\]

It rejects

\[
F=\text{fitted or post-hoc amplification of physical content}
\]

and leaves unresolved authority as \(?\).  The first falsifier is
retrospective but predeclared: classify representative extensions by their
provenance and readout behavior, then inspect their outcomes.

## Result

The bounded census contains eighteen claim atoms:

\[
\#U=3,
\qquad
\#P=6,
\qquad
\#F=7,
\qquad
\#?=2.
\]

Mixed historical objects are split by claim.  For example, adjoining a formal
Koszul cell is a universal formal completion, while identifying that same cell
with a pre-existing physical \(Q\)-cell is fitted content amplification.
Likewise the primitive Verdier quotient is a valid diagnostic, while erasing
the physical \(A_2\) contact sector is not.

Within this census:

\[
\boxed{
\text{every established physical success is route }P,
\qquad
\text{no route }F\text{ attempt is a physical success}.}
\]

The result is evidence, not a universal theorem.  It is vulnerable to one
clean counterexample: an established physical readout whose new content was
neither independently source-provenanced nor the readout-silent part of a
conservative/universal extension.

## Deeper structure

The census supports an authority-monotonicity separation:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{operation}&\text{admission}\
\hline
\text{restriction/quotient}&
\text{internal when the discarded kernel is invariant}\\
\text{conservative re-expression}&
\text{universal property or equivalence}\\
\text{new physical readout}&
\text{source provenance or independent prediction}.
\end{array}
\]

An obstruction is therefore a typed request for additional structure, not an
authority token authorizing whichever structure removes it.

## Next prospective test

Retrospective agreement is susceptible to classification bias.  The next new
extension proposal should have its route frozen before its outcome is known.
The record must state:

1. whether it claims new governed readout;
2. its universal property or source provenance;
3. its forgetting image;
4. its independent prediction;
5. the result that would reject admission even if the motivating obstruction
   disappears.

Only that prospective test can substantially raise confidence in the rule.
