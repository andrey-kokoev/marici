# Physical Distinguishability Belongs to a Jointly Faithful Probe Family

A single physical readout \(q\) defines an equivalence relation through its
fibers.  Distinctions inside one fiber are invisible to that readout, but need
not be invisible to every legal experiment.

For a source-admissible family \(\{q_i\}\), the relevant local condition is

\[
\bigcap_i\ker(dq_i)=0.
\]

This is joint faithfulness: no nonzero tangent direction is erased by every
probe.

In the exact two-coordinate model, the diagonal probe

\[
R(x,y)=x^2+y^2
\]

kills the phase tangent at \((1,1)\).  Adding one framed protocol port
\(P(x,y)=x\) makes the stacked Jacobian invertible.  The phase direction is
therefore invisible diagonally but locally distinguishable by the augmented
probe family.

This supplies the operational Yoneda correction to Entry 2114: a sector
object should be compared through its complete source-generated legal probe
profile.  One quotient readout is only a projection of that profile.

The word **legal** remains load-bearing.  Arbitrarily invented probes would
make almost any hidden coordinate observable.  The sector source must derive
the probe, its framing, and its comparison map before it contributes to
physical distinguishability.
