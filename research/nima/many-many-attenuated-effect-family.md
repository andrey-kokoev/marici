# Many-many attenuated effect family

With bounded detector coupler `C` and shell attenuation `V_t`, define the detected effect

$$
E_{\rm det}(t)=V_t^*C^*CV_t.
$$

The complementary attenuation loss is

$$
E_{\rm loss}(t)=I-V_t^*V_t.
$$

The pair `(E_det(t), E_loss(t))` retains both detected and undetected outcomes. For a passive coupler satisfying `C^*C <= I`, the total effect obeys

$$
E_{\rm det}(t)+E_{\rm loss}(t)\le I.
$$

At finite cutoff this is an ordinary normalized effect construction. At infinite cutoff, the bounded row estimate for `C` and contractivity of `V_t` provide continuity in the direct-sum mode carrier.

The countable shell coherencer uses the weighted family `alpha_n E_det(t_n)` together with the corresponding loss records. This gives a bounded joint observation record while preserving the collision-sector information.
