# Many-many antisymmetric coupling candidate

For an ordered pair response, define the candidate oriented endpoint coupling

$$
L_{-,nm}(z)
=\frac{m^2-n^2}{n^2+m^2}
\left(
\rho_{nm}(0)+\kappa_{nm}\partial_zR_{nm}(z)
\right).
$$

It obeys the reciprocal sign law

$$
L_{-,mn}(z)=-L_{-,nm}(z),
$$

because `kappa_nm=kappa_mn` while the coefficient difference changes sign.

This candidate preserves the pair endpoint and flux coordinates and is compatible with reciprocal orientation. Its continuity and agreement with the source wall Wronskian remain to be proved.
