# Higher-coherence topology iteration 39: central Hilbert-module topology would force fiberwise closure, but the Xi cycle law is only scalar-valued

## Candidate topology

Let the coefficient algebra retain prime labels centrally, for example

\[
A=c_0(\mathbb P)
\quad\text{or a weighted projective Köthe analogue},
\]

and form a Hilbert `A`-module

\[
\mathcal H_A=\bigoplus_p^A H_p.
\]

Its inner product is prime-coordinate valued:

\[
\langle x,y\rangle_A
=(\langle x_p,y_p\rangle_{H_p})_p.
\]

The central idempotent/cutoff coordinates preserve local energies before any
scalar trace or codiagonal.

## Fiberwise residual

The reciprocal mismatch becomes the central element

\[
R_A(z)
=
\left(
(1-p^{-2\operatorname{Re}z})E_p(b_{p,z})
\right)_p.
\]

If a source theorem established

\[
R_A(z)=0
\]

in the module, then every coordinate would vanish. Noncollapse of one prime
would force `Re(z)=0`. No adelic compensation is possible because the prime
fibers are orthogonal central coordinates.

This is stronger and cleaner than a scalar product formula.

## Current typing gap

The constructed Xi/Evans cycle law is scalar or globally bordered. It does not
yet take values in the central prime module. Applying a scalar codiagonal

\[
A\to\mathbb C
\]

before testing closure permits cancellation and cannot be inverted.

Thus the desired result is a vector-valued refinement of the cycle law, not a
new norm on the already scalar identity.

## Hilbert-margin obstruction

The raw theta-to-labelled-prime incidence has coefficients tending to zero. On
the unweighted labelled Hilbert direct sum it is injective but not bounded
below and has nonclosed range. Hence one cannot simply identify the global Xi
state with a uniformly faithful Hilbert-module vector.

The projective exponential Köthe source has a continuous inverse on its
source-generated range, but its geometry is not an ordinary Hilbert module.
A viable central topology likely needs a rigged/projective module rather than
`ell^2` labels.

## Higher cones

Componentwise cone attachments preserve central orthogonality and can be
assembled cutoff-naturally. They cannot convert a scalar nullhomotopy into a
central one. Every local boundary equation must be sourced before applying the
codiagonal.

This recovers the five-port residual-jet requirement: cancellation must be
verified as a labelled vector identity, not only after aggregate readout.

## Potential unlock

Construct a lift

\[
\widetilde\Delta_{\rm Ev}(z)
\in\mathcal H_A
\]

of the independent bordered defect such that:

1. its scalar codiagonal is the known `tau(z)H(z)`;
2. its `p`th coordinate is the local Haar cycle residual;
3. the lift is Xi-divisible coordinatewise;
4. the source-generated range is noncollapsed in the projective module.

This would prove confinement immediately. No such coordinatewise lift is
currently materialized.

## Verdict for topology 39

Central Hilbert-module topology would solve the localization problem by making
global zero equivalent to fiberwise zero. The obstruction is not topology but
the absence of a prime-labelled vector-valued Xi cycle law and the failure of
uniform Hilbert observability.

The next nonredundant topology to test is a projective Köthe or nuclear sequence
module over prime labels, which matches the actual source decay and may support
the central lift without demanding a false Hilbert lower bound.
