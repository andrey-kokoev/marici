# Local prime dilations do not form the naive vacuum tensor product

## Local success

For each prime, let

\[
r_p=p^{-1/2}.
\]

The scalar contraction with return amplitude \(r_p\) has a canonical minimal Sz.-Nagy unitary dilation. In its Poisson-kernel realization there is a reference vector \(\Omega_p\) and unitary \(U_p\) satisfying

\[
\langle\Omega_p,U_p^k\Omega_p\rangle=r_p^k,
\qquad k\ge0.
\]

After suspension over \(\log p\), this reproduces the local prime-power weights on the critical seam with one fixed vector functional.

## Naive global tensor obstruction

Consider the incomplete infinite tensor product based at

\[
\Omega=\bigotimes_p\Omega_p.
\]

For the simultaneous tensor product \(\bigotimes_p U_p\) to act in this vacuum sector, the transformed reference vectors must be strongly equivalent. A standard necessary condition is

\[
\sum_p\left\|(U_p-I)\Omega_p\right\|^2<\infty
\]

after compatible phase choices.

Here the return amplitude is real and positive, so

\[
\left\|(U_p-I)\Omega_p\right\|^2
=2\left(1-\operatorname{Re}\langle\Omega_p,U_p\Omega_p\rangle\right)
=2(1-p^{-1/2}).
\]

For every prime \(p\ge2\),

\[
2(1-p^{-1/2})\ge2(1-2^{-1/2})>0.
\]

The sum therefore diverges. The naive simultaneous prime tensor product does not exist in the reference vacuum representation.

## Meaning

Local unitarity does not globalize by simply multiplying all prime dilations. The obstruction is stronger than the earlier Hilbert–Schmidt tail issue: the one-step local vacuum displacement does not even approach zero as \(p\) grows.

This does not falsify the wave-trace programme. It rules out one global architecture.

## Surviving architectures

The viable alternatives are structurally different:

1. a direct-sum spectral representation with a distributional fixed functional;
2. a suspended groupoid in which a finite scale window activates only finitely many return events;
3. a relative or projective implementation after subtracting declared primitive and square currents;
4. a second-quantized restricted product whose implementability criterion is applied to a near-identity comparison, not to the raw \(U_p\);
5. a rigged trace formula that never asserts a bounded global tensor-product unitary.

Each alternative needs its own domain, topology, and trace functional. They are not equivalent presentations of the failed tensor product.

## Finite falsifier

For a prime cutoff \(P\), compute the accumulated vacuum displacement

\[
D(P)=\sum_{p\le P}2(1-p^{-1/2}).
\]

It must remain bounded for the raw tensor-product proposal to survive. The uniform positive lower bound per prime makes \(D(P)\) grow at least linearly in the number of admitted primes.

Any proposed global tensor construction must identify a different near-identity local operator and prove its source authority before applying an implementability criterion.

## Scope correction to the adjoint decomposition

Adjoint symmetry forces reflected arithmetic return events, but it does not confine the archimedean channel to zero length. The gamma contribution generally supplies an extended reflection-symmetric kernel, possibly with an additional singular term at the fixed locus. Only genuinely zero-length terms belong to the \(q=0\) channel.
