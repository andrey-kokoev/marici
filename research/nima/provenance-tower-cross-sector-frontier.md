# Provenance tower across active sectors

## Question

Which native objects in each active sector instantiate endpoint record, action
provenance, sheet or frame provenance, and factorization provenance, and what is
the first finite gate separating adjacent levels?

## Claim boundary

This packet is a typed frontier map. It does not assert that every sector has a
Clifford or spinorial sheet, that analogous positions are mathematically
identical, or that any missing physical constructor already exists. A level is
included only when the sector supplies a native distinction requiring it.

## Common tower

The reusable interface has four possible levels:

1. endpoint record;
2. action provenance on a declared recovery or probe domain;
3. sheet, frame, orientation, or central-extension provenance when native;
4. factorization provenance modulo source-authorized process equations.

The last level is not raw history. It separates operative history classes after
quotienting by relations authorized by the source process theory.

## Flavor

The endpoint record is the measured scalar or angular distribution. The action
level is the source-derived analyzer from the two coupling directions to the
two calibrated ports. The native frame level is chirality and complex relative
phase, not an imposed Clifford rotor. The factorization level is the ordered
cascade through the protected parent decay and chiral secondary decay.

The current first finite gate is whether the exact finite-mass analyzer remains
uniformly observable after a separately calibrated detector metric and admitted
uncertainty interval are supplied. Threshold collapse is a domain boundary.
Equal endpoint distributions with distinct coupling fibers are the immediate
falsifier.

## Nonlinear control

The endpoint record is an output trajectory. Action provenance is the
input-output behavior on a declared experiment family. The next native level is
a realization class modulo source-authorized state-coordinate equivalence; no
spinorial sheet should be inserted unless a central cover arises from the
system. Factorization provenance records plant, observer, controller, and
feedback interconnection modulo authorized block identities.

The first finite gate is the Galois closure between admitted experiments and
the maximal observable state quotient. Equal input-output behavior with
inequivalent internal interconnection is the factorization hostile.

## Topological charge measurement

The endpoint record is the visible charge probability. Action provenance is
the charge or Wilson operator acting on the logical carrier. The native sheet
level is the simple-current, ribbon, or sign-frame torsor when present. The
factorization level is the flux-class measurement followed by conditional
centralizer Fourier analysis and complete ancilla uncomputation.

The first finite gate is instrument equality, not probability equality.
Retaining a based group element, conjugating frame, or Fourier row and column
implements a finer noncentral measurement and falsifies the claimed charge
instrument.

## Authority and repair compilation

The endpoint record is the completed safe state or emitted capability. Action
provenance is the composite repair or authority transformation. The native
sheet level is a source-typed central phase or anomaly when it survives the
declared equivalences. Factorization provenance is the constructor path modulo
authorized swap, braid, cancellation, and process relations.

The first finite gate is faithful response on the source-authorized history
quotient. Raw-path separation overfits authorized relations, while equal
endpoint bytes underfit inequivalent constructor histories.

## Optics

The endpoint record is detected intensity. Action provenance is the complex
field, Jones, Mueller, or scattering action on a declared probe domain. Phase,
polarization orientation, or a double-cover sheet supplies the third level when
physically present. Factorization provenance is the ordered route through
sources, propagation, interfaces, cavities, and detectors modulo authorized
optical identities.

The first finite gate is the smallest source-derived interferometric or
polarimetric port that separates each successive quotient.

## Arithmetic and spectral analysis

The endpoint record is the scalar completed readout. Action provenance is the
operator-valued tail, seam, or transport action on its declared domain. The
native frame level is reciprocal-sector orientation and determinant or
metaplectic sheet data when source-derived. Factorization provenance is the
ordered adelic, boundary, and completion construction modulo proven Tate and
Fourier relations.

The first finite gate remains whether operator-valued source provenance and its
completion descend before scalar projection. Scalar functional equality cannot
reconstruct the operator or factorization levels.

## Cross-sector compiler rule

Every proposed upward promotion must name:

- the ambiguity class left by the lower level;
- the new source-derived port or law;
- the maximal domain it closes;
- the equivalence relations already authorized;
- the finite hostile that survives if the lift is absent;
- separate existence, synthesis, execution, fault, and completion verdicts.

## Disposition

The cross-sector frontier is now retyped by provenance level. The immediate
work is not to search for a universal fourth observer, but to compute each
sector's surviving ambiguity stabilizer and identify the smallest native port
or source law that removes it. No cross-sector equivalence theorem is claimed.
