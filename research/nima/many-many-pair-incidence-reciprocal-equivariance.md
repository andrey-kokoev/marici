# Many-many pair-incidence reciprocal equivariance

Let `sigma` exchange tensor factors:

$$
\sigma(f\otimes\overline g)=g\otimes\overline f.
$$

On ordered pair responses, reciprocal transport satisfies

$$
\mathcal B_{\mu\lambda}^{[a,b]}
=\mathsf R_{\rm wall}\mathcal B_{\lambda\mu}^{[a,b]},
$$

where `R_wall` exchanges the two endpoint channels and reverses the odd Wronskian orientation.

Therefore

$$
B_{\rm rad}\sigma
=\mathsf R_{\rm wall}B_{\rm rad}
$$

on the finite projective tensor core. The relation passes to the completed projective tensor product by continuity.
