# Many-many finite-family incidence estimate

For a finite pair family `F` and shell lower endpoint `a_0>0`, the Gaussian pair decay gives

$$
\sum_{(n,m)\in F}|g_{nm}(a)|
\le
\sum_{n,m\ge1}e^{-\pi(n^2+m^2)a_0^2}<\infty.
$$

The bounded coefficient `alpha_nm` and the uniform damping floor `kappa_nm >= pi` propagate this estimate through the pair-response solution and its Laplace coordinate.

Consequently, the arithmetic incidence map lands continuously in every fixed finite-family graph away from the endpoint.

The endpoint row is retained as a finite/projective coordinate. Uniformity as `a_0` tends to zero requires the endpoint graph norm and remains a separate gate.
