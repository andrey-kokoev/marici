# Half-density translation Hopf six-port core

Let `M` be the additive label group generated by the logarithmic prime lengths, and put

$$
w(a)=e^{-a/2},\qquad Q_a=w(a)S_a.
$$

Because `w(a+b)=w(a)w(b)` and `S_aS_b=S_(a+b)`,

$$
Q_aQ_b=Q_{a+b}.
$$

On the completed labelled group algebra define

$$
\mu(Q_a\otimes Q_b)=Q_{a+b},
\qquad
\eta(1)=Q_0,
$$

$$
\Delta(Q_a)=Q_a\otimes Q_a,
\qquad
\varepsilon(Q_a)=1,
\qquad
\mathsf S(Q_a)=Q_{-a}.
$$

These maps satisfy associativity, coassociativity, unit, counit, bialgebra compatibility

$$
\Delta\mu=(\mu\otimes\mu)(1\otimes\tau\otimes1)(\Delta\otimes\Delta),
$$

and the antipode identities

$$
\mu(\mathsf S\otimes1)\Delta
=\eta\varepsilon
=\mu(1\otimes\mathsf S)\Delta.
$$

Thus the algebraic six-port core

$$
(\eta,\varepsilon;\mu,\Delta;\rho,\rho^\vee)
$$

has a genuine Hopf foundation once `rho` is any source representation of the labelled `Q_a` and `rho^vee` is its contragredient.

## Important distinction

The endpoint augmentation previously used on the unweighted translation basis obeys `epsilon(S_a)=1`. In the weighted basis,

$$
S_a=w(a)^{-1}Q_a,
$$

so its linear extension gives `epsilon(S_a)=w(a)^(-1)`, not `1`, if it is to be the Hopf counit above. Therefore the endpoint coboundary augmentation and the Hopf counit are different maps unless one works in the unweighted basis or explicitly twists the counit by the half-density character.

The endpoint relative trace

$$
\operatorname{Tr}^{\rm rel}_G(T)=E_0(TG)-\varepsilon_{\rm end}(T)E_0(G)
$$

is a groupoid one-coboundary, not the Hopf counit.

## Claim boundary

This proves a bialgebraic/Hopf six-port core for the labelled translation algebra. It does not yet prove that determinant totalization, linking incidence, or the Xi pencil is a Hopf module/comodule realization. Those require explicit module-algebra and comodule compatibility squares.
