# Many-many finite polarized pair-source coproduct

For a finite labelled source basis `e_lambda`, define the polarized pair carrier

$$
U_{\rm pair}^{(N)}
=\operatorname{span}\{e_\lambda\otimes\overline{e_\mu}:\lambda,\mu\le N\}.
$$

The diagonal source lift is

$$
\Delta_N(e_\lambda)=e_\lambda\otimes\overline{e_\lambda}.
$$

Its polarization is the linear extension to matrix units

$$
\Delta_N^{\rm pol}(e_\lambda,e_\mu)
=e_\lambda\otimes\overline{e_\mu}.
$$

Define the finite pair incidence by

$$
B_{\rm rad}^{(N)}
(e_\lambda\otimes\overline{e_\mu})
=\rho_{\lambda,\mu}^{\rm graph}.
$$

The finite construction retains ordered orientation and reciprocal exchange. For `N<=M`, coordinate inclusion satisfies

$$
B_{\rm rad}^{(M)}\,\iota_{M,N}
=B_{\rm rad}^{(N)}.
$$

This supplies a finite directed system. A source coalgebra law is still required to make `Delta_N` canonical beyond the formal matrix-unit lift.
