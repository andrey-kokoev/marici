# qRB microstep 69: global Douglas gate

Prior research converts the open common-subfeature problem into two source-form inequalities. For aligned positive features, define

$$
D_\alpha=G_\alpha^T-G_\alpha^0,
\qquad
D_{\alpha,+},D_{\alpha,-}
$$

as its global Jordan parts. The canonical common edge is

$$
C_\alpha=G_\alpha^T-D_{\alpha,+}
=G_\alpha^0-D_{\alpha,-}.
$$

It exists exactly when

$$
D_{\alpha,+}\preceq G_\alpha^T,
\qquad
D_{\alpha,-}\preceq G_\alpha^0.
$$

Under these dominations, Douglas factorization constructs one packet-independent common feature, and the residual feature has Gram

$$
F_\alpha^*F_\alpha=|D_\alpha|.
$$

This is the correct global replacement for packetwise Jordan subtraction. The inequalities may be stated as closed-form domination when operators are unbounded.

Status: first absolute-completion gate reduced to explicit Douglas inequalities; their source proof remains open.
