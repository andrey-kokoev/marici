## Paired source equations

Let \(B:U\to H\) be the retained arithmetic-to-history incidence and let
\(D_U(z):U\to U\) be a source arithmetic law.  The paired pencil equations
are

\[
(A-z)u=Bx,
\]

\[
B^\dagger u+D_U(z)x=0.
\]

The two-sided Evans construction supplies a natural history \(u_z\) at every
Xi zero.  To promote it into this paired kernel, one must also supply a source
state \(x_z\).

## Incidence lift

The first equation requires

\[
Bx_z=\Phi
\]

for the normalized theta forcing.  On the retained labelled carrier, the
bi-bounded front-to-cut comparison provides a candidate source lift whenever
\(\Phi\) lies in the declared incidence range.

This range statement must be made before terminal prime or label summation.
A scalar coefficient reproducing one Mellin value is insufficient.

## Arithmetic residual

After choosing a source-authorized lift \(x_z\), define

\[
\mathcal R_U(z)
=
B^\dagger u_z+D_U(z)x_z.
\]

Then

\[
\binom{u_z}{x_z}
\in\ker\mathcal P(z)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal R_U(z)=0.
\]

The entire Evans-to-Green promotion problem is therefore one vector-valued
identity on the source space \(U\).

## Fixed-lift specialization

If the forcing has a fixed labelled lift \(x_\Phi\) satisfying

\[

\[
\mathcal R_U(z)

\]

All parameter dependence lies in the stable history and the arithmetic law.
This is directly testable at finite cutoff without forming a scalar

## Why scalar Xi cancellation does not imply the residual

The Evans condition gives one seam equation

\[
u_-(0;z)-u_+(0;z)=0.
\]

The residual \(\mathcal R_U(z)\) retains every prime, grade, and reciprocal
source coordinate.  A single scalar seam cancellation cannot force this
vector equation unless a source intertwining theorem relates the endpoint
mismatch to the complete adjoint return.

Thus the residual must not be tested only after prime pushforward.

## Multiplicity chain

At a zero of order \(m\), differentiate the paired equations through order
\(m-1\).  Promotion preserves algebraic multiplicity only if

\[
\partial_z^j\mathcal R_U(z_0)=0,
\qquad
0\le j<m,
\]

with the derivatives of \(u_z\), \(x_z\), and \(D_U(z)\) retained.  Pointwise
vanishing of \(\mathcal R_U(z_0)\) gives only geometric kernel inclusion.

## Green consequence

If \(D_U\) is a source law whose Hermitian part completes the forcing Green
pairing, then \(\mathcal R_U(z)=0\) promotes the Evans state into the
maximal-isotropic paired kernel.  The resulting Green identity can then
exclude off-seam states.

Accordingly, vanishing of \(\mathcal R_U\) on the Xi divisor is the precise
RH-bearing interpolation theorem.  It cannot be obtained by defining

\[
D_U(z)x_\Phi=-B^\dagger u_z

zeros.

A valid completion requires cutoff-natural residual identities and uniform
control of all derivative chains.
