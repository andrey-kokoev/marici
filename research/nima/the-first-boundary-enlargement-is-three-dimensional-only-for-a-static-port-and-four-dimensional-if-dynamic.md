# The first boundary enlargement is three-dimensional only for a static port and four-dimensional if dynamic

## Correction to the rank lower bound

The rigid reciprocal wall–jump plane is too small for the completed RH feedback. Adding one source direction gives the first algebraic determinant enlargement,

\[
\mathcal B_{\mathrm{wj}}\oplus\mathbb C e_{\mathrm{extra}}.
\]

But a \(3\times3\) return is not automatically an admissible dynamic boundary system.

If the enlarged carrier is required to support a nondegenerate real symplectic or canonical-system form, odd dimension is impossible. The role of the extra port must therefore be fixed before constructing the next matrix.

## Static attachment route

A single extra direction may be retained as an external boundary functional or quadratic energy port. Then it is not an evolved state coordinate.

The dynamic state remains the reciprocal two-plane, while the port contributes through incidence and observation:

\[
u:\mathbb C\to\mathcal X_{\mathrm{wj}},
\qquad
v^*:\mathcal X_{\mathrm{wj}}\to\mathbb C.
\]

The resulting bordered or Schur matrix can be \(3\times3\), but its third coordinate is a boundary variable, not a canonical state.

For this route the source must prove:

1. the extra port is independently authorized;
2. its mixed propagated return is nonzero;
3. the boundary elimination is legitimate in the declared rigging;
4. no symplectic nondegeneracy is claimed on the full odd-dimensional border.

## Dynamic enlargement route

If the extra feature propagates as a genuine state, it needs a conjugate partner. The minimal real dynamic carrier is

\[
\mathcal X_{\mathrm{dyn}}
=
\mathcal X_{\mathrm{wj}}
\oplus
\operatorname{span}\{e_{\mathrm{extra}},\pi_{\mathrm{extra}}\},
\]

of dimension four.

A nondegenerate skew form can then have block structure

\[
\Omega=
\begin{pmatrix}
\Omega_{\mathrm{wj}}&K\\
-K^*&\Omega_{\mathrm{extra}}
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det\Omega\ne0.
\]

The conjugate coordinate may not be supplied merely to repair parity. It must arise from a named source operation.

## The archimedean even–odd pair

The additive Tate source already supplies a candidate conjugate pair.

The even port is the theta/Gaussian channel. Additive differentiation gives

\[
P
=
e^{-q}\left(\partial_q-\frac12\right),
\]

which maps the even Tate port to the odd port. Fourier covariance gives

\[
K_-P
=
2\pi i\,M_{e^q}K_+.
\]

This identity supplies:

- an independently derived odd coordinate;
- a fixed quarter-phase;
- a source incidence between the even and odd ports;
- a candidate conjugate pairing for dynamic enlargement.

Thus the first source-authorized dynamic candidate is not a lone anomaly coordinate. It is the archimedean even–odd pair attached to the reciprocal wall–jump pair.

## What remains missing

The additive derivative square does not yet couple to prime propagation. The decisive mixed operators are

\[
G_{\mathrm{wj},+},
\quad
G_{\mathrm{wj},-},
\quad
G_{+,\mathrm{wj}},
\quad
G_{-,\mathrm{wj}},
\]

where \(+\) and \(-\) denote the even and odd Tate ports.

At least one cross-pair must be nonzero, and reciprocal reflection must transform the full block with the correct parity and adjoint orientation.

Scalar gamma multiplication does not construct these maps.

## Determinant versus canonical-system typing

Two finite pilots are therefore distinct:

### Bordered determinant pilot

Use a three-coordinate algebraic border

\[
\mathcal B_{\mathrm{wj}}\oplus\mathbb C e_{\mathrm{static}}.
\]

Audit the finite determinant and controllable–observable quotient. Do not call it a symplectic state space.

### Dynamic Green pilot

Use four dynamic coordinates

\[
(\mathrm{wall},\mathrm{jump},\mathrm{even\ Tate},\mathrm{odd\ Tate}).
\]

Audit the nondegenerate Green/symplectic form, source generator, reciprocal involution, and mixed propagated return.

Passing one pilot does not pass the other.

## Prime coupling gate

The finite-prime operation sought earlier now has a precise target: a map from primitive and square seam currents into the additive derivative square that intertwines the doubled Mellin grade and Fourier parity.

Schematically,

\[
\text{prime incidence}
\longrightarrow
\text{even Tate port}
\xrightarrow{P}
\text{odd Tate port}.
\]

It must preserve prime labels before global summation. Agreement only after the gamma or Euler scalar readout is insufficient.

## Hostiles

1. Treat a \(3\times3\) bordered determinant as a nondegenerate canonical system.
2. Add a dynamic wall coordinate without a conjugate partner.
3. Add an odd Tate coordinate but omit the derivative–Fourier covariance square.
4. Obtain a nonzero archimedean phase while every prime-to-Tate mixed block vanishes.
5. Use a fitted fourth coordinate with no named source operation.

## Verdict

The previous dimension-three lower bound is correct only for a static bordered feedback variable. A genuinely dynamic Green or canonical realization requires at least four real coordinates.

The strongest current source candidate is the reciprocal wall–jump pair plus the archimedean even–odd Tate pair. The earliest missing calculation is their prime-labelled mixed incidence, not another free boundary coefficient.
