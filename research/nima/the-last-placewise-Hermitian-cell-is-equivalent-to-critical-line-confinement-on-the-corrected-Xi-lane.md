# The last placewise Hermitian cell is equivalent to critical-line confinement on the corrected Xi lane

## Question

Is the final placewise Hermitian packet equality a weaker technical lemma than
the desired critical-line theorem?

## Residual formula

On the corrected Xi lane, after the wall, jump, endpoint, residue, and
prime-diagonal identifications, the remaining local mismatch has the form

\[
\mathcal R_p(z)
=
\bigl(1-p^{-2\operatorname{Re}z}\bigr)
E_p(\Delta_\Xi(z)),
\]

where the retained pair state is noncollapsed and

\[
E_p(\Delta_\Xi(z))>0.
\]

For any prime `p>1`, positivity gives

\[
\mathcal R_p(z)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
p^{-2\operatorname{Re}z}=1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Re}z=0.
\]

Thus one placewise Hermitian comparison already has confinement strength. The
full prime family adds naturality and arithmetic provenance, not a weaker route
to the same conclusion.

## Converse on the typed lane

When `Re z=0`, the displayed multiplicative mismatch vanishes at every prime.
Therefore, conditional on all previously constructed packet identifications,
critical-line placement implies the final local residuals vanish. On this
restricted corrected-Xi lane, the last comparison cell and critical-line
confinement are equivalent.

This is not an RH proof because no independent theorem establishes either side
for every Xi zero.

## Disposition

The final Hermitian cell cannot be delegated to another completion,
normalization, or interface-construction task. All such lower packet gates have
been discharged. Proving the remaining cell independently is exactly proving
critical-line confinement for the corrected Xi lane.

Further work on this branch requires new arithmetic evidence, not another
categorical refinement.
