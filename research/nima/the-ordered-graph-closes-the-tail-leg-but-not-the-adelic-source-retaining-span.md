## Why a boundary inverse is forbidden

The ordered boundary record need not determine its adelic source. If two
adelic test functions have the same translated Euler and tail coordinates,
a direct map from the tail boundary to `Ran(tau)` would identify them without
an injectivity theorem. Retaining `phi` in `Gamma_z` avoids this unauthorized
quotient.

Thus the common ordered carrier solves codomain typing but cannot replace the
common-source correspondence.
