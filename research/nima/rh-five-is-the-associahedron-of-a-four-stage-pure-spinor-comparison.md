## Correction

The earlier five-channel search asked whether five state coordinates carried
an order-five source symmetry. Source naturality rejected that interpretation
and lifted the linear carrier to \(V\oplus V^*\).

There is nevertheless a canonical categorical place where five can occur.
Four composable correspondences have exactly five binary
parenthesizations. Their elementary associator moves form the pentagonal
associahedron \(K_4\).

Thus five can count coherent presentations of one composite operation while
six counts the linear rank of the object on which that operation acts.

## Candidate four-stage composite

The rigged pure-spinor architecture has the following proposed stages:

1. preparation of the product-formula pure-spinor boundary condition;
2. Fourier transport into the reciprocal chart;
3. theta smoothing between the distributional and test polarizations;
4. terminal pure-spinor evaluation into the scalar determinant line.

Schematically, write these typed arrows as

\[
\eta,
\qquad J,
\qquad K_\Phi,
\qquad \epsilon.
\]

Their full composite is a scalar-valued correspondence. In a bicategory of
rigged or Lagrangian correspondences, its five bracketings are

\[
((\epsilon K_\Phi)J)\eta,
\qquad
(\epsilon(K_\Phi J))\eta,
\qquad
\epsilon((K_\Phi J)\eta),
\qquad
\epsilon(K_\Phi(J\eta)),
\qquad
(\epsilon K_\Phi)(J\eta).
\]

These are not five distinct physical outputs and not five freely permutable
ports. They are five factorization presentations with the same typed external
boundary.

## The pentagon coherencer

An elementary reassociation replaces

\[
(fg)h
\quad\longleftrightarrow\quad
f(gh).
\]

Applying this move at every internal node connects the five bracketings in a
five-cycle. The bicategorical associator supplies comparison 2-cells along
its edges. Mac Lane's pentagon condition says that the two composite
reassociations between the extreme presentations agree.

This is the legitimate categorical pentagon. Its dihedral graph automorphism
group appears only after forgetting arrow labels, directions, analytic
domains, and boundary support. Therefore the visible \(D_5\) graph is again a
shadow, while the associator coherence itself may be source-real.

## Lagrangian interpretation

The initial and terminal pure-spinor lines represent maximal isotropic
relations. Fourier transport and theta smoothing should define intermediate
correspondences. Their composite is defined only when the relevant fiber
products are clean or Fredholm.

At finite rank, the invariant pairing of two compatible pure spinors is
nonzero on the transverse locus. Its vanishing records failure of
transversality, subject to the standard parity and component qualification.
This supplies a categorical interpretation of a scalar zero:

> The scalar readout vanishes when the completed source and reciprocal
> boundary relations cease to compose transversely.

This is stronger than calling a zero a missing scalar value and more accurate
than calling it a puncture before a determinant bridge exists.

## Where RH now lives

The source-local finite hyperbolic and pure-spinor pieces are constructed.
The unresolved arrow is \(K_\Phi\). Until theta smoothing is derived as a
typed correspondence, the four-stage composite and hence its source
associator are conditional.

If \(K_\Phi\) is constructed, the RH-bearing theorem is not merely that the
five bracketings agree. Bicategorical coherence guarantees equality of
well-defined composites; it does not guarantee their transversality.

The required additional theorem is:

1. every intermediate composition is Fredholm on each open half-plane;
2. reassociation preserves its determinant-line section and domain;
3. the completed initial and terminal Lagrangians remain transverse there;
4. loss of transversality can occur only at the common unitary seam.

The fourth condition remains the analytic core. The pentagon organizes its
presentation invariance but does not prove it.
