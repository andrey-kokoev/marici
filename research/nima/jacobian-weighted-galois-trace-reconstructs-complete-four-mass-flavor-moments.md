# Jacobian-weighted quadratic field trace of the complete four-mass flavour restriction

At the two rational rank-six targets whose four-mass inverse polynomial `P(q)` has genuinely irrational conjugate roots, the COMPLETE sourced form weight has now been evaluated rather than merely inferred to be rational. Work in the exact quadratic algebra `Q[q]/P(q)`, reconstruct the normalized positive-cell matrix and its eight source coordinates as field elements, and compute the full **`8×8` target-chart Jacobian** over that field. The local residue is the independently oriented dlog density divided by this Jacobian.

At BOTH witnesses, the one-sheet weighted coefficient is `A+Bq` with **`B≠0`**: a single sheet really is irrational. Its exact Galois trace `2A−B·P₁/P₂` (where `P₂q²+P₁q+P₀=0`) is rational and nonzero. More stringently, form all FIVE SU(4)-flavor-selected degree-four coefficients `Tr[(source_density/J)·x^(4−j)y^j]`, `j=0,…,4`, for the two fermion pair minors `x=(local1,5)` and `y=(local2,6)`. Each is an exact rational number, and their `3×3` Hankel matrix has rank **two**. A separately constructed, high-precision numerical Jacobian from the original `D` and `Z` matrices agrees with the quadratic-field weight at an irrational root to better than `10⁻²⁵` relative tolerance for each witness.

This closes the earlier gap between an unweighted rational pair-ratio trace and an actually **Jacobian-weighted** bosonic/flavor trace on genuinely irrational fibres. It gives an exact local complex-contour realization of rational four-mass form data at two generic non-`Y₀` targets, with square roots cancelling between conjugate sheets. It is not an expanded universal closed rational function of arbitrary external `Y,Z`, nor does it equate this complex contour with the canonical positive-image integration cycle or prove image coverage.
