# Many-many collision-aware coherence object

Define the higher relative object

$$
\mathfrak C_{\rm many}
=\left(\mathcal V_{\rm lab},\mathcal N_{\rm ret},\mathcal Z_{\rm coll},C_{\rm lab},C_{\rm scalar}\right).
$$

Its components are:

- `V_lab`: fully labelled prime, grade, shell, and ordered-pair carrier;
- `N_ret`: labelwise Gram radical;
- `Z_coll`: cross-shell collision-cycle sector;
- `C_lab`: labelled wall codiagonal;
- `C_scalar`: scalar output after collision handling.

The coherence laws are

$$
C_{\rm lab}(\mathcal N_{\rm ret})=0,
$$

and refinement, reciprocal exchange, Hadamard transport, and shell concatenation preserve `Z_coll` and commute with `C_lab`.

Scalarization is defined after the collision sector has been retained or quotiented by a proved radical relation. This object is the natural many-many analytic layer above the scalar RH observable.
