# The physical five-pole residue does not descend to isolated T7

The printed triangle source terms containing \(q_{\mathcal G_{12}}\) belong to
two five-pole families.  Existing replicated calculations give, for either
occurrence,

\[
\dim M_{5\mathrm{p}}=35,
\qquad
\dim M_{\mathrm{del}}=15,
\qquad
\dim M_{\mathrm{res}}=20.
\]

After taking the \(q_{\mathcal G_{12}}\) residue, the physical class lives in

\[
H^2(S_E\setminus W),
\]

where \(W\) is the union of the four retained wall sections.  The closed
q-only nine-master object instead lives in

\[
H^2(S_E).
\]

The localization sequence is

\[
H^2(S_E)
\longrightarrow
H^2(S_E\setminus W)
\longrightarrow
H^1(W)(-1)
\longrightarrow
H^3(S_E).
\]

Descent from the open marked object to the closed nine-master object requires
all wall residues to vanish.  The existing physical occupancy audit finds a
nonzero residue class and nonzero normalized wall components; only the mixed
occurrence component vanishes.  In particular, the single-site wall residues
remain.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\text{physical }q_{\mathcal G_{12}}\text{ residue}
\not\rightsquigarrow
T_7\text{ as a closed q-only class}.
}
\]

This settles the interpretation of the T7 ultraviolet census.  Its two
residual UV directions are genuine properties of the universal closed
nine-master system, but they are not missing directions of the literal
physical triangle readout.  The latter stays in the rank-20 marked-relative
coefficient object.

The active physical frontier is consequently not another quotient of \(T_7\).
It is the Gauss--Manin/localization transport of the rank-20 marked residue,
including its three surviving single-site wall maps and their cyclic sewing.
