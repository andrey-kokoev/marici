# Reciprocal reflection commutes with labelled cut synthesis at every finite packet

## Independent chart formulas

Let $\Phi$ be the real-even theta forcing. For $p\ge 0$, define the positive
chart directly by

$$
g_p^+(t)=\Phi(t+p),\qquad t\ge 0,
$$

and

$$
h_p^+(t)=\mathbf 1_{[0,p]}(t)\Phi(p-t).
$$

Define the negative chart independently by

$$
g_p^-(x)=\Phi(x-p),\qquad x\le 0,
$$

and

$$
h_p^-(x)=\mathbf 1_{[-p,0]}(x)\Phi(x+p).
$$

These are the two source translations in their native coordinates; the
negative formulas are not defined by applying the desired comparison.

## Exact naturality

Let $J$ be conjugate reflection on the carrier and let $R$ conjugate the
coefficient while preserving the positive translation length and its
arithmetic type. Real-evenness gives

$$
J(cg_p^+)(x)
=\overline c\,\Phi(p-x)
=\overline c\,\Phi(x-p)
=(R c)g_p^-(x),
$$

and, on the seam support,

$$
J(ch_p^+)(x)
=\overline c\,\Phi(p+x)
=(R c)h_p^-(x).
$$

Therefore

$$
J U_+=U_-R
$$

for every finite labelled packet. The identity holds separately in the tail
and seam channels and preserves primitive, square, and connected prime-power
typing.

This closes the finite action-naturality square. It does not prove
observability, positivity, or RH.

## Categorical placement

For the finite constructor family generated by labelled synthesis, reciprocal
coefficient transport, and carrier reflection, the action is now compatible.
The earlier arithmetic transpose identity is its dual readout shadow, not its
source.

Kitaev's constructor-indexed correction remains essential. Enlarging the
family to include valuation operations, cutoff refinement, archimedean
completion, or unbounded boundary traces creates new naturality cells. The
finite identity cannot be transported into those families without proof.

## Remaining completion cell

At cutoff $N$, finite evaluation produces a residual $\delta_N$, which is zero
for this square. Completion is a separate cell involving the pro-family,
bonding maps, and the chosen coefficient topology.

The remaining questions are:

1. do the synthesis maps extend to the constructor-generated coefficient
   completion;
2. does reciprocal transport remain continuous there;
3. do the completed tail and seam carriers share a closed graph domain;
4. does the archimedean endpoint extend the same naturality square;
5. is there a source-authorized uniform margin for the later action and
   observation tests?

A zero finite residual removes one obstruction. It does not supply the margin.

## Hostile tests

The square fails if any of the following is introduced:

- a non-even forcing;
- coefficient transport without conjugation;
- type erasure that treats every composite as a prime power;
- seam support transported without reflection;
- a cutoff bonding map that does not intertwine $R$.
