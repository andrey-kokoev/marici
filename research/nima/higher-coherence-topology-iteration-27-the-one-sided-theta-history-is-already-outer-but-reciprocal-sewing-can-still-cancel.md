# Higher-coherence topology iteration 27: the one-sided theta history is already outer, but reciprocal sewing can still cancel

## Candidate topology

Use Hardy shift-cyclicity. For `f in H^2`, the invariant subspace generated by
`f` is the whole Hardy space exactly when `f` is outer. Cyclicity excludes a
hidden inner/all-pass factor in the corresponding one-sided channel.

## Source theorem already available

The one-sided theta transfer

\[
m_\Phi(z)=\int_0^\infty\Phi(u)e^{-zu}\,du,
\qquad \operatorname{Re}z>0,
\]

is already proved outer. The source argument uses the Stieltjes representation

\[
m_\Phi(z)
=\frac1z\int_{(0,\infty)}(1-e^{-zs})\,d\mu(s),
\qquad \mu=-d\Phi>0,
\]

which excludes open-half-plane zeros, finite singular inner measure, and an
exponential delay factor.

Therefore multiplication by `m_Phi` is injective with dense range. The causal
history channel has no exact dark state.

## Why this does not imply confinement

The completed bilateral section is the reciprocal boundary combination

\[
\Xi(\omega)
=m_\Phi(i\omega)+m_\Phi(-i\omega)
=2\operatorname{Re}m_\Phi(i\omega)
\]

in the corresponding normalization. Two nonzero outer one-sided amplitudes can
cancel in this sum. Cyclicity of each propagation leg does not control their
relative boundary phase.

Thus the bulk is already minimum phase; the zero mechanism lives entirely in
reciprocal sewing.

## Multi-anchor trap

Adding higher-cone anchors can make a family jointly cyclic even when one
transfer has an inner factor. Joint cyclicity says that the anchors have no
common inner divisor. It does not show that the original Evans transfer is
outer. A new anchor can observe or bypass an inner defect without removing it
from the original channel.

Therefore repeated systems cannot prove confinement merely by making the total
source family cyclic.

## Boundary-return target

After outer bulk factorization, the remaining transfer has the form

\[
G(z)=V^*(I-S(z))^{-1}U
\]

or its boundary-triple equivalent. The unresolved condition is outerness or
invertibility of this filtered reciprocal return, with `U,V,S` derived from the
wall--jump incidence.

Declaring that return outer is simply zero exclusion. A useful theorem must
derive it from a source law controlling reciprocal phase, ordered signature,
or a coupled signed-operator coercivity estimate.

## Approximate spectrum warning

Although `m_Phi` is outer,

\[
|m_\Phi(i\omega)|\sim\frac{\Phi(0)}{|\omega|}
\]

so multiplication is not bounded below. Cyclicity gives exact faithfulness but
no uniform completion margin. Higher coherent limits may still develop
approximate dark states unless another observer controls the boundary return.

## Verdict for topology 27

Cyclic/outer topology completely solves the one-sided theta-history channel:
it is source-derived, outer, injective, and dense-range. It does not constrain
the reciprocal boundary cancellation where Xi zeros occur.

The next nonredundant topology to test is a Toeplitz-corona or coupled
signed-operator coercivity topology for the filtered boundary return, seeking a
uniform lower bound without asserting scalar outerness directly.
