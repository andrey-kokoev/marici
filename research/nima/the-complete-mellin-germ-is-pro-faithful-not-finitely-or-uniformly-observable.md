# The complete Mellin germ is pro-faithful, not finitely or uniformly observable

## Correction

The Mellin-grade obstruction is not specifically parity-based. On distinct
positive log-scales, every set of distinct grades forms a generalized
Vandermonde system. Omitting any one independent grade removes one detectable
direction.

The source-derived replacement is the complete analytic grade tower.

## Three distinct properties

Let

\[
J_N:X\longrightarrow Y_N
\]

record the first \(N\) source-authorized Mellin grades, and let \(J_\infty\)
record the complete derivative germ.

These properties must not be conflated.

### Pro-faithfulness

\[
\bigcap_N\ker J_N=\{0\}.
\]

Equivalently, the map into the inverse-limit record object is monic:

\[
J_\infty:X\longrightarrow\varprojlim_NY_N.
\]

Under the declared source decay, analytic uniqueness proves this property.

### Finite observability

There exists one fixed \(N\) such that \(J_N\) is injective.

This holds on a fixed fiber once the number of independent grades reaches its
size. It fails uniformly when fiber size is unbounded.

### Uniform observability

There is a source norm and a cutoff-independent constant \(c>0\) such that

\[
\lVert J_\infty x\rVert\ge c\lVert x\rVert.
\]

Pro-faithfulness does not imply this. The adjacent-label and alternating escape
families remain valid hostiles until a source topology and record norm exclude
them.

## Categorical picture

The complete Mellin germ is a monomorphism into a pro-object even though no
fixed stage projection is monic on the unbounded source category.

This explains why finite channel counting repeatedly fails. Primitive and
square currents are not a complete finite port list; they are initial
coordinates of a pro-boundary constructor.

It also constrains determinant arguments. A finite determinant line can orient
one truncation, but the completed programme needs compatible determinant
comparisons across the entire tower. An infinite wedge cannot be assumed from
finite Vandermonde signs.

## Survival test

A candidate anomaly survives the full grade tower only if it defines a
compatible class at every truncation and is not killed at any later stage.
Apparent classes present only at one sparse grade selection are projection
aliases.

The exact next gates are:

1. construct the pro-boundary transition maps;
2. prove source decay makes the analytic germ well-defined;
3. type the Klein-four action on the whole germ;
4. derive a completion norm or accept faithfulness without coercivity;
5. identify a compatible determinant or orientation class.
