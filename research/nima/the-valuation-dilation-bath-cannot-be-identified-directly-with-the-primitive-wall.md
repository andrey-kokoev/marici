# The valuation dilation bath cannot be identified directly with the primitive wall

The two frontier branches from event 10300 have compatible scalar shadows but
incompatible source ranks.

Let

\[
A e_p=p^{-1/2}e_p
\]

be the prime attenuation on \(\ell^2(\mathcal P)\). Its defect operator is

\[
D_A=(I-A^*A)^{1/2},
\]

so

\[
D_Ae_p=\sqrt{1-p^{-1}}\,e_p.
\]

Every defect eigenvalue is nonzero. Therefore

\[
\overline{\operatorname{Ran}D_A}
=
\ell^2(\mathcal P).
\]

The minimal Julia dilation of the attenuation requires one defect/bath
direction for every prime:

\[
\mathcal D_A\cong\ell^2(\mathcal P).
\]

At finite cutoff \(X\),

\[
\dim\mathcal D_{A_X}=\pi(X).
\]

By contrast, the completed primitive bilateral wall trace has two global
half-density channels,

\[
M_-,M_+,
\]

and hence target rank at most two before prime labels are tensored back in.

## Rank obstruction

For every cutoff with \(\pi(X)>2\), no injective linear map exists from the
minimal attenuation defect space into the unlabelled primitive wall plane:

\[
\mathcal D_{A_X}\not\hookrightarrow\mathbb C^2.
\]

Thus the first sewing cell cannot identify the valuation defect bath directly
with the global primitive wall.

Any such identification necessarily has a kernel of dimension at least

\[
\pi(X)-2.
\]

Those kernel directions are passive-network states invisible to the wall
observer.

## Correct typed alternatives

There are only two lawful architectures.

### Labelled wall bundle

Retain one bilateral wall fiber per prime:

\[
\bigoplus_p
\left(
\mathbb C M_{-,p}\oplus\mathbb C M_{+,p}
\right).
\]

The valuation defect bath can map prime-diagonally into this labelled bundle.
A later source-authorized synthesis may aggregate the fibers globally.

### Declared quotient/synthesis

Use a map

\[
\Sigma:
\ell^2(\mathcal P)
\longrightarrow
\mathbb C^2
\]

with an explicitly declared kernel, and prove that every discarded defect
direction is irrelevant to the boundary pencil. This is an observability
theorem, not a passive dilation theorem.

Scalar primitive trace agreement only determines the two rows of \(\Sigma\);
it cannot prove kernel irrelevance.

## Smallest hostile

Take three primes. Their attenuation dilation has a three-dimensional defect
space. Map all three bath directions into the two primitive wall ports.
A nonzero linear combination is dark. Local prime transfers, total scalar
trace, and both wall coordinates can remain correct, while feedback through
the dark combination changes the completed network.

This is the first finite hostile requiring only three prime labels.

## Consequence for the first sewing cell

The tentative direct Cayley relation from event 10300 was too coarse. The
first source-authorized meeting must factor as

\[
\text{valuation defect bundle}
\to
\text{prime-labelled primitive history bundle}
\to
\text{global wall synthesis}
\to
\text{boundary pencil}.
\]

The first arrow needs the missing per-prime two-channel incidence
\(c_{p,+},c_{p,-}\). The second needs a kernel-reference/observability theorem
and completion bounds.

Therefore the passive-dilation and primitive-wall cells do not meet at one
rank-two port. A labelled intermediate object is categorically forced. No
coercivity or Xi identification is implied.
