# qRB microstep 17: independent-window defect

For an independently selected sharp window `\widetilde Q_X`, define

$$
E_X=\widetilde Q_X\mathcal M_{\log}-\mathcal M_{\log}P_X.
$$

The refinement square is then lax rather than strict. The correct relative requirement is not `E_X=0`, but that its contribution to the admitted observer pairing is controlled:

$$
\rho(h)^*C^*J_2E_X\rho(g)
\in\mathcal S_1
$$

(or belongs to the declared projective boundary class).

Thus the defect is an allowed boundary/observation term only after regulator type is retained. It cannot be erased by identifying place and window regulators.

Status: defect typed; a quantitative trace-class estimate for a concrete sharp-window family remains open.
