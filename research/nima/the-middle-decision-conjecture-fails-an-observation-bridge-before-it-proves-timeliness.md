# The middle-decision conjecture fails an observation bridge before it proves timeliness

## Verdict

The falsification attempt found an actual source-level obstruction to one proposed mechanism: **the frozen task's complete three-channel observation does not factor through the assembled ideal observer.** Its separately acquired vacuum channel cannot be recovered from that observer.

This does **not** refute sufficiency of the frozen task's already-retained three readings. The vacuum reading is already one of those inputs, and must not be discarded. Nor did the test produce two realizable opposing histories for the frozen middle case.

A separate, predeclared 408-dimensional proof-only pairing attempt exceeded its 180-second worker limit. Both frozen middle cases remain UNRESOLVED. That is a failed bounded protocol, not a universal deadline impossibility or an information obstruction.

The earlier conjecture therefore needs a different statement: task compatibility and certified analytical separation first; retained-history and distributed timing claims only after their actual interfaces are supplied.

## 1. What was frozen

The attack pins hashes of the actual private middle-threshold input, its order-16 source prior, the deployed time-bin filters, the three-channel protocol, existing theta-Taylor calibration and analytical evidence, and the assembled observer presentations. The reuse middle case is a control.

Allowed definite outputs are CERTIFIED_FEASIBLE and CERTIFIED_INFEASIBLE. UNRESOLVED is not success. No new measurement, detector change, prior change, or substituted source history is permitted.

A single local worker has the listed files initially. Its permitted numerical attempt is one proof-only fixed-filter Hilbert pairing refinement with 408 auxiliary exponentials, 9216-bit arithmetic and a 180-second worker limit. This is not a physical or distributed delivery deadline. The contract is written before launching that worker.

The frozen middle task supplies no additional dynamic history or observed values for all 3,591 ideal observer rows. An operator presentation is not a packet of measured values. No history from the separate synthetic coordination experiments is silently attached to this task.

## 2. Explicit obstruction to the observation bridge

Let

    k = a0(0,1) a0(2,3) a0(4,5),

where a0(i,j) is the difference of the two forgotten event orders. This is an eight-term source element in I^3 at the full six-event corner.

Every assembled observer row evaluates to zero on k:

- rows at other corners cannot evaluate this typed source;
- every full-corner row in the assembled observer has two retained features;
- k has none.

The actual separately acquired task vacuum channel evaluates k to one. Its source signature and unit forward gain are checked against the owning three-channel protocol. Both protocols use background 2 and the same six primes.

Thus

    epsilon_assembled(k)=0,   vacuum_task(k)=1.

No source-compatible map from the assembled observer to the complete task observation can exist. This is a genuine source separator, independent of uncertain theta coefficients.

The declared moment cost of k is 8*2^16, below the task budget 40*2^16. Zero and k are both prior-admissible and indistinguishable to the assembled observer. However, neither satisfies the frozen middle positive-channel interval: their positive readings are zero. **They are not opposing witnesses for the frozen middle decision.**

The correct interface must retain the task's separate vacuum reading, or explicitly enlarge the assembled observer. An admitted retained history could supply additional information too, but none is supplied by this frozen task, and it cannot be assumed.

## 3. The middle threshold is a compatibility question

Write E for the fixed actual positive-channel gain, and y_- for the frozen positive interval's lower endpoint. The other frozen observations are

    crossed = 0,   vacuum = 1/100.

Positive channel gains force the crossed source coefficient to zero. The vacuum coefficient at A=2 is 1/100. The remaining positive coefficient capacity is exactly

    a_max = (40 - 8/100)/32 = 499/400.

Define

    tau = y_- / (499/400).

Under the owning source domain and acquisition model, there is an exact equivalence:

    the frozen source/prior fiber is nonempty  iff  E >= tau.

Necessity: the positive source coefficient is at least y_-/E; every additional coefficient or background adds nonnegative prior cost and cannot alter this separately labelled local reading.

Sufficiency: take the positive coefficient y_-/E, crossed coefficient zero, vacuum coefficient 1/100, and every other source coefficient and background zero. Its positive reading is exactly y_-, and its cost meets the budget precisely when E>=tau.

Equality belongs to the feasible branch. A robust interval witness, if found, is stronger: it must fit every calibration value in the retained enclosure.

This exposes a logical trap in the previous formulation. Requiring the admitted fiber to be nonempty already assumes compatibility. Deleting low-gain branches conditional on that assumption cannot then prove nonemptiness.

## 4. Why the C endpoints do not refute source sufficiency

The exact endpoint tests were replayed:

    maximum gain at C_low < tau < minimum gain at C_high.

They show opposite outcomes in an outer calibration model. But C_bin is a pairing of fixed deployed filters with fixed analytical fields. It is not an independently adjustable source coefficient.

Moreover, the low-gain branch has an EMPTY source fiber under the frozen observations and prior. It is not a second admitted source history requiring an opposite action. The high-gain endpoint gives a conditional source construction, not proof that the actual detector takes that endpoint value.

Accordingly, the attack explicitly rejects any claim of realizable opposing histories based solely on these interval endpoints.

This refutes success of that particular timed attempt. It does not rule out another implementation, a structured solver, another analytical certificate, or a larger separately declared budget. The exact verifier does not pretend to independently certify wall-clock timing from a JSON report.

## 6. Restated conjecture

### Fixed-calibration separation, with the complete acquisition retained

**For the frozen middle-threshold task, including its separately acquired vacuum reading, refinement of the existing fixed-filter analytical evidence suffices to certify either E>=tau or E<tau without new source measurements.**

A sharper sufficient hypothesis is E!=tau: a convergent certified enclosure procedure would then eventually separate the two sides. That strict separation is a conjecture, not an established property of the current enclosure. Equality must remain on the feasible side if it is proved directly.

A timely version must additionally specify, before another run, a particular certificate procedure and its resource/delivery bounds. The tested 408-dimensional procedure has not met its bound. No claim about all possible procedures follows.

Retained coherence remains necessary interface bookkeeping where observer comparisons are used. It is not the reason the analytical separation should hold. An integrated joint-history conjecture additionally needs actual history admission, numerical observation bindings and a calibrated bridge between the ideal and deployed protocols.
