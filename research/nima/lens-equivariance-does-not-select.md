# Equivariance Does Not Select a Sector Lens

Requiring a positive lens to preserve label swap and unit exterior volume
leaves the rational family

\[
G(t)=
\begin{pmatrix}a(t)&b(t)\\b(t)&a(t)\end{pmatrix},
\quad
a=\frac{1+t^2}{1-t^2},
\quad
b=\frac{2t}{1-t^2}.
\]

Every member is positive, swap-equivariant, and determinant one. Their
observable overlaps vary. Therefore naturality is a validation condition, not
a selection mechanism.

A sector source must supply additional symmetry-breaking or normalization
data. Calling a lens canonical requires exhibiting that datum or a universal
property that uniquely determines it.
