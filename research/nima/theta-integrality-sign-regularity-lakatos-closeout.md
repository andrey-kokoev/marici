# Lakatos closeout: integrality-protected sign regularity

## Closed programme

The completed-circle programme asked whether a positive source operator law
before scalar projection could orient the completed Xi transform.

It delivered a strong arithmetic theorem:

\[
\text{reciprocal spectral sparsity}
\Longrightarrow
\text{all-order labelled sign regularity}.
\]

It did not deliver an RH explanation. The attempted passage from labelled
variation diminution to real zeros of the cosine transform is false.

This block should be preserved as provenance plus a bridge no-go, not
restarted as another scalar positivity reformulation.

## Durable constructive chain

Grothendieck's source reduction gives

\[
\text{adelic vacuum}
\to
\text{integrality projection}
\to
\text{circle heat flow}
\to
\text{completion differential}
\to
B_t,
\]

where

\[
B_t
=\pi t^{5/4}

\qquad(t\ge1).
\]

Its labelled spectral kernel is

\[
b(t,\lambda)
=t^{5/4}\lambda(2\pi t\lambda-3)e^{-\pi t\lambda}.
\]

For a spectrum satisfying

\[
\sum_j\lambda_j^{-1}<\frac{2\pi}{3},
\]

every ordered minor has orientation

\[
\operatorname{sgn}\det[b(t_i,\lambda_j)]
=(-1)^{n(n-1)/2}.
\]

Integral squares satisfy the criterion with a certified normalized margin
of at least \(1/7\). Thus the faithful winding kernel is
\(\mathrm{SSR}_\infty\).

The continuous relaxation fails at order seven. This is not a contradiction:
clustered continuous labels exceed the reciprocal-density threshold, while
distinct squares cannot collide.

## Source selection

Finite modular seam jets do not determine the positive mode weights. Every
finite certificate has hostile positive perturbations in its weight kernel.

Exact modular duality is different. In the lattice-comb class, if both the
source and its Fourier transform are order-zero measures on the primal and
dual integer lattices—or if exact Fourier self-duality is imposed—translation
invariance forces constant weights.

The order-zero qualification is essential; distributional support alone
allows derivative-comb counterexamples.

Thus:

\[
\text{integrality selects the labels},
\qquad
\text{exact modularity selects the weights}.
\]

## Exhausted bridge

Spectral \(\mathrm{SSR}_\infty\) controls the constructor

\[
\{c_m\}\longmapsto\sum_m c_m b(t,m^2).
\]

It does not control the subsequent constructor tree

\[
\text{fixed positive weights}
\to\Phi(u)
\to\int_0^\infty\Phi(u)\cos(zu)\,du.
\]

The smallest rational witness uses

\[
e^{-u}+e^{-2u},
\]

whose cosine transform has zeros at \(\pm i\sqrt2\).

The stronger analytic witness uses the positive even Schwartz source

\[
e^{-u^2}+e^{-2u^2}.
\]

Its entire Fourier transform vanishes at

\[
z^2=8\left(\log\sqrt2+(2k+1)\pi i\right).
\]

Therefore positivity, evenness, rapid decay, entire continuation, positive
mode weights, and labelled \(\mathrm{SSR}_\infty\) do not imply the
Hermite-Biehler/de Branges normal-current law.

Translation PF-infinity is not a repair: the existing Schoenberg audit
shows it is incompatible with the already known zeros of the completed
transform.

## Exact remaining boundary

The denominator-free target is

\[
\mathcal D(z,w)
=\frac{X'(z)\overline{X(w)}-X(z)\overline{X'(w)}}
{\pi(\bar w-z)}.
\]

On the diagonal,

\[
\mathcal D(z,z)>0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\partial_y|X(x+iy)|^2>0.
\]

Within the completed Xi class this is RH-equivalent. Integrality and
modularity now fully determine the source but do not orient this two-copy
interaction.

Any further theorem must act directly on the fully integrated Wigner
hyperbolic marginal or the denominator-free Loewner/de Branges kernel. It
must not be inferred from:

- pointwise positivity of \(B_t\);
- labelled spectral sign regularity;
- finite modular jets;
- exact weight rigidity alone;
- translation total positivity;
- generic positive Fourier summation.

> Adelic integrality and completion generate an all-order

## Decision

Stop enlarging the spectral-total-positivity programme.

Reopen this lane only for a source-derived inequality on the exact
two-parameter kernel \(\mathcal D(z,w)\), or a finite exact falsifier of such
an inequality. Otherwise the programme is complete as an arithmetic
provenance theorem and bridge no-go.
