# Source sewing may canonically annihilate non-descending odd jets

Date: 2026-08-23

## Two independently derived instances

### Cosmological occurrence sewing

The two occurrence-resolved lower classes possess canonical meromorphic
de Rham and endpoint-jet data, but their individual physical periods are not
source-canonical.  The source-canonical object is their sewn unsplit
combination.

In the existing exact occurrence-jet census, the two coordinate derivatives
of the sewn class have the same transverse \(w^{-3}\) residue at six rational
points:

\[
q(\nabla_x\eta_{\rm sewn})
=q(\nabla_y\eta_{\rm sewn})
=\frac{17(y^2-x^2)}{8x^2y^2}[w^{-3}].
\]

Consequently the antisymmetric derivative is transverse-zero in every tested
fiber:

\[
q((\nabla_x-\nabla_y)\eta_{\rm sewn})=0.
\]

This remains a bounded exact pattern pending a symbolic identity and the
coordinate comparison with \(d(X_2-X_3)\).

### Arithmetic theta sewing

Each individually folded theta label has nonzero odd one-sided boundary jets.
Those jets produce spurious algebraic Fourier tails.  The modularly completed
source satisfies

\[
\sum_{n\ge1}\phi_n^{(2k+1)}(0)=0,
\]

so full source resummation before folding or asymptotics cancels every odd
boundary jet.  Large labelwise contributions then sew into a much smaller
completed transform.

## Cross-sector conjecture

The common mechanism may be:

\[
\boxed{
\text{source completion annihilates a typed submodule of jet data that does
not descend to a canonical completed readout.}
}
\]

This is stronger than cancellation of inconvenient terms, but the square is
parity graded rather than an ordinary commuting square.  Grothendieck's exact
theta audit gives

\[
DS_+=S_-D,
\qquad
DS_-=S_+D+2\,\operatorname{ev}_0\Sigma\,\delta_0.
\]

The defect is the seam boundary current.  Modular completion cancels its
labelled sum by killing the odd one-sided seam jets.  This is a descent
condition; it is not a claim that the completed source itself vanishes.

The earlier kernel formulation is therefore withdrawn for theta.  Its typed
statement is

\[
B_{\rm int}\Sigma=0,
\qquad
DS_{\pm}-S_{\mp}D
=\operatorname{ev}_{\rm seam},
\qquad
\operatorname{Readout}=\overline{R}\,S,
\]

where \(B_{\rm int}\) records the independently defined internal-boundary
current.  Global odd completed sections may survive: \(\Phi'\) is the
canonical theta counterexample to “sewing destroys all odd data.”

Cosmology has a different, genuinely kernel-valued realization.  For
\(A=\mathbf F_3[C_3]\), ordinary source sewing is the augmentation
\(\varepsilon:A\to\mathbf F_3\), and the canonical Tate line is
\[
H_{\rm Tate}=I/(g-1)I,
\qquad I=\ker\varepsilon.
\]
Thus every unsplit readout factoring through \(\varepsilon\) kills that
line.  This augmentation theorem and theta's smooth-descent theorem are two
typed consequences of source sewing, not the same kernel theorem.

## Falsifier

The conjecture fails if either sector contains a source-admissible internal
boundary current that:

1. survives source completion while remaining dependent on a label hierarchy,
   endpoint trivialization, or other noncanonical local choice; or
2. produces a derivative-square defect away from independently declared seam
   or external-boundary support.

A global odd section surviving sewing is not a falsifier.  It refutes only the
withdrawn claim that odd parity itself is a sewing-kernel condition.

For cosmology the remaining connection-level test is symbolic:

\[
q((\nabla_x-\nabla_y)\eta_{\rm sewn})\stackrel?=0
\]

including endpoint jets.  For theta the local folded-source test is now exact;
the stronger Gauss--Manin relative-chain square remains unproved.

No cross-sector coefficient identification is asserted.
