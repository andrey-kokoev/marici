# Stable-exchange to Haar-dilation defect

The Xi-relative identity compares the two stable histories:

$$
u_-(z)-u_+(z)=\tau(z)e_z.
$$

The relative-Haar modular law compares a state with its prime dilation:

$$
\Psi\longmapsto T_p\Psi.
$$

These are different arrows. The fixed-forcing correlation graph places both constructions on one carrier but does not identify them.

Let

$$
J_\Phi(u)=\bigl(C_\Phi u,\Phi\otimes u\bigr)
$$

be the joint-graph embedding. Define the prime route-matching defect

$$
\Omega_p(z)
=J_\Phi(u_+(z))-T_pJ_\Phi(u_-(z)),
$$

with the source-prescribed orientation convention. The required closure theorem is

$$
\Omega_p(z)=\tau(z)H_p(z)
$$

for a holomorphic graph-valued section `H_p`, together with the correct normalization ensuring that at `tau(z)=0`

$$
J_\Phi(u_+(z))=T_pJ_\Phi(u_-(z)).
$$

Only this equality makes the Xi cycle close the relative-Haar energy square. The already known identity `u_- - u_+ = tau e_z` corresponds to an identity edge, not a prime-dilation edge, and cannot substitute for this theorem.

If the defect is Xi-divisible and the common state is nonzero, relative-Haar covariance gives

$$
(1-p^{-2\operatorname{Re}z})
\mathcal E_z(J_\Phi u_-(z))=0
$$

at every Xi zero, forcing `Re z=0`.
