# The Gram Vertical Excess Has Two Characters in Two Tor Grades

On the rank-two special fiber, the full algebra is

\[
B=k[w]/(w^2+R),
\qquad R\ne0.
\]

It has rank two over the external coefficient field. The deck involution

\[
\sigma(w)=-w
\]

gives the canonical decomposition, in characteristic different from two,

\[
\boxed{B=B^+\oplus B^- = k\langle1\rangle\oplus k\langle w\rangle.}
\]

Equivalently, the algebra trace satisfies

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(a+bw)=2a,
\]

so the anti-invariant Kummer line is canonically the trace-zero part

\[
B^- = \ker(\operatorname{Tr}).
\]

Thus selecting \(w\) is not an arbitrary splitting, but it is only half of
the full special-fiber algebra. The invariant unit line remains present.

## Derived specialization

Formally near either rank-drop branch point, the previous one-parameter
equations make the excess module

\[
M\simeq (k[e]/(e))\otimes_k B.
\]

Using the length-one Cartier resolution

\[
[k[e]\xrightarrow{e}k[e]],
\]

derived specialization to \(e=0\) gives

\[
\operatorname{Tor}_0^{k[e]}(M,k)\simeq B,
\qquad
\operatorname{Tor}_1^{k[e]}(M,k)\simeq B,
\]

and no higher Tor. Each grade contains one trivial and one sign character:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{gr}_{\rm Tor}
\simeq
(\mathbf1\oplus\mathrm{sgn})_{0}
\oplus
(\mathbf1\oplus\mathrm{sgn})_{1}.
}
\]

This is the minimal supported coefficient complex generated by the nonflat
Gram specialization. A construction retaining only the odd line must derive
a trace-zero or reduced operation; ordinary specialization retains both
characters and both Tor grades.

Physical activation remains separate. Benincasa's external-Gram current may
pair with some part of this complex, but the source chain/Gysin map must
determine which character and grade survive.
