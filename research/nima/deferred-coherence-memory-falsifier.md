## Falsification

\[
\boxed{\text{nonzero memory}\not\Rightarrow\text{coherence defect}.}
\]

The broad Deferred-Coherence Conjecture is therefore false. It conflated
three typed channels:

\[
\text{interaction}
\longmapsto
\bigl(
\text{value/boundary data},
\text{coherence-defect data},
\text{record interface}
\bigr).
\]

- **Value/boundary data** may be nonzero even when every comparison square
  commutes.
- **Coherence-defect data** is defined only relative to a declared composition,
  transport, or comparison law and records its failure or higher filler.
- **Operational records** arise only after a source, admitted effect, positive
  pairing, and record map are supplied.

## Surviving Deutsch--Popperian conjecture

Interactions transport both ordinary boundary values and coherence-defect
data. Witness objects type their evaluation separately. Only the second
channel is deferred coherence debt; neither persistence nor observability
alone makes a class an obstruction.

This retains the useful part of the proposal while making it falsifiable:
every claimed coherence residue must name the square, differential, or
composition law whose defect it represents.
