# Many-many order-exchange codimension-two filler

For any two restriction directions `r_i,r_j`, the side-face cells must satisfy

$$
\beta_{ij}\,r_ir_j
=
 r_ir_j\,\beta
$$

with equality of the two ordered routes

$$
 r_i(\beta_j)\,\beta_i
=
 r_j(\beta_i)\,\beta_j.
$$

The relevant pairs are:

- input/output arity;
- arity/endpoint attachment;
- arity/wall-jump transport;
- endpoint/wall-jump transport.

These identities are checked on the labelled common core. Their simultaneous validity supplies the codimension-two fillers of the Gray cube. A nonzero discrepancy is the corresponding face-curvature class and must be retained as relative data.
