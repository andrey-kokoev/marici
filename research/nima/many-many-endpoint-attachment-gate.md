# Many-many endpoint attachment gate

Let `G_int` be the interior projective graph and `G_end` the independent endpoint graph sector. The minimal global carrier is their direct sum:

$$
\mathcal G_{\rm global}
=\mathcal G_{\rm int}\oplus\mathcal G_{\rm end}.
$$

A further pushout identification requires agreement of the finite endpoint rows and compatibility of the boundary readout:

$$
B_{\rm int}|_{\rm core}=B_{\rm end}|_{\rm core}.
$$

The resulting topology is the final/projective graph topology generated by the two sectors. Endpoint evaluation remains a projective coordinate, while interior pair responses retain their graph norm.

Status: independent endpoint sector constructed as the minimal carrier model; pushout identification remains an optional stronger gate.
