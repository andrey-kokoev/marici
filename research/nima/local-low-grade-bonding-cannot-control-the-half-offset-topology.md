# Local low-grade bonding cannot control the half-offset topology

## Question

Can the established finite-cutoff and locally uniform laws on the coupled
low-grade source packet force its half-offset weighted bound?

## Translated-defect family

Fix a forbidden decay rate \(0<a<1/2\) and a compactly supported continuous
profile \(\varphi\) with \(\varphi(0)=1\). For \(X>0\), set

\[
d_X(x)=e^{-aX}\varphi(x-X).
\]

On every fixed compact interval, \(d_X\) eventually vanishes. It therefore
converges to zero pointwise, locally uniformly, and in every fixed local
Sobolev norm. Any finite family of observations supported in a fixed bounded
window also eventually reports exactly zero.

For every \(\alpha>a\), however,

\[
\|d_X\|_\alpha
\geq e^{\alpha X}|d_X(X)|
=e^{(\alpha-a)X}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]

Thus local convergence and cutoffwise coherence do not imply continuity in
the weighted topology required by the half-offset gate.

## Source interpretation

The hostile does not alter the already-closed connected Euler tail. It may be
placed entirely in the unresolved coupled packet consisting of the primitive,
square, endpoint, gamma, seam, and projective-infinity channels. It models a
defect whose amplitude decreases while its support escapes to larger spectral
height. Every bounded observation sees less of it, but the half-offset weight
sees more.

This is the exact completion-at-infinity failure in the new coordinates. The
missing result cannot be assembled from:

- finite-cutoff identities;
- pointwise convergence;
- locally uniform convergence;
- fixed-window Sobolev control;
- any fixed finite family of compactly supported probes.

## Required new constructor

A successful pre-divisor theorem must supply at least one source-authorized
global mechanism:

1. a cutoff-independent weighted energy estimate coupling all six low-grade
   channels;
2. a transport law whose graph norm contains the exponential height weight;
3. a conserved boundary flux that forbids translated low-rate packets; or
4. a compactness/tightness theorem derived from labelled source incidence.

Merely declaring the weighted topology is insufficient. The source bonding
maps must be uniformly continuous in it, and the completion maps must preserve
that uniform constant.

## Finite falsifier

Choose \(a=1/4\), \(\alpha=0.49\), and a triangular profile supported on
\([-1,1]\). For centers \(X=4,8,16,32\), every fixed observation window
\([-2,2]\) sees zero, whereas

\[
e^{\alpha X}|d_X(X)|=e^{0.24X}
\]

grows strictly and exceeds two thousand at the last center.

## Verdict

The current local and cutoffwise source laws do not reach the half-offset
topology. The next admissible claim must be a genuinely global tightness or
weighted-energy theorem for the entire low-grade packet. Without it, the
pre-divisor route remains open but unsupported.
