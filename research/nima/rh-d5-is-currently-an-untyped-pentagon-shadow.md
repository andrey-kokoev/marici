# RH D5 Is Currently an Untyped Pentagon Shadow

## Question

Does the abstract (D_5) extension of the five-channel reciprocal reflection
come from a source-authorized order-five constructor, or does it appear only
after forgetting channel and adapter types?

## Raw-channel obstruction

The five-channel packet contains four state channels and one arrow-level
coherence channel:

\[
(P,Q,M_+,M_-;L).
\]

Every order-five permutation is transitive on the five positions. It must send
the coherence channel into a state position and eventually send a state channel
into the coherence position.

Therefore no order-five permutation preserves the raw type partition

\[
4+1.
\]

The known reciprocal reflection is admissible because it exchanges the two
tail states, exchanges the two directed seam states, and fixes the coherence
line. The missing rotation is not an automorphism of this typed raw packet.

## Associativity pentagon

Four composable adapters have five parenthesizations. Forgetting the adapter
labels and retaining only pentagon adjacency gives an abstract graph with ten
automorphisms. Their orders are

\[
1,2,2,2,2,2,5,5,5,5.
\]

This is the dihedral group (D_5).

However, each parenthesization carries a distinct ordered interval signature
recording which adjacent adapters were composed at each internal node. Once
those signatures are retained, the only strict automorphism is the identity.

A separately sourced reversal duality can admit one reflection. It does not
produce an order-five rotation.

Thus the categorical pentagon explains why (D_5) appears combinatorially,
but it does not supply a physical or source-derived (D_5) action.

## Comparison with Flavor

Flavor's (D_5) acted on two actual source fields transforming in inequivalent
faithful doublets. The rotation and reflection were both defined on the field
space before the invariant census.

The current RH construction has only the reflection. Its provisional
representation

\[
\mathbf1\oplus V_1\oplus V_2
\]

is a valid extension of the observed (C_2) character data, but the order-five
generator has no typed source action.

This makes the invariant census conditional. It answers what a (D_5)
completion would permit, not what theta/Tate data currently possess.

## Possible categorical repair

An order-five constructor could exist at a higher categorical level if all five
positions were reified as objects of one common type. For example, it could act
on five factorization presentations together with comparison cells rather than
on four states and one line.

Such a lift would require:

1. five source-derived presentations of one complete boundary constructor;
2. typed equivalences between adjacent presentations;
3. a rotation functor carrying each presentation to the next;
4. a fifth-power coherence cell identifying the full rotation with identity;
5. compatibility with reciprocal reversal;
6. preservation of the distinguished source state and scalar readout.

None of these data follows from the untyped pentagon graph.

## Disposition

The direct search for an RH (D_5) symmetry stops at a typing obstruction:

- the five-channel reflection has the correct pentagonal cycle type;
- the provisional (D_5) invariant census is exact;
- raw state/coherence typing forbids an order-five permutation;
- the categorical associativity pentagon has (D_5) only after labels are
  forgotten;
- a source-derived higher categorical rotation remains possible but wholly
  unconstructed.

The durable gain is the cubic hostile from the conditional census. Even the
maximal abstract (D_5) completion would allow two mixed cubic constructors,
and one scalar zero would not annihilate them.

The next productive move is therefore not to proclaim (D_5). It is to ask
whether the actual theta/Tate constructor category contains five equivalent
factorization presentations of one boundary operation. Without that common
type, the pentagonal symmetry is a shadow created by forgetting distinctions.
