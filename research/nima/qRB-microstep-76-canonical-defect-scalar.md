# qRB microstep 76: canonical defect scalar

For each adapted finite packet, define

$$
T_\alpha
=B_\alpha^{-1/2}C_\alpha B_\alpha^{-1/2}.
$$

The least balancing coefficient is exactly

$$
\varepsilon_\alpha^{\min}=\|(T_\alpha)_-\|.
$$

Equivalently, it is the negative of the smallest generalized eigenvalue of `C_alpha` relative to `B_alpha`, when that eigenvalue is negative.

This replaces an unspecified stabilization by one measurable scalar. The asymptotic common-edge problem is now precisely:

$$
\varepsilon_\alpha^{\min}\longrightarrow0.
$$
