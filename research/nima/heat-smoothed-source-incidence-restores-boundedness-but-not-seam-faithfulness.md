# Heat-smoothed source incidence restores boundedness but not seam faithfulness

## Result

The Fourier hostile showing that derivative evaluation is unbounded in the bare first-order Jacobi energy lies outside the source-generated heat orbit.

On every positive-time heat range, derivative evaluation is bounded by the first-order energy with an explicit constant. Hence the source may avoid a sixth-order ambient Sobolev completion if its incidence factors through a uniformly positive heat time.

This repairs upper boundedness. It does not produce a lower observer margin at the reciprocal seam, where the scale-odd current must vanish.

## Positive-time heat range

Let

\[
P_\tau=e^{\tau\partial_z^2/(4\pi)}
\]

on the circle, with Fourier multiplier

\[
\widehat{P_\tau f}(n)=e^{-\pi\tau n^2}\widehat f(n).
\]

Let

\[
F=P_\tau f.
\]

The derivative-comb observer is

\[
O(F)=-F'(0)
=
-2\pi i\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}
n e^{-\pi\tau n^2}\widehat f(n).
\]

The first-order heat energy is

\[
\mathcal E(F)
=
4\pi^2\sum_n
n^2e^{-2\pi\tau n^2}|\widehat f(n)|^2.
\]

To compare point evaluation with this energy, split the heat time:

\[
F=P_{\tau/2}G,
\qquad
G=P_{\tau/2}f.
\]

Then

\[
F'(0)
=
2\pi i\sum_n
n e^{-\pi(\tau/2)n^2}\widehat G(n).
\]

Cauchy--Schwarz yields

\[
|F'(0)|^2
\le
\left(
\sum_{n\ne0}e^{-\pi\tau n^2}
\right)
\left(
4\pi^2\sum_n n^2|\widehat G(n)|^2
\right).
\]

Therefore

\[
|O(P_\tau f)|^2
\le
C(\tau)\,
\mathcal E(P_{\tau/2}f),
\qquad
C(\tau)=\sum_{n\ne0}e^{-\pi\tau n^2}.
\]

Equivalently, if the source graph norm retains the midpoint history \(P_{\tau/2}f\), derivative evaluation is bounded without imposing an arbitrary ambient \(H^s\) norm.

For every \(\tau\ge\tau_0>0\),

\[
C(\tau)\le C(\tau_0)<\infty.
\]

As \(\tau\downarrow0\),

\[
C(\tau)\sim\tau^{-1/2},
\]

so the bound degenerates exactly when positive-time smoothing is lost.

## Source orbit

The Jacobi theta packet is itself a heat orbit. On a compact reciprocal-scale region

\[
0<r_-\le r\le r_+<\infty,
\]

one may choose a positive common sub-time \(\tau_0<r_-\) and factor

\[
\Theta(\,\cdot\,,r)
=
P_{\tau_0}\Theta(\,\cdot\,,r-\tau_0).
\]

All source-derived spatial jets commute with \(P_{\tau_0}\). Thus the odd fifth-order jet and its derivative-comb contraction lie in a uniformly smoothed graph range on every compact positive-scale region.

This proves a source-native upper bound for the local odd contraction, conditional on retaining the intermediate heat-history energy used by the factorization.

It also explains why the arbitrary Fourier hostile is irrelevant to that restricted range: its coherent high-frequency stack is not uniformly in the image of a fixed positive heat time with bounded preimage energy.

## No seam lower margin

Upper boundedness must remain separate from observer faithfulness.

The completed source satisfies

\[
\Phi(-u)=\Phi(u),
\]

so

\[
\Phi'(0)=0.
\]

The exact odd realization gives

\[
\langle\Delta',J_{\mathrm{odd}}(0)\rangle=0.
\]

Therefore no lower frame estimate of the form

\[
|\langle\Delta',J_{\mathrm{odd}}(u)\rangle|
\ge
m\|J_{\mathrm{odd}}(u)\|
\]

can hold on a parameter set containing \(u=0\), unless the source jet itself vanishes there in the same quotient. That vanishing has not been established and should not be assumed.

This is the correct seam behavior for a reciprocal-odd scalar. The seam value is a forced zero, not evidence that orientation has disappeared.

The appropriate alternatives are:

- demand lower margins only on compact sets separated from the seam;
- retain the first normal derivative at the seam as the typed odd jet;
- pair the odd scalar with an additional port so the joint observer remains faithful at \(u=0\).

## Prime-labelled completion

Because positive-time smoothing acts in the Jacobi factor and prime idempotents act in the label factor, the smoothing factorization preserves exact prime diagonality and cutoff naturality.

In the source Euler half-density metric, the upper constant is controlled by

\[
C(\tau_0)
\]

uniformly over prime cutoffs. Raw prime norms remain the wrong frame for lower estimates.

## Remaining theorem

The local boundedness gate contracts to a source-history question:

> Does the actual adjacent-window-to-Jacobi incidence retain a uniformly positive heat sub-time and its midpoint energy on every compact off-end parameter region?

If yes, the derivative-comb contraction is a bounded relative Green observer on the source incidence range. The remaining seam-faithfulness problem must then be handled by a jet-valued, not scalar-valued, seam port.
