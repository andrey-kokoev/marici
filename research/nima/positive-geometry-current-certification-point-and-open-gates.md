## Open gates

### Primary mathematical gates

1. Prove positivity of the source-derived completed bulk-boundary Schur form on every finite observer packet.
2. Prove compatibility of these positive forms under refinement.
3. Prove source faithfulness and density at projective completion.
4. Prove the uniform signed weighted-Hankel discrepancy estimate, or an equivalent asymptotic threshold theorem. This remains the RH-bearing gate.
5. Verify the compact Weil source identity against the external authoritative normalization.

### Tertiary certification gates

1. Assemble prime boundary-jet orders 6 through 149 as one coupled vector.
2. Bound the analytic gamma and endpoint coefficient tails.
3. Replace the unstable forward interval Legendre recurrence by direct evaluation, backward recurrence, or stable antiderivative assembly.
4. Extend unconditional finite-window certificates beyond the current L=0.75 packet, including L=0.55 and the remaining threshold interval.

## Priority decision

Further numerical tail certification is tertiary. It can validate individual finite cells, but it cannot close the primary uniform positivity, source-faithfulness, or asymptotic arithmetic gates.

The project should therefore move on from incremental L=0.75 tail certification. The next work should target the source-level coupled positivity theorem or the weighted-Hankel discrepancy gate rather than additional numerical blocks.
