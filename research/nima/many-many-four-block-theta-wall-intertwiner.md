# Many-many four-block theta-to-wall intertwiner

For every finite family `F`, retain the ordered Hadamard block tuple

$$
\mathbf H_{\mathcal F}=(P_{\mathcal F},Q_{\mathcal F},R_{\mathcal F},N_{\mathcal F}).
$$

Define the theta-side ordered cross block by

$$
C^{\theta}_{\mathcal F}
=\frac12(P_{\mathcal F}-N_{\mathcal F}-Q_{\mathcal F}+R_{\mathcal F}).
$$

Define the wall-side tuple `H^wall_F` from the four faithful Pauli/linking blocks and set

$$
C^{\rm wall}_{\mathcal F}
=\frac12(P^{\rm wall}_{\mathcal F}-N^{\rm wall}_{\mathcal F}
-Q^{\rm wall}_{\mathcal F}+R^{\rm wall}_{\mathcal F}).
$$

The completion-stable intertwiner `I_F` is directed from the theta carrier to the window/wall carrier and satisfies

$$
I_{\mathcal F}:\mathcal E^{\theta}_{\mathcal F}\to\mathcal E^{\rm wall}_{\mathcal F},
\qquad
I_{\mathcal F}C^{\theta}_{\mathcal F}
=C^{\rm wall}_{\mathcal F}I_{\mathcal F}
$$

on the common source core and commutes with finite-family refinement.

This retains the ordered polarization before scalar output summation. Its projective limit supplies the many-many ordered wall-tail form. The reverse comparison requires a uniform lower frame bound and is treated as a separate upgrade.
