# The composite radial kernel is an extension of interval cycles by realized endpoint–Wronskian balances

## Question

How do the interval-cycle kernel and the internal endpoint–Wronskian balance combine when a polarized source is sent first to an unlabelled radial feature and then through the fixed codiagonal?

## Claim boundary

For any declared full-feature synthesis \(U\) followed by the radial codiagonal \(D\), the composite kernel fits one exact sequence. Its first term is the source feature kernel, including interval cycles. Its quotient is exactly the part of the endpoint–Wronskian balance realized by the source range. This separates the two null mechanisms without assuming that either is a Green radical.

## Two-stage map

Let \(C\) be the polarized source and let \(X\) be the full oriented radial feature space retaining

\[
(-\rho(0),E_+,W_+;+\rho(0),E_-,W_-).
\]

Take a continuous source-derived synthesis

\[
U:C\longrightarrow X
\]

and the fixed codiagonal

\[
D:X\longrightarrow Y.
\]

On the minimal radial graph,

\[
N_{\rm bal}:=\ker D
=
\left\{
(0,e_+,2e_+;0,e_-,2e_-)
\right\}.
\]

The composite observable is

\[
K=DU.
\]

## Exact kernel sequence

By definition,

\[
\ker K
=U^{-1}(N_{\rm bal}).
\]

Restriction of \(U\) to \(\ker K\) has kernel \(\ker U\) and image

\[
\operatorname{ran}U\cap N_{\rm bal}.
\]

The first isomorphism theorem gives the exact sequence

\[
0
\longrightarrow
\ker U
\longrightarrow
\ker(DU)
\overset{U}{\longrightarrow}
\operatorname{ran}U\cap N_{\rm bal}
\longrightarrow
0.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\ker(DU)/\ker U
\cong
\operatorname{ran}U\cap N_{\rm bal}.
\]

Thus every composite null packet has two typed components:

1. source provenance already erased by \(U\);
2. a nonzero full radial feature in the balanced endpoint–Wronskian channel.

## Interval-cycle specialization

If \(U\) is the common unlabelled full-feature synthesis and its feature kernel is the translated interval-cycle space, then

\[
\ker U=Z_1(G).
\]

The sequence becomes

\[
0
\longrightarrow
Z_1(G)
\longrightarrow
\ker(DU)
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{ran}U\cap N_{\rm bal}
\longrightarrow
0.
\]

The composite kernel equals the interval-cycle kernel precisely when

\[
\operatorname{ran}U\cap N_{\rm bal}=\{0\}.
\]

It is strictly larger precisely when the source realizes a nonzero complete endpoint–Wronskian balance.

## Quotient order

Because \(D\) is applied after \(U\), it descends to the feature quotient by \(\ker U\). Quotienting source cycles first and then taking the balanced kernel gives the same composite quotient as taking \(\ker(DU)\) directly:

\[
C/\ker(DU)
\cong
(C/\ker U)
\big/
\left(
\operatorname{ran}U\cap N_{\rm bal}
\right),
\]

with the second denominator transported through the injective realization of \(C/\ker U\) in \(X\).

This is a quotient-composition identity, not evidence that the two kernels are physically equivalent.

## Source-fixed restriction

The completed-theta shell ray misses the interval-cycle space. Existing positivity excludes individual diagonal shells and same-sign diagonal loadings from \(N_{\rm bal}\), while the signed complete source intersection has been reduced to endpoint-tail independence in the scalar shell sector. The full polarized intersection

\[
\operatorname{ran}U\cap N_{\rm bal}
\]

remains a separate question when off-diagonal source coefficients are allowed.

## Green-radical boundary

Neither term in the exact sequence is automatically a Green radical. A downstream form pulled back through \(DU\) has radical containing \(\ker(DU)\); equality requires nondegeneracy on \(\operatorname{ran}(DU)\).

Therefore a G4 radical claim requires three independent identifications:

1. \(\ker U\), including whether interval cycles are erased;
2. \(\operatorname{ran}U\cap N_{\rm bal}\);
3. nondegeneracy of the declared Green form on the composite range.

## Disposition

The combined source and radial null structure is now exact: the composite kernel is an extension of feature-erasure cycles by the balanced features actually realized in the source range. G4 still does not expose \(U\), its range, or the downstream form, so the numerical size and Green interpretation of this extension remain blocked. No RH conclusion is authorized.
