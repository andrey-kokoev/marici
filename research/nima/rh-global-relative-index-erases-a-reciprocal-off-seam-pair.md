# A global relative index erases a reciprocal off-seam pair

## Natural seam orientation

For two sectors glued along a common boundary, Green and Stokes formulas give
opposite induced boundary orientations. At the charge-lattice level, the
natural seam incidence therefore has the form

\[
j(k)=(-k,k).
\]

This is also the incidence needed to identify the two opposite regularizations
of one boundary zero.

## Exact collision of meanings

The right-half-plane Blaschke factor has Toeplitz index `-1`. Its reciprocal
left-sector inverse has index `+1`. The paired off-seam charge is therefore

\[
q_{\mathrm{off}}=(-1,1).
\]

But

\[
q_{\mathrm{off}}=j(1).
\]

Hence the global relative quotient

\[
(K_+\oplus K_-)/\operatorname{im}j
\]

declares the reciprocal off-seam pair trivial. The same relation that removes
the boundary-regularization ambiguity also removes the hostile pair.

## No contradiction

The quotient is behaving correctly for the information it retains. It records
only total sector charge and forgets where that charge is supported. A seam
kernel and a reciprocal pair of interior kernels can have the same oriented
charge vector.

Therefore a global integer relative index cannot distinguish:

- one state genuinely supported on the seam;
- two states supported in opposite open sectors and related by reciprocity.

This is exactly the distinction RH requires.

## Required refinement

The obstruction must retain support before quotienting. Candidate carriers
include:

- a sheaf of local index classes over the spectral plane;
- divisor-valued or cycle-valued relative K-theory;
- a constructible cosheaf with separate seam and bulk stalks;
- a filtered index by normal distance from the seam;
- a microlocal boundary class whose wavefront distinguishes boundary support;
- a relative complex whose homology is supported on typed loci.

In such a refinement, seam incidence lands in classes supported at normal
coordinate zero. An off-seam reciprocal pair remains supported at nonzero
opposite normal coordinates and cannot be killed merely because its integrated
charge agrees.

## New circularity danger

Support cannot be assigned by inspecting the unknown scalar divisor. The
support grading must arise from the source construction itself: valuation
sector, boundary carrier, normal semigroup, or another independently typed
locus.

Otherwise divisor-valued refinement merely records the zero locations one is
trying to constrain.

## C2 interpretation

The fifth tower is not a single higher cell or one global obstruction group.
It must be a support-sensitive family of higher cells. Integration to a global
index is a lossy projection that identifies seam and reciprocal-bulk defects.

The order of operations matters:

1. construct the locus-typed source complex;
2. compute local or supported obstruction classes;
3. apply seam incidence only to seam-supported classes;
4. test whether any bulk-supported class remains;
5. integrate globally only after exclusion.

## DPC

Reject a proposed index proof if:

- it uses only the pair of sector integers;
- opposite sector charges are quotiented before support is typed;
- a reciprocal off-seam Blaschke pair becomes a seam boundary;
- localization is defined from the completed scalar zero set;
- finite-height support is lost at completion;
- the source cannot distinguish seam states from states approaching the seam.

A surviving construction must provide source-derived support functors and show
that seam incidence has image only in the seam-supported subcategory.

## Verdict

The global relative integer index is falsified as an RH obstruction. It erases
the exact reciprocal off-seam pair that must be excluded. The route survives
only after sheafifying or otherwise localizing the relative class before the
seam quotient.
