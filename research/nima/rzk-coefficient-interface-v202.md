# v202: exact three-channel restriction of W

The target certificate gives

`W=Delta*theta-omega_E`,

where `Delta` is the product of the six short normal parameters. Since `pi(omega_E)=Delta*theta` and `kV(W)=dV(omega_V)`, its complete boundary restriction is

`rho(W)=(0,dV(omega_V)_plus,dV(omega_V)_minus)`.

The endpoint primitive `omega_V` has exactly two terms, one on each endpoint cell:

- `[02,04,24;02,04,24]` with coefficient `X02 X04 X24 u03 u13 u14 u15 u25 u35`;
- `[13,15,35;13,15,35]` with coefficient `X13 X15 X35 u02 u03 u04 u14 u24 u25`.

Its endpoint differential has six nonzero terms. Thus W has zero generic-Q detector and a nonzero paired endpoint boundary. It is an appropriate reciprocal/reflection channel, but cannot alone provide all three amplitude coordinates.

A scalar reciprocal detector must first compare the two displayed monomials under a physical specialization. Their raw coefficients are both positive; assigning an odd difference before constructing that specialization would fit the desired sign. The primary amplitude detector must come from a supported class other than W.
