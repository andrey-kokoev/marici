# Spherical multiplicity one does not fill the based rung-four horn when the terminal probe vanishes on the line

## Question

Can the multiplicity-one mechanism in the spherical-variety reference supply
the missing based rung-four filler?

## Fixed spectral fiber

Tate's zeta construction is multiplicity one at a fixed Mellin character: the
spherical spectral fiber generated by the unramified vector is a line. Suppose
the corrected response at `z` lies in a source line

\[
V_z=\mathbb C b_z.
\]

Let the terminal scalar probe be

\[
\ell_z(cb_z)=c\,\Xi(z),
\]

and let the positive bulk form be

\[
H_z(cb_z)=|c|^2E_p(b_z),
\qquad E_p(b_z)>0.
\]

At a Xi zero, `ell_z` is the zero map on the entire multiplicity-one line,
while `H_z` remains positive on every nonzero vector. Hence

\[
\ker\ell_z=V_z
\]

and

\[
H_z|_{\ker\ell_z}\ne0.
\]

The bulk form cannot descend through the terminal probe, even though the source
fiber has dimension one.

## Consequence

Multiplicity one controls source ambiguity and can justify uniqueness of the
selected response up to scale. It supplies no relation between terminal
nullity and the boundary-work defect at a parameter where the terminal
functional itself vanishes.

A filler still requires a second source-derived probe or boundary relation
whose restriction to `V_z` remains nonzero and whose compatibility equation
forces

\[
\mathcal R_p(b_z)=0.
\]

## Disposition

The spherical-period reference does not reopen the rank-one route. The viable
new-evidence target is narrower: an independent based boundary probe that does
not vanish identically on the Xi spectral line.
