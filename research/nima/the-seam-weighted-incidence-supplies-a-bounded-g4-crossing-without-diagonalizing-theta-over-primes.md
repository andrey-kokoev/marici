# The seam-weighted incidence supplies a bounded G4 crossing without diagonalizing theta over primes

## Two arithmetic realizations

The retained G1--G3 carrier uses a labelled realization

\[
B_{\rm lab}x=(b_px_p)_p
\]

so that prime fibres remain orthogonal.

The seam return construction also defines the later unlabelled incidence

\[
B_\Sigma x
=
\sum_pb_px_p
\]

into the common analytic history space \(H_\theta\).  This is not the
unweighted codiagonal on arbitrary labelled history packets. It is a
source-weighted map defined only on the arithmetic coefficient space.

## Boundedness from the seam metric

The source norm is

\[
\|x\|_U^2
=
\sum_p(\log p)|x_p|^2.
\]

After identifying \(U\) with ordinary \(\ell^2\), the incidence columns are

\[
\frac{b_p}{\sqrt{\log p}}.
\]

The exact theta-cut estimate gives

\[
\sum_p
\frac{\|b_p\|^2}{\log p}
<\infty.
\]

Therefore

\[
B_\Sigma:U\to H_\theta
\]

is Hilbert--Schmidt and hence bounded.

## No unweighted theta diagonal

The common history map is

\[
C=(V\;B_\Sigma):
\mathbb C_\theta\oplus U\to H_\theta,
\]

\[
C(c,x)=c\Phi+B_\Sigma x.
\]

This does not send \(c\Phi\) to the divergent labelled packet
\((c\Phi)_p\). The theta source remains one unlabelled vector in
\(H_\theta\), while arithmetic labels are aggregated only through the
summable seam-weighted incidence.

Thus the earlier objection to \(\Phi_{\rm diag}\in H_{\rm lab}\) does not
block the G4 common-history pencil built with \(B_\Sigma\).

## Constructor order

The two maps have different authorized stages:

- \(B_{\rm lab}\): retained prime-diagonal G1--G3 Green carrier;
- \(B_\Sigma\): later G4 seam-weighted boundary crossing.

Using \(B_\Sigma\) inside G1--G3 would erase prime diagonality too early.
Using it after complement margins and seam topology are fixed is the declared
boundary-return operation.

## Adjoint

The source-metric adjoint is

\[
(B_\Sigma^\dagger f)_p
=
\frac{\langle b_p,f\rangle}{\log p}.
\]

Hence

\[
B_\Sigma^\dagger R_HB_\Sigma
\]

is trace class wherever \(R_H\) is bounded, and the mixed theta/arithmetic
Weyl blocks

\[
V^\dagger R_HB_\Sigma,
\qquad
B_\Sigma^\dagger R_HV
\]

are defined in the common history space.

## Restored dressed scalar

With this explicit crossing, the completed off-seam Schur formula

\[
F_\theta
=
V^\dagger R_HV
-
V^\dagger R_HB_\Sigma
Q_U^{-1}
B_\Sigma^\dagger R_HV
\]

is well-typed on every history-resolvent chart, where

\[
Q_U=D_U+B_\Sigma^\dagger R_HB_\Sigma.
\]

The seam still requires limiting absorption or a rigged boundary value.

## No-dark condition remains open

The relevant question is now

\[
\Phi\in\operatorname{ran}B_\Sigma
\]

or quantitative separation from its closed range. The previous proof about
the divergent labelled diagonal does not answer this unlabelled range
question.

Thus common-object membership is repaired, while dark-source exclusion must
be audited anew for \(B_\Sigma\).

## Disposition

The seam-weighted Hilbert--Schmidt incidence supplies the bounded
labelled-to-unlabelled crossing needed by the G4 three-port pencil. It does so
without placing the unweighted theta diagonal in the labelled Hilbert space.
The dressed scalar is restored off the seam. Remaining gates are the
unlabelled range angle, seam boundary values, and
\(F_\theta=E_\theta\tau\). No RH conclusion is authorized.
