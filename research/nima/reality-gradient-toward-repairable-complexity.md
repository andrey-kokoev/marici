# Speculation: reality has a gradient toward repairable complexity

## Operator intuition

The operator's conjecture is not that reality tends toward complexity without qualification. The intended direction is:

> The natural gradient of persistent reality is toward higher repairable complexity: greater generative and relational capacity whose induced incoherence remains within an expandable repair envelope.

This is a non-evidential search direction. It is not established physics, biology, or metaphysics.

## Typed quantities

For a protocol epoch \(v\), let \(G_v\) denote authorized generative capacity, \(I_v\) weighted unresolved incoherence, and \(R_v\) source-derived repair capacity.

The repair margin is

\[
M_v=R_v-I_v.
\]

A state is repairable on the declared horizon when

\[
M_v\geq0.
\]

The conjectured direction is not simply increasing \(G_v\). It is increasing generative capacity while preserving or restoring nonnegative repair margin.

## Weakest falsifiable form

For comparable resource budgets and one frozen environment class, persistent source-generated systems should preferentially admit transitions satisfying

\[
\Delta G_v>0
\]

without crossing permanently into

\[
M_{v+1}<0.
\]

Temporary negative margin is allowed only when a typed recovery path exists within the declared horizon and resource budget.

This predicts selection against two extremes:

- sterile coherence: large repair margin but no expansion of generative capacity;
- runaway complexity: rapid generative growth whose defect load outruns every admitted repair mechanism.

## Why total incoherence may rise

The conjecture permits

\[
I_{v+1}>I_v
\]

provided repair capacity grows sufficiently:

\[
R_{v+1}-R_v
\geq
I_{v+1}-I_v.
\]

Thus a later system may contain more total unresolved structure while being more viable, more capable, and less fragile relative to its repair envelope.

The relevant comparison is not raw incoherence but incoherence relative to correction capacity, resources, and continuation horizon.

## Error correction interpretation

This makes error correction architecturally central. A new logical degree of freedom is durable only when the system also constructs:

- a syndrome or criticism channel;
- a repair operation;
- a threshold or margin;
- retained identity through repair;
- a recovery path that does not erase the capability being protected.

Generativity without these structures is transient proliferation rather than higher organization.

## Protocol interpretation

A protocol upgrade adds primitive continuation operations. This increases possible behavior and may refine Carrier identity. It also creates new coherence and repair obligations.

A progressive upgrade therefore requires:

1. new authorized continuation capacity;
2. preservation of old decisions under restriction;
3. typed detection of new failure modes;
4. repair constructors for the new distinctions;
5. a nonnegative resource-aware repair margin.

The forgetful map to the old protocol measures preservation. The witness-indexed lift into the new protocol measures constructive growth.

## Candidate empirical domains

The conjecture can be attacked in several bounded settings:

- error-correcting codes: logical dimension versus correctable fault volume;
- biological evolution: functional repertoire versus repair and homeostatic capacity;
- actor systems: command alphabet versus recoverable protocol faults;
- scientific theories: prediction space versus independently executable criticism;
- civic systems: institutional capability versus authorized correction and appeal capacity.

Each domain needs its own non-fitted definitions of \(G\), \(I\), \(R\), resources, and persistence.

## Immediate hostile cases

The conjecture fails in a domain if persistent trajectories systematically favor one of the following under comparable resources:

1. increasing generative capacity with permanently negative repair margin;
2. decreasing generative capacity despite abundant unused repair margin;
3. complexity growth whose apparent repair is only erasure of the protected distinctions;
4. post-hoc expansion of the repair definition after each failure;
5. no predictive difference between repairable and irreparable complexity.

## Current metaphysical reading

The disciplined survivor is:

> Reality may not tend toward maximum complexity. Persistent organization may tend toward the frontier where new capabilities are created as quickly as their induced incoherence can still be detected, bounded, and repaired.

This frontier is neither equilibrium nor chaos. It is an expanding zone of correctable novelty.
