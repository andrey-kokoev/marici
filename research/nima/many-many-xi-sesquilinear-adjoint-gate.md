# Many-many Xi sesquilinear adjoint gate

The formal transpose relation

$$
K\mathsf M(s)=\mathsf M(s)^T K
$$

controls algebraic reciprocal pairing. Spectral estimates require the graph-adjoint relation

$$
K\mathsf M(s)=\mathsf M(\overline s)^*K,
$$

interpreted on the common joint domain, together with the declared graph transpose for unbounded radial coordinates.

For a characteristic state `M(s)u=0`, pairing against `u` and taking real parts must produce the radial flux identity. The desired reduction is

$$
(2\operatorname{Re}s-1)\|u_{\rm radial}\|^2
=\operatorname{Re}\mathfrak F_{\partial}(u).
$$

Endpoint cancellation or maximal-neutrality must then control the right-hand side.

Status: sesquilinear adjoint gate isolated; domain-level adjointness and flux cancellation remain open.
