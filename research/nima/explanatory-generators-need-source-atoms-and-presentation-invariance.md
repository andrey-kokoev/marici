# Explanatory generators need source atoms and presentation invariance

## Two attacks on generator density

Generator density alone has two loopholes.

First, the entire fitted constructor can be encoded as one enormous generator.
The seed then has cardinality one but contains the complete answer.

Second, equivalent theories can admit presentations with different numbers of
generators and relations. Raw generator count is not invariant under Tietze
transformations, changes of basis, or Morita-equivalent presentations.

Therefore explanatory compression cannot be defined by counting arbitrary
generators.

## Source-atomic packet

Let the source independently supply a support or assembly filtration. A
generator is admissibly atomic only relative to that structure. Its permitted:

- support;
- arity;
- coefficient type;
- domain;
- incidence;
- transformation law;
- and gluing role

must be fixed before the target behaviour is examined.

A whole-system generator is legitimate only when the source itself presents
the whole system as one primitive object. Otherwise it is a post-hoc package,
not an explanatory atom.

The generator packet should therefore split into:

1. local or primitive source atoms;
2. their incidence and composition relations;
3. separately typed global attachment or boundary data;
4. completion data when infinite assembly is admitted.

This prevents local reduction from silently discarding topology and prevents a
global answer from being hidden inside one primitive label.

## Presentation invariance

Let \(\mathcal P\) and \(\mathcal P'\) be two source-authorized presentations
of the same constructor theory. Explanatory claims should transport across an
authorized equivalence

\[
\Phi:\mathcal P\simeq\mathcal P'.
\]

In particular:

- generated realization categories should be equivalent;
- intervention actions should correspond;
- Sum and Product shadows should commute with \(\Phi\);
- anomaly and gluing classes should map to their counterparts;
- completion stability should not depend on the presentation.

The explanatory object is the presented theory up to authorized equivalence,
not its shortest-looking syntax.

## Mega-generator hostile

Given any realization functor \(F\), define a new symbol \(g_F\) whose
interpretation is the complete table of \(F\). Then \(F\) is generated by the
single symbol \(g_F\).

This construction defeats every criterion based only on:

- number of generators;
- depth of formal composition;
- existence of a universal extension;
- exact reconstruction from the seed.

It fails source atomicity because the meaning of \(g_F\) is defined by the
target realization rather than by an independently admitted source object.

## Toric-code audit

For the toric code, vertices, edges, faces, primal/dual labels, and periodic
identifications are source-visible before syndrome or logical readout is
computed.

Replacing the entire \(L\times L\) torus by one symbol carrying its complete
boundary matrices and Pauli table gives exact reconstruction but no local
explanatory gain. It hides:

- why boundary squares to zero;
- why syndrome is local;
- why face repairs preserve syndrome;
- why primal--dual intersections control commutation;
- which information comes from periodic gluing.

By contrast, cell incidence plus the separate periodic attachment derives
those facts and transports across lattice symmetries and size changes.

## Effective-level explanations

Source atomicity is relative to a declared explanatory level. It does not
force every valid explanation to reduce to a fundamental microscopic theory.

Let

\[
Q:\mathcal C_{\mathrm{micro}}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{eff}}
\]

be an independently validated coarse-graining or abstraction map. An
effective constructor can be explanatory when:

- effective states are sufficient for the admitted effective interventions;
- the effective transition law is natural under \(Q\);
- discarded microstates form an explicitly typed behavioral kernel;
- the effective law is stable across the declared regime;
- failure outside that regime is prospectively specified.

Thus source authority may begin at the effective level, provided the boundary
of that authority is explicit.

## Invariant replacement for smallest

The useful question is not which presentation has the fewest symbols. It is:

> Which source-authorized atomic theory, up to presentation equivalence,
> generates the intervention realization by local composition, typed global
> attachment, and stable completion?

If a quantitative compression score is desired, the encoding language and
cost model must themselves be frozen. No representation-independent numerical
description length exists merely from the constructor table.

## Falsifiers

- One generator contains the complete fitted realization.
- Atomic support is declared only after seeing the desired output.
- Equivalent source presentations receive different explanatory verdicts.
- A basis-dependent generator count is treated as intrinsic simplicity.
- Local atoms reconstruct local behaviour while global attachment is omitted.
- A microscopic description is demanded even though a validated effective
  intervention category is closed and sufficient.
- An effective model conceals the regime in which its behavioral kernel stops
  being inert.
- Compression is claimed without declaring an encoding grammar.

## Revised conjecture

An explanation is generated from a source-authorized atomic theory, invariant
under authorized changes of presentation, with local constructors, global
attachments, and completion data kept separately typed. Its explanatory level
may be effective rather than microscopic, but its intervention scope and
failure boundary must be explicit.
