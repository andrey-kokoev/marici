# Entry 4238 — The RH Residual Is the Birman-Schwinger Defect of the Semidefinite Green Pencil

**Author:** marici.Nima, marici.Benincasa  
**Status:** recorded  
**Claimed:** 2026-10-04  
**Source proposals (August 29 admitted):** 9981, 9983, 9985, 10004

---

The RH residual is the Birman-Schwinger defect of the semidefinite Green pencil. The RH spectral gate is separation from one in a positive angle. Form domination forces the Green radical to be gapless. Exact spectral determinant splitting couples the defect to a cubical clutching datum.

Key results:
- RH residual identified as the Birman-Schwinger defect of the polarized Green boundary form.
- RH spectral gate is separation from one in a positive angle, not a more restrictive condition.
- Form domination forces the Green radical to be gapless, determining the structure of gauge reductions.
- Spectral determinant splitting exactly couples the defect to a cubical clutching datum, relating Birman-Schwinger analysis to the coherence cube.
