# The Strict Graded Zero Equalizer Is RH-Equivalent Without a Source Lift

## Canonical data supplied by a scalar zero

A critical-strip zero canonically supplies two relative Euler germs. Their
nonzero leading boundary classes have grades

\[
g_+(s)=1-\operatorname{Re}s,
\qquad
g_-(s)=\operatorname{Re}s.
\]

This constructs a map from the scalar zero locus into the product of the two
graded boundary carriers. It does not construct a map into their diagonal.

## Equalizer criterion

The pair enters the strict graded equalizer precisely when its two nonzero
homogeneous classes have the same grade:

\[
g_+(s)=g_-(s).
\]

That equation is equivalent to

\[
\operatorname{Re}s=\frac12.
\]

Therefore the statement that every scalar zero lifts to the strict graded
equalizer is equivalent to RH. The equalizer is an exact classifier of the
desired outcome, but it is not yet an explanation or proof.

## Where noncircular content could enter

The programme advances only if a source operation independently factors the
zero-germ map through the diagonal. Such an operation must be constructed
without:

- testing equality of the two grades;
- inspecting zero locations;
- declaring null events to be fixed by definition;
- applying scalar reciprocity after grade erasure.

Its output must be one boundary object whose two presentations are obtained
by source-authorized maps. The diagonal factorization must then follow from a
universal property, conservation law, exact sequence, or constructor relation
that exists before the zero is evaluated.

## Hostile multiplier diagnosis

A symmetric multiplier can insert an off-seam zero while preserving scalar
completion. Its two relative germs land outside the graded diagonal. The
equalizer rejects it, but only because the equalizer was defined to reject
unequal grades.

That is a valid falsifier for a proposed source lift. It is not evidence that
the actual source lift exists.

## Decisive status

The categorical picture has isolated the missing theorem but has not reduced
it:

> Construct a source-derived diagonal factorization of the zero-germ pair.

Without such a constructor, the fixed-null equalizer is another exact
presentation of RH. The next search must target a pre-zero source law that
forces diagonal factorization, or close this route as classificatory rather
than explanatory.
