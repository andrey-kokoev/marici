# Explanation needs natural transport across a source family

## Attack on proof-carrying realization

An isolated derivation

\[
S\xrightarrow{\pi}E
\]

can be made vacuous by adding one source axiom for every transition of \(E\).
The proof then verifies its own table without explaining why the same
constructor should apply under any source variation.

The anti-replay requirement is therefore family-level. An explanatory law
must transport across independently admitted changes of source.

## Source and realization categories

Let \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{src}}\) contain source packets as objects and
independently admitted source transformations as morphisms. Let
\(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{real}}\) contain typed intervention realizations and
authorized realization maps.

A constructor law is a functor

\[
F:\mathcal C_{\mathrm{src}}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{real}}.
\]

For every source morphism

\[
f:S\longrightarrow S',
\]

the realization map

\[
F(f):F(S)\longrightarrow F(S')
\]

must transport states, intervention ports, transition actions, and readouts
according to the frozen source typing.

The Sum and Product lenses are then natural readouts of the Endo-valued
constructor, rather than separately fitted invariants.

## Nonidentity requirement

If \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{src}}\) is discrete and contains only identity
morphisms, functoriality imposes no cross-instance constraint. Every collection
of unrelated lookup tables defines a functor on a discrete category.

Explanatory transport requires nonidentity source morphisms such as:

- subsystem inclusion;
- quotient or coarse-graining;
- change of lattice size or boundary;
- parameter specialization;
- symmetry action;
- composition of software components;
- controlled perturbation;
- restriction to a local chart;
- completion from a finite stage.

At least one nontrivial composition square must be predicted before its target
behaviour is inspected.

## Naturality square

Let \(R\) be a declared readout. For a source transformation \(f:S\to S'\),
the constructor must satisfy a square of the form

\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
F(S) & \xrightarrow{F(f)} & F(S')\\
\downarrow R_S & & \downarrow R_{S'}\\
Y(S) & \xrightarrow{Y(f)} & Y(S').
\end{array}
\]

The square is explanatory only when \(f\), \(Y(f)\), and the typing of
\(F(f)\) were fixed independently of the desired equality.

A post-hoc comparison is covariance evidence for one diagram, not yet a
general constructor law.

## Hard-to-vary at family level

Let \(F\) and \(\widetilde F\) be source-admissible constructor functors with
the same declared lower-lens behaviour. Family-level identifiability requires
that every behaviour-preserving natural comparison between them be an
authorized natural isomorphism.

An invisible deformation is now a coherent family

\[
\delta F_S
\]

that:

1. changes the realization at one or more source objects;
2. respects every frozen source morphism;
3. leaves every declared readout unchanged;
4. is not generated by authorized natural gauge.

Such a deformation is a stronger hostile than a single-instance
determinant-one shear because it survives the complete source variation
calculus.

## Positive toric-code family

The finite toric-code packet is not one fitted transition table. For every
periodic square lattice it constructs

\[
C_2\xrightarrow{\partial_2}C_1\xrightarrow{\partial_1}C_0
\]

from incidence, with

\[
\partial_1\partial_2=0.
\]

Translations and lattice symmetries act on cells and commute with the
boundary maps. Local syndrome and repair equivalence therefore transport
naturally across these source automorphisms.

The coefficient lift sends edge labels to Pauli strings. Primal--dual
intersection determines the central commutation phase, while ordered Pauli
multiplication supplies the Endo action. The Sum and Product shadows are
derived from one family-level construction.

This earns explanatory content beyond the finite census: the same incidence
law predicts syndrome preservation, homology, and loop anticommutation for
new lattice sizes and translated representatives.

It still does not select a decoder. A decoder is an additional non-natural
section unless a noise model or dynamics supplies one.

## Software interpretation

A table of endpoint responses explains one deployed instance only
extensionally. A constructor law explains a software family when it commutes
with component composition, version transformation, retry semantics, and
declared failure propagation.

Compilation can replace a law by a table at runtime without loss of
explanatory authority when the compiler map and its correctness square are
retained. Erasing those maps leaves only executable behaviour.

## Falsifiers

- The source category contains only identities.
- Each new source instance receives a separately fitted transition table.
- A claimed naturality square uses a target map chosen after seeing the
  mismatch.
- Composition of two source morphisms requires a new correction not generated
  by the constructor law.
- Readout naturality holds while intervention transport is undefined.
- A family-level invisible deformation survives all declared source maps.
- The law predicts only re-presentations of one object and no genuine
  perturbation, inclusion, quotient, or completion.
- A compiled artifact is presented without its source-to-artifact correctness
  map.

## Revised conjecture

An explanation is a proof-carrying, intervention-bearing constructor functor
on a nontrivial source category. Its realization moduli must embed stably into
family-level behaviour, and its lower lenses must arise as natural shadows of
the ordered action.

Human understanding is then tested not by how well one object is summarized,
but by whether one constructor law survives lawful variation of the source
without fitted repairs.
