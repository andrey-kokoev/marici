# v209: all pullback-homological detectors collapse to one primitive coordinate

The Entry-436 pullback matrices determine the complete integral detector lattice on the degree-one physical class. A row `ell=(l0,...,l4)` annihilates the four columns of `d2` exactly when

`ell=(a,-a,c,c,c)`.

Thus the cocycle lattice is generated by the endpoint row `(1,-1,0,0,0)` and road row `(0,0,1,1,1)`. The row `d1=(1,-1,-1,-1,-1)` is their difference, so after quotienting coboundaries the dual of `H1` is one primitive integral line. Both generators evaluate the oriented cycle `z=(1,0,1,0,0)` to `+1`.

Consequently every target detector that genuinely descends to this physical pullback homology and carries the chosen primitive orientation must evaluate `z` to `+1`. The remaining comparison is no longer a free three-coordinate calculation: it is the descent/orientation check for the already constructed `s`, `W`, and `v` functionals. If all three descend with the fixed orientation, the normalized primitive has packet coordinates `(1,1,1)`; coefficient multiples scale all three together.

Verified by `research/nima/checkers/check_physical_pullback_detector_coordinates.py` and recorded in module 231. This does not assume that a target functional descends merely because it has a scalar value on one representative.
