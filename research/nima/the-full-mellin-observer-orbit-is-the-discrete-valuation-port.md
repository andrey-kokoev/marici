# The full Mellin observer orbit is the discrete valuation port

## The apparent alternatives

The preceding completion obstruction left two candidates:

1. retain the entire orbit of the seam observer under Mellin-position action;
2. add a genuinely label-discrete valuation port.

They are not necessarily different constructions. With the correct invariant-mean topology, the full Mellin orbit is a Fourier realization of the discrete port.

## Source construction

For a finite prime packet \(c=(c_p)\), define its Mellin observation signal

\[
(\mathcal Mc)(t)=\sum_p c_p e^{it\log p}.
\]

This uses only the source-labelled prime scales and the Mellin-character action. Equip finite signals with the long-window mean

\[
\lVert \mathcal Mc\rVert_{\mathrm B^2}^2
=
\lim_{T\to\infty}
\frac1{2T}\int_{-T}^{T}
|(\mathcal Mc)(t)|^2\,dt.
\]

For distinct primes \(p\ne q\),

\[
\lim_{T\to\infty}
\frac1{2T}\int_{-T}^{T}
e^{it(\log p-\log q)}\,dt=0.
\]

The diagonal terms equal one. Therefore

\[
\lVert \mathcal Mc\rVert_{\mathrm B^2}^2
=
\sum_p |c_p|^2.
\]

The Mellin synthesis is an isometry from finite prime packets, and it extends to their \(\ell^2\) completion. The lower frame bound is exactly one.

## Why the earlier hostile does not contradict this

Every fixed finite derivative stack samples only finitely many moments at one parameter point. Every finite or integrably weighted observation window uses a kernel continuous at zero frequency difference. Crowded logarithmic prime scales then produce approximate kernel vectors.

The invariant mean is different. It observes the entire translation orbit and takes an asymptotic average. It separates every pair of distinct frequencies regardless of how close they are. It is not an ordinary finite-window \(L^2(\mathbb R)\) norm, and point evaluation is not continuous in its completion.

Thus the bounded-gap hostile proves a minimality statement:

- no fixed finite orbit fragment is completion-stable;
- the complete invariant orbit is completion-stable;
- the complete orbit is unitarily equivalent to a discrete spectral register.

Let \(\mathcal V_{\mathrm{fin}}\) be the finite labelled valuation module, \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{Mellin}}\) the almost-periodic orbit module, and \(\mathcal V_2\) the discrete Hilbert completion. The Mellin synthesis factors as

## What this does not solve

This construction separates prime labels and repairs the dynamic observability collapse. It does not yet attach:

- prime powers with their correct shared primitive typing;
- the endpoint anchor;
- the archimedean boundary object;
- the completed Green or determinant form;
- the coherence maps relating those objects to Fourier-Bohr coefficients.

The coefficient carrier must therefore be the typed prime-power Fock module rather than a bare \(\ell^2\) register on integers. The next gate is whether the Fourier-Bohr equivalence respects Adams operations and the primitive, square, connected, seam, endpoint, and archimedean incidence maps.

## Finite falsifier

Any proposed orbit topology that claims completion stability must send distinct Mellin characters to vectors with a cutoff-independent lower separation. If its two-character Gram kernel \(K(\lambda-\mu)\) is continuous at zero, then

\[
\lVert e_\lambda-e_\mu\rVert_K^2
=
2(K(0)-\operatorname{Re}K(\lambda-\mu))
\longrightarrow0
\]

as \(\lambda-\mu\to0\), and the topology fails. The invariant mean passes because its character Gram kernel is the discrete Kronecker kernel on the frequency set, not a continuous kernel at zero.

## Authority qualification

The invariant mean is not generated by norm-continuous Mellin observation. Its Kronecker character kernel is discontinuous at coincident frequency, and that discontinuity is precisely the discrete-label structure needed to defeat logarithmic crowding. Therefore Fourier-Bohr completion does not derive the valuation port from continuous Mellin transport for free. It shows that, once the invariant mean is independently authorized, the resulting orbit completion and the discrete port are equivalent presentations.

A source derivation must still explain why the arithmetic constructor theory supplies this invariant mean, or directly supplies the discrete valuation/Fock carrier. Without that authority, the construction is only an exact candidate.

## Verdict

A completion-stable dynamic carrier exists abstractly: the Fourier-Bohr completion of the full Mellin orbit, equivalently the discrete prime valuation port. It is not a repair internal to norm-continuous Mellin observation. The live gate is source authority for the discontinuous invariant-mean/discrete-label structure, followed by typed arithmetic and boundary compatibility.
