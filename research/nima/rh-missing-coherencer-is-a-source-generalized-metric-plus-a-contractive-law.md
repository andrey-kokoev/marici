## What the hyperbolic double lacks

The canonical pairing on

\[
\mathbb H(V)=V\oplus V^*
\]

is split. It evaluates a source direction against an observer but assigns no
positive energy to either one. Pure-spinor incidence and half-spinor parity
therefore type the relationship without measuring its size.

The missing positive structure is an independently sourced isomorphism

\[
g:V\longrightarrow V^*
\]

whose quadratic form on \(V\) is positive definite.

It induces the involution

\[
\mathcal G_g=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&g^{-1}\\
g&0
\end{pmatrix}
\]

on \(V\oplus V^*\). For the split evaluation matrix \(Q\), one has

\[
\mathcal G_g^2=I,
\qquad
Q\mathcal G_g=
\begin{pmatrix}
g&0\\
0&g^{-1}
\end{pmatrix}>0.
\]

Thus \(Q\mathcal G_g\) is a genuine relationship energy. It turns the
state--observer duality into a positive geometry without erasing their types.

## Why the metric is still insufficient

A positive generalized metric exists on every finite hyperbolic double after
choosing \(g\). Its existence alone does not keep a transported Lagrangian
away from the Maslov divisor.

The reciprocal hostile family

\[
L_{t(z)},
\qquad
t(z)=z^2-a^2,
\]

can be placed in a fixed positive generalized metric while still becoming
nontransverse at \(z=\pm a\). The metric measures the collapsing angle but
does not forbid its collapse.

The RH-bearing addition must therefore be a dynamical law for the source
correspondence. In intrinsic terms, it must show that theta transport is
strictly contractive, monotone, or chamber-preserving relative to the
source-derived generalized metric.

The necessary structure is the pair

\[
(\mathcal G_g,\mathcal T_\Phi),
\]

where \(\mathcal T_\Phi\) is the completed theta correspondence and obeys a
source-authorized inequality strong enough to preserve a positive angle from
the Maslov divisor.

## Completion formulation

Let \(J_X\) map an admissible cutoff state into its complete feature carrier,
and let \(G_X=J_X^*J_X\). Finite faithfulness asks only

\[
\ker J_X=0.
\]

Strict completion asks for a source topology and a cutoff-independent lower
bound

\[
G_X\ge cG_{\mathrm{src},X},
\qquad c>0.
\]

This is exactly the condition that the feature-induced generalized metric
remain nondegenerate during completion. If the best \(c_X\) tends to zero,
the state--observer angle collapses and the completed Lagrangian may enter the
Maslov divisor.

This recovers the earlier control-theoretic observability obstruction as the
metric-completion form of the categorical transversality obstruction.
