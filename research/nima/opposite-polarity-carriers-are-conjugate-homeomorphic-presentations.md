# Opposite-polarity carriers are conjugate-homeomorphic presentations

On each minimal source-generated Schur or Blaschke feature carrier, source star induces an antiunitary

\[
D_{q,r}:\overline{\operatorname{ran}A_{q,r}^+}
\xrightarrow{\cong}
\overline{\operatorname{ran}A_{q,r}^-},
\qquad
D_{q,r}(A_{q,r}^+p)=A_{q,r}^-p^*.
\]

It satisfies

\[
D_{q,r}^-D_{q,r}^+=I
\]

and preserves norms. Therefore it is a homeomorphism of the underlying real topological spaces, although it is conjugate-linear rather than complex-linear.

After adjoining the signed Krein and endpoint coordinates,

\[
D_r^{aug}=D_{S,r}\oplus D_{B,r}\oplus J_\partial
\]

remains an involutive antiunitary homeomorphism. It commutes with the fundamental symmetry and with aperture restriction, so it extends to the projective graph completion.

## Categorical interpretation

The two polarity sheets are not unrelated objects. They form a `C2`-orbit under a Real or dagger groupoid action:

\[
\mathcal X^+
\xrightleftarrows[D^-]{D^+}
\mathcal X^-.
\]

Dagger reverses arrows and composition:

\[
(gf)^\dagger=f^\dagger g^\dagger.
\]

Thus the correct quotient is not an ordinary identification that forgets polarity. It is an equivariant presentation of one unoriented signed carrier together with a contravariant `C2` action. Orientation, endpoint Krein sign, and left/right successor typing remain encoded in that action.

Left and right convolution successors are likewise conjugate presentations:

\[
\iota L_a=R_{a^*}\iota.
\]

In the commutative source they coincide after polarity transport. In a noncommutative source they remain distinct oriented arrows exchanged by dagger.

## Scope boundary

The homeomorphism is unconditional on the minimal generated signed carrier and endpoint-augmented graph. Descent to a defect-absorbed positive quotient is conditional on Douglas acceptance. Dagger transports an accepted quotient to the opposite sheet but cannot manufacture acceptance.

Hence polarity is presentation symmetry at the signed topological level, but it remains genuine equivariant structure rather than a disposable bit.
