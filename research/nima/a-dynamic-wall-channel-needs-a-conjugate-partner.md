# A dynamic wall channel needs a conjugate partner

## Symplectic parity obstruction

The reciprocal tail already supplies two real state coordinates. Adjoining the constant wall as one additional dynamic coordinate produces a three-dimensional state.

No nondegenerate skew-symmetric form exists in odd dimension. Therefore a state space of the form

\[
\mathcal X_{\mathrm{tail}}^{+}
\oplus
\mathcal X_{\mathrm{tail}}^{-}
\oplus
\mathbb R_{\mathrm{wall}}
\]

cannot itself carry the nondegenerate symplectic structure required for a canonical system.

This forces a distinction that the rank-one loading calculation did not need to make.

## Two legitimate wall roles

### Quadratic energy port

The wall may remain an external boundary/energy port whose direct pullback adds

\[
\alpha v_{\mathrm{coh}}v_{\mathrm{coh}}^{*}
\]

to the two-dimensional tail Hamiltonian. Then the canonical state remains two-dimensional. The wall is not evolved as an independent coordinate, and no parity obstruction occurs.

This route requires a source Green identity proving that the wall contribution is a direct positive pullback.

### Dynamic state channel

If the wall itself is evolved and participates in the transfer generator, it must be accompanied by a conjugate source coordinate. The minimal dynamic enlargement has real dimension four:

\[
(\text{reciprocal tail pair})
\oplus
(\text{wall conjugate pair}).
\]

The natural candidate partner may be the principal-value, endpoint-jet, or archimedean channel, but the pairing must be source-derived.

## Consequence for the finite pilot

One must not simultaneously:

- count the wall as an independent dynamic coordinate;
- use a nondegenerate canonical symplectic form;
- and omit its conjugate partner.

A \(3\times3\) skew matrix necessarily has a radical. Calling that radical an authorized wall gauge requires a separate presymplectic reduction theorem; it is not an ordinary canonical system.

## Minimal four-dimensional audit

For a dynamic wall realization, freeze coordinates

\[
Y=(G_+,G_-,w,\pi_w)
\]

only after identifying \(\pi_w\) from source data. Then derive:

\[
\Omega=
\begin{pmatrix}
\Omega_{\mathrm{tail}}&K\\
-K^{*}&\Omega_{\mathrm{wall}}
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det\Omega\neq0,
\]

and the full generator pencil

\[
\mathcal A(z)=\mathcal A_0+z\mathcal A_1.
\]

The test is

\[
\mathcal H=\Omega\mathcal A_1\ge0,
\qquad
\mathcal A_0^{*}\Omega+\Omega\mathcal A_0=0,
\]

with complete boundary isotropy.

## Source-specific decision

The next question is therefore binary:

1. Does the constant wall enter only through the Green boundary energy?
2. Or does it propagate as a genuine constructor state?

If the first, prove the direct rank-one pullback and audit critical observability on the tail plane. If the second, identify the missing conjugate wall coordinate before any canonical positivity calculation.

This prevents a hidden odd-dimensional presymplectic radical from being mistaken for the protected physical dark line.
