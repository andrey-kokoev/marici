# RH as a stable apex section of the 405-profile topology

## Claim boundary

The 405-profile classifier does not prove RH and does not encode the full theta/Tate coherence lattice. It classifies the capability of a frozen realization packet at a declared assembly scale.

An RH certificate requires three independently typed components:

1. a stable apex section in the 405-profile topology over both open half-planes;
2. source-authorized fillers for the required cells of the 28-box reconciler lattice;
3. a source-derived determinant or wave-trace bridge identifying the classified realization with the completed \(\Xi\) section.

Only their conjunction can carry zero-confinement authority.

## The 405 profile space

Let

\[
\mathcal P
=S\times C\times A\times O\times E,
\]

with five assembly scales and three ordered values on each capability axis:

\[
|\mathcal P|=5\cdot3^4=405.
\]

The axes are:

- \(S\): local, packet, finite system, pro-system, completion;
- \(C\): absent, split, compatible;
- \(A\): absent, typed, faithful;
- \(O\): absent, natural, faithful;
- \(E\): absent, bounded, uniform.

For a fixed scale, order profiles coordinatewise on the four capability axes. The Alexandrov open sets are upward-closed capability sets. Failure strata are downward-closed sets.

The completion apex is

\[
T=(\mathrm{completion},\mathrm{compatible},\mathrm{faithful},\mathrm{faithful},\mathrm{uniform}).
\]

This point says that the completed carrier is compatible, the action and observation families are faithful, and the estimate is uniform. It does not say that the realization is the theta/Tate realization or that its determinant is \(\Xi\).

## The base of the profile section

Let \(\mathfrak K_+\) and \(\mathfrak K_-\) be the posets of compact subsets of the two open half-planes

\[
H_+=\{s:\operatorname{Re}s>1/2\},
\qquad
H_-=\{s:\operatorname{Re}s<1/2\},
\]

ordered by inclusion.

A frozen source realization assigns a profile

\[
\Phi(K)\in\mathcal P
\]

to every compact \(K\) in either sector. This is geometric restriction over parameter space, not evolution in physical time.

Restriction to a smaller compact may improve a bound. Enlargement may expose a kernel, undefined comparison, or collapsed margin. The profile assignment is therefore a presheaf-like diagnostic, not a monotone time trajectory through the 405 points.

## Stable apex section

The realization has a stable apex section on the open sectors when:

1. \(\Phi(K)=T\) for every compact \(K\subset H_+\) and \(K\subset H_-\);
2. the carrier, action, observation, and estimate witnesses restrict compatibly under compact inclusion;
3. the uniform estimate is local on each compact and survives every declared cutoff limit;
4. reciprocal transport carries the positive-sector section to the negative-sector section in the source-authorized frame;
5. the archimedean and seam channels remain typed and are not reconstructed from the scalar projection.

The condition is a section because it requires compatible witnesses, not merely repeated assignment of the same profile label.

## Source bridge to RH

Assume additionally that the theta/Tate construction supplies:

- a source-derived completed determinant or fixed wave-trace functional;
- an exact zero-to-state or pole-to-spectrum bridge;
- identity of its scalar section with \(\Xi\) up to a source-fixed nonvanishing factor;
- the required reciprocal and archimedean fillers in the 28-box lattice.

Then a stable apex section excludes zeros in both open half-planes:

- a completed off-seam zero producing a nonzero invisible state contradicts faithful observation;
- a zero entering only through completion contradicts the uniform estimate;
- a sector erased or spuriously identified contradicts compatible carrier typing;
- an unimplemented cross-sector comparison contradicts faithful action.

Therefore, under the source bridge, a stable apex section implies that every nontrivial zero lies on the seam.

## Non-equivalence warning

RH by itself does not force an arbitrary proposed realization to occupy the apex. A badly chosen observer may remain unfaithful even if RH is true; a poor topology may lose uniformity; a fabricated action may fail source authority.

Thus RH follows from the conjunction of the source bridge, the required
28-cell fillers, and the stable 405 apex section.

The reverse implication requires an independently proved completeness theorem for the chosen realization and must not be assumed.

## Finite falsifiers

The formalization is rejected if any of the following occurs:

1. a profile is promoted without source references for all four capability witnesses;
2. a compact receives the apex label while its constructor-closed observer has a nonzero joint kernel;
3. finite faithfulness is promoted to uniformity while the smallest margin tends to zero;
4. a scalar equality substitutes for a missing 28-cell comparison;
5. the \(\Xi\) bridge is reconstructed backward from zero locations;
6. witnesses from distinct source contexts are joined without an authorized common frame;
7. the profile assignment treats compact inclusion as physical time.
