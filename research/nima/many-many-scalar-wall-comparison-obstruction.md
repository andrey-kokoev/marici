# Many-many scalar-wall comparison obstruction

$$
\kappa_{nm}=2\pi\frac{n^2m^2}{n^2+m^2}.
$$

A coefficient match on one shell cannot define this map. The minimal valid target is an enlarged pair-labelled wall carrier, followed by a separately specified scalar codiagonal.
