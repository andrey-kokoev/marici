# Boundary preparation plus witness-dependent local updates

## Declared source operations and type

Use a rational amplitude field A in End(V), with V the faithful two-dimensional
S3 representation already constructed from retained comparison witnesses.
Write T_h(A)=rho(h) A rho(h)^-1. Choosing this operator-valued field type is an
explicit realization choice, not a physical derivation.

Separate two elementary operations:

    Prepare(B): A -> A+B,
    Transport(h): A -> T_h(A).

B is supplied boundary/preparation data. Neither the witness nor the comparison
count determines it. Updates have source and target ports and retain their
operation parents. No physical time parameter, dissipation or preparation
probability is imposed.

For normalized comparison responses the fixed reference remains explicit:

    C = d4*(I+A).

Preparation changes C, not d4. In the exact fixture d4=2I. Pure witness transport
fixes A=0; preparation need not do so.

## Ordered composition law

Represent an update by (h,B), acting as

    U_(h,B)(A)=T_h(A)+B.

Then the source operations force

    (h2,B2) after (h1,B1)
      = (h2*h1, B2+T_h2(B1)).

This semidirect composition is associative. Its identity is (identity,0), and
its inverse is (h^-1,-T_(h^-1)(B)). The checker verifies both application order
and the algebraic laws. Equal composite payloads retain distinct parent trees;
associativity does not erase their provenance.

Under port-frame changes G_i, transport becomes G_j*rho(h)*G_i^-1, while the
input field and preparation transform at their respective ports. The update
then transforms covariantly without changing its decoded meaning. This is a
coordinate check, not selection of a physical gauge group or metric.

## Apply preparation to shared legs, not arbitrary slots

For a common witness action T_h on the primitive leg spaces and supplied leg
increments B_x,B_y, set

    x' = T_h(x)+B_x,
    y' = T_h(y)+B_y.

The actual slot response follows from multiplying those updated legs:

    y'x' = T_h(yx)+T_h(y)B_x+B_y T_h(x)+B_y B_x.

Thus the cross terms are generated by the chosen source operation; no separate
interaction coefficient is fitted to them. The same rule applies in the state
branch. The checker updates the actual30 primitive-leg pattern, reconstructs
all137 slots, verifies every mixed rectangle identity, and compares two updates
with their direct composite. Shared-leg factorization survives.

This checked assembly case uses a common witness action. Arbitrary independent
slot updates or unrelated actions on intermediate spaces are not justified by
it; those would require their own typed compatibility equations.

## Why preparation cannot be hidden inside the finite witness

Suppose a kick B_g is required to depend only on a pointed comparison arrow g,
not its preparation history, and to satisfy

    B_(g after f)=B_g+T_g(B_f).

The finite four-object comparison groupoid has24 incoming arrows at each object.
Define K_j as the average of their kicks. Left composition by g:i->j bijects
incoming arrows at i with those at j. Averaging the displayed identity gives

    K_j=B_g+T_g(K_i),
    B_g=K_j-T_g(K_i).

Hence every such rational affine cocycle is a boundary-potential difference.
After the explicit shift A_i-K_i, its action is homogeneous witness transport.
The checker constructs potential-based updates and verifies this averaging
reconstruction. Changing these coordinates does not authorize discarding their
offset in the fixed-reference physical readout.

This theorem is limited to the finite groupoid, the declared linear action and
witness/endpoint-only affine kicks. It does not exclude history-dependent
preparation or other source dynamics.

## A preparation word contains additional information

Take a transposition h and a supplied preparation B=I after each transport.
Applying the combined operation twice gives

    witness = identity,   accumulated preparation = 2I.

The operation is not the identity even though its finite witness projection is.
Its inverse needs a negative preparation as well as the inverse witness. This
is coherent in the enlarged retained-update system; it would be inconsistent
if silently identified with the finite witness alone.

Order also matters in a directly checked example. For the endpoint swap,
preparing H and then transporting gives K, while transporting zero and then
preparing H gives H. The difference follows from the declared representation
and preparation order, not an added coupling constant.

## Status and next physical input

We now have an executable conditional preparation/transport law, including its
ordered composition, inverses, coordinate covariance, retained histories and
a shared-leg realization of the137-slot responses. It makes predictions once
boundary preparations and witness operations are supplied.

What it does not supply is a physical rule selecting those preparations, their
magnitudes, or their schedule. The next task is source instantiation: declare
which operation prepares each primitive-leg increment and what observation
checks it. Do not replace that missing preparation by a convenient cost minimum,
a hidden finite-witness kick, or the desired numerical constant.
