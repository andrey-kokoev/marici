# Six-point NMHV local-boundary conjecture: falsification attempt

## Question

Can the corrected six-point NMHV conjecture be subjected to its preregistered signed-facet falsifier using admitted repository evidence?

## Claim boundary

No scientific falsification was obtained. The test is not executable because its first required source object is absent. This is an authority and construction blocker, not survival evidence for the conjecture.

## Preregistered falsifier

The test requires two source-cited triangulations of one fixed bosonized six-point NMHV positive geometry, with generic positive external data, orientation, top-cell list, codimension-one facet incidences, cell canonical forms, boundary-to-kinematics dictionary, and published normalization. It rejects the conjecture upon one unpaired internal facet, wrong residue sign, uninterpreted surviving boundary, unequal triangulation sum, or mismatch with the cited form.

## Repository audit

A repository-wide search for `six-point NMHV`, `6-point NMHV`, `R-invariant`, `five-bracket`, `positroid`, and `triangulation` found no amplitude fixture containing those required objects. A search of `research/sources/nima/` found no amplituhedron source packet. Existing Nima amplitude artifacts concern scalar-scaffolded gluons, cosmological-polytope divisor descent, or abstract realization gates; they do not provide the six-point bosonized geometry or either triangulation.

## First missing typed object

The first missing object is a source-cited fixture

\[
B=(Z,\mathcal A_{6,1},T_1,T_2,o,\Omega,\rho,\kappa),
\]

where `Z` is generic positive external data, `T1` and `T2` are explicit triangulations, `o` is the orientation convention, `Omega` assigns normalized cell forms, `rho` is the signed residue/facet table, and `kappa` is the boundary-to-kinematics dictionary.

An authored combinatorial fixture now supplies `T1`, `T2`, the orientation convention, and the signed simplex-facet table. It records the standard six-term five-bracket relation but does not promote that relation to a verified canonical-form identity.

## Executed falsifier

The exact checker verifies that each triangulation has three internal facets with opposite incidence, both oriented chains have the same nine external facets with the same signs, and flipping one top-cell orientation leaves a nonzero boundary residual. All four checks pass.

## Analytic identity attempt

The fixture uses explicit moment-curve data `Z_i=(1,i,i^2,i^3,i^4)`. Every ordered five-bracket is a positive Vandermonde determinant. Six exact-rational probes found zero residual and detected the orientation mutation.

A direct symbolic simplification exceeded the 120-second command bound. A replacement checker factors each simplex denominator into canonical linear forms, constructs the 15-factor common denominator, and adds the six exact polynomial numerators. The identity residual has zero terms. Flipping one simplex orientation leaves 1,001 nonzero numerator terms.

## Supersymmetric numerator probe

Using the standard authored five-bracket formula, the first checker expands the Grassmann delta componentwise at three positive rational momentum-twistor probes. A second checker fixes the generic projective chart with `Z1,...,Z4` the standard basis and `Z5=a`, `Z6=b`. Symmetry reduces the 1,296 ordered Grassmann components to 126 multiset representatives. Every generic common numerator vanishes. Flipping the first term leaves 625 nonzero ordered components; the first witness `(1,1,1,1)` has 24 polynomial terms.

## Disposition

Both the bosonic rational canonical-form identity and the super five-bracket identity are proved exactly on generic projective charts with their declared denominators nonzero. The nine surviving facets exhaust the source-matched physical pole set, and all six internal facets are disjoint from it. The amplitude identity is independently replicated. The local-boundary explanation remains open at one precise edge: the Poincare residue of every oriented simplex form has not yet been proved equal to the corresponding oriented facet form.
