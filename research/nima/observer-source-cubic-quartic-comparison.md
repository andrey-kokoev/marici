## Fixed ground grammar

Use the full dependent families and routes A0--A3 and B0--B4 from `dependent-sigma-pi-full-chain-square.md`. Rename its source X to Q. Its two composite equivalences are a,b:Q~=N, with specified beta(x):a(x)=b(x). The actual intermediate chains stay in the data representation.

Let TraceA and TraceB be the canonical complete traces generated by their respective source values. Each trace includes its source and every intermediate dependent package. Reading its first entry supplies an equivalence TraceA~=Q or TraceB~=Q. Here the routes and their proofs are fixed background structure; freely choosing new paths or proofs would define a different trace type.

## The asserted observer equivalence realized explicitly

Define O_free := TraceA x TraceB.

The comparison e:O_free~=Q x Q reads the two source entries. Its inverse constructs the full A chain from the first source and the full B chain from the second. Thus e retains a specified reconstruction of every intermediate expression.

This gives

O_free x Q ~= (Q x Q) x Q,
O_free x O_free ~= (Q x Q) x (Q x Q).

With associativity recorded, these are Q^3 and Q^4.

## The requested Q1/Q3/Q4 and Q1/Q2/Q4 routes

These Q_i are presentation labels, not four equal coordinate types:

Q1 := O_free x O_free
Q2 := (Q x Q) x O_free
Q3 := O_free x (Q x Q)
Q4 := (Q x Q) x (Q x Q)

Q1 -> Q2 applies e to the first observer.
Q2 -> Q4 applies e to the second observer.
Q1 -> Q3 applies e to the second observer.
Q3 -> Q4 applies e to the first observer.

At (o1,o2), both routes produce (e(o1),e(o2)). Their pointwise comparison follows by product elimination; function extensionality packages it as equality of maps. Each application of e includes its inverse full-chain reconstruction. This is a written type-theoretic argument.

## Source attachment is a dependent type over those coordinates

A paired observer has an attachment type

Attach(x,y) := (a(x)=b(y)).

The coherently attached observer is

O_att := Sigma(x:Q). Sigma(y:Q). Attach(x,y).

At y=x, beta(x) supplies the canonical attachment. Since b is an equivalence, for each x the type Sigma(y:Q).(a(x)=b(y)) is contractible. Consequently O_att~=Q. The second coordinate and attachment are reconstructed using b's equivalence data. This statement retains homotopies; it is not truncation of the comparison to a truth value.

The proposed O~=Q x Q therefore has an explicit realization as the paired presentation carrier O_free. Its attachment is additional dependent structure over that carrier. Identifying the complete attached observer with O_free would require a separate equivalence, and it fails in the finite example below.

Under e x e, the two attachment types on O_free x O_free become

Attach(x,y) x Attach(z,w).

Thus the quartic ambient representation carries its coherent attachment fibre explicitly. The full chain and the attachment are both retained; a count of ambient coordinates does not count independent coherent choices.

In this set-valued case the attachment condition is precisely equality of the two source entries.
