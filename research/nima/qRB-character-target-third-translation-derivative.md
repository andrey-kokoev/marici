# qRB character target: third translation derivative

The endpoint and gamma channels are even in `d`, so their third derivatives vanish at zero.

For the prime channel,

$$
\partial_d^3K_{\rm prime}(t,0)
=
\frac1{\sqrt{\pi t}}
\sum_{n\ge2}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}
 e^{-(\log n)^2/(4t)}
\left(
\frac{3\log n}{8t^2}
-
\frac{(\log n)^3}{16t^3}
\right).
$$

Thus every odd translation derivative at the origin is a prime-sensitive diagnostic, while the archimedean and endpoint channels contribute only to even derivatives.

The third derivative supplies an independent test of whether a proposed relative linking block has retained the correct prime orientation and normalization.
