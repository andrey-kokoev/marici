# Theta label summation and completion differentiation commute with the oriented history boundary

## Bivariate label history

For each theta label \(n\), let

\[
F_n(u,t)
\]

be a translated half-density Gaussian history on
\(t\in[L,2L]\). Define its oriented boundary by

\[
\partial_t^{\mathrm{cell}}F_n
=
F_n(\cdot,2L)-F_n(\cdot,L).
\]

The previous graph theorem places every fixed finite Gaussian jet in a common
\(H^1_t\) core.

## Completion differential commutes exactly

Let

\[
P_u=\partial_u^2-\frac14.
\]

Because \(P_u\) acts only in the \(u\)-coordinate,

\[
P_u\partial_tF_n
=
\partial_tP_uF_n
\]

on the bivariate Schwartz core. Integrating in \(t\) gives

\[
P_u\partial_t^{\mathrm{cell}}F_n
=
\partial_t^{\mathrm{cell}}P_uF_n.
\]

Thus theta completion does not create an interface defect with the adjacent
history boundary, provided the \(t\)-coordinate is retained until after
Stokes descent.

The earlier mismatch came from centering in \(t\), not from the completion
differential.

## Theta label sum

Let

\[
\Sigma_\theta F
=
\sum_{n\ge1}F_n.
\]

For translated Gaussian jets on a compact \(t\)-interval and on every compact
\(u\)-set, the label terms and all fixed \(u\)- and \(t\)-derivatives have
Gaussian majorants of the form

\[
C_{K,r,m}
n^r
e^{-c_Kn^2}.
\]

Therefore the sum converges normally with the derivatives required by the
finite Green identity. Dominated convergence yields

\[
\Sigma_\theta
\partial_t^{\mathrm{cell}}F
=
\partial_t^{\mathrm{cell}}
\Sigma_\theta F,
\]

and

\[
P_u\Sigma_\theta F
=
\Sigma_\theta P_uF.
\]

Hence

\[
P_u\Sigma_\theta
\partial_t^{\mathrm{cell}}F
=
\partial_t^{\mathrm{cell}}
P_u\Sigma_\theta F.
\]

This closes the linear comparison square through theta label summation and
completion differentiation.

## Polarized form transport

Let

\[
\mathfrak g_t(F,G)
=
\int_L^{2L}
\left(
\langle\partial_tF,G\rangle
+
\langle F,\partial_tG\rangle
\right)\,dt.
\]

Its boundary value is

\[
\mathfrak g_t(F,G)
=
\langle F(2L),G(2L)\rangle
-
\langle F(L),G(L)\rangle.
\]

Normal convergence of the theta sums authorizes termwise polarization on the
rapid core. Therefore the label sum and \(P_u\) transport the full
sesquilinear Stokes form, not only its diagonal energy.

This still does not assert that \(P_u\) is unitary or positive. It asserts
only exact chain compatibility with the oriented \(t\)-boundary.
