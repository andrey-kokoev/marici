# Deutsch--Popperian physical-coupling necessity conjecture

## Conjecture

Let \(\tau\) be a source-derived supported coefficient class and let
\(\Gamma_{\rm phys}\) be a source-defined physical relative chain.  A coupling

\[
c_{\rm phys}:\Gamma_{\rm phys}\longrightarrow\operatorname{supp}(\tau)
\]

belongs to physical readout if and only if it is required for the coherent
composition of independently admitted physical operations.

For the all-soft cosmology packet,

\[
\tau_{\rm soft}\in
H^2(\Delta^2,\partial\Delta^2;\mathbb Z/3)
\]

has the forced coefficient readout

\[
\tau_{\rm soft}
\longmapsto I^2/I^3
\longmapsto A_2^*/A_2
\longmapsto\mu_3.
\]

The unresolved datum is not this phase character but a physical comparison

\[
c_{\rm CM}:
(\Gamma_{\rm CM},\partial\Gamma_{\rm CM})
\longrightarrow
(\Delta^2,\partial\Delta^2).
\]

The conjecture forbids inserting \(c_{\rm CM}\) merely because its target and
desired readout are known.  It must be forced by a composition defect in the
source constructor calculus.

\]

## First bounded test

The first test is an authority and typing gate before a curvature or rank
calculation:

1. verify that the supported relative class and its coefficient readout are
   established;
2. verify that the literal physical relative chain is source-defined;
3. locate a legal composition square containing both;
4. compute its uncoupled defect \(\Theta_0\);
5. derive, rather than choose, a comparison \(c\);
6. test whether \(\Theta_0\ne0\) and \(\Theta_c=0\);
7. test uniqueness modulo already admitted orientation/Real symmetry.

If step 2 or 3 fails, the result is **underdetermined**, not zero.  If the
uncoupled square already commutes, coupling necessity is falsified for that
square.  If only a fitted comparison kills the defect, the conjecture is not
supported.

## Hard falsifiers

The conjecture fails if:

1. a source-derived nonzero coupling changes no legal composition or
   observable;
2. a legal composition requires repair but the supported coupling does not
   repair it;
3. inequivalent source-canonical repairs yield different readouts without a
   further source selector;
4. necessity appears only after a basis, projector, or continuation is fitted;
5. a nonzero coupling is asserted despite disjoint support and no derived
   boundary current;
6. a composition defect is silently inferred from the mere absence of a map.

## Extension-admission correction

The strongest naive formulation—every extension must be derived as physical
content from the pre-extension source—is false.  Resolutions, auxiliary
variables, free completions, and conservative language extensions may be
introduced after a problem is discovered.  Their legitimacy does not come
from pretending that they were already physical; it comes from a different
admission route.

There are exactly two relevant routes here:

1. **Conservative or universal admission.**  The extension is fixed by a
   predeclared universal property, quasi-isomorphism, or equivalence and adds
   no new governed physical readout.
2. **Contentful admission.**  The extension adds a new observable, support,
   physical cell, or readout direction and therefore requires independent
   source provenance or novel falsifiable consequences.

An observed obstruction may motivate either construction, but cannot by
itself authorize new physical content.  In particular, a localization is
formal only after its inverted maps or discarded supports have been declared
independently of the result it is meant to produce.

This yields an authority-monotonicity principle.  Restriction and quotient
operations may be internally canonical when their discarded kernel is
differential-stable:

\[
dK\subseteq K
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
C\longrightarrow C/K.
\]

By contrast, an amplification

\[
C/K\longrightarrow C'
\]

that restores discarded information or creates a new readout cannot be
derived from \(C/K\) alone.  It requires rehydration from the source, a
universal completion whose new directions are readout-silent, or independent
evidence.  This is the same asymmetry as policy revocation versus permission
grant: forgetting is canonical; authority or information amplification is
not.

Applied to the established cases:

- the Cut overlap quotient is admissible because its incompatible cells form
  an invariant kernel;
- the primitive Verdier quotient is a valid diagnostic, while the full
  physical object must retain the discarded \(A_2\) contact sector by
  recollement;
- the formal Koszul cell may define a formal completion but cannot be renamed
  an existing physical \(Q\)-cell;
- the weighted blowup is source-derived by the Newton ideal, but its regular
  transformed form cannot activate a physical class without a separately
  derived relative chain.

The corrected Deutsch--Popperian rule is therefore

\[
\boxed{
\text{no endogenous amplification of physical content or explanatory
authority}.}
\]

Formal machinery may grow conservatively.  Physical meaning may grow only by
source provenance or new independently testable consequences.
