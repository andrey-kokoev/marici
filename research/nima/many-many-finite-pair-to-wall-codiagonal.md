# Many-many finite pair-to-wall codiagonal

Let `B_rad` be the finite pair-response map. Define the wall codiagonal on each bordered packet by

$$
C_{\rm wall}\mathcal B_{\lambda\mu}
=E_{\lambda\mu}^{+}+E_{\lambda\mu}^{-}
-\frac12\left(W_{\lambda\mu}^{+}+W_{\lambda\mu}^{-}\right).
$$

For a reciprocal pair, the symmetric sector contributes to the scalar wall output, while the antisymmetric sector is retained in the oriented relative output.

The ordered pair-to-wall map is

$$
\mathcal C_{\rm pair}^{[a,b]}
=C_{\rm wall}B_{\rm rad}^{[a,b]}.
$$

Laplace/Stokes gives the equivalent response relation

$$
 zR_{\lambda\mu}-\rho_{\lambda\mu}(0)
=E_{\lambda\mu}-\frac12W_{\lambda\mu}.
$$

The codiagonal is applied after pair labels and reciprocal orientation have been retained.
