# Correction: the residue role, not the retained base, is the fifth vertex of the full four-simplex

## Frozen role simplex

The established ordered simplex is

\[
[S,A,R,C,G]=\Delta^4,
\]

where `R` is the residue role. Its middle self-dual facet is

\[
d_2\Delta^4=[S,A,C,G].
\]

Therefore the fifth role omitted from the active rung-four horn is `R`. The retained corrected state

\[
b_z=\Delta_\Xi(z)
\]

is a basepoint carried by every role and coherence cell; it is not a replacement for the residue vertex.

## Two legitimate higher objects

There are two distinct constructions.

### Facet-level homotopy

Two defining systems for the self-dual facet form

\[
\Delta^1\times\Delta^3_{SACG}.
\]

Coning this prism from `b_z` gives

\[
b_z\star(\Delta^1\times\Delta^3_{SACG}),
\]

a five-dimensional based carrier. The existing rung-five contract correctly describes this local null-homotopy problem.

### Full-system homotopy

Two complete five-role laws form

\[
\Delta^1\times\Delta^4_{SARCG}.
\]

Its five side prisms compare the facets obtained by omitting each of

\[
S,A,R,C,G.
\]

The side omitting `R` is exactly the active presentation prism

\[
\Delta^1\times\Delta^3_{SACG}.
\]

Retaining the common base gives the six-dimensional carrier

\[
\boxed{
b_z\star(\Delta^1\times\Delta^4_{SARCG}).
}
\]

It has eleven labelled vertices: one common base plus two phases of the five role vertices.

## Consequence

The previous nine-vertex cone is the correct based higher cell for the self-dual facet alone. It is not the complete next-level equivalence between the full `[S,A,R,C,G]` construction laws.

The residue role matters because the other four facets are reflection-paired while `[S,A,C,G]` is fixed. A full-system homotopy can compare the paired facets and isolate whether their coherence forces, shifts, or leaves unchanged the self-dual Toda representative.

## Revised hierarchy

- local rung five: null-homotopy of the self-dual `SACG` facet;
- global rung five: homotopy between two complete `SARCG` four-simplex laws;
- based global carrier: the cone of that full-system prism from `b_z`.

Neither construction makes the self-dual residual vanish automatically.
