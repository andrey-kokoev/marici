# Relationship-count scaling conjecture for the doubled theta/Tate system

## Operator intuition

The completed two-sector system should not be expected to have a
scale-independent number or weight of relationships away from its
balanced seam.  Under dilation, its effective relationship content
should grow or decay.

Let (R>0) denote multiplicative scale and let

\[
q=\log R.
\]

A power law in (R),

\[
\mathcal R(R)\sim R^\alpha,
\]

is exactly exponential evolution in logarithmic scale:

\[
\mathcal R(q)\sim e^{\alpha q},
\qquad
\partial_q\mathcal R=\alpha\mathcal R.
\]

Thus “power-law change” and “exponential change” are coordinate versions
of the same proposed mechanism.

## Connection to the doubled conservation identity

For the centered spectral parameter

\[
z=s-\frac12,
\]

the doubled tail system gives

\[
2\Re(z)\mathcal N=-\partial_qJ-2\mathcal F.
\]

The coefficient (2\Re z) has the form of a logarithmic growth/decay
rate.  This suggests the interpretation:

\[
\boxed{
\Re z\ne0
\Longrightarrow
\text{relationship weight flows exponentially in }q
\text{ (equivalently by a power law in }R).
}
\]

The seam (Re z=0) is the scale-balanced locus.  It is not necessarily
the place where the full relationship object vanishes; it is where its
net dilation exponent vanishes.

The unresolved forcing difference

\[
\mathcal F=
\Re\left(
f_+c_+\overline{G_+}
-
f_-c_-\overline{G_-}
\right)
\]

may therefore measure transfer of relationship weight among the
primitive, square, connected-tail, and archimedean channels.  If it is
the derivative of their complete modular current, the global system
conserves total relationship content while redistributing it across
types and scales.

## Required typing

“Number of relationships” is not yet a defined invariant.  A valid
version must specify one of:

- rank or dimension of a finite-cutoff relation module;
- trace of a source-derived relation projector;
- weighted incidence mass;
- logarithm of a determinant or torsion after retaining boundary
  channels;
- a current density in the nuclear rigging.

The definition must be source-derived, additive or functorial under
authorized composition, and reconstruct every finite Euler cutoff.

## Conjectural theorem shape

There exists a typed relationship current (mathscr R_s(q)) such that

\[
\partial_q\mathscr R_s
=
2\Re(s-\tfrac12)\mathscr R_s
+
\text{typed inter-channel transfer},
\]

where the transfer sums to a modular boundary divergence in the complete
Ubersector.

If the total endpoint flux vanishes, a nonzero two-endpoint state can be
scale-compatible only when

\[
\Re(s-\tfrac12)=0.
\]

## Falsifiers

The conjecture fails if:

1. no source-defined relationship measure can be identified;
2. its cutoff evolution is neither regularly varying in (R) nor
   exponential in (q);
3. the apparent exponent depends on arbitrary normalization;
4. a nonzero residual bulk term remains after all typed currents are
   included;
5. hostile prime data preserve the claimed scaling law while inserting
   off-seam scalar zeros.

This is a registered research intuition, not an established theorem.
