# Six-point NMHV twisted-cohomology conjecture: first falsification

## Question

Does a fundamental six-point BCJ combination vanish already in the worldsheet scattering-equation quotient, as required by the proposed twisted-cohomology carrier?

## Test

For the base order `(2,3,4,5,6)`, insert leg `1` in each of its four nontrivial positions and weight the resulting Parke–Taylor factors by

\[
W_k=\sum_{j=2}^{k+1}s_{1j}.
\]

Impose only momentum conservation in the form

\[
s_{16}=-(s_{12}+s_{13}+s_{14}+s_{15}).
\]

The exact rational reduction gives

\[
\sum_{k=1}^{4}W_k\,\mathrm{PT}(2,\ldots,k,1,k+1,\ldots,6)
=-\mathrm{PT}(2,3,4,5,6)E_1,
\]

where

\[
E_1=\sum_{j=2}^{6}\frac{s_{1j}}{z_1-z_j}
\]

is the leg-one scattering equation. The BCJ combination therefore vanishes in the scattering-equation quotient. Reversing one insertion weight leaves a nonzero rational residual.

## Boundary-residue test

The five pair-collision divisors involving the moved leg also pass. Explicit residue computation gives

\[
\operatorname{Res}_{z_1=z_j}
\left(\sum_k W_k\,\mathrm{PT}_k\right)
=-s_{1j}\,\mathrm{PT}(2,3,4,5,6),
\qquad j=2,\ldots,6.
\]

Thus the BCJ worldsheet relation has the expected lower-point Parke–Taylor factor on every pair collision tested.

## Quotient-rank test

After Kleiss–Kuijf reduction, the full set of 120 fundamental BCJ rows has rank 18 in the 24-dimensional DDM module at the exact generic fixture. The quotient therefore has dimension six:

\[
24-18=6=(6-3)!.
\]

This matches the expected rank of generic twisted cohomology on `M0,6`. A hostile matrix-entry sign change alters the row space. Dimension agreement proves compatibility of the proposed carrier size; it does not construct the NMHV class.

## Disposition

The conjecture survives its algebraic, local-boundary, and quotient-rank falsifiers. This also explains the prior amplituhedron-chain obstruction: the BCJ relation is generated by the worldsheet scattering equation rather than by cancellation of the six-point NMHV simplex chains.

The result does not establish a global twisted-cohomology primitive or the NMHV kinematic cocycle. The pair-collision residues supply only a local part of compactified-boundary factorization.

An explicit authored candidate now supplies the CHY reduced-Pfaffian integrand, its four-dimensional three-negative-helicity refinement, a fixed normalization contract, and the existing momentum-twistor-to-on-shell map; see `research/nima/six-point-nmhv-worldsheet-integrand-construction.md`. Primary-source equation matching remains absent, so these definitions have no source authority yet. Their acceptance test is direct residue evaluation against the established components with no fitted multiplier. Reconstructing a six-vector from those component values remains inadmissibly circular.
