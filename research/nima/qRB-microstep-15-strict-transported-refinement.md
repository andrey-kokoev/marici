# qRB microstep 15: strict transported refinement

Choose a radial cutoff projection `P_X` and define

$$
Q_X=\mathcal M_{\log}P_X\mathcal M_{\log}^{-1}.
$$

Then the refinement square is exactly commutative:

$$
Q_X\mathcal M_{\log}=\mathcal M_{\log}P_X.
$$

Consequently every observer pulled back through the transported refinement remains source-typed, and the observer radical descends whenever the endpoint graph is retained:

$$
R_X(\operatorname{Rad}_{\rm source})
\subseteq
\operatorname{Rad}_{\rm target}.
$$

This is the first explicit refinement for which the qRB quotient transport is strict rather than merely lax.

The statement is regulator-typed: replacing `Q_X` by an independently selected sharp window invalidates the equality and introduces the comparison defect `E_X`.
