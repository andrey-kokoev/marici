## Revised obligations

1. **Common source and transport:** specify H's objects, partial operations, execution histories and domains, with maps P_minus and P_plus to two target categories. On each admitted domain require preservation of identities, composition and applicable comparison cells. A projection may forget information; it must state what it forgets. Do not demand that every reversible arrow is future-directed in either target.
2. **Geometric identification:** derive or characterize the Lorentzian metrics, curvature signs and causal assignments carried by the two projections from explicit common-source structure and comparison laws. Supplying g_plus/g_minus is a conditional example, not this derivation. A pair or a family of scales is admissible; no unique absolute scale or preferred sign is required. State chart/domain/continuum assumptions. Local two-dimensional constant-curvature geometry is not a theorem about global AdS/dS topology or arbitrary dimension.
3. **Joint compatibility:** verify both maps on one source, their shared operations and their separate future subcategories. They need not identify the same source arrows as future-directed, and no direct AdS-to-dS isometry is required.
4. **Optional reconstruction, a stronger claim:** if either shadow is said to retain the entire higher machinery, specify retained auxiliary data and a reconstruction map on a certified image. A commuting projection alone does not establish this. Reconstruction is not a prerequisite for the weaker two-shadow claim.

The meaning of 'either can transport it' in the core conjecture is operation-compatible projection, not automatic invertibility or losslessness. Neither global equivalence nor causal reflection is implied without a separate theorem.
