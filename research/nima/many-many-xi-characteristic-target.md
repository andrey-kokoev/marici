# Many-many Xi characteristic target

Use the proposed G4 characteristic as the target analytic interconnection. Its endpoint node is

$$
(\mathbb C^2,J),
\qquad J=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1),
$$

and its radial bulk state is `gamma_rad(z)c`. The coupled characteristic matrix is

$$
\mathsf M(s)=
\begin{pmatrix}
 s&0&1\\
 0&s-1&1\\
 1&-1&H(s)
\end{pmatrix},
$$

with direct-sum metric

$$
K=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,1).
$$

The target identities are

$$
K\mathsf M(s)=\mathsf M(s)^T K,
\qquad
\det\mathsf M(s)=2\xi(s).
$$

Direct determinant expansion forces

$$
\det\mathsf M(s)=1+H(s)s(s-1),
\qquad
H(s)=\frac{2\xi(s)-1}{s(s-1)}.
$$

Thus the characteristic normalization uniquely determines the radial transfer function.

The six-coordinate prime carrier remains label-retained until radial return. The radial codiagonal is applied afterward.
