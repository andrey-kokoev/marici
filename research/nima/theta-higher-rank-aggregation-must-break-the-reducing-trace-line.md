# A higher-rank aggregation must break the reducing trace line

## Status

Research packet. It gives the nontriviality gate for any proposed
higher-dimensional lift of arithmetic aggregation. It does not establish that
the theta/Tate source passes the gate.

## The vacuous dilation problem

Let the current arithmetic aggregation on the common trace line be

\[
m_X(s):L_X\longrightarrow L_X.
\]

It is always possible to embed this scalar into a larger operator:

\[
\mathcal A_X(s)=
\begin{pmatrix}
m_X(s)&0\\
0&D_X(s)
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

This is not a distinction-preserving lift. The trace line is reducing, the
extra directions are dark, and the minimal reachable-observable realization
of the scalar channel remains one-dimensional. Every zero of (m_X) survives
unchanged.

Consequently, higher ambient rank alone adds no information.

## Minimal block gate

A candidate pre-trace lift must be constructed from source atoms in the form

\[
\mathcal A_X(s)=
\begin{pmatrix}
a_X(s)&b_X(s)\\
c_X(s)&D_X(s)
\end{pmatrix}
\]

relative to the trace line and its typed complement.

The complement can affect the scalar channel only if both incidence
directions are present:

- (c_X) makes the complement reachable from the trace input;
- (b_X) makes it observable at the trace output.

If either arrow vanishes, the higher carrier cannot provide a closed
source-to-trace correction. This is the same directional distinction already
found in the tail/forcing realization.

When (D_X(s)) is invertible on its declared graph domain, elimination gives
the effective trace channel

\[
m_X(s)=a_X(s)-b_X(s)D_X(s)^{-1}c_X(s).
\]

This formula is explanatory only when every block and its domain is
constructed before the scalar multiplier. Choosing blocks after (m_X) is
known is an arbitrary realization of the answer.

## Uniform minimality

Finite reachability and observability at each cutoff are insufficient. A
coupling can tend to zero with the cutoff, leaving a higher-rank system at
every finite stage whose completed minimal part collapses back to the trace
line.

The lift therefore needs, in one source-authorized topology:

1. cutoff-compatible block operators;
2. a common graph domain;
3. uniform reachability of the complement from the trace port;
4. uniform observability of the complement at the trace port;
5. continuity of every boundary-current incidence;
6. a completion-stable sewing map between direct and reciprocal
   factorizations.

The existing (1/N) incidence family is the canonical finite falsifier for
failure of either directional lower bound.

## Kernel version

The two-variable source kernel

\[
K(z,w)=J(z)^{*}J(w)
\]

retains the full generated feature space. Its minimal Kolmogorov carrier is
the closed span of all (J(w)x). Adding an orthogonal dark summand leaves
(K) unchanged and has no physical or arithmetic content.

Thus any operator lift inferred from a kernel must use its minimal generated
carrier. Equality of scalar compressions does not identify operator kernels,
and unitary equivalence of minimal mathematical factorizations does not
supply physical sewing authority.

## Relation to the actual adelic lift

Additive Fourier transform and the Weil representation already provide large
source-derived operators. Their existence alone does not orient the zeta
matrix coefficient. The distinguished scalar overlap may vanish while the
operator and transported state remain perfectly nonzero.

Therefore the live question is not whether a large adelic operator exists.
It is whether the arithmetic/Poisson boundary currents create a minimal,
bidirectionally coupled pre-trace realization in which the trace line is not
reducing.

## Finite compiler

For each labelled cutoff, the audit should:

1. declare the trace line and every complementary source type;
2. derive (a_X,b_X,c_X,D_X) without using (m_X^{-1}), zero locations, or a
   fitted factorization;
3. test whether the trace line is reducing;
4. compute reachability and observability ranks separately;
5. verify the Schur-complement identity exactly;
6. insert primitive, square, seam, and archimedean currents before
   elimination;
7. test the directional lower bounds over increasing cutoffs;
8. reject the lift at the first dark summand, missing incidence, domain
   mismatch, or collapsing singular value.

## Decisive outcomes

There are only three meaningful outcomes:

1. A source-derived uniformly minimal lift exists and exposes a new operator
   law before scalar compression.
2. A higher-rank lift exists but the trace line is reducing or asymptotically
   reducing. It adds no RH information.
3. No common-domain lift exists. Arithmetic aggregation is intrinsically a
   scalar or distributional readout in the present architecture.

## Conclusion

The surviving programme is not “find a bigger operator.” It is “derive a
minimal bidirectionally coupled arithmetic aggregation before trace.” Dark
dimensions, unitary ambient dynamics, and positive kernels do not constrain
the scalar divisor. Only source-reachable and source-observable off-trace
directions can possibly add new information.
