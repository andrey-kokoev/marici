# Flavor Source Cycle to Physical Orientation Is a Sign Torsor

## Position

Flavor already contains two valid oriented-cycle constructions, but they live
in different quotients.

The source-domain construction uses three labelled phases modulo common
(U(1)) and cyclic permutation. Its oriented triangular carrier removes the
accidental reflection and selects a chiral source orbit.

The physical quotient uses two noncommuting Hermitian three-generation
operators modulo simultaneous weak-basis conjugation. Its first CP-odd
orientation coordinate is the degree-six commutator cycle, equivalently the
imaginary part of a reversal-asymmetric trace word.

The optical Bargmann triangle therefore does not transfer literally. Flavor's
full weak-basis quotient raises the minimum physical cycle from a three-ray
triangle to a degree-six trace word.

## Portal problem

Let the two source chiralities carry the sign representation
(S={-1,+1}), and let the physical CP orientations carry
(P={-1,+1}). CP acts by sign reversal on both.

A CP-equivariant portal

[
Phi:Slongrightarrow P
]

must satisfy

[
Phi(-s)=-Phi(s).
]

There are still two faithful possibilities:

[
Phi_+(s)=s,
qquad
Phi_-(s)=-s.
]

If the codomain also includes the CP-degenerate value zero, the collapsed map

[
Phi_0(s)=0
]

is equivariant as well.

Therefore:

- CP equivariance does not prove that source chirality reaches nonzero
  physical CP violation;
- equivariance plus noncollapse still does not select which source chirality
  corresponds to positive physical orientation;
- the faithful portals form a two-sheet sign torsor.

## Existing exact hostiles

The flavor packets already instantiate all three failures.

1. A Yukawa plaquette phase descends through diagonal rephasings but not the
   full weak-basis groupoid. It is not a physical portal coordinate.
2. Three complex projectors can produce a nonzero commutator cubic, while
   complex conjugation preserves CP-even spectra and reverses the orientation.
3. A CP-even source potential can select a nonzero phase magnitude while
   retaining two degenerate conjugate minima.
4. A dynamical pseudoscalar reference selects only relative orientation. After
   forgetting the reference, both absolute signs remain.

## DPC

A claimed source-to-physical CP-orientation portal is admitted only if it
supplies:

1. a source-derived map from the selected phase orbit to Yukawa or Hermitian
   flavor operators;
2. descent through the complete weak-basis groupoid;
3. CP equivariance;
4. noncollapse of the source chiral pair to zero physical orientation;
5. a source-derived comparison fixing the sign sheet of the faithful portal;
6. stability under allowed coefficient and threshold variations;
7. a calibrated physical CP-odd readout.

The finite falsifier is the pair of equivariant faithful portals
(Phi_+) and (Phi_-). If every declared source and physical invariant is
preserved while the orientation correspondence reverses, the proposed data
do not select the portal.

The zero portal is the independent noncollapse falsifier.

## Cross-compiler interpretation

The source triangle and physical commutator cycle should be treated as two
compilers for orientation only after a comparison law is derived. Equality of
the labels “CP odd” is not that law.

The missing comparison has the form

[
J_{mathrm{phys}}(Phi(z))
=c,J_{mathrm{src}}(z)
]

on the selected source orbit, with (c) nonzero and its sign fixed by the
same source architecture. A fitted positive convention for (c) would merely
name one sheet of the torsor.

## Outcome

The cycle-holonomy route does not discover a missing Flavor cycle. Both the
source and physical cycles already exist. It discovers the actual missing
constructor: a weak-basis-descending, noncollapsing, sign-fixed orientation
portal between them.
