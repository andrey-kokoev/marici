# Flavor Mediator Is a Closed Compatibility Locus

## Result

The positive repair to the physical16 quotient is not a larger flat record.
It is a compatibility locus joining several typed channels before scalar
projection.

For the finite model, retain

\[
\Gamma_\phi=g_\phi^2,
\qquad
\Gamma_\psi=g_\psi^2,
\qquad
I=g_\phi g_\psi,
\]

the portal relation

\[
J_{16}=-\kappa I,
\]

and the ordered phase-port relations

\[
R_+=B+\Gamma_\phi+\Gamma_\psi+2\nu I,
\]

\[
R_-=B+\Gamma_\phi+\Gamma_\psi-2\nu I.
\]

Here \(B\) is the common background and \(\nu>0\) is the visibility. The mass
normalization has been fixed to one in the finite checker; restoring it only
requires retaining the calibrated nonzero mass channel in the portal relation.

The mediator object is the subobject of the channel product cut out by these
equations. Arbitrary tuples of widths, portal values, and detector records are
not admitted.

## Reconstruction on the locus

The ordered port difference gives

\[
R_+-R_-=4\nu I.
\]

Because the widths determine

\[
|I|=\sqrt{\Gamma_\phi\Gamma_\psi}
\]

and \(\nu\) is positive, the difference determines the relative sign and the
visibility magnitude. The port sum determines the common background:

\[
B=\frac{R_++R_-}{2}-\Gamma_\phi-\Gamma_\psi.
\]

Once \(I\neq0\), the portal determines \(\kappa\):

\[
\kappa=-\frac{J_{16}}{I}.
\]

The individual coupling signs remain determined only up to simultaneous
global sign. That is the expected gauge identification, because

\[
(g_\phi,g_\psi)
\sim
(-g_\phi,-g_\psi)
\]

preserves every joined channel.

## Exact finite census

The checker evaluates 576 source states built from six nonzero signed values
for each coupling, four nonzero signed values of \(\kappa\), two positive
visibilities, and two backgrounds.

After quotienting simultaneous global sign, 288 states remain. The complete
joined record has 288 values, and every record fiber has size one. Thus the
joined compatibility locus is faithful on this finite domain modulo precisely
the declared global sign.

## Deletion falsifiers

The theorem has three independent gates.

1. If \(\nu=0\), the relative sign disappears. Flipping \(I\) and \(\kappa\)
   together preserves all retained records.
2. If the ordered ports are summed, their difference is erased and the same
   ambiguity returns even when visibility is nonzero.
3. If the portal channel is removed, \(\kappa\) is invisible.

Accordingly, positivity of visibility, preservation of ordered ports, and the
portal relation are all necessary for this finite reconstruction.

## Categorical interpretation

Let \(X\) be the upstream coupling object and let the separate observations
land in width, portal, and detector objects. Their Cartesian product contains
many incompatible tuples. The physical mediator is the image of \(X\), or an
equivalent source-defined subobject, inside that product.

This is Sontag's closed-correspondence picture in Flavor form: upstream and
downstream descriptions are joined by the graph of their action, closed under
the declared continuation laws, rather than treated as independently versioned
objects.

The compatibility equations define the locus, but do not manufacture its
source. A fitted tuple satisfying the equations has no more authority than a
fitted scalar. The mediator grammar must derive the equations, phase ports,
visibility process, and background from one microscopic construction.

## Cross-sector boundary

Grothendieck's de Rham result supplies an important warning. Exact
compatibility identities can leave an entire family of invariant metrics
unselected. Likewise, the Flavor equations prove faithfulness conditional on
the declared source locus; they do not select a microscopic mediator or prove
that physical16 realizes it.

Aspect's correlated-outage hostile also means \(\nu\) cannot generally be a
single static scalar. For repeated experiments, the compatibility locus must
include the temporal phase-monitor or correlation process required by the
record horizon.

## Status

The finite reconstruction problem is closed positively: the joined locus is
faithful modulo global sign. The remaining physical problem is source
construction—derive one mediator whose reachable image satisfies these
relations and whose temporal coherence survives the declared experiment.
