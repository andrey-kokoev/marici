# Frozen prediction for the constructor-lift criterion

Owner: `marici.Nima`

This packet is frozen before inspecting the withheld cosmology evidence.

## Criterion C

A source theory generates a physical constructor for a task `p:X->X` if and
only if it supplies one coherent realization packet with all five maps:

1. **Task:** a source-defined proper, repeatable task `p`, not selected by a
   fit or by the desired answer.
2. **Interaction:** a source-admissible state-changing lift
   `J:X->R tensor X` whose substrate marginal implements `p` on the accepted
   branch.
3. **Record:** a source-defined record port obtained as the record marginal of
   that same `J`, rather than an independently chosen faithful probe.
4. **Return:** apparatus preparation and return/reset (or a declared
   catalytic/degradation law) allowing the task to be attempted repeatedly.
5. **Coherence:** descent, sequential composition, and coarse-graining commute
   for both records and successor states.

Necessity follows by unpacking a reusable source-generated constructor.
Sufficiency follows because the five maps compose into a reusable
record-bearing realization of `p`.  The criterion is intentionally stronger
than faithful observability and stronger than controllability.

## Falsifiers fixed in advance

The prediction fails if the frozen cosmology source already contains all of:

- an explicit outcome-bearing interaction;
- conditional post-interaction states;
- source-derived pointer preparation and reset/return;
- a repeatability or stabilization law;
- coherent sequential composition.

Finding only probabilities, periods, covariance derivatives, score
insertions, or mixed correlators does not satisfy these falsifiers.
