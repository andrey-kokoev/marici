# qRB microstep 36: uniform readout constant

For each finite packet `P`, define the relative operator constant

$$
\Lambda_P
=
\sup_{u,v\ne0}
\frac{|T_P(u,v)|}{\|u\|_*\|v\|_*}.
$$

A bounded extension to the comparison completion exists if

$$
\sup_P\Lambda_P<\infty.
$$

Alternatively, a weaker closability argument may suffice, but it must be proved independently. The finite-packet positive certificates establish only

$$
\Lambda_P<\infty
$$

for each fixed `P`; they provide no control of the supremum.

This reduces the next analytic task to estimating one explicit quantity, while preserving the relative—not scalar-positive—target.
