# Theta two-prime incidence square is flat while Gaussian weight has negative mixed curvature

## Status

Exact two-prime classification. The half-line boundary-incidence cocycle is
coherent under the two possible orders of prime translation: its square has
zero residual. The Gaussian valuation weight nevertheless fails to factor into
independent prime weights. That failure is measured by a strictly negative
mixed logarithmic difference.

Thus transport coherence and interaction curvature are different objects. The
first is flat; the second is nonzero but does not by itself orient scalar zeros.

## Two-prime boundary square

Let

\[
\ell_p=\log p,
\qquad
\ell_q=\log q.
\]

The half-line interval current is

\[
B_\ell[A](z)=\int_0^\ell A(v)e^{izv}\,dv.
\]

Taking the (p)-step first and the (q)-step second gives

\[
B_{\ell_p}[A]
+e^{iz\ell_p}B_{\ell_q}[S_{\ell_p}A].
\]

Taking the (q)-step first gives

\[
B_{\ell_q}[A]
+e^{iz\ell_q}B_{\ell_p}[S_{\ell_q}A].
\]

Both expressions equal

\[
B_{\ell_p+\ell_q}[A].
\]

Therefore the two-prime cocycle-square residual

\[
\mathfrak B_{p,q}
=B_{\ell_p}[A]
+e^{iz\ell_p}B_{\ell_q}[S_{\ell_p}A]
-B_{\ell_q}[A]
-e^{iz\ell_q}B_{\ell_p}[S_{\ell_q}A]
\]

vanishes exactly.

The interval port retains ordered staging, but completed endpoint incidence is
independent of the order because addition of logarithmic displacements
commutes.

## Two-prime valuation module

On basis states (e_{a,b}\), let

\[
S_pe_{a,b}=e_{a+1,b},
\qquad
S_qe_{a,b}=e_{a,b+1}.
\]

Then

\[
S_pS_q=S_qS_p.
\]

Let the common Gaussian scale operator be

\[
Qe_{a,b}=p^{2a}q^{2b}e_{a,b}.
\]

It obeys

\[
QS_p=p^2S_pQ,
\qquad
QS_q=q^2S_qQ.
\]

Consequently

\[
QS_pS_q=p^2q^2S_pS_qQ
=p^2q^2S_qS_pQ.
\]

The algebraic two-prime transport square is also coherent.

## Gaussian interaction weight

At heat scale (t>0\), assign

\[
w_{a,b}=e^{-\pi t p^{2a}q^{2b}}.
\]

On the smallest valuation square,

\[
\begin{aligned}
w_{0,0}&=e^{-\pi t},\\
w_{1,0}&=e^{-\pi t p^2},\\
w_{0,1}&=e^{-\pi t q^2},\\
w_{1,1}&=e^{-\pi t p^2q^2}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Primewise separability would require

\[
w_{0,0}w_{1,1}=w_{1,0}w_{0,1}.
\]

Instead,

\[
\log\frac{w_{0,0}w_{1,1}}{w_{1,0}w_{0,1}}
=-\pi t(p^2-1)(q^2-1)<0.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
w_{0,0}w_{1,1}-w_{1,0}w_{0,1}<0.
\]

The Gaussian coupling has strictly negative mixed logarithmic curvature on
every nontrivial two-prime square.

## Curvature is not holonomy

The negative mixed difference measures failure of the scalar weight to factor
as a product of independent (p)- and (q)-weights. It is not a commutator
anomaly:

- the label shifts commute;
- the common scale operator satisfies compatible (q)-commutation laws;
- the interval boundary cocycle square closes exactly.

Normalized edge ratios derived from one global vertex potential also telescope
around the square. Calling the mixed difference holonomy would confuse
interaction energy with connection curvature.

## One-prime finite truncation remains hostile

For one prime, the first two Gaussian-weighted valuation states give

\[
F_p(\lambda)=w_0+w_1\lambda,
\qquad
w_k=e^{-\pi t p^{2k}}.
\]

Its zero is

\[
\lambda_*=-\frac{w_0}{w_1}
=-e^{\pi t(p^2-1)}.
\]

Thus

\[
|\lambda_*|>1.
\]

Under the canonical local coordinate

\[
\lambda=p^{1/2-s},
\]

the seam has \(|\lambda|=1\), while the finite Gaussian-weighted zero lies
strictly off it. Gaussian sampling destroys the local seam confinement of the
unweighted geometric Fock polynomial at the first nontrivial truncation.

## Primitive and square typing

The primitive and square boundary intervals are

\[
B_{\log p}[A]
\]

and

\[
B_{2\log p}[A]
=B_{\log p}[A]
+p^{iz}B_{\log p}[S_{\log p}A].
\]

The second is coherently generated by two staged (p)-translations, but it is
not identical to twice the primitive interval. The translated second segment
must remain visible.

This gives the exact finite distinction between primitive and square incidence
before their arithmetic weights or completion classes are applied.

## Consequence

The two-prime audit produces a clean result:

- transport and boundary incidence are coherent and anomaly-free;
- Gaussian scale coupling introduces genuine nonseparable interaction;
- that interaction already permits off-seam zeros at finite truncation;
- its negative mixed curvature is not an orientation mechanism.

The remaining hope cannot be a two-prime commutator anomaly. It must lie in the
full typed completion of primitive, square, and higher interval currents, or in
a source law showing that the infinite restricted product reorganizes the
finite Gaussian interaction without erasing its boundary provenance.
