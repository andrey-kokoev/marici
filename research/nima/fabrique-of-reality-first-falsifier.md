# Fabrique of Reality: First Falsifier

## Broad conjecture tested

The initial formulation suggested that failed gluing or transport is retained
as physical information.  Taken literally, that is false.

The flavor sector supplies the counterexample.  The nine-link loop phase is
invariant inside the sparse presentation groupoid, but a general weak-basis
transformation preserves every tested physical invariant while destroying the
sparse chart and changing the phase.  The sparse textures are not an atlas on
the physical quotient, so there is no admitted cross-chart morphism whose
failure could define a Čech or derived obstruction.  The mismatch is
presentation dependence, not remembered physical information.

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\text{failed attempted comparison}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{physical remainder}.
}
\]

The census separates three logically distinct outcomes:

3. **Populated remainder.** A source-derived chain or readout pairs nontrivially
   with the invariant defect.  The leading radiative soft–BMS–memory triangle
   exemplifies this; the tested scattering line does not.

## Surviving conjecture: authority-qualified Fabrique

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\text{remembered physical failure}
= {}& \text{admitted comparison}\\
&+\text{equivalence-invariant defect}\\
&+\text{functorial composition/coherence}\\
&+\text{source-derived population/readout}.
\end{aligned}}
\]

The first three terms define a candidate remainder in the mathematical
Fabrique.  The fourth distinguishes a physically active remainder from an
unpopulated but admissible port.

This is a substantial narrowing.  The Fabrique is not a machine that turns
every mismatch into ontology.  It is an authority-sensitive calculus: only a
failure of a morphism that was validly typed before the calculation can be
retained.  An illegal comparison is rejected rather than reified.

\[
g^*\operatorname{Def}(f)

\]

The strongest available pair is:

* the scattering normalization–conductor symbol under a physical
  residue/Gysin functor, once that functor exists;

Failure of this law in either mature packet would falsify the claim that the
remainders belong to one compositional calculus.  Until then, the
authority-qualified conjecture is supported but not established.
