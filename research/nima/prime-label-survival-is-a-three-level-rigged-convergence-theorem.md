# Prime-label survival is a three-level rigged convergence theorem

## Rigged triple

The primitive channel must be audited on a rigged triple

[
mathcal E_Psubset H_Psubsetmathcal E_P'.
]

A single operator-norm statement cannot certify all three levels.

## Test-space level

For every continuous seminorm (q_delta), finite prime truncations must obey

[
q_delta(P_Fx)le C_delta q_{delta'}(x)
]

with (C_delta) independent of (F), and cutoff truncations must satisfy

[
P_{{ple X}}xlongrightarrow x
]

in the topology of (mathcal E_P).

For a solid Köthe rigging the ideal law is

[
q_delta(P_Fx)le q_delta(x).
]

This is equicontinuity of the coordinate family, not merely continuity of each
coordinate projection.

## Hilbert level

On (H_P), require

[
sup_F|P_F|<infty
]

and strong cutoff convergence. This is the ordinary unconditional Hilbert
decomposition.

The square history channel can often be treated directly at this level because
its seam coefficients are already square summable.

## Exact hostile

Take

[
mathcal E_P=ell^1,qquad
H_P=ell^2,qquad
mathcal E_P'=ell^infty
]

with ordinary coordinate truncations.

On (ell^1), all truncations are contractive and converge in norm. On
(ell^2), they are orthogonal projections and converge strongly. Let

[
u=(1,1,1,ldots)inell^infty.
]

Then (P_X'u	o u) weak-* because every (ell^1) test has a summable tail.
But

[
|P_X'u-u|_{ell^infty}=1
]

for every (X). Since the strong dual of the Banach space (ell^1) is the
norm topology on (ell^infty), strong-dual convergence fails.

Thus prime labels survive perfectly on the test and Hilbert levels and weakly
on the dual, while any constructor defined as a strong-dual cutoff limit is
unauthorized.

## Constructor typing table

The completion packet must declare the required convergence mode for each map:

- distributional seam endpoint: weak dual may suffice;
- Hilbert square history: norm or strong Hilbert convergence;
- closed mixed operator: graph convergence;
- quadratic Green form: closed-form convergence;
- sewing on the strong dual: bounded-set convergence;
- scalar distributional readout: weak dual convergence, provided the test is
  fixed before the limit.

A later constructor may not silently demand a stronger topology than the
earlier limit provides.

## Intertwining at three levels

For a sewing map (S) and wall map (R_{mathrm{wall}}), require

[
SP_F=P_FS,
qquad
R_{mathrm{wall}}P_F=P_FR_{mathrm{wall}}
]

on the test core. Their transposes then intertwine weakly on the dual. Strong
dual intertwining additionally requires continuity on bounded sets and
strong-dual convergence of the truncated images.

## Frontier

The immediate source calculation is to identify the actual primitive test
space and its bounded sets. If it is a nuclear Montel Köthe space, weak and
strong convergence may coincide on suitable bounded families; this must be
proved rather than assumed. If it behaves like the (ell^1) hostile, seam
values remain weak distributions and strong-dual sewing cannot be defined by
cutoff completion.

This is the precise three-level label-survival certificate for the first Adams
edge.
