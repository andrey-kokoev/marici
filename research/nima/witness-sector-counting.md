# Witness-based counting of the four-sector presentation

## Question

Use the prior structure p,q in Fill(K), with h filling q-p, as a proof of concept for counting the existing 1836-arrow construction while retaining constituent occurrences.

## Explicit additive realization

Keep the four parent-face sectors and incident-arrow cover of `1836-constituent-gluing.md`. Construct free abelian groups:

- C0 has one generator K_e for each original typed coefficient arrow e.
- C1 has one generator (i,e) for every sector i incident to e. Its boundary is d1(i,e)=K_e.
- C2 has one generator h_e whenever e connects two sectors i<j. Its boundary is d2 h_e=(j,e)-(i,e).

Thus p_e=(i,e) and q_e=(j,e) are two fillers of the same additive boundary K_e, and h_e is explicitly adjoined as their comparison filler. Both occurrences retain e's original id; that id recovers the exact complex coefficient and typed endpoints. The original arrow becomes a boundary generator in this auxiliary presentation; this is not an identification of its native geometric degree with degree zero.

The distinction matters: this constructs incidence-level comparisons between two retained descriptions of the same arrow. It does not discover new geometric fillers between arbitrary independently supplied terms, or assert native identity types inhabited without a comparison map.

## Exact count

The complex has ranks

    C2: 1296 -> C1: 3132 -> C0: 1836.

For each arrow, its one or two occurrences form a separate summand. On a two-occurrence summand, d1 maps both basis vectors to K_e and its kernel is freely generated by q_e-p_e. The associated d2 is injective. On a one-occurrence summand d1 is an isomorphism. Therefore the sequence is exact over the integers, not merely after taking dimensions over a field.

Each sector contributes 783 occurrences. Each of the six sector pairs has 216 shared boundary labels and 216 comparison fillers:

    1836 = 4*783 - 6*216.

This subtraction computes reconstructed independent arrow labels, not a deletion of the witness records. The full retained presentation still includes C1 and C2. Charging every comparison filler as an additional primitive operation would be a different cost policy.

## Recovery and falsifier

Choosing the first incident owner gives an explicit section of d1. The difference between any other occurrence and that section is the boundary of its retained h_e. The checker verifies this recovery on every occurrence. Replacing q_e-p_e by q_e+p_e yields boundary 2K_e rather than zero; all cross-arrow controls detect that orientation error.

There are no triple-incidence arrow supports in this cover, so this realization introduces no further comparison generators. That does not deny higher geometric coherence in an interior tetrahedral subdivision; it describes the existing primitive-arrow cover only.
