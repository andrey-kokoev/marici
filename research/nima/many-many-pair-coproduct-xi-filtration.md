# Many-many pair coproduct Xi-filtration

Let `F^r U_ar` be the source Xi-adic filtration. The pair carrier receives the product filtration

$$
F^rU_{\rm pair}
=\sum_{a+b=r}F^aU_{\rm ar}\widehat\otimes
\overline{F^bU_{\rm ar}}.
$$

A divisor-preserving pair coproduct must satisfy

$$
\Delta_{\rm pair}(F^rU_{\rm ar})
\subseteq
F^rU_{\rm pair}.
$$

The pair incidence must preserve filtration degree:

$$
B_{\rm rad}(F^rU_{\rm pair})
\subseteq
F^r\mathcal G_{\rm pair,rad}.
$$

For polarized matrix units, reciprocal exchange sends

$$
F^aU\otimes\overline{F^bU}
\longmapsto
F^bU\otimes\overline{F^aU},
$$

so the product filtration is exchange-stable.
