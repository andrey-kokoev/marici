# Double residue closes the unaugmented logarithmic p-normal route

In the punctured-bidisc chart

\[
u=q_1,\qquad v=q_2,\qquad q_3=p+u+v,
\]

with \(p\) a unit, the target is

\[
p\eta=\frac{p\,du\wedge dv}{uv(p+u+v)}.
\]

Its double residue at \(u=v=0\) is one. For every Laurent one-form
\(Adu+Bdv\), the coefficient of \(u^{-1}v^{-1}\) in
\(d(Adu+Bdv)\) is zero termwise. Hence \(p\eta\) is not exact in the
ordinary logarithmic or completed Laurent denominator carrier, regardless of
pole depth.

This independently strengthens the minimal Čech no-go. The bounded rational
systems at depths one and two have ranks \((9,10)\) and \((24,25)\) over both
\(\mathbf F_{101}\) and \(\mathbf F_{103}\), while the exponent-local residue
argument is unbounded.

All unaugmented local primitive routes are therefore closed. Only a
source-derived relative bulk/face or exceptional mapping cone whose face
residue changes the target complex can reopen the \(p\)-normal programme. No
ordinary increase in denominator depth can do so.
