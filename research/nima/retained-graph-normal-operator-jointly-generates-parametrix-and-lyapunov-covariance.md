# Retained graph normal operator jointly generates the parametrix and Lyapunov covariance

## Source graph

Let `E` be a finite-dimensional Hilbert source and let

\[
B:E\to H_B,
\qquad
M:E\to H_M
\]

be the two retained source operations. Define the joint graph map

\[
R:E\longrightarrow E\oplus H_B\oplus H_M,
\qquad
Rf=(f,Bf,Mf).
\]

Its normal operator is

\[
L=R^*R=I+B^*B+M^*M.
\]

The retained identity coordinate forces

\[
L\ge I.
\]

Hence `L` is invertible before any scalar codiagonal is applied.

## Canonical source parametrix

Define

\[
H=L^{-1}R^*.
\]

Then

\[
HR=L^{-1}R^*R=I.
\]

Moreover,

\[
P_{\operatorname{Ran}R}=RH=RL^{-1}R^*
\]

is the orthogonal projection onto the graph range. Thus the retained graph has closed complemented range, and `H` is its canonical left parametrix.

## Canonical variance-changing covariance

Use the same positive source generator `L` in the Lyapunov equation

\[
LG+GL=I.
\]

Because `L` is positive self-adjoint, the canonical solution is

\[
G=\frac12L^{-1}.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
G=\int_0^\infty e^{-tL}e^{-tL}\,dt.
\]

Therefore the parametrix and covariance are generated by the same inverse:

\[
H=L^{-1}R^*,
\qquad
G=\frac12L^{-1}.
\]

This is the first coupled construction rather than two independently selected repairs.

## Analytical continuation of the construction

For closed densely defined `B` and `M`, equip

\[
D=D(B)\cap D(M)
\]

with the graph norm

\[
\|f\|_D^2
=
\|f\|^2+
\|Bf\|^2+
\|Mf\|^2.
\]

The graph map `R:D -> E direct_sum H_B direct_sum H_M` is isometric and has closed range. Its Hilbert adjoint gives the canonical graph parametrix. The associated closed form defines the positive self-adjoint operator

\[
L=I+B^*B+M^*M
\]

in the form sense, with bounded inverse on the middle Hilbert carrier.

This closes retained-graph strictness. It does not yet prove strictness after the analytic--arithmetic pushout or after output codiagonalization.

## Completed variance disposition

Boundedness of `L^(-1)` on the middle Hilbert space does not establish a map

\[
L^{-1}:E'\to E
\]

for the prime test-space rigging

\[
E\subset H\subset E'.
\]

The existing prime-rigging hostile closes this extension for every finite logarithmic differential order. For the natural label generator `L_p=(log p)^2`,

\[
(Gb)_p=\frac1{2\sqrt p\log p},
\qquad
(Ga)_p=\frac1{2(\log p)^2},
\]

and neither sequence is rapidly decreasing in `p`. Division by any fixed power of `log p` has the same defect.

Infinitely smoothing Gaussian pivots map dual to test grade but fail fixed-scale Adams naturality:

\[
K_\tau\psi_k=\psi_kK_{k^2\tau}.
\]

Invariant averaging over the heat-scale orbit collapses to a scalar multiple of the identity and removes the smoothing. Therefore the common-inverse theorem remains valid on the retained Hilbert graph but does not provide the completed dual-to-test variance change.

The surviving source-native route retains the two covectors as the bivector `a wedge b` and seeks a test two-cycle or relative Green correspondence only at final evaluation. Reciprocal doubling supplies a finite exact oriented-flux cancellation, while its complete current normalization and completion remain open.
