# The bilateral Euler first jet is injective but not coercive on the full valuation Hilbert space

## Result

The natural upstream reciprocal Euler observer supplied by the bilateral valuation shift is not uniformly faithful on the full local Hilbert space.

Its central first jet is an injective multiplication operator with a symbol that vanishes at two spectral points. Hence it has dense nonclosed range and is not bounded below.

Therefore the arithmetic port can provide essential coercivity only on a source-selected current subspace or after adding a complementary spectral observer.

## Bilateral current

For one prime \(p\), let

\[
a=p^{-1/2},
\qquad
L=\log p,
\]

and let \(U\) be the bilateral shift on

\[
\ell^2(\mathbb Z).
\]

The operator-valued reciprocal current is

\[
\mathcal J_p(z)
=
\sum_{k\ge1}
\frac{a^k}{k}
\left(
e^{kLz}U^k
-
e^{-kLz}U^{-k}
\right).
\]

Its central first jet is

\[
D_p
=
\mathcal J_p'(0)
=
L\sum_{k\ge1}a^k
\left(
U^k+U^{-k}
\right).
\]

The series converges in operator norm because \(0<a<1\). The operator is bounded and self-adjoint.

## Fourier symbol

Under the Fourier transform

\[
\ell^2(\mathbb Z)\cong L^2(\mathbb T),
\]

the shift \(U\) becomes multiplication by \(e^{i\theta}\). Therefore \(D_p\) becomes multiplication by

\[
d_p(\theta)
=
2L\sum_{k\ge1}a^k\cos(k\theta).
\]

Summing the geometric series gives

\[
d_p(\theta)
=
2L
\frac{a\cos\theta-a^2}
{1-2a\cos\theta+a^2}.
\]

The denominator is strictly positive. The numerator vanishes exactly when

\[
\cos\theta=a.
\]

Since \(0<a<1\), there are two such points on the circle.

## Injective but not bounded below

The zero set of \(d_p\) has measure zero. Hence multiplication by \(d_p\) has trivial kernel on \(L^2(\mathbb T)\):

\[
\ker D_p=\{0\}.
\]

But

\[
\operatorname*{ess\,inf}_\theta|d_p(\theta)|=0.
\]

Therefore \(D_p\) is not bounded below.

Choose normalized functions \(f_n\) supported on shrinking arcs around a zero of \(d_p\). Then

\[
\|f_n\|_2=1,
\qquad
\|D_pf_n\|_2\to0.
\]

Thus the local arithmetic observer already has a singular sequence before any prime completion is formed.

Its range is dense because the kernel of its adjoint is zero, but the range is not closed because the operator is not bounded below.

## Scalar augmentation does not repair the Hilbert defect

The augmentation distribution \(\ell_p(e_j)=1\) recovers the scalar Euler derivative:

\[
\ell_pD_pe_0
=
2L\sum_{k\ge1}a^k
=
\frac{2La}{1-a}.
\]

This number is strictly positive.

However, \(\ell_p\) is not a vector in \(\ell^2(\mathbb Z)\). A nonzero vacuum matrix coefficient does not imply that \(D_p\) is coercive on the whole valuation Hilbert space.

The scalar port observes the distinguished vacuum channel while the spectral packets near

\[
\cos\theta=a
\]

remain arbitrarily dark.

## Source-current line

If the admitted arithmetic odd object is only the one-dimensional current line generated by the source vacuum incidence, then the scalar Euler port is faithful after using its source pullback metric.

But this is a restriction of object:

\[
\ell^2(\mathbb Z)
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
\mathbb C e_0
\ \text{or its cyclic current packet}.
\]

It cannot be promoted to full-Hilbert coercivity without a selection theorem showing that the mixed Adams edge sees no other valuation spectral directions.

## Complementary observer

A second bounded multiplier \(q_p(\theta)\) can jointly repair the local defect only if

\[
|d_p(\theta)|^2+|q_p(\theta)|^2
\ge
\delta_p^2
\]

almost everywhere.

The smallest natural complementary coordinate is one that does not vanish at

\[
\cos\theta=a.
\]

For example, the seam-skew zeroth current has a sine-type symbol, but its exact source role and metric must be derived. Merely choosing \(q_p=1\) would insert an unauthorized identity observer.

Completion then requires a lower bound uniform in \(p\), not only a positive \(\delta_p\) at each prime.

## Consequence

The earlier architectural hope that “upstream arithmetic observability” automatically supplies the essential lower margin is false on the full bilateral valuation Hilbert space.

Both available routes have precise limitations:

- theta incidence is compact across primes;
- the bilateral Euler first jet has local spectral zeros.

A coercive joint arithmetic–analytic observer therefore requires one of:

1. a source selection theorem restricting to the vacuum/current line;
2. an independently authorized complementary valuation observable;
3. a different complete arithmetic energy that dominates both spectral coordinates.

## Next gate

The smallest source-native test is now local:

> Compute the symbol of the reciprocal-odd zeroth or seam-skew valuation port and test whether it is nonzero at the two zeros \(\cos\theta=p^{-1/2}\) of the Euler first-jet symbol.

If the two symbols form a uniformly positive frame over all primes, the arithmetic plane can supply the essential margin that compact theta realization cannot.
