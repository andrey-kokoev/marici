# Three Response Cases Compose if Residues Carry Structure

The response type

\[
\operatorname{Value}(T)
\;|\;
\operatorname{Qualified}(T,R)
\;|\;
\operatorname{Obstruction}(R)
\]

is closed under sequential composition. Prior and later residues concatenate,
and an obstruction preserves the residue accumulated before it.

Exact enumeration verifies associativity for all combinations of ordinary,
qualified, and obstructed steps in the finite model. No fourth top-level
response case is required.

But \(R\) cannot be a flat error string or unordered bag. The traces

\[
(\text{restriction},\text{boundary})
\quad\text{and}\quad
(\text{boundary},\text{restriction})
\]

have the same unordered contents but different compositional histories.
When two paths are declared equivalent, their comparison requires a coherence
cell inside \(R\).

Higher residues enrich \(R\); they do not create a fourth outer response
variant.
