# Finite synthesis and its adjoint form the reciprocal linking pair on the source-generated range

## Finite synthesis

Let \(V_X\) be the finite labelled arithmetic carrier and let \(H_X\) be the
finite tail--seam boundary carrier. The weighted synthesis map is

\[
U_X:V_X\longrightarrow H_X.
\]

Its columns are the typed weighted cut atoms. Finite source faithfulness means

\[
\ker U_X=0.
\]

The canonical reciprocal candidate is not a fitted inverse. It is the adjoint

\[
U_X^*:H_X\longrightarrow V_X
\]

in the declared finite inner-product structures.

Thus set

\[
M_X=U_X,
\qquad
N_X=U_X^*.
\]

This is the first source-derived two-sided linking pair.

## Gram and frame operators

The two compositions are

\[
G_X=U_X^*U_X
\]

on the arithmetic carrier and

\[
F_X=U_XU_X^*
\]

on the analytic carrier.

Since \(U_X\) is injective,

\[
G_X>0.
\]

However, \(F_X\) vanishes on the orthogonal complement of
\(\operatorname{ran}U_X\). Therefore the pair is not faithful on an analytic
carrier containing unrelated directions.

The minimal reciprocal locus is

\[
H_X^{\mathrm{src}}=\operatorname{ran}U_X.
\]

On \(V_X\) and \(H_X^{\mathrm{src}}\), both \(G_X\) and \(F_X\) are positive
and invertible.

## Polar normalization

Define

\[
W_X
=
U_XG_X^{-1/2}.
\]

Then

\[
W_X^*W_X=I_{V_X}
\]

and

\[
W_XW_X^*=I_{H_X^{\mathrm{src}}}.
\]

Thus \(W_X\) is unitary from the arithmetic carrier onto the source-generated
analytic range.

The normalized links

\[
\widetilde M_X=W_X,
\qquad
\widetilde N_X=W_X^*
\]

satisfy exact two-sided fullness:

\[
\widetilde N_X\widetilde M_X=I_{V_X},
\qquad
\widetilde M_X\widetilde N_X=I_{H_X^{\mathrm{src}}}.
\]

At finite cutoff, the reciprocal linking pair is therefore constructed
canonically from synthesis and its Gram operator.

## Why range restriction is required

Keeping all of \(H_X\) would leave every vector in
\((\operatorname{ran}U_X)^\perp\) invisible to the arithmetic source. No
observer or determinant can promote such directions to source-derived states.

The correct analytic object is either:

1. the source-generated range \(H_X^{\mathrm{src}}\); or
2. the graph carrier retaining \(V_X\) and \(U_XV_X\) together.

Range restriction is not deletion of source data. It removes analytic
directions with no declared arithmetic incidence. Conversely, quotienting
\(V_X\) by a small analytic norm is forbidden because it can erase labelled
source directions.

## Morita interpretation

On the finite source-generated carriers, \(W_X\) implements an equivalence
between the endomorphism algebras:

\[
\operatorname{End}(V_X)
\longleftrightarrow
\operatorname{End}(H_X^{\mathrm{src}}).
\]

The linking bimodules generated by \(W_X\) and \(W_X^*\) are full, and their
pairings recover both identities.

This is stronger than nonzero off-diagonality. It gives a faithful action on
both source-generated sectors.

## Typed boundary carrier

The range must be formed before erasing boundary types. The synthesis target
is the direct typed packet containing tail, seam, primitive endpoint, square
endpoint, connected endpoint, and the declared archimedean line.

If one first projects to the reassembled scalar function, distinct arithmetic
labels can acquire nearly equal analytic images and the Gram margin can
collapse prematurely.

Therefore \(G_X\) must be computed in the complete graph or typed boundary
norm, not in a scalar projection norm selected for convenience.

## Completion obstruction

Let

\[
\gamma_X
=
\lambda_{\min}(G_X).
\]

Finite injectivity gives \(\gamma_X>0\) for every \(X\). A completion-stable
reciprocal link requires a compact-local lower bound

\[
\inf_X\gamma_X>0
\]

in the declared source topology, or a source-authorized renorming that makes
the polar maps compatible with cutoff inclusions.

The known smooth-Gram phenomenon permits

\[
\gamma_X\longrightarrow0.
\]

Then \(G_X^{-1/2}\) becomes unbounded, \(W_X\) fails to descend as an
equivalence, and finite Morita fullness escapes at completion.

This is the exact action-margin obstruction in the 405-profile classifier.

## Source topology warning

One can force \(W_X\) to be unitary by defining the arithmetic norm through

\[
\lVert v\rVert_{U_X}=\lVert U_Xv\rVert.
\]

That is not automatically source-authorized. It may erase valuation/Fock
directions whose analytic visibility tends to zero.

The admissible topology must be generated independently by the arithmetic
constructors. The Gram lower bound must then be proved relative to that
topology, not installed by pullback from the analytic image.

The graph norm

\[
\lVert(v,U_Xv)\rVert^2
=
\lVert v\rVert_V^2+\lVert U_Xv\rVert_H^2
\]

preserves source identity but does not by itself make \(U_X\) bounded below
into the analytic factor.

## Reciprocity and sector doubling

Apply the construction separately to the two reciprocal sector syntheses

\[
U_{+,X}:V_{+,X}\to H_{+,X},
\qquad
U_{-,X}:V_{-,X}\to H_{-,X}.
\]

Source reciprocal transport must identify their polar links:

\[
W_{-,X}(s)
\longleftrightarrow
W_{+,X}(1-s)^*
\]

in the declared frame.

Only then may the two pairs be inserted as \(M_X,N_X\) in the complete
reciprocal linking block. Independent polar decompositions with unrelated
phases do not supply the functional-equation orientation.

## Determinant consequence

Using unnormalized links, the Schur interaction contains

\[
U_X^*A_{+,X}^{-1}U_X.
\]

Using normalized links, it contains the unitary transport \(W_X\) but also the
Gram factors that were moved into the diagonal blocks.

No normalization removes the Gram determinant; it only relocates it. The
primitive, square, seam, and archimedean anomaly lines must track that
relocation so that the completed determinant frame remains fixed.

## Hostile tests

1. Using \(U_X\) without \(U_X^*\) leaves a triangular determinant.
2. Keeping \((\operatorname{ran}U_X)^\perp\) as source-derived analytic state
   creates invisible directions.
3. Declaring finite injectivity to be completion fullness ignores
   \(\gamma_X\to0\).
4. Defining the source norm by \(U_X\) silently transports analytic authority
   backward.
5. Polar normalization without phase-compatible reciprocal comparison loses
   orientation.
6. Moving Gram factors between links and diagonals without anomaly-line
   bookkeeping changes the determinant frame.

## Consequence for categorical RH

The finite reciprocal links now have a canonical source construction:

\[
M_X=U_X,
\qquad
N_X=U_X^*,
\]

or their polar-normalized versions on the source-generated range.

The remaining action problem is no longer existence at finite cutoff. It is
the uniform Gram lower bound and reciprocal phase compatibility needed for
completion. If the lower bound fails in the authorized topology, the
Morita-full route closes unless an additional source observer enlarges the
typed synthesis before completion.

## Verdict

Finite weighted synthesis and its adjoint form the required two-sided linking
pair on the source-generated analytic range. Polar decomposition proves exact
finite Morita fullness. The categorical RH obstruction is the possible
collapse of the Gram margin and reciprocal frame at completion, not the absence
of a finite reciprocal arrow.
