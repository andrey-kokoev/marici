# The sector-relative future quotient

Owner: `marici.Nima`

## Construction

Let `X` be the source-constructible state set of one sector and let
`U={u_i:X->Y_i}` be its source-authorized successor probes.  Define

\[
x\sim_{\mathcal U}x'
\iff
u_i(x)=u_i(x')\quad\text{for every }i.
\]

Write

\[
q_{\mathcal U}:X\to L_{\mathcal U}=X/{\sim_{\mathcal U}}.
\]

Every authorized successor factors uniquely through `q_U`.  Moreover,
`q_U` is the coarsest quotient with that property: merging any two distinct
classes destroys factorization of at least one authorized successor.

Thus `L_U` is the minimal memory sufficient for all currently admitted
futures.  This is a sector-relative Yoneda construction: the state is retained
only through its relations to the admitted probe family.

## Access dependence

The quotient depends on the authorized family, not merely on the upstream
state set.

- Adding successors refines the quotient and can expose previously hidden
  residue.
- Removing access coarsens the quotient and can erase operative distinctions.
- Enlarging the constructor grammar can enlarge `X` before the quotient is
  formed.

These are three different operations and must not be identified.

## Cross-sector instances

### Magnetic

The fractional interference configurations `d=17/3,37/3` are excluded before
forming the quotient: they are not in the integer source-constructible set
`X`.  Passing to a cubic cover makes monomials representable but deck typing
still prevents their circuit from entering one equivariant state sector.

### Cosmology

The scalar sum probe identifies route loss `(0,0)` with interference
`(8C,-8C)`.  Exceptional restriction retains the two route components and
therefore refines the future quotient, separating them whenever `C!=0`.

### Topological quantum sector

One-use effects identify QND and outcome-conditioned flip instruments.
Repeat measurement separates them.  The instrument surface is therefore the
minimal memory for sequential futures; the effect surface is sufficient only
for one-use probability queries.

## Deutsch--Popperian conjecture

For every mature Marici sector, its coefficient lens is equivalent to the
sector-relative future quotient generated by all source-authorized successor
operations.

This is falsified if:

- the proposed lens distinguishes states no authorized future can separate;
- an authorized successor does not factor through the lens;
- the lens changes under a mere presentation/gauge change that preserves all
  successor signatures;
- a supposedly virtual state is actually generated by the frozen source
  grammar.

## Scope boundary

The finite theorem is set-theoretic.  Linear, derived, sheaf, and enriched
sectors require the corresponding coequalizer or image object, and physical
authority over the successor family remains source-specific.
