# Many-many G4 generator-equality contract

For every elementary source tensor `f tensor g` in the common rapid core, the authoritative G4 map must return the ordered bordered packet

$$
\bigl(\rho_{f,g}(0),E_{f,g},W_{f,g},R_{f,g}\bigr).
$$

The witness record must include:

- source labels and variance lane;
- shell and endpoint data;
- four bordered coordinates;
- reciprocal transform;
- Laplace-jet restrictions;
- equality residual between the G4 map and `T_pair->border`.

The equality residual must vanish on the dense generator set. Continuity then extends the equality to the completed projective pair carrier.

This contract precedes rank, radical, determinant, and spectral witnesses.
