## Verdict

The string test is **partially positive and globally open**.

* Five-point physical periods provide a typed positive example: the assembled
  scalar circuit is insensitive to the internal presentation of the
  Pochhammer cells.
* Six-point exceptional modules have the required cyclic/dihedral covariance,
  but no existing artifact constructs the global physical readout on those
  modules.  Consequently commutator descent of that readout is not yet a
  well-typed proposition.

In particular, trivial cyclic holonomy of a coefficient module must not be
promoted to physical-readout descent.  Flavor already shows why that shortcut
is invalid.
