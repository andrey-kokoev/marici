# An explicit residual observer detects the nonzero attachment class

## Result

The residual-port comparison preserves a genuinely nonzero first filtered attachment, not just its scalar pairing identities. An explicit continuous two-sector observer detects the actual four-event product. Its value depends on a strictly positive difference of window moment ratios; the arithmetic multiplier cancels.

The finite source-equivariant nullhomotopy obstruction then proves that the completed attachment roof is nonzero in the declared strict localization. No projectivity of completed ideals, source-dual surjectivity, or full-line Tate operator limit is invoked.

## 1. Fix one genuine finite source witness

Use the first diamond on primes 2 and 3, with the route of arithmetic vertices 2,4,12. Its successive forcings are

`f_1=1_[log(2),log(4)] Phi`,

`f_2=1_[log(4),log(12)] Phi`.

Let a be the singly retained diamond relation. Append the forgotten diamond relation c on primes 5 and 7, starting at 12. Put v=ac. It has eight path coefficients of modulus one and lies in I^2. In the four-event packet I^3=0.

The joint image is

`j_Psi(v)=D_Psi(a) tensor_balanced D_Psi(c)`.

Select the first forgotten edge 12->60 of the second diamond. In the first factor select, in turn, the retained edge 2->4 and the retained edge 4->12. These give two distinct balanced sectors, each with coefficient +1, one feature seam and all other buffers/letters vacuum. Their feature values are Psi(f_1) and Psi(f_2), respectively.

## 2. A residual-isolating one-slot observer

Choose z_0=i y in the compact Euler spectral set, with y>gamma>1/2. Put

`L_0=L(1/2+y)`, `e_0(u)=exp(-y u)`.

L_0 is real. In the residual coordinates (U,V), choose the observer

`o_0=(e_0,-2 L_0 e_0)`.

It is an admissible element of D_gamma direct-sum H_gamma. For the function-valued port carrier one may take the constant family with this value; observation is evaluation at z_0, which is bounded in the C(K) norm.

Recall

`Q_res((u,v),(u',v'))=q_ar(u,u')+<u,v'>_0+<v,u'>_0`.

On the exponential fibre at z_0, L_ar acts by L_0. Therefore

`Q_res(Psi(f)(z_0),o_0)=<V_f(z_0),e_0>_0`.

The choice -2L_0 cancels the arithmetic/even contribution. This is a choice of observer in the already prescribed form, not a modification of that form.

For the positive actual window f let

`X_f=X_f(i y)>0`,

`mu_f=i X_f'(i y)/X_f(i y)`.

Since V_f(z_0)=sqrt(2)X_f(mu_f-L_0)e_0 and ||e_0||_0^2=1/(2y),

`Q_res(Psi(f)(z_0),o_0)
 =sqrt(2) X_f (mu_f-L_0)/(2y)`.

## 3. The two-sector detector has a strictly positive value

Write X_i=X_(f_i)(i y), mu_i=mu_(f_i)(y). In the selected second sector place o_0/X_2, and in the first sector place -o_0/X_1. All other observer coordinates are zero, and the spectator forgotten edge and buffers carry their unit vacuum observations. Call this finite observer eta.

Retaining the original positive seam-feature multiplier w_seam, its value on the joint image is

`Q_joint(j_Psi(v),eta)
 = w_seam sqrt(2) (mu_2-mu_1)/(2y) >0`.

Indeed, mu_f is the cosh-weighted mean of x tanh(yx). That function is strictly increasing on x>0, and the two positive forcing windows are successive. Thus

`mu_1 < log(4) tanh(y log(4)) < mu_2`.

The value does not depend on the sign or magnitude of L_0. This detector isolates a difference of actual residuals rather than only testing a nonzero even component.

All normalizations here concern this fixed finite witness. The divisions by X_1 and X_2 are finite positive scalars, not a claimed uniformly bounded reconstruction procedure over translated windows.

## 4. Continuity and degrees

The observer uses two finite shapes, outer event length four, seam count two and feature count one. With

`C_o=[sqrt(1+y^2)+2|L_0|]/sqrt(2(y-gamma))`,

its port norm is at most C_o. In the endpoint-radius/depth-moment control scale its norm is bounded by

`R^4 3^q b_s(2) b sqrt(w_seam) C_o (1/X_1+1/X_2)`.

Thus it belongs to every such declared seminorm. More directly, the bounded form Q_res and bounded evaluation at z_0 make it a continuous conjugate-linear observation on the completed joint target. Projective tensoring with unit vacuum observations preserves this bound.

In T=J_(Psi,2)[-1] it observes the bottom degree -1. Its continuous-dual degree is +1. There is no lower primal target degree, so this observation is a cocycle in the scalar continuous-dual complex. This observation alone is not being called a nonzero source-derived class; that requires the source-equivariance argument next.

No general Green-mate extension on the entire residual-port Banach carrier is needed to define this bounded bottom-degree functional.

## 5. Source equivariance prevents a nullhomotopy

Restrict to the finite four-event packet. The source complex for the extension is

`K=[I^2 -> I]`, in degrees -1,0,

because I^3=0 there. These ideals are projective as one-sided modules over the finite hereditary path source. The joint component F2:K->T is zero in degree zero and equals j_Psi in degree -1.

A source-equivariant nullhomotopy would have to give H:I->T^(-1) with

`j_Psi(v)=H(v)=H(ac)=a H(c)`.

The target's outer actions factor through the terminal recorder, so I acts by zero. The right side is zero, whereas eta detects a strictly positive value on the left. There is no T^(-2) term to supply another homotopy component. This is a contradiction.

Since K is a bounded projective complex over this finite one-sided source, the non-nullhomotopy statement detects a nonzero morphism in its algebraic derived category, not merely a nonzero chain map. No enveloping-algebra projectivity is assumed.

## 6. The completed roof remains nonzero

On the specified common factorial source, or on the inherited actual-letter quotient domain where the attachment construction is defined, write

`C <-q- K_src --F2--> T`.

The augmentation q is a strict quasi-isomorphism. Restriction to the finite packet is a continuous idempotent projection, preserves the strict exact comparisons, and followed by forgetting topology gives the finite derived comparison above.

If the completed roof were zero in the declared strict localization, its finite restriction would be zero. Section 5 rules that out. The residual-port comparison therefore retains genuine nonsplit extension data.

This argument does not assert that every non-nullhomotopic map from an arbitrary completed complex is nonzero after localization. The finite projective restriction is essential.

## 7. Boundaries

Established: an explicit continuous residual-sensitive observer, a nonzero actual joint product observation, and a nonzero completed source-equivariant attachment roof.

Not established: a uniform inverse, full source-dual surjectivity, a perfect-module tensor-Hom equivalence, or an arithmetic-only comparison with the residual removed. The observer keeps exactly the source moment data whose omission caused the window obstruction.

The construction uses the convergent half-line arithmetic operator/form. The all-prime full-line Tate leakage obstruction is unaffected; this residual is not that leakage and does not cancel it.
