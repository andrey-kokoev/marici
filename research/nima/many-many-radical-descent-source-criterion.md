# Many-many radical descent source criterion

$$
Q_{\rm bulk}(r,r)=0
\Longrightarrow
\langle Dr,zv\rangle=0
\quad\text{and}\quad
\langle Dv,zr\rangle=0

1. identify the radical as the closed even zero-trace subspace;
2. evaluate the ordered source commutator on that subspace;
3. pass the vanishing relation through joint graph closure.
