# The labelled reservoir repairs the commutator but not the Cartan factor

## Label-resolved lift

The derivative-current obstruction says that the scalar forcing port must be
resolved into its labelled exponential modes.  At a finite cutoff, let the
reservoir coordinates satisfy

\[
(P+\lambda_j)r_j=0,
\qquad P=\partial_q,
\]

and let their incidence into the tail equation be the labelled row
\(b=(b_1,\ldots,b_N)\).  The source-resolved triangular operator is

\[
\mathcal D_{z,X}=
\begin{pmatrix}
P+z&b_1&\cdots&b_N\\
0&P+\lambda_1&&0\\
\vdots&&\ddots&\\
0&0&&P+\lambda_N
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

All entries now belong to the commutative polynomial ring generated by \(P\).
The earlier residual \([P,M_f]=M_{f'}\) disappears because differentiation is
represented internally by the diagonal label action \(-\lambda_j\).

## Exact adjugate identity

The ordinary adjugate is now defined and satisfies

\[
\operatorname{adj}(\mathcal D_{z,X})\mathcal D_{z,X}
=
\mathcal D_{z,X}\operatorname{adj}(\mathcal D_{z,X})
=
\Delta_{z,X}(P)I,
\]

where

\[
\Delta_{z,X}(P)
=(P+z)\prod_{j=1}^{N}(P+\lambda_j).
\]

Thus label resolution really does repair noncommutativity.  It does not produce
the desired Cartan relation with the normal displacement
\(a=\operatorname{Re}(z)\).  It produces a differential polynomial of order
\(N+1\).

## Why this is not zero confinement

The factor \(\Delta_{z,X}(P)\) has kernel whenever the chosen function-space
domain admits any of the elementary modes

\[
e^{-zq},e^{-\lambda_1q},\ldots,e^{-\lambda_Nq}.
\]

Those kernels are mode equations, not evidence that \(a=0\).  Their number and
the differential order both grow with the cutoff.  Consequently the classical
adjugate proves algebraic exactness of the labelled finite system but supplies
no off-seam contraction and no source-derived orientation.

## DPC verdict

The source-labelled reservoir is the minimal repair of the commutator leak.
The finite reverse arrow then exists, but its composite is the characteristic
differential operator \(\Delta_{z,X}(P)\), not the normal scalar \(a\).

This separates two obligations that had been conflated:

1. compositional closure requires retaining all labelled reservoir modes;
2. RH-bearing contraction requires an additional source-derived reduction from
   the characteristic operator to a signed normal factor.

No algebraic adjugate supplies the second obligation.  Any proposed reduction
must also preserve boundary domains, reciprocal sewing, and cutoff completion.
The smallest falsifier is a single admitted exponential kernel mode off the
critical seam: it is killed by \(\Delta_{z,X}(P)\) while
\(\operatorname{Re}(z)-1/2\ne0\).
