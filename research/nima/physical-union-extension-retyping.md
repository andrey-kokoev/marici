# The proposed rank-41 physical localization extension is withdrawn

Status: superseded typing proposal. The rank-26 union packet remains valid;
the maps needed to place it in a short exact sequence do not.

The earlier proposal inferred

\[
15\longrightarrow41\longrightarrow26
\]

by adding a proposed rank-15 branch intersection to the established rank-26
five-mark quotient. That inference presupposed that the rational-form
inclusions of four-mark branches descend through the independently truncated
four- and five-mark quotient presentations.

The complete cutoff-seven relation audit in
`physical-branch-union-relation-descent-failure.md` disproved that premise. Of
5,780 fully represented domain relations, 852 have nonzero remainder after
the proposed map is reduced in the target. No target term is missing.
Therefore the finite packets do not currently carry a chain map from which a
rank-15 image, a rank-41 middle object, or an off-diagonal
\(15\times26\) block can be defined.

## What survives

- The intrinsic five-mark quotient has stable rank 26 and the expected Euler
  characteristic.
- The literal physical numerator horizontally generates that rank-26 packet.
- Labelled cyclic transport and the marked-line crossing geometry survive.

## Correct frontier

Construct the four-mark branches and five-mark union inside one
source-labelled localization/deletion complex *before* quotienting or
truncating. Only maps induced by that common complex may define branch images,
an extension cocycle, or a support test for \(\mathcal Q\).
