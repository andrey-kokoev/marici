# The literal physical localization extension must be retyped

The rank-26 stabilization changes the extension problem.  The earlier packet

\[
15+20=35
\]

uses a single four-mark branch of the residue.  The literal source is the sum
of the two branches.  After passage to their common denominator it lives on
the five-line union, whose marked-relative restriction has established rank
26 and is horizontally generated by the physical numerator.

Therefore the source-defined unsplit localization triangle has ranks

\[
15\longrightarrow 41\longrightarrow 26
\]

provided its cohomology is concentrated in the expected degree and the
localization sequence is short exact there.  Equivalently, the full
off-diagonal connection block has type

\[
\Theta_{\rm phys}:V_{26}\longrightarrow L_{15}\otimes\Omega^1,
\]

with (15\cdot26=390) scalar coordinates before flatness and triangular-gauge
relations.

This is a typing correction, not a reconstruction of those 390 coordinates.
It implies that the old rank-35 block and the reconstructed (4\times3) final
block are only projections of the literal extension problem.  Their failure
to show \(\mathcal Q\), or a candidate \(\mathcal Q\)-factor within them, cannot
settle the full source-normalized extension.

The immediate canonical target is consequently the gauge class

\[
[\Theta_{\rm phys}]
\in
\frac{\Omega^1\otimes\operatorname{Hom}(V_{26},L_{15})}
{\nabla_{L}H-H\nabla_{V}+dH},
\]

constructed from the localization triangle rather than from a chosen
primitive section.  Only the intrinsic support of this class is an admissible
place to test the algebraic quartic \(\mathcal Q\).
