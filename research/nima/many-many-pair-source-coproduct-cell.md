# Many-many pair-source coproduct cell

Let `U_ar` be the labelled arithmetic source and define the pair carrier

$$
U_{\rm pair}=U_{\rm ar}\widehat\otimes\overline{U_{\rm ar}}.
$$

The minimal diagonal coproduct on basis labels is

$$
\Delta_{\rm pair}(e_\lambda)
=e_\lambda\otimes\overline{e_\lambda}.
$$

To represent ordered cross-pair responses, retain the polarized matrix units

$$
 e_\lambda\otimes\overline{e_\mu},
$$

rather than applying only the diagonal map. The pair incidence is then a linear map

$$
B_{\rm rad}(e_\lambda\otimes\overline{e_\mu})
=\rho_{\lambda,\mu}^{\rm graph},
$$

with endpoint, Wronskian, ratio, grade, and shell labels retained.

The source-to-response comparison must commute with reciprocal exchange and Xi-adic filtration. The diagonal coproduct supplies autocorrelations; polarized matrix units supply the full ordered bulk polarization.
