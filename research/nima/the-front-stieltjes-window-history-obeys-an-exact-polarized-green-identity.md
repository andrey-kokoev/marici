# The front Stieltjes window history obeys an exact polarized Green identity

## Result

In the source Stieltjes metric \(d\nu=-dH\), the comoving window path satisfies an exact Hilbert-valued fundamental theorem and polarized Green identity.

Thus the Stieltjes endpoint pairing is not merely a positive candidate: it is dynamically compatible with the same scale history that produces the adjacent-window boundary.

This closes the Green/Stokes identity on the cyclic window-history subspace. It does not identify that cyclic form with the complete auxiliary Green block on every source sector.

## Regularity assumptions

Assume the source front \(H\) is absolutely continuous and smooth enough that

\[
\rho=-H'\ge0,
\qquad
\int_{\mathbb R}\rho(q)\,dq=1,
\]

and that the translated densities below belong to \(L^2(\nu)\) on compact positive \(t\)-intervals.

The Stieltjes metric is

\[
\langle f,g\rangle_\nu
=
\int\overline f(q)g(q)\,d\nu(q).
\]

## Window derivative

The window is

\[
W_t(q)=H(q+t)-H(q-t).
\]

Differentiating in \(t\),

\[
\partial_tW_t(q)
=
H'(q+t)+H'(q-t)
=
-\rho(q+t)-\rho(q-t).
\]

Hence

\[
\partial_tW_t(q)\le0.
\]

Under the stated regularity, the map

\[
t\longmapsto W_t
\]

is strongly \(C^1\) into \(L^2(\nu)\) on compact positive \(t\)-intervals.

## Hilbert-valued Stokes identity

For \(0<L<2L\),

\[
W_{2L}-W_L
=
\int_L^{2L}\partial_tW_t\,dt
\]

as an identity in \(L^2(\nu)\).

This is exactly the adjacent-window propagator:

\[
D_L=W_{2L}-W_L.
\]

The orientation is fixed by increasing scale. Reversing the path changes the sign of the integral.

## Polarized Green identity

Let \(F_t,G_t\) be two source window histories, or finite linear combinations for which the same regularity holds. Then

\[
\frac d{dt}
\langle F_t,G_t\rangle_\nu
=
\langle \partial_tF_t,G_t\rangle_\nu
+
\langle F_t,\partial_tG_t\rangle_\nu.
\]

Integrating gives

\[
\langle F_{2L},G_{2L}\rangle_\nu
-
\langle F_L,G_L\rangle_\nu
=
\int_L^{2L}
\left[
\langle \partial_tF_t,G_t\rangle_\nu
+
\langle F_t,\partial_tG_t\rangle_\nu
\right]dt.
\]

For \(F_t=G_t=W_t\),

\[
\|W_{2L}\|_\nu^2-\|W_L\|_\nu^2
=
2\int_L^{2L}
\langle W_t,\partial_tW_t\rangle_\nu\,dt.
\]

Since both \(W_t\) and \(\partial_tW_t\) are nonpositive,

\[
\langle W_t,\partial_tW_t\rangle_\nu\ge0.
\]

Thus endpoint energy increases with scale, consistently with interval-mass inclusion.

## Arithmetic pullback

For a scale measure \(\mu\), the comoving comparison is

\[
\mathcal K\mu
=
\int W_t\,d\mu(t).
\]

On finite endpoint packets, the primitive and square deltas map to

\[
W_L,
\qquad
W_{2L}.
\]

Therefore the polarized Stokes identity pulls back to the arithmetic endpoint plane through \(\mathcal K\).

The source coefficients

\[
p^{-1/2-\sigma-it},
\qquad
\frac12p^{-1-2it}
\]

remain external scalar labels and pass linearly through the identity.

## Boundary form

Define the history boundary form

\[
\mathfrak B_L(F,G)
=
\langle F_{2L},G_{2L}\rangle_\nu
-
\langle F_L,G_L\rangle_\nu.
\]

The Green identity proves that

\[
\mathfrak B_L(F,G)
=
\int_L^{2L}
\left[
\langle \partial_tF_t,G_t\rangle_\nu
+
\langle F_t,\partial_tG_t\rangle_\nu
\right]dt.
\]

Thus the boundary form is generated by the interior scale derivative, not fitted from endpoint scalar values.

This is the exact constructor property previously missing from an arbitrary endpoint Gram.

## Radical

On the cyclic history span, the Stieltjes inner product radical consists of functions vanishing \(\nu\)-almost everywhere.

Strict fronts give

\[
W_t\ne0
\]

in \(L^2(\nu)\) for every \(t>0\). Therefore neither primitive nor square endpoint is killed.

If two histories differ only off the support of \(\nu\), the form identifies them. This is the natural GNS quotient of the front state.

The separately typed coefficient wall is not represented by this quotient and must remain attached through its Wronskian connecting morphism.

## Uniform endpoint control

For all primes,

\[
m_\nu^2
\le
\|W_L\|_\nu^2
\le
\|W_{2L}\|_\nu^2
\le1,
\]

with \(L=\log p\).

Hence the cyclic boundary form has uniform endpoint scales. The history derivative norm may grow with \(L\), but the earlier graph estimate shows only logarithmic completion cost.

## Scope boundary

This theorem proves exact Green/Stokes compatibility for:

- the source front state;
- the comoving window kernel;
- the cyclic window-history range;
- its finite polarized span;
- the strict primitive-square endpoint pullback.

It does not yet prove that the complete RH auxiliary Green energy—including wall, causal odd history, tail/PV carrier, and all reciprocal sectors—is identical to this cyclic Stieltjes form.

The complete form may contain additional orthogonal or coupled sectors.
