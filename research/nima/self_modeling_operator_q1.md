# First finite self-modeling operator: the Q-1 microprogramme

## Status

This packet is a first construction, not evidence that the physical Carrier is
self-modeling. It asks whether a finite research system can extend itself with
a typed internal account of its own claims, evidence, legal moves, and
reopening conditions while remaining in the same organizational class.

Excitement before construction: 8.5/10. The high score concerns the prospect
of a falsifiable closure test, not confidence that the conjecture is true.

## Base object

Let \(X_{Q1}\) be the settled Q-1 research state. Its physical hard core is

\[
\mathcal Q\text{ is apparent at generic, nonsoft, homogeneous three-site
one-loop kinematics}.
\]

Represent a finite research state by a typed directed system

\[
X=(O,M,H,R),
\]

where \(O\) are objects or claims, \(M\) are admissible transformations,
\(H\) are typed obstructions or higher-coherence records, and \(R\) are
persistent readouts/evidence records. Every arrow has declared source and
target types.

## The operator

Define the depth-one self-modeling operator

\[
\mathsf U_1(X)=X\cup \operatorname{Model}_1(X).
\]

For each governed claim or action in \(X\), \(\operatorname{Model}_1(X)\)
adds only:

1. a concept object naming its role and scope;
2. dependency arrows to the source records that warrant it;
3. obstruction records for illegal composites;
4. a readout classifying a proposed move as allowed, forbidden, or retyped;
5. a projection back to the governed object.

It may not add physical evidence, alter a physical claim, or treat its own
classification as evidence. Thus \(\mathsf U_1\) is intended to be a
conservative reflective extension, not a self-certifying theory.

The same grammar types both \(X\) and \(\mathsf U_1(X)\): object, morphism,
obstruction/coherence, and record/readout. This is the first, weak meaning of
same-type closure.

## First prospective outputs

Before another Q calculation is attempted, the operator classifies three
moves:

- **Forbidden:** use the missing source-normalized Leray covector to reopen
  generic \(\mathcal Q\)-support. The covector can refine physical readout but
  cannot overturn the already derived trivial \(\mathcal Q\)-monodromy.
- **Allowed:** derive that covector to determine which portions of the
  nonsplit rank-26 coefficient system the physical contour observes.
- **Retyped:** investigate a successor quartic at a soft/discriminant
  intersection. This is a protective-belt test outside the generic hard-core
  scope, not a refutation of Q-1.

These classifications are prospective commitments. A future source-derived
result that forces a different classification is evidence against this
particular \(\mathsf U_1\), not something to be silently absorbed.

## Finite closure test

The companion checker verifies:

1. **Typing:** every node and arrow remains in the declared Carrier-like
   grammar.
2. **Referential closure:** all arrow endpoints exist.
3. **Conservativity:** the physical subobject is unchanged by extension.
4. **No self-certification:** meta-level records cannot warrant physical
   claims.
5. **Recursive definability:** applying the same constructor at depth two is
   well typed.
6. **Coherent truncation:** deleting the newly added depth-two layer recovers
   the depth-one induced subgraph exactly.

The final two tests do **not** establish
\(X_\infty\simeq\mathsf U(X_\infty)\). They establish only that the second
rung is definable and truncates coherently. The truncation has not yet been
typed as a total morphism, so it is not a proved retraction. Convergence,
functoriality, universality, and observational indistinguishability remain
open.

## Falsifiers

This first construction fails if any of the following occurs:

- a required model node needs a type absent from the base grammar;
- a legal physical operation is rejected only because of administrative
  metadata;
- an illegal move becomes legal after renaming or gauge change;
- the extension changes the physical subobject;
- its conclusions depend on its own records as physical evidence;
- iteration requires an ad hoc new constructor;
- its prospective move classifications fail against later source-derived
  results.

## Interpretation

Passing the finite checker would show a small formal fact: the Q-1 research
programme admits a conservative, recursively typable self-description. It
would not show that nature, consciousness, or the full Marici Carrier has a
self-modeling fixed point. The scientifically interesting threshold is later:
the internal extension must predict a nontrivial admissible operation or
obstruction before the object-level calculation reveals it.

## Verification

`check_q1_self_modeling_operator.py` passes all six finite conditions and a
deliberate self-certification failure. Depth-two induced-subgraph truncation
is exact in the finite representation:

\[
\operatorname{Trunc}_2(\mathsf U_1^2(X))=\mathsf U_1(X).
\]

This equality is not a categorical retraction until the admissible morphisms
and the action of \(\mathsf U_1\) on them are independently defined.

Post-construction excitement: 8.2/10. The slight decrease is healthy: the
operator is now concrete and recursively typed, but the test also exposes the
large remaining gap between a split finite tower and an observational fixed
point.
