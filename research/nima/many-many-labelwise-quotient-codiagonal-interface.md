# Many-many labelwise quotient-to-codiagonal interface

Let

$$
\mathcal V_{\rm lab}
=\bigoplus_p\mathcal V_p,
\qquad
\mathcal N_{\rm lab}
=\bigoplus_p\operatorname{Rad}(U_p^*U_p).
$$

The labelled relative carrier is

$$
\overline{\mathcal V}_{\rm lab}
=\mathcal V_{\rm lab}/\mathcal N_{\rm lab}.
$$

The oriented codiagonal `C` descends to this quotient when

$$
\mathcal N_{\rm lab}\subseteq\ker C
$$

on the source-labelled return domain. Its induced map is

$$
\overline C:\overline{\mathcal V}_{\rm lab}	o\mathcal W_{\rm pair}.
$$

Only after `overline C` is defined may cross-prime blocks be assembled in the common wall carrier. This order preserves source faithfulness and keeps the antisymmetric reciprocal channel available for the ordered polarization.

Status: labelwise quotient and induced codiagonal typed; kernel inclusion for the full physical codiagonal remains open.
