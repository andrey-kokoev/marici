# Arithmetic-analytic incidence needs a convergent interface-preserving rewrite certificate

## Admission boundary

The arithmetic-to-analytic incidence, moving-seam transport, and positive Adams coefficient cannot enter the categorical RH operator packet merely as formulas. They must first be admitted as typed rewrite rules on the complete interaction-net invariant.

For each proposed rule
\[
\rho:L\longrightarrow R,
\]
the certificate must record:

1. the source and target schemas;
2. the active typed ports;
3. the external boundary interface;
4. a strict well-founded measure decrease;
5. every overlap with existing rules;
6. the unique reduct or coherent critical join;
7. preservation of the observer-theory version;
8. preservation of source energy and authorized gauge typing.

Only after this certificate may determinant, Green, Mellin, or Birman–Schwinger observers be applied as representations of the quotient.

## Why quotient-first matters

Let \(\mathcal N\) be the admitted interaction nets and \(\Rightarrow\) the generated rewrite relation. If termination and confluence hold, each closed admitted net has a unique normal-form class
\[
[N]_{\mathrm{nf}}.
\]
The domain invariant is this typed normal-form class with its retained boundary interface.

An observer
\[
\mathcal O:\mathcal N/{\sim}\to Y
\]
may have a kernel. Therefore equality of determinant, quadratic, optical, or analytic values does not imply equality of normal-form classes. Observer kernels must remain explicit throughout the RH reduction.

This guards the coherent diagonal: a sector representation can annihilate a boundary state that remains nontrivial in the quotient invariant.

## The three new rule families

### Arithmetic-to-analytic incidence

A prime-power incidence atom must rewrite into an analytic feature packet without collapsing primitive, square, connected, endpoint, or archimedean types. The rule must preserve the external arithmetic label and expose the seam port used by later comparison.

### Moving-seam transport

Continuation between varying fibers is a typed transport rule. Its source and target fibers differ, so the rule must carry the horizontal identification explicitly. Treating it as an endomorphism of a fixed fiber is only the trivial-bundle control.

### Positive Adams coefficient

The transport edge carries a positive coefficient \(a(\alpha)\) satisfying
\[
a(\beta\circ\alpha)=a(\beta)a(\alpha).
\]
This semigroup law must be a critical-pair coherence condition: composing two transport rewrites must agree with the single composite rewrite, including type and boundary interface.

## Termination measure

A candidate lexicographic measure is
\[
\mu(N)=
\big(
n_{\mathrm{unresolved}},
n_{\mathrm{seam\ defects}},
n_{\mathrm{untransported}},
n_{\mathrm{noncanonical\ weights}},
n_{\mathrm{internal\ redexes}}

Every oriented rule must strictly decrease \(\mu\).

Weight normalization cannot be allowed to cycle with seam transport. If a transport rule changes the coefficient presentation without reducing an earlier component, termination is not established.

The measure is a proposal until checked against every admitted rule. No analytic convenience justifies orienting an equation that fails strict decrease.

## Interface preservation

Let \(\partial N\) denote the typed external boundary packet. Every internal rewrite must satisfy
\[
\partial L=\partial R
\]
up to the explicitly admitted boundary isomorphism.

The packet includes:

- prime and grade labels;
- seam source and target fibers;
- endpoint and archimedean ports;
- reciprocal/Real typing;
- source-energy version;
- observer-theory version;
- authorized gauge class.

Dropping any of these may preserve a scalar sector observer while changing the categorical state.

## Interaction with the four margins

The quotient theorem and analytic margins solve different problems:

- termination and confluence ensure presentation independence;
- Yoneda separation ensures the full admitted observer family distinguishes finite quotient classes;
- \(\delta_P,\delta_A\) stabilize arithmetic and analytic source realization;
- \(\delta_{\mathrm{glue}}\) stabilizes disagreement correction;
- \(\delta_{\mathrm{diag}}\) stabilizes observation of coherent classes.

Finite Yoneda separation does not provide uniform completion margins. Conversely, positive analytic margins do not prove that two rewrite presentations define the same categorical state.

Both layers are required before the Birman–Schwinger pencil is source-authorized.

## Hostile

Suppose two moving-seam rules act on the same incidence atom:
\[
\rho_1:\text{incidence}\to\text{transport then weight},
\]
\[
\rho_2:\text{incidence}\to\text{weight then transport}.
\]
A determinant observer may assign the same scalar to both reducts because multiplication commutes in the target. But if the coefficient is fiber-typed, the two nets can expose different intermediate seam ports and interact differently with a later clutching rule.

Thus observer equality hides a nonconfluent critical pair. The repair is a typed join proving the Adams coefficient is natural with respect to moving-seam transport, not a scalar equality.
