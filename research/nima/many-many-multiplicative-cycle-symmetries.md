# Many-many multiplicative-cycle symmetries

The rectangle cycle is oriented. Reversing either shell reverses the cycle sign:

$$
Z_{q,p;r,s}=-Z_{p,q;r,s},
\qquad
Z_{p,q;s,r}=-Z_{p,q;r,s}.
$$

Exchanging the two shell directions preserves the rectangle with the corresponding relabelling:

$$
Z_{p,q;r,s}=Z_{r,s;p,q}
$$

up to the chosen ordering convention for the four vertices.

Reciprocal pair exchange acts termwise by the ordered-pair involution and preserves the generated span `Z_coll`. Hence `Z_coll` is an oriented, reciprocal-stable defect sector.
