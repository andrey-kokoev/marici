# q-C-O closes on finite and projective endpoint graphs

The q-C intertwiner holds on the common Schwartz core:

$$
\mathcal M_{\log}W_{\mathrm{or}}
=
G(t)R_t\mathcal M_{\log}.
$$

Endpoint observation uses meromorphic evaluations at

$$
t=\frac{i}{2}
$$

and

$$
t=-\frac{i}{2}.
$$

These evaluations are continuous on every fixed Paley--Wiener or Gaussian graph rung. The completed finite-window carrier retains the bulk gamma sector and both endpoint lines:

$$
\mathcal H_{m bulk}^{m even}
\oplus
\mathcal H_{m bulk}^{m odd}
\oplus
E_{+1/2}
\oplus
E_{-1/2}.
$$

Meromorphic continuation of the exact q-C identity, with endpoint residues retained, gives the q-C-O cube on each finite rung. Taking the projective system of these graph rungs gives a coordinatewise continuous q-C-O modification.

The endpoint evaluation norm on a Paley--Wiener window of radius L satisfies

$$
\left\|e_{i/2}^{(L)}\right\|
\asymp
e^{L/2}
$$

up to polynomial factors. Therefore

$$
\sup_L
\left\|e_{i/2}^{(L)}\right\|
=
\infty.
$$

The q-C-O modification has two valid topological forms:

1. bounded on every finite endpoint graph rung;
2. continuous in the projective graph topology.

It has no uniform realization on the bare spectral Hilbert space and no cutoff-independent endpoint operator norm.

The endpoint and gamma residues must remain coupled in the signed Green carrier. Orthogonal endpoint adjunction changes the completion problem and loses the required cancellation.
