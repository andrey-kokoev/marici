# The rung-five endpoint primitive closes a missing port scale, but does not yet authorize the five-port G4 codiagonal

## Attempted insertion

The compensator

\[
K_a=-\frac12|q-a|+\delta_a
\]

supplies a continuous, prime-loaded endpoint channel with the required late
shell order. It can be added to the retained wall graph without an undefined
distribution product.

However, the exact Evans promotion is not a sum of provisionally normalized
ports. It is

\[
r_U(z)=CR_{\rm sat}(z)
\]

for the final source-weighted codiagonal `C`, equivalently the complete joint
G4 adjoint.

## Why the exact test is not yet executable

The repository fixes the ordinary derivative/wall relative coefficient and the
native ordered Stokes/Wronskian coefficient. It does not yet fix their common
metrics and comparison maps inside one final G4 column. Two inputs remain
open:

1. completed polarized Stokes/Wronskian descent on the retained source graph;
2. contragredient Fourier--Poisson transport on every primitive, square,
   connected, wall, and response stratum.

The rung-five primitive supplies an additional eligible summand, but it does
not determine either missing comparison. In particular, its endpoint
coefficient cannot be inserted into `C` merely because its asymptotic order is
correct.

## Local nondivisibility warning

The endpoint term alone is

\[
\Phi(a)u_+(a;z).
\]

At a Xi zero, seam matching gives `u_-(0;z)=u_+(0;z)`; it does not force
`u_+(a;z)=0`. Therefore the new endpoint component is not separately divisible
by `tau`. Its contribution must cancel with the other four normalized ports
through the final codiagonal.

## Exact readiness condition

The divisibility test becomes authoritative only after source construction of
all component metrics `G_j` and maps `B_j`, giving

\[
B_\Sigma^\dagger y
=\sum_jB_j^*G_jy_j.
\]

Then one may test

\[
(B_\Sigma^\dagger\widehat u)^{(k)}(z_0)=0,
\qquad 0\le k<m,
\]

at a multiplicity-`m` Xi zero, or equivalently perform holomorphic division by
`tau` before zero specialization.

## Disposition

The compensator closes provenance, continuity, and dominant-scale tests for a
previously missing endpoint response. Exact Xi divisibility remains
nonevaluable because the final source-normalized five-port codiagonal is still
unconstructed. Assigning its coefficient now would fit the operator to the
known divisor.
