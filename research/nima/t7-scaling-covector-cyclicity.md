# One-Parameter Physical Transport Cannot Resolve All of T7

The source-normalized scaling connection contains two exactly identical,
uncoupled algebraic blocks:

\[
A_{23}=A_{45}=
\begin{pmatrix}0&1/\lambda\\0&-1/\lambda\end{pmatrix}.
\]

A scalar covector and all of its \(\lambda\)-derivatives therefore see only
one diagonal copy of these two isomorphic differential modules. Two
directions remain indistinguishable under scaling transport alone.

An exact truncated-jet calculation at \((\rho,\lambda)=(2,1/5)\), replicated
over two independent primes and a predeclared 42-covector census, finds
maximum observability rank five on \(T_7\).

Thus kinematic differentiation improves the fixed-fiber rank bound, but the
one-parameter orbit still cannot reconstruct the rank-seven algebraic sector.

This is not yet a bivariate no-go. Transport in the independent shape
direction may distinguish the duplicate blocks. The next decisive object is
the missing full bivariate \(T_7\) connection or an independently derived
shape-direction residue acting differently on the two copies.
