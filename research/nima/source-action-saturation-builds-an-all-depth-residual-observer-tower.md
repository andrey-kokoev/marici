# Source-action saturation builds an all-depth residual observer tower

## Result

The residual-gap hierarchy extends to EVERY finite depth. Moreover its source-action saturation gives a genuine inverse tower of finite-dimensional observer modules with explicit restriction maps and compatible evaluation from the filtered source.

This is stronger than a list of positive witness values: the observer modules carry nontrivial action of the source ideal, and evaluation respects that action. All finite reassociations commute through the actual ordered balanced records.

The analytical conclusion has a definite boundary. The tower is continuous in the inverse-limit/product topology, but its normalized scalar detectors need not even be bounded across depth on one actual summable source. An explicit source in BOTH the actual-letter and common-path domains proves this. A uniformly bounded or summed all-depth observation therefore requires additional receiver weights or source budgets.

Inputs are the existing ordered Fox convolution/filtration theorem, the cubic and four-seam residual witnesses, and the actual-letter completion and convex-height results. No new source, ideal, root-state product or perfect-duality assertion is introduced.

## 1. Every depth has an actual detector

Fix the globally ordered primes in pairs (p_i,q_i), beginning (2,3),(5,7),(11,13),(17,19). Put

`T_i=2 product_(j<i) p_j q_j`.

For r>=2 use mixed-mark diamond relations a_1,...,a_(r-1), followed by the forgotten diamond a_r, in their actual consecutive typed corners. Put v_r=a_1...a_r. It has 2r events, r-1 retained features, and 2*4^(r-1) distinct marked paths, all with coefficient magnitude one. It belongs to I^r. In its minimal 2r-event packet, I^(r+1)=0.

For r=1 take the single forgotten diamond. It supplies a vacuum base detector, not a new retained root state.

For each i<r select the two existing retained windows

`F_i^-=[log T_i,log(p_i T_i)]`,

`F_i^+=[log(p_i T_i),log(p_i q_i T_i)]`.

In the last diamond select its first forgotten seam. There are 2^(r-1) selected balanced sectors, each with coefficient +1, with r-1 ordered features and unit vacuum observations in the other positions.

Fix 1/2<gamma<y<beta and the same port o_0=(e_y,-2L_0 e_y), L_0=(xi'/xi)(1/2+y). Normalize each retained slot by its actual positive X_F and use signs minus/plus. The finite elementary tensor observer eta_r is admitted by the existing slotwise paired comparison. Write

`delta_i=mu_(F_i^+)-mu_(F_i^-)>0`.

Repeated scalar evaluation gives EXACTLY

`d_r:=<D_r(v_r),eta_r>
     =(w_seam/(sqrt(2)y))^(r-1) product_(i<r) delta_i >0`.

The empty product gives d_1=1. Every L_0 term cancels in its own difference. Positivity follows from the increasing function x tanh(yx) and successive positive theta windows, not from a fitted form.

At each finite r the observer norm is bounded by the product of the fixed slot-test bounds and the finite factors (1/X_(F_i^-)+1/X_(F_i^+)), with the original physical and presentation multipliers. No uniform-in-r bound is inferred from this finite statement.

## 2. Ordered derivatives and all finite coherence

The existing convolution identity implies

`D_k(I^r)=0 for k<r`,

`D_r(a_1...a_r)=D(a_1) tensor_bal ... tensor_bal D(a_r)`.

Indeed a distribution of fewer than r cuts leaves a whole relation factor under D_0=rho and vanishes. At exactly r cuts, only one cut per factor survives. On I^r/I^(r+1), D_r is a cycle and is source-equivariant through the terminal outer actions. Neither property is asserted for D_r on the whole I.

Joining balanced records concatenates touching buffer words and keeps every ordered seam and endpoint. Any binary tree evaluates to the same ordered seam list and concatenated buffer words. Every reassociation route consequently agrees on raw records, respects balancing, and agrees on the completed fixed-depth presentations by their established bounds. Prefix-degree signs in the differential do not depend on parentheses.

This proves all finite associahedral reassociation diagrams, not merely the pentagon. It does not assert a new nontrivial higher associator, A-infinity structure or simplicial degeneracy system.

For r-1 response features, finite projective tensor evaluation is bounded. If ||O||,||O_P||<=M and ||O-O_P||<=3T_N, its cutoff error is bounded by

`3(r-1)M^(r-2)T_N`, for r>=2.

The final vacuum seam is exact. This is a finite-depth transport estimate; its growth is not an infinite-depth summability theorem.

## 3. Nonzero adjacent roofs at every depth

For r>=2 retain the existing strict adjacent model

`K_r=[J_r/J_(r+1) -> J_(r-1)/J_(r+1)]`

in degrees -1,0, representing J_(r-1)/J_r. The shifted r-seam target has bottom cycles in degree -1. Use D_r on degree -1 and zero on degree zero.

Restrict to the finite 2r-event path source. The model becomes [I^r->I^(r-1)]. Its two ideals are one-sided projective over the finite hereditary path algebra. If its observation were source-equivariantly nullhomotopic, a homotopy H would give

`D_r(v_r)=H(v_r)=a_1 H(a_2...a_r)=0`,

because a_1 belongs to I and the target's outer action factors through rho. There is no lower target degree providing another homotopy term. Pairing with eta_r gives the contradiction d_r>0. Bounded projectivity of the FINITE model makes this a derived nonvanishing statement, not just failure of a particular chain homotopy.

The admitted finite strict restriction therefore proves nonvanishing of the completed adjacent roof. No completed ideal is declared projective.

There is also an explicit compatible refinement at any N>=r+1:

`[J_r/J_N -> J_(r-1)/J_N] -> K_r`.

Its kernel is [J_(r+1)/J_N --identity--> J_(r+1)/J_N], with the bounded identity contraction. Since D_r kills J_(r+1), the observations commute with these refinements. Adjacent nonvanishing is not a claim that iterated Yoneda products of all these classes are nonzero.

## 4. Scalar readouts alone do not form the required module tower

Let E_m=J_1/J_(m+1), and define the finite-support scalar readout

`ell_r(x)=<D_r(x),eta_r>`.

Here eta_r evaluates its fixed selected corner and vanishes elsewhere. This is a bounded functional on the record space even when D_r(x) is not a cycle. Since D_r kills J_(r+1), ell_r is well defined on E_r, and hence on E_m for m>=r.

The vector (ell_1,...,ell_m) is compatible with dropping the last coordinates. But these scalars, with trivial source action, would NOT be a source-module observer tower. On general x in I, the higher Fox terms in D_r(a x b) do not vanish. The original source action is triangular, not a separate terminal action on each scalar coordinate.

We correct this by source-action saturation, rather than incorrectly declaring those scalar coordinates equivariant.

## 5. Construct the observer modules and transition maps

Let S_alg be the original finite marked-path source algebra. Define

`Omega_m=span { x -> ell_r(a x b) : 1<=r<=m, a,b in S_alg }`.

Vertex identities supply the unmultiplied ell_r. The formula is linear in a,b, so path-basis contexts suffice. Every such functional kills J_(m+1), since it is an ideal and ell_r kills J_(r+1).

Omega_m is FINITE dimensional. A context contributing to ell_r must begin/end in its selected outer corner and fit into a path of length 2r. There are only finitely many such typed paths in that interval. Contexts outside it vanish. Consequently each generator is supported on finitely many subcorners. This uses source local finiteness and preserves every retained background label.

Give the dual the standard bimodule action

`(a phi b)(x)=phi(b x a)`.

Omega_m is invariant: applying another left/right context merely composes the two contexts, or gives zero. This finite-support action extends continuously from S_alg to the declared completed source via finite convex restriction.

Set `O_m=Omega_m^*`. The inclusions Omega_m subset Omega_(m+1), using pullback along E_(m+1)->E_m, give SURJECTIVE restriction maps

`pi_m:O_(m+1)->O_m`.

Surjectivity is finite-dimensional linear extension. No source-equivariant splitting of pi_m is asserted. Define

`Obs_m:E_m->O_m`, `Obs_m([x])(phi)=phi(x)`.

These maps are source-bimodule maps by the displayed dual action and satisfy

`pi_m Obs_(m+1)=Obs_m q_m`.

Scalar conjugation can be inserted throughout if using the first-slot Hermitian convention; equivalently take the conjugate functionals before forming the linear dual spaces. The same module statements then hold with that fixed convention.

Each Obs_m is onto: a proper image in its finite-dimensional target would have a nonzero annihilator phi in Omega_m vanishing on every source, contradicting the definition of Omega_m as a space of source functionals. It need not be injective. There is no general source reconstruction claim from these selected observers.

## 6. The tower sees every new witness and retains ideal action

For r>m, every saturated functional in Omega_m kills v_r, because a v_r b belongs to I^r. But ell_r(v_r)=d_r>0. Thus Obs_r(v_r) is a nonzero new observation whose image at stage r-1 is zero. The tower does not stabilize at a finite depth.

For r>=2 the ideal acts NONTRIVIALLY on O_r. Indeed source equivariance gives

`Obs_r(v_r)=a_1 Obs_r(a_2...a_r) !=0`.

This explicitly rules out interpreting the tower as merely a direct sum of modules each annihilated by I. Source-action saturation retains the extension-sensitive action that raw scalar readouts would lose.

The transition kernel is not asserted to equal just the dual of one associated graded layer: saturation can add contexts on previously unobserved subcorners as well. The algebraic tower, its compatibility with the ideal filtration, and its new-layer detection are the precise claims.

## 7. Finite-stage topology and the product inverse limit

Choose the finite path-context generators of Omega_m, keeping the old lists when increasing m. The maximum of the absolute evaluations on these generators is a norm on O_m. Restriction pi_m is contractive in these norms. Each generator is a continuous finite-corner functional, so Obs_m is continuous on the actual-letter source and on the common-path source wherever required.

Thus the inverse limit of O_m is a genuine complete product-topology observer space, with a continuous map from the realized source and a compatible map from its filtered inverse limit. Its construction does not imply that every observer-tower point is realized by a source, or that the selected observers separate all sources.

Height is charged once per complete source/record corner. The existing convex projections, factorization lifts and balancing estimates preserve each finite-stage construction. Independent per-factor height products retain their previously stated moment loss.

## 8. An actual summable source with unbounded normalized readouts

There is no automatic unweighted bounded-sequence realization of the scalar detectors ell_r on the full source domain.

Their witness corners c_r have distinct endpoints. Set

`x=sum_(r>=2) (r/d_r) v_r`.

Then ell_r(x)=r: its readout is supported on c_r, so all other summands vanish there. We now check that this is nevertheless a genuine source in BOTH relevant domains.

The positive boundary-Laplace estimates used in `translated-cubic-observers-have-sharp-gaussian-source-growth.md` are uniform for windows [log T,log(pT)] with p>=2. Their upper tail beyond 2T is already exponentially negligible. Hence

`mu(T,p)=log T+o(1)` uniformly for p>=2,

and delta_i=log p_i+o(1)->infinity. Therefore

`d_(r+1)/d_r=(w_seam/(sqrt(2)y))delta_r ->infinity`.

The unweighted path mass of v_r is 2*4^(r-1). At any fixed common-path radius, the ratio of successive bounds for (r/d_r)v_r tends to zero, even with its prescribed fixed polynomial event factor. This proves common-path summability.

For actual-letter factorial summability, the theta tail gives constants C,c>0 such that every event beginning at log T has gamma_e<=C exp(-c T^2), for all sufficiently large T, uniformly over its upper endpoint. The total forcing norm also bounds every gamma_e globally.

The last retained diamond starts at T_(r-1)>=2*6^(r-2). Every path in v_r has one retained edge in that diamond. Therefore, absorbing the other letters and path count into an exponential K^r,

`||v_r||_Gamma <= C K^r exp(-c'36^(r-2))`.

The factor (2r)!R^(2r), the scalar r, and even an exponential upper bound on 1/d_r are dominated by this decay. Thus Q_R(x)<infinity for EVERY R. Endpoint truncation places x in the closed source ideal, and the preceding common-path proof places it in the required intersection as well.

Accordingly a full forcing-resolved summed jet can exist while these separately normalized observations are unbounded across depth. This does not contradict continuity at every finite observer stage. It demonstrates the need for a specified weighted observer sequence norm or stronger source budget before claiming bounded or summable all-depth scalar evaluation.

## 9. Disposition and next analytical gate

Established: all-depth actual residual detectors; nonzero adjacent completed roofs; compatible refinements; all finite balanced reassociations; and an explicit source-equivariant observer tower obtained by finite source-action saturation.

Not established: a faithful reconstruction from this selected tower, a simplicial object with new face/degeneracy maps, a nontrivial higher-associator theory, or an unweighted bounded/summed observation at infinite depth. The latter has an explicit counterexample, not merely an unproved estimate.

The remaining analytical target is to choose and justify a useful depth/height-weighted observer topology with corresponding source priors, rather than silently promoting the product tower to a bounded one.
