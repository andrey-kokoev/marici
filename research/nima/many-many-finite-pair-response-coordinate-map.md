# Many-many finite pair-response coordinate map

For each matrix unit `e_lambda tensor conjugate(e_mu)`, define the bordered response packet

$$
\mathcal B_{\lambda\mu}^{[a,b]}
=\left(\rho_{\lambda\mu}(0),E_{\lambda\mu},W_{\lambda\mu},R_{\lambda\mu}\right).
$$

The finite pair map is

$$
B_{\rm rad}^{(N)}(e_\lambda\otimes\overline{e_\mu})
=\mathcal B_{\lambda\mu}^{[a,b]}.
$$

On the common rapid core, the coordinates obey

$$
\partial_t\rho_{\lambda\mu}
=e_{\lambda\mu}-\frac12w_{\lambda\mu},
$$

and

$$
 zR_{\lambda\mu}-\rho_{\lambda\mu}(0)
=E_{\lambda\mu}-\frac12W_{\lambda\mu}.
$$

The ordered matrix of packets retains the full polarization. Reciprocal exchange transposes the ordered labels and applies the prescribed sign to the odd Wronskian coordinate.

Status: finite pair-response coordinate map fixed and compatible with the Laplace/Stokes relation.
