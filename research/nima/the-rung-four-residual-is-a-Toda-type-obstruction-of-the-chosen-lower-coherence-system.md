# The rung-four residual is a Toda-type obstruction of the chosen lower coherence system

## Defining system

The vertices `S,A,C,G`, their pair maps, and their triangle homotopies form a
defining system for a higher composition. Once all lower composites have been
null-homotoped, the remaining tetrahedral boundary determines a Toda-type
obstruction class

\[
\langle S,A,C,G\rangle_{\mathfrak d}
\]

in the appropriate relative Green comparison group. The subscript
`mathfrak d` records the chosen lower homotopies; a higher bracket is not a
single value until its defining system is fixed.

The present scalar is an evaluation of that class on the retained base:

\[
\operatorname{ev}_{p,b_z}
\bigl(
\langle S,A,C,G\rangle_{\mathfrak d}
\bigr)
=
(1-p^{-2\operatorname{Re}z})E_p(b_z).
\]

## Two phases

Forward and backward coherence systems give two defining systems

\[
\mathfrak d_+,
\qquad
\mathfrak d_-.
\]

A homotopy prism `Delta^1 x Delta^3` compares them. Its four side homotopies
measure the change of the lower defining data. Consequently the difference of
the two Toda representatives is a coboundary determined by those sides:

\[
\tau_+-\tau_-=\delta H.
\]

This is the precise meaning of an oppositely oriented residual. It is a change
of representative induced by a change of defining system, not an independently
chosen cancelling number.

## Indeterminacy

The bracket is defined modulo lower-composition indeterminacy. Replacing a
triangle homotopy shifts the top representative by a boundary. Hence a new
higher equivalence can move the residual only within the indeterminacy already
allowed by source-derived lower cells.

Closure occurs exactly when

\[
0\in\langle S,A,C,G\rangle,
\]

meaning that one authorized defining system has a vanishing top
representative. Contractibility of the abstract cone does not prove this: an
arbitrary defining-system change would be fitted homotopy.

## Based cone

Retaining `b_z` forms the cone

\[
b_z\star(\Delta^1\times\Delta^3).
\]

The cone is the geometric carrier of a null-homotopy of the Toda
representative. To realize it in the source category, one needs based
extensions of all side homotopies and a top modification. Those extensions
are exactly the components of the proposed Green-system equivalence
`eta_G`.

## Finite interpretation

On the rank-two prime cell, the Toda representative is matrix-valued before
evaluation. A valid defining-system change must satisfy the complete four
matrix-unit naturality table and the valuation-transport intertwining law. The
scalar residual may be declared null only after that matrix representative is
a coboundary.

## Consequence

The persistent rung-four residue is evidence for a nontrivial higher
composition relative to the present lower coherences. The next-level simplex
law is a homotopy between defining systems. Its mathematical question is not
whether another level exists formally, but whether the source-authorized Toda
bracket contains zero.
