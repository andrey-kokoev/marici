# DPC: Carrier as provenance-bearing stabilization

## Status and scope

This is a bounded distinction-preserving construction for operative Carrier objects. It is not a claim that all mathematical or physical existence is literally a closure, nor that complete causal history must be stored.

The target distinction is:

> Two source histories with the same current readout can remain operationally different when an admitted future intervention distinguishes their residual capabilities.

## Frozen problem

Given:

- a source category \(\mathcal C\);
- an admitted stabilization constructor \(L\);
- a readout \(q\);
- an admitted future-intervention family \(\mathcal U\);
- an authority and coherence contract \(A\);

determine when a stabilized presentation may be compiled as one operative Carrier object without erasing distinctions relevant to \(\mathcal U\).

## Proposed construction

For a source presentation \(S\), define

\[
\operatorname{Carrier}_{L,\mathcal U}(S)
=
(S,L(S),\eta_S,K_S,B_S,A_S),
\]

where:

- \(\eta_S:S\to L(S)\) is the source-to-stabilization map;
- \(K_S\) records distinctions identified by stabilization and readout;
- \(B_S\) exposes the residual interface used by future interventions;
- \(A_S\) records source authority, domains, resources, and coherence cells.

The extensional value is

\[
q_S:L(S)\longrightarrow O(S).
\]

It is one projection of the Carrier packet, not the Carrier identity.

## Coalgebraic center

The mathematical center is not provenance. For a deterministic protocol with command alphabet \(U\), output type \(O\), and state space \(X\), the active Carrier core is a coalgebra

\[
\gamma:X\longrightarrow O\times X^U.
\]

It records present output together with successor behavior under every admitted command. In probabilistic or resource-sensitive sectors, the codomain is replaced by the corresponding typed effect or transition functor.

This stationary form requires a stabilized or unbounded continuation semantics. At bounded horizon \(h\), the correct resource-graded form is

\[
\gamma_h:X_h\longrightarrow O_h\times X_{h-1}^{U}.
\]

The horizon is consumed by action and cannot be omitted from the Carrier type.

Provenance belongs in the operative interface only when deleting it changes \(\gamma\), changes the interpretation or authority of a transition, or changes the admitted continuation alphabet. Genealogy with no such effect is quotiented away.

## Governing equivalence

Complete genealogy is not required. Define two Carrier packets to be equivalent relative to \(\mathcal U\) when every admitted future intervention gives equivalent typed traces and residual capabilities.

For \(u\in\mathcal U\), let \(T_u(C)\) be the complete typed trace produced by applying \(u\) to Carrier packet \(C\). Then

\[
C\simeq_{\mathcal U}C'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
T_u(C)\cong T_u(C')
\qquad
\forall u\in\mathcal U.
\]

## Admission gates

A Carrier compilation is admitted only if:

1. source generation: \(L(S)\) is constructed from \(S\) by the declared operations;
2. stabilization: the claimed fixed-point or completion law holds on the declared domain;
3. readout typing: \(q_S\) is separate from the stabilization map;
4. distinction ledger: every identified source pair relevant to \(\mathcal U\) is represented in \(K_S\);
5. boundary sufficiency: \(B_S\) supports every declared future intervention;
6. behavioral adequacy: compiled equivalence agrees with \(\simeq_{\mathcal U}\);
7. ordered composition: closure interchange is used only when an authorized comparison cell exists;
8. temporal typing: a later Carrier state arises from a declared source extension or transition, not from fictional iteration of an idempotent closure;
9. deletion survival: removing any claimed provenance or boundary field either preserves all declared behavior or produces a named lost capability.

## Exact finite witness

Let the state set be \(\{0,1,2,3\}\). Two idempotent closures stabilize \(0\) toward different representatives:

\[
L_A(0)=1,
\qquad
L_B(0)=2,
\]

while fixing \(1,2,3\). Their ordered composites differ, but a scalar readout identifies \(0,1,2\) and therefore reports the same value for both composites.

The first composite identifies source pair \((0,1)\); the second identifies \((0,2)\). An intervention that distinguishes representatives \(1\) and \(2\) separates the packets.

A value-only compiler fails. A provenance-bearing compiler passes by retaining the kernel pair or equivalent residual capability.

## Hostile suite

### Identity-closure trivialization

Every object is a fixed point of the identity functor. This representation is rejected unless it predicts a nontrivial stabilization, distinction ledger, boundary contract, or intervention law not already present in the source syntax.

### History maximalism

Two different histories with identical behavior under all admitted interventions must compile as equivalent. The DPC rejects unnecessary genealogy as well as destructive forgetting.

### Reset confusion

A reset maps several predecessor states to one known successor. Terminal equality does not prove predecessor observability. The complete intervention trace must retain the erasure event or classify the predecessor distinction as unavailable.

### Unauthorized interchange

Two closure orders produce equal readout bytes but different residual capabilities. Without an invertible authorized comparison cell, the compiler must keep the ordered trees distinct.

### Boundary laundering

An implementation may not preserve every lost distinction in an unbounded opaque boundary field. Every boundary component must name the future constructor that consumes it, or be removed as inert history.

### Adaptive provenance

A boundary or source reference chosen after observing a failure cannot retroactively authorize the earlier closure. Temporal authority is part of \(A_S\).

## Success condition

The DPC succeeds on a domain when the Carrier representation is sufficient and minimal relative to \(\mathcal U\):

- sufficient because it predicts every admitted future typed trace;
- minimal because deleting any retained distinction either changes no admitted behavior, in which case deletion is required, or produces a named capability failure.

This turns the closure ontology into a finite compiler question rather than a slogan.

## Strong critic

### Criticism 1: closure may be vacuous

Any object can be called closed under the identity operation. Without a nontrivial compression, completion, or invariance theorem, closure vocabulary explains nothing.

Response: require the construction to predict a deletion failure, a new invariant, or a constrained future-intervention law. Otherwise the closure representation is rejected as idle redescription.

### Criticism 2: provenance is not identity

Physical and computational systems routinely forget history while preserving every future behavior. Treating genealogy as identity would distinguish states that no admissible experiment can distinguish.

Response: use intervention-relative behavioral equivalence, not literal history equality. Provenance is retained only when it changes lawful continuation, authority, or interpretation.

### Criticism 3: idempotence excludes living dynamics

Adaptive, dissipative, learning, and driven systems may never reach an idempotent fixed point. Calling every operative object a closure risks excluding precisely the open processes Marici wants to describe.

Response: weaken the universal ontology from closure to stabilization packet. A packet may use an idempotent completion, an attractor, a coalgebraic behavior, a controlled invariant, or a finite checkpoint. The specific stabilization law remains typed.

This is the most serious criticism of the word closure.

### Criticism 4: noncommutativity is not causality

Gauge choices, projections, database migrations, and incompatible measurements can fail to commute without establishing physical causal order.

Response: noncommuting stabilization is only an order witness. Causality additionally needs source-local influence, resource transfer, no-signalling structure, or an independently typed temporal dependency. The stronger slogan equating noncommutativity with causality must be rejected.

### Criticism 5: time is not exhausted by restabilization

Reversible autonomous dynamics carries a time parameter even when no new closure or record is formed. Psychological passage, thermodynamic arrows, and relativistic proper time cannot be identified merely with source-extension checkpoints.

Response: the closure tower may explain record time or constructive time, not time universally. Its scope must remain operational.

### Criticism 6: boundary fields can hide everything

If every counterexample is answered by adding more boundary data, the programme becomes unfalsifiable.

Response: impose consumer typing and minimality. A boundary component without a named admitted consumer or demonstrated deletion failure is prohibited.

### Criticism 7: one categorical shape may not fit every sector

Reflective closure, probabilistic completion, causal stabilization, and authority admission have different mathematical laws. Forcing them into one closure operator can erase the distinctions the programme claims to preserve.

Response: retain a common packet interface while allowing different stabilization algebras. The shared claim concerns source, stabilization, boundary, and continuation—not one universal functor.

### Criticism 8: the source boundary remains unexplained

The construction begins from \(S\). It does not explain why those generators exist.

Response: primitiveness is explicitly relative to the declared rung. This is a disciplined stopping condition, not an ultimate ontology.

## Current verdict

The universal statement that everything is a closure is too strong and too easy to trivialize.

The bounded survivor is:

> Every operative Carrier object should be compiled around a minimal source-authorized coalgebra of outputs and successor capabilities. Stabilization, provenance, and boundary data remain in its interface exactly when they change declared future behavior or authority. A bare present value is admissible only when it is already a sufficient statistic for those interventions.

This survivor is both stronger operationally and weaker metaphysically than the original conjecture.
