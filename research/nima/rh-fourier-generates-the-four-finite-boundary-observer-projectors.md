# RH Fourier transport generates the four finite boundary-observer projectors

## Question

Are the four character observer ports merely a convenient dual basis, or are
they generated by the source transport itself?

## Canonical projector formula

On the finite boundary quotient, Fourier transport satisfies

\[
\mathcal F^4=1.
\]

For each character 

\[
\lambda\in\{1,-1,i,-i\},
\]

define

\[
P_\lambda
=
\frac14\sum_{k=0}^3\lambda^{-k}\mathcal F^k.
\]

These projectors use only the source-derived Fourier action. They satisfy

\[
P_\lambda^2=P_\lambda,
\qquad
\mathcal FP_\lambda=\lambda P_\lambda,
\qquad
P_\lambda P_\mu=0\quad(\lambda\ne\mu),
\]

and

\[
\sum_\lambda P_\lambda=1.
\]

Each projector has rank one on the finite five-cell boundary quotient.

## Why this removes basis fitting

The character decomposition is multiplicity-free. Therefore the image of
each projector is a canonical line, not a chosen coordinate inside a larger
isotypic block. The source vectors (1) and (K), together with their
Fourier images, fix the scale of the even and odd pairs. No fitted dual basis
is needed to separate the quotient.

The canonical preprojection observer is the representation-valued map

\[
w\longmapsto
(P_1w,P_{-1}w,P_iw,P_{-i}w).
\]

It is invertible because the projectors sum to the identity. Finite boundary
descent is therefore separated at the universal five-cell level.

## What remains undetermined

This does not yet construct the completed Riemann scalar. A scalar readout is
a subsequent source-authorized matrix coefficient on the four-port packet.
Its coefficients must come from the adelic, archimedean, and reciprocal
sewing laws. Choosing them to reproduce the completed section would be
circular.

Nor does finite multiplicity-free separation automatically survive
completion. Two failures remain possible:

1. new boundary directions or repeated character multiplicities appear in
   the completed carrier;
2. the finite spectral projectors fail to extend continuously in the
   constructor-derived topology.

If a character occurs with multiplicity greater than one, Fourier alone
selects only its isotypic component and a residual frame torsor survives
inside that block.

## Refined frontier

The next audit is no longer rank separation of the universal five-cell. It
is whether the arithmetic restricted-product completion preserves this
multiplicity-free character decomposition. The exact checks are:

- cutoff naturality of every (P_\lambda);
- continuity in the pro-Gram or rigged topology;
- absence, or source separation, of new same-character boundary copies;
- derivation of the final scalar matrix coefficient after these checks.

Only after that descent is complete can the mixed Green cell be tested for
the orientation needed by RH.
