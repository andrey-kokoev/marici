# Full interval energy is coercive on the Mellin-null space in the open right sector

## Coefficient carrier

Let

\[
H=\ell^2(\mathbb N)
\]

and define the Mellin observer

\[
\ell_s(c)=\sum_{n\ge1}c_n n^{-s}.
\]

For

\[
\sigma=\operatorname{Re}s>\frac12,
\]

this observer is bounded because `(n^(-s))` belongs to `ell^2`.

The full interval energy is

\[
\mathcal E(c)=2\sum_{n\ge1}|c_n|^2\log n.
\]

Its kernel is the vacuum line generated by `e_1`.

## Mellin-null coercivity

If `ell_s(c)=0`, then

\[
c_1=-\sum_{n\ge2}c_n n^{-s}.
\]

Cauchy--Schwarz gives

\[
|c_1|^2
\le
\left(\sum_{n\ge2}|c_n|^2\right)
\left(\sum_{n\ge2}n^{-2\sigma}\right).
\]

Put

\[
C_\sigma^2=\zeta(2\sigma)-1.
\]

Then

\[
\|c\|_2^2
\le
(1+C_\sigma^2)
\sum_{n\ge2}|c_n|^2
=
\zeta(2\sigma)
\sum_{n\ge2}|c_n|^2.
\]

Since `log n >= log 2` for `n>=2`,

\[
\mathcal E(c)
\ge
2\log2\sum_{n\ge2}|c_n|^2
\ge
\frac{2\log2}{\zeta(2\sigma)}\|c\|_2^2.
\]

Thus the interval energy is uniformly coercive on `ker ell_s` for each closed half-plane

\[
\operatorname{Re}s\ge\frac12+\varepsilon.
\]

## Completion consequence

No unit-norm Mellin-null sequence in this `ell^2` carrier can lose all interval energy at a fixed positive distance from the seam. The vacuum-escape hostile is excluded there.

The coercivity constant tends to zero as `sigma` decreases to `1/2`, because `zeta(2 sigma)` diverges. Hence this theorem does not supply seam-uniform coercivity or define the exterior observer at the seam.
