# Three-atom associator coherence is the next irreducible constructor gate

## Correction to the binary gate

For distinct primes \(p\neq q\), the forbidden datum is not every \(pq\) mixed term. It is an unauthorized primitive arithmetic contribution:
\[
\Lambda(pq)=0.
\]
An authorized analytic Green interaction between the \(p\)- and \(q\)-packets may be nonzero.

Therefore the binary checker must project the assembled packet into four separately typed channels:

1. authorized mixed analytic interaction;
2. primitive arithmetic flux;
3. seam cross-term;
4. scalar terminal shadow.

The primitive projection must vanish at \(pq\), while the mixed analytic and seam projections must equal their source-derived assembly formulas. Scalar agreement cannot substitute for any of these tests.

## Binary comparison record

For packets \(\mathcal M_p,\mathcal M_q\), define
\[
\mathsf{Asm}_{p,q}
=
\big(
\Gamma^{G}_{p,q},
\Sigma_{p,q},
\Pi^{\mathrm{prim}}_{pq},
\mathcal E_{p,q}
\big),
\]
where:

- \(\Gamma^G_{p,q}\) is the mixed Green block;
- \(\Sigma_{p,q}\) is the seam cross-term;
- \(\Pi^{\mathrm{prim}}_{pq}\) is primitive arithmetic flux;
- \(\mathcal E_{p,q}\) is the scalar Euler/Mellin shadow.

The admission conditions are
\[
\Gamma^G_{p,q}
=
\Gamma^{G,\mathrm{src}}_{p,q},
\qquad
\Sigma_{p,q}
=
\Sigma^{\mathrm{src}}_{p,q},
\qquad
\Pi^{\mathrm{prim}}_{pq}=0,
\]
together with the prescribed scalar readout.

This prevents a legitimate analytic interaction from being rejected merely because \(\Lambda(pq)=0\), while still detecting arithmetic leakage.

## Four binary hostiles

A complete two-atom checker must include:

### Legitimate mixed interaction

The Green cross-block is nonzero and source-authorized, primitive flux is zero, and the seam term is correct. This must pass.

### Primitive leakage

A feature is incorrectly typed as primitive \(pq\) incidence. Even if its scalar effect cancels, this must fail.

### Seam corruption

Primitive flux remains zero and scalar output is correct, but the cross-seam port or orientation is wrong. This must fail.

### Scalar concealment

Primitive leakage or seam corruption is paired with an opposite scalar contribution. The total Euler/Mellin readout is correct. This must still fail.

## Three-cell escalation

Passing every binary overlap does not establish path independence for three atoms. For distinct atoms \(p,q,r\), compare
\[
(\mathcal M_p\widehat\otimes\mathcal M_q)
\widehat\otimes\mathcal M_r
\]
with
\[
\mathcal M_p\widehat\otimes
(\mathcal M_q\widehat\otimes\mathcal M_r).
\]

The constructor must provide an associator cell
\[
\alpha_{p,q,r}:
(\mathcal M_p\widehat\otimes\mathcal M_q)
\widehat\otimes\mathcal M_r
\overset{\sim}{\longrightarrow}
\mathcal M_p\widehat\otimes
(\mathcal M_q\widehat\otimes\mathcal M_r)
\]
preserving the complete boundary interface.

The associator must identify accumulated:

- seam transport;
- determinant/Schur annotation;
- Adams coefficient;
- Green mixed blocks;
- grade and sheet labels;
- endpoint and archimedean incidence;
- cutoff and source-energy versions;
- orientation and relative phase.

Equality of terminal scalars proves none of these equalities.

## Three-atom hostile

Let every atomic packet be correct and every binary assembly record pass. Introduce a path-dependent phase \(\omega(p,q,r)\) only when the third atom is attached:
\[
((p\otimes q)\otimes r)
\mapsto
\omega(p,q,r)\,\mathcal N,
\]
while
\[
(p\otimes(q\otimes r))
\mapsto
\mathcal N.
\]
Suppose the scalar observer annihilates or forgets this phase. Then every one- and two-atom test passes, but the two assembly paths define different typed normal forms and can interact differently with a later Green evaluator.

The defect is the associator cocycle
\[
\omega(p,q,r)\neq1.
\]

## Path-coherence equations

For three atoms, test the associator itself. For four atoms, the associators must satisfy the pentagon. Thus three-atom coherence is the next irreducible gate, but the all-finite theorem requires the higher path equations generated by the common constructor domain.

If Adams coefficients contribute to the associator, their accumulated products must agree along both paths:
\[
a_{p,q}\,a_{pq,r}
=
a_{q,r}\,a_{p,qr},
\]
with all terms interpreted in their typed fibers rather than as bare scalars.

Likewise, seam transports must compose in the same target fiber before they are compared.

## Aspect antichain interface

The three-cell antichain is realized here as three noninterchangeable proof obligations:

1. arithmetic incidence typing;
2. analytic/action assembly;
3. completion/interface coherence.

No cell is derivable from the other two. In particular:

- primitive-flux correctness does not determine Green cross terms;
- binary Green correctness does not determine associator coherence;
- finite associator coherence does not establish completion continuity.

## Finite attack path

The common nine-operation domain can now be approached in stages:

### One atom

Construct the typed packet and all scalar shadows.

### Two atoms

Verify mixed Green, seam, primitive-flux, and scalar channels independently.

### Three atoms

Verify associator/path coherence with complete annotations.

### All finite atoms

Prove the generated rewrite/assembly category is terminating, coherently confluent, and closed under the nine authorized operations. Check pentagon and all induced critical overlaps.

### Completion

Prove uniform continuity in the projective exponential topology and equality between bounded-energy compatible finite assemblies and completed source realizations.

## Next executable checker

For a finite triple of distinct primes, compute both parenthesizations and compare a typed digest containing:
\[
(\text{seam},\text{determinant},\text{Adams},
\text{Green},\text{grade},\text{sheet},
\text{endpoint},\text{archimedean}).
\]
The checker must reject if any component differs, even when scalar Euler/Mellin outputs agree.

This is the first checker that can certify path coherence rather than only objectwise and pairwise correctness.
