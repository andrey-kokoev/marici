# Higher-coherence topology iteration 37: factorization/cosheaf topology separates prime residuals only with jointly faithful local probes

## Candidate topology

Treat each prime shell as a local observable region and assign it a cone package
`F_p`. Disjoint unions assemble by a completed tensor or direct-sum product,
while interval/shell inclusions supply cosheaf extension maps. Global arithmetic
coherence is then factorization homology of the labelled local system.

This topology matches the source's prime locality, cutoff excision, and
shell-concatenation laws.

## Local residuals

Each prime stalk carries

\[
r_p(z)
=
(1-p^{-2\operatorname{Re}z})E_p(b_{p,z}).
\]

If the global factorization object retains orthogonal prime idempotents `e_p`,
then projections

\[
\pi_p:\mathcal F_{\rm global}\to\mathcal F_p
\]

are jointly faithful. A global cycle identity would imply every local identity:

\[
r_{\rm global}=0
\Longrightarrow
r_p=0.
\]

In that case one noncollapsed prime stalk gives confinement.

## Why ordinary global cancellation is insufficient

Without jointly faithful prime projections, a global sum or determinant can be
neutral while local residuals remain nonzero. Reciprocal or archimedean factors
may compensate prime moduli in the aggregate. The global functional equation
therefore does not imply local energy equality.

This is the previously identified adelic-factorization obstruction in cosheaf
language.

## Excision and higher cones

Local higher fillers can be glued by excision when their restrictions agree on
shell overlaps. This can organize infinitely many primewise cone systems and
make cutoff compatibility automatic.

But excision propagates existing local nullhomotopies; it does not manufacture
one in a stalk. If `r_p` is nonzero in one local cone package, its image remains
nonzero under a faithful cosheaf inclusion.

A nonfaithful codiagonal may cancel it globally, but then prime confinement
cannot be read back.

## Archimedean stalk

The completed source also has an archimedean component coupled to all finite
places. A valid factorization system must retain it as a distinguished stalk or
boundary condition rather than distribute it arbitrarily among primes. Any
localization theorem must prove that this common component does not permit
cross-prime compensation in the positive metric.

## Potential unlock

A genuine advance would be a source theorem constructing:

\[
L_{\rm jet}^{\mathbb A}
\longrightarrow
\bigotimes_p' L_{p,z}\otimes L_{\infty,z}
\]

as an isometric factorization map with labelled prime projections jointly
faithful on the retained Xi ray. Then global reciprocal neutrality would
localize to each prime.

No current object supplies this metric factorization. The existing labelled
carriers preserve prime names but not the required local decomposition of the
global reciprocal energy.

## Verdict for topology 37

Factorization-algebra/cosheaf topology is the correct local-to-global language
for prime cones and cutoff excision. It would unlock confinement if the global
Xi energy admitted a jointly faithful isometric factorization into local prime
stalks. Constructing exactly that factorization is the missing arithmetic
input; topology alone permits global compensation.

The next nonredundant topology to test is an adelic restricted-product topology
with product formulas and local norms, asking whether the product formula can
supply the missing metric factorization rather than only scalar neutrality.
