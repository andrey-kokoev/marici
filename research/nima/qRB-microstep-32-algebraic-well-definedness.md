# qRB microstep 32: algebraic well-definedness

Let `E_count` be the finite span of the enumerated source generators. The limiting form is

$$
T_\infty(u,v)=W_S(u*v^*).
$$

If a finite presentation satisfies `u=0` in the source test space, then `u*v^*=0` as a source test function, hence

$$
T_\infty(u,v)=0.
$$

The same holds in the second variable. Therefore the finite-span formula does not depend on the chosen generator representation.

This is an algebraic statement using the source distribution/form identity. It does not require positivity, a Hilbert norm, or uniform regulator bounds.

Status: finite-span limit is well-defined as a sesquilinear relative observation. Topological completion remains a separate gate.
