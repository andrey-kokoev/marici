# Twisted valuation covariance is the exact prime-diagonality theorem

## Reduction

Prime orthogonality should not be proved by comparing scalar Euler coefficients. Because the Adams edge changes grade (1\to2), it follows from twisted spectral equivariance rather than ordinary Mellin commutation.

Let the primitive (p)-fiber have frequency (log p), and let the square (q)-fiber have frequency (2log q):

[
U_t^P P_p=e^{itlog p}P_p,
qquad
U_t^Q Q_q=e^{2itlog q}Q_q.
]

The required covariance is

[
U_t^QB=BU_{2t}^P,
]

up to the frozen convention for which side carries the doubled action. Then

[
Q_qBP_p=0
qquad(p
e q).
]

## Proof

Sandwiching twisted covariance between (Q_q) and (P_p) gives

[
e^{2itlog q}Q_qBP_p
=
e^{2itlog p}Q_qBP_p
]

for every (t). If (p
e q), the two frequencies are distinct, so the block vanishes.

Equivalently, Fourier--Bohr invariant-mean averaging of

[
tlongmapsto
(U_t^Q)^{-1}BU_{2t}^P
]

projects onto equal prime frequencies. This argument is operator-valued. It does not pass through a scalar trace or Euler port.

On the discrete valuation carrier the same theorem says that (B) preserves prime-support idempotents while changing grade (1\to2).

## Constructor factorization

For

[
B=L_QDL_P^{*},
]

twisted covariance follows from three separately typed intertwiners:

[
U_t^QL_Q=L_QV_t^Q,
]

[

[

Thus prime diagonality reduces to a finite commutative audit from primitive valuation action, through the primitive endpoint lift, adjacent-cell propagation, and square endpoint lift, to the square valuation action.

The adjacent window is prime-local, but its grade-changing covariance must still be proved from the scale-path constructor. The nontrivial source obligations are:

1. doubled-action covariance of the local scale path;
2. equivariance of both endpoint lifts;
3. compatibility of wall subtraction with prime spectral projections;
4. preservation of covariance under rigged closure.

## Authority fork

Fourier--Bohr spectral projection requires an authorized invariant mean on the declared completion. There are therefore two legitimate proofs.

### Fourier--Bohr route

Prove the invariant mean exists on the operator-valued orbit, preserves the mixed form domain, and commutes with cutoff completion. Spectral separation then gives exact prime diagonality.

### Valuation-carrier route

Supply prime-support idempotents (P_p,Q_p) directly from the valuation or Fock carrier and prove

[
Q_qBP_p=0
qquad(p
e q)
]

as a grade-changing idempotent intertwining law. This avoids importing invariant-mean authority.

Scalar Mellin agreement authorizes neither route.

## Completion gate

At completion one must show:

1. the Mellin actions are strongly continuous on the declared riggings;
2. endpoint lifts are closed intertwiners on a common invariant core;
3. wall subtraction commutes with the prime decomposition;
4. twisted covariance survives closure;
5. finite spectral projections converge to the same closed operator, not merely the same scalar shadow.

If these hold, the coherent cross-prime hostile is eliminated exactly, and uniform contraction of each diagonal cell yields seam-bounded mixed transport.

If they fail, the source does not authorize prime diagonality and a full two-index Schur, Hilbert--Schmidt, or rigged-form estimate remains mandatory.
