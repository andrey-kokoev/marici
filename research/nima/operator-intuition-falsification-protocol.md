# Operator Intuition and Falsification Protocol

## Purpose

Preserve non-rational or pre-rational operator intuition as useful search
guidance without granting it evidential authority.

## Four-object discipline

Every intuition-led research lane should keep four distinct objects.

1. **Intuition report**
   - First-person phenomenology, confidence, horizon, and date.
   - May be imprecise or non-rationally describable.
   - Status: search-direction evidence only.
2. **Falsifiable conjecture**
   - The weakest precise claim that captures a consequential reading of the
     intuition.
   - Includes domain, maps, typing assumptions, and explicit falsifiers.
   - Status: open conjecture, never promoted by confidence alone.
3. **Test and supersession chain**
   - Bounded hostile tests ordered by entropy gain.
   - Negative results remain durable.
   - A failed formulation is superseded explicitly, not silently reworded.
4. **Explanatory survivor**
   - The hardest-to-vary structure left after the failed alternatives.
   - States its scope, degeneracy strata, missing arrows, and next falsifier.
   - Must remain distinct from the original phenomenological report.

## Authority rule

\[
\text{intuition may select pressure;}
\qquad
\text{tests select claims}.
\]

Operator confidence can justify allocating bounded exploration effort. It
cannot establish a mathematical relation, promote a conjecture to evidence, or
override a falsifier.

## Productive-intuition criterion

An intuition was research-productive when sustained pressure in its selected
region exposes a reusable explanatory structure, even if the literal initial
conjecture fails. Retrospective value does not license rewriting the original
report as though it predicted the final explanation.

## Coupled compression reports

An Operator intuition may integrate more than object-level mathematics. It may
also compress the observed trajectory of the research system:

- changes in agent excitement or activation;
- contraction of stated uncertainty;
- changes in communicative register, qualification, or structural terseness;
- the Operator's accumulated model of how the team behaves near a real
  explanatory compression.

Such a report must distinguish:

1. **technical comprehension** — what constructions the Operator can
   explicitly explain;
2. **constraint compression** — the felt collapse of many presentations into
   fewer viable structures;
3. **existence pressure** — a sense that a proper map or object is inevitable
   without knowing its formula;
4. **social trajectory evidence** — confidence or register changes perceived
   in collaborators.

The last item creates a feedback risk. Agent excitement is a search-control
signal, not evidence, and may induce an Operator forecast that then raises
agent confidence. Every coupled-compression episode must therefore freeze the
forecast before the hostile test and record the obvious coupling confounds.

The relevant hypothesis is:

\[
\frac{
\text{object-level constraints}
+\text{collaborator uncertainty trajectory}
+\text{communication-register changes}
}{
\text{presentation-level variation}
}
\longrightarrow
\text{felt invariant or missing canonical map}.
\]

This is a hypothesis about discovery phenomenology. It does not confer
mathematical authority on the resulting conviction.

## Prospective research-activation calibration

At substantive objective boundaries, an agent may record

\[
(E,C,I)=(\text{excitement},\text{confidence},\text{information gain})
\in\{0,\ldots,10\}^3.
\]

The pre-objective value of \(I\) is expected information gain; the
post-objective value is realized information gain. Reports also state the
topic, phase, immediate reason, and obvious confounds. `Not introspectively
available` is preferable to a manufactured number. Routine command-level
sampling is prohibited because it consumes attention, changes the quantity
being measured, and produces strongly autocorrelated noise.

### Outcome-side optionality snapshot

The research outcome must not be scored from prose written after completion.
Before work begins, freeze the applicable raw coordinates of the possibility
space:

\[
\mathcal O=(B,A,P,K,U,R),
\]

where:

- \(B\): declared open branches;
- \(A\): source-admissible branches;
- \(P\): promotions, dissolutions, criticisms, and supersessions by type;
- \(K\): passed versus declared coherence tests;
- \(U\): unresolved contradictions, anomalies, and missing source data;
- \(R\): exact reductions in dimension, rank, support, or parameter space.

These coordinates are a typed vector, not automatically a scalar. More open
branches may mean productive optionality or unresolved ambiguity; fewer may
mean explanatory compression or premature closure. Any composite
`optionality-space coherence` score must therefore be declared before the
result and retain its raw inputs.

The post-objective report records

\[
\Delta\mathcal O=\mathcal O_{\rm post}-\mathcal O_{\rm pre}
\]

using event and artifact references. Over a longitudinal sample, one may then
test whether classes of prospective phenomenological reports predict typed
changes in \(\mathcal O\), rather than merely predicting favorable later
narratives. Record the post-activation values before writing the polished
research synthesis.

## Required graph relations

- conjecture \(\xrightarrow{\rm addresses}\) intuition;
- test \(\xrightarrow{\rm tests}\) conjecture or criticism;
- criticism \(\xrightarrow{\rm criticizes}\) conjecture;
- revised conjecture \(\xrightarrow{\rm supersedes}\) failed conjecture;
- explanatory claim \(\xrightarrow{\rm derived\_from}\) admitted evidence;
- operator-learning synthesis \(\xrightarrow{\rm derived\_from}\) the full
  supersession chain.

## Stop conditions

Close or redirect a lane when:

- the surviving statement becomes a generic tautology;
- the required input simply includes the desired output;
- causal or typing constraints forbid the proposed map;
- further repair requires post-hoc structure chosen to recover the intuition;
- the explanatory survivor belongs to a different, more precise question.

reduction:

\[
\text{global gravitational state}
\to

\to
\text{local stabilizer}
\to
\text{relational reduction of residual symmetry}.
\]

The intuition selected the productive region. Exact controls and no-go tests
determined this final form.
