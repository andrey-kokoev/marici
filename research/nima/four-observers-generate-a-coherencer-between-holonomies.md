# Four observers generate a coherencer between holonomies

## Question

What new constraint appears when a fourth observer is added to the
three-observer transport triangle?

## Claim boundary

Four observers generate a tetrahedral identity relating four face defects.
This is a coherence law between coherencers. It follows from one common ordered
edge system and cannot be replaced by independent validation of each face.

## Face defects

Let \(t_{ij}\) be invertible ordered transports with
\(t_{ji}=t_{ij}^{-1}\). For an oriented triangle define

\[
c_{ijk}=t_{ij}t_{jk}t_{ik}^{-1}.
\]

The face is coherent exactly when \(c_{ijk}=1\). A nonidentity value records
the failure of the two-step path \(i\to j\to k\) to equal the direct path
\(i\to k\).

## Tetrahedral identity

For four vertices \(0,1,2,3\), associativity of the common edge system forces

\[
c_{012}c_{023}
=
(t_{01}c_{123}t_{01}^{-1})c_{013}.
\]

Both sides compare the three-step path \(0\to1\to2\to3\) with the direct edge
\(0\to3\). The face \(c_{123}\) must be transported to the frame at vertex
\(0\) before comparison. Omitting this conjugation is a typing error in a
noncommutative transport system.

This identity is present even when individual faces are defective. It says
that face defects derived from one edge assignment cannot vary independently.

## Why this is the next coherencer

Three observers create a comparison between paths. Four observers create two
factorizations of a comparison between paths. The tetrahedral identity is the
cell comparing those factorizations.

The hierarchy is therefore:

1. observer frames;
2. relative edges;
3. triangular face defects;
4. tetrahedral coherence among face defects.

The fourth rung is not another face. It constrains the relationships among all
four faces.

## Hostile independent-face assignment

Assign group elements to the four faces independently. Each element may be
individually legal, yet the tetrahedral identity can fail, proving that no
single ordered edge system realizes them.

In \(S_3\), choose

\[
c_{012}=c_{023}=c_{013}=1
\]

and let \(c_{123}\) be a three-cycle. The left side is the identity, while the
right side is a conjugate three-cycle. The sign character sends both sides to
\(+1\), so scalar parity again falsely reports coherence.

## Categorical interpretation

The edge system is a one-cochain, the face residual is its nonabelian
curvature, and the tetrahedral equation is the first Bianchi-type compatibility
condition. It is generated by associativity and common source incidence, not
declared as an independent fitted cell.

A family of face coherencers is realizable only if it lies in the image of the
edge-to-face constructor and satisfies this higher relation. Pairwise
coherence data without the tetrahedral gate are incomplete.

## Cross-sector gate

- Multi-apparatus calibration requires tetrahedral compatibility before
  pairwise phase comparisons can be treated as one global frame system.
- Flavor transition maps across three acquisition stages and a final readout
  must satisfy the transported face relation, not merely pairwise matching.
- Boundary-line exchange, dagger, augmentation, and completion comparisons
  require a higher compatibility cell if they are to form one common
  constructor diagram.

## DPC

For four observers or four comparison stages:

1. derive ordered edges from source operations;
2. compute all oriented face defects;
3. transport defects to a common vertex before multiplying;
4. verify the tetrahedral identity in the full operator object;
5. reject independently fitted face cells;
6. reject scalar projections that are not faithful on possible residuals.

## Disposition

The fourth observer generates the first uber-coherencer: a nonabelian
compatibility law among triangle holonomies. A violation proves that the
claimed face relations do not arise from one common transport system.
