# Collective comparison excitation from an integer Gauss law

## Forward-realization trial

The previous soft dressing model did not protect its zero mode. This trial
changes the type of mechanism: the complete configuration is enforced by a
physical-state constraint rather than selected by a finite penalty. The prior
slot, dressing and symmetry checks are freshly rerun.

This is a conditional compact-rotor model. Compactness, its gauge law and its
charging Hamiltonian are NEW assumptions. Existing carrier relabellings do not
by themselves establish that comparison channels are physical rotors or that
the proposed equalities are Gauss laws. No electromagnetic interpretation of
the earlier endpoint-phase adapter is inferred.

## Integer charges and collective raising

Give each comparison slot an angle theta_s and integer momentum n_s on
L2(U(1)^N), N=1836. For edges of a declared comparison graph impose

    G_st = n_s-n_t,
    G_st |physical> = 0.

These commuting integer generators implement a compact gauge action. On a
connected graph, the physical charge basis is

    |k,k,...,k>,  k in Z.

The incidence matrix has rank N-1 over the rationals and its integer kernel is
Z*(1,...,1). Thus its primitive positive charge vector occupies ALL channels.
This is a lattice statement, not a choice of normalization for a wavefunction.

A single rotor raising operator exp(i theta_s) violates the constraints. The
collective operator

    W = product_s exp(i theta_s)

commutes with every G_st and increments k. The all-channel excitation is therefore
an allowed elementary collective transition; partial occupation is not physical
in this constrained model. Smooth changes of penalty weights do not remove its
allowed state because the Gauss law, not a soft energetic preference, defines
the physical Hilbert space.

Gauge invariance alone still allows W+W^dagger in the Hamiltonian. To keep k as
a stationary charge sector one also needs a conserved global U(1) charge. This
is an additional law, not a consequence of G_st=0.

## Does the existing slot structure connect the channels?

Use edges generated by every transposition of the three nonreference labels,
independently in each of the three comparison roles. This deliberately generous
relabelling envelope gives6831 undirected edges but THIRTY connected components.
The integer kernel has rank30, not1. Its primitive component excitations occupy
27,54,108 or216 slots. Existing relabellings do not force one1836-slot excitation.
These facts describe the graph; using its edges as Gauss constraints is itself
a separate physical assumption.

For a conditional completion test the checker adds29 bridges joining component
representatives in a chain. This is the minimum number of additional pairwise
edges needed to connect30 components. Then the kernel becomes one primitive
collective charge. These particular bridges are label-dependent illustrative
choices, NOT source-derived interactions or symmetry-invariant physical links.
A source-derived completion may require more edges to respect its symmetry.

## Stored energy and the surviving ambiguity

For a diagonal charging Hamiltonian

    H = Delta_e n_e^2 + sum_s Delta_s n_s^2,

the constrained core sector has energy k^2*sum_s Delta_s. A separate elementary
rotor has its first excitation at Delta_e. Equal coefficients and |k|=1 give

    E_core/E_elementary = N = 12(12^2+3^2).

No execution-time resource is being identified with rest energy here. Both are
stored-energy eigenstates. But the equality of coefficients is still input.
Doubling the state-channel coefficients preserves every Gauss constraint and
the relabelling symmetries, and gives1944 instead of1836.

Even full slot-permutation symmetry permits

    gamma*(sum_s n_s)^2.

At gamma=Delta_e/N with otherwise unit gaps, the first collective energy becomes
3672. This explicit term need not be physically local: it falsifies gauge and
permutation invariance ALONE as a sufficient energy-selection law. The unequal
onsite coefficients already supply a local counterexample for the typed source.
A derived capacitance/energy form and locality assumptions would have to decide
which terms are admitted.

A different construction, a single-particle graph Laplacian with a uniform zero
mode, must not be confused with this rotor product Hilbert space. For H=Delta I+L,
its normalized constant state has energy Delta, not N*Delta. The norm squared N
of an unnormalized constant vector is not a mass ratio.

gauge constraint. It does not source that constraint, connect the thirty original
components, fix the energy coefficients, or identify a proton/electron sector.
There is no spacetime, statistics or particle stability derivation.
