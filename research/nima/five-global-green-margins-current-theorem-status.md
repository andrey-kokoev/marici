## Exact obstruction to a shortcut

Let the Euler-normalized prime basis satisfy

\[
S_\theta e_p=\eta_p e_p,
\qquad \eta_p\to0.
\]

For every bounded analytic postprocessor \\(A\\),

\[
\|AS_\theta e_p\|
\le \|A\|\,|\eta_p|
\longrightarrow0.
\]

Therefore

\[
\inf_{\|x\|_E=1}\|AS_\theta x\|=0.
\]

The graph intersection of the Euler metric with the weaker theta pullback metric is equivalent to the Euler topology, so it does not remove this singular sequence. Passing instead to the theta pullback completion changes the completed object and requires new constructor authority. Thus no topology may be selected merely to make a margin positive.
