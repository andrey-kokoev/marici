# Jointly conservative readout families

## Exact scattering theorem

On the one-loop QED D12 \(\Phi_1\) coefficient plane with coordinates

\[
v=\left(\frac{g_{4,1}}{g_2},\frac{g_{4,2}}{g_2}\right),
\]

the transverse Bell specialization and the infinitesimal-transfer filtration
define the readouts

\[
R_{\rm Bell}(v)=g_{4,1}+\frac32g_{4,2},
\qquad
R_T(v)=g_{4,1}+g_{4,2}.
\]

Their matrix is

\[
R=\begin{pmatrix}1&3/2\\1&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \det R=-\frac12.
\]

Hence neither one-dimensional readout is injective, but their product is.
They reconstruct

\[
\frac{g_{4,1}}{g_2}=\frac{41}{4620},
\qquad
\frac{g_{4,2}}{g_2}=\frac5{924}.
\]

This is an exact finite-dimensional theorem for the declared QED coefficient
plane. It does not assert that the readouts are complete on the full amplitude
or at all EFT orders.

## Cross-sector conjecture

The evidence suggests replacing the single-lens picture by:

\[
\boxed{
\text{filtered Carrier calculus}
+\text{sector coefficient objects}
+\text{jointly conservative families of supported readout functors}.
}
\]

A family is *jointly conservative* on a declared coefficient subcategory when
a morphism or coefficient class annihilated by every readout is already zero
there. Individual readouts may be lossy quotients.

The cosmology falsifier is not to demand one universal projection. It is to
construct independently source-derived residue, infinity-Gysin, nearby-cycle,
and physical-pairing maps, then test whether their product is conservative on
the frozen marked-relative coefficient object. Fitted splittings, rank
coincidences, and untyped associated-grade identifications do not count.

## Interpretation

The verified scattering circuit is therefore not

\[
\text{object}\to\text{one lens}\to\text{number},
\]

but a filtered object observed through complementary partial views whose
compatibility reconstructs otherwise hidden coefficients. This remains a
sector theorem plus a cross-sector conjecture—not a proof of a universal
Marici object.
