$$
R_{\beta\alpha}(\mathcal Z_{\rm coll,\alpha})
\subseteq\mathcal Z_{\rm coll,\beta},

$$
\sigma(\mathcal Z_{\rm coll})=\mathcal Z_{\rm coll},
$$

$$
T_\theta(\mathcal Z_{\rm coll})

$$
C_{\rm lab,\beta}R_{\beta\alpha}

$$
