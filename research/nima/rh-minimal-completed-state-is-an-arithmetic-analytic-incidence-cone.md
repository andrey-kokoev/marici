# An arithmetic–analytic incidence cone is a safe presentation, not yet the minimal germ

## Minimality correction

This packet correctly requires arithmetic, analytic, and residual incidence
data to remain available before quotienting. It overclaimed that the full
incidence cone is minimal. Aspect's full-fiber germ theorem requires first
declaring the complete RH target family and then quotienting every direction
invisible to that family.

The corrected minimal object is the target-relative joint-incidence germ
defined in `rh-marked-germ-is-the-target-relative-joint-incidence-quotient.md`.
The cone below is a safe prequotient presentation of that germ.

## The product is too large

The smooth-Gram no-go forces the arithmetic valuation carrier \(V\) to remain
separate from the analytic tail–seam carrier \(H\). Their unconstrained product
\(V\times H\) is not yet a physical state: it admits arithmetic and analytic
packets with unrelated boundary data.

Let \(B\) be the common boundary carrier and let

\[
I_V:V\longrightarrow B,
\qquad
I_H:H\longrightarrow B
\]

be source-derived incidence maps. The matched state is the pullback

\[
V\times_B H
=
\ker D,
\qquad
D(v,h)=I_Vv-I_Hh.
\]

The map \(D\) is the boundary mismatch observable. It is not an optional
comparison certificate.

## The operative extra port

The correct categorical object retains the mismatch target:

\[
V\oplus H
\xrightarrow{D}
B.
\]

Its degree-zero kernel contains coherently matched arithmetic–analytic states.
Its cokernel records boundary residues not generated by either carrier. A
source-derived contraction or coherence morphism on this two-term complex is
the operative extra port. Replacing it by the static assertion \(D=0\) discards the
mechanism that must remain meaningful under completion and perturbation.

This gives a precise form to the operative coherencer: it acts on mismatch
states and either transports them into declared boundary currents or proves
that the completed residual vanishes.

## Why the arithmetic carrier remains necessary after matching

Even if \(I_Hh=I_Vv\), the analytic boundary value need not determine the
arithmetic anomaly trace. A trace \(\ell:V\to\mathbb C\) descends through the
incidence map only when

\[
\ker I_V\subseteq\ker\ell.
\]

The smooth-Gram obstruction exhibits precisely the danger: arithmetically
distinct directions can become analytically invisible. The pullback therefore
does not authorize eliminating \(V\) unless this kernel condition is proved
for both primitive and square traces.

## Completion gates

The finite cone is insufficient. Its completed version requires:

1. continuous extensions of \(I_V\) and \(I_H\) in their different riggings;
2. a closed graph for \(D\);
3. strictness or closed range appropriate to the chosen exact category;
4. continuous primitive and square traces on \(V\);
5. a cutoff-natural contraction of the residual boundary complex;
6. reciprocal dagger compatibility of the entire cone.

Failure of any one gate prevents the scalar determinant character from being
a functor of the completed source state.

## Finite falsifiers

There are three minimal rejection witnesses:

- a pair \((v,h)\) with nonzero mismatch \(D(v,h)\);
- a nonzero \(v\in\ker I_V\) detected by an anomaly trace;
- a normalized sequence whose mismatch tends to zero while its graph norm or
  anomaly value does not.

The second witness proves that equality of analytic boundary data cannot
justify arithmetic quotienting. The third is its completion-at-infinity
version.

## DPC verdict

A safe well-typed RH presentation is not one sector and not a bare product of
two sectors. It is an arithmetic–analytic incidence cone with a retained
boundary-residual port. Its minimal marked germ is obtained only after
quotienting the complete fibers of every declared target operation. The
unknown source law acts on that germ and remains compatible with the anomaly
ports and reciprocal sewing.
