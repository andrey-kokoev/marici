# Correction: the collective prototype did not confirm seven conjectures

## Superseded inference

The earlier collective/discriminant scripts explored executable constructions. Their OK/FAIL flags did not independently validate the conjectures, establish native successor admission, or justify eliminating all but conjectures 1 and 10.

Several tests returned declared policy decisions or descriptive dictionaries rather than checking the claimed output contract. In particular, inserting A->A endpoint fields was an assumption, and missing dataclass fields demonstrated an absent adapter, not a no-go theorem for such an adapter. The chosen right triangle occurrence order (BA,AD,DB) is based at B, not A.

Likewise, an intertwining equation does not collapse distinct history records into one identity; writing a zero projector difference does not construct a typed higher-cell connector; and creating a tuple does not prove promotion admissibility. Matrix dimensions or member counts alone cannot establish those claims.

## Correct mathematical conclusions

- The existing independent occurrence constructor produces 36 ordered pairs from six records, grouped into 16 target families. Restricting to the selected left-to-right triangle product gives nine pairs under an explicit restriction. It is not selected by the count.
- Same-mode spectral projectors coincide after the specified symmetry transport. Their average is therefore the SAME projector, not a new mode.
- Different-mode projectors are orthogonal after transport. Their equally weighted average is NOT idempotent. Commutativity alone never justified the earlier conjecture-4 claim.
- The difference of unequal projectors is an ambient endomorphism after transport; it is not automatically a map from one eigenline into the other. The prior reference-cone construction does not generally require invertible connectors.
- A formal seam path sum cannot be traced against these projectors without a path-to-operator adapter. This blocks the proposed adapter-free formula, not all future representations.
- Symmetry covariance does not require a record to be literally fixed: ordered products can transport by exchanging parents. Thus the earlier preference for conjecture 10 based on symmetry was not established.

## Replacement evidence

`check_spectral_successor_many_many.py` now performs a genuine exact reconstruction audit rather than reporting successful object creation. The complete nine mode-pair projectors reconstruct the nine-dimensional occurrence-pair coefficient space. The three same-mode projectors select only rank three and omit rank six. Retaining those residual components restores the state; retaining histories restores provenance separately.

See `spectral-successor-combined-picture.md` and `results/spectral-successor-reconstruction.json` for the fresh verification and corrected weights.

The old collective/discriminant scripts remain exploratory prototypes, not confirmation/rejection authorities. Their policy branches cannot select a source law by construction. This correction does not deny that multiple successor hypotheses are constructible; it separates constructibility from proof of their claimed properties or architectural selection.
