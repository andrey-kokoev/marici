# Explanation is problem-relative and cannot authorize its own source

## Regress problem

The current programme requires source-authorized atoms, interventions, probes,
gauges, and completion rules. It is natural to ask what explains that source
packet.

Two bad answers are available:

1. let the proposed realization derive the source rules that authorize it;
2. require one final source theory that explains every source assumption.

The first is circular. The second makes ordinary explanatory progress
impossible until an ultimate foundation is obtained.

## Relative explanation packet

An explanatory claim is relative to a frozen problem situation

\[
\mathfrak P
=
(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{src}},
\mathcal U,
\mathcal R,
\mathcal G_{\mathrm{auth}},
\mathcal K),
\]

where:

- \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{src}}\) is the declared source category;
- \(\mathcal U\) is the admitted intervention family;
- \(\mathcal R\) is the probe family;
- \(\mathcal G_{\mathrm{auth}}\) is the authorized realization gauge;
- \(\mathcal K\) is the declared completion or regime.

A constructor can explain behaviour relative to \(\mathfrak P\) without
explaining why \(\mathfrak P\) is the final source description of nature.

Every assumption in \(\mathfrak P\) must nevertheless have explicit authority,
scope, and falsifiers.

## Self-authorization no-go

Suppose a realization \(E\) is derived from a source packet \(S\):

\[
S\xrightarrow{\pi}E.
\]

If the only authority for the load-bearing rules of \(S\) is a reverse map
constructed from \(E\),

\[
E\xrightarrow{\rho}S,
\]

then mutual consistency

\[
\rho\pi=I
\]

does not provide independent authority. It proves only that the two fitted
presentations encode each other.

A self-consistent compiler cycle may be useful operationally, but it cannot by
itself explain why the cycle describes the source.

## Deeper explanation

A deeper theory introduces a new source packet \(S_1\) and a typed bridge to
the previous packet \(S_0\):

\[
S_1
\xrightarrow{B_{10}}
S_0
\xrightarrow{\pi_0}
E_0
\xrightarrow{\Sigma_0}
Y.
\]

The bridge \(B_{10}\) must derive the earlier atoms, interventions, and regime
or explain their approximation and failure boundary.

The deeper theory earns progress when it:

- reduces previously independent assumptions;
- predicts new cross-source relations;
- preserves the successful intervention behaviour of \(S_0\);
- explains a former residue or regime boundary;
- creates new prospective falsifiers.

It need not make the explanatory tower terminate.

## No automatic upward authority

Success of \(E_0\) does not prove \(S_0\). It supports the composite

\[
S_0\to E_0\to Y
\]

against tested hostiles. Likewise, a deeper theory does not inherit authority
merely because its bridge reproduces \(S_0\). It must have independent
evidence or solve a problem that \(S_0\) leaves open.

This prevents both downward circularity and upward metaphysical inflation.

## Control interpretation

A controller may be exactly identified relative to:

- a chosen state boundary;
- an actuator alphabet;
- a sensor suite;
- a sampling rate;
- a disturbance class.

That is a legitimate explanation of closed-loop behaviour within the declared
plant model. It does not explain why the plant boundary or disturbance class
is fundamental.

A deeper control model can expose actuator dynamics, sensor latency, or
environment coupling. Its value lies in predicting where the earlier boundary
fails, not in retroactively making the earlier model nonexplanatory.

## Toric-code interpretation

The chain complex explains syndrome, local repair, and homological capability
relative to the periodic lattice and Pauli coefficient lens.

The quantum Hamiltonian explains why those chain operations are physically
realized as excitations and logical operators.

A hardware model would then explain how the Hamiltonian and measurements are
implemented and where actuator or decoder faults enter.

These are successive typed explanatory levels: chain geometry, quantum
coefficient realization, and hardware constructor.

No level acquires the authority of the next merely by anticipating its shadow.

## Falsifiers

- A realization is used as the sole authority for the source grammar that
  generated it.
- Mutual compilation is treated as independent evidence.
- A source assumption has no declared scope or falsifier.
- A deeper theory reproduces the earlier outputs but supplies no typed bridge
  between state and intervention meanings.
- An effective explanation is rejected solely because it is not fundamental.
- A local explanation is advertised as universal outside its declared regime.
- The explanatory tower is declared complete because no deeper source is
  currently available.

## Revised conjecture

Explanation is always relative to a declared problem situation. It consists of
a proof-carrying constructor that derives and stably predicts the admitted
intervention behaviour from that source packet. Deeper understanding replaces
or extends the source packet through a separately authorized bridge; it does
not arise from the realization authorizing its own premises.

Human understanding is therefore an open tower of typed explanatory
transitions, not a final self-certifying model.
