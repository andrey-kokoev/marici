\[
q_{\mathfrak g_{23}}=b-X_1,

\[

\[

sum activates that local class because motion changes both labelled normals.

substitution into the Cayley--Menger polynomial gives

\[

\[

+2X_1X_2X_3+X_2^3-X_2^2X_3+2X_3^3.

\[
\gcd(\mathcal Q,E_TC_{23,31})=1.
\]

and from the generic quartic divisor.  The transverse line is not a new carrier

Recovering physical significance for \(\mathcal Q\) would require genuinely
additional Betti-chain/readout data, not another refinement of this divisor
