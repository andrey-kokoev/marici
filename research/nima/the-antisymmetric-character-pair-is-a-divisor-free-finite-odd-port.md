# The antisymmetric character pair is a divisor-free finite odd port

## Opposite character twists

Fix a finite prime \(p\) and a parameter \(\eta\notin\mathbb Z_p\). On the spherical support define

\[
f_{\eta}(x)
=
\psi_p(\eta x)\mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p}(x),
\qquad
f_{-\eta}(x)
=
\psi_p(-\eta x)\mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p}(x).
\]

Each individual twist has a nontrivial relative Tate divisor.

Now form the antisymmetric combination

\[
f_{\eta}^{\mathrm{odd}}
=
\frac{f_\eta-f_{-\eta}}{2i}.
\]

For a unitary additive character, this is the finite analogue of a sine port.

## Exact vanishing of the scalar Tate integral

The multiplicative local Tate integral is invariant under \(x\mapsto-x\):

\[
Z_p(f,s)
=
\int_{\mathbb Q_p^\times}
f(x)|x|^s\,d^\times x.
\]

Since

\[
f_{-\eta}(x)=f_\eta(-x),
\]

Haar invariance gives

\[
Z_p(f_{-\eta},s)=Z_p(f_\eta,s).
\]

Therefore

\[
Z_p(f_\eta^{\mathrm{odd}},s)=0
\]

throughout the convergence chamber, and hence identically as the associated local Tate germ.

The antisymmetric port carries no scalar Euler factor and imports no scalar divisor.

## Fourier image

With the self-dual convention,

\[
\mathcal F_p
\left(
\psi_p(\eta x)\mathbf 1_{\mathbb Z_p}(x)
\right)
\]

is, up to the frozen Fourier sign convention, the indicator of the translated dual ball

\[
\eta+\mathbb Z_p.
\]

Hence the odd pair transforms to the oriented coset difference

\[
\mathcal F_p f_\eta^{\mathrm{odd}}
=
\frac{
\mathbf 1_{\eta+\mathbb Z_p}
-
\mathbf 1_{-\eta+\mathbb Z_p}
}{2i},
\]

again up to the conventionally fixed overall sign.

This is a genuine nonspherical reciprocal direction. It is not visible in valuation projection.

## Nondegeneracy condition

The odd port is nonzero exactly when the two characters differ on \(\mathbb Z_p\), equivalently when the translated dual balls are distinct:

\[
2\eta\notin\mathbb Z_p.
\]

For odd \(p\), every conductor-one choice \(\eta=p^{-1}u\) passes. At \(p=2\), conductor one collapses because the two cosets coincide; one must use a deeper conductor satisfying \(2\eta\notin\mathbb Z_2\).

This dyadic exception must remain explicit.

## Resolution of the ramified-divisor trap

An individual ramified twist has relative factor

\[
p^{-(m-1)s}\frac{p^{1-s}-1}{p-1}.
\]

The antisymmetric pair does not cancel this factor by division. It cancels the entire scalar Tate readout by a source involution before determinant formation.

Therefore the odd port can be adjoined as a scalar-invisible boundary coordinate without altering the distinguished unramified zeta section.

This is source-authorized because the pairing is generated by the involution

\[
\eta\longleftrightarrow-\eta.
\]

## What scalar invisibility does not prove

The new port is useful only if it survives an operator-valued mixed return. Scalar vanishing creates a serious dark-channel risk.

Let \(U_{\mathrm{odd}}\) inject the antisymmetric local vector into the additive Tate carrier and let \(V_{\mathrm{wj}}^*\) observe the reciprocal wall–jump packet. The required quantity is

\[
G_{\mathrm{wj},\mathrm{odd}}(s)
=
V_{\mathrm{wj}}^*
(I-S_{\mathrm{Tate}}(s))^{-1}
U_{\mathrm{odd}}.
\]

The port contributes to RH feedback only if this map is nonzero and its reciprocal adjoint is correctly typed.

Its scalar Tate integral being zero neither forces nor forbids this mixed operator.

## Global assembly

A global auxiliary vector may replace one finite spherical factor by \(f_\eta^{\mathrm{odd}}\) while retaining spherical factors elsewhere.

Its distinguished scalar Tate section is zero, so it does not define a second \(L\)-function competing with zeta. Instead it is a boundary test direction in the global Schwartz–Bruhat carrier.

Multiple odd primes may be included as labelled auxiliary ports. Their assembly must preserve prime support and avoid cancellation after global summation.

## Verdict

Opposite finite character twists have equal scalar Tate integrals. Their antisymmetric combination therefore gives a canonical finite odd Weyl port with zero scalar shadow and no imported divisor.

This reopens the ramified route in a controlled form. The next irreducible calculation is the operator-valued mixed return between this odd port and the spherical theta wall–jump carrier.
