# Cosmology Requires Sequential, Not Parallel, Readouts

The established maps form two nested exact sequences:

\[
0\to M_9\to M_{12}\xrightarrow{\operatorname{Res}_W}W_3\to0,
\]

\[
0\to T_7\to M_9\xrightarrow{R_\infty}V_{\rm ell}(-1)\to0.
\]

Therefore residue and infinity-Gysin are not naturally parallel maps on one
domain. Extending \(R_\infty\) to \(M_{12}\) requires a splitting of the first
sequence and is forbidden by the established typing.

The canonical readout census is instead

\[
12\xrightarrow{\operatorname{Res}_W}3,
\qquad \ker=9,
\]

followed by

\[
9\xrightarrow{R_\infty}2,
\qquad \ker=7.
\]

Thus the two established readouts peel off five dimensions without choosing
a splitting. The surviving falsifier lives entirely on \(T_7\): independently
source-derived nearby-cycle and physical-pairing maps must have jointly
trivial kernel there.

This refines the cross-sector conjecture. Complementary information need not
arrive as parallel projections; it may be a typed decision tree whose later
readouts are defined only on kernels left by earlier ones.
