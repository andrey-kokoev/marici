# Reciprocal parity needs state, observable, and parameter transport

## Correction

The statement that simultaneous sign reversal of \(\Lambda\) and \(S\)
leaves \([\Lambda,S]\) invariant is correct as a matrix identity. It does not
by itself determine the parity of the physical current under every RH
involution.

There are two different involutions in centered coordinates:

\[
J(s)=1-s,
\qquad
I(s)=1-\overline{s}.
\]

The first is holomorphic reciprocity. The second is the anti-holomorphic
reflection whose fixed set is the critical seam. They differ by complex
conjugation and act differently on the vertical parameter.

## Required typed datum

A parity claim for a current requires the tuple

\[
(U,\tau,\varepsilon_t),
\]

where:

- \(U\) transports states and is declared linear or anti-linear;
- \(\tau\) transports observables between the two sector frames;
- \(\varepsilon_t\) records whether the differentiated parameter preserves
  or reverses orientation.

For

\[
J_S(v)=-i\langle v,Sv\rangle,
\]

anti-linearity matters because it conjugates \(i\). A bare relation such as
\(S\mapsto-S\) is therefore insufficient to infer the sign of \(J_S\) or
its derivative.

## Minimal warning

Let \(R\) exchange two labels and let \(C\) be complex conjugation. Each
operation separately reverses the scalar order current in the minimal model,
while their anti-linear composition \(RC\) preserves it. The corresponding
real quadratic virial can remain invariant. Thus the Klein-four element must be
named explicitly; the word reciprocal does not determine the sign.

Thus these are distinct questions:

1. Is the operator matrix invariant?
2. Is the scalar expectation invariant?
3. Is its derivative invariant after parameter orientation is included?
4. Is the target observable compared in the same frame or through \(\tau\)?

## Correct parity audit

For every endpoint, gamma, primitive, square, seam, archimedean, order, and
scale channel, the theta/Tate audit must state:

- source and target sector;
- linear or anti-linear state transport;
- observable comparison map;
- parameter-orientation sign;
- induced scalar-current sign;
- induced boundary-current sign.

Only then can one ask whether reciprocal sheets impose equal or opposite
crossing laws.

## Revised status

The scale-order commutator is even under the linear simultaneous substitution

\[
(\Lambda,S)\mapsto(-\Lambda,-S).
\]

Its parity under the anti-holomorphic seam involution is not established by
that substitution. Any confinement claim must use the fully typed transport,
not the unqualified word reciprocal.
