# Haar energy ideal coherence would add a zero-simplicity theorem

## Question

Can the desired metric-preserving coherence cell be constructed by promoting the holomorphic Xi-divisibility witness to membership of the Hermitian energy defect in the ideal generated by tau and its conjugate?

SCC obligation: route/coherencer compatibility at the local metric cell. This tests a specific proposed filler type; it does not assume that every admissible higher-type representation must use this ideal.

## Claim boundary

Let z_0 lie on the critical seam, put w=z-z_0, and suppose tau has order m at z_0. Assume that the two route energies are defined in a common neighborhood and have a real-analytic positive noncollapsed baseline E, with E(0)>0. If their difference modulo the Xi/conjugate-Xi ideal is the relative-Haar baseline

\[
\delta_p\equiv(1-e^{-L(w+\bar w)})E(w,\bar w),\qquad L=\log p>0,
\]

then at a multiple zero m>=2,

\[
\delta_p\notin(\tau,\overline\tau).
\]

Nevertheless delta_p vanishes at the reduced zero point. Therefore reduced-fiber energy conservation and nonreduced Xi-ideal membership are different requirements. A global noncollapsed filler of the latter kind would additionally establish simplicity of every seam zero to which these assumptions apply. No existence of a multiple Xi zero is asserted.

If the histories or energies do not extend to a common smooth neighborhood, the proposed real-analytic ideal formulation first encounters a domain problem; the theorem does not manufacture that extension.

## Why the actual two-history expansion has this baseline

Under the source identity u_-=u_++tau e and a common Hermitian energy form,

\[
E(u_-)-E(u_+)=\tau A+\overline\tau B+|\tau|^2C
\]

for the corresponding mixed pairings. Hence

\[
E(u_+)-e^{-L(w+\bar w)}E(u_-)
\equiv(1-e^{-L(w+\bar w)})E(u_+)
\pmod{(\tau,\overline\tau)}.
\]

This uses the complete mixed terms rather than replacing an energy difference by the square of the holomorphic mismatch.

## First-jet proof

Write w=x+iy. Since E(0)>0,

\[
\delta_p(w,\bar w)
=2L E(0)x+O(|w|^2)
\]

when m>=2. In particular its derivative in the real-normal direction is nonzero. Every element tau A+conjugate(tau) B with coefficients regular at the zero has vanishing first jet when tau has order at least two. The proposed identity is impossible.

Only C^1 coefficients and sufficient differentiability of the defect are needed for this first-jet contradiction. Allowing singular coefficients would change the filler domain and does not solve the regular lifting problem.

## Exact nonreduced obstruction

Complexify conjugation by an independent variable eta. Since the leading coefficients of tau and conjugate(tau) are units, the local quotient is

\[
\mathcal A_m=\mathbb C\{w,\eta\}/(w^m,\eta^m).
\]

The baseline factors as

\[
(1-e^{-L(w+\eta)})E(w,\eta)
=(w+\eta)V(w,\eta),\qquad V(0)=LE(0)\ne0.
\]

Thus its class is a unit times w+eta. It has exact nilpotency index 2m-1:

\[
(w+\eta)^{2m-1}=0,
\]

whereas

\[
(w+\eta)^{2m-2}
=\binom{2m-2}{m-1}w^{m-1}\eta^{m-1}\ne0.
\]

For m=1 the class is already zero. For m>=2 it is a nonzero nilpotent class which vanishes on the reduced point. Higher jets expose this distinction; they do not make the class disappear.

## Smallest hostile

Take the model section tau(w)=w^2, whose only zero lies on the seam, and positive baseline E=1+|w|^2. Its Haar baseline vanishes at w=0 but has first jet L(w+conjugate(w)), so it does not belong to (w^2,conjugate(w)^2).

This is also realized by positive finite-dimensional histories u_+=(1,w,0) and u_-=(1,w,w^2). Their difference is tau times a fixed vector. Their exact energy difference includes an additional -exp(-2Lx)|w|^4 term, which belongs to the divisor ideal and cannot remove the obstruction.

The hostile refutes a general formal equivalence between seam confinement and this ideal-membership condition. It is not an assertion about the multiplicity of an actual Xi zero.

The baseline alone cannot have a positive-square factorization `(1-exp(-2Lx))E=|tau|^2 Q` with regular Q: its right side has zero first jet even for m=1, whereas the baseline has a nonzero normal first jet. This is not a no-go for the full defect at a simple zero, where mixed terms proportional to tau and conjugate(tau) can cancel the baseline's first jet.

## Disposition

The strongest tested conjecture was that an all-jet Xi/conjugate-Xi filler is just another presentation of the reduced metric-coherence law. Rivals were reduced-fiber vanishing without such a filler, or a filler that silently enforces simplicity. The exact double-root hostile rejects the conjecture, and the general first-jet theorem identifies the extra simplicity condition.

The RH-bearing target remains reduced-fiber energy conservation on every nonzero realized Xi state. An argument using the nonreduced ideal must explicitly prove simplicity or justify a different multiplicity-aware construction. This audit does not derive the required energy conservation and does not prove RH.
