# The retained graph lifts Adams reindexing to completed constructor coherence

## Source monoid action

On the labelled source, Adams multiplication is

\[
S_re_{p,k}=e_{p,rk}.
\]

It satisfies

\[
S_1=I,
\qquad
S_sS_r=S_{sr},
\]

and is continuous on the projective exponential completion.

## Lift to the retained graph

Let

\[
\mathcal Jx=(x,Ax,Cx)
\]

be the selected completed constructor, including the low-grade and connected
summands.  Define the lifted Adams map on its range by

\[
\widetilde S_r\mathcal Jx
=\mathcal JS_rx.
\]

This is well-defined because \(\mathcal J\) is injective.  It is not a fitted
map between analytic outputs: its first coordinate is the source operation
\(S_r\), while its other coordinates are the already constructed images of
the same reindexed source vector.

The graph topology is the initial topology for the component maps.  Therefore
\(\mathcal J\) is a topological isomorphism from the source graph domain onto
its closed range, and \(\widetilde S_r\) is continuous exactly when \(S_r\) is
continuous in the corresponding source graph seminorms.  Labelwise analytic
transport and the projective-rung estimate provide this continuity.

## Strict completed composition

For every source vector,

\[
\widetilde S_s\widetilde S_r\mathcal Jx
=\mathcal JS_sS_rx
=\mathcal JS_{sr}x
=\widetilde S_{sr}\mathcal Jx.
\]

Hence

\[
\widetilde S_s\widetilde S_r=\widetilde S_{sr},
\qquad
\widetilde S_1=I
\]

on the completed retained graph.  Associativity and both unitors are strict;
the corresponding pentagon and triangle diagrams commute without an extra
coherence scalar.

## Analytic naturality

For the weighted cut/history incidence,

\[
\mathcal IS_r=M_{\rho_r}U_r\mathcal I,
\qquad
\rho_r(p,k)=\frac1r p^{-(r-1)k/2}.
\]

The transport and coefficient cocycles imply

\[
M_{\rho_s}U_sM_{\rho_r}U_r
=M_{\rho_{sr}}U_{sr}.
\]

Thus both analytic paths are the image of the same source composite.  The
window, cut-atom, causal-history, and Wronskian factors require no independent
associator.

Endpoint traces remain labelwise until evaluation.  Their naturality square is

\[
\operatorname{Tr}_{p,rk}U_{r;p,k}
=\operatorname{Tr}_{p,rk}\circ U_{r;p,k},
\]

with codomain in the reindexed endpoint fibre.  No claim equates the numerical
trace at \(k\log p\) with that at \(rk\log p\).

## Reciprocal and Fourier compatibility

Reciprocal reflection preserves \((p,k)\), so

\[
\mathcal RS_r=S_r\mathcal R.
\]

The moving-seam Fourier transport satisfies

\[
F_{rkL}U_{r;p,k}=U_{r;p,k}F_{kL}.
\]

Consequently the lifted Adams maps commute with reciprocal sewing and with the
source-defined Fourier transport on every labelled fibre.  This is a
non-temporal transport statement.

## Dagger compatibility

In the unweighted labelled coefficient pairing, define grade division

\[
D_re_{p,m}
=
\begin{cases}
e_{p,m/r},&r\mid m,\\
0,&r\nmid m.
\end{cases}
\]

Then

\[
S_r^*=D_r,
\qquad
D_rS_r=I,
\qquad
S_rD_r=P_{r\mid k}.
\]

Also

\[
(S_sS_r)^*=D_rD_s=D_{sr}.
\]

The same formula defines the continuous transpose between the corresponding
Köthe dual rungs.  On the retained graph, transport the dagger through
\(\mathcal J\); no output-only pseudoinverse is introduced.

## Green polarization

The Adams lift need not be isometric for one fixed Green norm.  Its exact
polarized pullback is

\[
G_r(x,y)=G(\mathcal JS_rx,\mathcal JS_ry).
\]

Functoriality is automatic:

\[
G_{sr}(x,y)
=G_r(S_sx,S_sy)
\]

with the order interpreted by the declared composition convention.  Positivity
and radical compatibility follow from the retained source coordinate.  This is
the appropriate covariance statement; replacing it by equality with the
unshifted metric would erase the source weights.

## Cutoff compatibility

Prime cutoffs commute with every \(S_r\).  Grade cutoffs satisfy

\[
P_{\le K}S_r=S_rP_{\le\lfloor K/r\rfloor}.
\]

Therefore every finite constructor diagram is the restriction of the same
completed diagram.  No cutoff-dependent normalization is recomputed.

## Disposition

On the retained labelled architecture, finite and completed Adams constructor
coherence is generated by one strict source monoid action and inherited by the
closed graph:

- associator and unitors are strict;
- triangle and pentagon diagrams commute;
- the grade-six diamond is filled;
- reciprocal, Fourier, dagger, Green-pullback, and cutoff compatibilities are
  explicit;
- prime and grade labels remain typed.

This makes G2 a closure candidate for the retained constructor system.  It does
not identify the resulting spectral pencil with completed Xi zeros, prove the
five global margins, or authorize RH.
