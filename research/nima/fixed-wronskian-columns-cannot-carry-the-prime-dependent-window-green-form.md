# Fixed Wronskian columns cannot carry the prime-dependent window Green form

## Two finite structures

The theta Wronskian boundary columns are source-fixed:

\[
w_\theta
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\frac12\\[2pt]
\frac12
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
j_\theta
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\frac14\\[2pt]
-\frac14
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Their endpoint Gram is independent of the prime label.

The Stieltjes window endpoints at prime \(p\) are

\[
W_L,
\qquad
W_{2L},
\qquad
L=\log p,
\]

with Gram

\[
G_p^{\mathrm{win}}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\langle W_L,W_L\rangle_\nu&
\langle W_L,W_{2L}\rangle_\nu\\
\langle W_{2L},W_L\rangle_\nu&
\langle W_{2L},W_{2L}\rangle_\nu
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

This Gram varies with \(L\).

## Strict scale dependence

The polarized Stieltjes identity gives

\[
\frac d{dt}\|W_t\|_\nu^2
=
2\langle W_t,\partial_tW_t\rangle_\nu.
\]

For a strict Gaussian front, both \(W_t\) and \(\partial_tW_t\) have the
same sign and are nonzero on a set of positive \(\nu\)-measure. Therefore

\[
\frac d{dt}\|W_t\|_\nu^2>0
\qquad(t>0).
\]

Hence

\[
\|W_L\|_\nu^2
\]

is strictly prime-dependent through \(L=\log p\).

## Constant-incidence no-go

Suppose one attempted to define one prime-independent matrix \(C\) sending

\[
(W_L,W_{2L})
\longmapsto
(w_\theta,j_\theta)
\]

and to obtain Green functoriality from a fixed theta endpoint metric
\(G_\theta\):

\[
G_p^{\mathrm{win}}
=
C^*G_\theta C
\qquad
\text{for every }p.
\]

The right-hand side is independent of \(p\), while the first diagonal entry
on the left is strictly dependent on \(p\). This is impossible.

Therefore the universal Wronskian columns fix only the wall and orientation
ports. They do not by themselves realize the full prime-dependent window
Green form.

## Required label-dependent incidence

Any quadratic comparison must contain a prime-dependent transport

\[
C_p:
\operatorname{span}\{W_L,W_{2L}\}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal H_{\theta,p}^{\mathrm{graph}}
\]

such that

\[
G_p^{\mathrm{win}}
=
C_p^*G_{\theta,p}^{\mathrm{graph}}C_p
\]

or a source-authorized uniformly equivalent form.

The dependence of \(C_p\) must come from declared constructors:

- translation by \(L=\log p\);
- moving-center ray splitting;
- half-density label transport;
- theta label synthesis;
- completion curvature;
- tail convolution.

It cannot be inserted as a fitted Cholesky factor.

## Role of the universal columns

The fixed Wronskian columns impose boundary conditions on \(C_p\):

\[
C_p(e_{\mathrm{wall}})
=
w_\theta,
\]

and the ordered curvature calculation imposes

\[
C_p(e_{\mathrm{odd}})
=
j_\theta
=
\frac14S_{\mathrm{ord}}
\]

on the corresponding reduced ports.

The remaining bulk action of \(C_p\) is still label-dependent.

## Completion requirement

Finite-dimensional congruence always allows a fitted \(C_p\) when both
Grams are positive. Source authority requires much more:

1. \(C_p\) must be generated by the window-to-theta constructor chain;
2. its norm and inverse on reduced support must obey the prime exponent
   budget;
3. it must preserve reciprocal and wall typing;
4. it must intertwine cutoff restriction;
5. the family must extend to the global Laplace-rigged completion.

## Hostile

For every prime, choose the algebraic matrix

\[
C_p=G_\theta^{-1/2}(G_p^{\mathrm{win}})^{1/2}.
\]

This proves abstract Gram congruence perfectly but explains nothing: it is
constructed from the desired equality and need not preserve any source label,
orientation, or completion topology.
