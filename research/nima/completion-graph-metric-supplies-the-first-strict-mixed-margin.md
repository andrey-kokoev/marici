# Completion graph metric supplies the first strict mixed margin

The completed theta differential

$$
C=\partial_q^2-\frac14
$$

has the source-derived graph metric

$$
G_C=I+C^*C.
$$

For `D=partial_q` and spectral variable `lambda>=0`, the normalized derivative incidence has square symbol

$$
r_C(\lambda)
=\frac{\lambda}{1+(\lambda+1/4)^2}.
$$

Its maximum occurs at

$$
\lambda_*=\frac{\sqrt{17}}4,
$$

and

$$
\sup r_C
=\frac{\sqrt{17}-1}{8}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\|DG_C^{-1/2}\|
\le
\sqrt{\frac{\sqrt{17}-1}{8}}
\approx0.625<1.
$$

This supplies a genuine source-derived strict mixed margin for the first-derivative/Wronskian incidence. It improves the first-order history graph, whose normalized derivative norm approaches one at high frequency.

It does not by itself prove

$$
\left\|B_+^{\dagger/2}K_{\rm link}B_-^{\dagger/2}\right\|<1,
$$

because the complete linking/loading row also contains wall, reciprocal, prime, and grade components. The next finite calculation is the normalized `2x2` Gram matrix of the fixed even wall and odd Wronskian columns in the `G_C` graph norm. Its largest eigenvalue must remain below one uniformly in the prime label before connected grades are added.
