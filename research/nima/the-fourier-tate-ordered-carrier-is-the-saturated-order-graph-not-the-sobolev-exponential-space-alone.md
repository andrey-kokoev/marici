# The Fourier--Tate ordered carrier is the saturated order graph, not the Sobolev--exponential space alone

## Correction

The Sobolev--exponential refinement makes endpoint jets continuous, but it is
not by itself a Fourier-invariant carrier. Superexponential decay in the
physical variable need not be preserved as superexponential decay after
Fourier transform.

Fourier--Tate naturality therefore requires the already source-authorized
Fourier-invariant Gelfand--Shilov bulk core `G`, together with a boundary
extension generated by the order operator.

## Saturated order graph

Let `G` be the common metaplectic core carrying Fourier transform, reflection,
complex characters, and the completed theta bulk. Define the ordered graph
map

\[
\Gamma_S:g\longmapsto(g,Sg).
\]

Its endpoint traces are

\[
\partial_-Sg=\int g,
\qquad
\partial_+Sg=-\int g.
\]

Define the Fourier-saturated ordered carrier to be the completed linear span

\[
\mathfrak B_S
=
\overline{\operatorname{span}}
\{\Gamma_Sg,\ \mathcal F\Gamma_Sg,
\ R\Gamma_Sg,\ \mathcal FR\Gamma_Sg:g\in\mathscr G\},
\]

with the quotient/projective topology induced from the bulk seminorms and the
two endpoint traces. Fourier and reflection act continuously by construction,
and no value-only compression is made.

## Distributional Fourier coordinate

For the convention

\[
\widehat g(\xi)=\int g(q)e^{-i\xi q}\,dq,
\]

the ordered coordinate is

\[
\widehat{Sg}
=2i\operatorname{pv}\!\left(\frac1\xi\right)\widehat g.
\]

The possible added delta term is excluded by

\[
RSR=-S.
\]

Thus each graph vector retains all four relevant data:

1. the bulk source `g`;
2. the two opposite endpoint constants of `Sg`;
3. the odd principal-value Fourier coordinate;
4. the zero-frequency bulk value `hat g(0)=integral g`.

For the Gaussian generator this reduces to the known five-component linear
cell

\[
(\phi,1,\delta_0,K,V),
\]

with constant--delta exchange and tail--principal-value quarter-turn. For a
general source, the saturated graph is necessarily infinite-dimensional:
`pv(hat g/xi)` cannot be compressed to one fixed principal-value basis vector.

## Ordered naturality cell

On the saturated graph, the relative Fourier arrow is not an inferred scalar
identity. It is the defining continuous map

\[
J_-\mathcal F_A(g)
=
\left(\widehat g,
2i\operatorname{pv}\!\left(\frac1\xi\right)\widehat g\right),
\]

with the endpoint/zero-mode coordinate transported simultaneously. The target
map `F_{+-}J_+` is the same graph vector in the Fourier chart. Hence the
naturality cell commutes before boundary compression.

Reflection negates the principal-value plane, dilation gives degree minus one,
and the zero mode remains in the constant--delta exchange plane. The full
relative response is therefore retained.

## Arithmetic assembly

The mixed carrier for labelled synthesis is now

\[
\mathcal S_{\mathrm{lab}}
\widehat\otimes_\pi
\mathfrak B_S,
\]

not the tensor product with the Sobolev--exponential bulk alone. Rapid label
seminorms still absorb logarithmic translations and polynomial-logarithmic
Euler rows. The earlier cutoff-independence argument applies to continuous
coordinates of this saturated graph.

What remains unproved is not Fourier transport of the order port. It is the
identification of every primitive, square, and archimedean wall incidence
with a continuous coordinate of this same saturated graph, including equality
of their polarized cross-current rows.
