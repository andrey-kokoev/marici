## Minimum admissible N2MHV source packet

Before implementing the first `N2MHV` benchmark, acquire and record:

1. a primary-source equation for one explicit tree object, preferably the minimal nontrivial multiplicity;
2. the exact momentum-supertwistor or Grassmannian convention;
3. the complete term list, including composite residues if present;
4. Grassmann degree and normalization relative to the MHV prefactor;
5. cyclic-label and orientation conventions;
6. the physical/spurious pole classification used by the source;
7. a source location for every extracted formula.

The first executable acceptance test must verify at least:

- the expected Grassmann degree;
- independent projective weight zero in every external supertwistor;
- cyclic covariance;
- cancellation of every declared spurious codimension-one pole;
- survival of every declared physical pole;
- equality with a second representation only when the source supplies one.

## Minimum admissible loop source packet

2. one explicit integrand formula at fixed multiplicity and helicity sector;
3. whether equality is meant at rational-integrand, canonical-form, or integrated-function level;

5. physical propagator poles, spurious poles, and any poles at infinity;
6. the forward-limit or recursion prescription, including contour and sign conventions;
7. the equivalence relation for total derivatives or integrands integrating to zero, if used.

The first executable acceptance test must be performed before integration and check exact rational equality, all declared projective weights, and a complete denominator/residue census.
