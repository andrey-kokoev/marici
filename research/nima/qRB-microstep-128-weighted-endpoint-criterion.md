# qRB microstep 128: weighted endpoint criterion

If the endpoint pair channel is assigned source weights `w_nm`, the bound

$$
|w_{nm}e_{nm,b}|
\le\frac{|w_{nm}|}{2\sqrt{n^2+m^2}}
$$

shows that absolute endpoint summability follows from

$$
\sum_{n,m\ge1}
\frac{|w_{nm}|}{\sqrt{n^2+m^2}}<\infty.
$$

The weights must be source-derived and compatible with reciprocal symmetry. They cannot be introduced merely to repair divergence.

Without such weights, the endpoint remains a projective boundary coordinate rather than an ordinary pair-summed feature.

Status: weighted endpoint criterion isolated; canonical endpoint weights remain unspecified.
