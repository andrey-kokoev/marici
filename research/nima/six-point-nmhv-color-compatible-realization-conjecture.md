# Six-point NMHV color-compatible realization conjecture

## Problem

The completed positive-geometric construction produces one color-ordered six-point NMHV tree superamplitude. A full gauge-theory amplitude retains nonabelian color tensors and must be independent of the chosen trace or Del Duca–Dixon–Maltoni basis. Direct abelianization would erase the Jacobi data that relates those bases.

## Bold conjecture

There is an `S_6`-equivariant, residue-compatible descent from an atlas of order-labelled six-point NMHV positive geometries to the color-dressed tree amplitude. More precisely, let

\[
\mathsf{OrdGeom}_6=\bigoplus_{\sigma\in S_4}
C_\bullet(\mathcal G_{1\sigma 6})
\]

be the direct sum of the 24 oriented cell complexes indexed by the DDM orderings, and let `Jac_6` be the Jacobi ideal in the module of cubic color trees. The conjecture asserts the existence of a decorated cellular relation module `Rel_6` and a commutative descent diagram

\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
\mathsf{Lie}(6)\otimes\mathsf{OrdGeom}_6
&\xrightarrow{\quad c\otimes\Omega\quad}&
\mathsf{Lie}(6)\otimes\mathsf{RatSuperForm}_6\\
\downarrow && \downarrow\\
(\mathsf{Lie}(6)/\mathsf{Jac}_6)\otimes
(\mathsf{OrdGeom}_6/\mathsf{Rel}_6)
&\longrightarrow&
\mathcal A^{\mathrm{full}}_{6,\mathrm{NMHV}},
\end{array}
\]

such that:

1. every ordering component maps to its source-normalized color-ordered NMHV superamplitude;
2. cellular residues commute with oriented boundaries in every component;
3. Jacobi changes of cubic color basis are paired with signed, kinematically decorated relations in `Rel_6`;
4. the resulting color-dressed amplitude is independent of DDM basis choice;
5. the nonabelian color tensors remain present until the final gauge-invariant pairing.

The hard claim is item 3: color Jacobi is realized by geometry-level relations across order chambers, rather than imposed only after evaluating the partial amplitudes.

## Named rivals

1. **Color bookkeeping only.** Positive geometry constructs independent partial amplitudes; standard color decomposition combines them, with no cross-order geometric descent.
2. **Planarity obstruction.** Positivity depends essentially on one cyclic ordering, so no common oriented atlas supports Jacobi moves.
3. **Form-level descent only.** Kleiss–Kuijf or BCJ identities relate evaluated rational forms, while their cell chains have no corresponding relation.
4. **Numerator obstruction.** Jacobi-compatible kinematics requires cubic-graph numerators or scattering equations that are absent from amplituhedron canonical forms.
5. **Invariant-shadow substitute.** A commutative invariant ring records only color orbits and cannot reconstruct the covariant color-dressed amplitude.

## Risky consequences

- The 24 DDM-ordered six-point NMHV components can be generated by permutation from one source-normalized formula with consistent orientation and prefactor conventions.
- Exact Kleiss–Kuijf relations hold for the reconstructed partial amplitudes; BCJ relations hold only after the required Mandelstam decoration is included.
- At least one generating color Jacobi move has a signed chain representative whose canonical form is the corresponding decorated amplitude relation.
- Two distinct DDM basis choices reconstruct exactly the same color-dressed amplitude modulo symbolic Jacobi reduction.
- Flipping one cell orientation or one structure-constant sign produces a nonzero residual.

Failure of the third consequence rejects the bold conjecture even if ordinary color decomposition reconstructs the amplitude.

## Strongest falsification attempt

Use six-point NMHV tree level with fixed generic rational spinor-helicity or momentum-twistor data.

1. Construct all 24 DDM orderings `A(1,sigma,6)` from source-normalized five-bracket sums and Parke–Taylor factors.
2. Verify cyclic and reflection covariance and the full finite Kleiss–Kuijf matrix exactly.
3. Verify a basis of fundamental BCJ relations with exact Mandelstam coefficients.
4. Choose one explicit internal edge of a six-point cubic tree and form its three Jacobi-related color graphs.
5. Search the order-labelled cell atlas for a signed, decorated chain relation mapping to that BCJ/Jacobi triple.
6. Reduce the color-dressed result in two DDM bases and compare coefficients after symbolic Jacobi reduction.
7. Mutate one orientation and one Jacobi sign; require explicit nonzero residuals.

The first missing typed object is currently the source-fixed convention map from each permuted momentum-twistor expression to the corresponding full color-ordered superamplitude on one common spinor-helicity chart. Its acceptance test is equality with independently evaluated six-point NMHV partial amplitudes for all 24 DDM orderings at several exact generic points before any cross-order chain claim is tested.

## First executed step: common ordering chart

An exact rational momentum-twistor fixture now supplies one physical bosonic kinematic point for every DDM ordering. Starting from one cyclic momentum-twistor configuration, the checker derives conserved spinor-helicity momenta, reconstructs gauge-fixed momentum twistors separately for all 24 orderings, and recovers every original anti-holomorphic spinor exactly. All adjacent angle-bracket denominators remain nonzero.

This removes the chart-comparability blocker. It does not supply the supersymmetric variable transport or evaluate partial amplitudes. The next executable object is a common Grassmann-component map from the reordered momentum-twistor variables to one fixed external-state component.

## First cross-order component theorem

The common chart and relation conventions pass both controls and the target test. For the MHV component, all 120 Kleiss–Kuijf tests and all 24 fundamental BCJ tests vanish exactly. For the fixed six-point NMHV three-negative-gluon component, reflection covariance, all 120 Kleiss–Kuijf tests, and all 24 fundamental BCJ tests also pass exactly at one nonsingular rational point.

The earlier nonzero residual was a fixture defect: its momentum twistors lay on vanishing four-brackets for reordered chambers. Replacing that degenerate fixture by a generic rational one removes every residual without changing the relation matrices or the Grassmann transport. The checker now retains exact nonzero component values across all 24 DDM orderings.

## Color-dressed amplitude theorem

The verified partial amplitudes now reconstruct a basis-independent colored component by standard DDM dressing. Two independent checks pass:

- for `su(2)`, the endpoint bases `(1,6)` and `(2,6)` agree for all 729 assignments of six adjoint color indices; changing one half-ladder sign produces 48 nonzero assignment residuals;
- in the free cyclic single-trace word module, the two endpoint bases agree coefficientwise on all 120 cyclic words; changing one half-ladder sign produces 16 nonzero word residuals.

The free-word result covers tree-level adjoint color algebras admitting a commutator trace realization without choosing a numerical gauge group. The construction has now been checked for all 20 pure-gluon NMHV helicity assignments on three independent exact rational fixtures. All 60 component-fixture pairs are DDM endpoint-basis independent, all 2,880 ordered partial-amplitude evaluations are finite, and the hostile sign mutation remains detectable. Nonabelian words are retained until the final contraction.

## Strongest falsification result

For every one of the 24 fundamental BCJ relations, the evaluated NMHV component vanishes while the corresponding sum of oriented three-simplex chains remains nonzero. Adding the source Parke–Taylor denominator to each chain coefficient also leaves a nonzero chain in every case. Thus neither Mandelstam weighting alone nor Mandelstam-plus-Parke–Taylor weighting lifts BCJ to a relation in the existing order-labelled simplex module.

## Disposition

The amplitude-level color problem is solved for the tested six-point NMHV component: standard DDM dressing is constructed and basis-independent in both `su(2)` and the free cyclic trace module. The bold geometric conjecture is rejected for the declared carrier `OrdGeom_6`: its decisive chain-level consequence fails in all 24 tests even though the canonical-form components obey BCJ. Rival 3, form-level descent only, survives; rival 4, a missing numerator or scattering-equation decoration, remains viable.

The first missing typed object for any successor is a source-derived enriched chain complex carrying Jacobi-compatible numerator or scattering-equation data. No such object is present in the current amplituhedron simplex fixture, so this branch stops rather than defining `Rel_6` as the kernel of evaluation after the fact.
