# RH multiplicative incidence compresses labels but does not orient the completed readout

## The labelwise hostile is real

Independent tail equations do not couple arithmetic endpoints. Two decaying
tails can have opposite nonzero endpoint amplitudes, satisfy their source
equations exactly, and cancel in the scalar sum while remaining visible to a
labelled Ward observer. Therefore the raw tail solution map does not satisfy
the restricted kernel-inclusion DPC.

## Source-derived cross-label compression

Prime multiplication supplies the natural arithmetic incidence. On an edge

\[
n\longrightarrow pn,
\]

Mellin covariance requires

\[
a_{pn}=p^{-s}a_n.
\]

At a connected finite multiplicative cutoff, these equations determine every
endpoint amplitude from the vacuum amplitude. The arithmetic coherence locus
is one-dimensional and generated by

\[
a_n=n^{-s}a_1.
\]

This is genuine dynamic compression. It rejects arbitrary two-label endpoint
cancellations without requiring each label to vanish independently.

The four-label fixture 

\[
\{1,2,3,6\}
\]

has incidence rank three. The two paths from (1) to (6) agree because
prime multiplication commutes, so the coherence square is flat.

## Scalar restriction to the coherent line

After incidence compression, the scalar output is multiplication by one
coefficient:

\[
h_X(s)=\sum_{n\in X}n^{-s}.
\]

For the four-label Euler rectangle it factors as

\[
h_X(s)=(1+2^{-s})(1+3^{-s}),
\]

and is nonzero in the ordinary finite Euler chamber.

Thus finite multiplicative incidence closes the rank obstruction: the
admissible endpoint space has dimension one, so scalar output can be
injective there.

## Where the DPC becomes RH-bearing

On the coherent line, the kernel inclusion

\[
\ker L_s\subseteq\ker W_s
\]

reduces to nonvanishing of the scalar coefficient whenever the Ward map is
nonzero on the line. At restricted-product completion, that scalar coefficient
is no longer the ordinary finite sum. It becomes the source-derived exterior
boundary value whose zeros are under investigation.

Therefore multiplicative coherence solves label compression but cannot itself
orient the completed readout. Claiming that coherence forces kernel inclusion
at completion would simply restate the desired nonvanishing theorem.

## Exact separation of responsibilities

The present architecture now has three sharply distinct achievements.

1. Labelwise tail flow constructs the forward local solution but permits
   cancellation between labels.
2. Multiplicative incidence compresses endpoints to the Mellin-covariant
   coherent line.
3. A still-missing mixed Green orientation law must show that the completed
   exterior observer is nonzero on that line away from the seam.

The third law must use the representation-valued boundary packet and positive
interval Ward energy before scalar projection. It cannot be obtained from
flat prime squares, unique factorization, or scalar analytic continuation
alone.

## Falsifiers

- A nonzero labelwise packet with zero scalar endpoint disproves tail-only
  compression.
- A nonflat prime square disproves multiplicative coherence.
- A completed coherent-line state with zero scalar readout and nonzero Ward
  packet disproves the proposed Green orientation.
- Defining the coherence locus by excluding scalar zeros is circular.

## Verdict

The source already provides the missing cross-label compression through
weighted multiplicative incidence. That compression reduces the finite
endpoint problem to one coherent Mellin line. It does not solve the completed
nonvanishing problem; the remaining advance must be an independently derived
mixed Green orientation on that line.
