# Lower-filtration corrections make saturated observer frames coherent

## Result

Cubic source-image agreement does NOT automatically extend to the source-action-saturated observer tower. The original and private tests differ on an explicit genuine I^2 source. Their raw stage-three observer modules are inequivalent over source evaluation.

There is a canonical repair: retain the discrepancy as a lower-filtration observer and close it under source actions. The resulting common refinement is minimal among observer modules containing both frames. Its frame changes satisfy exact cocycle identities, commute with tower restriction and preserve the source bimodule action.

This construction works at every depth for any declared finite family of bounded finite-support extensions agreeing on the corresponding graded layer. It does not invent new response tests: corrections are differences and source-context pullbacks of the supplied tests. Rational calibrations with nonzero same-layer error require separate error budgets.

## 1. The obstruction is an actual lower-layer source

Keep the fixed cubic packet and ideal test conventions from `cubic-observer-frames-are-coherent-on-the-common-visible-source-quotient.md`. Use a spectral/background choice with m_(B_2)!=0, as guaranteed for sufficiently large background and by the calibrated A=2,y=3 fixture.

Set

`z=mixed(2,3) * [(5,forgotten)(7,retained)] * forgotten(11,13)`.

This has eight distinct actual marked paths, two retained features and six events. It belongs to I^2, since the two exterior factors are relations. The middle factor is an actual path, not a relation or a new edge.

Its five selected-sector coefficients are

`(A_1B_1,A_1B_2,A_2B_1,A_2B_2,A_1D_1)=(0,1,0,1,0)`.

For the ORIGINAL and ideal PRIVATE readouts, extended as finite record functionals ell_3^o and ell_3^p on J_1/J_4, this gives

`ell_3^o(z)=w_seam^2/(2y^2) Delta_A m_(B_2) !=0`,

`ell_3^p(z)=0`.

These are evaluations of D3 records; D3(z) is not asserted to be a bottom cycle on I^2. The tower's scalar record functionals are defined there nevertheless.

Let delta=ell_3^p-ell_3^o. The previous all-basis comparison proves delta vanishes on J_3: on the selected homogeneous I^3 corner the tests agree, and outside its degree/corner both are zero. Hence delta factors through E_2=J_1/J_3, but delta(z)!=0. It is a NONZERO lower-filtration correction.

## 2. Raw saturated stages really differ

The original Omega_2 is generated by contexts of detectors supported at lengths two and four. Such contexts cannot see a source with outer length six: source event lengths are nonnegative and concatenate additively. Thus delta is not already in Omega_2.

On the FULL six-event corner, only zero-length vertex contexts of ell_3 contribute to its saturation. Positive-length contexts would exceed the detector's support. Consequently the original and private Omega_3 restrict there to the one-dimensional spans of ell_3^o and ell_3^p, respectively.

They are not proportional: both give the same nonzero S_0 on v_0, but differ on z. More explicitly,

`z'=z-[m_(B_2)/Delta_B]v_0`

has zero original readout and nonzero private readout, while z has the reverse property. Both sources lie in I^2. All lower-detector and nontrivial context rows vanish on this full outer corner for the length reason just given.

Thus neither raw observer evaluation factors through the other at stage three. In particular there is no evaluation-compatible isomorphism of the two raw saturated stages. This is a stronger obstruction than disagreement on arbitrary noisy data: the inputs here are genuine sources.

## 3. Minimal corrected cubic tower

For a functional phi let Sat(phi) be the span of its existing left/right source-context pullbacks x->phi(a x b). Define

`D_3=Sat(delta)`.

This is finite dimensional, supported on subcorners of the fixed finite packet, and annihilates J_3. It is a source-bimodule subspace of E_2^*. Its finite-support source action extends through the existing convex packet restriction.

Keep all other detector choices fixed. For m>=3,

`Omega_m^sharp=Omega_m^o+D_3=Omega_m^p+D_3
              =Omega_m^o+Omega_m^p`.

For m<3 use the unchanged earlier stages. This is the minimal common source-action-invariant refinement containing both raw frame spaces. The correction is introduced at observation stage three but depends on source filtration depth at most two; those notions must not be confused with its length-six support.

Dualizing gives the corrected observer tower O_m^sharp=(Omega_m^sharp)^*. The inclusions in m yield surjective restriction maps. Evaluation from E_m is a source-bimodule map and commutes with restriction, exactly as in the all-depth saturation construction.

In generating coordinates the frame change is simply

`ell_3^p(a x b)=ell_3^o(a x b)+delta(a x b)`.

The correction coordinates are retained, not discarded. Therefore the corrected frames are invertibly related over the source, although the uncorrected ones are not. Relations among redundant context generators are respected because all rows are actual functionals on the same E_m.

## 4. The all-depth correction theorem

More generally, at each r let F_r be a declared FINITE set of bounded, finite-support record readouts ell_r^f on E_r=J_1/J_(r+1). Assume their restrictions to J_r/J_(r+1) agree. This is a hypothesis to verify for actual frames, not permission to adjoin arbitrary tensor-dual functionals.

For f,g in F_r,

`delta_r^(g,f)=ell_r^g-ell_r^f`

annihilates J_r and therefore factors through E_(r-1). At r=1 it is zero. Put

`D_r=Sat span {delta_r^(g,f): f,g in F_r}`.

A finite union of source supports has finite convex closure. Only finitely many typed path contexts can contribute to these finite-support readouts, so each D_r is finite dimensional and source invariant.

For any choice of frame f_r at each depth, define

`Omega_m^(f,corr)
 =Sat span {ell_r^(f_r):r<=m}+sum_(r<=m) D_r`.

Then this space is INDEPENDENT of the choices:

`Omega_m^(f,corr)=Sat span {ell_r^g:r<=m, g in F_r}`.

Proof: any other generator differs from the selected one by an element of D_r, and every correction is already a difference of generators in the right-hand side. This also proves minimality of the common refinement.

The corrected spaces embed in E_m^*, are nested in m and carry the same source action. Their duals and evaluations consequently form a canonical source-equivariant inverse tower.

## 5. Cocycle and triangular character

Choose one reference frame at each depth and retain common finite lists of context generators for every difference. In these generating presentations, changing f to g replaces each detector row by

`ell_r^g(a x b)=ell_r^f(a x b)+delta_r^(g,f)(a x b)`.

The correction factors through a strictly lower source filtration. This is a filtered triangular change of GENERATORS; the spaces are not asserted freely generated by those rows.

The differences obey exactly

`delta_r^(h,g)+delta_r^(g,f)=delta_r^(h,f)`.

All frame routes therefore agree, all round trips are identities on the corrected observer object, and transitions commute with truncation to smaller m. Because context pullback commutes with taking these differences, they also intertwine source actions. The identities descend through every relation among the generators.

This is a tower-frame coherence theorem for the specified finite frame families. At present the cubic family supplies the nontrivial worked example; depths with only the original detector have zero frame correction. It is not a construction of as-yet-unspecified optimized frames at every depth or a theorem about spectral-chart changes.

## 6. Corrections have a real measurement cost

Coherence does not erase conditioning. On the five-sector measurement protocol, the actual source z above has norm

`||Y_5(z)||=[||r_(A_1)||+||r_(A_2)||]||r_(B_2)||`,

while |delta(z)|=w_seam^2 Delta_A |m_(B_2)|/(2y^2).

The supplied response and theta asymptotics give

`||Y_5(z)|| ~2c_r^2 X_(A_1)X_(B_2)(log A)^2`,

`|delta(z)| ~[w_seam^2 log 2/(2y^2)]log A`.

Thus EVERY bounded five-sector representative of this correction has norm at least a positive constant times

`A^(-2y-5)(log A)^(-1) exp(901pi A^2)`.

The exponent-101 optimum applied to preserving the top cubic functional on I^3. It does not automatically apply to retaining its original lower-layer extension as part of the saturated tower. On this five-sector protocol, the new correction channel has an unavoidable larger cost. No global optimum over all additional receiver sectors is claimed by this bound.

Each finite corrected stage remains continuous. Differences are bounded by the sums of the existing test bounds, and contexts use the supplied source-operation bounds. The all-depth product topology is therefore valid. Uniform bounded or summed depth norms still need the additional analytical budgets: the earlier unbounded-readout summable source remains an obstruction, since its original rows are retained in the corrected tower.

## 7. Calibration and boundary

An IDEAL recalibration agrees on the graded layer and gives a lower-filtration correction. A rational approximation with a nonzero error on v_0 or v_x does not satisfy this hypothesis: its discrepancy has a same-layer component. Such errors use the separately certified coefficient/noise budgets, rather than being silently absorbed into D_3 as exact lower-depth data.

The corrected tower does not change the terminal-record ideal, the arithmetic form, or the source action. It adds the actual observer information needed to compare different extensions of the same graded detector. It does not identify arbitrary noisy records, reconstruct the full source, or assert perfect duality.
