# Correction: maximal-isotropic response descent does not require a scalar anomaly-line trivialization

## Corrected claim

The previous reduction placed anomaly-line scalar trivialization inside SCC
candidate two. That was too strong. The stratified Fourier equalizer and its
maximal-isotropic response graph are already closed before scalarization of the
determinant line. Candidate two is a response-intertwining theorem, not a
scalar determinant-provenance theorem.

## Three distinct statements

Keep separate:

1. response descent into a maximal-isotropic sewing graph;
2. descent of the determinant line as a line bundle or twisted line;
3. an invertible scalar trivialization of that line.

Statement 1 requires the transition and contragredient response actions to
preserve the hyperbolic Green pairing. It does not require statement 3.

Statement 2 permits an authorized cocycle or metaplectic character. Horizontal
sections are then twisted sections.

Statement 3 may fail on a divisor-bearing line: a scalar section equal to Xi
vanishes at Xi zeros and is not an everywhere-invertible trivialization there.

\(\det_2\) gluing and the archimedean line must remain coherent with them, but

Thus anomaly-line scalar descent belongs to determinant comparison and
multiplicity bookkeeping, not to the existence of the maximal-isotropic
response relation.
