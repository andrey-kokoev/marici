# The finite-rank cross-prime boundary factor matches under the weighted Hadamard isometry

## Question

Is the boundary-mediated cross-prime factor still an independent interface
obstruction?

## Endpoint metrics

The adelic boundary observer at each place is the pair

\[
(\epsilon_v,
\mu_v),
\]

with Fourier acting by coordinate exchange. The source-fixed state metric is
`2I` in the completed theta endpoint frame. The wall and jump eigenlines are
orthogonal in this metric.

The weighted Hadamard map sends the normalized raw endpoint characters to the
completed theta columns,

\[
U_\partial v_w=w_\theta,
\qquad
U_\partial v_j=-\widehat j_\theta,
\]

and satisfies

\[
U_\partial^*(2I)U_\partial=I.
\]

Its primewise direct sum is an isometry with cutoff-independent inverse.
Therefore the global boundary aggregation map has the same pullback Gram in
the Tate observer frame and the retained Wronskian frame.

## Cross-prime factor

If local boundary rows are aggregated by `B_partial`, the induced finite-rank
form is

\[
B_\partial^*H_\partial B_\partial.
\]

Conjugating by the direct-sum weighted Hadamard map leaves this form unchanged
as a pullback. The off-diagonal prime entries created by common endpoint
aggregation therefore agree automatically once the local wall/jump
orientation is fixed.

The Sokhotski half-jump calculation already fixes the odd amplitude and sign.
The wall coordinate uses the same principal-value average on both sides.
Hence no independent endpoint coefficient remains.

## Scope boundary

This establishes equality only for the finite-rank boundary-mediated part. It
does not compare the place-diagonal interior forms

\[
\bigoplus_p q_p^{\rm Tate}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\bigoplus_p G_p^{\rm ret}.
\]

## Disposition

Boundary-mediated cross-prime sewing is closed by the weighted Hadamard
isometry and the fixed Sokhotski orientation. The sole remaining packet
component is the placewise Hermitian bulk equality on the noncollapsed
corrected pair state.
