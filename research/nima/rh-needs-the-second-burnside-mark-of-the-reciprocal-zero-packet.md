# RH Needs the Second Burnside Mark of the Reciprocal Zero Packet

## Orbit algebra

A finite reciprocal zero packet is a finite \(C_2\)-set. Its orbit types are:

- a fixed orbit, representing a zero on the critical seam;
- a free two-point orbit, representing a reciprocal off-seam pair.

The Burnside ring \(A(C_2)\) is freely generated by these two orbit types.
Consequently a reciprocal packet has two independent additive invariants.

## Two marks

For a finite \(C_2\)-set \(X\), define

\[
m_{\rm total}(X)=|X|,
\qquad
m_{\rm fixed}(X)=|X^{C_2}|.
\]

On the two orbit generators these marks are:

| Orbit | Total mark | Fixed mark |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fixed | 1 | 1 |
| Free | 2 | 0 |

The pair of marks is injective. Either mark alone is not.

## Exact RH criterion

At any reciprocal- and conjugation-stable finite height truncation, absence of
free reciprocal orbits is equivalent to

\[
m_{\rm total}=m_{\rm fixed}.
\]

Thus the familiar total zero count is insufficient. One free orbit and two
fixed orbits both contribute two to the total mark.

## Pull-push detector

Let \(q:X\to X/C_2\) be the orbit map. On an orbit, the pull-push norm
\(q_*q^*\) is multiplication by the orbit cardinality. It acts by \(1\) on a
fixed orbit and by \(2\) on a free orbit.

This supplies an exact categorical detector of orbit type. It does not yet
supply a source computation of the detector on the zero packet.

## Circularity boundary

Computing either \(m_{\rm fixed}\) or \(q_*q^*\) directly from the divisor
already inspects whether zeros lie on the seam. That is equivalent to the
desired conclusion.

The RH-bearing advance would be a source-side realization of the second mark:

> Construct a trace, index, or boundary charge from labelled theta/Tate data
> whose transfer to the divisor equals the fixed-point mark.

The ordinary argument principle supplies the total mark. A second source
observer must supply the fixed mark without locating the zeros. Equality of
the two source computations would then exclude free orbits.

## Hostile falsifier

Compare a packet of two fixed points with one free two-point orbit. They have
the same total mark and different fixed marks. Any proposed scalar index that
assigns them the same value is not fixed-point sensitive and cannot prove RH.

## Cross-sector meaning

The missing operation is not another coherencer between reciprocal sectors.
It is an independent observer of stabilizer type. This is the same structural
pattern seen elsewhere in Marici: one readout measures aggregate content,
while a second signed or typed observer measures how that content sits under
the symmetry.
