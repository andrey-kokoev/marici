# Does the retained spectral residual feed back into the visible channels?

## Carrier and question

Reuse the checked nine-dimensional complex occurrence-pair carrier and its projectors

    D = sum_lambda E_left,lambda tensor E_right,lambda,
    R = I-D.

For a supplied linear operation T on THIS carrier, the next visible state is

    D T v = D T D v + D T R v.

Thus DTR=0 is exactly the all-input criterion for the current D-visible coefficients to determine the next D-visible coefficients without consulting the hidden state. This concerns full complex components, not their intensities or an unspecified physical measurement.

## Existing controls specialized to the tensor representation

Test seven controls, with exact rational/complex arithmetic:

- left cyclic successor C_left tensor I;
- right cyclic successor I tensor C_right;
- both cyclic successors;
- left short half-phase S_left tensor I;
- right short half-phase I tensor S_right;
- both short half-phases;
- the seed automorphism exchanging the triangle factors.

C is extracted with the existing packet-cycle function. S uses the existing short-root formula Q+(I-Q)/2+(C-C^T)/2, checked against S^2=C and orthogonality. The seed automorphism sends (AB,BC,CA) to (BA,AD,DB) and back. Its tensor action is factor exchange in those corresponding ordered bases; complete occurrence identities are transported, not identified.

These are representation-level controls. Lifting local operations to the declared tensor carrier is explicit; this is not a claim that spectral promotion executes them physically.

## Exact result

For every control,

    DTR=0, RTD=0, DT=TD,
    T = DTD + RTR.

The checker also verifies 72 normal-form schedules built from the two six-step short-phase actions and optional factor exchange. More generally, commutation with D is preserved by composition and inversion. Local short-phase operations are polynomials in their cyclic operators, hence preserve the mode labels. Factor exchange sends (lambda,mu) to (mu,lambda), preserving both the same-mode and cross-mode sectors. This explains closure for arbitrary words in these controls, beyond finite regression.

Therefore visible and residual coefficients evolve as separate blocks:

    d_next = DTD d,
    r_next = RTR r,
    v_next = d_next + r_next.

Both blocks preserve their squared norm for these unitary controls. No environment-sensitive interaction or residual-to-visible feedback is generated by these operations.

## Important distinction: hidden does not mean frozen

Take a nonzero vector in the (positive,common) channel. A left cyclic step multiplies it by omega. Its current and subsequent D-readings are both zero, but its actual coefficient state changes.

So the residual may be omitted from ACTIVE VISIBLE PREDICTION for this operation family. It must still be evolved, or retained with enough operator history for exact replay, to recover the complete current state. Archiving an initial residual and silently treating it as unchanged would fail recovery.

This is a conditional closed observation sector, not a license to delete histories, phases or cross-mode components from the retained object.

## Intended successor: still outside the tested action contract

The inspected implementations supply different operation types:

- `SpectralLedger.promote` constructs a fresh mode identity from a retained nested root. It does not act on an arbitrary nine-component pair amplitude.
- Recursive independent family comparison constructs endpoint-dependent ordered record products. Its numerical action on this tensor state has not been supplied.
- `check_joint_seed_triangle_cycle.py` has six-coordinate simultaneous and serial endpoint-lift operators. Those act on a DIRECT SUM of occurrence coefficient spaces, not this nine-dimensional tensor carrier. Importing them as 9x9 matrices or asserting a tensor lift without a map would repeat the earlier domain error.

Consequently this test establishes closure under the existing slot controls, NOT under the intended next-rung successor. No invented coupling was added to produce a nonzero DTR. A future actual successor adapter can be tested against exactly this criterion once its input/output carrier is specified. If the carrier changes, the corresponding criterion uses its specified output reader rather than reusing D by dimension alone.
