# Physical Selectors Must Descend

A source-generated numerical selector can still be chart data. It is physical
only if it descends through the sector's declared equivalence relation.

The finite swap model separates three notions:

- the coordinate \(x\) is a valid chart observable but does not descend;
- \(x+y\) descends but collapses distinct quotient points;
- \((x+y,xy)\) descends and faithfully coordinates the quotient.

Therefore:

\[
\text{source generation}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{physical descent}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{faithful reconstruction}.
\]

This types the flavor result cleanly. A texture-loop phase may be exactly
generated inside its sparse chart and still fail to define a physical
constant when weak-basis equivalence moves or destroys that chart. Physical
constant claims must be made in faithful quotient coordinates.
