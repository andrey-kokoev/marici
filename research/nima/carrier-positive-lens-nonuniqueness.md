# The Bare Carrier Does Not Select Its Positive Lens

Fix a two-label Carrier with label swap and oriented exterior volume one.
Two positive pairings compatible with all that data are

\[
G_0=
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
G_1=
\begin{pmatrix}5/3&4/3\\4/3&5/3\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Both are swap-invariant and have determinant one, but their normalized
overlaps are different:

\[
|\gamma_0|^2=0,\qquad |\gamma_1|^2=\frac{16}{25}.
\]

This is not an isolated ambiguity. The rational family

\[
G(t)=
\begin{pmatrix}a(t)&b(t)\\b(t)&a(t)\end{pmatrix},
\quad
a=\frac{1+t^2}{1-t^2},
\quad
b=\frac{2t}{1-t^2}
\]

preserves positivity, swap, and determinant one while changing the overlap.

Therefore the strongest DPC is false: the common labelled/exterior Carrier
does not uniquely generate the sector's positive lens. The surviving layered
version is:

\[
\text{Carrier constrains a space of admissible lenses},
\qquad
\text{sector source selects a lens},
\qquad
\text{lens closure generates readouts}.
\]

The next test is whether the sector selection itself is a canonical section
generated by a higher source object, or genuinely independent coefficient
data.
