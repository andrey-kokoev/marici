# Physical cut coaction is continuous on the projective Laurent completion

## Required localization

The occurrence-level cut map multiplies each term by

\[
-\frac{X_{\mathrm{slot}}}{X_{\mathrm{edge}}}.
\]

Therefore the positive valuation monoid completion used for rooted convolution is not, by itself, stable under physical cuts. The minimal cut-stable carrier is its Laurent localization.

Let `G` be the free valuation group generated by the finitely supported edge and slot labels, and choose an additive generator weight `lambda`. Put

\[
|g|_\lambda=\sum_s |g_s|\lambda_s,
\qquad
q_\delta(c)=\sum_{g\in G}e^{\delta|g|_\lambda}\|c_g\|.
\]

The cut-stable projective carrier is

\[
\mathcal A_{\exp}^{\mathrm{Laur}}
=
\bigcap_{\delta>0}\ell^1(G,e^{\delta|\cdot|_\lambda};T).
\]

The original positive-cone test algebra embeds continuously in this carrier.

## Translation estimate

For a Laurent monomial `X^h`, multiplication is translation `T_h` on coefficients. The triangle inequality gives

\[
|g+h|_\lambda\le |g|_\lambda+|h|_\lambda,
\]

and hence

\[
q_\delta(T_hc)
\le e^{\delta|h|_\lambda}q_\delta(c).
\]

For a cut edge `e` with two sink slots `s_1,s_2`, the physical coaction is a signed finite sum

\[
G_e=-M_{s_1-e}\otimes m_{s_1}-M_{s_2-e}\otimes m_{s_2},
\]

where the marking updates are isometries between discrete occurrence fibers. Therefore

\[
q_\delta(G_ec)
\le
\left(e^{\delta|s_1-e|_\lambda}
+e^{\delta|s_2-e|_\lambda}\right)q_\delta(c).
\]

Every one-edge cut is continuous for every projective seminorm and extends uniquely through completion. A finite cut set is a composite of such maps and is likewise continuous.

## Coaction laws after completion

The exact occurrence theorem proves on finite Laurent packets that

\[
G_EG_F=G_FG_E
\]

for disjoint cuts, that every cut order gives the same marked forest expansion, and that the empty cut is the unit. Both sides are continuous maps and finite Laurent packets are dense. They therefore remain equal on completion.

Thus the completion map satisfies

\[
\kappa G_E=\widehat G_E\kappa,
\]

and all 64 generic `R x V` square instances are strict on the localized projective carrier.

## Difference from the chart face

Laurent translation can move mass across a finite support window, so a sharp diagnostic projection need not commute with a cut. That finite-window residual is not a failure of completion continuity. The theorem asserts extension to the projective Laurent completion, not preservation of every cutoff subspace.

Fourier transport remains different: its omitted-to-visible leakage is nonlocal and must remain a lax `R x q` cell.

## Boundary

This theorem covers the source-derived finite physical cut coaction on the marked occurrence carrier. It does not cover nontransverse loaded-current divisors or prove that an independently chosen Hilbert completion is cut-stable. Those require additional domain estimates.
