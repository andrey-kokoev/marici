## Transported translation generator

For
\[
(\mathcal M_n f)(u)
=
\sqrt n\,e^{u/2}f(ne^u),
\]
write
\[
x=ne^u.
\]

Differentiation gives
\[
\left(\partial_u-\frac12\right)\mathcal M_nf
=
x\,\mathcal M_n(D_xf).
\]

Therefore
\[
\mathcal M_nD_x
=
\frac1n e^{-u}
\left(\partial_u-\frac12\right)\mathcal M_n.
\]

Define
\[
B_n
=
\frac1n e^{-u}
\left(\partial_u-\frac12\right).
\]
Then
\[
\mathcal M_nD_x=B_n\mathcal M_n.
\]

Thus the comoving opposite-translation connection
\[
D_x\otimes\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)
\]
is transported exactly to
\[
B_n\otimes\operatorname{diag}(-1,1).
\]

No scalar calibration is involved.
