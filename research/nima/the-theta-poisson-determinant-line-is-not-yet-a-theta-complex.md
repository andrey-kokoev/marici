# The theta--Poisson determinant line is not yet a theta complex

> **Superseded object claim.** The finite Fock successor constructs cutoff
> complexes but not their bounded completion. The later packet
> `the-xi-dual-section-defines-a-canonical-koszul-complex-with-the-correct-divisor.md`
> observes that the completed source Mellin dual section itself canonically
> defines a line-valued Koszul complex with the exact Xi divisor. The audit
> below remains valid for a stronger state-bearing theta complex assembled
> from primitive, square, connected, seam, endpoint, and archimedean operator
> blocks; it is no longer valid as a claim that no completed theta complex of
> any kind exists.

## Typing audit

The theta Mellin--Poisson construction currently supplies:

- primitive, square, and connected determinant-line strata;
- seam, endpoint, and archimedean lines;
- reciprocal clutching;
- a scalar trivialization equal to \(\xi(s)\).

It does not yet supply a completed cochain object

\[
T_\theta(s)=(E_\theta^\bullet,d_\theta(s))
\]

with declared domains and \(d_\theta(s)^2=0\).

A determinant line with a distinguished section does not canonically recover
a complex.  Many non-quasi-isomorphic complexes have isomorphic determinant
lines and the same scalar determinant section.

## Correction to the mapping-cone frontier

The proposed comparison

\[
F:T_\theta\longrightarrow C_{\rm FP}
\]

and its mapping cone are therefore not yet defined.  The acyclic-mapping-cone
criterion remains the correct sufficient theorem once both complexes exist,
but it is not the next executable construction.

The earliest missing object is \(T_\theta\) itself.

## Minimum theta-complex data

A source-authorized theta complex must declare:

1. graded objects for primitive distributional, square Hilbert, connected
   determinant-class, seam, endpoint, and archimedean components;
2. a common dense domain for every differential block;
3. the source maps occupying each block;
4. all relations needed for \(d_\theta^2=0\);
5. reciprocal chain transport;
6. cutoff bonding maps commuting with the differential;
7. a determinant-functor identification with the existing theta--Poisson
   line;
8. an Euler-domain comparison with the symmetric-Fock character.

Without item 7, the complex may have source provenance but the wrong divisor.
Without items 2--4, it is only a diagram of operators.

## Existing pieces do not yet form a differential

The retained architecture provides candidate arrows:

- prime-loop cyclic returns;
- arithmetic-to-analytic incidence;
- history and endpoint traces;
- front-to-cut comparison;
- reciprocal sewing;
- connected nuclear return.

Their established commutative squares and Adams coherence do not by themselves
imply that an alternating sum squares to zero.  Signs, domains, and adjacent
source/target objects must be fixed before totalization.

The bordered scalar and endpoint-current complexes are genuine two-term
complexes, but their stable reductions show that they encode their scalar
readouts unless completion blocks the elimination.  They cannot be renamed
as the missing theta complex.

## Toeplitz candidate boundary

A Toeplitz family can provide a genuine two-term Fredholm complex and a defect
sheaf, but deriving it from the completed scalar Xi multiplier would be
circular.  Its symbol, Hardy polarization, and boundary projection must be
constructed from labelled theta/Tate operations before determinant
comparison.

No current packet completes that derivation.

## Revised G4 order

The admissible order is now:

1. assemble \((E_\theta^\bullet,d_\theta)\) from retained labelled source maps;
2. verify domains, \(d_\theta^2=0\), reciprocal transport, and cutoff
   naturality;
3. identify its determinant line with the existing theta--Poisson Xi line;
4. construct the chain map to \(C_{\rm FP}\);
5. contract the mapping cone with uniform completed bounds.

The mapping-cone theorem packages the final comparison, but steps 1--3 cannot
be skipped.

## Disposition

G4 has not yet reached a defined theta-to-cone chain comparison.  Its earliest
missing constructor is the completed source theta differential.  Scalar Xi
trivialization is established; a kernel-bearing theta complex is not.  No RH
conclusion is authorized.
