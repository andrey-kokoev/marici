# No equivalent Hilbert-Fock topology extends the Euler trace to the critical strip

## Infinite prime carrier

Let

\[
L(s)e_p=p^{-s}e_p
\]

on a Hilbert completion of the labelled prime carrier.  Changing to any
equivalent diagonal Hilbert metric rescales the basis vectors but does not
change the eigenvalues \(p^{-s}\).

The symmetric second quantization has occupation-number eigenvalues

\[
\prod_p p^{-s n_p}
\]

for finitely supported occupation vectors \((n_p)\).

## Exact trace-norm criterion

For \(\sigma=\operatorname{Re}s>0\), the trace norm is

\[
\|\Gamma(L(s))\|_1
=
\prod_p\left(1-p^{-\sigma}\right)^{-1}.
\]

This product is finite exactly when

\[
\sum_p p^{-\sigma}<\infty,
\]

which holds exactly for \(\sigma>1\).

Therefore

\[
\Gamma(L(s))\in\mathcal S_1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Re}s>1.
\]

The obstruction already occurs in the one-particle sector: if
\(\Gamma(L(s))\) were trace class, its compression to \(E\) would make
\(L(s)\) trace class.

## Equivalent metrics do not help

Let \(T\) be a bounded invertible diagonal change of Hilbert metric.  Then

\[
TL(s)T^{-1}=L(s).
\]

More generally, bounded similarity preserves the spectrum and trace-class
membership.  Hence no equivalent prime-label Hilbert topology can move the
Fock trace threshold from \(\operatorname{Re}s>1\) to the critical strip.

A nonequivalent topology may change the completed object.  It requires a new
source comparison and cannot be selected merely to make the trace converge.

## The trace observer fails first

For \(1/2\le\operatorname{Re}s\le1\), the second-quantized operator remains a
bounded contraction, but it is not trace class.  The ordinary trace observer

\[
y_\Delta=\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal F}
\]

is therefore unavailable on the proposed state.  The finite multiplicative
border does not pass to an ordinary Hilbert-Fock Fredholm border.

This identifies the first failed triangular datum precisely: the completed
trace row is not continuous on the critical-strip Fock state class.

## Rigged completion does not supply a Fredholm kernel automatically

The existing primitive-distributional, square-Hilbert, connected-\(\mathcal
S_3\), and Fourier--Poisson construction can renormalize the determinant
line.  Its theta Mellin functional gives a scalar section.  It does not turn
\(\Gamma(L(s))\) into a trace-class operator or make the ordinary Fock trace
continuous.

A distributional or finite-part trace can define a line functional, but its
zero need not be noninvertibility of a closed operator.  That kernel arrow
requires a separate boundary complex.

## Consequence for G4

The symmetric-Fock state gives exact finite multiplicative provenance and the
correct inverse Euler variance, but it cannot itself be the completed
critical-strip Fredholm carrier in any equivalent Hilbert topology.

The viable completed architecture must keep two objects distinct:

1. the theta--Poisson determinant-line section carrying \(\xi\);
2. a closed maximal-isotropic boundary pencil carrying kernel states.

G4 is the construction of a divisor-preserving comparison between those
objects.  It cannot be replaced by an ordinary completed Fock trace.  No RH
conclusion is authorized.
