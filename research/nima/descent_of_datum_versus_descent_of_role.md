# Cross-sector result: descent of a datum is not descent of its claimed role

## Result

The Q and flavor replays expose a distinction that a binary
physical/unphysical label erases.

Let \(d\) be a presentation-level expression and let \(\rho\) be a claimed
operational role for it, such as coordinate, singular support, observable, or
selection rule. Two independent questions must be asked:

1. Does \(d\) descend through the relevant quotient or chart transition?
2. Does the pair \((d,\rho)\) descend through the support and physical-readout
   interfaces required by that role?

Thus

\[
\boxed{d\text{ descends}\;\not\Rightarrow\;(d,\rho)\text{ descends}.}
\]

## Two complementary failures

### Flavor

The nine-link loop phase \(\phi\) is invariant under the sparse chart
groupoid, but an exact weak-basis transformation preserves the physical point
while changing or destroying the chart phase. The datum itself does not
descend to the full physical quotient:

\[
\phi\neq\bar\phi\circ q_{U(3)^3}.
\]

Failure level: **datum descent**.

### Cosmological \(\mathcal Q\)

The polynomial \(\mathcal Q\) is a legitimate algebraic expression in the
kinematic/master presentation. The failed claim is stronger: that its zero
locus supplies intrinsic Carrier or physical singular support. Generic source
rank, transport, and resolved-cycle variation all remain trivial there.

Failure level: **role descent**. The expression survives; the proposed
singularity role does not.

## Revised self-modeling operator contract

For every candidate, \(\mathsf U\) must represent at least

\[
(d,\rho,I_d,I_\rho),
\]

where \(I_d\) is the next unverified interface for the datum and \(I_\rho\) is
the next unverified interface demanded by its claimed role. It must never
transfer evidence between these coordinates without a typed comparison map.

This predicts a common error class: a calculation proves that an expression
is invariant or well-defined, then silently promotes that success to a
stronger operational role. The operator should reject that promotion and ask
for the role-specific interface test.

## Prospective test

For the rank-26 cosmological Leray readout, a future candidate covector may be
a perfectly valid algebraic functional while failing the role of physical
readout. Before acceptance it must satisfy, in source normalization:

1. annihilation of exact/source-relation directions;
2. dual Gauss--Manin horizontality;
3. labelled-chart descent;
4. normalization by the derived relative cycle;
5. compatibility with established trivial generic \(\mathcal Q\)-monodromy.

The prospective prediction is not the covector's value. It is that any
failure will localize to one of these role interfaces, and that algebraic
existence alone will not repair it.

## Falsifier

The distinction is empty if no case can be found where the datum descends but
its role fails, or if role descent is always automatic from datum descent.
Generic \(\mathcal Q\) currently supplies a counterexample to that collapse;
future sectors must test whether the distinction continues to predict missing
maps rather than merely classify results afterward.
