# qRB microstep 34: comparison-domain candidate

A noncircular completion candidate can combine the source-core seminorm with the observer seminorm:

$$
\|u\|_*^2
=
\|u\|_{\rm src}^2+\|u\|_{\rm obs}^2.
$$

Its completion `D_*` maps continuously to the source and observer completions. The relative readout is then tested as a bounded form on `D_*`.

This is a comparison span, not yet the desired minimal completion. In particular, a bound in `\|\cdot\|_*` does not imply a bound in the source norm alone, and therefore does not imply scalar Weil positivity.

Status: a noncircular domain candidate is available; the required comparison estimate for the actual relative readout remains open.
