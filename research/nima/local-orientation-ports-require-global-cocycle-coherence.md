# Local orientation ports require global cocycle coherence

## Question

When a sector has an unresolved sign or conjugation sheet, what source data
locally separates it, and what additional condition makes those local choices a
globally coherent frame?

## Claim boundary

This packet connects two independently derived sector results. It does not
assert that flavor kinematics and authority anomalies are the same physical
object. They instantiate two successive structural gates:

1. a local oriented port must break the surviving reflection stabilizer;
2. the resulting local frames must satisfy a source-authorized cocycle law on
   overlaps.

## Flavor local obstruction

In the parent rest frame, momentum closure gives

\[
p_n+p_q+p_X=0.
\]

Therefore

\[
p_n\cdot(p_q\times p_X)=0.
\]

Every pseudoscalar built only from the three native momenta vanishes. The
unpolarized cascade consequently retains the conjugation action

\[
\phi\longmapsto-\phi
\]

on the unresolved phase direction.

A source-derived oriented parent-spin or beam vector (s) permits the signed
port

\[
S=s\cdot(p_n\times p_q).
\]

This is the local Clifford pseudoscalar missing from the unpolarized record. It
changes sign under orientation reversal and can separate the two conjugate
sheets when nonzero and physically calibrated.

The added vector must come from an actual polarized production process. A
coordinate normal chosen after observing the event does not define a physical
port.

## Stabilizer interpretation

The unpolarized records are invariant under a (C_2) reflection. Their
observable quotient therefore retains a two-sheet fiber. A nonzero signed port
reduces the local stabilizer to the identity on the admitted domain.

This is local separatedness only. It does not prove that orientation choices in
different production, detector, or reconstruction charts agree globally.

## Global gluing gate

Let local oriented frames be related on overlaps by source-typed transition
data. Triangle comparisons may carry central signs. These signs define local
anomaly faces. On every complete tetrahedral boundary, their oriented product
must be the identity.

For sign-valued faces, two negative faces close while one negative face leaves
a nontrivial defect. A missing face makes the global test undefined rather than
successful.

Algebraic cancellation is still insufficient without source authority for the
transition and anomaly constructors.

## Two-stage DPC

A proposed global orientation mechanism must pass:

1. Local separation: exhibit a source-derived signed port whose response is
   nonzero on the sheet difference.
2. Domain stability: prove that the port remains calibrated and nondegenerate on
   the admitted uncertainty and context-saturated domain.
3. Transition typing: derive the comparison maps between local frames.
4. Complete-cover gate: supply every face needed by the overlap complex.
5. Cocycle closure: verify trivial oriented boundary product.
6. Authority gate: show that every transition and coherence cell is
   source-authorized.
7. Fault and completion gates: preserve local rank and global closure under
   noise, cutoff enlargement, and completion.

## Finite falsifiers

- Momentum closure forces the native three-momentum pseudoscalar to zero.
- A proposed oriented port vanishes at a kinematic or polarization boundary.
- One negative face on a complete tetrahedron leaves a cocycle defect.
- A missing face prevents evaluation of the global law.
- Numerically closing signs without a source transition constructor supplies no
  authority.

## Disposition

The finite local and cocycle witnesses are closed. Flavor currently has a
source-shaped candidate for the local signed port but lacks the physical
polarized production and calibrated detector analysis. The general global gate
requires a complete source-typed transition atlas; local sign separation alone
does not provide it.
