# The Planar QND Lens Is Preparation-Independent

Let \(p\) be any normalized real pointer state and let

\[
R=
\begin{pmatrix}c&-s\\s&c\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then

\[
\langle p,Rp\rangle=c,
\qquad
\det(p,Rp)=s.
\]

Therefore every normalized pointer preparation produces the same occurrence
Gram lens and the same complementarity point. In this planar model, the
boundary state is not the numerical selector. The rotation carried by the
interaction is.

The remaining constant question is consequently sharper:

> What source dynamics selects the interaction angle?
