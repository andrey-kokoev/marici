# The triangular cut predicts the two D12 \(\Phi_1\) coefficients

The source-typed D12 basis is

\[
\Phi_1^{(12)}
=g_{4,1}s^4+g_{4,2}s^2(s^2+t^2+u^2).
\]

The nonforward crossed-cut filtration measures

\[
\frac{g_{4,1}+g_{4,2}}{g_2}=\frac1{70}.
\]

An independent exact-amplitude calculation at \(x=1/4\) and \(x=1/2\)
separates the two angular structures and certifies

\[
\boxed{
\frac{g_{4,1}}{g_2}=\frac{41}{4620},\qquad
\frac{g_{4,2}}{g_2}=\frac5{924}.
}
\]

Their sum is \(1/70\), while the transverse combination is

\[
\frac{g_{4,1}+\frac32g_{4,2}}{g_2}=\frac{157}{9240}.
\]

Thus the transfer filtration exposes coefficient information that the
transverse Bell readout alone cannot separate.
