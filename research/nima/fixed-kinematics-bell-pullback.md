# Fixed-kinematics Bell pullback

Sinha and Zahed explicitly treat massless outgoing states at fixed momenta as
helicity qubits.  Their theoretical Bell parameter therefore begins with the
fiber pullback

\[
i_p^*\mathcal A\in V_{p_3}\otimes V_{p_4},
\]

followed by projective normalization and the Born readout.  It does not begin
with integration over a detector acceptance region.

This corrects the scope of Entry 1581.  A positive relative pushforward is
needed for an angle bin, inclusive cross section, or postselected experimental
sample.  It is not needed to define the paper's pointwise theoretical Bell
function.  Exact two-fiber evaluation shows that pullback commutes with
pointwise fiber maps, whereas averaging distinct fibers generally changes the
Bell value.

The immediate Marici target is consequently smaller: construct the helicity
fiber of the amplitude at fixed physical kinematics and apply the Born lens.
The relative-support theorem remains the subsequent experimental/inclusive
extension.
