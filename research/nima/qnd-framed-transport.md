# Open Interaction Transport Requires Endpoint Framing

For an open interaction history, transport transforms as

\[
H\longmapsto Q_{\rm out}HQ_{\rm in}^{-1}.
\]

A naive overlap between input and output coordinates is not invariant under
independent endpoint gauges. It becomes invariant only after including a
comparison map

\[
F:\mathcal H_{\rm out}\to\mathcal H_{\rm in},
\qquad
F\longmapsto Q_{\rm in}FQ_{\rm out}^{-1}.
\]

Therefore the QND selector is

\[
\text{connection + open history + endpoint comparison},
\]

not an unframed holonomy. For closed cycles, conjugacy-class readouts such as
traces recover the usual holonomy situation.

This correction is important cross-sectorally: physical chains, asymptotic
states, \(i\epsilon\) prescriptions, and marked boundaries can be precisely
the framing data that turns transport into an observable.
