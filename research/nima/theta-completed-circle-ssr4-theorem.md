# Completed circle spectral kernel is globally SSR4

## Result

For Grothendieck's completed-circle spectral kernel

\[
b(t,x)=t^{5/4}x(2\pi tx-3)e^{-\pi tx},
\qquad t,x\ge1,
\]

all ordered minors through order four have the strict reverse-sign-regular
pattern

\[
\operatorname{sgn}\det[b(t_i,x_j)]_{i,j=1}^k
=(-1)^{k(k-1)/2},
\qquad k=1,2,3,4.
\]

Orders one through three were proved in the preceding gates. The new
fourth-order sign is positive.

## Fourth derivative column

Set \(y=\pi t\) and remove positive row and column factors. For

\[
f_y(x)=(2yx-3)e^{-yx},
\]

the derivative columns through order three are

\[
\begin{aligned}
p_0(y)&=2xy-3,\\
p_1(y)&=5y-2xy^2,\\
p_2(y)&=2xy^3-7y^2,\\
p_3(y)&=9y^3-2xy^4.
\end{aligned}
\]

Every column contains only degrees \(j\) and \(j+1\). In the alternant
determinant, any choice that repeats a degree vanishes. The only surviving
choices switch once from the lower to the upper degree, leaving five terms.

## Alternant factorization

For \(y_1<y_2<y_3<y_4\), the Wronskian factors as

\[
W_4
=e^{-x\sum_i y_i}
\prod_{i<j}(y_j-y_i)\,P_4(z_1,z_2,z_3,z_4),
\]

where \(z_i=xy_i\) and

\[
\boxed{
P_4
=945-210e_1(z)+60e_2(z)-24e_3(z)+16e_4(z).
}
\]

The five coefficients come from the five nonrepeating staircase selections:

\[
945,\quad -210,\quad 60,\quad -24,\quad 16.
\]

## Global sign

Differentiate with respect to \(z_1\), writing \(a,b,c\) for the remaining
three coordinates:

\[
\frac{\partial P_4}{\partial z_1}
=-210+60(a+b+c)-24(ab+ac+bc)+16abc.
\]

Its derivative with respect to \(a\) is

\[
60-24(b+c)+16bc.
\]

For \(b,c\ge3\), this is minimized at \(b=c=3\) and equals \(60>0\).
Therefore \(\partial P_4/\partial z_1\) is increasing in each of
\(a,b,c\), and its minimum is

\[
-210+60(9)-24(27)+16(27)=114>0.
\]

By symmetry, \(P_4\) is strictly increasing in every coordinate on
\(z_i\ge3\). Its minimum is

\[
P_4(3,3,3,3)
=945-210(12)+60(54)-24(108)+16(81)
=369>0.
\]

Since the source domain satisfies \(z_i=\pi tx_i\ge\pi>3\), the fourth
Wronskian is strictly positive everywhere.

Together with the nonzero Wronskians of orders one, two, and three, the
extended-complete-Chebyshev criterion gives the claimed positive sign for
every ordered fourth-order evaluation determinant.

## Verification and boundary

The exact checker verifies:

- the derivative-column formulas;
- the alternant factorization on a separating rational family;
- the five staircase coefficients;
- the exact boundary values \(114\), \(60\), and \(369\);
- representative rational signs inside the chamber.

No floating-point scan is used as proof.

This establishes a source-derived \(\mathrm{SSR}_4\) law. It still does not
prove all-order sign regularity, a variation-diminishing theorem for the
infinite trace mixture, or RH.

## Next structural clue

The order-three and order-four Wronskian polynomials are alternating
elementary-symmetric sums with coefficients generated by the two-term
derivative staircase. The next useful move is to derive the general
\(k\)-formula and decide whether its sign on \(z_i\ge3\) follows by induction.
That would either prove all-order sign regularity or produce a finite first
order of failure.
