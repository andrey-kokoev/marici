# Many-many prime-loaded pro-limit

Let `X_p` be the six-coordinate prime feature and let `omega_p` be a label-diagonal loading satisfying

$$
\sum_p|\omega_p|(1+(\log p)^{M_j})<\infty
$$

for every compact-local jet order `j`. Then

$$
X^{\rm ar}=\sum_p\omega_pX_p
$$

converges in every compact-local feature seminorm, with parameter derivatives commuting with the prime sum.

The oriented codiagonal satisfies

$$
C(X^{\rm ar})=\sum_p\omega_pC_pX_p.
$$

Consequently the prime-loaded many-many object is a well-defined projective relative analytic realization under the majorant. The construction supports each admitted loading separately; canonical loading selection remains a source/G4 datum.
