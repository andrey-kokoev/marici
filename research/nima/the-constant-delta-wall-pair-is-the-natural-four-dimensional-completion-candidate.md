# The constant-delta wall pair is the natural four-dimensional completion candidate

## Source-derived conjugate candidate

The programme already distinguishes a constant wall coordinate from a delta or zero-section wall coordinate. Fourier–Tate transport exchanges these two types, up to the frozen normalization and sign convention.

This makes

\[
(w_{\mathrm{const}},w_{\delta})
\]

the first source-native candidate conjugate pair for the dynamic wall channel.

Together with the two reciprocal tail coordinates, it gives the minimal even-dimensional state

\[
Y=
(G_+,G_-,w_{\mathrm{const}},w_{\delta})^{T}.
\]

No principal-value coordinate needs to be invented merely to satisfy symplectic parity.

## Candidate symplectic structure

In a provisional typed frame, the wall block should have the form

\[
\Omega_{\mathrm{wall}}
=
\beta
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-1\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\beta\neq0,
\]

where \(\beta\) and its sign must come from the source Fourier–Tate pairing.

The full four-dimensional form is not necessarily a direct sum:

\[
\Omega=
\begin{pmatrix}
\Omega_{\mathrm{tail}}&K\\
-K^{*}&\Omega_{\mathrm{wall}}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The cross block \(K\) records the actual incidence of the wall pair into reciprocal histories. Setting \(K=0\) by presentation choice would erase the potential repair mechanism.

## Required distinction

The Fourier exchange

\[
w_{\mathrm{const}}
\leftrightarrow
w_{\delta}
\]

does not by itself prove that these coordinates form a canonical pair in the Green geometry. The source must establish a nonzero antisymmetric pairing between them.

Three outcomes remain possible:

1. Symplectic pair: the wall block is nondegenerate and supplies the missing dynamic conjugate.
2. Dual but isotropic types: Fourier exchanges them, but their Green pairing vanishes; another partner is needed.
3. Presymplectic pair: the pairing exists only after quotient or endpoint attachment.

Thus Fourier covariance is evidence for typing, not authority for \(\Omega_{\mathrm{wall}}\).

## Spectral incidence gate

The full spectral symbol must be derived as

\[
\mathcal A_1=
\begin{pmatrix}
A_{tt}&A_{tw}\\
A_{wt}&A_{ww}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The earlier real-eigenpair no-go requires genuine coupling or constraint. Therefore at least one of

\[
A_{tw},\qquad A_{wt}
\]

must be source-nonzero unless the reciprocal tail modes are removed before the canonical quotient.

A constant-delta pair that lives entirely in \(A_0\) can close symplectic parity while still failing to repair spectral positivity.

## Finite certificate

The smallest useful source packet now contains exactly four typed coordinates and must report:

- the complete \(\Omega\);
- its Pfaffian or determinant;
- \(\mathcal A_0\) and \(\mathcal A_1\);
- \(\mathcal H=\Omega\mathcal A_1\);
- inertia and radical of \(\mathcal H\);
- boundary isotropy;
- the projection of the wall incidence onto coherent and disagreement tail modes.

The hostile swaps the Fourier-dual wall labels or reverses their orientation. Scalar wall energies remain unchanged, while the Pfaffian sign and reciprocal phase reverse.

## Reduced frontier

The dynamic canonical-system route has therefore contracted to a concrete source question:

> Does the independently defined constant-delta wall pair carry a nondegenerate Green symplectic pairing and nontrivial spectral incidence with the reciprocal tail pair?

If yes, the first complete \(4\times4\) positivity calculation is authorized. If not, the wall must remain an external energy port or acquire a different source-derived partner.
