# RH det3 anomaly coherence is divisor-blind

## Result

The order-three anomaly packet explains determinant composition but does not constrain the divisor of an independently supplied endpoint section.

Fix the same regularized bulk operator, the same primitive-square counterterm, and the same exact anomaly cocycle. Compare two endpoint readouts:

\[
F_0(z)=1,
\qquad
F_1(z)=1-z^2.
\]

The hostile section (F_1) preserves reflection symmetry (z\mapsto-z) and introduces zeros at (z=\pm1). Nothing in the determinant anomaly packet changes.

Therefore anomaly coherence cannot establish zero confinement unless the endpoint readout is itself derived from the same boundary-bearing normal form.

\[
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{source}}
\longrightarrow

\[

It must be a natural isomorphism on each open half-plane and remain invertible under completion. Its component cannot be defined as the quotient of the two scalar sections, because that merely restates divisor agreement.

It identified:

- the typing of primitive and square currents as anomaly data;
- the mixed two-prime comparison cell;
- automatic higher coherence of those cells;
- the two independent completion gates.

It did not derive the endpoint observation functor. Hence it supplies provenance and a precise interface, not RH orientation.
