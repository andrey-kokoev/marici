# A Physical Covector Frames Transport into a Period

For transport \(T:V_{\rm in}\to V_{\rm out}\), a scalar observable requires an
input state and output covector:

\[
\operatorname{Per}_{\ell}(v)=\ell_{\rm out}Tv_{\rm in}.
\]

Under independent endpoint gauges,

\[
T\mapsto Q_{\rm out}TQ_{\rm in}^{-1},
\quad
v\mapsto Q_{\rm in}v,
\quad
\ell\mapsto\ell Q_{\rm out}^{-1},
\]

the period is invariant. Holding the covector fixed while changing the output
frame gives a different scalar.

Therefore the source-normalized Leray covector has a precise architectural
role: it is the physical output framing that converts coefficient transport
into an observable period. It cannot reopen singular-support questions already
settled by monodromy; it selects which transported coefficient directions the
physical chain reads.
