## Lift trilemma

Every proposed higher boundary module falls into one of three cases.

### Decoupled lift

If

\[
b=c=0,
\]

the extra states do not affect the scalar divisor. Their positivity or small gain cannot confine the zeros of \(x\).

### One-way triangular lift

If one of \(b,c\) vanishes, the determinant factorizes without two-way feedback through the added state. The added carrier may record provenance or observability, but it cannot generate a new closed-loop orientation mechanism for the scalar boundary state.

### Coupled lift

\[

\]
