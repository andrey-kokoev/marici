# Many-many relative pro-limit

The directed finite positive-edge system defines a relative pro-object

$$
\{\mathcal E_{L,F,N}\}_{(L,F,N)\in\mathfrak I_S^{\rm phys}}.
$$

Its projective limit consists of compatible coordinate families

$$
(e_{L,F,N})
$$

whose finite restrictions agree under the source-labelled refinement maps. Each coordinate carries the finite positive relative realization, while the limiting boundary readout is evaluated projectively.

A cofinal diagonal realizes every countable compatible family by a sequence of finite positive stages. The pro-limit topology uses finite-coordinate seminorms and retains the endpoint sector.

A single ordinary Hilbert realization requires an additional uniform norm estimate. The relative pro-limit itself requires only compatibility of the finite coordinates.

Status: many-many relative pro-limit defined; physical scalar pushout and uniform Hilbert realization remain optional upgrades.
