# Many-many corrected theta-port Green pencil

Retain the theta forcing as an independent port:

$$
(A-z)u=V1+B_\Sigma x,
$$

with `V1=Phi` and `B_Sigma` the injective centred arithmetic incidence. The lower source equation is

$$
B_\Sigma^\dagger u+D_U(z)x=0.
$$

The natural Xi history uses the distinguished state `x=0`, so its promotion condition is

$$
B_\Sigma^\dagger u_z=0.
$$

A modified history may use a nonzero source state `x_z`, provided it is generated by a divisor-preserving chain comparison and satisfies

$$
B_\Sigma^\dagger u_z+D_U(z)x_z=0.
$$

This pencil respects the no-exact-lift theorem: `Phi` remains in the independent theta port and is not forced into `ran(B_Sigma)`.
