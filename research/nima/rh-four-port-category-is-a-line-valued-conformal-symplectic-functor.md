# The Four-Port RH Category Is a Line-Valued Conformal Symplectic Functor

## Source category

Let \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{src}}\) be the typed partial category generated by
labelled Euler blocks, the archimedean block, cutoff extension, and reciprocal
sewing. Its objects are finite source stages. Its arrows are admitted source
constructions, not arbitrary equalities between completed scalar functions.

Each multiplicative source block \(g\) has a first jet

\[
j^1g=(g,g').
\]

The product law is

\[
(g,g')(h,h')=(gh,g'h+gh').
\]

This is associative and supplies the relevant arrow algebra.

## Boundary transport category

Let \(E_X\) be the two-dimensional seam value–flux object at source stage
\(X\), and let \(L_X\) be its one-dimensional determinant line. The boundary
form is line-valued:

\[
\omega_X:\bigwedge^2E_X\longrightarrow L_X.
\]

A transport arrow consists of

\[
(S_g,\ell_g):(E_X,L_X,\omega_X)
\longrightarrow(E_Y,L_Y,\omega_Y),
\]

with

\[
\omega_Y(S_gu,S_gv)=\ell_g\omega_X(u,v).
\]

Grothendieck's finite source calculation gives

\[
S_g=
\begin{pmatrix}
g&0\\
g'&g
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\ell_g=g^2.
\]

Moreover,

\[
S_gS_h=S_{gh},
\qquad
\ell_g\ell_h=\ell_{gh}.
\]

Thus the assignment from source constructors to value–flux transports is a
functor into a line-valued conformal symplectic category. The determinant-line
coherencer is part of the morphism type, not another state observer.

## Normalized transport

Dividing the transport by its authorized nonzero multiplier gives

\[
g^{-1}S_g=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0\\
g'/g&1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The remaining shear is additive under composition because

\[
\frac{(gh)'}{gh}=\frac{g'}g+\frac{h'}h.
\]

Categorically, the first-jet transport separates into two route effects:

- a multiplicative determinant-line character \(g^2\);
- an additive logarithmic-derivative shear \(g'/g\).

This explains the arithmetic filtration structurally. For an Euler block, the
logarithm of the square character expands into primitive, prime-square, and
higher connected currents. Those currents are coefficients of arrow
composition, not separately positive objects.

## The two-sector Ubersector

The two half-plane source categories give reciprocal functors

\[
F_+,F_-:\mathcal C_{\mathrm{src}}
\longrightarrow\mathcal{CSp}_{\mathrm{line}}.
\]

Reciprocal sewing is a typed comparison between these functors. It exchanges
the tail orientation and reverses seam flux while preserving the total
four-port pseudoscalar. The complete Ubersector is therefore a glued or
comma-type object containing:

- the positive-sector functor;
- the reciprocal-sector functor;
- the seam boundary object;
- the sewing comparison;
- the determinant-line coherence carried by each arrow.

It is not a single object obtained by identifying the two sectors.

## Scalar completion is a nonfaithful functor

The completed scalar readout is a projection from this structured category to
complex-valued functions. It forgets the odd tail channel, seam flux, higher
Clifford grades, and parts of the continuation law. Equal scalar images need not
be equal Ubersector objects.

This is why functional symmetry and pseudoscalar coherence alone cannot prove
RH. They establish a well-typed source functor but do not establish that its
distinguished scalar projection is nonzero away from the seam.

## Categorical RH gate

The missing theorem can now be stated without geometric ambiguity. Construct a
source-derived line-valued Clifford or conformal-symplectic functor and a
distinguished boundary state. Then prove that, in either open half-plane, a
zero scalar readout would force a forbidden degeneration of the full structured
state or violate the functorial boundary relation.

The hostile multiplier remains decisive: if it changes scalar zeros while
preserving the same categorical data, the chosen category is still too coarse.
The next invariant must therefore be sensitive to the distinguished state and
its sequential source orbit, not merely to the abstract transport functor.
