\[

\]

physical activation.  The physical question is typed only after lifting the
response into the minimal sewn relative extension:

\[

\longrightarrow
\mathcal E_{\rm sew}

\mathcal P_{\rm unsplit}.
\]

Thus the decisive alternatives are:

1. the odd jet fails to define a class in \(\mathcal E_{\rm sew}\);
2. it defines a class but dies under source sewing;
3. it survives sewing and gives a nonzero physical response.
