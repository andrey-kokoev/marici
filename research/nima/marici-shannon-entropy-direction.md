# Marici--Shannon entropy direction

## Epistemic origin

The Operator reports sensing that Shannon entropy is somehow inherent in and
can manifest from Marici. This is a non-evidential search-direction stimulus.

## Weakest conjecture

Shannon entropy is not a scalar primitive of the bare Carrier. It manifests
whenever a sector construction derives:

1. a finite exhaustive outcome algebra;
2. a positive normalized state on that algebra;
3. canonical coarse-graining;
4. product composition for independent readouts.

On this domain, the continuous symmetric grouping-additive valuation is

\[
H(p)=-\sum_i p_i\log p_i.
\]

The Marici burden is to derive the probability object and its maps. Invoking
the Shannon formula after importing probabilities does not establish Carrier
inherence.

\[

\frac{\Phi_1|00\rangle+\Phi_2|11\rangle}
{\sqrt{|\Phi_1|^2+|\Phi_2|^2}},
\]

\[

The exact checker verifies product additivity and the grouping identity. It
also retains the relative-support obstruction: scalar/fair acceptance
preserves the normalized distribution, whereas outcome-dependent acceptance
changes it. Entropy therefore belongs to the supported physical readout, not
merely to an absolute amplitude vector.

## Fourth result: the shared interface is projective-positive

The two sectors do not, however, share the category of stochastic maps at

Flavor permutations and transition matrices occupy its trace-preserving
part; accepted-event Bell filters use the larger projective-positive part.
Shannon entropy is a valuation of the terminal probability state, with its
grouping law typed by deterministic partitions and conditional fiber states.
It is not monotone under every admitted positive constructor.

## Sixth result: entropy is the refinement cocycle

The positive and negative sectors isolate the additional structure:
operationally typed alternatives and their source-derived refinements. For a
finite refinement \(\Omega'\to\Omega\), Shannon's grouping identity is

\[
H(\Omega',p')
=H(\Omega,p)
+\sum_{\omega\in\Omega}p(\omega)H(\Omega'_\omega,p'_\omega).
\]

The exact four-leaf audit verifies that this increment is associative under
different refinement parenthesizations. The strongest surviving inherence
claim is therefore not that the Carrier stores an entropy scalar, but that
Marici may generate a coherent refinement calculus of distinguishable
alternatives, on which Shannon entropy is the universal additive cocycle.

Scattering and flavor currently instantiate the terminal finite-outcome
stage. Cosmology supplies a positive ray but no operational refinement object,
and hence serves as the negative control rather than a third positive
instance.

## Ninth result and correction: effects require an instrument completion

Effects specify probabilities but not state change. In scattering and flavor,
the formally available projective instruments

\[
\mathcal I_i(\rho)=E_i\rho E_i
\]

produce normalized outcome probabilities and repeatable conditional states.
They show what a read/write completion would require, but neither physical
source currently selects these particular state updates.

The exact diagnostic-refinement control is decisive. For
\(E=E_0+E_1\), direct coarse interaction and fine interaction followed by
forgetting have the same coarse probability, but the former preserves
within-block coherence while the latter destroys it. Hence

\[
\boxed{
\text{same public probability record}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{same physical interaction}.}
\]

The common conjectural target is a capability-indexed instrument surface
containing preparations, effects, state-transforming channels, public records,
and diagnostic or side-channel refinements. Observable implementation detail
can be accessible to a stronger capability without belonging to the stable
public semantic surface.

The Deutsch-style attack therefore downgrades the cross-sector evidence:
shared source-derived effect algebras are established; shared physical
instruments are not. Entry 1576 explicitly leaves detector conditionalization
outside the amplitude trace, and the flavor packet contains no measurement
backaction. The next theorem must derive an update map from source physics
rather than choose a Lüders completion.
