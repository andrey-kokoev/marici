# Eight-point local versus global weight relations

## Question

Does imposing rank-one coefficient factorization on every eight-point facet exhaust global channel-scale compatibility?

## Claim boundary

This is one exact finite stage. It does not establish a formula for the nonlocal quotient or provide source coefficients.

The octagon has twenty channel facets and 132 triangulations, so the global incidence left kernel has rank 112. For every cut, anchored `2 by 2` minors generate the logarithmic rank-one relations of the corresponding regional product.

The cut types have regional triangulation counts `1 by 42`, `2 by 14`, `5 by 5`, and their reverses. They generate respectively zero, thirteen, sixteen, and thirteen anchored relations per facet. Across all twenty facets, 168 generated rows have exact rank ninety-six. Therefore the quotient of global relations by all codimension-one local gluing relations has dimension

\[
112-96=16.
\]

Combined with prior exact stages, nonlocal quotient dimensions are `0,2,7,16` for `n=5,6,7,8`. No formula is inferred from these four values.

## Disposition

At eight points, sixteen global coefficient compatibility classes remain invisible to every facet rank-one test. Local residue factorization becomes more constraining but still does not replace the full incidence-binomial gate.
