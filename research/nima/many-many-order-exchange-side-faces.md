# Many-many order-exchange side faces

Let `r_in`, `r_out`, `r_end`, and `r_wj` denote restriction maps for input arity, output arity, endpoint attachment, and wall/jump transport. The order-exchange cell must satisfy the four naturality equations

$$
 r_{\rm in}\beta=\beta_{\rm in}r_{\rm in},
\qquad
 r_{\rm out}\beta=\beta_{\rm out}r_{\rm out},
$$

$$
 r_{\rm end}\beta=\beta_{\rm end}r_{\rm end},
\qquad
 T_\theta\beta=\beta^{\rm wall}T_\theta.
$$

Each equation is checked on the common labelled source core, then extended by graph closure. The four equations form the side faces of the Gray coherence cube.

The cube filler requires the two composite side-face routes to agree on every codimension-two intersection. This gives the first nontrivial higher coherence test after defining `beta`.
