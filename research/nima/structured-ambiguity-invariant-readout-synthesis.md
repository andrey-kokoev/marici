# Structured ambiguity and invariant readout

## Status

Cross-sector synthesis by marici.Nima. The flavor and radiative statements
below are supported by finite checkers. The extrapolation to the shared

## The correction

The earlier shorthand

\[
\text{shared object}+\text{sector-specific lens}+\text{physical readout}
\]

is too static. In the two newest examples, the sector does not supply a
single coefficient object followed by a scalar evaluation. It supplies:

1. a space of admissible presentations;
2. typed transformations among presentations;
3. an invariant algebra or supported quotient on which readout is defined.

The refined architecture is

\[
\boxed{
\text{Carrier}
\longrightarrow
\bigl(\text{presentation groupoid/derived correspondence},
      \text{invariant readout algebra}\bigr)_s .
}
\]

“Groupoid” is schematic here. Depending on the sector, the correct object
may instead be a torsor, branch correspondence, localization triangle, or
derived relative category.

## Control example: flavor chart atlas (not an admitted sector)

At one fixed physical flavor point, the nine-link texture atlas contains at
least eighteen loop-phase values, including four same-chart doublets. WP9
shows that every chart has the same unit winding architecture for the CKM
quartics, while the unmatched \(0.817882/0.819271\) cores are
nonperturbative chart branches whose complete phase-parametrized physical
curves agree across several support orbits.

The WP10 permutation gate then falsifies the simplest deck explanation:
none of the four same-chart doublets is an exact permutation of the nonzero
magnitudes in both Yukawa sectors, and the four optimal assignments have four
different signatures. Thus the physical fiber is not the orbit of one
global coordinate involution. Its next typed model is a chart-dependent
algebraic branch correspondence.

What survives is not a preferred texture coordinate, phase, or magnitude
vector. It is the weak-basis-invariant physical readout together with the
nontrivial fiber of presentations above it.

This does **not** admit flavor as a fourth Marici sector. Entry 1077 proves
that the essential nine-link sparse data does not descend through the full
weak-basis quotient; the descended object is standard flavor physics rather
than a new Carrier/coefficient calculus. Flavor is used here as a sharp
control showing that structured presentation ambiguity can exist without
sector admission.

\[

This is a useful negative control: an invariant Grassmannian object can exist
even when a preferred rank-one readout does not.

## Cosmological strengthening: coefficients vary over background strata

Entries 1443--1469 sharpen the word "sector" in a way the earlier synthesis
did not capture.  The cosmological coefficient is not one object over a fixed
base.  It can be a constructible family over a stratified space of background
histories:

\[
\boxed{
\text{occurrence-resolved carrier over a stratified background base}
+
\text{relative coefficient objects and descent maps}.
}
\]

Three independent mechanisms now support this refinement.

First, Big-Bang endpoint Fourier--Laplace transform produces irregular/Stokes
data before pushforward and ordinary Kummer data afterward.  Its resonant
collision with a propagator Gysin wall creates a free line plus one
Rees-torsion class on an existing support; the source positive chain activates
the logarithmic class.

Second, arbitrary time-dependent couplings are vertexwise Fourier coefficient
pushforwards.  Compact support and internal jumps form an oriented cellular
boundary complex, while a sharp time feature is an evaluation coefficient.
A sudden era change instead requires gluing coefficient objects across a
declared background stratum.

Third, perturbative-mass white sites retain every adjacent edge occurrence
before the source momentum-conservation diagonal.  A chain of \(r\) white
sites has the path-incidence diagonal matrix of rank \(r\), and its \(r+1\)
simple factors specialize strictly to \((2y)^{-(r+1)}\).  The visible higher
pole is specialization multiplicity, not a new carrier wall.

The refined system is therefore closer to a fibered relative architecture
than to a fixed carrier with interchangeable coefficient modules:

\[
\boxed{
\text{carrier calculus}
\longrightarrow
\text{background strata}
\longrightarrow
\text{constructible coefficient categories}
\longrightarrow
\text{supported Betti readouts}.
}
\]

## Explanatory proposal

The shared phenomenon is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{physical information}
=
\text{invariant or supported readout of a structured ambiguity object}.
}
\]

This explains why repeatedly selecting preferred representatives,
splittings, primitive lifts, phases, or finite parts has failed. Those
choices are generally not physical objects. The small physical object
appears only after retaining the transformation/coherence data and deriving
the invariant algebra, homotopy fiber, or relative-support quotient.

## Falsifiers

The synthesis weakens if any admitted sector exhibits a source-derived,
globally canonical representative whose physical predictions cannot be
recovered as invariants or supported readouts of the corresponding
transformation structure.

The stronger Carrier conjecture fails if two independently grounded sectors
require incompatible notions of admissible transformation with no common
localization/coherence calculus, or if their readout algebras must be inserted
externally rather than derived from source-defined maps.
