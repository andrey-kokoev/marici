# Six-point NMHV realization conjecture: critical replacement

## Problem

The proposed conjecture conflated a replication benchmark with a new explanatory theorem and assigned categorical structure that has not been constructed.

## Defects in the prior statement

1. A positroid-cell incidence category and a category of physical factorization divisors were named without defining objects, morphisms, identities, or composition.
2. A functor between those categories cannot itself push forward differential forms. Form pushforward requires a geometric map with dimension, orientation, and degree data.
3. Positroid incidence alone does not determine a canonical form. The positive geometry, its embedding through generic positive external data, orientation, and normalization are required.
4. “Physical poles are precisely unpaired boundary images” was too strong. The boundary-to-physics dictionary must distinguish factorization and collinear loci and exclude boundaries killed or identified by the embedding.
5. Equality with the published six-point NMHV tree expression and triangulation independence are established targets in the source theory. Rechecking them is a replication result, not a new physical conjecture.
6. Label forgetting need not destroy every cancellation proof; rational identities may still prove cancellation. The relevant claim is that label forgetting destroys the proposed local boundary explanation.
7. A single hostile orientation mutation tests an implementation, not the universality or uniqueness of the explanation.

## Replacement conjecture

Fix one explicit bosonized six-point NMHV tree positive geometry with generic positive external data, one orientation convention, and two explicitly declared triangulations. There is a chain-level canonical-form assignment on the cells used by those triangulations such that taking an oriented boundary corresponds to taking the associated logarithmic residue. Consequently, paired internal facets cancel in each triangulation sum. After a separately verified boundary dictionary, the remaining external residues agree with the expected factorization or collinear residues, and both sums equal the same published six-point NMHV tree form.

The explanatory content is bounded: for this benchmark, spurious-pole cancellation has a local oriented-boundary certificate. It does not assert that all rational identities among representations are generated by internal-facet pairings.

## Named rivals

1. The two formulas agree algebraically, but at least one spurious cancellation lacks a paired internal-facet certificate.
2. The cell decomposition is correct, but the canonical-form residue signs do not define the proposed chain map.
3. The geometric sums agree, but the external-boundary dictionary fails for at least one claimed physical residue.
4. The selected published expression uses a convention or normalization not supplied by the geometric packet.

## Risky consequences

For each triangulation, the packet must provide every oriented top cell, every codimension-one facet, incidence signs, and the cell canonical form. Every internal facet must occur with net coefficient zero. Every surviving facet must be classified through an explicit bosonization and kinematic dictionary. The summed forms from the two triangulations must agree exactly and match the cited expression under one recorded normalization. A deliberately flipped incidence sign must leave the predicted nonzero residue.

## Strongest falsification attempt

Build the complete signed facet table before simplifying the rational forms. Reject the conjecture if one internal facet is unpaired, one paired residue has equal rather than opposite sign, one surviving boundary lacks the stated physical interpretation, or the two normalized sums differ. Algebraic equality without the facet table supports replication but rejects the local-boundary explanation.

## Tested disposition

The conjecture passes for this benchmark. Both triangulations have complete signed facet tables; their internal facets cancel; their external boundaries agree; the bosonic and super five-bracket identities hold generically; orientation mutations leave nonzero residuals; and the nine external facets map bijectively to the source-listed physical poles. In homogeneous barycentric coordinates, the Poincare residue of each simplex form equals the facet canonical form with the simplicial incidence sign. This is verified for all 30 incidences, while the hostile normal-first convention fails all 30 tests. The result establishes a residue-compatible cellular realization for this finite tree fixture only.
