# The typed grade-six diamond is undefined until four Adams edge maps exist

## Scope correction

The source-extracted grade-six geometric-coefficient complex is contractible:
\[
1\to2\to6,
\qquad
1\to3\to6,
\]
with
\[
H_1=0,
\qquad
\pi_1=1.
\]

This proves that the local geometric-coefficient packet contributes no Wilson sector.

It does not define a type-fiber holonomy. The four type-fiber Adams maps have not been constructed, so the typed path composites are currently undefined.

### State I: geometric cell exists

The moving-seam and Euler coefficient transports exist and satisfy
\[
\Psi^{gc}_{3;p,2}\Psi^{gc}_{2;p,1}
=
\Psi^{gc}_{2;p,3}\Psi^{gc}_{3;p,1}.
\]

The corresponding cellular diamond is filled and contractible.

### State II: typed edges exist, filler unresolved

All four maps
\[

\]

Only in this state is an ordered residual or holonomy meaningful.

### State III: typed edges absent

At least one of the four maps is not source-constructed. The path comparison is ill-formed or undefined.

The present source status is State III.

## Forbidden premature residual

The expression
\[
\Omega
=
(A_{2;p,3}A_{3;p,1})^{-1}
A_{3;p,2}A_{2;p,1}
\]
requires:

- all four maps;
- invertibility of the reference composite;
- common source and target typing;
- authorized composition.

Without these hypotheses, \(\Omega\) is not zero, nonzero, trivial, or nontrivial. It is undefined.

When maps are noninvertible, the comparison must be made directly between the two composites rather than through \(\Omega\).

Each edge record must include:

## First typed comparison

Once the maps exist, form
\[
C_L=A_{3;p,2}A_{2;p,1},
\qquad
C_R=A_{2;p,3}A_{3;p,1}.
\]

Both maps must have type
\[
F_{p,1}\to F_{p,6}.
\]

Before comparing values, verify that the compositions use the same:

- target topology;
- sheet;
- endpoint/seam port;
- grade-six operator class;
- phase and orientation;
- cutoff and source-energy version.

Failure here is ill-typed composition, not holonomy.

## Outcome classifier

### Literal equality

\[
C_L=C_R.
\]
The type lift is strict on the grade-six square.

### Authorized 2-cell

There is a source-derived intertwiner
\[
\eta:C_L\Rightarrow C_R
\]
preserving every external interface.

The square is coherent but not strict.

### Legitimate unitary cocycle

The intertwiner is nontrivial and unitary in the source metric. It may encode an authorized coefficient lens.

Its compatibility with higher cells remains to be checked.

### Metric-preserving ordered holonomy

When the paths are invertible and close to a common fiber, the residual is nontrivial but preserves one positive source-authorized metric.

This is a legitimate local logical coefficient, not geometric topology.

### Metric-distorting obstruction

The residual fails
\[
\Omega^*G\Omega=G
\]
for every authorized positive metric, or has determinant/spectral/Jordan obstruction.

### Ill-typed composition

The composites do not inhabit the same hom-space. No equality or holonomy claim is meaningful.

## Source candidates and nonclaims

The current records identify three grade classes:

- primitive \(k=1\);
- square \(k=2\);
- connected \(k\ge3\).

They do not provide canonical identifications among their type fibers.

Potential source data may come from:

- primitive incidence operators;
- Hilbert–Schmidt square channels;
- nuclear connected channels;
- functorial maps induced by an explicit Fock or divisor constructor.

Equality of reassembled analytic vectors cannot define the missing maps because it forgets operator-ideal typing.

## Minimal source theorem

> For each prime \(p\), there exist source-authorized type fibers at grades \(1,2,3,6\) and four continuous or appropriately closable Adams maps with the declared domain/codomain typing. Their two grade-six composites are joined by a source-authorized interface-preserving 2-cell.

This theorem moves the packet from State III to State II and, if the filler is included, to a typed coherent State I analogue.

## Checker order

1. fibers exist;
2. four edge maps exist;
3. edge maps type-check;
4. two composites type-check;
5. compare strict equality;
6. otherwise inspect authorized filler;
7. classify its metric behavior;
8. only then form any residual holonomy.

A checker must reject “nontrivial holonomy” as a category error when steps 1–4 fail.

## Deferred attachments

Endpoint, archimedean, reciprocal-sheet, and global sewing cells remain excluded until the typed grade-six diamond is constructed.

Those later attachments may create or kill global cycles. They do not alter the present conclusion that the local geometric-coefficient diamond is contractible and the typed residual is undefined.
