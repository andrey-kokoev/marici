# The soft disagreement defines a closed unbounded Adams relation with a trace-class bounded mate

## Prime-labelled spaces

Let

\[
\Theta
=
\ell^2(\mathbb P)
\]

be the normalized theta odd-port coordinate space.

Let

\[
\mathcal W_{\mathrm{dis}}
=
\bigoplus_{p}
\operatorname{span}\{d_p\}
\]

with the inherited Stieltjes norm, where

\[
d_p=W_{2\log p}-W_{\log p}.
\]

Each \(d_p\neq0\).

## Bounded incidence

Define

\[
J:\Theta\to\mathcal W_{\mathrm{dis}},
\qquad
Je_p=d_p.
\]

Its singular values are \(\|d_p\|_\nu\), so

\[
J\in\mathfrak S_1.
\]

In particular, \(J\) is bounded, compact, and injective.

Its range is dense: every finite-support vector in
\(\mathcal W_{\mathrm{dis}}\) lies in \(\operatorname{ran}J\), and
finite-support vectors are dense.

## Uniform contraction

Pointwise,

\[
|d_p(q)|<1
\]

for every finite \(p\) and almost every \(q\), so

\[
\|d_p\|_\nu<1.
\]

Also

\[
\|d_p\|_\nu\to0.
\]

Therefore the supremum is achieved among finitely many initial primes and

\[
\eta
=
\sup_p\|d_p\|_\nu
<1.
\]

Hence

\[
\|J\|=\eta<1.
\]

The bounded theta-to-window incidence has a genuine uniform contraction
margin

\[
\delta_J=1-\eta>0.
\]

## Closed inverse relation

Define the inverse operator

\[
A=J^{-1}:
\operatorname{ran}J
\subset
\mathcal W_{\mathrm{dis}}
\longrightarrow
\Theta.
\]

This is unbounded because the singular values of \(J\) tend to zero.

It is nevertheless closed. If

\[
w_n\to w
\quad\text{in }\mathcal W_{\mathrm{dis}},
\qquad
Aw_n\to c
\quad\text{in }\Theta,
\]

then

\[
Jc
=
\lim JAw_n
=
\lim w_n
=
w.
\]

Thus \(w\in\operatorname{ran}J\) and \(Aw=c\).

Since \(\operatorname{ran}J\) is dense, \(A\) is densely defined and
closed.

## Graph form

The analytic Adams relation is

\[
\Gamma_A
=
\{(Jc,c):c\in\Theta\}
\subset
\mathcal W_{\mathrm{dis}}\oplus\Theta.
\]

Read from theta to window, it is the bounded trace-class map \(J\). Read
from window to theta, it is the closed unbounded operator \(A\).

This is exactly the directional asymmetry demanded by the completion audit.

## Mixed form

The bounded mate defines

\[
b(w,c)
=
\langle w,Jc\rangle_{\mathcal W_{\mathrm{dis}}}.
\]

Then

\[
|b(w,c)|
\le
\eta\|w\|\|c\|.
\]

Thus radical annihilation and domination are automatic on the reduced
disagreement spaces, and the Riesz factor is the strict contraction \(J\).

The inverse relation need not satisfy a bounded domination estimate in the
opposite direction.

## Prime cutoff

Let \(J_X=JP_X\). Then

\[
J_X\to J
\]

in trace norm. The finite graph relations converge to \(\Gamma_A\) in the
sense of their bounded mates and graph resolvents.

No cutoff-dependent normalization is required.

## Arithmetic direction qualification

The coefficient-level Adams label arrow may point primitive-to-square. The
analytic defect relation \(A\) points from the soft window disagreement
toward the normalized theta port and is unbounded, while its mate \(J\)
points backward and is bounded.

The final constructor must declare whether the arithmetic arrow is realized
by \(A\), \(A^*\), or the unoriented graph relation. This cannot be decided
from boundedness alone.

## What this closes

An analytically valid first type-edge candidate now exists on the global
disagreement sector:

- bounded strict contraction in one direction;
- closed densely defined inverse in the other;
- trace-class completion;
- cutoff-natural graph.

## Remaining source gate

The relation is built from the source window disagreement and normalized
theta odd coordinate. To promote it to the Adams edge, prove that its mixed
form is exactly the pullback of the curvature-completed Green/Stokes form,
including the source Euler coefficients and reciprocal sign.

## Hostile

Reject \(A\) because it is unbounded and normalize every \(d_p\) to force a
bounded inverse. This destroys trace class and the source completion
topology.

## Frontier

The topology no longer blocks the first Adams relation:

\[
\mathcal W_{\mathrm{dis}}
\xrightarrow{\ J^{-1}\ }
\Theta
\]

is a closed unbounded realization with trace-class bounded mate \(J\).

Only constructor identification of its mixed form remains.
