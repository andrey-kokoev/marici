# Adelic Height Fixes the Clock, Not the Boundary Morphism

## Closed gate

For a nonzero rational label,

\[
H(r)=\prod_v\max(1,|r|_v)
\]

satisfies \(H(r^{-1})=H(r)\). On positive integers, \(H(n)=n\).
Therefore direct labels and reciprocal labels have one canonical cofinal
cutoff. Arbitrary independent power clocks are not source-authorized.

This is a genuine closure: the regulator synchronization is supplied by the
product formula rather than fitted to the critical line.

## Remaining distinction

A common filtered base does not force a morphism over it to have degree zero.
At cutoff \(N\), the direct and reciprocal leading boundary lines have sizes

\[
N^{1-\sigma},
\qquad
N^\sigma,
\]

where \(\sigma=\operatorname{Re}s\). The algebraic comparison is invertible
at every finite cutoff for every \(0<\sigma<1\). Off the seam it simply has
nonzero filtration degree.

Thus three claims must remain separate:

1. the sectors share one cutoff object;
2. reciprocal sewing is algebraically invertible at every cutoff;
3. reciprocal sewing is a uniformly bounded degree-zero isomorphism after
   completion.

Adelic height proves the first. Scalar reciprocity and finite Fourier–Tate
transport can supply the second. The third is still the RH-bearing claim.

## Fourier–Tate warning

Fourier transformation naturally exchanges homogeneous distributions of
complementary degree. After centering by the half-density, the spectral grade
is reflected rather than fixed. Such transport is continuous in a rigged
distribution category even when it is not a bounded isomorphism between the
two cutoff-normalized Hilbert boundary lines.

Therefore source derivation of Fourier–Tate sewing does not automatically
prove the required bounded degree-zero action. The topology and associated
graded boundary object must be stated explicitly.

## Finite hostile system

Let the two boundary lines at cutoff \(N\) carry norms scaled by
\(N^{1-\sigma}\) and \(N^\sigma\). The unique map matching their distinguished
generators exists and is invertible for every finite \(N\). Its norm and the
norm of its inverse are uniformly bounded exactly when \(\sigma=1/2\).

Hence finite algebraic coherence cannot establish completion coherence. This
is the same finite-versus-uniform distinction already seen in observability
and pro-Gram continuity.

## Next exact audit

Construct the associated graded boundary action of the actual global
Fourier–Tate operator. Determine which of these verdicts holds:

- it kills the leading Euler class;
- it transports the class with nonzero degree;
- it induces a degree-zero uniformly invertible map only on the seam;
- an additional source current cancels the degree shift.

Only the last two verdicts retain the confinement route. The adelic clock is
now settled; the operator topology is not.
