# qRB microstep 43: prime common-mode obstruction

Prior research gives the actual large-prime decomposition

$$
 b_p=p^{-1/2}\phi+r_p,
 \qquad
 r_p=-p^{-1/2}\phi\mathbf 1_{(\log p,\infty)}.
$$

The common mode has squared synthesis norm

$$
\|\phi\|^2\sum_{p\le X}\frac1p,
$$

which diverges. Therefore the required cancellation condition cannot be ordinary operator cancellation on unweighted `ell^2` prime coefficients.

The common mode must instead be retained as a relative/distributional boundary functional, while only the residual `r_p` is subjected to a Hilbert–Schmidt or bounded synthesis estimate.

This validates the relative qRB target and rules out the naive uniform absolute prime observer.
