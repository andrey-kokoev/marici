# RH positive interval energy does not determine the exterior observer

## Correction of the immediate frontier

Finite vacuum transversality is already exact. The Mellin observer sends the
multiplicative vacuum to one, so no nonzero finite scalar-null packet is
supported only on that line. The native first-order tail operator also has a
bounded inverse on its range at fixed spectral parameter inside the strip.

The unresolved gate is therefore not another vacuum calculation and not
ordinary tail closed range. It is the construction of the exterior observer
as a source-authorized boundary morphism.

## Interior and exterior are different types

The polarized source kernel is positive on admitted interior states, and its
logarithmic derivative gives the full interval energy. The Riemann readout,
however, pairs an interior state with the formal constant observer. That
observer is not a vector in the coefficient Hilbert space.

This distinction has an exact two-label witness. Give the vacuum weight zero
and one nonvacuum label positive weight two. The state `(1,-1)` has positive
interval energy two but zero aggregation readout. Thus positive state energy
cannot forbid cancellation against the observer.

## Boundary-extension DPC

Adjoin one boundary coordinate to the interior state space. The functionals

\[
\beta_0(x,z)=\ell(x)
\]

and

\[
\beta_1(x,z)=\ell(x)+z
\]

agree on every interior state `(x,0)` but disagree on the boundary unit.
Consequently, agreement throughout the Euler or positive-kernel chamber does
not determine the extension once a genuinely new boundary carrier is
adjoined.

The source must therefore construct the comparison cell between admissible
approaches to the exterior observer. Analytic uniqueness of the scalar
continuation cannot substitute for this cell because it acts after the
boundary information has been compressed.

## Decisive constructor

The required object is a boundary morphism from the five-wall carrier to the
dual of the polarized interval kernel. It must:

1. restrict to the ordinary aggregation observer in its convergence chamber;
2. retain constant, delta, primitive, square, and archimedean boundary types;
3. intertwine reciprocal Fourier–Tate sewing;
4. have the same value and Green current along every source-authorized
   approach to the exterior observer;
5. send the completed scalar zero to the mixed boundary condition without
   manufacturing a labelled zero-state backward from that scalar.

The finite falsifier is two authorized boundary approaches that agree on all
interior states but differ on one retained wall coordinate. The completion
falsifier is a bounded interior sequence whose boundary currents have two
different limits.

## Cross-sector meaning

Kitaev's relative-framing result exhibits the same distinction: equal typed
endpoints can conceal a state-dependent process filter. Benincasa's two-edge
transport likewise cannot authorize cyclic composition until the independent
third edge is constructed. Here the missing datum is the comparison between
boundary approaches, not another diagonal energy estimate.

## Verdict

The positive interval tower has solved the interior arithmetic geometry. It
