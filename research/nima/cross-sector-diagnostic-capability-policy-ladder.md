# The diagnostic--capability--policy structure is a cross-sector ladder

Owner: `marici.Nima`

## Strict criterion

A sector realizes the proposed triple only if it independently supplies:

1. an operational diagnostic record generated by an instrument;
2. a fiber of admissible successor operations indexed or constrained by that
   record; and
3. a source-derived policy/dynamics selecting a section of the fiber.

Presentation ambiguity, fitted projection, or a mathematically available
completion does not count.

\[

\]

The numbers count completed stages, not sector quality.

## Sector details

### Scattering

The Cut supplies the event/no-event diagnostic effect.  The completions
`U sqrt(E_empty)` form a genuine successor-state fiber.  The oriented Cut and
its source boundary jet derive the elastic `S` channel, selecting the physical
section.  Laboratory implementation still requires wavepacket and exposure
normalization, so the claim stops at instrument density.

### Topology

The syndrome is an operational record and indexes a recovery fiber.  No
canonical section follows from it: recovery needs a source noise model,
metric/cost, boundary data, and history.  This is the clean capability-without-
policy example.

### Cosmology

The connected boundary-score insertion exposes a hidden contact response in
the original state.  It is a source-defined relational diagnostic port, but
no state-updating instrument or admissible successor family has been derived.
Calling the coefficient alternatives a capability fiber would erase this
missing operational arrow.

### Flavor

The faithful quotient and its fibers classify physical/presentation
ambiguity, but the declared source has no proper-image task or record-producing
instrument.  These are mathematical fibers, not diagnostic capability fibers.

## Surviving theorem

The cross-sector commonality is an **ordered lifting skeleton**:

```text
operational diagnostic
    -> admissible successor fiber
    <- source-derived selector
```

The audit rejects the stronger claim that every sector already possesses all
three objects.  The missing arrow is itself informative and predicts each
sector's next legitimate construction:

- topology: derive the decoder policy from a noise/dynamics source;
- cosmology: lift the relational response to a state-updating instrument;
- flavor: first derive a noninvertible task and its apparatus record;
- scattering: complete laboratory normalization and reusable resources.

This ladder refines `Carrier + lens + readout`: sector lenses are not free
parameters, but neither are they all yet projections of one completed common
machine.
