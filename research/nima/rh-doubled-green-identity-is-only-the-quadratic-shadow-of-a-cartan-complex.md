# The doubled Green identity is only the quadratic shadow of a Cartan complex

## What the current source calculation already supplies

The doubled theta-tail flow uses centered spectral coordinate `z` and signed
normal displacement

\[
a=\Re z.
\]

Its exact conservation identity is

\[
2a\mathcal N=-\partial_qJ-2\mathcal F.
\]

The independently derived two-variable reflection coboundary permits the
forcing decomposition

\[
2\mathcal F
=
\partial_qJ_{\mathrm{boundary}}
+2a\mathcal M.
\]

Therefore the completed quadratic form satisfies

\[
2a(\mathcal N+\mathcal M)
=
-\partial_q(J+J_{\mathrm{boundary}}).
\]

This has exactly the normal-divisibility required by the proposed
Cartan–Clifford mechanism. The term `a M` is not mixed-port leakage merely
because it remains in the bulk. It belongs in the completed number form.

## What it does not yet supply

A quadratic identity does not construct:

- a graded source module;
- a nilpotent differential `d`;
- a nilpotent contraction `Q`;
- an operator anticommutator `dQ+Qd`;
- a chain-level seam incidence;
- a determinant bridge.

Even a two-variable polarized form identifies only the resulting operator or
closed form. It does not factor that operator into two odd nilpotent source
constructors.

## Exact one-dimensional falsifier

On a one-dimensional ungraded space, let

\[
H=aI,
\qquad
N=I.
\]

Then

\[
\langle x,Hx\rangle=a\langle x,Nx\rangle
\]

holds exactly for every state. But the only nilpotent one-dimensional
operators are zero. Hence no nonzero factorization

\[
H=dQ+Qd
\]

with `d^2=Q^2=0` exists.

The quadratic normal factor is therefore necessary but insufficient evidence
for a Cartan complex. A graded enlargement is genuine new structure.

## Minimal graded promotion

On a `1|1` enlargement, the same scalar operator admits the factorization

\[
d_a=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&a\\
0&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
Q=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&0\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
d_aQ+Qd_a=aI.
\]

But this construction is not automatically authorized by the scalar energy
identity. The two graded states and both arrows must be identified in the
labelled theta/Tate source.

## Exact promotion gates

### Gate one: graded differential

Treat each augmented tail operator as an ordered source incidence from its
declared domain to its declared codomain. Any such arrow canonically defines a
two-term graded differential on the direct sum; its square is zero by degree.
The remaining gate is to establish the source domain, codomain, and completion
of that incidence. One must not instead square the ungraded tail operator with
itself.

### Gate two: contraction port

Construct the normal contraction independently from reciprocal sewing. It may
not be defined by inverting the completed Green operator.

### Gate three: operator polarization

Upgrade the two-variable reflection identity to a closed operator or form
identity on the labelled state module, before diagonal scalar expectation.

### Gate four: number-operator invertibility

Show that the completed form `N+M` defines a source-invertible number operator
on the relative bulk complex. Feature-level Gram positivity is insufficient if
the feature map has a state kernel or loses its lower margin at completion.

### Gate five: boundary as chain incidence

Realize `J+J_boundary` as the boundary term of the same graded Green identity,
with the seam carrier and ordered two-port incidence retained.

### Gate six: determinant bridge

Prove that cohomology of the resulting complex is equivalent to a zero of the
completed scalar section, without fitting the complex to that section.

## Revised finite falsifier

At each finite labelled cutoff, form the complete polarized residual

\[
R_X^{\mathrm{Cartan}}
=
\{d_X,Q_X\}
-2aN_X
-B_X,
\]

where `B_X` is the declared chain-level boundary incidence. Any nonzero typed
matrix block rejects the factorization. A scalar integrated cancellation is
not sufficient.

If no source definitions of `d_X` and `Q_X` exist, the verdict is not a failed
identity but an unconstructed graded promotion.

## Verdict

The actual doubled Green programme is compatible with the normal Koszul idea
and already supplies its quadratic shadow. It has not yet supplied the Cartan
complex. The next decisive step is not another positivity estimate; it is the
source-authorized graded factorization of the polarized Green operator and its
boundary current.
