# A bounded WG(D, WG) specialization pilot

`agda/MetaWitnessGenerator.agda` makes the proposed higher-level expression typed for a common state type `S` and relation `R`. The input `Compatible` retains both a desired domain structure (`compute`, admission predicate, and a declared law) and an existing base Witness Generator `G : (s:S) → Σ(t:S) R(s,t)`. It additionally retains the compatibility equality `fst (G s) = compute s` and an admission-closure map. These two proofs are required inputs; no match is inferred from identical carriers.

`meta-WG` is itself a `TypedGeneratorLayers.Layer1` over tagged requests and results. Applying it to a certified `(D,G)` produces a domain-specific Layer-1 generator on admitted states, together with a meta-witness that identifies the exact input pair and produced layer. On each admitted state, the resulting transition calls the **original** `G`, keeps its original `R`-witness, attaches its compatibility equality, and computes admission of the successor. The Boolean-negation instance is nonidentity and checks the transition from `false` to `true`; its declared double-negation law remains available as a certificate.

This validates a *relative specialization* reading of `WG(D,WG) = WG_D`, not a universal synthesis theorem: `D`, `G`, compatibility, closure and the law are supplied. The generated relation now includes `LawEvidence` for every declared law request whose source matches this step. `law-from-step` derives the expected output using the **actual generated edge witness**; `law-history` retains that edge in Layer-2's explicit history. The Boolean double-negation fixture checks both the law extracted from the step at `true` and its nonempty history. The law itself remains a supplied field of `D`; this does not establish all higher law coherence or native twelve-rule derivations. The new meta-WG raises the universe level, as its state contains types and generator functions.
