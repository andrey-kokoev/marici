# Many-many endpoint-flux normalization

In the Laplace readout identity

$$
\mathcal L_z(F_{nm})
=zR_{nm}(z)-\rho_{nm}(0)-\kappa_{nm}\partial_zR_{nm}(z),
$$

the term `rho_nm(0)` is the explicit endpoint coordinate. The term

$$
\kappa_{nm}\partial_zR_{nm}(z)
$$

records the pair-dependent radial flux generated by the linear-in-`t` potential.

Thus the endpoint package for the pair-response graph is

$$
B_{\rm end}(n,m;z)
=\bigl(\rho_{nm}(0),\kappa_{nm}\partial_zR_{nm}(z)\bigr).
$$

The direct and composite observer routes agree when they use this ordered pair of endpoint coordinates with the same signs.
