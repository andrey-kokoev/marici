# The weighted valuation cut cannot populate the odd Tate port

## Existing crossing

The source-derived arithmetic-to-analytic incidence sends a prime-power label to the theta cut atom

\[
\mathcal I e_{p,k}
=
\frac1k p^{-k/2}
\begin{pmatrix}
g_{k\log p}\\
h_{k\log p}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

This map retains:

- the prime label \(p\);
- the valuation grade \(k\);
- the Mellin displacement \(k\log p\);
- the Euler half-density;
- the tail–seam decomposition.

It does not retain a finite additive coordinate.

## Unit-orbit invariance

A valuation label identifies every element of one local unit orbit. If

\[
x=p^ku,
\qquad
u\in\mathbb Z_p^\times,
\]

then the valuation projection records only \(k\).

Consequently every operator factored through the valuation carrier is constant on the fibers

\[
p^k\mathbb Z_p^\times.
\]

The weighted theta-cut incidence has exactly this invariance: its output depends on \(p\) and \(k\), not on the unit \(u\).

## The odd Tate port needs additive phase

The finite-place Fourier/Weyl port uses

\[
(T_hf)(x)=f(x+h),
\qquad
(M_\eta f)(x)=\psi_p(\eta x)f(x).
\]

The multiplier \(\psi_p(\eta x)\) is generally not constant on a valuation shell. Additive translation also fails to preserve valuation fibers.

Therefore neither \(T_h\) nor \(M_\eta\) descends through the valuation projection. The Weyl commutator

\[
T_hM_\eta
=
\psi_p(\eta h)M_\eta T_h
\]

cannot be reconstructed from the weighted cut atoms.

## Exact factorization obstruction

Let

\[
\pi_{\mathrm{val}}:\mathcal S(\mathbb Q_p)\to\mathcal V_p
\]

be valuation projection. Suppose an odd-port operator \(P_{\mathrm{fin}}\) on \(\mathcal V_p\) represented additive translation or character multiplication. Then the corresponding local operation would have to satisfy

\[
\pi_{\mathrm{val}}T_h
=
P_{\mathrm{fin}}\pi_{\mathrm{val}}
\]

or its multiplier analogue.

But there exist \(x,y\) with

\[
\pi_{\mathrm{val}}x=\pi_{\mathrm{val}}y
\]

and

\[
\pi_{\mathrm{val}}(x+h)\ne
\pi_{\mathrm{val}}(y+h).
\]

Hence the required factorization is impossible.

This is a kernel criterion:

\[
\ker\pi_{\mathrm{val}}
\not\subseteq
\ker(\pi_{\mathrm{val}}T_h).
\]

## Consequence for the four-port candidate

The reciprocal wall–jump pair can be reached from valuation-weighted theta cuts. The archimedean odd Tate port is generated by additive differentiation.

A source-derived mixed block between them cannot be constructed solely from

\[
(p,k)\longmapsto k\log p.
\]

Any nonzero odd block written after valuation projection must either:

1. import additional additive-character data;
2. arise from a separately authorized global operation;
3. or be a fitted phase.

Thus the proposed four-dimensional dynamic carrier is correctly typed only after a finite additive Tate lift is supplied.

## Minimal repaired source carrier

The next source object must retain both kinds of information:

\[
\text{additive local Tate coordinate}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\text{multiplicative valuation grade}.
\]

A suitable presentation begins on a Schwartz–Bruhat carrier before radialization:

\[
\mathcal S(\mathbb Q_p)
\xrightarrow{\text{Weyl/Fourier}}
\mathcal S(\mathbb Q_p)
\xrightarrow{\pi_{\mathrm{val}}}
\mathcal V_p.
\]

The weighted prime-power cut is then attached after the Weyl square, not before it.

The source must prove what part of the additive phase survives the valuation readout. No abstract lifting of a valuation vector back to \(\mathcal S(\mathbb Q_p)\) is canonical.

## Revised boundary order

The constructor order is forced:

1. local additive Tate carrier;
2. translation–character Weyl pair;
3. local Fourier transform;
4. comparison with the real even–odd derivative square;
5. valuation projection;
6. Euler half-density and grade typing;
7. theta tail–seam cut;
8. four-port mixed return.

The existing weighted cut closes steps five through seven but cannot supply steps one through four retroactively.

## Hostiles

1. Define a finite derivative directly on valuation labels.
2. Assign one translated valuation output to an entire unit shell.
3. Recover the Weyl phase from the real scalar \(k\log p\).
4. Add an imaginary odd coupling after theta synthesis and call it Fourier-derived.
5. Choose a noncanonical section of valuation projection without recording it as extra source data.

## Verdict

The weighted valuation-to-theta incidence is genuine but parity-even. It cannot populate the odd Tate port because valuation projection has already erased the additive Weyl coordinate.

The earliest missing constructor is now the local additive Tate lift and its comparison to valuation projection. Until it exists, a nonzero prime-labelled odd mixed return is undefined rather than merely unbounded.
