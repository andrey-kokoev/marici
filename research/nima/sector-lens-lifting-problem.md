# Sector Lens Selection Is a Lifting Problem

Let

\[
U:\mathsf{EnrichedCarrier}\to\mathsf{Carrier}
\]

forget coefficient structure. For a Carrier \(C\), its admissible lenses form
the fiber of \(U\) over \(C\). A sector source does not merely attach a lens;
it must construct a lift into this fiber.

The lift has three possible statuses:

- empty after auditing degenerate/derived replacements: the proposed sector is
  incompatible with the Carrier constraints;
- multiple: the source leaves genuine lens moduli;
- uniquely selected: extra source comparison data impose a universal
  pullback or equivalent uniqueness condition.

The exact two-dimensional model realizes all relevant mechanisms. Positivity
cannot coexist with negative exterior volume; swap and unit volume leave a
rational family; the QND record map selects one Gram lens by pullback.

The refined DPC is:

> The common Carrier constrains a fiber of enriched realizations. A physical
> sector is a source-derived lift into that fiber, together with framed
> transport and a readout that descends to the physical quotient.

Entry 2086 adds a required gate: a failed positive lift may first produce a
canonical quotient by the radical. Only after such source-derived
degenerate/derived replacements fail may emptiness count as incompatibility.
