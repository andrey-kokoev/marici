# The next RH rung is source-observer chamber coherence

## Question

After relative colligation supplies a mate-coherent conservative dynamics, why
can the selected source-observer coefficient still vanish, and what kind of
higher coherencer could forbid that vanishing without assuming RH?

## Residue ladder

The current RH construction has three distinct levels.

### Incidence residue

The direct sector has a forcing-to-tail arrow but no writable reverse arrow.
Reciprocal transport locates a dual arrow without authorizing it as direct
feedback.

### Dynamical coherencer

A source-derived adjoint-mate or relative colligation can relate direct and
dual incidence while retaining boundary data. This is the proposed filler of
the incidence residue.

An ordinary finite positive skew-adjoint extension is not such a filler: it
adds backreaction and cannot project onto the original nonzero nilpotent tail
generator as an invariant quotient.

### Source-observer residue

Even a valid unitary, orientation-preserving, mate-coherent evolution can have
a vanishing selected matrix coefficient. A quarter-turn is the exact finite
hostile. Therefore conservation of the internal carrier does not imply
nonvanishing of the source-observer pairing.

This is the residue left after the dynamical coherencer succeeds.

## Candidate next coherencer

The next object must live on the source-observer incidence relationship. It
should consist of a source-derived open chamber or oriented generalized minor
that is preserved by theta/Tate transport in each open half-plane.

Schematically, let (L_s) be the source line, (L_o) the observer line, and
(U_z) the completed transport. The scalar coefficient is a projection of
their relative incidence:

\[
m(z)=\langle L_o,U_zL_s\rangle.
\]

Unitarity controls (U_z). It does not control whether (U_zL_s) enters the
incidence divisor defined by (m(z)=0). The higher coherencer must instead
preserve an open cell in the appropriate relative flag space. Nonvanishing of
(m) would then be a shadow of chamber preservation, not an assumed scalar
property.

## DPC

The chamber route is admissible only if all of the following are derived
before inspecting zeros:

1. The source and observer flags come from labelled theta/Tate operations.
2. The relevant generalized minor is fixed by those flags and their ordering.
3. The off-seam transport preserves one declared open oriented chamber.
4. Boundary, primitive, square, seam, and archimedean incidences are retained.
5. The chamber law survives cutoff composition and restricted-product
   completion.
6. The quarter-turn hostile passes conservation but fails the chamber gate.
7. Hostile symmetric scalar multipliers cannot preserve the complete chamber
   data while inserting cross zeros.

Failure to construct the flags or the preserved open cell closes this route.
Fitting a chamber after examining the scalar coefficient is circular.

## Interpretation

The next rung does not repair the previous coherencer's internal residue.
Rather, the successful lower coherencer exposes a new relationship—transported
source versus observer—and that relationship has its own residue, namely entry
into the incidence divisor. Chamber coherence is the candidate higher filler.

## Claim boundary

This packet identifies and types the next DPC. It does not construct the
theta/Tate chamber, prove chamber preservation, or prove RH. The quarter-turn
hostile proves only that conservation and mate coherence are insufficient.

## Disposition

The next-rung interpretation survives and is sharper than another internal
operator repair. The live construction problem is a source-derived
source-observer chamber functor whose preserved open cell has scalar
nonvanishing as a faithful shadow.
