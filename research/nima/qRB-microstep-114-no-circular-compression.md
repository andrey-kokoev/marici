# qRB microstep 114: no-circular compression rule

The operators `J_n=P_nJ_linkP_n` are canonical approximants to the already constructed limiting linking operator, but they cannot be substituted for physical regulator readouts by definition.

A legitimate source construction must prove

$$
T_{\alpha_n}(u,v)-\langle P_nJ_{\rm link}P_nu,v\rangle\to0
$$

on the source core. Otherwise the compression merely repackages the target operator and supplies no source-derived convergence theorem.

This preserves the distinction between:

- analytic approximation of a known bounded linking form;
- derivation of that form from finite positive regulators.

Status: circular shortcut excluded; physical-to-compressed comparison remains the live gate.
