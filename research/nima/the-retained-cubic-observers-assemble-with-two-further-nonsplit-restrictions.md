## 1. Fixed analytical coefficients and exact aggregate semantics

Use the same background-two event packet `(2,3,5,7,11,13)` and the actual source ideal. Write

`S0=(1-rho)(1-sigma)>0`, `Sx=-rho<0`,

in the normalized original-detector units established by the previous export.

The two-private and sixteen-private tests use the fixed positive residual-window functional R. The matched test uses the fixed simultaneous norming functional N. At the owning receiver calibration `y=3`, `gamma=1`,

`R(F)=sqrt(2)*h*X_F*(mu_F-L)`,

`N(F)=sqrt(2)*X_F*(C_even+h*(mu_F-L))`,

with `h=1/sqrt(8)`.

Both are additive interval functionals. Their window values are correlated actual analytical quantities, not free independent channel parameters. The exported coefficient rule preserves those definitions and uses endpoint-potential additivity in its exact checks.

Each aggregate is

`S0 * positive_test / positive_normalizer`

`+ Sx * crossed_test / crossed_normalizer`.

The normalizers are exported as positive sums of products of actual window responses. They are independently recovered from the factored cubic records. No floating-point calibration or generic symbolic rank is used.

These are the ideal source-exact observers. The rounded rational coefficients and approximate finite numerical implementations in the owning noise certificates are different protocols and are not substituted here.

## 4. A two-term source separates the sixteen-private observer

Let

`p16=path(2_retained,3_forgotten,7_retained,5_forgotten)`,

`t=forgotten(11,13)`,

`h16=p16*t`.

The two terms have total absolute path coefficient two. The terminal recorder kills h16, but its first jet is nonzero, so it lies in I and not I^2.

`-2 S0 R([2,4]) R([12,84]) / D16,0`,

## 5. A second two-term source separates the memory-block observer

Let

`pM=path(2_retained,3_retained,5_forgotten,7_forgotten)`,

Again hM lies in I but not I^2 and has path cost two. Its retained features occupy both positions of the first two-event block.

## 6. Both new restrictions are nonsplit

At the terminal `(11,13)` corner, the source ideal has dimension two. Its recorder has rank six on eight paths. The private core already observes its forgotten and mixed directions independently.

Consequently every subsequent restriction is an isomorphism on that corner: no enlargement can add a direction beyond the entire two-dimensional source ideal image there.

A hypothetical source-bimodule section must lift the forgotten tail t to its same-source evaluation. Left multiplication by p16 or pM would then send a zero previous-observer state to a nonzero enlarged-observer state, contradicting equivariance.

Thus these are nonsplit restrictions, not invertible frame changes. The proof does not depend on picking a convenient failed section.

Each new aggregate contributes only one full-corner scalar coordinate. The separating source proves that coordinate independent of the previous evaluation, while the two-private comparison proves redundancy. Together with the 270 private pivots and the previously verified original-frame separator, this proves the full-corner dimension sequence stated above.
