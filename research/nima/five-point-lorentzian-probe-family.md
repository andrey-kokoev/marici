# Joint faithfulness of an open five-point Lorentzian probe family

## Question

Does a source-derived family of real massless conserved five-point configurations jointly detect universal planar Laurent functions, rather than merely separate one finite list at one point?

## Claim boundary

The theorem below concerns the universal five-point algebraic kinematic domain and a nonempty real Lorentzian sector. It supplies no amplitude normalization, pole prescription, positive-energy measure, analytic continuation, or theorem for arbitrary multiplicity.

## Construction

Let the five adjacent planar invariants be coordinates `a_0,...,a_4`. Conservation and masslessness uniquely determine the nonadjacent invariants by

\[
b_i=-a_i-a_{i+1}+a_{i+3},
\]

with indices modulo five. Let `G(a)` be the resulting symmetric Gram matrix with zero diagonal and entries `s_ij/2`. Its rows sum to zero. The quotient form on the span modulo the all-ones radical is four-dimensional whenever one quotient minor is nonzero.

Define `U` inside the real channel torus by the following strict conditions:

1. all `a_i` are nonzero;
2. the quotient Gram form is nondegenerate with inertia `(1,3)` or `(3,1)`;
3. the Gram-entry signs admit the fixed `2 -> 3` energy-orientation pattern;
4. the five triangulation Laurent monomials are pairwise distinct;
5. the universal weighted sum and all five coefficient-residue functions are nonzero.

The exact point `(-3,-3,-2,1,-1)` lies in `U`. Its nonadjacent invariants are `(7,4,-2,-3,2)`; it has inertia `(1,3)`, energy signs `(-,+,-,+,+)`, distinct triangulation products `(9,3,6,-2,-1)`, weighted sum `-8/9`, and residue values `(-4/3,-5/6,2/3,-3/2,2/3)`.

## Theorem

`U` is a nonempty Euclidean-open subset of the real points of the universal five-point channel torus. Nonvanishing and strict-sign conditions are open. Inertia is locally constant on nondegenerate symmetric forms. Each `G(a)` in this sector admits real Lorentzian vectors by Sylvester congruence; zero diagonal makes them null, and the one-dimensional radical plus rank four makes their sum zero.

Every nonempty Euclidean-open subset of `R^5` is Zariski dense. If a Laurent polynomial vanishes at every point of `U`, multiplication by one monomial clears its denominator and gives an ordinary polynomial vanishing on a nonempty open set, hence the zero polynomial. Therefore evaluation on `U` is jointly faithful on

\[
\mathbb R[a_0^{\pm1},\ldots,a_4^{\pm1}].
\]

Because every route-coherence equality first descends to the same Laurent function, all probes in `U` preserve it. Distinct Laurent functions are separated by at least one probe in `U`. This is cross-probe coherence plus joint detection at five points.

## Disposition

The five-point presentation-to-kinematics map is faithful on a nonempty Lorentzian `2 -> 3` probe sector, not merely at an inserted point. The next unresolved arrow is from universal Laurent coefficient extraction to a normalized analytic residue or scattering-amplitude form; that arrow requires a declared local coordinate, differential normalization, and pole prescription.
