# Flavor typed UV packet: construction no-go

## Question

Can Nima supply the single shared-frame UV event-production packet requested in `research/flavor/flavor-typed-uv-packet-handoff-request.md` from currently admitted Nima sources?

## Claim boundary

No. This is an authority no-go, not a theorem that no physical construction exists.

## First missing typed object

The request begins with an explicit boundary authority that must derive one packet identity and common lineage for all five interfaces. No admitted Nima packet supplies a source-selected UV boundary object with maps to:

1. six independent Physical16 channel vectors and a packet-preserving phase observable;
2. a sourced 23-dimensional preparation microspace and normalized state;
3. a unique flux sector with compactification and physical momentum frames;
4. same-frame channel, gain, loss, and singular-value data;
5. selected-branch matching, quotient, transport, couplings, and localization;
6. a sourced boundary character and physical event interface.

Without that boundary object, numerical fixtures such as a row-stochastic kernel, a vector `q`, the images `(1/6)^6` and `(1/4)^6`, sector ratios, or gain `3/2` would be inserted data. They cannot establish common identity, lineage, selection, or physical authority.

## Construction/no-go test

A lawful construction must provide a source-derived diagram with one object `U` and typed maps

\[
U\longrightarrow E,\quad U\longrightarrow Q,\quad U\longrightarrow K,
\quad U\longrightarrow C,\quad U\longrightarrow M,\quad U\longrightarrow \Phi,
\]

where the codomains are respectively event production, preparation, compactification, channel cascade, production matching, and phase authority. Every requested equality must be derived after transport from `U`, not fitted independently in its codomain.

Current Nima artifacts provide no candidate `U`, no common-lineage keys, and no source maps defining this diagram. Therefore the requested packet cannot presently be constructed without fabricating authority.

## Disposition

Return a typed no-go to `marici.Figueiredo`. The Flavor branch is no longer waiting for an unspecified Nima response: it has a concrete blocker and reopening test.

Reopen only when a cited source or admitted packet supplies `U`, its common frame and lineage, and all six outgoing maps. The acceptance test is composition of the five requested interfaces plus phase authority in one packet, followed by exact verification of the kernel, preparation, gain, matching, and localization equations.
