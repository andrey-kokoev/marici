# The five-port residual is topologically defined but not yet source-normalized as one G4 adjoint

## Question

Do the five portwise rigging estimates already define the final G4 arithmetic
adjoint whose Xi divisibility must be tested?

## Claim boundary

Not completely. The estimates prove that every proposed component is a
continuous \(U\)-valued section once its port map is supplied. They do not by
themselves prove that the five maps share the final source-normalized G4 Green
metric. The residual is therefore topologically admissible but still partial as
a source adjoint.

## Closed portwise topology

For the exact split Evans section, the following operations are continuous on
the retained wall graph:

- ordinary history pairing;
- regular graph derivative;
- wall trace;
- reciprocal reflection;
- ordered Stokes linking functional.

After the centered prime half-density and source adjoint weight, each component
obeys

\[
 \|r_U^{(j)}(z)\|_U^2
 \le C_j^2\|\widehat u(z)\|_{G,\rm wall}^2
 \sum_p\frac1{p\log p}.
\]

Thus no proposed component fails by divergence or an undefined distributional
trace.

## Source-normalization audit

### Ordinary centered column

The ordinary component is fixed by

\[
 b_p^{(0)}=p^{-1/2}\Phi\mathbf1_{[0,\log p]}
\]

and the declared \((\log p)^{-1}\) source adjoint. Its late shell sign is
source-computable.

### Regular derivative and wall

Distributional differentiation of the cut atom fixes the regular derivative
and jump simultaneously:

\[
 D(\mathbf1_{q\le a}\Phi(a-q))
 =-\mathbf1_{q<a}\Phi'(a-q)-\Phi(0)\delta_a.
\]

Their relative coefficient is therefore fixed. Retaining the delta as a wall
coordinate makes the pair continuous.

### Reciprocal port

Reflection is unitary on the whole-line wall graph and fixes the reciprocal odd
sign. The global Fourier--Poisson multiplier and its contragredient action must
still be shown to act on every primitive, square, connected, wall, and response
stratum of the final G4 carrier. Scalar functional-equation agreement is not
that theorem.

### Ordered linking port

The wall/incidence graph makes the ordered linking functional continuous, and
the endpoint quarter-column fixes its linear sign and magnitude on its native
frame. However, transporting that local Stokes/Wronskian normalization into the
final G4 joint column requires the completed polarized comparison.

The raw primewise Stokes-to-Wronskian comparison also exhibits the forced factor

\[
 \lambda_p=\frac{-\kappa_p}{2s_p}\longrightarrow0
\]

superexponentially. Consequently this comparison is not a uniformly invertible
unweighted identification. A source pullback metric or retained source
coordinate is required; inserting \(\lambda_p^{-1}\) by hand is forbidden.

## Consequence for the joint adjoint

If the five final component maps are denoted \(B_j\) and their source metrics
by \(G_j\), then the joint adjoint is uniquely

\[
 B_\Sigma^\dagger y=\sum_j B_j^*G_jy_j.
\]

The portwise continuity theorems show that every summand is eligible. They do
not determine an unresolved \(G_j\) or comparison map.

Therefore the notation \(B_\Sigma^\dagger u_z\) currently has two levels:

1. a well-defined adjoint for the frozen centered column components;
2. the final Fourier--Poisson/ordered-linking G4 adjoint, conditional on the
   remaining completed comparison and response-stratum transport.

Only the second can carry the terminal chain-map claim.

## Correct order of work

1. Construct the completed polarized Stokes/Wronskian comparison on the retained
   source graph.
2. Construct the contragredient Fourier--Poisson action on every response
   stratum.
3. Freeze the resulting five component metrics and signs.
4. Form the unique joint-column adjoint.
5. Test its complete prime-shell jets for Xi divisibility.

Testing step 5 with provisional coefficients would fit the operator to the
divisor rather than derive it.

## Disposition

The rigging objective is closed only at the level of admissible continuous
ports. The final G4 residual still lacks two source-normalization comparisons:
completed polarized Stokes/Wronskian descent and full stratified reciprocal
response transport. Exact Xi divisibility cannot be tested authoritatively
until those maps are frozen. No RH conclusion is authorized.
