# A labelled boundary port restores a uniform off-seam equalizer margin

Scope correction: \(z\) below denotes the real normal coordinate.  For complex
\(s\), the estimate is generated by the conjugate-reciprocal difference
\(s-(1-\overline{s})=2\operatorname{Re}(s)-1\).

## Combined law

For a labelled cutoff, write the tail and reservoir coordinates as vectors
\(A=(A_j)\) and \(c=(c_j)\).  The direct and reciprocal residuals are

\[
r_{+,j}=(z-\lambda_j)A_j+b_jc_j
\]

and

\[
r_{-,j}=(1-z-\lambda_j)A_j+b_jc_j.
\]

Let the boundary port retain the labelled reservoir vector itself:

\[
J_X(A,c)=c.
\]

This is the coefficient-side form of the full boundary-jet observer, before
the potentially ill-conditioned Vandermonde synthesis into scalar endpoint
derivatives.

## Uniform estimate

The reciprocal difference eliminates every rate and incidence coefficient:

\[
r_+-r_-=2\left(z-\frac12\right)A.
\]

Let \(a=z-1/2\ne0\).  Then

\[
4|a|^2\|A\|^2
=\|r_+-r_-\|^2
\le2\left(\|r_+\|^2+\|r_-\|^2\right).
\]

Therefore

\[
\|A\|^2+\|c\|^2
\le
\max\left(1,\frac1{2|a|^2}\right)
\left(
\|r_+\|^2+\|r_-\|^2+\|J_X(A,c)\|^2
\right).
\]

The constant is independent of the cutoff, the rates \(\lambda_j\), and the
nonzero incidences \(b_j\).  On every compact set separated from the seam, it
is uniformly bounded.

## Why a scalar endpoint port is insufficient

Replacing the labelled port by

\[
J_{\mathrm{sum}}c=\sum_jc_j
\]

reintroduces completion escape.  Take two high-rate modes with
\(c_1=1\), \(c_2=-1\), and

\[
A_j=\frac{b_jc_j}{\lambda_j-1/2}.
\]

Then \(J_{\mathrm{sum}}c=0\), while both sector residual norms tend to zero as
the rates grow.  The state norm tends to \(2\).  Thus a scalar aggregate seam
readout cannot replace the labelled boundary carrier.

## DPC theorem

The finite native equalizer plus the labelled reservoir boundary port has a
cutoff-independent off-seam inverse margin.  In this model, completion cannot
create an off-seam equalizer state.

The theorem is conditional in exactly one source-specific place: the actual
theta/Tate construction must supply a continuous labelled boundary incidence
equivalent to \(c\) in the chosen completion topology.  The earlier arithmetic
Gram results show that this cannot be inferred from an aggregate analytic
trace.  It must descend from the valuation/Fock source or an equivalent typed
boundary module.

This identifies the missing law more precisely than positivity:

> Reciprocal sector comparison controls the normal tail coordinate, while a
> source-labelled boundary port controls the common arithmetic reservoir.

Tangential seam spectrum remains possible because the constant diverges as
\(a\to0\).  The law is coercive only away from the critical seam.
