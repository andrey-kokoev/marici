# Third-order boundary totalization is a strict determinant character after anomaly normalization

## Relative increments

Let a finite relative boundary operation be represented by \(A\), with
invertible \(I+A\). Composition is

\[
A\star B=A+B+AB,
\]

so that

\[
I+(A\star B)=(I+A)(I+B).
\]

For the order-three regularized determinant, set

\[
r(A)=-\operatorname{Tr}A+\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(A^2).
\]

Then

\[
\det_3(I+A)=\det(I+A)e^{r(A)}.
\]

The regularized determinant is not strictly multiplicative. Its anomaly is

\[
\alpha_3(A,B)
=
r(A\star B)-r(A)-r(B).
\]

## Strict totalized character

Define the normalized third-order boundary section

\[
\Delta_3(A)
=
e^{-r(A)}\det_3(I+A).
\]

At finite cutoff,

\[
\Delta_3(A)=\det(I+A).
\]

Therefore

\[
\Delta_3(A\star B)
=
\Delta_3(A)\Delta_3(B).
\]

The primitive and square counterterms are not auxiliary corrections. They are
exactly the line normalization that turns the connected
\(\det_3\) section into a strict multiplicative character.

This is the finite relative determinant sought by the endpoint complex.

## Cocycle coherence

Because \(\alpha_3=\delta r\), one has

\[
\alpha_3(A,B)+\alpha_3(A\star B,C)
=
\alpha_3(B,C)+\alpha_3(A,B\star C).
\]

Hence all associativity cells are generated by the single normalization
\(r\). No independent three-fold coherencer is needed.

For endpoint interval concatenation, if \(A_a\) represents the relative
operation carried by the interval of length \(a\), source compatibility must
give

\[
A_{a+b}=A_a\star S_a^*A_b.
\]

Then

\[
\Delta_3(A_{a+b})
=
\Delta_3(A_a)\,
\Delta_3(S_a^*A_b).
\]

This is the multiplicative line law corresponding to the additive interval
identity.

## Three boundary strata

For the sign convention \(I-K\), define

\[
P_1(K)=\operatorname{Tr}K,
\qquad
P_2(K)=\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(K^2).
\]

The finite totalization is

\[
\det(I-K)^{-1}
=
e^{P_1(K)+P_2(K)}
\det_3(I-K)^{-1}.
\]

Thus:

- primitive current supplies \(P_1\);
- square current supplies \(P_2\);
- connected grades supply the \(\det_3\) section.

The order-three packet is a single determinant-line character precisely
because all three strata use the same relative operator \(K\). Matching only
their scalar values after separate construction does not establish the
character.

## Endpoint realization gate

Let \(K_a\) be the finite operator assigned to the oriented endpoint interval
of scale \(a\). To identify the endpoint relative complex with the normalized
determinant section, the source must prove

\[
P_1(K_a)=J_a^{(1)}
\]

and

\[
P_2(K_a)=J_a^{(2)},
\]

where \(J_a^{(1)}\) and \(J_a^{(2)}\) are the independently derived primitive
and square endpoint currents. It must also identify the connected interval
synthesis with \(\det_3(I-K_a)\).

These are typed equalities in one common graph carrier. They cannot be inferred
from the final product \(\Delta_3(K_a)\), since a counterterm may be moved
between \(P_1\) and \(P_2\) while preserving their exponentiated sum.

The first decisive finite test is therefore not another determinant identity.
It is coefficientwise comparison of the endpoint currents with the first two
operator traces of the same interval block.

## Archimedean trivialization

The finite arithmetic totalization becomes a completed scalar only after its
primitive and square anomaly lines are paired with the seam and archimedean
boundary presentation.

The Gaussian Mellin character

\[
\chi_s(a)=a^{-s}
\]

provides the local archimedean line under dilation. A completed
trivialization must give a source-derived isomorphism

\[
\tau_s:
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{prim}}
\otimes
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{sq}}
\otimes
\mathcal L_{\det_3}
\otimes
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{seam}}
\otimes
\mathcal L_{\infty}
\longrightarrow
\mathbb C
\]

that is compatible with interval concatenation and whose restriction to
\(\mathcal L_\infty\) transforms by \(\chi_s\).

The finite strict character proves coherence once \(\tau_s\) exists. It does
not construct \(\tau_s\).

## Zero-sieve consequence

Suppose the pointed readout is obtained from the same totalized relative
operator packet. Then every composable boundary operation acts on its
determinant line by the nonzero character

\[
\lambda(A)=\Delta_3(A).
\]

Whenever \(\Delta_3(A)\ne0\),

\[
rF(A)=\Delta_3(A)\,r
\]

propagates the present kernel through the boundary operation.

This is conditional on invertibility of \(I+A\). A zero of
\(\Delta_3(A)\) is not an admissible zero-sieve multiplier; it is precisely a
failure of the relative operation to remain in the determinant unit group.
The RH-bearing bridge must relate that failure to the completed distinguished
state without circularly excluding it.

## Completion obstruction

At infinite cutoff, \(\det_3(I-K)\) may converge while \(P_1(K)\) and
\(P_2(K)\) do not exist as ordinary traces. The normalized finite character
then lives in a relative determinant line rather than as a scalar limit.

Completion requires:

1. cutoff-compatible primitive and square anomaly lines;
2. a closed connected \(S_3\) operator family;
3. an archimedean and seam trivialization of the two anomaly lines;
4. absence of a derived-limit anomaly;
5. a uniform nonvanishing margin on each compact where the action is claimed
   to lie in the determinant unit group.

Without these conditions, strict finite multiplicativity does not produce a
completed zero-sieve character.

## Hostile tests

1. Omitting \(e^{-r(A)}\) leaves the \(\det_3\) anomaly uncompensated.
2. Adding an independent associator duplicates the exact coboundary
   coherencer.
3. Using different operators for endpoint currents and the connected
   determinant destroys provenance.
4. Moving a finite counterterm between primitive and square strata while
   preserving the total scalar exposes an unfixed frame.
5. A cutoff family with convergent \(\det_3\) but divergent untrivialized
   \(P_1\) or \(P_2\) has no global scalar determinant.
6. A determinant multiplier that vanishes cannot serve as a nonzero
   zero-sieve character.

## Consequence for categorical RH

The finite third-order relative determinant character is now explicit:

\[
\Delta_3=e^{-r}\det_3=\det.
\]

Its coherence is automatic from the common full operator and the exact anomaly
coboundary. The unresolved constructor is narrower: realize the independently
derived endpoint currents as \(P_1\) and \(P_2\) of that same interval
operator, then trivialize their completed anomaly lines with the seam and
Gaussian Mellin boundary.

## Verdict

Third-order boundary totalization is already a strict determinant character
after primitive and square anomaly normalization. Categorical RH does not need
a new coherence law at this rung. It needs a source-derived common interval
operator and a completion-stable boundary trivialization connecting its
normalized determinant line to the pointed \(\Xi\) readout.
