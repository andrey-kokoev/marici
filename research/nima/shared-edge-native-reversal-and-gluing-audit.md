# Shared-edge gluing: what native reversal actually supplies

## Directly inspected definitions

- `agda/TableFibrationCycle.agda` supplies `group-from`, `group-to`, `unpack` and `unpack-reversed`: the same retained row is presented with different endpoint wiring.
- `check_arrow_history_window.py` retains an occurrence ID and ancestry window while toggling a reversal flag. It refuses physical path reassembly from a merely reversed view. A new inverse execution is not generated by displaying an old occurrence backward.
- `agda/WholePackageSigmaPi.agda` and `agda/NativeTableRules.agda` define `inverse-comparison` from an actual pointed equivalence e and its witness. They construct invEquiv(e) and retain the original comparison as a parent.
- `source-swap-transport-scope.md` and its implementation distinguish this supplied witness from all other fillers with the same boundary. Opposite endpoint labels do not uniquely determine a filler.

## Three meanings that must remain separate

| Operation | What is determined | What is not determined |
|---|---|---|
| Display AB backward | BA endpoints for the SAME occurrence, with transported orientation flag | A newly executed inverse path or identification with another BA occurrence |
| Construct inverse of actual comparison e_AB | e_AB^-1, with pointed compatibility and retained parent | That an independently supplied e_BA equals this inverse |
| Glue two seed occurrences AB and BA | A shared undirected support is known | An equality between their actual values/witnesses, without a specified reversal relation |

The source definition of inverse is not scalar negation. For a linear representation it ordinarily gives matrix inverse. Negation is appropriate for endpoint differences or additive cochains under an additional representation law. It is not a generic interpretation of inverse equivalence, and positive traversal cost does not change sign when a path reverses.

## Consequence for the recent two-triangle models

The five-coordinate model in `two-triangles-shared-edge-half-phase.md` explicitly selects BA=-AB as an oriented-cochain rule. Its synchronized subspace and occurrence metric are correct under that convention. The six-coordinate model instead allows independent scalar values on the two directed occurrences; its two uncoupled blocks reflect that choice.

Neither choice is selected by the inspected generic grouping or inverse constructors alone. Sharing endpoint support does not prove scalar anti-symmetry, and retaining two occurrence IDs does not prove their values are independent. One must specify the actual typed gluing witness.

A native comparison-valued gluing contract would require the supplied BA witness to agree with invEquiv(e_AB), with its marked-point witness and occurrence/provenance relationship kept explicit. If one instead works with scalar cochains, an adapter must show that the chosen cochain reading sends the admitted reversal to negation. A matrix-valued or path-resource reading has different laws.

## Primitive seed traced to its executable definitions

`check_two_triangle_half_phase.py` constructs two separate objects `TwoPacket('AB','A','B')` and `TwoPacket('BA','B','A')`. The `TwoPacket` declaration in `check_triangle_half_phase.py` contains only label, source and target. It has no amplitude, inverse-parent field or inverse-equivalence witness. The negative BA reading is introduced later, explicitly, in the local restriction matrix R2. It is not recovered from a stored inverse in the seed constructor.

`check_natural_tower_return.py` independently spells out the same six labelled occurrences and constructs its incoming-incidence matrix directly by adding one per source/target entry. It does not identify AB and BA values. In A,B,C,D order this gives

    M=[[0,1,1,0],[1,0,0,1],[0,1,0,0],[1,0,0,0]].

`natural-tower-return-audit.md` already completes this model's spectral reconstruction. Its eigenvalues are phi, psi, omega and conjugate(omega), where phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2, psi=(1-sqrt(5))/2. Full spectral descriptors reconstruct M and hence all six endpoint pairs; retained occurrence IDs restore order. Pure regrouping does not execute M.

The raw M has no finite return because phi>1. The separately extracted spectral phase has order six, but reconstructing raw M also needs its modulus. That extraction is not the triangle short-root construction or a physical update selected by grouping. M acts on four vertex/family coefficients, not on the six independent edge amplitudes of the recent model.

The existing natural-tower checker was freshly rerun and passes. Thus a source-incidence model already exists that avoids both unproved scalar gluing and independent local triangle dynamics. It still supplies a representation choice (unit incidence weights), not a physical Hamiltonian or native seed-arrow value.

This resolves the narrow provenance question in the inspected implementations: BA is supplied as a separate directed packet record; its inverse relationship is not encoded there. It does not prove that no additional relation is intended at the conceptual source level.

## Result and stopping boundary

The native machinery already supplies reversal of views and inversion of specified equivalences. It does NOT by itself supply the seed-specific identification needed to choose between our five- and six-coordinate coefficient models. The current mathematical findings therefore remain representation-conditional.

The productive next source inspection is the definition of the primitive seed's arrow value and the provenance of its BA occurrence: is BA generated as the inverse view/execution of AB, or supplied as a separate comparison with a retained relation? Without that source-level datum, deriving a unique joint dynamics would require inventing a gluing law. This audit records that boundary rather than adding a coupling postulate.
