# Many-many pair-labelled wall carrier

Define the enlarged wall carrier by

$$
\mathcal W_{\rm pair}
=\bigoplus_{n,m\ge1}\mathcal W_{nm},
$$

where each `W_nm` retains

$$
\bigl(\rho_{nm}(0),\kappa_{nm}\partial_zR_{nm}\bigr).
$$

For a finite pair family `F`, the scalar codiagonal is a source-labelled map

$$
C_{\mathcal F}:\bigoplus_{(n,m)\in\mathcal F}\mathcal W_{nm}
\longrightarrow\mathcal W_{\rm scalar}.
$$

Its coefficients must preserve reciprocal symmetry and the pair flux weights. The global codiagonal is obtained projectively from the finite maps when their restrictions agree.

The positive relative carrier remains separate from this signed wall output. A scalar codiagonal may be non-injective; its kernel requires the relative quotient criterion.
