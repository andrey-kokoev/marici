# Prime-power grades are cyclic returns, not boundary sectors

## Typing theorem

Let the primitive labelled prime space have basis \(e_p\), and define the primitive feedback operator

\[
L_s e_p=p^{-s}e_p.
\]

Then

\[
-\log\det(I-L_s)
=
\sum_{k\ge1}
\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(L_s^k)}{k}.
\]

For each \(k\),

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(L_s^k)
=
\sum_p p^{-ks}.
\]

After logarithmic differentiation, these cyclic returns produce the von Mangoldt prime-power currents.

Therefore:

- \(k=1\) is the primitive return length;
- \(k=2\) is the square return length;
- \(k\ge3\) is the connected higher-return tail.

They are not independent boundary state channels.

## Why direct-sum promotion is invalid

A proposed boundary carrier of the form

\[
\mathcal B_1\oplus\mathcal B_2\oplus\mathcal B_{\ge3}
\]

treats cyclic word lengths as separately existing state spaces. That changes the source ontology.

The source contains one primitive loop \(L_s\). Its powers are repeated traversal of the same labelled prime modes. Promoting those powers to independent ports:

- double-counts returns;
- invents cross-grade state vectors;
- creates quadratic couplings absent from the determinant expansion;
- permits positivity tests on matrices that the source never supplied.

The three Tate regularity classes remain meaningful as analytic summability classes of cyclic traces. They do not become three physical or boundary sectors.

## Strict grading is doubly excluded

The prime-power index \(k\) cannot be used as a well-founded state grade raised by the loop.

The operator \(L_s\) is diagonal on prime labels, and each power

\[
L_s^k e_p=p^{-ks}e_p
\]

returns to the same prime mode. Word length records repeated closed traversal, not movement to a new state grade.

Thus the triangular determinant argument was not merely too strong. It was typed on the wrong object.

## Two legitimate realizations

The programme now has two exact but differently variant realizations.

### Additive theta Schur line

The doubled terminal-tail system gives a scalar boundary Schur complement equal to the bilateral completed theta transform, up to an audited nonvanishing unit and separately typed archimedean incidence.

This realization is additive in its source tails and boundary closure.

### Multiplicative Euler determinant line

The labelled prime feedback operator gives the Euler determinant and its cyclic logarithmic traces.

This realization is multiplicative in primitive prime loops.

These are not two presentations of one already constructed state operator. Their join is a determinant-line comparison derived through the global Tate–Poisson correspondence.

## What the comparison proves

The source-derived comparison proves that the additive theta section and multiplicative Euler determinant define the same completed scalar section up to a nonvanishing unit.

It establishes:

- exact arithmetic and archimedean provenance;
- the correct prime-power grammar;
- the determinant origin of the \(1/k\) coefficients;
- reciprocal completion;
- rejection of hostile multipliers that change the source.

It does not create a higher boundary carrier or an independent orientation law.

## No remaining lift from the known grades

The proposed higher-boundary route has no admissible input:

- the scalar theta boundary is rank one;
- the prime-power classes are cyclic traces, not state ports;
- the natural infinite labelled transport module was already closed as nonorienting;
- direct-sum promotion invents unsupported cross terms;
- scalar positivity remains RH-equivalent.

A future lift would need a genuinely new source object with independently declared states and two-way incidence. It cannot be assembled from the known cyclic return lengths.

## Cross-sector categorical rule

A trace grade, word length, moment order, or perturbative coefficient is not automatically an object type.

Given one endomorphism \(L\), the family

\[
L,L^2,L^3,\ldots
\]

belongs to the path algebra of one object. It does not authorize a direct-sum family of new objects.

A functor may map cyclic words to scalar currents. It may not reify those words as independent ports without a separate source constructor.

## Finite falsifier

Use one prime mode with scalar loop value \(\lambda\). The determinant expansion is

\[
-\log(1-\lambda)
=
\lambda+\frac{\lambda^2}{2}+\frac{\lambda^3}{3}+\cdots.
\]

A direct-sum state model for the first two lengths introduces a matrix on two supposed channels. Any off-diagonal entry between the \(\lambda\) and \(\lambda^2/2\) channels has no source preimage. Even a diagonal two-state determinant generally fails to reproduce the one-loop logarithm without fitting.

This one-mode witness rejects the proposed grade lift before any RH calculation.

## Disposition

The higher-boundary lift from primitive, square, and connected grades is closed as a typing error.

The RH lane now stands at an honest structural boundary: the source-derived additive and multiplicative realizations meet exactly on a determinant line, but no source-derived higher state object currently exists on which an independent orientation law could act.
