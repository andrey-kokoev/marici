# Theta physics try: native Krein energy fails the equivalent-metric gate

## Status

Source-level application of the neutral-mode criterion to the native
logarithmic translation carrier. The easy physical mechanism would be a
positive, boundedly invertible energy metric that commutes with the carrier and
collocates the ordered endpoint and source ports. The theta source forbids that
mechanism before zero analysis.

The surviving possibilities are a rigged or indefinite metric on a constrained
state class, or an enlarged system retaining the seam as an independent port.

## Native carrier and ordered ports

On the full logarithmic line, take

\[
A=-i\partial_q,
\qquad
b_0=\delta_0,
\qquad
b_f=f.
\]

Any bounded metric \(K\) commuting with \(A\) is a Fourier multiplier:

\[
\widehat{K\psi}(\xi)
=
k(\xi)\widehat\psi(\xi).
\]

The framed symmetrizer condition

\[
Kb_0=\alpha b_f
\]

forces

\[
k(\xi)=\alpha\widehat f(\xi)
\]

up to the fixed Fourier normalization.

The metric is therefore not freely selectable. Its spectral energy density is
the Fourier transform of the actual theta source.

## First physical gate: Hermiticity

A translation-commuting Hermitian metric requires

\[
k(\xi)\in\mathbb R
\]

almost everywhere.

For a one-sided source profile, \(\widehat f\) is generally complex. The native
forward system therefore does not admit a constant Hermitian commutant metric
without reciprocal doubling or an independently derived phase correction.

This matches the two-sector lesson: the Hermitian physical energy cannot be
constructed from one causal half alone.

## Second physical gate: positivity and invertibility

After a reciprocal doubling that makes the effective source real and even,
\(\widehat f\) may become real. A positive equivalent Hilbert metric would
still require constants \(m,M>0\) satisfying

\[
m
\leq
\alpha\widehat f(\xi)
\leq
M
\]

almost everywhere.

But the completed logarithmic theta source is integrable. The
Riemann--Lebesgue lemma gives

\[
\widehat f(\xi)
\longrightarrow0
\]

as \(|\xi|\) tends to infinity.

Therefore no boundedly invertible positive translation-commuting metric can
collocate the endpoint and source ports.

The easy physical strong-stability theorem is closed.

## Third gate: indefinite energy

Allowing \(k\) to change sign produces a Krein metric rather than a positive
energy. Then the neutral-state mechanism becomes available. Opposite-sign
spectral bands can combine into states satisfying

\[
\langle x,Kx\rangle=0.
\]

The oscillatory separation transform found in the doubled theta forcing
calculation is the same warning in another coordinate: a positive source
measure multiplied by oscillatory characters does not retain one sign at all
spectral heights.

Thus source positivity in the logarithmic coordinate does not become spectral
energy definiteness after Fourier rotation.

## Fourth gate: degeneracy at infinity

Even if \(\widehat f\) has one sign on a declared region, its decay destroys a
uniform lower energy bound on the full translation-cyclic space. Normalized
high-frequency packets can have arbitrarily small \(K\)-charge.

This fact alone does not construct approximate theta zero dynamics: the
resolvent and source-invisibility equations impose additional constraints. It
does prove that global metric coercivity cannot be the missing theorem.

Any surviving definite-type result must be restricted to the actual
endpoint-reachable, source-invisible, boundary-compatible state class.

## Physical interpretation

The native theta system resembles a Hamiltonian medium whose energy density
softens to zero at high frequency. Such a medium cannot be strongly stable by
a uniform positive-energy theorem on its entire state space.

There are two possible repairs:

1. constraints forbid the soft directions from satisfying zero dynamics;
2. missing seam and boundary channels restore the energy omitted by the tail
   representation.

The second possibility matches the exact no-bounded-reconstruction theorem:
the seam carries independent norm and cannot be recovered from the retained
tail.

## Restricted constructor task

Let \(\mathcal Z_X(\Omega)\) denote the finite states over an off-seam spectral
region \(\Omega\) that satisfy the declared endpoint incidence, source-output,
seam, residue, and transport constraints.

The only viable energy theorem has the form

\[
\left|
\langle x,K_Xx\rangle
\right|
\geq
\kappa_\Omega\lVert x\rVert_{\rm full}^2,
\qquad
x\in\mathcal Z_X(\Omega),
\]

with \(\kappa_\Omega>0\) independent of the cutoff.

The full norm must include the retained tail, seam, primitive, square, and
archimedean components. A tail-only norm cannot pass the existing escape
tests.

## Immediate calculations

The physics route now asks for four source computations:

1. derive the exact doubled Fourier multiplier implementing modular port
   exchange;
2. locate its positive, negative, and null spectral bands without using zero
   locations as input;
3. compute the restriction of its quadratic form to the finite zero-dynamics
   incidence class;
4. test whether seam and boundary-current augmentation removes every neutral
   direction uniformly under completion.

## Falsifiers

The constrained strong-stability route fails if:

- the doubled modular multiplier is non-Hermitian;
- a finite admissible zero-dynamics state is neutral;
- the augmented metric remains mixed on the constrained class with a realized
  hostile transmission zero;
- the smallest constrained energy eigenvalue tends to zero with cutoff;
- or a hostile reciprocal source satisfies the same independently derived
  constrained bound.

## Decisive conclusion

The physical Krein analogy survives, but its easiest theorem does not. The
native translation carrier admits no equivalent positive metric that
collocates the ordered ports. Any RH-bearing energy law must arise only after
reciprocal doubling, seam augmentation, and restriction to the true
zero-dynamics state class. This is a smaller and more source-specific target
than global coercivity, but it remains unproved.
