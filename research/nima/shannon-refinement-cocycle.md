# Shannon entropy as the universal refinement cocycle

## Refined inherence conjecture

The scattering and flavor sectors generate operationally typed finite
alternatives; the positive cosmological period currently does not. This
separates the relevant structure from positivity alone.

Let an admitted readout supply a finite outcome object \(\Omega\), a positive
normalized state \(p\), and source-defined refinements

\[
\Omega'\longrightarrow\Omega
\]

with conditional states on their fibers. Shannon entropy satisfies

\[
H(\Omega',p')
=H(\Omega,p)
+\sum_{\omega\in\Omega}p(\omega)
H(\Omega'_\omega,p'_\omega).
\]

The exact checker verifies that this increment is associative: two different
parenthesizations of a four-leaf refinement tree give the same total entropy.
Thus \(H\) behaves as an additive cocycle on the category of typed finite
outcome refinements.

## Marici interpretation

The strongest surviving form of the Operator's intuition is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Marici generates typed alternatives and their refinement calculus;}
\quad
\text{Shannon entropy is the universal additive valuation of that calculus}.}
\]

This does not require the bare Carrier to store a number called entropy.
It predicts that whenever a sector reaches an operational finite-outcome
readout, its source-derived localization, support, or coarse-graining maps
must supply a coherent refinement category. Entropy then manifests
canonically and path-independently.

## Falsifiers

- two source-derived refinement paths between the same outcome objects give
  incompatible conditional states;
- the terminal readout lacks positivity or normalization;
- a purported outcome split is only a presentation refinement and is
  annihilated by every physical effect;
- independent readouts fail monoidal product composition.

Cosmology presently stops at the third falsifier: splitting the positive
period integrand changes presentation but no admitted physical outcome.
