# Many-many theta–Green mapping-cone target

Define the next-level comparison as a holomorphic chain map

$$
F(s):T_\theta(s)\longrightarrow C_{\rm FP}(s),
$$

where `T_theta` carries the completed theta–Poisson source data and `C_FP` carries the closed maximal-isotropic Green boundary pencil.

The chain map must preserve:

- prime and grade labels;
- primitive, square, and connected ports;
- reciprocal Fourier–Poisson sewing;
- endpoint traces and contragredient responses;
- finite-family restriction maps.

Form the cone

$$
\operatorname{Cone}F(s).
$$

The analytic completion target is a bounded holomorphic contraction

$$
 d_{\rm cone}h(s)+h(s)d_{\rm cone}=I.
$$

Such a contraction yields quasi-isomorphism, determinant-line transport, and a nonvanishing torsion factor multiplying the Xi section.
