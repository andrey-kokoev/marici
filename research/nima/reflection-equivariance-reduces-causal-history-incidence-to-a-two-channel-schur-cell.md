# Reflection equivariance reduces the causal-history incidence to a two-channel Schur cell

## Parity decomposition

Use the bilateral reciprocal trace basis

\[
E=E_+\oplus E_-,
\]

generated by the even theta forcing and its odd derivative. Decompose the causal-history auxiliary carrier similarly:

\[
H=H_+\oplus H_-.
\]

If the incidence \(C:E\to H\) intertwines reciprocal reflection, then Schur's parity selection gives

\[
C=
\begin{pmatrix}
c_+&0\\
0&c_-
\end{pmatrix}
\]

in the minimal one-dimensional parity model. Thus both trace directions enter independently, while forbidden even-odd incidence is absent.

## Minimal causal auxiliary block

The reflection-even history energy is diagonal by parity. The reciprocal-odd causal skew couples opposite parities. The smallest Hermitian auxiliary block is therefore

\[
D=
\begin{pmatrix}
s_+&i\tau\\
-i\tau&s_-
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
s_+,s_->0,
\qquad
\tau\in\mathbb R.
\]

Reflection fixes the diagonal energy and sends \(\tau\mapsto-\tau\). Positivity is equivalent to

\[
\Delta=s_+s_--\tau^2>0.
\]

Its inverse is

\[
D^{-1}
=
\frac1\Delta
\begin{pmatrix}
s_-&-i\tau\\
i\tau&s_+
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

## Emergent endpoint orientation

Let the real endpoint block be \(A\). The Schur return is

\[
G_{\mathrm{eff}}=A-CD^{-1}C^{*}.
\]

For real source incidence coefficients, its off-diagonal odd coordinate is

\[
\operatorname{Im}(G_{\mathrm{eff},+-})
=
\frac{c_+c_-\tau}{\Delta}.
\]

Thus the three ingredients have separate roles:

- \(c_+c_-\): both reciprocal trace channels must be incident;
- \(\tau\): causal versus anti-causal orientation and sign;
- \(\Delta^{-1}\): resolved auxiliary propagation.

No imaginary endpoint entry is inserted directly.

## Exact finite gates

The source-derived incidence theorem has now reduced to:

1. reflection equivariance of \(C\);
2. nonvanishing and source normalization of both \(c_+\) and \(c_-\);
3. positivity margin \(s_+s_--\tau^2>0\);
4. identification of \(c_+c_-\tau/\Delta\) with the Euler or Wronskian odd port;
5. transport covariance under the Mellin half-density metric;
6. prime-label preservation.

If either incidence coefficient vanishes, the causal auxiliary block may remain positive but the odd Schur return is dark.

## Operator version

In higher multiplicity, reflection equivariance makes \(C\) parity diagonal while the odd history operator is parity off-diagonal. The scalar formula becomes the compressed operator

\[
C_+D^{-1}_{+-}C_-^{*}.
\]

The required completion estimate is a lower frame bound on the two diagonal incidence channels together with an upper resolvent bound for \(D^{-1}\) on their ranges.
