# Six-point local ideal equals the triangle toric ideal

## Question

Does the local-minor description hold scheme-theoretically, rather than only on field-valued points?

## Claim boundary

Fix six points and the triangulation ordering in results/zero_support.json. Work over any field k. The toric ideal means the kernel of the triangle monomial map on polynomial coordinate rings, not just its restriction to units.

The local ideal is generated by

\[
x_0x_6-x_1x_5,\quad x_{11}x_7-x_8x_9,\quad x_{13}x_3-x_{12}x_4.
\]

These use three disjoint four-variable blocks, with x_2 and x_10 free. Each block is the rank-at-most-one 2-by-2 determinantal cone. Its ring k[a,b,c,d]/(ab-cd) is geometrically integral: the determinant remains irreducible over every field extension, as its primitive linear polynomial in a has relatively prime coefficient b and constant cd. Products of these geometrically integral affine varieties remain integral. Thus the local ideal is prime, has height three, and defines a dimension-eleven complete intersection.

Every local generator maps to zero under triangle evaluation, as checked by equality of the corresponding incidence rows. The triangle incidence matrix has rank eleven over Q, hence its monomial image algebra has Krull dimension eleven over any field. The triangle toric kernel is prime of height three. Inclusion of the local prime in this prime, with equal heights in the polynomial ring, forces equality.

Independently, the three generators have pairwise coprime squarefree leading monomials. They form a Groebner basis by the product criterion; the squarefree initial ideal certifies radicality. This is consistent with, but not a substitute for, the primeness proof.

## Disposition

The six-point local ideal equals the triangle toric ideal over every field. The checker verifies disjoint blocks, leading terms, incidence identities and rank. Removing a cross term incorrectly forces a nonzero normal form to vanish, providing a deliberate control. No corresponding ideal equality at higher polygon size follows from this disjoint-block argument.
