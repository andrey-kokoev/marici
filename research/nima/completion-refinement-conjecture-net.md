## Reference lifecycle

`exports/standard-local-lift.head.json` declares the active exported revision. The immutable export pins source contract, result and evidence hashes. Any source/evidence change invalidates the live import. Marking the head revoked or replacing it with a reviewed new export also invalidates the old pointer. The consumer must explicitly adopt the replacement after reviewing scope, outcome and verification level. There is no automatic re-export/re-pinning on a check.

The successor checker writes a blocked receipt and no scientific outcome if reference or contract validation fails. Validation must be rerun before consuming a saved result; an old pass is not a permanently valid authorization. Its receipt carries the imported dependency hashes so a later export can retain the ancestor dependency chain.

The successor checker admits the recorded incomplete half-turn attempts and the specifically validated fresh unconditional norm certificate. It checks source, compiler and library hashes for that proof and rejects cached-only or conditional norm promotion. Other ++ outcomes still require theorem-specific admission, not just editing `outcome`. The checker retains continuity and convergent-Machin checkpoints as historical supporting evidence. Full regularity, flow, accepted pi, and assembled half-turn each have separate theorem-specific admission; component receipts alone cannot promote them. Historically this removed the Cayley bridge from the pi node's remaining obligations without resolving that node.
