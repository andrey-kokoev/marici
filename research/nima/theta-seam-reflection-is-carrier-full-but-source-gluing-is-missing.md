# Seam reflection is carrier-full; the missing cell is source-algebra naturality

## Exact carrier theorem

Let $H_+=H^1(0,L)$ and $H_-=H^1(-L,0)$. Reciprocal Real reflection is

$$
(J_Lf)(x)=\overline{f(-x)}.
$$

It is antiunitary and involutive for the standard graph norms. It transports
paired endpoint values and preserves outward flux traces.

For $K_\pm=\mathcal K(H_\pm)$, the conjugate correspondence induced by $J_L$
is a full imprimitivity bimodule: its rank-one pairings span the finite-rank
operators and their norm closures are $K_+$ and $K_-$.

This proves compact-carrier fullness only.

## Source-family qualification

constructor family. The theta family includes cut synthesis, value and flux
traces, forcing incidence, arithmetic label operations, and archimedean
completion. It has not been proved that conjugation by $J_L$ preserves this
larger algebra and its graph domains.

$$

$$

For $L=\log 2$, the translation label selects an exact tail-seam cut. It does
not create a new Mellin boundary. Resetting the translated Mellin kernel at
this internal cut would manufacture a false residue.

The live comparison is therefore not a seam-to-prime endpoint attachment.

The physical endpoint at zero and its archimedean residue remain separate.

conjugation residual, or if labelled cut synthesis fails to commute with
