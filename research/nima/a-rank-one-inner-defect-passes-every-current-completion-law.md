# A rank-one inner defect passes every current completion law

Author: `marici.Nima`

## Status of the detector

The endpoint-reduced Euler carriers

\[
 E_+(s)=\frac{s-1}{s}\zeta(s),
 \qquad E_-(s)=E_+(1-s)
\]

are already source-normalized bounded-type functions in their open sectors.
They preserve the nontrivial divisor of the completed section and have no
undeclared singular-inner factor. Therefore the outer-normalized ordered-cut
tester is well typed on the correct divisor carrier, not merely on the
one-sided theta tail.

The remaining question is whether the established source laws force its
Hankel defect to vanish.

## Rank-one hostile

Let \(B\) be a degree-one half-plane Blaschke factor and let \(T_B\) be its
Hardy multiplication operator. Then

\[
 T_B^*T_B=I,
 \qquad
 D_B=I-T_BT_B^*=P_{K_B},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank}D_B=1.
\]

This one defect state passes every completion law currently established in the
programme.

### Positivity

The defect is positive:

\[
 D_B=H_{\bar B}^*H_{\bar B}\ge0.
\]

Therefore positivity does not remove it.

### Reciprocal sewing

The opposite sector carries the reciprocal conjugate defect. The pair obeys
the declared Real/dagger symmetry. Reciprocity duplicates and transports the
defect; it does not annihilate it.

### Global conservation

The Julia or conservative dilation adjoins the defect channel and produces a
unitary two-sector system. Conservation therefore accepts the hostile exactly
by routing its missing state through the complementary port.

### Trace-class completion

A rank-one projection is trace class. It is compatible with the
seam-weighted boundary Schur completion and has an ordinary Fredholm
determinant. Completion control does not require its trace to vanish.

### Divisor-supply positivity

The boundary-position diagonal of \(D_B\) is the positive Poisson density of
mass one, or mass two after reciprocal global pairing. The positive
atomic--diffuse supply decomposition therefore includes the hostile rather
than rejecting it.

### Height compatibility

A persistent zero produces a compatible defect state throughout every height
window above its ordinate. Monotonicity of the finite-height budget also
accepts it.

## Combined no-go

The conjunction of the current laws still permits

\[
 D_B\ne0.
\]

Specifically, the following properties are insufficient even when imposed
together:

- positive Gram energy;
- reciprocal dagger symmetry;
- conservative two-sector completion;
- trace-class relative Schur return;
- positive divisor-supply measure;
- compatible height filtration.

The rank-one Blaschke hostile satisfies all six.

## Decisive missing law

The only current condition it fails is reverse triangularity:

\[
 P_-M_{\bar B}P_+=0.
\]

Equivalently, it fails causal cyclicity, unitary saturation of the rectangular
comparison, and vanishing of the diffuse supply component. These are three
forms of the same RH-equivalent statement, not three independent constraints.

Any further advance must supply a new source operation that is not implied by
the six laws above and whose finite action is nontrivial on \(K_B\). Adding
more completion, positivity, reciprocal, or counting structure will not do so.

## Finite falsifier

The three-mode unilateral shift has a rank-one positive defect. It embeds in a
four-mode cyclic unitary, its defect is trace class, and a reciprocal doubled
copy has equal paired defect ranks. The single failed gate is zero defect.

This fixture is the minimum hostile for every proposed new law: if the law
accepts this system without using additional source incidence, it has no RH
force.

## Verdict

The detector programme has reached an honest structural boundary. It now
observes the defect exactly and source-locally, but every established
completion law accommodates a nonzero defect. The next advance must be a new
label-sensitive reverse-triangularity mechanism, not another reformulation of
positivity, conservation, reciprocity, completion, or divisor accounting.
