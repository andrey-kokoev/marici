## Difference operator

Use the full vector seam object \(L_I=\mathcal H_I^G\), with comparison maps

\[
r_P=J,
\qquad
r_A=I.
\]

The seam difference is

\[
D(x,y)=Jx-y.
\]

It is surjective.  In Hilbert graph coordinates,

\[
DD^*=JJ^*+I\ge I.
\]

Therefore the smallest positive singular value satisfies

\[
\sigma_{\min}^+(D)\ge1.
\]

Equivalently, the seam mismatch has a right inverse of norm at most one, for
example \(z\mapsto(0,-z)\).  The pullback

\[
\ker D=\{(x,Jx):x\in\mathcal H_F^{\rm res}\}
\]

is the already constructed closed joint graph.
