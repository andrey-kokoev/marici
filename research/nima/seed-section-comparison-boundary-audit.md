# Comparing the two seed sections: actual filler-policy audit

## Reused prior results

`boundary-generated-questions.md` defines a pointed comparison filler K(a,b) as an equivalence of interpreted carriers with a witness taking the marked value of a to that of b. An actual compare-rule application also needs derivations of both endpoints under a specified source policy. The generic filler does not automatically preserve extra seed structure.

`voevodsky-overlap-readout-gates-review.md` records the observation-respecting refinement: every input's reading must commute with the supplied equivalence. This is a presentation-identification requirement, not a demand that all physical dynamics preserve every observation.

`reference-cone-and-typed-successor.md` separates shared reference, source alignment, and actual higher filler. A common target alone does not supply parallel higher boundaries. `typed-assembly-boundary-and-mixed-defect.md` shows that retaining shared legs can carry nonzero mixed responses; additive discrepancy cancellation cannot replace the response-compatible filler.

## Apply the comparison test to the actual seed

Take X to be its four source sections (the finite carrier underlying the unit-family adapter in `SeedAttachedObserver.agda`). Mark

    q=(AD,BC,CA,DB),
    r=(AB,BC,CA,DB).

Both contain BC, CA, DB. Their differences are AD versus AB. These chosen endpoints were supplied in the earlier bridge; no constructor has selected this pair as physical species.

| Declared comparison policy | Fillers carrying q to r | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| All pointed bijections of X | 6 | Generic pointed comparison exists |
| Bijections induced by vertex automorphisms preserving the six seed arrows | 0 | No such seed symmetry identifies them |
| Bijections preserving the target-image cardinality reader on all X | 0 | Their named structural readings differ |

The seed has exactly two vertex automorphisms: identity and the simultaneous swap A<->B, C<->D. Both fix q as a section. The reader |{target(e):e in section}| is four for q and three for r and is invariant under both seed automorphisms. Thus the failure of a structure-respecting identification is witnessed by an explicit structural observable, not merely by different labels.

This is a finite set specialization of the prior boundary policies. The checker does not construct an admitted native compare-rule derivation or claim a fresh formal observation-gate theorem.

## Result

The sections are distinguishable relative to a declared seed-structural observation. Generic package comparison alone would allow their identification, because it does not require this observation. This makes concrete why the prior two-observer package did not establish an interacting pair.

Rejecting a presentation identification does NOT prohibit a state-changing operation from q to r. Such an operation could change the reader. Nor does shared arrow support establish a coupling: the replacement AD->AB specifies an edit but not its admissibility, mechanism, cost or stability.

The next relevant source requirement is a seed-derived rule permitting and responding to such edits, with its typed boundary, retained witnesses and rung4 observation. We should not manufacture an equivalence that erases the detected distinction, or treat a vanishing additive difference as a realized interaction filler. The prior mixed-response machinery supplies a concrete pattern to investigate, but its 137-slot matrices cannot be transferred to these sections without an explicit adapter.
