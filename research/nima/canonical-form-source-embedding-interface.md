# Source-embedding interface for canonical-form promotion

## Question

What exact source-owned object would close the remaining gap between the derived residue calculus and an associahedral canonical-form identification?

## Claim boundary

This packet specifies an interface and acceptance tests. It does not construct or authorize the positive-geometry embedding.

For each polygon size `n`, the missing object is a source-derived tuple

\[
E_n=(K_n,\iota_n,\{X_c\},P_n,\nu_n),
\]

where `K_n` is a dimension-`n-3` affine or projective kinematic space, `iota_n` maps it to the planar-channel space, each pulled-back channel `X_c` is a nonzero affine facet equation, `P_n` is the positive region, and `nu_n` is an oriented volume normalization.

The interface is accepted only if:

1. the `X_c=0` facets are distinct and their nonempty intersections are exactly the noncrossing dissections;
2. every facet restriction identifies with the product of the two regional source spaces, including support constants and orientations;
3. for every triangulation `T`, the determinant of the channel gradients indexed by `T`, measured against `nu_n`, gives the coefficient and sign required to turn the scalar triangulation sum into the top form;
4. the resulting form has only simple channel poles and no additional infinity-divisor pole;
5. the zero-dimensional base form and its sign are stated;
6. any claimed physical interpretation includes the sector map into `K_n` and its normalization or boundary-value prescription.

At five points the existing reference slice passes items 1, 3, and 4 as a declared affine construction, but no source arrow selects it. Exact positive common-Jacobian rivals pass the same recursion and infinity tests. Consequently recursion, positivity, and projective regularity cannot substitute for `iota_n` and its provenance.

## Disposition

The remaining blocker is the first missing typed object `E_n`, not another residue calculation. A supplied packet passes when it provides formulas and provenance for the tuple and reproduces the six tests above at five points before any generic promotion is attempted.
