# Many-many Xi characteristic-state vector

For `s` away from `0` and `1`, solving the first two rows of

$$
\mathsf M(s)u=0
$$

with radial amplitude `c` gives

$$
 u(s,c)=c\begin{pmatrix}-1/s\\-1/(s-1)\\1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The third row reduces to

$$
H(s)+\frac1{s(s-1)}=0,
$$

which is equivalent to

$$
1+H(s)s(s-1)=0
$$

and hence to `xi(s)=0` under the forced normalization.

This explicit state vector is the input for the sesquilinear Green calculation. The endpoint amplitudes are rational functions of `s`, while the radial amplitude is the free normalization `c`.
