# Arbitrary-n orientation coherence by ambient restriction

Fix `alpha`. Its triangulation complex is the boundary complex of the associahedron and hence an orientable simplicial sphere. Choose an orientation of every top-dimensional `alpha`-planar tree so that the two facets incident to each ridge induce opposite ridge orientations.

For any second order `beta`, the common-tree complex is generated by a subset of these oriented facets. Restrict the ambient facet orientation to that subset. Every ridge has one of three incidences:

1. zero selected facets: it is absent;
2. one selected facet: it is a boundary ridge;
3. two selected facets: their coefficients cancel because they already cancel in the oriented ambient sphere.

Therefore the selected top chain satisfies

\[
\partial[K(\alpha,\beta)]_{rel}
=\sum_{R\text{ incident to exactly one common facet}}\epsilon_R[R].
\]

No finite census is needed. Every connected common-tree pseudomanifold is orientable at arbitrary `n`; if it has no free ridges, its oriented chain is an absolute cycle, and otherwise it is a relative chain supported exactly on its free-ridge boundary.

This proves globally the orientation-coherence property checked exhaustively at nine points. It does not by itself prove purity, connectedness, shellability, or that the only closed selection is the full sphere.
