1. `tower-generator-singleton-obstruction.md`: repeated key-preserving promotion yields singleton wrappers; a common-target policy gives a different coherent progression. Reconstruction and coherence alone do not select the generator.
2. `promotion-factor-independence.md`: copied/retained presentations do not create another independently variable operand. Independent products must be explicit, with their two projection classes checked.

5. `proton-electron-shared-state-comparison-hypothesis.md`: the proposed 1836 domain does not independently construct both particle energies or the electron unit.

## What counts as the full-cycle specification currently

There is an established typed presentation/retention calculus and a confirmed rung transport interface. There are several explicit successor and dynamics candidates. In the inspected chain, there is NOT one demonstrated identification of all these candidates as the intended physical cycle. Section promotion, line-graph promotion, incoming-family promotion, source-square expansion and spectral body replacement have different domains and outputs. Their shared terminology does not make them interchangeable.

Therefore the audit cannot honestly name a uniquely selected full physical generator already implemented. It also cannot claim an exhaustive absence theorem over the repository. The concrete evidence is that the inspected primary notes explicitly state their remaining selection/adapter assumptions, and the implementations preserve those assumptions.
