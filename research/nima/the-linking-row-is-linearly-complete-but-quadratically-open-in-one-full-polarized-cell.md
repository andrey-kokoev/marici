# The linking row is linearly complete but quadratically open in one full polarized cell

## Question

What exactly remains missing before the ordered linking port becomes a frozen row of the five-port G4 adjoint?

## Claim boundary

The linking row is complete as a continuous source-level linear port: its wall functional is bounded, its ordered sign is fixed, and the Stokes-to-Wronskian comparison is a nuclear projective map with a defined transpose. The remaining obstruction is quadratic and finite primewise: identify one full two-output Green cell, not another scalar loading or completion estimate.

## Constructed linear data

On the retained wall/incidence graph, the ordered linking form

\[
\mathfrak L_c(f,g)
=ic\left(
\overline{\gamma_0f}\,\eta(g)
-
\overline{\eta(f)}\,\gamma_0g
\right)
\]

is bounded. Reciprocal slot reversal changes its sign, the graph radical is zero, and finite label cutoffs commute.

The source-derived Stokes-to-Wronskian coefficient is

\[
\lambda_p=\frac{-\kappa_p}{2s_p}>0.
\]

The diagonal comparison

\[
Te_p=\lambda_pe_p
\]

is nuclear on the Hilbert rung, continuous and regularizing on the projective arithmetic source, cutoff-diagonal, and equipped with the same diagonal transpose. No inverse comparison is required.

These facts close topology, sign, coefficient, cutoff, and transpose existence for the linear linking port.

## Exact quadratic target

Let the source and target two-output Hermitian cells be

\[
G_S=
\begin{pmatrix}
a_S&r_S+i\ell_S\\
r_S-i\ell_S&d_S
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
G_W=
\begin{pmatrix}
a_W&r_W+i\ell_W\\
r_W-i\ell_W&d_W
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

With

\[
T_p=\operatorname{diag}(\alpha_p,\lambda_p),
\]

the required comparison is

\[
G_S=T_p^*G_WT_p.
\]

It is equivalent to

\[
a_S=\alpha_p^2a_W,
\]

\[
d_S=\lambda_p^2d_W,
\]

\[
r_S+i\ell_S
=
\alpha_p\lambda_p(r_W+i\ell_W).
\]

The last equality contributes independent real-even and oriented-odd tests unless a source symmetry removes the even mixed entry.

## What existing scalar results prove

The forced positive scalar \(\lambda_p\) and bounded ordered Stokes form determine the odd-line normalization and orientation. They do not prove:

- the even comparison coefficient \(\alpha_p\);
- the two diagonal energy identities;
- the real mixed-tail identity;
- simultaneous placement of positive and linking forms on one G4 carrier.

A correct determinant, odd norm, or positivity margin is necessary but not sufficient.

## Minimal G4 witness

The linking row can be frozen after G4 exposes, for every prime block:

1. source and target ordered two-output bases;
2. the matrices \(G_S\) and \(G_W\) in those bases;
3. the source-derived even coefficient \(\alpha_p\);
4. the already forced \(\lambda_p\);
5. verification of the three displayed matrix identities;
6. continuity of all four output-channel blocks under projective completion;
7. compatibility with the reciprocal response action.

No additional free linking scalar is permitted.

## Hostile basis

A checker must reject:

1. reversed \(\ell_W\) with the correct odd norm;
2. correct diagonals with mixed blocks erased;
3. determinant equality without matrix equality;
4. correct \(\lambda_p\) with a fitted \(\alpha_p\);
5. primewise identities whose mixed block is not closable globally;
6. the summed Pauli frame substituted for the full output linking Gram.

## Five-port consequence

The native linking row is not “unevaluated” because of missing topology or arithmetic loading. It is unevaluated because G4 has not supplied the full polarized target cell. Once that cell is exposed, the remaining test is finite primewise matrix equality followed by projective continuity and reciprocal assembly.

The reciprocal row remains a separate global response-intertwining problem.

## Disposition

The active linking audit terminates with a sharp reduction: linear construction is complete; quadratic Green transport is exactly one two-output matrix identity per prime, with three or four real scalar checks. The current SCC witness does not expose those matrices or \(\alpha_p\), so promotion to a G4 adjoint row remains blocked. No RH conclusion is authorized.
