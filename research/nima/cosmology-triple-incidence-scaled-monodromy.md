# The local triple-incidence family has trivial punctured-base monodromy

For \(p\neq0\), set

\[
u=pU,\qquad v=pV.
\]

Then

\[
q_1=pU,\qquad q_2=pV,\qquad q_3=p(U+V+1),
\]

so every punctured-base fiber is identified with the fixed arrangement

\[
UV(U+V+1)=0.
\]

A full loop around \(p=0\) returns this trivialization without permuting the
walls. The relative two-form has integral scaling exponent \(2-3=-1\), hence
its leading \(p^{-1}\) factor is meromorphic and single-valued. The
Cayley--Menger function is generically nonzero at the collision, so the chosen
square-root germ also has trivial local \(p\)-inertia.

Consequently local Betti monodromy is the identity and

\[
\operatorname{rank}(T_p-I)=0.
\]

The source-authorized \(p-i0\) prescription selects a boundary value of the
meromorphic germ, but it does not generate a \(\mu_2\)-odd thimble. Global deck
oddness of the coefficient is not induced by the local \(p\)-loop.

This statement is local. It does not classify contours encountering remote
singularities on other continued sheets.
