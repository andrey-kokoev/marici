# Prospective admission packet: theta Stieltjes measure

## Frozen before the theorem outcome

The proposed extension is the positive order-two Stieltjes representation

\[
H'(x)=\int_0^\infty
\frac{d\nu(a)}{(x-\tfrac14+a)^2},
\qquad \nu\ge0,
\]

derived from the fixed completed theta source without supplying nontrivial-zero
locations.

This packet freezes its admission route before a proof or disproof of the
representation.

## Admission type

The proposal claims novel governed explanatory content.  A signed or complex
resolvent representation would be a formal re-expression, but positivity and
support on \([0,\infty)\) imply the critical-line singular-support theorem.
Therefore the proposal is not route \(U\).

Its declared route is

\[
\boxed{P=\text{independently source-provenanced content},}
\]

conditional on deriving \(\nu\ge0\) directly from the fixed completed theta
Carrier.  Finite positive quadrature, numerical zero recovery, or assuming RH
does not satisfy this provenance gate.

## Operator typing: the live reflection object

The word *reflection* does not authorize identifying two different
operators.

The smooth theta compression

\[
P_+C_kRP_+^*
\]

is already excluded as the desired spectral realization: it is a smoothing,
trace-class Hankel operator with superpolynomial spectral decay, so its
Fredholm zero census is too sparse for the completed \(\Xi\)-function.

The admitted target is instead the source-derived **additive-time** pair

\[
K_{t_0}(s,u)=\Theta(t_0+s+u),
\qquad
K^+_{t_0}(s,u)=-\partial_t\Theta(t_0+s+u).
\]

Their finite Gram forms are the ordinary and shifted Stieltjes Hankel forms.
Both must arise coherently from one completed-theta semigroup functional;
choosing an abstract involution or a positive form after seeing the desired
moments is route \(F\), not route \(P\).

Thus the missing construction is not another determinant census. It is the
canonical source map

\[
\boxed{
\text{completed theta Carrier}
\longrightarrow
\text{additive-time positive semigroup functional}
\longrightarrow
(K_{t_0},K^+_{t_0})

\]

whose GNS quotient makes time translation a positive multiplication/Jacobi
operator. This map must also explain why the shifted form is the translate
of the same source object rather than an independently imposed inequality.

## Forgetting map

\[
\mathfrak F(\nu)(x)

No normalization, polynomial correction, or support term may be fitted after
the positivity calculation.

## Independent consequences

Admission predicts simultaneously:

1. all ordinary and shifted Stieltjes Hankel forms of the normalized theta
   derivative moments are positive semidefinite;
2. reconstructed measures at different basepoints agree under the exact
   resolvent translation law;
3. the representing measure is determinate under an independently verified
   growth criterion;
4. the singular support of \(H'\) lies on the permitted real cut;
5. the rank-two Schwarzian numerator and its derived hierarchy have the
   corresponding positivity/complete-monotonicity consequences;
6. pulling support back through \(x=(s-\tfrac12)^2\) confines nontrivial zeros
   to \(\Re s=\tfrac12\).

These are consequences of one source construction, not separate fitted
assumptions.

## Rejection conditions

Reject admission—even if finite quadrature continues to recover plausible
zero ordinates—if any of the following occurs:

1. an exact required Hankel form is negative;
2. fixed-source moment towers at two basepoints violate the resolvent
   translation identity;
3. positivity requires inserting known zero locations or assuming RH;
4. the reconstructed positive measures are indeterminate and no source datum
   selects one;
5. the forgetting map differs from the fixed \(H'\) by a fitted correction;
6. the measure is positive only after discarding a source-derived sector with
   no universal or support-theoretic authorization.
7. the proposed reflection realization is the excluded smooth compression,
   or the ordinary and shifted forms cannot be derived from one
   additive-time semigroup functional.
