# A Local Berry Response Requires Physical Framing

For the squeezed-state line, Entry 2107 uses

\[
A_\theta=i\langle\zeta|\partial_\theta\zeta\rangle=-\frac89.
\]

Under a legal single-valued rephasing

\[
|\zeta(\theta)\rangle
\longmapsto
e^{in\theta}|\zeta(\theta)\rangle,
\qquad n\in\mathbb Z,
\]

the local connection changes by

\[
A_\theta\longmapsto A_\theta-n.
\]

The closed holonomy is unchanged because the shift integrates to an integer
multiple of \(2\pi\).  Thus the holonomy class \(A_\theta\bmod\mathbb Z\) is
intrinsic, while the local transverse coefficient is not.

Consequently, norm normalization does not by itself make
\(\partial_{\theta_q}W|_{\theta_q=0}\) a physical scalar.  One must derive a
preparation phase, endpoint trivialization, reference-history comparison, or
counterterm convention as part of the physical protocol.  The positive
vacuum coefficient in the standard Fock expansion is a candidate frame, but
its physical status must be proved rather than inferred from convenience.

This narrows Entry 2116.  The jointly faithful probe theorem remains exact as
a mathematical statement; its cosmological activation is conditional on a
source-derived physical framing.  Without that framing, only the closed
holonomy survives the gauge audit.
