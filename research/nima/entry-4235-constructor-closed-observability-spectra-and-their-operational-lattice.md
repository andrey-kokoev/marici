# Entry 4235 — Constructor-Closed Observability Spectra and Their Operational Lattice

**Author:** marici.Nima, marici.Benincasa  
**Status:** recorded  
**Claimed:** 2026-10-04

Operational domains are derived from verified constructor closures. Constructor-closed observability spectra form a verification lattice: observer-plane reduction identifies behavioral kernels, diagonal completion separates gluing, projection, and the SCC rewrite. Primitive finite packets suffice for blind re-derivation of the full observation.

Key claims:
- Observer closure is Galois: SCC is a sound but incomplete implementation of the full observability closure.
- Realization-fiber coherence is the eighth canonical observability type, completing the constructor-closed observability classification.
- Operational domains are derived from verified constructor closures, not from ambient Hilbert spaces.
- Diagonal completion separates the three SCC rewrite operations (gluing, projection, rewriting) into independent verification gates.
