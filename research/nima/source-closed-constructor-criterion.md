## Source-closed constructor theorem

Let `A:S->C` define source authority.  A task `p` has a source-generated
constructor precisely when its task, interaction, record marginal, reusable
resource return, and sequential/coarse-graining coherence form one diagram in
the authority closure of `A`.

This statement is more restrictive than merely listing required resources:
the maps must be **source-closed**.  They may not be assembled from mutually
incompatible ambient completions.  In particular:

- a faithful family `X->R_i` cannot supply the missing successor leg;
- controllable endomorphisms of `X` cannot supply a pointer comodule;
- an arbitrary dilation cannot supply source authority;
- an idempotent selected after a fit cannot supply the task;
- separate valid maps do not form a constructor unless their descent and
  sequential squares commute.

Necessity is immediate from restricting an actual constructor diagram to its
five faces.  Sufficiency follows by composing the source-authorized faces:
the interaction implements the task, its record marginal supplies the
outcome, resource return permits another attempt, and coherence makes repeated
and coarse-grained executions well typed.

## What the withheld test establishes

The test does not prove that Criterion C is the only possible formulation.
It does establish a nontrivial prediction not used in deriving it:

> Jointly faithful, source-normalized, horizontally compatible observer maps
> can still fail to define any physical instrument, because faithfulness is a
> property of outgoing distinctions while an instrument also requires an
> authorized successor-state leg.

That distinction is now replicated in flavor and cosmology by different
coefficient systems.  It is therefore not an artifact of finite-dimensional
control algebra.

## Remaining severe falsifier

The criterion should next be tested on a sector whose source genuinely
contains an apparatus cycle rather than another passive observer complex.  A
failure would be decisive if all five source-closed faces exist but no
constructor can be composed, because that would reveal a missing higher
coherence condition in the theorem.
