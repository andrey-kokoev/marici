# An active coherencer is an open resource-bearing transducer

## Question

What additional structure distinguishes a transistor-like controlled channel
from a passive modulator, and where does the residue go when the observed
relationship appears to close?

## Five irreducible roles

The four-port electrical picture still conflates signal with resource. The
minimal active abstraction has five roles:

1. source signal;
2. drain readout;
3. gate or control;
4. body, orientation, or reference frame;
5. resource/environment port carrying bias supply, exported flux, noise, heat,
   or completion-at-infinity data.

These are roles, not necessarily five physical wires. One physical terminal
may combine several roles, but that reduction needs a source law.

## Residue routing

An active coherencer need not annihilate a lower residue. It may route the
residue into the declared resource port while making the enlarged system
coherent. If (E_s) is signal energy, (E_r) supplied resource, (E_o)
observed output, and (E_h) exported residue, the enlarged balance is

\[
E_s+E_r=E_o+E_h.
\]

Projecting away the resource port makes (E_o>E_s) look like unexplained
gain. Projecting away the exported residue makes loss or decoherence appear to
have vanished.

Thus the coherencer is the constitutive law on the enlarged carrier. The
residue is a boundary flow of that law, not necessarily a defect to be set to
zero.

## Exact hostile

Let an amplitude-one signal produce amplitude three. The input and output
quadratic energies are one and nine. A passive two-port account violates its
resource equation by eight. An active account can close with supplied resource
eight and no exported loss, or with supplied resource ten and exported loss
two.

The same scalar output cannot distinguish these two resource histories. A
resource or thermal readout is therefore required for provenance of gain.

## Connectivity and orientation are distinct controls

A bridge may connect two sectors while remaining blind to an internal complex
orientation. Conversely, an orientation character may exist only as a global
ancillary phase and fail to act on the bridge. A working active coherencer
requires both:

- a connectivity port constructing the mixed carrier;
- an orientation port acting nontrivially on that carrier.

The coupling between them is another source constructor. Neither matching
characters nor simultaneous availability proves that coupling.

## Current sector readings

### Flavor

Visible Higgs operations identify opposite mixing signs through hidden-port
conjugation. A Higgs-to-exit cross port would create a new relational
experiment over a smaller stabilizer groupoid. It is not a passive refinement
of the old readout.

### Topological computation

The (B)-charge bridge supplies sector connectivity but is blind to
three-cycle orientation. An oriented (C_3) character port and an explicit
action on the bridge are separately required. A global ancillary phase is not
a gate on the logical carrier.

### RH

The endpoint, primitive, square, seam, archimedean, and completion-at-infinity
channels may collectively occupy the resource/environment role. This is only
a typing proposal. They cannot be discarded as divergences if they carry the
boundary flow that closes the enlarged conservation law.

The chamber gate, source-observer channel, body/reference data, and resource
port must remain distinct. A scalar completed coefficient projects all of them
and cannot reconstruct their incidence or resource provenance.

## DPC

An active-transistor interpretation is admitted only if it supplies:

1. an independent resource port or an exact source reduction showing where
   the resource enters;
2. a resource balance including exported residue;
3. a gate operation that modulates the source-drain channel without being
   identified with its supply;
4. a separate orientation/body reference or an authorized reduction;
5. a connectivity constructor and an orientation action on the constructed
   carrier;
6. readouts jointly faithful on signal, control, reference, and resource
   histories;
7. a fault model for hidden-port corruption and environmental feedback.

## Claim boundary

This is a structural DPC and exact finite resource witness. It does not assert
that RH is dissipative, identify arithmetic currents with physical heat, or
prove gain in any sector. The resource/environment interpretation remains a
source question.

## Disposition

The transistor analogy deepens into an open-system theorem: active coherence
is conservation on an enlarged resource-bearing carrier. Apparent residue
elimination may instead be lawful residue export. Any sector claiming gain or
closure must preserve that port or derive its elimination.
