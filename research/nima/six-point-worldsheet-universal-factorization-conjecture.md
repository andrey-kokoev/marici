# Six-point worldsheet universal-factorization theorem programme

## Problem

Specify a non-tautological sense in which every residue-compatible cross-order color descent must pass through scattering-equation or worldsheet structure.

## Bold conjecture

Let `V_6` be the 24-dimensional DDM ordering module over the generic six-point kinematic field, and let `B_6` be the submodule generated by fundamental BCJ relations. Let

\[
W_6:V_6\longrightarrow H^3_\omega(\mathcal M_{0,6})
\]

send each ordering to its Parke–Taylor twisted-cohomology class. The conjecture is:

1. `ker(W_6)=B_6`;
2. `W_6` is compatible with every stable-boundary residue;
3. any cross-order realization that annihilates BCJ and commutes with factorization residues factors uniquely through `W_6`;
4. the factorization is natural under all six-point-to-lower-point boundary maps.

## First theorem proved

At the exact generic fixture, `B_6` has rank 18. The quotient `V_6/B_6` has dimension six. An explicit quotient matrix `P` and section `S` satisfy

\[
PS=I_6,
\qquad
PB_6^{\mathsf T}=0.
\]

For every tested admissible linear map `F=L P`, the unique factor is recovered as

\[
L=FS,
\qquad
F=(FS)P.
\]

This proves the finite linear universal property of the BCJ quotient. It does not identify that quotient with worldsheet twisted cohomology.

## Worldsheet kernel theorem

Represent each of the 24 DDM Parke–Taylor factors as an element of the six-dimensional saturated scattering-equation coordinate algebra. The resulting class map

\[
W_6:V_6\longrightarrow A_{\mathrm{SE}}
\]

has rank six and kernel dimension 18. Every one of the 120 BCJ rows lies in its kernel. Since the BCJ rowspace also has dimension 18,

\[
\ker W_6=B_6.
\]

The Parke–Taylor and BCJ rowspaces are complementary in the 24-dimensional ordering module. Therefore the previously constructed universal quotient is canonically identified, at the fixture, with the worldsheet solution algebra.

## Named rivals

1. Another six-dimensional quotient realizes the same BCJ relations without a worldsheet interpretation.
2. Parke–Taylor classes span the quotient but have a larger kernel on special kinematic loci.
3. The vector-space factorization exists while residue naturality fails on a stable boundary.
4. Boundary-compatible factorization requires additional numerator data and is not universal for Parke–Taylor classes alone.

## Strongest next falsifier

Construct the Parke–Taylor class map in one explicit six-element twisted-cohomology basis and verify symbolically that its kernel is exactly `B_6`. Then compute its maps on every stable divisor of `Mbar_0,6`. A single extra kernel vector or one noncommuting boundary square rejects the conjecture.

## Complete Parke–Taylor boundary functor

All 25 stable divisors of `Mbar_0,6` and all 24 DDM Parke–Taylor orderings have been checked, giving 600 ordering-divisor pairs. A subset `S` has a logarithmic residue exactly when it forms one cyclic interval in the ordering. In that case the residue factors into the two lower-point Parke–Taylor cyclic words obtained by replacing the node on each component. Each ordering has exactly nine such planar divisors, for 216 nonzero logarithmic residues overall.

## Scattering-equation boundary naturality

For every one of the 25 stable divisors and every external leg, the exact Laurent-leading scattering equation has the required lower-point form. On the clustered component,

\[
\lim_{\varepsilon\to0}\varepsilon E_i^{(6)}
=E_i^{(S)}.
\]

On the complementary component, all clustered legs combine into the nodal momentum:

\[
\lim_{\varepsilon\to0}E_i^{(6)}
=E_i^{(S^c\cup\{*\})},
\qquad k_* = \sum_{j\in S}k_j.
\]

All 150 leg-divisor pairs pass. Together with complete Parke–Taylor residue factorization, this proves that scattering-equation-generated BCJ exactness is stable under every six-point boundary residue.

## Disposition

The conjecture passes. On the Zariski-open generic six-point locus, the Parke–Taylor scattering-equation map has kernel exactly the BCJ module, realizes the universal six-dimensional quotient of the 24 ordering module, and commutes with all stable-boundary residues. Consequently every linear cross-order color descent that annihilates BCJ and respects these residues factors uniquely through the worldsheet quotient.

The generic promotion uses symbolic BCJ membership and boundary identities together with exact nonzero maximal minors at the rational fixture. Exceptional kinematic loci may have larger kernels and are excluded. The theorem identifies the necessary quotient structure; it does not claim that `Mbar_0,6` is the only geometric presentation of that quotient.
