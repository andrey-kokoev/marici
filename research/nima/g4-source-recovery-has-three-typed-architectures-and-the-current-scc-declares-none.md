# G4 source recovery has three typed architectures and the current SCC declares none

## Question

What exact interface declaration would decide whether G4 supports source recovery and radical descent after common-history synthesis?

## Claim boundary

The preceding recovery and no-go results leave three non-equivalent architectures. Each has a distinct required observer field and verification law. The current coarse G4 descriptor declares none of them, so injectivity, closed range, radical annihilation, and prime-return placement cannot be inferred from `all_interfaces_checked`.

## Architecture A: retained labels

G4 retains the projective direct sum through the return:

\[
\mathcal A_{\exp}
\overset{\mathcal I}{\longrightarrow}
\mathfrak H_{\exp}^{\rm lab}
\overset{\mathcal R}{\longrightarrow}
\mathcal A_{\exp},
\qquad
\mathcal R\mathcal I=I.
\]

The verified continuity law is

\[
q_\delta(\mathcal Rh)
\le C_{\varepsilon}
Q_{\delta+1/2+\varepsilon}(h).
\]

Required G4 fields are:

- prime and grade projections;
- orientation projections;
- labelled target seminorms;
- return placement before codiagonalization;
- metric compatibility with those projections.

This is the only architecture already constructed on the source range.

## Architecture B: codiagonal plus deconvolved entire observer

G4 first forms the common history but exposes

\[
F_c(z)=\frac{\widehat h_c(z)}{\Xi(z)}
\]

as an independently typed observer. Two horizontal lines at heights \(\pm R\) then satisfy

\[
q_\delta(c)
\le K_{R-\delta-1/2}S_R(F_c),
\qquad
R>\delta+\frac32.
\]

Required G4 fields are:

- an entire deconvolved response object distinct from the raw history;
- two-line supremum seminorms for every requested source rung;
- Bohr coefficient extraction;
- proof that the observer is source-derived rather than defined by the desired inverse.

This architecture is continuously invertible once the observer is supplied, but no current G4 witness supplies it.

## Architecture C: codiagonal plus arithmetic localization

G4 codiagonalizes and then applies an observer tied to

\[
\{\pm k\log p\}.
\]

Such an observer is necessarily non-equivariant under arbitrary real translation if it avoids the Xi inverse. Required G4 fields are:

- the distinguished displacement lattice;
- localization or interpolation functionals indexed by \((p,k,\pm)\);
- a projective conditioning estimate;
- covariance under the admitted arithmetic translations, not all real translations;
- compatibility with the G4 metric and return.

No such observer has been constructed or exposed.

## Excluded implicit fourth architecture

A raw-history-only, translation-equivariant inverse in the declared topology is not available. Translation covariance forces multiplier \(1/\Xi\), whose gamma-factor growth is exponentially unbounded in horizontal frequency. Real-history exponential weights provide vertical Fourier extension, not the missing horizontal compensation.

Therefore G4 cannot leave the observer implicit and still claim continuous recovery.

## Conformance decision

A future G4 interface passes the recovery gate only if it selects exactly one of A, B, or C and supplies its corresponding witness law. The selection also decides prime-return placement:

- A returns before codiagonalization and is prime diagonal;
- B returns through Bohr coefficients after deconvolution;
- C returns through explicit arithmetic localization after codiagonalization.

Without this selection, cross-prime Gram blocks, radical annihilation, quotient descent, and closed-range claims remain untyped.

## Direction rescore

- Architecture A source theorem: completed; G4 comparison ownership-blocked.

- Architecture C: 6/10 mathematically, but source/interface absent.

## Disposition

The recovery frontier is now an interface decision rather than another scalar estimate. G4 must retain labels, expose the deconvolved two-line observer, or construct an arithmetic localization family. Until its owner supplies one of these interfaces, no G4 source-recovery or radical-descent theorem is authorized. No RH conclusion is authorized.
