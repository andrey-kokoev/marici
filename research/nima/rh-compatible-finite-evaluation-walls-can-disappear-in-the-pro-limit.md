# RH compatible finite evaluation walls can disappear in the pro-limit

## Result

Exact and truncation-compatible finite evaluation walls need not define a continuous wall on the inverse-limit carrier.

At cutoff \(N\), take

\[
M_N=I_N
\]

and

\[
R_N=(1,\ldots,1).
\]

Then

\[
R_N=L_NM_N
\]

with \(L_N=R_N\). Every finite kernel inclusion holds, and the factors are compatible under truncation.

But the inverse-limit carrier with its ordinary product topology consists of arbitrary sequences, while the formal limiting readout is

\[
R(x)=\sum_{n\ge1}x_n.
\]

This is not defined on every sequence and is not a continuous functional on the product. For the constant packet \(x_n=1\), the finite readouts grow as \(N\).

## Categorical meaning

Inverse limit and continuous dual do not commute here. The continuous dual of a product of coordinate lines contains only functionals depending on finitely many coordinates, while the compatible family \((1,\ldots,1)\) requires all coordinates.

Thus a compatible pro-morphism in the algebraic diagram need not become a morphism of completed topological carriers.

This is the exact +1-wall version of a partner escaping to infinity.

## Required source topology

The coefficient completion must make the endpoint aggregation continuous. Possible architectures include:

- an absolutely summable source space;
- a weighted sequence space with the evaluation coefficients in its continuous dual;
- a non-diagonal source-Gram completion;
- a rigged pairing in which the endpoint wall is a declared distribution.

The topology cannot be chosen merely to make the desired sum converge. It must be generated by the same arithmetic constructors and support prime shifts, seam jets, primitive and square currents, and reciprocal completion.

## DPC verdict

Candidate: finite exact factorization plus truncation compatibility.

Verdict: rejected by the constant-packet divergent sum.

Candidate: take the algebraic inverse limit and declare the formal sum.

Verdict: rejected because the sum is not a continuous morphism on the product carrier.

Surviving candidate: a source-selected coefficient topology in which the bordered equations, carrier port, local syzygies, and factorization are all continuous with cutoff-independent bounds.

## Finite falsifiers

For each cutoff, compute:

1. the sharp dual norm of the evaluation factor;
2. the readout of normalized packets supported near the top moment wall;
3. compatibility under the cutoff bonding maps.

Unbounded dual norms or a normalized packet with diverging or nonconvergent readout closes the pro-evaluation wall.
