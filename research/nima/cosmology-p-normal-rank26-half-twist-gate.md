# The physical half-twist adds one p-supported direction, but it is tangent

The earlier rank-26 p-normal computations used the generic twist `gamma=5`.
That coefficient does not type the physical square-root system. Recomputing at
`gamma=-1/2` changes the degree-eight Rees census over both `F_32003` and
`F_32009`:

- support excess changes from 13 to 14;
- length-one summands change from 7 to 8;
- length-two summands remain 6.

Resolved special-relation syzygies were recomputed through ambient degrees
8, 10, 12, 14, and 16. Their image ranks are respectively 8, 7, 11, 14, and 18 over
both primes. At every cutoff the two unit-normal images and the p-tangent
image are the same subspace. The intrinsic normal quotient is therefore zero
at every tested physical cutoff.

Thus generic-twist evidence cannot certify the physical target. The corrected
physical computation adds one degree-eight source direction, but it disappears
under cutoff enlargement and never survives modulo p-tangent transport. Additional finite cutoffs cannot promote this pattern to an unbounded theorem.
A source-level factorization identity would be needed for that promotion; none
is currently available. No unbounded no-go, tau map, or period is constructed.
