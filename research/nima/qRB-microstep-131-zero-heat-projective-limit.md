# qRB microstep 131: zero-heat projective limit

Let `E_t` be the heat-weighted endpoint functional at scale `t>0`. The zero-heat object is defined by its compatible finite-coordinate restrictions:

$$
E_0|_{F}=\lim_{t\downarrow0}E_t|_{F}
$$

for every finite pair packet `F`, rather than by an unweighted global pair sum.

This limit exists coordinatewise because each fixed endpoint coordinate has a finite limit. The family is therefore a projective boundary object even when its global pair norm diverges.

A topology on the zero-heat object must be specified by finite-packet seminorms or a weighted graph norm. No ordinary `ell2` realization follows.

Status: zero-heat endpoint defined projectively at finite coordinates; global norm realization remains open.
