# Two tower-four structures need a mixed interchange cell

## Two independent level-two structures

The current programme contains two different tower-four objects.

The forward-composition tower contains:

- graded states;
- differentials and contractions;
- ordered composites;
- Cartan anticommutators;
- normal and tangential ports;
- domains and completion-stable homotopies.

The source-coherence tower contains:

- labelled theta/Tate constructors;
- primitive, square, seam, and archimedean channels;
- source refinements and cutoff maps;
- ordered boundary incidence;
- reciprocal provenance;
- determinant and readout authority.

Each tower has its own objects, arrows, and two-cells. Neither determines the
other.

## Double-category formulation

Place forward operations horizontally and source refinements vertically.
A square asserts that one forward operation is realized by a particular
source constructor packet.

The required interchange law says that horizontal composition and vertical
source realization commute up to a declared comparison cell:

\[
\Omega_{f,g}:
\operatorname{Realize}(g\circ f)
\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{Realize}(g)
\circ
\operatorname{Realize}(f).
\]

For a strict realization, `Omega` is the identity. For a projective or
boundary-bearing realization, it may carry a source-derived phase, current,
or anomaly. It may not be fitted after the composite residual is known.

## Exact projective hostile

Let the abstract forward algebra have two commuting involutions `p` and `q`:

\[
p^2=q^2=1,
\qquad
pq=qp.
\]

Realize them separately by

\[
X=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
Z=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0\\
0&-1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Both source realizations satisfy the involution law exactly:

\[
X^2=Z^2=I.
\]

But

\[
XZ=-ZX.
\]

Every generator is valid, and the composites agree after forgetting the
central sign. The mixed interchange square nevertheless fails by `-I`.

This is the smallest demonstration that complete generator authority plus
complete forward algebra does not imply authority for their composition.

## Cartan relevance

The RH candidate depends on the ordered sum

\[
dQ+Qd.
\]

If source realization changes the relative sign between the two ordered
composites, then the abstract anticommutator can become a commutator, vanish,
or acquire a boundary anomaly. A scalar quadratic expectation may erase that
difference.

Therefore the decisive object is not separate source authority for `d` and
`Q`. It is source authority for the complete ordered pair of composites and
their comparison.

## Two additional axes

The right and left half-planes duplicate this double-category structure with
opposite contraction orientations. The full packet is therefore a `2 by 2`
array:

| | Right sector | Left sector |
|---|---|---|
| Forward composition | `T_comp+` | `T_comp-` |
| Source coherence | `T_src+` | `T_src-` |

Horizontal interchange compares composition with source realization inside
each sector. Reciprocal interchange compares the two sector columns and
reverses the normal contraction sign. These two comparisons must themselves
satisfy a higher coherence law.

## Strong DPC

For the proposed theta/Tate Cartan identity, require:

1. source entities realizing `d`, `Q`, and `N` separately;
2. source entities realizing `dQ` and `Qd` as ordered composites;
3. exact domains after both orders;
4. the mixed interchange cell and any central phase or boundary current;
5. proof that their authorized sum equals the normal number operator;
6. reciprocal transport of the entire square, including the adjoint sign;
7. braid or higher coherence for triples involving seam incidence and
   completion;
8. uniform validity through the restricted-product limit.

Finite falsifiers:

- the Pauli `X,Z` projective square;
- equal scalar expectations with opposite operator signs;
- a boundary term present in only one order;
- one composite losing the common graph domain;
- a central phase that cancels only after reciprocal aggregation;
- exact finite interchange cells with a nonzero completion anomaly.

## Verdict

The two tower-four hypothesis is structurally necessary. Forward
compositionality and source-tied coherence are independent level-two systems.
The fifth-tower datum is their mixed interchange cell, duplicated across the
two reciprocal sectors and itself subject to higher coherence.
