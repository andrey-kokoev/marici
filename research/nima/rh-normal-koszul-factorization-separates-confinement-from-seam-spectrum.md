# Normal Koszul factorization separates confinement from seam spectrum

## Factorized source complex

Let `a` be signed normal displacement from the critical seam and `t` the
tangential spectral coordinate. The cleanest Cartan architecture factors the
source family into:

- a normal Koszul direction generated by exterior multiplication `epsilon_n`;
- a tangential seam complex with differential `delta_t`.

The total differential is

\[
d_{a,t}=\delta_t+a\epsilon_n.
\]

Let `iota_n` be contraction in the normal exterior direction. Require

\[
\epsilon_n^2=\iota_n^2=0,
\qquad
\epsilon_n\iota_n+\iota_n\epsilon_n=I,
\qquad
\delta_t\iota_n+\iota_n\delta_t=0.
\]

Then

\[
d_{a,t}\iota_n+
\iota_nd_{a,t}=aI.
\]

For nonzero `a`, `iota_n/a` contracts the full complex independently of the
tangential spectrum. At `a=0`, the normal contraction disappears and the
cohomology reduces to that of the seam differential `delta_t`.

This separates two questions exactly:

- Why can cohomology occur only on the critical seam? Normal Koszul
  contraction.
- At which seam heights does cohomology occur? Tangential seam complex.

RH needs only the first statement. It does not need to solve the seam spectrum.

## Exact two-generator exterior model

On the exterior algebra generated by a normal symbol `e_n` and a tangential
symbol `e_t`, set

\[
d_{a,b}=a\,e_n\wedge+b\,e_t\wedge,
\qquad
Q=\iota_n.
\]

The canonical anticommutation relations give

\[
d_{a,b}^2=0,
\qquad
d_{a,b}Q+Qd_{a,b}=aI.
\]

For every nonzero `a`, the complex is contractible regardless of `b`. On the
seam `a=0`, the remaining differential is `b e_t` wedge. It is acyclic when
`b` is nonzero and acquires cohomology when `b=0`.

The model therefore permits discrete seam zeros without permitting any
off-seam zero.

## Mixed-port falsifier

The entire mechanism depends on the ordered distinction between normal and
tangential ports. If the proposed contraction leaks into the tangential port,

\[
Q_c=\iota_n+c\iota_t,
\]

then

\[
d_{a,b}Q_c+Q_cd_{a,b}
=(a+cb)I.
\]

The contraction scalar is tilted by tangential data. It can vanish at
nonzero `a` whenever `a=-cb`.

This does not by itself prove the complex has cohomology there; another
contraction may exist. But it exactly falsifies the claimed normal Cartan
identity and shows that an untyped mixture cannot establish seam confinement.

Likewise, a mixed differential whose normal coefficient is `a+r(t)` shifts the
candidate degeneration locus away from the seam unless the source proves that
`r(t)` vanishes.

## Relation to the doubled Green frontier

The unresolved forcing term and boundary currents should now be typed by
Clifford port:

- normal wedge contribution;
- normal contraction contribution;
- tangential seam differential;
- mixed normal–tangential residual;
- boundary-domain residual.

The decisive finite calculation is the mixed anticommutator. If primitive,
square, seam, or archimedean channels leave a term not annihilated by
`iota_n`, the factorization fails. If they assemble exactly into `delta_t` and
anticommute with `iota_n`, the normal contraction theorem follows.

## Categorical interpretation

The fifth tower is a tensor-product or bicomplex decomposition:

\[
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{src}}
\simeq
K(a)\widehat\otimes\mathcal C_{\mathrm{seam}}(t),
\]

where `K(a)` is the one-generator normal Koszul complex. The seam inclusion is
the specialization at `a=0`. Reciprocal reflection sends `a` to `-a` and
reverses the normal contraction orientation while leaving the tangential
complex paired by conjugation.

This is stronger than a relative-index quotient because it preserves support
and constructs the contraction before taking cohomology.

## DPC

Require:

1. independent source definitions of normal and tangential generators;
2. exact nilpotence of the total differential;
3. canonical normal contraction;
4. vanishing of the mixed anticommutator;
5. reciprocal sign reversal on the normal port;
6. a seam specialization identifying the residual tangential complex;
7. the determinant bridge;
8. completion-stable tensor product, domains, and contraction bounds.

Finite falsifiers:

- `Q_c=iota_n+c iota_t` with nonzero `c`;
- a normal coefficient `a+r(t)` with nonzero source residual `r`;
- a boundary term in the normal anticommutator;
- a primitive or square current occupying both Clifford ports;
- a cutoff sequence whose normal contraction loses its common domain.

## Verdict

Normal Koszul factorization is a sharper and more economical RH mechanism than
global positivity or index vanishing. It confines all possible cohomology to
the seam while leaving the actual seam spectrum unresolved. The immediate
source calculation is whether the doubled theta/Tate differential has a pure
normal Clifford port whose contraction anticommutes with every tangential and
boundary channel.
