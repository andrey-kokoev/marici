# qRB microstep 123: pair forcing coefficient bound

The asymmetric pair coefficient is

$$
\alpha_{nm}=\frac{m^2}{n^2+m^2}.
$$

For positive labels,

$$
0<\alpha_{nm}<1.
$$

Its reciprocal coefficient satisfies

$$
\alpha_{nm}+\alpha_{mn}=1.
$$

Therefore the ordered pair and reciprocal pair have uniformly bounded total forcing weight. The asymmetry is retained, but it cannot by itself cause growth in the pair sum.

This combines with the damping floor `kappa_nm >= pi`; the remaining summability issue lies in the Gaussian source amplitudes and the number of pair labels, not in `alpha_nm`.

Status: coefficient bound closed; pair-source counting and wall readout summability remain open.
