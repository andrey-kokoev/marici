# Source overlap generates the objective-record scale

## Question

Can record formation, local readability, redundancy, and decoherence be derived
from one reversible source interaction rather than represented by separately
assigned rates?

## Claim boundary

Consider a pointer system with alternatives (|0\rangle,|1\rangle). One
source-specified collision correlates an initially fixed environment carrier
with conditional states (|e_0\rangle,|e_1\rangle), whose overlap is

\[
c=|\langle e_0|e_1\rangle|,
\qquad 0<c<1.
\]

After (N) independent collisions, reversible global dynamics produces

\[
a|0\rangle|e_0\rangle^{\otimes N}
+b|1\rangle|e_1\rangle^{\otimes N}.
\]

The same source parameter then controls two different projections. The system
coherence factor is

\[
c^N,
\]

and the overlap of the two conditional states of an (m)-carrier fragment is

\[
c^m.
\]

For equal prior probabilities, the optimal binary discrimination error of that
fragment is

\[
p_{\rm err}(m)=
\frac{1-\sqrt{1-c^{2m}}}{2}.
\]

Thus a record-resolution tolerance (arepsilon) is met exactly when

\[
c^{2m}\leq4\varepsilon(1-\varepsilon).
\]

The smallest such (m) defines the fragment scale. At total size (N), the
number of disjoint fragments of that size is

\[
R_{\varepsilon}(N)=\left\lfloor\frac{N}{m_{\varepsilon}}\right\rfloor.
\]

The construction derives a static information scale from the interaction. It
does not yet derive persistence, observer access, fault independence, or an
irreversible arrow of time.

## Exact finite witness

Take (c=3/5) and (arepsilon=1/20). A one-carrier fragment fails the
resolution gate, while a two-carrier fragment passes. Eight independent
carriers therefore provide four disjoint readable fragments.

For the separate system-coherence tolerance (1/100), nine collisions fail
and ten pass. Readability and suppression of system coherence are generated by
the same overlap but are not the same threshold.

## Source-level obstruction

The power law (c^m) requires conditional product structure. If all nominal
observers read one common conditional carrier, adding observer labels does not
replace (c) by (c^m). Apparent copy count then grows while distinguishability
does not.

Accordingly, multiplicative overlap is the first finite independence witness:

\[
\langle E_0^{(m)}|E_1^{(m)}\rangle
=
\prod_{j=1}^{m}\langle e_{0,j}|e_{1,j}\rangle.
\]

Failure of this factorization does not automatically forbid objectivity, but
it invalidates the independent-fragment redundancy formula and requires a
correlated-environment analysis.

## Reversibility boundary

The global collision operator remains invertible. Applying the source-derived
inverse to every carrier restores the initial system and environment state.
The locally suppressed coherence is therefore stored in global relations, not
destroyed by the static quotient.

Practical irreversibility requires a further theorem about the control cost,
causal availability, recurrence scale, or instability of this inverse. It
cannot be inferred from small (c^N) alone.

## Disposition

The finite source-overlap model is closed. It replaces phenomenological record
formation rates with one interaction-derived contraction parameter and
separates readability, redundancy, decoherence, and reversibility. The next
physical gate is to derive the conditional overlap and its independence
structure from an actual Hamiltonian and to quantify the resources required
for reversal.
