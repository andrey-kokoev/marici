# Primitive and square incidences embed continuously in the saturated ordered graph

## Labelled graph incidence

Fix a source vector `g` in the metaplectic bulk core and let `b=(b_n)` be a
coefficient row of polynomial-logarithmic growth. Define on finite label
packets

\[
I_b(c)
=
\sum_{n\ge1}b_nc_n\Gamma_S(\tau_{\log n}g),
\qquad
\Gamma_S(h)=(h,Sh).
\]

Physical translation costs an exponential factor in the bulk seminorms,
which becomes a polynomial factor `n^a` at displacement `log n`. On the
Fourier side it becomes modulation; derivatives of the modulation contribute
only powers of `log n`. Both costs are absorbed by a stronger rapid-label
seminorm. Therefore

\[
I_b:\mathcal S_{\mathrm{lab}}
\longrightarrow\mathfrak B_S
\]

extends continuously.

## Arithmetic grades

Take the rows

\[
b_n^{(P)}=\Lambda(n)\mathbf 1_{n\text{ prime}},
\]

\[
b_n^{(2)}=\Lambda(n)\mathbf 1_{n=p^2},
\qquad
b_n^{(Q)}=\Lambda(n)\mathbf 1_{n=p^k,\ k\ge2},
\]

and

\[
b_n^{(\Lambda)}=b_n^{(P)}+b_n^{(Q)}=\Lambda(n)
\quad\text{on prime-power labels}.
\]

All are bounded by `log n`, so their incidences `I_P`, `I_2`, `I_Q`, and
`I_Lambda` are continuous on the same carrier and satisfy

\[
I_\Lambda=I_P+I_Q
\]

before scalar aggregation. Primitive and repeated-valuation grades therefore
remain distinct coordinates while sharing one ordered boundary target.

The alternative occupancy/excess presentation is handled identically. Its
primewise identity `P_p(r)+Q_p(r)=r` pushes forward to the full coefficient
`log n` without collapsing the two input lanes.

## Fourier naturality

Because the series converges in the saturated graph topology and Fourier is
continuous there,

\[
\mathcal F I_b(c)
=
\sum_{n\ge1}b_nc_n
\mathcal F\Gamma_S(\tau_{\log n}g).
\]

Thus Fourier transport commutes with arithmetic synthesis. In the ordered
coordinate every summand carries

\[
2i\operatorname{pv}\!\left(\frac1\xi\right)
\widehat{\tau_{\log n}g}(\xi),
\]

while its zero-frequency and endpoint coordinates are transported in the
constant--delta plane. No prime label is generated by the universal Hardy
multiplier, and no existing label is erased by it.

## Polarized current

For two finite packets define

\[
\mathcal G_{b,d}(c,e)
=
\left\langle
\sum_n b_nc_n\tau_{\log n}g,
S\sum_m d_me_m\tau_{\log m}g
\right\rangle.
\]

The radial ordered pairing is continuous, and both labelled syntheses converge
unconditionally. Hence `G_{b,d}` extends to a continuous bilinear form on
`S_lab x S_lab`, with the double series independent of prime ordering and of
joint versus iterated cutoffs.

Applying Fourier before or after synthesis gives the same distributional
principal-value pairing because both operations are continuous coordinates of
`B_S`. Reflection changes its sign and preserves the two arithmetic grade
labels.

## Result and remaining normalization gate

Primitive, square/repeated-valuation, full von Mangoldt, zero-mode, endpoint,
and ordered Hardy coordinates now inhabit one Fourier-saturated carrier, and
their polarized cross-current rows are well-defined and cutoff-independent.

The remaining question is coefficient identification, not carrier existence:
the incidence rows extracted independently from the completed Tate trace must
be shown to equal these source graph rows with the exact local normalization,
including the archimedean completion terms. A scalar functional equation does
not prove that equality.
