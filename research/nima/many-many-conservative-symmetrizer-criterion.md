# Many-many conservative symmetrizer criterion

For the transverse bordered pencil

$$
L_0=\begin{pmatrix}0&c^*\\b&A\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
J=\operatorname{diag}(\alpha,K),
$$

real-parameter `J`-self-adjointness is equivalent to

$$
AK=KA,
\qquad
Kb=\alpha c,
$$

with the adjoint row following when `K` is Hermitian and `alpha` is real.

Nondegeneracy requires

$$
\alpha\ne0,
\qquad
\ker K=\{0\}.
$$

If `A` has simple spectrum and `K` is diagonal in its spectral basis, then at every nonzero port coordinate

$$
k_j=\alpha\frac{c_j}{b_j}.
$$

Thus existence requires matching support and real nonzero ratios `c_j/b_j`. These conditions are exact finite falsifiers. Satisfying them determines a candidate metric; source authority must still identify that metric independently.

Status: finite symmetrizer criterion closed; source-derived nondegenerate `K` remains open.
