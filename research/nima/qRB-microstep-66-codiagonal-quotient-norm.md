# qRB microstep 66: codiagonal quotient norm

The hostile finite test shows that the raw joint norm is not constant on codiagonal fibers. The correct positive object after codiagonalization is therefore the quotient norm

$$
\|[q]\|_{\rm quot}
=
\inf_{k\in\ker D}\|q+k\|_{\rm joint}.
$$

This norm is well-defined on `Q / ker(D)` and is positive after removing the zero class. The codiagonal then factors as

$$
Q\longrightarrow Q/\ker(D)\xrightarrow{\,\bar D\,}H_{\rm phys}.
$$

To use it analytically, one must prove that the relevant kernel is closed and that the source-labelled relative structure is preserved by the quotient.

This avoids the overly strong requirement that the original joint energy be fiberwise constant.
