# qRB microstep 64: codiagonal descent criterion

Let `Q` be the labelled relative quotient and let

$$
D:Q\longrightarrow H_{\rm phys}
$$

be the codiagonal physical readout. A positive joint energy `E` descends through `D` precisely when

$$
Dq_1=Dq_2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
E(q_1-q_2)=0.
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\ker D\subseteq\operatorname{Rad}(E).
$$

This is weaker than requiring `D` to be injective and is the correct quotient test. Labelled radical descent does not imply it, because codiagonalization can create new fibers through cross-prime overlap.

Status: physical pushout criterion isolated; its kernel and energy radical require an explicit codiagonal packet.
