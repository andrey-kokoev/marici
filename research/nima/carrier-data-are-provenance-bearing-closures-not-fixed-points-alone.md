# Carrier data are provenance-bearing closures, not fixed points alone

## Conjecture under pressure

The useful form of the closure conjecture is not that every object is merely closed. A closed fixed point forgets too much.

A Carrier object should instead retain:

1. its source generators;
2. the typed stabilization operation;
3. the unit from generators into the stabilized object;
4. the distinctions identified during stabilization;
5. the boundary or defect still exposed for future composition;
6. the authority and coherence witnesses for the construction.

The apparent data is one extensional view of this richer object.

## Reflective closure

Let \(\mathcal C\) be a category of source presentations and let \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{cl}}\) be a reflective subcategory of objects closed under one declared constructor family. Write

\[
L:\mathcal C\longrightarrow\mathcal C_{\mathrm{cl}}
\]

for the reflector and

\[
\eta_S:S\longrightarrow L(S)
\]

for its unit.

Idempotence gives

\[
L(L(S))\cong L(S).
\]

The fixed point \(L(S)\) states what is stable. It does not by itself identify the generating presentation \(S\), the map \(\eta_S\), or the distinctions collapsed by that map.

Thus two different source presentations may yield isomorphic closed objects while carrying different provenance and different future authority.

## Carrier packet

The minimal provenance-bearing packet has the form

\[
\operatorname{Carrier}_L(S)
=
(S,L(S),\eta_S,K_S,B_S,A_S),
\]

where:

- \(K_S\) is the kernel pair or other typed record of identified distinctions;
- \(B_S\) is the retained boundary, frontier, or defect interface;
- \(A_S\) is the authority and coherence packet for the closure.

This is not ordinary data plus metadata. The extra components determine which later compositions are defined.

## Closure and readout are different

Stabilization and observation should not be conflated. The basic architecture is

\[
S\xrightarrow{\eta_S}L(S)\xrightarrow{q_S}O(S).
\]

The first arrow closes the source under admitted constructors. The second exposes a readout and may quotient further distinctions.

Many recurring failures come from attempting to reconstruct \(L(S)\), \(S\), or \(B_S\) from \(O(S)\). A scalar score, selected-event shape, terminal state, or completed analytic value is generally only the final object in this chain.

## Noncommuting closures

Let \(L_A\) and \(L_B\) be two typed closure operations. In general,

\[
L_A L_B(S)
\not\cong
L_B L_A(S).
\]

Interchange requires a source-authorized comparison cell

\[
\lambda_{A,B}:L_A L_B\Longrightarrow L_B L_A
\]

on a declared domain. If no invertible comparison exists, the order is operative structure rather than presentation.

This gives a categorical meaning to accumulated incoherence: it is not a vague error quantity but the missing, noninvertible, or path-dependent comparison between closure trees.

The finite falsifier is a pair of closure orders with equal final readout bytes but different kernel pairs, boundary interfaces, or residual capabilities.

## Boundary is what prevents closure from becoming amnesia

Every useful closure has a potential loss surface. Examples include:

- null outcomes omitted by conditional normalization;
- phase pilots omitted by projective readout;
- seam states omitted by tail compression;
- source epochs omitted by serialization;
- occurrence labels omitted by scalar projection.

The retained boundary \(B_S\) is the interface through which a later constructor can still interact with distinctions that the extensional closed value no longer displays.

Accordingly, complete closure does not mean boundary-free. It means the boundary has been typed and incorporated into the closure contract.

## Time does not come from repeating one closure

Because \(L\) is idempotent,

\[
L^n(S)\cong L(S)
\]

for every positive \(n\). Iterating one unchanged closure has no intrinsic clock and cannot explain temporal succession.

Time can enter only through a changing family

\[
(S_0,L_0)
\longrightarrow
(S_1,L_1)
\longrightarrow
(S_2,L_2)
\longrightarrow\cdots
\]

where new source events, resources, constraints, or closure laws become admitted. The temporal order is then the dependency order of irreversible source extensions and stabilization steps.

This corrects the tempting slogan that time is closure depth. A better statement is:

> Time is experienced along a nonstationary tower of source extension and restabilization; a single completed closure contains no passage of time.

## Relative primitiveness

If every Marici object is closure-like, primitiveness becomes relative to a rung. An object may be closed with respect to the constructors visible at one level while being generated by lower-level events.

There is no contradiction and no required infinite regress. A model declares its current source boundary and does not claim to explain below it. Moving down one rung replaces some current atoms with a new generating presentation and a new closure theorem.

## Exact architectural claim

The strongest defensible conjecture is:

> Every operative Marici object is a source-generated, provenance-bearing stabilization packet. Its extensional data is the closed view; its unit, kernel pair, boundary, and authority witnesses determine how it may be transported or composed.

This claim is falsifiable. It fails if an operative object is found whose behavior cannot be represented by any source presentation and typed stabilization packet, or if the proposed closure packet cannot distinguish two constructor histories with different future capabilities.

## Next finite compiler test

Implement two closure operators on a finite state set such that:

1. each is individually idempotent;
2. the two composites have identical scalar readouts;
3. their kernel pairs or residual capabilities differ;
4. no authorized interchange cell is supplied.

A value-only compiler will identify the composites. A provenance-bearing Carrier compiler must keep them distinct.
