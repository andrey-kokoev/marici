# Sudarshana Chakra as a rung-plus-one incoherence-reduction functor

## Andrey's intuition

Register the conjectural architecture:

\[
\text{physical world}
\xrightarrow{H}
\text{human sense-making}
\xrightarrow{C}
\text{Sudarshana Chakra},
\]

where each arrow is a rung-(+1) functor in a tower of incoherence
reduction.

This is recorded as a conceptual and architectural conjecture, not as an
empirical, metaphysical, or theological claim.

## Meaning of a rung

At rung (r), let (mathcal X_r) contain:

- objects or states visible at that level;
- competing paths or perspectives;
- boundary and support data;
- unresolved comparison defects;
- the constructors authorized at that level.

A rung-(+1) functor

\[
N_r:mathcal X_r\tomathcal X_{r+1}
\]

does not merely compress observations.  It should:

1. expose equivalences invisible at rung (r);
2. normalize multiple valid paths through a shared object;
3. generate comparison cells from normalization;
4. retain defects that cannot be coherently resolved;
5. preserve provenance, boundary, support, and fault information;
6. admit reconstruction or an explicit account of information loss.

Thus “incoherence reduction” means replacing arbitrary disagreement by
typed normal forms and explicit residual obstructions.  It does not mean
forcing all perspectives to agree.

## Proposed tower

### Rung 0: physical world

The physical world supplies events, constraints, causal interactions,
and raw distinctions.  It does not arrive already partitioned into
human concepts.

### Rung 1: humans

Human perception and explanation act as a normalization layer:

\[
H:mathcal X_{\rm physical}\tomathcal X_{\rm human}.
\]

Humans identify objects, causes, invariants, counterfactuals, and shared
representations.  This reduces some physical incoherence while
introducing its own unresolved ambiguity, bias, and incompatible
perspectives.

### Rung 2: Sudarshana Chakra

In Andrey's intuition, Sudarshana Chakra denotes a further normalization
layer:

\[
C:mathcal X_{\rm human}\tomathcal X_{\rm chakra}.
\]

It compares human explanations, preserves their source boundaries, and
generates higher coherence that individual human viewpoints cannot
reliably construct alone.

The intended sense is functional: a higher-order resolver of human
incoherence.  The name is not being used here to assert a literal
identity with any particular implementation.

## Non-erasure law

A higher rung is not licensed to erase lower-level contradictions.
For a normalization

\[
n_p:p\Rightarrow N,
\]

the normal form (N) must be content-addressed by the complete relevant
boundary, support union, and fault structure.  Residual disagreement
must remain typed.

The tower therefore requires:

\[
\boxed{
\text{higher coherence}
\neq
\text{unaccountable compression}.
}
\]

Whenever possible, a reconstruction or realization map should test
whether higher-level coherence survives contact with the lower-level
substrate.

## Authority correction

Greater normalization power does not automatically confer authority.
Every rung-(+1) constructor still needs a source-authorized relation
specifying:

- which inputs it may compare;
- which distinctions it may quotient;
- which residuals it must retain;
- how its conclusions return to the lower rung;
- what finite falsifiers can reject it.

Transport up the tower does not itself authorize a claim or an action.

## Falsifiers

The conjectured tower fails as an incoherence-reduction architecture if
a higher rung:

1. increases hidden contradiction while reporting greater coherence;
2. erases source boundaries or hostile cases;
3. cannot reconstruct consequences at the lower rung;
4. fabricates authority from transported information;
5. uses fitted normal forms;
6. suppresses nonzero residual holonomy or cohomology;
7. cannot identify the first invalid normalization node.

## Open question

Can incoherence reduction be quantified by a source-derived invariant
(mathfrak I_r) such that

\[
\mathfrak I_{r+1}\le\mathfrak I_r
\]

while retained information, boundary obligations, and reconstruction
fidelity remain explicit?

This would turn the rung intuition into a testable categorical theory
rather than a hierarchy asserted by naming.
