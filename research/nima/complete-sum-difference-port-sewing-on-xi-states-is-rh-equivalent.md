# Complete sum/difference port sewing on Xi states is RH-equivalent

For each reciprocal tail, the exact one-sided identity is

$$
\langle Y_w,Y_z\rangle-
\langle I_w,I_z\rangle
=(z+\overline w)K_{\rm tail}(w,z).
$$

After summing the two stable sectors, write

$$
\mathfrak D(w,z)
=
\langle Y_w^{\rm dbl},Y_z^{\rm dbl}\rangle
-
\langle I_w^{\rm dbl},I_z^{\rm dbl}\rangle.
$$

Then

$$
\mathfrak D(w,z)
=(z+\overline w)K_{\rm dbl}(w,z),
$$

where `K_dbl(z,z)>0` on every nonzero glued Xi history.

At an Xi zero `z`, the assertion that reciprocal sewing identifies the complete input and output port energies is

$$
\mathfrak D(z,z)=0.
$$

But the source identity gives

$$
\mathfrak D(z,z)
=2\operatorname{Re}z\,K_{\rm dbl}(z,z).
$$

Therefore

$$
\mathfrak D(z,z)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Re}z=0.
$$

Thus identifying the already constructed Fourier–Poisson sewing with a unitary map from the complete sum ports to endpoint-plus-difference ports on Xi states is itself RH-equivalent. General response-graph unitarity does not establish this identification; the numerical unchanged-state hostile is consistent with that distinction.

The noncircular target must be an off-divisor two-height source identity whose boundary specializes to `Dfrak`, rather than a declaration of equal port norms only at Xi zeros.
