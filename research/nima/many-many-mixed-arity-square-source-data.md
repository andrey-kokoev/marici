# Many-many mixed-arity square source data

For each finite labelled family `F`, define the four corner carriers

$$
E_{11}=VU(E_F),\quad E_{1*}=VM(E_F),\quad E_{*1}=NU(E_F),\quad E_{**}=NM(E_F).
$$

The primary square requires a source comparison

$$
\beta_{\mathcal F}:E_{1*}\to E_{*1}
$$

with

$$
\beta_{\mathcal F}(VMx)=NUx.
$$

The square commutes when this equality holds on the common source core and extends continuously to the joint graph closure. The remaining three faces are obtained by restricting input/output arity and applying reciprocal/Hadamard transport.

A complete square certificate must record the four corner maps, the comparison cell, the two composites, and their difference. The difference is the face-curvature coordinate.
