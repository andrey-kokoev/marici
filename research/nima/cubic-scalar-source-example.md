## Claim boundary

Use labelled external legs, cubic vertex -ig, and internal propagator i/(s+i0), where s is the squared internal momentum. Work at tree level about the perturbative vacuum; no nonperturbative stability claim is made for the cubic potential.

For one fixed cyclic order retain only planar tree diagrams. This is a specified subset of the identical-scalar amplitude, not its full scattering amplitude and not an independently authenticated color-ordered observable. External wavefunctions are amputated. The algebra below uses the rational functions obtained away from poles, not products or residues of distributions.

Label polygon vertices 0 through 6 and external legs by the intervening boundary edges. The diagonal (i,j) carries q_ij=p_i+...+p_(j-1), with s_ij=q_ij^2. Momentum conservation identifies the complementary channel. The seven-point planar graph sum has the form

\[
i\mathcal M_{\rm planar}=-i A_7,\qquad A_7=g^5\sum_T\prod_{(i,j)\in T}s_{ij}^{-1}.

Every labelled planar cubic tree has five vertices and four internal edges. Its phase is (-i)^5 i^4=-i; its coefficient in this fixed graph-labelled expansion is therefore g^5. The 42 terms are reconstructed independently by polygon recursion and matched against the stored ordering. Kinematic relations might permit alternative rational presentations; the coefficient assignment here is specifically to Feynman graphs, not asserted to be a unique rational-function basis.

### Quadrics and triangle factors

Each triangulation has five triangles, dual to its five cubic vertices. Assigning z_ijk=g to every triangle gives w_T=product(z_ijk)=g^5. All 63 local quadrics vanish exactly as g^10-g^10. For nonzero g, coupling-stripped coefficients are all one. Their factorization is a consequence of identical vertex couplings and tree locality, not evidence of new dynamics or a preferred triangle gauge.

## Disposition

The requested baseline is supplied: source-rule coefficients g^5, 63 passed quadrics, normalization A_3=g, and triangle factors equal to the cubic coupling at dual vertices. This is deliberately a uniform-coupling case. It does not instantiate the earlier embedding bundle E_n: no positive-geometry slice, orientation-to-form Jacobian, or cosmological wavefunction map has been constructed. The source is the explicitly declared theoretical model, not a retrieved publication or measured dataset.

A nonredundant next test is a source with a quartic contact interaction. Pole residues need not determine its contact contribution. That test can decide whether forcing a pure triangulation presentation introduces momentum-dependent or nonunique weights, rather than mistaking the cubic baseline for universality.
