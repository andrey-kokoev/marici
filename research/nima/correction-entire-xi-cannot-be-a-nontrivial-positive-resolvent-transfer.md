# Correction: entire Xi cannot be a nontrivial positive resolvent transfer

A positive collocated scalar resolvent has the spectral representation

$$
F(z)=\langle b,(A-zI)^{-1}b\rangle
=\int_{\mathbb R}\frac{d\mu_b(\lambda)}{\lambda-z},
$$

where `mu_b` is a positive measure. It is Herglotz off the real axis, but it also has a nonremovable real spectral measure whenever `b` is nonzero.

If `F` extended to an entire function, Stieltjes inversion would force

$$
\mu_b=0,
$$

hence `b=0` and `F=0`. Equivalently, an entire Herglotz function has the form

$$
az+b,
\qquad a\ge0,
$$

and a pure resolvent transfer decays at infinity, so only the zero function is possible.

Since `Xi_centered` is a nonzero entire function, there is no nontrivial positive collocated pure-resolvent realization satisfying

$$
F(z)=\Xi_{\rm centered}(z)
$$

globally. Multiplication by a nowhere-zero entire unit does not generally repair the positive-measure singularity; any claimed unit must explicitly account for the spectral denominator and its winding.

Therefore the previous Herglotz implication is only a conditional confinement observation, not a viable exact realization architecture. The Xi section must remain a bordered determinant/Evans characteristic, or occur as a numerator relative to a source-authorized denominator. Positivity may govern the ambient conservative system while Xi is its relative characteristic, not its scalar Weyl resolvent.

Status: exact positive-resolvent realization of entire Xi ruled out; relative determinant or Evans-cone promotion remains the admissible route.
