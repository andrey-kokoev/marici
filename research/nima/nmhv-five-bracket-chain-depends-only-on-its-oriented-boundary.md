# An NMHV five-bracket chain depends only on its oriented boundary

## Theorem

Let `T` and `T'` be finite oriented simplicial four-chains on the same finite momentum-supertwistor label set. Suppose

$$
\partial T=\partial T'.
$$

Map every oriented four-simplex to its momentum-supertwistor five-bracket and extend linearly:

$$
R([a,b,c,d,e])=[a,b,c,d,e].
$$

Then

$$
R(T)=R(T').
$$

Thus a simplicial NMHV representation is determined by its oriented codimension-one boundary, not by its internal triangulation.

## Proof

The difference

$$
Z=T-T'
$$

is a four-cycle. The full simplex on the common finite label set is contractible, so its reduced simplicial homology vanishes. Therefore there is a five-chain `H` with

$$
Z=\partial H.
$$

Concretely, choose a cone vertex `v`. The standard cone operator obeys

$$
\partial h_v+h_v\partial=I
$$

in positive degrees. Since `partial Z=0`, one may take `H=h_vZ`.

For every oriented five-simplex, the six-term identity is

$$
R(\partial[a,b,c,d,e,f])=0.
$$

By linearity,

$$
R(T)-R(T')
=R(Z)
=R(\partial H)
=0.
$$

## Consequences

### BCFW triangulation independence

Any two finite simplicial NMHV BCFW chains with the same canonical physical boundary chain `P_n` give the same superamplitude. Internal spurious facets may differ, but they are boundaries inside the filling chain and disappear by the six-term identity.

### Dihedral invariance

Cyclic and reflected BCFW chains have boundary `P_n`; their equality is a special case.

### Local moves

A bistellar replacement supported on six labels changes a chain by the boundary of one oriented five-simplex. The six-term identity is therefore the local algebraic relation implementing every such elementary retriangulation.

## Required hypotheses

The theorem requires:

1. a common finite label simplex containing both chains;
2. equality of oriented boundaries, not only equality of unoriented facet sets;
3. the standard antisymmetric five-bracket convention;
4. finite chains, so the simplicial contraction is an ordinary finite sum.

It does not identify whether an arbitrary rational representation comes from such a chain. Nor does it prove equality when the two constructions use different label spaces without a declared embedding.

## Claim boundary

This is a tree-level NMHV simplicial-chain theorem. It does not establish triangulation independence for non-simplicial cells, loop integrands, general `N^kMHV` residues, or representations whose local identities are not generated by the six-term five-bracket relation.
