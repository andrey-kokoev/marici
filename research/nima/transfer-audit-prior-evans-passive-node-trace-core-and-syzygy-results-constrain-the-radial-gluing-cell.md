# Transfer audit: prior Evans, passive-node, trace-core, and syzygy results constrain the radial gluing cell

## Question

Which previous results transfer to the missing comparison between the two entire oriented radial kernels?

## Claim boundary

Four constructions transfer as exact constraints. They show that the gluing defect must be generated by the difference of source-derived local factors, carried by directed traces with a polarized Green identity, and extended from a trace-preserving dense core. They do not identify the radial defect with Xi or construct the arithmetic lower equation.

## 1. The Evans mismatch is the model gluing defect

For the completed theta forcing, the two stable history factors satisfy

\[
(\partial_q-z)u_\pm=\Phi
\]

and their difference is the homogeneous solution

\[
u_-(q;z)-u_+(q;z)=e^{zq}\tau(z).
\]

At the seam,

\[
u_-(0;z)-u_+(0;z)=\tau(z).
\]

This transfers the correct architecture: local right- and left-stable factors are primary; their difference generates the gluing defect and automatically solves the homogeneous equation. Zero defect yields a global two-ended state without fitted subtraction.

It does not transfer the scalar \(\tau\) to the radial problem. The radial kernels act on ordered-pair densities and Wronskian currents, not the original one-body theta forcing. Identifying their homogeneous-tail difference with Xi requires a source map between these carriers.

## 2. Local-factor differences generate coherence

Prior syzygy analysis proves that if two local factors satisfy

\[
R=L_+M=L_-M,
\]

then

\[
(L_--L_+)M=0.
\]

Thus their difference is a kernel or syzygy element of the common operator. Any overlap comparison should be generated by this difference, not supplied as an independent fitted matrix. With more charts, triangle coherence telescopes automatically.

Applied to the radial rigging, the two entire oriented kernels

\[
\mathcal R_{z,+}^{\rm rig},
\qquad
\mathcal R_{z,-}^{\rm rig}
\]

must first be shown to factor the same source equation on a common domain. Their difference then lands in the homogeneous doubled radial solution module. The missing datum is local membership and source identification of that homogeneous tail, not an arbitrary gluing operator.

Finite syzygy coherence does not settle completion: graph bounds, domains, closed ranges, and cutoff compatibility remain separate.

## 3. Directed ports require a Green-derived Gram

The earlier seam correction blocks inference of port multiplicity from matrix squareness or dimension deficit. The radial reciprocal double supplies two directed traces because its Green form explicitly has signature \((-1,+1)\), not because two channels happen to be present.

The passive tail theorem supplies the transferable polarized pattern. Source quadratures resolve a forcing pairing into incoming and outgoing features satisfying

\[
\langle u_\eta,u_\zeta\rangle
-
\langle v_\eta,v_\zeta\rangle
=(\zeta+\overline\eta)
\langle G_\eta,G_\zeta\rangle.
\]

For the radial gluing cell, the endpoint, Wronskian, and arithmetic source terms must similarly enter a joint incoming/outgoing Gram identity before lossless sewing is asserted. A scalar equality of wall values does not prove that identity.

The passive node also distinguishes nonexpansive source flow from strict observability. Lossless seam sewing does not imply the arithmetic adjoint cancellation or RH confinement.

## 4. Fixed-trace dense cores transfer extension authority

Previous weighted-history work constructs smooth rapid approximants while preserving both endpoint traces exactly. Consequently, a Green identity proved on that core extends uniquely to the closed relative graph; no hidden trace-kernel extension remains.

The same method transfers to the radial exponential rigging. Approximation must preserve the wall trace and any declared integrated endpoint defect. A compactly supported correction with prescribed trace or integral repairs ordinary approximants, while continuity of those functionals makes the correction vanish in the graph topology.

Therefore the gluing comparison should be proved first on a rapid trace-preserving core. If both oriented factors and the joint Green form are continuous in one closed graph topology, their extension is unique. Separate extensions in unrelated weighted topologies do not provide a common gluing cell.

## 5. Lossless cross-sewing transfers only after strict passivity and observability

The abstract two-sector theorem proves that unitary cross-sewing cancels total boundary supply. At paired off-seam parameters, strict positive defect coefficients then force both internal defect states to vanish; observability excludes a nonzero closed mode.

This transfers a terminal test for the radial gluing cell, not its construction. The sewing map must act unitarily on the full typed port carrier containing endpoint, Wronskian, arithmetic, seam, and archimedean features. Scalar functional-equation symmetry or a unit-modulus determinant is insufficient. A nonunit typed residual can cancel the positive defects and invalidate confinement.

The theorem also requires observability after closure. Lossless sewing alone permits a decoupled unitary mode. Thus the radial gluing audit must report both the sewing residual \(J_X^*J_X-I\) and the closed-system observability margin.

## 6. Half-line kernels cannot inherit bilateral symbol tests

Previous Volterra analysis separates the source half-line history from any bilateral convolution dilation. The radial local factors are triangular, nonnormal kernels. Their finite invertibility or entire test-to-dual continuation does not authorize multiplier-range estimates or uniform inverse bounds.

A bilateral representation may be used only after a comparison theorem controls the compressed resolvents uniformly. Boundary reflections and homogeneous tails can produce pseudospectral growth invisible in a bilateral symbol. Therefore the gluing cell must be tested on the actual directed half-line kernels before any Fourier-symbol simplification.

## 7. Reciprocal closures and Fourier exchange constrain, but do not define, gluing

The prior \(\pm1\) closure construction gives two canonical scalar Evans sections only after a common protected Schur return \(g(z)\) has been source-derived. It cannot be transferred now to define the radial overlap defect: no scalar protected return has yet been extracted from the full radial feature carrier.

At wall level, Fourier--Tate exchange is anti-symplectic:

\[
F_{\rm wall}^*\Omega_{\rm wall}F_{\rm wall}
=-\Omega_{\rm wall}.
\]

Thus reciprocal gluing must satisfy anti-symplectic covariance of the complete tail--wall form, including cross blocks. It is not equality of the two oriented kernels and not a freely phased quarter-turn. Only after this full covariance and the common return are established may the two reciprocal closure characters be tested.

## 8. Reciprocal reflection fixes the derivative sign and boundary sewing

The logarithmic half-density calculation gives the exact antiunitary law

\[
\mathcal R\widehat{\mathsf D}_+\mathcal R
=-\widehat{\mathsf D}_-.
\]

On the two-level boundary module, the canonical sewing is therefore

\[
R_\partial=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)C,
\]

which anticommutes with the derivative incidence, preserves the Krein metric, and squares to the identity. This transfers directly to the doubled radial gluing cell: the opposite derivative orientation is source-fixed, and the odd boundary level changes sign. There is no remaining free sewing phase at this local level.

## 9. Reflection parity reduces the finite mixed return but requires both channels

When incidence intertwines reciprocal reflection, its finite parity matrix is diagonal while the causal odd auxiliary term is parity off-diagonal. The induced odd Schur response has the source pattern

\[
C_+D^{-1}_{+-}C_-^*.
\]

Thus both even and odd incidence channels must be nonzero and correctly normalized. A perfectly equivariant gluing with one dark incidence channel cannot carry the Wronskian odd response. This gives a sharper finite hostile for the radial feature map than scalar wall matching.

The bilateral history symbol can estimate the auxiliary block only after a proved dilation/compression comparison. It does not alter the half-line authority gate recorded above.

## 10. Bilateral Volterra splitting fixes the candidate wall and jump coordinates

On the rapid bilateral source core, causal and anticausal primitives satisfy

\[
(V_-+V_+)h=\left(\int_{\mathbb R}h\right)\mathbf 1,
\qquad
S_{\rm ord}=V_+-V_-=-2H_{\rm jump}.
\]

Hence orientation supplies two distinct generated coordinates: a constant-wall sum and an ordered-jump difference. For a nonzero even completed-theta forcing \(\Phi\), the relative Green incidences are already fixed:

\[
c_+=\frac12\left(\int_{\mathbb R}\Phi\right)^2,
\qquad
c_-=-\|\Phi\|_2^2.
\]

Neither channel is dark. The sign of the odd incidence is source-derived rather than a sewing convention.

These identities constrain the radial gluing computation but do not replace it. The constant wall and jump approach nonzero limits and are relative boundary classes, not ordinary unweighted \(L^2\) states. Moreover, reciprocal doubling preserves the forward incidence direction; it does not construct the return adjoint. The radial comparison must therefore retain separate forward, reciprocal-forward, and metric-adjoint maps until a variance-reversing full-history comparison is proved.

## Exact transferred construction order

The next admissible radial gluing theorem has five steps:

1. choose one rapid doubled source core preserving wall and endpoint traces;
2. prove that both oriented kernels factor the same radial source equation there;
3. define the overlap defect as their difference;
4. prove that this difference lies in the homogeneous doubled radial module and compute its directed boundary trace;
5. identify its polarized Gram and extend uniquely to the closed rigged graph.

Only after these steps may reciprocal/Real transport compare the defect with the G4 boundary response.

If the added solutions have incompatible boundary traces or norms diverging with cutoff, no closed lossless gluing exists. This separates generated algebraic coherence from the required Green and completion bounds.

## Disposition

Previous research supplies the form of the gluing cell but not its radial source value. The cell must be the generated homogeneous-tail difference of the two oriented factors, tested in a joint directed Green metric on a trace-preserving dense core. The remaining calculation is to compute that difference for the explicit radial kernels and determine whether the completed-theta pair source cancels it or maps it into the G4 arithmetic boundary feature. No RH conclusion is authorized.
