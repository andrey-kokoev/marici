# Five observers separate strict closure from weak pentagon data

## Question

Does adding a fifth observer necessarily create another independent
coherencer above the tetrahedral identity?

## Claim boundary

Not for strict transport. In a strict groupoid, all parenthesizations of an
ordered path agree by equality, so the higher nerve is generated by the lower
composition data. A genuinely new pentagon gate appears only when composition
is weak and associators are retained as nonidentity cells.

## Strict transport closes

Take five frames \(g_0,\ldots,g_4\) and adjacent transports

\[
a=t_{01},\quad b=t_{12},\quad c=t_{23},\quad d=t_{34}.
\]

There are five binary parenthesizations arranged as the vertices of the
associahedral pentagon:

\[
((ab)c)d,\quad (ab)(cd),\quad a(b(cd)),\quad a((bc)d),\quad (a(bc))d.
\]

For strict group multiplication they are equal, and telescoping gives

\[
abcd=t_{04}.
\]

No fifth primitive coherence cell is required. The nerve of a strict category
is determined by its objects, arrows, composition, identities, and
associativity; higher simplices record consequences of those operations.

## Weak transport continues

If the two bracketings of a triple are related by a nonidentity associator,
then the five routes around the fourfold product are comparison morphisms
rather than equalities. Their composite around the associahedral boundary must
be the identity. This is the pentagon law.

Individually invertible associator cells do not imply the pentagon. A loop of
five invertible scalar cells with product different from one is the smallest
falsifier.

Thus the fifth observer is diagnostic:

- strict composition: no new independent datum;
- weak composition: an independently verified pentagon is required.

## Consequence for coherence towers

The appearance of new objects under repeated rotation does not by itself prove
an infinite tower. One must first ask whether the current level is strict,
weak, or projected from a weak level.

If strict, higher cells may be generated uniquely and adding them as primitive
data creates bureaucracy rather than information. If weak, the associator and
its pentagon carry real obstruction data. If a scalar projection makes a weak
associator look strict, the apparent termination is false.

## Cross-sector typing

- Ordered software transformations are usually strict only after exact
  implementation identity and domain equality are established. Equal output
  bytes do not make their composition strict.
- Optical route amplitudes may compose strictly as operators while calibrated
  presentations carry weak comparison phases.
- Flavor threshold and frame transports are weak until their matching
  intertwiners and domains are proved coherent.
- Boundary-line pairing, exchange, dagger, augmentation, and completion cannot
  use strict closure unless their comparison maps are literal equalities in one
  admitted category.

## DPC

Before adding a higher coherencer:

1. type the composition law;
2. determine whether associativity is equality or an explicit cell;
3. if strict, derive all higher path equalities and stop adding primitive
   cells;
4. if weak, retain the associator and test the pentagon;
5. reject projected equality when the underlying comparison cell is
   nontrivial;
6. use a nonidentity loop product as the finite falsifier.

## Disposition

The coherence tower terminates automatically only in a strict transport
category. Five observers do not add information there. In weak transport they
expose the pentagon, which is the first gate not reducible to independent
tetrahedral checks.
