# The first theta-label completion packet has three explicit Jordan grades and zero wall image

The first finite part of the window-to-theta comparison can be computed
exactly from the completion differential.

## Conjugated completion operator

Set

\[
y=e^{2u}-1,
\qquad
h(u)=e^{u/2}f(y).
\]

Then

\[
\mathcal C_y
=
e^{-u/2}
\left(\partial_u^2-\frac14\right)
e^{u/2}
=
4(1+y)^2\partial_y^2
+
6(1+y)\partial_y.
\]

The zero-mode wall is the constant coefficient \(f_0(y)=1/2\), and

\[
\mathcal C_y f_0=0.
\]

Thus the wall column of the direct completion map is exactly zero.

## One nonzero theta label

For one theta label, let

\[
\lambda_n=\pi n^2,
\qquad
f_n(y)=e^{-\lambda_n(1+y)}
=
e^{-\lambda_n}e^{-\lambda_n y}.
\]

Since

\[
\partial_y f_n=-\lambda_n f_n,
\qquad
\partial_y^2f_n=\lambda_n^2 f_n,
\]

one obtains

\[
\mathcal C_y f_n
=
\left[
4\lambda_n^2(1+y)^2
-
6\lambda_n(1+y)
\right]f_n.
\]

Therefore

\[
\mathcal C_y f_n
=
e^{-\lambda_n}
\left(
A_{0,n}+A_{1,n}y+A_{2,n}y^2
\right)e^{-\lambda_n y},
\]

with exact coefficients

\[
A_{0,n}=4\lambda_n^2-6\lambda_n,
\]

\[
A_{1,n}=8\lambda_n^2-6\lambda_n,
\]

\[
A_{2,n}=4\lambda_n^2.
\]

All three are positive for every \(n\ge1\), because
\(\lambda_n\ge\pi>3/2\).

## First-label packet

At \(n=1\), \(\lambda_1=\pi\), so the packet is

\[
e^{-\pi}
\left[
(4\pi^2-6\pi)
+
(8\pi^2-6\pi)y
+
4\pi^2y^2
\right]e^{-\pi y}.
\]

Hence the first label genuinely occupies all three Jordan grades. No
rank-one scalar theta-history channel can represent the completion map before
a further source-authorized compression.

The coefficient ratios are fixed:

\[
A_{0,1}:A_{1,1}:A_{2,1}
=
(4\pi^2-6\pi):(8\pi^2-6\pi):4\pi^2.
\]

They are not normalization parameters.

## Minimal finite comparison matrix

On the source subspace generated by the wall and first positive label, the
completion map has columns

\[
C_{\mathrm{first}}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&A_{0,1}\\
0&A_{1,1}\\
0&A_{2,1}
\end{pmatrix}
\]

in the target basis \(j=0,1,2\), up to the common nonzero factor
\(e^{-\pi}e^{-\pi y}\) attached to the positive-label column.

This matrix has rank one, but its wall kernel is essential:

\[
\ker C_{\mathrm{first}}
=
\mathbb C e_{\mathrm{wall}}.
\]

Quotienting by that kernel before forming the relative Green object erases the
constant wall. Conversely, adjoining a unit wall after completion chooses a
split extension not supplied by \(\mathcal C_y\).

## Consequence for the common carrier

The direct comparison cannot land in completed histories alone. The minimal
relative carrier must retain

\[
\ker\mathcal C_y
\oplus
\operatorname{span}\{f_1\}
\oplus
\operatorname{span}\{j=0,1,2\}.
\]

The connecting or boundary morphism from the killed wall class into the
relative Green complex remains the missing datum. Only after that morphism is
constructed can Schur elimination generate an effective identity term.

## What this resolves and what remains

Resolved:

- exact wall image: zero;
- exact \(n=1\) three-grade packet;
- positivity and normalization of all three coefficients;
- impossibility of a direct scalar wall-preserving completion map.

Still unresolved:

- images of the two oriented Gaussian fronts;
- endpoint-jet contributions;
- reciprocal sewing of the \(+u\) and \(-u\) charts;
- the relative connecting morphism;
- Green polarization and Schur return.

Thus the next calculation is no longer whether the wall is pure or mixed. It
is killed. The next constructor is the connecting morphism retaining that
kernel class in the relative completion complex.
