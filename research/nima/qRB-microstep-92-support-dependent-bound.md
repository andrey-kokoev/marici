# qRB microstep 92: support-dependent graph bound

On each fixed-support domain `H_log,L`, the source identity gives a bound

$$
|Q_L(f,g)|
\le C_L\|f\|_{\log,L}\|g\|_{\log,L}.
$$

The constant `C_L` includes endpoint evaluation and therefore may grow with `L`. The fixed-support extension does not imply

$$
\sup_L C_L<\infty.
$$

This identifies the precise obstruction to upgrading the countable-core diagonal observer to one global Banach/Hilbert form: support growth and endpoint control must be renormalized together.
