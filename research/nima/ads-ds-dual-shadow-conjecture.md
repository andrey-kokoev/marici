# A precise conditional dual-shadow conjecture

## Proposition DSH-0, version 2

The following is a **conditional local two-dimensional theorem target**, not yet a derivation of projective geometry from bare interval incidence. Its source, admissible comparisons and target class are fixed below. No factor containing an independently constructed AdS or dS space may be added to the source.

### Fixed source

Take the existing reversible interval execution category: objects p_ij are nonempty consecutive paths with complete compiler records; arrows are legal retained words in L,R,l,r, with composition by concatenation and empty-word identities. Inverse operations recover objects but their nonempty execution words are not identified with empty histories. Use m=3 for the cavity comparison. Arbitrary finite m belongs to the abstract path theorem only, not to the ten-mode cavity claim.

Metric-visible objects satisfy i<j, and a visible execution has every intermediate object in this domain. Choose ordered real endpoint coordinates x_1<...<x_m, with p_ij represented by (x_i,x_j). Adjoin the **supplied** diagonal local PSL(2,R) action: x maps to (ax+b)/(cx+d), ad-bc=1, on an interval containing all relevant endpoints with nonzero denominator. Transform the entire labelled chart and execution together. The numerical choice x_i=i is one fixture, not an invariant rule under this action.

This projective action, smooth real chart and endpoint order are independent declared inputs. They are not inferred from the photon response or from the desired curvature signs. The source does not contain either target metric, a curvature sign, a preferred branch or a length unit.

### Fixed admissible geometric output

On U={(u,v):u<v}, classify **all smooth, symmetric, nondegenerate covariant two-tensors invariant under the supplied simultaneous local projective action**. Quotient this family only by positive constant rescaling. Do not weaken nondegeneracy, silently add endpoint-dependent weights or choose metrics from measured/desired curvature.

Conjectured classification: exactly two positive-rescaling classes remain, represented by

    g_plus = 4 du dv/(v-u)^2,
    g_minus = -4 du dv/(v-u)^2.

Both are Lorentzian. Under the curvature convention fixed in `projective-interval-metric-selection.md`, their sectional curvatures are respectively +1 and -1; multiplying by ell^2 gives +/-1/ell^2. Thus the targets are **local dS2-type and AdS2-type geometries**, with arbitrary positive radius, not selected global completions or four-dimensional spacetimes.

The existing signed-scale classification is evidence for this clause and must be reused, not relabelled as a new discovery. Positive-rescaling equivalence is part of the stated goal: no absolute scale theorem is claimed.

### Projection rules and preserved structure

For each metric class, map p_ij to (x_i,x_j). Map each visible execution to its ordered endpoint transitions, retaining step labels and intermediate points. Compose by concatenation. The two functors have the same endpoint/transition map and differ only in the invariant metric class and its induced causal assignment. Their image subcategories are the categories generated by those transitions, not every possible spacetime curve.

Require:

1. identities, domains and concatenation are preserved;
2. a simultaneous source chart change intertwines both projections and pulls back each target metric to itself;
3. the common append operation R is the same future null ray in both targets;
4. with negative norm timelike, the time orientation is the component whose closure contains the positive R ray: the other future null ray is L in the positive class and l in the negative class;
5. on the shared R execution subcategory the projections have identical labelled transition records; their differing future assignments elsewhere are not an inconsistency;
6. the m=3 cavity dictionary's based correction E(p')E(p)^T implements the same source edit at its fixed numerical labels. The projective action changes endpoint charts, not the optical coefficients or round labels. No PSL action on the ten-mode field is claimed.

The L/R/l/r transitions are not required to be spacetime isometries. Isometry is required of the supplied projective chart comparison, not of arbitrary source dynamics. No requirement makes all reversible arrows future-directed in either target.

## Why this is not the trivial product construction

There is one pre-existing interval execution source and one declared projective action. The admissible metric class is fixed by smoothness, nondegeneracy and invariance before its solutions are classified. Both projections use the same endpoints and labelled executions. Adding an arbitrary pair of target metrics, appending an AdS-by-dS factor, assigning a different source to each projection or fitting target-dependent endpoint maps does not satisfy this proposition.

This excludes arbitrary target decoration but **does not make the projective-action premise native**. The stronger source-origin question is separate: construct and justify that action and continuum comparison from the unaugmented interval/WG source. It remains an unformulated research problem until its admissible realization/refinement laws are specified. The conditional theorem must never be reported as resolving it.

## Information loss and reconstruction

Endpoint-only readout forgets edit histories. Labelled execution projections retain the tested source labels by definition; any inverse must state that retained data. No lossless reconstruction from an unlabelled geometric point or from three photon counts is claimed. Reconstruction is not a premise of DSH-0.

## Acceptance, counterexamples and verification scope

Acceptance needs the all-parameter invariant-tensor classification and a written proof of the transport rules on every admitted chart/domain, together with exact finite checks of the m=3 source/cavity comparison. Finite tests alone do not prove smooth classification or a continuum limit.

Counterexamples are explicit: an additional nondegenerate invariant tensor class; a metric/curvature calculation incompatible with the fixed convention; a legal projective chart comparison breaking a projection square; a visible execution violating its assigned causal cone; or a failure of the based cavity edit comparison. Reject product-padding, degenerate tensors, singular chart changes, non-visible intermediate objects and claims of endpoint-only history recovery as out-of-contract inputs or claims.
