# The completed boundary carrier is necessarily a three-grade rigged object

## Scalar continuation is circular for the first two currents

The bordered prime shift derives the logarithmic cumulants

\[
-\log(1-p^{-s})
=
p^{-s}
+\frac12p^{-2s}
+\sum_{k\ge3}\frac{p^{-ks}}{k}.
\]

Prime-zeta Möbius inversion gives

\[
P(s)
=
\sum_{m\ge1}\frac{\mu(m)}{m}\log\zeta(ms).
\]

Therefore continuing the primitive scalar \(P(s)\) through the critical strip
already imports the divisor and logarithmic monodromy of \(\zeta\). The square
scalar \(P(2s)/2\) inherits the same problem at its shifted arguments.

Any construction that first scalarizes these currents and then uses
\(\log\zeta\) to continue them has read the unresolved divisor backward. The
primitive and square currents must remain operator-valued or
distribution-valued boundary ports until reciprocal and archimedean sewing is
complete.

## Exact topology on the critical seam

Write

\[
s=\frac12+it.
\]

The primitive seam current has Fourier measure

\[
\nu_1
=
\sum_p p^{-1/2}\delta_{\log p}.
\]

Its total variation below frequency \(R\) grows on the scale

\[
|\nu_1|([0,R])
\asymp
\frac{e^{R/2}}{R}.
\]

This is exponential rather than polynomial growth. Hence \(\nu_1\) is not a
tempered measure on the additive logarithmic coordinate. It requires an
exponential or Laplace test-function rigging.

The square current has measure

\[
\nu_2
=
\frac12\sum_p p^{-1}\delta_{2\log p}.
\]

Its cumulative variation grows only logarithmically:

\[
|\nu_2|([0,R])
\sim
\frac12\log R.
\]

It is therefore tempered.

For the connected tail,

\[
\nu_{\ge3}
=
\sum_p\sum_{k\ge3}
\frac{p^{-k/2}}{k}\delta_{k\log p},
\]

and

\[
\|\nu_{\ge3}\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
\le
\sum_p\sum_{k\ge3}\frac{p^{-k/2}}{k}
<
\infty.
\]

This is the trace-class or finite-measure grade.

## Corrected boundary architecture

The three grades below are filtered cumulant components of one determinant
packet. They are not three freely addable operators or independent Hilbert
ports. Their distinct topologies specify how the single source operator may
be evaluated and reconstructed. Any cross-grade form requires an additional
source constructor.

The common boundary carrier cannot be an arbitrarily chosen finite matrix
space. Its arithmetic part has at least three analytically distinct grades:

\[
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{arith}}
=
\mathcal E_1^{\mathrm{Laplace}}
\oplus
\mathcal E_2^{\mathrm{temp}}
\oplus
\mathcal E_{\ge3}^{\mathrm{trace}}.
\]

These grades are generated by one local determinant but admit different
completion operations.

The full boundary object must additionally include the seam and
archimedean endpoint data:

\[
\mathcal E
=
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{endpoint/gamma}}
\oplus
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{seam}}
\oplus
\mathcal E_1^{\mathrm{Laplace}}
\oplus
\mathcal E_2^{\mathrm{temp}}
\oplus

\]

This notation records types, not an assertion that every coupling is an
orthogonal direct sum. The actual source incidence may form extensions
between these grades.

## Why finite compression fails

A finite-dimensional boundary matrix can store finitely many moments of
\(\nu_1\) and \(\nu_2\), but it cannot retain their independently labelled
frequency support. Compressing either current to a scalar analytic function
before sewing performs precisely the forbidden prime-zeta continuation.

Therefore the earlier finite Schur reduction must be interpreted cutoff by
cutoff. At each finite prime cutoff, the kernel is carried by a finite
boundary space. In the limit, those spaces form a directed rigged system
whose primitive dimension and topology do not stabilize finitely.

The RH completion gate is strict exactness of this directed boundary system,
not convergence of one fixed finite matrix.

## Source operations by grade

A legitimate completion functor must declare:

- which test functions pair continuously with the primitive current;
- how Mellin translation acts on the Laplace rigging;
- how the square current embeds into the tempered dual;
- how the connected tail produces the order-three determinant;
- how reciprocal reflection exchanges the direct and dual boundary ports;
- how endpoint and gamma components cancel the remaining divergences;
- which relative extension data survive comparison to the physical or Betti
  realization.

No authority passes from convergence of the trace-class tail to continuity of
the first two grades.

## Finite and asymptotic falsifiers

For a proposed boundary completion:

1. measure the growth of its primitive frequency measure; polynomially
   bounded topology is insufficient;
2. verify that the square port remains continuous in the tempered topology;
3. verify absolute summability of the connected tail;
4. reject any primitive or square scalar continuation expressed through
   \(\log\zeta\);
5. test whether cutoff boundary null vectors have bounded graph norm in every
   retained grade;
6. detect a partner escaping to infinite Laplace or tempered norm even when
   the scalar determinant converges.

The fifth and sixth tests are the exact completion-at-infinity gate.

## Disposition

The locally acyclic prime interiors still reduce every finite kernel to the
boundary. But the completed boundary is not finite: it is a graded rigged
carrier forced by the growth of the determinant cumulants.

This localizes the remaining RH problem more precisely. One must prove
strict exactness and an independent orientation law on the coupled
Laplace–tempered–trace boundary system before scalar continuation. Scalar
prime-zeta regularization cannot provide either theorem without circularity.
