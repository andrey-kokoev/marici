# Predictive carrier and record morphisms programme

## Scope

This microprogramme studies which state distinctions a declared future
experiment family can expose, how instruments change that family, and what
additional source authority turns a mathematical branch label into a stable
record and feedback port.

It does not claim that every mathematical tester is physical, that every
environment is retained, or that operational equivalence implies realization
identity.

## Hard core

1. Equivalence is always relative to a frozen constructor and tester packet.
2. Present scalar agreement is weaker than predictive equivalence.
3. Predictive equivalence must be a congruence for every admitted future
   constructor.
4. A physical record is produced by an instrument: probabilities, state update,
   and an accessible record port belong to one typed packet.
5. Forgetting, coarse-graining, retaining, and acting conditionally on a record
   are different maps.
6. Mathematical representation supplies no physical retention or interface
   authority.

The hard core is reopened by a source-derived example in which two states are
predictively equivalent under a declared complete future packet yet an admitted
future experiment distinguishes them, or by an instrument composition law that
cannot be represented without contradicting one of the typed distinctions.

## Positive heuristic

- Compute future-indistinguishability before proposing state quotients.
- Close base tests under authorized contexts before semantic saturation.
- Treat instruments as morphisms between predictive-carrier packets.
- Identify the backward transport of future tests through every instrument.
- Separate semantic tests, jointly recordable tests, and physically implemented
  instruments.
- Search for the first missing port, congruence law, compatibility condition, or
  source authority.

## Negative heuristic

- Do not infer predictive equivalence from one scalar.
- Do not infer instrument equality from equal effects.
- Do not infer a retained record from a Kraus label or dilation.
- Do not infer joint measurability from semantic saturation.
- Do not infer nondisturbance from compatibility.
- Do not infer physical identity from operational equivalence.

## Superseded formulations

- “Interaction is automatically recorded by closure” is superseded. Recording
  requires an instrument, stable port, storage or transport, and downstream
  access.
- “Any hidden environment behavior can underlie the same channel” is
  superseded. Minimal dilations of one fully specified channel are unique up to
  environment isometry.
- “Saturated tests are jointly executable” is superseded. Semantic descent and
  instrument compatibility are different gates.
- “Observation reads an unchanged carrier” is superseded. A recording
  instrument changes the future carrier unless its state transition is proved
  nondisturbing for the relevant algebra.

## Live frontier

The immediate frontier is a category of finite predictive-carrier packets and
recorded instruments. Required structure:

- composition of record-indexed state transitions;
- backward transport of future effects;
- record retention and record forgetting;
- coarse-graining of record alphabets;
- conditional downstream constructors;
- congruence preservation under composition;
- and the boundary between a formal classical register and a stable physical
  record.

Later frontiers are coherent record systems, infinite histories, approximate
predictive equivalence, and completion-stable instrument realization.

## Programme appraisal

The line is progressive so far: each refinement predicted a new finite
falsifier before application. Examples include the bit-swap parity witness, the
qubit joint-measurability threshold, the Lüders commutant theorem, and the
Stinespring named-port residual.

The line becomes degenerating if every counterexample is answered by adding an
undeclared tester, record, environment, or controller port after failure.

## Claim boundary

This index organizes bounded successor packets. It is not an additional theorem
and does not promote any graph admission to evidence or physical authority.
