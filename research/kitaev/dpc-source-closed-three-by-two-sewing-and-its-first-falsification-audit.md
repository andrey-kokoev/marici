# DPC: source-closed three-by-two sewing, and its first falsification audit

## The conjecture

### DPC--3x2

The frozen theta/Tate source determines, up to behaviorally trivial framed
gauge, one minimal boundary relation

\[
\mathcal A_\partial:
T_+\times T_-
\rightharpoonup
L_{global},
\qquad
T_\pm=(P,Q,C,H_\infty,H_{seam})_\pm,
\]

with these properties:

1. `P`, `Q`, and `C` retain their distributional, Hilbert/non-absolutely-
   summable, and determinant-class codomains.
2. Every finite Euler cutoff determinant is reconstructed before scalar
   completion.
3. Cutoff bonding, Fourier transport, reciprocal reflection, reverse
   incidence, and parity/index balance commute as typed relations.
4. Each open sector produces an accretive denominator operator and therefore
   a zero-free framed determinant unit.
5. The framed sector units form compact-open normal families with one
   source-fixed nonzero anchor.
6. Their fifth-level sewing equals the independently constructed completed
   theta/Tate section up to a holomorphic unit.
7. Every non-gauge choice in the relation changes a predicted response under
   some admitted source perturbation.

The conjecture predicts that no additional arithmetic grade, reciprocal
sector, or sixth-level associator is required.

## Why this would explain rather than merely confirm

The causal order of the construction is part of the claim:

```text
labelled source incidence
  -> typed sector packets
  -> accretive denominator relations
  -> compact-open sector completion
  -> reciprocal sewing
  -> completed scalar section.
```

Accretivity makes each sector denominator invertible before the completed
scalar exists.  Hurwitz preserves its unit character under normalized
compact-open completion.  Sewing then transfers that prior impossibility to
the global section.

The explanation is counterfactual: source perturbations preserving the seven
laws must retain off-seam zero confinement, while a perturbation breaking a
specified face predicts which sector, grade, or completion margin can fail.

By contrast, factoring the completed scalar first and declaring its factors
accretive would be the old RH-equivalent positivity criterion in new words.

## First attack: unrestricted graph topology

Any finite family of maps can be made continuous by imposing its graph
topology.  Therefore existence of a Fourier-saturated multigraph carrier does
not support the DPC by itself.

Survival condition: the row family, target topologies, and perturbation
grammar must be frozen independently.  Otherwise clauses 1 and 3 are
realization by fiat.

Disposition: broad topology version falsified; source-frozen version survives.

## Second attack: fitted renormalization

For zero-free finite sections `E_N` and a zero-free target `X`, the choice

\[
R_N=XE_N^{-1}
\]

manufactures an exact zero-free completion and satisfies cutoff cocycle
naturality.  Thus clauses 2, 5, and 6 are circular unless the augmentation
relation is constructed without `X`.

Disposition: unrestricted counterterm version falsified.  Clause 7 is
mandatory, not decorative.

## Third attack: odd exponential gauge

The transformation

\[
D_+(z)\mapsto e^{g(z)}D_+(z),
\qquad
D_-(z)\mapsto e^{-g(z)}D_-(z),
\qquad
g(-z)=-g(z),
\]

preserves reciprocal sewing and centered normalization.  Scalar reconstruction
therefore does not determine unique sector frames.

This falsifies literal uniqueness.  The DPC survives only after quotienting
gauge transformations invisible to every admitted future composition.  If an
odd reframe changes a later source operation, it is not gauge and clause 7
must distinguish it.

Disposition: literal unique-frame DPC falsified; behavioral-quotient version
survives.

## Fourth attack: cross-port zeros

A self-adjoint carrier with distinct source and observer ports can have
nonreal zeros in a cross-resolvent coefficient.  Adjoint mate closure and
conservative dynamics do not prevent this.

Survival condition: the finite boundary relation must derive positive port
collocation or a bordered-determinant identity.  Full Weyl determinant
positivity does not orient an off-diagonal cross entry.

Disposition: any DPC based only on self-adjoint carrier closure is falsified.
Clause 4 must apply to the actual scalar-bearing denominator.

## Fifth attack: incompatible sector metrics

In the minimal adjacent-Weyl model, one sign of the sector parameter admits a
positive metric, the opposite sign is Krein-only, and the seam degenerates.
One global positive carrier is impossible.

The DPC survives only if “accretive sector denominator” is chart-relative:
each reciprocal half-plane must be placed in its own positive chart before
sewing.  If either sector requires positivity in the other's fixed frame,
clause 4 is inconsistent.

Disposition: monolithic positive DPC falsified; paired-chart version remains
open.

## Sixth attack: non-Fredholm seam

The uncompressed moving-seam projection difference has infinite-dimensional
range and is noncompact on continuum `L2`.  It does not define a determinant
line transition.

This may falsify minimality of the declared packet: the seam could require an
additional infinite boundary module not encoded by the five displayed
coordinates.  A scalar seam current is insufficient.

Survival condition: a source-authorized analytic compression must place the
seam defect in an admitted operator ideal without changing its divisor.

Disposition: clause 2 is presently blocked; no source compression has been
constructed.

## Seventh attack: hostile sources with the same visible packet

Common Fourier cores, reciprocal symmetry, Poisson pairing, positive local
weights, and several completed current identities are shared by hostile
Gaussian mixtures whose transforms have off-ray zeros.  Universal analytic
typing does not select theta orientation.

Therefore the DPC is false if its source hypotheses mention only regularity,
Fourier covariance, reciprocal symmetry, and finite determinant algebra.
The admitted perturbation grammar must detect a theta-specific response that
the hostile family lacks.

Disposition: universal `3x2 -> 1` zero-confinement theorem falsified.
Theta-specific hard-to-vary clause remains unproved.

## Eighth attack: sixth-level absence

Known determinant anomalies are exact `P/Q` coboundaries, odd holomorphic
gauges have no higher class over the current complex-vector coefficients, and
the native trace--Fourier--boundary triple is strictly associative on its
source core.

This supports the DPC prediction that no sixth tower is required.  But it is
weak evidence: current completion arrows are not all closable, so the decisive
triple graph-closure test cannot yet be formed.

Disposition: no falsification of the no-sixth clause, but no completed proof.

## Current verdict

The strong universal DPC is falsified.  The following claims do not survive:

- every typed `3x2` packet admits a zero-confining sewing;
- graph continuity supplies explanatory provenance;
- scalar reconstruction determines sector frames;
- self-adjoint or reciprocal closure alone confines zeros;
- one global positive metric exists;
- finite scalar seam data guarantee determinant geometry.

The theta-specific behavioral-quotient DPC remains logically possible, but
its load-bearing content is concentrated in two unconstructed maps:

1. a source-authorized seam compression and boundary augmentation relation;
2. a source-specific perturbation grammar proving that the resulting relation
   is hard to vary and rejects hostile Fourier-stable sources.

Without those, DPC--3x2 is an exact research charter rather than an
explanation.

## Sharpened surviving conjecture

The most defensible version is:

> The finite labelled theta/Tate constructor algebra uniquely determines,
> modulo transformations invisible to all admitted future compositions, a
> cutoff-natural boundary relation on the typed paired-sector packet.  Its
> source-derived sector denominators are chartwise accretive, its compressed
> seam is determinant-class, and its normalized compact-open sewing is the
> completed section.

The first decisive falsifier is finite: find two inequivalent boundary
relations satisfying every frozen finite reconstruction, covariance, parity,
and perturbation test.  The second is analytic: show that the unique finite
candidate has a nonclosable seam or fails compact-open completion.
