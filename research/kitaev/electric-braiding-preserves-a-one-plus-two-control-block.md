# Electric braiding preserves a one-plus-two control block

Owner: `marici.Kitaev`

## Question

Does the already frozen noncommutative electric braid action control the full
three-dimensional `C,C,C`-to-`C` multiplicity space?

No. The two elementary braid operators generate a faithful finite copy of
`S3`. Their three-dimensional representation decomposes as a trivial line plus
the standard doublet. The generated associative operator algebra has complex
dimension five rather than nine, and the continuous Hamiltonian Lie algebra
generated by the two braid reflections has real dimension four rather than
the eight dimensions required for projective qutrit control.

One Hermitian Hamiltonian with a nonzero matrix element between the invariant
line and its doublet complement is algebraically sufficient to enlarge the
continuous Lie closure to the full unitary algebra. No such source-authorized,
localized, fault-controlled bridge Hamiltonian has yet been derived.

## Claim boundary

This packet computes exact control algebras from the pre-existing electric
braid matrices. It does not run the earlier checker again, construct a bridge
Hamiltonian, prove physical pulse access to braid reflections, or extend the
result to flux and dyon sectors.

The continuous Lie result assumes independently variable real pulse times for
the declared Hermitian generators. Discrete braid availability alone gives
only the six-element group image.

## Frozen multiplicity basis

Use the left-associated channel basis

\[
|A_L\rangle,
\qquad
|B_L\rangle,
\qquad
|C_L\rangle.
\]

The established electric packet gives

\[
B_{12}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0\\
0&-1&0\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}
\]

and

\[
B_{23}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\frac12&\frac12&\frac1{\sqrt2}\\
\frac12&\frac12&-\frac1{\sqrt2}\\
\frac1{\sqrt2}&-\frac1{\sqrt2}&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

They are real symmetric involutions and satisfy

\[
B_{12}B_{23}B_{12}
=
B_{23}B_{12}B_{23}.
\]

These are microscopic electric tensor-exchange operators, not matrices fitted
from the desired control conclusion.

## Discrete braid image

Write

\[
s_1=B_{12},
\qquad
s_2=B_{23}.
\]

The relations are

\[
s_1^2=s_2^2=I,
\qquad
s_1s_2s_1=s_2s_1s_2.
\]

They imply

\[
(s_1s_2)^3=I.
\]

The two reflections are distinct, so their product is not the identity and
has order three. Hence the generated discrete image is the six-element group
`S3`, not a dense subgroup of the qutrit unitary group.

Repeated elementary electric braiding therefore supplies only six distinct
unitaries on this multiplicity space.

## Exact invariant line

Define

\[
|u\rangle
=
\frac{
\sqrt2|A_L\rangle+|C_L\rangle
}{\sqrt3}.
\]

Direct multiplication gives

\[
B_{12}|u\rangle=|u\rangle,
\qquad
B_{23}|u\rangle=|u\rangle.
\]

Let

\[
P_u=|u\rangle\langle u|,
\qquad
W=(I-P_u)\mathcal H.
\]

Every braid-generated operator commutes with `P_u`. The line spanned by `u`
and the two-dimensional space `W` are invariant.

This invariant line is not the fusion channel `A`. It is a particular
superposition of the `A` and `C` intermediate channels selected by the strand
permutation representation.

## Standard-doublet restriction

Choose the orthonormal basis of `W`

\[
|w_1\rangle=|B_L\rangle,
\qquad
|w_2\rangle
=
\frac{|A_L\rangle-\sqrt2|C_L\rangle}{\sqrt3}.
\]

In the decomposition

\[
\mathcal H
=
\operatorname{span}\{|u\rangle\}\oplus W,
\]

the braid matrices have the form

\[
B_{12}=1\oplus r_1,
\qquad
B_{23}=1\oplus r_2,
\]

with

\[
r_1
=
\begin{pmatrix}
-1&0\\
0&1
\end{pmatrix}
\]

and

\[
r_2
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\frac12&\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\\
\frac{\sqrt3}{2}&-\frac12
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The restrictions are two nonparallel reflections with trace zero. Their
product is a rotation of order three. Thus the three-dimensional braid
representation is

\[
\mathbf 1\oplus\mathbf 2,
\]

the permutation representation of `S3` decomposed into its trivial and
standard irreducibles.

## Associative operator algebra

Because the two irreducible summands are distinct and each appears once, the
complex associative algebra generated by the braid image is

\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{braid}}
\simeq
\mathbb C\oplus M_2(\mathbb C).
\]

Therefore

\[
\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathcal A_{\mathrm{braid}}
=
1^2+2^2
=
5.
\]

The ambient qutrit operator algebra has dimension

\[
\dim_{\mathbb C}M_3(\mathbb C)=9.
\]

The missing four complex directions are precisely the operators connecting
the invariant line to the standard doublet. Equivalently,

\[
P_uX(I-P_u)=0
\]

for every braid-generated associative operator `X`.

The commutant is two-dimensional:

\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{braid}}'
=
\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}
\{P_u,I-P_u\}.
\]

This conserved projector is the exact control obstruction.

## Continuous Hamiltonian closure

Now strengthen the gate model hypothetically: assume one may apply the
Hermitian braid reflections for arbitrary real times. Define

\[
\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{braid}}
=
\operatorname{Lie}_{\mathbb R}
\{iB_{12},iB_{23}\}.
\]

On `W`, the two nonparallel traceless reflections and their commutator generate

\[
\mathfrak{su}(2).
\]

Differences and commutators of the two full generators isolate this doublet
algebra. Subtracting an isolated doublet reflection from either full generator
then yields the scalar generator on the invariant line. Consequently

\[
\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{braid}}
=
\mathfrak u(1)_{u}
\oplus
\mathfrak{su}(2)_{W},
\]

and

\[
\dim_{\mathbb R}\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{braid}}
=
1+3
=
4.
\]

Full projective qutrit control requires

\[
\mathfrak{pu}(3)
\simeq
\mathfrak{su}(3),
\qquad
\dim_{\mathbb R}\mathfrak{su}(3)=8.
\]

Even the continuously pulsed braid model therefore has a four-dimensional
projective Lie deficit and preserves `P_u` exactly.

## Single-bridge sufficiency lemma

Let

\[
\mathfrak g_0
=
\mathfrak u(1)_{u}
\oplus
\mathfrak{su}(2)_{W}
\]

on

\[
\mathbb C|u\rangle\oplus W.
\]

Let `H` be Hermitian and suppose

\[
(I-P_u)HP_u\neq0.
\]

Then

\[
\operatorname{Lie}_{\mathbb R}
\bigl(
\mathfrak g_0\cup\{iH\}
\bigr)
=
\mathfrak u(3).
\]

The reason is finite and structural:

1. commutators with `iP_u` isolate the two Hermitian quadratures of the
   nonzero off-block vector;
2. the irreducible `su(2)` action on `W` moves that vector through all four
   real off-block directions;
3. commutators of off-block directions generate the missing relative diagonal
   and all doublet block directions;
4. the existing traceful `iP_u` then supplies the global scalar direction.

Thus one nonzero bridge Hamiltonian is algebraically sufficient for full
continuous qutrit control when arbitrary pulse composition is admitted.

The lemma does not construct `H`, show that it is local, or bound its fault
spread. A fixed discrete application of one bridge unitary is also weaker than
arbitrary-time Hamiltonian access.

## Observation does not supply the bridge

The negative Bargmann loop proves that the left and right fusion projectors
have noncommutative ordered geometry. The cyclic-shift test can observe that
geometry on prepared copies.

Neither result supplies a data-plane Hamiltonian with

\[
(I-P_u)HP_u\neq0.
\]

The distinction is two-sorted:

- the tester algebra acts on prepared copies, controls, and readout records;
- the actuator algebra acts on an unknown encoded data state.

An informationally complete tester may reconstruct the entire operator model
while the actuator image remains the six-element braid group. No reverse arrow
from tomography to controllability exists without an admitted synthesis map.

## Minimal hostile pair

Consider two implementations with the same accepted state preparations,
fusion-channel effects, electric braid words, and cyclic-loop tester.

### Read-only realization

The data actuator family is exactly

\[
\langle B_{12},B_{23}\rangle\simeq S_3.
\]

The external tester may estimate every declared overlap and loop on supplied
copies, but no operation couples the invariant line to `W`.

### Bridged realization

The same tester and braid operations are supplemented by one continuously
pulsed Hermitian `H` with nonzero off-block incidence. Its Lie closure is the
full unitary algebra.

All pre-bridge scalar diagnostics agree. The implementations differ in future
constructor capability. Therefore scalar and even coherent diagnostic
equivalence do not imply actuator equivalence.

## Physical source question

The exact compiler target is now

> Derive one localized, accepted, continuously controllable interaction whose
> projection onto the total-`C` multiplicity space has nonzero incidence
> between the braid-invariant line and its standard doublet, while preserving
> total charge and bounding leakage and fault spread.

Candidate mechanisms could include an ancilla-mediated fusion-channel
interaction, code deformation, measurement with coherent feed-forward, or a
non-braid local Hamiltonian. Listing them does not authorize them. Each must be
derived from the microscopic lattice source and audited after projection.

This electric bridge would establish only qutrit multiplicity control. It
would not by itself give full control of the 36-dimensional `D(S3)` endpoint
algebra or the mixed flux/dyon sectors.

## Disposition

The electric control gap is now exact. Elementary braiding gives a faithful
six-element `S3` action, a five-dimensional associative block algebra, and a
four-dimensional continuous Hamiltonian Lie algebra. All preserve one
explicit invariant line.

A single nonzero off-block Hamiltonian is algebraically sufficient to close
the qutrit Lie deficit, but its physical realization remains wholly open. The
highest-information constructor test in this electric fragment is therefore
not another associator observable. It is the source derivation or exclusion of
one localized bridge interaction.
