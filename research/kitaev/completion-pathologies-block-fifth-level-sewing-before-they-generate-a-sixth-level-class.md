# Completion pathologies block fifth-level sewing before they generate a sixth-level class

## Question

Does passing from finite algebra to rigged, projective, or Fredholm completion
invalidate the provisional model of two sector towers plus fifth-level sewing?
In particular, do known completion defects create an independent sixth-level
coherence class?

The current answer is no.  The known defects prevent the fifth-level arrows
from existing in the required category; they do not leave a residual after
valid fifth-level composition.

## Moving-seam obstruction

On continuum `L2`, translating the half-line projection gives a seam
difference equal to multiplication by the indicator of a finite interval.
Its range is infinite-dimensional and the operator is noncompact.  Hence the
projection pair is not Fredholm and does not canonically define a determinant
line transition.

This failure occurs before determinant sewing.  It means that one proposed
fifth-level edge is not typed.  Calling its nonexistence a sixth-level
coherence obstruction would reverse the categorical order:

```text
no Fredholm determinant edge
  -> no determinant square
  -> no cube whose holonomy could be sixth-level data.
```

A source-derived Hardy, de Branges, or reproducing-kernel compression might
repair the edge.  Only after that compression is frozen can higher coherence
be audited.

## Global reciprocal implementability

Every local Tate seam is unitary on the critical line, but the naive global
infinite tensor product fails because the squared local deviations have a
logarithmically divergent prime sum.  First-cumulant subtraction leaves a
square-summable projective family while exporting the primitive current as
typed boundary data.

Again, the defect concerns existence of the global sector implementer.  A
projective implementer may carry a multiplier

\[
U_gU_h=c(g,h)U_{gh}.
\]

Associativity requires `c` to satisfy the two-cocycle identity.  This central
extension is fifth-level sewing data.  It produces a sixth-level obstruction
only if the multiplier itself fails the cocycle identity and leaves a
nontrivial associator three-cocycle.  No such failure is currently derived.

## Maslov phase carry

The lifted Maslov area law has an integer carry, but it is exactly associative
and commutative.  Finite refinement therefore supplies cross rectangles, not
a braid or associator anomaly.  Its integer grade diverges at infinite cutoff,
requiring relative normalization or boundary export, but finite coherence
remains strict.

This is further evidence that completion changes the coefficient topology
without raising the coherence degree.

## Unbounded-domain frontier

There is one credible route to genuine sixth-level data.  Algebraic linear
relations compose associatively, but closed unbounded operators do not form a
category under naive composition.  Domains can fail to be dense, composites
can fail to be closable, and taking graph closure after each binary composite
need not commute with alternative parenthesizations.

A genuine witness would require three source-derived fifth-level relations
`R`, `S`, and `T` such that both parenthesized composites exist but

\[
\overline{(TS)R}
\ne
\overline{T(SR)}
\]

as closed relations, with the difference invariant under allowed graph
equivalences and visible to a later admitted composition.

No current theta/Tate packet supplies such a triple.  Known adjoint-domain and
range-density failures instead say that one binary composite is not yet
admissible.

## Refined architecture decision tree

1. **An edge is not continuous, closable, Fredholm, or implementable.**
   The relevant sector or fifth-level constructor is blocked.  Do not promote
   the defect.
2. **Edges exist and pairwise composition is projective with a valid
   two-cocycle.**
   Retain the central extension inside fifth-level sewing.
3. **Every face closes, but triple composition has a gauge-invariant
   associator.**
   A sixth-level compositor is justified.
4. **The associator is a coboundary of retained boundary data.**
   The paired towers plus fifth sewing remain sufficient.

## Fit against current results

| Completion phenomenon | Current classification | Tower consequence |

| Noncompact moving seam | Determinant edge absent | Fifth level blocked |
| Nondense adjoint domain | Reverse/boundary relation nonclosable | Sector or fifth edge blocked |

## Falsifiers of the paired-tower model

- Three individually source-authorized closed relations whose two graph-
  closed parenthesizations differ.
- A projective multiplier with a nontrivial, source-visible associator class.
- A determinant-line gerbe with nonzero Dixmier--Douady-type obstruction on
  the actual parameter/cutoff base.
- Integral phase holonomy surviving every allowed sector reframing and
  changing a later constructor.

Merely finding noncompactness, nonclosability, or divergent norms does not
meet these criteria.

## Verdict

The paired second-level sector towers plus fifth-level sewing survive the
known completion stress test.  Present analytic defects say that required
edges are absent or only projectively defined.  They do not yet say that valid
fifth-level compositions require a sixth-level associator.

The sharp next audit is therefore not to postulate a sixth rung.  It is to
freeze three closable source-derived boundary relations and compare their two
graph-closed parenthesizations.  That is the first experiment capable of
distinguishing a genuine higher coherence class from an earlier typing
failure.
