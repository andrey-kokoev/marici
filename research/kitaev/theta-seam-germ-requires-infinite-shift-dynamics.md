# The theta seam germ requires infinite-dimensional shift dynamics

Owner: `marici.Kitaev`

## Bounded question

Can the exact theta seam germ

\[
y_c(t)=\sum_jc_j\Phi(t+q_j)
\]

be realized by a finite-dimensional constant coefficient state equation

\[
\dot x=Ax,qquad y=Jx?
\]

## Finite-realization obstruction

Let

\[
W_Q=\operatorname{span}\{\Phi(\cdot+q_j):1\le j\le N\}.
\]

An exact coefficient-state realization requires differentiation to preserve
this span:

\[
\partial_tW_Q\subseteq W_Q.
\]

If this holds, Cayley--Hamilton applied to \(\partial_t|_{W_Q}\) gives a
nonzero constant-coefficient polynomial (p\) with

\[
p(\partial_t)f=0
\]

for every (f\in W_Q\), hence for every retained translate of \(\Phi\).
Solutions of finite constant-coefficient ODEs are exponential polynomials.

Grothendieck's explicit theta expansion has a super-exponentially decaying
leading mode: for every fixed (d>0\),

\[
\frac{\Phi(t+d)}{\Phi(t)}\to0
\quad(t\to+\infty).
\]

A nonzero exponential polynomial cannot have this property. Therefore no
nonzero finite translate span of the theta profile is derivative-invariant,
and no finite-dimensional constant matrix (A\) produces the exact seam germ.

The earlier diagonal Vandermonde theorem remains a valid compiler theorem for
exponential-mode dynamics, but it is not the theta source generator.

## Correct source dynamics

Let (Y\) be a function space carrying the right-shift semigroup

\[
(S_tf)(u)=f(u+t).
\]

For the initial state (g_q(u)=\Phi(u+q)\), endpoint observation gives

\[
\operatorname{ev}_0S_tg_q=\Phi(t+q).
\]

Thus the exact theta germ is generated by the infinite-dimensional shift
operator (D=\partial_u\), with endpoint observation interpreted on a rigged
domain where traces exist.

Point evaluation is not bounded on bare (L^2(0,\infty)\). Nevertheless the
integrated observation map is canonical:

\[
f\longmapsto(t\mapsto\operatorname{ev}_0S_tf)=(t\mapsto f(t)).
\]

As a map (L^2(0,\infty)\to L^2(0,\infty)\), it is the identity and satisfies

\[
\int_0^\infty|\operatorname{ev}_0S_tf|^2dt=\|f\|_2^2.
\]

This is infinite-horizon exact observability with constant one. It is an
admissible unbounded observation construction, not a bounded endpoint
functional at one time.

## Finite-horizon obstruction

For a horizon (L<\infty\),

\[
\int_0^L|\operatorname{ev}_0S_tf|^2dt
=\int_0^L|f(t)|^2dt.
\]

States supported after (L\) are completely invisible. On analytic finite
translate packets the map remains injective by unique continuation, but
translated tails can make the finite-horizon observability constant collapse.
Thus infinite-horizon mathematical faithfulness does not yield a uniformly
finite executable detector.

## Small discrete shift model

On (\mathbb F^N\), let the shift advance coordinates toward the scalar output
row (e_1^T\). Over (N\) samples, the observability Gramian is (I_N\). Over
only (L<N\) samples, it has rank (L\) and the remaining tail coordinates are
an exact hidden subspace. This is the finite model of the rigged shift theorem.

## Consequences

1. The full seam germ has an exact source dynamics, but it is
   infinite-dimensional.
2. Its infinite-horizon (L^2\) observability is an identity, analogous to
   pro-Gram isometry; it is not an independent RH positivity theorem.
3. Any physical or finite constructor must specify horizon, endpoint trace
   domain, and resource cost.
4. The completed Green/Poisson energy must be compared with this shift graph
   norm by a separately derived uniform inequality.

## Claim strength

Source-typed realization obstruction and exact shift-semigroup replacement.
No claim is made that the full infinite-horizon observation is physically
executable or that its identity norm proves off-seam nonvanishing.
