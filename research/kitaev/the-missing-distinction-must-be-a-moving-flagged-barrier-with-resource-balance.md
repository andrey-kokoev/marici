# The missing distinction must be a moving flagged barrier with resource balance

## Static mechanisms are closed

The ordered-signature candidate cannot be implemented as one fixed pointed cone or one fixed Hermitian charge on the full source module.

Vertical Mellin transport acts on a pure nonzero label by

\[
M_te_q=e^{itq}e_q.
\]

At \(t=\pi/q\), it sends \(e_q\) to \(-e_q\). Any invariant real cone containing \(e_q\) therefore contains a line and is not pointed.

Likewise, a nonconstant holomorphic family of rank-two shears cannot preserve one fixed nondegenerate Hermitian form on an open complex domain unless all coefficients lie on one real line. The theta coefficient family does not admit such a static explanation by determinant-one algebra alone.

## Moving cone correction

An ordered structure must move with spectral height or source transport. Let \(v(t)\) satisfy

\[
\dot v=A(t)v
\]

and let \(H(t)=H(t)^*\) define a moving indefinite metric or cone boundary. Put

\[
q(t)=v(t)^*H(t)v(t).
\]

Then

\[
\dot q
=
v^*S(t)v,
\qquad
S=\dot H+A^*H+HA.
\]

The matrix \(S\) is the resource or boundary supply. Exact conservation is the special case \(S=0\). A one-sided barrier can instead follow from an inward-pointing condition on the null boundary.

## Barrier theorem

Let the authorized source states initially satisfy \(q>0\). Suppose every admissible state on the relevant null boundary satisfies

\[
q=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
v^*Sv>0
\]

for the orientation in which increasing \(t\) enters the allowed chamber. Then no admissible trajectory can cross from \(q>0\) to \(q<0\) through that boundary.

With a nonstrict inequality, tangential contact remains possible and needs a higher-order audit. A cutoff-uniform strict margin gives robust exclusion.

This is an ordinary first-crossing argument, but its explanatory force depends on deriving \(H\), \(A\), and \(S\) from the source constructor family before zero inspection.

## Why a moving cone is not enough

Choosing

\[
H(t)=M_t^{-*}H(0)M_t^{-1}
\]

can make a cone covariant by definition. If source and observer are transported by the same connection, their relative incidence is constant and no theta divisor is explained.

The meaningful datum is the relative connection between:

- the moving source-order flag;
- the reciprocal observer flag;
- the modular signature splitting;
- the seam and completion boundary ports.

A fitted moving cone defined from the sign of the completed scalar is circular.

## Flagged null generator

The Krein null cone contains more than the scalar-zero line. In the reciprocal boundary plane, both diagonal and antisymmetric lines are null. Therefore the charge equation \(q=0\) does not identify the desired divisor.

The typed object must retain an ordered flag selecting the antisymmetric generator corresponding to scalar cancellation. Curvature of the moving subspace alone cannot do this: the same curved line bundle admits flags whose overlap vanishes and flags whose overlap does not.

The required barrier is consequently flagged and relational, not merely metric or topological.

## Resource-port interpretation

The supply term \(S\) records where orientation can enter or leave the reduced two-sector system. Primitive, square, seam, archimedean, and completion-at-infinity currents cannot be discarded if they contribute to this balance.

The enlarged conservation law has the form

\[
\dot q=j_{\mathrm{primitive}}+j_{\mathrm{square}}+j_{\mathrm{seam}}+j_{\infty}+j_{\mathrm{completion}}.
\]

This is a typing template. Grothendieck must derive the actual currents and signs. Calling divergent terms a resource port does not prove the balance.

## Deutschian impossible task

An off-seam zero would require a source trajectory to:

1. remain endpoint-reachable;
2. enter the source-output kernel;
3. reach the flagged antisymmetric null generator;
4. balance every boundary/resource current at the crossing;
5. retain this construction through completion.

The task is impossible if the source-derived relative flow has a uniform inward-pointing barrier at that flagged boundary.

This is more explanatory than global Herglotz positivity when the barrier sign is derived from local labelled incidence and remains valid for a counterfactual family of authorized source perturbations.

## Exact falsifiers

- a pure label and its Mellin half-turn are both claimed to lie in one fixed pointed cone;
- two non-collinear theta shear coefficients are claimed to preserve one fixed nondegenerate Hermitian metric;
- nonzero curvature is used to infer nonvanishing of an ordered overlap;
- the moving metric is defined from the completed scalar sign;
- the supply matrix has a negative direction on the flagged null boundary;
- finite inward margins collapse with cutoff;
- removing one resource port leaves the same claimed balance;
- the barrier protects the wrong null generator.

## Smallest finite audit

On the smallest source-derived framed carrier:

1. derive the relative generator \(A_X(s)\);
2. derive the moving modular metric \(H_X(s)\);
3. compute \(S_X=\partial_sH_X+A_X^*H_X+H_XA_X\) in the chosen real transport direction;
4. restrict to endpoint-reachable, source-invisible states on the flagged null boundary;
5. determine the minimum of \(v^*S_Xv\) on normalized states;
6. attribute every term to an authorized boundary/resource current;
7. test a cutoff-independent positive margin.

## Bottom line

The missing conserved distinction is not static conservation. It is a covariantly transported, flag-sensitive separation whose balance includes every source-authorized boundary current. The remaining theorem is an inward-pointing law for the relative connection on the admissible neutral boundary. Without that law, the constructor vocabulary explains the shape of an off-seam zero but not its impossibility.
