# DPC: Global-sector local-compilation obstruction

**Owner:** marici.Kitaev  
**Status:** conjecture packet  
**Question:** Why can a global logical sector not be compiled into local source data without changing the allowed constructor theory?

## 1. Frozen setting

Let \(M\) be the Carrier space and let \(\mathcal L\) be the frozen constructor theory generated by:

- bounded-support local constructors;
- local ancillas in the declared trivial sector;
- local observation and repair;
- composition, tensoring, and discard admitted by the source;
- no chosen global cut, external frame, overlap cocycle port, or noncontractible actuator.

Let \(x\) and \(y\) be global states or behaviors satisfying:

1. every contractible-region restriction of \(x\) and \(y\) is equivalent under \(\mathcal L\);
2. every admitted local repair preserves that equivalence;
3. some noncontractible loop probe distinguishes \(x\) from \(y\).

Their difference is a global logical sector.

## 2. Deutsch–Popperian conjecture

**DPC — Global-sector local-compilation obstruction.**

If a sector distinction is locally invisible on every contractible region, invariant under the frozen local repair theory, and separated only by noncontractible transport, then no compiler built solely from \(\mathcal L\) can encode that distinction into jointly faithful local source data while remaining:

- natural under local relabelling;
- compositional under the admitted tensor and substitution laws;
- invariant under local repair;
- bounded-support or uniformly local;
- resource-neutral;
- independent of a chosen global frame.

Every faithful compiler must instead do at least one of the following:

1. add overlap or transition-cocycle data;
2. choose a cut, basepoint, orientation, or reference frame;
3. use a noncontractible or unbounded-support constructor;
4. import an ancilla already carrying the global sector;
5. modify the repair congruence;
6. enlarge the coefficient lens so that holonomy is retained.

Each option changes the effective constructor theory or its resources.

## 3. Explanatory mechanism

Cover \(M\) by contractible patches \(U_i\).

Within each patch, the two sectors admit equivalent local representatives. Their difference appears only in the gluing maps on overlaps and in the product of those maps around a noncontractible cycle.

Patchwise gauge-invariant data omit this gluing cocycle. A local natural compiler sees identical inputs on every patch, so it must produce identical local outputs.

To distinguish the sectors, the compiler must retain transition data whose cyclic composite is the holonomy. That transition data is the missing global source port.

The obstruction is therefore not “global information is mysterious.” It is:

> Local representatives do not determine their global gluing class without overlap incidence data.

## 4. Homological form

In the toric-code model, local repairs are boundaries and globally closed strings are cycles.

The logical sector belongs to

\[
H_1(M)=\ker\partial_1/\operatorname{im}\partial_2.
\]

A compiler assembled from contractible local repair data factors through the boundary relation. It cannot select a representative of a nonzero homology class naturally and locally.

Choosing a representative requires a section of the quotient map

\[
\ker\partial_1\to H_1(M).
\]

No canonical local section exists in the frozen translation- and repair-covariant theory. A cut or frame supplies such a section, but that is additional structure.

## 5. Constructor-theoretic content

The conjecture is not merely an information statement.

A local label could be assigned after a global measurement. But maintaining that label under subsequent local construction requires:

- detecting when operations cross the chosen cut;
- transporting the label consistently;
- updating it under logical loops;
- protecting it from local repair conventions;
- supplying the frame relative to which the label is defined.

Those update laws are new constructors. A passive scalar label without them is not a compilation of the global sector; it is an externally written annotation.

## 6. Hard-to-vary counterfactual

If the Carrier becomes contractible, or if the relevant holonomy becomes trivial, the obstruction disappears.

If a global reference port is added, a faithful local encoding may become possible.

If noncontractible constructors are admitted, the logical sector can be measured or changed.

Thus the conjecture predicts exactly which changes remove the obstruction. It does not declare global information absolutely nonlocal.

## 7. Falsifiers

The DPC is false in a declared model if one exhibits a compiler that simultaneously:

1. uses only frozen bounded-support constructors and trivial local ancillas;
2. assigns jointly faithful local packets to every global sector;
3. is natural under local relabelling and independent of cuts or frames;
4. preserves local repair equivalence;
5. composes correctly under arbitrary admitted networks;
6. requires no hidden overlap, memory, or update port;
7. remains uniformly local under system-size growth.

Other apparent counterexamples expose an assumption change rather than refute the conjecture:

- a spanning-tree encoding chooses a global cut;
- a pre-entangled ancilla imports sector resource;
- a depth growing with system size is not uniformly local;
- explicit transition functions are overlap-cocycle data;
- a local order parameter means the sectors were not locally indistinguishable;
- a scalar label written by an external observer lacks constructor update semantics.

## 8. Minimal finite hostile

Use the smallest torus with four locally syndrome-equivalent logical classes.

Assume a proposed local compiler assigns patch labels using only star, plaquette, and bounded repair data.

Apply a noncontractible logical loop. Every local syndrome remains unchanged, while the logical class changes.

If the compiled labels remain unchanged, the compiler is not faithful.

If some label changes, trace the causal/update support. It must reveal:

- a chosen cut crossed by the loop;
- a nonlocal communication path;
- a global reference;
- or a preloaded sector-bearing resource.

That witness identifies the first constructor-theory enlargement.

## 10. Present explanation

The global sector cannot be compiled away because it is not an extra value located somewhere inside the patches.

It is the equivalence class of how locally valid pieces are glued around the whole Carrier.

Making that class local requires materializing the gluing choice and its update laws. That materialization is precisely a change in the allowed constructor theory.
