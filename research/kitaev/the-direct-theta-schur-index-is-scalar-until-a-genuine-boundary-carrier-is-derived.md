# The direct theta Schur index is scalar until a genuine boundary carrier is derived

## Question

Can the programme bypass the unauthorized feedback homotopy by computing the
index of the physical theta boundary Schur complement directly?

## Minimal theta realization

The pinned doubled-tail realization has a one-dimensional boundary space. Its
Schur complement is the additive theta endpoint section

\[
S_\partial^\theta(z)=G_+(0;z)+G_-(0;z).
\]

For this realization, the operator index is simply the winding number of the
scalar function \(S_\partial^\theta\). Computing it on contours is the
ordinary argument principle.

This is mathematically exact, but it adds no explanatory state geometry. To
prove that its winding is zero throughout the claimed domain is already to
prove that the completed scalar has no zeros there.

Thus direct index computation on the minimal boundary line is a valid
diagnostic, not a new constructor-level exclusion mechanism.

## Why the arithmetic grades cannot be appended as ports

The Euler source has one primitive diagonal loop

\[
L_se_p=p^{-s}e_p.
\]

Its prime-power terms arise from cyclic returns:

\[
-\log\det(I-L_s)
=
\sum_{k\ge1}\frac1k\operatorname{Tr}(L_s^k).
\]

The primitive, square, and connected channels correspond to \(k=1\),
\(k=2\), and \(k\ge3\) regularity grades of this same loop. They are not
independent state coordinates.

Adjoining them as separate Schur boundary ports would:

- turn cyclic composition into direct addition;
- introduce cross terms absent from the determinant source;
- double-count repeated returns;
- manufacture a finite-dimensional boundary matrix.

Therefore the provisional list of primitive, square, connected, seam, and
archimedean channels is a typed reconstruction packet, not automatically a
five-dimensional state space.

## Three different global objects

The current source provides three inequivalent realizations:

1. an additive theta boundary line;
2. a multiplicative Euler loop and relative determinant line;
3. a candidate infinite Hardy or Clark boundary quotient retaining the tail
   as a function.

They may have the same completed scalar shadow after sewing, but they are not
related by an already derived linear compression.

Their roles are:

- the theta line locates scalar zeros;
- the Euler loop retains arithmetic cyclic provenance;
- the infinite tail carrier could retain kernel and observability geometry.

Only the third could turn the scalar index into a genuinely richer operator
index, and its source incidence remains incomplete.

## Finite-channel obstruction

The finite canonical-system programme independently found that moment-based
boundary repairs become terminal-singular. Positive tail weights vanish at
the terminal boundary, and every finite normalized repair accumulates a
logarithmic connection pole.

This supports the same conclusion: the faithful boundary object is likely
the moving tail function or a relative Hardy quotient, not a finite list of
moment coordinates.

It does not prove that every finite realization is impossible. It excludes
the presently natural class of finitely tail-scaled positive repairs.

## Correct direct-index packet

There are now two honest versions.

### Scalar theta index

Use \(S_\partial^\theta(z)\) directly. Report explicitly that the index is the
scalar zero count and supplies no independent exclusion mechanism.

### Infinite boundary index

First construct an analytic Fredholm family on a source-derived boundary
space, such as a moving-tail Hilbert fiber or Clark model quotient. Then
establish:

1. the common dense or rigged domain;
2. the endpoint and arithmetic incidences;
3. Fredholmness and determinant class;
4. contour invertibility;
5. a stable analytic index;
6. identification of its scalar determinant with the completed section.

Only this route has operator information beyond the scalar argument
principle.

## Determinant-line comparison is another legitimate target

The additive theta line and multiplicative Euler determinant should meet
through a source-derived line correspondence

\[
\alpha_s:
\mathcal L_\theta(s)
\longrightarrow
\mathcal L_\infty(s)\otimes
\operatorname{Det}_{\rm rel}(I-L_s).
\]

This comparison preserves the nonlinear cyclic construction without
pretending that its cumulants are state ports. It can transport divisor data
and normalization frames.

But a determinant-line isomorphism alone still does not provide kernel
vectors or inverse margins. It remains below the operator-boundary level.

## Minimal hostile

Unless its off-diagonal incidences are derived from the source loop, many

This combines the cyclic-port obstruction with the identical-determinant

## Disposition

The proposed direct physical Schur-index shortcut does not yet produce new
information. At the only fully derived theta boundary realization it reduces
to scalar winding. The apparent finite multiport enrichment is ill-typed
because Euler cumulants are repeated loop words, not independent ports.

The highest-information route is the genuine infinite boundary carrier. It is
the first level at which an operator index could exceed scalar bookkeeping.
