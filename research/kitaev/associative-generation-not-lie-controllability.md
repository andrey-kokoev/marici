# Associative endpoint generation does not imply Lie controllability

## Question

Does generation of the full semisimple endpoint algebra by two typed ports imply that the corresponding physical controls can execute every within-block operation?

Let source-authorized Hermitian controls be \(H_1,\ldots,H_r\). Their dynamical Lie algebra is

\[
\mathfrak L
=
\operatorname{Lie}_{\mathbf R}\langle iH_1,\ldots,iH_r\rangle.
\]

Controllability is governed by \(\mathfrak L\), not by the complex associative algebra generated through arbitrary products.

## Claim boundary

### Irreducible single-block hostile

Take the spin-one irreducible representation of \(\mathfrak{su}_2\) on \(\mathbf C^3\), generated by \(J_x,J_y,J_z\). Irreducibility implies, by Burnside's theorem, that the unital complex associative algebra generated by these matrices is

\[
M_3(\mathbf C).
\]

But their real dynamical Lie algebra is only the represented

\[
\mathfrak{su}_2,
\]

of dimension three, rather than \(\mathfrak{su}_3\), of dimension eight. Full associative envelope therefore does not imply full unitary controllability.

### Correlated two-block hostile

In

\[
A=M_2(\mathbf C)\oplus M_2(\mathbf C),
\]

take

\[
H_x=(\sigma_x,\sigma_x),
\qquad
H_z=(\sigma_z,\sigma_z),
\qquad
Z=(I,-I).
\]

The central separator \(Z\) and the diagonal Pauli controls generate the full associative algebra. Indeed,

\[
P_\pm=\frac{I\pm Z}{2}
\]

are block projectors, and associative products \(P_\pm H_x\), \(P_\pm H_z\) isolate each block.

The dynamical Lie algebra generated by \(iH_x,iH_z,iZ\) is different. Since \(Z\) is central, commutators cannot form the products \(ZH_x\) or \(ZH_z\). The noncentral Lie closure remains the diagonal copy

\[
\{(K,K):K\in\mathfrak{su}_2\},
\]

together with the available central direction. Independent \(\mathfrak{su}_2\oplus\mathfrak{su}_2\) block control is absent.

This is the precise hostile for the \(D(S_3)\) port claim. Associative words may construct central projectors and block-isolating products algebraically, while switchable Hamiltonian controls supply only real linear combinations and commutators. Multiplication of two available Hamiltonians is not automatically an available Hamiltonian.

For

\[
D(S_3)
\cong
\mathbf C^{\oplus2}
\oplus M_2(\mathbf C)^{\oplus4}
\oplus M_3(\mathbf C)^{\oplus2},
\]

full within-block unitary controllability requires the noncentral dynamical Lie algebra

\[
\mathfrak{su}_2^{\oplus4}
\oplus
\mathfrak{su}_3^{\oplus2},
\]

of dimension \(28\), with independent block factors. Central phase controls are additional and must be typed separately.

This packet is an obstruction theorem and finite hostile. It does not determine the dynamical Lie algebra of the actual transposition and three-cycle ports because their source-derived Hermitian control matrices have not been supplied here.

## Disposition

The endpoint programme now has three independent generation tests:

1. associative generation of the 36-dimensional algebra;
2. Lie generation of the 28-dimensional within-block control algebra;
3. source-authorized physical implementation of the generating Hamiltonians.

Passing an earlier test does not imply a later one.

The first falsifier is one of:

1. arbitrary matrix products are treated as switchable Hamiltonians;
2. irreducibility is used to infer \(\mathfrak{su}_n\) controllability;
3. central block projectors are assumed to decouple correlated controls under Lie brackets;
4. the Lie closure dimension is reported without its block projections;
5. full Lie rank is claimed without checking independent action on repeated-size blocks;
6. abstract Hamiltonians are used without source authority.

The next required input is the explicit Hermitian realization of both flux-resolved ports on all eight simple blocks. Once supplied, compute the real Lie closure, its dimension, its projection to every block, and the kernel of the joint block-projection map.
