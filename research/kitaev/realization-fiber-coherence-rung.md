# Realization-fiber coherence is the eighth tower rung

## Question

What structure remains after two systems receive the same strongest SCC profile, and should comparison of that residual structure be represented as the next rung of the canonical tower?

## Claim boundary

The finite counterexample proves that SCC profile, behavioral kernel, and viewing Gramian do not determine constructor equivalence. The proposed eighth rung organizes comparisons inside one profile fiber. It does not assert that every fiber comparison exists physically, that every fiber is a groupoid before authority checks, or that theta/Tate realization moduli have been classified.

## Disposition

### Fiber object

For an SCC profile (a), define the realization fiber

\[
\mathcal C_a=\{c:\alpha(c)=a\}.
\]

A verified morphism from (c) to (d) must intertwine their named constructors, observations, viewing-strength forms, authority, completion topology, and conditional instrument transitions. In the finite linear core this includes

\[
T A_c=A_dT,
\qquad
O_dT=R O_c,
\qquad
T^*W_dT=W_c.
\]

Invertible verified morphisms generate the realization-intertwiner groupoid. Its connected components are operational domains lying over one SCC profile. Automorphisms within a component are frame or gauge freedoms; nontrivial comparison loops are fiber holonomy.

### Exact same-profile falsifier

Let both carriers be \(\mathbf R^2\), both observers be the identity, and both viewing Gramians be (I_2). Let the named generator of (C_2) act as

\[
A=-I_2
\]

in one realization and

\[
B=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)
\]

in the other. Both actions are faithful (C_2)-representations and both systems occupy the finite strongest profile

\[
(\mathrm{compatible},\mathrm{faithful},\mathrm{faithful},\mathrm{uniform}).
\]

Yet their characters differ:

\[
\operatorname{tr}A=-2,
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}B=0.
\]

Writing (T=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}), the intertwiner equation

\[
TA=BT
\]

forces (a=b=0). Every intertwiner has rank at most one, so no constructor equivalence exists. The exact checker records eight passing gates.

The canonical tower now contains


### Meaning

The new rung is not another stronger observer within one realization. It compares implementations satisfying the same abstract law and certification profile. It is therefore the first rung whose primary output is a moduli decomposition:

\[
\text{profile}
\longmapsto
\text{realization fiber}
\longmapsto
\text{intertwiner components}.
\]

This is where operational domains become derived structural objects rather than names assigned to regions of the 405 classifier.

1. Equal SCC profile is treated as constructor equivalence without an intertwiner.
2. Equal spectra replace comparison of eigenspaces and named actions.
3. An invertible carrier map fails to intertwine a named constructor or instrument transition.
4. Realizations from unrelated authority contexts are placed in one fiber groupoid without a crossing.

6. Pairwise intertwiners are promoted to global fiber coherence without triangle and higher checks.
