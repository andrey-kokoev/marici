# A four-copy cyclic shift measures the associator loop exactly

Owner: `marici.Kitaev`

## Question

What is the smallest explicit coherent circuit presently available for the
gauge-invariant electric associator loop, and which of its ingredients are
source-derived rather than merely mathematically sufficient?

A four-copy cyclic-shift interferometer measures the ordered Bargmann trace
directly. It replaces four separate transition probabilities by one coherent
controlled permutation. For the frozen electric `D(S3)` fragment, the control
qubit has exact expectations

\[
\langle X\rangle=-\frac18,
\qquad
\langle Y\rangle=0.
\]

This gives a finite circuit specification. It does not prove that a controlled
four-copy permutation is generated by the admitted lattice Hamiltonians,
ribbon operations, measurements, or fault-tolerant gadgets.

## Claim boundary

The construction is an exact mathematical realization of the already derived
ordered loop. It is not claimed to be the unique realization, the minimum-copy
realization among all measurement models, or an authorized physical
constructor in the current `D(S3)` compiler.

The exact transposition count applies only when a cyclic permutation is built
from whole-copy swaps. Depth and fault overhead depend on the hardware and
gate library.

## Cyclic-shift identity

Let `H` be a finite-dimensional Hilbert space and let

\[
R_1,R_2,R_3,R_4
\]

be trace-class operators on `H`. Define the left cyclic shift on four copies
by

\[
V_4
|x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4\rangle
=
|x_2,x_3,x_4,x_1\rangle.
\]

Direct index contraction gives

\[
\operatorname{Tr}
\left[
V_4
(R_1\otimes R_2\otimes R_3\otimes R_4)
\right]
=
\operatorname{Tr}(R_1R_2R_3R_4).
\]

This identity retains the order of the four factors. Replacing the left side
by a product of four two-copy swap expectations would retain only pairwise
magnitudes and would not reproduce the ordered phase.

## Electric associator specialization

On the total-`C` multiplicity space, use the rank-one projectors

\[
P_A,
\qquad
Q_A,
\qquad
P_B,
\qquad
Q_C
\]

from the frozen left and right fusion-tree frames. Prepare the four-copy state

\[
\rho_4
=
P_A\otimes Q_A\otimes P_B\otimes Q_C.
\]

Then

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(V_4\rho_4)
=
\operatorname{Tr}(P_AQ_AP_BQ_C)
=
-\frac18.
\]

The negative sign is the gauge-invariant four-edge associator holonomy. The
four basis transition probabilities determine the magnitude `1/8` but not
this ordered sign.

## Control-qubit readout

Prepare a control qubit in

\[
|+\rangle
=
\frac{|0\rangle+|1\rangle}{\sqrt2}
\]

and apply

\[
C(V_4)
=
|0\rangle\langle0|\otimes I
+
|1\rangle\langle1|\otimes V_4.
\]

For a target state `rho`, the reduced control state has off-diagonal entry
proportional to

\[
z=\operatorname{Tr}(V_4\rho).
\]

With the above control convention,

\[
\langle X\rangle=\operatorname{Re}z,
\qquad
\langle Y\rangle=\operatorname{Im}z.
\]

For `rho_4`, the exact prediction is therefore

\[
\langle X\rangle=-\frac18,
\qquad
\langle Y\rangle=0.
\]

The control measurement is a record of a coherent ordered word. Measuring the
four target ports independently before the controlled permutation destroys
the very cross-route coherence the circuit is meant to expose.

## Whole-copy swap decomposition

Let `S_ij` exchange complete copies `i` and `j`. The declared left shift is

\[
V_4=S_{34}S_{23}S_{12},
\]

where the rightmost swap acts first. Indeed,

\[
(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)
\longmapsto
(x_2,x_1,x_3,x_4)
\longmapsto
(x_2,x_3,x_1,x_4)
\longmapsto
(x_2,x_3,x_4,x_1).
\]

Using the same control on every factor gives

\[
C(V_4)
=
C(S_{34})C(S_{23})C(S_{12}).
\]

A four-cycle has one permutation cycle on four symbols. A transposition can
change the number of permutation cycles by at most one. Reaching it from the
identity, which has four cycles, therefore requires at least three
transpositions. The displayed factorization is transposition-count minimal.

This is not a depth theorem. The three controlled swaps share one control and
their target supports overlap. Parallel depth depends on whether multi-target
control fanout, cat controls, long-range swaps, or native controlled
permutations are admitted.

## Source-authority audit

The current electric source packet authorizes the mathematical states and
effects

\[
P_A,
Q_A,
P_B,
Q_C
\]

as representation-theoretic objects. It does not yet authorize four physical
preparations of encoded total-`C` multiplicity states.

The endpoint algebra result authorizes abstract projectors and associative
generation. It does not supply a coherent control qubit, a Fredkin operation
on encoded copies, or an accepted nondestructive cyclic permutation.

Consequently the controlled-shift circuit is a compiler target, not evidence
that the compiler already exists.

## Minimal resource packet for this realization

An executable instance requires:

1. four independently accepted preparations in one common total-`C`
   multiplicity convention;
2. one coherent control qubit with calibrated `X` and `Y` readout;
3. either one native controlled four-cycle or three accepted controlled
   whole-copy swaps;
4. preservation of the total-charge and code-space constraints under every
   swap;
5. a causal account of how the shared control reaches all swap locations;
6. a one-fault propagation contract for control, target, and measurement
   faults;
7. an incoherent null fixture that preserves every edge probability while
   removing control-path coherence.

Nothing in the exact trace identity supplies these resources automatically.

## Visibility and phase calibration

A useful phenomenological interferometer model is

\[
z_{\mathrm{obs}}
=
\eta e^{i\chi}z+b,
\]

where `eta` is coherent visibility, `chi` is control-path phase offset, and `b`
is an additive coherent or readout bias.

For the exact electric value, positive visibility alone preserves the negative
real sign only when the phase offset is known not to rotate it across a
quadrature boundary. Edge-probability calibration constrains none of `eta`,
`chi`, or `b`.

The minimum calibration packet therefore contains:

- an identity-cycle fixture with known positive real trace;
- a phase-quadrature fixture distinguishing `X` from `Y` and fixing orientation;
- an incoherent randomized-control fixture with zero expected fringe;
- the target four-cycle;
- repetition under reversed cyclic order, for which the ideal value is the
  complex conjugate.

For the present real loop, reversal leaves the ideal value unchanged but still
tests orientation-dependent systematic errors.

## Shared-control fault obstruction

The same control participates in every controlled swap. A control fault can
therefore corrupt several route segments while leaving the four target
preparations individually valid.

- A phase fault rotates or reverses the recorded loop quadrature.
- A branch-selection fault applies only a prefix or suffix of the cycle.
- A common preparation error in the control is repeated across every shot if
  all shots inherit one drifting reference.
- Repetition after the shared control copies the corrupted branch meaning and
  does not diagnose the common-mode fault.

Fault-tolerant realization therefore needs either a verified distributed
control state, independently calibrated control instances, or a native local
instrument whose fault spread is bounded by construction. Abstract controlled
permutation does not choose among these repairs.

## Hostile fixtures

### Four disconnected edge measurements

Measure each `P_e,Q_f` overlap separately and multiply the resulting
probabilities. The product gives

\[
|z|^2=\frac1{64}
\]

but contains no sign or complex phase.

### Classical cyclic routing

Conditionally relabel four classical channel records by the same four-cycle.
This realizes the permutation on labels but supplies no superposition between
the identity and cyclic branches. It cannot generate a control-qubit fringe.

### Control dephasing

Apply complete dephasing to the control before its final quadrature readout.
The target edge statistics and target permutation branch remain available,
but

\[
\langle X\rangle=\langle Y\rangle=0.
\]

### Uncalibrated coherent phase

A stable phase offset of `pi` changes the reported real sign while preserving
the fringe magnitude and every target edge probability. An identity fixture
or equivalent independent phase reference is therefore mandatory.

## Falsifiers

- The cyclic-shift trace is replaced by a product of pairwise traces.
- The declared left shift is implemented with the opposite operator order
  without conjugating the predicted loop.
- Three whole-copy transpositions are claimed to imply physical depth three.
- A controlled permutation is inferred from availability of an uncontrolled
  permutation.
- Four representation-theoretic projectors are called four accepted encoded
  state preparations.
- Control dephasing is claimed to preserve the loop because it preserves target
  probabilities.
- Repeating one drifting control reference is called independent calibration.
- The circuit is called source-authorized without a gate, locality, and fault
  contract.
- This sufficient realization is called universally copy-minimal.

## Disposition

The gauge-invariant electric associator loop now has an exact finite circuit
target. Four copies and one controlled cyclic shift convert the ordered
projector trace into control-qubit quadratures. In the frozen source frame the
ideal signal is purely real and equal to `-1/8`.

The construction exposes rather than hides the missing capability. In a
whole-copy transposition library it requires three controlled swaps sharing one
coherent control. The outstanding theorem is whether the `D(S3)` lattice
source can realize that controlled permutation with accepted preparations,
bounded fault spread, and independent phase calibration.

No checker, build, or Git operation was run for this research-only packet.
