# A coherence filler is typed process data, not necessarily contextual equality

Owner: marici.Kitaev

## Question

Must a legitimate coherence filler prove that its boundary paths are equivalent
under every authorized context, or can a higher constructor theory contain
nontrivial fillers between paths that remain distinguishable?

## Claim boundary

The contextual-equivalence criterion is false as a universal account of
fillability.

A 2-cell

[
alpha:PLongrightarrow Q
]

need not assert (P=Q), need not make (P) and (Q) operationally
indistinguishable, and need not be invertible. It may be an authorized
conversion, homotopy, natural transformation, defect surface, recovery,
simulation, or refinement between genuinely different boundary paths.

The error was to conflate three operations:

1. attaching a typed filler between two paths;
2. declaring the filler invertible;
3. quotienting the filler to an identity and erasing the distinction.

Only the third requires contextual indistinguishability strong enough to
support identification.

### Falsifier 1: a natural isomorphism between unequal functors

Let (F,G:mathcal A	omathcal B) be unequal functors connected by a natural
isomorphism

[
eta:FLongrightarrow G.
]

The components of (eta) provide coherent fillers for every object, and
naturality controls every constructor square. Nevertheless (F) and (G)
remain distinct typed routes. A context can refer to the component
(eta_X), its cost, its phase, or its implementation.

Thus a source-derived and context-stable filler exists without endpoint
equality. The previous criterion would incorrectly reject it.

### Falsifier 2: irreversible refinement

Let (P) be a detailed behavior and (Q) its authorized coarse-graining.
There may be a noninvertible comparison

[
alpha:PLongrightarrow Q
]

that discards information. Every context transports this comparison
functorially, yet no inverse comparison exists and (P,Q) are plainly
distinguishable.

This filler is legitimate as a directed constructor law but cannot contract
the boundary cycle as an equivalence.

### Falsifier 3: physical defect or surface insertion

Two boundary transports can differ by a physical defect operator
(Omega). A surface whose boundary is the two paths realizes

[
alpha:PLongrightarrow Omega Q.
]

The filler carries the very flux or curvature data that an identity filler
would erase. Its existence demonstrates physical structure rather than
path-independence.

Topological and braided systems routinely require this typed possibility. A
nontrivial associator or braiding cell is not evidence that its boundary paths
were already equal as raw words.

### Falsifier 4: multiple inequivalent fillers

Even when (P) and (Q) have the same endpoints, there can be several
2-cells

[
alpha,eta:PLongrightarrow Q
]

whose difference is detected by a higher context. Declaring only that the
boundary paths are equivalent loses the space of fillers and its possible
logical information.

A 3-cell may compare (alpha) and (eta). If no such 3-cell exists, their
difference is a higher logical sector.

### Falsifier 5: local fillers with a global anomaly

Every elementary face of a constructor complex may possess a natural,
invertible filler, while the fillers fail to satisfy a global pentagon,
tetrahedral, or anomaly-cancellation law. Pairwise contextual equivalence of
boundary paths does not guarantee simultaneous coherent choice of all
fillers.

Therefore fillability of each face and closure of the whole complex are
separate statements.

### The correct contextual law

Contexts should transport fillers, not necessarily collapse endpoints.

For an authorized context (C[-]), the required operation is

[
C[alpha]:C[P]Longrightarrow C[Q].
]

Composition must preserve vertical and horizontal cell composition and the
relevant identities. In a weak theory it must preserve them up to specified
higher cells.

The appropriate question is therefore:

> Is (alpha) a source-authorized 2-morphism whose images under all authorized
> contexts satisfy the required naturality and higher coherence laws?

Only if the task additionally declares

[
alphasimeq operatorname{id}
]

may the cycle be quotiented away. A merely invertible nonidentity filler still
retains transport data. A noninvertible filler retains direction and loss.

### Revised four-way classification

For a boundary pair (P,Q), distinguish:

- **unfilled:** no authorized comparison exists;
- **process-filled:** a directed 2-morphism (PRightarrow Q) exists;
- **equivalence-filled:** an invertible comparison exists;
- **contracted:** the comparison is authorized as identity or gauge-trivial in
  the declared task localization.

These levels have different constructor consequences.

An unfilled cycle can carry free holonomy. A process-filled cycle permits a
conversion but not reversal. An equivalence-filled cycle permits reversible
transport but may retain phase, cost, or defect data. Only a contracted cycle
removes the corresponding logical distinction.

### Effect on the Morita programme

A Morita context contains process fillers given by its two module pairings.
Strict Morita equivalence requires fullness and invertibility at the level of
module categories, but individual interface passages remain typed data. The
double-centralizer theorem does not say those passages are identities.

Similarly, the (D(S_3)) pair-channel fills a missing off-diagonal incidence.
It does not prove the one- and two-dimensional fusion sectors were previously
the same. It connects them inside a larger linking algebra.

### New falsifier schema

A proposed classification fails if it reports a cycle as contracted when any
authorized context can detect:

- which filler was used;
- its orientation;
- its phase or central class;
- its resource cost;
- its environment record;
- its noninvertibility;
- a higher inconsistency among several fillers.

Conversely, reporting every detectable filler as an unfilled obstruction is
also wrong: detectable higher cells can be legitimate constructor resources.

## Disposition

The contextual-equivalence conjecture is replaced by a cell-transport
principle:

> A legitimate filler is source-authorized typed process data that is natural
> under authorized contexts and coherent with other cells. Contraction requires
> the stronger, task-relative proof that this filler is identity or
> gauge-trivial.

This preserves the earlier Popperian critic. A context can falsify the claimed
type, naturality, invertibility, or triviality of a filler. But different
claims require different witnesses.

The next finite model should therefore contain two paths and two distinct
fillers. A higher context should distinguish the fillers while leaving their
boundary maps fixed. Adding or withholding a 3-cell between them will cleanly
separate process fillability, equivalence, contraction, and higher logical
sector.
