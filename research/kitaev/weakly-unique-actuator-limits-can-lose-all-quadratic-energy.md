# Weakly Unique Actuator Limits Can Lose All Quadratic Energy

Let (B_N:\mathbb C\to\ell^2) be

\[
B_N(1)=e_N.
\]

For every fixed probe (f\in\ell^2),

\[
\langle f,B_N(1)\rangle=\overline{f_N}\to0.
\]

Thus (B_N\) converges to zero in the weak operator topology, and zero is its
unique weak cluster point. Nevertheless

\[
\|B_N\|=1,
\qquad
B_N^*B_N=1
\]

for every (N). There is no strong operator convergence on the input (1),
and the quadratic energy does not converge to the energy of the weak limit.

A moving probe (e_N) sees

\[
\langle e_N,B_N(1)\rangle=1,
\]

while every fixed finite probe becomes blind. Weak compactness plus uniqueness
therefore closes linear scalar readouts only; it does not close norms,
quadratic Green forms, actuator graphs, or moving seam observations.

## Sufficient upgrades

To promote weak convergence (B_N\rightharpoonup B) to strong convergence on
a state, one may prove convergence of norms:

\[
\|B_Nx\|\to\|Bx\|.
\]

Uniformly over a state family, one needs an appropriate compactness or
collective-tightness theorem. For quadratic identities, directly require
control of (B_N^*B_N) or a polarization-complete graph limit; weak convergence
of (B_N) alone is insufficient.

In the theta/Tate setting, a unique weak scalar limit cannot authorize passage
of the completed Green energy or seam actuator. The tail energy may escape into
moving arithmetic directions exactly as in the hostile.

## Falsifiers

- All fixed probes converge while a moving probe stays order one.
- Weak actuator limits are substituted into quadratic forms.
- Norms fail to converge to the norm of the weak limit.
- A unique weak cluster point is reported as graph convergence.
- Tail tightness is assumed from boundedness alone.
