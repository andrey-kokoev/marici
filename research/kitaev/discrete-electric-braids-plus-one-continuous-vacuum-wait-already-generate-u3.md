## Explicit Lie-closure proof

Let

\[
E_{ij}=|q_i\rangle\langle q_j|.
\]

Define the real skew generators

\[
K_{ij}=E_{ij}-E_{ji}.
\]

A direct multiplication of the three edge projectors gives

\[
K_{01}
=
-2
\left(
[H_{01},H_{02}]
-[H_{01},H_{12}]
\right).
\]

Cyclic permutation of the axes gives `K_02` and `K_12`. Since the dynamical
Lie algebra starts from `iH_ij`, these real skew matrices belong to it up to
irrelevant real signs.

Next,

\[
[K_{ij},H_{ij}]
=
E_{ii}-E_{jj}.
\]

Hence every traceless diagonal anti-Hermitian direction is generated. Writing

\[
D_{ij}=E_{ii}-E_{jj},
\]

one also has

\[
[D_{ij},K_{ij}]
=
2(E_{ij}+E_{ji}).
\]

Thus both Hermitian quadratures of every off-diagonal matrix unit are present:

\[
E_{ij}+E_{ji}
\]

and

\[
-iE_{ij}+iE_{ji}.
\]

Finally,

\[
2H_{ij}
=
E_{ii}+E_{jj}+E_{ij}+E_{ji}.
\]

Subtracting the already generated symmetric off-diagonal part gives every
pairwise diagonal sum. Pairwise sums and differences yield each `E_ii`, and
their total yields the scalar identity.

Therefore

\[
\operatorname{Lie}_{\mathbb R}
\{iH_{01},iH_{02},iH_{12}\}
=
\mathfrak u(3).
\]

Equivalently, completed electric braids together with arbitrary-time evolution
under the vacuum-wait corridor generate a dense subgroup of `U(3)` and, under
standard piecewise-constant control closure, full qutrit controllability.

This stronger result depends on the exact incidence

## Fault implications

wrong edge. Because every edge is valid, internal group relations need not
detect the error. The logical fusion-frame record must be anchored.

### Timing fault

Waiting-time error becomes a continuous logical overrotation. The native
storage syndrome does not discretize it.

Replacing `P_A` by the axis projector `P_C` leaves continuous control inside
the monomial group when only discrete braids are available. Nonzero bridge
incidence under the earlier continuously pulsed model is not enough to infer
the present discrete-routing theorem.
