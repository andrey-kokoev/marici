# Continuity compiler for constructor-generated pro-Gram completions

## Finite constructor topology

At cutoff \(N\), let \(K_N\ge0\) be the analytic Gram matrix and let an
authorized constructor \(C\) act by \(D_{C,N}\). For a finite family \(F\),
put

\[
Q_{F,N}=\sum_{C\in F}D_{C,N}^*K_ND_{C,N}.
\]

The topology generated by the constructor seminorms is the directed family
of these finite sums. Continuity of a target requires domination by **one
fixed finite authorized family**, not a cutoff-dependent family.

## Linear-current theorem

For a row or row block \(L_N\), the following are equivalent at fixed cutoff:

1. \(L_N^*L_N\le M_N^2Q_{F,N}\) for some finite \(M_N\);
2. \(\ker Q_{F,N}\subseteq\ker L_N\).

When the kernel gate holds, the sharp squared constant is

\[
M_N^2=\lambda_{\max}
\left(Q_{F,N}^{\dagger/2}L_N^*L_NQ_{F,N}^{\dagger/2}\right).
\]

The current extends to the pro-Gram completion exactly when some fixed finite
\(F\) satisfies the kernel gate for every cutoff and

\[
\sup_NM_N<\infty.
\]

## Hermitian and mixed Green forms

A Hermitian target \(B_N\) is continuous precisely when

\[
-CQ_{F,N}\le B_N\le CQ_{F,N}
\]

with one cutoff-independent \(C\). Its kernel must therefore contain the
kernel of \(Q_{F,N}\), and its sharp constant is the largest absolute
generalized eigenvalue on the Gram support.

For a mixed form \(c^*B_Nd\), with left and right constructor Gramians
\(Q_{+,N}\) and \(Q_{-,N}\), form

\[
\widehat B_N=
\begin{pmatrix}0&B_N\\B_N^*&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\widehat Q_N=\operatorname{diag}(Q_{+,N},Q_{-,N}).
\]

Then

\[
-C\widehat Q_N\le\widehat B_N\le C\widehat Q_N
\]

is equivalent to

\[
|c^*B_Nd|le C
(c^*Q_{+,N}c)^{1/2}(d^*Q_{-,N}d)^{1/2}.
\]

It enforces both kernel conditions
\(\ker Q_{-,N}\subseteq\ker B_N\) and
\(\ker Q_{+,N}\subseteq\ker B_N^*\).

## Minimal authorized witness

For a frozen finite constructor registry, enumerate subsets by increasing
cardinality. A subset controls a target only if it passes both:

1. every finite-cutoff kernel gate;
2. a common domination bound over all cutoffs.

Return all inclusion-minimal subsets at the first successful cardinality,
together with ranks and sharp constants. Full rank alone is not a continuity
certificate.

The exact checker includes a three-coordinate synthetic registry and compiles
minimal controls for primitive, square, seam, archimedean, mixed Green, and
defect targets. These names are test roles only; they are not theta-source
identifications.

## Six hostile fixtures

1. **Kernel failure:** \(Q=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)\), \(L=(0\;1)\), with
   hidden witness \((0,1)^T\).
2. **Divergent constants:**
   \(Q_N=\operatorname{diag}(1,N^{-2})\), \(L=(0\;1)\), giving
   \(M_N=N\).
3. **Rank-complete but nonuniform:** every \(Q_N\) has rank two, while its
   smallest eigenvalue is \(N^{-2}\).
4. **No fixed finite family:** at cutoff \(N\), only constructor \(C_N\)
   observes the target coordinate. Every cutoff has a controller, but no
   fixed finite subset controls all cutoffs.
5. **Scalar cancellation:** two nonzero typed residual matrices \(B\) and
   \(-B\) sum to zero. The scalar total closes while neither typed channel
   vanishes.
6. **Unauthorized repair:** adding a non-admitted complementary coordinate
   repairs domination, but provides no source-authorized continuity theorem.

## Continuity is not observability

- **Continuity** asks whether a target current extends to the completion.
- **Faithfulness** asks whether selected observations separate admissible
  states.
- **Uniform observability** asks for a cutoff-independent lower energy bound.

A continuous current can vanish on an invariant state sector. Conversely, a
finite family can have full rank at every cutoff while its observability
constant collapses.

## Theta/Tate boundary

The compiler is ready only after Grothendieck supplies

\[
K_N,D_{C,N},L_{k=1,N},L_{k=2,N},L_{\rm seam,N},L_{\infty,N},
B_{\rm Green,N}
\]

and the typed defect residuals. It may optimize solely over the frozen
authorized constructor monoid. A predicate or row added because it improves
the inequality is an unauthorized repair.

The completed doubled Green identity is legitimate only if every constituent
current and mixed form is continuous in one common constructor-generated
topology and the typed residual identity holds before scalar cancellation.
