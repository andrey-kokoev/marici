# Dualizable task completeness explains bridge, relational volume, and self-closure

Owner: marici.Kitaev

## Question

What independently motivated constructor principle could make the source
bridge, minimal commutant, and self-closure consequences of one explanation
rather than three fitted observations?

## Claim boundary

The best current candidate is **dualizable task completeness**.

For every authorized constructor \(f:X\to Y\), states move forward and
requirements move backward:

\[
f_!: \operatorname{Reach}(X)\to\operatorname{Reach}(Y),
\qquad
f^*: \operatorname{Req}(Y)\to\operatorname{Req}(X).
\]

They must be mates for the operational evaluation:

\[
\langle f^*r,x\rangle_X=\langle r,f_!x\rangle_Y,
\qquad
(gf)^*=f^*g^*.
\]

This law is independently motivated. An intervention and the requirement used
to test it must give the same answer whether the state is transported forward
or the test is transported backward. Without it, the constructor theory has no
composition-stable meaning of successful action.

The principle has three logically distinct clauses.

1. **Duality:** forward interventions and backward tests preserve evaluation.
2. **Task completeness:** after quotienting by distinctions irrelevant to the
   declared task, every remaining distinguishable direction is reached or
   tested by an authorized constructor context.
3. **Coherent base change:** the mate squares for composition, localization,
   and completion satisfy their coherence laws, with a noncollapsing lower
   gain when a completion is involved.

These clauses explain the three structures under study.

### The bridge is evaluation naturality

The source-to-behavior bridge is not an extra comparison chosen after the
fact. It is the mate identifying forward action with backward testing. In the
toric code this appears as the chain--cochain evaluation behind Stokes:

\[
\langle \delta a,c\rangle=\langle a,\partial c\rangle.
\]

Together with \(\partial_1\partial_2=0\), it makes local repair transport
compatible with syndrome and logical pairing. In \(D(S_3)\), coproduct,
antipode, and the universal \(R\)-matrix provide the corresponding common
parent: fusion and braiding are not independent matrices but representations
of one Hopf constructor theory.

### Relational volume is task-relative completeness

Let \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{task}}\) be the algebra generated by authorized
contexts on the task-localized state object. Then

\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{task}}'
\]

contains precisely the distinctions invisible to every generated action. The
correct completeness test is therefore not always scalar commutant. It is

\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{task}}'=Z_{\mathrm{authorized}},
\]

where \(Z_{\mathrm{authorized}}\) is the superselection or multiplicity algebra
that the task explicitly permits. Excess commutant is a hidden relational
direction. A smaller commutant would mean that the proposed constructors
illegally mix an authorized superselection distinction.

The toric four-port Pfaffian is the abelian, skew-form shadow of this test. Its
nonvanishing says that the logical pairing is nondegenerate. The noncommutative
successor is minimal authorized commutant, not a universal scalar Pfaffian.

### Self-closure is coherence of the mate calculus

Once constructors and requirements are mates, alternate routes through a
composition square have a canonical comparison cell. Self-closure occurs when
all remaining comparison cells are forced by the constructor laws:

- boundary nilpotence in a chain theory;
- cocycle coherence for transported frames;
- pentagon and hexagon identities in a monoidal or braided theory;
- Beck--Chevalley coherence under localization or completion.

A new rung is required exactly when one of these comparisons remains an
independent datum rather than a consequence. Thus the tower stops because the
mate calculus closes, not because a preselected number of levels has been
filled.

### Pinned sector tests

The toric code satisfies the algebraic bridge through chain--cochain duality.
Its logical pairing is nondegenerate, while local syndrome alone has an excess
logical kernel. Adding the two noncontractible loop ports removes that excess
modulo local repair. The remaining physical question is whether those ports
are executable with the required nondemolition and reset resources.

The pure-electric \(D(S_3)\) braid fragment provides a sharper test. Braiding
on the three-dimensional fusion space generates \(\mathbb C\oplus M_2\), so
the commutant is \(\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb C\): a one-dimensional sector and a
two-dimensional sector remain relationally disconnected. An authorized
pair-channel with unequal action on the two relevant fusion labels couples
those sectors and reduces the commutant to scalars. The missing physical
compiler for that channel remains separate from this algebraic fact.

## Disposition

Adopt dualizable task completeness as the current common-parent explanation,
but only as a conjectural compiler principle. It predicts three distinct
failure signatures:

- violation of duality produces evaluation drift: forward execution and
  backward verification disagree;
- violation of task completeness produces excess commutant: a hidden sector
  survives every authorized context;
- violation of coherent base change produces path dependence, a noninvertible
  mate, or a lower gain collapsing under completion.

The principle is stronger than scalar agreement and weaker than physical
implementation. A mathematical constructor family may satisfy every algebraic
law while its executable subfamily has a larger commutant because actuation,
reset, fault, or topology authority is absent.
