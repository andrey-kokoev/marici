# Local orbit compilation of the `G/H` separator

Owner: `marici.Kitaev`

Status: exact finite source-typed circuit theorem; hardware realization and
fault tolerance remain conditional.

## Bounded question

Can the additional imaginary three-cycle endpoint quadrature be obtained
from the frozen local `D(S3)` operations, rather than inserted as a 16-by-16
block matrix?

Let `c=(012)`, let `B^c` resolve based plaquette holonomy, and let `U_c` be
the based vertex gauge action.  The endpoint partial isometry is `B^c U_c`.
Its imaginary Hermitian quadrature is

\[
K_c=\frac{B^cU_c-U_c^{-1}B^c}{2i}.
\]

Because `c` commutes with itself, `B^c` commutes with `U_c`.  Thus `K_c` is
supported on the four boundary edges and, on the `c`-flux fiber, is the
oriented current of the order-three gauge shift.

## Exact orbit structure and sector signatures

The `c`-flux fiber has dimension 216 and splits into 72 free `C3` orbits.
On every orbit,

\[
K_c=\frac{S-S^{-1}}{2i},
\]

where `S` is the three-cycle shift.  The three-point Fourier transform gives
the exact eigenvalues

\[
0,\quad \frac{\sqrt3}{2},\quad-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}.
\]

At the endpoint-sector level the normalized signatures on `(F,G,H)` are

\[
\left(0,\frac{\sqrt3}{4},-\frac{\sqrt3}{4}\right).
\]

The sign is the imaginary part of the conjugate centralizer characters
`1,omega,omega^2`.  A diagonal `B^c` phase cannot distinguish `G` from `H`;
the oriented gauge current is essential.

## Thirteen-gate compiler

One clean six-level plaquette ancilla first accumulates holonomy in four
edge--ancilla gates.  On the two base-vertex edges, the reversible coordinate
change

\[
(g_0,g_3)\longmapsto(g_0,g_0^{-1}g_3)
\]

isolates the common left-gauge coordinate.  Decompose `g0=c^k r`, with
`r` the even/odd coset representative, Fourier transform `k in Z3`, apply the
mode phases generated by `0,+sqrt(3)/2,-sqrt(3)/2` only when the holonomy
ancilla equals `c`, and reverse every step.

The serial inventory is `4+1+1+1+1+1+4=13` gates: holonomy compute,
relative-coordinate change, Fourier transform, conditional mode phase,
inverse Fourier transform, coordinate uncompute, and holonomy uncompute.
The ancilla returns clean.

## Assumptions and unresolved typing

The construction assumes a clean six-level ancilla, four edge--ancilla group
multiplication couplings, one reversible two-edge relative-coordinate gate,
a local `C3` Fourier gate within each parity coset, and an exact
ancilla-conditioned irrational phase rate.  Locality and algebraic synthesis
are proved; geometric scheduling, coupling strengths, phase calibration,
leakage during intermediate gates, and fault propagation are deferred to the
later audit moves.

## Verification and falsifiers

Run

```text
uv run --with sympy python research/kitaev/checkers/check_s3_gh_separator_compiler.py
```

The checker enumerates the 216-state flux fiber and all 36 base-edge pairs,
proves the 72-orbit decomposition and coordinate invariance, exactly
diagonalizes the orbit matrix, and derives the three endpoint signatures.
Nine aggregate gates are declared.  Saved output:
`research/kitaev/results/s3-gh-separator-compiler.json`.

Falsifiers are a nonfree `C3` orbit, a non-Hermitian `K_c`, failure of the
relative coordinate to remain invariant, incorrect Fourier eigenvalues,
equal `G/H` signatures, or absence of any required physical gate.
