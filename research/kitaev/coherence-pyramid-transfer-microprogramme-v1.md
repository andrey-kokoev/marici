# Coherence-pyramid transfer microprogramme v1

## Question

Which structures and certificates may be transported between coherence-pyramid realizations without silently promoting a finite identity, quotient presentation, or comparison diagram into a stronger claim?

## Claim boundary

This packet governs transfers proposed from the protected-sector locus. It does not alter Voevodsky-owned coherence-pyramid specifications, prove a partial equipment, or authorize a physical, radial, arithmetic, or spectral interpretation. Canonical integration into the coherence-pyramid programme requires owner review.

## Hard core

A transfer is admitted only through the tuple

\[
(S,T,f,I,C,E),
\]

where `S` and `T` are typed source and target objects, `f:S->T` is source-derived, `I` names the invariant claimed to survive, `C` is the required descent or coherence certificate, and `E` is reproducible evidence at an explicitly stated strength.

No equality of dimensions, matching formula, common label name, finite sample, commuting boundary, or successful presentation-level checker substitutes for this tuple.

## Certificate dependency poset

The falsification packet `coherence-pyramid-transfer-chain-falsification-v1.md` shows that descent and completion are logically independent: either can hold while the other fails. Transfer strength is therefore not a universal chain.

Each record declares a target claim and the subset of certificate predicates it requires. Current predicates include finite identity, quotient descent, faithfulness, coherent finite composition, completion or unbounded extension, and source-typed physical/readout realization. Dependencies are recorded as a directed acyclic graph. A record may claim only the demonstrated predicates and their proved consequences. Every promotion requires a separate proved arrow.

When several incomparable prerequisites fail, the residual is their complete minimal antichain. Audit order does not manufacture a unique first mathematical failure.

## Transfer classes

The registry may admit:

- labelled generators, supports, orientations, gradings, and declared equivalences;
- exact finite Green matrices, kernels, radicals, ranks, signatures, and positivity certificates;
- gauge-invariant quantities proved to descend;
- quotient coordinates proved well-defined, with faithfulness recorded separately;
- finite commuting-square certificates;
- exact comparison maps preserving all declared interface labels;
- compositions whose interfaces and certificate bundles agree;
- pullbacks and amalgamations over verified interface descriptors;
- individually certified finite-to-closed formulas;
- countermodels and no-go obstructions preserved by the comparison diagram.

## Prohibited promotions

The registry refuses:

- finite positivity as completed positivity;
- a commuting square as a coherence cell;
- spectral labels as radial or arithmetic meaning without a source map;
- equal dimensions as identification;
- a gauge presentation as a quotient form without descent;
- measured coordinates as faithful quotient coordinates;
- a synthetic bridge as a sourced realization;
- mathematical compatibility as stochastic or physical nondeterminism.

## Failure coproduct

Every rejected or incomplete transfer has one disposition:

- `undefined`: a required typed map or operation is absent;
- `representative_dependent`: the formula does not descend;
- `underdetermined`: admitted observations permit multiple inequivalent lifts;
- `inconsistent`: admitted routes yield unequal values;
- `unstable`: finite approximants do not determine the claimed completion;
- `counterexample`: a typed hostile fixture directly violates the claim;
- `authority_blocked`: the first required source object is not owner-authorized.

The record names the first failed condition and cheapest discriminating test. An authority blocker creates no waiting leaf.

## Positive heuristic

For each proposed transfer:

1. freeze source and target interface descriptors;
2. type the map before reducing matrices or choosing representatives;
3. state the invariant and target strength;
4. run descent, route-independence, and hostile tests appropriate to that strength;
5. record the exact residual;
6. admit only the surviving scope to the map registry.

## Negative heuristic

Do not repair a failed map by renaming a synthetic basis, fitting a quotient, adding an untyped coherence cell, changing completion topology after failure, or weakening the target after observing the residual.

## Executable frontier

Issue-tree nodes are reserved for bounded theorem work: closure of analytic certificate bundles, sourced associators/interchange, Beck--Chevalley invertibility, completion pseudofunctoriality, or a named sector comparison. Registry maintenance and owner requests are not executable research leaves.

## Versioning and reopening

Version 1 changes only when a new transfer class, strength arrow, failure type, or coherence obligation is justified by a bounded packet and test. A failed sector instance updates the registry but does not revise the hard core unless it falsifies the admission rule itself.

## Disposition

This is a structured protected-sector proposal for owner review. Its immediate use is to prevent unsupported cross-sector promotion while preserving exact finite identities and transferable obstructions at their demonstrated strength.
