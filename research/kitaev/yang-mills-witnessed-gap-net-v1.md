## Governing conjecture and rivals

The conjectured mechanism is quantitative: every non-vacuum physical state has a faithfully detectable excitation, and producing that detected excitation costs a uniformly positive physical energy.

For a nonnegative self-adjoint Hamiltonian H, vacuum-sector projection P and physical quadratic form q, seek a source-defined detector D and constants c, epsilon greater than zero such that

\[
\|D\psi\|^2\ge c\|(I-P)\psi\|^2,
\qquad q(\psi)\ge\epsilon\|D\psi\|^2.
\]

They imply the form bound with Delta=epsilon c. Both inequalities quantify over the relevant physical form domain. Closure, common cores, vacuum policy and the physical energy scale must be supplied. Choosing D from an already presumed spectral gap is circular.

The rivals are concrete: a blind observable misses low modes; finite gaps collapse; units change; positive local costs cancel on collective configurations; witness-sensitive distinctions have no energy meaning; or the limit is trivial rather than the required interacting theory.
