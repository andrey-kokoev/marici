# RH SCC source-positivity microprogram

## Objective

Reduce the Riemann Hypothesis to the earliest noncircular constructor required to transport prime-power source data into a positive, completion-stable comparison that detects every reciprocal off-seam disagreement direction.

## Scope

- The completed Riemann \(\Xi\)-function with frozen normalization.
- A declared Mellin/Schwartz test class.
- Prime-power, archimedean, endpoint, reciprocal, and seam contributions.
- The exact Hermitian Weil form as the terminal arithmetic-analytic comparison.
- No positivity imported from a zero divisor already assumed to lie on the critical line.

## Twelve moves

### M1 — Freeze the completed source packet

Record \(\Xi\), functional equation, Mellin convention, test space, Fourier convention, prime-power weights, gamma term, endpoints, and coefficient lenses.

### M2 — Derive the explicit-formula incidence

Construct the prime-power and archimedean currents on the same authorized test packet. Scalar equality after separate completion is insufficient.

### M3 — Preserve prime labels through completion

Require uniformly bounded coordinate truncations and convergence in each declared rigged rung. Reject label smearing introduced by sewing or wall subtraction.

### M4 — Construct the exact Weil form

Derive the Hermitian form

\[
Q(f,g)=
\sum_\rho
\widehat f(\rho)
\overline{\widehat g(1-\overline\rho)}
\]

through the explicit formula, including every endpoint and archimedean term.

### M5 — Derive reciprocal-pair polarization

For an off-seam pair \(\rho,\rho^\vee\), retain the evaluation block

\[
\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its coherent and disagreement eigenlines have signs \(+1\) and \(-1\). Seam restriction must occur later.

### M6 — Freeze the Weil positivity criterion

State precisely the authorized test-class theorem relating \(Q\ge0\) to RH. Keep criterion equivalence separate from construction of a positive source factorization.

### M7 — Construct the undecomposed Green packet

Seek a source-derived polarized Green form before projecting into prime, seam, or scalar channels. Require Poisson/Fourier covariance and radical control.

### M8 — Prove constructor coherence

Verify atomic incidence, two-atom mixed terms, associator and pentagon cells, reciprocal sewing, and source authority before forming the completed identity.

### M9 — Prove continuity through the rigged completion

For every required current or quadratic form, prove radical descent and one cutoff-independent domination bound in a fixed authorized constructor family.

### M10 — Prove completion-stable coercivity

Control the independent sector, seam, endpoint, mixed-cancellation, and auxiliary margins without fitting a positive form from the desired conclusion.

### M11 — Localize one hypothetical negative direction

For one hypothetical off-seam reciprocal pair, construct one authorized Mellin probe whose fixed negative disagreement contribution dominates the entire remaining divisor tail. Uniform interpolation of all packets is not required.

### M12 — Issue the first-failure certificate

Return exactly one:

1. missing source incidence;
2. constructor-coherence failure;
3. noncontinuous Green/current lift;
4. completion margin collapse;
5. localization/tail failure;
6. a noncircular positive Weil form excluding all off-seam pairs.
