# RH interaction-net to Nima mathematics audit

## Claim boundary

This packet audits Aspect's
`theta-rh-interaction-net-state.v1.json` against the current Nima research
packets.  It does not certify RH, promote a formal SCC slot, or treat a scalar
shadow as an operator constructor.

The audit uses four statuses:

- **inhabited**: a source-derived formula supplies the declared object or map
  on its stated domain;
- **qualified**: a formula exists, but an authority, topology, or
  representation condition remains explicit;
- **partial**: only a strict factor or shadow of the SCC cell exists;
- **open**: no source-derived inhabitant is currently available.

## Executive result

Aspect's dependency topology is broadly consistent with Nima's mathematics,
but its cells are not all at the same mathematical resolution.

1. The seven cells marked `constructed` have formulas, but the
   Fourier--Bohr port retains an explicit invariant-mean authority
   qualification in its cited Nima packet.
2. The formal `type_fiber_adams_composition` cell is too coarse for failure
   localization.  Nima has resolved it into a partially inhabited chain from
   scale history through coefficient-space wall removal, mixed Green pairing,
   typed pushforward, and oriented graph relation.
3. The five-margin theorem supplies the analytic shape of
   `completion_stable_five_margin_coercivity`, but none of the five required
   uniform source estimates is yet established for the complete RH system.
4. The grade-six diamond supplies a contractible geometric 2-cell, not a typed
   Adams filler.  The four type-fiber edge maps remain absent.
5. Endpoint, archimedean, reciprocal, determinant, spectral-identification,
   and terminal cells remain open.  Local traces or scalar determinants do not
   inhabit them.

## Required refinement of the Adams box

The current SCC cell `type_fiber_adams_composition` should expose the following
internal stages for audit and first-failure localization.  These may remain a
subgraph inside one public SCC cell if SCC supports nested witnesses.

```text
coefficient five-cell wall split
        -> source scale path W_t
        -> closed local history H_p
        -> typed endpoint traces at log p and 2 log p
        -> independent incidence lifts L_P,p and L_Q,p
        -> relative mixed Green/Stokes form b_alpha,p
        -> radical descent and contractive block
        -> chart-covariant intact block pushforward
        -> odd-port faithfulness / wall-support selection
        -> boundary-oriented defect operator C_2;p,1
        -> Adams graph relation Gamma_2;p,1
        -> four-edge grade-six typed diamond
```

Current occupancy of that chain:

- the coefficient wall, scale path, local history, local endpoint traces, and
  incidence coefficients are inhabited;
- the primitive global history is Laplace/distributionally rigged, whereas the
  square history is Hilbert/tempered;
- the mixed Green/Stokes form coupling the exact incidence lifts remains open;
- therefore radical descent, typed pushforward, oriented defect operator, graph
  relation, and grade-six typed filler remain downstream;
- the geometric grade-six diamond has \(H_1=0\) and trivial geometric
  \(\pi_1\), so typed holonomy is undefined rather than nontrivial.

## Representation and residue typing

The seam residue must be split before the multiplication representation.

In the five-cell coefficient space, the wall is one-dimensional:

\[
R_{\mathrm{coeff}}
=
|e_{\mathrm{wall}}\rangle\langle e_{\mathrm{wall}}|.
\]

The linear representation sends the wall vector to an infinite-rank operator,

\[
\pi(e_{\mathrm{wall}})=-I,
\]

while the quadratic lift sends its Gram projector to

\[
\Gamma_\pi(R_{\mathrm{coeff}})=I.
\]

Consequently:

- applying \(\pi\) directly to the coefficient projector is ill typed;
- subtracting \(I/(2\sigma)\) after representation is authorized only as the
  image of a prior coefficient-space wall decomposition;
- removing the identity residue does not make a nonzero multiplication
  remainder compact on \(L^2(\mathbb R)\);
- determinant-class promotion still needs a smoothing, compression, relative
  ideal, or pre-representation determinant-line construction.

## Formula obligations for every future SCC cell

Every promoted RH cell should carry the following record:

```text
cell_id
cell_kind
source_locator
authority_class
domain
codomain
coefficient_lens
formula_or_relation
finite_status
completion_topology
uniform_bound
kernel_or_radical
first_missing_law
minimal_falsifier
```

For a box, `formula_or_relation` defines its carrier and topology.  For an edge,
it defines an actual map, form, or closable relation.  For a 2-cell, it defines
an equality, authorized intertwiner, or typed residual.  For a completion cell,
it includes a cutoff-independent bound.  For an observer, it includes its
kernel on the reachable module.  For a terminal claim, it includes the exact
spectral-identification theorem.

## Verdict

The Aspect net is a sound dependency skeleton, but it is not yet a formula-complete
interaction net.  Its most important coarse edge, type-fiber Adams composition,
contains multiple mathematically independent constructors and failure modes.
The audit makes those stages visible without promoting any of them.  The first
live source formula remains the relative Green/Stokes pairing between the frozen
primitive and square incidence lifts on the two-chart comoving scale cell.
