# Parallel invertible fillers form a torsor and carry higher logical information

Owner: marici.Kitaev

## Question

When two boundary paths admit several invertible fillers, what algebraic object
classifies their differences, and what exactly does the next coherence rung do
to that object?

### The higher logical sector

If no authorized 3-cell compares (alpha) and (eta), their relative
automorphism is task-visible higher information. The boundary paths alone do
not determine which filler occurred.

This is the direct higher analogue of a topological logical sector:

- ordinary loop holonomy distinguishes parallel 1-paths;
- filler holonomy distinguishes parallel 2-paths with the same boundary;
- a still higher cell is required to compare those filler choices.

The logical object has moved up one categorical dimension without changing the
underlying pattern.

### What a 3-cell actually does

A 3-cell

between them. As before, distinguish:

- no 3-cell;

- invertible 3-equivalence;
- task-localized contraction of the 3-difference.

Consequently the next rung can expose new composition capabilities rather than
merely close a residue. It may transport, control, or measure filler frames.

### Smallest finite witness

Consider the strict 2-group with:

- one object;
- one 1-morphism (I);
- two 2-automorphisms (1,epsilon:IRightarrow I);
- relation (epsilon^2=1);
- no nonidentity 3-cell.

The two fillers (1) and (epsilon) have identical boundary. Their difference
is a (C_2) higher logical bit. A coefficient lens using the nontrivial
character of (C_2) distinguishes them, while the trivial scalar lens does
not.

Adjoining a 3-cell that contracts (epsilon) changes the constructor theory.
It does not reveal that the bit was absent before; it explicitly removes it in
the enlarged theory.

This is the smallest counterexample to any claim that fixed boundary data
uniquely determine a filler.

### Composition produces cocycle data

Suppose fillers are chosen on composable faces. Their relative frames must
compose consistently. Changing local filler references acts like a gauge
transformation. Products of relative automorphisms around a closed surface give
a higher holonomy or cocycle.

In an abelian coefficient group this yields familiar cohomological classes. In
a nonabelian or weak setting, order and associators must be retained. Scalar
summation sees only an abelian shadow.

Therefore the filler torsor is not isolated local ambiguity. Networks of
fillers naturally generate the next coherence law.

### Noninvertible fillers are not torsors

The theorem applies only to invertible 2-cells. Directed or lossy fillers form
a hom-category or module-like correspondence, not a torsor. Their differences
cannot be obtained by group division.

This supplies an immediate typing falsifier: any compiler that subtracts or
divides two noninvertible fillers as though they belonged to a group has
invented unavailable structure.

### Sector fit

The earlier (B)-sheet ambiguity had an invertible torsor action whose frame
was fixed by the tensor unit. The present theorem shows that the same
architecture recurs for higher comparison cells.

The (D(S_3)) pair-channel gives a 1-morphism-level bridge. If two distinct
source compilers realize the same pair incidence but differ by an endpoint
automorphism or seam action, their comparison belongs to this filler torsor.
Identical scalar endpoint algebra would not select between them.

For toric-code deformations, two repair homotopies with the same boundary can
differ by a closed logical surface operator. Again the boundary syndrome does
not determine the filler class.

## Disposition

Replace any presumed unique coherence filler by the typed packet:

- boundary paths (P,Q);
- filler space and invertibility status;
- left and right automorphism actions;
- source-supplied reference, if any;
- authorized 3-cells between fillers;
- surviving quotient torsor;
- coefficient lens and contextual readout;
- execution and completion authority.

The first higher falsifier is a pair (alpha,eta) with identical boundaries
and nontrivial relative automorphism that survives every admitted 3-cell.

The next finite construction should couple two or three (C_2) filler torsors.
For two, test whether the relative bits compose additively or share a
common-mode ambiguity. For three, test the surface cocycle and whether a
Pfaffian-like scalar is merely an abelian shadow of an ordered higher
holonomy.
