# Endpoint-algebra span versus executable control in `D(S_3)`

Owner: `marici.Kitaev`

## Bounded question

Does the finite `D(S_3)` endpoint algebra contain the block-selective matrix
directions required by the random-unitary center compilation?

## Exact image theorem

Use the 36 endpoint-algebra basis elements `(g,x)` and the eight frozen
induced simple representations.  On each simple block `V_a`, their exact
matrix-image ranks are

\[
(1,1,4,9,9,4,4,4)=(d_a^2).
\]

On the direct sum of all simple modules, the joint image rank is 36.  This
equals

\[
\dim\bigoplus_a\operatorname{End}(V_a)=\sum_a d_a^2=36.
\]

Therefore the represented endpoint algebra is exactly the full
block-diagonal matrix algebra.  In particular, all sector projectors,
block-selective matrix units, and the Weyl unitaries used in the preceding
compilation lie in its algebraic span.

## Hostile restriction

The gauge-action elements sum over the flux projector label.  Their joint
image has rank only six.  Thus the claim that gauge actions alone span the
required controls fails with rank deficit

\[
36-6=30.
\]

Flux-resolved endpoint access is essential at the algebraic level.

## The surviving typing gap

An element lying in the complex linear span of represented endpoint
operators does not imply that the source Hamiltonian can implement its
unitary exponential, address it independently, choose its coefficient, or
randomize it.  The span theorem establishes algebraic availability only.
Executable control requires a source-derived control Lie algebra or explicit
ribbon-pulse protocol with locality, energy, and leakage bounds.

This also separates two earlier algebras.  The oriented fixed-open-ribbon
composition packet establishes its local multiplication convention; the
present endpoint `D(S_3)` action is the algebra acting on irreducible endpoint
modules.  Equality of their raw 36-element counts would not by itself identify
them.

## Carrier versus coefficient lens

Carrier geometry supplies endpoint support, ribbon addressability, and the
distinction between flux-resolved and flux-summed actions.  The quantum-double
coefficient lens supplies the `(g,x)` action, induced simple modules, and
matrix ranks.  The promotion from span to control belongs to neither without
a source dynamics map.

## Falsifiers and verification

The theorem fails if any simple image rank is below `d_a^2`, if the joint rank
is below 36, or if gauge-only rank reaches 36.  All ranks are exact over the
algebraic numbers generated by `sqrt(3)` and the cubic root of unity.
