# Equivariant and Nondisturbing Actuator Lifts Have an Affine Obstruction

Let the symmetry-fixed actuator lifts be

\[
\mathcal T^G=B_G+V^G,
\]

where (V^G) is the invariant residual-kernel shear space. Let protected
nondisturbance require

\[
LBH=0.
\]

Define the linear map

\[
\Phi:V^G\to\operatorname{Hom}(X,W),
\qquad
\Phi(N)=LNH.
\]

An equivariant nondisturbing lift exists exactly when

\[
-LB_GH\in\operatorname{im}\Phi.
\]

When it exists, the compatible lifts form a torsor over (\ker\Phi). Hence the
intersection is:

- empty if the affine defect is outside (\operatorname{im}\Phi);
- unique if the defect is in the image and (\ker\Phi=0);
- a residual torsor if (\ker\Phi\ne0).

## Minimal incompatibility

For the one-dimensional actuator torsor (B_t), nondisturbance requires
(t=0). The affine (C_2) symmetry

\[
t\longmapsto1-t
\]

has the unique fixed lift (t=1/2). Here (V^G=0), so no invariant shear is
available to cancel the logical defect. Both conditions are individually
consistent and jointly incompatible.

By contrast, reflection (t\mapsto-t) fixes (t=0), so symmetry and
nondisturbance agree. A trivial symmetry leaves (V^G=V), and nondisturbance
then selects (t=0) from the remaining torsor.

This supplies a finite incompatibility certificate rather than permission to
drop either law. If the actual source symmetry selects a disturbing actuator,
the model must revise the claimed symmetry action, protected record, or
actuator family from upstream evidence.

## Completion gate

Across cutoffs, solvability is insufficient unless right inverses for
(\Phi_N) on the affine defects are uniformly bounded. Otherwise compatible
lifts exist at every finite stage but their corrective invariant shears escape
in norm. This is the same finite-versus-uniform distinction as the seam decoder.

## Falsifiers

- The symmetry-fixed origin violates nondisturbance and (V^G=0).
- Compatibility is restored by altering the symmetry after seeing the defect.
- Finite solutions require invariant shears with diverging norm.
- A protected probe is discarded merely because it conflicts with symmetry.
- Multiple compatible lifts remain but are reported as canonical.
