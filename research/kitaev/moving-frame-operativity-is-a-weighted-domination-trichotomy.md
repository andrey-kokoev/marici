# Moving-Frame Operativity Is a Weighted Domination Trichotomy

Let (Q_N\succeq0) be the authorized analytic Gram form, (R_N) the
real-structure transport residual, and (H_N) an independently authorized
seam or discrete row. On a moving state (x_N), write

\[
q_N=x_N^*Q_Nx_N,
\quad r_N=\|R_Nx_N\|,
\quad h_N=\|H_Nx_N\|.
\]

The completion audit has three typed outcomes.

1. **Harmless quotient:** every (Q)-null sequence also has
   (r_N\to0) and (h_N\to0). The drifting frame is nonoperative.
2. **Frame non-descent:** (q_N\to0) but (r_N\not\to0). No uniform bound
   (R_N^*R_N\le C^2Q_N) exists, so the conjugation residual does not extend
   through the analytic completion.
3. **Hybrid-port obligation:** (q_N\to0), the analytic frame residual is
   harmless or quotiented, but (h_N\not\to0). The analytic quotient erases an
   authorized distinction; the seam/discrete port must be retained in an
   extended Gram form.

For a one-coordinate witness these conditions are exact:

\[
C_{R,N}^2=\frac{r_N^2}{q_N},
\qquad
C_{H,N}^2=\frac{h_N^2}{q_N}.
\]

Finite kernel inclusion is insufficient when either ratio diverges. Adding
the seam form

\[
Q_N^{\mathrm{full}}=Q_N+H_N^*H_N
\]

repairs seam continuity with constant at most one, but it repairs the frame
residual only if (R_N^*R_N\) is uniformly dominated by the same extended
form. A seam row cannot be added merely because it cures the hostile; it must
already belong to the frozen source constructor family.

The smallest exact family takes (q_N=N^{-2}). With (r_N=2), frame
domination grows as (4N^2). With (h_N=1), seam domination grows as (N^2).
After adjoining the authorized seam square, seam domination becomes uniform,
while the frame ratio becomes

\[
\frac{4}{1+N^{-2}}<4.
\]

Thus one typed port can repair both only when it actually observes the same
moving direction. An orthogonal seam row leaves the frame obstruction intact.

## Source-facing decision

Grothendieck's completion must supply (Q_N,R_N,H_N). The compiler then asks:

- does (R_N) descend through the analytic Gram family;
- if not, is the witness detected by an already authorized seam/current row;
- does the fixed extended family uniformly dominate both residual and row;
- or is the proposed repair an unauthorized constructor?

This decides operativity without assuming that every analytic null sequence is
physically null.
