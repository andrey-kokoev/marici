# The tiny modular tail is a forward-coherence residue, not yet the final mate

Owner: marici.Kitaev

## Question

Is Grothendieck's exponentially tiny theta tail the coherence residue left by
an already implemented RH ubercoherencer?

## Claim boundary

It is strongly evidenced as a residue of the implemented forward modular
coherencer, but not of a completed ubercoherencer.

The complete modular source obeys the exact seam identity

\[
V'''(0)=0.
\]

A finite two-label truncation does not obey that identity. The first omitted
tail scale is of order \(e^{-8\pi}\), while the first outward two-label repair
grows as \(u e^{-3\pi}\). Their balance creates the boundary-layer scale

\[
u_{\rm mod}\asymp e^{-5\pi}.
\]

Thus the omitted tail is small in norm but decisive in type: at the seam it
restores an exact modular relation that the finite dominant packet violates.
Away from the boundary layer, the two-label repair dominates and the tail can
be treated as an analytic error envelope.

This is the characteristic signature of a coherence residue. Its importance
is not proportional to its absolute size because the leading finite
constructor vanishes in the coherence-sensitive direction. The relevant
relative condition number therefore becomes singular at the seam.

However the repository's current 3+2+1 audit says:

    forward witness   substantially implemented
    backward witness  only partially implemented
    mate cell         missing

Therefore the tail cannot yet be called the residue left by the full
ubercoherencer. It is the cutoff defect of the forward global
Tate--Poisson/modular construction. It is a candidate input to the final mate
cell.

Let \(W_\infty\) denote the completed modular witness and \(W_X\) its finite
cutoff presentation. The typed residue is

\[
\Delta_X
=
W_\infty|_X-W_X.
\]

The tiny tail is a genuine coherence residue if it satisfies all three tests:

1. deleting it breaks a naturality or seam identity already forced by
   \(W_\infty\);
2. restoring it repairs that identity without adding a new independent source
   row;
3. residues compose under cutoff refinement by the corresponding cocycle law.

It is instead new source information if its remainder is nonzero modulo the
ideal generated by the completed forward witness and independently shrinks
the hostile null kernel.

## Disposition

Treat the tiny tail as a forward-coherence residue with high confidence, not
as negligible noise and not yet as the final RH defect.

The next audit should compute its image under the missing backward
boundary-defect map. There are two outcomes:

- it maps exactly to the Green/Ward mate discrepancy and closes the final cell;
- it lies in the existing forward syzygy and cannot shrink the scalar-null
  kernel.

The first would identify the tail as the boundary value carried by the final
coherencer. The second would show that modular seam coherence is already
satisfied but supplies no new RH constraint.

The finite falsifier is an exact hostile scalar-null state on which the
completed tail residue vanishes while the Green/Ward discrepancy remains
nonzero.
