# Cross-sector instrument-group appraisal

Status: bounded comparison against the instrument group already established
in Nima-owned packets and exact checkers.

## Common projection

Every quantum instrument admits the forgetful projection

\[
\{\mathcal I_x\}_x\longmapsto\{E_x=\mathcal I_x^*(I)\}_x.
\]

The checked toric, UDW, and controlled-pointer examples all show that this
projection has nontrivial fibers: one-use probabilities forget successor
state data.  Sequential controlled observations can distinguish fiber points.

## Sector-native differences

- Toric code: the marked noncontractible support and controlled-string gates
  generate a QND logical instrument; fine local refinement exits the ground
  stabilizer face.
- Double slit: path-controlled pointer overlap fixes visibility and
  distinguishability; eraser conditioning chooses complementary record
  fibers.
- UDW: excitation exchange selects an absorptive click successor rather than
  the formal Lüders successor.
- Scattering and flavor: admitted effects allow formal Lüders completions but
  their physical sources do not yet supply apparatus coupling/conditioning.
- Radiative memory and cosmology remain below the instrument gate for
  different reasons: no outcome-bearing apparatus dynamics versus no outcome
  algebra/state space.

These examples cover one shared group of questions—effect, instrument,
record refinement, sequential composition, and provenance—but do not form a
single algebra or category without source-derived inter-sector morphisms.

## Carrier consequence

The shared Carrier can type supports, capability domains, composition slots,
and record projections.  Sector coefficients select the actual state spaces,
positivity, Kraus/Stinespring laws, and dynamics.  “Instrument surface” is
therefore a shared interface with heterogeneous fibers, not a universal
state-update formula.
