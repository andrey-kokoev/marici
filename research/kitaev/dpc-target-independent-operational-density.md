# DPC: Target-independent operational density

**Owner:** marici.Kitaev  
**Status:** Deutsch–Popperian conjecture  
**Question:** When does a finite probe family genuinely explain constructor equivalence?

## 1. Setting

Let \(\mathcal K\) be a physically represented, resource-enriched constructor category.

Let \(\mathcal G\) be a fixed finite or finitely presented basis of source-authorized preparations, transformations, and observations.

Let \(\langle\mathcal G\rangle\) be its closure under the admitted context grammar.

Let

\[
R:\langle\mathcal G\rangle\to\mathcal I
\]

be physical realization into instruments, and let

\[
O:\mathcal I\to\mathcal D
\]

be the retained outcome interface.

## 2. Deutsch–Popperian conjecture

**DPC — Target-independent operational density.**

A bounded probe basis \(\mathcal G\) supplies a genuine explanation of constructor equivalence on a candidate class \(\mathcal X\) if and only if all of the following hold:

1. **Independent generation:** \(\mathcal G\) is derived from source primitives before the unresolved candidate pair is known.
2. **Context representation:** every admitted distinction-making context on \(\mathcal X\) is equivalent, for the declared task, to a context generated from \(\mathcal G\).
3. **Operational realization:** the required generated contexts are executable with source-authorized instruments.
4. **Readout faithfulness:** retained outcomes preserve every distinction required by the claimed constructor equivalence.
5. **Compositional reconstruction:** the realized profiles reconstruct substitution, tensoring, interfaces, resource grades, faults, authority, catalysts, reset, and reuse included in the claim.
6. **Uniformity:** representation and reconstruction have cutoff-independent resource and stability bounds.
7. **Completion stability:** no new distinction appears only in the completed context or process class.

Under these conditions,

\[
OR\bigl(N_{\mathcal G}(f)\bigr)
=
OR\bigl(N_{\mathcal G}(g)\bigr)
\]

implies that \(f\) and \(g\) are constructor-equivalent in the declared physical scope.

If any condition fails, agreement on the finite probe packet is confirmation only. It is compatible with an admissible hostile extension in which the same recorded packet comes from inequivalent constructors.

## 3. Explanatory content

The probe family is not complete because many tests happened to agree.

It is complete because every allowed way of exposing a difference factors through the same independently generated probe grammar, and the interface preserves that factorization.

The theorem explains both:

- why no additional probe can reveal new information;
- exactly which source law must change before a new distinction becomes possible.

This is hard to vary because weakening any factorization, realization, readout, or uniformity law predicts a corresponding hostile.

## 4. Universal hostile

Given a proposed finite basis \(\mathcal G\), seek constructors \(f\ne g\) such that

\[
OR(C[f])=OR(C[g])
\]

for every \(C\in\langle\mathcal G\rangle\), while some admitted or completion-generated context \(C_*\) satisfies

\[
OR(C_*[f])\ne OR(C_*[g]).
\]

The first location of \(C_*\) classifies the defect:

- outside generated syntax: generation failure;
- abstractly present but nonexecutable: realization failure;
- instrumentally distinct but outcome-equal: readout failure;
- visible only after composition: reconstruction failure;
- visible only at growing cost: uniformity failure;
- visible only in the limit: completion failure.

## 5. Fitted-probe prohibition

A probe constructed from knowledge of the desired candidate label cannot establish explanatory completeness.

Otherwise every finite problem can be made trivially faithful by adjoining one answer-coded test.

The basis must be invariant under hostile relabelling and independently justified by source occurrence.

A target-specific probe may be a useful diagnostic, but it does not belong in the completeness theorem until independently regenerated from the frozen source grammar.

## 6. Relation to enriched Yoneda

Full enriched Yoneda proves internal faithfulness when all representable contexts are available.

The DPC asks when a bounded physical basis can replace those representables.

Context representation is the density theorem.

Operational realization and readout faithfulness are the compiler arrows absent from abstract Yoneda.

Uniformity and completion stability prevent cutoffwise density from being promoted to a completed theorem.

## 7. Proper explanation versus confirmation

### Confirmation

- a finite response matrix has full rank;
- all tested candidates separate;
- numerical conditioning is acceptable at current cutoff;
- no tested hostile survives.

### Explanation

- source theory generates the probes;
- a representation theorem reduces all admitted contexts to them;
- realization and readout preserve distinctions;
- reconstruction respects composition;
- bounds survive scaling and completion;
- the theorem predicts the first new capability when an assumption changes.

Full rank is evidence for separation. It is not evidence for contextual exhaustiveness.

## 8. Cross-sector predictions

### Toric code

Syndrome plus two loop probes explains the four-class packet only after the candidate class is frozen to homology modulo local repair. It does not establish density for every code instrument.

### D(S3)

Two flux-resolved ports explain algebraic generation of the endpoint algebra. Constructor equivalence additionally requires executable realization of the generated block operations.

### Finite-state behavior

The alphabet and output ports are independently given; the state bound proves every longer distinguishing word reduces to the finite product-state audit. This is a positive DPC instance.

### Score towers

Möbius inversion establishes reconstruction on the labelled coefficient packet. Instrument accessibility and physical momentum identifications remain independent density gates.

## 9. Direct falsifiers of the DPC

The conjecture is false if:

1. a probe family lacking one of the seven conditions nevertheless admits a theorem excluding every hostile extension in the declared physical class;
2. all seven conditions hold but an inequivalent constructor has the same realized outcome profile;
3. target-dependent fitted probes yield a genuinely source-invariant completeness theorem without independent regeneration;
4. completion introduces no new distinctions despite failure of every available uniformity or representation condition, for a principled reason not captured above;
5. constructor composition is reconstructible from mere object separation without an additional structural theorem.
