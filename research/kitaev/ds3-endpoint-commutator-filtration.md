# Commutator filtration of the \(D(S_3)\) endpoint algebra

## Question

For the full complex endpoint algebra \(A=D(S_3)\), what portion is visible to blockwise scalar traces, what portion is generated by commutators, and does nested commutation create further linear directions?

## Claim boundary

Irreducible \(D(S_3)\)-modules are indexed by a conjugacy class and an irreducible representation of its centralizer. Their dimensions are

\[
1,1,2,3,3,2,2,2.
\]

Therefore the semisimple algebra decomposes as

\[
A
\cong
\mathbf C
\oplus\mathbf C
\oplus M_2(\mathbf C)^{\oplus4}
\oplus M_3(\mathbf C)^{\oplus2}.
\]

The dimension check is

\[
1+1+4\cdot4+2\cdot9=36.
\]

The center has one scalar coordinate per simple block:

\[
\dim Z(A)=8.
\]

Treat \(A\) as a Lie algebra under \([x,y]=xy-yx\). For a matrix block,

\[
[M_n(\mathbf C),M_n(\mathbf C)]
=
\mathfrak{sl}_n(\mathbf C).
\]

Hence

\[
[A,A]
\cong
0\oplus0
\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbf C)^{\oplus4}
\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_3(\mathbf C)^{\oplus2},
\]

with dimension

\[
4(2^2-1)+2(3^2-1)=12+16=28.
\]

Thus the eight block traces and the 28 traceless block directions account for all 36 dimensions. More precisely, the block-trace map

\[
\tau:A\longrightarrow\mathbf C^8
\]

has kernel \([A,A]\). The quotient \(A/[A,A]\) is an eight-dimensional vector-space trace quotient. Since \([A,A]\) is a Lie subspace rather than generally an associative ideal, this statement must not be misreported as an associative algebra quotient.

For \(n\ge2\), \(\mathfrak{sl}_n(\mathbf C)\) is perfect:

\[
[\mathfrak{sl}_n,\mathfrak{sl}_n]
=
\mathfrak{sl}_n.
\]

Consequently,

\[
[[A,A],[A,A]]=[A,A].
\]

Nested commutators remain nonzero but generate no new linear subspace beyond the first 28-dimensional commutator layer.

This refines the earlier hierarchy:

- central or block-trace readout: eight scalar coordinates;
- ordered commutator residue: 28 traceless coordinates;
- full endpoint algebra: \(8+28=36\) dimensions.

The result is algebraic. It does not show that all 36 directions are executable controls or that eight particular physical measurements implement the abstract block-trace map.

## Disposition

The \(D(S_3)\) ordered hierarchy has a different closure pattern from the toric Pauli group:

- toric group commutators are central and nested group commutators vanish;
- \(D(S_3)\) algebra commutators are noncentral and nested commutators persist;
- nevertheless, the linear commutator span stabilizes after the first layer at dimension 28.

Thus “coherence depth” requires two coordinates:

1. whether nested commutators vanish;
2. whether nested commutators enlarge the linear span.

The first falsifier is one of:

1. the simple-block dimensions do not square-sum to 36;
2. central dimension is inferred from algebra dimension without counting blocks;
3. scalar block traces are claimed to recover traceless directions;
4. \([A,A]\) is treated as an associative ideal;
5. persistence of nested commutators is confused with growth of their span;
6. algebraic span is promoted to physical executability.

The next finite port audit should decompose the previously identified transposition and three-cycle port generators into the eight trace coordinates and 28 commutator coordinates, then determine at what word length their spans reach each component.
