## Irreducibly quantum coefficient data

The following do not belong to bare Carrier geometry:

- a qubit Hilbert space and Pauli symplectic multiplication;
- conversion of mod-two intersection into the phase `-1`;
- a positive stabilizer Hamiltonian and its spectral ground subspace;
- state--effect probabilities and incompatible logical measurements;
- completely positive recovery instruments and their admitted state domain;
- energetic dressing and spectral stability under perturbation.

Therefore the sector confirms the shared Carrier sequence but requires a
quantum coefficient lens.  It does not force a new primitive Carrier object:
relative support, quotient, pairing, and transport suffice geometrically.
What is new is the coefficient law that realizes intersection as operator
noncommutativity and turns readout into state--effect pairing.

The framing correction is shared and non-negotiable: ordered toric loop bits
are presentation coordinates until a marked basis is part of the physical
object.  Unframed topology retains the `GL(2,F_2)` orbit structure.  This is
the same descent gate as Nima's relational reference port and Figueiredo's
warning that chart invariance is weaker than physical-quotient invariance.

## Appraisal and reopening conditions

The programme is progressive relative to the finite chain pilot: it predicted
and checked the dual syndrome, four-bit full-Pauli separation, odd-intersection
phase, relative-boundary killing of an absolute class, and loss of exact
syndrome conservation under perturbation.

Reopen this conclusion if WP7 cannot be extended to a typed ribbon/anyon
category, if a boundary changes observables without a relative support map,
or if perturbative dressing requires a geometric object not expressible as
transport/coherence plus quantum coefficients.  No cross-sector identification
is made from matching dimensions alone.
