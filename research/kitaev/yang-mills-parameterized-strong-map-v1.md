# R4: a jointly Borel strong map for every initial law

Owner: `marici.Kitaev`. Post-audit iteration2/10. Selected issue: `issue-tree:f57ade80940345418b04f880:parameterized-strong-map:v3:selected`.

## DPC and disposition

**Problem.** Strong existence for each fixed initial law does not on its own exhibit a single jointly Borel map F(x,W) valid for every initial law. Full-path Borel measurability does not on its own imply adaptedness.

**Conjecture.** The all-start-point Feller martingale kernel and bounded smooth link coframes admit a Borel marked-noise lift via one dyadic approximation scheme. A parameterized disintegration using the common Wiener marginal works for EVERY x. Compatible conditional copies and pathwise uniqueness make the conditional law Dirac; separate past kernels then give adaptedness for arbitrary independent initial preparations.

**Rivals.** Use a single preparation measure's almost-everywhere disintegration for all x; silently choose x-dependent subsequences and lose joint measurability; discard the noise marking; infer causality from a Dirac conditional law; require a universal exceptional Wiener set; use conditional independence without showing the driver remains Brownian in the joint filtration.

**Risky consequence.** One Borel map F:X times W -> S, with Q_x the law of (F(x,W),W) for every deterministic x, yielding adapted strong solutions for every initial probability law independent of W. Exceptional sets may depend on x or on the preparation law. No perfect flow or everywhere pathwise causal representative is asserted.

**Strong falsifiers.** At the actual free-SU2 source, changing a strong map only on one Haar-null initial-link row leaves the Haar-prepared law unchanged but freezes a deterministic identity start; its required tangent bracket has defect1 at kappa=1/2. Finite anticipative kernels are Dirac and Borel yet have future/past compatibility defect1. A non-Dirac conditional kernel gives independent copies off-diagonal mass1/2. These guards distinguish the missing hypotheses; they are not counterexamples to the theorem below.

**Disposition.** A conditional parameterized construction is given below, including the source noise kernel and the every-x quantifier. Upstream source well-posedness, R1-R3 estimates and standard stochastic/measure-theoretic analytic inputs remain explicit and unreviewed independently. The two declared post-audit proof-obligation cells are supplied at written conditional scope; independent review and physical/continuum/root gates remain open, not silently discharged.

## 1. Spaces, assumptions and exact statement

X=SU(2)^E is the countable compact spatial-link product. Put S=C([0,infinity),X) with compact-open product-path topology, and W=product_(i in E) C_0([0,infinity),R3). Both are Polish. The input Wiener law gamma is the countable product of standard three-dimensional Wiener laws; there is no ell2-valued Brownian motion.

Assume the upstream fixed-lattice construction supplies:

(A1) For every x, a unique continuous state-path law P_x starting at x, whose cylinder martingale generator is the same all-start-point Feller generator L_U. Its pointwise drift components are continuous and bounded uniformly in x and link index. Its coordinate martingale brackets are the declared marked diffusion covariances.

(A2) In radius-two quaternion coordinates p_i in S3_2 subset R4, the invariant field matrix V(p) is the4-by3 matrix of the fixed orthonormal frame. Thus V^T V=I3 and VV^T=I4-pp^T/4. The embedded Ito drift is ell_i=L_U p_i, with a uniform bound B0. These are the SOURCE fields, not an arbitrary covariance-equivalent rotation.

(A3) Pathwise uniqueness holds for two solutions with the same initial configuration and marked Brownian family, also in a common enlarged filtration in which the noise remains Brownian. Source existence/uniqueness and its leaf-membership argument remain upstream inputs; the new theorem does not prove them by disintegration.

The source bounds give B0=kappa*(3/2+2R+2L), where L bounds the ell2 probe force and the block beta bound2R was verified in R3. The countable cylinder bracket tests suffice by coordinate embeddings and polarization.

Then there is a Borel F:X times W -> S such that for each x, (F(x,W),W) is a weak marked solution with initial x and F(x,W) is adapted to the completed Wiener filtration. For any initial law mu and Xi independent of W, F(Xi,W) is adapted to the completed filtration generated by Xi and past W and is the strong solution with the actual mixed initial state. There is one F for all mu, not one chosen after selecting mu.

Analytic imports used explicitly are finite-dimensional Levy characterization, Ito isometry/Doob L2, Borel-Cantelli, regular conditional probabilities on Polish spaces, upward conditional-expectation convergence, and the standard Borel structure of probability measures. Their roles are exhibited, not mechanically verified by finite controls.

## 2. A Borel state kernel, without using a strong-map conclusion

The finite-dimensional distributions of P_x are the iterated Feller semigroup expectations. For finite products of continuous state readers, these depend continuously on x. Bounded monotone-class extension gives Borel dependence for cylinder events. Rational evaluation maps generate the Borel sigma-field of continuous path space, so x->P_x is a Borel probability kernel on S. This uses the ALREADY specified all-start-point path laws and their uniqueness, not a choice of strong solutions.

## 3. A marked-noise lift with one fixed approximation scheme

On the canonical state path law define the four-component martingale

M_i(t)=p_i(t)-p_i(0)-integral_0^t ell_i(Y_s)ds.

As a function of Y, this is Borel (indeed continuous on bounded path horizons), because ell_i is continuous on compact X. The source brackets are

d<M_i,M_j>_t=2kappa*delta_ij*V(p_i)V(p_i)^T dt.

Let Theta(p)=V(p)^T. It is the ambient smooth coframe, with Theta V=I3. For the invariant quaternion frame, V(p) has columns (p times e_a)/2; hence

||Theta(p)-Theta(q)||_HS^2=(3/4)|p-q|^2.

For a fixed P_x, stochastic integration defines

W_i=(2kappa)^-1/2 integral Theta(p_i)dM_i.

Its cross brackets are delta_ij I3 dt, so all finite projections are standard Brownian in the STATE filtration by Levy. This identifies the countable independent Wiener law gamma. Moreover M_i-sqrt(2kappa) integral V(p_i)dW_i has zero bracket, since VV^T is the tangent projection. Thus the same marked embedded equation is recovered; the ordinary Ito-to-Stratonovich correction gives precisely the prescribed invariant-field SDE.

### Make this construction Borel in the path, simultaneously as a formula

Use the global dyadic grid k*2^-n for every n, every initial x, every link and every horizon. Let I_(i,n)(Y)(t) be (2kappa)^-1/2 times the left sum

sum_k Theta(p_i(k*2^-n))
 [M_i((k+1)*2^-n wedge t)-M_i(k*2^-n wedge t)],

where only k*2^-n<t is included. Each I_(i,n) is a Borel continuous-path functional. No x-dependent selection of a mesh or subsequence is made.

For0<=s<t<=T, bounded drift and trace of the coordinate bracket give

E_x|p_i(t)-p_i(s)|^2<=K_T*(t-s),
K_T=12kappa+2B0^2*T.

Doob L2 and Ito isometry therefore give

sup_x E_x sup_(t<=T)|I_(i,n)(t)-W_i(t)|^2
<=C_T*2^-n,
C_T=3*K_T*T.

Indeed the normalized bracket operator is bounded by identity, the coframe HS Lipschitz square is3/4, and integrating the grid lag bounded by2^-n yields this conservative constant. It is independent of x, link and box. The inequality uses W_i only to estimate the Borel approximants under a fixed P_x; it does not assume a previously jointly measurable W_i version.

The probability bounds are summable over n for each fixed accuracy. Borel-Cantelli, countably many links and integer horizons show that for EVERY x, all these approximants converge in local uniform path topology P_x-almost surely. Let G be their common Cauchy set in S; it is Borel because the target product path space is completely metrizable. Define A(Y) as their limit on G and the zero noise path otherwise. Then A:S->W is Borel and, for every x, A(Y) is the above marked Wiener family under P_x.

Set Q_x=(Id,A)_*P_x. This is a Borel kernel x->Q_x on S times W, with EXACT same noise marginal gamma for every x. The good-state-path set G is one Borel definition having P_x mass1 for every x; this does not assert a Wiener exceptional set common to all initial x for the later strong map.

## 4. Joint disintegration that is valid for every x

This step does not integrate x against one preferred initial measure.

Choose finite partitions Pi_n of W generated by the first n members of a countable Borel generator, increasing to the full Borel sigma-field. For an atom C with gamma(C)>0 define

K_n(x,w;B)=Q_x(B times C)/gamma(C), w in C.

On zero-mass atoms use the point mass at the constant state path x. This is a jointly Borel probability kernel; its denominator is independent of x because ALL Q_x have marginal gamma. Zero-mass atoms have zero Q_x mass for every x.

Equip Prob(S) with a complete metric for weak convergence. Define K(x,w) as the weak limit of K_n(x,w) when that sequence converges and as the point mass at constant x otherwise. The convergence set and limit map are Borel, so K is jointly Borel.

To verify its disintegration property, FIX ANY x. An ordinary regular conditional probability K_x^*(w) exists for Q_x. For a countable convergence-determining class of bounded continuous functions f on S, K_n f is the conditional expectation of f(Y) given Pi_n(W). Upward martingale convergence implies K_n f->K_x^* f for gamma-almost every w, simultaneously for the countable class. Consequently K_n converges weakly to K_x^*(w), and K=K_x^* gamma-almost surely for this x. Thus

Q_x(dy,dw)=gamma(dw)K(x,w;dy) FOR EVERY x.

For completeness a countable convergence-determining class can be obtained from finite maxima of continuous bump functions supported in a countable open-ball basis. Convergence of their integrals to those of a known probability measure gives the open-set Portmanteau inequalities. This avoids an unstated compactness assumption on the noncompact path space S.

The regular conditional probability used to VERIFY the result may depend on x; the constructed Borel K does not involve choosing that version measurably. The exceptional w-set may depend on x. No inference from a statement mu-almost everywhere in x to every x is made.

## 5. Compatibility and conditional copies

For each fixed x, the recovered W is Brownian in the canonical state filtration. Future Wiener increments are therefore independent of the joint past (Y,W) up to time t. For bounded past reader h(Y_[0,t]),

E_[Q_x](h|W)=E_[Q_x](h|W_[0,t]).

Thus the marginal of K(x,w) on past state paths depends only on past noise, gamma-almost surely for this x. Apply the parameterized construction of section4 separately on each rational horizon to the past pair law. It gives jointly Borel past kernels K_t(x,w_[0,t]) with this equality for every x (up to the appropriate Wiener null set).

Now, conditionally on w, sample two independent state paths with law K(x,w). Their joint past conditional on w is the product of their two past kernels, depending only on w_[0,t]. Hence W remains Brownian in their JOINT filtration. This must be shown; Brownianity in its own natural filtration alone would not suffice.

The solution martingale identities also survive the enlargement. For an increment of a first-copy martingale, multiply by bounded readers of both pasts. Integrating the second past reader conditionally on w replaces it by K_t of that reader, a bounded function of past w. The original Q_x martingale identity then gives zero. The same calculation applies to self-bracket and mixed-with-W product martingales. A determining class and monotone class extend the tests; unbounded Wiener factors are handled by the finite-horizon moment bounds already used in the source comparison. Thus each copy is a solution of the same MARKED SDE in the enlarged filtration, either directly or by the zero-residual-bracket argument. Initial states equal x almost surely.

Pathwise uniqueness now forces the two state paths to coincide. Therefore

(K(x,w) times K(x,w))(diagonal)=1

for gamma-almost every w, for every fixed x. A probability measure on a Polish space with this property is Dirac: otherwise two disjoint open sets have positive mass, yielding a positive off-diagonal product mass.

## 6. Extract one Borel map and prove adaptedness for every preparation

The Dirac embedding y->delta_y has a Borel inverse on its image in Prob(S). Its image is Borel (indeed closed; variances of a countable continuous point-separating family vanish exactly on Dirac measures). Define F(x,w) to be that atom when K(x,w) is Dirac and the atom starts at x; otherwise use the constant path x. This is jointly Borel, F(x,w)(0)=x everywhere, and

Q_x=Law_gamma(F(x,W),W)

for EVERY x. The default operation changes no Q_x law.

Do not infer adaptation just from this formula. For each rational t, compatibility and the full-path Dirac property imply the past kernel K_t is also Dirac gamma_t-almost surely for every x. Extract its atom by the same Borel operation, giving a jointly Borel f_t(x,w_[0,t]) in the past path space. Then

F(x,w)|_[0,t]=f_t(x,w|_[0,t])

gamma-almost surely for every x. There are only countably many rational t.

Let Xi have ANY law mu and be independent of W. The failure sets in this equality are jointly Borel and each x-section is gamma-null. Fubini therefore makes them mu times gamma-null, simultaneously over rational t. Rational evaluations of F(Xi,W) are measurable with respect to the completed sigma-fields generated by Xi and past W. Continuity gives the same for every t by rational times increasing to t; time0 is Xi exactly. Continuous adapted paths are progressively measurable. No universal Wiener null set for all x has been introduced.

Integrating Q_x against mu gives the actual mixed-initial solution law. Its joint state/noise/initial filtration agrees after completion with the input filtration, since the state past is already measurable there. The preserved martingale/bracket identities consequently identify the strong SDE in that filtration. The same F can be pulled back to any input probability space carrying Xi and an independent marked Wiener family, or to a larger filtration in which that family is still Brownian and Xi is initial-measurable.

This proves the stated parameterized strong-map theorem, conditional on A1-A3 and the explicitly named analytic imports.

### A genuine free-source initial-row counterexample

Under product Haar at R=0,U=0, the initial first link has no atom at identity. Modify a correct free strong map by freezing its first-link path whenever that link starts at identity. The modified map is still Borel and agrees with the correct map for Haar-almost every initial state, even though Haar has full support. But it fails for deterministic identity starts. In the radius-two coordinate p_1 at identity, the frozen martingale bracket is0 whereas the source requires2kappa. At kappa=1/2 this gives defect1. Full support identifies continuous versions, not arbitrary Borel maps across initial rows.

### No universal noise exception and no adaptation from Dirac alone

On the unit square with independent uniform x,w, each section {w=x} is null but their union over x covers every w. Thus every-x almost-sure validity does not imply one noise-null set outside which validity holds for all x. Correlating an initial parameter with future noise can hit such a graph with probability1; the all-law conclusion concerns INDEPENDENT initial inputs.

A finite binary analogue F(x,w) whose first state after time0 reads the last future noise bit is Borel with Dirac conditional law but anticipative. Its future/past parity correlation is1 rather than0. It violates compatibility,not the construction above. Without pathwise uniqueness a conditional fair two-point kernel retains off-diagonal copy mass1/2 and cannot be replaced by a Dirac atom.
