# Constructor networks become presentation complexes when coherence cells are attached

Owner: marici.Kitaev

## Question

How does a bridge graph become a higher coherence tower, and what exact
criterion distinguishes an unfilled logical cycle from a cycle that has been
closed by an authorized constructor law?

## Claim boundary

A bridge graph records constructor generators but not their relations.
Attaching a typed 2-cell for each authorized path comparison turns the graph
into a presentation complex.

Let (G) be the directed bridge graph. A path is a composable constructor word.
For two paths (P,Q:j	o i), an authorized comparison

[
alpha_{P,Q}:U_PLongrightarrow U_Q
]

is a 2-cell whose boundary is the loop (PQ^{-1}). Attaching that cell declares
the loop contractible in the constructor theory, possibly only after retaining
a prescribed central phase or operator-valued defect.

This gives a structural tower:

1. vertices are sector objects;
2. edges are primitive bridge constructors;
3. paths are composite constructors;
4. 2-cells are relations between paths;
5. 3-cells are identities among relations;
6. higher cells are syzygies among the preceding coherence laws.

The rungs are therefore not assigned by numerology. Each is forced by
ambiguity remaining in composition at the preceding level.

### Unfilled cycles are logical sectors

Choose a spanning tree. Every non-tree edge creates one fundamental cycle.
Before adding coherence cells, these cycles generate the fundamental group of
the graph. A coefficient lens sends them into its transport target:

[
ho:Pi_1(G)	omathcal G,
]

where (mathcal G) may be an additive group, a line-phase group, or a
noncommutative operator groupoid.

A cycle remains a global logical sector when no authorized 2-cell fills it and
its image under (ho) is task-visible. Local vertex and edge data cannot
remove it because doing so would add a new relation to the constructor theory.

This recovers the toric distinction exactly. Plaquette cells fill contractible
boundary cycles. The two noncontractible torus cycles are not plaquette
boundaries, so their Wilson values survive local repair. Compiling them into
local syndrome would require adjoining new cells that change the allowed
constructor theory.

### Filled cycles are coherence laws

If a 2-cell fills a cycle, the cycle is not automatically sent to the identity.
Its boundary transport must equal the value prescribed by that 2-cell. Three
common cases are:

[
H_C=I
]

for strict closure,

[
H_Cin Z_{mathrm{authorized}}
]

for projective closure, and

[
H_C=Omega_C
]

for a specified operator-valued associator, braid, defect, or curvature cell.

The residual is therefore typed. A scalar trace of (H_C-Omega_C) can vanish
while the operator residual is nonzero. This is why the coefficient lens
controls both the value and the order-of-operation machinery.

### The next rung is an identity among relations

Attaching 2-cells can create closed surfaces assembled from several relation
cells. Two reductions of a constructor word may then use different sequences
of 2-cells. A 3-cell is required when those reductions are not already equal.

This is the presentation-complex form of:

- a cocycle identity among transition functions;
- the pentagon among associators;
- the hexagon relating associator and braiding;
- a Bianchi-type identity among curvature cells;
- a confluence certificate among rewriting rules.

Thus self-closure at the 2-cell level means more than every basic cycle having a
declared relation. It means every task-visible identity among those relations is
generated by the admitted canonical 3-cells, or that no such independent
identity remains.

In homotopical language, vanishing of the relevant (pi_2) or obstruction
class is a sufficient model-dependent criterion. In rewriting language,
termination plus confluence can provide a different sufficient criterion.
Neither criterion may be imported without establishing its hypotheses.

### Why a tree cannot certify higher coherence

A tree has no independent 1-cycles. Consequently it has no nontrivial path
comparison internal to the graph and no relation cell to test. Its apparent
coherence is vacuous.

The first added edge creates a 1-cycle. The first attached 2-cell can close that
cycle. Only after several 2-cells interact can an identity among relations
become visible. Therefore:

[
	ext{connectivity}

e
	ext{cycle observability}

e
	ext{relation closure}

e
	ext{coherence among relations}.
]

These are successive capabilities, not repeated confirmations.

### A minimal triangle audit

Let three objects carry invertible bridges (U_{12},U_{23},U_{31}). The graph
has one independent cycle with holonomy

[
H=U_{31}U_{23}U_{12}.
]

There are four distinct constructor theories on the same graph:

- no 2-cell: (H) is a free logical holonomy;
- a strict 2-cell: (H=I);
- a projective 2-cell: only the authorized central class of (H) is fixed;
- an operator-valued 2-cell: (H=Omega) with order retained.

The scalar edge magnitudes and even the graph topology are identical in all
four. What differs is the allowed constructor theory. This is the smallest
finite witness that topology alone does not determine closure.

A single triangle has no independent identity among multiple relation cells.
To expose a genuine next-rung obstruction, one needs a complex with competing
fillings, such as a tetrahedral boundary, an associativity polytope, or an
overlapping rewriting diamond.

### Carrier geometry and coefficient lens

The Carrier contribution is:

- incidence of vertices, edges, cycles, and attached cells;
- boundaries of cells;
- which paths share endpoints;
- the homotopy type of the presentation complex.

The coefficient lens contributes:

- whether path composition is additive, multiplicative, or ordered;
- whether a filled loop is trivial, central, or operator-valued;
- the representation in which holonomy is tested;
- norm, conditioning, topology, and physical executability.

Accordingly, the first Betti number counts independent geometric cycle
generators but not the amount of coefficient-valued information on them.
Likewise, the number of 2-cells does not determine the dimension of the
operator residual.

## Disposition

The bridge programme should now audit a finite constructor presentation rather
than only a generated algebra:

- generator graph;
- declared path relations and their typed 2-cells;
- surviving fundamental cycles;
- identities among relations;
- coefficient representation of every holonomy;
- task localization and authorized center;
- quantitative lower gain and completion stability;
- physical authority for edges, adjoints, and comparison cells.

The first-failed rung is then intrinsic: it is the lowest-dimensional cell
whose boundary is defined by already authorized lower constructors but whose
required filler is absent, nonfaithful, or unstable.

This supplies a non-arbitrary meaning of “first.” It is ordered by dependency
in the presentation complex, not by checker order or human preference.

The highest-information next model is a tetrahedral or associativity-diamond
network. Every face can close while the assembled surface retains a
higher-coherence obstruction. Such a model would decide whether the observed
self-closure is genuinely forced by the relation theory or only invisible in
single-cycle tests.
