# Diagonal completion separates gluing, projection, and actuation defects

## Question

How should several typed completions of one finite source module be combined without pretending that they are disjoint operational domains or coordinate charts in an already constructed ambient space? What distinct obstructions can remain after combination?

## Claim boundary

The diagonal-completion statement is a standard locally convex completion construction after the source module and seminorm families are frozen. Its application to theta/Tate is presently a typing programme: primitive, square, and connected-tail families are available, while the common seam and archimedean incidence needed for the full boundary packet remains incomplete.

The construction does not prove RH, exclude a completed scalar kernel, manufacture reverse incidence, or show that pairwise completed compatibility implies global compatibility. It also corrects earlier informal language: the completed presentations need not have a defined set-theoretic intersection before a common ambient object is constructed.

## Disposition

### Typed completion presentations

Let (D) be a finite-support source module. For each typed seminorm family \(\mathcal Q_i\), define

\[
N_i=\bigcap_{q\in\mathcal Q_i}\ker q,
\qquad
\widehat D_i=\operatorname{Completion}(D/N_i,\mathcal Q_i).
\]

Each presentation receives the same source through \(\iota_i:D\to\widehat D_i\). Sharing this dense source image is meaningful; intersection of the completed spaces is not meaningful until a common ambient realization is supplied.

### Diagonal-completion theorem

For a finite family \(\{\mathcal Q_i\}_{i=1}^m\), let

\[
\mathcal Q=\bigcup_{i=1}^m\mathcal Q_i,
\qquad
N=\bigcap_iN_i.
\]

The diagonal source map is

\[
\Delta:D/N\longrightarrow\prod_{i=1}^m\widehat D_i,
\qquad
\Delta x=(\iota_1x,\ldots,\iota_mx).
\]

Then the Hausdorff completion generated by \(\mathcal Q\) is canonically the closure of the diagonal image:

\[
\widehat D_{\mathcal Q}
\cong
\overline{\Delta(D/N)}
\subseteq
\prod_i\widehat D_i.
\]

The proof is the universal property of the initial locally convex topology generated by the coordinate seminorms, followed by Hausdorff completion. The coordinate projections

\[
\pi_i:\widehat D_{\mathcal Q}\to\widehat D_i
\]

are the stable replacements for informal chart maps.

### Three different defects

#### Gluing defect

A coordinatewise legal tuple \((x_i)_i\in\prod_i\widehat D_i\) fails to lie in \(\overline{\Delta(D)}\). No single finite-source net realizes all coordinates simultaneously. This is a descent or completion-coherence obstruction.

#### Projection defect

A nonzero joint state (x\in\widehat D_{\mathcal Q}) satisfies \(\pi_i(x)=0\), or vanishes under a further scalar readout. The joint source state exists but one presentation forgets it. This is an observability obstruction.

#### Actuation defect

The joint state and its invisible coordinate are defined, but the authorized constructor graph contains no operation with the required codomain to act on that direction. This is a controllability or authority obstruction. An observer cannot be promoted to an actuator by transport alone.

These defects require different repairs. Stronger common topology addresses gluing; an independent observer addresses projection; a new source-authorized constructor addresses actuation.

### Domination preorder and pro-system arrows

Write \(\mathcal Q_i\preceq\mathcal Q_j\) when every \(q\in\mathcal Q_i\) admits a finite domination

\[
q(x)\le C\max_{1\le k\le r}q_{j,k}(x).
\]

Then the identity on (D) is continuous from the \(\mathcal Q_j\)-topology to the \(\mathcal Q_i\)-topology and may extend as

\[
\widehat D_j\longrightarrow\widehat D_i.
\]

For cutoff families, the constructor subset and constants must be cutoff-independent. Cutoffwise domination with diverging constants does not define a completion arrow.

### Reconciliation fibers

Let each presentation carry an observation (B_i). A record tuple \((y_i)_i\) has reconciliation fiber

\[
\mathcal F(y_1,\ldots,y_m)
=
\{x\in\widehat D_{\mathcal Q}:B_i\pi_i(x)=y_i\text{ for every }i\}.
\]

An empty fiber is inconsistent, one class modulo declared equivalence is deterministic reconciliation, and multiple classes are residual invisibility. Stability additionally requires a uniform lower bound for the joint behavioral map.

Pairwise nonempty reconciliation fibers need not provide one global point. The higher reconciler must test simultaneous diagonal realizability rather than infer it from scalar pairwise agreement.

### Theta/Tate placement

The current typed families are

\[
\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{prim}},
\qquad
\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{sq}},
\qquad
\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{tail}}.
\]

They share the finite prime-power source module but encode inequivalent completion grades. Fourier saturation preserves each grade and does not make primitive and square norms uniformly equivalent. The intended full family must also contain source-derived seam and archimedean graph seminorms:

\[
\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{full}}
=
\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{prim}}
\cup\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{sq}}
\cup\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{tail}}
\cup\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{seam}}
\cup\mathcal Q_{\infty}.
\]

Until the last two families are frozen on the common source module, the full diagonal completion is a target construction.

The known antisymmetric tail direction is a projection defect of the even completed scalar-plus-seam observation: the joint (P,Q,M) packet exists at finite level, while the readout retaining (P+Q) and (M) forgets (P-Q). Separately, the missing reverse incidence from tail state to forcing state is an actuation defect. Neither statement proves or refutes a global gluing defect.

### Falsifiers and first-failure typing

1. A proposed joint tuple is not a limit of one diagonal source net.
2. A comparison uses an identity map that lacks uniform seminorm domination.
3. A completion is called Hausdorff before quotienting its common radical.
4. Separate completed spaces are said to intersect without a common ambient embedding.
5. Pairwise compatible records are promoted to a global state without a simultaneous reconciliation witness.
6. A projection kernel is misreported as failure of the joint source state to exist.
7. An observation row is used as reverse actuation without a source-authorized codomain change.
8. An unauthorized seminorm is added solely to force diagonal closure or injectivity.

The first failed law must be reported as exactly one of `gluing`, `projection`, `actuation`, `uniform_domination`, or `source_authority`, with its source module, cutoff, and witness.

### Programme consequence

The three completion grades are not yet three operational domains. They are typed presentations over one finite source core. Their candidate common operational carrier is the diagonal closure. The remaining RH-bearing audit must determine, in order:

1. whether the complete source-authorized seminorm family is defined;
2. whether the relevant finite packets glue through completion;
3. whether the completed joint observation is faithful on admissible reachable states;
4. whether any remaining invisible state is excluded by an authorized constructor rather than by an imported positivity assertion.
