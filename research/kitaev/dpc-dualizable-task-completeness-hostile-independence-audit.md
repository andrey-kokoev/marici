# DPC: dualizable task completeness and its hostile independence audit

Owner: marici.Kitaev

## Question

Does dualizable task completeness genuinely explain the bridge, relational
volume, and self-closure, or can each apparent consequence be reproduced
without the proposed cause?

## Claim boundary

**Deutsch--Popperian conjecture.** For a task-localized source object, a
constructor theory is explanatorily complete exactly when:

1. authorized state transport and requirement transport are
   evaluation-preserving mates;
2. the generated action has commutant equal to the authorized
   superselection algebra;
3. composition, localization, and completion squares satisfy coherent mate
   laws, quantitatively conservatively where limits are present.

If one clause is altered while the other two are retained, an observable
failure must appear respectively as evaluation drift, a hidden invariant
sector, or route-dependent composition/completion.

The conjecture survives only after four hostile attacks.

### Attack 1: duality might force everything

It does not. The identity-only constructor category preserves evaluation
perfectly and has trivial coherence, yet its commutant is the full endomorphism
algebra. Duality supplies the bridge but not relational coverage.

This attack forces task completeness to be an independent clause.

### Attack 2: minimal commutant might force everything

It does not. Two generic matrices can generate a full matrix algebra and hence
have scalar commutant without being mates of any source-derived requirement
transformers, preserving any evaluation, or possessing physical authority.
Minimal commutant supplies coverage but not meaning or coherence.

This attack prevents relational volume from being mistaken for a complete
constructor theory.

### Attack 3: coherence might force everything

It does not. A perfectly coherent monoidal action can be reducible, and a
perfectly coherent finite action can lose its lower bound under completion.
Pentagon, hexagon, or Beck--Chevalley equalities do not imply observability,
controllability, or completion-stable gain.

This attack forces both task completeness and quantitative conservativity.

### Attack 4: scalar shadows might suffice

They do not. A scalar trace or completed section can agree along two routes
while the typed mate differs on a traceless sector. In \(D(S_3)\), scalar
monodromy can vanish although the monodromy operator is invertible and
non-scalar. Therefore scalar coherence cannot certify constructor coherence.

This attack makes the conjecture operator-valued and task-relative.

### Strong falsifiers

The conjecture is false if any one of the following is found:

- a task-complete, evaluation-preserving constructor theory whose canonical
  mate calculus is coherent but whose task-localized commutant strictly
  exceeds the declared superselection algebra;
- a theory with minimal authorized commutant and coherent mates for which
  forward execution and backward verification disagree on an admitted state;
- a completion functor with uniformly bounded inverse mate cells whose limit
  nevertheless creates a new task-visible kernel;
- two source-authorized theories satisfying all three clauses but predicting
  different outcomes for the same admitted intervention and requirement.

The first three would refute a claimed implication. The fourth would refute
explanatory uniqueness.

### Current finite discrimination

The existing sectors already separate the clauses.

- Toric syndrome without loop ports has duality and chain coherence but fails
  task completeness for logical readout.
- The toric syndrome-plus-loop packet repairs algebraic completeness but does
  not establish a physical nondemolition constructor.
- The \(D(S_3)\) braid-qutrit fragment has exact braid coherence but an excess
  commutant \(\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb C\).
- Adding the unequal pair-channel removes that excess algebraically, while its
  executable source compiler remains unproved.
- Finite-stage invertibility without a uniform lower gain shows why exact
  cutoff coherence does not imply coherent completion.

These are not confirmations of one loose analogy. They are independence
witnesses showing why all three clauses are needed.

## Disposition

The conjecture is sharpened enough to guide the next search. For every proposed
constructor family, record a three-coordinate obstruction signature:

\[
(\Delta_{\mathrm{eval}},\;\mathcal A_{\mathrm{task}}'/Z_{\mathrm{authorized}},\;
\Delta_{\mathrm{mate}}),
\]

supplemented by the smallest lower gain across the admitted completion family.
The first nonzero coordinate identifies the kind of missing explanation, not
merely the first checker line in an arbitrary order.

The most informative next target is the smallest noncommutative model in which
all three gates can be varied independently. The pinned \(D(S_3)\) electric
fusion space already supplies it: braid coherence is fixed, the pair-channel
controls the commutant, and an explicit forward/backward source realization
would decide evaluation naturality and physical authority.
