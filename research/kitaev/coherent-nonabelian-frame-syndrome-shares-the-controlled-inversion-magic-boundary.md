# Coherent non-Abelian frame syndrome shares the controlled-inversion magic boundary

Owner: `marici.Kitaev`

## Bounded question

Can the exact six-valued relative syndrome of the three-frame `S3` code be
extracted nondestructively using only the frozen qubit--qutrit stabilizer
resource theory?

The answer is no for the native factorization. Componentwise differences are
Clifford-extractable, but converting them into a gauge-invariant group
difference requires the same hybrid controlled inversion already obstructing
full `S3` multiplication.

## Native semidirect-product coordinates

Write an element of (S_3) as

\[
g=(e,k),
\qquad
e\in\mathbb Z_2,
\quad
k\in\mathbb Z_3,
\]

with multiplication

\[
(e,k)(f,l)
=
(e+f,\;k+(-1)^e l).
\]

The six-state rail is therefore one qubit tensored with one qutrit. The
conditional action

\[
|e,l\rangle\longmapsto|e,(-1)^e l\rangle
\]

is the frozen controlled-inversion gate (CI). It is not Clifford for the
product Pauli structure.

The inverse is

\[
(e,k)^{-1}
=
(e,\;-(-1)^e k).
\]

For two frames

\[
g=(e,k),
\qquad
h=(f,l),
\]

their exact right-relative coordinate is

\[
d=g^{-1}h=(\delta,c),
\]

where

\[
\delta=e+f,
\qquad
c=(-1)^e(l-k).
\]

The parity difference is linear over (\mathbb Z_2). The raw rotation
difference

\[
u=l-k
\]

is linear over (\mathbb Z_3). The exact group-relative rotation is not (u),
but

\[
c=(-1)^e u.
\]

That last conversion is precisely controlled inversion.

## Gauge action and why the sign is necessary

Let a common left frame displacement be

\[
x=(a,b).
\]

Left multiplication sends

\[
(e,k)
\longmapsto
(a+e,\;b+(-1)^a k).
\]

Consequently,

\[
\delta\longmapsto\delta,
\qquad
u\longmapsto(-1)^a u.
\]

The raw qutrit difference changes sign under a common reflection. It is not a
gauge-invariant syndrome coordinate.

By contrast,

\[
(-1)^{a+e}(-1)^a u=(-1)^e u=c.
\]

Thus the controlled sign is not a chosen convention that can be omitted. It
is exactly what makes the relative rotation coordinate invariant under the
common left gauge action.

## Exact coherent extractor

An exact coherent syndrome extractor is an isometry satisfying

\[
V|g,h\rangle|0\rangle
=
|g,h\rangle|g^{-1}h\rangle
\]

on every computational-basis pair, with a clean syndrome register. Such an
extractor is nondemolition on each relative-displacement sector and preserves
coherence in the global frame coordinate.

It decomposes conceptually into three steps:

1. compute (\delta=e+f) by qubit SUM;
2. compute (u=l-k) by qutrit SUM and inversion;
3. compute (c=(-1)^e u) by controlled inversion.

The first two steps are component Clifford operations. The third is not.

## Product-Clifford obstruction

The frozen Pauli group is the product of a qubit Pauli group and a qutrit
Pauli group. Because the local dimensions are coprime, its order-two and
order-three Sylow subgroups are characteristic. A normalizer must preserve
them separately. Hence a product Clifford cannot make a qutrit output depend
on a qubit input through the nonlinear sign

\[
u\longmapsto(-1)^e u.
\]

Equivalently, an exact basis-state extractor contains (CI) as its only
cross-species step. If the extractor were available in the frozen stabilizer
theory, the controlled-inversion obstruction would be bypassed. The existing
Pauli-normalizer calculation rules this out: only one of the four product
Pauli generators remains Pauli under (CI), and deterministic Choi
teleportation has non-Clifford feed-forward in 32 of 36 branches.

Therefore the exact coherent six-valued relative syndrome is not executable
from stabilizer states, product-Pauli measurements, Clifford resources, and
Pauli feed-forward alone.

## Coprime stabilizer-projector lemma

The same obstruction can be stated without choosing a particular extraction
circuit. Let (S) be a stabilizer subgroup of the product qubit--qutrit Pauli
group. For any element (s=(s_2,s_3)), powers of (s) isolate its order-two and
order-three components. Up to the admitted scalar phases,

\[
s^3=(s_2,I),
\qquad
s^4=(I,s_3).
\]

Hence (S) is generated by its qubit and qutrit Sylow subgroups. Its joint
fixed-space projector factorizes:

\[
\Pi_S=\Pi_{S_2}\otimes\Pi_{S_3}.
\]

In particular, a diagonal product-stabilizer projector has Cartesian support
between the qubit and qutrit computational coordinates.

For nonzero (c), the exact relative-sector projector below does not have
Cartesian support: its qutrit constraint changes when the first qubit changes.
It is therefore not a product-stabilizer projector.

A stabilizer protocol can measure the two Cartesian branches separately and
coarse-grain their classical labels. Its outcome map then has at least two
distinguishable Kraus branches and removes their cross term. Exact
nondemolition projection onto their coherent union requires one Kraus action
proportional to the nonfactorizing projector, or an equivalent coherent
control. That is the controlled-inversion resource in another form.

## Relative-sector projector view

For (d=(\delta,c)), the exact syndrome projector is

\[
\Pi_{\delta,c}
=
\sum_{e,k}
|e,k;\ e+\delta,\ k+(-1)^e c\rangle
\langle e,k;\ e+\delta,\ k+(-1)^e c|.
\]

When (c=0), its support is a direct product of a qubit parity subspace and a
qutrit equality subspace.

When (c\neq0), the allowed qutrit affine subspace depends on the qubit value
(e):

- for (e=0), the qutrit difference is (c);
- for (e=1), the qutrit difference is (-c).

The support is not a Cartesian product across the coprime qubit and qutrit
factors. It is the union of two component-stabilizer branches joined by the
controlled sign.

Measuring those two branches separately and forgetting which occurred does
not implement the Lüders instrument

\[
\rho\longmapsto
\Pi_{\delta,c}\rho\Pi_{\delta,c}.
\]

It dephases coherence between the two global-reflection branches. Coarse
classical equality of outcome probabilities is not equivalence of
instruments.

## Destructive classical escape

There is a cheaper procedure:

1. measure both frame registers in the basis (|e,k\rangle);
2. calculate (g^{-1}h) classically;
3. prepare or relabel a replacement frame.

All arithmetic after measurement is classical, so no coherent (CI) gate is
needed. But this procedure reads the global orientation and destroys any
superposition over the frame torsor. It is not a nondemolition syndrome
instrument for the coherent frame code.

This escape is valid only if the frame has already been typed as a sharp
classical controller and destructive refresh is authorized. It cannot be used
to claim protection of a coherent quantum reference.

## Gauge-leaking component syndrome

One may instead extract

\[
(\delta,u)=(e+f,l-k)
\]

with component Clifford operations. This detects component disagreement, but
(u) changes sign under a common reflection. For a rotation displacement, the
measured value becomes correlated with the global parity (e).

Reading (u) therefore leaks a gauge-frame coordinate into the syndrome
environment. On a coherent global-frame superposition, it can decohere the
logical frame even when the subsequent classical correction restores basis
labels.

The distinction is exact:

- component syndrome: Clifford, gauge-covariant, potentially frame-leaking;
- group-relative syndrome: gauge-invariant, nondemolition, non-Clifford;
- destructive full readout: classically complete, but consumes the frame.

## Detection versus correction

For a basis-classical frame, component comparison detects every nonidentity
right displacement: either the parity difference is nonzero or the qutrit
difference is nonzero.

Exact correction of a rotation displacement needs its signed group coordinate
(c), not merely the unordered alternative ({c,-c}). Choosing the wrong sign
applies the inverse correction in the wrong frame and leaves a nontrivial
right displacement.

Thus the stabilizer-accessible component information can support destructive
detection. It does not supply coherent exact correction of the full right
shift family.

## Shared magic-resource theorem

The same resource obstruction occurs twice in the finite constructor:

1. full `S3` multiplication for the endpoint apparatus requires controlled
   qutrit inversion by a qubit;
2. exact coherent protection of the six-state relational frame requires the
   same controlled inversion to turn component differences into the
   gauge-invariant relative coordinate.

This is not two unrelated demands for magic. Both arise from the semidirect
product action defining (S_3).

An admitted verified (CI) resource would therefore have two consumers:

- the data-plane non-Abelian multiplication compiler;
- the control-plane frame-syndrome compiler.

Its fault-tolerant resource census must price both. Supplying it to only one
side does not complete the physical constructor.

## DPC: non-Abelian control and its reference share a resource boundary

The conjecture is:

> In a semidirect-product quantum controller, the operation that makes the
> data action non-Abelian also appears in the gauge-invariant syndrome of the
> controller's relational frame. Exact coherent control and exact coherent
> protection of its meaning therefore cross the same non-Clifford resource
> boundary.

The finite `S3` case explains why the frame cannot be treated as free
classical metadata after demanding coherent element-resolved endpoint
operations.

## Critics

### Relative subtraction is a Clifford operation

Componentwise subtraction is Clifford. Group-relative subtraction includes
the semidirect sign (c=(-1)^e u), which is controlled inversion.

### Measure both components and compute the sign classically

That measures the global frame coordinate and destroys coherent frame
superposition. It changes the instrument being implemented.

### Coarse-graining the two sign branches restores coherence

Forgetting a classical branch label removes the record but does not reverse
the dephasing caused by distinguishable environment branches.

### The frame is only a controller, so destructive refresh is harmless

That is an additional classicality and fresh-source assumption. It may define
a valid architecture, but it is not a consequence of the coherent frame code.

### A different code might make controlled inversion transversal

Possibly. That would be a code switch or a new coefficient/resource lens. The
present theorem concerns the frozen product-Pauli encoding and does not rule
out a verified alternative code.

## Exact falsifiers

- Failure of the semidirect multiplication or inverse formulas.
- The exact relative coordinate remaining invariant after deleting the
  controlled sign.
- A product-Clifford circuit computing (c=(-1)^e u) coherently.
- A stabilizer-only nondemolition six-outcome extractor preserving coherence
  in every (\Pi_{\delta,c}) sector.
- Destructive component measurement leaving an arbitrary coherent global
  frame state unchanged.
- Exact correction of both nonzero rotation shifts from an unsigned syndrome
  without an additional frame reference.
- Controlled inversion becoming admitted through a verified source or code
  switch; that would supersede, not contradict, the frozen-resource verdict.

## Claim boundary

This packet proves the resource obstruction for exact basis-state coherent
extraction and the corresponding nondemolition relative-sector instrument in
the frozen product-Pauli theory. It does not rule out a nonstabilizer magic
factory, a code switch, a destructive classical-frame architecture, or a
different source-generated syndrome interaction.

The new structural conclusion is that non-Abelian data control and coherent
protection of its relational meaning inherit the same semidirect-product
resource boundary.
