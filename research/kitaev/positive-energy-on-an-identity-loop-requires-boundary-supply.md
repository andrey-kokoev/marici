# Positive energy on an identity loop requires boundary supply

## Bounded question

Can positive observation energy accumulated along a path with identity total
transport be represented as a pure change of endpoint storage?

## Storage defect and its cocycle

Let \(S_{a,b}:X_a\to X_b\) be state transport and let \(P_a,P_b\) be
Hermitian storage forms at the endpoints. Define the storage-loss defect

\[
D_{a,b}=P_a-S_{a,b}^*P_bS_{a,b}.
\]

For consecutive segments,

\[
D_{a,c}
=D_{a,b}+S_{a,b}^*D_{b,c}S_{a,b}.
\]

This follows by inserting and cancelling
\(S_{a,b}^*P_bS_{a,b}\). It has the same transported-additive form as the
observation Gramian, but it is a coboundary of endpoint storage.

## Identity-loop obstruction

If \(S_{a,c}=I\) and \(P_c=P_a\), then

\[
D_{a,c}=0.
\]

Suppose each segment defect is positive semidefinite. Congruence by an
invertible transport preserves positivity, so

\[
0=D_{a,b}+S_{a,b}^*D_{b,c}S_{a,b}
\]

is a sum of positive semidefinite forms. Both terms must vanish. Therefore no
strictly positive path energy around identity holonomy can be a pure endpoint
storage loss.

This obstruction is independent of whether the endpoint storage is positive,
indefinite, or parameter-dependent; only equality of the initial and terminal
storage on the loop is used.

## Dynamic supply identity

Let \(W_{a,b}\ge0\) be the source-authorized observation Gramian. Define the
required supply form

\[
Q_{a,b}
=W_{a,b}+S_{a,b}^*P_bS_{a,b}-P_a
=W_{a,b}-D_{a,b}.
\]

Then the balance law is

\[
W_{a,b}
=P_a-S_{a,b}^*P_bS_{a,b}+Q_{a,b}.
\]

Both \(W\) and \(D\) satisfy the transported cocycle, hence so does supply:

\[
Q_{a,c}
=Q_{a,b}+S_{a,b}^*Q_{b,c}S_{a,b}.
\]

On an identity loop with equal endpoint storage,

\[
Q_{a,c}=W_{a,c}.
\]

Every positive unit of accumulated observation energy must therefore be matched
by explicit path supply. It cannot be inferred from the endpoint shear.

## Exact rank-two hostile

Use \(S_1=S(F)\), \(S_2=S(-F)\), so \(S_2S_1=I\), and the rank-one sensor
\(C=(1,0)\) on both segments. Milestone 2636 gives

\[
W_{a,c}=
\begin{pmatrix}
2&F\\
\overline F&|F|^2
\end{pmatrix}>0
\]

for \(F\ne0\). With any common endpoint storage \(P_a=P_c\), a no-supply
identity would require \(W_{a,c}=D_{a,c}=0\), contradicting positive
definiteness. The minimal repair is not a fitted endpoint metric; it is a
nonzero supplied path form \(Q_{a,c}=W_{a,c}\).

## Theta/Tate typing consequence

Grothendieck's finite source witness has \(F_L(s)=0\), hence identity total

\[
2\bigl(\cosh(2\delta L)-1\bigr)\|f\|^2.
\]

Off seam this is positive. Abstractly, it must be typed as boundary supply or

## Conserved forms and observation energy

An invariant indefinite form has \(D_{a,b}=0\). It says transport conserves a
bilinear quantity. It does not make \(W_{a,b}\) vanish; instead the balance law
reduces to \(Q_{a,b}=W_{a,b}\). Thus indefinite conservation and positive
observation coexist only with an explicit supply channel.

A fixed positive KYP inequality would demand a one-signed defect. For a
nonidentity determinant-one Jordan shear that branch is already algebraically
closed. Dynamic storage remains possible only when its endpoint forms and
supply are source-derived and composition-compatible.

## Exact audit and falsifiers

The checker verifies both cocycles on exhaustive small exact Hermitian
fixtures, the identity-loop telescoping law, the positive rank-two hostile,
and the supplied balance on each segment and after composition.

The theorem is falsified by a closed identity-transport loop with equal
endpoint storage and a strictly positive pure storage defect. A proposed supply
law is falsified if its segment forms fail the transported cocycle. A physical
interpretation is rejected if the supply row is fitted from the desired energy
rather than derived independently from a boundary current.

## Claim boundary

boundary supply, the tail/seam/prime sensor rows, a uniform lower bound,
passivity, a physical energy flux, or RH.
