# Actuator Lifts Form a Torsor over Protected-Kernel Shears

Suppose (R=SH) and an actuator lift (B_0:Y\to X) satisfies

\[
RB_0=S.
\]

Every other lift is

\[
B=B_0+N,
\qquad
RN=0.
\]

Hence actuator lifts form an affine torsor over
(\operatorname{Hom}(Y,\ker R)). Residual data cannot distinguish its points.

Let (L:X\to W) be a protected logical or boundary record. The correction

\[
C_B=I-BH
\]

preserves that record exactly when

\[
LC_B=L,
\qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad
LBH=0.
\]

Residual cancellation (RC_B=0) does not imply logical nondisturbance.

## Minimal torsor hostile

Take (X=\mathbb R^2), residual (R=(1,0)), seam (H=(1,0)), and protected
record (L=(0,1)). The decoder is (S=1). Every

\[
B_t=\begin{pmatrix}1\\t\end{pmatrix}
\]

satisfies (RB_t=1), so all lifts cancel the residual. But

\[
C_t=I-B_tH
=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\-t&1\end{pmatrix},
\]

and

\[
LC_t=(-t,1).
\]

Only (t=0) preserves (L). The other lifts convert corrected residual into a
logical shear invisible to the current syndrome. A later logical probe
distinguishes them.

Thus a preferred decoder is exactly a torsor-origin problem. It requires a
source-derived splitting, locality rule, minimum-support criterion, symplectic
constraint, energy principle, or other authorized reference. Syndrome and
residual cancellation alone cannot select it.

## Completion gate

For cutoff families, nondisturbance requires a uniform estimate on the shear
channel (L_NB_NH_N), not merely exact or stable residual cancellation. A
sequence of lifts may satisfy (R_NC_{B_N}=0) while logical shear norms grow or
remain nonzero. Any quotient declaring the shear irrelevant must prove that
all authorized successors descend through that quotient.

## Falsifiers

- Two lifts have identical residual action but different protected outputs.
- A minimum-norm pseudoinverse is selected without a source metric.
- Residual cancellation is reported as logical preservation.
- The lift differs by a nonzero map into (\ker R) detected by a successor.
- Cutoffwise nondisturbance holds only after changing the protected probe.
