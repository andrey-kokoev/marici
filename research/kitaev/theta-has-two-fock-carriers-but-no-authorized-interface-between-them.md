# Theta has two Fock carriers but no authorized interface between them

## Question

Does the pinned theta/Tate Fock construction provide the fresh-vacuum
factorization needed for boundary-feedback attenuation?

## Two different Fock constructions

The programme currently contains two structures called Fock.

### Prime occupation Fock

The one-particle arithmetic space is

\[
\mathcal H_{\rm prime}=\ell^2(\{\text{primes}\}).
\]

Its symmetric Fock space carries coherent or occupation states assembled from
prime modes. The normalized unramified state exists in the direct vacuum
representation exactly when

\[
\operatorname{Re}s>\frac12.
\]

The reciprocal state exists in its dual representation exactly when

\[
\operatorname{Re}s<\frac12.
\]

At the seam, neither ordinary vacuum representation contains the completed
state.

### Spectral jet Fock

The factorially normalized derivatives

\[
j_k=\frac{r^k}{k!}\partial_z^kG
\]

form a Hardy coefficient space over jet order. The differentiated scale flow
acts through a bounded unilateral shift.

This Fock index is spectral derivative order, not prime occupation.

The two constructions organize different carrier directions.

## Prime factorization is genuine but limited

For an orthogonal decomposition of one-particle spaces,

\[
\mathcal H_1\oplus\mathcal H_2,
\]

symmetric Fock space has the canonical factorization

\[
\Gamma_s(\mathcal H_1\oplus\mathcal H_2)
\simeq
\Gamma_s(\mathcal H_1)\otimes\Gamma_s(\mathcal H_2).
\]

Hence disjoint prime subsets provide independent vacuum factors inside the
direct open sector. This is a genuine fresh-increment structure for prime
occupation modes.

But allocating a disjoint prime subset changes arithmetic support. It does
not automatically produce an auxiliary copy of the seam or endpoint boundary
port. A feedback environment must have the same incidence type as the port
it attenuates.

## Missing interface

To use prime Fock factorization for boundary attenuation, the source must
provide an isometric or rigged correspondence

\[
\mathcal I_s:P_s\longrightarrow\mathcal H_{\rm prime}
\]

from the completed boundary port into a one-particle prime module, together
with an independent orthogonal copy supporting the vacuum rotation.

No such map is presently pinned. In particular:

- finite prime incidence does not identify the seam port with a prime
  one-particle mode;
- the archimedean line is a tensor extension, not a prime summand;
- global Poisson sewing has no finite-Euler port action;
- the primitive current is distributional at the seam;
- the spectral jet coordinate lives in a different Fock grading.

Transporting the prime vacuum through any of these unproved interfaces would
be authority by analogy.

## Seam obstruction

The normalized prime Fock state leaves its direct vacuum representation at

\[
\operatorname{Re}s=\frac12
\]

because

\[
\sum_p\frac1p
\]

diverges. Therefore no single ordinary Hilbert-Fock dilation derived from
that representation can remain uniform across the critical seam.

A seam-crossing attenuation constructor would require a relative or rigged
Fock correspondence that simultaneously retains:

- the distributional primitive current;
- the square Hilbert current;
- the archimedean and polar channels;
- the reciprocal representation change.

This is exactly the unresolved boundary rigging, not an already available
vacuum port.

## Vacuum overlap is not detector transversality

In each direct open sector, the normalized prime state has nonzero overlap
with its reference vacuum. This proves state implementability and fixes a
frame.

The completed Poisson detector is a different, distributional observation.
Its overlap with the state may vanish. Thus:

\[
\text{nonzero source-vacuum overlap}
\]

does not imply nonzero completed boundary readout.

Consequently the prime vacuum cannot be substituted for the missing
boundary detector or its passive loss row.

## Jet-Fock positivity obstruction

The spectral jet Fock also fails to supply attenuation for a separate reason.
The native prolonged Green diagonal gives an identity-type vacuum reservoir,
while the Jordan field quadrature is unbounded across jet level.

A fixed vacuum line cannot dominate it. Positivity requires number-operator
growth:

\[
N+\lambda P+\lambda^2I
=(a+i\lambda I)^*(a+i\lambda I).
\]

No source incidence of the prime-square current with this spectral number
operator has yet been proved.

Thus even a formal vacuum rotation would not establish the passive defect
identity needed for stable boundary inversion.

## Smallest hostile

Take two Hilbert spaces \(H_{\rm prime}\) and \(P_{\rm seam}\) with normalized
vacua in both, but no declared map between them. A beam splitter on
\(H_{\rm prime}\oplus H_{\rm prime}\) produces a valid prime attenuation.
It defines no operation on \(P_{\rm seam}\).

Identifying the spaces solely because both contain a vacuum vector is
ill-typed. This is the minimal falsifier of vacuum transport by name.

## Disposition

Theta/Tate has authentic independent prime-mode vacuum increments, but they
live in the wrong carrier for the missing boundary-feedback homotopy. The
spectral jet Fock has the right odd coordinate but lacks the number-energy
and boundary-incidence lift.

The missing constructor is now an interface between these Fock carriers and
the completed boundary port. Until it is derived, the direct physical Schur
index route requires fewer unsupported arrows than vacuum attenuation.
