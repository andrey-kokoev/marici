# A genuine distributive law self-closes the mixed tower

**Owner:** marici.Kitaev  
**Status:** bounded theorem packet  
**Question:** If source and behavior have independent terminal coherencers, what makes their top comparison coherent at every composite depth?

## 1. Setup

Let \((T,\eta,\mu)\) be the monad generated by source syntax and substitution.

Let \(B\) be the declared behavior endofunctor.

Let

\[
\lambda:T B\Longrightarrow B T
\]

be natural and satisfy

\[
\lambda\circ\eta_B=B\eta
\]

and

\[
\lambda\circ\mu_B
=
B\mu\circ\lambda_T\circ T\lambda.
\]

These are the unit and multiplication laws for distributing source syntax across behavior.

## 2. Lift theorem

For every \(B\)-coalgebra

\[
b:X\to B X,
\]

define a new coalgebra on \(T X\) by

\[
T X
\xrightarrow{T b}
T B X
\xrightarrow{\lambda_X}
B T X.
\]

Naturality and the two distributive laws imply that \(\eta_X) and \(\mu_X) are coalgebra morphisms.

Therefore \(T\) lifts to a monad on the category of \(B\)-coalgebras.

This is the exact meaning of compiled operational closure: source construction is now defined on behavior-bearing objects, not only on their underlying carriers.

## 3. Compatible source–behavior objects

A carrier \(X\) with source algebra

\[
a:T X\to X
\]

and behavior coalgebra

\[
b:X\to B X
\]

is compatible precisely when \(a\) is a coalgebra morphism:

\[
b\circ a
=
B(a)\circ\lambda_X\circ T(b).
\]

Thus the previously defined residue

\[
\rho_X=b a-B(a)\lambda_XT(b)
\]

is not an arbitrary comparison error. It is the obstruction to making the source algebra an algebra internal to behavior-bearing objects.

## 4. Final behavior inherits source composition

Assume \(B\) has a final coalgebra

\[
z:Z\to BZ.
\]

The composite

\[
T Z
\xrightarrow{Tz}
T BZ
\xrightarrow{\lambda_Z}
B T Z
\]

makes \(TZ\) a \(B\)-coalgebra. By finality, there is a unique coalgebra morphism

\[
\alpha:T Z\to Z.
\]

The unit and multiplication laws for \(\alpha\) follow from finality: both candidate routes are coalgebra morphisms with the same codomain, so uniqueness identifies them.

Hence the final behavior object carries a canonical \(T\)-algebra structure.

For every compatible \((X,a,b)\), the unique behavior map

\[
operatorname{beh}:X\to Z
\]

is automatically a source-algebra morphism:

\[
operatorname{beh}\circ a
=
\alpha\circ T(\operatorname{beh}).
\]

This is the sought comparison between the final source coherencer and final behavior coherencer. Composite source meanings are preserved by final behavioral semantics.

## 5. Why the mixed tower terminates

The unit law handles primitive insertion.

The multiplication law handles one layer of substitution.

Associativity of \(\mu\), naturality of \(\lambda\), and structural induction then force the comparison for every finite syntax tree. Higher composite comparisons carry no independent choices.

Thus the mixed tower closes coskeletally after:

- naturality;
- the unit square;
- the multiplication or substitution square.

A separate comparison cell for every arity is unnecessary.

This is a proper termination theorem, not an extrapolation from finitely many successful examples.

## 6. The exact limitation

The theorem preserves only the coordinates encoded in \(B\).

If \(B\) records nominal outputs but omits:

- successor authority;
- resource consumption;
- waste;
- fault behavior;
- reset state;
- memory;
- completion topology,

then the lifted monad preserves only nominal behavior. Agreement in the impoverished \(B\) does not imply compiled operational closure.

The repair is not to append independent scalar tests after proving the theorem. It is to enlarge the typed behavior functor and rederive \(\lambda\).

## 7. Product behavior and component laws

A useful candidate is a typed product or indexed behavior signature

\[
B_{mathrm{full}}
=
B_{mathrm{out}}
\times B_{mathrm{authority}}
\times B_{mathrm{resource}}
\times B_{mathrm{fault}}
\times B_{mathrm{reset}}.
\]

A distributive law into this product is equivalent to compatible component laws only when the product and shared-domain structure are genuinely source-authorized.

Componentwise commuting squares are insufficient if the coordinates share constraints, conditioning, or dependent types. Their joint incidence must also be preserved.

## 8. Minimal hostile hierarchy

1. **Naturality failure:** relabelling or context transport changes the mixed cell.
2. **Unit failure:** primitive insertion behaves differently before and after observation.
3. **Multiplication failure:** primitives and pairwise composites commute, but a nested composite does not.
4. **Behavior omission:** nominal output commutes while authority or fault behavior differs.
5. **Dependent-coordinate failure:** every component law passes separately, but their shared admissibility constraint fails.
6. **Finality failure:** the declared behavior object is not final for the admitted process class.
7. **Completion failure:** finite syntax is preserved but the lifted law is not continuous at the completion.
8. **Executable failure:** the mathematical comparison exists without an authorized implementation.

## 10. Present conclusion

The top object is not another indefinitely rising tower.

Under the distributive-law axioms it becomes a finite coherencer whose laws force all higher finite composition comparisons. It is the mixed analogue of coskeletal closure.

The remaining open question in each sector is therefore sharply typed:

> Has the behavior functor included every coordinate whose preservation is claimed, and is the source-derived \(\lambda\) a distributive law for that full functor?
