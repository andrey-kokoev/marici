# One fixed irrational vacuum phase is dense control but not yet a fault-tolerant gate set

Owner: `marici.Kitaev`

## Bounded question

Can the arbitrary-time vacuum wait in the protected `D(S3)` fusion-qutrit
controller be replaced algebraically by one fixed vacuum phase?

Yes, under a simple sufficient condition. If the fixed phase angle divided by
`2 pi` is irrational, its integer powers are dense in the full vacuum-wait
circle. Discrete electric braid permutations conjugate that one dense circle
onto all three edge-projector circles. The previously proved edge-projector
Lie closure then makes the generated fixed gate set dense in `U(3)`.

This replaces a continuously selected actuator by one repeated gate at the
level of qualitative reachability. It does not yet supply an efficient
compiler or fault tolerance. Both depend on quantitative phase recurrence and
on how physical error scales under repeated use.

## Claim boundary

The irrational-angle condition is sufficient, not necessary. Some rational
angles may also enlarge the finite monomial group to an infinite or dense
group through noncommuting products; this packet does not classify them.

The theorem assumes exact access to the fixed gate, the admitted discrete
braid permutations, their inverses, and coherent sequential composition. It
does not derive the phase angle from the microscopic lattice, prove that the
gate is topological, or establish a threshold theorem. Qualitative density
does not imply a uniform synthesis rate.

## Frozen qutrit controls

Use the orthonormal monomial basis

\[
|q_0\rangle,\quad |q_1\rangle,\quad |q_2\rangle.
\]

Completed electric braid words supply every coordinate permutation. The
native vacuum projector is one edge projector

\[
P_A=H_{12}
=
\frac12
(|q_1\rangle+|q_2\rangle)
(\langle q_1|+\langle q_2|).
\]

For a fixed real angle `vartheta`, define

\[
V_\vartheta=e^{-i\vartheta P_A}.
\]

This is one declared gate, not an arbitrary waiting-time family.

## Irrational cyclic-density lemma

Assume

\[
\alpha=\frac{\vartheta}{2\pi}
\]

is irrational. Kronecker's circle theorem gives

\[
\overline{\{n\alpha\bmod 1:n\in\mathbb Z\}}
=
\mathbb R/\mathbb Z.
\]

Functional calculus for the projector then gives

\[
V_\vartheta^n=e^{-in\vartheta P_A}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\overline{\langle V_\vartheta\rangle}
=
\{e^{-itP_A}:t\in\mathbb R\}.
\]

If only nonnegative powers are taken as literal words, their closure is the
same circle. Compactness supplies recurrence arbitrarily close to the
identity and hence approximates the inverse. This is a closure statement, not
a bounded-cost inverse compiler.

## Conjugating one clock onto three edges

Let `S3` denote the finite coordinate-permutation image supplied by the two
electric braids. For each coordinate pair `i,j`, choose a permutation `g_ij`
such that

\[
g_{ij}P_Ag_{ij}^{-1}=H_{ij}.
\]

Then

\[
g_{ij}V_\vartheta^ng_{ij}^{-1}
=
e^{-in\vartheta H_{ij}}.
\]

Taking closures yields every one-parameter edge subgroup

\[
\{e^{-itH_{01}}\},
\qquad
\{e^{-itH_{02}}\},
\qquad
\{e^{-itH_{12}}\}.
\]

The earlier exact commutator calculation proved

\[
\operatorname{Lie}_{\mathbb R}
\{iH_{01},iH_{02},iH_{12}\}
=
\mathfrak u(3).
\]

The closed subgroup generated by the three edge circles therefore has Lie
algebra `u(3)` and equals `U(3)`. Hence

\[
\overline{\langle S_3,V_\vartheta\rangle}=U(3).
\]

Modulo physically irrelevant global phase, the same statement gives dense
projective qutrit control.

## What the theorem has removed

The algebraic controller no longer needs a continuously set dwell-time input.
Its finite generator list can be:

1. two completed electric braids generating coordinate permutations;
2. one fixed vacuum-phase gate `V_vartheta`;
3. inverses as physical primitives or recurrence-based approximations.

This is a finite alphabet. Arbitrary logical gates arise from longer words,
not from specifying a real-valued pulse duration on each use.

The replacement is important but limited. A fixed analog-calibrated gate is
not automatically a topological gate merely because the command alphabet is
finite.

## Phase-recurrence condition number

For a target vacuum phase `phi`, define the circular distance

\[
d_{\mathbb T}(x,y)
=
\min_{k\in\mathbb Z}|x-y-2\pi k|.
\]

Define the worst-case recurrence cost

\[
K_\vartheta(\epsilon)
=
\sup_{\phi\in[0,2\pi)}
\min\{n\geq0:
d_{\mathbb T}(n\vartheta,\phi)\leq\epsilon\}.
\]

Irrationality implies that this number is finite for every positive
`epsilon`. It does not provide a useful uniform rate as `epsilon` tends to
zero. That rate depends on the continued-fraction data of
`vartheta/(2 pi)`.

Large partial quotients can create long accuracy scales on which the orbit
has poor covering. A source that needs a complexity theorem must therefore
provide a Diophantine or explicit compilation bound, not only certify
irrationality.

This condition number is the fixed-gate analogue of the bridge-margin and
observability constants elsewhere in the programme: qualitative separation
or reachability survives while stable access can collapse.

## From circle approximation to qutrit compilation

Let a continuous-control synthesis of a target qutrit gate use `m` edge
pulses with angles

\[
t_1,\ldots,t_m.
\]

Approximate each pulse by a power of the corresponding conjugate of
`V_vartheta`, with operator error at most `epsilon/m`. The telescoping bound
for products then gives total operator error at most `epsilon`.

Thus qualitative continuous controllability plus irrational recurrence gives
a finite fixed-gate word for every target and tolerance.

This proof deliberately supplies no optimal word length. The continuous
synthesis length, recurrence costs for its pulse angles, and approximation
error allocation all enter the compiler cost.

## Physical error accumulation

Suppose one physical implementation of the fixed gate differs from its ideal
unitary channel by diamond distance at most `eta`. With no intervening
fault-tolerant correction, a word using it `n` times has worst-case channel
error bounded only by

\[
n\eta
\]

from telescoping.

If recurrence requires `n` uses to obtain phase resolution `epsilon`, the raw
gate error must be much smaller than `epsilon/n` for the synthesis to remain
useful. Longer recurrence can therefore amplify the very analog calibration
error the finite alphabet was meant to avoid.

Coherent systematic overrotation is worse than independent stochastic noise
for naive repetition. If every use implements angle

\[
\vartheta+\delta,
\]

then the `n`th power carries phase error `n delta` modulo `2 pi`. Repetition
does not average it away.

## Density is not a threshold theorem

A fault-tolerant finite gate set needs additional structure beyond density:

- encoded implementations or error-detecting gadgets for each generator;
- a fault model with bounded causal spread;
- recovery boundaries before one fault becomes logical;
- a compilation theorem whose word length is controlled;
- physical error suppression strong enough for that word length;
- an independent phase reference excluding common-mode overrotation.

Solovay--Kitaev-type polylogarithmic compilation cannot be inferred from the
density proof alone. It requires the appropriate inverse-closed finite set,
quantitative hypotheses, and a separate algorithmic theorem. Nor does a short
word imply that its microscopic locations satisfy a threshold model.

## Rational-angle boundary

If

\[
\frac{\vartheta}{2\pi}\in\mathbb Q,
\]

the powers of `V_vartheta` form a finite cyclic group, so the present proof
cannot recover the continuous vacuum circle.

It does not follow that the complete noncommuting gate group is finite. Two
finite-order generators can generate an infinite group. Rational cases must
be classified using the complete generated group, not rejected merely from
the order of the vacuum gate.

The trivial phase gives no enlargement. Other rational phases are a separate
finite-generator classification problem and may be attractive if they admit
exact preparation or distillation.

## Source-authority boundary

The source must identify what fixes `vartheta`.

- A microscopic energy and clock calibration defines a dynamical gate.
- A geometric phase may improve sensitivity but needs its own curvature and
  deformation theorem.
- A braid or defect path defines a topological gate only if logical action is
  invariant on its admitted path class.
- A prepared resource state plus teleportation defines a measurement-assisted
  gate whose preparation and correction records must be typed.

The abstract density theorem grants authority to none of these constructors.
It only evaluates the logical group after one of them has been independently
admitted.

## Exact falsifiers

- Irrationality of `vartheta/(2 pi)` fails while the cyclic subgroup is still
  claimed dense in the vacuum circle.
- Qualitative circle density is assigned a cutoff-independent polynomial
  recurrence bound without a Diophantine hypothesis.
- Approximate inverse availability in compact closure is called a bounded-cost
  physical inverse.
- The three braid conjugates fail to produce all edge projectors.
- The edge-projector Lie algebra has dimension below nine.
- A finite rational-order vacuum gate is declared useless without examining
  its full noncommuting generated group.
- Density is promoted to exact finite synthesis.
- Exact finite synthesis is promoted to fault tolerance.
- Repeated coherent overrotation is modeled as independent zero-mean noise.
- The fixed phase is called topological without a deformation-invariance
  theorem.
- The fixed gate is used as its own sole phase-calibration reference.

## Shared Carrier geometry and quantum coefficient lens

Shared Carrier geometry supplies finite constructor alphabets, orbit closure,
recurrence cost, word composition, condition numbers, and the distinction
between reachability and stable compilation.

The quantum coefficient lens supplies projective unitary gates, projector
functional calculus, braid conjugation, Lie closure, diamond-distance fault
accumulation, and coherent phase error.

## Disposition

One fixed irrational vacuum phase, together with the admitted discrete
electric braids, is algebraically enough for dense qutrit control. Arbitrary
real-valued timing commands are unnecessary for qualitative reachability.

The price has moved rather than vanished. Irrational recurrence may be badly
conditioned, repeated physical error accumulates, and no topological or
threshold protection follows from density. The next physical theorem must
provide a source-derived fixed phase, a quantitative compiler for it, and an
encoded or otherwise fault-tolerant realization. Rational fixed phases remain
an open classification lane rather than a failed case.
