# Symmetry Selects an Actuator Origin Only When Invariant Shears Vanish

Let the actuator lifts form an affine space

\[
\mathcal T=B_0+V,
\qquad
V=\{N:RN=0\}.
\]

Suppose a finite group (G) acts affinely on (\mathcal T) and linearly on
its shear space (V), compatibly with the source, observation, residual, and
actuator typing.

For any lift (B), the average

\[
B_G=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}g\cdot B
\]

is a mathematically (G)-equivariant lift, provided the actuator space is a
real or complex affine space closed under this averaging. Therefore finite
symmetry removes the existence obstruction at the mathematical level.

The fixed lifts form either the empty set outside these hypotheses or, under
them, an affine space over

\[
V^G=\{N\in V:gN=N\text{ for all }g\in G\}.
\]

Consequently symmetry selects a unique origin exactly when

\[
V^G=0.
\]

## Minimal (C_2) fixtures

On the one-dimensional torsor (B_t):

- reflection (t\mapsto-t) has the unique fixed lift (t=0);
- the trivial action (t\mapsto t) leaves every lift fixed and selects
  nothing;
- affine reflection (t\mapsto1-t) selects the unique lift (t=1/2), showing
  that symmetry can choose a shearing origin unless its affine action is
  independently source-derived and compatible with the protected record.

Symmetry equivariance and logical nondisturbance are therefore separate. The
unique fixed lift need not satisfy (LBH=0).

## Authority boundary

Group averaging constructs a mathematical affine combination. A physical or
arithmetic actuator family may be discrete, nonlinear, resource constrained,
or not closed under coherent averaging. In that case the averaged lift is not
an executable constructor. One must verify:

1. the group action is source-derived;
2. the authorized actuator set is closed under the required average;
3. the fixed lift preserves protected records and successor coherence;
4. averaging constants and actuator norms remain uniform through completion.

Thus symmetry may trivialize the mathematical torsor without compiling a
physical repair.

## Falsifiers

- (V^G\ne0), leaving multiple equivariant lifts.
- The unique equivariant lift shears a protected record.
- The affine group action is chosen to fix the desired actuator.
- The authorized actuator set is not closed under averaging.
- Cutoff symmetries drift or their averaged lift norms diverge.
