# Pairwise block-decoupling criterion for \(D(S_3)\) control

## Question

Suppose the dynamical Lie algebra projects surjectively onto every simple endpoint block. Does that prove independent control of all blocks?

For the noncentral \(D(S_3)\) target, write

\[
\mathfrak g
=
\mathfrak{su}_2^{\oplus4}
\oplus
\mathfrak{su}_3^{\oplus2}.
\]

Let \(\mathfrak L\subseteq\mathfrak g\) be the Lie algebra generated by the source-authorized Hamiltonians.

## Claim boundary

Surjectivity of every single-block projection

\[
\pi_i:\mathfrak L\longrightarrow\mathfrak g_i
\]

does not imply \(\mathfrak L=\mathfrak g\). Isomorphic simple factors can be diagonally correlated.

For two simple factors \(\mathfrak g_i,\mathfrak g_j\), a subalgebra projecting surjectively to both is either their full direct sum or, when the factors are isomorphic, the graph of an isomorphism after quotienting by the projection kernels. In the graph case,

\[
\pi_{ij}(\mathfrak L)
=
\{(X,\varphi(X)):X\in\mathfrak g_i\}
\]

for a Lie algebra isomorphism \(\varphi\). Its dimension is \(\dim\mathfrak g_i\), not twice that dimension.

For a subdirect product of finite-dimensional simple Lie algebras, these graph correlations partition isomorphic factors into diagonal equivalence classes. Therefore \(\mathfrak L\) is the full direct sum if and only if:

1. every single-block projection is surjective;
2. every pair of isomorphic blocks has full pair projection.

For the \(D(S_3)\) target, this requires:

- four single-block \(\mathfrak{su}_2\) rank tests;
- two single-block \(\mathfrak{su}_3\) rank tests;
- six pair tests among the four \(\mathfrak{su}_2\) blocks;
- one pair test between the two \(\mathfrak{su}_3\) blocks.

The target pair dimensions are

\[
\dim(\mathfrak{su}_2\oplus\mathfrak{su}_2)=6,
\qquad
\dim(\mathfrak{su}_3\oplus\mathfrak{su}_3)=16.
\]

A correlated graph has dimension three or eight, respectively.

Pairs of nonisomorphic simple factors cannot be locked by a graph isomorphism, so no \(\mathfrak{su}_2\)-to-\(\mathfrak{su}_3\) decoupling test is needed once both single projections are surjective.

An equivalent constructive witness for decoupling blocks \(i,j\) is a Lie word whose projection vanishes on \(i\) but not on \(j\), together with the converse direction. A central separator alone is insufficient because it commutes and cannot create such a Lie word.

This theorem assumes the target factors are simple and that the generated algebra is evaluated as a real compact Lie algebra. It does not supply the actual port Hamiltonians or prove their source authority.

## Disposition

The full \(28\)-dimensional rank is a consequence of block surjectivity plus seven same-size pair-decoupling tests. A total-dimension computation remains useful but hides which blocks are correlated.

The first falsifier is one of:

1. only single-block surjectivity is checked;
2. repeated-size blocks are assumed independent because their labels differ;
3. a central projector is counted as a noncentral decoupling direction;
4. complex associative projections are substituted for real Lie projections;
5. a pair projection has graph dimension but is reported as independent;
6. a non-simple block is included without refining the criterion.

Once explicit transposition and three-cycle Hamiltonians are supplied, the smallest diagnostic report is the six single-block ranks, seven same-size pair ranks, total Lie rank, and a decoupling witness for every full pair.
