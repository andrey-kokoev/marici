# What the deletion shadows reconstruct

## Question

What structural information follows from the specific ways that one-dimensional deletion broke the Fourier boundary mate?

## Claim boundary

The failure pattern reconstructs the dependency geometry of the boundary object, not merely its dimension.

First, the native coordinates form constructor orbits:

\[
1\leftrightarrow\delta_0,\qquad
K\mapsto V\mapsto-K.
\]

Thus the boundary is not a direct list of four unrelated observables. It is a representation generated by transport. Removing any native coordinate cuts an orbit edge, and closing the remainder under Fourier transport regenerates the deleted coordinate.

Second, over the real numbers the representation type is

\[
W_{\mathbb R}\cong
W_{+1}\oplus W_{-1}\oplus W_{\mathrm{rot}},
\qquad
\dim W_{\mathrm{rot}}=2.
\]

The symmetric and antisymmetric combinations of \(1,\delta_0\) are real one-dimensional characters. The pair \(K,V\) is a real rotation plane with \(\mathcal F^2=-I\); neither orientation quadrature is a real invariant line. Hence the intrinsic factorization is \(1+1+2\), not four scalar channels.

Third, the endpoint pair and orientation pair have different algebraic types. The first is involutive and splits over the reals. The second is complex-type and splits only after complexification into the \(i\) and \(-i\) characters. Any real constructor claiming to preserve only \(K\) or only \(V\) is therefore structurally incomplete.

Fourth, asymmetric deletion identifies directionality:

- source-only deletion produces a cokernel and diagnoses a controllability or synthesis deficit;
- behavior-only deletion produces a kernel and diagnoses an observability deficit;
- bilateral deletion produces a coherent quotient and diagnoses task erasure rather than repair.

Therefore kernel and cokernel are shadows of opposite missing arrows. Their difference gives the index, while their ordered pair retains which side failed.

Fifth, covariance and completeness are independent. Character-line deletion preserves Fourier covariance, so internal commutativity tests cannot detect it. A theory may be perfectly coherent on a proper invariant summand. Completeness requires a conservative comparison against the declared ambient source object or against external contexts that couple to every character.

Sixth, the full four-dimensional packet is minimal relative to the admitted Fourier constructor. This is not absolute ontological minimality. If the constructor theory is weakened, dimensions become removable. Conversely, preserving the full constructor orbit forces them back. Minimality is therefore a property of an object together with its allowed operations.

Seventh, the shadows constrain the missing Green–Ward construction. Since all four boundary sectors are already forced by transport closure, the present obstruction is unlikely to be solved by appending an untyped fifth scalar observable. The more economical missing datum is a typed incidence or mate arrow connecting the source and behavior copies of the existing \(1+1+2\) representation.

Eighth, the tiny modular tail should be located by the orbit edge it repairs. If it repairs seam orientation, it must act in or couple to the \(K,V\) rotation plane. If it changes only the trivial scalar character, it cannot repair the missing orientation sector. If it couples the endpoint and rotation blocks, that coupling is new constructor data and must be derived from the source.

The deletion graph determines adjacency, orbit length, invariant subspaces, and sidedness of failure. It does not by itself determine normalization, the sign of every transport coefficient, analytic topology, or source authority. Those require the actual operator and Gram data.

## Disposition

The strongest inference is that the boundary object is an operation-generated representation with real type \(1+1+2\), and that the unresolved problem is probably relational rather than dimensional. We appear to have enough boundary coordinates; what is missing is the source-authorized mate that connects them without collapsing their character structure.

The next highest-information experiment is therefore arrow deletion rather than further object deletion: remove each incidence map between the source, endpoint, seam, and orientation blocks one at a time. The resulting kernel/cokernel signature should reconstruct the minimal Green–Ward wiring diagram and identify the unique edge on which the modular tail can act.
