# Five lanes share a kernel, image, and section obstruction

Owner: `marici.Kitaev`

## Bounded question

What common theorem now connects the constructor-algebra, objective-record,
predictive-realization, common-mode-fault, and scrambling lanes? Which
independent experiment has the highest information gain in each lane?

The common object is an exposed quotient together with its source-authorized
constructor image. Hidden distinctions live in the quotient kernel. Missing
capabilities live outside the constructor image. Near-kernel directions are
measured by a reconstruction margin. A decoder, actuator, realization map, or
recording instrument is a section or lift whose physical typing remains an
independent theorem.

## Frozen interface schema

Let (X) be a source carrier and let

\[
q:X\longrightarrow Q
\]

be the exposed observation, record, or operational quotient. Let
(\mathcal A) be the frozen source-authorized constructor family on (X).

A constructor (C\in\mathcal A) descends when there is a map (\bar C) on (Q)
such that

\[
qC=\bar Cq.
\]

Let (\mathcal A_q) be the descending constructors. Their exposed action is

\[
\Pi_q:\mathcal A_q\longrightarrow\operatorname{End}(Q),
\qquad
C\longmapsto\bar C.
\]

This schema has four independent structural coordinates.

### Hidden state directions

In a linear model,

\[
K_{\mathrm{state}}=\ker q.
\]

These source differences are invisible to the current exposed packet.

### Hidden transformations

For a group or category of reversible constructors, define

\[
K_{\mathrm{trans}}
=
\{g:qg=q\}.
\]

These transformations preserve every current exposed value while moving the
source realization or its semantic frame.

### Constructor image deficit

A desired exposed operation (T) is source-liftable only when

\[
T\in\operatorname{im}\Pi_q.
\]

In a linearized setting, its obstruction is the class of (T) in

\[
\operatorname{coker}\Pi_q.
\]

For groups, semigroups, and instrument categories, use image membership or
reachable-orbit language rather than forcing an additive cokernel.

### Section or lift

An implementation of (T) is a source-authorized (C) satisfying

\[
\Pi_q(C)=T.
\]

If one lift exists, other lifts can differ by invisible source action. In
linear problems they form an affine torsor over a kernel shear space. A
preferred lift therefore requires locality, symmetry, nondisturbance, minimum
cost, or another source-derived selection law.

## Linear exact-sequence shadow

When the constructor spaces are linear, the common skeleton is

\[
0
\longrightarrow

\longrightarrow

\xrightarrow{\Pi_q}
\operatorname{im}\Pi_q
\longrightarrow
0.
\]

Embedding the image in the desired exposed constructor space adds the
obstruction quotient

\[
\operatorname{End}_{\mathrm{desired}}(Q)
\big/
\operatorname{im}\Pi_q.
\]

This is not asserted as one universal abelian exact sequence for every quantum
instrument category. It is the finite linear shadow of a more general
kernel--image--lifting pattern.

## Quantitative near-kernel

Equip source and observation spaces with frozen norms. For linear (q), define

\[
\mu(q)=\inf_{\|x\|=1}\|qx\|.
\]

Exact kernel absence is (\mu>0) at one finite stage. Uniform stable recovery
requires a lower bound independent of size, time, or cutoff.

When (\mu\to0), finite injectivity survives while every left inverse has norm
at least (1/\mu). The interface approaches a genuine kernel.

Thus the exact and approximate obstructions are:

- kernel: invisible distinctions;
- cokernel or image deficit: unavailable controls;
- small singular value: unstable distinction;
- unbounded section: unstable implementation.

## Two orthogonal repairs

The schema predicts two fundamentally different repairs.

### Refine the exposed packet

Add a source-derived observation or reference

\[
r:X\longrightarrow R
\]

and replace (q) by ((q,r)). This shrinks the hidden kernel when (r) is
nontrivial on (\ker q) or separates a hidden transformation orbit.

Examples include a seam port, noncontractible loop probe, independent gauge
anchor, ancillary entanglement test, or region label.

### Enlarge the authorized constructor family

Add a source-authorized constructor to (\mathcal A). This enlarges
(\operatorname{im}\Pi_q) and may remove a lift obstruction.

Examples include a controlled-inversion magic resource, code switch, coherent
fusion-tree interferometer, environment access port, or long-range
communication channel.

Adding an observation cannot synthesize a missing actuator. Adding an actuator
cannot make an invisible semantic distinction observable. The two repairs are
orthogonal.

## Cross-lane theorem

A claimed capability or explanation is operationally closed only when:

1. every distinction used by the claim survives the exposed quotient with a
   uniform margin;
2. every hidden transformation that changes the claim is separated by an
   independent reference;
3. every claimed continuation lies in the source-authorized constructor image;
4. a physical section exists with the required positivity, locality, record,
   and fault structure;
5. the packet remains uniform under the declared completion or scaling limit.

Failure modes are classified, not merely detected:

- item one fails by state kernel or singular collapse;
- item two fails by common-mode symmetry;
- item three fails by image deficit;
- item four fails by nonphysical or unauthorized lift;
- item five fails by escaping cost, depth, correlation, or inverse norm.

## Critics

### Kernels and cokernels are only linear algebra

The exact additive terms apply to linearized finite packets. For groups,
semigroups, channels, and categories, use stabilizers, fibres, images, orbits,
and lifting spaces. The common explanatory pattern is typed rather than forced
into one coefficient language.

### Adding enough tests always removes the kernel

Only mathematically. Tests must be source-authorized, physically executable,
and independently calibrated. Some distinctions are intentionally quotiented
by superselection, privacy, gauge, or target irrelevance.

### Universal control removes every image deficit

Only if universal control is actually generated with bounded resources and the
required instruments, references, and fault contracts. Abstract algebraic
universality is not that theorem.

### A section can be chosen by pseudoinverse

The pseudoinverse uses a metric and may violate positivity, locality,
nondisturbance, symmetry, or gate authority. A preferred section is a source
selection theorem.

### These five experiments are too implementation-heavy

Each is the smallest test crossing a currently hidden cut. A purely scalar
successor would remain inside the existing saturation and have lower
information gain.
