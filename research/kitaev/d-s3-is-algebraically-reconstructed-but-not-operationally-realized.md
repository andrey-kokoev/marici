# `D(S3)` is algebraically reconstructed but not operationally realized

Owner: `marici.Kitaev`

## Bounded question

Against the complete-order and bounded-propagation realization ladder, what is
the strongest `D(S3)` equivalence actually proved by the current finite
programme? What is the smallest pair of realizations that the present central
and rank certificates still fail to distinguish?

## Verdict first

The programme has an exact source-presented algebraic topological model, not a
single complete operational reconstruction theorem.

It has separately established:

- the eight simple sectors and exact fusion ring;
- exact modular (S,T) data and normalized monodromy;
- the eight-dimensional central readout algebra;
- the 36-dimensional represented endpoint block algebra;
- associative generation by one transposition and one three-cycle port;
- projective block Lie control with a typed central deficit;
- local square and ribbon source formulas;
- several exact conditional instruments and an executable stabilizer
  subcompiler;
- a finite obstruction to the full frozen fault-tolerant compiler.

These packets do not jointly prove that one physically executable,
ancilla-complete, region-labelled tester tower reconstructs the local
instrument net. Source derivation, algebraic span, operational tomography, and
physical compilation remain different authorities.

The strongest concise status is:

```text
source-presented algebraic topological realization
    + exact central scalar tomography
    + partial conditional instruments
    + executable stabilizer subtheory
    - complete operational endpoint tomography
    - bounded-propagation realization equivalence
    - full fault-tolerant constructor compiler
```

## What the central tester packet reconstructs

On

\[
\mathcal H=\bigoplus_{a=1}^8V_a,
\qquad
\sum_a\dim V_a=16,
\]

every frozen central probe acts as

\[
O_f=\bigoplus_af(a)I_{V_a}.
\]

The selected normalized monodromy probes have rank eight on sector values.
They therefore reconstruct exactly the eight sector weights.

The real Hermitian ambient operator space has dimension 256. The central
readout kernel has dimension

\[
248=220+28,
\]

with 220 inter-sector coherence directions and 28 traceless within-block
directions.

Thus central operational equivalence is an exact theorem, but its quotient is
the classical sector simplex. It is not endpoint-state, channel, or instrument
equivalence.

## What the endpoint algebra establishes

The represented quantum-double endpoint algebra is

\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{end}}
=
\bigoplus_a\operatorname{End}(V_a),
\]

with complex dimension

\[
\sum_a(\dim V_a)^2=36.
\]

The exact image rank is 36, and the two selected element-flux ports together
with gauge actions generate this algebra associatively.

This proves that no further algebraic matrix direction is missing inside
(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{end}}). It does not prove:

- that every algebra element is an independently addressable Hamiltonian;
- that its exponential is reachable with permitted signs and times;
- that controlled versions exist;
- that a complete set of input states and ancillary effects can perform
  process tomography;
- that the resulting operations preserve the microscopic gauge and local
  excitation constraints.

The endpoint span is a coefficient-algebra theorem, not yet a complete-order
operational reconstruction.

## Associative generation versus dynamical realization

The two-port Hermitian Lie closure has dimension 33 rather than 36. Its derived
algebra contains all 28 traceless block directions, so conjugation control is
projectively complete. Its central trace rank is five.

The compiled source extension reaches dimension 34 with center rank six and
separates all eight sectors. Two independent central phase directions remain
absent from full blockwise unitary control.

Consequently:

- associative generation proves matrix-algebra availability;
- Lie generation proves continuous directions under an ideal control model;
- named pulse synthesis would prove reachability of specified targets;
- an instrument compiler would prove controlled use and records;
- a fault-tolerant compiler would prove stable physical realization.

The current programme reaches each line to a different depth. No one line may
stand in for the next.

## Fusion and modular reconstruction boundary

The fusion ring has been derived directly from the Hopf coproduct and again
from exact modular data through Verlinde. This independent agreement is a
strong consistency theorem.

It still reconstructs only isomorphism classes and multiplicities. The modular
pair records closed-link and twist invariants. Neither packet fixes:

- bases of trivalent spaces;
- associator phases or (F) moves;
- channel-wise braiding maps;
- pentagon and hexagon witnesses;
- boundary and defect categories;
- microscopic ribbon instruments;
- stable equivalence after stacking invertible phases.

Multiplicity-free fusion reduces some basis freedom but does not convert
fusion coefficients into coherent reassociation constructors.

\qquad

Equivalently, compare both exact element ports with a trusted ordered pair. The

## State-level hostile witness inside one realization

There is an even smaller state-tomography witness. Inside the two-dimensional
sector (C), choose two distinct density operators with the same trace, such as
the two computational basis projectors. Every central probe gives them the
same expectation because it is scalar on (V_C).

This witnesses the 28-dimensional within-block kernel. It is not a realization

## Locality hostile family

For each lattice size, conjugate the frozen algebraic model by a global unitary
whose image of one edge operator has diameter proportional to system size.
The transported global scalar and endpoint-algebra packets can remain
isomorphic.

No uniform bounded-propagation radius exists. Therefore any reconstruction
using only global algebra and scalar link data leaves a nonlocal hostile family
unless region-labelled contexts are included.

The right-frame pair is the minimal semantic witness; the global-unitary family
is the necessary all-size locality witness.

## Instrument hostile pair

A native controlled-inversion gate and a magic-state injection gadget may
implement the same ideal logical channel. They differ in consumed resource
state, Bell records, feed-forward, factory correlations, and fault propagation.

Channel tomography that discards these ports identifies them. Instrument and
implementation tomography does not. This is the smallest current example of
why equal ideal endpoint channels do not prove equal physical constructors.

## DPC: do not confuse knowing the model with reconstructing the realization

The conjecture is:

> A source-presented topological model and a predictively reconstructed
> topological realization are different achievements. Reconstruction requires
> a complete operational tower that removes state, transpose, branch,
> reference, and locality ambiguities. Physical realization additionally
> requires a compiler for the transported generators. The smallest surviving
> ambiguity identifies the next experiment.

In the present `D(S3)` packet, the smallest ambiguity is the right-frame
three-cycle reversal. The largest is the absence of a uniform executable local
instrument net.

## Critics

### The exact source matrices already determine everything

They determine the declared mathematical model. They do not prove that an
unknown apparatus is that realization or that its source-authorized controls
implement those matrices.

### Modular data plus fusion is a complete anyon theory here

It is a strong invariant packet and recovers the fusion ring in this model. The
programme has not proved a uniqueness theorem for coherent associator,
boundary, defect, and implementation data from those scalars.

### Simultaneous conjugation is only gauge

It is gauge if all external specifications and consumers are transported. It
is a calibration error if an independently anchored boundary or recovery
action is held fixed. The equivalence packet must state which convention is
quotiented.

### Full endpoint rank proves complete operational tomography

Rank 36 proves algebraic span of represented endpoint operators. Tomography
also requires executable preparations, ancillas, effects, and branch access.

### The local square proves bounded-propagation equivalence

It proves support for one source model at one finite cell. A realization theorem
compares systems uniformly across sizes and requires an inverse support bound.

### The full compiler is only an engineering detail

The missing operations include controlled inversion, controlled phases, and
coherent lookup. They determine which abstract contexts are executable and
which faults correlate. They are part of the theory's counterfactual content.
