# Unconstrained zero-free renormalization is equivalent to the target zero-free theorem

## Question

Raw Euler stages are zero-free but fail compact-open completion across the
critical strip.  Can the programme now seek an arbitrary parameter-dependent
zero-free counterterm `R_N` that repairs the family?

Not without additional source restrictions.  Existence of such a repair is
equivalent to the desired zero-free conclusion.

## Equivalence theorem

Let `Omega` be connected, let every `E_N` be a holomorphic unit on `Omega`,
and let `X` be a nonzero holomorphic target section.

The following are equivalent:

1. `X` is a holomorphic unit on `Omega`.
2. There are holomorphic units `R_N` such that

\[
R_NE_N\longrightarrow X
\]

locally uniformly on `Omega`.

The forward implication from item 2 to item 1 is Hurwitz: the limit is either
a unit or identically zero, and the latter is excluded.

The reverse implication is tautological.  If `X` is a unit, set

\[
R_N=XE_N^{-1}.
\]

Then every `R_N` is a unit and `R_NE_N=X` at every cutoff.

Therefore an unrestricted counterterm compiler neither weakens nor explains
the target theorem.  It merely moves the desired divisor statement into the
admissibility of `R_N`.

## Cocycle naturality does not repair the circularity

Suppose the finite transition is

\[
E_{N+1}=g_{N+1}E_N.
\]

The tautological repair satisfies

\[
R_{N+1}=R_Ng_{N+1}^{-1}.
\]

Thus exact cutoff cocycle compatibility, by itself, does not distinguish a
source-derived renormalization from the fitted answer.  Nor do base-point
normalization, zero-free transition factors, or exact agreement with `X`.
All are inherited automatically by the quotient construction whenever `X`
is already a unit.

## What makes a repair explanatory

The admissible counterterm class must be frozen before the target section or
its divisor is inspected.  At minimum it needs:

1. generators derived from labelled theta/Tate source operations;
2. a bounded locality or finite-constructor rule at each cutoff;
3. fixed sheet character and determinant-line frame;
4. endpoint, gamma, primitive, square, and seam incidence laws;
5. a composition rule independent of the completed scalar;
6. counterfactual predictions under admitted source perturbations;
7. a completion theorem for the resulting generator orbit.

Only then can failure to construct `R_N` be informative and success be a
mechanism rather than a restatement.

## Parity and index as prior constraints

Aspect's optical reverse-incidence audit supplies a model of a genuine source
restriction.  If `d` and an odd return `Q` obey

\[
dQ+Qd=aI,
\qquad
a\ne0,
\]

then the complex is contractible.  In finite dimension its even and odd mode
counts must balance.  In a Fredholm completion the corresponding index must
vanish.

This constraint cannot be manufactured by a scalar counterterm.  An unpaired
hidden mode obstructs the contracting identity even when a visible even probe
falsely certifies it.  The odd probe or hidden-port residual exposes the
defect.

For theta/Tate this suggests a prior audit of every proposed reverse
incidence and renormalization system:

- freeze the source grading;
- prove finite even--odd balance under every cutoff inclusion;
- identify the completed Fredholm index;
- reject counterterms that remove a scalar divergence while leaving an
  unpaired hidden boundary mode.

Index balance does not prove zero confinement.  It constrains the admissible
counterterm orbit before the RH-equivalent Hurwitz gate is reached.

## Behavioral formulation

Let `G_src` be the monoid generated by authorized counterterm operations.  The
noncircular question is not whether some unit `R_N` exists.  It is whether

\[
R_N\in G_{src}
\]

for a sequence determined without `X`, and whether its orbit is compact-open
normal.

Two target sections with different divisors must induce distinguishable
responses under some admitted generator or perturbation.  If the generator
monoid is defined only by successful convergence to the chosen target, it has
no independent empirical or constructor content.

## Falsifiers

- Defining `R_N=X/E_N` or an algebraically disguised equivalent.
- Choosing counterterm coefficients by fitting completed scalar values.
- Treating cutoff cocycle compatibility as provenance.
- A visible scalar identity with nonzero hidden odd-port residual.
- Finite contracting homotopies with an unbalanced limiting Fredholm index.
- A generator family that changes when the hostile target divisor changes.
- Counterterms authorized solely because they make the Pick or Hurwitz test
  pass.

## Verdict

The completion problem cannot be stated as unrestricted counterterm
synthesis.  That problem is exactly equivalent to the zero-free target.

The productive question is narrower:

> Does the independently frozen theta/Tate counterterm monoid contain a
> parity-balanced, source-natural orbit whose normalized determinant sections
> form a compact-open family?

The source restrictions, especially hidden-mode index balance, must be proved
before taking the scalar limit.  They are the possible explanatory content;
the mere existence of a zero-free renormalization is not.
