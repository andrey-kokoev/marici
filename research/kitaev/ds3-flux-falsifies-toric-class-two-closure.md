# The \(D(S_3)\) flux sector falsifies toric class-two closure

## Question

Does the toric result

\[
\Gamma_3=1
\]

extend to the nonabelian \(D(S_3)\) endpoint sector?

It is enough to test the \(S_3\) flux subgroup represented inside the endpoint constructor algebra.

## Claim boundary

Use the commutator convention

\[
[x,y]=xyx^{-1}y^{-1}.
\]

Let

\[
a=(12),
\qquad
b=(23).
\]

Since transpositions are self-inverse,

\[
[a,b]=abab=(132).
\]

This commutator is not central in \(S_3\). A second commutator remains nontrivial:

\[
[[a,b],a]
=
[(132),(12)]
=
(123).
\]

Therefore the class-two identity

\[
[[x,y],z]=1
\]

already fails on the smallest transposition pair.

Group-theoretically,

\[
\Gamma_1(S_3)=S_3,
\qquad
\Gamma_2(S_3)=A_3,
\qquad
\Gamma_3(S_3)=[S_3,A_3]=A_3.
\]

The lower central series stabilizes at the nontrivial subgroup \(A_3\); \(S_3\) is not nilpotent.

Consequences for coefficient lenses:

1. Abelianized flux labels see only
   \[
   S_3^{\mathrm{ab}}\simeq C_2.
   \]
2. First commutators recover three-cycle information in \(A_3\).
3. Nested commutators remain nontrivial and cannot be reduced to one central bilinear sign.
4. A pairwise intersection form is therefore insufficient to determine all ordered flux words.

This does not contradict the earlier result that one transposition-resolved port and one three-cycle-resolved port generate the full 36-dimensional \(D(S_3)\) endpoint algebra. Port generation and commutator depth are different invariants. Two typed ports may generate an algebra whose ordered words have noncentral nested structure.

The calculation uses the \(S_3\) flux subgroup as a finite obstruction. It does not classify every braid or ribbon relation of \(D(S_3)\), establish physical executability of the two ports, or determine a minimal coherent word-interferometry basis for the full quantum double.

## Disposition

Toric self-closure is not a property of Carrier geometry alone. It depends on the abelian anyon coefficient lens producing a class-two Heisenberg extension.

The first discriminator between sectors is:

\[
\text{Are all represented commutators central?}
\]

If yes, additive labels plus the central 2-cocycle can close the ordered tower. If no, nested commutator probes are required until the represented constructor relations are separated.

The first falsifier is one of:

1. the claimed flux operations do not represent the stated \(S_3\) multiplication;
2. the commutator convention changes without recomputation;
3. a noncentral commutator is compressed to a scalar phase;
4. two generating ports are claimed to imply class-two nilpotence;
5. the \(S_3\) subgroup calculation is promoted to a complete \(D(S_3)\) braid classification;
6. algebraic nested structure is used to infer executable physical control.

The next finite audit should compute the filtration of the 36-dimensional endpoint algebra generated by words of increasing commutator depth and determine the first depth at which its linear span stabilizes.
