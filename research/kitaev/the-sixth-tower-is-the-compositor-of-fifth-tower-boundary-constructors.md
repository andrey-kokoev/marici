# The sixth tower is the compositor of fifth-tower boundary constructors

## Question

Is the parameter-dependent completion law merely another operation inside the
fifth tower, or is it a sixth-tower coherence law governing how several fifth
towers compose?

It is sixth-tower data exactly when it compares distinct composites of
already authorized fifth-tower constructors.  Parameter dependence alone is
not enough.

## Fifth-tower objects in the present programme

The current candidate fifth-tower constructors are active or boundary-valued
operations, not scalar observations:

- forward and reverse source--tail incidence;
- exponentiation of primitive and square boundary currents;
- cutoff bonding of sector determinant lines;
- reciprocal sheet sewing;
- archimedean and seam attachment;
- determinant formation from the completed operator relation.

Each answers a local composition question at one authority locus.  For
example, a cutoff bond says how a framed determinant at `X` is transported to
`Y`; reciprocal sewing says how plus and minus sector lines are paired.

## Promotion criterion

Let `B_YX` denote cutoff bonding, `S` reciprocal sewing, and `E` typed boundary
exponentiation.  There are multiple routes from the same source packet to the
same completed boundary object.  Two basic comparisons are:

```text
exponentiate at X, then transport to Y
versus
transport the currents to Y, then exponentiate;
```

and

```text
build the plus-sector frame, then sew
versus
reflect the source currents, build the minus-sector frame, then sew.
```

If these composites are not definitionally equal, a comparison cell is
required.  That cell is the next rung.  It specifies whether the two fifth-
tower operations are jointly composable and how their outputs are identified.

Thus a completion law belongs to the sixth tower when it provides coherent
cells such as

\[
\alpha_{Y,X}:B_{Y,X}E_X\Longrightarrow E_YT_{Y,X}
\]

and

\[
\rho_X:S_X(E_X^+\otimes E_X^-)
\Longrightarrow E_X^{\mathrm{sewn}}.
\]

Here `T_YX` transports the typed currents.  The cells are not new scalar
counterterms.  They license and compare compositions of fifth-tower
constructors.

## Coherence among many fifth towers

For `X subset Y subset Z`, cutoff composition creates two comparisons between
`B_ZY B_YX E_X` and `E_Z T_ZX`.  A sixth-tower associativity law requires
their agreement.  With four cutoffs, the corresponding comparison cells obey
the usual pentagon-shaped coherence.

There is also an interchange problem among independent axes:

- cutoff inclusion;
- reciprocal reflection;
- prime-power grade filtration;
- archimedean attachment;
- reverse incidence;
- determinant projection.

The sixth tower determines which rectangles and higher cubes commute, which
commute only up to a declared unit, and which composites are prohibited.  In
that sense it genuinely creates network semantics: it defines the admissible
paths through a net of fifth-tower objects.

## Odd-exponential gauge as a sixth-tower obstruction

The refactoring

\[
D_+(z)\mapsto e^{g(z)}D_+(z),
\qquad
g(-z)=-g(z),
\]

preserves the sewn scalar.  It therefore cannot be detected inside either
sector's scalar fifth-tower output.

Under cutoff composition, the differences of odd gauges form a cocycle.  A
source-framed compositor must trivialize that cocycle compatibly with both
cutoff bonding and reciprocal sewing.  Failure to do so is not a residual
scalar error.  It means the fifth-tower sector constructors do not assemble
into one global functor.

This is the best current candidate for a genuine sixth-tower obstruction.

## Relation to the Uber tower

The Uber tower should not be treated as one more local closure operation.  It
is the functorial network in which independently completed sector objects can
be composed.  On that reading, the sixth rung is the compositor data of the
Uber functor:

- it maps local fifth-tower constructors into network arrows;
- it supplies comparison cells between different paths;
- it records obstruction classes when no coherent comparison exists;
- it exposes new legal composites once a cell is supplied.

This matches the earlier correction that a higher tower need not close the
residue of lower towers.  It may instead expose and regulate new composition
capabilities.

## Falsification criteria

The sixth-tower interpretation fails in any of these cases:

1. There is only one fifth-tower constructor and no competing composite.
2. Every relevant square commutes definitionally after the source frame is
   frozen.
3. The apparent cocycle is removed by a unique local gauge with no effect on
   any other composition.
4. The proposed comparison cell changes scalar values but does not compare
   two typed routes with common endpoints.
5. The law is fitted from the completed scalar rather than defined on the
   fifth-tower network.

In cases 1--3, the parameter-dependent completion remains internal fifth-
tower structure.  A new tower number would add no information.

## Immediate application to theta/Tate

The decisive finite diagram is not one denominator identity but a cube:

```text
primitive/square currents
  x cutoff inclusion
  x reciprocal sheet
  -> framed sector determinants
  -> sewn determinant line.
```

One must compute all faces before scalar projection.  The sixth-tower law is
the assertion that the face comparisons themselves satisfy the cube
coherence.  The hidden-port parity/index condition adds another face: the
reverse incidence must commute with the same bonding and sewing cells.

If this cube closes, the parameter-dependent renormalization is no longer an
arbitrary repair.  It is the unique or classified compositor of the declared
fifth-tower network.  If it does not close, the obstruction identifies which
pair of local completion capabilities cannot coexist globally.

## Verdict

The proposed shift is structurally sound:

> The sixth tower is not a stronger scalar completion.  It is the coherence
> system that determines whether independently valid fifth-tower boundary
> constructors can be composed into one Ubersector functor.

The next test is to build the smallest cutoff--sheet--grade cube and determine
whether its odd-exponential and parity defects form a trivializable cocycle.
That result will decide whether a sixth rung is genuinely present or whether
the completion law remains strict fifth-tower functoriality.
