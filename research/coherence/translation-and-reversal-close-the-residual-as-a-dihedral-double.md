# Translation and reversal close the residual as a dihedral double

## Transport of effective charges

Under a common multiplicative displacement

\[
y_i\mapsto z y_i,
\]

the effective charges transform oppositely:

\[
q_{\rm in}\mapsto zq_{\rm in},
\qquad
q_{\rm out}\mapsto z^{-1}q_{\rm out}.
\]

On the stationary doubled carrier

\[
T=L_{\rm in}\oplus L_{\rm out},
\]

translation and reversal are therefore

\[
M(z)=
\begin{pmatrix}
z&0\\
0&z^{-1}
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
R=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

They satisfy

\[
R^2=I,
\qquad
RM(z)R=M(z)^{-1}.
\]

Thus the admitted context action closes as the two-dimensional representation of a multiplicative group extended by inversion.

## Two equivalent presentations

### Varying oriented type

\[
L_{\rightarrow}\xrightleftarrows[R]{}L_{\leftarrow}
\]

with translation weights \(z\) and \(z^{-1}\).

### Stationary double

\[
L_{\rm in}\oplus L_{\rm out}
\]

with the displayed diagonal translation and swapping reversal matrices.

These are not competing theories. They are the indexed and internalized presentations of the same context action.

## Minimality

A generic charge pair has a two-dimensional orbit under reversal and translation. The carrier collapses to one dimension only on special self-dual or anti-self-dual loci, or when the admitted context alphabet is reduced.

Hence the first stationary type generated by a residual is not an arbitrary two-state system. It is a dual-weight pair:

\[
\boxed{
T\cong L\oplus L^\vee.
}
\]

Its natural bilinear form is hyperbolic, and reversal exchanges the two isotropic lines.

## Consequence for the abstraction tower

The sequence is now more specific:

```text
oriented residual line
→ admit translation and reversal contexts
→ dual line appears
→ stationary hyperbolic double
```

This is a context completion. The second dimension is not hidden internal complexity; it is the minimal representation needed to make both directions available without changing types.

The relation

\[
RM(z)R=M(z)^{-1}
\]

also explains why direct and reciprocal presentations repeatedly occur together: they are the two weights of one completed residual type.
