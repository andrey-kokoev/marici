# The full signed-context realization is the massive Green RKHS

## Kernel realization

The full-rank context kernel

\[
k(x,y)=e^{-|x-y|}
\]

is the Green kernel of the one-dimensional massive operator:

\[
(1-\partial_x^2)e^{-|x-y|}=2\delta_y.
\]

Equip \(H^1(\mathbb R)\) with

\[
\langle f,g\rangle_{\mathcal H}
=
\frac12\int_{\mathbb R}
\left(f\overline g+f'\overline{g'}\right)dx.
\]

For

\[
k_y(x)=e^{-|x-y|},
\]

integration by parts gives

\[
\langle f,k_y\rangle_{\mathcal H}=f(y).
\]

Therefore \(\mathcal H=H^1(\mathbb R)\), with the displayed normalization, is the reproducing-kernel Hilbert space of the complete context kernel.

## Hankel factorization

For signed displacement words \(u,v\), let their real coordinates be \(x_u,x_v\). Then

\[
H(u,v)=e^{-|x_u+x_v|}
=\langle k_{x_u},k_{-x_v}\rangle_{\mathcal H}.
\]

Thus the infinite Hankel matrix has the canonical Kolmogorov factorization

\[
\text{prefix }u\mapsto k_{x_u},
\qquad
\text{suffix }v\mapsto k_{-x_v}.
\]

Every finite full-rank matrix is a Gram corner of this one Hilbert realization.

## Minimality

The closed linear span of kernel sections at the admitted context points is the minimal RKHS realization. For at least two multiplicatively independent labels, the signed logarithmic subgroup is dense in \(\mathbb R\). Since point evaluations are continuous and kernel sections vary continuously, their span is dense in all of \(H^1(\mathbb R)\).

Hence the complete prime-labelled signed-context realization is

\[
\mathcal H_{\rm min}=H^1(\mathbb R).
\]

It is infinite-dimensional but generated by a local differential operator of order two.

## Symmetry action

Translations and reflection act by

\[
(U_af)(x)=f(x-a),
\qquad
(Jf)(x)=f(-x).
\]

They are unitary on \(\mathcal H\) and satisfy

\[
JU_aJ=U_{-a}.
\]

On kernel states,

\[
U_ak_x=k_{x+a},
\qquad
Jk_x=k_{-x}.
\]

Thus the full infinite realization carries the same translation/reversal relation as the finite hyperbolic double, but without collapsing independently observable context points.

## Main distinction

Three complexities must now remain separate:

```text
orbit dimension       = 1
local differential order = 2
behavioral state dimension = infinite
```

Infinite contextual memory does not require an infinite-order law. A second-order local Green operator generates it.

## Relation to the finite double

The two-dimensional incoming/outgoing carrier is a boundary jet or protocol quotient of this RKHS. It is exact for the frozen common-translation/reversal protocol but cannot represent all independently labelled signed evaluations.

The next useful map is therefore not another abstract completion. It is an explicit boundary observation

\[
H^1(\mathbb R)\longrightarrow L_{\rm in}\oplus L_{\rm out}
\]

with a declaration of which context distinctions its kernel discards.
