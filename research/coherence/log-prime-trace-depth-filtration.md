# Four-cup readout requires a signed-log trace-depth filtration

## Why subset-sum traces are insufficient

The trace bundle on positive subset sums is adequate for the directed four-cube itself, but not for the mixed window operators. If \(b>a\),

\[
K_{q,p}=P_aR_{b-a},
\]

so even its endpoint value contains

\[
(K_{q,p}f)(0)=f(b-a).
\]

The point \(b-a=\log(q/p)\) is generally not a positive subset sum of prime logarithms. Hence \(K_{q,p}\) does not factor through the previous 31-dimensional trace fiber. Interior evaluation data were missing.

## Depth-filtered evaluation fibers

For word depth \(k\), define

\[
\Gamma_k^+
=
\left\{
\sum_{i=0}^3n_i\log p_i\ge0:
 n_i\in\mathbb Z,
 \sum_i|n_i|\le k
\right\}.
\]

Let \(\mathcal D^{(k)}\) be the broken \(H^1\) graph with break/evaluation coordinates at the positive points of \(\Gamma_k^+\), and let \(\mathcal T^{(k)}\) retain their one-sided traces.

A prime shift changes one coefficient by \(\pm1\), so it has the exact type

\[
R_p,S_p:
\mathcal D^{(k)}\longrightarrow\mathcal D^{(k+1)}.
\]

Consequently:

- an edge lives from depth zero to one;
- a mixed square residual is readable at depth two;
- its covariant cube comparison is readable at depth three;
- the four-cup is readable at depth four.

This realizes the power sequence as graph-domain depth rather than as an unsupported dimensional analogy.

## Four-prime census

For \((2,3,5,7)\), the nonnegative evaluation counts are

\[
1,\ 5,\ 21,\ 65,\ 161
\]

at depths zero through four. Excluding the endpoint, the corresponding positive breakpoint counts are

\[
0,\ 4,\ 20,\ 64,\ 160.
\]

All are finite. The checker verifies that every admitted \(\pm\log p\) displacement sends a depth-\(k\) coordinate into depth \(k+1\).

## Interpretation

There is no fixed finite boundary observer through which every higher operation factors. The observer itself is filtered by relational depth:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal T^{(0)}
\to
\mathcal T^{(1)}
\to
\mathcal T^{(2)}
\to
\mathcal T^{(3)}
\to
\mathcal T^{(4)}.
}
\]

The fourth-rank readout belongs naturally to \(\mathcal T^{(4)}\). Compressing it back to the subset-sum trace space before evaluation discards the signed-log coordinates generated by mixed direct/adjoint transport.

For unbounded depth the signed-log set is dense, so the total object remains a projective diagram of finite-depth fibers, not one locally finite broken line.
