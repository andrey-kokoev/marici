# Observation pencils and coherence rank reset

## Status

Exploratory conjecture. This note records a geometric intuition before choosing a categorical implementation. It does not establish a new Coherence Pyramid, a physical observer, or an iterable rank-reset operation.

## Root intuition

The expression

\[
\operatorname{observation}=\operatorname{primitive}^{2}
\]

is an incidence-rank statement, not a numerical or metaphysical identity.

A primitive has positional rank within one Coherence Pyramid. An observation is a possible typed line connecting primitive positions across adjacent Pyramid contexts:

\[
(\mathfrak P_k,p_k)\xrightarrow{\ell_{k,k+1}}
(\mathfrak P_{k+1},p_{k+1}).
\]

The line retains both primitive occurrences and the connecting movement. Two endpoints without an admitted connection do not constitute an observation. Reversal is not assumed.

## From observation to observer

The observer need not be presupposed as an entity. A family of stable observation lines may form a pencil

\[
\Pi=\{\ell_\lambda\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}.
\]

Its observer is the coherence invariant of that pencil: depending on the geometry, a common center, base locus, or object classifying the family.

Thus the proposed order is

\[
\text{observations}
\longrightarrow
\text{stable pencil}
\longrightarrow
\text{observer}.
\]

Three cases must remain distinct:

1. a concurrent pencil yields a global observer position;
2. local concurrency with holonomy yields only a twisted or local observer;
3. a nonconcurrent family may contain observations without forming one observer.

This permits observation to exist globally as relational data even when no global endpoint observer can be selected.

## Dimensional ascent

The smallest proposed ladder is

\[
\begin{aligned}
P^1&=\text{primitive position},\\
P^2&=\text{observation line},\\
P^3&=\text{oriented comparison between direct and mediated routes},\\
P^4&=\text{tetrahedral coherence boundary and its curvature}.
\end{aligned}
\]

For three positions, a comparison face relates

\[
\ell_{12}\circ\ell_{01}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\ell_{02}.
\]

Its direction is source-derived; lax, oplax, invertible, and absent comparisons are different cases.

For four positions, four comparison faces can form a tetrahedral shell. A closed shell does not imply a filler. Its boundary may carry nontrivial curvature \(\Omega\).

## Contextual rank reset

The central conjecture is that classified coherence at one level can become primitive geometry at the next:

\[
P_n^1\to P_n^2\to P_n^3\to P_n^4
\xrightarrow{\tau}
P_{n+1}^1.
\]

This is a contextual rank reset: externally extended geometry at level \(n\) becomes internally retained structure of a point-like primitive at level \(n+1\).

Rank reset is distinct from filling. Classification may produce a marked residual

\[
\tau_{\mathrm{class}}(S,\Omega)
=
\langle[\Omega],M_{\mathrm{regenerative}}\rangle,
\]

while possible fillers form a separate fiber

\[
\mathcal F_\Omega
=
\{K\mid \delta K=\Omega\text{ and the required frame is preserved}\}.
\]

The fiber may be empty, noncanonical, or multiply connected. The classified residual can remain a next-level primitive even when no filler exists.

## Regenerative provenance

A bare quotient class may forget too much, while retaining the entire implementation may preserve irrelevant history. Rank reset should retain exactly the provenance needed to determine admissible next observations, including any source-required support, orientation, integral marking, exposed interface, and residual modality.

This requirement is relative to a frozen constructor depth. A later higher-order constructor may distinguish classes identified by an earlier contextual quotient. Primitive identity may therefore form a compatible refinement tower rather than a final context-independent quotient.

## Conductors and ramification

A curvature need not map directly to a selected primitive. The proposed turn may factor as

\[
\Omega\longrightarrow C_\Omega\rightsquigarrow p_{n+1},
\]

where \(C_\Omega\) is a primitive-bearing conductor and the second arrow denotes an optional section or selection.

The conductor is the interface where a next primitive becomes positionable; it is not automatically that primitive. A turn may therefore produce a point, a family of points, an unsectioned conductor, several destination branches, or no continuation.

The resulting geometry is potentially a ramified spiral:

\[
\text{dimensional expansion}
\longleftrightarrow
\text{conductor reconciliation and contextual restart}.
\]

Branches reconcile through a common conductor rather than by losing their identities.

## Higher-level interpretation

The Pyramid may be an invariant form for changing what counts as primitive:

\[
\text{primitive from above}
=
\text{classified coherence from below}.
\]

This would make computation capable of generating its next vocabulary from prior coherence. Residuals would not merely be errors; suitably classified residuals could seed new domains of possible observation.

## Live rivals

1. **Terminal curvature:** curvature is only an obstruction and cannot seed further geometry.
2. **Lossy compression:** rank reset discards information needed to regenerate observation structure.
3. **No canonical turn:** curvature yields only an unresolved family with no adequate next primitive.
4. **Same-level interpretation:** residuals remain higher cells of one Pyramid; no contextual restart occurs.
5. **Static-total-geometry rival:** the apparent spiral consists only of successive views of one pre-existing higher incidence geometry.
6. **Notational recurrence:** the repeated Pyramid shape is imposed by the chosen description.

The strongest rival is the static-total-geometry interpretation. The conjecture differs by predicting genuinely new admissible continuations after coherence classification.

## First discriminators

A useful conceptual hostile should provide two primitive-bearing conductors with no global sections and ask whether:

1. a globally coherent observation relation exists between them;
2. it composes without selecting global endpoint observers;
3. its route comparisons carry nontrivial curvature;
4. classifying that curvature creates admissible continuations unavailable at the prior level.

Failure of (1) rejects relation-first observation. Success through (3) but failure of (4) favors the static-total-geometry rival. Success of all four gives distinctive support to the rank-reset spiral.

A second hostile should compare:

- shells with different hidden factorizations but identical admissible continuations, which rank reset should identify; and
- shells with equal residual readouts but different exposed continuations, which rank reset must distinguish.

## Open questions

- What exact stability law turns a family of observation lines into a pencil?
- Is the observer the pencil's center, its base locus, or the pencil as a coherent whole?
- Does the second primitive rank belong to the destination, the transition, or only their mixed incidence?
- What structure is conserved by contextual rank reset?
- When do ramified turns reconcile, and when do they remain separate?
- Does the spiral stabilize, remain open-ended, or reveal one higher geometry through progressively available primitives?
