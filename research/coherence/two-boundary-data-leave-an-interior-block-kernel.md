# Two boundary data leave an interior block kernel

## Boundary map

For a finite block \(u_0,\ldots,u_{n-1}\), the two opposite terminal readouts are

\[
P_{n-1}=
\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\rho^{n-1-j}u_j,
\]

\[
F_0=
\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\rho^ju_j.
\]

For \(n\ge2\) and \(0<\rho<1\), these covectors are independent. Hence the boundary map

\[
u\longmapsto(P_{n-1},F_0)
\]

has rank two.

Fixing both terminal values leaves an affine fiber of dimension

\[
n-2.
\]

Two-boundary data do not determine the interior block once it has more than two events.

## Compact compensated packets

Choose three interior sites \(a<b<c\). There is a nonzero coefficient triple supported at those sites that satisfies both boundary moment equations. It is obtained as the cross product of the two restricted boundary covectors.

For the resulting perturbation \(\delta u\),

\[
\delta P_{n-1}=0,
\qquad
\delta F_0=0.
\]

More strongly, its tail fields vanish outside the support interval:

\[
\delta P_i=
\delta F_i=0
\quad(i<a\text{ or }i>c).
\]

The disturbance is a compact interior consistency cell invisible to every observer cut outside \([a,c]\).

## Meaning for the block model

The global block has two layers:

```text
boundary class:
  the two terminal moments

interior class:
  compensated packets in the two-moment kernel
```

Boundary conditions select the first layer but leave the second free. A unique block requires additional interior equations, a variational principle, a source prior, or enough movable-cutoff observers to resolve the kernel.

## Locality of hidden freedom

The unresolved sector is not only an abstract global nullspace. It is generated by localized compensated packets. Interior modifications can therefore preserve both asymptotic boundaries without changing arbitrarily distant observer states.

This prevents an overly rigid interpretation of two-boundary determination:

```text
two boundaries constrain the block globally,
but do not generally determine it uniquely.
```

## Relation to rank reset

The dimension \(n-2\) is exactly the kernel previously discarded by the endpoint quotient. In the block interpretation, that same kernel is the space of boundary-invisible interior histories.

Thus “rank reset to two” means retaining the boundary class and forgetting which compensated interior block occurred.
